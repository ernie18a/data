Calculation: Source = a selected price derived from the bar's open, high, low, and close; high source and low source are separately selectable bar prices.
Calculation: Channel = volume-weighted polynomial least-squares regression of source against bar age from an anchor through the current bar; replace missing or nonpositive volume with equal unit weight. Choose a rolling anchor, the latest confirmed swing-low anchor, the latest confirmed swing-high anchor, or the more recent of the two swing anchors; fall back to the rolling anchor before a swing is confirmed. Swing pivots use equal left and right confirmation lengths. On a new anchor, seed with at most a maximum number of most recent bars; thereafter add each new bar. If the regression is singular, use the weighted mean. The center is the fitted value at the current bar; residual deviation is the square root of weighted residual sum of squares divided by total weight. Inner and outer bands are center plus or minus their respective deviation multiples.
Calculation: Divergence = compare the latest two confirmed price pivots of the same kind and their oscillator values at the pivot bars. The oscillator is RSI of close or a smoothed Stochastic of that RSI. A bearish divergence has a higher high pivot and a lower oscillator value; a bullish divergence has a lower low pivot and a higher oscillator value. Optionally require the latest pivot to have reached its corresponding inner or outer channel band at the pivot bar. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Sweep = track the latest two confirmed high pivots and low pivots, using a sweep-specific symmetric pivot length. When two successive highs or lows differ by at most an ATR multiple, retain their higher high or lower low as the respective equal-level until replaced. A high sweep occurs when high exceeds the retained equal-high level and close is below it, OR high exceeds the latest high pivot and close is below that pivot and below open. A low sweep occurs when low is below the retained equal-low level and close is above it, OR low is below the latest low pivot and close is above that pivot and above open.
Calculation: Consensus = count enabled and available factors whose rolling z-scores are at or beyond a positive extreme threshold or at or below its negative. A z-score is current factor minus its rolling mean divided by its rolling population standard deviation, with zero returned for zero deviation. The factors are: source minus session VWAP; the z-score of high source minus low source multiplied by the sign of the source change over a range lookback, using positive sign for zero change; signed efficiency ratio, defined as source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero; directional parabolic-SAR extension, defined as the nonnegative ATR-normalized distance from SAR to high source when source is above SAR or from low source to SAR when source is below SAR, capped at a maximum, z-scored and multiplied by the SAR direction; RSI or smoothed Stochastic RSI of source; source minus its rolling linear-regression fitted value divided by ATR, using zero when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive; Know Sure Thing of source using its ordered rate-of-change lookbacks, smoothing lengths and increasing weights; and cumulative signed volume minus its EMA, where signed volume is volume times twice the close's clamped fractional position in the high-source to low-source range minus one, using a midpoint fraction for a zero range. VWAP and signed-volume factors require cumulative volume to be positive; VWAP also requires an available session VWAP. The external-reference basis factor is unavailable from the specified data and is excluded. The consensus threshold is a configurable count, optionally selected by chart timeframe, capped at the number of available factors and at least one; consensus requires at least one available factor.
Calculation: Regime = variance of multi-bar log returns divided by the return horizon times variance of one-bar log returns, with a fallback ratio of one for zero one-bar variance; reversion score is the nonnegative part of the negative rolling correlation between the prior bar's close deviation from its moving average and the current close change. Select either ratio at or below one minus a threshold, score at or above a threshold, either condition, both conditions, or an unrestricted regime.
Calculation: Raw fade cooldown = bars since the most recent raw fade of either side; update the last raw-fade bar whenever either side tags its outer band, has at least one corresponding tell, and satisfies the cooldown, regardless of the regime or consensus gate. A tell is a same-bar close back inside the tagged outer band, a corresponding divergence within the divergence recency window, a corresponding sweep within the sweep recency window, or a current corresponding consensus when enabled.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Short: Bearish divergence.
