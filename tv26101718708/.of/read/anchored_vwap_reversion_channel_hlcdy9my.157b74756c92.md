Calculation: Anchored regression center and residual standard deviation = volume-weighted polynomial least-squares fit of the selected price source from the active anchor; the default anchor is the most recent confirmed swing high or low. Residual standard deviation is calculated around that fit.
Calculation: Upper and lower outer bands = anchored regression center plus and minus the configured outer-band multiple of residual standard deviation.
Calculation: Reversion regime = variance ratio at or below one minus its neutral band OR negative correlation between prior close deviation from its moving average and current close change, with the negative correlation magnitude at least its configured threshold. Variance ratio = variance of k-bar log returns divided by k times the variance of one-bar log returns.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high while its oscillator value is lower; the price pivot must reach the configured upper band. Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low while its oscillator value is higher; the price pivot must reach the configured lower band. Oscillator is RSI or smoothed Stochastic RSI.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price wicks beyond an equal-high or equal-low level and closes back inside; equal levels are consecutive confirmed pivot highs or lows within an ATR-based tolerance. A single confirmed pivot can also qualify when the close returns past it in the opposite candle direction.
Calculation: Multi-factor extreme consensus = at least the configured minimum number of enabled factors have z-scores beyond the configured positive or negative extreme: price minus session VWAP; high-low range signed by price change over its lookback; price efficiency (net change divided by summed absolute changes); Parabolic SAR extension in ATR units signed by SAR direction; RSI or Stochastic RSI; close deviation from a linear regression in ATR units; KST; and volume-based cumulative signed volume deviation from its EMA. Each factor is standardized by its rolling z-score. The external-reference basis factor is excluded because it is not derivable from the specified backtest fields.
Long: low <= lower outer band AND at least one of (close > lower outer band, a bullish divergence occurred within its recency window, a qualifying low sweep occurred within its freshness window, or negative multi-factor extreme consensus) AND the minimum-bar gap since the previous fade has elapsed.
Long: negative multi-factor extreme consensus.
Long: bullish divergence.
Short: high >= upper outer band AND at least one of (close < upper outer band, a bearish divergence occurred within its recency window, a qualifying high sweep occurred within its freshness window, or positive multi-factor extreme consensus) AND the minimum-bar gap since the previous fade has elapsed.
Short: positive multi-factor extreme consensus.
Short: bearish divergence.
