Calculation: Timeframe data = calculations run independently on each selected timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the chart bar for the chart timeframe, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Each timeframe has its own calculation parameters and weight.
Calculation: ATR trend = initialize bullish. On each timeframe bar, set basic lower and upper stops to source minus and plus ATR times a multiplier. Set the lower stop to the maximum of its basic value and previous lower stop if the previous close exceeded the previous lower stop; otherwise use its basic value. Set the upper stop to the minimum of its basic value and previous upper stop if the previous close was below the previous upper stop; otherwise use its basic value. Use the current basic stop when its previous stop is unavailable. Change bearish to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper stop; change bullish to bearish when close is below the previous lower stop; otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI trend = compute RSI of close, then its stochastic position over the same lookback; smooth that with an SMA to obtain K and smooth K with another SMA to obtain D. Set the trend bullish when K crosses above D and bearish when K crosses below D; otherwise retain the previous trend. The trend is undefined until the first cross.
Calculation: Timeframe bullish and bearish scores = respectively sum the indicator weights for ATR trend, stochastic RSI trend, and close versus its SMA when each is bullish or bearish. ATR has a greater weight than stochastic RSI and SMA, which have equal weights. An undefined trend or close equal to its SMA contributes to neither score.
Calculation: Overall bullish and bearish scores = separately sum each timeframe's corresponding score multiplied by its timeframe weight, divided by the sum of timeframe weights. The bias score is overall bullish score minus overall bearish score.
Long: ATR trend is bullish OR stochastic RSI trend is bullish OR timeframe close > its SMA
Short: ATR trend is bearish OR stochastic RSI trend is bearish OR timeframe close < its SMA
Long: timeframe bullish score > timeframe bearish score OR overall bullish score > overall bearish score
Short: timeframe bearish score > timeframe bullish score OR overall bearish score > overall bullish score
Long: overall bullish score > half the total possible score + alert threshold
Short: overall bearish score > half the total possible score + alert threshold
Long: bias score >= signal threshold AND previous chart bar's bias score < signal threshold
Short: bias score <= negative signal threshold AND previous chart bar's bias score > negative signal threshold
