Calculation: ATR trend = persistent direction, initially bullish; set the lower band to source − multiplier × ATR and raise it to the prior lower band when prior close is above that band; set the upper band to source + multiplier × ATR and lower it to the prior upper band when prior close is below that band; switch bearish to bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band, and bullish to bearish when close falls below the prior lower band. Compute on each selected timeframe; use its last completed bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its latest available bar within the chart bar when lower.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI trend = persistent direction, updated to bullish when smoothed Stochastic RSI %K crosses above %D and bearish when %K crosses below %D; otherwise retain the previous direction. Compute RSI, normalize it within its own rolling RSI range, then smooth it into %K and %D. Compute on each selected timeframe, using the same higher/lower timeframe bar handling as ATR trend.
Calculation: Moving-average direction = bullish when close is above its simple moving average, bearish when below, and neutral when equal. Compute on each selected timeframe, using the same higher/lower timeframe bar handling as ATR trend.
Calculation: Per-timeframe bullish score = weighted sum of bullish ATR, Stochastic RSI, and moving-average directions; bearish score = weighted sum of their bearish directions. Overall bullish and bearish scores = weighted sums of the corresponding per-timeframe scores, normalized by the sum of timeframe weights. Bias score = overall bullish score − overall bearish score. Use configured indicator and timeframe weights.
Long: bias score ≥ bullish signal threshold AND prior bar's bias score was below that threshold OR overall bullish score > 50 + bullish alert threshold
Short: bias score ≤ negative bearish signal threshold AND prior bar's bias score was above that negative threshold OR overall bearish score > 50 + bearish alert threshold
