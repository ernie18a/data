Calculation: OR_High, OR_Low = highest high and lowest low of the bars in the opening range window at the start of the cash session of the current day (window is shorter than the breakout window that follows it)
Calculation: Trend_Ref = EMA of the daily close, taken from the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart, previous day, not the forming bar), together with that same bar's close
Long: the bar is in the breakout window (after the opening range window ends, before the cash session close) AND no breakout close beyond the range, long or short, has occurred earlier today (whether or not it was filtered out) AND close > OR_High AND the bar's month is not June, July or August AND the previous completed daily close > the daily EMA
Short: the bar is in the breakout window (after the opening range window ends, before the cash session close) AND no breakout close beyond the range, long or short, has occurred earlier today (whether or not it was filtered out) AND close < OR_Low AND the bar's month is not June, July or August AND the previous completed daily close < the daily EMA
Exit: long, low <= OR_Low (stop at the opposite side of the opening range) OR high >= entry price + multiple * (entry price - OR_Low) (profit target)
Exit: short, high >= OR_High (stop at the opposite side of the opening range) OR low <= entry price - multiple * (OR_High - entry price) (profit target)
