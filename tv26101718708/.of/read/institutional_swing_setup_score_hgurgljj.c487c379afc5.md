Calculation: Composite setup score = weighted average of trend, relative-strength, price-structure, volatility-contraction, volume/accumulation, and breakout-readiness scores, each scaled 0–100; weights are configurable.
Calculation: Trend score = score for close above short-, medium-, and long-term simple moving averages in that order; rising long-term average; close above long-term average; close sufficiently above the long-lookback low; and close sufficiently near the long-lookback high. The three moving-average lookbacks are short-, medium-, and long-term; the slope and range lookbacks are configurable.
Calculation: Relative-strength score = score for the stock/benchmark close ratio above its moving average, at a new long-lookback high, and for stock performance exceeding benchmark performance over a configurable lookback. Benchmark data uses the same timeframe and current bar.
Calculation: Price-structure score = score for a narrow high-low range over a configurable lookback, close near that range's high, and confirmed pivot lows rising versus the prior confirmed pivot low; pivot confirmation uses equal left/right bar counts, and the count is capped.
Calculation: Volatility-contraction score = score for ATR as a percentage of close below its moving average, Bollinger Band width below its moving average, and a low historical percentile rank of ATR percentage. ATR, band, smoothing, and percentile lookbacks are configurable.
Calculation: Volume/accumulation score = score for short-term average volume below long-term average volume times a configurable factor; up-volume/down-volume ratio above a configurable threshold; cumulative Accumulation/Distribution above its moving average; a down-close on below-long-term-average volume; and an up-close with volume above a configurable multiple of long-term average volume within a configurable recent window. The short-volume lookback is shorter than the long-volume lookback.
Calculation: Breakout-readiness score = weighted combination of trend, relative-strength, volume/accumulation, price-structure, and volatility-contraction scores, plus configurable bonuses for close near the base high, a tight base with repeated resistance tests, and confirmed breakout. Confirmed breakout means high at a fresh base high, recent breakout participation, aligned trend, and rising relative strength.
Long: Composite setup score crosses above the configurable entry threshold OR confirmed breakout changes from false to true.
Exit: Long, composite setup score crosses below the configurable exit threshold.
