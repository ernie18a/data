Calculation: Bullish zone = On a chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe completed bar, low > high two bars earlier; its top is that bar's low and its bottom is the high two bars earlier. In breakaway-only mode, also require close > the previous bar's high.
Calculation: Bearish zone = On a chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe completed bar, high < low two bars earlier; its top is the low two bars earlier and its bottom is that bar's high. In breakaway-only mode, also require close < the previous bar's low.
Calculation: Zone target = At zone formation, use the most recently confirmed same-timeframe pivot high for a bullish zone or pivot low for a bearish zone. If unavailable, use the formation bar's high plus an ATR multiple for a bullish zone or low minus an ATR multiple for a bearish zone. A pivot is confirmed after the same number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Active zones = Retain enabled, bias-permitted zones with available targets, subject to limits on the number retained per timeframe and in total, removing the oldest first. Remove a bullish zone when a later completed bar of its own timeframe has min(open, close) <= its bottom; remove a bearish zone when max(open, close) >= its top. Also remove a zone when chart high >= its bullish target or chart low <= its bearish target, provided it has been touched after formation or the target-before-touch option is disabled. A touch requires chart low <= zone top AND chart high >= zone bottom on a chart bar later than formation.
Calculation: Dashboard zone = For each enabled timeframe, the oldest active zone on that timeframe. If none exists, use the most recently formed bias-permitted zone on that timeframe when the last completed bar of that timeframe touches it without its body passing through its far boundary.
Long: A bullish zone forms AND its target is available AND its timeframe and bullish bias are enabled.
Long: The dashboard zone is bullish.
Short: A bearish zone forms AND its target is available AND its timeframe and bearish bias are enabled.
Short: The dashboard zone is bearish.
