Calculation: Three-bar breakout above an SMA = current and prior two closes are above their corresponding SMA values, and the close three bars ago was below its corresponding SMA value
Calculation: Ratchet stop = while long, use entry price minus the initial stop percentage until profit reaches the first profit threshold; then use the close at that first threshold minus the stage-one stop percentage until profit reaches the second profit threshold; thereafter use the highest high since entry minus the trailing percentage. The stop can only rise. Profit is measured from average entry price using the close.
Long: daily market close on the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than chart) > its 200-period SMA AND daily VIX close on the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than chart) < threshold AND (shorter-lookback SMA crosses above longer-lookback SMA (25 vs. 50) OR three-bar breakout above the 50-period SMA OR three-bar breakout above the 100-period SMA OR three-bar breakout above the 200-period SMA)
Exit: long, daily VIX close on the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than chart) > threshold OR shorter-lookback SMA crosses below longer-lookback SMA (25 vs. 50) OR low <= Ratchet stop
