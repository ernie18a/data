Calculation: Short-term oscillator = RSI of (shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close), minus 50.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = T3 moving average of the short-term oscillator, using a configurable smoothing period and volume factor.
Calculation: Long-term oscillator = RSI of an EMA of close, minus 50.
Calculation: Bull pullback = at least one bar in the inclusive pullback lookback, including the current bar, has 100 * (low - middle-period EMA of close) / low <= the pullback percentage.
Calculation: Bear pullback = at least one bar in the inclusive pullback lookback, including the current bar, has 100 * (middle-period EMA of close - high) / middle-period EMA of close <= the pullback percentage.
Long: Smoothed oscillator > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago.
Short: Smoothed oscillator < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago.
Long: Bull pullback is true AND bull pullback on the previous bar is false.
Short: Bear pullback is true AND bear pullback on the previous bar is false.
Long: Current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND long trading is enabled AND position side is not long AND short-period EMA of close > middle-period EMA of close + minimum EMA gap AND middle-period EMA of close > long-period EMA of close + minimum EMA gap AND bull pullback is true AND smoothed oscillator > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago AND long-term oscillator > 0 AND long-term oscillator > its previous value AND close > short-period EMA of close AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > its threshold).
Short: Current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND short trading is enabled AND position side is not short AND short-period EMA of close < middle-period EMA of close - minimum EMA gap AND middle-period EMA of close < long-period EMA of close - minimum EMA gap AND bear pullback is true AND smoothed oscillator < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago AND long-term oscillator < 0 AND long-term oscillator < its previous value AND close < short-period EMA of close AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > its threshold).
Exit: long, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND close < entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage).
Exit: short, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND close > entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage).
Exit: long, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND close signal is enabled AND ((smoothed oscillator > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago) OR (smoothed oscillator < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago)).
Exit: short, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND close signal is enabled AND ((smoothed oscillator > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago) OR (smoothed oscillator < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago)).
Exit: long, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND high >= entry price plus a take-profit distance; use each of four configurable target distances, rounded to the nearest price tick, with configurable partial position percentages for the first three and the remaining position for the fourth.
Exit: short, current datetime is within the enabled backtest date bounds, exclusive AND low <= entry price minus a take-profit distance; use each of four configurable target distances, rounded to the nearest price tick, with configurable partial position percentages for the first three and the remaining position for the fourth.
