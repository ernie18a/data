Calculation: Regression channel = Fit a linear regression to chart-timeframe closes over the regression length. The basis is its current endpoint; the slope is a simple moving average of the regression slope over the slope-smoothing length. Upper and lower offsets are respectively the highest high-minus-basis and lowest low-minus-basis over the channel lookback, multiplied by the residual-width multiplier and bounded away from zero by the minimum price increment. Channel width is upper offset minus lower offset, also bounded away from zero. The current upper and lower rails are basis plus the respective offsets.
Calculation: Reconstructed channel and profile = On each evaluated bar, reconstruct the preceding channel-lookback bars using the current basis, smoothed slope and offsets: the basis at an earlier bar is current basis minus slope times its age. Divide the channel width into profile rows. For each included bar, optionally filter and cap volume against its volume moving average; distribute the selected volume between its high-low range and open-close body using the body-weight parameter, allocating each portion to rows in proportion to overlap after clamping prices to the channel. Smooth row volumes with the configured neighboring-row weight. Choose the point-of-control row from raw or smoothed volume, scanning upward: replace the current choice if volume is greater, or if volume is within the tie tolerance and the row is closer to the current close. Starting at that row, expand the value area toward the adjacent row with greater selected raw or smoothed volume until the target volume fraction is reached; on equal volume choose the row closer to the point of control, favoring the upper row on an equal distance. Point of control, value-area high and value-area low are the respective row centers, optionally clamped inside the rails by the configured padding. Project these levels backward with the reconstructed channel slope. Use the configured fallback fractions if total profile volume is zero.
Calculation: Structure state = Replay the reconstructed lookback from oldest to newest, initially setting direction from the current smoothed channel slope. A confirmed pivot high or low is eligible when its distance from its own close is at least the pivot-distance multiplier times its own ATR. Retain the latest eligible high and low as liquidity levels. A close above the latest high changes direction to rising; a close below the latest low changes direction to falling. After processing each bar's signals, an eligible low confirmed in rising direction becomes the current low inducement candidate; an eligible high confirmed in falling direction becomes the current high candidate. A direction change clears the candidate. A low candidate is swept when low is below the candidate by more than the sweep-tolerance multiplier times current ATR and close is above it; a high candidate uses the opposite comparisons. A validated candidate is cleared after recording it. Respect the configured minimum spacing between candidate-bar timestamps and the configured maximum number of inducement records within the replay. The latest recorded inducement is in confluence when the absolute distance between its candidate bar and the evaluated bar is within the confluence window.
Calculation: Historical sweep = At each replayed bar, a bullish sweep requires a lower-wick-to-body ratio at least the rejection multiplier times the sweep factor and at least one of: low below the latest eligible pivot low by more than the ATR sweep tolerance and close above that pivot; low at or below reconstructed value-area low and close above it; or low at or below the reconstructed lower rail plus channel width times the rail-touch tolerance and close above the rail. A bearish sweep mirrors these comparisons at the pivot high, value-area high and upper rail. Body is the greater of absolute close-minus-open and the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Confluence score = For each side, add 20 for its rail sweep, 20 for its value-area sweep, 20 if its wick-to-body ratio reaches the rejection multiplier or otherwise 10 if it reaches the multiplier times the lower-wick threshold factor, 10 if volume exceeds its moving average times the volume multiplier, 15 if price crosses the reconstructed point of control intrabar and closes beyond it in that side's direction, and 15 for inducement confluence. The rail and value-area components use the comparisons in Historical sweep without the wick requirement.
Calculation: Historical signal eligibility = Replay signals chronologically within the reconstructed lookback. Drops share one minimum-spacing clock across both sides; confluence signals share another. Both kinds share a maximum signal count. Evaluate bullish then bearish drops, followed by bullish then bearish confluence signals; spacing eligibility for each kind is computed once at the start of the bar. A confluence signal also requires its score to reach the minimum score, the optional volume filter to pass, and, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close at least open for bullish or at most open for bearish.
Long: Low at or below the current lower rail plus channel width times the rail-touch tolerance AND close above the lower rail AND lower wick at least body times the rejection multiplier.
Short: High at or above the current upper rail minus channel width times the rail-touch tolerance AND close below the upper rail AND upper wick at least body times the rejection multiplier.
Long: Historical bullish sweep AND either drop spacing and shared signal count permit a drop OR confluence spacing, shared signal count and bullish confluence requirements permit a confluence signal.
Short: Historical bearish sweep AND either drop spacing and shared signal count permit a drop OR confluence spacing, shared signal count and bearish confluence requirements permit a confluence signal.
Long: In early-candidate mode, an eligible pivot low is confirmed while structure direction is rising AND inducement spacing and count permit recording it; otherwise, a rising-direction low candidate is swept AND inducement spacing and count permit recording it AND, in strict mode, close is above the latest eligible pivot high.
Short: In early-candidate mode, an eligible pivot high is confirmed while structure direction is falling AND inducement spacing and count permit recording it; otherwise, a falling-direction high candidate is swept AND inducement spacing and count permit recording it AND, in strict mode, close is below the latest eligible pivot low.
