Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of per-bar delta, with configurable daily reset or continuous accumulation; per-bar delta is either volume × (2 × close − high − low) ÷ (high − low) when high > low (otherwise 0), or volume for close > open, −volume for close < open, and 0 otherwise
Long: Cumulative volume delta crosses above 0 OR cumulative volume delta >= prior bar's value OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at that pivot is higher than at the prior pivot low
Short: Cumulative volume delta crosses below 0 OR cumulative volume delta < prior bar's value OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior confirmed pivot high AND cumulative volume delta at that pivot is lower than at the prior pivot high
