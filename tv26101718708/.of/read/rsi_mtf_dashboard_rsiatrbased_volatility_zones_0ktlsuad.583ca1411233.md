Calculation: MTF RSI stack bias = EMA of the normalized sum across enabled timeframes of four scores per row: +1 if RSI is at or above its upper neutral threshold, otherwise −1 if at or below its lower neutral threshold, otherwise 0; +1/−1/0 according as RSI is above/below/equal to prior RSI; and +1/−1/0 according as RSI is above/below/equal to each of its shorter-lookback and longer-lookback simple moving averages. Normalize by the maximum possible score for available comparisons. Use each timeframe’s still-forming bar, whether higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Adaptive pressure oscillator = RSI of (high − midpoint) / half-range and RSI of (low − midpoint) / half-range, where the midpoint is halfway between the Wilder moving averages of high and low; choose the greater value when both exceed 50, the lesser when both are below 50, otherwise choose whichever is closer to 50.
Calculation: Adaptive upper trigger = adaptive EMA of oscillator pivot highs above the upper neutral threshold; a pivot high is the oscillator value three bars ago when it is at least the highest oscillator value over the current eight-bar lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive lower trigger = adaptive EMA of oscillator pivot lows below the lower neutral threshold; a pivot low is the oscillator value three bars ago when it is at most the lowest oscillator value over the current eight-bar lookback.
Long: Chart-timeframe RSI > upper neutral threshold OR MTF RSI stack bias >= bullish threshold OR adaptive pressure oscillator > adaptive upper trigger (and, if stack confirmation is enabled, MTF RSI stack bias >= bullish threshold).
Short: Chart-timeframe RSI < lower neutral threshold OR MTF RSI stack bias <= negative bearish threshold OR adaptive pressure oscillator < adaptive lower trigger (and, if stack confirmation is enabled, MTF RSI stack bias <= negative bearish threshold).
