Calculation: Timeframe RSI = RSI of close on each selected chart, higher, or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Stack bias = EMA of the normalized sum of scores across selected timeframes. For each timeframe, score RSI against the upper and lower neutral thresholds as +1 when RSI is at or above the upper threshold, -1 when at or below the lower threshold, otherwise 0. Separately score RSI against its previous value, its shorter-lookback SMA, and its longer-lookback SMA as +1 when greater, -1 when less, otherwise 0. Divide the total score by the number of available comparisons; use 0 if none are available.
Calculation: Pressure = RSI of (high - channel midpoint) / channel half-range and RSI of (low - channel midpoint) / channel half-range, where the channel boundaries are Wilder moving averages of high and low over the same lookback. If both RSI values are above the RSI midpoint, select the larger; if both are below, select the smaller; otherwise select the one closer to the midpoint, selecting the smaller on a tie. Pressure is unavailable when the channel half-range is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Adaptive upper and lower pressure levels = held event-based EMAs of confirmed pressure pivots. A high pivot is the pressure at a fixed earlier-bar offset when it is at least the highest pressure over a pivot lookback ending on the current bar; update the upper level only when that pivot exceeds the upper RSI neutral threshold. A low pivot is defined symmetrically using the lowest pressure and updates the lower level only when below the lower RSI neutral threshold. Initialize each level with its first qualifying pivot and update it using EMA alpha based on the adaptive smoothing length; hold it between qualifying pivots.
Long: Chart-timeframe RSI > upper RSI neutral threshold.
Short: Chart-timeframe RSI < lower RSI neutral threshold.
Long: On any selected timeframe, RSI > previous RSI OR RSI > shorter-lookback SMA of RSI OR RSI > longer-lookback SMA of RSI OR RSI > upper RSI neutral threshold.
Short: On any selected timeframe, RSI < previous RSI OR RSI <= shorter-lookback SMA of RSI OR RSI <= longer-lookback SMA of RSI OR RSI < lower RSI neutral threshold.
Long: Stack bias > 0 OR stack bias >= positive stack-regime threshold.
Short: Stack bias < 0 OR stack bias <= negative stack-regime threshold.
Long: Pressure > adaptive upper pressure level AND adaptive upper pressure level > 0 AND (stack confirmation disabled OR stack bias >= positive stack-regime threshold).
Short: Pressure < adaptive lower pressure level AND adaptive lower pressure level > 0 AND (stack confirmation disabled OR stack bias <= negative stack-regime threshold).
