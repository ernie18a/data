Calculation: Normalized OBV = rolling z-score of cumulative OBV; initialize OBV at zero and add volume when close rises, subtract volume when close falls, and otherwise add zero; use zero when the rolling standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Fast and slow lines = Hull moving average and double exponential moving average, respectively, of normalized OBV; the fast length is shorter than the slow length.
Calculation: Fast slope and slow slope = each line's change over the same slope lookback divided by that lookback; slope differential = fast slope minus slow slope.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price-low pivot and normalized-OBV-low pivot on the same bar, with price lower and normalized OBV higher than at the immediately preceding paired low pivot, whose distance is within the maximum pivot range, AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, slope differential on the confirmation bar exceeds a positive threshold. Register every paired low pivot, including those that do not qualify. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed price-high pivot and normalized-OBV-high pivot on the same bar, with price higher and normalized OBV lower than at the immediately preceding paired high pivot, whose distance is within the maximum pivot range, AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, slope differential on the confirmation bar is below the negative confirmation threshold. Register every paired high pivot, including those that do not qualify. Evaluate on the confirmation bar. Use the same left and right pivot lookbacks for price and normalized OBV.
Calculation: Divergence strength = the absolute normalized-OBV difference between the qualifying pivot and its preceding paired pivot, multiplied by a positive scale and capped at a maximum. If both divergences occur together, use the bullish divergence's strength.
Calculation: Regime score = clamp to the score range the weighted sum of a neutral anchor, a slope component, and a distance component, plus divergence bias and signed volume adjustment. The slope component is the anchor plus a positive scale times the rolling z-score of slope differential; the distance component is the anchor plus a positive scale times fast line minus slow line; clamp each component to the score range. The slope component has greater weight than the distance component, which has greater weight than the anchor. Divergence bias is positive strength times a factor for bullish divergence, negative strength times that factor for bearish divergence, and zero otherwise or when divergence contribution is disabled; bullish takes precedence if both occur. The volume adjustment is a scaled and symmetrically capped value of volume divided by its rolling mean minus one, multiplied by a positive factor and by +1 when fast slope is nonnegative or -1 otherwise; use a volume ratio of one when its mean is nonpositive. Use the normalized-OBV lookback for the slope z-score and volume mean.
Calculation: Confirmed regime = bullish when the regime score is at or above the upper threshold for every bar in the confirmation window; bearish when it is at or below the lower threshold for every bar in that window; otherwise neutral. The upper threshold exceeds the lower threshold.
Calculation: Last shifted regime = initially neutral; update to bullish or bearish only when the corresponding confirmed regime differs from the last shifted regime and the minimum number of bars has elapsed since the last shift; the first shift has no waiting restriction. Displayed regime = confirmed regime when non-neutral, otherwise last shifted regime.
Long: Fast line > slow line.
Short: Fast line <= slow line.
Long: Slope differential > zero.
Short: Slope differential <= zero.
Long: Bullish divergence is present.
Short: Bearish divergence is present.
Long: Confirmed regime is bullish AND last shifted regime before this bar is not bullish AND bars since the last shift >= minimum shift spacing, or no previous shift exists.
Short: Confirmed regime is bearish AND last shifted regime before this bar is not bearish AND bars since the last shift >= minimum shift spacing, or no previous shift exists.
Long: Displayed regime is bullish.
Short: Displayed regime is bearish.
Long: Fast slope > zero AND fast slope > previous fast slope, OR fast slope < zero AND fast slope > previous fast slope; substitute zero for an unavailable previous fast slope.
Short: Fast slope > zero AND fast slope < previous fast slope, OR fast slope < zero AND fast slope < previous fast slope; substitute zero for an unavailable previous fast slope.
