Calculation: ZVWAP = (close − volume-weighted mean of close over a lookback) ÷ square root of the simple moving average over the same lookback of (close − that mean)²
Long: Not currently long AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND at least one of the previous 1–10 chart bars has ZVWAP ≤ the oversold threshold AND current ZVWAP crosses above zero
Long: Currently long AND close < average long entry price AND current ZVWAP crosses above zero
Exit: Long, close < average long entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR current ZVWAP crosses below the overbought threshold
