Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar high greater than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes available only after the bars on its right close.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar low less than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes available only after the bars on its right close.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = the low of a bar is above the high from two bars earlier, with the intervening displacement close above its low plus an ATR-based threshold; zone spans that earlier high to the later low.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = the high of a bar is below the low from two bars earlier, with the intervening displacement close below its high minus an ATR-based threshold; zone spans that later high to the earlier low.
Calculation: Bullish rejected block = the zone from a confirmed pivot low's low to the bottom of its candle body, where the lower wick meets the configured minimum share of the candle range and minimum ATR size.
Calculation: Bearish rejected block = the zone from a confirmed pivot high's high to the top of its candle body, where the upper wick meets the configured minimum share of the candle range and minimum ATR size.
Long: A confirmed close (or high when wick breaks are configured) crosses above the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high while the prior market direction is bearish (bullish CHoCH) OR while prior market direction is bullish (bullish BOS) OR a bullish rejected block forms after bullish CHoCH and, after price first closes above its zone, price retests the zone without closing below it OR after that retest, a bullish BOS and bullish fair value gap form within the configured association window OR after that pair, price first touches the final bullish fair value gap without mitigating it OR a bearish fair value gap is mitigated and becomes a bullish inverse gap OR, after a bullish CHoCH, price touches the 0.382–0.618 retracement zone and later closes above its upper edge OR a validated bearish liquidity trend line is first broken upward.
Short: A confirmed close (or low when wick breaks are configured) crosses below the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low while the prior market direction is bullish (bearish CHoCH) OR while prior market direction is bearish (bearish BOS) OR a bearish rejected block forms after bearish CHoCH and, after price first closes below its zone, price retests the zone without closing above it OR after that retest, a bearish BOS and bearish fair value gap form within the configured association window OR after that pair, price first touches the final bearish fair value gap without mitigating it OR a bullish fair value gap is mitigated and becomes a bearish inverse gap OR, after a bearish CHoCH, price touches the 0.382–0.618 retracement zone and later closes below its lower edge OR a validated bullish liquidity trend line is first broken downward.
Calculation: CHoCH retracement zone = the price interval between 0.382 and 0.618 of the distance from the pre-break opposite pivot to the CHoCH candle extreme.
