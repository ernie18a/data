Calculation: Structure levels = The latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and pivot low, each replaced when a new pivot of its kind is confirmed and marked unbroken until its first break. A break above uses close or high, and a break below uses close or low, according to a shared choice. If both break on the same bar, select the upward break when close >= open, otherwise the downward break. Market direction becomes the direction of each break; initially it is neutral. A reversal break from a nonneutral market direction starts a new directional regime. A same-direction break is a continuation break only if it matches the current regime.
Calculation: Rejected block = After a regime starts, confirm a symmetric pivot low for a bullish regime or pivot high for a bearish regime, with the pivot candle strictly after the regime-start bar. Its range and relevant lower or upper wick must be positive; wick divided by range must be at least a minimum fraction, and wick must be at least a chosen multiple of ATR on the pivot candle. The bullish zone extends from pivot low to the lower body extreme; the bearish zone extends from the upper body extreme to pivot high. Until a retest, replace the zone with each newly qualified pivot. From the bar after confirmation, discard it if close is below the bullish zone or above the bearish zone; otherwise arm it on a bar whose low is above the bullish zone or high is below the bearish zone. A retest occurs on a later bar when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom. Reset the block and sequence at each new regime.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On a completed chart bar, a bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier AND the intervening close > intervening low + a chosen multiple of ATR on the intervening bar; its zone is from that earlier high to the current low. A bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier AND the intervening close < intervening high - the same multiple of ATR on the intervening bar; its zone is from the current high to that earlier low. Recognize each gap on the next chart bar. Bullish mitigation means lower body extreme < zone bottom, low < zone bottom, or low < zone midpoint, according to the selected mitigation method; bearish mitigation means upper body extreme > zone top, high > zone top, or high > zone midpoint, respectively. In breaker mode a mitigated gap becomes an opposite-direction inverse gap; remove that inverse gap when the opposite mitigation condition applies. Optionally remove overlapping gaps after mitigation processing: remove older same-direction gaps overlapping the newest of their direction, then bullish gaps overlapping the newest bearish gap, then bearish gaps overlapping the newest remaining bullish gap. Overlap requires strictly positive price intersection.
Calculation: Gap and structure sequence = After a rejected-block retest, record the latest matching-direction continuation break and matching-direction new gap occurring strictly after the retest. A pair is confirmed when their bar indices differ by no more than a chosen association window; its final zone is the recorded gap. Starting after both event bars, invalidate the final zone if its directional mitigation condition holds; on invalidation resume searching for a pair with both recorded events cleared. Otherwise its first bullish retest is low < zone top, and its first bearish retest is high > zone bottom. Complete the sequence on that retest and restart only at a new regime.
Calculation: Regime liquidity extremes = At a new regime, retain the previous regime's running high and low as its completed extremes. Initialize the new bullish regime's high to the current high and its low to the minimum of current low, available previous regime low, and available latest structure pivot low. Initialize a bearish regime's low to the current low and its high to the maximum of current high, available previous regime high, and available latest structure pivot high. On subsequent bars update the running high and low with the current high and low.
Calculation: Liquidity trend line = At a bullish regime start, anchor at the previous regime's completed low and its bar time; at a bearish regime start, anchor at its completed high and bar time. Connect that point to the first subsequently confirmed structure pivot whose pivot time is after the regime start and anchor time and whose low is higher than the bullish anchor or whose high is lower than the bearish anchor. Extend the line linearly by elapsed time. Only the first break after the pivot-confirmation bar counts; reset at each new regime.
Calculation: Retracement entry zone = At a bullish regime start, anchor at the latest structure pivot low, or current low if unavailable, and extend to current high; at a bearish regime start, anchor at the latest structure pivot high, or current high if unavailable, and extend to current low. Require a positive bullish or negative bearish anchor-to-extreme difference exceeding the minimum price increment. The zone lies between two chosen retracement fractions of that signed difference added to the anchor. From the next bar, mark it touched when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom; reset at each new regime.
Long: An unbroken structure pivot high breaks AND the prior market direction is bearish, OR it breaks in the bullish market direction AND the active regime is bullish.
Short: An unbroken structure pivot low breaks AND the prior market direction is bullish, OR it breaks in the bearish market direction AND the active regime is bearish.
Long: A bullish rejected block is confirmed, OR its armed zone is retested.
Short: A bearish rejected block is confirmed, OR its armed zone is retested.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is recognized, OR a bearish gap is mitigated in breaker mode.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is recognized, OR a bullish gap is mitigated in breaker mode.
Long: A bullish gap and structure sequence pair is confirmed, OR its final gap receives its first valid retest.
Short: A bearish gap and structure sequence pair is confirmed, OR its final gap receives its first valid retest.
Long: A bearish regime's validated liquidity trend line is first broken upward by close or high, according to the selected break method.
Short: A bullish regime's validated liquidity trend line is first broken downward by close or low, according to the selected break method.
Long: In a bullish regime, the retracement entry zone has been touched AND close first exceeds its top after the regime-start bar.
Short: In a bearish regime, the retracement entry zone has been touched AND close first falls below its bottom after the regime-start bar.
Long: Positive directional indicator >= negative directional indicator.
Short: Positive directional indicator < negative directional indicator.
Long: Close > close at the momentum lookback, OR the EMA of (close - previous close) multiplied by volume > 0.
Short: Close < close at the momentum lookback, OR the EMA of (close - previous close) multiplied by volume < 0.
