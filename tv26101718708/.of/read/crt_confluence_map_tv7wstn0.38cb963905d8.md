Calculation: 8AM CRT range = highest high and lowest low of chart-timeframe bars in the configured CRT session; use only during its configured projection session.
Calculation: Previous completed higher-timeframe range = high and low of the last completed bar aggregated from chart data; compare with the preceding completed bar for trend labels.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured number of chart bars on each side; confirm after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume if high = low; estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume; pressure delta = estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume.
Calculation: Strong buying pressure = pressure delta greater than its recent average absolute value times the configured strength multiplier, with close > open and volume > recent average volume. Strong selling pressure = pressure delta less than negative recent average absolute value times the configured strength multiplier, with close < open and volume > recent average volume.
Long: Within the configured projection session, low < 8AM CRT low AND close > 8AM CRT low (at most once per range); OR low < previous completed higher-timeframe bar low AND close > that low (at most once per range); OR low < previous trading-session low AND close > that low (at most once per range); OR first close above the configured opening-range high within the configured signal window; OR low < latest unswept confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low (at most once per swing, during the configured reversal session if session-only filtering is enabled, and at a configured key level if key-level-only filtering is enabled); OR after a bullish swing-failure signal, close > the latest confirmed swing high within the configured confirmation window and close has not fallen below the swing-failure bar low; OR confirmed swing high > preceding confirmed swing high; OR confirmed swing low > preceding confirmed swing low; OR strong buying pressure; OR price retests an active buying-pressure zone (low <= zone top AND prior bar low > zone top).
Short: Within the configured projection session, high > 8AM CRT high AND close < 8AM CRT high (at most once per range); OR high > previous completed higher-timeframe bar high AND close < that high (at most once per range); OR high > previous trading-session high AND close < that high (at most once per range); OR first close below the configured opening-range low within the configured signal window; OR high > latest unswept confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high (at most once per swing, during the configured reversal session if session-only filtering is enabled, and at a configured key level if key-level-only filtering is enabled); OR after a bearish swing-failure signal, close < the latest confirmed swing low within the configured confirmation window and close has not risen above the swing-failure bar high; OR confirmed swing high < preceding confirmed swing high; OR confirmed swing low < preceding confirmed swing low; OR strong selling pressure; OR price retests an active selling-pressure zone (high >= zone bottom AND prior bar high < zone bottom).
