Calculation: Session range = high and low aggregated over a configurable New York session; it becomes active when that session ends and remains active during its configured projection session.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ranges = highs and lows of the last completed bar and the preceding completed bar, aggregated at each of two configurable higher timeframes. A new range becomes active when the next higher-timeframe bar begins.
Calculation: Opening range = high and low aggregated from chart bars whose opening times fall within a configurable New York opening interval. It becomes active on the first bar after that interval and expires at a configurable New York time; its signal window ends a configurable duration after that first bar opens.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same configurable number of chart bars on each side, available only on the confirmation bar. Track the latest two confirmed highs and lows. Reset a swing's swept state when a new swing of that kind is confirmed; mark it swept after any bar exceeds it, even if that bar does not reclaim it.
Calculation: Key level = high, low or midpoint of an active session range, last completed higher-timeframe range at either timeframe, or opening range; also include the high and low of the preceding completed range at the shorter higher timeframe. A swing is at a key level when its absolute distance from any such level is at most a configurable tolerance.
Calculation: Structure-shift setup = after a qualifying swing-low sweep, record that bar's low and the latest confirmed swing high; after a qualifying swing-high sweep, record that bar's high and the latest confirmed swing low. A new qualifying sweep replaces the same-side setup and cancels the opposite-side setup. Starting on subsequent bars, expire a setup after a configurable number of bars or after a prior bar closes beyond its recorded sweep extreme; consume it on its first structure-shift signal. Evaluate the signal before cancellation on each bar.
Calculation: Pressure delta = estimated buying volume minus estimated selling volume; buying volume is volume times (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), or half of volume when high equals low; selling volume is volume minus buying volume.
Calculation: Strong buying pressure = pressure delta > a configurable multiple of the SMA of absolute pressure delta AND close > open AND volume > the SMA of volume, using the same configurable lookback for both SMAs. Strong selling pressure = pressure delta < the negative of that multiple of the SMA of absolute pressure delta AND close < open AND volume > the SMA of volume.
Calculation: Pressure zones = on a strong buying bar, create a buying zone from its low to the greater of its lower body edge and low plus a configurable fraction of its range; on a strong selling bar, create a selling zone from the lesser of its upper body edge and high minus that fraction of its range to its high. Skip a new zone if it overlaps an existing same-side zone; retain at most a configurable number per side, removing the oldest when exceeded. Before creating new zones on each bar, remove buying zones when close < zone bottom and selling zones when close > zone top.
Calculation: Buying share = rolling sum of estimated buying volume divided by rolling sum of volume, using a configurable lookback; use one-half when the volume sum is zero.
Long: During the active session-range projection session, low < session-range low AND close > session-range low AND no earlier bullish session-range sweep has occurred since the range completed.
Short: During the active session-range projection session, high > session-range high AND close < session-range high AND no earlier bearish session-range sweep has occurred since the range completed.
Long: Low < last completed higher-timeframe range low AND close > that low AND no earlier bullish sweep of that range has occurred during the current higher-timeframe bar; evaluate independently for each of the two higher timeframes.
Short: High > last completed higher-timeframe range high AND close < that high AND no earlier bearish sweep of that range has occurred during the current higher-timeframe bar; evaluate independently for each of the two higher timeframes.
Long: Opening range is active AND bar opening time is within its signal window AND close > opening-range high AND no earlier bullish opening-range break has occurred since that range completed.
Short: Opening range is active AND bar opening time is within its signal window AND close < opening-range low AND no earlier bearish opening-range break has occurred since that range completed.
Long: Latest confirmed swing low is unswept AND low < that swing low AND close > that swing low AND the bar is within the configurable reversal session if the session filter is enabled AND the swing is at a key level if the key-level filter is enabled.
Short: Latest confirmed swing high is unswept AND high > that swing high AND close < that swing high AND the bar is within the configurable reversal session if the session filter is enabled AND the swing is at a key level if the key-level filter is enabled.
Long: A bullish structure-shift setup is active AND close > its recorded swing high AND elapsed bars since its sweep <= the configurable setup window.
Short: A bearish structure-shift setup is active AND close < its recorded swing low AND elapsed bars since its sweep <= the configurable setup window.
Long: A newly confirmed swing high > the preceding confirmed swing high.
Short: A newly confirmed swing high <= the preceding confirmed swing high.
Long: A newly confirmed swing low > the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A newly confirmed swing low <= the preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe high > preceding completed higher-timeframe high AND last completed higher-timeframe low > preceding completed higher-timeframe low; evaluate independently for each of the two higher timeframes.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe high <= preceding completed higher-timeframe high AND last completed higher-timeframe low <= preceding completed higher-timeframe low; evaluate independently for each of the two higher timeframes.
Long: Latest confirmed swing high > preceding confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing low > preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: Latest confirmed swing high < preceding confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing low < preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: Strong buying pressure.
Short: Strong selling pressure.
Long: An existing buying zone remains unbroken AND low <= its top AND previous bar low > its top.
Short: An existing selling zone remains unbroken AND high >= its bottom AND previous bar high < its bottom.
Long: Buying share >= one-half.
Short: Buying share < one-half.
Long: Buying share >= a configurable bullish pressure threshold.
Short: Buying share <= a configurable bearish pressure threshold, lower than the bullish threshold.
