Calculation: Displacement = a bar with absolute close-to-open body greater than a configurable multiple of the simple moving average of absolute bodies, including the current bar; bullish if close > open and bearish if close < open.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows, optionally the completed session high and low, and optionally the previous completed higher-timeframe day high and low. Add a pool when its pivot is confirmed or its session ends, or at the configured daily reopen for the previous-day levels. Merge each new pool into the first unswept pool on the same side within a configurable ATR-based tolerance, retaining the higher high or lower low and incrementing its touch count; session and previous-day pools start with a touch count of at least two. Retain a bounded queue of pools. A high pool is swept when high > its level; a low pool is swept when low < its level. A swept pool cannot be swept again.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish zone from high two bars ago to current low when current low > that high, or a bearish zone from current high to low two bars ago when current high < that low. Optionally require matching displacement on the intervening bar. Retain a bounded queue of zones. An active bearish zone inverts bullish when close > its top; an active bullish zone inverts bearish when close < its bottom. An inverted bullish zone is invalidated when close < its bottom; an inverted bearish zone is invalidated when close > its top. A zone can be tapped only once across its active and inverted states. A bullish tap requires low <= zone top and close >= zone bottom; a bearish tap requires high >= zone bottom and close <= zone top. An active zone cannot be tapped on its creation bar. Process inversion or invalidation before taps on each bar.
Calculation: Setup state = a low-pool sweep arms a long setup and a high-pool sweep arms a short setup, replacing any previous setup on that side and clearing its displacement confirmation. While armed, track the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short from the sweep through the current bar; matching displacement on or after the sweep confirms the setup. A setup expires after a configurable number of bars and is cleared after its entry signal.
Calculation: Stop distance = close minus the long setup's tracked low plus a configurable price offset, or the short setup's tracked high plus that offset minus close. The optional stop cap is either a configurable session-dependent fixed distance or a configurable multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Setup score = add one for entry close or stop inside an unfilled higher-timeframe fair value gap, one for entry within an ATR of a configured session open or opening-gap midpoint, one if the swept pool has at least two touches, one if at least two unswept target-side pools lie within a configurable ATR distance, and one for a long at or below or a short above the midpoint of the rolling high-low range; subtract one if at least two unswept pools lie behind entry within a separate configurable ATR distance. Higher-timeframe gaps use completed bars from either of two higher timeframes and are removed when close passes their far edge. The source also awards a point for correlated-instrument divergence, which cannot be calculated from the specified data; this score is reproducible with that feature disabled.
Long: close > open AND absolute close-to-open body > configurable multiple of its moving average.
Short: close < open AND absolute close-to-open body > configurable multiple of its moving average.
Long: current low > high two bars ago AND matching intervening-bar displacement if the displacement-only gap filter is enabled.
Short: current high < low two bars ago AND matching intervening-bar displacement if the displacement-only gap filter is enabled.
Long: minimum of open and close > maximum of previous open and close AND low <= previous high.
Short: maximum of open and close < minimum of previous open and close AND high >= previous low.
Long: low < an unswept low-pool level.
Short: high > an unswept high-pool level.
Long: close > the top of an active bearish fair value gap.
Short: close < the bottom of an active bullish fair value gap.
Long: long setup is armed AND bullish displacement has occurred since its latest sweep AND current bar is in an eligible session if the session filter is enabled AND a bullish gap inversion or bullish zone tap occurs AND stop distance <= stop cap if the cap filter is enabled AND setup score >= configured minimum if the grade filter is enabled.
Short: short setup is armed AND bearish displacement has occurred since its latest sweep AND current bar is in an eligible session if the session filter is enabled AND a bearish gap inversion or bearish zone tap occurs AND stop distance <= stop cap if the cap filter is enabled AND setup score >= configured minimum if the grade filter is enabled.
