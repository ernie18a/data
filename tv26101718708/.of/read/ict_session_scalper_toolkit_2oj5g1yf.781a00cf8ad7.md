Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed chart swing highs and lows, completed Asia-session high and low, and prior completed day high and low; merge unswept same-side pools within the configured ATR tolerance.
Calculation: Displacement = candle body exceeds the configured multiple of the 20-bar average body, with close above open for bullish and below open for bearish.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when low > high two bars earlier; bearish when high < low two bars earlier. By default, the middle candle must be a same-direction displacement.
Calculation: Inverted FVG = a bearish gap becomes bullish when a candle closes above its top; a bullish gap becomes bearish when a candle closes below its bottom. A zone retrace occurs when price enters the zone and closes beyond its opposite boundary; each zone can trigger at most one retrace.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG = a three-candle gap formed from completed bars aggregated to a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Setup grade = start at 0; add 1 if entry or stop is in a higher-timeframe FVG, 1 if entry is within 1 ATR(14) of the midnight open or the latest daily or weekly opening-gap midpoint, 1 if same-direction SMT occurred within the sweep-to-entry window, 1 if the swept pool is an equal-level pool, 1 if at least two unswept target-side pools are within 12 ATR(14), and 1 for buying below or selling above the midpoint of the configured chart-bar dealing range; subtract 1 if at least two unswept opposite-side pools are within 3 ATR(14). Grade is A+ at 4 or more, B at 2–3, otherwise C.
Long: low < an unswept sellside liquidity pool OR bullish displacement OR bullish fair value gap OR bearish fair value gap closes above its top OR price retraces into a bullish fair value gap or bullish inverted FVG OR bullish volume imbalance (min(open, close) > max(open[1], close[1]) AND low <= high[1]) OR a bullish higher-timeframe FVG forms OR a long entry signal where a sellside pool was swept within the configured window AND bullish displacement occurred on or after that sweep within the window AND (a bullish inverted FVG was validated OR price retraced into a bullish zone) AND the bar is in a configured killzone AND the bar is confirmed AND entry close minus (lowest low since the sweep minus 2 minimum ticks) does not exceed the configured stop cap AND setup grade meets the configured minimum.
Short: high > an unswept buyside liquidity pool OR bearish displacement OR bearish fair value gap OR bullish fair value gap closes below its bottom OR price retraces into a bearish fair value gap or bearish inverted FVG OR bearish volume imbalance (max(open, close) < min(open[1], close[1]) AND high >= low[1]) OR a bearish higher-timeframe FVG forms OR a short entry signal where a buyside pool was swept within the configured window AND bearish displacement occurred on or after that sweep within the window AND (a bearish inverted FVG was validated OR price retraced into a bearish zone) AND the bar is in a configured killzone AND the bar is confirmed AND (highest high since the sweep plus 2 minimum ticks) minus entry close does not exceed the configured stop cap AND setup grade meets the configured minimum.
