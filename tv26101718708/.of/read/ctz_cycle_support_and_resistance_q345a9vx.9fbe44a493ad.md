Calculation: Anchor age = the greater of the ages of the highest high and lowest low in the selected lookback. In automatic mode, select the shortest of three successively longer lookbacks whose anchor age is outside the configurable edge buffer; use the longest if neither shorter lookback qualifies.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low AND accumulation/distribution at the pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot low AND the pivot age is at most the anchor age AND the high-low range from the anchor through the current bar is greater than zero.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high AND accumulation/distribution at the pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot high AND the pivot age is at most the anchor age AND the high-low range from the anchor through the current bar is greater than zero.
