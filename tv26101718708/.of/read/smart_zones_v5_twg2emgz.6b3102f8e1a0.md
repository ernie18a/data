Calculation: Tenkan-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Kijun-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than Tenkan-sen
Calculation: Senkou A = midpoint of Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen, displaced forward by the Kijun lookback
Calculation: Senkou B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than Kijun-sen, displaced forward by the Kijun lookback
Long: RSI >= bullish threshold OR current low - high two bars ago > minimum ATR multiple OR displaced Senkou A > displaced Senkou B OR at least 2 of (displaced Senkou A > displaced Senkou B, Tenkan-sen crosses above Kijun-sen, close > high from the Kijun lookback bars ago)
Short: RSI <= bearish threshold OR low two bars ago - current high > minimum ATR multiple OR displaced Senkou A <= displaced Senkou B OR at least 2 of (displaced Senkou A < displaced Senkou B, Tenkan-sen crosses below Kijun-sen, close < low from the Kijun lookback bars ago)
