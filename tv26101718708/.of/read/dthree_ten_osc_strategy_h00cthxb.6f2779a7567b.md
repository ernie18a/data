Calculation: 3-10 oscillator = short-lookback EMA of the higher-timeframe bar’s (high + low) / 2 minus a longer-lookback EMA of the same price; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Oscillator signal line = longer-lookback SMA of the 3-10 oscillator; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Long: 3-10 oscillator > oscillator signal line
Short: 3-10 oscillator < oscillator signal line
