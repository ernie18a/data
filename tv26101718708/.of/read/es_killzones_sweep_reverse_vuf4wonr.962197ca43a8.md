Calculation: Session = each enabled, parameterized intraday time window in the fixed UTC-5 timezone, on chart timeframes at or below a parameterized limit. A session range is the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars in its latest completed occurrence; retain it until that session starts again.
Calculation: Session activity = the union of all enabled sessions. A session cycle starts when session activity changes from false to true and ends when it changes from true to false.
Calculation: Swing state = initially high. For lookback N, on each chart bar set it to high if high N bars ago exceeds the maximum high of the latest N bars; otherwise set it to low if low N bars ago is below the minimum low of the latest N bars; otherwise retain it. On a change to high or low, store the corresponding high or low N bars ago as the latest swing of that side and mark it unbroken.
Calculation: Order block = during a session cycle, when the previous close first exceeds the latest unbroken swing high whose bar is within the cycle, mark that swing broken and form a bullish block from the selected low and high of the bar with the lowest selected low between the swing bar and the current bar, excluding both endpoints. When the previous close first falls below the latest unbroken swing low and the latest swing high's bar is within the cycle, mark that swing low broken and form a bearish block from the selected low and high of the bar with the highest selected high between the swing low bar and the current bar, excluding both endpoints. Selected highs and lows are either candle body extremes or wick extremes, according to a parameter; initialize the extremum search from the previous bar. Retain blocks until the next cycle if extension is enabled, otherwise only through the current cycle.
Calculation: Breaker block = a bullish order block first breached when the minimum of the previous open and the previous close or low, according to the mitigation-price parameter, falls below its bottom; or a bearish order block first breached when the maximum of the previous open and the previous close or high, according to that parameter, exceeds its top. The former is bearish and the latter bullish.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral and reset to neutral outside session activity. Confirm a pivot high or low using equal numbers of bars on each side; store the latest confirmed pivot of each side and mark it unbroken. Outside session activity reset the stored high to a parameterized upper sentinel and the stored low to a parameterized lower sentinel. During session activity, when the previous close exceeds an unbroken stored pivot high, mark it broken and set the state to bullish; when the previous close falls below an unbroken stored pivot low, mark it broken and set the state to bearish. Process the high before the low.
Long: For any completed session, current low < its range low AND current close > its range low AND either repeated sweeps are enabled OR no earlier low sweep has occurred since that session started.
Short: For any completed session, current high > its range high AND current close < its range high AND either repeated sweeps are enabled OR no earlier high sweep has occurred since that session started.
Long: A bullish order block forms AND the configured block selection permits it.
Short: A bearish order block forms AND the configured block selection permits it.
Long: A bullish breaker block forms AND the configured breaker selection permits it.
Short: A bearish breaker block forms AND the configured breaker selection permits it.
Long: During session activity, previous close > latest unbroken pivot high AND previous structure state is neutral or bearish AND the configured structure selection permits it.
Short: During session activity, previous close < latest unbroken pivot low AND previous structure state is neutral or bullish AND the configured structure selection permits it.
Long: During session activity, current low - high two bars ago > ATR multiplied by a parameter AND current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND current low <= previous high AND previous low <= high two bars ago AND the configured gap selection permits it.
Short: During session activity, low two bars ago - current high > ATR multiplied by a parameter AND current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND current high >= previous low AND previous high >= low two bars ago AND the configured gap selection permits it.
Calculation: Selection = all, first, or last per direction and session cycle, as parameterized for each marking type. First permits only the first occurrence; all and last permit each occurrence at the time it forms. For breaker blocks, first is tracked independently per originating block side. ATR is replaced by zero until available.
