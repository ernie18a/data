Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over its specified left and right windows, recognized only after the right window has completed; retain the latest and previous confirmed pivot of each kind.
Calculation: Structure direction = initially neutral; a close above the latest confirmed pivot high sets it bullish and a close below the latest confirmed pivot low sets it bearish. Each pivot can be broken only once; a newly confirmed pivot rearms its respective side. Process high breaks before low breaks.
Calculation: Order-block pressure = on each bullish or bearish structure break, create a block from the high and low of the most recent opposite-direction candle within the origin lookback. Retain at most the specified number of blocks, including mitigated blocks. A bullish block is mitigated when close is below its low; a bearish block is mitigated when close is above its high. Sum +1 for each unmitigated bullish block and -1 for each unmitigated bearish block whose range extended by ATR on both sides contains close; clamp the sum to [-1,1]. Block creation is optional.
Calculation: Gap pressure = create a bullish gap from the high two bars ago to the current low when the latter exceeds the former by at least a specified ATR multiple; create a bearish gap from the current high to the low two bars ago under the mirrored condition. On each bar, including the creation bar, remove every gap whose range intersects the bar's high-low range. Sum +1 for each remaining bullish gap and -1 for each remaining bearish gap whose range extended by ATR on both sides contains close; clamp to [-1,1]. After computing pressure, discard oldest gaps exceeding the specified capacity. Gap creation is optional.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = when consecutive confirmed pivot highs or lows differ by no more than a specified ATR multiple, add the new pivot price as an unswept high or low level. Process levels newest first, then discard oldest levels exceeding the specified capacity. Level creation is optional.
Calculation: Sweep pressure = initially zero; multiply the previous value by a decay factor each bar. For each unswept liquidity level, processed newest first, set it to -1 when high exceeds a high level by more than a specified ATR multiple and close is below the level, or to +1 when low falls below a low level by more than that multiple and close is above the level; mark that level swept. Clamp the final value to [-1,1].
Calculation: Range position = (close minus the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low) divided by half that range, with a small positive denominator floor, clamped to [-1,1].
Calculation: Price deviation = (close minus its rolling SMA) divided by its rolling standard deviation, with a small positive denominator floor.
Calculation: Trend feature = (shorter-period close EMA minus longer-period close EMA) divided by ATR with a small positive floor, clamped to a symmetric limit.
Calculation: ML probability = online logistic regression on seven features: trend feature, order-block pressure, gap pressure, sweep pressure, range position, price deviation clamped to a symmetric limit, and close change over a momentum lookback divided by ATR times the square root of that lookback, also clamped. Replace unavailable raw features with zero. Initialize feature means to zero, variances to one, and all weights including bias to zero. On each closed bar, update each mean m to m+a(x-m), then variance v to (1-a)(v+a(x-m_new)^2); standardize features with the updated mean and square root of variance floored above zero, and clamp standardized values to a symmetric limit. After the forecast horizon has elapsed, standardize the raw features from that many bars ago using the current statistics and train once by logistic-loss gradient descent: target is 1 if current close exceeds that past close, otherwise 0; apply L2 regularization to feature weights but not bias. Predict the current probability after training using current standardized features and a numerically clamped sigmoid. When ML is disabled, probability is one half and its statistics and weights do not update.
Calculation: RL action = discretize the trend feature, ATR deviation from its rolling SMA in rolling-standard-deviation units, and price deviation each into lower, middle, and upper buckets using their respective symmetric thresholds. Initialize all state-action values for flat, long, and short to zero. On each closed bar, update the previous state and action by Q <- Q + learning rate * (reward + discount * maximum current-state Q - Q), where return is current close minus previous close divided by current ATR, long reward is return, short reward is negative return, and flat reward is negative penalty times absolute return; replace unavailable return with zero. Then choose the current state's maximum-Q action, breaking ties in the order flat, long, short, and save state and action for the next bar. When RL is disabled, action is flat and Q is not updated.
Calculation: Composite score = a weighted average of ML probability and RL contribution, scaled and rounded; the contribution is sigmoid of a positive multiple of maximum current-state Q minus mean current-state Q for a long action, one minus that confidence for a short action, and one half for flat. Use zero confidence when RL is disabled.
Long: A confirmed pivot high or low is greater than the previous confirmed pivot of the same kind.
Short: A confirmed pivot high or low is less than or equal to the previous confirmed pivot of the same kind OR no previous pivot of that kind exists.
Long: Close exceeds the latest armed confirmed pivot high.
Short: Close is below the latest armed confirmed pivot low.
Long: Current low exceeds the high two bars ago by at least the specified ATR gap multiple AND current low is greater than that high.
Short: Current high is below the low two bars ago by at least the specified ATR gap multiple AND current high is less than that low.
Long: Low is below an unswept liquidity low by more than the specified ATR sweep multiple AND close is above that level.
Short: High is above an unswept liquidity high by more than the specified ATR sweep multiple AND close is below that level.
Long: Price deviation is below the negative stretch threshold.
Short: Price deviation is above the positive stretch threshold.
Long: RL action equals long.
Short: RL action equals short.
Long: Structure direction equals bullish.
Short: Structure direction equals bearish.
Long: Sweep pressure exceeds the positive display threshold.
Short: Sweep pressure is below the negative display threshold.
Long: ML probability exceeds one half.
Short: ML probability is less than or equal to one half.
Long: Composite score is at least its midpoint.
Short: Composite score is below its midpoint.
Long: Bar index exceeds the warmup length AND ML probability exceeds the long threshold AND (RL is disabled OR RL action equals long) AND (ML probability crosses above the long threshold OR close breaks an armed pivot high OR sweep pressure exceeds the positive trigger threshold) AND (confluence is disabled OR (structure direction is bullish AND (order-block pressure > 0 OR gap pressure > 0 OR sweep pressure > 0) AND range position is below the upper confluence threshold)).
Short: Bar index exceeds the warmup length AND ML probability is below the short threshold AND (RL is disabled OR RL action equals short) AND (ML probability crosses below the short threshold OR close breaks an armed pivot low OR sweep pressure is below the negative trigger threshold) AND (confluence is disabled OR (structure direction is bearish AND (order-block pressure < 0 OR gap pressure < 0 OR sweep pressure < 0) AND range position is above the lower confluence threshold)).
