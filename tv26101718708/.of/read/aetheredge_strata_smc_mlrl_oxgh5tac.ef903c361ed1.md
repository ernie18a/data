Calculation: Up probability = online logistic-regression estimate of price rising over a forward horizon, trained on lagged outcomes using standardized features: EMA(8) minus EMA(21) divided by ATR; signed proximity to active bullish/bearish order blocks and unfilled bullish/bearish fair value gaps; decaying signed liquidity-sweep score; close’s position relative to the midpoint of the rolling high-low range; close’s z-score over a shorter lookback; and close-to-close momentum over five bars divided by ATR times √5.
Calculation: RL action values = tabular Q-learning values for flat, long, and short, updated each confirmed bar using ATR-scaled return for the previous action (flat receives a penalty proportional to absolute return). State is the combination of three buckets each for EMA trend, ATR volatility z-score, and close z-score; choose the action with the highest value, breaking ties in favor of flat, then long, then short.
Long: RL action values: long > flat AND long ≥ short
Short: RL action values: short > flat AND short > long
Long: after model warmup AND up probability > long threshold AND (RL action is disabled OR RL action is long) AND (up probability crossed above long threshold OR close crossed above the latest confirmed swing high OR a bullish liquidity sweep score exceeds its trigger threshold) AND (confluence is disabled OR (structure trend is bullish OR an upward change of character occurred) AND (bullish order-block proximity OR bullish fair-value-gap proximity OR positive liquidity-sweep score) AND close is below the rolling range midpoint plus half the range)
Short: after model warmup AND up probability < short threshold AND (RL action is disabled OR RL action is short) AND (up probability crossed below short threshold OR close crossed below the latest confirmed swing low OR a bearish liquidity sweep score exceeds its trigger threshold) AND (confluence is disabled OR (structure trend is bearish OR a downward change of character occurred) AND (bearish order-block proximity OR bearish fair-value-gap proximity OR negative liquidity-sweep score) AND close is above the rolling range midpoint minus half the range)
