Calculation: IWM directional efficiency = absolute change in IWM close over the longer lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Composite score = weighted sum of: positive/negative points for IWM close above/below its shorter-term EMA; IWM shorter-term EMA above/below its longer-term EMA; QQQ close above/below its shorter-term EMA; IWM ROC minus SPY ROC over the same lookback; positive points when IWM directional efficiency meets its minimum; positive/negative points when IWM volume-to-average-volume ratio meets its threshold and shorter-term IWM ROC is positive/negative; negative points when 10-year yield change meets its upward pressure threshold; and positive points when yield change meets its downward pressure threshold. These calculations use the selected analysis-timeframe bars, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; the current analysis-timeframe bar is used, including while it is still forming.
Long: composite score >= bullish threshold AND IWM directional efficiency >= minimum
Short: composite score <= bearish threshold OR (IWM close < IWM longer-term EMA AND longer-term IWM ROC < 0 AND IWM directional efficiency >= minimum)
