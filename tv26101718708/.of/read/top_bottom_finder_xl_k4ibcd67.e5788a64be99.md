Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = the high of the bar a configurable number of calculation bars ago is greater than or equal to all highs in a configurable number of preceding bars and greater than all highs in the subsequent bars through the current calculation bar. A confirmed pivot low uses the corresponding less-than-or-equal and less-than comparisons on lows.
Calculation: Calculation bars = chart bars or bars of a selected higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within each chart bar. Carry the last available result forward between higher-timeframe completions. Evaluate pivot-bar RSI, EMA, ATR, volume, range and their averages on the pivot bar. Range is high minus low; volume and range averages are simple moving averages with the same lookback.
Long: A confirmed pivot low is present on this chart bar and was absent on the previous chart bar, with watch signals enabled.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is present on this chart bar and was absent on the previous chart bar, with watch signals enabled.
Long: A filtered pivot low is present on this chart bar and was absent on the previous chart bar, where a filtered pivot low requires a confirmed pivot low AND pivot-bar RSI at or below the weak bottom threshold AND either the EMA filter is disabled OR pivot low < pivot-bar EMA minus pivot-bar ATR multiplied by the extension threshold AND either confirmation is disabled OR the selected volume and/or range confirmation passes. Volume confirmation requires pivot-bar average volume > 0 AND pivot-bar volume >= pivot-bar average volume multiplied by the volume threshold; range confirmation requires pivot-bar average range > 0 AND pivot-bar range >= pivot-bar average range multiplied by the range threshold. If both confirmations are selected, both must pass.
Short: A filtered pivot high is present on this chart bar and was absent on the previous chart bar, where a filtered pivot high requires a confirmed pivot high AND pivot-bar RSI at or above the weak top threshold AND either the EMA filter is disabled OR pivot high > pivot-bar EMA plus pivot-bar ATR multiplied by the extension threshold AND either confirmation is disabled OR the selected volume and/or range confirmation passes, using the same confirmation comparisons as for a filtered pivot low.
