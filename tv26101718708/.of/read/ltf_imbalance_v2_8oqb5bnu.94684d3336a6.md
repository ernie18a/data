Calculation: Lower-timeframe bars = the first and third bars, in chronological order, within the current chart bar for each enabled lower timeframe; use their values as available while the chart bar is forming, or their completed values at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Recent pivots = the most recent bounded list of confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows, each confirmed after its right-hand lookback; when a high and low are confirmed on the same bar, the low is more recent.
Long: The first lower-timeframe bar's low > the third lower-timeframe bar's high AND their difference >= minimum gap size in price units AND (pivot filter is disabled OR the absolute percentage difference between their midpoint and at least one recent pivot <= pivot proximity threshold).
Short: The first lower-timeframe bar's high < the third lower-timeframe bar's low AND their difference >= minimum gap size in price units AND (pivot filter is disabled OR the absolute percentage difference between their midpoint and at least one recent pivot <= pivot proximity threshold).
