Calculation: Key levels = previous regular-session high, low, and close; previous overnight-session high and low; current regular-session open; and the high and low of the first six regular-session bars (usable only after bar six). Update previous-session levels from the completed prior regular session and overnight levels from the overnight session ending at the current regular-session open.
Calculation: Near a level = current high or low, as applicable, is within the level-zone distance of that level, inclusive.
Calculation: Sell failure score at a level = count of: prior bar bullish and prior close below level; prior high at or above level and current close below level; current bar bearish with body/range at least 0.5; current low below prior low; current high below prior high; two-bars-back high within the level zone and close below level, and current high within the zone and close below level.
Calculation: Buy failure score at a level = count of: prior bar bearish and prior close above level; prior low at or below level and current close above level; current bar bullish with body/range at least 0.5; current high above prior high; current low above prior low; two-bars-back low within the level zone and close above level, and current low within the zone and close above level.
Calculation: Sell signal-bar score = count of: close below bar midpoint; nonzero body and upper tail at least body size; close below prior low; prior high at least the level minus the level-zone distance; range at least half the 10-bar simple average of high minus low.
Calculation: Buy signal-bar score = count of: close above bar midpoint; nonzero body and lower tail at least body size; close above prior high; prior low at most the level plus the level-zone distance; range at least half the 10-bar simple average of high minus low.
Calculation: Sell setup = at least the minimum failure-score parameter and minimum signal-bar-score parameter at a key level, with current high within its zone and close below it.
Calculation: Buy setup = at least the minimum failure-score parameter and minimum signal-bar-score parameter at a key level, with current low within its zone and close above it.
Calculation: Planned short entry = pullback-bar low minus one tick; short stop = the greater of the reversal high and the high of the bar before follow-through confirmation, plus one tick. Planned long entry = pullback-bar high plus one tick; long stop = the lesser of the reversal low and the low of the bar before follow-through confirmation, minus one tick.
Calculation: Initial risk = distance between planned entry and stop. Profit target = planned entry plus (long) or minus (short) two times initial risk.
Short: Flat position, daily trade count below its maximum, during the first configured signal-window bars of the regular session, and a sell setup at any key level; then within the configured follow-through window, a bearish bar with body/range at least the configured minimum or a close below the reversal-bar low; then within the configured pullback wait limit, a bar closing above its open or midpoint and pullback depth/range of the follow-through move at most the configured maximum; initial risk positive and within its maximum; enter when price reaches the planned short entry.
Long: Flat position, daily trade count below its maximum, during the first configured signal-window bars of the regular session, and a buy setup at any key level; then within the configured follow-through window, a bullish bar with body/range at least the configured minimum or a close above the reversal-bar high; then within the configured pullback wait limit, a bar closing below its open or midpoint and pullback depth/range of the follow-through move at most the configured maximum; initial risk positive and within its maximum; enter when price reaches the planned long entry.
Exit: Long, low at or below initial stop or high at or above profit target, or the regular session has just ended.
Exit: Short, high at or above initial stop or low at or below profit target, or the regular session has just ended.
