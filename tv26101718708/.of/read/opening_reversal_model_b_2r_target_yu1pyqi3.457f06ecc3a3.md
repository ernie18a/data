Calculation: Sessions and levels = Define a regular session and an overnight session in a chosen timezone. At the start of each regular session, retain the preceding regular session's high, low, and last close, the completed overnight session's high and low, and the current session's opening price. Track the high and low of an initial number of regular-session bars; these levels become eligible only after that number of bars has passed. Count regular-session bars from one and reset the daily submitted-order count at each regular-session start.
Calculation: Scores for level L = Each satisfied comparison contributes one point. The short failure score counts: previous close > previous open AND previous close < L; previous high >= L AND close < L; close < open AND high > low AND absolute body / range >= minimum failure-body fraction; low < previous low; high < previous high; high two bars ago within L plus or minus the zone width AND close two bars ago < L AND high >= L minus zone width AND close < L. The long failure score counts: previous close < previous open AND previous close > L; previous low <= L AND close > L; close > open AND high > low AND absolute body / range >= minimum failure-body fraction; high > previous high; low > previous low; low two bars ago within L plus or minus the zone width AND close two bars ago > L AND low <= L plus zone width AND close > L. The short signal score counts: close < bar midpoint; absolute body > 0 AND upper wick >= absolute body; close < previous low; previous high >= L minus zone width; range >= a fraction of the simple moving average of range including the current bar. The long signal score counts: close > bar midpoint; absolute body > 0 AND lower wick >= absolute body; close > previous high; previous low <= L plus zone width; range >= the same fraction of the same simple moving average of range.
Calculation: Short setup = Within the signal window, at least one eligible level among the prior regular-session high, overnight high, current regular-session open, initial regular-session high, and prior regular-session close satisfies high within the level plus or minus the zone width AND close < the level AND short failure score >= its minimum AND short signal score >= its minimum AND high - low + twice the tick offset > 0 AND high - low + twice the tick offset <= maximum risk. Long setup = Within the signal window, at least one eligible level among the prior regular-session low, overnight low, current regular-session open, initial regular-session low, and prior regular-session close satisfies low within the level plus or minus the zone width AND close > the level AND long failure score >= its minimum AND long signal score >= its minimum AND high - low + twice the tick offset > 0 AND high - low + twice the tick offset <= maximum risk. The signal window is the first configurable number of regular-session bars.
Calculation: Setup state = While flat, with no active setup and the daily submitted-order count below its limit, record a qualifying setup's direction and signal-bar high and low; a short setup takes precedence if both directions qualify. Reset the setup at the start of each regular session and at the first bar outside the regular session. Evaluate the follow-through stage on the setup bar and subsequent bars: short follow-through is close < open AND range > 0 AND absolute body / range >= minimum follow-through body fraction, OR close < signal-bar low; long follow-through is close > open AND range > 0 AND absolute body / range >= minimum follow-through body fraction, OR close > signal-bar high. On short follow-through, store the current low as the follow-through low and the greater of signal-bar high and previous-bar high as the reversal high. On long follow-through, store the current high as the follow-through high and the lesser of signal-bar low and previous-bar low as the reversal low. If follow-through has not occurred, reset after the follow-through stage's bar count exceeds its limit; test follow-through before that reset. Evaluate the pullback stage on the follow-through bar and subsequent bars, resetting after its bar count exceeds its limit and testing for an order before that reset. Increment the daily submitted-order count and reset the setup when an entry order is submitted.
Calculation: Entry orders = A qualifying long pullback submits a buy stop at pullback-bar high plus the tick offset, with an attached stop at reversal low minus the tick offset and a target above the submitted stop-entry price by a configurable multiple of the distance between those two prices. A qualifying short pullback submits a sell stop at pullback-bar low minus the tick offset, with an attached stop at reversal high plus the tick offset and a target below the submitted stop-entry price by the same multiple of the distance between those two prices. Orders become eligible for execution after submission and remain pending until filled or replaced by another order with the same direction's entry identifier. Attached exit prices use the submitted entry price, even if the fill differs.
Long: Long setup state AND long follow-through has occurred AND (close < open OR close < bar midpoint) AND follow-through high - reversal low > 0 AND (follow-through high - low) / (follow-through high - reversal low) <= maximum pullback fraction AND pullback-bar high plus tick offset - (reversal low minus tick offset) > 0 AND pullback-bar high plus tick offset - (reversal low minus tick offset) <= maximum risk.
Short: Short setup state AND short follow-through has occurred AND (close > open OR close > bar midpoint) AND reversal high - follow-through low > 0 AND (high - follow-through low) / (reversal high - follow-through low) <= maximum pullback fraction AND reversal high plus tick offset - (pullback-bar low minus tick offset) > 0 AND reversal high plus tick offset - (pullback-bar low minus tick offset) <= maximum risk.
Exit: Long, low <= attached stop OR high >= attached target OR previous bar is inside the regular session AND current bar is outside it.
Exit: Short, high >= attached stop OR low <= attached target OR previous bar is inside the regular session AND current bar is outside it.
