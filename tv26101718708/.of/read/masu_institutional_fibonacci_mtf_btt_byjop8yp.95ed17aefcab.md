Calculation: ATR = average true range.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA, and the trend filter EMA uses a longer lookback than both.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on the higher timeframe's last completed bar, fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA for bullish; fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA for bearish.
Calculation: Ribbon bullish = at least the alignment threshold minus one of the seven successive pairs of moving averages are ordered from shorter-lookback above longer-lookback; bearish = at least that many are ordered from shorter-lookback below longer-lookback. The eight lookbacks increase successively.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bollinger lower/upper = middle ± multiplier × standard deviation of close; middle is the moving average of close.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: ADX = average directional index.
Calculation: Fib swing high/low = latest bar whose high/low is the highest high/lowest low over the swing lookback plus current bar, provided the high-to-window-low / window-high-to-low range is at least minimum ATR multiple × ATR; retain the most recent qualifying high and low and their bar indices.
Calculation: Fib levels = swing high − {0.382, 0.500, 0.618} × (swing high − swing low); upswing when the retained swing high is later than the retained swing low, otherwise downswing.
Calculation: Fib bullish = valid retained swings each at least two bars old AND upswing AND low is within zone ATR multiple × ATR of any enabled Fib level AND ((min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ minimum wick fraction OR (close > open AND close > the touched level)).
Calculation: Fib bearish = valid retained swings each at least two bars old AND downswing AND high is within zone ATR multiple × ATR of any enabled Fib level AND ((high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ minimum wick fraction OR (close < open AND close < the touched level)).
Long: Fib bullish OR ((ADX > threshold AND EMA fast > EMA slow AND close > trend filter EMA AND EMA fast crosses above EMA slow AND volume spike) OR (close > prior bar's highest high over breakout lookback AND volume spike AND close > trend filter EMA) OR (ADX ≤ threshold AND close < lower Bollinger band AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI crosses above oversold level)) AND (higher-timeframe bullish trend OR MTF filter disabled) AND (ribbon bullish OR ribbon filter disabled) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR (CVD bullish divergence OR strong buying) AND (close crosses above VWAP OR close > VWAP) OR close > VWAP) AND (Fib filter disabled OR Fib bullish OR upswing) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is in trading session).
Short: Fib bearish OR ((ADX > threshold AND EMA fast < EMA slow AND close < trend filter EMA AND EMA fast crosses below EMA slow AND volume spike) OR (close < prior bar's lowest low over breakout lookback AND volume spike AND close < trend filter EMA) OR (ADX ≤ threshold AND close > upper Bollinger band AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI crosses below overbought level)) AND (higher-timeframe bearish trend OR MTF filter disabled) AND (ribbon bearish OR ribbon filter disabled) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR (CVD bearish divergence OR strong selling) AND (close crosses below VWAP OR close < VWAP) OR close < VWAP) AND (Fib filter disabled OR Fib bearish OR downswing) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is in trading session).
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiple OR (trailing enabled AND high reaches entry price + ATR × trailing multiple AND low ≤ highest high since activation − ATR × trailing multiple) OR (trailing disabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple).
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR (trailing enabled AND low reaches entry price − ATR × trailing multiple AND high ≥ lowest low since activation + ATR × trailing multiple) OR (trailing disabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR × take-profit multiple).
