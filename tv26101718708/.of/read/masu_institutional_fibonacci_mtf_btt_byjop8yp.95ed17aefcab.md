Calculation: Swing high and swing low = most recent chart-bar high and low respectively that equal the highest high or lowest low over the swing lookback including the current bar and whose distance from the opposite rolling extreme is at least the swing ATR multiplier times ATR; update both independently and record their bar indices.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = swing high minus each enabled retracement fraction times (swing high minus swing low); both swings must exist and have bar indices greater than the first bar index. The nearest touched level is the first touched enabled level in increasing retracement-fraction order.
Calculation: Upward swing = both swings exist with bar indices greater than the first bar index AND swing-high bar index > swing-low bar index.
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci bounce = Fibonacci enabled AND both swings are at least a minimum age in bars AND upward swing AND absolute difference between low and an enabled Fibonacci level <= zone multiplier times ATR AND (lower wick / (high - low) >= wick threshold OR (close > open AND close > nearest touched level)); use zero for the wick ratio when high = low.
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci rejection = Fibonacci enabled AND both swings are at least a minimum age in bars AND upward swing is false AND absolute difference between high and an enabled Fibonacci level <= zone multiplier times ATR AND (upper wick / (high - low) >= wick threshold OR (close < open AND close < nearest touched level)); use zero for the wick ratio when high = low.
Calculation: Ribbon bullish or bearish alignment = count of adjacent pairs of moving averages of close, ordered by increasing lookback, whose shorter-lookback average is respectively greater or less than the longer-lookback average; alignment holds when the count >= alignment threshold minus one. Use the selected EMA, SMA, or HMA for all ribbon averages.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of volume times (high + low + close) / 3 divided by cumulative volume since the start of the trading day; use close when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars with close > open minus volume on bars with close < open, with zero contribution on equal-close bars.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = order flow enabled AND ((cumulative volume delta > its SMA AND close < previous close AND cumulative volume delta > its previous value) OR (buy volume / max(sell volume, 1) > 1 + volume-ratio threshold)) AND close > daily VWAP; buy volume is current volume when close > open and zero otherwise, and sell volume is current volume when close < open and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Bearish order flow = order flow enabled AND ((cumulative volume delta < its SMA AND close > previous close AND cumulative volume delta < its previous value) OR (buy volume / max(sell volume, 1) < 1 - volume-ratio threshold)) AND close < daily VWAP; buy and sell volume use the same definitions as bullish order flow.
Calculation: Long filters = (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > shorter EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar)) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bullish ribbon alignment) AND (order flow disabled OR close > daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci disabled OR bullish Fibonacci bounce OR upward swing) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session).
Calculation: Short filters = (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < shorter EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar)) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bearish ribbon alignment) AND (order flow disabled OR close < daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci disabled OR bearish Fibonacci rejection OR upward swing is false) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session).
Calculation: Long confluence score = sum of one point each for the long higher-timeframe, ribbon, order-flow and Fibonacci filter clauses, excluding the session clause, and one point for ADX > ADX threshold; a disabled filter contributes one point.
Calculation: Short confluence score = sum of one point each for the short higher-timeframe, ribbon, order-flow and Fibonacci filter clauses, excluding the session clause, and one point for ADX > ADX threshold; a disabled filter contributes one point.
Calculation: Buy-volume percentage = sum of volume on close > open bars divided by the sum of volume on close > open and close < open bars over the volume-pressure lookback; use the neutral percentage when the denominator is zero and before sufficient bars exist.
Long: ((ADX > threshold AND short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA AND close > long-term filter EMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) OR (close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND close > long-term filter EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above oversold threshold)) AND long filters.
Short: ((ADX > threshold AND short-term EMA crosses below medium-term EMA AND close < long-term filter EMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) OR (close < lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND close < long-term filter EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below overbought threshold)) AND short filters.
Long: Bullish Fibonacci bounce.
Short: Bearish Fibonacci rejection.
Long: Long confluence score > short confluence score.
Short: Short confluence score > long confluence score.
Long: Bullish ribbon alignment.
Short: Bullish ribbon alignment is false AND bearish ribbon alignment.
Long: Bullish order flow.
Short: Bullish order flow is false AND bearish order flow.
Long: Buy-volume percentage > upper pressure threshold.
Short: Buy-volume percentage < lower pressure threshold.
Long: Gradient display enabled AND RSI > upper gradient threshold AND close > open.
Short: Gradient display enabled AND RSI < lower gradient threshold AND close < open.
Long: On each dashboard timeframe, absolute difference between shorter and longer EMA >= ATR times neutrality multiplier AND shorter EMA > longer EMA; use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and the last available completed intrabar when lower.
Short: On each dashboard timeframe, absolute difference between shorter and longer EMA >= ATR times neutrality multiplier AND shorter EMA <= longer EMA; use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and the last available completed intrabar when lower.
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR (trailing disabled AND high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times profit multiplier) OR (trailing enabled AND high since entry reaches entry price plus the signal-bar ATR trailing distance AND low <= highest high since trailing activation minus that trailing distance).
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR (trailing disabled AND low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times profit multiplier) OR (trailing enabled AND low since entry reaches entry price minus the signal-bar ATR trailing distance AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation plus that trailing distance).
