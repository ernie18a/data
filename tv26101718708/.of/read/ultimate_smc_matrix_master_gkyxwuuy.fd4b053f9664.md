Calculation: Displacement = 100 × (current open − open at the displacement lookback) / open at the displacement lookback.
Calculation: Order block = on a displacement crossing either positive sensitivity upward or negative sensitivity downward, record the crossing bar even if no block is created; create a block only if there was no prior crossing or the distance from the prior crossing exceeds the minimum separation. For a positive crossing, use the high and low of the most recent bearish candle in a specified historical search window; for a negative crossing, use the most recent bullish candle in that window. If none exists, use the candle at the start of the window. The window starts at the displacement lookback and extends further into the past. A bullish block remains active until the selected mitigation price is below its low; a bearish block remains active until that price is above its high. The selected mitigation price is either the previous close for both sides or the current low for bullish blocks and current high for bearish blocks. Apply invalidation before testing the block on each bar.
Calculation: Confirmed fractal high = the high at the center of a fractal-length window, confirmed when each of the preceding half-length high changes is positive, each of the following half-length high changes is negative, and that center high equals the highest high in the window. The half-length is the fractal length divided by two and truncated. A confirmed fractal low uses negative preceding low changes, positive following low changes, and the lowest low in the window. Retain the most recently confirmed fractal of each kind and mark it unbroken until its first qualifying close crossover or crossunder, respectively; a new fractal replaces the previous one before the current bar's crossover test.
Calculation: Trailing support = on each qualifying upward break of a confirmed fractal high, replace support with the minimum low from the bar after that fractal through the bar before the break and mark it unbroken. Trailing resistance = on each qualifying downward break of a confirmed fractal low, replace resistance with the maximum high from the bar after that fractal through the bar before the break and mark it unbroken. On subsequent bars, mark the respective level broken on the first close beyond it; do not test that level for a break on a bar that replaces it.
Long: Displacement crosses above positive sensitivity AND the distance from the previous displacement crossing exceeds the minimum separation OR displacement crosses above positive sensitivity AND no previous displacement crossing exists.
Short: Displacement crosses below negative sensitivity AND the distance from the previous displacement crossing exceeds the minimum separation OR displacement crosses below negative sensitivity AND no previous displacement crossing exists.
Long: Current low < the high of any active bullish order block.
Short: Current high > the low of any active bearish order block.
Long: Current low − high two bars ago > minimum gap size AND previous close > high two bars ago.
Short: Low two bars ago − current high > minimum gap size AND previous close < low two bars ago.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed fractal high AND that fractal high is unbroken.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed fractal low AND that fractal low is unbroken.
Long: Close > unbroken trailing resistance AND no new trailing resistance is created on the current bar.
Short: Close < unbroken trailing support AND no new trailing support is created on the current bar.
