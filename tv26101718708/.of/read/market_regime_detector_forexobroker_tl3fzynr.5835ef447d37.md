Long: current bar is confirmed AND more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the previous long signal AND volume > volume SMA(20) × volume multiplier AND ((ADX > trend threshold AND BB width percentile rank > 30 AND ATR percentile rank < volatility threshold AND BB width percentile rank <= 80 AND EMA(fast, close) crosses above EMA(slow, close) AND close > EMA(50, close)) OR (ATR percentile rank <= volatility threshold AND BB width percentile rank <= 80 AND NOT (ADX > trend threshold AND BB width percentile rank > 30 AND ATR percentile rank < volatility threshold) AND close <= lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold))
Short: current bar is confirmed AND more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the previous short signal AND volume > volume SMA(20) × volume multiplier AND ((ADX > trend threshold AND BB width percentile rank > 30 AND ATR percentile rank < volatility threshold AND BB width percentile rank <= 80 AND EMA(fast, close) crosses below EMA(slow, close) AND close < EMA(50, close)) OR (ATR percentile rank <= volatility threshold AND BB width percentile rank <= 80 AND NOT (ADX > trend threshold AND BB width percentile rank > 30 AND ATR percentile rank < volatility threshold) AND close >= upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold))
Calculation: BB width percentile rank = percentile rank of ((upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / Bollinger basis × 100) over the latest 100 chart bars
Calculation: ATR percentile rank = percentile rank of ATR over the latest 100 chart bars
