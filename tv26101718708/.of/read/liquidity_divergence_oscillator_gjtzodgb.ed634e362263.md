Calculation: Liquidity health = 100 × tanh(−z(smoothed Kyle’s lambda) / 3), where Kyle’s lambda is rolling covariance of price change and signed volume divided by rolling variance of signed volume; signed volume = volume × sign(price change). Smooth lambda over a shorter window and normalize it over a longer window.
Long: At a confirmed price pivot low, the low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low AND liquidity health at the pivot is higher than at the previous pivot (regular divergence), OR the low is higher AND liquidity health is lower (hidden divergence); the two pivots are within the maximum pivot separation.
Short: At a confirmed price pivot high, the high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high AND liquidity health at the pivot is lower than at the previous pivot (regular divergence), OR the high is lower AND liquidity health is higher (hidden divergence); the two pivots are within the maximum pivot separation.
