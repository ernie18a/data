Calculation: Price pivot = a high or low of a selected OHLC source confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; for a high, the pivot is at least as high as every bar to its left and strictly higher than every bar to its right; reverse the inequalities for a low. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, and make the decision on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Liquidity health = calculate price change from a selected OHLC source and signed volume as volume multiplied by the sign of that change. Kyle's lambda is rolling covariance of price change and signed volume divided by rolling population variance of signed volume, or zero when that variance is zero. Smooth lambda using either an SMA or the Ehlers UltimateSmoother; alternatively leave it unsmoothed. Standardize the result using either rolling mean and population standard deviation or rolling median and median absolute deviation scaled to be comparable to standard deviation; return zero when the scale is zero. Optionally use the median of standardized values from shorter, medium, and longer windows, with the shorter and longer windows respectively half and twice the medium window. Negate the selected standardized value, divide by a squash scale, clamp it symmetrically, and apply the hyperbolic tangent.
Long: A low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding low pivot is at most the maximum pivot spacing AND ((its price is lower AND its liquidity health is higher than at the preceding low pivot) OR (hidden divergences are enabled AND its price is higher AND its liquidity health is lower than at the preceding low pivot)).
Short: A high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding high pivot is at most the maximum pivot spacing AND ((its price is higher AND its liquidity health is lower than at the preceding high pivot) OR (hidden divergences are enabled AND its price is lower AND its liquidity health is higher than at the preceding high pivot)).
