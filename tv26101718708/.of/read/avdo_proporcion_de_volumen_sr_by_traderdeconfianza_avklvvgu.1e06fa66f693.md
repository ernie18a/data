Calculation: Volume profile = Maintain a histogram of volume over 400 equal-width logarithmic price bins spanning the padded rolling high–low range. Rebuild it when a bar leaves that range or bin width changes by more than the rebuild-drift parameter; otherwise update it incrementally. Retain at most the profile lookback or 250 bars, whichever is smaller, with each bar’s volume exponentially decayed by its age using exp(-1/sqrt(profile lookback)). Allocate a fixed body share of volume across bins in proportion to their overlap with the log-price candle body, using at least one bin width as the denominator; distribute the remaining volume equally across all bins touched by the candle’s high–low range. On each bar remove the oldest retained contribution. The point of control is the largest-volume bin, refined to the volume-weighted mean index of that bin and its two neighbors when available. Find the lower and upper boundaries of the central inner and outer volume fractions by cumulative-volume interpolation. Convert any fractional bin index i to price as exp(log-range-low + (i + 0.5) × bin width). The value center is the geometric mean of the inner boundaries blended toward the volume-weighted mean bin price, with blend weight equal to their relative difference divided by the symmetry threshold, capped at one. Smooth the point of control, inner boundaries, and value center with a short EMA and the outer boundaries with a longer EMA; both lengths are derived from the profile lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive trail = Blend the smoothed value center and point of control using the profile-center bias. Set the base flip distance and transition distance to fixed multiples of a long-lookback ATR, and the minimum distance to a smaller ATR multiple. Divide the base flip distance by an acceleration factor: one plus the acceleration sensitivity times a weighted sum of absolute one-bar close change and absolute close-to-profile-base distance, both normalized by ATR; cap this factor at its maximum and floor the resulting distance at the minimum distance. Initialize the trail at the profile base and its direction bullish if close is at least the base, otherwise bearish. On each subsequent bar, if bullish and close is below the previous trail, flip bearish and set the trail to the profile base plus the flip distance; if bearish and close is above the previous trail, flip bullish and set it to the profile base minus the flip distance. Then ratchet the bullish trail upward toward base minus flip distance, or the bearish trail downward toward base plus flip distance. If its distance behind the base exceeds the transition distance, ratchet it toward the base to the minimum distance.
Calculation: Distribution oscillator = For each of the adaptive trail and smoothed point of control, clamp that anchor between the ordered inner volume boundaries and expand the ordered outer boundaries to include them. Normalize close piecewise linearly so the anchor maps to zero, the inner boundaries to plus or minus 25, and the outer boundaries to plus or minus 50; clamp the result to that range. Let confidence equal one minus the absolute difference between the two normalized values divided by 100, clamped to zero through one. The oscillator is an EMA of 0.75 times the trail-normalized value plus 0.25 times the point-of-control-normalized value times confidence. Its signal is a longer EMA of the oscillator; its candle-direction value is a shorter EMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Volume-zone trigger = Previous volume is positive AND current volume divided by previous volume is at least the volume-ratio threshold AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, high minus low is at least ATR times its multiplier AND, if the body filter is enabled, absolute close minus open divided by high minus low is at least the minimum body fraction; use zero for that fraction when high equals low.
Calculation: Active volume zone = The high–low range of a volume-zone trigger bar, bullish when its close is at least its open and bearish otherwise. A bullish zone remains active until a later close is below its low; a bearish zone remains active until a later close is above its high.
Long: The volume-zone trigger is true AND close is at least open.
Short: The volume-zone trigger is true AND close is below open.
Long: The candle-direction value is above zero AND the bar is not marked as critical volume.
Short: The candle-direction value is below zero AND the bar is not marked as critical volume.
Calculation: Critical volume = Normalize volume by the highest volume over its lookback, multiply by a fixed scale, and subtract an EMA of that normalized volume. Critical volume is present when this difference is positive and at least a threshold fraction of its highest value over the volume lookback.
Long: Adaptive-trail direction is bullish.
Short: Adaptive-trail direction is bearish.
Long: The oscillator crosses above its signal OR crosses above zero OR close crosses above the adaptive trail OR adaptive-trail direction flips bullish.
Short: The oscillator crosses below its signal OR crosses below zero OR close crosses below the adaptive trail OR adaptive-trail direction flips bearish.
Long: An EMA of the oscillator crosses above its own previous value AND the oscillator is below its lower extreme threshold AND low is below the lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback AND more than the reversal-spacing lookback has elapsed since the preceding bullish or bearish reversal, or no reversal has occurred.
Short: An EMA of the oscillator crosses below its own previous value AND the oscillator is above its upper extreme threshold AND high is above the highest high of the preceding sweep lookback AND more than the reversal-spacing lookback has elapsed since the preceding bullish or bearish reversal, or no reversal has occurred.
Long: Price returns to an active bullish volume zone after moving away AND adaptive-trail direction is bullish.
Exit: long, adaptive-trail direction flips bearish OR price reaches the stop below the entry zone’s low.
