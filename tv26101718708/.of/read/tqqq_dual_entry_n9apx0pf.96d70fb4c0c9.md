Calculation: MA entry state = (time buffer disabled OR bars since the most recent close < entry SMA or EMA >= the entry time-buffer length) AND (percentage buffer disabled OR close >= entry SMA or EMA × (1 + entry buffer percentage)); the state is false when a required bars-since value is unavailable.
Calculation: EMA entry state = close > entry EMA AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR entry EMA > its previous value on every bar in the selected trend lookback).
Calculation: MA exit state = close < exit SMA or EMA AND (time buffer disabled OR bars since the most recent close >= exit SMA or EMA >= exit time-buffer length minus one) AND (percentage buffer disabled OR close <= exit SMA or EMA × (1 - exit buffer percentage)); the state is false when a required bars-since value is unavailable.
Long: position is flat AND previous bar's MA entry state = true AND the state on the bar before it = false.
Long: position is flat AND previous bar's EMA entry state = true AND the state on the bar before it = false AND previous bar's MA entry state did not become true; classify the entry as EMA-based.
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND current bar low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage).
Exit: long, current bar low > entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage) OR stop loss disabled, AND previous bar's MA exit state = true AND the state on the bar before it = false AND (entry was MA-based OR entry was EMA-based and close exceeded the entry SMA or EMA at least once during the position through the previous bar).
Exit: long, current bar low > entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage) OR stop loss disabled, AND entry was EMA-based AND close has never exceeded the entry SMA or EMA during the position through the previous bar AND previous bar's close < entry EMA AND the close on the bar before it >= its entry EMA.
