Calculation: Pressure = EMA of raw pressure on chart bars. For each completed lower-timeframe bar within the Chicago-time session from 17:00 inclusive to 16:00 exclusive, assign its volume a positive sign if its close exceeds the preceding lower-timeframe close, a negative sign if below, and zero if equal. Sum signed and total volume into fixed, epoch-aligned flow buckets; their timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. Retain a configurable number of the latest completed nonempty buckets plus the bucket still forming. Reset the buckets when a chart bar enters the session after an out-of-session chart bar. Raw pressure is 100 times retained signed volume divided by retained total volume when the chart bar is in session and retained total volume is positive, and zero otherwise. Continue the EMA across session resets.
Long: Chart bar is in session AND pressure > 0
Short: Chart bar is in session AND pressure < 0
