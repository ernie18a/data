Calculation: Buy pressure percentage = candle-structure pressure percentage estimated from close location within the candle range, directional body conviction, upper and lower wicks, and volume
Calculation: Sell pressure percentage = complementary candle-structure pressure percentage
Calculation: Local trail = selectable EMA, RMA, or SMA of close, HLC3, or OHLC4 on the chart timeframe
Calculation: HTF trail = the same selected moving average and source on a higher timeframe, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar
Long: Buy pressure percentage > 50% OR close > Local trail OR close > HTF trail
Short: Sell pressure percentage > 50% OR close < Local trail OR close < HTF trail
