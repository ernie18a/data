Calculation: Adjusted OHLC = optionally transform each chart bar: close = (raw open + raw high + raw low + raw close) / 4; open = (previous adjusted open + previous adjusted close) / 2, initialized to (raw open + adjusted close) / 2; high = max(raw high, adjusted open, adjusted close); low = min(raw low, adjusted open, adjusted close). Otherwise use raw OHLC. All OHLC references below are adjusted unless marked raw.
Calculation: Trend line = if CCI(close) >= 0, max(low - multiplier × SMA(raw true range), previous trend line); otherwise min(high + multiplier × SMA(raw true range), previous trend line). Replace an unavailable previous trend line with zero.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression endpoint of close minus the average of (highest high and lowest low over the momentum lookback)/2 and SMA(close) over the same lookback; regression uses that same lookback.
Calculation: Delta candles = cumulative sum of signed volume, where upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low, body = abs(close - open), and signed volume = volume × 0.5 × (upper wick + lower wick + 2 × body) / (upper wick + lower wick + body), positive when close >= open and negative otherwise; replace a zero or undefined fraction with 0.5. Candle open is previous cumulative sum and close is current cumulative sum. Optionally transform these candles to Heikin Ashi using high = max(open, close), low = min(open, close), transformed close = (open + high + low + close) / 4, and transformed open = (previous transformed open + previous transformed close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: Trend long state = CCI(close) >= 0 AND close > trend line + optional price offset; trend short state = CCI(close) < 0 AND close < trend line - optional price offset. Bars since a state is undefined until that state has occurred.
Long: low crosses above trend line.
Short: high crosses below trend line.
Long: datetime is after the optional start and before the optional end AND long trading is enabled AND position side is not long AND trend long state AND bars since trend short state > trend lookback AND squeeze momentum > 0 AND bars since squeeze momentum < 0 > momentum lookback AND delta candle open <= delta candle close AND bars since delta candle open > delta candle close > delta lookback AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold).
Short: datetime is after the optional start and before the optional end AND short trading is enabled AND position side is not short AND trend short state AND bars since trend long state > trend lookback AND squeeze momentum < 0 AND bars since squeeze momentum > 0 > momentum lookback AND delta candle open > delta candle close AND bars since delta candle open <= delta candle close > delta lookback AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold).
Exit: long, datetime is after the optional start and before the optional end AND (close < entry price × (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR raw high >= entry price + take-profit distance).
Exit: short, datetime is after the optional start and before the optional end AND (close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR raw low <= entry price - take-profit distance).
Calculation: Take-profit distance = entry price × target fraction, rounded to the nearest price tick; use each of four independently parameterized targets, with the first three closing their specified position fractions and the fourth closing the remainder.
