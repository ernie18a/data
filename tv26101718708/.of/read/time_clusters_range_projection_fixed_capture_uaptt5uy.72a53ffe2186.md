Calculation: Slot range = For each enabled hourly or custom time slot, capture the high and low returned for the first capture-timeframe bar whose reported opening time changes while the chart bar opens within that slot's hour and at or after its start minute. With a higher capture timeframe, use the last completed bar; with a lower capture timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; with the same timeframe, use the chart bar. Keep an hourly slot's range until the optional day-of-month reset; keep a custom slot's range until the chart bar opens outside its time window or the optional day-of-month reset occurs. Use exchange-local time.
Long: For any enabled slot with a captured range, close > slot range high AND previous close <= the same slot range high.
Short: For any enabled slot with a captured range, close < slot range low AND previous close >= the same slot range low.
