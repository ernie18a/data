Calculation: Zones = At each bar whose zero-based index is a multiple of the refresh interval and exceeds the lookback, discard all unbroken zones and rebuild them from the current bar and preceding lookback bars. For each bar, collect its high, low, and close; weight each high and low by min(volume / volume SMA, 3) when both volume and its SMA are positive, otherwise by 1, and weight each close by 1.2 times that weight. Sort the unweighted prices and seed the specified number of centroids at rounded indices (k + 0.5) / number of centroids × (number of prices − 1), with k starting at 0. For the specified number of iterations, assign each price to its nearest centroid, resolving ties in favor of the earlier centroid, then update each nonempty centroid to the weighted mean of its assigned prices. Use the assignment counts from the final iteration as densities. In centroid order, create a zone when its density is at least the minimum density and its center is not within 0.7 current ATR of an already-created unbroken zone. Fix its upper and lower bounds at center ± current ATR × zone-width parameter / 2, and classify it as resistance if its center is above the current close, otherwise support. A zone remains unbroken until it triggers a breakout or is discarded at refresh; mark it broken immediately upon breakout. Process refresh before breakout detection on each bar.
Long: An unbroken resistance zone exists AND close > its upper bound + current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier.
Short: An unbroken support zone exists AND close < its lower bound − current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier.
