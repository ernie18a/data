Calculation: Zigzag pivots = On each bar, identify a high candidate when the selected price source equals its highest value over the lookback, and a low candidate when it equals its lowest value over the lookback; use close for both sources or high and low respectively. Maintain alternating high and low pivots, initially seeking a low. A candidate in the current pivot direction replaces that pivot only if strictly more extreme; a candidate in the opposite direction creates a new pivot. If both occur, process the current-direction candidate first. Store the oscillator value and bar at each pivot. A pivot is confirmed once an opposite pivot exists; alternatively use the latest unconfirmed pivot. Evaluate a pivot when it is created, replaced, or becomes confirmed, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Pivot codes = For a high pivot, price code is 2 if its price is strictly greater than the previous high pivot, otherwise 1; oscillator code is 2 if its oscillator value is strictly greater than at the previous high pivot, otherwise 1. For a low pivot, price code is -2 if its price is strictly lower than the previous low pivot, otherwise -1; oscillator code is -2 if its oscillator value is strictly lower than at the previous low pivot, otherwise -1. Before a previous same-side pivot exists, both codes equal the pivot direction: 1 for high, -1 for low.
Calculation: Oscillator = Select CCI, CMO, center of gravity, ADX, MACD line, MACD histogram, MFI, momentum, rate of change, RSI, TSI, Williams %R, Bollinger percent B, Keltner percent K, Donchian percent D, adaptive Donchian percent D, or a selected backtest data field. Use close as the source where applicable. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA length, with signal EMA length equal to the shorter length; ADX uses the longer length for directional movement and the shorter length for smoothing. Bollinger and Keltner bands use the same selected multiplier. Percent B, K, and D equal (close minus lower band) divided by (upper band minus lower band). The adaptive Donchian bands update to the current rolling high and low when high is at least the previous rolling high or low is at most the previous rolling low; otherwise retain their previous values. Replace an unavailable oscillator value with close.
Calculation: Pivot-based trend = Start with direction 1. On each bar before updating the zigzag, take the highest and lowest of the configured number of most recent pivots excluding the latest pivot, provided more pivots exist than that number plus one. Set candidate long stop to that lowest minus ATR times its multiplier and candidate short stop to that highest plus ATR times its multiplier. Change direction from 1 to -1 if the previous close is below the previous long stop, or from -1 to 1 if the previous close is above the previous short stop; otherwise retain direction. In direction 1, the long stop is the greater of its previous value and its candidate; otherwise it is its candidate. In direction -1, the short stop is the lesser of its previous value and its candidate; otherwise it is its candidate. If a prior stop is unavailable, use its candidate. Use the direction recorded at the pivot bar for pivot decisions.
Calculation: Pivot sentiment = Let P and O be the price and oscillator pivot codes and T the pivot-based trend direction at the pivot. Continuation holds when P equals O AND ((absolute P equals 2 AND P / 2 equals T) OR (absolute P equals 1 AND P differs from T)). Divergence holds when P equals 2 times O AND O equals T. Hidden divergence holds when O equals 2 times P AND P equals negative T. Sentiment bias is 1 when P and O both equal 2, -1 when both equal -2, -1 for any other positive P, and 1 for any other negative P.
Long: Pivot-based trend direction equals 1.
Short: Pivot-based trend direction equals -1.
Long: A selected zigzag pivot is evaluated AND its sentiment bias equals 1 AND (continuation OR divergence OR hidden divergence).
Short: A selected zigzag pivot is evaluated AND its sentiment bias equals -1 AND (continuation OR divergence OR hidden divergence).
