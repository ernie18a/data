Calculation: Price pivot direction = +2 for a price high above the preceding price high pivot, +1 otherwise; -2 for a price low below the preceding price low pivot, -1 otherwise. Find candidate pivots from rolling highs and lows on the chart timeframe, using close by default; retain alternating high/low pivots, replacing a pivot with a more extreme one in the same direction.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot direction = encode the oscillator value at each price pivot as +2 if above, or +1 otherwise, versus the preceding price high pivot; encode as -2 if below, or -1 otherwise, versus the preceding price low pivot. Use the selected oscillator (RSI by default) on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Trend direction = custom supertrend direction using ATR and recent price pivots: candidate lower and upper stops are the minimum and maximum of the configured prior pivots, excluding the latest, minus and plus ATR times a multiplier. In an up state, raise the lower stop to the greater of its prior value and candidate; switch down when the preceding close is below the prior lower stop. In a down state, lower the upper stop to the lesser of its prior value and candidate; switch up when the preceding close is above the prior upper stop. Direction is +1 up, -1 down.
Long: (price pivot direction < 0 AND NOT (price pivot direction = -2 AND oscillator pivot direction = -2)) AND ((price pivot direction = oscillator pivot direction AND ((ABS(price pivot direction) = 2 AND SIGN(price pivot direction) = trend direction) OR (ABS(price pivot direction) = 1 AND price pivot direction != trend direction))) OR (price pivot direction = 2 * oscillator pivot direction AND oscillator pivot direction = trend direction) OR (2 * price pivot direction = oscillator pivot direction AND price pivot direction = -trend direction))
