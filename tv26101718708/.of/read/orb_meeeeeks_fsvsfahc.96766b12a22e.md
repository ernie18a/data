Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening-range window(s), fixed when the window completes; in rolling mode, use each completed rolling window.
Calculation: Signed volume delta = volume × sign(close − open); smoothed delta = its EMA; cumulative volume delta = daily cumulative sum of signed volume delta.
Calculation: Long stop / short stop = selected stop level (opposite opening-range boundary, signal-bar low/high, entry price minus/plus ATR distance, entry price minus/plus opening-range distance, or VWAP), adjusted by the configured offset.
Calculation: Take-profit levels = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the selected distance: risk × configured R multiple, ATR × configured multiple, opening-range size × configured multiple, or configured fixed distance.
Long: Close crosses above the completed opening-range high OR (a qualifying breakout has armed a retest and low ≤ opening-range high and close > opening-range high), AND (bias RSI on the higher 5-minute timeframe, using its last completed bar, > its bullish threshold when enabled), AND (close > VWAP when enabled), AND (smoothed delta > its threshold and cumulative volume delta > its prior value when order-flow filtering is enabled), AND (entry RSI on the configured entry timeframe, using its latest available bar, > its bullish threshold when enabled), AND trading session is active, long direction is allowed, and the daily long-signal limit is not reached.
Short: Close crosses below the completed opening-range low OR (a qualifying breakout has armed a retest and high ≥ opening-range low and close < opening-range low), AND (bias RSI on the higher 5-minute timeframe, using its last completed bar, < its bearish threshold when enabled), AND (close < VWAP when enabled), AND (smoothed delta < negative its threshold and cumulative volume delta < its prior value when order-flow filtering is enabled), AND (entry RSI on the configured entry timeframe, using its latest available bar, < its bearish threshold when enabled), AND trading session is active, short direction is allowed, and the daily short-signal limit is not reached.
Exit: Long, low ≤ current stop (configured stop, moved to entry price after the first active take-profit when enabled) OR high ≥ final active take-profit OR (optional) close < opening-range high OR (optional) close < VWAP OR (optional) trading session ends OR a valid short entry signal occurs.
Exit: Short, high ≥ current stop (configured stop, moved to entry price after the first active take-profit when enabled) OR low ≤ final active take-profit OR (optional) close > opening-range low OR (optional) close > VWAP OR (optional) trading session ends OR a valid long entry signal occurs.
