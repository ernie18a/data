Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of the most recently completed configured opening window; in rolling mode, use the most recently completed rolling window. Reset the range at the start of each day; retain it while the next window forms.
Calculation: Signed volume = volume × sign(close − open); smoothed signed volume = EMA of signed volume; cumulative signed volume = daily running sum of signed volume.
Calculation: Long breakout = close > opening-range high AND previous close <= previous opening-range high. Short breakout = close < opening-range low AND previous close >= previous opening-range low.
Calculation: Long filter = (bias RSI > bullish threshold OR bias RSI filter disabled) AND (close > VWAP OR VWAP filter disabled) AND (smoothed signed volume > minimum strength AND cumulative signed volume > its previous value OR order-flow filter disabled). Short filter = (bias RSI < bearish threshold OR bias RSI filter disabled) AND (close < VWAP OR VWAP filter disabled) AND (smoothed signed volume < negative minimum strength AND cumulative signed volume < its previous value OR order-flow filter disabled). Each RSI uses its configured timeframe: if higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed bar; if lower, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; if equal, use the chart bar.
Calculation: Armed direction = the direction of the latest breakout satisfying its corresponding filter, reset at the start of each day and cleared for that direction when its signal occurs. A qualifying opposite breakout replaces it; a new opening range does not reset it.
Calculation: Long signal = direction permitted AND (trading-session filter disabled OR bar within trading session) AND (daily signal limit disabled OR no earlier long signal today) AND long filter AND (entry RSI filter disabled OR entry RSI > bullish threshold) AND ((retest disabled AND long breakout) OR (retest enabled AND armed direction = long AND low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high)). Short signal = direction permitted AND (trading-session filter disabled OR bar within trading session) AND (daily signal limit disabled OR no earlier short signal today) AND short filter AND (entry RSI filter disabled OR entry RSI < bearish threshold) AND ((retest disabled AND short breakout) OR (retest enabled AND armed direction = short AND high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low)). A breakout can arm and satisfy a retest on the same bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, select the opposite opening-range boundary, signal-bar low for long or high for short, entry price minus or plus an ATR multiple, entry price minus or plus an opening-range-width multiple, or VWAP; subtract the configured offset for long and add it for short. Initial risk = entry price minus initial stop for long, or initial stop minus entry price for short.
Calculation: Each enabled take-profit level = entry price plus for long or minus for short a configured distance, selected as a multiple of initial risk, a multiple of entry-bar ATR, a multiple of entry-bar opening-range width, or a fixed price distance. Use separately configured distances and position fractions for the first, second and third levels; if none is enabled, enable the first. Normalize enabled fractions to sum to the whole position.
Calculation: Current stop = initial stop, or entry price when break-even is enabled and the first enabled take-profit level or a higher-index enabled level has been reached. On each bar after entry, update reached take-profit levels from high for long and low for short before evaluating this stop.
Long: bias RSI > bullish threshold.
Short: bias RSI < bearish threshold AND bias RSI <= bullish threshold.
Long: long signal.
Short: short signal.
Exit: long, short signal AND short initial risk > minimum price increment.
Exit: short, long signal AND long initial risk > minimum price increment.
Exit: long, low <= current stop OR high >= an enabled take-profit level; close the corresponding take-profit fraction at each level and the remaining position at the stop or final enabled level.
Exit: short, high >= current stop OR low <= an enabled take-profit level; close the corresponding take-profit fraction at each level and the remaining position at the stop or final enabled level.
Exit: long, bar after entry AND ((opening-range exit enabled AND close < opening-range high) OR (VWAP exit enabled AND close < VWAP)).
Exit: short, bar after entry AND ((opening-range exit enabled AND close > opening-range low) OR (VWAP exit enabled AND close > VWAP)).
Exit: long, session filter enabled AND session-end exit enabled AND previous bar inside trading session AND current bar outside trading session.
Exit: short, session filter enabled AND session-end exit enabled AND previous bar inside trading session AND current bar outside trading session.
