Calculation: Trend context = EMA of close; its slope is the current EMA minus the EMA a fixed number of bars earlier. Auto selects long when close >= EMA AND slope >= 0, and selects short otherwise. The side can instead be fixed to long or short.
Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low over the same lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Tracking window = starts on a selected-side trigger when no window is active and the cooldown since the last terminal bar has elapsed. Store trigger close, trigger ATR, and a risk shelf equal to prior low minus an ATR buffer for long or prior high plus an ATR buffer for short. Track the highest high for long or lowest low for short since the trigger, including the trigger bar. A terminal event occurs when the risk shelf is touched, accepted progress is reached, or elapsed bars exceed the tracking limit, which is longer than the evaluation window. The window remains active for a hold period after the most recent terminal event; risk-shelf touches can replace an earlier accepted-progress terminal event. A new window cannot start until the active window has closed.
Calculation: Favorable travel = max(highest high since trigger minus trigger close, 0) for long, or max(trigger close minus lowest low since trigger, 0) for short, divided by trigger ATR. Close progress is calculated the same way using current close instead of the extreme.
Calculation: Progress score = 0.38 times travel score + 0.20 times efficiency score + 0.16 times volume score + 0.16 times time score + 0.10 times trigger-control score. Travel score is favorable travel divided by the accepted-travel threshold, scaled to 0–100 and capped at 100. Efficiency score is close progress divided by favorable travel, scaled to 0–100 and capped at 100, or 0 when favorable travel is 0. Volume score linearly maps current volume divided by its SMA from 0.70 times to 1.35 times a volume-ratio threshold onto 0–100, capped at both ends; use a ratio of 1 when the volume average is not positive. Time score linearly falls from 100 at the evaluation-window length to 0 at the tracking limit, capped at both ends. Trigger-control score is 76 until close loses the trigger level, 24 after a loss, and 84 after the first subsequent reclaim while the window has neither failed nor reached accepted progress.
Calculation: Accepted progress = favorable travel >= accepted-travel threshold AND progress score >= accepted-score threshold AND the risk shelf is not touched on the same bar. The accepted-travel threshold is greater than the minimum-travel threshold. The risk shelf is touched when low <= shelf for long or high >= shelf for short.
Long: Selected side = long AND no tracking window is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND ((selected trigger model = range break AND close > prior high AND previous close <= current prior high) OR (selected trigger model = impulse AND close > open AND close > previous high AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= impulse threshold AND close >= trend EMA) OR (selected trigger model = trend reclaim AND close > trend EMA AND previous close <= previous trend EMA)).
Short: Selected side = short AND no tracking window is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND ((selected trigger model = range break AND close < prior low AND previous close >= current prior low) OR (selected trigger model = impulse AND close < open AND close < previous low AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= impulse threshold AND close <= trend EMA) OR (selected trigger model = trend reclaim AND close < trend EMA AND previous close >= previous trend EMA)).
