Calculation: Prior-day high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar; prior-day midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured session, locked when that session ends and cleared at the start of each new day.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < prior-day low - ATR × sweep buffer AND close > prior-day low, OR low < locked session low - ATR × sweep buffer AND close > locked session low. Bearish sweep = high > prior-day high + ATR × sweep buffer AND close < prior-day high, OR high > locked session high + ATR × sweep buffer AND close < locked session high.
Calculation: Bar range = high - low; body = absolute value of close - open; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Divide each by bar range for its ratio, using zero when range is zero. Bullish close strength = (close - low) / range and bearish close strength = (high - close) / range, each using a neutral value when range is zero.
Calculation: Strong volume = volume / SMA(volume) >= strong-volume threshold, using a neutral ratio when the SMA is zero. Dead volume = volume / SMA(volume) < dead-volume threshold.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND range > ATR × displacement multiplier AND body ratio > body-ratio threshold AND bullish close strength > close-strength threshold. Bearish displacement = close < open AND range > ATR × displacement multiplier AND body ratio > body-ratio threshold AND bearish close strength > close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = lower-wick ratio > wick threshold AND body ratio < absorption body threshold AND volume > SMA(volume) × strong-volume multiplier AND range < SMA(range) × small-range multiplier AND bullish close strength > absorption close-strength threshold. Bearish absorption = upper-wick ratio > wick threshold AND body ratio < absorption body threshold AND volume > SMA(volume) × strong-volume multiplier AND range < SMA(range) × small-range multiplier AND bearish close strength > absorption close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bullish structure shift = close > most recently confirmed pivot high; bearish structure shift = close < most recently confirmed pivot low. A pivot is confirmed only after the same number of chart bars on each side of it.
Calculation: Bullish aggression = rounded, bounded SMA of the weighted sum of close > open, body > SMA(body), range > SMA(range), bullish close strength > close-strength threshold, bullish displacement, and strong volume. Bearish aggression uses close < open, bearish close strength and bearish displacement with the corresponding shared weights.
Calculation: Upward exhaustion = rounded, bounded SMA of the weighted sum of close rising above every close in its preceding lookback, upper-wick ratio > wick threshold, close < high - range × midpoint multiplier, the rising-close condition AND volume ratio declining on each of the last two bars, and close > prior-day high. Downward exhaustion uses falling closes, lower wick, close > low + range × midpoint multiplier, the falling-close condition with the same volume-ratio decline, and close < prior-day low. Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), using a neutral ratio when the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Compression = highest high minus lowest low over the compression lookback < SMA(range) over that lookback × lookback length × compression multiplier.
Calculation: Average daily range proxy = SMA, on chart bars, of the range of the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming. Its upper and lower levels are the open of that forming daily bar plus and minus the proxy. Near upper level = close >= upper level - ATR × proximity multiplier; near lower level = close <= lower level + ATR × proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Liquidity above = absolute difference between current and previous highs <= ATR × equality tolerance OR previous high >= prior-day high - ATR × equality tolerance. Liquidity below = absolute difference between current and previous lows <= ATR × equality tolerance OR previous low <= prior-day low + ATR × equality tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish setup score = weighted sum of bullish sweep, time inside any configured UTC trading window, strong volume, bullish aggression > bearish aggression, downward exhaustion > exhaustion threshold, close < prior-day midpoint, bullish structure shift, bullish displacement, bullish absorption, compression, close < upper level - ATR × proximity multiplier, and liquidity above. Bearish setup score uses bearish sweep, the same time and volume conditions, bearish aggression > bullish aggression, upward exhaustion > exhaustion threshold, close > prior-day midpoint, bearish structure shift, bearish displacement, bearish absorption, compression, close > lower level + ATR × proximity multiplier, and liquidity below. Corresponding bullish and bearish terms use the same weights.
Calculation: Setup state = start neutral and reset to neutral at each new day. While neutral, a bullish sweep starts a bullish setup; otherwise a bearish sweep starts a bearish setup. Record the sweep bar. A bullish setup reaches reclaim when, no later than the allowed number of bars after the sweep, close > prior-day low AND close > open; a bearish setup reaches reclaim when close < prior-day high AND close < open within that limit. After reclaim, including on the reclaim bar, bullish displacement OR bullish absorption with bullish close strength > confirmation threshold confirms the bullish setup; bearish displacement OR bearish absorption with bearish close strength > confirmation threshold confirms the bearish setup. Apply the close-strength condition to both alternatives. If still unconfirmed after the allowed number of bars, reset to neutral. A confirmed state persists until the next new day. Transitions are evaluated in the stated order, so sweep, reclaim and confirmation may occur on one bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = higher-timeframe close > midpoint of its high and low AND close > EMA(close); bearish bias uses < for both comparisons. The request uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming in real time and the final values of that same bar on historical chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish reversal score = bounded weighted sum of bullish sweep, lower-wick ratio > wick threshold, close > open, downward exhaustion > reversal exhaustion threshold, higher-timeframe bias not bearish, bullish structure shift, and bullish absorption. Bearish reversal score uses bearish sweep, upper wick, close < open, upward exhaustion, higher-timeframe bias not bullish, bearish structure shift, and bearish absorption.
Calculation: Bullish continuation score = bounded weighted sum of bullish aggression > aggression threshold, close > prior-day midpoint, strong volume, higher-timeframe bullish bias, no bearish sweep, close > prior-day high AND close > prior-day midpoint, close < upper level - ATR × proximity multiplier, and bullish displacement. Bearish continuation score uses bearish aggression, close < prior-day midpoint, strong volume, higher-timeframe bearish bias, no bullish sweep, close < prior-day low AND close < prior-day midpoint, close > lower level + ATR × proximity multiplier, and bearish displacement.
Calculation: Bullish context score = weighted sum of bullish aggression, bullish reversal score and bullish continuation score; bearish context score uses the corresponding bearish scores and the same weights.
