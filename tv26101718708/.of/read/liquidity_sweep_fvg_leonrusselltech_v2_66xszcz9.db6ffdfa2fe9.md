Calculation: Tracked swing levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks, maintained separately in chronological order; add a pivot only if its distance from the most recently stored level of the same kind exceeds an ATR multiple, and retain only a bounded number of the newest levels. Update levels before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = for any tracked swing low, current low < level AND (close-back is disabled OR current close > level), OR close-back is enabled AND previous low < level AND previous close < level AND current close > level. Its extreme is the minimum low among the triggering current and previous bars.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = for any tracked swing high, current high > level AND (close-back is disabled OR current close < level), OR close-back is enabled AND previous high > level AND previous close > level AND current close < level. Its extreme is the maximum high among the triggering current and previous bars.
Calculation: Displacement gap = for a bullish gap, current low minus the high two bars earlier, or current low minus the previous high; for a bearish gap, the low two bars earlier minus current high, or the previous low minus current high. A gap is valid when positive, at least a minimum ATR multiple, and no greater than an optional maximum ATR multiple. Prefer the two-bars-earlier gap when both are valid. The zone boundaries are the two prices defining the selected gap.
Calculation: Armed setup = on each bar store the latest sweep and reset its elapsed counter; otherwise increment that counter only on bars inside the configured session, or on every bar if the session filter is disabled. A valid displacement gap in the sweep direction arms a setup when the corresponding sweep counter is within the sweep window, including on the sweep bar. Store the gap boundaries and a stop at the sweep extreme minus an ATR buffer for a bullish setup or plus an ATR buffer for a bearish setup, using ATR on the gap bar. A new setup replaces any prior setup; process bullish before bearish if both occur. Disarm when the number of bars since arming exceeds the entry window, or when current low is below the bullish stop or current high is above the bearish stop. A successful entry also disarms the setup. Arming and entry can occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Planned entry = current close, or an adjustable fraction of the gap width from its upper boundary toward its lower boundary for a long, and from its lower boundary toward its upper boundary for a short. Risk is planned entry minus stored stop for a long, and stored stop minus planned entry for a short.
Calculation: Target = planned entry plus risk times a target multiple for a long, or minus risk times that multiple for a short. If liquidity targeting is enabled, instead use the nearest tracked swing high above a long entry or nearest tracked swing low below a short entry, provided it lies strictly beyond the fixed target; otherwise retain the fixed target. Fix the target and initial stop at entry.
Calculation: Tracked stop = initial stop until optional break-even activation; activate when high reaches entry plus a risk multiple for a long, or low reaches entry minus a risk multiple for a short, then use entry as the stop. Evaluate activation before stop and target checks, including on the entry bar. A new armed setup clears the previous tracked trade.
Calculation: Dashboard sweep = on each dashboard timeframe, compare each bar with the latest confirmed pivot low and high using an equal-sided pivot lookback; bullish when low < pivot low AND close > pivot low, bearish when high > pivot high AND close < pivot high. Retain the most recent direction through a configurable number of bars; a simultaneous bearish sweep takes precedence. Use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, and the last completed aggregated bar within the chart bar when it is lower.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, a bullish zone forms when its low > the high two bars earlier and persists until a higher-timeframe close < its lower boundary; a bearish zone forms when its high < the low two bars earlier and persists until a higher-timeframe close > its upper boundary. A newer zone of the same direction replaces the older one.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: Valid bullish displacement gap.
Short: Valid bearish displacement gap.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish gap exists.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish gap exists.
Long: Dashboard timeframe close > its simple moving average, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Dashboard timeframe close <= its simple moving average, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Dashboard sweep direction = bullish.
Short: Dashboard sweep direction = bearish.
Long: Bullish setup is armed AND low <= gap upper boundary plus a configurable fraction of gap width AND high >= gap lower boundary minus that fraction AND close > open AND (close - open) / (high - low + a small positive constant) > a momentum threshold AND close > gap lower boundary AND session filter is disabled OR satisfied AND risk > 0 AND ATR risk cap is disabled OR risk <= an ATR multiple AND trend filter is disabled OR close > chart-timeframe simple moving average.
Short: Bearish setup is armed AND low <= gap upper boundary plus a configurable fraction of gap width AND high >= gap lower boundary minus that fraction AND close < open AND (open - close) / (high - low + a small positive constant) > a momentum threshold AND close < gap upper boundary AND session filter is disabled OR satisfied AND risk > 0 AND ATR risk cap is disabled OR risk <= an ATR multiple AND trend filter is disabled OR close < chart-timeframe simple moving average.
Exit: long, low <= tracked stop OR high >= target OR time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= tracking limit.
Exit: short, high >= tracked stop OR low <= target OR time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= tracking limit.
