Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above either the high 2 bars ago or the previous bar’s high, and the resulting gap is within the configured minimum and maximum ATR thresholds.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high is below either the low 2 bars ago or the previous bar’s low, and the resulting gap is within the configured minimum and maximum ATR thresholds.
Calculation: Long entry price = configured FVG-edge price, or current close in market-on-close mode; long stop = swept swing-low extreme minus the configured ATR buffer; long target = nearest swing high above entry if it exceeds the configured fixed-R target, otherwise the fixed-R target.
Calculation: Short entry price = configured FVG-edge price, or current close in market-on-close mode; short stop = swept swing-high extreme plus the configured ATR buffer; short target = nearest swing low below entry if it is beyond the configured fixed-R target, otherwise the fixed-R target.
Long: a current or prior bar sweeps below any of the four most recent confirmed chart-timeframe swing lows and, when close-back is required, current close is above the level (direct sweep) or the prior bar’s low and close were below it and current close is above it (delayed sweep); OR a bullish FVG forms; OR a recent bullish sweep is followed within the configured sweep window by a bullish FVG, and within the configured entry window price overlaps the FVG zone expanded by the configured tolerance, current close is above the zone bottom, current close is above open, candle body/range exceeds the configured momentum threshold, current bar is in the configured session, entry price exceeds stop, risk is within the configured ATR cap if enabled, and close is above the configured moving average if the trend filter is enabled.
Short: a current or prior bar sweeps above any of the four most recent confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs and, when close-back is required, current close is below the level (direct sweep) or the prior bar’s high and close were above it and current close is below it (delayed sweep); OR a bearish FVG forms; OR a recent bearish sweep is followed within the configured sweep window by a bearish FVG, and within the configured entry window price overlaps the FVG zone expanded by the configured tolerance, current close is below the zone top, current close is below open, candle body/range exceeds the configured momentum threshold, current bar is in the configured session, stop exceeds entry price, risk is within the configured ATR cap if enabled, and close is below the configured moving average if the trend filter is enabled.
Exit: long, low is at or below the stop (or entry after the configured break-even trigger) OR high is at or above target OR the configured tracking time limit is reached.
Exit: short, high is at or above the stop (or entry after the configured break-even trigger) OR low is at or below target OR the configured tracking time limit is reached.
