Calculation: Opening range = maximum high and minimum low, or maximum and minimum candle-body extremes, over chart bars overlapping the configured opening session; reset at each session start. Its width is high minus low and its midpoint is the average of its edges. The trading window runs from the session start to the configured end or the next opening session, with session days and timezone applied.
Calculation: Break state = separately for each range edge, mark outside when the close is beyond that edge by more than the break-buffer multiple of chart ATR; a break occurs when outside changes from false to true. Count breaks per side, record the latest break bar and its high or low, and update that extreme while outside. Reset the outside state when the close no longer exceeds the buffered edge. Reset the retest and failure flags on a new break, except preserve the retest flag if a retest fired on that same bar.
Calculation: Candidates = a breakout is a new break within the per-side session break limit, unless a retest fired on that bar; a retest is a bar after the latest break and within the retest window whose low reaches the upper edge plus an ATR tolerance for an upside break, or whose high reaches the lower edge minus that tolerance for a downside break, and whose close is beyond the edge and beyond its open in the break direction; a failure is a bar after the latest break and within the failure window whose close has returned inside the broken edge by at least the configured fraction of range width. Allow one retest and one failure per break; failure takes priority and also prevents a subsequent retest. Update these flags even if the corresponding candidate type is disabled or filtered.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when the last completed higher-timeframe close exceeds its EMA and that EMA is at least its previous value; bearish when the close is below its EMA and that EMA is at most its previous value; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Bar volume and delta = sum volume and volume times signed aggression over the completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; use the chart bar itself when lower-timeframe data is unavailable or synthetic mode is selected. Signed aggression is selectable as (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), (close − open) / (high − low), or their average; for a zero-range bar use the sign of its close change from the preceding bar at that calculation timeframe. Use unit volume for missing volume.
Calculation: Width rank = fraction of prior opening-range widths, over the configured session lookback, that are strictly below the current width, each normalized by ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar. Use a neutral rank if fewer than the required prior widths are available.
Calculation: Signal score = a weighted average, normalized to the score scale, of these components: clipped volume surge relative to the chart-bar volume moving average; clipped directional close location within the bar; clipped directional displacement from the broken edge in chart ATR units; width rank for failures or its complement for breakouts and retests; higher-timeframe bias alignment, neutral bias receiving half credit; clipped directional bar delta divided by bar volume; and whether close is on the directional side of VWAP anchored at the opening-session start. Volume surge and displacement are normalized by their configured reference levels. Use neutral values for unavailable width rank or VWAP.
Calculation: Trade plan = enter at signal close. For a failure, set the raw stop beyond the failed break's recorded extreme by an ATR buffer; for a breakout or retest, select the opposite range edge, range midpoint, an ATR distance from entry, or the signal-bar extreme, applying the ATR buffer to edge, midpoint, and bar-extreme stops. Clamp directional entry-to-stop risk between configured minimum and maximum ATR distances and place the stop that risk away from entry. Select three successive targets from directional range extensions for breakouts and retests, or midpoint, opposite edge, and first opposite extension for failures; alternatively select risk-multiple targets. An extension target too close to entry or the preceding target falls back to its risk-multiple target, or to a positive risk-based increment from the preceding target if that multiple is not beyond it.
Calculation: Trade exit state = starting on the bar after entry, check the existing stop before targets; a gap through the stop fills at the open. At each successive target reached by the bar high for a long or low for a short, exit one third of the original position. Optionally move the stop to entry after the first target. If a target is reached and the same bar also touches a moved stop, exit the remainder at that stop after processing all reached targets. Optionally, after the second target, tighten the stop using close minus an ATR multiple for a long or close plus an ATR multiple for a short; the updated trail applies from the next bar. End the remaining position at the open on a new opening session or outside the trading window, or at the close when the configured maximum holding period is reached.
Calculation: First-break trail = independently for each configured ATR multiplier, enter at the close of the first break on either side of a session if that trail has no open position; initialize the long stop to signal-bar low minus the trail ATR multiple and the short stop to signal-bar high plus it. On subsequent bars tighten the long stop to the maximum of its previous value and low minus the trail ATR multiple, or the short stop to the minimum of its previous value and high plus the trail ATR multiple.
Long: Opening range is complete AND the trading window is active AND the opening session is over AND close > opening-range high.
Short: Opening range is complete AND the trading window is active AND the opening session is over AND close < opening-range low.
Long: Opening range is complete AND the trading window is active AND the opening session is over AND an upside breakout or retest candidate occurs OR a downside failure candidate occurs.
Short: Opening range is complete AND the trading window is active AND the opening session is over AND a downside breakout or retest candidate occurs OR an upside failure candidate occurs.
Long: An enabled upside breakout or retest candidate occurs OR an enabled downside failure candidate occurs, AND the signal score is at least its minimum AND the session signal count is below its cap AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish when counter-bias blocking is enabled.
Short: An enabled downside breakout or retest candidate occurs OR an enabled upside failure candidate occurs, AND the signal score is at least its minimum AND the session signal count is below its cap AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish when counter-bias blocking is enabled.
Exit: long, after entry low <= trade stop OR high >= the next trade target OR a new opening session starts OR the trading window ends OR the maximum holding period is reached OR close < first-break trail.
Exit: short, after entry high >= trade stop OR low <= the next trade target OR a new opening session starts OR the trading window ends OR the maximum holding period is reached OR close > first-break trail.
