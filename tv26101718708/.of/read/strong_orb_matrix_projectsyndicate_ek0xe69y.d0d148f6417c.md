Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low (or highest/lowest open/close, according to the configured source) among bars overlapping the configured opening-range session; signals are evaluated after that range is complete and during the configured trading window
Calculation: Signal score = 0–10 weighted mean of seven normalized factors: volume surge (bar volume / its moving-average baseline, scaled from 0.8 to 1.5× the configured high-volume multiple); directional close location within the bar; close displacement beyond the signal edge in chart-timeframe ATR units; opening-range-width percentile (percentile for fades, inverse percentile for breakouts); higher-timeframe alignment (aligned=1, neutral=0.5, opposed=0); directional bar delta / bar volume (clamped after scaling by 0.5); and whether close is on the directional side of session VWAP (yes=1, no=0; unavailable=0.5)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = last completed higher-timeframe close above its EMA with non-falling EMA for bullish bias, below its EMA with non-rising EMA for bearish bias, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Bar delta = sum of lower-timeframe bar volume times signed aggression within the chart bar; signed aggression is the mean of close-location value and body/range ratio, or the configured alternative; if intrabars are unavailable, estimate from the chart bar
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, anchored at the opening-range session start
Long: After range completion and within the trading window, a confirmed close beyond the upper edge by the configured ATR buffer on a first close-break for that side within its signal limit OR a retest within the configured bar window that touches within the configured ATR tolerance and closes above the edge with a bullish bar OR a downside break closes back inside by the configured range-width depth within its failure window; AND signal score ≥ minimum; AND signal count < session maximum; AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish when counter-bias blocking is enabled
Short: After range completion and within the trading window, a confirmed close beyond the lower edge by the configured ATR buffer on a first close-break for that side within its signal limit OR a retest within the configured bar window that touches within the configured ATR tolerance and closes below the edge with a bearish bar OR an upside break closes back inside by the configured range-width depth within its failure window; AND signal score ≥ minimum; AND signal count < session maximum; AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish when counter-bias blocking is enabled
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ third target OR configured maximum holding period reached OR trading window ends or a new opening-range session starts
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ third target OR configured maximum holding period reached OR trading window ends or a new opening-range session starts
