Calculation: Filtered washout = Let W = 100 × (highest close over a lookback − low) / highest close over that lookback, U = SMA(W) + multiplier × standard deviation(W), and R = multiplier × highest W over a separate lookback. The condition is (previous W ≥ previous U OR previous W ≥ previous R) AND W < U AND W < R AND low > previous low AND close > previous high AND (close < close at a longer lookback OR close < close at a shorter lookback) AND close > close at a strength lookback.
Calculation: Trend quality = Mean of efficiency ratio, volatility quality, structure quality, and momentum quality. Efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, capped at 1 and set to 0 when the denominator is 0. Volatility quality is clamp((ATR / SMA(ATR over twice the ATR length) − 0.5), 0, 1), with the ATR ratio set to 1 when its denominator is 0. Structure quality is clamp(((close − SMA(close)) / ATR + 2) / 4, 0, 1), with the ATR-normalized distance set to 0 when ATR is 0. Momentum quality is clamp((percentage close change over its lookback + 5) / 10, 0, 1), with the percentage change set to 0 when the earlier close is not positive.
Calculation: Signed-volume accumulation = Cumulative sum of volume when close ≥ open and negative volume otherwise.
Calculation: Armed confluence = A condition is armed when filtered washout occurred within its arm window AND the most recent bar satisfying the condition is no older than the most recent filtered washout plus that condition's allowed lookback. Use separate allowed lookbacks for the EMA/VIDYA conditions, signed-volume EMA condition, and lower-band break condition.
Calculation: Confluence score = Sum of configurable weights for armed short EMA > long EMA, armed close > VIDYA, signed-volume accumulation > its value at an earlier lookback, armed EMA of signed volume > 0, a bullish gap within the washout arm window, trend quality ≥ its threshold, and ATR percentile rank ≥ its upper threshold; subtract a configurable weight when ATR percentile rank < its lower threshold. Signed volume is volume when close ≥ open and negative volume otherwise. A bullish gap is low > high two bars earlier AND (low − that high) / close ≥ a minimum gap fraction. The EMA, VIDYA, gap, and trend-quality contributions can each be disabled. VIDYA uses absolute Chande momentum as its adaptive factor, with separate momentum and smoothing lengths, and is initialized at close.
Calculation: Entry tier = On each enabled evaluation timeframe, use the last completed bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe and the last available completed intrabar when it is lower. If the optional washout gate passes and confluence score ≥ the base threshold, assign the base tier; assign the scale tier instead when score ≥ the higher scale threshold; assign the full tier instead when score ≥ the scale threshold AND ADX < its threshold. Otherwise assign zero. If the lower-band break is armed and its optional anti-signal is enabled, reduce any tier above base by one. If optional scale suppression is enabled, replace the resulting scale tier with zero. If optional trend-quality exclusion is enabled and trend quality ≥ its lower exclusion bound AND < its upper exclusion bound, replace the tier with zero. The exclusion bounds may differ across evaluation timeframes.
Calculation: Volatility exhaustion = Let V = 100 × (lowest close over a lookback − high) / lowest close over that lookback, L = SMA(V) − multiplier × standard deviation(V), and R = multiplier × lowest V over a separate lookback. The condition is (previous V ≤ previous L OR previous V ≤ previous R) AND V > L AND V > R.
Calculation: Range position = (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over a lookback, or 0.5 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Momentum bias = Normalize close minus close at a momentum lookback by EMA(high − low) over that lookback. Separately sum positive and absolute negative normalized values over a bias lookback, smooth each with a Hull moving average, and floor each at zero. The bound is EMA of their mean plus a multiplier times the standard deviation of that mean.
Calculation: Trend-panel score = For each evaluation timeframe, use its last completed bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available completed intrabar when lower. Add a trend component of +1 when chart close > both the evaluation-timeframe EMA and session VWAP, −1 when chart close < both, otherwise 0; a momentum component of +0.5 when the evaluation-timeframe close change over its lookback > 0, −0.5 when < 0, otherwise 0; and a volatility component of +0.5 when evaluation-timeframe ATR > its SMA, otherwise 0 or optionally −0.5.
Calculation: Sweep levels = On the chart timeframe, support is the nearest of the current session low and lower Bollinger band that is below close; resistance is the nearest of the current session high and upper Bollinger band that is above close. Arm support when support − low ≥ an ATR multiple AND (open > support OR close > support); arm resistance when high − resistance ≥ an ATR multiple AND (open < resistance OR close < resistance). Preserve each armed level until it fires or its reclaim window expires. Volume confirmation is current OR previous volume ≥ a multiple of its same-bar volume SMA.
Long: Filtered washout on the chart timeframe.
Long: Chart low ≤ lower Bollinger band AND chart close > lower Bollinger band.
Long: An enabled entry tier > 0 AND differs from its previous chart-bar value, after a configurable chart-bar delay; a later tier change replaces any pending delayed signal.
Long: Within the re-entry watch window after a full exit, no new entry tier change occurs AND filtered washout is armed on any evaluation timeframe AND an enabled entry tier ≥ base.
Long: Range position crosses above its lower threshold.
Long: Negative momentum bias turns down AND is above its bound AND above positive momentum bias.
Long: Short EMA crosses above the Bollinger basis AND close > Bollinger basis AND short SMA of midpoint price minus longer SMA of midpoint price ≥ 0 AND that difference > its previous value.
Long: Armed support has not expired AND close > its stored level AND close > open AND volume confirmation holds.
Long: Trend-panel score > its positive threshold.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA on an evaluation timeframe, using the last completed bar when higher than the chart timeframe and the last available completed intrabar when lower.
Long: Chart short EMA > chart long EMA AND short EMA > its value at an earlier lookback.
Short: Positive momentum bias turns down AND is above its bound AND above negative momentum bias.
Short: Short EMA crosses below the Bollinger basis AND close < Bollinger basis AND short SMA of midpoint price minus longer SMA of midpoint price < 0 AND that difference < its previous value.
Short: Armed resistance has not expired AND close < its stored level AND close < open AND volume confirmation holds.
Short: Trend-panel score < its negative threshold.
Short: Shorter EMA ≤ longer EMA on an evaluation timeframe, using the last completed bar when higher than the chart timeframe and the last available completed intrabar when lower.
Short: Chart short EMA ≤ chart long EMA AND short EMA < its value at an earlier lookback.
Exit: long, close ≤ lower Bollinger band on the chart timeframe OR volatility exhaustion on the chart timeframe OR range position crosses below its upper threshold on the chart timeframe OR previous chart trend quality > its upper threshold AND current chart trend quality < its lower threshold.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop fraction) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + target fraction) OR elapsed evaluation bars ≥ timeout; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, the stop takes precedence.
