Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = calculate on completed bars of the specified higher timeframe, merge onto chart bars without lookahead, then use the previous chart bar's merged value. Use one higher timeframe for ALMA and both linear regressions and another for DEMA and stochastic.
Calculation: Signal lines = ALMA of higher-timeframe close, shorter-lookback linear regression of higher-timeframe close, and longer-lookback linear regression of higher-timeframe close. Trend line = DEMA of higher-timeframe close. EMA and the shorter- and longer-lookback SMAs use chart-timeframe close.
Calculation: Event change = directional chart-close change from the close recorded when the first series most recently changed from the opposite side of the second series, scaled by an adjustable price normalization factor; zero if the recorded close is nonpositive or price has not moved in the event direction. Count chart bars on the current side, starting at one on the change of side and retaining the count when the series are equal. A rise or fall event requires positive configured change and rate thresholds, event change at least its direction-specific threshold, and event change divided by the count at least its direction-specific rate threshold. Calculate separate events for ALMA versus shorter linear regression and EMA versus ALMA. Initialize recorded closes and counts to zero.
Calculation: Z-score = ALMA minus its chart-bar SMA, divided by the chart-bar standard deviation of that difference over the same lookback. Record whether it has exceeded the positive threshold or fallen below the negative threshold since entry.
Calculation: Trend stochastic = stochastic of higher-timeframe close, high and low, then a chart-bar SMA; slow trend stochastic = a second chart-bar SMA of that result. Chart stochastic = stochastic of chart-timeframe close, high and low, followed by two chart-bar SMAs with the same smoothing lengths as the trend stochastic.
Calculation: Rising stochastic event = trend stochastic above its first upper threshold AND EMA > ALMA > shorter regression > longer regression OR slow trend stochastic above its second upper threshold AND EMA > ALMA AND ALMA > longer regression > DEMA AND shorter regression > longer regression OR trend stochastic above its second upper threshold AND EMA > ALMA AND ALMA > longer regression AND shorter regression > longer regression AND close > the positive close recorded at the most recent rising ALMA event. Falling stochastic event = trend stochastic below its first lower threshold AND EMA < ALMA < shorter regression < longer regression OR slow trend stochastic below its second lower threshold AND EMA < ALMA AND ALMA < longer regression < DEMA AND shorter regression < longer regression OR trend stochastic below its second lower threshold AND EMA < ALMA AND ALMA < longer regression AND shorter regression < longer regression AND close < the positive close recorded at the most recent falling ALMA event.
Calculation: Long pullback = since entry or the latest long pyramid, chart stochastic below its lower threshold AND either a falling ALMA event or falling stochastic event has occurred. Short pullback = since entry or the latest short pyramid, chart stochastic above its upper threshold AND either a rising ALMA event or rising stochastic event has occurred. Last level = entry close initially, then the close of the latest pyramid. A new impulse wave begins whenever the sign of shorter regression minus longer regression changes, including a change to or from equality.
Long: Shorter SMA crosses above longer SMA.
Short: Shorter SMA crosses below longer SMA.
Long: No position AND shorter SMA crosses above longer SMA AND ((mode permits trend entries AND close > DEMA) OR (nascent long entries enabled AND ALMA > longer regression AND shorter regression > longer regression AND longer regression < DEMA)).
Short: No position AND shorter SMA crosses below longer SMA AND ((mode permits trend entries AND close < DEMA) OR (nascent short entries enabled AND ALMA < longer regression AND shorter regression < longer regression AND longer regression > DEMA)).
Long: Long position AND pyramiding enabled AND ((shorter SMA crosses above longer SMA AND close > DEMA AND ALMA > DEMA AND shorter regression > DEMA AND close > last level AND long pullback) OR (last level - close > positive pyramid span AND close > entry close AND (falling ALMA event OR falling EMA event OR falling stochastic event)) OR (last level - close > positive pyramid span AND close < entry close AND falling ALMA event AND falling stochastic event AND long accumulation enabled AND impulse wave changed since the last pyramid)).
Short: Short position AND pyramiding enabled AND ((shorter SMA crosses below longer SMA AND close < DEMA AND ALMA < DEMA AND shorter regression < DEMA AND close < last level AND short pullback) OR (close - last level > positive pyramid span AND close < entry close AND (rising ALMA event OR rising EMA event OR rising stochastic event)) OR (close - last level > positive pyramid span AND close > entry close AND rising ALMA event AND rising stochastic event AND short accumulation enabled AND impulse wave changed since the last pyramid)).
Exit: long, close - entry close >= minimum profit AND open profit per unit >= minimum profit AND ((natural exit enabled AND ALMA < shorter regression) OR (signal exit enabled AND ALMA > shorter regression AND rising ALMA event AND positive Z-score threshold reached since entry) OR (price event exit enabled AND rising EMA event AND EMA > ALMA > shorter regression AND ALMA > DEMA AND shorter regression > DEMA) OR (stochastic exit enabled AND rising stochastic event)).
Exit: short, entry close - close >= minimum profit AND open profit per unit >= minimum profit AND ((natural exit enabled AND ALMA > shorter regression) OR (signal exit enabled AND ALMA < shorter regression AND falling ALMA event AND negative Z-score threshold reached since entry) OR (price event exit enabled AND falling EMA event AND EMA < ALMA < shorter regression AND ALMA < DEMA AND shorter regression < DEMA) OR (stochastic exit enabled AND falling stochastic event)).
Exit: long, flip-flop mode AND close < entry close AND falling EMA event AND EMA < ALMA < shorter regression AND ALMA < DEMA AND shorter regression < DEMA AND longer regression < DEMA AND ((nascent entry AND close has been above DEMA since entry) OR (nascent entry AND ALMA < longer regression AND shorter regression < longer regression AND falling ALMA event AND long accumulation disabled) OR (non-nascent entry above DEMA AND long-only mode disabled)).
Exit: short, flip-flop mode AND close > entry close AND rising EMA event AND EMA > ALMA > shorter regression AND ALMA > DEMA AND shorter regression > DEMA AND longer regression > DEMA AND ((nascent entry AND close has been below DEMA since entry) OR (nascent entry AND ALMA > longer regression AND shorter regression > longer regression AND rising ALMA event AND short accumulation disabled) OR (non-nascent entry below DEMA AND short-only mode disabled)).
Exit: long, bale-out enabled AND open profit per unit > minimum profit multiplied by the bale-out factor AND close > DEMA AND rising stochastic event AND ALMA > longer regression AND shorter regression > longer regression AND longer regression > DEMA.
Exit: short, bale-out enabled AND open profit per unit > minimum profit multiplied by the bale-out factor AND close < DEMA AND falling EMA event AND EMA < ALMA < shorter regression AND ALMA < DEMA AND shorter regression < DEMA AND longer regression < DEMA.
Exit: long, positive stop offset AND low <= entry-bar low - stop offset.
Exit: short, positive stop offset AND high >= entry-bar high + stop offset.
