Calculation: Let s be +1 for bullish and -1 for bearish. The upper and lower structure edges are the highest high and lowest low over the same lookback, excluding the current bar; structure height is upper edge minus lower edge. The breakout level L is the upper edge for s = +1 and the lower edge for s = -1. Use ATR, falling back to true range when ATR is unavailable; use a positive denominator floor for ATR and bar range.
Calculation: N(x,a,b) = 100 × clamp((x − a)/(b − a), 0, 1), or 0 if a = b. Relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Body share is abs(close − open)/(high − low). Directional close location is (close − low)/(high − low) for s = +1 and (high − close)/(high − low) for s = -1.
Calculation: Trend score = 36 if s × (close − slow EMA) >= 0, otherwise 12, plus 34 if s × (fast EMA − slow EMA) >= 0, otherwise 0, plus 0.30 × N(abs(slow EMA − slow EMA at the slope lookback)/ATR, 0.01, 0.28) if s × (slow EMA − slow EMA at the slope lookback) >= 0, otherwise 0. The fast EMA has a shorter period than the slow EMA; an unavailable past slow EMA is replaced by its current value.
Calculation: Break quality = clamp(0.20 × N(s × (close − L)/ATR, break buffer, break buffer + 0.90) + 0.18 × N(body share, 0.28, 0.82) + 0.18 × N(directional close location, 0.55, 0.95) + 0.12 × N(relative volume, 0.75, full-volume threshold) + 0.18 × trend score + 0.14 × structure score, 0, 100). Structure score = N(structure height/ATR, minimum structure height, minimum structure height + 1.80) when structure height/ATR <= (minimum structure height + maximum structure height)/2; otherwise it is 100 − N(structure height/ATR, maximum structure height − 2.25, maximum structure height).
Calculation: A qualified breakout requires structure height/ATR >= the minimum structure height AND <= the maximum structure height AND at least the breakout cooldown bars since the preceding qualified breakout, if any, AND s × (close − L) > ATR × break buffer AND break quality >= the minimum break quality. If both directions qualify, select the higher break quality, favoring bullish on a tie. Each qualified breakout replaces the active plan and stores its side, bar, level, close, ATR, structure height, and break quality.
Calculation: For the active plan, best progress is the maximum of zero and s × (favorable intrabar extreme − breakout close)/stored ATR since the breakout, including its bar; deepest retest is the maximum of zero and s × (breakout level − adverse intrabar extreme)/stored ATR over the same bars. Favorable extreme is high for bullish and low for bearish; adverse extreme is low for bullish and high for bearish. Follow closes count subsequent completed bars whose close satisfies s × (close − breakout close) > 0. Failure rail = breakout level − s × stored ATR × failure distance. Target = breakout close + s × max(stored structure height × target multiple, stored ATR × required progress × 1.50).
Calculation: Follow-through score = clamp(0.24 × stored break quality + 0.26 × N(best progress, 0, required progress × 1.35) + 0.16 × N(follow closes, 0, required follow closes) + 0.18 × (100 − N(deepest retest, 0, maximum retest depth)) + 0.08 × room score + 0.08 × N(current relative volume, 0.75, full-volume threshold), 0, 100). Room score is 100 if s × (favorable intrabar extreme − target) >= 0; otherwise it is N(s × (target − close)/stored ATR, 0, required progress × 2).
