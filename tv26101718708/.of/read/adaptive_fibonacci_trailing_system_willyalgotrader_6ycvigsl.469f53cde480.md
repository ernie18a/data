Calculation: Warm-up = the chart bar index is at least the greater of a minimum warm-up length, a multiple of the pivot lookback, and a multiple of the ADX lookback.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by the longer-lookback SMA of that ATR; use a neutral ratio if the denominator is zero or unavailable. Volume ratio = volume divided by its SMA when both are positive; otherwise use a neutral ratio.
Calculation: Confirmed swings = after warm-up, identify pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lookbacks. Score each pivot as its absolute distance from the latest accepted opposing swing divided by ATR at the pivot, multiplied by the greater of a volume-ratio floor and the volume ratio at the pivot; if current volume or its SMA is not positive, use a neutral volume factor. If no opposing swing exists, use the pivot ATR as the distance; if pivot ATR is zero, use a zero score. Accept pivots whose score is at least the minimum swing score, process highs before lows, and retain only the most recent configured number of accepted swings.
Calculation: Swing range = the latest retained swing high minus the latest retained swing low, provided both exist and the high exceeds the low. The midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Regime = initially ranging. After warm-up, classify as volatile when volatility ratio reaches its upper threshold; remain volatile until it falls below a lower exit threshold, then classify as trending if ADX reaches its trend-entry threshold and ranging otherwise. Outside the volatile regime, enter trending when ADX reaches its trend-entry threshold; remain trending until ADX falls below a lower trend-exit threshold, then classify as ranging.
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute close minus open divided by high minus low; use zero when the range is zero. The regime gate passes in a trending regime, passes in a volatile regime when body ratio reaches its minimum, and passes in a ranging regime when ADX reaches its ranging-breakout threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive trail = choose a retracement parameter: tight, intermediate, or wide; in adaptive mode choose wide for trending, intermediate for volatile, and tight for ranging. Multiply the swing range by one minus the retracement and by the inverse volatility ratio bounded between its lower and upper limits. Add that distance to the swing low for the long candidate and subtract it from the swing high for the short candidate, bounding both candidates to the swing range. On the first valid warmed-up bar, initialize direction long if close is above the midpoint and short otherwise, and set the stop to the corresponding candidate. On subsequent bars ratchet a long stop upward to the greater of its previous value and the long candidate, or a short stop downward to the lesser of its previous value and the short candidate. When close is on the wrong side of the ratcheted stop, increment a consecutive wrong-side counter; otherwise reset it. Flip direction and replace the stop with the opposite candidate when close is on the wrong side AND either body ratio reaches its minimum AND penetration beyond the stop exceeds a multiple of ATR AND the regime gate passes, OR the enabled consecutive wrong-side limit is reached. Reset the counter on a flip. Initialization is also a direction change.
Calculation: Confidence for each direction = a weighted sum of six normalized factors. Structure is the latest accepted low's score for long or high's score for short, divided by a scale and bounded to the unit interval; use zero if unavailable. Regime alignment depends on regime, whether trail direction matches, whether close is on the matching side of a longer-lookback close SMA, signal mode, and cross strictness: in a trend use the maximum score for aligned trail, a strictness-dependent intermediate score for an unaligned cross, or a low score for an unaligned trail; in a volatile regime use higher or lower scores according to SMA alignment; in a range use a bounded strictness-dependent cross score increased by SMA alignment, or a higher or lower trail score according to SMA alignment. ADX strength is ADX divided by a scale and bounded to the unit interval. Volume is neutral when unavailable; otherwise it rises linearly with volume ratio to a maximum, stays at the maximum through a higher spike threshold, then declines linearly to a positive floor. Volatility favorability rises linearly from zero at a low volatility ratio to a maximum at the neutral ratio, then falls linearly to zero at a higher ratio, bounded to the unit interval. Confluence is one minus the absolute distance between the longer-lookback close SMA and the midpoint in cross mode or the corresponding current trail candidate in trail mode, divided by an ATR multiple, bounded to the unit interval; use a neutral value if unavailable. Apply configured factor weights and scale the sum to a percentage.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry close, freeze risk distance as risk-management ATR times its stop multiplier. Set the initial stop one risk distance below long entry or above short entry; set three targets at independently configured risk multiples above long entry or below short entry. Test the stop and targets starting on the next bar. A stop touch takes precedence over all target touches on the same bar. If break-even is enabled, the first target touch without a stop touch moves the stop to entry starting on the next bar. Process closure before evaluating new entries, allowing a new entry on the closure bar.
Long: A confirmed swing low score is at least the minimum swing score.
Short: A confirmed swing high score is at least the minimum swing score.
Long: Warm-up is complete AND no managed position remains open after exit processing AND confidence filtering is disabled OR confidence for long is at least its threshold AND either trail-flip mode is selected AND adaptive trail direction changes to long, OR midpoint-cross mode is selected AND the current and previous midpoints exist AND close is above the current midpoint AND previous close is at or below the current midpoint AND the selected cross filter passes. The cross filter passes unconditionally in unfiltered mode; in relaxed mode it requires body ratio at least its minimum AND close minus midpoint greater than an ATR multiple; in strict mode it additionally requires the regime gate.
Short: Warm-up is complete AND no managed position remains open after exit processing AND confidence filtering is disabled OR confidence for short is at least its threshold AND either trail-flip mode is selected AND adaptive trail direction changes to short, OR midpoint-cross mode is selected AND the current and previous midpoints exist AND close is below the current midpoint AND previous close is at or above the current midpoint AND the selected cross filter passes. The cross filter passes unconditionally in unfiltered mode; in relaxed mode it requires body ratio at least its minimum AND midpoint minus close greater than an ATR multiple; in strict mode it additionally requires the regime gate.
Exit: long, low is at or below the active stop OR high is at or above the third target AND low is above the active stop.
Exit: short, high is at or above the active stop OR low is at or below the third target AND high is below the active stop.
Exit: long, high first reaches the first or second target AND low is above the active stop; each such target is a partial exit when the scale-out model is selected.
Exit: short, low first reaches the first or second target AND high is below the active stop; each such target is a partial exit when the scale-out model is selected.
