Calculation: Active value shelves = On each refresh using completed chart-timeframe bars, divide the rolling lookback range from lowest low to highest high into equal price bins; allocate each bar’s volume equally among bins touched by its low–high range; repeatedly select the highest-volume bin that meets the minimum ratio to the maximum bin volume, record its center price, and zero it and bins within the minimum ATR separation. Merge each selected price with an existing nonbroken shelf within that separation by confidence-weighted averaging and increase its confidence; otherwise add a new Held shelf in creation order. On each completed chart bar, mark a Held shelf Broken when the required number of latest closes are beyond its price by the ATR break distance adjusted for shelf confidence and age. Mark a Broken shelf Reclaimed when enough closes in the reclaim window are within 0.5 ATR of its price; return a Reclaimed shelf to Held after its stabilization age while the close remains in that band. Expire stale Broken shelves and enforce the shelf-count cap. Active shelves are Held or Reclaimed shelves within the maximum ATR distance of the close, ordered by creation.
Long: No shelf was newly Broken or Reclaimed on this completed bar AND at least two active shelves exist AND each later-created active shelf has a higher price than the preceding active shelf.
Short: No shelf was newly Broken or Reclaimed on this completed bar AND at least two active shelves exist AND each later-created active shelf has a lower price than the preceding active shelf.
