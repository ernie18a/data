Calculation: ATM High/Low = highest high / lowest low during the selected session’s ATM range
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]
Calculation: Significant FVG = FVG gap size >= max(ATR × configured threshold, configured minimum tick-size multiple)
Calculation: Reversal stop = configured choice of sweep extreme or IFVG invalidation boundary, offset by configured buffer
Calculation: Reversal target = configured choice of opposing ATM boundary or entry price ± absolute(entry price − stop) × configured risk multiple
Calculation: Continuation stop = pullback swing extreme, offset by configured buffer
Calculation: Continuation target = entry price ± absolute(entry price − stop)
Long: Confirmed bar in the selected session’s execution window; exactly one ATM boundary is swept and low < ATM Low; before the session ends, a current-session significant bearish FVG no older than its configured maximum age inverts (close > its upper boundary and previous close <= that boundary); no opposing ATM target was reached before entry; no hard continuation switch occurred; and reversal stop < close < reversal target
Long: Confirmed bar in the selected session’s execution window after a sweep of ATM High and high reaches ATM High + configured switch distance; on a later bar, price overlaps an active bullish 1-minute FVG formed on the chart timeframe or bullish FVG from the higher timeframe’s last completed bar, formed no earlier than the sweep and within its configured maximum age; then on a later bar before the execution window ends, close > the highest high from the switch through bars before the FVG tap, with continuation stop < close < continuation target
Short: Confirmed bar in the selected session’s execution window; exactly one ATM boundary is swept and high > ATM High; before the session ends, a current-session significant bullish FVG no older than its configured maximum age inverts (close < its lower boundary and previous close >= that boundary); no opposing ATM target was reached before entry; no hard continuation switch occurred; and reversal stop > close > reversal target
Short: Confirmed bar in the selected session’s execution window after a sweep of ATM Low and low reaches ATM Low − configured switch distance; on a later bar, price overlaps an active bearish 1-minute FVG formed on the chart timeframe or bearish FVG from the higher timeframe’s last completed bar, formed no earlier than the sweep and within its configured maximum age; then on a later bar before the execution window ends, close < the lowest low from the switch through bars before the FVG tap, with continuation stop > close > continuation target
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar after entry when low <= stop or high >= target
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar after entry when high >= stop or low <= target
