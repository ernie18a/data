Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low during each enabled New York-time range window; its execution window follows that range window. Reset the session state at the first execution-window bar if its range was observed.
Calculation: Sweep = the first execution-window bar with high > session-range high or low < session-range low; if both occur on that bar, end the session setup. After a single-sided sweep, track the lowest low for a low sweep or highest high for a high sweep through the current bar.
Calculation: Structure level = at the sweep, the latest confirmed pivot high after a low sweep or pivot low after a high sweep, provided its pivot bar is no older than the maximum pivot age; otherwise use the highest high or lowest low, respectively, over a fallback lookback ending on the preceding bar. A pivot has equal numbers of bars on each side.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; bearish [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Significant gap = a chart-timeframe fair value gap whose width >= max(minimum price increment threshold, ATR multiplied by a threshold). Store at most a configured number of the most recent gaps per direction. A gap is active until its age exceeds a maximum, it is used, or a confirmed close passes its far boundary; check entry before retiring gaps and store new gaps afterward.
Calculation: Continuation gap = the most recent fair value gap of the relevant direction on either the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The chart-timeframe gap is available only when the chart has the configured shorter timeframe. Replace each stored gap when a newer gap of the same direction and timeframe appears; invalidate a bullish gap when chart close < its lower boundary and a bearish gap when chart close > its upper boundary. A gap is eligible if its source-bar opening time >= sweep-bar opening time and its age by opening time <= the maximum age.
Calculation: Continuation switch = after a high sweep, high >= session-range high + distance; after a low sweep, low <= session-range low - distance. It is available during the execution window while waiting for a reversal, while a reversal is active, or after its stop. When waiting for a reversal, first end the setup if the opposing range boundary has already been reached on the current or an earlier bar. The switch permanently replaces the reversal setup for that session, including any active reversal trade.
Calculation: Continuation tap = on a bar after the switch, the bar range overlaps an eligible, enabled continuation gap in the switch direction. Freeze the highest high or lowest low, respectively, from the switch bar through the bar before the first tap as the break level; track the lowest low or highest high, respectively, from the tap through the current bar as the pullback extreme.
Calculation: Reversal stop = the sweep extreme or the selected significant gap's lower boundary for a long and upper boundary for a short, minus a configured price buffer for a long or plus it for a short. Reversal target = the opposing session-range boundary or entry price plus the configured risk multiple times absolute entry-to-stop distance for a long, minus it for a short.
Calculation: Continuation stop = pullback extreme minus a configured price buffer for a long or plus it for a short. Continuation target = entry price plus entry-to-stop risk for a long or minus it for a short.
Long: During the execution window, the first sweep is a low sweep AND this is the sweep bar.
Short: During the execution window, the first sweep is a high sweep AND this is the sweep bar.
Long: After a low sweep while waiting for a reversal, structure has not previously been confirmed AND close > structure level.
Short: After a high sweep while waiting for a reversal, structure has not previously been confirmed AND close < structure level.
Long: Continuation switch occurs after a high sweep.
Short: Continuation switch occurs after a low sweep.
Long: During the execution window after a long continuation switch, the first continuation tap overlaps an eligible bullish gap.
Short: During the execution window after a short continuation switch, the first continuation tap overlaps an eligible bearish gap.
Long: During the execution window after a low sweep, while waiting for a reversal and before the continuation switch, current and earlier highs since the sweep are < session-range high AND the optional reversal time limit is satisfied AND close crosses above the upper boundary of an active significant bearish gap formed no earlier than the session range start and no more than the maximum gap age ago AND reversal stop < close AND reversal target > close. Use the most recently formed qualifying gap; entry price = close.
Short: During the execution window after a high sweep, while waiting for a reversal and before the continuation switch, current and earlier lows since the sweep are > session-range low AND the optional reversal time limit is satisfied AND close crosses below the lower boundary of an active significant bullish gap formed no earlier than the session range start and no more than the maximum gap age ago AND reversal stop > close AND reversal target < close. Use the most recently formed qualifying gap; entry price = close.
Long: During the execution window after a long continuation tap, bar index > tap bar index AND close > break level AND continuation stop < close; entry price = close.
Short: During the execution window after a short continuation tap, bar index > tap bar index AND close < break level AND continuation stop > close; entry price = close.
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= the entry's stop OR high >= the entry's target).
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= the entry's stop OR low <= the entry's target).
