Long: A completed higher-timeframe bearish candle has body ÷ (high − low) ≥ the body threshold and (high − low) ≥ higher-timeframe ATR × the size threshold; before a later completed higher-timeframe candle sweeps below its low and closes above it, no completed higher-timeframe candle closes below that low, and price does not exceed the maximum pullback from that low. OR an active long pattern exists and chart close is between the sweep candle low and the selected discount border, inclusive.
Short: A completed higher-timeframe bullish candle has body ÷ (high − low) ≥ the body threshold and (high − low) ≥ higher-timeframe ATR × the size threshold; before a later completed higher-timeframe candle sweeps above its high and closes below it, no completed higher-timeframe candle closes above that high, and price does not exceed the maximum pullback from that high. OR an active short pattern exists and chart close is between the selected discount border and the sweep candle high, inclusive.
Exit: long, chart high ≥ range candle high OR a completed higher-timeframe close < sweep candle low
Exit: short, chart low ≤ range candle low OR a completed higher-timeframe close > sweep candle high
