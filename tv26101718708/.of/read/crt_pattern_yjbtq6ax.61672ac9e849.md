Calculation: Range candle = a completed higher-timeframe bar with high > low, absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction, and high - low >= ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar multiplied by a minimum size parameter. Each qualifying bar starts an independent pending pattern: long if close < open, otherwise short.
Calculation: Pending pattern = a pattern tracked from its range candle. At each higher-timeframe boundary, inspect the last completed higher-timeframe bar: discard a long pattern if its close < range low, or a short pattern if its close > range high; otherwise retain it as an inside bar if its high < range high AND low > range low; otherwise confirm a long pattern if its close > range low AND low < range low, or a short pattern if its close < range high AND high > range high. After this boundary check on every chart bar, discard each still-pending long pattern if chart high > range low + (range high - range low) multiplied by the maximum pullback fraction, or each still-pending short pattern if chart low < range high - (range high - range low) multiplied by that fraction. Track multiple patterns independently.
Calculation: Active pattern = a confirmed pattern with its range and sweep bars fixed, tracked through and including the first chart bar where its target or failure condition is met. A long pattern ends if chart high >= range high OR, at a higher-timeframe boundary, the last completed higher-timeframe close < sweep low. A short pattern ends if chart low <= range low OR, at a higher-timeframe boundary, the last completed higher-timeframe close > sweep high.
Calculation: Discount border = the selected fixed level from the range midpoint, range low for a long pattern or range high for a short pattern, sweep high for a long pattern or sweep low for a short pattern, sweep open, or sweep close.
Long: A pending long pattern is confirmed on the current chart bar.
Short: A pending short pattern is confirmed on the current chart bar.
Long: An active long pattern has not reached its target or failed on the current chart bar AND sweep low <= chart close AND chart close <= discount border.
Short: An active short pattern has not reached its target or failed on the current chart bar AND discount border <= chart close AND chart close <= sweep high.
Exit: long, an active long pattern has chart high >= range high OR, at a higher-timeframe boundary, the last completed higher-timeframe close < sweep low.
Exit: short, an active short pattern has chart low <= range low OR, at a higher-timeframe boundary, the last completed higher-timeframe close > sweep high.
