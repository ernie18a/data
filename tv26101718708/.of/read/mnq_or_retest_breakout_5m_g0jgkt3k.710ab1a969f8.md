Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low of the session’s initial range-building bars, finalized before its trading window
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; zone spans from that earlier high to current low
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; zone spans from current high to that earlier low
Long: Valid session trading window AND opening range available and within its maximum size AND session trade allowance available AND bullish candle body extends above opening-range high by the minimum breakout distance AND, on a later bar, its low touches or crosses opening-range high or overlaps the latest bullish FVG zone AND, on a still later confirmed bar, close > open AND close > breakout candle body top AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average)
Short: Valid session trading window AND opening range available and within its maximum size AND session trade allowance available AND bearish candle body extends below opening-range low by the minimum breakout distance AND, on a later bar, its high touches or crosses opening-range low or overlaps the latest bearish FVG zone AND, on a still later confirmed bar, close < open AND close < breakout candle body bottom AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average)
