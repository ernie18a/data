Calculation: Opening range = the maximum high and minimum low of the designated opening bars for each configured session, finalized before that session's trading window; use the range belonging to the current trading window.
Calculation: Bullish gap = the zone from the high two bars ago to the current low when current low > high two bars ago; bearish gap = the zone from the current high to the low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago. Retain the most recent gap of each kind, reset both at each session start, and update them on every bar, including the current bar.
Calculation: Setup state = reset both directions and the session trade flag at each session start. During an eligible trading window, if a direction has no breakout and trading is permitted, a bullish bar with close > opening-range high + breakout distance establishes a long breakout and stores its close; a bearish bar with close < opening-range low - breakout distance establishes a short breakout and stores its close. A new breakout clears the opposite breakout and retest. The first qualifying retest after a breakout arms that direction and stores its bar and low for a long or high for a short. An entry clears its own breakout and retest and sets the session trade flag. Breakout and retest state otherwise persists across bars.
Calculation: Trading permitted = the chart uses the configured chart timeframe AND the bar is in a configured session trading window AND the opening range is available AND the opening-range size, rounded to the nearest price increment, < the maximum range size AND (the one-entry-per-session filter is disabled OR the session trade flag is unset).
Long: Trading permitted AND long breakout exists AND long retest is not armed AND current bar is after the long breakout bar AND (low <= opening-range high OR (a bullish gap exists AND low <= its upper boundary AND high >= its lower boundary)).
Short: Trading permitted AND short breakout exists AND short retest is not armed AND current bar is after the short breakout bar AND (high >= opening-range low OR (a bearish gap exists AND high >= its lower boundary AND low <= its upper boundary)).
Long: Trading permitted AND long retest is armed AND current bar is after the long retest bar AND close > open AND close > stored long breakout close AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA).
Short: Trading permitted AND short retest is armed AND current bar is after the short retest bar AND close < open AND close < stored short breakout close AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA).
