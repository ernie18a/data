Calculation: Directional notation = Evaluate every calculation separately for direction d = +1 for long and d = -1 for short. Directional open, close and EMA equal d times their ordinary values; directional high is high for d = +1 and -low for d = -1; directional low is low for d = +1 and -high for d = -1. Opposite direction means -d. Previous and recent conditions include the current bar unless stated otherwise. A recent condition is true when it occurred within its own validity lookback. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks and become available only upon confirmation. Boolean conditions in sums contribute their specified independently adjustable weights when true and zero otherwise. Fixed numerical constants in these calculations are adjustable parameters.
Calculation: Trend(d, timeframe) = Directional close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA. Use chart bars for local trend and two distinct higher timeframes for higher and intermediate trend, with higher timeframe > intermediate timeframe > chart timeframe. Higher-timeframe requests use the last completed higher bar on historical chart bars, except at its closing chart bar; during realtime formation they use the still-forming higher bar.
Calculation: Sweep(d) = Directional low < latest confirmed directional pivot low AND directional close > that pivot low. Structure break(d) = directional close > latest confirmed directional pivot high using a separate structure lookback. Structure direction starts neutral and is updated to d whenever structure break(d) occurs; if both directions break on one bar, the short update is last. Change of character(d) = structure break(d) AND the previous structure direction = -d. Internal shift(d) = directional close > latest confirmed directional pivot high using a separate internal lookback.
Calculation: Structure zone(d) = On each structure break(d) with an available latest opposite structure pivot, replace the active zone and direction: directional upper endpoint = directional close, lower endpoint = latest directional pivot low; zone bounds are adjustable shallow and deep retracements of that range. Zone touch(d) = active zone direction = d AND directional low <= shallow bound AND directional high >= deep bound.
Calculation: Displacement(d) = directional close > directional open AND absolute candle body > ATR times a displacement multiplier. Momentum confirmation(d) = momentum confirmation enabled AND displacement(d) is recent AND short EMA has risen directionally over a slope lookback AND directional close > directional short EMA. Weak momentum(d) = momentum confirmation enabled AND local trend(d) AND NOT momentum confirmation(d) AND absolute body < ATR times a weakness multiplier.
Calculation: Inverted gap(d) = Store the newest active gap for each direction: directional low > directional high two bars ago AND the gap size > current ATR times a gap multiplier. An opposite-direction gap is inverted when directional close exceeds its directional upper boundary; then deactivate that gap. A newer gap in the same direction replaces the older one.
Calculation: Sweep confirmation(d) = When sweep(d) occurs, arm d at that bar's directional high and disarm -d; if both sweeps occur on one bar, the short arm is last. Confirmation occurs on a later bar within a validity lookback when directional close > the armed level; then disarm. Expire the arm after the validity lookback. A new same-direction sweep resets its level and age. This calculation operates only when internal sweep confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: Acceptance(d) = Current directional close and each of the preceding closes in an acceptance lookback > the current directional short EMA. Rejection(d) = directional low < directional short EMA AND directional close > it AND the lower directional wick > ATR times a wick multiplier. Expansion(d) = local trend(d) AND absolute short-to-long EMA spread > its value a spread lookback ago. Compression = absolute short-to-long EMA spread < ATR times a compression multiplier. Near opposite liquidity(d) = directional close <= lowest directional low over a liquidity lookback plus ATR times a proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Divergence(d) = On a bar where both price and RSI confirm a directional pivot low using the same pivot lookback, the price pivot is lower and the RSI pivot is higher than their respective previous jointly confirmed pivot lows. Update both stored pivots whenever a joint confirmation occurs. Divergence is considered recent only when divergence warnings are enabled and it occurred within its validity lookback.
Calculation: Retest(d) = A directional short-EMA crossover occurred within a retest lookback AND directional low <= directional short EMA AND directional close > directional short EMA AND directional close >= directional open. A crossover requires current directional close > current directional short EMA AND previous directional close <= previous directional short EMA.
Calculation: Market matrix = On a separate higher timeframe, using the same historical and realtime bar availability as the other higher-timeframe requests, count bullish and bearish votes from four ordered EMAs: each adjacent EMA pair contributes a bullish vote if the shorter EMA is greater and a bearish vote if less; RSI above or below its midpoint; MACD above or below its signal; MACD histogram above or below zero; positive directional indicator above or below negative directional indicator; MFI above or below its midpoint; OBV rising contributes bullish, otherwise bearish; and Supertrend direction contributes bullish for its uptrend and bearish for its downtrend. Equal comparisons give neither vote except for OBV. Matrix percentage(d) = rounded 100 times directional votes divided by max(total votes, 1). Matrix support(d) = market matrix enabled AND matrix percentage(d) >= support threshold. Strong matrix trend = matrix enabled AND ADX >= strong threshold; weak matrix trend = matrix enabled AND ADX < weak threshold, where the weak threshold is below the strong threshold.
Calculation: Directional probability(d) = Clamp each directional point total at zero, then round 100 times the directional total divided by max(sum of both totals, 1). A directional total is the weighted sum of local, higher and intermediate trend; recent sweep, structure break, change of character, internal shift and displacement; momentum confirmation; recent inverted gap and structure-zone touch; acceptance, rejection, expansion and proximity to opposite liquidity. Add the compression weight only when expansion(d) is false and compression is true. Subtract weights for weak momentum(d) and recent divergence(-d). Add a time-of-day session weight selected from mutually exclusive configurable sessions, with zero outside them. Add weights for matrix support(d) and for matrix support(d) AND strong matrix trend; subtract weights for matrix support(-d) and weak matrix trend. All weights are independently adjustable, including distinct weights for compression and expansion.
Calculation: Distance acceptable = distance filter disabled OR absolute difference between close and short EMA <= ATR times a distance multiplier.
Calculation: Directional grade score(d) = Clamp to a score range the weighted sum of intermediate trend(d), recent sweep(d), any recent structure break/change of character/internal shift(d), expansion(d) OR momentum confirmation(d), acceptance(d) OR rejection(d), recent displacement(d) OR momentum confirmation(d), retest(d), matrix support(d), and matrix support(d) AND strong matrix trend; subtract weights for matrix support(-d) and weak matrix trend. If sweep is required and sweep(d) is not recent, cap the score at a configurable sweep cap. If divergence(-d) is recent or distance is unacceptable, cap it at a configurable safety cap.
Calculation: Forming grade(d) = grade engine enabled AND grade score(d) >= forming threshold AND grade score(d) < ready threshold AND grade score(d) > grade score(-d) AND NOT retest(d). Ready grade(d) = grade engine enabled AND grade score(d) >= ready threshold AND grade score(d) > grade score(-d) AND retest(d) AND (matrix support requirement disabled OR matrix support(d)).
Calculation: Scalp tier(d) = probability(d) >= scalp threshold AND probability(d) < forming probability threshold AND NOT expansion(-d) AND NOT acceptance(-d). Forming tier(d) = probability(d) >= forming probability threshold AND probability(d) < ready probability threshold AND NOT expansion(-d). Ready tier(d) = probability(d) >= ready probability threshold AND probability(d) < immediate probability threshold AND any recent structure break/change of character/internal shift(d) AND NOT acceptance(-d). Immediate tier(d) = probability(d) >= immediate probability threshold AND distance acceptable AND acceptance(d) AND recent structure break(d) AND (momentum confirmation(d) OR matrix momentum support enabled AND recent displacement(d) AND matrix support(d)) AND (final shift filter disabled OR recent inverted gap(d) OR recent sweep confirmation(d)) AND NOT recent divergence(-d) AND probability(d) > probability(-d) + probability edge. The four probability thresholds are ordered from lowest to highest.
Calculation: Watch grade(d) = NOT ready grade(d) AND NOT forming grade(d) AND probability(d) >= watch threshold AND probability(d) > probability(-d) + watch edge AND any recent structure break/change of character/internal shift(d) AND acceptance(d) AND (momentum confirmation(d) OR expansion(d) OR matrix support(d)) AND NOT recent divergence(-d). Lower watch grade(d) = NOT watch grade(d) AND NOT ready grade(d) AND NOT forming grade(d) AND probability(d) >= lower watch threshold AND probability(d) > probability(-d) AND NOT acceptance(-d) AND NOT recent divergence(-d), with lower watch threshold < watch threshold.
Long: Scalp tier(+1) AND NOT scalp tier(+1) on the previous bar OR forming tier(+1) AND NOT forming tier(+1) on the previous bar OR ready tier(+1) AND NOT ready tier(+1) on the previous bar OR immediate tier(+1) AND NOT immediate tier(+1) on the previous bar OR forming grade(+1) AND NOT forming grade(+1) on the previous bar OR ready grade(+1) AND NOT ready grade(+1) on the previous bar.
Short: Scalp tier(-1) AND NOT scalp tier(-1) on the previous bar OR forming tier(-1) AND NOT forming tier(-1) on the previous bar OR ready tier(-1) AND NOT ready tier(-1) on the previous bar OR immediate tier(-1) AND NOT immediate tier(-1) on the previous bar OR forming grade(-1) AND NOT forming grade(-1) on the previous bar OR ready grade(-1) AND NOT ready grade(-1) on the previous bar.
Long: Local trend(+1) OR higher trend(+1) AND intermediate trend(+1) AND (local trend(+1) OR local trend(-1)) AND local trend(+1) OR recent sweep(+1) OR recent structure break(+1) OR recent change of character(+1) OR recent structure-zone touch(+1) OR acceptance(+1) OR rejection(+1) OR expansion(+1) OR near opposite liquidity(+1) OR momentum confirmation(+1) OR weak momentum(+1) OR recent divergence(+1) OR matrix support(+1) OR probability(+1) > probability(-1) OR grade score(+1) > grade score(-1) OR watch grade(+1) OR lower watch grade(+1).
Short: Local trend(-1) OR higher trend(-1) AND intermediate trend(-1) AND (local trend(+1) OR local trend(-1)) AND local trend(-1) OR recent sweep(-1) OR recent structure break(-1) OR recent change of character(-1) OR recent structure-zone touch(-1) OR acceptance(-1) OR rejection(-1) OR expansion(-1) OR near opposite liquidity(-1) OR momentum confirmation(-1) OR weak momentum(-1) OR recent divergence(-1) OR matrix support(-1) OR probability(-1) > probability(+1) OR grade score(-1) > grade score(+1) OR watch grade(-1) OR lower watch grade(-1).
Calculation: Structure strength(d) = Count recent structure break(d), recent change of character(d), recent internal shift(d), local trend(d), and recent displacement(d). Classify using configurable strong and weaker count thresholds, with strong > weaker; when both directions qualify, select strong long, strong short, weak long, then weak short in that order.
Long: Structure strength(+1) >= its applicable threshold AND its priority-selected classification is long.
Short: Structure strength(-1) >= its applicable threshold AND its priority-selected classification is short.
