Calculation: Indicator set = enabled MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI of close, SMA-smoothed stochastic of high/low/close, CCI of close, momentum of close, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin money flow, money flow index of close, and optionally an external indicator computable from the permitted data; volume-weighted MACD = short-period VWMA of close minus long-period VWMA of close; Chaikin money flow = SMA of volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) divided by SMA of volume.
Calculation: Pivot = confirmed pivot high or low of close, or respectively high or low, using equal left and right pivot periods; consider only the most recent configured number of confirmed pivots whose distance from the current bar is greater than the minimum and no greater than the maximum lookback.
Calculation: Low divergence for an indicator = the indicator at the selected endpoint is above its value at a pivot low AND the selected low-price source at the endpoint is below its value at that pivot, OR the indicator at the endpoint is below its value at a pivot low AND the selected low-price source at the endpoint is above its value at that pivot; in either case, every intervening indicator value and close must be at or above its respective straight line joining the endpoint and pivot values.
Calculation: High divergence for an indicator = the indicator at the selected endpoint is below its value at a pivot high AND the selected high-price source at the endpoint is above its value at that pivot, OR the indicator at the endpoint is above its value at a pivot high AND the selected high-price source at the endpoint is below its value at that pivot; in either case, every intervening indicator value and close must be at or below its respective straight line joining the endpoint and pivot values.
Calculation: Divergence count = number of enabled indicator and divergence-type pairs detected on the current bar, counting at most one pivot per pair and including both directions; enable regular and hidden types independently. The endpoint is the current bar when confirmation is disabled and the previous bar when confirmation is enabled. With confirmation enabled, a low divergence additionally requires the current indicator value > its previous value OR current close > previous close; a high divergence additionally requires the current indicator value < its previous value OR current close < previous close.
Long: At least one enabled low divergence is detected AND divergence count >= the configured minimum.
Short: At least one enabled high divergence is detected AND divergence count >= the configured minimum.
