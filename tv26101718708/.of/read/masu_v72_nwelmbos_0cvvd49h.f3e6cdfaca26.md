Calculation: Gaussian endpoint = the weighted mean of the source over a lookback, with weight exp(-0.5 × (bar offset / bandwidth)²) and missing source values replaced by zero. Calculate a ribbon with progressively increasing bandwidths and a common lookback; optionally multiply all bandwidths by a bounded ratio of ATR/close to its moving average. The ribbon direction is determined by the ordering of adjacent lines, either requiring all pairs, requiring a threshold count, or requiring the shortest-bandwidth line to be on the directional side of the longest-bandwidth line while their absolute separation exceeds its value a specified number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Directional order-flow test = close is on the directional side of daily-reset volume-weighted typical price AND either cumulative signed candle volume is on the directional side of its moving average and moves in that direction while close moves oppositely, OR bullish-candle volume divided by max(bearish-candle volume, 1) is beyond a directional threshold. Signed candle volume is volume for close > open, -volume for close < open, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Bullish structure events = a bullish candle after a bearish candle with upper wick shorter than a specified fraction of its range; OR low above high two bars earlier by more than an ATR multiple; OR low below the preceding rolling low with close above both open and that rolling low; OR close above the latest confirmed pivot high with previous close at or below that same pivot. A pivot uses equal numbers of bars on either side and becomes available only after its right-side bars complete. Bearish structure events mirror these comparisons using a bearish candle, lower wick, downward gap, rolling high, and pivot low.
Calculation: Online directional probability = sigmoid of a bounded linear combination of normalized RSI, rate of change, percentage distance from a long-term EMA, ADX, percentage slope of the shortest-bandwidth ribbon line, difference between bullish and bearish ordered-pair counts, and volume divided by its moving average. On each completed bar, compare close with close a target lookback earlier; use that binary target and the features from that earlier bar to update each weight by weight decay plus learning rate × prediction error × earlier feature, and update the bias similarly. Predict with the updated weights and current features. Track the correctness of the probability recorded at the target lookback against the current target; use cumulative accuracy initially and exponentially smoothed correctness thereafter. The optional confidence multiplier is a bounded linear transformation of accuracy.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = weighted sum of the following tests: higher-timeframe short EMA and close both on the directional side of its longer EMA and short EMA respectively; directional ribbon; directional order-flow test OR close on the directional side of daily-reset volume-weighted typical price; a directional structure event; ADX above its threshold; online directional probability above its bullish threshold or below its bearish threshold, weighted by the optional confidence multiplier; and shortest-bandwidth ribbon slope in the direction. Disabled higher-timeframe, ribbon, order-flow, or structure filters contribute their full respective weights; a disabled probability filter contributes zero. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.

Short: bearish structure event when its corresponding marking is enabled.

Short: directional order-flow test is bearish when order-flow analysis is enabled.

Short: online directional probability < bearish soft-confidence threshold when the probability filter is enabled.
Long: bullish ribbon direction when ribbon display is enabled.
Short: bearish ribbon direction when ribbon display is enabled AND bullish ribbon direction is false.
