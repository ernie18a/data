Calculation: Four close-based moving averages use the same selected type (SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, RMA, VWMA, SWMA, ALMA, or VWAP) with shortest, shorter, longer, and longest lookbacks; SWMA and VWAP ignore the lookback.
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR on the most recent long or short entry-signal bar.
Calculation: Long stop = on a long entry-signal bar, signal-bar close minus either a configured percentage of that close or a configured multiple of entry ATR; subsequently, the maximum of the previous long stop and the same formula applied to the entry price, or to the current high if trailing is enabled.
Calculation: Short stop = on a short entry-signal bar, signal-bar close plus either a configured percentage of that close or a configured multiple of entry ATR; subsequently, the minimum of the previous short stop and the same formula applied to the entry price, or to the current low if trailing is enabled.
Calculation: Long target = entry-signal close plus either a configured percentage of that close, a configured multiple of entry ATR, or a configured risk-reward multiple of the distance from that close to the initial long stop. Hold the target fixed until the take-profit order executes.
Calculation: Short target = entry-signal close minus either a configured percentage of that close, a configured multiple of entry ATR, or a configured short risk-reward multiple of the distance from that close to the hypothetical long stop calculated using the long stop parameters. Hold the target fixed until the take-profit order executes.
Long: Shortest moving average >= shorter moving average AND shorter moving average >= longer moving average AND longer moving average >= longest moving average.
Long: Long entries enabled AND datetime within the configured trading interval AND position side is not long AND shortest moving average >= shorter moving average AND shorter moving average >= longer moving average AND longer moving average >= longest moving average AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR >= SMA of ATR).
Short: Short entries enabled AND datetime within the configured trading interval AND position side is not short AND shortest moving average <= shorter moving average AND shorter moving average <= longer moving average AND longer moving average <= longest moving average AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR >= SMA of ATR).
Exit: long, long entries enabled AND datetime within the configured trading interval AND previous bar's shortest moving average >= shorter moving average AND previous bar's shorter moving average >= longer moving average AND previous bar's longer moving average >= longest moving average AND (shortest moving average < shorter moving average OR shorter moving average < longer moving average OR longer moving average < longest moving average).
Exit: short, short entries enabled AND datetime within the configured trading interval AND previous bar's shortest moving average <= shorter moving average AND previous bar's shorter moving average <= longer moving average AND previous bar's longer moving average <= longest moving average AND (shortest moving average > shorter moving average OR shorter moving average > longer moving average OR longer moving average > longest moving average).
Exit: long, stop and target enabled AND low <= active long stop; exit the full position.
Exit: long, stop and target enabled AND high >= active long target; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: short, stop and target enabled AND high >= active short stop; exit the full position.
Exit: short, stop and target enabled AND low <= active short target; exit the configured fraction of the position.
