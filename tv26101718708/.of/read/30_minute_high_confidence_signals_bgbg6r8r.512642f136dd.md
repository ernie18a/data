Calculation: Bullish MACD = MACD line above signal line, histogram above zero and not falling; bearish MACD = MACD line below signal line, histogram below zero and not rising
Calculation: Bullish DMI strength = ADX at or above its minimum and +DI above -DI; bearish DMI strength = ADX at or above its minimum and -DI above +DI
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average of volume

Calculation: Strong bullish candle = close above open and absolute(close - open) / (high - low) at or above its minimum; strong bearish candle is the reverse
Calculation: Primary bullish score = count of bullish EMA structure, bullish RSI, bullish MACD, bullish DMI strength, relative volume at or above its minimum, bullish higher-timeframe confirmation, and strong bullish candle; primary bearish score counts their bearish counterparts, using the same volume condition
Calculation: Breakout score = count of bullish or bearish EMA structure, momentum RSI, MACD, DMI strength, higher-timeframe confirmation, and strong candle in the corresponding direction
Calculation: Previous range = highest high or lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Flash momentum score = count of directional breakout, momentum RSI, MACD, directional DI dominance, and strong candle
Calculation: ATR-normalized move = absolute(close - open) / ATR; ATR-normalized range = (high - low) / ATR
