Calculation: Source = a selected price series derived from chart-bar OHLC.
Calculation: Trendiness = either efficiency ratio of Source, fractal trendiness, or their average. Efficiency ratio is absolute Source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar Source changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero. For fractal trendiness, divide an even lookback into two halves; calculate each half's high-low range divided by its length and the whole window's high-low range divided by its length. Fractal dimension is the logarithm of the sum of the two half-window values minus the logarithm of the whole-window value, divided by log(2), clamped to [1,2]; use 1.5 if any of those values is nonpositive. Fractal trendiness is 2 minus fractal dimension.
Calculation: Trending = ADX exceeds its fixed threshold or its rolling percentile threshold, as selected, AND trendiness exceeds its fixed threshold or its rolling percentile threshold, respectively.
Calculation: Dominant cycle = homodyne-discriminator period of chart-bar HL2: smooth HL2 with weights 4, 3, 2, 1; apply the Hilbert filter with lag coefficients 0.0962, 0.5769, -0.5769, -0.0962 at lags 0, 2, 4, 6, multiplied by 0.075 times the preceding period plus 0.54. Apply that filter to the smoothed series to get the detrended series, and to the detrended series to get Q1; I1 is the detrended series lagged three bars. Apply the same filter to I1 and Q1 to get jI and jQ. Smooth I1-jQ and Q1+jI recursively with weights 0.2 and 0.8 to get I2 and Q2. Smooth I2 times prior I2 plus Q2 times prior Q2, and I2 times prior Q2 minus Q2 times prior I2, with the same weights to get Re and Im. If both are nonzero, the new period is 2*pi/abs(atan(Im/Re)); otherwise it is the preceding period. Clamp it first to 0.67–1.5 times the preceding period and then to 6–50; recursively smooth it with weights 0.2 and 0.8. Initialize the period to 12 and missing filter and recursive-history values to zero.
Calculation: Primary ATR = either fixed-length ATR or an ATR selected from the fixed lengths 5, 8, 13, 21, 34. For adaptive length, round dominant cycle times a factor, clamp to [5,34], and select those lengths for targets <=6, <=10, <=17, <=27, and above 27, respectively.
Calculation: Self-tuned base multiplier = optionally run parallel fixed-multiplier Supertrends using Source, primary ATR, and a grid of multipliers. For each, count direction flips and flips occurring fewer than a chosen number of bars after its preceding flip; its quality is one minus the fast-flip fraction once a minimum flip count is reached. Optionally replace quality with the mean quality of the candidate and its available immediate neighbors minus a chosen multiple of their population standard deviation. Start with the middle grid candidate and switch only when another candidate's score exceeds the current score by a hysteresis threshold. Otherwise use the fixed base multiplier.
Calculation: Primary multiplier = base multiplier, optionally multiplied by a linear interpolation between calm and volatile scaling factors using the percentile rank of fixed-length ATR over its lookback, optionally multiplied by 1 plus a chop factor times (1 minus trendiness), and optionally multiplied by a path-volatility adjustment. For the latter, divide the shorter EMA of squared Source changes by the longer EMA, take its square root, and add a downside coefficient times the absolute negative part of the shorter EMA of Source changes divided by primary ATR and an upside coefficient times its positive part; use activity 1 when the longer EMA is nonpositive and normalized drift zero when ATR is nonpositive. Clamp this factor to its floor and cap, blend it with 1 using its influence weight, and clamp the resulting multiplier to its allowed range.
Calculation: Primary bands and direction = basic lower and upper bands are Source minus and plus primary multiplier times primary ATR. Initialize each trailing band to its basic band and direction to up. Subsequently replace the lower band with its basic band if that band is above the preceding lower band OR the preceding close is below the preceding lower band; otherwise retain the preceding lower band. Replace the upper band with its basic band if that band is below the preceding upper band OR the preceding close is above the preceding upper band; otherwise retain the preceding upper band. If preceding direction is up, direction becomes down when close is below the preceding lower band, otherwise it stays up; if preceding direction is down, direction becomes up when close is above the preceding upper band, otherwise it stays down.
Calculation: Secondary bands and direction = the same band and direction recursion using Source and a fixed-multiplier ATR Supertrend with a longer ATR length and larger multiplier than the primary configuration.
Long: Primary direction = up.
Short: Primary direction = down.
Long: Secondary direction changes from down to up.
Short: Secondary direction changes from up to down.
Long: Trending = true AND close > trend EMA.
Short: Trending = true AND close <= trend EMA.
