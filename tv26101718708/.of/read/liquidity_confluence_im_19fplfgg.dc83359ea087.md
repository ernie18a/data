Calculation: Rolling volume profile = Over a configurable lookback including the current bar, divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high into equal price rows. For each bar, distribute its volume equally among all rows touched by its low-to-high range. The point of control is the midpoint of the row with the greatest accumulated volume, choosing the lowest row on ties. Starting with that row, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time until its accumulated volume reaches a configurable fraction of total profile volume or no rows remain. Add the higher-volume adjacent row first, choosing the upper row on ties when available. The value-area low and high are the lower edge of the lowest included row and the upper edge of the highest included row.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = A pivot high or low using the same configurable number of bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have completed. Store the latest confirmed pivot separately for highs and lows. Reset its breach state whenever a new pivot of that kind is confirmed; mark it breached on the first subsequent bar, including the confirmation bar, whose close exceeds the swing high or falls below the swing low, respectively.
Long: FVG display enabled AND low > high two bars ago AND (value-area filter disabled OR the midpoint of low and high two bars ago is between the current value-area low and high inclusive).
Short: FVG display enabled AND high < low two bars ago AND (value-area filter disabled OR the midpoint of high and low two bars ago is between the current value-area low and high inclusive).
Long: BOS display enabled AND close > latest confirmed swing high AND that swing high has not previously been breached.
Short: BOS display enabled AND close < latest confirmed swing low AND that swing low has not previously been breached.
