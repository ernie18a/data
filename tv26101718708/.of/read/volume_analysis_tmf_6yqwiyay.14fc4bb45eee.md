Calculation: High-volume zone = a zone with the high and low of a bar whose volume exceeds a multiplier times the simple moving average of volume, including that bar. During each consecutive run of such bars, create a zone on the first bar and replace it whenever a later bar's volume exceeds the highest volume previously seen in that run; equal volume does not replace it. Keep zones from earlier runs active. Each new zone starts unfired. On replacement, remove the last active zone if any, then create the replacement. After creation or replacement on each bar, remove any active zone whose age is at least the age threshold and whose high or low is crossed by the close on that bar. Zone age is the number of bars since its creation.
Long: An active zone's age is less than the age threshold AND the zone is unfired AND the previous close <= the zone high AND the current close > the zone high; mark the zone fired.
Short: An active zone's age is less than the age threshold AND the zone is unfired AND the previous close >= the zone low AND the current close < the zone low; mark the zone fired.
