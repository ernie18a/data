Calculation: Momentum z-score = (close change minus the simple moving average of close changes) divided by the standard deviation of close changes, using the same lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low identified using equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind until replaced or broken. Reset a pivot after its corresponding market-structure break.
Calculation: Bullish market-structure break = close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high AND momentum z-score > positive threshold.
Calculation: Bearish market-structure break = close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low AND momentum z-score < negative threshold.
Calculation: Order block = on a market-structure break, use the high and low of the most recent opposite-direction candle within a lookback; if none exists, use the break bar. A bullish break creates a bullish block and a bearish break creates a bearish block. If overlap hiding is enabled, do not create the block when either boundary lies inside an existing unmitigated block. Mark a bullish block mitigated when low < its bottom and a bearish block mitigated when high > its top; perform this update before evaluating entries, including on the creation bar. Retain at most the configured number of blocks, deleting the oldest first, including mitigated blocks in the count.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus hard-stop distance until close >= entry price plus break-even trigger distance; on the first such bar initialize the dynamic stop to entry price. On each later bar satisfying the trigger, set the dynamic stop to the greater of its previous value and close minus ATR times the trail multiplier. The active stop is the greater of the hard stop and the dynamic stop when initialized. Submit the updated stop for subsequent bars.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus hard-stop distance until close <= entry price minus break-even trigger distance; on the first such bar initialize the dynamic stop to entry price. On each later bar satisfying the trigger, set the dynamic stop to the lesser of its previous value and close plus ATR times the trail multiplier. The active stop is the lesser of the hard stop and the dynamic stop when initialized. Submit the updated stop for subsequent bars.
Long: Bullish market-structure break.
Short: Bearish market-structure break.
Long: Position is flat AND an unmitigated bullish order block exists AND low <= its top AND close > its bottom.
Short: Position is flat AND an unmitigated bearish order block exists AND high >= its bottom AND close < its top.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price plus profit-target distance OR low <= active long stop.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price minus profit-target distance OR high >= active short stop.
