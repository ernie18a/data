Calculation: Unless otherwise specified, chart-timeframe indicator values and OHLCV inputs are from the preceding chart bar; the previous value of an indicator is from two chart bars ago.
Calculation: Directional movement = positive DM is high[1] - high[2] when that difference exceeds both low[2] - low[1] and zero, otherwise zero; negative DM is low[2] - low[1] when that difference exceeds both high[1] - high[2] and zero, otherwise zero. Positive and negative DI are the EMA of their respective DM divided by ATR of the same period, multiplied by 100; use zero when ATR is zero. DX is 100 times the absolute DI difference divided by their sum, or zero when the sum is zero. ADX is the EMA of DX. Use the preceding chart bar's DI and ADX; the DM inputs are already offset as specified.
Calculation: Bullish price action = (close > open AND body / range > body-fraction threshold AND close > low + range × close-location threshold) OR (lower wick >= body × long-wick multiplier AND upper wick <= body × short-wick multiplier AND range > ATR × range multiplier) OR (close > open AND close > maximum of the preceding bar's open and close AND open < minimum of the preceding bar's open and close). Body is absolute close minus open; range is high minus low; body / range is zero when range is zero; upper wick is high minus maximum of open and close; lower wick is minimum of open and close minus low.
Calculation: Bearish price action = (close < open AND body / range > body-fraction threshold AND close < high - range × close-location threshold) OR (upper wick >= body × long-wick multiplier AND lower wick <= body × short-wick multiplier AND range > ATR × range multiplier) OR (close < open AND close < minimum of the preceding bar's open and close AND open > maximum of the preceding bar's open and close). Body, range and wicks are defined as for bullish price action.
Calculation: Squeeze released = upper Bollinger Band > upper Keltner Channel AND lower Bollinger Band < lower Keltner Channel; Bollinger Bands use an SMA of close plus or minus a standard-deviation multiple, and Keltner Channels use an EMA of close plus or minus an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions among: shortest-period EMA > short-period EMA > medium-period EMA > longest-period EMA; higher-timeframe last completed close > shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA; ADX >= threshold AND positive DI > negative DI; RSI within its bullish inclusive bounds AND RSI > previous RSI; MACD line > signal line AND histogram > zero AND histogram > previous histogram; bullish price action; squeeze released AND volume > its SMA × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of true conditions among: shortest-period EMA < short-period EMA < medium-period EMA < longest-period EMA; higher-timeframe last completed close < shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA; ADX >= threshold AND negative DI > positive DI; RSI within its bearish inclusive bounds AND RSI < previous RSI; MACD line < signal line AND histogram < zero AND histogram < previous histogram; bearish price action; squeeze released AND volume > its SMA × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Initial long stop = maximum of entry-signal bar close minus a larger ATR multiple and the minimum of entry-signal bar close minus a smaller ATR multiple and the preceding-bar rolling lowest low minus an ATR padding multiple. Initial short stop = minimum of entry-signal bar close plus the larger ATR multiple and the maximum of entry-signal bar close plus the smaller ATR multiple and the preceding-bar rolling highest high plus the ATR padding multiple. ATR is measured on the preceding chart bar and both stops are fixed at entry.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry-signal bar close plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the initial stop distance multiplied by each of three increasing reward-to-risk parameters. The first and second targets each close a configurable portion; the third closes the remainder.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after the first target is reached, when trailing is enabled, start at the initial stop and update it at each chart close to the maximum of its previous value and current close minus preceding-bar ATR times a trailing multiplier for a long, or the minimum of its previous value and current close plus preceding-bar ATR times the multiplier for a short. The updated stop applies to subsequent price movement.
Long: Bullish score >= minimum score AND preceding close > longest-period EMA AND preceding close > short-period EMA AND (preceding low <= short-period EMA × touch multiplier OR absolute distance between preceding close and short-period EMA < ATR × proximity multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR preceding bar's UTC hour is within either configured session) AND ATR > ATR SMA × lower volatility multiplier AND ATR < ATR SMA × upper volatility multiplier AND position side = flat.
Short: Bearish score >= minimum score AND preceding close < longest-period EMA AND preceding close < short-period EMA AND (preceding high >= short-period EMA × touch multiplier OR absolute distance between preceding close and short-period EMA < ATR × proximity multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR preceding bar's UTC hour is within either configured session) AND ATR > ATR SMA × lower volatility multiplier AND ATR < ATR SMA × upper volatility multiplier AND position side = flat.
Long: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed close > shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA.
Short: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed close < shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA.
Long: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed RSI < lower RSI color threshold OR last completed RSI > upper RSI direction threshold.
Short: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed RSI > upper RSI color threshold OR last completed RSI < lower RSI direction threshold.
Long: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed MACD histogram > zero.
Short: On any dashboard timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, last completed MACD histogram <= zero.
Exit: long, low <= initial long stop OR high >= an unfilled profit target OR (trailing enabled AND first target reached AND low <= trailing stop).
Exit: short, high >= initial short stop OR low <= an unfilled profit target OR (trailing enabled AND first target reached AND high >= trailing stop).
