Calculation: Trend SMA = SMA of the selected price source on the selected timeframe; for a higher timeframe use its last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Active order blocks = for a bullish block, the previous bar's low-to-high range when that bar closed below its open and the current close exceeds its high; for a bearish block, the previous bar's low-to-high range when that bar closed above its open and the current close is below its low. Optionally require the current bullish or bearish body respectively to exceed an ATR multiple. On each confirmed bar, remove bullish blocks when close, or optionally low, falls below their bottom and bearish blocks when close, or optionally high, rises above their top; then add newly formed blocks and retain only the most recent configurable number per side.
Calculation: Pattern state = start with the current confirmed bar as C1; test the next bar as C2. If C2 satisfies neither sweep condition, replace C1 with C2. If C2 satisfies a sweep condition, test only the next bar as C3. After a confirmed pattern, start a new C1 on the next bar; otherwise replace C1 with C3. Record whether C1 or a valid C2 overlapped an active matching order block expanded by an ATR-based proximity margin at the time of that bar.
Long: Close > trend SMA.
Short: Close < trend SMA.
Long: C2 low < C1 low AND C2 high <= C1 high AND C2 close >= C1 low AND C2 close <= C1 high AND C3 low >= C1 low AND C3 high >= C1 high AND C3 close > C1 high - ATR-based tolerance AND (trend filter disabled OR C3 close > trend SMA OR C3 close is within an active bullish order block expanded by an ATR-based proximity margin OR order-block MA bypass enabled AND C1 or C2 overlapped an active bullish order block expanded by its bar's ATR-based proximity margin).
Short: C2 high > C1 high AND C2 low >= C1 low AND C2 close >= C1 low AND C2 close <= C1 high AND C3 high <= C1 high AND C3 low <= C1 low AND C3 close < C1 low + ATR-based tolerance AND (trend filter disabled OR C3 close < trend SMA OR C3 close is within an active bearish order block expanded by an ATR-based proximity margin OR order-block MA bypass enabled AND C1 or C2 overlapped an active bearish order block expanded by its bar's ATR-based proximity margin).
