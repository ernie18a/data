Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback
Calculation: Bullish order block = previous bar bearish, current close above previous high, and (displacement filter disabled or current close − open > ATR × displacement multiplier); its price zone is the previous bar’s low–high range
Calculation: Bearish order block = previous bar bullish, current close below previous low, and (displacement filter disabled or open − current close > ATR × displacement multiplier); its price zone is the previous bar’s low–high range
Calculation: Active order blocks = created bullish and bearish order blocks not yet mitigated; remove a bullish block when close falls below its bottom (or low falls below its bottom if mitigation uses wicks), and remove a bearish block when close rises above its top (or high rises above its top if mitigation uses wicks)
Calculation: Near a matching order block = close is within the block’s price zone expanded on both sides by ATR × proximity multiplier
Calculation: C1/C2 touched a matching order block = either candle’s range overlaps the matching active order-block zone expanded on both sides by that candle’s ATR × proximity multiplier
Calculation: Trend average = simple moving average of close over a lookback on a configurable timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the latest available value from that timeframe (completed bar historically, possibly still-forming bar in real time)
Long: After a reference bar C1, the immediately following bar C2 has low < C1 low, high <= C1 high, and close between C1 low and C1 high; the immediately following bar C3 has low >= C1 low, high >= C1 high, and close > C1 high − ATR × close-tolerance multiplier; and (close > trend average or C3 close is near an active bullish order block or C1/C2 touched an active bullish order block)
Short: After a reference bar C1, the immediately following bar C2 has high > C1 high, low >= C1 low, and close between C1 low and C1 high; the immediately following bar C3 has high <= C1 high, low <= C1 low, and close < C1 low + ATR × close-tolerance multiplier; and (close < trend average or C3 close is near an active bearish order block or C1/C2 touched an active bearish order block)
