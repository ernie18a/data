Calculation: Profile levels = Divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over the profile lookback, including the current chart bar, into equal price rows. For each bar, let body, upper wick, and lower wick be their price lengths, and let D = body + 2 × upper wick + 2 × lower wick. Allocate volume × body / D to the body and volume × 2 × wick length / D to each wick. Distribute each allocation across rows in proportion to its overlap with that segment, halving each wick's distributed contribution; zero-length or undefined contributions are zero. Sum contributions by row. POC is the midpoint of the first lowest-priced row with maximum volume. Starting at the POC row, extend the value area one adjacent row at a time toward the larger adjacent volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until its volume reaches the specified fraction of total profile volume or both adjacent volumes are zero. VAH is the upper edge of the highest included row; VAL is the lower edge of the lowest included row.
Calculation: Signal selection = At each chart bar, hold its profile levels fixed and scan the buffered chart bars from newest to oldest, excluding the oldest buffered bar. Evaluate each candidate using that candidate's close, preceding close, RSI, and volume, against the fixed profile levels. Accept a candidate only if its bar index minus the last accepted same-direction bar index is at least the minimum signal gap; initialize each last accepted index to a sufficiently negative index. Give a qualifying long priority over a qualifying short on the same bar. Stop when the total accepted signal count reaches its cap. Recompute this selection on each chart bar.
Long: Selected long candidate AND ((signal source includes value-area breakout AND candidate close > VAH AND preceding close <= VAH) OR (signal source includes POC bounce AND candidate close > POC AND preceding close <= POC AND candidate RSI > RSI midpoint)) AND (RSI confirmation disabled OR candidate RSI > bullish threshold) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR (candidate close > candidate open AND candidate volume > 0)).
Short: Selected short candidate AND ((signal source includes value-area breakout AND candidate close < VAL AND preceding close >= VAL) OR (signal source includes POC bounce AND candidate close < POC AND preceding close >= POC AND candidate RSI < RSI midpoint)) AND (RSI confirmation disabled OR candidate RSI < bearish threshold) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR (candidate close < candidate open AND candidate volume > 0)).
