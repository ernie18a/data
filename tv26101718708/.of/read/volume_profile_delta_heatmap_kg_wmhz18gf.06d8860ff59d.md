Calculation: Volume Profile POC, VAH, VAL = Over the lookback, divide the range from the highest high to the lowest low into equal price bins. Allocate each bar’s volume across bins in proportion to price overlap: body volume is proportional to body length, and each wick volume is proportional to twice its length; split each wick’s allocated volume equally across overlapping bins. POC is the midpoint of the bin with greatest volume. Starting at POC, expand the value area toward the adjacent bin with greater volume (upper bin on a tie) until it contains the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges.
Calculation: RSI = RSI of close over its lookback.
Calculation: RSI-weighted bar delta = volume if close > open, −volume if close < open, otherwise 0; when RSI weighting is enabled, multiply by 0.35 if RSI is between the bearish and bullish thresholds, otherwise by 1.
Long: (close > VAH AND previous close <= VAH OR close > POC AND previous close <= POC AND RSI > 50) AND (RSI confirmation disabled OR RSI > bullish threshold) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR RSI-weighted bar delta > 0) AND same-side signal gap satisfied AND total signal cap not reached.
Short: (close < VAL AND previous close >= VAL OR close < POC AND previous close >= POC AND RSI < 50) AND (RSI confirmation disabled OR RSI < bearish threshold) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR RSI-weighted bar delta < 0) AND same-side signal gap satisfied AND total signal cap not reached.
