Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each session; typical price is (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Annual VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each year boundary.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when the short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA; bearish when short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA. Both require absolute short-to-medium EMA separation divided by medium EMA >= a threshold AND no squeeze. A squeeze occurs when both Bollinger Bands are inside the Keltner Channel; the channel uses an EMA center and ATR-based width.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on each of two higher timeframes, using the bar still forming, bullish when short EMA > medium EMA AND close > long EMA; bearish when short EMA < medium EMA AND close < long EMA. One higher timeframe is longer than the other.
Calculation: Directional volume = volume > its SMA AND close is at or above the bar midpoint for bullish volume, or below the midpoint for bearish volume; a zero-range bar has no directional volume.
Calculation: Session levels = prior regular-session high and low, and prior stored premarket high and low. Update both sets at the day boundary from the corresponding running session extremes; the premarket levels therefore come from the premarket accumulated before that boundary. The opening-range high and low are the running extremes during a configurable interval beginning at the regular-session open, fixed after that interval.
Calculation: Sweep = for a bullish sweep, low is below a level by more than a buffer AND close > the level AND close > open; for a bearish sweep, high is above a level by more than a buffer AND close < the level AND close < open. Eligible levels are the prior regular-session low or high, the last completed higher-timeframe week low or high, and the short EMA; an EMA sweep additionally requires short EMA > medium EMA for bullish or short EMA < medium EMA for bearish. An equal-extreme sweep uses the highest high or lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar as its level, requires above-average volume, and requires the preceding bar's corresponding extreme to be within a threshold of the equivalent lookback extreme ending two bars ago.
Calculation: Level retest = after close crosses a stored premarket or prior regular-session high upward with bullish trend, above-average volume and within the permitted session, retain the broken state until close falls below that level by a reset margin; bullish retest requires that state, a positive number of bars since the break no greater than a lookback limit, low within a margin above the current level, close >= the level, close > open and bullish directional volume. For a low-level bearish retest, reverse the inequalities and directions. Reset the bars-since-break count on a new break or when the broken state is false; do not reset the state solely at a day boundary.
Calculation: Opening-range retest = after the opening range is fixed, bullish trend, permitted session and above-average volume coincide with close crossing above its high, low within a margin above that high, close > open and bullish directional volume; reverse the inequalities and directions for the bearish case.
Calculation: EMA pullback = bullish trend AND low within a margin above the short EMA AND close > open AND bullish directional volume AND permitted session; reverse the inequalities and directions for bearish. EMA reclaim additionally requires a retained prior downside crossing state for bullish or upside crossing state for bearish. Set that state when close crosses the current short EMA using the previous close against the current EMA; clear it when close moves beyond the EMA by a reset margin in the reclaim direction.
Calculation: Regime score = count of true conditions among: chart trend exists; the shorter higher-timeframe trend agrees; the longer higher-timeframe trend agrees; close is on the trend side of session VWAP; no squeeze; and the bar is in the permitted session outside the opening exclusion window.
Calculation: Confluence score = count of true conditions among: chart trend agrees; shorter higher-timeframe trend agrees; longer higher-timeframe trend agrees; close is on the direction's side of session VWAP, counted twice; directional volume agrees; a directional sweep occurs; an eligible level retest, opening-range retest, EMA reclaim or EMA pullback occurs; regime score reaches its high threshold; and the bar is in the permitted session outside the opening exclusion window.
Calculation: Wall = bullish when the rolling low equals its previous value AND low is within a margin above that low AND close > that low AND each of a fixed number of consecutive bars, including the current bar, has volume above its own volume SMA; bearish uses the rolling high, a margin below it and close < that high.
Calculation: Hidden order = bullish when a newly confirmed pivot low, with equal left and right confirmation lengths, is within a threshold of the rolling lowest low AND low is within a margin above that rolling low AND close > that low AND volume > its SMA; bearish uses the pivot high, rolling highest high, a margin below it and close < that high.
Calculation: Large fill = volume > a multiple of its SMA AND high - low < a fraction of the SMA of high - low AND close > open for bullish or close < open for bearish.
Calculation: Reversal fill = volume > a multiple of its SMA AND high - low > a multiple of the SMA of high - low AND the preceding closes rose consecutively over a fixed lookback AND close < open for bearish; reverse the close directions for bullish. Use the same volume baseline and multiplier as the large fill.
Calculation: Session bias = track each named session's high and low, and use the close of the bar at its end as its close. A session is bullish or bearish when its close's fractional position in its range is respectively above an upper threshold or below a lower threshold; a zero or unavailable range is neutral. At the regular-session open, use the most recently completed earlier overnight and later overnight sessions. The later session sweeps the earlier session's low when its low < the earlier low AND its end-bar close > that low; reverse for a high sweep.
Long: A bullish sweep occurs.
Short: A bearish sweep occurs.
Long: Bullish trend AND regime score >= its minimum threshold AND the selected mode permits entry at the current time AND (a bullish EMA pullback OR bullish EMA reclaim OR bullish level retest OR bullish opening-range retest OR bullish sweep).
Short: Bearish trend AND regime score >= its minimum threshold AND the selected mode permits entry at the current time AND (a bearish EMA pullback OR bearish EMA reclaim OR bearish level retest OR bearish opening-range retest OR bearish sweep).
Long: Bullish trend AND bullish confluence score >= its entry threshold.
Short: Bearish trend AND bearish confluence score >= its entry threshold.
Long: A bullish wall OR bullish hidden order OR bullish large fill OR bullish reversal fill occurs.
Short: A bearish wall OR bearish hidden order OR bearish large fill OR bearish reversal fill occurs.
Long: Low is within a threshold of the rolling lowest low AND low > previous low AND close > previous close AND close > the midpoint of the rolling high and low AND volume > its SMA AND bullish trend.
Short: High is within a threshold of the rolling highest high AND high < previous high AND close < previous close AND close < the midpoint of the rolling high and low AND volume > its SMA AND bearish trend.
Long: Close crosses above annual VWAP OR session VWAP, using each VWAP's previous value for the previous-bar comparison.
Short: Close crosses below annual VWAP OR session VWAP, using each VWAP's previous value for the previous-bar comparison.
Long: Close > the last completed higher-timeframe week high AND previous close <= the current week-high level AND volume > its SMA.
Short: Close < the last completed higher-timeframe week low AND previous close >= the current week-low level AND volume > its SMA.
Long: Close > annual VWAP AND close > session VWAP.
Short: Close < annual VWAP AND close < session VWAP.
Long: The opening range is fixed AND close > its high.
Short: The opening range is fixed AND close < its low.
Long: At the regular-session open, the later overnight session sweeps the earlier session's low OR, if neither sweep has priority, its close > the earlier session high OR, if neither sweep nor outside-range close has priority, both sessions are bullish OR, if none of those conditions applies and the session biases are not opposed, the later session is bullish.
Short: At the regular-session open, the later overnight session sweeps the earlier session's high AND no low sweep occurs OR, if neither sweep has priority, its close < the earlier session low OR, if neither sweep nor outside-range close has priority, both sessions are bearish OR, if none of those conditions applies and the session biases are not opposed, the later session is bearish.
Exit: long, outside the opening exclusion window AND the selected mode permits entry at the current time AND bullish trend AND regime score >= its minimum threshold AND neither entry decision is true AND (close < short EMA OR the selected short-expiry mode is active AND the no-bounce counter >= its threshold OR the unchanged-close counter >= its threshold).
Exit: short, outside the opening exclusion window AND the selected mode permits entry at the current time AND bearish trend AND regime score >= its minimum threshold AND neither entry decision is true AND (close > short EMA OR the selected short-expiry mode is active AND the no-bounce counter >= its threshold OR the unchanged-close counter >= its threshold).
Calculation: Unchanged-close counter = consecutive bars on which close equals the preceding close, reset to zero otherwise.
Calculation: No-bounce counter = set to one when low is within a margin above the short EMA AND close > that EMA, OR high is within a margin below the short EMA AND close < that EMA; otherwise reset to zero when close differs from the EMA, or increment when close equals it.
