Calculation: CISD state = At each new higher-timeframe bar, set the state to the opposite of the direction of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, or zero if that bar is a doji; reset the prerequisite. During the forming higher-timeframe bar, set the prerequisite once a chart bar closes below its open for a bullish previous bar, or above its open for a bearish previous bar. Track the open and index of the first bar of the most recent bullish and bearish chart-bar runs; dojis do not end a run. On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, retain only the run continued by that bar; otherwise clear both levels. Check breaks before updating runs. Discard a level if the immediately following bar closes through it. After a CISD, set the state to its direction.
Calculation: Swing level = The latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high or low, using configurable left and right strengths. Replace the corresponding level when a new pivot is confirmed, and consume it on the first bar whose high exceeds the pivot high or whose low falls below the pivot low.
Calculation: Shared sweep condition = Close <= the consumed swing high if the high is breached; if the swing low is also breached on the same bar, replace this condition with close >= the consumed swing low.
Long: Chart-bar low > chart-bar high two bars earlier.
Short: Chart-bar high < chart-bar low two bars earlier.
Long: Chart-bar high > the unconsumed swing high AND (the shared sweep condition is false OR the close requirement is disabled).
Short: Chart-bar low < the unconsumed swing low AND (the shared sweep condition is false OR the close requirement is disabled).
Long: A swing-high breach was classified as a sweep AND the close exceeds that swing high within the next two chart bars.
Short: A swing-low breach was classified as a sweep AND the close falls below that swing low within the next two chart bars.
Long: The CISD prerequisite is met AND the chart bar opens in the first half of the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the CISD state is bearish AND the chart bar is bullish AND its close exceeds the most recent bearish-run level AND the level was set at least two chart bars earlier.
Short: The CISD prerequisite is met AND the chart bar opens in the first half of the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the CISD state is bullish AND the chart bar is bearish AND its close falls below the most recent bullish-run level AND the level was set at least two chart bars earlier.
