Calculation: Opening range high and low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configurable opening-range session in the configured timezone; reset at the start of each session and retain after the session ends until the next reset.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend values = close, short-period EMA of close, long-period EMA of close, and session VWAP of typical price, calculated on higher-timeframe bars using either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, consistently selected by a parameter.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise 0.
Long: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA > higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe VWAP).
Short: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA < higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe VWAP).
Long: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA > higher-timeframe long-period EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA < higher-timeframe long-period EMA.
Long: Opening range completed AND bar opening time within the configurable entry session in the configured timezone AND close > opening range high + breakout price buffer AND previous close <= opening range high + breakout price buffer AND close > open AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (chart-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close > chart-timeframe session VWAP of typical price) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR (higher-timeframe short-period EMA > higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe VWAP))).
Short: Opening range completed AND bar opening time within the configurable entry session in the configured timezone AND close < opening range low − breakout price buffer AND previous close >= opening range low − breakout price buffer AND close < open AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (chart-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close < chart-timeframe session VWAP of typical price) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR (higher-timeframe short-period EMA < higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe VWAP))).
