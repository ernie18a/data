Calculation: Source = a configurable OHLC-derived price; the default is (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Calculation: Range distance = max(Hull moving average of high − low, minimum price increment); the minimum price increment is a configurable parameter.
Calculation: Active factor = base factor, or, when ATR adaptation is enabled, base factor × ATR / simple moving average of ATR, bounded by the configured minimum and maximum factors; use an ATR ratio of 1 when the ATR average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Trailing bands = raw upper and lower bands are source + active factor × range distance and source − active factor × range distance. For each band, use its current raw value as its previous value if the previous trailing value is unavailable. The lower trailing band is the current raw lower band if it is greater than the previous lower trailing band or the previous close is below the previous lower trailing band; otherwise it is the previous lower trailing band. The upper trailing band is the current raw upper band if it is less than the previous upper trailing band or the previous close is above the previous upper trailing band; otherwise it is the previous upper trailing band.
Calculation: Trend direction = down when the previous range distance is unavailable; otherwise, if the previous trend line equals the previous upper trailing band, up when close > current upper trailing band and down otherwise; otherwise, down when close < current lower trailing band and up otherwise. The trend line is the current lower trailing band in an uptrend and the current upper trailing band in a downtrend.
Long: Trend direction = up
Short: Trend direction = down
