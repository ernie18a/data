Calculation: For a bar, body = abs(close - open), range = high - low, upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low, and close position = (close - low) / range; use a positive minimum price increment as the range when range is zero.
Calculation: A bullish five-bar start occurs when the low two bars ago is strictly below the lows of the two bars before it and the two bars after it AND that center bar satisfies an enabled bullish rejection test OR enabled doji test, with the alternatives grouped together. The bullish rejection test is lower wick >= body times a wick ratio AND upper wick <= 0.75 times body AND close position >= 0.45. The doji test is body / range <= a maximum body fraction. If the sweep filter is enabled, the center low must also be below the preceding confirmed five-bar pivot low. A bearish five-bar start reverses the high/low comparisons; its rejection test is upper wick >= body times the wick ratio AND lower wick <= 0.75 times body AND close position <= 0.55, and its sweep compares the center high above the preceding confirmed five-bar pivot high.
Calculation: A bullish two-bar start occurs when the previous bar's lower wick >= its body times the wick ratio AND current close > open AND current lower wick <= 0.5 times current body; if the sweep filter is enabled, the previous low must also be below the low two bars ago. A bearish two-bar start occurs when the previous upper wick >= its body times the wick ratio AND current close < open AND current upper wick <= 0.5 times current body; if the sweep filter is enabled, the previous high must also be above the high two bars ago.
Calculation: A selected start is the five-bar start, two-bar start, or either, according to the swing-mode parameter. Its anchor is two bars ago if a five-bar start exists on the current bar, even if the selected mode is two-bar; otherwise its anchor is one bar ago.
Calculation: Confirmed structure pivots are pivot highs and lows with two bars on each side. Bullish two-point structure requires each of the latest two confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows to be higher than its predecessor; bearish structure requires both to be lower. Three-point structure requires the same ordering across the latest three highs and latest three lows. The structure-mode parameter selects two-point, three-point, or both.
Calculation: Bullish trend passes when the trend filter is disabled OR its mode is off OR the selected EMA and/or structure requirements pass. The bullish EMA requirement is close > shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA. The bearish requirements reverse these comparisons and use bearish structure.
Calculation: A bullish FVG is low > high two bars ago AND the difference is at least a minimum FVG price width; its top is current low and bottom is the high two bars ago. A bearish FVG is high < low two bars ago AND the difference is at least that width; its top is the low two bars ago and bottom is current high. Create and retain these zones only when FVG creation is enabled. If IFVG detection is enabled, each retained valid bearish FVG of at least the minimum IFVG price width becomes invalid and produces a bullish IFVG when close > its top; each retained valid bullish FVG of at least that width becomes invalid and produces a bearish IFVG when close < its bottom. If several zones flip in one bar, use the last-created qualifying zone for that direction. FVG and IFVG minimum widths are configurable price equivalents of their tick thresholds.
Calculation: Maintain independent bullish and bearish setups. A selected start with its matching trend passing activates and resets that side's setup at its anchor, clearing any previous zone, touch and completion state. On any bar after the anchor, including the start-confirmation bar, an active setup without a zone acquires the matching newly created FVG, or otherwise the matching newly flipped IFVG, if the matching displacement condition passes. Bullish displacement is close > open AND close - open >= ATR times a multiplier AND close > shorter-period EMA; bearish displacement is close < open AND open - close >= ATR times the multiplier AND close < shorter-period EMA. Once acquired, retain the zone until the setup is reset or deactivated. Evaluate setup creation before entry and retest conditions on each bar.
Calculation: Volume passes when its filter is disabled OR volume > its moving average times a multiplier. In gap-close mode, a setup signals and deactivates only on the bar its zone is acquired and volume passes; if volume fails, the acquired zone remains and that setup cannot subsequently produce a gap-close signal.
Calculation: In retest mode, after zone acquisition, deactivate the setup before testing a retest if bars since anchor > expiry OR, when full-break invalidation is enabled, bullish low < zone bottom AND close < zone bottom, or bearish high > zone top AND close > zone top. Otherwise, remember a bullish touch when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom, or, if refined-half retest is required, low <= zone midpoint AND high >= zone bottom. Remember a bearish touch when high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top, or, if refined-half retest is required, high >= zone midpoint AND low <= zone top. A touch remains remembered until reset or deactivation and can be confirmed on the same bar. A confirmed retest deactivates the setup.
Long: Selected bullish start AND bullish trend passes.
Short: Selected bearish start AND bearish trend passes.
Long: Bullish zone is ready AND current bar index = bullish anchor index + 1.
Short: Bearish zone is ready AND current bar index = bearish anchor index + 1.
Long: Gap-close mode AND bullish zone acquired on the current bar AND volume passes.
Short: Gap-close mode AND bearish zone acquired on the current bar AND volume passes.
Long: Retest mode AND bullish setup remains active AND bullish zone is ready AND a bullish touch has occurred AND close > open AND volume passes AND (close-out filter disabled OR close > zone top).
Short: Retest mode AND bearish setup remains active AND bearish zone is ready AND a bearish touch has occurred AND close < open AND volume passes AND (close-out filter disabled OR close < zone bottom).
