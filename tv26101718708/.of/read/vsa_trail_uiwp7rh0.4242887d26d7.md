Calculation: Displacement = abs(close − open) + abs(open − previous close)
Calculation: ATR trailing-stop direction = direction changes to up when previous close < previous stop and current close > previous stop; changes to down when previous close > previous stop and current close < previous stop; otherwise retains its prior direction. The stop is updated each bar using ATR × multiplier: when current and previous closes are both above the previous stop, use max(previous stop, close − ATR × multiplier); when both are below, use min(previous stop, close + ATR × multiplier); otherwise use close − ATR × multiplier if close > previous stop, and close + ATR × multiplier otherwise.
Long: last signal side is not Long AND ATR trailing-stop direction is up AND close > open AND (volume is unavailable or volume ≤ 0 OR volume > its SMA baseline) AND Displacement > its SMA baseline
Short: last signal side is not Short AND ATR trailing-stop direction is down AND close < open AND (volume is unavailable or volume ≤ 0 OR volume > its SMA baseline) AND Displacement > its SMA baseline
