Calculation: n_i = rolling z-score, (x - SMA(x,N))/stdev(x,N), or 0 if stdev=0, applied separately to five chart-timeframe oscillators x: RSI, MACD line, Stochastic %K, MFI (on hlc3), CCI. All use standard lengths, and N is the same for all five.
Calculation: futRet = close/close[k] - 1, where k is a short lag.
Calculation: w_i = max(0, corr(n_i[k], futRet, M)) / sum over the five oscillators of the same quantity. M is a longer lookback than k. Treat na correlation as 0. If the sum is 0, use w_i = 1/5.
Calculation: neuralOsc = sum over the five of w_i * n_i.
Calculation: a pivot low (high) of a series is confirmed on the current bar when the value L bars ago is the lowest (highest) among the L bars on each side of it. Apply this to price (low for pivot lows, high for pivot highs) and separately to neuralOsc. Price pivots: PL1/PL0 are the most recent and previous confirmed price pivot lows (price and bar index); PH1/PH0 are the same for pivot highs. Oscillator pivots: OL1/OL0 and OH1/OH0 are the two most recent confirmed neuralOsc pivot lows and highs. They are tracked independently of the price pivots and are updated on the current bar before the conditions are evaluated.
Calculation: D_low = bar index of PL1 - bar index of PL0; D_high = bar index of PH1 - bar index of PH0.
Calculation: scoreBull = 0.5*min(1, a*|PL1-PL0|/PL0 + b*|OL1-OL0|) + 0.5*(A_bull/5), with a much larger than b. A_bull is the number of the five n_i for which n_i at the PL1 pivot bar > n_i at the PL0 pivot bar (L bars before PL1 confirmation and D_low further back). scoreBear = 0.5*min(1, a*|PH1-PH0|/PH0 + b*|OH1-OH0|) + 0.5*(A_bear/5). A_bear is the number of the five n_i for which n_i at the PH1 pivot bar < n_i at the PH0 pivot bar.
Long: a price pivot low is confirmed on the current bar AND minBars <= D_low <= maxBars (minBars < maxBars) AND scoreBull >= minScore AND ((PL1 < PL0 AND OL1 > OL0) OR (PL1 > PL0 AND OL1 < OL0)) AND bars since the last accepted Long signal > cooldown (or no prior Long signal). The first branch is regular divergence and the second is hidden divergence. Either branch may be disabled independently.
Short: a price pivot high is confirmed on the current bar AND minBars <= D_high <= maxBars AND scoreBear >= minScore AND ((PH1 > PH0 AND OH1 < OH0) OR (PH1 < PH0 AND OH1 > OH0)) AND bars since the last accepted Short signal > cooldown (or no prior Short signal). The first branch is regular divergence and the second is hidden divergence. Either branch may be disabled independently.
