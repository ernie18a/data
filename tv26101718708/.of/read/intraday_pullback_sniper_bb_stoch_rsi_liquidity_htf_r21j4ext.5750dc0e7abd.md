Calculation: Swing = A high or low that is an extreme over an equal configurable number of bars on each side, recognized only after the right-side bars complete. Accept alternating highs and lows only when their pivot bars are separated by at least the configured backstep and their price difference meets the optional ATR-based or percentage deviation threshold. Before an opposite swing, replace a same-side swing only with a more extreme pivot. Compare each accepted swing with the preceding swing of the same side; equality is lower. Use a shorter lookback for internal swings and lower-timeframe entry swings than for main swings. For lower-timeframe swings, the pivot must be strictly more extreme than every neighboring bar; the optional ATR-mode deviation uses the lower-timeframe mean high-low range, and the percentage deviation uses its close.
Calculation: Structure break = Close above the latest unbroken accepted swing high or below the latest unbroken accepted swing low; consume the broken level and set structure direction to the break direction. A break in the previous structure direction, including an initially neutral state, is a continuation; an opposite break is a change of character. Use main or internal swings as selected. Higher-timeframe structure uses the last completed bar; lower-timeframe entry structure processes all completed lower-timeframe bars inside each completed chart bar.
Calculation: Bias = Select EMA, main structure, or both on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar. EMA direction is determined by that bar's close relative to its EMA. Structure direction is its latest structure-break direction. With both selected, agreement gives that direction; if structure is neutral, use EMA direction; if they disagree, the direction is the structure direction only in offensive mode, otherwise neither direction is allowed. Strict bias off permits both directions.
Calculation: Band rejection = For a long, low <= lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower band AND close <= lower band + configured fraction of full band width; for a short, high >= upper band AND close < upper band AND close >= upper band - the same fraction of full band width.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = From enabled chart and higher timeframes, retain accepted swing highs and lows until their own timeframe closes strictly beyond them; higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed bar. A swing low is significant when the following swing high exceeds the preceding swing high; a swing high is significant when the following swing low is at or below the preceding swing low. Select significant levels or both significant and simple levels, and retain the configured number nearest price per side and significance class across sources. Optionally include the previous completed day's high and low as significant levels. A bullish sweep exists when the lowest chart low in the configured sweep lookback is below an eligible low level AND current close is above it; a bearish sweep exists when the highest chart high in that lookback is above an eligible high level AND current close is below it.
Calculation: Order block = After an eligible chart-timeframe main structure break, find the most recent opposite-close candle within the configured search window, including the break bar; its low and high define the block. Require the configured minimum displacement from that candle's low to the bullish break close, or from its high to the bearish break close, measured in chart ATR. Eligible breaks are all breaks, changes of character only, or breaks of significant swings, according to the selected mode. A bullish block closes through when close < its low; a bearish block closes through when close > its high. On a close through, delete it or change its direction and classify it as a breaker; delete a breaker on a subsequent close through. Retain the configured number of newest blocks. Evaluate a setup against blocks as they stood before the current bar's block updates.
Calculation: Setup zone = An optional OR of enabled conditions: close inside a same-direction order block or breaker; a same-direction significant liquidity sweep; a same-direction main chart structure break within the configured freshness window, excluding the current bar; the chart bar's range within the configured ATR tolerance of the running session low for long or high for short; the bar's range within that tolerance of either edge of an enabled session's shorter or longer opening range; or unconditional acceptance. Session extremes include the current chart bar. Opening ranges use chart bars from the beginning of each enabled session and may still be forming.
Calculation: Setup = A long setup occurs on a long band rejection AND the minimum Stochastic RSI K over the configured chart-bar lookback <= the oversold threshold AND long bias is allowed AND the optional long setup zone passes. A short setup occurs on a short band rejection AND the maximum Stochastic RSI K over that lookback >= the overbought threshold AND short bias is allowed AND the optional short setup zone passes. Each new setup refreshes its own expiration. Before each signal check, expire setups whose age in chart bars >= the configured expiration converted to chart bars by ceiling division; after checking signals, create or refresh current setups. Any signal clears both setups before current setups are created.
Calculation: Entry sequence = On completed lower-timeframe bars, a higher low followed by a higher high confirms long, and a lower-or-equal high followed by a lower-or-equal low confirms short. An opposite lower-or-equal low cancels the pending and completed long sequence; an opposite higher high cancels the pending and completed short sequence. A sequence qualifies for an active setup if its completion count is no earlier than the lower-timeframe bar count at the latest setup minus the configured lead; once qualified it stays qualified until that setup expires or is cleared. Process the current chart bar's lower-timeframe bars and update qualification after its signal check, so signals use only state available before the current chart bar.
Calculation: Signal timing = A setup must be active from a prior chart bar. Entry structure may be disabled, or selected as chart close beyond the latest accepted lower-timeframe swing high or low available before the current chart bar, the qualified entry sequence, or both. A fresh band rejection is required except when structure is enabled and the selected mode is structure break alone. The optional sweep filter requires a current same-direction sweep. If any sessions are enabled, the chart-bar opening time must be in an enabled session and, if the optional opening-hours limit is enabled, no later than that limit after the first chart bar in the session; otherwise all times pass. The chart-bar distance from the last signal of either direction must be greater than the configured cooldown converted to chart bars by ceiling division. If both signals occur together, retain only long.
