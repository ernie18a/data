Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the configured numbers of bars on its left and right; recognize it only when the right-hand bars have completed. Keep the most recently confirmed high and low separately.
Calculation: Structure bias = initially neutral; before updating pivots on each bar, set bullish when close crosses above the previously established swing high and bearish when close crosses below the previously established swing low. A cross above requires current close > current level and previous close <= previous bar's level; a cross below requires current close < current level and previous close >= previous bar's level. Process an upward cross before a downward cross. Record the bar of each cross.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when the higher-timeframe close > its EMA, optionally requiring the EMA > its value on the preceding chart bar and higher-timeframe ADX >= its threshold; bearish when close < EMA, optionally requiring EMA < its value on the preceding chart bar and ADX >= its threshold; otherwise neutral. Use the higher timeframe's still-forming bar in real time; historical higher-timeframe values become available on the final chart bar of each higher-timeframe period and otherwise carry forward the last completed value.
Calculation: Qualifying low sweep = after updating confirmed pivots, low < latest swing low - sweep-pierce multiplier * ATR AND close > latest swing low AND, if the proximity filter is enabled, abs(low - last completed higher-timeframe low) <= proximity multiplier * ATR. A qualifying high sweep substitutes high > latest swing high + sweep-pierce multiplier * ATR, close < latest swing high, and proximity of high to the last completed higher-timeframe high.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = high - low >= displacement multiplier * ATR AND close > open AND close position >= 1 - close-position threshold, where close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 if high = low. Bearish displacement uses close < open AND close position <= close-position threshold.
Calculation: Pending sweep = maintain independent bullish and bearish states. A qualifying low sweep starts or replaces the bullish state, storing its low, its pierce depth divided by ATR, and a confirmation counter; a qualifying high sweep does the same for the bearish state using its high. On the same bar and each subsequent bar, first confirm and clear the state if the matching displacement occurs AND volume >= volume multiplier * SMA(volume). Otherwise invalidate and clear the bullish state if close < stored low, or the bearish state if close > stored high. Otherwise decrement the counter; when it reaches zero, reset it once to the grace-period length if grace is enabled and unused, or else clear the state. A replacement sweep resets the counter and grace status. Confirmation clears the state even if a later signal filter blocks it.
Calculation: Reversal quality = 100 times the mean of four components: min(stored sweep pierce in ATR / (3 * max(sweep-pierce multiplier, 0.01)), 1); min((high - low) / ATR / (1.5 * displacement multiplier), 1); min((volume / SMA(volume)) / (1.5 * volume multiplier), 1), or 0.5 if SMA(volume) <= 0; and 1 if higher-timeframe bias agrees with the reversal, 0 if it opposes it, or 0.5 if neutral.
Long: A newly confirmed swing high > the preceding confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low > the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A newly confirmed swing high < the preceding confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low < the preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: Close crosses above the previously established swing high.
Short: Close crosses below the previously established swing low.
Long: Qualifying low sweep.
Short: Qualifying high sweep.
Long: Low > high two bars earlier OR bullish displacement AND previous close < previous open.
Short: High < low two bars earlier OR bearish displacement AND previous close > previous open.
Long: Pending bullish sweep confirms AND (higher-timeframe bias agreement filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bearish) AND (structure alignment filter disabled OR structure bias is bullish OR bars since the last structure break >= staleness threshold) AND (quality filter disabled OR reversal quality >= quality threshold) AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous bullish reversal signal exists OR bars since the previous bullish reversal signal >= cooldown threshold).
Short: Pending bearish sweep confirms AND (higher-timeframe bias agreement filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bullish) AND (structure alignment filter disabled OR structure bias is bearish OR bars since the last structure break >= staleness threshold) AND (quality filter disabled OR reversal quality >= quality threshold) AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous bearish reversal signal exists OR bars since the previous bearish reversal signal >= cooldown threshold).
