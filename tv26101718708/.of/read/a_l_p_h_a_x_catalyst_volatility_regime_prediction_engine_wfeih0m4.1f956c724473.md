Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands are inside Keltner Channels; squeeze release = squeeze was on the prior bar and is off now
Calculation: Compression score = 30 if squeeze is on, else 15 if it was on the prior bar, else 0; +20 if the current high-low range is the lowest in its lookback; +15 if the number of narrow-range bars in the coil-duration window is at least coil duration minus 1; +15 if volume is below its moving average times a fraction; +10 if the number of bars with volume below that threshold in the coil-duration window is at least coil duration minus 1; +10 if ATR percentile rank is below 25, else +5 if below 40; cap at 100
Calculation: Fully coiled = compression score at least 75 and squeeze is on
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of volume weighted by close position within the bar range minus EMA of the remaining volume, using the same lookback; bullish delta = positive and rising; bearish delta = negative and falling
Calculation: Bias vector = +25 for bullish delta, else -25 for bearish delta, else +10 for positive volume delta or -10 for negative; +20 if EMA of close position over its lookback is above 0.55, else -20 if below 0.45; +20 if the current high exceeds the prior lookback highs or current low exceeds the prior lookback lows, else -20 if current low is below the prior lookback lows or current high is below the prior lookback highs; +20 if higher-timeframe close is above its EMA, else -20 if below (use the last completed higher-timeframe bar); +15 if close is above the Bollinger basis, else -15 if below; clamp to -100 through 100
Calculation: Strong bullish candle = close above open and absolute close-open is at least ATR times a threshold; strong bearish candle = close below open and absolute close-open is at least ATR times the same threshold
Calculation: ATR expanding = ATR is above prior-bar ATR and above its short-term moving average
Calculation: Long ignition score = 20 if fully coiled or compression score at least 55, else 10 if score at least 40; +20 if squeeze released, else +10 if squeeze was on the prior bar; +25 if bias vector at least 50, else +15 if at least 35; +15 if higher-timeframe bias is bullish or disabled; +15 for a strong bullish candle, else +8 if close is above open; +5 if ATR is expanding; +10 for bullish delta, else +5 if volume delta is positive; -15 if bias vector is negative; clamp to 0 through 100
Calculation: Short ignition score = 20 if fully coiled or compression score at least 55, else 10 if score at least 40; +20 if squeeze released, else +10 if squeeze was on the prior bar; +25 if bias vector at most -50, else +15 if at most -35; +15 if higher-timeframe bias is bearish or disabled; +15 for a strong bearish candle, else +8 if close is below open; +5 if ATR is expanding; +10 for bearish delta, else +5 if volume delta is negative; -15 if bias vector is positive; clamp to 0 through 100
Long: regime is COILED (compression score at least 75 with squeeze on, reached from BUILDING) AND ((strong bullish candle OR squeeze released and close > open)) AND bias vector >= 35 AND ATR is expanding AND (squeeze release is not required OR squeeze released OR squeeze was on the prior bar) AND long ignition score >= threshold AND session filter passes if enabled AND no signal occurred within the cooldown lookback
Short: regime is COILED (compression score at least 75 with squeeze on, reached from BUILDING) AND ((strong bearish candle OR squeeze released and close < open)) AND bias vector <= -35 AND ATR is expanding AND (squeeze release is not required OR squeeze released OR squeeze was on the prior bar) AND short ignition score >= threshold AND session filter passes if enabled AND no signal occurred within the cooldown lookback
Exit: Long, close <= the lower of entry price minus ATR times the stop multiple and coil low minus 0.15 ATR OR close >= entry price plus ATR times the first target multiple OR close >= entry price plus ATR times the second target multiple OR regime is TRENDING in the long direction and (squeeze is on AND volume is below its moving average times the dry-up fraction OR RSI is at or above the overbought threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times the climax multiple AND upper wick is more than the wick fraction of bar range OR strong bearish candle closes below the 21-period EMA)
Exit: Short, close >= the higher of entry price plus ATR times the stop multiple and coil high plus 0.15 ATR OR close <= entry price minus ATR times the first target multiple OR close <= entry price minus ATR times the second target multiple OR regime is TRENDING in the short direction and (squeeze is on AND volume is below its moving average times the dry-up fraction OR RSI is at or below the oversold threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times the climax multiple AND lower wick is more than the wick fraction of bar range OR strong bullish candle closes above the 21-period EMA)
