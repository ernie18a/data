Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band; Bollinger bands use SMA(close) plus or minus a standard-deviation multiple, and Keltner bands use EMA(close) plus or minus an ATR multiple. Squeeze release = previous-bar squeeze AND NOT current-bar squeeze.
Calculation: Compression score = the capped sum of configurable points for current squeeze, otherwise previous-bar squeeze; current high-low range being no greater than the lowest range in its lookback; the count of such narrow-range bars in the coil-duration window being at least that window's length minus one; volume below a fraction of its SMA; the count of such dry-volume bars in the same coil-duration window being at least that window's length minus one; and ATR percentile rank below a lower threshold, otherwise below a higher threshold. The current-squeeze award exceeds the previous-squeeze award, and the lower-percentile award exceeds the higher-percentile award. Coiling = compression score >= coiling threshold. Fully coiled = compression score >= higher, fully-coiled threshold AND squeeze.
Calculation: Directional volume delta = EMA of volume × (close − low) / range minus EMA of volume × (high − close) / range, using the same lookback; range is high − low. Rising positive delta and falling negative delta are the accelerating directions. Close-position bias = EMA of (close − low) / range, using its own lookback. On zero-range bars these ratios cannot be reproduced from the supplied fields because the source uses the instrument's unavailable minimum tick.
Calculation: Bias score = capped sum of signed configurable points: accelerating positive or negative directional volume delta, otherwise smaller points for its sign; close-position bias above an upper threshold or below a lower threshold; high above the previous structure-lookback highs OR low above the previous structure-lookback lows, otherwise low below the previous structure-lookback lows OR high below the previous structure-lookback highs; higher-timeframe close above or below its EMA; and chart close above or below the Bollinger SMA. Apply positive points for the first condition in each pair and negative points for the second; test the bullish structure condition first. When higher-timeframe alignment is disabled, award its positive points. Higher-timeframe close and EMA use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Strong bullish or bearish candle = close respectively above or below open AND absolute close − open >= a multiple of ATR. ATR expansion = ATR > previous ATR AND ATR > its shorter-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Long ignition score = capped sum of configurable points for coiling, otherwise compression score >= a lower threshold; squeeze release, otherwise previous-bar squeeze; bias score >= a high positive threshold, otherwise >= a lower positive threshold; higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA; strong bullish candle, otherwise close > open; ATR expansion; accelerating positive directional volume delta, otherwise positive delta; minus configurable points if bias score < zero. The first award in each alternative exceeds the second. Short ignition score mirrors the directional comparisons and uses the same compression, squeeze and ATR-expansion components.
Calculation: Regime = a persistent state initialized to quiet, updated once per bar using the previous state: quiet becomes building when compression score >= the building threshold; building becomes coiled when fully coiled, otherwise quiet when compression score < a lower reset threshold; coiled becomes ignition with bullish or bearish trend direction when the corresponding ignition event occurs, otherwise quiet when compression score < a higher reset threshold AND not coiling; ignition becomes trending on the next bar; trending becomes exhaustion on the applicable exhaustion condition; exhaustion becomes building when compression score >= the building threshold, otherwise quiet. An ignition event is evaluated before the ignition-score, session and cooldown filters. In coiled state, test the long event first: strong bullish candle OR squeeze release AND close > open; bias score >= the positive ignition threshold; squeeze release required only if enabled, in which case current squeeze release OR previous-bar squeeze must hold; and ATR expansion. The short event mirrors directional comparisons. On an event, retain its trend direction through exhaustion.
Calculation: Bullish-trend exhaustion = RSI >= an upper threshold AND volume > a multiple of its volume SMA AND upper wick / range > a rejection threshold; OR squeeze AND dry volume; OR strong bearish candle AND close < a separate EMA(close). Bearish-trend exhaustion mirrors RSI, wick, candle and EMA comparisons. Exhaustion is evaluated only when the previous regime is trending.
Long: Regime becomes ignition with bullish trend direction AND long ignition score >= its threshold AND session filtering is disabled OR the bar's UTC time is in an enabled session AND the cooldown has elapsed since the last displayed ignition signal. Interpret the session alternative as a single grouped condition.
Short: Regime becomes ignition with bearish trend direction AND short ignition score >= its threshold AND session filtering is disabled OR the bar's UTC time is in an enabled session AND the cooldown has elapsed since the last displayed ignition signal. Interpret the session alternative as a single grouped condition.
Long: Regime is building OR coiled, grouped together, AND bias score >= the positive arrow threshold.
Short: Regime is building OR coiled, grouped together, AND bias score <= the negative arrow threshold.
Exit: long, regime becomes exhaustion AND retained trend direction is bullish.
Exit: short, regime becomes exhaustion AND retained trend direction is bearish.
