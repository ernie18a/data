Calculation: Selected OHLC = chosen price source and OHLC requested at a selectable timeframe; for a higher timeframe use either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar available within each chart bar. Repeat requested values across chart bars when necessary. All subsequent lookbacks operate on chart bars.
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA of the selected source requested again at an automatically selected higher timeframe with lookahead enabled, then calculated across chart bars; where no higher timeframe is mapped, use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression endpoint of selected source minus the average of (the average of the highest selected high and lowest selected low over the momentum lookback) and the SMA of selected close over the same lookback; use that lookback for the regression.
Calculation: Selected PSAR = either standard PSAR calculated from chart OHLC and shifted back one chart bar when completed-bar mode is selected, or adaptive PSAR calculated from selected OHLC as follows. Adaptive PSAR starts in an uptrend on the second chart bar, with its up stop at the lower of the current and previous selected lows and its acceleration at the starting factor. On later bars, choose current high and low as the reversal prices, or use selected source for both; the two previous reversal prices are the previous two selected highs and lows. Set the adaptive period to 2 divided by maximum acceleration minus 1. The adaptive input is selected source in Kaufman mode, or the absolute difference between selected source and the previous nonzero stop in Ehlers mode. In Kaufman mode, efficiency is the absolute input change over the rounded-up adaptive period divided by the sum of absolute one-bar input changes from offset zero through that period inclusive, or zero if the denominator is zero. In Ehlers mode, efficiency is the input's position within its range over offsets zero through the rounded-up maximum of a fixed minimum lookback and a multiple of the adaptive period inclusive, or zero if the range is zero. Smooth efficiency with an EMA; step equals minimum step plus smoothed efficiency times the difference between maximum and minimum step. If the prior trend persists, increase acceleration by step up to its maximum when the previous selected high exceeds the high two bars ago in an uptrend, or when the previous selected low is below the low two bars ago in a downtrend; reset acceleration to its starting factor for the opposite comparisons. Otherwise retain prior acceleration. Advance the active stop by acceleration times the difference between the prior selected high or low, respectively, and the prior stop; cap an up stop at the previous two reversal lows and floor a down stop at the previous two reversal highs. If the stop's favorable change is below a specified minimum price increment and both current and prior stops are nonzero, retain the prior stop. An up stop cannot decrease and a down stop cannot increase. Reverse from down to up when the current reversal high is at least the down stop plus a specified price filter: set the up stop to the minimum of the current reversal low and prior selected low, reset acceleration, and clear the down stop. Reverse from up to down when the current reversal low is at most the up stop minus the filter: set the down stop to the maximum of the current reversal high and prior selected high, reset acceleration, and clear the up stop. The active nonzero stop is the adaptive PSAR.
Calculation: Long stop = at long entry, the lowest selected low over the stop lookback multiplied by a stop multiplier; if the current selected low equals that lowest low, use the backup multiplier instead. Retain the prior stop if the lowest low is not positive and does not equal the current low.
Calculation: Short stop = at short entry, the highest selected high over the stop lookback multiplied by one plus the difference between one and the stop multiplier; if the current selected high equals that highest high, use the backup multiplier instead. Retain the prior stop if the highest high is not positive and does not equal the current high.
Calculation: Long target = at long entry, selected close plus the risk-reward ratio multiplied by selected close minus long stop.
Calculation: Short target = at short entry, selected close multiplied by one minus the risk-reward ratio multiplied by one minus selected close divided by short stop.
Long: position is flat AND selected source > trend EMA AND selected source crosses above selected PSAR AND squeeze momentum crosses above zero
Short: position is flat AND selected source < trend EMA AND selected source crosses below selected PSAR AND squeeze momentum crosses below zero
Exit: long, selected close crosses below long stop OR selected close crosses above long target
Exit: short, selected close crosses above short stop OR selected close crosses below short target
