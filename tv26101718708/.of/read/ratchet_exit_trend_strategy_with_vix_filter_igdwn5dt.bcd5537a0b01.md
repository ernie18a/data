Calculation: Profit (%) = (close − entry price) / entry price × 100
Calculation: Ratchet stop = the highest of the previous ratchet stop and the current stage stop; current stage stop is entry price × (1 − initial stop %) before Profit reaches stage 1, entry price × (1 − stage 1 stop %) from stage 1 until stage 2, and the highest high since entry × (1 − trailing distance %) from stage 2 onward. Advance to stage 1 when Profit ≥ stage 1 trigger and to stage 2 when Profit ≥ stage 2 trigger; retain the highest stage reached. Initialize the stop on the first bar of a position and reset the stage, stop, and highest high when flat.
Long: (VIX filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe daily VIX close < threshold) AND (shorter-lookback SMA crosses above longer-lookback SMA OR close > 50-lookback SMA AND previous close > its 50-lookback SMA AND close two bars ago > its 50-lookback SMA AND close three bars ago < its 50-lookback SMA OR close > 100-lookback SMA AND previous close > its 100-lookback SMA AND close two bars ago > its 100-lookback SMA AND close three bars ago < its 100-lookback SMA OR close > 200-lookback SMA AND previous close > its 200-lookback SMA AND close two bars ago > its 200-lookback SMA AND close three bars ago < its 200-lookback SMA); the crossover uses 25- and 50-lookback SMAs, with 25 shorter than 50, and the breakout lookbacks are 50, 100, and 200.
Exit: Long, low ≤ ratchet stop OR enabled VIX filter AND last completed higher-timeframe daily VIX close > threshold OR shorter-lookback SMA crosses below longer-lookback SMA (25- and 50-lookback SMAs)
