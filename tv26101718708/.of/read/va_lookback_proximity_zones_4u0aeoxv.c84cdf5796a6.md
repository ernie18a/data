Calculation: Value-area low and high = For each chart bar in a configurable rolling lookback, fixed datetime range, or range beginning at an anchor datetime, take the high and low returned by the block-timeframe series. For a higher block timeframe, use the last completed block on historical bars and the forming block on the live bar; for a lower block timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within each chart bar. Count a returned block once per chart bar even when consecutive chart bars return the same block. Divide the maximum-to-minimum range of these highs and lows into a configurable number of equal price bins. Count each returned block in every bin its low-to-high range touches, including boundaries. Start at the lowest bin with the maximum count; expand the included bins one at a time toward the adjacent bin with the larger count, choosing the upper bin on ties, until their combined count reaches the configured fraction of all bin counts or no bins remain. The value-area low is the bottom of the lowest included bin and the value-area high is the top of the highest included bin. No value area exists in periodic mode.
Long: Value-area high > value-area low AND close >= value-area low AND (close - value-area low) / (value-area high - value-area low) <= the long-zone maximum fraction
Short: Value-area high > value-area low AND close <= value-area high AND (close - value-area low) / (value-area high - value-area low) >= the short-zone minimum fraction
