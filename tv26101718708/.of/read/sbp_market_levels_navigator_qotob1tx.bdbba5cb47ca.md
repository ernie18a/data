Calculation: Active levels = Maintain an ordered, capacity-limited list of levels. On each completed chart bar, add confirmed pivot highs as resistance and pivot lows as support; pivots use configurable left and right confirmation lookbacks. Optionally, at the start of each configured higher-timeframe period, add the last completed period's high as resistance and low as support. For one shorter higher-timeframe period, optionally also add its classic pivot point as support if the current bar's open is at or above it, otherwise resistance, and its first and second classic pivot supports and resistances with their respective roles. Process candidates in that order. Merge each candidate into the nearest active level within a configurable ATR multiple: replace its price with the confirmation-count-weighted mean of its old price and the candidate price, increment its confirmation count, and replace its role with the candidate role. Otherwise create a level with confirmation count 1, no previous touch, zero acceptance count, and creation bar equal to the current bar; if at capacity, first remove the oldest stored level, including inactive levels. After adding candidates, deactivate levels whose age exceeds a configurable maximum. For each remaining level, a touch occurs when high >= level price minus a configurable ATR multiple AND low <= level price plus that multiple AND the level has no previous touch or enough bars have elapsed since its previous touch; record the current bar as its last touch. On each bar, increment a support level's acceptance count if close < level price minus a configurable ATR multiple, or a resistance level's acceptance count if close > level price plus that multiple; otherwise reset the count to zero. When the count reaches a configurable consecutive-bar threshold, change support to resistance or resistance to support, reset the count, and reset the creation bar to the current bar. Evaluate touches and rejections before changing roles.
Calculation: Bullish evidence count = Sum of two binary conditions: at least one active support is touched AND at least one active support is rejected on the current bar; at least one active resistance changes to support on the current bar. The touched and rejected levels can differ. A support rejection requires low <= level price plus the touch ATR multiple AND close > level price AND (min(open, close) - low) / candle range >= a configurable threshold AND (close - low) / candle range >= a configurable threshold. Candle range is high - low, floored at a configurable positive price increment.
Calculation: Bearish evidence count = Sum of two binary conditions: at least one active resistance is touched AND at least one active resistance is rejected on the current bar; at least one active support changes to resistance on the current bar. The touched and rejected levels can differ. A resistance rejection requires high >= level price minus the touch ATR multiple AND close < level price AND (high - max(open, close)) / candle range >= a configurable threshold AND (high - close) / candle range >= a configurable threshold.
Calculation: Signal state = Initially neutral; set to long after a long decision and short after a short decision, retaining the state on all other bars.
Long: Bullish evidence count > 0 AND bullish evidence count > bearish evidence count AND signal state is not long.
Short: Bearish evidence count > 0 AND bearish evidence count > bullish evidence count AND signal state is not short.
