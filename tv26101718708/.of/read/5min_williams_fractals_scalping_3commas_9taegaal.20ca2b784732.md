Calculation: Williams lower fractal = a low N chart bars before the current bar that is strictly below the N preceding lows and has N following lows strictly above it, allowing a plateau of up to four equal lows immediately after the pivot before those N strictly higher lows. Williams upper fractal = the inverse condition on highs.
Calculation: Pullback filter = the count of opposite-direction candles among the configured lookback bars, starting one bar before the signal bar, does not exceed the configured maximum.
Calculation: RSI = RSI of close on the selected timeframe; when that timeframe differs from the chart timeframe, use its last completed bar. RSI rising/falling means current RSI is higher/lower than each RSI value in the configured lookback.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + configured reward multiple × (entry price − slow EMA at entry). Short target = entry price − configured reward multiple × (slow EMA at entry − entry price).
Long: Williams lower fractal
Long: Williams lower fractal AND current date/time is within the configured trading window AND fast EMA >= slow EMA AND close N chart bars ago is below fast EMA and above slow EMA AND (EMA gap filter disabled OR absolute difference between fast and slow EMA >= configured gap percentage of current close) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is within the configured long range AND (RSI direction confirmation disabled OR RSI is rising)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR pullback filter passes) AND no position is open
Short: Williams upper fractal
Short: Williams upper fractal AND current date/time is within the configured trading window AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close N chart bars ago is above fast EMA and below slow EMA AND (EMA gap filter disabled OR absolute difference between fast and slow EMA >= configured gap percentage of current close) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is within the configured short range AND (RSI direction confirmation disabled OR RSI is falling)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR pullback filter passes) AND no position is open
Exit: Long position, after the configured minimum holding period, low <= slow EMA at entry OR high >= long target
Exit: Short position, after the configured minimum holding period, high >= slow EMA at entry OR low <= short target
