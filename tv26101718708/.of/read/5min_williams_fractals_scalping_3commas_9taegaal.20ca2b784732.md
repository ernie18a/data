Calculation: Fractal low = for candidate bar n bars before the current bar, each of the n newer lows is strictly higher than the candidate low, and for at least one k in 0 through 4, each of the first k older lows is greater than or equal to the candidate low and each of the next n older lows is strictly higher than the candidate low.
Calculation: Fractal high = for candidate bar n bars before the current bar, each of the n newer highs is strictly lower than the candidate high, and for at least one k in 0 through 4, each of the first k older highs is less than or equal to the candidate high and each of the next n older highs is strictly lower than the candidate high.
Calculation: RSI source = chart close, or a configurable higher- or lower-timeframe close mapped to chart bars; historical higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar, and historical lower-timeframe values use the last intrabar. RSI is calculated on the resulting chart-bar series.
Long: Fractal low = true.
Short: Fractal high = true.
Long: long entries enabled = true AND datetime within configured date range AND open position count = 0 AND Fractal low = true AND short-period EMA >= long-period EMA AND selected close < selected short-period EMA AND selected close > selected long-period EMA AND (EMA gap filter disabled OR absolute difference between current EMAs >= current close multiplied by configured percentage) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI within configured long range AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR current RSI > every RSI in the configured preceding lookback)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR imported bullish pullback count is nonzero). Selected close and EMAs are either current values or values at the fractal candidate bar, according to configuration.
Short: short entries enabled = true AND datetime within configured date range AND open position count = 0 AND Fractal high = true AND short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND selected close > selected short-period EMA AND selected close < selected long-period EMA AND (EMA gap filter disabled OR absolute difference between current EMAs >= current close multiplied by configured percentage) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI within configured short range AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR current RSI < every RSI in the configured preceding lookback)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR imported bearish pullback count is nonzero). Selected close and EMAs are either current values or values at the fractal candidate bar, according to configuration.
Exit: long, bars since last flat bar >= configured delay AND (low <= long-period EMA at entry signal OR high >= entry signal close + configured long reward multiple multiplied by (entry signal close - long-period EMA at entry signal)).
Exit: short, bars since last flat bar >= configured delay AND (high >= long-period EMA at entry signal OR low <= entry signal close - configured short reward multiple multiplied by (long-period EMA at entry signal - entry signal close)).
