Calculation: Confirmed swing high = high of a pivot bar that is at least as high as every high in the specified number of preceding bars and strictly higher than every high in the specified number of following bars; it becomes available after the following bars close. Use the most recently confirmed swing high, updating it before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = low of a pivot bar that is at least as low as every low in the specified number of preceding bars and strictly lower than every low in the specified number of following bars; it becomes available after the following bars close. Use the most recently confirmed swing low, updating it before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = ATR multiplied by a configurable multiplier when enabled, otherwise zero.
Long: Current close > current confirmed swing high + current breakout buffer AND previous close <= current confirmed swing high + current breakout buffer AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > current volume SMA multiplied by a configurable multiplier) AND no long signal has occurred since the current swing high was confirmed.
Short: Current close < current confirmed swing low - current breakout buffer AND previous close >= current confirmed swing low - current breakout buffer AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > current volume SMA multiplied by a configurable multiplier) AND no short signal has occurred since the current swing low was confirmed.
