Calculation: Structure bars = chart-timeframe bars or higher-timeframe bars; process each last completed structure bar once, when the next structure bar begins.
Calculation: Live swings = a structure-bar high or low strictly greater or less than every high or low, respectively, in its left and right confirmation windows. Confirm after the right window completes. A newly confirmed swing replaces the previous live swing of the same kind; a swing stops being live when broken.
Calculation: Structure direction and break count = initially neutral and zero. On each completed structure bar, first update live swings, then test its close against the live swing high and low in that order. A close above a live swing high is a bullish break; a close below a live swing low is a bearish break. Each break consumes its swing. If its direction matches the current structure direction, increment the break count; otherwise set the direction to the break direction and the count to one. If both breaks occur, process both in that order and retain the bearish break as the bar's event.
Calculation: Continuation zone = on a new completed structure bar, a directional break with break count at least the selected continuation threshold qualifies when enough structure bars have accumulated for the readiness requirement, structure ATR is available, and an opposing candle exists within the origin lookback before the break bar. The threshold represents either the direction-changing break plus one subsequent break or the direction-changing break plus two subsequent breaks. Select the most recent opposing candle as origin and use its high and low as zone boundaries. For a bullish zone, require break close minus origin low >= displacement multiplier times completed structure-bar ATR, OR origin high < low two structure bars after the origin; for a bearish zone, require origin high minus break close >= displacement multiplier times completed structure-bar ATR, OR origin low > high two structure bars after the origin. The selected qualification mode may require both comparisons instead of either. A new qualifying zone replaces the active zone of the same direction. Deactivate an active zone when structure direction or break count no longer satisfies its continuation state, or on a later completed structure bar when its age exceeds the maximum or its close is below the bullish zone low or above the bearish zone high. Evaluate deactivation before creating a new zone. Track whether each active zone has had an interaction, initially false.
Long: A newly processed completed structure-bar close > its live swing high.
Short: A newly processed completed structure-bar close < its live swing low, after the swing-high test.
Long: A bullish continuation zone is active AND it has not previously interacted AND chart-bar low <= zone high.
Short: A bearish continuation zone is active AND it has not previously interacted AND chart-bar high >= zone low.
