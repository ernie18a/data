Calculation: Close-extreme wave state = start with the first valid close as an anchor; begin an up-wave after a close rises by at least the reversal threshold from the anchor, or a down-wave after a close falls by at least the threshold. In an up-wave, update the extreme on closes at or above it; in a down-wave, update it on closes at or below it. Keep the threshold fixed for each wave and recalculate it when the next wave starts.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the wave is not up and close is at least the reversal threshold minus the tick tolerance above the wave's extreme close (or the initial anchor close).
Short: On a confirmed bar, the wave is not down and close is at least the reversal threshold minus the tick tolerance below the wave's extreme close (or the initial anchor close).
