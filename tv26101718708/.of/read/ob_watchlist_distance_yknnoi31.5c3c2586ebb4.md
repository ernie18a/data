Calculation: Bullish OB = evaluated on bars of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. When a bar's close is above the latest confirmed swing high (pivot high with equal left and right bar counts), the previous bar's close was <= that swing high, and (close - open) > multiplier * ATR, take the most recent bearish candle (close < open) among the lookback bars before the breakout bar. Zone top = that candle's high, zone bottom = its low. A newer bullish OB replaces the old one. The OB is active from creation until a higher-timeframe close falls below the zone bottom.
Calculation: Bearish OB = same timeframe and bar-state as Bullish OB, mirrored. When a bar's close is below the latest confirmed swing low (pivot low), the previous close was >= that swing low, and (open - close) > multiplier * ATR, take the most recent bullish candle (close > open) among the lookback bars before the breakout bar. Zone top = its high, zone bottom = its low. A newer bearish OB replaces the old one. The OB is active from creation until a higher-timeframe close rises above the zone top.
Long: Bullish OB is active AND (Bearish OB is not active OR |higher-timeframe close - bullish zone bottom| <= |higher-timeframe close - bearish zone top|)
Short: Bearish OB is active AND (Bullish OB is not active OR |higher-timeframe close - bullish zone bottom| > |higher-timeframe close - bearish zone top|)
