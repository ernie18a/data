Calculation: Three independently configured moving averages use selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA, source, lookback, and timeframe. Their usual lookback order is shortest, intermediate, longest, but the lookbacks are independently selectable. On a higher timeframe, historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; a realtime bar uses the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. On a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar.
Long: For any ordered pair among shortest/intermediate, shortest/longest, and intermediate/longest moving averages, both averages are enabled AND the first crosses above the second: its current value is greater than the second and its previous chart-bar value was less than or equal to the second.
Short: For any such ordered pair, both averages are enabled AND the first crosses below the second: its current value is less than the second and its previous chart-bar value was greater than or equal to the second.
Exit: long, the moving-average pair that produced the entry is enabled for trading AND both averages are enabled AND the first crosses below the second.
Exit: long, high reaches or exceeds entry price plus a configurable profit-target percentage of the entry signal bar's close OR low reaches or falls below entry price minus a configurable stop-loss percentage of the entry signal bar's close.
