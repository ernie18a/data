Calculation: Zones = On each chart bar, retain confirmed pivot highs and lows whose pivot bars are within a lookback. Process retained pivots from oldest to newest, with a high before a low when both occur on the same bar. Each pivot has its pivot price, pivot-bar volume, and high or low direction. For each pivot, find the first existing zone of the same direction whose absolute price distance from the pivot is no greater than the greater of a minimum price distance and a percentage of the pivot price. If found, add the pivot volume to the zone volume and set the zone price to the volume-weighted average of its previous price and the pivot price; if the resulting volume is zero, use the pivot price. Otherwise create a zone at the pivot price with the pivot volume and direction. Rank zones by volume descending, breaking ties by creation order. A zone's relative strength is its volume divided by the maximum zone volume; relative strength is defined only when the maximum zone volume is positive.
Long: A low-direction zone ranks within the top zone count AND its relative strength is at least the minimum strength.
Short: A high-direction zone ranks within the top zone count AND its relative strength is at least the minimum strength.
