Calculation: All decisions use closed chart bars. A session is either a configured trading session or, when session filtering is disabled, a calendar day; an active bar is inside the configured session or any bar when filtering is disabled. Reset session statistics at each new session.
Calculation: Bar range = high − low; body fraction = |close − open| / bar range; upper tail = high − max(open, close); lower tail = min(open, close) − low. A bullish bar has close > open; a bearish bar has close < open. A strong bullish bar is bullish with body fraction >= a threshold and upper tail / bar range <= a threshold; a strong bearish bar is bearish with body fraction >= the same body threshold and lower tail / bar range <= the same tail threshold. A doji has body fraction <= a threshold. Zero-range bars require a minimum price increment, which is not supplied in the backtest fields.
Calculation: A bullish reversal bar is bullish AND close is at or above a threshold fraction of its range measured from low AND (it is strong bullish OR its lower tail is at least a threshold fraction of its range OR close > previous high). A bearish reversal bar is bearish AND close is at or below the corresponding fraction of its range measured from high AND (it is strong bearish OR its upper tail is at least the same tail fraction of its range OR close < previous low).
Calculation: Prior range high and low = highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending at the previous bar; prior range width = prior range high − prior range low. Overlap fraction = fraction of bars over that lookback whose high >= previous low AND low <= previous high. Range efficiency = |close − close from that lookback bars ago| / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback including the current bar).
Calculation: Uptrend = EMA > its value a trend-lookback ago AND close − close a trend-lookback ago >= an ATR multiple AND fraction of closes above their EMA over the trend lookback >= a threshold AND range efficiency >= a threshold. Downtrend = EMA < its value a trend-lookback ago AND close − close a trend-lookback ago <= the negative of the same ATR multiple AND fraction of closes below their EMA >= the same threshold AND range efficiency >= the same threshold. Range efficiency uses the range lookback, which is independent of the trend lookback.
Calculation: A range-breaking up bar is strong bullish AND its range >= an ATR multiple AND close > prior range high; a range-breaking down bar is strong bearish AND its range >= the same ATR multiple AND close < prior range low. On either event, set the breakout direction to its direction and reset a countdown to a configured number of bars; decrement it on subsequent bars without another range-breaking bar, and clear the direction when it reaches zero. Range context = prior range width <= an ATR multiple AND overlap fraction >= a threshold AND range efficiency <= a threshold AND countdown = 0. Wide up channel = uptrend AND prior range width >= an ATR multiple AND overlap fraction >= a threshold; wide down channel = downtrend with the same width and overlap tests. Always-in long = uptrend AND close > EMA AND range context is false; always-in short = downtrend AND close < EMA AND range context is false.
Calculation: A level is touched when low <= level + buffer AND high >= level − buffer. Key-level touch = touch of previous session high, low or close, current session open, or prior range high or low using a key ATR buffer, OR touch of EMA using a separate EMA ATR buffer. Manual levels cannot be reproduced from the permitted data fields.
Calculation: A confirmed pivot low or high has a configured equal number of bars on each side. Store the last three confirmed pivot lows and highs. A double bottom or top is a newly confirmed pivot within an ATR tolerance of the preceding same-side pivot. A downward wedge has three successive confirmed pivot lows strictly decreasing; an upward wedge has three successive confirmed pivot highs strictly increasing. A bottom head-and-shoulders has its middle pivot low below both others and its outer pivot lows within an ATR tolerance; a top head-and-shoulders has its middle pivot high above both others and its outer pivot highs within that tolerance. Wedges and head-and-shoulders are recognized only when the newest pivot confirms.
Calculation: On a newly confirmed double bottom, downward wedge or bottom head-and-shoulders, store its pivot bar as the latest bullish structure; similarly store the latest bearish structure for a double top, upward wedge or top head-and-shoulders. A structure is ready when the current bar follows its pivot bar by no more than a configured confirmation window. Clear the corresponding stored structure after a key-level reversal decision in that direction. An inside bar has high < previous high AND low > previous low; an outside bar has high > previous high AND low < previous low.
Calculation: Two-leg down = low < low two bars ago AND previous high > high two bars ago; two-leg up = high > high two bars ago AND previous low < low two bars ago. Impulse midpoint = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over an independent impulse lookback including the current bar.
Calculation: A first upside break is an active bar with close > prior range high AND open <= prior range high AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold AND prior range width >= an ATR multiple. A first downside break uses close < prior range low AND open >= prior range low with the same body and width tests. Each first break replaces the stored same-direction break bar and level. A break-fail-break is ready when a later close crosses back beyond that stored level against the break and the current bar remains within a configured window of the first break; clear it after its directional decision or expiration. A new same-direction first break resets this sequence.
Calculation: A true break is a first break that is strong in its direction AND is not a doji. Each true break replaces the stored trap direction, bar and level, and clears its follow-through and used flags. On the immediately following bar, require a non-doji strong bar in the break direction whose close advances beyond the previous close; otherwise clear the trap. Clear the trap when its configured window expires. A trap entry can occur only once per true break, after the follow-through bar.
Calculation: Store the low and bar index of each strong bullish bar in an always-in long context, and the high and bar index of each strong bearish bar in an always-in short context; each new qualifying bar replaces the previous same-direction value.
Calculation: Reset EMA-pullback state and its used flags at a new session. On entering always-in long from a different state, set state long and clear both used flags; on entering always-in short from a different state, set state short and clear both used flags. When neither always-in condition holds and range context holds, set the state neutral. After an EMA-pullback decision, set its side's used flag until the next applicable reset.
Long: Always-in long is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Short: Always-in short is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Long: Active bar AND previous bar was in an uptrend AND current bar is not in a wide up channel AND two-leg down AND bullish bar AND no strong bearish bar among the current and preceding three bars.
Short: Active bar AND previous bar was in a downtrend AND current bar is not in a wide down channel AND two-leg up AND bearish bar AND no strong bullish bar among the current and preceding three bars.
Long: Upside break-fail-break is ready AND close > its stored level AND strong bullish bar.
Short: Downside break-fail-break is ready AND close < its stored level AND strong bearish bar.
Long: Active bar AND key-level touch AND ((bullish structure is ready AND (strong bullish bar OR bullish reversal bar) AND close > previous high) OR ((previous bar is inside OR outside) AND bullish reversal bar AND close > previous high) OR the trend-pullback long condition).
Short: Active bar AND key-level touch AND ((bearish structure is ready AND (strong bearish bar OR bearish reversal bar) AND close < previous low) OR ((previous bar is inside OR outside) AND bearish reversal bar AND close < previous low) OR the trend-pullback short condition).
Long: Active bar AND range context AND low <= prior range low + key ATR buffer AND (bullish reversal bar OR close > prior range low).
Short: Active bar AND range context AND high >= prior range high − key ATR buffer AND (bearish reversal bar OR close < prior range high).
Long: Active bar AND wide down channel on the newly confirmed pivot's bar AND newly confirmed downward wedge AND key-level touch on the current bar.
Short: Active bar AND wide up channel on the newly confirmed pivot's bar AND newly confirmed upward wedge AND key-level touch on the current bar.
Long: Active bar AND uptrend AND range context is false AND impulse midpoint is touched using the key ATR buffer AND (two-leg down OR current consecutive bearish-bar count <= a threshold) AND current consecutive strong-bearish-bar count < a threshold.
Short: Active bar AND downtrend AND range context is false AND impulse midpoint is touched using the key ATR buffer AND (two-leg up OR current consecutive bullish-bar count <= the same threshold) AND current consecutive strong-bullish-bar count < the same threshold.
Long: Stored true-break direction is up AND its follow-through passed AND its entry is unused AND current bar follows the follow-through bar AND its stored level is touched using the current key ATR buffer AND close > that level AND current bar is not a doji.
Short: Stored true-break direction is down AND its follow-through passed AND its entry is unused AND current bar follows the follow-through bar AND its stored level is touched using the current key ATR buffer AND close < that level AND current bar is not a doji.
Long: Active bar AND always-in long AND current bar follows the stored always-in strong-bullish bar AND low <= its stored low AND close > its stored low AND the current and preceding two bars are not all bearish.
Short: Active bar AND always-in short AND current bar follows the stored always-in strong-bearish bar AND high >= its stored high AND close < its stored high AND the current and preceding two bars are not all bullish.
Long: Active bar AND previous bar was always-in long AND current and previous bars are bearish AND the bar before them is not bearish.
Short: Active bar AND previous bar was always-in short AND current and previous bars are bullish AND the bar before them is not bullish.
Long: Active bar AND EMA-pullback state is long AND its long flag is unused AND low <= EMA + EMA ATR buffer AND close >= EMA − EMA ATR buffer AND current bar is not strong bearish.
Short: Active bar AND EMA-pullback state is short AND its short flag is unused AND high >= EMA − EMA ATR buffer AND close <= EMA + EMA ATR buffer AND current bar is not strong bullish.
Long: Active bar AND always-in long AND bullish bar AND close >= low + a threshold fraction of bar range AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold.
Short: Active bar AND always-in short AND bearish bar AND close <= high − the same threshold fraction of bar range AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold.
Exit: long, previous bar was in an uptrend AND current bar is strong bearish AND close < current impulse midpoint.
Exit: short, previous bar was in a downtrend AND current bar is strong bullish AND close > current impulse midpoint.
Exit: long, stored true-break direction is up AND its follow-through passed AND close < its stored level AND strong bearish bar.
Exit: short, stored true-break direction is down AND its follow-through passed AND close > its stored level AND strong bullish bar.
Exit: long, previous bar was strong bearish AND previous close < previous EMA AND current bar is bearish AND close < EMA AND close < previous close.
Exit: short, previous bar was strong bullish AND previous close > previous EMA AND current bar is bullish AND close > EMA AND close > previous close.
Exit: long, uptrend AND current and preceding two bars are bearish.
Exit: short, downtrend AND current and preceding two bars are bullish.
