Calculation: Reference line = linear-regression fitted value at the current bar from close over a lookback, optionally smoothed with an EMA or SMA.
Calculation: Tether = (close - reference line) / ATR; use zero when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Tether rank = percentile rank of absolute tether over a lookback; use zero when unavailable.
Calculation: Directional cooldown = ready initially; set the corresponding direction to not ready on an entry signal, and set both directions to ready whenever tether rank is below the cooldown threshold.
Calculation: Initial stop = reference line on the entry signal bar minus an ATR buffer for a trend-following long, or plus the buffer for a trend-following short; for mean reversion, additionally subtract an ATR rung distance for a long or add it for a short.
Calculation: Target = entry signal bar close plus the risk-reward multiplier times (entry signal bar close - initial stop) for a long, or minus the multiplier times (initial stop - entry signal bar close) for a short; use separate multipliers for the two modes.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop, optionally updated for a long to the maximum of its previous value and the current reference line whenever close - entry price >= ATR, or for a short to the minimum of its previous value and the current reference line whenever entry price - close >= ATR.
Long: Mean-reversion mode AND position is flat AND tether < zero AND tether rank >= mean-reversion entry threshold AND tether rank < previous tether rank AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by volume threshold) AND (cooldown filter disabled OR long cooldown is ready).
Short: Mean-reversion mode AND position is flat AND tether > zero AND tether rank >= mean-reversion entry threshold AND tether rank < previous tether rank AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by volume threshold) AND (cooldown filter disabled OR short cooldown is ready).
Long: Trend-following mode AND position is flat AND tether crosses above zero AND tether rank < trend-following entry threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by volume threshold) AND (cooldown filter disabled OR long cooldown is ready).
Short: Trend-following mode AND position is flat AND tether crosses below zero AND tether rank < trend-following entry threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by volume threshold) AND (cooldown filter disabled OR short cooldown is ready).
Long: tether < zero AND tether rank >= extreme threshold for at least the required consecutive bars.
Short: tether > zero AND tether rank >= extreme threshold for at least the required consecutive bars.
Exit: long, (mean-reversion mode AND tether crosses above zero) OR (trend-following mode AND tether crosses below zero) OR low <= trailing stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, (mean-reversion mode AND tether crosses below zero) OR (trend-following mode AND tether crosses above zero) OR high >= trailing stop OR low <= target.
