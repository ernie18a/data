Calculation: High sweep status for each of the three correlated markets and each prior bar in the configured lookback = invalid if any intervening bar’s high exceeds that prior bar’s high; otherwise swept if the still-forming comparison-timeframe bar’s high exceeds the prior bar’s high, and unswept otherwise. Use the same lookback offsets for all three markets. The comparison timeframe may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower; use its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Low sweep status for each of the three correlated markets and each prior bar in the configured lookback = invalid if any intervening bar’s low is below that prior bar’s low; otherwise swept if the still-forming comparison-timeframe bar’s low is below the prior bar’s low, and unswept otherwise. Use the same lookback offsets for all three markets. The comparison timeframe may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower; use its still-forming bar.
Long: For any prior bar in the configured lookback, all three low sweep statuses are valid and exactly one market’s status differs from the other two.
Short: For any prior bar in the configured lookback, all three high sweep statuses are valid and exactly one market’s status differs from the other two.
