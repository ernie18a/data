Calculation: Squeeze momentum = sum for q = 0..19 of (q - 9.5) × (close[q] - (highest(high, 20)[q] + lowest(low, 20)[q]) / 2)
Calculation: Weis Wave signed volume = cumulative volume within a consecutive run of rising or falling closes, signed positive for rising and negative for falling; on unchanged closes, retain the prior run's signed volume
Calculation: Long confluence score = 1(close > fast EMA) + 1(RSI > 55) + 1(MACD histogram > 0) + 1(SuperTrend direction positive) + 1(CMF > 0)
Calculation: Short confluence score = 1(close < fast EMA) + 1(RSI < 45) + 1(MACD histogram < 0) + 1(SuperTrend direction negative) + 1(CMF < 0)

Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR × stop multiplier OR (trailing enabled AND high since entry >= entry price + ATR × trail multiplier AND low <= highest high since entry - ATR × trail multiplier) OR (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier)
