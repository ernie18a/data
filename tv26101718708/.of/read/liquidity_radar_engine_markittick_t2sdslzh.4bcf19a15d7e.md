Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low equal to the highest/lowest value across a centered lookback window, confirmed after the window’s later bars complete.
Calculation: Average candle body = simple moving average of absolute close minus open.
Long: Current low < previous-day low AND current close > previous-day low; within the configured CISD window after that sweep, current close crosses above the most recent confirmed swing high (previous close ≤ that swing high); AND, when displacement is required, current body > average candle body × configured multiplier OR current low > high two bars earlier; AND, when enabled, each configured RSI, divergence, session, relative-volume, trend, delta, ADX, volatility, correlation, and news filter passes; AND, when the FVG entry model is enabled, current low > high two bars earlier.
Short: Current high > previous-day high AND current close < previous-day high; within the configured CISD window after that sweep, current close crosses below the most recent confirmed swing low (previous close ≥ that swing low); AND, when displacement is required, current body > average candle body × configured multiplier OR current high < low two bars earlier; AND, when enabled, each configured RSI, divergence, session, relative-volume, trend, delta, ADX, volatility, correlation, and news filter passes; AND, when the FVG entry model is enabled, current high < low two bars earlier.
Exit: Long, close < stop price OR low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ third profit target.
Exit: Short, close > stop price OR high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ third profit target.
