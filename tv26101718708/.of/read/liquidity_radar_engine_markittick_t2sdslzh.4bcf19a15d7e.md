Calculation: Previous-period high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Swing high or low = the high or low N chart bars ago if it equals the highest high or lowest low, respectively, over a window of 2N+1 bars ending N bars ago; confirm it on the current bar. Store the latest confirmed swing and the RSI and MACD line at its bar.
Calculation: Sweep state = a high sweep occurs when high > previous-period high AND close < previous-period high; a low sweep occurs when low < previous-period low AND close > previous-period low. Each sweep activates its side and replaces the shared sweep bar and sweep price with its bar and high or low; process the high sweep before the low sweep. An executed long clears the low-sweep state, an executed short clears the high-sweep state, and both states clear when the shared sweep bar is older than twice the shift window.
Calculation: Bullish shift = low-sweep state is active AND bars since the shared sweep bar <= shift window AND close > latest swing high AND previous close <= current latest swing high AND either displacement filtering is disabled OR absolute candle body > body-size SMA times a multiplier OR low > high two bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish shift = high-sweep state is active AND bars since the shared sweep bar <= shift window AND close < latest swing low AND previous close >= current latest swing low AND either displacement filtering is disabled OR absolute candle body > body-size SMA times a multiplier OR high < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = shared sweep price < latest swing low AND current RSI > RSI at that swing low, or current MACD line > MACD line at that swing low, or both, according to the selected divergence source. Bearish divergence = shared sweep price > latest swing high AND current RSI < RSI at that swing high, or current MACD line < MACD line at that swing high, or both, according to the selected divergence source.
Calculation: Volume delta = volume times (close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish signal = bullish shift AND optional filters: RSI > threshold AND RSI > previous RSI; bullish divergence; bar time inside the selected session; sweep-bar volume / sweep-bar volume SMA >= threshold when that ratio is available and its denominator is positive; close >= trend EMA; volume delta > 0; ADX > threshold AND +DI > -DI; ATR / ATR SMA >= threshold, using a ratio of one when the ATR SMA is not positive; bar time outside the selected blackout. Each filter applies only when enabled. The previous executed long must be more than a cooldown number of bars ago, or absent.
Calculation: Bearish signal = bearish shift AND optional filters: RSI < threshold AND RSI < previous RSI; bearish divergence; bar time inside the selected session; sweep-bar volume / sweep-bar volume SMA >= threshold when that ratio is available and its denominator is positive; close <= trend EMA; volume delta < 0; ADX > threshold AND -DI > +DI; ATR / ATR SMA >= threshold, using a ratio of one when the ATR SMA is not positive; bar time outside the selected blackout. Each filter applies only when enabled. The previous executed short must be more than a cooldown number of bars ago, or absent.
Calculation: Executed entry = a bullish or bearish signal with entry at close, or, when the gap entry model is enabled, a bullish signal AND low > high two bars ago with entry at that earlier high, or a bearish signal AND high < low two bars ago with entry at that earlier low. Entry must differ from the shared sweep price. Initial stop = shared sweep price; risk = absolute entry minus initial stop. Long targets are entry plus risk times their respective multipliers; short targets are entry minus risk times their respective multipliers. The third target uses the longest target multiplier. With dynamic targets enabled, replace the first target with the latest swing high for a long when above entry, or latest swing low for a short when below entry; replace the second target with the previous-period high for a long when above entry, or previous-period low for a short when below entry.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop until updated after the exit checks. Once the first target has been reached, optionally move the stop to entry if that improves it; optionally trail it using the current high minus ATR times a multiplier for a long or current low plus ATR times a multiplier for a short, the latest swing low for a long or swing high for a short, or the highest high over a lookback minus ATR times a multiplier for a long or lowest low over that lookback plus ATR times a multiplier for a short. Apply only updates that improve the stop.
Long: Bullish signal.
Short: Bearish signal.
Long: close > trend EMA OR RSI > momentum midpoint when the corresponding bias or RSI display is selected.
Short: close < trend EMA OR RSI < momentum midpoint when the corresponding bias or RSI display is selected.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close <= its EMA.
Exit: long, bearish signal.
Exit: short, bullish signal.
Exit: long, close < managed stop OR low <= managed stop OR high >= third target.
Exit: short, close > managed stop OR high >= managed stop OR low <= third target.
Exit: long, partial profit-taking enabled AND high >= first target OR partial profit-taking enabled AND high >= second target AND the second target is enabled.
Exit: short, partial profit-taking enabled AND low <= first target OR partial profit-taking enabled AND low <= second target AND the second target is enabled.
