Calculation: Adaptive trailing stop = initialize to close; thereafter let previous stop be S, distance D = ATR × sensitivity × (1 + momentum gain × abs((close − close N bars ago) / close)) × clamp(volume / SMA(volume), lower volume limit, upper volume limit). Use volume ratio 1 when its SMA is zero and momentum 0 when close is zero. If current and previous closes are both above S, stop = max(S, close − D); if both are below S, stop = min(S, close + D); otherwise stop = close − D when close > S, else close + D.
Calculation: Adjusted zero-lag average = weighted sum of HMA(source) + (EMA(shorter length, source) − EMA(longer length, source)) × prediction factor and EMA(shorter length, source), plus an offset.
Calculation: Adaptive trend bands = source ± volatility × multiplier × (base factor + correlation factor × abs(correlation(close, bar index))) × sqrt(max(volume floor, volume / SMA(volume))); use a fallback correlation magnitude when unavailable and volume ratio 1 when its average is zero. Volatility is ATR or SMA(true range). Ratchet the lower band upward when close is above its previous value and the upper band downward when close is below its previous value. Initialize direction from close relative to source; switch to long when previous direction is short and close exceeds the previous upper band, and to short when previous direction is long and close falls below the previous lower band.
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close − open); candle range = high − low + price adjustment; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Initial stop = at the entry bar, select the nearest available level on the protective side of entry from the adaptive trailing stop, the adaptive trend band when its direction agrees, anchored VWAP when active, prior-bar Donchian boundary when enabled otherwise the current rolling low or high, and long moving average. If none qualifies, use entry minus or plus an ATR-based fallback distance. Add an ATR-based protective buffer and optional price-unit buffer away from entry, then enforce an ATR-based minimum risk distance. Hold the result fixed until the next adaptive trailing-stop entry.
