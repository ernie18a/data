Calculation: Directional bias = clamp(0.38 × range pressure + 0.27 × EMA bias + 0.20 × candle pressure + 0.15 × midpoint bias, -1, 1). Range pressure = 2 × clamp((close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low), 0, 1) - 1, using the compression lookback including the current bar. Candle pressure = 2 × (close - low) / (high - low) - 1. EMA bias = 1 when the shorter close EMA is above the longer close EMA AND the longer EMA is above its value at a prior slope lookback; -1 when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise 0. Midpoint bias = clamp((recent range midpoint - older range midpoint) / ATR, -1, 1), where the recent and immediately preceding older ranges each cover approximately half the compression lookback. Use a minimum positive price increment for zero price ranges and ATR; if the prior EMA is unavailable, use the current EMA.
Long: Directional bias >= positive bias threshold
Short: Directional bias <= negative bias threshold of equal magnitude
