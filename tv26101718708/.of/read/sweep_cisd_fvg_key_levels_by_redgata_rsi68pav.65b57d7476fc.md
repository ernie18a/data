Calculation: Log midpoint of a bar = exponentiate the midpoint of the longer log-price wick if that wick is longer than the log-price body; otherwise exponentiate the midpoint of log(high) and log(low). The upper wick runs from max(open, close) to high; the lower wick runs from low to min(open, close).
Calculation: Outside rejection of X relative to Y = X.high > Y.high AND X.low < Y.low AND X.close > Y.low AND X.close < Y.high.
Calculation: Bullish wick-lick setup = A.close > log midpoint of A AND A is not an outside rejection of B AND (A.low < B.low AND A.close > B.low OR B.low < C.low AND A.close > min(B.open, B.close) AND (B.close <= log midpoint of B OR B.close <= C.low OR B is an outside rejection of C) OR A.low < log midpoint of B AND A.low > B.open AND A.close > B.high).
Calculation: Bearish wick-lick setup = A.close < log midpoint of A AND A is not an outside rejection of B AND (A.high > B.high AND A.close < B.high OR B.high > C.high AND A.close < max(B.open, B.close) AND (B.close >= log midpoint of B OR B.close >= C.high OR B is an outside rejection of C) OR A.high > log midpoint of B AND A.high < B.open AND A.close < B.low).
Calculation: A wick-lick level is A.close when its setup occurs; retain the most recent setup and its direction. On each chart bar, replace the bullish touch with the current bar when low < level OR open < level, AND close > level; replace the bearish touch with the current bar when high > level OR open > level, AND close < level. Reset the touch when a new setup occurs or its confirmation occurs.
Calculation: A chart-bar pivot high or low is confirmed after a configurable number of bars on each side. In wick mode use the high or low pivot. In body mode a high pivot exists when open is a pivot high OR both close and high are pivot highs; its value is max(open, close) at the pivot bar. A low pivot exists when open is a pivot low OR both close and low are pivot lows; its value is min(open, close) at the pivot bar. Retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind.
