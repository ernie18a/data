Calculation: Logarithmic candle midpoint = for a candle, take the midpoint of its high and low in log-price space, except when the larger wick exceeds the body: use the midpoint of the longer wick in log-price space; convert back to price.
Calculation: Full-range sweep-and-reclaim = high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close is between the previous low and previous high.
Calculation: Body pivot high/low = a 2-left, 2-right pivot in open or close; for a high, prefer the open pivot, otherwise use the close pivot only when close and high are both pivots; use the pivot candle's higher open/close as the level. For a low, use the corresponding low pivots and the pivot candle's lower open/close.
Long: Higher-timeframe last completed candle close > the previous completed candle high OR (last completed candle low < previous completed candle low AND last completed candle close > previous completed candle low AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim AND last completed candle close > its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (previous completed candle low < the candle before it low AND last completed candle close > min(previous candle open, close) AND (previous candle close <= its Logarithmic candle midpoint OR previous candle close <= the candle before it low OR previous candle is a Full-range sweep-and-reclaim) AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim on the last completed candle AND last completed candle close > its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (last completed candle low < previous completed candle Logarithmic candle midpoint AND last completed candle low > previous completed candle open AND last completed candle close > previous completed candle high AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim AND last completed candle close > its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (an active bullish T-spot has been touched by a bar whose low or open is below its upper boundary and whose close is above that boundary, and a later bar has open < the latest body pivot high and close > that pivot high, with the pivot formed before the touch and its level above the T-spot touch level) OR (the last completed higher-timeframe candle swept below the prior candle low and closed above that low, with no sweep above the prior high).
Short: Higher-timeframe last completed candle close < the previous completed candle low OR (last completed candle high > previous completed candle high AND last completed candle close < previous completed candle high AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim AND last completed candle close < its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (previous completed candle high > the candle before it high AND last completed candle close < max(previous candle open, close) AND (previous candle close >= its Logarithmic candle midpoint OR previous candle close >= the candle before it high OR previous candle is a Full-range sweep-and-reclaim) AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim on the last completed candle AND last completed candle close < its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (last completed candle high > previous completed candle Logarithmic candle midpoint AND last completed candle high < previous completed candle open AND last completed candle close < previous completed candle low AND NOT Full-range sweep-and-reclaim AND last completed candle close < its Logarithmic candle midpoint) OR (an active bearish T-spot has been touched by a bar whose high or open is above its lower boundary and whose close is below that boundary, and a later bar has open > the latest body pivot low and close < that pivot low, with the pivot formed before the touch and its level below the T-spot touch level) OR (the last completed higher-timeframe candle swept above the prior candle high and closed below that high, with no sweep below the prior low) OR (both prior-candle extremes were swept by the last completed higher-timeframe candle and its lower wick is longer than its upper wick).
