Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by volume since the active session reset
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the active session’s opening-range period
Calculation: Directional score = weighted sum of its listed true conditions; each condition’s weight and the minimum score are configurable
Calculation: 15-minute long score = directional score of (completed higher-timeframe close > fast EMA > slow EMA and close > KAMA), (fast EMA rising), (efficiency ratio above its minimum), (ATR / its longer-lookback ATR average above its minimum), and (chart close > active session VWAP); short score uses the mirrored inequalities
Calculation: 5-minute long score = directional score of (completed higher-timeframe close > fast EMA > slow EMA and close > KAMA), (fast EMA rising), (chart close > active session VWAP), (chart close above opening-range high with recent chart closes accepted above it, or simply above it), (absolute close change on the completed higher-timeframe bar >= a fraction of its ATR), and (chart EMA rising); short score uses the mirrored inequalities
Calculation: Matrix long = long 15-minute score meets its minimum and exceeds its short score, and long 5-minute score meets its minimum and exceeds its short score; Matrix short is the mirrored condition
Calculation: Bull impulse = close > open, body / (high - low) meets its minimum, and close is in the upper portion of the bar; bear impulse is mirrored
Calculation: Core long stalk = Matrix long and any of: price above active VWAP with low near VWAP; price above execution KAMA with low near KAMA; price above opening-range high with low near that high; a tight recent higher-timeframe or chart range with close near its high; or a downside sweep beyond prior-session low, active-session low, or opening-range low followed by close back above that level. Core short stalk is mirrored using highs and upside sweeps
Calculation: Core long trigger = (VWAP/KAMA pullback or opening-range retest stalk and bull impulse and (close > prior high or close > execution EMA)) OR (compression stalk and close > prior compression high and bull impulse) OR (failed-auction stalk and bull impulse and close > execution EMA); core short trigger is mirrored
Calculation: MicroPulse trail = adaptive trailing line using chart close and ATR; its direction is long when close is above the line and short when close is below it
Calculation: MicroPulse long stack = close > fast EMA > slow EMA, close > KAMA, and fast EMA rising; short stack is mirrored
Calculation: MicroPulse long patterns = (P1: close near or above the prior pulse-lookback high; trigger requires close above it, bull impulse, and enough bullish bars in the pulse lookback) OR (P2: low pulls back near the higher of fast EMA and KAMA, close above both, bull impulse, and close > prior high) OR (P3: prior pulse-lookback range is tight, close near its high; trigger requires close above it and bull impulse); short patterns are mirrored
Calculation: MicroPulse context score = directional score of Matrix agreement, matching trail direction, matching EMA/KAMA stack, adequate efficiency, adequate relative volume, extension within the chase limit, at least one matching pattern stalk, opening-range acceptance in that direction, and a matching trail flip
Calculation: MicroPulse score = context score plus configured points for each triggered pattern and for enough same-side pulse bars
Calculation: Core stop = lowest low (long) or highest high (short) over the core stop lookback, buffered by ATR; core target = nearest mapped level beyond the minimum ATR distance in the trade direction, or an ATR-based fallback; core risk filter requires correctly sided stop and target, minimum reward/risk, and maximum stop width in ATR
Calculation: MicroPulse stop = the tighter of the pulse trail and recent structural extreme, buffered by pulse ATR; targets are risk-multiple targets, with the second target capped by the nearest mapped level when present; micro risk filter requires correctly sided stop and targets, maximum stop width, and minimum risk score
Long: Long command (15-minute long score >= its minimum and > short score) OR long structure (5-minute long score >= its minimum and > short score) OR long setup armed (core or MicroPulse long stalk, its minimum score, and risk filter) OR long entry (core long trigger with core score >= minimum and core risk filter, or MicroPulse long pattern trigger with MicroPulse score >= fire minimum and micro risk filter); entry/armed signals also require Matrix agreement where specified, trading/session/time permission, completed-session warm-up, eligible chart type/timeframe, no active cooldown or quota lock, no active pulse ride, and bar confirmation when enabled
Short: Short command (15-minute short score >= its minimum and > long score) OR short structure (5-minute short score >= its minimum and > long score) OR short setup armed (core or MicroPulse short stalk, its minimum score, and risk filter) OR short entry (core short trigger with core score >= minimum and core risk filter, or MicroPulse short pattern trigger with MicroPulse score >= fire minimum and micro risk filter); entry/armed signals also require Matrix agreement where specified, trading/session/time permission, completed-session warm-up, eligible chart type/timeframe, no active cooldown or quota lock, no active pulse ride, and bar confirmation when enabled
Exit: Long, close < pulse trail OR close < MicroPulse KAMA OR trail direction turns short OR configured maximum pulse-ride bars reached
Exit: Short, close > pulse trail OR close > MicroPulse KAMA OR trail direction turns long OR configured maximum pulse-ride bars reached
