Calculation: Direction notation = d is +1 for long and -1 for short; directional comparisons such as d × a > d × b reverse when d is -1. Evaluate all chart-timeframe calculations on each completed chart bar. Inputs for lookbacks, thresholds, score weights, session windows, price increment and optional features are parameters.
Calculation: Session map = Classify bars by New York time into regular, overnight and post-regular sessions. Reset regular-session high, low and volume-weighted HLC3 at the regular open; reset overnight high and low and the continuous-session volume-weighted HLC3 at the overnight open. Freeze overnight high and low at the regular open and prior regular-session high and low at the regular close. Continue the continuous-session high, low and VWAP through regular and post-regular trading. For the active session, use regular VWAP and its volume-weighted HLC3 standard deviation during regular hours, otherwise continuous-session VWAP and standard deviation. Build each session's opening-range high and low from its first configured interval; the range is ready after that interval. The active session extreme is the frozen overnight extreme during regular hours and the continuous-session running extreme otherwise; for a sweep outside regular hours use the previous bar's running extreme.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Directional candle = d × (close - open) > 0 AND absolute candle body / (high - low) >= the applicable body threshold AND d × close >= d × the directional extreme minus the permitted fraction of the candle range; use body ratio zero when high equals low. The directional extreme is high for long and low for short. Core and pulse candles have separate body thresholds.
Calculation: Opening-range acceptance = the range is ready AND d × close > d × the directional opening-range boundary AND every close in the acceptance lookback is beyond that boundary in direction d after allowing the configured execution-ATR buffer. The directional boundary is the range high for long and range low for short.
Calculation: Command score for d = the weighted sum of these conditions: last completed higher-timeframe close, fast EMA, slow EMA and KAMA are directionally stacked as close beyond fast EMA beyond slow EMA and close beyond KAMA; the last completed fast EMA slopes in direction d versus its preceding higher-timeframe bar; the last completed higher-timeframe efficiency ratio meets its threshold; its ATR divided by the SMA of that ATR meets its threshold; chart close is beyond active VWAP in direction d. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Structure score for d = the weighted sum of these conditions: last completed shorter higher-timeframe close, fast EMA, slow EMA and KAMA have the same directional stack; its fast EMA slopes in direction d versus its preceding bar; chart close is beyond active VWAP in direction d; opening-range acceptance holds, otherwise chart close is beyond the ready range boundary in direction d with a smaller weight; the absolute change of that higher-timeframe close from its preceding close meets a multiple of its ATR; chart-timeframe EMA slopes in direction d. This higher timeframe is shorter than the command timeframe but higher than the chart timeframe; both use their last completed bars.
Calculation: Matrix direction d = command score for d >= its threshold AND command score for d > command score for -d AND structure score for d >= its threshold AND structure score for d > structure score for -d.
Calculation: Pulse trail = initialize to close minus a multiple of pulse ATR. Thereafter, if current and previous close are both above the previous trail, use max(previous trail, close minus the ATR multiple); if both are below it, use min(previous trail, close plus the ATR multiple); otherwise use close minus the ATR multiple when close is above the previous trail, and close plus the ATR multiple otherwise. Trail direction is the sign of close minus trail, retaining the preceding direction on equality.
Calculation: Core setup components for d = pullback when matrix direction is d AND close is beyond active VWAP or execution KAMA in direction d AND the bar's opposite extreme reaches within the respective ATR tolerance; range retest when matrix direction is d AND the range is ready AND close is beyond its directional boundary AND the opposite extreme reaches within its ATR buffer; compression when matrix direction is d AND either the last completed shorter higher-timeframe range or the chart range over the preceding compression lookback is at most its ATR width limit AND close is within the configured buffer of the preceding chart range's directional extreme; failed auction when enabled AND the bar sweeps the prior regular-session, active-session or ready opening-range extreme opposite d by more than the ATR sweep buffer and closes back beyond that level in direction d AND the opposite matrix direction is absent AND either matrix direction is d or command score for d meets the failed-auction threshold. The higher-timeframe compression range includes its last completed bar; the chart compression range excludes the current bar.
Calculation: Core score for d = weighted sum of matrix direction, range retest, pullback, compression, failed auction, relative volume meeting its threshold, core directional candle, and opening-range acceptance; cap at the score maximum. Relative volume is volume divided by its chart-timeframe SMA.
Calculation: Core risk for d = round the lowest low for long or highest high for short over the core stop lookback, offset outward by an execution-ATR buffer, to the price increment. Select the nearest level beyond close by more than the minimum execution-ATR target distance from the prior regular-session directional extreme, active-session directional extreme, opening-range directional boundary, and active VWAP plus or minus each configured standard-deviation band; if none exists, use close plus d times a fallback execution-ATR distance. Round the target to the price increment. Risk is d × (close - stop); reward is d × (target - close).
Calculation: Pulse setup components for d = require matrix direction d, trail direction d, chart close beyond the pulse fast EMA and pulse KAMA in direction d, pulse fast EMA beyond the slower pulse EMA in direction d, and fast EMA slope in direction d. Ignition additionally requires close within the execution-ATR buffer of the directional extreme of the preceding pulse lookback; pullback requires the bar's opposite extreme to reach within an execution-ATR tolerance of the more directional of the fast EMA and KAMA, while close remains beyond both in direction d; compression additionally requires the preceding pulse-lookback range to be no wider than its execution-ATR limit and close within the execution-ATR buffer of its directional extreme. The pulse fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the pulse slow EMA.
Calculation: Pulse count for d = number of bars including the current bar in the pulse lookback with d × (close - open) > 0 AND body ratio >= the pulse-count body threshold.
Calculation: Pulse context score for d = weighted sum of matrix direction, trail direction, pulse EMA/KAMA stack and slope, pulse efficiency ratio meeting its threshold, relative volume meeting its pulse threshold, directional extension from the more directional of the fast EMA and KAMA divided by pulse ATR not exceeding its chase limit, any pulse setup component, opening-range acceptance, and a trail-direction flip to d; cap at the score maximum. Pulse fire score adds separate weights for each triggered pulse component and for pulse count meeting its threshold, then caps at the score maximum.
Calculation: Pulse risk for d = round the more directional of the trail and the lowest low for long or highest high for short over the shorter pulse stop lookback, offset opposite d by a pulse-ATR buffer, to the price increment. Risk is d × (close - stop). Target one is close plus d times risk times its target multiple. Target two is the less distant of close plus d times risk times its larger target multiple and the nearest level beyond close by more than a pulse-ATR distance from the prior regular-session directional extreme, active-session directional extreme, opening-range boundary, first directional VWAP deviation band and preceding pulse-lookback directional extreme; use the risk-multiple target if no level qualifies. Round both targets to the price increment. Pulse risk score is the weighted sum of stop distance divided by pulse ATR falling within the tighter or, with a smaller weight, wider stop limit; target-two reward/risk meeting the higher or, with a smaller weight, lower threshold; a qualifying mapped level existing or, with a smaller weight, not existing; and risk exceeding the price increment. Cap at the score maximum.
Calculation: Trading permission = selected regular or extended session AND configured session trade window AND opening range is not building AND chart timeframe does not exceed its configured limit when that limit is enabled AND the first complete applicable session has opened after the first dataset bar AND bars since the latest core or pulse fire >= cooldown AND fires in the active session < session quota AND no pulse ride was active at the start of the bar. Reset the quota at each enabled regular-session or overnight-session open.
