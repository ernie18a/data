Calculation: Pivots = confirmed symmetric swing highs and lows, each confirmed after its right-hand lookback; use independent lookbacks for sweeps and trend changes.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed sweep pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, positive price floor) > wick threshold. Bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed sweep pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, positive price floor) > wick threshold.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = on each completed bar, create a bullish zone from high two bars ago to current low when current low > that high and the gap meets a minimum price size; create a bearish zone from current high to low two bars ago when current high < that low and the gap meets the same minimum. Retain up to a configurable number of newest zones per direction. A bullish zone becomes a bearish inverted zone when close falls below its bottom; a bearish zone becomes a bullish inverted zone when close rises above its top. Remove an inverted bearish zone when high > its top, and an inverted bullish zone when low < its bottom. A bar overlaps a retained zone when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom.
Calculation: Order blocks = on a completed bar with close > prior high AND close - open > ATR times a displacement multiplier, create a bullish zone spanning the prior candle body if its size <= ATR times a size limit. Mirror these conditions for a bearish zone. Retain up to a configurable number of newest zones per direction; remove a bullish zone when close < bottom + zone size times (1 - mitigation fraction), or a bearish zone when close > top - zone size times (1 - mitigation fraction). A bar overlaps a retained zone when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom.
Calculation: Trend = initially neutral; switch to bullish when close > latest confirmed trend pivot high and the trend is not already bullish; then switch to bearish when close < latest confirmed trend pivot low and the trend is not already bearish. Apply the bullish test before the bearish test on each bar.
Calculation: Close-sequence confirmation = close > previous close AND previous close > the close before it for bullish, with inequalities reversed for bearish. Emit only on completed bars, alternating direction, with at least a configurable number of bars since the previous emitted confirmation.
Calculation: Premium and discount = the upper and lower quarters, respectively, of the rolling range from the lowest low to the highest high over a configurable lookback; the boundaries are excluded.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish pattern = close > open AND optional absolute candle-body displacement filter AND (enabled bullish sweep with close - latest sweep pivot low <= ATR times a maximum distance, or no pivot low available; OR overlap with an enabled bullish inverted gap; OR overlap with a bullish order block). Confirmed bearish pattern mirrors these comparisons using the latest sweep pivot high. The distance restriction applies only to the sweep branch.
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = sum of one point each for a confirmed bullish pattern, a bullish sweep within a configurable recent window, bullish trend, overlap with a bullish fair value gap or bullish inverted gap or bullish order block, a bullish close-sequence confirmation on this bar, close in discount, and close above the most recently recorded session-open price. Bearish confluence reverses direction and uses premium. Record the open of a completed bar whose New York local opening time matches the configured session-open time. Cross-instrument divergence is disabled because the supplied data contains only one instrument.
Long: low < latest confirmed sweep pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, positive price floor) > wick threshold
Short: high > latest confirmed sweep pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, positive price floor) > wick threshold
Long: close > top of a retained non-inverted bearish fair value gap
Short: close < bottom of a retained non-inverted bullish fair value gap
Long: bullish close-sequence confirmation is emitted
Short: bearish close-sequence confirmation is emitted
Long: trend changes to bullish
Short: trend changes to bearish
Long: confirmed bullish pattern AND bullish confluence count >= lower grade threshold AND bullish confluence count < higher grade threshold AND optional short ATR > long ATR times chop threshold AND bars since the last emitted same-side lower- or higher-grade signal > cooldown, or no such prior signal
Short: confirmed bearish pattern AND bearish confluence count >= lower grade threshold AND bearish confluence count < higher grade threshold AND optional short ATR > long ATR times chop threshold AND bars since the last emitted same-side lower- or higher-grade signal > cooldown, or no such prior signal
Long: confirmed bullish pattern AND bullish confluence count >= higher grade threshold AND optional enabled-session gate AND optional short ATR > long ATR times chop threshold AND optional volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND optional close outside premium AND bars since the last emitted same-side lower- or higher-grade signal > cooldown, or no such prior signal
Short: confirmed bearish pattern AND bearish confluence count >= higher grade threshold AND optional enabled-session gate AND optional short ATR > long ATR times chop threshold AND optional volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND optional close outside discount AND bars since the last emitted same-side lower- or higher-grade signal > cooldown, or no such prior signal
Exit: long, low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier times first-target multiplier
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier times first-target multiplier
