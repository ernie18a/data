Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the medium and slow EMAs.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: MACD = difference between fast and slow EMAs of close, with a signal EMA and histogram equal to MACD minus its signal EMA.
Calculation: +DI, -DI, ADX = directional movement indicators and average directional index.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: ATR = average true range.
Calculation: Bull breakout = close > highest high of the prior breakout lookback bars + ATR × breakout buffer; bear breakout = close < lowest low of the prior breakout lookback bars − ATR × breakout buffer. Raw breakouts omit the ATR buffer.
Calculation: MTF state = bullish when close > EMA(21), EMA(9) > EMA(21), and RSI > 50; bearish when close < EMA(21), EMA(9) < EMA(21), and RSI < 50; otherwise neutral. Evaluate the last available bar on each of three higher timeframes (short-, medium-, and long-term, in increasing duration); a bar still forming may be used.
Calculation: Bull Score and Bear Score = each direction’s weighted sum of trend, RSI, MACD, DMI/ADX, volume, breakout proximity, Bollinger volatility, MTF, and relative-strength contributions, divided by the maximum available weight and multiplied by 100. Bull trend contributions test close > EMA(21), EMA(9) > EMA(21), EMA(21) > EMA(50), EMA(50) > EMA(200), both EMA(9) and EMA(21) rising over a lookback, and a recent EMA(9)/EMA(21) bullish cross; bear contributions reverse these tests. RSI contributions test the directional RSI threshold and RSI change over a lookback. MACD contributions test line versus signal, histogram sign, and histogram change. DMI/ADX contributions test which DI leads, ADX above its threshold, and ADX rising. Volume contributions require a directional candle and relative-volume thresholds. Structure contributions reward a buffered breakout, raw breakout, or proximity within ATR multiples to the prior high/low. Volatility contributions test a recent Bollinger squeeze, band expansion, and close versus the basis. MTF contributions are proportional to the count of matching directional states. Relative-strength contributions test the price/benchmark ratio versus its EMA and its change over a lookback. Weights, thresholds, lookbacks, and multipliers are parameters.
Calculation: RSI divergence = at consecutive confirmed price pivot lows, the newer low is lower while its RSI is higher; at consecutive confirmed pivot highs, the newer high is higher while its RSI is lower. A divergence remains recent for a parameterized number of bars after its pivot.
Calculation: Retest level = prior breakout lookback high for a bullish breakout or prior lookback low for a bearish breakout, recorded when a buffered breakout first occurs.
Long: Bull Score crosses above the early-score threshold OR crosses above the setup-score threshold OR a bullish RUN condition first becomes true OR a bullish STRONG RUN condition first becomes true OR the bullish state increases to at least the early state after the signal cooldown, with signals restricted to completed chart bars when close-only confirmation is enabled; bullish RUN requires Bull Score ≥ run threshold, buffered bull breakout, relative volume ≥ run-volume threshold, and close > open and prior close; bullish STRONG RUN requires Bull Score ≥ strong threshold, buffered bull breakout, relative volume ≥ strong-volume threshold, +DI > -DI, and ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold; OR a bullish RSI divergence occurs; OR within the retest window after a bullish breakout, low ≤ retest level + ATR × retest tolerance, close ≥ retest level, close > open, and this held-retest condition was false on the prior bar.
Short: Bear Score crosses above the early-score threshold OR crosses above the setup-score threshold OR a bearish FALL condition first becomes true OR a bearish STRONG FALL condition first becomes true OR the bearish state increases to at least the early state after the signal cooldown, with signals restricted to completed chart bars when close-only confirmation is enabled; bearish FALL requires Bear Score ≥ run threshold, buffered bear breakout, relative volume ≥ run-volume threshold, and close < open and prior close; bearish STRONG FALL requires Bear Score ≥ strong threshold, buffered bear breakout, relative volume ≥ strong-volume threshold, -DI > +DI, and ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold; OR a bearish RSI divergence occurs; OR within the retest window after a bearish breakout, high ≥ retest level − ATR × retest tolerance, close ≤ retest level, close < open, and this held-retest condition was false on the prior bar.
