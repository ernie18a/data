Calculation: Bull and bear zones = On each of several higher timeframes, use the last completed bar. A bullish impulse has close > open, close > the highest high of the preceding impulse-lookback bars, and either absolute body or high-low range >= ATR multiplied by its respective displacement threshold. A bearish impulse reverses the price comparisons, using the lowest preceding low. For a bullish impulse, take the most recent bearish candle within a separate search lookback; for a bearish impulse, take the most recent bullish candle. Alternatively, take up to a parameterized maximum number of the most recent non-bullish or non-bearish candles respectively. The zone spans the selected candles' bodies or wicks, according to the range setting, and requires top > bottom. Each new valid impulse replaces that timeframe's current zone of its direction. Invalidate a bullish zone when the higher-timeframe close or low, according to the invalidation setting, falls below its bottom; invalidate a bearish zone when the higher-timeframe close or high exceeds its top.
Calculation: Stored zones = For each enabled higher timeframe and direction, store a valid current zone when its boundaries differ from the last stored boundaries for that timeframe and direction; retain the last-seen boundaries even after invalidation. Discard the oldest stored zone when the storage limit is exceeded. On each chart bar, invalidate a stored bullish zone when close or low, according to the same invalidation setting, falls below its bottom; invalidate a stored bearish zone when close or high exceeds its top. A zone is touched when high >= its bottom AND low <= its top. Mark a zone mitigated on a touch after its creation bar; mitigation does not invalidate it. A zone is visible when it is not invalidated AND either mitigated zones are enabled or it is not mitigated. When nearest-only labels are enabled, a visible zone is labeled only if it is the non-invalidated zone of its direction whose midpoint is nearest to close, with ties resolved in favor of the oldest stored zone.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < the lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback chart bars AND close > open AND the current bar touches a non-invalidated stored bullish zone. Bearish sweep = high > the highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback chart bars AND close < open AND the current bar touches a non-invalidated stored bearish zone. Remember the most recent sweep bar separately for each direction; replace it on a new sweep and clear it when its corresponding confirmation is displayed.
Long: A stored bullish zone is visible AND qualifies for a zone label.
Short: A stored bearish zone is visible AND qualifies for a zone label.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: The most recent bullish sweep is within the confirmation window AND occurred before the current bar AND close > the previous chart bar's high AND close > open AND the current bar touches a non-invalidated stored bullish zone.
Short: The most recent bearish sweep is within the confirmation window AND occurred before the current bar AND close < the previous chart bar's low AND close < open AND the current bar touches a non-invalidated stored bearish zone.
