Calculation: Valid price bar = open, high, low and close are present, high >= max(open, close), and low <= min(open, close).
Calculation: Directional impulse = for direction d, where d is +1 for long and -1 for short, the current bar and every bar in the preceding breakout lookback have valid prices, prior-bar ATR is available and positive, current high > low, d × (close - open) >= the impulse-body threshold × prior-bar ATR, and, for long, close > the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback and close >= high - an outer-range fraction × (high - low), or, for short, close < the lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback and close <= low + the outer-range fraction × (high - low). A fresh impulse is a directional impulse whose same-direction condition was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Episode state = initially idle. A fresh impulse starts an episode only when idle at the start of the bar and past the recovery-attempt cooldown; freeze the preceding bar's ATR, direction and seed bar. Set the origin to seed low minus an origin-padding fraction of frozen ATR for long, or seed high plus that padding for short. Set the running directional extreme to seed high for long or seed low for short. Ignore fresh impulses while an episode or plan is active. End an episode before other processing if its age reaches the setup expiry, current or previous required prices are missing, the current bar has invalid prices, or long low <= origin or short high >= origin. Do not start a new episode on the bar an episode or plan ends.
Calculation: Cohort means = from the seed onward, separately average HLC3 for bars whose close-to-close change agrees with or opposes the episode direction; include the seed in the agreeing cohort and exclude subsequent unchanged closes. Use volume-weighted means unless equal weighting is selected or any included bar has missing or nonpositive volume; in either case use equal-bar means for both cohorts for the entire episode. On a switch to equal weighting, clear readiness and prohibit recovery on that bar. The outer mean is the higher cohort mean for long and the lower for short; it is unavailable until both means exist.
Calculation: Pullback readiness = after testing recovery on each surviving episode bar, reset the pullback, adverse-bar count and readiness if the close exceeds the earlier running directional extreme in the episode direction. Otherwise, start a pullback on the first adverse close-to-close change, recording the earlier running extreme as its peak; count all adverse changes, including nonconsecutive ones, and track the lowest low for long or highest high for short from the first adverse bar onward. Update the running extreme with each bar's high for long or low for short. After adding the current bar to the cohort means, latch readiness when the adverse count >= its threshold, the directional distance from pullback peak to pullback extreme >= the depth fraction of frozen ATR, and the close is at or behind the current outer mean plus the directional recovery buffer. Readiness remains latched until a reset or weighting switch.
Calculation: Structural recovery = readiness was latched at the start of the bar, no weighting switch occurs on the bar, and long close > the preceding cohort outer mean plus the recovery-buffer fraction of frozen ATR AND close > previous high, or short close < the preceding cohort outer mean minus that buffer AND close < previous low. The first structural recovery consumes the episode whether accepted or rejected and starts the recovery-attempt cooldown.
Calculation: Recovery risk = on the structural recovery bar, extend the pullback extreme to include its low for long or high for short. The structural stop is that extreme minus the stop-padding fraction of frozen ATR for long or plus it for short. Risk is the directional distance from stop to signal close; chase is the directional distance from the preceding cohort outer mean to signal close. A recovery is accepted when risk > 0 AND risk <= the maximum-risk multiple of frozen ATR AND chase <= the maximum-chase multiple of frozen ATR.
Calculation: Plan state = an accepted recovery starts a plan at signal close with its structural stop and a target at a fixed reward-to-risk multiple of risk in the entry direction. Starting on the next bar, end the plan when the bar has invalid prices, its range reaches the stop or target, or its age reaches the plan observation limit. The plan blocks new episodes through its terminal bar.
Long: A fresh long impulse starts an episode.
Short: A fresh short impulse starts an episode.
Long: Episode direction is long AND structural recovery occurs AND recovery is accepted.
Short: Episode direction is short AND structural recovery occurs AND recovery is accepted.
