Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot, updating it before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low. Bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Bullish break = low > high two bars earlier AND close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high on the current bar or either of the preceding two bars AND the elapsed bars since the previous bullish break exceed the break cooldown. Bearish break = high < low two bars earlier AND close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low on the current bar or either of the preceding two bars AND the elapsed bars since the previous bearish break exceed the break cooldown. Initialize each previous-break bar index to the first bar index. A crossover compares each bar's close with that bar's latest confirmed pivot and requires the previous close to be at or below its corresponding pivot; a crossunder uses at or above.
Calculation: Active setup = each bullish or bearish break replaces the previous setup, resets its alert and retracement flags, records whether a same-direction sweep has occurred since the preceding break, and clears both sweep flags. A bullish setup's origin is the lowest low from the broken swing high's bar through the break bar; a bearish setup's origin is the highest high from the broken swing low's bar through the break bar. If that interval is outside the origin lookback limit, use the break bar's low or high respectively. Initialize the extreme to the break bar's high for bullish or low for bearish. Until a same-direction swing high or low is confirmed, including on the break bar, update the extreme to the highest high or lowest low since the break; then lock it. A bullish setup is invalidated when close < origin; a bearish setup is invalidated when close > origin.
Calculation: Retracement level = bullish extreme - (extreme - origin) × retracement fraction, or bearish extreme + (origin - extreme) × retracement fraction. For each setup, latch bullish retracement when low <= retracement level AND close >= origin; latch bearish retracement when high >= retracement level AND close <= origin. Evaluate the level after updating or locking the extreme.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: Bullish break.
Short: Bearish break.
Long: Active bullish setup AND no prior alert for that setup AND (sweep requirement disabled OR recorded bullish sweep) AND (break requirement disabled OR bullish break recorded for the setup) AND (retracement requirement disabled OR bullish retracement latched) AND (trend requirement disabled OR close > chart-timeframe EMA of close). Mark the setup alerted after this decision.
Short: Active bearish setup AND no prior alert for that setup AND (sweep requirement disabled OR recorded bearish sweep) AND (break requirement disabled OR bearish break recorded for the setup) AND (retracement requirement disabled OR bearish retracement latched) AND (trend requirement disabled OR close < chart-timeframe EMA of close). Mark the setup alerted after this decision.
Exit: Long, low <= bullish setup origin OR high >= bullish setup extreme.
Exit: Short, high >= bearish setup origin OR low <= bearish setup extreme.
