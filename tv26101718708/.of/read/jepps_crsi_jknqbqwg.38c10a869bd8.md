Calculation: cRSI = EMA of 2 × RSI(close, cycle-length) minus that RSI lagged by phase lag; cycle-length is half the dominant cycle length, and the EMA period is the vibration period
Calculation: cRSI moving average = selected moving average of cRSI over its lookback
Long: cRSI crosses above its moving average
Short: cRSI crosses below its moving average
