Calculation: Direction-normalized prices = multiply each OHLC price and each configured nonzero level by +1 for a long decision or -1 for a short decision; after multiplication, swap high and low if necessary. In this coordinate system every decision is bullish. A same-side level is configured for the decision direction; an opposite-side level is configured for the other direction.
Calculation: Zone = the interval around a configured level whose half-width is a configurable price distance or a configurable fraction of the level. A bar touches the zone when its high is at least the zone bottom and its low is at most the zone top. Excursion distance is the zone half-width multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or one-half when high equals low. Bullish confirmation means close position is at least the upper-quartile boundary OR close position is at least the midpoint AND close > open.
Calculation: Merged bar = previous chart bar's open, maximum of previous and current highs, minimum of previous and current lows, and current close.
Calculation: Developing point of control = the close of the most recent highest-volume source bar within the current higher-timeframe interval. The source bars are chart-timeframe bars or lower-timeframe bars, depending on the chart; use completed source bars through the current chart close. Developing delta = sum of source-bar volume for bullish bars minus volume for bearish bars over that interval. For higher-timeframe OHLC and volume, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar at a historical chart-bar close; use the bar still forming in real time.
Calculation: Optional bias score = the number of these four conditions that pass: the session filter is disabled OR the chart bar is inside an enabled configured session; the higher-timeframe direction filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe close > open; the delta filter is disabled OR developing delta > 0 OR neutral delta is allowed AND absolute developing delta divided by higher-timeframe volume is at most the configured neutral threshold, treating the ratio as zero when volume is zero; the point-of-control filter is disabled OR chart close is at least the developing point of control plus a configurable buffer. Apply these comparisons to direction-normalized prices and delta.
Calculation: Arrival validity = previous bar touches the zone AND close two bars ago > zone bottom AND previous close > previous open AND, if required, previous lower wick > previous upper wick AND current bar touches the zone.
Calculation: Approach = the approach filter is disabled OR at least one high among chart bars from two bars ago through the configured approach lookback is at least the level plus excursion distance.
Calculation: Reaction = same-side level AND approach AND arrival validity AND current high >= previous high AND bullish confirmation AND, if required, current close > level.
Calculation: Breakout = breakout enabled AND same-side level AND approach AND close two bars ago > zone bottom AND previous bar touches the zone AND arrival validity is false AND current high > previous high AND bullish confirmation AND, if required, current close > level AND current volume > a configurable multiple of either previous volume or the prior-bar simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Continuation = continuation enabled AND opposite-side level AND that level's continuation is unconsumed AND, among chart bars from two bars ago through the configured continuation lookback, at least one low <= level minus excursion distance AND at least one close > level AND, if retest touch is required, previous bar or current bar touches the zone AND bullish confirmation AND current close > level. The excursion and acceptance can occur in either order. Mark a continuation consumed when a signal selects that continuation; reset it when current low <= level minus excursion distance or at the configured daily reset.
Calculation: Merged setup = merged setups enabled AND same-side level AND approach AND close two bars ago > zone bottom AND merged bar touches the zone AND reaction and breakout are both false AND, if merged-body confirmation is required, the merged bar satisfies bullish confirmation AND, if required, current close > level. Classify it as a merged reaction when merged close position >= midpoint AND continuation is false; otherwise classify it as a merged fallback.
Calculation: Signal candidate = reaction OR breakout OR continuation OR merged setup, with optional bias score >= its configurable minimum and, if level locking is enabled, the level unlocked. Reset a locked level when current high >= level plus excursion distance OR current low <= level minus excursion distance, or at the configured daily reset.
Calculation: Candidate priority = continuation before breakout before merged reaction before reaction before merged fallback. For each direction and setup type, retain the highest-scoring level, breaking ties by configured level order. Reaction score is arrival validity + current high >= previous high + current close position >= midpoint + optional bias score + current low < previous low + optional extra score. Breakout score is one + current high > previous high + current close position >= midpoint + optional bias score + current low < previous low + optional extra score. Continuation score is two + current close position >= midpoint + bullish confirmation + optional bias score + optional extra score. Merged reaction score is one + merged close position >= midpoint + the merged-body requirement passing + optional bias score + current low < previous low + optional extra score. Merged fallback score is one + the merged-body requirement passing + optional bias score + current low < previous low + optional extra score. Boolean terms count one when true and zero otherwise. Optional extra score adds one if its VWAP option is enabled and normalized close < normalized session VWAP, and one if its EMA option is enabled and the shorter EMA of normalized close > the longer EMA AND normalized close > the shorter EMA.
Calculation: Selected level = while scanning levels in configured order and setup types in reaction, breakout, continuation, merged reaction, merged fallback order, update the direction's selected level whenever a setup type obtains its first candidate or a strictly higher score for that type. The direction's selected setup type is its highest-priority available type. On a signal, consume the selected level if the selected type is continuation, and lock the selected level if locking is enabled.
Calculation: Spacing passes = no previous signal in the same direction OR bars since that signal > the configured spacing OR current normalized low < the previous same-direction signal bar's normalized low AND current normalized close > that bar's normalized close.
Long: At least one long signal candidate exists AND long spacing passes.
Short: At least one short signal candidate exists AND short spacing passes.
