Calculation: Candidate magnets = confirmed swing highs (upside) and lows (downside), using equal left/right swing lookbacks; retain only the most recent maximum per side. Remove a candidate when its age exceeds the maximum age or its close is swept beyond its level by the sweep tolerance in ATR units. Count a touch when the bar’s high (upside) or low (downside) is within the touch tolerance in ATR units of its level.
Calculation: Best magnet score = highest weighted sum among unswept candidates on the correct side of close: distance factor exp(−distance/(5×ATR)); volume factor min(pivot-bar volume / rolling average volume, 3)/3; recency factor exp(−candidate age/(normalization lookback/2)); touch factor min(touch count, 10)/10; range-position factor (level−rolling range low)/(rolling range high−low) for upside, and its complement for downside. The rolling range lookback is five times the swing lookback. Use the score of the highest-scoring candidate on each side; no candidate means no magnet.
Calculation: Side tension = 100×(current best magnet score−its rolling minimum)/(its rolling maximum−its rolling minimum) over the normalization lookback; use 0 if maximum equals minimum.
Calculation: Side dominance percentage = 100×side tension/(upside tension+downside tension); if the tension sum is zero, each side is 50%.
Calculation: Qualified side = a magnet exists and its tension is at least the minimum probability threshold.
Calculation: Fired signal cooldown = at least the cooldown number of bars since the most recent fired bullish, bearish, or coil signal. A coil signal occurs when both sides qualify, both tensions are at least the coil floor, and the greater dominance percentage is below the dominance threshold.
Long: Both sides qualify AND upside dominance percentage ≥ dominance threshold AND downside dominance percentage ≤ 100−dominance threshold AND fired signal cooldown.
Short: Both sides qualify AND downside dominance percentage ≥ dominance threshold AND upside dominance percentage ≤ 100−dominance threshold AND fired signal cooldown.
