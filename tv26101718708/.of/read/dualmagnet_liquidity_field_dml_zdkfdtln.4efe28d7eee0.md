Calculation: Candidate sets = Confirmed pivot highs and lows, using the same number of bars on each side, are added to separate upside and downside sets on confirmation; store pivot price, pivot bar, pivot volume, and zero touches. Keep only the most recent configured maximum per side. On each bar, remove upside candidates when close > level + ATR × sweep tolerance, downside candidates when close < level − ATR × sweep tolerance, and either candidate when its age exceeds the maximum age. After removals, increment an upside candidate's touches when high is within level ± ATR × touch tolerance, or a downside candidate's touches when low is within that band. Age is bars since the pivot bar, including the confirmation delay.
Calculation: Side score = For each surviving upside candidate above close or downside candidate below close, sum weighted factors: exp(−distance in ATR units / distance scale); min(pivot volume / max(current volume SMA, volume floor), volume cap) / volume cap; exp(−age / (normalization lookback × age scale)); min(touches, touch cap) / touch cap; and range position for upside or 1 − range position for downside. Range position = (level − rolling lowest low) / max(rolling highest high − rolling lowest low, minimum price increment); the range lookback is a multiple of the pivot lookback. Distance in ATR units = abs(level − close) / max(ATR, minimum price increment). Each side's score is its highest positive candidate score, or zero if none; a magnet exists only when a candidate has a positive score.
Calculation: Side tension = 100 × (side score − rolling minimum side score) / (rolling maximum side score − rolling minimum side score), using the same normalization lookback for both sides and including the current bar; use zero if the maximum equals the minimum.
Calculation: Upside dominance = 100 × upside tension / (upside tension + downside tension), or 50 if their sum is zero; downside dominance = 100 − upside dominance.
Calculation: Last signal bar = Most recent bar on which a cooldown-eligible bullish bias, bearish bias, or coil occurred; initially undefined. A coil occurs when both magnets exist, both tensions meet the minimum tension threshold and the coil floor, and the greater dominance is below the dominance threshold. A signal is cooldown-eligible when the last signal bar is undefined or the elapsed bars since it are at least the cooldown; update the last signal bar whenever any of the three cooldown-eligible events occurs.
