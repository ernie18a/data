Calculation: Latest pivot high and low = most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using the same number of bars on either side; update them on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Structure direction and retracement zone = initialize direction to neutral. On a close crossing above the latest pivot high while direction is neutral or bearish, set direction bullish, fix the zone low anchor at the latest pivot low, and set its high anchor to the current high. On a close crossing below the latest pivot low while direction is neutral or bullish, set direction bearish, fix the high anchor at the latest pivot high, and set the low anchor to the current low. Crosses compare the current and previous close against the respective current and previous latest pivot values. While bullish, raise the high anchor to each new high; while bearish, lower the low anchor to each new low. For bullish direction, zone top and bottom are high anchor minus anchor range multiplied by the smaller and larger retracement parameters, respectively. For bearish direction, zone bottom and top are low anchor plus anchor range multiplied by the smaller and larger retracement parameters, respectively. Reset the zone-touch and exit-consumed states on each structure break. After updating the anchors on each bar, mark the zone touched if low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom. Once touched, consume the first bar with close > zone top in bullish direction or close < zone bottom in bearish direction, even if the EMA condition fails; the touch and first exit may occur on the same bar.
Long: Structure direction is bullish AND the current bar is the first consumed zone exit AND the shorter EMA of close crosses above the longer EMA of close.
Short: Structure direction is bearish AND the current bar is the first consumed zone exit AND the shorter EMA of close crosses below the longer EMA of close.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open AND latest pivot high >= high two bars ago AND latest pivot high <= low.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open AND latest pivot low <= low two bars ago AND latest pivot low >= high.
Long: The last completed bar of either of two calendar timeframes has close > open; the two timeframes have a shorter and a longer period and may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: The last completed bar of the shorter calendar timeframe has close <= open OR the last completed bar of the longer calendar timeframe has close < open.
