Calculation: Signal EMA = exponential moving average of close; each participating EMA uses its configured timeframe and length, with the length ordered from shortest to longest. For a higher-timeframe EMA, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: EMA slope = current Signal EMA minus its value a configurable number of its own timeframe bars earlier; long alignment requires the configured majority of slope-member EMAs to rise, short alignment requires the same majority to fall. For higher-timeframe EMAs, use completed bars.
Calculation: Range = latest confirmed Williams pivot high and pivot low on a higher timeframe, or the higher-timeframe lookback high and low when the pivot pair is invalid. Use completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Signal retracement zone = configurable retracement levels between the Range high and low, oriented by regime direction; when regime is neutral, orient from which range anchor formed last. The zone is the interval between those levels.
Calculation: EMA-Fibonacci confluence = selected Signal EMAs are inside or within a configurable fraction of the Range span from the 38.2%–61.8% retracement zone, AND (when enabled) close is in that zone, AND selected EMA slopes meet the configured direction and AND/OR rule. Higher-timeframe EMA values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Qualified spring = price undercuts the Range low by no more than a configurable fraction of Range height, then closes above the Range low within a configurable bar window; the Range is not broken down, undercut-bar volume meets its configured rule, and the event cooldown has elapsed. Use the last completed higher-timeframe Range.
Calculation: Qualified upthrust = price overshoots the Range high by no more than a configurable fraction of Range height, then closes below the Range high within a configurable bar window; the Range is not broken up, overshoot-bar volume meets its configured rule, and the event cooldown has elapsed. Use the last completed higher-timeframe Range.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = at confirmed pivots on a higher timeframe, price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low. Bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while RSI makes a lower pivot high. Compare consecutive pivots; use completed higher-timeframe bars.
Long: Qualified spring OR bullish RSI divergence OR signal engine confirms long: no participating Signal EMA is below price beyond its tolerance; configured slope alignment is rising and fresh; configured fastest distinct Signal EMAs are in descending EMA order; price and configured EMA reference are in the Signal retracement zone; EMA-Fibonacci confluence is bullish; close is inside the zone; regime is not trending down; and a trigger occurs while the engine is eligible. A trigger is a fresh reclaim of all participating EMAs or the first eligible EMA 1 test during a fresh reclaim, qualified fresh stack, or recent spring. Apply enabled volume and score gates, confirmation closes, and cooldown.
Short: Qualified upthrust OR bearish RSI divergence OR signal engine confirms short: participating Signal EMAs are below price beyond tolerance, except EMA 1 may be within its test tolerance; configured slope alignment is falling and fresh; configured fastest distinct Signal EMAs are in ascending EMA order; price and configured EMA reference are in the Signal retracement zone; EMA-Fibonacci confluence is bearish; close is inside the zone; regime is not trending up; and a trigger occurs while the engine is eligible. A trigger is a fresh reclaim of all participating EMAs from above or the first eligible EMA 1 test during a fresh reclaim, qualified fresh stack, or recent upthrust. Apply enabled volume and score gates, confirmation closes, and cooldown.
Exit: long, close falls below any participating Signal EMA beyond its configured tolerance OR close falls below the configured invalidation reference minus its buffer.
Exit: short, close rises above any participating Signal EMA beyond its configured tolerance OR close rises above the configured invalidation reference plus its buffer.
