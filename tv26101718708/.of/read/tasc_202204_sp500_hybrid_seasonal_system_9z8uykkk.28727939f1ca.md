Calculation: ATR rise = 100 × (current ATR / highest ATR over the preceding volatility lookback bars − 1).
Calculation: Volume Flow Indicator = EMA of normalized volume flow; typical price is (high + low + close) / 3, money flow is the change in typical price, and cutoff is a coefficient × close × standard deviation of the log return of typical price. Cap volume at a coefficient × the previous bar's volume SMA. Assign capped volume positively when money flow > cutoff, negatively when money flow < −cutoff, and otherwise zero. Normalized volume flow is the sum of assigned volume over the volume-flow lookback divided by the previous bar's volume SMA, replacing an undefined result with zero. The EMA smoothing lookback is shorter than the volume-flow lookback.
Long: (month >= seasonal entry month OR month < earlier seasonal exit month) AND previous bar's Volume Flow Indicator > critical threshold AND previous bar's ATR rise < ATR rise threshold.
Short: month = seasonal exit month OR (Volume Flow Indicator crosses the critical threshold in either direction AND its SMA < its previous-bar SMA) OR (previous bar's Volume Flow Indicator crosses the critical threshold in either direction AND its SMA < its preceding-bar SMA).
Exit: long, month = seasonal exit month OR (previous bar's Volume Flow Indicator crosses the critical threshold in either direction AND its SMA < its preceding-bar SMA).
Exit: short, (month >= seasonal entry month OR month < earlier seasonal exit month) AND previous bar's Volume Flow Indicator > critical threshold AND previous bar's ATR rise < ATR rise threshold.
Exit: long OR short, enabled observation start time >= bar datetime OR enabled observation end time <= bar datetime.
