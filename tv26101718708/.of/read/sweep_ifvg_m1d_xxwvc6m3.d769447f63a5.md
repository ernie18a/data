Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot with equal configurable numbers of bars on both sides, available only after its right-side bars close; optionally require the pivot high or low to equal the corresponding extreme over a configurable lookback ending at the pivot. Retain the latest qualifying swing on each side.
Calculation: Sweep = a bar whose high exceeds the latest swing high and whose close is below it, or whose low is below the latest swing low and whose close is above it. Retain the latest sweep bar on each side; a sweep is active through a configurable number of bars after it, including the sweep bar.
Calculation: Minimum displacement = a configurable price distance or a configurable multiple of chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = current low > high two bars ago AND their difference >= minimum displacement, OR, when suspension blocks are enabled, all three bars close above their opens AND each of the last two opens is above the preceding close AND current open minus the first close >= minimum displacement AND both consecutive timestamp gaps are within a configurable multiple of the chart bar duration. Bearish displacement reverses all price inequalities and uses first close minus current open.
Calculation: Displacement zone = for a bullish wick gap, bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low; for a bearish wick gap, bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago. If a suspension block qualifies, use first close to current open as the bullish zone and current open to first close as the bearish zone. Otherwise, when body-gap absorption is enabled, extend a bullish zone bottom to the lesser of its bottom and the first bar's body top if the middle body bottom > first body top, and extend its top to the greater of its top and current body bottom if current body bottom > middle body top; for a bearish zone, extend its top to the greater of its top and first body bottom if middle body top < first body bottom, and extend its bottom to the lesser of its bottom and current body top if current body top < middle body bottom.
Calculation: Candidate zone = a bullish displacement zone formed while a low sweep is active, or a bearish displacement zone formed while a high sweep is active. Optionally reject a new zone if its price interval strictly overlaps any retained candidate or confirmed inversion. Create bullish candidates before bearish candidates. Store the latest corresponding sweep and the extreme from that sweep through the displacement bar: lowest low for a bullish candidate, highest high for a bearish candidate. On each bar process candidates newest first: confirm a bullish candidate when close < its bottom or a bearish candidate when close > its top; otherwise remove it when its age exceeds the configurable grace window or its rank among same-side candidates exceeds the configurable candidate cap. Confirmation takes priority over removal. Retain confirmed inversions subject to a configurable per-side cap and optionally remove one when the smaller absolute distance from close to either zone edge exceeds a configurable ATR multiple; these retained zones affect subsequent overlap checks.
Calculation: Setup stop = selected at inversion confirmation from the inclusive displacement-to-confirmation leg: for a long, its lowest low, the lower body edge of the first bar making that low, or the inverted zone bottom; for a short, its highest high, the upper body edge of the first bar making that high, or the inverted zone top. The stop is present only when enabled.
Calculation: Setup target = when enabled, the nearest untaken higher-timeframe pivot high above a long entry or pivot low below a short entry. Build pivots from the last completed higher-timeframe bars: the middle of three consecutive bars is a high pivot if its high >= both neighbors' highs, and a low pivot if its low <= both neighbors' lows. Retain a configurable maximum number of recent pools per side and remove high pools when chart high > pool or low pools when chart low < pool, before selecting a target. If no pool is selected, an enabled fallback target is entry plus or minus the largest configured reward multiple times the absolute entry-to-stop distance; the fallback requires an enabled stop, enabled reward targets, and positive risk. Freeze the selected target at entry.
Long: Current low < latest confirmed swing low AND current close > that swing low.
Short: Current high > latest confirmed swing high AND current close < that swing high.
Long: Bullish displacement >= minimum displacement AND a low sweep is active AND the candidate passes the optional overlap restriction.
Short: Bearish displacement >= minimum displacement AND a high sweep is active AND the candidate passes the optional overlap restriction.
Long: Current close > the top of a retained bearish candidate zone.
Short: Current close < the bottom of a retained bullish candidate zone.
Long: Current close > the top of a retained bearish candidate zone AND the optional setup filter is disabled OR current close < the candidate's stored highest high from its high sweep through displacement.
Short: Current close < the bottom of a retained bullish candidate zone AND the optional setup filter is disabled OR current close > the candidate's stored lowest low from its low sweep through displacement.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has low <= the enabled setup stop OR high > the selected higher-timeframe pool target OR high >= the selected fallback reward target.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has high >= the enabled setup stop OR low < the selected higher-timeframe pool target OR low <= the selected fallback reward target.
