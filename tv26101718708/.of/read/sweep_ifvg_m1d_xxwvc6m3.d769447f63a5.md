Calculation: Qualified swing high/low = on the chart timeframe, a high/low that is the maximum/minimum across N bars on each side of its middle bar; require the middle bar’s high/low also to be the maximum/minimum over the configured liquidity lookback ending at that bar (unless that lookback is zero). Use only after the N right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Active high/low sweep = a confirmed chart-timeframe bar with high > the latest qualified swing high and close < that high / low < the latest qualified swing low and close > that low; remains active through the configured number of bars after the sweep, inclusive.
Calculation: Minimum displacement size = configured threshold in ticks, or chart-timeframe ATR(14) times a configured multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, respectively, low > high two bars earlier with gap size at least the minimum displacement size, or high < low two bars earlier with gap size at least that size; alternatively, three consecutive bullish/bearish candles whose opens gap beyond the preceding closes at both joins, with span at least the minimum size and each join no more than 1.5 chart-bar durations apart. The latter is the suspension-block option and can qualify without a wick gap.
Calculation: Resolved bullish/bearish zone edges = use the suspension-block span when that option qualifies: bullish [close two bars earlier, current open], bearish [current open, close two bars earlier]. Otherwise use the wick-gap edges [high two bars earlier, current low] for bullish or [current high, low two bars earlier] for bearish; when volume-imbalance absorption is enabled, extend either edge to the adjoining candle-body edge where those bodies gap while the wicks bridge the gap.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe liquidity pool = a three-bar swing high/low on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, confirmed when the next higher-timeframe bar completes; retain only pools not subsequently traded through (high > pool high or low < pool low). Choose the nearest retained pool above a long entry or below a short entry.
Calculation: Stop price = configured placement: the far edge of the inverted zone; or the body edge of the candle making the most extreme low/high from displacement start through inversion confirmation; or that leg’s lowest low/highest high. For long/short, respectively.
Calculation: Target price = nearest retained higher-timeframe pool in the position direction when available; otherwise, if configured, the furthest configured R multiple from entry, where R is the absolute entry-to-stop distance.
Long: (low < latest qualified swing low AND close > latest qualified swing low) OR (active low sweep AND bullish displacement) OR (active low sweep AND bullish displacement candidate closes below its resolved zone bottom within the configured inversion window) OR (bullish inversion confirms AND, when required, confirmation close < break-even price)
Short: (high > latest qualified swing high AND close < latest qualified swing high) OR (active high sweep AND bearish displacement) OR (active high sweep AND bearish displacement candidate closes above its resolved zone top within the configured inversion window) OR (bearish inversion confirms AND, when required, confirmation close > break-even price)
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR (target is a higher-timeframe pool AND high > target price) OR (target is an R level AND high >= target price)
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR (target is a higher-timeframe pool AND low < target price) OR (target is an R level AND low <= target price)
