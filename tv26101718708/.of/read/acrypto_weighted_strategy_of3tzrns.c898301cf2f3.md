Calculation: MACD line = configurable moving average of a configurable price source minus a second configurable moving average of a configurable price source; its signal is an SMA of the MACD line.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = SMA of 100 × (RSI of close − lowest RSI)/(highest RSI − lowest RSI), using a configurable stochastic lookback and smoothing length; RSI and Stochastic RSI have separate upper and lower thresholds.
Calculation: Supertrend direction = initialize direction to +1; calculate lower band as source − ATR multiplier × ATR and upper band as source + ATR multiplier × ATR, with ATR optionally calculated as SMA of true range. If previous close > previous lower band, replace the lower band with the greater of its current and previous values; if previous close < previous upper band, replace the upper band with the lesser of its current and previous values. Change direction from −1 to +1 when close > previous upper band, or from +1 to −1 when close < previous lower band; otherwise retain direction.
Calculation: Delayed transition of condition A with delay d = A now AND A d−1 bars ago AND NOT A d bars ago; it does not require A to hold on intervening bars.
Calculation: Oscillator cooldown = bars since entry >= exit delay AND bars since the most recent full or partial close >= exit delay; substitute the exit delay for bars since entry when flat and for bars since close when no trade has ever closed.
Calculation: Five long component conditions = delayed transition of MACD line > signal; Stochastic RSI d bars ago <= its lower threshold AND current Stochastic RSI < its upper threshold AND oscillator cooldown; RSI d bars ago <= its lower threshold AND current RSI < its upper threshold AND oscillator cooldown; delayed transition of Supertrend direction = +1; delayed transition of first configurable moving average > second configurable moving average. Each component has its own delay; the two moving averages have independently configurable types, sources and lengths.
Calculation: Five short component conditions = delayed transition of MACD line < signal; Stochastic RSI d bars ago >= its upper threshold AND current Stochastic RSI > its lower threshold AND oscillator cooldown; RSI d bars ago >= its upper threshold AND current RSI > its lower threshold AND oscillator cooldown; delayed transition of Supertrend direction = −1; delayed transition of first configurable moving average < second configurable moving average. Each component has its own delay.
Calculation: Directional weight = sum of the configurable component weights for the satisfied component conditions of that direction; disabled components have zero weight in weighted entry mode during the configured date window.
Calculation: Volume peak = volume two bars ago > volume three bars ago AND > volume four bars ago AND > volume five bars ago.
Calculation: Top condition = volume peak AND ((RSI >= its upper threshold AND (Stochastic RSI >= its upper threshold OR volume < half its SMA)) OR (volume two bars ago / volume one bar ago > 1 + configurable long volume factor AND Stochastic RSI >= its upper threshold)). Bottom condition = volume peak AND ((RSI <= its lower threshold AND (Stochastic RSI <= its lower threshold OR volume < half its SMA)) OR (volume two bars ago / volume one bar ago > 1 + configurable short volume factor AND Stochastic RSI <= its lower threshold)). A repeated top or bottom requires its respective condition on the previous bar AND on at least one of the second through seventh preceding bars.
Calculation: Profit target n = entry price × (1 + n × long profit interval) for a long, or entry price × (1 − n × short profit interval) for a short; n ranges from 1 through the configured maximum number of targets. Submit each target as a limit exit for a configured percentage of the original position when the position opens.
Calculation: Stop price = initially entry price × (1 − stop percentage) for a long or entry price × (1 + stop percentage) for a short. On a bar with a full or partial close, select the largest enabled target index n whose target is strictly exceeded by the high for a long or strictly undershot by the low for a short. In percentage move mode, set the stop to entry price × (1 + n × long profit interval − stop percentage × move factor) for a long, or entry price × (1 − n × short profit interval + stop percentage × move factor) for a short. In target-offset mode with offset k, set it to target n−k if n > k; if n <= k, set it to entry price only when the move-to-entry option is enabled. In no-move mode, or when no update applies, retain the stop. Reset it to the initial stop on the entry bar.
Long: Any long component condition OR long directional weight >= weight threshold OR repeated bottom condition.
Short: Any short component condition OR short directional weight >= weight threshold OR repeated top condition.
Exit: Long, within the configured date window AND long trading enabled AND short trading disabled AND ((weighted mode enabled AND short directional weight >= weight threshold) OR (weighted mode disabled AND an enabled component satisfies its long-close condition)); long-close conditions are MACD line < signal now AND NOT (MACD line > signal) d bars ago, short Stochastic RSI component, short RSI component, short Supertrend component, or first moving average < second now AND NOT (first > second) d bars ago, respectively.
Exit: Short, within the configured date window AND short trading enabled AND long trading disabled AND ((weighted mode enabled AND long directional weight >= weight threshold) OR (weighted mode disabled AND an enabled component satisfies the same respective long-close condition used for a long exit)).
Exit: Long, take-profit exits enabled AND high >= an outstanding long profit target.
Exit: Short, take-profit exits enabled AND low <= an outstanding short profit target.
Exit: Long, within the configured date window AND long trading enabled AND stop loss enabled AND bars since entry > 0 AND configurable stop source <= long stop price.
Exit: Short, within the configured date window AND short trading enabled AND stop loss enabled AND bars since entry > 0 AND configurable stop source >= short stop price.
Exit: Long, within the configured date window AND long trading enabled AND stop loss enabled AND potential-top exit enabled AND bars since entry > 0 AND repeated top condition; close the configured percentage immediately if the top exit percentage is 100, otherwise submit a limit exit for that percentage at the previous bar's configurable top price source.
Exit: Short, within the configured date window AND short trading enabled AND stop loss enabled AND potential-bottom exit enabled AND bars since entry > 0 AND repeated bottom condition; close the configured bottom percentage immediately if the top exit percentage is 100, otherwise submit a limit exit for the bottom percentage at the previous bar's configurable bottom price source.
