Calculation: Tactical channel = Fit a volume-weighted polynomial of configurable degree to a configurable OHLC-derived price source from the selected anchor through the current bar; use weight 1 when volume is nonpositive or unavailable. The anchor is either a rolling-window start, the latest confirmed swing low, the latest confirmed swing high, or the more recent of the two swings, with the rolling start as fallback. A swing is confirmed only after the configured number of bars on each side. The center is the fitted current value; residual deviation is the square root of volume-weighted mean squared residual. Inner and outer bands equal the center plus or minus their respective deviation multiples, with the outer multiple larger.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = A newly confirmed price swing low is lower than the previous confirmed swing low AND the RSI or smoothed stochastic RSI at the new pivot is higher than at the previous pivot AND, if band confluence is enabled, the new pivot's low is at or below the selected inner or outer lower tactical band at that pivot. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = A newly confirmed price swing high is higher than the previous confirmed swing high AND the RSI or smoothed stochastic RSI at the new pivot is lower than at the previous pivot AND, if band confluence is enabled, the new pivot's high is at or above the selected inner or outer upper tactical band at that pivot. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Lower sweep = The low is below the retained equal-low level AND the close is above that level, OR the low is below the latest confirmed swing low AND the close is above that swing low AND the close is above the open. On confirmation of two consecutive swing lows within an ATR-multiple tolerance, retain their lower value as the equal-low level until replaced.
Calculation: Upper sweep = The high is above the retained equal-high level AND the close is below that level, OR the high is above the latest confirmed swing high AND the close is below that swing high AND the close is below the open. On confirmation of two consecutive swing highs within an ATR-multiple tolerance, retain their higher value as the equal-high level until replaced.
Calculation: Macro frame = Over a longer context window, fit the selected price source with either a simple moving average, an unweighted linear regression, or a volume-weighted quadratic regression; use weight 1 for nonpositive or unavailable volume. Macro deviation is the standard deviation of the price source over that window. The linear macro slope is the fitted current value minus the fitted preceding value; the slope is zero for the other fit degrees.
Calculation: Band density = Divide a symmetric range of macro-deviation offsets into equal bins. For each bar in a shorter live-density window, bin its price source's distance from the current macro fit evaluated at that historical bar, divided by current macro deviation, and sum its volume weight. The density at a tactical band is its current offset from the macro center's bin volume divided by the largest bin volume. Density outside the bin range is unavailable. A thin node has density at or below the thin threshold; a thick node has density at or above the higher thick threshold.
Calculation: Favored density = Thin by default. If adaptive density is enabled, after both thin and thick nodes have a minimum number of resolved gated fades, favor the node with the higher historical follow-through rate, breaking ties in favor of thin. Classify each raw fade by its entry-bar density, with thin taking precedence over thick; a gated fade for this statistic passes the regime and hard-density gates, irrespective of walk or ride-risk gates. At the fixed follow-through horizon, a fade is successful when its directional close change from the entry close reaches the configured multiple of entry-bar ATR. Update the favored node after resolving fades, for use on subsequent bars.
Calculation: Regime pass = According to the selected mode, require either, both, or neither of: variance of the configured multibar log return divided by its horizon times the variance of the one-bar log return is at or below one minus a neutral threshold; and the greater of zero and the negative correlation between the previous bar's close deviation from its moving average and the current close change is at or above a reversion threshold. Use variance ratio 1 when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Band walk = The close has remained strictly beyond the relevant outer tactical band for at least a configured consecutive number of bars, comparing each close with that bar's band.
Calculation: Ride risk = Round 100 times the weighted sum of adverse macro slope magnitude, adverse nonconverging centerline spread magnitude, same-side band-walk indicator, adverse close rate-of-change magnitude, slow mean-reversion indicator, and adverse supertrend consensus, with respective weights 0.14, 0.20, 0.18, 0.16, 0.14, and 0.18. Normalize slope as min(abs(macro slope × macro window length / macro deviation), 1); normalize spread as min(abs((tactical center − macro center) / macro deviation) / spread threshold, 1), setting it to zero when its absolute value decreased from the preceding bar; normalize momentum as min(abs(rate of change) / (2 × its standard deviation), 1). Each directional component is zero unless it points against the fade. The slow indicator is 1 when the lag-one correlation of close minus tactical center is outside (0, 1), unavailable, or implies an AR(1) half-life greater than the follow-through horizon; otherwise it is 0. Consensus is the fraction of configured supertrend timeframes trending against the fade, treating unavailable directions as down. For each selected higher timeframe use the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's supertrend; for each selected lower timeframe use the previous lower-timeframe bar as returned at chart-bar evaluation. If ride-risk calculation is disabled, risk is zero.
Calculation: Raw lower fade = The low is at or below the outer lower band AND at least one of: close is above that band; a bullish divergence was confirmed within the configured recency window; a lower sweep occurred within its shorter recency window; or density is configured as an extra tell and the lower-band node is favored. A shared cooldown requires the configured minimum number of bars since the most recent raw lower or upper fade; update the cooldown on every raw fade, including one rejected by gates.
Calculation: Raw upper fade = The high is at or above the outer upper band AND at least one of: close is below that band; a bearish divergence was confirmed within the configured recency window; an upper sweep occurred within its shorter recency window; or density is configured as an extra tell and the upper-band node is favored. Apply the same shared raw-fade cooldown.
Long: Bullish divergence is confirmed.
Short: Bearish divergence is confirmed.
Long: Raw lower fade AND ((regime pass AND (hard-density gate is disabled OR lower-band node is favored) AND (walk gate is disabled OR lower band is not walking) AND (ride-risk gate is disabled OR ride-risk calculation is disabled OR lower-side ride risk is below its threshold)) OR (ungated markers are enabled AND (regime pass fails OR (hard-density gate is enabled AND lower-band node is not favored)))).
Short: Raw upper fade AND ((regime pass AND (hard-density gate is disabled OR upper-band node is favored) AND (walk gate is disabled OR upper band is not walking) AND (ride-risk gate is disabled OR ride-risk calculation is disabled OR upper-side ride risk is below its threshold)) OR (ungated markers are enabled AND (regime pass fails OR (hard-density gate is enabled AND upper-band node is not favored)))).
