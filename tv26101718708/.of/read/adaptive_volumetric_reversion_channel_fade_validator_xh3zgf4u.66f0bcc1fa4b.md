Calculation: Tactical regression centerline = volume-weighted polynomial least-squares fit of the selected price source from the current anchor through the current bar; the anchor is the selected rolling window or most recent confirmed swing high/low, whichever the configured anchor mode specifies
Calculation: Tactical residual standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted mean squared residual from the tactical regression fit
Calculation: Outer channel bands = tactical regression centerline ± configured outer-band multiple × tactical residual standard deviation
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = smoothed stochastic of RSI
Calculation: Momentum oscillator = configured choice of RSI or Stochastic RSI
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = price pivot with the configured number of bars on each side; the pivot is known only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low AND its momentum oscillator value is higher than at the preceding swing low AND the pivot low reaches the configured lower channel band, unless the band requirement is off
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high AND its momentum oscillator value is lower than at the preceding swing high AND the pivot high reaches the configured upper channel band, unless the band requirement is off
Calculation: Equal-level liquidity sweep = price exceeds the latest confirmed swing high (or equal-high level within configured ATR tolerance) and closes back below it; the bearish sweep requires a bearish candle only when no equal-high level is available. The bullish case is the inverse at swing lows/equal lows.
Calculation: Reversion variance ratio = variance of k-bar log returns divided by k times the variance of one-bar log returns
Calculation: Reversion correlation = correlation of prior-bar close-minus-moving-average deviation with the current one-bar close change
Long: Bullish divergence OR (low ≤ lower outer channel band AND at least one of (close > lower outer channel band, bullish divergence occurred within the configured recency window, bullish liquidity sweep occurred within its freshness window)) AND the configured minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed; when regime gating is enabled, also require the configured reversion regime test to pass, and when density is configured as a hard gate or extra tell, require its configured favored-node condition as applicable
Short: Bearish divergence OR (high ≥ upper outer channel band AND at least one of (close < upper outer channel band, bearish divergence occurred within the configured recency window, bearish liquidity sweep occurred within its freshness window)) AND the configured minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed; when regime gating is enabled, also require the configured reversion regime test to pass, and when density is configured as a hard gate or extra tell, require its configured favored-node condition as applicable
