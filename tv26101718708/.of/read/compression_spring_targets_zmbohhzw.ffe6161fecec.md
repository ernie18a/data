Calculation: Block range = high − low of each aggregated block on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using completed blocks.
Calculation: Block volume = sum of volume in each aggregated block.
Calculation: Range ratio = current completed block range ÷ average range of the preceding comparison-length completed blocks.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current completed block volume ÷ average volume of the preceding comparison-length completed blocks.
Long: A spring is active with at least the minimum number of compression blocks, where each compression block has range ratio ≤ the compression maximum and volume ratio ≥ the compression minimum, and consecutive non-compression gaps have not exceeded the allowed maximum; and the current completed block closes above the spring high (or its high exceeds the spring high in wick mode), its range ratio ≥ the breakout minimum, and, when breakout volume expansion is required, its volume ratio ≥ the breakout minimum.
Short: A spring is active with at least the minimum number of compression blocks, where each compression block has range ratio ≤ the compression maximum and volume ratio ≥ the compression minimum, and consecutive non-compression gaps have not exceeded the allowed maximum; and the current completed block closes below the spring low (or its low falls below the spring low in wick mode), its range ratio ≥ the breakout minimum, and, when breakout volume expansion is required, its volume ratio ≥ the breakout minimum.
