Calculation: VIO = EMA of the sum of top raw and bottom raw values, where top raw is volume excess × range position when a qualifying up move occurred within the watch window and range position ≥ top-zone threshold (otherwise 0), and bottom raw is −volume excess × (1 − range position) when a qualifying down move occurred within the watch window and range position ≤ bottom-zone threshold (otherwise 0); qualifying up/down moves are close − close[move lookback] > threshold × ATR / < −threshold × ATR; range position = (close − lowest low over range lookback) / (highest high over range lookback − lowest low over range lookback), or 0.5 if the range is zero; volume excess = max(volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) − 1, 0), or 0 if the SMA is zero. A move qualifies on the current bar and remains in the watch window for the specified number of bars.
Long: VIO crosses below −signal threshold
Short: VIO crosses above signal threshold
