Calculation: Bollinger basis = selected moving average of the selected price source; the average may be SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA.
Calculation: Bollinger upper and lower bands = basis plus and minus a multiplier times the population standard deviation of the same source over the basis lookback.
Long: Selected price source > Bollinger basis.
Short: Selected price source < Bollinger basis.
Long: Position is flat AND selected price source crosses above the Bollinger upper band.
Short: Position is flat AND selected price source crosses below the Bollinger lower band.
Exit: Long position, selected price source crosses below the Bollinger lower band.
Exit: Short position, selected price source crosses above the Bollinger upper band.
