Calculation: VWAP and deviation = anchored VWAP of typical price (H+L+C)/3 and its standard deviation; use max(standard deviation, minimum price tick) as the deviation denominator.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, +1 if its close is above the latest fresh confirmed swing high plus one tick, -1 if below the latest fresh confirmed swing low minus one tick, otherwise 0; a swing is confirmed using equal left and right lookbacks, and either swing expires after its maximum age.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep down = low penetrates by at least the sweep threshold below any of the previous completed daily low, active session low, ready opening-range low, or fresh confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low, then closes above that level. Liquidity sweep up = high penetrates by the threshold above any corresponding high, then closes below it.
Calculation: VFI = sum over the longer flow lookback of capped volume signed positive/negative when the typical-price change exceeds the volatility-based cutoff, divided by the prior-bar volume average over that lookback; cutoff is the shorter-lookback standard deviation of log typical-price changes times a coefficient and close, and capped volume is the lesser of current volume and the prior-bar volume average times a cap. VFI signal = EMA of VFI over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: VFI bullish = VFI > 0 AND VFI >= its signal AND VFI change from the prior bar >= minimum slope. VFI bearish = VFI < 0 AND VFI <= its signal AND VFI change <= negative minimum slope.
Calculation: Volume effort = volume / volume SMA over its baseline lookback. Body result = absolute(close-open) / ATR from the prior bar. Bullish initiative = effort >= 1 AND body result >= its threshold AND close > open AND (close-low)/max(high-low,tick) >= directional-close threshold. Bearish initiative is the converse. Bullish absorption = effort >= its high-effort threshold AND body result <= its poor-result threshold AND lower wick/range >= its wick threshold AND close location >= directional-close threshold. Bearish absorption uses the upper wick and opposite close location. Bullish/bearish support = corresponding absorption OR initiative.
Calculation: Flow turn up = VFI change > its reversal threshold AND (VFI crosses above its signal OR VFI >= its signal OR VFI change exceeds its prior change). Flow turn down is the converse. Signed-candle-volume turn up = current candle-volume fraction is positive OR its change is at least its threshold while the fraction > -0.35; turn down = fraction is negative OR its change is at most negative its threshold while the fraction < 0.35. Fraction is +1 for an up candle, -1 for a down candle, 0 for a doji, and unavailable without volume.
Calculation: Remembered reversal confirmation = within the configured memory age, flow turns in the seed direction and, when required and available, signed-candle-volume turns in that direction; clear it on invalid flow or strong opposing flow. Remembered reversal support = bullish/bearish support occurred within that same memory age.
Calculation: Continuation seed = while ready, no news block, continuation enabled, and higher-timeframe direction matches: close is above/below VWAP and either (when continuation sweep is required) a down/up liquidity sweep occurs, or a local pullback occurs. A local long pullback is (low < prior low OR prior close < close two bars ago) AND close > open; local short is (high > prior high OR prior close > close two bars ago) AND close < open. Both require highest high minus lowest low over the pullback lookback <= its ATR multiple using prior-bar ATR. First-break mode freezes the first such seed's low/high and trigger (max(prior structure high, seed high) for long; min(prior structure low, seed low) for short); later seeds do not refresh it. Expire after the maximum seed age, on a one-tick adverse breach, or when higher-timeframe direction or VWAP side fails. A first later close beyond the frozen trigger consumes the seed even if another gate blocks the signal.
Calculation: Reversal seed = while ready, no news block, and reversal enabled, a down/up liquidity sweep when sweep is required, otherwise the corresponding local pullback; record seed low/high, whether price reached at least the configured VWAP-deviation excursion, and whether the sweep occurred. Expire after the maximum seed age or a one-tick adverse breach.
Calculation: Consecutive VWAP acceptance = count consecutive closes above/below VWAP, reset to zero on a failure or news block; cap at the configured acceptance count.
Calculation: Repeat-direction permission = if repeat filtering is off or the last signal was not this direction, pass; otherwise require a confirmed pivot pullback after the last signal in the same leg, with depth from the leg extreme at least the configured multiple of prior ATR, and a new seed at or after that pivot.
Long: ready AND no news block AND (when enabled, volatility filter passes: prior ATR >= session minimum AND (same-time relative volume unavailable OR >= session minimum) AND current range <= prior ATR times shock limit) AND repeat-direction permission AND ((continuation enabled AND continuation seed active AND last completed higher-timeframe direction is +1 AND close > VWAP AND VWAP deviation <= continuation maximum AND required consecutive VWAP acceptance reached since seed AND (continuation sweep not required OR seed was swept) AND (flow filter off OR VFI bullish AND bullish initiative AND no opposing VFI/bearish initiative AND (continuation flow-shift requirement off OR remembered reversal confirmation exists)) AND (trend filter off OR Supertrend bullish) AND close > frozen trigger AND close > open AND close-open >= body threshold times prior ATR) OR (reversal enabled AND reversal seed active AND (sweep not required OR seed was swept) AND (flow filter off OR no opposing VFI/bearish initiative, no strong bearish initiative, and remembered reversal confirmation exists OR current flow turns up with required signed-candle-volume turn when applicable) AND (close > VWAP minus one deviation OR close > active-session midpoint OR close > VWAP OR bullish structure break) AND seed reached the configured downside VWAP excursion AND VWAP deviation is between -2 and +0.75 AND (trend filter off OR Supertrend bullish) AND close > seed trigger AND close > open AND close-open >= body threshold times prior ATR). On a continuation first-break attempt, evaluate only the first later closing break and consume that attempt whether or not all gates pass.
Short: ready AND no news block AND (when enabled, volatility filter passes: prior ATR >= session minimum AND (same-time relative volume unavailable OR >= session minimum) AND current range <= prior ATR times shock limit) AND repeat-direction permission AND ((continuation enabled AND continuation seed active AND last completed higher-timeframe direction is -1 AND close < VWAP AND VWAP deviation >= negative continuation maximum AND required consecutive VWAP acceptance reached since seed AND (continuation sweep not required OR seed was swept) AND (flow filter off OR VFI bearish AND bearish initiative AND no opposing VFI/bullish initiative AND (continuation flow-shift requirement off OR remembered reversal confirmation exists)) AND (trend filter off OR Supertrend bearish) AND close < frozen trigger AND close < open AND open-close >= body threshold times prior ATR) OR (reversal enabled AND reversal seed active AND (sweep not required OR seed was swept) AND (flow filter off OR no opposing VFI/bullish initiative, no strong bullish initiative, and remembered reversal confirmation exists OR current flow turns down with required signed-candle-volume turn when applicable) AND (close < VWAP plus one deviation OR close < active-session midpoint OR close < VWAP OR bearish structure break) AND seed reached the configured upside VWAP excursion AND VWAP deviation is between -0.75 and +2 AND (trend filter off OR Supertrend bearish) AND close < seed trigger AND close < open AND open-close >= body threshold times prior ATR). On a continuation first-break attempt, evaluate only the first later closing break and consume that attempt whether or not all gates pass.
