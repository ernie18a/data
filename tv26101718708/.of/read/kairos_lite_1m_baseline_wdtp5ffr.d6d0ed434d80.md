Calculation: Directional notation = Use side s = +1 for long and s = -1 for short. For a long use low as the near extreme and high as the far extreme; for a short use high as the near extreme and low as the far extreme. A directional comparison s × a > s × b means a > b for long and a < b for short. Evaluate each chart bar at close; unavailable required values fail their conditions.
Calculation: VWAP and deviation = Anchored volume-weighted average of the selected price source and its volume-weighted standard deviation, reset at the selected calendar anchor; floor the deviation at the price tick. The standardized close is (close - VWAP) / deviation. Event-based anchors requiring external corporate-action data are unavailable.
Calculation: VFI = Let typical price be (high + low + close) / 3, cutoff be a coefficient times the rolling standard deviation of its log change times close, and average volume be the previous bar's volume SMA. Cap current volume at a multiple of average volume. Assign that capped volume a positive or negative sign when the typical-price change exceeds the positive or negative cutoff, otherwise assign zero. Divide the rolling sum of signed volume by average volume; its signal is an EMA and its slope is the current VFI minus the previous VFI.
Calculation: Volume spread measures = Effort is volume divided by its rolling SMA; body result is absolute candle body divided by prior-bar ATR; close location is (close - low) / max(high - low, tick); lower and upper wick fractions use that same denominator. Directional initiative requires effort at least its baseline, body result at least its strong-result threshold, a candle in the direction, and close location at or beyond the directional control threshold. Directional absorption requires effort at least its high-effort threshold, body result at most its poor-result threshold, the opposite-side wick fraction at least its minimum, and close location at or beyond the directional control threshold.
Calculation: Directional flow = Directional VFI requires VFI on the corresponding side of zero and its signal, with directional slope at least its minimum. A directional VFI turn requires directional slope strictly above its turn minimum and either VFI on the directional side of its signal or directional slope greater than its previous value, treating a missing previous slope as zero. Signed candle fraction is +1, -1, or 0 for an up, down, or doji candle when volume is positive. A directional candle turn requires positive volume and either a fraction in the direction or a directional fraction change at least its threshold with the current fraction within its directional bound. A flow turn is a VFI turn AND, when the candle-turn option is enabled and volume is positive, a candle turn. Opposing flow is opposing directional VFI AND opposing initiative; strong opposing effort is opposing initiative AND effort at least its opposition threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, each confirmed using the same number of bars on either side. Set direction to neutral when either pivot is older than its maximum age measured in higher-timeframe bars; otherwise set it long when close exceeds the pivot high plus a tick, short when close is below the pivot low minus a tick, and retain its previous direction otherwise.
Calculation: Sweep and pullback = A long sweep has low at least a configured tick distance below any of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar's low, the active session's pre-bar low, the completed opening-range low, or the latest fresh confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low, AND closes above that level. A short sweep mirrors this using highs. Session and opening-range extremes exclude the current bar; a pivot is usable only after confirmation and within its maximum age. A long local pullback requires (low < previous low OR previous close < close two bars ago) AND close > open AND the rolling pullback high minus low <= a multiple of prior ATR; a short local pullback mirrors these comparisons.
Calculation: Context and common gate = Select the first enabled configured session containing the bar, otherwise select the all-hours daily context if session restriction is disabled; otherwise there is no context. Reset session extremes at each session start and all-hours extremes at each day start. The common gate requires an active context, available VWAP, prior ATR, VFI and effort, and no enabled datetime blackout overlapping the bar. If the optional volatility gate is enabled, prior ATR must be at least the context threshold, current volume divided by mean volume at the same exchange-local time on available previous days must be at least its context threshold or unavailable, and max(high - low, tick) must be at most a multiple of prior ATR.
Calculation: Shared reversal and baseline seeds = For each side, a continuation seed condition requires continuation enabled, the optional higher-timeframe direction filter passed, close on the directional side of VWAP, and either a sweep or local pullback according to the continuation sweep setting. A reversal seed condition requires reversal enabled and either a sweep or local pullback according to the reversal sweep setting. At each confirmed bar clear both seeds and accepted-close counts on context reset, unavailable flow, inactive context, or blackout; expire a seed after its maximum age or when its near extreme is breached by more than a tick. If the common data are available and exactly one side has either seed condition, replace that side's seed with the current bar: record its time, sweep flag, whether its near extreme reaches the directional VWAP excursion threshold, and a trigger equal to the highest high or lowest low over the preceding structure-break lookback. Its near boundary is the rolling pullback extreme when a continuation seed exists without a sweep, otherwise the current near extreme. Count consecutive closes on each side of VWAP while data are ready and there is no blackout, resetting the respective count otherwise.
Calculation: Seed flow memory = While a seed exists, record the latest directional flow-turn bar; clear that memory if flow data are unavailable or opposing flow occurs, or when its age exceeds the configured memory lifetime. If memory is disabled, the lifetime is zero. A continuation flow gate, when enabled, requires directional VFI AND directional initiative AND no opposing flow AND, if the additional turn option is enabled, an unexpired turn memory. A reversal flow gate, when enabled, requires no opposing flow AND no strong opposing effort AND either unexpired turn memory or a current directional flow turn. Flow data must be available even when the flow gate is disabled.
Calculation: First-break continuation seed = In first-break mode maintain separate continuation seeds. Clear a seed on context reset, inactive context, blackout, unavailable flow, disabled continuation, excessive age, breach of its near boundary by more than a tick, failure of the optional higher-timeframe filter, or close no longer on the directional side of VWAP. When exactly one side has a continuation seed condition and has no existing first-break seed, freeze the current bar as its seed: near boundary is the current near extreme for a sweep and otherwise the rolling pullback extreme; trigger is the more distant of the current far extreme and the preceding structure-break extreme. Ignore subsequent seed conditions until cleared. Maintain its flow memory independently. Its first later close beyond the trigger consumes the seed even if other gates fail.
Calculation: Structure confirmation = Close is beyond the seed trigger in its direction AND the candle body points in that direction AND its absolute size is at least a multiple of prior ATR. First-break continuation additionally requires a bar later than its seed. Baseline continuation and reversal can confirm on the seed bar.
Calculation: Continuation eligibility = The selected continuation seed is valid AND continuation is enabled AND the optional higher-timeframe direction filter passes AND close is on the directional side of VWAP AND directional standardized close is at most its maximum AND the required number of consecutive VWAP-accepted closes has occurred AND the continuation flow gate passes AND, if enabled, the seed records a sweep AND, if enabled, Supertrend points in the direction. In baseline mode the seed age plus one must also be at least the required accepted-close count.
Calculation: Reversal eligibility = A shared seed is valid AND reversal is enabled AND, if required, the seed recorded a sweep AND the seed recorded the required directional VWAP excursion AND directional standardized close is between its configured lower and upper reversal bounds AND the reversal flow gate passes AND, if enabled, Supertrend points in the direction AND (close reclaims the directional VWAP deviation edge OR the pre-bar session midpoint OR VWAP OR the seed trigger with structure confirmation).
Calculation: Repeat gate = After a confirmed signal retain its direction, bar, and running favorable high for long or low for short. A repeated same-direction signal requires a newly confirmed pullback pivot after the signal: its depth from the favorable extreme recorded before the pivot must be at least a multiple of the ATR preceding the pivot bar. The pivot remains valid until its level is breached by more than a tick; replace it when another qualifying pivot confirms. The selected seed must be dated at or after that pivot. This gate can be disabled; an opposite-direction signal needs no pivot. A confirmed signal resets all seeds and initializes a new favorable extreme.
Calculation: Directional candidate = Common gate passes AND either (continuation eligibility AND its structure confirmation) OR (reversal eligibility AND its structure confirmation). When both branches qualify, select continuation for the repeat gate. Evaluate candidates for both sides before applying the repeat gate; if both exist, suppress both signals regardless of their repeat gates.
Long: long directional candidate AND short directional candidate is false AND long repeat gate passes.
Short: short directional candidate AND long directional candidate is false AND short repeat gate passes.
