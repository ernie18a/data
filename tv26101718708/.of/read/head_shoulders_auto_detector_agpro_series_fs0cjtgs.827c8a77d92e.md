Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a pivot high or low with the same pivot-length number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Keep the most recent limited number of confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Pattern candidate = the latest three confirmed same-side pivots, in chronological order, as left shoulder, head, and right shoulder. For a bearish candidate use highs and require the head above both shoulders; for a bullish candidate use lows and require the head below both shoulders. Head extension is the smaller absolute head-to-shoulder price difference. Require head extension >= a prominence multiple of current ATR, absolute shoulder price difference <= a shoulder-tolerance multiple of current ATR, and each shoulder-to-head bar span >= pivot length and < a maximum span. Select the latest confirmed opposite-side pivot strictly between the left shoulder and head and the latest strictly between the head and right shoulder as neckline anchors; require both to exist and their absolute price difference <= a neckline-tolerance multiple of current ATR. Time symmetry = 1 - absolute difference between the two shoulder-to-head bar spans / their maximum; price symmetry = clamp(1 - absolute shoulder price difference / max(head extension, current ATR), 0, 1). Require the average of time symmetry and price symmetry >= the minimum symmetry threshold.
Calculation: New pattern = a valid candidate evaluated when a new pivot of either side is confirmed and bars since the last accepted pattern >= twice the pivot length; initialize the last accepted pattern bar to bar index zero. Evaluate the bearish candidate first and accept the bullish candidate only if the bearish candidate is invalid. Store each accepted pattern independently, including its pivot prices and bar indices.
Calculation: Neckline = the straight line through a pattern's two neckline anchors, extrapolated to the bar being evaluated.
Calculation: Forming pattern = a newly accepted pattern, evaluated for confirmation on the same bar. On each bar, confirm it if the close breaks its neckline in the pattern's direction and the bar is after the right shoulder; otherwise expire it if bars since the right shoulder > the maximum lifetime, or invalidate it if the close is beyond the head in the opposite direction. Confirmation takes precedence over expiration and invalidation.
Calculation: Confirmed pattern = a forming pattern at its first qualifying neckline break. Fix its target at the confirmation-bar neckline minus the head-to-neck distance for a bearish pattern or plus that distance for a bullish pattern. Head-to-neck distance is the absolute difference between head price and either the average neckline-anchor price or the neckline at the head bar, according to the selected target method. Fix its stop at head price plus an ATR buffer for a bearish pattern or minus an ATR buffer for a bullish pattern, using confirmation-bar ATR. Starting on the following bar, end the confirmed state when the target or stop is touched; target takes precedence when both are touched.
Long: A new bullish pattern is accepted.
Short: A new bearish pattern is accepted.
Long: A forming bullish pattern has close > neckline AND current bar > right-shoulder bar.
Short: A forming bearish pattern has close < neckline AND current bar > right-shoulder bar.
Long: A confirmed bullish pattern has not previously retested AND current bar is more than the retest delay after its confirmation bar AND low <= current neckline AND high >= current neckline AND its target and stop have not been touched on the current bar.
Short: A confirmed bearish pattern has not previously retested AND current bar is more than the retest delay after its confirmation bar AND low <= current neckline AND high >= current neckline AND its target and stop have not been touched on the current bar.
Exit: long, a confirmed bullish pattern's high >= its target OR its low <= its stop, starting after the confirmation bar.
Exit: short, a confirmed bearish pattern's low <= its target OR its high >= its stop, starting after the confirmation bar.
