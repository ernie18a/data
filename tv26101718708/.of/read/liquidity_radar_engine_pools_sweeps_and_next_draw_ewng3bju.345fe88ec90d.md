Calculation: Pivot high/low = a high/low confirmed after its configured number of right-hand bars, using its configured number of left-hand bars. Chart, higher-timeframe, and lower-timeframe pivots may use different left and right counts.
Calculation: Pool ledger = add chart pivot highs and lows when confirmed, completed session highs and lows when each configured session ends, and the highs and lows of the last completed higher-timeframe periods when a new period starts. Classify highs as upper pools and lows as lower pools. If equal-level merging is enabled, merge each new level into the first untouched pool on the same side within an ATR-based tolerance, replacing its level with the touch-count-weighted average and classifying it as equal-level. Retain at most the configured number of pools, discarding the oldest. Process additions only within the configured detection history.
Calculation: Pool events = on a confirmed chart bar, an untouched upper/lower pool is broken if close is strictly above/below its level; otherwise it is swept if high/low penetrates its level by at least the configured ATR-based minimum depth and close is at or below/above the level. Use a configurable minimum price increment for the depth floor. A swept pool is reclaimed on a later bar when close is strictly below/above its level; if the body requirement is enabled, close must also be below/above open. Check reclaim before expiring an unreclaimed sweep whose age exceeds the reclaim window. A reclaimed pool is rejected on a later bar if high/low reaches within the ATR-based retest tolerance of the level and close is strictly below/above it; otherwise it is broken if close is strictly above/below it. Broken, expired, and rejected pools are no longer untouched.
Calculation: Story = each sweep starts a story if none is live, or replaces a live story if its source has at least the current story's priority, except that a sweep on the reclaimed story's target side with equal or lower priority is a leg and does not replace it. Priority is longer-period extremes, shorter-period extremes, equal levels, session extremes, then chart pivots. Process pools in ledger order. Store the owning pool's side, level, sweep bar, and sweep wick; only that pool's reclaim reclaims the story. A story is long after a lower-pool sweep and short after an upper-pool sweep. Reset its confirmation and trailing structure when replaced. Clear a completed story after a configurable delay; optionally clear an unclaimed or completed story at the configured daily reset.
Calculation: Structure confirmation = after the story's sweep and within a configurable chart-bar window, latch confirmation when chart close exceeds the latest confirmed lower-timeframe pivot high for a long story or falls below the latest confirmed lower-timeframe pivot low for a short story. The lower timeframe may equal the chart timeframe; use the last available lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. Confirmation may precede reclaim. It is optional.
Calculation: Story structure and stop = on the reclaim bar initialize the running extreme to high for a long story or low for a short story. Thereafter, a new higher high/lower low promotes the preceding pullback's lowest low/highest high to the confirmed trailing level and resets the pullback; otherwise update the pullback extreme. The stop is the confirmed trailing level if it is more protective than the sweep wick, otherwise the sweep wick. Complete the story if close crosses adversely through the sweep wick, if an unreclaimed story closes adversely through its swept level after the sweep bar, if an unreclaimed story exceeds the reclaim window, or if close crosses adversely through the confirmed trailing level. Evaluate these completions before fresh-entry decisions.
Calculation: Target = the nearest untouched upper pool strictly above close for a long story, or the nearest untouched lower pool strictly below close for a short story.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = track the latest confirmed pivot high and low calculated on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. On confirmed chart bars, if close exceeds the latest pivot high and bias is not already bullish, or close falls below the latest pivot low and bias is not already bearish, change bias to that direction unless the previous such break was within the configured chart-bar chop window, in which case set it neutral. Update the last-break bar even when the resulting bias is unchanged. When both breaks qualify, choose bullish.
Calculation: Chart structure = bullish when both the latest confirmed chart pivot high and low exceed their respective preceding pivots; bearish when both are lower; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend = bullish or bearish when at least two of chart structure, higher-timeframe bias, and the direction of the most recent pool break within a configured chart-bar window agree; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Draw direction = start with the side of the nearest untouched pool strictly beyond close, choosing the upper side on equal distance. Optionally favor the nonneutral trend, or higher-timeframe bias if trend is neutral, when its side has a valid pool and its distance disadvantage is less than a configured ATR multiple. When this differs from the previous draw direction, retain the previous direction until the new side is closer by a configured ATR multiple. If the retained side has no valid pool, use the other valid side or neutral. A live reclaimed story instead uses its own direction for the displayed next draw.
Long: An untouched lower pool is swept OR a swept lower pool is reclaimed OR a reclaimed lower pool is rejected.
Short: An untouched upper pool is swept OR a swept upper pool is reclaimed OR a reclaimed upper pool is rejected.
Long: A lower-pool story has structure confirmation on this bar.
Short: An upper-pool story has structure confirmation on this bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias changes to bullish OR trend is bullish OR draw direction is upward.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias changes to bearish OR trend is bearish OR draw direction is downward.
Long: A live lower-pool story is reclaimed AND optional structure confirmation is satisfied AND target exists AND stop < close AND (target - close) / (close - stop) >= the configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio AND (close - stop) / ATR <= the configured maximum stop width.
Short: A live upper-pool story is reclaimed AND optional structure confirmation is satisfied AND target exists AND stop > close AND (close - target) / (stop - close) >= the configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio AND (stop - close) / ATR <= the configured maximum stop width.
Exit: long, a live story exists AND its stop exists AND ((story is long AND entry price <= stop) OR (story is short AND entry price >= stop)).
Exit: short, a live story exists AND its stop exists AND ((story is long AND entry price <= stop) OR (story is short AND entry price >= stop)).
