Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression over the momentum lookback of close minus the average of (highest high + lowest low)/2 and the simple moving average of close over that lookback
Calculation: Bull volume estimate = volume × (close − low)/(high − low); if high = low, use volume/2. Bear volume estimate = volume − bull volume estimate. Smooth each estimate with an EMA using the same lookback; volume delta = smoothed bull volume − smoothed bear volume; smooth volume delta with a shorter EMA.
Calculation: OBV slope = cumulative signed volume (add volume when close rises from the prior bar, subtract when it falls, otherwise add zero) minus its value at the configured lookback bars earlier.
Calculation: Session VWAP deviation bands = session-anchored, volume-weighted average of hlc3 and its volume-weighted standard deviation; outer bands are VWAP ± the configured outer-band multiple × standard deviation.
Calculation: Confluence score = count of bullish or bearish votes among the EMA ribbon, squeeze momentum, volume pressure, and VWAP deviation layers; compare each score with the configured minimum layer count.
Calculation: Active session = bar time falls within the configured session window.
Calculation: Long stop = entry signal bar low − ATR × configured stop multiple; long targets = entry signal bar close + ATR × each configured target multiple. Short stop = entry signal bar high + ATR × configured stop multiple; short targets = entry signal bar close − ATR × each configured target multiple.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest value so far of entry signal bar low − ATR × configured trail multiple, updated on subsequent bars using each bar's low − ATR × trail multiple. Short trailing stop = lowest value so far of entry signal bar high + ATR × configured trail multiple, updated on subsequent bars using each bar's high + ATR × trail multiple.
Long: Bullish score >= configured minimum AND ADX >= configured minimum (when ADX filter is enabled) AND bar is in the configured active session (when session filter is enabled) AND this full entry condition was false on the preceding bar; bullish EMA vote: fast EMA > slow EMA > signal EMA AND close > fast EMA; bullish momentum vote: squeeze momentum > 0 AND greater than its prior-bar value; bullish volume vote: smoothed bull volume > smoothed bear volume AND volume delta > 0 AND OBV slope > 0; bullish VWAP vote: close > session VWAP AND close < upper outer deviation band (when VWAP filter is enabled; otherwise this layer votes bullish).
Short: Bearish score >= configured minimum AND ADX >= configured minimum (when ADX filter is enabled) AND bar is in the configured active session (when session filter is enabled) AND this full entry condition was false on the preceding bar; bearish EMA vote: fast EMA < slow EMA < signal EMA AND close < fast EMA; bearish momentum vote: squeeze momentum < 0 AND less than its prior-bar value; bearish volume vote: smoothed bear volume > smoothed bull volume AND volume delta < 0 AND OBV slope < 0; bearish VWAP vote: close < session VWAP AND close > lower outer deviation band (when VWAP filter is enabled; otherwise this layer votes bearish).
Exit: Long, high >= long first target (50% partial exit; only when at least one bar has elapsed since entry)
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR low <= long trailing stop (stop applies after at least one bar; trail applies after more than one bar)
Exit: Long, high >= long full target (full exit; only when at least one bar has elapsed since entry)
Exit: Short, low <= short first target (50% partial exit; only when at least one bar has elapsed since entry)
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR high >= short trailing stop (stop applies after at least one bar; trail applies after more than one bar)
Exit: Short, low <= short full target (full exit; only when at least one bar has elapsed since entry)
