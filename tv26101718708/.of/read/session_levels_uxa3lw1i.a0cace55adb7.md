Calculation: Previous-day high and low = the high and low of the most recent completed calendar day, using either all its bars or only its regular-session bars. Optionally skip weekends, New Year's Day and its Monday observance, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth and its Friday or Monday observance, Independence Day and its Friday or Monday observance, weekday July 3, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the following Friday, Christmas and its Friday or Monday observance, and weekday Christmas Eve. Determine dates and session membership in the configured time zone.
Long: The current bar is in the regular session AND its low is below the previous-day low AND the preceding bar's low is greater than or equal to the preceding bar's previous-day low.
Short: The current bar is in the regular session AND its high is above the previous-day high AND the preceding bar's high is less than or equal to the preceding bar's previous-day high.
