Calculation: Fractal High = the high of the bar N bars ago is strictly greater than the highs of the N bars before it and the N bars after it (same N on both sides; confirmed on the current bar once N bars have followed it).
Calculation: Fractal Low = the low of the bar N bars ago is strictly lower than the lows of the N bars before it and the N bars after it (same N on both sides; confirmed on the current bar once N bars have followed it).
Long: SMA(close, short-term) crosses above SMA(close, long-term) OR EMA(close, short-term) crosses above EMA(close, long-term) OR SMA(close, short-term) crosses above EMA(close, long-term) OR Fractal Low is confirmed on the current bar OR SuperTrend direction is uptrend (price above the SuperTrend line)
Short: SMA(close, short-term) crosses below SMA(close, long-term) OR EMA(close, short-term) crosses below EMA(close, long-term) OR Fractal High is confirmed on the current bar OR SuperTrend direction is downtrend (price below the SuperTrend line)
