Calculation: Trend SMA = simple moving average of close
Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close, or of low when close is above Trend SMA and high otherwise, if dynamic source is enabled
Calculation: Slow SMA = simple moving average of close, or of low when close is above Trend SMA and high otherwise, if dynamic source is enabled
Long: Fast EMA crosses above Slow SMA, Fast EMA, or Trend SMA, according to the selected entry pair; when the in-trend restriction is enabled, Fast EMA >= Slow SMA >= Trend SMA; when its entry filter is enabled, volume > multiplier × its average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, and RSI(14) is between its configured lower and upper bounds
Short: Fast EMA crosses below Slow SMA, Fast EMA, or Trend SMA, according to the selected entry pair; when the in-trend restriction is enabled, Fast EMA <= Slow SMA <= Trend SMA; when its entry filter is enabled, volume > multiplier × its average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, and RSI(14) is between its configured lower and upper bounds
Exit: Long, Fast EMA crosses below the selected pair (Fast EMA/Slow SMA, Fast EMA/Trend SMA, or Slow SMA/Trend SMA) OR, when enabled, close <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR close >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage); for a trailing stop, close <= the greater of close × (1 − stop-loss percentage) and the previous stop level
Exit: Short, Fast EMA crosses above the selected pair (Fast EMA/Slow SMA, Fast EMA/Trend SMA, or Slow SMA/Trend SMA) OR, when enabled, close >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR close <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage); for a trailing stop, close >= the lesser of close × (1 + stop-loss percentage) and the previous stop level
