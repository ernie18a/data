Calculation: Average True Range = 14-bar ATR on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Demand zone = from each confirmed pivot low (equal left/right lookback), a zone from the pivot low to pivot low + ATR × zone-width parameter; retain as fresh until swept or flipped.
Calculation: Supply zone = from each confirmed pivot high (equal left/right lookback), a zone from pivot high − ATR × zone-width parameter to the pivot high; retain as fresh until swept or flipped.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Wick rejection percentage = for a demand sweep or bullish failure, (close − low) / (high − low) × 100; for a supply sweep or bearish failure, (high − close) / (high − low) × 100.
Calculation: Stop = for a zone sweep, zone boundary beyond the sweep by ATR × stop-buffer parameter, or the sweep bar's low/high beyond the wick by that buffer, according to stop mode; for a failure entry, the pivot low/high beyond the pivot by that buffer.
Calculation: Target 2 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the absolute entry-to-stop distance multiplied by the target-risk multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a fresh demand zone is swept (low < zone bottom and (zone bottom − low) / zone bottom × 100 ≥ minimum penetration percentage) AND close > zone bottom AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiple) AND (wick filter disabled OR wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum) AND bar index ≥ last long signal bar + cooldown bars AND failure-entry mode is disabled.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a fresh supply zone is swept (high > zone top and (high − zone top) / zone top × 100 ≥ minimum penetration percentage) AND close < zone top AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiple) AND (wick filter disabled OR wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum) AND bar index ≥ last short signal bar + cooldown bars AND failure-entry mode is disabled.
Long: With failure-entry mode enabled, on a confirmed chart bar, low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND prior bar low ≥ that pivot low AND pivot low − low ≥ ATR × minimum failure-wick parameter AND wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiple) AND bar index ≥ last long signal bar + cooldown bars.
Short: With failure-entry mode enabled, on a confirmed chart bar, high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND prior bar high ≤ that pivot high AND high − pivot high ≥ ATR × minimum failure-wick parameter AND wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiple) AND bar index ≥ last short signal bar + cooldown bars.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target 2.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target 2.
