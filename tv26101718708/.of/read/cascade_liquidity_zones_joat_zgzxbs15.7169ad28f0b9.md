Calculation: Pivot = a high or low confirmed after an equal configurable number of bars on each side; use separate lookbacks for zones and failures.
Calculation: Pivot quality = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 40 × body/range, 35 × min(volume/volume SMA, 4)/4, and 25 × min(range/ATR, 3)/3, all evaluated at the pivot; use 0 for body/range when range is zero and 1 for either ratio when its denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Zones = on each confirmed pivot with positive quality, create a demand zone from pivot low to pivot low plus confirmation-bar ATR × width multiplier, or a supply zone from pivot high minus confirmation-bar ATR × width multiplier to pivot high. Separately for each side, compare the new midpoint to existing zones from newest to oldest; at the first midpoint distance below confirmation-bar ATR × separation multiplier, discard the new zone if the existing quality is at least as high, otherwise remove the existing zone and accept the new zone. If accepted, append it as fresh and remove the oldest zone on that side if the side's maximum count is exceeded. Process new zones before signals on the confirmation bar. A zone becomes nonfresh after its first sweep. Flips do not move zones between the demand and supply collections or change their sweep direction.
Calculation: Rejection percentage = 100 × (close − low)/(high − low) for a long and 100 × (high − close)/(high − low) for a short; use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Same-side cooldown = current bar index minus the most recent same-side signal bar index, initialized with the most recent index equal to zero; update immediately on each signal.
Calculation: Sweep stop = the swept zone's lower boundary for a long or upper boundary for a short, alternatively the signal bar's low for a long or high for a short, minus ATR × buffer for a long or plus ATR × buffer for a short, using signal-bar ATR. Sweep target = entry price plus the absolute entry-to-stop distance × reward/risk multiplier for a long, or minus that distance × reward/risk multiplier for a short. Store both at entry.
Long: Sweep mode AND a fresh demand zone exists AND low < zone lower boundary AND (zone lower boundary > 0 ? 100 × (zone lower boundary − low)/zone lower boundary : 0) >= minimum penetration percentage AND close > zone lower boundary AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × sweep volume multiplier) AND (rejection filter disabled OR long rejection percentage >= sweep rejection threshold) AND same-side cooldown >= sweep cooldown.
Short: Sweep mode AND a fresh supply zone exists AND high > zone upper boundary AND (zone upper boundary > 0 ? 100 × (high − zone upper boundary)/zone upper boundary : 0) >= minimum penetration percentage AND close < zone upper boundary AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × sweep volume multiplier) AND (rejection filter disabled OR short rejection percentage >= sweep rejection threshold) AND same-side cooldown >= sweep cooldown.
Long: Failure mode AND low < most recently confirmed failure pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND previous low >= that same pivot low AND pivot low − low >= ATR × failure wick multiplier AND long rejection percentage >= failure rejection threshold AND (failure volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × failure volume multiplier) AND same-side cooldown >= failure cooldown.
Short: Failure mode AND high > most recently confirmed failure pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND previous high <= that same pivot high AND high − pivot high >= ATR × failure wick multiplier AND short rejection percentage >= failure rejection threshold AND (failure volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × failure volume multiplier) AND same-side cooldown >= failure cooldown.
Exit: long, sweep-mode position AND a bar after entry AND (high >= stored sweep target OR low <= stored sweep stop).
Exit: short, sweep-mode position AND a bar after entry AND (low <= stored sweep target OR high >= stored sweep stop).
