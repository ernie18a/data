Calculation: WaveTrend = WT1 is an EMA of the channel index ((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3))))); WT2 is a shorter simple moving average of WT1. Use the configured channel and smoothing lookbacks.
Calculation: Money Flow = the configured lookback simple moving average of ((close − open) / (high − low)) × a multiplier, minus an offset.
Calculation: EMA = close EMA; use a longer lookback for the trend EMA and a shorter lookback for the pullback EMA. Calculate on the configured EMA timeframe; if it differs from the chart timeframe, use the security value with lookahead off (the last completed bar for a higher timeframe).
Long: close > trend EMA AND WT1 != 0 AND WT2 < 0 AND WT1 crosses above WT2 AND WT2 <= the oversold threshold AND WT1 >= WT2 AND Money Flow > 0 AND close > pullback EMA AND prior close < prior pullback EMA AND ((closes at offsets 2, 3, and 4 are all above their pullback EMAs) OR (closes at offsets 5, 6, and 7 are all above their pullback EMAs) OR (closes at offsets 8, 9, and 10 are all above their pullback EMAs))
Short: close < trend EMA AND WT1 != 0 AND WT2 > 0 AND WT1 crosses below WT2 AND WT2 >= the overbought threshold AND WT1 <= WT2 AND Money Flow < 0 AND close < pullback EMA AND prior close > prior pullback EMA AND ((closes at offsets 2, 3, and 4 are all below their pullback EMAs) OR (closes at offsets 5, 6, and 7 are all below their pullback EMAs) OR (closes at offsets 8, 9, and 10 are all below their pullback EMAs))
Exit: long, low <= entry-time ATR-based stop OR high >= entry-time ATR-based target OR low <= entry-time lowest low minus buffer OR high >= entry price + (entry price − entry-time lowest low) × profit factor + buffer
Exit: short, high >= entry-time ATR-based stop OR low <= entry-time ATR-based target OR high >= entry-time highest high plus buffer OR low <= entry price − (entry-time highest high − entry price) × profit factor − buffer
