Calculation: Active pools = separately store confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows using equal left and right lookbacks; add each pivot on its confirmation bar, retain only the most recently confirmed pools up to a configurable limit per side, and remove the selected pool whenever it is swept, even if the sweep does not produce a signal.
Calculation: Selected high pool = the lowest active pivot-high pool strictly above the current low; selected low pool = the highest active pivot-low pool strictly below the current high. Select pools after adding newly confirmed pivots and before removing swept pools.
Calculation: Absorption = volume SMA > 0 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND body-to-range ratio <= threshold, where body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) if high > low, otherwise 0.
Long: low < selected low pool AND (close > selected low pool in rejection mode OR close < selected low pool - ATR × displacement multiplier in displacement mode) AND (absorption confirmation disabled OR absorption).
Short: high > selected high pool AND (close < selected high pool in rejection mode OR close > selected high pool + ATR × displacement multiplier in displacement mode) AND (absorption confirmation disabled OR absorption).
