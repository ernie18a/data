Calculation: Directional notation = For each side let d be +1 for long and -1 for short. Let reference R be the highest high for long or lowest low for short over the reference lookback excluding the current bar. Let extreme E be high for long or low for short. Let A be ATR, falling back to true range when ATR is unavailable, with a positive price floor. Let candle range C be max(high - low, positive price floor).
Calculation: Sweep = d × (E - R) > A × minimum sweep depth, where minimum sweep depth is max(0, sweep-depth parameter + sensitivity adjustment). If both sides sweep, select the side with the higher continuation score; select long on a tie. Otherwise select the side that swept.
Calculation: Directional measures = Sweep depth is d × (E - R) / A; close acceptance is d × (close - R) / A; close location is (close - low) / C for long and (high - close) / C for short; rejection is (high - close) / C for long and (close - low) / C for short; body efficiency is abs(close - open) / C. Express location, rejection, and efficiency as percentages. Volume ratio is volume divided by its SMA, or 1 when the SMA is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Trend state = Supported when close is on the directional side of the longer EMA and the shorter EMA is on or beyond the longer EMA in that direction; neutral when either of those comparisons holds; otherwise conflicting.
Calculation: Plan levels = Trigger = R + d × A × trigger-buffer parameter; invalidation = R - d × A × invalidation-buffer parameter; risk distance = max(d × (trigger - invalidation), A × minimum-risk parameter); target = trigger + d × max(risk distance × target-risk multiple, A × target-distance parameter, max(prior highest high - prior lowest low, A) × range-fraction parameter). The prior extrema use the reference lookback and exclude the current bar. Target room = max(d × (target - close) / A, 0).
Calculation: Score normalization = High-good(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / max(strong - weak, positive floor), 0, 1) × 100. Low-good(x, strong, weak) = clamp((weak - x) / max(weak - strong, positive floor), 0, 1) × 100. Peak score uses high-good below or at its ideal and low-good above its ideal.
Calculation: Continuation score = Clamp to 0–100 the weighted sum of peak-scored sweep depth, close score, high-good volume ratio, trend score, and high-good target room, minus weighted reversal risk. Close score is the weighted sum of high-good close acceptance, close location, and body efficiency. Trend score has separate values for supported, neutral, and conflicting states. Reversal risk is the clamped 0–100 sum of weighted rejection, weighted (100 - close score), a trend-state penalty, and a low-volume penalty when volume ratio is below its threshold. All weights, scoring bounds, ideal depth, trend scores, and penalties are parameters; the minimum sweep depth is the weak bound for sweep depth and sensitivity selects the close-location weak bound.
Calculation: Ready = Selected side has a sweep AND close is beyond R in that direction by more than A × (trigger-buffer parameter + sensitivity close adjustment) AND continuation score >= ready threshold AND, if volume confirmation is selected, volume ratio >= its confirmation threshold AND, if strict confirmation is selected, close location >= its sensitivity threshold and trend state is supported or neutral. A ready event occurs when ready becomes true or the selected side changes while ready remains true.
Calculation: Watch = Selected side has a sweep AND continuation score >= watch threshold AND ready is false. A watch event occurs when watch becomes true or the selected side changes while watch remains true; watch labels are additionally separated by a minimum number of bars.
Calculation: Active plan = Store the selected side, invalidation, target, and current bar on each ready event, or on a watch event if no unresolved plan remains within its review window. A new ready event replaces the previous plan. A plan is active through its review-window limit and resolves on the first subsequent bar touching invalidation or target; invalidation takes precedence when both are touched on the same bar.
Long: Selected side = long AND ready event = true
Long: Selected side = long AND watch event = true AND bars since the preceding watch label >= watch-label spacing
Short: Selected side = short AND ready event = true
Short: Selected side = short AND watch event = true AND bars since the preceding watch label >= watch-label spacing
Exit: long, active plan side = long AND current bar is after the plan bar AND plan is unresolved AND (low <= stored invalidation OR high >= stored target)
Exit: short, active plan side = short AND current bar is after the plan bar AND plan is unresolved AND (high >= stored invalidation OR low <= stored target)
