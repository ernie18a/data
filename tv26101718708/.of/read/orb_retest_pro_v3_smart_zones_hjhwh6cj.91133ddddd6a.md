Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured daily session; retain them after the session until the next day or session start.
Calculation: Breakout bar = the first chart bar after the opening-range session and since the latest daily or session-start reset that satisfies the corresponding breakout condition. An upward breakout has high >= opening-range high + breakout distance AND close > opening-range high; a downward breakout has low <= opening-range low - breakout distance AND close < opening-range low. If the optional displacement filter is enabled, also require absolute close - open >= ATR multiplied by a displacement factor. Do not replace a breakout bar with a later breakout in the same reset period.
Calculation: Confluence zones = keep the most recently created zone of each type and direction, including across daily resets; replace it when another zone of that type and direction is created. A bullish fair value gap is [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; a bearish fair value gap is [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. A bullish inversion gap copies the latest bearish fair value gap when close crosses above its upper bound; a bearish inversion gap copies the latest bullish fair value gap when close crosses below its lower bound. Evaluate crosses against the current and previous bars' respective latest gap bounds. A bullish order block is [previous low, previous open] when the previous bar is bearish AND current close > previous high AND current close > current open; a bearish order block is [previous open, previous high] when the previous bar is bullish AND current close < previous low AND current close < current open. If strict structure is enabled, an order block also requires current absolute close - open > the moving average of absolute close - open, including the current bar. A bullish breaker copies the latest bearish order block whenever close > its upper bound; a bearish breaker copies the latest bullish order block whenever close < its lower bound. Evaluate zone creation in the order stated, so a newly created zone can be used by a later calculation on the same bar. Each zone type participates only if its detection is enabled; inversion gaps depend on detected fair value gaps and breakers depend on detected order blocks. A zone is in confluence when its age in chart bars <= the confluence lookback AND current low <= its upper bound AND current high >= its lower bound. Zone-box removal does not remove these stored zones.
Long: Chart timeframe is within the optional permitted range AND the long signal is enabled AND the current bar is after the opening-range session AND the upward breakout bar exists AND current bar index > upward breakout bar index AND current bar index - upward breakout bar index <= retest window AND low <= opening-range high + retest tolerance AND high >= opening-range high - retest tolerance AND (close confirmation is disabled OR close > opening-range high) AND (confluence is optional OR at least one enabled bullish confluence zone is in confluence) AND (the per-side limit is disabled OR no long signal has occurred since the latest daily or session-start reset) AND (the total limit is disabled OR no signal has occurred since that reset).
Short: Chart timeframe is within the optional permitted range AND the short signal is enabled AND the current bar is after the opening-range session AND the downward breakout bar exists AND current bar index > downward breakout bar index AND current bar index - downward breakout bar index <= retest window AND high >= opening-range low - retest tolerance AND low <= opening-range low + retest tolerance AND (close confirmation is disabled OR close < opening-range low) AND (confluence is optional OR at least one enabled bearish confluence zone is in confluence) AND (the per-side limit is disabled OR no short signal has occurred since the latest daily or session-start reset) AND (the total limit is disabled OR no signal has occurred since that reset).
