Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high from the start through the end of the configured opening session; opening-range low = lowest low over the same bars
Calculation: Real-body size = absolute value of close minus open
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; zone spans from that earlier high to current low
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier; zone spans from current high to that earlier low
Calculation: Bullish order block = after a bullish candle closes above the prior high (and, if strict structure is enabled, its body exceeds the 10-bar average body), the prior candle was bearish; zone spans from its low to its open
Calculation: Bearish order block = after a bearish candle closes below the prior low (and, if strict structure is enabled, its body exceeds the 10-bar average body), the prior candle was bullish; zone spans from its open to its high
Calculation: Bullish inversion fair value gap = close crosses above the most recent bearish fair value gap's upper boundary; zone is that gap
Calculation: Bearish inversion fair value gap = close crosses below the most recent bullish fair value gap's lower boundary; zone is that gap
Calculation: Bullish breaker = most recent bearish order block after close rises above its upper boundary; zone is that order block
Calculation: Bearish breaker = most recent bullish order block after close falls below its lower boundary; zone is that order block
Long: after the opening session, a bar closes above the opening-range high and its high reaches at least the configured minimum breakout distance above it, with real-body size at least the configured ATR multiple if the ATR displacement filter is enabled; on a later bar within the configured retest window, low <= opening-range high + retest tolerance AND high >= opening-range high - retest tolerance AND (close > opening-range high if close confirmation is enabled) AND (the bar overlaps at least one bullish fair value gap, inversion fair value gap, order block, or breaker created within the configured confluence lookback if confluence is required) AND the configured daily signal limits allow a signal
Short: after the opening session, a bar closes below the opening-range low and its low reaches at least the configured minimum breakout distance below it, with real-body size at least the configured ATR multiple if the ATR displacement filter is enabled; on a later bar within the configured retest window, high >= opening-range low - retest tolerance AND low <= opening-range low + retest tolerance AND (close < opening-range low if close confirmation is enabled) AND (the bar overlaps at least one bearish fair value gap, inversion fair value gap, order block, or breaker created within the configured confluence lookback if confluence is required) AND the configured daily signal limits allow a signal
