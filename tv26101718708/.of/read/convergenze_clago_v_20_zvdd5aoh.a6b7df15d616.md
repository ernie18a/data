Calculation: Wave = On each chart bar, take the configured price source from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, held forward between higher-timeframe closes. Apply the selected RMA, EMA, or SMA on chart bars to obtain five averages with successively longer lengths. Replace unavailable averages with zero. For each of the four longer averages, calculate momentum as scale × (shortest average − longer average) / longer average, or zero if the denominator is zero. Wave is the mean of those four momentums weighted by longest average length / corresponding longer average length, multiplied by a volume factor. The volume factor is an elevated multiplier when volume weighting is enabled AND volume > volume SMA × volume threshold; otherwise it is 1.
Calculation: Wave signal = DEMA of Wave using the shortest signal length when ADX > the upper threshold, the intermediate signal length when ADX > the trend threshold, and the longest signal length otherwise. Evaluate these tests in that order.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = Cumulative sum of volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), using zero delta when high = low. Optionally reset the sum at each configured calendar period boundary. Its signal is an EMA of the cumulative sum.
Calculation: Wave velocity = EMA of the bar-to-bar change in Wave.
Calculation: Confirmed Wave peak = Wave at the pivot bar is a pivot high with configured left and right confirmation lengths AND exceeds a positive amplitude threshold AND either the peak ADX filter is disabled OR current ADX > trend threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed Wave peak are at least the cooldown length. Update the previous peak bar only when this condition is met.
Calculation: Confirmed Wave trough = Wave at the pivot bar is a pivot low with configured left and right confirmation lengths AND is below the negative amplitude threshold AND either the peak ADX filter is disabled OR current ADX > trend threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed Wave trough are at least the cooldown length. Update the previous trough bar only when this condition is met.
Long: Shortest price EMA > intermediate price EMA AND intermediate price EMA > longest price EMA AND cumulative volume delta >= its signal AND Wave > Wave signal.
Short: Shortest price EMA < intermediate price EMA AND intermediate price EMA < longest price EMA AND cumulative volume delta < its signal AND Wave < Wave signal.
Long: ADX > trend threshold AND ((Wave > 0 AND previous Wave <= 0) OR (Wave > 0 AND Wave velocity > 0 AND previous Wave velocity <= 0)).
Short: ADX > trend threshold AND ((Wave < 0 AND previous Wave >= 0) OR (Wave < 0 AND Wave velocity < 0 AND previous Wave velocity >= 0)).
Exit: long, shortest price EMA <= intermediate price EMA OR intermediate price EMA <= longest price EMA OR Wave <= Wave signal.
Exit: short, shortest price EMA >= intermediate price EMA OR intermediate price EMA >= longest price EMA OR Wave >= Wave signal.
Exit: long, confirmed Wave peak.
Exit: short, confirmed Wave trough.
