Calculation: Wave = volume-adjusted average of normalized differences between a smoothed higher-timeframe price average and several progressively longer smoothed averages; update separate rising and falling components only while the corresponding unsmoothed value is positive or negative. Use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Wave signal = double EMA of Wave, using a shorter, medium, or longer smoothing length according to whether ADX is above the trend threshold, above 25, or neither.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative per-bar volume delta, where buy volume is volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and sell volume is volume × (high − close) / (high − low); use half the volume for each when high = low. Reset continuously, daily, or weekly according to the configured reset mode. CVD signal is its EMA.
Calculation: Confirmed wave peak/trough = a wave pivot high/low confirmed by bars to its right, respectively above/below a minimum amplitude, with ADX above the trend threshold when the peak ADX filter is enabled, and at least the configured cooldown bars since the previous same-side pivot.
Long: (shorter-period EMA of close > medium-period EMA of close AND medium-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND CVD >= CVD signal AND Wave > Wave signal AND not already long) OR (ADX > trend threshold AND (Wave crosses above 0 OR (Wave > 0 AND EMA of Wave change crosses above 0)))
Short: (shorter-period EMA of close < medium-period EMA of close AND medium-period EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND CVD < CVD signal AND Wave < Wave signal AND not already short) OR (ADX > trend threshold AND (Wave crosses below 0 OR (Wave < 0 AND EMA of Wave change crosses below 0)))
Exit: long, EMA stack is no longer bullish OR Wave <= Wave signal OR confirmed positive wave peak AND Wave > 0 AND lower-timeframe Wave > 0
Exit: short, EMA stack is no longer bearish OR Wave >= Wave signal OR confirmed negative wave trough AND Wave < 0 AND lower-timeframe Wave < 0
