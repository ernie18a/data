Calculation: bullish/bearish trend = price is above/below both EMAs on the relevant higher timeframe’s still-forming bar; for 1-hour and 4-hour trend pairs, price is above/below the faster EMA and the faster EMA is above/below the slower EMA on that higher timeframe’s still-forming bar
Calculation: premium/discount = close is above/below the midpoint of the chart-timeframe high-low range over a longer lookback; deep premium/discount = close is above 70% / below 30% of that range
Calculation: bullish/bearish order block = the previous bar is bearish/bullish, the current close is above its high/below its low, and the current body is greater than 1.2 times the previous bar’s range; an order block is active when the current bar overlaps its range and it is younger than the configured order-block lookback
Calculation: recent sell-side/buy-side sweep = within the configured recent-bar window, a bar made a lower low/higher high than the preceding liquidity lookback, closed bullish/bearish, and closed in the upper/lower 40% of its range
Calculation: near bullish/bearish fair-value gap = the current bar overlaps the most recent unfilled three-bar gap, formed when current low exceeds high two bars earlier / current high is below low two bars earlier, with gap size at least the configured minimum
Calculation: bullish/bearish break of structure = close is above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low; pivots use equal left and right confirmation spans
Calculation: volume confirmation = volume exceeds 1.2 times its 20-bar average
Calculation: daily score = 2 points if 4-hour and daily trends align in either direction; 2 if an order block is in the matching premium/discount zone and 4-hour trend agrees; 1 if either sweep is recent; 1 for volume confirmation; 1 during configured optimal entry hours and outside avoid hours; 1 if either break of structure occurs; 1 if either fair-value gap is near; plus 1
Calculation: weekly score = 2 points if weekly and monthly trends align; 2 if daily and weekly trends align; 2 if weekly trend aligns with deep premium/discount; 1 if either sweep is recent; 1 if either order block is active; 1 if either order block is fresh; plus 1
Calculation: monthly score = 2 points if monthly and weekly trends align; 2 if an order block is active in matching deep premium/discount; 2 if either sweep is recent; 2 if daily, weekly, and monthly trends all align; 1 if close is below 20% or above 80% of the swing range; plus 1
Long: daily score >= configured minimum AND bullish 4-hour trend AND discount AND (active bullish order block OR recent sell-side sweep OR near bullish fair-value gap) AND outside avoid hours OR weekly score >= configured minimum AND bullish weekly trend AND bullish daily trend AND discount AND (active bullish order block OR recent sell-side sweep) OR monthly score >= configured minimum AND bullish monthly trend AND bullish weekly trend AND deep discount AND active bullish order block
Short: daily score >= configured minimum AND bearish 4-hour trend AND premium AND (active bearish order block OR recent buy-side sweep OR near bearish fair-value gap) AND outside avoid hours OR weekly score >= configured minimum AND bearish weekly trend AND bearish daily trend AND premium AND (active bearish order block OR recent buy-side sweep) OR monthly score >= configured minimum AND bearish monthly trend AND bearish weekly trend AND deep premium AND active bearish order block
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus configured stop distance OR high >= entry price plus configured stop distance times configured reward/risk ratio
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus configured stop distance OR low <= entry price minus configured stop distance times configured reward/risk ratio
