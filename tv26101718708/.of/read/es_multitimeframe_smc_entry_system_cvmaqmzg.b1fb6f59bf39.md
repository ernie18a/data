Calculation: Timeframe trends = On each of four successively higher timeframes, calculate short- and long-period EMAs of close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. For the lowest of these timeframes, bullish means chart close > short EMA > long EMA and bearish means chart close < short EMA < long EMA. For each other timeframe, bullish means chart close > both EMAs and bearish means chart close < both EMAs.
Calculation: Range position = (close − lowest chart low over the swing lookback) / (highest chart high over the same lookback − lowest chart low over that lookback); use the range midpoint when the denominator is zero. Discount and premium mean range position below and above the midpoint; deep discount and deep premium use stricter lower and upper thresholds; extreme zones use still stricter thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish order block = The previous candle is bearish AND close > previous high AND current candle body > previous candle range × an impulse multiplier. Store the previous high, low and bar index, replacing the previous bullish block. Bearish order block = the previous candle is bullish AND close < previous low AND current bearish body > previous candle range × the same multiplier; store its previous high, low and bar index separately. A block is active when current low ≤ its stored high AND current high ≥ its stored low AND its age measured from the stored bar is < the order-block lookback. A block is fresh when its age is < the freshness lookback, whether or not it is active.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier AND their difference ≥ the minimum gap. Bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars earlier AND their difference ≥ the minimum gap. Store the latest gap boundaries separately by direction. A stored gap is near when current low ≤ its upper boundary AND current high ≥ its lower boundary. After evaluating nearness, clear a bullish gap when low ≤ its lower boundary and a bearish gap when high ≥ its upper boundary; a newly detected gap replaces the previous gap before the nearness test.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < the lowest low over the liquidity lookback ending at the previous bar AND close > open AND close > low + a sweep fraction × (high − low). Bearish sweep = high > the highest high over that lookback AND close < open AND close < high − the same fraction × (high − low). A sweep is recent from its occurrence through the configured maximum age, inclusive.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > the most recently confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = close < the most recently confirmed pivot low. Confirm pivots using equal numbers of chart bars on both sides.
Calculation: First score = 1 + 2 if the lowest and second-lowest timeframe trends agree in either direction + 2 if an active bullish block, discount and lowest-timeframe bullish trend coincide OR an active bearish block, premium and lowest-timeframe bearish trend coincide + 1 each for any recent sweep, volume > its SMA × a volume multiplier, the timestamp being in an optimal window AND outside an avoid window, either structure break, and nearness to either gap.
Calculation: Second score = 1 + 2 each if the third and fourth timeframe trends agree, if the second and third timeframe trends agree, and if deep discount coincides with the third-timeframe bullish trend OR deep premium coincides with its bearish trend + 1 each for any recent sweep, either active block, and either fresh block. Trend agreement may be bullish or bearish.
Calculation: Third score = 1 + 2 each if the third and fourth timeframe trends agree, if an active bullish block coincides with deep discount OR an active bearish block coincides with deep premium, if either sweep is recent, and if the second, third and fourth timeframe trends all agree + 1 if range position is in either extreme zone. Trend agreement may be bullish or bearish.

Short: Bearish order block is active.
Long: Bullish fair value gap is present.
Short: Bearish fair value gap is present.
Long: Bullish sweep is present.
Short: Bearish sweep is present.
Long: Bullish structure break is present.
Short: Bearish structure break is present.
Long: First score ≥ its threshold AND lowest-timeframe trend is bullish AND range position is in discount AND (bullish block is active OR bullish sweep is recent OR bullish gap is near) AND timestamp is outside the avoid windows.
Short: First score ≥ its threshold AND lowest-timeframe trend is bearish AND range position is in premium AND (bearish block is active OR bearish sweep is recent OR bearish gap is near) AND timestamp is outside the avoid windows.
Long: Second score ≥ its threshold AND second- and third-timeframe trends are bullish AND range position is in discount AND (bullish block is active OR bullish sweep is recent).
Short: Second score ≥ its threshold AND second- and third-timeframe trends are bearish AND range position is in premium AND (bearish block is active OR bearish sweep is recent).
Long: Third score ≥ its threshold AND third- and fourth-timeframe trends are bullish AND range position is in deep discount AND bullish block is active.
Short: Third score ≥ its threshold AND third- and fourth-timeframe trends are bearish AND range position is in deep premium AND bearish block is active.
Exit: long, low ≤ the latest corresponding entry-signal close − its stop distance OR high ≥ that close + its stop distance × its reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: short, high ≥ the latest corresponding entry-signal close + its stop distance OR low ≤ that close − its stop distance × its reward-to-risk ratio.
