Calculation: SuperTrend = with n = one lookback used for both SMA and ATR, and m = multiplier: basicUpper = SMA(close,n) + m*ATR(n); basicLower = SMA(close,n) - m*ATR(n); finalLower = (prev close > prev finalLower) ? max(basicLower, prev finalLower) : basicLower; finalUpper = (prev close < prev finalUpper) ? min(basicUpper, prev finalUpper) : basicUpper; dir = close > prev finalUpper ? 1 : close < prev finalLower ? -1 : prev dir (initial 1); ST = dir==1 ? finalLower : finalUpper.
Calculation: HTF_dir = dir of the same SuperTrend computed on bars of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming (the one containing the current bar).
Calculation: Stoch %K = unsmoothed (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) * 100 over a lookback; %D = SMA of %K over a period shorter than the %K lookback.
Calculation: FastLongVotes = count of true among: [CCI < CCI oversold level], [Williams %R < its oversold level], [close > ST AND dir == 1].
Calculation: FastShortVotes = count of true among: [CCI > CCI overbought level], [Williams %R > its overbought level], [close < ST AND dir == -1].
Calculation: LongVotes = count of true among: [RSI < RSI oversold level], [MACD line crosses above MACD signal line], [close < lower Bollinger Band (SMA of close +/- k*stdev)], [Stoch %K < stochastic oversold level AND %K crosses above %D], [fast SMA of close crosses above slow SMA of close (fast period shorter than slow period)], [CCI < CCI oversold level], [Williams %R < its oversold level], [close > ST AND dir == 1].
Calculation: ShortVotes = count of true among: [RSI > RSI overbought level], [MACD line crosses below MACD signal line], [close > upper Bollinger Band], [Stoch %K > stochastic overbought level AND %K crosses below %D], [fast SMA crosses below slow SMA], [CCI > CCI overbought level], [Williams %R > its overbought level], [close < ST AND dir == -1].
Long: (FastLongVotes >= fast-vote threshold OR LongVotes >= total-vote threshold) AND volume > SMA(volume) AND HTF_dir == 1
Short: (FastShortVotes >= fast-vote threshold OR ShortVotes >= total-vote threshold) AND volume > SMA(volume) AND HTF_dir == -1
