Calculation: SuperTrend = SMA(close, length) ± multiplier × ATR(length); carry the lower band upward as max(current lower band, prior final lower band) when prior close is above it, otherwise reset it to the basic lower band; carry the upper band downward as min(current upper band, prior final upper band) when prior close is below it, otherwise reset it to the basic upper band. Direction turns up when close crosses above the prior final upper band, down when close crosses below the prior final lower band, and otherwise retains its prior direction. The higher-timeframe version uses the same parameters and the current higher-timeframe bar, including while it is forming.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of enabled true conditions among RSI below oversold; MACD line crosses above signal line; close below lower Bollinger Band; Stochastic %K below oversold and crosses above %D; short-period SMA crosses above long-period SMA; CCI below oversold; Williams %R below oversold; close above SuperTrend with up direction.
Calculation: Bearish count = number of enabled true conditions among RSI above overbought; MACD line crosses below signal line; close above upper Bollinger Band; Stochastic %K above overbought and crosses below %D; short-period SMA crosses below long-period SMA; CCI above overbought; Williams %R above overbought; close below SuperTrend with down direction.
Calculation: Fast bullish count = number of enabled true conditions among CCI below oversold, Williams %R below oversold, and close above SuperTrend with up direction.
Calculation: Fast bearish count = number of enabled true conditions among CCI above overbought, Williams %R above overbought, and close below SuperTrend with down direction.
Long: (fast-trio enabled AND fast bullish count ≥ its threshold OR bullish count ≥ its threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 20-bar SMA of volume) AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction is up)
Short: (fast-trio enabled AND fast bearish count ≥ its threshold OR bearish count ≥ its threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 20-bar SMA of volume) AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction is down)
