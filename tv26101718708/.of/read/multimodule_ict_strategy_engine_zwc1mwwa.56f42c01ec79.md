Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot resistance/support = the latest pivot high/low, requiring the same lookback number of bars on each side
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; zone = [high two bars earlier, current low], CE = zone midpoint
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; zone = [current high, low two bars earlier], CE = zone midpoint
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current bar is bullish, prior bar is bearish, current close > prior open, and current open < prior close
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current bar is bearish, prior bar is bullish, current close < prior open, and current open > prior close
Long: Position is flat AND bar is confirmed AND (after the required consecutive closes above latest pivot resistance, a later bar has low <= that breakout level and close > it OR current low < previous completed higher-timeframe weekly low and prior bar low < that level and (bullish engulfing OR current bar is bullish and open - low > 2 × abs(close - open) and abs(close - open) > 0) and close > the chart-timeframe macro moving average OR an active bullish displacement setup has not expired, current low has not fallen below its origin, low <= its zone top, and close >= its zone bottom OR an active unfilled, untraded bullish FVG has not expired, low <= its configured entry level (CE or upper edge), and close > its bottom)
Short: Position is flat AND bar is confirmed AND (after the required consecutive closes below latest pivot support, a later bar has high >= that breakout level and close < it OR current high > previous completed higher-timeframe weekly high and prior bar high > that level and (bearish engulfing OR current bar is bearish and high - open > 2 × abs(close - open) and abs(close - open) > 0) and close < the chart-timeframe macro moving average OR an active bearish displacement setup has not expired, current high has not risen above its origin, high >= its zone bottom, and close <= its zone top OR an active unfilled, untraded bearish FVG has not expired, high >= its configured entry level (CE or lower edge), and close < its top)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR × stop multiplier
