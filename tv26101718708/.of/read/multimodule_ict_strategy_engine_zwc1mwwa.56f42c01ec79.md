Calculation: Resistance and support = the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, using the same number of chart bars on each side; update each when its pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Previous higher-timeframe high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, falling back to the current chart bar's high and low when unavailable.
Calculation: Accepted breakout level = the resistance or support recorded on the first of a configurable number of consecutive closes above the then-current resistance or below the then-current support. Maintain separate bullish and bearish pending levels until their respective retest signals; both resistance and support must exist while counting. A retest may occur on the acceptance bar.
Calculation: Sweep state = set the bullish state when the previous chart bar's low is below the current previous higher-timeframe low, and set the bearish state when the previous chart bar's high is above the current previous higher-timeframe high. Update these states on every completed bar. Clear the corresponding state after its entry, or when its module is enabled, no earlier module has signaled on the bar, sufficient moving-average history exists, and the corresponding reaction is absent.
Calculation: Bullish reaction = (close > open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close AND previous close < previous open) OR (close > open AND open - low > 2 * abs(close - open) AND abs(close - open) > 0). Bearish reaction = (close < open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close AND previous close > previous open) OR (close < open AND high - open > 2 * abs(close - open) AND abs(close - open) > 0).
Calculation: Displacement zone = on a bar with ATR > 0, abs(close - open) > ATR * a configurable multiplier and abs(close - open) / (high - low) > a configurable ratio, record the latest bullish zone when close > open AND close > resistance, or the latest bearish zone when close < open AND close < support. Its origin is that bar's open, its other boundary is its close, and its midpoint is the average of those prices. Replace an older zone of the same direction on a new displacement. A zone remains active through a configurable maximum age, including its creation bar, unless a bullish zone's low falls below its origin, a bearish zone's high rises above its origin, or its entry signals.
Calculation: Gap = when high two chart bars ago < current low, record a bullish gap with bottom at that earlier high and top at current low; when low two chart bars ago > current high, record a bearish gap with top at that earlier low and bottom at current high. Record gaps from the fourth chart bar onward. Track gaps newest first; after processing each bar retain at most a configurable number by removing the oldest. Remove a gap when its age exceeds a configurable limit or on the bar after it is filled. A bullish gap is filled when close <= its bottom; a bearish gap is filled when close >= its top. A gap can signal only once and cannot signal on a bar that fills it. Its entry level is either its midpoint or its near edge, selected by configuration: the top for bullish gaps and the bottom for bearish gaps.
Calculation: Entry priority = evaluate enabled modules in this order: accepted breakout retest, higher-timeframe sweep, sweep reaction, displacement zone, gap. Within each module evaluate long before short; evaluate gaps newest first. Allow at most one entry signal per completed chart bar and only when no trade is open.
Long: Entry priority permits an accepted breakout retest AND a bullish accepted breakout level is pending AND low <= that level AND close > that level.
Short: Entry priority permits an accepted breakout retest AND a bearish accepted breakout level is pending AND high >= that level AND close < that level.
Long: Entry priority permits a higher-timeframe sweep AND sufficient initial chart bars exist AND low < previous higher-timeframe low AND close > previous higher-timeframe low.
Short: Entry priority permits a higher-timeframe sweep AND sufficient initial chart bars exist AND high > previous higher-timeframe high AND close < previous higher-timeframe high.
Long: Entry priority permits a sweep reaction AND chart-bar count exceeds the moving-average lookback AND close > the configurable EMA or SMA of close AND bullish sweep state is set AND bullish reaction is true.
Short: Entry priority permits a sweep reaction AND chart-bar count exceeds the moving-average lookback AND close < the configurable EMA or SMA of close AND bearish sweep state is set AND bearish reaction is true.
Long: Entry priority permits a displacement zone AND a bullish zone is active AND low >= its origin AND low <= its displacement close AND close >= its midpoint.
Short: Entry priority permits a displacement zone AND a bearish zone is active AND high <= its origin AND high >= its displacement close AND close <= its midpoint.
Long: Entry priority permits a gap AND an unfilled, untraded bullish gap is within its age limit AND low <= its entry level AND close > its bottom.
Short: Entry priority permits a gap AND an unfilled, untraded bearish gap is within its age limit AND high >= its entry level AND close < its top.
Calculation: Exit distance = ATR on the entry-signal bar times a configurable multiplier; if ATR is unavailable, use a configurable fraction of the entry-signal bar's close in place of ATR. Fix both exit levels from the entry-signal bar's close.
Exit: long, low <= entry-signal close - exit distance OR high >= entry-signal close + exit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry-signal close + exit distance OR low <= entry-signal close - exit distance.
