Calculation: Heikin-Ashi OHLC4 = (open + high + low + close) / 4 of the last completed bar from a higher timeframe aggregated from the input bars.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of the higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi OHLC4 series, calculated on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: chart OHLC4 crosses above EMA OR chart OHLC4 crosses above higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi OHLC4.
Short: chart OHLC4 crosses below EMA OR chart OHLC4 crosses below higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi OHLC4.
Exit: Long, chart OHLC4 crosses below EMA OR chart OHLC4 crosses below higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi OHLC4.
