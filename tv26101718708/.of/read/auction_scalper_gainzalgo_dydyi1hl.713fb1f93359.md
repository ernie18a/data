Calculation: Previous-day profile = On the last completed higher-timeframe calendar-day interval, divide the range of its chart bars into equal price bins. Distribute each chart bar's volume equally among every bin touched by its low-to-high range. The point of control is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest bin on a tie. Starting there, add the adjacent bin with greater volume until the selected fraction of total volume is reached, choosing the upper bin on a tie. Value-area low and high are the outer boundaries of the selected bins. Previous-day high and low are the extremes of those same chart bars. A zero-range day has no profile.
Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, configurable minimum price increment). Delta ratio = volume > 0 ? ((close - low) - (high - close)) / candle range : 0. Volume is available when current volume is present and the highest volume over a shorter availability lookback is positive. Volume rank and ATR rank are percentile ranks over a longer lookback, defaulting to the midpoint rank when unavailable.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = sign of close minus its EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use zero when either is unavailable.
Calculation: Auction state = Reset to neutral and disarm both directions at each new calendar day. With a valid profile, if state is not up and close is above value-area high, close > open, and absolute candle body >= a fraction of candle range, set state to up, record value-area high as the break level and the current bar as the break bar, arm up and disarm down. Otherwise, if state is not down and close is below value-area low, close < open, and absolute candle body >= the same fraction of candle range, set state to down, record value-area low and the current bar, arm down and disarm up. Otherwise, reset up state to neutral and disarm up when close < value-area high - tolerance × ATR, or reset down state to neutral and disarm down when close > value-area low + tolerance × ATR. Disarm both directions after the retest window expires; state otherwise persists. A fired continuation signal disarms its direction.
Calculation: Chop = state is neutral AND the count over a lookback of bars where sign(close - current value-area high) differs from sign(previous close - current value-area high) OR sign(close - current value-area low) differs from sign(previous close - current value-area low) >= a threshold.
Calculation: Reversal setup = For long, on a bar other than the first bar of a day, either low < value-area low AND close > value-area low AND the preceding run of closes below value-area low has length zero, OR close crosses above value-area low AND that preceding run has length between one and a maximum. For short, reverse high/low and above/below around value-area high. The sweep extreme is the lowest low or highest high recorded during the latest respective outside-close run, retained after that run ends; use the current extreme if none exists.
Calculation: Continuation setup = For long, state is up AND up is armed AND the bar is later than the break bar AND low <= break level + tolerance × current ATR AND close > break level. For short, state is down AND down is armed AND the bar is later than the break bar AND high >= break level - tolerance × current ATR AND close < break level.
Calculation: Candidate risk and targets = Entry is current close. For a reversal, long swing is min(current low, retained lower sweep extreme) and short swing is max(current high, retained upper sweep extreme); for a continuation use the more adverse extreme of current and previous bars. A classic stop is swing beyond the extreme by an ATR buffer; a slippage-saver stop is inside the extreme by a configurable price increment. If the stop is on the wrong side of entry, retry the classic stop, then use entry minus one ATR for long or plus one ATR for short if still on the wrong side. In classic mode enforce a minimum ATR stop distance. Risk is the distance from entry to stop. For a reversal, target 1 is point of control if it lies sufficiently far beyond entry in the trade direction, otherwise entry plus or minus a fallback risk multiple; target 2 is the opposite value-area edge if sufficiently far beyond target 1, otherwise target 1 plus or minus one risk. For a continuation, target 1 is the previous-day high for long or low for short if sufficiently far beyond entry, otherwise the fallback target; target 2 is entry plus or minus a runner risk multiple, adjusted beyond target 1 by a fraction of risk if necessary. Fix stop and targets at entry.
Calculation: Candidate score = Weighted sum of participation, directional pressure, rejection, location, higher-timeframe alignment, volatility, reward-to-risk and session scores. Clamp component scores to the score range where indicated. Participation is volume rank. Directional pressure is clamp(midpoint score + direction × delta ratio × pressure multiplier), with direction +1 for long and -1 for short. Directional close position is (close - low) / candle range for long and (high - close) / candle range for short, scaled to the score range. For reversal rejection, combine directional close position and clamp(base + sweep depth in ATR × multiplier), where sweep depth is (value-area low - swing) / ATR for long or (swing - value-area high) / ATR for short; reversal location is clamp(maximum score - distance from close to the reclaimed value edge in the trade direction divided by ATR × multiplier). For continuation rejection, combine directional close position and retest precision, where precision is one minus min(1, distance from the retest extreme to break level / max(tolerance × ATR, minimum price increment)); continuation location is clamp(maximum score - absolute distance from close to break level / ATR × multiplier). Higher-timeframe alignment takes distinct scores for matching, neutral and opposing bias, in descending order. Volatility score increases linearly below a lower ATR-rank threshold, stays at maximum through an upper threshold, then decreases linearly and is clamped. Reward-to-risk score is clamp(target-1 reward / risk × multiplier), or zero when risk is not positive. Session score is a fixed value when the session filter is disabled; otherwise it is a stepped score based on elapsed time since session open, with earlier intervals scoring more highly. All weights, thresholds, fractions and multipliers are parameters.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = Chart timeframe is intraday AND bar is confirmed AND previous-day profile exists AND no tracked trade is open AND bars since the latest signal or completed stop/target-2 event > cooldown AND, if session filtering is enabled, the bar is inside the configured session and elapsed time from the first session bar is between the opening lockout and the last-entry cutoff inclusive. Reset per-direction daily usage at each new calendar day. Evaluate entry signals before tracking exits on each bar. Select reversal when both reversal and continuation setups exist.
Long: Signal eligibility AND close > open AND (participation gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR (volume rank >= participation threshold AND delta ratio > positive pressure threshold)) AND (daily direction limit disabled OR no long signal yet today) AND (long reversal setup OR (long continuation setup AND chop is false)) AND candidate score >= minimum score + chop surcharge when in chop + opposing-bias surcharge when reversal and higher-timeframe bias < 0 AND candidate risk <= maximum ATR multiple.
Short: Signal eligibility AND close < open AND (participation gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR (volume rank >= participation threshold AND delta ratio < negative pressure threshold)) AND (daily direction limit disabled OR no short signal yet today) AND (short reversal setup OR (short continuation setup AND chop is false)) AND candidate score >= minimum score + chop surcharge when in chop + opposing-bias surcharge when reversal and higher-timeframe bias > 0 AND candidate risk <= maximum ATR multiple.
Exit: long, bar is later than entry bar AND low <= fixed stop OR bar is later than entry bar AND low > fixed stop AND high >= target 2 AND target 1 has been reached on this or an earlier bar.
Exit: short, bar is later than entry bar AND high >= fixed stop OR bar is later than entry bar AND high < fixed stop AND low <= target 2 AND target 1 has been reached on this or an earlier bar.
Long: Delta ratio >= positive dashboard pressure threshold.
Short: Delta ratio <= negative dashboard pressure threshold.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Long: Valid profile AND auction state is up AND session eligibility and opening lockout conditions hold.
Short: Valid profile AND auction state is down AND session eligibility and opening lockout conditions hold.
