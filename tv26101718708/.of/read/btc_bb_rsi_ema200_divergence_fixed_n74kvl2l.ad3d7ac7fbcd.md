Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a pivot high or low of chart-bar high or low with configurable left and right confirmation widths; it becomes available on the bar completing the right width. Compare each pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type and use RSI sampled at each pivot bar. The maximum separation test measures from the preceding pivot bar to the current confirmation bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the same length as the chart-timeframe EMA; historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while a realtime bar can use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed AND confirmation bar minus preceding pivot-low bar <= maximum separation AND pivot low < preceding pivot low AND RSI at pivot > RSI at preceding pivot.
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed AND confirmation bar minus preceding pivot-low bar <= maximum separation AND pivot low > preceding pivot low AND RSI at pivot < RSI at preceding pivot.
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed AND confirmation bar minus preceding pivot-high bar <= maximum separation AND pivot high > preceding pivot high AND RSI at pivot < RSI at preceding pivot.
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed AND confirmation bar minus preceding pivot-high bar <= maximum separation AND pivot high < preceding pivot high AND RSI at pivot > RSI at preceding pivot.
Long: Regular bullish divergence is confirmed on the current bar AND low <= lower Bollinger Band AND (RSI crosses above the oversold threshold OR (RSI <= oversold threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND previous RSI <= RSI two bars ago)) AND (trend filter is off OR (close > chart-timeframe EMA AND (trend filter is soft OR EMA > previous EMA OR bar index < EMA length) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is off OR close > higher-timeframe EMA))) AND (volume filter is off OR volume >= volume SMA multiplied by volume multiplier) AND (no previous long entry exists OR bars since previous long entry >= cooldown).
Short: Regular bearish divergence is confirmed on the current bar AND high >= upper Bollinger Band AND (RSI crosses below the overbought threshold OR (RSI >= overbought threshold AND RSI < previous RSI AND previous RSI >= RSI two bars ago)) AND (trend filter is off OR (close < chart-timeframe EMA AND (trend filter is soft OR EMA < previous EMA OR bar index < EMA length) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is off OR close < higher-timeframe EMA))) AND (volume filter is off OR volume >= volume SMA multiplied by volume multiplier) AND (no previous short entry exists OR bars since previous short entry >= cooldown).
