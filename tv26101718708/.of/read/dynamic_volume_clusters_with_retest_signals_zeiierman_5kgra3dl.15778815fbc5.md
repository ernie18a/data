Calculation: Rolling range = highest high and lowest low over the same configurable lookback of chart bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Upper zone = from the rolling high minus the configurable percentage of the rolling range up to the rolling high; lower zone = from the rolling low up to the rolling low plus that same percentage of the rolling range.
Calculation: Upper level = reset to close and its update count to zero whenever close is inside the upper zone. Otherwise, once initialized, increment the count each bar and update the level to the cumulative average of closes only when the count before incrementing is at least two. Record the chart-bar index on each reset.
Calculation: Lower level = reset to close and its update count to zero whenever close is inside the lower zone. Otherwise, once initialized, increment the count each bar and update the level to the cumulative average of closes only when the count before incrementing is at least two. Record the chart-bar index on each reset.
Calculation: Short-term close average = simple moving average of close over a configurable short lookback of chart bars.
Long: Current close crosses above the upper level (current close > current upper level AND previous close <= previous upper level) AND the short-term close average is greater than each of its values over the preceding configured rising-confirmation lookback AND bars since the upper-level reset are between the configurable minimum and maximum retest ages, inclusive AND the upper level is nondecreasing (current upper level >= previous upper level).
Short: Current close crosses below the lower level (current close < current lower level AND previous close >= previous lower level) AND the short-term close average is less than each of its values over the preceding configured falling-confirmation lookback AND bars since the lower-level reset are between the configurable minimum and maximum retest ages, inclusive AND the lower level is nonincreasing (current lower level <= previous lower level).
