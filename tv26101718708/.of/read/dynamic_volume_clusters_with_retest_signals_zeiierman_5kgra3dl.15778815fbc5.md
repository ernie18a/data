Calculation: Upper and lower zone averages = On each bar, calculate the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback and define an upper and a lower zone, each with width equal to a parameterized fraction of that range. For each zone independently, when close is inside it, reset its average to close, its sample count to 1, and its reset bar to the current bar. The upper zone excludes its bottom boundary and includes its top; the lower zone includes its bottom boundary and excludes its top. After a reset, skip the first two bars outside the zone, then include each subsequent outside-zone close in the arithmetic average until the next reset. Each reset restarts the count of bars outside the zone.
Calculation: Dynamic price cluster = Let the spread be the upper zone average minus the lower zone average. Normalize it as (spread + rolling maximum absolute spread) / (2 × rolling maximum absolute spread). Interpolate a dynamic length between a shorter and a longer length using this normalized spread. Divide the absolute change in spread by its rolling maximum over a separate lookback, substituting 1 for a zero maximum and 0 for an unavailable previous spread. Set alpha to the lesser of 1 and 2 / (dynamic length + 1) × (1 + acceleration factor × multiplier). Recursively smooth close with alpha, initializing with close when the previous smoothed value is unavailable; then recursively smooth that result with a fixed smoothing factor, initializing with the first available result.
Calculation: Cluster touches = The number of bars in the range lookback whose high is at least the current dynamic price cluster and whose low is at most it.
Long: Close crosses below the lower zone average AND the close SMA is lower than every value of that SMA in its preceding lookback AND bars since the lower zone reset are between the minimum and maximum retest limits inclusive AND the lower zone average is less than or equal to its previous value.
Short: Close crosses above the upper zone average AND the close SMA is higher than every value of that SMA in its preceding lookback AND bars since the upper zone reset are between the minimum and maximum retest limits inclusive AND the upper zone average is greater than or equal to its previous value.
Long: Close is above the dynamic price cluster AND cluster touches are below the peak touch threshold.
Short: Close is below the dynamic price cluster AND cluster touches are below the peak touch threshold.
