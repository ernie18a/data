Calculation: Median = selected moving average of the selected price source over the sampling lookback (default source: close)
Calculation: Outer band distance = either smoothed standard deviation, ATR, or their weighted combination times the calibrated multiplier for the configured outer range; or, in percentile mode, the configured rolling percentile of absolute deviations from the median, adjusted relative to the rolling maximum deviation
Calculation: Upper/lower outer bands = median +/− outer band distance
Long: (close > upper outer band AND previous close ≤ previous upper outer band) OR (high > upper outer band AND close < upper outer band AND close > median AND (rejection filters disabled OR bearish filters pass) AND (candle rejection filter disabled OR upper wick ≥ candle body × configured ratio)) OR (close > median AND previous close ≤ previous median), subject to bullish alert filters when enabled
Short: (close < lower outer band AND previous close ≥ previous lower outer band) OR (low < lower outer band AND close > lower outer band AND close < median AND (rejection filters disabled OR bullish filters pass) AND (candle rejection filter disabled OR lower wick ≥ candle body × configured ratio)) OR (close < median AND previous close ≥ previous median), subject to bearish alert filters when enabled
