Calculation: Selected-period high = high of the selected higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Selected-period low = low of the selected higher-timeframe bar still forming
Long: close > selected-period high AND prior close <= prior selected-period high OR close > selected-period low AND prior close <= prior selected-period low
Short: close < selected-period high AND prior close >= prior selected-period high OR close < selected-period low AND prior close >= prior selected-period low
Exit: long or short, bars since entry >= holding period
