Calculation: Average entry price = total quote-currency cost of the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of their quantities; each quantity equals its order's configured quote-currency size divided by its fill close. Use the average calculated before any averaging order on the current bar for the current bar's exit decision.
Long: Position is flat AND RSI of close is below its threshold. The RSI timeframe is configurable: use the chart timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time, or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Position is long AND the number of filled averaging orders is less than the number of configured averaging orders AND close <= base entry price × (1 − the next unfilled averaging order's deviation). Fill at most one averaging order per chart bar, in configured order, at the close.
Exit: long, close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage).
