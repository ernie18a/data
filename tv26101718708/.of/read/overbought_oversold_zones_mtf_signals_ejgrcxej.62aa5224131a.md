Calculation: Pressure Index = EMA of a weighted combination of (1) the average over a measurement window of each bar’s ATR-normalized close-minus-baseline distance, clipped to [0, 2] and scaled to [0, 1], and (2) the current ATR-normalized close-minus-baseline distance, clipped symmetrically and scaled to [0, 1]; the baseline is a long-term SMA(close) minus an adaptive coefficient times a long-term standard deviation of close, with the coefficient increasing as the ratio of short-term ATR to long-term ATR increases and bounded; the EMA smoothing and measurement window are sensitivity-scaled.
Long: Pressure Index crosses into the oversold region (current value is at or below the oversold threshold AND previous value was above it)
Short: Pressure Index crosses into the overbought region (current value is at or above the overbought threshold AND previous value was below it)
