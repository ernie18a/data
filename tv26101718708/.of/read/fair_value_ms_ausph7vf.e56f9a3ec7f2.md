Calculation: Upward gap = low > high two bars ago AND close one bar ago > high two bars ago; downward gap = high < low two bars ago AND close one bar ago < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Structure state = initially direction 0 and swing values undefined. Process each bar in this order: if direction is 0, an upward gap sets direction to 1 and initializes the tracked high and low to this bar's high and low; a downward gap sets direction to -1 and initializes them likewise. If direction is 1, a high >= tracked high resets both tracked values to this bar's high and low, then a low <= tracked low updates the tracked low. A downward gap then sets direction to -1, records the tracked high as the latest swing high, and resets the tracked high to this bar's high. If direction is -1, a low <= tracked low resets both tracked values to this bar's high and low, then a high >= tracked high updates the tracked high. An upward gap then sets direction to 1, records the tracked low as the latest swing low, and resets the tracked low to this bar's low. These direction blocks are evaluated sequentially, so a change to -1 can cause the last block to run on the same bar.
Calculation: Pending bullish area = when a new swing low is lower than the previous swing low, record that low if no bullish area is pending or it is <= the pending area's low. Pending bearish area = when a new swing high is higher than the previous swing high, record that high if no bearish area is pending or it is >= the pending area's high. Both start absent. Clear the corresponding pending area after its bullish or bearish area decision, respectively.
Calculation: Reflection levels = the swing low of each bullish area decision and the swing high of each bearish area decision, appended in that order when both occur on the same bar; after processing the bar, remove the oldest level once if the number of levels exceeds the configured maximum.
Calculation: Smoothed price = initialize to close; on each bar add sign(close - previous smoothed price) multiplied by max(abs(close - previous smoothed price) - ATR, 0), treating an unavailable ATR as zero.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND close one bar ago > high two bars ago.
Short: high < low two bars ago AND close one bar ago < low two bars ago.
Long: pending bullish area exists AND its swing low <= tracked low AND close > latest swing high.
Short: pending bearish area exists AND its swing high >= tracked high AND close < latest swing low.
Long: smoothed price > any retained reflection level.
Short: smoothed price < any retained reflection level.
