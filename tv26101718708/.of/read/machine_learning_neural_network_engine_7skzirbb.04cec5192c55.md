Calculation: Safe ratio(a, b, fallback) = a / b when both operands are available and abs(b) exceeds a small tolerance; otherwise fallback.
Calculation: Features = six values, each clipped to [-1, 1]: (1) safe ratio(short EMA(close) - longer EMA(close), ATR, 0) divided by a scale; (2) RSI(close) centered and scaled to [-1, 1]; (3) safe ratio(close - linear regression endpoint of close, ATR, 0) divided by a scale; (4) safe ratio(close - close at an earlier lookback, sum of absolute close changes over that lookback, 0); (5) safe ratio(ATR, SMA of ATR over the normalization lookback, 1) - 1; (6) safe ratio(close - open, high - low, 0) multiplied by relative volume and divided by a scale. Relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, using a fallback of 1 for missing volume or an unavailable denominator, and clipped to a nonnegative upper limit.
Calculation: Neural prediction = an online feedforward network with six feature inputs, one hidden layer and a single output, using tanh activations. Initialize input and output weights deterministically with sine functions of their indices; initialize biases and RMS gradient moments to zero. On each completed bar after the normalization lookback, forecast horizon and additional warmup offset, train once on the features from one forecast horizon ago. The target is the horizon close change divided by the greater of ATR at the feature bar and a small price floor, clipped to [-1, 1]. Clip target minus prediction, backpropagate through tanh using the output weights from before the update, and update weights and biases with exponentially smoothed squared-gradient normalization; apply weight decay to weights only. Calculate the current prediction after this update, or use zero if features are unavailable.
Calculation: Bull probability = sigmoid of a scaled combined score. The combined score is the clipped weighted average of the neural prediction and a structural score. The structural score is a clipped weighted sum of the first four features minus a weighted fifth feature. Audit the absolute errors of the prediction and structural score from one forecast horizon ago against the current resolved target using separate exponentially smoothed averages. Skill is one minus the ratio of neural error to structural error. Before the required number of resolved samples, increase neural weight linearly from zero to a capped preliminary weight; afterward use a clipped base weight plus skill when skill is positive, and a lower fixed weight otherwise. Give the structural score the remaining weight.
Calculation: Crisis = (close return over a short lookback <= negative fast-drop threshold AND ATR / SMA(ATR) >= a high volatility threshold) OR (close / highest close over a longer lookback - 1 <= negative drawdown threshold AND close < a long EMA AND ATR / SMA(ATR) >= a lower volatility threshold). The fast-drop threshold is the greater of a minimum decline and a multiple of ATR / close; use safe ratios with neutral fallbacks.
Calculation: State = initialize long. On completed bars with all features available, while long, count consecutive bars satisfying Crisis OR (bull probability <= exit threshold AND close < long EMA AND close < longer EMA); otherwise reset that count. Change to cash immediately on Crisis or when the exit count reaches its confirmation requirement. While cash, count consecutive bars satisfying bull probability >= entry threshold AND close > short EMA AND (close > close at a prior lookback OR close > long EMA) AND Crisis is false; otherwise reset that count. Change to long when the entry count reaches its confirmation requirement. Reset the relevant count on each transition; the entry threshold is higher than the exit threshold.
Long: State changes from cash to long.
Exit: long, state changes from long to cash.
