Calculation: Bull probability = sigmoid of the combined score formed from (a) a causal online 6-input, 5-hidden-unit neural network predicting normalized future close change and (b) a structural score; the neural network’s blend weight adapts to its recent forecast error relative to the structural score’s error.
Calculation: Neural-network features = ATR-normalized short-term versus longer-term EMA spread; normalized RSI momentum; ATR-normalized close deviation from linear regression; close change divided by the sum of absolute close changes over the same lookback; ATR relative to its longer-term average; and candle body divided by high-low range, weighted by relative volume.
Calculation: Structural score = weighted sum of the neural-network features: positive weights for EMA spread, RSI momentum, regression deviation and directional price efficiency, and a negative weight for relative volatility.
Calculation: Crisis alarm = (close’s multi-bar return is at or below a negative threshold that scales with ATR/close AND ATR relative to its long-term average exceeds a threshold) OR (drawdown from the rolling highest close exceeds a threshold AND close is below the long-term EMA AND ATR relative to its long-term average exceeds a threshold).
Long: Bull probability >= entry threshold AND close > shorter-term EMA AND (close > close from a longer lookback OR close > longer-term EMA) for the required consecutive confirmation bars while not long and no crisis alarm.
Exit: Long, crisis alarm OR (bull probability <= exit threshold AND close < longer-term EMA AND close < still longer-term EMA) for the required consecutive confirmation bars.
