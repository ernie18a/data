Calculation: Multi-timeframe SRSI close = On each of four higher timeframes, calculate RSI of close, stochastic RSI over a longer lookback, smooth it with a shorter K lookback, smooth K with a D lookback, and add K and D. Use each higher timeframe’s still-forming bar; average the four results and divide by 2.
Long: Multi-timeframe SRSI close <= oversold threshold while neither extreme watch is active, then >= midpoint (long marker); OR after a high-watch is armed at or above the overbought threshold, Multi-timeframe SRSI close <= midpoint, then for the confirmation lookback has no strictly lower value (reset the count whenever a new lower value occurs, counting the trigger bar; confirmed-low marker)
Short: Multi-timeframe SRSI close >= overbought threshold while neither extreme watch is active, then <= midpoint (short marker); OR after a low-watch is armed at or below the oversold threshold, Multi-timeframe SRSI close >= midpoint, then for the confirmation lookback has no strictly higher value (reset the count whenever a new higher value occurs, counting the trigger bar; confirmed-high marker)
