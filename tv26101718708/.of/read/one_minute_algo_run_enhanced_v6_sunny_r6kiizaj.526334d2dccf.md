Calculation: Opening range = the high and low of either the first completed bar or all completed bars in the configured opening window, using a lower timeframe than the chart or the chart timeframe if equal; lock the range after the selected measurement ends and reset it each local calendar day. The midpoint is the average of the locked high and low. Freeze the last completed measurement-bar close and regular-session VWAP at range lock.
Calculation: Opening bias = at range lock, compare the short-lookback and long-lookback close EMAs on each of two higher timeframes, using their last completed bars; freeze the comparisons until the next local calendar day. The first higher timeframe is shorter than the second.
Calculation: Daily profile POC = divide the developing regular-session high-low range into equal price rows; distribute each included bar's volume among overlapping rows in proportion to price overlap, assigning a zero-range bar's volume to its containing row. Select the midpoint of the first row with maximum volume. Use completed lower-timeframe bars when the profile timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, otherwise chart bars; recalculate at the configured chart-bar interval and retain the last calculated POC between updates.
Calculation: Quality thresholds = starting with baseline ADX and candle-body-ratio thresholds, optionally increase both by a smaller or larger configured penalty when the success rate of a configured number of recent completed journal trades falls below the upper or lower configured success-rate threshold, respectively, after a minimum sample count. Cap the body threshold at a configured maximum. A journal trade succeeds if its first target was reached; otherwise it fails at the initial stop. Track only one journal trade at a time, starting on an eligible breakout when no journal trade is active; discard an active journal trade at the next local calendar day.
Calculation: Outlook score = sum six votes, each equal to positive one, negative one, or zero: short EMA versus long EMA on each of the two higher timeframes using the last completed bars; current close versus developing regular-session VWAP calculated from completed lower-timeframe bars or chart bars if equal; close versus locked range midpoint, or zero before lock; positive versus negative directional indicator; and close above the opening-range high versus below its low. Equality or an unavailable comparison contributes zero.
Calculation: Targets = for a long, opening-range high plus opening-range width times the first-target or second-target multiplier; for a short, opening-range low minus opening-range width times the corresponding multiplier. First and second targets have separately configurable multipliers for each side.
Long: At range lock, the short EMA is greater than the long EMA on both higher timeframes AND the frozen lock close is greater than or equal to the frozen session VWAP if VWAP confirmation is enabled.
Short: At range lock, the short EMA is less than the long EMA on both higher timeframes AND the frozen lock close is less than or equal to the frozen session VWAP if VWAP confirmation is enabled.
Long: Outlook score is greater than or equal to the positive outlook threshold AND either the range is unlocked OR the locked range width is at least the minimum AND ADX is at least its effective threshold if the chop filter is enabled AND the daily POC is unavailable or its distance from close is greater than the configured ATR multiple if the POC filter is enabled.
Short: Outlook score is less than or equal to the negative outlook threshold AND either the range is unlocked OR the locked range width is at least the minimum AND ADX is at least its effective threshold if the chop filter is enabled AND the daily POC is unavailable or its distance from close is greater than the configured ATR multiple if the POC filter is enabled.
Long: The range is locked AND the bar is inside the trading window AND range width is at least the minimum AND ADX and absolute candle body divided by candle range are each at least their effective thresholds if the chop filter is enabled AND the daily POC is unavailable or its distance from close is greater than the configured ATR multiple if the POC filter is enabled AND frozen opening bias is long if the bias filter is enabled AND close is above the range high AND previous close was at or below the previous bar's range high AND either re-entry is enabled or no earlier long breakout occurred on the local calendar day.
Short: The range is locked AND the bar is inside the trading window AND range width is at least the minimum AND ADX and absolute candle body divided by candle range are each at least their effective thresholds if the chop filter is enabled AND the daily POC is unavailable or its distance from close is greater than the configured ATR multiple if the POC filter is enabled AND frozen opening bias is short if the bias filter is enabled AND close is below the range low AND previous close was at or above the previous bar's range low AND either re-entry is enabled or no earlier short breakout occurred on the local calendar day.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high is at least the second long target OR the first long target has been reached since entry including the current bar AND low is at or below entry price OR the first long target has not been reached since entry including the current bar AND low is at or below the opening-range midpoint.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low is at or below the second short target OR the first short target has been reached since entry including the current bar AND high is at or above entry price OR the first short target has not been reached since entry including the current bar AND high is at or above the opening-range midpoint.
