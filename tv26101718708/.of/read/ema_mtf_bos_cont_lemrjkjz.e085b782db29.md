Calculation: Requested-timeframe value = for a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the bar still forming in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar. Configured timeframes may also equal the chart timeframe.
Calculation: EMA alignment = for each enabled requested timeframe, compare its close with its EMA using the same EMA length; bullish or bearish alignment requires the corresponding count to be at least the configured required count, or all enabled timeframes when that mode is selected. Apply the configured direction restriction. When both alignments hold, bullish direction takes precedence.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on both sides; update its value only when the right-hand bars have completed. Use a separate, longer or shorter configurable pivot length for each of the BOS and target calculations.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND low minus high two bars ago >= ATR multiplied by a configured factor. Bearish FVG = high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago minus high >= ATR multiplied by the same factor. Evaluate on the chart timeframe and separately on two configured requested timeframes; the first requested timeframe's nonzero direction takes precedence over the second.
Calculation: Bullish inverse FVG = chart close > the high from two bars before the latest bearish chart-timeframe FVG AND that FVG is no older than the configured lookback. Bearish inverse FVG = chart close < the high from two bars before the latest bullish chart-timeframe FVG AND that FVG is no older than the same lookback. Replace each stored FVG when a newer FVG of the same direction occurs.
Calculation: Target direction = choose the nearest available level strictly above close from the last completed bars' highs on two configured higher timeframes and the latest confirmed target swing high; also choose the nearest available level strictly below close from their lows and the latest confirmed target swing low. Direction is bullish if only the upper level exists or its distance is <= the lower distance; bearish if only the lower exists or its distance is smaller; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Continuation direction = according to the selected mode, use EMA alignment direction, or use bullish direction when bullish inverse FVG OR close > latest BOS swing high, otherwise bearish direction when bearish inverse FVG OR close < latest BOS swing low, otherwise target direction. A manually selected direction is an optional external setting.
Calculation: BOS watch = maintain a neutral, bullish, or bearish state. On each bar, first cancel an existing bullish watch if bullish alignment fails or close < latest BOS swing low; otherwise confirm it and reset to neutral if close > latest BOS swing high. Apply the mirrored rules to a bearish watch. Then, if neutral, start a bullish watch when bullish alignment holds AND the optional bullish EMA stack and pullback filters pass AND the bearish setup does not hold; otherwise start a bearish watch when bearish alignment holds AND the optional bearish filters pass AND the bullish setup does not hold. A watch can restart on its confirmation or cancellation bar. The bullish stack is short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; the bearish stack reverses these comparisons. The bullish pullback is low <= maximum of the three EMAs plus ATR times a configured buffer AND close >= short EMA; the bearish pullback is high >= minimum of the three EMAs minus the same buffer AND close <= short EMA. The short EMA uses the alignment EMA length.
Long: An enabled requested-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: An enabled requested-timeframe close < its EMA.
Long: Bullish EMA alignment holds.
Short: Bearish EMA alignment holds AND bullish EMA alignment does not hold.
Long: A bullish BOS watch starts OR an existing bullish BOS watch is confirmed.
Short: A bearish BOS watch starts OR an existing bearish BOS watch is confirmed.
Long: The selected requested-timeframe FVG direction is bullish OR a bullish chart-timeframe inverse FVG holds.
Short: The selected requested-timeframe FVG direction is bearish OR a bearish chart-timeframe inverse FVG holds AND a bullish inverse FVG does not hold.
Long: Continuation direction is bullish.
Short: Continuation direction is bearish.
Long: Continuation direction is bullish AND bullish inverse FVG holds AND, if the optional FVG filter is enabled, the selected requested-timeframe FVG direction is bullish.
Short: Continuation direction is bearish AND bearish inverse FVG holds AND, if the optional FVG filter is enabled, the selected requested-timeframe FVG direction is bearish.
