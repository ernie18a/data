Calculation: EMA alignment = On each enabled scan timeframe, close above its EMA indicates bullish and close below indicates bearish; alignment requires either all enabled timeframes or the configured minimum count, subject to the configured direction filter. Scan timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe and use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: EMA stack = Fast EMA above middle EMA above slow EMA for bullish; fast EMA below middle EMA below slow EMA for bearish.
Calculation: EMA pullback = Bullish when low reaches or falls below the highest of the fast, middle, and slow EMAs plus an ATR-scaled buffer, and close is at or above the fast EMA; bearish when high reaches or exceeds the lowest of those EMAs minus the buffer, and close is at or below the fast EMA.
Calculation: Swing high/low = Confirmed pivot high/low using equal lookback on each side; retain the most recent confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Break of structure = Close above the most recent swing high (bullish) or below the most recent swing low (bearish).
Calculation: Fair value gap = Bullish when low exceeds the high two bars earlier, the previous close exceeds that earlier high, and the gap is at least an ATR-scaled minimum; bearish when high is below the low two bars earlier, the previous close is below that earlier low, and the gap is at least an ATR-scaled minimum. For HTF gaps, use the current bar on each configured higher timeframe, which may still be forming; chart-timeframe gaps use chart bars.
Calculation: Inverse fair value gap = Within the configured validity window after a bearish gap, bullish when close exceeds that gap's upper boundary; within the validity window after a bullish gap, bearish when close falls below that gap's lower boundary.
Calculation: Directional liquidity level = Nearest level above close among prior completed daily high, prior completed weekly high, and latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high; or nearest level below close among prior completed daily low, prior completed weekly low, and latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low.
Long: Bullish EMA alignment OR (bullish EMA alignment and bullish EMA stack when required and bullish EMA pullback when required, with no bearish BOS watch active) OR (bullish BOS watch active and close breaks above the latest swing high) OR continuation direction is bullish and a bullish inverse fair value gap occurs, with a bullish HTF fair value gap if required.
Short: Bearish EMA alignment OR (bearish EMA alignment and bearish EMA stack when required and bearish EMA pullback when required, with no bullish BOS watch active) OR (bearish BOS watch active and close breaks below the latest swing low) OR continuation direction is bearish and a bearish inverse fair value gap occurs, with a bearish HTF fair value gap if required.
