Calculation: H[i] and L[i] = the price of the ith most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, where i=0 is most recent; their bar indices are h[i] and l[i]. A pivot high has no higher high in its left and right confirmation windows and no equal high in its right window; a pivot low has no lower low in those windows and no equal low in its right window. A pivot becomes available only after its right window has completed.
Calculation: T = current ATR multiplied by its tolerance parameter, or current close multiplied by a percentage tolerance parameter. Near(a,b) = abs(a-b) <= T.
Calculation: Up(N) = close > N + current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND previous close <= N + current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND (body confirmation is disabled OR max(open,close) > N) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable OR volume > its moving average × volume multiplier). Down(N) = close < N - current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND previous close >= N - current ATR × breakout-buffer parameter AND (body confirmation is disabled OR min(open,close) < N) AND the same volume condition.
Calculation: Valid(N,S,D,X) = bars since the last signal >= cooldown AND current bar index - X is between the minimum and maximum pattern widths inclusive AND abs(N-S) >= current ATR × minimum-height parameter × 0.5 AND abs(D) >= current ATR × minimum-height parameter AND abs(D) / max(abs(N-S), minimum price increment) + 0.15 >= minimum risk/reward parameter. Initialize the last signal bar index to -10000; update it after all patterns have been evaluated on a signal bar. The minimum price increment is a parameter.
