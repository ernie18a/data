Calculation: Trailing bands and trend = Start in an uptrend. For bands indexed from innermost to outermost, use increasing proportional ATR multipliers: base × (1 + band offset × spacing). On each bar, compare the selected outer band's previous value with the selected price source. In an uptrend, flip down if source < previous band; otherwise each band is the maximum of its previous value and source minus its ATR distance. In a downtrend, flip up if source > previous band; otherwise each band is the minimum of its previous value and source plus its ATR distance. On a flip, reset every band to source plus its ATR distance for a downtrend or source minus its ATR distance for an uptrend. Treat unavailable initial ATR as zero and initialize each band from its current candidate. Update the trend even during warm-up.
Calculation: Pending retest = For the current trend, compare the current bar's low in an uptrend or high in a downtrend against the current bands, and record the deepest touched band. At the start of each bar decrement the remaining window; clear its depth when the window expires and clear both directions on a trend flip. A touch then resets the window and retains the maximum depth since the pending retest began. A successful scored retest clears its pending state. Touch and reclaim can occur on the same bar, including a flip bar.
Calculation: Retest score = Sum depth, candle, volume, bias and trend-age points. Depth points for innermost through outermost bands are 15, 25, 18 and 10. Candle points are 20 if directional close location > 0.7, 12 if > 0.5, otherwise 5; directional close location is (close − low)/(high − low) for long and (high − close)/(high − low) for short, or 0.5 when the range is zero. Volume points are 20 if current volume > a fixed multiple of the previous bar's volume SMA, 12 if > that SMA, otherwise 5; use 12 if cumulative volume is zero, and use the current SMA if the previous SMA is unavailable. Bias uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close and close EMA, or the previous completed chart bar when using chart timeframe: award 20 if close > EMA for long or close ≤ EMA for short, 0 for the opposite bias, and 10 if either value is unavailable. Trend-age points are 8 below a lower age threshold, 15 from that threshold through a higher age threshold inclusive, and 5 above it; age is bars since the latest flip, initially measured from the first bar.
Calculation: Trade levels = Enter at the signal bar's close. Using a separate risk ATR, choose either an ATR stop at entry minus an ATR multiple for long or plus it for short, or a wick stop at the lower of entry-bar low minus an ATR buffer and entry minus a minimum ATR distance for long, mirrored above entry for short. Initial risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. The first and final targets are entry plus their respective risk multiples for long, minus them for short; the final target multiple is larger. Optionally move the stop to entry after the first target is touched without the bar-start stop being touched; the new stop applies starting on the next bar. Check stop and final target only after the entry bar, using the bar-start stop, and resolve a simultaneous stop and target touch as a stop. Process these closures before signals; open or reverse at close on a signal if the risk ATR and initial risk are positive, and ignore a same-direction signal while a position remains open.
Long: Bars elapsed since the start of the dataset ≥ the larger of the warm-up minimum and a multiple of the trailing ATR lookback AND previous trend = down AND selected price source > previous selected trailing band.
Short: Bars elapsed since the start of the dataset ≥ the larger of the warm-up minimum and a multiple of the trailing ATR lookback AND previous trend = up AND selected price source < previous selected trailing band.
Long: Warm-up complete AND trend = up AND pending long retest window > 0 AND close > current innermost band AND close > open AND long retest score ≥ score threshold AND bars since the last retest signal in either direction ≥ cooldown.
Short: Warm-up complete AND trend = down AND pending short retest window > 0 AND close < current innermost band AND close < open AND short retest score ≥ score threshold AND bars since the last retest signal in either direction ≥ cooldown.
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND (low ≤ bar-start stop OR high ≥ final target) OR a short signal occurs.
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND (high ≥ bar-start stop OR low ≤ final target) OR a long signal occurs.
