Calculation: RSI(close) = RSI of close on the selected analysis timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the currently forming bar when available
Calculation: Stochastic RSI(close) = smoothed stochastic of RSI(close), using a shorter smoothing lookback than the stochastic lookback
Calculation: RSI trend = rising or falling simple moving average of close, using a longer lookback than the trend-change lookback
Calculation: RSI divergence = recent 3-bar price low below the 5-bar price low while RSI(close) is above its 5-bar low; bearish divergence is the inverse using highs
Calculation: Stochastic RSI direction = 100 if its derived upper band rises versus the prior bar; otherwise 0 if its derived lower band falls; otherwise 0 if its upper band falls; otherwise 100 if its lower band rises; otherwise 50. The derived upper band is stochastic RSI(high) when it exceeds Stochastic RSI(close), otherwise prior Stochastic RSI(close). The derived lower band is the lesser of stochastic RSI(high) and stochastic RSI(low) when that value is below Stochastic RSI(close), otherwise prior Stochastic RSI(close)
