Calculation: Signed volume = optionally, sum volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1) over all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, using zero for a zero-range sub-bar; otherwise, chart-bar volume × (2 × Φ((close − previous close)/standard deviation of close changes over a fallback lookback) − 1), using a standardized change of zero when the standard deviation is zero. Φ is the standard normal CDF approximated with t = 1/(1 + 0.2316419 × |x|), d = 0.3989422804014327 × exp(−x²/2), p = d × t × (0.319381530 + t × (−0.356563782 + t × (1.781477937 + t × (−1.821255978 + t × 1.330274429)))); Φ(x) = 1 − p for x ≥ 0 and p otherwise.
Calculation: Detrended cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of signed volume minus its EMA over the normalization lookback.
Calculation: Normalized flow = optionally, detrended cumulative volume delta divided by its standard deviation over the normalization lookback, with zero when the deviation is zero; or its rolling median-centered robust z-score using median absolute deviation multiplied by 1.4826 over that lookback.
Calculation: Flow oscillator = selectable normalized flow, Wilder RSI of detrended cumulative volume delta, or money flow index calculated from typical price and volume. Optionally smooth the selected series with an EMA or UltimateSmoother. For UltimateSmoother, q = exp(−1.414π/period), c1 = 2q × cos(1.414π/period), c2 = q², a = (1 + c1 + c2)/4, and output = (1 − a) × input + (2a − c1) × previous input + (c2 − a) × input two bars ago + c1 × previous output − c2 × output two bars ago; initialize the first four outputs to the input.
Calculation: Oscillator bands = selectable symmetric inner overbought and oversold levels and wider symmetric outer extreme levels for normalized flow; for RSI or money flow index, use corresponding upper and lower inner levels and more extreme outer levels on the 0–100 scale. The RSI inner bands differ from the money flow index inner bands.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with the selected number of bars on each side; evaluate it on the last right-hand bar. For equal values, the rightmost equal high or low is the pivot.
Calculation: Divergence comparison = compare each newly confirmed price high or low pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, even if the preceding pivot produced no signal. For each price pivot, use either the oscillator at that pivot or, optionally, the highest oscillator for a high pivot or lowest oscillator for a low pivot in the entire window from the leftmost through the rightmost pivot-confirmation bar. The required oscillator gap is a selectable multiplier times the oscillator standard deviation over a separate lookback measured on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Agreement = the sum of the signs of normalized flow relative to zero, RSI of detrended cumulative volume delta relative to its midpoint, and money flow index relative to its midpoint; use the unsmoothed series.
Calculation: VSA volume = optionally raw volume or volume divided by its time-of-day-slot EMA, updated with the current bar before division; use a ratio of one when the slot EMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Volume and range percentile ranks use a common history lookback, and prior price extremes use a separate lookback excluding the current bar.
