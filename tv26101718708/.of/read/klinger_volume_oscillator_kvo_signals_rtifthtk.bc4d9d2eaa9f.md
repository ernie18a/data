Calculation: Klinger Volume Oscillator (KVO) = EMA of signed volume with the shorter lookback minus EMA of signed volume with the longer lookback; signed volume is volume × 100 when (high + low + close) / 3 is greater than its prior-bar value, otherwise −volume × 100.
Calculation: Trigger = EMA of KVO, using a shorter lookback than either signed-volume EMA.
Calculation: Spread = KVO − Trigger.
Calculation: KVO standard deviation = standard deviation of KVO over the normalization lookback.
Calculation: Run-up gap above = the largest Spread over the run-up lookback, excluding the current bar, divided by KVO standard deviation × 100.
Calculation: Run-up gap below = the negative of the smallest Spread over the run-up lookback, excluding the current bar, divided by KVO standard deviation × 100.
Calculation: Cross strength = absolute change in Spread over the strength lookback, divided by KVO standard deviation × 100.
Calculation: Donchian midline = average of the rolling highest high and rolling lowest low over the Donchian lookback.
Calculation: Donchian midline change = current Donchian midline − its value at the slope lookback bars ago.
Long: KVO crosses above Trigger; if the zero-line filter is enabled, KVO at the cross is below the negative configured zero-line distance × KVO standard deviation; if the minimum run-up gap filter is enabled, Run-up gap below at the cross is at least its configured minimum; if the minimum cross strength filter is enabled, Cross strength at the cross is at least its configured minimum; if the Donchian slope filter is enabled, Donchian midline change is at least negative flat tolerance; at the configured confirmation bar after the cross, the lowest Spread from the cross through that bar is greater than zero, current Spread is at least the configured close-through margin × KVO standard deviation, and, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close > open.
Short: KVO crosses below Trigger; if the zero-line filter is enabled, KVO at the cross is above the configured zero-line distance × KVO standard deviation; if the minimum run-up gap filter is enabled, Run-up gap above at the cross is at least its configured minimum; if the minimum cross strength filter is enabled, Cross strength at the cross is at least its configured minimum; if the Donchian slope filter is enabled, Donchian midline change is at most flat tolerance; at the configured confirmation bar after the cross, the highest Spread from the cross through that bar is less than zero, current Spread is at most negative configured close-through margin × KVO standard deviation, and, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close < open.
