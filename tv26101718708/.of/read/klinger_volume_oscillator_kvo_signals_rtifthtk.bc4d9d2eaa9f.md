Calculation: Signed volume = volume × 100 when (high + low + close) / 3 > its previous-bar value; otherwise −volume × 100.
Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-length EMA of signed volume − longer-length EMA of signed volume; trigger = EMA of oscillator; spread = oscillator − trigger.
Calculation: Normalization = standard deviation of oscillator over the normalization lookback; unavailable normalization is treated as zero except when used as a divisor.
Calculation: Strength = absolute change in spread over the strength lookback / normalization × 100; an unavailable result is treated as zero.
Calculation: Prior upward gap = highest spread over the run-up lookback ending on the previous bar / current normalization × 100; prior downward gap = negative lowest spread over the same window / current normalization × 100; an unavailable result is treated as zero.
Calculation: Channel midline = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the channel lookback; channel change = current channel midline − channel midline at the slope lookback.
Long: Oscillator crosses above trigger AND normalization > 0.
Short: Oscillator crosses below trigger AND normalization > 0.
Long: At a bar the confirmation lookback ago, oscillator crosses above trigger AND normalization > 0 AND (zero filter disabled OR oscillator < −zero-distance fraction × normalization) AND (minimum strength = 0 OR strength >= minimum strength) AND (minimum gap = 0 OR prior downward gap >= minimum gap) AND (channel filter disabled OR channel change >= −channel tolerance in price units) AND no later bar through the current bar satisfies either direction's corresponding filtered cross conditions AND spread > 0 on every bar from that cross through the current bar AND current spread >= margin fraction × current normalization AND (candle filter disabled OR current close > current open).
Short: At a bar the confirmation lookback ago, oscillator crosses below trigger AND normalization > 0 AND (zero filter disabled OR oscillator > zero-distance fraction × normalization) AND (minimum strength = 0 OR strength >= minimum strength) AND (minimum gap = 0 OR prior upward gap >= minimum gap) AND (channel filter disabled OR channel change <= channel tolerance in price units) AND no later bar through the current bar satisfies either direction's corresponding filtered cross conditions AND spread < 0 on every bar from that cross through the current bar AND current spread <= −margin fraction × current normalization AND (candle filter disabled OR current close < current open).
