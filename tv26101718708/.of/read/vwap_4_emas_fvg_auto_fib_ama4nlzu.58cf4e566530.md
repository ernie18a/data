Calculation: Short-term EMA = EMA of close with a shorter lookback, calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long-term EMA = EMA of close with a longer lookback, calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= minimum FVG size
Short: Short-term EMA < long-term EMA OR high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= minimum FVG size
