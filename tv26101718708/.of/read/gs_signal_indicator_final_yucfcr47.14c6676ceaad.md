Calculation: XGS score = sum of points for bullish trend (price above the main EMA, fast EMA above main EMA, main EMA above base EMA, base EMA above long EMA, and healthy trend); structure (support, breakout, price above Donchian midpoint, and higher-timeframe support); momentum (RSI above its bullish floor and strength level, positive and nondecreasing MACD histogram); leadership (relative strength above its EMA with rising EMA, and relative-strength breakout); and risk (no trap and safe price stretch). Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for support: its close is above its main EMA, which is at or above its base EMA.
Calculation: DTS score = sum of points for price above the main EMA, price above VWAP, liquidity reclaim, close above the prior-day pivot; candle authority, A/B candle grade, stochastic K at or above D, and bullish close position; required relative volume, rising OBV, and positive CMF; and session, bullish phase, and no-trap conditions.
Calculation: Apex layer count = number of true conditions among: XGS score at or above its buy threshold; DTS score at or above its buy threshold; healthy trend with main EMA above base EMA; RSI at or above strength level and below 68 with positive MACD histogram; nondecreasing MACD histogram with MACD line at or above signal line; required relative volume with participation; positive CMF or MFI at or above 55; bullish relative strength; higher-timeframe support; candle authority with A candle grade; no trap and no distribution; bullish phase and safe price stretch without overextension.
Calculation: Healthy trend = close above main EMA, main EMA above base EMA, and main EMA nondecreasing.
Calculation: Candle authority = bullish candle with body at least the required body percentage and close position at least the required close-position level.
Calculation: A candle grade = candle authority, required relative volume, and close above main EMA. B candle grade = bullish candle, close position at least 0.55, body at least 35%, and close at or above main EMA.
Calculation: Liquidity reclaim = sweep below the prior swing low with required lower wick, or close crossing above VWAP, or close crossing above main EMA.
Calculation: Trap = fake breakout, hidden distribution, or sweep above prior swing high with upper wick at least 40%. Fake breakout = high above prior Donchian upper band, bearish candle, upper wick at least 35%, and relative volume below required level. Hidden distribution = relative volume at or above risk level, MFI below 50, CMF below zero, and bearish candle.
Calculation: Safe price stretch = close above main EMA and not overextended. Overextended = close above main EMA plus ATR times the maximum stretch, or RSI at or above exhaustion level.
Long: confirmed bar AND (Apex layer count at or above the A required-layer threshold OR (XGS score at or above buy threshold AND DTS score at or above buy threshold AND Apex layer count at or above 10)) AND buy cooldown elapsed AND (trade-state blocking disabled OR not in a trade).
Long: confirmed bar AND Apex layer count at or above early-layer threshold AND below close-layer threshold, and this condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: confirmed bar AND Apex layer count at or above close-layer threshold AND below 11, and this condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: DTS score at or above buy threshold AND XGS score below buy threshold AND Apex layer count at or above close-layer threshold, and this condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: XGS score at or above buy threshold AND DTS score below buy threshold AND Apex layer count at or above close-layer threshold, and this condition was false on the previous bar.
Exit: Long, confirmed bar AND (close below the active stop OR (close below base EMA AND MACD histogram below zero) OR (RSI below bullish floor AND close below main EMA)), and this condition was false on the previous bar. Active stop is the lower of the entry-price ATR stop and the buffered swing-low stop, calculated at entry.
