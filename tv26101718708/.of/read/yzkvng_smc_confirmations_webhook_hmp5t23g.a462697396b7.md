Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = highest high / lowest low over the swing lookback with that many bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot of each type
Calculation: EMA slope = current trend EMA − trend EMA from the slope lookback bars ago
Calculation: Bullish structure = both pivot types exist and EMA slope > 0; bearish structure = both exist and EMA slope < 0
Calculation: Trending up = ADX ≥ threshold AND EMA slope > 0.5 × ATR; trending down = ADX ≥ threshold AND EMA slope < −0.5 × ATR
Calculation: Ranging market = close is between the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback, their difference > 2 × ATR, and market is neither trending up nor trending down
Calculation: Bullish confirmation count = sum of five checks: (bullish candle close, unless disabled, where close > open and close > previous close); (volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier, unless disabled); (RSI ≥ bullish threshold and RSI < 70, unless disabled); (lower wick ≥ candle range × wick ratio, unless disabled); (no strong bearish bar among the preceding opposing-pressure lookback bars, unless disabled). Each disabled check counts as passed. A strong bearish bar has close < open and body > 0.5 × ATR.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation count = sum of five checks: (bearish candle close, unless disabled, where close < open and close < previous close); (volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier, unless disabled); (RSI ≤ bearish threshold and RSI > 30, unless disabled); (upper wick ≥ candle range × wick ratio, unless disabled); (no strong bullish bar among the preceding opposing-pressure lookback bars, unless disabled). Each disabled check counts as passed. A strong bullish bar has close > open and body > 0.5 × ATR.
Calculation: Long zone = low is between last confirmed pivot low − 0.5 × ATR and pivot low + zone-buffer × ATR; consecutive bars in the zone are counted and reset to zero outside it. Long trigger latches true when the count reaches the wait-bars threshold and the selected trigger passes (bullish candle close, bullish engulfing, or bullish pin bar); it resets outside the zone. Bullish engulfing: current candle up, previous candle down, current close > previous open, and current open < previous close. Bullish pin bar: lower wick > 2 × body and upper wick < 0.5 × body.
Calculation: Short zone = high is between last confirmed pivot high − zone-buffer × ATR and pivot high + 0.5 × ATR; consecutive bars in the zone are counted and reset to zero outside it. Short trigger latches true when the count reaches the wait-bars threshold and the selected trigger passes (bearish candle close, bearish engulfing, or bearish pin bar); it resets outside the zone. Bearish engulfing: current candle down, previous candle up, current close < previous open, and current open > previous close. Bearish pin bar: upper wick > 2 × body and lower wick < 0.5 × body.
Calculation: Long stop/target = for a pivot-zone setup, pivot low − ATR × stop multiplier / pivot low + (pivot low − stop) × reward/risk; for a range setup, lowest low over the range lookback − ATR × stop multiplier / lowest low + (lowest low − stop) × reward/risk. Set levels when the entry signal occurs.
Calculation: Short stop/target = for a pivot-zone setup, pivot high + ATR × stop multiplier / pivot high − (stop − pivot high) × reward/risk; for a range setup, highest high over the range lookback + ATR × stop multiplier / highest high − (stop − highest high) × reward/risk. Set levels when the entry signal occurs.
Long: long trigger AND bullish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations AND (trending up OR bullish structure) OR close ≤ lowest low over the range lookback + 0.5 × ATR AND bullish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations AND ranging market
Short: short trigger AND bearish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations AND (trending down OR bearish structure) OR close ≥ highest high over the range lookback − 0.5 × ATR AND bearish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations AND ranging market
Exit: Long position, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target
Exit: Short position, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target
