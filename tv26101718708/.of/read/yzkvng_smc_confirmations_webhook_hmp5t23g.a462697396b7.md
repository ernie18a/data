Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low at the center of equal-length left and right lookbacks, confirmed only after the right lookback has completed; retain the latest confirmed value of each.
Calculation: EMA slope = close EMA minus its value at a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Trending up or down = ADX at least its threshold AND EMA slope respectively greater than positive ATR times a multiplier or less than negative ATR times the same multiplier.
Calculation: Range high and low = highest high and lowest low over the same lookback, including the current bar. Ranging = close between those levels inclusively AND their difference greater than ATR times a multiplier AND neither trending up nor trending down.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish close = close respectively greater than open and previous close, or less than open and previous close. Bullish engulfing = close greater than open AND previous close less than previous open AND close greater than previous open AND open less than previous close; bearish engulfing reverses all comparisons. Bullish pin bar = lower wick greater than body times a multiplier AND upper wick less than body times a smaller multiplier; bearish pin bar swaps the wicks. Body = absolute difference between close and open; lower wick = min(open, close) minus low; upper wick = high minus max(open, close).
Calculation: Bullish or bearish confirmation count = sum of five indicators, each equal to 1 when its requirement is disabled or its condition holds: respectively bullish or bearish close; volume greater than its SMA times a multiplier; RSI respectively at least a lower threshold and below an upper bound, or at most an upper threshold and above a lower bound; respectively lower or upper wick at least the high-low range times a multiplier; and no respectively bearish or bullish bar among the preceding configurable number of bars with body greater than that bar's ATR times a multiplier. A disabled requirement contributes 1.
Calculation: Bullish zone = latest confirmed pivot low minus ATR times a lower multiplier through that pivot low plus ATR times an upper multiplier. Bearish zone = latest confirmed pivot high minus ATR times the same upper multiplier through that pivot high plus ATR times the same lower multiplier. Recalculate the boundaries on every bar.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish zone trigger = at least a configurable number of consecutive bars whose low or high respectively lies within the corresponding zone inclusively AND, on at least one of those bars after the wait is met, the selected bullish or bearish candle condition holds. The selected condition is close, engulfing, or pin bar, or is always satisfied when the trigger requirement is disabled. Once triggered, retain the trigger until the respective zone is left or an automatic trade using it is submitted; leaving the zone also resets its consecutive-bar count.
Long: Bullish zone trigger AND bullish confirmation count at least its minimum AND (trending up OR both confirmed pivot types exist AND EMA slope greater than zero).
Short: Bearish zone trigger AND bearish confirmation count at least its minimum AND (trending down OR both confirmed pivot types exist AND EMA slope less than zero).
Long: Ranging AND close at most range low plus ATR times a multiplier AND bullish confirmation count at least its minimum.
Short: Ranging AND close at least range high minus ATR times the same multiplier AND bearish confirmation count at least its minimum.
Long: Latest confirmed pivot low exists AND (trending up OR both confirmed pivot types exist AND EMA slope greater than zero).
Short: Latest confirmed pivot high exists AND (trending down OR both confirmed pivot types exist AND EMA slope less than zero) AND the preceding long projection condition is false.
Exit: Long, low at most the stop OR high at least the target; for a zone entry, freeze the stop at the entry-signal bar's latest confirmed pivot low minus ATR times the stop multiplier and the target at that pivot low plus the pivot-to-stop distance times the reward-to-risk multiplier; for a range entry, use the entry-signal bar's range low in place of the pivot low.
Exit: Short, high at least the stop OR low at most the target; for a zone entry, freeze the stop at the entry-signal bar's latest confirmed pivot high plus ATR times the stop multiplier and the target at that pivot high minus the stop-to-pivot distance times the reward-to-risk multiplier; for a range entry, use the entry-signal bar's range high in place of the pivot high.
