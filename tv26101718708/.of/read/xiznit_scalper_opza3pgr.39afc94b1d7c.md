Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, resetting both sums on the first bar of any enabled session; if cumulative volume is zero, use (high + low + close) / 3. Accumulate on every bar, including outside sessions.
Calculation: Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin Ashi open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: Session entry count = entries submitted since the first bar of each enabled session; reset each count on that session's first bar. Determine sessions from the bar's opening hour in America/Chicago: the first session includes hours at or after its start OR before its end, treating an end at midnight as zero; the other sessions include hours at or after their start AND before their end. When sessions overlap, use and increment the first active session's count in first, second, third priority order.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR on the signal bar × stop multiplier; retain this distance and the signal bar's close until exit.
Long: position is flat AND an enabled session is active AND its entry count is below its limit AND bars since the last submitted entry >= cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR deviation mode selected OR close > session VWAP) AND (momentum filter disabled OR rate of change of close > 0) AND ((EMA mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= trend threshold AND shorter EMA of close crosses above longer EMA of close) OR (deviation mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= trend threshold OR deviation mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= range threshold) AND (close - session VWAP) / session VWAP < negative deviation threshold OR (Heikin Ashi mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= trend threshold AND current and previous Heikin Ashi close > their respective Heikin Ashi open AND RSI of close > upper threshold) OR (structure mode selected AND (efficiency ratio >= trend threshold OR efficiency ratio >= range threshold) AND each high and each low is greater than its predecessor throughout the selected structure lookback)).
Short: position is flat AND an enabled session is active AND its entry count is below its limit AND bars since the last submitted entry >= cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR deviation mode selected OR close < session VWAP) AND (momentum filter disabled OR rate of change of close < 0) AND ((EMA mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= trend threshold AND shorter EMA of close crosses below longer EMA of close) OR ((deviation mode selected AND (efficiency ratio >= trend threshold OR efficiency ratio >= range threshold)) AND (close - session VWAP) / session VWAP > deviation threshold) OR (Heikin Ashi mode selected AND efficiency ratio >= trend threshold AND current and previous Heikin Ashi close < their respective Heikin Ashi open AND RSI of close < lower threshold) OR (structure mode selected AND (efficiency ratio >= trend threshold OR efficiency ratio >= range threshold) AND each high and each low is less than its predecessor throughout the selected structure lookback)).
Exit: long, low <= signal bar close - stop distance OR high >= signal bar close + stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: short, high >= signal bar close + stop distance OR low <= signal bar close - stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio.
