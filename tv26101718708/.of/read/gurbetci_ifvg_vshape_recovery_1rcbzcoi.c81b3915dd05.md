Calculation: Swing levels = The latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, using equal left and right pivot lengths. Replace a level when a newer pivot of the same kind is confirmed. Clear a swing low after a bullish sweep or a close below it; clear a swing high after a bearish sweep or a close above it. Update pivots before evaluating sweeps.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = Low < current swing low AND close > current swing low.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = High > current swing high AND close < current swing high.
Calculation: Bullish reversal level = At a bullish sweep, start with the preceding bar if the sweep bar closes above its open; otherwise start with the sweep bar. Scan backward through consecutive bars whose close < open, up to the scan limit. Use the open of the oldest bar in that run, or the starting bar's open if the run is empty. Replace any previous pending bullish level; expire it when its age exceeds the sweep-to-confirmation window or after its first confirmation.
Calculation: Bearish reversal level = At a bearish sweep, start with the preceding bar if the sweep bar closes below its open; otherwise start with the sweep bar. Scan backward through consecutive bars whose close > open, up to the scan limit. Use the open of the oldest bar in that run, or the starting bar's open if the run is empty. Replace any previous pending bearish level; expire it when its age exceeds the sweep-to-confirmation window or after its first confirmation.
Calculation: Inversion gaps = Create a bullish gap when low > high two bars earlier and the gap width >= an ATR multiple; its lower edge is that earlier high and its upper edge is the current low. Create a bearish gap when high < low two bars earlier and the gap width >= an ATR multiple; its lower edge is the current high and its upper edge is that earlier low. Keep only the most recent configured number of gaps, counting both ordinary and inverted gaps. Invert a bullish gap when close, or low if wick detection is selected, < its lower edge; invert a bearish gap when close, or high if wick detection is selected, > its upper edge. An inverted gap can be retested starting on the bar after inversion and can signal only once. Remove an inverted bullish gap when close > its upper edge; remove an inverted bearish gap when close < its lower edge. Evaluate retests before removal.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: A pending bullish reversal level exists AND close > that level AND absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND (the hard session filter is disabled OR the bar is in the configured New York session).
Short: A pending bearish reversal level exists AND close < that level AND absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND (the hard session filter is disabled OR the bar is in the configured New York session).
Long: An unsignaled inverted bearish gap exists AND low <= its upper edge AND close > its upper edge.
Short: An unsignaled inverted bullish gap exists AND high >= its lower edge AND close < its lower edge.
