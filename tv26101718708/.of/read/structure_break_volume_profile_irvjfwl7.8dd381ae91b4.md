Calculation: Confirmed pivots = A pivot high is a bar whose high is at least every high in an equal-length window before it and greater than every high in the window after it; a pivot low is a bar whose low is at most every low in the preceding window and less than every low in the following window. Both are known only when the following window completes.
Calculation: Structure levels = Within the configured trailing calculation window, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low as separate levels. Each new pivot replaces its corresponding level and marks it unbroken. A break marks that level broken until replaced. Process new pivots before breaks. An upward break occurs when the configured source, either close or high, is greater than the unbroken high level; a downward break occurs when the configured source, either close or low, is less than the unbroken low level. Process upward before downward breaks when both occur on one bar. Structure direction starts neutral and becomes the direction of each processed break.
Calculation: Active POC = At each break that reverses a non-neutral structure direction, record the preceding structure's highest and lowest confirmed pivot levels and their times. When a new direction starts, initialize those extremes to the current pivot high and low; while that direction persists, update them with more extreme confirmed pivots before processing breaks. For the latest reversal, use bars from the earlier extreme's pivot bar through the bar before the reversal, restricted to the configured retained source-bar window. Divide the range between the extremes into equal price rows. Replace missing or nonpositive volume with one. Allocate each bar's volume among rows in proportion to overlap with its high-low range clipped to the structure range; if the clipped range has no positive width and the close lies in the structure range, allocate all volume to the close's row. The POC is the midpoint of the lowest-index row with maximum volume, provided that volume is positive. The range must have positive height and its end time must be later than its start time. The source calculates the POC only on the last available chart bar; it is unavailable on preceding historical bars. Retain the latest reversal's direction and time.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed.
Long: An upward structure break occurs.
Short: A downward structure break occurs.
Long: The latest reversal direction is bullish AND the current bar is later than that reversal AND low <= active POC AND high >= active POC AND the preceding bar did not touch its then-active POC after its latest reversal.
Short: The latest reversal direction is bearish AND the current bar is later than that reversal AND low <= active POC AND high >= active POC AND the preceding bar did not touch its then-active POC after its latest reversal.
