Calculation: Paired high pivots = consecutive bars where both high and RSI have confirmed pivot highs using the same left and right bar counts; store their high, RSI, and pivot bar index. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right bars.
Calculation: Paired low pivots = consecutive bars where both low and RSI have confirmed pivot lows using the same left and right bar counts; store their low, RSI, and pivot bar index. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right bars.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = regular divergence enabled AND current paired low price < previous paired low price AND current paired low RSI > previous paired low RSI, OR hidden divergence enabled AND current paired low price > previous paired low price AND current paired low RSI < previous paired low RSI; in either case, the paired pivot bar distance <= maximum distance AND a price pivot low is confirmed on the current bar. The most recent paired pivots are used even if the current price pivot has no corresponding RSI pivot.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = regular divergence enabled AND current paired high price > previous paired high price AND current paired high RSI < previous paired high RSI, OR hidden divergence enabled AND current paired high price < previous paired high price AND current paired high RSI > previous paired high RSI; in either case, the paired pivot bar distance <= maximum distance AND a price pivot high is confirmed on the current bar. The most recent paired pivots are used even if the current price pivot has no corresponding RSI pivot.
Long: bullish divergence
Short: bearish divergence
Long: bullish divergence AND volume > volume SMA AND bars since the last filtered long or short signal >= cooldown AND (trend filter disabled OR close <= close EMA × (1 + tolerance)); initialize the last filtered signal bar index to zero.
Short: bearish divergence AND volume > volume SMA AND bars since the last filtered long or short signal >= cooldown AND (trend filter disabled OR close >= close EMA × (1 - tolerance)); initialize the last filtered signal bar index to zero.
