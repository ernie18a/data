Calculation: ATR percentage = higher-timeframe ATR divided by chart close, multiplied by 100; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for ATR.
Calculation: ATR percentage average = EMA or SMA of ATR percentage on the higher timeframe, using the same lookback throughout; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest chart-bar close since entry multiplied by (1 − trailing-stop fraction).
Long: DEMA crosses above EMA AND ATR percentage is below its higher-timeframe average.
Exit: long, DEMA crosses below EMA OR chart-bar low is at or below the long trailing stop OR chart-bar high is at or above entry price multiplied by (1 + take-profit fraction).
