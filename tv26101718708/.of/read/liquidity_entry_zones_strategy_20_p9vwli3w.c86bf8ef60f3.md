Calculation: Stored pivot levels = retain the most recent configured number of confirmed pivot highs and lows separately; a pivot has the same configurable number of bars on each side and is stored when confirmed.
Calculation: Swept low level = the most recently confirmed stored pivot low for which the current low is below the level by at least the minimum sweep distance; swept high level = the most recently confirmed stored pivot high for which the current high is above the level by at least the minimum sweep distance.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = swept low level exists AND close > swept low level AND (strong reclaim disabled OR close > (high + low) / 2) AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= maximum body fraction AND high - low >= minimum range; use zero for the fractions when high = low.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = swept high level exists AND close < swept high level AND (strong reclaim disabled OR close < (high + low) / 2) AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= maximum body fraction AND high - low >= minimum range; use zero for the fractions when high = low.
Calculation: Pending sweep = the latest sweep of the respective direction, including the current bar; replace it on every new same-direction sweep, and clear it after a same-direction confirmed signal or when its age exceeds the confirmation window.
Calculation: Confirmed signal = a pending sweep has age <= confirmation window AND the current close passes the optional same-direction candle-body, EMA and sweep-midpoint filters AND no prior confirmed signal exists or its age > cooldown; if both directions qualify on the same bar, choose long. Update cooldown and clear the corresponding pending sweep even if an active trade blocks the signal.
Long: bullish sweep.
Short: bearish sweep.
Long: confirmed long signal AND (simulation disabled OR trade blocking disabled OR no simulated trade active before this bar's exits).
Short: confirmed short signal AND (simulation disabled OR trade blocking disabled OR no simulated trade active before this bar's exits).
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance); stop takes priority if both are reached.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance); stop takes priority if both are reached.
