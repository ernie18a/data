Calculation: Upper wick share = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0; lower wick share = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bearish wick = upper wick share >= minimum upper wick share AND lower wick share <= maximum lower wick share AND (ATR range filter disabled OR high - low >= ATR × range multiplier). Bullish wick = lower wick share >= minimum lower wick share AND upper wick share <= maximum upper wick share AND (ATR range filter disabled OR high - low >= ATR × range multiplier).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = bearish wick count in the window >= minimum count AND bullish wick count in the window < minimum count. Bullish pattern = bullish wick count in the window >= minimum count AND bearish wick count in the window < minimum count.
Calculation: Active formation = when no formation is active and either pattern changes from false to true, fix the top at the highest high and the bottom at the lowest low of that pattern's window. Initialize buy and sell volume by summing, over window bars with high > bottom AND low < top, volume × (close - low) / (high - low) and volume × (high - close) / (high - low), respectively; each contribution is 0 when high = low. On each later bar without a breakout, add its corresponding contributions if high > bottom AND low < top. A breakout is close > top or close < bottom in close mode, otherwise high > top or low < bottom. End the formation after any breakout, or after a non-breakout bar whose distance in bars from the formation bar exceeds the enabled wait limit. A breakout on that bar takes precedence over timeout.
Calculation: Breakout size condition = breakout size filter disabled OR high - low >= size multiplier × simple moving average of high - low over the preceding bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at entry × stop multiplier, or entry price × stop percentage. The target distance is risk distance × the risk-reward parameter of the highest-numbered enabled target; if no target is enabled, there is no target exit.
Long: Bullish wick AND bearish wick = false.
Short: Bearish wick.
Long: Active formation top < breakout price (close in close mode, otherwise high) AND accumulated buy volume >= accumulated sell volume AND breakout size condition.
Short: Active formation bottom > breakout price (close in close mode, otherwise low) AND accumulated sell volume > accumulated buy volume AND breakout size condition.
Exit: long, bar is later than entry bar AND (low <= entry price - risk distance OR high >= entry price + target distance).
Exit: short, bar is later than entry bar AND (high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= entry price - target distance).
