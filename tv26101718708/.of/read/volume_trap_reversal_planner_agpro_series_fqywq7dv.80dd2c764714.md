Calculation: ATR used = maximum of ATR, current true range, and a positive price increment parameter; unavailable ATR is treated as zero.
Calculation: Reference high and low = highest high and lowest low over the reference lookback ending at the preceding bar; require both to exist and the high to exceed the low.
Calculation: Candle range = maximum of high minus low and the positive price increment parameter. Body share = absolute close minus open divided by candle range. Upper wick share = (high minus maximum of open and close) divided by candle range. Lower wick share = (minimum of open and close minus low) divided by candle range. Bullish close location = (close minus low) divided by candle range; bearish close location = (high minus close) divided by candle range.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its SMA including the current bar; treat missing volume as zero, use current volume when the SMA is unavailable, and use a relative volume of one when the resulting baseline is not positive.
Calculation: Normalize(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / (strong - weak), 0, 1), or zero if strong equals weak. Base score = clamp(weighted sum of normalized relative volume, directional wick share, directional close location, and sweep depth in ATR, plus a setup-fit component, 0, base-score cap). The volume component's strong threshold is the greater of the full-volume threshold and the effective spike threshold plus a positive margin; its weak threshold is a fraction of the effective spike threshold. The sweep component uses the unadjusted sweep-buffer parameter as its weak threshold. The setup-fit component is its maximum when relative volume reaches the effective spike threshold, otherwise its maximum multiplied by relative volume normalized between a baseline and that threshold. Use separate thresholds and weights for each component.
Calculation: Confirmation score = clamp(weighted sum of normalized body share, directional close location, and directional distance from the trap reference in current ATR, 0, confirmation-score cap); use separate thresholds and weights for each component. Total score = clamp(base score plus confirmation score, 0, total-score cap).
Calculation: Effective spike threshold, minimum wick share, sweep buffer and recovery buffer are parameters selected together by sensitivity; aggressive sensitivity lowers the spike threshold, wick requirement and sweep buffer, while conservative sensitivity raises all four. Confirmation minimum body share, minimum directional close location and ATR close buffer are parameters selected together by confirmation mode; stricter modes increase all three.
Calculation: A bullish trap candidate requires low < reference low minus effective sweep buffer times ATR used AND close > reference low plus effective recovery buffer times ATR used AND lower wick share >= minimum wick share AND relative volume >= effective spike threshold. A bearish trap candidate requires high > reference high plus effective sweep buffer times ATR used AND close < reference high minus effective recovery buffer times ATR used AND upper wick share >= minimum wick share AND relative volume >= effective spike threshold. Apply the selected allowed sides before resolving simultaneous candidates; if both qualify, select the side with the higher base score, selecting bullish on a tie.
Calculation: A new trap is the selected candidate with base score >= watch-score threshold and bars since the preceding new trap >= trap cooldown; the first trap has no cooldown restriction. It replaces any existing active trap before other events are evaluated. Store its side, bar, reference, base score, bullish risk edge = trap low minus risk-buffer parameter times trap ATR used, bearish risk edge = trap high plus risk-buffer parameter times trap ATR used, bullish confirmation level = maximum of trap open and close, bearish confirmation level = minimum of trap open and close, bullish target = maximum of reference high, trap close plus minimum target-distance parameter times trap ATR used, and trap close plus fallback target-distance parameter times trap ATR used, and bearish target = minimum of reference low and the corresponding two distances subtracted from trap close.
Calculation: An active trap fails when its bullish side has close < stored risk edge or its bearish side has close > stored risk edge. An unconfirmed trap expires when bars since its creation > confirmation window. Failure takes priority over expiration and confirmation. A new trap takes priority over failure, expiration, confirmation and target review. Clear terminal trap state after bars since failure, expiration or target review > terminal reset period.
Calculation: A bullish confirmation requires bars since the active bullish trap > 0 and <= confirmation window AND close > open AND body share >= confirmation minimum AND bullish close location >= confirmation minimum AND close > stored bullish confirmation level plus confirmation ATR buffer times current ATR used. A bearish confirmation uses close < open, bearish close location, and close < stored bearish confirmation level minus confirmation ATR buffer times current ATR used. Confirmation occurs only once for an active, unfailed, unexpired trap; the trap becomes confirmed even when total score is below the readiness threshold.
Long: A new bullish trap occurs OR an active bullish trap confirms OR relative volume >= effective spike threshold AND low < reference low minus effective sweep buffer times ATR used AND bullish base score >= watch-score threshold AND bullish side is allowed AND no new trap occurs AND bars since the last event label >= label cooldown, with no restriction for the first label.
Short: A new bearish trap occurs OR an active bearish trap confirms OR relative volume >= effective spike threshold AND high > reference high plus effective sweep buffer times ATR used AND bearish base score >= watch-score threshold AND bearish side is allowed AND NOT (low < reference low minus effective sweep buffer times ATR used AND bullish side is allowed) AND no new trap occurs AND bars since the last event label >= label cooldown, with no restriction for the first label.
Exit: long, active bullish trap fails OR active bullish trap was confirmed on an earlier bar AND high >= its stored target AND target has not previously been touched.
Exit: short, active bearish trap fails OR active bearish trap was confirmed on an earlier bar AND low <= its stored target AND target has not previously been touched.
