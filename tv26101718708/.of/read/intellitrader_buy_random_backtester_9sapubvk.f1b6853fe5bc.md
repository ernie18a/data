Calculation: Price-change value = timeframe close − (previous timeframe close / previous timeframe close) × 100.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = SMA of an SMA of the stochastic oscillator calculated from close, high, and low; the two smoothing lengths are equal.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic RSI = SMA of an SMA of the stochastic oscillator calculated with RSI of close as its close, high, and low inputs; the RSI and stochastic lookbacks are equal, and the two smoothing lengths are equal.
Calculation: Timeframe values = calculations on each of six ordered, distinct timeframes. On a higher timeframe, Pine v3 historical lookahead maps the final value of the higher-timeframe bar to all chart bars within it; on a lower timeframe, it maps the first lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. On the chart timeframe, use the current bar. Rising compares the mapped value with its value on the preceding chart bar.
Long: Chart bar datetime is within the inclusive backtest period AND position is flat AND no close signal occurred on the preceding chart bar AND, for every enabled indicator on every timeframe, its value is greater than its minimum AND less than its maximum AND, if its rising option is enabled, greater than its preceding chart-bar value; the independently enabled indicators are shorter-lookback RSI of close, longer-lookback RSI of close, smoothed stochastic, smoothed stochastic RSI, and CCI of close AND, if the price-change filter is enabled, the price-change value on every timeframe is greater than or equal to its minimum AND less than or equal to its maximum.
Exit: long, chart bar datetime is within the inclusive backtest period AND chart bar is after the entry-signal bar AND take profit is enabled AND highest high since and including the entry-signal bar is greater than or equal to entry-signal close × (1 + take-profit fraction) AND current low is less than or equal to that highest high × (1 − trailing-profit fraction); trailing-profit fraction is capped at the take-profit fraction.
Exit: long, chart bar datetime is within the inclusive backtest period AND chart bar is after the entry-signal bar AND stop loss is enabled AND current low is less than or equal to entry-signal close × (1 − stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, chart bar datetime is within the inclusive backtest period AND chart bar is after the entry-signal bar AND trailing stop is enabled AND current low is less than or equal to the highest high since and including the entry-signal bar × (1 − trailing-stop fraction).
