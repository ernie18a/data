Calculation: Confirmed pivots = a pivot high or low has the highest high or lowest low over an equal number of chart bars on each side; it becomes available only when the right-hand bars have completed. Use the latest three confirmed highs H1,H2,H3 and lows L1,L2,L3 in chronological order, with their pivot bar indices.
Calculation: Range scale A = average high minus low over a lookback of completed bars on a higher timeframe; use close times a fallback fraction when the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Equality tolerance E and slope tolerance S are independent fractions of A.
Calculation: Pattern candidates = evaluate only when a new pivot is confirmed, at least three highs and lows exist, and the distance from the earliest of H1 and L1 to the latest of H3 and L3 is within the minimum and maximum pattern spans. Let R=max(H1,H2,H3), U=min(L1,L2,L3), convergence mean H1-L1>0 AND H3-L3<=(H1-L1) times a convergence fraction, and each high or low direction be up if its third pivot exceeds its first by more than S, down if below its first by more than S, otherwise flat. Candidate fields are direction, trigger, invalidation, height and priority: (1) short head-and-shoulders if H2>H1+E AND H2>H3+E AND abs(H1-H3)<=1.6E; trigger=(L2+L3)/2, invalidation=H3, height=H2-trigger; (2) long inverse head-and-shoulders if L2<L1-E AND L2<L3-E AND abs(L1-L3)<=1.6E; trigger=(H2+H3)/2, invalidation=L3, height=trigger-L2; (3) short double top if abs(H2-H3)<=E AND H2>L3+E AND (H2+H3)/2-L3>=E times a minimum-depth multiplier; trigger=L3, invalidation=max(H2,H3), height=(H2+H3)/2-L3; (4) long double bottom if abs(L2-L3)<=E AND H3>L2+E AND H3-(L2+L3)/2>=E times the minimum-depth multiplier; trigger=H3, invalidation=min(L2,L3), height=H3-(L2+L3)/2; (5) long ascending triangle if high direction is flat AND low direction is up; trigger=R, invalidation=L3; (6) short descending triangle if low direction is flat AND high direction is down; trigger=U, invalidation=H3; (7) symmetrical triangle if high direction is down AND low direction is up AND convergence holds: long if H1+L1<H3+L3, otherwise short; long trigger=R and invalidation=U, short trigger=U and invalidation=R; (8) short rising wedge if both directions are up AND convergence holds; trigger=L3, invalidation=H3; (9) long falling wedge if both directions are down AND convergence holds; trigger=H3, invalidation=L3. All triangle and wedge heights equal R-U. Pattern families may be independently enabled.
Calculation: Candidate selection = choose the first qualifying priority group in this order: head-and-shoulders, double, ascending or descending triangle, symmetrical triangle, wedge. Within a group choose the highest score, retaining the earlier candidate in the listed order on a tie. For head-and-shoulders and double patterns, score=round-to-one-decimal(10*(0.65*max(0,1-absolute difference between the two shoulders or double pivots/(E+1e-12))+0.35*min(1,height/(0.6*A)))). For triangles and wedges, score=round-to-one-decimal(10*(0.55*(1 if convergence holds else 0.4)+0.45*min(1,height/(0.7*A)))).
Calculation: Pending setup = when flat with no pending setup and at least the minimum signal spacing since the previous entry, snapshot the selected candidate on its detection bar. Starting on the next bar, while flat, first confirm it if close crosses its trigger in its direction and the minimum signal spacing is met; otherwise cancel a long setup if low<invalidation, cancel a short setup if high>invalidation, or cancel either if the current bar index exceeds detection bar index plus the expiry length. A confirmed setup becomes an active trade and is cleared. Process active-trade exits before pending confirmation, then scan for a new setup; a new setup cannot confirm on its detection bar.
Calculation: Entry levels = at confirmation, entry price is close; clamp the snapshotted pattern height between a minimum and maximum multiple of the current A. Long target levels equal entry plus the clamped height times independent target multipliers; short targets subtract those amounts. Long stop equals invalidation minus an A-based buffer, replaced by entry minus twice the buffer if the original stop is at or above entry. Short stop equals invalidation plus the buffer, replaced by entry plus twice the buffer if the original stop is at or below entry. Freeze all levels at entry.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND pivot markings are enabled.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND pivot markings are enabled.
Long: A newly selected pending setup has long direction.
Short: A newly selected pending setup has short direction.
Long: A pending long setup exists AND no active trade exists AND close>trigger AND bars since the previous entry>=minimum signal spacing.
Short: A pending short setup exists AND no active trade exists AND close<trigger AND bars since the previous entry>=minimum signal spacing.
Exit: long, high>=either target level OR low<=stop level, starting on the bar after entry; when targets and stop are both reached on the same bar, targets take precedence.
Exit: short, low<=either target level OR high>=stop level, starting on the bar after entry; when targets and stop are both reached on the same bar, targets take precedence.
