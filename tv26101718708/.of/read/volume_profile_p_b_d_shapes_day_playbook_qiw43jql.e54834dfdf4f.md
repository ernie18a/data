Calculation: Previous-day volume profile = distribute each intraday bar’s volume across price bins in proportion to the overlap of its high-low range with each bin; use volume 1 when missing or nonpositive. POC = midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Value area = expand from the POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent bin until the parameter share of daily volume is reached; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges.
Calculation: Previous-day shape = P if the POC lies at or above the parameter upper fraction of the day’s high-low range; b if it lies at or below the parameter lower fraction; otherwise D. Use the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Volume spike = current or prior chart-timeframe volume is greater than its corresponding 20-bar chart-timeframe volume average multiplied by a parameter.
Calculation: Entry stop = structural stop level beyond the setup’s relevant prior low, prior high, VAL, VAH, or POC by a parameter multiple of chart-timeframe ATR(14). For D fades, use the larger of that ATR buffer and a parameter fraction of the prior value-area width.
Calculation: Targets = P continuation: prior-day high, then prior-day high plus prior value-area width; P failure: VAL minus prior value-area width, then prior-day low. b continuation: prior-day low, then prior-day low minus prior value-area width; b failure: VAH plus prior value-area width, then prior-day high. D fade: POC, then the opposite value-area edge.
Long: Flat, on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, with prior-day shape P, today’s open not below prior VAL, prior chart bar low at or below prior VAH and prior close at or above prior VAL, current low at or above prior low, current close above open and at or above prior POC, and continuation not already fired today.
Short: Flat, on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, with prior-day shape b, today’s open not above prior VAH, prior chart bar high at or above prior VAL and prior close at or below prior VAH, current high at or below prior high, current close below open and at or below prior POC, and continuation not already fired today.
Short: Flat, prior-day shape P, consecutive chart-timeframe closes below prior VAL reaching the parameter acceptance count, and volume spike.
Long: Flat, prior-day shape b, consecutive chart-timeframe closes above prior VAH reaching the parameter acceptance count, and volume spike.
Short: Flat, prior-day shape D, today opened inside prior value area, range not invalidated by acceptance beyond either edge, VAH fade count below its parameter limit, and current high at or above prior VAH while current close is below prior VAH.
Long: Flat, prior-day shape D, today opened inside prior value area, range not invalidated by acceptance beyond either edge, VAL fade count below its parameter limit, and current low at or below prior VAL while current close is above prior VAL.
Exit: Long, high reaches the second target OR low reaches the active stop; after high reaches the first target, move the stop to entry if break-even management is enabled.
Exit: Short, low reaches the second target OR high reaches the active stop; after low reaches the first target, move the stop to entry if break-even management is enabled.
