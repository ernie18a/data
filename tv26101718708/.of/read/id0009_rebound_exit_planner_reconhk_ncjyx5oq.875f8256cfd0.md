Calculation: Dip sample = Start an episode when the current low is at least the minimum dip percentage below the highest high over the peak lookback and the minimum gap since the previous episode has elapsed. Record that rolling peak and the lowest low reached during the episode, together with the close on the bar of that low. End the episode when a later close is at least the rebound-confirmation percentage above the episode low or the maximum episode length is reached. Retain it if its dip percentage from the recorded peak is within the permitted range and its selected base, either the episode low or its associated close, is positive. Assign the dip percentage to the nearest bounded dip-grid bucket. Starting on the next bar after the episode ends, measure the highest high over the rebound window; the sample rebound percentage is that high divided by the selected base minus one.
Calculation: Target band = At the entry bar, select either all completed dip samples or samples in buckets around the current dip percentage, measured from the current rolling highest high to the current low. For similar samples, use the smallest permitted bucket radius containing at least the minimum sample count, or the maximum radius if none does. For each target percentage on an ascending bounded grid, calculate the fraction of selected samples whose rebound percentage reaches that target; optionally use the lower Wilson confidence bound, except for a full-hit-rate threshold, which uses the raw fraction. For each configured hit-rate threshold, select the largest target meeting it, provided the selected sample count reaches the minimum event count. If the first threshold has no qualifying target and the minimum event count is met, use the lowest target-grid value. For each subsequent threshold with a qualifying target, raise its target if necessary to at least the preceding available band's target. The selected band price is the entry-bar projection base, either close or low, multiplied by one plus the selected target percentage. A band is available only if the sample count also reaches the plotting minimum or the low-sample override is enabled.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND close > longer-period EMA of close AND (RSI of close > its midpoint OR volume > its simple moving average).
Exit: long, high >= the selected available target band fixed at entry AND bars since entry <= the rebound window.
Exit: long, bars since entry >= the rebound window AND the selected available target band fixed at entry has not been reached.
