Calculation: Smoothed price = 2-pole Ehlers SuperSmoother of (high + low + close) / 3; with cutoff length L, a = exp(-sqrt(2) * pi / L), b = 2 * a * cos(sqrt(2) * pi / L), c = 1 - b + a², and S[t] = c * (price[t] + price[t-1]) / 2 + b * S[t-1] - a² * S[t-2]; initialize the first S to the first price and unavailable earlier values to zero.
Calculation: Velocity = EMA of (smoothed price - previous smoothed price) / max(ATR, positive numerical floor); use zero for unavailable previous smoothed price and ATR.
Calculation: Acceleration = EMA of velocity - previous velocity; use zero for unavailable previous velocity.
Calculation: Acceleration deviation = max(population standard deviation of acceleration over its lookback, positive numerical floor); use zero for an unavailable standard deviation. Acceleration score = acceleration / acceleration deviation.
Calculation: Efficiency = abs(smoothed price - smoothed price at the efficiency lookback) / sum of abs(smoothed price - previous smoothed price) over that lookback; use zero for unavailable historical prices and return zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / max(volume SMA, positive volume floor); use zero for an unavailable volume SMA.
Calculation: Regime = neutral initially; update to long on a Long decision, short on a Short decision, and neutral on a brake Exit decision. Count bars since the latest regime-changing decision, initializing the count above the cooldown. Evaluate decisions using the regime at the start of the bar; an opposite entry takes priority over a brake on the same bar.
Long: warm-up complete AND prior regime is neutral or short AND bars since the latest regime change >= cooldown AND velocity > positive velocity threshold AND acceleration score > positive launch threshold AND efficiency >= efficiency threshold AND (volume is unavailable OR relative volume >= volume threshold).
Short: warm-up complete AND prior regime is neutral or long AND bars since the latest regime change >= cooldown AND velocity < negative velocity threshold AND acceleration score < negative launch threshold AND efficiency >= efficiency threshold AND (volume is unavailable OR relative volume >= volume threshold).
Exit: long, Short decision OR (warm-up complete AND prior regime is long AND (acceleration < negative brake threshold * acceleration deviation OR velocity crosses below half the positive velocity threshold OR velocity crosses below zero OR (velocity <= positive velocity threshold AND acceleration < zero))).
Exit: short, Long decision OR (warm-up complete AND prior regime is short AND (acceleration > positive brake threshold * acceleration deviation OR velocity crosses above half the negative velocity threshold OR velocity crosses above zero OR (velocity >= negative velocity threshold AND acceleration > zero))).
