Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the preceding and following N chart bars; it is known only after the following N bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the preceding and following N chart bars; it is known only after the following N bars complete.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR on the signal bar × stop-distance multiplier.
Calculation: Entry price = close of the signal bar.
Long: confirmed bar AND ATR > 0 AND trade slot available AND cooldown elapsed AND no opposite-side position is open AND ((close crosses above session VWAP AND at least N consecutive prior closes were below session VWAP AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (EMA(21) > EMA(50) AND EMA(50) > its value three bars earlier AND low <= EMA(21) within the last N bars AND close > EMA(9) AND close > open AND close > prior high AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (after close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high, within the retest window a later bar has low <= that level + ATR × tolerance AND close > that level AND close > open) OR (low < the lowest low of the prior N bars AND close > that low AND close > open AND close - low > high - close AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (within the maximum pivot gap, a confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low and RSI at the newer pivot is higher than RSI at the preceding pivot) OR (during the session, after the opening range of the first N minutes is complete and before any range break has fired, close > opening-range high)); when several setups fire with limited capacity, process in this order: VWAP reclaim, EMA pullback, break and retest, liquidity sweep, RSI divergence, opening range break.
Short: confirmed bar AND ATR > 0 AND trade slot available AND cooldown elapsed AND no opposite-side position is open AND ((close crosses below session VWAP AND at least N consecutive prior closes were above session VWAP AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (EMA(21) < EMA(50) AND EMA(50) < its value three bars earlier AND high >= EMA(21) within the last N bars AND close < EMA(9) AND close < open AND close < prior low AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (after close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low, within the retest window a later bar has high >= that level - ATR × tolerance AND close < that level AND close < open) OR (high > the highest high of the prior N bars AND close < that high AND close < open AND high - close > close - low AND volume is unavailable OR volume >= its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier) OR (within the maximum pivot gap, a confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high and RSI at the newer pivot is lower than RSI at the preceding pivot) OR (during the session, after the opening range of the first N minutes is complete and before any range break has fired, close < opening-range low)); when several setups fire with limited capacity, process in this order: VWAP reclaim, EMA pullback, break and retest, liquidity sweep, RSI divergence, opening range break.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - risk distance
Exit: Long, low > entry price - risk distance AND high >= entry price + risk distance × TP3 multiple
Exit: Long, low > entry price - risk distance AND high < entry price + risk distance × TP3 multiple AND bars since entry >= expiry
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + risk distance
Exit: Short, high < entry price + risk distance AND low <= entry price - risk distance × TP3 multiple
Exit: Short, high < entry price + risk distance AND low > entry price - risk distance × TP3 multiple AND bars since entry >= expiry
