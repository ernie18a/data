Calculation: Valid bar = a completed chart bar with available ATR > 0.
Calculation: Volume filter = volume multiplier <= 0 OR volume is unavailable OR volume SMA is unavailable OR volume >= volume multiplier × volume SMA.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with equal left and right lookbacks, identified on its confirmation bar; its price and RSI are taken from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Break-and-retest levels = track the latest confirmed swing high and low independently. On each bar, update each level from a newly confirmed pivot unless its previous break is within the retest window, in which case retain only the latest new pivot as pending. Mark an unbroken high level broken when close > level and an unbroken low level broken when close < level; record the break bar. A break is live through the retest window inclusive. Clear its broken status when its retest signal fires. After signal evaluation, if a break is no longer live and a pending pivot exists, replace the level with that pivot and clear the broken status and pending pivot. A broken level without a pending pivot remains broken after expiry until replaced by a new pivot.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of session bars whose opening timestamps are less than the range duration after the first session bar's opening timestamp. The range is complete on the first in-session bar at or beyond that duration. Reset the range and its fired status at the start of each session.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the entry bar × stop-distance multiplier, fixed at entry; entry price = signal-bar close.
Long: Valid bar AND session VWAP is available AND close crosses above session VWAP AND at least the specified number of consecutive preceding closes were below their respective session VWAP values AND volume filter passes.
Short: Valid bar AND session VWAP is available AND close crosses below session VWAP AND at least the specified number of consecutive preceding closes were above their respective session VWAP values AND volume filter passes.
Long: Valid bar AND medium-period EMA > long-period EMA AND long-period EMA > its value at the specified earlier bar AND low <= medium-period EMA at least once within the specified number of bars including the current bar AND close > short-period EMA AND close > open AND close > previous high AND volume filter passes.
Short: Valid bar AND medium-period EMA < long-period EMA AND long-period EMA < its value at the specified earlier bar AND high >= medium-period EMA at least once within the specified number of bars including the current bar AND close < short-period EMA AND close < open AND close < previous low AND volume filter passes.
Long: Valid bar AND a swing-high break is live AND current bar is after its break bar AND low <= broken high level + current ATR × retest tolerance AND close > broken high level AND close > open.
Short: Valid bar AND a swing-low break is live AND current bar is after its break bar AND high >= broken low level - current ATR × retest tolerance AND close < broken low level AND close < open.
Long: Valid bar AND low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open AND close - low > high - close AND volume filter passes.
Short: Valid bar AND high > highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND close < open AND high - close > close - low AND volume filter passes.
Long: Valid bar AND a swing low is confirmed AND its pivot is within the maximum bar gap of the immediately preceding confirmed swing low AND its price < the preceding pivot price AND its pivot-bar RSI > the preceding pivot-bar RSI.
Short: Valid bar AND a swing high is confirmed AND its pivot is within the maximum bar gap of the immediately preceding confirmed swing high AND its price > the preceding pivot price AND its pivot-bar RSI < the preceding pivot-bar RSI.
Long: Valid bar AND inside the session AND opening range is complete AND neither opening-range direction has fired in this session AND close > opening-range high.
Short: Valid bar AND inside the session AND opening range is complete AND neither opening-range direction has fired in this session AND close < opening-range low.
Exit: long, valid bar AND bar is after entry AND (low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + entry risk × final target multiplier OR bars since entry >= expiry).
Exit: short, valid bar AND bar is after entry AND (high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - entry risk × final target multiplier OR bars since entry >= expiry).
