Calculation: Bar notation = O[k], H[k], L[k], C[k], V[k] are open, high, low, close and volume k bars ago; omit [0]. Body[k] = abs(C[k]-O[k]); range[k] = H[k]-L[k]; body fraction[k] = body[k]/range[k], or zero if range[k] is zero. Bull[k] means C[k]>O[k]; bear[k] means C[k]<O[k].
Calculation: Trend context = up when close>SMA(close) AND SMA(close)>its value a specified number of bars ago; down when close<SMA(close) AND SMA(close)<its value the same number of bars ago. When the trend-context filter is disabled, both up and down context are true.
Calculation: Swing direction = bullish when the most recent bar making a lookback high is later than the most recent bar making a lookback low; bearish otherwise. Previous swing high or low is the preceding recorded lookback high or low, including consecutive record bars.
Calculation: Trend quality = clamp(weighted sum of efficiency ratio, normalized volume z-score, normalized distance of close from the midpoint of its rolling high-low range, and the fraction of recent close changes matching the sign of the net close change, to the interval from zero to one). Efficiency ratio is abs(close minus close at the lookback) divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero when the denominator is zero. Normalize volume z-score by adding a parameter, dividing by a parameter and clamping to zero through one. Normalize range position as twice its absolute distance from the midpoint, or zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive trend and bands = multiply ATR by a weighted combination of a constant and efficiency ratio. Multiply a base band multiplier by a function that decreases with trend quality; smooth this multiplier separately with faster and slower recursive exponential smoothers. From close subtract the faster multiplier times adjusted ATR for the lower raw band in an uptrend and the slower multiplier in a downtrend; add the slower multiplier times adjusted ATR for the upper raw band in an uptrend and the faster multiplier in a downtrend. If the previous close was above the previous lower band, the lower band is the greater of its raw value and previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. If the previous close was below the previous upper band, the upper band is the lesser of its raw value and previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. Initialize trend up; change it to up when the previous trend is down AND close>previous upper band, and to down when the previous trend is up AND close<previous lower band; otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Exponentially weighted volume price = recursively smooth typical price times capped volume and capped volume with the same exponential coefficient, then divide the former by the latter and apply a Hull moving average. Replace nonpositive volume with rolling median volume and cap it between a positive minimum and a multiple of rolling mean volume. Derive the coefficient from a half-life equal to a base half-life divided by a power of ATR divided by smoothed ATR, clamped between minimum and maximum half-lives. On a swing-direction change, reinitialize at the most recent opposite swing and replay through the current bar using the current coefficient and current volume cap, gradually ramping the coefficient over the initial replay bars; otherwise update once per bar.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish school votes = four independent tests. OBV vote: bullish when price is near its rolling low while OBV is sufficiently above its rolling low, with both price and OBV ranges positive, OR OBV>SMA(OBV) AND OBV's positive change over a short lag exceeds that change at the same lag; bearish is the symmetric rolling-high and negative-change test. Volume-price vote: smooth short VWMA(close) minus long VWMA(close) with a recursive moving average; bullish when it is positive AND rising over a lag, bearish when negative AND falling. Pullback vote: record a pullback when close crosses from above to below EMA(close), or the reverse; accumulate its bars, volume and body fractions while close remains on the other side, and expire it after a maximum duration. On the first return across the EMA, vote in the original direction if the pullback lasted at least a minimum number of bars AND (mean pullback volume is below the pre-pullback volume SMA OR mean pullback body fraction is below a threshold) AND the return bar's body fraction exceeds a threshold AND volume exceeds mean pullback volume. Also vote bullish when low<previous swing low AND close>that low AND close>open AND volume>SMA(volume); vote bearish when high>previous swing high AND close<that high AND close<open AND volume>SMA(volume). EMA vote: bullish when short EMA crosses above medium EMA AND close>long EMA AND RSI is below an upper threshold; bearish when short EMA crosses below medium EMA AND close<long EMA AND RSI is above a lower threshold. Each school can independently be enabled or disabled.
Calculation: Recent votes and council signals = count each enabled school's most recent vote in each direction while its age is at most the agreement window; a school can count in both directions. A bullish council signal occurs when bullish votes meet a minimum AND exceed bearish votes AND bars since the previous bullish council signal exceed the cooldown; bearish is symmetric, with its own cooldown.
Calculation: Composite bias = clamp to the interval from negative one to one the sum of: the most recent council signal's direction multiplied by a linearly decaying factor that reaches zero at the cooldown; adaptive trend direction multiplied by the greater of a floor and trend quality, or zero if adaptive trend is disabled; and swing direction multiplied by normalized abs(close minus exponentially weighted volume price)/ATR, bounded by a floor and ceiling. Divide that sum by the number of components, add a signed pattern-strength boost for the selected pattern, and clamp again to the same interval.
Calculation: Selected pattern = among all matching patterns below, select the greatest strength; break equal-strength ties in the order listed. A bullish or bearish engulfing pattern has stronger strength if its body successively engulfs more than one prior body within a lookback; otherwise it has ordinary strength. All other patterns have the strength stated below. Pattern names have exactly these definitions, rather than conventional variants: bullish engulfing: bear[1] AND bull AND C>=O[1] AND C[1]>=O AND body>body[1] AND down context; bearish engulfing: bull[1] AND bear AND O>=C[1] AND O[1]>=C AND body>body[1] AND up context. Morning star: bear[2] AND body fraction[1]<small-body threshold AND max(O[1],C[1])<L[2] AND O>max(O[1],C[1]) AND bull AND C>(O[2]+C[2])/2 AND down context; evening star is its price-direction mirror with up context. Morning doji star uses bear[2], a doji at [1], max(O[1],C[1])<C[2], O>max(O[1],C[1]), bull, C>(O[2]+C[2])/2 and down context; evening doji star is the mirror. Bullish abandoned baby: bear[2] AND H[1]<L[2] AND H[1]<L AND bull AND doji[1] AND down context; bearish is the mirror. A doji has body fraction at or below a threshold. Bullish marubozu: bull AND body fraction at or above a threshold; bearish is the mirror. Bullish three-line strike: bear[3] AND bear[2] AND bear[1] AND C[2]<C[3] AND C[1]<C[2] AND bull AND C>O[3] AND O<=C[1] AND body fraction at or above a threshold AND down context; bearish is the mirror with up context. These patterns have ordinary strength except for the engulfing exception.
Calculation: Additional selected patterns = bullish three white soldiers: bull[2] AND bull[1] AND bull AND C>C[1]>C[2] AND O[1]>O[2] AND O[1]<C[2] AND O>O[1] AND O<C[1] AND each of the three upper shadows is at most a specified fraction of its body AND down context; bearish three black crows is the price-direction mirror with up context. Bullish kicker: bear[1] AND O>H[1] AND bull AND both body fractions meet a threshold; bearish kicker is the mirror. Rising three methods: bull[4] AND bear[3] AND bear[2] AND bear[1] AND each of bars [3], [2] and [1] has high<H[4] AND low>L[4] AND bull AND C>C[4] AND up context; falling three methods is the mirror with down context. Upside Tasuki gap: bull[2] AND bull[1] AND L[1]>H[2] AND bear AND O<C[1] AND O>O[1] AND C>H[2] AND C<=L[1] AND up context; downside Tasuki gap is the mirror with down context. These patterns have stronger strength. In the selected-pattern tie order, the ordinary-strength patterns precede these patterns in the order listed, followed by the stronger patterns in the order listed.
