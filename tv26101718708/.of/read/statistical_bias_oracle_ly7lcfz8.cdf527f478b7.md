Calculation: Sampled bias = For the chart data, sample high, low, and close on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. On historical bars, higher-timeframe samples use the last completed bar; on a forming chart bar, they use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Compare the samples from one and two chart bars ago. Set bias to bullish if the first sample's low < the second sample's low AND its close > the second sample's low, OR its close > the second sample's high. Otherwise, set bias to bearish if the first sample's high > the second sample's high AND its close < the second sample's high, OR its close < the second sample's low. Otherwise retain the prior bias, initially neutral. Bullish takes precedence if both patterns occur.
Long: Sampled bias = bullish
Short: Sampled bias = bearish
