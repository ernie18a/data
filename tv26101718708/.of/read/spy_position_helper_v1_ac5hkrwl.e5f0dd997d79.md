Calculation: Macro trend = bullish if the last completed higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA and that EMA > its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar; bearish if both inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Support = the highest available value strictly below the current close among the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low, the last completed shorter higher-timeframe low, the last completed daily low and close, current VWAP, and current long-term EMA. Resistance = the lowest available value strictly above the current close among the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high, the last completed shorter higher-timeframe high, the last completed daily high and close, current VWAP, and current long-term EMA. The macro timeframe is higher than the shorter higher timeframe. Confirm pivots using equal numbers of bars on each side and retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind. Daily bars are higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Zone width = ATR × zone-width multiplier. Near support = support exists AND low <= support + zone width AND close >= support - zone width. Near resistance = resistance exists AND high >= resistance - zone width AND close <= resistance + zone width. Near confluence = absolute difference between close and VWAP <= zone width OR absolute difference between close and long-term EMA <= zone width.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = previous resistance exists AND close > previous resistance AND previous close <= previous resistance. Bearish breakout = previous support exists AND close < previous support AND previous close >= previous support. Bullish rejection = near support AND close > previous high AND close > open. Bearish rejection = near resistance AND close < previous low AND close < open.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute value of close - open divided by high - low, or zero when high = low. Bullish trigger = (bullish breakout OR bullish rejection) AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram AND positive DI > negative DI. Bearish trigger = (bearish breakout OR bearish rejection) AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction AND MACD histogram < previous MACD histogram AND negative DI > positive DI. MACD uses a shorter fast EMA and a longer slow EMA.
Calculation: Long stop candidate = minimum of low and support, or low if support is unavailable, minus ATR × stop-buffer multiplier. Short stop candidate = maximum of high and resistance, or high if resistance is unavailable, plus ATR × stop-buffer multiplier. Long risk = close - long stop candidate; short risk = short stop candidate - close. Long room = resistance - close, or ATR × target multiplier if resistance is unavailable. Short room = close - support, or ATR × target multiplier if support is unavailable. Long room is sufficient if long risk > minimum price increment AND long room >= long risk × minimum room multiplier; short room is sufficient if short risk > minimum price increment AND short room >= short risk × minimum room multiplier.
Calculation: Long score = 2 points for bullish macro trend, 1 for neutral, otherwise 0; plus 2 if near support OR bullish breakout; plus 1 each if near confluence, RSI > midpoint, MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD line > signal line, positive DI > negative DI AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND ADX > previous ADX, bullish trigger, and long room is sufficient. Short score = 2 points for bearish macro trend, 1 for neutral, otherwise 0; plus 2 if near resistance OR bearish breakout; plus 1 each if near confluence, RSI < midpoint, MACD histogram < previous histogram AND MACD line < signal line, negative DI > positive DI AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND ADX > previous ADX, bearish trigger, and short room is sufficient.
Calculation: At entry, fix the stop at the corresponding stop candidate. Fix the long target at the lesser of entry price + long risk × target multiplier and entry-bar resistance when resistance exists, otherwise entry price + long risk × target multiplier. Fix the short target at the greater of entry price - short risk × target multiplier and entry-bar support when support exists, otherwise entry price - short risk × target multiplier.
Long: The chart bar is completed AND its opening time is within the configured New York session AND position is flat AND no prior signal exists OR the number of bars since the last entry signal > cooldown; AND bullish trigger AND long room is sufficient AND long score >= minimum score AND bearish trigger is false.
Short: The chart bar is completed AND its opening time is within the configured New York session AND position is flat AND (no prior signal exists OR the number of bars since the last entry signal > cooldown) AND bearish trigger AND short room is sufficient AND short score >= minimum score AND bullish trigger is false.
Exit: long, the chart bar is completed AND ((bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target OR (close < entry price AND MACD histogram < previous histogram))) OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR bar opening time is outside the configured New York session).
Exit: short, the chart bar is completed AND ((bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target OR (close > entry price AND MACD histogram > previous histogram))) OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR bar opening time is outside the configured New York session).
