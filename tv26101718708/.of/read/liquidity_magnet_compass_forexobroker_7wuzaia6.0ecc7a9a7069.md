Calculation: Tracked zones = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks. Add each zone on its confirmation bar, high before low if both confirm; its age starts at the pivot bar. After each addition, remove the oldest zone if the combined count exceeds the pool limit. Then remove zones older than the maximum age. Retain swept zones in the count until removed.
Calculation: Low sweep = an unswept pivot-low zone has low < zone price AND zone price - low >= ATR multiplied by the minimum sweep factor AND either rejection-close filtering is disabled OR close > zone price. Mark every qualifying zone swept immediately, even when no signal is produced.
Calculation: High sweep = an unswept pivot-high zone has high > zone price AND high - zone price >= ATR multiplied by the minimum sweep factor AND either rejection-close filtering is disabled OR close < zone price. Mark every qualifying zone swept immediately, even when no signal is produced.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = volume >= its SMA multiplied by the volume factor AND bars since the last eligible selected signal >= the cooldown; initialize the last signal bar to the first bar. On each completed bar, select the low-sweep signal if only a low sweep occurred, the high-sweep signal if only a high sweep occurred, or, if both occurred, the low-sweep signal when close >= open and the high-sweep signal when close < open. Update the last signal bar only when an eligible signal is selected.
Calculation: North and south magnet strengths = after marking sweeps, count all unswept zones above and below close respectively, regardless of zone type. For each side, divide the distance from close to the nearest zone on that side by ATR; use a fallback distance if no such zone exists. If the resulting distance is positive, strength is the lesser of a maximum strength and the sum of the zone count multiplied by a count factor plus a distance factor divided by the distance; otherwise strength is zero.
Long: Signal eligibility AND low sweep AND (no high sweep OR close >= open).
Short: Signal eligibility AND high sweep AND (no low sweep OR close < open).
Long: Low sweep AND no eligible selected signal.
Short: High sweep AND no low sweep AND no eligible selected signal.
Long: North magnet strength > south magnet strength + direction threshold.
Short: South magnet strength > north magnet strength + direction threshold.
