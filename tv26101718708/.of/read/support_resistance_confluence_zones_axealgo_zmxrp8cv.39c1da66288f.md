Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars, using a configurable left-hand lookback. Keep separate ordered buffers for unpaired highs and lows; discard pivots older than a configurable age and discard the oldest when a buffer exceeds its configurable capacity.
Calculation: Zones = on each confirmed chart bar with positive ATR, process a confirmed high pivot as resistance and a confirmed low pivot as support, in that order. Merge a pivot into the first same-role zone whose bounds extended by an ATR-based tolerance contain it; expand the bounds to include the pivot, increment touches, set the last-touch bar to the current bar, retain any high-volume flag, and replace the anchor if the pivot has higher volume. Otherwise pair it with the first buffered same-side pivot within the tolerance, creating a zone with two touches, the earlier pivot's bar as its birth bar, the current bar as its last-touch bar, a high-volume flag if either pivot had high volume, and the higher-volume pivot's price as its anchor; otherwise buffer it. High volume means volume exceeds its SMA by a configurable multiple. Pad or cap zone height around its midpoint to configurable ATR-based minimum and maximum heights; the minimum is also at least a configurable fraction of the clustering tolerance. After processing pivots, merge same-role zones whose bounds overlap after extension by the tolerance, iterating newer zones backward and merging each into the first matching older zone; combine their bounds, touches, prior touches and high-volume flags, keep the earlier birth and later last-touch bar, and keep the anchor with higher volume. Apply the same height bounds after every expansion or merge.
Calculation: Zone lifecycle = after zone creation and merging, process zones newest first on each confirmed bar with positive ATR. Unless the birth bar is the current bar, a close above resistance or below support flips its role if flipping is enabled: add current touches to prior touches, reset current touches to one, and set the last-touch bar to the current bar; otherwise remove the broken zone. For an unbroken zone overlapping the bar's high-low range, increment touches and update the last-touch bar and high-volume flag if the configurable minimum separation since the last touch has elapsed. Then remove zones whose anchor distance from close exceeds a configurable ATR multiple, and remove oldest zones until the configurable capacity is met. The zone anchor is its highest-volume pivot price if that price remains within its bounds and anchor mode is enabled; otherwise it is the bounds' midpoint.
Calculation: Zone score = truncate to an integer the capped sum of a touch score and optional alignment, round-number and high-volume bonuses. The touch score is an asymptotic function of total current and prior touches, with a configurable minimum touch count, ceiling, scale and curvature. Alignment means the anchor is within an ATR-based tolerance of the latest confirmed same-side pivot on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Round-number proximity means the absolute anchor is within an ATR-based tolerance of a rounded multiple of a price-magnitude-dependent decimal grid; use several progressively finer grids including half steps. The volume bonus applies if the zone's high-volume flag is set.
Calculation: Visible zones = zones whose score meets a configurable minimum, except that resistance zones wholly above close and support zones wholly below close are limited to a configurable number per role, ranked by increasing absolute anchor distance from close; equal distances share rank. Calculate visibility before the current bar's zone lifecycle processing.
Calculation: At-price zone = the nearest visible zone by absolute anchor distance among zones for which close is inside the bounds, or resistance has high at or above its bottom and close no more than an ATR-based tolerance above its top, or support has low at or below its top and close no more than that tolerance below its bottom. For each other visible zone, classify its anchor as above or below close.
Long: A support zone exists after pivot processing and merging AND its birth bar is before the current bar AND the bar overlaps its bounds AND its score after any eligible retest update is at least a configurable threshold AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top AND close > open.
Short: A resistance zone exists after pivot processing and merging AND its birth bar is before the current bar AND the bar overlaps its bounds AND its score after any eligible retest update is at least a configurable threshold AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND close < open.
Long: No at-price zone exists AND no other visible zone has an anchor above close AND at least one other visible zone has an anchor below close.
