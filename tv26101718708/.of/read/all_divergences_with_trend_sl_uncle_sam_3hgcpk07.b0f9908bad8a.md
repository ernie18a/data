Calculation: Divergence indicators = MACD line and histogram of close; RSI of close; SMA-smoothed stochastic of close, high, and low; CCI of close; momentum of close; OBV; short-period VWMA of close minus long-period VWMA of close; Chaikin money flow; MFI of close; negative Williams Vix Fix, -(highest close - low) / highest close; SMA-smoothed stochastic RSI of close; true strength index of close minus its EMA signal; volume-weighted MACD histogram, where the MACD line is EMA(volume × close) / EMA(volume) at a shorter period minus the same ratio at a longer period, and the histogram subtracts its EMA signal; bull-bear power, high + low - 2 × EMA(close); smoothed balance of power; smoothed relative volatility index; and logistic dominance. Each indicator may be enabled independently.
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power = TEMA of EMA(B), with independently chosen smoothing periods. Let D = high - low when high != low, otherwise a small positive constant; B = ((high - open) + (close - low) + max(close - open, 0) - (open - low) - (high - close) - max(open - close, 0)) / (3 × D).
Calculation: Smoothed relative volatility index = a selectable SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of 100 × U / (U + D), where S is the rolling standard deviation of close, U is the EMA of S when close rises and zero otherwise, and D is the EMA of S when close does not rise and zero otherwise. The standard-deviation, EMA, and final smoothing periods are independently chosen.
Calculation: Logistic dominance = the sign of the mean of a sequence multiplied by its population standard deviation. Let H(n) be the highest high over n bars, g(s, r, n) = r × s / H(n) × (1 - s / H(n)), r = -g(-close, close change over the dominance lookback / close at that lookback, dominance lookback) - g(close, close change over the dominance lookback / close at that lookback, dominance lookback), and m = g(close, r, map lookback). The sequence contains r × abs(m[i]) × (1 - m[i]) for successive historical offsets starting at the current bar, over a chosen sequence length.
Calculation: Bullish regular divergence = for any enabled indicator, current indicator > previous indicator OR current close > previous close, AND a recent confirmed close pivot low exists whose age is greater than a minimum and no greater than a maximum search age, among a limited number of most recent confirmed pivot lows, AND previous indicator > indicator at the pivot AND previous close < close at the pivot, AND every intervening indicator and close value is at or above its respective straight line joining the pivot value to the previous-bar value. A pivot low has the chosen number of bars on each side and is confirmed only after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bearish regular divergence = for any enabled indicator, current indicator < previous indicator OR current close < previous close, AND a recent confirmed close pivot high exists whose age is greater than a minimum and no greater than a maximum search age, among a limited number of most recent confirmed pivot highs, AND previous indicator < indicator at the pivot AND previous close > close at the pivot, AND every intervening indicator and close value is at or below its respective straight line joining the pivot value to the previous-bar value. A pivot high has the chosen number of bars on each side and is confirmed only after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Trend average = selectable SMA, HMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of a chosen backtest-data price source.
Long: Bullish regular divergence AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend average).
Short: Bearish regular divergence AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend average).
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop percentage × close at the entry signal OR high >= entry price + long profit percentage × close at the entry signal.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short stop percentage × close at the entry signal OR low <= entry price - short profit percentage × close at the entry signal.
Exit: long, MA risk exit enabled AND every close in a chosen consecutive-bar count, including the current close, < the current trend average.
Exit: short, MA risk exit enabled AND every close in a chosen consecutive-bar count, including the current close, > the current trend average.
Exit: long, bearish regular divergence AND short strategy enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend average).
Exit: short, bullish regular divergence AND long strategy enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend average).
