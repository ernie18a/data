Calculation: Candidate top = on each bar, if the previous bar’s high is the highest high in the lookback ending on that bar and either no top is active or that high exceeds the active top’s high, store the previous bar’s high and low as the active top; deactivate it after a short signal. Update it before testing for a short signal.
Calculation: Candidate bottom = on each bar, if the previous bar’s low is the lowest low in the lookback ending on that bar and either no bottom is active or that low is below the active bottom’s low, store the previous bar’s high and low as the active bottom; deactivate it after a long signal. Update it before testing for a long signal.
Calculation: Signal spacing = bars since the most recent long or short signal; evaluate short before long and update the most recent signal bar immediately after each signal.
Calculation: Stop = at a new long signal, candidate bottom low minus current ATR times a stop buffer; at a new short signal, candidate top high plus current ATR times a stop buffer. If a same-side signal occurs before the existing stop has been breached, retain the entry price and update the stop to the higher of the old and new stops for a long, or the lower for a short. A stop breach is tested using the previous bar’s close or wick, according to the confirmation setting, before processing a new signal.
Calculation: Targets = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the absolute entry-to-stop distance multiplied by each of three independently configured reward-to-risk ratios. Set all targets at a new signal using the previous close as entry price. At a same-side signal before a stop breach, retain the first target and recalculate the second and third using the updated stop; reset a recalculated target’s hit status if its price changes. Each target is hit only once until reset.
Calculation: Directional bias = initially neutral; a long signal sets it bullish and stores that signal’s initial long stop, while a short signal sets it bearish and stores that signal’s initial short stop. After processing signals, bullish bias becomes bearish when the previous close is below the stored long stop; bearish bias becomes bullish when the previous close is above the stored short stop. Retain the resulting bias until another such update.
Long: An active candidate bottom exists AND the previous close, or previous high when wick confirmation is selected, is above its high plus current ATR times a breakout multiplier AND (volume filtering is disabled OR previous volume is greater than its volume SMA times a volume multiplier) AND (session filtering is disabled OR the current bar is in the configured session) AND (no previous signal exists OR signal spacing is at least the configured minimum).
Short: An active candidate top exists AND the previous close, or previous low when wick confirmation is selected, is below its low minus current ATR times a breakout multiplier AND (volume filtering is disabled OR previous volume is greater than its volume SMA times a volume multiplier) AND (session filtering is disabled OR the current bar is in the configured session) AND (no previous signal exists OR signal spacing is at least the configured minimum).
Long: Directional bias is bullish.
Short: Directional bias is bearish.
Exit: long, a short signal occurs OR the previous close, or previous low when wick confirmation is selected, is at or below the stop OR current high is at or above an unhit target.
Exit: short, a long signal occurs OR the previous close, or previous high when wick confirmation is selected, is at or above the stop OR current low is at or below an unhit target.
