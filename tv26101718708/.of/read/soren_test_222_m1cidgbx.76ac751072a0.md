Calculation: Range filter = filter the open price using an EMA-smoothed EMA of absolute open-price changes multiplied by a range multiplier; upper/lower bands = filter ± smoothed range; upward/downward counts increment on consecutive rising/falling filter bars and reset when the filter moves the other way
Calculation: Relative Momentum Index = 100 − 100/(1 + EMA(max(close − close[momentum lag], 0), lookback) / EMA(max(close[momentum lag] − close, 0), lookback))
Calculation: Position reference price = average price for the current same-side signal sequence: include each signal bar’s close and each qualifying swap-stop price (long: last long signal close × (1 − swap percentage); short: last short signal close × (1 + swap percentage)); reset the sequence on an opposite-side signal or take-profit
Long: (RSI of VWAP(close) crosses above its oversold threshold AND high > upper range-filter band AND upward count > 0 AND NOT (negative DI > positive DI AND ADX > threshold)) OR (positive DI > negative DI AND ADX > threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) × volume multiplier AND RSI(low) < overbought threshold AND KAMA(open) > prior KAMA(open)) OR (Relative Momentum Index crosses above its oversold threshold AND high > upper range-filter band AND upward count > 0 AND NOT (negative DI > positive DI AND ADX > threshold))
Short: (RSI of VWAP(close) crosses below its overbought threshold AND low < lower range-filter band AND downward count > 0 AND NOT (positive DI > negative DI AND ADX > threshold)) OR (negative DI > positive DI AND ADX > threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) × volume multiplier AND RSI(low) > oversold threshold AND KAMA(open) < prior KAMA(open)) OR (Relative Momentum Index crosses below its overbought threshold AND low < lower range-filter band AND downward count > 0 AND NOT (positive DI > negative DI AND ADX > threshold))
Exit: long, high crosses above position reference price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low crosses below position reference price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: short, low crosses below position reference price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high crosses above position reference price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
