Calculation: OR High = highest high of the bars in the opening window of the session; OR Low = lowest low of the same bars; both are usable only after the window has ended, and are reset each day.
Calculation: Long Stop = min(low of the bar 2 bars before the setup bar, OR High); Long Target = OR High + RR * (OR High - Long Stop), where RR is a fixed reward-to-risk multiple.
Calculation: Short Stop = max(high of the bar 2 bars before the setup bar, OR Low); Short Target = OR Low - RR * (Short Stop - OR Low).
Long: Setup bar (completed bar, same timeframe as chart) is after the opening window AND bar time-of-day is before the entry cutoff AND no position is open AND no trade has been entered yet today AND no other setup is pending AND close > OR High AND low > high of the bar 2 bars before. Then place a buy limit at OR High, which is filled on the first following bar with low <= OR High while bar time-of-day is still before the entry cutoff and no trade has happened yet today; otherwise cancel the order at the entry cutoff or session end. Entry price = OR High.
Short: Setup bar (completed bar, same timeframe as chart) is after the opening window AND bar time-of-day is before the entry cutoff AND no position is open AND no trade has been entered yet today AND no other setup is pending AND close < OR Low AND high < low of the bar 2 bars before. Then place a sell limit at OR Low, which is filled on the first following bar with high >= OR Low while bar time-of-day is still before the entry cutoff and no trade has happened yet today; otherwise cancel the order at the entry cutoff or session end. Entry price = OR Low.
Exit: long, low <= Long Stop OR high >= Long Target OR bar time-of-day >= the end-of-session flatten time
Exit: short, high >= Short Stop OR low <= Short Target OR bar time-of-day >= the end-of-session flatten time
