Calculation: Major pivot = either a fixed-window pivot confirmed after equally many bars on each side, or an adaptive pivot. For the adaptive method, initialize the running high and low from the first bar and update them on equal or more extreme prices. If the last confirmed pivot was not a high and the running high minus current low is at least ATR times a multiplier, confirm the running high, then reset the running low to current low; otherwise, if the last confirmed pivot was not a low and current high minus the running low is at least that threshold, confirm the running low, then reset the running high to current high. Record each pivot on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Minor pivot = a fixed-window pivot confirmed after equally many bars on each side, using a shorter window than the fixed-window major pivot. If both a high and a low are confirmed on the same bar, retain both for sweep references but use the low for the pending break level.
Calculation: Pending break level = the latest confirmed pivot price of the specified kind and scale; replace it on a new pivot of that kind and clear it after its corresponding structure break. A break above a high requires close > pending high AND previous close <= that same pending high; a break below a low requires close < pending low AND previous close >= that same pending low.
Calculation: Minor bias = bullish after a minor high break and bearish after a minor low break, remaining unchanged until the next minor break; process a high break before a low break if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Sweep reference = the most recently confirmed minor pivot of the specified kind among a limited number of the latest minor pivots, whose pivot bar is older than a minimum age. Same-direction sweep cooldown starts at the first chart bar and resets on each sweep of that direction.
Long: A major pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A major pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Close breaks above a pending major or minor pivot high.
Short: Close breaks below a pending major or minor pivot low.
Long: Minor bias is bullish.
Short: Minor bias is bearish.
Long: Low < sweep reference low AND close > sweep reference low AND lower wick >= ATR times a multiplier AND lower wick / max(high - low, a positive price floor) >= a minimum ratio AND bars since the last bullish sweep > cooldown.
Short: High > sweep reference high AND close < sweep reference high AND upper wick >= ATR times a multiplier AND upper wick / max(high - low, a positive price floor) >= a minimum ratio AND bars since the last bearish sweep > cooldown.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND low - high two bars ago >= ATR times a minimum gap multiplier.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago - high >= ATR times a minimum gap multiplier.
