Calculation: Swing high/low = a chart-bar high/low confirmed as a pivot using equal numbers of bars on each side.
Calculation: Bearish CISD level = when a chart bar's high exceeds an unswept confirmed swing high no older than the maximum sequence length, save the open and bar index of the most recent bullish chart bar immediately preceded by a bearish chart bar. Keep each saved level until the close is below it or its source bar is older than the maximum sequence length; discard a swing when it becomes older than that length. Bullish and bearish bars have close > open and close < open, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish CISD level = when a chart bar's low falls below an unswept confirmed swing low no older than the maximum sequence length, save the open and bar index of the most recent bearish chart bar immediately preceded by a bullish chart bar. Keep each saved level until the close is above it or its source bar is older than the maximum sequence length; discard a swing when it becomes older than that length.
Calculation: Sweep alert availability = initially available; after any higher-timeframe sweep-and-return alert, unavailable until the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar. Evaluate the alert before resetting availability on that first chart bar.
Long: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the forming higher-timeframe close > the last completed higher-timeframe high OR (the forming higher-timeframe close >= the last completed higher-timeframe low AND the forming higher-timeframe close <= the last completed higher-timeframe high AND the forming higher-timeframe high <= the last completed higher-timeframe high AND the forming higher-timeframe low < the last completed higher-timeframe low).
Short: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the forming higher-timeframe close < the last completed higher-timeframe low OR (the forming higher-timeframe close >= the last completed higher-timeframe low AND the forming higher-timeframe close <= the last completed higher-timeframe high AND the forming higher-timeframe high > the last completed higher-timeframe high AND the forming higher-timeframe low >= the last completed higher-timeframe low).
Long: Chart close > any unexpired bullish CISD level.
Short: Chart close < any unexpired bearish CISD level.
Long: Sweep alert availability = available AND forming higher-timeframe low < last completed higher-timeframe low AND forming higher-timeframe close >= last completed higher-timeframe low AND (forming higher-timeframe high <= last completed higher-timeframe high OR forming higher-timeframe close > last completed higher-timeframe high).
Short: Sweep alert availability = available AND forming higher-timeframe high > last completed higher-timeframe high AND forming higher-timeframe close <= last completed higher-timeframe high.
