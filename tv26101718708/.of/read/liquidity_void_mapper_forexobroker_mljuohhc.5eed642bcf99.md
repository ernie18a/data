Calculation: Void zones = On confirmed chart bars whose zero-based bar index is divisible by the recalculation interval, take the highest high and lowest low over the profile lookback including the current bar and divide that range into equal, ascending price-grid cells. For each available bar in the lookback with positive volume, if the range is positive, divide its volume equally among all grid cells from floor((low - range low) / cell width), clipped to at least zero, through floor((high - range low) / cell width), clipped to at most the last cell index. Sum the allocated volume in each cell. Divide each cell's volume by the average cell volume, using a ratio of one if the average is zero. Select cells with ratio below the void threshold in ascending price order, up to the maximum zone count. Retain their fixed lower and upper boundaries until the next recalculation; use the newly calculated zones on the recalculation bar.
Calculation: Bars since signal = Current zero-based bar index minus the index of the most recent long or short signal; initialize the most recent signal index to zero and update it after evaluating both signals.
Long: Current close > lower boundary AND current close < upper boundary of any retained void zone AND previous close <= that same lower boundary AND close > EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume AND bars since signal >= cooldown.
Short: Current close > lower boundary AND current close < upper boundary of any retained void zone AND previous close >= that same upper boundary AND close < EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume AND bars since signal >= cooldown.
