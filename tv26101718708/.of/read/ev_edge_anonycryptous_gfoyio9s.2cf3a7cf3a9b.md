Calculation: Consolidation bounds = highest high and lowest low over the consolidation lookback, including the current bar. A bar is compressed when their difference is less than ATR multiplied by the consolidation lookback and the compression factor. A consolidation is confirmed after the required number of consecutive compressed bars.
Calculation: Volume-weighted RSI = 100 - 100/(1 + RS), where each close change's positive and negative parts are multiplied by volume divided by its SMA, or by 1 when that SMA is not positive; RS is the Wilder-smoothed weighted gain divided by the Wilder-smoothed weighted loss, or 100 when the latter is zero.
Calculation: Money flow index = 100 - 100/(1 + R), where typical price is (high + low + close)/3, positive and negative money flow are typical price times volume when typical price respectively rises or falls from the preceding bar and zero otherwise, and R is the SMA of positive money flow divided by the greater of the SMA of negative money flow and 1.
Calculation: EV score = initialize to 60 at entry and update on the entry bar and every subsequent tracked bar. Save the entry close and the preceding bar's consolidation bounds. Let direction be +1 for long and -1 for short, drift = direction × (close - entry price)/ATR when ATR > 0, otherwise 0, and depth = max(0, (saved upper bound - close)/ATR) for long or max(0, (close - saved lower bound)/ATR) for short. Price delta = 8 × drift, minus 15 + 10 × depth when the long close is below the saved upper bound or the short close is above the saved lower bound. Volume ratio = volume/SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is not positive. Volume delta = 10 × (volume ratio - 1), minus 5 when absolute drift < 0.1 AND volume ratio > 1.2. With volume enabled, blended delta = (price weight × price delta + volume weight × volume delta)/(price weight + volume weight); otherwise blended delta = price delta. Add 0.3 × blended delta to the score and clamp it to [0, 100]. Count the entry bar as the first tracked bar. Stop updating after the maximum tracked count; the timeout occurs on the following bar. Evaluate entries before exits, so an exit bar cannot also start a new tracked trade.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot = a confirmed local high or low of volume-weighted RSI with the same configurable number of bars on each side; compare consecutive pivots of the same type and use the price high or low at each oscillator pivot, whether or not price itself is a pivot. Report the comparison on the pivot-confirmation bar.
Calculation: Timeframe trend = shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close). Evaluate on the chart timeframe and on each selected other timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar within the chart bar.
