Calculation: Eligible level = the high or low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, the high or low of the last completed higher-timeframe weekly bar on Monday, or the open of a chart bar at any configured session-open time in New York time; reset session-open levels at each new daily bar and retain the first open observed at each configured time. A session-open level is eligible only until an entry using that level occurs that day; an entry marks every matching session-open level as used.
Calculation: Morning bias = 0 at the start of each daily bar; when the first chart bar at the configured morning-bias time occurs, set it to 1 if close > open and -1 otherwise.
Calculation: Long stop = current low minus a buffer in wick-based mode, or current close minus a fixed distance in fixed mode; short stop = current high plus a buffer in wick-based mode, or current close plus a fixed distance in fixed mode. Recalculate these stops on every chart bar.
Calculation: Long targets = entry price plus each of two target multipliers times (entry price minus current long stop); short targets = entry price minus each multiplier times (current short stop minus entry price). The second target multiplier is greater than the first.
Long: Position is flat AND no entry has been made on the current bar AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is in either configured New York session) AND an eligible level exists with low between that level minus the sweep distance and that level plus the sweep distance inclusive AND close > open AND close > previous close AND previous volume >= previous volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > chart-timeframe EMA) AND (morning-bias filter is disabled OR morning bias >= 0) AND close > higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Position is flat AND no entry has been made on the current bar AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is in either configured New York session) AND an eligible level exists with high between that level minus the sweep distance and that level plus the sweep distance inclusive AND close < open AND close < previous close AND previous volume >= previous volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < chart-timeframe EMA) AND (morning-bias filter is disabled OR morning bias <= 0) AND close < higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Exit: Long, high >= first long target for the configured partial position OR high >= second long target for the remaining position OR low <= (entry price if high has reached the then-current first long target on any bar while the position is open, otherwise current long stop) OR bars since the position opened >= maximum bars OR bar time is within the configured forced-close session.
Exit: Short, low <= first short target for the configured partial position OR low <= second short target for the remaining position OR high >= (entry price if low has reached the then-current first short target on any bar while the position is open, otherwise current short stop) OR bars since the position opened >= maximum bars OR bar time is within the configured forced-close session.
