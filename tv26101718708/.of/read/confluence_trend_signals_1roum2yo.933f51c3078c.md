Calculation: Source = a selectable price derived from the bar fields; use close unless another source is selected.
Calculation: Coral = for smoothing period s and constant d, let a = 2 / (((s - 1) / 2 + 1) + 1), b = 1 - a, x0 = source, and xj = a * x(j-1) + b * prior xj for j = 1 through 6, with missing prior values replaced by zero. Coral = -d^3*x6 + 3*(d^2+d^3)*x5 - 3*(2*d^2+d+d^3)*x4 + (3*d+1+d^3+3*d^2)*x3. Calculate fast and slow Coral with the same smoothing period and different constants, with the slow constant larger. Direction is the sign of the change from the preceding bar, replacing a missing prior Coral with zero; a directional flip occurs when the direction becomes that sign from any other sign.
Calculation: Pivot percentages = EMA of 100 * close / latest confirmed pivot high and EMA of 100 * latest confirmed pivot low / close. A pivot is confirmed after its specified right-hand bars, using the specified left-hand and right-hand lengths. Calculate these both on the chart timeframe and, when enabled, on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar, with the same pivot and smoothing parameters.
Calculation: MACD = shorter-period EMA of source minus longer-period EMA of source; its signal is the SMA of MACD.
Calculation: VWAP = session cumulative sum of close * volume divided by session cumulative volume; rising means its current value is greater than every value in the specified preceding lookback, and falling means it is less than every such value.
Calculation: Order blocks = separately track symmetric confirmed high and low pivots at a longer swing length and a shorter internal length. On the first close crossover of each latest high pivot, create a bullish block; on the first close crossunder of each latest low pivot, create a bearish block. Search from the pivot bar through the break bar, inclusive, for the lowest parsed low for a bullish block or highest parsed high for a bearish block, retaining the most recent bar on ties. Parsed high and low equal high and low except when high - low >= twice the order-block volatility ATR, when they are swapped. Initialize the selected edges to the break bar's parsed high and low; when a better candidate is found, use its parsed low and actual high for a bullish block, or its parsed high and actual low for a bearish block. If the pivot-to-break distance is at least the maximum supported search distance, retain the break bar's parsed edges. Keep only the newest limited number of blocks per direction, then remove bullish blocks when close < block low and bearish blocks when close > block high. Bullish proximity means close >= block low AND close <= block high + risk ATR * proximity multiplier for any remaining bullish block; bearish proximity means close <= block high AND close >= block low - risk ATR * proximity multiplier for any remaining bearish block. Risk ATR falls back to high - low when unavailable.
Calculation: Directional maintenance score = sum of fixed weights for true conditions: fast Coral direction 1; slow Coral direction 0.5; chart pivot percentages aligned 1; corresponding chart high-pivot percentage for bullish or low-pivot percentage for bearish above its directional threshold 1; enabled higher-timeframe pivot percentages aligned 0.75; MACD aligned 1; matching order-block proximity 1.5; enabled and valid VWAP with close on the matching side 1; and the same VWAP condition with VWAP rising for bullish or falling for bearish 0.5. Alignment means high-pivot percentage > low-pivot percentage and MACD > signal for bullish, with both inequalities reversed for bearish.
Calculation: Directional entry score = directional maintenance score plus 0.5 for a matching fast Coral flip and 0.5 for MACD crossing its signal in the matching direction.
Calculation: Confirmed entry signal = on a completed chart bar, after the bar index reaches the maximum of the swing pivot length, order-block volatility ATR length, and the fixed minimum warmup, the corresponding gated entry condition holds and the last confirmed entry direction differs. Initialize the last direction to neutral; update it on each confirmed entry and reset it when the tracked trade closes by stop, terminal take profit, or decay. A new entry replaces the tracked trade at close.
Calculation: Initial stop and targets = at entry, risk ATR is captured. The ATR stop is entry price minus the ATR stop distance for a long or plus it for a short. If structure stops are enabled, compare the ATR stop with the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over the current bar and the specified preceding swing lookback, offset away from price by a fixed ATR fraction, and with the newest matching order-block edge offset the same way if one exists; choose the highest for a long or lowest for a short. If its absolute distance from entry is below the minimum ATR distance, replace it with a stop that minimum distance below or above entry respectively. Initial risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Three targets are entry plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, initial risk multiplied by their respective configured risk multiples. Record each target as hit when a subsequent bar's high reaches it for a long or low reaches it for a short. The stop starts at the initial stop; when trailing is enabled, target 1, 2, and 3 hits move it respectively to entry, target 1, and target 2, processing targets in that order. Test the stop against its value at the start of the bar, before that bar's target updates.
Calculation: Decay threshold = a configured absolute threshold or a configured fraction of the matching maintenance score captured at entry. Count consecutive post-entry bars for which the optional prerequisite target has been hit and the current matching maintenance score is below the threshold; reset the count otherwise.
Long: Fast Coral > preceding fast Coral.
Short: Fast Coral < preceding fast Coral.
Long: Slow Coral > preceding slow Coral.
Short: Slow Coral < preceding slow Coral.
Long: Chart pivot high percentage > chart pivot low percentage.
Short: Chart pivot high percentage < chart pivot low percentage.
Long: Higher-timeframe pivot calculation enabled AND higher-timeframe pivot high percentage > higher-timeframe pivot low percentage.
Short: Higher-timeframe pivot calculation enabled AND higher-timeframe pivot high percentage < higher-timeframe pivot low percentage.
Long: MACD > MACD signal.
Short: MACD < MACD signal.
Long: VWAP enabled AND close > VWAP.
Short: VWAP enabled AND close < VWAP.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken confirmed swing or internal pivot high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken confirmed swing or internal pivot low.
Long: Bullish order-block proximity condition holds.
Short: Bearish order-block proximity condition holds AND bullish order-block proximity condition does not hold.
Long: Confirmed entry signal AND fast Coral direction is bullish AND chart pivot high percentage > chart pivot low percentage AND MACD > signal AND bullish entry score >= minimum score AND (flip requirement disabled OR fast Coral flips bullish) AND (slow Coral requirement disabled OR slow Coral direction is bullish) AND (higher-timeframe pivot alignment requirement disabled OR higher-timeframe pivot high percentage > higher-timeframe pivot low percentage) AND (order-block requirement disabled OR bullish order-block proximity holds) AND (VWAP requirement disabled OR close > valid enabled VWAP).
Short: Confirmed entry signal AND fast Coral direction is bearish AND chart pivot high percentage < chart pivot low percentage AND MACD < signal AND bearish entry score >= minimum score AND (flip requirement disabled OR fast Coral flips bearish) AND (slow Coral requirement disabled OR slow Coral direction is bearish) AND (higher-timeframe pivot alignment requirement disabled OR higher-timeframe pivot high percentage < higher-timeframe pivot low percentage) AND (order-block requirement disabled OR bearish order-block proximity holds) AND (VWAP requirement disabled OR close < valid enabled VWAP).
Exit: long, a subsequent bar's low <= the stop at the start of that bar.
Exit: short, a subsequent bar's high >= the stop at the start of that bar.
Exit: long, trailing disabled AND single-target mode enabled AND a subsequent bar's high >= the selected target.
Exit: short, trailing disabled AND single-target mode enabled AND a subsequent bar's low <= the selected target.
Exit: long, trailing disabled AND partial-exit mode enabled AND a subsequent bar's high >= an unhit target; exit its configured fraction, dividing all three fractions by their sum if their sum exceeds one, and retain any residual.
Exit: short, trailing disabled AND partial-exit mode enabled AND a subsequent bar's low <= an unhit target; exit its configured fraction, dividing all three fractions by their sum if their sum exceeds one, and retain any residual.
Exit: long, decay enabled AND stop not hit on the current bar AND consecutive qualifying decay bars >= the confirmation length.
Exit: short, decay enabled AND stop not hit on the current bar AND consecutive qualifying decay bars >= the confirmation length.
Exit: long, a confirmed short entry signal occurs.
Exit: short, a confirmed long entry signal occurs.
