Calculation: Daily VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the calendar-day start
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the week start
Calculation: Daily VWAP deviation bands = Daily VWAP plus or minus volume-weighted standard deviation from the calendar-day start, using nearer and farther band multipliers
Long: (bar high > Daily VWAP AND bar low < Daily VWAP AND close > Daily VWAP AND EMA rising AND close > Weekly VWAP) OR (bar low <= nearer lower deviation band AND close > nearer lower deviation band OR bar low <= farther lower deviation band AND close > farther lower deviation band) AND EMA rising AND close > Daily VWAP AND close > open; more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous long or short signal
Short: (bar high > Daily VWAP AND bar low < Daily VWAP AND close < Daily VWAP AND EMA falling AND close < Weekly VWAP) OR (bar high >= nearer upper deviation band AND close < nearer upper deviation band OR bar high >= farther upper deviation band AND close < farther upper deviation band) AND EMA falling AND close < Daily VWAP AND close < open; more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous long or short signal
