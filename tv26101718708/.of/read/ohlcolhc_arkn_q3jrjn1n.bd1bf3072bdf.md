Calculation: Cycle high/low = highest high / lowest low in each cycle, compared with the immediately preceding cycle of the same type (micro, 90-minute, daily, weekly, or monthly).
Calculation: Cycle body high/low = highest max(open, close) / lowest min(open, close) in each cycle, compared with the immediately preceding cycle of the same type.
Long: For any cycle type and configured comparison instrument, the chart instrument’s current-cycle low and the comparison instrument’s current-cycle low rank oppositely versus their respective previous-cycle lows, and the two lows are not both unchanged; OR their cycle body lows rank oppositely versus their respective previous-cycle body lows, and the two body lows are not both unchanged. For an inverse comparison, compare the chart instrument’s lows with the comparison instrument’s highs, requiring matching rank direction instead.
Short: For any cycle type and configured comparison instrument, the chart instrument’s current-cycle high and the comparison instrument’s current-cycle high rank oppositely versus their respective previous-cycle highs, and the two highs are not both unchanged; OR their cycle body highs rank oppositely versus their respective previous-cycle body highs, and the two body highs are not both unchanged. For an inverse comparison, compare the chart instrument’s highs with the comparison instrument’s lows, requiring matching rank direction instead.
