Calculation: Bands = close SMA over the band lookback plus or minus a band multiplier times close standard deviation for Bollinger bands, and the same SMA plus or minus a separate multiplier times the SMA of true range over the same lookback for the second bands.
Calculation: Z = (close − close EMA) / close standard deviation, using the same lookback for both; Z = 0 when standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Up and down exhaustion = round(100 × (0.30 × run component + 0.30 × Z component + 0.25 × RSI component + 0.15 × range component)). The run component is min(1, consecutive higher closes / run threshold) for up or min(1, consecutive lower closes / run threshold) for down; an equal close resets both runs. The Z component is min(1, max(0, Z) / positive Z threshold) for up or min(1, max(0, −Z) / positive Z threshold) for down. The RSI component is min(1, max(0, RSI − overbought threshold) / max(1, 100 − overbought threshold)) for up or min(1, max(0, oversold threshold − RSI) / max(1, oversold threshold)) for down. The range component is min(1, (high − low) / ATR / range threshold), or 0 if ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = (close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close) OR ((low < lower Bollinger band OR low < lower second band) AND close > lower Bollinger band AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) >= wick fraction AND close > open). The wick condition requires high > low.
Calculation: Bearish reversal = (close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close) OR ((high > upper Bollinger band OR high > upper second band) AND close < upper Bollinger band AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) >= wick fraction AND close < open). The wick condition requires high > low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / volume SMA, or 1 when the volume SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = the number of true conditions among: low <= lower Bollinger band OR low <= lower second band; RSI <= oversold threshold; close < previous close AND RSI > previous RSI AND RSI < 50; relative volume >= volume threshold; Z <= −Z threshold.
Calculation: Bearish confluence count = the number of true conditions among: high >= upper Bollinger band OR high >= upper second band; RSI >= overbought threshold; close > previous close AND RSI < previous RSI AND RSI > 50; relative volume >= volume threshold; Z >= Z threshold.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = bars elapsed since the most recent long or short signal; before the first signal it is unrestricted.
Calculation: Entry stop = for a long, the lower of the entry bar's low and its previous bar's low minus entry-bar ATR times a stop-padding multiplier; for a short, the higher of the entry bar's high and its previous bar's high plus entry-bar ATR times the same multiplier. Entry risk = absolute difference between entry price and entry stop, or entry-bar ATR if that difference is zero. First target = entry price plus entry risk times a target multiplier for a long, or minus that amount for a short. These levels remain fixed from the signal bar, whose close is the entry price.
Long: Bullish reversal AND down exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bullish confluence count >= confluence threshold AND down exhaustion > up exhaustion AND signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold.
Short: Bearish reversal AND up exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bearish confluence count >= confluence threshold AND up exhaustion >= down exhaustion AND signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, high >= first target OR low <= entry stop OR a new long or short signal occurs.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, low <= first target OR high >= entry stop OR a new long or short signal occurs.
