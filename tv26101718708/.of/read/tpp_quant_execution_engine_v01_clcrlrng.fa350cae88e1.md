Calculation: Direction d = +1 for long and -1 for short; inequalities expressed in direction d compare d times each operand. A recent condition occurred on the current bar or within its specified lookback.
Calculation: Structure support and resistance = low and high of the last completed higher-timeframe structure bar. Bias support and resistance, close, and slow EMA = their values on the last completed higher-timeframe bias bar; the previous bias EMA is from the bar before it. The bias timeframe is higher than the structure timeframe.
Calculation: Fast and slow trend averages = EMAs of chart close, with the fast EMA shorter than the slow EMA. Trend direction d requires d × fast EMA > d × slow EMA AND d × slow EMA >= d × slow EMA at the slope lookback. Directional bias requires d × bias close > d × bias slow EMA AND d × bias slow EMA > d × previous bias slow EMA.
Calculation: Regime strength = weighted sum of normalized ADX, price efficiency, normalized EMA separation, and normalized slow-EMA slope. Price efficiency = absolute close change over the regime lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero. EMA separation = absolute fast-minus-slow EMA divided by ATR; slope = absolute slow-EMA change over the slope lookback divided by ATR. Each normalized component is divided by its respective scale and clipped to [0, 1]. Expansion in direction d requires trend direction d AND regime strength >= expansion threshold. Compression requires regime strength <= compression threshold OR ADX < compression ADX threshold.
Calculation: Directional reaction = for long, low <= structure support + ATR zone AND high >= structure support - ATR zone AND close > structure support AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND close > open; for short, high >= structure resistance - ATR zone AND low <= structure resistance + ATR zone AND close < structure resistance AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND close < open. Treat a zero candle range as a positive minimum price increment.
Calculation: Directional momentum score = sum of weighted conditions: RSI in each of three ordered directional bands, awarding only the most directional qualifying band; RSI moving in direction d; EMA of close change having sign d; fast EMA on the d side of slow EMA; and close rate of change having sign d. The momentum EMA and rate-of-change lookbacks are shorter than the trend EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA when both are available and positive; otherwise it equals 1. Volume gate passes when volume confirmation is disabled, volume is unavailable, or relative volume >= its minimum. Strong volume requires unavailable volume OR relative volume >= its separate strong-volume threshold.
Calculation: Equilibrium = session VWAP of typical price in the scalping profile, otherwise fast EMA. A directional pullback requires the candle range to overlap either the directional structure level's ATR zone or the equilibrium ATR zone AND d × close > d × open AND d × close >= d × equilibrium. Directional micro breakout requires d × close > d × the previous bars' highest high for long or lowest low for short, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Directional stop candidate = lowest low over the stop lookback minus ATR padding for long, or highest high plus ATR padding for short. Risk = directional distance from close to stop candidate, floored at a positive minimum price increment. Structural reward-to-risk = distance from close to structure resistance for long or structure support for short, divided by risk; if that level is not ahead of close, use the minimum reward-to-risk threshold.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe pulse score = on each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, sum weights for: an extreme RSI reversal, otherwise a less extreme RSI turn, otherwise RSI on the directional side of its midpoint and moving in direction d; candle direction d; close beyond the previous bar's directional high or low; lower-timeframe fast EMA on the d side of its slower EMA; relative volume >= its baseline; and RSI moving in direction d for two consecutive changes. An extreme reversal requires previous RSI beyond the extreme threshold, moving farther into the extreme on the previous bar, and turning in direction d now. A less extreme turn requires previous RSI between the extreme and the corresponding less extreme threshold and turning in direction d. A pulse requires score >= pulse threshold AND an extreme reversal, less extreme turn, or prior-bar breakout. A high-quality pulse additionally requires score >= the higher quality threshold.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe evidence in direction d = the inspector is disabled OR the current chart bar contains at least the required number of completed lower-timeframe pulses in direction d AND either the last pulse is in direction d or the best directional pulse score exceeds the best opposite-direction score. If both directions pulse on the same lower-timeframe bar, the short pulse is last. The inspector is active only in the scalping profile when enabled. Directional pulse gate passes outside the scalping profile, when the pulse requirement is disabled, or when directional lower-timeframe evidence exists.
Calculation: Continuation candidate in direction d = expansion in direction d AND directional bias AND a recent directional reaction or pullback AND directional micro breakout AND directional momentum score >= continuation threshold AND volume gate AND structural reward-to-risk >= minimum AND directional pulse gate. Reversal candidate in direction d = recent opposite-direction expansion AND no current opposite-direction expansion AND recent directional reaction AND d × close > d × fast EMA AND directional micro breakout AND directional momentum score >= reversal threshold AND volume gate AND structural reward-to-risk >= minimum AND directional pulse gate.
Calculation: Continuation score = capped sum of weights for directional expansion, otherwise directional trend in transition; directional bias; recent directional reaction, otherwise recent directional pullback; directional momentum score multiplied by its weight; directional micro breakout; strong volume; sufficient structural reward-to-risk; and lower-timeframe evidence. Reversal score = capped sum of weights for recent ended opposite expansion, recent directional reaction, weighted directional momentum score, directional micro breakout, close on the directional side of fast EMA, strong volume, sufficient structural reward-to-risk, and lower-timeframe evidence. For either score, the lower-timeframe contribution is zero when the inspector is disabled, the larger evidence weight when any high-quality directional pulse exists, otherwise the smaller evidence weight when directional evidence exists, otherwise zero. Transition requires neither directional expansion and no compression.
Calculation: Directional readiness = at least one enabled directional candidate AND the maximum score among enabled qualifying candidates >= entry-score threshold AND, when close confirmation is enabled, the chart bar is complete. Fresh readiness requires readiness now and not on the previous chart bar. The active trade is initially flat, becomes long or short at an execution entry, and becomes flat on its corresponding exit. Entry cooldown is measured from the most recent execution entry.
Calculation: Entry stop = the directional stop candidate at the execution entry. Initial risk = directional distance from entry price to entry stop, floored at a positive minimum price increment. Final target = entry price plus initial risk times the final target multiple for long, or minus that amount for short.

Short: Active trade is flat AND bars since the last execution entry > cooldown, or no earlier execution entry exists, AND short readiness is fresh AND (long readiness is not fresh OR short execution score > long execution score).

Exit: short, current bar is after the execution-entry bar AND (high >= entry stop OR low <= final target OR (at least two of close > fast EMA, RSI > midpoint, and EMA of close change > 0) AND short momentum score < exit threshold).
