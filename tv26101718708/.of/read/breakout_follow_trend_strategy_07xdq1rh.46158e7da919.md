Calculation: Stop distance = ATR on the entry signal bar multiplied by a stop-distance multiplier, fixed separately for each entry.
Long: close > upper Bollinger Band AND ATR > 0 AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (EMA overlap filter disabled OR EMA filter disabled OR low > EMA OR high < EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume SMA = 0 OR volume > volume SMA) AND (time filter disabled OR hour is in the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window, including an overnight window) AND (weekend filter disabled OR the bar is outside the configured Friday cutoff through Sunday and, when the time filter is enabled, outside Monday before the start hour).
Short: close < lower Bollinger Band AND ATR > 0 AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (EMA overlap filter disabled OR EMA filter disabled OR low > EMA OR high < EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume SMA = 0 OR volume > volume SMA) AND (time filter disabled OR hour is in the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window, including an overnight window) AND (weekend filter disabled OR the bar is outside the configured Friday cutoff through Sunday and, when the time filter is enabled, outside Monday before the start hour).
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance multiplied by the full take-profit multiplier OR weekend filter enabled AND the bar is at or after the Friday cutoff, on Saturday or Sunday, or on Monday before the start hour when the time filter is enabled.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance multiplied by the full take-profit multiplier OR weekend filter enabled AND the bar is at or after the Friday cutoff, on Saturday or Sunday, or on Monday before the start hour when the time filter is enabled.
Exit: long, partial take profit enabled AND partial take profit has not already occurred for this entry AND high >= entry price + stop distance multiplied by the partial take-profit multiplier; close the configured fraction of the entry.
Exit: short, partial take profit enabled AND partial take profit has not already occurred for this entry AND low <= entry price - stop distance multiplied by the partial take-profit multiplier; close the configured fraction of the entry.
