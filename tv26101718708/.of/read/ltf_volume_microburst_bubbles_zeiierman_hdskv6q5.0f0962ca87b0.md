Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume ratio = lower-timeframe volume divided by its EMA volume baseline; use a separate baseline per enabled session, or one baseline when session filtering is disabled.
Calculation: Directional efficiency = (lower-timeframe close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Microburst Score = For each active lower-timeframe bar with volume ratio at least the spike threshold and absolute directional efficiency at least the minimum efficiency, set energy to (0.25 + max(volume ratio − spike threshold, 0)) × absolute directional efficiency. Sum energy separately for positive and negative efficiency, and let total energy be their sum. Let dominance = (positive energy − negative energy) / total energy. Let concentration be 1 for one qualifying bar; otherwise clamp to [0,1] the value (sum of squared energies / total energy² − 1 / qualifying-bar count) / (1 − 1 / qualifying-bar count). Let participation = 1 − exp(−total energy / saturation parameter). Score = 100 × dominance × sqrt(participation × (0.35 + 0.65 × concentration)).
Long: At least one active lower-timeframe bar qualifies as a Microburst AND Microburst Score > 0
Short: At least one active lower-timeframe bar qualifies as a Microburst AND Microburst Score < 0
