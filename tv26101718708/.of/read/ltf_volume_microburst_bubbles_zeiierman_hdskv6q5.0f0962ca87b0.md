Calculation: Lower-timeframe spike energy = For each lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar, including a bar still forming, replace missing or negative volume with zero. If any sessions are enabled, the bar is active when its open or close is inside an enabled session, using that session’s configured time zone; otherwise every bar is active. Maintain a separate volume EMA for each enabled session, updating it only on active bars in that session and including the current bar before calculating its ratio. With no sessions enabled, maintain one EMA over all lower-timeframe bars. Initialize each EMA with its first volume. The volume ratio is the maximum of current volume divided by each positive EMA for an active session, or volume divided by the positive all-bars EMA when sessions are disabled; otherwise it is zero. Body efficiency is (close - open) / (high - low), or zero when the range is zero. An active bar qualifies when its volume ratio >= the spike threshold AND absolute body efficiency >= the minimum body efficiency. Its energy is (0.25 + max(volume ratio - spike threshold, 0)) × absolute body efficiency; otherwise its energy is zero.
Calculation: Bullish energy = Sum of spike energy for lower-timeframe bars in the current chart bar whose body efficiency > 0.
Calculation: Bearish energy = Sum of spike energy for lower-timeframe bars in the current chart bar whose body efficiency < 0.
Long: Bullish energy > bearish energy.
Short: Bearish energy > bullish energy.
