Calculation: Donchian midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over its lookback.
Calculation: Chandelier state = calculate long stop as highest high minus an ATR multiple and short stop as lowest low plus an ATR multiple. If the previous close was above the previous long stop, the long stop is the greater of its new value and previous value; otherwise use its new value. If the previous close was below the previous short stop, the short stop is the lesser of its new value and previous value; otherwise use its new value. State is long when close exceeds the previous short stop, short when close is below the previous long stop, and otherwise retains its previous state; initialize long.
Calculation: Ichimoku lines = short-, medium-, and long-lookback Donchian midpoints; leading A is the average of the short and medium lines, leading B is the long line, and displaced cloud top is the greater of the earlier leading A and leading B at the displacement lookback.
Calculation: Long filter = AND of enabled conditions: close > open; volume > previous volume; close SMA > every SMA value in the preceding lookback; close − long entry stop < close × maximum risk fraction; close < previous close × (1 + maximum change fraction); ((leading A > leading B AND displaced leading A > displaced leading B AND close > displaced cloud top) OR (short Ichimoku line > medium line AND medium line > displaced cloud top AND short line > displaced cloud top AND close > displaced cloud top AND both lines > their previous values)); RSI <= upper threshold; Chandelier state = long; (short EMA > long EMA AND absolute difference between the EMAs >= ATR × multiplier).
Calculation: Short filter = AND of enabled conditions: close < open; volume > previous volume; close SMA < every SMA value in the preceding lookback; short entry stop − close < close × maximum risk fraction; close > previous close × (1 − maximum change fraction); ((leading A < leading B AND displaced leading A < displaced leading B AND close < displaced cloud top) OR (short Ichimoku line < medium line AND medium line < displaced cloud top AND short line < displaced cloud top AND close < displaced cloud top AND both lines < their previous values)); RSI >= lower threshold; Chandelier state = short; (short EMA < long EMA AND absolute difference between the EMAs >= ATR × multiplier).
Calculation: Entry stop = for a long, the selected one of min(open, close) − ATR × multiplier, min(open, close) × (1 − fraction), or max(min(open, close) − fixed distance, 0); for a short, the selected one of max(open, close) + ATR × multiplier, max(open, close) × (1 + fraction), or max(open, close) + fixed distance. Set the trade's stop using the entry bar and retain it; risk distance is the absolute difference between entry price and this stop.
Calculation: Volatility stop = maintain a running maximum and minimum close since the last trend change. In the previous uptrend, candidate = running maximum − ATR × multiplier and stop = max(previous stop, candidate); in the previous downtrend, candidate = running minimum + ATR × multiplier and stop = min(previous stop, candidate). The new trend is up if close >= stop, otherwise down. On a trend change reset both extremes to close and reset the stop to close minus the ATR multiple for an uptrend or close plus it for a downtrend. Initialize the previous trend as up and missing previous extremes and stop as zero.
Calculation: Trade stop = initialize to the entry stop on the entry bar. Subsequently select a candidate: most favorable high since entry minus a risk-distance multiple for longs or most favorable low plus that multiple for shorts; the same favorable extreme reduced by a percentage for longs or increased by it for shorts; that extreme minus a fixed distance for longs or plus it for shorts; Donchian midpoint; the average of the previous two bars' min(open, close) minus an ATR multiple for longs or their max(open, close) plus it for shorts; the corresponding Chandelier stop; the volatility stop when its trend matches the position side, otherwise the previous trade stop; or previous low for longs and previous high for shorts. Reject a long candidate above the current high or a short candidate below the current low. Replace the prior stop only if the candidate improves it and strictly passes the selected activation level: entry stop, entry price plus or minus a risk-distance multiple, entry price adjusted by a percentage, or entry price plus or minus a fixed distance, with the latter three also required to pass the entry stop in the favorable direction. Never move the stop against the position and floor it at zero.
Long: Long filter = true.
Short: Short filter = true.
Long: RSI crosses above a lower threshold AND long filter = true.
Short: RSI crosses below an upper threshold AND short filter = true.
Long: Shorter-lookback Donchian midpoint crosses above longer-lookback Donchian midpoint AND long filter = true.
Short: Shorter-lookback Donchian midpoint crosses below longer-lookback Donchian midpoint AND short filter = true.
Exit: long, short filter changes from false to true.
Exit: short, long filter changes from false to true.
Exit: long, RSI crosses below an upper threshold.
Exit: short, RSI crosses above a lower threshold.
Exit: long, shorter-lookback Donchian midpoint crosses below longer-lookback Donchian midpoint.
Exit: short, shorter-lookback Donchian midpoint crosses above longer-lookback Donchian midpoint.
Exit: long, close > entry price + risk distance × profit multiplier.
Exit: short, close < entry price − risk distance × profit multiplier.
Exit: long, close < trade stop on the entry bar OR close < previous bar's trade stop on subsequent bars.
Exit: short, close > trade stop on the entry bar OR close > previous bar's trade stop on subsequent bars.
