Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side satisfying the pivot comparison; report it on the confirmation bar. Use a shorter lookback for internal pivots and a longer lookback for swing pivots. If multiple structure pivots confirm on the same bar, process only the first in this order: internal high, internal low, swing low, swing high. Optionally adjust the internal lookback using the z-score of its lookback-period close return: when its absolute value reaches the first configured threshold, set the lookback to the corresponding configured length; otherwise retain the previous length.
Calculation: Structure break = for each pivot scale, retain all confirmed pivot highs and lows, and separately retain the latest unbroken high and low. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest unbroken pivot high and at least two pivot lows have been confirmed; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest unbroken pivot low and at least two pivot highs have been confirmed. After a break, clear the unbroken pivots on the broken side, retain the historical pivot values, and set that scale's trend to the break direction. Initialize trend as neutral. A break against the previous trend is a change of character; otherwise it is a break of structure. A bullish change of character is the enhanced type if the latest pivot low exceeds the preceding pivot low; a bearish change of character is the enhanced type if the latest pivot high is below the preceding pivot high.
Calculation: Zone pivots = confirmed internal pivot highs and lows in chronological order, including both if they confirm together; clear the sequence when its latest two pivots have the same type, and clear it after a zone is detected.
Calculation: Mapped gap bars = OHLC from the configured gap timeframe, with the prior values obtained by indexing the mapped series on chart bars. For a higher timeframe, historical mapping uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar and realtime mapping uses the bar still forming; for a lower timeframe, historical mapping uses the last lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. Evaluate gap comparisons only when the configured timeframe changes, and report them on the next chart bar.
Long: A bullish internal or swing structure break occurs.
Short: A bearish internal or swing structure break occurs.
Long: Internal structure trend on a configured higher timeframe is bullish, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the bar still forming in realtime; OR internal structure trend on a configured lower timeframe is bullish, using the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Internal structure trend on a configured higher timeframe is bearish, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the bar still forming in realtime; OR internal structure trend on a configured lower timeframe is bearish, using the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: The latest alternating zone pivots end in a low AND their successive lows rise AND their successive highs fall, using either the shorter or longer configured zone pattern.
Short: The latest alternating zone pivots end in a high AND their successive highs fall AND their successive lows rise, using either the shorter or longer configured zone pattern.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its absolute difference from the preceding pivot low is less than ATR multiplied by a configured fraction. Use the equal-level pivot lookback, shorter than the internal structure lookback; if a pivot high and low confirm together, process only the high.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its absolute difference from the preceding pivot high is less than ATR multiplied by a configured fraction. Use the equal-level pivot lookback, shorter than the internal structure lookback; if a pivot high and low confirm together, process only the high.
Long: The mapped gap bar's low is greater than the high two gap bars earlier; OR its open is greater than the previous mapped close AND the previous mapped high is greater than its low AND its close is greater than the previous mapped close AND its open is greater than the previous mapped open AND the previous mapped high is below both its open and close; OR its low is greater than the previous mapped high.
Short: The mapped gap bar's high is less than the low two gap bars earlier; OR its open is less than the previous mapped close AND the previous mapped low is less than its high AND its close is less than the previous mapped close AND its open is less than the previous mapped open AND the previous mapped low is above both its open and close; OR its high is less than the previous mapped low.
