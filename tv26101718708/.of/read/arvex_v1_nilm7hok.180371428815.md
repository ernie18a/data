Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close − open
Calculation: Candle range = high − low
Calculation: Doji = candle body ≤ candle range × 0.10
Calculation: Hammer or hanging man = candle body ≤ candle range × 0.30 AND (close − low ≥ candle body × 2 OR high − close ≥ candle body × 2)
Long: previous close > previous open AND close < open AND volume > previous volume AND low ≥ previous low AND NOT Doji AND NOT Hammer or hanging man
Short: previous close < previous open AND close > open AND volume > previous volume AND high ≤ previous high AND NOT Doji AND NOT Hammer or hanging man
