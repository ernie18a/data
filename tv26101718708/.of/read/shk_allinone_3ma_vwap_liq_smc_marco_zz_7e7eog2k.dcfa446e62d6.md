Calculation: Signal score = sum of the enabled filter votes: Heikin-Ashi close above or below Heikin-Ashi open gives +1 or -1; bullish or bearish Supertrend gives +1 or -1; Heikin-Ashi close above or below the ATR trailing stop gives +1 or -1; equality gives 0. The ATR trailing stop starts at 0. Let source be Heikin-Ashi close, loss be ATR multiplied by a factor, and previous stop be the previous stop or 0 if unavailable. If current and previous source are both above the previous stop, the stop is max(previous stop, source - loss); if both are below it, the stop is min(previous stop, source + loss); otherwise it is source - loss when source is above the previous stop and source + loss otherwise.
Calculation: Structure pivots = for each of two lookbacks, one shorter than the other, maintain a leg initially bearish. A high from lookback bars ago greater than every high since then changes the leg to bearish; otherwise a low from lookback bars ago lower than every low since then changes it to bullish. On a leg change, record the corresponding high or low from lookback bars ago as the latest pivot and mark it unbroken. Track each lookback separately.
Calculation: Reversal pivots = a high or low from depth bars ago qualifies when no subsequent high is greater or no subsequent low is lower, respectively; prefer the high if both qualify. Keep the last candidate price and side, initially 0 and low. A candidate on the same side replaces the stored price only if it is more extreme. A candidate on the opposite side is accepted and replaces the stored price and side when the stored price is 0 or abs(100 * (candidate price - stored price) / candidate price) > ATR / close * 100 * deviation factor. Evaluate acceptance on the current bar.
Calculation: ZigZag pivots = run separately for a longer and a shorter lookback and backstep. A high candidate has high >= highest high over the lookback including the current bar; a low candidate has low <= lowest low over that lookback. Initially select a high candidate in preference to a low candidate and start an up leg or down leg respectively. During an up leg, update the current high on a high candidate if high > current high or bars since the last leg change < backstep; otherwise change to a down leg on a low candidate when bars since the last leg change >= backstep, saving the completed high. During a down leg, apply the symmetric low update and high reversal rules, saving the completed low. The initial last leg-change bar is the first bar. Each initialization, update, or reversal produces a pivot on the current bar. Compare high pivots with the last completed high and low pivots with the last completed low; when no prior completed high or low exists, classify the high as higher and the low as lower.
