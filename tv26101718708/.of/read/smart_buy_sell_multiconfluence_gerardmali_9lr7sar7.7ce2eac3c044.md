Calculation: Smoothed stochastic K = SMA of the stochastic calculated from close, high, and low; stochastic D = SMA of smoothed stochastic K.
Calculation: Bullish bonus = sum of double weight for fast EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > slow EMA, and single weight each for close > trend EMA, RSI > its bonus threshold AND RSI > RSI at the slope lookback, stochastic K > stochastic D AND stochastic K at the longer stochastic lookback < its low-zone threshold, and positive DI > negative DI. Each unmet condition contributes zero.
Calculation: Bearish bonus = sum of double weight for fast EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < slow EMA, and single weight each for close < trend EMA, RSI < its bonus threshold AND RSI < RSI at the slope lookback, stochastic K < stochastic D AND stochastic K at the longer stochastic lookback > its high-zone threshold, and negative DI > positive DI. Each unmet condition contributes zero.
Long: ADX > minimum ADX AND fast EMA > fast EMA at the slope lookback AND medium EMA > medium EMA at the slope lookback AND RSI > lower long RSI threshold AND RSI < upper long RSI threshold AND MACD histogram > its previous value AND MACD histogram > its value at the slope lookback AND close >= low + (high - low) * close-location fraction AND (close <= trend EMA OR absolute percentage distance from trend EMA <= overextension threshold) AND (close <= close at the divergence lookback OR RSI >= RSI at the divergence lookback OR RSI <= bearish-divergence threshold) AND bullish bonus >= minimum bonus AND ((MACD line crosses above its signal line AND previous MACD histogram < 0) OR (stochastic K crosses above stochastic D AND previous stochastic K < long trigger threshold) OR (RSI crosses above its crossing threshold AND RSI at the slope lookback < long trigger threshold) OR (fast EMA crosses above medium EMA AND medium EMA > medium EMA at the slope lookback AND ADX > EMA-cross ADX threshold)) AND bars since the previous occurrence of this long condition > cooldown, or no previous occurrence exists.
Short: ADX > minimum ADX AND fast EMA < fast EMA at the slope lookback AND medium EMA < medium EMA at the slope lookback AND RSI < upper short RSI threshold AND RSI > lower short RSI threshold AND MACD histogram < its previous value AND MACD histogram < its value at the slope lookback AND close <= high - (high - low) * close-location fraction AND (close >= trend EMA OR absolute percentage distance from trend EMA <= overextension threshold) AND (close >= close at the divergence lookback OR RSI <= RSI at the divergence lookback OR RSI >= bullish-divergence threshold) AND bearish bonus >= minimum bonus AND ((MACD line crosses below its signal line AND previous MACD histogram > 0) OR (stochastic K crosses below stochastic D AND previous stochastic K > short trigger threshold) OR (RSI crosses below its crossing threshold AND RSI at the slope lookback > short trigger threshold) OR (fast EMA crosses below medium EMA AND medium EMA < medium EMA at the slope lookback AND ADX > EMA-cross ADX threshold)) AND bars since the previous occurrence of this short condition > cooldown, or no previous occurrence exists.
Long: fast EMA > fast EMA at the slope lookback.
Short: fast EMA < fast EMA at the slope lookback.
Calculation: Fast, medium, slow, and trend EMAs use progressively longer lengths; the divergence lookback is longer than the slope lookback, and the longer stochastic lookback exceeds one bar.
