Calculation: Normalized volatility = standard deviation of (close − lowest low over the normalization lookback) / (highest high over the normalization lookback − lowest low over the normalization lookback), using a separate volatility lookback.
Calculation: Volatility recency lines = for the highest and lowest normalized volatility separately, (detection lookback − bars since the most recent corresponding extreme within the current bar and the preceding detection-lookback bars) / detection lookback.
Calculation: Channel duration = max(1, bars since the lowest-volatility recency line last crossed above the highest-volatility recency line); use 1 before the first such cross.
Calculation: Active channels = when the highest-volatility recency line crosses above the lowest-volatility recency line and channel duration exceeds a minimum, create a channel with top equal to the highest high and bottom equal to the lowest low over the channel duration, including the current bar. If nested channels are disabled, create it only if its price interval does not overlap any active channel. Insert new channels first. On each bar, process active channels from newest to oldest, advancing the index after each channel even if it is removed; remove a processed channel when the breakout price is above its top or below its bottom. Process newly created channels on their creation bar.
Calculation: Breakout price = (open + close) / 2 when strong closes are enabled; otherwise close.
Long: Breakout price > the top of a processed active channel.
Short: Breakout price < the bottom of a processed active channel.
