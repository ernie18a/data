Calculation: Basis = selected SMA, EMA, HMA, WMA, VWMA, or RMA of the selected OHLC-derived source. Upper band = basis + band-width parameter × ATR; lower band = basis − band-width parameter × ATR.
Calculation: Up burst = high > upper band AND (closing-burst filter disabled OR close > upper band). Down burst = low < lower band AND (closing-burst filter disabled OR close < lower band). Up magnitude = (high − upper band) / ATR when up burst, otherwise 0; down magnitude = (lower band − low) / ATR when down burst, otherwise 0. Stack = min(maximum stack, 1 + floor(magnitude / ATR-per-stack parameter)).
Calculation: A directional peak qualifies when its previous-bar magnitude > 0, >= its magnitude two bars ago, and > its current magnitude; its previous-bar stack >= minimum stack and previous-bar magnitude >= minimum magnitude. If enabled, require the previous-bar rejection wick / range >= its threshold and previous-bar RSI >= the overbought threshold for an up peak or <= the oversold threshold for a down peak. Up rejection wick = high − max(open, close); down rejection wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Setup score uses the qualifying peak's previous-bar magnitude, stack, RSI and rejection, and the current bar's close, basis and ATR. Clamp each component to [0,1]: extension = magnitude / extension-saturation parameter; stack = stack / maximum stack; exhaustion = (50 − RSI) / 30 for long or (RSI − 50) / 30 for short; rejection = rejection-wick share / 0.6; trend = (direction × (basis − basis three bars ago) / ATR + 1) / 2, with direction +1 for long and −1 for short; calm = 1 − ATR percent rank over its lookback / 100; room = abs(close − basis) / (1.5 × band-width parameter × ATR). Score = 10 × weighted average of the seven components, or 0 if total weight is 0. Tier = 4 if score >= 7.5, 3 if >= 6.5, 2 if >= 5.5, 1 if >= 4.5, otherwise 0.
Calculation: At entry close, fix risk as min(max(abs(entry − raw stop), ATR × minimum-risk parameter), ATR × maximum-risk parameter). Raw stop is entry minus or plus an ATR multiple, the lowest low or highest high over the swing lookback minus or plus an ATR buffer, or the lower low or higher high of the entry and previous bars minus or plus that buffer, respectively for long or short. Stop = entry − risk for long and entry + risk for short. Third target = entry plus or minus risk × third-target multiple for long or short; in mean-reversion mode, if the entry-bar basis is above a long entry or below a short entry, third target = basis plus or minus half the ATR band width instead. Keep stop and target fixed after entry.
Long: Up burst = false AND down burst = true.
Short: Up burst = true.
Long: On either selected higher timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has up burst = false AND down burst = true.
Short: On either selected higher timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has up burst = true.
Long: Down peak qualifies AND long side enabled AND (up peak does not qualify OR down peak magnitude > up peak magnitude) AND setup tier >= minimum tier AND no trade is active AND no trade exited on this bar AND bars since the last entry >= minimum spacing AND ATR is available.
Short: Up peak qualifies AND short side enabled AND (down peak does not qualify OR up peak magnitude >= down peak magnitude) AND setup tier >= minimum tier AND no trade is active AND no trade exited on this bar AND bars since the last entry >= minimum spacing AND ATR is available.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed third target OR bars since entry >= time-stop parameter.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed third target OR bars since entry >= time-stop parameter.
