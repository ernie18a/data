Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = independently selected SMA, EMA, SMMA, HMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, ZLEMA, ALMA, KAMA, or DEMA of close, with shorter and longer lookbacks respectively; HMA rounds the half-length and square-root lengths to integers.
Calculation: KAMA = initialize with the SMA of close; subsequently add the squared smoothing constant times (close minus previous KAMA). The smoothing constant is the efficiency ratio times (fast EMA smoothing constant minus slow EMA smoothing constant) plus the slow EMA smoothing constant. The efficiency ratio is absolute(close minus close lookback bars ago) divided by the sum over offsets from zero through lookback minus one of absolute(current close minus close at that offset), or zero when that sum is zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend averages = short- and long-lookback SMAs of higher-timeframe close, using the same respective lookbacks as the chart moving averages; on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, made available on its final constituent chart bar; on a realtime bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Trailing distance = ATR multiplied by the trailing multiplier, interpreted as a number of minimum price ticks and converted to price units.
Long: Position is flat AND close > fast moving average AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND close > slow moving average + ATR multiplied by the entry multiplier AND (trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe short-lookback SMA >= higher-timeframe long-lookback SMA) AND volume >= minimum volume AND volume > volume SMA multiplied by the volume multiplier AND bar opening hour >= start hour AND bar opening hour <= end hour AND ATR / close <= volatility threshold, substituting zero when the ratio is unavailable.
Short: Position is flat AND close < fast moving average AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND close < slow moving average - ATR multiplied by the entry multiplier AND (trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe short-lookback SMA <= higher-timeframe long-lookback SMA) AND volume >= minimum volume AND volume > volume SMA multiplied by the volume multiplier AND bar opening hour >= start hour AND bar opening hour <= end hour AND ATR / close <= volatility threshold, substituting zero when the ratio is unavailable.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR multiplied by the target multiplier OR (high since entry >= entry price + trailing distance AND low <= highest price since trailing activation - trailing distance).
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR multiplied by the target multiplier OR (low since entry <= entry price - trailing distance AND high >= lowest price since trailing activation + trailing distance).
