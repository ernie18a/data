Calculation: Robust channel = over the close lookback, ordered oldest to newest, slope is the median of all pairwise close differences divided by their bar-index differences; intercept is the median of close minus slope times index; midline is intercept plus slope times the latest index; deviation is the median absolute residual from that line. Upper and lower rails are midline plus and minus a rail-width multiplier times deviation; upper and lower break levels use a separate break-width multiplier.
Calculation: Tail stop distance = calculate absolute one-bar log returns over the return window and their threshold percentile. Fit a generalized Pareto distribution by method of moments to returns strictly above that threshold, with shape clamped to stability bounds. Convert the selected tail quantile to price distance by multiplying by close and the square root of the holding horizon. If there are too few exceedances, their mean or variance is nonpositive, or the estimated quantile is unavailable or nonpositive, use an ATR multiple instead.
Calculation: Volume profile = divide the range from the window's lowest low to highest high into equal bins; assign each bar's positive volume to the bin containing its typical price, clamped to the range. The point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest bin on a tie. Starting there, add the adjacent bin with greater volume, choosing the upper bin on a tie, until the selected fraction of total volume is included. The value-area low and high are the outer edges of the included bins. The profile is unavailable if disabled, its price range is zero, or total positive volume is zero.
Calculation: Long stop distance = maximum of tail stop distance, zero, close minus lower rail, and close minus value-area low when the profile and its stop anchor are enabled, the value-area low is below close, and that difference does not exceed a multiple of tail stop distance; otherwise the value-area contribution is zero. Short stop distance is the maximum of tail stop distance, zero, upper rail minus close, and value-area high minus close under the corresponding enabled, above-close, and distance-cap conditions.
Calculation: Long stop = close minus long stop distance; short stop = close plus short stop distance. In R-multiple target mode, long targets are close plus their respective target multipliers times long stop distance, and short targets are close minus their respective target multipliers times short stop distance. In volume-profile target mode, when the profile is available and value-area high is above close, long target 1 is the point of control if above close and otherwise value-area high, and long target 2 is value-area high; when value-area low is below close, short target 1 is the point of control if below close and otherwise value-area low, and short target 2 is value-area low. Fall back to R-multiple targets when the relevant profile condition is unmet.
Calculation: Active stop, targets, and break level = the long stop, long targets, and lower break level when robust channel slope is positive; otherwise the short stop, short targets, and upper break level. Recalculate these levels on every bar; previous levels are those selected on the previous bar using its own slope.
Long: Robust channel slope > 0
Short: Robust channel slope <= 0
Exit: Long, robust channel slope > 0 AND ((low <= active stop AND previous low > previous active stop) OR (high >= active target 1 AND previous high < previous active target 1) OR (high >= active target 2 AND previous high < previous active target 2) OR (low <= active break level AND previous low > previous active break level))
Exit: Short, robust channel slope <= 0 AND ((high >= active stop AND previous high < previous active stop) OR (low <= active target 1 AND previous low > previous active target 1) OR (low <= active target 2 AND previous low > previous active target 2) OR (high >= active break level AND previous high < previous active break level))
