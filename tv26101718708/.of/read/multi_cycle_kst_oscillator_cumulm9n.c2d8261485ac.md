Calculation: KST = the sum of four SMAs of percentage rate of change of a selected OHLC-derived price source, weighted respectively 1, 2, 3 and 4; use separate ROC and SMA lengths for each component and short-, intermediate- and long-term parameter sets.
Calculation: Oscillator and signal = short-term KST and its SMA, respectively, either raw or both standardized using the short-term KST's rolling mean and standard deviation, or both transformed as 0.6745 × (value − rolling median) / rolling median of absolute deviations. Each absolute deviation uses the rolling median at its own bar. Return zero when the selected denominator is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Divergences = compare each newly confirmed oscillator pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same type, provided their pivot bars are no more than a configurable maximum apart. Sample the selected OHLC-derived low or high source at each oscillator pivot bar. A bullish regular divergence has a higher oscillator low and lower price low; bullish hidden has a lower oscillator low and higher price low. A bearish regular divergence has a lower oscillator high and higher price high; bearish hidden has a higher oscillator high and lower price high. Evaluate on the confirmation bar; regular and hidden detection are independently configurable.
Calculation: Score = 100 × weighted sum of four votes divided by the greater of 1 and the sum of their nonnegative weights. The oscillator vote is +1 when oscillator > signal, otherwise −1; the long-term vote is +1 when long-term KST > its previous value, −1 when lower, otherwise 0; the price vote is +1 when close > a configurable EMA or SMA of close, otherwise −1; the divergence vote is +1 for a bullish divergence, otherwise −1 for a bearish divergence, otherwise 0. Divergence detection may be disabled.
Calculation: Confidence = min(100, abs(score) × (chop multiplier + efficiency ratio × (trend multiplier − chop multiplier))); efficiency ratio = abs(close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over N bars, or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = sign of short-term KST minus its signal SMA calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; substitute zero when unavailable.
Long: Oscillator crosses above signal AND long-term KST > its previous value AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction >= 0) AND confidence >= threshold AND bar index >= warm-up length, where warm-up length is the statistical window in standardized modes and the maximum of the fourth short-term ROC and SMA lengths plus the signal SMA length in raw mode.
Short: Oscillator crosses below signal AND long-term KST < its previous value AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction <= 0) AND confidence >= threshold AND bar index >= warm-up length, defined as for the long signal.
Long: Bullish regular or hidden divergence.
Short: Bearish regular or hidden divergence.
Long: Score >= 0.
Short: Score < 0.
Long: Short-term KST > 0 AND intermediate-term KST > 0 AND long-term KST > 0.
Short: Short-term KST < 0 AND intermediate-term KST < 0 AND long-term KST < 0.
