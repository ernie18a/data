Calculation: Liquidity level = a pivot high or low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side. Its age starts at the pivot bar. A level is active until its age exceeds the expiry lookback, its permitted number of qualifying grabs is reached, a close breaks beyond it, or it is removed as the oldest level when the level storage limit is exceeded.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe order block = on the higher timeframe’s still-forming bar, a close crossing above the highest high of a prior-bar lookback selects the most recent bearish candle within a separate lookback; a close crossing below the lowest low selects the most recent bullish candle. Store the selected candle’s high and low separately for bullish and bearish shifts until replaced. The source uses lookahead and therefore exposes the completed higher-timeframe bar’s values historically.
Calculation: Bullish grab = low below an active pivot-low level AND close at or above it AND, if the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, low within the latest bullish order block. Bearish grab = high above an active pivot-high level AND close at or below it AND, if that filter is enabled, high within the latest bearish order block.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = close above the grab bar’s high within the confirmation lookback after a bullish grab. Bearish confirmation = close below the grab bar’s low within the confirmation lookback after a bearish grab. Each grab is consumed by its first confirmation; confirmation is checked on the expiry bar before expiry.
Calculation: Breaker zone = the high and low of the candle immediately before the grab, provided breaker zones are enabled and that candle is bullish for a bullish setup or bearish for a bearish setup. Bullish gap zone = high two bars earlier to current low when current low is higher; bearish gap zone = current high to low two bars earlier when current high is lower. Gap zones are created only when enabled, from bars after the grab through the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Golden setup = a breaker zone exists AND, on a bar after the grab through confirmation, a bullish gap has its current low above the breaker low and its earlier high below the breaker high, or a bearish gap has its current high below the breaker high and its earlier low above the breaker low.
Calculation: Delayed continuation = a zone created at confirmation has waited at least the specified number of bars; its first subsequent touch is low at or below the zone top for a bullish zone or high at or above the zone bottom for a bearish zone. At that touch, freeze the highest high for a bullish zone or lowest low for a bearish zone from the confirmation bar through the touch bar. Continuation is a close beyond that frozen extreme in the setup direction. Zones remain eligible after touch and have no implemented expiry.
Long: Bullish confirmation AND optional golden-only mode is disabled.
Short: Bearish confirmation AND optional golden-only mode is disabled.
Long: Optional golden-only mode is enabled AND a bullish golden setup was confirmed AND delayed continuation occurs for any zone created by that confirmation.
Short: Optional golden-only mode is enabled AND a bearish golden setup was confirmed AND delayed continuation occurs for any zone created by that confirmation.
