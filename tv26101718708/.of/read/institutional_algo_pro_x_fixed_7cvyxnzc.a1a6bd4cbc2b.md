Calculation: Swing high/low = the high/low of a bar that equals the highest/lowest value in a centered window with equal lookbacks on both sides; confirm it only after the right-side bars have closed. Use the most recently confirmed level, updating it before testing the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest swing high/below the latest swing low, respectively, and that level differs from the last level previously broken in the same direction. Track the last broken high and low independently. A crossover requires the previous close to be at or below the previous bar's level; a crossunder requires it to be at or above the previous bar's level.
Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low accumulated during the configured opening session in its configured timezone; reset at each new trading day and lock after the session ends.
Calculation: Signed volume = volume multiplied by the sign of close minus open. Cumulative signed volume resets to the current signed volume on the first bar of each new trading day and otherwise adds the current signed volume.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR at entry multiplied by the stop-distance multiplier. The entry price is the entry bar's close.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus entry risk; if the optional break-even adjustment is enabled, replace it on the first bar whose high reaches the first profit target with entry price plus that bar's ATR multiplied by the break-even buffer, and retain that stop thereafter. Short stop = entry price plus entry risk; on the first bar whose low reaches the first profit target, optionally replace it with entry price minus that bar's ATR multiplied by the break-even buffer, and retain it thereafter. The first and final profit targets are entry price plus entry risk multiplied by their respective reward-to-risk multipliers for a long, and entry price minus those amounts for a short; the final target uses the larger multiplier.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Bullish structure break AND at least one earlier bearish candle exists within the order-block lookback.
Short: Bearish structure break AND at least one earlier bullish candle exists within the order-block lookback.
Long: (Close crosses above the locked opening-range high OR bullish structure break) AND short-period EMA of close > medium-period EMA of close AND medium-period EMA of close > long-period EMA of close AND RSI of close > bullish threshold AND EMA of signed volume > minimum delta AND cumulative signed volume > its previous-bar value AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA of close). The higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime.
Short: (Close crosses below the locked opening-range low OR bearish structure break) AND short-period EMA of close < medium-period EMA of close AND medium-period EMA of close < long-period EMA of close AND RSI of close < bearish threshold AND EMA of signed volume < negative minimum delta AND cumulative signed volume < its previous-bar value AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA of close). The higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime.
Exit: long, high >= final profit target OR low <= long stop after applying any first-target stop adjustment on the current bar.
Exit: short, low <= final profit target OR high >= short stop after applying any first-target stop adjustment on the current bar.
