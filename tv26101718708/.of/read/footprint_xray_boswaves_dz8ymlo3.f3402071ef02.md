Calculation: Price rows = equal-height subdivisions of the current chart bar’s high-low range, sized from chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Row buy/sell volume = allocate each lower-timeframe bar’s volume to each overlapping price row in proportion to range overlap; assign it to buy volume when close > open, sell volume when close < open, and split it equally when close = open. Use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including any still-forming bar.
Calculation: Row delta ratio = (row buy volume − row sell volume) / (row buy volume + row sell volume).
Calculation: Bar delta = change in cumulative volume delta from the prior chart bar, calculated using lower-timeframe data, including any still-forming lower-timeframe bar.
Long: There are at least the required number of consecutive price rows with row delta ratio above the bullish dominance threshold OR close < open AND buy volume / total volume across the bottom extreme rows is at least the absorption threshold OR the current low is below the prior lookback lows AND bar delta > 0 OR the top row’s buy volume / total volume is at least the unfinished-business threshold OR bar delta > 0 AND prior bar delta <= 0.
Short: There are at least the required number of consecutive price rows with row delta ratio below the negative bearish dominance threshold OR close > open AND sell volume / total volume across the top extreme rows is at least the absorption threshold OR the current high is above the prior lookback highs AND bar delta < 0 OR the bottom row’s sell volume / total volume is at least the unfinished-business threshold OR bar delta < 0 AND prior bar delta >= 0.
