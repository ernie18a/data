Calculation: Lower-timeframe delta and pressure = sum of estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume across lower-timeframe (below chart timeframe) OHLCV bars inside the current chart bar, including any still-forming bar; estimate buy volume as volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), sell volume as volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), and pressure as delta / (buy volume + sell volume) × 100, or 0 when total volume is 0
Calculation: Block volume, delta, and pressure = the current chart bar’s lower-timeframe volume, delta, and pressure, sampled every fixed number of chart bars and held between samples
Calculation: Relative volume = block volume / moving average of block volume
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of lower-timeframe delta
Calculation: Average true range = average true range over a fixed lookback
Calculation: Last big-buy high = high of the most recent bar meeting the big-buy condition
Calculation: Last big-sell low = low of the most recent bar meeting the big-sell condition
