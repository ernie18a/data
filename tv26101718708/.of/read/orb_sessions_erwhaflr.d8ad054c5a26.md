Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled session, the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in its configured opening window; midpoint = (high + low) / 2. Reset the range and session entry state at the start of the trade window or a new local calendar day within it.
Calculation: Initial stop = opening-range midpoint, opposite boundary, or entry price minus a fixed distance for a long and plus that distance for a short, according to the selected method. Risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus risk times each configured target multiple for a long, or minus it for a short. Optionally multiply each target multiple by the ratio of the mean of recent positive completed opening-range widths for the same session, including the current width, to the current width, clamped to configured minimum and maximum factors; use a factor of one if either width or mean is nonpositive. Targets are fixed at entry.
Calculation: Stop = initial stop; when staircase trailing is enabled and the position remains open, a completed chart bar reaching the first, second, or third target moves the stop respectively to entry price, first target, or second target, choosing the third before the second before the first if several are reached; never move the stop away from profit. The updated stop applies to subsequent bars.
Long: An enabled session permits longs AND the local weekday is allowed AND the bar is outside the flat window AND the session has not just ended AND its entry state is armed AND ((breakout mode is selected AND the opening range is complete AND the bar is in the trade window AND close > opening-range high + entry buffer AND previous close <= opening-range high + entry buffer) OR (a pending upward breakout exists AND a later bar has low <= the selected retest level AND that bar or a subsequent bar closes > opening-range high + entry buffer before cancellation)). For retests, the selected level is the opening-range high or midpoint; start the pending breakout on the same upward crossover used in breakout mode, cancel it after the configured timeout or when close <= opening-range high for a boundary retest or close < opening-range low for a midpoint retest, and evaluate a successful retest before cancellation on each bar. If re-entry direction is restricted, the long must match the configured relationship to the last trade direction.
Short: An enabled session permits shorts AND the local weekday is allowed AND the bar is outside the flat window AND the session has not just ended AND its entry state is armed AND ((breakout mode is selected AND the opening range is complete AND the bar is in the trade window AND close < opening-range low - entry buffer AND previous close >= opening-range low - entry buffer) OR (a pending downward breakout exists AND a later bar has high >= the selected retest level AND that bar or a subsequent bar closes < opening-range low - entry buffer before cancellation)). For retests, the selected level is the opening-range low or midpoint; start the pending breakout on the same downward crossover used in breakout mode, cancel it after the configured timeout or when close >= opening-range low for a boundary retest or close > opening-range high for a midpoint retest, and evaluate a successful retest before cancellation on each bar. If re-entry direction is restricted, the short must match the configured relationship to the last trade direction.
Calculation: Entry state = armed at session reset and disarmed by an entry. If re-entry is enabled, re-arm after the position is flat and the session's tracked trade has ended, while the trade window remains active and the session entry count is below its limit, provided the tracked exit type matches the configured stop or non-stop filter and the configured cooldown has elapsed. If the configured waiting-bar count is positive, first require the selected gate: close inside the opening range after applying the entry buffer to both boundaries, low <= midpoint AND high >= midpoint, low <= opening-range low OR high >= opening-range high, or no price gate; then count that bar and every subsequent bar until the waiting count is reached. If the count is zero, re-arm after cooldown without a gate. A new entry still requires a fresh crossover; re-arming itself does not create one.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR risk > 0 AND high >= an active profit target, exiting the quantity allocated to that target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR risk > 0 AND low <= an active profit target, exiting the quantity allocated to that target.
Exit: long or short, an enabled session's trade window has just ended OR the enabled flat window has just started OR the active entry session's configured flatten condition is enabled AND its trade window is inactive OR all session trade windows are inactive.
