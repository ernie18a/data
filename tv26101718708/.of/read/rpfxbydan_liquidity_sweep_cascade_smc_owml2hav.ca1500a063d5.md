Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a bar whose high or low is respectively extreme over equal configurable numbers of preceding and following bars; make it available only after the following bars complete. Retain the latest confirmed high and low. For the first tier use a higher timeframe; for the second tier use a higher timeframe shorter than the first tier; for the third tier use the chart timeframe. Higher-timeframe pivots use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish shift level = at the sweep bar, scan backward from that bar through the consecutive bearish candles, up to a configurable lookback. Use either the open of the oldest candle in the run or the highest high of the run including the sweep bar's high; if the run is empty use the sweep bar's open or high respectively. Bearish shift level = the symmetric calculation using consecutive bullish candles, the oldest open or lowest low including the sweep bar's low.
Calculation: Bullish gap = chart low > chart high two bars earlier, with bottom = that earlier high and top = current low. Bearish gap = chart high < chart low two bars earlier, with bottom = current high and top = that earlier low. Retain only the latest gap of each origin for inverse-gap tracking. A bullish-origin gap inverts when close < its bottom; a bearish-origin gap inverts when close > its top. A new gap of the same origin replaces the previous one and resets its inversion.
Calculation: Tier sequence = maintain independent bullish and bearish states for each tier. A permitted sweep starts a pending sequence, snapshots the latest opposite pivot and shift level, and records the sweep bar and its low for bullish or high for bearish. Until the shift, update that extreme to the lowest low or highest high. The shift occurs when close crosses beyond the saved shift level in the sequence direction, optionally requiring high minus low >= a configurable multiple of ATR. It may occur on the sweep bar. Cancel if the shift has not occurred within a configurable number of chart bars after the sweep. After the shift, accept the first direction-matching gap whose size meets a configurable minimum price difference, including on the shift bar; cancel if none appears within a configurable number of chart bars. Its midpoint must be below, for bullish, or above, for bearish, the midpoint of the saved opposite pivot and updated sweep extreme. A qualifying gap fixes entry at its top for bullish or bottom for bearish, stop at the sweep extreme, and target at entry plus or minus a configurable multiple of risk. Risk must be positive and the distance from entry to the saved opposite pivot must be at least that multiple of risk whenever the risk/reward gate is enabled. If a retest is required, accept only a later chart bar within a configurable timeout with low <= gap top AND close >= gap bottom for bullish, or high >= gap bottom AND close <= gap top for bearish; the first such retest ends the attempt even if another completion filter fails. Without a required retest, the attempt ends on the first size-qualified gap even if its midpoint or another completion filter fails. A retest is required only when entry/stop/target calculation and the risk/reward gate are both enabled for that tier. A new sweep can start only when that direction's tier is not pending.
Calculation: Cascade permission = the first tier may always start. The second tier may start when the matching first tier remains complete, its sweep bar is newer than the first-tier sweep last consumed by a successful second tier, and the chart bar is in an enabled session if the session filter is enabled. The third tier may start when the matching second tier remains complete and its sweep bar is newer than the second-tier sweep last consumed by a successful third tier. Failed attempts do not consume their parent. Starting a new parent sweep clears that parent's completion. First- and second-tier risk/reward gating and entry/stop/target calculation are independently configurable; third-tier risk/reward gating is always enabled. Third-tier completion additionally requires an enabled session if the session filter is enabled. Session membership is determined from datetime using configurable session windows and timezone.
Long: Permitted tier sweep AND chart low < the latest confirmed low pivot for that tier AND, if rejection is required, chart close > that pivot.
Short: Permitted tier sweep AND chart high > the latest confirmed high pivot for that tier AND, if rejection is required, chart close < that pivot.
Long: Pending bullish tier sequence AND close > its saved bullish shift level AND, if displacement is required, high minus low >= a configurable multiple of ATR.
Short: Pending bearish tier sequence AND close < its saved bearish shift level AND, if displacement is required, high minus low >= a configurable multiple of ATR.
Long: A bullish gap occurs AND inverse-gap tracking is enabled.
Short: A bearish gap occurs AND inverse-gap tracking is enabled.
Long: A bearish-origin gap is inverted AND close > its top AND inverse-gap tracking is enabled.
Short: A bullish-origin gap is inverted AND close < its bottom AND inverse-gap tracking is enabled.
Long: A bullish tier sequence completes under its cascade permission, midpoint, optional risk/reward, retest, and session conditions.
Short: A bearish tier sequence completes under its cascade permission, midpoint, optional risk/reward, retest, and session conditions.
Long: Third-tier bullish sequence is pending after its shift with no gap accepted AND third-tier cascade permission still holds AND the latest bearish-origin gap is inverted AND high >= its top AND close > its top AND its midpoint < the midpoint of the saved opposite pivot and sweep extreme AND its top minus the sweep extreme > 0 AND the saved opposite pivot minus its top >= a configurable risk multiple times that risk AND the optional session filter passes; use the gap top as entry and sweep extreme as stop.
Short: Third-tier bearish sequence is pending after its shift with no gap accepted AND third-tier cascade permission still holds AND the latest bullish-origin gap is inverted AND low <= its bottom AND close < its bottom AND its midpoint > the midpoint of the saved opposite pivot and sweep extreme AND the sweep extreme minus its bottom > 0 AND its bottom minus the saved opposite pivot >= a configurable risk multiple times that risk AND the optional session filter passes; use the gap bottom as entry and sweep extreme as stop.
Long: The latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high > the previous confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high AND the latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low > the previous confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low.
Short: The latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high < the previous confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high AND the latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low < the previous confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low.
Exit: long, low <= the entry sequence's saved stop OR high >= entry price + the configured risk multiple × (entry price - saved stop).
Exit: short, high >= the entry sequence's saved stop OR low <= entry price - the configured risk multiple × (saved stop - entry price).
