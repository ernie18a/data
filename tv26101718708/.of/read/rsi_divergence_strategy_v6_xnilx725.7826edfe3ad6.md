Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = an RSI high or low with specified left and right lookbacks, confirmed after the right lookback has elapsed; associate a low pivot with that bar's low and a high pivot with that bar's high. Compare the two latest confirmed pivots of the same type, including on bars after confirmation until another pivot of that type is confirmed.
Calculation: Divergence strength = absolute percentage change in pivot price multiplied by absolute change in pivot RSI and by the current volume SMA divided by the current longer-baseline volume SMA; use a volume ratio of 1 when the ratio is unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the distance between the latest two confirmed RSI low pivots is within the inclusive pivot-distance bounds AND divergence strength meets its minimum OR the minimum is disabled, combined with either enabled regular bullish divergence where the latest pivot price is lower and RSI is higher than the previous pivot, or enabled hidden bullish divergence where the latest pivot price is higher and RSI is lower than the previous pivot. The distance, strength, and enable conditions all apply to their respective alternatives.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = the distance between the latest two confirmed RSI high pivots is within the inclusive pivot-distance bounds AND divergence strength meets its minimum OR the minimum is disabled, combined with either enabled regular bearish divergence where the latest pivot price is higher and RSI is lower than the previous pivot, or enabled hidden bearish divergence where the latest pivot price is lower and RSI is higher than the previous pivot. The distance, strength, and enable conditions all apply to their respective alternatives.
Calculation: Long entry candidate = current RSI is at or below the long threshold AND either divergence entry mode is disabled, or divergence entry mode is enabled and the bullish divergence holds on the current bar when the delay is zero, or the number of bars since the most recent bullish divergence is at least the positive delay. In divergence entry mode, the delay condition applies only after a bullish divergence has occurred.
Calculation: Short entry candidate = current RSI is at or above the short threshold AND either divergence entry mode is disabled, or divergence entry mode is enabled and the bearish divergence holds on the current bar when the delay is zero, or the number of bars since the most recent bearish divergence is at least the positive delay. In divergence entry mode, the delay condition applies only after a bearish divergence has occurred.
Long: Bullish divergence holds AND the latest low pivot is at least the minimum drawing separation in bars after the last marked bullish pivot OR no bullish pivot has previously been marked.
Short: Bearish divergence holds AND the latest high pivot is at least the minimum drawing separation in bars after the last marked bearish pivot OR no bearish pivot has previously been marked.
Long: Long entry candidate holds AND either short entry candidate does not hold OR RSI is below its midpoint.
Short: Short entry candidate holds AND either long entry candidate does not hold OR RSI is above its midpoint.
Exit: Long, low reaches the entry price reduced by the rigid stop percentage OR, after a high since entry reaches the entry price increased by the trailing activation percentage, low reaches the highest high since entry reduced by the trailing percentage.
Exit: Short, high reaches the entry price increased by the rigid stop percentage OR, after a low since entry reaches the entry price reduced by the trailing activation percentage, high reaches the lowest low since entry increased by the trailing percentage.
