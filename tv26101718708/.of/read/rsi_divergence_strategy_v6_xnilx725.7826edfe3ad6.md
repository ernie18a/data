Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot low/high = an RSI low/high with the configured left and right bars; it is confirmed only after the right bars complete. Associate each pivot with the price low/high on its pivot bar, and compare the latest two confirmed pivots of the same type.
Calculation: Divergence strength = absolute percentage change between the paired pivot prices × absolute change between their RSI values × (shorter-lookback volume SMA ÷ longer-lookback volume SMA).
Calculation: Long stop = the greater of entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) and, after trailing activates, the highest high since entry × (1 − trailing percentage).
Calculation: Short stop = the lesser of entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) and, after trailing activates, the lowest low since entry × (1 + trailing percentage).
Long: (latest RSI pivot low is lower in price and higher in RSI than the previous RSI pivot low, OR higher in price and lower in RSI) AND pivot separation is within the configured minimum and maximum AND divergence strength meets the configured minimum; OR (divergence-entry mode AND ((delay is zero AND a qualifying bullish divergence is confirmed now) OR (delay is positive AND at least the configured number of bars have elapsed since a qualifying bullish divergence)) AND RSI ≤ the long threshold); OR (RSI-only mode AND RSI ≤ the long threshold). If both long and short entry conditions are true, take the long only when RSI < 50.
Short: (latest RSI pivot high is higher in price and lower in RSI than the previous RSI pivot high, OR lower in price and higher in RSI) AND pivot separation is within the configured minimum and maximum AND divergence strength meets the configured minimum; OR (divergence-entry mode AND ((delay is zero AND a qualifying bearish divergence is confirmed now) OR (delay is positive AND at least the configured number of bars have elapsed since a qualifying bearish divergence)) AND RSI ≥ the short threshold); OR (RSI-only mode AND RSI ≥ the short threshold). If both long and short entry conditions are true, take the short only when RSI > 50.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop.
