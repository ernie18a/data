Calculation: Use completed chart bars. Let epsilon be a positive price-resolution parameter, clamp(x,a,b) restrict x to [a,b], and prior refer to the preceding chart bar. All thresholds, weights, smoothing lengths, profile settings, and minimum vote counts are parameters; paired bullish and bearish thresholds have opposite signs where indicated.
Calculation: Developing volume profile = for the current session, using the still-forming higher timeframe session and at most a maximum number of its most recent chart bars, divide the high-low range into equal price rows. Distribute each bar's nonnegative volume equally among every row touched by its low-high range. The control price is the center of the first row with maximum volume. Starting at that row, expand the included area one adjacent row at a time until the specified volume fraction is included, selecting the larger-volume adjacent row and selecting the lower row on ties. The value bounds are the outer edges of the included rows. Use at least epsilon times the row count for the profile range.
Calculation: Smoothed value channel = raw center is the midpoint of the value bounds; raw half-width is the greater of the value-area width divided by a parameter and ATR times a minimum-width parameter. Initialize center and half-width to their raw values. On each subsequent bar, move each toward its raw value by a smoothing fraction, clamping center movement to an ATR-based limit with an epsilon floor and half-width movement to separate ATR-based expansion and contraction limits with epsilon floors. Floor the resulting half-width at an epsilon multiple. Upper and lower channel bounds equal center plus and minus half-width.
Calculation: Direction score = weighted sum of (positive DI minus negative DI) divided by their sum, or zero when the sum is zero; the sign of short EMA of close minus long EMA of close; clamped EMA of close change divided by ATR and a scale parameter; clamped close minus EMA of typical price divided by ATR and a scale parameter; and value position. Clamp each normalized component to [-1,1]. Value position is 1 above the upper value bound, -1 below the lower bound, otherwise (close minus value center) divided by half-width floored at epsilon and clamped to [-1,1]; it is zero before the channel exists. ATR is floored at epsilon and uses epsilon before it is available.
Calculation: Candle range = max(high minus low, epsilon); body efficiency = absolute close minus open divided by candle range; close location = (close minus low) divided by candle range; upper wick = high minus max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) minus low.
Calculation: Compression efficiency = absolute change in close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute successive close changes over that lookback, or 1 if the sum is at most epsilon. Compression is the highest high minus lowest low over the associated lookback at most an ATR multiple AND compression efficiency at most its threshold.
Calculation: Dynamic trendline = EMA of a WMA of (twice a shorter WMA of close minus a longer WMA of close). Trend slope = its change divided by ATR; use zero for an unavailable prior slope.
Calculation: Bullish response = lower wick at least a parameter multiple of upper wick AND any of: low below the prior lookback's lowest low and close above that low and open; close above the prior shorter lookback's highest high and open; close above the upper value bound on both the current and prior bars; or low at most the dynamic trendline plus an ATR buffer and close above the trendline and open and lower wick greater than upper wick and trend slope at least a negative threshold and at least prior slope minus a tolerance and direction score above a positive threshold. Bearish response reverses all price, slope, score, and wick comparisons, using the prior highest high, prior shorter-lookback lowest low, and lower value bound.
Calculation: Bullish votes = count of: close above open; close above prior close; EMA of close change above its prior value; short EMA of close above long EMA of close; positive DI above negative DI; direction score above a positive threshold; close above developing control price; body efficiency at least its threshold AND close location at least its upper threshold. Bearish votes use the opposite comparisons, a negative score threshold, and a lower close-location threshold, with the same body-efficiency threshold.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = value channel available AND compression is false AND (volume or its SMA is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume divided by its SMA is at least the participation threshold) AND bullish response AND bullish votes at least the minimum count AND direction score at least a positive threshold AND bullish votes greater than bearish votes. Bearish candidate uses bearish response and votes, direction score at most the corresponding negative threshold, and bearish votes greater than bullish votes.
Calculation: Signal state starts neutral and changes to bullish when a long candidate is accepted or bearish when a short candidate is accepted; it remains unchanged otherwise.
Calculation: Ribbon center = VWMA of typical price, falling back to EMA of typical price over the same lookback. Ribbon deviation = standard deviation of typical price over that lookback, falling back to ATR, floored at an ATR multiple. Replace unavailable or nonpositive volume with 1 for ribbon participation. Ribbon participation = clamp(EMA of effective volume times twice (clamped close location minus one half) divided by EMA of effective volume, -1, 1), or zero when the denominator is below a small positive threshold. Ribbon direction = weighted sum of ribbon participation, clamped change in ribbon center divided by ATR, and clamped (close minus ribbon center) divided by ribbon deviation; use zero for an unavailable center change. Ribbon basis slope = change in a shorter EMA of typical price divided by ATR, or zero when unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish ribbon condition = ribbon direction at least a positive threshold AND ribbon basis slope at least a negative tolerance AND close at least ribbon center. Bearish ribbon condition = ribbon direction at most the corresponding negative threshold AND ribbon basis slope at most the corresponding positive tolerance AND close at most ribbon center.
Long: bullish candidate AND (signal state is bearish OR signal state is neutral AND bearish candidate is false).
Short: bearish candidate AND (signal state is bullish OR signal state is neutral AND bullish candidate is false).
Long: dynamic trendline at least its prior value.
Short: dynamic trendline below its prior value.
Long: bullish ribbon condition on the current and prior bars.
Short: bearish ribbon condition on the current and prior bars.
