Calculation: Accumulation range = after warm-up and cooldown, require the percentile rank of the 20-bar Donchian width over the statistical lookback to be at or below the compression threshold, and the width to meet the minimum percentage of close; trim oldest bars while removing one shrinks range width by more than the trim threshold, then set boundaries to the highest confirmed pivot high and lowest confirmed pivot low in the remaining window, falling back to its absolute high/low if that pivot is absent; accept only if the close is within the boundaries and width still meets the minimum.
Calculation: Boundary tolerance = accumulation range width × tolerance fraction, fixed when the range forms.
Calculation: Confirmed upper sweep = after minimum range maturity, high exceeds range high plus tolerance, and within the return deadline a close returns between range low and range high; reject if the opposite boundary is breached, the range expires, or any enabled killzone, sweep-depth, or equal-high liquidity filter fails.
Calculation: Confirmed lower sweep = after minimum range maturity, low falls below range low minus tolerance, and within the return deadline a close returns between range low and range high; reject if the opposite boundary is breached, the range expires, or any enabled killzone, sweep-depth, or equal-low liquidity filter fails.
Calculation: Stop = beyond the full sweep extreme by the configured buffer multiplied by ATR(14) from the bar before the accumulation range began; round outward to a tick.
Calculation: Target = for a lower sweep, range high + (fib extension − 1) × (range high − sweep low); for an upper sweep, range low − (fib extension − 1) × (sweep high − range low); round to nearest tick.
Long: Confirmed lower sweep AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > EMA(50) of that same higher timeframe) AND target ≥ close + one tick; entry price is the confirming bar close.
Short: Confirmed upper sweep AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < EMA(50) of that same higher timeframe) AND target ≤ close − one tick; entry price is the confirming bar close.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target OR (neither stop nor target was touched and distribution age reaches its timeout); evaluate from the bar after entry, count a bar touching both stop and target as a stop, and on timeout exit at close.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target OR (neither stop nor target was touched and distribution age reaches its timeout); evaluate from the bar after entry, count a bar touching both stop and target as a stop, and on timeout exit at close.
