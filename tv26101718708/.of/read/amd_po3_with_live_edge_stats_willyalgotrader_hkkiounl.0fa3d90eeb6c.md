Calculation: Accumulation range = On confirmed chart bars, while no cycle is active, wait until the bar index is at least the sum of the Donchian lookback and the longer percentile lookback, and the cooldown since the previous cycle ended has elapsed. Start with the Donchian high and low over the shorter lookback when close > 0, the percentile rank of Donchian width over the longer lookback <= the compression threshold, and width >= the minimum width fraction × close. Optionally trim the oldest bar repeatedly while its removal reduces width by more than the trim fraction of the current width and the oldest bar's offset exceeds the minimum maturity. In pivot mode, replace each boundary independently with the most extreme confirmed pivot of that side whose pivot bar is in the trimmed window, falling back to the absolute extreme if none exists; pivots use equal left and right confirmation lengths. Accept the range only if its resulting width >= the minimum width fraction × close and low boundary <= close <= high boundary. Freeze tolerance as the accepted width × the tolerance fraction and the stop ATR as ATR from the bar immediately before the trimmed window. The range starts at the oldest retained bar. On subsequent accumulation bars, expire it when bars since its expiry anchor > maximum age; initially the expiry anchor is the range start. A simultaneous breach of both boundaries or any breach before minimum maturity ends the cycle. A breach is high > upper boundary + frozen tolerance or low < lower boundary - frozen tolerance. On bars with no breach, absolute mode expands each boundary to include the bar's extreme within the frozen tolerance; pivot mode expands a boundary only to a newly confirmed pivot originating within the range and lying beyond that boundary but within its frozen tolerance. A filtered breach ends the cycle only if close is outside the boundaries; otherwise the range remains active.
Calculation: Confirmed sweep = A mature, unexpired accumulation range has a single-sided breach. If session filtering is enabled, the breach bar is in either configured session. If the depth cap is enabled, the excursion beyond the breached boundary <= the configured nearest-rank percentile of true range over the percentile lookback, taken from the preceding bar and frozen at the breach. If liquidity filtering is enabled, at least two stored confirmed same-side pivots within the liquidity lookback are each within the liquidity tolerance fraction × current range width of the breached boundary, and the maximum minus minimum of all such pivots <= that tolerance. After a rejected first sweep has re-armed the range, the breach must be on the opposite side. Track the most extreme wick from the breach through the return bar. Confirmation occurs on the breach bar or a later bar when close is within both boundaries and bars since breach <= the return deadline, provided the opposite boundary has not been breached and the cumulative excursion has not exceeded the frozen depth cap. Check opposite breach, depth cap, and deadline before accepting a later return. If the optional higher-timeframe bias rejects a confirmed sweep and its data is available, optionally re-arm once with the same range, a fresh expiry anchor at the rejection bar, and the first side blocked; otherwise end the cycle. End the cycle on an unavailable enabled bias, opposite breach, exceeded depth cap, missed deadline, or rejected sweep without re-arm.
Calculation: Distribution target = At sweep confirmation, for a low sweep use upper boundary + (extension factor - 1) × (upper boundary - sweep low); for a high sweep use lower boundary - (extension factor - 1) × (sweep high). Round to the nearest price increment.
Calculation: Distribution stop = At sweep confirmation, for a low sweep use sweep low - stop buffer factor × anchored ATR, rounded down to the price increment; for a high sweep use sweep high + stop buffer factor × anchored ATR, rounded up to the price increment. Fix the stop and target at entry.
Long: A low-side sweep is confirmed AND (higher-timeframe bias filtering is disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND distribution target - close >= one price increment.
Short: A high-side sweep is confirmed AND (higher-timeframe bias filtering is disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND close - distribution target >= one price increment.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias display is enabled AND the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias display is enabled AND the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (low <= distribution stop OR high >= distribution target OR bars since entry >= distribution timeout).
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (high >= distribution stop OR low <= distribution target OR bars since entry >= distribution timeout).
