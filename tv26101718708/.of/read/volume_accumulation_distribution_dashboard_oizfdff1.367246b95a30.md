Calculation: Scheduled-event day = a third Friday of any month, the fourth Friday of June, or an ex-dividend day when dividend-calendar data is available.
Calculation: Up/down volume ratio = sum of volume on non-event bars where close rose from the prior bar divided by the greater of 1 and the sum of volume on non-event bars where close fell from the prior bar, over the rolling regime window.
Calculation: Adaptive upper/lower bands = exp(median(log(up/down volume ratio)) plus/minus a configurable width times its standard deviation over a calibration lookback longer than the regime window); use manual bands until adaptive bands are available.
Calculation: Average up/down volume ratio = average volume on rising-close days divided by average volume on falling-close days over the rolling regime window, excluding event days.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the simple average volume over a lookback of prior completed chart bars.
Calculation: Closing location value = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high equals low.
Calculation: Weighted closing location = sum(closing location value × volume) divided by the greater of 1 and sum(volume), over the rolling regime window, excluding event days.
Calculation: Clean OBV change = change in cumulative signed volume over the rolling regime window, adding volume on rising-close bars, subtracting it on falling-close bars, and adding zero on event or unchanged bars.
Calculation: Clean A/D change = change in cumulative closing location value × volume over the rolling regime window, adding zero on event bars.
Calculation: Heavy selling day = close-to-close return below −0.2%, current volume greater than prior-bar volume, relative volume above 1, and not an event day.
Long: non-event bar and (up/down volume ratio meets or exceeds the upper band for the configured number of consecutive confirmed chart bars, changing the regime to accumulation OR up/down volume ratio meets or exceeds the upper band OR average up/down volume ratio > 1.05 OR clean OBV change > 0 OR clean A/D change > 0 OR weighted closing location > 0.10 OR relative volume > 1.3 and close > open).
Short: non-event bar and (up/down volume ratio is at or below the lower band for the configured number of consecutive confirmed chart bars, changing the regime to distribution OR up/down volume ratio is at or below the lower band OR average up/down volume ratio < 0.95 OR clean OBV change < 0 OR clean A/D change < 0 OR weighted closing location < −0.10 OR count of heavy selling days in the rolling lookback >= 5 OR relative volume > 1.3 and close < open).
