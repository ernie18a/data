Calculation: On each completed chart bar, a swing high is the current high if the previous close >= previous open AND current close < current open AND current high >= previous high; otherwise it is the previous high if the previous close >= previous open AND current close < current open AND current high <= previous high. A swing low is the current low if the previous close < previous open AND current close >= current open AND current low <= previous low; otherwise it is the previous low if the previous close < previous open AND current close >= current open AND current low >= previous low.
Calculation: Peak and trough are the latest swing high and swing low, respectively; previous peak and previous trough are the preceding swing high and swing low, respectively. The low ratio is 100 × (peak − trough) / (peak − prior-bar trough). The high ratio is 100 × (peak − trough) / (prior-bar peak − trough). The stored low ratio is the low ratio at the latest swing low; the stored high ratio is the high ratio at the latest swing high.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = current bar has a swing low AND trough < previous trough AND peak < previous peak AND stored high ratio >= BC lower bound AND stored high ratio <= BC upper bound AND low ratio >= CD lower bound AND low ratio <= CD upper bound.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = current bar has a swing high AND peak > previous peak AND trough > previous trough AND stored low ratio >= BC lower bound AND stored low ratio <= BC upper bound AND high ratio >= CD lower bound AND high ratio <= CD upper bound.
Long: Bullish pattern.
Short: Bearish pattern.
Long: Bullish pattern AND (candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio)) AND (bounce filter disabled OR 100 × (close − trough) / trough >= minimum bounce percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 100 × ATR / close >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= long RSI maximum) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Short: Bearish pattern AND (candle filter disabled OR (close < open AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio)) AND (bounce filter disabled OR 100 × (peak − close) / peak >= minimum bounce percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 100 × ATR / close >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= short RSI minimum) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Calculation: A trailing stop activates when price reaches the take-profit level; thereafter its level is the most favorable price reached since activation minus the entry-price-based trailing offset for a long, or plus that offset for a short.
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)) OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price <= trailing stop).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR (trailing disabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage)) OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price >= trailing stop).
