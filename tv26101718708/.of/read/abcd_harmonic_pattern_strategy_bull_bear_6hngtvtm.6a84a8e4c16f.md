Calculation: Confirmed swing high = on a confirmed bar whose previous candle closes at or above its open and current candle closes below its open, use current high if current high >= previous high; otherwise use previous high.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = on a confirmed bar whose previous candle closes below its open and current candle closes at or above its open, use current low if current low <= previous low; otherwise use previous low.
Calculation: BC ratio at a swing low = 100 × (latest swing high − latest swing low) / (latest swing high − prior-bar latest swing low); at a swing high, BC ratio = 100 × (latest swing high − latest swing low) / (prior-bar latest swing high − latest swing low).
Calculation: CD ratio at a swing low = 100 × (latest swing high − latest swing low) / (latest swing high − prior-bar latest swing low); at a swing high, CD ratio = 100 × (latest swing high − latest swing low) / (prior-bar latest swing high − latest swing low).

Long: bullish ABCD condition AND (candle closes above open AND absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≥ minimum body ratio) AND (close is at least the minimum bounce percentage above the latest swing low OR bounce filter is disabled) AND (volume ≥ its simple moving average × volume multiplier OR volume filter is disabled) AND (ATR / close × 100 ≥ minimum ATR percentage OR ATR filter is disabled) AND (RSI ≤ long RSI maximum OR RSI filter is disabled) AND (close > higher-timeframe EMA OR higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled)
Short: a new confirmed swing high AND latest swing high > previous swing high AND latest swing low > previous swing low AND BC ratio sampled at the latest swing low is between 61.8% minus its lower tolerance and 78.6% plus its upper tolerance AND CD ratio at the new swing high is between 127.2% minus its lower tolerance and 161.8% plus its upper tolerance
Short: bearish ABCD condition AND (candle closes below open AND absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≥ minimum body ratio) AND (close is at least the minimum bounce percentage below the latest swing high OR bounce filter is disabled) AND (volume ≥ its simple moving average × volume multiplier OR volume filter is disabled) AND (ATR / close × 100 ≥ minimum ATR percentage OR ATR filter is disabled) AND (RSI ≥ short RSI minimum OR RSI filter is disabled) AND (close < higher-timeframe EMA OR higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled)
