Calculation: Confirmed close pivot high/low = a bar whose close is the highest/lowest among the same number of bars before and after it; available after the bars after it complete
Calculation: W pattern = three successive alternating confirmed close pivots low-high-low; the two lows differ by no more than a tolerance and the middle high exceeds their average by at least a minimum depth
Calculation: M pattern = three successive alternating confirmed close pivots high-low-high; the two highs differ by no more than a tolerance and their average exceeds the middle low by at least a minimum depth
Calculation: Bullish trendline = line through the current confirmed pivot low and an earlier confirmed pivot low lower than it; use the immediately preceding pivot low in recent mode, or the lowest qualifying pivot low among the configured number of preceding pivots in major mode
Calculation: Bearish trendline = line through the current confirmed pivot high and an earlier confirmed pivot high higher than it; use the immediately preceding pivot high in recent mode, or the highest qualifying pivot high among the configured number of preceding pivots in major mode
Calculation: Trendline price = linear interpolation between its two pivot anchors, extended forward; pivots use close or wick prices as configured. Source timeframe is the chart timeframe or a selected higher/lower timeframe; for another timeframe, use confirmed pivots from completed bars
Long: W pattern forms OR close > W neckline + breakout buffer after the W's right pivot OR low <= W breakout neckline + retest tolerance AND close > W breakout neckline after the breakout bar OR close crosses above the bearish trendline + breakout buffer
Short: M pattern forms OR close < M neckline - breakout buffer after the M's right pivot OR high >= M breakout neckline - retest tolerance AND close < M breakout neckline after the breakdown bar OR close crosses below the bullish trendline - breakout buffer
