Calculation: Pattern pivots = confirmed chart-timeframe close pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks. Process a high before a low when both confirm on the same bar. Keep an alternating sequence: append an opposite-type pivot; replace the latest same-type pivot only if the new high is greater than or equal to it or the new low is less than or equal to it. Evaluate a pattern whenever the sequence changes.
Calculation: W pattern = the latest three pattern pivots are low, high, low; the absolute difference between the lows is at most the shoulder tolerance; and the middle high minus the average of the lows is at least the minimum depth. Its neckline is the middle high. A new W requires a different right-pivot bar from the previous W.
Calculation: M pattern = the latest three pattern pivots are high, low, high; the absolute difference between the highs is at most the shoulder tolerance; and the average of the highs minus the middle low is at least the minimum depth. Its neckline is the middle low. A new M requires a different right-pivot bar from the previous M.
Calculation: Pattern state = a new pattern activates its direction and cancels any pending retest in that direction. On the first subsequent evaluation where its breakout condition holds, deactivate the pattern and record its neckline and breakout bar. A pending retest expires when the elapsed chart bars exceed the maximum wait; its first qualifying retest consumes it. Process new patterns, breakouts, expiration, and retests in that order on each bar.
Calculation: Trendlines = use confirmed pivot highs and lows of either close or high/low on the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe. For another timeframe, use the last completed source bar; for a lower timeframe use its last completed bar within each chart bar. For each new pivot high, connect it to either the preceding pivot high or the highest higher pivot high among a lookback of preceding pivot highs; retain the line only when the new high is lower. For each new pivot low, connect it to either the preceding pivot low or the lowest lower pivot low among a lookback of preceding pivot lows; retain the line only when the new low is higher. Extend each latest valid line indefinitely, replacing it only when a new valid line is formed. Use chart bar index for chart-timeframe lines and bar opening time for other-timeframe lines.
Long: A new W pattern is confirmed.
Long: An active W exists AND the current bar is after its right pivot AND close > its neckline + breakout buffer.
Long: A W breakout is pending AND the current bar is after its breakout bar AND elapsed chart bars <= maximum wait AND low <= recorded neckline + retest tolerance AND close > recorded neckline.
Long: Close crosses above the current descending high-pivot trendline + trendline breakout buffer.
Short: A new M pattern is confirmed.
Short: An active M exists AND the current bar is after its right pivot AND close < its neckline - breakout buffer.
Short: An M breakout is pending AND the current bar is after its breakout bar AND elapsed chart bars <= maximum wait AND high >= recorded neckline - retest tolerance AND close < recorded neckline.
Short: Close crosses below the current ascending low-pivot trendline - trendline breakout buffer.
