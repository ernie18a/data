Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = Calculate each indicator on higher-timeframe bars. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on a realtime chart bar, use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Direction score = Assign the highest positive score when higher-timeframe close is above all three EMAs and +DI > -DI; a lower positive score when close is above all three EMAs otherwise; the lowest negative score when close is below all three EMAs and -DI > +DI; a higher negative score when close is below all three EMAs otherwise; a smaller positive score when close > shortest EMA AND close < middle EMA; a smaller-magnitude negative score when close < shortest EMA AND close > middle EMA; otherwise zero. Evaluate these cases in order. The EMA lookbacks are shortest, middle, and longest.
Calculation: Trend score = In order, assign the highest positive or lowest negative score when ADX > the higher ADX threshold and respectively +DI > -DI or -DI > +DI; assign a smaller positive or negative score when ADX > the lower ADX threshold and respectively +DI > -DI or -DI > +DI; assign a smaller positive score when ADX > the weakest ADX threshold; otherwise assign zero. The weakest threshold is below the lower threshold.
Calculation: Momentum score = Compare RSI with six ascending thresholds. In descending threshold order, assign the highest, middle, and lowest positive scores above the upper three thresholds; zero above the next threshold; the smallest-magnitude and middle negative scores above the next two thresholds; otherwise the largest-magnitude negative score.
Calculation: Composite score = Average direction, trend, and momentum scores. Divide higher-timeframe ATR by its higher-timeframe simple moving average plus a small positive parameter. If this ratio exceeds the higher volatility threshold, multiply the average by a reduction factor; otherwise, if it exceeds the lower volatility threshold, multiply by a larger reduction factor. Both factors are positive and less than one. Clamp the result to the minimum and maximum score parameters.
Long: Higher-timeframe close > shortest EMA AND close > middle EMA AND close > longest EMA OR higher-timeframe close > shortest EMA AND close < middle EMA
Short: Higher-timeframe close < shortest EMA AND close < middle EMA AND close < longest EMA OR higher-timeframe close < shortest EMA AND close > middle EMA
Long: Higher-timeframe ADX > lower trend threshold AND +DI > -DI
Short: Higher-timeframe ADX > lower trend threshold AND -DI > +DI
Long: Higher-timeframe RSI > lowest bullish momentum threshold
Short: Higher-timeframe RSI <= highest bearish momentum threshold
Long: Composite score > positive composite threshold
Short: Composite score <= negative composite threshold
