Calculation: Swing levels = most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using the same number of bars on both sides. A new pivot activates its level; a break deactivates it. Optionally reset levels, activation, and previous break direction at each new exchange day. The pivot lookback may be increased according to chart timeframe.
Calculation: Break direction = direction of the latest active-level break, initially neutral. In stop-entry mode, a high >= the active swing high sets it to up and a low <= the active swing low sets it to down; in confirmed-entry mode use close > the swing high and close < the swing low. Process the high break before the low break. A break is continuation if the preceding break direction is the same or neutral, and reversal otherwise; update direction even when no entry is placed.
Calculation: Trend filter = optionally require one selected mode: all three SMA slopes have the trade direction; at least two do; the shorter and medium SMA slopes do; the shorter SMA slope does; or close is on the trade-direction side of all three SMAs. Optionally also require the shorter, medium, and longer SMAs to be strictly ordered in the trade direction, and independently require their slopes to be strictly ordered in that direction. SMA slope is current SMA minus previous SMA.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of volume times (open + high + low + close) / 4 divided by cumulative volume, reset at the first bar at or after a configurable daily anchor time in its configured timezone. Two independently configured anchored VWAP filters may be enabled.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = the selected trade direction is permitted, the position is flat or opposite, the daily entry count is below its configured limit or unlimited, and the current bar time is within the optional configured trading window, including an overnight window. The count resets on a new exchange day and increments when a position opens from flat. The same-side swing level must differ from the last level on which an entry order was placed that day by more than a configurable minimum price increment; reset this guard on a new exchange day. If continuation-only or reversal-only is selected exclusively, require that break type; otherwise accept either. Require the trend filter and, for each enabled anchored VWAP filter, close above its VWAP for long or below it for short.
Calculation: Entry reference = the swing level for a stop-entry order, or the signal bar close for a confirmed-entry order. The confirmed-entry market order fills at the next bar open. A stop-entry order is placed at the swing level and remains pending only while its setup remains eligible; it fills when price reaches that level. Save its stop and target when the order is placed, and cancel it when the setup fails or at a new exchange day while flat.
Calculation: Stop and target = at order placement, choose the stop base as the opposite swing, a configurable fraction of the distance from the entry reference toward the opposite swing, the signal bar low for a long or high for a short, or the entry reference adjusted by a configurable percentage against the trade. Subtract a configurable fixed buffer and entry-reference percentage buffer for a long; add them for a short. Set the target at the entry reference plus the long risk times a configurable reward-to-risk ratio, or minus the short risk times that ratio; risk is the distance from reference to stop. Ratios may differ by side. Retain these levels for the position.
Long: Entry eligibility holds AND swing high > swing low AND long stop < entry reference < long target AND ((stop-entry mode AND swing high is active AND swing high > current high at order placement AND subsequent price >= swing high while the order remains pending) OR (confirmed-entry mode AND swing high is active AND close > swing high AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND abs(close - open) >= (high - low - abs(close - open)) * configurable factor)))).
Short: Entry eligibility holds AND swing low < swing high AND short target < entry reference < short stop AND ((stop-entry mode AND swing low is active AND swing low < current low at order placement AND subsequent price <= swing low while the order remains pending) OR (confirmed-entry mode AND swing low is active AND close < swing low AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close < open AND abs(close - open) >= (high - low - abs(close - open)) * configurable factor)))).
Exit: long, low <= saved long stop OR high >= saved long target OR saved long stop >= entry price OR saved long target <= entry price OR saved long stop >= saved long target OR (daily-close option enabled AND a new exchange day begins) OR an eligible short entry fills.
Exit: short, high >= saved short stop OR low <= saved short target OR saved short stop <= entry price OR saved short target >= entry price OR saved short stop <= saved short target OR (daily-close option enabled AND a new exchange day begins) OR an eligible long entry fills.
