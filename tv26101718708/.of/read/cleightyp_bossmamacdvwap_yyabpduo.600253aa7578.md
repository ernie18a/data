Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high higher than the highs of the same lookback number of bars on both sides; it becomes available after the bars on the right complete
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar low lower than the lows of the same lookback number of bars on both sides; it becomes available after the bars on the right complete
Calculation: SMA slope = current SMA minus its value one bar earlier; use short-, medium-, and long-lookback SMAs
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative OHLC4 × volume divided by cumulative volume since the selected daily anchor
Calculation: Long stop = selected previous-swing, entry-candle-low, or entry-price-percentage stop, adjusted down by configured buffers
Calculation: Short stop = selected previous-swing, entry-candle-high, or entry-price-percentage stop, adjusted up by configured buffers
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × configured risk/reward
Calculation: Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × configured risk/reward
Long: an active confirmed swing high exists AND the swing high is above the active confirmed swing low AND (intrabar mode: swing high > current high; place a buy stop at swing high, filled when high reaches it; OR confirmed-close mode: close > swing high) AND (BOS-only mode and prior break direction is not down OR CHoCH-only mode and prior break direction is down OR both types allowed) AND long entry is allowed by direction setting AND current position is flat or short AND the pivot has not already been used for a long entry today AND the daily trade limit is not reached AND the trading window is open if enabled AND (SMA slope filter disabled OR selected mode passes: all three slopes > 0, at least two slopes > 0, short- and medium-term slopes > 0, short-term slope > 0, or close > all three SMAs) AND (SMA stacking disabled OR short-term SMA > medium-term SMA > long-term SMA) AND (slope divergence disabled OR short-term slope > medium-term slope > long-term slope) AND (each enabled anchored VWAP filter has close > its VWAP) AND (confirmed-close mode OR strong-candle filter disabled OR close > open AND abs(close − open) >= (high − low − abs(close − open)) × (1 + configured body-over-wick fraction)) AND entry price > long stop AND long target > entry price
Short: an active confirmed swing low exists AND the swing low is below the active confirmed swing high AND (intrabar mode: swing low < current low; place a sell stop at swing low, filled when low reaches it; OR confirmed-close mode: close < swing low) AND (BOS-only mode and prior break direction is not up OR CHoCH-only mode and prior break direction is up OR both types allowed) AND short entry is allowed by direction setting AND current position is flat or long AND the pivot has not already been used for a short entry today AND the daily trade limit is not reached AND the trading window is open if enabled AND (SMA slope filter disabled OR selected mode passes: all three slopes < 0, at least two slopes < 0, short- and medium-term slopes < 0, short-term slope < 0, or close < all three SMAs) AND (SMA stacking disabled OR short-term SMA < medium-term SMA < long-term SMA) AND (slope divergence disabled OR short-term slope < medium-term slope < long-term slope) AND (each enabled anchored VWAP filter has close < its VWAP) AND (confirmed-close mode OR strong-candle filter disabled OR close < open AND abs(close − open) >= (high − low − abs(close − open)) × (1 + configured body-over-wick fraction)) AND short stop > entry price AND short target < entry price
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR a new day starts while end-of-day closing is enabled OR long stop >= long target OR long stop >= average position price OR long target <= average position price
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR a new day starts while end-of-day closing is enabled OR short stop <= short target OR short stop <= average position price OR short target >= average position price
