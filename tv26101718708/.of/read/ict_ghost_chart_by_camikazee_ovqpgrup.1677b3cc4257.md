Calculation: Higher-timeframe midpoint = the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback of higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the extreme among the same number of preceding and following chart bars; it becomes available only after the following bars have completed. Maintain the latest confirmed pivot for each lookback.
Calculation: Market state = initially neutral; set bullish after an internal bullish change of character or continuation break, and bearish after its bearish counterpart. Evaluate both using the state before updating it; if both occur, bearish takes precedence.
Calculation: Internal bullish break = close > latest internal pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high. Internal bearish break = close < latest internal pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low. A bullish change of character is an internal bullish break AND either confirmation is disabled OR market state is neutral or bearish AND close > open AND body size > ATR times the structure displacement threshold. A bearish change of character is the mirror image. A bullish continuation break is an internal bullish break AND bullish market state AND close > open AND body size > ATR times the structure displacement threshold; a bearish continuation break is the mirror image.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low AND close > that low, OR low < the lowest low of a lookback excluding the current bar AND close > that lowest low. Bearish sweep = high > the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high AND close < that high, OR high > the highest high of a lookback excluding the current bar AND close < that highest high.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND body size > ATR times the displacement threshold. Bearish displacement = close < open AND body size > ATR times the displacement threshold.
Calculation: Bullish structure shift = a bullish sweep occurred within the maximum sweep age AND after its bar close > latest internal pivot high AND open < that pivot high AND body size > ATR times the minimum body threshold AND either the two-displacement requirement is disabled OR bullish displacement occurred on both the current and previous bars OR current bullish displacement AND body size > ATR times an additional larger body threshold AND absolute distance from close to the fast EMA > ATR times the distance threshold AND either EMA slope confirmation is disabled OR the fast EMA > its value at a prior lookback AND either the optional trend filter is disabled OR fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA. Bearish structure shift is the mirror image. Record the latest sweep only while the Fibonacci range is unlocked; clear its bar after a same-side structure shift if the range is unlocked.
Calculation: Fibonacci range lock = lock when a valid directional impulse creates a range; unlock on origin invalidation, opposite structure shift with displacement and the corresponding midpoint and EMA break, or after both expansion targets are hit. A valid impulse requires a same-direction structure shift, change of character or continuation break AND either the two-displacement requirement is disabled OR displacement on both the current and previous bars AND the distance from the current directional extreme to the opposite extreme over a longer prior lookback >= ATR times the impulse threshold. A new range also requires the reset cooldown and either no active range, an invalidated range, both targets inactive, or an external structure break. Use the prior-lookback opposite extreme as origin and the current directional extreme as endpoint; alternatively use the stored sweep extreme or latest opposite internal pivot when the corresponding origin options are selected. Expansion targets are configurable multiples of the range measured from the origin; mark each hit when high reaches it for a bullish range or low reaches it for a bearish range.
