Calculation: Prior range = highest high and lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the efficiency lookback divided by the greater of the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback and a small positive floor.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by the greater of its simple moving average and a small positive floor.
Calculation: Auction state = a confirmed-bar state machine initialized in balance. In balance, if close is above the prior range high plus the breakout ATR distance and is not below the prior range low minus that distance, lock both range edges, start an upward test, and set the qualifying-close count to one; apply the symmetric rule for a downward test. During a test, first mark failure and return to balance if close crosses inside the tested locked edge by more than the failure ATR distance. Otherwise, increment the count whenever close is beyond the locked edge by more than the breakout ATR distance; the count is cumulative, not consecutive. Starting on the bar after the test begins, accept the test if the count reaches the required count AND efficiency ratio is at least its threshold AND either volume confirmation is disabled OR relative volume is at least its threshold. If acceptance does not occur and the test age reaches its limit, return to balance. After acceptance, first mark failure and return to balance if close crosses inside the tested locked edge by more than the failure ATR distance; otherwise return to balance when the age since acceptance reaches the discovery limit. Before that limit, mark the first upward retest when low is at or below the locked high plus the retest ATR tolerance AND close is at or above the locked high; mark the first downward retest when high is at or above the locked low minus the retest ATR tolerance AND close is at or below the locked low. Retests can occur only after the acceptance bar and at most once per acceptance. Test and accepted states persist until their transitions; failure and retest marks last one bar.
Long: Auction state = upward test OR auction state = accepted upward discovery OR auction state = upward retest OR auction state = failed downward auction.
Short: Auction state = downward test OR auction state = accepted downward discovery OR auction state = downward retest OR auction state = failed upward auction.
