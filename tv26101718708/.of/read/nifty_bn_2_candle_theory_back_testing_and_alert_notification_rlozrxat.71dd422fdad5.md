Calculation: Trailing stops = For each bar, calculate raw long stop as source minus ATR times a multiplier and raw short stop as source plus ATR times the multiplier. Use the current raw stop as its previous stop if no previous stop exists. For each stop, retain its previous value if its raw value is not positive or open = high = low = close. Otherwise, set the long stop to the greater of its raw and previous values if the previous low-price input > the previous long stop, or to its raw value otherwise; set the short stop to the lesser of its raw and previous values if the previous high-price input < the previous short stop, or to its raw value otherwise. The high-price and low-price inputs are respectively high and low, or both close, according to a parameter.
Calculation: Trailing-stop direction = Initially long. Change from short to long when the high-price input > the previous short stop; change from long to short when the low-price input < the previous long stop; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Session VWAP = Cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting both sums on the bar after the timestamp returned by the higher-timeframe session-bar request increases. On historical bars that request uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on realtime bars it uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Trade stop = Previous bar's low when a long entry is submitted or previous bar's high when a short entry is submitted; retain that value until another entry is submitted.
Long: Trailing-stop direction = long
Short: Trailing-stop direction = short
Long: EMA filter is enabled AND close > EMA
Short: EMA filter is enabled AND close <= EMA
Long: Position is flat AND bar is complete AND datetime is within the configured date range AND (session filter is disabled OR the lower-timeframe session check is outside the excluded session) AND RSI > long lower threshold AND RSI <= long upper threshold AND close > session VWAP AND close > long trailing stop AND close > volume-weighted moving average AND close > parabolic SAR AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close > their respective open AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA)
Short: Position is flat AND bar is complete AND datetime is within the configured date range AND (session filter is disabled OR the lower-timeframe session check is outside the excluded session) AND RSI < short upper threshold AND RSI >= short lower threshold AND close < session VWAP AND close < short trailing stop AND close < volume-weighted moving average AND close < parabolic SAR AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close < their respective open AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA)
Long: Long trailing stop crosses above session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses above session VWAP on the same bar AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close > their respective open AND RSI > long lower threshold AND RSI <= long upper threshold
Short: Short trailing stop crosses below session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses below session VWAP on the same bar AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close < their respective open AND RSI < short upper threshold AND RSI >= short lower threshold
Exit: long, trade stop != 0 AND (low <= trade stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price - trade stop) times the reward multiplier)
Exit: short, trade stop != 0 AND (high >= trade stop OR low <= entry price - (trade stop - entry price) times the reward multiplier)
Exit: long, short trailing stop crosses below session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses below session VWAP on the same bar AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close < their respective open AND RSI < short upper threshold AND RSI >= short lower threshold
Exit: short, long trailing stop crosses above session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses above session VWAP on the same bar AND current and previous volume > volume cutoff AND current and previous close > their respective open AND RSI > long lower threshold AND RSI <= long upper threshold
