Calculation: Fibonacci volatility bands = let basis be the volume-weighted moving average of HLC3 and deviation be the configured multiplier × standard deviation of HLC3 over the same lookback; upper band = basis + deviation; lower band = basis − deviation; upper target = basis − 0.001 × configured level × deviation; lower target = basis + 0.001 × configured level × deviation
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − ATR × configured stop multiplier; long take-profit = entry price + ATR × configured take-profit multiplier; short stop = entry price + ATR × configured stop multiplier; short take-profit = entry price − ATR × configured take-profit multiplier
Long: low < lower band AND RSI crosses upward through oversold level AND high < upper target captured at entry AND higher-timeframe ADX from the last completed higher-timeframe bar < configured maximum AND (time filter disabled OR UTC hour is within the configured session and (lunch filter disabled OR UTC hour is outside the configured lunch interval))
Short: high > upper band AND RSI crosses downward through overbought level AND low > lower target captured at entry AND higher-timeframe ADX from the last completed higher-timeframe bar < configured maximum AND (time filter disabled OR UTC hour is within the configured session and (lunch filter disabled OR UTC hour is outside the configured lunch interval))
Exit: long, high > upper target captured at entry OR low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long take-profit
Exit: short, low < lower target captured at entry OR high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short take-profit
