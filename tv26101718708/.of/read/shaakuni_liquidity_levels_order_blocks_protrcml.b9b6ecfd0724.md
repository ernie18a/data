Calculation: Prior high swing = the most recent bar with the highest high in the structure lookback immediately preceding the current bar.
Calculation: Prior low swing = the most recent bar with the lowest low in the structure lookback immediately preceding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish intermediate extreme = the most recent bar with the lowest low strictly between the prior high swing and the current bar.
Calculation: Bearish intermediate extreme = the most recent bar with the highest high strictly between the prior low swing and the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = the earliest bar after the bullish intermediate extreme through the current bar whose close > open AND whose close - open >= current-bar ATR multiplied by the impulse threshold.
Calculation: Bearish impulse = the earliest bar after the bearish intermediate extreme through the current bar whose close < open AND whose open - close >= current-bar ATR multiplied by the impulse threshold.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = the closest bar before the bullish impulse, through and including the bullish intermediate extreme, whose close < open; if none exists, use the bullish intermediate extreme.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = the closest bar before the bearish impulse, through and including the bearish intermediate extreme, whose close > open; if none exists, use the bearish intermediate extreme.
Long: Current close > prior high swing high AND prior high swing age >= minimum swing age AND bullish impulse exists AND every low from two bars after the bullish candidate through the current bar > bullish candidate high AND bullish candidate bar != the most recently emitted bullish candidate bar.
Short: Current close < prior low swing low AND prior low swing age >= minimum swing age AND bearish impulse exists AND every high from two bars after the bearish candidate through the current bar < bearish candidate low AND bearish candidate bar != the most recently emitted bearish candidate bar.
