Calculation: Custom supertrend stop = use upper band HL2 − factor × ATR and lower band HL2 + factor × ATR; trail each band using the prior band and prior Heikin-Ashi close (upper = max(current upper, prior upper) when prior Heikin-Ashi close > prior upper, else current upper; lower = min(current lower, prior lower) when prior Heikin-Ashi close < prior lower, else current lower); trend = 1 when Heikin-Ashi close > prior lower band, −1 when Heikin-Ashi close < prior upper band, otherwise prior trend (initially 1); stop = upper band when trend is 1, else lower band
Calculation: CCI trailing stop = CCI-based trailing buffer: upper buffer = low − multiplier × SMA(true range, ATR lookback); lower buffer = high + multiplier × SMA(true range, ATR lookback); when CCI crosses from negative to nonnegative, set upper buffer to prior lower buffer; when CCI crosses from positive to nonpositive, set lower buffer to prior upper buffer; while CCI is nonnegative, upper buffer cannot decrease; while CCI is negative, lower buffer cannot increase; stop = upper buffer when CCI is nonnegative, else lower buffer (use prior stop if CCI is neither)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe CCI trailing stop = prior completed higher-timeframe bar's CCI trailing stop
Calculation: Long signal state = 1 when the long setup is true; else 2 when close crosses the current-timeframe CCI trailing stop; else prior state (initially 0)
Calculation: Short signal state = 1 when the short setup is true; else 2 when close crosses the current-timeframe CCI trailing stop; else prior state (initially 0)
Long: Heikin-Ashi open = Heikin-Ashi low AND ((Heikin-Ashi close > HMA(Heikin-Ashi close, short lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi low < HMA(Heikin-Ashi close, short lookback)) OR (Heikin-Ashi close > EMA(Heikin-Ashi close, longer lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi low < EMA(Heikin-Ashi close, longer lookback))) AND (Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the custom supertrend stop OR prior Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the current stop OR Heikin-Ashi close > current stop) AND close > higher-timeframe CCI trailing stop AND prior long signal state != 1
Short: Heikin-Ashi open = Heikin-Ashi high AND ((Heikin-Ashi close < HMA(Heikin-Ashi close, short lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi high > HMA(Heikin-Ashi close, short lookback)) OR (Heikin-Ashi close < EMA(Heikin-Ashi close, longer lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi high > EMA(Heikin-Ashi close, longer lookback))) AND RSI(close) < threshold AND (Heikin-Ashi close crosses below the custom supertrend stop OR prior Heikin-Ashi close crosses below the current stop OR Heikin-Ashi close < current stop) AND close < higher-timeframe CCI trailing stop AND prior short signal state != 1
Exit: long, close crosses the current-timeframe CCI trailing stop OR this is the first bar at or after the configured exit time
Exit: short, close crosses the current-timeframe CCI trailing stop OR this is the first bar at or after the configured exit time
