Calculation: Momentum z-score = (change in close − SMA of change in close) / standard deviation of change in close, using the same lookback for the SMA and standard deviation.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high AND momentum z-score > positive threshold. Bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low AND momentum z-score < negative threshold. Confirm pivots using equal numbers of bars on each side; replace each stored pivot when a new one is confirmed and clear it after its corresponding break.
Calculation: Order block = the high-to-low range of the most recent opposite-direction candle within a preceding lookback at a structure break; use the break bar if none exists. A bullish block comes from a bullish break and a bearish block from a bearish break. Optionally suppress a new block if either endpoint lies inside an existing unmitigated block. Retain at most the configured number of blocks, removing the oldest when exceeded. Mark a bullish block mitigated when low < its bottom and a bearish block mitigated when high > its top; update mitigation before checking entries. Each block permits one primary entry.
Calculation: Macro alignment = for each enabled higher timeframe, compare its close with its close EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. The longer higher timeframes use shorter EMA lookbacks than the shorter higher timeframes. The macro filter may be disabled, and each timeframe may be enabled independently.
Calculation: Tracked block = the order block used for the latest primary entry. After a stagnation exit, retain its direction for one secondary entry until close breaks through its opposite boundary or a primary entry resets the pending direction.
Calculation: Long active stop = entry price minus a stop distance until close >= entry price plus a break-even trigger distance; on the first qualifying bar set the dynamic stop to entry price, and on each subsequent qualifying bar set it to the greater of its previous value and close minus an ATR multiple. Thereafter the active stop is the greater of the hard stop and dynamic stop.
Calculation: Short active stop = entry price plus a stop distance until close <= entry price minus a break-even trigger distance; on the first qualifying bar set the dynamic stop to entry price, and on each subsequent qualifying bar set it to the lesser of its previous value and close plus an ATR multiple. Thereafter the active stop is the lesser of the hard stop and dynamic stop.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Macro filter enabled AND close > EMA on every enabled higher timeframe AND at least one enabled higher timeframe close >= its EMA.
Short: Macro filter enabled AND close < EMA on every enabled higher timeframe AND at least one enabled higher timeframe close <= its EMA.
Long: Flat AND bullish block unmitigated AND primary entry for that block not yet taken AND low <= block top AND close > block bottom AND (macro filter disabled OR close > EMA on every enabled higher timeframe).
Short: Flat AND bearish block unmitigated AND primary entry for that block not yet taken AND high >= block bottom AND close < block top AND (macro filter disabled OR close < EMA on every enabled higher timeframe).
Long: Flat AND pending bullish secondary entry AND close > tracked block top AND no primary entry is taken on the same bar.
Short: Flat AND pending bearish secondary entry AND close < tracked block bottom AND no primary entry is taken on the same bar.
Exit: Long, stagnation exit enabled AND bars since entry >= linger threshold AND close <= tracked block top AND close >= tracked block bottom.
Exit: Short, stagnation exit enabled AND bars since entry >= linger threshold AND close <= tracked block top AND close >= tracked block bottom.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price plus profit target distance OR low <= long active stop.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price minus profit target distance OR high >= short active stop.
