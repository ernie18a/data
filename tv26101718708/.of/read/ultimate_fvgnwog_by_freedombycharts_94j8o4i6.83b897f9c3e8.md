Calculation: Bullish FVG = a zone with bottom equal to the high of the first bar and top equal to the low of the third bar when the first high is below the third low; bearish FVG = a zone with top equal to the low of the first bar and bottom equal to the high of the third bar when the first low is above the third high. Use consecutive completed chart bars, or consecutive higher-timeframe bars ending with the last completed higher-timeframe bar; detect the latter on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe period.
Calculation: Weekend gap = the zone between the previous chart bar's close and the current chart bar's open, created on a confirmed chart bar opening Sunday at 18:00 America/New_York when those prices differ; it is bullish if the open is higher and bearish if the open is lower.
Calculation: Zone mitigation = for a bullish zone, chart low or min(chart open, chart close) is at or below the zone bottom or midpoint; for a bearish zone, chart high or max(chart open, chart close) is at or above the zone top or midpoint. Select wick or body and full or midpoint as parameters. Check from the creation bar onward, and retain only the most recent configured number of unmitigated and mitigated zones per source, pruning after each bar's checks.
Long: A bullish FVG or bullish weekend gap is created.
Short: A bearish FVG or bearish weekend gap is created.
Long: A retained unmitigated bullish zone is touched for the first time, with chart low at or below its top.
Short: A retained unmitigated bearish zone is touched for the first time, with chart high at or above its bottom.
Long: A retained unmitigated bearish FVG is mitigated.
Short: A retained unmitigated bullish FVG is mitigated.
