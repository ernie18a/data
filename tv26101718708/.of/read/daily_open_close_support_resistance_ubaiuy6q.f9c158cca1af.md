Calculation: Support/resistance levels = cluster the selected source-bar opens and closes over the lookback; merge prices within an ATR-scaled distance, and keep clusters meeting the minimum count. Use completed higher-timeframe source bars; when source and chart timeframes match, use the current chart bar.
Calculation: Nearest support = the closest qualifying level below the close; nearest resistance = the closest qualifying level at or above the close.
Long: (high reaches within the ATR-scaled touch tolerance of nearest support AND low reaches within that tolerance of nearest support AND previous bar was not touching nearest support) OR (close > previous bar's nearest resistance AND previous close <= that resistance)
Short: (high reaches within the ATR-scaled touch tolerance of nearest resistance AND low reaches within that tolerance of nearest resistance AND previous bar was not touching nearest resistance) OR (close < previous bar's nearest support AND previous close >= that support)
