Calculation: Candle measures = range is high minus low; body percentage is 100 × abs(close − open) / range; lower wick percentage is 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / range; upper wick percentage is 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / range; close position is 100 × (close − low) / range. For zero range, use zero for body and wick percentages and 50 for close position.
Calculation: Exhaustion measures = absolute change from the close at the exhaustion lookback divided by current ATR, or zero if ATR is zero; count rising and falling close-to-close changes separately over that lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume / SMA(volume) at least the volume multiplier threshold; use a ratio of 1 when the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. If the volume filter is disabled, confirmation is true.
Calculation: Long momentum confirmation = RSI greater than RSI at the momentum lookback AND RSI at most the long RSI ceiling. Short momentum confirmation = RSI less than RSI at the momentum lookback AND RSI at least the short RSI floor. Each confirmation is true when the momentum filter is disabled.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of capped components: exhaustion measure divided by max(required exhaustion, 0.10), capped at 1, times 25; opposing-direction close-change count divided by exhaustion lookback, capped at 1, times 10; relevant wick percentage divided by max(required wick percentage, 1), capped at 1, times 20; directional close position divided by max(close-pressure threshold, 1), capped at 1, times 15; body percentage divided by max(minimum body percentage, 1), capped at 1, times 10; 10 if volume confirmation is true; and 10 if the corresponding momentum confirmation is true. For long use falling count, lower wick and close position; for short use rising count, upper wick and 100 minus close position.
Calculation: Risk and target = at the signal close, set the wick stop beyond the signal low for long or high for short by a configurable price buffer, and the ATR stop at entry minus an ATR multiple for long or plus it for short. Choose the wick stop, ATR stop, or the farther of the two according to risk mode. Risk is abs(entry − stop); target is entry plus risk times reward-to-risk for long or minus it for short. The buffer and minimum price increment are configurable price parameters.
Calculation: Selected parameters = normally use the mode-adjusted exhaustion requirement, wick requirement and score threshold, and the configured reward-to-risk. Optionally run a configurable grid of exhaustion, wick, score and reward-to-risk combinations as independent virtual trades using the same entry conditions, score, cooldown, risk and target; each combination holds at most one trade and can re-enter on its exit bar. Record target wins and stop losses from bars after entry, resolving simultaneous touches using the configured priority. On each bar, among combinations with at least the required number of closed trades, select by wins then win rate, or win rate then wins, according to ranking mode; retain the first grid combination on an exact tie. When automatic selection is enabled and an eligible combination exists, use its parameters; otherwise use the normal parameters.
Long: On a completed bar, bar index greater than exhaustion lookback plus momentum lookback plus a warm-up offset AND ATR available AND close below its value at the exhaustion lookback AND exhaustion measure at least selected exhaustion requirement AND falling close-change count at least the directional-count threshold AND lower wick percentage at least selected wick requirement AND close position at least the close-pressure threshold AND close greater than open AND body percentage at least the body threshold AND volume confirmation true AND long momentum confirmation true AND long score at least selected score threshold AND long score at least short score AND bars since the last accepted signal at least the cooldown, or no previous accepted signal exists AND one-active-trade restriction disabled or no tracked trade active.
Short: On a completed bar, bar index greater than exhaustion lookback plus momentum lookback plus a warm-up offset AND ATR available AND close above its value at the exhaustion lookback AND exhaustion measure at least selected exhaustion requirement AND rising close-change count at least the directional-count threshold AND upper wick percentage at least selected wick requirement AND close position at most 100 minus the close-pressure threshold AND close less than open AND body percentage at least the body threshold AND volume confirmation true AND short momentum confirmation true AND short score at least selected score threshold AND short score greater than long score AND bars since the last accepted signal at least the cooldown, or no previous accepted signal exists AND one-active-trade restriction disabled or no tracked trade active.
Calculation: Accepted signal and active trade = a long or short decision creates or replaces the tracked trade and updates the last accepted signal bar only when risk exceeds the minimum price increment. Evaluate the entry gate before updating the existing trade's result on that bar. When result tracking is disabled, the tracked trade remains active until replaced.
Exit: long, result tracking enabled AND bar after entry AND (high at least the entry-time target OR low at most the entry-time stop).
Exit: short, result tracking enabled AND bar after entry AND (low at most the entry-time target OR high at least the entry-time stop).
