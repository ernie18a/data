Calculation: ATR-to-average-ATR ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple average.
Calculation: Adaptive Fibonacci trail = use the latest confirmed swing high and low to form a range; select a retracement level according to the configured trail mode and regime, adjust it by the inverse ATR-to-average-ATR ratio clamped to a configured range, then ratchet the trail upward while long-biased or downward while short-biased.
Calculation: Signal confidence grade = convert a weighted 0–100 score to a grade; the score combines swing-range-to-ATR quality, regime alignment, ADX strength, volume relative to its shorter-lookback EMA, ATR-ratio favorability, and trail proximity to the 50-period SMA. Grade thresholds and factor weights are configurable/fixed by the indicator.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the prior trail bias is short, close is above the prior trail, (close − open absolute value) / (high − low) ≥ 0.45, close − prior trail > 0.2 × ATR, and confidence filtering is disabled or the confidence grade meets its configured minimum.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the prior trail bias is long, close is below the prior trail, (close − open absolute value) / (high − low) ≥ 0.45, prior trail − close > 0.2 × ATR, and confidence filtering is disabled or the confidence grade meets its configured minimum.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple × configured second-target multiple.
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple × configured second-target multiple.
