Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by max(SMA of ATR, a positive denominator floor); the SMA uses a longer lookback than ATR.
Calculation: Regime = bullish trend if ADX > trend threshold AND close > SMA of close; bearish trend if ADX > trend threshold AND close < SMA of close; otherwise high-volatility range if volatility ratio > volatility threshold; otherwise low-volatility range.
Calculation: Swing range = latest confirmed pivot high minus latest confirmed pivot low, using the same number of preceding and following bars to confirm each pivot. Whenever both exist and the high > the low, retain them as the range endpoints; otherwise retain the previous valid endpoints.
Calculation: Fibonacci candidates = clamp(range low + range size × multiplier × volatility adjustment, range low, range high) for the bullish candidate and clamp(range high - range size × multiplier × volatility adjustment, range low, range high) for the bearish candidate. Volatility adjustment is the reciprocal of max(volatility ratio, a positive floor), clamped between lower and upper bounds. The multiplier is selected from three ordered retracement parameters: aggressive uses the largest, balanced the middle, conservative the smallest; automatic mode uses the largest in a bullish trend, the middle in a high-volatility range, and the smallest otherwise.
Calculation: Trail state = initialize the trail to the bullish Fibonacci candidate if available, otherwise close; initialize direction long if close > SMA of close, otherwise short. On each completed bar with both candidates available, if direction is long, first set trail to max(previous trail, bullish candidate), then change direction to short and set trail to the bearish candidate if close < trail AND (high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= body threshold) AND trail - close > penetration multiplier × ATR. If direction is short, first set trail to min(previous trail, bearish candidate), then change direction to long and set trail to the bullish candidate if close > trail AND (high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= body threshold) AND close - trail > penetration multiplier × ATR. Otherwise retain direction and trail. The SMA is the same as in regime detection.
Calculation: Confidence = weighted sum of six factors, evaluated after any trail change: structural factor is clamp((latest pivot high - latest pivot low) / ATR × structural multiplier, lower bound, upper bound), multiplied by the long-direction factor or the smaller short-direction factor; use zero if pivots are unavailable or ATR is not positive. Regime factor takes its highest value when direction agrees with a bullish or bearish trend, an intermediate value in a high-volatility range, and a lower value otherwise. ADX factor is ADX divided by its scaling parameter, clamped to the factor bounds. Volume factor is volume / max(EMA of volume, a positive floor) when volume > 0 and a neutral constant otherwise, then divided by its scaling parameter and clamped to the factor bounds. Volatility factor is zero outside lower and upper volatility-ratio thresholds; otherwise it is a neutral constant minus the absolute difference between volatility ratio and that constant divided by a scaling parameter, clamped to the factor bounds. Trail-confluence factor is the neutral constant minus abs(trail - SMA of close) / (ATR × scaling parameter), clamped to the factor bounds, or a fallback constant if SMA or trail is unavailable or ATR is not positive. Structural and regime factors have equal larger weights; the other four have equal smaller weights. Map the sum to ordered grades using ascending score thresholds; the lowest grade has no score threshold.
Calculation: Trade risk = ATR on the signal bar × the selected stop-distance multiplier. Entry is the signal-bar close. The profit target is entry plus risk × target multiplier for a long, or entry minus risk × target multiplier for a short; the stop is entry minus risk for a long, or entry plus risk for a short. Use the middle of three ordered profit-target multipliers for the backtest exit.
Long: Trail direction changes from short to long AND (confidence filter is disabled OR confidence grade >= minimum grade).
Short: Trail direction changes from long to short AND (confidence filter is disabled OR confidence grade >= minimum grade).
Long: Trail direction = long.
Short: Trail direction = short.
Long: Shorter-period Hull moving average of close > longer-period Hull moving average of close.
Short: Shorter-period Hull moving average of close <= longer-period Hull moving average of close.
Long: Regime = bullish trend.
Short: Regime = bearish trend.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= profit target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= profit target.
