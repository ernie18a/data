Calculation: Trend = initially neutral; track the rolling highest high and lowest low over the trend lookback. When no breakout is being watched, watch an upward breakout and store the current rolling high if it exceeds its value two bars earlier; otherwise watch a downward breakout and store the current rolling low if it is below its value two bars earlier. While watching upward, set trend to up and stop watching if the rolling high exceeds the stored high; otherwise cancel the watch if the rolling low falls below its value two bars earlier. While watching downward, set trend to down and stop watching if the rolling low falls below the stored low; otherwise cancel the watch if the rolling high exceeds its value two bars earlier. Preserve trend until another breakout is confirmed.
Calculation: Adaptive percentage = the base percentage at the initial position layer; for each additional current DCA layer, either add a fixed increment or multiply by one plus a percentage increment, according to the selected scaling mode. Calculate take-profit and DCA percentages separately.
Calculation: Target price = average position entry price increased by the adaptive take-profit percentage for a long, or decreased by it for a short. Set it after entry or an additional DCA order and retain it until the next such order.
Calculation: Effective profit = unrealized profit on the remaining position plus profit realized by partial reductions since the position opened. Peak close profit = highest unrealized profit measured at a bar close since the position opened. Estimated target profit = absolute position quantity multiplied by average entry price and the adaptive take-profit percentage.

Entry model conditions are: structure break: (low < prior rolling lowest low AND close < previous high) OR close < prior rolling lowest low; RSI extremity: RSI <= lower threshold; RSI reversal: RSI > lower threshold AND previous RSI <= lower threshold; trend flip: trend = up AND previous trend != up. Entry filter conditions are: none: unrestricted; reward space: prior rolling highest high > close multiplied by one plus the adaptive take-profit percentage; entry quality: the reward-space condition AND (close - low) / (high - low) < a threshold; RSI extremity: RSI <= lower threshold; fair value: close <= weighted moving average of close; trend direction: trend = up. Use the entry lookback for both prior rolling extremes.

Entry model conditions are: structure break: (high > prior rolling highest high AND close > previous low) OR close > prior rolling highest high; RSI extremity: RSI >= upper threshold; RSI reversal: RSI < upper threshold AND previous RSI >= upper threshold; trend flip: trend = down AND previous trend != down. Entry filter conditions are: none: unrestricted; reward space: prior rolling lowest low < close multiplied by one minus the adaptive take-profit percentage; entry quality: the reward-space condition AND (high - close) / (high - low) < a threshold; RSI extremity: RSI >= upper threshold; fair value: close >= weighted moving average of close; trend direction: trend = down. Use the entry lookback for both prior rolling extremes.
