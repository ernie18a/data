Calculation: Rolling high/low = highest high / lowest low over a lookback, excluding the current bar when referenced as the prior bar
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Fair value = weighted moving average of close
Calculation: Structural trend = Start neutral. When neutral, watch up if the rolling high over the trend lookback exceeds its value 2 bars earlier; otherwise watch down if the rolling low is below its value 2 bars earlier. While watching up, switch to up if the rolling high exceeds the recorded candidate high, or cancel the watch if the rolling low falls below its value 2 bars earlier. While watching down, switch to down if the rolling low falls below the recorded candidate low, or cancel the watch if the rolling high exceeds its value 2 bars earlier. A canceled watch returns to neutral; an established trend persists until the opposite trend is established.
Calculation: Adaptive take-profit percentage = base take-profit percentage, adjusted once per DCA step after the first: multiply by 1 + increment percentage in percent-scaling mode, or add the increment in fixed-scaling mode
Calculation: Adaptive take-profit price = average entry price × (1 + adaptive take-profit percentage / 100) for long positions; average entry price × (1 − adaptive take-profit percentage / 100) for short positions
Calculation: Adaptive DCA percentage = base DCA percentage, adjusted once per DCA step after the first: multiply by 1 + increment percentage in percent-scaling mode, or add the increment in fixed-scaling mode
Long: Flat position AND (selected entry model is structural break AND ((low < prior rolling low AND close < prior rolling high) OR close < prior rolling low) OR selected entry model is RSI extremity AND RSI ≤ oversold threshold OR selected entry model is RSI reversal AND RSI > oversold threshold AND prior RSI ≤ oversold threshold OR selected entry model is trend flip AND structural trend is up and was not up on the prior bar) AND selected entry filter is none OR (selected entry filter is reward space AND prior rolling high > close × (1 + adaptive take-profit percentage / 100)) OR (selected entry filter is entry quality AND prior rolling high > close × (1 + adaptive take-profit percentage / 100) AND (close − low) / (high − low) < 0.4) OR (selected entry filter is RSI extremity AND RSI ≤ oversold threshold) OR (selected entry filter is fair value AND close ≤ fair value) OR (selected entry filter is trend direction AND structural trend is up)
Short: Flat position AND (selected entry model is structural break AND ((high > prior rolling high AND close > prior rolling low) OR close > prior rolling high) OR selected entry model is RSI extremity AND RSI ≥ overbought threshold OR selected entry model is RSI reversal AND RSI < overbought threshold AND prior RSI ≥ overbought threshold OR selected entry model is trend flip AND structural trend is down and was not down on the prior bar) AND selected entry filter is none OR (selected entry filter is reward space AND prior rolling low < close × (1 − adaptive take-profit percentage / 100)) OR (selected entry filter is entry quality AND prior rolling low < close × (1 − adaptive take-profit percentage / 100) AND (high − close) / (high − low) < 0.4) OR (selected entry filter is RSI extremity AND RSI ≥ overbought threshold) OR (selected entry filter is fair value AND close ≥ fair value) OR (selected entry filter is trend direction AND structural trend is down)
Long: Position is long, close > average entry price, take-profit has been reached on this or a prior bar in flexible mode (or close ≥ adaptive take-profit price in strict mode), and effective PNL > 0; AND the selected exit is fixed OR close ≤ prior rolling lowest close over the exit lookback OR low ≤ prior rolling lowest low over the exit lookback
Short: Position is short, close < average entry price, take-profit has been reached on this or a prior bar in flexible mode (or close ≤ adaptive take-profit price in strict mode), and effective PNL > 0; AND the selected exit is fixed OR close ≥ prior rolling highest close over the exit lookback OR high ≥ prior rolling highest high over the exit lookback
Exit: Long, close ≤ prior rolling lowest close over the exit lookback OR low ≤ prior rolling lowest low over the exit lookback
Exit: Short, close ≥ prior rolling highest close over the exit lookback OR high ≥ prior rolling highest high over the exit lookback
