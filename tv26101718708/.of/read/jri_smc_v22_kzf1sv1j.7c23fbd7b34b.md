Calculation: Swing bias = bullish when the three latest confirmed swing highs and the three latest confirmed swing lows are each strictly increasing; bearish when each sequence is strictly decreasing.
Calculation: Upward structure break = close crosses above the second most recent confirmed swing high; downward structure break = close crosses below the second most recent confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Retest = after a downward break, high reaches within the configured proportional buffer below the broken low and close remains below it; after an upward break, low reaches within the buffer above the broken high and close remains above it. Each break can produce one retest.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = high exceeds the highest high of the preceding lookback bars and close falls below that high, or low falls below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars and close rises above that low.
Calculation: Large-body candle = absolute body exceeds a multiple of its longer-lookback average.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when current low exceeds the high two bars earlier and the intervening close exceeds that high; bearish when current high is below the low two bars earlier and the intervening close is below that low.
Calculation: Confirmed supply or demand zone = a proportional band around a confirmed swing high or low, respectively, whose swing-based touch count reaches the confirmation threshold; price is in the zone when close is within the band.
Calculation: Confirmation score = number of true conditions among: either directional swing bias; either liquidity sweep; large-body candle; either fair value gap; either structure break; either retest; RSI above its high threshold with bullish bias or below its low threshold with bearish bias; close in a confirmed supply or demand zone; and ATR multiplied by the configured stop multiplier is positive.
Long: bullish swing bias AND upward structure break AND confirmation score >= configured minimum AND no open or pending position AND trading lock is off AND session filter is disabled or current bar is in the configured NY or London session AND no long signal occurred within the configured long-signal cooldown.
Short: bearish swing bias AND downward structure break AND confirmation score >= configured minimum AND no open or pending position AND trading lock is off AND session filter is disabled or current bar is in the configured NY or London session AND no short signal occurred within the configured short-signal cooldown.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR × configured stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + 3 × ATR × configured stop multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR × configured stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - 3 × ATR × configured stop multiplier.
