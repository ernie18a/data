Calculation: Trend alignment = compare a shorter-period SMA of close with a longer-period SMA of close on the chart timeframe and on each of three successively higher timeframes; for higher timeframes use the last completed bar. Bullish alignment requires the shorter SMA > the longer SMA on every timeframe; bearish alignment requires the shorter SMA < the longer SMA on every timeframe.
Calculation: EMA stack = compare short-, medium-, and long-period EMAs of chart close. A bullish stack requires short > medium > long; a bearish stack requires short < medium < long.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = the high or low of a chart bar with the specified number of bars on each side satisfying the standard pivot-high or pivot-low condition. A pivot becomes available only after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bull setup = maintain an independent state, initially idle. While idle, if bullish trend alignment AND bullish EMA stack AND close > short EMA AND low < lower Bollinger Band, enter breach state, save the low and the latest confirmed swing high as the fixed break target, and clear the prep-fired flag. While in breach state, update the saved low to the minimum low since breach; if the break target exists AND close > break target, enter armed state and initialize impulse high to the current high. While armed, update impulse high to the maximum high since arming; define prep level = impulse high − (impulse high − saved low) × prep retracement parameter and entry level = impulse high − (impulse high − saved low) × entry retracement parameter, with entry retracement greater than prep retracement. After evaluating the armed and prep decisions on the current bar, return to idle if low <= entry level + price buffer OR bullish EMA stack is false OR bullish trend alignment is false OR close < saved low. Process consecutive state transitions on the same bar in the order specified.
Calculation: Bear setup = maintain an independent state, initially idle. While idle, if bearish trend alignment AND bearish EMA stack AND close < short EMA AND high > upper Bollinger Band, enter breach state, save the high and the latest confirmed swing low as the fixed break target, and clear the prep-fired flag. While in breach state, update the saved high to the maximum high since breach; if the break target exists AND close < break target, enter armed state and initialize impulse low to the current low. While armed, update impulse low to the minimum low since arming; define prep level = impulse low + (saved high − impulse low) × prep retracement parameter and entry level = impulse low + (saved high − impulse low) × entry retracement parameter, with entry retracement greater than prep retracement. After evaluating the armed and prep decisions on the current bar, return to idle if high >= entry level − price buffer OR bearish EMA stack is false OR bearish trend alignment is false OR close > saved high. Process consecutive state transitions on the same bar in the order specified.
Long: Bullish trend alignment OR bullish EMA stack OR bull setup state = breach OR bull setup state = armed OR bull setup transitions from breach to armed OR (bull setup state = armed AND prep-fired flag = false AND low <= bull prep level); set the bull prep-fired flag when the last condition occurs.
Short: Bearish trend alignment OR bearish EMA stack OR bear setup state = breach OR bear setup state = armed OR bear setup transitions from breach to armed OR (bear setup state = armed AND prep-fired flag = false AND high >= bear prep level); set the bear prep-fired flag when the last condition occurs.
