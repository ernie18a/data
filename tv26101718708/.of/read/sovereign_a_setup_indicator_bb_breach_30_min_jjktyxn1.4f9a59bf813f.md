Calculation: Bullish retracement levels = impulse high minus (impulse high minus breach low) times the prep or entry-zone retracement fraction; update impulse high to the highest high since BOS.
Calculation: Bearish retracement levels = impulse low plus (breach high minus impulse low) times the prep or entry-zone retracement fraction; update impulse low to the lowest low since BOS.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low with the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed swing at breach.
Long: While idle, chart close > fast chart EMA > medium chart EMA > slow chart EMA, chart close > fast chart EMA, 50-period SMA of close > 200-period SMA of close on the chart and each higher timeframe, and chart low < lower Bollinger Band; then chart close > the swing high captured at breach; while armed, the EMA stack and SMA alignment remain bullish and chart close >= breach low; signal once when chart low <= bullish prep retracement level. Cancel the armed setup if chart low <= bullish entry-zone retracement level plus buffer.
Short: While idle, chart close < fast chart EMA < medium chart EMA < slow chart EMA, chart close < fast chart EMA, 50-period SMA of close < 200-period SMA of close on the chart and each higher timeframe, and chart high > upper Bollinger Band; then chart close < the swing low captured at breach; while armed, the EMA stack and SMA alignment remain bearish and chart close <= breach high; signal once when chart high >= bearish prep retracement level. Cancel the armed setup if chart high >= bearish entry-zone retracement level minus buffer.
