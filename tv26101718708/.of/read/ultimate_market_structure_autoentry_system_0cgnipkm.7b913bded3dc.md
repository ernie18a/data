Calculation: Regression SuperTrend = linear regression of close over a lookback, with upper/lower bands at the regression value plus/minus a multiple of the lookback RMSE; while trend is bullish, trail the lower band upward with max(previous lower band, current lower band), and while bearish, trail the upper band downward with min(previous upper band, current upper band). Trend flips bearish when close crosses below the trailing lower band and bullish when close crosses above the trailing upper band.
Calculation: Range regime = ADX is below a threshold AND (the count of Regression SuperTrend flips over a lookback is at least a threshold OR the absolute percentage change in EMA(close, long lookback) over a shorter lookback is below a threshold).
Calculation: Break quality = absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) and absolute(close − open) / ATR; quality passes when both ratios meet their respective minimums, unless the quality filter is off.
Calculation: Swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars to the right.
Long: Regression SuperTrend flips bullish AND (range filter is off OR regime is not range) AND bar is confirmed.
Short: Regression SuperTrend flips bearish AND (range filter is off OR regime is not range) AND bar is confirmed.
Long: A bullish BOS/CHOCH occurs when close crosses above the last swing high, structure direction is bearish for CHOCH or bullish for BOS, the long state filter passes (if enabled), the trend regime and ADX filters pass (if enabled), and break quality passes when the hard quality filter is enabled.
Short: A bearish BOS/CHOCH occurs when close crosses below the last swing low, structure direction is bullish for CHOCH or bearish for BOS, the short state filter passes (if enabled), the trend regime and ADX filters pass (if enabled), and break quality passes when the hard quality filter is enabled.
Long: After a bullish Regression SuperTrend flip, price retraces by at least the required ATR distance from the flip candle's higher body edge, then touches within the ATR tolerance of that edge and closes above it (and bullish if candle direction is required), followed by a confirmed next bar closing above the setup bar's high; the optional PREP and entry-gate filters pass.
Short: After a bearish Regression SuperTrend flip, price retraces by at least the required ATR distance from the flip candle's lower body edge, then touches within the ATR tolerance of that edge and closes below it (and bearish if candle direction is required), followed by a confirmed next bar closing below the setup bar's low; the optional PREP and entry-gate filters pass.
Long: After a bullish Regression SuperTrend flip, price retests the flip close, then close crosses above it within the wait limit; the optional bar-close confirmation and entry-gate filters pass.
Short: After a bearish Regression SuperTrend flip, price retests the flip close, then close crosses below it within the wait limit; the optional bar-close confirmation and entry-gate filters pass.
Long: After a bullish BOS/CHOCH break, the candle body returns to or below the break candle's upper body edge, then close crosses above that edge before timeout; the break filters pass.
Short: After a bearish BOS/CHOCH break, the candle body returns to or above the break candle's lower body edge, then close crosses below that edge before timeout; the break filters pass.
Long: Low crosses below the last swing low and close finishes above it; then price retests within ATR tolerance and closes above the swing low; then a confirmed close exceeds the retest candle's high.
Short: High crosses above the last swing high and close finishes below it; then price retests within ATR tolerance and closes below the swing high; then a confirmed close falls below the retest candle's low.
