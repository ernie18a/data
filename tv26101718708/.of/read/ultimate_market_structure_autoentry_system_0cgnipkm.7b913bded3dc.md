Calculation: Regression trend = Fit a rolling linear regression to the selected price source. Compute error as the square root of the sum, over the regression lookback, of (source at each bar minus the regression value calculated at that bar) squared, divided by lookback minus one. Raw bands are regression plus or minus an error multiple. Initialize trend up. While the prior trend is up, the lower band is the maximum of its raw value and previous lower band; otherwise it is raw. While the prior trend is down, the upper band is the minimum of its raw value and previous upper band; otherwise it is raw. Switch from up to down when close is below the lower band, and from down to up when close is above the upper band. A flip is a change in trend.
Calculation: Range regime = ADX is at or below its range threshold AND either the count of regression-trend flips within a lookback is at or above its threshold OR the absolute change in the bias EMA over its slope lookback, divided by the current EMA and expressed as a percentage, is below its threshold. A zero EMA gives a zero slope percentage.
Calculation: Break quality = Absolute candle body divided by candle range is at or above a threshold AND absolute body is at or above an ATR multiple. Apply this only when hard filtering is selected; soft filtering does not block decisions.
Calculation: Preparation = Arm long when CCI of typical price is at or below a negative extreme, or short when it is at or above a positive extreme; optionally require close above the bias EMA and ADX at or above its threshold for long, or close below the EMA and ADX at or above its threshold for short. An armed long fires when CCI crosses above a less-negative trigger; an armed short fires when CCI crosses below a less-positive trigger, subject to the same optional filter and a shared cooldown since the last preparation. Firing disarms that side. If long remains armed, disarm short; if short remains armed, disarm long.
Calculation: Structure break = Use the latest confirmed pivot high and low, each confirmed after its specified right-side bars. Initialize structure direction when close first exceeds the latest pivot high or falls below the latest pivot low. An upward break occurs when close crosses above the latest pivot high; a downward break occurs when close crosses below the latest pivot low. Optionally require a non-range regime, minimum ADX, and, respectively, close above or below the bias EMA with ADX at or above its state threshold. A break passing break quality sets structure direction to its direction and replaces the active structure setup with its direction, bar and body level: maximum of open and close for upward breaks, minimum for downward breaks. Expire the setup after its maximum wait. A long body retest occurs when min(open, close) is at or below the level; a short body retest occurs when max(open, close) is at or above it. The break bar itself can retest.
Calculation: Liquidity setup = A low sweep is low below the latest confirmed pivot low AND close above it; a high sweep is high above the latest confirmed pivot high AND close below it. Each sweep replaces the active setup, saving its direction, pivot level and bar; if both occur, select the low sweep. Expire it after its maximum wait. A long retest is low at or below the saved level plus an ATR tolerance AND close above the level; a short retest is high at or above the level minus an ATR tolerance AND close below the level. Save the first retest bar's high and low. The sweep bar itself can retest. A new sweep resets the retest.
Calculation: Entry gate = When enabled, a qualifying structure break or liquidity sweep sets the gate direction and break bar only if no gate is active and no gate retest is already recorded. A matching structure or liquidity retest records a gate retest and deactivates the gate. Reset the gate when its age exceeds its maximum, or when a two-candle regression entry, aggressive regression entry, or liquidity breakout fires. A gate passes when disabled, when its direction is unset, or when its direction matches the entry and either its retest is recorded or retests are optional. Evaluate regression entries before current-bar structure and liquidity events.
Calculation: Regression entry setup = An allowed regression flip passing break quality replaces the setup, sets its direction and flip bar, clears its fired and retraced flags, and saves the maximum of flip-bar open and close for long or minimum for short. A later bar retraces when its low is at or below the long level minus an ATR multiple, or its high is at or above the short level plus an ATR multiple. Once retraced, a long rejection is low at or below the level plus an ATR tolerance AND close above the level; a short rejection is high at or above the level minus the tolerance AND close below the level. Optionally require a bullish candle for long or bearish candle for short. Select the tolerance and retracement multiples from range or trend parameters when automatic regime selection is enabled, otherwise use fallback parameters. Only the first entry per setup fires.
Calculation: Aggressive regression setup = An allowed, quality-passing regression flip saves its close, direction and bar and replaces the previous setup. Expire it after its maximum wait. Record a long retest when low is at or below the saved close, or a short retest when high is at or above it; the flip bar itself can retest. Deactivate the setup after its entry.
Long: Regression trend flips up AND either the regime filter is disabled OR the regime is not range.
Short: Regression trend flips down AND either the regime filter is disabled OR the regime is not range.
Long: Regression trend is up AND close is above the bias EMA AND ADX is at or above both the state threshold and the weakest positive ADX-mode threshold.
Short: Regression trend is down AND close is below the bias EMA AND ADX is at or above both the state threshold and the weakest positive ADX-mode threshold.
Long: An upward structure break passes break quality.
Short: A downward structure break passes break quality.
Long: An active upward structure setup has retested its body level AND close crosses above that level; fire once per setup.
Short: An active downward structure setup has retested its body level AND close crosses below that level; fire once per setup.
Long: A low liquidity sweep occurs.
Short: A high liquidity sweep occurs.
Long: An active long liquidity setup has its first retest.
Short: An active short liquidity setup has its first retest.
Long: An active long liquidity setup has retested AND close is above the saved retest high; fire once and deactivate the setup.
Short: An active short liquidity setup has retested AND close is below the saved retest low; fire once and deactivate the setup.
Long: A long regression entry setup has a rejection AND the gate passes AND either preparation is optional OR long preparation fires on the entry bar, when two-candle confirmation is disabled.
Short: A short regression entry setup has a rejection AND the gate passes AND either preparation is optional OR short preparation fires on the entry bar, when two-candle confirmation is disabled.
Long: A long regression entry setup had a rejection on the immediately preceding bar AND close is above that bar's high AND the gate passes AND either preparation is optional OR long preparation fires on both the rejection and confirmation bars, when two-candle confirmation is enabled. An armed rejection blocks a replacement until after its immediately following bar.
Short: A short regression entry setup had a rejection on the immediately preceding bar AND close is below that bar's low AND the gate passes AND either preparation is optional OR short preparation fires on both the rejection and confirmation bars, when two-candle confirmation is enabled. An armed rejection blocks a replacement until after its immediately following bar.
Long: An active long aggressive regression setup has retested AND close crosses above its saved close AND the gate passes.
Short: An active short aggressive regression setup has retested AND close crosses below its saved close AND the gate passes.
