Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest over equal lookbacks before and after the pivot; recognize it only after the later lookback completes. Retain a limited number of the most recent confirmed pivots of each kind.
Calculation: Unbroken pivot = a retained pivot that has not previously been broken by a close above a pivot high or below a pivot low while its age was greater than zero and less than the maximum break lookback; mark it broken on the first such close even if the other decision conditions fail.
Long: Close > an unbroken pivot high AND its age > 0 AND its age < the maximum break lookback AND the minimum low from that pivot through the current bar < the most recent retained pivot low preceding that pivot high AND at least one candle from the pivot high backward through the order-block lookback has close > open.
Short: Close < an unbroken pivot low AND its age > 0 AND its age < the maximum break lookback AND the maximum high from that pivot through the current bar > the most recent retained pivot high preceding that pivot low AND at least one candle from the pivot low backward through the order-block lookback has close < open.
