Calculation: Detrended linear-regression momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint between the highest high and lowest low and the average close, using the same lookback
Calculation: Momentum acceleration = smoothed change in momentum slope
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands contained within a Keltner Channel; loose, medium, and deep squeezes use progressively narrower Keltner widths, with loose wider than medium and medium wider than deep
Calculation: Release = current bar is outside the applicable squeeze and the preceding bar was inside it
Calculation: Confirmed bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low while momentum at the pivot is higher; bearish divergence is the inverse. Evaluate on the pivot confirmation bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average
Calculation: Long and short confluence scores = normalized active weighted totals of squeeze, trend, momentum, relative volume, and ADX/directional movement; momentum slope with matching acceleration scores more strongly than slope alone. Exclude disabled or unavailable components and renormalize the remaining weights.
Long: Confirmed bullish divergence OR (confirmed bar AND (bullish entry conditions for the selected engine OR an early bullish setup)); an early bullish setup requires an in-squeeze duration at least the minimum, rising momentum that exceeds its value two bars earlier, and bullish trend. Classic bullish entry requires release and positive close momentum, or rising close momentum when its slope option is enabled. PRO/Early bullish entry requires an eligible setup (allowed no-squeeze entry, fresh release after a sufficiently long squeeze, or Early mode during a sufficiently long squeeze), bullish trend, required momentum zero-side and impulse conditions, enabled volume and ADX filters passing, and long score at or above its threshold. Early mode requires rising momentum; standard PRO requires rising momentum with positive acceleration; stronger-confirmation PRO additionally requires rising momentum on the two preceding bars. Bullish trend requires close above the local EMA and, when enabled, the preceding completed higher-timeframe close above its EMA. A no-squeeze entry must be a readiness transition; a squeeze-based entry requires an available, untraded squeeze episode. Enter only while flat, outside cooldown, with valid positive ATR, and not on an exit bar; record entry at the signal-bar close.
Short: Confirmed bearish divergence OR (confirmed bar AND (bearish entry conditions for the selected engine OR an early bearish setup)); an early bearish setup requires an in-squeeze duration at least the minimum, falling momentum that is below its value two bars earlier, and bearish trend. Classic bearish entry requires release and negative close momentum, or falling close momentum when its slope option is enabled. PRO/Early bearish entry requires an eligible setup (allowed no-squeeze entry, fresh release after a sufficiently long squeeze, or Early mode during a sufficiently long squeeze), bearish trend, required momentum zero-side and impulse conditions, enabled volume and ADX filters passing, and short score at or above its threshold. Early mode requires falling momentum; standard PRO requires falling momentum with negative acceleration; stronger-confirmation PRO additionally requires falling momentum on the two preceding bars. Bearish trend requires close below the local EMA and, when enabled, the preceding completed higher-timeframe close below its EMA. A no-squeeze entry must be a readiness transition; a squeeze-based entry requires an available, untraded squeeze episode. Enter only while flat, outside cooldown, with valid positive ATR, and not on an exit bar; record entry at the signal-bar close.
Exit: Long, current low at or below the stop OR (non-Classic mode and current high at or above the target) OR (Classic mode and momentum falls OR fade exits are enabled and momentum falls on two consecutive bars)
Exit: Short, current high at or above the stop OR (non-Classic mode and current low at or below the target) OR (Classic mode and momentum rises OR fade exits are enabled and momentum rises on two consecutive bars)
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR times the configured stop multiple (Classic uses its fixed ATR multiple); non-Classic target distance = initial stop distance times the reward-to-risk multiple. Non-Classic trailing stop follows the favorable price extreme by an ATR multiple and takes effect on the next bar.
