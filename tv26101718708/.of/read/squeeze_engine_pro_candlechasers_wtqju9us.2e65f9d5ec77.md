Calculation: Compression = Bollinger Bands of close lie strictly inside Keltner Channels whose center is SMA of close and whose width is a multiplier of SMA of true range or high minus low. Use wide, medium, and tight channel multipliers in descending order; classify compression by the tightest containing channel. Classic uses the medium channel; PRO and Early use the wide channel.
Calculation: Release = compression is absent on the current bar and present on the preceding bar. A compression episode begins when compression changes from absent to present. Compression duration is the number of consecutive compressed bars; a fresh release is outside compression, within a maximum number of bars after release inclusive, and follows at least the minimum compression duration.
Calculation: Momentum = in Classic, close minus close a lookback ago; otherwise, the current endpoint of linear regression over close minus the average of SMA of close and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the same lookback. Acceleration is an EMA of the change in momentum's one-bar change.
Calculation: Trend = close compared with its EMA AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled outside Classic, the last completed higher-timeframe close compared in the same direction with its EMA using the same length.
Calculation: Directional strength = ADX at or above its threshold AND the direction's DI strictly above the opposite DI. Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA; volume is available when both exist and the SMA is positive.
Calculation: Confluence score = round the percentage of earned active weights. The always-active weights are trend and momentum; compression is inactive only when no-squeeze entries are enabled and the bar is neither compressed nor a fresh release; volume is active only when its filter is enabled and volume is available; directional strength is active only when its filter is enabled. Award full compression weight for a fresh release after at least twice the minimum duration or for deep compression, a smaller weight for another fresh release or medium compression, and a still smaller weight for loose compression. Award full trend weight for matching trend; full momentum weight for momentum moving in the direction with matching acceleration, or a smaller momentum weight for movement in the direction without matching acceleration. Award full volume weight at or above the relative-volume threshold, a smaller weight at or above a lower threshold, and full directional-strength weight when directional strength matches. Divide earned weights by active maximum weights. Full compression and trend weights are equal; full momentum weight is smaller; full volume and directional-strength weights are equal and smaller still. The medium compression award is smaller than full compression but larger than the weak momentum award; the loose compression and weak momentum awards are equal; the partial volume award is smaller than those awards.
Calculation: Entry readiness = for Classic, release AND either momentum has the direction's sign or, when the slope option is selected, momentum moves in the direction. For PRO and Early, either no-squeeze entries are enabled, or a fresh release exists, or Early mode is active and compression duration is at least its minimum; AND optionally momentum has the direction's sign; AND trend matches; AND the volume filter is disabled, volume is unavailable, or relative volume is at or above its threshold; AND the directional-strength filter is disabled or directional strength matches; AND the confluence score is at or above its threshold; AND momentum moves in the direction. Outside Early mode, acceleration must also have the direction's sign; under the stronger-confirmation option outside Early mode, momentum must additionally have moved in the direction on the preceding bar. The earlier-signals option activates Early mode.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = position is flat AND no exit occurred on the current bar AND more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the last exit, if any AND ATR is available and positive AND either the current bar is outside compression and outside a fresh release with no-squeeze entries enabled outside Classic and same-direction readiness was false on the preceding bar, or a compression episode exists and has not already produced an entry. On entry, record the signal-bar close as entry price and mark the episode as traded unless this was a no-squeeze entry. If both directions qualify, long takes precedence.
Calculation: Risk levels = initial risk is the greater of entry-bar ATR times a mode-specific stop multiplier and the minimum price increment. Initial stop is entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short; outside Classic, target is entry price plus risk times a reward multiplier for a long or minus it for a short. Initialize favorable extreme to entry price. After each subsequent bar that does not exit, update the extreme with that bar's high for a long or low for a short; if trailing is enabled outside Classic, set the next bar's stop to the more favorable of the existing stop and the updated extreme minus current ATR times a trail multiplier for a long or plus it for a short.
Long: Entry eligibility AND long entry readiness.
Short: Entry eligibility AND short entry readiness AND long entry does not qualify.
Long: Compression duration is at least its minimum AND momentum is above both its preceding value and its value two bars ago AND bullish trend matches AND this entire condition was false on the preceding bar.
Short: Compression duration is at least its minimum AND momentum is below both its preceding value and its value two bars ago AND bearish trend matches AND this entire condition was false on the preceding bar.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND momentum at that pivot is above momentum at the preceding pivot AND the distance between pivots is at most the maximum. Evaluate on the confirmation bar after the configured right-side pivot bars.
Short: A confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND momentum at that pivot is below momentum at the preceding pivot AND the distance between pivots is at most the maximum. Evaluate on the confirmation bar after the configured right-side pivot bars.
Long: Momentum is at or above zero.
Short: Momentum is below zero.
Exit: long, the bar is after the entry bar AND (low is at or below the active stop OR outside Classic high is at or above target OR momentum is below its preceding value AND (Classic mode is active OR fade exits are enabled AND the preceding momentum is below its own preceding value)).
Exit: short, the bar is after the entry bar AND (high is at or above the active stop OR outside Classic low is at or below target OR momentum is above its preceding value AND (Classic mode is active OR fade exits are enabled AND the preceding momentum is above its own preceding value)).
