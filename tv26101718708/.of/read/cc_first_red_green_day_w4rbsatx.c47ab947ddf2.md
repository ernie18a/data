Calculation: Candle source = either ordinary OHLC or Heikin Ashi OHLC, calculated recursively on each relevant timeframe.
Calculation: Signal bars = chart-timeframe bars when the configured signal timeframe is no higher than the chart timeframe; otherwise use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. A new signal bar event occurs at each chart-bar close in the first case and at the start of each higher-timeframe bar in the second.
Calculation: Red and green = respectively close below and above the previous close, close below and above open, or both comparisons, according to the selected definition.
Calculation: Bearish setup = on the latest signal bar, find the most recent highest high over a window whose length is the maximum of the extreme lookback and the maximum setup age. Its age must be no greater than the maximum setup age. In the window ending at that high, at least the required number of closes must exceed their preceding closes; the window length equals that required number plus the allowed pauses. Find the first red signal bar on or after that high. The setup exists if such a bar exists and enough signal-bar history exists to cover the extreme window. The bullish setup mirrors this using the lowest low, lower closes and first green bar.
Calculation: Moving-average reference = the selected SMA or EMA of source closes. If its timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and detect crosses on that timeframe at the start of its next bar. Otherwise use the value returned from the configured same or lower timeframe, including its still-forming bar, and detect crosses of the chart close against that value at chart-bar close. Moving-average slope = its change over the slope lookback divided by ATR on the moving-average timeframe, using the same completed or forming bar as the reference.
Calculation: Recent downward cross = the latest downward cross occurred within the configured maximum gap plus one signal-bar duration, measured in elapsed time, and has not been followed by an upward cross or consumed by a bearish confirmation. Recent upward cross is the mirror. A cross is the current close below or above the moving average after the preceding close was respectively at or above or at or below its corresponding moving average.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation = a bearish setup whose peak has not already produced a bearish confirmation AND either moving-average confirmation is disabled AND the first red bar is the new signal bar, OR moving-average confirmation is enabled AND chart close is below the moving-average reference AND slope filtering is disabled or slope is unavailable or slope is below its positive limit AND either a downward cross occurs now or the first red bar is the new signal bar and a recent downward cross exists. Bullish confirmation mirrors this with the trough, green bar, close above the average, slope above the negative limit and upward cross.
Calculation: Bearish trigger setup = at bearish confirmation, choose the signal-bar zone if signal-bar mode is selected, or if automatic mode is selected AND chart close is at or below the red bar's high AND red bar close minus chart close is no greater than the allowed ATR distance. Otherwise choose the moving-average zone. The signal-bar zone threshold is the red bar close and its trigger is the red bar low; the moving-average zone threshold is min(moving-average reference, max(chart close, moving-average reference minus ATR times the zone-width multiplier)) and its trigger is the confirmation chart bar low. Record the peak and confirmation time; activate the trigger only if confirmation close is above the trigger. A bullish trigger setup mirrors this: choose the signal-bar zone in automatic mode when chart close is at or above the green bar low AND chart close minus green bar close is no greater than the allowed ATR distance; its threshold is the green bar close and trigger is the green bar high. Otherwise its threshold is max(moving-average reference, min(chart close, moving-average reference plus ATR times the zone-width multiplier)) and its trigger is the confirmation chart bar high. Record the trough and confirmation time; activate only if confirmation close is below the trigger. ATR is calculated on chart-timeframe source bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a signal-bar high or low identified as a pivot using equal configured numbers of bars on each side. Store the latest confirmed pivot of each kind, replacing the previous one, and mark it unused until its first close beyond it.
Long: A bullish setup exists AND its first green bar is the new signal bar AND its trough has not already produced a bullish confirmation.
Short: A bearish setup exists AND its first red bar is the new signal bar AND its peak has not already produced a bearish confirmation.
Long: Bullish confirmation occurs.
Short: Bearish confirmation occurs.
Long: A bullish trigger setup is active AND the current chart bar closes after its confirmation bar AND elapsed time since confirmation is no greater than the configured zone duration in signal-bar durations AND chart close is at or above the stored trough AND no bearish confirmation occurs now AND chart close is above the trigger AND either the no-touch restriction is disabled OR no chart-bar low since confirmation, including the current bar, is at or below the zone threshold.
Short: A bearish trigger setup is active AND the current chart bar closes after its confirmation bar AND elapsed time since confirmation is no greater than the configured zone duration in signal-bar durations AND chart close is at or below the stored peak AND no bullish confirmation occurs now AND chart close is below the trigger AND either the no-touch restriction is disabled OR no chart-bar high since confirmation, including the current bar, is at or above the zone threshold.
Long: On a new signal bar event, signal-bar close is above the latest unused confirmed swing high.
Short: On a new signal bar event, signal-bar close is below the latest unused confirmed swing low.
