Calculation: Pivot range = the latest confirmed pivot high H and pivot low L, each confirmed using the same number of bars on either side; a new pivot event requires its value to differ from the preceding confirmed value. Compute on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Quick range = highest high H and lowest low L over a lookback including the current completed chart bar, or the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Golden Pocket = for range H > L and lower and higher retracement parameters, the bullish zone runs from H − (H − L) × higher parameter to H − (H − L) × lower parameter; the bearish zone runs from L + (H − L) × lower parameter to L + (H − L) × higher parameter. A pivot-based zone is fixed when its corresponding pivot event occurs and is replaced by the next same-side pivot event; optionally remove it when the selected open or close is beyond its far edge for a specified number of consecutive chart bars. A Quick zone updates with its range on each completed chart bar.
Calculation: Golden Pocket filter = optionally require either the shorter-period MA to rise or exceed the longer-period MA for bullish zones and fall or be below it for bearish zones, with an optional reversal of those trend directions; alternatively require H and L both above the shorter-period MA for bullish zones or both below it for bearish zones, optionally also requiring the shorter-period MA above or below the longer-period MA respectively. Compute MAs on the zone's timeframe using the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher.
Calculation: Zone touch = (low ≤ current zone top AND previous low > current zone top) OR (high ≥ current zone bottom AND previous high < current zone bottom).
Calculation: Zone approach = (close > current zone top AND close − zone top ≤ ATR × distance parameter AND previous close − current zone top > ATR × distance parameter) OR (close < current zone bottom AND zone bottom − close ≤ ATR × distance parameter AND current zone bottom − previous close > ATR × distance parameter).
Calculation: Order-block zones = on an upward or downward impulse, find the most recent opposing candle within the structure lookback and use its high and low as the demand or supply zone. Cap its height at an ATR multiple, retaining the top for demand and the bottom for supply. Merge same-side zones when their overlap divided by the smaller height is at least the overlap parameter, using their union capped in the same way; remove additional matching zones. Remove an opposing zone when overlap divided by the smaller height exceeds that parameter, unless the opposing zone was created on above-average volume and the new impulse was not. Limit the number of zones per side by removing the oldest. Optionally remove demand when close is below its bottom and supply when close is above its top. Count a retest only after price has cleared the proximal edge and then the selected low or close reaches the demand top, or the selected high or close reaches the supply bottom; remove the zone at the retest limit, otherwise shrink its proximal edge by its current height divided by that limit. Update zones before evaluating touches and approaches.
Calculation: Fair value gap zones = a bullish gap has bottom at the high two bars ago and top at the current low; a bearish gap has bottom at the current high and top at the low two bars ago. Keep a limited number per side, removing the oldest first. Remove a bullish gap when low reaches its original midpoint or bottom, and a bearish gap when high reaches its original midpoint or top, according to the selected fill rule; optionally shrink the remaining bullish top to the low or bearish bottom to the high. Update gaps before evaluating touches and approaches.
Calculation: Extension zone = for the range frozen at a same-side pivot event, or the current Quick range, the bullish zone runs from H + (H − L) × lower retracement parameter to H + (H − L) × higher retracement parameter; the bearish zone runs from L − (H − L) × higher parameter to L − (H − L) × lower parameter. A pivot extension is replaced at the next same-side pivot event and can optionally be removed when its Golden Pocket is invalidated. A separate extension can use the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe period.
Long: A new pivot high occurs AND the latest pivot high > the latest pivot low AND the bullish Golden Pocket filter passes.
Short: A new pivot low occurs AND the latest pivot high > the latest pivot low AND the bearish Golden Pocket filter passes.
Long: Quick range high > Quick range low.
Short: Quick range high > Quick range low.
Long: Close > open AND close − open > ATR × impulse parameter AND an earlier bearish candle exists within the structure lookback AND, optionally, close > the highest high over that lookback excluding the current bar.
Short: Close < open AND open − close > ATR × impulse parameter AND an earlier bullish candle exists within the structure lookback AND, optionally, close < the lowest low over that lookback excluding the current bar.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND low − high two bars ago ≥ ATR × minimum gap parameter.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − high ≥ ATR × minimum gap parameter.
Long: Zone touch OR zone approach of an active bullish Golden Pocket or bullish fair value gap.
Short: Zone touch OR zone approach of an active bearish Golden Pocket or bearish fair value gap.
Long: Zone touch of an active demand zone OR close > demand zone top AND close − demand zone top ≤ ATR × distance parameter AND previous close − current demand zone top > ATR × distance parameter.
Short: Zone touch of an active supply zone OR close < supply zone bottom AND supply zone bottom − close ≤ ATR × distance parameter AND current supply zone bottom − previous close > ATR × distance parameter.
Exit: long, high ≥ active bullish extension zone bottom OR zone touch of the active bullish extension zone OR zone approach of the active bullish extension zone.
Exit: short, low ≤ active bearish extension zone top OR zone touch of the active bearish extension zone OR zone approach of the active bearish extension zone.
