Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across a parameterized number of bars on each side; update the latest pivot value only when confirmed.
Calculation: Golden Pocket range = latest confirmed pivot high − latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish Golden Pocket = [pivot high − range × longer retracement level, pivot high − range × shorter retracement level]; create on a new confirmed pivot high.
Calculation: Bearish Golden Pocket = [pivot low + range × shorter retracement level, pivot low + range × longer retracement level]; create on a new confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Quick Golden Pockets = the same bullish and bearish bands computed from the highest high and lowest low over a parameterized lookback; update only on completed chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Average true range = moving average of true range over a parameterized lookback.
Calculation: Demand zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle within a parameterized lookback before a bullish impulse; bullish impulse has a body larger than a parameterized multiple of average true range, closes above its open, and, when enabled, closes above the prior lookback's highest high. Clamp zone height to a parameterized multiple of average true range.
Calculation: Supply zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bullish candle within a parameterized lookback before a bearish impulse; bearish impulse has a body larger than a parameterized multiple of average true range, closes below its open, and, when enabled, closes below the prior lookback's lowest low. Clamp zone height to a parameterized multiple of average true range.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = the interval between the high two bars ago and current low when current low is higher; bearish fair value gap = the interval between current high and the low two bars ago when current high is lower. Create only after the current bar closes and when gap height meets a parameterized minimum relative to average true range.
Long: a new bullish Golden Pocket forms and its optional moving-average filter passes, OR the current chart-timeframe bar's low <= the highest high over a parameterized lookback minus its range times the longer retracement level and the prior bar's low > that boundary, OR a demand zone forms, OR a bullish fair value gap forms.
Short: a new bearish Golden Pocket forms and its optional moving-average filter passes, OR the current chart-timeframe bar's high >= the lowest low over a parameterized lookback plus its range times the shorter retracement level and the prior bar's high < that boundary, OR a supply zone forms, OR a bearish fair value gap forms.
Exit: Long, the configured invalidation price (open or close) is below an active bullish Golden Pocket's lower boundary for a parameterized number of consecutive bars.
Exit: Short, the configured invalidation price (open or close) is above an active bearish Golden Pocket's upper boundary for a parameterized number of consecutive bars.
