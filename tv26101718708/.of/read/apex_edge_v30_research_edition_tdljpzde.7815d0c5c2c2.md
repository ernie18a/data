Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = EMAs of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the short EMA has a shorter lookback than the long EMA.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when SMA(volume) <= 0.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = (lower wick > body × wick multiplier AND upper wick < body × smaller wick multiplier AND close > (high + low) / 2) OR (close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close AND body > previous body), where body = abs(close - open), lower wick = min(open, close) - low, and upper wick = high - max(open, close).
Calculation: Bearish reversal = (upper wick > body × wick multiplier AND lower wick < body × smaller wick multiplier AND close < (high + low) / 2) OR (close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close AND body > previous body), using the same body and wick definitions.
Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening session, reset on each new exchange day. The range ends on the first chart bar outside that session. A breakout is eligible through a configured number of bars after that bar and until the first raw opening-range breakout of either direction that day, including breakouts suppressed by cooldown.
Calculation: Latest swing high and low = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, using equal numbers of bars on each side of the pivot. A previous-bar sweep uses the latest confirmed swing level as it stood on the previous bar.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = chart bars since the latest combined long or short signal; both directions share the same cooldown, and signals update it even while a position is open.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the entry bar × stop multiplier. Initial stop = entry price minus entry risk for a long, or entry price plus entry risk for a short. First target and final target = entry price plus the respective reward multiplier × entry risk for a long, or minus that amount for a short; the final target multiplier is larger. Once the first target is reached, including on the entry bar, the stop becomes the entry price before exit conditions are evaluated on that bar. Bars held includes the entry bar.
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI AND higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA AND (low <= chart short EMA OR abs(close - chart short EMA) < ATR × proximity multiplier) AND close > chart short EMA AND abs(close - open) > ATR × body multiplier AND close > open AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND longer-lookback RSI < upper RSI threshold AND close < chart long EMA + ATR × extension multiplier.
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND negative DI > positive DI AND higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA AND (high >= chart short EMA OR abs(close - chart short EMA) < ATR × proximity multiplier) AND close < chart short EMA AND abs(close - open) > ATR × body multiplier AND close < open AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND longer-lookback RSI > lower RSI threshold AND close > chart long EMA - ATR × extension multiplier.
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND opening-range breakout is eligible AND close > opening-range high AND close > open AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA.
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND opening-range breakout is eligible AND close < opening-range low AND close < open AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA.
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND close > long-term SMA(close) AND shorter-lookback RSI < lower extreme threshold AND close < chart short EMA AND bullish reversal AND volume > SMA(volume).
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND close < long-term SMA(close) AND shorter-lookback RSI > upper extreme threshold AND close > chart short EMA AND bearish reversal AND volume > SMA(volume).
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI AND higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA AND previous close <= previous session VWAP AND close > session VWAP AND close > open AND volume > SMA(volume) AND longer-lookback RSI < upper RSI threshold.
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND negative DI > positive DI AND higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA AND previous close >= previous session VWAP AND close < session VWAP AND close < open AND volume > SMA(volume) AND longer-lookback RSI > lower RSI threshold.
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX < range threshold AND close < session VWAP - ATR × deviation multiplier AND bullish reversal AND volume > SMA(volume).
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND ADX < range threshold AND close > session VWAP + ATR × deviation multiplier AND bearish reversal AND volume > SMA(volume).
Long: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND previous low < previous-bar latest swing low AND previous close > previous-bar latest swing low AND close > open AND close > current latest swing low AND volume > SMA(volume).
Short: Signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold AND previous high > previous-bar latest swing high AND previous close < previous-bar latest swing high AND close < open AND close < current latest swing high AND volume > SMA(volume).
Long: low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open.
Short: high < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open.
Exit: long, high >= final target OR low <= current stop OR bars held >= time-stop threshold.
Exit: short, low <= final target OR high >= current stop OR bars held >= time-stop threshold.
