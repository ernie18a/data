Calculation: Volume efficiency = (volume / SMA of volume) / ((high - low) / ATR); use 1 for volume / SMA of volume when the SMA is not positive, and 1 for the efficiency when (high - low) / ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = high - low >= ATR times the range-expansion threshold AND high - low >= ATR times the minimum-height threshold AND volume efficiency <= its threshold AND abs(close - open) >= a fraction of high - low AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish impulse = high - low >= ATR times the range-expansion threshold AND high - low >= ATR times the minimum-height threshold AND volume efficiency <= its threshold AND abs(close - open) >= a fraction of high - low AND close < open.
Calculation: Active void = a zone created on an impulse signal bar, with bounds min(open, close) and max(open, close) and midpoint equal to their average. Maintain separate lists by direction, removing the oldest active zone when its side exceeds its capacity. Starting on the bar after creation, deactivate a bullish zone when low <= midpoint in wick-touch mode, close <= lower bound in full-fill mode, or close <= midpoint in close-cross mode; deactivate a bearish zone when high >= midpoint in wick-touch mode, close >= upper bound in full-fill mode, or close >= midpoint in close-cross mode. Also deactivate a zone once its age reaches the fill-review window, after checking mitigation. Create and prune zones before checking mitigation and determining whether close is inside a zone.
Long: Bullish impulse AND gap confirmation disabled OR previous bar's bullish impulse AND gap confirmation enabled AND low > previous close times a factor below 1.
Short: Bearish impulse AND gap confirmation disabled OR previous bar's bearish impulse AND gap confirmation enabled AND high < previous close times a factor above 1.
Long: Close >= lower bound AND close <= upper bound of an active bullish void AND close is not inside any active bearish void.
Short: Close >= lower bound AND close <= upper bound of an active bearish void.
