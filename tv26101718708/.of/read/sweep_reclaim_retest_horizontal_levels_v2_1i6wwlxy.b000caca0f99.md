Calculation: Trading session = either configured trading session, evaluated using each chart bar's opening time in the configured session time zone.
Calculation: Previous-day high and low = high and low of the last completed daily bar, aggregated from the data.
Calculation: Premarket high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured premarket session, using bar opening times in the configured session time zone; make them available when that session ends and clear them when the next premarket session starts.
Calculation: Swing high and low = most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, each using equal numbers of chart bars on either side of the pivot; update on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Sweep buffer, retest tolerance, and invalidation distance = absolute value of the relevant level multiplied by their respective configurable percentages.
Calculation: Bullish sweep at a level = low < level − sweep buffer AND close > level + sweep buffer. Bearish sweep at a level = high > level + sweep buffer AND close < level − sweep buffer.
Calculation: Pending levels = maintain independent bullish and bearish pending setups. On each bar, first clear a setup if its age in chart bars exceeds the configured maximum or, for bullish setups, close < pending level − invalidation distance or, for bearish setups, close > pending level + invalidation distance. During a trading session, if a side has no pending setup, store the level and bar index of its first matching sweep, prioritizing previous-day, then premarket, then swing levels. Bullish setups use lows; bearish setups use highs. A stored level stays fixed. After evaluating a retest, clear that side's setup when all its retest conditions pass, even if the higher-timeframe bias filter subsequently rejects the signal. A new setup cannot retest on its sweep bar.
Calculation: Mid-range exclusion = previous-day high − previous-day low > 0 AND absolute difference between the pending level and the midpoint of those two levels < their range multiplied by a configurable percentage.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close and EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with the EMA calculated on that higher timeframe.
Long: Trading session AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: Trading session AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA.
Long: Trading session AND bullish sweep at previous-day low OR trading session AND bullish sweep at premarket low OR trading session AND bullish sweep at swing low.
Short: Trading session AND bearish sweep at previous-day high OR trading session AND bearish sweep at premarket high OR trading session AND bearish sweep at swing high.
Long: Trading session AND bullish pending setup exists AND current bar index > sweep bar index AND low <= pending level + retest tolerance AND close > pending level AND (directional-body filter disabled OR close > open) AND (prior-bar-break filter disabled OR high > previous bar high) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR mid-range exclusion is false) AND (bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: Trading session AND bearish pending setup exists AND current bar index > sweep bar index AND high >= pending level − retest tolerance AND close < pending level AND (directional-body filter disabled OR close < open) AND (prior-bar-break filter disabled OR low < previous bar low) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR mid-range exclusion is false) AND (bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA).
