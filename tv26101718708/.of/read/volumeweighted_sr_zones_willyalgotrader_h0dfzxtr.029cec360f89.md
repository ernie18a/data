Calculation: Zones = Confirm equal-left-and-right-lookback pivot highs as resistance and pivot lows as support after the right lookback has elapsed. For each confirmed pivot, calculate volume score from pivot volume divided by the simple average volume before the pivot, and reaction score from the maximum move away from the pivot during the shorter of the reaction window and pivot confirmation lookback, divided by ATR at the pivot. Use a fixed fallback volume score when current volume is unavailable or the pivot's prior average volume is zero. Add a base bonus and cap the score. Process pivot highs before pivot lows. Merge each pivot into the first unbroken zone of the same type whose midpoint is within a configurable multiple of current ATR: expand its boundaries to include the pivot, increment touches, and add a fraction of the pivot score plus a capped square-root touch bonus to its score, capped at the maximum score. Otherwise create a zone around the pivot with boundaries offset by a configurable multiple of current ATR. On every bar, decay each zone's score; remove zones exceeding maximum age, falling below minimum viable score, or remaining broken for more than twice the maximum retest window. If the zone count exceeds its limit, remove the lowest-scoring zones first. Start zone creation only after the maximum of pivot lookback plus reaction window, ATR lookback, and volume lookback, plus pivot lookback and a warmup buffer.
Calculation: Latest break = On each bar, scan unbroken zones in creation order. A resistance break is close above zone top AND previous close at or below that same top; a support break is close below zone bottom AND previous close at or above that same bottom. Optionally require positive volume and volume at least a multiple of the preceding-bars' average volume; optionally also require that average to be positive and volume at most a higher multiple of it. Optionally require high minus low at least a multiple of current ATR. Mark at most one zone broken in each direction per bar. Store the boundaries, direction and bar of each break in scan order; the last break replaces the previous one and resets its retest flag. Zone scores do not filter breaks.
Calculation: Retest = The latest break's age is between the minimum and maximum retest lookbacks inclusive. Optionally require that this break has not previously produced a retest. A successful retest sets its retest flag; a later break replaces it, even if the earlier broken zone remains.
Calculation: Trend weights = Sum zone score multiplied by max(minimum proximity weight, 1 minus min(1, distance from zone midpoint to close divided by an ATR multiple)) for unbroken support zones with midpoint below close and, separately, for unbroken resistance zones with midpoint above close. Include only zones with score at or above the display threshold; use unit proximity weight when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Trade levels = At a flat-position entry, use close as entry price and current ATR. The initial long stop is entry minus an ATR multiple; optionally replace it with the maximum of that stop and the latest broken zone bottom minus an ATR buffer. The initial short stop is entry plus an ATR multiple; optionally replace it with the minimum of that stop and the latest broken zone top plus an ATR buffer. Risk is the absolute difference between entry and initial stop. Set three targets at entry plus, for longs, or minus, for shorts, risk multiplied by the respective target multiple; the target multiples are ordered from nearest to farthest. Optionally move the stop to entry starting on the bar after the first target is touched, provided the stop was not touched on that target bar. Evaluate stops and targets only after the entry bar; a stop takes precedence over targets touched on the same bar.
Long: Resistance break OR (latest break direction is up AND retest window condition is met AND low <= latest broken zone top AND close > latest broken zone top AND close - low >= current ATR multiplied by the minimum reaction factor)
Short: Support break OR (latest break direction is down AND retest window condition is met AND high >= latest broken zone bottom AND close < latest broken zone bottom AND high - close >= current ATR multiplied by the minimum reaction factor)
Long: Support trend weight > resistance trend weight multiplied by the trend dominance factor
Short: Resistance trend weight > support trend weight multiplied by the trend dominance factor
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND low <= active stop
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND high >= active stop
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND low > active stop AND high >= a target not previously reached
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND high < active stop AND low <= a target not previously reached
