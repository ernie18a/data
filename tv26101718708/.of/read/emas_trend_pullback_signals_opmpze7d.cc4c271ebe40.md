Calculation: Short EMA zone = the minimum and maximum of four EMAs of the selected price source, with increasing periods, all shorter than the two trend EMA periods.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of 100 × (RSI of close − lowest RSI)/(highest RSI − lowest RSI) over a lookback; D = SMA of K.
Calculation: Pending first signal = the bar of the first qualifying signal of a direction since its pending first signal was cleared. Clear it before evaluating the current bar when the opposite trend is present or its age exceeds the signal window. A qualifying signal sets it if absent; subsequent qualifying signals leave it unchanged. Track the last confirmed signal bar separately for each direction; a confirmation occurs when a pending first signal exists, its age is at least the minimum spacing, and the same-direction confirmation cooldown has elapsed or no previous confirmation exists.
Long: Shorter-period trend EMA > longer-period trend EMA.
Short: Shorter-period trend EMA <= longer-period trend EMA.
Long: Both trend EMAs > their respective previous-bar values AND shorter-period trend EMA > longer-period trend EMA AND, within the bounce lookback including the current bar, open < short EMA zone maximum AND close > short EMA zone maximum AND close > open on the same bar AND (EMA touch filter disabled OR low <= the longest-period short EMA within its touch lookback including the current bar) AND MACD histogram < 0 AND (MACD turn filter disabled OR histogram > previous histogram) AND (short EMA zone minimum ATR multiple = 0 OR zone maximum − zone minimum >= ATR × minimum multiple) AND (position filter disabled OR zone minimum >= longer-period trend EMA) AND (trend EMA minimum ATR multiple = 0 OR absolute trend EMA difference >= ATR × minimum multiple) AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K crossed above D within its crossover lookback including the current bar AND (extreme-zone filter disabled OR current K < oversold threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period trend EMA > longer-period trend EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (pending first signal absent OR pending first signal age >= minimum spacing AND same-direction confirmation cooldown elapsed or no previous confirmation exists).
Short: Both trend EMAs < their respective previous-bar values AND shorter-period trend EMA < longer-period trend EMA AND, within the bounce lookback including the current bar, open > short EMA zone minimum AND close < short EMA zone minimum AND close < open on the same bar AND (EMA touch filter disabled OR high >= the longest-period short EMA within its touch lookback including the current bar) AND MACD histogram > 0 AND (MACD turn filter disabled OR histogram < previous histogram) AND (short EMA zone minimum ATR multiple = 0 OR zone maximum − zone minimum >= ATR × minimum multiple) AND (position filter disabled OR zone maximum <= longer-period trend EMA) AND (trend EMA minimum ATR multiple = 0 OR absolute trend EMA difference >= ATR × minimum multiple) AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K crossed below D within its crossover lookback including the current bar AND (extreme-zone filter disabled OR current K > overbought threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period trend EMA < longer-period trend EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (pending first signal absent OR pending first signal age >= minimum spacing AND same-direction confirmation cooldown elapsed or no previous confirmation exists).
