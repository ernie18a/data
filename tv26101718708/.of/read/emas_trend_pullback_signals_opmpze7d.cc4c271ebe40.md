Calculation: Short EMA zone = range from the highest to the lowest of four shorter-lookback EMAs
Calculation: Long EMA pair = two longer-lookback EMAs, one with a shorter lookback than the other
Long: Both long EMAs are rising AND the shorter-lookback long EMA > the longer-lookback long EMA AND open < highest short EMA AND close > highest short EMA AND close > open AND (MACD histogram < 0 AND (histogram is rising when the turn filter is enabled)) AND (short EMA zone height >= ATR × minimum zone threshold when enabled) AND (lowest short EMA >= longer-lookback long EMA when enabled) AND (absolute difference between long EMAs >= ATR × minimum spread threshold when enabled) AND (Stochastic RSI K crossed above D within the configured lookback, and K is below the oversold threshold when required, if enabled) AND (shorter-lookback long EMA > longer-lookback long EMA on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar, if enabled) AND (low <= the shorter-lookback long EMA in the configured recent-bar window when EMA touch is enabled) AND (this is the first qualifying long signal after reset, or it is at least the minimum spacing after that first signal and at least the cooldown after the prior long arrow)
Short: Both long EMAs are falling AND the shorter-lookback long EMA < the longer-lookback long EMA AND open > lowest short EMA AND close < lowest short EMA AND close < open AND (MACD histogram > 0 AND (histogram is falling when the turn filter is enabled)) AND (short EMA zone height >= ATR × minimum zone threshold when enabled) AND (highest short EMA <= longer-lookback long EMA when enabled) AND (absolute difference between long EMAs >= ATR × minimum spread threshold when enabled) AND (Stochastic RSI K crossed below D within the configured lookback, and K is above the overbought threshold when required, if enabled) AND (shorter-lookback long EMA < longer-lookback long EMA on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar, if enabled) AND (high >= the shorter-lookback long EMA in the configured recent-bar window when EMA touch is enabled) AND (this is the first qualifying short signal after reset, or it is at least the minimum spacing after that first signal and at least the cooldown after the prior short arrow)
