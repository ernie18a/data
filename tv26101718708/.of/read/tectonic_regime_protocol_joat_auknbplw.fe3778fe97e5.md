Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by the shorter-lookback SMA of ATR; use 1 when that SMA is not positive. Volatility ratio = ATR divided by the longer-lookback SMA of ATR, floored at a small positive parameter.
Calculation: Fractal efficiency = absolute change in close over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use 0 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Pivot high and pivot low = highs and lows confirmed with equal numbers of preceding and subsequent bars. Retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind and whether it is higher or lower than the preceding pivot of that kind; these comparisons persist until the next pivot of the same kind. For the first pivot, compare with configurable initial reference values.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of independently weighted structure, volume, momentum, liquidity, volatility, and efficiency components. For long, award the structure weight when the latest pivot high is higher OR the latest pivot low is higher, AND close is above the latest pivot low; award the volume weight when the linear-regression slope of OBV is positive; award one third of the momentum weight for each of close above KAMA, RSI above its midpoint, and Williams %R above its upper threshold; award the liquidity weight when low is below the latest pivot low AND close is above it. For short, use lower pivot high OR lower pivot low AND close below the latest pivot high; negative OBV slope; close below KAMA, RSI below its midpoint, and Williams %R below its lower threshold; and high above the latest pivot high AND close below it. For both directions, award the volatility weight when the volatility ratio is strictly between its lower and upper thresholds, and the efficiency weight when fractal efficiency is at least its threshold. Otherwise each component contributes zero.
Calculation: Long setup = ADX above its trend threshold AND close above chart-timeframe EMA AND close above VWMA AND shorter HMA above longer HMA AND close above the higher-timeframe EMA from the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND long score at least its threshold AND long score minus short score at least the direction-lead threshold. When the higher-timeframe filter is disabled, use the chart-timeframe EMA in its place. Short setup uses the same ADX condition, reverses every price and HMA comparison, and requires short score at least its threshold AND short score minus long score at least the direction-lead threshold.
Calculation: Entry risk distance = ATR on the signal bar multiplied by a volatility-dependent stop multiplier: use the low-volatility multiplier when ATR ratio is below its lower threshold, the high-volatility multiplier when above its upper threshold, and the medium multiplier otherwise. Record the signal-bar close and risk distance whenever an entry signal occurs. Targets are the signal-bar close plus, for long, or minus, for short, the recorded risk distance multiplied by the respective target multiplier; the second multiplier is greater than the first.
Calculation: Long stop starts at signal-bar close minus entry risk distance and, on each bar with an open long, ratchets upward to the greater of its previous value and close minus current ATR times the current volatility-dependent multiplier. Short stop starts at signal-bar close plus entry risk distance and ratchets downward to the lesser of its previous value and close plus current ATR times that multiplier. Starting on the bar after the first target has been reached, also constrain the long stop to at least the entry price and the short stop to at most the entry price.
Long: ADX > trend threshold AND close > chart-timeframe EMA.
Short: ADX > trend threshold AND close < chart-timeframe EMA.
Long: long score >= score threshold.
Short: short score >= score threshold.
Long: long setup is true AND (long setup was false on the preceding bar OR position is flat AND long score >= score threshold plus an additional score margin).
Short: short setup is true AND (short setup was false on the preceding bar OR position is flat AND short score >= score threshold plus an additional score margin).
Exit: long, low <= active long stop OR high >= second long target OR bars held >= maximum holding period.
Exit: long, first long target has not previously been filled AND high >= first long target; exit half the original position.
Exit: short, high >= active short stop OR low <= second short target OR bars held >= maximum holding period.
Exit: short, first short target has not previously been filled AND low <= first short target; exit half the original position.
