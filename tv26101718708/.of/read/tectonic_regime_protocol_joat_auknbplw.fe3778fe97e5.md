Calculation: Regime = ADX is above its threshold and close is above the 50-period EMA for bullish trend, or below it for bearish trend; otherwise it is a range regime.
Calculation: ATR ratio = 14-period ATR divided by its 50-period SMA; stop multiplier is the low-volatility multiplier when this ratio is below 0.85, high-volatility multiplier when above 1.35, otherwise the medium-volatility multiplier.
Calculation: Stop distance = 14-period ATR × active stop multiplier.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with a five-bar lookback on each side, available only after the five right-side bars complete. Track the latest and preceding confirmed swing high and low.
Calculation: Bull structure points = structure weight when the latest swing high is higher than the preceding swing high OR the latest swing low is higher than the preceding swing low, and close is above the latest swing low; otherwise zero. Bear structure points = structure weight when the latest swing high is lower than the preceding swing high OR the latest swing low is lower than the preceding swing low, and close is below the latest swing high; otherwise zero. Retain each comparison’s most recent value until that pivot type updates.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of sign(close change) × volume. Bull volume points = volume weight when the current 20-period linear regression of OBV is above its one-bar-offset value; bear volume points = volume weight when it is below; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Momentum points = momentum weight × the fraction of the three directional conditions met. Bull conditions: close above KAMA(21), RSI(14) above 50, and Williams %R(14) above −40. Bear conditions: close below KAMA(21), RSI(14) below 50, and Williams %R(14) below −60.
Calculation: Liquidity points = liquidity weight when the current low is below the latest confirmed swing low and close is above it (bull), or current high is above the latest confirmed swing high and close is below it (bear); otherwise zero.
Calculation: Volatility points = volatility weight for both sides when 14-period ATR divided by its 100-period SMA is strictly between 0.8 and 1.6; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Fractal efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback. FER points = FER weight for both sides when this ratio is at least its minimum threshold; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Bull score and bear score = sum of their respective structure, volume, momentum, liquidity, volatility, and FER points.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = 50-period EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Initial long stop and targets = signal-bar close − stop distance; signal-bar close + stop distance × TP1 multiple; signal-bar close + stop distance × TP2 multiple. Initial short stop and targets = signal-bar close + stop distance; signal-bar close − stop distance × TP1 multiple; signal-bar close − stop distance × TP2 multiple.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = ratchet upward to the greater of the existing stop and current close − stop distance; after TP1 is hit, raise it to at least the average entry price. Short trailing stop = ratchet downward to the lesser of the existing stop and current close + stop distance; after TP1 is hit, lower it to at most the average entry price.
Long: ADX above its threshold AND close above EMA(50) AND close above VWMA(long-term) AND fast HMA above slow HMA (fast lookback shorter than slow) AND close above higher-timeframe EMA(50) AND bull score at least the minimum score AND bull score minus bear score at least the minimum directional lead AND current bar confirmed AND (the preceding bar did not meet these long conditions OR position is flat AND bull score at least minimum score + 10).
Short: ADX above its threshold AND close below EMA(50) AND close below VWMA(long-term) AND fast HMA below slow HMA (fast lookback shorter than slow) AND close below higher-timeframe EMA(50) AND bear score at least the minimum score AND bear score minus bull score at least the minimum directional lead AND current bar confirmed AND (the preceding bar did not meet these short conditions OR position is flat AND bear score at least minimum score + 10).
Exit: long, low ≤ long trailing stop OR high ≥ long TP1 (close 50%) OR high ≥ long TP2 (close remaining position) OR bars in position ≥ maximum hold bars.
Exit: short, high ≥ short trailing stop OR low ≤ short TP1 (close 50%) OR low ≤ short TP2 (close remaining position) OR bars in position ≥ maximum hold bars.
