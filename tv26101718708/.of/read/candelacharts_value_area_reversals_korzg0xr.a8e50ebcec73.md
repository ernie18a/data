Calculation: Previous value-area high and low = Use chart bars belonging to the last completed higher-timeframe session. Divide its low-to-high range into equal price bins. Allocate each chart bar's volume to bins in proportion to the overlap with its high-low range; allocate a zero-range bar's entire volume to the bin containing its close, clamped to the profile range. Starting at the lowest-price bin with maximum volume, add the larger-volume adjacent bin until the selected fraction of total volume is reached, choosing the upper bin on ties. The high is the upper edge of the highest included bin; the low is the lower edge of the lowest included bin. The levels are unavailable when the session range is zero.
Calculation: Volume spike = Current volume is greater than every volume in the selected lookback: either the previous chart bars regardless of direction, treating unavailable volumes as zero, or the previous chart bars of the current candle's direction, excluding dojis and requiring the full number of matching bars.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation level = For each bullish value-area signal, search backward from the bar before the signal for the nearest candle with close <= open within a bounded lookback. From that candle, continue backward through its consecutive candles with close <= open, up to a bounded lookback, and use the oldest such candle's open. If the first search finds none, use the bar before the signal as the starting candle; if it has close > open, use its open. Keep the level until confirmed or until its signal falls outside the retained higher-timeframe session history.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation level = For each bearish value-area signal, search backward from the bar before the signal for the nearest candle with close > open within a bounded lookback. From that candle, continue backward through its consecutive candles with close > open, up to a bounded lookback, and use the oldest such candle's open. If the first search finds none, use the bar before the signal as the starting candle; if it has close <= open, use its open. Keep the level until confirmed or until its signal falls outside the retained higher-timeframe session history.
Long: Close > open AND volume spike.
Short: Close < open AND volume spike.
Long: Previous value-area high and low are available AND low <= previous value-area low AND close > open AND volume spike.
Short: Previous value-area high and low are available AND high >= previous value-area high AND close < open AND volume spike.
Long: A bullish value-area signal occurred on an earlier bar AND its bullish confirmation level is unconfirmed and retained AND close > that level.
Short: A bearish value-area signal occurred on an earlier bar AND its bearish confirmation level is unconfirmed and retained AND close < that level.
