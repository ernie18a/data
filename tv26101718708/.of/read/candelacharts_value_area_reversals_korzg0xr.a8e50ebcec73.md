Calculation: Previous-session VAH and VAL = build a volume profile from the bars in the last completed higher-timeframe session: divide its high–low range into price bins, allocate each bar’s volume across bins in proportion to its range overlapping each bin, and expand the value area from the highest-volume bin toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until it includes the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are the upper and lower edges of that area.
Calculation: Bullish CISD level = open of the oldest candle in the same-color bearish run found by scanning backward from the bar before a bullish reclaim; bearish CISD level = open of the oldest candle in the same-color bullish run found by scanning backward from the bar before a bearish reclaim.
Long: low <= previous-session VAL AND close > open AND volume > each of the N most recent earlier bullish-candle volumes (or each of the N most recent earlier candle volumes in all-types mode), OR close > bullish CISD level after a bullish reclaim
Short: high >= previous-session VAH AND close < open AND volume > each of the N most recent earlier bearish-candle volumes (or each of the N most recent earlier candle volumes in all-types mode), OR close < bearish CISD level after a bearish reclaim
