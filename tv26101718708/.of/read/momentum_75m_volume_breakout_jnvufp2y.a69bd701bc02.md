Calculation: Long-, medium-, and short-lookback rate of change = (higher-timeframe close / close from the corresponding lookback ago − 1) × 100; use the higher-timeframe bar, which is last completed historically and still forming in realtime.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ATR percentage = Wilder ATR / higher-timeframe close × 100; use the higher-timeframe bar, which is last completed historically and still forming in realtime.
Calculation: Prior higher-timeframe multi-bar high = highest high over the prior lookback bars, excluding the current higher-timeframe bar.
Long: (long-lookback rate of change > threshold OR medium-lookback rate of change > threshold OR short-lookback rate of change > threshold) AND higher-timeframe ATR percentage > threshold AND (chart-timeframe close > any of the four chart-timeframe EMAs, from shortest to longest lookback, multiplied by a buffer OR chart-timeframe close > prior higher-timeframe multi-bar high multiplied by a buffer) AND chart-timeframe close > chart-timeframe open AND chart-timeframe volume > EMA of volume + multiplier × standard deviation of volume, using the same lookback for both volume calculations
