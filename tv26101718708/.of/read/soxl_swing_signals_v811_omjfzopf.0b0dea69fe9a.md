Calculation: Bollinger position = (close − lower Bollinger band) / (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band), using either a shorter or a longer lookback.
Calculation: Regression position = (close − lower regression band) / (upper regression band − lower regression band), using either a shorter or a longer lookback. For each lookback, the center is the current endpoint of the linear regression of close; the bands are the center plus or minus a multiplier times the rolling standard deviation of close minus its own contemporaneous regression endpoint.
Calculation: Normalized regression slope = the difference between the current and preceding fitted points of the same long-lookback regression, divided by its current endpoint.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = SMA of stochastic %K computed from close, high, and low.
Long: (long-lookback regression position <= its lower threshold OR long-lookback Bollinger position <= its lower threshold OR short-lookback regression position <= its lower threshold OR short-lookback Bollinger position <= its lower threshold) AND (smoothed stochastic <= its oversold threshold OR (normalized long-lookback regression slope > its positive threshold AND close <= short-term SMA of close)).
