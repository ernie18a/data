Calculation: Opening range = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar at the end of the configurable opening-range session; retain these levels until the next opening-range session begins.
Calculation: Long and short trend filters = respectively, the last completed higher-timeframe close above or below its EMA, optionally requiring that EMA to be respectively above or below its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Stochastic K = SMA of the raw stochastic of close, high and low; stochastic D = SMA of K, with independently configurable smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low); lower-wick fraction = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low); upper-wick fraction = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low). Each fraction is zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Long and short breakout levels = opening-range high plus a nonnegative breakout buffer and opening-range low minus that buffer, respectively. Possible-alert levels use a separately configurable nonnegative buffer.
Calculation: Fresh long breakout = close above the long breakout level AND previous close at or below that same level AND close above open AND body fraction at least the minimum AND the enabled long VWAP and trend filters pass. Fresh short breakout = close below the short breakout level AND previous close at or above that same level AND close below open AND body fraction at least the minimum AND the enabled short VWAP and trend filters pass.
Calculation: Breakout confirmation = a fresh breakout, optionally followed on the immediately next bar by a close beyond the same breakout level with the corresponding enabled VWAP and trend filters passing. A new fresh breakout replaces any pending confirmation; the next-bar confirmation expires after that bar.
Calculation: Retest confirmation = after breakout confirmation, a later bar within the maximum retest-bar count with low at or below the long breakout level, close above that level and close above open for a long, or high at or above the short breakout level, close below that level and close below open for a short. Cancel an armed long retest if close falls below the opening-range high, an armed short retest if close rises above the opening-range low, either retest if it expires, or an opposite retest when a fresh breakout occurs.
Calculation: Long and short stops = respectively, the opening-range low minus the stop buffer or high plus the stop buffer in breakout mode; the entry bar's low minus the stop buffer or high plus the stop buffer in sweep mode. Long target = entry price plus reward-to-risk parameter times entry price minus long stop; short target = entry price minus reward-to-risk parameter times short stop minus entry price. Fix stop and target at entry.
Long: Opening range available AND within the entry session AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND this direction has not previously produced a possible alert since the opening-range session began AND high is at or above the long possible-alert level AND, if enabled, close is above session VWAP of typical price AND, if enabled, the long trend filter passes.
Short: Opening range available AND within the entry session AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND this direction has not previously produced a possible alert since the opening-range session began AND low is at or below the short possible-alert level AND, if enabled, close is below session VWAP of typical price AND, if enabled, the short trend filter passes.
Long: At or after the backtest start AND opening range available AND within the entry session AND bar closed AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND breakout mode selected AND breakout confirmation occurs, or, if retest is required, an armed long retest confirmation occurs with enabled long VWAP and trend filters passing AND, if enabled, stochastic K is above D and above its midpoint.
Short: At or after the backtest start AND opening range available AND within the entry session AND bar closed AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND breakout mode selected AND breakout confirmation occurs, or, if retest is required, an armed short retest confirmation occurs with enabled short VWAP and trend filters passing AND, if enabled, stochastic K is below D and below its midpoint.
Long: At or after the backtest start AND opening range available AND within the entry session AND bar closed AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND sweep mode selected AND low is below the opening-range low AND close is above that low AND lower-wick fraction is at least the minimum AND, if enabled, close is above session VWAP AND, if enabled, the long trend filter passes AND, if enabled, stochastic K is above D and above its midpoint.
Short: At or after the backtest start AND opening range available AND within the entry session AND bar closed AND no active trade AND the optional one-trade-per-day limit is not reached AND sweep mode selected AND high is above the opening-range high AND close is below that high AND upper-wick fraction is at least the minimum AND, if enabled, close is below session VWAP AND, if enabled, the short trend filter passes AND, if enabled, stochastic K is below D and below its midpoint.
Calculation: An actual trade opens at the signal bar's close only if the distance from that close to its proposed stop is at least the minimum price increment; long has priority if both signals occur. Signal markings do not require this risk check. Reset daily trade and possible-alert state at the start of the opening-range session.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar AND low is at or below the long stop OR high is at or above the long target OR the management session has just ended. At session end use the previous bar's close; otherwise use the stop if both levels are hit, and the reached level if only one is hit.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar AND high is at or above the short stop OR low is at or below the short target OR the management session has just ended. At session end use the previous bar's close; otherwise use the stop if both levels are hit, and the reached level if only one is hit.
