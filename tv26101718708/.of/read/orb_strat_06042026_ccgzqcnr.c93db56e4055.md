Calculation: Opening-range high and low = high and low of the completed opening-range bar from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, during 9:30–9:45 AM Eastern.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × 100; use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick percentage = upper/lower wick length ÷ (high − low) × 100; use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe close and EMA = close and EMA of the last completed bar from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; previous EMA = EMA of the completed bar before it.
Long: During the entry session after the opening range is ready, when no position is open and the daily trade limit allows it, high reaches or exceeds the opening-range high plus the alert buffer, AND close is above VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled, AND the completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA (and that EMA is rising if slope confirmation is enabled); OR a confirmed bar closes above the opening-range high plus the breakout buffer after the previous close was at or below that level, is bullish, meets the minimum body-percentage threshold, passes enabled VWAP and higher-timeframe trend filters, and passes the stochastic filter if enabled; OR, when two-close confirmation is enabled, the next bar closes above that level and passes enabled VWAP and trend filters, and passes the stochastic filter if enabled; OR, when retest confirmation is enabled, after a qualifying breakout and within the allowed bars, a later bar's low reaches the breakout level and it closes above that level, is bullish, and passes enabled VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters; OR, in sweep mode, a confirmed bar's low is below the opening-range low and its close is above the opening-range low, its lower-wick percentage meets the minimum, and it passes enabled VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters.
Short: During the entry session after the opening range is ready, when no position is open and the daily trade limit allows it, low reaches or falls below the opening-range low minus the alert buffer, AND close is below VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled, AND the completed higher-timeframe close is below its EMA (and that EMA is falling if slope confirmation is enabled); OR a confirmed bar closes below the opening-range low minus the breakout buffer after the previous close was at or above that level, is bearish, meets the minimum body-percentage threshold, passes enabled VWAP and higher-timeframe trend filters, and passes the stochastic filter if enabled; OR, when two-close confirmation is enabled, the next bar closes below that level and passes enabled VWAP and trend filters, and passes the stochastic filter if enabled; OR, when retest confirmation is enabled, after a qualifying breakout and within the allowed bars, a later bar's high reaches the breakout level and it closes below that level, is bearish, and passes enabled VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters; OR, in sweep mode, a confirmed bar's high is above the opening-range high and its close is below the opening-range high, its upper-wick percentage meets the minimum, and it passes enabled VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters.
Exit: Long, low <= stop (opening-range low minus stop buffer in breakout mode; entry-bar low minus stop buffer in sweep mode) OR high >= target (entry price + initial risk × reward-to-risk) OR the manage session ends; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: Short, high >= stop (opening-range high plus stop buffer in breakout mode; entry-bar high plus stop buffer in sweep mode) OR low <= target (entry price − initial risk × reward-to-risk) OR the manage session ends; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, exit at the stop.
