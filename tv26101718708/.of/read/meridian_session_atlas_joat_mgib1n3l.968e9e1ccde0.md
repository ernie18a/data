Calculation: Session extremes = for each independently configured session window and timezone, the highest high and lowest low of its most recently completed session; use only outside that session and retain the extremes until the next session starts.
Calculation: Session sweep cooldown = for each session, current bar index minus the bar index of its most recent qualifying long or short sweep; initialize the previous sweep index to zero, update it on every qualifying sweep, and evaluate both directions on the same bar using the cooldown at the start of that bar.
Long: For any enabled session, session sweep cooldown >= cooldown parameter AND low < session low AND close > session low AND session low - low >= depth parameter × ATR (use high - low when ATR is unavailable) AND (body confirmation disabled OR close >= (open + close) / 2).
Short: For any enabled session, session sweep cooldown >= cooldown parameter AND high > session high AND close < session high AND high - session high >= depth parameter × ATR (use high - low when ATR is unavailable) AND (body confirmation disabled OR close <= (open + close) / 2).
