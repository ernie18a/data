Calculation: Reference range = choose the high and low of the last completed reference-period bar, updated at each new reference period, or the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in a configured session, locked on the first bar outside that session. The reference-period timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. The range is ready only when high > low; session ranges become unready when the session opens. The trading period resets at the start of each reference period for reference-period ranges and at each calendar-day boundary for session ranges.
Calculation: Average range = SMA of high minus low on the last completed reference-period bar of a separate configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; freeze its value when the reference range is set. Range ratio = reference range size / frozen average range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Directional trend alignment is true when the trend filter is disabled, the EMA is unavailable, or close is above the EMA for a long or below it for a short.
Calculation: Breakout trigger = in direct mode, close crosses the relevant range edge from inside or at the edge and penetrates it by at least a configured multiple of ATR. In retest mode, arm the first direction whose close crosses its range edge from inside or at the edge, retaining that edge; mark it retested when low <= armed upper edge + tolerance × ATR for a long or high >= armed lower edge - tolerance × ATR for a short, including on the arming bar. Cancel the arm if close < armed upper edge - tolerance × ATR for a long or close > armed lower edge + tolerance × ATR for a short. Trigger while armed and retested when close is beyond the armed edge in its breakout direction. Reset the arm when a range forms, a trading period resets, or an entry occurs.
Calculation: Strength score = clamp to a configured score range and round to configured precision the sum of a base score and tiered contributions for absolute candle body / candle range, candle range / ATR, volume / volume SMA, directional agreement of close changes over a shorter and a longer lookback, penetration beyond the relevant range edge / ATR, directional RSI posture, directional trend alignment, first entry of the trading period, and range ratio within configured bounds. Each tier, contribution, threshold and lookback is a parameter. When candle range is zero use a neutral body ratio; when ATR is not positive use zero for candle-range and penetration ratios; when volume or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is not positive use a neutral volume ratio.
Calculation: Entry risk = at entry close, take the absolute distance to a raw stop chosen as the opposite range edge plus an outward ATR buffer, the range midpoint plus an outward ATR buffer, or entry close plus an outward ATR multiple; clamp that distance between configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples. Freeze entry risk and entry price at entry. Stop = entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short; final target = entry price plus risk times the final target multiple for a long or minus it for a short.
Long: Reference range is ready AND long breakout trigger is true AND directional trend alignment is true AND (range filter is disabled OR range ratio is within its configured inclusive bounds) AND strength score >= minimum score AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR strength score >= strong threshold) AND entries in the trading period < configured maximum AND no position is active AND ATR is available AND no exit occurred on this bar.
Short: Reference range is ready AND short breakout trigger is true AND directional trend alignment is true AND (range filter is disabled OR range ratio is within its configured inclusive bounds) AND strength score >= minimum score AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR strength score >= strong threshold) AND entries in the trading period < configured maximum AND no position is active AND ATR is available AND no exit occurred on this bar AND the long entry condition is false.
Long: close >= session-anchored VWAP.
Short: close < session-anchored VWAP.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= final target OR a new trading period begins.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= final target OR a new trading period begins.
