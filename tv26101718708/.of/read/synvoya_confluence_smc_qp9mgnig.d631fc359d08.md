Calculation: Short-term swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than those of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete. Long-term swing high/low = the same calculation with a longer lookback than the short-term one.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = confirmed close (or high when wick confirmation is selected) above the active swing-high level; bearish structure break = confirmed close (or low when wick confirmation is selected) below the active swing-low level. In strict mode, require a minimum pivot-to-opposite-pivot leg, a later confirmed opposite pivot when inducement is required, a break beyond the level by an ATR buffer, and a break outside any active mother-candle range. After an upward break, set bullish structure bias and protect the latest pivot low; after a downward break, set bearish bias and protect the latest pivot high. In strict mode, use the protected opposite level for a break against current bias; otherwise use the active pivot level. Evaluate both short-term and long-term structure; a short-term break may also promote long-term structure when it exceeds the last long-term break level by the ATR promotion buffer.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low above the high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high below the low two bars earlier. Gap size must be at least ATR times the configured size threshold. The gap midpoint is the average of its bounds.
Calculation: Equal-high/equal-low liquidity level = the average price of two confirmed long-term swing highs/lows whose difference is within the configured tolerance; default tolerance is an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish breaker = a former bearish/bullish order block after a close beyond its midpoint; the breaker is invalidated by a close beyond its far zone edge.
Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = the chart makes a lower swing low while the correlated instrument makes a higher low; bearish SMT divergence = the chart makes a higher swing high while the correlated instrument makes a lower high. Compare corresponding confirmed swing points.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA bias = close above both the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs, or below both; neutral when between them. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: A confirmed short-term or long-term swing low forms; OR an upward bullish structure break occurs; OR a bullish order block forms as the last bearish candle before an upward long-term structure break; OR a bullish breaker exists after a bearish order block flips; OR a bullish fair value gap forms; OR a bearish fair value gap closes above its upper edge and becomes an inverse gap; OR price sweeps below an equal-low liquidity level (low below level AND close above level); OR a confirmed candle sweeps both the prior candle high and low (high above prior high AND low below prior low) and closes above its open; OR bullish SMT divergence occurs; OR higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish; OR higher-timeframe close is above both EMAs.
Short: A confirmed short-term or long-term swing high forms; OR a downward bearish structure break occurs; OR a bearish order block forms as the last bullish candle before a downward long-term structure break; OR a bearish breaker exists after a bullish order block flips; OR a bearish fair value gap forms; OR a bullish fair value gap closes below its lower edge and becomes an inverse gap; OR price sweeps above an equal-high liquidity level (high above level AND close below level); OR a confirmed candle sweeps both the prior candle high and low (high above prior high AND low below prior low) and closes below its open; OR bearish SMT divergence occurs; OR higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish; OR higher-timeframe close is below both EMAs.
