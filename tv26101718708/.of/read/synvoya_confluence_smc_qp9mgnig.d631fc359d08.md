Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low at a bar that is the corresponding extreme over configurable numbers of bars to its left and right; recognize it only after the right-hand bars have completed. Use separate shorter-lookback internal and longer-lookback swing pivots.
Calculation: Structure state = maintain separate internal and swing states, each initially neutral, with the latest unconsumed pivot high and low, protected high and low, and swing continuation extreme. Evaluate targets from the state before adding the current bar's newly confirmed pivots; use the updated pivots for leg and pullback checks. In classic mode the upward and downward targets are the latest unconsumed pivot high and low. In strict mode, an upward reversal targets the protected high when bearish, and a downward reversal targets the protected low when bullish; otherwise a swing continuation targets its locked continuation extreme if present, falling back to its latest pivot, while an internal continuation targets its latest pivot. A break uses either close or the corresponding high or low, as configured. Strict mode additionally requires that both directional targets are not broken on the same bar, the absolute distance from the target to the latest opposite pivot is at least an ATR multiple, the break exceeds the target by an ATR buffer, and, if enabled, the opposite pivot occurred after the target pivot. The internal minimum leg multiplier is half the swing multiplier; a missing swing opposite pivot falls back to the internal opposite pivot. If the optional mother-candle filter is enabled, when a bar is inside its predecessor and no mother exists or that predecessor extends beyond the existing mother, store the predecessor's high and low; require an upward break above the mother high or a downward break below the mother low, and clear the mother after a close outside it. On an accepted upward break set bias bullish, protect the latest opposite low, clear the protected high and consumed pivot high, and clear the swing continuation high; mirror for a downward break. After an internal countertrend break, lock the most extreme subsequently available swing pivot in the swing trend direction until the next swing break. A qualifying internal continuation break is also promoted to a swing break when swing bias agrees, no same-direction swing break occurred, and the internal target is beyond the last same-direction swing continuation break by an ATR buffer.
Calculation: Dashboard structure state = on each selected aggregation timeframe maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and low and protected levels, updating newly confirmed pivots before evaluating breaks. Use close for breaks and the same classic or strict target, ATR buffer, minimum leg, optional later opposite pivot, dual-break exclusion, and mother-candle rules as structure state, except the minimum leg is the distance between the latest high and low pivots. On a break, set the corresponding bias, protect the latest opposite pivot, clear the other protected level and the broken pivot. The state persists until the opposite break. For a higher timeframe use the bar still forming; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Equal-liquidity level = when a swing pivot is confirmed, first extend the oldest unswept same-side level within an ATR-based tolerance; otherwise pair it with the most recent matching same-side pivot among a configurable number of recent pivots and set the level to their average. Keep a configurable maximum number of levels, evicting the oldest; mark a level swept on its first qualifying sweep.
Calculation: Order block = on a swing structure break, find the most recent opposite candle within a configurable lookback; a bullish block spans that bearish candle's low to open, and a bearish block spans a candle with close greater than or equal to open from open to high. Keep a configurable maximum number of active blocks per side, evicting the oldest. A bullish block becomes a bearish breaker when close falls below its midpoint; a bearish block becomes a bullish breaker when close rises above its midpoint. A bullish breaker is invalidated below its bottom and a bearish breaker above its top.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish gap spans the high two bars ago to the current low; a bearish gap spans the current high to the low two bars ago. Require gap size at least an ATR multiple and keep a configurable maximum number of gaps, evicting the oldest. Starting after its creation bar, a bullish gap progresses from active to touched when low reaches its top, to mitigated when close falls below its midpoint, and to bearish inverted when close falls below its bottom; a bearish gap mirrors these rules. Multiple phases can occur on one bar. A mitigated gap is removed unless retention is enabled; an inverted bullish-origin gap is removed on close above its top, and an inverted bearish-origin gap on close below its bottom.

Short: A confirmed internal or swing pivot high exists.
Long: A newly confirmed internal or swing pivot high or low is greater than its preceding same-side pivot, OR no preceding same-side pivot exists.
Short: A newly confirmed internal or swing pivot high or low is less than or equal to its preceding same-side pivot.
Long: An upward internal or swing structure break is accepted.
Short: A downward internal or swing structure break is accepted.
Long: Swing structure bias is bullish.
Short: Swing structure bias is bearish.
Long: A bullish order block is created, OR close rises above the midpoint of an active bearish order block and the resulting bullish breaker has not been invalidated.
Short: A bearish order block is created, OR close falls below the midpoint of an active bullish order block and the resulting bearish breaker has not been invalidated.
Long: Low is greater than the high two bars ago AND the gap is at least the ATR-based minimum, OR a retained bearish fair value gap becomes bullish inverted.
Short: High is less than the low two bars ago AND the gap is at least the ATR-based minimum, OR a retained bullish fair value gap becomes bearish inverted.
Long: Low is below an unswept equal-low liquidity level AND close is above that level.
Short: High is above an unswept equal-high liquidity level AND close is below that level.
Long: The latest confirmed swing high is greater than the latest confirmed swing low AND close is below their midpoint.
Short: The latest confirmed swing high is greater than the latest confirmed swing low AND close is greater than or equal to their midpoint.

Short: High is greater than the previous high AND low is less than the previous low AND close is less than open.

Short: On a selected higher timeframe's still-forming bar, OR a selected lower timeframe's last completed bar within the chart bar, close is below both the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs.
