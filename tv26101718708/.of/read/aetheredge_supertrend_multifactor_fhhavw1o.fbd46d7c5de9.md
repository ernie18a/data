Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend bands = Calculate ATR and its simple moving average. Use a selectable fixed ATR multiplier, or multiply it by the ATR-to-average ratio clamped between lower and upper bounds; use a ratio of 1 when the ATR average is not positive. Basic upper and lower bands are the high-low midpoint plus and minus the resulting multiplier times ATR. Initialize each band to its basic band. Thereafter, replace the upper band with its basic band when the basic band is lower than the previous upper band OR the previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Replace the lower band with its basic band when the basic band is higher than the previous lower band OR the previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band.
Calculation: SuperTrend direction = Initialize bullish. Change from bearish to bullish when close is above the previous upper band; change from bullish to bearish when close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous direction. The SuperTrend line is the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish.
Long: SuperTrend direction is bullish AND previous SuperTrend direction is bearish
Short: SuperTrend direction is bearish AND previous SuperTrend direction is bullish
Exit: long, SuperTrend direction is bearish OR low <= SuperTrend line - ATR multiplied by a stop buffer
Exit: short, SuperTrend direction is bullish OR high >= SuperTrend line + ATR multiplied by a stop buffer
