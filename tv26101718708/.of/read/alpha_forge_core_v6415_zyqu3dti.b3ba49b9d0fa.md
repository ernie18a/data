Calculation: Directional coordinates = evaluate each formula twice, with direction s equal to long or short; for long use the original OHLC prices, and for short negate open and close and use negative low as high and negative high as low. All inequalities below use these directional coordinates. Previous means the previous chart bar. An event is a condition true now and false on the previous bar.
Calculation: Basis = EMA of close + (close − close lagged by floor((trend length − 1) / 2)), using the trend length. Width = highest ATR over a lookback proportional to the trend length, multiplied by a band multiplier. Upper band = basis + width; lower band = basis − width. Raw oscillator = (close − basis) / width, or zero when width is zero. Oscillator = EMA of raw oscillator; signal = EMA of oscillator with a separate smoothing length; histogram = oscillator − signal.
Calculation: Trend = initially neutral; change to bullish when close crosses above the upper band and bearish when close crosses below the lower band; otherwise retain the preceding trend. Strong bullish trend = basis > previous basis AND close > upper band. Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe basis AND higher-timeframe basis > its previous value; bearish under the reversed comparisons; otherwise neutral. Calculate the higher-timeframe basis from higher-timeframe bars using the same trend length. Select either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming; alternatively disable higher-timeframe influence, making its bias neutral.
Calculation: Structure = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, where a pivot is confirmed after equal numbers of bars on each side. A bullish raw break is close > latest pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high. A bullish structure break additionally requires close − pivot high > trend-length ATR times a break buffer AND the elapsed bars since either directional structure break >= a cooldown. A bearish break is the mirrored condition. Structure direction starts neutral and changes to the direction of each structure break. A bullish change of character is a bullish break while the preceding structure direction is bearish.
Calculation: Momentum confirmation = oscillator > signal AND histogram > previous histogram. Mature bullish momentum = oscillator > a positive momentum threshold AND momentum confirmation. Hot bullish expansion = bullish trend AND momentum confirmation AND close > basis AND absolute histogram > a momentum-threshold multiple AND absolute basis change > a trend-length ATR multiple. Chop = absolute raw oscillator < a threshold AND absolute histogram < a momentum-threshold multiple.
Calculation: Adaptive regime = calculate ADX and compare a shorter EMA of ATR with a longer EMA of the same ATR. The candidate is high-volatility trend when ADX >= its trend threshold AND absolute raw oscillator >= its trend threshold AND the shorter ATR EMA > the longer; ordinary trend when the first two conditions hold and the shorter ATR EMA <= the longer; range when the trend candidate is false AND (ADX < its range threshold OR chop is true); otherwise transition. Change the retained regime only after the same candidate persists for a confirmation lookback. When adaptive scoring is disabled, treat the regime as trend.
Calculation: Breakout = bullish trend AND bullish structure AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND close > upper band AND close > previous high AND momentum confirmation AND raw oscillator < breakout stretch limit AND chop is false. Impulse = bullish trend AND bullish structure AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND close > basis AND momentum confirmation AND histogram > zero AND ((close > previous close AND histogram > previous histogram) OR hot bullish expansion) AND raw oscillator < breakout stretch limit AND chop is false. Early reclaim = bullish trend AND bullish structure AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND close > basis AND previous close <= current basis AND momentum confirmation AND oscillator > zero AND raw oscillator < entry stretch limit AND chop is false. Each of these three setups has its own enable option.
Calculation: Reversal = previous oscillator < negative reversal-extreme threshold AND close > basis AND previous close <= current basis AND oscillator > signal AND histogram > previous histogram AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND close > open AND close − open > a directional fraction of high − low AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND strong bearish trend is false; in stricter mode also require a bullish change of character. The candle-body fraction may differ by direction.
Calculation: Premium continuation = bullish trend AND basis > previous basis AND mature bullish momentum AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND bullish structure AND close crosses above basis AND oscillator > zero AND raw oscillator < entry stretch limit AND (range blocking is disabled OR regime is not range). Premium setup = premium continuation OR reversal; in stricter mode this part also requires non-neutral trend, non-neutral enabled higher-timeframe bias, mature momentum in either direction, bullish trend and bullish structure. Add breakout OR impulse when the regime is trend, and add early reclaim regardless of regime.
Calculation: Directional score = sum configured weights for bullish trend, bullish higher-timeframe bias or a smaller neutral higher-timeframe contribution, bullish structure, mature bullish momentum or a smaller momentum-confirmation contribution, raw oscillator below the entry stretch limit, trend regime or a smaller transition contribution, and any of breakout, impulse, early reclaim or reversal. When higher-timeframe influence is disabled, omit its contribution and normalize the remaining available weight to the full score scale. Premium qualification = premium setup AND score >= premium threshold.
Calculation: Early countertrend condition = early signals enabled AND ((bearish trend AND momentum confirmation AND close > basis AND structure is not bullish) OR (hot bearish expansion AND momentum confirmation AND close > basis)) AND chop is false AND histogram > a momentum-threshold multiple AND oscillator > signal AND absolute basis change > a trend-length ATR multiple. The bearish condition is mirrored.
Calculation: Momentum turn = close > basis AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND histogram > previous histogram AND histogram > negative momentum-threshold multiple. Pullback turn = low touched or crossed basis within a turn lookback AND close > basis AND close > open AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND histogram > previous histogram. Microstructure = close > basis AND basis > previous basis AND low > lowest preceding low over a microstructure lookback. Reclaim = close > basis AND previous close <= current basis. Countertrend follow-through = an early bullish countertrend condition occurred within a follow-through lookback AND trend is bullish AND momentum turn AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND raw oscillator < breakout stretch limit.
Calculation: High expansion = breakout OR impulse OR hot bullish expansion OR (shorter ATR EMA > longer ATR EMA AND ADX >= an expansion threshold AND momentum confirmation AND close > basis). Continuation eligibility is selectable as always, trend regime only, trend regime OR high expansion, or trend regime OR (transition AND high expansion) OR (range AND high expansion AND shorter ATR EMA > longer ATR EMA AND ADX >= expansion threshold).
Calculation: Responsive setup = (bullish trend AND basis > previous basis AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND close > basis AND NOT (bearish structure AND strong bearish trend AND higher-timeframe influence is disabled or bearish) AND raw oscillator < breakout stretch limit AND (range blocking is disabled OR regime is not range) AND continuation eligibility) OR event of countertrend follow-through. Responsive qualification = responsive setup AND score >= responsive threshold; the responsive threshold is lower than the premium threshold. If both grades qualify, select premium.
Calculation: Initial stop = at the entry close, select no stop, an ATR-distance stop, a percentage-distance stop, or a structure stop. The long structure stop is the greater of (latest confirmed pivot low − entry ATR times a structure buffer, if that pivot is below entry; otherwise entry − entry ATR times the ATR stop multiplier) and (entry − entry ATR times the maximum structure-stop distance). Mirror for short. Planned reward-to-risk = entry ATR times the final-target multiplier divided by entry-to-stop distance; if there is no positive usable risk, use an effectively unlimited ratio. An optional minimum reward-to-risk filter applies to premium, responsive and early countertrend signals.
Calculation: Confirmed directional candidate = selected premium or responsive qualification AND the optional reward-to-risk filter AND, if directional confirmation is enabled, close >= previous close. Optionally require the chart bar to be closed. Enable each grade independently; if premium qualifies but is disabled, do not substitute responsive.
Calculation: Premium candidate = confirmed premium candidate AND elapsed bars since the last premium chart entry in either direction >= premium cooldown AND, when the opposite-cycle lock is enabled, the same-direction premium lock is clear. Set that lock on a same-direction premium chart entry; clear it on an opposite confirmed candidate that passes its final gate or an opposite change of character.
Calculation: Responsive arm event = bullish trend transition OR reclaim OR event of momentum turn OR event of countertrend follow-through; when responsive re-entry is enabled also allow events of pullback turn or microstructure. While flat, arm a responsive setup on a confirmed responsive candidate AND elapsed bars since the last responsive chart entry in either direction >= responsive cooldown AND same-direction rearm is ready AND an arm event. Store that bar's high, low and basis and cancel the opposite arm. Disarm when the setup age exceeds its expiry, trend turns bearish, close < stored low, enabled higher-timeframe bias turns bearish, or a trade is active.
Calculation: Responsive candidate = confirmed responsive candidate AND responsive cooldown passed AND same-direction rearm ready AND a bullish setup remains armed AND the bar is later than its arm bar AND (high − low) / current target ATR <= entry-candle limit AND max(0, close − basis) / current target ATR <= entry-distance limit AND ((close > stored high AND close > open AND histogram > previous histogram) OR (low <= stored basis + current target ATR times pullback buffer AND close > stored basis AND close > open AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND histogram > previous histogram)). Treat the ratios as effectively unlimited when target ATR <= zero. After a same-direction chart entry, rearm only while flat and on a later bar with low <= basis OR oscillator <= signal OR trend not bullish.
Calculation: Confirmed signal = premium candidate OR responsive candidate; suppress both directions if both occur on the same bar. Early signal = early countertrend condition AND optional reward-to-risk filter AND optional chart-bar close confirmation AND elapsed bars since the last early signal in either direction >= early cooldown AND neither confirmed signal occurs AND, when the early opposite-cycle lock is enabled, the same-direction early lock is clear. Suppress both early signals if both occur together. An early signal sets its direction's lock and clears the opposite lock; an opposite confirmed signal also clears the lock.
Calculation: Entry state = a confirmed signal opens its direction only while flat; an early signal opens its direction only while flat and early-grade tracking is enabled. A short chart entry still consumes cooldown and lock state when short execution is disabled. An early signal remains directional guidance when early-grade tracking is disabled. On an actual entry, store the entry close, entry bar, target ATR, initial stop, and three targets at increasing configured entry-ATR distances from entry.
Calculation: Protection = start with the initial stop. After favorable excursion since entry reaches an optional risk-reduction threshold measured in entry ATR, schedule a reduced-risk stop at entry minus a configured entry-ATR distance for the next bar; retain the tighter stop. After the selected first or second target is reached, optionally move the stop to entry starting on the following bar. After the second target is reached, optionally start a trail on the following bar: track the highest close or highest high since trailing began, subtract a configured multiple of entry ATR, and retain the highest resulting trail. The protective level is the tighter of stop and trail. Mirror all levels for short.
Long: trend = bullish.
Short: trend = bearish.
Long: mature bullish momentum = true.
Short: mature bearish momentum = true.
Long: bullish structure break = true.
Short: bearish structure break = true.
Long: bullish confirmed signal = true AND position is flat.
Short: bearish confirmed signal = true AND position is flat.
Long: bullish early signal = true AND (early-grade tracking is disabled OR position is flat).
Short: bearish early signal = true AND (early-grade tracking is disabled OR position is flat).
Long: bullish trend = true AND bullish structure = true AND bullish momentum confirmation = true AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND bullish confirmed signal = false AND bullish early signal = false.
Short: bearish trend = true AND bearish structure = true AND bearish momentum confirmation = true AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish AND bearish confirmed signal = false AND bearish early signal = false.
Long: previous oscillator < negative reversal-extreme threshold AND bullish momentum confirmation = true AND ((close > basis AND previous close <= current basis) OR (close > lower band AND previous close <= current lower band)) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND both confirmed signals = false AND bullish early signal = false.
Short: previous oscillator > reversal-extreme threshold AND bearish momentum confirmation = true AND ((close < basis AND previous close >= current basis) OR (close < upper band AND previous close >= current upper band)) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish AND both confirmed signals = false AND bearish early signal = false.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has low <= protective level OR a bar after entry has high >= the selected target OR the selected opposite-signal exit policy permits a bearish confirmed signal or bearish early signal. For a simultaneous stop and selected target, apply the configured stop-first or target-first priority; an opposite-signal exit is also valid on that bar.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has high >= protective level OR a bar after entry has low <= the selected target OR the selected opposite-signal exit policy permits a bullish confirmed signal or bullish early signal. For a simultaneous stop and selected target, apply the configured stop-first or target-first priority; an opposite-signal exit is also valid on that bar.
