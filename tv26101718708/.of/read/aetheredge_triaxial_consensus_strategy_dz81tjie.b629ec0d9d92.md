Calculation: Trend score = Clamp a weighted sum of: the difference between the counts of rising and falling adjacent pairs in five progressively longer close EMAs; the difference between counts of rising and falling pairs of the two latest confirmed pivot highs and two latest confirmed pivot lows; and a signed ADX score whose magnitude is larger above a higher ADX threshold, smaller above a lower threshold, and zero otherwise, with its sign positive when +DI > -DI and negative otherwise. Confirm each pivot using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Momentum score = Clamp a weighted sum of an EMA-smoothed RSI centered and scaled around its midpoint and the MACD histogram divided by ATR and scaled. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Volatility score = Clamp the sum of a scaled, clamped close change over a lookback divided by ATR and a signed candle component. The candle component is nonzero only when Bollinger Band width exceeds a multiple of its longer SMA, ATR divided by its longer SMA is between lower and upper bounds, and absolute candle body divided by high minus low exceeds a threshold; its sign is the sign of close minus open. Division by zero returns zero.
Calculation: Axis direction = Positive, negative, or neutral according to whether an axis score is above a positive direction threshold, below its negative, or between them. Use a separate, smaller absolute threshold for consensus lean.
Calculation: Regime = Trend when ADX exceeds its trend threshold and enough adjacent EMA pairs are ordered in either direction; otherwise volatile when ATR divided by its longer SMA exceeds its upper threshold; otherwise quiet when Bollinger Band width is below a multiple of its longer SMA or the ATR ratio is below its lower threshold; otherwise range. Apply the corresponding configurable multiplier to each axis weight.
Calculation: Adaptive axis weight = Initialize each axis reliability equally. When enabled, record a sample at close whenever trend or momentum direction changes to a nonneutral direction, and on every bar with a nonneutral volatility direction. After the outcome horizon, process samples newest first: success is whether the direction-signed close change from the sample price is at least a multiple of current ATR. Update the axis reliability by an exponentially weighted step toward success or failure, clamped to its bounds; map reliability linearly to the configured minimum and maximum weights. When adaptation is disabled, use equal base weights. Multiply by the regime multiplier and set disabled axes' weights to zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe verdict = On each of two higher timeframes, using the last completed bar, return positive when the shorter close EMA exceeds the longer close EMA and RSI exceeds its midpoint; return negative when both comparisons reverse; otherwise return neutral.
Calculation: Final score = Divide the sum of each axis score times its weight by the sum of weights, returning zero on division by zero. For each enabled higher-timeframe verdict, add a positive bonus if its nonzero direction matches the raw score's sign, subtract that bonus if the verdict is nonzero and does not match, and otherwise add zero. Clamp the result to the score bounds.
Calculation: Consensus = Among enabled axes, count scores above the positive lean threshold and below its negative. Ignore other scores. Spread passes if fewer than two axes lean or the difference between the highest and lowest leaning scores is at most a multiple of the tolerance. Positive consensus requires at least one positive lean and more positive than negative leans; negative consensus is symmetric.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = Divide the preset threshold range into equally spaced candidates and initialize their quality scores equally. On each bar, select the highest-quality candidate, breaking ties in favor of the lowest candidate; optionally select a candidate instead using a deterministic pseudo-random function of bar index and an exploration probability. When an unsuppressed strong signal fires, record its direction, close, and selected candidate. After the outcome horizon, process samples newest first and update the corresponding quality score by an exponentially weighted step toward success or failure, using the same ATR-based outcome test as adaptive axis weights. Select the threshold before resolving samples on that bar. When adaptation is disabled, use the midpoint of the preset range.
Calculation: Strong signal lock = Initially neutral. An unsuppressed strong long or short requires the lock to differ from that direction. After evaluating signals on each bar, set the lock to the direction of any strong condition; reset it to neutral when neither strong condition holds and the absolute final score is below the adaptive threshold minus a reset margin.
Calculation: Exit levels = At entry, fix the initial stop at entry price minus direction times entry-bar ATR times a stop multiplier, and fix progressively more distant profit targets at entry price plus direction times the initial stop distance times progressively larger reward multiples. Each enabled target has its own stop and limit order; the first and second reserve configured partial quantities and the last reserves the remainder. Orders fill when the bar reaches their stop or limit, subject to order activation and intrabar fill ordering.
Calculation: Trailing stop = Initialize at the initial stop. After the first target has been touched while a position remains open, optionally reset it to entry price. While trailing is enabled and momentum direction agrees with the position, update it to the more protective of its current value and close minus position direction times current ATR times a trailing multiplier. A changed trailing stop is submitted as a separate stop order for the remaining unreserved position and becomes active after its calculation.
Long: Final score >= adaptive threshold AND trend score > positive lean threshold AND positive consensus AND spread passes AND strong signal lock differs from long.
Short: Final score <= negative adaptive threshold AND trend score < negative lean threshold AND negative consensus AND spread passes AND strong signal lock differs from short.
Long: Final score >= adaptive threshold times a configurable weak-signal fraction.
Short: Final score <= negative adaptive threshold times a configurable weak-signal fraction.
Long: Any trend, momentum, or volatility score > its directional display threshold.
Short: Any trend, momentum, or volatility score < the negative directional display threshold.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA for any of the displayed EMA pairs.
Short: Shorter EMA <= longer EMA for any of the displayed EMA pairs.
Long: Either higher-timeframe verdict > neutral.
Short: Either higher-timeframe verdict < neutral.
Exit: long, low <= an active stop OR high >= an active profit target.
Exit: short, high >= an active stop OR low <= an active profit target.
