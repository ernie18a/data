Calculation: RSI crossunder = RSI of closes on a selectable timeframe was greater than or equal to the threshold on its previous bar and is below the threshold on its current bar. On a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; on a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar in the chart bar; on the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Averaging-order threshold = base entry price × (1 + cumulative deviation), where cumulative deviation for the next unfilled order is the sum of a first deviation and subsequent deviations multiplied successively by a step multiplier.
Calculation: Average entry price = total quote amount of the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order's quote amount divided by its fill close. Each successive averaging-order amount is the preceding amount multiplied by a size multiplier.
Calculation: Trailing low = the lowest close since entry among bars whose close is at or below the average entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction), evaluated using the average entry price on each respective bar.
Short: Position is flat AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR position is flat AND datetime is within the inclusive active window; AND RSI crossunder is true.
Short: Position is short AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR position is short AND datetime is within the inclusive active window; AND the number of filled averaging orders is below the configured maximum AND close is greater than or equal to the averaging-order threshold. Fill at most one averaging order per bar at close, before evaluating exits.
Exit: short, the optional active-window filter is disabled OR datetime is within the inclusive active window; AND close is greater than or equal to average entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, the optional active-window filter is disabled OR datetime is within the inclusive active window; AND close is less than average entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) AND trailing low exists AND close is greater than or equal to trailing low × (1 + trailing fraction).
