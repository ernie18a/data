Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) when high > low; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Aggression ratio = absolute estimated delta / SMA of absolute estimated delta over the preceding delta-lookback bars when that SMA > 0; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Delta shock = estimated delta − previous bar's estimated delta.
Calculation: Shock ratio = absolute delta shock / SMA of absolute delta shock over the preceding shock-lookback bars when that SMA > 0; otherwise 0.
