Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in the selected price source over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero. Optionally smooth with an EMA.
Calculation: Adaptive average = initialize to the selected price source; thereafter add (source minus previous average) multiplied by the square of (efficiency ratio × (fast smoothing constant minus slow smoothing constant) + slow smoothing constant), where each smoothing constant is 2 / (its period + 1) and the fast period is shorter than the slow period. The anchor is the previous bar's adaptive average.
Calculation: Dead-zone width = ATR × dead-zone multiplier × (2 − efficiency ratio). Bull regime begins after a configurable number of consecutive closes above anchor + dead-zone width; bear regime begins after the same number of consecutive closes below anchor − dead-zone width. Each regime persists until the opposite regime is confirmed; initially neither is active.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR floored at a configurable positive price increment. On each bar, append (anchor − low) / safe ATR to the buy penetration history if low < anchor, and append (high − anchor) / safe ATR to the sell penetration history if high > anchor; retain only the most recent configurable number of qualifying observations on each side, including the current bar. Each side's learned multiplier starts at a configurable initial value and, once its history reaches a configurable minimum count, becomes its mean penetration × sensitivity, clamped between configurable minimum and maximum multipliers.
Calculation: Buy band = anchor − ((1 − learning weight) × dead-zone width × buy-band multiplier + learning weight × safe ATR × buy learned multiplier). Sell band = anchor + ((1 − learning weight) × dead-zone width × sell-band multiplier + learning weight × safe ATR × sell learned multiplier).
Calculation: Pressure z-score = the rolling z-score of pressure, using population standard deviation and zero when that deviation is zero. Pressure = percentage difference between close and cost basis, divided by cost basis, multiplied by current volume / its SMA, or by 1 when the volume SMA is not positive. Cost basis is a weighted average of longer-lookback and shorter-lookback rolling volume-weighted typical prices, with greater weight on the shorter lookback; use current typical price if a window's volume is zero and use zero percentage difference if cost basis is zero.
Calculation: Double money-flow oscillator = EMA(2 × smoothed MFI − second-pass MFI) − 50. Smoothed MFI is an EMA of MFI calculated from typical price and volume. For the second pass, sum smoothed MFI on bars where it rises and separately where it falls over a lookback; apply the MFI formula to their ratio, using a configurable fallback ratio when the falling sum is zero. Use the same fallback ratio when negative money flow in the first pass is zero.
Calculation: Buy signal = low <= buy band AND bear regime active AND (buy separation filter disabled OR no previous buy signal in this bear phase OR absolute difference between low and the previous buy signal's close >= ATR × buy separation multiplier) AND (efficiency filter disabled OR efficiency ratio <= buy threshold) AND (pain filter disabled OR pressure z-score <= negative override threshold OR lowest pressure z-score over the pain lookback <= negative capitulation threshold) AND (money-flow filter disabled OR double money-flow oscillator <= its threshold) AND (lower-low filter disabled OR no previous buy signal in this bear phase OR low < lowest buy-signal low in this bear phase). Clear the previous buy price and buy-signal low when bull regime begins; update them on every buy signal, including signals that do not execute.
Calculation: Sell signal = high >= sell band AND bull regime active AND (sell cooldown disabled OR no prior sell signal in the current bull phase OR bars since the last sell signal >= rounded(base cooldown × (1 + min(maximum cooldown multiplier − 1, max(0, (close − anchor) / safe ATR) × cooldown scale)))) AND (pain filter disabled OR pressure z-score >= override threshold OR highest pressure z-score over the pain lookback >= euphoria threshold) AND (higher-high filter disabled OR no previous sell signal in this bull phase OR high > highest sell-signal high in this bull phase). Clear the sell-signal count while bear regime is active and clear the sell-signal high when bear regime begins; update sell-signal bar, count and high on every sell signal, including signals that do not execute.
Long: low <= buy band
Short: low > buy band AND high >= sell band
Long: buy signal = true
Short: sell signal = true
Exit: long, sell signal = true
