Calculation: Swing high and low = the most recent high and low, respectively, that equal the highest high or lowest low over the swing lookback including the current bar and whose distance from the opposite rolling extreme is at least an ATR multiple; retain each value and its bar index until replaced. A valid swing requires both indices to be greater than zero. An upswing has a more recent swing high than swing low; a downswing has a swing high no more recent than the swing low.
Calculation: Retracement levels = swing high minus each enabled retracement fraction times (swing high minus swing low). When multiple levels are touched, select the level with the smallest retracement fraction.
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci bounce = valid upswing with both swing points at least a minimum age, low within an ATR-multiple distance of an enabled retracement level, and either (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) at least a wick threshold or close > open AND close > selected touched level; the wick ratio is zero when high = low.
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci rejection = valid downswing with both swing points at least a minimum age, high within an ATR-multiple distance of an enabled retracement level, and either (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) at least a wick threshold or close < open AND close < selected touched level; the wick ratio is zero when high = low.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of volume × (high + low + close) / 3 divided by cumulative volume since the start of the day; use close when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars where close > open minus volume on bars where close < open; unchanged when close = open.
Calculation: Buy volume ratio = volume on the current bar if close > open, otherwise zero, divided by the greater of a positive denominator floor and volume on the current bar if close < open, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = order flow enabled AND close > daily VWAP AND ((cumulative volume delta > its SMA AND close < previous close AND cumulative volume delta > its previous value) OR buy volume ratio > an upper threshold).
Calculation: Bearish order flow = order flow enabled AND close < daily VWAP AND ((cumulative volume delta < its SMA AND close > previous close AND cumulative volume delta < its previous value) OR buy volume ratio < a lower threshold).
Calculation: Bullish ribbon = at least a threshold number of adjacent pairs in a sequence of moving averages with increasing lookbacks have the shorter-lookback average > the longer-lookback average. Bearish ribbon uses < instead. Use the same configurable EMA, SMA, or HMA type for every average.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish filter = higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe short EMA; bearish filter reverses both comparisons. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Long filter = (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish filter) AND (ribbon disabled OR bullish ribbon) AND (order flow disabled OR close > daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci disabled OR valid upswing) AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar opens within the configured trading session).
Calculation: Short filter = (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish filter) AND (ribbon disabled OR bearish ribbon) AND (order flow disabled OR close < daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci disabled OR swing is not an upswing) AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar opens within the configured trading session).
Calculation: Long confluence score = sum of the truth values of (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish filter), (ribbon disabled OR bullish ribbon), (order flow disabled OR close > daily VWAP), (Fibonacci disabled OR valid upswing), and ADX > its threshold. Short confluence score uses the corresponding bearish conditions and swing not an upswing, with the same ADX condition.
Calculation: Directional volume share = sum of volume on bars with close > open divided by the sum of volume on bars with close > open or close < open over the volume-pressure lookback; use a neutral share when that sum is zero.
Calculation: Timeframe trend = short EMA and long EMA of close and ATR calculated on each selected timeframe; on a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time, and on a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar. The timeframe is neutral when abs(short EMA - long EMA) < an ATR multiple.
Long: Long filter AND ((ADX > threshold AND short EMA crosses above long EMA AND close > longer-term filter EMA AND volume > an SMA of volume multiplied by a threshold) OR (close > the previous bar's rolling highest high AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a threshold AND close > longer-term filter EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above that threshold)).
Short: Short filter AND ((ADX > threshold AND short EMA crosses below long EMA AND close < longer-term filter EMA AND volume > an SMA of volume multiplied by a threshold) OR (close < the previous bar's rolling lowest low AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a threshold AND close < longer-term filter EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below that threshold)).
Long: Fibonacci enabled AND bullish Fibonacci bounce.
Short: Fibonacci enabled AND bearish Fibonacci rejection.
Long: Long confluence score > short confluence score.
Short: Short confluence score > long confluence score.
Long: Bullish ribbon.
Short: Bearish ribbon AND bullish ribbon is false.
Long: Bullish order flow.
Short: Bearish order flow AND bullish order flow is false.
Long: Valid upswing.
Short: Valid upswing is false.
Long: Directional volume share > upper threshold AND the volume-pressure lookback is available.
Short: Directional volume share < lower threshold AND the volume-pressure lookback is available.
Long: Timeframe trend is not neutral AND timeframe short EMA > timeframe long EMA.
Short: Timeframe trend is not neutral AND timeframe short EMA <= timeframe long EMA.
Long: Candle-gradient setting enabled AND RSI > upper threshold AND close > open.
Short: Candle-gradient setting enabled AND RSI < lower threshold AND close < open.
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close - signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR trailing disabled AND high >= signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR × take-profit multiplier OR trailing enabled AND high reaches entry price + signal-bar ATR × trailing activation multiplier in ticks AND subsequent low <= highest price since activation - signal-bar ATR × trailing offset multiplier in ticks.
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR trailing disabled AND low <= signal-bar close - signal-bar ATR × take-profit multiplier OR trailing enabled AND low reaches entry price - signal-bar ATR × trailing activation multiplier in ticks AND subsequent high >= lowest price since activation + signal-bar ATR × trailing offset multiplier in ticks.
