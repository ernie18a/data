Calculation: Fast and slow trend EMAs = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback; trend filter EMA = longer-lookback exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages of close on a higher timeframe, with fast lookback shorter than slow; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA.
Calculation: Ribbon alignment = at least the configured threshold minus one of seven adjacent comparisons among eight moving averages of close, ordered from shortest to longest lookback; bullish when shorter-lookback averages exceed the next longer ones, bearish when they are below.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > its moving average.
Calculation: Volume-weighted average price = cumulative sum of volume × (high + low + close) / 3 since the start of the day, divided by cumulative volume since the start of the day.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on up-close bars minus volume on down-close bars.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = (cumulative volume delta > its moving average and close < prior close and cumulative volume delta > prior cumulative volume delta, or up-bar volume / max(down-bar volume, 1) > 1 + configured threshold) and (close crosses above VWAP or close > VWAP). Bearish order flow = (cumulative volume delta < its moving average and close > prior close and cumulative volume delta < prior cumulative volume delta, or up-bar volume / max(down-bar volume, 1) < 1 - configured threshold) and (close crosses below VWAP or close < VWAP).
Calculation: Fibonacci swing high/low = latest high/low that is respectively the highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback plus one bars, and whose distance from the opposite extreme over that window is at least the configured ATR multiple; retain each latest qualifying value and its bar. A valid swing has both bars after the first bar; it is an upswing when the swing-high bar is later than the swing-low bar. Fibonacci retracements = swing high minus 38.2%, 50%, or 61.8% of the swing-high-to-low range. A level is touched when the bar low (long) or high (short) is within configured ATR multiple × ATR of it. Ignore levels disabled by configuration. Require both swing bars to be at least two bars old.
Calculation: Long Fibonacci bounce = lower-wick fraction of bar range >= configured minimum, or close > nearest touched retracement and close > open. Short Fibonacci rejection = upper-wick fraction of bar range >= configured minimum, or close < nearest touched retracement and close < open.
Long: Fibonacci swing is an upswing AND a valid retracement is touched AND a long Fibonacci bounce.
Short: Fibonacci swing is not an upswing AND a valid retracement is touched AND a short Fibonacci rejection.
Long: position is flat AND inside the configured trading session (or the time filter is disabled) AND higher-timeframe filter is disabled or bullish AND ribbon filter is disabled or bullish AND order-flow filter is disabled or bullish order flow or close > VWAP AND Fibonacci filter is disabled or a bullish Fibonacci signal or an upswing AND (ADX > threshold AND fast trend EMA crosses above slow trend EMA AND fast trend EMA > slow trend EMA AND close > trend filter EMA AND volume spike OR close > prior-bar highest high over the breakout lookback AND volume spike AND close > trend filter EMA OR ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI crosses above oversold level).
Short: position is flat AND inside the configured trading session (or the time filter is disabled) AND higher-timeframe filter is disabled or bearish AND ribbon filter is disabled or bearish AND order-flow filter is disabled or bearish order flow or close < VWAP AND Fibonacci filter is disabled or a bearish Fibonacci signal or a downswing AND (ADX > threshold AND fast trend EMA crosses below slow trend EMA AND fast trend EMA < slow trend EMA AND close < trend filter EMA AND volume spike OR close < prior-bar lowest low over the breakout lookback AND volume spike AND close < trend filter EMA OR ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI crosses below overbought level).
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR × stop-loss multiple OR (trailing enabled AND price has risen by the configured activation distance from entry and low reaches the trailing stop, set at the highest price since entry minus ATR × trailing multiple) OR (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple).
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiple OR (trailing enabled AND price has fallen by the configured activation distance from entry and high reaches the trailing stop, set at the lowest price since entry plus ATR × trailing multiple) OR (trailing disabled AND low <= entry price - ATR × take-profit multiple).
