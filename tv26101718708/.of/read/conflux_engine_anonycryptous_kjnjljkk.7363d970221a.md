Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after the configured left and right lookbacks; retain the latest two of each, and update the higher-high, lower-high, higher-low, and lower-low states when a pivot confirms.
Calculation: Bull score = sum of configured weights for: bullish structure when either the latest pivot high is higher than its predecessor or the latest pivot low is higher than its predecessor, and close is above the latest pivot low; rising OBV linear-regression slope; momentum points proportional to the fraction of these conditions met (close above KAMA, RSI above 50, Williams %R above its bullish threshold); a low sweep (low below latest pivot low and close back above it); volatility points when ATR divided by its moving average is within the configured sweet spot and bullish momentum leads bearish momentum with at least two bullish momentum conditions; session points proportional to close's normalized distance below the developing day's range midpoint, capped at the session weight; and the configured bonus when close is above the 50-period EMA of a higher-timeframe bar (uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming).
Calculation: Bear score = sum of configured weights for: bearish structure when either the latest pivot high is lower than its predecessor or the latest pivot low is lower than its predecessor, and close is below the latest pivot high; falling OBV linear-regression slope; momentum points proportional to the fraction of these conditions met (close below KAMA, RSI below 50, Williams %R below its bearish threshold); a high sweep (high above latest pivot high and close back below it); volatility points when ATR divided by its moving average is within the configured sweet spot and bearish momentum leads bullish momentum with at least two bearish momentum conditions; session points proportional to close's normalized distance above the developing day's range midpoint, capped at the session weight; and the configured bonus when close is below the 50-period EMA of a higher-timeframe bar (uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming).
Long: Bull score >= configured signal threshold AND Bull score - Bear score >= configured directional lead AND all enabled filters pass (ADX >= configured minimum; close above VWMA; Supertrend bullish; fastest ribbon HMA above slowest ribbon HMA; and, if enabled, current bar is within the configured session) AND previous bar did not meet this long condition.
Short: Bear score >= configured signal threshold AND Bear score - Bull score >= configured directional lead AND all enabled filters pass (ADX >= configured minimum; close below VWMA; Supertrend bearish; fastest ribbon HMA below slowest ribbon HMA; and, if enabled, current bar is within the configured session) AND previous bar did not meet this short condition.
Exit: long, high >= target price OR low <= stop price
Exit: short, low <= target price OR high >= stop price
