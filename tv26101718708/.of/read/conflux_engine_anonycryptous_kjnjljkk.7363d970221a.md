Calculation: Confirmed pivots = swing highs and lows confirmed after equal numbers of bars on each side; retain the latest and previous confirmed pivot of each type. At the first pivot, compare against the source's initialization defaults.
Calculation: Adaptive average = recursively update the prior average toward close by the squared smoothing coefficient; the coefficient interpolates between faster and slower EMA smoothing coefficients using absolute close change over the lookback divided by cumulative absolute one-bar close changes since the series began. Initialize the average to close.
Calculation: Momentum counts = for long, count close above the adaptive average, RSI above its midpoint, and Williams %R above an upper threshold; for short, count close below the adaptive average, RSI below its midpoint, and Williams %R below a lower threshold.
Calculation: Directional pillar scores = structure weight when the latest confirmed high OR low is respectively higher or lower than its preceding same-type pivot AND close is respectively above the latest pivot low or below the latest pivot high; volume weight when the linear-regression slope of OBV is respectively positive or negative; momentum weight multiplied by the corresponding momentum count divided by the number of momentum tests; liquidity weight when low is below the latest pivot low AND close is above it for long, or high is above the latest pivot high AND close is below it for short; volatility weight when ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA is between lower and upper thresholds AND the corresponding momentum count exceeds the opposite count AND meets a minimum count; session weight multiplied by the capped absolute distance of close from the developing daily range midpoint divided by half the range when close is respectively below or above the midpoint and session scoring is enabled; higher-timeframe weight when enabled and chart close is respectively above or below the higher-timeframe EMA. Otherwise each component is zero. The daily range uses the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming; the higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the bar still forming in real time.
Calculation: Directional total = sum of the corresponding directional pillar scores.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = freeze at entry the ATR, latest confirmed pivot, Supertrend, slowest ribbon HMA, and ADX regime. Select stop and target multipliers by whether entry ADX is below a lower threshold, between the thresholds, or at or above the upper threshold. The target is entry price plus the target multiplier times entry ATR for a long, and minus it for a short. The stop is, according to the selected mode, entry price minus or plus the stop multiplier times entry ATR, the latest pivot low or high with that ATR stop as fallback, entry Supertrend, or entry slowest ribbon HMA, respectively for long or short. The selected stop remains fixed after entry.
Long: Long total >= signal threshold AND long total minus short total >= directional lead AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured UTC session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (VWMA filter disabled OR close > VWMA) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend is bullish) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR shorter-lookback HMA > longer-lookback HMA).
Short: Short total >= signal threshold AND short total minus long total >= directional lead AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured UTC session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (VWMA filter disabled OR close < VWMA) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend is bearish) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR shorter-lookback HMA < longer-lookback HMA).
Long: Shorter-lookback HMA > longer-lookback HMA.
Short: Shorter-lookback HMA < longer-lookback HMA.
Long: Any displayed long pillar score > its corresponding short pillar score OR long total > short total.
Short: Any displayed short pillar score > its corresponding long pillar score OR short total >= long total.
Exit: long, high >= entry target OR low <= entry stop.
Exit: short, low <= entry target OR high >= entry stop.
