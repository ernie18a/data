Calculation: Trend Template pass = close above short-, intermediate-, and long-term SMAs; short-term SMA above intermediate- and long-term SMAs; intermediate-term SMA above long-term SMA; long-term SMA above its value at an earlier lookback; close at least a configured fraction above the rolling 52-week low and at least a configured fraction of the rolling 52-week high. Use daily bars: higher than an intraday chart and lower than a chart above daily; use the current daily bar (forming live, completed in historical data).
Calculation: VCP score = weighted sum of points for base depth within its configured range; shorter rolling high-low range contracted relative to the next longer range and medium range contracted relative to the longer range; shorter ATR contracted relative to medium ATR and medium ATR below longer ATR; current relative volume below its dry-up threshold and short-term average volume divided by medium-term average volume below its threshold; and right-side tightness, defined as the shorter-range contraction condition. Use progressively longer lookbacks for the short-, medium-, and long-term calculations. Use the current daily bar.
Calculation: Pivot = highest high over the configured lookback, excluding the current daily bar. Pivot distance = (close / Pivot − 1) × 100. Use the current daily bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current daily volume divided by its medium-term average daily volume. Use the current daily bar.
Long: Trend Template pass AND VCP score at least its minimum AND close above Pivot AND close no farther above Pivot than the maximum chase distance AND relative volume at least the breakout threshold.
Long: Trend Template pass AND VCP score at least its minimum AND close at or below Pivot AND close no farther below Pivot than the READY-zone distance.
