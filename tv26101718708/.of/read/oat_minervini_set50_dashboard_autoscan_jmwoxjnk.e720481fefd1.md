Calculation: Analysis bars = bars on the analysis timeframe; when it is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed analysis bar; when it is lower, use the last available analysis bar within the chart bar. All calculations below use analysis bars.
Calculation: Trend pass = close > intermediate SMA AND close > long SMA AND intermediate SMA > long SMA AND long SMA > its value at a prior lookback AND short SMA > intermediate SMA AND short SMA > long SMA AND close >= lowest low over a lookback multiplied by a threshold AND close >= highest high over the same lookback multiplied by a threshold AND close > short SMA.
Calculation: VCP score = sum of the following points when each condition is true: 20 if base depth is between its minimum and maximum thresholds inclusive; 27.5 if short range < intermediate range multiplied by a threshold; 12.5 if intermediate range < long range multiplied by another threshold; 10 if short ATR < intermediate ATR multiplied by a threshold; 10 if intermediate ATR < long ATR; 20 if relative volume < its dry-up threshold AND short volume SMA / long volume SMA < its dry-up threshold. Base depth = (highest high - lowest low) / highest high over the base lookback; each range = (highest high - lowest low) / close over its respective lookback. The short, intermediate, and long range and ATR lookbacks increase in that order; the short volume SMA lookback is shorter than the long volume SMA lookback. Undefined conditions contribute zero points.
Calculation: Pivot = highest high over a lookback ending at the previous analysis bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / long volume SMA, provided the long volume SMA > 0.
Long: Trend pass AND VCP score >= minimum score AND pivot > 0 AND close > pivot AND close / pivot - 1 <= maximum chase threshold AND relative volume >= breakout volume threshold.
