Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the required number of bars on both sides, confirmed when the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = short-period EMA of close > long-period EMA of close, evaluated on each selected timeframe; for a higher timeframe use its last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use its last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Long: A confirmed pivot high > the preceding confirmed pivot high.
Long: A confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Short: A confirmed pivot high <= the preceding confirmed pivot high.
Short: A confirmed pivot low <= the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Long: Timeframe bias is true.
Short: Timeframe bias is false.
Long: The number of selected timeframes with bullish bias >= the number with bearish bias.
Short: The number of selected timeframes with bullish bias < the number with bearish bias.
Long: Low > high two chart bars ago AND this is the first bullish or bearish fair value gap in the specified daily New York time window.
Short: High < low two chart bars ago AND this is the first bullish or bearish fair value gap in the specified daily New York time window.
