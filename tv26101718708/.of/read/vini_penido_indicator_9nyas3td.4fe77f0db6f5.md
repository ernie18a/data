Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over equal lookbacks before and after the candidate bar; confirmation occurs after the right-side lookback.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current bar low > high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current bar high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: EMA 9 and EMA 21 = exponential moving averages of close, calculated on each requested timeframe above or below the chart timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Confirmed swing high > previous confirmed swing high
Long: Confirmed swing low > previous confirmed swing low
Long: EMA 9 > EMA 21 on a requested timeframe above or below the chart timeframe
Long: Bullish fair value gap AND New York time >= 08:30 AND New York time < 09:30 AND no earlier fair value gap of either direction has occurred in this window that day
Long: Bullish fair value gap AND New York time >= 09:30 AND no earlier fair value gap of either direction has occurred since 09:30 that day
Short: Confirmed swing high < previous confirmed swing high
Short: Confirmed swing low < previous confirmed swing low
Short: EMA 9 <= EMA 21 on a requested timeframe above or below the chart timeframe
Short: Bearish fair value gap AND New York time >= 08:30 AND New York time < 09:30 AND no earlier fair value gap of either direction has occurred in this window that day
Short: Bearish fair value gap AND New York time >= 09:30 AND no earlier fair value gap of either direction has occurred since 09:30 that day
