Calculation: Signed efficiency ratio = (close − close directional-window bars ago) / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that window; use 0 when the denominator is no greater than the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Directional gate = maximum of the directional floor and the rolling mean of absolute signed efficiency ratio plus 0.5 times its population standard deviation, including the current bar.
Calculation: Trend direction = +1 when signed efficiency ratio > directional gate AND absolute directional-window close displacement / max(ATR, minimum price increment) >= minimum displacement; −1 when signed efficiency ratio < −directional gate AND the same displacement condition holds; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Commitment = sign(close − open, with equality positive) times either (absolute candle body / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) × min(volume / max(volume SMA, 1), 3) / 3 in volume mode when volume > 0, or 2 × absolute(close − (high + low) / 2) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) in wick mode or when volume is missing or nonpositive. Select volume or wick mode as a parameter; the original automatic mode also requires instrument type, which is absent from the data fields.
Calculation: Failure conditions for nonzero trend direction d = velocity condition d × (close − previous close) < 0; wick condition trend-side wick / max(absolute candle body, minimum price increment) >= wick-ratio threshold; commitment condition (mean − d × current commitment) / sample standard deviation >= anomaly threshold, using only positive d × commitment values from the preceding statistics-lookback bars and setting the deviation to 0 when the standard deviation is 0; range condition max(high − low, minimum price increment) >= range multiplier × ATR. The trend-side wick is high − max(open, close) for d = +1 and min(open, close) − low for d = −1.
Calculation: Failure validity = all four failure conditions in conservative mode; at least three in balanced mode; velocity condition AND at least two conditions in aggressive mode.
Calculation: Failure score = absolute(close − previous close) / max(ATR, minimum price increment) × (1 + min(trend-side wick / max(absolute candle body, minimum price increment), 5) / 5) × (1 + min(max(commitment deviation, 0), 3) / 3) × (1 + min(max(high − low, minimum price increment) / ATR, 3) / 3) when failure validity holds; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Pressure = failure score formula without the failure-validity restriction, but with its velocity factor halved when the velocity condition is false; use 0 when trend direction is 0. Pressure baseline = rolling mean plus baseline multiplier × population standard deviation of pressure, including the current bar.
Calculation: Entry levels = at the signal-bar close, freeze ATR and calculate stop distance = stop multiplier × ATR and target distance = target multiplier × ATR × (1 + min(failure score / max(pressure baseline, 0.0001), 1.5) × score nudge); long stop and target are entry price minus stop distance and entry price plus target distance; short stop and target are entry price plus stop distance and entry price minus target distance.
Long: chart bar is closed AND bar index >= directional window + statistics lookback + pressure-baseline lookback AND no trade is active AND bars since the previous exit >= cooldown, or there has been no previous exit AND trend direction = −1 AND failure validity holds AND failure score > 0.
Short: chart bar is closed AND bar index >= directional window + statistics lookback + pressure-baseline lookback AND no trade is active AND bars since the previous exit >= cooldown, or there has been no previous exit AND trend direction = +1 AND failure validity holds AND failure score > 0.
Exit: long, chart bar is closed AND bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen target).
Exit: short, chart bar is closed AND bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen target).
