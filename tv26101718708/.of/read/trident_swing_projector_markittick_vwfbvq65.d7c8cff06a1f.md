Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high strictly above, or a low strictly below, every high or low respectively in configurable left and right windows; publish it one bar after the right window ends. Track the latest and previous confirmed highs and lows with their pivot-bar indices; evaluate setups before updating these records. If both directional setups qualify on the same bar, select the long setup.
Calculation: Setup = for long, A is the latest pivot low, B the latest pivot high and C the newly confirmed pivot low, with A before B before C, C > A and B > A; for short, A is the latest pivot high, B the latest pivot low and C the newly confirmed pivot high, with A before B before C, C < A and A > B. Pullback is abs(B - C) / abs(B - A); it must be within an inclusive configurable range. If the trend filter is enabled, B must exceed the previous pivot high for long or be below the previous pivot low for short. At confirmation, require ADX >= its threshold if the ADX filter is enabled, and require the last completed higher-timeframe close > its preceding close for long or < its preceding close for short if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled. Arm only when no setup was armed or active at either the start or end of the bar and the projected leg is positive.
Calculation: Projected leg = abs(B - A), or C * abs(B - A) / A in percent mode when A is nonzero; multiply by a smoothing ratio. The ratio is the selected SMA, RMA, WMA, HMA or VWMA of the chart-bar series of abs(latest pivot high - latest pivot low) divided by its current value; use zero before both pivots exist, use ratio 1 when the current value is zero, and use ratio 1 when smoothing is disabled. Compute this series before updating pivots on each bar.
Calculation: Levels = at arming, freeze entry at C plus or minus one quarter of the projected leg, targets at C plus or minus one half, three quarters and all of the leg, and stop at C minus or plus a configurable price buffer or ATR fraction; upper signs apply to long. Initially the managed stop equals the stop. If break-even is enabled, move the managed stop to the projected entry after the first target is reached, provided the third target was not reached on that bar; use the previous managed stop for all checks on that bar.
Calculation: Setup state = armed from the arming bar until entry or cancellation; cancel before checking entry when the preceding bar's low < C for long or high > C for short, or when bars elapsed since arming >= the expiry limit. After entry, track active, first-target-hit and second-target-hit states; stop or third-target hit closes the setup. Evaluate stop before targets, and evaluate the third, second and first targets in that priority order. The first target is eligible only on entry or from active state; the second only on entry or before the second-target-hit state; the third is eligible throughout an open setup. Target and stop checks include the entry bar.
Long: A long setup is armed AND it is not cancelled AND the preceding bar's close >= its projected entry.
Short: A short setup is armed AND it is not cancelled AND the preceding bar's close <= its projected entry.
Exit: long, an entry triggers or the setup is open AND the current low <= the managed stop OR the current low > the managed stop AND an eligible target is reached by the current high.
Exit: short, an entry triggers or the setup is open AND the current high >= the managed stop OR the current high < the managed stop AND an eligible target is reached by the current low.
