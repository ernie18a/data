Calculation: RSI layers = RSI of close using short, intermediate, and long lookbacks, respectively. Their slopes are current RSI minus RSI a fixed number of chart bars earlier.
Calculation: Oversold and overbought = lower and higher nearest-rank percentiles, respectively, of intermediate RSI over the same lookback.
Calculation: Bull stack = short RSI > midline AND intermediate RSI > midline minus a smaller tolerance AND long RSI > midline minus a larger tolerance. Bear stack = short RSI < midline AND intermediate RSI < midline plus the smaller tolerance AND long RSI < midline plus the larger tolerance.
Calculation: Bull slopes = short RSI slope > 0 AND intermediate RSI slope > 0 AND long RSI slope >= 0. Bear slopes = short RSI slope < 0 AND intermediate RSI slope < 0 AND long RSI slope <= 0.
Calculation: RSI bandwidth = highest intermediate RSI minus lowest intermediate RSI over a lookback. Compression = bandwidth < its simple moving average over the same lookback multiplied by a compression factor. Release = compression on the previous bar AND no compression on the current bar AND absolute change in short RSI from the previous bar >= minimum release movement.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull bias = higher-timeframe short EMA of close > higher-timeframe long EMA of close AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe short EMA. Bear bias = higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe short EMA. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar with gaps filled; on a realtime bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 times log10 of the sum of true ranges over the lookback divided by the difference between the highest high and lowest low over that lookback, divided by log10 of the lookback; use the neutral fallback value when that high-low difference is zero.
Calculation: Bull divergence = a low-price pivot and an intermediate-RSI pivot low are confirmed on the same bar using the same number of bars on each side AND the pivot low price < the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND the pivot RSI > the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND the pivot RSI <= current oversold plus a tolerance. Bear divergence = a high-price pivot and an intermediate-RSI pivot high are confirmed on the same bar AND the pivot high price > the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND the pivot RSI < the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high AND the pivot RSI >= current overbought minus the same tolerance. Compare each pivot series with its own preceding pivot, which need not have coincided. A divergence remains live from confirmation until the number of bars since its pivot exceeds twice the pivot-side lookback.
Calculation: Bull snapback = short RSI crosses above oversold AND intermediate RSI > its previous value AND long RSI > midline minus an anchor tolerance AND close > open AND close > previous close. Bear snapback = short RSI crosses below overbought AND intermediate RSI < its previous value AND long RSI < midline plus the anchor tolerance AND close < open AND close < previous close.
Calculation: Bull harmonic = short RSI crosses above midline AND bull stack AND bull slopes. Bear harmonic = short RSI crosses below midline AND bear stack AND bear slopes.
Calculation: Bull divergence setup = bull divergence is live AND close > open AND close > previous close AND short RSI > its previous value AND close > the low the pivot-side lookback bars ago. Bear divergence setup = bear divergence is live AND close < open AND close < previous close AND short RSI < its previous value AND close < the high the pivot-side lookback bars ago.
Calculation: Bull coil setup = release AND short RSI > oversold AND bull slopes AND close > open. Bear coil setup = release AND short RSI < overbought AND bear slopes AND close < open.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of stack, slope, hierarchy, higher-timeframe, volume, choppiness, and EMA points, capped at 10. Stack points are 2 for the corresponding full stack, otherwise 1 when short and intermediate RSI are both strictly on the direction's side of midline, otherwise 0. Slope points are 2 for the corresponding full slopes, otherwise 1 when short and intermediate slopes are strictly in the direction, otherwise 0. Bull hierarchy gives 1 if short RSI > intermediate RSI AND intermediate RSI >= long RSI minus a hierarchy tolerance; bear hierarchy gives 1 if short RSI < intermediate RSI AND intermediate RSI <= long RSI plus that tolerance. Higher-timeframe points are 1 if its bias setting is disabled, otherwise 2 for matching bias and 0 for other bias. Volume gives 1 if its filter is disabled OR volume >= its simple moving average multiplied by a volume factor. Choppiness gives 1 if its filter is disabled OR choppiness < its threshold. EMA gives 1 if its filter is disabled OR close is strictly above the EMA for bull or strictly below it for bear.
Calculation: Bull signal = an enabled bull snapback, harmonic, divergence, or coil setup AND bull score >= minimum score AND the shared signal cooldown has elapsed since the last bull or bear signal AND the choppiness filter is disabled OR, with the same preceding conditions, choppiness < its threshold; additionally, hard higher-timeframe alignment must be disabled OR bull higher-timeframe bias must hold, and the EMA filter must be disabled OR close > its EMA. Bear signal uses the corresponding bear setups and score, the same cooldown and choppiness gate, bear higher-timeframe bias when hard alignment is enabled, and close < EMA when the EMA filter is enabled. Evaluate both signals before updating the shared last-signal bar.
