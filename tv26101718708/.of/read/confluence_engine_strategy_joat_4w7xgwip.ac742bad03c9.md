Calculation: Linear regression close = linear regression of close over a lookback; signal = shorter-lookback SMA of linear regression close
Calculation: Confluence score = 25 if fast EMA > slow EMA, plus 20 if higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA, plus volume score, plus RSI score, plus 15 if ATR(shorter lookback) / ATR(longer lookback) > regime threshold; cap at 100 and convert to integer. For shorts, reverse both EMA comparisons and use RSI score for shorts.
Calculation: Volume score = 20 if volume > 1.2 × volume SMA, 10 if volume > volume SMA, otherwise 0
Calculation: Long RSI score = min((55 − RSI) × 0.5, 20) if RSI < 50, otherwise 0; short RSI score = min((RSI − 45) × 0.5, 20) if RSI > 50, otherwise 0; convert to integer
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare fast and slow EMAs, using the current higher-timeframe bar (which may still be forming); fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR sampled on the entry bar
Long: linear regression close crosses above its SMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND (volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier OR volume filter disabled) AND RSI < long RSI maximum AND (ATR(shorter lookback) / ATR(longer lookback) > regime threshold OR regime filter disabled) AND long confluence score ≥ minimum score AND long entries enabled AND warmup elapsed AND bar confirmed AND position flat AND cooldown elapsed
Short: linear regression close crosses below its SMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA AND (volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier OR volume filter disabled) AND RSI > short RSI minimum AND (ATR(shorter lookback) / ATR(longer lookback) > regime threshold OR regime filter disabled) AND short confluence score ≥ minimum score AND short entries enabled AND warmup elapsed AND bar confirmed AND position flat AND cooldown elapsed
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × take-profit multiple OR low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × stop-loss multiple OR bars since entry ≥ timeout OR (fast EMA < slow EMA AND bar confirmed)
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × take-profit multiple OR high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × stop-loss multiple OR bars since entry ≥ timeout OR (fast EMA > slow EMA AND bar confirmed)
