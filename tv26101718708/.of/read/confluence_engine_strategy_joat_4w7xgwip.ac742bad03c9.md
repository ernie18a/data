Calculation: Regime ratio = short-lookback ATR divided by max(long-lookback ATR, minimum price tick), or 0 if the denominator is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Trending = regime filter disabled OR regime ratio > regime threshold.
Calculation: Volume score = 20 if volume > 1.2 × SMA(volume), otherwise 10 if volume > SMA(volume), otherwise 0.
Calculation: Long score = min(100, 25 if short-lookback EMA(close) > long-lookback EMA(close) else 0, plus 20 if higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) > higher-timeframe long-lookback EMA(close) else 0, plus volume score, plus truncation toward zero of min((55 − RSI(close)) × 0.5, 20) if RSI(close) < 50 else 0, plus 15 if trending else 0). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Short score = min(100, 25 if short-lookback EMA(close) < long-lookback EMA(close) else 0, plus 20 if higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) < higher-timeframe long-lookback EMA(close) else 0, plus volume score, plus truncation toward zero of min((RSI(close) − 45) × 0.5, 20) if RSI(close) > 50 else 0, plus 15 if trending else 0). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Long: Long trading enabled AND completed chart bar AND zero-based bar index >= 2 × max(long EMA lookback, long ATR lookback) AND linear regression of close crosses above its SMA AND short-lookback EMA(close) > long-lookback EMA(close) AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) > higher-timeframe long-lookback EMA(close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier) AND RSI(close) < long RSI threshold AND trending AND long score >= minimum score.
Short: Short trading enabled AND completed chart bar AND zero-based bar index >= 2 × max(long EMA lookback, long ATR lookback) AND linear regression of close crosses below its SMA AND short-lookback EMA(close) < long-lookback EMA(close) AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) < higher-timeframe long-lookback EMA(close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier) AND RSI(close) > short RSI threshold AND trending AND short score >= minimum score.
Calculation: Entry orders additionally require a flat position and either no prior exit or bars since the position became flat >= cooldown; orders are submitted at the signal bar close and normally fill at the next bar open. Signal markings use the Long and Short conditions without these additional restrictions.
Calculation: Entry ATR = short-lookback ATR on the bar where the position first becomes open; entry bar = that bar.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + entry ATR × take-profit multiplier OR low <= entry price − entry ATR × stop-loss multiplier OR bars since entry bar >= timeout OR short-lookback EMA(close) < long-lookback EMA(close) on a completed chart bar.
Exit: short, low <= entry price − entry ATR × take-profit multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry ATR × stop-loss multiplier OR bars since entry bar >= timeout OR short-lookback EMA(close) > long-lookback EMA(close) on a completed chart bar.
