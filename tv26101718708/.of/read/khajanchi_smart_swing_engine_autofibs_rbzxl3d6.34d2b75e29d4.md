Calculation: Trend regime = ADX at or above its threshold and close above the long-term SMA for an uptrend or below it for a downtrend; if the SMA is unavailable, use the shorter-term EMA instead, and if that is unavailable, compare close with the open at a fixed lookback. Neither trend applies when ADX is below its threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive pivot lookback = base lookback divided by ATR divided by the SMA of ATR, rounded to an integer and clamped to lower and upper bounds; use a volatility ratio of 1 when the ATR average is zero. Confirm pivot highs and lows using this lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Swing boundaries = maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and low and their pivot times, plus pending highs and lows. On a confirmed pivot high during a downtrend, if its pivot time is later than the current low, replace the high if it is unavailable or the pivot is higher; otherwise store the pivot as the pending high. For any other confirmed pivot high, first promote a pending low during an uptrend, then replace the high with the pivot. On a confirmed pivot low during an uptrend, if its pivot time is later than the current high, replace the low if it is unavailable or the pivot is lower; otherwise store the pivot as the pending low. For any other confirmed pivot low, first promote a pending high during a downtrend, then replace the low with the pivot. Process highs before lows. Save these boundaries as the pre-extension boundaries, then extend the high to the current high in an uptrend, the low to the current low in a downtrend, or both in a ranging regime whenever a new extreme is reached. If the resulting low is greater than or equal to the high, replace them with the lowest low and highest high over a fixed lookback and clear both pending pivots.
Calculation: Swing direction = uptrend when ADX is at or above its threshold; otherwise the swing is upward when the high boundary is more recent than the low boundary. The swing is downward in all other cases.
Calculation: Fibonacci level at a ratio = high boundary minus the boundary range times the ratio for an upward swing, or low boundary plus the boundary range times the ratio for a downward swing. The pocket is the interval between two retracement levels; the deeper level has the larger ratio. The risk level uses a still larger retracement ratio.
Calculation: Requested timeframe bars = use two requested timeframes, one longer than the other. Each may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. On historical bars, a higher-timeframe request supplies the last completed bar and a lower-timeframe request supplies the last intrabar; on a live bar, use the bar still forming when the requested timeframe is higher and the latest available intrabar when lower.
Calculation: Pocket overlap = the shorter requested timeframe bar's low is at or below the pocket's upper boundary AND its high is at or above the pocket's lower boundary.
Calculation: Trap = pocket overlap AND shorter requested timeframe volume exceeds a multiple of its volume SMA, OR shorter requested timeframe low is below the pre-extension low boundary AND its close is above that boundary, OR shorter requested timeframe high is above the pre-extension high boundary AND its close is below that boundary.
Calculation: Ready = ADX at or above its threshold AND ((swing direction is upward AND longer requested timeframe close is above its long-term close SMA) OR (swing direction is downward AND longer requested timeframe close is below its long-term close SMA)) AND pocket overlap AND trap occurred on the current or previous chart bar AND absolute distance from close to the corresponding swing boundary divided by absolute distance from close to the risk level is at or above a reward-to-risk threshold; use zero as the ratio when the risk distance is zero.
Long: ADX at or above its threshold AND close above the long-term SMA AND low below the pre-extension low boundary AND close above that boundary.
Short: ADX at or above its threshold AND close below the long-term SMA AND high above the pre-extension high boundary AND close below that boundary.
Long: Ready is true AND Ready was false on the previous chart bar AND swing direction is upward.
Short: Ready is true AND Ready was false on the previous chart bar AND swing direction is downward.
