Calculation: Effort z-score = z-score over the effort baseline lookback of volume when volume > 0, otherwise high − low; use 0 when the baseline standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Mirrored prices = for accumulation, high, low and close as observed; for distribution, −low, −high and −close respectively. A mirrored pivot high or low is a confirmed pivot with the same pivot-strength lookback on both sides; evaluate it on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Active range = track one range at a time. When scanning, start an accumulation climax if effort z-score >= climax threshold AND high − low >= climax ATR multiple × ATR AND low < the lowest low of the preceding extreme lookback AND close < EMA; otherwise start a distribution climax if effort z-score >= climax threshold AND high − low >= climax ATR multiple × ATR AND high > the highest high of the preceding extreme lookback AND close > EMA. If both qualify, choose accumulation. Set the mirrored lower edge and climax bar to that bar's mirrored low and index. While awaiting a range, replace the lower edge and climax bar whenever a later mirrored low is lower. Confirm the range when a mirrored pivot high whose pivot bar is after the latest climax bar exceeds the lower edge by at least the minimum range ATR multiple × current ATR; set the upper edge to that pivot high. If no such pivot is confirmed and the current bar is more than the maximum AR wait after the latest climax bar, discard the setup. Process range events only after the range confirmation bar. Range height = upper edge − lower edge; midpoint = (upper edge + lower edge) / 2.
Calculation: Range event state = initially no secondary test, pending break, spring, or breakout. Before a spring or breakout, count a confirmed mirrored pivot low as a secondary test if its pivot bar is after the range's pivot-high bar AND its value >= the lower edge AND <= lower edge + secondary-test zone fraction × range height, subject to the maximum test count. Before a breakout, start a pending break when mirrored low < lower edge; track the lowest mirrored low and its bar. While pending, update that low on a new lower low. If mirrored close > lower edge, clear the pending break: if a secondary test is required and none has occurred, replace the lower edge with the tracked low; otherwise record a spring on the current bar. Otherwise, if mirrored close < lower edge − failure ATR multiple × ATR OR bars since the pending break began >= reclaim window, terminate the range. After processing the pending break, record a breakout when no break is pending AND mirrored close > upper edge + breakout buffer ATR multiple × ATR AND (effort z-score >= breakout effort threshold OR high − low >= ATR). After breakout, terminate the range when a later mirrored close < midpoint OR bars since breakout > maximum pullback wait. Terminate a range without a breakout when bars since the latest climax bar > maximum range duration. A qualifying pullback terminates the range before these failure and timeout checks. Resume scanning on the next bar after termination.
Long: Active accumulation range AND spring recorded on the current bar.
Short: Active distribution range AND spring recorded on the current bar.
Long: Active accumulation range AND breakout recorded on the current bar.
Short: Active distribution range AND breakout recorded on the current bar.
Long: Active accumulation range AND breakout previously recorded AND a mirrored pivot low is confirmed with pivot bar > breakout bar AND pivot value >= range midpoint.
Short: Active distribution range AND breakout previously recorded AND a mirrored pivot low is confirmed with pivot bar > breakout bar AND pivot value >= range midpoint.
