Calculation: Basis on any timeframe = (shorter-lookback EMA of close + longer-lookback EMA of close) / 2; use the same respective EMA lookbacks on all timeframes.
Calculation: Score = clamp to a symmetric range the sum of: a positive weight times (shorter EMA − longer EMA) / ATR on the primary higher timeframe; a larger positive weight times (shorter EMA − signal EMA) / ATR on the primary higher timeframe; a positive weight times (shorter EMA − longer EMA) / ATR on the secondary higher timeframe; a positive weight times (shorter EMA − signal EMA) / ATR on the secondary higher timeframe; a positive weight times (chart close − primary basis) / primary ATR; a positive weight times (chart shorter EMA − chart longer EMA) / chart ATR; and a positive weight times (chart close − chart basis) / chart ATR. The secondary spread weight is greater than the primary spread weight; the primary spread weight is greater than the location and chart weights; the location weight is greater than the chart EMA-spread weight; the chart EMA-spread weight is greater than the secondary signal-spread weight; the secondary signal-spread weight is greater than the equal primary signal-spread and chart price-spread weights. The primary signal-spread weight is smaller than the primary EMA-spread weight. Use the same ATR lookback on all timeframes and floor each ATR denominator at a positive minimum. The signal EMA has its own lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA and ATR values = the final values of the higher-timeframe bar containing the preceding chart bar. On historical bars this uses the final value of a still-forming higher-timeframe bar when the preceding chart bar belongs to it; on the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar it uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe close comparisons use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The secondary timeframe is higher than the primary timeframe, and both are higher than the chart timeframe.
Long: Score >= positive state threshold.
Short: Score <= negative state threshold of equal magnitude.
Long: Primary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed primary close >= primary basis.
Short: Primary higher-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND last completed primary close <= primary basis AND NOT (primary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed primary close >= primary basis).
Long: Secondary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed secondary close >= secondary basis.
Short: Secondary higher-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND last completed secondary close <= secondary basis AND NOT (secondary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed secondary close >= secondary basis).
Long: Chart close >= primary basis.
Short: Chart close < primary basis.
Long: Score >= positive alignment threshold AND primary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed primary close >= primary basis AND secondary higher-timeframe shorter EMA >= longer EMA AND last completed secondary close >= secondary basis AND chart close >= chart basis.
Short: Score <= negative alignment threshold of equal magnitude AND primary higher-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND last completed primary close <= primary basis AND secondary higher-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND last completed secondary close <= secondary basis AND chart close <= chart basis.
Long: The long alignment condition is true AND was false on the preceding chart bar.
Short: The short alignment condition is true AND was false on the preceding chart bar.
Exit: long, chart close < chart basis OR score < 0.
Exit: short, chart close > chart basis OR score > 0.
