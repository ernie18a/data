Calculation: A confirmed high or low pivot is a high or low surrounded by the configured numbers of left and right bars, confirmed on the bar completing the right-hand window. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. Regular bearish divergence is a higher pivot high with lower pivot RSI; regular bullish divergence is a lower pivot low with higher pivot RSI. Hidden bearish divergence is a lower pivot high with higher pivot RSI; hidden bullish divergence is a higher pivot low with lower pivot RSI.
Calculation: Session VWAP is cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the session began, falling back to close when unavailable. Upward extension is the average of close minus its EMA and close minus session VWAP, divided by ATR; downward extension is its negative. Both are zero when ATR is not positive. Stochastic RSI is the SMA of the stochastic oscillator of RSI.
Calculation: For either side, evaluate these enabled factors at the specified bar: RSI at or beyond its corresponding extreme; volume greater than its SMA times a multiplier; high at or above the upper Bollinger band for a top or low at or below the lower band for a bottom; corresponding extension at or above its threshold; stochastic RSI at or beyond its corresponding extreme; upper wick at least body times a multiplier and at least lower wick for a top, or lower wick at least body times the multiplier and at least upper wick for a bottom; last completed higher-timeframe RSI at or beyond the corresponding RSI extreme; high greater than the highest high of the preceding sweep lookback AND close below high for a top, or low below the lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback AND close above low for a bottom. The higher-timeframe RSI uses the same RSI length as the chart RSI.
Calculation: Confirmed factors additionally include regular divergence at the newly confirmed pivot and engulfing at the pivot: for a top, close below open AND previous close at or above previous open AND close at or below previous open AND open at or above previous close; for a bottom, close above open AND previous close at or below previous open AND close at or above previous open AND open at or below previous close. All other confirmed factors use values at the pivot bar, including the last completed higher-timeframe RSI as available at that bar. Each enabled true factor contributes one vote and its own configurable weight.
Calculation: Trend is up when the short-term EMA exceeds the long-term EMA AND the extension EMA exceeds its value at the slope lookback; trend is down when both comparisons are reversed. Efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero. The regime adjustment magnitude is the trend weight when efficiency ratio reaches its threshold and half that weight otherwise.
Calculation: Confirmed confidence for each side is the weighted sum of its true enabled factors, plus the regime adjustment for an uptrend on bottoms or a downtrend on tops, minus the adjustment for the opposite trend, minus the hidden-divergence penalty when the corresponding hidden divergence is present. Divide by the sum of all enabled confirmed-factor weights plus the trend weight and clamp to the unit interval; use zero if the denominator is not positive. Hidden divergence applies its penalty independently of whether the divergence factor is enabled.
Calculation: Early confidence for each side is the sum of weights of its true enabled current-bar factors divided by the sum of the weights of all enabled current-bar factors, capped at the unit maximum, or zero if the denominator is not positive. It excludes divergence and engulfing and evaluates the forming chart bar while retaining the last completed higher-timeframe RSI.
Calculation: For follow-through, select the latest confirmed top or bottom signal; if both occur on the same bar select the higher-confidence signal, with the top winning a tie. At that confirmation bar, fix the reference entry at close and the hard level at the pivot high plus an ATR multiple for a top or the pivot low minus an ATR multiple for a bottom, using confirmation-bar ATR. For a top, the target is the highest of the confirmation-bar extension EMA, session VWAP, and latest confirmed pivot low that are below the reference entry; if none qualify, subtract a configurable multiple of hard-level minus reference entry from the reference entry. For a bottom, use the lowest of the confirmation-bar extension EMA, session VWAP, and latest confirmed pivot high that are above the reference entry; if none qualify, add a configurable multiple of reference entry minus hard level to the reference entry. Track the minimum low for a top or maximum high for a bottom from the confirmation bar onward. A hard invalidation or target hit ends tracking until a new selected signal; hard invalidation takes priority if both occur on the same bar.
Long: Early-warning layer enabled AND bottom early confidence is at or above its threshold AND previous-bar bottom early confidence is below that threshold.
Short: Early-warning layer enabled AND top early confidence is at or above its threshold AND previous-bar top early confidence is below that threshold.
Long: A low pivot is confirmed AND bottom confirmed confidence is at or above its threshold AND bottom votes are at or above their minimum AND no prior confirmed bottom signal exists OR a low pivot is confirmed AND bottom confirmed confidence is at or above its threshold AND bottom votes are at or above their minimum AND bars since the preceding confirmed bottom signal are at or above the cooldown.
Short: A high pivot is confirmed AND top confirmed confidence is at or above its threshold AND top votes are at or above their minimum AND no prior confirmed top signal exists OR a high pivot is confirmed AND top confirmed confidence is at or above its threshold AND top votes are at or above their minimum AND bars since the preceding confirmed top signal are at or above the cooldown.
Long: Regular bullish RSI divergence is confirmed.
Short: Regular bearish RSI divergence is confirmed.
Exit: long, follow-through enabled AND the selected signal is a bottom AND tracking is active AND close is below its hard level.
Exit: long, follow-through enabled AND the selected signal is a bottom AND tracking is active AND close is at or above its hard level AND the tracked maximum high is at or above its target.
Exit: short, follow-through enabled AND the selected signal is a top AND tracking is active AND close is above its hard level.
Exit: short, follow-through enabled AND the selected signal is a top AND tracking is active AND close is at or below its hard level AND the tracked minimum low is at or below its target.
