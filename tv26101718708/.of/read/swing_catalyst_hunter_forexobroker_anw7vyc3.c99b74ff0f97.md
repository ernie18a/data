Calculation: Catalyst strength = min(volume ratio / volume multiplier × 30, 30) + min(range ratio / range multiplier × 30, 30) + close-position score + counter-trend score. Volume ratio is volume / volume SMA when the SMA > 0, otherwise 0. Range ratio is (high − low) / ATR when ATR > 0, otherwise 0. Close position is (close − low) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise 0.5. Close-position score is close position × 20 when close > open, otherwise (1 − close position) × 20. Counter-trend score is 20 when (close > close EMA AND close < open) OR (close < close EMA AND close > open), otherwise 0.
Calculation: Qualified catalyst = on confirmation of a pivot high or pivot low, select the highest-strength bar from the pivot bar through the scan lookback preceding bars, inclusive; select the most recent bar on a tie. Qualify it when its strength >= the strength threshold. Its direction is low if a pivot low is confirmed, otherwise high. Keep the most recently qualified catalyst until another qualifies, including its bar index and direction. Pivot highs and lows use equal left and right lookbacks and are recognized on their confirmation bar.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = current bar index − the index of the most recent long or short confirmation signal, initialized to index 0 before any signal.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND the selected catalyst strength >= the strength threshold.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND no pivot low is confirmed on the same bar AND the selected catalyst strength >= the strength threshold.
Long: The last qualified catalyst direction = low AND current bar index − catalyst bar index <= scan lookback + pivot lookback + 2 AND close > open AND close > close EMA AND volume > volume SMA AND signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold.
Short: The last qualified catalyst direction = high AND current bar index − catalyst bar index <= scan lookback + pivot lookback + 2 AND close < open AND close < close EMA AND volume > volume SMA AND signal cooldown >= cooldown threshold.
