Calculation: Catalyst strength = min((volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / volume multiplier × 30, 30) + min(((high − low) / ATR(ATR lookback)) / range multiplier × 30, 30) + close-position score + counter-trend score; close-position score is ((close − low) / (high − low)) × 20 when close > open, otherwise (1 − (close − low) / (high − low)) × 20, using 0.5 for the ratio when high = low; counter-trend score is 20 when close is above the trend EMA and close < open, or close is below the trend EMA and close > open, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the pivot lookback bars before and after it, recognized after those later bars complete.
Calculation: Latest qualifying catalyst = on each confirmed pivot high or low, the bar with maximum catalyst strength among bars from the pivot bar through the scan lookback bars before it; update the stored catalyst only if its strength ≥ catalyst threshold. If a high and low pivot confirm together, classify the stored catalyst as a swing low. Its age is measured from the catalyst bar.
Long: Latest qualifying catalyst is classified as a swing low AND its age ≤ pivot lookback + scan lookback + 2 bars AND close > open AND close > trend EMA AND volume > volume SMA AND bars since the prior signal ≥ cooldown AND the current bar is complete.
Short: Latest qualifying catalyst is classified as a swing high AND its age ≤ pivot lookback + scan lookback + 2 bars AND close < open AND close < trend EMA AND volume > volume SMA AND bars since the prior signal ≥ cooldown AND the current bar is complete.
