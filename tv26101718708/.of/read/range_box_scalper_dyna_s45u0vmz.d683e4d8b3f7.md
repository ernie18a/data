Calculation: Range state = On each completed chart bar, if no range is active, start one with the bar's high and low and a count of one. Otherwise, first evaluate the breakout decisions against the existing boundaries. If either breakout occurs, deactivate the range. If neither occurs and max(range high, high) - min(range low, low) <= range-width multiplier * ATR, with unavailable ATR replaced by zero, update the boundaries to those maximum and minimum values and increment the count; confirm the range when the count first reaches the minimum range-bar count. If neither breakout occurs and the bar does not fit, deactivate the range. A deactivated range starts again on the next bar.
Long: Range is active and confirmed AND range-bar count >= minimum range-bar count AND close > range high AND close - range high >= breakout-strength multiplier * ATR, with unavailable ATR replaced by a minimum-tick parameter AND (volume SMA is unavailable or <= 0 OR volume > volume multiplier * volume SMA) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > EMA of close).
Short: Range is active and confirmed AND range-bar count >= minimum range-bar count AND close < range low AND range low - close >= breakout-strength multiplier * ATR, with unavailable ATR replaced by a minimum-tick parameter AND (volume SMA is unavailable or <= 0 OR volume > volume multiplier * volume SMA) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA of close).
