Calculation: Alligator lines = smoothed moving averages of (high + low) / 2, each shifted forward by its own offset; the longest, intermediate, and shortest smoothing periods use the longest, intermediate, and shortest offsets, respectively. Initialize each smoothed average with a simple moving average, then update it as (previous average × (period − 1) + current midpoint) / period.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator = short-period simple moving average of (high + low) / 2 minus its long-period simple moving average.
Calculation: Squat bar = (high − low) / volume is less than its previous-bar value AND volume > previous-bar volume.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = close > (high + low) / 2 AND low = lowest low over the lookback including the current bar AND high < each Alligator line AND (AO filter disabled OR Awesome Oscillator < previous-bar Awesome Oscillator) AND (MFI filter disabled OR a squat bar occurred on the current bar or either of the two preceding bars).
Long: Datetime is within the inclusive trading window AND bullish reversal AND (no open layer OR (one open layer AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 2 threshold)) OR (two open layers AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 3 threshold)) OR (three open layers AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 4 threshold))) AND a subsequent bar reaches the signal bar's high. Submit a stop entry at that high; an unfilled stop remains pending, and a later qualifying signal for the same layer replaces it. Layer thresholds are independently configurable.
Exit: long, high >= volume-weighted average entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier.
