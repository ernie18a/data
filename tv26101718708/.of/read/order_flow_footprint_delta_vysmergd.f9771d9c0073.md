Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Bullish impulse = close > open AND body ratio >= impulse body threshold AND volume >= volume SMA * impulse volume multiplier. Bearish impulse = close < open AND body ratio >= impulse body threshold AND volume >= volume SMA * impulse volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume >= volume SMA * absorption volume multiplier AND body ratio <= absorption body threshold AND close >= open. Bearish absorption = volume >= volume SMA * absorption volume multiplier AND body ratio <= absorption body threshold AND close <= open.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot highs and lows use the same number of bars on each side; update their latest and preceding values on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish impulse zone = highest high and lowest low of the latest consecutive bullish impulse stack; bearish impulse zone = highest high and lowest low of the latest consecutive bearish impulse stack. Replace the corresponding zone and its bar index whenever another stack completes.
Calculation: Sweep high level = max(highest high over the preceding sweep lookback bars, previous completed higher-timeframe daily high); sweep low level = min(lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback bars, previous completed higher-timeframe daily low). If the daily value is unavailable, use the corresponding lookback level.
Calculation: Latest high sweep = the most recent bar with high > its sweep high level AND close < its sweep high level; store its high and bar index. Latest low sweep = the most recent bar with low < its sweep low level AND close > its sweep low level; store its low and bar index. Update these before evaluating signals.
Calculation: Upper breakout = close > highest high of the preceding breakout lookback bars + ATR * breakout padding multiplier AND volume >= volume SMA * breakout volume multiplier AND close > open AND body ratio >= breakout body threshold. Lower breakout = close < lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback bars - ATR * breakout padding multiplier AND volume >= volume SMA * breakout volume multiplier AND close < open AND body ratio >= breakout body threshold.
Calculation: Latest breakout = the most recent upper or lower breakout, storing its direction, bar index, and corresponding preceding-range high or low as its level. Replace it before evaluating signals whenever a new breakout occurs; allow at most one breakout-retest signal per stored breakout.
Long: Latest confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low > preceding confirmed pivot low AND, if the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, higher-timeframe close > its EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time AND current bar is at least one bar and at most the pullback limit after the latest bullish impulse stack AND low is between the bullish impulse zone low - ATR * pullback padding multiplier and zone high + ATR * pullback padding multiplier, inclusive AND (bullish impulse OR (close > open AND volume > volume SMA)) AND bars since the previous long continuation signal >= continuation cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
Short: Latest confirmed pivot high < preceding confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low AND, if the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, higher-timeframe close < its EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time AND current bar is at least one bar and at most the pullback limit after the latest bearish impulse stack AND high is between the bearish impulse zone low - ATR * pullback padding multiplier and zone high + ATR * pullback padding multiplier, inclusive AND (bearish impulse OR (close < open AND volume > volume SMA)) AND bars since the previous short continuation signal >= continuation cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
Long: Current bar is at least one bar and at most the reclaim limit after the latest low sweep AND close > current sweep low level AND low >= the stored low sweep low AND (bullish absorption OR bullish impulse) AND bars since the previous long absorption signal >= continuation cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
Short: Current bar is at least one bar and at most the reclaim limit after the latest high sweep AND close < current sweep high level AND high <= the stored high sweep high AND (bearish absorption OR bearish impulse) AND bars since the previous short absorption signal >= continuation cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
Long: Latest breakout direction = upper AND current bar is at least the minimum retest delay and at most the retest limit after that breakout AND no signal has used that breakout AND low <= stored breakout level + ATR * breakout padding multiplier AND close > stored breakout level AND volume > volume SMA AND (bullish impulse OR (close > open AND body ratio >= retest body threshold)) AND bars since the previous long breakout-retest signal >= breakout cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
Short: Latest breakout direction = lower AND current bar is at least the minimum retest delay and at most the retest limit after that breakout AND no signal has used that breakout AND high >= stored breakout level - ATR * breakout padding multiplier AND close < stored breakout level AND volume > volume SMA AND (bearish impulse OR (close < open AND body ratio >= retest body threshold)) AND bars since the previous short breakout-retest signal >= breakout cooldown, or no previous such signal exists.
