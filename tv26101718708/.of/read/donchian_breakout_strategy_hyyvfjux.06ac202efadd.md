Calculation: daily ADR percentage = average of (high − low) / abs(low) × 100 over a lookback of daily bars, using the last completed daily bar
Calculation: channel width percentage = (upper Donchian channel − lower Donchian channel) / upper Donchian channel × 100
Calculation: active long stop = wider lower Donchian channel when the tight-stop option is off; when on, tight lower Donchian channel until entry price ≤ tight lower Donchian channel, then the greater of the tight-stop level from the prior bar and the wider lower Donchian channel; before profit, the tight-stop level updates to the current tight lower Donchian channel, and afterward it holds
Long: high ≥ current upper Donchian channel (wick mode) OR close ≥ prior bar’s upper Donchian channel (close mode), AND, when enabled, upper Donchian channel > current-timeframe moving average, AND, when enabled, upper Donchian channel > moving average from a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, AND, when enabled, moving average from a higher timeframe is rising versus its prior completed bar, AND, when enabled, channel width percentage < configured percentage of daily ADR percentage, AND bar time is after the configured start and before the configured finish
Exit: long, low ≤ prior bar’s active long stop (wick mode) OR close ≤ prior bar’s active long stop (close mode)
