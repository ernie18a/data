Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the configured session’s opening-range period
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low during the configured session’s opening-range period
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2
Calculation: Initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop price
Calculation: Profit target at each configured reward multiple = entry price + (long) or − (short) initial risk × that target’s reward multiple
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; calculated on the chart timeframe or configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); for a different timeframe, use the latest value available with lookahead off
Long: Opening range complete AND within its configured trading session AND allowed day AND outside the flat window AND (configured entry mode is breakout AND close crosses above opening-range high plus entry buffer OR configured entry mode is retest zone AND, after a breakout above opening-range high plus entry buffer, low touches opening-range high and close is above opening-range high plus entry buffer OR configured entry mode is retest midpoint AND, after a breakout above opening-range high plus entry buffer, low touches opening-range midpoint and close is above opening-range midpoint) AND long direction is allowed AND (EMA filter disabled or not applied to this session or close > EMA) AND re-entry limits and direction rules are satisfied
Short: Opening range complete AND within its configured trading session AND allowed day AND outside the flat window AND (configured entry mode is breakout AND close crosses below opening-range low minus entry buffer OR configured entry mode is retest zone AND, after a breakout below opening-range low minus entry buffer, high touches opening-range low and close is below opening-range low minus entry buffer OR configured entry mode is retest midpoint AND, after a breakout below opening-range low minus entry buffer, high touches opening-range midpoint and close is below opening-range midpoint) AND short direction is allowed AND (EMA filter disabled or not applied to this session or close < EMA) AND re-entry limits and direction rules are satisfied
Exit: long, low <= current stop price OR high >= any active profit target OR configured session end or flat-window start
Exit: short, high >= current stop price OR low <= any active profit target OR configured session end or flat-window start
