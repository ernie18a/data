Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled session and local calendar day, the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in its configured opening-range window; its midpoint is their average. Reset the range and session entry state at session start and at a local date change during the session.
Calculation: Initial stop = opening-range midpoint, opposite opening-range boundary, or entry price minus a fixed distance for a long and plus that distance for a short, according to the session's selected stop method.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the absolute distance from entry price to initial stop multiplied by each of three independently configured risk multiples. Allocate the position to the targets in order, capped by remaining size; a configured zero allocation for the last target assigns it the remainder.
Calculation: Staircase stop = initial stop; when enabled and a position remains open, after a bar reaches the third, second, or first target, in that priority order, move the stop to the more protective of its existing level and the second target, first target, or entry price respectively. The updated stop orders apply after that calculation.
Long: Session is enabled AND its opening range is complete AND no entry has fired since session reset or re-arm AND session entry count is below its configured maximum when re-arm is enabled AND the local weekday and long direction are allowed AND the bar is outside the enabled flat window AND the session has not just ended AND (breakout mode AND bar is in the trading session AND close > opening-range high + entry buffer AND previous close <= opening-range high + entry buffer OR retest mode AND a pending upward breakout exists AND bar is later than the breakout bar AND low <= selected retest level AND close > selected confirmation level) AND (re-arm is disabled OR no prior session entry has a recorded exit direction OR the configured re-arm direction permits long) AND (re-arm is disabled OR no prior session entry has a recorded exit direction OR EMA filter is disabled for the session OR EMA scope is first entry only OR close > EMA).
Short: Session is enabled AND its opening range is complete AND no entry has fired since session reset or re-arm AND session entry count is below its configured maximum when re-arm is enabled AND the local weekday and short direction are allowed AND the bar is outside the enabled flat window AND the session has not just ended AND (breakout mode AND bar is in the trading session AND close < opening-range low - entry buffer AND previous close >= opening-range low - entry buffer OR retest mode AND a pending downward breakout exists AND bar is later than the breakout bar AND high >= selected retest level AND close < selected confirmation level) AND (re-arm is disabled OR no prior session entry has a recorded exit direction OR the configured re-arm direction permits short) AND (re-arm is disabled OR no prior session entry has a recorded exit direction OR EMA filter is disabled for the session OR EMA scope is first entry only OR close < EMA).
Calculation: Pending retest = in retest mode, record the first upward or downward breakout satisfying the corresponding crossover, session, entry-count, and no-fired-entry conditions. For a zone retest, the retest level is the broken range boundary and the confirmation level is that boundary plus the buffer for an upward breakout or minus the buffer for a downward breakout. For a midpoint retest, both levels are the range midpoint. On subsequent bars cancel an upward pending retest if zone-mode close <= range high, or midpoint-mode close < range low, or midpoint was touched and close <= midpoint; reverse inequalities for a downward retest. Cancel after evaluating the retest on the timeout bar. Clear the pending retest when a retest signal is produced, even if an entry filter rejects it, and on session reset or re-arm.
Calculation: Re-arm state = after a recorded stop or full profit exit, when re-arm is enabled, the session is active, the opening range is complete, the position is flat, entry count is below the maximum, and the recorded exit type matches the configured stop/profit selection, wait until the configured cooldown has elapsed. If the configured gate-bar count is positive, start counting at the first subsequent bar satisfying the selected gate: close inside the range inset by the entry buffer, bar spans the midpoint, low <= range low OR high >= range high, or immediate. Count every bar thereafter, including bars outside the gate. Once the count reaches its threshold, or immediately after cooldown when the count is zero, clear the fired-entry state. A new entry still requires a fresh crossover; re-arm direction can require the same direction, the opposite direction, or either direction relative to the recorded exit.
Calculation: EMA = EMA of the selected chart-bar source on the chart timeframe, or EMA of close on a selected higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the forming bar in realtime; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR risk > 0 AND high >= an allocated, unfilled profit target, exiting that target's allocated quantity.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR risk > 0 AND low <= an allocated, unfilled profit target, exiting that target's allocated quantity.
Exit: long or short, an enabled session has just ended OR the enabled flat window has just started OR the position belongs to a session whose optional end flatten is enabled and the bar is outside that session OR the bar is outside all configured trading sessions.
