Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is more extreme than the highs/lows in the selected lookback bars on both sides; accept alternating pivots only when the move from the prior opposite pivot meets the minimum ATR-based swing size, and replace a pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type.
Calculation: Active leg = the direction and anchor selected by the configured mode: each confirmed swing starts a leg in the opposite direction anchored at that swing; a structure-break leg starts when close breaks the prior swing high/low and anchors at the intervening lowest low/high; a fast leg starts when a rolling highest high/lowest low appears after an opposite extreme and anchors at that prior extreme; manual mode starts a bullish leg at the selected time.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = the weighted average of the configured price source from the active leg's anchor through the current bar, using volume as weight (or equal weights when volume is unavailable); optional half-life weighting decays older weights.
Calculation: Anchored sigma = the weighted standard deviation of the configured price source over the same anchored leg and with the same weights.
Calculation: Touch tolerance = anchored sigma × configured tolerance; while sigma is zero, use 0.1 × ATR.
Calculation: VWAP retest = the bar range overlaps the VWAP within touch tolerance, after at least the configured number of consecutive prior bars stayed entirely on the leg's side beyond that tolerance; reset the away count on a touch or any bar that is neither a touch nor outside.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × configured stop multiplier; target levels are entry price plus/minus stop distance times the configured target risk multiples, in the position direction.
Long: Active leg is bullish AND VWAP retest AND bar is confirmed and warmed up AND position is flat AND stop distance > 0.
Short: Active leg is bearish AND VWAP retest AND bar is confirmed and warmed up AND position is flat AND stop distance > 0.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar after entry, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ third target; after first target is touched without the stop being touched on that bar, move the active stop to entry price when break-even is enabled.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar after entry, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ third target; after first target is touched without the stop being touched on that bar, move the active stop to entry price when break-even is enabled.
