Calculation: Swing structure = confirm pivot highs and lows after their right lookback, using independent left and right lookbacks. Process a high before a low if both confirm on the same bar. Accept an opposite-type pivot if its distance from the last opposite swing is at least a multiplier of current ATR; accept a same-type pivot only if it is more extreme, replacing that swing without changing its previous same-type reference. Retain each accepted swing's price, bar and previous same-type swing price.
Calculation: Leg direction and anchor = select an anchor mode. Swing: each accepted swing high starts a bearish leg at that high; each accepted swing low starts a bullish leg at that low, including same-type replacements. Fast: identify current highs at least as high as the lookback highest high and lows at most as low as the lookback lowest low; if both occur, select the high when close >= open, otherwise the low. A high following a low starts a bullish leg at the latest low bar; a low following a high starts a bearish leg at the latest high bar. Manual: the first bar at or after the selected anchor time starts a bullish leg at that bar. Structure break: if the current leg is not bullish and close > latest swing high, start a bullish leg at the lowest-low bar strictly after that swing high through the current bar; otherwise, if the current leg is not bearish and close < latest swing low, start a bearish leg at the highest-high bar strictly after that swing low through the current bar. Limit the search to the maximum leg lookback and resolve equal extremes in favor of the most recent bar. Limit every anchor's age to the maximum leg lookback.
Calculation: Leg VWAP and dispersion = from the anchor through each bar, calculate the weighted mean and weighted standard deviation of the selected price source. Use volume as weight, or one until any positive volume has appeared; optionally cap volume at a multiplier of its rolling median when that median is positive. Optionally decay each earlier weight exponentially according to its age and a half-life parameter. On re-anchoring, rebuild these values from the new anchor through the current bar.
Calculation: Retest = for each bar in the current leg, tolerance is dispersion times a multiplier when dispersion > 0, otherwise ATR times a fallback multiplier. A touch is low <= leg VWAP + tolerance AND high >= leg VWAP - tolerance. An outside bar has low > leg VWAP + tolerance in a bullish leg or high < leg VWAP - tolerance in a bearish leg. Starting at the anchor, count consecutive outside bars, resetting the count to zero on every other bar; a retest occurs when a touch follows a count at least equal to the away threshold. Rebuild this count on re-anchoring; when an old anchor rolls forward, retain the existing count.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry close, fix risk distance as entry-bar risk ATR times a multiplier. Initial stop is entry minus risk distance for a long and entry plus risk distance for a short. First and final targets are entry plus their respective risk-distance multiples for a long and entry minus them for a short. If break-even is enabled, after the first target is touched on a post-entry bar without the current stop also being touched, move the stop to entry starting on the next bar. If stop and target are both touched on a bar, the stop takes precedence.
Long: accepted swing high > previous same-type swing high + current ATR times equality-tolerance multiplier.
Long: accepted swing low > previous same-type swing low + current ATR times equality-tolerance multiplier.
Short: accepted swing high < previous same-type swing high - current ATR times equality-tolerance multiplier.
Short: accepted swing low < previous same-type swing low - current ATR times equality-tolerance multiplier.
Long: a bullish leg is newly anchored AND bar index >= the greater of the sum of the pivot lookbacks and the volume-median lookback.
Short: a bearish leg is newly anchored AND bar index >= the greater of the sum of the pivot lookbacks and the volume-median lookback.
Long: position is flat AND leg direction is bullish AND retest occurs AND risk distance > 0 AND bar index >= the greater of the sum of the pivot lookbacks and the volume-median lookback.
Short: position is flat AND leg direction is bearish AND retest occurs AND risk distance > 0 AND bar index >= the greater of the sum of the pivot lookbacks and the volume-median lookback.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= active stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= active stop OR low <= final target).
