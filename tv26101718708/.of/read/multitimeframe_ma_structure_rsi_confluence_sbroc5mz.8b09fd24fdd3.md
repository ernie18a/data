Calculation: RSI swing state = Track the most recent RSI extreme: overbought when RSI of close is at or above its upper threshold, oversold when it is at or below its lower threshold. From the first overbought bar until the next oversold bar, track the highest high; from the first oversold bar until the next overbought bar, track the lowest low. Confirm each tracked extreme when the opposite RSI extreme occurs. A developing swing is marked only after a transition between extremes; on its transition bar and whenever its extreme is equaled or exceeded, compare it with the previous confirmed swing of the same type. Also compare it when it is confirmed. If no previous confirmed same-type swing exists, classify it as bullish.
Calculation: Regular RSI divergence = At confirmation of a price pivot, compare its price and RSI of close at the pivot bar with those of the immediately preceding confirmed price pivot of the same type. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-hand bars and is the extreme over the specified left-hand and right-hand bars. Bullish divergence requires a lower pivot low and higher RSI; bearish divergence requires a higher pivot high and lower RSI. Detect it on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Timeframe RSI state = Calculate RSI of close and its SMA on each selected timeframe. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; for a lower timeframe, use its last completed bar within the chart bar. The same calculations also apply when the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Recent timeframe divergence = On each selected timeframe, retain the direction and pivot-bar age of the most recently confirmed regular RSI divergence while its age is at most the divergence age limit. If bullish and bearish divergences are confirmed on the same bar, retain bearish. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; the selected timeframe can also equal the chart timeframe.
Long: A developing or newly confirmed RSI swing high or low is greater than its previous confirmed same-type swing, OR no previous confirmed same-type swing exists.
Short: A developing or newly confirmed RSI swing high or low is less than or equal to its previous confirmed same-type swing.
Long: Bullish regular RSI divergence is confirmed on the chart timeframe.
Short: Bearish regular RSI divergence is confirmed on the chart timeframe.
Long: Recent timeframe divergence is bullish.
Short: Recent timeframe divergence is bearish.
Long: Timeframe RSI is at or above its midpoint AND below its upper regime threshold.
Short: Timeframe RSI is below its midpoint AND above its lower regime threshold.
Long: Timeframe RSI is greater than its previous-bar value.
Short: Timeframe RSI is less than its previous-bar value.
Long: Timeframe RSI is greater than its SMA.
Short: Timeframe RSI is less than its SMA.
Long: The most recent timeframe RSI/SMA crossover is upward AND its age is at most the cross age limit.
Short: The most recent timeframe RSI/SMA crossover is downward AND its age is at most the cross age limit.
