Calculation: Basis = selected moving average of close (SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, VWMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, ALMA, or linear regression); use the prior bar’s close in non-repainting mode, otherwise the current bar’s close.
Calculation: Normalized basis change = (Basis − Basis from the longer trend lookback ago) / ATR over its configured lookback.
Long: Trend Color mode is enabled AND normalized basis change > neutral threshold
Short: Trend Color mode is enabled AND normalized basis change < −neutral threshold
