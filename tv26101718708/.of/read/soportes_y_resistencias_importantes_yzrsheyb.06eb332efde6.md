Calculation: candidate level = close of a confirmed local volume maximum, where its volume is at least a configurable multiple of the 20-bar volume average at that bar
Calculation: support level = candidate level below the current close, after merging nearby candidate levels by averaging their prices and retaining the highest-volume levels
Calculation: resistance level = candidate level above the current close, after merging nearby candidate levels by averaging their prices and retaining the highest-volume levels
Long: close > any support level
Short: close < any resistance level
