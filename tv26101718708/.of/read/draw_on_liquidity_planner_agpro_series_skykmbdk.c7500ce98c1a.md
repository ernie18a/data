Calculation: ATR = the average true range, falling back to current true range until ATR is available.
Calculation: Swing pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of chart bars on each side; retain the most recent specified number separately for highs and lows. A high pool is taken when a subsequent or confirmation-bar high exceeds it; a low pool is taken when a subsequent or confirmation-bar low falls below it. Taken pools remain in memory but cannot be selected.
Calculation: Side pool = the nearest untaken swing high strictly above close for the upper side, or the nearest untaken swing low strictly below close for the lower side. Optionally include the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high or low as a candidate when it is strictly on the corresponding side of close; replace a swing candidate only if the higher-timeframe candidate is strictly closer.
Calculation: Side score = 0 if no side pool exists; otherwise clamp to 0–100 the sum of 0.34 × quality + 0.26 × proximity + 0.24 × trend + 0.16 × freshness. Quality is min(100, 25 × the number of untaken same-side swing pools within an ATR-based tolerance of the selected pool), raised to at least 72 if the selected pool is from the higher timeframe. Proximity is clamp(100 − 100 × absolute distance from close to the pool / ATR / maximum distance parameter, 0, 100). For the upper side, trend is 100 if close > EMA and EMA > its value a specified number of bars ago, otherwise 62 if close > EMA, otherwise 26 if close < EMA and EMA < its earlier value, otherwise 50; reverse the inequalities for the lower side. Freshness is 62 for a higher-timeframe pool, otherwise clamp(100 − 100 × pool age in chart bars / freshness lookback, 0, 100); measure age from the pivot bar.
Long: Upper side score − lower side score > balance gap parameter.
Short: Lower side score − upper side score > balance gap parameter.
