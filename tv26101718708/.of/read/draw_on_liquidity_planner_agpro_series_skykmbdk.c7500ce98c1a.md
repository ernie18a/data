Calculation: Active buy-side pool = nearest untapped confirmed swing high above close or prior higher-timeframe high, whichever is nearer; retain recent swing pools up to the pool-memory parameter. A swing high is confirmed when its high is the maximum across the swing-strength number of chart bars before and after it. Mark it taken when a later bar’s high exceeds it.
Calculation: Active sell-side pool = nearest untapped confirmed swing low below close or prior higher-timeframe low, whichever is nearer; retain recent swing pools up to the pool-memory parameter. A swing low is confirmed when its low is the minimum across the swing-strength number of chart bars before and after it. Mark it taken when a later bar’s low falls below it.
Calculation: Prior higher-timeframe high/low = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar: prior day on intraday charts, prior week on daily charts, or prior month on higher timeframes.
Calculation: Buy-side score = clamp(0.34 × quality + 0.26 × proximity + 0.24 × trend alignment + 0.16 × freshness, 0, 100). Sell-side score uses the same formula for the sell-side pool. Quality = clamp(100 × count of untapped swing pools within equal-level tolerance × ATR of the active pool / 4, 0, 100), raised to at least 72 for a prior-period pool. Proximity = clamp(100 − pool distance / (maximum draw distance × ATR) × 100, 0, 100). Freshness = 62 for a prior-period pool; otherwise clamp(100 − pool age in chart bars / freshness window × 100, 0, 100). ATR is chart-timeframe ATR. Trend alignment uses chart-timeframe EMA and its slope over a longer lookback: buy score component is 100 when close is above a rising EMA, 62 when close is above EMA, 26 when below a falling EMA, otherwise 50; sell component is the converse. A missing side’s score is 0.
Long: an active buy-side pool exists AND buy-side score > sell-side score + balance gap
Short: an active sell-side pool exists AND sell-side score > buy-side score + balance gap
