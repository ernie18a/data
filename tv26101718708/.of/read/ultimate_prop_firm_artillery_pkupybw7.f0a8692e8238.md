Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low at a bar preceding the current bar by the right-side lookback that is the lowest low over the specified left-side and right-side lookbacks.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high at a bar preceding the current bar by the right-side lookback that is the highest high over the specified left-side and right-side lookbacks.
Calculation: Daily entry count = number of entries since the day of week last changed; reset to zero on that change.
Long: Confirmed pivot low exists on the current bar AND low <= confirmed pivot low + current ATR * zone-width multiplier AND RSI >= long threshold AND close > open AND volume >= volume SMA * volume multiplier AND New York bar-open time >= session start AND New York bar-open time <= session end AND daily entry count < daily entry limit AND position side = flat.
Short: Confirmed pivot high exists on the current bar AND high >= confirmed pivot high - current ATR * zone-width multiplier AND RSI <= short threshold AND close < open AND volume >= volume SMA * volume multiplier AND New York bar-open time >= session start AND New York bar-open time <= session end AND daily entry count < daily entry limit AND position side = flat.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR * stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR * first target multiplier for the first partial exit OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR * second target multiplier for the second partial exit OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR * third target multiplier for the remaining position OR New York bar-open time >= session end.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR * stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR * first target multiplier for the first partial exit OR low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR * second target multiplier for the second partial exit OR low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR * third target multiplier for the remaining position OR New York bar-open time >= session end.
