Calculation: Swing series = Confirm equal-left-and-right-leg pivot highs and lows using either high/low or close. Process a simultaneous high before a low. Append opposite-type pivots; replace a same-type last pivot only if the new pivot is more extreme. Retain at most the configured number of pivots. Record each pivot's bar, price, type, RSI and volume divided by max(volume SMA, 1), evaluated at the pivot bar.
Calculation: Primary wave candidate = From the latest swing, build candidates in this order: impulses using the last 3, 4, 5 and 6 pivots; zigzags using the last 3 and 4; a flat using the last 4; and a triangle using the last 6. Include only candidates with enough pivots. The candidate direction is bullish if its first pivot is a low and bearish if it is a high. Let consecutive pivot prices be p0, p1, ... and consecutive absolute price differences be w1, w2, .... Define proximity(x, target, tolerance) = max(0, 100 × (1 − abs(x − target) / tolerance)). Score each candidate as follows, with each numerical target, tolerance, weight, multiplier and threshold a parameter: impulse Fibonacci score is the average proximity of the available ratios w2/w1, w3/w1, w4/w3 and w5/w1 to their respective targets; its proportion score is a higher base score when w3 exists and w3 >= w1, otherwise a lower base score, averaged, when w4 exists, with min(100, abs(w2/w1 − w4/w3) / an alternation scale × 100). Its energy starts at a neutral score; when w3 exists, assign a high, middle or low score according to whether both, one or neither of these hold: wave-3 endpoint RSI is above the bullish RSI threshold or below the bearish RSI threshold according to direction, and its distance from neutral RSI is >= that of the wave-1 endpoint. When w5 exists, multiply energy by a penalty if wave-5 endpoint RSI is farther from neutral than wave-3 endpoint RSI. If both endpoint relative volumes are available and wave-3 relative volume > 0, replace energy with a weighted average of energy and a volume score: high, middle or low according to whether both, one or neither of wave-3 relative volume >= wave-1 relative volume and wave-3 relative volume > its threshold hold. The impulse score is the weighted sum of Fibonacci, proportion and energy divided by max(1, the sum of their weights); multiply it by a penalty if p2 fails to remain beyond p0 in the candidate direction, or, when p4 exists, p4 fails to remain beyond p1 in that direction, or, when w5 exists, w3 is shorter than both w1 and w5. Zigzag score is the average proximity of w2/w1 and, when available, w3/w1 to their respective targets; apply a penalty if p2 is not below p1 for a bullish candidate or above p1 for a bearish candidate. Flat score is the average proximity of w2/w1 and w3/w1 to their respective targets; apply a penalty if w2 is below a threshold multiple of w1. Triangle score is the percentage of the four successive comparisons w2 < w1, w3 < w2, w4 < w3 and w5 < w4 that are true; apply a penalty if fewer than a threshold number are true. Multiply each score by its family multiplier, using separate impulse and corrective multipliers selected according to whether ADX is above its trend threshold. The primary candidate is the highest-scoring candidate, with ties resolved by the candidate order above. Evaluate only when at least four swings exist.
Calculation: Swing trend = Up when the latest swing high and low are each above their respective preceding swing high and low; down when both are below their respective preceding values; otherwise neither. Require two highs and two lows for either trend.
Long: Primary wave candidate direction = bullish.
Short: Primary wave candidate direction = bearish.
Long: Latest swing high and low exist AND close > latest swing high.
Short: Latest swing high and low exist AND close < latest swing low.
Long: Swing trend = up AND close <= latest swing high AND close >= latest swing low.
Short: Swing trend = down AND close >= latest swing low AND close <= latest swing high.
