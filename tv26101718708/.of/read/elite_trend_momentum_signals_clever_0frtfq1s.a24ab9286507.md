Calculation: ATR trailing stop = let loss = sensitivity × ATR; on each bar, if previous source > previous stop, max(previous stop, previous source − loss); if previous source < previous stop, min(previous stop, previous source + loss); otherwise previous source + loss; initialize stop at 0. Source and ATR use the chart timeframe or, when MTF is enabled, the configured higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Calculation: Trend state = 1 when source > 20-bar SMA of highs, −1 when source < 20-bar SMA of lows, otherwise the prior trend state (initialize at 1). Source and highs/lows use the chart timeframe or, when MTF is enabled, the configured higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Calculation: Pulse oscillator = linear regression, with zero offset, of (source − its N-bar SMA), over N bars.
Calculation: Power = clamp(((close − daily low) / max(daily high − daily low, 0.000001) × 100 + RSI) / 2, 0, 100); use the latest available daily bar, including the still-forming daily bar.
Long: Source crosses above the ATR trailing stop AND source > 200-period EMA AND (RSI > 50 if the RSI filter is enabled) AND (ADX(14, 14) > threshold if the ADX filter is enabled) AND (volume > its 20-bar SMA if the volume filter is enabled) AND (source > fast EMA if the fast EMA filter is enabled) AND bars since the last buy or sell signal > cooldown; when candle-close confirmation is enabled, evaluate these conditions on the prior chart bar, otherwise on the current bar. Source, RSI, EMA, ATR, and volume use the chart timeframe or, when MTF is enabled, the configured higher timeframe's still-forming bar; ADX uses the chart timeframe.
Short: Source crosses below the ATR trailing stop AND source < 200-period EMA AND (RSI < 50 if the RSI filter is enabled) AND (ADX(14, 14) > threshold if the ADX filter is enabled) AND (volume > its 20-bar SMA if the volume filter is enabled) AND (source < fast EMA if the fast EMA filter is enabled) AND bars since the last buy or sell signal > cooldown; when candle-close confirmation is enabled, evaluate these conditions on the prior chart bar, otherwise on the current bar. Source, RSI, EMA, ATR, and volume use the chart timeframe or, when MTF is enabled, the configured higher timeframe's still-forming bar; ADX uses the chart timeframe.

Short: Trend state changes to −1.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed price pivot low AND the pulse oscillator at that pivot is higher than at the previous pivot low AND the pivot bars are no more than the maximum divergence lookback apart.
Short: A confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed price pivot high AND the pulse oscillator at that pivot is lower than at the previous pivot high AND the pivot bars are no more than the maximum divergence lookback apart.
Long: Power ≥ 50.
Short: Power < 50.
