Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows confirmed after the same number of bars used on each side; retain a limited number of the most recently confirmed highs and lows separately. Process new pivots before sweeps. A pivot is swept only on the first subsequent bar whose high exceeds a pivot high or whose low falls below a pivot low. Process highs before lows and each group newest to oldest; the last processed sweep determines the latest sweep direction, bar and extreme.
Calculation: Market structure shift = after the latest sweep of a pivot low, close crosses above any retained pivot high; after the latest sweep of a pivot high, close crosses below any retained pivot low. Test retained pivots newest first, including swept pivots. A new sweep replaces the pending sweep; a shift clears it. Sweeps and shifts can occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when low > high two bars earlier AND the intervening close > open; its upper edge is the current low and its lower edge is the earlier high. Bearish when high < low two bars earlier AND the intervening close < open; its lower edge is the current high and its upper edge is the earlier low. Retain a limited number of the newest gaps. Before testing entries, invalidate a bullish gap when low < its lower edge and a bearish gap when high > its upper edge.
Calculation: Shift-and-gap setup = each market structure shift replaces the previous setup and records its direction and the latest sweep bar and extreme. Starting on the shift bar, test retained gaps newest first; a qualifying gap starts at or after the sweep bar, has the shift direction and is not invalidated. The first bar touching its near edge consumes both the gap and setup even if an entry is blocked. For a bullish gap the near edge is its upper edge; for a bearish gap it is its lower edge.
Calculation: Retracement zone = on each enabled market structure shift, replace the previous zone. For a bullish shift, fix the upper endpoint at the shift bar's high and the lower endpoint at the lowest low over a separate lookback including that bar. For a bearish shift, fix the lower endpoint at the shift bar's low and the upper endpoint at the highest high over that lookback. The zone lies between two configurable retracement fractions of the endpoint difference measured from the shift bar's endpoint; one fraction is greater than the other. The first bar overlapping the zone consumes it even if an entry is blocked; overlap can occur on the shift bar.
Calculation: Session reversal setup = at the start of each configured reversal session, clear both waiting directions. During the session and within a configurable number of bars from its start, a low-pivot sweep with close above the open of the still-forming bias-timeframe bar sets bullish waiting; a high-pivot sweep with close below that open sets bearish waiting. The bias timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe when chart bars are shorter, and lower when chart bars are longer. Waiting persists after the session until the next session start or the matching market structure shift; the matching shift consumes waiting even if an entry is blocked. A sweep and shift can occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Trade levels = the stop is the swept bar's low or high for a pivot-sweep entry, the recorded sweep extreme for a shift-and-gap entry, the latest sweep extreme for a session reversal entry, and the fixed lower or upper endpoint for a bullish or bearish retracement entry. Entry price is close for pivot-sweep and session reversal entries; for other entries it is either close or the touched near gap edge or near retracement-zone edge, according to the execution setting. The target is entry price plus a configurable reward-to-risk multiple of entry price minus stop for a long, or entry price minus that multiple of stop minus entry price for a short. A trade is created only when entry price differs from stop; shift-and-gap trades additionally require the stop below long entry or above short entry. Evaluate exits on the entry bar as well. If both stop and target are reached, the stop takes precedence.
Calculation: Entry availability = overlapping entries are enabled OR no active trade exists. On each bar process pivot-high sweeps, pivot-low sweeps, shift-and-gap entries, session reversal entries and retracement entries in that order; update active trades immediately after each entry and evaluate exits after all entries.
Long: Entry availability AND an enabled, previously unswept confirmed pivot low is older than twice the pivot confirmation length AND low < pivot low AND close > pivot low.
Short: Entry availability AND an enabled, previously unswept confirmed pivot high is older than twice the pivot confirmation length AND high > pivot high AND close < pivot high.
Long: Entry availability AND the shift-and-gap setup is bullish AND low <= the qualifying bullish gap's upper edge AND high >= that edge AND (the unrestricted model is enabled OR the session-restricted model is enabled AND the bar is in one of its configured sessions).
Short: Entry availability AND the shift-and-gap setup is bearish AND high >= the qualifying bearish gap's lower edge AND low <= that edge AND (the unrestricted model is enabled OR the session-restricted model is enabled AND the bar is in one of its configured sessions).
Long: Entry availability AND bullish session reversal waiting is set AND a bullish market structure shift occurs.
Short: Entry availability AND bearish session reversal waiting is set AND a bearish market structure shift occurs.
Long: Entry availability AND an active bullish retracement zone exists AND low <= its upper edge AND high >= its lower edge.
Short: Entry availability AND an active bearish retracement zone exists AND high >= its lower edge AND low <= its upper edge.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target.
