Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = request each higher-timeframe close, high, low, or ATR with gaps filled and lookahead off: on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including a higher-timeframe bar on its closing chart bar; on the live chart bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Modified SuperTrend = for each of two independently parameterized higher timeframes, calculate on chart bars a center as the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, or VWMA of higher-timeframe close times chart volume divided by the same average of chart volume. Add and subtract a factor times higher-timeframe ATR to obtain raw upper and lower bands. Retain the previous lower band unless the raw lower band is higher or the previous chart close is below the previous lower band; retain the previous upper band unless the raw upper band is lower or the previous chart close is above the previous upper band. Treat missing previous bands as zero. Initialize direction bearish when the previous higher-timeframe ATR is unavailable; otherwise, if the previous SuperTrend equals the previous upper band, direction is bullish when chart close exceeds the upper band and bearish otherwise; if not, direction is bearish when chart close is below the lower band and bullish otherwise. SuperTrend is the lower band in the bullish direction and the upper band in the bearish direction.
Calculation: AI score = for each modified SuperTrend, label each of the most recent chart bars, including the current bar, 1 when a WMA of chart close exceeds a separately parameterized WMA of that SuperTrend, and 0 otherwise. Rank those bars by absolute distance between their SuperTrend and the current SuperTrend, preserving more recent bars first on ties. The score is the weighted mean of the labels of the nearest neighbors, with weight 1 divided by distance plus a small positive constant. Both SuperTrends use the first model's sample count, which is at least its neighbor count; their neighbor counts and WMA lengths are independently parameterized.
Calculation: Pivot strength = take nearest-rank upper-quartile highs and lower-quartile lows over chart-bar lookbacks of one, two, three, six, and seven times a base lookback, plus two zero-length lookbacks. Compare each high and low percentile with a separately parameterized reference upper-quartile high and lower-quartile low: count each percentile above the reference high as bullish and each below the reference low as bearish; count each low percentile strictly between the references as weak bullish and each high percentile strictly between them as weak bearish. Strength equals twice the bullish count minus twice the bearish count plus the weak bullish count minus the weak bearish count. The source passes zero lengths to the percentile function; if the implementation rejects zero lengths, execution stops and no decisions are produced.
Calculation: Directional filter = on chart bars, use the requested higher-timeframe high, low, and close series. Positive movement is the high change when it exceeds both the previous-low-minus-current-low change and zero, otherwise zero; negative movement is the previous-low-minus-current-low change when it exceeds both the high change and zero, otherwise zero. Divide each movement's WMA by the WMA of abs(high-low) plus abs(high-previous close) plus abs(low-previous close), and multiply by 100, to obtain positive and negative DI. ADX is the WMA of 100 times the absolute DI difference divided by their sum. All changes and WMAs are evaluated on chart bars.
Long: Either AI score equals 1 and its previous score was not 1.
Long: Either modified SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish and its AI score equals 1.
Long: Pivot strength > 0.
Long: Both modified SuperTrends are bullish AND both AI scores equal 1 AND pivot strength > 0 AND (directional filter disabled OR ADX exceeds its threshold AND positive DI > negative DI).
Short: Either AI score equals 0 and its previous score was not 0.
Short: Either modified SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish and its AI score equals 0.
Short: Pivot strength < 0.
Short: Both modified SuperTrends are bearish AND both AI scores equal 0 AND pivot strength < 0 AND (directional filter disabled OR ADX exceeds its threshold AND negative DI > positive DI).
Exit: long, neither modified SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish with its AI score equal to 1 OR pivot strength < 0.
Exit: short, neither modified SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish with its AI score equal to 0 OR pivot strength > 0.
Exit: long, an exit stop was submitted while the long exit condition held AND price reaches the submitted first SuperTrend minus its higher-timeframe ATR times its factor.
Exit: short, an exit stop was submitted while the short exit condition held AND price reaches the submitted first SuperTrend plus its higher-timeframe ATR times its factor.
