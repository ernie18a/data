Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe swing high = a high greater than the highs of the same configurable number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the bars on the right have completed. Use the same lookback for both sides.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe swing low = a low less than the lows of the same configurable number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the bars on the right have completed. Use the same lookback for both sides.
Calculation: Confirmed lower-timeframe swing high and low = the corresponding confirmed swing high and low, using a separately configurable lookback from the higher-timeframe pivots; use the last completed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Support and resistance zone boundaries = each of the two most recent confirmed lower-timeframe swing lows or highs, respectively, multiplied by one plus or minus half a configurable price-proportional zone width.
