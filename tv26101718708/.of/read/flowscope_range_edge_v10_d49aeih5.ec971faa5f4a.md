Calculation: Range = either two fixed price parameters ordered as high and low, or the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending at the previous bar. The range is valid when the current bar is in the configured session, high > low, and its size is between the configured minimum and maximum, inclusive.
Calculation: Edge width = ATR multiplied by a parameter, clamped between configured minimum and maximum widths. An edge is tested when the bar's high >= edge - edge width AND its low <= edge + edge width.
Calculation: Long reversal confirmation = valid range AND lower edge tested AND low <= range low AND close > range low AND close > open. Short reversal confirmation = valid range AND upper edge tested AND high >= range high AND close < range high AND close < open.
Calculation: Reversal readiness = initially false for both directions. On confirmation, set that direction ready, clear the opposite direction, and store the confirmation high and low; process long confirmation before short confirmation. A reversal trigger uses readiness and stored prices from the previous bar: current bar in session AND long ready AND high > stored high, or current bar in session AND short ready AND low < stored low. After confirmation updates, clear the corresponding readiness on a trigger. Readiness otherwise persists across sessions.
Calculation: Upward breakout = valid range AND close > range high + breakout buffer AND previous close <= current range high + breakout buffer. Downward breakout = valid range AND close < range low - breakout buffer AND previous close >= current range low - breakout buffer.
Calculation: Continuation state = initially inactive and not hold-ready or retest-ready in both directions. On a breakout, activate its direction, record its bar index, deactivate the opposite direction, and clear both directions' hold and retest readiness; process the upward breakout before the downward breakout. While active, set hold-ready when the lowest close over the hold lookback > current range high for an upward breakout, or the highest close over that lookback < current range low for a downward breakout. Hold readiness persists. A long retest confirmation requires a valid range AND upward hold readiness AND upper edge tested AND close > range high AND close > open; a short retest confirmation requires a valid range AND downward hold readiness AND lower edge tested AND close < range low AND close < open. Each confirmation sets retest-ready and stores its high and low, replacing any earlier retest prices. After retest confirmation, if the number of bars since breakout > the timeout AND retest-ready is false, clear that direction's active and hold-ready states. A continuation trigger uses previous-bar retest readiness and stored prices: current bar in session AND long retest-ready AND high > stored high, or current bar in session AND short retest-ready AND low < stored low; additionally, if the optional volume filter is enabled, current volume / its SMA >= the volume threshold. After all preceding updates, a continuation trigger clears its direction's active, hold-ready and retest-ready states. States otherwise persist across sessions.
Long: Long reversal confirmation OR upward breakout OR long retest confirmation OR long reversal trigger OR long continuation trigger OR long reversal readiness after current-bar updates OR upward hold readiness after current-bar updates.
Short: Short reversal confirmation OR downward breakout OR short retest confirmation OR short reversal trigger OR short continuation trigger OR short reversal readiness after current-bar updates OR downward hold readiness after current-bar updates.
