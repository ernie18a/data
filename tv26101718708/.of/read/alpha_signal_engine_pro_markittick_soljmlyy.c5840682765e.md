Calculation: Volatility regime = rank ATR as a percentage of the previous completed close against its rolling historical distribution; classify it into four ordered quartile regimes and use regime-specific scalars to adapt the band, smoothing, and filter thresholds.
Calculation: Dynamic band half-width = EMA-smoothed product of ATR and the sum of a base band multiplier, ATR divided by the previous completed close times an ATR weight, and the lesser of the previous close-to-close absolute percentage move and its rolling 95th percentile times a price-move weight. The regime, when enabled, scales these weights and the smoothing length.
Calculation: Dynamic trailing line = median price of the previous completed bar plus or minus the dynamic band half-width, trailed recursively: when the previous completed close is above the prior line, set direction bullish and line to the greater of the lower band and prior line; otherwise set direction bearish and line to the lesser of the upper band and prior line. Initialize the line at the upper band.
Calculation: Flat guard = any enabled condition holds: absolute change in the trailing line is below an ATR-scaled threshold; absolute linear-regression slope of the trailing line, divided by ATR, is below a threshold (regression lookback); previous completed volume is at or below its moving average (volume lookback); previous completed high-low range is at or below its rolling percentile (range lookback and percentile); or trailing-line change in basis points of the close is below a threshold. A breakout override disables the guard when the absolute close-to-close move exceeds an ATR multiple.
Long: Current direction is bullish AND previous displayed direction was bearish AND flat guard is false.
Short: Current direction is bearish AND previous displayed direction was bullish AND flat guard is false.
Exit: Long, Short condition.
Exit: Short, Long condition.
