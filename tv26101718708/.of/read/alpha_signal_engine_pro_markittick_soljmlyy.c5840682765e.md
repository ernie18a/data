Calculation: Regime = rank the previous bar's ATR divided by the previous close against rolling nearest-rank lower, middle, and upper percentiles of that ratio. Select the corresponding ordered regime. When adaptation is enabled, apply separately parameterized regime-specific multipliers to the band multiplier, ATR weight, price-move weight, smoothing length, stall threshold, slope threshold, volume average, range percentile, BPS threshold, and breakout threshold; otherwise use their base values. The smoothing length has a minimum of one.
Calculation: Trailing line = use the previous bar's ATR and the absolute change from the close two bars ago to the previous close, divided by the close two bars ago and capped at a rolling nearest-rank upper percentile of that normalized change. Raw half-band = ATR × (effective band multiplier + ATR / previous close × effective ATR weight + capped normalized change × effective price-move weight). Recursively smooth the raw half-band with alpha = 2 / (effective smoothing length + 1), initializing it to the first available raw half-band. Upper and lower bands equal the previous bar's median price plus and minus the smoothed half-band. Initialize the trailing line to the upper band. On each bar, if the previous close is greater than the previous trailing line, set the raw direction to long and the trailing line to the greater of the lower band and previous trailing line; otherwise set the raw direction to short and the trailing line to the lesser of the upper band and previous trailing line. Where a price or ATR is used as a guarded denominator, use the greater of that value and a small positive price increment.
Calculation: Displayed direction = initialize to long. On each bar, update it to the raw direction if the breakout override is enabled and the absolute change between the previous two closes is greater than previous-bar ATR × effective breakout threshold, OR if all enabled filters pass. Otherwise retain the previous displayed direction. The stall filter passes when the absolute change between the trailing lines one and two bars ago is at least previous-bar ATR × effective stall threshold. The slope filter passes when the absolute difference between the current and one-bar-offset values of linear regression on the trailing line shifted back one bar, divided by guarded previous-bar ATR, is at least the effective slope threshold. The volume filter passes when previous-bar volume is greater than the previous bar's volume SMA multiplied by the previous bar's effective volume multiplier. The range filter passes when the previous bar's high-low range is greater than its rolling nearest-rank percentile multiplied by the current effective range multiplier. The BPS filter passes when the absolute change between the trailing lines one and two bars ago, divided by the guarded close two bars ago and expressed in basis points, is at least the effective BPS threshold. If the volume SMA or range percentile is unavailable, its enabled filter fails.
Long: Displayed direction is long AND previous displayed direction is short.
Short: Displayed direction is short AND previous displayed direction is long.
Exit: long, displayed direction is short AND previous displayed direction is long.
Exit: short, displayed direction is long AND previous displayed direction is short.
