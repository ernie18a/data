Calculation: Previous-period high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Swing high or low = the most recent confirmed pivot high or low, using equal numbers of bars on each side; confirmation occurs after the right-side bars complete. Higher-timeframe swings use the last completed bar on each of two higher timeframes, one shorter than the other.
Calculation: Upward structure break = close > latest swing high + break buffer AND previous close <= that same swing high + break buffer. Downward structure break = close < latest swing low - break buffer AND previous close >= that same swing low - break buffer.
Calculation: Directional zone = on an upward structure break, retain the most recent bearish candle within a lookback as the long zone; on a downward structure break, retain the most recent bullish candle as the short zone. Zone bounds are either the candle's high and low or its body bounds. Replace the same-side zone on a later qualifying break. Remove it when its source candle exceeds the expiry lookback or close breaches its far edge by more than the stop buffer. A zone is external if, within a lookback ending at its creation, the corresponding previous-period sweep, previous-period breakout, or local sweep occurred. A touch occurs when the bar's high-low range overlaps the zone; remember the latest touch even if the zone later changes.
Calculation: Bias = in priority order, long on a previous-period low sweep, short on a previous-period high sweep, long when close > previous-period high + breakout distance, short when close < previous-period low - breakout distance; otherwise optionally use either long when chart close > both higher-timeframe swing highs and short when chart close < both higher-timeframe swing lows, or long when both higher-timeframe closes > their moving averages and short when both closes < their moving averages. The higher-timeframe closes and moving averages use the last completed bars of the shorter and longer higher timeframes. If neither fallback is selected or satisfied, bias is neutral. An optional fixed bias overrides this calculation.
Calculation: Entry reference = chart close, zone midpoint, near zone edge, or far zone edge, according to the selected mode. Long stop = min(zone bottom - stop buffer, entry reference - zone height × coverage fraction); short stop = max(zone top + stop buffer, entry reference + zone height × coverage fraction). Risk distance = absolute difference between entry reference and stop.
Calculation: Final target = entry reference plus or minus risk distance × target reward multiple, optionally capped at the nearest favorable obstacle: previous-period high, latest swing high, or active short-zone bottom minus target buffer for a long; previous-period low, latest swing low, or active long-zone top plus target buffer for a short. Ignore obstacles that are not strictly favorable. Reward-to-risk = absolute target-to-entry distance divided by risk distance.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop fixed at entry; optionally, once per position, increase it by a stop increment when high reaches the swing high recorded at long entry, or decrease it by that increment when low reaches the swing low recorded at short entry. Optionally, once per position, when price reaches a specified fraction of the distance from entry reference to final target, move the stop to the more protective of its current value and entry reference plus a buffer for a long or minus a buffer for a short.
Long: low < previous-period low AND close > previous-period low.
Short: high > previous-period high AND close < previous-period high.
Long: low < lowest low of the preceding local lookback AND close > that lowest low.
Short: high > highest high of the preceding local lookback AND close < that highest high.
Long: close > latest swing high + break buffer AND previous close <= that same swing high + break buffer.
Short: close < latest swing low - break buffer AND previous close >= that same swing low - break buffer.
Long: bias = long AND active long zone exists AND entry reference >= zone bottom AND entry reference <= zone top AND risk distance > 0 AND risk distance <= maximum stop distance AND reward-to-risk >= minimum AND ((anticipated mode enabled AND current bar touches zone AND (zone is external OR internal zones are allowed)) OR (confirmed mode enabled AND latest long-zone touch is within confirmation lookback AND (structure confirmation disabled OR upward structure break))) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR (low <= chart moving average AND close > chart moving average)) AND (lateral-range filter disabled OR highest high minus lowest low over its lookback > ATR × range multiplier) AND (no-pullback filter disabled OR lowest low over its lookback <= chart moving average) AND (progression cancellation disabled OR neither side has a recent zone touch with opposing close progression and no corresponding structure break) AND datetime is within the selected trading session AND (news blackout disabled OR datetime is outside the selected blackout session).
Short: bias = short AND active short zone exists AND entry reference >= zone bottom AND entry reference <= zone top AND risk distance > 0 AND risk distance <= maximum stop distance AND reward-to-risk >= minimum AND ((anticipated mode enabled AND current bar touches zone AND (zone is external OR internal zones are allowed)) OR (confirmed mode enabled AND latest short-zone touch is within confirmation lookback AND (structure confirmation disabled OR downward structure break))) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR (high >= chart moving average AND close < chart moving average)) AND (lateral-range filter disabled OR highest high minus lowest low over its lookback > ATR × range multiplier) AND (no-pullback filter disabled OR highest high over its lookback >= chart moving average) AND (progression cancellation disabled OR neither side has a recent zone touch with opposing close progression and no corresponding structure break) AND datetime is within the selected trading session AND (news blackout disabled OR datetime is outside the selected blackout session).
Calculation: Opposing close progression = for a long-zone touch, when the progression lookback is at most three, two consecutive lower closes OR current close < every close in the preceding progression lookback; otherwise current close < every close in that lookback. For a short-zone touch, reverse the inequalities. A recent touch is within the confirmation lookback.
Exit: long, low <= managed stop OR high >= entry reference + one-third of the entry-to-final-target distance OR high >= entry reference + two-thirds of that distance OR high >= final target.
Exit: short, high >= managed stop OR low <= entry reference - one-third of the entry-to-final-target distance OR low <= entry reference - two-thirds of that distance OR low <= final target.
