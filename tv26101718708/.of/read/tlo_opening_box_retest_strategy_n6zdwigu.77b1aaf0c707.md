Calculation: Opening box = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times are inside a configured daily session in a configured time zone; retain the completed box until the next session starts. Box height = box high − box low.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = current ATR × a configurable multiplier. Retest tolerance = current ATR × a separate configurable multiplier. Relative volume = current volume divided by its SMA, including the current bar; use 1 when that SMA is not positive, and treat missing volume as 0.
Calculation: Regime EMA = EMA of closes on a configurable timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar and the preceding bar. Regime slope = last completed regime EMA − preceding regime EMA; use 0 if either is unavailable.
Calculation: Qualified upward breakout = outside the opening session AND a completed box exists AND close > box high + current breakout buffer AND previous close <= box high + current breakout buffer AND (regime filter disabled OR (close > last completed regime EMA AND regime slope > 0)) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= threshold). Qualified downward breakout = outside the opening session AND a completed box exists AND close < box low − current breakout buffer AND previous close >= box low − current breakout buffer AND (regime filter disabled OR (close < last completed regime EMA AND regime slope < 0)) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= threshold).
Calculation: First breakout = the first qualified breakout in either direction after the box completes; lock its direction and bar until the next opening session. First retest = the first bar after that breakout and within a configurable maximum number of chart bars for which low <= the breakout boundary + current retest tolerance AND high >= the breakout boundary − current retest tolerance; retain its occurrence until the next opening session. A trade is used when an entry order is submitted, and resets at the next opening session.
Calculation: Planned long stop = box high − box height × a configurable stop multiplier, fixed at entry signal. Planned short stop = box low + box height × the same multiplier, fixed at entry signal. Initial risk = entry price − planned long stop for a long, or planned short stop − entry price for a short. When breakeven is enabled and initial risk > 0, permanently move the long stop to max(planned stop, entry price) after high >= entry price + initial risk, or the short stop to min(planned stop, entry price) after low <= entry price − initial risk; otherwise use the planned stop.
Long: First breakout is upward.
Short: First breakout is downward.
Long: First breakout is upward AND first retest occurs.
Short: First breakout is downward AND first retest occurs.
Long: Trade not used AND first breakout is upward AND first retest has occurred AND low <= box high + current retest tolerance AND ((outside-close confirmation enabled AND close > box high) OR (outside-close confirmation disabled AND close > open AND close >= box high − current retest tolerance)).
Short: Trade not used AND first breakout is downward AND first retest has occurred AND high >= box low − current retest tolerance AND ((outside-close confirmation enabled AND close < box low) OR (outside-close confirmation disabled AND close < open AND close <= box low + current retest tolerance)).
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR (measured-box target mode AND high >= entry-signal box high + box height × configurable target multiplier) OR (fixed-risk target mode AND high >= entry price + initial risk × configurable risk multiplier) OR (next-box mode AND a new opening session starts).
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR (measured-box target mode AND low <= entry-signal box low − box height × configurable target multiplier) OR (fixed-risk target mode AND low <= entry price − initial risk × configurable risk multiplier) OR (next-box mode AND a new opening session starts).
