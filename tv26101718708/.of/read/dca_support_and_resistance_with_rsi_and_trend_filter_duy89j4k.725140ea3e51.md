Calculation: Price bars = chart bars or Heikin Ashi bars, as selected; support and resistance use untransformed chart lows and highs. Selectable price sources are OHLC-derived series.
Calculation: Oscillator = RSI, MFI, or Connors RSI, as selected. Connors RSI is the mean of RSI of price, RSI of the signed consecutive up/down streak, and the percent rank of one-bar price rate of change; the three lookbacks are independently selectable.
Calculation: Wave trend = EMA(channel index, average lookback), where channel index = (source − EMA(source, channel lookback)) / (scale × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel lookback)), channel lookback)); its signal is an SMA of wave trend over a separate lookback.
Calculation: Bullish wave signal = wave trend crosses above its signal AND wave trend < oversold wave threshold. Bearish wave signal = wave trend crosses below its signal AND wave trend > overbought wave threshold.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a newly confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed RSI pivot low AND price low at that pivot is lower than at the previous pivot, OR the RSI pivot is lower AND the price low is higher; the pivot confirmations must be separated by the configured minimum through maximum number of bars. Bearish divergence uses RSI pivot highs and price highs, with respectively lower RSI and higher price, OR higher RSI and lower price. Each pivot requires configured left and right lookbacks and is evaluated on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Gaussian trend = apply normalized Gaussian weights exp(−0.5 × ((lag − length/2)/sigma)^2) to price for each of a sequence of increasing lengths; aggregate the resulting values by selectable mean, median, or mode. Bands equal the aggregate plus or minus a selectable multiple of SMA(high − low). Set the trend line to the lower band after price crosses above the upper band, or to the upper band after price crosses below the lower band, retaining that direction until the opposite crossing. Score is a fixed increment for each filtered value greater than the shortest-length filtered value.
Calculation: Trend score = apply enabled filters in order, starting undefined: correlation of price and bar index gives +1 if positive and −1 otherwise; the selectable moving-average Schaff trend cycle gives +1 if the preceding score is undefined or positive AND the cycle is nondecreasing and above its upper-direction threshold, −1 if the preceding score is undefined or negative AND the cycle is nonincreasing and below its lower-direction threshold, otherwise 0; MACD histogram gives +1 if the preceding score is undefined or positive AND histogram > 0, otherwise −1 if the preceding score is undefined or negative, otherwise 0; directional movement gives +1 if the preceding score is undefined or positive AND positive DI > negative DI, otherwise −1 if the preceding score is undefined or negative, otherwise 0; Gaussian trend gives 1 − score if the preceding score is undefined or positive AND price > Gaussian trend line, otherwise −score if the preceding score is undefined or negative, otherwise 0. MACD uses the same selectable moving-average type for its fast, slow, and signal averages, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Fast and slow trend scores, oscillators, wave signals, and the slow EMA use higher timeframes and the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the slow timeframe is higher than the fast timeframe. Trend-score inputs and higher-timeframe oscillator and wave inputs are shifted by one higher-timeframe bar before their calculations.
Calculation: Uptrend = trend score > 0 OR oscillator < oversold threshold AND −trend score >= entry strength threshold. Downtrend = trend score < 0 OR oscillator > overbought threshold AND trend score >= entry strength threshold.
Calculation: Oversold setup = oscillator crosses above its oversold threshold when wave and divergence filters are both disabled, OR oscillator < oversold threshold AND an enabled bullish wave signal or bullish divergence occurs. Overbought setup = oscillator crosses below its overbought threshold when wave and divergence filters are both disabled, OR oscillator > overbought threshold AND an enabled bearish wave signal or bearish divergence occurs.
Long: Oversold setup AND selected price close >= most recent chart low that equaled the lowest low over the support lookback when support filtering is enabled AND selected price close < lower Bollinger band when Bollinger filtering is enabled AND fast uptrend and fast oscillator < fast overbought threshold when the fast trend filter is enabled AND slow uptrend and slow oscillator < slow overbought threshold when the slow trend filter is enabled AND no enabled slow bearish wave full-exit signal AND position side is flat or long AND selected price close >= last completed higher-timeframe slow EMA when both trade directions are enabled and the position is flat.
Short: Overbought setup AND selected price close <= most recent chart high that equaled the highest high over the resistance lookback when resistance filtering is enabled AND selected price close > upper Bollinger band when Bollinger filtering is enabled AND fast downtrend and fast oscillator > fast oversold threshold when the fast trend filter is enabled AND slow downtrend and slow oscillator > slow oversold threshold when the slow trend filter is enabled AND no enabled slow bullish wave full-exit signal AND position side is flat or short AND selected price close <= last completed higher-timeframe slow EMA when both trade directions are enabled and the position is flat.
Exit: long, enabled fast bearish wave full-exit signal AND selected price close > entry price × (1 + fast signal profit threshold), OR enabled slow bearish wave full-exit signal AND selected price close > entry price × (1 + slow signal profit threshold).
Exit: short, enabled fast bullish wave full-exit signal AND selected price close < entry price × (1 − fast signal profit threshold), OR enabled slow bullish wave full-exit signal AND selected price close < entry price × (1 − slow signal profit threshold).
Exit: long, overbought setup AND selected price close > entry price × (1 + trend-exit profit threshold) AND ((fast full-exit filter enabled AND fast uptrend is false AND (slow uptrend is false OR slow trend filter is disabled)) OR (slow full-exit filter enabled AND slow uptrend is false) OR (fast trend score > fixed strength floor AND fast trend score < fast exit strength threshold) OR (slow trend score > fixed strength floor AND slow trend score < slow exit strength threshold)).
Exit: short, oversold setup AND selected price close < entry price × (1 − trend-exit profit threshold) AND ((fast full-exit filter enabled AND fast downtrend is false) OR (slow full-exit filter enabled AND slow downtrend is false) OR fast trend score > −fast exit strength threshold OR slow trend score > −slow exit strength threshold).
