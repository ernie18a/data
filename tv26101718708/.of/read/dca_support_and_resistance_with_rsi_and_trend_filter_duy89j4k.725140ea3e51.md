Calculation: Selected oscillator = RSI, MFI, or Connors RSI, calculated from the configured price source; Connors RSI is the mean of RSI, RSI of consecutive up/down streak length, and the percentile rank of one-bar rate of change.
Calculation: WaveTrend bullish signal = the channel-smoothed WaveTrend line crosses above its 4-bar average below the oversold level; bearish signal = it crosses below its average above the overbought level.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low; hidden bullish divergence = price makes a higher pivot low while RSI makes a lower pivot low. Bearish and hidden bearish divergence use the corresponding pivot highs with price/RSI making higher/lower or lower/higher highs, respectively; the prior matching pivot must be within the configured pivot-age range.
Calculation: Support = latest low equal to the rolling lowest low; resistance = latest high equal to the rolling highest high.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = the moving average of price plus/minus configured multiples of price standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Trend score = +1 when the configured MACD histogram is positive, otherwise −1; MACD uses a shorter-period and a longer-period moving average, then compares their difference with its signal average. Optional enabled trend calculations further constrain this score in source order: Schaff Trend Cycle, ADX, and Gaussian trend.
Calculation: Fast and slow trend states and oscillators = the corresponding trend score and selected oscillator calculated on the last completed bar of their respective higher timeframes.
Calculation: Average entry price = position cost divided by absolute position size.
Long: (chart oscillator crosses above its oversold threshold when WaveTrend and divergence signals are both disabled OR chart oscillator is below its oversold threshold AND (chart WaveTrend bullish signal when enabled OR bullish/hidden bullish divergence when enabled) OR fast higher-timeframe oscillator is below its oversold threshold AND fast higher-timeframe WaveTrend bullish signal AND additional fast entries enabled AND this is a new fast-timeframe bar) AND (close >= support when support/resistance is enabled) AND (close < lower Bollinger band when Bollinger bands are enabled) AND (fast trend is up when fast trend filter is enabled) AND (slow trend is up OR open trades are below the configured slow-entry threshold OR the fast-timeframe entry signal applies when slow filter is enabled) AND fast oscillator < its overbought threshold when fast filter is enabled AND slow oscillator < its overbought threshold when slow filter is enabled AND within configured backtest date range AND entry enabled AND bars since last entry >= cooldown AND current bar is not the last exit bar AND position size >= 0 AND (trade mode is LONG OR trade mode is BOTH and (close >= slow-timeframe EMA or position size > 0)).
Short: (chart oscillator crosses below its overbought threshold when WaveTrend and divergence signals are both disabled OR chart oscillator is above its overbought threshold AND (chart WaveTrend bearish signal when enabled OR bearish/hidden bearish divergence when enabled) OR fast higher-timeframe oscillator is above its overbought threshold AND fast higher-timeframe WaveTrend bearish signal AND additional fast entries enabled AND this is a new fast-timeframe bar) AND (close <= resistance when support/resistance is enabled) AND (close > upper Bollinger band when Bollinger bands are enabled) AND (fast trend is down when fast trend filter is enabled) AND (slow trend is down OR open trades are below the configured slow-entry threshold OR the fast-timeframe entry signal applies when slow filter is enabled) AND fast oscillator > its oversold threshold when fast filter is enabled AND slow oscillator > its oversold threshold when slow filter is enabled AND within configured backtest date range AND entry enabled AND bars since last entry >= cooldown AND current bar is not the last exit bar AND position size <= 0 AND (trade mode is SHORT OR trade mode is BOTH and (close <= slow-timeframe EMA or position size < 0)).
