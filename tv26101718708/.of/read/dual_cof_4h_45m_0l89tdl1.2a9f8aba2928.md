Calculation: Active candle of focus (COF) = For each of two independently selected COF timeframes, initialize from the first available last-completed bar. On each new COF-timeframe bar, replace the active COF with the last-completed bar if its close is strictly above the active COF high or strictly below the active COF low. Store the active COF high, low, open, and close. The two COF timeframes are ordered longer and shorter; each may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe. When a COF timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, sample its last available intrabar as request.security does.
Calculation: Entry level = active COF low + a configurable fraction of the active COF range; use a lower fraction for a bullish COF and a higher fraction for a bearish COF.
Calculation: Entry state = Track separately for each COF timeframe. Reset on COF replacement. Starting on a chart bar without COF replacement, arm immediately if the return requirement is disabled; otherwise arm when chart high > bullish entry level for a bullish COF or chart low < bearish entry level for a bearish COF. Arming and the first zone hit may occur on the same chart bar. Once the zone is hit, retain that state until COF replacement. After a zone hit and before a confirmed entry, each newly available last-completed confirmation-timeframe bar sets the confirmation close to its close if that close is within the active COF high and low, inclusive; otherwise clear the confirmation close. The confirmation timeframe is shorter than both COF timeframes and may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; when lower, sample its last available intrabar. Allow only one confirmed entry per active COF. Use a configurable momentum distance in price units.
Long: For either COF timeframe, a COF is initialized or replaced AND its close >= its open.
Short: For either COF timeframe, a COF is initialized or replaced AND its close < its open.
Long: For either COF timeframe, the active COF close >= its open AND the COF was not replaced on the current chart bar AND the entry state is armed AND the zone has not previously been hit AND chart low <= bullish entry level.
Short: For either COF timeframe, the active COF close < its open AND the COF was not replaced on the current chart bar AND the entry state is armed AND the zone has not previously been hit AND chart high >= bearish entry level.
Long: For either COF timeframe, the active COF close >= its open AND its zone has been hit AND a confirmation close exists AND no confirmed entry has occurred for that COF AND chart close >= confirmation close + momentum distance.
Short: For either COF timeframe, the active COF close < its open AND its zone has been hit AND a confirmation close exists AND no confirmed entry has occurred for that COF AND chart close <= confirmation close - momentum distance.
