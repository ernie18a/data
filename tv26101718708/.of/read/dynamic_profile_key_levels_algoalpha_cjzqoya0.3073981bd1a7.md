Calculation: Volume profile = On each scheduled refresh after the minimum history, use a fixed lookback or the bars since the latest confirmed symmetric high or low pivot, capped by the lookback. Divide the window's high-low range into equal price rows, with row width at least a price increment parameter. Allocate each candle's nonnegative volume uniformly over its low-high range; allocate a zero-range candle entirely to its row. Optionally use all completed lower-timeframe bars within each completed chart bar, falling back to the chart bar when lower-timeframe volume is unavailable or zero. The point of control is the center of the first row with maximum volume. Starting at that row, add the larger adjacent row volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until the target share of total volume is reached; the outer row edges are the value-area boundaries.
Calculation: Profile candidates = Select the point of control, both value-area boundaries, local volume peaks and troughs, and the highest and lowest ranked volume rows according to enabled level types and counts. Smooth row volumes with a centered moving average only for peak and trough detection. A peak or trough must exceed or fall below at least one neighboring smoothed row within its detection radius, have no more extreme neighbor, and lose equal-value ties to the lower-index row; exclude edge rows for troughs. Node rows must have positive unsmoothed volume and meet the minimum fraction of the maximum row volume. Select peaks in descending smoothed-volume order, troughs in ascending order, then ranked rows in descending or ascending unsmoothed-volume order, excluding rows already selected except value-area rows. A line has equal upper and lower bounds at its row center, or at the value-area boundary; a zone spans the selected row and the configured number of adjacent rows on each side, clipped to the profile.
Calculation: Active levels = Evaluate reactions before each profile refresh. At refresh, match each candidate to the nearest unmatched active level of the same type within the configured row-width tolerance for nodes or a small price-increment tolerance for other levels; matched levels retain their original bounds and state. Stop unmatched non-node levels and, optionally, unmatched nodes. Create unmatched candidates unless overlap prevention is enabled and their node bounds overlap an active node or point-of-control level. Initialize each level's side from the creation close: above its upper bound = 1, below its lower bound = -1, otherwise 0. Levels cannot signal on their creation bar. Stop a level after a break if configured, or when its age reaches the expiry limit. Enforce the storage limit by removing the oldest stopped level first, otherwise the oldest active level. Profile updates and level creation occur only within the configured calculation-history window.
Calculation: Level reaction = For each active eligible level on every completed chart bar, let side be 1 if close is above its upper bound, -1 if close is below its lower bound, otherwise 0; touching means high is at least the lower bound AND low is at most the upper bound. On the first bar of each touching episode, record a pending touch and the level's most recent nonzero side. A break occurs when the current side and previous nonzero side are both nonzero and differ. A retest occurs when there is no break, a retest is pending, the bar is after the break within the retest window, touching is true, and side equals the recorded break side. A rejection occurs when neither break nor retest occurs, a touch is pending, its recorded side is nonzero, and current side equals that recorded side. Break takes priority over retest, then rejection. Every break records its bar and side and starts a pending retest only if levels continue after breaks; a successful retest or expiry of its window clears the pending retest. A break, retest, or pending touch with nonzero current side clears the pending touch. Update the stored side whenever current side is nonzero. These state updates occur even when a reaction fails the signal filters.
Long: An eligible level has a break, retest, or rejection AND its current side = 1 AND the time since its last accepted reaction meets the cooldown AND either the minimum relative-volume filter is disabled OR current volume divided by the preceding bar's volume moving average meets the minimum. Eligible levels are nodes, optionally including enabled fixed point-of-control and value-area levels. Record the accepted reaction time for that level.
Short: An eligible level has a break, retest, or rejection AND its current side = -1 AND the time since its last accepted reaction meets the cooldown AND either the minimum relative-volume filter is disabled OR current volume divided by the preceding bar's volume moving average meets the minimum. Eligible levels are nodes, optionally including enabled fixed point-of-control and value-area levels. Record the accepted reaction time for that level.
