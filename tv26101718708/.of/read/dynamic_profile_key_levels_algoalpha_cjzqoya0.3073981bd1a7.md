Calculation: Profile rows = divide the price range of the selected completed chart bars into configurable equal-width rows; distribute each bar’s volume uniformly across the rows its low–high range overlaps
Calculation: Profile levels = POC at the highest-volume row; value-area boundaries by expanding from the POC toward the adjacent row with greater volume until the configured share of total volume is covered; local volume peaks and troughs from smoothed row volumes; ranked rows by row volume
Calculation: Active key levels = enabled profile levels, retained until broken, expired, or replaced according to level settings; each has a price or zone bounds and a side set by the last close above (+1) or below (-1) it
Calculation: Level event = on a confirmed chart bar after the level’s creation, touched when high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top; current side is +1 when close > zone top, -1 when close < zone bottom, otherwise 0; break when prior side and current side are nonzero and differ; rejection when a touched level closes on its prior nonzero side; retest when, after a break, price touches the level and closes on the break side within the configured window
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Long: Any bullish break OR bullish rejection OR bullish retest at an eligible active key level AND relative volume >= configured minimum (or minimum is zero) AND bars since that level’s prior signal >= configured cooldown
Short: Any bearish break OR bearish rejection OR bearish retest at an eligible active key level AND relative volume >= configured minimum (or minimum is zero) AND bars since that level’s prior signal >= configured cooldown
