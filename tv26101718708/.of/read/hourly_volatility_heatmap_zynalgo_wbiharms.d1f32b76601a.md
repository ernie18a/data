Calculation: Hour statistics = At each new local day, use the completed higher-timeframe daily bars in a rolling lookback. For each hour, average the high-minus-low ranges of all chart bars starting in that hour; count the days whose first occurrence of the day's highest high or lowest low was in that hour; and divide bullish completed hourly candles by bullish plus bearish completed hourly candles, excluding unchanged candles and using 50% when there are none. The hourly candles are completed higher-timeframe bars when the chart timeframe is lower. Reversal frequency is the sum of the high-hour and low-hour counts divided by twice the number of tracked days. Divide each hour's average range and reversal frequency by their respective maximum across all hours, using zero when the maximum is zero; their mean is the hot-hour score. Rank scores descending, breaking ties by earlier hour. Before the first completed day, every rank points to the first hour and every bias is 50%.
Calculation: Sweep RSI = RSI at the most recent bar attaining the relevant highest high or lowest low in the preceding swing lookback.
Calculation: Trade levels = The initial stop is the signal bar's low for a long or high for a short; risk is the positive distance from the next bar's open to that stop. Targets are entry price plus risk multiplied by each target parameter for a long, or minus it for a short. The final target is the third target if enabled, otherwise the second if enabled, otherwise the first. The stop is initially the initial stop, moves to entry after the first target is reached, and moves to the first target after the enabled second target is reached. Test the existing stop before the final target, then update target progress; the updated stop applies from the next bar.
Long: Current hour rank is within the selected top ranks AND low <= the lowest low of the preceding swing lookback AND close > open AND (bias filter disabled OR current hour bullish share >= 50%) AND (RSI divergence filter disabled OR current RSI > sweep RSI).
Short: Current hour rank is within the selected top ranks AND high >= the highest high of the preceding swing lookback AND close < open AND (bias filter disabled OR current hour bullish share <= 50%) AND (RSI divergence filter disabled OR current RSI < sweep RSI).
Long: The preceding bar satisfies the long condition AND its distance from the previous accepted signal is at least the cooldown, or no previous signal exists, AND (trade mode disabled OR stability filter disabled OR no trade was open at that signal) AND (trade mode disabled OR alternation filter disabled OR the previous accepted signal was not long) AND no new accepted signal occurs on the current bar AND current open > preceding bar low.
Short: The preceding bar satisfies the short condition AND its distance from the previous accepted signal is at least the cooldown, or no previous signal exists, AND (trade mode disabled OR stability filter disabled OR no trade was open at that signal) AND (trade mode disabled OR alternation filter disabled OR the previous accepted signal was not short) AND no new accepted signal occurs on the current bar AND current open < preceding bar high.
Exit: long, trade mode enabled AND (low <= current stop OR high >= final target OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration).
Exit: short, trade mode enabled AND (high >= current stop OR low <= final target OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration).
