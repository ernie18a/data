Calculation: Safe division = numerator / denominator, or zero when the absolute denominator is at or below a small tolerance; clamp(x, a, b) limits x to [a, b]. Unavailable historical values used in differences are replaced by the current value.
Calculation: Candle measures = range is max(high - low, minimum price increment); body is abs(close - open); body ratio is clamp(body / range, 0, 1); close position is clamp((close - low) / range, 0, 1); upper wick is max(high - max(open, close), 0); lower wick is max(min(open, close) - low, 0); wick pressure is clamp((lower wick - upper wick) / range, -1, 1); expansion is clamp(range / SMA(range), 0, expansion cap). Use safe division throughout.
Calculation: Trend measures = fast and slow EMAs of close, with the fast lookback shorter; phase line is HMA of typical price; phase slope is phase line minus its value at a prior lag; safe ATR is max(ATR, minimum price increment); phase distance is safe division of close - phase line by safe ATR. Bullish structure requires fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > phase line AND phase slope > 0; bearish structure requires fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < phase line AND phase slope < 0.
Calculation: Momentum change = change in safe division of EMA(change in close) by safe ATR. Directional pressure change = change in safe division of +DI - -DI by max(+DI + -DI, a positive floor). ADX and DI use their respective smoothing lookbacks.
Calculation: Pressure index = clamp(smoothed pressure / highest absolute smoothed pressure over a lookback, -1, 1), or zero if that highest value is zero. Smoothed pressure is EMA(EMA(bull raw) - EMA(bear raw)), using equal inner lookbacks and a shorter outer lookback. Bull raw is the weighted sum of body ratio when close > open, close position, max(wick pressure, 0), and max(expansion - 1, 0); bear raw uses body ratio when close < open, 1 - close position, max(-wick pressure, 0), and the same expansion term. Use the same corresponding weights for both sides. Pressure change is pressure index minus its previous value.
Calculation: Volume measures = volume is available when it is present AND > 0. Relative volume is volume / SMA(volume) when volume is available AND the SMA > 0, otherwise 1. Directional volume is volume × (2 × close position - 1) when volume is available, otherwise 0. Volume flow is clamp(EMA(directional volume) / SMA(abs(directional volume)), a negative cap, a positive cap) when volume is available AND its denominator > 0, otherwise 0. Effort result is relative volume / max(abs(close - previous close) / safe ATR, a positive floor), using safe division.
Calculation: Bull volume confirmation = volume filtering disabled OR volume unavailable OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume AND volume flow > 0. Bear volume confirmation uses volume flow < 0. Bull effort confirmation = volume filtering disabled OR volume unavailable OR close > open AND volume flow > 0 AND (effort result <= effort threshold OR body ratio >= body threshold). Bear effort confirmation uses close < open AND volume flow < 0.
Calculation: Fading phase = abs(phase slope / safe ATR) < slope threshold OR abs(phase slope / safe ATR) < its previous absolute value. Body shrinking = body < previous bar's SMA(body), substituting current body if unavailable. Top exhaustion is the count of body shrinking, fading phase, momentum change < 0, directional pressure change < 0, upper wick / range >= wick threshold, and ADX >= ADX threshold AND ADX <= previous ADX. Bottom exhaustion substitutes momentum change > 0, directional pressure change > 0, and lower wick / range >= wick threshold.
Calculation: Reversal setup = on each completed bar, bullish structure AND high >= highest high of the preceding extreme lookback starts or replaces a top setup with current bar index and top exhaustion; otherwise bearish structure AND low <= lowest low of the preceding extreme lookback starts or replaces a bottom setup with current bar index and bottom exhaustion. Otherwise update an existing setup's score to the maximum of its score and the current matching exhaustion count. Clear a setup when its age exceeds its lifetime or after either final signal. An armed setup has the matching side AND score >= exhaustion threshold.
Calculation: Bull evidence = sum of a full weight for each of close > fast EMA, close > phase line, phase slope > 0, momentum change > 0, directional pressure change > 0, and pressure index > 0; plus a smaller equal weight for each of pressure change > 0, bull volume confirmation, bull effort confirmation, and close > open AND close position >= upper close-position threshold. Bear evidence uses the opposite inequalities, bear confirmations, and close < open AND close position <= lower close-position threshold.
Calculation: Raw long = completed bar AND ((bullish structure AND phase distance >= positive distance threshold AND bull evidence >= entry threshold AND bull evidence > bear evidence + evidence margin) OR (armed bottom setup AND close > open AND close > fast EMA AND momentum change > 0 AND bull evidence >= reversal threshold)). Raw short = completed bar AND ((bearish structure AND phase distance <= negative distance threshold AND bear evidence >= entry threshold AND bear evidence > bull evidence + evidence margin) OR (armed top setup AND close < open AND close < fast EMA AND momentum change < 0 AND bear evidence >= reversal threshold)).
Calculation: Signal state = initially neutral; set to long after a final long signal and short after a final short signal; retain it otherwise. Evaluate both final signals using the previous state, then update the state and clear the reversal setup after a signal.
Calculation: Structured trendline = EMA(2 × blend - EMA(blend, smoothing lookback), smoothing lookback), where blend = fast line EMA × (1 - volatility weight) + slow line EMA × volatility weight; both line EMAs use line raw = EMA(typical price, slow trend lookback) + safe ATR × pressure factor × pressure index, and the fast line lookback is shorter than the slow line lookback. Volatility weight is clamp((ATR / SMA(ATR) - lower volatility bound) / volatility range, 0, 1), using safe division and the normalization lookback.
Long: Raw long AND raw short = false AND signal state != long AND bull evidence >= bear evidence + switch advantage.
Short: Raw short AND raw long = false AND signal state != short AND bear evidence >= bull evidence + switch advantage.
Long: Structured trendline >= its value at a prior slope lag, substituting its current value when unavailable.
Short: Structured trendline < its value at the same prior slope lag, substituting its current value when unavailable.
