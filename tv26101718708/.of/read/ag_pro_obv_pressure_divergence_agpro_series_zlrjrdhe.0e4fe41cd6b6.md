Calculation: Smoothed OBV = EMA of cumulative volume added when close rises, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged when close is equal.
Calculation: Pivot = a high or low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same kind, using smoothed OBV and ATR at each pivot. Evaluate the decision on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Divergence quality = clamp the sum of: capped positive price displacement divided by ATR and multiplied by a weight; capped positive smoothed OBV displacement divided by its rolling standard deviation and multiplied by a weight; capped pivot separation divided by minimum pivot separation and multiplied by a weight; one of two weights according to whether a shorter EMA of smoothed OBV is above its longer EMA for bullish divergence or below it for bearish divergence; one of two weights according to whether the EMA of the one-bar change in smoothed OBV is positive for bullish divergence or negative for bearish divergence; one of two weights according to whether the pivot is within an ATR-scaled distance of the rolling lowest low for bullish divergence or rolling highest high for bearish divergence; and one of three weights according to whether the shorter close EMA is below, above, or equal to the longer close EMA for bullish divergence, with the above and below weights reversed for bearish divergence. Use the newer pivot's values, a positive denominator floor for ATR, and a substitute denominator when the OBV standard deviation is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Pending divergence = the latest accepted divergence of its direction, replacing any earlier pending divergence of that direction. Store its pivot index, pivot price, and opposite extreme of the pivot bar. Evaluate confirmation immediately on the acceptance bar and on each subsequent bar. Resolve it on confirmation, otherwise on invalidation, otherwise when current bar index minus pivot index exceeds the confirmation-window parameter; confirmation takes precedence over invalidation and expiry, and invalidation takes precedence over expiry.
Long: Shorter close EMA > longer close EMA.
Short: Shorter close EMA < longer close EMA.
Long: A newly confirmed low pivot is below the preceding low pivot AND their low-price difference >= ATR at the newer pivot multiplied by the minimum swing parameter AND smoothed OBV at the newer pivot > smoothed OBV at the preceding pivot AND pivot separation >= minimum separation AND (quality >= quality threshold OR weak events are enabled) AND (no prior accepted bullish event exists OR newer pivot index minus prior accepted bullish pivot index >= cooldown OR quality >= premium threshold).
Short: A newly confirmed high pivot is above the preceding high pivot AND their high-price difference >= ATR at the newer pivot multiplied by the minimum swing parameter AND smoothed OBV at the newer pivot < smoothed OBV at the preceding pivot AND pivot separation >= minimum separation AND (quality >= quality threshold OR weak events are enabled) AND (no prior accepted bearish event exists OR newer pivot index minus prior accepted bearish pivot index >= cooldown OR quality >= premium threshold).
Long: A bullish divergence is pending AND (close > the pending pivot bar's high when close-based confirmation is selected OR high > that high when intrabar confirmation is selected) AND shorter EMA of smoothed OBV > longer EMA of smoothed OBV.
Short: A bearish divergence is pending AND (close < the pending pivot bar's low when close-based confirmation is selected OR low < that low when intrabar confirmation is selected) AND shorter EMA of smoothed OBV < longer EMA of smoothed OBV.
Calculation: Bullish pending invalidation = low < pending low pivot price; bearish pending invalidation = high > pending high pivot price.
