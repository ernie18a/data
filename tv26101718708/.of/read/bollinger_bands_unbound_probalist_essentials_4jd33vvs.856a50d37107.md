Calculation: Bollinger bands = SMA or EMA of a selected price source as the basis, plus and minus a configurable multiple of the standard deviation of the same source over the same lookback.
Long: On the bar a configurable confirmation delay ago, close > lower band AND close < upper band AND the preceding close < its lower band AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI of the source on that preceding bar <= an oversold threshold AND bars since the previous long signal >= the per-direction signal spacing.
Short: On the bar a configurable confirmation delay ago, close < upper band AND close > lower band AND the preceding close > its upper band AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI of the source on that preceding bar >= an overbought threshold AND bars since the previous short signal >= the per-direction signal spacing.
Exit: long, high >= Bollinger basis AND the current bar is within the configurable horizon from the long signal, including the signal bar AND no earlier bar since that signal has high >= its Bollinger basis.
Exit: short, low <= Bollinger basis AND the current bar is within the configurable horizon from the short signal, including the signal bar AND no earlier bar since that signal has low <= its Bollinger basis.
