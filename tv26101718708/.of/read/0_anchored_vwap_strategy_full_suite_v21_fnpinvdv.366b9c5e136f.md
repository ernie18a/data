Calculation: Smoothed stochastic RSI = calculate RSI of a selected OHLC-derived source, then its stochastic over a separate lookback; smooth with a selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, or volume-and-linear-time-weighted MA. The latter weights each observation by its volume times its linear recency weight. The signal is an SMA of the smoothed stochastic RSI over a separate smoothing length.
Calculation: High and low anchored VWAPs = initialize active and candidate high anchors to the first high, active and candidate low anchors to the first low, and state to neutral. On each bar, in this order: reset the candidate high anchor to the current high if the signal is below the lower band OR high exceeds the candidate high; reset the candidate low anchor to the current low if the signal is above the upper band OR low is below the candidate low. Reset the active high anchor if high exceeds it and the active low anchor if low is below it. Each reset starts that anchor's volume and price-times-volume sums with the current bar. Otherwise add the current bar to those sums. Use HLC3 for both prices, or optionally high for the high anchors and low for the low anchors. Set state to lower if the signal is below the lower band, otherwise set it to upper if the signal is above the upper band; otherwise retain state. If active high exceeds candidate high AND state is upper AND smoothed stochastic RSI is below its signal AND below the lower reversal threshold, replace the active high anchor and sums with the candidate high anchor and sums. If active low is below candidate low AND state is lower AND smoothed stochastic RSI is above its signal AND above the upper reversal threshold, replace the active low anchor and sums with the candidate low anchor and sums. Each active VWAP is its active price-times-volume sum divided by its active volume sum.
Calculation: Trend moving average = selectable SMA or EMA of close on the chart timeframe, or on a selected higher or lower timeframe. On historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on a realtime bar use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the latest available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Entry reference price = selectable chart-bar open or close.
Long: Entry reference price > high anchored VWAP AND datetime is within the configured inclusive backtest period AND position is flat or short AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > trend moving average) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a configured factor) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no exit has been recorded OR bars since the most recent closed trade >= cooldown length).
Short: Entry reference price < low anchored VWAP AND datetime is within the configured inclusive backtest period AND position is flat or long AND short trading is enabled AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < trend moving average) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a configured factor) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no exit has been recorded OR bars since the most recent closed trade >= cooldown length).
Exit: long, entry reference price < low anchored VWAP AND (minimum hold is disabled OR bars since entry >= minimum hold length).
Exit: short, entry reference price > high anchored VWAP AND (minimum hold is disabled OR bars since entry >= minimum hold length).
Exit: long, ATR stop is enabled AND low <= entry signal bar's close minus its ATR multiplied by a configured factor.
Exit: short, ATR stop is enabled AND high >= entry signal bar's close plus its ATR multiplied by a configured factor.
Exit: long, partial take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price multiplied by one plus the configured profit fraction; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: short, partial take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price multiplied by one minus the configured profit fraction; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: long or short, current datetime is outside the configured backtest period AND previous bar's datetime was inside it.
