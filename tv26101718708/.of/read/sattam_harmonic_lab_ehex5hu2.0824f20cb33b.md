Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = alternating high and low extrema; use only pivots known by the current bar. Candidate legs may skip intervening pivots only when those pivots stay within the leg’s endpoint prices.
Calculation: Harmonic completion = an alternating X-A-B-C-D pivot sequence whose AB/XA, BC/AB, CD/BC, and AD/XA ratios (or the family-specific substitute ratios for Cypher, AB=CD, and 5-0) fall within the selected family’s completion bands, expanded by the configured error tolerance; D must lie in the intersection of the family’s completion bands and reverse from C. AB=CD also requires its configured duration symmetry.
Calculation: Classical completion = double top/bottom has two same-side extrema within the configured height tolerance, a preceding approach, contained intervening legs, and a break of the intervening pivot; triangle has contracting boundaries and a close beyond the projected boundary; head-and-shoulders has a dominant head, approximately level neckline, shoulders within configured symmetry and ratio tolerances, and a break beyond the outer neckline. 3-Drive has three same-direction drives with alternating retracements and configured extension/retracement ratios.
Calculation: Pattern side = long when D is a swing low or the formation is bullish; short when D is a swing high or the formation is bearish. Families: Gartley, Butterfly, Bat, Crab, Alternate Bat, Deep Crab, Cypher, AB=CD, White Swan, Black Swan, Shark, Nen Star, Leonardo, Partizan, 5-0, Anti-Gartley, Anti-Butterfly, Anti-Bat, Anti-Crab, Anti-Shark, Anti-Cypher, Anti-Nen Star, Double Bottom, Double Top, Inverse Head and Shoulders, Head and Shoulders, ascending/descending/symmetrical triangle, and 3-Drive.
Calculation: Entry level = D plus the pattern side multiplied by the configured percentage of the selected pattern leg; the selected leg is AD for configured families/modes and CD otherwise. Target levels = D plus the pattern side multiplied by the configured target percentages of that same leg, or entry plus the configured reward/risk multiple of initial risk. Initial stop = the adverse edge of the validation band, or the configured manual percentage from entry; break-even and trailing modes update the stop after their configured trigger.
Long: A bullish pattern is detected and accepted at bar close when its completion geometry and configured size, direction, session, trend, validation-zone, entry-side, quality, volume, and active-pattern filters pass; enter when price touches or closes above its entry level, or when a bullish engulfing/pin bar touches its validation zone, according to the selected entry trigger, provided the pattern is not invalidated or already beyond Target 1.
Short: A bearish pattern is detected and accepted at bar close when its completion geometry and configured size, direction, session, trend, validation-zone, entry-side, quality, volume, and active-pattern filters pass; enter when price touches or closes below its entry level, or when a bearish engulfing/pin bar touches its validation zone, according to the selected entry trigger, provided the pattern is not invalidated or already beyond Target 1.
Exit: long, the selected stop trigger is at or below the stop, or price reaches the configured finishing target (Target 1 or Target 2).
Exit: short, the selected stop trigger is at or above the stop, or price reaches the configured finishing target (Target 1 or Target 2).
