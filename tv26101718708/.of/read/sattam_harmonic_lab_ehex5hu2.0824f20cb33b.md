Calculation: Swing pivots = For each enabled lookback, at each chart-bar close identify highs equal to the rolling highest high and lows equal to the rolling lowest low. Maintain alternating extremes, replacing a same-side endpoint only with a more extreme price and appending an opposite endpoint only when it is beyond the previous endpoint. On a two-sided extreme bar, use the configured continuation, reversal, or continuation-then-reversal rule. Alternatively, use highs and lows confirmed by the configured numbers of left and right bars, discard bars confirmed as both, and maintain alternating extremes in the same way. In developing-pivot mode, test the current rolling extreme as a provisional endpoint without changing the confirmed stream. Multiple enabled lookbacks are integer multiples of the shortest lookback.
Calculation: Pattern candidates = Enumerate the latest alternating swing endpoints as X,A,B,C,D, or A,B,C,D for AB=CD. When nested search is enabled, allow skipped alternating pivots only when every skipped price lies between its leg's endpoints; enumerate nearest endpoints first up to the candidate limit. Use only candidates within the configured minimum and maximum duration, minimum C-to-D duration, and minimum price height relative to D. AB=CD additionally satisfies the optional minimum and maximum CD-to-AB duration ratios.
Calculation: Harmonic completion zone = For each enabled harmonic family, use its configurable lower and upper ratio bounds, widened by the error allowance, for AB/XA, BC/AB, CD/BC and AD/XA; Cypher instead uses AB/XA, XC/XA and CD/XC, AB=CD uses BC/AB, CD/BC and CD/AB, and 5-0 uses AB/XA, BC/AB and CD/BC. All lengths are absolute pivot-price differences. Require alternating price geometry, the family-specific containment of B within XA and C within AB, and the family-specific requirement that D remain inside or extend beyond X. Intersect the price intervals implied by the completion ratios; D must reverse C and, for a CD-extension family, pass B. The intersection is the completion zone.
Calculation: Classical formation = A double bottom or top has two same-side extrema within the configured height-relative tolerance, a contained intervening neck and an approach from beyond that neck; a later high reaches the neck for a bottom or a later low reaches it for a top. A head-and-shoulders formation has alternating left shoulder, first neck, head, second neck and right shoulder, with allowed contained intermediate swings; the head exceeds both shoulders and is the corresponding extreme of the maximum pattern window, both shoulders satisfy the configured symmetry and retracement bounds, the necks are within the height-relative error allowance, the approach starts beyond the first neck, and a later high or low breaks the outer neck in the reversal direction. A triangle has four alternating contained pivots, nondiverging upper and lower boundaries, configured minimum successive retracements, and either a flat upper boundary and rising lower boundary, a flat lower boundary and falling upper boundary, or a falling upper and rising lower boundary; the close breaks the boundary opposite the last pivot after the configured minimum duration. A three-drive formation has three successively more extreme same-side tips and two successively more extreme corrections, with each drive-to-preceding-correction extension and each correction-to-preceding-drive retracement inside its configured error-adjusted bounds; if a preceding swing exists, its first retracement must also satisfy its configured bounds. Each formation must satisfy its applicable duration and minimum-height bounds.
Calculation: Accepted pattern = A harmonic candidate has D inside its completion zone, or a classical formation has its stated breakout or completion. Its direction is opposite the final pivot for harmonic and three-drive patterns, upward for a double bottom or inverse head and shoulders, downward for a double top or head and shoulders, and in the breakout direction for triangles. At discovery the close must remain on the valid side of the padded invalidation boundary; optional filters require the close inside the validation band, the close on the configured side of the proposed entry, the D-bar volume at least a multiple of its volume moving average, or the harmonic ratio-quality score at least its threshold. The quality score is the average of the defining ratios' scores, each equal to full credit inside its nominal band and declining linearly to zero across the error margin. An optional trend filter requires the discovery close above the previous bar's moving average for a long pattern or below it for a short pattern. Apply the configured session, start time, direction, per-bar and active-pattern limits, same-family blocking, prior-performance blocking, candidate ordering and geometry deduplication; freeze each accepted pattern at discovery.
Calculation: Pattern levels = For harmonic patterns, set the anchor to D and the unit to the configured absolute AD or CD length, selected by family when family-based selection is enabled. For a double formation use the neck as anchor and a unit proportional to its neck-to-second-extreme height; for head and shoulders use the outer neck and the first-neck-to-head height; for a triangle use its broken boundary and initial width; for three-drive use its last tip and the absolute distance from the first correction to the last tip. Entry and targets equal anchor plus the direction sign times their respective configured fractions of the unit; alternatively targets equal entry plus the direction sign times configured multiples of initial risk. The initial stop is the adverse edge of the padded completion or formation-validation zone, or a configured percentage beyond entry; maintain a positive initial risk. After target 1, an optional break-even stop moves toward target 1 by a configured fraction. An optional trailing stop follows the best high for a long or best low for a short since entry, less or plus a configured multiple of the entry-to-target-1 distance; activate it after entry or target 1 as configured, never loosen it, and apply updates from the next bar.
Long: A newly accepted upward pattern is detected OR an unexpired pending upward pattern from an earlier bar remains valid AND open is below target 1 AND the configured trigger is satisfied AND, for a close or candle trigger, close is below target 1; the trigger is close at or above entry, high at or above entry, or low at or below the validation-band upper edge AND a bullish confirmation candle. A bullish confirmation candle is a bullish body with close above the previous high and open at or below the previous close, OR a positive lower wick at least a configured multiple of the body with close above the bar midpoint.
Short: A newly accepted downward pattern is detected OR an unexpired pending downward pattern from an earlier bar remains valid AND open is above target 1 AND the configured trigger is satisfied AND, for a close or candle trigger, close is above target 1; the trigger is close at or below entry, low at or below entry, or high at or above the validation-band lower edge AND a bearish confirmation candle. A bearish confirmation candle is a bearish body with close below the previous low and open at or above the previous close, OR a positive upper wick at least a configured multiple of the body with close below the bar midpoint.
Exit: long, pattern age from discovery reaches the expiry limit OR the configured stop source, close or low, is at or below the current stop OR high reaches the configured finishing target; on a touch-entry bar evaluate the stop first, then targets, and on subsequent bars evaluate expiry first and stop before targets.
Exit: short, pattern age from discovery reaches the expiry limit OR the configured stop source, close or high, is at or above the current stop OR low reaches the configured finishing target; on a touch-entry bar evaluate the stop first, then targets, and on subsequent bars evaluate expiry first and stop before targets.
