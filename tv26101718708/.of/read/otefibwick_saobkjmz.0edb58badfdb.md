Calculation: HTF bar = the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe (use the previous completed HTF bar for the whole duration of the current HTF bar); range = HTF high - HTF low. Two higher timeframes may be used, evaluated independently, one longer than the other.
Calculation: retracement band fractions a < b (a = shallower, b = deeper)
Calculation: bull zone = [HTF high - b*range, HTF high - a*range]; bear zone = [HTF low + a*range, HTF low + b*range]
Long: HTF bar close > HTF bar open AND chart bar close is inside the bull zone (for either higher timeframe)
Short: HTF bar close <= HTF bar open AND chart bar close is inside the bear zone (for either higher timeframe)
