Calculation: Prior-bar ATR = ATR on the last completed chart-timeframe bar before the impulse bar
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback
Calculation: Acceptance boundary = impulse low + configured fraction of impulse range for a bullish impulse; impulse high − configured fraction of impulse range for a bearish impulse
Calculation: Required extension = impulse high + configured ATR multiple × prior-bar ATR for a bullish impulse; impulse low − configured ATR multiple × prior-bar ATR for a bearish impulse
Calculation: Follow-through efficiency = max(0, directional progress from impulse close) ÷ cumulative absolute close-to-close change since the impulse close × 100
Long: bullish impulse bar with high − low ≥ configured range multiple × prior-bar ATR AND abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low) ≥ configured minimum body fraction AND close > open AND (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≥ 1 − configured extreme fraction AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume ≥ configured multiple × volume average) OR active bullish episode AND age in bars ≥ configured hold-close count AND the current consecutive closes above the acceptance boundary ≥ configured hold-close count AND high ≥ required extension AND follow-through efficiency ≥ configured minimum
Short: bearish impulse bar with high − low ≥ configured range multiple × prior-bar ATR AND abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low) ≥ configured minimum body fraction AND close < open AND (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≤ configured extreme fraction AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume ≥ configured multiple × volume average) OR active bearish episode AND age in bars ≥ configured hold-close count AND the current consecutive closes below the acceptance boundary ≥ configured hold-close count AND low ≤ required extension AND follow-through efficiency ≥ configured minimum
Exit: long, active bullish episode AND close < acceptance boundary
Exit: short, active bearish episode AND close > acceptance boundary
