Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on the chart timeframe, taken at the bar before the candle being tested.
Calculation: Episode = when a Long impulse or Short impulse candle closes while no episode is active, start an episode (direction = bullish or bearish) and freeze that candle's high H, low L, close C and A = ATR at the bar before it. Bullish: accept = L + f*(H-L), ext = H + m*A. Bearish: accept = H - f*(H-L), ext = L - m*A, with 0<f<1 and m>=0. From the next bar onward the episode is active until it ends by Exit, acceptance, or after a maximum number of bars since the impulse bar (age) with no other result. Per bar after the impulse: holdStreak = consecutive closes beyond accept in the impulse direction (reset to 0 on any other close); path = cumulative sum of |close - previous close| since the impulse bar; progress = close - C (bullish) or C - close (bearish); eff = max(0, progress)/path. On each bar the checks run in this order: Exit first, then acceptance, then timeout.
Long: no episode active AND (high-low) >= k*A AND |close-open|/(high-low) >= minimum body ratio AND close > open AND (close-low)/(high-low) >= 1 - e (close within the top e fraction of the range) AND (optional filter) volume >= v * moving average of volume
Long: bullish episode active AND age >= N AND holdStreak >= N AND high >= ext AND eff >= minimum efficiency AND the Exit conditions for long are not true on this bar
Short: no episode active AND (high-low) >= k*A AND |close-open|/(high-low) >= minimum body ratio AND close < open AND (close-low)/(high-low) <= e (close within the bottom e fraction of the range) AND (optional filter) volume >= v * moving average of volume
Short: bearish episode active AND age >= N AND holdStreak >= N AND low <= ext AND eff >= minimum efficiency AND the Exit conditions for short are not true on this bar
Exit: long, bullish episode active AND (close < frozen L OR close < accept)
Exit: short, bearish episode active AND (close > frozen H OR close > accept)
