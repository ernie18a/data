Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the configured opening range session; available after that session ends
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low during the configured opening range session; available after that session ends
Calculation: Session volume z-score = (current volume − mean volume of earlier completed bars in the current main session) / population standard deviation of those volumes
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a shorter lookback
Long: Opening range is complete AND (close > opening range high AND previous close <= previous opening range high OR main session is active AND current bar is confirmed AND ((at least the required minimum number of earlier session bars exist AND session volume z-score >= the configured impulse threshold AND session volume z-score >= 3 AND close > open) OR (relative volume >= the configured relative-volume threshold AND relative volume >= 3 AND close > open)))
Short: Opening range is complete AND (close < opening range low AND previous close >= previous opening range low OR main session is active AND current bar is confirmed AND ((at least the required minimum number of earlier session bars exist AND session volume z-score >= the configured impulse threshold AND session volume z-score >= 3 AND close < open) OR (relative volume >= the configured relative-volume threshold AND relative volume >= 3 AND close < open) OR (close = open AND close < previous close AND ((at least the required minimum number of earlier session bars exist AND session volume z-score >= the configured impulse threshold AND session volume z-score >= 3) OR (relative volume >= the configured relative-volume threshold AND relative volume >= 3)))))
