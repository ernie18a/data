Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening-range window, retained until the next main session resets them.
Calculation: Session volume z-score = (current volume − mean volume of preceding bars in the current main session) / population standard deviation of those preceding volumes; use zero until the minimum number of preceding bars is available or if their standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume including the current bar; use zero if the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute close change over the lookback / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero if the denominator is negligible or the initial close is unavailable.
Calculation: Structure bias = (count of consecutive bar pairs with higher high AND higher or equal low − count with lower high AND lower or equal low) / number of pairs in the lookback.
Calculation: Trend strength = rounded, bounded weighted sum of trend efficiency, absolute structure bias, and bounded absolute separation of the shorter and longer close EMAs normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Trend direction score = sign(shorter EMA − longer EMA) + sign of structure bias outside a symmetric threshold + sign(close − longer EMA); each component is zero when its comparison is equal or, for structure bias, within the threshold.
Calculation: Previous-session efficiency = absolute difference between the last close and first open of the last completed main session / the sum of the absolute first-bar close-to-open change and subsequent consecutive close changes; use zero if the denominator is negligible.
Calculation: Previous-session net-range share = absolute difference between the last close and first open of the last completed main session / its high-low range.
Long: Opening range is complete AND close crosses above the opening-range high.
Short: Opening range is complete AND close crosses below the opening-range low.
Long: Current bar is in the main session AND (session volume z-score is at least both the session impulse threshold and the extreme impulse threshold OR relative volume is at least both the relative-volume threshold and the extreme relative-volume threshold) AND (close > open OR close = open AND close >= previous close).
Short: Current bar is in the main session AND (session volume z-score is at least both the session impulse threshold and the extreme impulse threshold OR relative volume is at least both the relative-volume threshold and the extreme relative-volume threshold) AND (close < open OR close = open AND close < previous close).
Long: Trend strength >= trend-strength threshold AND trend efficiency >= efficiency threshold AND trend direction score > 0.
Short: Trend strength >= trend-strength threshold AND trend efficiency >= efficiency threshold AND trend direction score < 0.
Long: Last completed main-session range is positive AND its last close >= its first open AND (previous-session efficiency >= trend threshold AND previous-session net-range share >= lower range-share threshold OR previous-session net-range share >= higher range-share threshold).
Short: Last completed main-session range is positive AND its last close < its first open AND (previous-session efficiency >= trend threshold AND previous-session net-range share >= lower range-share threshold OR previous-session net-range share >= higher range-share threshold).
Long: Session volume z-score × sign(close − previous close) > 0.
Short: Session volume z-score × sign(close − previous close) < 0.
