Calculation: Pivot high = a high greater than or equal to all highs in a specified number of preceding bars and strictly greater than all highs in a specified number of subsequent bars; it is confirmed on the last subsequent bar.
Calculation: Pivot low = a low less than or equal to all lows in a specified number of preceding bars and strictly less than all lows in a specified number of subsequent bars; it is confirmed on the last subsequent bar.
Calculation: Level = a confirmed pivot price multiplied by one plus a configurable upward shift percentage. Optionally retain a pivot only if its volume exceeds a configurable multiple of the simple moving average of volume at its confirmation bar. Retain only a configurable maximum number of the most recently confirmed levels separately for pivot highs and pivot lows, including broken levels. A level becomes broken on the first bar at or after confirmation whose close, or optionally high for a pivot-high level or low for a pivot-low level, crosses strictly beyond it; it remains broken even if it fails the age filter.
Long: A retained pivot-high level becomes broken on the current completed bar AND the number of bars since its confirmation is at least the configurable minimum age.
Short: A retained pivot-low level becomes broken on the current completed bar AND the number of bars since its confirmation is at least the configurable minimum age.
