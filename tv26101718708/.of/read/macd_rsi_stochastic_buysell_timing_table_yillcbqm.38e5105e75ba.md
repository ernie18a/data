Calculation: Stochastic %K = Stochastic %K smoothed by a moving average; %D = moving average of %K
Calculation: MACD line = fast moving average of close minus slow moving average of close; signal line = moving average of MACD line; histogram = MACD line minus signal line. The fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator score = +2 if RSI is at or above its bull-bias threshold, else −2 if at or below its bear-bias threshold; +1 if RSI crosses above its buy threshold, else −1 if it crosses below its sell threshold; +2 if Stochastic %K > %D and %K > 50, else −2 if %K < %D and %K < 50; +1 if %K crosses above %D while %K is below its sell threshold, else −1 if %K crosses below %D while %K is above its buy threshold; +1 if %K crosses above its buy threshold, else −1 if it crosses below its sell threshold; +2 if MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, else −2 if MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; +1 if MACD line crosses above signal line, else −1 if it crosses below; when the EMA trend filter is enabled, +1 if fast EMA > slow EMA and close > long-term trend EMA, else −1 if fast EMA < slow EMA and close < long-term trend EMA; when the volume filter is enabled, +1 if volume > its moving average and close > open, else −1 if volume > its moving average and close < open. Otherwise each component contributes 0.
Long: Oscillator score ≥ 3 and previous-bar score < 3 OR oscillator score ≥ 6 and previous-bar score < 6 OR RSI crosses above its buy threshold OR Stochastic %K crosses above its buy threshold OR Stochastic %K crosses above %D while %K is below its sell threshold OR MACD line crosses above signal line
Short: Oscillator score ≤ −3 and previous-bar score > −3 OR oscillator score ≤ −6 and previous-bar score > −6 OR RSI crosses below its sell threshold OR Stochastic %K crosses below its sell threshold OR Stochastic %K crosses below %D while %K is above its buy threshold OR MACD line crosses below signal line
