Calculation: Stochastic K = SMA of raw stochastic calculated from close, high, and low; Stochastic D = SMA of K, with independently configurable lookbacks.
Calculation: MACD line = shorter-lookback moving average of the source minus longer-lookback moving average of the source; MACD signal = moving average of the MACD line; MACD histogram = MACD line minus MACD signal. The oscillator moving averages and signal moving average can each independently be SMA or EMA.
Calculation: Decision score = sum of the following contributions, evaluated in order within each contribution: +2 if RSI >= RSI bull-bias level, else -2 if RSI <= RSI bear-bias level; +1 if RSI crosses above its buy level, else -1 if it crosses below its sell level; +2 if Stochastic K > D AND K > stochastic midpoint, else -2 if K < D AND K < stochastic midpoint; +1 if K crosses above D AND K < stochastic sell level, else -1 if K crosses below D AND K > stochastic buy level; +1 if K crosses above its buy level, else -1 if K crosses below its sell level; +2 if MACD line > signal AND histogram > 0, else -2 if MACD line < signal AND histogram < 0; +1 if MACD line crosses above signal, else -1 if it crosses below signal; if EMA trend confirmation is enabled, +1 if shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > longest trend EMA, else -1 if shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < longest trend EMA; if volume confirmation is enabled, +1 if volume > SMA of volume AND close > open, else -1 if volume > SMA of volume AND close < open. RSI and moving averages use a configurable source derived from the available price fields. Crosses require a strict current-bar inequality and the opposite or equal relationship on the previous bar.
Long: Decision score >= positive watch threshold OR RSI >= RSI bull-bias level OR Stochastic K > D OR MACD histogram >= 0 OR RSI crosses above its buy level OR Stochastic K crosses above its buy level OR Stochastic K crosses above D OR MACD line crosses above signal.
Short: Decision score <= negative watch threshold OR (RSI < RSI bull-bias level AND RSI <= RSI bear-bias level) OR Stochastic K <= D OR MACD histogram < 0 OR RSI crosses below its sell level OR (Stochastic K crosses below its sell level AND K does not cross above D AND K does not cross above its buy level) OR (Stochastic K crosses below D AND K does not cross above its buy level) OR MACD line crosses below signal.
