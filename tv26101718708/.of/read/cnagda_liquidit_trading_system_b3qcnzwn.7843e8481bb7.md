Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same number of chart bars on each side, known only when the right-side bars have completed. The latest swing high and low are updated before other calculations on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Swing bias = +1 when the latest newly confirmed swing low or high is greater than the preceding swing of the same kind, otherwise -1; retain the previous bias between confirmations. If both kinds confirm on one bar, process the low first and the high second. A buy block exists after a swing low has been compared with its predecessor; a sell block exists after a swing high has been compared with its predecessor.
Calculation: Pool = the average price of two consecutive confirmed swing highs, for a high pool, or lows, for a low pool, when their price difference is at most the current tolerance and their pivot bars are separated by at least the minimum gap. Tolerance is either ATR times a multiplier or close times a percentage. Keep only the most recent configured number of pools of each kind. A high pool becomes inactive when close exceeds its price plus current tolerance; a low pool becomes inactive when close falls below its price minus current tolerance. The nearest pool of each kind is the active pool with the smallest absolute distance from close, regardless of which side of close it is on.
Calculation: Bullish grab = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing low - low > a body-size multiple AND absolute close - open < a fraction of high - low AND volume > a multiple of its moving average. Bearish grab = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND high - latest confirmed swing high > the same body-size multiple AND absolute close - open < the same fraction of high - low AND volume > the same multiple of its moving average.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close AND (a swing low is confirmed on this bar OR sufficient history exists AND previous close < close). Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close AND (a swing high is confirmed on this bar OR sufficient history exists AND previous close > close).
Calculation: Lower-body rejection = high - low > a multiple of absolute close - open AND both (close - low) and (open - low), divided by high - low plus a small offset, exceed a fraction. Upper-body rejection = high - low > the same multiple of absolute close - open AND both (high - close) and (high - open), divided by high - low plus the same offset, exceed the same fraction.
Calculation: Hammer = lower-body rejection AND previous high - previous low > current ATR. Shooting star = upper-body rejection AND (a swing high is confirmed on this bar OR sufficient trend history exists AND previous close > close). Hanging man = lower-body rejection AND the same swing-high or trend-history condition. High-volume doji = absolute close - open <= a fraction of high - low AND volume > a multiple of its moving average. When several of these patterns or the engulfing patterns occur together, only the last in this order is marked: bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing, hammer, shooting star, hanging man, high-volume doji.
Calculation: Bullish iceberg = high > low AND close > open AND absolute close - open divided by high - low < a fraction AND volume > a multiple of its moving average. Bearish iceberg uses the same conditions except close < open. The iceberg volume-average lookback may differ from the grab volume-average lookback.
Calculation: Bullish average alignment = close > higher-timeframe session VWAP AND close > chart-timeframe VWAP AND close > chart-timeframe SMA of close. Bearish average alignment uses close < each average. On historical chart bars, the higher-timeframe VWAP is updated with the just-completed higher-timeframe bar only on its closing chart bar and otherwise retains the last completed value. Chart-timeframe VWAP is either session VWAP or cumulative close-volume divided by cumulative volume.
Calculation: Volume participation = volume > a fraction of cumulative volume since the start of the current day, including the current bar.
Calculation: Buy score = the number of true conditions among bullish average alignment, bullish grab, volume participation, bullish iceberg and bullish engulfing. Sell score is the corresponding count of bearish conditions plus the same volume participation condition. Directional scores use the same respective conditions excluding volume participation. A lite buy state is buy score > sell score OR buy score = sell score AND buy score >= a positive minimum AND bullish directional score > bearish directional score; lite sell is the mirror image. A lite trigger occurs when its state is true and was false on the previous bar.
Calculation: Signal bias = the sign of the sum of the most recent configured number of accepted lite triggers. Accept a lite buy trigger only when the accumulation/distribution line rises, and a lite sell trigger only when it falls. Each accepted trigger contributes +1 for buy or -1 for sell; an empty or tied history has zero bias.
Long: swing bias = +1 AND a buy block exists.
Short: swing bias = -1 AND a sell block exists.
Long: bullish grab.
Short: bearish grab.
Long: bullish engulfing AND bearish engulfing = false AND hammer = false AND shooting star = false AND hanging man = false AND high-volume doji = false.
Short: bearish engulfing AND hammer = false AND shooting star = false AND hanging man = false AND high-volume doji = false.
Long: hammer AND shooting star = false AND hanging man = false AND high-volume doji = false.
Short: shooting star AND hanging man = false AND high-volume doji = false.
Short: hanging man AND high-volume doji = false.
Long: absolute close - nearest active low pool < an ATR multiple AND close > open AND open - low > a multiple of absolute open - close AND volume > the grab volume-average multiple.
Short: absolute close - nearest active high pool < the same ATR multiple AND close < open AND high - close > the same multiple of absolute open - close AND volume > the grab volume-average multiple.
Long: bullish iceberg.
Short: bearish iceberg.
Long: bullish grab AND bullish engulfing AND volume participation AND bullish iceberg AND bullish average alignment.
Short: bearish grab AND bearish engulfing AND volume participation AND bearish iceberg AND bearish average alignment.
Long: signal bias = +1 AND previous signal bias != +1.
Short: signal bias = -1 AND previous signal bias != -1.
Exit: long, previous signal bias = +1 AND signal bias <= 0.
Exit: short, previous signal bias = -1 AND signal bias >= 0.
