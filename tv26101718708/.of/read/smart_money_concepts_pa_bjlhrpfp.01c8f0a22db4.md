Calculation: Confirmed pivots = For each lookback, initialize the leg as bearish. On each chart bar, set it to bearish if the high lookback bars ago exceeds every subsequent high through the current bar; otherwise set it to bullish if the low lookback bars ago is below every subsequent low through the current bar; otherwise retain the leg. A change to bearish confirms a pivot high at the high lookback bars ago; a change to bullish confirms a pivot low at the low lookback bars ago. Maintain separate internal and swing pivots, with the internal lookback shorter than the swing lookback.
Calculation: Structure break = For each internal or swing pivot high and low, reset its crossed flag when a new pivot of that type is confirmed. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest pivot high and its crossed flag is false; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest pivot low and its crossed flag is false. Set the relevant flag on a break. For internal breaks, also require that the corresponding internal and swing pivot levels differ; if the optional confluence filter is enabled, require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for bullish breaks and high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low) for bearish breaks.
Calculation: Fair value gap inputs = Use a configurable higher or lower timeframe. At the start of a new gap-timeframe bar, use its high and low, the preceding completed bar's open and close, and the high and low of the bar two gap-timeframe bars ago. The current gap-timeframe bar is still forming in real time; historical lookahead supplies its final high and low. Let displacement = (previous close - previous open) / (previous open * 100). The optional threshold is twice the cumulative sum of absolute displacement on chart bars that start a new gap-timeframe bar, divided by the zero-based chart bar index; when the filter is disabled, the threshold is zero.
Long: Swing bullish structure break OR internal bullish structure break.
Short: Swing bearish structure break OR internal bearish structure break.
Long: A newly confirmed swing pivot low is greater than or equal to the preceding swing pivot low.
Short: A newly confirmed swing pivot low is less than the preceding swing pivot low.
Long: A newly confirmed swing pivot high is greater than the preceding swing pivot high.
Short: A newly confirmed swing pivot high is less than or equal to the preceding swing pivot high.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its low is above the high two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the preceding gap-timeframe close is above that high AND displacement is greater than the threshold.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the preceding gap-timeframe close is below that low AND negative displacement is greater than the threshold.
