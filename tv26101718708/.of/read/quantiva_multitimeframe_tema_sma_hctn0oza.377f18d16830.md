Calculation: Mean TEMA = arithmetic mean of close-based TEMA values across the five configured timeframes; for a timeframe higher than the chart, use its last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe, use its last available bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Mean SMA = arithmetic mean of close-based SMA values across the same five configured timeframes, using the same bar selection.
Long: Mean TEMA > Mean SMA
Short: Mean TEMA <= Mean SMA
