Calculation: Supertrend = ATR bands around HL2, optionally smoothed; lower band = source − factor × ATR and upper band = source + factor × ATR; ratchet the lower band upward unless the prior close is below its prior value, and ratchet the upper band downward unless the prior close is above its prior value; switch to uptrend when close crosses above the active upper band and to downtrend when close crosses below the active lower band.
Calculation: Adaptive factor = map the current ATR’s percentile rank over a lookback linearly to a minimum-to-maximum multiplier.
Calculation: Fast Supertrend uses a shorter ATR lookback and smaller multiplier than the core; slow Supertrend uses a longer ATR lookback and larger multiplier than the core.
Calculation: For a long, stop = entry price − ATR × stop multiple; targets = entry price + (entry price − stop) × each target risk:reward multiple. For a short, stop = entry price + ATR × stop multiple; targets = entry price − (stop − entry price) × each target risk:reward multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, the core Supertrend changes from downtrend to uptrend AND (triple consensus is disabled OR at least two of the core, fast, and slow Supertrends are uptrend) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX ≥ its threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend is uptrend) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable/zero OR volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no long cooldown is active).
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, the core Supertrend changes from uptrend to downtrend AND (triple consensus is disabled OR at least two of the core, fast, and slow Supertrends are downtrend) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX ≥ its threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend is downtrend) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable/zero OR volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no short cooldown is active).
Exit: Long, confirmed short entry signal OR low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ any active target.
Exit: Short, confirmed long entry signal OR high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ any active target.
