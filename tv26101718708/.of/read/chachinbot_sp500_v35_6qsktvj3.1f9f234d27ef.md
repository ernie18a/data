Calculation: Directional setup state = Maintain separate long and short armed and pullback flags and an arm-bar index. On each bar, process in this order: (1) Arm long and clear its pullback flag when close crosses above the short-term EMA, the short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA, the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR close > the medium-term EMA calculated on the higher timeframe, RSI > its neutral midpoint AND RSI < its upper threshold, volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold, the bar is within the enabled New York session AND outside the enabled New York exclusion period, and the ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= its threshold; simultaneously disarm short and clear its pullback flag. Arm short symmetrically on a close cross below the short-term EMA, short-term EMA < medium-term EMA < long-term EMA, the higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < the higher-timeframe medium-term EMA, RSI < its neutral midpoint AND RSI > its lower threshold, with the same volume, session and ADX filters; simultaneously disarm long and clear its pullback flag. Record the current bar index when arming. (2) Set the long pullback flag when long is armed AND low <= short-term EMA + ATR multiplied by the pullback-depth parameter; set the short pullback flag when short is armed AND high >= short-term EMA - ATR multiplied by that parameter. (3) Evaluate the final rebound decisions. (4) Disarm and clear the corresponding pullback flag after its rebound decision, or when bars since arming > the pullback window, or when its EMA trend ordering no longer holds. Also disarm long when its pullback flag is false AND close > short-term EMA + ATR multiplied by the maximum-distance parameter; disarm short when its pullback flag is false AND close < short-term EMA - ATR multiplied by that parameter. Re-arming resets the arm-bar index and pullback flag. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the medium-term EMA length; with lookahead off, historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe value as it becomes available, while a realtime bar uses the still-forming higher-timeframe value.
