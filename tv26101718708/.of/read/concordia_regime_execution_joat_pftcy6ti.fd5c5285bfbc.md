Calculation: Anchored VWAPs = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of the shorter and longer calendar anchor periods respectively; use the current, still-forming anchor period and fall back to typical price when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Trend vote = sum of +1 for each true comparison and -1 otherwise: short EMA > intermediate EMA, intermediate EMA > long EMA, short EMA slope > 0, intermediate EMA slope > 0, and close > shorter-period anchored VWAP; optionally add +1 if close > longer-period anchored VWAP and -1 otherwise. Both slopes use the same lookback.
Calculation: Trend bias = 1 when trend vote >= positive vote threshold, -1 when trend vote <= negative vote threshold, otherwise 0; the thresholds have equal magnitude.
Calculation: Swing high and swing low = most recent confirmed pivot high and low, each confirmed by the same number of bars on either side. A bullish break occurs when close > current swing high AND previous close <= current swing high; a bearish break occurs when close < current swing low AND previous close >= current swing low. Structure bias starts at 0 and retains 1 after a bullish break or -1 after a bearish break; a bearish break takes precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Downward sweep = low < current swing low AND close > current swing low. Upward sweep = high > current swing high AND close < current swing high.
Calculation: Ribbon spread = average of the three shorter-lookback smoothed RSIs minus average of the three longer-lookback smoothed RSIs. The six RSI lookbacks increase by equal steps. Each smoothed RSI is EMA(RSI + RSI - RSI lagged by floor((smoothing length - 1) / 2), smoothing length).
Calculation: Momentum bias = 1 when ribbon spread > positive spread threshold, -1 when ribbon spread < negative spread threshold, otherwise 0; the thresholds have equal magnitude.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 times log10(sum of true range over its lookback divided by highest high minus lowest low over that lookback), divided by log10 of the lookback; use 50 when the high-low range is zero.
Calculation: Compression ratio = (highest high minus lowest low over the compression lookback) / (ATR times that lookback); use 1 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Expansion = ADX >= ADX floor AND choppiness < choppiness ceiling AND compression ratio >= compression threshold.
Calculation: Z-score = (value - moving average of value) / population standard deviation of value over the same normalization lookback; use 0 when standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Pressure = 0.35 times Z-score of volume times (close - open) / (high - low), plus 0.25 times Z-score of close minus shorter-period anchored VWAP, plus 0.20 times Z-score of close minus the mean of the three trend EMAs, minus 0.20 times Z-score of ATR. Use zero for the volume term when high equals low.
Calculation: Crowding = 0.50 times Z-score of RSI minus 50 plus 0.50 times Z-score of close minus longer-period anchored VWAP; the RSI has its own lookback.
Calculation: Risk = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 50, 14 times absolute crowding, 10 times the greater of zero and the Z-score of ATR, and 8 if either sweep occurs.
Calculation: Long score = sum of 18 if expansion, 22 if trend bias = 1, 14 if structure bias = 1, 12 if bullish break, 8 if downward sweep, 16 if momentum bias = 1, 10 if pressure > positive pressure threshold, 8 if close > shorter-period anchored VWAP, and, if longer-period VWAP alignment is enabled, 6 if close > longer-period anchored VWAP; subtract 30 if risk >= risk ceiling.
Calculation: Short score = sum of 18 if expansion, 22 if trend bias = -1, 14 if structure bias = -1, 12 if bearish break, 8 if upward sweep, 16 if momentum bias = -1, 10 if pressure < negative pressure threshold, 8 if close < shorter-period anchored VWAP, and, if longer-period VWAP alignment is enabled, 6 if close < longer-period anchored VWAP; subtract 30 if risk >= risk ceiling. The positive and negative pressure thresholds have equal magnitude.
Long: Trend bias = 1.
Short: Trend bias = -1.
Long: Structure bias = 1.
Short: Structure bias = -1.
Long: Ribbon spread > positive spread threshold.
Short: Ribbon spread < negative spread threshold OR ribbon spread = positive spread threshold OR ribbon spread = negative spread threshold.
Long: Pressure > positive pressure threshold.
Short: Pressure < negative pressure threshold.
Long: Close >= shorter-period anchored VWAP AND longer-period VWAP alignment is enabled.
Short: Close < shorter-period anchored VWAP AND longer-period VWAP alignment is enabled.
Long: Bars since the last entry >= cooldown OR no prior entry, AND long score >= entry threshold AND long score >= short score minus score tolerance AND (expansion OR ADX >= ADX floor times soft-expansion factor) AND trend bias >= 0 AND momentum bias >= 0 AND pressure > negative soft-pressure threshold AND (bullish break OR downward sweep OR shorter-RSI average crosses above longer-RSI average OR close crosses above short EMA) AND risk < soft-risk ceiling.
Short: Bars since the last entry >= cooldown OR no prior entry, AND short score >= entry threshold AND short score >= long score minus score tolerance AND (expansion OR ADX >= ADX floor times soft-expansion factor) AND trend bias <= 0 AND momentum bias <= 0 AND pressure < positive soft-pressure threshold AND (bearish break OR upward sweep OR shorter-RSI average crosses below longer-RSI average OR close crosses below short EMA) AND risk < soft-risk ceiling. The soft-pressure thresholds have equal magnitude.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price minus ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= entry price plus ATR times target multiplier OR (trailing is active AND low <= highest high over trailing lookback minus ATR times trailing multiplier). Trailing is active when close >= entry price plus ATR times stop multiplier times trailing-trigger multiplier; evaluate the stop and target submitted after the preceding bar using that bar's ATR and trailing state.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price plus ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= entry price minus ATR times target multiplier OR (trailing is active AND high >= lowest low over trailing lookback plus ATR times trailing multiplier). Trailing is active when close <= entry price minus ATR times stop multiplier times trailing-trigger multiplier; evaluate the stop and target submitted after the preceding bar using that bar's ATR and trailing state.
Exit: Long, bias-failure exit enabled AND (trend bias != 1 OR momentum bias = -1 OR pressure < negative pressure threshold OR risk >= risk ceiling).
Exit: Short, bias-failure exit enabled AND (trend bias != -1 OR momentum bias = 1 OR pressure > positive pressure threshold OR risk >= risk ceiling).
