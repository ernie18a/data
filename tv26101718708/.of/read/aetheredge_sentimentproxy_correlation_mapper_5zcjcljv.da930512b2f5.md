Calculation: Fear-greed score = EMA of the output of a seeded, one-hidden-layer tanh neural network with five inputs: volume anomaly (clamp(((volume − rolling mean(volume)) / rolling standard deviation(volume)) × candle direction, −3, 3)); volatility (clamp the z-score of ATR / its longer-term rolling mean − 1, normalized by its longer-term rolling mean and standard deviation, to −3…3); velocity (clamp((close − close from a shorter lookback) / (lookback × ATR), −3, 3)); wick asymmetry (clamp(2 × (lower wick − upper wick) / candle range, using ATR if range is zero, −3, 3)); and directional run length (clamp(3 × rolling sum of +1 for an up-close, −1 for a down-close, or 0 for unchanged, divided by its lookback, −3, 3)). Use zero biases initially. Train online on confirmed bars within the configured recent training horizon: store the previous bar’s five inputs with the current bar’s normalized forward close change (clamped to −1…1 and normalized by ATR); retain at most 200 samples; perform four seeded random replay-sample gradient-descent updates per bar to minimize squared error through the tanh output and hidden layer. The score’s EMA uses the configured smoothing parameter.
