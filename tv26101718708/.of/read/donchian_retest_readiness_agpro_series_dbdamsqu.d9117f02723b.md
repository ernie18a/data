Calculation: Prior-bar Donchian high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Retest readiness score = weighted 0–100 score combining wick response, close distance beyond the broken edge, proximity to the edge, breakout impulse, relative volume, and channel width; relative volume may be omitted
Long: Channel width ≥ minimum width × ATR AND (close > prior-bar Donchian high + ATR break buffer AND previous close ≤ prior-bar Donchian high OR an active bullish break is within its retest window AND bar range intersects the pocket around the broken edge AND close ≥ broken edge + ATR hold buffer AND readiness score ≥ minimum score AND close is not beyond the failure buffer AND readiness has not already been marked for this break)
Short: Channel width ≥ minimum width × ATR AND (close < prior-bar Donchian low − ATR break buffer AND previous close ≥ prior-bar Donchian low OR an active bearish break is within its retest window AND bar range intersects the pocket around the broken edge AND close ≤ broken edge − ATR hold buffer AND readiness score ≥ minimum score AND close is not beyond the failure buffer AND readiness has not already been marked for this break)
Exit: Long, an active bullish break is within its retest window AND close < broken edge − ATR failure buffer
Exit: Short, an active bearish break is within its retest window AND close > broken edge + ATR failure buffer
