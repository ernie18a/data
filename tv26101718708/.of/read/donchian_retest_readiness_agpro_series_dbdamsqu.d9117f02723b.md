Calculation: Channel high and low = highest high and lowest low over the Donchian lookback ending on the previous bar; channel width = channel high minus channel low.
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR, minimum price increment), using true range in place of ATR while ATR is unavailable. Replace the minimum price increment with a positive numerical tolerance when it is unavailable from the data.
Calculation: Break context = on each closed-bar break, store its direction d (+1 for an upward break, -1 for a downward break), channel edge E, safe ATR A, and bar index; replace any prior context and reset its ready latch. A context remains active through the configured retest window, including the break bar, unless replaced by a later break or invalidated by d × (close − E) < −A × failure-buffer parameter. Evaluate readiness and invalidation before deactivating the context on the invalidation bar; deactivate it when the window expires.
Calculation: Retest score = 22 × clamp(wick fraction / 0.55, 0, 1) + 24 × clamp(d × (close − E) / safe ATR / 0.75, 0, 1) + 18 × clamp(1 − abs(close − E) / max(A × 1.6, minimum price increment), 0, 1) + 14 × clamp(impulse / 0.80, 0, 1) + volume contribution + 12 × clamp(channel width / max(safe ATR × 4, minimum price increment), 0, 1). For d = +1, wick fraction = (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) and impulse = (close − channel high) / safe ATR; for d = −1, wick fraction = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) and impulse = (channel low − close) / safe ATR. Volume contribution = 10 × clamp(relative volume / 1.6, 0, 1) when volume scoring is enabled, otherwise 7; relative volume = current volume / its simple moving average, or 1 when that average is not positive. Clamp the total to [0, 100].
Calculation: Required retest score = clamp(minimum ready score − confirmation adjustment, 1, 100), where the adjustment is 5 for fast, 0 for balanced, and −8 for strict confirmation.
Long: Channel width >= safe ATR × minimum-width parameter AND close > channel high + safe ATR × break-buffer parameter AND previous close <= current channel high.
Short: Channel width >= safe ATR × minimum-width parameter AND close < channel low − safe ATR × break-buffer parameter AND previous close >= current channel low.
Long: Active upward break context AND ready latch = false AND high >= E − A × pocket-width parameter AND low <= E + A × pocket-width parameter AND close >= E + A × hold-buffer parameter AND close >= E − A × failure-buffer parameter AND retest score >= required retest score; set the ready latch on this bar.
Short: Active downward break context AND ready latch = false AND high >= E − A × pocket-width parameter AND low <= E + A × pocket-width parameter AND close <= E − A × hold-buffer parameter AND close <= E + A × failure-buffer parameter AND retest score >= required retest score; set the ready latch on this bar.
Exit: long, active upward break context AND close < E − A × failure-buffer parameter.
Exit: short, active downward break context AND close > E + A × failure-buffer parameter.
