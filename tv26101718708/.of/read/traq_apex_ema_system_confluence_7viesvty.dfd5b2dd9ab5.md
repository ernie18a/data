Calculation: Ribbon = eight EMAs of the selected price source, ordered from shortest to longest lookback; the pullback EMA is selectable among the first, third, and fourth EMAs. The bias EMA has a separate lookback.
Calculation: Bullish pullback age = bars since the latest bar with open > shortest ribbon EMA AND low <= pullback EMA, reset to unavailable whenever close < fifth ribbon EMA OR the first five ribbon EMAs are not strictly descending in lookback order. Bearish pullback age is symmetric: open < shortest EMA AND high >= pullback EMA, reset when close > fifth EMA OR the first five EMAs are not strictly ascending in lookback order. A qualifying pullback bar has age zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = on each of two higher timeframes, using either the last completed bar or the bar still forming as configured, bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its value at the slope lookback AND their absolute spread >= an ATR multiple; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < its value at the slope lookback AND their absolute spread >= that multiple; otherwise neutral. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Base long = bullish pullback age <= pullback lookback AND close > shortest ribbon EMA AND close > open AND absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND, if enabled, close > bias EMA AND, if enabled, bias EMA minus its value at the slope lookback >= negative close times the flat threshold AND, if enabled, the first five ribbon EMAs are strictly descending in lookback order AND, if enabled, absolute spread between shortest and longest ribbon EMAs >= an ATR multiple AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, either both higher-timeframe states are bullish in strict mode OR neither is bearish in no-opposition mode. Base short reverses all directional comparisons, using bearish pullback age and bias EMA change <= close times the flat threshold. The pullback reset applies even when the optional ribbon alignment filter is disabled.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of preceding and following bars in its pivot lookback. A bullish break occurs when close > the latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND close minus swing high >= a minimum distance; a bearish break is symmetric. A break remains recent through the expiry lookback unless an opposite break occurs; a new break resets its age to zero.
Calculation: Internal low sweep = low <= latest confirmed swing low; internal high sweep = high >= latest confirmed swing high. Each remains active through the expiry lookback after its latest occurrence, but a newly confirmed pivot of the same side first clears its active state; the new pivot can be swept on that same bar.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars earlier AND their difference >= minimum gap size, with boundaries at those two prices. Bearish gap = current high < low two bars earlier AND their difference >= minimum gap size, with boundaries at those two prices. On completed chart bars retain up to a configured maximum number of the newest gaps of each side. A later bar retests a bullish gap when its range overlaps the gap AND close > gap midpoint; a bearish retest requires range overlap AND close < midpoint. Record retests before removing mitigated gaps. Remove a gap on the configured mitigation condition: range overlap for touch, low <= midpoint for bullish or high >= midpoint for bearish midline, or low <= lower boundary for bullish or high >= upper boundary for bearish full fill. Midline and full-fill removal also apply on the creation bar.
Calculation: External low sweep = current low <= the low of the last completed higher-timeframe calendar period AND previous chart-bar low > that same level; external high sweep reverses these comparisons. Evaluate separately for a shorter and a longer calendar period. A sweep remains fresh through the expiry lookback, resetting at the start of its corresponding calendar period; process a new sweep after the reset.
Calculation: Long confluence score = one point each for recent bullish break, active internal low sweep, and bullish gap retest, plus one point each when enabled for an allowed session, close > session VWAP AND VWAP >= its value at the slope lookback, and a fresh external low sweep. Short score uses bearish break, internal high sweep, bearish gap retest, close < VWAP AND VWAP <= its lookback value, and external high sweep. Allowed sessions are configurable time intervals evaluated in the configured session timezone. The effective minimum score is the lesser of the configured minimum and the number of enabled score components.
Calculation: Long confluence passes when confluence is disabled OR the score >= effective minimum AND each enabled requirement among recent bullish break, active internal low sweep, bullish gap retest, and fresh external low sweep is satisfied. Short confluence is symmetric.

Short: Base short = true AND short confluence passes = true
