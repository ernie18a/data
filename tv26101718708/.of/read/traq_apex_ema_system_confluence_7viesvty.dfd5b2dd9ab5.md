Calculation: Partial bullish EMA stack = the five shortest-lookback EMAs, ordered from shortest to longest, each greater than the next; partial bearish EMA stack = each less than the next.
Calculation: Recent bullish pullback = within the pullback lookback, while the partial bullish stack holds and close is at or above its fifth EMA, a bar has open above its shortest EMA and low at or below the selected pullback EMA; reset if the stack fails or close falls below the fifth EMA. Recent bearish pullback = inverse: partial bearish stack holds and close is at or below its fifth EMA, a bar has open below its shortest EMA and high at or above the selected pullback EMA; reset if the stack fails or close rises above the fifth EMA.
Calculation: Bias EMA slope = current bias EMA minus its value at the slope lookback; flat band = close times the flat-threshold percentage. Rising if slope exceeds the band; falling if slope is below the negative band.
Calculation: Trending regime = absolute difference between the shortest- and longest-lookback ribbon EMAs is at least ATR times the minimum ribbon-spread factor.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = bullish when its fast EMA is above its slow EMA, its fast EMA is above its value at the slope lookback, and their spread is at least its ATR times the minimum spread factor; bearish when the inverse holds; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bar of each of the two higher timeframes.
Long: Recent bullish pullback AND close > shortest-lookback EMA AND close > open AND absolute(close − open) >= ATR × minimum rejection-body factor AND (bias agreement disabled OR close > bias EMA) AND (bias-slope requirement disabled OR bias EMA slope >= negative flat band) AND (partial-stack requirement disabled OR partial bullish EMA stack) AND (ranging-regime suppression disabled OR trending regime) AND (higher-timeframe requirement disabled OR higher-timeframe mode is no-opposition with neither state bearish, or strict alignment with both states bullish) AND confirmed bar.
Short: Recent bearish pullback AND close < shortest-lookback EMA AND close < open AND absolute(close − open) >= ATR × minimum rejection-body factor AND (bias agreement disabled OR close < bias EMA) AND (bias-slope requirement disabled OR bias EMA slope <= flat band) AND (partial-stack requirement disabled OR partial bearish EMA stack) AND (ranging-regime suppression disabled OR trending regime) AND (higher-timeframe requirement disabled OR higher-timeframe mode is no-opposition with neither state bullish, or strict alignment with both states bearish) AND confirmed bar.
