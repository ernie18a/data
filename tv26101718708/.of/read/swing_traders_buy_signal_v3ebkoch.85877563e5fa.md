Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close.
Calculation: StochRSI K, D = raw = 100*(RSI - lowest RSI over lookback)/(highest RSI over lookback - lowest RSI over lookback), 0 if highest equals lowest; K = SMA(raw, short smoothing); D = SMA(K, short smoothing).
Calculation: RSmom = (RS / RS lookback bars ago - 1)*100, where RS = chart close / benchmark market-index close on the same bar (benchmark at the same timeframe as the chart, no lookahead).
Calculation: Score = count of true among these 11 conditions: close > SMA_short; close > SMA_mid; close > SMA_long; SMA_short > SMA_mid; SMA_mid > SMA_long; RSI > mid-level; RSI > higher level; RSmom > 0; RSmom > positive higher level; K > D; volume > multiplier(>1) * SMA(volume). SMA lookbacks: short < mid < long.
Long: (Buy is true on this bar AND was false on the previous bar) OR (StrongBuy is true on this bar AND was false on the previous bar), where Buy = Score >= buy-threshold AND close > SMA_short AND close > SMA_mid AND RSmom > 0, and StrongBuy = Score >= strong-threshold AND close > SMA_short AND SMA_short > SMA_mid AND SMA_mid > SMA_long AND RSmom > 0 AND RSI > mid-level (strong-threshold > buy-threshold).
