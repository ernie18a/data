Calculation: Recent pivot high/low = most recent confirmed local high/low pivot, respectively, among the latest scan-depth stored pivots, at least the minimum age; a pivot is confirmed after the same lookback number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over its lookback.
Long: low < recent pivot low AND close > recent pivot low AND (low - min(open, close)) >= ATR(14) × wick multiplier AND (low - min(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick ratio AND bars since last low-piercing sweep > cooldown AND (overlap is allowed OR no unresolved bullish sweep is pending)
Long: a bullish sweep is pending AND elapsed bars since the sweep are within the cluster window AND count of subsequent bars with close >= open >= minimum cluster bars AND sum of bodies of those qualifying bars / sum of their ranges >= minimum body ratio AND cumulative volume since the sweep / (average volume captured on the sweep bar × elapsed bars) >= minimum volume multiplier
Short: high > recent pivot high AND close < recent pivot high AND (high - max(open, close)) >= ATR(14) × wick multiplier AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick ratio AND bars since last high-piercing sweep > cooldown AND (overlap is allowed OR no unresolved bearish sweep is pending)
Short: a bearish sweep is pending AND elapsed bars since the sweep are within the cluster window AND count of subsequent bars with close < open >= minimum cluster bars AND sum of bodies of those qualifying bars / sum of their ranges >= minimum body ratio AND cumulative volume since the sweep / (average volume captured on the sweep bar × elapsed bars) >= minimum volume multiplier
