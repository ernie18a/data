Calculation: Adaptive ATR = ATR smoothed recursively as previous adaptive ATR + smoothing coefficient × (ATR − previous adaptive ATR), initialized to ATR. The coefficient is the square of the linear interpolation between a slow and a fast EMA smoothing constant, weighted by the efficiency ratio: absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: Rescaled range = for a lookback, the range of cumulative deviations of log(close / previous close) from its lookback mean, accumulated from newest to oldest bar, divided by the lookback standard deviation. It is unavailable when the standard deviation is zero or insufficient history exists; use the current close as the previous close when the latter is unavailable.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = log(longer-lookback rescaled range / shorter-lookback rescaled range) / log(longer lookback / shorter lookback), clamped between its lower and upper bounds; use the neutral value when either rescaled range is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Cascade stages = three recursive Supertrends sharing the adaptive ATR. The first uses the midpoint of the lookback highest high and lowest low as its source and its own factor. The second uses the first stage's line as its source and its own factor multiplied by a clamped linear function of the volume Z-score; calculate the Z-score using the volume lookback mean and standard deviation, floored by a small positive constant. The third uses the second stage's line as its source and its own factor multiplied by a clamped linear function of the Hurst estimate relative to its neutral value. Each stage has independent factors and band history.
Calculation: Recursive Supertrend = raw upper and lower bands are source plus and minus factor × adaptive ATR. Set the lower band to the raw lower band when it exceeds the previous lower band or the previous source is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Set the upper band to the raw upper band when it is below the previous upper band or the previous source is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Replace unavailable previous bands and previous line with zero. If the previous adaptive ATR is unavailable, direction is bearish; otherwise, if the previous line equals the previous upper band, direction is bullish when the current source exceeds the current upper band and bearish otherwise; if it does not equal the previous upper band, direction is bearish when the current source is below the current lower band and bullish otherwise. The line is the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish.
Long: Any cascade stage direction = bullish.
Short: Any cascade stage direction = bearish.
Long: Third-stage direction = bullish AND previous third-stage direction = bearish.
Short: Third-stage direction = bearish AND previous third-stage direction = bullish.
Long: All cascade stage directions = bullish AND at least one previous stage direction = bearish.
Short: All cascade stage directions = bearish AND at least one previous stage direction = bullish.
Long: Third-stage direction = bullish AND open < second-stage line AND close > second-stage line AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar.
Short: Third-stage direction = bearish AND open > second-stage line AND close < second-stage line AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar.
Long: Third-stage direction changes from bearish to bullish AND second-stage direction is bullish on the current bar OR either of the two preceding bars.
Short: Third-stage direction changes from bullish to bearish AND second-stage direction is bearish on the current bar OR either of the two preceding bars.
