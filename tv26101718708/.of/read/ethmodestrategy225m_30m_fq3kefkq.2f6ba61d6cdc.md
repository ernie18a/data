Calculation: CRSI = average of RSI(close, short lookback), RSI(consecutive close streak, streak lookback; streak increments on consecutive rises, decrements on consecutive falls, and resets to zero when close is unchanged), and percentile rank of close - prior close over a longer lookback; use the last completed 15-minute and 1-hour bars, both higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Keltner bands = EMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × ATR; use last completed 15-minute bars, higher than the chart timeframe
Long: On each completed 15-minute bar, (1-hour CRSI is between 40 and 60 AND 15-minute low is below the lower Keltner band AND 15-minute CRSI is at or below its oversold threshold AND 15-minute close is above its open AND candle body is at least 30% of its high-low range) OR (1-hour CRSI is above 60 AND 15-minute CRSI is at or below its oversold threshold AND 15-minute close is at or below 1.02 × lower Keltner band) OR (1-hour CRSI is above 60 AND 15-minute high is above the prior completed 15-minute upper Keltner band AND 15-minute close is below the current upper Keltner band AND 15-minute CRSI is at or below its oversold threshold)
Short: On each completed 15-minute bar, (1-hour CRSI is between 40 and 60 AND 15-minute high is above the upper Keltner band AND 15-minute CRSI is at or above its overbought threshold AND 15-minute close is below its open AND candle body is at least 30% of its high-low range) OR (1-hour CRSI is below 40 AND 15-minute CRSI is at or above its overbought threshold AND 15-minute close is at or above 0.98 × upper Keltner band) OR (1-hour CRSI is below 40 AND 15-minute low is below the prior completed 15-minute lower Keltner band AND 15-minute close is above the current lower Keltner band AND 15-minute CRSI is at or above its overbought threshold)
Exit: Long, close is at or below entry-signal close minus stop-multiple × ATR-at-entry OR close is at or above entry-signal close plus reward-risk ratio × stop-multiple × ATR-at-entry OR trailing stop is hit after activation OR a short signal occurs after the minimum hold
Exit: Short, close is at or above entry-signal close plus stop-multiple × ATR-at-entry OR close is at or below entry-signal close minus reward-risk ratio × stop-multiple × ATR-at-entry OR trailing stop is hit after activation OR a long signal occurs after the minimum hold
