Calculation: Scan bar = a chart-timeframe bar or the last completed bar of an enabled higher timeframe; evaluate each scan timeframe once when its returned bar time changes.
Calculation: Pattern bars = on a scan timeframe, the base precedes the impulse, followed by a parameterized number of continuation bars ending at the scan bar; ATR is evaluated at the scan bar.
Calculation: Zone = for demand, base low to base open; for supply, base open to base high.
Calculation: Active zones = previously accepted zones of the same direction, excluding zones broken when the close of the last completed bar of a separate break timeframe is below a demand zone or above a supply zone. The break timeframe can be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe. When a new zone is accepted, remove overlapping active zones of the same direction with lower configured timeframe priority.
Calculation: Zone overlap = intersection height divided by the smaller zone height, or zero if the smaller height is zero.
Calculation: Zone acceptance = zone top > zone bottom AND the base time is within the optional maximum age AND no active zone of the same direction and scan timeframe has both boundaries within a price-tolerance parameter AND no active zone of the same direction with equal or higher configured timeframe priority has overlap greater than the enabled overlap threshold.
