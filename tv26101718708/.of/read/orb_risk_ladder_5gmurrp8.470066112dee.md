Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of bars in the opening-range session for the current day, reset at the start of each day
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of bars in the opening-range session for the current day, reset at the start of each day
Long: current bar is in the breakout window AND opening-range high is available AND close > opening-range high AND no earlier long or short signal has occurred today
Short: current bar is in the breakout window AND opening-range low is available AND close < opening-range low AND no earlier long or short signal has occurred today
