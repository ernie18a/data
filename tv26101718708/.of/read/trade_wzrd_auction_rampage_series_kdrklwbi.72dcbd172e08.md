Calculation: Auction samples = chart bars in the higher-timeframe bar still forming, including the current chart bar; missing volume is zero. Whenever the sample count exceeds a maximum, merge adjacent pairs: use their maximum high, minimum low, summed volume, and volume-weighted close, or the second close if their combined volume is zero; retain an unpaired final sample. Repeat whenever the maximum is exceeded.
Calculation: Auction profile = divide the range from the samples' minimum low to maximum high into equal price rows. For each sample with positive volume and high > low, assign buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), and sell volume = volume − buy volume. Distribute each across overlapping rows in proportion to the overlap's share of the sample's range. Auction delta = (total buy volume − total sell volume) / (total buy volume + total sell volume), or zero if the denominator is zero. Auction control = sum of volume × (high + low + close) / 3 over eligible samples divided by total buy plus sell volume; it is undefined if that denominator is zero. The previous auction delta is the value calculated on the previous chart bar, even across a higher-timeframe boundary.
Calculation: Kinetic fuel = volume × absolute close-to-previous-close change, normalized to its rolling minimum and maximum over a lookback; use zero when unavailable or the range is zero.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = ATR is available AND the cooldown since either previous signal has elapsed AND kinetic fuel meets its optional minimum AND optional auction alignment holds, requiring positive delta for a long and negative delta for a short AND the corresponding side's delta-tier win rate meets its optional minimum unless that tier has fewer than the required number of completed trades. Assign tiers by absolute auction delta using increasing thresholds. Count only stop or target outcomes of the latest signal's tracked trade, by its side and entry delta tier; a target is a win. A new signal replaces the tracked trade before evaluating that bar's stop or target, without recording the replaced trade.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = fix both at the signal bar's close. For a long, stop = auction control − ATR × buffer; if control is unavailable use low − ATR × buffer, and if the result is at or above entry use entry − ATR. For a short, stop = auction control + ATR × buffer; if control is unavailable use high + ATR × buffer, and if the result is at or below entry use entry + ATR. Target is entry plus the stop distance × reward-to-risk multiple for a long, or entry minus that distance × reward-to-risk multiple for a short.
Long: Delta-drive model enabled AND auction sample count >= minimum AND auction control is available AND auction delta > positive trigger AND previous chart bar's auction delta <= positive trigger AND close > auction control AND signal eligibility holds.
Short: Delta-drive model enabled AND auction sample count >= minimum AND auction control is available AND auction delta < negative trigger AND previous chart bar's auction delta >= negative trigger AND close < auction control AND signal eligibility holds AND no long signal occurs on the same bar.
Long: Volume / its moving average >= high-volume threshold AND (high − low) / its moving average <= narrow-range threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= midpoint threshold; use a neutral midpoint when high = low and use a relative value of one when the respective average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Short: Volume / its moving average >= high-volume threshold AND (high − low) / its moving average <= narrow-range threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) < midpoint threshold; use a neutral midpoint when high = low and use a relative value of one when the respective average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Exit: long, opposite-signal closing enabled AND short delta-drive signal occurs.
Exit: short, opposite-signal closing enabled AND long delta-drive signal occurs.
Exit: long, bar is after the latest tracked signal bar AND no new delta-drive signal occurs on this bar AND (low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target); if both are hit, count the stop first.
Exit: short, bar is after the latest tracked signal bar AND no new delta-drive signal occurs on this bar AND (high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target); if both are hit, count the stop first.
