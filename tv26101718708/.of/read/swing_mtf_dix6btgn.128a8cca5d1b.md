Calculation: Higher-timeframe open RSI and close RSI = RSI of open and close, respectively, on the higher timeframe, sampled when that bar completes.
Calculation: Swing low = the close RSI value k chart bars ago is less than every older value at offsets k+1 through 2k−1 and less than or equal to every newer value at offsets 0 through k−1, where k is the swing lookback.
Calculation: Swing high = the close RSI value k chart bars ago is greater than every older value at offsets k+1 through 2k−1 and greater than or equal to every newer value at offsets 0 through k−1, where k is the swing lookback.
Long: Swing low AND higher-timeframe open RSI > higher-timeframe close RSI
Short: Swing high AND higher-timeframe open RSI < higher-timeframe close RSI
