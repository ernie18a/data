Calculation: Swing-range event = Using a short lookback and a twice-as-long lookback, require the current short-window highest high and lowest low to equal the corresponding long-window extremes, and either long-window extreme to occur at the oldest bar of the short window. Require at least the short lookback number of bars since the preceding valid event. On the first consecutive event, save the long-window high and low until replaced. A bullish range break occurs when close crosses above the saved high and changes the retained range direction to bullish; a bearish range break occurs when close crosses below the saved low and changes that direction to bearish. Initialize the direction as undefined, treating its previous value as bearish when detecting a change.
Calculation: Pivot-trend event = Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. At each confirmed pivot, save the current slope, chosen as ATR divided by the pivot lookback and multiplied by a slope factor, close standard deviation divided by the lookback and multiplied by the factor, or half the absolute linear-regression slope of close multiplied by the factor. Initialize the upper and lower trend lines and their saved slopes to zero. Reset the corresponding line to each confirmed pivot; otherwise subtract its saved slope from the upper line or add its saved slope to the lower line each bar. Reset each corresponding breakout state to zero at its pivot. Otherwise set the upper state to one when close exceeds the upper line minus its saved slope times the pivot lookback, and set the lower state to one when close is below the lower line plus its saved slope times the pivot lookback; retain each state otherwise. A bullish or bearish pivot-trend event occurs when its corresponding state changes from zero to one.
Calculation: Paired event = For each direction independently, match a range break and a pivot-trend event of that direction within a pairing window, in either order. An unmatched event replaces the pending event of the same direction and restarts its timer; a match clears the pending event. Expire pending events when their timer exceeds the window. Process the range break before the pivot-trend event, allowing the latter to overwrite the result on the same bar.
Calculation: Gaussian envelope = A trailing Gaussian-weighted mean of close, with weights exp(-offset²/(2 × bandwidth²)) over a fixed trailing window and missing closes treated as zero, plus and minus a multiplier times the simple moving average of the absolute close-to-mean difference over a slightly shorter window. Retain the last envelope direction as bullish when close crosses below the lower band and bearish when it crosses above the upper band, initially neutral.
Calculation: ATR trailing-stop direction = Initialize the stop and direction to zero. Let distance be ATR times a multiplier. If both current and previous close exceed the previous stop, set the stop to the greater of the previous stop and close minus distance; if both are below the previous stop, set it to the lesser of the previous stop and close plus distance; otherwise set it to close minus distance when close exceeds the previous stop and close plus distance otherwise. Set direction bullish when close exceeds the previous stop and previous close was at or below it; set direction bearish when close is below the previous stop and previous close was at or above it; otherwise retain the direction.
Calculation: Envelope-stop event = A bullish event occurs when close crosses below the lower Gaussian band while the ATR trailing-stop direction is bullish, or when that direction changes to bullish while the retained envelope direction is bullish. A bearish event uses a cross above the upper band and bearish directions. If liquidity confirmation is enabled, additionally require within its lookback a low below the preceding liquidity-window lowest low with close above that level for bullish events, or a high above the preceding liquidity-window highest high with close below that level for bearish events. If the ADX filter is enabled, additionally require ADX at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Phase event = Initialize phase to zero. On every bar where volume exceeds its threshold and close differs from its EMA, set phase to one before processing envelope-stop events. In phase one, a bearish envelope-stop event produces a bullish phase event, otherwise a bullish envelope-stop event produces a bearish phase event; set phase to two. In phase two, a bullish envelope-stop event produces a bullish phase event, otherwise a bearish envelope-stop event produces a bearish phase event; set phase to zero.
Calculation: Combined event = For each direction independently, match a phase event and a paired event of that direction within a second pairing window, in either order. An unmatched event replaces the pending event of that direction and restarts its timer; a match clears it. Expire pending events when their timer exceeds the window. Process the phase event before the paired event, allowing the latter to overwrite the result on the same bar.
Long: Bearish combined event AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, the lowest RSI in its lookback is at or above the lower RSI threshold AND, if the MACD filter is enabled, the MACD histogram is nonnegative OR is not falling AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, the largest bearish candle body in its lookback is at or below current ATR times the body multiplier.
Short: Bullish combined event AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, the highest RSI in its lookback is at or below the upper RSI threshold AND, if the MACD filter is enabled, the MACD histogram is nonpositive OR is not rising AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, the largest bullish candle body in its lookback is at or below current ATR times the body multiplier.
Exit: short, long condition.
