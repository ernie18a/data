Calculation: Bar notation = [k] means k chart bars ago; all calculations use chart bars. A condition involving an unavailable value is false. Enabled features and optional filters are independent Boolean parameters.
Calculation: Candle measures = body = abs(close - open); range = max(high - low, positive range floor); body fraction = body / range; upper wick = (high - max(open, close)) / range; lower wick = (min(open, close) - low) / range; bullish = close > open; bearish = close < open. A centered doji has body fraction below its threshold and (open + close)/2 within a symmetric central interval of the range.
Calculation: Trend = an uptrend has close > short EMA > long EMA; a downtrend has close < short EMA < long EMA. The EMA used for the slope filter is the long EMA.
Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows with a symmetric configurable number of bars on either side; update the latest and previous pivot only when the right-hand bars have completed. Pivot trendlines extend the line through the latest two confirmed same-side pivots to the current bar.
Calculation: Session levels = premarket high and low accumulate during the configured New York premarket session, reset at its start and lock at the regular-session open. Regular-session high and low accumulate through the current bar, reset at the regular-session open and at the weekly premarket open, and retain their last values outside the regular session. Record the bar of each strictly new extreme. The opening-range high and low accumulate over a configurable initial regular-session window and become available on the following bar; reset them at the regular-session open and weekly premarket open. Session membership uses the bar opening time and configured New York session and weekdays.
Calculation: Key proximity = abs(close - level) / level < key proximity threshold. Before Fibonacci levels are added, resistance is proximity to the latest pivot high or an enabled premarket or regular-session high; support is proximity to the latest pivot low or an enabled premarket or regular-session low. A prior-bar high or low is at an extreme if it exceeds the preceding lookback high or low by an ATR margin, respectively, or is within the key proximity threshold of the latest corresponding pivot, using the prior bar's high or low.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = four ordered retracement ratios of the current regular-session high-low range. If the high was set at or after the low, subtract each ratio of the range from the high; otherwise add it to the low. A level is nearby if its distance from close is below a configurable fraction of close. The physically upper two nearby levels augment resistance and the lower two augment support. A nearby level is confluent if its distance from any premarket extreme, regular-session extreme or latest pivot is below the same fraction of close. Fibonacci proximity requires the feature to be enabled. Pattern calculations below use resistance and support before this Fibonacci augmentation; engulf scoring and EMA exceptions use the augmented levels.
Calculation: Strict high extreme = high > highest high of the preceding extreme lookback + a configurable ATR multiple. Strict low extreme = low < lowest low of the preceding extreme lookback - that ATR multiple. A high or low extreme also accepts proximity to pre-Fibonacci resistance or support, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish doji engulf = the previous bar is a centered doji AND the current bar is bullish AND close > previous high AND current body > previous body. Bearish doji engulf = the previous bar is a centered doji AND the current bar is bearish AND close < previous low AND current body > previous body. These require their feature enabled.
Calculation: Strong bullish engulf = bullish AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND previous body < a configurable fraction of current body AND previous bar is not a centered doji. Strong bearish engulf = bearish AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND previous body < the same fraction of current body AND previous bar is not a centered doji. These require their feature enabled.
Calculation: Hammer = low extreme AND lower wick >= wick threshold AND min(open, close) > low + range times an upper-body threshold. Shooting star = high extreme AND upper wick >= wick threshold AND max(open, close) < low + range times a lower-body threshold. These require their feature enabled.
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = volume falls strictly on each successive bar over the configured short sequence AND, if enabled, volume < volume SMA AND strict low extreme AND body fraction < exhaustion threshold AND (bullish OR body fraction < doji threshold) AND (not a downtrend OR close is near the latest pivot low). Bearish exhaustion is symmetric at a strict high, with bearish candle and latest pivot high. The sequence uses one of two fixed lengths depending on whether its input crosses a cutoff. These require their feature enabled.
Calculation: Bullish climax = volume >= volume SMA times a multiplier AND strict low extreme AND (bullish OR lower wick > rejection threshold). Bearish climax = volume >= volume SMA times the multiplier AND strict high extreme AND (bearish OR upper wick > rejection threshold). These require their feature enabled.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = low < lowest low of the preceding divergence lookback AND RSI > lowest RSI of that same preceding lookback AND RSI < its midpoint. Bearish divergence = high > highest high of the preceding divergence lookback AND RSI < highest RSI of that same preceding lookback AND RSI > its midpoint. These and RSI overbought/oversold tests require RSI scoring enabled.
Calculation: Bullish score components = add one point for each true condition: hammer; strong bullish engulf; bullish exhaustion; bullish doji engulf AND bullish exhaustion on the current or previous bar; bullish climax; close > latest pivot high AND downtrend; close near latest pivot low AND lower wick > rejection threshold AND bullish; descending pivot-high trendline AND close > its current value AND previous close <= its current value; uptrend AND low <= short EMA times an upper touch factor AND close > short EMA times a higher decisive-close factor AND bullish AND at least a configured number of consecutive closes above the short EMA immediately before this bar; opening range available AND close > its high AND previous close <= its high AND breakout-volume filter passes; bullish flag breakout; a newly confirmed pivot high above the previous pivot high AND uptrend; RSI <= oversold threshold; bullish divergence; centered doji AND strict low extreme; nearby Fibonacci level AND (hammer OR bullish doji engulf OR strong bullish engulf OR body fraction < doji threshold); confluent Fibonacci level AND bullish. Also add one point for bullish doji engulf, plus one additional point if the prior bar was at a low extreme or current close is at augmented support. Each feature-specific component requires its feature enabled.
Calculation: Bearish score components = add one point for each true condition: shooting star; strong bearish engulf; bearish exhaustion; bearish doji engulf AND bearish exhaustion on the current or previous bar; bearish climax; close < latest pivot low AND uptrend; close near latest pivot high AND upper wick > rejection threshold AND bearish; ascending pivot-low trendline AND close < its current value AND previous close >= its current value; downtrend AND high >= short EMA times a lower touch factor AND close < short EMA times a lower decisive-close factor AND bearish AND at least a configured number of consecutive closes below the short EMA immediately before this bar; opening range available AND close < its low AND previous close >= its low AND breakout-volume filter passes; bearish flag breakout; a newly confirmed pivot low below the previous pivot low AND downtrend; RSI >= overbought threshold; bearish divergence; centered doji AND strict high extreme; enabled daily resistance proximity AND (shooting star OR bearish doji engulf OR strong bearish engulf OR body fraction < doji threshold); nearby Fibonacci level AND (shooting star OR bearish doji engulf OR strong bearish engulf OR body fraction < doji threshold); confluent Fibonacci level AND bearish. Also add one point for bearish doji engulf, plus one additional point if the prior bar was at a high extreme or current close is at augmented resistance. Each feature-specific component requires its feature enabled.
Calculation: Flag breakout = compare close at the end and beginning of a longer historical pole interval that ends a shorter lookback ago; the signed percentage move, divided by the appropriate endpoint close subject to a positive floor, must exceed the pole threshold. The high-low range of the most recent shorter lookback including the current bar must be less than a configurable fraction of the absolute pole move. A bullish breakout additionally requires close > highest high of the preceding shorter lookback; a bearish breakout requires close < lowest low of that lookback. The breakout-volume filter passes when disabled or volume > volume SMA. Opening-range and flag components require their respective features enabled.
Calculation: Display score = the sum of the applicable score components capped at a configurable maximum. The required bullish score is that maximum when the enabled slope filter finds long EMA minus its lookback value < negative slope threshold times close, otherwise the minimum score; the bearish requirement is the maximum when that difference > slope threshold times close, otherwise the minimum score. Same-side cooldown requires the number of bars since the last displayed same-side signal > cooldown parameter; premarket alerts and score backgrounds do not update it.
Calculation: Bullish EMA exception = (bullish doji engulf OR strong bullish engulf) AND (augmented support proximity OR prior-bar low extreme) OR (centered doji AND strict low extreme AND RSI <= oversold threshold). Bearish EMA exception = (bearish doji engulf OR strong bearish engulf) AND (augmented resistance proximity OR prior-bar high extreme) OR (centered doji AND strict high extreme AND RSI >= overbought threshold). The RSI branch requires RSI scoring enabled.
Long: Bullish display score >= required bullish score AND bullish raw score > 0 AND same-side cooldown passes AND (regular-session-only filter disabled OR bar is in regular session) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish divergence) AND (signal-volume filter disabled OR volume > previous volume times a configurable factor) AND (close > short EMA OR bullish EMA exception).
Short: Bearish display score >= required bearish score AND bearish raw score > 0 AND same-side cooldown passes AND (regular-session-only filter disabled OR bar is in regular session) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish divergence) AND (signal-volume filter disabled OR volume > previous volume times a configurable factor) AND (close < short EMA OR bearish EMA exception).
Long: Bar is outside the regular session AND bullish display score >= required bullish score AND bullish raw score > 0 AND same-side cooldown passes AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish divergence) AND (signal-volume filter disabled OR volume > previous volume times a configurable factor) AND (close > short EMA OR bullish EMA exception).
Short: Bar is outside the regular session AND bearish display score >= required bearish score AND bearish raw score > 0 AND same-side cooldown passes AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish divergence) AND (signal-volume filter disabled OR volume > previous volume times a configurable factor) AND (close < short EMA OR bearish EMA exception).
Long: Score background enabled AND bullish display score = maximum score AND same-side cooldown passes.
Short: Score background enabled AND bearish display score = maximum score AND same-side cooldown passes AND NOT (bullish display score = maximum score AND bullish cooldown passes).
Long: Hammer OR bullish climax OR (change-of-character feature enabled AND close > latest pivot high AND downtrend) OR (opening-range feature enabled AND opening range available AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND breakout-volume filter passes) OR bullish divergence.
Short: Shooting star OR bearish climax OR (change-of-character feature enabled AND close < latest pivot low AND uptrend) OR (opening-range feature enabled AND opening range available AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND breakout-volume filter passes) OR bearish divergence.
