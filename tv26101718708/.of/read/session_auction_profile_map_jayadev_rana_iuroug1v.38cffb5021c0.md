Calculation: Initial balance = The highest high and lowest low among bars whose opening times fall within the initial-balance duration from the first bar of a session. After that duration, record whether a bar's high exceeds the initial-balance high and whether a bar's low falls below the initial-balance low; each record remains true until the next session starts.
Calculation: Developing value-area boundaries = At each session start, set bin height to the greater of a minimum price increment and a multiple of ATR, substituting the first bar's high minus low if ATR is unavailable. Index price bins by floor(price / bin height). For each session bar, distribute its volume equally across all bins from floor(low / bin height) through floor(high / bin height), using volume of one when volume is missing or nonpositive. Expand the histogram to include touched bins, stopping expansion and accumulation when the maximum bin count is exceeded. The point of control is the lowest-index bin with the greatest accumulated volume. Starting there, add the adjacent bin with greater volume until the accumulated volume reaches the specified fraction of total accumulated volume; choose the upper bin on a tie. The lower boundary is the lower edge of the lowest included bin, and the upper boundary is the upper edge of the highest included bin. Retain the profile until the next session starts.
Long: The initial-balance high has been broken AND the initial-balance low has not been broken.
Short: The initial-balance low has been broken AND the initial-balance high has not been broken.
Long: Close > developing value-area upper boundary.
Short: Close < developing value-area lower boundary.
