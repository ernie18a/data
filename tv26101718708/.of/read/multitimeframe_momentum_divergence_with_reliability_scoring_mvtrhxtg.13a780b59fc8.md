Calculation: Momentum oscillator = RSI of a selectable price source minus its center, standardized either by rolling mean and standard deviation or by rolling median and scaled median absolute deviation. Use either one normalization window or the median of standardized values from shorter, medium, and longer windows. Optionally smooth with EMA or Ehlers UltimateSmoother.
Calculation: Divergence pivots = consecutive confirmed pivot lows or highs of the momentum oscillator, with equal left and right confirmation lengths; compare the price source at each oscillator pivot. Evaluate a divergence on the confirmation bar. The latest two pivots must be no more than a maximum number of chart bars apart, and their absolute oscillator difference must be at least a threshold times the oscillator's rolling standard deviation on the confirmation bar. If the three-pivot option is selected, the same directional inequalities must also hold between the preceding two pivots; the gap and magnitude tests apply only to the latest pair.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = the count of three higher timeframes whose close-based RSI is respectively greater or less than its value a fixed number of that timeframe's bars earlier. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Signal gate = higher-timeframe agreement in the divergence direction is at least the required count AND, if the external regime gate is enabled, its selected data-derived source is within the permitted range and rounds to a nonpositive value AND, if the time-of-day gate is enabled, the current time slot is significant. The time-of-day significance calculation is in an imported library whose implementation is not provided, so that optional gate cannot be reproduced from the document alone.
Long: A confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than the previous pivot low AND its corresponding price is lower than the previous pivot's price AND the divergence pivot requirements hold AND either connecting lines are enabled OR the signal gate passes.
Long: Hidden divergences are enabled AND a confirmed oscillator pivot low is lower than the previous pivot low AND its corresponding price is higher than the previous pivot's price AND the divergence pivot requirements hold AND either connecting lines are enabled OR the signal gate passes.
Short: A confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than the previous pivot high AND its corresponding price is higher than the previous pivot's price AND the divergence pivot requirements hold AND either connecting lines are enabled OR the signal gate passes.
Short: Hidden divergences are enabled AND a confirmed oscillator pivot high is higher than the previous pivot high AND its corresponding price is lower than the previous pivot's price AND the divergence pivot requirements hold AND either connecting lines are enabled OR the signal gate passes.
