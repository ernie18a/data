Calculation: 08:30 open = open of the first bar in the 08:30–17:00 New York session after the 18:00 New York trading-day reset
Calculation: Asia low/high = lowest low / highest high from 20:00 through 03:30 New York in the current trading day
Calculation: London low/high = lowest low / highest high from 03:30 through 08:00 New York in the current trading day
Calculation: Current cycle low/high = lowest low / highest high of the active cycle through the previous bar
Calculation: Sellside sweep = low below and close above any available Asia low, London low, or current cycle low
Calculation: Buyside sweep = high above and close below any available Asia high, London high, or current cycle high
Long: close < 08:30 open AND sellside sweep
Short: close > 08:30 open AND buyside sweep
