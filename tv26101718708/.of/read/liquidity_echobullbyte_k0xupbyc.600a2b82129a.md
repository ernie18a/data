Calculation: Calibration = over a calibration window, wick-body ratio is SMA(high - max(open, close) + min(open, close) - low) / SMA(abs(close - open)), with a fallback when average body is zero; volume variation is standard deviation(volume) / SMA(volume), with a fallback when mean volume is zero; volatility rank is (ATR - lowest ATR) / (highest ATR - lowest ATR) over the calibration window, with a fallback when the range is zero; average volatility rank is the SMA of that rank over the calibration window. Derive narrowing threshold by clamping a decreasing linear function of average volatility rank; vacuum threshold by clamping an increasing linear function of volume variation; minimum wick threshold by clamping an increasing linear function of wick-body ratio; and gradient threshold by clamping a decreasing linear function of wick-body ratio. Apply the selected sensitivity multipliers and limits, or use fixed thresholds when automatic calibration is disabled. Use a longer volume SMA window when volume variation exceeds its cutoff, otherwise a shorter window. Enable the grind path when explicitly enabled OR wick-body ratio is below its cutoff.
Calculation: Pressure gradient = (current close-location delta - close-location delta two chart bars ago) / 2, where delta = (2 * close - high - low) / max(high - low, minimum positive price increment).
Calculation: Active zones = on each confirmed chart bar, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and its two preceding higher-timeframe bars. Register its high and low as a zone when its range is less than narrowing threshold times the mean range of those three bars and it does not overlap an existing zone. Count a touch once per chart bar when high >= zone high - ATR * touch tolerance AND low < zone high, OR low <= zone low + ATR * touch tolerance AND high > zone low. Count touches before selecting zones. Remove zones older than the lifetime and retain at most the configured number, newest first. The bullish boundary is the highest eligible zone low strictly below close; the bearish boundary is the lowest eligible zone high strictly above close; eligibility requires the minimum touch count. When higher-timeframe zones are disabled, use the most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low and pivot high respectively, with longer left and shorter right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume) using the calibrated volume window, or a fallback ratio when the average is zero. A volume vacuum requires positive, available volume and an available volume average, with volume ratio below the vacuum threshold for at least the required consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish grind = grind path enabled AND a prior low within the grind lookback < current bullish boundary - current ATR * excursion tolerance AND close > bullish boundary AND close < bullish boundary + current ATR * maximum return distance AND close > open AND pressure gradient > gradient threshold. Bearish grind = grind path enabled AND a prior high within the grind lookback > current bearish boundary + current ATR * excursion tolerance AND close < bearish boundary AND close > bearish boundary - current ATR * maximum return distance AND close < open AND pressure gradient < -gradient threshold.
Calculation: Bullish spike sweep = volume vacuum AND low < bullish boundary AND close >= bullish boundary - ATR * maximum body distance AND min(open, close) - low >= ATR * minimum wick threshold AND (bullish boundary - low) / ATR >= minimum wick threshold AND pressure gradient > gradient threshold. Bearish spike sweep = volume vacuum AND high > bearish boundary AND close <= bearish boundary + ATR * maximum body distance AND high - max(open, close) >= ATR * minimum wick threshold AND (high - bearish boundary) / ATR >= minimum wick threshold AND pressure gradient < -gradient threshold.
Calculation: Pending sweep = while flat, record the latest confirmed bullish or bearish grind or spike sweep and its boundary; a bearish sweep overwrites a bullish sweep on the same bar. Replace the record on each subsequent sweep, and expire it when its age exceeds the sweep window. A sweep may be confirmed on its own bar. Clear it on entry.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry close, fix first target, second target and initial stop at their respective ATR multiples from entry, using entry-bar ATR; targets are above and stop below entry for a long, reversed for a short. Process trade management on the entry bar and every subsequent bar: first record a first-target hit and reduce the position by the configured fraction; optionally move the stop to entry on that hit; then record a second-target hit; then, if trailing is enabled and its start condition is met, set the long stop to max(previous trail or current stop, lowest low over the trailing lookback - current ATR * trailing offset), or the short stop to min(previous trail or current stop, highest high over the trailing lookback + current ATR * trailing offset). Once initialized, the trail uses its previous value even if breakeven changes the stop. Evaluate the resulting stop and timeout after these updates.
Long: close > EMA.
Short: close <= EMA.
Long: bullish grind.
Short: bearish grind.
Long: flat AND calibration history exceeds its required window AND ATR and EMA are available AND bars since the last signal of either direction >= minimum spacing AND pending sweep direction = long AND sweep age <= sweep window AND close > open AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= minimum commitment body ratio AND volume > 0 AND volume average is available AND volume ratio >= minimum commitment volume ratio AND (zone-cross filter disabled OR close > recorded sweep boundary) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (two-bar confirmation disabled OR prior close > prior open AND prior close > recorded sweep boundary).
Short: flat AND calibration history exceeds its required window AND ATR and EMA are available AND bars since the last signal of either direction >= minimum spacing AND pending sweep direction = short AND sweep age <= sweep window AND close < open AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= minimum commitment body ratio AND volume > 0 AND volume average is available AND volume ratio >= minimum commitment volume ratio AND (zone-cross filter disabled OR close < recorded sweep boundary) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (two-bar confirmation disabled OR prior close < prior open AND prior close < recorded sweep boundary).
Exit: long, first target not previously hit AND high >= first target; exit the configured fraction.
Exit: short, first target not previously hit AND low <= first target; exit the configured fraction.
Exit: long, high >= second target OR low <= updated stop OR (first target not hit AND bars since entry > timeout).
Exit: short, low <= second target OR high >= updated stop OR (first target not hit AND bars since entry > timeout).
