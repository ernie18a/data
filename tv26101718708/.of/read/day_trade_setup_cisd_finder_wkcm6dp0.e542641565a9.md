Calculation: Directional sequence = consecutive candles with close > open or consecutive candles with close < open; a candle with close = open ends a sequence. Its source is its first candle and its level is that candle's open.
Calculation: Monitored level = maintain separate bullish and bearish levels on closed chart bars. In sequence mode, a new bearish sequence proposes a bullish level and a new bullish sequence proposes a bearish level. In swing mode, a confirmed swing high proposes a bullish level from the first bearish sequence found scanning from the pivot through the confirmation bar; a confirmed swing low similarly proposes a bearish level from the first bullish sequence. If the pivot belongs to that sequence, use the sequence's original source even if it precedes the pivot. Discard a proposal if, from the pivot through the bar before confirmation, any close was above the bullish level or below the bearish level, respectively. If no required sequence exists by confirmation, wait for the first candle of the required direction, expiring the wait when the pivot's age exceeds the maximum level age. A newer swing of the same kind replaces that wait. Swing confirmation uses independently parameterized counts of preceding and following bars. Accept a proposal only if its source is newer than every previously accepted source on that side and its age is at most the maximum level age; acceptance replaces that side's existing level and arms the new level. Each source can arm only once. On each bar, first remove levels whose source age exceeds the maximum level age, then test existing armed levels, then process and test newly accepted levels. A successful test disarms the level.
Long: Bullish monitored level is armed AND current bar is later than its source AND close > bullish monitored level.
Short: Bearish monitored level is armed AND current bar is later than its source AND close < bearish monitored level.
