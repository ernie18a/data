Calculation: Hybrid momentum = (RSI of close + MFI of typical price using the RSI lookback) / 2.
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative close times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each trading day.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA, or 1 if that SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper Bollinger band - lower Bollinger band) / Bollinger basis, or 0 if the basis is zero; its SMA uses the same lookback as the bands.
Calculation: Bullish strong-bar condition = close > VWAP AND close > EMA AND EMA > previous EMA AND previous EMA > EMA two bars ago AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > 0 AND RSI > bullish lower bound AND RSI < bullish upper bound AND RSI > previous RSI AND RSI < bullish exhaustion threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND ADX > previous ADX AND +DI > -DI AND +DI - -DI > directional spread threshold AND bar opening time is within the selected session or sessions in the selected time zone, unless the session filter is disabled AND (hybrid filter disabled OR (hybrid momentum > bullish strong threshold AND hybrid momentum > previous hybrid momentum)).
Calculation: Bearish strong-bar condition = close < VWAP AND close < EMA AND EMA < previous EMA AND previous EMA < EMA two bars ago AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < 0 AND RSI < bearish upper bound AND RSI > bearish lower bound AND RSI < previous RSI AND RSI > bearish exhaustion threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND ADX > previous ADX AND -DI > +DI AND -DI - +DI > directional spread threshold AND bar opening time is within the selected session or sessions in the selected time zone, unless the session filter is disabled AND (hybrid filter disabled OR (hybrid momentum < bearish strong threshold AND hybrid momentum < previous hybrid momentum)).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish condition = close > EMA AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI > RSI midpoint, calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar and the corresponding chart-timeframe indicator parameters.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish condition = close < EMA AND MACD line < signal line AND RSI < RSI midpoint, calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar and the corresponding chart-timeframe indicator parameters.
Calculation: Bullish raw expansion condition = bullish strong-bar condition on this bar and the previous bar AND higher-timeframe bullish condition AND previous Bollinger width < its previous SMA times compression factor AND Bollinger width > previous Bollinger width AND ATR > previous ATR AND ATR > SMA of ATR using the ATR lookback AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND ATR > 0 AND (close - VWAP) / ATR > minimum VWAP distance AND (close - VWAP) / ATR < maximum VWAP distance AND (close - EMA) / ATR < maximum EMA distance AND (highest high over the room lookback including this bar - close) / ATR > room threshold AND close > highest high over the breakout lookback excluding this bar AND (close - that highest high) / ATR > breakout quality threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume) AND (hybrid filter disabled OR (hybrid momentum > bullish expansion threshold AND hybrid momentum > previous hybrid momentum)).
Calculation: Bearish raw expansion condition = bearish strong-bar condition on this bar and the previous bar AND higher-timeframe bearish condition AND previous Bollinger width < its previous SMA times compression factor AND Bollinger width > previous Bollinger width AND ATR > previous ATR AND ATR > SMA of ATR using the ATR lookback AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND ATR > 0 AND (VWAP - close) / ATR > minimum VWAP distance AND (VWAP - close) / ATR < maximum VWAP distance AND (EMA - close) / ATR < maximum EMA distance AND (close - lowest low over the room lookback including this bar) / ATR > room threshold AND close < lowest low over the breakout lookback excluding this bar AND (that lowest low - close) / ATR > breakout quality threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume) AND (hybrid filter disabled OR (hybrid momentum < bearish expansion threshold AND hybrid momentum < previous hybrid momentum)).
Long: bullish strong-bar condition on this bar AND bullish strong-bar condition on the previous bar.
Short: bearish strong-bar condition on this bar AND bearish strong-bar condition on the previous bar.
Long: bullish raw expansion condition AND (no previous bullish raw expansion condition OR bars since the most recent bullish raw expansion condition through the previous bar > cooldown length).
Short: bearish raw expansion condition AND (no previous bearish raw expansion condition OR bars since the most recent bearish raw expansion condition through the previous bar > cooldown length).
Long: higher-timeframe bullish condition.
Short: higher-timeframe bearish condition.
Long: EMA > previous EMA AND previous EMA > EMA two bars ago.
Short: EMA < previous EMA AND previous EMA < EMA two bars ago.
Long: hybrid momentum > bullish strong threshold.
Short: hybrid momentum <= bullish strong threshold AND hybrid momentum < bearish strong threshold.
