Calculation: PSMA = Percentage of configured moving-average lengths from minimum through maximum for which the configured price source is above its simple moving average; also calculate the EMA of that percentage.
Calculation: SuperTrend oscillator = Build trailing upper and lower bands from hl2 ± ATR × multiplier; choose the lower band in the bullish trend and upper band in the bearish trend, then clamp (close − chosen band) / (upper band − lower band) to [-1, 1].
Calculation: Linear correlation = Rolling Pearson correlation of the configured price source with bar time over a lookback window.
Calculation: Stochastic SuperTrend = Calculate RSI, then stochastic RSI %K and smooth it; apply trailing SuperTrend bands at %K ± multiplier. Use the configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use its last completed bar when wait-for-close is enabled, otherwise its still-forming bar.
Calculation: RROF = Normalize bar effort from price movement and bar position relative to rolling average effort, weight it by normalized volume, smooth positive and negative flows separately, and convert their ratio to an oscillator; smooth the oscillator.
Long: Stochastic SuperTrend direction changes to bullish AND smoothed %K < 50 AND SuperTrend oscillator crosses above -0.50 AND linear correlation < configured lower level AND smoothed RROF > its prior value AND prior-bar Williams %R < configured oversold level AND raw PSMA and its EMA are both below the configured buy level.
Short: Stochastic SuperTrend direction changes to bearish AND smoothed %K > 50 AND SuperTrend oscillator crosses below 0.50 AND linear correlation > configured upper level AND smoothed RROF <= its prior value AND prior-bar Williams %R > configured overbought level AND raw PSMA and its EMA are both above the configured sell level.
