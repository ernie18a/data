Calculation: Price-above-average percentage = 100 times the number of integer-length simple moving averages of the source, from a minimum through a maximum length inclusive, that are strictly below the source, divided by the number of those averages. Calculate each average as the difference between the cumulative source sum and its value that many bars ago, with missing past cumulative sums treated as zero, divided by its length.
Calculation: SuperTrend oscillator = clamp((close − selected band) / (upper band − lower band), −1, 1). Raw bands are midpoint of high and low plus or minus ATR times a multiplier. The upper band is the lesser of its raw value and previous upper band when the previous close is below the previous upper band; otherwise it is the raw upper band. The lower band is the greater of its raw value and previous lower band when the previous close is above the previous lower band; otherwise it is the raw lower band. Trend is bullish when close is above the previous upper band, bearish when close is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retains its previous state. Select the lower band in a bullish trend and the upper band otherwise. Initialize missing previous bands and trend to zero.
Calculation: Stochastic trend = RSI of the requested close, followed by stochastic normalization of that RSI over a lookback and a simple moving average. Perform these calculations on chart bars after merging the requested closes. The requested timeframe may equal, be higher than, or be lower than the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar when waiting for timeframe close and the still-forming bar otherwise; historical lookahead-on values use the final higher-timeframe close from the start of its interval. For a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar when waiting for timeframe close and the first historical intrabar otherwise. Set raw upper and lower bands to the smoothed stochastic plus and minus a fixed offset. Retain the previous upper band unless the raw upper band is lower or the previous stochastic is above the previous upper band; retain the previous lower band unless the raw lower band is higher or the previous stochastic is below the previous lower band. Treat missing previous bands as zero. Initialize direction bearish on the second chart bar. Thereafter, if the previous selected band equals the previous upper band, direction is bullish when the stochastic exceeds the current upper band and bearish otherwise; in all other cases it is bearish when the stochastic is below the current lower band and bullish otherwise. Select the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish; treat a missing previous selected band as zero.
Calculation: Effort oscillator = a configurable moving average of 2 × (100 − 100 / (1 + average positive flow / average negative flow)) − 100. Positive flow is max(flow, 0), negative flow is −min(flow, 0), and each is averaged with the same configurable moving-average type and length. Flow is the mean of six price measures multiplied by normalized volume divided by 100. The six measures are: 200 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 100; 100 × (close − open) / (high − low); 100 × sign(close − open) × normalized absolute close-to-open change; 200 × (close − lowest low over the current and previous bars) / (highest high over those bars − lowest low over those bars) − 100; 100 × (close − previous close) / that two-bar range; and 100 × sign(close − previous close) × normalized absolute close-to-close change. Normalize each absolute change and volume by dividing it by its own lookback moving average, then mapping ratios greater than 1.50, 1.20, 1.00, 0.80, 0.60, 0.40, and 0.20 to 1.00, 0.90, 0.80, 0.70, 0.60, 0.50, and 0.25 respectively, and all other ratios to 0.10. Multiply normalized volume by 100; use 100 if volume is unavailable. The lookback average is either a simple moving average or the flow averaging type.
Long: Price-above-average percentage and its EMA are both below the buy threshold AND the SuperTrend oscillator crosses above its lower threshold AND the previous bar's Williams %R is below its oversold threshold AND Pearson correlation of source with chronological bar index over a rolling lookback is below its lower threshold AND the effort oscillator is greater than its previous value AND stochastic trend changes from bearish to bullish AND its smoothed stochastic is below the midpoint.
Short: Price-above-average percentage and its EMA are both above the sell threshold AND the SuperTrend oscillator crosses below its upper threshold AND the previous bar's Williams %R is above its overbought threshold AND Pearson correlation of source with chronological bar index over a rolling lookback is above its upper threshold AND the effort oscillator is less than or equal to its previous value AND stochastic trend changes from bullish to bearish AND its smoothed stochastic is above the midpoint.
