Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish gap = at the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it, a zone from the earlier bar's high to the latest completed bar's low when the latter is greater and the difference is at least the earlier high multiplied by the higher-timeframe minimum gap size in basis points divided by 10,000.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish gap = at the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it, a zone from the latest completed bar's high to the earlier bar's low when the latter is greater and the difference is at least the latest completed high multiplied by the higher-timeframe minimum gap size in basis points divided by 10,000.
Calculation: Active higher-timeframe gaps = gaps formed at a higher-timeframe boundary inside the configured session in America/Chicago; clear all gaps at session open. If mitigation removal is enabled, remove bullish gaps when chart close < zone bottom and bearish gaps when chart close > zone top, including on the bar the gap is created, before checking nested signals.
Calculation: Shared signal cooldown = chart bars since the last nested long or short signal; reset at session open. Before any signal in the session, the cooldown condition is satisfied.
Calculation: Nested signal entry price = high two chart bars ago for a long, or low two chart bars ago for a short. Evaluate exits starting on the bar after the signal; a new nested signal replaces the currently tracked trade, and session open clears it.
Long: Current chart bar starts a new higher-timeframe bar AND current bar is in the configured session in America/Chicago AND a higher-timeframe bullish gap has just formed AND higher-timeframe gap display is enabled.
Short: Current chart bar starts a new higher-timeframe bar AND current bar is in the configured session in America/Chicago AND a higher-timeframe bearish gap has just formed AND higher-timeframe gap display is enabled.
Long: Current chart bar is complete AND its time is in the configured session in America/Chicago AND low > high two chart bars ago AND low minus that high >= that high multiplied by the chart-timeframe minimum gap size in basis points divided by 10,000 AND there is an active bullish higher-timeframe gap whose bottom minus its bottom multiplied by proximity in basis points divided by 10,000 <= that high AND whose top plus the same proximity amount >= current low AND shared signal cooldown >= configured cooldown.
Short: Current chart bar is complete AND its time is in the configured session in America/Chicago AND high < low two chart bars ago AND that low minus high >= current high multiplied by the chart-timeframe minimum gap size in basis points divided by 10,000 AND there is an active bearish higher-timeframe gap whose bottom minus its bottom multiplied by proximity in basis points divided by 10,000 <= current high AND whose top plus the same proximity amount >= that low AND shared signal cooldown >= configured cooldown.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop distance OR high >= entry price plus main target distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop distance OR low <= entry price minus main target distance.
Exit: long, partial exit if low > entry price minus stop distance AND high < entry price plus main target distance AND high >= entry price plus partial target distance AND no partial exit has occurred for this trade.
Exit: short, partial exit if high < entry price plus stop distance AND low > entry price minus main target distance AND low <= entry price minus partial target distance AND no partial exit has occurred for this trade.
