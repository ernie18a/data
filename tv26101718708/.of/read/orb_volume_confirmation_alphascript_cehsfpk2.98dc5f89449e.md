Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of in-session bars whose opening times are before the session's opening time plus the opening-range duration. The session opening time is the opening time of the first bar entering the configured session and timezone.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the selected baseline, undefined if volume or baseline is nonpositive or unavailable. The rolling baseline is the SMA of volume including the current bar. The adaptive baseline is the EMA of positive volumes from prior sessions at the same bar index within the session, initialized with the first positive volume and using the volume lookback as its EMA length; use the rolling baseline until the slot has the minimum number of positive-volume observations or its baseline is unavailable.
Calculation: Eligible breakout = the bar is in session, its opening time is at or after the opening-range end and before that end plus the signal-window duration, and the opening-range high is greater than the opening-range low.
Long: Eligible breakout AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND relative volume >= threshold AND confirmed long count in the current session = 0.
Long: Eligible breakout AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND (relative volume < threshold OR relative volume is undefined) AND confirmed long count in the current session = 0 AND unconfirmed long count in the current session = 0.
Short: Eligible breakout AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND relative volume >= threshold AND confirmed short count in the current session = 0.
Short: Eligible breakout AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND (relative volume < threshold OR relative volume is undefined) AND confirmed short count in the current session = 0 AND unconfirmed short count in the current session = 0.
