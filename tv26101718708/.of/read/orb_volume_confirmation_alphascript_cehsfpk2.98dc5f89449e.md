Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low from the start of each configured session through the configured opening-range duration.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / volume baseline; baseline is either the rolling SMA of chart-timeframe volume (including the current bar) or the EMA of volume for the same bar-of-session across prior sessions, falling back to the rolling SMA until enough sessions are available.
Long: During the session, after the opening range completes and before the configured signal window expires, opening-range height > 0 AND close crosses above opening-range high (current close > high AND previous close <= high) AND current volume > 0 AND relative volume is valid AND ((relative volume >= threshold AND no confirmed long has fired this session) OR (relative volume < threshold AND no confirmed or unconfirmed long has fired this session)).
Short: During the session, after the opening range completes and before the configured signal window expires, opening-range height > 0 AND close crosses below opening-range low (current close < low AND previous close >= low) AND current volume > 0 AND relative volume is valid AND ((relative volume >= threshold AND no confirmed short has fired this session) OR (relative volume < threshold AND no confirmed or unconfirmed short has fired this session)).
