Calculation: Cumulative volume delta (CVD) = running sum from zero of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low + price-increment parameter).
Calculation: Classifier features = CVD ROC, price ROC minus CVD ROC, and the slope of a linear regression of CVD; normalize each by subtracting its rolling mean and dividing by its rolling population standard deviation over the same normalization window, using zero when the standard deviation is unavailable or not positive. CVD ROC = (CVD − CVD at the momentum lookback) / (absolute CVD at that lookback + price-increment parameter); price ROC = (close − close at the same lookback) / (close at that lookback + price-increment parameter). The regression slope uses a separate lookback.
Calculation: Classifier posteriors = online three-class Gaussian naive Bayes probabilities. Label each bar bull if both price ROC and CVD ROC are positive, bear if both are negative, and diverged otherwise. Starting at zero-based bar index normalization window + momentum lookback + 2, add the previous bar's three features to its class's cumulative count, sums, and squared sums before evaluating the current bar. For each class and feature, mean = sum/count, or zero if count is zero; standard deviation = square root of max(squared sum/count − mean², variance floor), or one if count is less than two. Class prior = class count / total count; before any training, use a bull prior parameter and split the remaining prior equally between bear and diverged. Class likelihood is the product of the three Gaussian densities of the current features using those class means and standard deviations, with each standard deviation floored at a small positive parameter. Posterior = prior × likelihood / (sum of all three prior × likelihood products + small positive parameter). The classifier is warmed up when total training count reaches the classifier lookback parameter.
Calculation: Long state = classifier warmed up AND bull posterior >= entry threshold AND close > EMA of close AND CVD > previous CVD. Short state = classifier warmed up AND bear posterior >= entry threshold AND close < EMA of close AND CVD < previous CVD.
Long: Long state AND previous long state is false AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND bars since the most recent long or short entry signal >= cooldown parameter.
Short: Short state AND previous short state is false AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND bars since the most recent long or short entry signal >= cooldown parameter.
Long: Classifier warmed up AND close > EMA of close.
Short: Classifier warmed up AND close < EMA of close.
Long: Bull posterior >= entry threshold.
Short: Bear posterior >= entry threshold.
Long: CVD > previous CVD.
Short: CVD <= previous CVD.
