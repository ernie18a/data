Calculation: Smoothed regression = EMA of the linear regression of close at the current bar.
Calculation: Rising or falling = greater or less than every value in the preceding trend lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = the latest high or low confirmed as a pivot using equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot; confirmation occurs after the right-hand bars complete.
Calculation: Cloud bounds = maximum and minimum of the short-term EMA of close, smoothed regression, and the trend line two bars ago. In either pivot mode, the trend line is Supertrend. In cloud-MA mode, initialize it to zero; when RSI is at least its threshold, set it to the maximum of its previous value and low minus a multiplier times the SMA of true range; otherwise set it to the minimum of its previous value and high plus that product.
Calculation: Cloud MA = initialize to zero; when the bullish Andean Oscillator component exceeds the bearish component, set it to the maximum of its previous value and the lower cloud bound; when the bearish component exceeds the bullish component, set it to the minimum of its previous value and the upper cloud bound; otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Cloud trend = 1 when cloud MA is rising, -1 when it is falling, and otherwise its previous value.
Calculation: Pivot-mode long action = position is flat or short AND close is above the confirmed pivot high AND close is above the upper cloud bound AND datetime is inside the configured backtest interval AND, if the regression filter is enabled, smoothed regression is rising. Pivot-mode short action = position is flat or long AND close is below the confirmed pivot low AND close is below the lower cloud bound AND datetime is inside the configured backtest interval AND, if the regression filter is enabled, smoothed regression is falling.
Calculation: Cloud-MA-mode long action = cloud trend is positive AND position is flat or short AND close is above the upper cloud bound AND datetime is inside the configured backtest interval. Cloud-MA-mode short action = cloud trend is negative AND position is flat or long AND close is below the lower cloud bound AND datetime is inside the configured backtest interval.
Calculation: Swing low or high = lowest confirmed pivot low or highest confirmed pivot high over the sizing lookback.
Calculation: Tracked high and low = while flat, current high and low; while in a position, replace the tracked high with current high only when current high exceeds the preceding bar's high, and replace the tracked low with current low only when current low is below the preceding bar's low; otherwise retain the respective previous value.
Calculation: Long and short reference prices = on any change of the respective selected action, use the current open position's entry price when the position is respectively flat or long, or flat or short; otherwise retain the previous reference price, and set it to unavailable when the position is on the opposite side. On any action change, set the risk reference to the corresponding reference price plus entry price minus swing low for a long action change, or corresponding reference price minus swing high plus entry price for a short action change; otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Long stop = swing low whenever the short trend-exit condition is true; otherwise, when long and tracked high exceeds the risk reference, the long reference price; otherwise its previous value. Short stop = swing high whenever the long trend-exit condition is true; otherwise, when short and tracked low is below the risk reference, the short reference price; otherwise its previous value.
Calculation: Long permission state = 1 when cloud trend changes to 1; otherwise 0 when tracked low crosses under long stop; otherwise 1 when long; otherwise 0 when cloud trend is -1; otherwise its previous value. Short permission state = 1 when cloud trend changes to -1; otherwise 0 when tracked high crosses over short stop; otherwise 1 when short; otherwise 0 when cloud trend is 1; otherwise its previous value. Initialize both states to 0.
Calculation: Long targets = on any long action change, long reference price plus a reward multiplier times entry price minus swing low; otherwise retain the targets while long and reset them to zero when flat or short. Short targets = on any short action change, short reference price minus a reward multiplier times swing high minus entry price; otherwise retain the targets while short and reset them to zero when flat or long. Use two reward multipliers, the second longer than the first, and apply their respective configured partial-exit percentages.
Long: Selected long action changes in either direction AND previous long permission state equals 0.
Short: Selected short action changes in either direction AND previous short permission state equals 0.
Long: Close is above open AND volume is above a multiplier times the SMA of volume.
Short: Close is below open AND volume is above a multiplier times the SMA of volume.
Long: In pivot mode, close is above the upper cloud bound AND, if the regression filter is enabled, smoothed regression is rising.
Short: In pivot mode, close is below the lower cloud bound AND, if the regression filter is enabled, smoothed regression is falling.
Long: In cloud-MA mode, cloud MA is rising.
Short: In cloud-MA mode, cloud MA is falling.
Exit: long, in cloud-MA mode cloud trend changes to -1 OR in pivot mode close is below the lower cloud bound AND close is below the confirmed pivot low.
Exit: short, in cloud-MA mode cloud trend changes to 1 OR in pivot mode close is above the upper cloud bound AND close is above the confirmed pivot high.
Exit: long, stop and target orders are enabled AND low reaches or falls below long stop OR high reaches or exceeds a long target.
Exit: short, stop and target orders are enabled AND high reaches or exceeds short stop OR low reaches or falls below a short target.
