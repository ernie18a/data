Calculation: FastMA, SlowMA = EMA (or SMA) of close computed on bars of a timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed bar of that timeframe; FastMA lookback is shorter than SlowMA lookback.
Calculation: BullLevel = close of the higher-timeframe bar on which a bullish cross is detected. It is created only when the chart bar time is inside the active intraday session (exchange-local time). It stays active until it is removed. Removal happens when chart close < level - buffer, or when a later bearish-cross level is created at a price below it. An active bull level is called armed once a chart close > level + buffer has occurred on an earlier bar. Each level can fire a retest once.
Calculation: BearLevel = close of the higher-timeframe bar on which a bearish cross is detected, under the same session restriction. It stays active until it is removed. Removal happens when chart close > level + buffer, or when a later bullish-cross level is created at a price above it. An active bear level is called armed once a chart close < level - buffer has occurred on an earlier bar. Each level can fire a retest once.
Long: (FastMA crosses above SlowMA, i.e. FastMA > SlowMA and previous FastMA <= previous SlowMA) AND chart bar time is inside the active session
Long: an armed, not yet retested BullLevel exists AND chart low <= level AND chart close > level
Short: (FastMA crosses below SlowMA, i.e. FastMA < SlowMA and previous FastMA >= previous SlowMA) AND chart bar time is inside the active session
Short: an armed, not yet retested BearLevel exists AND chart high >= level AND chart close < level
