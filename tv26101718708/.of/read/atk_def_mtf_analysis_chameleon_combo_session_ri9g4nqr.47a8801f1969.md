Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = values from the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; calculate changes over chart bars after mapping the values to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Process score = assign the highest positive score when the higher-timeframe ADX change and RSI change both exceed their respective higher positive thresholds; otherwise assign the lower positive score when both exceed their respective lower positive thresholds; otherwise assign zero when both exceed their respective upper negative thresholds; otherwise assign the smaller-magnitude negative score when both exceed their respective lower negative thresholds; otherwise assign the larger-magnitude negative score.
Calculation: Radar value = clamp the sum of scaled higher-timeframe ADX, scaled deviation of higher-timeframe RSI from its midpoint, and the clamped scaled deviation of higher-timeframe ATR from its longer-period SMA, to lower and upper limits. The ATR deviation is ATR divided by its SMA minus one.
Calculation: Radar score = assign scores in descending order as the radar value exceeds each of four descending thresholds; assign the lowest score otherwise. The three highest scores are positive and the two lowest are negative.
Calculation: Depth position = (higher-timeframe close minus the lowest low over a lookback) divided by (the highest high over that lookback minus the lowest low plus a small positive constant), expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Depth score = assign scores in descending order as depth position exceeds each of four descending thresholds; assign the lowest score otherwise. The three highest scores are positive and the two lowest are negative.
Calculation: Direction score = assign the highest positive score when higher-timeframe close exceeds all three higher-timeframe close EMAs and positive DI exceeds negative DI; otherwise assign a lower positive score when close exceeds all three EMAs; otherwise assign the lowest negative score when close is below all three EMAs and negative DI exceeds positive DI; otherwise assign a less negative score when close is below all three EMAs; otherwise assign a smaller positive score when close exceeds the shortest EMA and is below the middle EMA; otherwise assign a smaller-magnitude negative score when close is below the shortest EMA and exceeds the middle EMA; otherwise assign zero. The three EMA periods are short, medium, and long.
Calculation: Final score = clamp the average of process score, radar score, depth score, and direction score to lower and upper limits.
Long: Higher-timeframe close exceeds its short, medium, and long EMAs OR higher-timeframe close exceeds its short EMA AND is below its medium EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe close is below its short, medium, and long EMAs OR higher-timeframe close is below its short EMA AND exceeds its medium EMA.
Long: Final score exceeds a positive buy threshold.
Short: Final score is less than or equal to a negative sell threshold.
