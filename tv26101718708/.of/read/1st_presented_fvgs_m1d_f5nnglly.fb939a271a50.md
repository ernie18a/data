Calculation: Refined gap boundaries = For a bullish gap, start with top = current low and bottom = high two bars ago; if body refinement is enabled, replace bottom with the earlier bar's body high when it is below the middle bar's body low, and replace top with the current bar's body low when it is above the middle bar's body high. For a bearish gap, start with top = low two bars ago and bottom = current high; if body refinement is enabled, replace top with the earlier bar's body low when it is above the middle bar's body high, and replace bottom with the current bar's body high when it is below the middle bar's body low.
Calculation: Eligible first gap = On a confirmed chart bar within the configured intraday chart-timeframe limit, the current bar opens on an enabled weekday in New York time, the middle bar opens at or after the configured New York window start, the current bar closes by the window end, the bar two bars ago and current bar have the same New York day of month, and no qualifying gap has been marked since the start of the current exchange day. The refined gap height is at least the configured minimum price increment threshold and, if the displacement filter is enabled, at least ATR multiplied by its configured multiplier.
Long: Eligible first gap AND current low > high two bars ago.
Short: Eligible first gap AND current high < low two bars ago.
