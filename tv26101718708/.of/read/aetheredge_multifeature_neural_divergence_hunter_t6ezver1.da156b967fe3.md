Calculation: Oscillators = RSI of close; MACD histogram of close using a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA; SMA of stochastic %K calculated from close, high, and low; CCI of close; MFI of typical price and volume; and momentum of close. Normalize MACD histogram, CCI, and momentum separately to 0–100 using each oscillator's rolling minimum and maximum over the same normalization lookback; use the midpoint when the range is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low with the same number of bars on each side; a high is greater than or equal to every high on its left and greater than every high on its right, and a low is less than or equal to every low on its left and less than every low on its right. Evaluate on the bar that confirms the pivot. Compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, regardless of whether that pivot produced a divergence, and sample each oscillator at both pivot bars.
Long: The gap between consecutive confirmed low pivots is within the inclusive minimum and maximum gap AND at least one enabled oscillator has a defined value at both pivots AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the latest pivot low < the preceding pivot low AND the latest oscillator value > its preceding value) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the latest pivot low > the preceding pivot low AND the latest oscillator value < its preceding value)).
Short: The gap between consecutive confirmed high pivots is within the inclusive minimum and maximum gap AND at least one enabled oscillator has a defined value at both pivots AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the latest pivot high > the preceding pivot high AND the latest oscillator value < its preceding value) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the latest pivot high < the preceding pivot high AND the latest oscillator value > its preceding value)).
