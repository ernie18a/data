Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows in an equal number of bars before and after it; confirm after those right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high higher than the highs in an equal number of bars before and after it; confirm after those right-side bars complete.
Calculation: MACD histogram = the fast-period EMA of close minus the slow-period EMA of close, minus the signal-period EMA of that difference.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = the simple moving average of the stochastic value, where stochastic = 100 × (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over its lookback.
Calculation: Momentum = close minus close from the momentum lookback bars ago.
Calculation: Normalized MACD histogram, CCI, and momentum = 100 × (value − its lowest value over the normalization lookback) / (its highest value over the normalization lookback − its lowest value over that lookback); use 50 if the range is zero. Use the same normalization lookback for all three.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence for an oscillator = at a confirmed pivot low, the current pivot low is below the previous pivot low and the oscillator value at the current pivot is above its value at the previous pivot; the pivot gap is within the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence for an oscillator = at a confirmed pivot low, the current pivot low is above the previous pivot low and the oscillator value at the current pivot is below its value at the previous pivot; the pivot gap is within the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence for an oscillator = at a confirmed pivot high, the current pivot high is above the previous pivot high and the oscillator value at the current pivot is below its value at the previous pivot; the pivot gap is within the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence for an oscillator = at a confirmed pivot high, the current pivot high is below the previous pivot high and the oscillator value at the current pivot is above its value at the previous pivot; the pivot gap is within the configured minimum and maximum.
Long: at a confirmed pivot low, the number of enabled oscillators (RSI, normalized MACD histogram, smoothed stochastic, CCI, MFI, and normalized momentum) with regular or hidden bullish divergence is at least the configured minimum confluence.
Short: at a confirmed pivot high, the number of enabled oscillators (RSI, normalized MACD histogram, smoothed stochastic, CCI, MFI, and normalized momentum) with regular or hidden bearish divergence is at least the configured minimum confluence.
