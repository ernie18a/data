Calculation: Timeframe score = On each of seven ordered timeframes, from shortest to longest, use either the last completed bar or, for the live preview, the bar still forming. Each timeframe may be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe. Compute short-, medium-, and long-period EMAs of close; ATR and its longer-period SMA baseline; and an EMA-smoothed RSI. Clamp ATR divided by its baseline to a lower and upper bound. Add these components: a positive full-stack weight if short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA, its negative if short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA, a smaller positive weight if short EMA > long EMA, its negative if short EMA < long EMA, otherwise zero; the long EMA's change over a lookback divided by ATR, multiplied by a positive weight and clamped symmetrically; a positive or negative RSI regime weight selected from two upper and two lower RSI thresholds, otherwise zero; the smoothed RSI's change over a lookback multiplied by a positive weight and clamped symmetrically; a positive full location weight if close > the highest EMA, its negative if close < the lowest EMA, a smaller positive weight if close > the midpoint of the highest and lowest EMAs, its negative if close < that midpoint, otherwise zero; and a positive VWAP weight if close > session VWAP of typical price, its negative if close <= VWAP, or zero if VWAP is unavailable. Multiply the sum by a reduced volatility factor when the ATR ratio is below its lower quality threshold or above its upper quality threshold, otherwise by one, and clamp the result symmetrically.
Calculation: Confluence score = The weighted average of the last-completed-bar timeframe scores of the six shortest timeframes, multiplied by a configurable session factor determined by chart-bar datetime and by a reduced factor when the similarly weighted average ATR ratio is below its low-volatility threshold, then clamped symmetrically. Session factors distinguish overlapping sessions, either main session alone, the reference session, and off-hours; session weighting can be disabled.
Long: Any timeframe score >= its positive trend threshold, using either the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
Short: Any timeframe score <= the negative trend threshold, using either the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
Long: Confluence score >= its positive display threshold.
Short: Confluence score <= the negative display threshold.
Long: On any of the seven timeframes' last completed bars, short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA AND close > medium EMA.
Short: On any of the seven timeframes' last completed bars, short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA AND close < medium EMA.
Long: For the conservative preset, the fifth-shortest and sixth-shortest completed-bar timeframe scores >= the positive regime threshold; OR, for the aggressive preset, the fifth-shortest score >= the positive regime threshold AND the third-shortest score > zero AND the second-shortest score > the negative regime threshold; OR, for the balanced preset, the fifth-shortest score >= the positive regime threshold AND the third-shortest score >= the positive regime threshold AND the fourth-shortest score > the negative regime threshold.
Short: For the conservative preset, the fifth-shortest and sixth-shortest completed-bar timeframe scores <= the negative regime threshold; OR, for the aggressive preset, the fifth-shortest score <= the negative regime threshold AND the third-shortest score < zero AND the second-shortest score < the positive regime threshold; OR, for the balanced preset, the fifth-shortest score <= the negative regime threshold AND the third-shortest score <= the negative regime threshold AND the fourth-shortest score < the positive regime threshold.
