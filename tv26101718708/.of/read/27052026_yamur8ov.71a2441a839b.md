Calculation: Trend strength = absolute value of close minus EMA of close, divided by ATR; use zero when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume divided by SMA of volume; use zero when the SMA is zero.
Long: Fast Supertrend direction is up AND mid Supertrend direction is up; the fast Supertrend has a shorter ATR lookback and a smaller factor than the mid Supertrend.
Short: Fast Supertrend direction is down AND mid Supertrend direction is down; the fast Supertrend has a shorter ATR lookback and a smaller factor than the mid Supertrend.
Long: Fast and mid Supertrend directions are both up AND they were not both up on the preceding bar AND (market filter is disabled OR trend strength is greater than its threshold) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume ratio is greater than or equal to its threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is within either configured trading session in the Europe/Warsaw timezone).
Short: Fast and mid Supertrend directions are both down AND they were not both down on the preceding bar AND (market filter is disabled OR trend strength is greater than its threshold) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume ratio is greater than or equal to its threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is within either configured trading session in the Europe/Warsaw timezone).
Long: Low is greater than the high two bars earlier.
Short: High is less than the low two bars earlier.
