Calculation: Altered OBV = cumulative sum of volume signed +1 when close > previous close or when close < previous close and open < close, and -1 otherwise
Calculation: MACD of Altered OBV = shorter-lookback moving average of Altered OBV minus longer-lookback moving average; its signal line is a moving average of that MACD
Long: MACD of Altered OBV crosses above its signal line AND MACD of Altered OBV > its value 2 bars ago
Short: MACD of Altered OBV < its value 1 bar ago AND MACD of Altered OBV < its signal line AND MACD of close < its signal line AND low < its value 2 bars ago
