Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs in equal-length windows before and after it; confirm after the following window completes
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows in equal-length windows before and after it; confirm after the following window completes
Calculation: High volume = volume exceeds a moving average of volume multiplied by a threshold
Long: EMA of close is greater than its value on the preceding bar
Short: EMA of close is less than or equal to its value on the preceding bar
Long: A confirmed swing low forms
Short: A confirmed swing high forms
Long: Close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high
Short: Close crosses below the most recently confirmed swing low
Long: Volume is not high AND (the most recently confirmed swing extreme is a low OR there is no confirmed swing extreme and close is greater than or equal to open)
Short: Volume is not high AND (the most recently confirmed swing extreme is a high OR there is no confirmed swing extreme and close is less than open)
