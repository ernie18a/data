Calculation: Features = On chart bars, let ATR be Wilder-smoothed true range with a configurable length, floored at a positive price-scale parameter. Let fast and slow be close EMAs, with fast shorter than slow. Clamp trend = (fast − slow)/(trend-scale × ATR) and mean reversion = (fast − close)/(reversion-scale × ATR) to [−1, 1]. Let each one-bar log return be log(close/previous close) when both closes are positive, otherwise zero. Let horizon return be log(close/close N bars ago) when both closes are positive, otherwise zero. Momentum = clamp(horizon return/(standard deviation of one-bar log returns over the normalization length × sqrt(N)), −momentum bound, momentum bound)/momentum bound, with the denominator floored at a small positive parameter. Relative volume = volume/SMA(volume, normalization length); it is unavailable when that SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Flow = clamp(clamp((2 × close − high − low)/max(high − low, positive price-scale parameter), −1, 1) × min(relative volume, volume cap)/volume cap, −1, 1). Efficiency = clamp(abs(close − close E bars ago)/sum(abs(close − previous close), E bars), 0, 1), or zero if the sum is unavailable or nonpositive. Log volatility = clamp(log(ATR/SMA(ATR, volatility normalization length)), −volatility bound, volatility bound); it is unavailable when the ATR average is unavailable or nonpositive. The model is ready only when the slow EMA, return deviation, close E bars ago, and flow are available and average volume is positive; Bayesian modes additionally require log volatility.
Calculation: Directional score = Choose fixed, Bayesian, or adaptive Bayesian mode. Let logistic(x) = 1/(1 + exp(−clamp(x, −logit bound, logit bound))). In fixed mode the score is logistic(overall scale × (efficiency × (trend coefficient × trend + momentum coefficient × momentum) + (1 − efficiency) × reversion coefficient × mean reversion + flow coefficient × flow)). In Bayesian modes, maintain four state probabilities for bull, bear, range and stress, initialized equally. On each completed Bayesian-ready bar, multiply the previous probabilities by a configurable transition matrix to obtain state priors: bull and bear have equal persistence and symmetric transitions to each other, range and stress; range and stress have symmetric transitions to bull and bear. For each state, sum Student-t log kernels over trend, momentum, efficiency, flow and log volatility: each kernel is −log(scale) − (degrees of freedom + 1)/2 × log(1 + ((feature − center)/scale)^2/degrees of freedom). Use configurable state-specific centers and positive scales: bull and bear centers have opposite signs for trend, momentum and flow and equal centers for efficiency and log volatility; range and stress have zero directional centers, and the stress log-volatility center is divided by a sensitivity parameter. Multiply each prior by the exponential of its state log likelihood and normalize to obtain current probabilities; if normalization fails, normalize the priors instead. Define four expert logits as configurable positive scales times trend, momentum, mean reversion and flow respectively. For each expert, its regime gate is the probability-weighted sum of its configurable gate coefficients across the four states; bull and bear use equal coefficients, trend and momentum receive greater weight in those states, mean reversion receives greater weight in range, and momentum and flow receive greater weight in stress. In adaptive Bayesian mode only, sample on completed Bayesian-ready bars whose epoch bar number modulo the outcome horizon is zero, where epoch bar number is floor(bar opening timestamp/chart-bar duration). At each sample, compare close with close one outcome horizon ago and assign outcome 1 if greater, otherwise 0. For each expert, compute binary log loss of logistic(expert logit from one outcome horizon ago), clipping that probability away from zero and one; retain the most recent configurable number of losses. Once each expert has a configurable minimum number of losses, multiply its regime gate by max(weight floor, exp(−greediness × (its mean loss − minimum mean loss among experts))); otherwise leave gates unchanged. Normalize the resulting four gates to sum to one. The Bayesian score is logistic((1 − stress discount × stress probability) × (directional coefficient × (bull probability − bear probability) + sum(normalized expert gate × expert logit))). The score is unavailable until the selected mode is ready.
Long: On a completed chart bar, directional score > signal threshold AND previous bar's directional score <= signal threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, 1 − directional score > signal threshold AND 1 − previous bar's directional score <= signal threshold.
