Calculation: Pivot high (pivot low) = bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) within a window of bars on both sides; it is confirmed only after the right-side bars have completed. A longer right-side window gives a slower, more reliable pivot and a shorter right-side window gives a faster, more sensitive pivot.
Calculation: Triangle = most recent pivot highs H1,H2,H3 and pivot lows L1,L2,L3, in chronological order, where H1,H2,L1,L2 use the longer right-side window and H3,L3 use the shorter one. Requires H1>H2>H3 and L1<L2<L3, and the line through H1,H3 has a smaller slope than the line through L1,L3 (converging, so the lines intersect). H3 and L3 must be newer than the previous triangle's H3 and L3. A triangle stays active until it produces an entry or its trap times out. Several triangles can be active at once and are evaluated independently.
Calculation: Real-breakout confirmation (up) = volume > multiplier * SMA(volume) AND RSI(close) > threshold. Real-breakout confirmation (down) = volume > multiplier * SMA(volume) AND RSI(close) < threshold (multiplier > 1). All on chart-timeframe bars, current bar close.
Long: active unbroken triangle AND close > H3 AND real-breakout confirmation (up).
Short: active unbroken triangle AND close < L3 AND real-breakout confirmation (down).
Long: active triangle had an earlier bar (the arm bar) with close < L3 and no real-breakout confirmation (down) on that bar, AND on a later bar, within the timeout bars after the arm bar, close > L3 and then fast EMA(close) crosses over slow EMA(close) on a still later bar, also within the timeout (fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA). Without the EMA confirmation: Long on the first later close > L3 within the timeout.
Short: active triangle had an earlier bar (the arm bar) with close > H3 and no real-breakout confirmation (up) on that bar, AND on a later bar, within the timeout bars after the arm bar, close < H3 and then fast EMA(close) crosses under slow EMA(close) on a still later bar, also within the timeout. Without the EMA confirmation: Short on the first later close < H3 within the timeout.
