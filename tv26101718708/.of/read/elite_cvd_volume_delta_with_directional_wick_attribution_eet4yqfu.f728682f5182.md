Calculation: Safe volume = volume if volume is nonnegative and available; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Buy volume = select one model: close-location, directional-wick, or their arithmetic mean. Close-location buy volume is safe volume multiplied by clamp((close - low) / (high - low), 0, 1), or half of safe volume when high <= low. For directional-wick buy volume, let body = abs(close - open), upper wick = max(high - max(open, close), 0), and lower wick = max(min(open, close) - low, 0). Candle direction is the sign of close - open, or, on a doji, the sign of close - previous close; on the first bar a doji has no direction. For an up candle, buy volume is safe volume multiplied by (body + lower wick * wick-attribution fraction + upper wick * (1 - wick-attribution fraction)) / (high - low). For a down candle, sell volume is safe volume multiplied by (body + upper wick * wick-attribution fraction + lower wick * (1 - wick-attribution fraction)) / (high - low), and buy volume is safe volume minus sell volume. If high <= low or candle direction is zero, buy volume is half of safe volume. Sell volume is safe volume minus buy volume.
Calculation: Relative volume = current safe volume divided by the previous bar's EMA, SMA, or RMA of safe volume; it is unavailable if current volume is invalid or the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bar delta = buy volume minus sell volume, optionally multiplied by the square root of relative volume clamped between a lower bound and an upper cap; use relative volume of 1 when it is unavailable. Set bar delta to zero outside the configured custom session if custom-session-only accumulation is selected.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = select a running sum of bar delta, a sum reset at each configured session start, a sum reset after each fixed block of bars, or a rolling sum over a fixed number of bars. The first bar starts a new accumulation epoch; session and block resets start new epochs, while rolling and running modes retain one epoch. Session boundaries are determined from datetime using the configured session calendar and timezone.
Calculation: Cumulative delta signal = EMA, SMA, or WMA of cumulative delta. Reset the EMA to cumulative delta at each accumulation-epoch start. For session and block modes, withhold SMA and WMA until their entire window is within the current epoch.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low with configured numbers of left and right bars; emit it only after its right bars have closed. For a high pivot, its high is greater than or equal to every left-bar high and greater than every right-bar high; reverse the inequalities for a low pivot. Compare each eligible pivot with the immediately preceding eligible pivot of the same type, even if that preceding pivot did not form a divergence. A pivot is eligible only if its cumulative delta is available and, when custom-session-only accumulation is selected, its bar is inside the custom session.
Calculation: Delta scale = EMA of absolute bar delta, evaluated at the pivot bar and floored at a small positive constant; use that constant when the EMA is unavailable.
Long: Buy volume >= sell volume.
Short: Buy volume < sell volume.
Long: Bar delta >= 0.
Short: Bar delta < 0.
Long: Cumulative delta >= 0.
Short: Cumulative delta < 0.
Long: Cumulative delta >= cumulative delta signal.
Short: Cumulative delta < cumulative delta signal.
Long: Cumulative delta crosses above zero OR crosses above cumulative delta signal.
Short: Cumulative delta crosses below zero OR crosses below cumulative delta signal.
Long: Bar delta > 0 AND relative volume >= spike threshold AND the current bar is included in accumulation.
Short: Bar delta < 0 AND relative volume >= spike threshold AND the current bar is included in accumulation.
Long: A price low pivot is confirmed AND its low is lower than the preceding eligible low pivot's low AND its cumulative delta is higher than that pivot's cumulative delta AND the cumulative delta increase is at least a configured multiple of delta scale at the new pivot AND both pivots are in the same accumulation epoch AND their bar distance is within the configured maximum.
Short: A price high pivot is confirmed AND its high is higher than the preceding eligible high pivot's high AND its cumulative delta is lower than that pivot's cumulative delta AND the cumulative delta decrease is at least a configured multiple of delta scale at the new pivot AND both pivots are in the same accumulation epoch AND their bar distance is within the configured maximum.
