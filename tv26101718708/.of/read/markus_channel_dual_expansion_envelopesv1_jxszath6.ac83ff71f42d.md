Calculation: Center = blend a selected moving average of close with a liquidity-weighted mean of close using a configurable weight. Select the moving average manually from SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA and VWMA, or automatically using the lowest score: SMA((average − close)²) + penalty × SMA((average − previous average)²), with the same lookback as the average. Break ties in the order EMA, SMA, WMA, RMA, VWMA. In automatic mode, retain the previously selected type unless the best score is less than its score multiplied by one minus a hysteresis parameter; initialize with the best type.
Calculation: Liquidity-weighted mean = weighted mean over the center lookback of close + wick adjustment × (lower wick − upper wick), where upper wick = max(0, high − max(open, close)), lower wick = max(0, min(open, close) − low), and weight = clamp(volume ratio / max(true-range ratio, floor), lower bound, upper bound) raised to a configurable exponent. Volume ratio and true-range ratio divide the current value by its SMA over the center lookback, substituting 1 when the respective denominator is not positive. If the sum of weights is zero, use SMA(close) over the center lookback.
Calculation: Tension = EMA of signed path efficiency, where path efficiency = abs(close − close at the path lookback) / max(sum of absolute close changes over that lookback, a small positive price increment), and its sign is positive when close > center and negative otherwise.
Long: close > center OR tension > positive tension threshold
Short: close <= center OR tension < negative tension threshold
