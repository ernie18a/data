Calculation: Volume-at-price profile = For each completed chart bar, cap its range at the body extremes plus or minus an ATR multiple. When enabled and more than one completed lower-timeframe bar is available within it, clamp each lower-timeframe close to that range, cap its volume at a multiple of the median lower-timeframe volume, and sum volumes into equal-width slots at their midpoint prices. If chart-bar volume is unavailable or nonpositive, use one count per lower-timeframe bar. Otherwise, distribute chart-bar volume across the slots in proportion to a higher weight for slot midpoints inside the candle body than outside it; use one count if chart-bar volume is unavailable or nonpositive. If the capped range has zero width, place that volume at the close. Over a rolling chart-bar window, divide the range of occupied slot prices into equal-width rows and sum the slot volumes into their rows. Optionally weight each bar by (1 - age in bars / window length) raised to a decay exponent. Smooth each row as a weighted sum of itself and its immediate neighbors, treating missing neighbors as zero.
Calculation: Value-area edges = Find the maximum smoothed-profile row, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Retain the row containing the previous bar's control price if it is within the grid and its volume is at least a specified fraction of the current maximum; otherwise use the maximum row. The control price is the selected row midpoint. Starting at that row, extend the cluster separately in each direction while the average of the next two rows, or the remaining single row at the grid boundary, is at least a specified fraction of the maximum row volume. Starting again at the control row, grow the value area one row at a time toward the side with the larger sum of the next two cluster rows, choosing the upper side on a tie, until it contains the specified share of cluster volume. Repeat with a lower and a higher share to form hysteresis bands. Retain each previous raw edge if its corresponding row is within its band and on the proper side of the control row; otherwise use the edge from the central share. Convert the lower row to its bottom price and the upper row to its top price. Apply a rolling median to each raw edge. Starting from the first available median, move each limited edge toward its current median by the smaller of the remaining distance and the larger of an ATR multiple or a fraction of the remaining distance. Apply an EMA to each limited edge to obtain the lower and upper value-area edges. Use the chart bar's high-low range when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Confirmed value-area state = Classify each completed chart bar as above when close is greater than the upper edge, below when close is less than the lower edge, and inside otherwise. Initialize the confirmed state as inside. Change it after a configurable number of consecutive bars in the same different classification, including when the change is not accompanied by a directional event. On a state change, select the upper edge for a move to above or from above to inside, and the lower edge otherwise. Set direction to +1 for a move to above or from below to inside, and -1 for a move to below or from above to inside. A price-driven change requires (close - close at the confirmation length bars ago) times direction > 0 AND that value >= (selected edge at the confirmation length bars ago - current selected edge) times direction.
Calculation: Confirmed control side = Hold the control price until the new control price differs from it by at least the current profile row height; initialize it to the first available control price and reset it when the control price is unavailable. Classify close as above or below this held price; equality retains the confirmed side. Initialize the confirmed side as neutral and set it immediately on the first non-neutral classification. Thereafter change it when a configurable number of consecutive classifications differ from the confirmed side; reset the count whenever classification equals the confirmed side.
Long: Confirmed value-area state changes to above AND the change is price-driven.
Long: Confirmed value-area state changes from below to inside AND the change is price-driven.
Long: Confirmed control side is above.
Short: Confirmed value-area state changes to below AND the change is price-driven.
Short: Confirmed value-area state changes from above to inside AND the change is price-driven.
Short: Confirmed control side is below.
