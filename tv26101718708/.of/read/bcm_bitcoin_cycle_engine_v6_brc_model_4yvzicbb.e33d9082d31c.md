Calculation: Cycle coordinate = linearly interpolate between parameterized timestamp and cycle-coordinate anchors; extrapolate before the first anchor using the first segment and after the last anchor using the interval from the penultimate whole cycle to the final anchor. Cycle phase is the fractional part of the coordinate.
Calculation: Rainbow and wave levels = let x be the cycle coordinate floored at a positive minimum, decay = decay base^(x + offset), power = 10^(intercept + slope × log10(x)), delay = a baseline plus a decaying cosine adjustment below a cycle-coordinate cutoff and the baseline otherwise, and sine = sin(2π × (x − delay)). Rainbow floor and upper bands are power × 10^(decay × their respective ordered band coefficients). Wave floor is power × 10^(decay × max(lower bound, sine − floor offset)); wave middle, upper and top are power × 10^(decay × (sine + their respective ordered offsets)).
Calculation: Accumulation score = clamp the weighted sum of rainbow-floor proximity, wave-floor proximity, bottom-cycle-phase, chart-timeframe RSI, higher-timeframe RSI, momentum recovery and volume confirmation scores. Each proximity and RSI score is a parameterized descending step function of its input; the bottom-cycle-phase score uses nested intervals around the bottom phase, with higher scores for narrower intervals. If the two floors differ by less than a proximity threshold, use only the rainbow-floor proximity score; otherwise use their weighted average. Momentum recovery uses ordered cases: MACD above its signal and RSI below a threshold; MACD above its signal; MACD histogram rising; otherwise. Volume confirmation is an ascending step function of volume divided by its moving average; use a neutral ratio when the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Use the last completed higher-timeframe RSI bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Distribution score = clamp the weighted sum of rainbow upper-band position, wave position, top-cycle-phase, chart-timeframe RSI, higher-timeframe RSI and volatility scores. Upper-band and wave scores are ascending step functions of close relative to their ordered levels; RSI scores are ascending step functions of RSI; the top-cycle-phase score uses nested intervals around the top phase, with higher scores for narrower intervals; volatility is an ascending step function of ATR divided by close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe RSI bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.

Long: accumulation score crosses above an accumulation-alert threshold
