Calculation: Chart trend state = In VWMA Trend mode, compare close with the chart-timeframe VWMA; in Anchored VWAP mode, compare close with the selected session-, week-, or month-anchored VWAP; in Order-Flow Momentum mode, compare candle body size with its same-length average and use candle direction. When the selected trend condition is met, assign a positive or negative state, upgraded in magnitude when delta confirms; require the configured ATR-distance threshold when enabled. Otherwise retain the prior state. Apply updates on bar close when close confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: Delta ratio = (estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume) / total estimated volume. Estimate volume from lower-timeframe bars, summing volume of up bars as buy and down bars as sell, splitting equal-close/open bars evenly; when the selected source is the candle proxy or lower-timeframe data is unavailable, estimate delta as volume × (2×close − high − low)/(high − low), with zero when high equals low. Lower-timeframe bars are aggregated from the chart bars and are lower than the chart timeframe; use the available bars within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (current volume − moving average of volume) / standard deviation of volume, using the configured lookback.
Calculation: Swing proximity = the distance from close to the rolling highest high or lowest low is no greater than the configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute(close − open)/(high − low), or zero when high equals low. Small and large body thresholds are respectively the configured lower and upper rolling percentiles, or the configured fixed thresholds.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = compare higher-timeframe close with its VWMA and higher-timeframe candle-proxy delta ratio with the bullish and bearish thresholds. The higher timeframe is above the chart timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe filter passes when disabled or not strict, or when strict and the signal direction is not opposed by the higher-timeframe state.
Calculation: Absorption confluence score = one point each for agreeing higher-timeframe state (when enabled), agreeing chart trend state, volume z-score above its threshold, delta ratio agreeing with signal direction, and swing proximity on the signal side.
Long: chart trend state > 0 OR trend state changes from nonpositive to positive OR near swing low AND delta ratio > the adaptive absolute-delta threshold AND volume z-score > 1 AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND absorption confluence score >= configured minimum OR small body AND volume z-score > 1.5 AND near swing low AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND absorption confluence score >= configured minimum OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND up candle AND large body AND close in the upper quarter of its range AND chart trend state > 0 AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND up candle AND large body AND near swing high AND chart trend state <= 0 AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND small body AND near swing low AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR up candle AND volume > moving average volume AND volume z-score < spike threshold.
Short: chart trend state < 0 OR trend state changes from nonnegative to negative OR near swing high AND delta ratio < negative adaptive absolute-delta threshold AND volume z-score > 1 AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND absorption confluence score >= configured minimum OR small body AND volume z-score > 1.5 AND near swing high AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND absorption confluence score >= configured minimum OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND down candle AND large body AND close in the lower quarter of its range AND chart trend state < 0 AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND up candle AND large body AND near swing high AND chart trend state <= 0 AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR volume z-score >= spike threshold AND small body AND near swing high AND higher-timeframe filter passes OR down candle AND volume > moving average volume AND volume z-score < spike threshold.
