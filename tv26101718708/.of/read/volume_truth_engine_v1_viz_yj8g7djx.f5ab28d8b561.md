Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - SMA(volume)) / standard deviation(volume), or 0 when the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low.
Calculation: Delta ratio = (buy volume - sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume), or 0 when their sum is 0. For each lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, assign its volume to buy volume if close > open, to sell volume if close < open, and half to each if close = open. Use all available lower-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming. Alternatively, or when no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar delta = volume * (2 * close - high - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low, with buy volume = (volume + delta) / 2 and sell volume = (volume - delta) / 2.
Calculation: Trend state = initially 0; set to 1 when close > the selected trend line and either the ATR-distance filter is disabled or close - trend line > an ATR multiple; set to -1 when close < the selected trend line and either the filter is disabled or trend line - close > the ATR multiple; otherwise retain the previous state. The trend line is either VWMA of close or anchored VWAP of typical price, reset at the selected session, week, or month boundary. Alternatively, in momentum mode, set state to 1 when close > open and abs(close - open) > SMA(abs(close - open)); set it to -1 when close < open and abs(close - open) > that SMA; otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = sign of close minus VWMA of close on the higher timeframe, with 0 for equality or unavailable data. Use the higher-timeframe result mapped with lookahead off and then shifted by one chart bar: historically, a completed higher-timeframe result first appears on its final constituent chart bar and is carried forward; take the previous chart bar's mapped result. Use 0 when higher-timeframe confluence is disabled.
Calculation: Near low = close - lowest low over the swing lookback <= an ATR multiple. Near high = highest high over the same swing lookback - close <= the same ATR multiple. Both lookbacks include the current bar.
Calculation: Small-body threshold = a lower rolling percentile of body ratio or a fixed threshold. Large-body threshold = a higher rolling percentile of body ratio over the same lookback or a separate fixed threshold. Delta threshold = the greater of a minimum and either a rolling percentile of abs(delta ratio) over the same adaptive lookback or a fixed threshold.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = the sum of one point each for higher-timeframe confluence enabled and higher-timeframe state in the signal direction; trend state in the signal direction; volume z-score above its elevated-volume threshold; delta ratio beyond its directional threshold in the signal direction; and near low for a long or near high for a short.
Long: Trend state > 0.
Short: Trend state < 0.
Long: Near low AND delta ratio > delta threshold AND volume z-score > elevated-volume threshold AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state >= 0).
Short: Near high AND delta ratio < -delta threshold AND volume z-score > elevated-volume threshold AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state <= 0).
Long: Near low AND body ratio < small-body threshold AND volume z-score > effort threshold AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state >= 0).
Short: Near high AND body ratio < small-body threshold AND volume z-score > effort threshold AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state <= 0).
Long: Volume z-score >= spike threshold AND close < open AND body ratio > large-body threshold AND near low AND trend state >= 0 AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state >= 0).
Short: Volume z-score >= spike threshold AND close > open AND body ratio > large-body threshold AND near high AND trend state <= 0 AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state <= 0).
Long: Volume z-score >= spike threshold AND body ratio < small-body threshold AND near low AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state >= 0).
Short: Volume z-score >= spike threshold AND body ratio < small-body threshold AND near high AND (strict higher-timeframe filtering disabled OR higher-timeframe state <= 0).
Long: Close > open AND volume > SMA(volume) AND volume z-score < spike threshold.
Short: Close < open AND volume > SMA(volume) AND volume z-score < spike threshold.
Long: Higher-timeframe confluence enabled AND soft filtering selected AND higher-timeframe state < 0 AND near low AND delta ratio > delta threshold AND volume z-score > elevated-volume threshold.
Short: Higher-timeframe confluence enabled AND soft filtering selected AND higher-timeframe state > 0 AND near high AND delta ratio < -delta threshold AND volume z-score > elevated-volume threshold.
