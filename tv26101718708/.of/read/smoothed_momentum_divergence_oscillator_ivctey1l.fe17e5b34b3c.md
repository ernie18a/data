Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = 100 × tanh((double-smoothed price change ÷ double-smoothed absolute price change) ÷ scale), where price change and its absolute value are each EMA-smoothed first with the longer lookback and then with the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed price low pivot, price is lower than at the previous low pivot while the oscillator is higher (regular), or price is higher while the oscillator is lower (hidden); the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum lookback.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a confirmed price high pivot, price is higher than at the previous high pivot while the oscillator is lower (regular), or price is lower while the oscillator is higher (hidden); the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish/bearish state = same-direction regular divergence on the higher timeframe, using completed higher-timeframe bars; the state persists until an opposite divergence.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the longer lookback ÷ sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; trending when the ratio is above the threshold, ranging otherwise.
