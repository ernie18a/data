Calculation: Trend direction = sign(price source − Parabolic SAR) when ATR is available and nonzero; otherwise zero. Price source is a selectable series derived from the backtest fields.
Calculation: Extension magnitude = clamp((high source − Parabolic SAR) / ATR, 0, maximum magnitude) for positive trend direction; clamp((Parabolic SAR − low source) / ATR, 0, maximum magnitude) for negative trend direction; otherwise zero. High and low sources are selectable series derived from the backtest fields. ATR is true range smoothed by a selectable SMA, EMA, or Wilder RMA.
Calculation: Extension oscillator = trend direction multiplied by either extension magnitude, its rolling Z-score, or its robust score. The Z-score is (extension magnitude − rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation, or zero when the standard deviation is unavailable or zero. The robust score is 0.6745 × (extension magnitude − rolling median) / rolling median of the series abs(extension magnitude − its contemporaneous rolling median), or zero when that median deviation is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = sign(price source − Parabolic SAR) on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use zero when unavailable.
Long: Trend direction > 0.
Short: Trend direction < 0.
Long: Higher-timeframe direction > 0.
Short: Higher-timeframe direction < 0.

Short: Extension oscillator crosses below the negative signal threshold AND the chart bar index is at least the statistical lookback in statistical modes or the ATR period in raw mode AND (the ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= its threshold) AND (the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe direction <= 0).
