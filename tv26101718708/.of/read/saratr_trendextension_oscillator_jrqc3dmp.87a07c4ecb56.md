Calculation: SAR/ATR extension oscillator = sign(close − Parabolic SAR) × capped distance from SAR to high (uptrend) or low (downtrend), divided by ATR; use the selected mode: raw value, rolling Z-score of the unsigned extension, or rolling robust Z-score (0.6745 × deviation from rolling median ÷ rolling median absolute deviation), then apply the SAR direction sign
Calculation: Higher-timeframe SAR direction = sign(close − Parabolic SAR) on the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Long: Oscillator crosses above the selected signal level AND ADX is at or above its threshold when the regime filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe SAR direction is nonnegative when that filter is enabled AND the oscillator’s rolling window is warmed up
Short: Oscillator crosses below the negative selected signal level AND ADX is at or above its threshold when the regime filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe SAR direction is nonpositive when that filter is enabled AND the oscillator’s rolling window is warmed up
Long: A confirmed pivot low has a higher oscillator low and lower price low than the preceding confirmed pivot low, within the maximum pivot spacing (regular bullish divergence); OR it has a lower oscillator low and higher price low (hidden bullish divergence)
Short: A confirmed pivot high has a lower oscillator high and higher price high than the preceding confirmed pivot high, within the maximum pivot spacing (regular bearish divergence); OR it has a higher oscillator high and lower price high (hidden bearish divergence)
