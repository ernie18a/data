Calculation: For a long decision let s = 1; for a short decision let s = -1. At each bar i, O[i] = s × open[i], C[i] = s × close[i], H[i] = high[i] when s = 1 and -low[i] when s = -1, and L[i] = low[i] when s = 1 and -high[i] when s = -1. Index 0 is the current closed bar and positive indices are earlier bars. B[i] = |C[i] - O[i]| and R[i] = H[i] - L[i].
Calculation: Base high and base low are the highest H and lowest L over the configured number of bars immediately before the current bar. Flag high and flag low are the corresponding extremes over the configured flag bars. The pole is the largest-body bar in the configured search window immediately preceding those flag bars; resolve equal bodies in favor of the most recent bar.
Calculation: Common filter = (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold OR (ADX-rising override enabled AND ADX > ADX at its configured lookback) OR (volatility-breakout override enabled AND R[0] >= current ATR × its range multiplier)) AND (ATR expansion filter disabled OR current ATR >= its simple moving average) AND (cooldown disabled OR the number of completed bars strictly between the current bar and the last accepted signal of either direction >= the cooldown). Calculation: A tracked trade enters at the signal bar's close; if both directions signal, track the long. At entry, select the stop using the first matching raw pattern in this order: compression uses base low; flag uses flag low; three-candle uses min(L[2], L[1], L[0]); otherwise use min(L[1], L[0]). These values use the entry direction's transformed prices. Track the trade only if the transformed stop < transformed entry. The transformed target = transformed entry + (transformed entry - transformed stop) × reward-to-risk multiplier. Keep each trade's entry-bar stop and target fixed.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= stop OR low <= target).
