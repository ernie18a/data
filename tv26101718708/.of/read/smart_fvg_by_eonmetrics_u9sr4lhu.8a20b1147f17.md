Calculation: A chart-timeframe bullish gap has top = current low and bottom = high two bars ago; a bearish gap has top = low two bars ago and bottom = current high. Its creation bar is the current bar. A higher-timeframe gap uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it; its creation bar is the first chart bar of the new higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: An active gap is retained until, on a chart bar after its first eligible retest bar, the selected price reaches its selected mitigation level. The selected price is either the chart low for a bullish gap and chart high for a bearish gap, or the chart close for both. The selected level is either the gap midpoint or the bottom of a bullish gap and top of a bearish gap. Mitigation occurs when the selected price is <= the level for a bullish gap or >= the level for a bearish gap. Evaluate retests before removing gaps mitigated on the same bar. A chart-timeframe gap's first eligible retest bar is the bar after creation; a higher-timeframe gap's is two chart bars after creation.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low > high two bars ago AND low minus high two bars ago >= a minimum-size multiplier times the previous bar's ATR, using zero if ATR is unavailable.
Short: On a completed chart bar, high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago minus high >= a minimum-size multiplier times the previous bar's ATR, using zero if ATR is unavailable.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low > high two bars ago AND low minus high two bars ago >= a minimum-size multiplier times the previous bar's ATR AND the gap size >= a strong-gap multiplier times the previous bar's ATR AND the previous bar's volume SMA > 0 AND the previous bar's volume >= a volume multiplier times that volume SMA, using zero for unavailable ATR or volume SMA.
Short: On a completed chart bar, high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago minus high >= a minimum-size multiplier times the previous bar's ATR AND the gap size >= a strong-gap multiplier times the previous bar's ATR AND the previous bar's volume SMA > 0 AND the previous bar's volume >= a volume multiplier times that volume SMA, using zero for unavailable ATR or volume SMA.
Long: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low > the high two higher-timeframe bars before it.
Short: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high < the low two higher-timeframe bars before it.
Long: An active bullish gap has reached its first eligible retest bar AND no earlier eligible chart bar has low <= its top AND current low <= its top.
Short: An active bearish gap has reached its first eligible retest bar AND no earlier eligible chart bar has high >= its bottom AND current high >= its bottom.
