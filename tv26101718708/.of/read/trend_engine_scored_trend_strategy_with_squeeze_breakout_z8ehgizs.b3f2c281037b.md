Calculation: Tracker = initialize direction bullish and tracker to low minus a multiplier times WMA of true range. On each later bar, if bullish, set tracker to max(previous tracker, low minus that distance), then if close < tracker set direction bearish and tracker to high plus that distance. If bearish, set tracker to min(previous tracker, high plus that distance), then if close > tracker set direction bullish and tracker to low minus that distance. A flip is a change in direction from the preceding bar.
Calculation: Equilibrium = Hull moving average of the zero-offset linear regression of close.
Calculation: Trend score = 100 times the sum of enabled votes divided by max(1, number of enabled votes). The votes are tracker direction (+1 bullish, -1 bearish), close > equilibrium, equilibrium > its previous value, RSI > its midpoint, RSI > its SMA, and +DI > -DI. Each comparison vote is +1 when true and -1 otherwise.
Calculation: Zone state = initially scanning. While scanning, if highest high minus lowest low over a lookback is <= an ATR multiple for every bar in a consecutive-bar window, fix the zone boundaries at the current lookback high and low and enter active-zone state; its start is the first bar of that lookback. On subsequent active-zone bars, close > upper boundary records an upward breakout, and close < lower boundary records a downward breakout; either enters breakout-tracking state. If neither occurs and bars since zone start > expiry, return to scanning. In breakout-tracking state, initialize the extreme to the breakout bar's high for an upward breakout or low for a downward breakout and the trail to extreme minus or plus an ATR multiple respectively. On subsequent bars update the extreme to the highest high or lowest low since breakout and ratchet the trail upward or downward respectively using the current ATR. Return to scanning when close < the upward trail or close > the downward trail. Retain the most recent breakout direction and bar.
Calculation: Entry signal = a direction's tracker flip, or, when delayed entry is enabled, that tracker direction within a configured number of bars after its start; require no previous entry signal in the same trend. Reset this restriction on each flip. Before any flip, the initial bullish trend starts at the first bar. Also require the enabled directional filters: trend score >= the positive threshold for bullish or <= its negative for bearish; ADX >= its minimum; RSI >= the long minimum or <= the short maximum; close > equilibrium for bullish or < equilibrium for bearish; volume unavailable or zero or > a multiple of its SMA; and at least min(configured minimum, number of enabled timeframes) enabled timeframes with matching tracker direction. Each other timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe and uses either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar according to the selected mode. Also require the enabled cooldown, measured from the last entry signal of either direction, to have elapsed; the selected zone filter to be off, or zone state to be other than active, or the latest breakout to match direction and be within the configured window; datetime to be within the enabled date window; and the selected trade direction to allow that direction. Record the signal even if the current position prevents an order.
Calculation: Trade levels = on an entry signal that opens or reverses a position, use signal-bar close as the planned entry and initialize the stop to the tracker, planned entry minus direction times an ATR multiple, planned entry adjusted by a percentage against the trade, or the lookback low minus an ATR buffer for long and lookback high plus an ATR buffer for short; the stop may instead be disabled. If the selected stop is on or beyond the wrong side of planned entry, replace it with the ATR stop. Initial risk is the absolute distance from planned entry to stop, or the ATR stop distance if the stop is disabled. Set each enabled target at planned entry plus direction times either a risk multiple, an ATR multiple, or a percentage of planned entry. Up to three targets may be enabled; earlier targets close their configured portions and the last closes the remainder. Orders are submitted on the signal bar and normally fill no earlier than the next bar; stop and target orders use their prices, subject to intrabar fill ordering.
Calculation: Managed stop = start with the initial stop. On each bar after entry, mark the first target reached when high >= its level for long or low <= its level for short, even if the target order has not filled; if enabled, ratchet the stop to at least planned entry for long or at most planned entry for short. Track the highest high or lowest low from the signal bar onward. If trailing is enabled immediately or after the first target is marked reached, ratchet the stop in the profitable direction to the tracker when its direction matches the position, or to the tracked extreme minus direction times the current ATR multiple. A missing stop is replaced by the first available breakeven or trailing level. Updated orders apply after the bar on which they are calculated.
Long: tracker direction = bullish.
Short: tracker direction = bearish.
Long: trend score > 0 OR trend score >= a configured bullish classification threshold.
Short: trend score < 0 OR trend score <= a configured negative bearish classification threshold.
Long: RSI > its SMA OR RSI <= the oversold threshold.
Short: RSI <= its SMA OR RSI >= the overbought threshold.
Long: previous RSI < RSI two bars ago AND current RSI > previous RSI AND previous RSI <= the oversold threshold.
Short: previous RSI > RSI two bars ago AND current RSI < previous RSI AND previous RSI >= the overbought threshold.
Long: close > the active zone's upper boundary.
Short: close < the active zone's lower boundary.
Long: bullish entry signal.
Short: bearish entry signal.
Exit: long, low <= the active managed stop OR high >= an enabled target; target exits close the configured portion.
Exit: short, high >= the active managed stop OR low <= an enabled target; target exits close the configured portion.
Exit: long, bearish entry signal AND reversal disabled OR bearish tracker flip AND flip exit enabled OR bars since the entry fill bar >= the enabled maximum OR datetime > the enabled backtest end.
Exit: short, bullish entry signal AND reversal disabled OR bullish tracker flip AND flip exit enabled OR bars since the entry fill bar >= the enabled maximum OR datetime > the enabled backtest end.
