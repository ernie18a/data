Calculation: Local baselines = EMAs of close with short, medium, and long lengths; their slopes are the current EMA minus its value a specified number of chart bars earlier. Higher-timeframe baselines are the same EMAs on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trend score = sum of: positive or negative weight for respectively bullish or bearish ordering of the local baselines; the same weight for the corresponding ordering of the higher-timeframe baselines; a smaller positive or negative weight for the sign of each local short and medium EMA slope; the same smaller weight for the sign of DI+ minus DI− when ADX exceeds its gate; and the same smaller positive or negative weight when volatility is expanding without a squeeze, according to whether close is above the long local EMA. A squeeze means the EMA-centered standard-deviation bands are inside the EMA-centered bands offset by an EMA of true range; expansion means normalized standard-deviation-band width exceeds a multiple of its moving average.
Calculation: Bull regime = trend score at least its positive regime threshold AND ADX above a fraction of its gate. Bear regime = trend score at most the negative regime threshold AND ADX above the same fraction of its gate. Soft bull regime = trend score > 0 AND short local EMA > medium local EMA > long local EMA AND short higher-timeframe EMA >= medium higher-timeframe EMA. Soft bear regime reverses those comparisons.
Calculation: Structure high and low = most recently confirmed symmetric pivot high and low, respectively. A low sweep occurs when low is below the last completed daily or, if enabled, weekly low by more than an ATR buffer AND close is above that level; a high sweep reverses these comparisons. Daily and weekly bars are higher or lower than the chart timeframe according to the chart timeframe and use the last completed bar. A sweep remains active through a specified number of chart bars after its most recent occurrence. Bullish liquidity stack = active low sweep AND close > current structure high AND previous close <= current structure high AND, if displacement is required, close > open AND candle body exceeds a multiple of its moving average. Bearish liquidity stack reverses the price comparisons.
Calculation: Local retracement anchors = most recently confirmed symmetric pivot high and low; initialize each from the highest high or lowest low over a lookback until its first pivot is confirmed, and retain each pivot until replaced. The local swing is down when the low anchor occurred after the high anchor. Higher-timeframe anchors are the highest high and lowest low over the same lookback ending at the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the higher-timeframe swing is down when the low is more recent than the high. For either swing, a retracement level at a ratio is low + ratio × (high − low) for a down swing, otherwise high − ratio × (high − low). Confluence count is the number of corresponding local and higher-timeframe retracement levels, from a specified set of ratios, whose absolute difference is no more than a specified percentage of close. Bull retracement = local swing is up AND close is between the two specified retracement-band levels; bear retracement = local swing is down AND close is between those levels. Swing agreement = local and higher-timeframe swing directions match, or the agreement filter is disabled.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average, or a neutral fallback when that average is zero. Delta z-score = z-score of volume × (close − open), with zero when its standard deviation is zero. Pressure = weighted sum of the z-score of cumulative flow, centered and scaled RSI, and delta z-score. Flow per bar = volume × max(relative volume, neutral baseline) × (2 × close − low − high) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low; use zero for the cumulative-flow z-score when its standard deviation is zero. Bull pressure = pressure > 0 AND pressure > previous pressure; bear pressure = pressure < 0 AND pressure < previous pressure.
Calculation: Regression channel = fit close against chronological bar index for each candidate lookback from a minimum to a longer maximum in fixed increments; select the fit with the highest R² that meets a minimum R². Extrapolate its fitted line one bar beyond the latest observation for the center; bands are center plus or minus a multiple of the residual standard error, calculated with length minus two degrees of freedom. Bull channel = close crosses above the lower band AND selected slope > 0; bear channel = close crosses below the upper band AND selected slope < 0.
Calculation: Bull participation = volume divided by the highest volume over the retracement lookback exceeds an intensity threshold AND delta z-score > 0 AND relative volume >= its gate. Bear participation reverses the delta comparison. Use zero intensity when the highest volume is zero.
Calculation: Directional entry score = sum of a larger weight for the matching regime and for the matching liquidity stack; a smaller weight for each of matching retracement or its filter being disabled, swing agreement, matching pressure, matching regression channel, matching participation, and relative volume meeting its gate; and a full or half smaller weight when confluence count reaches a higher or lower count threshold, respectively. The soft score gate is the greater of the main score gate minus an offset and a minimum gate.
Calculation: Session pass = true on non-intraday charts; otherwise the bar is in an enabled trading session, or all trading sessions are disabled, AND it is outside the closing session when that exclusion is enabled.
Calculation: Initial risk = on the first chart bar observed with a newly opened position, use close minus the long stop candidate for a long, or the short stop candidate minus close for a short; if unavailable, use ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier. The long candidate is the minimum of current low, last completed daily low, last completed weekly low, and latest structure low, minus ATR times the sum of the structure-buffer and stop multipliers; substitute current low for unavailable levels. The short candidate uses the corresponding maximum highs plus that ATR amount. Retain initial risk until flat.
Calculation: Long live stop = maximum of entry price minus initial risk, entry price when current close reaches the break-even trigger above entry, and close minus an ATR multiple. Short live stop = minimum of entry price plus initial risk, entry price when current close reaches the break-even trigger below entry, and close plus an ATR multiple. Recalculate each bar without retaining the previous stop.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND session pass AND ((long entry score >= main gate AND bull regime AND swing agreement) OR (long entry score >= soft gate AND soft bull regime AND bull pressure AND bull channel AND (confluence count >= lower count threshold OR bull participation))).
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND session pass AND ((short entry score >= main gate AND bear regime AND swing agreement) OR (short entry score >= soft gate AND soft bear regime AND bear pressure AND bear channel AND (confluence count >= lower count threshold OR bear participation))).
Long: Bull regime OR active low sweep OR bullish liquidity stack OR bull retracement OR pressure >= 0.
Short: Bear regime OR active high sweep OR bearish liquidity stack OR bear retracement OR pressure < 0.
Exit: Long, low <= previously submitted long live stop OR high >= entry price + initial risk × either profit-target multiplier; each of the two targets closes its assigned partial position, and the stop applies to both remaining portions.
Exit: Short, high >= previously submitted short live stop OR low <= entry price − initial risk × either profit-target multiplier; each of the two targets closes its assigned partial position, and the stop applies to both remaining portions.
Exit: Long, trend score <= 0 AND pressure < 0.
Exit: Short, trend score >= 0 AND pressure > 0.
Exit: Long, time exit enabled AND bars since the position was first observed open >= maximum holding period.
Exit: Short, time exit enabled AND bars since the position was first observed open >= maximum holding period.
