Calculation: Pivot sources = Select either high and low or max(open, close) and min(open, close) for pivot highs and lows, respectively. A pivot is confirmed after the same number of bars on each side; retain each confirmed pivot, including repeated values, for the optional structural gates.
Calculation: Trendline break = Run an independent state machine for each enabled short-, medium-, and long-lookback pivot tier. Initialize trend to neutral and the line inactive. A changed pivot high or low is a confirmed pivot whose value differs from the previous confirmed pivot of that type, provided the other pivot type did not change on the same bar. Process highs before lows. Extend an active line by its stored slope each bar; deactivate it when its age exceeds the line-age limit. On a changed pivot high, if trend is not up and the high exceeds the previous pivot high, is separated from it by more than the minimum spacing, and the previous pivot low is within the line-age limit, set trend up and start a horizontal line at that previous low. Otherwise, if trend is not up and a line is active, calculate the slope from its stored anchor to the new pivot high. If the pivot high is below the line's first price plus that slope and is above its current endpoint plus that slope or the stored slope is zero, compare the pivot-bar close with the existing line at the pivot bar: a close at or above the line deactivates it and produces an upward break; otherwise move the endpoint to the new pivot projected to the current bar. If the prior slope was zero, repeatedly find the largest positive close-minus-line deviation over the bounded history from the line start, move the line start and anchor to that bar's high, and recalculate the slope to the pivot until no positive deviation remains. Store the resulting slope. On bars without a changed pivot high, an active line with trend not up produces an upward break and becomes inactive when close exceeds its current endpoint. Always update the previous pivot high after processing a changed high. Apply the exact mirror for changed pivot lows and downward breaks: reverse all price comparisons and deviations, use lows instead of highs and highs instead of lows, and replace up with down. Update the previous pivot low after processing a changed low. The high and low branches share the same trend and line state and execute in that order.
Long: An enabled tier produces an upward trendline break AND, if the pivot-sequence gate is enabled, its latest confirmed pivot high and low are each above their respective preceding pivots AND, if the pivot-break gate is enabled, close is above its latest confirmed pivot high AND, if the volume gate is enabled, volume is at least its rolling percentile threshold and, if rising volume is required, volume is above the previous bar's volume.
Short: An enabled tier produces a downward trendline break AND, if the pivot-sequence gate is enabled, its latest confirmed pivot high and low are each below their respective preceding pivots AND, if the pivot-break gate is enabled, close is below its latest confirmed pivot low AND, if the volume gate is enabled, volume is at least its rolling percentile threshold and, if rising volume is required, volume is above the previous bar's volume.
