Calculation: Profile levels = On each completed chart bar, retain the most recent max(profile lookback, row count) bars. Set canvas high to max(rolling highest high over the profile lookback, its EMA) and canvas low to min(EMA of the rolling lowest low, max(rolling lowest low, minimum price increment)); both EMAs have length max(minimum smoothing length, round(sqrt(profile lookback))). Divide the log-price interval from canvas low to canvas high into equal rows. For each cached bar, add its volume, or a fallback volume if missing or nonpositive, to every row from its log low through its log high, with row indices truncated and clamped to the canvas. Multiply that volume by clamp(max(1 - age / max(1, cache capacity - 1), 1 - age / max(1, recency span)), minimum weight, 1), where age is zero for the newest bar. Find the first maximum-volume row and, when it has two neighbors and their combined volume is positive, replace its index with the volume-weighted mean index of the row and its neighbors. The raw volume point of control is the exponentiated log price at that index plus half a row. Compute the volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of row indices; round and clamp mean minus or plus one standard deviation to obtain the consensus-area lower and upper row indices, and likewise use two standard deviations for the boundary rows. Convert lower indices to their lower price edges and upper indices to their upper price edges. If profile volume is zero, use the canvas midpoint for all raw levels. Apply the same EMA smoothing length separately to each raw level.
Calculation: Consensus midpoint = sqrt(consensus-area high * consensus-area low).
Calculation: Anchor = selectable volume point of control, consensus midpoint, or sqrt(volume point of control * consensus midpoint).
Calculation: Normalized displacement from a reference = clamp((close - reference) / (boundary high - reference) when close >= reference, otherwise (close - reference) / (reference - boundary low), -displacement cap, displacement cap); undefined unless both boundary distances are positive.
Calculation: Consensus direction = mean of the normalized displacements from the volume point of control and the consensus midpoint.
Calculation: Absolute coherence = abs(2 * point-of-control displacement * consensus-midpoint displacement / (point-of-control displacement squared + consensus-midpoint displacement squared)); use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Consensus score = clamp(abs(consensus direction) * absolute coherence, 0, score cap).
Calculation: Synthetic delta = volume, with missing volume replaced by zero, * (close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment). Session delta is its cumulative sum reset at the start of each trading day. Delta bias = clamp(EMA(session delta, delta smoothing length) / max(EMA(abs(session delta), VWAP-deviation lookback), normalization floor), -1, 1).
Calculation: VWAP displacement = clamp((close - session VWAP of typical price) / max(rolling standard deviation of typical price minus session VWAP, minimum price increment), -VWAP displacement cap, VWAP displacement cap), using the VWAP-deviation lookback.
Calculation: Dynamic volume node = typical price of the most recent bar whose volume equals the highest volume over the node lookback.
Calculation: Institutional score = clamp(consensus score / consensus-score cap * consensus weight + absolute coherence * coherence weight + min(abs(VWAP displacement) / VWAP normalization scale, 1) * VWAP weight + abs(delta bias) * delta weight, 0, 1).
Long: Consensus direction > 0.
Short: Consensus direction < 0.
Long: Close crosses above anchor AND absolute coherence >= coherence floor AND consensus score >= signal threshold AND close > consensus-area low.
Short: Close crosses below anchor AND absolute coherence >= coherence floor AND consensus score >= signal threshold AND close < consensus-area high.
Long: Close crosses above anchor AND absolute coherence >= coherence floor AND consensus score >= signal threshold AND close > consensus-area low AND consensus direction > 0 AND close > session VWAP AND delta bias >= negative delta tolerance AND close >= dynamic volume node AND institutional score >= institutional threshold.
Short: Close crosses below anchor AND absolute coherence >= coherence floor AND consensus score >= signal threshold AND close < consensus-area high AND consensus direction < 0 AND close < session VWAP AND delta bias <= positive delta tolerance AND close <= dynamic volume node AND institutional score >= institutional threshold.
