Calculation: Dynamic Money Flow = In index mode, the Wilder moving average of money flow divided by the Wilder moving average of volume; in cumulative mode, the cumulative sum of money flow. Per bar, money flow is volume times the sum of (close-to-true-range-position × (1 − weight) + close-to-previous-close-direction × weight), where true range uses the current high and low and previous close, weight is the absolute close change divided by true range, close-to-true-range-position is (close − true-range low + close − true-range high) divided by true range, and close-to-previous-close-direction is +1 for a higher close, −1 for a lower close, and 0 for an unchanged close. In cumulative mode, also calculate shorter- and longer-lookback moving averages of Dynamic Money Flow.
Long: Index mode AND Dynamic Money Flow >= 0 OR Cumulative mode AND Dynamic Money Flow >= its shorter-lookback moving average
Short: Index mode AND Dynamic Money Flow < 0 OR Cumulative mode AND Dynamic Money Flow < its shorter-lookback moving average
