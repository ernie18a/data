Calculation: StochRSI %K = SMA of (RSI of open minus its lowest value over the stochastic lookback) divided by (its highest value minus its lowest value over the same lookback); undefined when that range is zero. %D = SMA of %K.
Long: The last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, has open > EMA of open using either the shorter or longer EMA lookback.
Short: The last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, has open <= EMA of open using either the shorter or longer EMA lookback.
Long: On the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, MACD histogram of open > 0 AND MACD line of open > 0 AND MACD signal line of open > 0.
Short: On the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, MACD histogram of open <= 0 AND MACD line of open <= 0 AND MACD signal line of open <= 0.
Long: On the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, StochRSI %K > %D.
Short: On the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, or the current chart bar, StochRSI %K < %D.
