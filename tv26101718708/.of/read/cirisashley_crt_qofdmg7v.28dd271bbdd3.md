Calculation: Displacement = high - low >= a multiple of ATR AND (high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= a body fraction, OR high = low AND the body fraction <= 0).
Calculation: Range sweep state = for each range, reset the state to neutral when the range resets; on each eligible bar, set it to high-swept if the high-sweep condition holds, then set it to low-swept if the low-sweep condition holds. Retain the latest state until reset. Each range permits only one confirmation per reset.
Calculation: CRT range = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, updated when that bar's reported time changes. Reset its sweep state and confirmation at each update. A CRT high sweep is high > range high AND close <= range high AND each of the preceding lookback bars closed within the range inclusive. A CRT low sweep is low < range low AND close >= range low AND each of those preceding bars closed within the range inclusive. Evaluate sweeps and confirmations only within either configured UTC trading window when the trading-window filter is enabled; otherwise evaluate every bar.
Calculation: Previous-day range = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe calendar-day bar. Reset its sweep state and confirmation at each new calendar day. A high sweep is high > range high AND close < range high AND close < open AND range high - close > a price threshold. A low sweep is low < range low AND close > range low AND close > open AND close - range low > the same price threshold.
Calculation: Session range = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars within a configured UTC session, excluding the first bar entering the session; reset the range and sweep state on that first bar, and lock the range when the session ends. The session membership condition is UTC hour >= session start hour OR UTC hour < session end hour. A high sweep is high > range high AND close < range high AND close < open. A low sweep is low < range low AND close > range low AND close > open.
Calculation: Opening range = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose UTC opening time lies within the configured opening window. Reset the range and sweep state on each new calendar day after processing that bar for the opening window, and lock the range when the window ends. Its high and low sweeps use the session range sweep conditions.
