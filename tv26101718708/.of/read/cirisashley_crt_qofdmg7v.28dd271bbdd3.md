Calculation: CRT range high/low = high/low of the last completed bar of the configured higher timeframe.
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar, a higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Displacement = current bar range >= configured multiple of ATR AND absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= configured minimum body fraction; the same displacement parameters are used for CRT and PDH/PDL signals.
Calculation: Asia range high/low = maximum high and minimum low during the configured UTC Asia session.
Calculation: Opening range high/low = maximum high and minimum low during the configured duration after the configured UTC market-open time.
Long: CRT: not yet confirmed in the current CRT range AND current UTC time is in the London or New York killzone AND the preceding configured number of closes are between CRT range low and high AND a bar has low < CRT range low AND close >= CRT range low AND a later bar is not another low sweep AND close > CRT range low AND close > open AND displacement. OR PDH/PDL: not yet confirmed today AND a bar has low < previous-day low AND close > previous-day low AND close > open AND close - previous-day low > minimum tick size AND a later bar is not another low sweep AND close > previous-day low AND close > previous-day midpoint AND close > open AND displacement. OR Asia: Asia range is locked AND not yet confirmed for this Asia range AND a bar has low < Asia range low AND close > Asia range low AND close > open AND a later bar is not another low sweep AND close > Asia range low AND close > open. OR ORB: opening range is locked AND not yet confirmed today AND a bar has low < opening range low AND close > opening range low AND close > open AND a later bar is not another low sweep AND close > opening range low AND close > open.
Short: CRT: not yet confirmed in the current CRT range AND current UTC time is in the London or New York killzone AND the preceding configured number of closes are between CRT range low and high AND a bar has high > CRT range high AND close <= CRT range high AND a later bar is not another high sweep AND close < CRT range high AND close < open AND displacement. OR PDH/PDL: not yet confirmed today AND a bar has high > previous-day high AND close < previous-day high AND close < open AND previous-day high - close > minimum tick size AND a later bar is not another high sweep AND close < previous-day high AND close < previous-day midpoint AND close < open AND displacement. OR Asia: Asia range is locked AND not yet confirmed for this Asia range AND a bar has high > Asia range high AND close < Asia range high AND close < open AND a later bar is not another high sweep AND close < Asia range high AND close < open. OR ORB: opening range is locked AND not yet confirmed today AND a bar has high > opening range high AND close < opening range high AND close < open AND a later bar is not another high sweep AND close < opening range high AND close < open.
