Calculation: Coppock curve = weighted moving average of the sum of the rate of change of the selected source over a longer lookback and a shorter lookback; the selected source may optionally be pre-smoothed by the configured method.
Calculation: LLAMA-smoothed source = simple moving average of source + ((source − source from one lookback ago) / lookback) × (lookback / 2).
Calculation: Kalman-smoothed source = recursively update estimate using gain = predicted error / (predicted error + 1), where predicted error = prior error + 1 / lookback; update estimate by gain × (source − prior estimate), and error by (1 − gain) × predicted error. Initialize estimate to source and error to 1.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price low is lower than the preceding confirmed price low while the Coppock value at that pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot; bearish divergence = a confirmed price high is higher than the preceding confirmed price high while the Coppock value at that pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot. Pivots are confirmed using equal-length bars before and after the pivot.
Long: Coppock curve crosses above its prior value AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND (divergence filter disabled OR no bearish divergence) AND (slope acceleration filter disabled OR current slope > prior slope) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR Coppock on the higher timeframe is rising, using its last completed bar) AND (persistence filter disabled OR the prior falling streak is at least the configured minimum length).
Short: Coppock curve crosses below its prior value AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND (divergence filter disabled OR no bullish divergence) AND (slope acceleration filter disabled OR current slope < prior slope) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR Coppock on the higher timeframe is not rising, using its last completed bar) AND (persistence filter disabled OR the prior rising streak is at least the configured minimum length).
Long: Coppock curve crosses above zero AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND (volatility-adjusted zero filter disabled OR |Coppock curve| > its standard deviation over the configured lookback × configured multiplier).
Short: Coppock curve crosses below zero AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND (volatility-adjusted zero filter disabled OR |Coppock curve| > its standard deviation over the configured lookback × configured multiplier).
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ any not-yet-hit take-profit price OR a short-entry condition occurs; test stop and take-profit prices only on bars after entry, and give the stop priority over take-profits.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ any not-yet-hit take-profit price OR a long-entry condition occurs; test stop and take-profit prices only on bars after entry, and give the stop priority over take-profits.
