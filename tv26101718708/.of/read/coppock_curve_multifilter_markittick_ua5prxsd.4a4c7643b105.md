Calculation: Source = a selected price series, optionally smoothed by SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, two successive WMAs of the same length, three successive VWMAs of the same length, linear-lag smoothing, or Kalman smoothing.
Calculation: Linear-lag smoothing = SMA(source, length) + (source - source[length]) / 2.
Calculation: Kalman smoothing = initialize estimate to source and error to 1; on each bar set predicted error = error + 1 / length, gain = predicted error / (predicted error + 1), estimate = estimate + gain * (source - estimate), and error = (1 - gain) * predicted error.
Calculation: Coppock = WMA(ROC(source, longer lookback) + ROC(source, shorter lookback)), using the optionally smoothed source.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = on each confirmed strict pivot high of price high, compare it with the preceding confirmed strict pivot high: the newer price high is greater and Coppock at the newer pivot is lower. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side; retain the result until the next pivot high.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = on each confirmed strict pivot low of price low, compare it with the preceding confirmed strict pivot low: the newer price low is lower and Coppock at the newer pivot is higher. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side; retain the result until the next pivot low.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the signal bar multiplied by the stop-distance parameter; retain it for that signal.
Long: Coppock > previous Coppock AND previous Coppock <= Coppock two bars ago AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish divergence is false) AND (acceleration filter disabled OR Coppock - previous Coppock > previous Coppock - Coppock two bars ago) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR Coppock on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > Coppock on the preceding higher-timeframe bar) AND (persistence filter disabled OR Coppock fell for at least the configured consecutive bars ending on the previous bar).
Short: Coppock < previous Coppock AND previous Coppock >= Coppock two bars ago AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish divergence is false) AND (acceleration filter disabled OR Coppock - previous Coppock < previous Coppock - Coppock two bars ago) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR Coppock on the last completed higher-timeframe bar <= Coppock on the preceding higher-timeframe bar) AND (persistence filter disabled OR Coppock rose for at least the configured consecutive bars ending on the previous bar).
Long: Coppock > 0 AND previous Coppock <= 0 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (volatility-band filter disabled OR absolute Coppock > standard deviation of Coppock multiplied by the band parameter).
Short: Coppock < 0 AND previous Coppock >= 0 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (volatility-band filter disabled OR absolute Coppock > standard deviation of Coppock multiplied by the band parameter).
