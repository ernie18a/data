Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or zero when the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Composite flow = EMA of a weighted sum of centered MFI, CMF pressure, OBV pressure, and price-volume pressure. Centered MFI = (MFI(source) - MFI midpoint) × scaling factor. CMF pressure = clamp(scaling factor × sum(volume × ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low)) / max(sum(volume), denominator floor), lower bound, upper bound), with each bar's multiplier zero when high = low. OBV pressure = clamp(scaling factor × (OBV - EMA(OBV)) / standard deviation(OBV), lower bound, upper bound), or zero when the standard deviation is zero. Price-volume pressure = clamp(scaling factor × (close-location weight × close location + body weight × body direction) × min(relative volume, volume cap), lower bound, upper bound), where close location = 2 × (close - low) / (high - low) - 1, or zero when high = low, and body direction is positive, negative, or zero according as close is above, below, or equal to open.
Calculation: Flow signal = EMA(composite flow) with a longer period than composite flow's EMA. Smart money = EMA(composite flow); its slope is its current value minus its previous value.
Calculation: Range position = (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low), using the specified lookback including the current bar; comparisons involving range position use the equivalent unnormalized expression when the range is zero.
Calculation: Flat price = highest high minus lowest low over the applicable range lookback <= ATR × flatness multiplier AND ATR > 0.
Calculation: Accumulation candidate = (close <= close at the accumulation lookback OR accumulation-range flat price) AND accumulation-range position <= lower-range threshold AND smart money > its previous value AND composite flow > flow signal + positive margin AND smart money > negative threshold AND relative volume >= minimum.
Calculation: Distribution candidate = (close >= close at the distribution lookback OR distribution-range flat price) AND distribution-range position >= upper-range threshold AND smart money < its previous value AND composite flow < flow signal - positive margin AND smart money < positive threshold AND relative volume >= minimum. The lower-range and upper-range thresholds are complementary.
Calculation: Accumulation zone = accumulation candidate holds for at least the required consecutive bars. Distribution zone = distribution candidate holds for at least the required consecutive bars. A zone start is a zone that was absent on the previous bar.
Calculation: Rising smart money = smart money > its previous value AND its previous value >= its value two bars ago. Falling smart money = smart money < its previous value AND its previous value <= its value two bars ago. Flow turning up = composite flow > its previous value AND its previous value <= its value two bars ago. Flow turning down = composite flow < its previous value AND its previous value >= its value two bars ago.
Calculation: Accumulation score = round(min(score cap, zone base if in accumulation zone, otherwise candidate base if an accumulation candidate, otherwise zero, + clamp((smart money + positive offset) × flow coefficient, zero, flow component cap) + turn bonus if smart money is rising OR flow is turning up, otherwise zero, + clamp(relative volume × volume coefficient, zero, volume component cap))). Distribution score uses the same formula with the distribution zone and candidate, positive offset - smart money, and falling smart money OR flow turning down. Zone base exceeds candidate base.
Calculation: Confirmation score = round(min(score cap, abs(composite flow) × flow coefficient + min(relative volume, volume cap) × volume coefficient + abs(smart money slope) × slope coefficient)). Divergence score = round(min(score cap, abs(composite flow - composite flow at the divergence lookback) × flow-change coefficient + abs(smart money slope) × slope coefficient + base score)).
Calculation: Bullish confirmation raw = composite flow > positive confirmation threshold AND smart money slope > 0 AND composite flow > flow signal AND relative volume >= confirmation volume minimum. Bearish confirmation raw = composite flow < negative confirmation threshold AND smart money slope < 0 AND composite flow < flow signal AND relative volume >= confirmation volume minimum AND, if the near-low filter is enabled, either divergence-range high = divergence-range low OR divergence-range position > near-low threshold.
Calculation: Accumulation event = accumulation zone start AND accumulation score >= its minimum AND bars since the last accumulation event > cooldown, or no previous accumulation event. Distribution event is defined analogously with its own score, minimum, and cooldown history. Bullish and bearish confirmation events require their respective raw condition AND confirmation score >= its minimum AND bars since the previous confirmation event of the same direction > cooldown, or no previous such event.
Calculation: Bullish divergence event = current low < lowest low over the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND composite flow > lowest composite flow over those preceding bars AND flow turning up AND smart money slope > 0 AND bullish confirmation raw is false AND divergence score >= its minimum AND bars since the last divergence event of either direction > cooldown, or no previous divergence event. Bearish divergence event = current high > highest high over the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND composite flow < highest composite flow over those preceding bars AND flow turning down AND smart money slope < 0 AND bearish confirmation raw is false AND divergence score >= its minimum AND bars since the last divergence event of either direction > cooldown, or no previous divergence event. Update both directions' cooldown histories on their events even if another marking takes priority.

Short: composite flow < 0
