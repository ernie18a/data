Calculation: WaveTrend = compute WT1 by applying EMA to the channel index (source minus EMA(source, channel length), divided by 0.015 times EMA of its absolute deviation from that EMA); WT2 is a short SMA of WT1. The channel and averaging lookbacks are distinct; WT2 uses a shorter lookback than WT1’s averaging lookback.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed five-bar trough in an oscillator (center bar lower than its two neighbors on each side), where price’s corresponding low is lower than the prior trough’s low and the oscillator’s trough is higher; regular bearish divergence is the mirrored five-bar peak, with a higher price high and a lower oscillator peak. The oscillator trough/peak must meet its configured oversold/overbought limit.
Calculation: Money-flow index used here = the configured-period SMA of (close − open) / (high − low), multiplied by a configured factor, then shifted by a configured offset.
Calculation: Gold-buy condition = a regular bullish WaveTrend or RSI divergence AND the prior WaveTrend trough was at or below the deepest oversold level AND current WT2 is above that level AND prior trough minus current WT2 is at most −5 AND RSI at the prior WaveTrend trough is below 30.
Calculation: Local trend average = EMA of close on the configured local-trend timeframe; use its latest available value (last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart, otherwise the current available bar).
Calculation: Global trend average = SMA of chart close over the configured global-trend lookback, evaluated on the configured global-trend timeframe for BTC perpetual data; compare with BTC perpetual close on that same timeframe. Use the latest available timeframe values (last completed bar when higher than the chart, otherwise the current available bar).
Calculation: Slope = (the 15-period EMA of close on the configured slope timeframe, currently 60 minutes, minus its value slope-lookback bars earlier) divided by the slope lookback. Use the latest available timeframe values (last completed bar when higher than the chart, otherwise the current available bar).
Calculation: Sommi bullish diamond = WT2 at or below its configured level AND WT1 crosses above WT2 AND both configured higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles are bullish; bearish diamond mirrors this with WT2 at or above its level, WT1 crossing below WT2, and both candles bearish. Use the latest available candles (last completed when higher than the chart, otherwise the current available bar).
Calculation: Sommi bullish flag = money-flow index above its configured bull level AND WT2 below its configured bull level AND WT1 crosses above WT2 AND WaveTrend VWAP (WT1 − WT2) on the configured flag timeframe is above its configured bull level; bearish flag mirrors these comparisons and uses the configured bear levels. The flag timeframe is higher than the chart; use its latest available bar (last completed bar).
Long: (WT1 crosses above WT2 AND WT2 at or below the oversold level OR a regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, Stochastic RSI, or RSI enabled by its divergence setting OR Gold-buy condition) AND (if divergence-only-long is enabled, a regular bullish divergence or Gold-buy condition) AND (close > local trend average if local trend filter is enabled) AND (RSI filter disabled OR 12-period EMA of 14-period RSI < 40) AND (price filter disabled OR close is within the configured long-price bounds) AND (slope filter disabled OR slope > configured long threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume is strictly between configured bounds) AND (either Sommi-diamond mode is enabled and bullish diamond is true and global trend is enabled or BTC close > global trend average or prior BTC close > prior global trend average; OR Sommi-diamond mode is disabled and the preceding signal conditions hold) AND no open position AND confirmed chart bar AND long entries enabled. Submit at close, or at close reduced by configured shift percentage when shifted-entry mode is enabled; fill only if a later bar's low reaches that limit.
Short: (WT1 crosses below WT2 AND WT2 at or above the overbought level OR a regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend, Stochastic RSI, or RSI enabled by its divergence setting) AND (if divergence-only-short is enabled, a regular bearish divergence) AND (close < local trend average if local trend filter is enabled) AND (RSI filter disabled OR 12-period EMA of 14-period RSI > 60) AND (price filter disabled OR close is within the configured short-price bounds) AND (slope filter disabled OR slope < configured short threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume is strictly between configured bounds) AND (either Sommi-diamond mode is enabled and bearish diamond is true and global trend is enabled or BTC close < global trend average or prior BTC close < prior global trend average; OR Sommi-diamond mode is disabled and the preceding signal conditions hold) AND no open position AND confirmed chart bar AND short entries enabled. Submit at close, or at close increased by configured shift percentage when shifted-entry mode is enabled; fill only if a later bar's high reaches that limit.
Exit: long, low <= long stop price OR high >= long target price OR (cancel-all enabled AND close < local trend average) OR (time-cancel enabled AND bars since the latest valid long setup > configured bar count).
Exit: short, high >= short stop price OR low <= short target price OR (cancel-all enabled AND close > local trend average) OR (time-cancel enabled AND bars since the latest valid short setup > configured bar count).
