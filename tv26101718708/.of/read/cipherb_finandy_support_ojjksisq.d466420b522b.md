Calculation: WaveTrend = fast line is EMA of (source − EMA(source, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel length)), channel length)); slow line is SMA of the fast line. The source is a selectable bar price.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA((close − open) / (high − low) × multiplier) − offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = SMA of the stochastic oscillator applied to RSI; its signal is an SMA of that result. The oscillator can instead be the average of those two lines, and RSI can use the logarithm of its source.
Calculation: A confirmed oscillator top or bottom is the value two bars ago when it is strictly greater or less, respectively, than each of the two bars on either side. Compare its oscillator value and corresponding price high or low with the most recent qualifying same-type fractal confirmed at least two bars earlier. A qualifying limited top is at or above its upper threshold; a qualifying limited bottom is at or below its lower threshold. Unlimited fractals have no threshold. Evaluate divergence on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = confirmed bottom with a lower corresponding price low and a higher oscillator value than the previous qualifying bottom. Regular bearish divergence = confirmed top with a higher corresponding price high and a lower oscillator value than the previous qualifying top. Hidden bullish divergence = confirmed bottom with a higher corresponding price low and a lower oscillator value than the previous qualifying bottom. Hidden bearish divergence = confirmed top with a lower corresponding price high and a higher oscillator value than the previous qualifying top.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; its open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Slope = (higher-timeframe close EMA − that EMA a configurable number of chart bars ago) / that number of chart bars. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: WaveTrend fast line crosses above its slow line AND slow line is at or below an oversold threshold.
Short: WaveTrend fast line crosses below its slow line AND slow line is at or above an overbought threshold.
Long: Regular bullish divergence of the WaveTrend slow line using either of two independently configurable oversold thresholds, OR regular bullish divergence of RSI with its oversold threshold, OR regular bullish divergence of stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Short: Regular bearish divergence of the WaveTrend slow line using either of two independently configurable overbought thresholds, OR regular bearish divergence of RSI with its overbought threshold, OR regular bearish divergence of stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Long: Hidden bullish divergence of the WaveTrend slow line or RSI, optionally using that oscillator's oversold threshold, OR hidden bullish divergence of stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Short: Hidden bearish divergence of the WaveTrend slow line or RSI, optionally using that oscillator's overbought threshold, OR hidden bearish divergence of stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Long: Regular bullish divergence of the WaveTrend slow line or RSI AND the previous qualifying WaveTrend bottom is at or below a deeper oversold threshold AND the current WaveTrend slow line is above that threshold AND the current slow line exceeds that previous bottom by at least a configurable amount AND RSI at that previous bottom is below a threshold.
Long: WaveTrend fast line crosses above its slow line AND slow line is below a threshold AND money flow is above a threshold AND the difference between higher-timeframe WaveTrend fast and slow lines is above a threshold. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: WaveTrend fast line crosses below its slow line AND slow line is above a threshold AND money flow is below a threshold AND the difference between higher-timeframe WaveTrend fast and slow lines is below a threshold. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: WaveTrend fast line crosses above its slow line AND slow line is at or below a threshold AND Heikin-Ashi close is greater than Heikin-Ashi open on each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other.
Short: WaveTrend fast line crosses below its slow line AND slow line is at or above a threshold AND Heikin-Ashi close is at or below Heikin-Ashi open on each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other.
Long: A regular bullish divergence or the oversold WaveTrend crossover occurs while flat AND, if enabled, close is above the local-timeframe close EMA AND close is within the configured long price range AND EMA of RSI is below its threshold AND slope is above its long threshold AND volume is within its configured range. The crossover is excluded when divergence-only mode is enabled. The local timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe.
Short: A regular bearish divergence or the overbought WaveTrend crossover occurs while flat AND, if enabled, close is below the local-timeframe close EMA AND close is within the configured short price range AND EMA of RSI is above its threshold AND slope is below its short threshold AND volume is within its configured range. The crossover is excluded when divergence-only mode is enabled. The local timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe.
Exit: long, bar low is at or below the entry limit price reduced by the stop percentage OR bar high is at or above the entry limit price increased by the target percentage; use a separate long target percentage for divergence entries.
Exit: short, bar high is at or above the entry limit price increased by the stop percentage OR bar low is at or below the entry limit price reduced by the target percentage.
Exit: long, local-trend exit is enabled AND the local filter is enabled AND close is below the local-timeframe close EMA.
Exit: short, local-trend exit is enabled AND the local filter is enabled AND close is above the local-timeframe close EMA.
Exit: long, timed exit is enabled AND bars since the latest valid long setup exceed the configured limit.
Exit: short, timed exit is enabled AND bars since the latest valid short setup exceed the configured limit.
