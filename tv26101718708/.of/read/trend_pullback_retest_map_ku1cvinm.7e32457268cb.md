Calculation: ATR = average true range; use a nonzero floor for divisions
Calculation: Candle range = max(high − low, minimum price tick); candle body = abs(close − open); close position = (close − low) / candle range × 100; bearish close position = (high − close) / candle range × 100; upper/lower wick percentage = corresponding wick / candle range × 100
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA with the trend lookback; fast EMA = EMA with a shorter lookback than the trend EMA; slow regime EMA = EMA with a longer lookback than the trend EMA
Calculation: Trend slope = abs(trend EMA − trend EMA 5 bars ago) / ATR; EMA gap = abs(fast EMA − trend EMA) / ATR
Calculation: Uptrend = fast EMA > trend EMA, trend EMA rising, trend slope and EMA gap meet their minimum thresholds, (close − lowest low over the trend progress lookback) / ATR meets its minimum, and slow regime is valid; downtrend is the reverse using falling trend EMA and (highest high over that lookback − close) / ATR
Calculation: Slow regime valid for longs = trend EMA and close are at or above slow regime EMA minus an ATR buffer, and slow regime EMA is above its value 3 bars ago; for shorts, trend EMA and close are at or below slow regime EMA plus the buffer, and slow regime EMA is below its value 3 bars ago. This filter may be disabled.
Calculation: Pullback depth for longs = (highest high over pullback lookback − low) / ATR during an uptrend; for shorts = (high − lowest low over pullback lookback) / ATR during a downtrend
Calculation: Recent impulse = range (highest high − lowest low) over the impulse lookback / ATR meets its minimum, and close is above close from that lookback for longs or below it for shorts
Calculation: Structure bias for longs = slow regime valid, close > trend EMA + minimum close gap × ATR, recent impulse valid when required, and no opposing candle body at least the configured size in ATR; reverse the inequalities and candle direction for shorts. This bias filter may be disabled.
Calculation: Setup gate = trend has persisted for at least the minimum trend age; high-low range over setup range lookback / ATR meets its minimum; current candle range / ATR is below its maximum; fast EMA slopes in the trade direction when required; range over chop lookback / ATR is not below the chop minimum when chop blocking is enabled; no recent hard-extension block; and structure bias is valid. The setup gate may be disabled.
Calculation: Healthy pullback = depth is between the configured minimum and maximum; volume ratio is at most the configured maximum when volume filtering is enabled
Calculation: SET score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: clamp((trend slope / (2 × minimum trend slope)) × 18, 0, 18); clamp((EMA gap / (2 × minimum EMA gap)) × 16, 0, 16); clamp(22 − abs(depth − midpoint of minimum and maximum pullback depths) / (maximum − minimum pullback depth) × 22, 0, 22); clamp(close position / 100 × 20, 0, 20); volume score (14 when volume filter is off, otherwise clamp((maximum pullback volume ratio − volume ratio + 0.45) / 0.85 × 14, 0, 14)); and clamp((setup range / ATR) / (1.6 × minimum setup range / ATR) × 10, 0, 10). Use bearish close position and short pullback depth for shorts.
Calculation: Long SET setup = uptrend; low ≥ trend EMA − hold buffer × ATR and close ≥ trend EMA; low ≤ fast EMA + touch buffer × ATR and low ≥ trend EMA − hold buffer × ATR; healthy long pullback; bullish candle with close > fast EMA, close ≥ prior close, and close position ≥ setup threshold; setup gate valid; SET score ≥ minimum; and no hard long extension context. Short SET setup is the directional reverse, using bearish close position.
Calculation: Retest structure break = close above the highest high of prior bars over the RT structure lookback for longs, or below the lowest low of those bars for shorts; this requirement may be disabled
Calculation: Long momentum retest = close > prior high + prior-bar break buffer × ATR, close > fast EMA and open, close position ≥ RT threshold, retest structure break valid, and RT structure bias valid. Short momentum retest reverses these comparisons and uses bearish close position.
Calculation: RT score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: clamp((trend slope / (2 × minimum trend slope)) × 16, 0, 16); clamp((EMA gap / (2 × minimum EMA gap)) × 14, 0, 14); clamp(close position / 100 × 24, 0, 24); volume score (18 when volume filtering is off, otherwise clamp((volume ratio / (1.5 × minimum retest volume ratio)) × 18, 0, 18)); and clamp(nonnegative trigger break distance in ATR / (trigger break buffer + 0.15) × 28, 0, 28). Use bearish close position and short trigger distance for shorts.
Calculation: Cycle retest = active same-side SET cycle has lasted at least the minimum bars and no more than the timeout; trend remains valid; trigger close break beyond the setup candle high (long) or low (short) by the trigger buffer × ATR, or momentum retest; volume ratio meets its minimum when volume filtering is enabled; RT score ≥ minimum; extension reset conditions pass; and no hard extension context. Extension reset, when required after same-direction EXT, requires a pullback since EXT of at least minimum pullback depth × reset multiplier, current price-to-trend-EMA gap within its maximum, and (when required) a trend EMA area touch since EXT. Apply configured post-EXT blocking intervals.
Calculation: Direct retest = direct RT enabled; no active same-side SET cycle; trend valid; a pullback into the fast EMA area occurred within the direct RT lookback; momentum retest and setup gate valid; extension reset and post-EXT blocking conditions pass; no hard extension context; and direct RT cooldown elapsed.
Calculation: Long extension context = uptrend and ((trend progress exceeds soft maximum OR close-to-trend-EMA distance / ATR exceeds soft maximum) AND (bearish candle OR close position < 45% OR upper wick percentage ≥ exhaustion wick threshold) OR an exhaustion candle). Exhaustion candle = uptrend, high reaches or exceeds the highest high of prior extension-lookback bars, upper wick percentage meets threshold, volume ratio meets exhaustion minimum, and close < open. Short context reverses direction and uses lows, lower wick, and close > open. Hard extension context uses the hard progress and gap maxima together with the same stall condition, or the exhaustion candle. Soft EXT marker cooldown applies; hard extension also blocks new SETs for its configured interval.
Calculation: Weak pullback = uptrend and pullback depth ≥ deep pullback threshold and close < fast EMA for longs; downtrend and depth ≥ threshold and close > fast EMA for shorts. Weak marker cooldown applies.
Calculation: Setup cycle failure for longs = active long SET cycle and (bearish early-failure candle with body ≥ early-failure ATR threshold, bearish close position ≤ failure threshold, close below fast EMA OR below invalidation low minus half the invalidation buffer × ATR, and volume ratio ≥ early-failure minimum when filtered; OR close below trend EMA minus hold buffer × ATR; OR close below invalidation low minus invalidation buffer × ATR; OR pullback depth ≥ deep threshold). Reverse directions and comparisons for shorts. FAIL cooldown applies. A cycle also ends on RT, timeout, or loss of its trend.
Long: Long SET setup and no active long cycle and SET cooldown elapsed OR cycle retest OR direct retest OR weak pullback OR long extension context
Short: Short SET setup and no active short cycle and SET cooldown elapsed OR cycle retest OR direct retest OR weak pullback OR short extension context
Exit: long, setup cycle failure for longs
Exit: short, setup cycle failure for shorts
