Calculation: Direction sign s = +1 for long and -1 for short; directed price P(x) = s × x; directed favorable extreme F = high for long and low for short; directed adverse extreme A = low for long and high for short. Apply each directional calculation independently for both signs. Comparisons of directed prices use > for favorable movement.
Calculation: ATR-normalized quantities = divide by ATR, floored at the instrument's minimum price increment; candle range is also floored at that increment. Volume ratio = volume / max(volume SMA, 1), using current volume if the SMA is unavailable. Favorable close fraction = (close - low) / candle range for long and (high - close) / candle range for short. Favorable wick fraction = (high - max(open, close)) / candle range for long and (min(open, close) - low) / candle range for short. Body = abs(close - open).
Calculation: Trend = directed fast EMA > directed trend EMA AND directed trend EMA > its previous value AND abs(trend EMA - its value at a slope lookback) / ATR >= minimum slope AND abs(fast EMA - trend EMA) / ATR >= minimum EMA gap AND progress >= minimum progress AND (slow-regime filter disabled OR directed trend EMA and directed close >= directed slow EMA - slow-regime buffer × ATR AND directed slow EMA > its value at a slope lookback). Fast, trend, and slow EMAs use progressively longer lengths. Progress = (directed close - lowest directed adverse extreme over the progress lookback) / ATR. Trend age = bars since Trend was false.
Calculation: Pullback depth = (highest directed favorable extreme over the pullback lookback - directed adverse extreme) / ATR when Trend is true, otherwise zero. Fast touch = Trend AND directed adverse extreme <= directed fast EMA + touch buffer × ATR AND directed adverse extreme >= directed trend EMA - hold buffer × ATR. Trend touch = Trend AND directed adverse extreme <= directed trend EMA + hold buffer × ATR AND directed close >= directed trend EMA.
Calculation: Setup bias = structure-bias filter disabled OR (slow regime passes AND directed close > directed trend EMA + close-gap threshold × ATR AND (impulse filter disabled OR directed close > directed close at impulse lookback AND (highest high - lowest low over impulse lookback) / ATR >= impulse threshold) AND (opposite-body filter disabled OR NOT (directed close < directed open AND body / ATR >= opposite-body threshold))). Retest bias = structure-bias filter disabled OR (slow regime passes AND directed close > directed trend EMA + close-gap threshold × ATR AND directed close > highest directed favorable extreme over preceding structure-lookback bars - structure buffer × ATR AND (opposite-body filter disabled OR NOT (directed close < directed open AND body / ATR >= opposite-body threshold))).
Calculation: Setup gate = setup gate disabled OR (trend age >= minimum age AND (highest high - lowest low over setup-range lookback) / ATR >= minimum setup range AND candle range / ATR <= maximum candle range AND (fast-slope filter disabled OR directed fast EMA > its previous value) AND (chop filter disabled OR (highest high - lowest low over chop lookback) / ATR >= minimum chop range) AND no hard extension in the preceding hard-extension block window AND setup bias). The hard-extension block is shared across directions.
Calculation: Stall = Trend AND (directed close < directed open OR favorable close fraction < stall-close threshold OR favorable wick fraction >= exhaustion-wick threshold). Exhaustion candle = Trend AND directed favorable extreme >= highest directed favorable extreme over preceding extension-lookback bars AND favorable wick fraction >= exhaustion-wick threshold AND volume ratio >= exhaustion-volume threshold AND directed close < directed open. Soft extension = Trend AND soft-extension option enabled AND (((progress > soft progress threshold OR (directed close - directed trend EMA) / ATR > soft gap threshold) AND stall) OR exhaustion candle). Hard extension = Trend AND hard-extension option enabled AND ((progress > higher hard progress threshold AND (directed close - directed trend EMA) / ATR > higher hard gap threshold AND stall) OR exhaustion candle).
Calculation: Setup score = sum of bounded components: slope component = clamp(slope / max(2 × minimum slope, small denominator) × 18, 0, 18); gap component = clamp(EMA gap / max(2 × minimum EMA gap, small denominator) × 16, 0, 16); depth component = clamp(22 - abs(pullback depth - midpoint of minimum and maximum healthy depth) / max(maximum healthy depth - minimum healthy depth, small denominator) × 22, 0, 22); close component = clamp(favorable close fraction × 20, 0, 20); volume component = clamp((maximum pullback volume ratio - volume ratio + 0.45) / 0.85 × 14, 0, 14) if volume quality is enabled, otherwise 14; range component = clamp(setup range / ATR / max(1.6 × minimum setup range, small denominator) × 10, 0, 10. Clamp the sum to 0–100.
Calculation: Retest score = sum of bounded components: slope component = clamp(slope / max(2 × minimum slope, small denominator) × 16, 0, 16); gap component = clamp(EMA gap / max(2 × minimum EMA gap, small denominator) × 14, 0, 14); close component = clamp(favorable close fraction × 24, 0, 24); volume component = clamp(volume ratio / max(1.5 × minimum retest volume ratio, small denominator) × 18, 0, 18) if volume quality is enabled, otherwise 18; break component = clamp(max((directed close - directed stored trigger) / ATR, 0) / max(trigger-break buffer + 0.15, small denominator) × 28, 0, 28), using zero break distance when no trigger is stored. Clamp the sum to 0–100.
Calculation: Setup = Trend AND directed adverse extreme >= directed trend EMA - hold buffer × ATR AND directed close >= directed trend EMA AND fast touch AND pullback depth between minimum and maximum healthy depth inclusive AND directed close > directed open AND directed close > directed fast EMA AND directed close >= previous directed close AND favorable close fraction >= setup-close threshold AND (volume quality disabled OR volume ratio <= maximum pullback volume ratio) AND setup gate AND setup score >= minimum setup score AND hard extension is false AND no active same-direction setup AND bars since either direction's previous setup > shared setup cooldown, or no previous setup. On setup, activate that direction and deactivate the opposite direction; store this bar's favorable extreme as trigger and adverse extreme as invalidation, and store the setup bar. This state update occurs even if setup markers are hidden.
Calculation: Extension reset = reset requirement disabled OR no previous extension OR previous extension was opposite-direction OR (a same-direction fast touch occurred strictly after the previous extension AND pullback depth >= minimum pullback depth × reset multiplier AND (directed close - directed trend EMA) / ATR <= reset gap threshold AND (trend-touch requirement disabled OR a same-direction trend touch occurred strictly after the previous extension)). The most recent displayed soft extension and its direction are shared across directions.
Calculation: Momentum retest = momentum option enabled AND directed close > previous bar's directed favorable extreme + previous-bar break buffer × ATR AND directed close > directed fast EMA AND directed close > directed open AND favorable close fraction >= retest-close threshold AND (structure-break requirement disabled OR directed close > highest directed favorable extreme over preceding retest-structure-lookback bars) AND retest bias.
Calculation: Cycle retest = same-direction setup active AND bars since setup >= minimum retest delay AND Trend AND extension reset AND (trigger option enabled AND directed close > directed stored trigger + trigger buffer × ATR OR momentum retest) AND (volume quality disabled OR volume ratio >= minimum retest volume ratio) AND retest score >= minimum retest score AND hard extension is false AND bars since either direction's previous retest > shared retest cooldown, or no previous retest. Direct retest = direct-retest option enabled AND extension reset AND no active same-direction setup AND Trend AND fast touch occurred within the direct-retest lookback AND momentum retest AND setup gate AND hard extension is false AND bars since either direction's previous retest > direct-retest cooldown, or no previous retest. A retest is cycle retest OR direct retest. The post-extension retest blocking windows are bypassed whenever extension reset passes.
Calculation: Failure = same-direction setup active AND ((early-failure option enabled AND directed close < directed open AND body >= early-failure body threshold × ATR AND favorable close fraction <= failure-close threshold AND (directed close < directed fast EMA OR directed close < directed stored invalidation - half the invalidation buffer × ATR) AND (volume quality disabled OR volume ratio >= early-failure volume threshold)) OR directed close < directed trend EMA - hold buffer × ATR OR directed close < directed stored invalidation - invalidation buffer × ATR OR pullback depth >= deep-pullback threshold) AND bars since either direction's previous failure > shared failure cooldown, or no previous failure.
Calculation: Weak pullback = Trend AND pullback depth >= deep-pullback threshold AND directed close < directed fast EMA AND bars since either direction's previous weak pullback > shared weak-pullback cooldown, or no previous weak pullback. Extension = soft extension AND bars since either direction's previous displayed extension > shared extension cooldown, or no previous extension.
Calculation: Process each bar in this order: evaluate and activate setups; evaluate retests and failures using the resulting setup state and the previously stored extension state; record displayed retests and failures; deactivate a direction's setup after its displayed retest or failure, when setup age exceeds timeout, or when its Trend becomes false; then evaluate and record weak pullbacks and displayed extensions, and record any hard extension. Setup, retest, failure, weak-pullback, and extension cooldowns are each shared across directions. Retest and failure visibility controls affect their state updates; extension visibility affects extension history. Stored trigger and invalidation remain available after setup completion until the configured completed-line retention expires, or are removed immediately if retention is disabled. Parameterize all thresholds, lookbacks, cooldowns, optional filters, visibility controls, retention settings, and the small denominator.
Long: long setup OR long retest
Short: short setup OR short retest
Exit: long, long failure OR long weak pullback OR long extension
Exit: short, short failure OR short weak pullback OR short extension
