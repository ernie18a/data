Calculation: Volume shares = Over the longer lookback ending at the current chart bar, divide the range from lowest low to highest high into equal-width price bins. Assign each bar's entire volume to the bin containing its close, clamping bin indices to the valid range. For the current close's bin, calculate its share of total volume over the longer lookback and over the shorter lookback, limited to bars in the longer lookback; use zero for a share when its total volume is not positive. Treat unavailable historical closes and volumes as zero.
Calculation: Cluster score = Shorter-lookback volume share divided by the longer-lookback volume share plus a small positive smoothing constant.
Calculation: Price drift = (current close minus close at the shorter-lookback offset) divided by that historical close; use zero if that close is zero.
Long: Highest high over the longer lookback > lowest low over the longer lookback AND shorter-lookback volume share >= minimum share threshold AND cluster score >= moderate threshold AND price drift > 0 AND (date filter disabled OR bar datetime within the inclusive date range).
Short: Highest high over the longer lookback > lowest low over the longer lookback AND shorter-lookback volume share >= minimum share threshold AND cluster score >= moderate threshold AND price drift < 0 AND (date filter disabled OR bar datetime within the inclusive date range).
