Calculation: Cycle displacement = close − EMA(close); cycle z-score = (cycle displacement − SMA(cycle displacement)) / standard deviation(cycle displacement), using the same lookback for all three calculations; use zero when the deviation is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Absorption = volume / SMA(volume) >= minimum relative volume AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) <= maximum body fraction; use zero relative volume when its average is unavailable or zero, and zero body fraction when high equals low.
Calculation: Eligible bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND its pivot bar differs from the last consumed pivot-low bar AND ((cycle z-score <= negative stretch threshold AND cycle displacement > previous cycle displacement) OR absorption in balanced mode; AND absorption in strict mode). Eligible bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND its pivot bar differs from the last consumed pivot-high bar AND ((cycle z-score >= positive stretch threshold AND cycle displacement < previous cycle displacement) OR absorption in balanced mode; AND absorption in strict mode). Pivot highs and lows use equal left and right strengths and become available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Setup state = initially neither side armed and neither pivot consumed. On each completed bar, first disarm any setup whose expiry bar is before the current bar; then evaluate both eligible sweeps. If exactly one side is eligible and that side is not armed, arm it, disarm the opposite side, consume its pivot bar, record this bar and its high for a bullish setup or low for a bearish setup, and set expiry to this bar plus the confirmation window. Next evaluate confirmation signals; disarm a side after its confirmation. A pivot remains consumed until a different pivot of that side is consumed.
Calculation: Last signal bias = the side of the most recent confirmation signal, initially neither side.
Long: Eligible bullish sweep AND eligible bearish sweep is false AND bullish setup was not armed before accepting this sweep.
Short: Eligible bearish sweep AND eligible bullish sweep is false AND bearish setup was not armed before accepting this sweep.
Long: Bullish setup is armed AND current bar is after its setup bar AND close > its recorded high AND ATR is available AND (EMA context filter is disabled OR close >= context EMA).
Short: Bearish setup is armed AND current bar is after its setup bar AND close < its recorded low AND ATR is available AND (EMA context filter is disabled OR close <= context EMA).
Long: Cycle z-score <= negative stretch threshold.
Short: Cycle z-score >= positive stretch threshold.
Long: Bullish setup remains armed after confirmation processing OR last signal bias is bullish.
Short: Bearish setup remains armed after confirmation processing OR last signal bias is bearish.
