Calculation: Stochastic %K = 100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback)
Calculation: Stochastic %D = simple moving average of Stochastic %K
Calculation: Middle zone = value > 1.75 × oversold level and value < overbought level
Calculation: Middle-zone-seen = true when both %K and %D are in the middle zone; otherwise retain its prior value. Initialize false, set it to the current both-in-middle-zone condition on a long entry, and reset it to false on exit.
Long: Flat, within the configured date range, and ((%K ≤ oversold level AND %D ≤ oversold level AND %K crosses above %D AND %K is rising AND %D is rising AND close > open AND volume / volume moving average ≥ configured volume threshold) OR (%K is rising AND %D is rising AND volume / volume moving average ≥ configured volume threshold AND close > open AND %K crossed above %D on the prior bar) OR (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND fast EMA is rising AND slow EMA is rising AND close > open))
Exit: Long, within the configured date range, no long entry on this bar, and ((%K ≤ oversold level AND %D ≤ oversold level) OR (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA) OR (%K crosses below %D AND prior %K ≥ overbought level AND prior %D ≥ overbought level AND %K is falling AND %D is falling AND %K ≤ overbought level) OR (close < entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage / 100))) AND (middle-zone-seen OR close < entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage / 100))
