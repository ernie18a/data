Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = values from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, carried forward on chart bars; moving averages and historical offsets applied after importing a series operate on chart bars.
Calculation: Modified SuperTrend = for each of two independently parameterized, distinct higher timeframes, import its close and ATR. On chart bars, compute center as the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, or VWMA of imported close times chart volume divided by the same moving average of chart volume. Set raw upper and lower bands to center plus and minus ATR times a multiplier. Retain the previous lower band unless the raw lower band is higher or the previous chart close is below the previous lower band; retain the previous upper band unless the raw upper band is lower or the previous chart close is above the previous upper band. Treat missing previous bands as zero. Initialize direction bearish when the previous imported ATR is unavailable; otherwise, if the previous SuperTrend equals the previous upper band, direction is bullish when chart close exceeds the current upper band and bearish otherwise; else direction is bearish when chart close is below the current lower band and bullish otherwise. The SuperTrend is the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish.
Calculation: KNN score = separately for each modified SuperTrend, label each chart bar 1 if a WMA of chart close exceeds a WMA of that SuperTrend, otherwise 0. Among the current and preceding chart bars in the first classifier's sample-size parameter, select the configured number of nearest SuperTrend values to the current SuperTrend by absolute distance, breaking ties by most recent bar. Return the weighted mean of their labels, with weight equal to the reciprocal of distance plus a positive smoothing constant. The second classifier has independent neighbor and WMA parameters but uses the first classifier's sample size.
Calculation: Directional filter = import higher-timeframe high, low, and close; calculate their changes on chart bars. Positive directional movement is the high increase when it exceeds both the low decrease and zero, otherwise zero; negative directional movement is the low decrease when it exceeds both the high increase and zero, otherwise zero. Divide each movement's WMA by the WMA of abs(high minus low) plus abs(high minus previous close) plus abs(low minus previous close), and multiply by 100 to obtain positive and negative DI. ADX is the WMA of 100 times abs(positive DI minus negative DI) divided by their sum.
Long: Either KNN score = 1
Short: Either KNN score = 0
Long: Both modified SuperTrend directions are bullish AND both KNN scores = 1 AND (directional filter disabled OR ADX > threshold AND positive DI > negative DI)
Short: Both modified SuperTrend directions are bearish AND both KNN scores = 0 AND (directional filter disabled OR ADX > threshold AND negative DI > positive DI)
Exit: long, neither modified SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish with its KNN score = 1 on the current bar
Exit: short, neither modified SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish with its KNN score = 0 on the current bar
Exit: long, low reaches the latest submitted stop at the first modified SuperTrend minus its higher-timeframe ATR times its multiplier; submit or update the stop when neither modified SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish with its KNN score = 1
Exit: short, high reaches the latest submitted stop at the first modified SuperTrend plus its higher-timeframe ATR times its multiplier; submit or update the stop when neither modified SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish with its KNN score = 0
