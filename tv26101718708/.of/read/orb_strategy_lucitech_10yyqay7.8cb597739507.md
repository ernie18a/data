Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening session in New York time; reset at the start of each session. The midpoint is the average of those two levels.
Calculation: Breakout eligibility = after the opening session and before the configured cutoff, with opening range levels available. Each direction is eligible until its first breakout of the session, including a breakout that fails an entry filter; reset both directions at the next opening session.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the simple moving average of volume including the current bar; use 1 when the average is not positive.
Calculation: Initial long stop = at the signal bar, either low minus a configured multiple of smoothed true range, the low at a configured bar offset, or the opening range midpoint. Initial short stop = at the signal bar, either high plus a configured multiple of smoothed true range, the high at a configured bar offset, or the opening range midpoint. Smooth true range with the selected RMA, SMA, EMA, or WMA.
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close plus a configured reward-to-risk multiple of signal-bar close minus initial long stop. Short target = signal-bar close minus the same multiple of initial short stop minus signal-bar close.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until breakeven is enabled and, on a bar with an open position, long high minus signal-bar close or signal-bar close minus short low reaches the configured multiple of the absolute difference between signal-bar close and initial stop; thereafter the stop is the signal-bar close. The updated stop order applies after the triggering bar is evaluated.
Long: Breakout eligibility AND close > opening range high AND previous close <= opening range high.
Short: Breakout eligibility AND close < opening range low AND previous close >= opening range low.
Long: Breakout eligibility AND close > opening range high AND previous close <= opening range high AND position is flat AND initial long stop < close AND position size rounded down to the instrument's minimum tick increment > 0 AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured inclusive time window) AND (day filter disabled OR bar day is selected) AND (month filter disabled OR bar month is selected) AND (position type filter disabled OR long is enabled) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= configured threshold).
Short: Breakout eligibility AND close < opening range low AND previous close >= opening range low AND position is flat AND initial short stop > close AND position size rounded down to the instrument's minimum tick increment > 0 AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured inclusive time window) AND (day filter disabled OR bar day is selected) AND (month filter disabled OR bar month is selected) AND (position type filter disabled OR short is enabled) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= configured threshold).
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= short target.
