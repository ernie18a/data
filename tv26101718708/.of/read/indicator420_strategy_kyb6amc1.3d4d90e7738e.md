Calculation: 3×-period VWMA = volume-weighted moving average of close over a lookback three times the VWMA period
Long: position is flat AND Hull moving average is rising AND Hull moving average is below the 3×-period VWMA
Short: position is flat AND Hull moving average is falling AND Hull moving average is above the 3×-period VWMA
Exit: long, Hull moving average is falling
Exit: short, Hull moving average is rising
