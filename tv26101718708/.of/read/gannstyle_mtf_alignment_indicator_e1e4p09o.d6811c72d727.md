Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low at a pivot bar that is the highest/lowest over an equal-length lookback on both sides; it becomes available only after the right-hand bars complete. Use the same pivot lookback on all timeframes.
Calculation: Swing direction = initially neutral; retain the latest confirmed swing high and low and their confirmation bars. Set direction to long when close exceeds the latest swing high at least a minimum number of bars after its confirmation; set it to short when close falls below the latest swing low at least that many bars after its confirmation. If both occur, short takes precedence; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Alignment = all three swing directions agree: chart timeframe and two higher timeframes, one longer than the other. For each higher timeframe use the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's direction, mapped to chart bars with lookahead off. Use the completed chart bar's direction.
Calculation: Flow score = weighted average of four components over the same chart-bar lookback: clamp the sum of open minus previous close divided by ATR times lookback, multiplied by 100, to [-1,1]; clamp the average of (lower wick minus upper wick) divided by max(abs(close-open), minimum price tick), divided by 3, to [-1,1]; divide up-bar volume minus down-bar volume by max(their sum, 1), substituting 1 for missing volume; and clamp (close minus close at the lookback) divided by max(ATR, minimum price tick), divided by 2, to [-1,1]. Exclude the volume component's weight when current volume is missing or nonpositive; divide by max(sum of included weights, a positive floor). Use chart-timeframe ATR, or its previous value while the chart bar is forming.
Calculation: Trade levels = record entry at signal-bar close and entry-bar ATR. Initial long stop is entry minus an ATR multiple and initial short stop is entry plus that multiple. The first and final profit targets are entry plus their respective ATR multiples for a long and entry minus them for a short; the final target uses the larger multiple. On first target touch, move the stop to entry. If trailing is enabled after that touch and before final target touch, raise the long stop to the maximum of its current value and close minus a current ATR multiple, or lower the short stop to the minimum of its current value and close plus that multiple. Update targets and stop before testing exits, including on the entry bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe swing direction = long OR either higher-timeframe swing direction = long OR alignment = long.
Short: Chart-timeframe swing direction = short OR either higher-timeframe swing direction = short OR alignment = short.
Long: A chart-timeframe swing low is confirmed.
Short: A chart-timeframe swing high is confirmed.
Long: Alignment = long AND previous chart bar's alignment != long AND session filtering is disabled OR alignment = long AND previous chart bar's alignment != long AND bar time is in the selected session; in either case all three position splits must be positive and their sum must be within the allowed tolerance of 100%, the bar must be completed, and bars since the most recent long or short alignment signal must be at least the cooldown. Initialize the last signal bar to bar index zero and update it on every alignment signal, including while a position is open.
Short: Alignment = short AND previous chart bar's alignment != short AND session filtering is disabled OR alignment = short AND previous chart bar's alignment != short AND bar time is in the selected session; in either case all three position splits must be positive and their sum must be within the allowed tolerance of 100%, the bar must be completed, and bars since the most recent long or short alignment signal must be at least the cooldown. Initialize the last signal bar to bar index zero and update it on every alignment signal, including while a position is open.
Long: Flow analysis is enabled AND flow score > positive threshold.
Short: Flow analysis is enabled AND flow score < negative threshold of equal magnitude.
Exit: long, high >= final long profit target OR low <= updated long stop OR alignment != long.
Exit: short, low <= final short profit target OR high >= updated short stop OR alignment != short.
