Calculation: Exponential moving average (EMA) = EMA of close on the selected EMA timeframe; 20-period EMA is shorter-term than the 50-period EMA. The selected timeframe may be the chart timeframe or another timeframe; when different, use its projected value with lookahead off.
Calculation: Supertrend direction = Supertrend direction on the selected Supertrend timeframe; use its projected value with lookahead off. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: 20-period EMA crosses above 50-period EMA OR, after that cross and within the setup-expiry window, a completed chart bar closes above the 20-period EMA and a subsequent bar has low <= the 20-period EMA while Supertrend direction is bullish.
Short: 20-period EMA crosses below 50-period EMA OR, after that cross and within the setup-expiry window, a completed chart bar closes below the 20-period EMA and a subsequent bar has high >= the 20-period EMA while Supertrend direction is bearish.
