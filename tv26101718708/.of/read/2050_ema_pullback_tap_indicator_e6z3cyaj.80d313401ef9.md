Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close on the chart timeframe; fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA
Calculation: Supertrend = standard ATR-based Supertrend computed on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; bullish when price is above the Supertrend line, bearish when below
Long: the most recent EMA cross is fast EMA crossing above slow EMA at cross bar C (no opposite cross since), AND no long signal has fired since C, AND current bar index minus C is greater than 0 and less than the setup expiry bar count, AND at least one bar after C up to and including the current bar has close > fast EMA, AND current low <= fast EMA, AND Supertrend is bullish
Short: the most recent EMA cross is fast EMA crossing below slow EMA at cross bar C (no opposite cross since), AND no short signal has fired since C, AND current bar index minus C is greater than 0 and less than the setup expiry bar count, AND at least one bar after C up to and including the current bar has close < fast EMA, AND current high >= fast EMA, AND Supertrend is bearish
