Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 0.5.
Calculation: Extension baseline = selected shorter-period EMA of close, longer-period EMA of close, or still longer-period SMA of close. The shorter and longer EMAs are the same EMAs used for wedge decisions.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a reference timeframe that may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. For a higher reference timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical data and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower reference timeframe, use the last available intrabar. The reference timeframe is the same for both extension decisions.
Calculation: Base ceiling = persistent state updated on every chart bar. When a pivot high is confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars, compare it with the existing ceiling. If no ceiling exists, or the pivot is higher and its percentage gain over the lowest low in a lookback ending at the pivot meets a threshold, set the ceiling to the pivot, its start to the pivot bar, and the base floor to the lowest low from the pivot bar through the current bar. When a ceiling exists, update the floor to the minimum of its existing value and the current low. Calculate depth as (ceiling - floor) / ceiling and duration as bars since the start. If high > ceiling and duration < minimum duration, reset the ceiling to high, the start to the current bar, and the floor to low. Then clear the ceiling if the previously calculated depth exceeds its maximum or the previously calculated duration exceeds its maximum. After evaluating the breakout decision, clear the ceiling when a breakout occurs.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, at least a threshold number of the preceding lookback bars closed below their respective shorter EMA AND close > shorter EMA AND close > longer EMA AND (previous close <= previous shorter EMA OR previous close <= previous longer EMA) AND close > open.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, close < extension baseline AND extension baseline - close >= a multiplier of the longer-period ATR AND close position >= a threshold AND high < extension baseline AND volume > SMA of volume AND close < higher-timeframe EMA.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, base ceiling exists AND bars since its start >= minimum duration AND close > base ceiling AND previous close <= base ceiling.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, previous high - max(previous open, previous close) > a fraction of previous high - previous low AND open > max(previous open, previous close) AND open < previous high AND close > open.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, low < previous low AND close > previous high AND close > open.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, abs(close - open) >= a multiplier of the shorter-period ATR AND close position >= a threshold AND volume > a multiplier of SMA of volume.
Long: On a non-intraday chart, volume >= a multiplier of SMA of volume AND close position >= a threshold.
Short: On a non-intraday chart, (previous close >= previous shorter EMA OR previous close >= previous longer EMA) AND close < shorter EMA AND close < longer EMA AND close < open.
Short: On a non-intraday chart, close > extension baseline AND close - extension baseline >= a multiplier of the longer-period ATR AND close position <= a threshold AND low > extension baseline AND volume > SMA of volume AND close > higher-timeframe EMA.
