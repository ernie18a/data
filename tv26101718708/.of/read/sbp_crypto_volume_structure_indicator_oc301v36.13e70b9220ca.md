Calculation: Safe division = numerator divided by denominator when the absolute denominator exceeds a small positive threshold; otherwise zero. Clamp restricts a value to the stated interval. A price increment is a configurable positive minimum price unit.
Calculation: Candle measures = range is max(high − low, price increment); body is abs(close − open); body ratio is body / range; close location is clamp((close − low) / range, 0, 1); upper wick is high − max(open, close); lower wick is min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Effective volume = volume when volume > 0, otherwise 1. Relative volume = effective volume / its SMA when the SMA > 0, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Volume center = VWMA of (high + low + close) / 3 over the structure lookback, falling back to its EMA over the same lookback when the VWMA is unavailable. Deviation = max(standard deviation of that price over the structure lookback, or ATR when unavailable; ATR multiplied by a floor parameter). ATR is floored at the price increment.
Calculation: Participation = clamp(safe division of EMA(effective volume × (2 × close location − 1)) by EMA(effective volume), −1, 1), using the same lookback for both EMAs. Direction = a weighted sum of participation, clamp(safe division of the change in volume center by ATR, −1, 1), and clamp(safe division of close − volume center by deviation, −1, 1).
Calculation: Control center = volume center + deviation × participation × a scaling parameter. Control half-width = max(deviation × a scaling parameter, ATR × a scaling parameter × profile scale). Outer boundaries = volume center ± deviation × a scaling parameter × profile scale. Value half-width = max(deviation × a scaling parameter, ATR × a scaling parameter × profile scale); value boundaries = volume center ± value half-width. Profile scale is a configurable positive parameter.
Calculation: Session control center, control boundaries, and outer boundaries = their corresponding current values captured on the first confirmed chart bar of each calendar day, or on the first confirmed bar when uninitialized, and held until the next reset.
Calculation: Bull ribbon state = direction at least a positive threshold AND safe division of the change in a short EMA of (high + low + close) / 3 by ATR at least a negative threshold AND close at least volume center, on both the current and previous confirmed bars. Bear ribbon state = direction at most the negative counterpart AND that normalized EMA change at most the positive counterpart AND close at most volume center, on both bars.
Calculation: Trend line = a twice-EMA-smoothed weighted average of current and preceding closes, with weights decreasing for older closes. Bull trend evidence = close > trend line + an ATR-scaled threshold AND safe division of the trend-line change by ATR at least a positive threshold. Bear trend evidence = close < trend line − the corresponding ATR-scaled threshold AND normalized trend-line change at most the negative threshold. Trend state starts bullish and changes to the opposite state only after opposite evidence persists for a configured number of consecutive confirmed bars; absent evidence or evidence matching the current state resets the candidate count.
Calculation: Fast and slow ribbons = EMAs of shorter- and longer-lookback EMAs of (high + low + close) / 3, respectively, using the same final smoothing lookback. Momentum = shorter-lookback EMA(close) − longer-lookback EMA(close).
Calculation: Bull sweep = low < lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > that low. Bear sweep = high > highest high over the same lookback ending on the previous bar AND close < that high. Bull break = close > highest high over a shorter lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > open. Bear break = close < lowest low over that shorter lookback AND close < open.
Calculation: Bull votes = count of true conditions among close > open, close > previous close, momentum > previous momentum, fast ribbon > slow ribbon, bull ribbon state, bullish trend state, lower wick > body, and direction > 0. Bear votes = count of true conditions among close < open, close < previous close, momentum < previous momentum, fast ribbon < slow ribbon, bear ribbon state, bearish trend state, upper wick > body, and direction < 0.
Calculation: Bull acceptance = close > upper value boundary AND close > upper session control boundary AND direction at least a positive threshold AND body ratio at least a threshold AND close location at least an upper threshold. Bear acceptance = close < lower value boundary AND close < lower session control boundary AND direction at most the negative counterpart AND body ratio at least the same threshold AND close location at most the corresponding lower threshold.
Calculation: Bull reclaim = low at most lower session control boundary AND close > upper session control boundary AND direction at least a positive threshold AND lower wick > upper wick AND body ratio at least a threshold. Bear reclaim = high at least upper session control boundary AND close < lower session control boundary AND direction at most the negative counterpart AND upper wick > lower wick AND body ratio at least the same threshold.
Calculation: Bull liquidity = bull sweep AND bull break AND relative volume at least a threshold selected according to whether profile scale reaches a configured cutoff AND direction at least a positive threshold AND close > session control center. Bear liquidity = bear sweep AND bear break AND the same relative-volume condition AND direction at most the negative counterpart AND close < session control center.
Calculation: Bull structure = (bull acceptance OR bull reclaim OR bull liquidity) AND (high < upper session outer boundary OR close at least upper session outer boundary) AND bull votes at least a configured count. Bear structure = (bear acceptance OR bear reclaim OR bear liquidity) AND (low > lower session outer boundary OR close at most lower session outer boundary) AND bear votes at least the same count.
Calculation: Turn high and turn low = highest high and lowest low over a turn lookback including the current bar. Bull turn qualification = safe division of turn high − turn low by ATR at least a threshold AND age of turn low between configured minimum and maximum ages inclusive AND safe division of close − turn low by ATR at least a threshold AND bull votes at least a count lower than the structure vote count AND (bull break OR bull sweep) AND value half-width at least an ATR-scaled compression threshold. Bear turn qualification substitutes age of turn high, turn high − close, bear votes, and bear break OR bear sweep. Bull turn = bull turn qualification AND NOT bear turn qualification for exactly a configured consecutive count of confirmed bars; bear turn is symmetric.
Calculation: Bull ribbon transition = bull ribbon state AND previous bar was not in bull ribbon state AND trend state bullish AND direction at least a positive threshold AND close > volume center. Bear ribbon transition is symmetric. Bull trend transition = trend state changes from bearish to bullish AND bull ribbon state AND direction at least a positive threshold. Bear trend transition is symmetric.
Calculation: Bull integrated condition = close > open AND close > trend line AND close > volume center AND fast ribbon > slow ribbon AND momentum > previous momentum AND bull votes at least the structure vote count AND value half-width at least the ATR-scaled compression threshold. Bear integrated condition reverses the directional comparisons and uses bear votes.
Calculation: Buy request = close > open AND (bull structure OR bull turn OR bull ribbon transition OR bull trend transition OR bull integrated condition). Sale request = close < open AND (bear structure OR bear turn OR bear ribbon transition OR bear trend transition OR bear integrated condition). Signal state starts neutral and changes to bullish on an accepted buy request or bearish on an accepted sale request; it changes only when the request is opposite to the current state.
Long: Confirmed bar AND buy request AND signal state before this bar is neutral or bearish.
Short: Confirmed bar AND sale request AND signal state before this bar is neutral or bullish.
Long: Confirmed bar AND bull ribbon state.
Short: Confirmed bar AND bear ribbon state.
Long: Close at least volume center.
Short: Close < volume center.
