Calculation: VWAP zone = VWAP ± clamp(ATR × zone-width factor, minimum zone width, maximum zone width)
Long: Within the active session, close crosses from at or below VWAP to above VWAP; the setup remains valid; the lowest of the configured hold-period closes is above VWAP; a later candle overlaps the VWAP zone, closes above VWAP, and closes above its open; then a subsequent bar's high exceeds the prior bullish retest-confirmation candle's high; VWAP cross count over the configured lookback is below the chop threshold; and either the volume requirement is disabled or volume meets its configured elevated-volume threshold
Short: Within the active session, close crosses from at or above VWAP to below VWAP; the setup remains valid; the highest of the configured hold-period closes is below VWAP; a later candle overlaps the VWAP zone, closes below VWAP, and closes below its open; then a subsequent bar's low falls below the prior bearish retest-confirmation candle's low; VWAP cross count over the configured lookback is below the chop threshold; and either the volume requirement is disabled or volume meets its configured elevated-volume threshold
