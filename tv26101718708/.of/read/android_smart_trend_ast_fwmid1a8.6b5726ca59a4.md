Calculation: Standardized value = (value − SMA(value)) / standard deviation(value), or 0 when the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Range-normalized value = 100 × (value − rolling minimum) / (rolling maximum − rolling minimum), or 50 when the maximum equals the minimum.
Calculation: Core score = 0.40 × organ score + 0.25 × band score + 0.20 × ribbon score + 0.15 × RSI. Organ score is clamp(50 + 50 × EMA(2 × standardized change in close EMA + 1.5 × standardized close ROC + standardized signed volume + 0.5 × standardized directed ATR change) / 6, 0, 100); signed volume is volume when close rises, negative volume when close falls, otherwise 0; directed ATR change is the ATR change multiplied by 1 when close is above its EMA, otherwise −1. For the band score, smooth upper and lower bands with EMAs, where each band is midpoint price multiplied by 1 plus or minus the band percentage times the mirror factor; band score is clamp(50 + 50 × (close − band midpoint) / max(upper band − lower band, minimum price increment), 0, 100). Ribbon score is 65, 35, or 50 when the shorter close EMA is respectively above, below, or equal to the longer close EMA. Use a common lookback for the standardized components.
Calculation: PVM score = 0.40 × EMA(50 × (1 + correlation of close change and volume change)) + 0.35 × EMA(range-normalized ATR expansion × relative volume) + 0.25 × EMA(range-normalized close ROC). Replace unavailable correlation with 0; relative volume is volume / max(volume EMA, 1); ATR expansion is ATR / max(ATR EMA, minimum price increment). Use a common normalization lookback and a common smoothing length for the three components.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe core and PVM scores = 0.30 × fast-timeframe score + 0.35 × middle-timeframe score + 0.35 × macro-timeframe score. The three timeframes increase from fast to middle to macro and can each be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Use the preceding completed bar of each requested timeframe; on a higher timeframe carry its value across chart bars, and on a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar's preceding bar.
Calculation: Power = clamp((1 − PVM weight) × core power + PVM weight × PVM power, 0, 100) when the PVM boost is enabled, otherwise clamp(core power, 0, 100). With multi-timeframe power enabled, each component power is 0.45 × its chart-timeframe score + 0.55 × its multi-timeframe score; otherwise it is its chart-timeframe score.
Calculation: Trend direction = initially neutral; on a completed chart bar set it to long when power is at least the bullish threshold and the preceding direction is not long, and set it to short when power is at most the bearish threshold and the preceding direction is not short; otherwise retain the preceding direction. The bullish threshold is above the bearish threshold.
Long: Power >= bullish threshold AND preceding trend direction != long.
Short: Power <= bearish threshold AND preceding trend direction != short.
Long: Trend direction = long.
Short: Trend direction = short.
Long: Power >= strong power threshold.
Long: Power >= normal power threshold AND power < strong power threshold.
Short: Power < weak power threshold.
Long: Fast-timeframe core score > bullish threshold AND middle-timeframe core score > bullish threshold AND macro-timeframe core score > bullish threshold.
Short: Fast-timeframe core score < bearish threshold AND middle-timeframe core score < bearish threshold AND macro-timeframe core score < bearish threshold.
