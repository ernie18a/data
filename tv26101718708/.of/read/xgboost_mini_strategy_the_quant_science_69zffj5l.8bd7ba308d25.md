Calculation: P = probability (0 to 1, 0.5 until a model exists) that close rises over the next 2 bars, from a gradient-boosted tree classifier. The model is retrained every fixed number of bars on a fixed-size rolling window of past samples, using only data already completed. Each sample's label is 1 if close 2 bars after the sample bar > close at the sample bar, else 0. Features at each bar: RSI/100; (close - long-period EMA of close)/close; short-period rate of change of close/10; ATR/close; volume/SMA(volume) - 1; (close - lowest low over N bars)/(highest high over N bars - lowest low over N bars), or 0.5 if that range is 0. P is evaluated on the current bar's features. T is a probability threshold above 0.5.
Calculation: trailing stop = (highest high since entry, starting from the entry price) * (1 - fixed percentage); it can only rise while the position is open.
Long: P > T AND close > short-period SMA of close AND more than 1 bar has passed since the last position exit
Exit: long, P < 1 - T AND position unrealized profit > 0 (close > entry price) AND more than a few bars have passed since the most recent bar on which the Long condition was true
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop (not evaluated on the entry bar)
