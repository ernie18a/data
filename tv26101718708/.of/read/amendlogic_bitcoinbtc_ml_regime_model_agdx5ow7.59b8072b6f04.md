Calculation: Final upward probability = model probability when training sample count meets its minimum and hit rate over the longer reliability window meets its minimum; otherwise fallback probability. Optionally blend the selected probability with a sensing probability using a configurable weight.
Calculation: Model probability = logistic function of bias plus the weighted sum of 13 clipped features: z-scored 1-, 6-, and 21-bar log returns; (RSI(14)−50)/25; z-scored distance of close from EMA(50×daily-bars-per-chart-bar), divided by ATR(14); z-scored volume; 1-bar return feature × volume feature / 3; z-scored ATR(14)/close; 6-bar return feature × volatility feature / 3; z-scored DXY and TIP close-to-EMA(50×daily-bars-per-chart-bar) percentage ratios; z-scored close-to-EMA(200×daily-bars-per-chart-bar) percentage ratio; and DXY feature × 6-bar return feature / 3. Each z-score uses the same feature-normalization lookback; clip features to [−3,3]. Weights and bias are updated by walk-forward logistic gradient steps with L2 regularization, replaying the longer prediction horizon plus successive older bars per confirmed bar; each training target is whether the current close exceeds the close at the prediction horizon. The hit rate is the moving average over resolved predictions of whether the model probability, evaluated at the prediction horizon, correctly classified the direction of the close change.
Calculation: Fallback probability = 0.5 + 0.2 × (2×daily trend direction + DXY direction + TIP direction)/4, where daily trend direction is +1 when the close-to-long-EMA ratio is positive and −1 otherwise, DXY direction is +1 when its close-to-EMA ratio is negative and −1 otherwise, and TIP direction is +1 when its close-to-EMA ratio is positive and −1 otherwise.
Calculation: Sensing probability = logistic function of 0.8 × (1-bar return feature + return-feature×volume-feature/3)/2.
Calculation: Anomaly = absolute 1-bar-return z-score ≥ anomaly threshold OR volume z-score ≥ anomaly threshold OR volatility z-score ≥ anomaly threshold.
Calculation: Daily trend = +1 when close is above the long-term EMA scaled to daily bars; otherwise −1. Use the current chart bar's close and EMA values.
Long: position is flat AND bars held ≥ minimum holding period AND final upward probability ≥ long-entry threshold AND NOT anomaly AND (daily-trend filter is disabled OR daily trend = +1)
Short: position is flat AND bars held ≥ minimum holding period AND final upward probability ≤ short-entry threshold AND NOT anomaly AND (daily-trend filter is disabled OR daily trend = −1)
Exit: long, final upward probability < long-exit threshold AND bars held ≥ minimum holding period
Exit: short, final upward probability > short-exit threshold AND bars held ≥ minimum holding period
