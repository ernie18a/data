Calculation: Liquidity pools = Maintain separate ordered pools for confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows. Confirm each pivot after the same number of bars on both sides. On each bar, first register the chart pivot, then the higher-timeframe pivot of the same side, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar with its pivot result carried forward until the next completed higher-timeframe bar. Process highs before lows. Register pivots only when ATR is available. Merge a chart pivot into the first pool within an ATR-based tolerance; increment touches, add the current bar's relative volume, set price to the touch-weighted average of its old price and the pivot, and update its last-activity bar. Otherwise create a fresh pool with one touch. Relative volume is current volume divided by its volume SMA, capped at a limit; use a fallback value if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. A chart pivot receives a higher-timeframe boost when the carried-forward same-side pivot is within a wider ATR-based tolerance. For each higher-timeframe pivot result, boost the first pool within that wider tolerance, or create a fresh, boosted pool with one touch and the current relative volume; boosting an existing pool does not change its price, touches, or last-activity bar. Pool density is weighted touches plus weighted average relative volume plus the largest boost received. Higher-timeframe registration and boosts are optional.
Calculation: Pool state and retention = A fresh pool becomes tapped when a later pivot merges into it or a bar spans its price. A fresh or tapped high pool is raided when high reaches or exceeds its price plus an ATR-based buffer and, if close confirmation is enabled, close is above its price; a fresh or tapped low pool is raided when low reaches or falls below its price minus the buffer and, if enabled, close is below its price. Record the raid bar and update last activity on touches and raids. A pivot merged into a raided pool within the restack window makes it restacked when restack tracking is enabled; restacked pools cannot be raided again. Perform registration and raids before retention. After the decisions on each bar, remove pools inactive beyond the age limit; if a side exceeds its pool limit, repeatedly remove the first pool with the lowest density after subtracting a fixed penalty from raided and restacked pools. If multiple pools on one side are raided on a bar, use the last raided pool in pool order for that side's decisions.
Long: The last raided low pool on the bar has density at or above the minimum.
Short: The last raided high pool on the bar has density at or above the minimum.
Long: A low pool is raided AND another fresh or tapped low pool has a price below the last raided low pool and density at or above the minimum.
Short: A high pool is raided AND another fresh or tapped high pool has a price above the last raided high pool and density at or above the minimum.
