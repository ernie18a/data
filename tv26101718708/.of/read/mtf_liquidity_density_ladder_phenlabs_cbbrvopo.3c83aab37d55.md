Calculation: Confirmed chart-timeframe swing high/low = a high/low with equal, parameterized lookbacks on both sides; use only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe swing high/low = the same pivot calculation on a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = register confirmed swing highs as buy-side pools and swing lows as sell-side pools; merge same-side levels within a parameterized ATR tolerance, updating the level to their touch-weighted mean. Also register higher-timeframe pivots, merging within the higher-timeframe tolerance. A pool’s density is touch count × touch weight + mean relative volume × volume weight + higher-timeframe boost, where each touch’s relative volume is min(volume / volume SMA, 4), and aligned higher-timeframe pivots add the boost.
Calculation: Buy-side raid = pool high ≥ pool level + ATR × raid buffer AND close > pool level; sell-side raid = pool low ≤ pool level − ATR × raid buffer AND close < pool level. The close-beyond condition may be disabled.
Long: Buy-side raid AND pool density ≥ minimum density OR a buy-side raid occurs AND the densest unraided buy-side pool above the raided level has density ≥ minimum density OR a new confirmed swing high merges into a buy-side pool raided within the restack window.
Short: Sell-side raid AND pool density ≥ minimum density OR a sell-side raid occurs AND the densest unraided sell-side pool below the raided level has density ≥ minimum density OR a new confirmed swing low merges into a sell-side pool raided within the restack window.
