Calculation: Price, high reference, low reference, and VWAP source = configurable series derived from the available bar fields.
Calculation: Z-score of a series = its value minus its rolling simple moving average, divided by its rolling population standard deviation; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Factor scores = Z-scores over a common normalization window of: price minus session VWAP; high minus low multiplied after standardization by the sign of price change over a configurable lookback, using positive sign when unchanged; signed efficiency ratio, defined as price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price changes over that lookback, using zero when the denominator is zero; RSI; price minus its linear regression fitted value divided by ATR, using zero when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive; Know Sure Thing; and cumulative signed volume minus its EMA over the normalization window, where signed volume is volume multiplied by twice the close's fractional position within the high-low range minus one, with fractional position one-half when the range is zero. Also include the Z-score of SAR extension multiplied after standardization by trend direction: direction is the sign of price minus Parabolic SAR; extension is high reference minus SAR for positive direction, SAR minus low reference for negative direction, and zero otherwise; divide extension by ATR, clamp it between zero and a configurable upper bound, and use zero when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Include only enabled factors; include VWAP only when cumulative volume is positive and VWAP is available, and volume delta only when cumulative volume is positive. The external-reference basis factor cannot be calculated from the supplied data. Exclude unavailable factor scores.
Calculation: Composite = arithmetic mean of available factor scores, or zero when none are available.
Calculation: Composite overbought and extreme thresholds = separate configurable positive multipliers of the composite's rolling standard deviation when adaptive scaling is enabled and that standard deviation is available and positive; otherwise use separate fixed positive thresholds, with the extreme threshold greater than the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Consensus requirement = configurable minimum factor count, optionally selected by chart timeframe, capped at the available factor count and floored at one.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high-reference pivot high or low-reference pivot low with equal configurable numbers of bars on each side; evaluate on its confirmation bar. Compare each pivot only with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, even if that preceding pivot produced no signal.
Calculation: Pivot composite = at a high or low price pivot, respectively the highest or lowest composite over the complete price-pivot window, including the confirmation bar; alternatively, the composite at the pivot bar. Use the same selected method for successive pivots.
Long: Available factor count > 0 AND count of factor scores <= negative fixed factor-extreme threshold >= consensus requirement.
Short: Available factor count > 0 AND count of factor scores >= positive fixed factor-extreme threshold >= consensus requirement.
Long: Composite <= negative composite overbought threshold.
Short: Composite >= composite overbought threshold.
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding low pivot <= maximum pivot gap AND current low reference < preceding low reference AND current pivot composite > preceding pivot composite AND their difference >= a configurable multiplier of the composite's rolling standard deviation at confirmation AND either the extreme filter is disabled OR current pivot composite <= negative composite overbought threshold at confirmation.
Long: Hidden divergence is enabled AND a price pivot low is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding low pivot <= maximum pivot gap AND current low reference > preceding low reference AND current pivot composite < preceding pivot composite AND their difference >= a configurable multiplier of the composite's rolling standard deviation at confirmation.
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding high pivot <= maximum pivot gap AND current high reference > preceding high reference AND current pivot composite < preceding pivot composite AND their difference >= a configurable multiplier of the composite's rolling standard deviation at confirmation AND either the extreme filter is disabled OR current pivot composite >= composite overbought threshold at confirmation.
Short: Hidden divergence is enabled AND a price pivot high is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding high pivot <= maximum pivot gap AND current high reference < preceding high reference AND current pivot composite > preceding pivot composite AND their difference >= a configurable multiplier of the composite's rolling standard deviation at confirmation.
Long: A composite pivot low with equal configurable numbers of bars on each side is confirmed AND its value <= negative composite extreme threshold at confirmation.
Short: A composite pivot high with equal configurable numbers of bars on each side is confirmed AND its value >= composite extreme threshold at confirmation.
