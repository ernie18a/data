Calculation: Price increment = a configurable minimum price increment; use it as the floor wherever a range, ATR divisor, round step, or price spacing would otherwise be smaller.
Calculation: Volume baseline = SMA of volume with the shorter volume lookback; the longer volume baseline uses the longer lookback. Before either SMA is available, use current volume for the shorter baseline and the shorter baseline for the longer baseline. Relative volume = volume / shorter baseline, with a fallback of one when the divisor is zero. Volume multiplier = the shorter baseline / longer baseline, clamped to its configured bounds, with a fallback of one.
Calculation: Pressure = EMA of the rolling sum of volume × clamp((close − open) / bar range, −1, 1) divided by rolling volume, with the current bar used before the rolling sums are available and zero used for division by zero. Alternatively, use lower-timeframe intrabar pressure: sum the same volume-weighted candle-body fraction across all available lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the still-forming lower-timeframe bar, and divide by their total volume. The hybrid is the weighted average of chart pressure and intrabar pressure, with weight equal to the configured intrabar weight × intrabar quality. Intrabar quality is the clamped sum of the absolute difference between the counts of rising and falling intrabars divided by the intrabar count and a configured multiple of intrabar participation; participation is the intrabar count divided by a configured count, clamped to one. If no intrabars are available, use chart pressure and zero intrabar quality. Bar range is max(high − low, price increment).
Calculation: Regime = high volatility when ATR / its longer SMA exceeds its threshold; otherwise low liquidity when relative volume is below its threshold; otherwise breakout when volume and bar range each exceed their respective baseline multiples; otherwise trending when ADX and the absolute difference between positive and negative directional indicators exceed their thresholds; otherwise ranging when ADX and relative ATR are below their thresholds; otherwise balanced. The low-liquidity, trending, and ranging conditions are also evaluated independently for scoring. Regime quality is a configurable score selected in this order: low liquidity, high volatility, ranging, trending, otherwise balanced.
Calculation: Synthetic depth = for each side and each configured level index, place a price at close plus or minus spacing × (index + 1) × (1 + index × spacing-growth coefficient), where spacing is the greater of an ATR multiple and a price-increment multiple. Depth at each level is (volume baseline × depth multiplier × Gaussian of distance from close in ATR units × round-number multiplier × side-specific pressure multiplier × side-specific OBV multiplier × regime multiplier + liquidity memory) × volume multiplier. The Gaussian is exp(−0.5 × (distance / decay width)^2). The round-number multiplier combines Gaussian proximity to the nearest configured minor and major round steps, with separate weights and widths, and is clamped; the step can be configured or derived from the order of magnitude of close. The bid pressure multiplier rises with pressure and the ask multiplier falls with pressure, both clamped. The OBV multiplier favors bids when OBV is above its SMA and favors asks otherwise. Liquidity memory sums, over a configured lookback, the volumes of bars whose ranges contain the level, weighted by recency and by the close's position within each bar, using the lower-to-close fraction for bids and the close-to-high fraction for asks. Apply configured calibration multipliers to the wall threshold, round-number strength, and memory length. Recompute the levels and depths at the configured refresh interval; on other bars retain level prices but set current depth totals and maxima to zero and the selected level index to the first level.
Calculation: Wall = the maximum-depth synthetic level on each side. Its strength ratio is maximum depth / average depth on that side, or zero if the average is zero. A bid wall is confirmed when its maximum depth exceeds its average depth × the wall threshold AND its strength ratio exceeds the ask strength ratio × a comparison multiplier; an ask wall uses the symmetric comparison. Track each side's active wall and age: on a confirmed wall, increment age and average its price with the active price if the two prices differ by no more than a configured spacing multiple; otherwise replace the active price and reset age to one. On an unconfirmed wall, decrement age toward zero. A wall is stable when it is confirmed AND its age reaches the stability threshold.
Calculation: Historical wall = maintain a bounded newest-first list of stable bid and ask walls. On each completed chart bar decay all stored strengths, then add each stable wall or merge it into the first same-side wall within a configured spacing multiple. On merge, set price to the strength-weighted average of the stored and current wall prices and strength to the greater of the current strength and the decayed stored strength plus a configured fraction of current strength. Remove oldest list entries beyond the configured capacity. Select the strongest stored bid at or below close and the strongest stored ask at or above close; these are the historical bid and ask.
Calculation: Bid absorption = low is within an ATR-multiple distance of the current bid wall OR the historical bid, AND volume is at least its baseline × the absorption-volume multiplier, AND lower wick exceeds body × the wick multiplier, AND close exceeds low plus a configured fraction of bar range. Ask absorption is symmetric using high, the ask walls, upper wick, and close below high minus that fraction.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low is below the current bid wall OR historical bid by more than a configured ATR multiple, AND close is above the same wall. Bearish sweep = high is above the current ask wall OR historical ask by more than that ATR multiple, AND close is below the same wall.
Calculation: Directional score = transform a clamped weighted sum to a score centered on its neutral midpoint. Its components are pressure; the clamped logarithm of total bid depth / total ask depth normalized by the logarithm of the imbalance threshold; the clamped difference between bid and ask wall strength ratios divided by the greater ratio or one; twice the difference between the fraction of rising candles in a configured lookback and one half; a positive or negative OBV component according to whether OBV is above its SMA; a positive component below a lower RSI threshold or negative component above a higher RSI threshold; a positive bid-absorption component or otherwise negative ask-absorption component; a positive bullish-sweep component or otherwise negative bearish-sweep component; and, when trending, a positive or negative component according to whether positive directional indicator exceeds negative directional indicator. Use configured component weights and magnitudes. Division by zero in the depth ratio falls back to one.
Calculation: Confidence = the clamped sum of configured agreement points for matching signs between the directional score's untransformed weighted sum and each of pressure, normalized imbalance, wall difference, and candle trend; points when OBV direction agrees with whether that sum is positive; intrabar quality points when intrabars exist or fallback points otherwise; points for either absorption and for either sweep; and regime quality multiplied by its configured weight.
Long: close >= previous close.
Short: close < previous close.
Long: OBV > SMA of OBV.
Short: OBV <= SMA of OBV.
Long: RSI < lower RSI threshold.
Short: RSI > upper RSI threshold.
Long: total bid depth / total ask depth > imbalance threshold.
Short: total bid depth / total ask depth < reciprocal of imbalance threshold.
Long: bid wall is stable.
Short: ask wall is stable.
Long: bid absorption is true.
Short: ask absorption is true.
Long: bullish sweep is true.
Short: bearish sweep is true.
Long: directional score >= long threshold AND confidence >= minimum confidence AND (noise filter is disabled OR (volume >= volume baseline × minimum-volume multiplier AND pressure > negative pressure threshold AND (bid wall is stable OR bid absorption is true OR bullish sweep is true) AND relative volume >= low-liquidity threshold)).
Short: directional score <= short threshold AND confidence >= minimum confidence AND (noise filter is disabled OR (volume >= volume baseline × minimum-volume multiplier AND pressure < positive pressure threshold AND (ask wall is stable OR ask absorption is true OR bearish sweep is true) AND relative volume >= low-liquidity threshold)).
Exit: long, bid wall is confirmed AND close < current bid wall − ATR × invalidation multiplier.
Exit: short, ask wall is confirmed AND close > current ask wall + ATR × invalidation multiplier.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × target multiplier OR low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars.
Exit: short, low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × target multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars.
