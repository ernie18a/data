Calculation: Volume-weighted smoothed average = initialize to the source; thereafter update as (previous average × (length − 1) + source × relative volume) / (length − 1 + relative volume), where relative volume is volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when unavailable.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when the shorter-length volume-weighted smoothed average of (high + low) / 2 is greater than the longer-length average; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Bear maturity = increment a counter on each bearish bar; on each bullish bar either reset it to zero or, if debounce is enabled, subtract a decay amount with a floor of zero. Maturity is reached when the counter is at least its threshold.
Calculation: Spread = volume-weighted smoothed average of (shorter-length average − longer-length average) / ATR.
Calculation: Penetration bands = previous bar's rolling lowest low minus ATR × lower multiplier, and previous bar's rolling highest high plus ATR × upper multiplier. For each side, maintain the most recent configured number of strictly positive penetrations beyond the corresponding previous-bar extreme, divided by the current ATR. Include the current bar's penetration before calculating its band. Until the minimum sample count is reached use a default multiplier; thereafter use the mean penetration × sensitivity, bounded by minimum and maximum multipliers.
Calculation: Price-change z-score = z-score over a lookback of (close − close from a separate lookback ago) / ATR; use zero when its standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Divergence = simultaneous confirmed price and RSI pivots with the same left and right pivot widths, compared with the previous simultaneous pivot of the same type within a maximum number of confirmation bars. A bullish divergence has a lower price low and higher RSI low; a bearish divergence has a higher price high and lower RSI high. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Buy spacing = ATR × base separation × (1 + min(maximum throttle multiplier − 1, max(0, −spread) × throttle scale)) when the buy throttle is enabled. Reset the last buy price on every bullish bar; otherwise record the close of each executed buy.
Calculation: Sell cooldown = round(base cooldown × (1 + min(maximum throttle multiplier − 1, max(0, spread) × throttle scale))).
Calculation: Position cycle = count executed buys and sells since the last cycle reset. Before executing a qualifying buy, reset both counts if the cycle contains a sell. Buys add to the long position at the signal close; sells reduce the remaining position at the signal close by the lesser of a maximum percentage and base sell percentage × min(depth ceiling, 1 + max(high − upper band, 0) / ATR / sell-depth scale), using zero depth when ATR is not positive.
Long: Bearish regime AND maturity reached AND low <= lower penetration band AND (buy spread gate disabled OR spread <= buy spread threshold).
Long: Bearish regime AND price-change z-score <= buy z-score threshold.
Long: Bearish regime AND maturity reached AND (low <= lower penetration band AND (buy spread gate disabled OR spread <= buy spread threshold) OR price-change z-score <= buy z-score threshold) AND (buy throttle disabled OR last buy price unavailable OR abs(low − last buy price) >= buy spacing) AND (cycle sell count > 0 OR cycle buy count < maximum buys per cycle).
Long: Bullish divergence AND divergence display enabled.
Short: Bearish divergence AND divergence display enabled.
Exit: long, bullish regime AND high >= upper penetration band AND (sell spread gate disabled OR spread >= sell spread threshold).
Exit: long, bullish regime AND price-change z-score >= sell z-score threshold.
Exit: long, bullish regime AND (high >= upper penetration band AND (sell spread gate disabled OR spread >= sell spread threshold) OR price-change z-score >= sell z-score threshold) AND (sell throttle disabled OR cycle sell count = 0 OR last sell bar unavailable OR bars since last sell >= sell cooldown) AND position quantity > 0.
