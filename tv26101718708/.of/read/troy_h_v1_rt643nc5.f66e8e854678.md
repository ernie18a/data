Calculation: Volume-weighted SMMA = recursively update a source as (previous value × (length − 1) + source × normalized volume) / (length − 1 + normalized volume), where normalized volume = volume / SMA(volume); initialize with the source. Fast and anchor SMMAs use shorter and longer lengths, respectively.
Calculation: Spread = volume-weighted SMMA of (fast SMMA − anchor SMMA) / ATR.
Calculation: Adaptive lower and upper bands = the previous-bar Donchian low minus ATR × its learned penetration multiplier, and the previous-bar Donchian high plus ATR × its learned penetration multiplier. Each multiplier is the sensitivity-scaled average of recent positive wick penetrations beyond that boundary, measured in ATR units and clamped to bounds; use a default multiplier until the minimum sample count is reached.
Calculation: PnL Z = z-score of (close − close from the PnL lookback bars ago) / ATR, using a longer lookback for the mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed price pivot low while its RSI pivot low is higher, with the pivots no more than the maximum lookback apart. Use the same pivot parameters for price and RSI.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed price pivot high while its RSI pivot high is lower, with the pivots no more than the maximum lookback apart. Use the same pivot parameters for price and RSI.
Long: Bullish divergence OR ((low ≤ adaptive lower band OR PnL Z ≤ buy threshold) AND fast SMMA ≤ anchor SMMA AND bear-regime maturity counter ≥ maturity threshold AND (buy throttle disabled OR no prior buy price since the latest bullish regime OR |low − last buy close| ≥ ATR × buy separation × (1 + min(throttle cap − 1, max(0, −Spread) × throttle scale))) AND (a prior sell occurred in the current cycle OR buy count in the current cycle < buy cap)); on a band-touch branch, also require Spread ≤ buy threshold if the buy spread gate is enabled. Count bear-regime bars; on bullish bars reset the count, or reduce it by the configured decay if maturity debounce is enabled. A buy closes at the signal bar close and updates the last buy price.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, ((high ≥ adaptive upper band AND fast SMMA > anchor SMMA AND Spread ≥ sell threshold if the sell spread gate is enabled) OR PnL Z ≥ sell threshold) AND (sell throttle disabled OR no prior sell in the current cycle OR enough bars have elapsed since the prior sell, where cooldown = round(base cooldown × (1 + min(throttle cap − 1, max(0, Spread) × throttle scale)))) AND long position quantity > 0. On the band-touch branch, require the high to reach the adaptive upper band. A cycle-closing buy resets cycle buy and sell counts before applying the buy-count cap.
