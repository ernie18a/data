Calculation: Keltner Channel bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± SMA(true range, same lookback) × multiplier
Long: each of the four higher-timeframe bars has open < close
Short: each of the four higher-timeframe bars has open ≥ close
Exit: long, each of the four higher-timeframe bars has open ≥ close OR close < upper Keltner Channel band
Exit: short, each of the four higher-timeframe bars has open < close OR close > lower Keltner Channel band
