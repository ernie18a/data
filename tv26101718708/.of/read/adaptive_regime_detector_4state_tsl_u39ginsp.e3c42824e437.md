Calculation: Trend score = percentile rank of a combined efficiency ratio and ATR-normalized absolute EMA slope over a lookback
Calculation: Volatility score = average of the percentile ranks of ATR and Bollinger Band width over a lookback
Calculation: Confirmed regime = trending when trend score meets its threshold; volatile when volatility score meets its threshold; regime changes only after the new state persists for a confirmation period
Long: Confirmed regime is trending AND EMA is above its lagged value
Short: Confirmed regime is trending AND EMA is below its lagged value
