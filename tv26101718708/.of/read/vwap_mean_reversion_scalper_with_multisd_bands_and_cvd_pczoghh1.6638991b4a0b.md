Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(hlc3 × volume) / cumulative volume, resetting at each anchor boundary
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative volume-weighted hlc3² / cumulative volume − Anchored VWAP²)
Calculation: Upper and lower SD bands = Anchored VWAP ± VWAP standard deviation × each band multiplier; the three multipliers are ordered from lower to higher
Calculation: Volume delta = sum over lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar of volume × sign(close − open), including any bar still forming
Long: close > open AND (low ≤ lower SD 1 band OR (low ≤ Anchored VWAP AND high ≥ Anchored VWAP)) AND Volume delta > delta threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR × range multiplier) AND (lower SD band touched OR (VWAP signals enabled AND VWAP touched)) AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown AND (clean signal mode disabled OR last signal direction is not long OR price has intersected the zone between the SD 1 bands since the last signal OR current lower touched level > last signal level)
Short: close < open AND (high ≥ upper SD 1 band OR (low ≤ Anchored VWAP AND high ≥ Anchored VWAP)) AND Volume delta < −delta threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR × range multiplier) AND (upper SD band touched OR (VWAP signals enabled AND VWAP touched)) AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown AND (clean signal mode disabled OR last signal direction is not short OR price has intersected the zone between the SD 1 bands since the last signal OR current upper touched level > last signal level)
