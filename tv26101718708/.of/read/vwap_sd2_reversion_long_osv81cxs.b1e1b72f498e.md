Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 since the session reset; lower band = session VWAP minus a multiplier times the corresponding volume-weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: Oscillator = RSI of close, or StochRSI %K, defined as the simple moving average of 100 times (RSI minus its rolling minimum) / (its rolling maximum minus its rolling minimum). Its comparison level is an RSI threshold, a StochRSI threshold, or StochRSI %D, the simple moving average of %K, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: MACD comparison level = MACD signal line or zero, according to the selected mode; MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA of close.
Calculation: Dip = close below the lower band AND session VWAP minus lower band at least a minimum price distance AND, if enabled, ADX of the last completed higher-timeframe bar above or below its threshold according to the selected filter direction.
Calculation: Setup state = initially idle. On each bar, first refresh the timeout bar if waiting for the oscillator and Dip is true; then reset to idle if the current bar is more than the confirmation window after the timeout bar. If idle and Dip is true, enter the oscillator-waiting state and set both the dip bar and timeout bar to the current bar. If waiting for the oscillator and oscillator confirmation is true, enter the MACD-waiting state and set the timeout bar to the current bar. If waiting for MACD and MACD confirmation is true, produce a long trigger and reset to idle. Apply these steps in order, allowing multiple transitions on one bar, including while a position is open.
Calculation: Oscillator confirmation = in strict mode, oscillator crosses above its comparison level; otherwise, oscillator is above its comparison level AND its most recent upward cross was within the confirmation window AND on or after the dip bar. MACD confirmation is defined identically using MACD and its selected comparison level. A cross above means the current value is greater and the previous value was less than or equal to the comparison level.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = a fixed price distance, or a multiple of ATR measured on the first bar where the long position is open. ATR target distance = a separate multiple of ATR measured on that same bar; both distances remain fixed for the trade.
Calculation: Breakeven is armed for the rest of the trade once an open-position bar has high at or above entry price plus a trigger distance. The stop is entry price minus initial stop distance, or, when breakeven is enabled and armed, the greater of that stop and entry price plus a lock distance; subtract the spread distance from the resulting stop.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus a fixed target distance, or the greater of session VWAP minus a buffer and entry price plus a minimum profit distance; in ATR mode, use the greatest of those latter two levels and entry price plus the frozen ATR target distance. Subtract the spread distance from the target. Submit updated stop and target orders after each position bar closes for subsequent execution.
Long: Dip is true.
Long: Setup state transitions from waiting for the oscillator to waiting for MACD.
Long: Long trigger is true; if flat, submit a market entry for the next bar's open.
Exit: long, price reaches or falls below the active stop.
Exit: long, price reaches or rises above the active target.
