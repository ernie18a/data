Calculation: Liquidity levels = Select one source: the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using equally many bars on either side; the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; or the high and low accumulated during each configured session. Replace both levels when a higher-timeframe bar starts or a session ends; replace each pivot level independently when its pivot is confirmed. Mark each new level unbroken. If sessions end together, use the first configured session. Session accumulators reset when selected at session end.
Calculation: Reversal state = Initially neutral. On a new higher-timeframe or session level, reset to neutral. When close exceeds an unbroken high level and upper wick divided by bar range is at least the minimum wick fraction, mark that level broken and track the bar's high, level and wick fraction as an upward sweep. When close falls below an unbroken low level and lower wick divided by bar range is at least the minimum wick fraction, mark that level broken and track the bar's low, level and wick fraction as a downward sweep. Process the downward sweep second if both occur. While tracking an upward sweep, update its extreme whenever high is at least the tracked extreme; otherwise finish the sweep on the confirmed bar. While tracking a downward sweep, update its extreme whenever low is at most the tracked extreme; otherwise finish the sweep on the confirmed bar. A finished sweep cannot signal again unless a new sweep starts.
Calculation: Average daily range = Mean high minus low over a lookback of completed higher-timeframe daily bars, using the value available from the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Strength score = At sweep completion, compute ATR, falling back to the cumulative mean bar range until ATR is available; a short-period and a longer-period close EMA; and the percentile rank of ATR over its lookback. Let direction be +1 for a downward sweep and -1 for an upward sweep, and clamp each factor to [0,1]: trend = ((direction * (long EMA - short EMA) / ATR) + trend offset) / trend scale; wick = sweep-bar rejection-wick fraction / wick scale; calm = 1 - ATR percentile rank / percentile scale; proximity = 1 - max(direction * (close - long EMA) / ATR, 0) / proximity scale; depth = absolute difference between the final sweep extreme and swept level / (ATR * depth scale). Score = score scale times the weighted mean of these factors, or zero if their weights sum to zero. Strength tier is determined by comparing score with ordered tier thresholds.
Long: A downward sweep finishes AND average daily range is positive AND strength tier is at least the minimum tier.
Short: An upward sweep finishes AND average daily range is positive AND strength tier is at least the minimum tier.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= entry price - stop distance OR (the first target has been reached on this or a previous post-entry bar AND high >= entry price + second target distance) OR bars since entry >= expiry).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= entry price + stop distance OR (the first target has been reached on this or a previous post-entry bar AND low <= entry price - second target distance) OR bars since entry >= expiry).
Calculation: Target and stop distances = Separate fractions of the average daily range at entry. The long first target is reached when a post-entry high >= entry price + first target distance; the short first target is reached when a post-entry low <= entry price - first target distance. If the stop and first target are reached on the same bar before the first target was previously reached, the stop takes precedence.
