Calculation: Frozen active value = On periodic refreshes, build a volume profile from prior completed chart bars over a longer lookback: distribute each bar’s footprint volume across five equally spaced prices in its footprint value area and add extra volume at its footprint POC; smooth the profile, separate clusters at intervening valleys, and calculate each cluster’s POC and 70% value area. Select the cluster containing the previous completed bar’s close, or the cluster with the nearest POC if none contains it. Freeze its VAH, VAL, POC, and adjacent cluster POCs between refreshes.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current footprint total volume divided by the average footprint total volume over a longer lookback of prior completed chart bars; use candle volume when footprint data is unavailable.
Calculation: Range expansion = Current bar’s high-low range divided by ATR.
Calculation: Footprint delta percentage = Current footprint delta divided by current footprint total volume.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish participation scores = Weighted combinations of normalized relative volume and, respectively, positive or negative footprint delta percentage.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish acceptance scores = Weighted combinations of normalized current footprint volume share outside the frozen value area on the respective side, whether current footprint POC is beyond that side’s value boundary, and the fraction of recent closes beyond that boundary.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish total scores = Weighted combinations of the respective participation score, range expansion score, and acceptance score.
Long: On a confirmed bar with valid frozen value and footprint data, the state is balance or compression and (low <= frozen VAL + ATR tolerance AND close >= frozen VAL AND close > open AND bearish total score < acceptance threshold), OR the state is balance, compression, or rotating up and high > frozen VAH AND close > frozen VAH, OR the state is probing down or accepted down and close >= frozen VAL, OR the state is accepted up and high >= next higher cluster POC minus ATR tolerance.
Short: On a confirmed bar with valid frozen value and footprint data, the state is balance or compression and (high >= frozen VAH - ATR tolerance AND close <= frozen VAH AND close < open AND bullish total score < acceptance threshold), OR the state is balance, compression, or rotating down and low < frozen VAL AND close < frozen VAL, OR the state is probing up or accepted up and close <= frozen VAH, OR the state is accepted down and low <= next lower cluster POC plus ATR tolerance.
