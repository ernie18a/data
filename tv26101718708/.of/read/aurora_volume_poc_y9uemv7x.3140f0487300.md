Calculation: Up volume and down volume = sum the volumes of completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar whose close is respectively above or below open; split doji volume equally. If lower-timeframe bars are unavailable, apply the same rule to the chart bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = chart-bar volume divided by the simple average volume of a lookback of previous completed chart bars.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low; lower wick fraction = (min(open, close) minus low) divided by high minus low; upper wick fraction = (high minus max(open, close)) divided by high minus low. If high equals low, body fraction is 1 and both wick fractions are 0.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = relative volume >= minimum reversal volume ratio AND body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND down volume > up volume AND lower wick fraction >= minimum wick fraction AND lower wick fraction >= upper wick fraction multiplied by wick dominance factor.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = relative volume >= minimum reversal volume ratio AND body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND up volume > down volume AND upper wick fraction >= minimum wick fraction AND upper wick fraction >= lower wick fraction multiplied by wick dominance factor.
Calculation: Bullish switch = up volume >= down volume multiplied by pressure switch factor AND up volume > 0 AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish switch = down volume >= up volume multiplied by pressure switch factor AND down volume > 0 AND close < open.
Long: Relative volume is defined AND (up volume > down volume OR (up volume = down volume AND close >= open)).
Short: Relative volume is defined AND (down volume > up volume OR (up volume = down volume AND close < open)).
Long: Bullish switch AND ((bullish candidate on the previous bar AND close >= midpoint of that bar's open and close) OR (two-bar confirmation is enabled AND bullish candidate two bars ago AND no bullish confirmation of that candidate occurred on the previous bar AND close >= midpoint of that candidate bar's open and close)).
Short: Bearish switch AND ((bearish candidate on the previous bar AND close <= midpoint of that bar's open and close) OR (two-bar confirmation is enabled AND bearish candidate two bars ago AND no bearish confirmation of that candidate occurred on the previous bar AND close <= midpoint of that candidate bar's open and close)).
