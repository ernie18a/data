Calculation: Chart MA = EMA, SMA, or HMA of chart-timeframe close, selected by parameter.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe close, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time. Use the same EMA and timeframe for the trend, distance, and slope comparisons.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA slope angle = arctangent(((current higher-timeframe EMA - higher-timeframe EMA a parameterized number of higher-timeframe bars earlier) / earlier EMA * 100) / number of higher-timeframe bars), expressed in degrees; use zero for the percentage change if the earlier EMA is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive filters active = force-on OR (NOT force-off AND (adaptive mode disabled OR ATR / close below a volatility threshold)); force-on takes precedence over force-off.
Calculation: Setup = on a close crossing either direction through the chart MA, start or reset the setup, set its high and low to the current high and low, and reset its pullback count and completed-pullback flag. While a setup exists and its completed-pullback flag was false at the start of the bar, extend its high and low with the current high and low. During the setup, count every bearish candle whose close is above the chart MA and every bullish candle whose close is below it; the candles need not be consecutive. Once the count reaches a threshold, mark the pullback complete. Once the pullback is complete and abs(close - chart MA) / (setup high - setup low) is at least a depth threshold and (adaptive filters are inactive OR abs(close - chart MA) / chart MA is below a distance threshold), set the completed-pullback flag. Use a denominator of one if the setup high equals the setup low. A new MA cross resets the setup; a long or short entry signal ends it. Update the setup before evaluating entry signals.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA of volume; use zero if the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Entry stop = setup low at the long entry signal or setup high at the short entry signal, fixed at that signal. Target = signal-bar close plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a parameterized reward-to-risk multiplier times the distance from signal-bar close to entry stop.
Long: Setup exists AND completed-pullback flag is set AND close > setup high AND (adaptive filters inactive OR relative volume >= volume threshold) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (higher-timeframe distance filter disabled OR abs(close - higher-timeframe EMA) / higher-timeframe EMA < distance threshold) AND (slope filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA slope angle >= positive angle threshold).
Short: Setup exists AND completed-pullback flag is set AND close < setup low AND (adaptive filters inactive OR relative volume >= volume threshold) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (higher-timeframe distance filter disabled OR abs(close - higher-timeframe EMA) / higher-timeframe EMA < distance threshold) AND (slope filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA slope angle <= negative angle threshold).
Long: close > higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: close <= higher-timeframe EMA.
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= target.
