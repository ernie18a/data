Calculation: Zero-lag MACD = (2 × moving average of close over the short lookback − moving average of that moving average over the short lookback) − (2 × moving average of close over the long lookback − moving average of that moving average over the long lookback); the long lookback is longer than the short lookback
Calculation: Signal line = 2 × EMA of zero-lag MACD over the signal lookback − EMA of that EMA over the signal lookback
Calculation: MACD histogram = zero-lag MACD − signal line
Calculation: Kijun-sen = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over its lookback
Calculation: Ease of Movement = moving average of (10,000 × change in typical price × (high − low) / volume), where typical price = (high + low) / 2
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Long: zero-lag MACD crosses above signal line AND zero-lag MACD < MACD histogram AND close > Kijun-sen AND Ease of Movement > 0
Short: zero-lag MACD crosses below signal line AND zero-lag MACD > MACD histogram AND close < Kijun-sen AND Ease of Movement < 0
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
