Calculation: Adaptive length = optionally use a fixed length; otherwise, apply the Ehlers Homodyne Discriminator to (high + low) / 2, with missing historical values initialized to zero, smooth its period with an EMA seeded by its first value, clamp it between minimum and maximum lengths, and round to the nearest integer.
Calculation: Bands = using the current adaptive or fixed length, calculate Bollinger Bands as SMA(close) plus or minus a multiplier times the population standard deviation of close; calculate Keltner Channels as the same SMA plus or minus another multiplier times SMA(true range).
Calculation: Squeeze = optionally use the percentile rank of (Bollinger upper - Bollinger lower) / (Keltner upper - Keltner lower) over a separate lookback at or below a threshold; otherwise, require Bollinger lower > Keltner lower AND Bollinger upper < Keltner upper. A zero Keltner width gives an unavailable ratio and no squeeze.
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression endpoint over the current adaptive or fixed length of close minus the average of SMA(close) and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over that length; optionally divide by SMA(true range) over the same length. A zero divisor gives unavailable momentum.
Calculation: Squeeze duration = number of consecutive squeeze bars immediately preceding the current bar; squeeze high and squeeze low are the highest high and lowest low across those bars.
Long: Previous bar is in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND momentum > 0 AND (qualified-release filter is off OR (close > open AND absolute momentum >= threshold AND squeeze duration >= minimum duration AND (range-break requirement is off OR close > squeeze high))).
Short: Previous bar is in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND momentum < 0 AND (qualified-release filter is off OR (close < open AND absolute momentum >= threshold AND squeeze duration >= minimum duration AND (range-break requirement is off OR close < squeeze low))).
