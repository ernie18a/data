Calculation: Candle measures = range = high - low; body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; opposing wick = upper wick when close > open, otherwise lower wick; body ratio = body / range; directional close position = (close - low) / range for a bullish candle, otherwise (high - close) / range; range ratio = range / ATR; opposing wick ratio = opposing wick / body; volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume). Use zero for ratios with a zero range or ATR, a large fallback for opposing wick ratio with zero body, and a neutral fallback for volume ratio when average volume is not positive.
Calculation: Strength = sum of five component scores, capped at a maximum. Each component uses an ordered, parameterized step table: body ratio and directional close position have increasing scores across four ascending thresholds; range ratio and volume ratio have increasing scores across four and three ascending thresholds respectively, starting at zero; opposing wick ratio has increasing scores across three descending thresholds, starting at zero. The minimum tier is an additional parameterized strength threshold, with higher tiers requiring higher strength.
Calculation: Setup entry = the qualifying candle's body midpoint, range midpoint, or close, according to the selected entry model. Long stop = qualifying candle low minus a price buffer; short stop = qualifying candle high plus a price buffer. Risk = abs(setup entry - stop). Long targets = setup entry plus risk multiplied by each selected target multiple; short targets = setup entry minus risk multiplied by each selected target multiple. Preserve these levels from the qualifying candle.
Long: Close > open AND the minimum-range filter is disabled OR close > open AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio, AND the rejection filter is disabled OR opposing wick <= body, AND the EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close), AND strength >= minimum strength AND strength >= minimum tier threshold AND momentum mode is selected.
Long: Close < open AND the minimum-range filter is disabled OR close < open AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio, AND the rejection filter is disabled OR opposing wick <= body, AND the EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close), AND strength >= minimum strength AND strength >= minimum tier threshold AND fade mode is selected.
Short: Close < open AND the minimum-range filter is disabled OR close < open AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio, AND the rejection filter is disabled OR opposing wick <= body, AND the EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close), AND strength >= minimum strength AND strength >= minimum tier threshold AND momentum mode is selected.
Short: Close > open AND the minimum-range filter is disabled OR close > open AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio, AND the rejection filter is disabled OR opposing wick <= body, AND the EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close), AND strength >= minimum strength AND strength >= minimum tier threshold AND fade mode is selected.
Long: A prior qualifying long setup remains pending AND the current bar is later than the setup bar AND low <= its setup entry.
Short: A prior qualifying short setup remains pending AND the current bar is later than the setup bar AND high >= its setup entry.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
