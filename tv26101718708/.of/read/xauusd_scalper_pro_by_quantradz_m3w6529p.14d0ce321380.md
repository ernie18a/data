Calculation: Bullish score = Count the true conditions: (1) short-term close EMA > medium-term close EMA; (2) each of those EMAs > its highest value over the preceding slope-lookback bars; (3) slow EMA filter disabled OR close > long-term close EMA; (4) within the current bar or preceding pullback-lookback bars, low <= short-term close EMA + pullback-distance multiplier × ATR OR low <= medium-term close EMA + pullback-distance multiplier × ATR; (5) close > open AND candle body percentage >= minimum; (6) RSI > bullish threshold; (7) ADX >= minimum; (8) positive DI > negative DI; (9) ATR ratio >= minimum AND ATR ratio <= maximum; (10) higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short-term close EMA > higher-timeframe medium-term close EMA. The higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. ADX and DI use the same length for DI and ADX smoothing.
Calculation: Bearish score = Count the true conditions: (1) short-term close EMA < medium-term close EMA; (2) each of those EMAs < its lowest value over the preceding slope-lookback bars; (3) slow EMA filter disabled OR close < long-term close EMA; (4) within the current bar or preceding pullback-lookback bars, high >= short-term close EMA - pullback-distance multiplier × ATR OR high >= medium-term close EMA - pullback-distance multiplier × ATR; (5) close < open AND candle body percentage >= minimum; (6) RSI < bearish threshold; (7) ADX >= minimum; (8) negative DI > positive DI; (9) ATR ratio >= minimum AND ATR ratio <= maximum; (10) higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short-term close EMA < higher-timeframe medium-term close EMA. The higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. ADX and DI use the same length for DI and ADX smoothing.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high = low.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR / SMA of ATR, or zero when that SMA is zero.
Calculation: Qualification = directional score >= minimum score in normal mode, OR directional score equals the total number of scored conditions in strict mode.
Calculation: Pullback index = Index of the most recent bar satisfying the directional pullback comparison in condition 4, regardless of its age. It is unavailable until such a bar exists.
Long: At chart-bar close, bullish qualification = true AND bearish qualification = false AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured exchange-time session) AND (news blackout disabled OR bar is outside the configured exchange-time blackout) AND (show-every-signal mode enabled OR ((no earlier signal exists OR bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown) AND (no pullback index was recorded for the previous long signal OR current bullish pullback index is available AND current bullish pullback index != the index recorded for the previous long signal))). Record the current bullish pullback index, including its unavailable state, on each long signal.
Short: At chart-bar close, bearish qualification = true AND bullish qualification = false AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured exchange-time session) AND (news blackout disabled OR bar is outside the configured exchange-time blackout) AND (show-every-signal mode enabled OR ((no earlier signal exists OR bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown) AND (no pullback index was recorded for the previous short signal OR current bearish pullback index is available AND current bearish pullback index != the index recorded for the previous short signal))). Record the current bearish pullback index, including its unavailable state, on each short signal.
Long: Short-term close EMA > medium-term close EMA.
Short: Short-term close EMA < medium-term close EMA.
Long: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND short-term close EMA > medium-term close EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND short-term close EMA < medium-term close EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
