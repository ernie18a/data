Calculation: Previous structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding structure lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high and low of the most recent bearish candle when the close breaks above the previous structure high; bearish order block = the high and low of the most recent bullish candle when the close breaks below the previous structure low. Each zone remains fixed until another break of the same direction.
Calculation: Active order block = the most recently created bullish or bearish order block, provided its structure break is no more than the validity lookback bars ago.
Calculation: Order block retest = current bar overlaps the active zone: low <= zone high AND high >= zone low.
Calculation: EMA trend confirmation = aggressive mode: close > fast EMA for long or close < fast EMA for short; balanced mode: fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA for long, or fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA for short; conservative mode adds fast EMA > its prior-bar value for long or fast EMA < its prior-bar value for short. Fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume > its simple moving average multiplied by the volume multiplier, or volume confirmation is disabled.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open OR close > bullish order block midpoint. Bearish rejection = close < open OR close < bearish order block midpoint.
Calculation: Buy stop = bullish order block low minus ATR buffer; buy target = entry-bar close plus (entry-bar close minus buy stop) multiplied by the risk-reward ratio. Sell stop = bearish order block high plus ATR buffer; sell target = entry-bar close minus (sell stop minus entry-bar close) multiplied by the risk-reward ratio.
Long: bullish order block retest AND EMA trend confirmation for long AND RSI > buy level AND volume confirmation OR bullish order block retest AND bullish rejection AND EMA trend confirmation for long AND RSI > buy level AND (stochastic confirmation disabled OR stochastic K > stochastic D OR stochastic K > 50) AND volume confirmation AND confirmed buy condition is true now and false on the prior bar AND buy risk > minimum tick.
Short: bearish order block retest AND EMA trend confirmation for short AND RSI < sell level AND volume confirmation OR bearish order block retest AND bearish rejection AND EMA trend confirmation for short AND RSI < sell level AND (stochastic confirmation disabled OR stochastic K < stochastic D OR stochastic K < 50) AND volume confirmation AND confirmed sell condition is true now and false on the prior bar AND sell risk > minimum tick.
Exit: Long, latest valid entry is long AND (low <= its buy stop OR high >= its buy target)
Exit: Short, latest valid entry is short AND (high >= its sell stop OR low <= its sell target)
