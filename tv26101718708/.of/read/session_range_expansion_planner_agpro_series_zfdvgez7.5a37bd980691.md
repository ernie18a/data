Calculation: Session balance = highest high and lowest low of the first balance-window bars of each configured intraday session; reset all session state at each new session or session-day boundary.
Calculation: Expansion direction = after the balance window, select the first qualifying direction in the session and retain it until the next session. An upside confirmation is close > balance high + buffer, or, in wick mode, high > balance high + buffer AND close > balance high − range size × wick tolerance, or, in strict mode, close > balance high + buffer AND previous close > balance high. A downside confirmation is close < balance low − buffer, or, in wick mode, low < balance low − buffer AND close < balance low + range size × wick tolerance, or, in strict mode, close < balance low − buffer AND previous close < balance low. Buffer is a sensitivity-dependent multiple of ATR. If both directions qualify, select upside when close >= balance midpoint, otherwise downside.
Calculation: Acceptance count = reset to zero at each new session; on each in-session bar after expansion is selected, including the expansion bar, add one when close is beyond the broken boundary in the expansion direction; otherwise subtract one, floored at zero. Acceptance occurs when the count >= the acceptance threshold.
Calculation: Target distance = target multiplier × max(balance high − balance low, ATR); target level = balance high + target distance for upside expansion, or balance low − target distance for downside expansion.
Calculation: Planner score = clamp to its score range the sum of break, acceptance, volume, volatility and room components. Break component is clamp(max(directional distance from close beyond the broken boundary, 0) / ATR × break weight, 0, break weight). Acceptance component is clamp(acceptance count / acceptance threshold × acceptance weight, 0, acceptance weight). Volume component is clamp((volume / volume SMA − volume baseline) / volume scale × volume weight, 0, volume weight), using a volume ratio of 1 when the SMA is unavailable or zero. Volatility component is clamp(1 − abs(ATR / ATR SMA − volatility center) / volatility width, 0, 1) × volatility weight, using an ATR ratio of 1 when the ATR SMA is unavailable or zero; center is the midpoint of the configured lower and upper regime thresholds and width is their half-distance floored at a minimum width. Room component is room weight × clamp(obstacle distance / target distance, 0, 1); obstacle distance is the distance from close to the previous-bar highest high over the obstacle lookback for upside, or to the previous-bar lowest low for downside, when that obstacle lies ahead of close, and otherwise equals target distance. Use a positive minimum price increment as the floor for ATR and balance range; if ATR is unavailable, substitute high − low.
Long: In session AND balance window completed AND expansion direction first becomes upside.
Short: In session AND balance window completed AND expansion direction first becomes downside.
Long: Expansion direction = upside AND acceptance count >= acceptance threshold AND planner score >= visual score threshold AND no prior acceptance marking this session AND event cooldown elapsed.
Short: Expansion direction = downside AND acceptance count >= acceptance threshold AND planner score >= visual score threshold AND no prior acceptance marking this session AND event cooldown elapsed.
