Calculation: Raw pressure = 50 + 50 × tanh(2 × Wilder average of [bar pressure location × volatility weight] / sensitivity); bar pressure location = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low; volatility weight = min((high − low) / ATR, 3), or 1 when ATR is nonpositive; ATR is Wilder-smoothed true range.
Calculation: PFI = 50 + (raw pressure − 50) × exp(−fatigue × fatigue impact); fatigue builds by normalized raw-pressure excess beyond the overbought or oversold threshold times the build rate, capped at 4, and otherwise decays by the release rate.
Calculation: Long episode armed = latched when raw pressure is below oversold and PFI − raw pressure meets the minimum gap; reset when raw pressure reaches or exceeds 50.
Calculation: Short episode armed = latched when raw pressure is above overbought and raw pressure − PFI meets the minimum gap; reset when raw pressure reaches or falls below 50.
Calculation: Long flush recorded = latched when raw pressure is below oversold, volatility weight meets the flush range threshold, and bar pressure location is at or below the negative flush close-location threshold; reset when raw pressure reaches or exceeds 50.
Calculation: Short blowoff recorded = latched when raw pressure is above overbought, volatility weight meets the flush range threshold, and bar pressure location meets the flush close-location threshold; reset when raw pressure reaches or falls below 50.
Calculation: Long divergence = while raw pressure is below oversold, current low is at or below the episode's prior lowest low and raw pressure exceeds the episode's prior lowest raw pressure by the divergence threshold; episode lows update on oversold bars and reset when raw pressure reaches 50. This filter applies only when enabled.
Calculation: Short divergence = while raw pressure is above overbought, current high is at or above the episode's prior highest high and raw pressure is below the episode's prior highest raw pressure by the divergence threshold; episode highs update on overbought bars and reset when raw pressure reaches 50. This filter applies only when enabled.
Calculation: Long conviction reversal = bar pressure location meets the positive trigger threshold, volatility weight meets the trigger range threshold, and raw pressure is rising from the prior bar.
Calculation: Short conviction rejection = bar pressure location meets the negative trigger threshold, volatility weight meets the trigger range threshold, and raw pressure is falling from the prior bar.
Calculation: Long marker lock = set after a long entry marker; clear when raw pressure reaches 50 or its cooldown expires. Cooldown resets to its configured length on a long marker and otherwise decreases by one bar toward zero.
Calculation: Short marker lock = set after a short entry marker; clear when raw pressure reaches 50 or its cooldown expires. Cooldown resets to its configured length on a short marker and otherwise decreases by one bar toward zero.
Long: (raw pressure < oversold AND volatility weight ≥ flush range threshold AND bar pressure location ≤ −flush close-location threshold) OR (long episode armed AND long flush recorded AND long conviction reversal AND raw pressure ≤ oversold + near-zone buffer AND long marker lock is false AND (divergence filter disabled OR long divergence)) OR (current PFI > 50 AND prior PFI ≤ 50)
Short: (raw pressure > overbought AND volatility weight ≥ flush range threshold AND bar pressure location ≥ flush close-location threshold) OR (short episode armed AND short blowoff recorded AND short conviction rejection AND raw pressure ≥ overbought − near-zone buffer AND short marker lock is false AND (divergence filter disabled OR short divergence)) OR (current PFI < 50 AND prior PFI ≥ 50)
