Calculation: True range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close)); ATR = either Wilder ATR or simple moving average of true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Trailing trend bands = initialize upper/lower bands as hl2 − multiplier × ATR and hl2 + multiplier × ATR; thereafter raise the upper band to at least its prior value when previous close is above it, and lower the lower band to at most its prior value when previous close is below it. Trend switches bearish when close falls below the prior upper band and bullish when close rises above the prior lower band; otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: CISD levels and state = track market-structure highs and lows and pullback sequences. A bearish pullback begins after an up-close bar; track its highest qualifying open as the bullish-origin level. A bullish pullback begins after a down-close bar; track its lowest qualifying open as the bearish-origin level. On a break below the tracked structure low, create a bullish-origin level; on a break above the tracked structure high, create a bearish-origin level. Confirm a bullish CISD when close is above the latest unconfirmed bearish-origin level and either (high − low > 0 and (close − low)/(high − low) ≥ the strong-close fraction) or the current and previous closes are both above it. Confirm a bearish CISD symmetrically below the latest unconfirmed bullish-origin level, using (high − close)/(high − low) ≥ the strong-close fraction or two consecutive closes below it. Retain the latest confirmed CISD direction until another confirms.
Calculation: Entry risk and targets = at a signal close, use either a configurable percentage of entry price or configurable ATR multiples captured on that bar for stop distance; targets are configurable risk-distance multiples (or ATR multiples) from entry in the trade direction.
Long: trend changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: trend changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: first bar trend is bullish and latest confirmed CISD direction is bullish.
Short: first bar trend is bearish and latest confirmed CISD direction is bearish.
Exit: long, low < stop or high > third target.
Exit: short, high > stop or low < third target.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop; otherwise, depending on selected exit model, high ≥ first target (exit all), high ≥ third target (exit all), or scale one third at each target while moving stop to entry after first target and to first target after second.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop; otherwise, depending on selected exit model, low ≤ first target (exit all), low ≤ third target (exit all), or scale one third at each target while moving stop to entry after first target and to first target after second.
