Calculation: Trailing trend = choose a price source, normally (high + low) / 2, and an ATR calculated either with Wilder smoothing or as the SMA of true range. Raw lower and upper bands are source minus and plus an ATR multiple. The lower band is the maximum of its raw value and its previous value if the previous close was above the previous lower band; otherwise it is the raw value. The upper band is the minimum of its raw value and its previous value if the previous close was below the previous upper band; otherwise it is the raw value. Initialize the trend bullish. Change bearish to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper band; change bullish to bearish when close falls below the previous lower band; otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Structure and delivery levels = initialize structure top and bottom to zero, with separate inactive upward-candle and downward-candle pullbacks and separate queues of unconfirmed upward-break and downward-break levels. On each bar, start an upward-candle pullback at the previous bar's open and index if the previous candle was bullish and that pullback is inactive; start a downward-candle pullback similarly if the previous candle was bearish. For an active downward-candle pullback, replace its stored open and index when the current open is lower than the stored open, or when the current candle is bearish and its open is higher than the stored open. For an active upward-candle pullback, replace its stored open and index when the current open is higher than the stored open, or when the current candle is bullish and its open is lower than the stored open. Process a downward structure break first: if low is below structure bottom, set bottom to low; if an upward-candle pullback is active and its stored index is earlier than the current bar, set structure top to the higher high of the stored-index bar and its preceding bar, deactivate that pullback, and append its stored open to the upward-break queue. Otherwise, if the previous candle is bullish and the current candle bearish, set structure top to the previous high, deactivate that pullback, and append its stored open to the upward-break queue. Then process an upward structure break: if high is above structure top, set top to high; if a downward-candle pullback is active and its stored index is earlier than the current bar, set structure bottom to the lower low of the stored-index bar and its preceding bar, deactivate that pullback, and append its stored open to the downward-break queue. Otherwise, if the previous candle is bearish and the current candle bullish, set structure bottom to the previous low, deactivate that pullback, and append its stored open to the downward-break queue. If old levels are not retained, remove the oldest level from each queue containing more than one level, once per bar. Examine only the oldest level in each queue, including completed levels. First, if the oldest downward-break level is unconfirmed and close is below it, confirm it when either high > low AND (high - close) / (high - low) is at least a strong-close fraction, OR the optional two-close rule is enabled AND the previous close is below that same level. Set delivery state bearish, record the confirmation method, and append the current stored upward-candle pullback open to the upward-break queue. Next, if the oldest upward-break level is unconfirmed and close is above it, confirm it when either high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) is at least the strong-close fraction, OR the optional two-close rule is enabled AND the previous close is above that same level. Set delivery state bullish, record the confirmation method, and append the current stored downward-candle pullback open to the downward-break queue. The strong-close method takes precedence when both methods qualify. Initialize delivery state neutral and retain it until another confirmation; if both confirmations occur on one bar, the bullish state is last.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry, set risk to either entry price times a positive percentage or an entry-bar ATR times a stop multiplier. Set the stop one risk below entry for a long or above entry for a short. Set three targets above a long entry or below a short entry: in percentage mode their distances are one, two, and three times risk; in ATR mode their distances are independently parameterized entry-bar ATR multiples. Percentage mode with zero percentage disables levels.
Calculation: Strength score = sum parameterized points for trend and delivery alignment, the latest matching delivery confirmation method, MACD histogram sign and slope in the candidate direction, close relative to same-length EMAs of high and low, first-target distance divided by risk for the most recent trend-flip entry in the candidate direction, and bars elapsed since the latest delivery confirmation. Use separate ordered thresholds and points for the risk-reward and freshness tiers. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Confidence score = sum parameterized points for agreement with the trailing trend calculated on a higher timeframe, persistence of the chart-timeframe trend measured by bars since the last flip and flip count over a lookback, current ATR divided by a longer-lookback SMA of that ATR, and absolute distance from the midpoint of the high and low EMAs divided by ATR. Use ordered thresholds and points for persistence, volatility, and extension tiers; use intermediate fallback points when the higher-timeframe trend or a ratio is unavailable. The higher-timeframe calculation optionally uses either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Signal filter = choose off, strength only, confidence only, both, or either. Strength passes when the strength score is at least its grade-floor threshold; confidence passes when the confidence score is at least its threshold. Both requires both comparisons; either requires at least one.
Long: Trailing trend changes from bearish to bullish AND the previous trend position is not long.
Short: Trailing trend changes from bullish to bearish AND the previous trend position is not short.
Long: A downward structure break creates an upward-break level OR an unconfirmed upward-break level is confirmed by a bullish close.
Short: An upward structure break creates a downward-break level OR an unconfirmed downward-break level is confirmed by a bearish close.
Long: Trailing trend is bullish AND delivery state is bullish AND this alignment was absent on the previous bar AND the selected signal filter passes.
Short: Trailing trend is bearish AND delivery state is bearish AND this alignment was absent on the previous bar AND the selected signal filter passes.
Exit: long, trend-position levels are enabled AND previous trend position is long AND (low < stop OR high > third target).
Exit: short, trend-position levels are enabled AND previous trend position is short AND (high > stop OR low < third target).
Exit: long, on a bar after an unfiltered alignment entry, low <= current stop OR (all-out-first-target model AND high >= first target) OR (runner model AND high >= third target) OR (scale-thirds model AND high >= the next outstanding target); in the scale-thirds model exit one third at each target, move the stop to entry after the first target and to the first target after the second, and permit successive targets on the same bar. Check the existing stop before targets and do not recheck a moved stop until the next bar.
Exit: short, on a bar after an unfiltered alignment entry, high >= current stop OR (all-out-first-target model AND low <= first target) OR (runner model AND low <= third target) OR (scale-thirds model AND low <= the next outstanding target); in the scale-thirds model exit one third at each target, move the stop to entry after the first target and to the first target after the second, and permit successive targets on the same bar. Check the existing stop before targets and do not recheck a moved stop until the next bar.
