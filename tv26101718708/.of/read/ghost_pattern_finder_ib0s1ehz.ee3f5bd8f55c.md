Calculation: Selected pattern = chart bars between the bars nearest the two selected timestamps, inclusive; resolve equal time distances toward the earlier bar. Use the earlier selected bar as the start. Require both timestamps to be set and the pattern length to be within the permitted range.
Calculation: Historical sources = enabled chart, lower, and higher timeframes. Use completed lower-timeframe bars and the last completed higher-timeframe bar. For each source, search only bars before the selected pattern start, with enough subsequent historical bars to cover the scaled forecast horizon. Search historical window lengths spanning the permitted range of duration ratios relative to the selected pattern, adjusted for the source timeframe.
Calculation: Pattern comparison = sample each candidate and the selected pattern at evenly spaced, rounded bar indices. Normalize sampled closes by their respective means and population standard deviations, using zero when the standard deviation is negligible. Compute Pearson correlation over the whole sample and separately over its recent tail; recency-weighted root-mean-square distance between normalized closes; candle similarity from the average differences in signed body, upper wick, and lower wick, each divided by its candle range; swing similarity from the agreement in direction and magnitude of changes at regularly spaced sample intervals; and robustness as the minimum correlation over the full sample and samples trimmed at the start, end, and both ends, clipped to the unit interval. A candidate qualifies when both correlations, candle similarity, swing similarity, and robustness meet their respective minimum thresholds AND distance is at most its maximum threshold.
Calculation: Selected historical match = search each source with a coarse pass over candidate end bars and lengths, retain the highest weighted similarity scores after a minimum coarse correlation filter and a penalty for timeframe distance, refine around those candidates with a finer sampled comparison and correlation filter, then search every end bar around the highest-ranked refined candidates. Retain qualifying matches ranked by weighted overall correlation, tail correlation, candle similarity, swing similarity, and robustness, less the timeframe-distance penalty. In descending rank order, group overlapping historical intervals into families using the overlap duration divided by the shorter interval duration; retain the permitted number of distinct families. In manual mode select the requested ranked family, capped at the last available family. In automatic mode retain the previously active family if its overlap with a current family meets the continuation threshold, unless the highest-ranked family exceeds its score by the switching threshold; otherwise select the highest-ranked family.
Calculation: Forecast return = if forecast candles are enabled, divide the close of the last available historical bar within the forecast horizon after the selected match by the match's final close and subtract one; scale the forecast horizon by the ratio of match length to selected-pattern length and round up. Use the selected pattern's final close as the fallback forecast close when no forecast candle is available, and use a price multiplier of one when the match's final close is zero. Return zero when the selected pattern's final close is zero or forecast candles are disabled.
Long: selected pattern is valid AND at least one qualified historical family exists AND forecast return >= 0
Short: selected pattern is valid AND at least one qualified historical family exists AND forecast return < 0
