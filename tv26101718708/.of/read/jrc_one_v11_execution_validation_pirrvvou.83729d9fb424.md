Calculation: Direction d = +1 for long and -1 for short; directional comparison x >d y means d*(x-y)>0.
Calculation: Bias = +1 when the last completed higher-timeframe close > its short EMA > its long EMA; -1 when that close < its short EMA < its long EMA; otherwise 0. At a fill, use the preceding chart bar's bias.
Calculation: Levels = previous regular-session high and low, current premarket high and low, completed opening-range high and low, and most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and low. Calculate session levels from completed lower-timeframe bars, or chart bars when the source and chart timeframes match. A previous regular-session level requires its opening bar; premarket levels are available after premarket ends and opening-range levels after the range ends. Confirm each pivot after an equal number of bars on each side. Process levels in the stated order, retaining the first price when another is within the configured tick tolerance of a retained price.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the average volume of the preceding configured number of regular-session chart bars. VWAP = cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting either on each local calendar day or at the regular-session open; in the latter case include only regular-session bars.
Calculation: Active zones = bands around confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows, with half-width equal to the greater of one tick and a configured fraction of ATR at the pivot. Create a zone on the pivot's confirmation bar only if its close has not passed the zone's outer edge; remove it when a later close passes that edge or its age exceeds the configured duration. Retain at most the configured number of zones, removing the oldest first.
Calculation: Setup = the first unused level in level order that qualifies in the current bias direction while no setup, plan or position is active. A break setup qualifies when close crosses the current level in direction d, relative volume is at least its threshold, and absolute close-to-level distance is at most a configured multiple of current ATR. A sweep setup qualifies when the directional extreme penetrates beyond the level by more than a configured multiple of current ATR, close is beyond the level in direction d, the candle body points in direction d, and close is in the directional half of its range. If both qualify at that level, choose the configured priority. Freeze the level, ATR, origin volume and direction. Initialize the tracked extreme to the level for a break or to the sweep bar's low for long and high for short; subsequently include each bar's low for long or high for short. Invalidate the setup if bias changes, its adverse extreme passes the level by more than the configured failure multiple of frozen ATR, its age exceeds the setup limit, or the time since its first retest exceeds the confirmation limit. Clear it when its signal window or date changes or ends, or a plan or position becomes active.
Calculation: Retest = the first bar after setup creation whose range intersects the level plus or minus a configured multiple of frozen ATR and whose close is beyond the level in direction d; for a break setup, its volume must also be below origin volume if the quiet-retest filter is enabled. Freeze its high as the long trigger or its low as the short trigger.
Calculation: Obstacle = the nearest level more than the configured tick distance ahead of the proposed entry in direction d, or the nearest active opposing zone edge ahead of it, whichever is closer. For a zone containing entry, use entry as its edge.
Calculation: Plan prices = adverse market adjustment is slippage plus half the spread, rounded up to whole ticks. Round the confirmation close plus d times this adjustment adversely to a tick for proposed entry E. Round the tracked extreme minus d times the configured frozen-ATR stop buffer outward to a tick for stop S. Let R=d*(E-S). Target distance is the lesser of the configured maximum multiple of R and directional distance from E to the obstacle minus a configured frozen-ATR clearance; if no obstacle exists, use the maximum multiple of R. Round E plus d times target distance toward E to a tick for target T.
Calculation: Risk gates at entry E = absolute entry-to-level distance <= the configured extension multiple of frozen ATR AND configured minimum tick risk <= d*(E-S) <= configured maximum multiple of frozen ATR AND modeled costs divided by d*(E-S) <= the configured cost limit AND net reward divided by total stop loss >= the configured minimum. Stop fill F=S-d*market adjustment; modeled costs = market adjustment + fee rate*(abs(E)+abs(F)); net reward = d*(T-E)-fee rate*(abs(E)+abs(T)); total stop loss = d*(E-F)+fee rate*(abs(E)+abs(F)).
Long: d=+1 AND bias=+1 AND a valid setup and retest exist AND a later bar closes above the retest trigger AND close>open AND (setup is sweep OR close>VWAP AND short chart-timeframe EMA>long chart-timeframe EMA) AND plan risk gates pass AND the level has not been used for this direction today AND the bar is within the configured test dates and signal window, before the last regular-session bar and test end AND required session data and indicator history are available AND daily plan count is below its configured cap or the cap is disabled AND configured cooldown since the last plan has elapsed AND no position or queued plan is active AND no position exited on this bar.
Short: d=-1 AND bias=-1 AND a valid setup and retest exist AND a later bar closes below the retest trigger AND close<open AND (setup is sweep OR close<VWAP AND short chart-timeframe EMA<long chart-timeframe EMA) AND plan risk gates pass AND the level has not been used for this direction today AND the bar is within the configured test dates and signal window, before the last regular-session bar and test end AND required session data and indicator history are available AND daily plan count is below its configured cap or the cap is disabled AND configured cooldown since the last plan has elapsed AND no position or queued plan is active AND no position exited on this bar.
Calculation: Queued fill = on the first bar after the plan plus the configured additional delay, round open plus d times the adverse market adjustment adversely to a tick. Retain the plan's frozen level, ATR, stop and target. Cancel the plan if its wait limit is exceeded or its date, signal window, regular session or test range changes; otherwise evaluate the fill once. Count and mark the level used when the plan is confirmed, even if the fill is rejected.
Long: queued long plan is due AND preceding chart bar bias=+1 AND fill>stop AND fill<target AND risk gates pass at the fill price.
Short: queued short plan is due AND preceding chart bar bias=-1 AND fill<stop AND fill>target AND risk gates pass at the fill price.
Exit: long, low<=frozen stop OR high>=frozen target OR the regular session has ended OR the current bar is the last regular-session bar OR the test end has been reached or passed.
Exit: short, high>=frozen stop OR low<=frozen target OR the regular session has ended OR the current bar is the last regular-session bar OR the test end has been reached or passed.
Calculation: Exit execution = exits can occur on the fill bar. If the prior session has ended or the test end has already been passed, exit at open minus d times the adverse market adjustment. Otherwise, if the stop is touched, exit at the worse of open and stop minus d times the adjustment; otherwise, if the target is touched, exit at target; otherwise exit at close minus d times the adjustment. Stop takes priority when both stop and target are touched.
