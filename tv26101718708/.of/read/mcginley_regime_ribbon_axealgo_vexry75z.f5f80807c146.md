Calculation: Shared-speed McGinley ribbon = Five lines with progressively longer Fibonacci-spaced lengths, scaled from one length parameter. Initialize each line to the selected OHLC-derived price. On each bar, calculate the shared speed factor as the fourth power of current price divided by the previous middle line, bounded by lower and upper limits. Update each line as its previous value plus (current price minus its previous value) divided by the product of smoothness, its length, and the shared speed factor; protect denominators with a small positive floor.
Calculation: Ribbon alignment = Count adjacent pairs ordered from fastest above slowest and, separately, from fastest below slowest; alignment is the larger count. Direction is up for a fully ascending stack, down for a fully descending stack, otherwise up if the middle line's change over a slope lookback is positive, down if negative, and neutral if zero. Ribbon spread is the difference between the maximum and minimum ribbon lines divided by ATR.
Calculation: Volatility percentile = Percentile rank over a lookback of ATR divided by the absolute selected price, with a small positive denominator floor.
Calculation: Raw regime = Initialize to ranging. Once the bar index exceeds the maximum of the slowest ribbon length, volatility lookback, and twice the ADX length, and ADX and volatility percentile are available, update each bar using the previous raw regime: use lower ADX and spread thresholds and require one fewer aligned pair when the previous raw regime is trending; otherwise use the normal thresholds and require a fully aligned ribbon. Use a lower volatility-percentile threshold when the previous raw regime is volatile. Set volatile when alignment is below the applicable requirement AND volatility percentile is at least its applicable threshold. Otherwise set trending when ADX and spread are at least their applicable thresholds AND alignment meets its applicable requirement AND (the previous raw regime is trending OR the count of official regime changes over a lookback ending on the previous bar is below a flip threshold). Set trend direction from ribbon direction, treating neutral as up. Otherwise set ranging.
Calculation: Official regime = Initialize to ranging. After warm-up, immediately change to volatile when the raw regime is volatile; for any other raw regime different from the official regime, change only after that same raw regime persists for the required consecutive bars. Reset the candidate count when the raw and official regimes match or the official regime changes.
Long: Warm-up is complete AND official regime is trend up.
Short: Warm-up is complete AND official regime is trend down.
