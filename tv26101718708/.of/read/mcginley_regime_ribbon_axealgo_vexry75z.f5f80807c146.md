Calculation: Five McGinley lines = initialize each line to the price source; thereafter update each as prior line + (price source − prior line) / (smoothness × its length × shared speed factor). The shared speed factor is the fourth power of price source / prior middle line, bounded by lower and upper limits; all five lines use it. Lengths increase from fastest to slowest.
Calculation: Ribbon alignment = up when all four adjacent line pairs are strictly ordered fastest to slowest; down when all four are strictly ordered slowest to fastest. Otherwise the ribbon is not fully aligned. Ribbon spread = (highest line − lowest line) / ATR.
Calculation: Normalized ATR percentile = percentile rank of ATR / absolute price source against its own longer lookback.
Calculation: Official regime = classify each bar as volatile when normalized ATR percentile is above its threshold and the ribbon fails the alignment requirement; otherwise classify as uptrend or downtrend when ADX is above its threshold, the ribbon meets the alignment requirement, and spread exceeds its minimum; otherwise classify as ranging. Use looser ADX, alignment, spread, and volatility thresholds to remain in the current trend or volatile regime than to enter it. Suppress a new trend classification when the number of official regime changes in the recent lookback reaches its threshold, except when already in a trend. Require a new raw regime to persist for its confirmation duration before making it official, except entry into volatile is immediate.
Long: Official regime changes to uptrend on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: Official regime changes to downtrend on a confirmed chart bar.
