Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a high or low that is the corresponding extreme over equal numbers of preceding and following chart bars; recognize it only when the following bars have completed.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear-regression endpoint of close minus the average of the rolling high-low midpoint and the SMA of close, using the same lookback for the rolling extremes, SMA and regression.
Calculation: Squeeze release = the preceding bar's Bollinger upper band is below its Keltner upper band AND its Bollinger lower band is above its Keltner lower band AND at least one of those comparisons is false on the current bar; the bands use the same lookback, with an EMA and ATR for the Keltner bands.
Long: A newly confirmed price swing low is lower than the preceding price swing low AND the RSI has a confirmed swing low at each of those same two pivot bars AND the newer RSI swing low is higher than the older one.
Short: A newly confirmed price swing high is higher than the preceding price swing high AND the RSI has a confirmed swing high at each of those same two pivot bars AND the newer RSI swing high is lower than the older one.
Long: The latest two confirmed swing highs are descending AND the latest two confirmed swing lows are descending AND close is above the latest confirmed swing high AND this combined condition was false on the preceding bar AND volume divided by its SMA is at least a threshold AND datetime is outside the specified session.
Short: The latest two confirmed swing highs are ascending AND the latest two confirmed swing lows are ascending AND close is below the latest confirmed swing low AND this combined condition was false on the preceding bar AND volume divided by its SMA is at least a threshold AND datetime is outside the specified session.
Long: Squeeze release AND squeeze momentum > 0.
Short: Squeeze release AND squeeze momentum < 0.
Long: Squeeze momentum > a positive magnitude threshold AND preceding squeeze momentum <= 0 AND volume divided by its SMA is at least a threshold AND datetime is outside the specified session.
Short: Squeeze momentum < the negative magnitude threshold AND preceding squeeze momentum >= 0 AND volume divided by its SMA is at least a threshold AND datetime is outside the specified session.
