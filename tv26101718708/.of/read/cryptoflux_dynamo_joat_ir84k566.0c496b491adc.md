Calculation: Ribbon = fast, middle, and slow EMAs of close, in order of increasing length.
Calculation: Smoothed ATR = EMA of ATR. ATR percentage = 100 × smoothed ATR / close, or 0 when close is 0. The volatility regime is low below a lower ATR-percentage threshold, medium from that threshold up to but excluding a higher threshold, and high otherwise.
Calculation: Smoothed histogram = EMA of the MACD histogram. Adaptive histogram baseline = a sensitivity multiplier × EMA of the absolute raw MACD histogram, using twice the smoothed-histogram EMA length.
Calculation: Volume impulse = volume > volume SMA × multiplier OR volume z-score > threshold, where volume z-score = (volume − volume SMA) / volume standard deviation, or 0 when the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Cycle oscillator = 100 × (close − cycle EMA) / cycle EMA, or 0 when the EMA is 0. The cycle EMA uses a longer length than the slow ribbon EMA.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Band width < Keltner Channel width, using the same lookback for both. Bollinger Bands use an SMA of close and standard deviation; Keltner Channels use an EMA of close and ATR. Release = the previous bar was in a squeeze AND the current bar is not.
Calculation: Long trend pathway = fast EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > slow EMA AND middle EMA > its value at the slope lookback AND close > the highest high over the structure lookback ending on the previous bar. Short trend pathway = fast EMA < middle EMA AND middle EMA < slow EMA AND middle EMA < its value at the slope lookback AND close < the lowest low over the structure lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Long momentum pathway = smoothed histogram > adaptive histogram baseline AND MACD line > MACD signal line AND RSI > its threshold AND MFI > its threshold AND volume impulse. Short momentum pathway = smoothed histogram < negative adaptive histogram baseline AND MACD line < MACD signal line AND RSI < 100 minus its threshold AND MFI < 100 minus its threshold AND volume impulse.
Calculation: Long squeeze pathway = squeeze release AND close > previous upper Bollinger Band AND smoothed histogram > 0 AND absolute smoothed histogram > adaptive histogram baseline × release multiplier. Short squeeze pathway = squeeze release AND close < previous lower Bollinger Band AND smoothed histogram < 0 AND absolute smoothed histogram > adaptive histogram baseline × release multiplier.
Calculation: Long pullback pathway = fast EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > slow EMA AND close <= fast EMA AND close > middle EMA AND previous low = lowest low over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar AND smoothed histogram > 0 AND volume impulse. Short pullback pathway = fast EMA < middle EMA AND middle EMA < slow EMA AND close >= fast EMA AND close < middle EMA AND previous high = highest high over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar AND smoothed histogram < 0 AND volume impulse.
Calculation: Directional strength = 30 if the corresponding trend pathway is true, plus 30 if the momentum pathway is true, plus 25 if the squeeze pathway is true, plus 15 if the pullback pathway is true, plus 5 if volume impulse is true or minus 5 otherwise, plus 5 in the high volatility regime or minus 2 in the low regime, plus 5 if the cycle oscillator exceeds its positive threshold for long strength or is below its negative threshold for short strength, or minus 5 otherwise; clamp the result to 0–100.
Calculation: Stop distance = smoothed ATR × the current regime's stop multiplier. Target distance = smoothed ATR × the current regime's target multiplier × base risk/reward multiplier × regime risk/reward adjustment. Trailing offset = smoothed ATR × base trailing multiplier × the current regime's trailing adjustment.
Long: Long strength >= minimum strength AND cycle oscillator > positive cycle threshold AND volume impulse AND datetime is within the configured session when the session filter is enabled.
Short: Short strength >= minimum strength AND cycle oscillator < negative cycle threshold AND volume impulse AND datetime is within the configured session when the session filter is enabled.
Long: Long strength − short strength > bias threshold.
Short: Long strength − short strength < negative bias threshold.
Exit: long, raw MACD histogram < 0 OR fast EMA <= middle EMA OR middle EMA <= slow EMA.
Exit: short, raw MACD histogram > 0 OR fast EMA >= middle EMA OR middle EMA >= slow EMA.
Exit: long, price <= entry price − stop distance OR price >= entry price + target distance.
Exit: short, price >= entry price + stop distance OR price <= entry price − target distance.
Exit: long, trailing is enabled AND close − trailing offset > entry price − stop distance AND price <= close − trailing offset.
Exit: short, trailing is enabled AND close + trailing offset < entry price + stop distance AND price >= close + trailing offset.
Exit: long, short strength >= minimum strength AND cycle oscillator < negative cycle threshold AND volume impulse AND datetime is within the configured session when the session filter is enabled.
Exit: short, long strength >= minimum strength AND cycle oscillator > positive cycle threshold AND volume impulse AND datetime is within the configured session when the session filter is enabled.
