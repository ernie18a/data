Calculation: Volume impulse = volume exceeds a configured multiple of its longer-lookback moving average OR its z-score exceeds a configured threshold.
Calculation: Cycle bias = close’s percentage deviation from its EMA exceeds a configured threshold; bullish above the positive threshold, bearish below the negative threshold.
Calculation: Long/short strength = weighted sum of the applicable trend-break, momentum, squeeze-release, and micro-pullback pathways, plus volume-impulse, volatility-regime, and cycle-bias adjustments; clamp to the configured score range.
Long: bullish EMA ribbon (fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA) AND positive middle-EMA slope OR close breaks above the prior structure-window high; OR smoothed MACD histogram exceeds its adaptive baseline AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI and MFI exceed their configured bullish thresholds AND volume impulse; OR a squeeze releases after being on AND close > prior upper Bollinger Band AND smoothed MACD histogram > 0 AND its absolute value exceeds the adaptive baseline times the configured release gate; OR bullish EMA ribbon AND close <= fast EMA AND close > middle EMA AND the prior bar’s low equals the lowest low of the pullback lookback AND smoothed MACD histogram > 0 AND volume impulse. A long signal additionally requires bullish cycle bias, volume impulse, and long strength >= the configured minimum. If both directional signals qualify, keep long only when smoothed MACD histogram > 0.
Short: bearish EMA ribbon (fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA) AND negative middle-EMA slope OR close breaks below the prior structure-window low; OR smoothed MACD histogram is below the negative adaptive baseline AND MACD line < signal line AND RSI and MFI are below their configured bearish thresholds; OR a squeeze releases after being on AND close < prior lower Bollinger Band AND smoothed MACD histogram < 0 AND its absolute value exceeds the adaptive baseline times the configured release gate; OR bearish EMA ribbon AND close >= fast EMA AND close < middle EMA AND the prior bar’s high equals the highest high of the pullback lookback AND smoothed MACD histogram < 0 AND volume impulse. A short signal additionally requires bearish cycle bias, volume impulse, and short strength >= the configured minimum. If both directional signals qualify, keep short only when smoothed MACD histogram <= 0.
Exit: Long, short signal OR raw MACD histogram < 0 OR EMA ribbon is no longer bullish OR low <= entry price - regime-adjusted ATR stop distance OR high >= entry price + regime-adjusted ATR target distance OR (close - regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset) > entry price - regime-adjusted ATR stop distance AND low <= close - regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset.
Exit: Short, long signal OR raw MACD histogram > 0 OR EMA ribbon is no longer bearish OR high >= entry price + regime-adjusted ATR stop distance OR low <= entry price - regime-adjusted ATR target distance OR (close + regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset) < entry price + regime-adjusted ATR stop distance AND high >= close + regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset.
