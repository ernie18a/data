Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar, on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Midnight open = open of the bar beginning at midnight New York time.
Calculation: Bullish CISD level = after a bar closes below both the previous-day low and midnight open during an active session, find a bullish candle among the preceding configured number of chart bars; use the higher open of the two bars immediately before that candle when both are available, otherwise the immediately preceding bar’s open. The level is valid for the configured number of chart bars from its originating bar.
Calculation: Bearish CISD level = after a bar closes above both the previous-day high and midnight open during an active session, find a bearish candle among the preceding configured number of chart bars; use the lower open of the two bars immediately before that candle when both are available, otherwise the immediately preceding bar’s open. The level is valid for the configured number of chart bars from its originating bar.
Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = at the latest and prior confirmed chart-timeframe pivot lows (each requiring the configured number of bars on both sides), the price lows and comparison-symbol lows move in opposite directions; both comparison-symbol lows are above zero; the latest pivot is less than 30 chart bars old and the bar-index difference (latest minus prior) is less than 30.
Calculation: Bearish SMT divergence = at the latest and prior confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs (each requiring the configured number of bars on both sides), the price highs and comparison-symbol highs move in opposite directions; both comparison-symbol highs are above zero; the latest pivot is less than 30 chart bars old and the bar-index difference (latest minus prior) is less than 30.
Long: a valid bullish CISD level exists AND close >= bullish CISD level; OR bullish SMT divergence AND close < previous-day low AND close < midnight open AND session filter is active
Short: a valid bearish CISD level exists AND close <= bearish CISD level; OR bearish SMT divergence AND close > previous-day high AND close > midnight open AND session filter is active
