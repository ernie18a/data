Calculation: Reference high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Midnight open = open of the most recent chart bar opening at midnight in the New York time zone; initialize to zero.
Calculation: Bearish level = when close is above both the reference high and midnight open during the selected session, find the nearest preceding bearish chart bar within the lookback. If it is one bar back, use the current open and current bar as the level and origin; otherwise use the lower of the opens one and two bars after that bearish bar, with its bar as the origin, choosing the later bar on a tie. Replace any existing bearish level. In first-signal mode, create only the first bearish level after the applicable reset; reset permission at the start of any configured session when all sessions are selected, otherwise at the New York midnight bar.
Calculation: Bullish level = when close is below both the reference low and midnight open during the selected session, find the nearest preceding bullish chart bar within the lookback. If it is one bar back, use the current open and current bar as the level and origin; otherwise use the higher of the opens one and two bars after that bullish bar, with its bar as the origin, choosing the later bar on a tie. Replace any existing bullish level. In first-signal mode, create only the first bullish level after the applicable reset; reset permission at the start of any configured session when all sessions are selected, otherwise at the New York midnight bar.
Long: An active bullish level exists AND current bar index minus its origin index is at most the validity limit AND close is greater than or equal to the level; deactivate the level after this decision.
Short: An active bearish level exists AND current bar index minus its origin index is at most the validity limit AND close is less than or equal to the level; deactivate the level after this decision.
