Calculation: ATR-safe = max(ATR, minimum price increment); candle range = high − low; candle body = abs(close − open); volume ratio = volume / max(SMA(volume), 1).
Calculation: Displacement score = min(100, min(35, 28 × break strength) + min(25, 24 × candle body / ATR-safe) + min(20, 12 × candle range / ATR-safe) + volume component + close-break component), where break strength is max(0, (close − prior-range high) / ATR-safe) for demand or max(0, (prior-range low − close) / ATR-safe) for supply; volume component is min(15, 9 × volume ratio) when volume confirmation is enabled and 12 otherwise; close-break component is 5 when close breaks the prior range in the displacement direction and 0 otherwise. The prior range excludes the current bar.
Calculation: Zone overlap = max(0, min(zone tops) − max(zone bottoms)) / max(min(zone heights), minimum price increment).
Calculation: Displacement zone = the most recent opposite-colored candle within the search lookback. Its boundaries are that candle's high and low, body high and low, or the averages of each wick extreme and its corresponding body extreme, according to the selected zone-source mode. Expand both boundaries equally around their midpoint if height is less than max(ATR-safe × minimum-height multiplier, 20 × minimum price increment). Its birth bar is the opposite candle's bar. Its tier is valid if its displacement score meets the valid-score threshold, otherwise weak. It is a core candidate if its score meets the core-score threshold and its close breaks the prior range and volume passes.
Calculation: Pivot zone = the base bars ending at the confirmed pivot bar. Wick bounds are their highest high and lowest low; body bounds are their highest body high and lowest body low. Wick mode uses wick bounds; body mode uses body bounds; hybrid supply uses wick top and body bottom, and hybrid demand uses body top and wick bottom. Expand both boundaries equally around their midpoint to the minimum ATR-safe height if needed; cap maximum height at the maximum ATR-safe height by raising the supply bottom or lowering the demand top. Its birth bar is the first base bar. Base-volume ratio is mean base volume divided by the volume SMA at the pivot, substituting mean base volume if that SMA is unavailable and 1 if the denominator is nonpositive. Reaction is pivot high minus the lowest low from pivot through confirmation for supply, or the highest high over that interval minus pivot low for demand, divided by ATR at the pivot and floored at 0. Pivot score is max(0.01, (base-volume ratio × volume weight + min(reaction, 3) / 2 × reaction weight) / max(volume weight + reaction weight, 0.01)). Its tier is core if score meets the strong threshold, valid if it meets the lower mid threshold, otherwise weak; core-tier pivot zones are core candidates.
Calculation: A new zone is accepted if its top exceeds its bottom and no stored same-side zone in active, mitigated, or breaker state has an equal or higher tier and either both boundary differences are at most max(0.18 × ATR-safe, 10 × minimum price increment) or zone overlap meets the merge threshold. Store accepted zones in creation order and discard the oldest when the maximum count is exceeded; delete zones when age exceeds twice the maximum zone age. Zone age is current bar index minus birth bar.
Calculation: Update each zone in reverse creation order on every bar, including its creation bar. Initially it is active. A demand breaker occurs when close < bottom − ATR-safe × breakout-tolerance multiplier; a supply breaker occurs when close > top + ATR-safe × breakout-tolerance multiplier. Active zones become breakers on a breaker condition; otherwise, once age meets the mitigation delay, they become mitigated when the selected mitigation condition holds. Wick-touch mitigation requires high >= bottom AND low <= top; close-inside mitigation requires bottom <= close <= top; penetration mitigation requires low <= top − zone height × penetration fraction AND high >= bottom for demand, or high >= bottom + zone height × penetration fraction AND low <= top for supply. Record the mitigation bar. Mitigated or reacted zones become breakers on a breaker condition. On a later bar, a breaker becomes invalid when demand close < bottom − ATR-safe × invalidation multiplier or supply close > top + ATR-safe × invalidation multiplier. After these transitions, a mitigated demand becomes held if bars since mitigation <= reaction window AND high >= top + ATR-safe × reaction multiplier AND close > top; a mitigated supply becomes rejected if bars since mitigation <= reaction window AND low <= bottom − ATR-safe × reaction multiplier AND close < bottom. Held and rejected states persist until broken.
Calculation: At the first bar when zone age meets the validation lookback, validate once: a core-candidate zone in active state with valid tier becomes core if demand's lowest close over the validation lookback >= bottom − ATR-safe × hold-buffer multiplier AND highest high over that lookback >= top + ATR-safe × continuation multiplier, or supply's highest close <= top + ATR-safe × hold-buffer multiplier AND lowest low <= bottom − ATR-safe × continuation multiplier. The validation window includes the current bar.
Long: close > open AND candle range >= ATR-safe × displacement-range multiplier AND candle body >= ATR-safe × displacement-body multiplier AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (close-break preference disabled OR close > prior-range high) AND displacement score >= weak-score threshold AND an opposite-colored candle exists within the search lookback AND the resulting demand zone is accepted.
Short: close < open AND candle range >= ATR-safe × displacement-range multiplier AND candle body >= ATR-safe × displacement-body multiplier AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (close-break preference disabled OR close < prior-range low) AND displacement score >= weak-score threshold AND an opposite-colored candle exists within the search lookback AND the resulting supply zone is accepted.
Long: pivot fallback and demand zones enabled AND a pivot low is confirmed with its left and right confirmation lookbacks AND bar index > right confirmation lookback + base lookback + 5 AND (pivot-volume filter disabled OR base-volume ratio >= pivot-volume threshold) AND the resulting demand zone is accepted.
Short: pivot fallback and supply zones enabled AND a pivot high is confirmed with its left and right confirmation lookbacks AND bar index > right confirmation lookback + base lookback + 5 AND (pivot-volume filter disabled OR base-volume ratio >= pivot-volume threshold) AND the resulting supply zone is accepted.
Long: a demand zone becomes core through validation OR becomes held.
Short: a supply zone becomes core through validation OR becomes rejected.
