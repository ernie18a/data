Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a local high or low with a specified number of bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars close. Keep the latest confirmed high and low, and compare each new pivot with the preceding pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Sweep event = high exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high or low falls below the latest confirmed pivot low; record the level and bar, allow one sweep per pivot, and reset sweep eligibility when a new pivot of that kind is confirmed. A later sweep replaces the pending sweep of the same kind.
Calculation: Bullish reclaim = after a low sweep, within the reclaim window, low <= the swept level AND close > the swept level; bearish reclaim = after a high sweep, within the reclaim window, high >= the swept level AND close < the swept level. Clear the pending sweep after its reclaim.
Calculation: Bullish stopping volume = volume >= its rolling volume percentile AND that percentile > 0 AND low <= the lowest low over the preceding extreme lookback AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= the rejection threshold AND high > low. Bearish stopping volume = volume >= its rolling volume percentile AND that percentile > 0 AND high >= the highest high over the preceding extreme lookback AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= the rejection threshold AND high > low. The extreme lookback is shorter than the swing lookback.
Calculation: Bullish expansion = the preceding bar's high-low range <= the lowest range in its compression lookback ending on that bar AND the current range >= an ATR multiple AND close > open. Bearish expansion uses the same range conditions AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > the latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high. Bearish structure break = close < the latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high-low range of a bearish candle immediately followed by a bullish candle whose body >= an ATR multiple; bearish order block = the high-low range of a bullish candle immediately followed by a bearish candle whose body >= an ATR multiple. Retain the latest block of each direction, replacing it when a new one forms. Bullish mitigation = low is within the bullish block inclusive AND close > its high; bearish mitigation = high is within the bearish block inclusive AND close < its low. Clear a block after mitigation.
Calculation: Directional pattern count = the number of enabled reclaim, stopping-volume, expansion, structure-break, and order-block-mitigation patterns of that direction that occurred within the confluence window, including the current bar; count each kind at most once.
Calculation: Pattern weight = for each pattern kind, pool both directions and record each occurrence's close and its maximum favorable high or low excursion from that bar through the evaluation-window ending bar. At resolution, a win requires excursion >= an ATR multiple using ATR on the resolution bar. Retain the most recent learning-memory number of resolved occurrences. Before the minimum sample count, use an initial follow-through rate; otherwise use wins divided by resolved occurrences. When adaptive weighting is enabled, weight = weight floor + (1 - weight floor) * follow-through rate; otherwise weight = 1.
Calculation: Regime = classify in priority order using efficiency, the absolute close displacement over the regime lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, with zero efficiency when that sum is zero: upward trend if efficiency >= trend threshold AND displacement > 0; downward trend if efficiency >= trend threshold AND displacement < 0; range if efficiency < range threshold; volatile if ATR percentile rank over a longer lookback >= volatility threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Directional raw score = sum the weights of the direction's active patterns, applying a structure-break multiplier and, when regime awareness is enabled, an additional range multiplier to structure breaks and a volatile-regime multiplier to expansions; multiply the sum by a countertrend multiplier when regime awareness is enabled and the direction opposes the trend.
Calculation: Directional residual score = current raw score when it is > 0; otherwise the previous residual score reduced by the decay amount and floored at zero if decay is enabled, or the previous residual score if disabled. Initialize both scores at zero and reset them on position closure.
Calculation: Required pattern count = minimum pattern count increased by the active-volatility multiplier, rounded up and capped at the number of pattern kinds, when phase filtering is enabled AND ATR percentile rank over the phase lookback >= the active threshold; otherwise the minimum pattern count.
Calculation: Hunting eligibility = statistics are warmed up AND no position is active AND the cooldown has expired AND neither confluence entry fires on the current bar. A structure-break, sweep, or reclaim marking is suppressed when a recent marking of the same kind lies within its ATR-based level tolerance; compare against both active and retained historical markings.
Calculation: Entry risk = the ATR at the entry bar multiplied by the invalidation-distance parameter; retain it unchanged until exit.
Long: On a completed bar, statistics are warmed up AND no position is active AND cooldown has expired AND bullish pattern count >= required pattern count AND bullish pattern count > bearish pattern count AND bullish pattern count meets the higher quiet-mode count when quiet mode is enabled AND bearish residual score < required pattern count.
Short: On a completed bar, statistics are warmed up AND no position is active AND cooldown has expired AND bearish pattern count >= required pattern count AND bearish pattern count > bullish pattern count AND bearish pattern count meets the higher quiet-mode count when quiet mode is enabled AND bullish residual score < required pattern count.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND bullish stopping volume.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND bearish stopping volume.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND bullish structure break AND no recent structure-break marking near the broken level.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND bearish structure break AND no recent structure-break marking near the broken level.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND low sweep event AND no recent sweep marking near the swept level.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND high sweep event AND no recent sweep marking near the swept level.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND bullish reclaim AND no recent reclaim marking near the swept level.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND bearish reclaim AND no recent reclaim marking near the swept level.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND a bullish order block forms.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND a bearish order block forms.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND a confirmed pivot high > the previous confirmed pivot high, or the first confirmed pivot high.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND a confirmed pivot high <= the previous confirmed pivot high.
Long: Hunting eligibility AND a confirmed pivot low >= the previous confirmed pivot low.
Short: Hunting eligibility AND a confirmed pivot low < the previous confirmed pivot low, or the first confirmed pivot low.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + target reward multiple * entry risk).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - target reward multiple * entry risk).
