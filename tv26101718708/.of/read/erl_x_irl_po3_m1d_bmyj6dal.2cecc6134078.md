Calculation: Range = the latest confirmed swing high and swing low on completed higher-timeframe bars; alternatively, initialize with the highest swing high and lowest swing low in a lookback and hold them. A higher-timeframe swing is strictly more extreme than the same number of completed bars on each side. After chart closes remain beyond either boundary for more than an allowed number of consecutive chart bars, set the opposite boundary to the latest higher-timeframe swing before the break and track the new extreme until a higher-timeframe swing formed after the break confirms. Require both boundaries to be confirmed and their distance to be at least a multiple of chart ATR.
Calculation: Sweep = after a range is available and price is inside its opposite boundary, a chart wick crosses one boundary from a preceding close strictly inside it. Track the most extreme wick and invalidate the sweep if consecutive closes beyond the boundary exceed an allowance, the opposite boundary is closed through, or the sweep expires. When the range first becomes available, also search preceding bars after the opposite-side push for an excursion from inside satisfying the same close allowance. A range boundary that has already produced a confirmed shift cannot produce another setup until that boundary changes.
Calculation: Push = the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing on the target side after the target swing that reaches a configured fraction of the range; if none exists, use the target swing when the optional failed-push requirement is disabled or when that target swing exceeded the preceding lookback's same-side swing extreme and every higher-timeframe close from the swing through its confirmation stayed inside that prior extreme. Higher-timeframe inputs use completed bars.
Calculation: Shift reference = the latest confirmed chart swing on the target side after the push and strictly inside the target boundary; if none exists, use the push price. A chart swing is a pivot confirmed by the same number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Directional gap = on three consecutive chart bars, bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier, bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier, and the gap height is at least a multiple of chart ATR.
Calculation: Directional body imbalance = on two consecutive chart bars, bullish when the current body bottom exceeds the previous body top while the current low is at or below the previous high, bearish when the current body top is below the previous body bottom while the current high is at or above the previous low, and the body gap is at least a multiple of chart ATR.
Calculation: Directional suspension block = on three consecutive chart bars, all three bodies close in the same direction and each later open gaps beyond the preceding close in that direction; the distance from the first close to the last open is at least a multiple of chart ATR.
Calculation: Inverted gap = a previously formed directional gap whose far edge has been closed through in the opposite direction; it remains valid until a close passes back through its other edge.
Calculation: Breaker = find a displacement candle whose body is at least a multiple of chart ATR, closes through the latest preceding chart swing in its direction, and has a directional gap between the preceding and following candles. Take the wick-to-wick range of the first opposite-closing candle within a limited lookback before the displacement and its immediately preceding consecutive opposite-closing candles, up to a configured count. The block becomes a breaker in the opposite direction when a later close passes through its far edge, and remains valid until a close passes back through its other edge.
Long: A sellside sweep is active AND the shift reference is below the target AND close is above the shift reference AND close is above the swept boundary AND close minus the sweep low is at least a multiple of chart ATR AND the configured session condition is satisfied.
Short: A buyside sweep is active AND the shift reference is above the target AND close is below the shift reference AND close is below the swept boundary AND the sweep high minus close is at least a multiple of chart ATR AND the configured session condition is satisfied.
Long: A bullish directional gap OR bullish directional body imbalance OR bullish directional suspension block forms.
Short: A bearish directional gap OR bearish directional body imbalance OR bearish directional suspension block forms.
Long: Close passes above the upper edge of a bearish directional gap OR above the upper edge of a bearish displacement's block.
Short: Close passes below the lower edge of a bullish directional gap OR below the lower edge of a bullish displacement's block.
Long: A confirmed chart swing high is higher than the previous confirmed swing high OR a confirmed chart swing low is higher than the previous confirmed swing low.
Short: A confirmed chart swing high is lower than the previous confirmed swing high OR a confirmed chart swing low is lower than the previous confirmed swing low.
