Calculation: Gaussian density = exp(-(feature - class mean)^2 / (2 * variance)) / sqrt(2 * pi * variance), where variance is clamped to at least 0.000001.
Calculation: Positive-class probability = positive likelihood / (positive likelihood + other likelihood + 0.000001), or 0.5 if unavailable. Each likelihood is the product of the two features' Gaussian densities for its class and that class's prior. The positive prior is the moving average of the positive-class indicator over the training length, or 0.5 if unavailable; the other prior is 1 minus the positive prior.
