Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after equal left and right lookbacks; retain the latest and previous pivot of each kind. The higher-high, lower-high, higher-low and lower-low flags update only when the corresponding pivot is confirmed and otherwise retain their previous values. For the first pivot comparison, use zero as the missing previous pivot for higher flags and a large positive fallback for lower flags.
Calculation: Momentum counts = bullish count is the number of close > KAMA, RSI > its midpoint, and Williams %R > an upper threshold; bearish count is the number of close < KAMA, RSI < its midpoint, and Williams %R < a lower threshold. KAMA uses the ratio of absolute net close change to the sum of absolute close changes over its lookback, and updates recursively with the square of the efficiency-weighted interpolation between fast and slow EMA smoothing constants.
Calculation: Signed volume = volume when close >= open, otherwise negative volume; cumulative signed volume is its running sum. Pressure score is a weighted sum of the absolute signed-volume-to-volume ratio, the clamped positive excess of volume over its EMA divided by that EMA, and the clamped absolute candle body divided by the high-low range; use zero for a component with a zero denominator.
Calculation: Directional pillar scores = weighted contributions, zero when their condition fails: structure contributes bullish weight when (higher-high OR higher-low) AND close > latest pivot low, and bearish weight when (lower-high OR lower-low) AND close < latest pivot high; volume contributes by the sign of the linear-regression slope of OBV; momentum contributes its weight multiplied by the corresponding momentum count divided by the number of momentum tests; liquidity contributes bullish weight when low < latest pivot low AND close > that pivot, and bearish weight when high > latest pivot high AND close < that pivot; volatility contributes the corresponding weight when ATR / max(SMA of ATR, a small positive floor) is strictly between its lower and upper thresholds AND the corresponding momentum count reaches its minimum; higher-timeframe trend contributes its weight, when enabled, according to whether chart close is above or below the EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; delta contributes its weight when pressure score > its threshold AND cumulative signed volume crosses its SMA in the corresponding direction. Missing pivots cannot satisfy the structure or liquidity price comparison.
Calculation: Session pillar scores = on each chart bar after the first, use that bar's high and low as the range. Normalize close's distance from their midpoint by the range; use zero for a zero range. Below the midpoint, bullish score is the smaller of its weight and the absolute normalized distance scaled by that weight, otherwise it is a fixed fraction of its weight; above the midpoint, calculate bearish score symmetrically, otherwise use the same fixed fraction.
Calculation: Bull and bear scores = sum the corresponding structure, volume, momentum, liquidity, volatility, session, higher-timeframe trend and delta pillar scores.
Calculation: Long setup = bull score >= minimum score AND bull score - bear score >= minimum lead AND (VWMA gate disabled OR close > VWMA) AND (Supertrend gate disabled OR Supertrend direction is bullish) AND (ribbon gate disabled OR shorter-lookback HMA > longer-lookback HMA) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX > minimum) AND (session gate disabled OR bar opening time is within the configured UTC session).
Calculation: Short setup = bear score >= minimum score AND bear score - bull score >= minimum lead AND (VWMA gate disabled OR close < VWMA) AND (Supertrend gate disabled OR Supertrend direction is bearish) AND (ribbon gate disabled OR shorter-lookback HMA < longer-lookback HMA) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX > minimum) AND (session gate disabled OR bar opening time is within the configured UTC session).
Calculation: Entry risk distance = at the entry bar, choose absolute entry price minus Supertrend; the smaller of that distance and ATR times a multiplier; entry price times a percentage; or absolute entry price minus the latest confirmed pivot low for a long or pivot high for a short, with entry price minus ATR or plus ATR respectively as the missing-pivot fallback. Hold the selected distance fixed after entry.
Long: Long setup = true.
Short: Short setup = true AND long setup = false.
Long: Bull score >= extreme threshold.
Short: Bear score >= extreme threshold AND bull score < extreme threshold.
Long: Bull score >= bear score.
Short: Bear score > bull score.
Long: Any bullish directional pillar score > its corresponding bearish pillar score OR bullish session pillar score > bearish session pillar score.
Short: Any bearish directional pillar score > its corresponding bullish pillar score OR bearish session pillar score > bullish session pillar score.
Long: Shorter-lookback HMA > longer-lookback HMA OR Supertrend direction is bullish.
Short: Shorter-lookback HMA < longer-lookback HMA OR Supertrend direction is bearish.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - entry risk distance OR high >= entry price + entry risk distance times the configured take-profit multiple.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry risk distance OR low <= entry price - entry risk distance times the configured take-profit multiple.
