Calculation: Bull score = sum of the bullish weights for structure, OBV slope, momentum, liquidity sweep, volatility, daily-session position, higher-timeframe trend, and delta pressure; each pillar contributes its configured weight or a fraction of it as defined below.
Calculation: Bear score = sum of the corresponding bearish pillar contributions.
Calculation: Structure contribution = its weight when the latest confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high OR the latest confirmed pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed pivot low, and close is above the latest confirmed pivot low; bearish counterpart uses a lower pivot high OR lower pivot low and close below the latest confirmed pivot high. Pivot high/low detection uses equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: OBV slope = current minus prior linear-regression value of OBV over one lookback; OBV adds volume on an up close and subtracts it on a down close. Bullish/bearish volume contribution is its weight when this slope is positive/negative, respectively.
Calculation: Momentum count = count of bullish comparisons (close above KAMA, RSI above 50, Williams %R above -40) or bearish comparisons (close below KAMA, RSI below 50, Williams %R below -60); momentum contribution is its weight multiplied by count/3. KAMA uses an efficiency ratio over its lookback and smoothing constants corresponding to fast and slow lengths 2 and 30.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = bullish when low is below the latest confirmed pivot low and close is above it; bearish when high is above the latest confirmed pivot high and close is below it. The matching contribution is its weight.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA. Volatility contributes its weight to the bull/bear score when the ratio is between 0.8 and 1.6 and the corresponding momentum count is at least 2.
Calculation: Daily-session position = close's displacement from the running daily high-low midpoint divided by the daily range, expressed as a percentage. Bull session contribution is its weight capped at the weight when position is below zero, scaled by absolute position/50; otherwise it is 40% of the weight. Bear session contribution is symmetric for position above zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of close; use a higher timeframe and its last completed bar. Bull/bear contribution is its weight when chart close is above/below this EMA, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.
Calculation: Delta pressure = signed volume (positive when close >= open, otherwise negative), cumulative over bars. Pressure score = 100 * (0.40 * absolute signed-volume/volume + 0.35 * max(volume/EMA(volume)-1, 0)/1.5 capped at 1 + 0.25 * absolute(open-close)/(high-low)); delta contribution is its weight when pressure score > 50 and cumulative signed volume crosses above/below its 14-bar SMA.
Calculation: Entry gates = when enabled, long requires close above VWMA, bullish Supertrend direction, shorter-anchor HMA above longer-anchor HMA, ADX above its minimum, and current bar within the configured session; short requires the inverse VWMA, Supertrend, and HMA comparisons, with the same ADX and session conditions.
Calculation: Stop distance = absolute(close - Supertrend); in ATR-cap mode use the minimum of that distance and ATR times its cap multiplier, in fixed-percent mode use close times the configured percentage, and in pivot mode use distance from close to the latest confirmed pivot low for long or pivot high for short.
Calculation: Stop level = entry price minus stop distance for long or plus it for short. Take-profit level = entry price plus/minus stop distance times the configured second reward-to-risk multiple for long/short.
Long: Bull score >= minimum score AND bull score - bear score >= minimum directional lead AND all enabled entry gates pass AND current bar is confirmed AND prior bar did not satisfy the long signal AND bars since the prior signal >= cooldown.
Short: Bear score >= minimum score AND bear score - bull score >= minimum directional lead AND all enabled entry gates pass AND current bar is confirmed AND prior bar did not satisfy the short signal AND bars since the prior signal >= cooldown.
Exit: long, low <= stop level OR high >= take-profit level.
Exit: short, high >= stop level OR low <= take-profit level.
