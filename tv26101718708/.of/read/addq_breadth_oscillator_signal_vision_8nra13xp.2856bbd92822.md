Calculation: Net advancing issues = the available ADDQ value, or advancing issues minus declining issues if ADDQ is unavailable; use the latest value from lower-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Smoothed breadth = short-term EMA of net advancing issues.
Calculation: Signal line = longer-term EMA of smoothed breadth.
Long: Smoothed breadth crosses above signal line.
Short: Smoothed breadth crosses below signal line.
