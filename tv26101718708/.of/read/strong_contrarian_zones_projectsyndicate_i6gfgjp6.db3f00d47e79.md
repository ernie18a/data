Calculation: Pivot = a high or low that is the corresponding extreme over configurable numbers of preceding and subsequent bars; confirm it on the last subsequent bar. Update the previous same-side pivot and its RSI after every confirmation, including pivots that do not create a zone.
Calculation: Conviction = the weighted average of seven factors, multiplied by 10; use zero if the weight sum is zero. Evaluate factors at the pivot bar and clamp each to [0,1]. For a low pivot, the factors are (oversold reference − RSI)/oversold reference; (RSI − previous low-pivot RSI)/divergence reference if the low is lower and RSI is higher, otherwise zero; lower wick divided by candle range and wick reference; (volume / volume SMA − 1)/(volume climax reference − 1); negative close z-score divided by stretch reference; (rolling buying-pressure percentage − 50)/pressure reference; and (lower Bollinger band − low)/(ATR × band reference). For a high pivot, use (RSI − overbought reference)/(100 − overbought reference); (previous high-pivot RSI − RSI)/divergence reference if the high is higher and RSI is lower, otherwise zero; upper wick instead of lower wick; the same volume factor; positive z-score; (50 − buying-pressure percentage)/pressure reference; and (high − upper Bollinger band)/(ATR × band reference). Buying-pressure percentage is 100 times the rolling sum of volume × (close − low)/(high − low), divided by the rolling sum of volume; use half the volume for a zero-range bar and 50 if the denominator is zero. Use zero wick fraction for a zero-range pivot bar and a small positive denominator where needed.
Calculation: Zone = fixed borders created at a qualifying pivot confirmation. Thickness is either confirmation-bar ATR times a multiplier or confirmation-bar close times a percentage. For normalized height, center the thickness on the pivot; otherwise put the full thickness above a low pivot or below a high pivot. Suppress a new zone when overlap suppression is enabled and its overlap with any active, non-sweeping zone divided by the smaller zone height meets the overlap threshold. Process low pivots before high pivots, discard the oldest zones above the zone cap, and remove zones at the maximum age measured from the pivot bar. A configurable historical window may restrict zone creation to confirmation bars within that many bars of the end of the dataset. ATR falls back to the cumulative mean high-low range until available, with a positive minimum.
Calculation: Zone state = initially active and unswept. On each bar, including its confirmation bar, a bullish zone is touched when low is within its borders and close is above its bottom; otherwise it is swept when low is below its bottom minus sweep-depth ATR. A bearish zone is touched when high is within its borders and close is below its top; otherwise it is swept when high is above its top plus sweep-depth ATR. A sweep remains pending from the next bar until reclaimed, accepted, or expired by zone age. Track its most extreme low or high. A bullish reclaim requires close above the selected bottom, midpoint, or top border; a bearish reclaim requires close below the selected top, midpoint, or bottom border. Check reclaim before acceptance. A bullish sweep is accepted when close is below the bottom minus acceptance-depth ATR, or its elapsed bars reach the reclaim window and close is below the bottom; reverse these comparisons around the top for a bearish sweep. A reclaim resets the zone to unswept. Acceptance removes the zone or, if flipping is enabled, reverses its direction and resets it to unswept while retaining its borders, birth time, conviction, and accumulated volume history.
Long: A low pivot confirms AND its conviction is at least the minimum AND its zone is created.
Short: A high pivot confirms AND its conviction is at least the minimum AND its zone is created.
Long: An active bullish zone has a pending sweep AND close exceeds its selected reclaim border.
Short: An active bearish zone has a pending sweep AND close falls below its selected reclaim border.
Long: An active bearish zone has a pending sweep AND its break is accepted AND zone flipping is enabled.
Short: An active bullish zone has a pending sweep AND its break is accepted AND zone flipping is enabled.
