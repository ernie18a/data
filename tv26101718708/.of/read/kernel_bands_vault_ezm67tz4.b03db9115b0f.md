Calculation: Kernel = one-sided Nadaraya-Watson weighted average of the selected OHLC source over a lookback, using Gaussian exp(-i²/(2h²)), Epanechnikov max(0, 1-i²/h²), or tricube max(0, (1-|i/h|³)³) weights for lag i; replace unavailable historical source values with the current source. Bandwidth h is fixed or adaptively multiplied by (1 + ATR/close × a scale factor), with unavailable ATR/close treated as zero.
Calculation: Bands = kernel ± max(sigma multiplier × EMA of rolling standard deviation of (source − kernel), ATR floor multiplier × ATR).
Calculation: State = initially neutral; bullish after the required consecutive closes above their respective upper bands, bearish after the required consecutive closes below their respective lower bands; retain the most recently confirmed state until the opposite confirmation.
Calculation: Dead-zone score = one point each for absolute kernel change over a slope lookback below an ATR multiple, rolling highest high minus lowest low over a range lookback below an ATR multiple, and current volume below a fraction of its positive volume SMA; add two points when state changes over a flip-count lookback meet or exceed a threshold. Missing volume is zero. Dead zone is active when enabled and the score reaches its threshold.
Calculation: Reversal patterns = doji when range > 0 AND absolute body <= a fraction of range; top pin when range > 0 AND upper wick >= a body multiple AND upper wick >= a range fraction; bottom pin uses the lower wick equivalently; bearish engulfing when close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND body > previous body; bullish engulfing when close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND body > previous body.
Calculation: Reversal cooldown = bars since the most recent top or bottom reversal, initialized as ready; when both occur on one bar, record the top reversal for subsequent cooldown.
Calculation: Armed entry = after exits are processed, arm or re-arm the bullish side when state is bullish AND position is not long AND (state just turned bullish OR dead zone just cleared OR position is flat AND close crosses above kernel AND kernel rises); arm or re-arm the bearish side when state is bearish AND position is not short AND (state just turned bearish OR dead zone just cleared OR position is flat AND close crosses below kernel AND kernel does not rise). Cancel an arm when state no longer matches or its age exceeds the entry window; clear it after an entry. A cross above requires current close > current kernel AND previous close <= previous kernel; a cross below reverses these inequalities.
Calculation: Open profit = close − entry price for a long, entry price − close for a short; peak profit is the maximum of zero and all open profits since entry, including the current bar. Process exits before entry triggers and entries on each bar; execute entries and exits at close.
Long: State is bullish.
Short: State is bearish.
Long: Kernel > previous kernel AND previous kernel <= kernel two bars ago.
Short: Kernel <= previous kernel AND previous kernel > kernel two bars ago.
Long: Reversal radar enabled AND reversal cooldown > its threshold AND low <= lower band AND (doji OR bottom pin OR bullish engulfing).
Short: Reversal radar enabled AND reversal cooldown > its threshold AND high >= upper band AND (doji OR top pin OR bearish engulfing).
Long: Divergence enabled AND bars since either divergence > divergence cooldown AND source − source at the divergence lookback < −ATR × divergence lookback × price threshold AND kernel − kernel at the same lookback > ATR × divergence lookback × kernel threshold.
Short: Divergence enabled AND bars since either divergence > divergence cooldown AND source − source at the divergence lookback > ATR × divergence lookback × price threshold AND kernel − kernel at the same lookback < −ATR × divergence lookback × kernel threshold.
Long: Bullish entry is armed AND position is not long AND dead zone is inactive AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in either configured UTC session) AND max(current range, previous range) <= ATR × blow-off threshold AND bars since last entry >= entry cooldown AND (band half-width <= 0 OR close − upper band <= extension limit × band half-width) AND (slope filter disabled OR kernel > previous kernel) AND (candle filter disabled OR close > open).
Short: Bearish entry is armed AND position is not short AND dead zone is inactive AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in either configured UTC session) AND max(current range, previous range) <= ATR × blow-off threshold AND bars since last entry >= entry cooldown AND (band half-width <= 0 OR lower band − close <= extension limit × band half-width) AND (slope filter disabled OR kernel <= previous kernel) AND (candle filter disabled OR close < open).
Exit: long, state just turned bearish OR (open profit > 0 AND reversal radar enabled AND reversal cooldown > its threshold AND high >= upper band AND (doji OR top pin OR bearish engulfing)) OR (peak profit >= minimum peak profit AND open profit <= peak profit × retained-profit fraction) OR (open profit > 0 AND close < lowest low of the preceding structure-lookback bars).
Exit: short, state just turned bullish OR (open profit > 0 AND reversal radar enabled AND reversal cooldown > its threshold AND low <= lower band AND (doji OR bottom pin OR bullish engulfing)) OR (peak profit >= minimum peak profit AND open profit <= peak profit × retained-profit fraction) OR (open profit > 0 AND close > highest high of the preceding structure-lookback bars).
