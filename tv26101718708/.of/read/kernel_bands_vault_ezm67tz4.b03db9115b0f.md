Calculation: Kernel centerline = one-sided close-weighted regression over a lookback, using Gaussian, Epanechnikov, or Tricube weights; bandwidth is fixed or scaled by 1 + ATR / close × a factor
Calculation: Band half-width = maximum of EMA-smoothed rolling standard deviation of close minus the kernel centerline, scaled by a multiplier, and ATR × a floor fraction; upper/lower bands = centerline ± half-width
Calculation: Bullish state = the most recent of the conditions that the required consecutive closes are above the upper band or below the lower band is above; bearish state = it is below
Calculation: Kernel slope = current centerline minus prior centerline
Calculation: Dead-zone score = 1 for each of: absolute centerline change over 10 bars < ATR × 0.6; 20-bar highest high minus lowest low < ATR × 3.5; current volume < 20-bar average volume × 0.8. Add 2 if at least 3 state changes occurred in 30 bars
Calculation: Reversal candle = doji (body ≤ 0.15 × range), top/bottom pin bar (corresponding wick ≥ 2 × body and ≥ 0.55 × range), or bearish/bullish engulfing (current candle has that direction, engulfs the prior body, and has a larger body)
Calculation: Bullish divergence = price change over a slope window < −ATR × window × a threshold and centerline change over that same window > ATR × window × a separate threshold; bearish divergence uses the opposite inequalities
Long: bullish state flips on after the required consecutive closes above the upper band
Short: bearish state flips on after the required consecutive closes below the lower band
Long: lower band is touched (low ≤ lower band) AND a reversal candle is a doji, bottom pin bar, or bullish engulfing AND reversal cooldown has elapsed
Short: upper band is touched (high ≥ upper band) AND a reversal candle is a doji, top pin bar, or bearish engulfing AND reversal cooldown has elapsed
Long: bullish divergence condition is met AND divergence cooldown has elapsed
Short: bearish divergence condition is met AND divergence cooldown has elapsed
Long: bullish state AND (bullish state flip OR dead zone clears OR flat position and close crosses above centerline while centerline rises); trigger arms the entry, which must occur before the state reverts and within the entry wait window; while armed, flat or short position AND session allowed (if session filter enabled, London or New York) AND dead-zone score below trigger (when shield enabled) AND maximum of current and prior candle ranges ≤ ATR × blow-off multiple AND minimum bars since prior entry elapsed AND close − upper band ≤ maximum extension × band half-width (or half-width ≤ 0) AND centerline rises (if slope confirmation enabled) AND close > open (if candle confirmation enabled)
Short: bearish state AND (bearish state flip OR dead zone clears OR flat position and close crosses below centerline while centerline does not rise); trigger arms the entry, which must occur before the state reverts and within the entry wait window; while armed, flat or long position AND session allowed (if session filter enabled, London or New York) AND dead-zone score below trigger (when shield enabled) AND maximum of current and prior candle ranges ≤ ATR × blow-off multiple AND minimum bars since prior entry elapsed AND lower band − close ≤ maximum extension × band half-width (or half-width ≤ 0) AND centerline does not rise (if slope confirmation enabled) AND close < open (if candle confirmation enabled)
Exit: long, bearish state flips on OR a short reversal condition occurs while open profit > 0 OR peak open profit reaches a minimum and current open profit ≤ peak open profit × (1 − giveback fraction) OR close < the lowest low of the prior structure lookback bars while open profit > 0
Exit: short, bullish state flips on OR a long reversal condition occurs while open profit > 0 OR peak open profit reaches a minimum and current open profit ≤ peak open profit × (1 − giveback fraction) OR close > the highest high of the prior structure lookback bars while open profit > 0
