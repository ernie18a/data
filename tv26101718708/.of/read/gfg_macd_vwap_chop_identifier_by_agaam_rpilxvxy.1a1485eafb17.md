Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus signal line, using the selected MACD timeframe (chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe) and the last completed available bar; the MACD line uses a shorter EMA length than its long-term EMA length.
Calculation: CHOP score = count of enabled conditions that are true: absolute MACD histogram divided by ATR is at or below its compression threshold; absolute MACD line divided by ATR is at or below its near-zero threshold; absolute change in MACD line divided by ATR is at or below its flat-slope threshold; absolute change in signal line divided by ATR is at or below its flat-slope threshold; histogram sign changes between consecutive nonzero signs meet or exceed the flip-count threshold within the flip lookback; ADX is below its weak-trend threshold; absolute distance between close and VWAP divided by ATR is at or below the no-trade threshold; that same distance is above the no-trade threshold and at or below the caution threshold; VWAP crosses meet or exceed the cross-count threshold within the VWAP-cross lookback. ATR is floored at the minimum tick size for these ratios. These calculations use the selected MACD timeframe and its last completed available bar.
Long: MACD histogram >= 0 AND CHOP score < minimum CHOP reasons
Short: MACD histogram < 0 AND CHOP score < minimum CHOP reasons
