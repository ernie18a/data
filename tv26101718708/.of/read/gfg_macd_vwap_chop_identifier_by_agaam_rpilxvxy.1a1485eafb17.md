Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line, using a shorter and a longer EMA of a selected price source. Calculate MACD on the chart timeframe, or on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe or the last completed intrabar of a lower timeframe, and carry the result forward between updates.
Calculation: Normalized ATR = the greater of ATR on the MACD timeframe and a positive minimum ATR parameter. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar.
Calculation: Chop score = the count of enabled conditions that are true: absolute MACD histogram divided by normalized ATR <= its threshold; absolute MACD line divided by normalized ATR <= its threshold; absolute change in the mapped MACD line from the previous chart bar divided by normalized ATR <= its threshold; absolute change in the mapped signal line from the previous chart bar divided by normalized ATR <= its threshold; count over a chart-bar lookback of consecutive nonzero mapped histogram values with opposite signs >= its threshold; ADX on the MACD timeframe < its threshold; absolute distance between close and session VWAP on the MACD timeframe divided by normalized ATR <= a smaller distance threshold; that distance > the smaller threshold AND <= a larger distance threshold; count of close crossing session VWAP over a lookback on the MACD timeframe >= its threshold. For higher-timeframe values and counts use the last completed bar; for lower-timeframe values and counts use the last completed intrabar. The two VWAP distance conditions require the VWAP filter to be enabled; the VWAP crossing condition also requires its crossing filter to be enabled.
Long: Chop score < chop threshold AND MACD histogram >= 0
Short: Chop score < chop threshold AND MACD histogram < 0
