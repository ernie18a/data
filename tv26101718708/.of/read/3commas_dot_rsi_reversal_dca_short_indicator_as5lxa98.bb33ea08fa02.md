Calculation: RSI crossing = RSI of closes crosses below its threshold within the configured RSI timeframe; when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time; when lower, use the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; when equal, use the chart bar. Map the crossing result to chart bars with lookahead disabled.
Calculation: Averaging-order level for order k = base entry price × (1 + cumulative deviation), where cumulative deviation is the sum of the first k terms of a geometric sequence starting at the first deviation and using the deviation-step multiplier. Fill at most the next unfilled order per chart bar at its close; its quote size equals the first averaging-order size × the order-size multiplier raised to k − 1.
Calculation: Average entry = total quote size of the base and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order's quote size divided by its fill price. The base fills at the entry bar's close; update average entry after any averaging fill and before checking exits.
Calculation: Trailing reference = lowest chart-bar close since the current short entry among bars where close ≤ the then-current average entry × (1 − take-profit fraction); retain it until the position exits.
Short: Position is flat AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the active window, AND RSI crossing occurs.
Short: Position is short AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the active window, AND filled averaging orders < maximum averaging orders AND close ≥ the next averaging-order level.
Exit: short, (the optional active-window filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the active window) AND close ≥ average entry × (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, (the optional active-window filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the active window) AND close < average entry × (1 + stop-loss fraction) AND trailing reference exists AND close ≥ trailing reference × (1 + trailing fraction).
