Calculation: Effort ratio = volume (or high − low when volume is unavailable) divided by its own moving-average baseline.
Calculation: Confirmed swing = running high confirmed when a completed bar closes at least a reversal distance below it, or running low confirmed when a completed bar closes at least that distance above it; record the completed leg’s mean effort, peak effort, ATR length, and bar count.
Calculation: Climactic run = consecutive lower-high/higher-low swing legs ending at a candidate low (or consecutive higher-low/lower-high legs ending at a candidate high); score it from mean effort, peak effort, ATR length, and ATR length per bar.
Calculation: Range coordinate = (price − range bottom) / range height for accumulation, or (range top − price) / range height for distribution; the climax edge is 0 and reaction edge is 1.
Calculation: Active stop and targets = stop set by the selected stop mode (wick-anchored, structure-anchored, or ATR-based); targets are entry price plus or minus configured multiples of the actual entry-to-stop distance, according to position side.
Long: A confirmed swing low is a selling climax when its run has enough lower lows, its peak effort meets the climax threshold, and its run score meets the score threshold; then a confirmed reaction-side swing forms a range when its height meets the minimum and it retraces enough of the climactic run. In an accumulation range, a confirmed climax-side swing below u=0 is a Spring when a prior secondary test exists (if required) and the bar closes back inside the range, or price closes back inside before the reclaim deadline; alternatively, after at least one secondary test or a Spring, a close beyond the reaction edge by the breakout buffer with a qualifying upward leg is SOS; alternatively, after SOS, the first confirmed climax-side swing with u at or above the LPS floor is LPS.
Short: A confirmed swing high is a buying climax when its run has enough higher highs, its peak effort meets the climax threshold, and its run score meets the score threshold; then a confirmed reaction-side swing forms a range when its height meets the minimum and it retraces enough of the climactic run. In a distribution range, a confirmed climax-side swing above u=0 is UTAD when a prior secondary test exists (if required) and the bar closes back inside the range, or price closes back inside before the reclaim deadline; alternatively, after at least one secondary test or a UTAD, a close beyond the reaction edge by the breakout buffer with a qualifying downward leg is SOW; alternatively, after SOW, the first confirmed climax-side swing with u at or above the LPS floor is LPSY.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target OR a confirmed short signal while holding long.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target OR a confirmed long signal while holding short.
