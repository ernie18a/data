Calculation: Effort ratio = volume, or high minus low when bar-range effort is selected, divided by its SMA; use 1 when the SMA is not positive. Auto selects volume if it exists on the first bar, otherwise bar range.
Calculation: Confirmed swings = alternating running highs and lows. Start with an upward leg; confirm its running high when close is strictly below that high minus a reversal multiple of ATR, or its running low when close is strictly above that low plus the reversal multiple of ATR. On confirmation, start the opposite leg at the confirmed extreme and initialize its extreme to the highest high or lowest low since that extreme. A leg's mean and peak effort use bars after its starting extreme through its ending extreme. Evaluate events on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Climax candidate = a confirmed swing with no active range or candidate. Walk backward through alternating swings from the preceding opposite swing while earlier opposite swings are progressively higher for a swing low or lower for a swing high, and each intervening same-kind swing is above the candidate low or below the candidate high. The run begins at the oldest qualifying opposite swing. Its length is the price distance from that swing to the candidate divided by current ATR; its duration is the difference between their bar indices. Its score is the rounded weighted sum of clamped components for mean run effort minus its baseline, peak run effort above its baseline, ATR length above a length offset, and ATR length per bar; each component is scaled separately and clamped to the unit interval. A candidate qualifies when the count of qualifying intervening swings, peak effort, and score each meet their respective thresholds. A swing low defines the long side and a swing high the short side.
Calculation: Active range = a qualifying climax candidate followed by an opposite-kind swing whose distance from the candidate is at least a minimum ATR multiple and at least a minimum fraction of the candidate's run price length. While waiting, extend the candidate and run length by any deeper same-kind swing; discard it when the current bar is beyond the reaction deadline measured from the latest candidate swing bar. The candidate and reaction swing form the climax and reaction edges. Normalize a price as its distance from the climax edge toward the reaction edge divided by range height, so the edges are 0 and 1. Before the first secondary test or spring, extend the reaction edge on a reaction-side swing beyond it; discard the range if the extended height exceeds a maximum ATR multiple. Before a required first secondary test, extend the climax edge on a reclaimed climax-side swing beyond it. A secondary test is a climax-side swing inside the range at or below a zone threshold, subject to a maximum test count, before a spring or breakout. Discard an unbroken-out range after its lifetime or when close exceeds the climax edge by more than a decisive-break ATR multiple.
Calculation: Spring event = in an active range before breakout, a confirmed climax-side swing beyond the climax edge and, when required, at least one earlier secondary test, with close back inside or on the climax edge. If the confirmation close is outside, hold the swing pending until a close reclaims the edge; discard the range if the reclaim deadline is exceeded or a reaction-side swing is confirmed beyond the climax edge. Apply expiry and decisive-break checks before reclaim. The event occurs on the reclaim bar and its structural level is the swing extreme.
Calculation: Breakout event = in an active range without a pending break or prior breakout, at least one secondary test or spring has occurred, the current leg points toward the reaction edge, close is strictly beyond that edge by a buffer ATR multiple, and mean effort from the last climax-side swing through the current bar meets its threshold OR the absolute distance from that swing to close divided by current ATR meets its threshold; optionally require both effort and length. Its structural level is the reaction edge.
Calculation: Retest event = after a breakout, the first confirmed climax-side swing whose swing bar is later than the breakout bar and whose normalized price is at or above the retest floor. Before checking the swing, discard the range if the search window has expired or, on a bar after breakout, close is below the range midpoint in normalized coordinates. Its structural level is the swing extreme.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry close, select an ATR-distance stop, a stop beyond the entry bar's adverse wick by an ATR buffer, or a stop beyond the event's structural level by that buffer. For either anchored stop, enforce a minimum ATR distance from entry. Use the separately parameterized risk ATR at entry. Initial risk is the absolute distance between entry and initial stop; the first and final profit targets are entry plus or minus increasing multiples of initial risk in the position direction. If enabled, after the first target is first touched, replace the stop with entry plus or minus a risk-ATR offset in the position direction, effective from the next bar.
Long: long-side spring event OR long-side breakout event OR long-side retest event
Short: short-side spring event OR short-side breakout event OR short-side retest event
Long: active short-side range AND a confirmed reaction-side swing has normalized price above 1 AND close has normalized price at or below 1 AND at least one secondary test or spring has occurred AND no breakout has occurred
Short: active long-side range AND a confirmed reaction-side swing has normalized price above 1 AND close has normalized price at or below 1 AND at least one secondary test or spring has occurred AND no breakout has occurred
Long: active short-side range AND no breakout has occurred AND close exceeds the climax edge by more than the decisive-break ATR multiple
Short: active long-side range AND no breakout has occurred AND close exceeds the climax edge by more than the decisive-break ATR multiple
Exit: long, a selected short-side spring, breakout, or retest event occurs
Exit: short, a selected long-side spring, breakout, or retest event occurs
Exit: long, a bar after entry has high at or above the final profit target OR low at or below the stop active at the start of the bar AND neither the first profit target nor the final profit target is touched for the first time on that bar
Exit: short, a bar after entry has low at or below the final profit target OR high at or above the stop active at the start of the bar AND neither the first profit target nor the final profit target is touched for the first time on that bar
