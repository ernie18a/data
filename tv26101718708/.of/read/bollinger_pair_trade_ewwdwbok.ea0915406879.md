Calculation: Bollinger bands = selected moving average of close plus or minus a selected multiple of close’s standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Daily mean and deviation bands = selected moving average of close, plus or minus one or two standard deviations, calculated on higher-timeframe daily bars using the daily bar still forming.
Long: Close crosses the selected lower Bollinger band in either direction, OR (expected-profit mode is enabled AND flat AND close < selected lower Bollinger band AND abs(close − Bollinger basis) × abs(position size) ≥ expected-profit threshold); AND (not against daily mean is disabled OR close ≤ daily mean); AND (one-deviation exclusion is disabled OR close ≤ lower one-deviation daily band); AND (two-deviation exclusion is disabled OR close ≤ lower two-deviation daily band); AND trading date/session filters permit trading; AND (expected-profit mode is disabled OR expected-profit condition is met).
Short: Close crosses the selected upper Bollinger band in either direction, OR (expected-profit mode is enabled AND flat AND close > selected upper Bollinger band AND abs(close − Bollinger basis) × abs(position size) ≥ expected-profit threshold); AND (not against daily mean is disabled OR close ≥ daily mean); AND (one-deviation exclusion is disabled OR close ≥ upper one-deviation daily band); AND (two-deviation exclusion is disabled OR close ≥ upper two-deviation daily band); AND trading date/session filters permit trading; AND (expected-profit mode is disabled OR expected-profit condition is met).
Long: In VIX reverse mode, short position AND close > Bollinger basis; OR (reverse-all mode AND flat AND short-entry condition is met).
Short: In VIX reverse mode, long position AND close < Bollinger basis; OR (reverse-all mode AND flat AND long-entry condition is met).
Exit: Long, enabled per-trade loss threshold is reached OR (SMA exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction) OR (SKP exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction AND Bollinger basis > daily mean) OR (SKPXDSMA exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction AND close crosses daily mean in either direction AND Bollinger basis > daily mean).
Exit: Short, enabled per-trade loss threshold is reached OR (SMA exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction) OR (SKP exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction AND Bollinger basis < daily mean) OR (SKPXDSMA exit mode AND close crosses either Bollinger basis exit band in either direction AND close crosses daily mean in either direction AND Bollinger basis < daily mean).
