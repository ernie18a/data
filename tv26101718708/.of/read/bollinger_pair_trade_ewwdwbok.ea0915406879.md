Calculation: Chart basis = SMA of chart-bar closes, or EMA of chart-bar closes when the EMA option is enabled; chart standard deviation uses the same chart-bar closes and lookback.
Calculation: Entry bands = chart basis plus or minus the selected standard-deviation multiplier times chart standard deviation.
Calculation: Exit bands = chart basis plus or minus the exit-band multiplier times chart standard deviation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe basis = SMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe standard deviation uses the same closes and lookback.
Calculation: Cross = current value is above the current level AND previous value was at or below the previous level, OR current value is below the current level AND previous value was at or above the previous level.
Long: Close crosses the lower entry band AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND the expected-profit filter is disabled AND the external volatility filter is disabled AND (position is not flat OR the trend filter is disabled OR close <= higher-timeframe basis) AND (position is not flat OR the first higher-timeframe band filter is disabled OR close <= higher-timeframe basis - higher-timeframe standard deviation) AND (position is not flat OR the second higher-timeframe band filter is disabled OR close <= higher-timeframe basis - 2 * higher-timeframe standard deviation) AND (the trend filter is disabled OR position is long OR close <= higher-timeframe basis).
Short: Close crosses the upper entry band AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND the expected-profit filter is disabled AND the external volatility filter is disabled AND (position is not flat OR the trend filter is disabled OR close >= higher-timeframe basis) AND (position is not flat OR the first higher-timeframe band filter is disabled OR close >= higher-timeframe basis + higher-timeframe standard deviation) AND (position is not flat OR the second higher-timeframe band filter is disabled OR close >= higher-timeframe basis + 2 * higher-timeframe standard deviation) AND (the trend filter is disabled OR position is not long OR close >= higher-timeframe basis).
Exit: short, close crosses the lower entry band AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND the external volatility filter is disabled AND the trend filter is enabled AND close > higher-timeframe basis.
Exit: long, close crosses the upper entry band AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND the external volatility filter is disabled AND the trend filter is enabled AND close < higher-timeframe basis.
Exit: long, datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND close crosses either exit band AND (exit mode is SMA OR exit mode is SKP AND chart basis > higher-timeframe basis OR exit mode is SKPXDSMA AND close crosses higher-timeframe basis AND chart basis > higher-timeframe basis).
Exit: short, datetime is within the selected backtest interval AND datetime is within an enabled trading session AND close crosses either exit band AND (exit mode is SMA OR exit mode is SKP AND chart basis < higher-timeframe basis OR exit mode is SKPXDSMA AND close crosses higher-timeframe basis AND chart basis < higher-timeframe basis).
Exit: long, close-after-interval is enabled AND datetime is outside the selected backtest interval.
Exit: short, close-after-interval is enabled AND datetime is outside the selected backtest interval.
