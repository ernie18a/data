Calculation: For each completed higher-timeframe bar, pivot = (high + low + close) / 3, BC = (high + low) / 2, TC = 2 × pivot − BC, CPR top = max(TC, BC), and CPR bottom = min(TC, BC).
Calculation: R1 = 2 × pivot − low, R2 = pivot + high − low, S1 = 2 × pivot − high, and S2 = pivot − high + low, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: CPR bottom of the last completed higher-timeframe bar > CPR top of the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Short: CPR top of the last completed higher-timeframe bar < CPR bottom of the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Close > R1 AND previous close <= previous R1.
Long: Close > R2 AND previous close <= previous R2.
Short: Close < S1 AND previous close >= previous S1.
Short: Close < S2 AND previous close >= previous S2.
