Calculation: Liquidation clusters = On each completed chart bar, add projections from each newly confirmed pivot high and pivot low, using equal left and right pivot lengths. For each enabled leverage tier, in order from the smallest to the largest projection distance, project a level above the pivot high or below the pivot low by that tier's fractional distance. Assign it mass equal to a tier-specific weight multiplied by a base mass plus a positive coefficient times the positive part of the current bar's volume z-score; use zero for the z-score when its standard deviation is unavailable or zero. Merge each projection into the first existing cluster on the same side whose level is within a configured multiple of current ATR, replacing its level with the mass-weighted average and adding its mass; otherwise append a new cluster. After adding projections, record and remove every upper cluster whose level is at or below the current high and every lower cluster whose level is at or above the current low. Then multiply remaining masses by a decay factor, remove clusters below a minimum mass, and remove the weakest remaining clusters until the cluster count is within its limit.
Calculation: Net pull = Sum, over surviving clusters after the current bar's removals and decay, of mass times sign of cluster level minus close, divided by one plus the square of (cluster level minus close) divided by a configured multiple of ATR.
Long: Current low <= at least one lower liquidation cluster level after new projections are added and before swept clusters are removed.
Short: Current high >= at least one upper liquidation cluster level after new projections are added and before swept clusters are removed.
Long: Net pull >= a positive display threshold.
Short: Net pull <= the negative of the positive display threshold.
