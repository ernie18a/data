Calculation: Source bars = chart bars or bars aggregated to a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and process it at the close of the first chart bar in the next higher-timeframe period.
Calculation: Equal-level tolerance = a configurable multiplier times the EMA of the average of the absolute bar-to-bar change in high and the absolute bar-to-bar change in low, calculated on source bars.
Calculation: Equal-high formation = on a source bar, either (1) its high differs from the preceding high by less than the current tolerance, the preceding pair of highs did not differ by less than its tolerance, and, if enabled, current RSI is above its neutral level plus a configurable strength; or (2) separated formations are enabled, the preceding bar is a strict local high relative to its immediate neighbors, neither the current nor preceding source bar has an adjacent equal-high formation, RSI on the preceding bar passes the same optional filter, and an earlier strict local high within a configurable lookback differs from that preceding high by less than the preceding bar's tolerance while every intervening high is below both endpoints. For case (2), use the nearest qualifying earlier high.
Calculation: Equal-low formation = on a source bar, either (1) its low differs from the preceding low by less than the current tolerance, the preceding pair of lows did not differ by less than its tolerance, and, if enabled, current RSI is below its neutral level minus a configurable strength; or (2) separated formations are enabled, the preceding bar is a strict local low relative to its immediate neighbors, neither the current nor preceding source bar has an adjacent equal-low formation, RSI on the preceding bar passes the same optional filter, and an earlier strict local low within a configurable lookback differs from that preceding low by less than the preceding bar's tolerance while every intervening low is above both endpoints. For case (2), use the nearest qualifying earlier low.
Calculation: Equal-high zone = on an equal-high formation whose first endpoint is within a configurable maximum age, store the higher endpoint high as its outer edge and the highest open or close of the two endpoints as its inner edge. Equal-low zone = on an equal-low formation subject to the same age rule, store the lower endpoint low as its outer edge and the lowest open or close of the two endpoints as its inner edge. A zone is confirmed at the close of its formation source bar. Keep zones in creation order across source timeframes and delete the oldest stored zone when a configurable storage limit is reached.
Calculation: Active zone = a stored zone that has not expired or been swept. On each completed source bar, including the formation bar, expire it when source bars elapsed since its first endpoint exceed the maximum age. In wick mode, sweep an equal-high zone when source high exceeds its outer edge and an equal-low zone when source low is below its outer edge. In body mode, use source close instead of high or low; if rejection allowance is enabled, the preceding source close must also be beyond the same outer edge. A zone swept but not expired at the current chart bar's close remains eligible for rejection on that bar.
Long: An equal-low zone forms.
Short: An equal-high zone forms.
Long: A chart bar opens at or after an equal-low zone's confirmation AND the zone is active or was swept without expiry at that chart bar's close AND chart low is at or below the zone's upper edge AND chart high is at or above its lower edge AND (chart open is at or above the lower edge OR preceding chart close is at or above the lower edge) AND chart close is above the upper edge.
Short: A chart bar opens at or after an equal-high zone's confirmation AND the zone is active or was swept without expiry at that chart bar's close AND chart low is at or below the zone's upper edge AND chart high is at or above its lower edge AND (chart open is at or below the upper edge OR preceding chart close is at or below the upper edge) AND chart close is below the lower edge.
