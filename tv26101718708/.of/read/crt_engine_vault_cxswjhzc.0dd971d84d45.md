Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep = compare a higher-timeframe bar with the immediately preceding completed higher-timeframe bar: a high sweep has high > preceding high AND close < preceding high; a low sweep has low < preceding low AND close > preceding low. The higher-timeframe bar may be the bar still forming or the last completed bar, as specified below.
Calculation: Manipulation level = for a completed higher-timeframe sweep, start with the chart bar that first established its extreme high for a high sweep or extreme low for a low sweep; include its body and the bodies of consecutive preceding bullish chart bars for a high sweep or consecutive preceding non-bullish chart bars for a low sweep, up to a lookback limit. The level is the lowest included body price for a high sweep or the highest included body price for a low sweep. If both sides were swept, use the high sweep.
Long: The still-forming higher-timeframe bar has a low sweep.
Short: The still-forming higher-timeframe bar has a high sweep.
Long: At the start of a new higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a low sweep and no high sweep, OR it has both sweeps AND its close > its open.
Short: At the start of a new higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a high sweep and no low sweep, OR it has both sweeps AND its close <= its open.
Long: During a higher-timeframe bar, after its first chart bar, a chart bar has breached the preceding completed higher-timeframe low during the current higher-timeframe bar AND close > that low AND no low sweep is currently active. A low sweep becomes inactive when a later chart bar has low <= the lowest price reached in the current higher-timeframe bar at the sweep, or when a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Short: During a higher-timeframe bar, after its first chart bar, a chart bar has breached the preceding completed higher-timeframe high during the current higher-timeframe bar AND close < that high AND no high sweep is currently active. A high sweep becomes inactive when a later chart bar has high >= the highest price reached in the current higher-timeframe bar at the sweep, or when a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Long: After the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a low sweep AND close > its manipulation level AND no manipulation-level confirmation has occurred in the current higher-timeframe bar AND a simultaneous high sweep has not also confirmed below the level on this chart bar.
Short: After the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a high sweep AND close < its manipulation level AND no manipulation-level confirmation has occurred in the current higher-timeframe bar.
Long: At the start of a new higher-timeframe bar, the low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar > the high of the higher-timeframe bar two bars before it.
Short: At the start of a new higher-timeframe bar, the high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar < the low of the higher-timeframe bar two bars before it.
Long: Chart close > a moving average of close calculated independently on the chart timeframe or on a higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe value or the last lower-timeframe value within the chart bar.
Short: Chart close < a moving average of close calculated independently on the chart timeframe or on a higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe value or the last lower-timeframe value within the chart bar.
