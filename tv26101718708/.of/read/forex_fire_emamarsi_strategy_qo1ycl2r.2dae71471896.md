Calculation: Moving average = SMA, EMA, WMA, or VWMA of close, using the same type and length on both timeframes.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = entry-signal close multiplied by one minus the trailing percentage; on each bar in a long position with close above entry price, update it to the greater of its previous value and close multiplied by one minus the trailing percentage. Reset it on every entry signal using that signal's side and close.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = entry-signal close multiplied by one plus the trailing percentage; on each bar in a short position with close below entry price, update it to the lesser of its previous value and close multiplied by one plus the trailing percentage. Reset it on every entry signal using that signal's side and close.
Long: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA.
Short: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA.
Long: Chart-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI.
Short: Chart-timeframe shorter RSI <= longer RSI.
Long: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Higher-timeframe shorter RSI <= longer RSI, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > chart-timeframe moving average AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI > RSI midpoint AND volume > SMA of volume.
Short: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < chart-timeframe moving average AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI < longer RSI AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI < RSI midpoint AND volume > SMA of volume.
Long: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA AND chart-timeframe close > higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe longer RSI > lower RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for all higher-timeframe calculations.
Short: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA AND chart-timeframe close < higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe longer RSI < higher RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe shorter RSI < longer RSI; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for all higher-timeframe calculations.
Long: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > chart-timeframe moving average AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI > RSI midpoint AND volume > SMA of volume AND higher-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe longer RSI > lower RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe shorter RSI > longer RSI AND (chart-timeframe shorter EMA crosses above longer EMA OR close crosses above chart-timeframe moving average); use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for all higher-timeframe calculations.
Short: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < chart-timeframe moving average AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI < longer RSI AND chart-timeframe shorter RSI < RSI midpoint AND volume > SMA of volume AND higher-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe longer RSI < higher RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe shorter RSI < longer RSI AND (chart-timeframe shorter EMA crosses below longer EMA OR close crosses below chart-timeframe moving average); use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for all higher-timeframe calculations.
Exit: long, chart-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA OR chart-timeframe shorter RSI > overbought threshold OR (stop loss enabled AND low < entry-signal close multiplied by one minus stop-loss percentage AND stop level > zero) OR (take profit enabled AND high > entry-signal close multiplied by one plus take-profit percentage AND take-profit level > zero) OR (trailing stop enabled AND low < long trailing stop AND long trailing stop > zero) OR (ATR exit enabled AND low <= close minus ATR multiplied by an ATR multiplier).
Exit: short, chart-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA OR chart-timeframe shorter RSI < oversold threshold OR (stop loss enabled AND high > entry-signal close multiplied by one plus stop-loss percentage AND stop level > zero) OR (take profit enabled AND low < entry-signal close multiplied by one minus take-profit percentage AND take-profit level > zero) OR (trailing stop enabled AND high > short trailing stop AND short trailing stop > zero) OR (ATR exit enabled AND high >= close plus ATR multiplied by an ATR multiplier).
