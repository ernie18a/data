Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of volume × (open + high + low + close) / 4 divided by cumulative volume, reset at each UTC day boundary for the daily session or at each regular-session open for the regular session; the regular-session calculation includes only bars in that session.
Calculation: VWAP deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative sum of volume × ((open + high + low + close) / 4)² divided by cumulative volume minus session VWAP²), using the same bars as the session VWAP. Upper and lower VWAP bands are session VWAP plus and minus a band multiplier × VWAP deviation.
Calculation: Session value-area boundaries = divide a fixed price range into equal rows at the start of each profile session. Center the range on the first bar's high-low midpoint and extend each side by a range multiplier × max(first bar's high − low, minimum price increment × a multiplier). For each included bar with positive volume, add to each overlapping row the bar volume × the overlap of its high-low range with that row divided by max(high − low, minimum price increment). The point of control is the lowest row with the greatest accumulated volume. Starting at that row, expand by one adjacent row per step toward the greater adjacent volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until the accumulated volume reaches the specified fraction of total profile volume or all rows are included; perform at least one expansion when more than one row exists. The value-area high is the upper edge of the highest included row and the value-area low is the lower edge of the lowest included row. Reset the daily profile at its configured UTC day offset; reset the regular-session profile at each regular-session open and include only bars in that session.
Long: close >= daily session VWAP OR close > daily upper VWAP band OR close > daily profile value-area high.
Short: close < daily session VWAP OR close < daily lower VWAP band OR close < daily profile value-area low.
Long: bar is in the regular session AND (close >= regular-session VWAP OR close > regular-session upper VWAP band OR close > regular-session profile value-area high).
Short: bar is in the regular session AND (close < regular-session VWAP OR close < regular-session lower VWAP band OR close < regular-session profile value-area low).
