Calculation: Liquidity mean = blend the simple average of close and the weighted average of adjusted close, using weights from relative volume divided by relative true range, clamped to [0.15, 6] and raised to 1.1; adjusted close = close + 0.105 × (lower wick − upper wick); blend = 90% simple average + 10% weighted average.
Calculation: Upper/lower deviation = square root of close dispersion around the liquidity mean squared plus (0.3 × RMS upper/lower wick)² plus (0.3 × RMS upper/lower liquidity stress)². Liquidity stress is wick size plus sweep overshoot terms when the current high/low reaches within 0.35 average true ranges of, or sweeps, the prior-window highest high/lowest low. Use a shorter lookback for sweep levels and the primary lookback for averages and RMS values.
Calculation: Normalized price distance = (close − liquidity mean) / upper deviation when close is at or above the mean; otherwise divide by lower deviation.
Calculation: Inner reclaim threshold = outer deviation threshold × reclaim ratio.
Calculation: Path efficiency = absolute net close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, capped at 1.
Calculation: Liquidity tension target = clamp(2 × (close − midpoint of the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high and low) / that bar's range × path efficiency, −maximum tension, +maximum tension). Update tension toward the target with coefficient 2/(build length+1) when target and prior tension have the same sign and target has greater magnitude; otherwise use 2/(release length+1). Bull regime: tension ≥ trend threshold. Bear regime: tension ≤ −trend threshold.
Calculation: OU filter = enabled setting is off, or the log of close/liquidity mean has an AR(1) half-life between 2 and 2 × primary lookback / strictness, and absolute change in liquidity mean over 6 × primary lookback divided by average band deviation is ≤ 1 / strictness.
Calculation: Long armed state = on a confirmed bar, clear both armed states if the OU filter fails or either reclaim triggers; otherwise set long armed and clear short armed when normalized price distance ≤ −outer deviation threshold, then clear long armed when distance ≥ 0. Short armed state is symmetric: set short armed and clear long armed when distance ≥ outer deviation threshold, then clear short armed when distance ≤ 0.
Long: Bull regime OR (OU filter passes AND long armed state AND normalized price distance crosses above −inner reclaim threshold AND normalized price distance < 0 AND (trend alignment is disabled OR Bull regime)).
Short: Bear regime OR (OU filter passes AND short armed state AND normalized price distance crosses below inner reclaim threshold AND normalized price distance > 0 AND (trend alignment is disabled OR Bear regime)).
