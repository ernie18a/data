Calculation: Rolling averages, weighted averages, root-mean-square values and deviations use available nonmissing observations in their lookback. True range is the maximum of high minus low, absolute high minus previous close, and absolute low minus previous close; use current close when previous close is unavailable.
Calculation: Liquidity mean = simple moving average of close plus 0.1 times the difference between the weighted moving average of rejection price and the simple moving average of close, using the primary lookback. Rejection price = close + 0.105 × (lower wick − upper wick). Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low. Weight = acceptance^1.1. Acceptance = clamp(relative volume / max(relative true range, 0.20), 0.15, 6); relative volume and relative true range are current volume and true range divided by their respective primary-lookback averages. If volume or its average is missing or nonpositive, acceptance = 1; if average true range is unavailable or too small, relative true range = 1. If the weighted average is unavailable, use the simple moving average as the liquidity mean.
Calculation: Upper and lower deviations = sqrt(close deviation² + (0.3 × corresponding wick RMS)² + (0.3 × corresponding excess RMS)²), using the primary lookback. Close deviation is the root mean squared difference between each close in the lookback and the current liquidity mean. Upper excess is zero unless high is at least the prior-high extreme minus 0.35 × average true range; otherwise it is upper wick plus, if high exceeds that extreme and close is below it, 2 × (high − extreme) + 0.5 × (high − close). Lower excess is zero unless low is at most the prior-low extreme plus 0.35 × average true range; otherwise it is lower wick plus, if low is below that extreme and close is above it, 2 × (extreme − low) + 0.5 × (close − low). The extremes are the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback ending at the previous bar; use current true range if its average is unavailable.
Calculation: Normalized displacement = (close − liquidity mean) / upper deviation when close >= liquidity mean, otherwise (close − liquidity mean) / lower deviation; it is unavailable if the selected deviation is too small.
Calculation: Mean-reversion filter passes when enough history exists beyond the regression and primary lookbacks, normalized displacement is available, and the autoregressive half-life is between a minimum and a maximum inclusive and normalized mean drift is at most its maximum. Fit an intercept-inclusive first-order autoregression to log(max(close, small positive constant) / liquidity mean) over a longer regression lookback; half-life = log(0.5) / log(slope), available only for slope between 0 and 1. Normalized mean drift = absolute change in liquidity mean over the primary lookback divided by the average of upper and lower deviations. The maximum half-life increases with the primary lookback and decreases with filter strictness; the maximum drift decreases with filter strictness. When the filter is disabled, it passes whenever normalized displacement is available.
Calculation: Liquidity tension starts at zero. On each chart bar, calculate normalized position = 2 × (close − midpoint) / range, using the high and low of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe for midpoint and range. Path efficiency = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, capped at 1; use zero when unavailable or the denominator is too small. Target tension = normalized position × path efficiency, clamped to ±maximum tension; use zero when the higher-timeframe range is unavailable or nonpositive. Update tension toward target by 2 / (build length + 1) if previous tension and target have the same sign or either is zero, and target has greater absolute magnitude; otherwise update by 2 / (release length + 1). Clamp the result to ±maximum tension.
Calculation: Long and short armed states start false and are read before updating them on each confirmed bar. After evaluating both reclaim decisions, clear both states if the mean-reversion filter fails or either reclaim decision occurs. Otherwise, arm long and clear short when normalized displacement <= negative outer-band multiplier; arm short and clear long when normalized displacement >= outer-band multiplier; then clear long when displacement >= 0 and clear short when displacement <= 0.
Long: Liquidity tension >= positive trend threshold.
Short: Liquidity tension <= negative trend threshold.
Long: Long state is armed AND mean-reversion filter passes AND normalized displacement crosses above negative outer-band multiplier × reclaim ratio AND normalized displacement < 0 AND (trend alignment is disabled OR liquidity tension >= positive trend threshold).
Short: Short state is armed AND mean-reversion filter passes AND normalized displacement crosses below outer-band multiplier × reclaim ratio AND normalized displacement > 0 AND (trend alignment is disabled OR liquidity tension <= negative trend threshold).
