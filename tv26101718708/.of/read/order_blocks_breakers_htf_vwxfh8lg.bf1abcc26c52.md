Calculation: Higher-timeframe data = For each enabled higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; use its preceding bar for the prior-bar comparisons and zone boundaries.
Calculation: Stored zones = On a new higher-timeframe detection, store the preceding higher-timeframe bar's high and low as the zone top and bottom if its height divided by the detection bar's higher-timeframe ATR meets the minimum-size filter, or the filter is disabled; use 1 as ATR if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Store bullish and bearish zones together per higher timeframe, removing the oldest when the capacity is exceeded. Update newly stored and existing zones on each chart bar: an unbroken bullish zone becomes a bearish breaker when close < bottom; an unbroken bearish zone becomes a bullish breaker when close > top. Otherwise, an unmitigated bullish zone becomes mitigated when low <= top, and an unmitigated bearish zone when high >= bottom; in the alternative mitigation mode, either becomes mitigated when bottom <= close <= top. Breakers remain breakers; mitigated unbroken zones remain mitigated.
Calculation: Bullish zone presence = Chart-bar low <= top AND high >= bottom for any stored unmitigated bullish zone or bullish breaker, after updating zones.
Calculation: Bearish zone presence = Chart-bar low <= top AND high >= bottom for any stored unmitigated bearish zone or bearish breaker, after updating zones.
Long: Previous higher-timeframe close < previous higher-timeframe open AND higher-timeframe close > previous higher-timeframe high AND this detected preceding bar differs from the preceding chart bar's detected bar or the preceding chart bar had no detection.
Short: Previous higher-timeframe close > previous higher-timeframe open AND higher-timeframe close < previous higher-timeframe low AND this detected preceding bar differs from the preceding chart bar's detected bar or the preceding chart bar had no detection.
Long: A stored unbroken bearish zone has chart-bar close > zone top.
Short: A stored unbroken bullish zone has chart-bar close < zone bottom.
Long: Bullish zone presence is true AND previous chart-bar bullish zone presence is false.
Short: Bearish zone presence is true AND previous chart-bar bearish zone presence is false.
