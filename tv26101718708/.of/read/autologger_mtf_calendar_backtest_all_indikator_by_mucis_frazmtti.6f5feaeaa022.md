Calculation: Analysis close = close from the selected analysis timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current bar, including a bar still forming.
Long: datetime is within the configured backtest date range AND ((strategy is EMA Crossover AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND analysis close > long-term EMA) OR (strategy is RSI Strategy AND RSI crosses below its oversold threshold) OR (strategy is MACD Strategy AND MACD line crosses above signal line) OR (strategy is Bollinger Bands Breakout AND analysis close crosses above the lower band) OR (strategy is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend direction changes to up on the chart timeframe) OR (strategy is Custom AND shorter-lookback SMA crosses above longer-lookback SMA))
Short: datetime is within the configured backtest date range AND ((strategy is EMA Crossover AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND analysis close < long-term EMA) OR (strategy is RSI Strategy AND RSI crosses above its overbought threshold) OR (strategy is MACD Strategy AND MACD line crosses below signal line) OR (strategy is Bollinger Bands Breakout AND analysis close crosses below the upper band) OR (strategy is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend direction changes to down on the chart timeframe) OR (strategy is Custom AND shorter-lookback SMA crosses below longer-lookback SMA))
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR, after high reaches entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction / 2), low <= highest high since trailing activation − entry price × stop-loss fraction / 2
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR, after low reaches entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction / 2), high >= lowest low since trailing activation + entry price × stop-loss fraction / 2
