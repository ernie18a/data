Calculation: RangeHigh/RangeLow/RangeMid = high, low and (high+low)/2 of the most recent confirmed volume-peak bar. A volume-peak bar has volume greater than each of the N bars before it and each of the N bars after it, with the same N on both sides. It is confirmed N bars after it occurs. When a new peak is confirmed, the new range replaces the old one and the signal state is cleared. The new range is also applied to the bars from the peak bar through the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Reset = close crosses RangeMid (close > RangeMid AND previous close <= RangeMid, OR close < RangeMid AND previous close >= RangeMid). Reset is evaluated before the signals on the same bar.
Long: a range exists AND no Long has fired since the latest of (Reset, a Short signal, a new range confirmation) AND [(close > RangeHigh AND previous close <= RangeHigh) OR (open > RangeHigh AND close < RangeHigh)]
Short: a range exists AND no Short has fired since the latest of (Reset, a Long signal, a new range confirmation) AND [(close < RangeLow AND previous close >= RangeLow) OR (open < RangeLow AND close > RangeLow)]
