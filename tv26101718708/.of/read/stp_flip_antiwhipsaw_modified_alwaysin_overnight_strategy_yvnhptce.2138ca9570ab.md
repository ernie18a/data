Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Trail bands = EMA(close, configurable lookback) ± ATR × configurable multiplier; optionally widen or narrow the multiplier by ATR's percentile rank over a longer lookback, and optionally ratchet the lower band upward in long direction or the upper band downward in short direction; reset the ratchet on a direction change
Calculation: Direction = long when close > prior upper trail band; short when close < prior lower trail band; otherwise retain prior direction
Calculation: Regime score = weighted combination of ADX trend strength, inverse Choppiness Index, and squared correlation of close with bar index; higher score means more trending
Calculation: Breakout distance = distance close crossed beyond the prior trail band, divided by ATR
Calculation: Quality score = normalized weighted average of directional higher-timeframe bias, volume confirmation, directional RSI momentum, regime score, and breakout strength; omit disabled or unavailable components from the normalization; higher-timeframe bias compares the last completed bar of a higher timeframe's close with its EMA
Long: direction changes from short to long AND bar is confirmed AND (quality score ≥ configured minimum, when applicable) AND (regime is not choppy, when hard choppy-signal suppression is enabled) AND (fast EMA > slow EMA and, when required, slow EMA is rising, when trend alignment is enabled) AND (ATR ≥ its configurable-lookback average × configured threshold, when volatility expansion is enabled) AND (breakout distance ≥ configured minimum, when minimum breakout is enabled)
Short: direction changes from long to short AND bar is confirmed AND (quality score ≥ configured minimum, when applicable) AND (regime is not choppy, when hard choppy-signal suppression is enabled) AND (fast EMA < slow EMA and, when required, slow EMA is falling, when trend alignment is enabled) AND (ATR ≥ its configurable-lookback average × configured threshold, when volatility expansion is enabled) AND (breakout distance ≥ configured minimum, when minimum breakout is enabled)
Exit: long, short decision occurs
Exit: short, long decision occurs
