Calculation: Trail bands and direction = On each chart bar, calculate ATR and EMA of close. Set the raw upper and lower bands to EMA plus and minus ATR times a band multiplier. Optionally adjust the multiplier by a low, middle, or high factor according to the percentile rank of ATR over a lookback. Initialize direction as neutral. After a warmup of the longest ATR, trail EMA, and regression lookbacks plus a warmup offset, set direction to long if close is above the previous upper band, to short if close is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain it. Without ratcheting, both bands equal their raw values. With ratcheting, on a direction change reset both bands to raw values; otherwise in long direction set the lower band to the maximum of its raw value and its previous value, and in short direction set the upper band to the minimum of its raw value and its previous value. The other band remains raw. The active trail is the lower band in long direction and the upper band in short direction.
Calculation: Regime score = A weighted sum of capped ADX, inverted and clamped Choppiness Index, and the squared correlation of close with bar index. Normalize ADX by a scale and cap it at the score maximum. Calculate Choppiness Index as the score maximum times log10(sum of true range over its lookback divided by the range from highest high to lowest low over that lookback) divided by log10 of the lookback; use maximum choppiness for a zero range. Use zero for unavailable ADX or correlation.
Calculation: Directional quality score = Divide the weighted sum of higher-timeframe alignment, volume confirmation, RSI alignment, regime score, and breakout strength by the sum of active weights, then scale to the score range. Higher-timeframe alignment contributes a full, half, or zero weighted score when aligned, equal or unavailable, or opposed, respectively; determine alignment by comparing the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA. Its weight is inactive when the option is disabled or data is unavailable, but its half-score contribution remains in the numerator. Volume contributes its full weight when current volume exceeds a multiple of its SMA, otherwise zero; its weight is inactive when disabled or volume data is unavailable. RSI contributes its full weight when RSI is above its midpoint for long or below its midpoint for short. Breakout strength is the absolute distance from close to the previous upper band for long or previous lower band for short, divided by ATR and capped and normalized by a breakout-strength cap. Regime and breakout contributions are proportional to their scores.
Calculation: Directional signal = A change in trail direction from short to long or long to short on a completed bar, AND directional quality score at or above its minimum, AND, if the choppiness filter is enabled, regime score at or above its choppy threshold, AND, if trend alignment is enabled, the shorter EMA above the longer EMA for long or below it for short, with the longer EMA rising for long or falling for short if its slope filter is enabled, AND, if volatility expansion is enabled, ATR at or above a multiple of its SMA, AND, if minimum breakout is enabled, absolute distance from close to the previous upper band for long or previous lower band for short divided by ATR at or above the minimum breakout threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed reversal = Start a pending reversal when an opposite directional signal occurs during the selected trading window and, if confirmation distance is positive, close is beyond the new active trail by more than that distance times ATR. On subsequent bars retain and increment the pending count while direction remains opposite to the position and close remains beyond the active trail by more than the confirmation distance times ATR; otherwise clear it. A reversal is confirmed when the count reaches its threshold. Clear the pending reversal after an accepted reversal. Without confirmation, the reversal occurs on the opposite directional signal within the selected trading window. In either mode, optionally require regime score at or above the choppy threshold, directional EMA alignment, ATR expansion, minimum absolute breakout distance from the applicable previous band divided by ATR, and a minimum number of bars since the last accepted reversal. The EMA slope requirement applies when enabled. Reset the reversal cooldown on an accepted reversal.
Long: Directional signal changes from short to long.
Short: Directional signal changes from long to short.
Long: Position side is short AND a long reversal is confirmed.
Short: Position side is long AND a short reversal is confirmed.
Exit: long, a short reversal is confirmed OR the selected trading window has just ended and session-end flattening is enabled.
Exit: short, a long reversal is confirmed OR the selected trading window has just ended and session-end flattening is enabled.
