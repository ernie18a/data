Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session window and time zone; lock them on the first bar outside the window and retain them until the next window starts.
Calculation: Opening range height = opening range high minus opening range low.
Long: Opening range is locked AND current bar is outside the session window AND (minimum range filter is disabled OR opening range height >= chart-timeframe ATR × minimum range multiplier) AND (close > opening range high in close-trigger mode OR high > opening range high in wick-trigger mode) AND (first-break-only mode is disabled OR no earlier upside breakout has occurred since the current session window started).
Short: Opening range is locked AND current bar is outside the session window AND (minimum range filter is disabled OR opening range height >= chart-timeframe ATR × minimum range multiplier) AND (close < opening range low in close-trigger mode OR low < opening range low in wick-trigger mode) AND (first-break-only mode is disabled OR no earlier downside breakout has occurred since the current session window started).
Long: Retest marking is enabled AND an upside breakout occurred since the current session window started AND no earlier upside retest occurred since that window started AND the current bar is not an upside breakout AND low <= opening range high AND close > opening range low.
Short: Retest marking is enabled AND a downside breakout occurred since the current session window started AND no earlier downside retest occurred since that window started AND the current bar is not a downside breakout AND high >= opening range low AND close < opening range high.
