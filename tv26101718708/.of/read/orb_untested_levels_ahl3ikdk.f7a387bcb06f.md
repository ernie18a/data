Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening-range session, updated during that session and held until the next session; midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2.
Calculation: Untested session levels = each completed configured session's high and low, retained for a configured lookback from the session start until tested; a level is tested when a subsequent bar has high >= level AND low <= level. Include a level on its first test bar.
Calculation: Active session extremes = highest high and lowest low through the previous bar of the configured active session, available after a configured elapsed time from its start.
Calculation: Opening-range overlap = opening range exists AND low <= opening-range high AND high >= opening-range low.
Calculation: Long sweep = previous bar's low <= an eligible untested session low or active session low AND min(previous open, previous close) > that level AND close two bars ago > that level AND previous bar has no opening-range overlap. Short sweep = previous bar's high >= an eligible untested session high or active session high AND max(previous open, previous close) < that level AND close two bars ago < that level AND previous bar has no opening-range overlap.
Calculation: Opening-range states = separate long and short states initialized to 0 each day. Update only within the configured opening-range trading window, while opening-range width < the configured maximum and no opening-range entry has occurred that day. Long state 0 becomes 1 on close > opening-range high AND close > open; state 1 remains 1 until low <= midpoint, then becomes 2 if close > midpoint AND close < open, otherwise 0; state 2 becomes 3 if close > midpoint AND close > open, otherwise 0. Short state 0 becomes 1 on close < opening-range low AND close < open; state 1 remains 1 until high >= midpoint, then becomes 2 if close < midpoint AND close > open, otherwise 0; state 2 becomes 3 if close < midpoint AND close < open, otherwise 0. Each state advances at most once per bar; the entry limit is shared by both sides.
Long: Within the configured trading session AND no sweep entry has occurred today AND long sweep AND close > open AND no opening-range overlap.
Short: Within the configured trading session AND no sweep entry has occurred today AND short sweep AND close < open AND no opening-range overlap AND no long sweep entry occurred on this bar.
Long: Within the configured opening-range trading window AND opening-range width < the configured maximum AND no opening-range breakout marking has occurred today AND close > opening-range high AND close > open.
Short: Within the configured opening-range trading window AND opening-range width < the configured maximum AND no opening-range breakout marking has occurred today AND close < opening-range low AND close < open.
Long: Long opening-range state changes from 1 to 2.
Short: Short opening-range state changes from 1 to 2.
Long: Long opening-range state changes from 2 to 3.
Short: Short opening-range state changes from 2 to 3.
