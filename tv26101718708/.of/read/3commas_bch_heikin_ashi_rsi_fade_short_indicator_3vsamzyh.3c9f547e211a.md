Short: Position is flat AND RSI of close on the configured timeframe is above a threshold (or below it when the opposite comparison is selected) AND at least a configured number of consecutive Heikin Ashi candles on a configured timeframe have close > open (or close < open when bearish direction is selected). For each requested timeframe, use the chart bar itself when equal to the chart timeframe, the last completed bar when higher, or the last completed intrabar within the chart bar when lower; calculate the streak on its own timeframe. Record the chart bar close as entry price.
Exit: short, chart bar close <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR chart bar close >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction)
