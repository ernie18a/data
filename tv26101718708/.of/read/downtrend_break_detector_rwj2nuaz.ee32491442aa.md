Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows that are respectively highest or lowest over equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot; recognize them only after the later bars complete. Store confirmed pivot highs newest first, and remove highs older than the history limit only when a new pivot high is confirmed. Keep the two latest confirmed pivot lows.
Calculation: Saved descending line = whenever a pivot high is confirmed or the bar containing the highest high in the recent pivot-lookback window changes, search lines from each stored pivot high to that recent highest high, choosing its most recent occurrence. Require the left anchor to precede and exceed the right anchor, a minimum anchor span and percentage drop, a maximum left-anchor age, and at most the permitted number of intervening highs above the line plus the pierce tolerance. Count only stored pivot highs strictly between the anchors that lie between the line minus the touch tolerance and the line plus the pierce tolerance; require the count within the permitted range. Save the qualifying line with the greatest span, retaining the first encountered on ties. If none qualifies, invalidate the saved line and reset its fired flag. Reset the fired flag when a newly selected line has a different left anchor; otherwise retain it. Keep the saved line and its touch statistics unchanged until the next search.
Calculation: Quality score = sum of touch-count, spacing, recency, and higher-low scores. Assign increasing touch scores to the configured ascending touch-count tiers. Spacing is the population standard deviation of gaps between consecutive interior touch bars divided by anchor span; assign decreasing scores to ascending spacing tiers. Recency is the age of the most recent interior touch at line-search time; assign decreasing scores to ascending age tiers. Assign the highest higher-low score when the latest confirmed pivot low exceeds the previous one by a configured percentage, a lower score when it merely exceeds the previous one, and zero otherwise or when either is unavailable. The higher-low component updates each bar; the other components use the saved line-search statistics.
Calculation: Confirmation count = a configurable manual count, or in automatic mode a shorter count when quality score reaches the high-score threshold and a longer count otherwise.
Calculation: Break condition = saved line is valid AND its fired flag is false AND right-anchor age is at most its limit AND close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot low reduced by a configured percentage AND quality score meets its minimum AND every bar in the confirmation count, including the current bar, has close above its projection of the current saved line plus the break buffer AND close above open AND current volume divided by its simple moving average meets the volume-surge threshold.
Long: Break condition is true AND break condition on the preceding bar was false; after the signal, set the saved line's fired flag.
Calculation: Long targets and stop = at entry close, fix an initial stop below entry and three successively higher targets above entry, using either configured multiples of entry-bar ATR or configured percentages of entry. Starting on the bar after entry, track whether each of the first two targets has been reached by high. On the first reach of target one set the stop to entry; on the first reach of target two set it to target one. Process these updates in target-one then target-two order on each bar, before testing exits.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND high is at or above target three.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND high is below target three AND low is at or below the current stop.
