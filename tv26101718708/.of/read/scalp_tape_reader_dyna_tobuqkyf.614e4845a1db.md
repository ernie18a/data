Calculation: Smoothed buying-pressure share = EMA, over a configurable period, of the rolling sum of (volume × |close − open| / max(high − low, minimum positive price increment)) for bars with close > open, divided by the greater of 1 and the rolling sum of those weights plus equivalent weights for bars with close < open; bars with close = open contribute zero.
Long: Smoothed buying-pressure share > dominance threshold OR prior bar's share > dominance threshold OR smoothed buying-pressure share > extreme-pressure threshold AND prior bar's share ≤ extreme-pressure threshold
Short: Smoothed buying-pressure share < 1 − dominance threshold OR prior bar's share < 1 − dominance threshold OR smoothed buying-pressure share < 1 − extreme-pressure threshold AND prior bar's share ≥ 1 − extreme-pressure threshold
