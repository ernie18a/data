Calculation: Volume change ratio = current bar volume divided by previous bar volume, multiplied by 100
Calculation: Average volume change ratio = arithmetic mean of the volume change ratios over a lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Ichimoku conversion line = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Ichimoku base line = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback
Long: Volume change ratio > average volume change ratio multiplied by a configurable factor AND close > upper Keltner Channel AND Ichimoku conversion line > Ichimoku base line
Short: Volume change ratio > average volume change ratio multiplied by a configurable factor AND close < lower Keltner Channel AND Ichimoku conversion line < Ichimoku base line
Exit: long, close < lower Keltner Channel AND Ichimoku conversion line < Ichimoku base line
Exit: short, close > upper Keltner Channel AND Ichimoku conversion line > Ichimoku base line
