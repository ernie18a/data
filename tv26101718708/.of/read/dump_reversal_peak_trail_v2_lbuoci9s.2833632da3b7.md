Calculation: Dump = close < open AND absolute percentage change from a selected bar source to another selected bar source > anomaly threshold AND (dump cap = 0 OR absolute percentage change <= dump cap); percentage change is 0 when the first source is 0.
Calculation: Low-break filter = disabled OR low < the previous chart bar's mapped higher-timeframe rolling lowest low. Historical mapping uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; realtime mapping can use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Entry filter = NOT (the previous chart bar's mapped higher-timeframe rolling highest high > 0 AND close < that high AND (that high - close) / that high <= resistance proximity threshold) AND (external-market trend filter disabled OR external-market higher-timeframe close > its close a specified number of higher-timeframe bars earlier). Historical mapping uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; realtime mapping can use the still-forming bar. The external-market filter requires data outside the specified backtest fields.
Calculation: Armed dump = the most recent bar satisfying Dump AND Low-break filter while cooldown has expired and position is flat or long with fewer than the allowed open trades. A new armed dump cancels any pending entry. For reclaim modes, its reference high and leg low start at its high and low; while waiting, a bar with low < leg low AND (dump cap = 0 OR absolute open-to-close percentage change <= dump cap) replaces both references and cancels any pending entry. Cooldown cancels waiting states. Cooldown starts when a long position closes and expires after a configured elapsed time.
Long: Classic mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND close > reference high AND Entry filter.
Long: Consecutive-closes mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND the lowest close over the configured consecutive bars > reference high AND Entry filter.
Long: Break-retest mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND close crosses above reference high AND Entry filter; place a limit entry at reference high plus a configured multiple of ATR, active until filled or its configured bar expiry.
Long: ATR-stop mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND Entry filter; place a stop entry at reference high plus a configured multiple of ATR, active until filled or its configured bar expiry.
Long: Auto mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND Entry filter AND close crosses above reference high; place a limit entry at reference high plus a sensitivity-dependent multiple of ATR, active until filled or its configured bar expiry. If it expires and Entry filter still holds and position is flat or eligible for an additional entry, place a stop entry at reference high plus a sensitivity-dependent multiple of ATR, active until filled or its configured bar expiry. Higher sensitivity uses a larger ATR multiple for each order.
Long: Auto mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND Entry filter AND no pending entry AND close does not cross above reference high AND the lowest close over a sensitivity-dependent number of consecutive bars > reference high; higher sensitivity uses more bars.
Long: Rebound mode AND Armed dump exists AND cooldown expired AND position flat or eligible for an additional entry AND close > open AND (rolling highest high - rolling lowest low) / rolling lowest low <= flatness threshold AND (close - lowest low since the most recent armed dump) / lowest low since that dump >= rebound threshold AND Entry filter; both ratios are 0 when their denominator is not positive.
Exit: long, highest close-based percentage profit since the position opened or its size last increased >= profit threshold AND that peak profit minus current close-based percentage profit >= retrace threshold; profit uses the current average entry price.
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND price reaches average entry price multiplied by (1 - stop-loss percentage).
Exit: long, loss-time limit enabled AND average entry price > 0 AND close < average entry price AND elapsed time since the position opened >= loss-time limit.
