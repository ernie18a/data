Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after the right-side pivot lookback, using separate left-side and right-side lookbacks. Process simultaneous highs before lows. Maintain a capped recent list: append an opposite-type pivot only if its distance from the last stored pivot is at least an ATR multiple; replace a same-type last pivot only if more extreme. Store each appended pivot’s confirmation-bar ATR and cumulative absolute close-to-close movement; on replacement update its price, pivot bar and cumulative movement, but retain its stored ATR.
Calculation: Protected-swing trend = start neutral and process pivots in order, keeping the latest high, latest low, protected high and protected low. In an uptrend, a higher high sets the protected low to the latest low; otherwise, a high above the protected high in a downtrend, or above the latest high when neutral, changes the trend to up and sets the protected low to the latest low. In a downtrend, a lower low sets the protected high to the latest high; otherwise, a low below the protected low in an uptrend, or below the latest low when neutral, changes the trend to down and sets the protected high to the latest high. Update the latest high or low after processing each pivot. Recalculate the chart trend from neutral over the retained pivot list when a pivot is confirmed. For each selected higher timeframe, apply the same trend rules directly to all confirmed pivots without the ATR swing filter, and use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Impulse = a consecutive pair of retained pivots whose direction does not oppose the protected-swing trend at the second pivot and whose quality score is at least a threshold. For each retained leg, displacement is its absolute price change divided by the mean stored ATR of its endpoints; efficiency is its absolute price change divided by the difference between endpoint cumulative movements, capped at one, or one if that difference is zero. The displacement percentile is the fraction of the other retained legs with strictly smaller displacement. Quality is the equally weighted mean of efficiency and displacement percentile. Recalculate all leg classifications when a pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = the sign of the difference between the numbers of selected higher timeframes with uptrend and downtrend; a tie is neutral. Conviction is the mean, among timeframes voting for that bias, of ADX transformed linearly between lower and upper bounds and clipped to its output range; use each higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Setup = on each confirmed chart pivot, select the latest impulse matching the higher-timeframe bias, or the chart trend if that bias is neutral; if both are neutral, select the latest impulse of either direction. Its origin and endpoint define the setup direction and range. The entry zone lies between a shallower and a deeper retracement of that range from the endpoint. The first and second targets are progressively farther extensions beyond the endpoint; the stop is beyond the origin by an ATR multiple. Retain the setup levels between pivot confirmations. A new endpoint pivot bar resets the setup’s terminal state; otherwise, latch the first stop or first-target hit on every bar, with the stop taking precedence if both are hit on the same bar.
Long: Chart protected-swing trend is up.
Short: Chart protected-swing trend is down.
Long: A selected higher-timeframe protected-swing trend is up.
Short: A selected higher-timeframe protected-swing trend is down.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias is positive.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias is negative.
Long: An upward leg is classified as an impulse.
Short: A downward leg is classified as an impulse.
Long: Close is above the latest retained pivot price.
Short: Close is at or below the latest retained pivot price.
Long: Setup direction is up AND setup terminal state is unset AND close is within the entry zone.
Short: Setup direction is down AND setup terminal state is unset AND close is within the entry zone.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the setup stop OR high is at or above either setup target.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the setup stop OR low is at or below either setup target.
