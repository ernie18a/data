Calculation: Directional bias = for each of three higher timeframes ordered longest to shortest, use either structure, momentum, or structure on the longest and momentum on the other two. Structure is bullish when both latest confirmed pivot high and latest confirmed pivot low exceed their respective preceding pivots, bearish when both are lower, and neutral otherwise; pivots use equal left and right confirmation lookbacks. Momentum is bullish when the shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA and close exceeds the shorter EMA, bearish when both comparisons are reversed, and neutral otherwise. Use the higher-timeframe values as returned with lookahead off, including the still-forming bar in live evaluation.
Calculation: Opening-gap bias = reset to neutral at each New York calendar day; compare the first chart-bar open at or after the regular-session open with the close immediately before the first bar at or after the regular-session close, retaining the last captured values. During the initial regular-session window, mark an upward gap filled if low reaches the earlier close and a downward gap filled if high reaches it. At the end of that window, lock the bias to neutral if filled or unchanged, bullish if the gap is upward, and bearish if downward.
Calculation: Directional gate = when enabled, permit a direction if at least one of the three directional biases agrees, none opposes, and, when the opening-gap filter is enabled, the opening-gap bias does not oppose; when disabled, permit both directions.
Calculation: Sweep = outside the overnight session, a high-side sweep occurs when any high in a breach lookback exceeds the current previous completed higher-timeframe daily high or retained overnight high and current close is below that level; a low-side sweep reverses those comparisons using the previous completed higher-timeframe daily low or retained overnight low. Overnight high and low update from the New York overnight-session start until its end and then remain fixed. Each level can sweep only once until its latch resets: daily levels at the New York day change and overnight levels at the next overnight start. Retain the latest sweep direction and bar for a separate sweep lookback; if both directions occur on one bar, retain the low-side sweep.
Calculation: Displacement = previous bar's body is at least a configurable multiple of current-bar ATR, and, if the volume filter is enabled, previous volume is at least a configurable multiple of its volume SMA on that previous bar; upward displacement also requires previous close > previous open, and downward displacement requires previous close < previous open.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars ago; its lower bound is that earlier high, upper bound is current low, and midpoint is their average. Bearish gap = current high < low two bars ago; its lower bound is current high, upper bound is that earlier low, and midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Armed setup state = maintain each setup and direction independently. An eligible setup arms only when that setup and direction has no existing armed or entered state; retain its arm bar, entry level, stop and target. An unentered setup is active starting on the next bar through its setup-specific expiry lookback. On an active bar, evaluate entry before stop invalidation; if no entry occurs and the stop is touched, cancel it. Cancel an unentered setup after expiry and clear an entered setup on exit. Evaluate exits for the gap, retracement and order-block setups starting on the bar after entry; evaluate sweep-reversal exits on the entry bar as well. If target and stop are both touched, target has priority.
Calculation: Gap setup = on the arm bar, require a gap of at least the configured minimum size, same-direction displacement, a latest opposite-side sweep within the sweep lookback, a permitted directional gate, and membership in a configurable New York session window. Entry level is the gap midpoint; long stop is the preceding bar's low minus a buffer and short stop is its high plus a buffer. The entry-to-stop distance must be positive and no greater than the setup's maximum. Target is entry level plus the target distance for long or minus it for short.
Calculation: Retracement setup = on confirmation of a chart-timeframe pivot high for long or pivot low for short, require the latest opposite pivot and a latest opposite-side sweep within the sweep lookback, plus the permitted directional gate. For long, the leg runs from the latest pivot low to the newly confirmed pivot high; for short, it runs from the latest pivot high to the newly confirmed pivot low. Define the zone by two configurable retracement fractions, with the deeper fraction defining the lower long bound and upper short bound; define a separate entry reference by an intermediate retracement fraction. Long stop is the leg low minus a buffer; short stop is the leg high plus a buffer. The reference-to-stop distance must be positive and no greater than this setup's maximum. Target is the reference plus the target distance for long or minus it for short; actual entry is the triggering bar's close.
Calculation: Order-block setup = require a same-direction gap and displacement, an opposite-direction candle two bars ago, and the permitted directional gate. Entry level is the midpoint of that earlier candle's body. Long stop is its low minus a buffer and short stop is its high plus a buffer. The entry-to-stop distance must be positive and no greater than the setup's maximum. Target is entry level plus the target distance for long or minus it for short.
Calculation: Sweep-reversal setup = require a current opposite-side sweep, the permitted directional gate, and, if enabled, membership in a configurable New York session window. Entry is current close; long stop is current low minus a buffer and short stop is current high plus a buffer. The entry-to-stop distance must be positive and no greater than this setup's maximum. Target is entry plus the target distance for long or minus it for short; each direction can enter only when its own prior position is absent.
Long: Low-side sweep occurs.
Short: High-side sweep occurs.
Long: Directional gate is enabled AND permits long.
Short: Directional gate is enabled AND permits short.
Long: Bullish gap setup arms OR bullish retracement setup arms OR bullish order-block setup arms.
Short: Bearish gap setup arms OR bearish retracement setup arms OR bearish order-block setup arms.
Long: Active bullish gap setup AND low <= its entry level OR active bullish order-block setup AND low <= its entry level OR active bullish retracement setup AND low <= its zone upper bound AND close >= its zone lower bound AND close > open OR bullish sweep-reversal setup is eligible.
Short: Active bearish gap setup AND high >= its entry level OR active bearish order-block setup AND high >= its entry level OR active bearish retracement setup AND high >= its zone lower bound AND close <= its zone upper bound AND close < open OR bearish sweep-reversal setup is eligible.
Exit: long, high >= retained setup target OR low <= retained setup stop OR bars since entry >= time-stop lookback.
Exit: short, low <= retained setup target OR high >= retained setup stop OR bars since entry >= time-stop lookback.
Long: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low > the high two higher-timeframe bars before it AND the gap size >= configured minimum.
Short: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high < the low two higher-timeframe bars before it AND the gap size >= configured minimum.
