Calculation: Resistance and support = the most recently confirmed pivot high of high and pivot low of low, respectively, using specified left and right confirmation lookbacks; update each level on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band; its average is an SMA over a longer lookback than the Bollinger Bands.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = 1 when resistance exists AND close > current resistance AND previous close <= current resistance AND volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier AND MACD histogram > 0 AND Bollinger width >= its average × squeeze threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bearish breakdown = 1 when support exists AND close < current support AND previous close >= current support AND volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier AND MACD histogram < 0 AND Bollinger width >= its average × squeeze threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish bonus = count of true conditions: fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA; close > trend EMA; RSI > RSI at a specified earlier bar; smoothed stochastic K crosses above its SMA D AND previous K < a lower threshold; positive DI > negative DI; RSI crosses above its midpoint; support exists AND absolute(close - support) < ATR × proximity multiplier; bullish breakout = 1. The trend EMA has the longest lookback, followed by the slow, middle, and fast EMAs.
Calculation: Bearish bonus = count of true conditions: fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA; close < trend EMA; RSI < RSI at the same earlier-bar offset used for the bullish bonus; smoothed stochastic K crosses below its SMA D AND previous K > an upper threshold; negative DI > positive DI; RSI crosses below its midpoint; resistance exists AND absolute(close - resistance) < ATR × proximity multiplier; bearish breakdown = 1. The stochastic bonus thresholds are more extreme than the corresponding trigger thresholds.
Long: bullish breakout = 1.
Short: bearish breakdown = 1.
Short: resistance exists AND close < current resistance AND at least one prior close within the false-breakout lookback > current resistance.
Long: support exists AND close > current support AND at least one prior close within the false-breakdown lookback < current support.
Long: ADX > trend threshold AND fast EMA > fast EMA at the slope lookback AND middle EMA > middle EMA at the same slope lookback AND RSI >= bullish-zone lower bound AND RSI <= bullish-zone upper bound AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD histogram > histogram two bars ago AND close >= low + candle fraction × (high - low) AND close <= trend EMA × (1 + extension fraction) AND (close <= close at divergence lookback OR RSI >= RSI at divergence lookback OR RSI <= bearish-divergence threshold) AND (resistance does not exist OR close >= current resistance OR every prior close in the false-breakout lookback <= current resistance) AND Bollinger width >= its average × squeeze threshold AND bullish bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses above signal line AND previous histogram < 0 OR smoothed stochastic K crosses above D AND previous K < lower trigger threshold OR RSI crosses above its midpoint AND RSI at the trigger-base lookback < lower base threshold OR fast EMA crosses above middle EMA AND middle EMA > middle EMA at the slope lookback AND ADX > crossover ADX threshold OR bullish breakout = 1) AND (no earlier gated long signal exists OR bars since the last gated long signal > cooldown).
Short: ADX > trend threshold AND fast EMA < fast EMA at the slope lookback AND middle EMA < middle EMA at the same slope lookback AND RSI >= bearish-zone lower bound AND RSI <= bearish-zone upper bound AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND MACD histogram < histogram two bars ago AND close <= high - candle fraction × (high - low) AND close >= trend EMA × (1 - extension fraction) AND (close >= close at divergence lookback OR RSI <= RSI at divergence lookback OR RSI >= bullish-divergence threshold) AND (support does not exist OR close <= current support OR every prior close in the false-breakdown lookback >= current support) AND Bollinger width >= its average × squeeze threshold AND bearish bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses below signal line AND previous histogram > 0 OR smoothed stochastic K crosses below D AND previous K > upper trigger threshold OR RSI crosses below its midpoint AND RSI at the trigger-base lookback > upper base threshold OR fast EMA crosses below middle EMA AND middle EMA < middle EMA at the slope lookback AND ADX > crossover ADX threshold OR bearish breakdown = 1) AND (no earlier gated short signal exists OR bars since the last gated short signal > cooldown).
