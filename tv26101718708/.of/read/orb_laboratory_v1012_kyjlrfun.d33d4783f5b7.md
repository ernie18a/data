Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled window, the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the window; finalize on the bar whose closing time reaches the window end, and reset each local day. Range = opening-range high minus opening-range low; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Breakout thresholds = opening-range high plus the long buffer and opening-range low minus the short buffer. Each buffer is independently a configurable absolute price increment, percentage of the range, or ATR multiple. A touch-order trigger may additionally shift the long threshold downward or the short threshold upward by a configurable spread.
Calculation: First break = the first bar after range completion, including the completion bar, whose high reaches the upper threshold without its low reaching the lower threshold, or whose low reaches the lower threshold without its high reaching the upper threshold. Record its side, bar and subsequent maximum high or minimum low until consumed. If both thresholds are touched on the same bar, record neither.
Calculation: Confirmed breakout = the first close above the upper threshold or below the lower threshold after range completion, including the completion bar. Record its side and bar for the rest of the local day.
Calculation: Fair value gap = after a confirmed breakout, a bullish gap has current low above the high two bars earlier and size at least a configurable ATR multiple; a bearish gap has current high below the low two bars earlier and size at least that multiple. Optionally require the bullish bottom to be at or above the range high or the bearish top at or below the range low. Store the latest qualifying gap in the breakout direction. Its entry level is its near edge, midpoint, or far edge, as configured.
Calculation: Previous close filter reference = the higher-timeframe daily close series with lookahead disabled, shifted by one chart bar; this is not necessarily the previous daily bar on every chart bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = select the signal-bar low minus a configurable price buffer for a long or high plus it for a short; reference price minus or plus an ATR multiple; range midpoint; range low minus the buffer for a long or range high plus it for a short; relevant breakout extreme minus or plus the buffer; reference price minus or plus a configurable fraction of the range; or bullish gap bottom minus the buffer for a long or bearish gap top plus it for a short. Clamp the long stop no higher than reference price minus a configurable price increment and the short stop no lower than reference price plus that increment. The reference is the intended entry threshold for touch orders and signal-bar close for other entries. The breakout extreme is the opposite range boundary for touch entries, signal-bar low or high for close, range-retest and continuation entries, gap bottom or top for gap entries, sweep-bar high or low for sweeps, and recorded first-break extreme for first-break failures. Optionally shift the stop downward for longs or upward for shorts by a configurable spread.
Calculation: Original risk = absolute difference between the entry reference and either the structural or spread-shifted initial stop, as configured. For touch orders, entry reference is the intended threshold; for other entries it is the signal-bar close.
Calculation: Target = for a continuation entry, select entry reference plus or minus original risk times a configurable multiple; range high plus or range low minus a configurable range multiple; entry reference plus or minus an ATR multiple; range midpoint; or range high for a long and range low for a short. For a fade entry, select midpoint, range high for a long and range low for a short, or the fixed-risk target. For touch orders the target entry reference can instead be the spread-shifted trigger, and target risk is measured from that selected reference to the selected structural or spread-shifted stop. ATR and target are fixed when the order is submitted.
Calculation: Active stop = initial structural stop, ratcheted upward for a long or downward for a short. If enabled, after high reaches entry price plus a configurable original-risk multiple for a long, or low reaches entry price minus that multiple for a short, ratchet to entry price plus or minus a configurable offset. If enabled, after the best confirmed close since entry reaches entry price plus or minus a separate configurable original-risk multiple, ratchet to that best close minus an entry-ATR multiple for a long or plus it for a short. Apply the optional stop spread to the resulting stop. Stop updates calculated at bar close apply to subsequent execution.
