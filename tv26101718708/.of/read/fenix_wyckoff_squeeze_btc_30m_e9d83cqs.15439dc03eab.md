Calculation: Squeeze breakout = Bollinger Bands are outside Keltner Channels on the current bar, and Bollinger Bands were inside Keltner Channels on at least one of the preceding squeeze-lookback bars; bands use the same close-based SMA basis, BB uses close standard deviation, and KC uses ATR.
Calculation: Phoenix composite = mean of (EMA-smoothed normalized HLC3 momentum, Money Flow, and RSI of HLC3); normalized momentum is EMA((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, N1)) / max(EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, N1)), N1), ε) / 0.025, N2) + 50; Money Flow is 100 − 100 / (1 + rolling sum(HLC3 × volume when HLC3 rises, N3) / max(rolling sum(HLC3 × volume when HLC3 falls, N3), ε)); use the same N3 for Money Flow and RSI.
Calculation: Phoenix signal = 6-bar SMA of the Phoenix composite.
Calculation: Phoenix LSMA = linear regression of the Phoenix composite over its LSMA lookback, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Volatility rank = percentile rank of ATR(14) over the volatility lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR multiplier = stop or target ATR multiplier × (volatility scale minimum + (volatility scale maximum − volatility scale minimum) × volatility rank / 100) when volatility adaptation is enabled; otherwise use the unscaled multiplier.
Long: Squeeze breakout AND close > chart-timeframe EMA(close, 4 × EMA length) AND HMA is rising AND Phoenix composite > Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite > its prior value AND Phoenix LSMA > 50 AND volatility rank >= minimum AND volatility rank <= maximum AND (ADX regime filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold)
Short: Squeeze breakout AND close < chart-timeframe EMA(close, 4 × EMA length) AND HMA is falling AND Phoenix composite < Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite < its prior value AND Phoenix LSMA < 50 AND volatility rank >= minimum AND volatility rank <= maximum AND (ADX regime filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold)
Exit: Long, Phoenix composite crosses below Phoenix signal OR Phoenix composite decreases on every bar of the exit-slope lookback OR close <= the greater of the prior trailing stop and (close − entry ATR × adaptive stop multiplier) OR close >= entry price + entry ATR × adaptive target multiplier OR bars held >= maximum hold bars
Exit: Short, Phoenix composite crosses above Phoenix signal OR Phoenix composite increases on every bar of the exit-slope lookback OR close >= the lesser of the prior trailing stop and (close + entry ATR × adaptive stop multiplier) OR close <= entry price − entry ATR × adaptive target multiplier OR bars held >= maximum hold bars
