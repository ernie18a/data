Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Phoenix composite = average of TCI, money flow, and RSI of typical price. TCI = EMA((typical price - EMA(typical price, first TCI length)) / max(EMA(abs(typical price - EMA(typical price, first TCI length)), first TCI length), small positive denominator floor) / TCI normalization factor, second TCI length) + 50. Money flow = 100 - 100 / (1 + rolling sum of typical price * volume on bars where typical price increased / max(rolling sum of typical price * volume on bars where typical price decreased, small positive denominator floor)); its lookback equals the RSI length. Unchanged typical price contributes zero to both sums.
Calculation: Phoenix signal = SMA of Phoenix composite; its length is independent of the composite lengths. Phoenix regression = least-squares moving average of Phoenix composite, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Squeeze bands = SMA of close plus and minus a Bollinger multiplier times standard deviation of close; channel bands = the same SMA plus and minus a Keltner multiplier times ATR. All use the same lookback.
Calculation: Squeeze breakout = upper squeeze band > upper channel band OR lower squeeze band < lower channel band, AND at least one of the preceding squeeze-lookback bars had upper squeeze band < upper channel band AND lower squeeze band > lower channel band.
Calculation: Hull average = WMA(2 * WMA(close, floor(Hull length / 2)) - WMA(close, Hull length), max(floor(sqrt(Hull length)), minimum smoothing length)).
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA of chart-timeframe close using the trend length multiplied by a fixed factor; it does not use aggregated higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Volatility rank = percentile rank of chart-timeframe ATR over a volatility-rank lookback. The ATR length and rank lookback are independent of the risk ATR length.
Calculation: Volatility scale = minimum scale + (maximum scale - minimum scale) * volatility rank / 100 when adaptive scaling is enabled; otherwise 1.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of entry price - entry-bar risk ATR * entry-bar stop multiplier * entry-bar volatility scale and, for every subsequent bar in the position through the current bar, close - entry-bar risk ATR * stop multiplier * that bar's volatility scale. Short trailing stop = corresponding minimum using addition instead of subtraction. Long target = entry price + entry-bar risk ATR * target multiplier * entry-bar volatility scale; short target = entry price - entry-bar risk ATR * target multiplier * entry-bar volatility scale.
Long: Squeeze breakout AND close > trend EMA AND Hull average > previous Hull average AND Phoenix composite > Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite > previous Phoenix composite AND Phoenix regression > 50 AND volatility rank >= lower rank threshold AND volatility rank <= upper rank threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR ADX >= regime threshold).
Short: Squeeze breakout AND close < trend EMA AND Hull average < previous Hull average AND Phoenix composite < Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite < previous Phoenix composite AND Phoenix regression < 50 AND volatility rank >= lower rank threshold AND volatility rank <= upper rank threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR ADX >= regime threshold).
Exit: long, close <= long trailing stop OR close >= long target OR bars held >= maximum hold length OR (Phoenix composite < Phoenix signal AND previous Phoenix composite >= previous Phoenix signal) OR Phoenix composite decreased on every bar in the exit-slope lookback.
Exit: short, close >= short trailing stop OR close <= short target OR bars held >= maximum hold length OR (Phoenix composite > Phoenix signal AND previous Phoenix composite <= previous Phoenix signal) OR Phoenix composite increased on every bar in the exit-slope lookback.
