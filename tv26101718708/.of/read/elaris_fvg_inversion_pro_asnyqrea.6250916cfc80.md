Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier; gap is the distance between them, and the zone spans those prices.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier; gap is the distance between them, and the zone spans those prices.
Long: Bullish FVG gap / high two bars earlier × 100 ≥ minimum gap percentage AND bullish FVG gap ≥ ATR × minimum ATR multiple
Long: An active, uninverted bearish FVG zone is inverted when close > its top
Long: An unmitigated bullish zone is tapped when low ≤ its midpoint AND high ≥ its bottom
