Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars ago AND (current low - high two bars ago) / high two bars ago * 100 >= minimum gap percentage, using zero for the percentage if high two bars ago is zero AND, if enabled, current low - high two bars ago >= ATR * minimum ATR multiplier AND, if enabled, close > EMA of close AND, if enabled, volume > SMA of volume * volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars ago AND (low two bars ago - current high) / low two bars ago * 100 >= minimum gap percentage, using zero for the percentage if low two bars ago is zero AND, if enabled, low two bars ago - current high >= ATR * minimum ATR multiplier AND, if enabled, close < EMA of close AND, if enabled, volume > SMA of volume * volume multiplier.
Calculation: Zone state = Each enabled bullish FVG creates a bullish zone with top = current low and bottom = high two bars ago; each enabled bearish FVG creates a bearish zone with top = low two bars ago and bottom = current high. New zones are uninverted and unmitigated. Add new zones before updating zones; if the zone count exceeds its limit, remove the oldest zone upon each addition. Update remaining zones newest first, including new zones. An uninverted bullish zone becomes bearish and inverted when close < bottom, or low < bottom in the selected wick mode; an uninverted bearish zone becomes bullish and inverted when close > top, or high > top in the selected wick mode. Inversion occurs only when enabled and resets the zone's mitigated state. Evaluate mitigation using the zone direction after inversion but its mitigated state from the start of that bar's zone update. Mark a zone mitigated when its mitigation condition is met. After its update, remove a zone if configured to delete mitigated zones and it is mitigated, or if configured to delete expired zones and its age in bars exceeds the validity limit.
Calculation: Mitigation level = zone midpoint, or in proximal mode the top of a bullish zone and bottom of a bearish zone, or in distal mode the bottom of a bullish zone and top of a bearish zone.
Long: Bullish FVG AND bullish FVG zones enabled.
Short: Bearish FVG AND bearish FVG zones enabled.
Long: An existing uninverted bearish zone becomes inverted.
Short: An existing uninverted bullish zone becomes inverted.
Long: A zone was unmitigated at the start of its update AND its direction after inversion is bullish AND low <= its mitigation level AND high >= its bottom.
Short: A zone was unmitigated at the start of its update AND its direction after inversion is bearish AND high >= its mitigation level AND low <= its top.
Long: EMA trend filter enabled AND close > EMA of close.
Short: EMA trend filter enabled AND close <= EMA of close.
