Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a pivot at n bars ago is set when high[n] > the highest high of the n bars since it, or low[n] < the lowest low of those bars; update the latest pivot when the detected leg changes direction. Use a longer lookback for swing pivots than for internal pivots.
Calculation: Auto FVG threshold = 0.3 × cumulative mean of the absolute percentage change in the previous completed selected-timeframe bar, accumulated only when a new selected-timeframe bar starts and divided by chart bar index.
Long: Close crosses above the latest swing pivot high and it has not already been crossed, OR close crosses above the latest internal pivot high and it has not already been crossed AND the internal pivot differs from the swing pivot AND (if enabled) high − max(close, open) > min(close, open − low)
Long: A new selected-timeframe bar starts AND its current low > the high two selected-timeframe bars earlier AND its previous completed bar's close > that high AND its previous completed bar's percentage change > the auto FVG threshold; use the current forming selected-timeframe bar and the previous completed bar (the selected timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe).
Long: Current low − high two chart bars earlier > ATR × the liquidity-void threshold AND current low > that earlier high AND the intervening bar's close > that earlier high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest swing pivot low and it has not already been crossed, OR close crosses below the latest internal pivot low and it has not already been crossed AND the internal pivot differs from the swing pivot AND (if enabled) high − max(close, open) < min(close, open − low)
Short: A new selected-timeframe bar starts AND its current high < the low two selected-timeframe bars earlier AND its previous completed bar's close < that low AND the negative of its previous completed bar's percentage change > the auto FVG threshold; use the current forming selected-timeframe bar and the previous completed bar (the selected timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe).
Short: Low two chart bars earlier − current high > ATR × the liquidity-void threshold AND current high < that earlier low AND the intervening bar's close < that earlier low
