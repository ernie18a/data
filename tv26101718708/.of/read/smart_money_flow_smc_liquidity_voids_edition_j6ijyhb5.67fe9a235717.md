Calculation: Structure pivot = For each lookback, maintain a leg initialized bearish. If the high that many bars ago exceeds every high in the following lookback bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low that many bars ago is below every low in the following lookback bars, set it bullish. A change to bearish confirms that earlier high as a pivot high; a change to bullish confirms that earlier low as a pivot low. Maintain separate short-lookback internal, longer-lookback swing, and equal-high/low pivot sequences. Each new pivot replaces the previous pivot of the same type and resets its crossed flag.
Calculation: Structure break = A close crossing above the current pivot high or below the current pivot low, with that pivot's crossed flag unset; then set the flag. A cross compares both the current close and pivot level with their respective previous-bar values. For internal breaks, require the internal pivot level to differ from the corresponding swing pivot level. If the optional internal confluence filter is enabled, additionally require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for an upward break, or high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low) for a downward break.
Calculation: Structure bias = Initially neutral; update to bullish on an upward structure break and bearish on a downward structure break, maintaining separate internal and swing biases.
Calculation: Order block = On a structure break, select a bar from the broken pivot bar inclusive to the break bar exclusive. For an upward break select the first bar with the minimum parsed low; for a downward break select the first bar with the maximum parsed high. Parsed high and low equal the bar's high and low unless its range is at least twice the selected volatility measure, in which case they are the bar's low and high respectively. The volatility measure is ATR or cumulative true range divided by the zero-based current bar index. Store the selected parsed high and low as the block boundaries; retain separate internal and swing blocks and discard a block after its first mitigation.
Calculation: Fair value gap threshold = Optionally, a multiplier times the cumulative sum of the absolute previous completed gap-timeframe bar's (close - open) / (open * 100), added only when a new gap-timeframe bar starts, divided by the zero-based chart bar index; otherwise zero. The gap timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current gap-timeframe bar still forming and the two preceding completed bars. Historical higher-timeframe requests use lookahead and therefore expose the final high and low of the current higher-timeframe bar at its start.
Long: Close crosses above an uncrossed swing pivot high OR close crosses above an uncrossed internal pivot high subject to the internal break conditions.
Short: Close crosses below an uncrossed swing pivot low OR close crosses below an uncrossed internal pivot low subject to the internal break conditions.
Long: A swing pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A swing pivot high is confirmed.
Long: An equal-sequence pivot low is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-sequence pivot low is less than a multiplier times ATR.
Short: An equal-sequence pivot high is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-sequence pivot high is less than a multiplier times ATR.
Long: Internal structure bias is bullish.
Short: Internal structure bias is bearish OR neutral.
Long: The selected mitigation source, either close or high, exceeds the upper boundary of a bearish internal or swing order block.
Short: The selected mitigation source, either close or low, is below the lower boundary of a bullish internal or swing order block.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar starts AND its low exceeds the high two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous completed gap-timeframe close exceeds that high AND the previous completed gap-timeframe bar's (close - open) / (open * 100) exceeds the fair value gap threshold.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar starts AND its high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous completed gap-timeframe close is below that low AND the negative of the previous completed gap-timeframe bar's (close - open) / (open * 100) exceeds the fair value gap threshold.
Long: Low minus high two chart bars ago exceeds a multiplier times ATR AND low exceeds that earlier high AND the previous close exceeds that earlier high.
Short: Low two chart bars ago minus high exceeds a multiplier times ATR AND high is below that earlier low AND the previous close is below that earlier low.
