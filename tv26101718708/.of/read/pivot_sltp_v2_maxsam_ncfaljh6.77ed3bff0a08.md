Calculation: Smoothed ATR = 5-period EMA of 14-period ATR.
Calculation: Demand/supply zone = cluster of at least two confirmed pivot lows/highs, respectively, within the pivot lookback; merge a pivot into a cluster when its price is within the clustering tolerance × Smoothed ATR of the cluster’s current average price. Zone bounds are its center ± half the zone height × Smoothed ATR. Merge overlapping zones of the same type; rank by score and retain at most the configured maximum.
Calculation: Zone score = min(100, 35 × min(1, ln(touches)/ln(max(maxTouches, 3))) + 25 × min(1, trendlineHits/3) + 20 × recency + 10 × age + 10 × volumeBonus), where age = min(1, zone age/lookback), recency = 1 − min(1, bars since last touch/(lookback/2)), and volumeBonus is zero unless enabled, otherwise max(0, min(1, (average relative volume at pivots − 1)/2)). Trendline hits count qualifying rising lines through pairs of pivot lows for demand or falling lines through pairs of pivot highs for supply, using the configured touch tolerance and minimum touches.
Calculation: Trade plan = demand entry at zone top and supply entry at zone bottom. Stop is beyond the zone’s far edge, or beyond the cluster’s most extreme pivot if pivot-extreme mode is selected, by the configured stop buffer × Smoothed ATR. TP1 is the nearest qualifying opposing zone edge beyond entry (score at least 70% of the minimum zone score), or the configured ATR fallback if none exists. Risk/reward = distance from entry to TP1 divided by distance from entry to stop.
Long: demand zone has score ≥ minimum score AND close ≥ zone bottom − 0.3 × Smoothed ATR AND close ≥ zone center − 0.75 × zone height × Smoothed ATR AND (nearby-zone filter disabled OR distance from close to zone center ≤ configured nearby range × Smoothed ATR) AND TP1 risk/reward ≥ minimum.
Short: supply zone has score ≥ minimum score AND close ≤ zone top + 0.3 × Smoothed ATR AND close ≤ zone center + 0.75 × zone height × Smoothed ATR AND (nearby-zone filter disabled OR distance from close to zone center ≤ configured nearby range × Smoothed ATR) AND TP1 risk/reward ≥ minimum.
