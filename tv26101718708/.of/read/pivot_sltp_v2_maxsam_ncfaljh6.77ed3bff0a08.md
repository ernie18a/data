Calculation: Smoothed ATR = EMA of ATR; use the current chart bar.
Calculation: Pivot clusters = Separately retain confirmed pivot lows and highs, subject to a maximum count and a bar lookback. Process each side's pivots oldest first. Assign each pivot to the first existing cluster whose current mean is within a smoothed-ATR-scaled tolerance; otherwise create a cluster. Track each cluster's mean price, touch count, oldest and newest pivot bars, mean relative volume at its pivots, and lowest low or highest high pivot. Relative volume is pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA, or a fallback value when that SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Trendline confluence = For each cluster, count pairs of retained trendline pivots whose first pivot is within the lookback and whose second pivot is later and higher for lows or lower for highs. The line through the pair must project within a smoothed-ATR-scaled tolerance of the cluster mean on the current bar. Count sampled bars from the first pivot through the current bar at a fixed stride whose low for a low-pivot line or high for a high-pivot line is within another smoothed-ATR-scaled tolerance of the line; the pair qualifies when that count meets the minimum. Use current smoothed ATR for all distances.
Calculation: Cluster score = A capped weighted sum of log(touches) normalized by log of the greater of the maximum touch count across all clusters and a minimum normalization count; capped trendline confluence; cluster age relative to the lookback; recency of its newest pivot relative to a fraction of the lookback; and, when enabled, a capped positive bonus based on mean relative volume above a baseline. Cap the total score.
Calculation: Eligible zones = For each cluster with at least the minimum touches, form a zone centered on its mean with smoothed-ATR-scaled height. Optionally require the center to be within a smoothed-ATR-scaled distance of close. Require a low-pivot cluster's close to be at or above both its bottom minus a smoothed-ATR-scaled break buffer and its center minus a multiple of its half-height; require a high-pivot cluster's close to be at or below both its top plus the break buffer and its center plus that half-height multiple. Require the cluster score to meet the minimum. Optionally process zones in low-cluster then high-cluster order, merging each into the first overlapping same-side zone: take the outer bounds, sum touches and confluences, add a fraction of its score to the existing score subject to a cap, and retain the oldest pivot and most extreme pivot. Stable-sort by descending score and retain the maximum permitted number.
Long: The number of eligible low-pivot zones is greater than zero.
Short: The number of eligible high-pivot zones is greater than zero.
