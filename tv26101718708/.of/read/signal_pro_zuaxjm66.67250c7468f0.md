Calculation: Common notation = Evaluate on the chart timeframe or a selected analysis timeframe. For a higher analysis timeframe, historical chart bars use the last completed analysis bar; in real time a completed chart bar can use the higher bar still forming. For a lower analysis timeframe, use its last available completed intrabar within each chart bar. Hold the preceding completed chart bar's result while the current chart bar is forming. Each indicator has independently configurable length, sensitivity, lookback, and coefficients. Use a small positive floor for denominators. If a referenced past value is unavailable, substitute its current value; apply the stated fallbacks to unavailable averages.
Calculation: Indicator strength = For each enabled indicator, calculate the corresponding expression below. ATR denominators are floored at a small positive value; the earlier value of a series is its value at that indicator's lookback. EMA, SMA, standard deviation, ATR, RSI, MACD, Supertrend, Parabolic SAR, CCI, OBV, accumulation/distribution line, and session VWAP use their standard definitions.
Calculation: EMA trend strength = (short EMA of close - long EMA of close)/ATR + slope coefficient × (short EMA - its earlier value)/ATR + positive offset if close > short EMA, otherwise negative offset. The long EMA length exceeds the short EMA length and depends on its length and lookback; ATR length depends on the short EMA length.
Calculation: MACD strength = (MACD line - signal line)/histogram standard deviation + histogram/histogram standard deviation + slope coefficient × (histogram - earlier histogram)/histogram standard deviation. The fast EMA is shorter than the slow EMA; the signal EMA length depends on the slow length and sensitivity; the standard-deviation length depends on lookback.
Calculation: Supertrend strength = (close - Supertrend line)/ATR + a positive stability offset if the current and earlier Supertrend directions agree and are bullish, a negative stability offset if they agree and are bearish, otherwise zero. Supertrend multiplier depends on sensitivity.
Calculation: Ichimoku strength = cloud bias + (short midpoint - medium midpoint)/ATR + momentum coefficient × (close - earlier close)/ATR. Each midpoint is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback; the short, medium, and long lookbacks increase in that order. The unshifted cloud spans are the average of the short and medium midpoints and the long midpoint. Cloud bias is distance from the nearer cloud boundary divided by ATR when close is outside the cloud; inside, it is a coefficient times distance from the cloud midpoint divided by ATR.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR strength = (close - SAR)/ATR + SAR slope coefficient × (SAR - earlier SAR)/ATR + momentum coefficient × (close - earlier close)/ATR. SAR acceleration parameters depend on sensitivity.
Calculation: RSI strength = centered RSI/scale + (RSI - earlier RSI)/slope scale, where centered RSI is RSI minus its neutral midpoint.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI strength = centered smoothed stochastic RSI/scale + (smoothed stochastic RSI - its signal SMA)/spread scale + (smoothed stochastic RSI - its earlier value)/slope scale. Calculate stochastic RSI over the RSI length, then smooth it and its signal with successive short SMAs.
Calculation: CCI strength = CCI of typical price/scale + slope coefficient × (CCI - earlier CCI)/scale.
Calculation: Williams %R strength = centered Williams %R/scale + (Williams %R - earlier Williams %R)/slope scale, where centered Williams %R is its displacement from its neutral midpoint.
Calculation: ROC strength = percentage rate of change of close/ATR percentage + slope coefficient × (ROC - earlier ROC)/ATR percentage. Floor ATR percentage at a positive parameter; use zero ROC if the base close is zero.
Calculation: VWAP strength = (close - session VWAP of typical price)/ATR + momentum coefficient × (close - earlier close)/ATR + volume coefficient × (volume/SMA(volume) - 1). Substitute close for unavailable VWAP, current volume for unavailable volume SMA, and zero for the volume term if its SMA is nonpositive.
Calculation: OBV strength = (short EMA of OBV - long EMA of OBV)/scale + slope coefficient × (OBV - earlier OBV)/scale. The long EMA length exceeds the short length and depends on the short length and lookback; scale is the standard deviation of OBV minus its long EMA, floored at a positive parameter.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution strength = (accumulation/distribution line - its EMA)/scale + slope coefficient × (line - earlier line)/scale. Scale is the standard deviation of the line minus its EMA, floored at a positive parameter; use zero money-flow multiplier when high equals low.
Calculation: Volume moving-average strength = (volume/SMA(volume) - 1) × candle direction + (close - open)/ATR + trend coefficient × (close - EMA(close))/ATR. Candle direction is positive when close >= open and negative otherwise; the price EMA length is the lookback. Substitute current volume for an unavailable volume SMA and a volume ratio of one if that average is nonpositive.
Calculation: Volume delta strength = rolling sum of delta over the lookback/scale + momentum coefficient × (close - earlier close)/ATR. Delta is volume × (close - open)/floored(high - low); scale is SMA(abs(delta)) × lookback, floored at a positive parameter, with current abs(delta) substituted for an unavailable SMA.
Calculation: ATR volatility strength = (close - EMA(close))/ATR × (1 + expansion coefficient × (ATR/SMA(ATR) - 1)). The ATR baseline SMA length is at least the ATR length and depends on lookback; substitute current ATR for an unavailable baseline and zero for expansion when the baseline is nonpositive.
Calculation: Bollinger strength = position coefficient × (close - SMA(close))/band width × (band width/average band width). Bands use a standard-deviation multiplier dependent on sensitivity; average width is an SMA whose length is at least the band length and depends on lookback. Floor width at a small positive value, substitute current width for an unavailable average, and use an expansion factor of one if the average is nonpositive.
Calculation: Keltner strength = position coefficient × (close - EMA(close))/channel width × (channel width/average channel width). Bands are the EMA plus and minus ATR multiplied by a sensitivity-dependent multiplier; average width is an SMA whose length is at least the channel length and depends on lookback. Floor width at a small positive value, substitute current width for an unavailable average, and use an expansion factor of one if the average is nonpositive.
Calculation: Donchian strength = position coefficient × (close - channel midpoint)/channel range + breakout bias + momentum coefficient × (close - earlier close)/ATR. The channel includes the current bar; breakout bias is positive when close > the previous bar's upper channel, negative when close < its lower channel, and otherwise zero. Floor channel range at a small positive value.
Calculation: Historical volatility strength = percentage close change over the lookback/max(historical volatility, positive floor) × scale × (historical volatility/average historical volatility). Historical volatility is the standard deviation of log close returns multiplied by an annualization parameter; use zero return when either close is nonpositive. Its average is an SMA with length at least the volatility length and dependent on lookback. Substitute current volatility for an unavailable average and use an expansion factor of one if the average is nonpositive; use zero percentage change when the earlier close is zero.
Calculation: Indicator classification = For each enabled indicator, assign a positive strong score when its strength >= its higher positive threshold, a positive score when strength >= its lower positive threshold, a negative strong score when strength <= the negative of its higher threshold, a negative score when strength <= the negative of its lower threshold, and zero otherwise. Thresholds depend on that indicator's sensitivity; the higher threshold exceeds the lower threshold. Disabled indicators contribute zero.
Calculation: Category score = Normalize the sum of enabled indicator classifications to a bounded percentage using the number enabled and the maximum positive and negative classification; use the neutral midpoint if none are enabled. The trend category contains EMA trend, MACD, Supertrend, Ichimoku, and Parabolic SAR; momentum contains RSI, stochastic RSI, CCI, Williams %R, and ROC; volume contains VWAP, OBV, accumulation/distribution, volume moving-average, and volume delta; volatility contains ATR volatility, Bollinger, Keltner, Donchian, and historical volatility.
Calculation: Overall score = Bounded weighted average of the four category scores, with trend weighted most, followed by momentum, volume, and volatility.
Long: Any enabled indicator's strength >= its lower positive threshold OR any category score >= the bullish score threshold OR overall score >= the bullish score threshold OR overall score > the buy-confidence alert threshold.
Short: Any enabled indicator's strength <= the negative of its lower positive threshold OR any category score < the bearish score threshold OR overall score < the bearish score threshold OR overall score < the complement of the sell-confidence alert threshold.
