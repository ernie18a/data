Calculation: All rolling sums, standard deviations, and ALMAs use the analysis lookback unless otherwise specified. ALMA offset and sigma are parameters.
Calculation: Processed volume = volume with missing values replaced by zero, capped at ALMA(raw volume) + 4 × standard deviation(raw volume) when that ALMA is positive; otherwise use raw volume. Replace a missing or zero volume standard deviation with a small positive constant.
Calculation: Previous close = previous bar's close, or current open if unavailable. Effective high = max(high, previous close); effective low = max(min(low, previous close), small positive constant). Define L(a,b) = 100 × ln(a/b) when both arguments are positive, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Hybrid volatility = max(sqrt(ALMA(0.5 × L(open, previous close)^2 + 0.25 × (max(0.5 × L(effective high, effective low)^2 − (2 × ln(2) − 1) × L(close, open)^2, 0) + max(L(effective high, close) × L(effective high, open) + L(effective low, close) × L(effective low, open), 0)))), minimum volatility); use the minimum volatility when the smoothed variance is unavailable or negative.
Calculation: Volume-weighted efficiency = ALMA(min(abs(sum((close − previous close) × processed volume)) / sum(abs(close − previous close) × processed volume), 1)); use 0.5 for the unsmoothed ratio when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Body = abs(close − open); range = high − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; total wicks = upper wick + lower wick. Close flow = 2 × ((close − low) / range − 0.5) when range > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Anomaly weight = tanh(effort / 2), where effort is the Mahalanobis distance of processed volume and body when volume z-score > 0 AND body z-score < volume z-score, otherwise 0. The volume z-score uses ALMA(raw volume) and standard deviation(raw volume); the body z-score uses ALMA(body) and standard deviation(body). The distance is sqrt(max((volume z-score^2 + body z-score^2 − 2 × correlation × volume z-score × body z-score) / (1 − correlation^2), 0)), with correlation = (ALMA(processed volume × body) − ALMA(raw volume) × ALMA(body)) / max(volume standard deviation × body standard deviation, small positive constant), clamped to [−0.99, 0.99]. Replace missing or zero volume standard deviation and clamp body standard deviation to a small positive constant.
Calculation: Price imbalance = (1 − weight) × L(close, (open + previous close) / 2) + weight × L(close, open) + optional anomaly adjustment, where weight = log-structure weight × tanh(volume-weighted efficiency × ln(1 + hybrid volatility)) and the anomaly adjustment, when enabled, is 0.4 × close flow × anomaly weight × hybrid volatility; otherwise it is zero.
Calculation: Direction weight = normal cumulative distribution function(price imbalance / hybrid volatility), using the source's error-function approximation with its input clamped to [−4, 4]. Buy power per bar = direction weight × processed volume when the rolling sum of processed volume is positive, otherwise direction weight. Sell power per bar = (1 − direction weight) × processed volume when that rolling sum is positive, otherwise 1 − direction weight. Replace unavailable per-bar power with zero before summing.
Calculation: Buy percentage = 100 × rolling sum(buy power) / rolling sum(buy power + sell power), or 50 when the denominator is zero. If it is at least 100 and close is below the rolling highest effective high, replace it with the upper cap; if it is at most 0 and close is above the rolling lowest effective low, replace it with the lower cap. Sell percentage = 100 − buy percentage. Smooth each percentage with a separate, shorter-lookback ALMA using the same parameters, then round each to the nearest percentage increment.
Calculation: Centroid = low + range × (0.5 + clamp(0.3 × close flow + 0.15 × wick asymmetry + 0.05 × candle momentum, −0.45, 0.45)), or (high + low) / 2 when range is zero. Wick asymmetry = (lower wick − upper wick) / total wicks when total wicks > 0, otherwise 0. Candle momentum = (close − open) / max(body, small positive constant).
Calculation: Wick pressure = 100 × (upper wick − lower wick) / range when range > 0, otherwise 0; smooth it with the same ALMA as the power percentages and round to the same increment.
Calculation: Velocity = 100 × body / range and wick ratio = 100 × total wicks / range when range > 0, otherwise both are 0. Each percentile is the normal cumulative distribution function of (current value − ALMA(value)) / max(standard deviation(value), small positive constant), using the same error-function approximation as direction weight.
Calculation: Normalized imbalance = min(100 × imbalance scale × micro imbalance / max(ALMA(micro imbalance), small positive constant), 100), where micro imbalance = body / max(total wicks, small positive constant) when total wicks > 0, otherwise 1.
