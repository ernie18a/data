Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal left and right lookbacks; use only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; zone = [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; zone = [current high, low two bars earlier].
Calculation: Eligible FVG = same-direction stored gap within the configured ATR-width, age, structure-distance, prior-touch, and formation-displacement limits; if multiple qualify, select the one with the highest configured candidate score.
Calculation: Armed setup = a confirmed break of the latest unbroken confirmed swing high/low, followed by selection of an eligible same-direction FVG within the configured post-break scan window; expire if the setup wait limit passes or price closes through the far edge of the zone.
Calculation: Retest contact = the selected configured pattern: (wick overlaps zone) OR (real body overlaps zone) OR (close lies inside zone) OR (for long, low reaches zone top and close > zone top and close > open; for short, high reaches zone bottom and close < zone bottom and close < open). Adaptive patterns are: First Contact = wick overlaps zone; Directional Recovery = directional wick overlap with close on the setup side of the midpoint and candle direction, OR body overlap plus boundary reclaim; Edge Reclaim = boundary reclaim; Rejection Wick = boundary reclaim plus directional candle and corresponding wick >= 30% of candle range.
Long: no active trade AND confirmed bullish break above latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND eligible bullish FVG selected and armed AND retest delay elapsed AND retest contact AND configured reaction-strength and minimum-grade checks pass AND (volume filter disabled or unavailable or volume >= configured multiple of its moving average) AND (DI filter disabled or +DI > -DI and ADX >= configured minimum) AND (HTF filter disabled, invalid, or previous completed higher-timeframe close > fast EMA > slow EMA) AND current bar is confirmed.
Short: no active trade AND confirmed bearish break below latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND eligible bearish FVG selected and armed AND retest delay elapsed AND retest contact AND configured reaction-strength and minimum-grade checks pass AND (volume filter disabled or unavailable or volume >= configured multiple of its moving average) AND (DI filter disabled or -DI > +DI and ADX >= configured minimum) AND (HTF filter disabled, invalid, or previous completed higher-timeframe close < fast EMA < slow EMA) AND current bar is confirmed.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target OR configured target fills reduce remaining position to zero.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target OR configured target fills reduce remaining position to zero.
