Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal left and right lookbacks, available only after the right lookback completes; each newly confirmed pivot replaces the previous same-side pivot and resets its broken state.
Calculation: Structure break = first crossing of the latest unbroken swing plus/minus an ATR clearance, using close or high/low according to the selected break mode; compare the previous bar with the current bar's clearance level. Optionally require absolute candle body divided by ATR to meet a minimum. Original mode uses no clearance or body requirement. Mark the swing broken only when the break passes its enabled requirements.
Calculation: FVG = bullish zone [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago, or bearish zone [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. Store it if width >= ATR times minimum width and, outside original mode when enabled, the preceding candle's absolute body divided by its ATR and a normalization factor, capped at one, >= minimum displacement. Store formation displacement and capped formation volume divided by its volume SMA and a normalization factor; use a neutral volume score when unavailable. Count separate runs of subsequent bar overlaps. Delete zones beyond maximum age, optionally on a close beyond the opposite boundary, and delete oldest zones beyond capacity.
Calculation: Candidate score = a weighted sum of capped components: proximity of zone midpoint to break price in ATR units, proximity of zone birth to break bar, zone width in ATR units, remaining fraction of permitted age, formation displacement, formation volume, favorable placement of the midpoint in the recent high-low dealing range, absence of prior touches, and break strength. Original mode uses only timing, width, price proximity, and freshness. Break strength is a weighted sum of capped absolute break body in ATR units and favorable close clearance beyond the swing in ATR units. Location score increases with distance into the favorable half of the dealing range and decreases with distance into the unfavorable half. Untouched score decreases with the number of prior touch runs.
Calculation: Armed zone = on each allowed confirmed structure break, replace any pending or armed setup and scan stored matching-direction FVGs from the break bar through the permitted follow-through window. Candidates must have been born no earlier than the permitted number of bars before the break; outside original mode they must also satisfy maximum prior touches and minimum candidate score. On the first scan bar with eligible candidates, select the highest-scoring candidate, resolving ties in favor of the oldest stored candidate. Freeze its score, boundaries, birth bar, break bar and break strength. Cancel it when a later allowed break replaces it, its break-relative expiry is exceeded, or its close crosses the opposite zone boundary. Breaks are allowed only after the post-trade cooldown; scanning and arming can occur while a position is active.
Calculation: Retest = use tolerance equal to current close times the selected margin. Wick overlap means low <= zone top plus tolerance AND high >= zone bottom minus tolerance; body overlap uses min(open, close) and max(open, close) in place of low and high. Close-inside means close lies between the tolerance-expanded boundaries. Bullish edge reclaim means low <= zone top plus tolerance AND close > zone top; bearish edge reclaim means high >= zone bottom minus tolerance AND close < zone bottom. Original modes select wick overlap, body overlap, close-inside, or edge reclaim AND a same-direction candle. Adaptive modes select wick overlap; OR wick overlap AND a same-direction candle AND close on the favorable side of the zone midpoint, or body overlap AND edge reclaim; OR edge reclaim; OR edge reclaim AND a same-direction candle AND favorable wick divided by candle range >= a rejection threshold.
Calculation: Reaction score = capped weighted sum of favorable close location in the candle range, favorable wick fraction, capped absolute body relative to ATR, same-direction candle indicator, and penetration quality. Penetration is the capped fraction of zone width reached by the low for bullish zones or high for bearish zones; penetration quality peaks at the zone midpoint and falls linearly toward either edge.
Calculation: Setup score = outside original mode, capped weighted sum of frozen candidate score, reaction score, capped current volume relative to its SMA, capped directional DI difference and ADX score, and higher-timeframe alignment score. The alignment score is neutral when the filter is disabled or its timeframe is invalid, otherwise it indicates whether alignment passes. Original mode uses the frozen candidate score and maps score thresholds to an ordered star rating; adaptive mode compares the setup score with the threshold for the selected minimum grade. Volume and DI scores contribute even when their filters are disabled.
Calculation: Risk distance = maximum of entry price times minimum stop percentage and the absolute difference between entry price and the selected raw stop. The raw stop is the selected zone's opposite boundary plus an adverse ATR buffer, or entry plus an adverse ATR multiple. Original stop is entry plus the adverse risk distance; targets are entry plus favorable risk multiples. Freeze all levels at entry. The active stop becomes entry on the bar after the first target is reached when breakeven is enabled. Process exits only after the entry bar; on a bar touching both stop and targets, use the selected stop-first or target-first priority. At the first and second targets reduce the remaining position by their selected shares, capped at the remaining amount; the third target closes all remaining shares.
Long: Confirmed bullish structure break.
Short: Confirmed bearish structure break.
Long: No active position AND armed bullish zone exists AND bars since its break >= minimum delay AND current bar is later than its birth AND selected retest condition passes AND, outside original mode, reaction score >= minimum AND selected rating or grade threshold passes AND (volume filter disabled OR adaptive mode has unavailable volume data OR volume >= volume SMA times multiplier) AND (DI filter disabled OR positive DI > negative DI AND ADX >= minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected timeframe is not higher OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its shorter EMA > its longer EMA).
Short: No active position AND armed bearish zone exists AND bars since its break >= minimum delay AND current bar is later than its birth AND selected retest condition passes AND, outside original mode, reaction score >= minimum AND selected rating or grade threshold passes AND (volume filter disabled OR adaptive mode has unavailable volume data OR volume >= volume SMA times multiplier) AND (DI filter disabled OR negative DI > positive DI AND ADX >= minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected timeframe is not higher OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its shorter EMA < its longer EMA).
Exit: long, a later bar's low <= active stop OR its high >= an unreached target, subject to selected same-bar priority and target position shares.
Exit: short, a later bar's high >= active stop OR its low <= an unreached target, subject to selected same-bar priority and target position shares.
