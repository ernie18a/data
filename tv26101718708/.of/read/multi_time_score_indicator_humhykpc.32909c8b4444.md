Calculation: Range score = ((previous completed chart-bar close − lowest low in the lookback range) / (highest high − lowest low) × 100) − 50; calculate for the chart timeframe and higher timeframes using completed bars, with each higher-timeframe range shifted back one higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Weighted total score = weighted average of the chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe range scores, using a separate configurable importance weight for each.
Long: Weighted total score > sensitivity threshold
Short: Weighted total score < 0
