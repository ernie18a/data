Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low from the exchange-session open through the configured opening-range window; freeze when the first confirmed bar reaches or passes the window end.
Calculation: Static key levels = enabled prior-session, overnight, pre-market, prior-day, prior-week, or prior-month reference prices, held fixed for the current session.
Calculation: Retest tolerance = configured multiple of daily ATR, configured multiple of chart-timeframe ATR, or configured number of ticks. Daily ATR is a 14-session RMA of true range using the last completed session; chart-timeframe ATR uses chart bars.
Long: Confirmed close > locked opening-range high AND range passes the optional width filter AND outside the optional killzone AND that range has not already broken up this session OR confirmed close crosses above an enabled static key level from a prior close at or below it AND outside the optional killzone OR after an upward break, low <= broken level + retest tolerance AND confirmed close > broken level
Short: Confirmed close < locked opening-range low AND range passes the optional width filter AND outside the optional killzone AND that range has not already broken down this session OR confirmed close crosses below an enabled static key level from a prior close at or above it AND outside the optional killzone OR after a downward break, high >= broken level - retest tolerance AND confirmed close < broken level
