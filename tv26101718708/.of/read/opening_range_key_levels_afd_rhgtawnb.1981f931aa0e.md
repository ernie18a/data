Calculation: Opening range = high and low of chart bars beginning within a configurable window starting at the exchange-session open; freeze on the first confirmed bar whose close time reaches the window end. Reset each session.
Calculation: Session ATR = RMA of completed higher-timeframe exchange-session true ranges, seeded with the first available true range and updated at each session open.
Calculation: Retest tolerance = a configurable multiple of session ATR, chart-timeframe ATR, or a configurable absolute price distance.
Calculation: Key level = the last completed higher-timeframe exchange-session open, high, low, or close; current exchange-session open; high or low between the preceding session close and current session open; high or low between local midnight and current session open; or high or low of the last completed higher-timeframe calendar week or month. Snapshot each level at the current session open.
Calculation: Opening-range break state = independently for each range and direction, record the first qualifying break per session; the latest break replaces the previous retest state. If the optional exclusion window is enabled, a close beyond an as-yet-unbroken boundary during that window blocks that direction for the rest of the session. Optionally require the frozen range width to be between configured minimum and maximum multiples of session ATR; if session ATR is unavailable, pass the width filter.
Calculation: Key-level break state = initialize the side from the session-opening bar close; thereafter retain the last nonzero side of each level. Record at most one crossing per direction per session, updating the side even during the optional exclusion window. Resolve retests before crossings and suppress a crossing on a bar that resolves a retest. The latest recorded break replaces the previous retest state.
Calculation: Retest state = after a recorded break, starting on a later bar, arm a retest when low is at or below the upward-broken level plus tolerance or high is at or above the downward-broken level minus tolerance. On the first armed bar with close unequal to the level, resolve the retest and do not rearm it until another break. During the optional exclusion window, reset an armed but unresolved retest.
Long: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, a width-valid frozen opening range has no earlier upward break or blocked upward direction this session AND close > opening-range high.
Short: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, a width-valid frozen opening range has no earlier downward break or blocked downward direction this session AND close < opening-range low.
Long: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, a key level has no earlier upward break this session AND its previous nonzero side is below AND close > key level AND no retest resolves on this bar.
Short: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, a key level has no earlier downward break this session AND its previous nonzero side is above AND close < key level AND no retest resolves on this bar.
Long: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, an armed retest of a width-valid opening range or key level resolves AND close > the tested level.
Short: During the exchange session on a confirmed bar outside the optional exclusion window, an armed retest of a width-valid opening range or key level resolves AND close < the tested level.
Long: The longest enabled opening range is frozen AND close > its high.
Short: The longest enabled opening range is frozen AND close < its low.
