Long: flat position AND current time is within the start/end window AND each of the three higher-timeframe RSI values (fast, middle, slow; last completed bars) is below its long threshold AND each of the three higher-timeframe CCI values (fast, middle, slow; last completed bars) is below its long threshold AND (if Bollinger filter enabled, close < lower Bollinger Band on the higher timeframe’s last completed bar) AND (if CCI range filter enabled, higher-timeframe CCI on its last completed bar is between its lower and upper thresholds)
Short: flat position AND current time is within the start/end window AND each of the three higher-timeframe RSI values (fast, middle, slow; last completed bars) is above its short threshold AND each of the three higher-timeframe CCI values (fast, middle, slow; last completed bars) is above its short threshold AND (if Bollinger filter enabled, close > upper Bollinger Band on the higher timeframe’s last completed bar) AND (if CCI range filter enabled, higher-timeframe CCI on its last completed bar is between its lower and upper thresholds)
Exit: long, high >= position average entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) OR (stop-loss enabled AND low <= first entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage)); previous bar position was long
Exit: short, low <= position average entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) OR (stop-loss enabled AND high >= first entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage)); previous bar position was short
