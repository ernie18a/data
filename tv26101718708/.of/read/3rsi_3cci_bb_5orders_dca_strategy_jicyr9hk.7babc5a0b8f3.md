Long: Every enabled and available long RSI is below its own threshold AND every enabled and available long CCI is below its own threshold AND, if enabled and available, chart source is below the lower Bollinger Band AND, if enabled and available, band CCI is strictly between its lower and upper thresholds; this condition also holds when no enabled calculation is available. Each RSI and CCI uses its independently selected timeframe and lookback; Bollinger Bands and band CCI use their own independently selected timeframes and lookbacks. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Every enabled and available short RSI is above its own threshold AND every enabled and available short CCI is above its own threshold AND, if enabled and available, chart source is above the upper Bollinger Band AND, if enabled and available, band CCI is strictly between its lower and upper thresholds; this condition also holds when no enabled calculation is available. Each RSI and CCI uses its independently selected timeframe and lookback; Bollinger Bands and band CCI use their own independently selected timeframes and lookbacks. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Exit: long, previous bar's position side is long AND (high >= average position entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction) OR, if stop loss is enabled, low <= first open trade's entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction)).
Exit: short, previous bar's position side is short AND (low <= average position entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction) OR, if stop loss is enabled, high >= first open trade's entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction)).
