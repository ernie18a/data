Calculation: Coral trend = let n be the smoothing parameter, d the coefficient, a = 2 / (((n - 1) / 2 + 1) + 1), and b = 1 - a; recursively calculate i1 = a × close + b × previous i1 and i2 through i6 the same way using the preceding series as input, with missing previous values replaced by zero. Coral trend = -d³ × i6 + 3(d² + d³) × i5 - 3(2d² + d + d³) × i4 + (3d + 1 + d³ + 3d²) × i3.
Calculation: Bars since an event = number of bars since its most recent occurrence, including zero on the event bar; undefined before its first occurrence. A cross above means current first value > current second value AND previous first value <= previous second value; a cross below reverses the inequalities. For Coral trend slope crosses, replace missing previous Coral values with zero.
Calculation: Directional indicators = +DI and -DI from directional movement and true range, each smoothed recursively as current input + previous smoothed value × (1 - 1 / length), initialized at zero. ADX = simple moving average over that same length of 100 × abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI).
Calculation: Absolute strength = apply the selected moving average first over an evaluation length and then over a smoothing length to separate bullish and bearish inputs. For the RSI method, these inputs are max(source - previous source, 0) and max(previous source - source, 0); for the stochastic method, source - its rolling lowest value and its rolling highest value - source; for the ADX method, max(high - previous high, 0) and max(previous low - low, 0). The selectable moving averages are SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, ALMA, and a smoothed method defined as (previous WMA × (length - 1) + current input) / length, using SMA when the previous WMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = let range = high - low, average range and average volume be their respective simple moving averages over the same lookback, and previous midpoint = (previous high + previous low) / 2. Let upper and lower = previous midpoint plus and minus previous range / divisor. Neutral = (range > average range AND close > lower AND close < upper AND volume > average volume AND volume < average volume × 1.5 AND volume > previous volume) OR (range < average range / 1.5 AND volume < average volume / 1.5) OR (close > lower AND close < upper). Bullish = NOT neutral AND (close > previous midpoint OR (range > average range AND close > upper AND volume > average volume) OR (high > previous high AND range < average range / 1.5 AND volume < average volume) OR (low < previous low AND range < average range / 1.5 AND volume > average volume)). Bearish = NOT neutral AND ((range > average range AND close < lower AND volume > average volume) OR close < previous midpoint).
Calculation: Entry confirmation = choose one method: none; directional indicators, requiring ADX > threshold AND ADX > previous ADX AND +DI > -DI for long or -DI > +DI for short; or absolute strength and volume, requiring bullish absolute strength > bearish absolute strength AND bullish volume confirmation for long, or bullish absolute strength <= bearish absolute strength AND bearish volume confirmation for short.
Calculation: Entry risk distance = at the signal bar, absolute difference between close and the rolling lowest low for a long or rolling highest high for a short, including the signal bar; round the distance to the nearest price tick. Profit distance = the unrounded risk distance × reward-to-risk parameter, rounded to the nearest price tick. Hold both distances fixed until the next entry.
Long: datetime >= start date AND datetime < end date AND position side is not long AND Coral trend > previous Coral trend AND bars since low > Coral trend AND high > Coral trend < previous bar's bars since close crossed above Coral trend AND bars since close crossed below Coral trend < bars since Coral trend crossed above its previous value AND close crosses above Coral trend AND long entry confirmation is satisfied.
Short: datetime >= start date AND datetime < end date AND position side is not short AND Coral trend < previous Coral trend AND bars since low < Coral trend AND high < Coral trend < previous bar's bars since close crossed below Coral trend AND bars since close crossed above Coral trend < bars since Coral trend crossed below its previous value AND close crosses below Coral trend AND short entry confirmation is satisfied.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - entry risk distance OR high >= entry price + profit distance OR short entry condition is satisfied.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry risk distance OR low <= entry price - profit distance OR long entry condition is satisfied.
