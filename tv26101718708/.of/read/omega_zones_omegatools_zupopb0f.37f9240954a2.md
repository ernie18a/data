Calculation: Average candle range = simple moving average of high minus low over a lookback
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - its simple moving average) / max(its standard deviation, 1), using the same lookback for both statistics
Calculation: Pivot low/high = low/high that is lower/higher than the bars in a longer left lookback and a shorter right confirmation window
Calculation: Origin candle = for a pivot low, the pivot bar if bearish, otherwise the bar immediately before it; for a pivot high, the pivot bar if bullish, otherwise the bar immediately before it
Calculation: Origin fit = origin candle range < 1.5 × average candle range
Calculation: Prior high/low = highest high/lowest low over a lookback ending immediately before the origin candle
Calculation: High sweep = origin high > prior high AND origin close < prior high
Calculation: Low sweep = origin low < prior low AND origin close > prior low
Calculation: Displacement = current close - origin close
Calculation: Bullish reclaim = pivot low AND at least one close after the pivot and before the current bar > pivot high AND the bar immediately before the pivot is bearish AND displacement > average true range AND origin fit
Calculation: Bearish reclaim = pivot high AND at least one close after the pivot and before the current bar < pivot low AND the bar immediately before the pivot is bullish AND displacement < -average true range AND origin fit
Calculation: Tight base = pivot bar range < 0.8 × average candle range
Calculation: Rally into base = the three closes immediately before the pivot rise consecutively
Calculation: Drop into base = the three closes immediately before the pivot fall consecutively
Calculation: Rally out of base = closes from the pivot through the current bar rise consecutively
Calculation: Drop out of base = closes from the pivot through the current bar fall consecutively
Calculation: Rally-base-rally = rally into base AND pivot bar bearish AND tight base AND rally out of base
Calculation: Drop-base-drop = drop into base AND pivot bar bullish AND tight base AND drop out of base
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low of the bar immediately after the origin > high of the bar immediately before the origin
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = high of the bar immediately after the origin < low of the bar immediately before the origin
Calculation: Strength = 1 + 1 if absolute displacement > 2 × average true range + 1 if absolute displacement > 4 × average true range + 1 each if origin volume z-score > 0, > 1, > 2, and > 3 + 1 if either fair value gap condition holds + 1 if either sweep condition holds
Calculation: Minimum strength = selected minimum threshold for displaying a zone
Long: (bullish reclaim OR rally-base-rally) AND NOT (bearish reclaim OR drop-base-drop) AND current close > origin low AND strength >= minimum strength
Short: (bearish reclaim OR drop-base-drop) AND NOT (bullish reclaim OR rally-base-rally) AND current close < origin high AND strength >= minimum strength
