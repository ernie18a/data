Calculation: Let C be the confirmation-window length, and let bar offsets be measured backward from the current bar. A confirmed pivot low or high is a pivot with a longer left window and a right window of C bars.
Calculation: Origin offset A = C if a confirmed pivot low exists and close[C] < open[C], otherwise C + 1 if a confirmed pivot low exists; otherwise C if a confirmed pivot high exists and close[C] > open[C], otherwise C + 1 if a confirmed pivot high exists; otherwise C. If both pivots exist, the pivot-low rule takes precedence.
Calculation: Displacement = close[0] - close[A]. Origin volume z-score = (volume[A] - SMA(volume)[A]) / max(standard deviation of volume[A], minimum denominator). The SMA and standard deviation use the same lookback.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = confirmed pivot low AND at least one close from offset 0 through C - 1 > high[C] AND close[C + 1] < open[C + 1] AND displacement > current ATR AND high[A] - low[A] < current SMA(high - low) multiplied by an origin-range limit.
Calculation: Bearish reversal = confirmed pivot high AND at least one close from offset 0 through C - 1 < low[C] AND close[C + 1] > open[C + 1] AND displacement < negative current ATR AND high[A] - low[A] < current SMA(high - low) multiplied by the same origin-range limit.
Calculation: Bullish continuation = close[C + 1] > close[C + 2] AND close[C + 2] > close[C + 3] AND close[C] < open[C] AND high[C] - low[C] < current SMA(high - low) multiplied by a base-range limit AND close[k] > close[k + 1] for every k from 0 through C - 1. The base-range limit is smaller than the origin-range limit.
Calculation: Bearish continuation = close[C + 1] < close[C + 2] AND close[C + 2] < close[C + 3] AND close[C] > open[C] AND high[C] - low[C] < current SMA(high - low) multiplied by the same base-range limit AND close[k] < close[k + 1] for every k from 0 through C - 1.
Calculation: Strength = 1 plus one point for each of two increasing absolute-displacement thresholds expressed as multiples of current ATR that |displacement| exceeds, plus one point for each of four increasing origin volume z-score thresholds exceeded, the first of which is zero, plus one point if low[A - 1] > high[A + 1] OR high[A - 1] < low[A + 1], plus one point if high[A] > highest high over a lookback ending at A + 1 AND close[A] < that highest high, OR low[A] < lowest low over the same lookback ending at A + 1 AND close[A] > that lowest low.
Long: (bullish reversal OR bullish continuation) AND NOT (bearish reversal OR bearish continuation) AND close[0] > low[A] AND strength >= minimum strength.
Short: (bearish reversal OR bearish continuation) AND NOT (bullish reversal OR bullish continuation) AND close[0] < high[A] AND strength >= minimum strength.
