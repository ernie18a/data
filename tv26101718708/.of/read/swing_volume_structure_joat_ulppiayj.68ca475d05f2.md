Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is respectively the highest/lowest over the configured left and right bars, confirmed after the right bars close; on ties, the most recent extreme wins. Update the latest pivot before evaluating the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Swing volume = volume accumulated from the bar after the preceding pivot confirmation through the current confirmation bar; if a high and a low confirm together, process the high first and set the low's swing volume to zero. An increase means the latest swing volume exceeds its value on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume divided by its simple moving average is at least the expansion threshold; use a ratio of one when the average is not positive.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low. Body = absolute value of close - open.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high. Bearish breakout = close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish retest = low <= latest confirmed pivot high AND close > that pivot high AND previous close > that same pivot high. Bearish retest = high >= latest confirmed pivot low AND close < that pivot low AND previous close < that same pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND min(open, close) - low > body times the wick multiplier. Bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND high - max(open, close) > body times the same wick multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish expansion candle = close > open AND close position >= the upper close-position threshold AND body / (high - low) >= the body-fraction threshold. Bearish expansion candle = close < open AND close position <= the complementary lower close-position threshold AND body / (high - low) >= the same body-fraction threshold. Neither applies when high equals low.
Calculation: Breakout score = base score plus separate weights for breakout, retest, volume expansion, same-direction expansion candle, favorable pivot class, and increased swing volume whenever each respective condition holds. Favorable pivot class means the latest pivot low is higher than the preceding pivot low for a long, or the latest pivot high is no higher than the preceding pivot high for a short. Use the same weights for both directions.
Calculation: Sweep score = a separate base score plus separate weights for the same-direction sweep, volume expansion, and favorable close position whenever each respective condition holds. Favorable close position means above an upper threshold for a long or below its complementary lower threshold for a short. Use the same weights for both directions.
Calculation: Cooldown ready = no previous qualifying breakout or sweep state exists OR the number of bars since the latest such state exceeds the cooldown parameter. Update the latest state bar when any qualifying breakout or sweep occurs, even if both directions occur together.
Long: Cooldown ready AND ((bullish breakout OR bullish retest) AND bullish breakout score >= the minimum score OR bullish sweep AND bullish sweep score >= the same minimum score).
Short: Cooldown ready AND ((bearish breakout OR bearish retest) AND bearish breakout score >= the minimum score OR bearish sweep AND bearish sweep score >= the same minimum score).
Long: A newly confirmed pivot high > the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a newly confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high <= the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a newly confirmed pivot low <= the preceding confirmed pivot low.
