Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) * volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each daily or weekly boundary; undefined when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA of close on the chart timeframe or, optionally, the last completed bar of a higher timeframe. Its lookback is longer than the slow EMA lookback, which is longer than the fast EMA lookback.
Long: close > trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > anchored VWAP) AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > previous fast EMA AND RSI < upper threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND low >= slow EMA * (1 - lower touch offset) AND low <= slow EMA * (1 + upper touch offset) AND close > open AND ((previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close) OR min(open, close) - low > wick-to-body multiplier * abs(close - open)).
Short: close < trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < anchored VWAP) AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < previous fast EMA AND RSI > lower threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND high >= slow EMA * (1 - upper touch offset) AND high <= slow EMA * (1 + lower touch offset) AND close < open AND ((previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close) OR high - max(open, close) > wick-to-body multiplier * abs(close - open)).
Long: close > trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > anchored VWAP) AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > previous fast EMA AND RSI < upper threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND low >= slow EMA * (1 - lower touch offset) AND low <= slow EMA * (1 + approach offset) AND the long entry condition above is false.
Short: close < trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < anchored VWAP) AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < previous fast EMA AND RSI > lower threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND high >= slow EMA * (1 - approach offset) AND high <= slow EMA * (1 + lower touch offset) AND the short entry condition above is false.
