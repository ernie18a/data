Calculation: Fast EMA = shorter-lookback exponential moving average of close on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Slow EMA = longer-lookback exponential moving average of close on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Trend EMA = long-term exponential moving average of close on the chart timeframe or the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, as configured
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the configured daily or weekly boundary
Long: (close > Trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > Anchored VWAP) AND Fast EMA > Slow EMA AND Fast EMA > prior-bar Fast EMA AND RSI < configured upper threshold AND session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND ((low <= Slow EMA × 1.001 AND low >= Slow EMA × 0.998 AND bullish reversal candle) OR (low <= Slow EMA × (1 + configured approach percentage) AND low >= Slow EMA × 0.998 AND NOT (low <= Slow EMA × 1.001 AND low >= Slow EMA × 0.998 AND bullish reversal candle)))
Short: (close < Trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < Anchored VWAP) AND Fast EMA < Slow EMA AND Fast EMA < prior-bar Fast EMA AND RSI > configured lower threshold AND session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND ((high >= Slow EMA × 0.999 AND high <= Slow EMA × 1.002 AND bearish reversal candle) OR (high >= Slow EMA × (1 - configured approach percentage) AND high <= Slow EMA × 1.002 AND NOT (high >= Slow EMA × 0.999 AND high <= Slow EMA × 1.002 AND bearish reversal candle)))
