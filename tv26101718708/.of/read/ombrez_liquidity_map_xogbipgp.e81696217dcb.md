Calculation: Liquidity levels = Enabled confirmed pivot highs and lows, completed session highs and lows, and the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high and low. A pivot is confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side. Treat highs as upper levels and lows as lower levels. For each new pivot, update the first active level of the same kind within an ATR-based tolerance to the touch-count-weighted average and increment its touch count; otherwise create a level. On session completion, replace the active level of that session and side. When the previous higher-timeframe high changes, replace both previous-period levels. New levels start with one touch. Remove levels after a configurable maximum age, and cap the collection by removing the lowest-scored levels. Process level creation and updates before verdicts on each closed chart bar.
Calculation: Level score = A level-type base score plus a configurable touch-count weight times touches minus a configurable age penalty times bars since creation. The previous-period base score is greater than the session base score, which is greater than the pivot base score. When the optional strength filter is enabled, a verdict requires the score to meet its threshold.
Calculation: Level verdict = For each active level, record the first bar whose high exceeds an upper level or whose low falls below a lower level, and count that bar and subsequent bars whose close is strictly beyond the level. Starting on the bar after the first breach, mark the level as a breakout when the cumulative count reaches a configurable threshold. Otherwise, after a configurable number of bars since the breach, mark it as a stop grab when the close is not strictly beyond the level. Breakout takes precedence. A verdict permanently deactivates the level; evaluate the optional strength filter on the verdict bar. Level prices may change after a breach if subsequent pivots update the level.
Long: An upper level has a breakout verdict AND the optional strength filter passes.
Long: A lower level has a stop-grab verdict AND the optional strength filter passes.
Long: Close > the open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Short: A lower level has a breakout verdict AND the optional strength filter passes.
Short: An upper level has a stop-grab verdict AND the optional strength filter passes.
Short: Close <= the open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
