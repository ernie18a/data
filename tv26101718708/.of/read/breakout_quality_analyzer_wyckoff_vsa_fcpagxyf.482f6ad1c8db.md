Calculation: Bar range R = high − low; close position P = (close − low) / R when R > 0, otherwise the neutral midpoint. Buy-volume ratio D = P when volume > 0, otherwise the neutral midpoint.
Calculation: Gap-adjusted range G = R + abs(open − previous close); gap-adjusted close position Q = (close − min(low, previous close)) / (max(high, previous close) − min(low, previous close)), or P if the denominator is zero. Gap-adjusted contraction compares G with previous R + abs(previous close − close before previous).
Calculation: Volume ratio V = volume / current simple moving average of volume; range ratio A = R / ATR; gap range ratio B = G / ATR. Use a denominator of one if the corresponding average is nonpositive. All averages include the current bar unless explicitly shifted.
Calculation: Prior weakness gate = at least one prior bar within the weakness lookback satisfies either (close > open AND volume > its volume average × a high multiplier AND its range / its ATR > a wide threshold AND its P < a low threshold) OR (close > open AND volume > its volume average × a lower high multiplier AND its range < its previous range AND its P < a higher low threshold).
Calculation: No supply = close < open AND V < low-volume threshold AND A < narrow-range threshold AND P ≥ high-close threshold. Shakeout = low < lowest low over the preceding support lookback AND close > that same lowest low AND V > high-volume threshold AND A > wide-range threshold AND P ≥ high-close threshold. No demand = close ≥ open AND V < low-volume threshold AND A < narrow-range threshold AND P ≤ low-close threshold AND prior weakness gate. Upthrust = high > highest high over the preceding resistance lookback AND close < that same highest high AND A > wide-range threshold AND P ≤ low-close threshold.
Calculation: Selling climax = close < open AND V > above-average threshold AND B > wide threshold AND Q > upper threshold. Stopping volume = current and previous two bars each have close < open AND their ranges strictly decrease AND their volumes strictly increase in chronological order AND current Q > lower upper threshold AND selling climax is false. Bottom reversal = close < open AND V > a lower above-average threshold AND Q > a higher upper threshold AND selling climax and stopping volume are both false.
Calculation: Bag holding = close ≥ open AND V > an above-average threshold AND Q > an upper threshold AND previous close < previous open AND previous volume > its volume average × an above-average multiplier AND (previous close − previous low) / max(previous range, small positive floor) > a threshold. Supply absorption = close ≥ open AND V > an above-average threshold AND B < a range threshold AND Q lies inclusively between lower and upper thresholds.
Calculation: Buying climax = close ≥ open AND V > above-average threshold AND B > wide threshold AND Q < low threshold. End of rising = close ≥ open AND V > above-average threshold AND gap-adjusted contraction AND Q < a higher low threshold AND buying climax is false. Top reversal = close ≥ open AND V > a lower above-average threshold AND Q < a lower low threshold AND buying climax and end of rising are both false. Supply over demand = close < open AND volume > previous volume × an above-one multiplier AND previous close ≥ previous open AND Q < a low threshold. Demand absorption = close < open AND V > above-average threshold AND B < a range threshold AND Q lies inclusively between lower and upper thresholds.
Calculation: Breakout = close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback AND previous close ≤ that same highest high AND volume > its current simple moving average × a breakout multiplier.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume × (2P − 1). Its change is the current value minus its value at the slope lookback; price change uses the same lookback. Pre-breakout changes compare the immediately preceding bar with an earlier bar over a separate lookback.
Calculation: Breakout quality score = max(zero, sum of these components): effort-result score, close-position score, absorption score, volume-surge score, volume-rank score, range score, cumulative-volume-delta score, buy-volume-ratio score, and VSA net score. Effort-result is (R / its current simple average) / (volume / its current simple average), or zero when volume effort is nonpositive; award successively higher points at three strictly increasing thresholds. Close position and buy-volume ratio each award successively higher points at their own three strictly increasing thresholds. Absorption awards one point each when the preceding shorter-window average volume / current longer-window average volume is below a threshold, the corresponding range ratio is below a threshold, and the ratio of counts of above-average-volume rising-close bars to falling-close bars over the shorter window including the current bar exceeds a threshold; use a fallback ratio above that threshold when the falling count is zero. Volume surge is current volume / preceding shorter-window average volume, or a neutral fallback if that average is nonpositive; award successively higher points at three increasing thresholds. Volume rank awards the higher point value if current volume ≥ the maximum over the breakout lookback including the current bar, otherwise a lower value if it exceeds the maximum over a shorter preceding lookback. Range score awards successively higher points when R / its current simple average exceeds either of two increasing thresholds. Delta score awards a higher base score when delta change > zero AND price change > zero, a lower base score when delta change < zero AND price change > zero, and an intermediate score otherwise; add a bonus when pre-breakout delta change > zero AND pre-breakout price change ≤ zero. VSA net adds the smaller bullish weight for no supply and the larger bullish weight for shakeout, subtracts the smaller bearish weight for no demand and the larger bearish weight for upthrust, and is floored at its minimum. Each tier's points and the score denominator are fixed scoring weights; comparison thresholds and lookbacks are parameters.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe quality score uses the last completed bar of a higher timeframe and the same effort-result, close-position and volume-surge scoring, plus the positive part of the VSA net score. For that higher-timeframe VSA net, replace current-bar range / ATR and P with G / ATR and Q; retain the prior weakness gate's ordinary range and close position. Its score denominator differs from the chart-timeframe denominator.
Calculation: Recent strength means any of selling climax, stopping volume, bottom reversal, bag holding, supply absorption, no supply or shakeout occurred within the recency lookback including the current bar. Recent weakness means any of buying climax, end of rising, top reversal, supply over demand, demand absorption, upthrust or no demand occurred within the same lookback.
Long: close < open AND D > hidden-buy threshold.
Long: no supply OR shakeout OR selling climax OR stopping volume OR bottom reversal OR bag holding OR supply absorption.
Long: breakout AND breakout quality score / chart-timeframe score denominator ≥ accumulation threshold.
Long: breakout AND breakout quality score / chart-timeframe score denominator ≥ lower likely-accumulation threshold.
Long: breakout AND breakout quality score / chart-timeframe score denominator ≥ accumulation threshold AND higher-timeframe quality score / higher-timeframe score denominator ≥ lower likely-accumulation threshold.
Long: recent strength AND no recent weakness.
Short: close > open AND D < hidden-sell threshold.
Short: no demand OR upthrust OR buying climax OR end of rising OR top reversal OR supply over demand OR demand absorption.
Short: breakout AND breakout quality score / chart-timeframe score denominator < exhaustion threshold.
Short: breakout AND cumulative-volume-delta change < zero AND price change > zero.
Short: recent weakness AND no recent strength.
Exit: long, end of rising occurred on an earlier bar within the weakness lookback AND no demand.
Exit: long, previous bar had recent strength AND no recent weakness AND current bar has recent weakness AND no recent strength.
Exit: long, bars since the most recent breakout > zero AND ≤ follow-through lookback AND close ≤ highest high over the preceding breakout lookback AND volume ≤ current volume average × sustained-volume threshold AND P ≤ midpoint.
