Calculation: Execution-bar values = OHLCV and indicators evaluated on the last completed execution-timeframe bar; the execution timeframe is the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Timeframe direction = bullish when short EMA > long EMA, close > long EMA, and long EMA is rising; bearish when short EMA < long EMA, close < long EMA, and long EMA is falling; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bar of each timeframe.
Calculation: MTF bullish/bearish count = number of bullish/bearish directions among the 4 lower or higher timeframes used for the main vote; the vote passes when its count meets the required minimum and exceeds the opposite count. The closed 5-minute direction is an additional lower-timeframe veto when scout confirmation is active.
Calculation: Bull/Bear confidence = round(100 × respective score / 36); rank = round(respective score / 3.6). Each score adds one for each true condition: MTF vote passes; own timeframe count exceeds the opposite count; 15-minute direction agrees; 1-hour direction agrees; 4-hour or daily direction agrees; close above/below fast EMA; fast EMA above/below slow EMA; close above/below slow EMA; slow EMA rising/falling; middle EMA above/below long EMA; close above/below middle EMA; Hull MA rising/falling; close above/below Hull MA; RSI above/below 50; RSI rising/falling; MACD line above/below signal; MACD histogram positive/negative; CCI positive/negative; stochastic K above/below D; stochastic K above/below 50; MFI above/below 50; Williams %R above/below -50; ADX > 15 and +DI/-DI agrees; close above/below Parabolic SAR; OBV rising/falling; VWMA rising/falling; close above/below VWMA; close above/below Bollinger basis; close above/below the midpoint of basis and upper/lower band; close above/below the midpoint of the 20-bar high/low range; close above/below the prior 20-bar high/low; candle body agrees and is at least 0.12 ATR; body is at least 0.12 ATR; lower/upper wick is longer; fractal ribbon agrees; and structure clearance passes.
Calculation: Fractal ribbon bullish/bearish = fast EMA above/below slow EMA of RSI, fast EMA above/below 50, and (close breaks the latest confirmed fractal high/low or close is above/below slow EMA). Use completed execution-timeframe data.
Calculation: Support/resistance zones = on a higher timeframe, the lowest low/highest high over a lookback period, with zone thickness equal to that timeframe's ATR times a parameter; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The support zone extends upward from the low; the resistance zone extends downward from the high.
Calculation: Zone qualification = support/resistance was touched within the allowed recent bars, then either price closes beyond the zone in the favorable direction with a matching candle body, or a breakout is retested within tolerance and closes beyond the zone with a matching body.
Calculation: Structure clearance = bullish when close exceeds the prior structure high or is at least a parameter times ATR below it; bearish when close is below the prior structure low or is at least a parameter times ATR above it. Depth passes when the 10-bar high-low range is at least a parameter times ATR.
Calculation: Body confirmation = bullish/bearish candle body in that direction and body size at least 0.12 ATR.
Calculation: Pattern direction and score = bullish/bearish when any corresponding defined pattern qualifies: structure break (6); doji at support/resistance (5); hammer at support or shooting star at resistance (8); double bottom/top at a recent support/resistance touch (7, or 9 on structure break); bull/bear flag (8, or 9 after post-retest); bullish/bearish engulfing (6, or 8 at a zone, or 9 after post-retest); bull/bear marubozu (7, or 9 after post-retest, or 10 with agreeing MTF vote and fractal ribbon); bear/bull trap reversal (9); or zone breakout post-retest (10). Pattern priority follows the source's sequential assignments, with later qualifying patterns overriding earlier ones. A doji has body/range ≤ 0.15; marubozu has body/range ≥ 0.75 and each wick/range ≤ 0.12; hammer/shooting star has its dominant wick ≥ 2× body, opposite wick ≤ 0.75× body, and close in the corresponding outer 45% of range. Engulfing requires opposite prior candle directions and current real body covering the prior real body.
Calculation: Pattern compatibility = no opposite-direction pattern with score ≥ 7.
Calculation: Buy/sell core = MTF vote passes AND confidence and rank meet their minimums AND depth passes AND directional structure clearance passes AND directional body confirmation passes AND matching zone qualification passes AND pattern is compatible AND (fractal-ribbon gate is off OR ribbon agrees).
Calculation: Early buy/sell = core is false AND at least two matching timeframe directions, including 15-minute and 1-hour, with opposite count ≤ 1 AND confidence meets early minimum AND rank meets minimum AND fractal ribbon and body confirmation agree AND matching zone qualification passes AND pattern is compatible.
Calculation: Bull/bear sentiment score = sum of the source's directional points: matching zone event +2; matching breakout/breakdown event +2; matching post-retest +1; matching pattern meeting label-score threshold +2; matching reversal +1; matching continuation +1; matching confidence exceeds opposite confidence +1; matching timeframe count exceeds opposite count +1; matching base ribbon +1; matching fractal ribbon +1; matching core/early/strong setup +2. Sentiment qualifies when the matching lower-timeframe veto passes, score ≥ 4, and exceeds the opposite score by more than 1.
Calculation: Projected ribbon code = compare bullish and bearish projection scores. Bull score adds: bullish sentiment +5; support-bounce override +6; bullish zone event +2; support-zone exit +1; bullish post-retest +2; bullish breakout event +2; bullish breakout retest +2; bullish pattern +3 if score ≥ 8, otherwise +2; bullish reversal +2; bullish continuation +1; bullish MTF vote +2; bullish count lead +1; bullish confidence lead +1; bullish fractal ribbon +1; bullish base ribbon +1; strong/core/early buy +3/+2/+1. Bear score uses the corresponding bearish terms and subtracts 4 when support-bounce override is active. Score difference ≥ 6 / ≥ 2 / ≤ -6 / ≤ -2 gives code 2 / 1 / -2 / -1; otherwise code 0. A support-bounce override raises any code below 1 to 1.
Long: Newly true core buy OR newly true early buy, with the lower-timeframe scout direction bullish when active, permitted by the one-signal-per-trend strength rule and buy cooldown.
Short: Newly true core sell OR newly true early sell, with the lower-timeframe scout direction bearish when active, permitted by the one-signal-per-trend strength rule and sell cooldown.
Long: Newly true strong-move buy candidate AND (scout is inactive OR lower-timeframe scout direction is bullish) AND projected ribbon code ≥ 1 AND bull confidence > bear confidence. Candidate requires bull confidence/rank minimums, bull confidence > bear confidence, body ≥ parameter × ATR, bullish candle closing in the top 35% of its range, a qualifying bullish pattern/structure break/marubozu/engulfing/flag/post-retest/resistance break, and at least one of bullish base ribbon, bullish fractal ribbon, or bullish MTF vote; also require dot cooldown and no repeated active buy candidate.
Short: Newly true strong-move sell candidate AND (scout is inactive OR lower-timeframe scout direction is bearish) AND projected ribbon code ≤ -1 AND bear confidence > bull confidence. Candidate requires bear confidence/rank minimums, bear confidence > bull confidence, body ≥ parameter × ATR, bearish candle closing in the bottom 35% of its range, a qualifying bearish pattern/structure break/marubozu/engulfing/flag/post-retest/support break, and at least one of bearish base ribbon, bearish fractal ribbon, or bearish MTF vote; also require dot cooldown and no repeated active sell candidate.
