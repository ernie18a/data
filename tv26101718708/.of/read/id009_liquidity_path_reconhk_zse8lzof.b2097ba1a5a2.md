Calculation: Liquidity pivot = Over a rolling chart-bar lookback, divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high into equal price bins. Add each bar's volume to the bin containing its (high + low + close) / 3, weighted by 1 minus its age in bars divided by the lookback length. Smooth each bin by the weighted average of neighboring bin volumes, using weight 1 / (1 + distance in bins). Select the first bin with maximum smoothed volume. Expand a value area from that bin by repeatedly adding the adjacent bin with greater smoothed volume, choosing the upper bin on a tie, until the selected fraction of total smoothed volume is reached. The pivot is the EMA of the selected bin's center, clamped between the value area's lower and upper bin edges. It is undefined without a full lookback, a positive price range, and positive total smoothed volume.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), clamped to the range from zero to one; use the midpoint when high equals low.
Calculation: Smoothed delta = EMA of signed volume, where signed volume is positive volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Structural liquidity inputs = Sample close and volume from a configurable higher or lower timeframe and volume from a second configurable higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar. On chart bars, calculate the moving average and lower percentile of sampled close times sampled volume over the same lookback, and the percentage of sampled volumes equal to zero over a separate lookback.

Short: Close < liquidity pivot.
