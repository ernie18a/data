Calculation: Trend alignment = the average of three values, each equal to +1 when its comparison is true and -1 otherwise: longer higher-timeframe close > its EMA, shorter higher-timeframe MACD line > its signal line, and chart close > its EMA. Use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, minus volume when close < open; add zero when close = open.
Calculation: Signal strength = the weighted sum of trend alignment, momentum, volume confirmation, divergence, and weekend scores. Momentum is positive when RSI < a lower threshold AND RSI > its previous value, negative when RSI > an upper threshold AND RSI < its previous value, and zero otherwise. Volume confirmation is positive when volume > a multiple of its shorter SMA AND volume / its shorter SMA > a threshold AND volume > a multiple of its longer SMA, and zero otherwise. Divergence is positive when close < close at a lookback AND cumulative volume delta > cumulative volume delta at the same lookback, negative when close > close at that lookback AND cumulative volume delta < cumulative volume delta at that lookback, and zero otherwise. The weekend score is positive on Saturday or Sunday and zero otherwise; multiply it by a prime-window factor during a specified Sunday hour range. Multiply the resulting sum by a weekend factor on Saturday or Sunday and by an additional factor during the prime window.
Calculation: Leverage = round to a configured precision the value obtained by clamping minimum leverage + (maximum leverage - minimum leverage) × safety score to the minimum and maximum leverage. Safety score = the average of volatility safety and signal confidence, multiplied by a configured risk factor. Volatility safety takes its high, middle, or low value according to whether ATR / close is below a lower threshold, below an upper threshold, or otherwise. Signal confidence = absolute signal strength divided by its scale.
Calculation: Entry stop distance = ATR at the entry signal × a stop multiplier divided by a leverage adjustment that increases linearly with entry-signal leverage. Entry target distance = ATR at the entry signal × a target multiplier × (1 + entry-signal confidence × a confidence factor).
Long: Signal strength > 0
Short: Signal strength <= 0
Long: Signal strength > entry threshold AND trend alignment > 0 AND position side = flat AND absolute fractional close change over the flash-move lookback <= flash-move threshold AND (weekend-only mode = disabled OR (day = Saturday AND hour >= Saturday start hour) OR (day = Sunday AND hour <= Sunday end hour))
Short: Signal strength < negative entry threshold AND trend alignment < 0 AND position side = flat AND absolute fractional close change over the flash-move lookback <= flash-move threshold AND (weekend-only mode = disabled OR (day = Saturday AND hour >= Saturday start hour) OR (day = Sunday AND hour <= Sunday end hour))
Exit: long, low <= entry-signal close - entry stop distance OR high >= entry-signal close + entry target distance
Exit: short, high >= entry-signal close + entry stop distance OR low <= entry-signal close - entry target distance
Exit: long, trailing enabled AND highest high since entry >= entry price + activation distance AND low <= highest high since trailing activation - trailing offset; activation distance and trailing offset are the entry-signal close multiplied by their respective configured factors and by the configured minimum tick size
Exit: short, trailing enabled AND lowest low since entry <= entry price - activation distance AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation + trailing offset; activation distance and trailing offset are the entry-signal close multiplied by their respective configured factors and by the configured minimum tick size
Exit: long, absolute value of (close - entry price) / entry price > liquidation factor / current leverage OR absolute fractional close change over the flash-move lookback > flash-move threshold OR day = Monday AND hour = weekend-end hour
Exit: short, absolute value of (entry price - close) / entry price > liquidation factor / current leverage OR absolute fractional close change over the flash-move lookback > flash-move threshold OR day = Monday AND hour = weekend-end hour
