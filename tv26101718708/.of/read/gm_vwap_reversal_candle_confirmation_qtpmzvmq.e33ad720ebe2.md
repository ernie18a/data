Calculation: VWAP standard-deviation bands = VWAP plus or minus standard deviation multiplied by configured inner or outer distance; the outer band is farther from VWAP. Use the selected session, week, or month anchor, which is higher than the chart timeframe. Evaluate setup and signal conditions at the chart bar close using that bar's final VWAP and bands.
Calculation: Round-level proximity = distance from the arming candle's high (short) or low (long) to the nearest configured round level is within the configured tolerance.
Long: With valid volume and warmed-up VWAP data, arm when low < lower inner band AND low < min(open, close) AND high < VWAP AND round-level proximity holds; after arming, a later bullish bar (close > open) is C1, then a later bullish bar closing above C1's close is C2, then a subsequent close strictly above C2's close while at or below the lower inner band signals long, provided ADX is not both above its threshold and rising. Each stage must occur on a later bar; intervening bars are allowed. A close below the locked setup low during the C2-to-entry stage, a high reaching VWAP while pending, expiry after the configured wait from C1, or a new VWAP anchor cancels the setup. The first qualifying close above C2 consumes the setup even if the entry-zone or ADX condition fails. A VWAP touch rearms the side; repeated wick setups are allowed only after the configured minimum gap.
Short: With valid volume and warmed-up VWAP data, arm when high > upper inner band AND high > max(open, close) AND low > VWAP AND round-level proximity holds; after arming, a later bearish bar (close < open) is C1, then a later bearish bar closing below C1's close is C2, then a subsequent close strictly below C2's close while at or above the upper inner band signals short, provided ADX is not both above its threshold and rising. Each stage must occur on a later bar; intervening bars are allowed. A close above the locked setup high during the C2-to-entry stage, a low reaching VWAP while pending, expiry after the configured wait from C1, or a new VWAP anchor cancels the setup. The first qualifying close below C2 consumes the setup even if the entry-zone or ADX condition fails. A VWAP touch rearms the side; repeated wick setups are allowed only after the configured minimum gap.
