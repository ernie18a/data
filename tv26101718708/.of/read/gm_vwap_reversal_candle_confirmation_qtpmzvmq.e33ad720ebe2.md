Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted mean and volume-weighted standard deviation of either close or (high + low + close) / 3, accumulated from the start of a configurable higher-timeframe anchor through the current completed chart bar. Inner and outer bands equal VWAP plus or minus their respective deviation multipliers; the inner multiplier is smaller.
Calculation: Setup state = At each completed chart bar, reset the active setup on a new anchor or when volume is not positive, VWAP or deviation is unavailable, deviation is below a minimum price increment, or the anchor has fewer than a minimum number of chart bars. A new anchor also rearms both sides and clears their last setup-start bars. Otherwise, process the following in order: (1) Rearm short when low <= VWAP in wick mode or close <= VWAP otherwise; rearm long when high >= VWAP in wick mode or close >= VWAP otherwise. (2) Cancel an active setup if its side's rearm condition holds, if the number of bars since C1 exceeds the maximum wait, or, after C2, if close exceeds the locked setup extreme for short or falls below it for long. (3) If idle, start a short setup when its arming condition and permission hold, otherwise start a long setup when its arming condition and permission hold; short has priority. In close mode, short arms when close > upper outer band and long arms when close < lower outer band. In wick mode, short arms when high > upper inner band AND high > max(open, close) AND low > VWAP; long arms when low < lower inner band AND low < min(open, close) AND high < VWAP. Optional wick filters require the relevant wick length to be at least both a configurable multiple of the absolute candle body and a configurable fraction of the candle range, with positive range and wick length; an optional round-level filter requires the wick tip to be within a configurable tolerance of the nearest multiple of a configurable grid spacing. For an automatically scaled grid, derive spacing from the previous completed bar's price and configurable market and tick-size parameters; do not arm on a bar where the grid spacing changes. In wick mode with repeats enabled, permission requires the distance from the previous same-side setup start to be at least a configurable number of bars; otherwise it requires that side to be rearmed. Starting a setup disarms that side and records its start bar and close as the setup extreme. (4) Advance at most one stage per bar: while waiting for C1, update the extreme to the highest close for short or lowest close for long, and select the first later bearish candle for short or bullish candle for long as C1. While waiting for C2, update that extreme; if close exceeds the previous extreme for short or falls below it for long, discard C1 and return to waiting for C1. Otherwise, select the first later bearish candle closing below C1 close for short or bullish candle closing above C1 close for long as C2, and lock its close and the extreme. (5) After C2, the first later close below C2 close for short or above C2 close for long consumes the setup, whether or not its entry filters pass. A cancelled setup may start again on the same bar if its arming and permission conditions hold.
Long: A long setup reaches its first close > C2 close AND close <= lower inner band AND the optional ADX filter is disabled OR a long setup reaches its first close > C2 close AND close <= lower inner band AND current and previous ADX are available AND (ADX <= its threshold OR ADX <= previous ADX).
Short: A short setup reaches its first close < C2 close AND close >= upper inner band AND the optional ADX filter is disabled OR a short setup reaches its first close < C2 close AND close >= upper inner band AND current and previous ADX are available AND (ADX <= its threshold OR ADX <= previous ADX).
