Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume since the start of the day ÷ cumulative volume since the start of the day
Calculation: Daily range = highest high since the start of the day − lowest low since the start of the day
Calculation: Fibonacci channel levels = Daily VWAP + (Daily range × level offset): 1.000 = +0.5; 0.786 = +0.286; 0.618 = +0.118; 0.500 = 0; 0.382 = −0.118; 0.236 = −0.286; 0.000 = −0.5
Long: close > 0.618 AND volume > previous bar volume AND time is 09:00–10:00 or 14:00–15:20
Long: previous close > 0.618 AND low ≤ 0.500 AND close ≥ 0.500 AND (low < min(open, close) OR close > open) AND time is 09:00–10:00 or 14:00–15:20
Long: low < 0.000 AND close ≥ 0.000 AND close ≤ 0.236 AND close > open AND time is 09:00–10:00 or 14:00–15:20
Exit: Long, high ≥ 0.786 OR high ≥ 1.000 OR (close < 0.500 AND volume < previous bar volume)
Exit: Long, high ≥ 0.618 OR high ≥ 0.786 OR (close ≤ 0.382 AND close < open)
Exit: Long, high ≥ 0.382 OR high ≥ 0.500 OR low < 0.000
