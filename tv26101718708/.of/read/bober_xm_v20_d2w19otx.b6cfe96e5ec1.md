Calculation: Price source = a selected chart-bar OHLC price or OHLC average, or the corresponding higher-timeframe price. For higher-timeframe sources assigned before the data cache updates, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar available on the preceding chart bar; for the RSI source, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar available on the current chart bar. Forward-fill higher-timeframe values and perform subsequent indicator calculations on chart bars. Higher-timeframe selection applies independently to each price source.
Calculation: Directional channel = separate long and short parameterizations of either a Keltner channel or a predictive channel. The Keltner basis is EMA of its selected price source; its upper and lower bands are the basis plus or minus a multiplier times EMA of chart-bar true range.
Calculation: Predictive channel = for window N, forecast F and noise s, let K(i,j) = exp(-(i-j)^2/(2*N^2))*(1+0.5*exp(-abs(i-j)/(N*0.5))), with training indices i,j = 0 through N-1. Let k(i) use the same formula with j = N+F-1, and w = k transposed times the pseudoinverse of K+s^2*I. Let m be the selected moving average of the source over N chart bars. The basis is m + sum over i of w(i)*(source[N-1-i]-m)*(1+0.5*(N-1-i)/N) + 0.2*(source[1]-basis[1]), with the correction zero when the previous basis is unavailable. Calculate it after more than N chart bars. Band width is the selected moving average over N bars of abs(source-basis), multiplied by a band multiplier and by clamp(ATR divided by its longer-period selected moving average, 0.5, 1.5). Upper and lower bands equal basis plus and minus that width.
Calculation: Predictive trend = initialize to 0; set to 1 when its selected trend source is above the upper predictive band AND the basis is rising; set to 0 when that source is below the lower band AND the basis is falling; otherwise retain its previous value. Long and short channels have independent parameters and trend sources.
Calculation: Pivot trend = find confirmed pivot highs and lows of the selected source using equal left and right lookbacks, preferring a high when both occur. Initialize the center to the first pivot and update it to (2*previous center+pivot)/3 on each later pivot. Set raw lower and upper levels to center minus and plus an ATR multiplier times chart-bar ATR. The trailing lower level is max(raw lower, previous trailing lower) when previous close exceeds the previous trailing lower, otherwise raw lower; the trailing upper level is min(raw upper, previous trailing upper) when previous close is below the previous trailing upper, otherwise raw upper. Set trend to 1 when close exceeds the previous trailing upper, to -1 when close is below the previous trailing lower, and otherwise retain the previous trend, initially 1.
Calculation: Kalman band state = initialize the estimate to the first selected source and covariance to 1. On each bar set predicted covariance to previous covariance plus process noise, gain to predicted covariance divided by predicted covariance plus measurement noise, estimate to previous estimate plus gain times the difference between source and previous estimate, and covariance to (1-gain)*predicted covariance. Apply EMA to the estimate; upper and lower bands are that EMA plus and minus independently multiplied chart-bar ATR. Initialize state to 0, set it to 1 when source exceeds the upper band, set it to -1 when source is below the lower band, and otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Entry filters = optionally require chart-bar ADX above its threshold and optionally the direction's DI above the opposite DI; optionally require RSI of its selected source below the overbought threshold for long or above the oversold threshold for short; optionally require volatility divided by its moving average below a threshold, where volatility is chart-bar ATR or standard deviation of its selected source and the average uses the same lookback; optionally require close below the upper Bollinger band for long or above the lower band for short, using the selected source and moving-average type; optionally require a moving average of chart-bar volume divided by another independently parameterized moving average of chart-bar volume above a threshold; optionally require Kalman band state 1 for long or -1 for short. All enabled filters must pass.
Long: Selected long entry source crosses above the long channel upper band, basis, or lower band for the selected breakout, pullback, or mean-reversion mode respectively AND pivot trend = 1 AND, if the predictive channel is selected, its long predictive trend = 1 AND, if enabled, the entry source > WMA of its selected source AND all enabled long entry filters pass AND position side is flat or short AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval.
Short: Selected short entry source crosses below the short channel lower band, basis, or upper band for the selected breakout, pullback, or mean-reversion mode respectively AND pivot trend = -1 AND, if the predictive channel is selected, its short predictive trend = 0 AND, if enabled, the entry source < WMA of its selected source AND all enabled short entry filters pass AND position side is flat or long AND datetime is within the selected backtest interval.
Long: Long predictive trend changes from 0 to 1 OR from 1 to 0.
Short: Short predictive trend changes from 0 to 1 OR from 1 to 0.
Long: Kalman band state = 1.
Short: Kalman band state = -1.
Long: Pivot trend = 1 AND previous pivot trend = 1.
Short: Pivot trend = -1 AND previous pivot trend = -1.
Exit: long, OBV of the selected price source using chart-bar volume crosses below its selected moving average AND, if exit confirmation is enabled, chart-bar +DI < -DI, OR pivot trend changes from 1 to -1; datetime is within the selected backtest interval.
Exit: short, OBV of the selected price source using chart-bar volume crosses above its selected moving average AND, if exit confirmation is enabled, chart-bar +DI > -DI, OR pivot trend changes from -1 to 1; datetime is within the selected backtest interval.
Exit: long, price reaches entry price minus its stop-loss percentage OR reaches entry price plus that stop distance multiplied by the risk-reward ratio OR reaches an active trailing stop. The trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price plus the activation percentage of the current close; its offset is max(0, close minus the long channel lower band), updated on each chart bar, and its stop follows the highest price reached after activation by that offset. Orders are updated within the selected backtest interval.
Exit: short, price reaches entry price plus its stop-loss percentage OR reaches entry price minus that stop distance multiplied by the risk-reward ratio OR reaches an active trailing stop. The trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price minus the activation percentage of the current close; its offset is max(0, the short channel upper band minus close), updated on each chart bar, and its stop follows the lowest price reached after activation by that offset. Orders are updated within the selected backtest interval.
