Calculation: Long/short MLMA channels = kernel regression forecast using an RBF kernel over a rolling source window, with recency-weighted residuals around a selected moving average and correction by 0.2 × the prior bar's source-minus-forecast error; upper/lower bands are forecast ± selected moving average of absolute forecast error × band multiplier × volatility weight, where volatility weight is the ATR-to-50-bar moving-average-of-ATR ratio clamped to 0.5–1.5. Long and short channels have independent parameters.
Calculation: Pivot trend = direction from a pivot-centered SuperTrend: update center to (2 × prior center + latest confirmed pivot high or low) / 3; form upper/lower bands at center ± ATR × factor, trail the bands, and set trend bullish when close crosses above the prior lower band or bearish when close crosses below the prior upper band; otherwise retain prior trend.
Calculation: KEMAD bands = EMA of a configurable-order Kalman-filtered selected price ± separate upper/lower multipliers × ATR; each Kalman state is updated from the price using gain = (prior covariance + process noise) / (prior covariance + process noise + measurement noise), then covariance is multiplied by (1 − gain).
Long: (selected long entry price crosses above the long channel upper band [breakout] OR crosses above its basis [pullback] OR crosses above its lower band [mean reversion]) AND pivot trend is bullish AND (MLMA channel selected implies long MLMA trend is bullish) AND (WMA confirmation enabled implies entry price > WMA) AND (RSI filter enabled implies RSI < overbought threshold) AND (volatility filter enabled implies volatility / its moving average < threshold) AND (Bollinger filter enabled implies close < upper band) AND (volume filter enabled implies smoothed volume / average volume > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter enabled implies price > KEMAD upper band) AND (ADX momentum filter enabled implies ADX > threshold and, when DI check enabled, +DI > −DI).
Short: (selected short entry price crosses below the short channel lower band [breakout] OR crosses below its basis [pullback] OR crosses below its upper band [mean reversion]) AND pivot trend is bearish AND (MLMA channel selected implies short MLMA trend is bearish) AND (WMA confirmation enabled implies entry price < WMA) AND (RSI filter enabled implies RSI > oversold threshold) AND (volatility filter enabled implies volatility / its moving average < threshold) AND (Bollinger filter enabled implies close > lower band) AND (volume filter enabled implies smoothed volume / average volume > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter enabled implies price < KEMAD lower band) AND (ADX momentum filter enabled implies ADX > threshold and, when DI check enabled, −DI > +DI).
Long: Long MLMA trend changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Short MLMA trend changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, OBV crosses below its moving average and (ADX exit confirmation enabled implies +DI < −DI), OR pivot trend changes from bullish to bearish, OR price reaches the stop-loss, take-profit, or activated trailing-stop level.
Exit: short, OBV crosses above its moving average and (ADX exit confirmation enabled implies +DI > −DI), OR pivot trend changes from bearish to bullish, OR price reaches the stop-loss, take-profit, or activated trailing-stop level.
