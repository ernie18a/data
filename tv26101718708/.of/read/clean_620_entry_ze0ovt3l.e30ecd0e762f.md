Calculation: MACD line = short-term EMA of close minus long-term EMA of close
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of MACD line
Long: short-term EMA of close crosses above long-term EMA of close AND MACD line > MACD signal line
Short: short-term EMA of close crosses below long-term EMA of close AND MACD line < MACD signal line
