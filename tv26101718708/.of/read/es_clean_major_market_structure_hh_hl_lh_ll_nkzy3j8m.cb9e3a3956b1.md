Calculation: SwingHigh = a bar whose high is the highest among N bars before and N bars after it; it is confirmed only on the Nth bar after it, and its value is that bar's high. SwingLow = the mirror case, using the lowest low.
Calculation: LatestHigh, PrevHigh = the most recent and second most recent confirmed SwingHigh values, held constant until the next SwingHigh is confirmed. LatestLow, PrevLow = the same for SwingLow.
Calculation: MajorHH = LatestHigh > PrevHigh AND LatestHigh - PrevHigh >= M. MajorLH = LatestHigh < PrevHigh AND PrevHigh - LatestHigh >= M. MajorHL = LatestLow > PrevLow AND LatestLow - PrevLow >= M. MajorLL = LatestLow < PrevLow AND PrevLow - LatestLow >= M. RawHH, RawLH, RawHL, RawLL = the same comparisons without the >= M requirement. Each requires both previous values to exist. M is a minimum swing size threshold.
Calculation: Bias = persistent state starting at neutral and never returning to neutral. It is re-evaluated on every bar, before the Long/Short checks, by applying these steps in order (later steps override earlier ones on the same bar): (1) MajorHH AND RawHL -> bullish; (2) MajorHL AND RawHH -> bullish; (3) MajorLL AND RawLH -> bearish; (4) MajorLH AND RawLL -> bearish; (5) if Bias is still neutral after steps 1-4: MajorHH -> bullish, then MajorLL -> bearish.
Long: (a SwingHigh is confirmed on this bar AND MajorHH AND Bias is bullish or neutral) OR (a SwingLow is confirmed on this bar AND MajorHL AND Bias is bullish)
Short: (a SwingHigh is confirmed on this bar AND MajorLH AND Bias is bearish) OR (a SwingLow is confirmed on this bar AND MajorLL AND Bias is bearish or neutral)
