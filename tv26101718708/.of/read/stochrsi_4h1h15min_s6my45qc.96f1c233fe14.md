Calculation: StochD = SMA(SMA(100*(close - lowest low over L)/(highest high over L - lowest low over L), k), d), with k and d as smoothing lengths
Calculation: StochA = StochD on the chart timeframe. StochB = StochD on a higher timeframe than the chart, taking the value of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar as of the current chart bar close. Shifts [n] count chart bars back.
Calculation: RSIState = Wilder RSI(close) computed on a lower timeframe than the chart, sampled at the previous chart bar close, then smoothed with a short SMA (call it RSIs). State is +1 after RSIs crosses above a low level, and -1 after RSIs crosses below a high level (low level < high level). It holds until the opposite cross. Initial state is -1.
Calculation: MACDhist = (EMA(close, fast) - EMA(close, slow)) - EMA(EMA(close, fast) - EMA(close, slow), signal), with fast length < slow length, on the chart timeframe
Calculation: RVSIhist = EMA(RVSI, fast) - EMA(RVSI, slow), with fast length < slow length, on the chart timeframe. RVSI = RSI(EMA(cumulative sum of (close - previous close)*volume, short length), RSI length).
Long: no open position AND StochA < long entry level AND StochA > StochA[1] AND StochB[shorter lookback] >= StochB[longer lookback] AND RSIState == +1 AND MACDhist > 0 AND RVSIhist > 0
Short: no open position AND StochA > short entry level AND StochA < StochA[1] AND StochB[shorter lookback] <= StochB[longer lookback] AND RSIState == -1 AND MACDhist < 0 AND RVSIhist < 0
Exit: long, (StochA[1] >= long exit level AND StochA < long exit level; long exit level > long entry level) OR close > entry price*(1 + take-profit fraction) OR close < entry price*(1 - initial stop fraction)*(1 + ramp fraction)^(bars since entry) OR low <= (highest close since entry through the previous bar)*(1 - trail fraction)
Exit: short, (StochA[1] <= short exit level AND StochA > short exit level; short exit level < short entry level) OR close < entry price*(1 - take-profit fraction) OR close > entry price*(1 + initial stop fraction)*(1 - ramp fraction)^(bars since entry) OR high >= (lowest close since entry through the previous bar)*(1 + trail fraction)
