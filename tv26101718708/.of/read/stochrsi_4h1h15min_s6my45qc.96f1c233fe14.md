Calculation: Smoothed stochastic %D = SMA(SMA(100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback), short smoothing length), longer smoothing length)
Calculation: Long RSI state = bullish after the RSI SMA crosses above its lower threshold; bearish after it crosses below its upper threshold; otherwise retain the previous state. RSI is calculated on a lower timeframe using the last completed bar.
Calculation: MACD histogram = EMA(close, shorter length) − EMA(close, longer length) − EMA(EMA(close, shorter length) − EMA(close, longer length), signal length)
Calculation: Volume RSI histogram = EMA(RSI(EMA(cumulative sum of change(close) × volume, smoothing length), RSI length), shorter length) − EMA(RSI(EMA(cumulative sum of change(close) × volume, smoothing length), RSI length), longer length)
Long: Flat position AND chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D < long-entry threshold AND chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D > its prior value AND higher-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D four bars ago ≥ its value eight bars ago AND long RSI state is bullish AND MACD histogram > 0 AND volume RSI histogram > 0
Short: Flat position AND chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D > short-entry threshold AND chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D < its prior value AND higher-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D four bars ago ≤ its value eight bars ago AND long RSI state is bearish AND MACD histogram < 0 AND volume RSI histogram < 0
Exit: Long, chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D crosses below long-exit threshold OR close > entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR low ≤ trailing stop (highest close since entry × (1 − trailing-stop fraction)) OR close < rising stop (entry price × (1 − initial stop fraction), increased by a per-bar fraction each bar)
Exit: Short, chart-timeframe smoothed stochastic %D crosses above short-exit threshold OR close < entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR high ≥ trailing stop (lowest close since entry × (1 + trailing-stop fraction)) OR close > falling stop (entry price × (1 + initial stop fraction), decreased by a per-bar fraction each bar)
