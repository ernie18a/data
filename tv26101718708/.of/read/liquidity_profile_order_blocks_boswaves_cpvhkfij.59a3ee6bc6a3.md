Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmation waits for the right-side bars to complete
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmation waits for the right-side bars to complete
Calculation: Bullish order block = confirmed swing low and the close at detection is at least the configured ATR multiple above the pivot low; its zone spans the formation candles ending at the pivot, from the lowest low to the highest open or close, with height clamped to configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples measured at the pivot
Calculation: Bearish order block = confirmed swing high and the close at detection is at least the configured ATR multiple below the pivot high; its zone spans the formation candles ending at the pivot, from the lowest open or close to the highest high, with height clamped to configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples measured at the pivot
Long: a valid bullish order block exists AND current high >= zone bottom AND current low <= zone top AND the zone has been touched and has not yet confirmed AND current close > zone midpoint AND (previous close <= zone midpoint OR current low <= zone midpoint)
Short: a valid bearish order block exists AND current high >= zone bottom AND current low <= zone top AND the zone has been touched and has not yet confirmed AND current close < zone midpoint AND (previous close >= zone midpoint OR current high >= zone midpoint)
