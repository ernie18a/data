Calculation: LongTrail = stop = highest high over N bars - M * ATR(N), where N equals the ATR length and M is a multiplier. On the first bar LongTrail = stop. On later bars, if close > previous-bar LongTrail then LongTrail = max(previous-bar LongTrail, stop), otherwise LongTrail = stop.
Calculation: ShortTrail = stop = lowest low over N bars + M * ATR(N), where N equals the ATR length and M is a multiplier. On the first bar ShortTrail = stop. On later bars, if close < previous-bar ShortTrail then ShortTrail = min(previous-bar ShortTrail, stop), otherwise ShortTrail = stop.
Calculation: LongFlags = number of the following that are true on the current bar: (1) close < EMA(close); (2) low < lowest low of the previous S bars, excluding the current bar; (3) RSI < L AND RSI < previous-bar RSI, where L is the RSI midline level; (4) close < open AND volume > K * SMA(volume), where K > 1 and the SMA includes the current bar; (5) close < LongTrail.
Calculation: ShortFlags = number of the following that are true on the current bar: (1) close > EMA(close), same EMA length as in LongFlags; (2) high > highest high of the previous S bars, excluding the current bar; (3) RSI > (100 - L) AND RSI > previous-bar RSI; (4) close > open AND volume > K * SMA(volume); (5) close > ShortTrail.
Exit: long position, LongFlags >= T (T is a flag-count threshold between 1 and 5) OR close < LongTrail
Exit: short position, ShortFlags >= T OR close > ShortTrail
