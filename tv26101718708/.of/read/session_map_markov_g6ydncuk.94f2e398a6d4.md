Calculation: Session range high and low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the specified range duration from the first regular-session bar's opening time; initialize both from that first bar. Calculate opening range and initial balance separately.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP and standard deviation = weighted mean and population standard deviation of chart-bar typical price (high + low + close) / 3 since the selected regular-session open, overnight-session open, or daily open; use volume as each bar's weight when positive, otherwise use a weight of one. Include the current bar.
Long: The bar is in the regular session AND the opening-range duration has ended AND close > opening-range high AND no previous confirmed bar in this regular session has closed above the completed opening-range high.
Short: The bar is in the regular session AND the opening-range duration has ended AND close < opening-range low AND no previous confirmed bar in this regular session has closed below the completed opening-range low.
Long: The bar is in the regular session AND the initial-balance duration has ended AND close > initial-balance high AND no previous confirmed bar in this regular session has closed above the completed initial-balance high.
Short: The bar is in the regular session AND the initial-balance duration has ended AND close < initial-balance low AND no previous confirmed bar in this regular session has closed below the completed initial-balance low.
Long: Anchored VWAP standard deviation > 0 AND low <= anchored VWAP minus the standard-deviation band multiplier times anchored VWAP standard deviation.
Short: Anchored VWAP standard deviation > 0 AND high >= anchored VWAP plus the standard-deviation band multiplier times anchored VWAP standard deviation.
