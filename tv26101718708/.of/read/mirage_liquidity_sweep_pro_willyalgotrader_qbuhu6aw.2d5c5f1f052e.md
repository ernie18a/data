Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is strictly higher/lower than the highs/lows of the same lookback on both sides; use only after the bars on both sides have completed
Calculation: Bullish sweep score = 100 × (0.30 × min(max((min(open, close) − low) / ATR, 0), 1) + 0.25 × min(max((close − swept low) / ATR, 0), 1) + 0.20 × (close − low) / (high − low) + 0.15 × clamp((volume / SMA(volume) − 1) / (volume spike multiple − 1), 0, 1) + 0.10 × higher-timeframe bias factor); bearish sweep score uses (high − max(open, close)) / ATR, (swept high − close) / ATR, and 1 − (close − low) / (high − low) in place of the corresponding bullish terms. The volume factor is 0.5 if volume is unavailable or its filter is disabled. The bias factor is 1 when the sweep agrees with the higher-timeframe trend, otherwise 0; when its filter is disabled it is 0.5. The trend compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of (sweep-bar low − ATR × stop buffer) and (entry price − ATR × minimum stop distance)
Calculation: Short stop = the higher of (sweep-bar high + ATR × stop buffer) and (entry price + ATR × minimum stop distance)
Calculation: Long TP1 and TP3 = entry price + risk distance × respective target multiple; short TP1 and TP3 = entry price − risk distance × respective target multiple, where risk distance is the absolute difference between entry price and initial stop
Long: On a confirmed chart bar after warm-up, an unused confirmed swing low within the maximum sweep age has low < swing low and close > swing low, and bullish sweep score ≥ minimum score
Short: On a confirmed chart bar after warm-up, an unused confirmed swing high within the maximum sweep age has high > swing high and close < swing high, and bearish sweep score ≥ minimum score
Long: Bullish sweep score ≥ minimum score, and either confirmation is disabled or, within the confirmation window after the sweep, close > the latest confirmed minor swing high; open only while flat, with no bearish signal on the same bar and ATR > 0
Short: Bearish sweep score ≥ minimum score, and either confirmation is disabled or, within the confirmation window after the sweep, close < the latest confirmed minor swing low; open only while flat, with no bullish signal on the same bar and ATR > 0
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop or high ≥ TP3
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop or low ≤ TP3
