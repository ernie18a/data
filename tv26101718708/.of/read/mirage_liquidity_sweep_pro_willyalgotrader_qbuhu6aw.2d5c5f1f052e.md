Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right swing lookbacks; retain the most recent 25 pivots per side, including used pivots. On each bar, inspect unused levels no older than the maximum sweep age, newest first. Mark a low level used if close < level OR low < level AND close > level; mark a high level used if close > level OR high > level AND close < level. Mark every matching level used, but select only the newest swept level per side. A bullish sweep has low < selected low level AND close > selected low level; a bearish sweep has high > selected high level AND close < selected high level.
Calculation: Sweep score = 100 × (0.30 × W + 0.25 × R + 0.20 × C + 0.15 × V + 0.10 × H). For a bullish sweep, W = clamp((min(open, close) − low)/ATR, 0, 1), R = clamp((close − swept level)/ATR, 0, 1), and C = (close − low)/(high − low). For a bearish sweep, W = clamp((high − max(open, close))/ATR, 0, 1), R = clamp((swept level − close)/ATR, 0, 1), and C = (high − close)/(high − low). V = clamp((volume/SMA(volume) − 1)/(volume spike multiplier − 1), 0, 1); use 0.5 when volume scoring is disabled or the ratio is unavailable. H = 0.5 when higher-timeframe scoring is disabled; otherwise H = 1 when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for a bullish sweep or below its EMA for a bearish sweep, and 0 otherwise. Score = 0 if high − low ≤ 0 or ATR ≤ 0.
Calculation: Qualified sweep = a directional sweep with score ≥ minimum score and zero-based bar index ≥ max(2 × swing lookback, 50), evaluated at bar close.
Calculation: Confirmed signal = when confirmation is disabled, the corresponding qualified sweep; otherwise maintain one pending qualified sweep, expiring when its age exceeds the confirmation window. A new qualified sweep replaces the pending sweep, with a bearish sweep taking precedence if both qualify on the same bar. On the same bar or a later bar within the window, a pending bullish sweep signals when close > the latest confirmed minor pivot high; a pending bearish sweep signals when close < the latest confirmed minor pivot low. Minor pivots use equal left and right lookbacks shorter than the swing lookback. Clear the pending sweep when it signals, including while a position is open.
Calculation: Trade levels = on an entry signal while flat, set entry to close and use the signal's originating sweep low for a long or sweep high for a short. At entry, set the raw stop to sweep low − ATR × stop buffer for a long or sweep high + ATR × stop buffer for a short. If the absolute distance from entry to raw stop is less than 0.5 × entry-bar ATR, set the stop to entry − 0.5 × ATR for a long or entry + 0.5 × ATR for a short; otherwise retain the raw stop. Set risk to the absolute entry-to-stop distance, the first target to entry ± risk × first target multiplier, and the final target to entry ± risk × final target multiplier, adding for long and subtracting for short. After the entry bar, if the first target is touched and the stop is not touched on that bar, move the stop to entry from the next bar when break-even is enabled.
Long: Qualified bullish sweep.
Short: Qualified bearish sweep.
Long: Confirmed bullish signal AND no simultaneous confirmed bearish signal AND ATR > 0 AND position is flat.
Short: Confirmed bearish signal AND no simultaneous confirmed bullish signal AND ATR > 0 AND position is flat.
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND (low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ final target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND (high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ final target).
