Calculation: Price SMA = SMA of close over the stage moving-average lookback. Its percentage slope is its percentage change over the slope lookback; rising means slope > positive slope threshold, falling means slope < negative slope threshold, and flat means neither. Curling up or down means flat and slope respectively greater or less than its value one slope lookback ago.
Calculation: Pivot structure = the two latest confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows, each confirmed after an equal number of bars on both sides using the pivot lookback. An uptrend requires a higher latest pivot high AND a higher latest pivot low; a downtrend requires a lower latest pivot high AND a lower latest pivot low.
Calculation: Volume averages = SMAs over the same volume lookback of volume, volume on bars where close >= open with zero on other bars, and volume on bars where close < open with zero on other bars. Accumulation means the up-volume average > the down-volume average times a multiplier; distribution means the down-volume average > the up-volume average times the same multiplier.
Calculation: Range expansion = ATR > its SMA over the pivot lookback times an expansion multiplier. Churning = volume > its SMA AND absolute candle body as a percentage of open < an assigned fraction of the SMA of (high - low) / low over the pivot lookback.
Calculation: Stage scores = sums of assigned positive weights for true conditions. Stage 1 conditions: flat SMA, neither pivot uptrend nor downtrend, close within a symmetric percentage band around the SMA, accumulation, no range expansion, and curling up. Stage 2 conditions: rising SMA, close > SMA, pivot uptrend, and no distribution. Stage 3 conditions: flat SMA, distribution, latest pivot high < previous pivot high, range expansion, churning, and curling down. Stage 4 conditions: falling SMA, close < SMA, pivot downtrend, and distribution. Relative-strength and market-breadth options are disabled because their external index data are unavailable.
Calculation: Stage = the highest-scoring stage, breaking ties in ascending stage order, only after the same raw stage wins for a consecutive confirmation lookback; otherwise retain the previous stage. Initialize the stage to 1.
Calculation: Stage 2 breakout price = close when the stage changes from 1 to 2; retain it while stage 2 continues and clear it outside stage 2. Stage 2 highest high = highest high since that breakout, updated through the current bar.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = sum of assigned positive weights for each true condition: close's percentage distance above the price SMA >= a mild threshold, >= a higher strong threshold, and >= a still higher extreme threshold; close's percentage gain over a short lookback >= its threshold and over a longer lookback >= a higher threshold; volume > its SMA times a climax multiplier; high > low AND upper wick / (high - low) >= a wick threshold AND close < (high + low) / 2; RSI > an overbought threshold; the previous bar's high >= the highest high over a prior lookback ending two bars ago AND close < previous low AND close < open; close crosses below a shorter-term close SMA; and close crosses below the stage 2 highest high minus ATR times a trailing multiplier. Each overextension tier contributes independently. The coded pivot RSI-divergence condition never contributes because its reference pivot is overwritten before comparison.
Calculation: Exhaustion window score = maximum exhaustion score over a rolling lookback including the current bar.
Long: stage = 2
Short: stage = 4
Exit: long, stage = 3 OR stage changes from 3 to 4
Exit: long, stage = 2 AND percentage gain from the stage 2 breakout price >= the minimum gain threshold AND exhaustion window score >= the caution threshold
Exit: short, stage changes from 4 to 1
