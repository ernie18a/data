Calculation: Long-term moving average = simple moving average of close over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Moving-average slope = percentage change in the long-term moving average over a configurable slope lookback; rising if above a positive threshold, falling if below its negative, otherwise flat.
Calculation: Swing highs/lows = confirmed pivot highs/lows using equal configurable left and right lookbacks; higher/lower highs and lows compare the latest two confirmed pivots. Uptrend structure = higher highs AND higher lows; downtrend structure = lower highs AND lower lows.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution = average volume on up-close bars versus average volume on down-close bars over the same configurable lookback; accumulation when up-volume average exceeds down-volume average by a configurable ratio, distribution when down-volume average exceeds up-volume average by that ratio.
Calculation: Range expansion = ATR over a fixed short lookback exceeds its moving average over the pivot/churn lookback by a configurable ratio. Churning = volume exceeds its moving average and the absolute open-to-close percentage move is below a fraction of the average percentage high-low range over the pivot/churn lookback.
Calculation: Stage scores = Stage 1 adds points for flat moving-average slope, neither uptrend nor downtrend structure, close within a configurable percentage band around the moving average, accumulation, no range expansion, and a flat slope whose rate of change is positive. Stage 2 adds points for rising slope, close above moving average, uptrend structure, positive relative strength when its filter is enabled, and no distribution. Stage 3 adds points for flat slope, distribution, lower highs, range expansion, churning, and a flat slope whose rate of change is negative. Stage 4 adds points for falling slope, close below moving average, downtrend structure, negative relative strength when its filter is enabled, and distribution. Relative strength is the percentage change over a configurable lookback in the ratio of instrument close to benchmark close; both use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Raw stage = stage with the highest score, resolving ties in order Stage 1, then Stage 2, then Stage 3, then Stage 4.
Calculation: Stage = raw stage only when every raw stage in the configurable confirmation window, including the current bar, equals the current raw stage; otherwise retain the prior stage.
Calculation: Stage transitions = current stage differs from prior stage; Stage 1-to-2, 2-to-3, 3-to-4, and 4-to-1 transitions require those respective prior and current stages.
Calculation: Breakout volume confirmation = volume exceeds its moving average over a configurable volume lookback multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Profit gain gate = while in Stage 2, close is at least a configurable percentage above the close at the Stage 1-to-2 transition; track the highest high since that transition.
Calculation: Profit exhaustion signals = (close is a configurable percentage above the long-term moving average, with stronger tiers at configurable multiples of that threshold); OR (gain over a shorter configurable lookback exceeds a configurable threshold); OR (gain over a longer lookback exceeds a configurable threshold, with the longer lookback greater than the shorter); OR (volume exceeds its moving average by a configurable climax factor); OR (upper wick is at least a configurable fraction of the bar range AND close is below the bar midpoint); OR (RSI over a configurable lookback exceeds a configurable overbought threshold); OR (a new high over a configurable lookback was followed by close below the prior bar's low and below open); OR (close crosses below a short-term moving average whose lookback is shorter than the long-term moving-average lookback); OR (close crosses below highest high since Stage 2 entry minus ATR over a configurable lookback multiplied by a configurable factor).
Calculation: Profit score = weighted sum of the profit exhaustion signals: mild and strong moving-average extensions each add one point, extreme extension adds two, each parabolic gain signal adds two, volume climax adds two, exhaustion candle adds one, overbought RSI adds one, failed swing adds one, short-term moving-average cross adds one, and trailing-stop cross adds two. Use the highest score from the current bar and preceding two bars.
Calculation: Market breadth score = add two for index Stage 4 or index Stage 3 with RSI declining across two successive two-bar comparisons; add two when index extension above its 30-bar SMA meets a configurable threshold, index RSI meets a configurable warning level, and RSI is declining; add one when index RSI meets the warning level OR index ATR is expanding OR index moving-average slope is flat; add one when index moving-average slope is falling. Index calculations use the chart timeframe. Breadth is active only when enabled.
Calculation: Profit strong condition = profit gain gate AND (profit score meets the configurable strong threshold, lowered when enabled breadth score meets a configurable weakness threshold; OR extreme extension; OR either parabolic gain signal AND volume climax; OR trailing-stop cross; OR enabled breadth is dangerous/climactic and profit score is at least one).
Calculation: Profit caution condition = profit gain gate AND NOT profit strong condition AND profit score meets the configurable caution threshold, lowered when enabled breadth score meets the weakness threshold; signal only if the configurable cooldown since the prior strong or caution signal has elapsed.
