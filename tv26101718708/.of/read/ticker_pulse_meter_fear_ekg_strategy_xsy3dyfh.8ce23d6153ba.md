Calculation: Short-term range position = (close − lowest low over the shorter lookback) / (highest high over the shorter lookback − lowest low over the shorter lookback)
Calculation: Long-term range position = (close − lowest low over the longer lookback) / (highest high over the longer lookback − lowest low over the longer lookback)
Calculation: Ticker Pulse = 100 × short-term range position × long-term range position
Calculation: Fear EKG = 100 × (1 − short-term range position) × (1 − long-term range position)
Long: Ticker Pulse crosses above its threshold on the prior chart bar AND Fear EKG is below its threshold on the prior chart bar OR orange-entry signals are enabled AND Fear EKG crosses below its threshold on the prior chart bar
Exit: Long, exit signals are enabled AND Ticker Pulse crosses below its threshold on the prior chart bar
