Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low, confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Sweep penetration = (bar high − confirmed swing high) / ATR for an upside sweep; (confirmed swing low − bar low) / ATR for a downside sweep.
Calculation: Wick ratio = upper wick / (high − low) for an upside sweep; lower wick / (high − low) for a downside sweep.
Calculation: Follow-through target = sweep-bar close − ATR × configured move for an upside sweep; sweep-bar close + ATR × configured move for a downside sweep.
Long: A downside sweep occurs when low is below the latest confirmed swing low by at least the configured minimum ATR penetration, penetration is no greater than the configured maximum, and close is above the swing low; also require either the configured wick-reclaim mode with wick ratio at least its minimum, or close-reclaim mode. Require low to be a local low within the configured lookback. Apply configured ATR/volume activity, ADX, DMI chop, EMA regime, Ichimoku context, spike, and mitigation filters; reject a sweep within the configured cooldown, at a previously used nearby level, or during the configured post-spike or post-trap blocking period. OR a bullish trap confirms: after a qualifying downside sweep, either immediately or within the configured confirmation window, close reaches the follow-through target; require the configured minimum age when follow-through is enabled, and the configured minimum gap from the previous trap.
Short: An upside sweep occurs when high is above the latest confirmed swing high by at least the configured minimum ATR penetration, penetration is no greater than the configured maximum, and close is below the swing high; also require either the configured wick-reclaim mode with wick ratio at least its minimum, or close-reclaim mode. Require high to be a local high within the configured lookback. Apply configured ATR/volume activity, ADX, DMI chop, EMA regime, Ichimoku context, spike, and mitigation filters; reject a sweep within the configured cooldown, at a previously used nearby level, or during the configured post-spike or post-trap blocking period. OR a bearish trap confirms: after a qualifying upside sweep, either immediately or within the configured confirmation window, close reaches the follow-through target; require the configured minimum age when follow-through is enabled, and the configured minimum gap from the previous trap.
