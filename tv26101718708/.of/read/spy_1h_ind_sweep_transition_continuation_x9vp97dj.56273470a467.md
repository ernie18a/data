Calculation: Direction sign d = +1 for long and -1 for short; use sign-adjusted prices d × price, with adjusted high H = high for long and -low for short, and adjusted low L = low for long and -high for short. Comparisons below use adjusted prices unless stated otherwise.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe inputs = the open of the higher-timeframe bar still forming; ATR, fast EMA, slow EMA, rolling log-return standard deviation, and the average of that standard deviation from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA. The previous slow EMA is from the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe liquidity levels are the highest high and lowest low over long and short lookbacks ending at the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the tactical lookback is shorter than the major lookback.
Calculation: Expected-range levels = forming higher-timeframe open ± forming higher-timeframe open × completed higher-timeframe log-return standard deviation × square root of the range horizon × range multiplier. The adjusted lower level is the lower level for long and the negated upper level for short.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by the EMA of volumes from previous completed bars at the same session slot, or by the chart-bar volume SMA if that slot has no positive average. If slot normalization is disabled or the chart is not intraday, use the chart-bar volume SMA. Return zero if the selected denominator is unavailable or nonpositive. Reset the slot at each higher-timeframe session boundary and increment it on regular-session bars; update the slot EMA only on completed regular-session bars, or on every completed bar for a non-intraday chart.
Calculation: Displacement = adjusted close > adjusted open AND absolute candle body ≥ completed higher-timeframe ATR × displacement multiplier AND body / max(high - low, minimum price increment) ≥ body-ratio threshold AND (adjusted close - L) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) ≥ close-location threshold.
Calculation: Swing break = adjusted close > latest confirmed adjusted pivot high AND previous adjusted close ≤ that same pivot high. A pivot is confirmed after its configured right-side bars; its left and right lookbacks are separately configurable. Swing sequence = latest confirmed adjusted pivot high > preceding confirmed adjusted pivot high. Internal break = adjusted close > highest adjusted high over the preceding internal lookback AND previous adjusted close ≤ that same highest high. Recent structure = a swing break OR internal break occurred within the structure-memory lookback.
Calculation: Trend alignment = higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR adjusted fast EMA > adjusted slow EMA AND adjusted slow EMA > its previous higher-timeframe value. Opposing trend = adjusted fast EMA < adjusted slow EMA AND adjusted slow EMA < its previous higher-timeframe value. Expanding volatility = completed higher-timeframe log-return standard deviation > its completed higher-timeframe average.
Calculation: Sweep = L < any adjusted major liquidity low, tactical liquidity low, or expected-range lower level AND adjusted close > that same level. Sweep count is the number of these levels swept. On a completed bar, arm the swept direction and record its bar, L, count, major-sweep status, and sweep score; replace any earlier arm and cancel the opposite arm. If both directions sweep, arm only the direction of the candle body; a doji arms neither. Expire an arm after the confirmation window, cancel both arms if an armed direction closes below its recorded L minus ATR × invalidation buffer, and clear the entered direction's arm on entry.
Calculation: Sweep score = major-sweep base score if the major level was swept, otherwise tactical-or-range base score, plus sweep count minus one when count ≥ the confluence threshold. Reversal score = armed sweep score + trend-alignment points + swing-break points, using the higher swing-break award when swing sequence holds, or internal-break points if there is no swing break and internal breaks are enabled + reclaim points if adjusted close > forming higher-timeframe open OR, on an intraday chart, adjusted close > session VWAP + points each for relative volume ≥ volume threshold, displacement, and expanding volatility. External-market score modifiers cannot be calculated from the supplied data and contribute no points.
Calculation: Reversal validity = an arm exists AND bars since arm ≥ minimum confirmation delay AND bars since arm ≤ confirmation window AND, if swing breaks are required, swing break AND reversal score ≥ aligned score threshold when trend-aligned or the higher countertrend threshold otherwise AND, if countertrend major-only filtering is enabled and trend is not aligned, the armed sweep included the major level OR its count ≥ confluence threshold. A reversal signal occurs when reversal validity becomes true after being false; a short reversal signal is suppressed by a simultaneous long reversal signal.
Calculation: Continuation regime = continuation enabled AND adjusted fast EMA > its value on the preceding chart bar AND adjusted close > adjusted fast EMA. Continuation score = regime points + points for swing sequence + internal break + swing break, with a higher swing-break award when swing sequence holds + displacement + relative volume ≥ volume threshold + expanding volatility. External-market score modifiers contribute no points. Continuation validity = continuation regime AND adjusted close > highest adjusted high over the preceding continuation lookback AND previous adjusted close ≤ that same high AND swing break OR internal break AND (adjusted close - adjusted fast EMA) / ATR ≤ continuation extension limit AND continuation score ≥ continuation threshold. Parenthesize the swing-break OR internal-break condition. A continuation signal occurs when continuation validity becomes true after being false.
Calculation: Transition arm = on a completed bar, adjusted close crosses above the adjusted fast EMA using the current and previous chart-bar EMA values; arm that direction, cancel the opposite arm and opposite qualification, and expire the arm after its transition window. Clear the entered direction's arm and qualification on entry. Transition regime = transition enabled AND adjusted close > adjusted fast EMA AND (adjusted fast EMA - its preceding chart-bar value) / ATR > negative slope limit AND opposing trend is false. Transition score = regime points + points each for recent structure, displacement, relative volume ≥ volume threshold, and expanding volatility. Qualify an active arm when transition regime AND recent structure AND (adjusted close - adjusted fast EMA) / ATR ≤ transition extension limit AND transition score ≥ transition threshold; retain the qualification for the execution window even if those conditions subsequently cease. Expire it after that window.
Calculation: Reversal stop = recorded sweep L - ATR × reversal stop buffer. Continuation stop = maximum of latest confirmed adjusted swing low, if available, and the highest adjusted high over the preceding continuation lookback minus ATR × continuation stop buffer. Transition stop = maximum of latest confirmed adjusted swing low, if available, adjusted fast EMA, and the highest adjusted high over the preceding transition lookback minus ATR × transition stop buffer. Risk = adjusted entry close - selected adjusted stop; valid risk is > zero AND ≤ ATR × maximum-risk multiplier. Select reversal before transition before continuation based on signal presence, even if the selected reversal's risk is invalid. Transition is a signal only while its qualification is active and its risk is valid. Store the selected stop and risk at entry.
Calculation: Locked structure = latest confirmed adjusted swing low at entry, subsequently raised to the maximum of its previous value and each new latest confirmed adjusted swing low. Runner activates after exit checks on a completed bar when (adjusted close - adjusted entry price) / initial risk ≥ runner activation threshold. The running adjusted high includes the entry bar and is updated after each bar's exit checks. Active trail = running adjusted high - current ATR × trail multiplier, raised to locked structure - current ATR × structure-trail buffer when structural trailing is enabled and locked structure exists. Profit target = adjusted entry price + initial risk × target multiplier.
Long: On a forming chart bar, adjusted low < an adjusted liquidity or expected-range lower level AND adjusted close > that level AND no long sweep arm exists.
Short: On a forming chart bar, adjusted low < an adjusted liquidity or expected-range lower level AND adjusted close > that level AND no short sweep arm exists.
Long: On an eligible completed chart bar, position is flat AND bars since the last exit > cooldown OR no exit has occurred AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND the selected long reversal, transition, or continuation signal has valid risk. Parenthesize the cooldown alternatives.
Short: On an eligible completed chart bar, position is flat AND bars since the last exit > cooldown OR no exit has occurred AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND the selected short reversal, transition, or continuation signal has valid risk AND no long entry occurs on the same bar. Parenthesize the cooldown alternatives.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND adjusted low ≤ stored stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND adjusted low ≤ stored stop.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND structure exit is enabled AND an opposing swing break occurs AND locked structure is unavailable OR adjusted close < locked structure - ATR × structure-exit buffer. Parenthesize the locked-structure alternatives.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND structure exit is enabled AND an opposing swing break occurs AND locked structure is unavailable OR adjusted close < locked structure - ATR × structure-exit buffer. Parenthesize the locked-structure alternatives.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND sessions held ≥ minimum holding period AND trailing is enabled AND runner is active AND adjusted low ≤ active trail.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND sessions held ≥ minimum holding period AND trailing is enabled AND runner is active AND adjusted low ≤ active trail.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND sessions held ≥ minimum holding period AND take profit is enabled AND adjusted high ≥ profit target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND higher-timeframe ATR is available AND sessions held ≥ minimum holding period AND take profit is enabled AND adjusted high ≥ profit target.
