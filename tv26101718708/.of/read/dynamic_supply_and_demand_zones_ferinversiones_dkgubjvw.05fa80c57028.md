Calculation: Supply zone = on confirmation of a pivot high (higher than the highs in the same-length lookback on both sides), set the top to the pivot high and the bottom to top minus ATR at the pivot bar × maximum zone-height multiplier × seller-volume percentage. Seller-volume percentage = 100 × volume at the pivot bar × (1 − buying ratio) ÷ (volume at the pivot bar if positive, otherwise 1); buying ratio = (close − low) ÷ (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Demand zone = on confirmation of a pivot low (lower than the lows in the same-length lookback on both sides), set the bottom to the pivot low and the top to bottom plus ATR at the pivot bar × maximum zone-height multiplier × buyer-volume percentage. Buyer-volume percentage = 100 × volume at the pivot bar × buying ratio ÷ (volume at the pivot bar if positive, otherwise 1); buying ratio = (close − low) ÷ (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Long: An active demand zone exists, volume at its pivot bar > the 20-bar average volume at that bar, low ≤ zone top AND close ≥ zone bottom.
Short: An active supply zone exists, volume at its pivot bar > the 20-bar average volume at that bar, high ≥ zone bottom AND close ≤ zone top.
