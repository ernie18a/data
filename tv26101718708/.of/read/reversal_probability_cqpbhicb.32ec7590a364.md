Calculation: Reversal state = Maintain separate bullish and bearish states. On each bar, a bullish trigger occurs when the number of bars in the lookback including the current bar whose low exceeds the current close equals the lookback length minus one; a bearish trigger occurs when the number whose high is below the current close equals the lookback length minus one. On a trigger, replace that side's stored trigger high and low, activate that side, clear its signal flag, and reset its count to zero. Then increment each active side's count. Deactivate the bullish side if close is below its stored trigger low; deactivate the bearish side if close is above its stored trigger high. A new trigger replaces the previous trigger even if it was still active.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish probabilities = On the signal bar, count enabled factors and divide the number of satisfied enabled factors by the number enabled, multiply by 100 and round to the nearest integer; if no factors are enabled, use 50%. Bullish factors are RSI below an oversold threshold; volume above a multiple of its moving average AND close above open; close below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the zone lookback including the current bar; min(open, close) minus low at least a specified fraction of high minus low; and close above EMA. Bearish factors are RSI above an overbought threshold; volume above the same multiple of its moving average AND close below open; close above that midpoint; high minus max(open, close) at least the specified fraction of high minus low; and close below EMA.
Long: Bullish state is active AND close is above its stored trigger high AND its signal flag is clear AND its count is at most the confirmation-window length plus one AND (probability display is disabled OR bullish probability is at least the minimum probability). Set the bullish signal flag when the first four conditions hold, even if the probability filter fails. If non-repainting mode is enabled, evaluate the first four conditions using the previous bar's final state and set the flag and evaluate the probability filter on the current bar.
Short: Bearish state is active AND close is below its stored trigger low AND its signal flag is clear AND its count is at most the confirmation-window length plus one AND (probability display is disabled OR bearish probability is at least the minimum probability). Set the bearish signal flag when the first four conditions hold, even if the probability filter fails. If non-repainting mode is enabled, evaluate the first four conditions using the previous bar's final state and set the flag and evaluate the probability filter on the current bar.
Long: RSI is below the oversold threshold.
Short: RSI is above the overbought threshold.
Long: Close is at or below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the zone lookback including the current bar.
Short: Close is above the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the zone lookback including the current bar.
