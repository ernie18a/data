Calculation: Fair value gap = On each newly completed source-timeframe bar, compare it with the source bar two bars earlier. A bullish gap exists when the completed bar's low is greater than the earlier bar's high; its top is that low and its bottom is that high. A bearish gap exists when the completed bar's high is less than the earlier bar's low; its top is the earlier low and its bottom is that high. The source timeframe is equal to or higher than the intraday chart timeframe. Register each gap on the first chart bar after the source bar completes. Keep a limited number of registered gaps, removing the oldest when the limit is reached, including inactive gaps.
Calculation: Gap state = A newly registered gap is active and unretested. Its safety edge is the bottom minus a safety fraction of the gap size for a bullish gap, or the top plus that fraction for a bearish gap. On every chart bar starting with registration, deactivate a bullish gap if low <= its safety edge or a bearish gap if high >= its safety edge. For an active gap, mark it retested after its first bar with low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom.
Long: Chart timeframe is intraday AND source timeframe >= chart timeframe AND a bullish gap is active and unretested AND low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND low > gap safety edge.
Short: Chart timeframe is intraday AND source timeframe >= chart timeframe AND a bearish gap is active and unretested AND low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND high < gap safety edge.
