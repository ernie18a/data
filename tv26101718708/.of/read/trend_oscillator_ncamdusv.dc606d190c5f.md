Calculation: Trend = 100 × (MA of Heikin-Ashi close − MA of Heikin-Ashi open) / (MA of Heikin-Ashi high − MA of Heikin-Ashi low), using the same selected moving-average type and length for all four series.
Calculation: Trend slope = the selected slope of the moving-average Heikin-Ashi body (Heikin-Ashi close MA − Heikin-Ashi open MA), blended as ER × fast slope + (1 − ER) × slow slope, then divided by its rolling maximum absolute value and multiplied by the slope scale. ER = absolute net close change over its lookback / sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback. Slope method is selected from the smoothed average of body changes, linear-regression slope, or n-bar body change divided by n.
Calculation: Bullish peak anchor = the most recent strong positive slope peak and its close. A positive slope peak is a local maximum on the previous bar (previous slope > current slope, previous slope ≥ slope two bars ago, and previous slope > 0); it is strong when its normalized slope exceeds the momentum threshold. Create or reactivate the anchor only at a strong peak when none is active or Trend has crossed to ≤ 0; while active, replace it only at a later strong peak. Trend ≤ 0 deactivates it.
Calculation: Bearish peak anchor = the most recent strong negative slope trough and its close. A negative slope trough is a local minimum on the previous bar (previous slope < current slope, previous slope ≤ slope two bars ago, and previous slope < 0); it is strong when its normalized slope is below negative momentum threshold. Create or reactivate the anchor only at a strong trough when none is active or Trend has crossed to ≥ 0; while active, replace it only at a later strong trough. Trend ≥ 0 deactivates it.
Long: Trend slope > deadband AND previous Trend slope ≤ deadband OR previous Trend slope > current Trend slope AND previous Trend slope ≥ Trend slope two bars ago AND previous Trend slope > 0 AND an active bullish peak anchor exists AND previous Trend slope < anchor slope AND previous close > anchor close AND previous Trend > 0.
Short: Trend slope < −deadband AND previous Trend slope ≥ −deadband OR previous Trend slope < current Trend slope AND previous Trend slope ≤ Trend slope two bars ago AND previous Trend slope < 0 AND an active bearish peak anchor exists AND previous Trend slope > anchor slope AND previous close < anchor close AND previous Trend < 0.
