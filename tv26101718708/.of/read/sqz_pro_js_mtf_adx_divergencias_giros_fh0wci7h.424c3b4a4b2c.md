Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression endpoint of close minus the average of (the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the squeeze lookback) and (the SMA of close over that same lookback), using a separate momentum lookback. Replace unavailable prior momentum values with zero when evaluating turns or histogram direction.
Calculation: Squeeze bands = SMA of close over the squeeze lookback plus or minus a Bollinger multiplier times the standard deviation of close over that lookback; Keltner bands = the same SMA plus or minus a Keltner multiplier times the SMA of true range over that lookback.
Calculation: Timeframe evaluation = evaluate momentum, bands, ADX and directional indicators on each selected timeframe; on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, and in real time use the forming higher-timeframe bar or the latest available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish timeframe state = momentum > 0 AND (lower Bollinger band <= lower Keltner band OR upper Bollinger band >= upper Keltner band) AND ADX > weak-trend threshold AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator.
Calculation: Bearish timeframe state = momentum < 0 AND (lower Bollinger band <= lower Keltner band OR upper Bollinger band >= upper Keltner band) AND ADX > weak-trend threshold AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator.
Calculation: Momentum pivot = a momentum high or low confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars against configurable numbers of left-hand and right-hand bars; associate it with the high or low of its pivot bar, respectively. Compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same kind, then replace that preceding pivot regardless of whether a divergence occurred.
Long: (momentum > 0 AND momentum > previous momentum) OR (momentum <= 0 AND momentum >= previous momentum).
Short: (momentum > 0 AND momentum <= previous momentum) OR (momentum <= 0 AND momentum < previous momentum).
Long: momentum < 0 AND momentum > previous momentum AND previous momentum <= momentum two bars ago.
Short: momentum > 0 AND momentum < previous momentum AND previous momentum >= momentum two bars ago.
Long: momentum > 0 AND previous momentum <= 0.
Short: momentum < 0 AND previous momentum >= 0.

Short: a momentum high pivot is confirmed AND chart timeframe >= divergence timeframe threshold AND distance from the previous high pivot <= maximum pivot distance AND ((pivot-bar high > previous pivot-bar high AND pivot momentum < previous pivot momentum) OR (pivot-bar high < previous pivot-bar high AND pivot momentum > previous pivot momentum)).
