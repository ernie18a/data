Calculation: Body high = max(open, close); body low = min(open, close).
Calculation: Long entry level = previous bar's body high if the current bullish body engulfs the previous body; otherwise the body high two bars ago. Short entry level = previous bar's body low if the current bearish body engulfs the previous body; otherwise the body low two bars ago.
Calculation: A winning exit occurs when the take-profit level is reached and the stop-loss level is not reached, or when the forced closing time is reached without either level being reached and the closing price is at least the entry price for a long or at most the entry price for a short. If both levels are reached on the same bar, the exit is a loss.
Long: At least two previous bars exist AND position is flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within the configured session in its configured timezone) AND entries today in that timezone < daily entry limit AND (stop-after-first-win filter disabled OR winning exits today = 0) AND close > open AND ((open <= previous body low AND close >= previous body high) OR (two-candle engulf enabled AND min(current body low, previous body low) <= body low two bars ago AND max(current body high, previous body high) >= body high two bars ago)) AND close > selected moving average of close AND long entry level > selected moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR moving average > previous moving average) AND (proximity filter disabled OR absolute(long entry level - moving average) <= proximity threshold) AND high >= long entry level. Enter at the long entry level.
Short: At least two previous bars exist AND position is flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within the configured session in its configured timezone) AND entries today in that timezone < daily entry limit AND (stop-after-first-win filter disabled OR winning exits today = 0) AND close < open AND ((open >= previous body high AND close <= previous body low) OR (two-candle engulf enabled AND max(current body high, previous body high) >= body high two bars ago AND min(current body low, previous body low) <= body low two bars ago)) AND close < selected moving average of close AND short entry level < selected moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR moving average < previous moving average) AND (proximity filter disabled OR absolute(short entry level - moving average) <= proximity threshold) AND low <= short entry level. Enter at the short entry level.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance OR (forced close enabled AND current bar's opening time >= the day's 16:55 New York time AND previous bar's opening time < that time).
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance OR (forced close enabled AND current bar's opening time >= the day's 16:55 New York time AND previous bar's opening time < that time).
