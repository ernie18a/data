Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = From the first observed anchor reset, accumulate chart-bar volume times price and price squared; VWAP is sum(volume × price) / sum(volume), and deviation is sqrt(max(sum(volume × price²) / sum(volume) - VWAP², 0)). Use a selectable chart-bar price, or the volume-weighted first and second moments of that price from completed lower-timeframe bars within each completed chart bar; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use the chart-bar price. If their total volume is zero, use their unweighted mean first and second moments. Reset the accumulators at each anchor-period change. The optional higher-timeframe VWAP filter uses the same calculation for the higher anchor period still forming.
Calculation: Bands = Anchored VWAP plus or minus deviation multiplied by a selectable multiplier; band 2 has a larger multiplier than band 1.
Calculation: Normalized slope = (current anchored VWAP - anchored VWAP from min(slope lookback, bars since anchor reset) chart bars ago) / ATR; use zero when the lookback or ATR is zero. Rising means slope > positive threshold, falling means slope < negative threshold, and flat means neither.
Calculation: Setup window = The current chart bar and the preceding setup-lookback bars. A qualifying zone touch or band tag must have occurred no more than min(setup lookback, bars since anchor reset) bars ago.
Calculation: Long risk = close - (lowest low in the setup window - ATR × stop-buffer multiplier); short risk = (highest high in the setup window + ATR × stop-buffer multiplier) - close. Reward is the distance from close to band 2 in the trade direction for a pullback, or from close to anchored VWAP for a mean reversion.
Calculation: Setup eligibility = Each direction and setup type starts eligible and resets to eligible at each anchor reset. A qualifying signal makes its direction and setup type ineligible; a long pullback becomes eligible again when close > upper band 1, a short pullback when close < lower band 1, a long mean reversion when close >= VWAP, and a short mean reversion when close <= VWAP. Apply re-eligibility before evaluating the current bar.
Long: Anchored VWAP is available AND deviation > 0 AND bars since anchor reset >= warm-up AND current bar is not the reset bar AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe VWAP is unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP) AND long risk > 0 AND reward > 0 AND reward / long risk >= minimum reward-to-risk AND ((long pullback is eligible AND normalized slope is rising AND a bar in the permitted window has low <= its VWAP AND low > its lower band 2 AND every close in the setup window >= its lower band 1 AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND (low rejection wick >= minimum fraction of a positive bar range OR previous close <= current VWAP)) OR (long mean reversion is eligible AND normalized slope is flat AND a bar in the permitted window has low <= its lower band 2 AND close > lower band 2 AND close < VWAP AND close > open AND (low rejection wick >= minimum fraction of a positive bar range OR previous close <= current lower band 2))).
Short: Anchored VWAP is available AND deviation > 0 AND bars since anchor reset >= warm-up AND current bar is not the reset bar AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe VWAP is unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP) AND short risk > 0 AND reward > 0 AND reward / short risk >= minimum reward-to-risk AND ((short pullback is eligible AND normalized slope is falling AND a bar in the permitted window has high >= its VWAP AND high < its upper band 2 AND every close in the setup window <= its upper band 1 AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND (high rejection wick >= minimum fraction of a positive bar range OR previous close >= current VWAP)) OR (short mean reversion is eligible AND normalized slope is flat AND a bar in the permitted window has high >= its upper band 2 AND close < upper band 2 AND close > VWAP AND close < open AND (high rejection wick >= minimum fraction of a positive bar range OR previous close >= current upper band 2))).
