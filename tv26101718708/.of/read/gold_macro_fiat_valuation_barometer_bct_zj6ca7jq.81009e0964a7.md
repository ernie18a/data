Calculation: Valuation Z-score = (close − liquidity/yield fair value) ÷ the standard deviation of the close-minus-fair-value spread over a shorter lookback; fair value is money supply multiplied by the long-lookback median of close ÷ money supply, adjusted inversely for real yield relative to a neutral yield. Use the last completed bar of each requested timeframe for real yield and money supply.
Calculation: Macro score = the smoothed weighted sum of five categorical scores: real yields, gold-to-money-supply percentile, breadth of gold prices above their long-term averages across fiat currencies, gold/silver ratio and valuation Z-score. Each factor awards its higher score for favorable conditions, lower score for unfavorable conditions, and an intermediate score otherwise.

Long: Macro score > accumulation threshold AND prior-bar macro score ≤ accumulation threshold
