Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; maintain separate longer-lookback and shorter-lookback swing series. A newly confirmed swing is unbroken. A structure break marks the latest swing of that side broken; a liquidity sweep marks the second-latest longer-lookback swing of that side broken.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open AND previous body > a fraction of previous range AND previous range > current ATR AND current low minus high two bars ago > a multiple of current ATR AND current volume > its SMA AND the nearest bearish candle in a limited preceding lookback has low < the low of its preceding candle; the final comparison is optional. Its lower and upper bounds are the high two bars ago and current low.
Calculation: Bearish gap = current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open AND previous body > a fraction of previous range AND previous range > current ATR AND low two bars ago minus current high > a multiple of current ATR AND current volume > its SMA AND the nearest bullish candle in a limited preceding lookback has high > the high of its preceding candle; the final comparison is optional. Its lower and upper bounds are current high and the low two bars ago.
Calculation: Active gap = a generated gap retained among the most recent limited number of gaps that have not been marked inversion-mitigated. A bullish gap inverts on close < its lower bound; a bearish gap inverts on close > its upper bound. After inversion, a bullish gap is inversion-mitigated on high > its lower bound and a bearish gap is inversion-mitigated on low < its upper bound, including on the inversion bar. Ordinary mitigation does not prevent inversion.
Long: For either swing lookback, a confirmed high and low both exist AND the latest high is unbroken AND close > that high; alternatively use high > that high.
Short: For either swing lookback, a confirmed high and low both exist AND the latest low is unbroken AND close < that low; alternatively use low < that low.
Long: The second-latest longer-lookback swing low is unbroken AND low < that swing low AND close > that swing low.
Short: The second-latest longer-lookback swing high is unbroken AND high > that swing high AND close < that swing high.
Long: Bullish gap.
Short: Bearish gap.
Long: An active, not-yet-inverted bearish gap exists AND close > its upper bound.
Short: An active, not-yet-inverted bullish gap exists AND close < its lower bound.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close on a configurable higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical evaluation and the forming bar in realtime, or the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close <= longer-period EMA of close on a configurable higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical evaluation and the forming bar in realtime, or the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
