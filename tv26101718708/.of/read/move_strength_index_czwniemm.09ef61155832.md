Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the RMA of prior bars' volume, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Relative-volume percentile rank = percentile rank of relative volume over its trailing lookback
Long: relative-volume percentile rank >= strong-signal percentile AND close >= open AND (high - low) / ATR > narrow-range threshold
Short: relative-volume percentile rank >= strong-signal percentile AND close < open AND (high - low) / ATR > narrow-range threshold
