Calculation: Upper/lower trend bands = a central line formed by an EMA smoothed with an SMA, plus/minus an EMA of absolute close-to-central-line deviation
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = a previously recorded swing low, formed when the prior bar's low is below both neighboring lows and above the lower trend band while trend is up, is later crossed by the current low while the lower band is at or below that level
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = a previously recorded swing high, formed when the prior bar's high is above both neighboring highs and below the upper trend band while trend is down, is later crossed by the current high while the upper band is at or above that level
Long: trend changes from nonpositive to positive OR a bullish liquidity sweep occurs with normalized volume greater than zero
Short: trend changes from nonnegative to negative OR a bearish liquidity sweep occurs with normalized volume greater than zero
Exit: Long, trend changes from nonnegative to negative
Exit: Short, trend changes from nonpositive to positive
