Calculation: Anchor levels = On the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe, confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks for each enabled pivot length. Create an anchor on the confirmation bar, initialized at the pivot price; accumulate price times volume and volume from the confirmation bar onward to calculate anchored VWAP. Use the configured price source on the chart timeframe and typical price on higher timeframes. Expire anchors when their age exceeds the maximum or their timeframe's total anchor cap is exceeded, removing oldest first; higher-timeframe age is measured from the pivot bar and chart-timeframe age from confirmation. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. For each anchor, maintain a price range initialized at the pivot price and expanded by subsequent highs and lows. Divide that range into equal bins, distribute each bar's volume equally across its covered bins, and retain accumulated bin volumes without redistributing them when the range expands. At the configured recomputation interval, calculate POC as the center of the highest-volume bin, breaking ties toward the lowest bin; expand the value area from that bin toward the larger adjacent bin, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the configured volume fraction is reached. Cache POC and value-area edges between recomputations. Chart-timeframe anchors contribute only when their weighted combination of range tightness relative to a configured fraction of close and recency relative to maximum age reaches the quality threshold, unless quality filtering is disabled. When an anchor is removed, retain its POC as a naked POC if no bar in the configured recent lookback straddled it; remove naked POCs when straddled, too old, or above their count cap. Include enabled anchor VWAPs, weighted chart-timeframe POCs and value-area edges, optional higher-timeframe POCs and value-area edges, and weighted naked POCs as contributors. Higher-timeframe profile levels contribute once each. Suppress VWAP accumulation when the cumulative fraction of positive-volume chart bars is below its threshold; calculate chart profiles only on positive-range bars when volume is reliable.
Calculation: Zone = On each chart bar close after the anchor-age warmup, sort contributors by price and group consecutive levels whenever their gap is at most the configured fraction of close. Select the qualifying group with the greatest weighted contributor count, breaking ties toward the lower-priced group. Its low and high are its extreme prices and its center is their midpoint. Merge it into the first active zone whose center is within the configured merge fraction of close, expanding its bounds and updating its last reinforcement bar; retain the contributor counts from its strongest group. Otherwise create an active zone, widening equal bounds symmetrically by a configured fraction of close, and record its initial pivot-low and pivot-high contributor counts and center. An active zone becomes pending when bars since its last reinforcement exceed the quiet-bar threshold. A pending zone resolves once bars since its last reinforcement reach the outcome lookback: support if percentage close change from its current center exceeds the positive outcome threshold, resistance if below the negative threshold, otherwise neutral. A resolved resistance becomes support after the configured number of consecutive closes above its upper edge by more than the flip fraction; resolved support becomes resistance after consecutive closes below its lower edge by more than that fraction. Reset the consecutive count when the condition fails or a flip occurs. A resolved support is broken when close is below its lower edge by more than the configured fraction of close; a resolved resistance is broken when close is above its upper edge by more than that fraction. Recalculate broken status each bar and clear it on a flip. Evict oldest zones when the zone cap is exceeded.
Calculation: Initial zone bias = Initial pivot-low contributor count minus initial pivot-high contributor count, plus one if the initial center is below close or minus one if above close, but only when its distance from close is within the configured ATR multiple. Keep this bias fixed for the zone's active and pending states.
Long: A newly created active zone has initial zone bias at or above the positive bias threshold.
Short: A newly created active zone has initial zone bias at or below the negative bias threshold.
Long: A pending zone resolves as support.
Short: A pending zone resolves as resistance.
Long: A resolved resistance flips to support.
Short: A resolved support flips to resistance.
Long: Previous close is outside a resolved support zone AND current close is inside its inclusive bounds.
Short: Previous close is outside a resolved resistance zone AND current close is inside its inclusive bounds.
Long: At least one unbroken resolved support has center at or below close AND no unbroken resolved resistance has center at or above close.
Short: At least one unbroken resolved resistance has center at or above close AND no unbroken resolved support has center at or below close.
