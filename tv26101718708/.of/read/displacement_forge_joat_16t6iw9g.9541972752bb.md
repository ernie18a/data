Calculation: Price-change Z-score = (current close-to-close change minus its rolling mean) divided by its rolling population standard deviation; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, resetting at each daily boundary.
Calculation: Order block zones = On a bullish or bearish displacement, use the high and low of the most recent opposite-direction candle within the impulse lookback; if none exists, use the previous bar. Optionally prevent creation when its range overlaps any existing zone, including at a boundary. After creation, remove bullish zones when close < zone low and bearish zones when close > zone high; remove zones whose age exceeds twice the maximum age, then remove the oldest zones until the configured maximum zone count is met. Perform creation, removal and count limiting before evaluating rejections, including on the creation bar.
Long: Price-change Z-score > positive threshold AND close > open AND close > highest close over the preceding impulse-lookback bars AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP).
Short: Price-change Z-score < negative threshold AND close < open AND close < lowest close over the preceding impulse-lookback bars AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < daily VWAP).
Long: A retained bullish zone exists AND low >= zone low AND low <= zone high AND min(open, close) <= zone high AND close > open AND close - low > 0.4 * (zone high - zone low).
Short: A retained bearish zone exists AND high >= zone low AND high <= zone high AND max(open, close) >= zone low AND close < open AND high - close > 0.4 * (zone high - zone low).
