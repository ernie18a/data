Calculation: Pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows, each confirmed after an equal left and right lookback; retain the most recent parameterized maximum number per side, including previously swept pools. Begin detecting sweeps only after the bar index exceeds three pivot lookbacks and ATR is available and positive.
Calculation: Sweep candidates = for each retained pivot low, a bullish candidate has low < pool - ATR × buffer, pool - low >= ATR × (minimum wick + buffer), and, if reclaim is required, close > pool; for each retained pivot high, a bearish candidate has high > pool + ATR × buffer, high - pool >= ATR × (minimum wick + buffer), and, if reclaim is required, close < pool. Select the greatest penetration per side, breaking ties in favor of the most recently added pool. If both sides qualify, select the greater penetration, with bullish winning a tie. The selected pool is the sweep level and the sweep bar's low or high is the bullish or bearish origin, respectively.
Calculation: Event state = detect sweeps only while idle and outside cooldown. A selected sweep arms its direction. On subsequent bars through the displacement-window limit, the first bullish displacement has close > sweep level, close - origin >= ATR × minimum displacement, close - open >= ATR × minimum body, and close > open; the first bearish displacement has close < sweep level, origin - close >= ATR × minimum displacement, open - close >= ATR × minimum body, and close < open. Store the displacement close and bar. If no displacement occurs by the first bar after the window, return to idle and start the timeout cooldown. After displacement, resolve on the first rejection, the first acceptance at least one bar after displacement, or the first bar after the acceptance window. Then return to idle and start the resolution cooldown. Decrement cooldown on the bar it starts and on each subsequent bar; permit a new sweep only when it is zero at the start of detection.
Calculation: Acceptance and rejection = for a bullish event, hold line = origin + hold fraction × abs(displacement close - origin), rejection means close < sweep level AND close < open, and acceptance means close >= hold line AND low > origin AND rejection is false. For a bearish event, hold line = origin - hold fraction × abs(displacement close - origin), rejection means close > sweep level AND close > open, and acceptance means close <= hold line AND high < origin AND rejection is false. A timeout resolves as rejection when acceptance is false and as acceptance otherwise.
Calculation: Acceptance score = on the resolution bar, let depth = abs(sweep level - origin) / ATR, displacement = abs(displacement close - origin) / ATR, body = abs(close - open) / ATR, and volume pulse = volume / volume SMA if the SMA is positive, otherwise 1. Structure break is close above the highest high of a prior-bar lookback OR close > sweep level + ATR × structure buffer for a bullish event; reverse the comparisons and use the lowest low for a bearish event. Higher-timeframe bias compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with the preceding higher-timeframe close; it is bullish, bearish, or neutral according to whether the former is greater, less, or equal. Score is the sum of min(2, depth / depth scale), min(2.5, 1.4 × displacement / max(minimum displacement, 0.01)), min(2, 1.2 × body / max(minimum body, 0.01)), and the volume component: min(2, 1 + 0.6 × (volume pulse - volume threshold)) if volume pulse >= volume threshold, otherwise min(1, volume pulse / volume threshold). Add 1 if structure break is true; add 1 for aligned higher-timeframe bias, 0.4 for neutral bias, or 0 for opposed bias; if higher-timeframe scoring is disabled, add 0.5 instead. Round the sum to one decimal place and cap it at 10.
