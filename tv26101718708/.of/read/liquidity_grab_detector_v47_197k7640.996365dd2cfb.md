Calculation: Pivot = a high or low surrounded on each side by the specified number of bars with no higher high or lower low, respectively; register it when the right-hand bars have completed.
Calculation: Pool = a resistance zone around a confirmed high pivot or a support zone around a confirmed low pivot. Sources are chart pivots, pivots from each of two higher timeframes using their last completed bars, the preceding completed daily and weekly highs and lows, and the latest of two consecutive chart pivots whose prices differ by less than an ATR-scaled threshold. Register a higher-timeframe or fixed level only when its value changes, and an equal-pivot level only when equality first becomes true. Initial midpoint is the source price, half-width is the greater of an ATR multiple and a minimum price increment, hits start at one, health starts at its maximum, and state starts unused. Local pivot age starts at the pivot bar and its width uses ATR at that bar; other pool ages start at registration and their widths use current ATR. Merge a new level into the nearest same-side pool within an ATR-scaled distance: replace midpoint with the hit-weighted mean, increment hits, widen the zone by a hit-dependent factor, and retain the greater source weight. Otherwise append a new pool and discard the oldest if the per-side limit is exceeded. On each session-passing bar, increment hits for a spaced touch inside the zone that closes on the interior side of the midpoint; reduce health by a volume-to-volume-average proportion when close is inside the zone. Remove a pool after its maximum age or after two consecutive closes beyond its outer edge. Process resistance pools newest first, then support pools newest first; a signaled pool becomes used and cannot generate another raw sweep.
Calculation: Adaptive thresholds = when calibration is enabled, derive pool half-width and minimum penetration from the ratio of average total wick length to average absolute body length, and derive the equal-pivot threshold from the average rolling min-max rank of short-term ATR; clamp and scale each using its respective configurable coefficients and sensitivity bias. Otherwise use the respective manual thresholds. The ATR used for pools and sweeps is shorter-term than the ATR used for anchored-VWAP stretch.
Calculation: Impulse anchored VWAP = cumulative typical-price-times-volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting on a new high above the preceding anchor-lookback highs or a new low below the preceding anchor-lookback lows. Daily anchored VWAP resets at the start of each day. Stretch is close minus the respective anchored VWAP divided by longer-term ATR, subject to a minimum price increment.
Calculation: Exhaustion percentile = rolling percentile rank of a bounded exhaustion score. The score is a scaled hyperbolic tangent of a weighted sum of smoothed exhaustion and price curvature. Smoothed exhaustion is an EMA of impulse stretch, optionally daily stretch, upper-minus-lower wick divided by true range, and positive volume z-score multiplied by the sign of impulse stretch. Price curvature is the negative difference between the rate of change of an EMA of close and the previous bar's short EMA of that rate of change. Use independently configurable weights, scaling, and lookbacks; the close EMA is longer than the curvature EMA.
Calculation: Sweep score = a weighted average of bounded components for penetration proximity to a target, reclaim fraction, sweep-side wick divided by true range, directional body, pool hits, health and source weight, absolute impulse stretch, higher-timeframe trend agreement, volume relative to its moving average, recent micro-structure trend reversal, distance beyond the preceding structure extreme in ATR units, and position between the nearest support and resistance midpoints. Normalize each component with its configurable bounds; combine hits, health and source weight with their own weights. The higher-timeframe trend uses the last completed higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA; when disabled its component is neutral. Micro-structure trend switches upward on a close above the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot high and downward on a close below the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low. Structure extremes exclude the current bar. Select component weights according to ADX and Bollinger-band-width percentile regime, multiply by the configured session quality in multiplier mode, by a coil bonus in the coil regime, by a bounded increasing function of pool age, and by a vacuum bonus when volume has remained below a volatility-adjusted fraction of its volume average for the required consecutive bars. Vacuum adjustment uses the calibration-window volume coefficient of variation. In gate mode, block processing when the UTC session's configured quality is below its threshold.
Calculation: Reversal class = absorption when volume z-score, sweep-side wick fraction and reclaim exceed their respective thresholds; otherwise rejection when wick fraction and reclaim exceed their thresholds and the candle body points in the reversal direction; otherwise divergence when volume z-score and reclaim exceed their thresholds and wick fraction is below its threshold; otherwise exhaustion. Each class has its own minimum sweep score, with the exhaustion minimum above attainable scores; a grind sweep has a separate minimum score.
Calculation: Raw support sweep = an unused, non-pending support pool with either low below its bottom, close above its midpoint, ATR-normalized penetration above its threshold and positive reclaim, OR grind enabled, a close in the preceding grind lookback below the current pool bottom by a specified current-ATR offset, current close above the bottom and below the top plus a current-ATR allowance, and close above open. Resistance sweeps reverse the inequalities and use high, top, bottom, and a bearish body. Spike takes precedence over grind. Reclaim is the fraction of the candle range recovered from the sweep extreme; for grind, floor reclaim at its configured minimum. Score using the raw-sweep bar and the pool's updated hits and health.
Calculation: Confirmed sweep = with zero confirmation window, a raw sweep whose score meets its reversal-class or grind minimum and, if enabled, the general score minimum. With a positive confirmation window, save the raw-sweep score and pool as pending; on a later bar within the window require a close above the support pool top or below the resistance pool bottom, the saved score to meet the optional general minimum and the current bar's reversal-class minimum, and classify the current bar using fixed reclaim. Clear pending after confirmation succeeds or fails its class minimum, after expiry, or when close breaches the previous bar's low for support or high for resistance. All sweep signals require the shared cooldown since the last sweep; immediate signals additionally require score greater than the best sweep score already selected on that bar. Update the shared cooldown immediately on selection.
Calculation: Armed state = independently for each direction, decrement its cooldown each bar; when its exhaustion percentile is beyond that direction's threshold, its directional liquidity condition is true, its armed counter is zero and its cooldown is zero, set its armed counter to its lifetime; otherwise decrement the counter toward zero. Long liquidity means sufficiently negative impulse stretch together with positive volume z-score above its threshold, or a long sweep; short liquidity mirrors this. On an SFP trigger, reset that side's armed counter and start its cooldown. Both SFP directions share a separate trigger cooldown.
Long: Session gate passes AND a confirmed support sweep is selected.
Short: Session gate passes AND a confirmed resistance sweep is selected.
Long: Long armed counter > zero AND short armed counter is not displayed in preference to it.
Short: Short armed counter > zero AND long armed counter = zero.
Long: SFP enabled AND session gate passes AND long armed counter > zero AND close > the highest high of the preceding structure-lookback bars AND bars since either SFP trigger >= shared trigger cooldown.
Short: SFP enabled AND session gate passes AND short armed counter > zero AND close < the lowest low of the preceding structure-lookback bars AND bars since either SFP trigger >= shared trigger cooldown.
