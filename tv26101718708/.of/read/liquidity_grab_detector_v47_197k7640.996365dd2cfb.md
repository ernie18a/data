Calculation: Liquidity pools = zones centered on confirmed swing highs (sell pools) and swing lows (buy pools), plus enabled previous-day/week highs and lows, equal highs/lows, and confirmed higher-timeframe swing highs/lows; merge nearby same-side levels and expire pools older than the maximum age. A pool zone extends by an ATR-scaled half-width on each side of its center.
Calculation: Sweep quality score = weighted score of penetration, reclaim, wick dominance, candle body direction, pool hits/health/source weight, impulse AVWAP stretch, higher-timeframe trend agreement, relative volume, micro trend change, break-of-structure distance, and position between the nearest active buy and sell pools; adjust for pool age, session, regime, and active volume vacuum.
Calculation: Exhaustion percentile = percentile rank over a longer lookback of a bounded exhaustion score combining impulse AVWAP stretch, optional daily AVWAP stretch, wick skew, positive volume z-score, and price-momentum curvature.
Calculation: Impulse AVWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3, reset when price makes a new high or low over its lookback. Daily AVWAP resets at each day boundary.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA; use the higher-timeframe bar without lookahead (forming in realtime, completed in historical data). Previous-day/week levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe swing pools use confirmed pivots.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND session gate passes AND an active buy pool has low < zone bottom, close > pool center, penetration > minimum, and close above low AND (either grind detection is enabled and a prior close within the grind lookback was below zone bottom by an ATR-scaled margin, current close is back inside/near the zone, and close > open, or the spike sweep condition holds) AND sweep quality passes the optional score filter and the sweep-type minimum AND cooldown passes; alternatively, SFP is enabled AND exhaustion percentile is at/below the oversold threshold AND (impulse AVWAP stretch is sufficiently negative with positive relative volume z-score OR a long sweep occurs) AND the long arm is active AND close exceeds the prior-bar highest high over the BOS lookback AND SFP cooldowns pass.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND session gate passes AND an active sell pool has high > zone top, close < pool center, penetration > minimum, and close below high AND (either grind detection is enabled and a prior close within the grind lookback was above zone top by an ATR-scaled margin, current close is back inside/near the zone, and close < open, or the spike sweep condition holds) AND sweep quality passes the optional score filter and the sweep-type minimum AND cooldown passes; alternatively, SFP is enabled AND exhaustion percentile is at/above the overbought threshold AND (impulse AVWAP stretch is sufficiently positive with positive relative volume z-score OR a short sweep occurs) AND the short arm is active AND close falls below the prior-bar lowest low over the BOS lookback AND SFP cooldowns pass.
