Calculation: ATR distance = 3 × ATR over the breakout channel’s lookback.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = while flat, close − ATR distance; on the first long bar, entry price − ATR distance; thereafter, if the highest close since entry ÷ entry price is at least 1.3, that highest close × 0.9 when the ratio is at least 1.3 but below 1.4, × 0.85 when at least 1.4 and at most 1.6, or × 0.8 when above 1.6; otherwise, raise the prior stop to close − ATR distance only when that value is higher and close is higher than the prior close, and otherwise retain the prior stop.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = while flat, close + ATR distance; on the first short bar, entry price + ATR distance; thereafter, lower the prior stop to close + ATR distance only when that value is lower and close is lower than the prior close, and otherwise retain the prior stop.
Long: On the next chart bar, high ≥ the prior bar’s highest high over the breakout channel’s lookback + 5 minimum ticks AND the prior bar was flat AND its year was greater than the base year AND its volume ÷ its simple moving average over the channel lookback × 100 met the volume threshold AND the trailing stops were defined.
Short: On the next chart bar, low ≤ the prior bar’s lowest low over the breakout channel’s lookback − 5 minimum ticks AND the prior bar was flat AND its year was greater than the base year AND its volume ÷ its simple moving average over the channel lookback × 100 met the volume threshold AND the trailing stops were defined.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long trailing stop OR long trailing stop > high OR (open − close ≥ 2/3 × ATR distance AND highest close since entry ÷ entry price < 1.3).
Exit: Short, high ≥ short trailing stop OR short trailing stop < low OR close − open ≥ 2/3 × ATR distance.
