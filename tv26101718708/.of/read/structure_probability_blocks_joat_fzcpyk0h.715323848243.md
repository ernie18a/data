Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side satisfying the symmetric pivot test; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind and its bar index.
Calculation: Candidate seed = the highest-ranked opposing candle among the preceding bars within both the search span and the distance to the broken pivot; ties select the most recent. Rank = 0.36 × body/range + 0.24 × directional wick/range + 0.22 × volume percentile rank/100 + 0.18 × (1 − (bars ago − 1)/search cap). For a bullish break use bearish candles and their lower wicks; for a bearish break use bullish candles and their upper wicks. Floor candle range at a positive price increment. Volume percentile rank uses its own lookback and defaults to 50 when unavailable.
Calculation: Block score = min(100, 58 × seed rank + 16 × break distance/ATR + min(10 × relative volume, 18) + 0.08 × current volume percentile rank). Break distance is close minus the broken pivot high for bullish breaks and the broken pivot low minus close for bearish breaks. ATR defaults to the current high minus low when unavailable and is floored at a positive price increment. Relative volume is current volume divided by its simple moving average, or 1 when the average is nonpositive; missing volume is zero and an unavailable average defaults to 1.
Calculation: Accepted block = a qualified break with an available seed and block score at least the minimum score. Its zone is the seed low to seed high. If overlap preference is enabled, inspect existing blocks newest first: for each unbroken block with strictly overlapping zones, remove it if the new score is greater; otherwise reject the new block and stop inspecting. Earlier removals remain even if the new block is rejected. Add an accepted block and, if the retained block count exceeds its limit, remove the oldest retained block. Then mark each unbroken bullish block broken when low <= its zone bottom, and each unbroken bearish block broken when high >= its zone top; optionally remove broken blocks. Overlap and capacity processing occur before this bar's break checks.
Calculation: Structure bias = the direction of the most recently accepted block, retained until another block is accepted. Active blocks are retained blocks that are not broken.
Long: Both pivot kinds are confirmed AND close > latest pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high AND (close − previous close)/floored ATR >= minimum impulse AND the bullish block is accepted.
Short: Both pivot kinds are confirmed AND close < latest pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low AND (previous close − close)/floored ATR >= minimum impulse AND the bearish block is accepted.
Long: Structure bias is bullish AND active block count > 0.
Short: Structure bias is bearish AND active block count > 0.
