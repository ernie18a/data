Calculation: Seed score = For each opposing candle in the backward search span (a bearish candle for an upward break, bullish for a downward break), calculate 0.36 × body size/range + 0.24 × direction-appropriate wick size/range + 0.22 × volume percentile rank over the volume lookback / 100 + 0.18 × (1 − (bars ago − 1) / search span); select the candle with the highest score, resolving ties in favor of the most recent. Range is high − low, floored at the minimum tick size.
Calculation: Block quality = min(100, 58 × selected seed score + 16 × break drive + min(10 × relative volume, 18) + 0.08 × current volume percentile rank). Relative volume = current volume / its simple moving average over the volume lookback. Break drive = distance from the broken pivot to the close / ATR(14).
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close ≤ that pivot high AND (close − previous close) / ATR(14) ≥ minimum break impulse AND a bearish seed candle exists in the backward search span since that pivot AND block quality ≥ minimum score AND (overlap guard disabled OR no active bearish or bullish block overlaps the seed candle’s high–low range with score ≥ block quality).
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close ≥ that pivot low AND (previous close − close) / ATR(14) ≥ minimum break impulse AND a bullish seed candle exists in the backward search span since that pivot AND block quality ≥ minimum score AND (overlap guard disabled OR no active bearish or bullish block overlaps the seed candle’s high–low range with score ≥ block quality).
