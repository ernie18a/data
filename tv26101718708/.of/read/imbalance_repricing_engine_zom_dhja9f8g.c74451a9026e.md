Calculation: Bullish imbalance zone = a confirmed bar whose low is above the high two chart bars earlier, with bullish displacement and a gap at least the configured minimum height; zone bounds are [high two bars earlier, current low]
Calculation: Bearish imbalance zone = a confirmed bar whose high is below the low two chart bars earlier, with bearish displacement and a gap at least the configured minimum height; zone bounds are [current high, low two bars earlier]
Calculation: Bullish or bearish displacement = candle body share ≥ configured minimum, range / ATR ≥ configured minimum, relative volume ≥ configured minimum, and respectively close > open or close < open
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its longer-lookback average volume
Calculation: Delta pressure = an EMA of close location within the bar × volume / average volume, scaled by 100; bullish when above its configured positive threshold and rising, bearish when below its configured negative threshold and falling
Calculation: VWAP bands = volume-weighted average price ± standard deviation of (close − VWAP) over a configured lookback, multiplied by a configured factor
Calculation: Zone score = weighted sum of zone freshness (declining linearly to zero at the configured maximum age), zone height / ATR, displacement ATR expansion, relative volume, VWAP context, delta-pressure confirmation, and rejection or repricing confirmation; the configured strictness selects the score threshold
Long: For a retained, unfailed bullish zone, price intersects the zone and either closes above its midpoint, closes above its bottom, and above its open, or closes above its midpoint, above its open, with lower-wick share above the configured rejection fraction; bullish zone score ≥ configured threshold; cooldown has elapsed; and this score exceeds the scores of all other qualifying long, short, or failure events
Short: For a retained, unfailed bearish zone, price intersects the zone and either closes below its midpoint, closes below its top, and below its open, or closes below its midpoint, below its open, with upper-wick share above the configured rejection fraction; bearish zone score ≥ configured threshold; cooldown has elapsed; and this score exceeds the scores of all other qualifying long, short, or failure events
