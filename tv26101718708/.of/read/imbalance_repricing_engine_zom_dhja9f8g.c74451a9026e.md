Calculation: Bar metrics = range is max(high - low, minimum price increment); body share is abs(close - open) / range; ATR expansion is range / max(ATR, minimum price increment); relative volume is volume / SMA(volume), or zero when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Delta pressure = EMA(((close - low) - (high - close)) / range * volume / max(SMA(volume), volume floor) * scale). Bullish delta means pressure is above a positive threshold AND above its previous value; bearish delta means pressure is below a negative threshold AND below its previous value.
Calculation: Imbalance zones = On each completed bar, create a bullish zone with top = low and bottom = high of the bar two bars earlier when low > that earlier high AND close > open AND body share, ATR expansion, relative volume, and zone height each meet their respective minimums. Create a bearish zone with top = low of the bar two bars earlier and bottom = high when high < that earlier low AND close < open AND the same minimums are met. Retain at most the configured number of zones, removing the oldest first. A zone's midpoint is (top + bottom) / 2; its age is bars since creation. A bullish zone permanently fails when close < bottom - ATR * failure buffer; a bearish zone permanently fails when close > top + ATR * failure buffer. Update failure before evaluating setups, including on the zone creation bar.
Calculation: Repricing conditions = Price intersects a zone when high >= bottom AND low <= top. Bullish rejection means intersection AND close > midpoint AND close > open AND (min(open, close) - low) / range > minimum wick share. Bearish rejection means intersection AND close < midpoint AND close < open AND (high - max(open, close)) / range > minimum wick share. Bullish touch means intersection AND close > bottom AND close > open AND (close > midpoint OR low <= midpoint). Bearish touch means intersection AND close < top AND close < open AND (close < midpoint OR high >= midpoint).
Calculation: Zone score = Sum the weighted components for freshness, zone size, ATR expansion, relative volume, VWAP context, delta, and repricing. Freshness is clamp(1 - age / maximum zone age, 0, 1). Zone size is clamp((top - bottom) / max(ATR, minimum price increment), minimum size score, maximum size score) / maximum size score. ATR expansion is clamp((ATR expansion - minimum displacement expansion) / expansion scale, 0, 1). Relative volume is clamp((relative volume - volume baseline) / volume scale, 0, 1). VWAP is session VWAP of typical price; its bands are VWAP plus or minus a multiplier times the rolling standard deviation of close - VWAP. When VWAP scoring is disabled, use a fixed neutral context score. Otherwise, for a bullish zone use the above-VWAP score if close >= VWAP, the lower-band score if low <= lower band, and the remaining score otherwise; for a bearish zone use the below-VWAP score if close <= VWAP, the upper-band score if high >= upper band, and the remaining score otherwise. Add the delta weight if the zone's corresponding delta condition holds. Add the rejection weight if its rejection condition holds, otherwise the touch weight if its touch condition holds, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Winning event = On every bar, scan retained zones from oldest to newest with best score initially zero. A bullish or bearish repricing candidate requires its zone not to have failed, its corresponding rejection OR touch condition, its score >= the configured signal threshold, and cooldown eligibility. A failure candidate requires the zone to have newly failed on this bar, age > minimum failure age, relative volume >= minimum displacement relative volume, and cooldown eligibility. Cooldown is eligible if no event has occurred yet OR bars since the last winning event > cooldown length. Replace the current winner only when a candidate's zone score > the current best score; for a failure winner set best score to max(zone score, signal threshold), otherwise set it to the zone score. Any winning repricing or failure event resets the shared cooldown. A failure event does not itself indicate a trade direction.
Long: Winning event = bullish repricing.
Short: Winning event = bearish repricing.
