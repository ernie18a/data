Calculation: Bullish alignment count = number of true conditions among: RSI below its oversold threshold AND rising from the previous bar; MACD histogram above zero OR MACD line crosses above its signal line; Williams %R below its oversold threshold AND rising from the previous bar; current close below the lower Bollinger Band OR previous close below the previous lower Bollinger Band. MACD uses a shorter and a longer moving-average length and a signal length.
Calculation: Bearish alignment count = number of true conditions among: RSI above its overbought threshold AND falling from the previous bar; MACD histogram below zero OR MACD line crosses below its signal line; Williams %R above its overbought threshold AND falling from the previous bar; current close above the upper Bollinger Band OR previous close above the previous upper Bollinger Band.
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = close > open AND close - open > upper-wick multiplier × (high - close) AND open - low > range multiplier × (high - low).
Calculation: Bearish reversal candle = close < open AND open - close > lower-wick multiplier × (close - low) AND high - open > range multiplier × (high - low).
Calculation: Bullish signal = bullish alignment count at least the entry threshold AND divergence requirement disabled AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle requirement disabled OR bullish reversal candle) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within the configured session).
Calculation: Bearish signal = bearish alignment count at least the entry threshold AND divergence requirement disabled AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle requirement disabled OR bearish reversal candle) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within the configured session).
Long: (flip disabled AND bullish signal) OR (flip enabled AND bearish signal).
Short: (flip disabled AND bearish signal) OR (flip enabled AND bullish signal).
Long: bullish alignment count at least the setup threshold AND the Long signal above is false AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within the configured session).
Short: bearish alignment count at least the setup threshold AND the Short signal above is false AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within the configured session).
Exit: long, stop enabled AND low at or below the signal-bar close × (1 - stop percentage) OR target enabled AND high at or above the signal-bar close × (1 + target percentage) OR an executable Short signal occurs.
Exit: short, stop enabled AND high at or above the signal-bar close × (1 + stop percentage) OR target enabled AND low at or below the signal-bar close × (1 - target percentage) OR an executable Long signal occurs.
