Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA; trend EMA uses a longer lookback than both.
Calculation: Buy score = count of these true conditions: close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA; fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR MACD line crosses above its signal line; RSI > buy threshold AND RSI < upper bound; volume > its moving average × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Sell score = count of these true conditions: close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA; fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR MACD line crosses below its signal line; RSI < sell threshold AND RSI > lower bound; volume > its moving average × volume multiplier.
Calculation: For each configured timeframe, calculate its trend from that timeframe's close, fast EMA, slow EMA, and trend EMA. Use requested-timeframe values with lookahead off: the last completed requested-timeframe bar historically and the forming requested-timeframe bar in real time. Configured timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR MACD line crosses above its signal line OR (close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close <= fast EMA AND close > slow EMA)) AND buy score >= 2 AND (multi-timeframe agreement disabled OR first timeframe trend is neutral or bullish AND every other configured timeframe trend is bullish) AND (buy score >= 3 OR previous signal direction is not long)
Short: (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR MACD line crosses below its signal line OR (close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close >= fast EMA AND close < slow EMA)) AND sell score >= 2 AND (multi-timeframe agreement disabled OR first timeframe trend is neutral or bearish AND every other configured timeframe trend is bearish) AND (sell score >= 3 OR previous signal direction is not short)
