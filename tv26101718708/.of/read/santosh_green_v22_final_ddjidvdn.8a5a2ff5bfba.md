Calculation: Higher-timeframe HMA = HMA calculated on the chart-timeframe series of higher-timeframe closes; each chart bar uses the last completed higher-timeframe close, updated on the closing chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar.
Long: For any enabled higher-timeframe HMA, its value > its previous chart-bar value.
Short: For any enabled higher-timeframe HMA, its value <= its previous chart-bar value.
Long: The shorter-period close EMA > the longer-period close EMA, when EMA confluence is enabled.
Short: The shorter-period close EMA <= the longer-period close EMA, when EMA confluence is enabled.
Long: The shorter-period close EMA crosses above the longer-period close EMA AND every enabled higher-timeframe HMA > its previous chart-bar value AND (EMA confluence is disabled OR the shorter-period EMA > the longer-period EMA) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI < the long RSI threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price × VWAP multiplier) AND (MACD filter is disabled OR MACD line > signal line) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND (price-action filter is disabled OR (high > previous high AND low > previous low)) AND position is flat AND (all-day trading is enabled OR bar opening time in New York is within any enabled entry window, including both endpoints) AND bar opening time in New York != configured exit time.
Short: The shorter-period close EMA crosses below the longer-period close EMA AND every enabled higher-timeframe HMA < its previous chart-bar value AND (EMA confluence is disabled OR the shorter-period EMA < the longer-period EMA) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI > the short RSI threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price × VWAP multiplier) AND (MACD filter is disabled OR MACD line < signal line) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND (price-action filter is disabled OR (high < previous high AND low < previous low)) AND position is flat AND (all-day trading is enabled OR bar opening time in New York is within any enabled entry window, including both endpoints) AND bar opening time in New York != configured exit time.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + profit-target price distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss price distance OR low <= previous bar's close - previous bar's ATR × ATR multiplier OR (timed exit is enabled AND bar opening time in New York = configured exit time).
Exit: short, low <= entry price - profit-target price distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss price distance OR high >= previous bar's close + previous bar's ATR × ATR multiplier OR (timed exit is enabled AND bar opening time in New York = configured exit time).
