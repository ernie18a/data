Calculation: Hull moving average = weighted moving average of [2 × weighted moving average of close over half the lookback − weighted moving average of close over the full lookback], over the square root of the lookback. For each active higher timeframe, calculate this from its close series mapped to the chart timeframe using only the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND every active higher-timeframe Hull moving average is higher than its prior value AND (EMA confluence is disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI < its long threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > VWAP × its multiplier) AND (MACD filter is disabled OR MACD line > signal line) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (price-action filter is disabled OR high > prior high AND low > prior low) AND (run-all-day is enabled OR current time is within a configured entry window) AND position is flat AND current time is not 16:45 New York time.
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND every active higher-timeframe Hull moving average is lower than its prior value AND (EMA confluence is disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI > its short threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < VWAP × its multiplier) AND (MACD filter is disabled OR MACD line < signal line) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (price-action filter is disabled OR high < prior high AND low < prior low) AND (run-all-day is enabled OR current time is within a configured entry window) AND position is flat AND current time is not 16:45 New York time.
Exit: Long, high reaches the fixed profit target from entry price OR low reaches the fixed stop-loss from entry price OR low reaches close minus ATR times its multiplier OR the 16:45 New York exit is enabled and current time is 16:45.
Exit: Short, low reaches the fixed profit target from entry price OR high reaches the fixed stop-loss from entry price OR high reaches close plus ATR times its multiplier OR the 16:45 New York exit is enabled and current time is 16:45.
