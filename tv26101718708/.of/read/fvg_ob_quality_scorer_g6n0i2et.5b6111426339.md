Calculation: Structure direction = initially neutral; track the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows, using the same number of bars on either side for confirmation. On a newly confirmed pivot high, if both pairs exist, set bullish when the new high is higher and the latest low is higher; otherwise set bearish when the new high is lower. Then, on a newly confirmed pivot low, if both pairs exist, set bearish when the new low is lower and the latest high is lower; otherwise set bullish when the new low is higher. Otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Zone candidate = on a bullish gap, either the interval from high two bars ago to current low, or the high-low interval of the most recent bearish candle found from two bars ago through the order-block scan depth; on a bearish gap, either the interval from current high to low two bars ago, or the high-low interval of the most recent bullish candle found over the same scan. A gap candidate uses the candle two bars ago as its sweep start and the preceding candle for volume; an order-block candidate uses its opposing candle for both. The preceding candle is the displacement candle for both types.
Calculation: Zone score = the sum of displacement, sweep, range position, structure, higher-timeframe bias, retracement, overlap, volume, session, and freshness scores. Each threshold, score, lookback, multiplier, and session is a parameter. Evaluate components at candidate creation and add a candidate to the active list only when its score reaches the display threshold. Process gap candidates before order-block candidates on each bar.
Calculation: Displacement score = add tiered scores for the displacement candle's absolute body divided by its range and its range divided by current ATR, using descending thresholds and the first matched tier; use zero for a zero denominator. Add a confirmation score if bullish and displacement close exceeds the preceding candle's open while that preceding candle is bearish, or if bearish and displacement close is below the preceding candle's open while that preceding candle is bullish. Cap the sum at its maximum.
Calculation: Sweep score = scan backward from the candidate's sweep start through a parameterized number of bars. At each bar, in order, test whether its low is below the latest confirmed pivot low, below the last completed higher-timeframe reference period's low, or within an ATR multiple of the pivot low for a bullish candidate; reverse low/below to high/above for a bearish candidate. Assign the corresponding score at the first match and stop. Separately add a capped bonus if any scanned low is at or below the current rolling lowest low, or any scanned high is at or above the current rolling highest high, respectively. The rolling extrema use chart bars and a separate lookback.
Calculation: Range-position score = locate the zone midpoint as a fraction of the current chart-bar rolling high-low range and assign a tiered score using ascending thresholds for bullish candidates and descending thresholds for bearish candidates; use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Structure score = assign the aligned, neutral, or opposed score according to structure direction and candidate direction. Higher-timeframe bias score = assign the aligned, neutral, or opposed score according to whether the last completed higher-timeframe bar closes above, at, or below its open and the candidate direction.
Calculation: Retracement score = construct a parameterized retracement band and target within the current rolling high-low range, measured down from the high for bullish candidates and up from the low for bearish candidates. Score the zone midpoint by whether it is inside the band and within a parameterized fraction of its width from the target, elsewhere inside the band, within an ATR-multiple extension of the band, or outside; use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Overlap score = scan active, unmitigated zones in insertion order for the first zone of the opposite type and same direction with positive price overlap. Divide the overlap width by the candidate width and assign a tiered score using descending thresholds; otherwise use zero.
Calculation: Volume score = divide the candidate's selected candle volume by the current chart-bar volume SMA and assign a tiered score using descending thresholds; use zero when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Session score = assign the score of the first matching parameterized New York time session in this priority: morning, London, either precision window, afternoon, Asian, otherwise outside sessions. Freshness score = the new-zone score at creation.
Calculation: Active zones = after creating candidates, mark a bullish zone mitigated when current low or close, according to the selected mitigation mode, is at or below its bottom; mark a bearish zone mitigated when current high or close is at or above its top. Exclude mitigated zones from overlap. After management, if the stored zone count exceeds its limit, remove the oldest zone.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND bullish gap-zone score >= display threshold.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND bearish gap-zone score >= display threshold.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND a bearish candle exists within the order-block scan AND bullish order-block score >= display threshold.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND a bullish candle exists within the order-block scan AND bearish order-block score >= display threshold.
Long: Structure direction = bullish.
Short: Structure direction = bearish.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe bar close > open.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe bar close < open.
