Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within a configured New York time window; reset on each exchange-calendar day change, and mark the range built on the first subsequent bar outside the window. Range size = range high minus range low.
Calculation: Breakout state = for each direction, count consecutive closes strictly beyond the corresponding range boundary while the range is built and the configured New York cutoff has not been reached. If the previous bar's count is at least the confirmation parameter and that direction's breakout is inactive, activate it and record the current bar index. After activation, deactivate an upward breakout when close < range high, or a downward breakout when close > range low, provided no trade is open and the cutoff has not been reached; also reset its count and recorded index. Reset both directions on each exchange-calendar day change.
Calculation: Entry price = range high for a long retest, range low for a short retest, or signal-bar close for a fallback. Evaluate both entry signals before updating position state; mark each signaled direction as traded for the day and reset these marks and position state on each exchange-calendar day change.
Calculation: Breakeven state = active once close minus entry price for a long, or entry price minus close for a short, reaches the configured profit threshold; evaluate from the entry bar onward and reset on exit.
Calculation: Trailing stop = from the first bar with breakeven active, the running maximum of close minus an ATR multiple for a long, or running minimum of close plus an ATR multiple for a short; include the current bar and reset on exit.
Long: Range built AND upward breakout active AND no long traded this day AND no trade open AND New York time before cutoff AND (low <= range high + retest buffer OR fallback enabled AND low > range high + retest buffer AND bars since upward breakout activation >= fallback wait).
Short: Range built AND downward breakout active AND no short traded this day AND no trade open AND New York time before cutoff AND (high >= range low - retest buffer OR fallback enabled AND high < range low - retest buffer AND bars since downward breakout activation >= fallback wait).
Exit: long, (breakeven inactive AND low <= range low) OR (breakeven active AND low <= max(trailing stop, entry price)) OR (range size < size threshold AND high >= range high + range size * target multiple) OR New York time >= cutoff.
Exit: short, (breakeven inactive AND high >= range high) OR (breakeven active AND high >= min(trailing stop, entry price)) OR (range size < size threshold AND low <= range low - range size * target multiple) OR New York time >= cutoff.
