Calculation: Opening Range High/Low = highest high and lowest low during the opening range window; Opening Range Size = Opening Range High − Opening Range Low
Calculation: Long Trailing Stop = highest prior Long Trailing Stop or (close − ATR × configured multiplier), whichever is greater
Calculation: Short Trailing Stop = lowest prior Short Trailing Stop or (close + ATR × configured multiplier), whichever is lower
Long: opening range is complete AND breakout is confirmed by the configured number of consecutive closes above Opening Range High AND breakout has not been reset by a close below Opening Range High AND no trade is open AND no long trade has been taken today AND before session end AND (low ≤ Opening Range High + configured retest buffer OR (fallback is enabled AND no retest occurs AND bars since breakout ≥ configured fallback wait))
Short: opening range is complete AND breakout is confirmed by the configured number of consecutive closes below Opening Range Low AND breakout has not been reset by a close above Opening Range Low AND no trade is open AND no short trade has been taken today AND before session end AND (high ≥ Opening Range Low − configured retest buffer OR (fallback is enabled AND no retest occurs AND bars since breakout ≥ configured fallback wait))
Exit: long, low ≤ Opening Range Low while breakeven has not been reached OR low ≤ max(Long Trailing Stop, entry price) after breakeven is reached OR (Opening Range Size is below the configured threshold AND high ≥ Opening Range High + Opening Range Size × configured reward-to-risk multiple) OR session end
Exit: short, high ≥ Opening Range High while breakeven has not been reached OR high ≥ min(Short Trailing Stop, entry price) after breakeven is reached OR (Opening Range Size is below the configured threshold AND low ≤ Opening Range Low − Opening Range Size × configured reward-to-risk multiple) OR session end
