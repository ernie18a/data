Calculation: Source bars = chart bars at close OR bars from either of two independently evaluated higher timeframes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and emitting its decisions once on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe period.
Calculation: Available swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low confirmed using the same number of source bars on each side; a newly confirmed pivot replaces the previous level and becomes available, and a sweep consumes its availability. Pivot confirmation and availability updates occur before sweep detection on each source bar.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = no active bearish setup AND an available swing high exists AND the lowest low of the preceding structure-lookback source bars exists AND high >= available swing high + sweep-penetration multiplier * ATR; use zero for ATR in this comparison if ATR is unavailable. On a sweep, save the swing high, the preceding structure-lookback lowest low and the source-bar index, and activate the bearish setup.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = no active bullish setup AND an available swing low exists AND the highest high of the preceding structure-lookback source bars exists AND low <= available swing low - sweep-penetration multiplier * ATR; use zero for ATR in this comparison if ATR is unavailable. On a sweep, save the swing low, the preceding structure-lookback highest high and the source-bar index, and activate the bullish setup.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation = an active bearish setup AND close has been below its saved swing high on at least one source bar since and including the sweep bar AND close < its saved lowest low AND ATR is available AND abs(close - open) >= displacement multiplier * ATR. Evaluate on the sweep bar and each subsequent source bar; confirmation deactivates the setup. If unconfirmed, deactivate it after evaluation when elapsed source bars since the sweep > maximum confirmation bars.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = an active bullish setup AND close has been above its saved swing low on at least one source bar since and including the sweep bar AND close > its saved highest high AND ATR is available AND abs(close - open) >= displacement multiplier * ATR. Evaluate on the sweep bar and each subsequent source bar; confirmation deactivates the setup. If unconfirmed, deactivate it after evaluation when elapsed source bars since the sweep > maximum confirmation bars.
Long: bullish sweep OR bullish confirmation
Short: bearish sweep OR bearish confirmation
