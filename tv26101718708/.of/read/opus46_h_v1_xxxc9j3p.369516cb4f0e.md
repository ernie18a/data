Calculation: Fast and slow smoothed moving averages = SMMAs of the same price source, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: ATR-normalized spread = (fast SMMA − slow SMMA) / ATR
Calculation: Spread z-score = (ATR-normalized spread − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation
Calculation: Learned buy and sell bands = prior-bar Donchian low minus ATR × learned buy penetration multiplier, and prior-bar Donchian high plus ATR × learned sell penetration multiplier; each multiplier is the sensitivity-scaled mean of that side’s observed positive Donchian penetrations, clamped to its configured bounds, or the configured default until enough observations exist
Calculation: Pressure z-score = z-score of (close’s percentage distance from a volume-weighted cost basis × volume / 20-bar average volume); cost basis weights the shorter rolling VWAP more heavily than the longer rolling VWAP
Calculation: Bullish divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while either ATR-normalized spread or RSI makes a higher value at that pivot; bearish divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while either indicator makes a lower value at that pivot
Long: low ≤ learned buy band AND fast SMMA < slow SMMA AND (bear-regime age ≥ configured minimum OR bear-regime maximum absolute ATR-normalized spread ≥ configured minimum depth) AND (pain gate disabled OR pressure z-score reached the configured capitulation threshold within its lookback) OR spread z-score ≤ configured buy extreme; apply enabled higher-timeframe band gate using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, buy cooldown, buy-count limit, breaker, lower-low-since-last-buy filter, and minimum ATR separation from last buy; OR spread z-score enters the configured buy extreme OR capitulation becomes recent OR bullish divergence
Short: spread z-score enters the configured sell extreme OR euphoria becomes recent OR bearish divergence
Exit: long, high ≥ learned sell band AND fast SMMA ≥ slow SMMA AND (bear-regime age ≥ configured minimum OR bull-regime maximum absolute ATR-normalized spread ≥ configured minimum depth) AND (pain gate disabled OR pressure z-score reached the configured euphoria threshold within its lookback) AND higher high since last sell AND minimum ATR separation from last sell AND sell cooldown AND sell-count limit AND sell throttle permits; OR spread z-score ≥ configured sell extreme; apply enabled higher-timeframe band gate using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
