Calculation: Ribbon spread = 100 × (shorter-period SMMA − longer-period SMMA) / ATR, using the midpoint of high and low as the SMMA source; use zero when ATR is zero. SMMA starts with an SMA and subsequently equals (previous SMMA × (period − 1) + source) / period.
Calculation: Spread z-score = (ribbon spread − its rolling SMA) / its rolling standard deviation; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Pressure z-score = the rolling z-score of ((close − cost basis) / cost basis × 100) × (volume / volume SMA). Cost basis is a weighted average of a longer-lookback and a shorter-lookback rolling volume-weighted typical price, with the shorter lookback receiving the greater weight. Use typical price when a rolling volume sum is zero, zero margin when cost basis is zero, a volume ratio of one when volume SMA is zero, and a z-score of zero when its standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Lower and upper penetration bands = the previous-bar rolling lowest low minus ATR × lower multiplier, and the previous-bar rolling highest high plus ATR × upper multiplier. Maintain separate rolling lists of the most recent positive lower and upper penetrations, measured in ATR units; include the current bar's penetration before calculating its band. Each multiplier is initially a default and, once its list reaches a minimum sample count, is the list average multiplied by a sensitivity factor and clamped between minimum and maximum multipliers. Higher-timeframe bands use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Regime age = consecutive chart bars in the current ribbon regime; regime depth = the maximum absolute ribbon spread during that regime divided by 100.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND either ribbon spread or RSI at the new pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot. Bearish divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND either ribbon spread or RSI at the new pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot. Confirm each pivot only after its specified number of right-hand bars.
Calculation: Double-pass money flow = SMA(2 × SMA(MFI of typical price) − RSI(SMA(MFI of typical price))); substitute neutral MFI or RSI when unavailable and zero for an unavailable final SMA.
