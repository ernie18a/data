Calculation: Zones = On each newly completed higher-timeframe bar, rebuild from a rolling history of completed higher-timeframe highs and lows. A pivot high is at least as high as all highs within a symmetric lookback; a pivot low is at most as low as all lows within the same lookback. Process bars newest first, pivot high before pivot low. Merge each pivot into the first existing cluster whose center is within a tolerance equal to a multiple of the latest completed higher-timeframe ATR; otherwise create a cluster if the cluster limit has not been reached. Each cluster stores its pivot count, arithmetic mean, minimum and maximum. Retain clusters meeting a minimum count, and set their lower and upper edges to the minimum of the pivot minimum and center minus padding, and the maximum of the pivot maximum and center plus padding. Padding is a fraction of the clustering tolerance.
Calculation: Key levels = Enabled prior completed higher-timeframe high, low and close; enabled prior completed longer higher-timeframe high and low; and enabled last completed higher-timeframe simple moving averages of a selected price source, using short, intermediate and long lookbacks. Refresh the stored levels when either the zone timeframe or the shorter key-level timeframe completes a bar.
Calculation: Opposing distances = For each eligible zone outside the current close, use its nearest edge; also use each stored key level. The distance above is the smallest level strictly above close minus close; the distance below is close minus the largest level strictly below close. A key level equal to close is ignored. A missing opposing level gives an infinite distance.
Calculation: Risk unit = A selected fixed price distance or a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR; if the result is unavailable or nonpositive, use the instrument's minimum price increment.
Long: Close is outside every eligible zone AND (higher-timeframe ATR is unavailable OR distance above >= a multiple of the last completed higher-timeframe ATR) AND distance above / risk unit >= minimum room.
Short: Close is outside every eligible zone AND (higher-timeframe ATR is unavailable OR distance below >= a multiple of the last completed higher-timeframe ATR) AND distance below / risk unit >= minimum room.
