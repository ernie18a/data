Calculation: Source = a selectable OHLC-derived price, defaulting to OHLC4.
Calculation: Tracker bands and direction = on the chart timeframe or a higher scan timeframe, using the last completed scan bar, calculate a short EMA and a longer EMA of source; use the short EMA as the basis in the reactive mode, or their mean in the smoother mode. Add and subtract a multiple of ATR, optionally calculated as the SMA of true range, to form upper and lower bands. Initialize direction as long and tracker as the lower band. Thereafter set direction long if source exceeds the previous upper band, short if source is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain the previous direction. In long direction, tracker is the maximum of the current lower band and the previous tracker if the previous direction was long, otherwise the current lower band. In short direction, tracker is the minimum of the current upper band and the previous tracker if the previous direction was short, otherwise the current upper band.
Calculation: Freshness = number of scan bars since the latest direction change; undefined before the first change.
Calculation: Tracker distance = absolute difference between source and tracker divided by ATR; use zero if ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA on the scan timeframe; use zero if the SMA is unavailable or not positive.
Calculation: Trade window score = for a continuously open market, capped relative volume scaled to a maximum score; otherwise add a capped relative-volume score to a session score selected by the first matching local-time interval in this order: London–New York overlap, New York cash open, London open, New York power hour, New York pre-open, New York mid-session, or other time; cap the result at the maximum score. Session intervals, caps, scales, and scores are parameters.
Long: Scan direction is long AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, direction on the scan timeframe or a higher timeframe is long, using its last completed bar AND, if fresh-only display is enabled, freshness is at most the freshness limit.
Short: Scan direction is short AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, direction on the scan timeframe or a higher timeframe is short, using its last completed bar AND, if fresh-only display is enabled, freshness is at most the freshness limit.
Long: Scan direction changes from short to long AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, higher-timeframe direction is long AND, if window gating is enabled and the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold AND, if action gating is enabled, relative volume is at least its threshold AND tracker distance is at most its threshold AND scan-timeframe RSI is at most the long RSI limit AND, if the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold.
Short: Scan direction changes from long to short AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, higher-timeframe direction is short AND, if window gating is enabled and the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold AND, if action gating is enabled, relative volume is at least its threshold AND tracker distance is at most its threshold AND scan-timeframe RSI is at least the short RSI limit AND, if the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold.
Long: Scan direction is long AND freshness is at most the freshness limit AND relative volume is at least its threshold AND tracker distance is at most its threshold AND scan-timeframe RSI is at most the long RSI limit AND, if the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, higher-timeframe direction is long.
Short: Scan direction is short AND freshness is at most the freshness limit AND relative volume is at least its threshold AND tracker distance is at most its threshold AND scan-timeframe RSI is at least the short RSI limit AND, if the window is displayed, trade window score is at least its threshold AND, if higher-timeframe filtering is enabled, higher-timeframe direction is short.
