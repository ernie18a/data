Calculation: A = Kalman-filtered Hull moving average of a selectable OHLC-derived source. The Hull moving average is WMA(2 × WMA(source, length/2) − WMA(source, length), round(sqrt(length))).
Calculation: B = Kalman-filtered WMA(3 × WMA(close, p/3) − WMA(close, p/2) − WMA(close, p), p), where p is half A's Hull lookback. For both A and B, apply independent Kalman filters with the same gain: delta = input − previous filtered value; velocity = previous velocity + gain/10000 × delta; filtered value = previous filtered value + delta × sqrt(2 × gain/10000) + velocity. Initialize the previous filtered value to the current input and previous velocity to zero.
Calculation: Long and short WaveTrend pairs = independently parameterized calculations: basis = EMA(selectable OHLC-derived source, channel length); deviation = EMA(abs(source − basis), channel length); fast = EMA((source − basis)/(0.015 × deviation), average length); slow = SMA(fast, 4).
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA(stochastic of RSI of a selectable OHLC-derived source, K smoothing length); D = SMA(K, D smoothing length).
Calculation: Volume threshold = multiplier × (SMA(volume) + VWMA(volume) + WMA(volume))/3, with independently parameterized averaging lengths; VWMA(volume) uses volume as both source and weight.
Calculation: Long and short profit levels = entry price × (1 + long profit percentage) and entry price × (1 − short profit percentage), respectively, with separately configured levels 1, 2 and 3 for each side. Long and short position-check levels = entry price × (1 + long level-1 percentage/long sensitivity) and entry price × (1 − short level-1 percentage/short sensitivity), respectively.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price × (1 − long stop percentage), except on a bar where long trailing is enabled and the current or previous high reaches the selected activation level, when it is max(close × (1 − long trailing percentage), previous long stop). Short stop = entry price × (1 + short stop percentage), except on a bar where short trailing is enabled and the current or previous low reaches the selected activation level, when it is min(close × (1 + short trailing percentage), previous short stop). The activation level is independently selectable for each side as its level-1 profit level or enabled position-check level. Revert to the fixed stop whenever the activation condition is false.
Long: Long entries enabled AND position is flat or long AND datetime is within the configured date range AND the optional long session and weekday restrictions pass AND B > A AND previous B < previous A AND (long WaveTrend fast crosses above slow now OR crossed above slow on the previous bar OR long WaveTrend fast from two bars ago crosses above current long WaveTrend slow) AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K > D) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume threshold) AND (ADX filter enabled AND ADX > configured threshold OR ADX filter disabled AND ADX < 100).
Short: Short entries enabled AND position is flat or short AND datetime is within the configured date range AND the optional short session and weekday restrictions pass AND A > B AND previous A < previous B AND short WaveTrend fast crosses below slow AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K < D) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume threshold) AND (ADX filter enabled AND ADX > configured threshold OR ADX filter disabled AND ADX < 100).
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured date range AND low reaches long stop OR high reaches long profit level 1; level 1 exits a configured percentage if level 2 is enabled, otherwise the entire position.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured date range AND level 2 enabled AND (low reaches long stop OR high reaches long profit level 2); level 2 exits a configured percentage if level 3 is enabled, otherwise the entire remaining position.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured date range AND level 3 enabled AND (low reaches long stop OR high reaches long profit level 3), exiting the entire remaining position.
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured date range AND high reaches short stop OR low reaches short profit level 1; level 1 exits a configured percentage if level 2 is enabled, otherwise the entire position.
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured date range AND level 2 enabled AND (high reaches short stop OR low reaches short profit level 2); level 2 exits a configured percentage if level 3 is enabled, otherwise the entire remaining position.
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured date range AND level 3 enabled AND (high reaches short stop OR low reaches short profit level 3), exiting the entire remaining position.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured date range AND ((high ≤ entry price OR high ≥ enabled long position-check level) AND A > B AND previous A < previous B AND short WaveTrend fast crosses below slow AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K < D) OR high ≥ enabled long position-check level AND selectable long check source < long position-check level AND (long WaveTrend fast crosses below slow OR short WaveTrend fast crosses below slow OR A > B AND (long WaveTrend fast < slow OR short WaveTrend fast < slow) AND volume filter enabled AND volume ≤ volume threshold OR A > B AND previous A < previous B) OR high ≥ long profit level 1 AND selectable long level-1 source ≤ long profit level 1 OR high ≥ long profit level 2 AND (selectable long level-2 source ≤ long profit level 2 OR A > B)).
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured date range AND ((low ≥ entry price OR low ≤ enabled short position-check level) AND B > A AND previous B < previous A AND (long WaveTrend fast crosses above slow now OR crossed above slow on the previous bar OR long WaveTrend fast from two bars ago crosses above current long WaveTrend slow) AND (Stochastic RSI filter disabled OR K > D) OR low ≤ enabled short position-check level AND selectable short check source > short position-check level AND (short WaveTrend fast crosses above slow OR long WaveTrend fast crosses above slow OR A < B AND (short WaveTrend fast > slow OR long WaveTrend fast > slow) AND volume filter enabled AND volume ≤ volume threshold OR B > A AND previous B < previous A) OR low ≤ short profit level 1 AND selectable short level-1 source ≥ short profit level 1 OR low ≤ short profit level 2 AND (selectable short level-2 source ≥ short profit level 2 OR A < B)).
