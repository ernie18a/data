Calculation: Structure pivots = pivot highs and lows on the configured structure timeframe, each requiring the same lookback on both sides; the lookback is selected by the chart-timeframe setting.
Calculation: Structure break level = the active swing high or low. A break of the swing high is bullish; a break of the swing low is bearish. A break in the existing trend is continuation (BOS); a break against it is a shift (MSS). After a break, switch the trend state and use the latest opposite pivot as the active swing. Test bullish breaks with chart high in a bullish trend and the last completed structure-timeframe close otherwise; test bearish breaks with the last completed structure-timeframe close in a bullish trend and chart low otherwise. Structure-timeframe pivots use that configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Current close > the latest bullish BOS level (or MSS level, as configured) AND previous close <= that level AND current close > the simple moving average of highs AND current close > the simple moving average of lows AND the prior signal state is not long
Short: Current close < the latest bearish BOS level (or MSS level, as configured) AND previous close >= that level AND current close < the simple moving average of highs AND current close < the simple moving average of lows AND the prior signal state is not short
Calculation: Signal state = set to long after a long signal, short after a short signal, and neutral when close is strictly between the simple moving averages of highs and lows.
