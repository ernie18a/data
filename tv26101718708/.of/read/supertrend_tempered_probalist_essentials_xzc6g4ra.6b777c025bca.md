Calculation: Tempered Supertrend bands and trend = Calculate Wilder ATR with a shorter lookback and a longer lookback. Scale the base band multiplier by the shorter-lookback ATR divided by the longer-lookback ATR, clamped to configured bounds. Around the bar midpoint, place the raw upper and lower bands one scaled ATR multiple above and below. Ratchet the upper band down while the previous close has not broken above it, otherwise reset it to the raw upper band; ratchet the lower band up while the previous close has not broken below it, otherwise reset it to the raw lower band. The trend turns bullish when the current close is above the previous upper band and bearish when it is below the previous lower band; otherwise it retains its previous direction.
Long: Previous trend was bearish AND current close > previous upper band
Short: Previous trend was bullish AND current close < previous lower band
