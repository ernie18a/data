Calculation: Order block = On each confirmed bar in the configured historical processing window, let N be the breadth lookback and let the anchor be N−1 bars ago. Create a bullish block if (N > 1 AND the anchor close < open AND every subsequent close through the current bar >= open) OR (the bar N bars ago has high − low > its ATR × an alternative-pattern multiplier AND close > open AND low < the preceding bar’s low). Create a bearish block if (N > 1 AND the anchor close > open AND every subsequent close through the current bar <= open) OR (the bar N bars ago has high − low > its ATR × the same multiplier AND close < open AND high > the preceding bar’s high). In full mode, either block’s top and bottom are the anchor high and low. In breadth mode, a bullish block’s top is the anchor high and its bottom is min(anchor low + current ATR × a breadth multiplier, anchor high); a bearish block’s bottom is the anchor low and its top is max(anchor high − current ATR × the same breadth multiplier, anchor low). Store the anchor volume. A new block removes all overlapping older blocks of the same direction. Before trade selection, remove bullish blocks when min(open, close) < bottom in close mitigation mode or low < bottom in wick mitigation mode; remove bearish blocks when max(open, close) > top in close mitigation mode or high > top in wick mitigation mode. Then limit each direction to the configured maximum number of blocks by removing the oldest anchors first. Block creation and mitigation precede trade selection on the same bar.
Calculation: Eligible block = An active block whose anchor volume divided by the current SMA of volume is >= a minimum strength, with strength treated as zero when the SMA is not positive, AND whose trigger is satisfied. In touch mode, the trigger is bottom <= close <= top. In rejection mode, the bullish trigger is low <= bottom AND close >= bottom; the bearish trigger is high >= top AND close <= top. Select the eligible block with greatest strength for each direction, breaking ties in favor of the most recently created block.
Calculation: Trade levels = At entry, save entry price as close. The stop is the selected bullish block’s bottom for a long or selected bearish block’s top for a short in block-edge mode; in ATR mode it is entry price minus ATR × a stop multiplier for a long or entry price plus ATR × the same multiplier for a short. Risk is the absolute difference between entry price and stop. The target is entry price plus risk × a reward multiplier for a long or entry price minus risk × the same multiplier for a short. Keep these levels fixed until exit; if stop and target are both hit on one bar, use the stop as the exit price.
Long: A bullish order block is created.
Short: A bearish order block is created.
Long: The trade engine is enabled AND the bar is confirmed AND position side is flat AND an eligible bullish block exists.
Short: The trade engine is enabled AND the bar is confirmed AND position side is flat AND an eligible bearish block exists AND no eligible bullish block exists.
Exit: long, the trade engine is enabled AND the current bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= target OR low <= stop).
Exit: short, the trade engine is enabled AND the current bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= target OR high >= stop).
