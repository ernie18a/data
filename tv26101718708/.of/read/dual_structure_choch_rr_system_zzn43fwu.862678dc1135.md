Calculation: Internal pivot high/low = local highest high/lowest low confirmed after an equal, shorter lookback on each side; retain the latest confirmed high and low, and reset each level’s break eligibility when it updates.
Calculation: Swing pivot high/low = local highest high/lowest low confirmed after an equal, longer lookback on each side than the internal pivot; retain the latest confirmed high and low, and reset each level’s break eligibility when it updates.
Long: (internal structure is bearish AND its latest pivot high has not been broken AND (close crosses above that pivot high in close-confirmation mode OR high crosses above it while the prior bar’s high was at or below it in wick-confirmation mode)) OR (swing structure is bearish AND its latest pivot high has not been broken AND (close crosses above that pivot high in close-confirmation mode OR high crosses above it while the prior bar’s high was at or below it in wick-confirmation mode))
Short: (internal structure is bullish AND its latest pivot low has not been broken AND (close crosses below that pivot low in close-confirmation mode OR low crosses below it while the prior bar’s low was at or above it in wick-confirmation mode)) OR (swing structure is bullish AND its latest pivot low has not been broken AND (close crosses below that pivot low in close-confirmation mode OR low crosses below it while the prior bar’s low was at or above it in wick-confirmation mode))
