Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = the low of the candidate bar is strictly lower than every low in the preceding pivot lookback and lower than or equal to every low in the equally long following confirmation window; the pivot is confirmed on the last bar of that window.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = the high of the candidate bar is strictly higher than every high in the preceding pivot lookback and higher than or equal to every high in the equally long following confirmation window; the pivot is confirmed on the last bar of that window.
Calculation: EMA for the trend gate = EMA of close on the chart timeframe, or on a selected higher or lower timeframe; on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, updating when that bar closes, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. On a forming chart bar, the higher-timeframe value can use the still-forming bar.
Long: position side = flat AND a pivot low is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the selected session in New York time) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > minimum ATR) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < long RSI threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA for the trend gate)
Short: position side = flat AND a pivot high is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the selected session in New York time) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > minimum ATR) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > short RSI threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA for the trend gate)
Long: RSI display enabled AND RSI < long RSI threshold
Short: RSI display enabled AND RSI >= long RSI threshold AND RSI > short RSI threshold
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + profit target distance OR (high since entry >= entry price + trailing activation distance AND price <= highest price since trailing activation - trailing offset)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - profit target distance OR (low since entry <= entry price - trailing activation distance AND price >= lowest price since trailing activation + trailing offset)
