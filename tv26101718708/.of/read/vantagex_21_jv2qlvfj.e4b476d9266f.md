Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 from the selected London or New York session start
Calculation: Previous-session close position = (previous New York regular-session close − session low) / (session high − session low)
Calculation: Rolling flow breakout = after a compressed, volume-concentrated base over a lookback, price breaks and closes beyond the stored base high or low with current bar range above its average-range threshold
Calculation: Previous-session flow = classify the previous New York regular session as bullish or bearish when its close-to-open direction agrees with its body-to-range efficiency threshold and it is not a compressed, high-volume base
Long: close > open OR close > session VWAP OR RSI > 50 OR smoothed Stochastic RSI %K > %D OR rolling flow breakout is bullish OR previous-session flow is bullish OR previous-session close position > its bullish threshold OR close > EMA (each of the 9, 20, 50, and 200 period EMAs on the chart timeframe or on any requested higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the current aggregated bar for a lower timeframe) OR shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA (each displayed pair on the chart timeframe)
Short: close < open OR close < session VWAP OR RSI < 50 OR smoothed Stochastic RSI %K < %D OR rolling flow breakout is bearish OR previous-session flow is bearish OR previous-session close position < its bearish threshold OR close < EMA (each of the 9, 20, 50, and 200 period EMAs on the chart timeframe or on any requested higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the current aggregated bar for a lower timeframe) OR shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA (each displayed pair on the chart timeframe)
