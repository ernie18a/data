Calculation: Timeframes = evaluate each enabled price-based axis on the chart timeframe and on two higher timeframes, with the bias timeframe higher than the signal timeframe; use the previous completed bar on each timeframe, including the previous chart bar when the trigger timeframe is the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Axis votes = momentum is the sign of the MACD histogram; stretch is the sign of close minus its EMA, divided by the standard deviation of that deviation, or zero if the standard deviation is zero; flow is the sign of cumulative signed volume minus its EMA, divided by the standard deviation of that residual, or zero if the standard deviation is zero. Signed volume is volume times (2 × normal cumulative distribution of the close change divided by the standard deviation of close changes − 1); use zero for the standardized change when its standard deviation is zero. The flow normalization and close-change standard deviation may use different lookbacks. Unavailable votes are zero.
Calculation: Axis weight = when calibration is disabled, one; otherwise a weight floor plus the positive part of the axis edge lower bound, or the floor alone until enough samples resolve. On each confirmed chart bar, sample each enabled nonzero signal-timeframe vote no more often than a minimum chart-bar gap, at chart close and chart ATR; assign uniqueness weight 1 / (1 + the number of all pending axis samples), counting samples already added on that bar. Independently sample both long and short baseline events at the same gap. Resolve all events on every chart bar, including their creation bar, at the first ATR-scaled profit barrier, ATR-scaled stop barrier, or horizon; if both barriers are hit, count a loss; at timeout count a win only if the direction-adjusted close change reaches the profit barrier. Resolve baseline events before axis events. For each resolved axis event, update its decayed uniqueness-weighted sample and win sums, and add the current resolved baseline win rate for that event's direction to an undecayed baseline sum, using one half when no baseline event has resolved. The edge lower bound is the decayed weighted win rate minus the Wilson interval half-width computed from the decayed weighted win and sample sums, minus the baseline sum divided by the number of resolved axis samples.
Calculation: Confluence = the sum of each enabled signal-timeframe vote times its axis weight divided by the sum of all enabled axis weights, or zero if the denominator is zero. Bias is the sum of enabled bias-timeframe votes. Method count is the number of enabled signal-timeframe votes with the same sign as confluence. Conviction is the rounded absolute confluence multiplied by a volatility scale and an efficiency scale. The optional volatility scale decreases linearly with the percentile rank of the rolling standard deviation of chart close changes, subject to a floor. The optional efficiency scale is a floored and capped affine function of the absolute chart close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero efficiency when the denominator is zero. A disabled scale equals one.
Calculation: Plotted score = optionally EMA-smooth either confluence or the weighted mean of graded signal-timeframe axis strengths. Momentum strength is the MACD histogram divided by its rolling standard deviation and a scale factor; stretch strength is its deviation divided by its rolling standard deviation and a scale factor; flow strength is its residual divided by its rolling standard deviation and a scale factor. Clamp each strength to the interval from negative one to positive one and use zero when its denominator is zero.
Long: An enabled axis vote on the trigger, signal, or bias timeframe > 0.
Short: An enabled axis vote on the trigger, signal, or bias timeframe < 0.
Long: Plotted score > 0.
Short: Plotted score < 0.
Long: Bias > 0 AND the bias gate is enabled.
Short: Bias < 0 AND the bias gate is enabled.
Long: Confluence > 0 AND conviction >= its minimum AND method count >= its minimum AND (the bias gate is disabled OR bias >= 0).
Short: Confluence < 0 AND conviction >= its minimum AND method count >= its minimum AND (the bias gate is disabled OR bias <= 0).
Long: A confirmed plotted-score pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND chart close at that pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND their separation <= the maximum pivot gap AND their separation from the last printed bullish divergence >= the divergence cooldown AND (opposite-verdict suppression is disabled OR confluence >= 0 OR conviction < the strong-conviction threshold).
Short: A confirmed plotted-score pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND chart close at that pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND their separation <= the maximum pivot gap AND their separation from the last printed bearish divergence >= the divergence cooldown AND (opposite-verdict suppression is disabled OR confluence <= 0 OR conviction < the strong-conviction threshold).
