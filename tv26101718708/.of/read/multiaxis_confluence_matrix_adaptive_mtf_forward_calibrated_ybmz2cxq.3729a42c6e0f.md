Calculation: Momentum vote = sign of MACD histogram (fast EMA minus slow EMA, minus its signal EMA); evaluate on the signal and bias timeframes using each timeframe’s last completed bar. The bias timeframe is higher than the signal timeframe; the signal timeframe is higher than or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Stretch vote = sign of (close minus its EMA fair value) divided by the standard deviation of that deviation over a longer lookback; use the same completed higher-timeframe bars as the momentum vote.
Calculation: Order-flow vote = sign of the z-score of cumulative signed volume, where each bar’s volume is signed by the normal-CDF transform of its return divided by the standard deviation of returns; normalize the cumulative-volume residual from its EMA using a longer lookback. Use the same completed higher-timeframe bars as the momentum vote.
Calculation: Fear vote = sign of the negative of implied-volatility close minus its EMA; use the same completed higher-timeframe bars as the momentum vote.
Calculation: Optional cross-market vote = sign of the correlation-signed, weighted average of leader instruments’ returns over a lookback; use the same completed higher-timeframe bars as the momentum vote.
Calculation: Axis weight = a minimum weight plus the greater of zero and the axis’s calibrated edge lower bound; use equal weights when calibration is disabled. The edge lower bound is the recency-decayed, uniqueness-weighted win rate’s Wilson lower confidence bound minus the direction-matched triple-barrier baseline win rate; apply it only after the minimum sample count is reached.
Calculation: Weighted vote score = weighted average of active signal-timeframe axis votes, each vote being −1, 0, or +1. Method agreement = count of active axes voting in the score’s direction. Higher-timeframe bias = sign of the sum of active axis votes on the bias timeframe; it allows only that direction when nonzero.
Calculation: Conviction = absolute weighted vote score × 100 × volatility scale × efficiency scale. Volatility scale decreases with the percentile rank of realized volatility; efficiency scale increases with the ratio of absolute net price change to the sum of absolute bar changes.
Calculation: Confluence score = weighted average of active axes’ clipped strengths: normalized MACD histogram, stretch z-score, order-flow z-score, negative implied-volatility deviation from EMA, and optional correlation-signed leader return. Smooth this score with an EMA for divergence comparisons.
Long: (weighted vote score > 0 AND method agreement ≥ its minimum AND conviction ≥ its minimum AND (higher-timeframe bias is zero OR higher-timeframe bias > 0) AND the long signal is armed AND cooldown has elapsed) OR (price makes a lower pivot low while confluence score makes a higher pivot low, the pivots are within the maximum gap, divergence cooldown has elapsed, and there is no strong short verdict).
Short: (weighted vote score < 0 AND method agreement ≥ its minimum AND conviction ≥ its minimum AND (higher-timeframe bias is zero OR higher-timeframe bias < 0) AND the short signal is armed AND cooldown has elapsed) OR (price makes a higher pivot high while confluence score makes a lower pivot high, the pivots are within the maximum gap, divergence cooldown has elapsed, and there is no strong long verdict).
