Calculation: Direction = +1 when the shortest-period close EMA > the middle-period close EMA AND the middle-period close EMA > the longest-period close EMA; -1 when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise 0. Require the middle-period EMA at the slope lookback and the longest-period EMA and ATR to be available.
Calculation: Normalized score N(x,a,b) = 0 when a = b; otherwise clamp(100 × (x - a) / (b - a), 0, 100).
Calculation: Pocket = [min(middle-period EMA + Direction × ATR × pocket-width factor, longest-period EMA - Direction × ATR × pocket-width factor), max(middle-period EMA + Direction × ATR × pocket-width factor, longest-period EMA - Direction × ATR × pocket-width factor)]. Invalidation level = longest-period EMA - Direction × ATR × invalidation-buffer factor.
Calculation: Invalidation state = latch on a close below the current bullish invalidation level or above the current bearish invalidation level, or on a close beyond the previous bar's invalidation level in its previous direction. Prefer the previous direction if the previous level is breached AND the current level is not breached or the direction changed. Reset the latch on a bar without a breach when the direction differs from the latched direction, or when the close is beyond the pocket in the latched trend direction.
Calculation: Pullback depth = (highest high over the pullback lookback - low) / ATR for Direction +1; (high - lowest low over the pullback lookback) / ATR for Direction -1. Ideal depth = ideal-depth parameter × sensitivity factor; deep depth = max(deep-depth parameter × sensitivity factor, ideal depth + 0.10); minimum depth = max(0.22, ideal depth × 0.28). Near pocket = low <= pocket upper edge + 0.35 × ATR for Direction +1, or high >= pocket lower edge - 0.35 × ATR for Direction -1. Touching pocket = high >= pocket lower edge AND low <= pocket upper edge.
Calculation: Target room = max(Direction × (prior directional extreme - close) / ATR, 0), where the prior directional extreme is the highest high for Direction +1 or lowest low for Direction -1 over the target lookback ending on the previous bar. If that extreme is unavailable or its directional distance from close is less than 0.30 ATR, use the clean-room parameter as target room.
Calculation: Continuation score = clamp(0.24 × trend score + 0.22 × depth score + 0.16 × volume score + 0.22 × defense score + 0.16 × target score, 0, 100). Trend score = clamp(62 + 0.38 × N(Direction × (middle-period EMA - its value at the slope lookback) / ATR, 0.04, slope-strength threshold) - sensitivity bonus, 0, 100). Depth score = N(depth, minimum depth, ideal depth) when depth <= ideal depth, otherwise N(depth, deep depth, ideal depth). Target score = N(target room, 0.35, clean-room parameter).
Calculation: Volume score = N(relative volume, 1.35, 0.62) when pullback depth >= minimum depth AND near pocket, or when close < open OR close < shortest-period EMA for Direction +1, or close > open OR close > shortest-period EMA for Direction -1; otherwise 58. Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when that SMA is unavailable or not positive.
Calculation: Defense score = 48 when the bar neither touches nor is near the pocket; otherwise 0.38 × N(close location, 0.42, 0.76) + 0.30 × N(wick response, 0.08, 0.34) + 0.32 × close-back score. For Direction +1, close location = (close - low) / (high - low), wick response = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), and close-back score = 100 if close > middle-period EMA; for Direction -1, close location = (high - close) / (high - low), wick response = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), and close-back score = 100 if close < middle-period EMA. Otherwise close-back score = 62 if close is inside the pocket, else 34.
Calculation: Watch condition = near pocket AND continuation score >= watch-score threshold AND (pullback depth > 0.72 × deep depth OR defense score < 50). Qualified continuation = Direction != 0 AND touching pocket AND invalidation state is not latched AND watch condition is false AND continuation score >= max(continuation-score threshold, watch-score threshold + 1) AND defense score >= defense threshold AND target room >= max(0.45 × clean-room parameter, follow-through distance parameter).
Calculation: Follow-through episode = start one episode when qualified continuation first becomes true or its direction changes, provided no episode is pending. Freeze its direction, target = close + Direction × ATR × follow-through distance parameter, and invalidation level. On each subsequent bar, resolve as adverse if low <= frozen invalidation for Direction +1 or high >= frozen invalidation for Direction -1; otherwise resolve as favorable if high >= frozen target for Direction +1 or low <= frozen target for Direction -1. Expire without a favorable result after the follow-through window. Adverse resolution takes priority if both levels are reached on the same bar.
Long: Direction = +1 AND qualified continuation is true AND (qualified continuation was false on the previous bar OR direction changed).
Short: Direction = -1 AND qualified continuation is true AND (qualified continuation was false on the previous bar OR direction changed).
Long: A pending Direction +1 follow-through episode resolves favorably.
Short: A pending Direction -1 follow-through episode resolves favorably.
