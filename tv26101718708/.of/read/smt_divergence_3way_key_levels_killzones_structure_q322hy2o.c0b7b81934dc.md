Calculation: Liquidity sweep = either low below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars or high above their highest high; low below or high above the latest unconsumed confirmed symmetric pivot, consuming that pivot unless a new same-side pivot confirms on the same bar; or the first low at or below, or high at or above, a previous higher-timeframe period low or high since that level last changed. Previous-period levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Select one source or combine pivot and previous-period sources.
Calculation: Candle run = consecutive candles with close below open for a bearish run or close above open for a bullish run. Store the first candle's open, start bar and run length; a new same-direction run replaces and rearms the previous run, while other candles leave it armed until a signal.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; store each latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low, replacing the previous same-side level. A close above the stored high sets state bullish and consumes that high; a close below the stored low sets state bearish and consumes that low. Process the high break before the low break. Evaluate on the chart timeframe or on a higher or lower timeframe using the last completed bar of that timeframe.
Long: A bearish run is armed AND its length is at least the minimum AND at least the required consecutive closes exceed its first open plus an optional ATR margin AND at least one of those consecutive candles has absolute body at least the optional ATR displacement threshold AND the first such close is no more than the allowed number of bars after the most recent low liquidity sweep AND, if required, that sweep is at or after the run start and has not already produced a signal AND the optional session and shared signal cooldown conditions are satisfied; disarm the run and record the sweep and signal bar.
Short: A bullish run is armed AND its length is at least the minimum AND at least the required consecutive closes are below its first open minus an optional ATR margin AND at least one of those consecutive candles has absolute body at least the optional ATR displacement threshold AND the first such close is no more than the allowed number of bars after the most recent high liquidity sweep AND, if required, that sweep is at or after the run start and has not already produced a signal AND the optional session and shared signal cooldown conditions are satisfied; disarm the run and record the sweep and signal bar.
Long: Close or high exceeds the latest unconsumed confirmed symmetric pivot high AND the candle body meets the optional ATR displacement threshold AND the preceding structure-break bias is bearish, or is bullish when continuation breaks are enabled; consume the high and set bias bullish. The first break only establishes bias. Process an upward break before a downward break.
Short: Close or low is below the latest unconsumed confirmed symmetric pivot low AND the candle body meets the optional ATR displacement threshold AND the preceding structure-break bias is bullish, or is bearish when continuation breaks are enabled; consume the low and set bias bearish. The first break only establishes bias. Process an upward break before a downward break.
Long: Structure state is bullish.
Short: Structure state is bearish.
