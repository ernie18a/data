Calculation: Delta-RSI = fit a polynomial of configurable order by least squares to the RSI of close over a rolling lookback, using bar indices from oldest to newest; evaluate the polynomial's first derivative at the newest index.
Calculation: Signal price = close on the most recent bar with a long or short signal after entry filters and direction settings, including signals outside the configured date range.
Calculation: Signal side = long if the current bar has a long signal, otherwise short if it has a short signal, otherwise the current position side.
Calculation: Take-profit level = signal price multiplied by (1 plus the configured profit fraction for a long signal side, or 1 minus it for a short signal side).
Calculation: Stop level = signal price multiplied by (1 minus the configured loss fraction for a long signal side, or 1 plus it for a short signal side); when trailing is enabled and a position is open, replace this with the maximum of the previous stop level and close multiplied by (1 minus the loss fraction) for a long position, or the minimum of the previous stop level and close multiplied by (1 plus the loss fraction) for a short position.
Long: Long entries enabled AND datetime within the optionally bounded backtest period AND (Delta-RSI crosses above zero OR Delta-RSI crosses above its EMA OR Delta-RSI > previous Delta-RSI AND previous Delta-RSI < Delta-RSI two bars ago AND previous Delta-RSI < zero), according to the selected long-entry mode AND, when enabled for long entries, each enabled filter: volume > a configurable multiple of its SMA AND ATR with one configurable lookback > ATR with another configurable lookback AND RSI of close with a separate configurable lookback > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold.
Short: Short entries enabled AND datetime within the optionally bounded backtest period AND (Delta-RSI crosses below zero OR Delta-RSI crosses below its EMA OR Delta-RSI < previous Delta-RSI AND previous Delta-RSI > Delta-RSI two bars ago AND previous Delta-RSI > zero), according to the selected short-entry mode AND, when enabled for short entries, each enabled filter: volume > a configurable multiple of its SMA AND ATR with one configurable lookback > ATR with another configurable lookback AND RSI of close with a separate configurable lookback > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold.
Exit: long OR short, (Delta-RSI crosses below zero OR Delta-RSI crosses below its EMA OR Delta-RSI < previous Delta-RSI AND previous Delta-RSI > Delta-RSI two bars ago AND previous Delta-RSI > zero), according to the selected exit mode AND, when enabled for exits, each enabled filter: volume > a configurable multiple of its SMA AND ATR with one configurable lookback > ATR with another configurable lookback AND RSI of close with a separate configurable lookback > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold.
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND low <= stop level OR take profit enabled AND high >= take-profit level.
Exit: short, stop loss enabled AND high >= stop level OR take profit enabled AND low <= take-profit level.
Exit: long OR short, datetime outside the optionally bounded backtest period.
