Calculation: MACD
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the higher of entry price and the highest close since entry, multiplied by (1 − trailing percentage)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the lower of entry price and the lowest close since entry, multiplied by (1 + trailing percentage)
Long: Current-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal line AND higher-timeframe MACD is above its signal line OR higher-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal line OR current-timeframe MACD crosses above zero AND higher-timeframe MACD is above zero OR higher-timeframe MACD crosses above zero
Short: Current-timeframe MACD crosses below its signal line AND higher-timeframe MACD is below its signal line OR higher-timeframe MACD crosses below its signal line OR current-timeframe MACD crosses below zero AND higher-timeframe MACD is below zero OR higher-timeframe MACD crosses below zero
Exit: long, current-timeframe MACD crosses below its signal line OR current-timeframe MACD crosses below zero OR low is at or below the long trailing stop
Exit: short, current-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal line OR current-timeframe MACD crosses above zero OR high is at or above the short trailing stop
