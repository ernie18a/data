Calculation: Source = selected backtest data price field.
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI of source.
Calculation: QQE trail = let distance be a factor times the EMA of the EMA of the absolute one-bar change in smoothed RSI; both EMA lengths equal twice the RSI length minus one. The candidate lower and upper bands are smoothed RSI minus and plus distance. Retain the greater of the prior lower band and candidate lower band when both current and prior smoothed RSI exceed the prior lower band; otherwise use the candidate lower band. Retain the lesser of the prior upper band and candidate upper band when both current and prior smoothed RSI are below the prior upper band; otherwise use the candidate upper band. Set trend to positive when smoothed RSI crosses the prior upper band, negative when the prior lower band crosses smoothed RSI, and otherwise retain the prior trend, initialized positive. The trail is the lower band in a positive trend and the upper band otherwise.
Calculation: Moving averages = independently selected moving average types of source at short, medium, and long lengths. The alternate moving averages use the same chart-timeframe bars and types with each length multiplied by the same adjustable multiplier; they do not use aggregated higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Medium direction = positive when the medium moving average exceeds every value in its preceding direction lookback, negative when it is below every value in that lookback, and zero otherwise. Alternate medium direction is calculated identically from the alternate medium moving average using the same direction lookback.
Calculation: Long filter = when the multiplier equals one, the optional moving-average filter requires source > medium moving average AND medium moving average > long moving average AND short moving average > medium moving average, and the optional direction filter requires medium direction > zero. When the multiplier exceeds one, the optional moving-average filter requires medium moving average > alternate medium moving average AND source > short moving average AND short moving average > medium moving average, and the optional direction filter requires medium direction > zero AND alternate medium direction > zero AND source > medium moving average. All enabled filters must pass.
Calculation: Short filter = when the multiplier equals one, the optional moving-average filter requires source < medium moving average AND medium moving average < long moving average AND short moving average < medium moving average, and the optional direction filter requires medium direction < zero. When the multiplier exceeds one, the optional moving-average filter requires medium moving average < alternate medium moving average AND source < short moving average AND short moving average < medium moving average, and the optional direction filter requires medium direction < zero AND alternate medium direction < zero AND source < medium moving average. All enabled filters must pass.
Calculation: Upward entry event = select one of: smoothed RSI crosses above the upper threshold around its midpoint; smoothed RSI crosses above the QQE trail, optionally requiring smoothed RSI outside either threshold; or smoothed RSI crosses to at least its midpoint. The selected event and long filter must both hold on the preceding bar. Optionally accept only the first such event since the most recent downward entry event.
Calculation: Downward entry event = select one of: smoothed RSI crosses below the lower threshold around its midpoint; smoothed RSI crosses below the QQE trail, optionally requiring smoothed RSI outside either threshold; or smoothed RSI crosses below its midpoint. The selected event and short filter must both hold on the preceding bar. Optionally accept only the first such event since the most recent upward entry event.
Calculation: Long close event = select one of: smoothed RSI crosses below the lower threshold; smoothed RSI crosses below its midpoint; or, for the QQE-trail close selection, smoothed RSI crosses below the QQE trail when the entry selection is also QQE-trail, otherwise smoothed RSI crosses below the QQE trail OR smoothed RSI is below its midpoint OR smoothed RSI is below the lower threshold. Trigger only on the first consecutive bar satisfying the selected condition.
Calculation: Short close event = select one of: smoothed RSI crosses above the upper threshold; smoothed RSI crosses to at least its midpoint; or, for the QQE-trail close selection, smoothed RSI crosses above the QQE trail when the entry selection is also QQE-trail, otherwise smoothed RSI crosses above the QQE trail OR smoothed RSI is at least its midpoint OR smoothed RSI crosses above the upper threshold. Trigger only on the first consecutive bar satisfying the selected condition.
Calculation: QQE alert state = initialize flat; while flat, change to long on an upward entry event, otherwise change to short on a downward entry event; while long, change to flat on a long close event; while short, change to flat on a short close event. Evaluate only one state transition per bar.
Long: Alternate medium direction changes from negative to zero or positive.
Short: Alternate medium direction changes from zero or positive to negative.
Long: QQE alert state changes from flat to long.
Short: QQE alert state changes from flat to short.
Exit: long, QQE alert state changes from long to flat.
Exit: short, QQE alert state changes from short to flat.
Exit: long, long moving average > alternate short moving average AND long moving average > alternate long moving average AND medium direction changes from zero or positive to negative.
Exit: short, long moving average < alternate short moving average AND long moving average < alternate long moving average AND medium direction changes from negative to zero or positive.
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − enabled stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + enabled stop-loss fraction).
