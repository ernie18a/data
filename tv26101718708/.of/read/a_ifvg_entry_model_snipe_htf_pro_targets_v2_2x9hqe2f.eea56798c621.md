Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar's high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed value.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a bullish/bearish candle whose body exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold.
Calculation: Recent event = event occurred within the setup window, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close above/below its EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Protected low/high = reset to the low/high of each corresponding sweep; while that sweep is recent, update to the lowest low/highest high since the sweep.
Calculation: Long/short stop = protected low minus/plus an ATR-scaled buffer; if no protected low/high exists, current low minus/current high plus the buffer.
Calculation: Equal high/low = current high/low differs from the preceding bar's high/low by less than an ATR-scaled tolerance; retain the latest such high/low.
Calculation: Snipe target = latest equal high/low if it is above/below the close; otherwise the highest high/lowest low over the shorter target lookback if it is above/below the close; otherwise close plus/minus an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Runner target = highest high/lowest low over a longer target lookback if it is above/below the close; otherwise close plus/minus a larger ATR multiple.
Long: low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low OR (a sweep low, bullish displacement, and bullish IFVG occurred within the setup window AND close > open AND close > previous bar's high AND (cooldown has elapsed for this entry type and direction) AND (the reward to the applicable snipe or runner target exceeds the risk to the long stop by the minimum reward-to-risk multiple, when that filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close > its EMA, for runner entries)).
Short: high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high OR (a sweep high, bearish displacement, and bearish IFVG occurred within the setup window AND close < open AND close < previous bar's low AND (cooldown has elapsed for this entry type and direction) AND (the reward to the applicable snipe or runner target exceeds the risk to the short stop by the minimum reward-to-risk multiple, when that filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close < its EMA, for runner entries)).
