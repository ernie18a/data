Calculation: Yield spread = base-currency government bond yield minus quote-currency government bond yield, using the selected maturity’s close on the configured yield timeframe (which may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA of yield spread on the configured EMA timeframe (which may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe); slow EMA uses a longer lookback on that same timeframe.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR fast EMA > slow EMA
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR fast EMA < slow EMA
