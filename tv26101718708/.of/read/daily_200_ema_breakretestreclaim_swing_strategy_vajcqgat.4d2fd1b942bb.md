Calculation: Long-term EMA = EMA of the close of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Active retest = After the latest chart-bar close crosses below the long-term EMA, at least one subsequent chart bar within a maximum lookback has high >= long-term EMA × (1 − zone fraction) AND low <= long-term EMA × (1 + zone fraction). A new downward cross restarts the setup; a long signal or expiry clears it.
Long: Active retest = true AND close > long-term EMA AND one selected confirmation is true: close > open; OR close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close; OR close > previous high AND close > open; OR high > previous high AND low > previous low. If enabled, each corresponding filter must also hold: long-term EMA >= its value a slope-lookback number of chart bars ago; still-forming higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA, or last completed higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA; still-forming higher-timeframe volume > its volume SMA × multiplier; chart close > shorter-term chart EMA; number of chart-close crossings of the long-term EMA over a chop lookback <= maximum crossings. This decision is reproducible from the specified data when the external-market filter is disabled.
Short: Chart close crosses below the long-term EMA.
