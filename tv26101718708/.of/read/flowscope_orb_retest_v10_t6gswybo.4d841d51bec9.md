Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range window; opening-range low = lowest low during that window
Calculation: Retest-zone width = clamp(ATR × zone multiplier, minimum zone width, maximum zone width)
Long: Opening range is complete AND current bar is in the active session outside the opening-range window AND no upside breakout has occurred this session AND close > opening-range high OR an upside breakout has occurred this session and has not failed AND low ≤ opening-range high + retest-zone width AND high ≥ opening-range high − retest-zone width AND close > opening-range high AND close > open OR the prior bar confirmed a long retest AND the upside breakout has not failed AND high > prior confirmation-bar high AND volume filter is disabled OR current volume ≥ elevated-volume threshold × average volume over its lookback
Short: Opening range is complete AND current bar is in the active session outside the opening-range window AND no downside breakout has occurred this session AND close < opening-range low OR a downside breakout has occurred this session and has not failed AND high ≥ opening-range low − retest-zone width AND low ≤ opening-range low + retest-zone width AND close < opening-range low AND close < open OR the prior bar confirmed a short retest AND the downside breakout has not failed AND low < prior confirmation-bar low AND volume filter is disabled OR current volume ≥ elevated-volume threshold × average volume over its lookback
