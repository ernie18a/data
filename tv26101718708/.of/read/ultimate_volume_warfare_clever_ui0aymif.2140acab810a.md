Calculation: Adaptive volume multiplier = clamp(SMA of ATR over a longer lookback / ATR, lower bound, upper bound).
Calculation: Volume average = ALMA or SMA of volume.
Calculation: Low pivot pair = a bar whose low and volume are both confirmed pivot lows using the same number of bars on each side; compare each pair with the immediately preceding confirmed low pivot pair. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: High pivot pair = a bar whose high and volume are both confirmed pivot highs using the same number of bars on each side; compare each pair with the immediately preceding confirmed high pivot pair. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bull filter = (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume) AND (ATR filter disabled OR high - low > ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND (accurate mode disabled OR (close > open AND close - low > (high - low) multiplied by a threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe volume > its SMA). Higher-timeframe volume and its SMA use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars; on a live chart bar they use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Bear filter = (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume) AND (ATR filter disabled OR high - low > ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND (accurate mode disabled OR (close < open AND high - close > (high - low) multiplied by a threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe volume > its SMA). Higher-timeframe volume and its SMA use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars; on a live chart bar they use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Climax = volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a climax threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier.
Calculation: Absorption = volume <= SMA of volume multiplied by the climax threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a lower absorption threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND high - low < ATR multiplied by a range threshold.
Calculation: Exhaustion = volume <= SMA of volume multiplied by the climax threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND absorption = false AND volume > SMA of volume multiplied by an exhaustion threshold lower than the absorption threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND high - low < ATR multiplied by a range threshold greater than the absorption range threshold.
Long: Current low pivot pair exists AND ((current pivot low < previous pivot low AND current pivot volume > previous pivot volume) OR (current pivot low > previous pivot low AND current pivot volume < previous pivot volume)).
Short: Current high pivot pair exists AND ((current pivot high > previous pivot high AND current pivot volume < previous pivot volume) OR (current pivot high < previous pivot high AND current pivot volume > previous pivot volume)).
Long: Volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a spike threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND close >= open.
Short: Volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a spike threshold and the adaptive volume multiplier AND close < open.
Long: Climax = false AND absorption = false AND exhaustion = false AND close < open AND volume > volume average AND close > low + (high - low) multiplied by an accumulation threshold.
Short: Climax = false AND absorption = false AND exhaustion = false AND close > open AND volume > volume average AND close < low + (high - low) multiplied by a distribution threshold.
