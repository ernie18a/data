Calculation: Swing high/low = the most recent confirmed pivot high/low, using equal numbers of bars on each side of the pivot; confirmation occurs only after the right-side bars are complete.
Calculation: Range high/low = the highest high/lowest low over a lookback ending at the preceding bar.
Calculation: Bearish reference = swing high if swing-break detection is enabled and high > swing high; otherwise range high. Bullish reference = swing low if swing-break detection is enabled and low < swing low; otherwise range low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 0 if the SMA is not positive. Volume Z-score = (volume - SMA(volume)) / standard deviation(volume), or 0 if the standard deviation is not positive; both use the same lookback including the current bar.
Calculation: Upper wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), and lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low); each is 0 when high = low. Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 1 when high = low.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = ((swing-break detection enabled AND low < swing low) OR (range-break detection enabled AND low < range low)) AND (close-back-inside filter disabled OR close > bullish reference) AND bullish reference - low >= ATR multiplied by a threshold AND lower wick ratio >= threshold AND relative volume >= threshold AND (volume Z-score filter disabled OR volume Z-score >= threshold) AND (effort-versus-result filter disabled OR body ratio <= threshold) AND (opposite-close filter disabled OR close > open).
Calculation: Bearish absorption = ((swing-break detection enabled AND high > swing high) OR (range-break detection enabled AND high > range high)) AND (close-back-inside filter disabled OR close < bearish reference) AND high - bearish reference >= ATR multiplied by a threshold AND upper wick ratio >= threshold AND relative volume >= threshold AND (volume Z-score filter disabled OR volume Z-score >= threshold) AND (effort-versus-result filter disabled OR body ratio <= threshold) AND (opposite-close filter disabled OR close < open).
Calculation: Active zones = when zone creation is enabled, create a bullish zone on each bullish absorption with bottom = low and top = min(bullish reference, low + ATR multiplied by a zone-thickness parameter), and a bearish zone on each bearish absorption with top = high and bottom = max(bearish reference, high - ATR multiplied by the same zone-thickness parameter). On each bar add new zones before testing touches; if the zone count exceeds its limit, remove the oldest. After testing touches, if broken-zone removal is enabled, remove bullish zones when close < bottom and bearish zones when close > top. Zones otherwise remain active indefinitely.
Long: bullish absorption
Short: bearish absorption
Long: any active bullish zone has low <= zone top AND close > zone bottom
Short: any active bearish zone has high >= zone bottom AND close < zone top
