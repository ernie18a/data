Calculation: Weighted move probabilities = over a history lookback, compare each of the preceding closes with its preceding close; weight the comparison i bars ago by 1 + (lookback - i) / lookback; divide the sum of positive-move weights and negative-move weights separately by their combined sum, or use zero for both if the sum is zero.
Calculation: Trend direction = +1 if the positive-move probability exceeds the negative-move probability, -1 if the negative-move probability exceeds the positive-move probability, otherwise 0; momentum = the absolute difference between the probabilities; confidence = their maximum.
Calculation: Memory = clamp(previous memory × decay + trend direction × (memory base + momentum × memory momentum coefficient), -1, 1), initially zero; memory direction = +1 above the positive memory threshold, -1 below its negative, otherwise 0; preferred direction = trend direction when nonzero, otherwise memory direction.
Calculation: Bull sniper score = the count of these true comparisons: close > session VWAP of typical price; RSI > RSI midpoint; MACD line > signal line; short EMA > long EMA; ADX > trend-strength threshold AND close > short EMA; volume > its sniper-lookback SMA AND close > open; RSI of the last completed other-timeframe bar > RSI midpoint. Bear sniper score uses the corresponding < comparisons, except ADX and volume remain > their thresholds. The other timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; when equal, use the preceding chart bar. Bull and bear percentages = their respective scores divided by the number of comparisons.
Calculation: Bull trend filter = close > trend EMA AND trend EMA > its previous value; bear trend filter reverses both comparisons. The other-timeframe trend filters apply the same comparisons to the last completed bar of a configurable higher or lower timeframe, or the preceding chart bar when the timeframes are equal; use the same EMA length as the chart trend EMA.
Calculation: Bull candle filter = close > open AND absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= body-ratio threshold; bear candle filter replaces close > open with close < open.
Calculation: Long hybrid score = 2 × indicator(preferred direction = +1) + indicator(trend direction = +1) + indicator(memory direction = +1) + indicator(confidence >= confidence threshold) + indicator(momentum >= momentum threshold) + indicator(trend EMA filter disabled OR bull trend filter) + indicator(other-timeframe filter disabled OR bull other-timeframe trend filter) + indicator(volume filter disabled OR volume > volume-filter SMA × volume multiplier) + indicator(ATR filter disabled OR ATR >= ATR SMA × ATR multiplier) + indicator(candle filter disabled OR bull candle filter) + 2 × indicator(bull sniper score > bear sniper score) + indicator(bull percentage - bear percentage >= strong-bias threshold) + indicator(close > session VWAP) + indicator(MACD line > signal line) + indicator(ADX > trend-strength threshold) + indicator(last completed other-timeframe RSI > RSI midpoint). The volume-filter SMA and sniper-volume SMA have independently configurable lookbacks.
Calculation: Short hybrid score = the long hybrid score with +1 replaced by -1, bull comparisons replaced by bear comparisons, bull percentage - bear percentage replaced by bear percentage - bull percentage, and directional > comparisons for close versus VWAP, MACD line versus signal line, and other-timeframe RSI versus its midpoint replaced by <; confidence, momentum, volume, ATR, and ADX comparisons are unchanged.
Calculation: Long candidate = (session filter disabled OR exchange-local hour within the configured session, including overnight sessions) AND (no prior entry OR bars since last entry >= ceiling(signal interval / chart-bar duration), with a minimum of one bar) AND (no prior entry OR last entry was short OR close >= last entry signal close + current ATR × rearm multiplier) AND (sniper-bias filter disabled OR (bull sniper score > bear sniper score AND bull percentage >= sniper threshold) OR bull percentage - bear percentage >= strong-bias threshold) AND long hybrid score >= score threshold AND long hybrid score - short hybrid score >= edge threshold; OR short EMA crosses above long EMA AND bull sniper score > bear sniper score AND bull percentage >= sniper threshold AND close > session VWAP AND MACD line > signal line AND last completed other-timeframe RSI > RSI midpoint, when the crossover trigger is enabled.
Calculation: Short candidate = the mirrored long candidate: last entry was long or close <= last entry signal close - current ATR × rearm multiplier; bear sniper score > bull sniper score or bear percentage - bull percentage >= strong-bias threshold; short hybrid score >= score threshold and short hybrid score - long hybrid score >= edge threshold; or the enabled short-EMA cross below long-EMA trigger with bear sniper bias, close < session VWAP, MACD line < signal line, and last completed other-timeframe RSI < RSI midpoint.
Calculation: Resolved long signal = long candidate AND (short candidate is false OR bull sniper score >= bear sniper score); resolved short signal = short candidate AND (long candidate is false OR bear sniper score > bull sniper score).
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR on the first bar where the position is observed open × stop multiplier; initial stop = entry price minus initial risk for a long, or entry price plus initial risk for a short. Track the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short starting with the entry signal bar. Activate trailing permanently when the favorable extreme minus entry price for a long, or entry price minus the favorable extreme for a short, >= current ATR × activation multiplier. Once active, the long trailing stop is the running maximum of favorable high - current ATR × trailing multiplier; the short trailing stop is the running minimum of favorable low + current ATR × trailing multiplier. The effective stop is the more protective of the initial and active trailing stops.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price + initial risk × target-step multiplier × tier for a long, or entry price - initial risk × target-step multiplier × tier for a short; tiers are consecutive positive integers. The first target is considered hit only after a prior open-position bar has high >= that target for a long or low <= that target for a short.
