Calculation: Weighted direction = over the configured history lookback, sum each positive close-to-close change's weight and each negative change's weight, where the weight is 1 + (lookback − bars-ago) / lookback; bull probability = up-weight / total nonzero-move weight, bear probability = down-weight / total, trend direction is whichever probability is greater (or neutral if equal), momentum = absolute difference between the probabilities, confidence = their maximum.
Calculation: Memory bias = recursively update memory as clamp(previous memory × decay + signed input, −1, 1), where signed input is direction × (base input + momentum × momentum weight), then classify as positive, negative, or neutral by comparing memory with the configured threshold. Preferred direction is trend direction when nonneutral, otherwise memory bias.
Calculation: Directional scores = for each side, add the points specified by the strategy: preferred direction (2), trend direction (1), memory bias (1), confidence threshold met (1), momentum threshold met (1), enabled EMA trend filter passes (1), enabled higher-timeframe trend filter passes (1), enabled volume filter passes (1), ATR volatility filter passes (1), enabled candle body filter passes (1), sniper score favors that side (2), sniper bias difference threshold met (1), close is on that side of VWAP (1), MACD favors that side (1), ADX strength threshold met (1), and higher-timeframe RSI favors that side (1). Disabled optional filters count as passing. Long edge = long score − short score; short edge = short score − long score.
Calculation: EMA trend filter = close above (bullish) or below (bearish) the trend EMA, with its current value respectively above or below its prior bar value. Higher-timeframe trend filter = the same comparison using the trend EMA on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Volume filter = volume exceeds its configured lookback volume average times the relative-volume threshold. ATR volatility filter = ATR is at least its longer-lookback ATR average times the configured threshold. Candle body filter = candle direction matches the side and absolute body / max(high − low, minimum tick) meets the configured minimum ratio.
Calculation: Sniper score = count of seven bullish checks or seven bearish checks: close on the corresponding side of VWAP; chart-timeframe RSI on the corresponding side of neutral; MACD line on the corresponding side of its signal line; shorter EMA on the corresponding side of the longer EMA; ADX above its strength threshold with close on the corresponding side of the shorter EMA; volume above its average with candle in the corresponding direction; and higher-timeframe RSI on the corresponding side of neutral. Sniper percentage = score / 7 × 100. Sniper bias requires that side's percentage to exceed the opposite side's and meet the configured minimum. Strong bias requires the percentage difference to meet the configured strong-bias threshold.
Calculation: Sniper cross trigger = shorter EMA crosses above (long) or below (short) the longer EMA, with the corresponding sniper bias, close on that side of VWAP, MACD on that side of its signal, and higher-timeframe RSI on that side of neutral. The higher-timeframe RSI uses the last completed bar.
Calculation: Signal gates = session filter passes; the configured minimum interval has elapsed since the last actual entry; and rearm passes (no prior signal price, prior signal was the other side, or close has moved at least the configured ATR distance from the prior same-side signal price in that side's direction). Bias gate passes when disabled, when that side's sniper bias holds, or when strong bias favors that side.
Calculation: ATR risk = ATR × configured stop multiplier. Initial long stop = entry price − ATR risk; initial short stop = entry price + ATR risk. Targets are five levels, each one configured risk increment farther in the profitable direction than the previous level. Effective long stop is the greater of initial stop and active trailing stop; effective short stop is the lesser. Trailing activates after favorable excursion from entry reaches the configured ATR activation distance; its stop follows the greatest high since entry minus ATR × trailing multiplier for longs, or the least low since entry plus ATR × trailing multiplier for shorts, and only moves toward profit.
Calculation: Early reversal conditions = for longs, close crosses below the configured fast exit EMA, or (if enabled) RSI falls below its configured overbought threshold after being above it, or a bearish engulfing candle forms; for shorts, close crosses above that EMA, or RSI rises above its configured oversold threshold after being below it, or a bullish engulfing candle forms. Engulfing means the current candle body covers the previous candle body and has the opposite direction.
Long: confirmed bar AND flat AND longs enabled AND ((signal gates pass AND bias gate passes AND long score meets its minimum AND long edge meets its minimum) OR sniper cross trigger); if both raw directions trigger, retain long when bull sniper percentage is at least bear sniper percentage.
Short: confirmed bar AND flat AND shorts enabled AND ((signal gates pass AND bias gate passes AND short score meets its minimum AND short edge meets its minimum) OR sniper cross trigger); if both raw directions trigger, retain short only when bear sniper percentage exceeds bull sniper percentage.
Exit: long, low <= effective long stop.
Exit: short, high >= effective short stop.
Exit: long, high >= any of the five targets; close 20% at each target.
Exit: short, low <= any of the five targets; close 20% at each target.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND minimum holding period passed AND (early reversal conditions pass AND (early exits need no first target or first target has been reached) OR close action is enabled and raw short signal holds).
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND minimum holding period passed AND (early reversal conditions pass AND (early exits need no first target or first target has been reached) OR close action is enabled and raw long signal holds).
