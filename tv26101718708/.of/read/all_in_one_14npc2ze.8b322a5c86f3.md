Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest among a parameterized equal number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Retain the latest confirmed high and low separately. Use a shorter pivot length for internal structure and a separate pivot length for reversal analysis.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially neutral; set bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high and bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain its previous state. A crossover requires the current close to be above the current level and the previous close to be at or below the previous level; a crossunder is the mirror. Calculate this separately on the chart and on three ordered timeframes used for reversal scoring. Relative to the intended chart timeframe, two are lower and one is the chart timeframe; for each lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Up/down volume ratio = sum of volume on bars whose close exceeds the previous close divided by sum of volume on bars whose close is below the previous close over the same lookback; use one when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the latest confirmed reversal pivot low AND close above that pivot low. Bearish sweep = high above the latest confirmed reversal pivot high AND close below that pivot high. A sweep is trend-valid when close is respectively below or above the trend EMA.
Calculation: Bullish ATR reclaim = low below the baseline EMA minus ATR times an extension multiplier AND close above the baseline EMA minus ATR times a separate inner-band multiplier. Bearish ATR rejection = high above the baseline EMA plus ATR times the extension multiplier AND close below the baseline EMA plus ATR times the inner-band multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish failed breakdown = low below previous low AND close above previous low AND close above open. Bearish failed breakout = high above previous high AND close below previous high AND close below open.
Calculation: Bullish reversal score = the sum of parameterized points for each satisfied condition, capped at a parameterized maximum: a trend-valid bullish sweep, otherwise a smaller award for a bullish sweep; the latest two confirmed reversal pivot lows make a lower low while RSI at those pivots makes a higher low; lower wick exceeds both candle body times a wick multiplier and ATR times a wick threshold; volume divided by its SMA reaches a climax threshold; bullish ATR reclaim; close above previous high OR bullish failed breakdown; previous Williams %R at or below its oversold threshold AND current Williams %R above its previous value; at least a required count of the three structure trends is bullish or neutral; low is at most the latest reversal pivot low plus an ATR multiple; RSI below a threshold and rising; up/down volume ratio below a threshold; and volume equals its running all-history maximum or its rolling high-volume maximum while close is below the trend EMA. The last volume award applies only on chart timeframes where that high-volume marking is enabled.
Calculation: Bearish reversal score = the sum of parameterized points for each satisfied condition, capped at the same maximum: a trend-valid bearish sweep, otherwise the same smaller award for a bearish sweep; the latest two confirmed reversal pivot highs make a higher high while RSI at those pivots makes a lower high; upper wick exceeds both candle body times the wick multiplier and ATR times the wick threshold; volume divided by its SMA reaches the climax threshold; bearish ATR rejection; close below previous low OR bearish failed breakout; previous Williams %R at or above its overbought threshold AND current Williams %R below its previous value; at least the required count of the three structure trends is bearish or neutral; high is at least the latest reversal pivot high minus the ATR multiple; RSI above a threshold and falling; up/down volume ratio above a threshold; and volume equals its running all-history maximum or its rolling high-volume maximum while close is above the trend EMA. The last volume award applies only on chart timeframes where that high-volume marking is enabled. Corresponding bullish and bearish score components use the same point weights.
Calculation: Bullish reversal signal = bullish reversal score reaches its signal threshold AND (bullish sweep OR bullish ATR reclaim OR bullish failed breakdown) AND no bullish reversal signal has occurred since the latest confirmed reversal pivot low AND the bars since the previous bullish reversal signal exceed the reversal cooldown, or no previous signal exists AND (the bars since the latest bearish chart structure change exceed the structure cooldown OR no such change exists OR the structure cooldown is zero OR a trend-valid bullish sweep occurs) AND (extension filtering is disabled OR close is below the trend EMA OR bullish ATR reclaim) AND (strong-trend filtering is disabled OR the bearish strong trend is absent OR a trend-valid bullish sweep OR bullish ATR reclaim). A bearish strong trend requires close below the short EMA, the short EMA below the medium EMA, the medium EMA below the long EMA, and ADX above its strong-trend threshold. Confirming a new reversal pivot low unlocks the signal before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bearish reversal signal = bearish reversal score reaches its signal threshold AND (bearish sweep OR bearish ATR rejection OR bearish failed breakout) AND no bearish reversal signal has occurred since the latest confirmed reversal pivot high AND the bars since the previous bearish reversal signal exceed the reversal cooldown, or no previous signal exists AND (the bars since the latest bullish chart structure change exceed the structure cooldown OR no such change exists OR the structure cooldown is zero OR a trend-valid bearish sweep occurs) AND (extension filtering is disabled OR close is above the trend EMA OR bearish ATR rejection) AND (strong-trend filtering is disabled OR the bullish strong trend is absent OR a trend-valid bearish sweep OR bearish ATR rejection). A bullish strong trend requires close above the short EMA, the short EMA above the medium EMA, the medium EMA above the long EMA, and ADX above its strong-trend threshold. Confirming a new reversal pivot high unlocks the signal before evaluating the current bar.

Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed chart pivot low AND the previous chart structure trend is not bearish.

Short: Volume exceeds its SMA times the high-volume multiplier AND close is below open.

Short: Bearish reversal signal.

Short: Bearish reversal score reaches the lower setup threshold AND (bearish sweep OR bearish ATR rejection OR bearish failed breakout) AND bearish reversal signal is false.

Short: High is below the low two bars earlier.
Long: Close is below the midpoint of the latest confirmed chart pivot high and low.
Short: Close is above the midpoint of the latest confirmed chart pivot high and low.

Short: RSI is at or below its midpoint OR RSI is at or above its overbought threshold.

Short: Williams %R is at or above its overbought threshold.
