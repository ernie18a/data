Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-lookback linear regressions of close = regress close against x=1 for current bar, increasing by 1 for each older bar; calculate slope, correlation, and R². The short lookback is shorter than the medium lookback, which is shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive R² threshold = 25th percentile of recent short-lookback R² values, clamped to [0.2, 0.7]; use the minimum R² threshold until more than 20 values are available, or when adaptive thresholds are disabled.
Calculation: Primary significance for a regression = R² >= adaptive R² threshold AND absolute correlation >= minimum correlation AND absolute slope >= minimum slope. Fallback significance = for short lookback, R² >= 0.2 AND absolute correlation >= 0.3; for medium and long lookbacks, R² >= 0.3 AND absolute correlation >= 0.4.
Calculation: Regression quality = 0.5 × R² + 0.3 × absolute correlation + 0.2 × min(1, absolute slope × 10000).
Calculation: Layer signal = sign of slope when primary significant; otherwise 0.6 × sign of slope × quality when fallback significant; otherwise 0.3 × sign of slope × quality when quality > 0.3; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Layer weights = normalize(0.6 × layer quality / total quality + 0.4 × base layer weight) when dynamic weights are enabled and at least 50 bars have elapsed; otherwise use normalized base weights. Base weights correspond to short, medium, and long layers.
Calculation: Ensemble score = sum of each layer signal × its weight.
Calculation: Validation accuracy = moving average over the validation lookback of max(0, 1 − 100 × absolute(actual close return over prediction horizon − weighted ensemble slope forecast over that horizon)); use 0.5 until more than 5 scores are available. Short-layer reliability = 1 − mean absolute error of the short-layer slope forecast over the validation lookback; use 0.5 until more than 5 errors are available. Actual close return compares current close with close at the prediction horizon bars earlier; each forecast uses the corresponding historical slope × prediction horizon.
Calculation: Overall confidence = (0.35 × agreement + 0.35 × weighted mean R² + 0.3 × validation accuracy) × min(1, short-layer reliability + 0.3), where agreement is absolute sum of layer signals whose absolute value exceeds 0.1 divided by their count, or 0 if none.
Calculation: Long flip gate = (most recent medium-slope zero cross was upward within the flip window AND medium slope has remained positive for at least the persistence bars) OR (most recent ensemble zero cross was upward within the flip window AND ensemble score has remained positive for at least the persistence bars).
Calculation: Short flip gate = (most recent medium-slope zero cross was downward within the flip window AND medium slope has remained negative for at least the persistence bars) OR (most recent ensemble zero cross was downward within the flip window AND ensemble score has remained negative for at least the persistence bars).
Long: ensemble score > 0.3 AND overall confidence > confidence threshold AND short-lookback regression is primary or fallback significant AND medium-lookback regression is primary or fallback significant AND long flip gate
Short: ensemble score < -0.3 AND overall confidence > confidence threshold AND short-lookback regression is primary or fallback significant AND medium-lookback regression is primary or fallback significant AND short flip gate
Calculation: ATR = average true range over its lookback. Initial long stop = entry price − stop multiple × ATR; initial short stop = entry price + stop multiple × ATR. 1R = stop distance from entry price. Long target = entry price + target multiple × 1R; short target = entry price − target multiple × 1R. Activate long trailing stop after high − entry price >= activation multiple × 1R; trailing stop = high − trailing ATR multiple × ATR. Activate short trailing stop after entry price − low >= activation multiple × 1R; trailing stop = low + trailing ATR multiple × ATR. Active long stop = tighter of initial and trailing stops; active short stop = tighter of initial and trailing stops.
Calculation: Time stop = elapsed chart bars since entry >= time-stop lookback.
Exit: Long, medium-lookback regression slope < 0 OR ensemble score <= 0 OR low <= active long stop OR high >= long target OR time stop
Exit: Short, medium-lookback regression slope > 0 OR ensemble score >= 0 OR high >= active short stop OR low <= short target OR time stop
