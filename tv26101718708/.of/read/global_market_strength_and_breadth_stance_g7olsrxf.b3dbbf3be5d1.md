Calculation: Analysis bars = chart-timeframe bars or, when the analysis timeframe is higher or lower, the last completed analysis bar available at each chart-bar close.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in analysis-bar close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: Channel midpoint = (highest analysis-bar high + lowest analysis-bar low) / 2 over the same lookback; channel width = highest high - lowest low over that lookback.
Long: Analysis-bar close > short-term close moving average OR short-term close moving average > medium-term close moving average OR medium-term close moving average > long-term close moving average OR (efficiency ratio >= minimum threshold AND channel width > 0 AND analysis-bar close > channel midpoint AND (analysis-bar close - channel midpoint) / channel width >= channel threshold AND NOT (analysis-bar close < short-term moving average AND short-term moving average < medium-term moving average AND medium-term moving average < long-term moving average)).
Short: Analysis-bar close <= short-term close moving average OR short-term close moving average <= medium-term close moving average OR medium-term close moving average <= long-term close moving average.
