Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that same lookback
Calculation: Donchian midline = (highest high + lowest low over a lookback) / 2; corridor width = highest high − lowest low over that lookback
Long: medium-term SMA > long-term SMA AND short-term SMA > medium-term SMA AND efficiency ratio ≥ 0.20 AND (close > short-term SMA OR (close ≤ short-term SMA AND close > medium-term SMA)) OR close > Donchian midline AND abs(close − Donchian midline) / corridor width ≥ 0.15 AND NOT (close < short-term SMA AND short-term SMA < medium-term SMA AND medium-term SMA < long-term SMA)
Short: medium-term SMA ≤ long-term SMA AND efficiency ratio ≥ 0.20 AND (short-term SMA ≤ medium-term SMA OR close ≤ medium-term SMA) OR close < Donchian midline AND abs(close − Donchian midline) / corridor width ≥ 0.15 AND NOT (close > short-term SMA AND short-term SMA > medium-term SMA AND medium-term SMA > long-term SMA)
