Calculation: Estimated delta = sum of lower-timeframe bar volume within the chart bar, positive when lower-timeframe close > open, negative when close < open, and zero when equal; use completed lower-timeframe bars, falling back to another lower timeframe or classifying the chart bar the same way if intrabar data is unavailable
Calculation: CVD = cumulative estimated delta since the selected reset point
Long: a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND CVD at that pivot is higher than CVD at the preceding pivot low OR estimated delta / volume > the imbalance threshold OR the configured number of consecutive bars each has estimated delta / volume > the imbalance threshold OR volume > SMA(volume) × the configured multiplier AND high − low < ATR × the configured factor AND |low − most recent confirmed swing low| ≤ ATR OR a confirmed swing low has positive estimated delta on its pivot bar
Short: a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND CVD at that pivot is lower than CVD at the preceding pivot high OR estimated delta / volume < −the imbalance threshold OR the configured number of consecutive bars each has estimated delta / volume < −the imbalance threshold OR volume > SMA(volume) × the configured multiplier AND high − low < ATR × the configured factor AND |high − most recent confirmed swing high| ≤ ATR OR a confirmed swing high has negative estimated delta on its pivot bar
