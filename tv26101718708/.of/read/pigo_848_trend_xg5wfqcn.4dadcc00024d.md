Calculation: Impulse histogram = displacement minus its SMA; displacement is zero when ZLEMA of typical price lies between SMMA of high and SMMA of low, otherwise it is ZLEMA minus the nearer violated SMMA. ZLEMA is twice EMA of typical price minus EMA of that EMA, using the same length; both SMMAs use that length and start at the SMA. The displacement SMA uses a separately parameterized length.
Long: On the chart timeframe or on an independently selected higher or lower timeframe, EMA of close > its previous-bar value AND (flat filter disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: On the chart timeframe or on an independently selected higher or lower timeframe, EMA of close < its previous-bar value AND (flat filter disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: On the chart timeframe or on an independently selected higher or lower timeframe, first EMA of close > second EMA of close AND (flat filter disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). The two EMA lengths are independently parameterized. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: On the chart timeframe or on an independently selected higher or lower timeframe, first EMA of close < second EMA of close AND (flat filter disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). The two EMA lengths are independently parameterized. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
