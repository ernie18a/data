Calculation: Directional movement and ADX = Wilder’s +DI, −DI, and ADX calculated on the chart timeframe; use the same DI lookback and ADX smoothing for chart and higher-timeframe calculations.
Calculation: Trend = Up when ADX ≥ the trend threshold and +DI > −DI; Down when ADX ≥ the trend threshold and −DI > +DI; otherwise Compression when Bollinger Band Width < 0.85 × its moving average, Range when it is below its moving average, and Chop otherwise. Bollinger Band Width = (upper band − lower band) / basis × 100; use the same band lookback, multiplier, and width-average lookback on both timeframes.
Calculation: Momentum = Rising when the absolute +DI/−DI gap has increased for two consecutive bars; otherwise Strong when the gap > 15; otherwise Fading when the gap has decreased for two consecutive bars; otherwise Collapsing.
Calculation: State = Exhaustion if ADX ≥ 30 and (Trend is Up and smoothed stochastic average ≥ 75, energy is Quiet or Normal, and momentum is Fading or Collapsing; or Trend is Down and smoothed stochastic average ≤ 25, energy is Quiet or Normal, and momentum is Fading or Collapsing). Otherwise Mature if ADX ≥ 35 and < 45, DI gap ≥ 10, and (Trend is Up and stochastic average > 60 or Trend is Down and stochastic average < 40). Otherwise Healthy if ADX ≥ trend threshold and < 35, DI gap ≥ 8, and (Trend is Up and stochastic average > 50 or Trend is Down and stochastic average < 50). Otherwise Early if ADX ≥ early threshold and < trend threshold and (Trend is Up and stochastic average > 50 or Trend is Down and stochastic average < 50); otherwise Early if ADX < 30, Healthy if ADX < 40, Mature if ADX < 50, and Exhaustion otherwise. Stochastic average = mean of the smoothed %K and %D, where raw %K is the close’s position within the lookback high-low range (50 if range is zero), then smooth %K and %D with their respective moving-average lookbacks. Energy = Quiet when ATR < 0.90 × its moving average; Normal when ATR < 1.05 × its moving average; Expanding when ATR < 1.25 × its moving average; Extreme otherwise. Use the same lookbacks for these calculations on both timeframes.
Calculation: Bias = Long when Trend is Up and the long score ≥ 55; Short when Trend is Down and the short score ≥ 55; Range Mode when Trend is Range; Wait Breakout when Trend is Compression; Neutral otherwise. Each score starts at 0 and adds: 20 if its matching Trend (Up for long, Down for short); 20 for Early state, 18 for Healthy, 8 for Mature; 16 for Rising momentum, 20 for Strong, 6 for Fading; 12 for Normal energy, 15 for Expanding, 8 for Extreme, 4 for Quiet; 15 if ADX ≥ trend threshold and DI gap ≥ 8, otherwise 8 if ADX ≥ early threshold and DI gap ≥ 5. Subtract 20 for Exhaustion state; clamp score to 0–100.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe context = calculations above on bars aggregated to a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Chart-timeframe context = calculations above on chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Long: Higher-timeframe Bias is Long AND chart-timeframe Trend is Up AND chart-timeframe State is neither Mature nor Exhaustion AND chart-timeframe Momentum is neither Fading nor Collapsing.
Short: Higher-timeframe Bias is Short AND chart-timeframe Trend is Down AND chart-timeframe State is neither Mature nor Exhaustion AND chart-timeframe Momentum is neither Fading nor Collapsing.
