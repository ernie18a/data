Calculation: Directional trend = +1 when ADX >= trend threshold AND +DI > -DI; -1 when ADX >= trend threshold AND -DI > +DI; otherwise 0.
Calculation: DI gap = absolute value of (+DI - -DI).
Calculation: Momentum = rising when DI gap > its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago; otherwise strong when DI gap > a threshold; otherwise fading when DI gap < its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago; otherwise collapsing.
Calculation: Energy = quiet when ATR < its SMA baseline multiplied by a lower threshold; otherwise normal when ATR < its baseline multiplied by a higher threshold; otherwise expanding when ATR < its baseline multiplied by a still higher threshold; otherwise extreme.
Calculation: Stochastic average = average of the smoothed stochastic %K and its SMA signal line; use raw stochastic 50 when the lookback high equals the lookback low.
Calculation: State = exhaustion when ADX >= an exhaustion threshold AND energy is quiet OR normal AND momentum is fading OR collapsing AND either directional trend = +1 AND stochastic average >= an upper exhaustion threshold OR directional trend = -1 AND stochastic average <= a lower exhaustion threshold; otherwise mature when ADX is in the mature interval AND DI gap >= its mature threshold AND either directional trend = +1 AND stochastic average > an upper mature threshold OR directional trend = -1 AND stochastic average < a lower mature threshold; otherwise healthy when ADX is in the healthy interval AND DI gap >= its healthy threshold AND either directional trend = +1 AND stochastic average > the stochastic midpoint OR directional trend = -1 AND stochastic average < the midpoint; otherwise early, healthy, mature, or exhaustion according to successive increasing ADX fallback thresholds. The healthy ADX interval starts at the trend threshold and ends at the start of the mature interval; the lower mature stochastic threshold is below the midpoint, the upper mature threshold is above it, and the exhaustion thresholds are farther from the midpoint. The mature DI gap threshold is higher than the healthy threshold.
Calculation: Directional score = clamp to the allowed score range the sum of a trend contribution when directional trend matches the side, a state contribution ranked early > healthy > mature > exhaustion, a momentum contribution ranked strong > rising > fading > collapsing, an energy contribution ranked expanding > normal > extreme > quiet, and a strength contribution selected in order: higher contribution when ADX >= trend threshold AND DI gap >= higher strength threshold, lower contribution when ADX >= early ADX threshold AND DI gap >= lower strength threshold, otherwise zero; subtract an exhaustion penalty when state is exhaustion. The early ADX threshold is below the trend threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe calculations use higher-timeframe bars with gaps filled and lookahead off: on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including a higher-timeframe bar that completes on the current chart bar; in real time use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: Chart-timeframe directional trend = +1
Short: Chart-timeframe directional trend = -1
Long: Higher-timeframe directional trend = +1
Short: Higher-timeframe directional trend = -1
Long: Higher-timeframe directional trend = +1 AND higher-timeframe long directional score >= score threshold
Short: Higher-timeframe directional trend = -1 AND higher-timeframe short directional score >= score threshold
Long: Higher-timeframe directional trend = +1 AND higher-timeframe long directional score >= score threshold AND chart-timeframe directional trend = +1 AND chart-timeframe state is early OR healthy AND chart-timeframe momentum is rising OR strong
Short: Higher-timeframe directional trend = -1 AND higher-timeframe short directional score >= score threshold AND chart-timeframe directional trend = -1 AND chart-timeframe state is early OR healthy AND chart-timeframe momentum is rising OR strong
