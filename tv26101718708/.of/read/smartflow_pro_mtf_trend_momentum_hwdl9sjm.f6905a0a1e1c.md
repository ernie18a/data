Calculation: Fast, slow, and trend MAs = the same selected moving-average type of close, with successively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Momentum = shorter-lookback EMA of close − longer-lookback EMA of close
Calculation: Momentum signal = EMA of momentum
Long: Current fast MA > current slow MA AND previous fast MA ≤ previous slow MA AND momentum > 0 AND momentum > momentum signal AND higher-timeframe fast MA > slow MA > trend MA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Short: Current fast MA < current slow MA AND previous fast MA ≥ previous slow MA AND momentum < 0 AND momentum < momentum signal AND higher-timeframe fast MA < slow MA < trend MA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
