Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = VWAP of the selected price source, defaulting to (high + low + close) / 3, and VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiplier times the volume-weighted standard deviation of that source since the start of the selected anchor period. The entry and target bands use independently configurable multipliers.
Calculation: Entry reference = close of the bar that generates the entry signal; retain that bar's ATR and target-band value for the position.
Long: position is flat AND close <= lower entry band AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (time filter disabled OR (bar opening hour in America/New_York >= configured start hour AND bar opening hour <= configured end hour AND (lunch filter disabled OR bar opening hour < 12 OR bar opening hour >= 14))).
Short: position is flat AND close >= upper entry band AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (time filter disabled OR (bar opening hour in America/New_York >= configured start hour AND bar opening hour <= configured end hour AND (lunch filter disabled OR bar opening hour < 12 OR bar opening hour >= 14))).
Exit: long, close >= anchored VWAP OR high >= entry reference + (upper target band at signal - entry reference) × target ratio OR low <= entry reference - ATR at signal × stop multiplier.
Exit: short, close <= anchored VWAP OR low <= entry reference - (entry reference - lower target band at signal) × target ratio OR high >= entry reference + ATR at signal × stop multiplier.
