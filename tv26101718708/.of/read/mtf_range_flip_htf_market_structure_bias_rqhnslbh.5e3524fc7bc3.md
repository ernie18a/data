Calculation: Chart range = initialize from the first chart bar; when a chart close exceeds the current range high or falls below its low, replace the range with that bar’s high and low and set its direction upward or downward.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range = apply the chart-range rule to higher-timeframe bars, using only the last completed bar; midline = (range high + range low) / 2.
Calculation: Confirmed swing highs and lows = pivot highs and lows confirmed by bars on both sides; use the higher-timeframe pivots for structure and chart-timeframe pivots for stops, with independently configured pivot lengths.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure bias = BOS: persist bullish after a completed higher-timeframe close above the latest confirmed swing high, or bearish after a close below the latest confirmed swing low; HH-HL sequence: bullish when the latest confirmed high and low both exceed their respective preceding pivots, bearish when both are lower; Both: bullish or bearish only when both methods agree, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Range depth = (close − higher-timeframe range low) / range width for longs; (higher-timeframe range high − close) / range width for shorts.
Calculation: Initial long stop = selected reference (latest confirmed chart swing low, chart flip-bar low, or prior chart-range low), combined with the chart range low by taking the lower valid level, then subtract the configured tick buffer; freeze at fill. Initial short stop = selected reference (latest confirmed chart swing high, chart flip-bar high, or prior chart-range high), combined with the chart range high by taking the higher valid level, then add the tick buffer; freeze at fill.
Calculation: Target = fill price plus (long stop distance × configured long reward/risk multiple) for longs, or fill price minus (short stop distance × configured short reward/risk multiple) for shorts.
Calculation: Optional volatility filter = selected-timeframe ATR percentile rank over its configured history, or ATR divided by its configured moving average; use the last completed selected-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Optional VWAP distance = absolute(close − session- or week-anchored VWAP) / chart-timeframe ATR.
Long: chart close > prior chart-range high AND prior chart-range direction is not upward AND flat AND direction permits longs AND within the configured general entry session AND (structure filter off OR higher-timeframe structure bias bullish) AND (midline rule off OR pullback mode and close < higher-timeframe midline OR breakout mode and close > higher-timeframe midline) AND (regime filter off OR selected daily/weekly structure regime is neutral or bullish) AND (volatility filter off OR volatility value is available and meets its configured minimum) AND (VWAP filter off OR distance-only mode and VWAP distance is within its maximum OR long-above mode and close > VWAP and distance is within its maximum OR long-below mode and close < VWAP and distance is within its maximum) AND (depth gate does not apply OR long range depth is available and below its configured cutoff) AND long stop distance is positive and meets any configured minimum and maximum stop-width limits.
Short: chart close < prior chart-range low AND prior chart-range direction is not downward AND flat AND direction permits shorts AND within both the configured general and short entry sessions AND (structure filter off OR higher-timeframe structure bias bearish) AND (midline rule off OR pullback mode and close > higher-timeframe midline OR breakout mode and close < higher-timeframe midline) AND (regime filter off OR selected daily/weekly structure regime is neutral or bearish) AND (volatility filter off OR volatility value is available and meets its configured minimum) AND (VWAP filter off OR distance-only mode and VWAP distance is within its maximum OR long-above mode and close < VWAP and distance is within its maximum OR long-below mode and close > VWAP and distance is within its maximum) AND (depth gate does not apply OR short range depth is available and below its configured cutoff) AND short stop distance is positive and meets any configured minimum and maximum stop-width limits.
Exit: long, low <= frozen stop OR high >= target
Exit: short, high >= frozen stop OR low <= target
Exit: long, when optional breakeven is enabled for longs and high >= fill price + initial risk × configured arming multiple, replace stop with fill price plus configured tick offset
Exit: short, when optional breakeven is enabled for shorts and low <= fill price − initial risk × configured arming multiple, replace stop with fill price minus configured tick offset
