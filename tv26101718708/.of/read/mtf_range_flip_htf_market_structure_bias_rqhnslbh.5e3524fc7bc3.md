Calculation: Range = initialize to the first bar's high and low with direction neutral; whenever a later close exceeds the stored high or falls below the stored low, replace both bounds with that bar's high and low and set direction up or down respectively. Preserve the previous bounds when replacing them. A flip occurs only when the new direction differs from the direction before replacement.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range = apply the range calculation to the last completed higher-timeframe bars, updating once per newly completed bar. Its midpoint is the average of its bounds.
Calculation: Structure = on the specified higher timeframe, identify pivot highs and lows confirmed by the same number of bars on each side. Retain the two latest distinct confirmed pivot highs and lows. Break-of-structure direction becomes up when the last completed bar's close exceeds the latest pivot high, down when it falls below the latest pivot low, and otherwise remains unchanged. Sequence direction is up when both latest pivots exceed their respective preceding pivots, down when both are lower, and neutral otherwise. Select break-of-structure, sequence, or their common direction when they agree; otherwise the selected direction is neutral. Confirmed pivots are taken from completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Stop reference = choose the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low for a long or pivot high for a short, the previous chart-timeframe range boundary, or the current chart-timeframe range boundary. For a long, if the chosen reference is below the signal close, use the lower of it and the current range low; otherwise use the current range low. For a short, if the chosen reference is above the signal close, use the higher of it and the current range high; otherwise use the current range high. Subtract a price buffer for a long or add it for a short; freeze the result at the signal bar. Signal risk is the distance from the signal close to this stop; filled risk is the absolute distance from the entry price to this stop.
Calculation: Depth = (close minus higher-timeframe range low) divided by range width for a long; (higher-timeframe range high minus close) divided by range width for a short. It is unavailable when the width is not positive.
Calculation: Volatility measure = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, either the percentile rank of ATR over its ranking lookback or ATR divided by its simple moving average over that lookback.
Calculation: VWAP distance = absolute difference between close and session- or week-anchored VWAP divided by chart-timeframe ATR; use zero when ATR is not positive.
Long: Chart-timeframe range flips up AND position is flat AND long trading is enabled AND datetime is inside the entry window and optional date range AND signal risk is positive and within any enabled minimum and maximum bounds AND (structure filter is off OR higher-timeframe structure is up) AND (regime filter is off OR structure on its separately selected higher timeframe is up or neutral) AND (midpoint filter is off or midpoint is unavailable OR pullback mode and close is below the higher-timeframe midpoint OR breakout mode and close is above it) AND (depth gate does not apply OR long depth is available and below the selected threshold) AND (volatility filter is off OR volatility measure is available and at least its threshold) AND (VWAP filter is off OR VWAP distance is at most its threshold AND (distance-only mode OR long-above mode and close is above VWAP OR long-below mode and close is below VWAP)).
Short: Chart-timeframe range flips down AND position is flat AND short trading is enabled AND datetime is inside both the entry window and short entry window and the optional date range AND signal risk is positive and within any enabled minimum and maximum bounds AND (structure filter is off OR higher-timeframe structure is down) AND (regime filter is off OR structure on its separately selected higher timeframe is down or neutral) AND (midpoint filter is off or midpoint is unavailable OR pullback mode and close is above the higher-timeframe midpoint OR breakout mode and close is below it) AND (depth gate does not apply OR short depth is available and below the selected threshold) AND (volatility filter is off OR volatility measure is available and at least its threshold) AND (VWAP filter is off OR VWAP distance is at most its threshold AND (distance-only mode OR long-above mode and close is below VWAP OR long-below mode and close is above VWAP)).
Exit: long, low is at or below the frozen stop OR high is at or above entry price plus filled risk multiplied by the long reward-to-risk parameter OR breakeven is enabled for longs and a bar since entry has reached entry price plus filled risk multiplied by the breakeven trigger parameter and low is at or below entry price plus the breakeven offset.
Exit: short, high is at or above the frozen stop OR low is at or below entry price minus filled risk multiplied by the short reward-to-risk parameter OR breakeven is enabled for shorts and a bar since entry has reached entry price minus filled risk multiplied by the breakeven trigger parameter and high is at or above entry price minus the breakeven offset.
