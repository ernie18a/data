Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the first configured number of bars in each entry session; complete after those bars.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Breakout arming = each direction is armed at the start of an entry session and outside entry sessions; both directions are rearmed when flat and the close is strictly inside the completed opening range. A direction is disarmed when its breakout is stored as a pending signal.
Calculation: Pending breakout = while flat and eligible to trade in an entry session, store the breakout bar, its close, and the corresponding opening-range boundary; a new pending breakout replaces the opposite direction. Clear it after its exact delay bar or after an entry.
Calculation: Structural stop = stored breakout boundary minus ATR times a buffer for a long, or plus ATR times a buffer for a short, evaluated on the delay bar. Risk distance = absolute difference between delay-bar close and structural stop.
Calculation: Quantity = floor of the smaller of the risk budget and hard risk limit divided by risk distance and point value. Actual risk = quantity times risk distance times point value. Target distance = the smaller of risk distance times the target reward-to-risk ratio and the per-trade profit cap divided by quantity and point value.
Calculation: Evaluation PnL = cumulative realized profit plus open profit at the current close. Peak PnL = highest evaluation PnL so far. Remaining drawdown = drawdown limit minus the difference between peak PnL and evaluation PnL. The evaluation locks permanently when evaluation PnL reaches the profit target or drawdown reaches the drawdown limit less its safety buffer. Trading eligibility requires the evaluation to be unlocked and remaining drawdown to exceed the hard risk limit plus the safety buffer.
Calculation: Live stop = the original structural stop, tightened only. Let R be the entry-to-original-stop distance. After a long bar reaches entry plus 1R, 2R, or 3R, raise the stop respectively to at least entry plus a tick offset, entry plus 1R, or entry plus 2R; reverse these directions for a short. If the optional dollar lock is enabled, each of three increasing favorable open-profit thresholds, measured at the bar high for a long or low for a short, tightens the stop to at least the corresponding configured locked profit divided by quantity and point value above entry for a long or below entry for a short. Apply stop changes to subsequent bars.
Long: Entry session AND opening range complete AND long armed AND close > opening-range high AND high > opening-range high AND close > open AND close location >= minimum breakout strength AND absolute candle body >= ATR times minimum body factor AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP).
Short: Entry session AND opening range complete AND short armed AND close < opening-range low AND low < opening-range low AND close < open AND close location <= 1 minus minimum breakout strength AND absolute candle body >= ATR times minimum body factor AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP).
Long: Entry session AND trading eligibility AND flat AND pending long breakout age equals the configured delay AND risk distance is within the configured minimum and maximum AND quantity >= 1 AND actual risk <= hard risk limit AND actual risk < remaining drawdown minus safety buffer AND close > stored breakout boundary AND close > structural stop AND close <= breakout close + (breakout close - structural stop) times maximum chase factor AND target distance >= risk distance times minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Short: Entry session AND trading eligibility AND flat AND pending short breakout age equals the configured delay AND risk distance is within the configured minimum and maximum AND quantity >= 1 AND actual risk <= hard risk limit AND actual risk < remaining drawdown minus safety buffer AND close < stored breakout boundary AND close < structural stop AND close >= breakout close - (structural stop - breakout close) times maximum chase factor AND target distance >= risk distance times minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: long, low <= live stop OR high >= entry price + target distance fixed at entry OR evaluation locked OR outside both management sessions.
Exit: short, high >= live stop OR low <= entry price - target distance fixed at entry OR evaluation locked OR outside both management sessions.
