Calculation: Reference high and low = either the highest high and lowest low over a lookback excluding the current bar, or the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Breakout direction and level = up and reference high when high > reference high AND close > reference high; otherwise down and reference low when low < reference low AND close < reference low; otherwise no breakout.
Calculation: Normalized pressure = sum over a pressure lookback including the current bar of volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), divided by the pressure lookback length × the average volume over a volume lookback excluding the current bar. A bar with high = low contributes zero; if the denominator is unavailable or not positive, normalized pressure is zero.
Calculation: Breakout score = count of these four conditions: current volume / average volume over the volume lookback excluding the current bar >= a volume threshold, with a positive average and available volume; absolute close − open / ATR >= a body threshold, with the ratio set to zero if ATR is not positive, AND, if body direction is required, close > open for an up breakout or close < open for a down breakout; normalized pressure >= a pressure threshold for an up breakout or <= its negative for a down breakout, with current volume available; breakout extension / ATR >= an extension threshold, with the ratio set to zero if ATR is not positive. Breakout extension is close − breakout level for an up breakout and breakout level − close for a down breakout.
Calculation: Zone = on every breakout whose score >= a configurable minimum, replace the previous zone with its breakout direction, level, current ATR, creation bar, and an unarmed retest state. On later bars, the zone is live until its age exceeds an expiry threshold or it fails. An up zone fails when close < level − a failure tolerance × frozen ATR; a down zone fails when close > level + a failure tolerance × frozen ATR. An unarmed live zone rearms when close >= level + a rearm distance × frozen ATR for an up zone, or close <= level − a rearm distance × frozen ATR for a down zone. An armed live zone records a bounce and becomes unarmed when it has not failed AND low <= level AND close >= level for an up zone, or high >= level AND close <= level for a down zone. A newly created zone cannot fail, rearm, or bounce on its creation bar; failure takes precedence over bounce, and bounce over rearming.
Long: Breakout direction = up.
Short: Breakout direction = down.
Long: Breakout direction = up AND breakout score >= three of four.
Short: Breakout direction = down AND breakout score >= three of four.
Long: Zone direction = up AND a zone bounce occurs.
Short: Zone direction = down AND a zone bounce occurs.
