Calculation: Supertrend direction = Supertrend trend direction on the chart timeframe and on each enabled aggregated timeframe above or below it, using the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus ATR times the configured stop multiplier, or the lowest low over the configured lookback; if that low is at or above entry, use entry minus half ATR
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus ATR times the configured stop multiplier, or the highest high over the configured lookback; if that high is at or below entry, use entry plus half ATR
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus ATR times the configured target multiplier, or entry price plus the greater of the absolute distance to the stop and minimum price tick, multiplied by the configured risk-reward ratio
Calculation: Short target = entry price minus ATR times the configured target multiplier, or entry price minus the greater of the absolute distance to the stop and minimum price tick, multiplied by the configured risk-reward ratio
Long: Active position count is below its configured maximum AND ((multi-timeframe filtering is enabled AND every enabled other-timeframe Supertrend direction is bullish AND the previous bar did not have every enabled direction bullish) OR (multi-timeframe filtering is disabled AND the previous chart-timeframe Supertrend direction was bearish AND the current direction is bullish))
Short: Active position count is below its configured maximum AND ((multi-timeframe filtering is enabled AND every enabled other-timeframe Supertrend direction is bearish AND the previous bar did not have every enabled direction bearish) OR (multi-timeframe filtering is disabled AND the previous chart-timeframe Supertrend direction was bullish AND the current direction is bearish))
Exit: long, high >= long target OR low <= long stop OR (reversal exits enabled AND previous chart-timeframe Supertrend direction was bullish AND current direction is bearish)
Exit: short, low <= short target OR high >= short stop OR (reversal exits enabled AND previous chart-timeframe Supertrend direction was bearish AND current direction is bullish)
