Calculation: Oscillator OHLC = the selected oscillator applied separately to chart open, high, low, and close; choices are RSI, stochastic, center of gravity, MACD line, TSI, CCI, CMO, or MFI
Calculation: Oscillator trailing-stop direction = initialize long; true range is max(current oscillator high, previous oscillator close) − min(current oscillator low, previous oscillator close); ATR is a selected moving average of true range multiplied by a factor. Long stop is oscillator close − ATR, ratcheted to max(current, previous long stop) when previous oscillator low (wick mode) or close (otherwise) is above the previous long stop. Short stop is oscillator close + ATR, ratcheted to min(current, previous short stop) when previous oscillator high (wick mode) or close (otherwise) is below the previous short stop. Switch to long when current oscillator high (wick mode) or close (otherwise) is above the previous short stop; switch to short when current oscillator low (wick mode) or close (otherwise) is below the previous long stop; otherwise retain direction.
Long: Oscillator trailing-stop direction is long AND datetime is within the configured start and end times
Short: Oscillator trailing-stop direction is short AND datetime is within the configured start and end times
Exit: long, Oscillator trailing-stop direction is short
Exit: short, Oscillator trailing-stop direction is long
