Calculation: SOPR = value of spent outputs divided by their acquisition value
Calculation: Smoothed SOPR ratio = SMA(short-lookback EMA of daily SOPR divided by long-lookback EMA of daily SOPR, smoothing lookback); daily is a higher timeframe than the chart and uses the last completed daily bar
Long: Smoothed SOPR ratio < oversold threshold
Short: Smoothed SOPR ratio > overbought threshold
