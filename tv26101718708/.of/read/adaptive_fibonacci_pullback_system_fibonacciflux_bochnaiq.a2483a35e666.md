Calculation: Average Supertrend = arithmetic mean of three Supertrend lines using the same ATR length and successively larger factors; unavailable on the first bar.
Calculation: Smoothed Supertrend = EMA of Average Supertrend.
Calculation: Adaptive midline = mean of an adaptive high band and adaptive low band. Set the adaptation coefficient to the square of the SMA of an indicator that is 1 when the rolling highest high increases or the rolling lowest low decreases from the previous bar, and 0 otherwise; use the same lookback for the extrema and SMA. Each band equals its previous value plus the coefficient times the difference between current high or low, respectively, and its previous value; initialize each band to its corresponding current price when the update is unavailable.
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR on the most recent bar satisfying either entry signal, updated even while a position is open.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price minus, for a long, or plus, for a short, either a side-specific percentage of entry price or a side-specific multiple of Entry ATR.
Calculation: Stop = initial stop when trailing is off; when trailing is on, the long stop is the maximum of initial stop, previous stop and current high less the selected percentage of high or multiple of Entry ATR, and the short stop is the minimum of initial stop, previous stop and current low plus the selected percentage of low or multiple of Entry ATR. Trailing can instead be enabled only after the profit target is touched. If break-even is enabled and the target has been touched, also constrain the long stop to at least entry price or the short stop to at most entry price. Initialize the previous stop to the initial stop; reset it when flat.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, either a side-specific percentage of entry price, a side-specific multiple of Entry ATR, or a side-specific multiple of the absolute distance from entry price to initial stop. The target is available until first touched during the position; record that touch and reset it when flat.
Calculation: Trailing profit stop = after price reaches the profit target, the highest subsequent price minus, for a long, or the lowest subsequent price plus, for a short, either a percentage of the profit target or a multiple of Entry ATR. The percentage-based offset becomes unavailable when the profit target becomes unavailable.
Long: Long signals enabled AND low < Average Supertrend AND close > Smoothed Supertrend AND previous close > current Adaptive midline AND close > Adaptive midline AND (RSI filter disabled OR lower-timeframe RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar > long threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR lower-timeframe MACD line on the last completed lower-timeframe bar > its signal line) AND (volume filter disabled OR lower-timeframe volume on the last completed lower-timeframe bar > its lower-timeframe SMA multiplied by a threshold). The global filter switch can disable all three filters.
Short: Short signals enabled AND high > Average Supertrend AND close < Smoothed Supertrend AND previous close < current Adaptive midline AND close < Adaptive midline AND (RSI filter disabled OR lower-timeframe RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar < short threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR lower-timeframe MACD line on the last completed lower-timeframe bar < its signal line) AND (volume filter disabled OR lower-timeframe volume on the last completed lower-timeframe bar > its lower-timeframe SMA multiplied by a threshold). The global filter switch can disable all three filters.
Long: Average Supertrend > previous Average Supertrend.
Short: Average Supertrend <= previous Average Supertrend.
Long: Smoothed Supertrend > previous Smoothed Supertrend.
Short: Smoothed Supertrend <= previous Smoothed Supertrend.
Long: Average Supertrend > Smoothed Supertrend.
Short: Average Supertrend <= Smoothed Supertrend.
Long: Adaptive midline > previous Adaptive midline.
Short: Adaptive midline <= previous Adaptive midline.
Exit: long, previous close < current Smoothed Supertrend AND close two bars ago >= previous Smoothed Supertrend.
Exit: short, previous close > current Smoothed Supertrend AND close two bars ago <= previous Smoothed Supertrend.
Exit: long, low <= active Stop OR high >= active Profit target OR trailing profit enabled AND low <= active Trailing profit stop; apply the configured exit quantity percentage.
Exit: short, high >= active Stop OR low <= active Profit target OR trailing profit enabled AND high >= active Trailing profit stop; apply the configured exit quantity percentage.
