Calculation: EWO = shorter-period moving average of a chosen price source minus longer-period moving average of the same source, using the same chosen moving-average method.
Calculation: Lobe = a maximal consecutive sequence of strictly positive or strictly negative EWO values; zero ends a lobe. Ignore the initial lobe if it began before the first valid EWO bar. Its oscillator pivot is its most extreme EWO value, using the latest bar on a tie; its price is the lowest low for a negative lobe or highest high for a positive lobe across the entire lobe. A lobe is ready when it has ended and at least a configurable number of bars have closed after its oscillator pivot. At each pivot update, its anchor strength is the pivot's absolute EWO divided by the maximum same-side EWO amplitude over a configurable lookback ending at that pivot, including the available partial history.
Calculation: Eligible anchor = a previously ready lobe on the current lobe's side, whose pivot is within the configurable minimum and maximum pivot distances, whose ready bar is no later than the current lobe's ready bar, and whose EWO has not been exceeded in the more extreme direction before the current pivot. Treat an excess no greater than max(1, absolute anchor EWO) multiplied by 1e-10 as no excess. If the anchor-strength filter is enabled, its strength must be at least the greater of two configurable thresholds. Select the eligible anchor with the most extreme EWO; choose the earliest pivot on a tie. Retain lobes until their pivot is older than the current bar by more than the maximum pivot distance plus the confirmation delay.
Calculation: Selected divergence = a divergence evaluated when the current lobe becomes ready against its selected eligible anchor. If the largest-divergence filter is enabled, accept it only if its absolute price difference exceeds the previously accepted difference for the same anchor and divergence type by more than max(1, absolute anchor price) multiplied by 1e-10, or is within that tolerance and its pivot is no earlier than the previous pivot. Retain these records until the anchor pivot is older than the current bar by more than the maximum pivot distance plus the confirmation delay. Evaluate chart-timeframe signals from the last completed chart bar. For a higher scanning timeframe use its last completed bar; for a lower scanning timeframe process each completed lower-timeframe bar.
Long: Selected negative-lobe divergence AND ((regular divergence enabled AND current lobe price < anchor price AND current lobe EWO >= anchor EWO) OR (hidden divergence enabled AND current lobe price > anchor price AND current lobe EWO < anchor EWO)).
Short: Selected positive-lobe divergence AND ((regular divergence enabled AND current lobe price > anchor price AND current lobe EWO <= anchor EWO) OR (hidden divergence enabled AND current lobe price < anchor price AND current lobe EWO > anchor EWO)).
