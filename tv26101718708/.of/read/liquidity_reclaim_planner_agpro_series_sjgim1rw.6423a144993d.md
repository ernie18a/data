Calculation: True range average = ATR on chart bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of chart bars on both sides; becomes available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Active liquidity reference = latest unswept confirmed swing high/low no older than the configured maximum age.
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = current low is below the active swing low by at least the configured minimum ATR fraction, with no simultaneous qualifying buy-side sweep or with the sell-side sweep depth at least as large; buy-side sweep is symmetric, and only one event is selected.
Calculation: Sweep depth = distance beyond the active reference divided by ATR; for a sell-side event use reference minus event low, for a buy-side event use event high minus reference, retaining the greatest depth until reclaim or failure.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its chart-bar moving average; use 1 when volume or its average is unavailable or the average is nonpositive.
Calculation: Reclaim close = close above the active sell-side reference plus the configured ATR buffer, or below the active buy-side reference minus that buffer.
Calculation: Timely reclaim = reclaim close occurs no later than the configured number of chart bars after the event.
Calculation: Reclaim score = clamp to 0–100 of 0.20 × sweep-depth score + 0.32 × reclaim-close score + 0.16 × time score + 0.14 × relative-volume score + 0.18 × room score. Sweep-depth score peaks at the configured ideal depth, rising linearly from the minimum depth to ideal and falling linearly to zero at maximum useful depth. Before reclaim, reclaim-close score is 66 × clamp(reclaim progress from event extreme toward reference to 0–1); after reclaim, it is 0.65 × normalized reclaim distance score + 0.35 × normalized close-location score. Normalize reclaim distance from 0 at 0.02 ATR to 100 at the configured strong-reclaim ATR; close-location score normalizes close position within the bar from 0 at 0.48 to 100 at 0.88, using the opposite position for a buy-side event. Time score falls linearly from 100 at event age 0 to 0 at reclaim-window length plus 2 bars. Relative-volume score rises linearly from 0 at 0.75 to 100 at the configured volume-response ratio. Room score rises linearly from 0 at 0.55 times minimum room ATR to 100 at 2.60 times minimum room ATR.
Calculation: Target room = distance from close to the highest high of prior chart bars over the configured room lookback for a sell-side event, or to the lowest low over that lookback for a buy-side event, divided by ATR; use zero unless the reference is beyond the active liquidity level in the reclaim direction.
Calculation: Failure line = event extreme minus 0.08 ATR for a sell-side event, or event extreme plus 0.08 ATR for a buy-side event.
Long: sell-side sweep OR (active sell-side event AND timely reclaim AND close above active swing low plus ATR buffer) OR (active sell-side event AND unreclaimed, unexpired, unfailed AND reclaim score at least the configured watch threshold) OR (active sell-side event AND reclaimed, unfailed AND ((reclaim score at least the configured ready threshold AND target room at least configured minimum room) OR target room below configured minimum room OR (reclaim score below ready threshold AND target room at least configured minimum room))).
Short: buy-side sweep OR (active buy-side event AND timely reclaim AND close below active swing high minus ATR buffer) OR (active buy-side event AND unreclaimed, unexpired, unfailed AND reclaim score at least the configured watch threshold) OR (active buy-side event AND reclaimed, unfailed AND ((reclaim score at least the configured ready threshold AND target room at least configured minimum room) OR target room below configured minimum room OR (reclaim score below ready threshold AND target room at least configured minimum room))).
Exit: long, close below the active sell-side failure line after reclaim.
Exit: short, close above the active buy-side failure line after reclaim.
