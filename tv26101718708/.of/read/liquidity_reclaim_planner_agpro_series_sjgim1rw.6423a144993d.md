Calculation: Liquidity reference = the most recently confirmed pivot high or low, using equal left and right pivot strengths. Its age is measured from the pivot bar. A reference is eligible until its maximum age is exceeded or it is selected for a sweep; a newly confirmed pivot replaces the previous reference on that side.
Calculation: Sweep depth = max(0, reference minus low) divided by ATR for a low reference, or max(0, high minus reference) divided by ATR for a high reference. A sweep occurs when the eligible reference's depth reaches the minimum sweep threshold. If both sides sweep on the same bar, select the greater depth, choosing the low reference on a tie.
Calculation: Active plan = the selected sweep's direction, reference, event bar and extreme; a new sweep replaces the previous plan. The direction is long for a swept low and short for a swept high. Until reclaimed or failed, update the extreme to the lowest low or highest high since the event, respectively. A plan is reclaimed when, within the reclaim window, close is above the reference plus an ATR buffer for a long plan or below the reference minus an ATR buffer for a short plan. An unreclaimed plan expires when its age exceeds the reclaim window. After reclaim, it fails when close is below the extreme minus an ATR buffer for a long plan or above the extreme plus an ATR buffer for a short plan. Reclaim, expiry and failure persist until a new sweep replaces the plan.
Long: Active plan direction = long AND plan is not failed AND plan is not expired.
Short: Active plan direction = short AND plan is not failed AND plan is not expired.
Exit: long, active plan direction = long AND plan becomes failed.
Exit: short, active plan direction = short AND plan becomes failed.
