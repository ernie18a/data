Calculation: Active swing levels = Confirm pivot highs and lows on last completed higher-timeframe bars using the same number of bars on each side. In all-pivots mode, retain the most recent unbroken levels per side. In primary mode, retain one level per side and accept a new pivot only when that side has no active level; track the opposite extreme since each active swing formed, and when a swing is removed, replace the opposite swing with that leg extreme if it has not already been exceeded by the current bar. A high level is swept when high >= level and broken when close > level; a low level is swept when low <= level and broken when close < level. Remove broken levels if the broken-level filter is enabled, and remove swept levels if the sweep-removal filter is enabled. Only the first sweep of a naked level starts a setup; if several levels on one side are swept together, use the highest high level or lowest low level.
Calculation: Sweep-rejection setup = On a swing-low sweep, record its level and the chart bar's low; until rejection, update the extreme to the lowest subsequent low. A confirmed close > the level within the rejection window establishes a long setup. On a swing-high sweep, record its level and the chart bar's high; until rejection, update the extreme to the highest subsequent high. A confirmed close < the level within the rejection window establishes a short setup. Replace an existing setup when another swing on that side is swept. Cancel an unrejected setup when the rejection window expires; cancel a rejected setup when its validity duration expires or price moves strictly beyond its recorded extreme.
Calculation: Range budget = A selectable mean, median, or exponential moving average of high minus low over completed higher-timeframe bars. Track the high and low of the current, still-forming higher-timeframe session from its configured session boundary. Lower projection = session high - range budget; upper projection = session low + range budget.
Long: At least one of the sweep-rejection and range-budget rules is enabled AND (the sweep-rejection rule is disabled OR a long sweep-rejection setup is valid) AND (the range-budget rule is disabled OR the reference low - lower projection <= the configured absolute limit or configured fraction of the range budget); the reference low is the recorded sweep extreme when a setup exists, otherwise the current low.
Short: At least one of the sweep-rejection and range-budget rules is enabled AND (the sweep-rejection rule is disabled OR a short sweep-rejection setup is valid) AND (the range-budget rule is disabled OR upper projection - the reference high <= the configured absolute limit or configured fraction of the range budget); the reference high is the recorded sweep extreme when a setup exists, otherwise the current high.
