Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed by equal numbers of bars on each side, using a shorter lookback for internal pivots and a longer lookback for external pivots. A structure is bullish when its latest high and low pivots both exceed their respective preceding pivots, and bearish when both are lower.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average, or one when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Structure breaks = on a completed chart bar, an upward break occurs when close exceeds the latest confirmed internal or external pivot high, the preceding close is at or below that same pivot, and relative volume is at least its threshold; a downward break is the corresponding crossing below a pivot low. Track internal and external breaks separately.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially neutral; set bullish on an upward structure break, otherwise set bearish on a downward structure break, otherwise retain the preceding trend.
Calculation: Failed structure breaks = retain the latest broken level and bar separately for upward and downward breaks, preferring the external level when both same-direction breaks occur together. An upward break fails when a subsequent close is below its retained level within the failure lookback; a downward break fails when a subsequent close is above its retained level within that lookback. Clear the retained level after failure or expiry.
Calculation: Log-regression slope = linear-regression endpoint of log(close) minus its value a slope-lookback number of chart bars earlier.
Calculation: Structure display direction = select the first applicable condition in this order: upward change of character, downward change of character, external upward break, external downward break, internal upward break, internal downward break, failed downward break, failed upward break, bullish external structure, bearish external structure, bullish internal structure, bearish internal structure, bullish structure trend, bearish structure trend. The direction is bullish for upward breaks, upward change of character, failed downward breaks, and bullish structures or trend; bearish for their opposites. An upward change of character requires an upward break, a preceding bearish structure trend or a current bearish internal or external structure, and positive log-regression slope; a downward change of character uses the opposite conditions and negative slope.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe regime = compare the higher-timeframe close with its shorter EMA and that EMA with a longer EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime. The regime is bullish when close > shorter EMA > longer EMA and bearish when close < shorter EMA < longer EMA.
Calculation: kNN forecast = on completed chart bars after a warmup, store a rolling dictionary of historical patterns ending a forecast horizon plus a purge gap before the current bar. Each pattern contains, for each bar in its pattern lookback, log(close/previous close)/(ATR/close), (high-low)/ATR, (close-open)/ATR, (upper wick-lower wick)/ATR, signed log(relative volume) with sign determined by close >= open, and (close-EMA(close))/ATR; volume and trend features can independently be disabled. Apply positive numerical floors to denominators and the volume logarithm. Label each pattern with the log return from its ending close to the close one forecast horizon later. Compare the current pattern to dictionary patterns by the square root of the weighted mean squared feature differences across pattern bars, normalized by total feature weight. Once the dictionary contains at least the larger of the neighbor count and a minimum sample count, average the labels of the nearest neighbors using either equal or inverse-distance weights, then optionally smooth with an EMA. Confidence is the larger of the positive-label and nonpositive-label neighbor counts divided by the neighbor count.
Calculation: Directional scores = clamp each component and the final scores to their score ranges. The kNN components start at a neutral midpoint and add or subtract a capped magnitude proportional to abs(forecast/(ATR/close times square root of forecast horizon)) plus a confidence adjustment proportional to confidence minus its neutral midpoint; the bullish component adds this amount when forecast >= zero and the bearish component adds it when forecast < zero, with opposite signs otherwise; use neutral scores when the forecast is unavailable. For regression, calculate the standard deviation of log-close regression residuals and position as the current residual divided by that deviation, or zero when the deviation is not positive. The bullish regression component is the neutral midpoint plus a weighted slope/deviation minus a penalty for positive position; the bearish component is the midpoint minus the weighted slope/deviation plus a penalty for negative position. The bullish structure component sums a base determined by whether structure trend is bullish and separate weights for bullish internal structure, bullish external structure, internal upward break, external upward break, upward change of character, and failed downward break; the bearish component mirrors these conditions. Each volume component sums weights for relative volume in its high, medium, or low category, a same-direction candle, a same-direction close change with increasing volume, the corresponding sign of the change in an EMA of OBV over a slope lookback, and volume above its moving average. Each final score is a weighted sum of its kNN, regression, structure, and volume components, plus an optional higher-timeframe adjustment that rewards its own regime and penalizes the opposite regime.
Long: Bullish directional score >= bearish directional score.
Short: Bearish directional score > bullish directional score.
Long: Structure display direction = bullish OR structure trend = bullish.
Short: Structure display direction = bearish OR structure trend = bearish.
Long: Internal upward structure break OR failed downward structure break.
Short: Internal downward structure break OR failed upward structure break.
Long: Log-regression slope > zero.
Short: Log-regression slope < zero.
Long: Higher-timeframe close > shorter higher-timeframe EMA AND shorter higher-timeframe EMA > longer higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe close < shorter higher-timeframe EMA AND shorter higher-timeframe EMA < longer higher-timeframe EMA.
