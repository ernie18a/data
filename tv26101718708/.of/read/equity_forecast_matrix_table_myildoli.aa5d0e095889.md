Calculation: Bull and bear scores = 40% directional kNN score + 20% directional regression score + 25% directional structure score + 15% directional volume score, plus weekly-regime adjustment; the weekly regime uses the higher-timeframe weekly close and 10- and 30-bar EMAs from the still-forming weekly bar.
Calculation: Bullish invalidation = latest confirmed internal swing low minus an ATR buffer; if unavailable, use the latest confirmed external swing low minus the buffer, otherwise the lower regression band. Bearish invalidation uses the corresponding swing highs plus the buffer, otherwise the upper regression band.
Long: bull score − bear score ≥ the minimum directional edge OR confirmed close crosses above the latest internal or external swing high with relative volume above its threshold OR a bullish change of character occurs OR a bearish break of structure fails within its lookback OR bullish structure is present (latest internal or external swing high and low both exceed their respective prior swings, or the persistent structure trend is bullish)
Short: bear score − bull score ≥ the minimum directional edge OR confirmed close crosses below the latest internal or external swing low with relative volume above its threshold OR a bearish change of character occurs OR a bullish break of structure fails within its lookback OR bearish structure is present (latest internal or external swing high and low both fall below their respective prior swings, or the persistent structure trend is bearish)
Exit: long, close < bullish invalidation
Exit: short, close > bearish invalidation
