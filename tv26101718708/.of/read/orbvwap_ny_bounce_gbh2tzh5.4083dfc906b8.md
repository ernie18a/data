Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low within the configured opening-range session; reset at the start of each trading session.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 over bars in the current trading session, reset at its start. Overnight VWAP uses only bars in the overnight session and holds its final value afterward. Previous-session-anchored VWAP combines the previous and current trading-session sums. Extended-session VWAP resets at the overnight-session start and includes every subsequent bar. High-of-day and low-of-day VWAPs reset whenever the current high equals or exceeds the calendar day's highest high or the current low equals or falls below its lowest low, respectively.
Calculation: Positioning score = sum of comparisons of close with session, overnight, previous-session-anchored, high-of-day and low-of-day VWAPs, assigning +1 above, -1 below and 0 at an unavailable or equal level, plus +1 above the opening-range high, -1 below the opening-range low, or 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Entry limit = selected session or extended-session VWAP plus a configurable nonnegative price offset for a long, or minus that offset for a short; refresh it on each qualifying bar and cancel it when its setup no longer qualifies.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a long, entry limit minus a fixed distance; OR entry limit minus a multiple of ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar minus a buffer, falling back to the fixed distance when ATR is unavailable; OR session entry VWAP minus a buffer; OR opening-range low minus a buffer; OR the lowest low over a configurable lookback including the current bar minus a buffer. For a short, mirror these formulas above the entry limit using the opening-range high and highest high.
Calculation: Initial risk = absolute difference between actual entry price and initial stop. Initial target = entry limit plus for a long or minus for a short a configurable multiple of the planned entry-limit-to-stop distance, a configurable multiple of opening-range size, or a fixed distance; alternatively no target.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop, optionally moved once to entry price when the current high reaches entry price plus the current entry-to-stop distance for a long, or the current low reaches entry price minus that distance for a short; optionally tighten further to the highest high since entry minus a fixed distance or a multiple of initial risk for a long, or the lowest low since entry plus that distance for a short, once the favorable extreme has moved by a configurable multiple of initial risk. The stop never loosens under trailing management.
Long: Close > any of the five defined VWAPs.
Short: Close < any of the five defined VWAPs.
Long: Close > opening-range high.
Short: Close < opening-range low.
Long: Positioning score >= a configurable positive trend threshold.
Short: Positioning score <= the negative of a configurable positive trend threshold.
Long: Flat position AND opening-range session completed AND at least one close since completion > opening-range high AND current close > selected entry VWAP AND current time within entry window AND session trade count < configured maximum AND weekday and month permitted AND optional opening-range size or opening-range size divided by ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar within configured bounds AND optional positioning score >= configured threshold AND planned entry limit > initial stop AND planned stop distance within configured bounds AND a previously placed and still valid buy limit is reached by price.
Short: Flat position AND opening-range session completed AND at least one close since completion < opening-range low AND current close < selected entry VWAP AND current time within entry window AND session trade count < configured maximum AND weekday and month permitted AND optional short-specific time, month and weekday restrictions satisfied AND optional opening-range size or opening-range size divided by ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar within configured bounds AND optional positioning score <= negative configured threshold AND planned entry limit < initial stop AND planned stop distance within configured bounds AND a previously placed and still valid sell limit is reached by price.
Exit: long, price <= managed stop OR price >= initial target when a target is enabled and trailing with target removal is not enabled OR current time >= configured forced-flat time when forced flat is enabled.
Exit: short, price >= managed stop OR price <= initial target when a target is enabled and trailing with target removal is not enabled OR current time >= configured forced-flat time when forced flat is enabled.
