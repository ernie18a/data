Calculation: Real body = abs(close − open); upper shadow = high − max(open, close); lower shadow = min(open, close) − low; range = high − low (use a small positive floor for zero body or range in ratios).
Calculation: Prior uptrend = close from the prior bar minus close from N bars before it exceeds 0.3 ATR, and prior close > its EMA; prior downtrend = the inverse. For Morning/Evening Star and Three White Soldiers/Three Black Crows, use the same price-change test over N bars ending at the close two bars ago, without the EMA test.
Calculation: Pattern score = round to 0.1 of 10 × (0.40 × shape fit + 0.22 × min(1, range/(1.3 ATR)) + 0.18 × context strength + 0.12 × min(1, volume/(1.5 × average volume)) + 0.08 × max(0, 1 − abs(close − EMA)/(2 ATR))). Context strength = min(1, favorable prior-trend move/(0.22 × ATR × N)); use the corresponding up or down move for the candidate pattern. Set a candidate score to zero unless its pattern, context, and minimum-range conditions pass. The minimum-range condition is range ≥ selected ATR multiple, unless that multiple is zero.
Calculation: Bullish candlestick patterns = bullish Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Dragonfly Doji, Marubozu, Engulfing, Harami, Piercing Line, Tweezer Bottom, Window, Morning Star, Three White Soldiers, or Rising Three Methods, as defined below. Bearish candlestick patterns are the corresponding Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Gravestone Doji, bearish Marubozu, Engulfing, Harami, Dark Cloud Cover, Tweezer Top, Window, Evening Star, Three Black Crows, or Falling Three Methods.
Calculation: Hammer shape = body ≤ selected small-body fraction of range, body > selected doji fraction of range, lower shadow ≥ selected multiple of body, upper shadow ≤ 0.6 body, and body low ≥ low + 0.45 × range. Inverted Hammer shape = same body tests, upper shadow ≥ selected multiple of body, lower shadow ≤ 0.6 body, and body high ≤ high − 0.45 × range. Dragonfly Doji = body ≤ selected doji fraction of range, lower shadow ≥ 0.55 range, upper shadow ≤ 0.12 range. Gravestone Doji = inverse shadows. Marubozu = candle direction agrees with pattern direction and body ≥ selected fraction of range.
Calculation: Bullish Engulfing = prior candle bearish, current bullish, current close ≥ prior body high, current open ≤ prior body low, current body ≥ selected multiple of prior body, and prior body > 0. Bearish Engulfing = inverse. Bullish Harami = prior bearish, current bullish, prior body contains current body, prior body > 1.4 × current body, and prior body > 0. Bearish Harami = inverse.
Calculation: Piercing Line = prior bearish, current bullish, current open < prior low, current close > prior body midpoint and < prior open, and prior body > 0. Dark Cloud Cover = prior bullish, current bearish, current open > prior high, current close < prior body midpoint and > prior open, and prior body > 0.
Calculation: Tweezer Bottom = prior bearish, current bullish, and absolute difference between lows ≤ selected fraction of ATR. Tweezer Top = prior bullish, current bearish, and absolute difference between highs ≤ selected fraction of ATR. Bullish Window = current low > prior high. Bearish Window = current high < prior low.
Calculation: Morning Star = candle two bars ago bearish with body > 0; prior body ≤ selected fraction of the average of its body and current body; current bullish and close > the midpoint of the candle two bars ago. Evening Star = inverse. Three White Soldiers = three bullish candles with successively higher closes; current open < prior close, prior open > prior open's preceding close, and each of those opens lies above the next older close but below the older open; current body > 0.5 × current range and the two older bodies > 0. Three Black Crows = inverse. Rising Three Methods = bullish candle four bars ago with body > 0.6 ATR, current bullish and close > that candle's close, and each of the three intervening candles is inside its high-low range. Falling Three Methods = inverse.
Calculation: Shape fit for Hammer = 0.6 × min(1, lower-shadow/body/(1.5 × selected shadow multiple)) + 0.4 × max(0, 1 − upper-shadow/body). Inverted Hammer uses upper and lower shadows reversed. For Dragonfly and Gravestone Doji use min(1, directional long shadow/(0.6 × range)); for Engulfing use min(1, current body/prior body/(selected body multiple + 0.6)); for Harami use min(1, prior body/current body/2.2); for Piercing Line and Dark Cloud Cover use min(1, penetration beyond the prior body midpoint/(0.5 × prior body)); for Tweezers use max(0, 1 − absolute difference/(selected ATR tolerance)); for Windows use min(1, gap/(0.5 ATR)); for Morning/Evening Star use min(1, directional close penetration beyond the older candle midpoint/(0.5 × older body)). Marubozu shape fit = min(1, body/(selected body fraction × range)); Three White Soldiers and Three Black Crows shape fit = 0.85; Rising and Falling Three Methods = 0.9.
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, at least one enabled bullish pattern is valid, its score is greater than zero and is the highest score among enabled candidates, the direction filter permits bullish signals, the score meets the selected strong-only threshold if that filter is enabled, and the minimum-bars-between-signals spacing condition passes.
Short: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, at least one enabled bearish pattern is valid, its score is greater than zero and is the highest score among enabled candidates, the direction filter permits bearish signals, the score meets the selected strong-only threshold if that filter is enabled, and the minimum-bars-between-signals spacing condition passes.
