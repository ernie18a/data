Calculation: Candle geometry = for bar i, where i=0 is the current completed bar and i>0 is i bars ago, B_i=abs(close_i-open_i), H_i=max(open_i,close_i), L_i=min(open_i,close_i), R_i=high_i-low_i, U_i=high_i-H_i, D_i=L_i-low_i; bullish means close_i>open_i and bearish means close_i<open_i. Replace a zero range or body with a positive minimum price increment only where it appears as a divisor or in a shadow comparison; retain the original value elsewhere.
Calculation: Trend context = let A be current ATR, E be EMA of close, N be the trend lookback, and enough mean the current zero-based bar index is greater than N+6. Set rise1=close_1-close_(1+N) and rise2=close_2-close_(2+N) when enough, otherwise set both to zero. Prior up1 means enough AND rise1>0.3*A AND close_1>E_1; prior down1 means enough AND -rise1>0.3*A AND close_1<E_1. Prior up2 means enough AND rise2>0.3*A; prior down2 means enough AND -rise2>0.3*A.

A candidate must also have its pattern group enabled and R_0>=A times the minimum range parameter, unless that parameter is nonpositive. A reversal candidate requires the stated prior trend when trend gating is enabled; a continuation candidate requires the stated prior trend when trend gating is enabled. Calculation: Candidate score = for each eligible candidate, calculate size=min(1,R_0/(1.3*A+epsilon)), volume quality=min(1,volume_0/(1.5*SMA(volume))) if that SMA is positive and 0.5 otherwise, confluence=max(0,1-abs(close_0-E_0)/(2*A+epsilon)), up1=min(1,max(0,rise1)/(A*N*0.22+epsilon)), down1=min(1,max(0,-rise1)/(A*N*0.22+epsilon)), up2=min(1,max(0,rise2)/(A*N*0.22+epsilon)), and down2=min(1,max(0,-rise2)/(A*N*0.22+epsilon)). Use a small positive epsilon. Shape quality by candidate number is: 0–1, 0.6*min(1,D_0/(positive B_0*1.5*T))+0.4*max(0,1-U_0/(positive B_0+epsilon)); 2–3, the same expression with U_0 and D_0 exchanged; 4, min(1,D_0/(0.6*positive R_0)); 5, min(1,U_0/(0.6*positive R_0)); 6, min(1,(U_0+D_0)/(0.8*positive R_0)); 7–8, min(1,B_0/(positive R_0*M)); 9–10, min(1,B_0/(B_1+epsilon)/(G+0.6)); 11–12, min(1,B_1/(B_0+epsilon)/2.2); 13, min(1,(close_0-(open_1+close_1)/2)/(0.5*B_1+epsilon)); 14, min(1,((open_1+close_1)/2-close_0)/(0.5*B_1+epsilon)); 15, max(0,1-abs(low_0-low_1)/(Q+epsilon)); 16, max(0,1-abs(high_0-high_1)/(Q+epsilon)); 17, min(1,(low_0-high_1)/(0.5*A+epsilon)); 18, min(1,(low_1-high_0)/(0.5*A+epsilon)); 19, min(1,(close_0-(open_2+close_2)/2)/(0.5*B_2+epsilon)); 20, min(1,((open_2+close_2)/2-close_0)/(0.5*B_2+epsilon)); 21–22, 0.85; 23–24, 0.9. Context quality is down1 for candidates 0, 2, 4, 9, 11, 13, 15 and 24; up1 for 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 14, 16, 17 and 23; down1 for 8 and 18; max(up1,down1) for 6; down2 for 19 and 21; up2 for 20 and 22. Score=round(10*clamp(10*(0.40*shape quality+0.22*size+0.18*context quality+0.12*volume quality+0.08*confluence),0,10))/10. Ineligible candidates have score zero.
Calculation: Selected candidate = the eligible candidate with the highest strictly positive score; on equal scores select the lowest candidate number. Selection includes the neutral candidate and occurs before direction and score filters. A signal passes spacing when the number of chart bars since the last confirmed signal of any direction, including neutral, is at least the spacing parameter; initially spacing passes. Update the last-signal bar only when a selected candidate passes the direction filter, optional strong-score threshold and spacing.
Long: The selected candidate is bullish AND its direction passes the direction filter AND its score passes the strong-score threshold when that filter is enabled AND spacing passes.
Short: The selected candidate is bearish AND its direction passes the direction filter AND its score passes the strong-score threshold when that filter is enabled AND spacing passes.
