Calculation: Structure = start at 0; confirm a pivot high or low after equal numbers of bars on both sides; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each type. Set structure to 1 when close exceeds the retained pivot high plus an ATR buffer, then discard that high; set structure to -1 when close falls below the retained pivot low minus an ATR buffer, then discard that low. If both occur on the same bar, apply the low break last.
Calculation: ALMA direction = on each of four ordered timeframes, calculate ALMA and standard deviation from the previous completed timeframe bar's close. Form upper and lower bands as ALMA plus and minus a standard-deviation multiple. Keep the previous upper band unless the new upper band is lower or the previous source close was above the previous upper band; keep the previous lower band unless the new lower band is higher or the previous source close was below the previous lower band. Set direction to 1 when the source close exceeds the resulting upper band, to -1 when it falls below the resulting lower band, and otherwise retain its previous direction, initially 0. Sum the four directions for the ALMA vote. Each timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar of that timeframe.
Calculation: Delta = EMA of volume multiplied by ((close - low) - (high - close)) divided by the greater of high - low and a positive price increment, divided by the greater of the same-length EMA of volume and a positive volume floor; treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by the greater of its SMA and a positive volume floor; treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Delta score = clamp a base score plus one of two signed contributions for each of delta > 0, OBV > its SMA, close > session VWAP of typical price, and relative volume > its threshold, to a bounded range. Each contribution uses its positive value when its condition is true and its negative value otherwise.
Calculation: Kinetic state = initialize estimated price to close and velocity and acceleration to zero. Predict price as prior estimated price + prior velocity + half prior acceleration, and velocity as prior velocity + prior acceleration. Residual = close - predicted price. Apply an increased gain when absolute residual divided by the greater of ATR and a positive price increment exceeds a threshold. Update estimated price, velocity, and acceleration by adding their respective gains times the gain multiplier times residual to predicted price, predicted velocity, and prior acceleration. Kinetic trend = updated velocity divided by the greater of ATR and the positive price increment.
Calculation: Regression confidence = clamp 1 - variance of regression residuals divided by variance of close to a bounded range; use zero when close variance is zero. Each residual is that bar's close minus the endpoint of its rolling linear regression of close. Regression slope = current regression endpoint minus its endpoint a fixed number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Directional score = sum the configured weights for conditions that hold, bounded to a configured range. Long conditions are structure = 1; last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA; ALMA vote >= positive threshold; kinetic trend > positive threshold AND acceleration >= 0; regression confidence >= threshold AND regression slope > 0; delta score > upper threshold; close > shorter EMA AND shorter EMA > longer EMA. Short conditions mirror these: structure = -1; last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA; ALMA vote <= negative threshold; kinetic trend < negative threshold AND acceleration <= 0; regression confidence >= threshold AND regression slope < 0; delta score < lower threshold; close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA. Corresponding long and short conditions have equal weights.
Calculation: Trailing stop = while long, the running maximum since position entry of close minus an ATR multiple; while short, the running minimum since position entry of close plus an ATR multiple. Initialize on the first bar with the position.
Long: Long score > short score.
Short: Short score > long score.
Long: Delta score >= its midpoint.
Short: Delta score < its midpoint.
Long: ALMA vote >= 0.
Short: ALMA vote < 0.
Long: Kinetic trend >= 0.
Short: Kinetic trend < 0.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND bars since the last executed entry >= cooldown, or no previous entry, AND ATR / close > volatility threshold AND long score >= entry threshold AND delta > positive pressure threshold AND relative volume >= threshold.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND bars since the last executed entry >= cooldown, or no previous entry, AND ATR / close > volatility threshold AND short score >= entry threshold AND delta < negative pressure threshold AND relative volume >= threshold.
Exit: Long, low <= the greater of entry price minus the current ATR stop multiple and the trailing stop when trailing is enabled, otherwise low <= entry price minus the current ATR stop multiple.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price plus the current ATR target multiple.
Exit: Short, high >= the lesser of entry price plus the current ATR stop multiple and the trailing stop when trailing is enabled, otherwise high >= entry price plus the current ATR stop multiple.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price minus the current ATR target multiple.
Exit: Long, bars since the position was first observed open >= time limit OR structure = -1 OR short score > long score plus a score margin.
Exit: Short, bars since the position was first observed open >= time limit OR structure = 1 OR long score > short score plus a score margin.
