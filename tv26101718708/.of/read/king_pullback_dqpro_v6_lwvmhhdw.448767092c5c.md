Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Bullish candle strength = close > open AND candle body ratio ≥ confirmation threshold AND close ≥ low + 60% of bar range
Calculation: Bearish candle strength = close < open AND candle body ratio ≥ confirmation threshold AND close ≤ high − 60% of bar range
Calculation: Bullish trend state = persistent uptrend state from trend engine; bearish trend state = persistent downtrend state from trend engine
Calculation: Light pullback cloud = trend-directional cloud zone; long touch means bar range or body intersects the zone, or price crosses its near edge; short touch is the mirrored condition
Calculation: RSI filters = RSI above long trend threshold and below long extreme threshold, or below short trend threshold and above short extreme threshold; additionally, the highest RSI over the history lookback is below the long history threshold for longs, or the lowest RSI is above the short history threshold for shorts, and RSI range over the lookback is within its maximum
Calculation: Strong directional trend = matching +DI/−DI dominance AND ADX above trend threshold
Calculation: Opposing pressure = volume above its 20-bar average multiplier AND an opposite-color candle with body ratio ≥ pressure threshold
Calculation: Directional confirmation candle = matching candle color, body ratio ≥ 0.28, close in the directional 55% of the bar, and, when volume is strong, directional close progress or volume at least the prior bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA(shorter lookback) above/below EMA(longer lookback), close on the matching side of a pullback EMA, and RSI above/below 50; use the chart’s mapped higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Long stop = configured stop price; short stop = configured stop price. Long first target = configured first profit target; short first target = configured first profit target
Long: confirmed bar AND uptrend state AND session allowed AND RSI filters AND strong directional trend AND (higher-timeframe trend is bullish on H1 or H4, OR uptrend state AND M15 trend is not bearish) AND NOT opposing pressure AND NOT (bar is a consecutive light-cloud touch without a bullish rebound) AND ((pullback zone AND bullish candle strength AND close above basis AND close is within the ATR-based distance of the applicable stop-pullback line) OR (fresh light-cloud touch OR consecutive light-cloud touch with bullish rebound) OR (within 3 bars after a light-cloud touch, no touch now, directional confirmation candle, close above basis)) AND order limits allow signal
Short: confirmed bar AND downtrend state AND session allowed AND RSI filters AND strong directional trend AND (higher-timeframe trend is bearish on H1 or H4, OR downtrend state AND M15 trend is not bullish) AND NOT opposing pressure AND NOT (bar is a consecutive light-cloud touch without a bearish rebound) AND ((pullback zone AND bearish candle strength AND close below basis AND close is within the ATR-based distance of the applicable stop-pullback line) OR (fresh light-cloud touch OR consecutive light-cloud touch with bearish rebound) OR (within 3 bars after a light-cloud touch, no touch now, directional confirmation candle, close below basis)) AND order limits allow signal
Exit: long position, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long first target
Exit: short position, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short first target
