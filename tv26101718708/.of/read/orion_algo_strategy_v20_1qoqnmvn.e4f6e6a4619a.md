Calculation: Orion signal = HMA of twice the average of the enabled components: MACD histogram; Williams %R; close’s position within its standard-deviation envelope; RSI of close minus its moving average; RSI of the difference between positive high and low directional movements normalized by true range; RSI of the volume-weighted close imbalance; and RSI of the stochastic close position. Each component uses its own lookback parameters.
Calculation: Prediction curve = weighted average of the Orion signal and 1.5 times its one-bar change, with the weight set by a bias parameter.
Calculation: DCA profit target = mean of the long entry fill prices × (1 + profit percentage).
Long: Orion signal crosses above its previous value OR Orion signal > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago OR, while flat in DCA mode, Orion signal > its previous value AND its previous value ≤ its value two bars ago OR, while long in DCA mode with remaining pyramid orders, Orion signal > its previous value AND its previous value ≤ its value two bars ago AND close ≤ last entry price × (1 − drop percentage).
Short: Orion signal crosses below its previous value OR Orion signal < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago OR Prediction curve > its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago.
Exit: Long, close ≥ DCA profit target AND Orion signal ≤ its previous value AND its previous value ≥ its value two bars ago OR Prediction curve < its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago.
