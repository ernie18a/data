Calculation: Signal = Hull moving average of twice the arithmetic mean of seven components; each disabled component contributes zero. The components are: (1) MACD histogram of close divided by a scaling constant, using a shorter fast EMA, a longer slow EMA, and a signal EMA; (2) Williams %R; (3) 100 × (close + 2 × standard deviation of close − SMA of close) / (4 × standard deviation of close); (4) 2 × RSI(close − SMA(close)) − 100; (5) 2 × RSI(P − M) − 100, where P is the last non-missing value of 100 × RMA(change in high if change in high > change in low AND change in high > 0, otherwise 0) / RMA(true range), and M is the last non-missing value of 100 × RMA(change in low if change in low > change in high AND change in low > 0, otherwise 0) / RMA(true range); (6) RSI(100 × (close − ((rolling sum of volume − volume) / rolling sum of volume + volume × close / rolling sum of volume)) / ((close + ((rolling sum of volume − volume) / rolling sum of volume + volume × close / rolling sum of volume)) / 2)) − 100; (7) 2 × RSI(SMA((close − rolling lowest low) / rolling highest high − rolling lowest low − 0.5)) − 100. Each indicator and rolling calculation has its own lookback parameter.
Calculation: Prediction = scaled one-bar change in Signal + bias × (Signal − scaled one-bar change in Signal).
Long: Signal > previous Signal AND previous Signal < Signal two bars ago.
Short: Signal < previous Signal AND previous Signal > Signal two bars ago.
Long: Prediction > previous Prediction AND previous Prediction < Prediction two bars ago.
Short: Prediction < previous Prediction AND previous Prediction > Prediction two bars ago.
Long: Simple strategy selected AND Signal > previous Signal AND previous Signal <= Signal two bars ago.
Short: Simple strategy selected AND Signal < previous Signal AND previous Signal >= Signal two bars ago.
Long: DCA strategy selected AND Signal > previous Signal AND previous Signal <= Signal two bars ago AND (position is flat OR (position is long AND close <= most recent entry signal close × (1 − entry drop fraction) AND number of entries <= entry limit)).
Exit: Long, DCA strategy selected AND close >= arithmetic mean of all entry signal closes in the current position × (1 + profit fraction) AND Signal <= previous Signal AND previous Signal >= Signal two bars ago.
