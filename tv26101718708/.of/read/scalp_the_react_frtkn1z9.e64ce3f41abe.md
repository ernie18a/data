Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low at the center of an odd-length window that qualifies as a Pine pivot with equal numbers of bars on each side; it becomes available only when the right side of the window has completed. The latest confirmed pivot high and low each replace their previous candidate, and each candidate can be broken once.
Calculation: Structure break = close above the latest unbroken pivot high or below the latest unbroken pivot low, or, under the wick setting, high above the pivot high or low below the pivot low. Process an upward break before a downward break when both occur on the same bar. A break is a change of character if the preceding processed break was in the opposite direction or no preceding break exists.
Calculation: Trailing moving average = the selected moving average of close, initialized to that average; on each subsequent bar use the greater of the average and previous trail when Supertrend is bullish, otherwise use the lesser. Macro bias is bullish when close > trail and bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Active Fibonacci leg = the most recently created leg, with start at its low for a bullish leg and at its high for a bearish leg, and range equal to the absolute difference between its endpoints. In scalp mode, create a bullish leg from the latest pivot low and broken pivot high, or a bearish leg from the broken pivot low and latest pivot high, only if no previous leg exists or close is outside the previous leg's endpoints. In intraday mode, create the corresponding leg only on a change of character. In higher-timeframe mode, create a leg at the start of each higher-timeframe bar using the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high and low; its direction is bullish if that bar's close >= open, otherwise bearish. In session mode, create a leg at the end of each configured session from that session's high and low, in the current macro bias direction. In opening-range mode, do the same at the end of the configured opening session. Replace the previous leg whenever a new one is created.
Calculation: Liquidity boundary = the active Fibonacci leg's start minus its direction multiplied by its range and the nearer of two liquidity projection ratios; direction is +1 for a bullish leg and -1 for a bearish leg.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = on a completed chart bar, low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > previous low + previous ATR multiplied by a threshold; its top is that bar's low and its bottom is the high two bars earlier. Bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < previous high - previous ATR multiplied by the threshold; its top is the low two bars earlier and its bottom is that bar's high. Add each gap to its directional collection on the next bar. Optionally remove overlapping older gaps using the source's strict endpoint-inside or strict containment comparisons against the newest gap of each direction.
Calculation: Gap state = an unmitigated gap becomes a breaker when, for a bullish gap, min(open, close) < bottom under body mitigation, low < bottom under wick mitigation, or low < midpoint under midpoint mitigation; reverse these comparisons for a bearish gap using max(open, close), high, and top. In gap mode remove it on mitigation. In breaker mode retain it until a bullish-origin breaker has max(open, close) > top, high > top, or high > midpoint respectively, or a bearish-origin breaker has min(open, close) < bottom, low < bottom, or low < midpoint respectively. A gap can be raided only once while unmitigated; process mitigation before raids. Only the configured number of most recent retained gaps of each direction are displayed.
Long: A new confirmed pivot high replaces the previous high candidate.
Short: A new confirmed pivot low replaces the previous low candidate.
Long: An upward structure break occurs.
Short: A downward structure break occurs.
Long: An active bullish Fibonacci leg exists AND no new leg is created on the current bar AND low < liquidity boundary AND close >= liquidity boundary.
Short: An active bearish Fibonacci leg exists AND no new leg is created on the current bar AND high > liquidity boundary AND close <= liquidity boundary.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is added AND it remains unmitigated and among the displayed retained gaps.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is added AND it remains unmitigated and among the displayed retained gaps.
Short: A bullish fair value gap becomes a retained breaker.
Long: A bearish fair value gap becomes a retained breaker.
Long: An unmitigated bullish fair value gap has not previously been raided AND low < its top AND close > its top AND it is among the displayed retained gaps.
Short: An unmitigated bearish fair value gap has not previously been raided AND high > its bottom AND close < its bottom AND it is among the displayed retained gaps.
