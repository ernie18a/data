Calculation: Range edges = highest high and lowest low over the lookback ending on the previous bar; the range is valid when its high exceeds its low.
Calculation: ATR = average true range, falling back to the current high minus low before ATR is available and floored at a positive price increment.
Calculation: Break candidate = upper when high exceeds the prior range high by more than ATR times the minimum break multiplier; lower when low is below the prior range low by more than that buffer. If both occur, choose the side with the greater absolute penetration, choosing upper on a tie.
Calculation: Active break event = maintain at most one event with its side, start bar, frozen range edges, tracked extreme, initial break quality and outcome. Before processing a candidate, clear an accepted event when close returns inside its broken edge; clear any resolved event after the resolved-event retention period; clear an unresolved event when its age exceeds the acceptance window and close is inside its broken edge, or when its age exceeds the acceptance window plus the retention period. Start a new event when a candidate exists and there is no event, its side differs from the active side, the active event is a previously confirmed trap, the active event is an accepted break on the same side and the candidate extends beyond its tracked extreme by more than the current break buffer, or the unresolved event age exceeds the acceptance window. A resolved event can be replaced by a same-side candidate only after its resolution bar. Freeze the new event's range edges and start bar, initialize its extreme to the current high for an upper event or low for a lower event, and mark it unresolved. While unresolved, update its extreme to the highest high for an upper event or lowest low for a lower event since its start, including the current bar. Event age is the number of bars since its start.
Calculation: Normalized high score = clamp 100 times (value minus lower bound) divided by (upper bound minus lower bound) to 0–100; return 0 when the bounds are equal.
Calculation: Break quality = 0.42 times the normalized high score of close distance beyond the frozen broken edge in current ATR units, with lower bound 0.03 and upper bound 2.2 times the minimum break multiplier, plus 0.18 times the normalized high score of tracked-extreme penetration beyond that edge in current ATR units, with bounds 0.7 and 3 times the minimum break multiplier, plus 20 times absolute candle body divided by candle range, plus 0.20 times the normalized high score of relative volume with lower bound 0.85 and upper bound 1.25 times the volume spike threshold. Floor candle range at a positive price increment. Relative volume is volume divided by its moving average, or 1 when that average is nonpositive; use current volume when the average is unavailable. Save this quality on the event's start bar.
Calculation: Trap risk = 0.22 times clamp(100 times (reclaim window minus event age) divided by reclaim window, 0, 100), plus 0.25 times the normalized high score of close distance inside the frozen broken edge in current ATR units with lower bound 0 and upper bound the strong-reclaim threshold, plus 0.18 times clamp(140 times the relevant wick divided by candle range, 0, 100), plus 0.15 times the normalized high score of relative volume with lower bound 0.90 and upper bound the volume spike threshold, plus 0.20 times the inside score. The relevant wick is the upper wick for an upper event and lower wick for a lower event. The inside score is 100 when close is strictly inside the broken edge, otherwise 55 when the candle touches or crosses that edge, otherwise 0. Multiply the sum by 0.65 when close remains strictly beyond the broken edge and current break quality exceeds 72; clamp the result to 0–100.
Calculation: Confirmed trap = an unresolved event whose age is at most the acceptance window, whose close is strictly inside its frozen broken edge, and whose trap risk is at least the confirmation threshold; resolve the event as a trap on that bar and retain that outcome until the event is cleared or replaced.
Calculation: Accepted break = an unresolved event with at least the acceptance-window number of consecutive closes strictly beyond its frozen broken edge, including the current close, and initial break quality at least 48; resolve the event as accepted on that bar unless it is a confirmed trap, and retain that outcome until the event is cleared or replaced. Reset the consecutive-close count to zero whenever close is not strictly beyond the edge.
