Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively highest or lowest over a configurable number of preceding bars and subsequent bars; it becomes available on the last subsequent bar. Keep the latest confirmed pivot high and low.
Calculation: Market bias = long when close is above the latest confirmed pivot high; otherwise short when close is below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the previous bias, initially neutral.
Calculation: Order block = on confirmation of a pivot low for support or pivot high for resistance, find the nearest earlier bearish or bullish candle, respectively, within the source lookback ending immediately before the pivot. Source ATR is ATR at that candle, falling back to current ATR if unavailable. Support displacement is pivot-bar high minus source low; resistance displacement is source high minus pivot-bar low. Create the block if source ATR > 0 AND displacement >= source ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold. Its midpoint is the source candle's high-low midpoint; its top and bottom are the midpoint plus and minus half a configurable multiple of source ATR. Its target is midpoint plus the target ATR multiple for support or minus it for resistance; its failure level is bottom minus the failure ATR multiple for support or top plus it for resistance. Keep at most a configurable number of active blocks per side, discarding the oldest first.
Calculation: Block origin score = displacement score plus structure-break, body-expansion, and volume-expansion points, capped at the score maximum. Displacement score is selected from an increasing step schedule based on displacement divided by the product of source ATR and the displacement threshold. Award structure-break points if detection-bar close is above source high for support or below source low for resistance. Award body-expansion points if the pivot-bar absolute candle body is at least a configurable multiple of its positive moving average; award volume-expansion points if pivot-bar volume is at least a configurable multiple of its positive moving average.
Calculation: Block location score = sum of premium/discount, pivot-confluence, trend, and bias points, capped at the score maximum, fixed at detection. Award premium/discount points when support midpoint is below or resistance midpoint is above the midpoint of the recent high-low range; if this filter is disabled, award partial points. Award pivot-confluence points when the midpoint is within a configurable multiple of source ATR of the latest pivot low for support or pivot high for resistance; award partial points if that pivot is unavailable or the filter is disabled. Award trend points when detection close is above the trend EMA for support or below it for resistance, otherwise award partial points. Award bias points when market bias matches the block side, partial points when neutral, and none otherwise.
Calculation: Block state = on each bar after creation, including the detection bar, support coverage is the top minus low divided by block height, and resistance coverage is high minus bottom divided by block height, each clipped to the interval from zero to one. Track maximum coverage and the first bar with coverage > 0. After calculating the current reaction score, resolve a touched block as a win if high reaches the support target or low reaches the resistance target; otherwise resolve it as a loss if low reaches the support failure level or high reaches the resistance failure level. Target takes precedence when both are reached on the same bar. Remove support when close < bottom and resistance when close > top, after updating resolution.
Calculation: Block reaction score = zero if current close invalidates the block; otherwise the maximum score for a resolved win, zero for a resolved loss, a neutral score if untouched, or a coverage-dependent score if touched and unresolved. The coverage schedule gives a modest score for shallow penetration, a higher score for medium penetration, a lower score for deep penetration, and a still lower score for full penetration. Block score is the weighted average of origin, location, and reaction scores, rescaled to the final score range; use equal weights if their sum is zero. Recalculate after resolution and before evaluating confirmation.
Calculation: Support confirmation state = at least one remaining active support block has score >= the minimum score minus a tolerance AND (recently touched OR near) AND (inside OR near) AND at least a configurable number of bullish checks pass. Inside means low <= top AND high >= bottom; near means low <= top plus a configurable current-ATR distance AND close >= bottom minus that distance; recently touched means the first touch occurred within a configurable number of bars. The checks are close >= fast EMA; fast EMA >= slower EMA OR fast EMA >= its previous value; RSI > a lower threshold AND RSI >= its previous value; MACD >= its signal OR MACD >= its previous value; close >= open OR close >= previous close; and volume divided by its positive moving average >= a relative-volume threshold, using zero when the average is not positive.
Calculation: Resistance confirmation state = at least one remaining active resistance block has score >= the minimum score minus a tolerance AND (recently touched OR near) AND (inside OR near) AND at least a configurable number of bearish checks pass. Inside means high >= bottom AND low <= top; near means high >= bottom minus a configurable current-ATR distance AND close <= top plus that distance; recently touched means the first touch occurred within a configurable number of bars. The checks are close <= fast EMA; fast EMA <= slower EMA OR fast EMA <= its previous value; RSI < an upper threshold AND RSI <= its previous value; MACD <= its signal OR MACD <= its previous value; close <= open OR close <= previous close; and volume divided by its positive moving average >= a relative-volume threshold, using zero when the average is not positive. The RSI upper threshold is above its lower threshold.
Long: At least one active support block remains after the current bar's removal step.
Short: At least one active resistance block remains after the current bar's removal step.
Long: At least one active support block has coverage > 0 during the current bar's block-state update AND no active support block had coverage > 0 during the preceding bar's update.
Short: At least one active resistance block has coverage > 0 during the current bar's block-state update AND no active resistance block had coverage > 0 during the preceding bar's update.
Long: Support confirmation state is true AND support confirmation state was false on the preceding bar.
Short: Resistance confirmation state is true AND resistance confirmation state was false on the preceding bar.
