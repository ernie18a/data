Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low using a left lookback and right-side confirmation; retain the latest of each
Calculation: Market bias = 1 after close > latest confirmed swing high; -1 after close < latest confirmed swing low; otherwise retain prior bias (initially 0)
Calculation: Support OB = on a confirmed swing low, find the nearest earlier bearish candle within the source lookback; create a zone from that candle's high/low if the distance from its low to the pivot bar's high ≥ ATR at the source candle × displacement multiplier; normalize the zone around its midpoint to 0.75 × source ATR in height
Calculation: Resistance OB = on a confirmed swing high, find the nearest earlier bullish candle within the source lookback; create a zone from that candle's high/low if the distance from its high to the pivot bar's low ≥ ATR at the source candle × displacement multiplier; normalize the zone around its midpoint to 0.75 × source ATR in height
Calculation: OB score = 3 × weighted mean of Origin, Location, and Reaction, each out of 10; weights are configurable. Origin adds a displacement score (1 below 1× threshold, 2.5 at 1×, 3 at 1.25×, 3.5 at 1.5×, 4 at 1.75×) plus 2 each for close breaking beyond the source candle, pivot-bar body ≥ 1.3× its 20-bar average body, and pivot-bar volume ≥ 1.05× its 20-bar average volume. Location adds 3 for midpoint below (support) or above (resistance) the highest-high/lowest-low midpoint over the lookback when that filter is enabled; 3 for midpoint within 0.8× source ATR of the latest corresponding pivot when the pivot filter is enabled (or 1.5 if no such pivot); 2 when close is above (support) or below (resistance) the EMA, otherwise 0.5; and 2 when market bias agrees, 1 when neutral, otherwise 0. Reaction is 5 before touch; after touch it is 6/7/4/3 when maximum zone coverage is <25% / <75% / <100% / 100%, respectively; it is 10 for a resolved target win, 0 for a resolved loss or invalidation
Calculation: Zone coverage = clamp((zone top − low)/zone height × 100, 0, 100) for support; clamp((high − zone bottom)/zone height × 100, 0, 100) for resistance
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its longer-lookback simple moving average
Calculation: Confirmation hit count = number of true checks: close ≥ (support) or ≤ (resistance) fast EMA; fast EMA ≥ (support) or ≤ (resistance) slow EMA OR its prior-bar value; RSI >42 and nondecreasing (support) or <58 and nonincreasing (resistance); MACD line ≥ (support) or ≤ (resistance) signal line OR its prior-bar value; close ≥ open OR prior close (support) or ≤ open OR prior close (resistance); relative volume ≥ 0.90
Long: A support zone is touched now (low < zone top) AND no support zone was touched on the prior bar OR a support zone is active, unresolved, touched within the current or previous 3 bars, market bias = 1, its midpoint is below the recent range midpoint if the premium/discount filter is enabled, its midpoint is within 0.8× current ATR of the latest swing low if the pivot-confluence filter is enabled, close > zone bottom AND close ≥ open, and its score ≥ minimum score; emit on the first bar this condition becomes true OR a support zone is active, (touched within the confirmation window OR price is near it within the configured ATR distance) AND (price overlaps or is near it), its score ≥ minimum score minus tolerance, and its confirmation hit count ≥ the configured minimum; emit on the first qualifying bar and only after bar close when close-only confirmation is enabled
Short: A resistance zone is touched now (high > zone bottom) AND no resistance zone was touched on the prior bar OR a resistance zone is active, unresolved, touched within the current or previous 3 bars, market bias = -1, its midpoint is above the recent range midpoint if the premium/discount filter is enabled, its midpoint is within 0.8× current ATR of the latest swing high if the pivot-confluence filter is enabled, close < zone top AND close ≤ open, and its score ≥ minimum score; emit on the first bar this condition becomes true OR a resistance zone is active, (touched within the confirmation window OR price is near it within the configured ATR distance) AND (price overlaps or is near it), its score ≥ minimum score minus tolerance, and its confirmation hit count ≥ the configured minimum; emit on the first qualifying bar and only after bar close when close-only confirmation is enabled
