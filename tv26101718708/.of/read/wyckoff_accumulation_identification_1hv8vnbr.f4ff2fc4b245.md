Calculation: Accumulation state = On each confirmed chart bar, execute only the branch for the state at the start of the bar. Start in scan state. In scan state, when low is at or below the lowest low of the preceding lookback window AND volume is at least its current simple moving average multiplied by a spike factor AND the close a bounded number of bars ago, with a minimum history requirement, is at least low multiplied by a prior-decline factor, record the bar as the selling climax, its low and volume; also record as preliminary support the highest-volume bar in a bounded earlier range whose low exceeds the climax low by a margin, if one exists; then enter rally-scan state. In rally-scan state, replace the climax with the current bar and clear the rally candidate if low falls below the climax low by a margin AND volume is at least a reduced multiple of its current moving average; otherwise, after a minimum delay from the climax, update the candidate to the highest high observed since that delay. When the candidate's percentage rise from the climax low and the percentage decline from the candidate high to the current close both meet their respective thresholds AND a longer minimum time since the climax has elapsed, record the candidate bar and high as the automatic rally and enter range state. Reset if the rally-scan time limit is exceeded, even if another transition occurred on that bar. In range state, reset if the time since the climax exceeds the lookback; otherwise record or replace a secondary test when low is within an asymmetric zone around the climax low AND volume is below a fraction of climax volume AND close is at or above a level slightly below the climax low AND the minimum gap since the preceding test has elapsed. Then enter spring state if low is below the climax low AND close is at or above a level slightly below the climax low AND close is at or below a level slightly above the rally high AND (close - low) / max(high - low, a small positive floor) exceeds a threshold. Otherwise enter strength state if close exceeds the rally high AND volume exceeds a reduced multiple of its current moving average AND close exceeds open. Otherwise reset if close falls sufficiently below the climax low. In spring state, reset if the time since the climax exceeds the lookback; otherwise enter strength state on the same strength condition. Record the strength bar when entering strength state. In strength state, after a minimum delay from the strength bar, record last support and enter post-support state when low lies within an asymmetric zone around the rally high AND volume is below its current moving average AND low exceeds the climax low AND close is at least open. In strength or post-support state, enter markup state when close exceeds the rally high by a margin; this transition takes priority over the state after recording last support. In markup state, track the highest high since entering that state and reset when close falls below that peak by a drawdown threshold. A reset clears all recorded events, levels and candidates and returns to scan state.
