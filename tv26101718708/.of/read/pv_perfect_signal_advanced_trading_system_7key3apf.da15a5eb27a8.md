Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume; strong-volume threshold = volume average × spike multiplier × 1.3; extreme-volume threshold = volume average × spike multiplier × 2; counter-trend threshold = volume average × a separate multiplier.
Calculation: Rising volume = current volume is greater than each of the preceding volume-trend-lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Upward divergence = close > close three bars ago AND the average volume of the current and two preceding bars < volume average × 0.8. Downward divergence uses close < close three bars ago and the same volume test.
Calculation: Uptrend = short EMA > medium EMA AND close > long EMA AND SuperTrend is bullish. Downtrend = short EMA < medium EMA AND close < long EMA AND SuperTrend is bearish. All EMAs use close.
Calculation: Sideways = Bollinger Band width as a percentage of its middle band < width threshold OR (ADX filter enabled AND ADX <= threshold). Bollinger Bands use a simple moving average of close and a fixed standard-deviation multiplier; ADX uses equal directional-movement and smoothing periods.
Calculation: Supply and demand zones = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. Store each zone at confirmation time, retaining the most recent fixed maximum per side; only zones within the persistence duration are candidates. On each bar iterate candidates in insertion order, updating the nearest whenever its absolute distance from close is strictly smaller than the best distance so far. For every candidate that becomes the nearest during that iteration, increment its stored test count after reading the count if distance < close × tolerance AND, for supply, close < zone × 1.02, or, for demand, close > zone × 0.98. The selected nearest zone and its count before that bar's increment persist if no valid candidate is found. At-zone means close lies within zone × (1 ± tolerance) AND the selected pre-increment count >= strength threshold. Near supply means zone × (1 − 1.5 × tolerance) <= close <= zone × (1 + 0.5 × tolerance); near demand means zone × (1 − 0.5 × tolerance) <= close <= zone × (1 + 1.5 × tolerance).
Calculation: Institutional buy = (at demand OR near demand) AND abs(close − open) > ATR × candle-size multiplier AND close > open AND volume > strong-volume threshold. Institutional sell substitutes supply and close < open.
Calculation: Recovery = volume > strong-volume threshold AND rising volume AND close > previous close AND no upward divergence AND close is below the highest high of the pattern lookback by more than the pattern threshold percentage AND above the lowest low by more than 0.6 × that percentage AND RSI is above its extreme-oversold threshold and below a separate recovery threshold; OR RSI was below its extreme-oversold threshold on any of the preceding three bars AND RSI > previous RSI > RSI two bars ago AND volume > strong-volume threshold AND close > previous close AND no upward divergence.
Calculation: Decline = volume > strong-volume threshold AND rising volume AND close < previous close AND no downward divergence AND close is above the lowest low of the pattern lookback by more than the pattern threshold percentage AND below the highest high by more than 0.6 × that percentage AND RSI is below its extreme-overbought threshold and above a separate decline threshold; OR RSI was above its extreme-overbought threshold on any of the preceding three bars AND RSI < previous RSI < RSI two bars ago AND volume > strong-volume threshold AND close < previous close AND no downward divergence.
Calculation: Bull score = one point each for uptrend; close > short EMA AND close > session VWAP of close; short EMA crossing above medium EMA; RSI > midpoint AND RSI < overbought threshold; volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND no upward divergence; low > SuperTrend. Add two points each for institutional buy AND not sideways, and recovery AND not sideways. Bear score mirrors these conditions: downtrend; close below short EMA and VWAP; short EMA crossing below medium EMA; RSI below midpoint and above oversold threshold; spike volume and no downward divergence; high < SuperTrend; institutional sell and not sideways; decline and not sideways.
Calculation: Regular buy condition = bull score >= minimum score AND bear score < opposing-score ceiling AND bars since last signal >= minimum spacing AND trading time allowed AND short EMA > its previous value AND close > previous close AND not sideways AND no upward divergence AND (not downtrend OR volume > counter-trend threshold AND rising volume). Regular sell condition mirrors scores, momentum, divergence and trend. Trading time is allowed when the opening-time filter is disabled OR the bar's local clock time minus the configured market-open time is at least the configured waiting duration.
Calculation: Signal state = initialize last signal bar to the first bar and last direction to none; at each new calendar day reset both to the current bar and none. Evaluate both directions using the state before either update. After evaluation, update state for a buy and then for a sell, so a simultaneous sell takes precedence.
Calculation: Buy signal = last direction is not buy AND ((regular buy condition is true AND was false on the preceding bar) OR (early entry enabled AND (institutional buy OR recovery) AND not sideways AND trading time allowed AND bars since last signal >= early spacing AND short EMA > previous short EMA AND close > previous close AND close > open AND volume > strong-volume threshold AND no upward divergence AND (downtrend is false OR bull score < minimum score OR volume > extreme-volume threshold))). Sell signal mirrors this using institutional sell or decline, falling EMA and close, close < open, downward divergence, uptrend and bear score.
Long: Buy signal = true OR regular buy condition = true OR (institutional buy OR recovery) AND sideways = false OR volume > extreme-volume threshold AND close > open.
Short: Sell signal = true OR (regular sell condition = true AND regular buy condition = false) OR (institutional sell OR decline) AND sideways = false OR volume > extreme-volume threshold AND close < open.
Exit: long, sell signal = true OR upward divergence = true OR downward divergence = true.
Exit: short, buy signal = true OR upward divergence = true OR downward divergence = true.
