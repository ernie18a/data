Calculation: Breakout levels = enabled previous-day and previous-week highs and lows, plus the most recent configured number of confirmed 1-hour swing highs and lows; merge levels within the configured distance and ignore levels beyond the configured maximum distance. Daily and weekly levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Swing levels use confirmed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Market-structure bias = long when the two most recent swing highs and lows form both a higher high and a higher low; short when they form neither; otherwise neutral. Manual direction setting may instead allow longs only, shorts only, or both.
Calculation: Confirmation-instrument bias = market-structure bias calculated from its confirmed higher-timeframe swing highs and lows.
Calculation: Confirmation-instrument breakout direction = direction of the latest crossing of a confirmation level by the confirmation instrument's chart-timeframe close, beyond that level by the configured percentage; retain the latest direction until another crossing.
Calculation: Stop price = entry price minus position side times risk, where long risk is entry price minus breakout-bar low and short risk is breakout-bar high minus entry price; cap risk at the configured maximum or skip the entry if the cap mode is set to skip, then apply the configured minimum risk.
Calculation: Target price = entry price plus position side times risk times the configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: NQ close > marked level + configured breakout distance AND prior NQ close <= that threshold (or, in wick mode, NQ high > threshold AND prior NQ high <= threshold) AND daily bias permits long AND enabled confirmation-bias filter permits long AND enabled confirmation-breakout filter has latest direction long AND within configured trading window AND marked level is unused AND below configured daily trade limit AND no open position AND risk is accepted
Short: NQ close < marked level - configured breakout distance AND prior NQ close >= that threshold (or, in wick mode, NQ low < threshold AND prior NQ low >= threshold) AND daily bias permits short AND enabled confirmation-bias filter permits short AND enabled confirmation-breakout filter has latest direction short AND within configured trading window AND marked level is unused AND below configured daily trade limit AND no open position AND risk is accepted
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price
