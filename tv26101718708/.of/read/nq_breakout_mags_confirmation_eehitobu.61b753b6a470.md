Calculation: Structure bias = At the daily markup bar, use confirmed pivot highs and lows from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Each pivot uses the same number of bars on each side. If at least two highs and two lows exist, bias is long when the newest high and newest low are both greater than their respective predecessors; bias is short when both are less than or equal to their predecessors. Otherwise bias is neutral. The chart's bias may instead be fixed to long, short, or neutral.
Calculation: Levels = At the first chart bar at or after the configured daily markup time, rebuild the ordered list from enabled previous completed longer-higher-timeframe high and low, previous completed shorter-higher-timeframe high and low, then the configured number of newest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot highs and pivot lows. Retain candidates within a configured absolute distance of the markup bar's close; discard each candidate within a configured merge distance of an already retained level. Reset all retained levels to unused.
Calculation: Entry and risk = For a close breakout, entry is the breakout bar's close. For a wick breakout, long entry is the greater of open and the upper threshold, and short entry is the lesser of open and the lower threshold. Initial long risk is entry minus breakout-bar low; initial short risk is breakout-bar high minus entry. In skip mode, reject risk above the configured maximum; otherwise cap risk at that maximum. Then raise risk to at least the configured minimum. Stop is entry minus risk for a long and entry plus risk for a short; target is entry plus risk times the configured reward-to-risk ratio for a long and entry minus that amount for a short.
Long: At the daily markup bar, chart structure bias = long.
Short: At the daily markup bar, chart structure bias = short.
Long: The daily levels are marked AND the chart bar is in the configured trading window AND no position is open AND the day's trade count is below its limit AND chart bias is long or neutral AND an unused level has either close > level + breakout distance AND previous close <= level + breakout distance, or, in wick mode, high > level + breakout distance AND previous high <= level + breakout distance AND the entry risk is accepted.
Short: The daily levels are marked AND the chart bar is in the configured trading window AND no position is open AND the day's trade count is below its limit AND chart bias is short or neutral AND an unused level has either close < level - breakout distance AND previous close >= level - breakout distance, or, in wick mode, low < level - breakout distance AND previous low >= level - breakout distance AND the entry risk is accepted.
Calculation: Level processing = Evaluate levels in their retained order. A raw breakout consumes its level even when the direction filter rejects it or the risk is skipped. Continue after a filter rejection; stop processing levels after an accepted direction, whether the risk is skipped or a trade is entered. Reset the trade count on each new day. Process exits before entries, permitting re-entry on an exit bar; do not test a newly entered position for exit until the next bar.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target. If both occur, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target. If both occur, exit at the stop.
