Calculation: Structure = Confirm swing highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. Keep the latest and previous confirmed high and low. Start direction at 0. On each bar, first update the confirmed swings, then calculate up break = (close > latest swing high AND prior direction <= 0) OR (close > previous swing high AND prior direction < 0), and down break = (close < latest swing low AND prior direction >= 0) OR (close < previous swing low AND prior direction > 0). Set direction to 1 on an up break, then to -1 on a down break; otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Gap overlap = Store a bullish zone from high two bars ago to current low when current low > high two bars ago, previous close > previous open, and zone size >= an ATR multiple. Store a bearish zone from current high to low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago, previous close < previous open, and zone size >= the same ATR multiple. Keep only the most recently created fixed number of zones, regardless of age. A bar overlaps a zone when low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom.
Calculation: Momentum direction = 1 when EMA-smoothed RSI > its midpoint AND its change over a lookback > 0 AND it is below the overbought threshold; -1 when smoothed RSI < its midpoint AND its change over the same lookback < 0 AND it is above the oversold threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume direction = Let average volume be SMA(volume), relative volume = volume / average volume, estimated buy volume = volume if close > open, otherwise volume * (close - low) / (high - low + small positive constant), and estimated sell volume = volume if close < open, otherwise volume * (high - close) / (high - low + the same constant). Delta = estimated buy volume - estimated sell volume. Direction is 1 when relative volume >= its threshold AND delta > 0 AND the rolling sum of delta > 0; -1 when relative volume >= its threshold AND delta < 0 AND the rolling sum of delta < 0; otherwise 0. Volume anomaly means absolute value of (volume - average volume) / standard deviation of volume > an anomaly threshold; the standard deviation can use a different lookback from average volume.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = Using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, trend is 1 if close > EMA(close), -1 if close < EMA(close), otherwise 0. Alignment is 1 if trend = 1 AND RSI > its midpoint, -1 if trend = -1 AND RSI < its midpoint, otherwise 0. The RSI length is the same as for chart-timeframe momentum.
Calculation: Smart money = Institutional delta is delta * relative volume. Accumulation means close and volume both increased over the same lookback AND institutional delta > 0. Distribution means close decreased AND volume increased over that lookback AND institutional delta < 0. Score is absolute institutional delta / average volume for accumulation, its negative for distribution, and 0 otherwise; multiply by an anomaly bonus when volume anomaly is present, scale and cap symmetrically.
Calculation: Quantum = Coherence is the absolute value of (structure direction + momentum direction + volume direction + higher-timeframe alignment) divided by the number of these directions. Entanglement is a positive half-unit if higher-timeframe trend = structure direction and a negative half-unit otherwise, plus a positive half-unit if higher-timeframe alignment = momentum direction and a negative half-unit otherwise. Superposition is the average of coherence and entanglement.
Calculation: Confluence score = Sum these points: structure gives 1 for any break if the break requirement is enabled, otherwise 1 for nonzero structure direction, plus 1 for overlap with a gap matching structure direction if the gap requirement is enabled; momentum gives 1 for nonzero momentum direction when enabled, otherwise 1; volume gives 1 for nonzero volume direction plus 1 for volume anomaly when enabled, otherwise 1; higher-timeframe alignment gives 1 for nonzero alignment when enabled, otherwise 1; session gives 1 when the bar opens within the configured session and timezone when enabled, otherwise 1; liquidity gives 1 when abs(delta) / volume exceeds its medium threshold plus 1 when abs(delta) / average volume exceeds its sweep threshold when enabled, otherwise 1; quantum gives 1 when superposition > its collapse threshold plus 1 when coherence > its high-coherence threshold when enabled, otherwise 1; smart money gives 1 when its score > a positive threshold plus 1 for accumulation OR distribution when enabled, otherwise 1; harmonic gives 1 when sin(2 * pi * (zero-based bar index modulo harmonic period) / harmonic period) * structure direction > its threshold when enabled, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Initial stop and target = At the signal bar, long stop = min(close - ATR * stop multiplier, lowest low over a lookback longer than the pivot strength - ATR * buffer multiplier); short stop = max(close + ATR * stop multiplier, highest high over the same lookback + ATR * buffer multiplier). Long target = signal close + abs(signal close - initial stop) * reward-to-risk multiplier; short target = signal close - abs(signal close - initial stop) * reward-to-risk multiplier. Initialize the trailing stop to the initial stop. While a position is open, after each bar update the long trailing stop to max(previous trailing stop, high - ATR * trail multiplier), or the short trailing stop to min(previous trailing stop, low + ATR * trail multiplier). The updated stop applies to subsequent price movement; if trailing is disabled, use the initial stop.
