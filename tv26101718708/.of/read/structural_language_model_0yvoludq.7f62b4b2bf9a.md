Calculation: Bar token = classify each bar using range = high − low, body = close − open, body fraction = abs(body) / range when range > 0, otherwise 0, and relative range = range / Wilder moving average of range when that average > 0, otherwise 1. Select either adaptive thresholds on the percentile rank of relative range over a lookback or fixed thresholds on relative range. An impulse has relative range or its rank at or above the upper threshold and body fraction at or above the minimum. Assign up impulse if it is an impulse and body >= 0, down impulse if it is an impulse and body < 0, compression if it is not an impulse and either relative range or its rank is at or below the lower threshold or body fraction is below the minimum, ordinary up if none of those apply and body >= 0, otherwise ordinary down. Optionally omit the first intraday bar of each calendar day; substitute compression for omitted or unavailable tokens when forming contexts.
Calculation: Next-token probabilities = maintain counts of transitions from a configurable context of recent bar tokens to the next token, initially zero. On each completed bar, starting when the zero-based bar index exceeds the context length plus two, add its transition if its token is not omitted. For the context ending at the current bar, divide each token's count plus a smoothing parameter by the sum of all five counts plus five times that parameter; if the denominator is zero, use zero for every probability.
Calculation: Directional bias = probability of ordinary up plus probability of up impulse minus probability of ordinary down minus probability of down impulse.
Long: Directional bias >= positive conviction threshold.
Short: Directional bias <= negative conviction threshold of the same magnitude.
Long: Probability of up impulse >= every other next-token probability OR probability of ordinary up > probability of up impulse AND probability of ordinary up >= each remaining next-token probability.
Short: Probability of ordinary down > each of the up impulse, ordinary up, and compression probabilities AND probability of ordinary down >= probability of down impulse OR probability of down impulse > every other next-token probability.
