Calculation: Volume profile = distribute each chart bar’s volume across price bins in proportion to the bar’s range overlapping each bin; POC is the highest-volume bin and VAH/VAL bound the contiguous bins around POC containing the configured share of total volume
Calculation: Key levels = previous regular-session high/low, overnight-session high/low, and high/low of the first 30 minutes of the regular session
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Average volume and average candle body = simple moving averages over configurable lookbacks
Calculation: Bullish absorption = confirmed green or doji bar with body/range below a threshold, volume above both a multiple of average volume and the highest-volume threshold, and its same-direction cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Bearish absorption = confirmed green bar with body/range below a threshold, volume above both a multiple of average volume and the highest-volume threshold, and its same-direction cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Trapped buyers = prior green bar body above a multiple of average body and volume above a multiple of average volume, followed by a confirmed close below the prior open with current body above a threshold and its cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Trapped sellers = prior red bar body above a multiple of average body and volume above a multiple of average volume, followed by a confirmed close above the prior open with current body above a threshold and its cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Look-above-and-fail = confirmed bar near VAH, previous-session high, overnight high, or opening-range high; high exceeds the prior swing high by a minimum wick threshold; close is below that swing high and below open; bearish EMA trend limit holds; and its cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Look-below-and-fail = confirmed bar near VAL, previous-session low, overnight low, or opening-range low; low falls below the prior swing low by a minimum wick threshold; close is above that swing low and above open; bullish EMA trend limit holds; and its cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Bullish volume divergence = a confirmed pivot low below the previous pivot low with pivot volume below a fraction of the previous pivot volume
Calculation: Bearish volume divergence = a confirmed pivot high above the previous pivot high with pivot volume below a fraction of the previous pivot volume
Calculation: Value-area re-entry levels = VAH and VAL captured at regular-session open and held fixed for that session
Calculation: Bullish VA re-entry = after a qualifying-duration close excursion sufficiently far below captured VAL, a confirmed green bar closes inside the captured value area and its cooldown has elapsed
Calculation: Bearish VA re-entry = after a qualifying-duration close excursion sufficiently far above captured VAH, a confirmed red bar closes inside the captured value area and its cooldown has elapsed
Calculation: Trend = bullish when the configured EMA is rising and close is above it, or close is above both fast and configured EMAs; bearish when the configured EMA is falling and close is below it, or close is below both fast and configured EMAs
Calculation: Value migration = POC sampled at intervals; higher when the latest two consecutive sample-to-sample changes are both upward, lower when both are downward, otherwise stable
Calculation: Long-allowed = value migration is stable or higher, or is lower while approaching extreme support; and trend filtering is disabled, bullish trend holds, or a long key-level condition holds
Calculation: Short-allowed = value migration is stable or lower, or is higher while approaching extreme resistance; and trend filtering is disabled, bearish trend holds, or a short key-level condition holds
Calculation: Long key level = price touches VAL, or touches a market level within an ATR-based zone while approaching from the applicable side and not beyond its breakout invalidation distance
Calculation: Short key level = price touches VAH, or touches a market level within an ATR-based zone while approaching from the applicable side and not beyond its breakout invalidation distance
Calculation: Long order flow = bullish absorption OR trapped sellers OR look-below-and-fail OR bullish VA re-entry
Calculation: Short order flow = bearish absorption OR trapped buyers OR look-above-and-fail OR bearish VA re-entry
Calculation: Long entry target = nearest valid resistance above entry among VAH, POC, previous-session high, overnight high, and opening-range high, within configured risk-distance bounds; otherwise entry plus a configured risk multiple
Calculation: Short entry target = nearest valid support below entry among VAL, POC, previous-session low, overnight low, and opening-range low, within configured risk-distance bounds; otherwise entry minus a configured risk multiple
Calculation: Long breakout retest = confirmed close crosses above a key level by more than an ATR-based distance; price holds above it for a minimum number of bars, then retests its zone within a time window with acceptable volume and body size, a shallow retracement, and a bullish close above the level with above-average volume or long order flow
Calculation: Short breakout retest = confirmed close crosses below a key level by more than an ATR-based distance; price holds below it for a minimum number of bars, then retests its zone within a time window with acceptable volume and body size, a shallow retracement, and a bearish close below the level with above-average volume or short order flow
Calculation: Long squeeze = following a short entry, within a time window and after a minimum delay, close exceeds that entry’s level by an ATR-based distance on a green bar with elevated volume, while the last entry side remains short
Calculation: Short squeeze = following a long entry, within a time window and after a minimum delay, close falls below that entry’s level by an ATR-based distance on a red bar with elevated volume, while the last entry side remains long
Calculation: Entry gate = confirmed bar, required entry cooldown elapsed, direction allowed, and no same-direction entry inside the cooldown; entries also require minimum reward-to-risk
Calculation: Aggressive long = entry gate and (long key level AND long order flow OR long order flow with close above VAL but within an ATR-based zone above it), green close, and volume above average
Calculation: Aggressive short = entry gate and (short key level AND short order flow OR short order flow with close below VAH but within an ATR-based zone below it), red close, and volume above average
Calculation: Pending confirmation long = after the pending cooldown, either a long key-level wick below the level followed by a green close above it, or bullish order flow near VAL; remains valid only within a configured window and before price breaks sufficiently below its level
Calculation: Pending confirmation short = after the pending cooldown, either a short key-level wick above the level followed by a red close below it, or bearish order flow near VAH; remains valid only within a configured window and before price breaks sufficiently above its level
Calculation: Confirmation long = pending confirmation long; later confirmed green close above its level and above the tracked dip low by an ATR-based amount; extra reversal-warning confirmation holds; volume exceeds a multiple of average volume or long order flow occurs; entry gate holds
Calculation: Confirmation short = pending confirmation short; later confirmed red close below its level and below the tracked bounce high by an ATR-based amount; extra reversal-warning confirmation holds; volume exceeds a multiple of average volume or short order flow occurs; entry gate holds
Calculation: Stop and structural target = long stop below the lower of entry-bar low and entry level by an ATR buffer, or short stop above the highest relevant high and entry level by an ATR buffer; structural targets are the nearest qualifying opposite-side profile or market levels
Calculation: Long exhaustion warning = near a key level, close above its value three bars earlier, at least three of shrinking bodies, declining volume, a bearish reversal candle, upper-wick rejection, bearish absorption, look-above-and-fail, or trapped buyers hold, and warning cooldown elapsed
Calculation: Short exhaustion warning = near a key level, close below its value three bars earlier, at least three of shrinking bodies, declining volume, a bullish reversal candle, lower-wick rejection, bullish absorption, look-below-and-fail, or trapped sellers hold, and warning cooldown elapsed
Long: bullish absorption OR trapped sellers OR look-below-and-fail OR bullish volume divergence OR bullish VA re-entry OR long exhaustion warning OR aggressive long OR confirmation long OR long breakout retest OR long squeeze
Short: bearish absorption OR trapped buyers OR look-above-and-fail OR bearish volume divergence OR bearish VA re-entry OR short exhaustion warning OR aggressive short OR confirmation short OR short breakout retest OR short squeeze
Exit: Long, close >= entry price + 1.5 × initial risk (exit half and move stop to entry price) OR price reaches the structural target OR price <= stop price
Exit: Short, close <= entry price - 1.5 × initial risk (exit half and move stop to entry price) OR price reaches the structural target OR price >= stop price
