Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; body ratio = body / range when range > 0, otherwise 1; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Volume profile = divide the rolling high-to-low range into equal price bins; allocate each bar's volume to bins in proportion to the overlap of the bar's high-low range with each bin, excluding zero-range bars. POC is the center of the first maximum-volume bin. Starting at the POC, expand the value area one adjacent bin at a time toward the larger adjacent volume, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the selected fraction of profile volume is included or both adjacent volumes are zero. VAH and VAL are the upper and lower edges of the included bins.
Calculation: Key levels = the prior regular-session high and low, the most recently completed overnight-session high and low, and the high and low accumulated from the regular-session open through the opening-range completion bar. Opening-range levels are usable only after completion. The value-area edges are recalculated on every chart bar.
Calculation: Absorption = on a completed bar, body ratio <= a small-body threshold AND volume > its moving average times a spike multiplier AND volume >= its rolling maximum AND bars since the last absorption of the same direction > its cooldown. Bullish absorption has close <= open; bearish absorption has close > open.
Calculation: Trap = the preceding bar has body > its body moving average times a large-body multiplier AND volume > its volume moving average times a high-volume multiplier; the current body > its body moving average times a reversal-body multiplier AND bars since the last trap of the same direction > its cooldown. A bullish trap has a bearish preceding bar and current close > preceding open; a bearish trap has a bullish preceding bar and current close < preceding open.
Calculation: Failed breakout = a completed bar exceeds the preceding rolling swing extreme by more than a fraction of current close, closes back through that extreme in the reversal direction, and occurs after a direction-specific cooldown. For a bullish failed breakout, low is within an ATR-scaled distance of VAL or a support-side key level, low < the preceding rolling swing low, close > that swing low, close > open, and close > EMA - an ATR buffer. For a bearish failed breakout, high is within an ATR-scaled distance of VAH or a resistance-side key level, high > the preceding rolling swing high, close < that swing high, close < open, and close < EMA + an ATR buffer. The swing lookback is longer than the failed-breakout cooldown.
Calculation: Value-area snapshot = current rolling VAH and VAL captured on the first bar of each regular session, including that bar in the profile. Reset the consecutive outside-bar counts and excursion extremes at that time. Count consecutive closes below snapshot VAL and above snapshot VAH separately; reset both counts on an inside close and reset the opposite count on an outside close. Track the lowest close below VAL and highest close above VAH since the session reset or the respective re-entry signal, retaining each extreme through intervening inside or opposite-side bars.
Calculation: Value-area re-entry = on a completed bar, the preceding consecutive outside count >= its minimum AND the tracked excursion beyond the corresponding snapshot edge, divided by that edge, >= its minimum fraction AND close is within both snapshot edges AND bars since the last re-entry of that direction > its cooldown. Bullish re-entry requires the preceding outside closes below VAL and close > open; bearish re-entry requires them above VAH and close < open. Reset the corresponding excursion extreme after a signal.
Calculation: Directional order flow = bullish absorption OR bullish trap OR bullish failed breakout OR bullish value-area re-entry for the long direction; bearish absorption OR bearish trap OR bearish failed breakout OR bearish value-area re-entry for the short direction.
Calculation: Value migration = sample the current POC at a fixed bar interval. Higher value requires each of the last three samples to be greater than its predecessor; lower value requires each to be less than its predecessor. Otherwise value is stable. Retain the last classification between samples.
Calculation: Directional level touch = for a long, low lies between the level minus a larger ATR-scaled tolerance and the level plus a smaller ATR-scaled tolerance; for a short, high lies between the level minus the smaller tolerance and the level plus the larger tolerance. VAL is eligible only for long touches and VAH only for short touches. Each key level is eligible in either direction only when a prior close at a fixed lag was on the entry side of the current level and the current close has not crossed beyond the opposite side by an ATR-scaled breakout distance. Select the first eligible level in this order: long: VAL, prior regular-session low, prior regular-session high, overnight low, overnight high, opening-range low, opening-range high; short: VAH, prior regular-session high, prior regular-session low, overnight high, overnight low, opening-range high, opening-range low.
Calculation: Directional allowance = long is allowed when value migration is not lower OR a long touch occurs at VAL, overnight low, or prior regular-session low with the lagged close above that level; the VAL exception additionally requires fewer than the permitted consecutive closes below current VAL. Short is allowed when value migration is not higher OR a short touch occurs at VAH, overnight high, or prior regular-session high with the lagged close below that level. If the optional EMA filter is enabled, also require a directional level touch OR, for long, close > slow EMA AND either slow EMA > its lagged value OR close > fast EMA; for short, close < slow EMA AND either slow EMA < its lagged value OR close < fast EMA. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = count these seven conditions: body < preceding body < body before that; volume < preceding volume < volume before that; reversal candle against the net close change over a fixed lookback; reversal-side wick > body times a multiplier AND > ATR times a multiplier; directional absorption; directional failed breakout; directional trap. A bullish score uses a bullish reversal candle, lower wick, and bullish signals; a bearish score uses a bearish reversal candle, upper wick, and bearish signals.
Calculation: Recent reversal warning = the last warning in either direction occurred within a fixed lookback. A warning occurs when close is within an ATR-scaled distance of VAH, VAL, or any key level AND the directional exhaustion score >= its threshold AND the net close change over the momentum lookback is down for a bullish warning or up for a bearish warning AND bars since the last warning of either direction > the shared warning cooldown.
Calculation: Entry risk and reward = use current close as entry. Long stop is the selected reference low minus an ATR buffer; short stop is the selected reference high plus an ATR buffer. Risk is entry minus stop for long or stop minus entry for short, floored at a positive minimum. Select the nearest eligible target in the entry direction among POC, the opposite value-area edge, and the corresponding prior-session, overnight, and completed opening-range extreme, provided its distance is between a lower and an upper multiple of risk. If none qualifies, use a fixed risk multiple as target. Reward-to-risk is target distance divided by risk, capped and rounded to the specified precision.
Calculation: Entry state = share one cooldown across aggressive, retest, confirmation, and squeeze candidates. A candidate updates the last-entry bar and direction even when its reward-to-risk is below the display threshold. Aggressive and retest candidates cancel the opposite pending setup; confirmation candidates cancel both pending setups. Process aggressive, retest, confirmation, then squeeze candidates in that order on each bar.
Calculation: Pending setup = after more than half the shared entry cooldown, create or replace a long setup when a long level touch has low < selected level AND close > selected level AND close > open AND long is allowed; symmetrically create or replace a short setup when high > selected level AND close < selected level AND close < open AND short is allowed. Alternatively, if no same-direction setup is pending, create one at current VAL for a bullish absorption, trap, or failed breakout with low below VAL plus an ATR buffer, or at current VAH for the bearish counterparts with high above VAH minus an ATR buffer, subject to directional allowance. Save setup bar, level, and wick; track the lowest low or highest high while pending for a limited tracking window. Expire a setup when its maximum age is exceeded or price breaches its saved level against the setup by an ATR-scaled distance.
Calculation: Breakout state = on a completed bar other than the first bar of a regular or overnight session, record a level and direction when close crosses beyond its current value plus or minus an ATR-scaled breakout distance, using the current level and current ATR for both the current and preceding close comparisons. Eligible levels are VAL, VAH, and all key levels. When several break simultaneously, select in this order: upward opening-range low, overnight low, prior-session low, VAL, opening-range high, overnight high, prior-session high, VAH; downward opening-range high, overnight high, prior-session high, VAH, prior-session low, overnight low, opening-range low, VAL. Save the level and breakout-bar volume and body. Reset the hold count on a new breakout; thereafter count consecutive closes on the breakout side of the saved level, resetting the count when that condition fails.
Long: Bullish absorption OR bullish trap OR bullish failed breakout OR bullish value-area re-entry.
Short: Bearish absorption OR bearish trap OR bearish failed breakout OR bearish value-area re-entry.
Long: A newly confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND a volume pivot low is confirmed on the same bar as each price pivot AND the new volume pivot is less than the preceding volume pivot times a weakening multiplier AND bars since the last divergence of either direction > the shared divergence cooldown. Each pivot uses equal left and right confirmation lookbacks; evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Short: A newly confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND a volume pivot high is confirmed on the same bar as each price pivot AND the new volume pivot is less than the preceding volume pivot times a weakening multiplier AND bars since the last divergence of either direction > the shared divergence cooldown. Each pivot uses equal left and right confirmation lookbacks; evaluate on the confirmation bar. Process the high-pivot divergence before the low-pivot divergence when both confirm together.
Long: Close is within an ATR-scaled distance of VAH, VAL, or any key level AND close is below its momentum-lookback close AND bullish exhaustion score >= its threshold.
Short: Close is within an ATR-scaled distance of VAH, VAL, or any key level AND close is above its momentum-lookback close AND bearish exhaustion score >= its threshold.
Long: Shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND bullish directional order flow AND close > open AND volume > its moving average AND long is allowed AND either a long level touch OR close > VAL AND close < VAL + an ATR buffer AND, if a reversal warning is recent, preceding close > preceding open AND aggressive-entry reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use the selected touched level when a touch exists, otherwise VAL; the stop reference is min(low, selected level).
Short: Shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND bearish directional order flow AND close < open AND volume > its moving average AND short is allowed AND either a short level touch OR close < VAH AND close > VAH - an ATR buffer AND, if a reversal warning is recent, preceding close < preceding open AND aggressive-entry reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use the selected touched level when a touch exists, otherwise VAH; the stop reference is the maximum of the selected level and the highs over the short stop lookback.
Long: An upward breakout is recorded AND its age is within the retest window AND its hold count >= the minimum AND low is within the asymmetric ATR-scaled retest zone around the saved level AND a low within a shorter preceding lookback was <= the saved level + a wider ATR buffer AND volume < breakout-bar volume times a multiplier AND body < breakout-bar body times a multiplier AND close > saved level - an ATR buffer AND close > saved level AND close > open AND either volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier OR bullish directional order flow AND long is allowed AND shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND the same level has not fired a retest within its level cooldown AND retest reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use min(low, saved level) as the stop reference and a larger ATR stop buffer than the aggressive-entry buffer. Clear the breakout level and record its level cooldown when the retest candidate fires, including when reward-to-risk is insufficient.
Short: A downward breakout is recorded AND its age is within the retest window AND its hold count >= the minimum AND high is within the asymmetric ATR-scaled retest zone around the saved level AND a high within a shorter preceding lookback was >= the saved level - a wider ATR buffer AND volume < breakout-bar volume times a multiplier AND body < breakout-bar body times a multiplier AND close < saved level + an ATR buffer AND close < saved level AND close < open AND either volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier OR bearish directional order flow AND short is allowed AND shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND the same level has not fired a retest within its level cooldown AND retest reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use the maximum of saved level and the highs over the short stop lookback as the stop reference and a larger ATR stop buffer than the aggressive-entry buffer. Clear the breakout level and record its level cooldown when the retest candidate fires, including when reward-to-risk is insufficient.
Long: A long setup is pending AND current bar is later than its setup bar AND close > saved level AND close > open AND close > tracked pending low + an ATR buffer AND either volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier OR bullish directional order flow AND long is allowed AND shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND, if a reversal warning is recent, preceding close > preceding open AND confirmation-entry reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use the setup-bar low as the stop reference.
Short: A short setup is pending AND current bar is later than its setup bar AND close < saved level AND close < open AND close < tracked pending high - an ATR buffer AND either volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier OR bearish directional order flow AND short is allowed AND shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND, if a reversal warning is recent, preceding close < preceding open AND confirmation-entry reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use max(tracked pending high, preceding high) as the stop reference.
Long: The last entry candidate was short AND its saved level exists AND its age is between the squeeze minimum and maximum AND more than half the shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND close > saved short level + an ATR buffer AND close > open AND volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier AND squeeze reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use min(low, saved short level) as the stop reference. Clear the saved short level when the squeeze candidate fires, including when reward-to-risk is insufficient.
Short: The last entry candidate was long AND its saved level exists AND its age is between the squeeze minimum and maximum AND more than half the shared entry cooldown has elapsed AND close < saved long level - an ATR buffer AND close < open AND volume > its average times a confirmation multiplier AND squeeze reward-to-risk >= its threshold. Use max(high, saved long level) as the stop reference. Clear the saved long level when the squeeze candidate fires, including when reward-to-risk is insufficient.
