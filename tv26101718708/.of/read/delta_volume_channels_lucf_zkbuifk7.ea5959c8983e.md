Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of signed volume of completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; sign is positive when intrabar close > open, negative when close < open, otherwise determined by close versus the previous intrabar close, retaining the last known sign if equal.
Calculation: Delta-weighted line = a selected moving average of source + sign(delta) × min(source × abs(delta / total intrabar volume) × relative-volume weight, cap × standard deviation of source); relative-volume weight is either 1 or the percentile rank of total intrabar volume over a selected lookback divided by 100. The reference line uses the same moving-average type, source and length.
Calculation: Divergence = delta != 0 AND sign(change in reference line) != sign(change in delta-weighted line).
Calculation: Divergence channel = on a divergence after a breach, start a new channel at the selected bar's high and low or maximum and minimum of open and close; until breached, expand its upper and lower levels on subsequent divergences to include the selected bar's corresponding levels. A selected upper breach price above the upper level gives a bullish state; a selected lower breach price below the lower level gives a bearish state. Breach prices are either low and high respectively, high and low respectively, or the same selected open, close, HL2, HLC3, HLCC4 or OHLC4. Before a breach, optionally assign a bullish or bearish bias when the number of upper-level expansions is respectively greater or less than the number of lower-level expansions. Calculation: Strong bullish delta channel = delta-weighted line > reference line AND close > reference line AND both lines > their previous values; strong bearish delta channel = delta-weighted line <= reference line AND close < reference line AND both lines < their previous values.
