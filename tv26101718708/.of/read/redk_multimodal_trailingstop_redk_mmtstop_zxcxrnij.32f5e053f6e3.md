Calculation: Stop candidates = select one method: ATR: anchor minus/plus ATR times multiplier, with ATR calculated on the chart timeframe or from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; Chandelier: highest high over the lookback minus ATR times multiplier for the lower candidate, and lowest low over the lookback plus ATR times multiplier for the upper candidate; Donchian: lowest low for the lower candidate and highest high for the upper candidate over the lookback; standard deviation: anchor minus/plus standard deviation of anchor times multiplier; fixed percentage: anchor minus/plus anchor times the percentage. Anchor is a selectable price source derived from OHLC.
Calculation: Ratcheted stops = on each bar, lower stop is the maximum of the lower candidate and previous lower stop if previous close is greater than previous lower stop, otherwise the lower candidate; upper stop is the minimum of the upper candidate and previous upper stop if previous close is less than previous upper stop, otherwise the upper candidate. Substitute zero for an unavailable previous ratcheted stop.
Calculation: Trend = initially up; on each bar set it to up if close is greater than the previous upper stop, otherwise set it to down if close is less than the previous lower stop, otherwise retain its previous value. Substitute the corresponding current stop if a previous stop is unavailable.
Calculation: Active stop = lower ratcheted stop when trend is up, otherwise upper ratcheted stop.
Calculation: Critical zone = absolute difference between close and active stop divided by chart-timeframe ATR is less than half the safe-zone ATR multiplier; if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive, the zone is not critical.
Long: Trend is up AND previous trend was down.
Short: Trend is down AND previous trend was up.
Exit: long, trend is down AND previous trend was up OR close crosses below active stop OR critical zone is entered.
Exit: short, trend is up AND previous trend was down OR close crosses above active stop OR critical zone is entered.
