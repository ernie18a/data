Calculation: TICK fast EMA = exponential moving average of 5-minute TICK close; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: TICK slow EMA = exponential moving average of 5-minute TICK close with a longer lookback than the fast EMA; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: TICK HMA = Hull moving average of 5-minute TICK close; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Smoothed TICK HMA = simple moving average of TICK HMA; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Daily ATR = RMA of daily true range; use the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming.
Calculation: RSI average = weighted moving average of chart-timeframe RSI.
Calculation: Fast RSI = chart-timeframe RSI with a shorter lookback than the RSI used in RSI average.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = smoothed stochastic of chart-timeframe RSI.
Calculation: Regular bullish TICK divergence = at a confirmed TICK pivot low, TICK is higher than at the preceding pivot low and chart low is lower than at that preceding pivot low; the preceding pivot is within the configured pivot-range limits.
Calculation: Hidden bullish TICK divergence = at a confirmed TICK pivot low, TICK is lower than at the preceding pivot low and chart low is higher than at that preceding pivot low; the preceding pivot is within the configured pivot-range limits.
Calculation: Regular bearish TICK divergence = at a confirmed TICK pivot high, TICK is lower than at the preceding pivot high and chart high is higher than at that preceding pivot high; the preceding pivot is within the configured pivot-range limits.
Calculation: Hidden bearish TICK divergence = at a confirmed TICK pivot high, TICK is higher than at the preceding pivot high and chart high is lower than at that preceding pivot high; the preceding pivot is within the configured pivot-range limits.
Long: TICK HMA crosses above its smoothed value OR (while flat, a bullish base setup occurred within the entry lookback and Stochastic RSI K is below its entry threshold and rising) OR (while flat, regular bullish TICK divergence AND TICK HMA > smoothed TICK HMA AND RSI > its weighted moving average AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA > 0 AND close > chart-timeframe EMA) OR (while flat, the last trade closed within the chase lookback AND close > close at that exit AND TICK HMA > smoothed TICK HMA AND TICK HMA is rising AND RSI average > 50 AND RSI < 50 plus its threshold AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA is rising AND close > chart-timeframe EMA AND Stochastic RSI K < 50 plus its chase threshold); bullish base setup = TICK HMA crosses above its smoothed value AND RSI average > 50 AND RSI < 50 plus its threshold AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA > 0 AND close > chart-timeframe EMA. Apply the trading-session/date window and confirmed-chart-bar filter to entries; apply the optional 10:00–10:30 entry exclusion when enabled.
Short: TICK HMA crosses below its smoothed value OR (while flat, a bearish base setup occurred within the entry lookback and Stochastic RSI K is above its entry threshold and falling) OR (while flat, regular bearish TICK divergence AND TICK HMA < smoothed TICK HMA AND RSI < its weighted moving average AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA < 0 AND close < chart-timeframe EMA) OR (while flat, the last trade closed within the chase lookback AND close < close at that exit AND TICK HMA < smoothed TICK HMA AND TICK HMA is falling AND RSI average < 50 AND RSI > 50 minus its threshold AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA is falling AND close < chart-timeframe EMA AND Stochastic RSI K > 50 minus its chase threshold); bearish base setup = TICK HMA crosses below its smoothed value AND RSI average < 50 AND RSI > 50 minus its threshold AND VIX close > its entry threshold AND TICK fast EMA < 0 AND close < chart-timeframe EMA. Apply the trading-session/date window and confirmed-chart-bar filter to entries; apply the optional 10:00–10:30 entry exclusion when enabled.

Exit: long, after the minimum holding period during the exit session, TICK HMA < smoothed TICK HMA AND RSI average is falling OR regular bearish TICK divergence OR 5-minute TICK high > 1000 OR low <= the long stop OR high >= entry price + daily ATR times the profit-target fraction when profit targets are enabled.
Exit: short, after the minimum holding period during the exit session, TICK HMA > smoothed TICK HMA AND RSI average is rising OR regular bullish TICK divergence OR 5-minute TICK low < -1000 OR high >= the short stop OR low <= entry price - daily ATR times the profit-target fraction when profit targets are enabled.
Exit: long, long stop = the lower of the lowest chart low over the stop lookback minus daily ATR times the stop fraction and the prior bar low minus that ATR amount, recalculated from the entry bar onward; exit when low <= long stop.
Exit: short, short stop = the higher of the highest chart high over the stop lookback plus daily ATR times the stop fraction and the prior bar high plus that ATR amount, recalculated from the entry bar onward; exit when high >= short stop.
