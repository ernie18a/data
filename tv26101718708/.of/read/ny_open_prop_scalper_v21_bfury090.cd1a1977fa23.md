Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price of (high+low+close)/3, reset each day
Calculation: StackUp(tf) = on bars of tf, EMA(close, short) > EMA(close, medium) AND EMA(close, medium) > EMA(close, long). StackDown(tf) is the mirror with all < .
Calculation: BiasUp = on a timeframe H1 higher than the chart (bar still forming), StackUp(H1) AND H1 close > H1 medium EMA. BiasDown is the mirror.
Calculation: MTFUp = on a timeframe H2 higher than the chart and lower than H1 (bar still forming), StackUp(H2) AND H2 close > H2 VWAP (VWAP computed on H2 bars); AND on chart bars StackUp AND close > VWAP. MTFDown is the mirror.
Calculation: TriggerUp = (short EMA crosses above VWAP: previous short EMA <= previous VWAP AND short EMA > VWAP) OR (StackUp AND close > VWAP AND low <= short EMA AND close > open). TriggerDown = (short EMA crosses below VWAP) OR (StackDown AND close < VWAP AND high >= short EMA AND close < open).
Calculation: BreakUp = high > highest high of the previous N bars (excluding current) OR close > latest confirmed pivot high (pivot = highest high among L bars left and R bars right, usable only after R bars) OR (low < lowest low of the previous M bars (excluding current), M shorter than N, AND close > open). BreakDown = low < lowest low of previous N bars OR close < latest confirmed pivot low OR (high > highest high of previous M bars AND close < open).
Calculation: ScoreUp = 2*[BiasUp] + 2*[StackUp on chart] + 2*[close > VWAP] + 2*[TriggerUp] + 1*[BreakUp] + 1*[MTFUp]. ScoreDown = same with BiasDown, StackDown on chart, close < VWAP, TriggerDown, BreakDown, MTFDown.
Long: bar time within the allowed time-of-day session window AND ScoreUp >= score threshold AND ATR(close-based Wilder, medium lookback) >= minimum ATR in price points AND NOT (current bar range (high-low) > multiplier * SMA of bar range over recent bars) AND 0 < (close - lowest low of last N bars including current) <= maximum stop distance in price points AND TriggerUp AND (no previous Long signal OR bars since previous Long signal > cooldown bars)
Short: bar time within the allowed time-of-day session window AND ScoreDown >= score threshold AND ATR >= minimum ATR in price points AND NOT (current bar range (high-low) > multiplier * SMA of bar range over recent bars) AND 0 < (highest high of last N bars including current - close) <= maximum stop distance in price points AND TriggerDown AND (no previous Short signal OR bars since previous Short signal > cooldown bars)
