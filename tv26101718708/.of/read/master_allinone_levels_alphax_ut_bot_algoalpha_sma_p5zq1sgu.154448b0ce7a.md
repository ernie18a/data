Calculation: Fusion baseline = EMA of the average of the long-lookback EMA, SMA, WMA, ALMA, and VWAP; slow and fast fusion lines are EMAs of this baseline, with the slow smoothing longer than the fast smoothing.
Calculation: Bullish fusion state = close above the slow fusion line and the slow fusion line rising; bearish fusion state = close below it and the slow fusion line falling; neutral = neither.
Calculation: Williams %R trend slopes = (current Williams %R − its value one trend-lookback ago) / trend-lookback; use shorter, medium, and longer lookbacks for fast, slow, and turtle Williams %R, respectively.
Calculation: Long AlphaX setup = any of: (a) bullish divergence between two successive confirmed price pivot lows and fast Williams %R pivot values, with the newer price low lower and its Williams %R higher, the newer pivot within three bars, and pivots five to fifty bars apart, plus positive fast-Williams-%R velocity above a weak-velocity threshold; (b) after fast Williams %R was below an extreme oversold level, its velocity exceeds that threshold and it crosses up through the oversold level, while slow or turtle Williams %R is above a trend floor; (c) the same crossing and velocity conditions after a less extreme oversold level, with the corresponding less restrictive slow/turtle floor; (d) that crossing during a strong uptrend, with slow or turtle Williams %R slope positive; (e) that crossing during a moderate uptrend or with slow Williams %R above its pullback floor, and velocity above the threshold; or (f) fast, slow, and turtle Williams %R are all oversold, then fast Williams %R crosses up with velocity above the threshold.
Calculation: Short AlphaX setup = the mirrored bearish conditions: successive confirmed pivot highs with the newer price high higher and Williams %R lower; extreme or less extreme overbought fast Williams %R followed by a cross down through the overbought level with negative velocity and the corresponding slow/turtle ceiling; a cross down during a strong downtrend with slow or turtle Williams %R slope negative; a cross down during a moderate downtrend or with slow Williams %R below its pullback ceiling; or all three Williams %R measures overbought followed by a cross down. The divergence trigger also requires the newer pivot within three bars and pivots five to fifty bars apart.
Calculation: Valid AlphaX long/short setup = corresponding setup above, its confidence score at least the minimum confidence, and its trend gate passes. Confidence adds tiered points for turtle and fast Williams %R levels, the highest-priority matching setup (triple, continuation, divergence, extreme snap, exhaustion, then pullback), fast-Williams-%R velocity quality, UTC session quality, EMA trend alignment, and stacked setup count; subtracts penalties for extreme velocity, adverse RSI, Asian or NY-close session, and low volume; clamp to 0–100. Long/short scores use mirrored Williams %R, EMA, RSI, and setup conditions. The trend gate blocks a long in a strong downtrend or a short in a strong uptrend unless the corresponding fast-Williams-%R recovery condition passes; strong-trend thresholds are stricter in strict mode.
Calculation: Bullish fusion signal = transition from bearish or neutral fusion state to bullish state, subject to the same-direction trend-label cooldown; bearish fusion signal = the mirrored transition and cooldown.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = recursively trail the source by the ATR multiplied by a sensitivity factor: while source and prior source are above the prior stop, use the greater of prior stop and source minus the loss distance; while both are below, use the lesser of prior stop and source plus the loss distance; otherwise use source minus loss distance when source is above the prior stop, or source plus loss distance. Source is close, or same-timeframe Heikin Ashi close if selected.
Calculation: Zero-lag basis = EMA of close plus the difference between close and close lagged by floor((lookback − 1) / 2); volatility band distance = the highest ATR over three times the lookback, multiplied by a band factor. Trend becomes bullish on a close crossover above basis plus band distance and bearish on a close crossunder below basis minus band distance; otherwise it retains its prior state.
