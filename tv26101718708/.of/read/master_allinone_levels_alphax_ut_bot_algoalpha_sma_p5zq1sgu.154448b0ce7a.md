Calculation: Fusion basis = arithmetic mean of the EMA, SMA, WMA, and ALMA of close using a common lookback, and session VWAP of typical price; smooth this mean with a shorter EMA for fast fusion and a longer EMA for slow fusion.
Calculation: Fusion bull state = close > slow fusion AND slow fusion > its previous value; fusion bear state = close < slow fusion AND slow fusion < its previous value.
Calculation: Fusion trend event = a transition from a non-bull state to the bull state or from a non-bear state to the bear state, permitted if the previous accepted event was in the opposite direction or at least a configurable cooldown has elapsed since the previous accepted event. Initialize the last event direction to neutral and its bar index to zero. The current fusion trend is the direction of the most recent accepted event, initially neutral. Reset the entry-seen flag on each accepted event and set it when a corresponding fusion-trend entry occurs; an exit does not reset it.
Calculation: Pro inputs = Williams %R on short, medium, and long lookbacks; short %R velocity is its current value minus its previous value; medium and long %R slopes are their changes over a common trend lookback divided by that lookback. Assign each UTC session a configurable quality and velocity multiplier. Weak, ideal-minimum, ideal-maximum, strong, and extreme velocity thresholds are configurable base thresholds multiplied by the session velocity multiplier. Velocity quality is ideal if absolute velocity is between ideal-minimum and ideal-maximum inclusive, strong if above ideal-maximum and at most strong, extreme if above strong, and weak otherwise.
Calculation: Pro long triggers = count the following true conditions: short %R crosses above its oversold threshold AND previous short %R is below a configurable extreme threshold AND velocity > weak threshold AND (long %R > its extreme-support threshold OR medium %R > its extreme-support threshold); short %R crosses above oversold AND previous short %R is below an exhaustion threshold AND (long %R > its exhaustion-support threshold OR medium %R > its exhaustion-support threshold) AND velocity > weak threshold; short %R crosses above oversold AND long and medium %R both exceed their respective strong-uptrend thresholds AND (medium slope > its positive threshold OR long slope > its positive threshold); short %R crosses above oversold AND (long and medium %R both exceed their respective moderate-uptrend thresholds OR medium %R > its pullback threshold) AND velocity > weak threshold; short %R crosses above oversold AND short, medium, and long %R are each below their respective oversold thresholds AND velocity > weak threshold. The exhaustion threshold is less extreme than the extreme threshold. Each trigger contributes at most one to the count.
Calculation: Pro short triggers = count the following true conditions: short %R crosses below its overbought threshold AND previous short %R is above a configurable extreme threshold AND velocity < negative weak threshold AND (long %R < its extreme-resistance threshold OR medium %R < its extreme-resistance threshold); short %R crosses below overbought AND previous short %R is above an exhaustion threshold AND (long %R < its exhaustion-resistance threshold OR medium %R < its exhaustion-resistance threshold) AND velocity < negative weak threshold; short %R crosses below overbought AND long and medium %R are both below their respective strong-downtrend thresholds AND (medium slope < its negative threshold OR long slope < its negative threshold); short %R crosses below overbought AND (long and medium %R are both below their respective moderate-downtrend thresholds OR medium %R < its pullback threshold) AND velocity < negative weak threshold; short %R crosses below overbought AND short, medium, and long %R are each above their respective overbought thresholds AND velocity < negative weak threshold. The exhaustion threshold is less extreme than the extreme threshold. Each trigger contributes at most one to the count.
Calculation: Pro confidence = clamp to a configurable range the sum of: a tiered score for long %R, favoring higher values for longs and lower values for shorts; a tiered score for short %R, favoring more oversold values for longs and more overbought values for shorts; the first applicable trigger score in priority order triple-oscillator, continuation, extreme, exhaustion, pullback; a score for velocity quality; session quality divided by a configurable divisor; a larger trend score if close and a shorter EMA are both on the trade side of a longer EMA, or a smaller score if only close is on that side; and a tiered bonus for multiple triggers; minus tiered penalties for excessive absolute velocity, RSI being too high for longs or too low for shorts, designated low-quality UTC sessions, and volume below a configurable multiple of its SMA. All tier boundaries, scores, penalties, and lookbacks are configurable; preserve their ordering as described.
Calculation: Pro long qualification = long trigger count > zero AND long confidence >= minimum confidence AND (trend gate disabled OR long and medium %R are not both below their respective downtrend-gate thresholds OR recovery enabled AND the lowest short %R over the recovery lookback is below its oversold recovery threshold AND current short %R exceeds that low by more than the minimum recovery move). Pro short qualification = short trigger count > zero AND short confidence >= minimum confidence AND (trend gate disabled OR long and medium %R are not both above their respective uptrend-gate thresholds OR recovery enabled AND the highest short %R over the recovery lookback is above its overbought recovery threshold AND current short %R is below that high by more than the minimum recovery move). Strict gating shifts the two uptrend thresholds upward and the two downtrend thresholds downward by a configurable amount.
Calculation: UT source = close or the chart-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close, according to configuration. UT stop = initially zero; on each bar, if current and previous source are both above the previous stop, use the greater of previous stop and source minus ATR times a configurable multiplier; if both are below, use the lesser of previous stop and source plus that amount; otherwise use source minus that amount when source > previous stop, and source plus that amount otherwise. ATR uses ordinary chart bars.
Calculation: Zero-lag basis = EMA of close plus the difference between close and close lagged by floor((EMA lookback minus one) / two), using that EMA lookback. Deviation = the highest ATR over a lookback longer than the ATR lookback, multiplied by a configurable factor; the ATR and EMA use the same lookback. Zero-lag trend starts neutral, becomes bullish when close crosses above basis plus deviation, becomes bearish when close crosses below basis minus deviation, and otherwise retains its previous state.
Calculation: Other-timeframe zero-lag trend = the same zero-lag trend calculated independently on each configured timeframe. For a higher timeframe, historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and realtime bars use the still-forming bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar in each chart bar. The displayed state is bullish when that trend equals bullish and bearish otherwise, including its initial neutral state.
Long: Fusion bull state becomes true AND the fusion trend event passes its cooldown.
Short: Fusion bear state becomes true AND the fusion trend event passes its cooldown.
Long: Current fusion trend is bullish AND pro long qualification is true AND the optional datetime range filter passes.
Short: Current fusion trend is bearish AND pro short qualification is true AND the optional datetime range filter passes.
Exit: long, current fusion trend is bullish AND the entry-seen flag is true AND pro short qualification is true AND the optional datetime range filter passes.
Exit: short, current fusion trend is bearish AND the entry-seen flag is true AND pro long qualification is true AND the optional datetime range filter passes.
Long: UT source > UT stop.
Short: UT source < UT stop.
Long: Zero-lag trend changes from neutral or bearish to bullish.
Short: Zero-lag trend changes from neutral or bullish to bearish.
Long: Close crosses above the zero-lag basis AND zero-lag trend is bullish on both the current and previous bars.
Short: Close crosses below the zero-lag basis AND zero-lag trend is bearish on both the current and previous bars.
Long: Other-timeframe zero-lag trend is bullish.
Short: Other-timeframe zero-lag trend is neutral OR bearish.
