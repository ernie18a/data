Calculation: Structure = Confirm a swing high or low only after the same configurable number of bars on each side. Store the latest unbroken confirmed swing high and low. On a confirmed chart bar, a close above the stored high is an upward structure break and consumes that high; a close below the stored low is a downward structure break and consumes that low. Set structure direction to the latest break direction and retain its bar index. If both breaks occur on one bar, process the upward break first and the downward break second.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = On each of two higher timeframes, direction is up when close > EMA AND EMA > its preceding bar's EMA; down when close < EMA AND EMA < its preceding bar's EMA; otherwise neutral. Use the value returned with lookahead off on the preceding chart bar: historically this exposes the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while a forming higher-timeframe bar may be used in real time. The two higher timeframes have different lengths but use the same EMA length. Alignment requires both directions to match structure direction in strict mode, or either direction to match in soft mode.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = On confirmed chart bars, create a bullish gap with bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low when current low > high two bars ago AND preceding close > high two bars ago AND gap size >= a configurable multiple of ATR. Create a bearish gap with bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago AND preceding close < low two bars ago AND gap size >= the same ATR multiple. Create and update gaps only when gap processing is enabled. Delete a bullish gap when close < its bottom and a bearish gap when close > its top. After updating, discard the oldest gaps exceeding a configurable total count. A gap is first retested on a later bar when high >= its bottom AND low <= its top, provided it was not deleted first. Process gaps newest to oldest; each first retest stores that gap as the latest basis for its side and starts or restarts that side's ready window. A stored basis persists even if its gap is deleted.
Calculation: Gap distance = Minimum distance from close to any retained gap of the specified side, divided by ATR; distance is zero inside or on the boundaries of a gap. It is unavailable if no such gap exists or ATR is zero. Use the same ATR as the gap-size filter.
Calculation: Gap grade = For the stored basis on the structure-direction side, grade is zero if no basis exists; otherwise grade is 3 if higher-timeframe grading is enabled AND the basis overlaps the latest retained same-direction higher-timeframe gap, 2 if close is below the midpoint of the chart's rolling high-low range for a bullish basis or above it for a bearish basis, and 1 otherwise. The grading timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the forming bar in real time. Higher-timeframe gaps use the same three-bar gap and middle-close conditions without the ATR filter; keep only the latest gap per direction, deleting a bullish one when its timeframe close < bottom and a bearish one when its timeframe close > top.
Calculation: Scores = Let distance be gap distance on the structure-direction side, age be chart bars since the latest structure break, and aligned equilibrium mean close below the rolling range midpoint for upward structure or above it for downward structure. Structure points are 40 if age <= the configurable setup window and the latest break direction matches structure direction, otherwise 0. Gap points are 0 if distance is unavailable; if distance = 0 they are 40, 30, or 20 for grade 3, 2, or any other grade respectively; if 0 < distance <= the configurable distance limit they are 15, otherwise 0. Equilibrium points are 30 if aligned, otherwise 0. Setup score is the sum capped at 100. Freshness points are 50 if age <= the shortest freshness threshold, 35 if age <= the longer freshness threshold, 20 if age <= the setup window, otherwise 0. Retest points are 0 if distance is unavailable, 50 if distance = 0, 30 if distance <= the distance limit, otherwise 10. Execution score is freshness plus retest points capped at 100. Both scores are 0 without a structure direction. Confluence score is the rounded sum of configurable setup and execution weights times their respective scores.
Calculation: Reversal confirmation = For upward structure, confirmation is (close > open AND open <= preceding close AND close > preceding open AND close > preceding high) OR (high > low AND close - low > a configurable fraction of high - low AND high - close < a smaller configurable fraction of high - low) OR an upward structure break OR an enabled lower-timeframe upward range break. For downward structure, confirmation is (close < open AND open >= preceding close AND close < preceding open AND close < preceding low) OR (high > low AND high - close > the same larger fraction of high - low AND close - low < the same smaller fraction of high - low) OR a downward structure break OR an enabled lower-timeframe downward range break. The lower-timeframe break uses completed lower-timeframe bars within the current confirmed chart bar only: its last close must exceed the highest high, or be below the lowest low, respectively, of the preceding configurable number of those bars; require that many preceding bars.
Calculation: Ready confirmation = A side is ready from its latest first gap retest through the configurable setup window, inclusive. Confirmation occurs when that side matches structure direction AND its ready state is active AND reversal confirmation occurs within the window. Clear that side's ready state immediately after confirmation, even if no entry candidate results, or when the window expires.
Calculation: Entry candidate = Structure direction is nonzero AND higher-timeframe alignment holds AND (ready confirmation occurs OR (the latest structure break is within the setup window AND its direction matches structure direction AND same-side gap distance <= the distance limit AND setup score >= a configurable threshold)) AND confluence score >= a configurable threshold AND the configurable session filter passes when enabled AND the last entry candidate is either in the opposite direction or more than the configurable cooldown number of chart bars ago AND opposite-side gap distance is unavailable or > 0. Update the last entry candidate's bar and direction only on confirmed chart bars.
Long: structure direction = up
Short: structure direction = down
Long: an upward structure break occurs
Short: a downward structure break occurs
Long: structure direction = up AND entry candidate holds on a confirmed chart bar
Short: structure direction = down AND entry candidate holds on a confirmed chart bar
