Calculation: Trend averages = close EMAs with shortest, next-shortest, short, medium-long, longer, and longest lookbacks, plus a close SMA whose lookback is between the short and medium-long EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: Trend state = shortest EMA relative to next-shortest EMA, RSI relative to its midpoint, and close relative to the short EMA, medium SMA, medium-long EMA, longer EMA, and longest EMA; bullish requires every comparison to be above, bearish requires every comparison to be below.
Calculation: Two-pole Butterworth = with length n, a=exp(-sqrt(2)*pi/n), b=2*a*cos(sqrt(2)*pi/n), B[t]=b*B[t-1]-a^2*B[t-2]+(1-b+a^2)*(close[t]+2*close[t-1]+close[t-2])/4; initialize missing past values to zero.
Calculation: Smoothed VIDYA = recursively smooth close with weight 2/(length+1) times the absolute ratio of summed positive minus summed negative close changes to their sum over a momentum lookback; initialize the recursive value to zero, then take its SMA.
Calculation: Direction smoother = cascade six recursive smoothers with I1=c*close+(1-c)*I1[1] and Ii=c*I(i-1)+(1-c)*Ii[1], initializing missing values to zero; c=2/(((smoothing length-1)/2+1)+1); output -d^3*I6+3*(d^2+d^3)*I5-3*(2*d^2+d+d^3)*I4+(3*d+1+d^3+3*d^2)*I3.
Calculation: Volume strength = round(volume/SMA(volume)*scale), bounded by configured minimum and maximum scores.
Calculation: Volatility score = round(center+(ATR/SMA(ATR)-1)*percentage scale*sensitivity), bounded by configured minimum and maximum scores; the ATR-SMA lookback is adjusted by chart timeframe.
Calculation: ATR channel = longest EMA of close plus or minus a multiplier times the longest-lookback EMA of true range.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = previous close < previous open AND close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open. Bearish engulfing = previous close > previous open AND close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over equal numbers of bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars complete; retain the latest two confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Balanced price range = intersection of a new fair value gap and each still-active earlier fair value gap; bullish gap has low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago, bearish gap has high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago. Gaps and intersections expire when the configured close or wick penetrates the configured fraction of their range from the relevant side. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a configured higher or lower timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last available completed lower-timeframe bar. Retain the configured maximum number of intersections.
Calculation: Order-block zone = on a qualifying impulse bar, use the high and low of the lowest-low bar in the preceding lookback for a bullish zone, or the highest-high bar for a bearish zone. The impulse requires the close-change z-score above a positive threshold or below its negative, respectively, a same-direction candle, and close beyond all preceding closes in that lookback. Optionally require close and the selected zone extreme on the corresponding side of an order-block EMA; apply the optional time, VWAP, longest EMA, Butterworth, ATR-channel, recent-sweep, balanced-range and Supertrend filters at creation. Optionally reject zones overlapping active same-side zones. Deactivate a bullish zone on close below its bottom, a bearish zone on close above its top, or either after its maximum age.
Calculation: Session levels = high, low and their midpoint for each configured completed session, retained through its configured extension from session start. On chart timeframes above the available lower timeframe, aggregate the completed lower-timeframe bars inside the session; otherwise use chart bars.
Calculation: Trend-signal level = open of a bar satisfying the trend crossover decision, retained for its configured extension and subject to the configured maximum retained levels.
Calculation: Initial risk = entry price times a configured percentage, a configured absolute price distance, or the entry-to-signal-wick distance bounded below by a configured price increment plus a configured buffer. Initial stop is entry minus risk for a long and entry plus risk for a short. Target is entry plus or minus a risk multiple, or the preceding confirmed pivot high for a long or pivot low for a short when that pivot is on the profitable side; otherwise use the risk-multiple target.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop, optionally moved to entry after price reaches a configured favorable risk multiple, and optionally trailed by a configured distance from the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short since a configured favorable risk multiple was reached. Apply stop updates starting on the following bar.
