Calculation: Momentum oscillator = let gain be the EMA of max(close − close at the momentum lookback, 0) and loss be the EMA of max(close at the momentum lookback − close, 0); return 0 if loss = 0, otherwise 50 − 100 / (1 + gain / loss). Use a shorter momentum lookback and shorter EMA length for the active oscillator than for the phase oscillator.
Calculation: Force = the one-bar change in the SMA of close divided by the previous bar's SMA of volume divided by 10000; use the same SMA length for close and volume. Fast force and slow force are SMAs of force with shorter and longer lengths, respectively.
Calculation: Bull state = 1 after fast force crosses above slow force while slow force < 0; reset to 0 when fast force crosses below slow force, or when fast force crosses below slow force while slow force > 0. Otherwise retain the previous state. The first reset requires slow force < 0.
Calculation: Bear state = 1 after fast force crosses below slow force while slow force > 0; reset to 0 when fast force crosses above slow force while slow force > 0 or while slow force < 0. Otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Bull-exit sweep = 1 when a fast-force cross below slow force with slow force < 0 occurred within the sweep lookback, including the current bar, AND bull state != 1; otherwise 0. Bear-exit sweep = 1 when a fast-force cross above slow force with slow force > 0 occurred within the same sweep lookback, including the current bar, AND bear state != 1; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Low phase-end price = close at the most recent phase-oscillator cross above its lower threshold. High phase-end price = close at the most recent phase-oscillator cross below its upper threshold.
Long: Active oscillator < its lower threshold AND close < SMA of close AND close < low phase-end price.
Long: Active oscillator < its lower threshold AND phase oscillator < its lower threshold AND bull state = 1.
Short: Active oscillator > its upper threshold AND close > SMA of close AND close > high phase-end price.
Short: Active oscillator > its upper threshold AND phase oscillator > its upper threshold AND bear state = 1.
Exit: short, bull-exit sweep changed from 1 to 0 on the previous bar AND bull state != 1.
Exit: long, bear-exit sweep changed from 1 to 0 on the previous bar AND bear state != 1.
