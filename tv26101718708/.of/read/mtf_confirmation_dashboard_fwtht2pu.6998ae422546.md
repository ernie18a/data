Calculation: Bull score = 20 for each of fast EMA > slow EMA, RSI > 50, MACD line > signal line, and smoothed stochastic K > D; plus 10 if +DI > -DI and ADX > threshold; plus 10 if relative volume > 1 and fast EMA > slow EMA. Calculate on each configured dashboard timeframe (which may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe); higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Bear score = 20 for each of fast EMA <= slow EMA, RSI <= 50, MACD line <= signal line, and smoothed stochastic K <= D; plus 10 if -DI > +DI and ADX > threshold; plus 10 if relative volume > 1 and fast EMA <= slow EMA. Use the same timeframe and bar-selection rules as the bull score. Relative volume = volume / volume SMA; smoothed stochastic K = SMA of stochastic(close, high, low); D = SMA of smoothed K. EMA and MACD each use fast and slow lengths, with fast shorter than slow; stochastic K and D each use a 3-bar SMA.
Calculation: Average bull score = mean of the four configured timeframe bull scores.
Calculation: Average bear score = mean of the four configured timeframe bear scores.
Long: Bull score >= 80 on any configured timeframe OR (bull score < 80 and bear score < 80 and bull score >= 50 on any configured timeframe) OR average bull score >= 70 OR (average bull score >= 70 AND first-timeframe bull score >= 80 AND first-timeframe relative volume > 1 AND second-timeframe bull score >= 80 AND second-timeframe relative volume > 1 AND this condition was false on the previous bar). The last condition is the global buy alert; its two timeframe references are the first and second configured timeframes.
Short: Bull score < 80 AND bear score >= 80 on any configured timeframe OR bull score < 80 AND bear score < 80 AND bull score < 50 AND bear score >= 50 on any configured timeframe OR average bull score < 70 AND average bear score >= 70 OR (average bear score >= 70 AND first-timeframe bear score >= 80 AND first-timeframe relative volume > 1 AND second-timeframe bear score >= 80 AND second-timeframe relative volume > 1 AND this condition was false on the previous bar). The last condition is the global sell alert; its two timeframe references are the first and second configured timeframes.
