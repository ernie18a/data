Calculation: Tick and contract pace = trades per second and volume per second, each divided by its own EMA baseline over chart bars; calculate from lower-timeframe tick data, updating with the still-forming tick bar.
Calculation: Selected speed = trade pace, contract pace, or their geometric mean, according to the selected speed basis.
Calculation: Selected direction = either price direction (net price move divided by total absolute price move over the recent lower-timeframe tick bars) or volume delta (volume on up moves minus volume on down moves, divided by total volume); update using the still-forming tick bar. For individual trades without bid/ask data, classify volume by uptick/downtick, with unchanged prices inheriting the prior classification.
Calculation: Tick velocity = weighted average of selected direction multiplied by selected speed capped at its configured maximum, across enabled scales with data; renormalize by the included weights.
Long: Tick velocity crosses above its upper threshold OR aligned upward surge becomes true: at least two enabled scales with data have selected direction above the positive neutral boundary and selected speed above the surge threshold, and every enabled scale with data meets both conditions.
Short: Tick velocity crosses below its lower threshold OR aligned downward surge becomes true: at least two enabled scales with data have selected direction below the negative neutral boundary and selected speed above the surge threshold, and every enabled scale with data meets both conditions.
