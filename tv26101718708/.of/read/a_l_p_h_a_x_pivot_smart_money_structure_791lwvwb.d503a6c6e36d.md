Calculation: Structure = Start neutral. Confirm pivot highs and lows after equal numbers of bars on each side; update the latest and previous confirmed swing levels. On each bar, test the high-side break first and then the low-side break: the selected price is close in close-only mode, otherwise high for the high-side break and low for the low-side break. A high-side break occurs when the selected price is above the latest swing high and its bar is later than the swing-high bar; classify it as bullish CHoCH if structure is neutral or bearish, otherwise bullish BOS, set structure bullish, and replace the swing high and its bar with the selected price and current bar. A low-side break is symmetric, classifying bearish CHoCH if structure is neutral or bullish, otherwise bearish BOS, setting structure bearish, and replacing the swing low and its bar. Process both breaks if both occur.
Calculation: Structure leg = On a bullish break, set leg direction bullish, leg low to the previous swing low, falling back to the latest swing low and then current low, and leg high to the selected high-side break price. On a bearish break, set leg direction bearish, leg high to the previous swing high, falling back to the latest swing high and then current high, and leg low to the selected low-side break price. If both breaks occur, apply the bearish reset last. On every active-leg bar, expand leg high to the maximum of itself and current high and leg low to the minimum of itself and current low. Equilibrium is their midpoint. For a bullish leg, the OTE band is from leg high minus leg range times the higher retracement fraction to leg high minus leg range times the lower fraction; for a bearish leg it is from leg low plus leg range times the lower fraction to leg low plus leg range times the higher fraction.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = Sweep filter enabled AND low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars minus sweep-depth multiple of ATR AND close > that lowest low AND close > open. Bearish sweep = sweep filter enabled AND high > highest high of the preceding lookback bars plus sweep-depth multiple of ATR AND close < that highest high AND close < open. A recent sweep occurs on the current bar or either of the two preceding bars.
Calculation: Bullish trigger = close > open AND ((close - low) / bar range > 0.55 OR (previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open)). Bearish trigger = close < open AND ((high - close) / bar range > 0.55 OR (previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open)).
Calculation: Directional confluence score = Sum one point each for matching structure direction; matching leg direction with the bullish low or bearish high inside the OTE band inclusive; the premium/discount requirement disabled or close below equilibrium for bullish and above equilibrium for bearish; the higher-timeframe filter disabled or the higher-timeframe short-period EMA above the long-period EMA and higher-timeframe close above the short-period EMA for bullish, with both comparisons reversed for bearish; a recent matching sweep; the matching trigger; and both ADX and volume filters passing. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, including the higher-timeframe bar closing at the current chart-bar close. ADX passes when disabled or ADX >= its threshold; volume passes when disabled or volume >= its SMA multiplied by the volume threshold.
Long: A bullish structure break occurs.
Short: A bearish structure break occurs.
Long: Bullish sweep occurs.
Short: Bearish sweep occurs.
Long: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA > higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe short-period EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe short-period EMA < higher-timeframe long-period EMA AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe short-period EMA.
Long: Close < leg equilibrium.
Short: Close > leg equilibrium.
Long: Bullish leg direction AND low within the OTE band inclusive AND bullish trigger AND (premium/discount requirement disabled OR close < equilibrium) AND ((OTE setup enabled AND structure bullish) OR (sweep-and-CHoCH setup enabled AND bullish CHoCH occurred within the preceding window including the current bar AND recent bullish sweep)) AND bullish confluence score >= minimum AND (session filter disabled OR current bar in session) AND bars since the last long or short entry signal >= cooldown, or no previous signal exists, AND ADX passes AND volume passes.
Short: Bearish leg direction AND high within the OTE band inclusive AND bearish trigger AND (premium/discount requirement disabled OR close > equilibrium) AND ((OTE setup enabled AND structure bearish) OR (sweep-and-CHoCH setup enabled AND bearish CHoCH occurred within the preceding window including the current bar AND recent bearish sweep)) AND bearish confluence score >= minimum AND (session filter disabled OR current bar in session) AND bars since the last long or short entry signal >= cooldown, or no previous signal exists, AND ADX passes AND volume passes.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = At a long entry, stop is the lower of the latest swing low and leg low, substituting entry-bar low for either missing value, minus an ATR buffer; target is entry price plus the reward multiple of entry price minus stop, or the reward multiple of entry-bar ATR if that risk is nonpositive. At a short entry, stop is the higher of the latest swing high and leg high, substituting entry-bar high for either missing value, plus an ATR buffer; target is entry price minus the reward multiple of stop minus entry price, or the reward multiple of entry-bar ATR if that risk is nonpositive. Hold these entry-bar levels for the position.
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target OR bearish CHoCH occurs.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target OR bullish CHoCH occurs.
