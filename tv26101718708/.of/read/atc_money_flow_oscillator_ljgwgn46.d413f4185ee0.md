Calculation: Money flow ratio = rolling sum of volume multiplied by (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), divided by rolling sum of volume; use zero for the multiplier when high equals low and zero for the ratio when the volume sum is zero.
Calculation: Money flow Z-score = (money flow ratio − its rolling mean) / its rolling population standard deviation over the longer normalization lookback; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Pressure state = initially zero; update on each available Z-score. When the Z-score is zero, set it to zero. Otherwise let direction be the sign of the Z-score and magnitude its absolute value. If the previous state equals direction × 2, retain it unless magnitude is below outer threshold minus hysteresis, in which case set it to direction if magnitude is at least the inner threshold, otherwise zero. If the previous state equals direction, set it to direction × 2 when magnitude is at least the outer threshold, to zero when magnitude is below inner threshold minus hysteresis, and otherwise retain it. For any other previous state, set it to direction × 2 when magnitude is at least the outer threshold, direction when magnitude is at least the inner threshold, and otherwise zero. The outer threshold is greater than the inner threshold.
Long: Pressure state > 0 OR money flow Z-score >= inner threshold OR HMA of money flow Z-score > its previous value OR money flow Z-score crosses above zero.
Short: Pressure state < 0 OR money flow Z-score <= negative inner threshold OR HMA of money flow Z-score < its previous value OR money flow Z-score crosses below zero.
