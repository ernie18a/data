Calculation: Auction samples = completed bars from the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, sampled at a timeframe lower than or equal to the chart timeframe; if their count exceeds a capacity parameter, repeatedly merge adjacent pairs into OHLCV bars until it does not. Use the chart bar's close as the current higher-timeframe close.
Calculation: Estimated buy share of a sample = 0.5 if high = low; otherwise clamp 0.5 + 0.43 × (0.48 × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1) + 0.32 × (close − open) / (high − low) + 0.20 × (lower wick − upper wick) / (high − low)) to [0.025, 0.975]. Estimated buy volume = volume × buy share; estimated sell volume = volume − buy volume. Ignore samples with nonpositive volume.
Calculation: Price-cell buy and sell volumes = divide the still-forming higher-timeframe high-to-low range into equal cells. For each positive-range sample and each touched cell, calculate overlap fraction = overlap of sample and cell ranges / sample range, and weight = overlap fraction × (1 + 2.6 × (1 − min(abs(cell midpoint − anchor) / sample range, 1))^concentration). Use buy anchor = 0.50 × close + 0.32 × high + 0.18 × open and sell anchor = 0.50 × close + 0.32 × low + 0.18 × open. Allocate each sample's buy and sell volumes to touched cells in proportion to their respective weights. Allocate a zero-range sample's volumes to the cell containing its close. Sum allocations by cell.
Calculation: Battle score = divide the still-forming higher-timeframe range into equal battle segments. Allocate each price cell's buy and sell volumes to segments in proportion to their price-range overlap. For each positive-range sample, add abs(2 × buy share − 1) × overlap of sample and segment ranges / sample range to buy evidence if buy share > 0.5 or sell evidence if buy share < 0.5. Let volume direction = (segment buy volume − segment sell volume) / segment total volume, or 0 if total volume is 0; let evidence direction = (buy evidence − sell evidence) / total evidence, or 0 if total evidence is 0. Score = 0.70 × volume direction + 0.30 × evidence direction. Strength = abs(score) × sqrt(segment total volume / maximum segment total volume).
Long: Current higher-timeframe close >= current higher-timeframe open AND total estimated volume > 0
Short: Current higher-timeframe close < current higher-timeframe open AND total estimated volume > 0
Long: Any price cell's estimated buy volume > its estimated sell volume
Short: Any price cell's estimated sell volume > its estimated buy volume
Long: Any battle segment's score > 0 AND its total volume > 0 AND its strength > 0.0001
Short: Any battle segment's score < 0 AND its total volume > 0 AND its strength > 0.0001
