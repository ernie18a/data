Calculation: Heikin Ashi = standard Heikin Ashi OHLC computed on chart bars; its body fraction is abs(HA close - HA open) / (HA high - HA low), or zero when the range is zero. Its run is the signed count of consecutive bullish or bearish HA candles, reset to zero on a doji.
Calculation: Wave components = using HA typical price as source, calculate an EMA channel midpoint and EMA absolute deviation, a same-length VWMA with SMA fallback and its EMA absolute deviation, and candle pressure (close - open) / (high - low) × volume × min(sqrt(volume / SMA(volume)), volume-ratio cap), with zero candle pressure for a zero range. Normalize each displacement or pressure by its respective EMA absolute deviation and an adaptive coefficient increasing with ATR / source; apply a separate pressure scale, then EMA-smooth each normalized component. Use zero when a deviation is zero.
Calculation: Wave = clamp the weighted mean of the three wave components plus a scaled EMA of the change in normalized channel displacement. Its signal is an SMA of the wave. A cross up means wave > signal and previous wave <= previous signal; a cross down is the reverse.
Calculation: Internal VWAP oscillator = clamp a scaled double EMA of the normalized displacement of HA typical price from its VWMA, with SMA fallback, plus a scaled wave acceleration. Normalize by EMA absolute displacement and an adaptive coefficient increasing with ATR / source.
Calculation: Anchored VWAPs = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume from each enabled anchor through the current chart bar. Anchors are the start of the current and previous higher-timeframe day, current and previous higher-timeframe week, and an earlier daily session. Previous-period anchors continue accumulating through the still-forming period. The nearest anchored VWAP minimizes absolute distance from close.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA of ((close - open) / (high - low)) × volume, divided by SMA(volume) and scaled; use zero for a zero candle range or zero average volume.
Calculation: Divergence = compare consecutive confirmed pivots of the wave signal within a maximum pivot separation, using the price low or high at each oscillator pivot. Regular bullish divergence requires a lower price low and higher oscillator low at a sufficiently oversold new pivot; hidden bullish divergence requires a higher price low and lower oscillator low at a new pivot between the oversold and overbought thresholds. Regular bearish divergence requires a higher price high and lower oscillator high at a sufficiently overbought new pivot; hidden bearish divergence requires a lower price high and higher oscillator high at a new pivot between the thresholds. Evaluate at pivot confirmation, after the configured number of right-hand bars. Major-zone qualification can be disabled. Regular divergence remains active for a configured number of bars; hidden divergence is active only on confirmation.
Calculation: Fibonacci legs = on each confirmed price pivot high, find the lowest low in the preceding longer leg lookback ending at that pivot, then the highest high from that low through the pivot; retain the valid low-before-high leg until replaced. On each confirmed price pivot low, find the highest high in the preceding leg lookback, then the lowest low from that high through the pivot; retain the valid high-before-low leg until replaced. Up-leg support levels are high - ratio × (high - low); down-leg resistance levels are low + ratio × (high - low), and down-leg support levels are high - ratio × (high - low). A rejection requires the bar range to intersect a level within a price-proportional tolerance and close above support or below resistance. Major bullish rejection uses the deeper down-leg support or either selected up-leg support; major bearish rejection uses any of the selected down-leg resistance levels.
Calculation: Wave rollover = at least a configured majority of the three wave components have the direction's sign, wave is on the same side of its signal and moving in that direction, and the wave has just crossed, the directional component count exceeds the previous bar's opposite-sign component count, or wave has moved in the direction over a longer comparison interval.
Calculation: Reversal confluence = major Fibonacci rejection AND directional wave rollover AND money flow has just shifted to the direction from the opposite sign within a short window AND regular directional divergence or the corresponding oscillator extreme; bearish confluence also accepts current hidden bearish divergence. Bullish regular divergence may be recent; bearish regular divergence must be current.
Calculation: Directional score = weighted sum of four directional pillars and Fibonacci bonuses. Oscillator pillar awards points for wave on the directional side of signal, all three components having the directional sign, a directional cross or eligible divergence, signal outside the opposite extreme, wave moving in the direction while aligned, and hidden divergence. VWAP pillar awards points for close beyond the nearest anchored VWAP, a test and reclaim of that VWAP, and close beyond each of the current-day and previous-day anchored VWAPs. HA pillar awards points for directional HA candle, sufficiently long directional run, sufficient body fraction, sufficiently small opposite wick, and an especially small opposite wick. Structure pillar awards points for HA close beyond its trend EMA, close beyond the latest confirmed directional price pivot, a wick sweep and close back through the opposite pivot, and directional money flow. Fibonacci bonuses depend on rejection, rollover, money-flow shift and reversal confluence; the bullish score also includes a bonus for deep support rejection with recent regular bullish divergence, bullish rollover and bullish money-flow shift. Each comparison contributes its own configurable weight.
Calculation: Strong setup = directional HA run, body and opposite-wick thresholds are satisfied AND one of the following holds: (1) a directional oscillator trigger, nearest-VWAP and trend alignment, the configured daily-VWAP alignment, minimum scores in all four pillars, directional money flow and component agreement, oscillator signal inside the permitted directional zone, ADX above its strong threshold, no Bollinger-band squeeze, sufficient HA body and wave-to-signal spread, directional score above its strong threshold, and oscillator/chart synchronization; (2) reversal confluence, directional component agreement, the same strong ADX, squeeze, body, spread and four-pillar requirements, directional score above a lower reversal threshold, and regular directional divergence or the specified bearish oscillator extreme; (3) an armed swing reversal fires. A directional oscillator trigger is a cross in or shortly after the corresponding extreme, eligible divergence, or a recent cross followed by aligned and directional wave movement within the permitted signal zone. Synchronization requires reversal confluence OR an oscillator extreme, directional rollover or cross, a fresh strengthening money-flow shift and a directional majority of components, together with structure, Fibonacci, VWAP or HA context; alternatively a higher score, all components aligned, strong trend regime and rollover suffice. Optional trend, VWAP, money-flow and component filters may be disabled.
Calculation: Armed swing reversal = arm at a recent price extreme within an ATR tolerance of the extreme over a longer major lookback when either recent regular divergence, a sufficiently separated oscillator-leg divergence, or an extreme oscillator reading with recent Fibonacci, pivot-sweep or VWAP-test context is present. Cancel the arm after the confirmation window or if price exceeds the armed extreme by an ATR buffer; refresh it while the arming conditions remain true. Fire when wave is aligned and turning in the direction, at least a configured majority of components agree, money flow agrees or has improved over a short interval, and close breaks the highest prior closes or highs for a bullish reversal, or lowest prior closes or lows for a bearish reversal. Block bearish fires when close is above a rising trend EMA and ADX and directional indicators show an up-thrust. On firing, lock the armed extreme; allow a new arm only at a more extreme price or after price moves away by a configured ATR multiple. A fire clears the opposite lock.
Calculation: Normal setup = directional HA run, body and opposite-wick thresholds are satisfied AND directional oscillator momentum is triggered AND wave is on the directional side of signal AND nearest-VWAP and trend filters align when enabled AND money flow agrees when enabled AND ADX is above its weaker threshold AND at least a configured number of pillars, including HA, exceed their lower thresholds AND directional score is at least its normal threshold but below its strong threshold AND oscillator signal is inside the permitted directional zone AND the same-direction strong setup is absent.
Calculation: Signal state = strong setups and enabled normal setups share a bar-count cooldown; a bullish reversal after a bearish signal uses a shorter cooldown. A strong swing setup may bypass cooldown when the previous signal was not in its direction. After a strong signal, block an opposite strong signal during a guard window unless close invalidates the strong signal bar's low for a prior long or high for a prior short. Normal signals do not use this guard. Update the last signal bar and direction on any fired signal, giving long precedence if both occur; update each strong guard reference on its respective fired strong signal.
Calculation: Virtual position = each fired strong signal resets the position to its direction at that bar's close, with risk distance equal to entry-bar ATR multiplied by a risk parameter. Its initial stop is entry minus risk distance for a long or plus risk distance for a short. At configured profit-in-risk thresholds, tighten the stop to entry and then to a partial profit. Once the trailing activation threshold is reached, keep trailing active and tighten the stop each bar using enabled candidates: current high minus ATR multiple and rolling lowest low minus ATR buffer for a long, or current low plus ATR multiple and rolling highest high plus ATR buffer for a short. Never loosen the stop. Close the virtual position on a manual exit or on a new stop touch, defined by low <= current stop and previous low > previous stop for a long, or high >= current stop and previous high < previous stop for a short; an unavailable previous stop also permits a touch. Process a new strong signal before exit checks.
Long: wave > signal AND close > nearest anchored VWAP
Short: wave < signal AND close < nearest anchored VWAP AND no bullish strong setup
Long: confirmed regular bullish divergence OR confirmed hidden bullish divergence
Short: confirmed regular bearish divergence OR confirmed hidden bearish divergence
Long: bullish strong setup AND (shared cooldown elapsed OR bullish swing reversal fired AND previous signal direction is not long) AND opposite-signal guard permits long
Short: bearish strong setup AND (shared cooldown elapsed OR bearish swing reversal fired AND previous signal direction is not short) AND opposite-signal guard permits short
Long: bullish normal setup AND shared cooldown elapsed
Short: bearish normal setup AND shared cooldown elapsed
Exit: long, virtual position is long AND minimum holding period elapsed AND bearish HA run and body exceed reversal thresholds AND (opposite-signal exit enabled AND longer opposite-signal holding period elapsed AND a strong short fires OR HA exit enabled AND (trailing is active OR profit in risk units exceeds its minimum))
Exit: short, virtual position is short AND minimum holding period elapsed AND bullish HA run and body exceed reversal thresholds AND (opposite-signal exit enabled AND longer opposite-signal holding period elapsed AND a strong long fires OR HA exit enabled AND (trailing is active OR profit in risk units exceeds its minimum))
