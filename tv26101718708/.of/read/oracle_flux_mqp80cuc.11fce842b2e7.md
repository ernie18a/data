Calculation: Heikin-Ashi candle = recursively calculated Heikin-Ashi OHLC from chart OHLC; body ratio = absolute HA body / HA range; run = consecutive same-direction HA candles
Calculation: Flux Wave = weighted average of three EMA-smoothed, deviation-normalized momentum series (price vs EMA channel, price vs VWMA, and volume-weighted candle pressure), plus an acceleration term from the price-channel series; signal line = SMA of Flux Wave. Use longer channel and averaging lookbacks than the signal-line smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Money-flow value = SMA of ((close − open) / (high − low) × volume), divided by 14-bar average volume and multiplied by 100; zero-range bars contribute zero.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = on confirmed Flux Wave signal-line pivots, respectively price lower low with oscillator higher low / price higher high with oscillator lower high, within a lookback; retain each regular divergence for a further lookback. Hidden bullish/bearish divergence = respectively price higher low with oscillator lower low / price lower high with oscillator higher high, within a lookback.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of (HLC3 × volume) / cumulative volume since each daily, weekly, prior-day, prior-week, or five-session-ago session-open anchor. Nearest VWAP = enabled anchored VWAP closest to close. VWAP test/reclaim = low within tolerance above nearest VWAP and close above it; VWAP test/reject = high within tolerance below nearest VWAP and close below it.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish Fib rejection = price touches within tolerance of a 0.65, 0.618, or 0.786 retracement of the latest confirmed corresponding swing leg and closes back above/below that level. Swing legs are found from confirmed market-structure pivots and the most extreme intervening chart-bar highs/lows; use a longer leg lookback than the structure-pivot lookback. Also count bullish rejection at 0.65/0.786 of a down-leg support retracement.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish wave rollover = at least two of the three momentum series point in the direction, Flux Wave is below/above its signal and falling/rising, and a cross, increased directional-series count, or two-bar momentum decline/advance confirms the turn.
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish reversal setup = major Fib rejection (bullish: down-leg 0.65/0.786 or up-leg 0.618/0.786 support; bearish: down-leg 0.618/0.65/0.786 resistance) AND matching wave rollover AND matching money-flow sign shift within the prior two bars AND bullish/bearish divergence or matching oscillator extreme. A strong bullish reversal additionally requires at least one bullish divergence or oversold oscillator; a strong bearish reversal requires bearish divergence or overbought oscillator.
Calculation: Swing reversal arm = price is at a major lookback extreme (within an ATR tolerance) AND either a matching divergence or an extreme oscillator reading with Fib rejection, liquidity sweep, or VWAP reclaim/rejection in the recent confirmation window. A divergence may be a regular divergence within that window or the recent oscillator leg extreme exceeds the older leg extreme by a minimum gap. Cancel an arm if price extends beyond its armed extreme by an ATR fraction or the confirmation window expires. Re-arm only at a new extreme, unless price has moved the required ATR distance from the prior signal.
Calculation: Oracle directional score = sum of four capped pillar scores (oscillator, anchored VWAP, Heikin-Ashi, structure/trend/money flow) plus Fib, reversal, and divergence bonuses. Oscillator pillar awards points for oscillator direction, all-three-series agreement, a directional cross/divergence, not being at the opposite extreme, and directional momentum. VWAP pillar awards points for being above/below nearest, reclaim/rejection, and above/below daily and prior-day VWAP. HA pillar awards points for candle direction, minimum run, body ratio, wick rejection, and strong candle. Structure pillar awards points for EMA trend, confirmed-pivot break, liquidity sweep, and money-flow sign. Directional score is the larger side's score; ties favor long. Each pillar and the total must meet their respective minimums as specified by the selected profile.
Calculation: Strong standard long/short = HA trend gate AND oscillator trigger AND VWAP/trend alignment AND required daily-VWAP confluence AND all required pillars, oscillator-series agreement, money-flow agreement, oscillator-zone, chop, and profile gates; directional Oracle score meets the strong threshold. Oscillator trigger is a cross in/recently after its oversold/overbought zone, a recent cross with matching oscillator direction and momentum (also within the permitted zone), or matching divergence. VWAP alignment requires price on the corresponding side of nearest VWAP or a reclaim/rejection. Trend uses HA close versus EMA. Daily-VWAP confluence requires corresponding daily and prior-day/nearest-VWAP alignment or a reclaim/rejection; in the soft profile, any daily alignment, reclaim/rejection with nearest-side alignment, or nearest-side plus prior-day alignment suffices. Chop gate requires ADX at/above weak threshold but below no: ADX at/above strong threshold, no Bollinger squeeze, minimum HA body and Flux Wave spread; score threshold is raised for mild ADX and squeeze bonuses. If regime gating is active, standard signals require trending ADX.
Calculation: Strong reversal long/short = strong reversal setup AND HA trend gate, money-flow shift, oscillator gate, chop gate, all required pillars with at least three qualifying, reversal Oracle score threshold, matching divergence/extreme confirmation, Flux synchronization, and reversal regime gate. Flux synchronization requires matching oscillator-zone/cross-or-momentum/divergence, strong money-flow shift and at least two directional series, plus chart context (structure, major Fib touch, liquidity sweep, reversal setup, or VWAP-side with HA gate); the reversal setup alone also synchronizes. Alternatively, standard synchronization may pass when Oracle score exceeds the strong threshold by a margin, all three series agree, chop gate passes, and wave rollover agrees.
Calculation: Strong swing long/short = active matching swing arm AND at least two directional momentum series, matching Flux Wave/signal direction and turn, and close breaks the highest/lowest prior closes (or highs/lows, according to setting) over the structure-break lookback. Block swing short during an up-thrust: close above a rising trend EMA, ADX above threshold, and positive DI above negative DI.
Calculation: Normal long/short = HA trend gate AND oscillator confirmation AND Flux Wave direction AND VWAP and trend alignment AND money-flow sign AND non-blocked chop regime AND at least three qualifying pillars including HA AND normal Oracle score threshold but below strong threshold AND oscillator-zone gate; exclude bars with a strong signal.
Calculation: Signal throttle = minimum bars since the preceding signal, shortened for an opposite-direction reversal setup; block an opposite signal during its guard window unless close has crossed the prior signal bar's low/high. Swing signals can bypass cooldown when the prior signal was not in the same direction.
Long: Strong standard long OR strong reversal long OR strong swing long OR normal long, subject to signal throttle and opposite-signal guard where applicable.
Short: Strong standard short OR strong reversal short OR strong swing short OR normal short, subject to signal throttle and opposite-signal guard where applicable.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR × stop multiplier at entry close. Ratchet the stop to breakeven at 1R and to entry plus/minus half an R at 2R. After the profit reaches the trail-activation R threshold, ratchet it using the more protective of the existing stop, an ATR trail from bar high/low, and a rolling swing low/high minus/plus an ATR buffer. Stop is hit when long-bar low <= stop (previous bar low > previous stop) or short-bar high >= stop (previous bar high < previous stop).
Calculation: HA reversal exit = opposite-direction HA run reaches the minimum consecutive-candle count and HA body ratio meets its minimum. Manual exit also requires the minimum holding bars; opposite-strong exit additionally requires its longer holding minimum and a fired opposite strong signal. HA-only exit requires trailing active or unrealized profit above the minimum R.
Exit: long, stop is hit OR minimum holding bars elapsed AND HA reversal exit is enabled and HA reversal exit condition holds with trailing active or minimum R reached OR opposite-strong exit is enabled and its holding minimum elapsed and an opposite strong short signal fires with HA reversal.
Exit: short, stop is hit OR minimum holding bars elapsed AND HA reversal exit is enabled and HA reversal exit condition holds with trailing active or minimum R reached OR opposite-strong exit is enabled and its holding minimum elapsed and an opposite strong long signal fires with HA reversal.
