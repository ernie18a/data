Calculation: GoldBar = In the selected detection mode, all of the following hold: (1) the current New York time is in that mode’s permitted session and is a GBT time (INDICATOR: hour + minute is within 1 of a canonical GBT value; DR: hour + minute equals a 3-multiple GBT value); (2) close is within the mode’s configured percentage of the current PO3 range from any of the 15 evenly specified gold levels at 0%, 3%, 11%, 17%, 29%, 41%, 47%, 50%, 53%, 59%, 71%, 83%, 89%, 97%, or 100%; and (3) the mode’s remaining gate holds. INDICATOR gate: a qualifying clue has occurred since the current partition opened, or a clue exists and the GBT has passed, or the bar is 11 or 17 bars after the partition opened. A clue is a bullish or bearish three-bar gap, or an upper or lower wick, whose size is within configured tolerance of the month’s partition number; it is found within the configured prior-bar lookback, capped at 58 bars. A clue hit occurs when its midpoint is within 0.5% of close and the current time is GBT-active or the bar is 11 or 17 bars after partition open. DR gate: Supertrend direction has persisted for at least its configured minimum bars, and close is below 41% of the PO3 range for bullish direction or above 59% for bearish direction. The PO3 range is the current price’s containing range of configured PO3 size; the size is selected by instrument in READ. The canonical GBT values are 3, 11, 17, 29, 41, 47, 53, 59, 71, 83, 89, and 97; the 3-multiple values are 3 through 99 in increments of 3. The INDICATOR session is 09:30–10:59 or 11:00–14:59 New York time; the DR session is 03:00–14:59. Partition dates and offsets are determined by calendar month using READ’s partition schedule, with the stated weekday adjustments.
Long: GoldBar AND no open position AND (INDICATOR mode AND close > open OR DR mode AND Supertrend is bullish AND close is below 41% of the PO3 range)
Short: GoldBar AND no open position AND (INDICATOR mode AND close <= open OR DR mode AND Supertrend is bearish AND close is above 59% of the PO3 range)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + reward:risk ratio × stop distance OR low <= entry price − stop distance (if both occur on the same bar, take-profit is processed first)
Exit: short, low <= entry price − reward:risk ratio × stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance (if both occur on the same bar, take-profit is processed first)
