Calculation: Price range = floor(floor(close) / range size) × range size to that lower bound plus range size; range size is a parameter.
Calculation: Level proximity = the minimum absolute distance from close to a level at a parameterized fraction of the current price range, divided by the range size; use separate level sets and proximity thresholds for the two detection modes.
Calculation: Calendar schedule = parameterized month-specific partition day, clue size, and active day, evaluated using the chart's exchange calendar. The partition period begins on the partition day if it is a weekday, or on the following Monday if it falls on a weekend. The active day includes its weekday date, the preceding Friday if it falls on a weekend, and the following Monday if it falls on a weekend. Count chart bars from the first bar of each partition period.
Calculation: Clue = search a parameterized lookback capped by a second parameter, excluding the current bar. First choose the most recent pair of bars separated by one bar for which newer low minus older high is within the month-specific clue size and its tolerance; use their midpoint. If none exists, choose the most recent such pair for which the absolute value of older low minus newer high is within that size and tolerance; use their midpoint. If neither exists, choose the most recent prior bar whose upper or lower wick length is within that size and tolerance, preferring the upper wick on a tie; use the midpoint of that wick.
Calculation: Indicator stage = true if, since the start of the current partition period, close has been within a parameterized relative distance of a nonzero clue on an active day or at either of two parameterized bar counts after the partition period began; OR a clue exists and the bar count exceeds the positive difference between the active day and partition day, floored at zero, and the active day is not active; OR the bar count equals either of those two bar counts. Reset the first condition at each new partition period.
Calculation: Indicator detection = New York local hour plus minute is within a parameterized tolerance of any member of a parameterized time-number set AND level proximity is below its indicator threshold AND the bar opens within a parameterized New York session AND indicator stage is true.
Calculation: Range-mode detection = New York local hour plus minute equals a member of a separate parameterized time-number set AND level proximity is below its range-mode threshold AND the bar opens within a separate parameterized New York session AND Supertrend has remained in its current direction for at least a parameterized number of consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: Stop distance = at entry, select a fixed price distance, a parameterized fraction of the entry bar's price range, or a parameterized multiple of ATR; hold the resulting distance fixed for the trade.
Long: No position is open at the start of the bar AND ((indicator mode is selected AND indicator detection is true AND close > open) OR (range mode is selected AND range-mode detection is true AND Supertrend is bullish AND close is below a parameterized fraction of the price range above its lower bound)); enter at close.
Short: No position is open at the start of the bar AND ((indicator mode is selected AND indicator detection is true AND close <= open) OR (range mode is selected AND range-mode detection is true AND Supertrend is bearish AND close is above a parameterized fraction of the price range above its lower bound)); enter at close.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio OR low <= entry price - stop distance; evaluate on the entry bar and subsequent bars, with the profit target taking priority if both levels are hit.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio OR high >= entry price + stop distance; evaluate on the entry bar and subsequent bars, with the profit target taking priority if both levels are hit.
