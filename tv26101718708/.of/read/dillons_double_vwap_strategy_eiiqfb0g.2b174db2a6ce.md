Calculation: Outer VWAP bands = anchored volume-weighted mean of the selected price source plus or minus a band multiplier times the square root of max(0, anchored volume-weighted mean of source squared minus the mean squared). Reset the anchored sums at each selected calendar-period boundary.
Calculation: Macro EMA = EMA of the selected price source on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the highest high since the most recent open entry, reduced by a trailing percentage, if that high exceeds entry price increased by an activation percentage; otherwise entry price reduced by a stop-loss percentage.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the lowest low since the most recent open entry, increased by the trailing percentage, if that low is below entry price reduced by the activation percentage; otherwise entry price increased by the stop-loss percentage.
Long: Low crosses below the lower outer VWAP band AND close > lower outer VWAP band AND ADX < threshold AND (time filter disabled OR bar time outside the restricted session in GMT-6) AND ((EMA filter enabled AND close > macro EMA AND position side is not short) OR (EMA filter disabled AND position side is not long)).
Short: High crosses above the upper outer VWAP band AND close < upper outer VWAP band AND ADX < threshold AND (time filter disabled OR bar time outside the restricted session in GMT-6) AND ((EMA filter enabled AND close < macro EMA AND position side is not long) OR (EMA filter disabled AND position side is not short)).
Exit: Long, close crosses the long trailing stop OR high > entry price increased by the take-profit percentage OR (early exit enabled AND high crosses above the upper outer VWAP band).
Exit: Short, close crosses the short trailing stop OR low > entry price reduced by the take-profit percentage OR (early exit enabled AND low crosses below the lower outer VWAP band).
