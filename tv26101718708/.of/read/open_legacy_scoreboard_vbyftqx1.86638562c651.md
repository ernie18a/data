Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = a gap where current low > high two bars earlier and the gap size is at least a configurable fraction of ATR(14); retain its bounds until used or expired.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = a gap where current high < low two bars earlier and the gap size is at least a configurable fraction of ATR(14); retain its bounds until used or expired.
Calculation: Bullish FVG pullback = an active bullish gap from an earlier bar, within its configurable lifetime, with current low <= gap top, close > open, and close > gap bottom.
Calculation: Bearish FVG pullback = an active bearish gap from an earlier bar, within its configurable lifetime, with current high >= gap bottom, close < open, and close < gap top.
Calculation: Uptrend start = fast EMA > slower EMA, close > fast EMA, and RSI(14) > 50 now, while that condition was false on the prior bar.
Calculation: Downtrend start = fast EMA < slower EMA, close < fast EMA, and RSI(14) < 50 now, while that condition was false on the prior bar.
Calculation: Eligible trading time = within either enabled session's configured hours and days, or unrestricted if no sessions are enabled; outside any enabled news-event window.
Long: On a completed chart bar, (selected entry method is FVG pullback and Bullish FVG pullback, or selected entry method is EMA trend and Uptrend start) AND (aggressive sensitivity, or close > EMA(50), or close > EMA(50) AND ADX(14,14) >= configurable threshold AND the last completed bar of a higher timeframe has close > EMA(50)) AND Eligible trading time AND no open long position.
Short: On a completed chart bar, (selected entry method is FVG pullback and Bearish FVG pullback, or selected entry method is EMA trend and Downtrend start) AND (aggressive sensitivity, or close < EMA(50), or close < EMA(50) AND ADX(14,14) >= configurable threshold AND the last completed bar of a higher timeframe has close < EMA(50)) AND Eligible trading time AND no open short position AND no long signal on this bar.
Exit: Long, on a completed chart bar after the entry bar, high >= entry price + configurable take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - configurable stop-loss distance; if both are reached within the bar, use the first hit in the lower-timeframe completed bars. Also exit at the close when a short signal occurs.
Exit: Short, on a completed chart bar after the entry bar, low <= entry price - configurable take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + configurable stop-loss distance; if both are reached within the bar, use the first hit in the lower-timeframe completed bars. Also exit at the close when a long signal occurs.
