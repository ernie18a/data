Calculation: Bullish gap = the most recent chart bar whose low exceeds the high two bars earlier by at least a configurable multiple of ATR; its upper boundary is that bar's low and its lower boundary is the earlier high. Replace it whenever a new bullish gap forms. Clear it when a bullish gap pullback occurs, even if a later filter rejects the signal. Evaluate the pullback before updating the gap on the current bar.
Calculation: Bearish gap = the most recent chart bar whose high is below the low two bars earlier by at least a configurable multiple of ATR; its lower boundary is that bar's high and its upper boundary is the earlier low. Replace it whenever a new bearish gap forms. Clear it when a bearish gap pullback occurs, even if a later filter rejects the signal. Evaluate the pullback before updating the gap on the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish gap pullback = bullish gap exists AND its age is greater than zero AND its age is at most the configured lifetime in chart bars AND low <= its upper boundary AND close > open AND close > its lower boundary.
Calculation: Bearish gap pullback = bearish gap exists AND its age is greater than zero AND its age is at most the configured lifetime in chart bars AND high >= its lower boundary AND close < open AND close < its upper boundary.
Calculation: Bullish trend state = short-term EMA of close > long-term EMA of close AND close > short-term EMA AND RSI > its neutral midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish trend state = short-term EMA of close < long-term EMA of close AND close < short-term EMA AND RSI < its neutral midpoint.
Calculation: Time filter = both trading sessions are disabled OR the chart bar's opening datetime is within an enabled session's configured hours and weekdays in the configured timezone; AND the chart bar's closing datetime is outside the inclusive configured pause around each enabled news-event datetime.
Calculation: Bullish signal = (gap method selected AND bullish gap pullback OR trend method selected AND bullish trend state is true AND its previous-bar state is false) AND (aggressive sensitivity selected OR close > trend-filter EMA AND (balanced sensitivity selected OR conservative sensitivity selected AND the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND ADX >= configured threshold)) AND time filter.
Calculation: Bearish signal = (gap method selected AND bearish gap pullback OR trend method selected AND bearish trend state is true AND its previous-bar state is false) AND (aggressive sensitivity selected OR close < trend-filter EMA AND (balanced sensitivity selected OR conservative sensitivity selected AND the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND ADX >= configured threshold)) AND bullish signal is false AND time filter.
Long: Bullish signal is true AND position side is flat or short.
Short: Bearish signal is true AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, current bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance).
Exit: short, current bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance).
Exit: long, bearish signal is true.
Exit: short, bullish signal is true.
Calculation: Process exits at take-profit or stop-loss before signals on each completed chart bar; enter or reverse at the bar's close. If both price levels are reached on one chart bar, inspect its completed lower-timeframe bars in chronological order; the first level reached determines the exit, with the stop-loss taking precedence if both are reached in the same lower-timeframe bar or lower-timeframe data is unavailable.
