Calculation: PWMA = On a higher timeframe, the source price averaged over the lookback with weights proportional to the square of each bar’s recency; use the current higher-timeframe bar, including while it is forming.
Calculation: Trend = Bullish after close is above PWMA for the confirmation lookback; bearish after close is below PWMA for the confirmation lookback; otherwise retain the previous state.
Long: Trend changes to bullish
Short: Trend changes to bearish
