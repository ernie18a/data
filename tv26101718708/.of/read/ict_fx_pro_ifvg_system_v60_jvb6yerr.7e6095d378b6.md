Calculation: Bullish FVG = the gap between a candle’s high and the low two bars later, where the later low is higher; bearish FVG = the gap between a candle’s low and the high two bars later, where the later high is lower.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Fresh 20-bar low/high = current low below / high above the lowest low / highest high of the preceding 20 chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG zone = the clean bullish FVG in the leg into a fresh 20-bar low, closest to that low, whose midpoint is in the lower half of the leg, whose size is at least 0.15 ATR, whose middle candle body is at least 0.85 ATR or whose gap is at least 0.4 ATR, and whose first close above its top occurs within 20 bars of the low without a later close below its bottom. Require the low to sweep a prior swing low: a low pivot within 60 bars, not taken before the final push, first taken within 15 bars before the fresh low. If a dealing range exists, require the IFVG midpoint in its lower half. Require untaken liquidity in the trade direction at least 2R from the entry edge, where R is the distance from that edge to the fresh low.
Calculation: Bearish IFVG zone = the clean bearish FVG in the leg into a fresh 20-bar high, closest to that high, whose midpoint is in the upper half of the leg, whose size is at least 0.15 ATR, whose middle candle body is at least 0.85 ATR or whose gap is at least 0.4 ATR, and whose first close below its bottom occurs within 20 bars of the high without a later close above its top. Require the high to sweep a prior swing high: a high pivot within 60 bars, not taken before the final push, first taken within 15 bars before the fresh high. If a dealing range exists, require the IFVG midpoint in its upper half. Require untaken liquidity in the trade direction at least 2R from the entry edge, where R is the distance from that edge to the fresh high.
Long: a valid bullish IFVG exists AND low <= its top after price has moved above its top by more than 1 ATR.
Short: a valid bearish IFVG exists AND high >= its bottom after price has moved below its bottom by more than 1 ATR.
Exit: long, close < the IFVG bottom OR low < the swept low OR high >= the selected untaken liquidity target.
Exit: short, close > the IFVG top OR high > the swept high OR low <= the selected untaken liquidity target.
