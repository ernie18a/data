Calculation: Session range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within a configured session in its specified timezone; retain the completed range until replaced.
Calculation: Swing high or low = a high or low at least as extreme as the highs or lows of a configured number of bars on each side, available after the right-hand bars complete. A liquidity pool is formed by merging confirmed same-side swings within an ATR-based tolerance at their most extreme price; remove it when price trades strictly through it.
Calculation: Key levels = completed session highs and lows, highs, lows and closes of the last completed higher-timeframe bars, and the opens of the first chart bars at or after configured daily clock times.
Calculation: Directional sweep = the first trade through a level after it is created or changes; for a high or low, require a strict trade through it. For a close or timed open, require the previous close to be on the opposite side and the current bar to reach the level. A first trade consumes the opportunity even if the close does not return across it.
Calculation: Fair value gap = the interval between the first and third bars of a three-bar sequence when the third low is above the first high for an upward gap, or the third high is below the first low for a downward gap. For higher-timeframe gaps, require the middle bar to close in the gap direction and use the last completed higher-timeframe bar as the third bar. A higher-timeframe gap reverses direction when a later completed bar of that same higher timeframe closes through its far edge; remove the inverted gap when a subsequent completed bar closes through its opposite edge.
Calculation: Dealing range = a directional impulse beginning when close breaks the latest unused confirmed swing in that direction. Set its origin at the earliest strong directional candle and its preceding directional run, including the preceding bar if it extends the origin; if there is no strong candle, use the nearest qualifying local origin swing, falling back to the leg extreme. Set the other edge at the most extreme price since the origin. A strong candle has a directional body at least an ATR multiple. Promote a candidate when its range size and number of closes beyond the broken swing meet their thresholds, its directional body efficiency and body-to-range ratio meet their thresholds, no opposing body reaches its ATR threshold, and it contains either a strong candle, a directional fair value gap, or a sufficiently long run of directional bodies each exceeding an ATR threshold. Use the lower body-ratio threshold when efficiency exceeds a higher threshold. Extend the extreme as price moves; upgrade an unqualified origin when a new strong candle extends the extreme. A same-direction break whose pullback remains beyond the current range midpoint continues that range. Discard a pending range on a close through its origin or a close back through its midpoint within a configured number of bars of its extreme. Replace the visible range when an opposite candidate is promoted; discard it on a close through its origin, and optionally on the same early midpoint failure. For the IFVG filter, calculate this range on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: IFVG setup = a run of consecutive chart bars whose closes move in the same direction, with equality counted as upward, that ends at the highest high or lowest low of a preceding lookback. The run must have a minimum length or consist of one bar whose body meets an ATR threshold. Arm the high for a short or low for a long, update it if a more extreme wick occurs, and replace an older same-side setup when a new extreme is armed. Search the leg from the opposite extreme within a longer lookback to the armed extreme for a fair value gap directed into the extreme. The gap must have no close through its far edge between its formation and the extreme; after the extreme, close must pass through that edge without a subsequent close back through the opposite edge. Select the qualifying gap whose midpoint is closest to the extreme. Optionally require its midpoint to be in the extreme-side half of the leg, its size to exceed an ATR multiple, its middle-bar body or its size to exceed an ATR multiple, and inversion to occur within a configured number of bars after the extreme. Optionally require the extreme to have strictly swept the nearest qualifying same-side swing within a longer lookback, with the first break occurring within a shorter lookback before the extreme. Optionally require the gap midpoint to be on the trade-direction side of the midpoint of the available higher-timeframe dealing range. Optionally require the nearest untaken opposing swing or completed session or higher-timeframe high or low beyond the gap entry edge to be at least a configured multiple of the distance from that edge to the extreme; a swing is untaken only if no later bar has reached it, and a key level only if no bar since the extreme has reached it. A setup ends when price trades strictly beyond its extreme or closes back through the gap's opposite edge.
Long: Close is above a completed session range high AND the midpoint of the current body is above that high AND the range has not expired AND no prior upward breakout has occurred since the last bar touching the range midpoint.
Short: Close is below a completed session range low AND the midpoint of the current body is below that low AND the range has not expired AND no prior downward breakout has occurred since the last bar touching the range midpoint.
Long: Low trades strictly below an untaken swing-low liquidity pool AND close is above the pool.
Short: High trades strictly above an untaken swing-high liquidity pool AND close is below the pool.
Long: A directional sweep of a key low closes above it OR a directional sweep of a key close or timed open approaches from above and closes above it.
Short: A directional sweep of a key high closes below it OR a directional sweep of a key close or timed open approaches from below and closes below it.
Long: Low first trades strictly below a completed session low whose extreme belongs to the current trading day.
Short: High first trades strictly above a completed session high whose extreme belongs to the current trading day.
Long: An upward higher-timeframe fair value gap or inverted gap exists AND, after its creation or inversion bar, low first reaches its upper edge; optionally require the gap midpoint to be at or below its own higher-timeframe dealing-range midpoint when that range exists.
Short: A downward higher-timeframe fair value gap or inverted gap exists AND, after its creation or inversion bar, high first reaches its lower edge; optionally require the gap midpoint to be at or above its own higher-timeframe dealing-range midpoint when that range exists.
Long: A long IFVG setup first qualifies OR, after qualification, high moves more than an ATR-based distance above its upper edge and low subsequently reaches within that distance of the edge for the first time while the setup remains active.
Short: A short IFVG setup first qualifies OR, after qualification, low moves more than an ATR-based distance below its lower edge and high subsequently reaches within that distance of the edge for the first time while the setup remains active.
Long: A visible upward chart-timeframe dealing range exists AND low first reaches its midpoint or an ATR- and range-size-limited distance above it after that level is armed. Arm on a new range only if low is above the respective threshold; rearm when close reaches a configured distance above the midpoint. Apply a cooldown to the near-midpoint event.
Short: A visible downward chart-timeframe dealing range exists AND high first reaches its midpoint or an ATR- and range-size-limited distance below it after that level is armed. Arm on a new range only if high is below the respective threshold; rearm when close reaches a configured distance below the midpoint. Apply a cooldown to the near-midpoint event.
Exit: long, low trades strictly below the long IFVG extreme OR close is below the IFVG lower edge OR high reaches the selected liquidity target.
Exit: short, high trades strictly above the short IFVG extreme OR close is above the IFVG upper edge OR low reaches the selected liquidity target.
Exit: long, low reaches the midpoint of the session range used for an upward breakout.
Exit: short, high reaches the midpoint of the session range used for a downward breakout.
