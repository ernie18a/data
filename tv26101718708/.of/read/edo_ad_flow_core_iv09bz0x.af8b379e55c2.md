Calculation: Normalized A/D = cumulative sum of ((close - open) / (high - low)) * volume (a bar contributes 0 if high equals low), then rescaled over a rolling lookback window as (A/D - (highest A/D + lowest A/D)/2) / ((highest A/D - lowest A/D)/2) * 100 (0 if the window range is 0)
Calculation: Fast line = moving average (EMA or SMA) of Normalized A/D, using the shorter of the two MA lengths (the other, longer one is not used by any decision)
Calculation: Flow pivot low / pivot high = bar whose Fast line value is strictly lower / higher than the Fast line of the pivot-length bars on each side of it; it is confirmed only pivot-length bars later, so the signal fires on the confirmation bar and refers to the pivot bar pivot-length bars back
Long: a Fast-line pivot low is confirmed on the current bar AND the previous Fast-line pivot low was confirmed no more than a maximum number of bars before (the maximum is larger than the pivot length) AND the pivot bar's low < the previous pivot bar's low (lower price low) AND the pivot bar's Fast line value > the previous pivot bar's Fast line value (higher flow low)
Short: a Fast-line pivot high is confirmed on the current bar AND the previous Fast-line pivot high was confirmed no more than a maximum number of bars before (the maximum is larger than the pivot length) AND the pivot bar's high > the previous pivot bar's high (higher price high) AND the pivot bar's Fast line value < the previous pivot bar's Fast line value (lower flow high)
