Calculation: Chart EMAs = four close EMAs ordered by increasing lookback: fastest, fast, medium, and slow. Pullback, reclaim, and retest levels each select one of the fastest, fast, or medium EMAs independently.
Calculation: EMA curvature = current EMA - 2 × previous-bar EMA + EMA two bars ago.
Calculation: Trend and chop filters = after a warmup of max(slow EMA lookback, ATR lookback, ADX lookback) plus a configurable number of bars, require either the full EMA stack in the trade direction or only medium EMA versus slow EMA in that direction; require both medium and slow EMAs to change in the trade direction from the previous bar and both curvatures to exceed the positive curvature threshold for long or be below its negative for short. If the chop filter is enabled, also require abs(medium EMA - slow EMA) / close >= minimum spread, ADX >= minimum ADX, ATR / close >= minimum ATR ratio, and either no medium/slow EMA cross has occurred or bars since the latest cross >= cross lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = optionally require the medium-lookback close EMA to be above the slow-lookback close EMA for long or below it for short on either or both of two selected higher timeframes; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including a bar that completes on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Long pullback = trend and chop filters and higher-timeframe alignment for long AND low <= pullback level AND close > slow EMA. Short pullback = the corresponding short filters AND high >= pullback level AND close < slow EMA.
Calculation: Long reclaim = trend and chop filters and higher-timeframe alignment for long AND close > current reclaim level AND previous close <= current reclaim level. Short reclaim = the corresponding short filters AND close < current reclaim level AND previous close >= current reclaim level.
Calculation: Retest state = maintain independent long and short states. An idle state becomes pullback state on its pullback; a pullback state becomes reclaimed state on its reclaim, recording that bar. Optionally an idle state can become reclaimed directly on a reclaim. Process these transitions in order, allowing pullback and reclaim on the same bar. Reset a reclaimed state when bars since its reclaim exceed the maximum retest delay; reset the corresponding state after its entry signal. A pullback state otherwise persists indefinitely.
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR on the entry-signal bar × stop multiplier. Initial stop and target are based on the entry-signal close: long stop = signal close - initial risk and target = signal close + initial risk × reward-to-risk multiplier; short stop = signal close + initial risk and target = signal close - initial risk × reward-to-risk multiplier.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop recalculated on each bar. If breakeven is enabled and close reaches entry price plus initial risk for a long or entry price minus initial risk for a short, replace the stop with the more protective of the initial stop and entry price. If trailing is enabled and the same close condition holds, also choose the more protective of that stop and close minus ATR × trailing multiplier for a long or close plus ATR × trailing multiplier for a short. These adjustments are not carried forward independently; submit the resulting stop for the next bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, long trend and chop filters AND long higher-timeframe alignment AND ((retest required AND long state is reclaimed AND low <= current retest level AND close > current retest level) OR (retest not required AND ((reclaim-close timing AND long reclaim) OR (next-bar timing AND previous bar had a long reclaim AND current close > current reclaim level)))).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, short trend and chop filters AND short higher-timeframe alignment AND ((retest required AND short state is reclaimed AND high >= current retest level AND close < current retest level) OR (retest not required AND ((reclaim-close timing AND short reclaim) OR (next-bar timing AND previous bar had a short reclaim AND current close < current reclaim level)))).
Exit: long, short entry signal OR (ATR risk enabled AND (low <= previously submitted active stop OR high >= initial target)).
Exit: short, long entry signal OR (ATR risk enabled AND (high >= previously submitted active stop OR low <= initial target)).
