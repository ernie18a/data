Calculation: CMF Velocity = moving average of changes in Chaikin Money Flow; Chaikin Money Flow = moving average of ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) × volume divided by moving average of volume
Long: CMF Velocity crosses above 0 AND close > EMA
Short: CMF Velocity crosses below 0 AND close < EMA
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × stop multiplier × profit/loss multiplier
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × stop multiplier × profit/loss multiplier
