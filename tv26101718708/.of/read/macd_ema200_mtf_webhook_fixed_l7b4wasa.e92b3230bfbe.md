Calculation: EMA trend persistence = close compared with its EMA on each of a configured set of increasingly distant chart-bar offsets.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bullish count = number of chart and configured other-timeframe close-versus-EMA comparisons where close > EMA; bearish count = total comparisons minus bullish count. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar within the chart bar. Apply the same EMA length on every timeframe.
Calculation: Choppiness index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback); undefined when the high-low range is zero.
Calculation: Initial stop = minimum of entry-bar low and preceding-bar low minus a multiple of the preceding bar's smoothed true range for a long, or maximum of those bars' highs plus that multiple for a short. Risk distance = absolute difference between entry close and initial stop. Target = entry close plus risk distance times the target multiple for a long, or minus it for a short. Breakeven trigger = entry close plus risk distance times the breakeven multiple for a long, or minus it for a short. Spread is a configured price distance.
Calculation: Entry calendar eligibility = bar close time in the configured Vienna trading window AND, if enabled, bar open time outside the configured Vienna exclusion window AND bar date outside the configured holiday and seasonal exclusion periods, including the configured Easter-relative period, weekday-specific holidays, quarter-end dates, and first-Friday Vienna time window; the exclusion set is configurable.
Long: Close > EMA AND every configured historical close > its corresponding EMA.
Short: Close <= EMA AND every configured historical close < its corresponding EMA.
Long: MACD line crosses above its signal line AND MACD line < 0 AND close > EMA AND every configured historical close > its corresponding EMA AND multi-timeframe bullish count >= configured minimum AND choppiness index >= lower threshold AND choppiness index <= upper threshold AND entry calendar eligibility = true AND position is flat AND bars since the last exit >= cooldown, or no previous exit exists AND no initial-stop loss has been detected on the current date AND risk distance >= configured minimum.
Short: MACD line crosses below its signal line AND MACD line > 0 AND close <= EMA AND every configured historical close < its corresponding EMA AND multi-timeframe bearish count >= configured minimum AND choppiness index >= lower threshold AND choppiness index <= upper threshold AND entry calendar eligibility = true AND position is flat AND bars since the last exit >= cooldown, or no previous exit exists AND no initial-stop loss has been detected on the current date AND risk distance >= configured minimum.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop + spread before breakeven activation OR high >= target OR breakeven is enabled AND a bar's high has reached the breakeven trigger AND low <= entry price + twice the spread OR the configured daily or Friday close condition is met.
Exit: short, high >= initial stop − spread before breakeven activation OR low <= target OR breakeven is enabled AND a bar's low has reached the breakeven trigger AND high >= entry price − twice the spread OR the configured daily or Friday close condition is met.
Calculation: Daily or Friday close condition = bar close time >= configured Vienna cutoff OR a new date has begun while a position remains open, AND the current day is Friday or daily closing is enabled.
