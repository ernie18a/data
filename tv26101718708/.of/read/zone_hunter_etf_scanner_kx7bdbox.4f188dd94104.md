Calculation: Scan bars = for each enabled scan timeframe, use its bars aggregated from the input data; the timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. On historical chart bars, a higher timeframe uses its last completed bar and a lower timeframe uses its last intrabar. On a realtime chart bar, the higher-timeframe bar may still be forming. All following calculations are per scan timeframe.
Calculation: Price floor = a positive minimum-price-increment parameter; use it wherever a denominator or ATR is floored.
Calculation: Impulse range = highest high minus lowest low over the impulse lookback. Impulse percent = 100 × impulse range / max(lowest low, price floor). Bull retracement = 100 × (highest high − close) / max(impulse range, price floor). Bear retracement = 100 × (close − lowest low) / max(impulse range, price floor).
Calculation: Flag range percent = 100 × (highest high − lowest low over the flag lookback) / max(close, price floor). Flag slope = arctangent, in degrees, of the difference between the current linear-regression close estimate and the preceding estimate from the same regression window.
Calculation: Demand candidate = signed bar offset of the lowest low over the zone lookback <= freshness limit. Supply candidate = signed bar offset of the highest high over the zone lookback <= freshness limit; offsets are zero for the current bar and negative for earlier bars.
Calculation: Bull flag candidate = impulse percent >= minimum impulse percent AND bull retracement <= maximum retracement percent AND flag range percent <= maximum flag range percent AND flag slope <= positive slope limit. Bear flag candidate = impulse percent >= minimum impulse percent AND bear retracement <= maximum retracement percent AND flag range percent <= maximum flag range percent AND flag slope >= negative slope limit of equal magnitude.
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR, price floor). Demand zone low = lowest low over the zone lookback; demand zone high = demand zone low + safe ATR × zone thickness multiplier. Supply zone high = highest high over the zone lookback; supply zone low = supply zone high − safe ATR × zone thickness multiplier.
Calculation: Active demand = demand candidate AND close > demand zone low AND (close-outside-zone disabled OR close > demand zone high). Active supply = supply candidate AND close < supply zone high AND (close-outside-zone disabled OR close < supply zone low). Active bull flag = bull flag candidate AND close > lowest low over the flag lookback. Active bear flag = bear flag candidate AND close < highest high over the flag lookback.
Calculation: Clamp(x) = max(0, min(1, x)). Impulse component = clamp((impulse percent − minimum impulse percent) / (minimum impulse percent × impulse scale)). Bull retracement component = clamp(1 − bull retracement / (maximum retracement percent × retracement scale)); bear retracement component uses bear retracement. Range component = clamp(1 − flag range percent / (maximum flag range percent × range scale)). Bull slope component = 1 − clamp(max(flag slope, 0) / slope scale); bear slope component = 1 − clamp(abs(min(flag slope, 0)) / slope scale).
Calculation: Bull flag score = clamp(weighted sum of impulse, bull retracement, range, and bull slope components) raised to the flag score exponent. Bear flag score uses the corresponding bear components and the same weights and exponent. Impulse weight > retracement weight > range weight = slope weight; weights sum to 1.
Calculation: Demand freshness = clamp(1 − signed lowest-low offset / (freshness limit × freshness scale)). Supply freshness uses the signed highest-high offset. Demand distance = clamp((close − demand zone high) / (safe ATR × distance scale)); supply distance = clamp((supply zone low − close) / (safe ATR × distance scale)). Demand displacement = clamp((close − demand zone low) / max(safe ATR × displacement scale, price floor)); supply displacement = clamp((supply zone high − close) / max(safe ATR × displacement scale, price floor)).
Calculation: Demand score = clamp(weighted sum of demand freshness, distance, and displacement) raised to the zone score exponent. Supply score uses the corresponding supply components and the same weights and exponent. Freshness weight > distance weight > displacement weight; weights sum to 1. Zone score exponent > flag score exponent.
Calculation: Selected setup for each scan timeframe = the candidate with the highest score; ties favor demand, then supply, then bull flag, then bear flag. Its selection score = its score if active, otherwise its score multiplied by an inactive penalty between 0 and 1. The final setup is the selected setup with the higher selection score across enabled scan timeframes; ties favor the first timeframe. If only one timeframe has a candidate, use that timeframe's selected setup.
Long: final setup = demand OR final setup = bull flag, AND (at least one timeframe's selected setup is active OR show-invalidated option is enabled).
Short: final setup = supply OR final setup = bear flag, AND (at least one timeframe's selected setup is active OR show-invalidated option is enabled).
