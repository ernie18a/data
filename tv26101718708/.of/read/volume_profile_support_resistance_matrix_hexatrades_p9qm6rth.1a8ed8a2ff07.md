Calculation: Nearest support/resistance = Build a volume profile from chart-timeframe bars in the configured window by distributing each bar’s volume evenly across the price bins its high-low range covers. Select sufficiently high-volume, spaced-apart peak bins as zones; classify zone centers below the last confirmed close as support and above it as resistance. Nearest support is the highest support center; nearest resistance is the lowest resistance center.
Long: Low ≤ nearest support AND close > nearest support OR close crosses above nearest resistance AND volume > its chart-timeframe moving average when volume confirmation is enabled
Short: High ≥ nearest resistance AND close < nearest resistance OR close crosses below nearest support AND volume > its chart-timeframe moving average when volume confirmation is enabled
