Calculation: Volume profile = On the last chart bar, divide the high-to-low range of a fixed rolling window, including the current bar, into equal price bins. Distribute each bar’s volume equally among all bins touched by its low-to-high range. The point-of-control bin is the first lowest-priced bin with maximum volume. Require a positive price range and positive maximum bin volume.
Calculation: Value-area boundaries = Starting at the point-of-control bin, repeatedly add the larger-volume adjacent bin, choosing the upper bin on a tie, until the included volume reaches a parameterized fraction of total profile volume or all bins are included. The boundaries are the centers of the highest and lowest included bins.
Calculation: Nearest support and resistance = On the last chart bar, select profile bins in descending volume order, choosing the lowest-priced bin on a tie, up to a parameterized number of attempts and while bin volume is at least a parameterized fraction of point-of-control volume. Each selected bin center defines a zone with half-width equal to a parameterized multiple of either ATR or bin size divided by two. After each attempt, suppress bins within the integer number of rows obtained by dividing the greater of a parameterized minimum row distance and the full zone width by bin size, with a minimum of one row. Reject a candidate if its center is closer than that greater distance to an accepted zone or its side has reached a parameterized zone limit. A center below the latest confirmed close is support; otherwise it is resistance. Nearest support is the highest accepted support center; nearest resistance is the lowest accepted resistance center. These levels update only on the last chart bar and otherwise retain their previous values, initially unavailable.
Long: On the last chart bar, close > value-area upper boundary.
Short: On the last chart bar, close < value-area lower boundary.
Long: Nearest resistance is available AND close > nearest resistance AND previous close <= previous nearest resistance AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume).
Short: Nearest support is available AND close < nearest support AND previous close >= previous nearest support AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume).
Long: Nearest support is available AND low <= nearest support AND close > nearest support.
Short: Nearest resistance is available AND high >= nearest resistance AND close < nearest resistance.
