Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = Volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of a selected source price (close, high-low average, high-low-close average, or open-high-low-close average) since the selected session, week, or month reset; in session mode, exclude bars outside the selected session. The inner bands are VWAP plus or minus one standard deviation; the outer bands are VWAP plus or minus twice that standard deviation.
Calculation: Price zone = Initially neutral. On each bar with available bands, set the zone to upper extreme if close > upper outer band + buffer; otherwise upper bias if close > upper inner band + buffer AND close < upper outer band - buffer; otherwise neutral if close > lower inner band + buffer AND close < upper inner band - buffer; otherwise lower bias if close > lower outer band + buffer AND close < lower inner band - buffer; otherwise lower extreme if close < lower outer band - buffer. Retain the previous zone when none of these conditions holds or bands are unavailable. Buffer is a configurable fraction of the absolute VWAP standard deviation.
Long: Price zone = upper bias OR price zone = upper extreme
Short: Price zone = lower bias OR price zone = lower extreme
