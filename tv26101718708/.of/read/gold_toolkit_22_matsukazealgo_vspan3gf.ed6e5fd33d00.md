Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Supertrend = trailing bands around hlc3 at an ATR multiple; direction flips bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band and bearish when close falls below the prior lower band
Calculation: DXY daily direction = daily close compared with daily open on the higher timeframe, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Session filter = current session is enabled
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high is below the low two bars earlier
Calculation: Inverse fair value gap = a bullish gap becomes bearish when close falls below its lower boundary; a bearish gap becomes bullish when close rises above its upper boundary
Calculation: Order block = the most recent opposite-direction candle within the lookback before a swing-pivot displacement of at least an ATR multiple
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = clusters of at least the minimum number of same-side swing pivots within an ATR-based tolerance; zone center is their recency-, volume-, and wick-weighted mean, with an ATR-based width
Calculation: Swing pivots = local high or low confirmed by the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Asymmetric trend direction = bullish after close rises above the trend line by the tighter entry threshold; bearish after close falls below it by the wider exit threshold, with the thresholds reversed while bearish
Calculation: Coral trend = EMA of WMA(close); upper and lower risk bands are coral plus or minus standard deviation(close) times a threshold
Calculation: Arc direction = bullish when smoothed RSI momentum rises above the arc by a buffer and bearish when it falls below the arc by a buffer
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted hlc3 since the selected session, week, or month anchor
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = VWAP plus or minus the volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3 times a multiplier
Calculation: Regression channel = linear regression of close over the lookback, with bands at a standard-deviation multiple
Calculation: Stop-run range = highest high and lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Stop-run score = minimum of 100 and wick ratio times 60 plus ATR-normalized penetration beyond the range times 30 plus volume divided by its average times 10, plus psychological-level and aligned Dollar Index bonuses
Calculation: Harmonic pattern = alternating confirmed swing pivots matching the configured Bat, Gartley, Butterfly, Crab, or Shark Fibonacci retracement ratios within tolerance
Calculation: Session high, low, open, and close = extrema and opening and latest closing prices accumulated during the session
Calculation: Whale candle = absolute candle body exceeds an ATR multiple
