Calculation: Allowed session = the session filter is disabled OR the chart is not intraday OR the bar is in an enabled session, using bar opening time in the session timezone.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is extreme over its specified left and right bar counts; report it only when the right-hand bars have completed.
Calculation: Inverse-gap setup = for a short setup, close is below the fast, medium, and slow EMAs and the absolute fast-to-medium EMA distance is at least the gap threshold; set the setup when high reaches the fast EMA and retain it until the trend condition fails. For a long setup, use close above all three EMAs and low reaching the fast EMA. Track bullish gaps where low exceeds high two bars earlier and the intervening candle closes above its open, and bearish gaps where high is below low two bars earlier and the intervening candle closes below its open. Keep at most the newest configured number of each; remove a bullish gap when close falls below its lower boundary and a bearish gap when close rises above its upper boundary.
Calculation: Inverse-gap trade state = start flat. Check exits before entries; after an exit, prohibit entries until the configured cooldown has elapsed. At entry, store the highest high and lowest low over the swing lookback, including the entry bar, as the fixed short stop and target respectively, or the fixed long target and stop respectively.
Calculation: Asymmetric trend = initialize the trend line to close and direction to neutral. The threshold is close standard deviation times a multiplier, reduced by another multiplier when close is within a configured distance of a configured round-number interval and above the configured price floor. If direction is nonnegative, close above the line plus a session-dependent fraction of the threshold sets direction up and raises the line to the greater of its old value and close minus a fraction of the threshold; otherwise close below the line minus the full threshold sets direction down and the line to close plus that fraction. If direction is negative, apply the symmetric rules: close below the line minus the session-dependent fraction sets direction down and lowers the line, while close above the line plus the full threshold sets direction up and resets the line below close. Use the previous line in each comparison.
Calculation: RSI swing state = classify each bar as overbought, oversold, or neither using session-dependent RSI thresholds. Remember the most recent overbought or oversold state. A new high event occurs on an overbought bar after the most recent state was oversold; a new low event occurs on an oversold bar after the most recent state was overbought. On an allowed-session high event, classify the current high as higher if it exceeds the previous recorded event high or none exists, otherwise lower; record it and set bias positive for higher, negative for lower. On an allowed-session low event, classify the current low as higher if it exceeds the previous recorded event low or none exists, otherwise lower; record it and set bias positive for higher, negative for lower. Compare with the bias before updating it.
Calculation: Structure trend = start neutral; update the latest confirmed pivot high and low before each decision. When the previous close exceeds the latest pivot high in an allowed session, set trend positive; otherwise, when the previous close is below the latest pivot low in an allowed session, set trend negative. Use the prior trend for the current decision.
Calculation: Anchored channel = retain an initial confirmed symmetric pivot and accept a subsequent opposite-type pivot only when its distance in bars and its absolute price distance in current ATR units both meet their minimums; rejected pivots do not replace the anchor. From the latest accepted pivot to the previous bar's close, draw a straight line and project it to the current bar. Its half-width is the greater of a multiple of the maximum absolute high-or-low deviation from that line over the segment and a multiple of current ATR. Direction is up after an accepted low pivot and down after an accepted high pivot. The micro channel is a linear regression of recent closes, with bands at a multiple of residual standard error; its fit is measured by R-squared. Maintain separate, direction-independent cooldowns for breakout, pullback, and continuation signals.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = on every new confirmed pivot, rebuild each side from its retained recent pivot highs or lows in chronological order. Greedily group unused pivots within an ATR-scaled price tolerance of the seed, requiring a minimum bar separation from every group member and a minimum group size. Weight each pivot by an age-decay factor multiplied by one plus capped relative pivot volume and capped ATR-normalized pivot wick contributions. The zone center is the weighted mean pivot price and its boundaries are the center plus or minus an ATR-scaled padding. Retain the strongest configured number on each side. Before rebuilding, mark a resistance broken when close exceeds its upper boundary and a support broken when close falls below its lower boundary; retain broken zones for retests. A rebuilt zone starts unbroken. For bounce and rejection signals, reject a zone if its center is within an ATR-scaled distance of any of the most recent configured number of previously signaled zone centers; record each accepted center.
Calculation: Institutional candle = volume is positive and at least a session-dependent multiple of its moving average AND either the absolute body divided by range meets its threshold with close above open for bullish or below open for bearish, OR the lower wick divided by range meets its threshold and close is at or above the range midpoint for bullish, or the upper wick meets its threshold and close is at or below the midpoint for bearish. Count bullish and bearish institutional candles separately over the cluster lookback; the denominator is the number of bars qualifying as either, including a bar only once if it qualifies as both.
Calculation: Volume vector = volume is at least a multiple of its moving average OR volume times candle range is at least its rolling maximum over that same lookback.
Calculation: Harmonic pattern = on configured scan bars, use the latest three confirmed pivot lows and two confirmed pivot highs for a bullish pattern, or the latest three highs and two lows for a bearish pattern, in alternating chronological X-A-B-C-D order. For bullish patterns require A > X, B < A, C < A, and D < C; for bearish patterns require A < X, B > A, C > A, and D > C. Compute absolute AB/XA, BC/AB, and AD/XA ratios and require them to fall in the tolerance-expanded ratio ranges of an enabled Bat, Gartley, Butterfly, Crab, or, for bullish patterns only, Shark. Suppress a pattern when a retained same-direction pattern has a D bar within the configured minimum distance; retain only the configured maximum number of patterns across both directions.
