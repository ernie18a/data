Calculation: Net volume pressure = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low + 0.0001)
Calculation: Volume-weighted MACD = EMA((EMA(close, shorter length) − EMA(close, longer length)) × volume factor × pressure weight, smoothing length), where volume factor = (volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback))^volume effect, or 1 when that SMA is not positive; pressure weight = 1 + clamp(0.1 × net volume pressure / SMA(abs(net volume pressure), same lookback), −0.3, 0.3), using 1 when that absolute-pressure SMA is not positive. The shorter EMA length is less than the longer EMA length.
Calculation: Signal line = EMA(volume-weighted MACD, signal length)
Calculation: Histogram = volume-weighted MACD − signal line
Calculation: Pivot low/high = a low/high confirmed after the right span, with a left span of max(2, floor(divergence lookback / 2)) bars and a right span equal to the divergence lookback; compare each confirmed pivot with the preceding pivot of the same series and type.
Long: Volume-weighted MACD crosses above signal line OR, on the same confirmation bar, price and volume-weighted MACD both form pivot lows with the price pivot lower than its preceding pivot low AND the MACD pivot higher than its preceding pivot low OR, on the same confirmation bar, price and histogram both form pivot lows with the price pivot lower than its preceding pivot low AND the histogram pivot higher than its preceding pivot low
Short: Volume-weighted MACD crosses below signal line OR, on the same confirmation bar, price and volume-weighted MACD both form pivot highs with the price pivot higher than its preceding pivot high AND the MACD pivot lower than its preceding pivot high OR, on the same confirmation bar, price and histogram both form pivot highs with the price pivot higher than its preceding pivot high AND the histogram pivot lower than its preceding pivot high
