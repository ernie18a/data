Calculation: Basis = SMA, EMA, HMA, KAMA, or VWMA of a selected price source over a configurable lookback; KAMA uses configurable fast and slow smoothing periods.
Calculation: Band deviations = either the population standard deviation of the selected source over the basis lookback for both sides, or, separately for each side, the greater of half that standard deviation and the square root of twice the mean squared source-minus-basis difference over the same lookback, counting only positive differences for the upper side and negative differences for the lower side; each historical difference uses its own bar's basis.
Calculation: Bands = basis plus the upper deviation times a configurable multiplier and basis minus the lower deviation times the same multiplier.
Calculation: Keltner bounds = basis plus or minus a configurable multiplier times the SMA of either true range or high minus low over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bandwidth = 100 times (upper band minus lower band) divided by basis, or zero when basis is zero. Bandwidth percentile rank is the percentage of bandwidth observations in a configurable lookback that are less than or equal to the current bandwidth.
Calculation: Squeeze = lower band > lower Keltner bound AND upper band < upper Keltner bound, OR bandwidth percentile rank < a configurable threshold, with the configured squeeze method selecting either condition alone or requiring both.
Calculation: Up walk = close > upper band for at least a configurable number of consecutive bars AND squeeze is false. Down walk = close < lower band for the same number of consecutive bars AND squeeze is false.
Calculation: %B = (close minus lower band) divided by (upper band minus lower band), or 0.5 when the band range is zero.
Calculation: Walk decay count = on entry into an up or down walk, initialize the extreme close and its %B to the current values and the count to zero; reset all tracking when neither walk is active. During an up walk, each close strictly above the tracked extreme replaces the extreme and its %B, increments the count if its %B is strictly less than the previous extreme's %B minus a configurable minimum difference, and otherwise resets the count to zero. During a down walk, apply the same procedure to each close strictly below the tracked extreme, incrementing only if its %B is strictly greater than the previous extreme's %B plus the minimum difference. Other bars leave the count and extreme unchanged.
Long: Down walk is active AND close < the preceding tracked extreme close AND walk decay count >= a configurable threshold.
Short: Up walk is active AND close > the preceding tracked extreme close AND walk decay count >= the same configurable threshold.
