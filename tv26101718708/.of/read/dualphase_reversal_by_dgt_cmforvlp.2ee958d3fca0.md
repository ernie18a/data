Calculation: Momentum count for each side = Initially zero. For the long side, compare close with close at a configurable lookback; for the short side, reverse both inequalities. If close is below its lookback close, set the long count to 1 only when its previous count was zero or the completion count AND the previous close was above its own lookback close; otherwise increment a nonzero, noncompleted count. Reset the long count to zero when close is at or above its lookback close, or when a new count cannot start. The short count follows the mirrored rules. The lookback and completion count are shared by both sides.
Calculation: Momentum resistance and support = At long momentum completion, set resistance to the highest high over the momentum completion count of bars; otherwise retain it until close is above it, then set it to zero. At short momentum completion, set support to the lowest low over the same number of bars; otherwise retain it until close is below it, then set it to zero. Initialize both to zero. Update these levels before terminal counts.
Calculation: Terminal count for each side = Initially inactive with count zero. Activate the long side when its momentum count completes AND its prior terminal count is zero; otherwise deactivate it when the opposite momentum count completes, its prior terminal count is complete, or close is above momentum resistance. Apply these tests in that order; otherwise retain the prior active state. When inactive set the count to zero. When active, a qualifying bar has close at or below the low at a configurable, shorter lookback than the momentum close lookback. On any long momentum completion bar reset the terminal count to 1 if the bar qualifies, otherwise zero; on other active bars increment it on qualification and otherwise retain it. Save close whenever the count first changes to a configurable reference step before completion. If the count reaches completion but low is at or above that saved close, reduce the count by one. The short side mirrors these rules using close at or above the lookback high, high at or below the saved close, and close below momentum support. Both sides share their corresponding parameters; the terminal completion count exceeds the momentum completion count.
Calculation: Volume pressure = Over a configurable lookback, sum volume × (close − open) / (high − low) separately for positive and negative close − open, excluding zero-range bars. Divide the positive sum by the positive sum plus the absolute negative sum. Use one half when the denominator is zero, and zero when current volume is missing or zero. The pressure signal is the simple moving average of pressure over its own lookback.
Calculation: Range resistance and support = From the last completed higher-timeframe bar, let X = high + 2 × low + close if close < open, 2 × high + low + close if close > open, and high + low + 2 × close otherwise. Resistance = X / 2 − low; support = X / 2 − high.
Long: Long momentum count > 0 OR long terminal count > 0
Short: Short momentum count > 0 OR short terminal count > 0
Long: Current volume > 0 AND volume pressure >= one half
Short: Current volume > 0 AND volume pressure < one half
Long: Volume pressure − pressure signal >= 0
Short: Volume pressure − pressure signal < 0
Long: Current volume > 0 AND (volume > volume simple moving average × high-volume multiplier OR volume < volume simple moving average × low-volume multiplier) AND close > open
Short: Current volume > 0 AND (volume > volume simple moving average × high-volume multiplier OR volume < volume simple moving average × low-volume multiplier) AND close <= open
Long: Range resistance > 0 AND range support > 0 AND close > range resistance
Short: Range resistance > 0 AND range support > 0 AND close < range support
Long: Volume pressure < lower pressure band AND previous volume pressure > lower pressure band
Short: Volume pressure > upper pressure band AND previous volume pressure < upper pressure band
