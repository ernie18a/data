Calculation: Engine bars = chart bars when the chart uses the engine timeframe; otherwise, each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Breaks use the engine bar still forming; acceptance uses the previous completed engine bar.
Calculation: Range = the highest high and lowest low of engine bars in a configured daily time window in New York time; freeze them when the window ends. Range width is the greater of high minus low and a configured minimum price increment; midpoint is the average of the boundaries.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a local high or low with the configured number of bars on each side, available only after the right-side bars have elapsed. Use the same pivot strength for sweep qualification and pivot entries.
Calculation: Sweep context = after the configured delay from range end, an upper sweep occurs when the engine bar's low > range high AND, if the pivot filter is enabled, its high > the latest confirmed pivot high; a lower sweep occurs when its high < range low AND, if the pivot filter is enabled, its low < the latest confirmed pivot low. A qualified opposite sweep replaces the current context and clears its candidates. Track the lowest low or highest high since the current lower or upper sweep, respectively.
Calculation: Reclaim candidate = the first completed bar after the sweep whose close is strictly inside the range AND whose body overlap with the range divided by the greater of its body size and the minimum price increment >= a configured fraction. Retain its high and low until broken; allow another candidate only after a completed bar closes back outside the swept boundary. Clear the candidate on a context change or new same-side excursion.
Calculation: Pivot candidate = the latest confirmed pivot high below the range low after a lower sweep, or pivot low above the range high after an upper sweep, whose pivot bar is after the sweep. Replace it when a newer qualifying pivot confirms; consume it on its first break after confirmation.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = time is within the configured signal window after the delay AND the number of signals for the range is below the configured maximum AND the opposite boundary has not been reached in the current sweep context. A same-side signal locks that side until price exceeds the extreme of the entire excursion that produced it; on that new extreme, clear old candidates and start a fresh same-side context from that bar. Reset all state when the next range completes.
Long: Signal eligibility AND lower-sweep context AND same-side lock is absent AND current high < range high AND ((current high > reclaim candidate high AND (reclaim candidate high - range low) / range width <= configured maximum reclaim depth) OR (range low - lowest low since sweep >= configured excursion in range widths AND current bar is after pivot candidate confirmation AND current high > pivot candidate high)).
Short: Signal eligibility AND upper-sweep context AND same-side lock is absent AND current low > range low AND ((current low < reclaim candidate low AND (range high - reclaim candidate low) / range width <= configured maximum reclaim depth) OR (highest high since sweep - range high >= configured excursion in range widths AND current bar is after pivot candidate confirmation AND current low < pivot candidate low)).
Calculation: Entry and target = use the reclaim candidate level when both routes break together and its depth is within the maximum; otherwise use the pivot candidate level. For a pivot entry, target the swept range boundary. For a reclaim entry, target the midpoint when entry depth from the swept boundary is <= a configured depth, otherwise target the opposite boundary. Consume a reclaim candidate on its first break, even if the signal is rejected.
Exit: long, high >= the target fixed at entry.
Exit: short, low <= the target fixed at entry.
