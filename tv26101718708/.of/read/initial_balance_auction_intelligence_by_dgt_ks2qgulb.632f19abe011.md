Calculation: Initial balance high and low = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening session and timezone; retain them after the session ends until the next session starts.
Calculation: Auction window = chart-bar OHLC accumulated from each change of the configured auction timeframe until its next change. The auction timeframe can be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe; use the still-forming window for candle decisions and the window as of the preceding chart bar for state transitions. If the auction timeframe is lower, changes are observed only at chart-bar boundaries.
Calculation: Auction state = reset to building at each session start and set to balanced when the initial balance completes. After completion, change balanced, two-sided, or either failed state to probing above if the chart high exceeds the initial balance high, otherwise to probing below if the chart low is below the initial balance low. At each auction-timeframe change, evaluate the preceding window in this priority order: if awaiting a retest, only evaluate a retest, and leave the state unchanged if the accepted boundary was not touched; otherwise, if both boundaries were exceeded and the close is strictly inside the balance, set two-sided; otherwise, if the low is below the balance low and the close is strictly inside, record a failed-below flag and set failed below, or two-sided if a failed-above flag was already recorded; otherwise, if the high is above the balance high and the close is strictly inside, record a failed-above flag and set failed above, or two-sided if a failed-below flag was already recorded; otherwise, if the close is above the balance high, set accepted above and await a retest of that high; otherwise, if the close is below the balance low, set accepted below and await a retest of that low. Failed flags persist until the next session. A retest touches the accepted high when the window low is at or below it, or the accepted low when the window high is at or above it. On a touch, set continuation if the close remains strictly beyond the accepted boundary; otherwise set rejection. Continue awaiting retests after continuation, but stop awaiting after rejection. Retain the accepted side until another acceptance or session reset. A transition occurs only when the resulting state differs from the state immediately before the auction-timeframe change, after any live probing update.
Calculation: Auction pressure = reset to zero at each session start; on each state transition add a positive acceptance increment for accepted above, subtract it for accepted below, subtract a smaller failure increment for failed above, add it for failed below, add a continuation increment for continuation above or rejection below, and subtract it for continuation below or rejection above. Clamp the result to a symmetric positive and negative bound after each update.
Long: Initial balance is complete AND the still-forming auction window spans either initial balance boundary AND its close is greater than its open.
Short: Initial balance is complete AND the still-forming auction window spans either initial balance boundary AND its close is less than or equal to its open.
Long: Auction state is accepted above OR failed below OR continuation with accepted side above OR rejection with accepted side below.
Short: Auction state is accepted below OR failed above OR continuation with accepted side below OR rejection with accepted side above.
Long: Auction pressure is greater than a positive threshold.
Short: Auction pressure is less than the negative of the positive threshold.
