Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 from the start of the active session; reset at each new session
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = session VWAP ± weighted standard deviation of HLC3, with inner multiplier shorter than outer multiplier
Calculation: POC, VAH, VAL = build a volume profile from the most recent session bars (chart timeframe), assigning each bar’s volume to the row containing its HLC3; POC is the highest-volume row’s midpoint; expand value area outward from POC, adding the higher-volume adjacent row until the configured fraction of total volume is reached; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower boundaries
Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(0.17 + 0.43 × (close − low)/max(high − low, tick) + 0.20 × abs(close − open)/max(high − low, tick) + 0.20 × (close ≥ open ? 1 : 0), 0.05, 0.95)
Calculation: CVD = cumulative volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1), reset at each new session
Calculation: CVD impulse = (shorter-lookback EMA of CVD − longer-lookback EMA of CVD) / max(abs(longer-lookback EMA of CVD), volume)
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = on a confirmed bar, a newly confirmed price low pivot is lower than the prior price low pivot while its corresponding CVD low pivot is higher than the prior CVD low pivot; pivots use equal left and right confirmation lookbacks
Calculation: Bearish CVD divergence = on a confirmed bar, a newly confirmed price high pivot is higher than the prior price high pivot while its corresponding CVD high pivot is lower than the prior CVD high pivot; pivots use equal left and right confirmation lookbacks
Calculation: Bid/ask shelf power = across profile rows, take the maximum positive/negative signed imbalance, where signed imbalance = (row buy-volume fraction − 0.5) × (row volume / maximum row volume) × 2; row buy volume uses estimated buy share
Calculation: Bid/ask quality = clamp(32 + location score + shelf power × 34 clamped to 0–18 + delta score + divergence score + compression score + favorable profile skew × 18, 0, 100); location score is 18 when close is respectively above/below session VWAP, otherwise 10 when close is respectively above/below POC, otherwise 0; delta score is clamp((bid/ask signed CVD impulse − configured impulse threshold) × 85, 0, 20); divergence score is 14 for the matching divergence, otherwise 0; compression score is 8 when value-area width / ATR ≤ 2.6, 4 when ≤ 4.0, otherwise 0; favorable profile skew is positive buy-volume minus sell-volume divided by total volume for bid, and its negation for ask
Long: confirmed bar AND active session AND close > session VWAP AND close ≥ VAL AND close ≤ upper outer session VWAP band AND short-lookback CVD EMA > long-lookback CVD EMA AND CVD impulse > configured threshold AND bid quality ≥ quality gate AND bid quality > ask quality AND (close > POC AND prior close ≤ prior POC OR low ≤ VAL + max(0.20 × ATR, 5 ticks) AND close > VAL AND close > open OR bullish CVD divergence OR bid shelf power > shelf gate) AND no signal occurred within the configured cooldown bars
Short: confirmed bar AND active session AND close < session VWAP AND close ≤ VAH AND close ≥ lower outer session VWAP band AND short-lookback CVD EMA < long-lookback CVD EMA AND CVD impulse < −configured threshold AND ask quality ≥ quality gate AND ask quality > bid quality AND (close < POC AND prior close ≥ prior POC OR high ≥ VAH − max(0.20 × ATR, 5 ticks) AND close < VAH AND close < open OR bearish CVD divergence OR ask shelf power > shelf gate) AND no signal occurred within the configured cooldown bars
Long: bullish CVD divergence
Short: bearish CVD divergence
Exit: long, bearish CVD divergence OR close < session VWAP OR close < POC
Exit: short, bullish CVD divergence OR close > session VWAP OR close > POC
