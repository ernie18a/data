Calculation: Price increment = a configurable minimum price increment; clamp(x, a, b) = max(a, min(x, b)).
Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(base + close-location weight × (close − low) / range + body weight × abs(close − open) / range + bullish-body weight × indicator(close >= open), lower bound, upper bound), where range = max(high − low, price increment).
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 since the active session began; session deviation = square root of the nonnegative volume-weighted variance of the same price. Both remain fixed outside the session until the next session begins. Upper and lower outer VWAP bands = session VWAP plus or minus outer-band multiplier × session deviation.
Calculation: Developing profile = divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high of the most recent min(session bars elapsed, profile lookback) chart bars into equal rows, using at least one price increment for the range. During the active session, assign each bar's entire volume and estimated buy and sell volumes to the row containing its typical price, clamping the row index to the profile. Outside the session, row volumes are zero; the profile range still uses the most recent min(session bars elapsed, profile lookback) chart bars.
Calculation: POC = midpoint of the highest-volume profile row, choosing the lowest row on ties. Value area starts at that row and repeatedly adds the larger-volume adjacent row, choosing the lower row on ties, until the included volume reaches the value-area fraction of total profile volume or no rows remain. Value-area low and high are the outer boundaries of the included rows.
Calculation: Profile skew = (total estimated buy volume − total estimated sell volume) / total profile volume, or zero when total volume is zero. Each row's signed shelf power = 2 × (estimated buy volume / row volume − 0.5) × row volume / maximum row volume; use a buy fraction of 0.5 for an empty row. Bid shelf power = maximum of zero and all signed shelf powers; ask shelf power = maximum of zero and their negations. Both are zero when total or maximum row volume is zero.
Calculation: Estimated CVD = cumulative volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1), reset to the current bar's estimated delta at each new session and accumulated on every other bar, including outside the session. CVD impulse = (shorter-period EMA of CVD − longer-period EMA of CVD) / max(abs(longer-period EMA of CVD), current volume), or zero when the longer-period EMA is zero.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price-low pivot and CVD-low pivot occur at the same pivot bar AND that price pivot is below the previous simultaneous price-low and CVD-low pivot pair AND that CVD pivot is above its previous paired value. Bearish divergence = the corresponding simultaneous high pivots have a higher price pivot and a lower CVD pivot than their previous pair. Compare successive simultaneous pairs only; evaluate on the confirmation bar using configurable left and right pivot lengths.
Calculation: VWAP distance = clamp((close − session VWAP) / ATR, negative distance bound, positive distance bound), or zero if VWAP is unavailable or ATR is not positive. Value width = (value-area high − value-area low) / ATR, or zero if ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Bid quality = clamp(base quality + bid location score + clamp(bid shelf power × shelf weight, zero, shelf-score cap) + clamp((CVD impulse − impulse threshold) × impulse weight, zero, impulse-score cap) + divergence bonus when bullish divergence occurs + compression score + max(profile skew, zero) × skew weight, zero, quality cap). Ask quality uses the same weights and caps, with ask location, ask shelf power, negative CVD impulse, bearish divergence, and negative profile skew. Bid location score takes its higher value when close > session VWAP, its lower value when that is false and close > POC, and zero otherwise; ask location reverses those comparisons. Compression score takes its higher value when value width <= lower width threshold, its lower value when value width <= higher width threshold, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Bid auction bias = clamp(bias base + profile skew × bias-skew weight + VWAP distance × bias-distance weight, zero, bias cap); ask auction bias reverses both weighted terms.
Calculation: Value tolerance = max(ATR × tolerance multiplier, price increment × tolerance-tick multiplier). Signal cooldown = chart bars elapsed since the most recent long or short absorption signal; it is unlimited before the first such signal.
Long: Active session AND close > session VWAP AND close >= value-area low AND close <= upper outer VWAP band AND shorter-period CVD EMA > longer-period CVD EMA AND CVD impulse > impulse threshold AND bid quality >= quality threshold AND bid quality > ask quality AND (close > current POC AND previous close <= current POC OR low <= value-area low + value tolerance AND close > value-area low AND close > open OR bullish divergence OR bid shelf power > shelf threshold) AND signal cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Short: Active session AND close < session VWAP AND close <= value-area high AND close >= lower outer VWAP band AND shorter-period CVD EMA < longer-period CVD EMA AND CVD impulse < negative impulse threshold AND ask quality >= quality threshold AND ask quality > bid quality AND (close < current POC AND previous close >= current POC OR high >= value-area high − value tolerance AND close < value-area high AND close < open OR bearish divergence OR ask shelf power > shelf threshold) AND signal cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Long: Bid auction bias > ask auction bias.
Short: Ask auction bias > bid auction bias.
Long: Bid quality > ask quality.
Short: Bid quality <= ask quality.
Long: Bid shelf power > ask shelf power.
Short: Bid shelf power <= ask shelf power.
Long: CVD impulse > zero.
Short: CVD impulse <= zero.
Long: Active session AND total profile volume > zero AND row imbalance >= shelf threshold AND row volume / maximum row volume >= intensity threshold AND estimated row buy fraction >= 0.5.
Short: Active session AND total profile volume > zero AND row imbalance >= shelf threshold AND row volume / maximum row volume >= intensity threshold AND estimated row buy fraction < 0.5.
Exit: long, bearish divergence OR close < session VWAP OR close < POC.
Exit: short, bullish divergence OR close > session VWAP OR close > POC.
