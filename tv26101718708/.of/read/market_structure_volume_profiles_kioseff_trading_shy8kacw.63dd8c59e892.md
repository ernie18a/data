Calculation: Swing reversal threshold = 14-bar ATR on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; each upswing tracks its highest high and each downswing its lowest low. Confirm an upswing-to-downswing turn when low is at or below the tracked high minus ATR, and a downswing-to-upswing turn when high is at or above the tracked low plus ATR.
Calculation: Structural pivot = the swing point two turns back.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the structural pivot (previous close <= pivot and current close > pivot) while structure is Up or Down; OR an upswing-to-downswing turn is confirmed and close < structural pivot while structure is Up; OR a downswing-to-upswing turn is confirmed and close > structural pivot while structure is Down. The pivot must differ from the most recent marked pivot for that event type.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the structural pivot (previous close > pivot and current close <= pivot) while structure is Down or Up; OR an upswing-to-downswing turn is confirmed and close < structural pivot while structure is Down; OR a downswing-to-upswing turn is confirmed and close > structural pivot while structure is Up. The pivot must differ from the most recent marked pivot for that event type.
