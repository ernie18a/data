Calculation: Band basis = simple moving average of close over a lookback
Calculation: Band deviation = standard deviation of close over the same lookback × a multiplier
Calculation: Upper band = band basis + band deviation
Calculation: Lower band = band basis − band deviation
Calculation: Upper midband = band basis + half the band deviation
Calculation: Lower midband = band basis − half the band deviation
Long: Higher-timeframe EMA of close is greater than its value on the prior higher-timeframe bar AND low is at or below the lower band AND close is above the lower midband AND prior close is at or below the prior lower midband
Short: Higher-timeframe EMA of close is less than its value on the prior higher-timeframe bar AND high is at or above the upper band AND close is below the upper midband AND prior close is at or above the prior upper midband
