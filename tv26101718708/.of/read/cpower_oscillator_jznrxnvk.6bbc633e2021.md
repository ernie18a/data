Calculation: Power oscillator = Use one analysis lookback N and a separate output ALMA length, offset, and sigma. Treat missing volume as zero and missing previous close as open. Let A(x) be ALMA(x, N, 0.85, 1), S(x) the population standard deviation over N bars, and sum(x) the rolling sum over N bars. Let v be volume, av=A(v), sv=S(v), with sv=0.00000001 when missing or zero, and V=min(v,av+4*sv) when av>0, otherwise V=v. Let P=previous close, H=max(high,P), L=max(min(low,P),0.00000001), and R(a,b)=100*ln(a/b) when a>0 AND b>0, otherwise 0. Let overnight=R(open,P)^2, GK=0.5*R(H,L)^2-(2*ln(2)-1)*R(close,open)^2, RS=R(H,close)*R(H,open)+R(L,close)*R(L,open), and hybrid=0.5*overnight+0.25*(max(GK,0)+max(RS,0)). Let volatility=max(sqrt(A(hybrid)),0.01), or 0.01 if A(hybrid) is missing or negative. Let E=min(abs(sum((close-P)*V))/sum(abs(close-P)*V),1) when the denominator is positive, otherwise 0.5; let w=log-structure-weight/100*tanh(A(E)*ln(1+volatility)). Let spread=abs(close-open), ss=max(S(spread),0.00000001), vz=(V-av)/sv, sz=(spread-A(spread))/ss, correlation=clamp((A(V*spread)-av*A(spread))/max(sv*ss,0.00000001),-0.99,0.99), and distance=sqrt(max((vz^2+sz^2-2*correlation*vz*sz)/(1-correlation^2),0)). Let anomaly=tanh(0.5*distance) when vz>0 AND sz<vz, otherwise 0. Let flow=2*((close-low)/(high-low)-0.5) when high>low, otherwise 0. Let imbalance=(1-w)*R(close,(open+P)/2)+w*R(close,open)+0.4*flow*anomaly*volatility. Let upper=high-max(open,close), lower=min(open,close)-low, asymmetry=(lower-upper)/(lower+upper) when upper+lower>0, otherwise 0, momentum=(close-open)/max(spread,0.00000001), and gravity=0.3*flow+0.15*asymmetry+0.05*momentum. Let direction=clamp(0.6*GaussianCDF(imbalance/volatility)+0.2*(1+2*clamp(gravity,-0.45,0.45)),0,1), using the Gaussian CDF approximation defined below. If sum(V)>0, let buy=V*direction and sell=V*(1-direction); otherwise let buy=direction and sell=1-direction. Replace missing buy and sell values with zero before summing. Let raw=100*sum(buy)/(sum(buy)+sum(sell)) when the denominator is positive, otherwise 50. Replace raw with 97.5 when raw>=100 AND close<highest(H,N), or with 2.5 when raw<=0 AND close>lowest(L,N). Apply the output ALMA to raw; replace the result with 50 when it lies inclusively within 50 plus or minus half the neutral-zone width.
Calculation: Gaussian CDF approximation = For input z, let x=clamp(z/sqrt(2),-4,4), t=1/(1+0.5*abs(x)), and a=1-t*exp(-x*x-1.26551223+t*(1.00002368+t*(0.37409196+t*(0.09678418+t*(-0.01862880+t*(0.02788680+t*(-1.13520398+t*(1.48851587+t*(-0.82215223+t*0.17087277))))))))); return 0.5*(1+a) when x>=0, otherwise 0.5*(1-a).
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = A pivot high or low of the power oscillator with the same configurable lookback on both sides; evaluate it on the confirmation bar, and associate the oscillator value and the price high or low with the pivot bar. Compare it with any earlier same-type confirmed pivot whose pivot bar is between the configurable minimum and maximum separation, inclusive.
Long: Power oscillator >= overbought threshold OR power oscillator > 50.
Short: Power oscillator < overbought threshold AND (power oscillator <= oversold threshold OR power oscillator < 50).
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND an earlier eligible pivot low exists AND ((the current oscillator pivot > the earlier oscillator pivot AND the current pivot-bar low < the earlier pivot-bar low) OR (the current oscillator pivot < the earlier oscillator pivot AND the current pivot-bar low > the earlier pivot-bar low)).
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND an earlier eligible pivot high exists AND ((the current oscillator pivot < the earlier oscillator pivot AND the current pivot-bar high > the earlier pivot-bar high) OR (the current oscillator pivot > the earlier oscillator pivot AND the current pivot-bar high < the earlier pivot-bar high)).
