Calculation: Smoothed buy-power percentage = ALMA-smoothed percentage of total buy power, where each bar’s buy power is filtered volume times a directional weight and the weight is derived from a Gaussian CDF of adjusted log-price imbalance divided by hybrid overnight/intraday volatility; cap volume outliers, apply the volume/price-efficiency and volume/spread-anomaly adjustments, sum buy and sell power over the analysis lookback, then set values within the neutral deadzone to 50.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot low/high = a low/high of the smoothed buy-power percentage with the pivot lookback number of chart bars on each side; available only after the right-side bars complete.
Long: A confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than an earlier confirmed oscillator pivot low 5–60 bars before it AND the chart-bar low at the current pivot is lower than the chart-bar low at the earlier pivot (regular bullish divergence), OR the current oscillator pivot low is lower than the earlier oscillator pivot low AND the current chart-bar low is higher (hidden bullish divergence).
Short: A confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than an earlier confirmed oscillator pivot high 5–60 bars before it AND the chart-bar high at the current pivot is higher than the chart-bar high at the earlier pivot (regular bearish divergence), OR the current oscillator pivot high is higher than the earlier oscillator pivot high AND the current chart-bar high is lower (hidden bearish divergence).
