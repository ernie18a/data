Calculation: Bias = At the start of each higher cycle, compare its last completed cycle A with the preceding completed cycle B. Set bias bullish if A closes above B's high, or if A closes strictly inside B's range and both A's high and low are below B's. Set bias bearish if A closes below B's low, or if A closes strictly inside B's range and both A's high and low are above B's. If A's high is at or below B's high and A's low is at or above B's low, retain the prior bias; otherwise set bias neutral when none of the preceding conditions apply. During the still-forming higher cycle, change bullish bias to invalid bullish when its low is at or below A's low, and change bearish bias to invalid bearish when its high is at or above A's high. Invalid bias remains invalid until the next higher cycle.
Long: Bias = bullish
Short: Bias = bearish
Long: A new higher cycle begins AND the last completed cycle's low > the high of the cycle two cycles before it AND the intervening cycle's close > that earlier high AND the intervening cycle's close > its open
Short: A new higher cycle begins AND the low of the cycle two cycles before the last completed cycle > the last completed cycle's high AND the intervening cycle's close < that earlier low AND the intervening cycle's close < its open
