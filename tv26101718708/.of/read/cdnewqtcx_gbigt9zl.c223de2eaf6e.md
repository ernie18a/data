Calculation: Cycle sweep = current cycle makes a lower low than the preceding cycle and closes above its low (bullish), or makes a higher high and closes below its high (bearish).
Calculation: SMT = the chart asset and a correlated asset make unequal or opposite-direction changes in their corresponding cycle highs (bearish divergence) or lows (bullish divergence); include the indicator’s inverse-correlation comparison when configured.
Calculation: Bullish FVG tap = a bullish three-cycle gap forms when the preceding cycle low is above the third-prior cycle high and the middle cycle closes above that high and opens below its close; later price reaches the gap before it is filled. Bearish FVG tap is the reverse: the third-prior cycle low is above the preceding cycle high, the middle cycle closes below that low and opens above its close, and later price reaches the gap before it is filled.
Calculation: Bullish setup = SSL SMT OR (cycle low sweep AND (low SMT OR bullish FVG tap)) OR (low SMT AND bullish FVG tap), including the corresponding correlated-asset sweep-and-SMT combinations.
Calculation: Bearish setup = BSL SMT OR (cycle high sweep AND (high SMT OR bearish FVG tap)) OR (high SMT AND bearish FVG tap), including the corresponding correlated-asset sweep-and-SMT combinations.
Calculation: Bullish CISD = after a cycle low, take the open of the most recent down-to-up reversal found before a lower low; if multiple down candles in that reversal sequence qualify, use the lowest open. It confirms when the prior bar closes above that open, the cycle low has not fallen below the recorded low, and the reversal bar precedes the confirmation bar. Bearish CISD is the reverse: after a cycle high, use the highest qualifying open from the most recent up-to-down reversal before a higher high; confirm when the prior bar closes below that open, the cycle high has not exceeded the recorded high, and the reversal bar precedes confirmation.
Long: bullish setup occurred and bullish CISD confirms AND (bias filter disabled OR cycle bias is bullish)
Short: bearish setup occurred and bearish CISD confirms AND (bias filter disabled OR cycle bias is bearish)
