Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = high is greater than the highs of the preceding and following pivot-length bars
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = low is lower than the lows of the preceding and following pivot-length bars
Calculation: Pivot swing quality = for a pivot high, the pivot price minus the higher of the highs one bar before and after the pivot, divided by ATR; for a pivot low, the lower of the lows one bar before and after the pivot, minus the pivot price, divided by ATR
Long: Confirmed pivot low AND pivot swing quality >= minimum pivot ATR
Short: Confirmed pivot high AND pivot swing quality >= minimum pivot ATR
Long: Close > resistance zone top + break close buffer ATR * ATR
Short: Close < support zone bottom - break close buffer ATR * ATR
