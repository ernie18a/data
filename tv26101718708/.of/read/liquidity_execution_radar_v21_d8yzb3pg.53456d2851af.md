Calculation: Previous-day high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe day; previous-day midpoint = their average. Session high and low = the highest high and lowest low accumulated during the configured session, reset at each new day and available only after that session ends.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the higher-timeframe close with both its high-low midpoint and EMA of close. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; the source uses lookahead, so historical values expose the final values of that bar before it completes.
Calculation: Range = high minus low; body = absolute close minus open; upper wick = high minus max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) minus low. Divide each by range for its ratio, using zero when range is zero. Bull close strength = (close minus low) / range; bear close strength = (high minus close) / range; use one-half when range is zero.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), using one when the average is not positive. Dead volume = volume ratio below its dead-volume threshold; strong volume = volume ratio at or above its strong-volume threshold.
Calculation: Bull displacement = close > open AND range > ATR times the displacement multiplier AND body ratio > body threshold AND bull close strength > close-strength threshold AND volume is not dead. Bear displacement = close < open AND range > ATR times the displacement multiplier AND body ratio > body threshold AND bear close strength > close-strength threshold AND volume is not dead.
Calculation: High-liquidity cluster = maximum of prior highs within ATR times a tolerance multiplier of the highest high over the cluster lookback including the current bar; inspect the preceding cluster-lookback number of bars and count the qualifying highs. Low-liquidity cluster is the symmetric minimum using lows. Liquidity above = high-cluster count at least the required hit count OR previous high >= previous-day high minus tolerance OR previous high >= completed session high minus tolerance. Liquidity below = low-cluster count at least the required hit count OR previous low <= previous-day low plus tolerance OR previous low <= completed session low plus tolerance.
Calculation: High sweep = high > a level plus ATR times the sweep-buffer multiplier AND close < that level, where the level is the previous-day high, completed session high, or high-liquidity cluster; the cluster case also requires liquidity above. Low sweep = low < a level minus the buffer AND close > that level, where the level is the previous-day low, completed session low, or low-liquidity cluster; the cluster case also requires liquidity below.
Calculation: Bull structure shift = close > the latest confirmed pivot high plus ATR times the structure-buffer multiplier AND bull displacement. Bear structure shift = close < the latest confirmed pivot low minus the buffer AND bear displacement. Confirm each pivot using the same number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Average daily range = SMA on chart bars of the higher-timeframe day’s high minus low, using the still-forming day; the source uses lookahead, exposing the completed day’s final range on historical chart bars. Upper and lower range boundaries = the current higher-timeframe day open plus and minus that average. Near upper boundary = close >= upper boundary minus an ATR buffer; near lower boundary = close <= lower boundary plus the buffer.
Calculation: Good regime = current UTC hour is in a configured trading window AND volume is not dead AND absolute distance from previous-day midpoint >= an ATR buffer AND the optional range filter does not find price near the upper boundary with bullish higher-timeframe bias or near the lower boundary with bearish higher-timeframe bias. Bad regime = dead volume OR the optional range filter finds price near the upper boundary with bullish higher-timeframe bias and no high sweep, or near the lower boundary with bearish higher-timeframe bias and no low sweep. Regime score is a high, low, or intermediate parameter for good, bad, or otherwise, respectively; good takes precedence.
Calculation: Compression = highest high minus lowest low over the compression lookback < SMA(range) over that lookback times the lookback length times a compression multiplier.
Calculation: Up exhaustion = rounded, bounded SMA of a weighted sum of: close higher than every close in a preceding lookback; upper-wick ratio above its threshold; close below high minus a range fraction; that rising-close condition together with volume ratio declining on each of the last two bars; and close above previous-day high. Down exhaustion is symmetric using lower closes, lower wick, close above low plus a range fraction, declining volume ratio, and close below previous-day low.
Calculation: Reversal score for each direction = weighted sum of the current bar’s same-direction sweep, reclaim, displacement, structure shift, opposite-direction exhaustion above its threshold, close on the favorable side of the previous-day midpoint, good regime, and liquidity on the opposite side. Continuation score = weighted sum of matching higher-timeframe bias, close on the matching side of the previous-day midpoint, matching displacement, strong volume, absence of an opposite sweep, close beyond the matching previous-day extreme, absence of proximity to the matching daily-range boundary, and compression. Each term has its own weight.
Calculation: For a long, candidate targets are previous-day midpoint, completed session high if available otherwise previous-day high, and the lesser of the upper daily-range boundary and the high-liquidity cluster if available otherwise previous-day high. For a short, use previous-day midpoint, completed session low if available otherwise previous-day low, and the greater of the lower daily-range boundary and the low-liquidity cluster if available otherwise previous-day low. Best reward/risk = maximum absolute target distance from close divided by absolute close distance from the sweep-bar invalidation; use zero when risk is zero. Reward/risk score is selected from descending score tiers by comparing best reward/risk with descending thresholds.
Calculation: Final score for each direction = max(reversal score, continuation score) + reward/risk score + truncated weighted regime score, capped below the entry threshold in a bad regime. The optional high-grade filter requires both final score and best reward/risk to reach their respective high-grade thresholds.
Calculation: Setup state = reset to idle at each new day; while idle, a low sweep starts a long setup and stores that bar’s low as invalidation, otherwise a high sweep starts a short setup and stores that bar’s high. Within the allowed bars after the sweep, a long reclaim requires close > previous-day low AND close > open; a short reclaim requires close < previous-day high AND close < open. After reclaim, including on the same bar, long confirmation requires bull displacement AND (bull structure shift OR close > previous-day midpoint); short confirmation requires bear displacement AND (bear structure shift OR close < previous-day midpoint). Evaluate confirmation before expiring an unconfirmed setup whose sweep age exceeds the allowance. A confirmed state persists until the next new day, even if its entry score is initially insufficient.
