Calculation: Previous-day high, low, and midpoint = high, low, and their midpoint from the last completed daily bar; current-day open = open of the daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Asia-session high and low = highest high and lowest low during the configured UTC session, fixed after that session ends.
Calculation: Liquidity cluster high/low = highest high/lowest low in the lookback, with hits counted among the preceding bars whose highs/lows are within an ATR-based tolerance of that extreme; a cluster qualifies with at least two hits. Liquidity above/below also qualifies when the preceding bar is within that tolerance of the previous-day or locked Asia high/low.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low is below the previous-day low, locked Asia low, or qualifying liquidity-cluster low by an ATR-based buffer, and close is above that level. Bearish sweep = high is above the previous-day high, locked Asia high, or qualifying liquidity-cluster high by an ATR-based buffer, and close is below that level.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open, bar range exceeds an ATR-based threshold, body/range and (close − low)/range exceed thresholds, and volume is not dead. Bearish displacement = close < open, bar range exceeds an ATR-based threshold, body/range and (high − close)/range exceed thresholds, and volume is not dead. Dead volume = volume below a threshold times its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Bullish market-structure shift = close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high by an ATR-based buffer and bullish displacement holds. Bearish market-structure shift = close is below the latest confirmed swing low by an ATR-based buffer and bearish displacement holds. Confirm swing highs/lows using equal left- and right-side lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish entry state = after a bullish sweep while idle, within the allowed bars after the sweep, close > previous-day low and close > open; then bullish displacement holds and either bullish market-structure shift holds or close > previous-day midpoint, with volume not dead. Bearish entry state = after a bearish sweep while idle, within the allowed bars after the sweep, close < previous-day high and close < open; then bearish displacement holds and either bearish market-structure shift holds or close < previous-day midpoint, with volume not dead. Reset the state at each new day or when the confirmation window expires.
Calculation: Bullish final score = greater of bullish reversal score and bullish continuation score, plus reward/risk score and regime contribution; cap at the weak-regime cap when regime is bad. Reversal score uses bullish sweep, reclaim, displacement, market-structure shift, bearish exhaustion, discount location, good regime, and liquidity above. Continuation score uses higher-timeframe bullish bias, close above previous-day midpoint, bullish displacement, strong volume, absence of bearish sweep, up-trend day, not near ADR high, and compression. Bearish final score is symmetric: use bearish reversal/continuation factors and bearish reward/risk; cap when regime is bad. Higher-timeframe bias uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming: close above/below its high-low midpoint and its 20-bar EMA. Regime is good when in a kill zone, volume is not dead, price is not near the previous-day midpoint, and the ADR-overextension exclusion does not apply; regime is bad when volume is dead or the ADR-overextension condition applies. ADR uses the average daily range over its lookback, including the daily bar still forming; compare close with current-day open plus/minus ADR, using an ATR-based proximity buffer. Reward/risk is the greatest reward/risk ratio across the three directional targets, using the sweep price as invalidation; targets are previous-day midpoint, locked Asia extreme (or previous-day extreme if unavailable), and the nearer of the liquidity/previous-day extreme and ADR boundary. Scores, thresholds, and lookbacks are configurable.
Long: Bullish sweep OR bullish market-structure shift OR (bullish entry state AND bullish final score meets the entry threshold)
Short: Bearish sweep OR bearish market-structure shift OR (bearish entry state AND bearish final score meets the entry threshold)
