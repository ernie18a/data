Calculation: CVD delta = sum over lower-timeframe bars (timeframe lower than the chart, using completed bars within each chart bar) of volume for each close rise, minus volume for each close fall; equal closes and the first intrabar close contribute zero. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use chart-bar volume signed by its close versus the previous chart-bar close.
Calculation: CVD = running sum of CVD delta, reset at the configured anchor boundary, or never reset if cumulative anchoring is selected.
Calculation: Confirmed price and CVD pivots = local highs or lows using the same configured lookback on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Divergence strength = 50 × [(1 − exp(− absolute price-pivot change / ATR)) + (1 − exp(− absolute CVD-pivot change / (5 × rolling standard deviation of CVD delta)))].
Long: At a confirmed price low pivot, the latest two price low pivots are within the configured maximum gap, each has a distinct CVD low pivot within the pivot-matching tolerance, the newer price low is lower than the prior low AND its matched CVD low is higher than the prior matched CVD low, divergence strength meets the configured minimum, regular bullish divergences are enabled, and the regular bullish cooldown has elapsed OR the same conditions hold with the newer price low higher than the prior low AND its matched CVD low lower than the prior matched CVD low, with hidden bullish divergences enabled and cooldown elapsed OR CVD crosses from at-or-below zero to above zero OR the smoothed CVD rises on this bar and did not rise on the prior bar.
Short: At a confirmed price high pivot, the latest two price high pivots are within the configured maximum gap, each has a distinct CVD high pivot within the pivot-matching tolerance, the newer price high is higher than the prior high AND its matched CVD high is lower than the prior matched CVD high, divergence strength meets the configured minimum, regular bearish divergences are enabled, and the regular bearish cooldown has elapsed OR the same conditions hold with the newer price high lower than the prior high AND its matched CVD high higher than the prior matched CVD high, with hidden bearish divergences enabled and cooldown elapsed OR CVD crosses from at-or-above zero to below zero OR the smoothed CVD falls on this bar and did not fall on the prior bar.
