Calculation: Delta = sum over completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, excluding the first: add the bar's volume if its close is above the preceding lower-timeframe close, subtract it if below, and add zero if equal; optionally use one instead of volume. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume with the sign of the chart close change, or zero if unchanged or the previous close is unavailable.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of Delta, optionally reset before adding the current Delta at each day boundary or week boundary; session reset uses the day boundary.
Calculation: Slope ratio = clamp(0.5 + EMA(normalized CVD change, slope lookback) / 4, 0, 1), where CVD change is current CVD minus previous CVD, replaced by zero when unavailable, and normalized CVD change is that change divided by EMA(abs(CVD change), the same slope lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Divergence pivot pair = on confirmation of a price pivot, take the two most recent confirmed price highs or lows of the same kind, retaining only a configurable maximum number of pivots per kind. A pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks. From the retained confirmed CVD pivots of the same kind, select the latest within twice the pivot lookback plus one bar of the newer price pivot, then the latest distinct CVD pivot within the same tolerance of the older price pivot. The selected CVD pivots need not be chronological. Evaluate on the newer price pivot's confirmation bar.
Calculation: Divergence strength = clamp(50 * (2 - exp(-price movement) - exp(-CVD movement / 5)), 0, 100), where price movement is the absolute price-pivot difference divided by current ATR, or zero if ATR is not positive, and CVD movement is the absolute selected CVD-pivot difference divided by current rolling standard deviation of Delta, or zero if that standard deviation is not positive. ATR and standard deviation have independently configurable lookbacks.
Long: Delta >= 0.
Short: Delta < 0.
Long: CVD > 0.
Short: CVD < 0.
Long: Slope ratio > 0.5.
Short: Slope ratio < 0.5.
Long: CVD crosses above zero.
Short: CVD crosses below zero.
Long: EMA of CVD > its previous value AND its previous value <= its value two bars ago.
Short: EMA of CVD < its previous value AND its previous value >= its value two bars ago.
Long: A price-low pivot pair and two matching CVD-low pivots exist AND the price-pivot gap <= the divergence window AND bars since the last regular bullish divergence >= its cooldown AND newer price low < older price low AND matched newer CVD low > matched older CVD low AND divergence strength >= minimum strength; optionally allow equality in both pivot comparisons.
Long: A price-low pivot pair and two matching CVD-low pivots exist AND the price-pivot gap <= the divergence window AND bars since the last hidden bullish divergence >= its cooldown AND newer price low > older price low AND matched newer CVD low < matched older CVD low AND divergence strength >= minimum strength; optionally allow equality in both pivot comparisons.
Short: A price-high pivot pair and two matching CVD-high pivots exist AND the price-pivot gap <= the divergence window AND bars since the last regular bearish divergence >= its cooldown AND newer price high > older price high AND matched newer CVD high < matched older CVD high AND divergence strength >= minimum strength; optionally allow equality in both pivot comparisons.
Short: A price-high pivot pair and two matching CVD-high pivots exist AND the price-pivot gap <= the divergence window AND bars since the last hidden bearish divergence >= its cooldown AND newer price high < older price high AND matched newer CVD high > matched older CVD high AND divergence strength >= minimum strength; optionally allow equality in both pivot comparisons.
