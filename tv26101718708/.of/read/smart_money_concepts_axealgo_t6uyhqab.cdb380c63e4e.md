Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low confirmed after the specified right-side confirmation bars, using a specified number of preceding bars. Swing pivots use a longer preceding-bar lookback than internal pivots; liquidity pivots use their own lookback.
Calculation: Structure state = separately for swing and internal pivots, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low until replaced or broken. On each bar, update pivots first; if close exceeds the retained high, set state bullish and consume that high; otherwise, if close is below the retained low, set state bearish and consume that low. Initially the state is neutral.
Calculation: Structure label eligibility = a structure break is eligible unless it continues the existing structure direction and the previous eligible swing-structure label had the same direction and occurred fewer than the throttle bars ago. A change of direction is always eligible. Update the previous-label record only when a label is eligible.
Calculation: Displacement = the greater of the absolute close change since the broken swing pivot bar and the absolute distance from close to the broken pivot price, divided by ATR. Limit the lookback to the configured maximum and at least one bar.
Calculation: Order-block candidate = after a swing break, scan the configured number of preceding bars for the lowest low for a bullish break or highest high for a bearish break. When automatic tuning is enabled, the scan is at least the swing pivot lookback plus its confirmation bars. From the older of that extreme plus the allowed slack and the scan limit, scan toward the preceding bar and select the first opposite-colored candle; if none exists, select the extreme candle. Use its high and low as the zone, extending the lower edge to the scanned low for bullish blocks or the upper edge to the scanned high for bearish blocks. Cap zone height at the configured ATR multiple, preserving the lower edge for bullish blocks and the upper edge for bearish blocks. If imbalance is required, at least one same-direction three-bar gap must occur between the selected candle and the current bar.
Calculation: Active zones = process new order blocks before new fair value gaps. Reject a candidate if its midpoint lies within an unmitigated existing zone of the same kind, regardless of direction. Keep only the newest configured maximum number of zones across both kinds. Starting on the bar after creation, remove a zone if its midpoint is farther from close than the configured ATR multiple. Otherwise, shrink a bullish gap's upper edge to the greater of current low and its lower edge when low enters it; shrink a bearish gap's lower edge to the lesser of current high and its upper edge when high enters it. Mark a zone mitigated when low reaches its lower edge for a bullish zone, high reaches its upper edge for a bearish zone, close falls below its lower edge for a bullish zone, or close rises above its upper edge for a bearish zone. Remove mitigated zones after the configured retention period. Zone state is shared by order-block and gap decisions.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = separately buffer confirmed liquidity pivot highs and lows. For each newly confirmed pivot, first merge it into the first same-side pool within the configured ATR tolerance, setting a high pool to the greater level or a low pool to the lesser level. Otherwise, remove buffered pivots older than the configured maximum age and pair it with the oldest buffered same-side pivot within tolerance; create a pool at the greater high or lesser low and remove the paired pivot from the buffer. If no pair exists, buffer the new pivot and discard the oldest buffered pivot when its configured capacity is exceeded. On each bar, after adding pools, remove a high pool when high exceeds its level and close is at or below it, when close exceeds it, or when its distance from close exceeds the configured ATR multiple; apply the mirrored conditions to low pools. Finally retain only the newest configured maximum number of pools.
Long: Swing structure breaks upward AND structure label eligibility holds.
Short: Swing structure breaks downward AND structure label eligibility holds.
Long: Swing structure state is bullish OR internal structure state is bullish.
Short: Swing structure state is bearish OR internal structure state is bearish.
Long: Swing structure breaks upward AND ATR is positive AND displacement is at least its threshold AND the bullish order-block candidate meets the imbalance requirement when enabled AND its height is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND its midpoint is outside every unmitigated order block.
Short: Swing structure breaks downward AND ATR is positive AND displacement is at least its threshold AND the bearish order-block candidate meets the imbalance requirement when enabled AND its height is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND its midpoint is outside every unmitigated order block.
Long: ATR is positive AND low exceeds the high two bars earlier AND the gap is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND its midpoint is outside every unmitigated fair value gap.
Short: ATR is positive AND high is below the low two bars earlier AND the gap is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND its midpoint is outside every unmitigated fair value gap.
Long: A low liquidity pool exists AND low is below its level AND close is at or above its level.
Short: A high liquidity pool exists AND high is above its level AND close is at or below its level.
