Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a local high/low using a lookback on both sides; evaluate only after the right-side bars have completed on the chart timeframe. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = on a confirmed bullish swing-structure break meeting the minimum-displacement filter, select the most extreme low in the preceding scan window, then use the nearest bearish candle at or before the configured slack from that extreme (or the extreme candle if none exists). The zone spans that candle’s high to low, extending its lower edge to the extreme low in auto mode; cap its height at the maximum ATR multiple and require its height to meet the minimum ATR multiple. Require a bullish three-bar gap between the current bar and the selected candle when imbalance is enabled.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = on a confirmed bearish swing-structure break meeting the minimum-displacement filter, select the most extreme high in the preceding scan window, then use the nearest bullish candle at or before the configured slack from that extreme (or the extreme candle if none exists). The zone spans that candle’s high to low, extending its upper edge to the extreme high in auto mode; cap its height at the maximum ATR multiple and require its height to meet the minimum ATR multiple. Require a bearish three-bar gap between the current bar and the selected candle when imbalance is enabled.
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, close > the most recent unbroken confirmed swing pivot high AND maximum of (absolute close movement since the structure pivot, absolute distance from close to pivot price) / ATR >= minimum displacement; do not mark a same-direction BOS if fewer than the configured throttle bars have elapsed since the prior BOS. OR low > high two bars earlier AND low - high two bars earlier >= minimum gap-size ATR multiple. OR a bullish order-block zone is created.
Short: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, close < the most recent unbroken confirmed swing pivot low AND maximum of (absolute close movement since the structure pivot, absolute distance from close to pivot price) / ATR >= minimum displacement; do not mark a same-direction BOS if fewer than the configured throttle bars have elapsed since the prior BOS. OR high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier - high >= minimum gap-size ATR multiple. OR a bearish order-block zone is created.
