Calculation: Fast and slow Supertrend = standard ATR-based Supertrend; the fast core uses a shorter ATR lookback and smaller multiplier than the slow core. The active fast core is the fixed fast core unless the adaptive core is enabled; the adaptive core uses the fast ATR lookback with a volatility-regime-specific multiplier, where regimes are based on percentile rank of ATR divided by close.
Calculation: Flip cluster = the number of active fast-core direction changes in the configured rolling bar window.
Calculation: ADX win rate by regime = the percentage of consensus candidates whose direction times the close change over the configured outcome horizon is positive, calculated separately for ADX below and at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive and fixed win rates = the same direction-adjusted outcome rate, calculated separately for first-alignments from each fast core against the slow core.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Supertrend = Supertrend direction on a timeframe above the chart timeframe, read from its last completed bar.
Long: Fast and slow Supertrend are up, and they were not both up on the previous bar, AND flip cluster is below its suppression threshold, AND (ADX gate is disabled, or ADX is not below its threshold, or the measured ADX win rate at/above the threshold exceeds the below-threshold win rate by the configured margin with sufficient samples), AND (strict higher-timeframe gate is disabled or higher-timeframe Supertrend is up).
Short: Fast and slow Supertrend are down, and they were not both down on the previous bar, AND flip cluster is below its suppression threshold, AND (ADX gate is disabled, or ADX is not below its threshold, or the measured ADX win rate at/above the threshold exceeds the below-threshold win rate by the configured margin with sufficient samples), AND (strict higher-timeframe gate is disabled or higher-timeframe Supertrend is down).
Exit: long, the selected trailing-stop Supertrend changes from up to down OR (fixed-stop mode and low <= entry price minus the configured ATR stop distance) OR high >= any configured ATR take-profit target above entry.
Exit: short, the selected trailing-stop Supertrend changes from down to up OR (fixed-stop mode and high >= entry price plus the configured ATR stop distance) OR low <= any configured ATR take-profit target below entry.
