Calculation: Fast and slow cores = Supertrends with respectively shorter and longer ATR lengths and smaller and larger ATR multipliers. The fixed fast core uses a constant multiplier. The adaptive fast core uses the fast ATR length and a multiplier selected by the percentile rank of ATR divided by close over a regime lookback: use the low, middle, or high regime multiplier according to two ordered percentile thresholds. Recalculate the Supertrend trailing bands using the current bar's selected multiplier.
Calculation: Core candidate = first bar on which a particular fast core and the slow core are both up or both down, compared with that same fast core's consensus on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Active fast core = fixed or adaptive by selection; in measured mode, use adaptive when both cores' independent candidate streams have at least a minimum number of resolved candidates and adaptive win rate minus fixed win rate is at least an improvement threshold, otherwise use fixed. Resolve each candidate after a fixed number of chart bars; it wins when candidate direction multiplied by the change in close since the candidate is greater than zero. Include all candidates, and select the active core using statistics available at the end of the preceding bar.
Long: The active fast core and slow core are both up AND they were not both up on the preceding bar in that fast core's own candidate stream.
Short: The active fast core and slow core are both down AND they were not both down on the preceding bar in that fast core's own candidate stream.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + a selected take-profit multiplier × fast ATR at entry.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= entry price - a selected take-profit multiplier × fast ATR at entry.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, the selected slow or active fast core flips down; for a fixed stop, low <= entry price - stop multiplier × fast ATR at entry OR the slow core flips down.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, the selected slow or active fast core flips up; for a fixed stop, high >= entry price + stop multiplier × fast ATR at entry OR the slow core flips up.
