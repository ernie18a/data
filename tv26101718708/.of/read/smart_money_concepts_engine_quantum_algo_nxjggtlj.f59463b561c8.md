Calculation: Structure levels and direction = For each of two symmetric pivot lengths, one longer for swing structure and one shorter for internal structure, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low. Reset each level's broken flag when a new pivot of that kind is confirmed. On a closed bar, the first close above an unbroken high sets its direction bullish and marks that high broken; the first close below an unbroken low sets its direction bearish and marks that low broken. Initialize direction as neutral. Process high breaks before low breaks.
Calculation: Swing range = The latest confirmed swing pivot high and low, valid only when the high is greater than the low; its midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Order block = On a swing structure break, create a demand block for a bullish break or a supply block for a bearish break, using the high and low of the most recent opposite-bodied candle within the origin lookback. Retain only the most recent configured number of blocks. A block starts fresh; on each closed bar, delete a demand block if close is below its low or a supply block if close is above its high. Otherwise, the first low at or below a demand block's high, or first high at or above a supply block's low, makes it tested, whether or not a tap decision occurs. Process newly created blocks on the creation bar.
Calculation: Equal liquidity level = When consecutive confirmed swing highs differ by no more than a specified multiple of ATR at confirmation, create a high level at their maximum; similarly create a low level at the minimum of consecutive swing lows within that tolerance. Retain only the most recent configured number of levels. A high level becomes inactive after a closed bar with high above and close below it, or close above it; a low level becomes inactive after a closed bar with low below and close above it, or close below it.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On a closed bar, a bullish gap exists when low exceeds the high two bars earlier and the difference is at least a specified multiple of ATR; its bounds are those two prices. A bearish gap exists when high is below the low two bars earlier and the difference is at least the same ATR multiple; its bounds are those two prices. Retain the most recent configured number of gaps. A fresh bullish gap becomes bearish on the first close below its lower bound; a fresh bearish gap becomes bullish on the first close above its upper bound. Remove an inverted bearish gap on a close above its upper bound and an inverted bullish gap on a close below its lower bound.
Long: Close > the latest unbroken swing or internal pivot high.
Short: Close < the latest unbroken swing or internal pivot low.
Long: Swing or internal structure direction = bullish.
Short: Swing or internal structure direction = bearish.
Long: Swing range is valid AND close < its midpoint.
Short: Swing range is valid AND close >= its midpoint.
Long: Low < the latest unbroken and unswept swing pivot low AND close > that low AND bars since the last single-pivot sweep of either side >= the cooldown.
Short: High > the latest unbroken and unswept swing pivot high AND close < that high AND bars since the last single-pivot sweep of either side >= the cooldown.
Long: Low < an active equal low AND close > that level.
Short: High > an active equal high AND close < that level.
Long: A fresh demand block exists AND close >= its low AND low <= its high AND swing structure direction = bullish AND internal structure direction = bullish AND swing range is valid AND close < its midpoint.
Short: A fresh supply block exists AND close <= its high AND high >= its low AND swing structure direction = bearish AND internal structure direction = bearish AND swing range is valid AND close > its midpoint.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is created OR close > the upper bound of a fresh bearish fair value gap.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is created OR close < the lower bound of a fresh bullish fair value gap.
