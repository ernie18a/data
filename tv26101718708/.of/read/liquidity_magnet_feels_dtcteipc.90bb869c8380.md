Calculation: Active liquidity zones = confirmed swing highs and lows, each requiring the lookback number of bars on both sides; merge same-direction pivots within a percentage tolerance, retaining the highest price for highs and lowest for lows. Remove a zone when price crosses it, it exceeds the maximum age, or its absolute log distance from the close exceeds the distance limit.
Calculation: Liquidity-zone score = strength weight × strength + proximity weight × proximity + age weight × age score + momentum weight × momentum score. Strength is pivot-bar volume divided by the 50-bar volume average, capped at 3, multiplied by an equal-zone bonus when merged. Proximity is 0.3 below 0.002 log distance; from 0.002 to 0.05 it is the greater of 0.5 and 1 − 10 × |log distance − 0.02|; above 0.05 it is the greater of 0.05 and 1 − 3 × (log distance − 0.05). Age score is 0.5 below the minimum age, 1 below 50 bars, and thereafter the greater of 0.1 and 1 − (age − 50)/450. Momentum score is 1 plus the directional alignment of (8-bar EMA − 21-bar EMA)/close × 100, capped between −0.5 and 0.5; alignment is positive when momentum points toward the zone and negative when it points away. Floor the total score at 0.01.
Calculation: Magnet pull = the share of total liquidity-zone score belonging to the side of the highest-scoring active zone, multiplied by +1 if that zone is a swing high or −1 if it is a swing low.
Long: magnet pull > 40
Short: magnet pull < −40
