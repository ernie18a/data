Calculation: Structure pivots = For each independent lookback, maintain a leg state initialized bearish. On each chart bar, if the high lookback bars ago is greater than every high in the subsequent lookback bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low lookback bars ago is less than every low in the subsequent lookback bars, set it bullish. When the state changes to bearish, record that earlier high as the latest high pivot; when it changes to bullish, record that earlier low as the latest low pivot. Each new pivot is initially uncrossed. Use a shorter lookback for internal structure and a longer lookback for swing structure; use a separate lookback for equal highs and lows.
Calculation: Structure bias = For each of internal and swing structure independently, initialize bias neutral. Set bias bullish when close crosses above its latest uncrossed high pivot, and bearish when close crosses below its latest uncrossed low pivot; mark that pivot crossed. An internal break additionally requires its pivot to differ from the corresponding swing pivot. Optionally require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for an internal bullish break, or high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low) for an internal bearish break. A crossover requires current close > current pivot and previous close <= previous pivot; a crossunder requires current close < current pivot and previous close >= previous pivot.
Calculation: Gap threshold = Optionally zero; otherwise twice the cumulative sum, over chart bars, of the absolute previous gap-timeframe candle change (previous close - previous open) / (previous open * 100), counted only on the first chart bar of each new gap-timeframe bar, divided by the number of elapsed chart bars, with a minimum divisor of one. The gap timeframe can be the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. On a higher timeframe, use the still-forming bar for its current high and low, and completed bars for prior values.
Long: Close crosses above an uncrossed swing high pivot.
Long: Close crosses above an uncrossed internal high pivot AND the internal high pivot differs from the swing high pivot AND the optional internal bullish filter passes.
Long: Swing structure bias = bullish OR internal structure bias = bullish.
Long: A new swing low pivot is confirmed.
Long: A new equal-low pivot is confirmed AND the absolute difference from the preceding equal-low pivot < a multiple of current ATR.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its current low > the high two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous gap-timeframe close > that high AND the previous gap-timeframe candle change > the gap threshold.
Short: Close crosses below an uncrossed swing low pivot.
Short: Close crosses below an uncrossed internal low pivot AND the internal low pivot differs from the swing low pivot AND the optional internal bearish filter passes.
Short: Swing structure bias = bearish OR internal structure bias = bearish.
Short: A new swing high pivot is confirmed.
Short: A new equal-high pivot is confirmed AND the absolute difference from the preceding equal-high pivot < a multiple of current ATR.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its current high < the low two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous gap-timeframe close < that low AND the negative of the previous gap-timeframe candle change > the gap threshold.
