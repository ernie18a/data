Calculation: Use completed chart bars. Let typical price be (high + low + close) / 3; candle range be high − low, floored by a positive price increment; volume ratio be volume divided by its moving average, clipped to a nonnegative range; normalized impulse be the change in typical price divided by ATR; close location be (2 × close − high − low) / range; body pressure be (close − open) / range; spread quality be absolute body pressure; upper and lower wick ratios be their respective wick lengths divided by range; wick balance be lower wick ratio − upper wick ratio. Clip ratios and intermediate scores to their stated natural signed or unsigned ranges. Treat missing previous values as current values.
Calculation: Money flow = EMA of centered, rescaled MFI of typical price, falling back to RSI and then its midpoint when unavailable. Trend bias = EMA of ATR-normalized difference between a shorter and a longer EMA of typical price. Volume pressure and aggressive pressure = separate EMAs of weighted sums of close location, body pressure and wick balance, each multiplied by the square root of volume ratio with a positive floor. Price ratio = EMA of normalized impulse divided by volume ratio with a positive floor. Clip each raw signed input before smoothing.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = EMA of an adaptive sensitivity times a weighted sum of lower wick ratio, positive shifted close location and positive excess of absolute volume pressure over absolute impulse, when volume ratio exceeds a threshold, lower wick ratio exceeds upper wick ratio and close location exceeds a negative threshold; otherwise its raw input is zero. Bearish absorption mirrors this using upper wick and negative close location. Sensitivity increases with volume ratio and is adjusted by spread quality. Absorption bias = bullish absorption − bearish absorption. Pressure quality = weighted sum of absolute aggressive pressure, excess volume ratio, spread quality, absolute close location and the larger absorption. Pressure control = short EMA of a weighted blend of shorter and longer EMAs of a weighted sum of aggressive pressure, volume pressure, money flow, trend bias and absorption bias. Pressure confidence = weighted sum of absolute pressure control and pressure quality. Pressure momentum = change in pressure control.
Calculation: For any series, slope means EMA of its one-bar change. Money-flow continuation is a weighted sum of money flow, its slope and the slope of that slope; pressure continuation is a weighted sum of pressure control, its slope, close location and body pressure; trend continuation is a weighted sum of trend bias and its slope; price continuation is a weighted sum of price ratio, smoothed impulse and the slope of smoothed impulse. Bullish preparation combines positive pressure slope, pressure control, absorption bias, money-flow slope and acceleration, trend bias and slope, and price continuation; bearish preparation uses the corresponding negative magnitudes. Core direction is an adaptively pressure-weighted sum of the four continuation scores, with the remaining weight shared among money flow, trend and price. Flow is a short EMA of a weighted blend of shorter and longer EMAs of a weighted sum of core direction and the difference between bullish and bearish preparation.
Calculation: Persistent run = EMA of a weighted sum of ATR-normalized distances between typical price and a long EMA, between a medium and the long EMA, and between a short and the medium EMA. Positioning = EMA of a volume-adjusted weighted sum of close location, body pressure and wick balance. Long participation is a weighted sum of positive positioning, positive pressure control, positive money flow and bullish absorption; short participation uses negative magnitudes and bearish absorption. Long and short crowding are the square roots of the products of the corresponding participation and positive or negative persistent run. Up-fade and down-fade are weighted sums of negative or positive slopes of pressure control, money flow and smoothed impulse. Bearish rotation score combines long crowding, up-fade, bearish absorption, upper-wick rejection and weak bullish close; bullish rotation score mirrors it. Blend each score first with its adaptive anchored-VWAP mean-reversion score, based on ATR-normalized distance and slopes plus a reclaim or loss crossing, and then with its adaptive RSI score, based on RSI being beyond its directional threshold and directional RSI slope and acceleration. VWAP anchor, RSI length, thresholds and base weights vary with chart timeframe. Price rotation = EMA of bullish rotation score − bearish rotation score.
Calculation: Directional result = weighted sum of normalized impulse, close location, body pressure and wick balance. Directional effort = weighted sum of pressure control, volume pressure, money flow, positioning and trend bias. Effort-result gap = positive excess of absolute effort over absolute result; efficiency = absolute result divided by absolute effort with a positive denominator floor. Clean continuation = weighted sum of directional effort, directional result and price continuation. Failure bias rewards negative effort with insufficient negative result and penalizes positive effort with insufficient positive result, scaled by the gap, corresponding wick and adverse close location. Efficient bias = clean continuation scaled by efficiency. Price effort = scaled and clipped short EMA of a weighted blend of shorter and longer EMAs of a weighted sum of flow, pressure control, price rotation, efficient bias and failure bias; divide by a longer EMA of its absolute core with a positive floor. Effort signal = EMA of price effort; effort momentum = price effort − effort signal; displayed effort and displayed signal are separately smoothed. Result score = weighted sum of absolute impulse, spread quality and absolute close location.
Calculation: Regime bias = longer EMA of shorter EMA of a weighted sum of price effort, pressure control, clipped effort momentum, money flow, trend bias, impulse and close location multiplied by spread quality. No-useful-read is true when any of these holds: very low volume ratio, weak result and small absolute regime bias; low volume ratio, weak result, small absolute regime bias and small absolute effort momentum; high volume ratio, weak result, small absolute regime bias and low pressure confidence; very high volume ratio, small absolute impulse, small spread quality, small absolute close location, small absolute regime bias and small absolute absorption bias; high volume ratio, large maximum wick ratio, small spread quality, small absolute close location, small absolute regime bias and small absolute absorption bias; strong pressure control and oppositely signed impulse with small absolute regime bias; or absolute regime bias below a volume- and confidence-adjusted dead band together with small absolute effort momentum, small absolute trend bias, weak result and low pressure confidence.
Calculation: Bullish pressure allowance means pressure control exceeds a negative volume- and quality-adjusted threshold OR bullish absorption exceeds a volume-adjusted threshold. Bearish allowance mirrors it. Bullish pressure confirmation means pressure control exceeds a positive volume- and quality-adjusted threshold OR bullish absorption exceeds a higher volume-adjusted threshold OR pressure momentum exceeds a positive threshold; bearish confirmation mirrors it. Bullish setup means no-useful-read is false AND bullish pressure allowance AND regime bias exceeds a positive entry threshold AND displayed effort ≥ displayed signal AND money flow exceeds a negative threshold. Bearish setup mirrors it. Bullish confirmation means bullish setup AND bullish pressure confirmation AND (previous bar had bullish setup OR regime bias exceeds a higher positive flip threshold OR pressure control exceeds a positive threshold); bearish confirmation mirrors it.
Calculation: Effort state is initially neutral. On each completed bar, a bullish state changes to bearish if bearish confirmation and regime bias is below the negative flip threshold; otherwise it becomes neutral if no-useful-read and regime bias is below a small positive threshold, or if regime bias is below a negative threshold AND displayed effort < displayed signal AND pressure control is below a small positive threshold. A bearish state changes to bullish if bullish confirmation and regime bias is above the positive flip threshold; otherwise it becomes neutral if no-useful-read and regime bias is above a small negative threshold, or if regime bias is above a positive threshold AND displayed effort > displayed signal AND pressure control is above a small negative threshold. From neutral, choose bullish on bullish confirmation, otherwise bearish on bearish confirmation, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Top premium means price effort or displayed effort exceeds a positive stretch threshold OR money flow is in its positive prime zone OR price rotation is below a negative threshold; bottom premium mirrors it. Active volatility means volume ratio, absolute impulse or the larger wick ratio exceeds its respective threshold. Bearish rotation evidence means pressure control, money flow, price rotation or effort momentum falls from the preceding bar OR bearish absorption exceeds a threshold; bullish evidence mirrors it. Top absorption means bearish absorption exceeds a threshold OR volume ratio and upper wick ratio exceed thresholds AND close location and spread quality are below thresholds; bottom absorption mirrors it. A top failed push means high > previous high AND close location is below a threshold AND close < open; a bottom failed push mirrors it. A top wick hunt means high reaches the prior swing high over either of two lookbacks AND upper wick ratio exceeds a threshold and a multiple of lower wick ratio AND close location is below a threshold; a bottom wick hunt mirrors it.
Calculation: A top money-flow zone hit means money flow crosses above its positive prime threshold OR reaches its higher positive extreme threshold; a bottom hit mirrors it. Each exhaustion and reversal direction has its own cooldown measured from its last confirmed occurrence.
Calculation: A divergence compares consecutive simultaneous price and money-flow pivots of the same side, confirmed after a right-hand swing lookback; update the stored previous pivot pair at every such confirmation, including pairs that do not produce a signal. Pivot spacing must be between minimum and maximum lookbacks, and a direction-specific cooldown is measured between signaled pivot bars. At a low pivot, price drop is the previous low minus current low divided by ATR at the current pivot; oscillator lift is current minus previous pivot money flow. At a high pivot, price push and oscillator drop mirror these. Divergence score is the sum of clipped price-move and oscillator-change scores, a fixed context contribution and a clipped recovery score combining directional absorption, directional pressure-control change and directional price rotation, all evaluated at the pivot.

Calculation: Positive pressure momentum means (low reaches the lowest prior low over a swing lookback AND falling context AND high participation AND bullish counter-wick) OR (the same swing and context AND stalled high-volume move AND lower wick ratio > upper wick ratio AND close location > 0) OR bullish absorption exceeds a threshold, AND none of the mirrored negative conditions holds. Falling context means negative trend bias beyond a threshold OR negative price effort beyond a threshold OR typical price is falling over a lookback. High participation means volume ratio exceeds a threshold. A bullish counter-wick means lower wick ratio exceeds a threshold and a multiple of upper wick ratio AND close location exceeds a positive threshold. A stalled high-volume move means volume ratio exceeds a higher threshold AND absolute impulse and spread quality are below thresholds. Negative pressure momentum mirrors these comparisons using a prior swing high and rising context.
