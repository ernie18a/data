Calculation: Features = RSI of close, volume divided by its moving average with missing ratio replaced by a neutral value, close Z-score using its moving average and standard deviation with a positive denominator floor, and ADX using the same period for directional movement and smoothing.
Calculation: Directional models = separate Arbor gradient-boosting classifiers and regressors for long and short, trained with the same configurable number of rounds and learning rate. At each scheduled retraining bar after the training lookback, fit on valid feature vectors from the oldest training-lookback bar through the bar one more than the forward-window length ago; require a minimum sample count. For a feature bar, calculate long excursion as the highest high from that bar through the bar immediately before the end of its forward window minus its close, divided by its ATR; calculate short excursion as its close minus the lowest low over the same bars, divided by its ATR. The classifier target is whether the corresponding excursion exceeds an ATR multiple; the regressor target is the excursion floored at zero. Retain the most recently trained models between retraining bars. Scores and predicted excursions are the respective model predictions for the current features; floor missing or negative predicted excursions at zero. Require all four models and valid features for predictions. ATR has a positive floor.
Calculation: Trade levels = at each signal, replace any tracked trade and set entry to the signal close. Initial long stop is the lower of signal low minus an ATR buffer and entry minus a minimum ATR distance; initial short stop is the higher of signal high plus an ATR buffer and entry plus a minimum ATR distance. Risk is the distance from entry to initial stop. The first target is entry plus risk times its target multiple for long, or minus that amount for short. The final target is entry plus, for long, or minus, for short, the greater of predicted directional excursion times signal-bar ATR and risk times a final-target floor. From the bar after entry, if the first target is touched and break-even is enabled, move the stop to entry before checking the stop on that bar. Check the final target before the stop; a new signal replaces the tracked trade before either check.
Long: Long score is available AND short score is available AND long score >= short score.
Short: Long score is available AND short score is available AND short score > long score.
Long: Low < lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close > midpoint of current high and low AND long score >= score threshold AND long score minus short score >= minimum edge AND ADX >= lower regime threshold AND ADX <= upper regime threshold AND bars since the last signal >= cooldown, or no prior signal exists AND either CCI confirmation is disabled or the lowest CCI of typical price over its recent lookback <= negative CCI threshold AND either the EMA filter is disabled or with-trend mode is selected and close > EMA of close or counter-trend mode is selected and close < EMA of close AND either the volume filter is disabled or relative volume >= its threshold.
Short: High > highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close < midpoint of current high and low AND short score >= score threshold AND short score minus long score >= minimum edge AND ADX >= lower regime threshold AND ADX <= upper regime threshold AND bars since the last signal >= cooldown, or no prior signal exists AND either CCI confirmation is disabled or the highest CCI of typical price over its recent lookback >= positive CCI threshold AND either the EMA filter is disabled or with-trend mode is selected and close < EMA of close or counter-trend mode is selected and close > EMA of close AND either the volume filter is disabled or relative volume >= its threshold.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND high >= final target OR bar is after the entry bar AND low <= current stop.
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND low <= final target OR bar is after the entry bar AND high >= current stop.
