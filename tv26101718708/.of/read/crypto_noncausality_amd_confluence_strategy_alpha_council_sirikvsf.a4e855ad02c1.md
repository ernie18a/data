Calculation: Hurst proxy = 1 - log10(sum of true range over the lookback / max(highest high minus lowest low over the lookback, epsilon)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Laguerre RSI = CU / (CU + CD), or zero when the denominator is zero, where L0 = (1 - gamma) × close + gamma × previous L0; L1 = -gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1; L2 = -gamma × L1 + previous L1 + gamma × previous L2; L3 = -gamma × L2 + previous L2 + gamma × previous L3; CU is the sum of positive adjacent differences L0 - L1, L1 - L2, and L2 - L3; CD is the sum of their absolute negative differences. Initialize previous levels to zero.
Calculation: Bull score = indicator(RSI > midpoint) + indicator(positive directional index > negative directional index AND ADX > trend threshold) + indicator(close > open AND volume > 0). Bear score = indicator(RSI < midpoint) + indicator(negative directional index > positive directional index AND ADX > trend threshold) + indicator(close < open AND volume > 0).
Calculation: Compression ratio = ATR / SMA of that ATR over a longer lookback, or one when that SMA is not positive.
Calculation: MAMA and FAMA = the MESA adaptive moving averages of the high-low midpoint, initialized to zero, using alpha = max(slow limit, fast limit / max(1, previous Hilbert phase - current Hilbert phase)); update MAMA = alpha × midpoint + (1 - alpha) × previous MAMA and FAMA = 0.5 × alpha × MAMA + (1 - 0.5 × alpha) × previous FAMA. Calculate phase in degrees as atan(Q1 / I1), retaining its previous value when I1 is zero. Smooth midpoint with weights 4, 3, 2, 1 divided by 10. For each of the smoothed midpoint, its detrender, I1, and Q1, the Hilbert transform is (0.0962 × current + 0.5769 × value 2 bars ago - 0.5769 × value 4 bars ago - 0.0962 × value 6 bars ago) × (0.075 × previous period + 0.54). The first two transforms give detrender and Q1; I1 is detrender 3 bars ago; the other two transforms give jI and jQ. Smooth I1 - jQ and Q1 + jI with weights 0.2 current and 0.8 previous to obtain I2 and Q2. Smooth I2 × previous I2 + Q2 × previous Q2 and I2 × previous Q2 - Q2 × previous I2 with the same weights to obtain Re and Im. When Re and Im are both nonzero, set period to 360 / degrees(atan(Im / Re)); otherwise retain its previous value. Clamp period first to 0.67–1.5 times its previous value, then to 6–50, and smooth it with weights 0.2 current and 0.8 previous. Treat unavailable historical values as zero.
Long: Laguerre RSI < lower threshold.
Short: Laguerre RSI > upper threshold.
Long: MAMA > FAMA.
Short: MAMA <= FAMA.
Long: Hurst proxy >= stationarity threshold AND close > SMA of close.
Short: Hurst proxy >= stationarity threshold AND close <= SMA of close.
Long: HMM enabled = true AND (compression ratio >= compression threshold OR Hurst proxy >= compression Hurst threshold) AND Hurst proxy > trend Hurst threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND bull score > bear score AND bull score >= minimum score.
Short: HMM enabled = true AND (compression ratio >= compression threshold OR Hurst proxy >= compression Hurst threshold) AND Hurst proxy > trend Hurst threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND bear score > bull score AND bear score >= minimum score.
Exit: long, (close - entry price) / entry price >= profit threshold AND close < EMA of close.
Exit: short, (entry price - close) / entry price >= profit threshold AND close > EMA of close.
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 - catastrophic stop fraction) OR close <= entry price × (1 - logical stop fraction).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + catastrophic stop fraction) OR close >= entry price × (1 + logical stop fraction).
