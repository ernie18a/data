Calculation: Hull moving average = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half length) − WMA(close, full length), square-root length), with half and square-root lengths rounded to the nearest integer and each at least 2 bars.
Calculation: Normalized slope = 100 × (Hull moving average − previous Hull moving average) / ATR; use 1% of close when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Squeeze = ATR / SMA(ATR, volatility lookback) < squeeze threshold; use a ratio of 1 when the ATR average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Trend state = initially 0; on each bar set to 0 during a squeeze, otherwise set to 1 if normalized slope > positive slope threshold AND (previous state != −1 OR the previous bar was in a squeeze), otherwise set to −1 if normalized slope < negative slope threshold AND (previous state != 1 OR the previous bar was in a squeeze), otherwise set to 0 if slope is between the thresholds inclusive; otherwise retain the previous state. The negative threshold is the negative of the positive threshold.
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum oscillator = sum((close − previous close) × volume ratio, momentum lookback), normalized to 100 × (sum − rolling minimum sum) / (rolling maximum sum − rolling minimum sum) over a longer normalization lookback; use 50 when the range is unavailable or nonpositive. Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, momentum lookback), or 1 when that average is unavailable or nonpositive. Smooth the normalized series recursively: output = c1 × (current normalized value + previous normalized value, replacing unavailable previous value with 0) / 2 + c2 × previous output + c3 × output two bars ago, replacing unavailable outputs with 0; a = exp(−sqrt(2) × pi / smoothing length), c2 = 2 × a × cos(sqrt(2) × pi / smoothing length), c3 = −a², c1 = 1 − c2 − c3.
Calculation: Overbought = oscillator > fixed upper threshold when fixed zones are enabled OR oscillator > SMA(oscillator, zone lookback) + zone multiplier × standard deviation(oscillator, zone lookback) when dynamic zones are enabled. Oversold = oscillator < fixed lower threshold when fixed zones are enabled OR oscillator < SMA(oscillator, zone lookback) − zone multiplier × standard deviation(oscillator, zone lookback) when dynamic zones are enabled. Fixed, dynamic, or both zone types can be enabled.
Calculation: Bullish zone cross = oscillator crosses above the enabled fixed lower threshold OR crosses above the enabled dynamic lower threshold. Bearish zone cross = oscillator crosses below the enabled fixed upper threshold OR crosses below the enabled dynamic upper threshold. A cross above requires current oscillator > current threshold AND previous oscillator <= previous threshold; a cross below requires current oscillator < current threshold AND previous oscillator >= previous threshold.
Long: Trend state = 1.
Short: Trend state = −1.
Long: Volume-weighted momentum oscillator > 50.
Short: Volume-weighted momentum oscillator <= 50.
Long: Previous bar was in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND normalized slope > positive slope threshold.
Short: Previous bar was in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND normalized slope < negative slope threshold.
Long: Oversold is true AND previous oversold is false.
Short: Overbought is true AND previous overbought is false.
Long: Bullish zone cross is true.
Short: Bearish zone cross is true.
Long: (Trend state = 1 OR previous bar was in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND normalized slope > positive slope threshold) AND (bullish zone cross is true OR oscillator > 50 AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND overbought is false) AND close > Hull moving average AND bars since the most recent confluence long or short decision > cooldown; if no confluence decision has occurred, the cooldown condition is true.
Short: (Trend state = −1 OR previous bar was in a squeeze AND current bar is not in a squeeze AND normalized slope < negative slope threshold) AND (bearish zone cross is true OR oscillator < 50 AND oscillator < previous oscillator AND oversold is false) AND close < Hull moving average AND bars since the most recent confluence long or short decision > cooldown; if no confluence decision has occurred, the cooldown condition is true.
