Calculation: Initial long stop = either the signal bar low adjusted downward by the configured stop percentage or the entry price adjusted downward by that percentage
Calculation: Initial short stop = either the signal bar high adjusted upward by the configured stop percentage or the entry price adjusted upward by that percentage
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the tighter of the initial stop and either the configured WMA of bar lows adjusted downward by its buffer, or the highest close since entry adjusted downward by the configured trail percentage
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the tighter of the initial stop and either the configured WMA of bar highs adjusted upward by its buffer, or the lowest close since entry adjusted upward by the configured trail percentage

Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= any configured profit target
