Calculation: Previous-session high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe session, using the selected regular-session, exchange-day, or UTC-day boundaries. For a regular session, accumulate only eligible New York trading-day bars between the configured open and session end, using the early session end on early-close days; update the levels at the next eligible open.
Calculation: Outer high and low = the most recent confirmed pivot high above the previous-session high and pivot low below the previous-session low, respectively, whose pivot bars precede the previous session; alternatively use the most recent individual bar high or low satisfying the same conditions. A pivot has the same configured number of chart bars on each side and is available only after confirmation. Use all loaded chart history.
Calculation: Large candle = absolute close-to-open body greater than or equal to a configured multiple of the average absolute body over a lookback of preceding chart bars; the test is always true when the multiple is nonpositive.
Calculation: Long anchor = outer low if the pattern's first candle low reaches it within the configured percentage tolerance; otherwise the previous-session low if reached within that tolerance. Short anchor = outer high if the first candle high reaches it within tolerance; otherwise the previous-session high if reached within tolerance.
Calculation: Setup prices = proposed long entry is the signal candle high plus a configured price increment, and proposed short entry is its low minus that increment. The stop is a configured percentage beyond the selected anchor, first-candle extreme, or pattern extreme; the pattern extreme includes the first candle, intervening candles, and signal candle. The target is a configured percentage before the previous-session opposite extreme or outer opposite level, falling back to the previous-session extreme if the outer level is unavailable, or a configured multiple of entry-to-stop risk beyond entry. For the open-session variant, round stop and target to the configured price increment. Reward-to-risk is target distance from entry divided by entry distance from stop.
Long: In the open-session variant, the bar starts at the configured New York open on an eligible weekday, the optional holiday filter passes, both previous-session extremes exist, the candle is large and closes below its open, its low reaches the previous-session low within tolerance, and, if the optional reclaim filter is enabled, its close is above the long anchor.
Short: In the open-session variant, the bar starts at the configured New York open on an eligible weekday, the optional holiday filter passes, both previous-session extremes exist, the candle is large and closes above its open, its high reaches the previous-session high within tolerance, and, if the optional reclaim filter is enabled, its close is below the short anchor.
Long: In the open-session variant, a qualifying long open candle occurred within the configured subsequent-bar window and in the same selected session, no earlier candle in that window qualified as the signal candle, no candle since the open candle has reached a precomputed anchor-based or first-candle-based stop when cancellation is enabled, the current close is above its open, the current low is at least the minimum low since the open candle if the optional extreme-hold filter is enabled, proposed entry is above stop, target is above entry, and reward-to-risk is at least the configured minimum.
Short: In the open-session variant, a qualifying short open candle occurred within the configured subsequent-bar window and in the same selected session, no earlier candle in that window qualified as the signal candle, no candle since the open candle has reached a precomputed anchor-based or first-candle-based stop when cancellation is enabled, the current close is below its open, the current high is at most the maximum high since the open candle if the optional extreme-hold filter is enabled, stop is above proposed entry, entry is above target, and reward-to-risk is at least the configured minimum.
Long: In the any-time variant, the preceding and current chart bars are in the same selected session, the preceding candle is large and closes below its open, its low reaches the long anchor within tolerance, the previous-session high exists, its close is above the anchor if the optional reclaim filter is enabled, the current close is above its open and above the anchor, the current low is at least the preceding low if the optional extreme-hold filter is enabled, proposed entry is above stop, target is above entry, and reward-to-risk is at least the configured minimum.
Short: In the any-time variant, the preceding and current chart bars are in the same selected session, the preceding candle is large and closes above its open, its high reaches the short anchor within tolerance, the previous-session low exists, its close is below the anchor if the optional reclaim filter is enabled, the current close is below its open and below the anchor, the current high is at most the preceding high if the optional extreme-hold filter is enabled, stop is above proposed entry, entry is above target, and reward-to-risk is at least the configured minimum.
Long: Close is below the previous-session high adjusted downward by the configured tolerance AND close is at or below the previous-session low adjusted upward by that tolerance.
Short: Close is at or above the previous-session high adjusted downward by the configured tolerance.
Exit: long, low is at or below the setup stop OR high is at or above the setup target OR the open-session variant's optional time exit is enabled and the configured New York exit time is reached.
Exit: short, high is at or above the setup stop OR low is at or below the setup target OR the open-session variant's optional time exit is enabled and the configured New York exit time is reached.
