Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the prior regular session or prior exchange day, according to the selected version and day setting; use completed bars.
Calculation: Outer high/low = the nearest confirmed swing high above the previous-day high / swing low below the previous-day low, found before the previous day; a swing uses equal left and right bar counts. If the first-bar setting is selected, use the nearest earlier bar high/low beyond the corresponding previous-day level instead.
Calculation: Lower/upper line = outer low/high when candle 1 reaches it within the selected tolerance; otherwise the previous-day low/high when candle 1 reaches that level within tolerance.
Calculation: Large candle = its body is at least the selected multiple of the average body over the selected lookback of preceding chart bars.
Calculation: Long stop = selected stop reference (lower line, candle 1 extreme, or pattern extreme) minus the selected percentage offset. Short stop = selected stop reference plus the selected percentage offset.
Calculation: Long target = selected opposite line (nearest or far), adjusted by the selected offset, or entry price plus selected risk multiple times entry-to-stop distance. Short target = corresponding opposite line adjusted by the selected offset, or entry price minus selected risk multiple times stop-to-entry distance.
Long: (Doug version: first New York open bar of a permitted trading day is bearish and large and reaches the lower line AND, if reclaim is enabled, closes above that line; then within the selected next-bar window a bullish bar occurs AND, if hold is enabled, its low is no lower than the lowest low since candle 1 AND candle 1's stop has not been hit first) OR (any-time version: previous bar is bearish and large, reaches the lower line, and, if reclaim is enabled, closes above it AND the current strictly next bar is bullish, closes above the lower line, and, if hold is enabled, its low is no lower than the previous bar's low) AND entry price above stop AND target above entry AND reward/risk meets the selected minimum.
Short: (Doug version: first New York open bar of a permitted trading day is bullish and large and reaches the upper line AND, if reclaim is enabled, closes below that line; then within the selected next-bar window a bearish bar occurs AND, if hold is enabled, its high is no higher than the highest high since candle 1 AND candle 1's stop has not been hit first) OR (any-time version: previous bar is bullish and large, reaches the upper line, and, if reclaim is enabled, closes below it AND the current strictly next bar is bearish, closes below the upper line, and, if hold is enabled, its high is no higher than the previous bar's high) AND entry price below stop AND target below entry AND reward/risk meets the selected minimum.
Exit: long, price reaches long stop or long target; Doug version also exits at the configured New York end-of-day time, or earlier on an early-close day.
Exit: short, price reaches short stop or short target; Doug version also exits at the configured New York end-of-day time, or earlier on an early-close day.
