Calculation: Fast and slow trends = independently selected ALMA, DEMA, Ehlers deviation-scaled moving average, EMA, HMA, Jurik moving average, KAMA, linear regression, RMA, SMA, SMMA, price source, TEMA, TMA, volatility-adjusted moving average, VIDYA, variable moving average, VWMA, WMA, Wilder's moving average, or zero-lag EMA, calculated on the selected chart-bar price source; the fast trend uses the shorter trend length and the slow trend uses the longer trend length.
Calculation: Volatility-adjusted moving average = EMA(source, trend length) + (highest deviation + lowest deviation) / 2, where deviation = source − EMA(source, trend length), and both extremes use the same volatility lookback.
Calculation: Zero-lag EMA = 2 × EMA(source, trend length) − EMA(EMA(source, trend length), trend length).
Calculation: Variable moving average = recursively smooth positive and negative source changes with weight 1 / trend length; divide each by their sum and smooth each ratio with the same weight; smooth the absolute difference of the resulting ratios divided by their sum with the same weight to obtain an index; normalize the index between its lowest and highest values over the fast trend length; recursively smooth the source with weight normalized index / trend length. Initialize prior recursive values to zero; undefined divisions remain undefined.
Long: long trading enabled AND bar datetime is within the inclusive date range AND fast trend > slow trend.
Short: short trading enabled AND bar datetime is within the inclusive date range AND fast trend < slow trend.
Exit: long, bar datetime is outside the inclusive date range OR (long trading enabled AND bar datetime is within the inclusive date range AND fast trend < slow trend).
Exit: short, bar datetime is outside the inclusive date range OR (short trading enabled AND bar datetime is within the inclusive date range AND fast trend > slow trend).
