Calculation: Rails = the rolling highest high and lowest low over the same lookback, including the current bar. The trend starts neutral, changes to bullish when close crosses above the preceding bar's upper rail, changes to bearish when close crosses below the preceding bar's lower rail, and otherwise retains its prior state. A trend change occurs only when the trend reverses from bullish to bearish or vice versa.
Calculation: Equilibrium = normalize typical price within the current rails and clamp it to [0,1]; average its EMA and volume-weighted moving average over the same lookback, substituting its weighted moving average when the volume-weighted average is unavailable. Shift that average toward the midpoint by a parameter, clamp it between symmetric edge buffers, and map it back into the current rail range. If the rail range is zero, use the midpoint of the rails. Equilibrium slope is its one-bar change divided by the rail range, or zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Candle measures = body, upper wick, lower wick, and close position, each divided by high minus low. For a zero-range bar, use zero for body and wicks and the midpoint for close position. Channel position is close normalized within the current rails and clamped to [0,1], or the midpoint when the rail range is zero. Volume ratio and range ratio are current volume and candle range divided by their respective same-lookback moving averages; each ratio is unavailable when its average is not positive.
Calculation: Momentum states = take an EMA of close, subtract its value a momentum lookback ago, and EMA-smooth the difference using the same smoothing length. Gamma is that smoothed momentum minus its value a gamma lookback ago. For each of momentum and gamma, form a buffer as a parameter times an EMA of its absolute value, using the same buffer lookback for both. Initialize each up-state as false; when previously true it remains true only above the negative buffer, and when previously false it becomes true only above the positive buffer. The combined state is the pair of up-states. Chop is enabled AND either both absolute values are below their respective EMA absolute values times their respective thresholds OR the count of combined-state changes over a lookback reaches a threshold.
Calculation: Participation score = a neutral value if volume ratio is unavailable; otherwise clamp a scaled volume ratio to a wide interval when volume ratio reaches a stress threshold AND body fraction is below a threshold, OR range ratio reaches an expansion threshold; otherwise clamp a differently scaled volume ratio to a narrower interval.
Calculation: Reversal score = a weighted sum of directional close position, directional wick fraction, directional body pressure, participation score, momentum-state score, and directional rail score. For bearish pressure use one minus close position, upper wick, full body fraction on a bearish candle and a reduced multiple otherwise; for bullish pressure use close position, lower wick, full body fraction on a bullish candle and the same reduced multiple otherwise. Momentum-state scores are parameterized by the four ordered pairs of momentum and gamma up-states, with separate bullish and bearish mappings. The bearish rail score is a scaled and clamped excess of channel position over a threshold; the bullish rail score is a scaled and clamped shortfall below another threshold. Use separate component weights shared by both directions.
Calculation: Zone pressure = for each bar, calculate close location pressure as ((close-low)-(high-close))/(high-low), using zero for zero range; calculate progress as the close change divided by true range and clamped to [-1,1], using zero when unavailable or true range is zero. Directional bar pressure is their average when both have the same strict sign, otherwise zero. Divide the rolling sum of directional bar pressure times volume by rolling volume over the same lookback, using zero when volume sum is not positive, and EMA-smooth the result. Resistance attack is the positive part of smoothed pressure; support attack is the positive part of its negation. Attack slope is current attack minus attack a shorter slope lookback ago.
Calculation: Zones = on each completed chart bar with positive ATR, arm a resistance candidate when the upper rail first becomes available or rises, and a support candidate when the lower rail first becomes available or falls; replace the respective candidate with that rail value. Commit an armed candidate after close retreats from it by at least a parameter times current ATR, then disarm it. A resistance band extends inward below its anchor by a parameter times ATR; a support band extends inward above its anchor by the same width. Merge a new candidate into the last matching unbroken same-side zone within a parameter times ATR of its anchor, increasing touches and moving the anchor and band only if the candidate is farther outward; otherwise create a zone with one touch. Merge overlapping or sufficiently nearby same-side bands using an ATR-scaled gap, keeping the zone with more touches, or the farther-out anchor on a tie; sum touches and retain the greater flip count. On a close beyond the outer edge by a breakaway ATR multiple, reverse the zone's role until its flip limit is reached, then mark it broken. Otherwise, start testing when high reaches the resistance inner edge or low reaches the support inner edge; after the required consecutive closes beyond the inner edge on the respected side, return it to active and increment touches. Mark it broken after the required consecutive closes beyond its outer edge by a bypass ATR multiple. Remove broken zones after their fade period and retain only the newest permitted number of unbroken zones per side. A zone is relevant when its anchor is within a parameter times ATR of close, and interacting when the bar overlaps its band expanded by a proximity ATR multiple.
Calculation: Zone pressure trail = on the first interacting bar reset the zone's peak attack and stored direction and side. While interacting, update peak attack and, when attack slope exceeds the positive deadband or falls below the negative deadband, store respectively an up or down direction and the current zone side. Preserve these values after interaction ends and when a zone flips; when zones merge, retain the trail belonging to the larger peak attack. A live pressure marking requires a relevant unbroken zone, interaction, and absolute attack slope above the deadband. A trail marking requires a relevant unbroken zone, no eligible live marking, enabled trails, peak attack at least its threshold, and a stored direction. Apply the selected bullish/bearish side filter to the current side for live markings and the stored side for trails.
Long: close >= open.
Short: close < open.
Long: trend is bearish AND no trend change occurred on this bar AND close <= equilibrium AND channel position <= the lower-rail proximity threshold AND (equilibrium rises while its preceding value is at least its value two bars ago OR equilibrium slope >= a negative slope threshold OR lower wick fraction >= its rejection threshold OR (close > open AND body fraction >= its rejection threshold) OR close position >= its rejection threshold) AND bullish reversal score >= its threshold AND the shared warning cooldown has elapsed AND chop is false.
Short: trend is bullish AND no trend change occurred on this bar AND close >= equilibrium AND channel position >= the upper-rail proximity threshold AND (equilibrium falls while its preceding value is at most its value two bars ago OR equilibrium slope <= a positive slope threshold OR upper wick fraction >= its rejection threshold OR (close < open AND body fraction >= its rejection threshold) OR close position <= its rejection threshold) AND bearish reversal score >= its threshold AND the shared warning cooldown has elapsed AND chop is false.
Calculation: Shared warning cooldown = the current bar index minus the index of the most recent bullish or bearish warning; it is ready when no warning has occurred or that difference is at least its threshold. Update the index whenever either warning occurs.
Long: an eligible live pressure marking has resistance as its current zone side OR an eligible trail marking has resistance as its stored side.
Short: an eligible live pressure marking has support as its current zone side OR an eligible trail marking has support as its stored side.
