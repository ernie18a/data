Calculation: Robust z-score of x = (x - rolling median of x) / (MAD scale factor × rolling median of the series abs(x - its contemporaneous rolling median) + epsilon), using the same long lookback for both medians.
Calculation: Relative effort = raw effort divided by its baseline, bounded between zero and a maximum. Raw effort is volume when volume is available and its short rolling mean is positive, otherwise true range. On intraday bars, use the previously stored EMA of raw effort for the same minute of the exchange day once that minute has enough samples; otherwise use the global EMA of raw effort. Update the minute EMA after calculating relative effort.
Calculation: Gravity measures = let r be log(close / previous close), or zero if either close is nonpositive; let signed move be log(close / close at the gravity lookback), path be the sum of abs(r) over that lookback, expected move be the rolling standard deviation of r over a separate lookback times the square root of the gravity lookback, displacement = abs(signed move) / (expected move + epsilon), and path efficiency = bound(abs(signed move) / (path + epsilon), zero, one). Effort is the gravity-lookback SMA of relative effort. Close location is bound((2 × close - high - low) / (high - low), -1, 1), or zero for a zero-range bar. Positive and negative effort are gravity-lookback SMAs of relative effort times max(close location, zero) and max(-close location, zero), respectively; effort imbalance = (positive effort - negative effort) / (positive effort + negative effort + epsilon). Upward and downward displacement are the gravity-lookback sums of max(r, zero) and max(-r, zero), respectively, divided by expected move + epsilon. Inefficiency = max(1 - path efficiency, epsilon). Raw gravity = log(1 + effort) - log(displacement + epsilon) + inefficiency weight × log(inefficiency + epsilon); gravity is its short EMA. Directional gravity spread is the difference between the same raw-gravity formula applied to positive effort and upward displacement and that applied to negative effort and downward displacement. Gravity acceleration is the change in the change in gravity. Compute robust z-scores of gravity, its one-bar change, effort, and displacement using the same long lookback.
Calculation: Downside release score = the count of these nine conditions: signed move < zero; path efficiency >= a lower efficiency threshold; path efficiency > its previous value; gravity-change z-score < zero; displacement z-score > its previous value; effort z-score > a low threshold; effort imbalance < zero; directional gravity spread > zero; and either a prior bar within a release lookback has gravity z-score > a high threshold or has effort z-score > that high threshold AND displacement z-score < the low threshold AND gravity-change z-score > a positive threshold AND path efficiency < a low efficiency threshold, OR gravity acceleration <= zero. The lower efficiency threshold is below the high efficiency threshold. All gravity measures and their z-scores must be available before evaluating the direct short.
Calculation: Adaptive structure = KAMA of close with a longer efficiency lookback than the separate structure-efficiency lookback and faster and slower smoothing lengths. Structure efficiency = abs(close - close at its lookback) / sum(abs(change in close), its lookback), or zero if the denominator is zero. Normalized slope = change in KAMA over a slope lookback / ATR, or zero if ATR is nonpositive. Corridor bands = KAMA ± ATR × (base width + noise expansion × (1 - bound(structure efficiency, zero, one))). Bull raw = close > KAMA AND normalized slope >= positive slope threshold AND structure efficiency >= minimum efficiency; bear raw = close < KAMA AND normalized slope <= negative slope threshold AND structure efficiency >= minimum efficiency. Initialize regime to neutral. From neutral set it to bull or bear when the respective raw condition holds. Retain bull until close is below the lower band for the required consecutive bars, then set bear if bear raw holds, otherwise neutral. Retain bear until close is above the upper band for the required consecutive bars, then set bull if bull raw holds, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Wedge = lines through the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows, projected to the current bar and to the later of the two older pivot bars. Pivot confirmation requires a specified number of bars on each side. Start width and current width are upper minus lower line at those respective bars; age is bars since the later older pivot. A falling wedge requires both newer pivots below their older counterparts AND upper slope < lower slope < zero; a rising wedge requires both newer pivots above their older counterparts AND lower slope > upper slope > zero. Both require age <= maximum age AND current width > minimum price increment AND start width > zero AND current width <= start width × tightness fraction. A wedge matures when its condition has held for exactly the required consecutive bars; use the current bar's latest confirmed pivots on every bar.
Calculation: Cup candidate = regime is neutral or bull AND cup depth >= ATR × minimum depth multiplier AND close >= cup rim - cup depth × rim tolerance AND handle depth fraction >= zero AND handle depth fraction <= maximum handle depth AND handle high >= cup rim - cup depth × rim tolerance. Cup rim is the highest high over the longer cup lookback ending on the preceding bar; cup base is the lowest low over that lookback including the current bar; cup depth = rim - base. Handle high is the highest high over the shorter handle lookback ending on the preceding bar; handle low is the lowest low over that lookback including the current bar; handle depth fraction = (cup rim - handle low) / cup depth when cup depth exceeds the minimum price increment, otherwise a large sentinel value.
Long: Falling wedge matures.
Short: Rising wedge matures.

Long: On the chart timeframe, or on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, close > long-term SMA of close AND close crosses above short-term EMA of close AND volume / its SMA >= volume threshold AND this combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar. The higher-timeframe calculation is disabled when the chart timeframe is higher than its selected timeframe.
Calculation: Managed trade levels = for the primary long or direct short only, enter at signal-bar close when flat, with long taking priority if both occur. Freeze distances at entry: either use entry-bar ATR times a stop multiplier with target and lock distances expressed as configurable multiples of that stop distance, or use configurable fixed price distances. The first target is nearer than the final target; the lock is in profit. Begin checking on the bar after entry. Once the first target is reached without the initial stop being hit on that bar, activate the profit-lock stop starting on the following bar. Check the active stop before targets on every bar; close the trade at the final target or holding-time limit. Intermediate targets do not close the trade.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= active stop OR high >= entry price + final target distance OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= active stop OR low <= entry price - final target distance OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars).
