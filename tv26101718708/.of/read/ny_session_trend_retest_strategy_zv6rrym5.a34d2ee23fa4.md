Calculation: Trading-session VWAP = cumulative sum of source price times volume divided by cumulative volume, starting at the first trading-session bar; undefined outside the session. Source price is configurable, defaulting to (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Premarket high and low = highest high and lowest low respectively during the configured premarket session so far, reset at the start of each local calendar day.
Calculation: Trading-day high and low = highest high and lowest low respectively during the configured trading session so far, including the current bar, reset at the start of each local calendar day.
Calculation: Body percentage above a level = 0 if the level is undefined or close <= the level; otherwise, 100 times max(0, max(open, close) - max(min(open, close), level)) divided by abs(close - open), or 100 when open = close.
Calculation: Body percentage below a level = 0 if the level is undefined or close >= the level; otherwise, 100 times max(0, min(max(open, close), level) - min(open, close)) divided by abs(close - open), or 100 when open = close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes; historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while realtime chart bars use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = current bar is in the trading session AND position size = 0 AND (cooldown is disabled OR no trade has closed OR elapsed time since the first flat bar after the last position closed >= cooldown duration) AND (regime filter is disabled OR (absolute VWAP change over its slope lookback >= minimum slope distance AND highest high minus lowest low over the range lookback >= minimum range distance)).
Long: Entry eligibility AND close > higher-timeframe trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > trading-session VWAP) AND (entry EMA filter disabled OR close > entry EMA) AND ((VWAP retest enabled AND low <= trading-session VWAP + retest buffer AND close > trading-session VWAP) OR (premarket breakout enabled AND premarket high and low are defined AND ((close mode selected AND body percentage above premarket high >= breakout threshold) OR (retest mode selected AND low <= premarket high + retest buffer AND close > premarket high))) OR (EMA retest enabled AND low <= entry EMA + retest buffer AND close > entry EMA)).
Short: Entry eligibility AND close <= higher-timeframe trend EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < trading-session VWAP) AND (entry EMA filter disabled OR close < entry EMA) AND ((VWAP retest enabled AND high >= trading-session VWAP - retest buffer AND close < trading-session VWAP) OR (premarket breakout enabled AND premarket high and low are defined AND ((close mode selected AND body percentage below premarket low >= breakout threshold) OR (retest mode selected AND high >= premarket low - retest buffer AND close < premarket low))) OR (EMA retest enabled AND high >= entry EMA - retest buffer AND close < entry EMA)).
Calculation: Long VWAP-exit override = enabled when a new long position is first observed and its average entry price < that bar's exit EMA; remains enabled through the first bar with close > exit EMA AND close > average entry price + minimum profit distance, and is disabled on subsequent bars. Reset when flat.
Calculation: Short VWAP-exit override = enabled when a new short position is first observed and its average entry price > that bar's exit EMA; remains enabled through the first bar with close < exit EMA AND close < average entry price - minimum profit distance, and is disabled on subsequent bars. Reset when flat.
Exit: long, (long VWAP-exit override enabled AND body percentage below trading-session VWAP >= VWAP exit threshold) OR (long VWAP-exit override disabled AND body percentage below exit EMA >= EMA exit threshold).
Exit: short, (short VWAP-exit override enabled AND body percentage above trading-session VWAP >= VWAP exit threshold) OR (short VWAP-exit override disabled AND body percentage above exit EMA >= EMA exit threshold).
Exit: long, low <= lowest low over the stop lookback ending on the entry-signal bar minus stop buffer; submit as a stop order while the position is open.
Exit: short, high >= highest high over the stop lookback ending on the entry-signal bar plus stop buffer; submit as a stop order while the position is open.
Exit: long, premarket partial enabled AND premarket high is defined AND high >= premarket high AND entry-signal close < premarket high; submit a configured partial-size limit order at premarket high, and if breakeven is enabled submit a stop order at entry-signal close.
Exit: short, premarket partial enabled AND premarket low is defined AND low <= premarket low AND entry-signal close > premarket low; submit a configured partial-size limit order at premarket low.
Exit: long, trading-day partial enabled AND trading-day high is defined AND high >= trading-day high; submit a configured partial-size limit order at trading-day high.
Exit: short, trading-day partial enabled AND trading-day low is defined AND low <= trading-day low; submit a configured partial-size limit order at trading-day low, and if breakeven is enabled submit a stop order at entry-signal close.
Exit: long, current bar is outside the trading session.
Exit: short, current bar is outside the trading session.
