Calculation: Primary magnet and touch count = Over the current bar and a scan lookback, divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high into equal-width bins. For each bar, increment every bin from floor((low - range low) / bin width) through floor((high - range low) / bin width), inclusive, clamping both indices to the valid bin range. The touch count is the largest bin count; the primary magnet is the midpoint of the lowest-index bin with that count.
Calculation: Magnet score = round(min(min(touch count * 6, 40) + min(max((volume / SMA of volume - 1) * 40, 0), 25) + 35, 100)) for a qualifying upward or downward break.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = Current bar index minus the most recent long or short signal bar index; initialize the most recent signal bar index to zero and update it only when a signal with the signal score threshold and cooldown fires.
