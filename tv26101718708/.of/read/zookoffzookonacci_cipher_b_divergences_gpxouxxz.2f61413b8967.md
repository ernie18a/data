Calculation: WaveTrend = for the selected price source, subtract its channel-length EMA and divide by 0.015 times the channel-length EMA of the absolute deviation; fast WaveTrend is the average-length EMA of that result, and slow WaveTrend is an SMA of fast WaveTrend.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA of multiplier × (close − open) / (high − low), minus an offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of the stochastic normalization of RSI of the selected source, optionally logarithmically transformed; optionally average that SMA with its own smoothing SMA.
Calculation: Confirmed fractal = an oscillator value strictly above or below each of the two values on either side, detected two bars after the pivot. For a limited fractal, the pivot must also be at or beyond its corresponding upper or lower threshold. Compare each pivot with the most recent qualifying same-side pivot whose confirmation occurred at least two bars earlier, using the high or low at each oscillator pivot.
Calculation: Classic bullish divergence = a confirmed bottom fractal with a lower price low and higher oscillator value; classic hidden bullish divergence = a confirmed bottom fractal with a higher price low and lower oscillator value. Classic bearish divergence = a confirmed top fractal with a higher price high and lower oscillator value; classic hidden bearish divergence = a confirmed top fractal with a lower price high and higher oscillator value. Apply the selected upper and lower thresholds to both pivots when limits are enabled; hidden divergence may instead use unlimited fractals.
Calculation: Realtime RSI divergence = scan the most recent bounded number of confirmed close-price pivots within a maximum age and beyond a minimum separation. Compare RSI at the pivot with RSI at the current bar, or at the previous bar when confirmation is required. For a low-pivot scan, bullish regular divergence requires endpoint close below pivot close and endpoint RSI above pivot RSI; bullish hidden requires endpoint close above pivot close and endpoint RSI below pivot RSI. For a high-pivot scan, bearish regular requires endpoint close above pivot close and endpoint RSI below pivot RSI; bearish hidden requires endpoint close below pivot close and endpoint RSI above pivot RSI. Every intermediate close and RSI value must be on or above its respective endpoint-to-pivot line for a low-pivot scan, or on or below it for a high-pivot scan. When confirmation is required, a low-pivot scan additionally requires current RSI > previous RSI OR current close > previous close; a high-pivot scan requires current RSI < previous RSI OR current close < previous close.
