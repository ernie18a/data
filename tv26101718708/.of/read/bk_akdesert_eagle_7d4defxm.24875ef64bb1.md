Calculation: Structural trend = Divide chart bars into consecutive equal-length blocks ending at the selected calculation bar. For each block, calculate its highest high, lowest low, geometric midpoint sqrt(highest high × lowest low), and center bar. Starting with the newest two blocks, extend backward while successive geometric midpoints move strictly in the same direction. Calculate the angle as atan((log(newest midpoint) − log(oldest midpoint)) / (Yang-Zhang volatility × difference in center bar indices)). The trend is up when the newest midpoint is higher and the angle exceeds the positive range threshold, down when the newest midpoint is lower and the angle is below the negative range threshold, and flat otherwise. Compute this once for the primary block length and for an ordered set of shorter-to-longer block lengths; the fast group contains the shorter half and the slow group the longer half. Calculations can use either the current chart bar or the previous chart bar consistently.
Calculation: Structural channel = Within the primary trend's monotonic block segment, find the highest and lowest block highs and the highest and lowest block lows, retaining their center bar indices. For a non-flat trend, project a straight line in log-price space through the two block-high extremes to the calculation bar for the upper rail, and through the two block-low extremes for the lower rail; if either pair has the same center, use its first endpoint. For a flat trend, use the highest block high and lowest block low as horizontal rails.
Calculation: Channel state = Start inside. If the primary trend changes after a non-flat trend, reset inside and make no other transition on that bar. Otherwise, from inside, move to breakout up when close is above the upper rail or breakout down when close is below the lower rail. During breakout up, remember if absolute log(close/upper rail) divided by Yang-Zhang volatility reaches the distance threshold; switch to breakout down if close falls below the lower rail, inside if close returns to or below the upper rail, or retest up if the remembered threshold was reached and the distance falls below it while close remains above the upper rail. During retest up, switch to breakout down below the lower rail, inside at or below the upper rail, or breakout up when the distance reaches the threshold again. Apply the symmetric rules to breakout down and retest down. When rails are invalid or upper rail is not above lower rail, channel position is inside.
Calculation: Compression ratio = (log(upper rail/lower rail) / Yang-Zhang volatility) divided by its simple moving average over a compression lookback.
Calculation: Fast and slow trend net = For each group of block lengths, sum +1 for each uptrend, −1 for each downtrend, and 0 for each flat trend.
Long: Primary structural trend = up.
Short: Primary structural trend = down.
Long: Any block-length structural trend = up.
Short: Any block-length structural trend = down.
Long: Channel state = breakout up OR channel state = retest up.
Short: Channel state = breakout down OR channel state = retest down.
Long: Fast trend net > 0 AND slow trend net < 0 AND the shared cascade-event freshness and cooldown conditions pass.
Short: Fast trend net < 0 AND slow trend net > 0 AND the shared cascade-event freshness and cooldown conditions pass.
Long: Previous compression ratio < compression threshold AND current compression ratio > expansion threshold AND primary structural trend = up AND fast trend net > 0 AND the shared release-event freshness and cooldown conditions pass.
Short: Previous compression ratio < compression threshold AND current compression ratio > expansion threshold AND primary structural trend = down AND fast trend net < 0 AND the shared release-event freshness and cooldown conditions pass.
Calculation: Event freshness and cooldown = For each event class separately, optionally require neither direction's raw event condition on the previous bar, and require the configured number of bars since the last qualifying event of either direction in that class; update the last-event bar whenever those conditions pass.
