Calculation: Bar notation = Subscript 0 denotes the current completed chart bar; subscripts 1 and 2 denote preceding bars. Body B = abs(close - open); range R = high - low; upper wick U = high - max(open, close); lower wick D = min(open, close) - low. ATR is calculated on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Swings = A confirmed swing high or low is a pivot with equal-length left and right lookbacks. H1 and H2 are the latest and preceding confirmed swing highs; L1 and L2 are the latest and preceding confirmed swing lows. Update swings before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Structure breaks = Up break: close0 > H1 + ATR0 × penetration AND close1 <= H1 + ATR0 × penetration. Down break: close0 < L1 - ATR0 × penetration AND close1 >= L1 - ATR0 × penetration. Structure starts neutral, changes to up on an up break and down on a down break; use the updated structure for candle classification.
Calculation: Directional patterns = Evaluate the following conditions on each completed bar. Parameters denoted by different threshold names are independently configurable. Engulfing up: close0 > open0 AND close1 < open1 AND close0 >= open1 AND open0 <= close1 AND B0 > B1; engulfing down reverses all price inequalities. Piercing: close0 > open0 AND R0 > 0 AND close1 < open1 AND open0 < close1 AND close0 > (open1 + close1)/2 AND close0 < open1 AND B0 > B1 × body threshold; dark cloud reverses the price inequalities. Bullish harami: max(open0, close0) < max(open1, close1) AND min(open0, close0) > min(open1, close1) AND open1 > close1 AND B0 < B1 × harami threshold; bearish harami replaces open1 > close1 with open1 < close1. Bullish belt: close0 > open0 AND R0 > 0 AND open0 - low0 <= R0 × opening-wick threshold AND B0 >= R0 × belt-body threshold AND U0 <= R0 × opposite-wick threshold; bearish belt: close0 < open0 AND R0 > 0 AND high0 - open0 <= R0 × opening-wick threshold AND B0 >= R0 × belt-body threshold AND D0 <= R0 × opposite-wick threshold. Bullish marubozu: close0 > open0 AND R0 > 0 AND B0 >= R0 × marubozu threshold; bearish marubozu replaces close0 > open0 with close0 < open0. Lower-wick pattern: R0 > 0 AND B0 > 0 AND D0 >= B0 × long-wick threshold AND U0 <= B0 × short-wick threshold; upper-wick pattern swaps D0 and U0. Tweezer bottom: abs(low0 - low1) <= ATR0 × tweezer threshold AND close0 > open0 AND close1 < open1; tweezer top uses highs and reverses the candle directions.
Calculation: Three-bar patterns = Soldiers: close0 > open0 AND close1 > open1 AND close2 > open2 AND close0 > close1 AND close1 > close2 AND open0 > open1 AND open0 < close1 AND open1 > open2 AND open1 < close2 AND U0 <= B0 × three-bar wick threshold; crows reverse all price inequalities and use D0. Morning star: close2 < open2 AND B2 > ATR0 × first-body threshold AND B1 <= B2 × middle-body threshold AND close0 > open0 AND close0 > (open2 + close2)/2; evening star reverses the candle-direction and midpoint inequalities.
Calculation: Structural patterns = Low sweep: low0 < L1 AND close0 > L1 AND close1 > L1; high sweep: high0 > H1 AND close0 < H1 AND close1 < H1. Double bottom: the bar of H1 is strictly between the bars of L2 and L1 AND abs(L1 - L2) <= ATR0 × double-pattern tolerance AND close0 > H1 AND close1 <= H1. Double top: the bar of L1 is strictly between the bars of H2 and H1 AND abs(H1 - H2) <= ATR0 × double-pattern tolerance AND close0 < L1 AND close1 >= L1. Ascending triangle: abs(H1 - H2) <= ATR0 × triangle tolerance AND L1 > L2. Descending triangle: abs(L1 - L2) <= ATR0 × triangle tolerance AND H1 < H2.
Calculation: Selected pattern = Select the first true condition in this order: up break with previous structure down; down break with previous structure up; low sweep; high sweep; up break with previous structure neutral or up; down break with previous structure neutral or down; double top; double bottom; morning star; evening star; soldiers; crows; engulfing up; engulfing down; piercing; dark cloud; bullish harami; bearish harami; bullish belt; bearish belt; bullish marubozu; bearish marubozu; tweezer top; tweezer bottom; lower-wick pattern; upper-wick pattern; outside bar; spinning top; inside bar; doji; ascending triangle; descending triangle; low-volatility range. Outside bar means high0 > high1 AND low0 < low1. Spinning top means R0 > 0 AND B0 > R0 × doji threshold AND B0 <= R0 × spinning-body threshold AND U0 >= B0 × spinning-wick threshold AND D0 >= B0 × spinning-wick threshold. Inside bar means high0 < high1 AND low0 > low1. Doji means R0 > 0 AND B0 <= R0 × doji threshold. Low-volatility range means ATR0 < SMA(ATR)0 × low-volatility threshold AND H1 - L1 < ATR0 × range threshold. Missing required swing values make a condition false.
Calculation: Order blocks = On an up break with previous structure neutral or up, replace the bullish block with the high and low of the L1 pivot bar if that bar is within the maximum lookback. On a down break with previous structure neutral or down, replace the bearish block with the high and low of the H1 pivot bar under the same limit. A bullish block is broken when close is below its low; a bearish block is broken when close is above its high. Each bar re-evaluates the broken condition, and a subsequent qualifying break replaces the respective block.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = A bullish gap forms when low0 > high2, with boundaries high2 and low0; a bearish gap forms when high0 < low2, with boundaries high0 and low2. Retain only the most recent configurable number of gaps across both directions. A bullish gap becomes filled when a subsequent or current low <= its lower boundary; a bearish gap becomes filled when a subsequent or current high >= its upper boundary. Filled gaps remain filled until discarded.

Short: Selected pattern = down break OR high sweep OR double top OR evening star OR crows OR engulfing down OR dark cloud OR bearish harami OR bearish belt OR bearish marubozu OR tweezer top OR descending triangle.

Short: Selected pattern = lower-wick pattern AND updated structure = up.
Long: Selected pattern = upper-wick pattern AND updated structure = down.
Short: Selected pattern = upper-wick pattern AND updated structure >= neutral.
Long: Selected pattern = outside bar AND close0 > open0.
Short: Selected pattern = outside bar AND close0 <= open0.
Long: Bullish order block exists AND close0 >= its low.
Short: Bearish order block exists AND close0 <= its high.
Short: Bullish order block exists AND close0 < its low.
Long: Bearish order block exists AND close0 > its high.
Long: At least one retained bullish fair value gap is unfilled.
Short: At least one retained bearish fair value gap is unfilled.
Long: close0 < (H1 + L1)/2 - (H1 - L1) × equilibrium threshold.
Short: close0 > (H1 + L1)/2 + (H1 - L1) × equilibrium threshold.
