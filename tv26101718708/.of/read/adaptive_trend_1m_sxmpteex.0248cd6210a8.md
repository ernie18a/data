Calculation: Market speed = absolute close rate of change divided by its highest value over an adaptation lookback; efficiency = absolute close change over a longer lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero if the sum is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR period = the rounded, bounded base period multiplied by 0.2 + 0.8 × (1 − market speed); adaptive ATR is recursively smoothed true range with alpha = 1 / adaptive ATR period, initialized to true range. The SuperTrend multiplier is its base multiplier × (0.7 + 0.3 × market speed).
Calculation: Adaptive trend = initialize trend as up; calculate raw lower and upper bands as the selected price source minus and plus adaptive ATR × SuperTrend multiplier. If the previous close is above the previous lower band, set the lower band to the greater of its raw value and previous value; otherwise use its raw value. If the previous close is below the previous upper band, set the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and previous value; otherwise use its raw value. Change a downtrend to up when close exceeds the previous upper band; change an uptrend to down when close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend. The default price source is (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Adaptive MACD = recursively smoothed fast close EMA minus slow close EMA, with each EMA initialized to close and alpha = 2 / (adaptive length + 1). The fast length is the rounded, bounded base fast length × (0.5 + 0.5 × efficiency); the slow length is the rounded, bounded base slow length × (0.6 + 0.4 × (1 − efficiency)). The signal is a recursively smoothed EMA of MACD, initialized to MACD, with a fixed signal length. The base slow length is longer than the base fast length and also defines the efficiency lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive RSI = RSI computed from recursively smoothed positive and negative close changes, each initialized to its first available change component, with alpha = 1 / adaptive RSI length. The length is the rounded, bounded base RSI length × (0.3 + 0.7 × (1 − efficiency)); RSI is 100 when smoothed losses are zero.
Calculation: Adaptive Bollinger bands = close SMA plus or minus close standard deviation × base deviation × (1 + 0.2 × (1 − efficiency)).
Calculation: Entry volatility filter = adaptive ATR / close > 0.5 × Wilder-smoothed standard ATR / close, using fixed ATR and smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > volume SMA × base volume multiplier × (1 − 0.2 × market speed).
Calculation: Trade levels = at an entry signal, capture the position's average entry price available before the new entry fills and the current adaptive ATR and efficiency. The stop distance is captured ATR × base stop multiplier × (1.2 − 0.2 × captured efficiency); each of three progressively farther profit distances is captured ATR × its base profit multiplier × (0.8 + 0.2 × captured efficiency). Levels based on an unavailable captured price are unavailable. Profit orders close their specified fractions at the first two levels and the remaining reserved quantity at the third; the stop and profit orders are separately reserved exit orders.
Calculation: Post-profit state = when breakeven and profit exits are enabled, set the state after any position-size change from the size recorded at the entry signal or previous bar. The optional trailing stop is also conditional on breakeven being enabled: activate it when price reaches the Bollinger midline, then trail the best price reached by the current stop multiplier × captured ATR, rounded to the minimum price increment.
Long: Adaptive trend changes from down to up.
Long: Adaptive trend changes from down to up AND adaptive MACD crosses above its signal if cross mode is enabled, otherwise adaptive MACD > its signal AND adaptive RSI > 50 AND close crosses above the adaptive upper Bollinger band in breakout mode, or close > the Bollinger midline in midline mode, or no Bollinger restriction in none mode AND volume spike if the volume filter is enabled AND entry volatility filter.
Short: Adaptive trend changes from up to down AND adaptive MACD crosses below its signal if cross mode is enabled, otherwise adaptive MACD < its signal AND adaptive RSI < 50 AND close crosses below the adaptive lower Bollinger band in breakout mode, or close < the Bollinger midline in midline mode, or no Bollinger restriction in none mode AND volume spike if the volume filter is enabled AND entry volatility filter.
Exit: long, stop enabled AND low <= captured entry price − stop distance OR profits enabled AND high >= captured entry price + a profit distance OR breakeven enabled AND profits enabled AND post-profit state AND low <= captured entry price OR trailing enabled AND breakeven enabled AND profits enabled AND post-profit state AND low <= activated trailing stop.
Exit: short, stop enabled AND high >= captured entry price + stop distance OR profits enabled AND low <= captured entry price − a profit distance OR breakeven enabled AND profits enabled AND post-profit state AND high >= captured entry price OR trailing enabled AND breakeven enabled AND profits enabled AND post-profit state AND high >= activated trailing stop.
