Calculation: Equilibrium = (highest high + lowest low over a chart-bar lookback) / 2.
Calculation: Directional score = with macro features disabled, fit a Bernoulli naive Bayes model over a rolling chart-bar training window. For each training bar, classify the outcome as up if its close is greater than the close one outcome horizon earlier, otherwise down; associate that outcome with the features from the bar one horizon earlier. The binary features are: chart close > chart EMA AND chart close > chart open; shorter higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND close > open; chart close < equilibrium; chart close > trading-day-anchored VWAP of close; previous chart high > high two chart bars ago; chart volume > its SMA; chart volume > previous chart volume; and chart MFI > its midpoint. Use the last completed shorter higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time. For each feature, let U and D be the counts of up and down outcomes, and u and d the counts of those outcomes for which the associated feature was true. Set pU = (u + 1) / (U + 2) and pD = (d + 1) / (D + 2). The score is ln((U + 1) / (D + 1)) plus, for each feature, ln(pU / pD) if the current feature is true, otherwise ln((1 - pU) / (1 - pD)). Before the training window, outcome horizon and additional warm-up have elapsed, use score = 0.
Long: Chart close > chart EMA AND chart close > chart open AND chart close < equilibrium.
Short: Chart close < chart EMA AND chart close < chart open AND chart close > equilibrium.
Long: Shorter higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND close > open AND longer higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND close > open; use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe historically and its still-forming bar in real time.
Short: Shorter higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND close < open AND longer higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND close < open; use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe historically and its still-forming bar in real time.
Long: Directional score >= 0.
Short: Directional score < 0.
Long: Absolute chart candle body > ATR multiplied by a displacement threshold AND close > previous high AND close > open AND previous close <= previous open.
Short: Absolute chart candle body > ATR multiplied by a displacement threshold AND close < previous low AND close < open AND previous close >= previous open.
