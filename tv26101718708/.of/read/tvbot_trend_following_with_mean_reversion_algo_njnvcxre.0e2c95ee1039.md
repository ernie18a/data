Calculation: Source = a selectable OHLC price series.
Calculation: Crossover(a, b) = a > b AND previous a <= previous b; crossunder(a, b) = a < b AND previous a >= previous b.
Calculation: Trend mean = EMA of source; reversion mean = EMA of source with a longer lookback than the trend mean.
Calculation: Momentum signals = calculate independently for a VWMA of source and an HMA of source, each with its own lookback. Initialize the allowed direction to long and both pending flags to false. On each bar, first evaluate the long signal as pending long AND crossover of the moving average above the recorded long close AND allowed direction is long; evaluate the short signal as pending short AND crossunder of the moving average below the recorded short close AND allowed direction is short. Then, if close crosses above the moving average, set pending long, record the current close as the long close, and clear pending short; if close crosses below it, set pending short, record the current close as the short close, and clear pending long. After those updates, a short signal clears pending short and sets the allowed direction to long; a long signal clears pending long and sets the allowed direction to short. Evaluate recorded-close crossovers using the recorded-close series as it stood on each respective bar.
Calculation: Trend touch = initialize false. On each bar, reset to false if the previous position size equals zero AND current position size is nonzero, OR previous position size multiplied by current position size equals -1; otherwise set to true if high > trend mean multiplied by one plus a nonnegative percentage AND low < that upper level, OR high > trend mean multiplied by one minus that percentage AND low < that lower level, OR close < the upper level AND close > the lower level, OR high > trend mean AND low < trend mean. Otherwise retain the previous value. Afterward reset to false on either VWMA momentum signal. Entry and exit conditions use the previous bar's trend touch.
Long: VWMA long momentum signal AND source > trend mean AND previous trend touch is true AND datetime >= start datetime.
Long: HMA long momentum signal AND source < reversion mean multiplied by one minus a positive percentage AND datetime >= start datetime.
Short: VWMA short momentum signal AND source < trend mean AND previous trend touch is true AND datetime >= start datetime.
Short: HMA short momentum signal AND source > reversion mean multiplied by one plus the same positive percentage AND datetime >= start datetime.
Exit: long entered by the VWMA condition, VWMA short momentum signal OR (HMA short momentum signal AND source > reversion mean multiplied by one plus the reversion percentage).
Exit: short entered by the VWMA condition, VWMA long momentum signal OR (HMA long momentum signal AND source < reversion mean multiplied by one minus the reversion percentage).
Exit: long entered by the HMA condition, HMA short momentum signal OR (VWMA short momentum signal AND source < trend mean AND previous trend touch is true).
Exit: short entered by the HMA condition, HMA long momentum signal OR (VWMA long momentum signal AND source > trend mean AND previous trend touch is true).
