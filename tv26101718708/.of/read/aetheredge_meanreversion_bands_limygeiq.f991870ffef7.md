Calculation: Basis = an EMA or SMA of close; standard deviation = the population standard deviation of close over the same lookback, floored at a small positive constant; stretch = (close − basis) / standard deviation.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute difference between a shorter-lookback and a longer-lookback EMA of close divided by ATR, floored at a small positive constant. Regime = lower, middle, or upper tercile of the rolling percent rank of trend strength.
Calculation: Reversion features = [absolute stretch; trend strength; side × (RSI − RSI midpoint) / RSI midpoint; standard deviation / its longer-lookback SMA − 1; volume / its SMA − 1; side × (basis − basis a fixed number of bars earlier) / ATR; consecutive bars with absolute stretch at least an exhaustion threshold divided by an exhaustion scale]. Side is +1 when stretch > 0 and −1 otherwise. Floor denominators at a small positive constant and clamp each feature to its configured bounds.
Calculation: Reversion probability = sigmoid of an intercept plus the dot product of feature values and their weights, with the sigmoid input clamped to configured bounds. Initialize all weights, including the intercept, to zero. On each completed bar, first process outstanding touch setups in creation order: increment age and label a setup successful if stretch changes side or absolute stretch falls to or below a reversion threshold; otherwise label it unsuccessful if absolute stretch rises to or above its initial absolute stretch plus an extension threshold; otherwise, at the horizon, label it successful if absolute stretch is at most a configured fraction of its initial value and unsuccessful otherwise. For each resolved setup, update weights by logistic gradient descent using its saved features and binary label, then recompute its probability using the updated weights. Update the overall success rate and, for every threshold arm in the setup's saved regime, update its count and running mean reward with the label if the recomputed probability reaches that arm's threshold, otherwise with the updated overall success rate. Remove resolved setups. Then save the current features, side, absolute stretch and regime as a new setup when absolute stretch crosses up through the touch threshold; discard the oldest setup if the configured capacity is exceeded. All touches train the model, including those that do not generate an entry.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = the greater of a minimum probability and the selected probability threshold arm for the current regime. Select the arm with the highest running mean reward plus exploration coefficient × sqrt(log(regime resolved count + 1) / arm count); prioritize untried arms and break ties by arm order. Evaluate current reversion probability and the adaptive threshold after processing resolved setups on the current bar.
Long: bar index > warmup AND stretch < 0 AND absolute stretch >= touch threshold AND previous absolute stretch < touch threshold AND reversion probability >= adaptive threshold.
Short: bar index > warmup AND stretch > 0 AND absolute stretch >= touch threshold AND previous absolute stretch < touch threshold AND reversion probability >= adaptive threshold.
