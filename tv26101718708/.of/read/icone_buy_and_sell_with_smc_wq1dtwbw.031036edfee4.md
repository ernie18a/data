Calculation: Structure high and low = highest high and lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar; the preceding structure range uses the immediately preceding, nonoverlapping window of the same length.
Calculation: Valid compression = current structure range is less than the preceding structure range times a compression factor AND ATR is below its structure-lookback SMA AND structure range divided by ATR is within its lower and upper bounds AND the linear-regression slope of prior-bar highs is negative OR the linear-regression slope of prior-bar lows is positive. Apply the final OR within the preceding AND conditions.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA, or a neutral value when the SMA is not positive; candle body ratio = absolute close minus open divided by high minus low, subject to a minimum price increment.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = close compared with its EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; if the EMA is unavailable, both bullish and bearish tests pass. Multi-timeframe bullish count = number of selected timeframes whose last completed bar closes above its EMA; selected timeframes can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Directional breakout score = capped sum of parameterized points for price on the directional side of the long EMA, short EMA slope and long EMA slope in the direction, directional impulse, price-path efficiency above either of two thresholds, contracting structure range, ATR below its structure-lookback SMA, structure width within bounds, both regression slopes converging, valid compression, directional breakout, strong directional candle, close beyond the structure boundary by an ATR buffer, directional RSI filter passing, volume filter passing, higher-timeframe trend agreeing, a structure-boundary sweep and recovery, close on the directional side of the structure midpoint, and close within an ATR distance of that midpoint. Directional impulse compares close with the rolling low for long or rolling high for short over a longer impulse lookback than the structure lookback, requiring an ATR-scaled move and close on the directional side of the short EMA. Price-path efficiency is absolute close change over the impulse lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero when the sum is zero. A strong directional candle has a directional body, body ratio at least its threshold, and a close within a parameterized fraction of the candle range from the directional extreme. A disabled RSI or volume filter counts as passing.
Calculation: Approved directional breakout = previous bar has valid compression AND close crosses beyond the current structure high plus an ATR buffer for long or below the current structure low minus an ATR buffer for short AND directional breakout score is at least its threshold AND, when strict filtering is enabled, directional impulse, higher-timeframe trend and enabled long-EMA, short-EMA slope, volume, RSI and strong-candle filters all pass. The crossing compares the previous close with the previous bar's own buffered structure boundary.
Calculation: Pending retest = after an approved breakout, retain its structure boundary and bar index until an opposite approved breakout, an approved retest, expiry after a maximum number of bars, or a close beyond an ATR-scaled failure distance on the wrong side of the retained boundary. An approved retest occurs at least one bar after breakout and before expiry, when the directional extreme touches the retained boundary within its asymmetric ATR tolerances, close finishes on the breakout side, and the candle is directional; in strict mode the enabled long-EMA and short-EMA slope filters and higher-timeframe trend must also agree.
Calculation: Fakeout = previous bar has valid compression AND the current high exceeds structure high by an ATR buffer, closes below structure high and is bearish, or the current low falls below structure low by an ATR buffer, closes above structure low and is bullish; require a shared minimum bar separation from the last confirmed fakeout of either direction.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, select either an ATR-offset stop, a structure stop, or the farther of the two. The long structure stop is the lower of the recent rolling low and the retained long breakout level minus an ATR buffer, falling back to structure low when no level is retained; the short structure stop is the corresponding maximum of the recent rolling high and retained short breakout level plus an ATR buffer. Risk is entry-to-stop distance subject to a minimum price increment. Targets are entry plus, for long, or minus, for short, successively larger parameterized multiples of risk.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, ratcheted toward profit after the first target is touched to entry plus an ATR buffer for long or entry minus it for short when break-even is enabled, and after the second target is touched to close minus an ATR distance for long or close plus it for short when trailing is enabled; never move the stop away from profit.
Calculation: Guide warning = a new occurrence of an opposing fakeout, a close crossing the short EMA against the position, two consecutive volumes below the current volume SMA with a profitable close and an opposing candle, opposing-candle volume over a rolling window exceeding directional-candle volume by a factor, or a profitable close within an ATR distance of the nearest opposing structure boundary, last completed higher-timeframe period high or low, or active opposing liquidity pool. A liquidity pool comes from two successive confirmed pivot highs or lows within an ATR tolerance and remains active until its level is touched. For the structure component, use max(structure high minus close, zero) for long or max(close minus structure low, zero) for short. Reset the guide level when position side changes; a hard structure break sets it to the highest level, otherwise each bar with at least one newly occurring warning increments it once up to that level, optionally only after the first target has been touched.
Long: Approved directional breakout OR approved directional retest.
Short: Approved directional breakout OR approved directional retest.
Long: Enabled bullish trend filters pass AND higher-timeframe trend agrees AND low touched or crossed below the short EMA within the pullback lookback AND close is above that EMA AND close is above open AND close is above the previous high AND, when high quality is selected, RSI, candle body ratio, relative volume and multi-timeframe bullish count meet their bullish thresholds.
Short: Enabled bearish trend filters pass AND higher-timeframe trend agrees AND high touched or crossed above the short EMA within the pullback lookback AND close is below that EMA AND close is below open AND close is below the previous low AND, when high quality is selected, RSI, candle body ratio, relative volume and multi-timeframe bullish count meet their bearish thresholds.
Long: Downward fakeout.
Short: Upward fakeout.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot high exceeds the preceding pivot high OR a newly confirmed pivot low exceeds the preceding pivot low.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high is less than or equal to the preceding pivot high OR a newly confirmed pivot low is less than or equal to the preceding pivot low.
Long: Close breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high for the first time since that pivot AND the most recent bearish candle within the order-block lookback has a range no greater than an ATR multiple.
Short: Close breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low for the first time since that pivot AND the most recent bullish candle within the order-block lookback has a range no greater than an ATR multiple.
Long: Low exceeds the high of an earlier bar by at least an ATR-scaled gap threshold OR close crosses above the upper boundary of an existing bearish gap that has not previously been inverted.
Short: High is below the low of an earlier bar by at least an ATR-scaled gap threshold OR close crosses below the lower boundary of an existing bullish gap that has not previously been inverted.
Exit: long, low reaches the active stop OR high reaches an unfilled profit target.
Exit: short, high reaches the active stop OR low reaches an unfilled profit target.
Exit: long, close falls below structure low OR the guide level increases after a new warning.
Exit: short, close rises above structure high OR the guide level increases after a new warning.
