Calculation: Smoothed EMAs = EMA of each of the short-period and long-period close EMAs, using the same smoothing period for both.
Calculation: Long setup = the short-period EMA is above the long-period EMA AND the close is above both EMAs and both smoothed EMAs AND the most recent upward crossover occurred within the permitted signal window, counting the crossover bar AND that crossover has not already produced a valid long entry.
Calculation: Short setup = the short-period EMA is below the long-period EMA AND the close is below both EMAs and both smoothed EMAs AND the most recent downward crossover occurred within the permitted signal window, counting the crossover bar AND that crossover has not already produced a valid short entry.
Calculation: Initial stop = the low a configurable number of bars before the entry bar minus a buffer for a long, or the corresponding high plus a buffer for a short.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, each configured target distance in target order. A target is reached only when its price is touched and all preceding targets have been reached; process targets in order, stopping at the first unreached target.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, updated only in the favorable direction. If target trailing is enabled, after the first target is reached use entry price plus a favorable offset; after each subsequent target is reached use the preceding target price. If the optional breakeven trigger is reached and no earlier trailing or breakeven update has marked breakeven complete, mark it complete and use entry price plus a favorable offset if that improves the stop. Check the existing stop before processing targets, and process the breakeven trigger only after target and EMA exits; updated stops take effect on the next bar.
Long: Trading is flat at the signal bar close AND long setup is true; enter at the next bar's open if the initial stop is below that open.
Short: Trading is flat at the signal bar close AND short setup is true; enter at the next bar's open if the initial stop is above that open.
Exit: long, low is at or below the active stop OR the final target is reached OR the selected early-exit mode is enabled AND close is below the short-period EMA, or below both close EMAs, or below both close EMAs and both smoothed EMAs, according to the selected mode.
Exit: short, high is at or above the active stop OR the final target is reached OR the selected early-exit mode is enabled AND close is above the short-period EMA, or above both close EMAs, or above both close EMAs and both smoothed EMAs, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Exit processing = check the stop first, then the final target, then the selected EMA condition, including on the entry bar; execute at the stop, final target, or close respectively. After an exit, a new setup may be evaluated at that bar's close.
