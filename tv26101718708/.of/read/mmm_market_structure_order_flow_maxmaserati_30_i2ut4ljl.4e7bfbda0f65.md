Calculation: Bullish body-close candle = close > high of previous bar OR open > high of previous bar
Calculation: Bearish body-close candle = close < low of previous bar OR open < low of previous bar
Calculation: Synthetic delta = for volume > 0 and high > low: let body = close - open, range = high - low, upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low, close position = (close - low) / range. If body > 0, buy ratio = min(0.85, 0.50 + abs(body)/range * 0.25 + (lower wick > upper wick ? 0.10 : 0) + (close position > 0.7 ? 0.05 : 0)), delta = volume * (2 * buy ratio - 1). If body < 0, sell ratio = min(0.85, 0.50 + abs(body)/range * 0.25 + (upper wick > lower wick ? 0.10 : 0) + (close position < 0.3 ? 0.05 : 0)), delta = -volume * (2 * sell ratio - 1). If body = 0, delta = upper wick > 1.5 * lower wick ? -0.40 * volume : lower wick > 1.5 * upper wick ? 0.40 * volume : close position > 0.5 ? 0.04 * volume : -0.04 * volume. If volume = 0, delta = 0.
Calculation: Recent body-close range average = mean of the ranges of the two most recent bullish or bearish body-close candles before the current bar
Calculation: Recent body-close absolute-delta average = mean of the absolute synthetic deltas of the two most recent bullish or bearish body-close candles before the current bar
Calculation: Valid swing high = the highest high tracked since the last bullish body-close swing confirmation, confirmed on a bearish body-close bar; valid swing low = the lowest low tracked since the last bearish body-close swing confirmation, confirmed on a bullish body-close bar. Initially track both extremes; while tracking a high, update only the high, and while tracking a low, update only the low. Each confirmed swing is removed when price reaches or passes its level by wick.
Long: bullish body-close candle AND current range >= configurable range factor * recent body-close range average AND synthetic delta > 0 AND absolute synthetic delta >= configurable delta factor * recent body-close absolute-delta average
Short: bearish body-close candle AND current range >= configurable range factor * recent body-close range average AND synthetic delta < 0 AND absolute synthetic delta >= configurable delta factor * recent body-close absolute-delta average
Long: valid swing high exists AND valid swing low exists AND high >= valid swing high AND close > valid swing high AND prior structure-break direction is not long
Short: valid swing low exists AND valid swing high exists AND low <= valid swing low AND close < valid swing low AND prior structure-break direction is not short
