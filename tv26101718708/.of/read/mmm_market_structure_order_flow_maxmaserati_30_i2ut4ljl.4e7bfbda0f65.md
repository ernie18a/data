Calculation: Bullish body close = close > previous high OR open > previous high.
Calculation: Bearish body close = close < previous low OR open < previous low.
Calculation: Synthetic delta = 0 if volume <= 0; otherwise let range = high - low, body = close - open, upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low, and close position = (close - low) / range. If range > 0 and body > 0, delta = volume * (2 * min(0.85, 0.50 + 0.25 * abs(body) / range + 0.10 if lower wick > upper wick else 0 + 0.05 if close position > 0.7 else 0) - 1); if range > 0 and body < 0, delta = -volume * (2 * min(0.85, 0.50 + 0.25 * abs(body) / range + 0.10 if upper wick > lower wick else 0 + 0.05 if close position < 0.3 else 0) - 1); if range > 0 and body = 0, delta = -0.40 * volume if upper wick > 1.5 * lower wick, else 0.40 * volume if lower wick > 1.5 * upper wick, else volume * (0.04 if close position > 0.5 else -0.04). If range <= 0, delta = 0.20 * volume if body > 0, -0.20 * volume if body < 0, otherwise 0. Conditional additions inside min are individually parenthesized.
Calculation: Swing levels = initialize trend as neutral and tracked high and low from the first bar. On each confirmed bar, first update both extremes on strict new extremes if neutral, the tracked high on high >= tracked high if rising, or the tracked low on low <= tracked low if falling. If bearish body close AND trend is rising or neutral, add the tracked high to active swing highs, set trend falling, and reset tracked low to current low; otherwise, if bullish body close AND trend is falling or neutral, add the tracked low to active swing lows, set trend rising, and reset tracked high to current high. Process active swing lows newest first, removing each when low <= its price; then process active swing highs newest first, removing each when high >= its price. Process breaks before removing the touched level. Initialize break direction as neutral; set it to short or long on the corresponding structure break.
Long: (bullish body close AND NOT bearish body close) OR (bullish body close AND bearish body close AND close >= open).
Short: (bearish body close AND NOT bullish body close) OR (bullish body close AND bearish body close AND close < open).
Long: close >= open AND min(open, close) - low <= tick-size parameter AND high - low > tick-size parameter.
Short: close <= open AND high - max(open, close) <= tick-size parameter AND high - low > tick-size parameter.
Long: bullish body close AND average range of the two most recent preceding body-close bars > 0 AND current range >= range-expansion parameter * that average AND synthetic delta > 0 AND (average absolute synthetic delta of those two bars <= 0 OR synthetic delta >= delta-expansion parameter * that average). The initial missing body-close ranges and deltas are zero.
Short: bearish body close AND average range of the two most recent preceding body-close bars > 0 AND current range >= range-expansion parameter * that average AND synthetic delta < 0 AND (average absolute synthetic delta of those two bars <= 0 OR abs(synthetic delta) >= delta-expansion parameter * that average). The initial missing body-close ranges and deltas are zero.
Long: close > an active swing high AND break direction != long AND at least one active swing low exists.
Short: close < an active swing low AND break direction != short AND at least one active swing high exists.

Short: open < previous close AND previous low < high AND close < previous close AND open < previous open AND previous low > max(open, close).
Long: low > previous high.
Short: high < previous low.

Short: high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND high >= previous low AND previous high >= low two bars ago.
