Calculation: Source OHLC = optionally use standard Heikin-Ashi OHLC calculated from chart bars; otherwise use chart OHLC.
Calculation: Rectangle high and low = highest source high and lowest source low over the same configurable lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Accumulation bias = choose one of the following, calculated through the previous bar: wick bias = (sum of lower wicks minus sum of upper wicks) / their sum; body bias = (sum of bullish bodies minus sum of bearish bodies) / their sum; RSI bias = sum of RSI multiplied by candle direction / sum of RSI on non-doji candles; TSI bias = previous-bar True Strength Index / 100, using longer and shorter smoothing lengths; CMF bias = Chaikin Money Flow using the rectangle lookback and source OHLC; combined bias = average of the four preceding biases and, when summed volume over the rectangle lookback is positive, CMF bias. Use the rectangle lookback for wick, body and RSI sums; use zero when a denominator is zero; clamp TSI and CMF biases to [-1, 1]. Candle direction is 1 when source close > source open, -1 when source close < source open, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Accumulation band = neutral when round(abs(accumulation bias) × 100) < neutral threshold; extreme when not neutral and the rounded value >= extreme threshold; normal otherwise. Extreme threshold is greater than neutral threshold.
Calculation: Raw long breakout = previous-bar rectangle height / previous source close × 100 < range threshold AND source close > rectangle high AND previous source close <= rectangle high AND the most recent raw breakout accepted by the session and alternating-direction filters was not long AND the optional session filter passes. Raw short breakout = previous-bar rectangle height / previous source close × 100 < range threshold AND source close < rectangle low AND previous source close >= rectangle low AND the most recent raw breakout accepted by the session and alternating-direction filters was not short AND the optional session filter passes. Initially neither direction is the most recent; update it on every accepted raw breakout, even when its accumulation band is disabled or its conflict filter fails. The optional session filter compares bar datetime with a configurable session in UTC+3 and optionally restricts days to Monday through Friday.
Calculation: Enabled setup = the current accumulation band is enabled AND either the optional conflict-only filter is disabled OR the band is not neutral AND ((raw long breakout AND accumulation bias <= 0) OR (raw short breakout AND accumulation bias > 0)).
Calculation: Stop buffer = a configurable percentage of either the real entry close or the entry-bar rectangle height. Long stop = entry-bar rectangle low minus stop buffer; short stop = entry-bar rectangle high plus stop buffer. Long target = entry price + (entry price - long stop) × target risk multiple; short target = entry price - (short stop - entry price) × target risk multiple. Fix these levels at entry.
Long: raw long breakout AND enabled setup.
Short: raw short breakout AND enabled setup.
Long: (raw long breakout OR raw short breakout) AND enabled setup AND accumulation band is not neutral AND accumulation bias > 0.
Short: (raw long breakout OR raw short breakout) AND enabled setup AND accumulation band is not neutral AND accumulation bias <= 0.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (source low <= long stop OR source high >= long target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (source high >= short stop OR source low <= short target).
