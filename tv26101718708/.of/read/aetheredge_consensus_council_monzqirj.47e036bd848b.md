Calculation: Bullish trend vote = 1 when the short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs are strictly ordered upward; otherwise 0. Bearish trend vote = -1 when they are strictly ordered downward; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish S/R vote = 1 when close is at or above the latest confirmed swing low and less than a parameterized ATR distance above it, and is not similarly near the latest confirmed swing high; bearish S/R vote = -1 for the inverse conditions; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish volume vote = 1 when volume's rolling z-score exceeds a parameterized threshold, OBV is higher than its value at a shorter lookback, and close > open; bearish volume vote = -1 when the same volume condition holds, OBV is lower than its value at that lookback, and close < open; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish momentum vote = 1 when RSI and smoothed stochastic K exceed their neutral thresholds and MACD histogram > 0; bearish momentum vote = -1 when both are below their neutral thresholds and MACD histogram < 0; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volatility vote = 1 during Bollinger Band width expansion when close > open; -1 during expansion when close < open; otherwise 0. Expansion means width exceeds its longer-term average by a parameterized factor.
Calculation: Consensus = trend vote + S/R vote + volume vote + momentum vote + volatility vote.
Long: bullish trend vote = 1 OR bullish S/R vote = 1 OR bullish volume vote = 1 OR bullish momentum vote = 1 OR consensus > 0 OR consensus >= threshold AND bullish trend vote = 1 AND (bullish S/R vote = 1 OR S/R vote = 0) AND no long signal was emitted on the preceding bar.
Short: bearish trend vote = -1 OR bearish S/R vote = -1 OR bearish volume vote = -1 OR bearish momentum vote = -1 OR consensus < 0 OR consensus <= -threshold AND bearish trend vote = -1 AND (bearish S/R vote = -1 OR S/R vote = 0) AND no short signal was emitted on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR-based distance adjusted by volatility regime and nearby protective swing structure; stop price is entry price minus this distance for long positions and plus it for short positions. If trailing is enabled, while momentum agrees with the position, move the long stop up to the greater of its current value and close minus an ATR-based distance; move the short stop down to the lesser of its current value and close plus that distance.
Calculation: Target 2 distance = stop distance × reward/risk parameter, increased by a parameterized factor when momentum agrees. Target 1 distance = the lesser of a parameterized fraction of target 2 and the distance to the nearest opposing confirmed swing, with an ATR-based minimum; use the parameterized fraction of target 2 if no such swing exists. Target 3 distance = target 2 distance × a parameterized factor. Targets are entry price plus distance for long positions and minus distance for short positions.
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target 3 price (when the configured maximum target level includes target 3).
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target 3 price (when the configured maximum target level includes target 3).
