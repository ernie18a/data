Calculation: Active range = When no range is active, calculate the highest high H and lowest low L over a consolidation lookback including the current bar. Activate a range with fixed upper boundary H and lower boundary L if (H − L) / L is greater than a minimum width and no greater than a maximum width, and every high and low in the preceding containment lookback lies within [L, H]. The maximum width is the greater of a minimum cap and either a fixed width or ATR over the consolidation lookback multiplied by a width multiplier and divided by L. Evaluate decisions on the activation bar and every subsequent active bar; deactivate the range after evaluating the bar if its close is above H or below L. A new range can activate no earlier than the next bar.
Calculation: Discount level = L + (H − L) multiplied by a discount fraction. Premium level = H − (H − L) multiplied by a premium fraction; the premium and discount fractions are equal.
Calculation: Retest states = On each range activation, initialize independent long and short states to 0. On each active bar, apply at most one transition per state, in order. Long state: 0 to 1 when close < discount level; 1 to 2 when close > discount level; 2 to 3 when low <= discount level AND close > discount level. Short state: 0 to 1 when close > premium level; 1 to 2 when close < premium level; 2 to 3 when high >= premium level AND close < premium level. State 3 persists until a new range activates.
Long: Range is active AND long state changes from 2 to 3.
Long: Range is active AND low < L AND close > L.
Short: Range is active AND short state changes from 2 to 3.
Short: Range is active AND high > H AND close < H AND (low >= L OR close <= L).
