Calculation: CCI channel = SMA over 20 bars of the average of the optimal line and eight EMAs of the CCI stop series, using lengths 8, 13, 21, 21, 55, 89, 144, and 233; the stop series is low minus ATR when CCI is nonnegative, otherwise high plus ATR; the optimal line is the maximum of the ratcheting low-minus-ATR and high-plus-ATR lines, which switch on CCI crossings of zero
Calculation: Lux TRAMA = recursively updated close, with smoothing coefficient equal to the square of the SMA of length N of 1 when the highest high or lowest low over N bars changes, otherwise 0
Calculation: IFT CCI = (exp(2 × WMA(0.1 × CCI(close, N) / 4, M)) − 1) / (exp(2 × WMA(0.1 × CCI(close, N) / 4, M)) + 1)
Calculation: Lux candle patterns = bullish hammer, inverted hammer, or white marubozu for long; shooting star, hanging man, or black marubozu for short, using the source's candle-body, wick, ATR, and stochastic-trend comparisons
Calculation: Trailing ATR stop = close during the first 15 bars; afterward, the highest over H bars of (high − multiplier × ATR) when close is above that highest value and above prior close, otherwise the prior stop
