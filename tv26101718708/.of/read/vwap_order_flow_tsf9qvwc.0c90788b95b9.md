Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = using typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset cumulative volume-weighted first and second moments at each selected calendar anchor boundary; VWAP is the first moment and deviation is the square root of the nonnegative difference between the second moment and VWAP squared. If cumulative volume is zero, VWAP is typical price and deviation is zero.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low); if high equals low, use volume, negative volume, or zero according to whether close is above, below, or equal to the previous close.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum of bar delta since the selected anchor boundary; its slope is current cumulative volume delta minus its value a lookback ago, and its earlier slope is the slope a separate lookback ago.
Calculation: Wick-to-body ratio = relevant wick length divided by absolute close minus open; when body size is at most a threshold fraction of close, use a fixed fallback ratio. Lower wick is min(open, close) − low; upper wick is high − max(open, close).
Calculation: Pivot divergence = a price high or low and cumulative volume delta high or low are both confirmed as pivots at the same bar using equal left and right lookbacks; compare the price pivot with the immediately preceding price pivot of the same kind and the delta pivot with the most recent delta pivot recorded at a price pivot of that kind. Update the recorded price at every price pivot and update the recorded delta only when a matching delta pivot exists. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
