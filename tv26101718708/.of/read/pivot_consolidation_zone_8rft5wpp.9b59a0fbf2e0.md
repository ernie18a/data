Calculation: Breakout margin = current ATR multiplied by a nonnegative breakout-margin parameter.
Calculation: Active zone = when no zone is active, select the valid tested pivot-high/pivot-low pair with the earliest pivot bar; preserve insertion order for ties. Confirm each pivot using the same number of bars on each side, retain a bounded number of the most recent pivots of each type, and remove a pivot high when close > its high + current breakout margin or a pivot low when close < its low - current breakout margin. From confirmation onward, a pivot is tested when the current bar overlaps the pivot candle's high-low range; consecutive overlapping bars count as one test. A pair is valid when both pivots have at least one test, pivot high > pivot low, the earlier pivot is within a maximum lookback from the current bar, every close from the earlier pivot through the preceding bar lies between pivot low - current breakout margin and pivot high + current breakout margin, and, if enabled, pivot high - pivot low <= current ATR multiplied by the zone-height limit. Set zone top and bottom to the selected pivot prices. The zone remains active through the first bar whose close is > top + current breakout margin or < bottom - current breakout margin; then deactivate it, even if volume confirmation fails. A new zone can be created on a later bar.
Calculation: Zone buy and sell volume = at zone creation, extend the earlier pivot's bar backward up to a maximum lookback while closes remain between bottom - the creation-bar breakout margin and top + that margin. Sum contributions from that left edge through the latest non-breakout bar. For each bar with high > low, count volume multiplied by the fraction of its high-low range inside the zone, or count its full volume if outside wicks are included. Multiply counted volume by (close - low)/(high - low) for buy volume and by (high - close)/(high - low) for sell volume. Bars with high = low contribute nothing. Exclude the breakout bar.
Calculation: Rounded buy percentage = zone buy volume divided by total zone buy and sell volume, expressed as a percentage and rounded to one decimal place; use an equal buy/sell split when total volume is zero.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at the breakout entry bar multiplied by a stop multiplier, or entry price multiplied by a fixed percentage. The stop and target levels are fixed at entry; the target uses the risk-reward parameter of the last enabled target in target order. A new breakout signal replaces the prior levels.
Long: An active zone closes above its top + current breakout margin AND volume confirmation is disabled OR an active zone closes above its top + current breakout margin AND zone buy volume >= zone sell volume.
Short: An active zone closes below its bottom - current breakout margin AND volume confirmation is disabled OR an active zone closes below its bottom - current breakout margin AND zone sell volume > zone buy volume.
Long: A zone is created AND its lower pivot or its recorded lower-pivot test is marked; assign the marking to the historical pivot or test bar when the zone is created.
Short: A zone is created AND its upper pivot or its recorded upper-pivot test is marked; assign the marking to the historical pivot or test bar when the zone is created.
Long: A zone is active AND rounded buy percentage > the equal buy/sell percentage.
Short: A zone is active AND rounded buy percentage < the equal buy/sell percentage.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND low <= entry price - risk distance OR after the entry bar AND an enabled target exists AND high >= entry price + risk distance multiplied by its risk-reward parameter.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND high >= entry price + risk distance OR after the entry bar AND an enabled target exists AND low <= entry price - risk distance multiplied by its risk-reward parameter.
