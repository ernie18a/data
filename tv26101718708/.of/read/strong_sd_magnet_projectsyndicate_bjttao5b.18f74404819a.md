Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the swing lookback on both sides; it becomes known after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Zone band = pivot price ± half the ATR-multiplied zone height; supply is centered on the pivot high, demand on the pivot low.
Calculation: Zone strength = clamp to 0–10 the weighted average of departure impulse, formation volume relative to its moving average, rejection-wick fraction, and freshness (1 − retests / touch limit), multiplied by the idle-time decay factor. Departure impulse is the post-pivot move in the opposing direction divided by the ATR-multiplied impulse threshold, capped at 1; wick fraction is the pivot bar’s rejection wick divided by its range. Retests also add the current bar’s rejection-wick fraction, capped at 1. Idle decay declines linearly from 1 to its floor over the decay lookback.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms a demand zone AND its pivot-bar range ≥ ATR × minimum swing size AND enough bars have elapsed since the prior demand zone; OR a valid, displayed demand source has at least the minimum retests and minimum source strength AND its nearest valid, displayed supply target in the same session is above it, meets minimum target strength, and is within the ATR-multiplied maximum reach, with the source among the session’s highest-strength qualifying pulls up to the per-session limit.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms a supply zone AND its pivot-bar range ≥ ATR × minimum swing size AND enough bars have elapsed since the prior supply zone; OR a valid, displayed supply source has at least the minimum retests and minimum source strength AND its nearest valid, displayed demand target in the same session is below it, meets minimum target strength, and is within the ATR-multiplied maximum reach, with the source among the session’s highest-strength qualifying pulls down to the per-session limit.
