Calculation: Sessions = assign each bar to the batch beginning at the configured daily boundary or the start of the configured session in its configured timezone; bars outside a configured session remain in the most recent batch.
Calculation: Supply and demand zones = on confirmation of a pivot high or low with equal left and right lookbacks, respectively, create a supply zone centered at the pivot high or a demand zone centered at the pivot low. Require the pivot candle range to be at least a threshold multiple of current ATR and the confirmation bar to be sufficiently far from the last same-side zone creation. Set zone thickness to a multiple of current ATR. If overlap merging is enabled, merge with the most recently created unbroken overlapping same-side zone in the same session among the latest stored zones; expand its boundaries, set its center to their midpoint, and retain its other attributes. Otherwise create a zone and update the last creation bar. Prune old zones according to the session retention and zone storage limits.
Calculation: Zone state = on every bar, including creation, count a touch when the bar's high-low range first overlaps the zone after a non-overlapping bar; record the touch bar and add the current candle's supply upper-wick or demand lower-wick fraction of its range to the zone's wick factor, capped at one. Mark supply broken when the configured close or high exceeds its top; mark demand broken when the configured close or low falls below its bottom. Broken zones remain broken.
Calculation: Zone score = at creation, store impulse as the pivot high minus the lowest low over the pivot lookback for supply, or the highest high over that lookback minus the pivot low for demand, divided by current ATR times an impulse normalization parameter and clamped to zero through one; store pivot volume divided by the current volume SMA, capped at one, or zero if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive; store the pivot candle's corresponding rejection wick divided by its range, capped at one. Freshness is max(0, 1 minus touches divided by the touch normalization parameter). Score is ten times the weighted mean of impulse, volume, wick factor and freshness, multiplied by the optional linear idle decay from one to its floor over the configured number of bars since the last touch, and clamped to zero through ten. If the weights sum to zero, score is zero; initialize the last touch bar to the creation bar.
Calculation: Eligible pull = for each retained session, rank zones of each side by current score, including broken zones, and retain the highest-ranked zones up to the per-side limit, optionally excluding zones below the strong-zone threshold. For each retained unbroken source with sufficient touches and source score, select the nearest retained unbroken opposite-side target in the same session whose score meets the target threshold, whose midpoint is below a supply source or above a demand source, and whose midpoint distance from the source is at most a configured multiple of current ATR. Rank resulting candidates across both sides by source score and retain up to the per-session pull limit. Preserve creation order for equal zone scores and supply-before-demand candidate order for equal source scores.
Long: A retained session has an eligible pull from a demand zone to a supply zone.
Short: A retained session has an eligible pull from a supply zone to a demand zone.
