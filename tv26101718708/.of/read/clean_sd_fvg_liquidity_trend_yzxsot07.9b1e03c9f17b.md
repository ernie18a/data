Calculation: Demand zone = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, close > open AND close minus close a displacement lookback ago >= higher-timeframe ATR times a displacement multiplier AND at least one bearish candle exists in the preceding base-search lookback. Its boundaries are the high and low, or the body high and low, of the most recent such bearish candle.
Calculation: Supply zone = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, close < open AND close a displacement lookback ago minus close >= higher-timeframe ATR times a displacement multiplier AND at least one bullish candle exists in the preceding base-search lookback. Its boundaries are the high and low, or the body high and low, of the most recent such bullish candle.
Calculation: Active zones = Retain the most recent configured maximum number of demand zones and supply zones separately, adding a zone only when its base candle differs from the last added base candle of the same type. If invalidation removal is enabled, remove a demand zone when chart close < its lower boundary and a supply zone when chart close > its upper boundary. Add zones before removing invalid zones.
Calculation: Distance to nearest active zone = The minimum distance from chart close to any active zone's nearest boundary, or zero if close is inside a zone; undefined if no zone is active.
Long: A demand zone is identified AND its base candle differs from the last added demand zone's base candle.
Short: A supply zone is identified AND its base candle differs from the last added supply zone's base candle.
Long: Chart low > chart high two bars ago AND their difference >= minimum gap size AND distance to nearest active zone <= chart ATR times a proximity multiplier.
Short: Chart high < chart low two bars ago AND their difference >= minimum gap size AND distance to nearest active zone <= chart ATR times a proximity multiplier.
Long: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the latest confirmed pivot high > the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low AND, if EMA confirmation is enabled, shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA.
Short: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the latest confirmed pivot high < the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the latest confirmed pivot low < the preceding confirmed pivot low AND, if EMA confirmation is enabled, shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA.
