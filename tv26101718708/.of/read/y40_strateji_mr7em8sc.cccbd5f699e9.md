Calculation: Directional component scores = For each direction, evaluate the following ordered conditions and assign the points of the first matching condition, or zero if none matches. Long/short comparisons are shown respectively; a cross requires the current ordering and the opposite or equal ordering on the preceding bar. RSI uses close: 3 if RSI <= oversold minus deep offset / >= overbought plus deep offset; 2 if RSI <= oversold / >= overbought; 1 if RSI <= oversold plus warning offset / >= overbought minus warning offset. Stochastic uses an SMA-smoothed %K and an SMA of that %K as %D: 3 if %K crosses above/below %D AND %K <= oversold minus deep offset / >= overbought plus deep offset; 2 if the cross occurs AND %K <= oversold / >= overbought; 1 if the cross occurs AND %K <= oversold plus buffer / >= overbought minus buffer, OR %K <= oversold / >= overbought. Bollinger Bands use close: 3 if close <= lower band minus deep offset / >= upper band plus deep offset, where deep offset is band width times a fraction; 2 if close <= lower band / >= upper band; 1 if close <= lower band plus a fraction of the distance to the middle band / >= upper band minus the same fraction of the distance to the middle band. CCI uses close: 3, 2, or 1 if CCI <= the negative deep, medium, or light threshold respectively / >= the corresponding positive threshold. Williams %R uses highest high and lowest low, with value -50 when they are equal: 3, 2, or 1 if %R <= the negative deep, medium, or light threshold / >= that threshold reflected around -50. MFI uses typical price: 3, 2, or 1 if MFI <= the deep, medium, or light threshold / >= that threshold reflected around 50. ADX/DI: 3 if +DI crosses above -DI / -DI crosses above +DI AND ADX >= strong-trend threshold; 2 if +DI > -DI / -DI > +DI AND ADX > ADX at a specified earlier bar; 1 if +DI > -DI / -DI > +DI. Divergence: when enabled, 3 if the latest corresponding divergence is within the fresh window, otherwise 2 if within the expiry window, otherwise 1 if within an extended expiry window. Deep, medium, and light thresholds are ordered from most extreme to least extreme; the fresh window is shorter than the expiry window, which is shorter than the extended window.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = At confirmation of a price pivot low/high with equal left and right lookbacks, compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind: the new low is lower AND its contemporaneous close-based RSI is higher AND below 50 / the new high is higher AND its contemporaneous RSI is lower AND above 50. Record divergence age from the confirmation bar, updating the previous pivot on every confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Directional voters and scores = For each direction, voters are the number of its eight component scores >= 1; raw score is their sum. Effective score is raw score multiplied by a volume factor: use the strong multiplier when volume / SMA(volume) >= the higher spike threshold, otherwise the weak multiplier when it >= the lower spike threshold, otherwise 1; use a ratio of zero when unavailable.
Calculation: Majority condition = Directional voters >= minimum voters AND directional effective score >= minimum score AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close >= trend EMA for long or close <= trend EMA for short. The trend EMA has a longer lookback than the slow EMA, which has a longer lookback than the fast EMA.
Calculation: Re-entry state = After a stop or trail exit, record the exited side, count flat bars, and track the highest high and lowest low on those bars, including the current bar. Expire this state after the maximum window. Count consecutive re-entries, incrementing on a re-entry and resetting on any other entry; a direct reversal does not create re-entry state.
Calculation: Pullback touch = At least one high or low from the current bar through the bounce lookback plus an additional touch lookback is within the touch-distance fraction of the current slow EMA; compare all historical highs and lows with the current slow EMA.
Calculation: Stop and trail levels = Initialize the stop at entry close minus/plus the stop-loss fraction for long/short; use a smaller fraction for a pullback entry that is neither a majority entry nor a re-entry. Initialize the favorable extreme to entry close. On each later bar without an entry or exit, update the long maximum with high or the short minimum with low. ATR factor = 1 + ATR weight times (clamp(ATR / SMA(ATR over a longer lookback), lower bound, upper bound) - 1), using a ratio of 1 when the ATR average is not positive. Score factor = clamp(1 - opposite raw score / maximum possible raw score times tightening rate + own raw score / maximum possible raw score times loosening rate, lower bound, upper bound). Activate the trail when favorable profit from the extreme / entry price >= activation fraction times ATR factor; once active, retain activation and, whenever that test passes, set the trail to the extreme times (1 minus/plus base trail fraction times ATR factor times score factor) for long/short. With breakeven and stop loss enabled, on each such bar move the stop only favorably to entry price when close profit reaches the lower activation threshold, or to entry price plus/minus the locked-profit fraction when close profit reaches the higher threshold. Test current-bar high/low against the stop and trail levels carried from the preceding bar before updating them.
Long: Majority condition for long AND position side != long AND long trading enabled.
Short: Majority condition for short AND position side != short AND short trading enabled.
Long: Re-entry enabled AND position flat AND last stop/trail exit side = long AND bars since exit >= cooldown AND bars since exit <= maximum window AND consecutive re-entries < maximum AND (highest high since exit - low) / highest high since exit >= pullback fraction AND close > fast EMA AND close > slow EMA AND long voters >= re-entry minimum AND long trading enabled.
Short: Re-entry enabled AND position flat AND last stop/trail exit side = short AND bars since exit >= cooldown AND bars since exit <= maximum window AND consecutive re-entries < maximum AND (high - lowest low since exit) / lowest low since exit >= pullback fraction AND close < fast EMA AND close < slow EMA AND short voters >= re-entry minimum AND short trading enabled.
Long: Pullback entry enabled AND position side != long AND close > trend EMA AND +DI > -DI AND ADX >= strong-trend threshold AND long voters >= pullback minimum AND pullback touch = true AND close > open on every bar in the bounce lookback AND close > slow EMA AND long trading enabled.
Short: Pullback entry enabled AND position side != short AND close < trend EMA AND -DI > +DI AND ADX >= strong-trend threshold AND short voters >= pullback minimum AND pullback touch = true AND close < open on every bar in the bounce lookback AND close < slow EMA AND short trading enabled.
Long: Long majority condition = false AND long voters >= minimum voters minus warning voter offset AND long effective score >= minimum score minus warning score offset.
Short: Short majority condition = false AND short voters >= minimum voters minus warning voter offset AND short effective score >= minimum score minus warning score offset.
Long: Divergence enabled AND bullish divergence age <= expiry window AND long entry condition = false.
Short: Divergence enabled AND bearish divergence age <= expiry window AND short entry condition = false.
Exit: long, short entry condition = true OR stop loss enabled AND low <= prior stop level OR trailing enabled AND trail active AND low <= prior trail level OR long-only trading enabled AND short majority condition = true.
Exit: short, long entry condition = true OR stop loss enabled AND high >= prior stop level OR trailing enabled AND trail active AND high >= prior trail level OR short-only trading enabled AND long majority condition = true.
