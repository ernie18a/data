Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the opening range; lock these levels when the opening range ends.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = daily cumulative sum of volume × sign(close − open), resetting each day.
Calculation: Long signal score = count of: close > VWAP; close > 9-period EMA; 9-period EMA rising for 2 consecutive bars; 9-period EMA > 21-period EMA; long retest condition; long rejection condition; bias RSI > 55; entry RSI > 55; cumulative volume delta rising; and not extended.
Calculation: Short signal score = count of: close < VWAP; close < 9-period EMA; 9-period EMA falling for 2 consecutive bars; 9-period EMA < 21-period EMA; short retest condition; short rejection condition; bias RSI < 45; entry RSI < 45; cumulative volume delta falling; and not extended.
Calculation: Not extended = absolute distance between close and 9-period EMA, divided by close, is at most the configured maximum.
Calculation: Not choppy = absolute distance between close and VWAP, divided by close, is at least the configured minimum.
Long: After close crosses above the locked opening-range high while close > VWAP and close > 9-period EMA and 9-period EMA is rising for 2 consecutive bars and 9-period EMA > 21-period EMA and not extended and not choppy, remain armed until an opposite breakout, day reset, or long signal; then low ≤ opening-range high and close > opening-range high and low ≤ 9-period EMA and close > 9-period EMA and close > open and close > VWAP and close > 9-period EMA and 9-period EMA is rising for 2 consecutive bars and 9-period EMA > 21-period EMA and not extended and not choppy and long signal score ≥ configured minimum, with at most one long signal per day.
Short: After close crosses below the locked opening-range low while close < VWAP and close < 9-period EMA and 9-period EMA is falling for 2 consecutive bars and 9-period EMA < 21-period EMA and not extended and not choppy, remain armed until an opposite breakout, day reset, or short signal; then high ≥ opening-range low and close < opening-range low and high ≥ 9-period EMA and close < 9-period EMA and close < open and close < VWAP and close < 9-period EMA and 9-period EMA is falling for 2 consecutive bars and 9-period EMA < 21-period EMA and not extended and not choppy and short signal score ≥ configured minimum, with at most one short signal per day.
Exit: Long, close < 9-period EMA OR close < VWAP.
Exit: Short, close > 9-period EMA OR close > VWAP.
