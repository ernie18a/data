Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in a configurable daily session window; reset each day and lock after the window ends.
Calculation: VWAP = daily cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by daily cumulative volume, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: RSI inputs = bias RSI and entry RSI of close on independently configurable timeframes; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar. The bias RSI has a longer lookback than the entry RSI.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = daily cumulative sum of volume × sign(close − open); on the first bar of a day, compare the reset value with the previous day's final value.
Calculation: Long base = close > VWAP AND close > short EMA AND (trend filter disabled OR short EMA > its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago AND short EMA > long EMA) AND (extension filter disabled OR abs(close − short EMA) / close ≤ maximum distance) AND (chop filter disabled OR abs(close − VWAP) / close ≥ minimum distance). The short EMA has a shorter lookback than the long EMA.
Calculation: Short base = close < VWAP AND close < short EMA AND (trend filter disabled OR short EMA < its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago AND short EMA < long EMA) AND (extension filter disabled OR abs(close − short EMA) / close ≤ maximum distance) AND (chop filter disabled OR abs(close − VWAP) / close ≥ minimum distance).
Calculation: Long breakout = opening range locked AND close crosses above opening-range high. Short breakout = opening range locked AND close crosses below opening-range low.
Calculation: Armed state = reset both sides at the start of each day; a long breakout with long base arms long and disarms short, and a short breakout with short base arms short and disarms long; a final signal disarms its own side. Update the armed state before evaluating the current bar's retest.
Calculation: Long retest condition = if retest is required, opening range locked AND long armed AND low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high; otherwise long breakout. Short retest condition = if retest is required, opening range locked AND short armed AND high ≥ opening-range low AND close < opening-range low; otherwise short breakout.
Calculation: Long rejection condition = rejection filter disabled OR low ≤ short EMA AND close > short EMA AND close > open. Short rejection condition = rejection filter disabled OR high ≥ short EMA AND close < short EMA AND close < open.
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions: close > VWAP; close > short EMA; short EMA > its previous value AND its previous value > its value two bars ago; short EMA > long EMA; long retest condition; long rejection condition; bias RSI > upper threshold; entry RSI > upper threshold; cumulative volume delta > its previous value; extension filter disabled OR abs(close − short EMA) / close ≤ maximum distance.
Calculation: Short score = count of true conditions: close < VWAP; close < short EMA; short EMA < its previous value AND its previous value < its value two bars ago; short EMA < long EMA; short retest condition; short rejection condition; bias RSI < lower threshold; entry RSI < lower threshold; cumulative volume delta < its previous value; extension filter disabled OR abs(close − short EMA) / close ≤ maximum distance. The lower RSI threshold is below the upper threshold.
Long: long retest condition AND long rejection condition AND long base AND long score ≥ minimum score AND (one signal per side per day disabled OR no earlier long signal today).
Short: short retest condition AND short rejection condition AND short base AND short score ≥ minimum score AND (one signal per side per day disabled OR no earlier short signal today).
Exit: long, (EMA exit enabled AND close < short EMA) OR (VWAP exit enabled AND close < VWAP).
Exit: short, (EMA exit enabled AND close > short EMA) OR (VWAP exit enabled AND close > VWAP).
Calculation: Take-profit unit = maximum of a configurable minimum price increment and either ATR at entry × a multiplier or the locked opening-range high minus low; use ATR if the opening range is unavailable. Fix three increasing target multiples and the unit at entry.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has high ≥ entry price + take-profit unit × a target multiple AND that target has not previously been hit since entry.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has low ≤ entry price − take-profit unit × a target multiple AND that target has not previously been hit since entry.
