Calculation: Opening range = for each of two independently configured sessions, the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times are in that session; lock the range on the first bar outside the session and reset all session-specific breakout, retest, and active-trade state at the next session start.
Calculation: Breakout distance = a selected fixed distance, a selected percentage of the opening-range width, or zero.
Calculation: Qualified upward breakout = after the opening range is locked and the elapsed time since the first bar outside the session is within the selected validity window, close > opening-range high AND close - opening-range high >= breakout distance AND, if first-break-only is selected, no earlier qualified upward breakout has occurred in this session. Record every qualified upward breakout even when a trade is active.
Calculation: Qualified downward breakout = after the opening range is locked and the elapsed time since the first bar outside the session is within the selected validity window, close < opening-range low AND opening-range low - close >= breakout distance AND, if first-break-only is selected, no earlier qualified downward breakout has occurred in this session. Record every qualified downward breakout even when a trade is active.
Calculation: Retest state = separately for each direction and session, a qualified breakout starts a pending retest at the corresponding locked range boundary unless that direction is already pending; a long retest is touched when low <= its boundary and a short retest is touched when high >= its boundary. A touch may occur on the breakout bar; retain it until the corresponding retest entry or the next session start. A pending retest can complete after the breakout validity window.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry close, select the opposite opening-range boundary adjusted outward by a fixed buffer, entry price adjusted adversely by a fixed distance, or entry price adjusted adversely by the greater of a fixed minimum distance and a multiple of ATR. For ATR on a higher timeframe use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; for ATR on a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on the chart timeframe use the current completed bar. Initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop.
Calculation: Target = entry price adjusted favorably by either a fixed distance or a multiple of initial risk. On bars after entry, if the break-even/partial option is enabled and the favorable extreme reaches a selected multiple of initial risk for the first time, move the stop to entry price before evaluating that bar's exits.
Long: Retest-entry mode AND qualified upward breakout.
Short: Retest-entry mode AND qualified downward breakout.
Long: Immediate-breakout mode AND qualified upward breakout AND no active trade for that session.
Short: Immediate-breakout mode AND qualified downward breakout AND no active trade for that session.
Long: Retest-entry mode AND a long retest is pending AND its boundary has been touched AND close > that boundary AND no active trade for that session.
Short: Retest-entry mode AND a short retest is pending AND its boundary has been touched AND close < that boundary AND no active trade for that session.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND break-even/partial option is enabled AND this trade has not previously triggered it AND high - entry price >= selected multiple of initial risk; take a partial exit.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND break-even/partial option is enabled AND this trade has not previously triggered it AND entry price - low >= selected multiple of initial risk; take a partial exit.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (high >= target OR low <= current stop OR bars since entry >= selected maximum); exit at the target or stop if reached, otherwise at close. If both target and stop are reached, use the level closer to the bar's open, choosing the stop on a tie.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (low <= target OR high >= current stop OR bars since entry >= selected maximum); exit at the target or stop if reached, otherwise at close. If both target and stop are reached, use the level closer to the bar's open, choosing the stop on a tie.
