Calculation: Confirmation bars = chart-timeframe bars or completed lower-timeframe bars, processed when completed; liquidity bars are completed bars at a timeframe at least as high as the confirmation timeframe, and competition windows are at a higher timeframe than the confirmation timeframe. Liquidity and competition timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Unresolved liquidity low or high = a completed liquidity bar's low or high within the liquidity lookback for which every newer completed liquidity bar has a strictly higher low or strictly lower high, respectively; retain the reference while its candidate is active.
Calculation: Delivery run = starting at the latest bearish or bullish confirmation bar within a search gap ending at the sweep bar, scan backward through consecutive matching-direction bars and qualifying inside bars, up to a scan limit; an inside bar qualifies when its high is no higher and its low is no lower than the preceding bar and an adjacent bar has the matching direction. Require a minimum count of actual matching-direction bars. The bullish setup's delivery level is the highest open of bearish bars in its run; the bearish setup's delivery level is the lowest open of bullish bars in its run.
Calculation: Prior swing = the nearest confirmation-bar pivot before the most recent delivery-run member within a swing lookback; a pivot high is strictly higher than each of a configured number of later highs and at least as high as each corresponding earlier high, and a pivot low is strictly lower than each later low and at most as low as each earlier low.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = an unconsumed unresolved liquidity low is freshly crossed below by the confirmation bar's low or, under the alternative sweep setting, close, beyond a tolerance; the preceding confirmation bar's corresponding value was at or above that threshold. Store the sweep low and time, delivery level, prior swing high, and competition window. Require the swing high to exceed the sweep low and, where the competition-window restriction applies, its pivot time to be within that window. A new lower low while the reference remains violated replaces the sweep extreme and recalculates the delivery level and swing, resetting confirmation. Discard the candidate after its lifetime or if its delivery level is not reclaimed within the shorter reclaim lifetime. Where the relaxed swing-window setting applies, a candidate initially lacking valid structure may acquire it on a later bar after the sweep extreme.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = an unconsumed unresolved liquidity high is freshly crossed above by the confirmation bar's high or, under the alternative sweep setting, close, beyond a tolerance; the preceding confirmation bar's corresponding value was at or below that threshold. Store the sweep high and time, delivery level, prior swing low, and competition window. Require the swing low to be below the sweep high and, where the competition-window restriction applies, its pivot time to be within that window. A new higher high while the reference remains violated replaces the sweep extreme and recalculates the delivery level and swing, resetting confirmation. Discard the candidate after its lifetime or if its delivery level is not reclaimed within the shorter reclaim lifetime. Where the relaxed swing-window setting applies, a candidate initially lacking valid structure may acquire it on a later bar after the sweep extreme.
Long: A bullish candidate has a valid delivery level and prior swing high.
Short: A bearish candidate has a valid delivery level and prior swing low.
Long: After the bullish candidate's latest sweep extreme, a completed confirmation bar closes above its delivery level AND that bar or a subsequent completed confirmation bar within the swing-break deadline closes above its prior swing high AND the swing high exceeds the sweep low.
Short: After the bearish candidate's latest sweep extreme, a completed confirmation bar closes below its delivery level AND that bar or a subsequent completed confirmation bar within the swing-break deadline closes below its prior swing low AND the swing low is below the sweep high.
Long: With comparison markets disabled, the low of the chart-timeframe bar or still-forming higher-timeframe bar is below the low of a completed bar in the same timeframe within a lookback AND every intervening completed bar has a strictly higher low.
Short: With comparison markets disabled, the high of the chart-timeframe bar or still-forming higher-timeframe bar is above the high of a completed bar in the same timeframe within a lookback AND every intervening completed bar has a strictly lower high.
