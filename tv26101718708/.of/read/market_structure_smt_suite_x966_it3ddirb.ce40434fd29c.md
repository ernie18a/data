Calculation: Untouched liquidity low/high = low/high of a completed bar on a timeframe higher than the confirmation timeframe, among the recent lookback bars, with no later completed bar in that lookback making a lower low / higher high.
Calculation: Bearish delivery run = consecutive bearish confirmation bars, optionally including an inside bar adjacent to a bearish bar; its CISD is the highest open among its bearish bars. Bullish delivery run and CISD are the mirror image, with the lowest open.
Calculation: Prior pivot high/low = a local high/low confirmed by completed bars on both sides; use the nearest such pivot preceding the delivery run.
Long: A completed confirmation bar sweeps below an untouched liquidity low after the preceding confirmation bar was at or above that low AND, after the sweep's lowest low, a bearish delivery run with at least two bearish bars forms AND a later confirmation close is above the run's CISD within its configured lifetime AND a later confirmation close breaks above the prior pivot high within its configured lifetime AND the candidate remains within its configured lifetime.
Short: A completed confirmation bar sweeps above an untouched liquidity high after the preceding confirmation bar was at or below that high AND, after the sweep's highest high, a bullish delivery run with at least two bullish bars forms AND a later confirmation close is below the run's CISD within its configured lifetime AND a later confirmation close breaks below the prior pivot low within its configured lifetime AND the candidate remains within its configured lifetime.
