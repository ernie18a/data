Calculation: Liquidity-market divergence = weighted average percentage growth of selected countries’ M2 balances, minus comparison-index percentage growth; each growth uses the same lookback. Convert M2 balances to USD for the weights, discount China’s M2 by 50%, and optionally blend Fed net-liquidity growth (Fed balance sheet − Treasury General Account − reverse repo) into the US M2 component, or use it as a standalone component if US M2 is not selected. Use values from the configured calculation timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, including its current bar while it is forming.
Long: Liquidity-market divergence >= buy threshold OR liquidity-market divergence crosses above buy threshold
