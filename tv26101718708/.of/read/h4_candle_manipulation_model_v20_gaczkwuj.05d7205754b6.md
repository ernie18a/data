Calculation: Ribbon direction = On a specified timeframe, use the last completed bar's close and EMAs of close with individually selectable, different lookbacks. Count enabled EMAs strictly below and strictly above the close. Direction is bullish if the count below is greater than zero AND the count above is at most a parameter; otherwise bearish if the count above is greater than zero AND the count below is at most the same parameter; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: ADR ratio = The range of the still-forming higher-timeframe daily bar divided by the simple moving average of the ranges of completed daily bars; use zero if the average is not positive.
Calculation: Setup bar = The last completed bar of a higher timeframe, evaluated when a new bar of that timeframe begins. The shorter ribbon timeframe is also higher than the chart timeframe; the longer ribbon timeframe is the setup timeframe.
Calculation: Stop = The setup bar's low for a long or high for a short, stored at entry.
Calculation: Target = Entry price plus the risk multiplied by a parameter for a long, or minus that amount for a short, where risk is the absolute difference between entry price and stop. If the liquidity target option is enabled, use the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short over a parameterized lookback of completed setup-timeframe bars ending with the setup bar, provided it is strictly more than one risk unit beyond entry; otherwise use the risk-multiple target.
Long: Ribbon direction is bullish on either the shorter or longer higher timeframe.
Short: Ribbon direction is bearish on either the shorter or longer higher timeframe.
Long: A new setup-timeframe bar begins AND the setup bar's low is below the lowest low of the preceding parameterized number of setup-timeframe bars AND its close is above the preceding setup-timeframe bar's close, or above its own open when the alternative close option is selected AND the ADR filter is disabled or ADR ratio is at most its threshold AND the alignment filter is disabled or both ribbon directions are bullish.
Short: A new setup-timeframe bar begins AND the setup bar's high is above the highest high of the preceding parameterized number of setup-timeframe bars AND its close is below the preceding setup-timeframe bar's close, or below its own open when the alternative close option is selected AND the ADR filter is disabled or ADR ratio is at most its threshold AND the alignment filter is disabled or both ribbon directions are bearish AND the long setup condition is false.
Exit: long, chart-bar low is at or below stop OR chart-bar high is at or above target; evaluate beginning on the bar after the setup, with stop taking precedence if both are reached.
Exit: short, chart-bar high is at or above stop OR chart-bar low is at or below target; evaluate beginning on the bar after the setup, with stop taking precedence if both are reached.
