Calculation: Lower Bollinger Band = SMA(close, lookback) − multiplier × standard deviation(close, same lookback)
Long: Short-term SMA(close) > long-term SMA(close) AND close crosses above the lower Bollinger Band
Short: Short-term SMA(close) < long-term SMA(close) AND close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band
Exit: Long, long-term SMA(close) crosses above short-term SMA(close) OR a trailing stop is hit after price reaches the trail activation level above entry price; stop trails the highest favorable price by the trail offset
Exit: Short, short-term SMA(close) crosses above long-term SMA(close) OR a trailing stop is hit after price reaches the trail activation level below entry price; stop trails the lowest favorable price by the trail offset
