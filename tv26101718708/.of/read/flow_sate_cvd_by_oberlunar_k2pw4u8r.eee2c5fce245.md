Calculation: CVD candle = For each completed source bar, classify each completed lower-timeframe bar's volume as positive if its close exceeds its open, negative if its close is below its open, and otherwise by the sign of its close change from the preceding lower-timeframe bar, carrying forward the preceding sign when unchanged. Accumulate signed volume within the source bar; its CVD open is the preceding CVD close or zero at a new anchor, its high and low are the extrema of that running cumulative sum, and its close is the final sum. The anchor resets CVD at each new anchor period. Use available aggregated lower-timeframe bars; tick-based classification cannot be reproduced from bar data.
Calculation: Source bars = For each configured source timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed source bar when its completion becomes available. For a gap event, evaluate the gap on that bar and the class, price high and low, and session membership on the preceding source bar. Evaluate continuous state calculations on the last completed primary source bar.
Calculation: Adaptive features = On source bars, let d = CVD close minus CVD open. Clamp unit scores to [0,1] and signed scores to [-1,1]. Volatility is a clamped affine function of shorter ATR divided by longer ATR. Form fast, middle and slow weights as affine functions of volatility, with fast weight increasing and slow weight decreasing as volatility rises. Trend is a weighted signed sum of the proportion of bullish minus bearish adjacent pairs in a fan of progressively longer close EMAs, the shortest-minus-longest EMA spread divided by shorter ATR, and ATR-normalized slopes of short, middle and long EMAs. Momentum is a weighted sum of fast, middle and slow normalized TRIX and RSI: normalized TRIX is tanh of half the triple-EMA percentage change divided by its rolling standard deviation; normalized RSI is centered and scaled to [-1,1]. Flow is the weighted sum of fast, middle and slow tanh-normalized z-scores of d. Flow intensity combines absolute flow and a clamped affine function of abs(d) divided by a medium EMA of abs(d). Persistence is the weighted sum of fast, middle and slow EMAs of sign(d). Noise is one minus a weighted sum of absolute trend, absolute momentum and absolute persistence. Impact efficiency is the clamped ratio of abs(close-open)/shorter ATR to normalized abs(d), with a positive denominator floor and a scale factor. Signed impact is sign(close-open) times impact efficiency, reduced by a factor when body and d do not have the same sign. Signed resistance is opposite sign(d), with magnitude equal to flow intensity times one minus impact efficiency times an affine function of noise. For each of fast, middle and slow lookbacks, compare the current low and high with the preceding rolling lowest low and highest high; weight these comparisons to obtain downward and upward extension. Compare negative d with the weighted magnitudes of the preceding rolling negative d minima, and positive d with the weighted preceding rolling positive d maxima, to obtain clamped selling and buying decay. Bullish decay is downward extension times selling decay times an affine weighted sum of the decreases in absolute momentum and absolute trend; bearish decay is the corresponding upward extension and buying decay. Signed decay is the larger decay, positive for bullish and negative for bearish, or zero on a tie. Liquidity is a clamped weighted sum of flow intensity, impact efficiency, one minus noise, and volatility.
Calculation: Divergence = For each fast, middle and slow lookback, compute separate price-close and CVD-close waves as triple EMAs. For each wave, combine its slope over a fixed lag, normalized by price ATR or Wilder-smoothed abs(d), with its one-bar percentage change normalized by the rolling standard deviation of that change; apply tanh normalization to both components and use their weighted sum only when their signs agree and both magnitudes exceed a threshold, otherwise use zero. A scale has divergence when the price and CVD signals have opposite signs and both exceed a minimum magnitude. Its score is the square root of the product of their magnitudes times a volatility-dependent scale weight. Select the greatest score, breaking ties in favor of the faster scale; divergence direction is the selected price signal's sign if the score reaches its threshold, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Directional lanes = Flow lane is a clamped weighted sum of flow and persistence; its intensity is a weighted sum of flow intensity and abs(persistence). Drive is flow intensity times abs(signed impact) times one minus abs(signed decay). Response selects the largest of drive, abs(signed resistance), and abs(signed decay), with that priority on ties, provided the largest reaches its minimum; its signed value is respectively sign(signed impact) times drive, signed resistance, or signed decay, otherwise zero. Structure base is a weighted sum of trend and momentum. If nonzero divergence opposes a sufficiently large structure base, reduce the base by a factor proportional to the divergence score; otherwise retain it. Structure is classified as diverging when that conflict score reaches its threshold, or transition when sufficiently large trend and momentum have opposite signs; otherwise its sign is compared with a threshold. Regime activity is a weighted sum of volatility and liquidity; dislocation is volatility times one minus liquidity. Regime intensity is the larger of volatility and dislocation when both exceed their dislocation thresholds, volatility when volatility and liquidity exceed their expansion thresholds, liquidity when liquidity exceeds and volatility is below their orderly thresholds, and regime activity otherwise.
Calculation: Composite = Clamp the weighted sum of flow lane, signed response and structure to [-1,1]. Directional coherence is its absolute value divided by the corresponding weighted sum of the absolute lane values, clamped to [0,1], or zero if the denominator is too small. Regime quality is a clamped affine weighted sum of regime intensity, liquidity, one minus noise, and negative dislocation. Composite equals the directional sum multiplied by affine functions of coherence and regime quality. Its threshold is an affine increasing function of noise and dislocation.
Calculation: Absorption = Bullish absorption requires d < 0, flow intensity at or above an adaptive threshold increasing with noise and abs(trend), signed resistance at or above another such threshold, (close-open)/shorter ATR above a negative tolerance increasing with volatility, and (close-low)/(high-low) at or above a threshold. Bearish absorption uses d > 0, resistance at or below the negative threshold, body/ATR below the positive tolerance, and close location at or below the complementary threshold. Use the source's zero-range and unavailable-ATR fallbacks.
Calculation: Acceptance and aggression = Acceptance score is a clamped weighted sum of range compression, one minus flow intensity, one minus abs(trend), and one minus abs(momentum); range compression is one minus the clamped ratio of (high-low)/shorter ATR to an affine function of volatility. A bullish acceptance occurs after the latest bullish absorption and within an adaptive window increasing with noise and decreasing with volatility, when acceptance score reaches a threshold increasing with noise, low stays above the absorption low minus an ATR tolerance increasing with volatility, and close stays at or above that absorption low. Bearish acceptance uses the latest bearish absorption, high below its high plus tolerance, and close at or below its high. Bullish aggression requires a bullish acceptance after the latest bullish absorption and before the current bar, the absorption still within the adaptive window, close above that absorption's high, flow above a positive threshold, a bullish aggression score at or above its adaptive threshold, and (close-open)/(high-low) at or above a volatility-dependent threshold. The aggression score is a clamped weighted sum of flow intensity, abs(signed impact), positive persistence, positive momentum and positive trend; its threshold increases with noise and decreases with volatility. Bearish aggression reverses directions, uses negative persistence, momentum and trend magnitudes, and compares close with the absorption low.
Calculation: Exhaustion = Bullish exhaustion requires signed decay at or above a threshold increasing with noise and abs(trend), and close location within the bar at or above a threshold. Bearish exhaustion requires signed decay at or below the negative threshold and close location at or below the complementary threshold. Class priority is bullish aggression, bearish aggression, bullish absorption, bearish absorption, bullish exhaustion, then bearish exhaustion.
Calculation: Gap event = A two-bar CVD gap exists when the current CVD low exceeds the previous CVD high OR the current CVD high is below the previous CVD low. A three-bar CVD gap exists when all three bars share the same anchor AND the current CVD low exceeds the CVD high two bars earlier OR the current CVD high is below the CVD low two bars earlier. Gap direction need not agree with class direction. The preceding source bar's start, midpoint or end must fall inside an enabled weekday session, or all three must fall outside every defined session when outside-session events are enabled. For absorption and aggression zones, suppress an event if the preceding source bar's price range has already been fully consumed after its close and before confirmation, or its remaining range is contained in an active higher-timeframe zone of the same signed class and gap kind. To determine consumption, a bullish zone arms after a chart bar is entirely above it, disarms after a complete jump below it, and on an armed overlap contracts its top to the bar low or is consumed if the low reaches its bottom; a bearish zone reverses these comparisons. A new higher-timeframe zone removes contained active lower-timeframe zones of the same signed class and gap kind.
Long: Primary source CVD close >= primary source CVD open.
Short: Primary source CVD close < primary source CVD open.
Long: Flow lane intensity >= its minimum AND flow lane > its positive threshold.
Short: Flow lane intensity >= its minimum AND flow lane < its negative threshold.
Long: Signed response > its positive directional threshold.
Short: Signed response < its negative directional threshold.
Long: Structure conflict score < its divergence threshold AND trend and momentum do not meet the opposing-sign transition condition AND structure > its positive threshold.
Short: Structure conflict score < its divergence threshold AND trend and momentum do not meet the opposing-sign transition condition AND structure < its negative threshold.
Long: Divergence direction > 0.
Short: Divergence direction < 0.
Long: Composite >= its adaptive threshold.
Short: Composite <= negative adaptive threshold.
Long: Gap event exists AND the preceding source bar's priority class is bullish absorption, bullish aggression or bullish exhaustion.
Short: Gap event exists AND the preceding source bar's priority class is bearish absorption, bearish aggression or bearish exhaustion.
