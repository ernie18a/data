Calculation: Direction-normalized bars = for long use the original OHLC; for short use open = -original open, high = -original low, low = -original high, close = -original close. Volume is unchanged. All calculations below use these bars unless stated otherwise. Index [i] means i completed chart bars ago; unindexed values are current. Use completed chart bars.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); body fraction = body / range if range > 0, otherwise 0. Minimum body = original, unnormalized current close times a body-size parameter.
Calculation: Downtrend = close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA AND (shorter EMA - shorter EMA[trend-slope lookback]) / (ATR * trend-slope lookback) < negative slope threshold. Both EMAs use close.
Calculation: Reversal patterns = the following conditions, evaluated in the listed priority order; a pattern qualifies only when its stated downtrend condition also holds. Engulfing: close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND close >= open[1] AND open <= close[1] AND body > engulfing multiplier * body[1] AND body > minimum body AND body[1] > minimum body AND downtrend[1]. Star: close[2] < open[2] AND body[2] > star body multiplier * minimum body AND body[1] / max(range[1], epsilon) < doji threshold AND close > open AND body > star body multiplier * minimum body AND close > (open[2] + close[2]) / 2 AND gap-down condition AND downtrend[2]; the gap-down condition is either high[1] < low[2] when true gaps are required, or open[1] < close[2] otherwise. Three-candle: close > open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[2] > open[2] AND close > close[1] AND close[1] > close[2] AND open <= close[1] AND open >= open[1] AND open[1] <= close[2] AND open[1] >= open[2] AND body > minimum body AND body[1] > minimum body AND body[2] > minimum body AND downtrend[3]. Hammer: body fraction <= hammer body-fraction limit AND lower wick >= hammer wick multiplier * ATR AND upper wick <= hammer opposite-wick multiplier * body AND range >= hammer range multiplier * ATR AND downtrend. Piercing: close[1] < open[1] AND body[1] > minimum body AND close > open AND opening-gap condition AND close > (open[1] + close[1]) / 2 AND close < open[1] AND body > minimum body AND downtrend[1]; the opening-gap condition is either open < low[1] when true gaps are required, or open < close[1] otherwise. Tweezer: close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND abs(low - low[1]) <= tweezer tolerance * ATR AND lower wick >= body AND downtrend.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of one point each for volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier; abs(low - latest confirmed pivot low) <= support tolerance * ATR; direction-normalized RSI < RSI threshold; ADX > ADX threshold; and low < lowest low over the divergence lookback ending at [1] AND direction-normalized RSI > lowest direction-normalized RSI over that same lookback. A pivot low uses equal left and right lookbacks and becomes available only after the right lookback completes. Direction-normalized RSI is RSI of original close for long and its complement for short.
Calculation: Pattern quality score = score of the first qualifying pattern in priority order. For an increasing two-tier test, award 2 points at or above the higher threshold, 1 at or above the lower threshold, otherwise 0; for a decreasing two-tier test, award 2 points at or below the lower threshold, 1 at or below the higher threshold, otherwise 0. Cap each pattern's total at 3. Engulfing: increasing two-tier score for body / max(body[1], epsilon), plus increasing two-tier score for (close - low) / range, using 0 when range is zero. Star: decreasing two-tier score for body[1] / max(range[1], epsilon), plus increasing two-tier score for (close - close[2]) / max(open[2] - close[2], epsilon). Three-candle: increasing two-tier score for the mean body fraction of current and prior two bars, plus 1 if the mean of their (close - low) / range values is at or above its threshold, using 0 for a zero-range bar. Hammer: increasing two-tier score for lower wick / max(ATR, epsilon), plus 1 if (min(open, close) - low) / range is at or above its threshold, using 0 when range is zero, plus 1 if upper wick <= body * wick-quality multiplier. Piercing: award 3, 2, 1, or 0 as (close - close[1]) / max(open[1] - close[1], epsilon) meets the highest, middle, lowest, or none of three ascending thresholds. Tweezer: decreasing two-tier score for abs(low - low[1]) / max(ATR, epsilon), plus 1 if lower wick / max(body, epsilon) >= wick-ratio threshold.
Calculation: Pattern volume score = increasing two-tier score for current volume divided by max(reference volume, epsilon), with 2 points at or above the higher threshold, 1 at or above the lower threshold, otherwise 0. Reference volume is previous-bar volume for engulfing, piercing, and tweezer; the mean volume of the previous two bars for star and three-candle; and current volume SMA for hammer. Use the reference for the first qualifying pattern in priority order.
Calculation: Strength = confluence score + pattern quality score + pattern volume score.
Long: at least one long-normalized reversal pattern qualifies AND long strength >= minimum strength threshold AND bars since the previous long signal > cooldown parameter.
Short: at least one short-normalized reversal pattern qualifies AND short strength >= minimum strength threshold AND bars since the previous short signal > cooldown parameter.
