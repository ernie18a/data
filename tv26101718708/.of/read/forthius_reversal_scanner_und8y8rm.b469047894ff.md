Calculation: Uptrend = close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND (fast EMA − its value three bars ago) / (ATR × 3) > positive slope threshold; Downtrend = close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND the same normalized slope < negative slope threshold. Fast and slow EMA use shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish reversal patterns = bullish engulfing (previous candle bearish, current bullish, current body engulfs previous body and exceeds it by the configured ratio, and both bodies exceed the configured minimum); hammer (body is at most the configured fraction of range, lower wick is at least the configured ATR multiple, upper wick is at most the configured fraction of body, and range is at least the configured ATR multiple); morning star (bearish first candle with body above the configured minimum multiple, middle candle body/range below the configured limit, bullish third candle with body above the configured minimum multiple and close above the first candle midpoint, and the configured gap condition); piercing pattern (previous bearish candle and current bullish candle, configured gap condition, current close above the previous midpoint but below its open, and both bodies exceed the configured minimum); three advancing candles (three bullish candles with rising closes, each open within the preceding body, and each body above the configured minimum); or bullish tweezer (previous bearish and current bullish, lows within the configured ATR tolerance, and current lower wick at least its body). Apply the corresponding downtrend context: previous bar for engulfing and piercing, two bars ago for morning star, three bars ago for three advancing candles, and current bar for hammer and tweezer.
Calculation: Bearish reversal patterns = bearish engulfing (previous candle bullish, current bearish, current body engulfs previous body and exceeds it by the configured ratio, and both bodies exceed the configured minimum); shooting star (body is at most the configured fraction of range, upper wick is at least the configured ATR multiple, lower wick is at most the configured fraction of body, and range is at least the configured ATR multiple); evening star (bullish first candle with body above the configured minimum multiple, middle candle body/range below the configured limit, bearish third candle with body above the configured minimum multiple and close below the first candle midpoint, and the configured gap condition); dark cloud pattern (previous bullish candle and current bearish candle, configured gap condition, current close below the previous midpoint but above its open, and both bodies exceed the configured minimum); three declining candles (three bearish candles with falling closes, each open within the preceding body, and each body above the configured minimum); or bearish tweezer (previous bullish and current bearish, highs within the configured ATR tolerance, and current upper wick at least its body). Apply the corresponding uptrend context: previous bar for engulfing and dark cloud, two bars ago for evening star, three bars ago for three declining candles, and current bar for shooting star and tweezer.
Calculation: Confluence score = count of: volume exceeds its moving average times the configured multiplier; current low is within the configured ATR distance of the latest confirmed pivot low (bullish) or current high is within that distance of the latest confirmed pivot high (bearish); RSI is below its configured bullish limit (bullish) or above its configured bearish limit (bearish); ADX exceeds its configured minimum; and bullish (low makes a lookback low while RSI does not) or bearish (high makes a lookback high while RSI does not) divergence.
Calculation: Pattern quality score = for engulfing, add a body-to-previous-body score and close-at-extreme score; for hammer/shooting star, add wick-to-ATR, body-position-in-range, and opposite-wick scores; for morning/evening star, add middle-candle doji and penetration scores; for piercing/dark cloud, use penetration score; for three-candle patterns, add average body/range and average close-at-extreme scores; for tweezers, add wick-to-body and distance-between-extremes scores. Each component awards the configured points at its configured thresholds, and each pattern score is capped at the configured maximum.
Calculation: Pattern volume score = score current volume relative to the 20-bar average for hammer/shooting star, relative to the previous bar for engulfing, piercing/dark cloud, and tweezer, or relative to the mean of the preceding two bars for star and three-candle patterns; award configured points at configured ratio thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish strength = quality score + bullish confluence score + applicable pattern volume score, selecting the first matching pattern in this order: engulfing, morning star, three advancing candles, hammer, piercing, tweezer. Bearish strength = corresponding bearish scores, selecting in this order: engulfing, evening star, three declining candles, shooting star, dark cloud, tweezer. Minimum strength is determined by the configured minimum grade.
Long: at least one bullish reversal pattern AND bullish strength >= configured minimum strength AND bar is confirmed when confirmation-only is enabled AND bars since the previous bullish signal > configured cooldown.
Short: at least one bearish reversal pattern AND bearish strength >= configured minimum strength AND bar is confirmed when confirmation-only is enabled AND bars since the previous bearish signal > configured cooldown.
