Calculation: Price-change Z-score = (close − close[1] − rolling mean of close − close[1]) / rolling standard deviation of close − close[1]; use the same lookback for mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the high and low of the most recent bearish candle within the impulse-origin scan; bearish order-block zone = the high and low of the most recent bullish candle within that scan. Use candle bodies unless full-wick zones are enabled.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; volume average = simple moving average of volume over a shorter lookback.
Long: Confirmed bar AND price-change Z-score > threshold AND close > open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (ATR-band filter disabled OR close ≤ long-term EMA + ATR-band multiplier × smoothed ATR) AND session filter passes AND close > highest close over the impulse-origin scan on prior bars
Long: Confirmed bar AND an active, unmitigated, unfired bullish order-block zone exists AND low is within the zone AND close > open AND close − low > zone height × rejection-move percentage AND (min(open, close) − low) / max(abs(close − open), (high − low) × 0.001) ≥ minimum wick-to-body ratio AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (ATR-band filter disabled OR close ≤ long-term EMA + ATR-band multiplier × smoothed ATR) AND session filter passes
Short: Confirmed bar AND price-change Z-score < −threshold AND close < open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (ATR-band filter disabled OR close ≥ long-term EMA − ATR-band multiplier × smoothed ATR) AND session filter passes AND close < lowest close over the impulse-origin scan on prior bars
Short: Confirmed bar AND an active, unmitigated, unfired bearish order-block zone exists AND high is within the zone AND close < open AND high − close > zone height × rejection-move percentage AND (high − max(open, close)) / max(abs(close − open), (high − low) × 0.001) ≥ minimum wick-to-body ratio AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (ATR-band filter disabled OR close ≥ long-term EMA − ATR-band multiplier × smoothed ATR) AND session filter passes
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
