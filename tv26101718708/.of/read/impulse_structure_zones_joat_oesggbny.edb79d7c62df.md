Calculation: Impulse z-score = (close change − SMA of close change) / standard deviation of close change over the same lookback; close change = close − previous close; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Directional filters = when enabled, require close above the long-term EMA of close for bullish decisions or below it for bearish decisions; when enabled, require close above session VWAP of (high + low) / 2 for bullish decisions or below it for bearish decisions; when enabled, require close at or below the long-term EMA plus a multiplier times the EMA of true range for bullish decisions, or at or above the long-term EMA minus that amount for bearish decisions. The two EMAs use the same length. When the session filter is enabled, the bar must fall within an enabled trading session; an enabled exclusion session always disqualifies the bar. Session times use a configurable UTC offset.
Calculation: Zones = on a bullish impulse, create a bullish zone from the most recent bearish candle within the origin lookback; on a bearish impulse, create a bearish zone from the most recent bullish candle within that lookback. If none exists, use the previous candle. Zone boundaries are either that candle's body or its full high-low range, as configured. A bullish impulse requires impulse z-score above its positive threshold AND close above open AND close above the highest close of the preceding origin-lookback bars AND bullish directional filters. A bearish impulse requires impulse z-score below the negative threshold AND close below open AND close below the lowest close of the preceding origin-lookback bars AND bearish directional filters. Process newly created zones on their creation bar. Before checking rejection, invalidate a zone when its age exceeds the maximum or, according to the configured mitigation method, bullish close or low is below its bottom, or bearish close or high is above its top. Each zone can produce only one rejection. After processing all zones on each bar, remove the oldest zones until the combined bullish and bearish zone count is within the configured maximum.
Long: Impulse z-score > positive threshold.
Short: Impulse z-score < negative threshold.
Long: An active, unrejected bullish zone exists AND low >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND close > open AND close − low > a configured fraction of zone height AND (min(open, close) − low) / max(abs(close − open), a small fraction of high − low) >= the minimum wick-to-body ratio AND bullish directional filters pass.
Short: An active, unrejected bearish zone exists AND high >= zone bottom AND high <= zone top AND close < open AND high − close > a configured fraction of zone height AND (high − max(open, close)) / max(abs(close − open), a small fraction of high − low) >= the minimum wick-to-body ratio AND bearish directional filters pass.
Calculation: Rejection trade stop distance = ATR on the rejection entry bar times a stop multiplier; the target distance is that stop distance times a reward-to-risk multiplier. The rejection entry price is that bar's close.
Exit: Long rejection trade, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance.
Exit: Short rejection trade, on a bar after entry, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price − target distance.
