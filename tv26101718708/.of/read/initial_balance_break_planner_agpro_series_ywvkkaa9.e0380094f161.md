Calculation: Initial balance = the maximum high and minimum low during the configured balance window; lock them when that window ends and reset all break, retest, acceptance, and failure states at the start of the next balance window. The midpoint is the average of the locked high and low, and the width is their difference floored at a positive price increment.
Calculation: ATR = the chart-timeframe ATR, falling back to current true range when unavailable and floored at a positive price increment. Break, retest, and failure buffers are separate adjustable multiples of current ATR.
Calculation: Normalize-high(x, weak, strong) = clamp(100 × (x − weak) / (strong − weak), 0, 100); return 0 if x is unavailable or the denominator is zero. Peak(x, low, ideal, high) = normalize-high(x, low, ideal) when x ≤ ideal, otherwise clamp(100 × (high − x) / (high − ideal), 0, 100); return 0 if x is unavailable.
Calculation: Width score = peak(initial balance width / current ATR, lower width bound, ideal width, upper width bound). The ideal and upper bounds vary with sensitivity.
Calculation: Volume score = normalize-high(current volume / SMA(volume), lower relative-volume bound, upper relative-volume bound); use relative volume of 1 if volume or its average is unavailable or the average is not positive.
Calculation: Volatility score = peak(current ATR / SMA(raw ATR), lower volatility bound, ideal volatility, upper volatility bound); use a ratio of 1 if the ATR average is unavailable or not positive. The ATR-average lookback is the greater of a multiple of the ATR lookback and the acceptance lookback.
Calculation: Close location = clamp((close − low) / max(high − low, positive price increment), 0, 1). Directional close location is close location for an upward break and 1 − close location for a downward break.
Calculation: Break quality = clamp(0.54 × normalize-high(break distance beyond the broken balance edge, break buffer at the break, an adjustable multiple of ATR at the break) + 0.46 × 100 × directional close location at the break, 0, 100); retain this value for the session.
Calculation: Retest held = after the break bar, an upward break has low ≤ broken edge + current retest buffer AND close ≥ broken edge − current failure buffer, or a downward break has high ≥ broken edge − current retest buffer AND close ≤ broken edge + current failure buffer; retain the first confirmed retest until the next balance window. Retest score is 100 if a retest has been held, otherwise 18 if price is back inside, otherwise 72 if close is outside the broken edge, otherwise 46. Back inside means close < broken edge − current failure buffer after an upward break or close > broken edge + current failure buffer after a downward break.
Calculation: Acceptance score = clamp(0.18 × width score + 0.30 × break quality + 0.20 × retest score + 0.17 × volume score + 0.15 × volatility score, 0, 100).
Calculation: Failure risk = clamp(34 if back inside, otherwise 0, plus 26 if close is beyond the balance midpoint against the break direction, otherwise 0, plus 12 if bars since break exceed the acceptance lookback, otherwise 0, plus 0.22 × (100 − 100 × directional close location) + 0.18 × (100 − volume score) + 0.12 × (100 − width score), 0, 100).
Long: The initial balance is locked AND the current bar is in the tracking session AND no break has occurred since the balance window began AND close > balance high + current break buffer.
Short: The initial balance is locked AND the current bar is in the tracking session AND no break has occurred since the balance window began AND close < balance low − current break buffer.
Long: The first break was upward AND at least one bar has elapsed since the break AND no retest has previously been held AND low ≤ broken edge + current retest buffer AND close ≥ broken edge − current failure buffer.
Short: The first break was downward AND at least one bar has elapsed since the break AND no retest has previously been held AND high ≥ broken edge − current retest buffer AND close ≤ broken edge + current failure buffer.
Long: The first break was upward AND no failure or acceptance has previously been recorded AND close > broken edge AND acceptance score ≥ its threshold AND failure risk ≤ its acceptance limit AND bars since break ≥ the confirmation requirement.
Short: The first break was downward AND no failure or acceptance has previously been recorded AND close < broken edge AND acceptance score ≥ its threshold AND failure risk ≤ its acceptance limit AND bars since break ≥ the confirmation requirement.
Exit: long, the first break was upward AND no failure has previously been recorded AND (close < balance midpoint OR (close < broken edge − current failure buffer AND failure risk ≥ its failure threshold)).
Exit: short, the first break was downward AND no failure has previously been recorded AND (close > balance midpoint OR (close > broken edge + current failure buffer AND failure risk ≥ its failure threshold)).
