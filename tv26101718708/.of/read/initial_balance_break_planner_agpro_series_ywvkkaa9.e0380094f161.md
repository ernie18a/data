Calculation: IB High and IB Low = highest high and lowest low during the configured Initial Balance session, held fixed after that session ends.
Calculation: IB Range = IB High − IB Low.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe over the configured short lookback.
Calculation: Break Buffer, Retest Buffer, Failure Buffer = ATR multiplied by their respective configured factors.
Calculation: Relative Volume Score = current volume divided by its moving average over the configured lookback, normalized so stronger relative volume scores higher.
Calculation: Volatility Fit = current ATR divided by its moving average over a longer lookback (at least three times the ATR lookback), scored highest near the configured ideal ratio and lower as the ratio moves away from it.
Calculation: IB Width Score = IB Range divided by ATR, scored highest within the configured ideal width range and lower outside it.
Calculation: Break Close Quality = a weighted score of distance beyond the broken IB edge (normalized from the break buffer to a configured larger distance) and close location within the bar; for a downside break, use the inverse close location.
Calculation: Retest Score = maximum when a retest has held; otherwise use the configured lower score for a return beyond the failure buffer, an intermediate score while price remains beyond the edge, or a neutral score.
Calculation: Acceptance Score = weighted sum of IB Width Score, Break Close Quality, Retest Score, Relative Volume Score, and Volatility Fit.
Calculation: Failure Risk = capped weighted sum of close weakness relative to the break side, participation weakness, and IB Width Score weakness, plus configured penalties for closing beyond the failure buffer back inside the IB, closing beyond the IB midpoint against the break, and exceeding the acceptance lookback after the break.
Long: confirmed bar during the tracking session after the IB session ends, no earlier break this session, and close > IB High + Break Buffer OR, after a first upside break, a later confirmed bar retests the IB High (low ≤ IB High + Retest Buffer and close ≥ IB High − Failure Buffer), has not already recorded a held retest, and has not closed beyond the failure buffer back inside OR, after a first upside break, close > IB High, bars since break ≥ configured confirmation bars, Acceptance Score ≥ configured minimum, Failure Risk ≤ configured maximum, and the break has not failed or already been marked accepted.
Short: confirmed bar during the tracking session after the IB session ends, no earlier break this session, and close < IB Low − Break Buffer OR, after a first downside break, a later confirmed bar retests the IB Low (high ≥ IB Low − Retest Buffer and close ≤ IB Low + Failure Buffer), has not already recorded a held retest, and has not closed beyond the failure buffer back inside OR, after a first downside break, close < IB Low, bars since break ≥ configured confirmation bars, Acceptance Score ≥ configured minimum, Failure Risk ≤ configured maximum, and the break has not failed or already been marked accepted.
Exit: long, after an upside break, close < IB midpoint OR (close < IB High − Failure Buffer AND Failure Risk ≥ configured failure threshold).
Exit: short, after a downside break, close > IB midpoint OR (close > IB Low + Failure Buffer AND Failure Risk ≥ configured failure threshold).
