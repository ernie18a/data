Calculation: VWAP bands = session-anchored VWAP of the selected price source plus or minus a multiplier times the rolling standard deviation of that source.
Calculation: Exit distances = fixed take-profit and stop-loss distances, or ATR at entry times a multiplier for both distances, or the smaller of that ATR distance and a percentage of entry price for both distances; hold the distances fixed after entry.
Long: The selected entry mode is enabled AND close crosses above VWAP, the upper VWAP band, or the lower VWAP band, as selected AND bar close time is within the selected entry window, inclusive of its start and exclusive of its end, including overnight windows AND the bar's weekday is an enabled Sunday through Friday AND long trading is enabled AND, if enabled, RSI of close is below its oversold threshold AND, if enabled, close is above EMA of close AND, if enabled, the MACD line is above its signal line and its histogram is above zero.
Short: The selected entry mode is enabled AND close crosses above VWAP, below the lower VWAP band, or below the upper VWAP band, as selected AND bar close time is within the selected entry window, inclusive of its start and exclusive of its end, including overnight windows AND the bar's weekday is an enabled Sunday through Friday AND short trading is enabled AND, if enabled, RSI of close is above its overbought threshold AND, if enabled, close is below EMA of close AND, if enabled, the MACD line is below its signal line and its histogram is below zero.
Exit: Long, exit strategy is enabled AND the selected fixed-distance, ATR, or capped-ATR exit is active AND high is at or above entry price plus the take-profit distance OR low is at or below entry price minus the stop-loss distance.
Exit: Short, exit strategy is enabled AND the selected fixed-distance, ATR, or capped-ATR exit is active AND low is at or below entry price minus the take-profit distance OR high is at or above entry price plus the stop-loss distance.
Exit: Long, exit strategy is enabled AND close crosses below VWAP, below the upper VWAP band, or above the lower VWAP band, as selected.
Exit: Short, exit strategy is enabled AND close crosses above VWAP, below the upper VWAP band, or above the lower VWAP band, as selected.
Exit: Long OR short, automatic close is enabled AND bar close time is within the selected one-minute closing window.
