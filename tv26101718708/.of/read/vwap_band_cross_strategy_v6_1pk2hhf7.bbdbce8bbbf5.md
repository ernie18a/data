Calculation: Upper band = VWAP + (standard deviation of the VWAP source × band multiplier); lower band = VWAP − (standard deviation of the VWAP source × band multiplier)
Long: (entry mode = Price Over VWAP AND close crosses above VWAP) OR (entry mode = Price Over Upper Band AND close crosses above upper band) OR (entry mode = Price Over Lower Band AND close crosses above lower band); AND entry-time window; AND selected trading day; AND daily limits not reached; AND daily trade limit not reached; AND long confirmations enabled (RSI below oversold threshold, close above EMA, MACD line above signal line and histogram above zero); AND trade direction allows long
Short: (entry mode = Price Under VWAP AND close crosses above VWAP) OR (entry mode = Price Under Lower Band AND close crosses below lower band) OR (entry mode = Price Under Upper Band AND close crosses below upper band); AND entry-time window; AND selected trading day; AND daily limits not reached; AND daily trade limit not reached; AND short confirmations enabled (RSI above overbought threshold, close below EMA, MACD line below signal line and histogram below zero); AND trade direction allows short
Exit: long, exit mode = VWAP Cross AND close crosses below VWAP OR exit mode = Price Cross Under Upper Band AND close crosses below upper band OR exit mode = Price Cross Over Lower Band AND close crosses above lower band OR fixed-tick, ATR, or capped-ATR target or stop is reached OR enabled daily PnL limit is reached OR enabled close-all time window
Exit: short, exit mode = VWAP Cross AND close crosses above VWAP OR exit mode = Price Cross Under Upper Band AND close crosses below upper band OR exit mode = Price Cross Over Lower Band AND close crosses above lower band OR fixed-tick, ATR, or capped-ATR target or stop is reached OR enabled daily PnL limit is reached OR enabled close-all time window
