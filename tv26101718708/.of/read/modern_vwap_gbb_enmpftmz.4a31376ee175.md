Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of (high + low + close) / 3 since the selected calendar-period boundary or confirmed swing pivot; initialize at the first bar. A swing pivot is a high strictly greater than, or a low strictly less than, all highs or lows respectively in equal-length windows on both sides. On confirmation, rebuild from the pivot bar through the current bar. VWAP and deviation are unavailable when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus a configurable deviation multiplier times the anchored deviation; optionally scale the multiplier by 1 + a configurable weight × (1 - Kaufman efficiency ratio).
Calculation: Regime = ranging when Kaufman efficiency ratio is less than or equal to its trailing median, trending when it is greater; undefined until both that median and the trailing median of ATR / close are available. Medians include the current bar and require a complete window.
Calculation: Side context = above when at least a configurable minimum number of the preceding lookback bars closed above their respective anchored VWAP; otherwise below when at least that minimum number did not close above it; otherwise neutral. Context is neutral until at least that lookback has elapsed since the latest anchor confirmation; an unavailable VWAP counts as not above.
Calculation: Continuation episode = for each side, cancel its pending episode when VWAP is unavailable or side context no longer matches. While context is above and low <= VWAP, start or extend the long deadline to the current bar plus a configurable hold window; while context is below and high >= VWAP, do the same for the short deadline. A pending episode triggers when its deadline has not passed and close is strictly on its context side of VWAP, including on the touch bar; then consume it regardless of regime. Process touches before triggers on each bar.
Long: Regime is ranging AND previous close < previous lower band AND current close >= current lower band AND current close <= current upper band.
Short: Regime is ranging AND previous close > previous upper band AND current close <= current upper band AND current close >= current lower band.
Long: Regime is trending AND a long continuation episode triggers.
Short: Regime is trending AND a short continuation episode triggers.
