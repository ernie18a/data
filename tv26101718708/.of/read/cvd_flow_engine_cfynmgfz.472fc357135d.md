Calculation: CVD delta = sum, over completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, of lower-timeframe volume multiplied by sign of that bar's close minus the preceding lower-timeframe close; use zero when the sum is unavailable.
Calculation: CVD balance = cumulative sum of CVD delta from the start of available data; CVD acceleration = current CVD delta minus preceding CVD delta, using zero when the difference is unavailable. Select CVD balance, delta, or acceleration as the CVD source.
Calculation: CVD retention candle = close at the selected CVD source, open at the preceding source value or the current value if unavailable, wick size equal to an EMA of the absolute close-to-open difference multiplied by a wick factor, high equal to the greater of open and close plus wick size, and low equal to the lesser of open and close minus wick size.
Calculation: CVD EMA slope = 100 times the change in the CVD source EMA divided by the greater of the absolute preceding EMA and 1; use zero if the preceding EMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Strength state = strong bull if the CVD source is above its EMA, CVD EMA slope is above its positive threshold, and the source is above its value at the momentum lookback; otherwise strong bear if the source is below its EMA, slope is below the negative threshold, and the source is below its value at the momentum lookback; otherwise weak bull if the source is above its EMA and slope is at or below the positive threshold; otherwise weak bear if the source is below its EMA and slope is at or above the negative threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Flow extension bands = for each of a standard CVD EMA and a longer-term CVD EMA, separately collect positive distances from confirmed CVD retention-candle pivot highs above the EMA at the pivot and from the EMA at the pivot to confirmed retention-candle pivot lows below it. For each side, after a minimum number of samples, select the running average, an event-by-event EMA of the distances, or a weighted average of both; add the selected upper distance to the corresponding current EMA and subtract the selected lower distance from it, then optionally smooth each resulting band with an EMA. The standard and longer-term bands have independently configurable pivot, sample, adaptive, weighting, and smoothing parameters.
Calculation: CVD Z-score = the selected CVD source minus its rolling SMA, divided by its rolling standard deviation; undefined when the standard deviation is zero.
Long: CVD retention close >= its EMA AND (retention high >= its high at the structure lookback OR retention low >= its low at the structure lookback).
Long: CVD retention close < its EMA AND retention high > its high at the structure lookback AND retention low > its low at the structure lookback.
Short: CVD retention close < its EMA AND (retention high <= its high at the structure lookback OR retention low <= its low at the structure lookback).
Short: CVD retention close > its EMA AND retention high < its high at the structure lookback AND retention low < its low at the structure lookback.
Long: CVD EMA slope > positive slope threshold.
Short: CVD EMA slope < negative slope threshold.
Long: strength state is strong bull or weak bull AND (every confirmed bar mode is selected OR strength state differs from the preceding bar).
Short: strength state is strong bear or weak bear AND (every confirmed bar mode is selected OR strength state differs from the preceding bar).
Long: a price pivot low is confirmed AND its low is below the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND the CVD source at the current pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot.
Short: a price pivot high is confirmed AND its high is above the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND the CVD source at the current pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot.
Long: CVD source <= standard lower flow extension band OR CVD source <= longer-term lower flow extension band.
Short: CVD source >= standard upper flow extension band OR CVD source >= longer-term upper flow extension band.
Long: CVD Z-score <= negative Z-score threshold AND (every confirmed bar mode is selected OR the positive-extreme or negative-extreme status differs from the preceding bar).
Short: CVD Z-score >= positive Z-score threshold AND (every confirmed bar mode is selected OR the positive-extreme or negative-extreme status differs from the preceding bar).
