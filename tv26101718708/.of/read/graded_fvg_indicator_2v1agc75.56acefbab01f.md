Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; zone spans that earlier high to current low. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; zone spans current high to the low two bars earlier.
Calculation: FVG grade = weighted score from displacement, structure, relative volume, gap size, and context. Displacement uses the middle candle’s body, body-to-range ratio, directional close location, and three-candle leg, each normalized by ATR from before the middle candle. Structure scores a break of the latest intact three-bars-per-side swing or the prior range, with a bonus for a narrow prior range; without a break, it scores continuation aligned with the last swing break. Relative volume = middle-candle volume / volume SMA ending before that candle. Gap size = zone height / ATR from before the middle candle. Context scores low overlap with the zone among bars before the first candle and a zone midpoint near or beyond the edge of the prior context range. Combine the factors with the indicator’s weights; when volume is unavailable or ignored, reweight the other factors to the full score. Apply the displacement and gap-size gates to cap the grade tier.
Long: A bullish FVG meets the selected display grade threshold; if it is an inversion FVG, it originated as a bearish FVG, price closed above its far edge, the selected break requirement is met, and the resulting grade meets the display threshold.
Short: A bearish FVG meets the selected display grade threshold; if it is an inversion FVG, it originated as a bullish FVG, price closed below its far edge, the selected break requirement is met, and the resulting grade meets the display threshold.
