Calculation: Ramp(x, lower, upper) = clamp((x − lower) / (upper − lower), 0, 1); if lower = upper, return 1 when x ≥ upper, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Gap = on a completed chart bar, bullish when low > high two bars ago, with top = low and bottom = high two bars ago; bearish when high < low two bars ago, with top = low two bars ago and bottom = high. Its direction is +1 for bullish and −1 for bearish. Use ATR two bars ago, falling back to current ATR when unavailable, as the reference ATR; gap size = (top − bottom) / reference ATR, or zero if reference ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Displacement score = weighted sum of ramps of directional body / reference ATR, directional body / previous bar range, previous close's directional position in its range, and max(direction × (close − open two bars ago), 0) / reference ATR. Directional body = max(direction × (previous close − previous open), 0); directional close position = (previous close − previous low) / previous range for bullish gaps and (previous high − previous close) / previous range for bearish gaps. A ratio with a nonpositive denominator is zero.
Calculation: Structure score = when a directional swing break occurred on the current or previous bar OR the current close breaks the directional extreme of a lookback ending two bars ago, the capped sum of a swing-break weight, a range-break weight, and a compression weight multiplied by one minus the ramp of the high-low range of a shorter lookback ending two bars ago divided by reference ATR. Include each break weight only when its break occurred. Otherwise use a continuation score when the most recent swing-break direction as of two bars ago equals the gap direction, and zero otherwise. A swing is a confirmed pivot with equal numbers of bars on both sides; track the latest high and low separately, replacing each when a new pivot is confirmed. An intact swing breaks once when close strictly exceeds its high or falls strictly below its low; record the break bar and direction. If reference ATR is not positive, use a large fallback compression ratio.
Calculation: Volume score = ramp(previous volume / SMA of volume ending two bars ago) when volume is enabled and both previous volume and that average are positive; otherwise volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Size score = zero below a noise threshold; ramp from zero to one between the noise threshold and a lower ideal-size threshold; one through an upper ideal-size threshold; above that, decrease linearly using a capped ramp to a minimum score at a larger size threshold.
Calculation: Context score = weighted sum of freshness and edge. Freshness = one minus the ramp of the fraction of an available context lookback, starting three bars ago, whose high-low ranges overlap the gap; use zero traffic when no bars are available. Edge = min(1, 2 × absolute value of (gap midpoint's position in the context-lookback high-low range ending two bars ago − 0.5)); use position 0.5 when that range has zero height.
Calculation: Gap grade = weighted sum of displacement, structure, size and context scores, plus volume score multiplied by a ramp of their normalized weighted sum when volume is available; otherwise rescale the weighted sum of those four scores to the full grade scale. Classify the grade using ordered high and medium thresholds. Cap the classification at medium when either the displacement score or gap size fails its respective gate, and at low when both fail. A gap passes the display filter when its capped classification meets the selected minimum classification.
Calculation: Active zones = store displayed gaps with their direction, bounds, grade, classification, inversion status and fresh status. Before processing new gaps on each completed bar, update existing zones; do not test a zone on its creation or flip bar. A fresh support zone is first tested when low < its top; a fresh resistance zone is first tested when high > its bottom. A support zone closes through when close < its bottom; a resistance zone closes through when close > its top. Retire a closed-through zone unless it qualifies for inversion. Under the selected alternative mitigation rule, also retire a support zone when low ≤ its bottom or close < its midpoint, respectively, and a resistance zone when high ≥ its top or close > its midpoint, respectively. A close-through takes precedence over mitigation. An inverted zone cannot invert again. Process existing zones newest first. After updates, merge a displayed new gap into the newest overlapping non-inverted zone of the same direction when overlap / smaller zone height meets the enabled merge threshold; replace the bounds by their union, retain the better grade and classification, reset the zone to fresh and make it newest. Otherwise create a fresh zone. After insertions and inversions, enforce separate support and resistance zone caps by removing the oldest zone on an over-cap side.
Calculation: Inversion = a closed-through non-inverted active zone flips direction when inversion is enabled AND the selected break requirement passes AND its new classification meets the display filter. The break requirement is either unrestricted close-through, directional breaking body / current ATR ≥ a threshold, or a new gap in the opposite direction on the breaking bar. Directional breaking body = max((close − open) × new direction, 0), and its ATR ratio is zero when current ATR is not positive. New grade = old grade × (base factor + strength factor × ramp(directional breaking body / current ATR)); classify it using the high and medium grade thresholds and cap its classification at the old classification. A flipped zone retains its bounds, becomes fresh and becomes the newest zone.
Long: A bullish gap passes the display filter.
Long: A fresh active support zone is first tested.
Long: An active bearish gap zone closes through AND qualifies for inversion.
Short: A bearish gap passes the display filter.
Short: A fresh active resistance zone is first tested.
Short: An active bullish gap zone closes through AND qualifies for inversion.
