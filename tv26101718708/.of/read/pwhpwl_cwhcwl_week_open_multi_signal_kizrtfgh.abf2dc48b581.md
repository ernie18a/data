Calculation: Range = high and low of an aggregated higher-timeframe bar; use the last completed bar for confirmed decisions and the bar still forming for preview decisions. Record the chart bar of the first occurrence of each extreme.
Calculation: Midpoint = (Range high + Range low) / 2.
Calculation: Reference opens = opens of two still-forming higher-timeframe bars, both longer than the Range timeframe, one longer than the other. For confirmed decisions, use their values on the last chart bar of the completed Range; for preview decisions, use their current values.
Calculation: Eligible round level = the sole integer multiple of a configurable price increment within the Range, inclusive; undefined if the Range contains zero or more than one such multiple.
Long: (a new Range begins AND the last completed Range high > its low AND the first occurrence of its low precedes the first occurrence of its high AND (either Reference open or the Eligible round level >= Range low AND that same level <= Midpoint)) OR (the current chart bar falls on the specified preview weekday AND the forming Range high > its low AND the first occurrence of its low precedes the first occurrence of its high AND (either Reference open or the Eligible round level >= Range low AND that same level <= Midpoint)).
Short: (a new Range begins AND the last completed Range high > its low AND the first occurrence of its high precedes the first occurrence of its low AND (either Reference open or the Eligible round level >= Midpoint AND that same level <= Range high)) OR (the current chart bar falls on the specified preview weekday AND the forming Range high > its low AND the first occurrence of its high precedes the first occurrence of its low AND (either Reference open or the Eligible round level >= Midpoint AND that same level <= Range high)).
