Calculation: Source bars = completed chart bars or last completed bars of either of two distinct higher timeframes; process each higher-timeframe bar once at the first chart-bar close after it completes.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same number of source bars on each side; use independent shorter and longer swing-confirmation lengths. Each newly confirmed swing replaces the previous swing of the same type and scale and becomes unused. A swing cannot be broken on its confirmation bar. A break consumes that swing. If an unused swing high and low are both broken on the same bar, consume both and produce no direction.
Calculation: Origin zone = on a structure break, find the nearest earlier opposite-direction candle between the break and the broken swing's origin, within a search lookback. Demand spans that candle's low to its upper body edge; supply spans its lower body edge to its high. Require positive width, no subsequent source bar through the break bar to close or wick strictly beyond the far edge, according to the selected invalidation method, and optionally require origin volume divided by the average volume of preceding source bars to be at least a threshold. Do not substitute an older candle if the nearest candidate fails. Retain valid zones until a later completed source bar breaks the far edge by the selected method; optionally reverse their side once on the first break and remove them on the next. Deduplicate unflipped zones with the same source timeframe, origin and side; retain only the newest configured maximum per timeframe.
Calculation: Fair value gap = the interval between the first and third bars of three source bars when the third low is strictly above the first high, or the third high is strictly below the first low. Optionally require consecutive timestamps. Gap width must be at least the larger of a price-width threshold and a configured multiple of source ATR. Retain each gap until a later completed source bar's wick reaches the selected near edge, midpoint or far edge; retain only the newest configured maximum per timeframe.
Calculation: Session level = high or low of a completed, fully aligned configured session, opening range, trading day, week or month, calculated from chart bars. Skip the first partial window and windows with missing or misaligned boundaries. Publish at the chart-bar close that detects the window ending. Remove a level when its age exceeds its retention limit, the storage limit evicts it, or a completed chart bar closes or wicks strictly beyond the level plus tolerance for a high or minus tolerance for a low, according to the selected mitigation method. Evaluate mitigation before reactions, including on the publication bar.
Calculation: Level reaction state = independently for each retained level and each configured confirmation timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, use completed confirmation bars whose opening time is at or after publication; process a higher-timeframe bar once at the first following chart-bar close. Let sign be +1 for a high level and -1 for a low level; beyond = sign × (confirmation close − level), before = sign × (previous confirmation close − level), and penetration = confirmation high − level for a high or level − confirmation low for a low. Touch means high >= level − touch band AND low <= level + touch band. Initially no break is pending and the sweep is armed. A break occurs when no break is pending AND beyond > close buffer AND before <= close buffer; start a pending break with age zero and arm the sweep. On each subsequent confirmation bar increment age; if age exceeds the observation limit, end the pending break. Otherwise, if beyond < −close buffer, mark failure and end the pending break; otherwise, if the break has not yet held AND touch AND beyond > close buffer, mark a held retest and keep the break pending. When no break is pending and no break occurs, a reclaim occurs if the sweep is armed AND penetration > penetration threshold AND beyond < −close buffer AND before <= 0; disarm the sweep. Rearm whenever touch is false AND beyond < −close buffer.
Long: Source close > latest unused confirmed swing high AND the swing was confirmed before the current source bar AND the same source bar does not also break the latest unused confirmed swing low.
Short: Source close < latest unused confirmed swing low AND the swing was confirmed before the current source bar AND the same source bar does not also break the latest unused confirmed swing high.
Long: A valid demand origin zone is created OR an existing supply origin zone is broken and reverses to demand.
Short: A valid supply origin zone is created OR an existing demand origin zone is broken and reverses to supply.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is formed.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is formed.
Long: A high session level has a break or held retest OR a low session level has a reclaim or failed break.
Short: A low session level has a break or held retest OR a high session level has a reclaim or failed break.
