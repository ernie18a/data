Calculation: Bull and Bear CISD levels = On each of two higher-timeframe streams, using only completed bars, maintain body-high and body-low structure boundaries, pullback flags and anchors, candidate levels, delivery state, and active level. A candle body is the interval between open and close. A valid pullback starts when the prior candle is bullish or bearish, its body is at least a configurable fraction of the body two bars back (which must be nonzero), and the prior candle’s direction is opposite the pullback direction; initialize that pullback’s anchor to the prior open. While active, update the bullish-pullback anchor to the lower of its current value and each open below it, or to a bearish candle’s open when that open is above it; update the bearish-pullback anchor symmetrically to the higher of its current value and each open above it, or to a bullish candle’s open when that open is below it. A bearish structure break occurs when the current body low is below the stored body-low boundary; a bullish structure break occurs when the current body high is above the stored body-high boundary. On a bearish break, if a bearish pullback is active and its anchor index is not the current bar, set the top boundary to the greater body high of the anchor candle and its next older candle and record the pullback’s top anchor as a Bull CISD candidate; otherwise, if the prior candle is bullish and current candle bearish, set the top boundary to the prior body high and record the bearish-pullback top anchor as a Bull CISD candidate. On a bullish break, apply the symmetric rule using an active bullish pullback or a prior bearish/current bullish pair, the lesser body lows, and the bullish-pullback bottom anchor to record a Bear CISD candidate. Update pullback anchors and boundaries as described; retain the most recently recorded candidate of each direction. Initially, the first Bull candidate sets bearish delivery and the active Bull level; otherwise the first Bear candidate sets bullish delivery and the active Bear level. In bearish delivery, a new Bull candidate replaces the active Bull level; a confirmed close above it changes delivery to bullish and activates the stored Bear candidate if available. In bullish delivery, a new Bear candidate replaces the active Bear level; a confirmed close below it changes delivery to bearish and activates the stored Bull candidate if available. If the opposite candidate is unavailable after a change, there is no active level until one becomes available. Evaluate each higher-timeframe stream independently.
Long: Bearish delivery AND confirmed higher-timeframe close > active Bull CISD level
Short: Bullish delivery AND confirmed higher-timeframe close < active Bear CISD level
