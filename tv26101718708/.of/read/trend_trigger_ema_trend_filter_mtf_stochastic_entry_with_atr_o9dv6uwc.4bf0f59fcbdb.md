Calculation: K = SMA(raw stochastic of close within the high-low range over a lookback L, short smoothing); D = SMA(K, short smoothing)
Calculation: mtfK, mtfD = linear regression value at the current bar (lookback L) of K and of D respectively, with K and D computed on bars of a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar as of the current chart bar
Calculation: TrendUp = fast EMA(close) > slow EMA(close); TrendDown = fast EMA(close) < slow EMA(close); fast length < slow length
Calculation: StopLevel (ATR = Wilder ATR on chart timeframe; R = ATR at the entry bar x initial-stop multiple, fixed for the trade; entry price = close of the signal bar). Long: initial StopLevel = entry - R. Short: initial StopLevel = entry + R. At each bar close after entry, compute currentR = (close - entry)/R for long or (entry - close)/R for short. If currentR >= trailR: long StopLevel = max(StopLevel, close - ATR x trail multiple); short StopLevel = min(StopLevel, close + ATR x trail multiple). Else if currentR >= breakevenR (breakevenR < trailR): long StopLevel = max(StopLevel, entry); short StopLevel = min(StopLevel, entry). Otherwise StopLevel is unchanged. The updated StopLevel applies from the next bar.
Long: mtfK crosses above 50 (previous mtfK <= 50 and current mtfK > 50) AND K > 50 AND K > previous bar K AND K > D AND mtfK > mtfD AND TrendUp (trend filter, optional)
Short: mtfD crosses below 50 (previous mtfD >= 50 and current mtfD < 50) AND K < 50 AND K < previous bar K AND K < D AND mtfK < mtfD AND TrendDown (trend filter, optional)
Exit: long, low <= StopLevel OR mtfD crosses below a high threshold above 50 (previous mtfD >= threshold and current mtfD < threshold) OR TrendDown OR bars since entry >= N
Exit: short, high >= StopLevel OR mtfK crosses above a low threshold below 50 (previous mtfK <= threshold and current mtfK > threshold) OR TrendUp OR bars since entry >= N
