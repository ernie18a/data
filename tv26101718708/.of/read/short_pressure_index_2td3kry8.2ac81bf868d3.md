Calculation: Short Pressure Index (SPI) = EMA of the equal-weight average of (bearish high-volume share over a rolling window, 0–100 rolling normalization of (moving average − close) / ATR, and percentage of bearish bars over a lookback); high volume means volume exceeds its average by a multiplier
Calculation: Signal = EMA of SPI, with a longer lookback than the SPI smoothing
Short: SPI crosses above the high-pressure threshold OR SPI crosses above Signal
Long: SPI crosses below the low-pressure threshold
Exit: short, SPI crosses below the high-pressure threshold OR SPI crosses below Signal
