Calculation: body = abs(close - open) of a bar
Long: (on chart-timeframe bars, last completed bar = bar 0, previous = bar 1, the one before = bar 2) bar 2 is bearish (close < open) AND bar 1 is bearish AND body(bar 1) < a fraction (<1) of body(bar 2) AND bar 0 is bullish (close > open) AND close(bar 0) > midpoint of open and close of bar 1; OR the same three-bar pattern evaluated on consecutive bars of a timeframe lower than the chart timeframe, taken within the chart bar that is still forming
Short: (on chart-timeframe bars, bars 0, 1, 2 as above) bar 2 is bullish (close > open) AND bar 1 is bullish AND body(bar 1) < a fraction (<1) of body(bar 2) AND bar 0 is bearish (close < open) AND close(bar 0) < midpoint of open and close of bar 1; OR the same three-bar pattern evaluated on consecutive bars of a timeframe lower than the chart timeframe, taken within the chart bar that is still forming
Exit: long, price < lowest low of the lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar in which the long signal occurred (as of entry) OR price >= highest high of those lower-timeframe bars (as of entry)
