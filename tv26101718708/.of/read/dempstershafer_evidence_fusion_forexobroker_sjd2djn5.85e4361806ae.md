Calculation: Evidence strengths = [tanh((EMA(close) - EMA(close) from the EMA-slope lookback bars ago) / (ATR × EMA-slope lookback)), (RSI(close) - 50) / 50, tanh((close - previous close) / ATR), tanh((volume - previous volume) / standard deviation of volume × sign(close - previous close) / 2), tanh((current high-low range / SMA(high-low range) - 1) × sign(close - previous close)), 2 × (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) - 1]. Use zero for the first and third strengths when ATR is nonpositive, for the fourth when volume standard deviation is nonpositive, for the fifth when average range is nonpositive, and for the sixth when highest high equals lowest low.
Calculation: Fused masses = for each evidence strength s, form (L,S,H) = (max(s,0) × abs(s), max(-s,0) × abs(s), 1 - abs(s)). Combine the six triplets in their listed order: for triplets a and b, set d = max(1 - a.L × b.S - a.S × b.L, 1e-10), c.L = (a.L × b.L + a.L × b.H + a.H × b.L) / d, c.S = (a.S × b.S + a.S × b.H + a.H × b.S) / d, and c.H = a.H × b.H / d; divide each c component by max(c.L + c.S + c.H, 1e-10) before combining with the next triplet.
Calculation: Signal state = initially flat, with the last signal bar index initially zero; after a gated long or short signal, set the state to that direction and the last signal bar index to the current bar index.
Long: fused long mass > threshold AND session restriction is disabled OR fused long mass > threshold AND current bar is in the configured session; signal state is flat or short AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND bar is closed.
Short: fused short mass > threshold AND (session restriction is disabled OR current bar is in the configured session) AND signal state is flat or long AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND bar is closed.
Long: fused long mass > threshold.
Short: fused short mass > threshold AND fused long mass <= threshold.
Long: fused long mass crosses above threshold OR fused long mass - fused short mass crosses above zero OR fused long mass - fused short mass > zero.
Short: fused short mass crosses above threshold OR fused long mass - fused short mass crosses below zero OR fused long mass - fused short mass < zero.
