Calculation: Six signed strengths = tanh((EMA(close,long lookback) − its value long lookback bars ago)/(ATR × long lookback)); (RSI(close) − 50)/50; tanh((close − prior close)/ATR); tanh(((volume − prior volume)/stdev(volume, lookback)) × sign(close − prior close)/2); tanh(((high − low)/SMA(high − low, lookback) − 1) × sign(close − prior close)); and 2 × ((close − lowest low over lookback)/(highest high over lookback − lowest low over lookback)) − 1. For each strength s, assign Long mass max(s,0)|s|, Short mass max(−s,0)|s|, and Hold mass 1−|s|. Combine the six mass triplets in order by Dempster's rule: conflict K = Long₁×Short₂ + Short₁×Long₂; Long = (Long₁Long₂ + Long₁Hold₂ + Hold₁Long₂)/(1−K); Short = (Short₁Short₂ + Short₁Hold₂ + Hold₁Short₂)/(1−K); Hold = Hold₁Hold₂/(1−K); normalize each resulting triplet to sum to 1. ATR, EMA, RSI, standard deviation, SMA, and range lookbacks use the chart timeframe.
Long: combined Long mass > configured threshold AND position is not long AND bars since last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session)
Short: combined Short mass > configured threshold AND position is not short AND bars since last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session)
