Calculation: Special K engine and signal line = Special K momentum oscillator and its signal line, calculated from the selected price source using primary and secondary lengths
Calculation: Distance ratio = absolute difference between the engine and signal line divided by the moving average of that difference over a longer lookback
Calculation: Signal quality score = weighted combination of directional alignment, capped distance ratio relative to the extreme-stretch threshold, zero-line alignment, EMA alignment, and strong-distance bonus
Calculation: Stop distance = average true range multiplied by a configurable risk factor
Calculation: Target levels = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the stop distance multiplied by the configured reward-to-risk ratio; first and second targets are one-quarter and one-half of the final target distance
Long: engine < signal line AND distance ratio >= the mode's strong-stretch threshold AND (zero filter disabled OR engine < 0) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (HTF filter disabled OR selected higher-timeframe bias is bullish or neutral) AND signal quality score >= mode threshold AND no same-direction signal is already active AND bar is confirmed when bar-close confirmation is selected AND no active projection is waiting for its first target
Short: engine > signal line AND distance ratio >= the mode's strong-stretch threshold AND (zero filter disabled OR engine > 0) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (HTF filter disabled OR selected higher-timeframe bias is bearish or neutral) AND signal quality score >= mode threshold AND no same-direction signal is already active AND bar is confirmed when bar-close confirmation is selected AND no active projection is waiting for its first target
Exit: long, high >= final target OR low <= stop OR a new signal occurs after at least the first target was reached
Exit: short, low <= final target OR high >= stop OR a new signal occurs after at least the first target was reached
