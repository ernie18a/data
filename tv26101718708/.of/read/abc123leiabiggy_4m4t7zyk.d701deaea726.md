Calculation: Fresh high sweep = current high is above a high from a lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, and every chart-bar high in the preceding freshness lookback is at or below that same high. Fresh low sweep = current low is below a low from the same lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, and every chart-bar low in the preceding freshness lookback is at or above that same low. If multiple levels qualify, select the most recent higher-timeframe bar; if both directions qualify, select the high sweep.
Calculation: Setup = on a fresh sweep while idle, awaiting a break, entered, or invalidated, replace any existing setup. For a high sweep, find the most recent prior bearish chart bar within a scan lookback; the base is the lower low of that bar and the next bar, and the running extreme is the highest high from that bearish bar through the sweep bar. For a low sweep, find the most recent prior bullish chart bar within the scan lookback; the base is the higher high of that bar and the next bar, and the running extreme is the lowest low from that bullish bar through the sweep bar. If no such bar exists, remain idle. Starting on the next chart bar, update the running extreme with each bar's high for a high sweep or low for a low sweep. A high-sweep setup breaks when low < base; a low-sweep setup breaks when high > base. Freeze the running extreme on the break bar, set the midpoint to (base + extreme) / 2, and begin watching for entry on the next bar. Ignore new sweeps while watching for entry. On each watched bar, invalidate the high-sweep setup if low < base - invalidation multiplier × (extreme - base), or the low-sweep setup if high > base + invalidation multiplier × (base - extreme), before checking for entry. After entry or invalidation, wait for a fresh sweep.
Calculation: Entry level = for a high-sweep setup, the current low if low >= base, otherwise base; for a low-sweep setup, the current high if high <= base, otherwise base. Freeze the setup extreme at entry for use as the stop level.
Long: low-sweep setup is watching for entry AND high <= base + invalidation multiplier × (base - extreme) AND low <= midpoint AND high >= midpoint AND low <= entry level AND high >= entry level.
Short: high-sweep setup is watching for entry AND low >= base - invalidation multiplier × (extreme - base) AND low <= midpoint AND high >= midpoint AND low <= entry level AND high >= entry level.
Exit: long, low <= frozen stop level OR high >= entry price + reward multiplier × (entry price - frozen stop level).
Exit: short, high >= frozen stop level OR low <= entry price - reward multiplier × (frozen stop level - entry price).
