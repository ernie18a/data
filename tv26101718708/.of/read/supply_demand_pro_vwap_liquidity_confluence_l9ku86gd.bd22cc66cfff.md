Calculation: Explosive bar = range > ATR × move multiplier AND absolute body > range × body threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > volume average × volume multiplier)
Calculation: Demand zone = zone from the preceding base candle(s) before a bullish explosive bar; top = max(base high, explosive open), bottom = base low. Supply zone = zone before a bearish explosive bar; top = base high, bottom = min(base low, explosive open). A base candle has absolute body < ATR × 0.5 and is not explosive. Keep zones active until the selected mitigation rule removes them: close through the far boundary (body-close mode) or wick through it (wick-touch mode).
Calculation: Zone grade = A+ if score ≥ 80, A if ≥ 65, B if ≥ 50, otherwise C. Score = min((explosive-bar range / ATR) × 18, 30) + (if volume confirmation is enabled, min((volume / volume average) × 13, 20), otherwise 10) + 25 if session VWAP is within the zone expanded by ATR × tolerance + 20 if the zone overlaps an active higher-timeframe zone + 5. Higher-timeframe zones use the same detection and boundaries, based on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: confirmed bar AND in enabled session (or session filter disabled) AND cooldown passed AND (active demand zone with grade at least the selected minimum, low ≤ zone top, close > zone top, lower wick ≥ minimum wick fraction × range, close > EMA AND (fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone not previously tested) AND (one-signal-per-zone disabled OR zone unused) OR enabled demand sweep with low[1] < zone bottom, close[1] > zone bottom, (zone bottom − low[1]) / close ≥ minimum sweep depth, close > open)
Short: confirmed bar AND in enabled session (or session filter disabled) AND cooldown passed AND (active supply zone with grade at least the selected minimum, high ≥ zone bottom, close < zone bottom, upper wick ≥ minimum wick fraction × range, close < EMA AND (fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone not previously tested) AND (one-signal-per-zone disabled OR zone unused) OR enabled supply sweep with high[1] > zone top, close[1] < zone top, (high[1] − zone top) / close ≥ minimum sweep depth, close < open)
