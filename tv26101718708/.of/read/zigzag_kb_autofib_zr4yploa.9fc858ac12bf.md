Calculation: Recent down-swing = latest confirmed ZigZag swing from a pivot high A to a lower pivot low B; confirm pivots using a fixed percentage price-deviation threshold and minimum bar separation.
Calculation: Entry-zone high = B + the longer entry retracement fraction × (A − B).
Calculation: Take-profit-zone low = B + the lower take-profit retracement fraction × (A − B).
Long: A recent down-swing exists, no bar from B through the current bar has high ≥ its take-profit-zone low, close > its entry-zone high, previous close ≤ its entry-zone high, close > the shorter-period EMA, and close > the longer-period EMA.
Exit: Long, high ≥ the recent down-swing's take-profit-zone low OR close < the shorter-period EMA AND close < the longer-period EMA.
