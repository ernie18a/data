Calculation: Swing liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of chart bars on each side; register each level on the confirmation bar. Process a new high before a new low. Optionally merge a new pivot into the newest unconsumed level of the same kind whose price differs by at most a multiple of current ATR; retain the existing level's price. Otherwise append a new level. After processing all levels on each bar, remove the oldest swing levels until their count is at most the configured maximum, counting consumed levels too.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe liquidity levels = the high and low of the last completed bar of each of two optionally enabled higher timeframes, one longer than the other. Replace each timeframe's levels at the start of each new bar of that timeframe.
Calculation: Active liquidity levels = swing and enabled higher-timeframe liquidity levels that have not been consumed. A high level is consumed when chart high >= its price; a low level is consumed when chart low <= its price, regardless of volume or close. Check newly registered levels on their registration bar before removing excess swing levels.
Long: Chart low <= any active low level AND close > that level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > a multiple of the simple moving average of volume including the current bar).
Short: Chart high >= any active high level AND close < that level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > a multiple of the simple moving average of volume including the current bar).
