Calculation: Exit classification = classify the last exit as take profit if its fill price is at least as close to the preceding bar's take-profit level as to its stop-loss level; otherwise classify it as stop loss.
Long: RSI of close < oversold threshold AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND number of open entries < maximum entries AND (number of open entries = 0 OR close < last entry price × (1 − minimum drop percentage)) AND (no previous exit OR bars since last exit > cooldown for its exit classification).
Exit: long, high >= active take-profit level OR low <= active stop-loss level. The take-profit level is the position's average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage); the stop-loss level is the lowest open entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage). Both levels are submitted when a long position is observed and updated on each subsequent bar; use the levels active during the bar.
