Calculation: Bullish gap = the interval from the high of the first bar to the low of the third bar in a three-bar window, where the first high is below the third low; the third bar is completed.
Calculation: Bearish gap = the interval from the high of the third bar to the low of the first bar in a three-bar window, where the first low is above the third high; the third bar is completed.
Calculation: Eligible gap = a gap on an enabled chart or higher timeframe within the configured timeframe ladder and upward limit, found within the configured buffer depth, whose height is at least a configured fraction of the current chart close, whose top is at least the chart close divided by a configured range multiplier, and whose bottom is at most the chart close multiplied by that multiplier. A bullish gap remains eligible only if the minimum low, or minimum close when close-based filling is selected, since the bar after its third bar is greater than its top minus a configured fill fraction of its original height. A bearish gap remains eligible only if the maximum high, or maximum close when close-based filling is selected, since the bar after its third bar is less than its bottom plus that fraction of its original height. Include the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in the fill calculation.
Calculation: Selected gap = an eligible gap containing the current chart close, or one of the configured number of eligible gaps nearest above the close by bottom price, or one of the configured number nearest below the close by top price, selected separately for each timeframe.
Long: A selected bullish gap exists.
Short: A selected bearish gap exists.
