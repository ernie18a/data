Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side; recognize it only when the right-side bars have completed. A higher high or lower high compares a confirmed swing high with the preceding confirmed swing high; a higher low or lower low compares a confirmed swing low with the preceding confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Structure state = initialize neutral; initialize bullish when high exceeds the latest swing high, otherwise bearish when low falls below the latest swing low. Track the highest confirmed swing high and lowest confirmed swing low, resetting the relevant extreme to the broken level on a change of character. In a bullish state, the reversal floor is the latest swing low if it formed at or after the structural origin low, otherwise the origin low; a break below it with displacement changes the state to bearish. In a bearish state, the reversal ceiling is the latest swing high if it formed at or after the structural ceiling, otherwise the structural ceiling; a break above it with displacement changes the state to bullish. A reversal takes priority over a continuation on the same bar. On a bearish reversal set the structural ceiling to the latest swing high, or the current high if the latest swing high is not a sufficiently recent confirmed high at least as high as the current high. On a bullish reversal set the origin low to the latest swing low if it is a sufficiently recent confirmed low no higher than the current low, otherwise the current low. On a bullish continuation raise the origin low to the latest swing low if higher; on a bearish continuation lower the structural ceiling to the latest swing high if lower. Each reversal level must differ from the last level broken by either reversal or continuation in that direction; each continuation extreme must differ from the last continuation level broken in that direction. A reversal also latches its level as the last continuation level in that direction and clears the opposite continuation latch.
Calculation: Displacement = absolute close minus open or high minus low is at least a threshold multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Macro bullish state = set bullish on bullish initialization or bullish change of character; set bearish on bearish initialization or when a valid Fibonacci leg has close below its low anchor. A bearish change of character alone does not change it.
Calculation: Fibonacci leg = after a bullish change of character or continuation, maintain a higher-low anchor and a later, higher swing-high anchor. A bullish continuation initializes both from the latest higher low and structural swing high only if the high is later and higher; otherwise clear both. A bullish reversal clears the high anchor and retains the latest higher low only if it precedes the broken high or is sufficiently recent. After a bullish event, a newly confirmed higher low becomes the low anchor; if it follows the high anchor, clear the high anchor. A newly confirmed higher high sufficiently close in time to the bullish event sets the high anchor and uses the latest earlier higher low if available, otherwise retains a valid earlier low anchor. Clear the leg and bullish event on a bearish change of character. The leg is valid only when both anchors exist, the high occurs later, and the high exceeds the low.
Calculation: Fibonacci level = high anchor multiplied by (low anchor / high anchor) raised to a retracement parameter. Use three ordered retracement parameters: shallow, middle, and deep; larger parameters produce lower levels.
Calculation: Chart gap = on three consecutive completed chart bars, a bullish gap requires three rising bodies, third low above first high by at least a minimum gap, middle high above first high, middle low below third low, third high above middle high, and the third body not entirely inside the middle bar's range. Its bounds are first high and third low. A bearish gap requires three falling bodies, third high below first low by at least the minimum gap, middle low below first low, middle high above third high, third low below middle low, and the third body not entirely inside the middle bar's range. Its bounds are third high and first low. Retain only a limited number of most recent gaps of either direction. A bullish gap is filled when low reaches its lower bound; a bearish gap is filled when high reaches its upper bound. For the current bar, test close inside retained unfilled gaps before applying that bar's fill.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap = apply the same three-bar body, middle-bar ownership, third-bar break, and body-outside tests to higher-timeframe bars; require the gap width to be at least a multiple of higher-timeframe ATR. Use the latest completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. The gap exists only while the current higher-timeframe three-bar pattern satisfies those conditions; its bounds are defined as for a chart gap.
Long: A confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is higher than the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A confirmed swing high is lower than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: Structure state is bearish AND high exceeds the eligible, unlatched reversal ceiling AND displacement is satisfied.
Short: Structure state is bullish AND low falls below the eligible, unlatched reversal floor AND displacement is satisfied.
Long: Structure state is bullish AND no bearish reversal occurs on the bar AND high exceeds the unlatched structural swing high.
Short: Structure state is bearish AND no bullish reversal occurs on the bar AND low falls below the unlatched structural swing low.
Long: A valid Fibonacci leg exists AND close is between its middle and deep retracement levels inclusive.
Long: Structure state or macro state is bullish AND close is at or above the valid leg's low anchor if a valid leg exists AND (close is between the shallow and deep Fibonacci levels inclusive OR close is inside a retained unfilled chart gap OR close is inside the higher-timeframe gap) AND close exceeds open AND (close exceeds the preceding high OR close crosses above the middle Fibonacci level).
Exit: long, a valid Fibonacci leg exists AND close is below its low anchor.
