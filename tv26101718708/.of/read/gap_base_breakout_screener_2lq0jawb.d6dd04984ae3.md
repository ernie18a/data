Calculation: Post-gap base = longest consecutive window ending on the prior bar whose high-to-low width is within the configured maximum; it must meet the configured minimum length, start within the configured maximum distance after the nearest qualifying gap, and have a low at or above the close before that gap. A qualifying gap has an open at least the configured percentage above the previous close and volume at least the configured multiple of the average volume on earlier bars.
Calculation: Average daily range % = 100 × (average of high / low over a shorter lookback − 1).
Calculation: Average dollar volume = average of close × volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Relative bar range = current high − low divided by the average high − low over earlier bars.
Long: Close ≥ configured minimum price AND average dollar volume ≥ configured minimum AND average daily range % ≥ configured minimum AND prior-bar-ending post-gap base length ≥ configured minimum AND base low ≥ pre-gap close AND high > base high AND close > open AND close location within current bar ≥ configured minimum AND relative bar range ≥ configured multiple AND volume ≥ configured multiple of the average volume over earlier bars.
