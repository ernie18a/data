Calculation: Confirmed swing high = the high of a candidate bar that is at least as high as every high in a specified number of preceding bars and strictly higher than every high in a specified number of subsequent bars; confirm it on the last subsequent bar.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = the low of a candidate bar that is at most as low as every low in a specified number of preceding bars and strictly lower than every low in a specified number of subsequent bars; confirm it on the last subsequent bar.
Calculation: Swing state = the side, price, and candidate-bar index of the most recently accepted swing. Start with no swing. Process a confirmed swing high before a confirmed swing low on the same bar. Update the state whenever a swing is accepted; a same-side acceptance replaces the preceding swing. Use the current confirmation bar's ATR, or max(high − low, minimum price increment) when ATR is unavailable.
Long: A confirmed swing low exists AND (the alternating-swing filter is disabled OR no swing has been accepted OR the last accepted swing is a low AND the candidate low is below its price OR the last accepted swing is a high AND the absolute price difference from that swing is at least a specified multiple of ATR AND the absolute difference between their candidate-bar indices is at least a specified minimum).
Short: A confirmed swing high exists AND (the alternating-swing filter is disabled OR no swing has been accepted OR the last accepted swing is a high AND the candidate high is above its price OR the last accepted swing is a low AND the absolute price difference from that swing is at least a specified multiple of ATR AND the absolute difference between their candidate-bar indices is at least a specified minimum).
