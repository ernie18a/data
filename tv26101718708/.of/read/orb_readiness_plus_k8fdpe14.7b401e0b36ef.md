Calculation: Opening range = each day, in the configured timezone, take the highest high and lowest low of bars in the configured opening session; the range is set on the first subsequent bar outside that session. Its midpoint is the average of its high and low. Reset it at the start of each day.
Calculation: Setup state = reset direction, mode, trigger bar, armed and pending reclaims, and reaction counts at the start of each day. Outside the opening session after the range is set, a close above the range high starts a long breakout setup if the active direction is not already long; a close below the range low starts a short breakout setup if the active direction is not already short. A fresh break above or below the range arms a short or long reclaim respectively and clears both pending reclaims; a fresh break means the corresponding outside-close condition was false on the preceding bar. An armed long reclaim becomes pending when the previous close is at or below the range low and the current close is strictly inside the range; an armed short reclaim becomes pending when the previous close is at or above the range high and the current close is strictly inside the range. Cancel a pending long reclaim on a close below the range low and a pending short reclaim on a close above the range high. On a later bar, confirm a pending long reclaim when close is above the midpoint OR above daily VWAP of typical price OR both above the range low and above the previous high; confirm a pending short reclaim when close is below the midpoint OR below daily VWAP of typical price OR both below the range high and below the previous low. Confirmation starts a setup in that direction with reclaim mode and clears both armed and pending reclaims. Each new setup records its trigger bar and resets reaction counts and preceding reaction states. Process breaks before warnings, cancellations, and confirmations.
Calculation: Reactions = after the setup trigger bar and outside the opening session, a long edge reaction has low at or below the applicable edge plus a proximity parameter AND close above that edge; a short edge reaction has high at or above the applicable edge minus the proximity parameter AND close below that edge. The applicable edge is the range high for a long breakout or short reclaim and the range low for a short breakout or long reclaim. A long midpoint reaction has low at or below the midpoint plus the proximity parameter AND close above the midpoint; a short midpoint reaction has high at or above the midpoint minus the proximity parameter AND close below the midpoint. Count a reaction when its condition changes from false to true, optionally counting only the first of each kind per setup. An edge or midpoint reaction is confirmed while its count is at least one; its fresh event occurs when that confirmed state changes from false on the preceding bar to true. The total reaction count is the sum of edge and midpoint counts.
Calculation: Entry event = on a completed chart bar, the range is set AND a setup is active AND the total reaction count is at or below a maximum AND the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its higher-timeframe close EMA for a long setup or is at or below that EMA for a short setup AND an edge reaction is confirmed OR reclaim mode is active OR midpoint reactions are allowed and a midpoint reaction is confirmed, with those three alternatives grouped together AND a fresh edge reaction OR fresh midpoint reaction OR reclaim confirmation OR reclaim continuation occurs, with those four alternatives grouped together AND the number of earlier entry events that day is below a daily maximum AND the number of bars since the last entry event is at least a cooldown parameter, or no earlier entry event exists that day. A long reclaim continuation requires reclaim mode, a bar after the trigger, close above the range low, and previous close at or below that low; a short continuation requires reclaim mode, a bar after the trigger, close below the range high, and previous close at or above that high.
Long: Active setup direction is long.
Short: Active setup direction is short.
Long: Entry event occurs AND active setup direction is long.
Short: Entry event occurs AND active setup direction is short.
Long: A long reclaim warning occurs AND the number of bars since the last recorded reclaim warning is at least the cooldown parameter OR no earlier warning was recorded that day, with the cooldown alternatives grouped together.
Short: A short reclaim warning occurs AND the number of bars since the last recorded reclaim warning is at least the cooldown parameter OR no earlier warning was recorded that day, with the cooldown alternatives grouped together.
Long: A fresh midpoint reaction occurs AND active setup direction is long.
Short: A fresh midpoint reaction occurs AND active setup direction is short.
