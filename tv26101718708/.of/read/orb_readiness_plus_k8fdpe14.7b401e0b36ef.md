Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, during the configured opening-range session for the current day; the range is set after that session ends
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2
Calculation: Active direction = long after a close above the set opening range high or a confirmed low reclaim; short after a close below the set opening range low or a confirmed high reclaim; reset at the start of each day
Calculation: HTF trend alignment = active direction matches whether the close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is above or below its EMA
Calculation: Bullish reclaim warning = after a fresh close below the opening range low arms a bullish reclaim, a close crosses above that low and remains below the opening range high; bearish reclaim warning = after a fresh close above the opening range high arms a bearish reclaim, a close crosses below that high and remains above the opening range low
Calculation: Confirmed low reclaim = after a bullish reclaim warning, on a later bar the close is above the opening range midpoint OR above VWAP OR above both the opening range low and the previous bar's high; confirmed high reclaim = after a bearish reclaim warning, on a later bar the close is below the opening range midpoint OR below VWAP OR below both the opening range high and the previous bar's low
Calculation: Long edge retest = after a long setup, low reaches within the configured proximity above the setup edge (opening range high for a breakout setup, low for a confirmed reclaim setup) and close finishes above that edge; short edge retest = after a short setup, high reaches within the configured proximity below the setup edge (opening range low for a breakout setup, high for a confirmed reclaim setup) and close finishes below that edge
Calculation: Long midline reaction = after a long setup, low reaches within the configured proximity above the opening range midpoint and close finishes above it; short midline reaction = after a short setup, high reaches within the configured proximity below the midpoint and close finishes below it
Calculation: Reclaim continuation = after a confirmed low reclaim, a later close crosses above the opening range low; after a confirmed high reclaim, a later close crosses below the opening range high
Long: bullish reclaim warning OR (active direction is long AND long midline reaction is newly confirmed) OR (opening range is set AND active direction is long AND HTF trend aligns AND total edge and midline retests do not exceed the configured maximum AND a new long edge retest, new long midline reaction, confirmed low reclaim, or reclaim continuation occurs)
Short: bearish reclaim warning OR (active direction is short AND short midline reaction is newly confirmed) OR (opening range is set AND active direction is short AND HTF trend aligns AND total edge and midline retests do not exceed the configured maximum AND a new short edge retest, new short midline reaction, confirmed high reclaim, or reclaim continuation occurs)
