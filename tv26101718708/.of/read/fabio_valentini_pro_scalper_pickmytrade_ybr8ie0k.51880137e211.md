Calculation: ATR = Average True Range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed delta = simple moving average of candle buy volume minus sell volume; buy volume is all volume when close > open, half when close = open, and volume × (high − close) / (high − low + a small positive constant) when close < open; sell volume is volume − buy volume.
Calculation: Buyers control = smoothed delta > 0 AND smoothed delta > its previous value; sellers control = smoothed delta < 0 AND smoothed delta < its previous value.
Calculation: Absorption = volume > its short-term average × a configurable multiplier AND absolute(close − open) < ATR × a configurable threshold; buyer absorption = absorption AND close > open; seller absorption = absorption AND close < open. Strong absorption = absorption on at least two consecutive bars.
Calculation: Ten-bar range = highest high − lowest low over a short lookback; average range = simple moving average of that range over a longer lookback. Contraction = ten-bar range < average range × a configurable factor. Expansion = ten-bar range > average range × a configurable factor AND volume > short-term average volume × a configurable factor.
Calculation: Aggressive buying = expansion AND close > open AND smoothed delta > 0; aggressive selling = expansion AND close < open AND smoothed delta < 0.
Calculation: Volume profile POC, VAH, and VAL = over a configurable lookback, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into configurable equal price bins and assign each bar's volume to the bin containing its close; POC is the center of the highest-volume bin. Starting at POC, add adjacent bins in descending volume order (ties go to the higher bin) until at least 70% of total binned volume is included; VAH and VAL are the upper and lower boundaries of the included bins.
Calculation: ORB high and low = highest high and lowest low during the first configurable number of minutes of each session; the range becomes defined after that period completes.
Calculation: VWAP bands = session VWAP ± ATR × a configurable band width.
Long: buyer absorption OR (strong absorption AND close > open) OR (buyer absorption 3, 4, or 5 bars ago AND contraction on the previous bar AND aggressive buying) OR (ORB defined AND close > ORB high AND previous close ≤ ORB high) OR (low within 0.1% of VAL AND buyer absorption AND buyers control), with the last three alternatives also requiring close > VWAP lower band when the VWAP filter is enabled; the ORB and value-area alternatives require the ORB or value-area setup respectively. Combined entry signals require session eligibility (if the session filter is enabled) AND daily losses below the configured limit. Actual strategy entry also requires no open position.
Short: seller absorption OR (strong absorption AND close < open) OR (seller absorption 3, 4, or 5 bars ago AND contraction on the previous bar AND aggressive selling) OR (ORB defined AND close < ORB low AND previous close ≥ ORB low) OR (high within 0.1% of VAH AND seller absorption AND sellers control), with the last three alternatives also requiring close < VWAP upper band when the VWAP filter is enabled; the ORB and value-area alternatives require the ORB or value-area setup respectively. Combined entry signals require session eligibility (if the session filter is enabled) AND daily losses below the configured limit. Actual strategy entry also requires no open position.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry-signal-bar low − ATR OR high ≥ entry-signal-bar close + (entry-signal-bar close − initial stop) × configured risk/reward ratio OR the trailing stop is hit after price advances by its configured ATR offset, with the stop trailing the highest price since entry by a configured ATR distance.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry-signal-bar high + ATR OR low ≤ entry-signal-bar close − (initial stop − entry-signal-bar close) × configured risk/reward ratio OR the trailing stop is hit after price declines by its configured ATR offset, with the stop trailing the lowest price since entry by a configured ATR distance.
