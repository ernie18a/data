Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR calculated from daily bars; use the last completed daily bar when daily is higher than the chart timeframe, and the bar still forming when daily is equal to or lower than the chart timeframe
Long: At a new daily session, the open is below the preceding bar’s close by at least the configured minimum gap size (a multiple of daily ATR) and by more than zero, and an unfilled gap-down is still within its configured observation window, and the current bar’s high is at or above that gap’s level
Short: At a new daily session, the open is above the preceding bar’s close by at least the configured minimum gap size (a multiple of daily ATR) and by more than zero, and an unfilled gap-up is still within its configured observation window, and the current bar’s low is at or below that gap’s level
