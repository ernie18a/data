Calculation: Opening zone = current higher-timeframe candle’s open ± (ATR from the configured timeframe × zone multiplier); snapshot the ATR and zone when a new higher-timeframe candle starts. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe candle’s open, high, and low.
Calculation: Bull setup = since the current higher-timeframe candle opened, its low reached at least the configured minimum-wick distance below its open, then a chart-timeframe bar closed inside the opening zone.
Calculation: Bear setup = since the current higher-timeframe candle opened, its high reached at least the configured minimum-wick distance above its open, then a chart-timeframe bar closed inside the opening zone.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (EMA of chart-timeframe volume over the shorter lookback − EMA over the longer lookback) ÷ EMA over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Bearish approach = every configured number of chart-timeframe bars immediately before the first close inside the zone closed below its open.
Calculation: Bullish approach = every configured number of chart-timeframe bars immediately before the first close inside the zone closed above its open.
Long: Bull setup is active AND previous chart-timeframe close was inside the opening zone AND current close is above the zone AND current bar is confirmed AND current bar starts outside the configured post-open buffer AND elapsed time since the last signal meets the configured cooldown AND (entry filter is disabled OR volume oscillator ≥ configured threshold OR entry filter is Last Candles and bearish approach is true).
Short: Bear setup is active AND previous chart-timeframe close was inside the opening zone AND current close is below the zone AND current bar is confirmed AND current bar starts outside the configured post-open buffer AND elapsed time since the last signal meets the configured cooldown AND (entry filter is disabled OR volume oscillator ≥ configured threshold OR entry filter is Last Candles and bullish approach is true).
