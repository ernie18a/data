Calculation: ATR snapshot = ATR on the selected timeframe sampled on the first chart bar of each new higher-timeframe candle and held constant until the next higher-timeframe candle. For historical bars, use the last completed selected-timeframe bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe, the last completed intrabar when it is lower, or the current completed bar when it is the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Opening zone = the forming higher-timeframe candle's open plus or minus the ATR snapshot multiplied by the zone-width parameter.
Calculation: First zone return = the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe candle whose close is inside the inclusive opening zone and on or before which the cumulative low is at most the higher-timeframe open minus the ATR snapshot multiplied by the minimum-wick parameter OR the cumulative high is at least the higher-timeframe open plus the ATR snapshot multiplied by the minimum-wick parameter. Include the return bar's high and low. Record both sweep comparisons independently at this bar and retain them until the next higher-timeframe candle.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 multiplied by (shorter-period EMA of volume minus longer-period EMA of volume) divided by the longer-period EMA of volume.
Long: A first zone return has occurred in the current forming higher-timeframe candle AND its recorded downward sweep is true AND the previous chart bar's close is inside the current inclusive opening zone AND the current confirmed chart bar's close is above the zone AND the current chart bar's opening time is at least the buffer duration after the higher-timeframe candle's opening time AND the current chart bar's opening time is at least the cooldown duration after the opening time of the most recent long or short signal, initialized to Unix epoch zero AND (the entry filter is disabled OR the volume filter is selected AND the volume oscillator is at least its threshold OR the candle filter is selected AND every chart bar in the configured lookback immediately preceding the first zone return has close less than open).
Short: A first zone return has occurred in the current forming higher-timeframe candle AND its recorded upward sweep is true AND the previous chart bar's close is inside the current inclusive opening zone AND the current confirmed chart bar's close is below the zone AND the current chart bar's opening time is at least the buffer duration after the higher-timeframe candle's opening time AND the current chart bar's opening time is at least the cooldown duration after the opening time of the most recent long or short signal, initialized to Unix epoch zero AND (the entry filter is disabled OR the volume filter is selected AND the volume oscillator is at least its threshold OR the candle filter is selected AND every chart bar in the configured lookback immediately preceding the first zone return has close greater than open).
