Calculation: Opening range = for each local calendar day, aggregate chart bars whose opening times are at or after the session start and before the opening-range end; use the first open, maximum high, minimum low, and last close. The range is complete on the first subsequent chart bar outside that window and resets on the next local calendar day.
Calculation: Retracement levels = for each of three enabled, increasing retracement fractions, opening-range high minus fraction times opening-range width when opening-range close > opening-range open; otherwise opening-range low plus fraction times opening-range width. Levels exist only when the opening range is complete and its width > 0.
Calculation: Rejection body = maximum of the absolute difference between current open and close and the instrument's minimum price increment, treated as a parameter.
Long: Opening range complete AND current bar opening time outside the opening range AND current bar opening time at or before the signal-window end AND opening-range close > opening-range open AND current low within ATR times a tolerance multiplier of any enabled retracement level, inclusive AND ((close > open AND minimum of open and close minus low >= rejection body times a wick multiplier) OR (close > open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close AND previous close < previous open)).
Short: Opening range complete AND current bar opening time outside the opening range AND current bar opening time at or before the signal-window end AND opening-range close <= opening-range open AND current high within ATR times a tolerance multiplier of any enabled retracement level, inclusive AND ((close < open AND high minus maximum of open and close >= rejection body times a wick multiplier) OR (close < open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close AND previous close > previous open)).
Long: Opening range complete AND current bar opening time outside the opening range AND current bar opening time at or before the signal-window end AND close > opening-range high AND volume > current simple moving average of volume times a volume multiplier AND (one-signal-per-direction-per-day restriction disabled OR previous long breakout signals today = 0).
Short: Opening range complete AND current bar opening time outside the opening range AND current bar opening time at or before the signal-window end AND close < opening-range low AND volume > current simple moving average of volume times a volume multiplier AND (one-signal-per-direction-per-day restriction disabled OR previous short breakout signals today = 0).
