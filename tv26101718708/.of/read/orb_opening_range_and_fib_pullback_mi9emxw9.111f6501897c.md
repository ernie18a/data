Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of bars timestamped within the configured daily opening-range interval.
Calculation: Opening-range direction = bullish when the last opening-range close > the first opening-range open; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: 50%, 61.8%, and 70% retracement levels = for a bullish opening range, opening-range high − range × (0.50, 0.618, or 0.70); for a bearish opening range, opening-range low + range × (0.50, 0.618, or 0.70), where range = opening-range high − opening-range low.
Calculation: Retracement zone = ATR × zone multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = (close > open AND (min(open, close) − low) ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price tick) × wick/body ratio) OR (close > open AND close ≥ previous open AND open ≤ previous close AND previous close < previous open).
Calculation: Bearish confirmation = (close < open AND (high − max(open, close)) ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price tick) × wick/body ratio) OR (close < open AND close ≤ previous open AND open ≥ previous close AND previous close > previous open).
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Long: opening range complete AND current bar is after the opening range and within the signal window AND ((opening-range direction is bullish AND (low is within the retracement zone of any enabled retracement level) AND bullish confirmation) OR (close > opening-range high AND volume > average volume × volume multiplier AND no earlier upward ORB signal that day, when one-per-direction-per-day is enabled))
Short: opening range complete AND current bar is after the opening range and within the signal window AND ((opening-range direction is bearish AND (high is within the retracement zone of any enabled retracement level) AND bearish confirmation) OR (close < opening-range low AND volume > average volume × volume multiplier AND no earlier downward ORB signal that day, when one-per-direction-per-day is enabled))
