Calculation: Smoothed CVD = EMA of cumulative signed volume, where signed volume is positive when close is above the selected reference, negative when below it, and zero when equal; the reference is either open or the previous close.
Calculation: Slope of a series = the difference between its current-bar linear-regression estimates at offsets zero and one, using the same lookback for close, smoothed CVD, and RSI.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high AND either smoothed CVD or volume at the new pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot. A pivot is confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; evaluate the divergence on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low AND either smoothed CVD is above or volume is below its value at the preceding pivot. Use the same pivot lookback and confirmation timing as for bearish divergence.
Calculation: Correlation = correlation of the change in close with the change in smoothed CVD; substitute zero when unavailable. Correlation score = correlation shifted and scaled from its natural range to the score range.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume divided by its SMA, or zero when that SMA is zero.
Calculation: Buyer loss = the sum of weights for these true conditions, capped at the score maximum: close slope > zero AND smoothed CVD slope < zero; correlation < zero, otherwise correlation < a positive correlation threshold; close > close at the slope lookback AND volume ratio < the weak-volume threshold; RSI > an upper RSI threshold AND RSI slope < zero AND close slope > zero; bearish divergence occurred within a lookback proportional to the pivot lookback; close < control EMA. The two correlation conditions are mutually exclusive.
Calculation: Seller exhaustion = the sum of weights for these true conditions, capped at the score maximum: close slope < zero AND smoothed CVD slope > zero; bullish divergence occurred within the same divergence lookback; close < close at the slope lookback AND volume ratio < the weak-volume threshold; RSI < a lower RSI threshold AND RSI slope > zero AND close slope < zero; close crosses above the control EMA.
Calculation: Range position = (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) over the same range lookback, or the range midpoint when the range is zero.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper Bollinger band - lower Bollinger band) / Bollinger basis, or zero when the basis is zero. Its average uses the same lookback as its bands. Average ATR uses that same lookback.
Calculation: Bottom drop risk = clamp to the score range the sum of weights for these true conditions: Bollinger width < its average times a compression factor; ATR < average ATR times an ATR compression factor; range position < a lower range threshold; close slope <= zero AND smoothed CVD slope <= zero; buyer loss >= its warning threshold; seller exhaustion < its activity threshold; correlation < the positive correlation threshold; volume ratio < a separate weak-volume threshold; close < control EMA; buyer loss >= its danger threshold; correlation score < its lower threshold; smoothed CVD slope < zero; MACD histogram < zero AND MACD histogram < its previous value; close < control EMA AND range position < the lower range threshold. Subtract weights for these true conditions: buyer loss < its warning threshold; MACD histogram > its previous value; MACD histogram > zero; correlation score >= its higher threshold AND smoothed CVD slope >= zero; close > control EMA.
Calculation: Trade quality = a baseline plus weighted contributions, clamped to the score range: add one weight if buyer loss < its lower quality threshold, subtract another if buyer loss > its upper quality threshold, otherwise subtract a third; add a weight if seller exhaustion > its quality threshold AND close > control EMA; add a weight if bottom drop risk < its lower quality threshold, subtract another if it > its upper quality threshold, otherwise subtract a third; add a weight if correlation score > its higher threshold, subtract a weight if it < its lower threshold, otherwise add zero; add or subtract a weight according to whether MACD histogram > zero; add or subtract a weight according to whether MACD histogram > its previous value; add or subtract a weight according to whether close > control EMA.
Calculation: Score weights are positive parameters shared wherever equal contributions are required; preserve their ordering from smallest to largest. The lower quality thresholds are below the upper quality thresholds, the buyer warning threshold is below its danger threshold, and the lower correlation-score threshold is below its higher threshold. MACD uses a shorter and a longer price EMA and a signal EMA of their difference.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Long: Bottom drop risk < its warning threshold AND trade quality >= its bullish threshold.
Long: Trade quality >= its high-quality threshold AND previous trade quality < that threshold; the high-quality threshold is above the bullish threshold.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Short: Bottom drop risk >= its warning threshold AND previous bottom drop risk < its warning threshold.
Short: Bottom drop risk >= its warning threshold AND close < the previous bar's lowest low over the range lookback AND volume > its SMA.
