Calculation: Confirmed major pivots = choose either symmetric fixed-window pivot highs and lows or adaptive ATR pivots. For adaptive pivots, initialize the running high and low from the first bar and the last confirmed kind as neutral; update the running high on high >= running high and the running low on low <= running low. If the last kind is not high and running high - low >= ATR times a threshold, confirm the running high, set the last kind to high, and reset the running low to the current low. Otherwise, if the last kind is not low and high - running low >= the threshold, confirm the running low, set the last kind to low, and reset the running high to the current high. For fixed-window pivots, confirm a pivot after equal numbers of bars on either side. If both fixed-window kinds confirm on the same bar, retain both but use the low as the new pivot for subsequent same-bar decisions.
Calculation: Confirmed minor pivots = symmetric fixed-window pivot highs and lows with a shorter window than the fixed major-pivot window; if both kinds confirm on the same bar, retain both but use the low as the new pivot for subsequent same-bar decisions.
Calculation: Pending structure levels = separately for major and minor pivots, store the latest newly confirmed high and low as pending levels. Replace a pending level when a new pivot of its kind confirms; clear it after its first qualifying break. A bullish break requires close > pending high AND previous close <= that same high. A bearish break requires close < pending low AND previous close >= that same low. Each layer's bias starts neutral and changes to the direction of its latest break.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish gap has top = current low and bottom = high two bars earlier; a bearish gap has top = low two bars earlier and bottom = current high. Retain a configurable maximum number per side, removing the oldest first. A bullish gap is mitigated when low < its bottom; a bearish gap is mitigated when high > its top. Choose whether mitigation removes the gap or retains it as mitigated. A retained mitigated gap can flip only once, within a configurable number of bars after mitigation; process mitigation before flips on each bar.
Calculation: Order block = on a major structure break, scan a configurable number of prior bars for opposite-direction candles whose range is less than the current ATR times a threshold AND whose body divided by range is at least a threshold AND, optionally, whose volume is at least its own volume SMA times a threshold. For a bullish block, select either the most recent qualifying bearish candle or the qualifying candle with the lowest low; for a bearish block, select either the most recent qualifying bullish candle or the qualifying candle with the highest high. Its top and bottom are that candle's high and low. Require the selected bar's index to be greater than the first bar's index and its score to be at least a threshold. Retain a configurable maximum number per side, removing the oldest first. A bullish block is mitigated when either low or close, according to the selected mitigation method, is below its bottom; a bearish block is mitigated when the selected high or close is above its top. Remove mitigated blocks after creation and before breaker confirmation on each bar.
Calculation: Order block score = a weighted capped ratio of block height to current ATR plus a weighted capped ratio of the breaking candle's absolute body to current ATR plus a higher or lower fixed contribution according to whether the breaking bar's volume is above its volume SMA, plus a fixed contribution if a same-direction retained fair value gap overlaps the block. Calculate overlap before updating gaps on the current bar; retained mitigated gaps also count.
Calculation: Breaker candidate = a mitigated order block with its direction reversed, its original top and bottom, and its mitigation bar recorded. Keep a configurable maximum number of candidates, removing the oldest first; expire a candidate after a configurable maximum age. It can confirm only once, including on its mitigation bar.
Calculation: Sweep reference = the most recent confirmed minor pivot of the relevant kind among a bounded number of most recent minor pivots whose pivot bar is more than a configurable number of bars old. Track the latest sweep bar separately for each direction, initialized to the first bar.
Calculation: Price-zone range = the highest high and lowest low from the latest major bias reversal bar through the current bar, subject to a maximum lookback; before the first reversal, anchor at the first bar. Price-zone position = (close - range low) / max(range high - range low, a small positive constant).
