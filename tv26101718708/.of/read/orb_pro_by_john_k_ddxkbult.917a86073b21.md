Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured formation session; it becomes available on the first bar after that session and remains fixed until the next formation session.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); body filter passes if disabled OR high > low AND body / (high - low) >= the configured minimum fraction.
Calculation: External liquidity = confirmed pivot highs above the opening-range high and confirmed pivot lows below the opening-range low, using equal configurable numbers of bars on both sides. At range completion, scan pivot confirmation bars from newest to oldest over the configured lookback; retain only levels whose confirmation bar closed by the configured cutoff when that filter is enabled and whose price has not been strictly exceeded by highs or lows respectively from confirmation through the last formation bar. Reject a level within the configured duplicate distance of any active level and stop adding at the configured capacity. If the cutoff filter is disabled, also add subsequently confirmed external pivots during the display session after processing that bar's sweeps, subject to the same duplicate and capacity limits. Clear all levels at the next formation-session start; deactivate each level when a subsequent high exceeds a high level or low falls below a low level.
Calculation: Pending low sweep = a low below an active external low during the signal window, after range completion; pending high sweep = a high above an active external high under the same conditions. Record the latest sweep bar for each side. Clear the low-sweep pending state when close >= opening-range high and the high-sweep pending state when close <= opening-range low. Clear both outside the signal window, during formation, at the next formation-session start, or after either liquidity entry.
Calculation: Opening-range rejection eligibility = since the formation-session start, no close has been below the range low and no external low has been swept for a long; no close has been above the range high and no external high has been swept for a short. Include the current bar in these checks.
Calculation: Ordinary breakout state = reset both directions and the session lock at formation-session start. During the signal window after range completion, arm the long side on close > range high, or high > range high if close confirmation is disabled; arm the short side on close < range low, or low < range low if close confirmation is disabled. Arming an unarmed side records the current bar and disarms the opposite side; process long before short. On a later bar, before processing new breakouts, lock both sides for the session if an armed long has close < range high - the configured close tolerance or an armed short has close > range low + that tolerance; use zero tolerance when the tolerance option is disabled. Disarm the corresponding side after its ordinary breakout entry and disarm both outside the signal window after formation.
Calculation: Ordinary long retest = an armed long on a bar later than its breakout bar, in the signal window, with low <= range high + the configured retest tolerance AND either close > range high OR the close-tolerance option enabled AND close >= range high - the configured close tolerance. Ordinary short retest = the symmetric conditions with high >= range low - retest tolerance AND either close < range low OR the close-tolerance option enabled AND close <= range low + close tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish body engulf = close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open; bearish body engulf = close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open. Ordinary breakout pattern = a directional candle AND, if engulf filtering is enabled, the previous candle has the opposite direction when that option is enabled AND the corresponding body engulf holds when that option is enabled.
Calculation: EMA breakout state = reset at formation-session start; record the EMA of close from the last formation bar. After formation, while in the signal window and unlocked, start a long setup on close > range high AND previous close <= range high, or a short setup on close < range low AND previous close >= range low. If the outside-EMA filter is enabled, additionally require the recorded EMA > range high for a long or < range low for a short. Before the EMA break, cancel a long setup on close <= range high or a short setup on close >= range low; a new setup may start on that same bar. Advance a long setup when close crosses above the current EMA AND close > range high, or a short setup when close crosses below the current EMA AND close < range low; this can occur on the opening-range breakout bar. On subsequent bars, before entry evaluation, lock both EMA directions until the next formation-session start if a long setup has close < EMA or a short setup has close > EMA. Clear the setup outside the signal window or when disabled, and after its entry.
Calculation: EMA retest seen = after the EMA breakout bar of the current setup, at least one bar through the current bar has low <= EMA + the configured EMA retest tolerance AND high >= EMA - that tolerance. A valid long EMA retest bar additionally has close >= EMA AND close > range high; a valid short EMA retest bar additionally has close <= EMA AND close < range low.
Calculation: Stop and target = for a signal at its close, choose the configured stop: signal-bar low minus a buffer for a long or high plus a buffer for a short; entry minus or plus a multiple of ATR; the opposite opening-range boundary minus or plus a buffer; or the latest confirmed swing low minus or swing high plus a buffer. Round long stops down and short stops up to the configured price increment. Choose the configured target: entry plus or minus a risk multiple, the opposite opening-range boundary where available, or the corresponding opening-range boundary plus or minus a multiple of the range width; round the target to the nearest price increment. Risk is abs(entry - stop). If multiple same-side signals coincide, select the target configuration in liquidity, rejection, EMA breakout, ordinary breakout priority order. Create levels only when exactly one side signals and its stop and target are strictly on their respective sides of entry.
Long: Range is complete AND bar is outside formation and inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND pending low sweep exists AND the current bar is later than its latest sweep bar if separate confirmation is enabled AND range low < close < range high AND close > open.
Short: Range is complete AND bar is outside formation and inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND pending high sweep exists AND the current bar is later than its latest sweep bar if separate confirmation is enabled AND range low < close < range high AND close < open.
Long: Range is complete AND bar is outside formation and inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND long opening-range rejection eligibility holds AND low <= range low AND range low < close < range high AND close > open AND body > previous body.
Short: Range is complete AND bar is outside formation and inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND short opening-range rejection eligibility holds AND high >= range high AND range low < close < range high AND close < open AND body > previous body.
Long: Inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND ordinary breakout is unlocked AND long breakout is armed AND current bar is later than its breakout bar AND close > range high AND body > previous body AND ((low <= range high + retest tolerance AND bullish ordinary breakout pattern) OR (previous bar was an ordinary long retest AND previous close < previous open AND close > open)).
Short: Inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND ordinary breakout is unlocked AND short breakout is armed AND current bar is later than its breakout bar AND close < range low AND body > previous body AND ((high >= range low - retest tolerance AND bearish ordinary breakout pattern) OR (previous bar was an ordinary short retest AND previous close > previous open AND close < open)).
Long: Inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND long EMA breakout setup is active and unlocked AND current bar is later than its EMA breakout bar AND close > EMA AND close > range high AND body > previous body AND ((EMA retest seen AND previous close < previous open AND bullish body engulf) OR (previous bar was a valid long EMA retest AND previous close < previous open AND close > open)).
Short: Inside the signal window AND body filter passes AND short EMA breakout setup is active and unlocked AND current bar is later than its EMA breakout bar AND close < EMA AND close < range low AND body > previous body AND ((EMA retest seen AND previous close > previous open AND bearish body engulf) OR (previous bar was a valid short EMA retest AND previous close > previous open AND close < open)).
Exit: long, a valid stop and target were created for the entry AND current bar is later than the entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, a valid stop and target were created for the entry AND current bar is later than the entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= target).
