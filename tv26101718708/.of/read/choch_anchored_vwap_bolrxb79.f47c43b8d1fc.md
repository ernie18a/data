Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar’s high that is the highest in a centered window of 2L+1 chart bars; it becomes available after the L bars to its right complete. Track the latest confirmed pivot high separately for each of six independently configured pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar’s low that is the lowest in a centered window of 2L+1 chart bars; it becomes available after the L bars to its right complete. Track the latest confirmed pivot low separately for each of six independently configured pivot lookbacks.
Long: For any lookback, close crosses above its latest confirmed pivot high (previous close ≤ that level and current close > it), that level has not previously been broken for that lookback, and the previous break direction for that lookback is down.
Short: For any lookback, close crosses below its latest confirmed pivot low (previous close ≥ that level and current close < it), that level has not previously been broken for that lookback, and the previous break direction for that lookback is up.
