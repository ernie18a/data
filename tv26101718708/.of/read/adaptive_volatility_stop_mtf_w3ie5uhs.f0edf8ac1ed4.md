Calculation: Price = a selectable OHLC-derived source, with close as the default. Index [n] means n chart bars ago unless applied inside a timeframe-specific calculation.
Calculation: Adaptive stop = independently calculate on the chart timeframe and on three progressively higher timeframes, called short, intermediate, and long; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, carried forward between completions. On each timeframe, calculate efficiency ratio as absolute price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price changes over that lookback, or zero when the denominator is zero; smooth it with an EMA. Normalize the smoothed ratio between its rolling minimum and maximum over a longer normalization lookback, using a small positive denominator floor and a neutral midpoint before the normalization window is warm. Its rolling mean and standard deviation determine upper and lower thresholds: mean plus or minus the greater of a configured offset and a fraction of the standard deviation, bounded to the normalized range. Interpolate the ATR multiplier between a lower trending multiplier, a middle multiplier, and a higher choppy multiplier according to the normalized ratio and thresholds. For adaptive ATR length, square the interpolation between the fast and slow EMA coefficients using the smoothed efficiency ratio, take its reciprocal, and clamp it to configured bounds. Length and multiplier can independently be fixed instead. Apply their respective lower bounds. Calculate true range from OHLC and recursively smooth it as ATR = previous ATR + (true range - previous ATR) / length, initialized with true range. Optionally scale stop distance by a weighted, bounded path factor: the square root of the ratio of shorter to longer EMAs of squared price changes, plus separate positive coefficients times the negative and positive parts of the shorter EMA of price changes divided by ATR. Use a neutral activity factor if the longer EMA is zero and zero normalized drift if ATR is zero. Distance = ATR × multiplier × path factor, or ATR × multiplier when scaling is disabled. Initialize direction bullish and stop at price minus distance. In a bullish state ratchet stop to max(previous stop, price - distance); if price < this stop, set direction bearish and reset stop to price + distance. In a bearish state ratchet stop to min(previous stop, price + distance); if price > this stop, set direction bullish and reset stop to price - distance.
Calculation: Bias score = sum of signed direction weights for the chart, short, intermediate, and long stops; bullish is positive and bearish negative, with weights increasing with timeframe.
Calculation: Score floor = min(configured maximum floor, configured choppiness floor + volatility lift). For volatility lift, calculate chart-bar log close returns, flag a shock when absolute return exceeds a configured multiple of its shorter rolling standard deviation, and calculate the baseline as a longer SMA of shock indicators. Recursively update intensity as previous intensity × exp(-decay) + excitation × shock indicator, starting at zero. The intensity ratio is intensity / baseline when baseline is positive, otherwise zero. When the filter is enabled and the ratio exceeds its warning threshold, lift = maximum lift × min(1, (ratio - warning threshold) / scaling span); otherwise lift = zero. The choppiness floor can be disabled.
Calculation: Flip = a stop direction changes from bearish to bullish or bullish to bearish between consecutive chart bars. For the intermediate and long stops, an optional persistence mode replaces the flip with either a direction change one chart bar ago that remains in the new direction, or a current flip with close beyond the previous chart bar's stop by a configured multiple of chart-timeframe ATR. The intermediate flip also requires absolute bias score > score floor; the long flip does not. An optional session filter suppresses these two flips during a configured number of chart bars following a daily date change on intraday charts.
Calculation: Pullback band = the lower and upper of two adjacent stops: chart and short, short and intermediate, or intermediate and long. Optional volume confirmation requires volume > its SMA when the filter is enabled and the cumulative fraction of bars with positive, available volume meets the reliability threshold; it applies only to the first two bands.
Long: Bias score > 0.
Short: Bias score < 0.
Long: Long stop flips bullish.
Short: Long stop flips bearish.
Long: Intermediate stop flips bullish.
Short: Intermediate stop flips bearish.
Long: Short stop flips bullish AND absolute bias score > score floor AND, if the higher-timeframe lock is enabled, long stop direction is bullish AND, if signal priority is enabled, neither the intermediate nor long stop has a qualifying flip in either direction.
Short: Short stop flips bearish AND absolute bias score > score floor AND, if the higher-timeframe lock is enabled, long stop direction is bearish AND, if signal priority is enabled, neither the intermediate nor long stop has a qualifying flip in either direction.
Long: For any pullback band, both constituent stop directions are bullish AND close > lower band boundary AND close[2] <= lower band boundary[2] AND, if pullback confirmation is enabled, close[1] > lower band boundary[1] AND absolute bias score > score floor AND applicable volume confirmation passes AND the corresponding short, intermediate, or long bullish flip is absent AND, for the chart/short band, the higher-timeframe lock is disabled OR the long stop direction is bullish AND, if signal priority is enabled, no qualifying flip in either direction exists at the corresponding or any higher tier.
Short: For any pullback band, both constituent stop directions are bearish AND close < upper band boundary AND close[2] >= upper band boundary[2] AND, if pullback confirmation is enabled, close[1] < upper band boundary[1] AND absolute bias score > score floor AND applicable volume confirmation passes AND the corresponding short, intermediate, or long bearish flip is absent AND, for the chart/short band, the higher-timeframe lock is disabled OR the long stop direction is bearish AND, if signal priority is enabled, no qualifying flip in either direction exists at the corresponding or any higher tier.
Long: Short, intermediate, and long stop directions are bullish AND at least one was bearish on the previous chart bar AND absolute bias score > score floor.
Short: Short, intermediate, and long stop directions are bearish AND at least one was bullish on the previous chart bar AND absolute bias score > score floor.
