Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range session, retained after that session ends until the next opening-range session begins.
Long: On the selected higher-timeframe bar, the shorter-period EMA of close > the longer-period EMA of close. Use either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming, as configured.
Short: On the selected higher-timeframe bar, the shorter-period EMA of close <= the longer-period EMA of close. Use either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming, as configured.
Long: The opening-range session has ended AND the chart bar opens within the configured entry session AND close > opening-range high + breakout buffer AND previous close <= opening-range high + breakout buffer AND close > open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND (chart-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR close > chart-timeframe VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR (higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA > higher-timeframe longer-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe VWAP))). Use the configured completed or forming higher-timeframe bar.
Short: The opening-range session has ended AND the chart bar opens within the configured entry session AND close < opening-range low - breakout buffer AND previous close >= opening-range low - breakout buffer AND close < open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND (chart-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR close < chart-timeframe VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR (higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA < higher-timeframe longer-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe VWAP))). Use the configured completed or forming higher-timeframe bar.
