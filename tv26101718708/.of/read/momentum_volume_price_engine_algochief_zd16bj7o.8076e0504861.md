Calculation: Directional close count = number of closes above (bullish) or below (bearish) the preceding bar’s close over the momentum lookback
Calculation: Volume surge = percentage by which the shorter-lookback average volume exceeds the longer-lookback average volume
Calculation: Lookback price change = percentage change from the close at the start of the lookback to the current close
Long: Bullish directional close count meets its minimum AND volume surge meets its minimum AND lookback price change meets its positive threshold
Short: Bearish directional close count meets its minimum AND volume surge meets its minimum AND lookback price change meets its negative threshold
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR at entry * stop-loss multiple
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry * stop-loss multiple
