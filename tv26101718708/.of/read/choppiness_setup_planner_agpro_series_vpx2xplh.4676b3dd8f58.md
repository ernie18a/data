Calculation: Normalize(x, lower, upper) = 100 × clamp((x − lower) / (upper − lower), 0, 1); return 0 when the bounds are equal. Use an independently configurable pair of bounds for each normalized component.
Calculation: Path efficiency = clamp(abs(close − close at the efficiency lookback) / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, 0, 1); use 0 when the denominator is effectively zero.
Calculation: Range tightness = Normalize(longer-baseline average of the rolling high-minus-low range divided into the current rolling high-minus-low range, upper ratio bound, lower ratio bound); use a ratio of 1 when the baseline is effectively zero.
Calculation: Wick noise = simple moving average of 100 × (bar range − absolute candle body) / bar range. Use a small positive price tolerance as the minimum bar range.
Calculation: Chop score = EMA of a weighted sum of Choppiness Index, 100 minus normalized ADX, 100 × (1 − path efficiency), range tightness, and wick noise. Weights descend in that order except that path efficiency has a greater weight than ADX.
Calculation: Chop range = the highest high and lowest low during each uninterrupted run of bars whose chop score is at least the chop threshold. Its age is the number of bars in that run. Retain the most recently completed range, its age, and its ending bar until another range completes.
Calculation: Decision range = the developing chop range while chop score is at least the chop threshold; otherwise the most recently completed chop range. Range position = clamp((close − range low) / (range high − range low), 0, 1), or 0.5 if the range is effectively zero. Edge pressure = 200 × abs(range position − 0.5).
Calculation: Age quality = normalized decision range age, with the lower bound equal to the minimum range age and the upper bound a larger multiple of it. Release quality, ADX lift quality, and efficiency lift quality are the normalized changes over the same lookback in, respectively, earlier chop score minus current chop score, current ADX minus earlier ADX, and current path efficiency minus earlier path efficiency. Use zero change when the earlier value is unavailable. ATR lift quality = normalized ratio of ATR to its longer simple moving average; use a ratio of 1 if the average is effectively zero.
Calculation: Readiness score = EMA of a weighted sum of age quality, release quality, ADX lift quality, efficiency lift quality, ATR lift quality, and edge pressure. Age and release have equal weights; the remaining weights decrease in the listed order.
Calculation: Volume quality = normalized current volume divided by its simple moving average, with a ratio of 1 when the average is zero; alternatively use a neutral midpoint when volume participation is disabled.
Calculation: Release quality score = weighted sum of readiness score, age quality, normalized release distance, normalized candle body percentage, directional close quality, and volume quality, clamped to 0–100. Weights decrease in that order except that release distance has a greater weight than age. Release distance is the absolute distance from close to the released boundary divided by ATR, normalized from the release buffer to an upper distance bound. Candle body percentage is 100 × abs(close − open) divided by the bar range. Directional close quality is 100 × (close − low) / bar range for an upside release and 100 × (high − close) / bar range for a downside release. Use a small positive price tolerance as the minimum bar range and ATR.
Calculation: Plan levels = at each valid release, store the close as entry and replace any previous plan. For a long, raw invalidation is the completed range high minus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the invalidation buffer; risk is the greater of entry minus raw invalidation and a minimum fraction of entry-bar ATR; invalidation is entry minus risk. For a short, raw invalidation is the completed range low plus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the invalidation buffer; risk is the greater of raw invalidation minus entry and the same minimum fraction of entry-bar ATR; invalidation is entry plus risk. Keep these levels fixed until the plan is replaced or ends.
Long: Chop score is below the chop threshold AND the most recently completed chop range age is at least the minimum range age AND bars since that range ended are at most the range watch limit AND close is above the completed range high plus current ATR multiplied by the release buffer AND previous close is at or below that same current-bar boundary AND release quality score is at least the minimum plan quality.
Short: Chop score is below the chop threshold AND the most recently completed chop range age is at least the minimum range age AND bars since that range ended are at most the range watch limit AND close is below the completed range low minus current ATR multiplied by the release buffer AND previous close is at or above that same current-bar boundary AND release quality score is at least the minimum plan quality.
Exit: long, bars since the latest long plan entry are greater than zero AND close is at or below its invalidation level OR bars since the latest long plan entry exceed the maximum plan age.
Exit: short, bars since the latest short plan entry are greater than zero AND close is at or above its invalidation level OR bars since the latest short plan entry exceed the maximum plan age.
