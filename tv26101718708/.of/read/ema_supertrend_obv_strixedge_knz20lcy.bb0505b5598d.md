Calculation: EMA stack score = +2 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA and close > all three; +1 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA or close > slow EMA; −2 if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA and close < all three; −1 if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA or close < slow EMA; otherwise 0. The fast, mid, and slow EMA lookbacks are progressively longer.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added when close rises and subtracted when close falls. OBV flow score = +1 if OBV > its moving average and that average is rising; −1 if OBV ≤ its moving average and that average is falling; otherwise 0. The slope compares the moving average with its value a longer lookback ago.
Calculation: Trend score = EMA stack score + Supertrend direction score (+1 when Supertrend is below price, otherwise −1) + OBV flow score.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above mid EMA; OR Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish; OR EMA stack score > 0; OR close > slow EMA; OR OBV > its moving average AND the moving average is rising; OR trend score ≥ 1; OR at least 3 of the 4 configured timeframe trend scores are > 0. For calculations on a higher timeframe, use its latest available bar (completed historically; possibly forming in real time); on a lower timeframe, use the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Fast EMA crosses below mid EMA; OR Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish; OR EMA stack score < 0; OR close < slow EMA; OR OBV ≤ its moving average AND the moving average is falling; OR trend score ≤ −1; OR at least 3 of the 4 configured timeframe trend scores are < 0. For calculations on a higher timeframe, use its latest available bar (completed historically; possibly forming in real time); on a lower timeframe, use the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
