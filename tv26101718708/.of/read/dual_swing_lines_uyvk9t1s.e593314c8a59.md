Calculation: pivot high/low(n) = bar whose high/low is the extreme of the n bars before and n bars after it; known only n bars later. Slow pivots use a larger n than fast pivots. All on chart timeframe.
Calculation: SlowHigh/SlowLow = most recent confirmed slow pivot high/low.
Calculation: Break = bullish when close > SlowHigh and previous close <= SlowHigh; bearish when close < SlowLow and previous close >= SlowLow. A break is accepted only if no earlier accepted break is still waiting for its fast pivot AND no fast pivot has been used since the last confirmed slow pivot (high or low). A new slow pivot clears the second block.
Calculation: FastLevel = the first fast pivot after an accepted break whose pivot bar is later than the break bar. After a bearish break use a fast pivot high; after a bullish break use a fast pivot low. Only one per break. It stays valid for a fixed number of bars after the pivot bar. It is invalidated by the first bar whose high > level (high-type) or low < level (low-type) that does not trigger the signal. It can trigger on its own confirmation bar.
Long: valid FastLevel is a fast pivot high (from a bearish break), AND on the first bar with high > FastLevel inside its validity window: close > FastLevel AND volume <= previous bar volume * max ratio AND range > 0 AND (close - low)/(high - low) >= min fraction. Entry price = close.
Short: valid FastLevel is a fast pivot low (from a bullish break), AND on the first bar with low < FastLevel inside its validity window: close < FastLevel AND volume <= previous bar volume * max ratio AND range > 0 AND (high - close)/(high - low) >= min fraction. Entry price = close.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(at entry bar) * multiplier OR (bar immediately after the entry bar has close < open)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(at entry bar) * multiplier OR (bar immediately after the entry bar has close > open)
