Calculation: Entry RSI = RSI of lower-timeframe closes, sampled from the last completed lower-timeframe bar for each chart bar; compare successive chart-bar samples.
Calculation: Averaging RSI = RSI of lower-timeframe closes, sampled from the last completed lower-timeframe bar for each chart bar; its timeframe, lookback, and threshold are independently configurable from the entry RSI.
Calculation: Averaging-order threshold for order k = base entry price × (1 − cumulative deviation), where cumulative deviation is the sum of the first k deviation steps, starting with the first-step deviation and multiplying each subsequent step by the deviation-step multiplier. Order k has quote-currency size equal to the first averaging-order size multiplied by the order-size multiplier to the power k − 1.
Calculation: Average entry price = total quote-currency cost of the base and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order's quote-currency size divided by its fill close. Fill the base and averaging orders at the signal bar's close; process base entry, at most one averaging order, and exit in that order on each bar, and reset the order count and position totals on exit.
Long: Position is flat AND the active-date filter is disabled OR position is flat AND chart-bar datetime is within the inclusive active-date window; AND previous chart-bar entry RSI > entry threshold AND current entry RSI <= entry threshold.
Long: Position is long AND the active-date filter is disabled OR position is long AND chart-bar datetime is within the inclusive active-date window; AND filled averaging-order count < maximum count AND averaging RSI < averaging threshold AND close <= the threshold for the next unfilled averaging order.
Exit: long, (the active-date filter is disabled OR chart-bar datetime is within the inclusive active-date window) AND close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage).
