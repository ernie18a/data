Calculation: Volume peaks = Recalculate at a configurable chart-bar interval, retaining the previous peaks between recalculations. Over the profile lookback, divide the range from lowest low to highest high into a configurable number of bins, with bin spacing equal to range divided by bin count minus one. For each bar with high > low, add volume × bin spacing / (high − low) to every bin from floor((low − range low) / bin spacing) through floor((high − range low) / bin spacing), clipped to the bin array. If the range is less than a configurable minimum price increment times a configurable multiplier, or the largest bin volume is zero, there are no peaks. A candidate bin has positive volume, at least as much as both neighbors and more than at least one; treat missing neighbors as negative. Repeatedly select the highest remaining candidate, breaking ties by lowest bin index, while its volume is at least a configurable fraction of the maximum bin volume and the configurable peak-count limit has not been reached. Its price is range low + bin spacing × (bin index + 0.5). After selection, discard candidates within a configurable rounded number of bins on either side.
Calculation: Order block = At confirmation of a pivot low or high with equal configurable left and right pivot lengths, classify a candle as green when close > open and otherwise as non-green. For a pivot low, create a bullish block from the pivot candle if it is non-green and the next candle is green; otherwise, if the pivot is green and the preceding candle is non-green, create a bullish block from the preceding candle; otherwise, if the pivot, preceding candle, and next candle are all green, create a bearish block from the preceding candle. For a pivot high, create a bearish block from the pivot candle if it is green and the next candle is non-green; otherwise, if the pivot is non-green and the preceding candle is green, create a bearish block from the preceding candle; otherwise, if the pivot, preceding candle, and next candle are all non-green, create a bullish block from the preceding candle. The block bounds are either the source candle's high and low or its body bounds, according to a configurable choice. Create it only if its height is at least a configurable multiple of the simple moving average of absolute candle bodies over a configurable lookback ending on the previous chart bar. Its confluence bounds are always the source candle's high and low.
Calculation: Fair value gap = A bullish gap has current low > high two bars earlier, with bounds at those two prices; a bearish gap has current high < low two bars earlier, with bounds at those two prices. Create the gap only if its height is at least a configurable multiple of the simple moving average of absolute candle bodies over a configurable lookback ending on the previous chart bar. Its confluence bounds are its gap bounds.
Calculation: Active zone = An order block or fair value gap starts active with its previous-inside state false. On each bar, before testing signals, update existing zones' confluence; newly created zones use confluence at creation. Confluence means at least one current volume peak lies between the zone's confluence bounds expanded on both sides by a configurable multiple of ATR. Test the current close against the zone bounds, inclusive, and then store that inside state for the next bar. Deactivate a bullish zone when close < its lower bound and a bearish zone when close > its upper bound, regardless of confluence. After signal processing, remove zones older than a configurable maximum age measured from the source candle for order blocks or from two bars before gap creation for gaps. Maintain separate configurable capacity limits for order blocks and gaps; when creating a zone at capacity, remove the oldest stored zone, including inactive zones.
Calculation: Zone volume dominance = At zone creation, sum contributions from every bar from the zone's source bar through the creation bar, inclusive. For each overlapping bar, where high > zone bottom and low < zone top, let range = max(high − low, configurable minimum price increment) and overlapping volume = volume × (min(high, zone top) − max(low, zone bottom)) / range. Add overlapping volume × (close − low) / range to bullish volume and overlapping volume × (high − close) / range to bearish volume. On each subsequent bar while the zone is active, add the current bar's contributions in the same way.
Long: A confluent active bearish zone AND close > its upper bound.
Long: A confluent active bullish zone AND its stored previous-inside state is true AND close > its upper bound.
Long: A confluent active zone AND its bullish volume >= its bearish volume.
Short: A confluent active bullish zone AND close < its lower bound.
Short: A confluent active bearish zone AND its stored previous-inside state is true AND close < its lower bound.
Short: A confluent active zone AND its bullish volume < its bearish volume.
