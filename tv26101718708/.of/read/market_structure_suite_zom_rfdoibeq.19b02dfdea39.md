Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the same lookback number of bars on both sides; confirm after the bars on the right are complete.
Calculation: Structural retracement level = latest swing high minus (absolute difference between latest swing high and latest swing low × retracement fraction) for bullish bias; latest swing low plus that amount for bearish bias.
Long: A confirmed swing high is higher than the previous confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is higher than the previous confirmed swing low OR bullish bias is set when both high and low of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar exceed the previous higher-timeframe bar's high and low OR price makes a bullish RSI divergence at a confirmed RSI pivot low (price low below its previous RSI-pivot low and RSI higher than at that previous pivot) OR low is below the previous completed daily bar's low and close is above that daily low OR a signal is armed, bullish bias is set by a higher confirmed swing high, both latest swing prices exist, low is below the bullish structural retracement level, close is above open, and close is above the previous bar's high.
Short: A confirmed swing high is lower than or equal to the previous confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is lower than or equal to the previous confirmed swing low OR bearish bias is set when both high and low of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar are below the previous higher-timeframe bar's high and low OR price makes a bearish RSI divergence at a confirmed RSI pivot high (price high above its previous RSI-pivot high and RSI lower than at that previous pivot) OR high is above the previous completed daily bar's high and close is below that daily high OR a signal is armed, bearish bias is set by a lower confirmed swing low, both latest swing prices exist, high is above the bearish structural retracement level, close is below open, and close is below the previous bar's low.
