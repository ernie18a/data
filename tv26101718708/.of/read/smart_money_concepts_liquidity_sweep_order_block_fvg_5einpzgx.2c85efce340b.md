Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows, using equal numbers of bars on either side. On confirmation, add the pivot to the first same-side pool within an ATR-scaled tolerance and replace that pool's price with its running mean; otherwise create a pool. Keep a bounded number of pools per side, evicting the oldest. Updating a pool resets its update bar but does not reset its swept state. A pool can be swept only after its update bar and only once.
Calculation: Sweep = an unswept high pool is swept when high exceeds its price and, if close-back confirmation is enabled, close is below it; an unswept low pool is swept when low is below its price and, if confirmation is enabled, close is above it. Record the latest sweep bar and direction; if both sides sweep together, record the high-pool sweep direction.
Calculation: Displacement = a bullish or bearish candle whose absolute body divided by ATR is at least a threshold. A displacement is sweep-born when the most recent sweep occurred within a lookback and was in the corresponding reversal direction.
Calculation: Zones = on each confirmed bar, create a bullish fair value gap from high two bars earlier to current low when bullish displacement occurs, the gap is positive and its height meets an ATR-scaled minimum; mirror this for bearish displacement, from current high to low two bars earlier. Create a bullish order block spanning the previous bar's low to high when bullish displacement follows a bearish candle; mirror for a bearish order block. If the corresponding gap lies entirely inside the order block, keep the gap and suppress that order block. Gap and order-block detection can each be enabled independently. Keep a bounded number of zones, evicting the oldest. A zone becomes spent when close, or optionally the wick, passes strictly beyond its far edge. A zone can arm only once and only after its creation bar; mark it armed on its first eligible touch even if its reward-to-risk test fails.
Calculation: Volume profile = at a configurable bar interval, divide the rolling high-low range into equal price bins and distribute each bar's volume equally among the bins its range intersects. The point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest bin on ties. Expand the value area from that bin by repeatedly adding the larger adjacent bin, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the specified volume fraction is covered. When the point of control moves by more than one current bin width, retain the previous point as a naked point of control, subject to a capacity limit; mark it tested when a later bar spans it.
Calculation: Delta = when order flow is enabled and volume exists, sum the volumes of completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, positive for close above open and negative for close below open. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable or has at most one bar, or proxy mode is selected, use volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or zero for a zero-range bar. Otherwise delta is zero. Normalized delta is delta divided by chart-bar volume, clipped to the range −1 to 1; cumulative delta is the running sum of delta.
Calculation: Zone score = at formation, take the weighted sum of displacement strength min(1, body / ATR / (2 × displacement threshold)); participation min(1, max(0, volume / its moving average − 1)), or one-half if volume is absent; sweep-born status as 1 or 0; gap height / ATR divided by its normalization scale and capped at 1, or zero for an order block; higher-timeframe alignment as 1 when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for a bullish zone or at or below its EMA for a bearish zone, otherwise 0; location as 1 when the formation close is at or below the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low for a bullish zone or above it for a bearish zone, otherwise 0, or one-half when disabled; profile location as 1 if the zone contains the point of control or an untested naked point of control, otherwise 0.6 if it overlaps the value area, otherwise 0, or one-half when the profile is disabled or volume is absent; and order flow as max(0, normalized delta × zone direction), or one-half when order flow is disabled or volume is absent. Divide by the sum of weights and scale to a percentage; use zero if the sum of weights is zero.
Calculation: Setup geometry = at an eligible zone touch, use the near zone edge as entry and the far edge plus an outward ATR-scaled buffer as stop. Risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Select the nearest unswept opposing pool beyond entry or, in the alternative mode, the nearest untested naked point of control beyond entry; if no selected target exists, or fixed-reward mode is selected, use a fixed risk multiple. Reward-to-risk is target distance divided by risk, capped at a maximum; the target is entry plus or minus the capped reward distance.
Long: Low is below an unswept low pool AND, if close-back confirmation is enabled, close is above that pool.
Short: High is above an unswept high pool AND, if close-back confirmation is enabled, close is below that pool.
Long: Bullish displacement occurs AND an enabled bullish fair value gap or bullish order block is created.
Short: Bearish displacement occurs AND an enabled bearish fair value gap or bearish order block is created.
Long: A bullish zone is unspent and unarmed AND the current bar is after its creation bar AND low is at or below its top AND high is at or above its bottom AND the zone is not penetrated beyond its far edge under the selected mitigation rule AND its formation score is at least the entry threshold AND risk is positive AND capped reward-to-risk is at least the minimum.
Short: A bearish zone is unspent and unarmed AND the current bar is after its creation bar AND low is at or below its top AND high is at or above its bottom AND the zone is not penetrated beyond its far edge under the selected mitigation rule AND its formation score is at least the entry threshold AND risk is positive AND capped reward-to-risk is at least the minimum.
Long: Order flow is enabled AND volume exists AND low is at or below the rolling lowest low AND cumulative delta is above its rolling lowest value over the same lookback.
Short: Order flow is enabled AND volume exists AND high is at or above the rolling highest high AND cumulative delta is below its rolling highest value over the same lookback.
Long: Close is at or below the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low.
Short: Close is above the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close is above its higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close is at or below its higher-timeframe EMA.
Long: Order flow is enabled AND volume exists AND the EMA of normalized delta is above its positive threshold.
Short: Order flow is enabled AND volume exists AND the EMA of normalized delta is below its negative threshold.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has low at or below the setup stop OR high at or above the setup target OR elapsed bars reach the grading horizon; if stop and target are both touched, stop takes precedence.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has high at or above the setup stop OR low at or below the setup target OR elapsed bars reach the grading horizon; if stop and target are both touched, stop takes precedence.
