Calculation: Chart trendline level = value at the current bar of a straight line through two pivot highs (resistance) or two pivot lows (support); use candle highs/lows or body extremes as the pivot source. Build live lines from shorter-lookback pivots and confirmed lines from longer-lookback pivots. Validate candidate lines by limiting intervening pierces beyond an ATR tolerance; rank valid lines by touch count within an ATR tolerance, then span, and remove near-duplicate projected lines.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trendline level = value at the current chart bar of a straight line through two higher-timeframe pivot highs (resistance) or lows (support), optionally anchored at the highest high or lowest low in a higher-timeframe lookback window. Use higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming; calculate the line in price or log-price space.
Long: For any live, confirmed, or higher-timeframe resistance line, the last N consecutive closes are above the line by more than the corresponding ATR pierce tolerance.
Long: The current close is above the dominant confirmed resistance or higher-timeframe resistance line by more than its ATR pierce tolerance, and the prior bar did not satisfy that same condition.
Short: For any live, confirmed, or higher-timeframe support line, the last N consecutive closes are below the line by more than the corresponding ATR pierce tolerance.
Short: The current close is below the dominant confirmed support or higher-timeframe support line by more than its ATR pierce tolerance, and the prior bar did not satisfy that same condition.
