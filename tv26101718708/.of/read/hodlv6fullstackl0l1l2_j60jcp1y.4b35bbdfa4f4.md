Calculation: Regime = classify the chart-timeframe close EMAs as bullish when short > medium > long, bearish when short < medium < long, and range otherwise; bullish or bearish is strong when ADX exceeds its threshold and weak otherwise.
Calculation: Drawdown band = compare (close - highest close since the start of the data) / highest close since the start of the data with five increasingly negative thresholds; assign the deepest satisfied band, or zero if none is satisfied.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted sum of a regime score, a momentum score, and a volatility score. Assign the regime score in descending order to strong bull, weak bull, range, weak bear, and strong bear. Momentum score is a linear function of percentage close change over a lookback, clamped to a minimum and maximum. Volatility score is the maximum score minus the percentile rank of ATR / close over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Asset score = clamp the sum of drawdown, RSI, regime, long-EMA distance, and ATR / close component scores minus an overheat penalty. Drawdown scores increase with band depth. RSI scores decrease across ordered RSI thresholds. Regime scores decrease in the order range, strong bear, weak bear, weak bull, strong bull. Long-EMA distance scores decrease across ordered thresholds of (close - long EMA) / long EMA. ATR / close scores increase across ordered thresholds. Apply the penalty when RSI exceeds its overheat threshold AND long-EMA distance exceeds its overheat threshold.
Calculation: Final score = weighted sum of trend score, asset score, and a constant neutral macro score, with macro data disabled.
Long: Final score >= light-buy threshold.
Exit: long, final score < light-buy threshold.
