Calculation: Sweep distance = ATR multiplied by a parameter, clamped between minimum and maximum distance parameters.
Calculation: Support and resistance = respectively the low and high of each enabled level source: the completed opening-range session, the latest premarket session accumulated through the current bar, and the last completed daily bar. The daily timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe according to their relative durations. For each side, select the available level closest to the current close; break equal-distance ties in the order listed. Opening-range levels are unavailable until the session ends and become unavailable again when the next opening-range session starts.
Calculation: Sweep and reclaim state = maintain separate long and short states. A new sweep stores its selected level and bar index and activates that side. An active sweep expires when the current bar index minus its sweep bar index exceeds the timeout parameter, clearing its active and reclaim-ready states before evaluating reclaims. Each reclaim sets reclaim-ready and replaces the stored reclaim high or low with the current bar's high or low. A trigger clears both states after evaluating reclaims and triggers. Reclaim-ready otherwise persists, including outside the active session. Evaluate triggers using the previous bar's reclaim-ready state and stored reclaim price, even if the current bar's sweep has just expired.
Long: Current bar is in the active session AND support is available AND low <= support - sweep distance AND close < support AND long sweep is inactive AND long reclaim-ready is false.
Long: Current bar is in the active session AND long sweep is active after timeout processing AND close > stored swept support AND close > open.
Long: Current bar is in the active session AND previous bar's long reclaim-ready state is true AND high > previous bar's stored reclaim high AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume / SMA of volume >= volume threshold).
Short: Current bar is in the active session AND resistance is available AND high >= resistance + sweep distance AND close > resistance AND short sweep is inactive AND short reclaim-ready is false.
Short: Current bar is in the active session AND short sweep is active after timeout processing AND close < stored swept resistance AND close < open.
Short: Current bar is in the active session AND previous bar's short reclaim-ready state is true AND low < previous bar's stored reclaim low AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume / SMA of volume >= volume threshold).
