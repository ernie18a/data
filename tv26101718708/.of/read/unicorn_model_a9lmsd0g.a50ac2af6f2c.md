Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a chart-bar high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over configurable numbers of bars on its left and right; it becomes available after the right-hand bars close.
Calculation: Equilibrium = the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high and low, provided the high exceeds the low.
Calculation: Midnight open = the open of the first intraday chart bar in the midnight hour in a configurable session timezone, retained until the next such bar.
Calculation: Automatic bias score = the number of available references among midnight open and equilibrium above the close minus the number below the close; equal references contribute zero. The selected bias is this score's sign unless manually overridden to bullish or bearish.
Calculation: Imbalance zone = for a bullish fair value gap, the interval from the high two bars ago to the current low when current low exceeds that high by at least a configurable ATR multiple; for a bearish gap, the interval from current high to the low two bars ago when that low exceeds current high by at least that multiple. Optionally extend either edge to include a body gap between adjacent bars in the three-bar pattern. A bullish suspension block instead spans the first bar's close to the current open when all three bars close above their opens and each of the latter two opens exceeds the preceding close; a bearish block mirrors these conditions. Both joins must be contiguous in time, and the block's height must meet the same ATR threshold. Prefer a suspension block when both patterns occur in the same direction.
Calculation: Live imbalance zones = retain new imbalance zones subject to a configurable total cap, removing the oldest first. Optionally, when a new zone overlaps existing zones of either direction, discard it if any overlapping zone is at least as tall and remove each overlapping zone that is shorter. Remove a zone when it inverts or becomes stale. A zone becomes stale when the number of bars since its last high-low overlap with price exceeds a configurable limit and close is outside the zone by more than a configurable multiple of the recent highest-high minus lowest-low range.
Calculation: Inverted imbalance zone = a live bullish imbalance whose close falls below its bottom becomes bearish; a live bearish imbalance whose close rises above its top becomes bullish. Retain inverted zones subject to a configurable total cap, removing the oldest first. Count cumulative closes beyond the adverse edge; remove an inverted zone after a configurable number of such closes or when stale, except while it overlaps a live same-direction Unicorn, when it is retained. Apply this removal after testing for new Unicorns on each bar.
Calculation: Pending order block = after an upward displacement, retain the high-to-low range of the latest completed down-close run; after a downward displacement, retain the latest completed up-close run. An upward displacement is an up-close bar whose body is at least a configurable ATR multiple and whose close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high; downward displacement mirrors it. Require the run to have ended within a configurable number of bars, use each run only once per direction, and optionally require the displacement close to extend beyond the run by a configurable multiple of its height. Doji bars end runs. Apply the same optional overlap-based largest-zone retention as for imbalance zones, across pending blocks and breakers, and cap their combined count by removing the oldest. Remove stale pending blocks.
Calculation: Breaker = a pending bearish order block becomes a bullish breaker when a later close exceeds its top; a pending bullish order block becomes a bearish breaker when a later close falls below its bottom. Starting on the following bar, remove a breaker when its cumulative adverse-edge closes reach a configurable count, it becomes stale, or an external liquidity level in its direction beyond the breaker is taken. External liquidity is the highest live high-side level or lowest live low-side level before the bar's raids. Live levels comprise capped confirmed swing highs and lows and, optionally, capped highs and lows of completed higher-timeframe periods; remove a high-side level when high exceeds it and a low-side level when low falls below it, and remove stale levels using the same stale-zone rule. Process new levels and raids before breakers.
Calculation: Unicorn = an existing breaker that survives its current-bar retirement checks and overlaps a live same-direction inverted imbalance zone, with interval endpoints included. A newly flipped breaker is first eligible on the next bar. Optionally require the selected bias to be nonbearish for a bullish breaker or nonbullish for a bearish breaker. Once confirmed, the Unicorn remains confirmed until its breaker is removed; a newer Unicorn supersedes older Unicorns.
Long: A retained bullish imbalance zone is created.
Short: A retained bearish imbalance zone is created.
Long: A bullish Unicorn is confirmed.
Short: A bearish Unicorn is confirmed.
Long: Selected bias is bullish.
Short: Selected bias is bearish.

Short: Close is greater than or equal to the midnight open.

Short: Close is greater than or equal to equilibrium.
