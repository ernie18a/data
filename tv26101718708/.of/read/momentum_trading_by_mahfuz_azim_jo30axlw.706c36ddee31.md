Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI(close); RSI lookback and EMA smoothing lookbacks are independently parameterized.
Calculation: Fast QQE line = a trailing band around Smoothed RSI. Band distance is an EMA-smoothed EMA of absolute bar-to-bar changes in Smoothed RSI, multiplied by a factor. The lower band trails upward while Smoothed RSI stays above it; the upper band trails downward while Smoothed RSI stays below it. The active line is the lower band in an upward trend and upper band in a downward trend; trend switches when Smoothed RSI crosses the prior opposite band.
Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow moving averages = selected moving average types of close, with progressively longer lookbacks. The alternate ribbon uses the same types and longer lookbacks than the corresponding primary ribbon, on the chart timeframe.
Long: Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses above the Fast QQE line OR Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 plus the threshold; for entry signals, use the selected signal from these three and evaluate it on the prior completed chart bar, AND the long trend filter passes (medium moving average is rising over its lookback; when the alternate ribbon is used, its medium moving average is also rising and close is above the primary medium moving average; if the moving average filter is enabled, either close is above the primary medium moving average, which is above the slow moving average, and the fast moving average is above the medium moving average, OR the primary medium moving average is above the alternate medium moving average, close is above the fast moving average, and the fast moving average is above the medium moving average; for a selected fast QQE line cross, Smoothed RSI is above the upper threshold or below the lower threshold when the threshold filter is enabled); position is flat.
Short: Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses below the Fast QQE line OR Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 minus the threshold; for entry signals, use the selected signal from these three and evaluate it on the prior completed chart bar, AND the short trend filter passes (medium moving average is falling over its lookback; when the alternate ribbon is used, its medium moving average is also falling and close is below the primary medium moving average; if the moving average filter is enabled, either close is below the primary medium moving average, which is below the slow moving average, and the fast moving average is below the medium moving average, OR the primary medium moving average is below the alternate medium moving average, close is below the fast moving average, and the fast moving average is below the medium moving average; for a selected fast QQE line cross, Smoothed RSI is above the upper threshold or below the lower threshold when the threshold filter is enabled); position is flat.
Exit: long, Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 OR crosses below the Fast QQE line OR crosses below the lower threshold.
Exit: short, Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 OR crosses above the Fast QQE line OR crosses above the upper threshold.
