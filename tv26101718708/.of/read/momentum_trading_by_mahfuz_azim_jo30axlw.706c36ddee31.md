Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI of the selected price source.
Calculation: QQE trailing line = Let distance be a configurable factor times the EMA of the EMA of the absolute bar-to-bar change in smoothed RSI, using length 2 × RSI length − 1 for both EMAs. Set candidate lower and upper bands to smoothed RSI minus and plus distance. The lower band is the maximum of its previous value and the candidate lower band when both the previous and current smoothed RSI exceed the previous lower band; otherwise it is the candidate lower band. The upper band is the minimum of its previous value and the candidate upper band when both the previous and current smoothed RSI are below the previous upper band; otherwise it is the candidate upper band. Set trend to +1 when smoothed RSI crosses the previous upper band, otherwise to −1 when the previous lower band crosses smoothed RSI, otherwise retain the previous trend, initially +1. The trailing line is the lower band for trend +1 and the upper band for trend −1. A cross in either direction qualifies in the trend calculation.
Calculation: Signal events = A bullish or bearish QQE event occurs on the first consecutive bar when smoothed RSI is respectively above or below the QQE trailing line. A bullish zero event occurs on the first bar when smoothed RSI is at or above the RSI midpoint; a bearish zero event occurs on the first bar when it is below the midpoint. A bullish or bearish channel event occurs on the first bar when smoothed RSI is respectively above the midpoint plus a configurable threshold or below the midpoint minus that threshold.
Calculation: Moving-average filters = Calculate fast, medium and slow selectable moving averages of the selected price source on chart bars, with respectively shorter, intermediate and longer lengths. Calculate an additional medium moving average on chart bars using the same type and a length multiplied by a configurable multiplier. Rising means the current average is greater than every value in its preceding configurable lookback; falling means it is less than every value in that lookback. The bullish filter requires: if the optional moving-average filter is enabled, source > medium > slow AND fast > medium when the multiplier equals one, otherwise medium > additional medium AND source > fast > medium; AND if the optional directional filter is enabled, medium is rising when the multiplier equals one, otherwise medium and additional medium are rising AND source > medium. The bearish filter reverses all inequalities and uses falling averages.
Calculation: Entry events = Select channel, QQE or zero events. A bullish or bearish entry event requires the corresponding selected event AND corresponding moving-average filter on the previous chart bar; for QQE events, if the optional threshold filter is enabled, the previous smoothed RSI must also be outside the threshold channel. If the optional first-entry-only setting is enabled, suppress an entry event when a previous entry event of the same direction has occurred more recently than an opposite entry event.
Calculation: Exit events = Select channel, QQE or zero exits. For a long exit, a channel selection requires a bearish channel event; a zero selection requires a bearish zero event; a QQE selection requires a bearish QQE event if QQE entries are selected, otherwise a bearish QQE event OR smoothed RSI below the midpoint OR smoothed RSI below the lower channel boundary. For a short exit, a channel selection requires a bullish channel event; a zero selection requires a bullish zero event; a QQE selection requires a bullish QQE event if QQE entries are selected, otherwise a bullish QQE event OR smoothed RSI at or above the midpoint OR a bullish channel event. An exit event occurs only on the first consecutive bar satisfying its selected condition.
Long: A bullish channel event occurs AND a long strategy entry does not occur on the same bar.
Long: A bullish QQE event occurs AND a bullish channel event does not occur AND a long strategy entry does not occur on the same bar.
Long: A bullish zero event occurs AND neither a bullish channel event nor a bullish QQE event nor a long strategy entry occurs on the same bar.
Long: A bullish entry event occurs AND the strategy state was flat on the previous bar AND datetime is within the configured backtest period.
Short: A bearish channel event occurs AND a short strategy entry does not occur on the same bar.
Short: A bearish QQE event occurs AND a bearish channel event does not occur AND a short strategy entry does not occur on the same bar.
Short: A bearish zero event occurs AND neither a bearish channel event nor a bearish QQE event nor a short strategy entry occurs on the same bar.
Short: A bearish entry event occurs AND no bullish entry event occurs AND the strategy state was flat on the previous bar AND datetime is within the configured backtest period.
Exit: long, a long exit event occurs AND the strategy state was long on the previous bar AND datetime is within the configured backtest period.
Exit: short, a short exit event occurs AND the strategy state was short on the previous bar AND datetime is within the configured backtest period.
