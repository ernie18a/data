Calculation: VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each trading-day boundary; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Upward VWAP event = close > VWAP AND previous close < previous VWAP; downward VWAP event = close < VWAP AND previous close > previous VWAP
Calculation: Bullish candle confirmation = (close > open AND close - open > candle-body fraction × (high - low)) OR (close > open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close AND previous close < previous open AND close > VWAP); bearish candle confirmation = (close < open AND open - close > candle-body fraction × (high - low)) OR (close < open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close AND previous close > previous open AND close < VWAP)
Calculation: Pivot support and resistance = using the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, pivot = (high + low + close) / 3, support levels = 2 × pivot - high and pivot - (high - low), resistance levels = 2 × pivot - low and pivot + (high - low); near support or resistance means absolute distance from close to the pivot or a corresponding level < a tolerance fraction × that bar's range
Calculation: Near psychological level = absolute distance from close to any generated level < a tolerance fraction × close; generate levels separately for one and two significant digits: reduce the digit count adjustment by one when close < 1, set unit = 10 raised to (truncate(log10(close)) - adjusted digit count + 1), and generate truncate(close / unit) × unit plus zero, half, or one unit
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = close and EMA of close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Long score = sum of the alignment weight for each of shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close and close > VWAP, the confirmation weight for each of upward VWAP event, bullish candle confirmation, near pivot support, and near psychological level, and the smaller trend weight for each of higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA and volume > volume SMA × surge multiplier; short score uses shorter EMA < longer EMA, close < VWAP, downward VWAP event, bearish candle confirmation, near pivot resistance, the same psychological-level condition, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA, and the same volume condition; alignment weight > confirmation weight > trend weight
Calculation: Long raw event = if confluence is required, an upward crossover of shorter EMA of close above longer EMA of close AND an upward VWAP event each occurred within the lookback ending on the current bar; otherwise, an upward EMA crossover OR upward VWAP event occurs on the current bar; short raw event uses the corresponding downward events
Calculation: Long trigger = long raw event is true AND previous-bar long raw event is false AND long score >= score threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time in America/New_York is within either configured session, inclusive of start and exclusive of end) AND bars since the last long trigger > cooldown; short trigger uses the short raw event, short score, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA, and bars since the last short trigger; each direction's cooldown resets on its trigger even if the trigger does not produce a label
Long: Long trigger AND (long score > score threshold OR (long score = score threshold AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND (near pivot support OR near psychological level)))
Short: Short trigger AND (short score > score threshold OR (short score = score threshold AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND (near pivot resistance OR near psychological level)))
Long: Shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close OR close > VWAP OR upward VWAP event OR bullish candle confirmation OR near pivot support OR near psychological level OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA OR volume > volume SMA × surge multiplier
Short: Shorter EMA of close < longer EMA of close OR close < VWAP OR downward VWAP event OR bearish candle confirmation OR near pivot resistance OR near psychological level OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA OR volume > volume SMA × surge multiplier
