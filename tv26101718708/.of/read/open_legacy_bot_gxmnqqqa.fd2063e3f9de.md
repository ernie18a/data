Calculation: Bullish gap = the most recently detected gap where low > high two bars earlier and the difference >= a configured multiple of ATR; its lower boundary is that earlier high, its upper boundary is the current low, and its age is the number of bars since detection. Before detecting a new gap on each bar, discard the existing gap if its age > 0 and <= the configured lifetime and low <= its upper boundary and close > open and close > its lower boundary, regardless of entry filters. A new gap replaces the existing gap.
Calculation: Bearish gap = the most recently detected gap where high < low two bars earlier and the difference >= a configured multiple of ATR; its upper boundary is that earlier low, its lower boundary is the current high, and its age is the number of bars since detection. Before detecting a new gap on each bar, discard the existing gap if its age > 0 and <= the configured lifetime and high >= its lower boundary and close < open and close < its upper boundary, regardless of entry filters. A new gap replaces the existing gap.
Calculation: Session membership = bar timestamp falls on an enabled day and within an enabled session in the configured timezone; for an overnight session, the day check uses the timestamp's calendar day. Entry room requires the bar's opening timestamp to be in a session and, if session-end closing is enabled, its closing time to be strictly inside that session with more than one chart bar's duration remaining before session end.
Calculation: News blackout = bar closing timestamp >= an enabled event timestamp minus the configured blackout duration AND < that event timestamp plus the same duration.
Long: Position is flat or short AND chart timeframe matches the configured lock or the lock is disabled AND entry room exists AND news blackout is false AND (configured method is gap pullback AND bullish gap age > 0 AND <= its lifetime AND low <= its upper boundary AND close > open AND close > its lower boundary OR configured method is EMA trend AND short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND close > short-period EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND the same conjunction was false on the previous bar) AND (sensitivity is aggressive OR close > trend-filter EMA AND (sensitivity is balanced OR last completed higher-timeframe close > last completed higher-timeframe trend-filter EMA AND ADX >= configured threshold)).
Short: Position is flat or long AND chart timeframe matches the configured lock or the lock is disabled AND entry room exists AND news blackout is false AND no qualified long signal exists on the bar AND (configured method is gap pullback AND bearish gap age > 0 AND <= its lifetime AND high >= its lower boundary AND close < open AND close < its upper boundary OR configured method is EMA trend AND short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND close < short-period EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND the same conjunction was false on the previous bar) AND (sensitivity is aggressive OR close < trend-filter EMA AND (sensitivity is balanced OR last completed higher-timeframe close < last completed higher-timeframe trend-filter EMA AND ADX >= configured threshold)).
Exit: long, high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR a short entry condition is met OR session-end closing is enabled AND the bar opens inside an enabled session and closes outside that same session OR news blackout is true and was false on the previous bar.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR a long entry condition is met OR session-end closing is enabled AND the bar opens inside an enabled session and closes outside that same session OR news blackout is true and was false on the previous bar.
