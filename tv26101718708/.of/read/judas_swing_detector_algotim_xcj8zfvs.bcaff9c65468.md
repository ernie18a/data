Calculation: Initial range = highest high and lowest low from the first in-session bar through the first in-session bar whose opening time is at least the configured duration after the session-start bar’s opening time, inclusive; lock the range after including that bar and reset it at the next session start.
Calculation: Pending sweep = a directional sweep stored with its bar index and extreme; accept at most one pending sweep per direction, clear it after a confirmed signal or when its age exceeds the timeout, and reset it at session start. Permit a new sweep only if that direction has not already produced a confirmed signal in the session. Confirmation can occur outside the session and on the sweep bar.
Calculation: Confidence score = rounded weighted mean of sweep depth, displacement, higher-timeframe separation, rejection quality, and speed; use zero if total weight is zero. Sweep depth is 100 times the lesser of 1 and the distance from the stored sweep extreme to the corresponding initial-range boundary divided by current ATR and a depth scale. Displacement is 100 times the lesser of 1 and current absolute candle body divided by current ATR and a displacement scale. Higher-timeframe separation is 100 times the lesser of 1 and the absolute difference between the higher-timeframe shorter and longer EMA divided by current chart-timeframe ATR and a separation scale. Use zero for these three factors when current ATR is zero. On the sweep bar, rejection quality is 100 times (close minus low) divided by candle range for a low sweep, or (high minus close) divided by candle range for a high sweep; use a neutral value when the range is zero. On later bars use a fixed rejection value. Speed is the greater of zero and a maximum score minus a per-bar penalty times (bars since sweep minus one).
Calculation: Post-sweep pivot = the latest confirmed pivot high for a low sweep or pivot low for a high sweep, with the same pivot lookback on each side and with the pivot bar strictly after the sweep bar; update it when a newer qualifying pivot confirms.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = shorter- and longer-period EMAs of higher-timeframe closes. On historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar as supplied by lookahead-off merging; on realtime chart bars use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Active entry zone = the interval between two configured retracements of the confirmed signal bar’s high-low range, measured downward from its high for a bullish signal or upward from its low for a bearish signal. Activate it at confirmation and deactivate it when close moves beyond the stored sweep extreme in the adverse direction. Reset stored sweep extremes at session start and replace them when a new same-direction sweep occurs.
Long: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA > higher-timeframe longer EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA < higher-timeframe longer EMA.
Long: Initial range is locked AND bar is in session AND no low sweep is pending AND no bullish signal has been confirmed in the session AND low < initial-range low AND close >= initial-range low AND initial-range low minus low >= current ATR times minimum sweep distance.
Short: Initial range is locked AND bar is in session AND no high sweep is pending AND no bearish signal has been confirmed in the session AND high > initial-range high AND close <= initial-range high AND high minus initial-range high >= current ATR times minimum sweep distance.
Long: Low sweep is pending AND its age <= timeout AND no bullish signal has been confirmed in the session AND (structure confirmation is disabled OR close > post-sweep pivot high) AND absolute candle body divided by current ATR >= displacement threshold AND (higher-timeframe bias filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA > higher-timeframe longer EMA) AND confidence score >= minimum score; use zero for the body-to-ATR ratio when ATR is zero.
Short: High sweep is pending AND its age <= timeout AND no bearish signal has been confirmed in the session AND (structure confirmation is disabled OR close < post-sweep pivot low) AND absolute candle body divided by current ATR >= displacement threshold AND (higher-timeframe bias filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA < higher-timeframe longer EMA) AND confidence score >= minimum score; use zero for the body-to-ATR ratio when ATR is zero.
Long: Bullish entry zone is active AND low <= zone upper boundary AND high >= zone lower boundary.
Short: Bearish entry zone is active AND low <= zone upper boundary AND high >= zone lower boundary.
