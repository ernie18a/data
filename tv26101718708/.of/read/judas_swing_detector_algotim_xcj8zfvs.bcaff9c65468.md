Calculation: Initial range = highest high and lowest low during the opening window of the selected session; lock after the window elapses.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback period.
Calculation: Minor swing high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; the pivot is confirmed only after those right-side bars complete.
Calculation: HTF fast/slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with the shorter/longer lookback, respectively, on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Confidence score = rounded weighted average of five 0–100 factors: sweep depth (pierce distance / ATR, capped at 1), confirming candle body / ATR (capped at 1.5), absolute HTF EMA separation / chart-timeframe ATR (capped at 0.5), rejection close position within the sweep candle’s range (bullish: (close−low)/range; bearish: (high−close)/range; use 70 when confirmation is on a later bar), and reversal speed (max(0, 100−20×(bars from sweep to confirmation−1))). Normalize by the sum of factor weights.
Long: During the selected session after the initial range locks, (low < initial range low AND close >= initial range low AND initial range low − low >= minimum ATR multiple) OR (a pending low-side sweep exists within its timeout AND (MSS disabled OR close > the latest confirmed post-sweep minor swing high) AND confirming candle body / ATR >= displacement threshold AND (HTF filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA) AND confidence score >= minimum threshold) OR (a confirmed bullish setup is active AND low <= bullish entry-zone top AND high >= bullish entry-zone bottom)
Short: During the selected session after the initial range locks, (high > initial range high AND close <= initial range high AND high − initial range high >= minimum ATR multiple) OR (a pending high-side sweep exists within its timeout AND (MSS disabled OR close < the latest confirmed post-sweep minor swing low) AND confirming candle body / ATR >= displacement threshold AND (HTF filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA) AND confidence score >= minimum threshold) OR (a confirmed bearish setup is active AND low <= bearish entry-zone top AND high >= bearish entry-zone bottom)
