Calculation: Zone swing = a higher-, lower-, or same-timeframe bar whose high is strictly higher, or whose low is strictly lower, than each of the specified number of bars on either side; confirm it one additional zone-timeframe bar after its right-side comparison bars. Use the last completed zone-timeframe bar when the zone timeframe is higher, the last completed intrabar when it is lower, and the chart bar when it is the same.
Calculation: Swing delta = clamp to [-100, 100] the swing bar's 100 × (close − open) / ATR × (volume / volume SMA) raised to a volume-weight exponent; use relative volume of 1 when the volume SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Neutral threshold = optionally EMA-smooth the lower rolling percentile of the absolute EMA-smoothed swing delta; calculate the delta EMA on every chart bar, replacing unavailable delta with zero, and append its absolute value to the rolling history whenever swing delta is available. Calculate the percentile by sorting the history and selecting index floor(history size × percentile), capped at the last index; use a fixed threshold until at least two observations exist, or whenever dynamic thresholds are disabled.
Calculation: Structure pivot = a chart-timeframe pivot high or low confirmed by the same number of bars on each side; retain the latest confirmed high and low separately and mark each unbroken until close crosses its level, resetting it when a new pivot of that side is confirmed.
Long: A zone swing is confirmed AND swing delta > neutral threshold.
Short: A zone swing is confirmed AND swing delta < −neutral threshold.
Long: The chart timeframe equals the designated structure timeframe AND close crosses above the latest unbroken structure pivot high.
Short: The chart timeframe equals the designated structure timeframe AND close crosses below the latest unbroken structure pivot low.
