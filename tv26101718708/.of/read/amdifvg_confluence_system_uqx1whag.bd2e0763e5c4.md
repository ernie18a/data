Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low at a pivot with the same configurable number of bars on each side; recognize it only when the right-side bars have completed. The latest and previous swings are updated before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Upward/downward structure break = close above/below the latest confirmed swing high/low AND previous close at or below/at or above that same current swing level.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = low below the previous confirmed swing low AND close above it / high above the previous confirmed swing high AND close below it, where previous means the swing before the latest confirmed swing.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish gap = low above high two bars ago / high below low two bars ago, with gap size at least the configurable ATR multiple when that filter is enabled. Store its lower and upper boundaries; discard the oldest stored gap when the configurable capacity is exceeded. A gap remains eligible for inversion after touches until it inverts or is discarded. A bullish/bearish inversion occurs when close first falls below the lower boundary of an eligible bullish gap / rises above the upper boundary of an eligible bearish gap; the resulting direction is bearish/bullish.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = sum of three terms, each +1 if its condition is true and -1 otherwise: close above long-term EMA, short-term EMA above medium-term EMA, and close above short-term EMA. Evaluate on each configured timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, for the chart timeframe use the current bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar available in the chart bar. Total bias is the sum across the configured timeframes.
Calculation: Windowed volume delta = rolling sum over a configurable window of volume for close above open, negative volume for close below open, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Bull score = 2 if total bias is at least the strong positive threshold, otherwise 1 if it is at least the lower positive threshold, plus 1 each for an upward structure break, bullish inversion, bullish sweep, close below the midpoint of the previous completed daily high and low, windowed volume delta above its previous value, close above the retained New York midnight open, bar time inside a configured London or New York session, and close below its value one delta window ago AND windowed volume delta above its value one delta window ago. The daily bar is higher or lower than the chart timeframe according to their durations; use the previous completed daily bar. The midnight open is the open of the latest chart bar identified as the start of the configured New York midnight session, retained until the next such bar.
Calculation: Bear score = 2 if total bias is at most the strong negative threshold, otherwise 1 if it is at most the higher negative threshold, plus 1 each for a downward structure break, bearish inversion, bearish sweep, close above the midpoint of the previous completed daily high and low, windowed volume delta below its previous value, close below the retained New York midnight open, bar time inside a configured London or New York session, and close above its value one delta window ago AND windowed volume delta below its value one delta window ago. Strong bias thresholds have greater absolute magnitude than mild thresholds and are symmetric around zero.
Long: Upward structure break OR bullish sweep OR bullish gap OR bullish inversion.
Short: Downward structure break OR bearish sweep OR bearish gap OR bearish inversion.
Long: Any configured timeframe bias OR total bias is at least the positive bias-label threshold.
Short: Any configured timeframe bias OR total bias is at most the negative bias-label threshold.
Long: Bull score > bear score.
Short: Bear score > bull score.
Long: Bull score >= configurable minimum score AND bull score > bear score.
Short: Bear score >= configurable minimum score AND bear score > bull score.
