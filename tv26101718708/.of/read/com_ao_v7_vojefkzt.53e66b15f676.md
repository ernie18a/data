Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a local high/low with the configured number of bars on each side; retain the latest two confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish structure = latest swing high > prior swing high AND latest swing low > prior swing low; bearish structure = latest swing high < prior swing high AND latest swing low < prior swing low.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish gap = current high < low two bars earlier. A gap retest occurs on a later bar when price reaches the gap and closes beyond its far edge in the gap's direction.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the latest bearish candle's high/low when close first breaks above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish order block = the latest bullish candle's high/low when close first breaks below the latest confirmed swing low. A retest occurs when the bar reaches the block and closes beyond its far edge in the block's direction.
Calculation: Fib retracement levels = use the absolute difference between the latest swing high and low as the range. Long levels are high minus 0.5, 0.618, or 0.786 of the range; short levels are low plus those fractions. The long zone is low <= long 0.5 level AND close >= long 0.786 level; the short zone is high >= short 0.5 level AND close <= short 0.786 level.
Calculation: Strong rise/fall = close has risen/fallen from its value at the configured lookback by at least the configured percentage.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < the prior swing low AND close > that swing low; bearish sweep = high > the prior swing high AND close < that swing high.
Calculation: Bullish momentum = RSI above 50 AND MACD line above signal line AND MACD histogram greater than its prior value; bearish momentum = RSI below 50 AND MACD line below signal line AND MACD histogram less than its prior value.
Calculation: Bullish accumulation mark = bullish sweep AND lower wick > 1.5 times body AND close > open AND volume > its configured moving average AND MFI rising; bearish distribution mark = bearish sweep AND upper wick > 1.5 times body AND close < open AND volume > its configured moving average AND MFI falling.
Long: confirmed bar AND close > EMA AND EMA rising AND bullish structure AND bullish momentum AND latest two swing highs are not approximately equal AND no bearish distribution mark AND long Fib zone AND (bullish gap retest OR bullish order block retest OR EMA within the configured percentage of the long 0.618 level OR bullish sweep) AND volume > its configured moving average AND close > open AND close > prior bar high AND NOT (strong rise AND NOT the long Fib zone) AND long setup was false on the prior bar.
Short: confirmed bar AND close < EMA AND EMA falling AND bearish structure AND bearish momentum AND latest two swing lows are not approximately equal AND no bullish accumulation mark AND short Fib zone AND (bearish gap retest OR bearish order block retest OR EMA within the configured percentage of the short 0.618 level OR bearish sweep) AND volume > its configured moving average AND close < open AND close < prior bar low AND NOT (strong fall AND NOT the short Fib zone) AND short setup was false on the prior bar.
