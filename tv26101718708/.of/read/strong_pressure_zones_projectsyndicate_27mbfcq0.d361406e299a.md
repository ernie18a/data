Calculation: ATR = average true range, falling back to the cumulative average high-low range when unavailable, with a minimum price increment floor.
Calculation: Zone = a band centered on a confirmed pivot low for a long zone or pivot high for a short zone; its fixed height is either ATR at confirmation times a multiplier or close at confirmation times a percentage. Pivots use configurable left and right confirmation lengths; an optional secondary pivot uses an equal left and right length. The zone's recorded birth bar is the confirmation bar minus the primary pivot's right length, including for secondary pivots.
Calculation: Eligible zone = a newly confirmed zone whose pivot-bar volume times high-low range, multiplied by a constant and normalized against the rolling minimum and maximum of that quantity on the confirmation bar, meets a minimum magnitude. Clamp the normalized value to the unit interval and apply a configurable exponent; if the rolling minimum equals the capped rolling maximum, replace the minimum with a configurable fraction of the maximum. If overlap suppression is enabled, reject a zone when its overlap with an existing unswept zone divided by the smaller band height meets an overlap threshold. Process primary lows, primary highs, secondary lows, then secondary highs; remove the oldest zones above the live-zone limit, and remove zones whose age from their recorded birth bar reaches the maximum age.
Calculation: Zone state = initially unswept. A long zone becomes swept when low is below its lower border minus an ATR sweep-depth multiple; a short zone becomes swept when high is above its upper border plus that multiple. Starting on the next bar, a long zone is reclaimed when close is above its selected lower border, midpoint, or upper border; a short zone is reclaimed when close is below its selected upper border, midpoint, or lower border. Reclaim returns the zone to unswept state. Otherwise, a swept long zone breaks when close is below its lower border minus an ATR acceptance multiple, or when elapsed bars since the sweep reach the reclaim window and close is below the lower border; a swept short zone breaks under the symmetric conditions above its upper border. A broken zone is either removed or flipped to the opposite side and reset to unswept. Reclaim takes precedence over break.
Long: An eligible long zone is created.
Short: An eligible short zone is created.
Long: A long zone is reclaimed AND reclaim alerts are enabled AND either the RSI filter is disabled OR the lowest RSI over its lookback is at or below the oversold threshold.
Short: A short zone is reclaimed AND reclaim alerts are enabled AND either the RSI filter is disabled OR the highest RSI over its lookback is at or above the overbought threshold.
