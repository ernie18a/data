Calculation: Pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the N bars before and N bars after it; signal when confirmed after those N following bars.
Calculation: Pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the N bars before and N bars after it; signal when confirmed after those N following bars.
Long: On a displayed timeframe, close > EMA and close > SMA and EMA > SMA; OR a pivot low is confirmed
Short: On a displayed timeframe, close < EMA and close < SMA and EMA < SMA; OR a pivot high is confirmed
