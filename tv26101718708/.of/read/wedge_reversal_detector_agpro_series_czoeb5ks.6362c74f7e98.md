Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs and lows confirmed using the same number of bars on each side; retain the latest configurable number of each.
Calculation: Wedge candidate = on each new pivot confirmation, scan every ordered pair of retained highs against every ordered pair of retained lows. Connect each pair with a straight line, extrapolated to any bar. The pairs must overlap in time; the span from the earlier first pivot to the later second pivot must meet the preview minimum, and the later second pivot must be within the candidate age limit. Both slopes must be positive with the lower slope greater than the upper for a rising wedge, or both negative with the upper slope less than the lower for a falling wedge. At the span endpoints, upper minus lower width must be positive and the ending width must exceed a configurable minimum positive price increment. Starting width must meet the preview ATR threshold, ending width divided by starting width must be at most the preview ratio, and absolute midpoint travel must meet the preview ATR threshold. A candidate is strict when it also meets the longer minimum span, higher starting-width threshold, lower ending-width ratio, higher midpoint-travel threshold, and has an additional retained pivot within the span and within an ATR tolerance of its corresponding rail. Measure ATR and all ATR thresholds at the scan bar. Rank candidates by a strict-candidate bonus plus a weighted sum of maturity and the later second-pivot bar index; maturity is the weighted sum of clamped compression and span scores, where compression is a scaled one-minus-width-ratio and span is a scaled span-to-strict-minimum-span ratio. Select the highest rank, keeping the first scanned candidate on ties; if no candidate qualifies, retain the previously selected candidate.
Calculation: Active wedge = replace the active wedge with the selected candidate when its pivot indices differ, its latest pivot is within the age limit, close is between its current extrapolated rails with an ATR tolerance, no broken wedge has an unresolved tracking episode, and the rearm cooldown since the last episode resolution has elapsed. Preserve its strict status and starting width from selection; expire it when its latest pivot exceeds the age limit and no episode is open. An active wedge can break only once. A break opens an episode, freezing the break close, starting width, ATR, and both current rail values. Resolve the episode on the first subsequent bar where the target is touched, the invalidation level is closed through, or the projection window expires; target touch takes precedence. Record the resolution bar for rearming. A resolved wedge remains active until replaced or expired.
Calculation: Radar slopes = divide the difference between the highest highs of the recent and preceding equal halves of a configurable lookback by the full lookback for the upper slope; use the corresponding lowest lows for the lower slope.
Calculation: Break target and invalidation = for a long break, target is break close plus starting width times a multiplier minus break-bar ATR times a zone half-width multiplier, and invalidation is the break-bar lower rail minus break-bar ATR times an invalidation multiplier. For a short break, target is break close minus starting width times the target multiplier plus break-bar ATR times the zone half-width multiplier, and invalidation is the break-bar upper rail plus break-bar ATR times the invalidation multiplier. Freeze these levels at the break.
Long: Active wedge is falling.
Short: Active wedge is rising.
Long: Radar is enabled AND sufficient lookback history exists AND active wedge is not strict AND radar upper slope < radar lower slope AND radar lower slope < 0.
Short: Radar is enabled AND sufficient lookback history exists AND active wedge is not strict AND radar lower slope > radar upper slope AND radar upper slope > 0.
Long: Active wedge is strict and falling AND it has not broken or been invalidated AND the current bar is later than its latest pivot AND the break cooldown has elapsed AND close > current upper rail + ATR break buffer when close confirmation is enabled, OR the same conditions AND high > current upper rail + ATR break buffer when close confirmation is disabled.
Short: Active wedge is strict and rising AND it has not broken or been invalidated AND the current bar is later than its latest pivot AND the break cooldown has elapsed AND close < current lower rail - ATR break buffer when close confirmation is enabled, OR the same conditions AND low < current lower rail - ATR break buffer when close confirmation is disabled.
Exit: long, a long-break episode is open AND a subsequent bar's high >= frozen long target, OR the active long break has not previously been invalidated AND close < frozen long invalidation, OR the long-break episode is open AND bars since break >= projection window.
Exit: short, a short-break episode is open AND a subsequent bar's low <= frozen short target, OR the active short break has not previously been invalidated AND close > frozen short invalidation, OR the short-break episode is open AND bars since break >= projection window.
