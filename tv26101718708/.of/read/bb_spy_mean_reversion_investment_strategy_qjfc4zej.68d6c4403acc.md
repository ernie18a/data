Calculation: Signal bands = Bollinger bands of close; optionally multiply the standard-deviation multiplier by clamp(1 + gain × (volatility-of-volatility / its SMA − 1), minimum scale, maximum scale), where volatility-of-volatility is the standard deviation of one-bar close rate of change and its SMA uses the same lookback. Use ratio 1 when that SMA is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Regime gate = true when disabled; in trend mode, Bollinger-band width percentile rank over its lookback > threshold AND absolute normalized basis slope > slope threshold; in mean-reversion mode, width percentile rank < 100 − threshold. Normalized basis slope = (Bollinger basis − basis at the slope lookback) / max(Bollinger-band width, minimum price increment). Use nonadaptive bands for these calculations.
Calculation: Long setup = close crosses above the lower signal band AND close > open AND close > long EMA of close. Short setup = close crosses below the upper signal band AND close < open AND close < short EMA of close AND ADX > threshold. The two EMA lengths are independent.
Calculation: Bias = the most recent long or short setup, ignoring the regime gate; initially unset. If both setups occur while bias is unset, choose long.
Calculation: Long plan = (long setup AND regime gate if the gate applies to longs, otherwise long setup) OR (bias is long AND position is not long AND (continuous rearming is enabled OR a long position exited since the previous bar)). Short plan = (short setup AND regime gate if the gate applies to shorts, otherwise short setup) OR (bias is short AND position is not short AND (continuous rearming is enabled OR a short position exited since the previous bar)).
Calculation: ATR = ATR requested on another timeframe with lookahead off: for a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar until the current higher-timeframe bar completes; for a lower timeframe, use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Long plan AND long entries are permitted AND price reaches the active buy-stop order at the lower signal band. Submit or update the order when the plan is true; cancel it when the plan and long rearm are both false. Cancel the opposing entry order when this order fills.
Short: Short plan AND short entries are permitted AND price reaches the active sell-stop order at the upper signal band. Submit or update the order when the plan is true; cancel it when the plan and short rearm are both false. Cancel the opposing entry order when this order fills.
Exit: long, long plan OR short entry fills.
Exit: short, long entry fills.
Exit: long, ATR exits enabled AND price reaches entry price + target multiplier × ATR OR ATR exits enabled AND price reaches entry price − stop multiplier × ATR, with the stop instead at entry price when close − entry price >= breakeven trigger × stop multiplier × ATR. The breakeven condition is recalculated each bar.
Exit: short, ATR exits enabled AND price reaches entry price − target multiplier × ATR OR ATR exits enabled AND price reaches entry price + stop multiplier × ATR, with the stop instead at entry price when entry price − close >= breakeven trigger × stop multiplier × ATR. The breakeven condition is recalculated each bar.
Exit: long, time exit enabled AND ((bars since entry >= minimum holding bars AND close − entry price < profit threshold × stop multiplier × ATR) OR bars since entry >= minimum holding bars + extension bars).
Exit: short, time exit enabled AND ((bars since entry >= minimum holding bars AND entry price − close < profit threshold × stop multiplier × ATR) OR bars since entry >= minimum holding bars + extension bars).
