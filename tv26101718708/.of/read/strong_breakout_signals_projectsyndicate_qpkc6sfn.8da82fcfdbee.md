Calculation: Confirmed pivots = swing highs and lows confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side. Store each side newest first; when a new pivot of that side is confirmed, remove older stored pivots beyond the history limit. The latest confirmed pivot of each side remains active until a new accepted entry in that direction; a new pivot reactivates that side.
Calculation: Zone width = zone-width multiplier times ATR, or times close times a fallback fraction if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bullish zone break = scan stored pivot highs newest first until the first pivot at or above close; require at least the minimum test count plus one pivots in the scanned prefix. Let the level be their maximum, count prefix pivots within one zone width below the level, and take the oldest counted pivot as the zone start. Require that count >= minimum tests, close > open, close > the highest high of the preceding pivot-period bars, open <= level, and that preceding highest high < level. Bearish zone break mirrors this using pivot lows, the first pivot at or below close, the minimum level, one zone width above the level, close < open, close < the preceding lowest low, open >= level, and preceding lowest low > level. Each zone engine is independently enabled.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = structure engine enabled AND latest pivot high active AND selected close or high > that pivot AND close > open. Bearish structure break = structure engine enabled AND latest pivot low active AND selected close or low < that pivot AND close < open.
Calculation: Breakout level, tests, and age = use the zone level, counted tests, and bars since zone start when the corresponding zone breaks; otherwise use the latest same-side pivot, a fixed baseline test count, and bars since that pivot. Confluence means both same-side engines break on the current bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = close above the EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar for a long, or below it for a short; alignment also holds when the filter is disabled or the EMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Breakout strength = sum parameterized tier points for test count, zone age, candle body divided by range, range divided by ATR, and volume divided by its SMA; each component uses increasing thresholds and corresponding increasing points. Use a fallback body ratio for zero range, a fallback range ratio for nonpositive ATR, and a fallback volume ratio for nonpositive volume SMA. Add momentum points from close changes over a shorter and a longer lookback: when both have the breakout direction, add the capped weighted sum of their absolute changes normalized by close times a scale; when only one agrees, add a separately capped weighted normalized shorter-lookback change; when neither agrees, subtract a penalty. Add a larger bonus when both close's distance beyond the breakout level exceeds a fraction of zone width and RSI agrees with direction, or a smaller bonus when only the distance condition holds. Add bonuses for confluence and higher-timeframe alignment; round to a parameterized precision and clamp to the score range. RSI agreement means RSI above an upper threshold for long or below a lower threshold for short.
Calculation: Eligible long = bullish zone break OR bullish structure break, AND breakout level available, AND long strength >= minimum strength, AND either the optional strong-only filter is disabled or long strength >= strong threshold, AND higher-timeframe alignment, AND either cooldown is disabled or there has been no accepted entry or bars since the last accepted entry >= cooldown, AND no active trade. Eligible short is the symmetric bearish condition. Evaluate eligibility before processing the current bar's exit.
Calculation: Accepted entry and trade levels = an entry signal becomes an active trade at close only if ATR is available and no trade closed on that bar. Freeze the breakout level, zone width, and ATR at entry. For a long, raw stop = level - zone width - ATR times stop buffer; for a short, raw stop = level + zone width + ATR times stop buffer. Risk = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), ATR times minimum-risk multiplier), ATR times maximum-risk multiplier). Stop = entry price minus risk for long or plus risk for short. Each of three targets = entry price plus its risk multiple for long or minus it for short; target multiples are independently configurable. Begin testing exits on the bar after entry.
Long: Eligible long AND (short is not eligible OR long strength >= short strength).
Short: Eligible short AND (long is not eligible OR short strength > long strength).
Exit: long, low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen third target.
Exit: short, high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen third target.
