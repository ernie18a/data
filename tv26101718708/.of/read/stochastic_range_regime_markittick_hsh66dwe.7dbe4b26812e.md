Calculation: Smoothed Stochastic %K = the close’s position from 0 to 100 within the lookback high-low range, smoothed by a short SMA and optionally by EMA, WMA, or RMA
Calculation: Confirmed regime = bullish or bearish raw conditions held for the required number of consecutive chart bars; otherwise neutral
Calculation: ATR-based stop and targets = entry price plus or minus ATR times configured multipliers; sort target multipliers from nearest to farthest
Long: (close > its longer-lookback SMA AND smoothed Stochastic %K is within the configured bull range for the required consecutive chart bars) OR (on a confirmed chart-bar close, confirmed regime changes to bullish from any other state AND, if enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a bullish regime AND, if enabled, ADX is at or above its threshold AND ATR is available, stop distance exceeds one minimum tick, and the long stop remains above zero)
Short: (close < its longer-lookback SMA AND smoothed Stochastic %K is within the configured bear range for the required consecutive chart bars) OR (on a confirmed chart-bar close, confirmed regime changes to bearish from any other state AND, if enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has a bearish regime AND, if enabled, ADX is at or above its threshold AND ATR is available, stop distance exceeds one minimum tick, and the farthest short target remains above zero)
Exit: long, bar high reaches any configured target OR bar close is below the ATR-based stop OR a new long or short entry signal occurs
Exit: short, bar low reaches any configured target OR bar close is above the ATR-based stop OR a new long or short entry signal occurs
