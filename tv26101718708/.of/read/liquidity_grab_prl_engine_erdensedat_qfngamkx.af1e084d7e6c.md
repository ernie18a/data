Calculation: Liquidity levels = selected mode: latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using equal lookbacks on both sides; OR the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; OR the high and low of the last ended configured session
Calculation: Tracked grab extreme = highest high since a top-level break, updating whenever a bar makes an equal or higher high; or lowest low since a bottom-level break, updating whenever a bar makes an equal or lower low
Calculation: Wick percentage = wick length divided by candle range, multiplied by 100; when candle range is zero, use 1 as the range
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback)
Long: bottom liquidity level is unbroken AND close < bottom liquidity level AND lower-wick percentage ≥ configured minimum OR bottom-level grab is active AND low > tracked grab extreme AND bar is confirmed
Short: top liquidity level is unbroken AND close > top liquidity level AND upper-wick percentage ≥ configured minimum OR top-level grab is active AND high < tracked grab extreme AND bar is confirmed
Long: bottom-level grab is active AND low > tracked grab extreme AND bar is confirmed AND (choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index ≤ configured threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR close > configured moving average) AND (ADX filter disabled OR NOT (ADX > configured threshold AND −DI > +DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≥ configured oversold threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR close > close at the configured shorter lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close > open)
Short: top-level grab is active AND high < tracked grab extreme AND bar is confirmed AND (choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index ≤ configured threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR close < configured moving average) AND (ADX filter disabled OR NOT (ADX > configured threshold AND +DI > −DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≤ configured overbought threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR close < close at the configured shorter lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close < open)
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit fraction × (top liquidity level − entry price) OR low ≤ entry price − configured stop multiplier × (top liquidity level − entry price)
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit fraction × (entry price − bottom liquidity level) OR high ≥ entry price + configured stop multiplier × (entry price − bottom liquidity level)
