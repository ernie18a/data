Calculation: Liquidity levels = Select one mode: latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed with equal numbers of bars on both sides; or high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, replaced at each new higher-timeframe bar; or high and low of the most recently ended configured session, using its configured timezone. Session mode takes precedence over higher-timeframe mode. Each new level is unbroken until its first qualifying grab; pivot highs and lows update independently.
Calculation: Upper grab = unbroken upper liquidity level AND close > upper liquidity level AND (wick filter disabled OR (high - max(open, close)) / bar range >= wick threshold). Lower grab = unbroken lower liquidity level AND close < lower liquidity level AND (wick filter disabled OR (min(open, close) - low) / bar range >= wick threshold). Use a bar range of 1 when high equals low. Mark the corresponding level broken when a grab occurs.
Calculation: Reversal tracking = When tracking is enabled, an upper grab starts tracking its high and a lower grab starts tracking its low. On each bar, including the grab bar, replace the tracked upper extreme when high >= the extreme, or the tracked lower extreme when low <= the extreme. The first confirmed bar with high < the upper extreme or low > the lower extreme completes the respective reversal. A new grab replaces the current tracking state; if both grabs occur on one bar, the lower grab takes precedence. A new higher-timeframe bar or ended session resets tracking in the corresponding liquidity mode; new pivots do not.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 * log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high - lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Long setup = completed lower reversal AND (choppiness filter disabled OR choppiness <= threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close > selected close moving average) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX <= threshold OR negative directional indicator <= positive directional indicator) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= lower threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR close - close at momentum lookback > 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close > open).
Calculation: Short setup = completed upper reversal AND (choppiness filter disabled OR choppiness <= threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close < selected close moving average) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX <= threshold OR positive directional indicator <= negative directional indicator) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= upper threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR close - close at momentum lookback < 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close < open).
Calculation: Setup levels = At a valid setup, entry price is the tracked extreme. For a long, range = current upper liquidity level - entry price; for a short, range = entry price - current lower liquidity level. If the opposite liquidity level is unavailable, range = ATR * fallback multiplier. Long target = entry price + target multiplier * range and long stop = entry price - stop multiplier * range; short target = entry price - target multiplier * range and short stop = entry price + stop multiplier * range. Freeze these levels at setup creation. Evaluate target and stop beginning on the next bar, with target taking precedence if both are reached on the same bar. A new valid setup cancels the previous active setup after that bar's target and stop checks.

Exit: long, high >= long target OR low <= long stop OR long setup OR short setup
Exit: short, low <= short target OR high >= short stop OR long setup OR short setup
