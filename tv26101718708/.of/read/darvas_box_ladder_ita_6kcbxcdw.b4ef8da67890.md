Calculation: Active box = Process one state per bar. Initially seek a high: when high is at least the highest high over the lookback including the current bar, set the tentative ceiling to high, the running low to low, and the countdown to the confirmation period; enter ceiling confirmation. During ceiling confirmation, update the running low to the minimum of itself and low. If high exceeds the ceiling, reset the ceiling to high, the running low to low, and the countdown to the confirmation period; otherwise decrement the countdown. When it reaches zero, set the tentative floor to the running low, reset the countdown, and enter floor confirmation. During floor confirmation, if high exceeds the ceiling, reset the ceiling to high, the running low to low, and the countdown to the confirmation period, and return to ceiling confirmation. Otherwise, if low is below the floor, set the floor to low and reset the countdown; otherwise decrement the countdown. When it reaches zero, activate the box if (ceiling - floor) / floor is within the inclusive minimum and maximum height thresholds; otherwise return to seeking a high on the next bar. Once active, keep the ceiling and floor fixed. After a breakout, seed a new ceiling-confirmation state with the breakout bar's high and low and the full countdown. After a breakdown, return to seeking a high on the next bar.
Long: An active box exists AND close > ceiling * (1 + breakout buffer).
Exit: long, an active box exists AND close < floor.
