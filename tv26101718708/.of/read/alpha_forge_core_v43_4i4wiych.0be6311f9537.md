Calculation: Basis = EMA of close + (close − close lagged by floor((basis length − 1) / 2) bars), using the basis length.
Calculation: Band width = highest ATR over a lookback proportional to the basis length, multiplied by a band-width parameter; upper band = basis + band width; lower band = basis − band width.
Calculation: Trend = initially neutral; set bullish when close crosses above the upper band and bearish when close crosses below the lower band; otherwise retain its previous state.
Calculation: Oscillator = EMA of (close − basis) / band width, using zero when band width is zero; oscillator signal = EMA of oscillator; histogram = oscillator − oscillator signal.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when the higher-timeframe close is above its basis and that basis is rising, bearish when the close is below its basis and that basis is falling, otherwise neutral. Use the same basis parameters as on the chart; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Swing high and swing low = the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, each using equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot. A bullish structure break occurs when close > the latest swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND close − swing high > ATR multiplied by a break threshold AND the required minimum number of bars has elapsed since either direction's last structure break. A bearish structure break uses close < swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND swing low − close > ATR multiplied by the break threshold, with the same spacing rule. Structure starts neutral and becomes bullish or bearish on the corresponding break, retaining its state otherwise.
Calculation: Bullish momentum = oscillator > positive momentum threshold AND oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram. Bearish momentum = oscillator < negative momentum threshold AND oscillator < oscillator signal AND histogram < previous histogram.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = previous oscillator < negative reversal threshold AND oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram AND close > basis AND previous close <= current basis AND oscillator > previous oscillator AND close > open AND close − open > candle-body threshold multiplied by high − low AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bearish) AND NOT (basis < previous basis AND close < basis AND close < lower band) AND (stock mode is disabled OR bullish structure break occurs while previous structure is bearish). Bearish reversal = previous oscillator > positive reversal threshold AND oscillator < oscillator signal AND histogram < previous histogram AND close < basis AND previous close >= current basis AND oscillator < previous oscillator AND close < open AND open − close > a separately parameterized candle-body threshold multiplied by high − low AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bullish) AND NOT (basis > previous basis AND close > basis AND close > upper band) AND (stock mode is disabled OR bearish structure break occurs while previous structure is bullish).
Calculation: Bullish continuation = trend is bullish AND basis > previous basis AND bullish momentum is true AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bearish) AND structure is bullish AND close crosses above basis AND oscillator > zero AND (close − basis) / band width < stretch threshold. Bearish continuation = trend is bearish AND basis < previous basis AND bearish momentum is true AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bullish) AND structure is bearish AND close crosses below basis AND oscillator < zero AND (close − basis) / band width > negative stretch threshold; use zero for the ratio when band width is zero.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = bullish continuation OR bullish reversal when stock mode is disabled; when stock mode is enabled, candidate = ((bullish continuation OR bullish reversal) AND trend is non-neutral AND higher-timeframe bias is non-neutral AND momentum is non-neutral AND trend is bullish AND structure is bullish) OR (trend is bullish AND structure is bullish AND close > basis AND previous close < current basis AND oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram). Bearish candidate = bearish continuation OR bearish reversal when stock mode is disabled; when stock mode is enabled, candidate = (bearish continuation OR bearish reversal) AND trend is non-neutral AND higher-timeframe bias is non-neutral AND momentum is non-neutral AND trend is bearish AND structure is bearish.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions among bullish trend, bullish higher-timeframe bias, bullish structure, bullish momentum, and oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram. Bearish score uses the corresponding bearish conditions. Grade A is the maximum score, grade B is one less than the maximum, and grade C is any lower score; the grade filter permits A only, A and B, or all grades.
Calculation: A final bullish signal requires a bullish candidate AND (confirmation is disabled OR close > previous close) AND an allowed bullish grade AND the bullish signal lock is off. A final bearish signal uses the bearish candidate AND (confirmation is disabled OR close < previous close) AND an allowed bearish grade AND the bearish signal lock is off. Both locks start off. After evaluating signals, a final bullish signal turns on the bullish lock and turns off the bearish lock; then a final bearish signal turns on the bearish lock and turns off the bullish lock. Finally, turn off the bullish lock if trend is not bullish OR structure is not bullish, and turn off the bearish lock if trend is not bearish OR structure is not bearish.
Long: Final bullish signal is true.
Short: Final bearish signal is true.
Long: Bullish structure break is true.
Short: Bearish structure break is true.
Long: Final bullish signal is false AND trend is bullish AND structure is bullish AND oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram AND normalized close displacement < stretch threshold AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish.
Short: Final bearish signal is false AND trend is bearish AND structure is bearish AND oscillator < oscillator signal AND histogram < previous histogram AND normalized close displacement > negative stretch threshold AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish.
Long: Final bullish signal is false AND final bearish signal is false AND previous oscillator < negative reversal threshold AND oscillator > oscillator signal AND histogram > previous histogram AND ((close > basis AND previous close <= current basis) OR (close > lower band AND previous close <= current lower band)) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish.
Short: Final bullish signal is false AND final bearish signal is false AND previous oscillator > positive reversal threshold AND oscillator < oscillator signal AND histogram < previous histogram AND ((close < basis AND previous close >= current basis) OR (close < upper band AND previous close >= current upper band)) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish.
Calculation: Normalized close displacement = (close − basis) / band width, or zero when band width is zero.
