Calculation: Mansfield relative strength = (chart close / chart close’s SMA) / (SPY close / SPY close’s SMA) − 1; both SMAs use the same lookback
Calculation: recent range = highest high − lowest low over a short lookback
Calculation: preceding range = highest high − lowest low over a longer lookback, ending one short lookback before the current bar
Long: close > medium-term SMA AND close > long-term SMA AND medium-term SMA > long-term SMA AND long-term SMA > its value one long-term lookback segment earlier AND short-term SMA > medium-term SMA AND short-term SMA > long-term SMA AND close > short-term SMA AND (close − lowest low over a long lookback) / lowest low over that lookback ≥ required minimum rise from the low AND (highest high over the same long lookback − close) / highest high over that lookback ≤ allowed maximum decline from the high AND Mansfield relative strength > 0 AND (recent range < required fraction of preceding range AND volume < required fraction of its SMA OR close > the previous bar’s short-lookback high AND previous close ≤ the short-lookback high as of the previous bar AND volume > its SMA)
