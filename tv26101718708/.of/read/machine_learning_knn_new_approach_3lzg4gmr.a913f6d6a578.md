Calculation: kNN prediction = average of N values, one for each consecutive pair in the selected-resolution close series: use the later close when that pair’s absolute close change is among the K smallest accumulated changes; otherwise use the earlier close. The selected resolution may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its current bar when prediction adjustment is off, or its last completed bar when it is on.
Long: kNN prediction < selected-resolution close (current close when prediction adjustment is on, otherwise previous close) AND (add-on is None OR add-on is Pivot Point AND current high is the highest high of the pivot lookback OR add-on is Z-Score AND selected-resolution close > its lookback SMA)
Short: kNN prediction > selected-resolution close (current close when prediction adjustment is on, otherwise previous close) AND (add-on is None OR add-on is Pivot Point AND current low is the lowest low of the pivot lookback OR add-on is Z-Score AND selected-resolution close < its lookback SMA)
