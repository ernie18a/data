Calculation: Live bands = Maintain separate upper and lower bands. On each bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low. For each pivot, calculate current ATR, falling back to the greater of the current high-low range and a minimum price increment when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Set band height to the greater of ATR times a height parameter and a minimum height. Place an upper band's top and bottom at the pivot plus an outer fraction and minus an inner fraction of height; reverse those fractions for a lower band, with the outer fraction greater than the inner fraction. Merge into the first same-side intact or tested band whose level is within a merge-distance multiple of current ATR, if merging is enabled: extend its boundaries to include the new band, move its level to the more extreme pivot, retain the greater volume ratio and prominence, increment its touch count, and set its last-touch bar to the current bar. Otherwise create a band only if its initial strength meets the minimum-strength parameter. Its birth bar and initial last-touch bar are the pivot bar, and its initial touch count is zero. After creation, if the live-band limit for that side is exceeded, remove the first band with the lowest relevance. Before evaluating retirement on each bar, process each live band's rupture, sweep, or test in that order. A rupture occurs when the close is beyond its level in the direction of the pivot; a sweep occurs when the wick is beyond its level but the close is not; a test occurs when the wick reaches the inner band boundary without a rupture or sweep. A rupture makes the band permanently non-live. A sweep makes it swept and increments touches up to the touch cap; subsequent sweeps do not produce another marking. A test changes an intact band to tested and increments touches only if the elapsed bars since last touch exceed the pivot length, updating last touch when incremented. After processing, retire bands whose absolute distance from close exceeds a retirement multiple of current ATR or whose age since the pivot bar exceeds a maximum age; either retirement condition can be disabled.
Calculation: Band strength = Sum capped weighted components for pivot-bar volume divided by the volume moving average at confirmation, pivot prominence divided by ATR at confirmation, and touch count. Use a default volume ratio when the average is unavailable or nonpositive and zero pivot volume when unavailable. Upper prominence is pivot high minus the lowest low over the pivot-confirmation window; lower prominence is the highest high over that window minus pivot low. On a merge retain the greater value of each of the existing and new volume ratios and prominences. Initial strength excludes the touch component. Relevance equals strength minus capped penalties for absolute distance between close and level in current ATR units and age since the pivot bar. All lookbacks, weights, caps, and penalty scales are parameters.
Long: A live lower band is not already swept AND low is below its level AND close is at or above its level
Long: A live upper band has close above its level
Short: A live upper band is not already swept AND high is above its level AND close is at or below its level
Short: A live lower band has close below its level
