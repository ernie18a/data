Calculation: Session range = the highest high and lowest low during the configured range-formation window, locked when that window ends; reset at the start of the next range-formation window.
Calculation: Completed low sweep = after a locked session range exists, a trade-window bar has low < session range low and a pending low excursion is set; the excursion completes on the first bar, including that same bar, with close > session range low. Clear the pending excursion on completion or at the next range-formation window. Count completions separately for each side and reset counts at the next range-formation window.
Calculation: Completed high sweep = after a locked session range exists, a trade-window bar has high > session range high and a pending high excursion is set; the excursion completes on the first bar, including that same bar, with close < session range high. Clear the pending excursion on completion or at the next range-formation window.
Calculation: Armed state = separately for each side, set armed on every corresponding completed sweep whose cumulative count is at least the sweep threshold; reset its bars-since-arm counter to zero on each such sweep and increment it on subsequent bars without a corresponding completed sweep. Clear both armed states at the start of the next range-formation window and when the trade window ends; clear the corresponding state after its entry signal. Evaluate entry signals before clearing their armed states.
Calculation: Post-sweep pivot = separately for each side, clear the stored high pivot on every completed low sweep and the stored low pivot on every completed high sweep. While the corresponding side is armed, replace it with each newly confirmed pivot high or low, respectively. A pivot is a high or low with the configured equal number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars have completed. The pivot's center may precede the sweep.
Calculation: Consolidation = the highest high minus the lowest low over the configured consolidation lookback, including the current bar, is at most a configured multiple of current ATR. The ATR and consolidation lookbacks are configured durations converted to chart bars by rounding duration divided by bar duration, with minimums of five and one bars respectively; convert the pivot duration the same way with a minimum of one bar.
Long: completed low sweep.
Short: completed high sweep.
Long: current bar is in the trade window AND long state is armed after entry-signal processing.
Short: current bar is in the trade window AND short state is armed after entry-signal processing.
Long: current bar is in the trade window AND no entry signal has occurred since the start of the current range-formation window AND long state is armed AND close > stored post-sweep pivot high AND (consolidation filter disabled OR (bars since long arm >= consolidation lookback in bars AND consolidation)) AND (trend filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Short: current bar is in the trade window AND no entry signal has occurred since the start of the current range-formation window AND short state is armed AND close < stored post-sweep pivot low AND (consolidation filter disabled OR (bars since short arm >= consolidation lookback in bars AND consolidation)) AND (trend filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
