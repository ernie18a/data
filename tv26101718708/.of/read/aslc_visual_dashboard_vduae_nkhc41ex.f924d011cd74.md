Calculation: Asian range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the range-formation window, locked when that window ends
Calculation: Completed downside/upside sweep count = count during the trade window of excursions below the locked range low followed by a close above it / excursions above the locked range high followed by a close below it; reset each range day
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = local high/low confirmed by the configured number of chart bars on each side
Calculation: Recent range = highest high minus lowest low over the consolidation lookback
Long: During the trade window, at least the configured minimum number of downside sweeps has completed, a confirmed pivot high formed after the latest completed downside sweep exists, close > that pivot high, no signal has already occurred that range day, and (consolidation is not required OR bars since arming >= consolidation lookback AND recent range <= configured ATR multiple × chart-timeframe ATR), and (higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA when the trend filter is enabled; both EMAs use the latest available higher-timeframe bar, which is still forming in real time and completed in historical data)
Short: During the trade window, at least the configured minimum number of upside sweeps has completed, a confirmed pivot low formed after the latest completed upside sweep exists, close < that pivot low, no signal has already occurred that range day, and (consolidation is not required OR bars since arming >= consolidation lookback AND recent range <= configured ATR multiple × chart-timeframe ATR), and (higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA when the trend filter is enabled; both EMAs use the latest available higher-timeframe bar, which is still forming in real time and completed in historical data). The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
