Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side; retain the latest confirmed value. Use one swing length for chart entry triggers and another, shared swing length for the dashboard timeframes.
Calculation: Structure bias = initially flat; on each completed bar, update the latest confirmed swings, then set bullish if close > latest swing high and set bearish if close < latest swing low; otherwise retain the preceding bias. If both comparisons hold, bearish takes precedence. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar; for the chart timeframe use the completed chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = among the configured timeframes strictly higher than the chart, count bullish and bearish structure biases using their last completed bars. The required count is the smaller of the configured agreement threshold and the number of higher timeframes, with a minimum of one.
Calculation: Chart structure break = close > the latest confirmed chart swing high for an upward break, or close < the latest confirmed chart swing low for a downward break. Each swing can break only once: mark it broken on the first qualifying close even if no signal results, and reset its broken status when a new swing of that kind is confirmed.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of (the lower of the latest chart swing low and current low, minus an ATR buffer) and (close minus a minimum ATR distance); if no chart swing low exists, use the lowest low over a separate lookback as the first anchor. Short stop = the higher of (the higher of the latest chart swing high and current high, plus an ATR buffer) and (close plus a minimum ATR distance); if no chart swing high exists, use the highest high over that lookback as the first anchor.
Calculation: Entry setup = an unbroken chart structure break in the entry direction AND at least one configured higher timeframe AND the count of higher-timeframe biases in that direction >= the required agreement count AND that count > the count in the opposite direction AND the distance from close to the corresponding stop <= a maximum ATR distance. Evaluate on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Active stop and final target = freeze the corresponding stop and entry close when an entry signal occurs; the final target is entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a configurable multiple of the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Evaluate stop and target only from the bar after entry; if both are touched on the same bar, the stop takes precedence. Process stop or target exits before evaluating new entry signals on that bar.
Long: close >= open.
Short: close < open.
Long: structure bias is bullish on a configured timeframe at or above the chart timeframe.
Short: structure bias is bearish on a configured timeframe at or above the chart timeframe.
Long: count of bullish structure biases among configured timeframes at or above the chart timeframe > count of bearish structure biases among those timeframes.
Short: count of bearish structure biases among configured timeframes at or above the chart timeframe > count of bullish structure biases among those timeframes.
Long: number of configured timeframes at or above the chart timeframe > 0 AND all their structure biases are bullish AND this condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Short: number of configured timeframes at or above the chart timeframe > 0 AND all their structure biases are bearish AND this condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Long: number of configured higher timeframes > 0 AND bullish higher-timeframe count >= required agreement count AND bullish higher-timeframe count > bearish higher-timeframe count.
Short: number of configured higher timeframes > 0 AND bearish higher-timeframe count >= required agreement count AND bearish higher-timeframe count > bullish higher-timeframe count.
Long: long entry setup AND position side is not long.
Short: short entry setup AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target OR short entry setup.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target OR long entry setup.
