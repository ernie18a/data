Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG data = data from the last completed bar of each enabled higher timeframe, carried forward across chart bars; ATR uses the same higher timeframe and is replaced by 1 if unavailable or nonpositive.
Long: Higher-timeframe low > higher-timeframe high two bars earlier AND the first bar's timestamp differs from the previous chart bar's bullish FVG timestamp or the previous timestamp is unavailable AND (minimum size is disabled OR 100 × (higher-timeframe low − higher-timeframe high two bars earlier) / higher-timeframe ATR >= minimum size).
Short: Higher-timeframe high < higher-timeframe low two bars earlier AND the first bar's timestamp differs from the previous chart bar's bearish FVG timestamp or the previous timestamp is unavailable AND (minimum size is disabled OR 100 × (higher-timeframe low two bars earlier − higher-timeframe high) / higher-timeframe ATR >= minimum size).
