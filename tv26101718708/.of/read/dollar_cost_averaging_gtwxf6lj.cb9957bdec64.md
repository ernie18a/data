Calculation: Rising and falling Supertrend levels = SMA of the Supertrend value on rising-trend bars and falling-trend bars, respectively; each SMA ignores unavailable values.
Calculation: Binned oscillator = the lower edge of the oscillator's configured equal-width bin on the 0–100 scale; retain its previous bin when the oscillator is 100 or unavailable, starting at zero.
Calculation: Relative volatility = 100 × EMA(standard deviation of source when source rises, otherwise zero) / (EMA(standard deviation of source when source rises, otherwise zero) + EMA(standard deviation of source when source does not rise, otherwise zero)).
Calculation: Bottom condition = binned long-lookback RSI <= its lower threshold AND binned medium-lookback RSI < binned long-lookback RSI AND binned short-lookback RSI <= binned medium-lookback RSI AND binned long-lookback relative volatility <= its lower threshold AND binned medium-lookback relative volatility <= binned long-lookback relative volatility AND binned short-lookback relative volatility <= binned medium-lookback relative volatility AND binned long-lookback MFI <= its lower threshold AND binned medium-lookback MFI <= binned long-lookback MFI AND binned short-lookback MFI <= binned medium-lookback MFI AND SMA(100 × (close − open) / (high − low) + 50) <= its lower threshold. RSI sources are OHLC4, high, OHLC4 for short, medium, long lookbacks; relative-volatility sources are high, close, close; MFI source is high. Each oscillator has short, medium and long lookbacks in increasing order.
Calculation: Critical bottom condition = bottom condition with its own lower thresholds.
Calculation: Critical high condition = binned long-lookback RSI >= its upper threshold AND binned medium-lookback RSI > binned long-lookback RSI AND binned short-lookback RSI >= binned medium-lookback RSI AND binned long-lookback relative volatility >= its upper threshold AND binned medium-lookback relative volatility >= binned long-lookback relative volatility AND binned short-lookback relative volatility >= binned medium-lookback relative volatility AND binned long-lookback MFI >= its upper threshold AND binned medium-lookback MFI >= binned long-lookback MFI AND binned short-lookback MFI >= binned medium-lookback MFI AND SMA(100 × (close − open) / (high − low) + 50) >= its upper threshold, using the same oscillator sources and lookback ordering as the bottom condition.
Calculation: Above a level = open >= level OR high >= level.
Calculation: Below a level = close < level OR low < level.
Calculation: Trend buy condition = above falling Supertrend level AND above rising Supertrend level AND above SMA(high) AND above Parabolic SAR AND ALMA(open) > its previous value.
Calculation: Alternative trend buy condition A = below SMA(high) AND above falling Supertrend level AND above rising Supertrend level AND above Parabolic SAR.
Calculation: Alternative trend buy condition B = below falling Supertrend level AND below rising Supertrend level AND above SMA(high) AND above Parabolic SAR AND ALMA(open) > its previous value.
Calculation: Trend sell condition = below falling Supertrend level AND below rising Supertrend level AND below SMA(high) AND below Parabolic SAR AND ALMA(open) < its previous value.
Calculation: Alternative trend sell condition = (below SMA(high) AND below Parabolic SAR AND above falling Supertrend level AND above rising Supertrend level) OR (below Parabolic SAR AND above falling Supertrend level AND above rising Supertrend level AND above SMA(high)) OR (above rising Supertrend level AND below falling Supertrend level AND below Parabolic SAR).
Long: Both-trends mode AND datetime within the configured trading window AND bottom condition was true on the previous bar AND bottom condition is false AND (position is flat OR SMA(low) < average entry price × a configured factor below one).
Long: Uptrend-only mode AND datetime within the configured trading window AND position is flat AND ((trend buy condition is true AND was false on the previous bar) OR (alternative trend buy condition A is true AND was false on the previous bar) OR (alternative trend buy condition B is true AND was false on the previous bar)).
Long: Bottom condition was true on the previous bar AND bottom condition is false AND SMA(low) < average entry price.
Long: Critical bottom condition is true AND SMA(low) < average entry price.
Short: Critical high condition is true AND SMA(low) > average entry price.
Exit: long, both-trends mode AND datetime within the configured trading window AND no higher-priority long entry on this bar AND an enabled profit level is crossed upward by SMA(low), where each profit level is average entry price multiplied by its configured factor above one; close the configured partial position percentage, giving higher profit levels priority.
Exit: long, both-trends mode AND datetime within the configured trading window AND no higher-priority long entry or enabled partial exit on this bar AND trend sell condition is true AND close > average entry price × configured profit factor.
Exit: long, uptrend-only mode AND datetime within the configured trading window AND (trend sell condition is true OR alternative trend sell condition is true).
