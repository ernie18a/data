Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; configured lookbacks increase from short to long.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe SMA = SMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; configured lookbacks increase from short to long.
Calculation: Tolerance band = prior completed chart-timeframe ATR(14) × configured tolerance factor.
Long: For any configured higher-timeframe EMA or SMA: (strict mode AND low ≤ MA AND close > MA AND close > open) OR (strict mode AND previous chart-timeframe close ≤ MA AND close > MA) OR (band mode AND low ≤ MA + tolerance band AND low ≥ MA − 2 × tolerance band AND close > MA AND close > open) OR (low > MA AND close > open AND distance from low to MA as a percentage of MA ≤ configured proximity percentage AND neither a long nor short entry condition fires for that MA on this bar)
Short: For any configured higher-timeframe EMA or SMA: (strict mode AND high ≥ MA AND close < MA AND close < open) OR (strict mode AND previous chart-timeframe close ≥ MA AND close < MA) OR (band mode AND high ≥ MA − tolerance band AND high ≤ MA + 2 × tolerance band AND close < MA AND close < open) OR (high < MA AND close < open AND distance from high to MA as a percentage of MA ≤ configured proximity percentage AND neither a long nor short entry condition fires for that MA on this bar)
