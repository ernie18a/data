Calculation: Active range = On each closed chart bar, if no range is active, the long-lookback ATR is available and positive, the short-lookback ATR has sufficient history, the simple moving average of short-lookback ATR / long-lookback ATR over a window shorter than the seed window is below a contraction threshold, the seed-window highest high minus lowest low is at least a minimum-width multiplier times the short-lookback ATR, and the cooldown since the last resolution or expiry has elapsed, start a range. Freeze its upper and lower bounds at the seed-window highest high and lowest low, its volume baseline at the seed-window average volume, and its start at the first seed-window bar. End the range on a qualifying breakout or when its age from that start exceeds the maximum range age; process breakout before expiry. Do not start another range until a later bar.
Calculation: Sweep confirmation = While a range is active, maintain at most one candidate. If none is active, start a low candidate when low is below the lower bound by at least a sweep-distance multiplier times current short-lookback ATR and volume exceeds a sweep-volume multiplier times the frozen baseline; start a high candidate symmetrically above the upper bound. If the frozen baseline is unavailable or nonpositive, use current volume as the baseline. Allow the first candidate on either side and one further confirmed sweep only on the opposite side; if both candidates qualify simultaneously, choose low. On the candidate bar and each subsequent bar, confirm the candidate when close is strictly between the range bounds; otherwise discard it when its age reaches the return-window limit. Check for confirmation before discarding. Store the confirmed side as the current sweep side; an opposite-side confirmation replaces it. Process sweep confirmation before breakout and expiry on each bar.
Calculation: Qualifying upward breakout = Close is greater than the active range upper bound plus a breakout-distance multiplier times current short-lookback ATR AND volume is greater than a breakout-volume multiplier times the frozen baseline, using current volume if the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Qualifying downward breakout = Close is less than the active range lower bound minus a breakout-distance multiplier times current short-lookback ATR AND volume is greater than a breakout-volume multiplier times the frozen baseline, using current volume if the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Long: Low sweep confirmation.
Short: High sweep confirmation.
Long: Active range AND qualifying upward breakout.
Short: Active range AND qualifying downward breakout.
Long: Higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA of close; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Short: Higher-timeframe close <= higher-timeframe EMA of close; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
