Calculation: Smoothed money flow = EMA of the Money Flow Index calculated from typical price and volume, minus its center value.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND smoothed money flow at that pivot is above its value at the preceding price pivot low. Bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND smoothed money flow at that pivot is below its value at the preceding price pivot high. Pivots require specified numbers of bars on both sides and are detected on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Active bullish divergence = bars since the latest bullish divergence are within the divergence lookback AND no greater than bars since the latest bearish divergence. Active bearish divergence = bars since the latest bearish divergence are within the divergence lookback AND less than bars since the latest bullish divergence. Treat a divergence that has never occurred as having an infinite age.
Calculation: Execution timeframe = the bias timeframe divided by sixteen, rounded to an available whole-second or whole-minute timeframe with the corresponding minimum resolution.
Long: On a configured higher timeframe, smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value; use either the still-forming bar or the last completed bar consistently for all higher-timeframe dashboard decisions.
Short: On a configured higher timeframe, smoothed money flow is less than its previous value; use the selected still-forming or last completed bar.
Long: On a configured higher timeframe, smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value AND on the preceding bar it was less than its previous value; use the selected still-forming or last completed bar.
Short: On a configured higher timeframe, smoothed money flow is less than its previous value AND on the preceding bar it was greater than or equal to its previous value; use the selected still-forming or last completed bar.
Long: Active bullish divergence on a configured higher timeframe, using the selected still-forming or last completed bar.
Short: Active bearish divergence on a configured higher timeframe, using the selected still-forming or last completed bar.
Long: On a configured higher bias timeframe, smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value using the selected still-forming or last completed bar AND on its shorter execution timeframe, smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value AND the shorter-period close EMA crossed above the longer-period close EMA within the crossover lookback. The execution timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its still-forming bar when higher and its last available completed bar when lower.
Short: On a configured higher bias timeframe, smoothed money flow is less than its previous value using the selected still-forming or last completed bar AND on its shorter execution timeframe, smoothed money flow is less than its previous value AND the shorter-period close EMA crossed below the longer-period close EMA within the crossover lookback. The execution timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its still-forming bar when higher and its last available completed bar when lower.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value AND on the lower execution timeframe, smoothed money flow is greater than or equal to its previous value AND the shorter-period close EMA crossed above the longer-period close EMA within the crossover lookback AND this combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar. Use the last available completed lower-timeframe bar.
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed money flow is less than its previous value AND on the lower execution timeframe, smoothed money flow is less than its previous value AND the shorter-period close EMA crossed below the longer-period close EMA within the crossover lookback AND this combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar. Use the last available completed lower-timeframe bar.
