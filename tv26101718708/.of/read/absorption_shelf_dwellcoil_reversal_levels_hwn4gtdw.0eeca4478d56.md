Calculation: Price-level width = a configurable price-grid increment. Map each bar’s low and high to their floor-divided grid levels; if the number of covered levels exceeds a configured cap, use only the level containing (high + low) / 2. Over a rolling window, add 1 divided by the number of covered levels to each level covered by each bar. Remove each bar’s contribution when it leaves the window.
Calculation: Candidate bands = when the occupation map has at least a minimum number of populated levels, select levels whose occupation is at least the configured percentile of populated-level occupations, using the sorted value at floor(percentile × (count − 1)). Merge successive selected levels separated by no more than a configured gap; discard bands narrower than a configured minimum. A band’s bounds are the lower edge of its lowest level and the upper edge of its highest level. Its anchor is the occupation-weighted mean of the centers of all levels within its bounds.
Calculation: Shelf = maintain an ordered, capacity-limited list of bands. On each completed chart bar, process candidate bands in ascending price order: match each to the first overlapping shelf not already matched on that bar and replace its bounds and anchor, or create a forming shelf if capacity remains. Increment the miss count of unmatched shelves and reset it for matched shelves. Remove shelves unseen for longer than a configured retention period and spent shelves whose miss count exceeds the containment lookback. Promote a forming shelf to active when 1 − clamp(shorter-period ATR / longer-period ATR, 0, 1) is at least a threshold AND the fraction of closes in a containment lookback within its current bounds expanded on both sides by a multiple of current ATR is at least a threshold. An active shelf is touched when the bar’s low–high range overlaps its bounds; on its first touch, record whether the previous close was at or above its current anchor. After any touch, mark the shelf flipped on a qualifying reversal or spent on a qualifying break in the opposite direction; neither state returns to active. Update matches, removals, promotion and touches before evaluating breaks. A qualifying upward break is close > current upper bound + ATR multiple AND previous low <= current upper bound + the same current ATR multiple. A qualifying downward break is close < current lower bound − ATR multiple AND previous high >= current lower bound − the same current ATR multiple.
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or the midpoint of the range when high equals low.
Calculation: Comparison volume = chart-bar volume, or optionally chart-bar volume divided by the updated exponential moving average of volumes from bars in the same time-of-day slot; update that slot’s average with the current volume before division, and use a neutral ratio when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Long: An active, previously touched shelf was first touched when the preceding close was at or above its anchor AND the qualifying upward break condition holds.
Short: An active, previously touched shelf was first touched when the preceding close was below its anchor AND the qualifying downward break condition holds.
Long: Volume > 0 AND low <= the lowest low of a preceding lookback excluding the current bar AND close position >= an upper threshold AND percentile rank of comparison volume over a rolling window >= a high-volume threshold.
Short: Volume > 0 AND high >= the highest high of a preceding lookback excluding the current bar AND close position <= a lower threshold AND percentile rank of comparison volume over a rolling window >= a high-volume threshold.
Long: Volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND percentile rank of high − low over a rolling window >= a wide-spread threshold AND percentile rank of comparison volume over the same window >= a higher high-volume threshold AND close position >= a threshold.
Long: Volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND comparison volume >= its highest value over the rolling window including the current bar AND percentile rank of high − low over that window >= a higher wide-spread threshold.
Short: Volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND comparison volume >= its highest value over the rolling window including the current bar AND percentile rank of high − low over that window >= a higher wide-spread threshold.
Long: Volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND percentile rank of high − low over the rolling window <= a narrow-spread threshold AND percentile rank of comparison volume over the same window <= a low-volume threshold.
Short: Volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND percentile rank of high − low over the rolling window <= a narrow-spread threshold AND percentile rank of comparison volume over the same window <= a low-volume threshold.
