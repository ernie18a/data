Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over a lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the same lookback)) / log10(lookback); evaluate on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = Extend the last two accepted swing pivots: level n = latest pivot + direction × |latest pivot − previous pivot| × n, where direction is −1 if the latest pivot is higher than the previous pivot, otherwise +1. A pivot is a centered local high or low, confirmed after the same number of bars on either side; process a high candidate before a low candidate. Replace a same-side pivot only with a more extreme one. Accept a reversal pivot only when its percentage deviation from the latest pivot exceeds ATR(14) divided by close, multiplied by the deviation threshold.
Long: Choppiness Index ≥ threshold AND close crosses above Fibonacci level 1 AND Fibonacci level 1 > Fibonacci level 0 AND Fibonacci level 1.618 > Fibonacci level 1
Exit: Long, close crosses above Fibonacci level 1.618 OR close crosses below Fibonacci level 0
