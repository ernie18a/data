Calculation: Bullish-candle and bearish-candle high/low scores = For each prior-candle direction and each outcome, maintain a score initialized to zero. Count all preceding bars of that direction; on each chart bar, include the immediately preceding bar in its direction's count. If that preceding bar has the direction and the current high is at least its high, update the direction's high score to the cumulative number of such high outcomes divided by the direction's count, expressed as a percentage and rounded to two decimal places. If the current low is at most the preceding low, update its low score similarly. Leave each score unchanged when its corresponding outcome does not occur. A preceding doji increments neither direction's count and updates neither score.
Long: Close >= current session open of the forming higher-timeframe bar, or the chart bar open when the session and chart timeframes coincide.
Short: Close < current session open of the forming higher-timeframe bar, or the chart bar open when the session and chart timeframes coincide.
Long: Previous close > previous open AND bullish-candle high score >= bullish-candle low score OR previous close <= previous open AND bearish-candle high score >= bearish-candle low score.
Short: Previous close > previous open AND bullish-candle high score < bullish-candle low score OR previous close <= previous open AND bearish-candle high score < bearish-candle low score.
