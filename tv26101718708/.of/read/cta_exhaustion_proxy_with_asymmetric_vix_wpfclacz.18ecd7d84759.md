Calculation: Positioning score = sum of three terms, one for each of the short-, medium-, and long-lookback moving averages of a selected price source; each term is +1 when the source is above its moving average and -1 otherwise. All three use the same selected moving-average type: SMA, EMA, or HMA.
Calculation: Extreme count = number of consecutive chart bars through the current bar on which the absolute positioning score equals its maximum, regardless of whether its sign changes; reset to zero otherwise.
Calculation: Score velocity = current positioning score minus its score at an earlier chart-bar lookback.
Long: Positioning score > 0
Short: Positioning score < 0
Long: VIX filter is disabled AND close crosses above the short-lookback moving average AND ((previous positioning score is at its negative extreme AND previous extreme count >= the longer crowding threshold) OR ((positioning score on either of the two preceding bars is at its negative extreme) AND previous extreme count >= the shorter crowding threshold AND previous extreme count < the longer crowding threshold AND score velocity >= a positive threshold AND (strong-candle filter is disabled OR (close > open AND high > low AND (close - open) / (high - low) >= a body-fraction threshold))) OR (previous positioning score <= a negative threshold AND score velocity >= a positive threshold))
Short: VIX filter is disabled AND close crosses below the short-lookback moving average AND ((previous positioning score is at its positive extreme AND previous extreme count >= the longer crowding threshold) OR ((positioning score on either of the two preceding bars is at its positive extreme) AND previous extreme count >= the shorter crowding threshold AND previous extreme count < the longer crowding threshold AND score velocity <= a negative threshold AND (strong-candle filter is disabled OR (close < open AND high > low AND (open - close) / (high - low) >= a body-fraction threshold))) OR (previous positioning score >= a positive threshold AND score velocity <= a negative threshold))
