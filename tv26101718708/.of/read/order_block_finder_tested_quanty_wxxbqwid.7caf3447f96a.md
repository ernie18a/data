Calculation: Swing high and swing low = On each closed chart bar, a bar the swing lookback bars ago is a confirmed swing high if its high equals the highest high over the symmetric window of that many bars on either side; a confirmed swing low is defined symmetrically. Retain the latest confirmed swing of each kind.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = Process existing zones before creating new ones on each closed bar. A support zone becomes inactive when close < its bottom; a resistance zone becomes inactive when close > its top. For each side, retain zones in creation order up to a capacity, discarding the oldest when exceeded. For a support zone, the first close above the latest confirmed swing high within the maximum swing age consumes that swing, even if no zone is created. Create a zone only if the optional strength filter is disabled or (close - open >= ATR OR low > the high two bars earlier). Find the earliest bar with the lowest low from the swing bar through the break bar, inclusive; select the most recent down candle within a lookback ending at that bar. Its high and low are the zone top and bottom. Create the zone only if such a candle exists and its range does not overlap or touch any active support zone. Resistance zones use the symmetric rules: first close below the latest confirmed swing low, optional strength condition open - close >= ATR OR high < the low two bars earlier, earliest highest high, and most recent up candle, with overlap checked against active resistance zones. New zones can first be touched on a subsequent bar. Mark each zone as touched after its first qualifying touch, whether or not it is displayed.
Long: An active, previously untouched support zone exists AND close >= its bottom AND low <= its top.
Short: An active, previously untouched resistance zone exists AND close <= its top AND high >= its bottom.
