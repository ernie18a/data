Calculation: Main VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the selected anchor period
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = Main VWAP plus or minus a selected multiple of its volume-weighted standard deviation
Long: close >= Main VWAP OR close crosses above Main VWAP OR close crosses above the upper VWAP deviation band
Short: close < Main VWAP OR close crosses below Main VWAP OR close crosses below the lower VWAP deviation band
