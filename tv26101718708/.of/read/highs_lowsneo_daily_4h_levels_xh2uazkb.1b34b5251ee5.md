Calculation: Active levels = for each of two enabled higher timeframes, one longer than the other, add the last completed bar's high and low at the start of each new timeframe period; retain only the configured number of most recently added levels per timeframe and side, including inactive levels. A level cannot break on its creation bar. Before checking breaks, deactivate each active shorter-timeframe level equal to an active longer-timeframe high or low. On subsequent chart bars, deactivate a high when the selected mitigation source, high or close, is >= the level, and deactivate a low when the selected mitigation source, low or close, is <= the level.
Calculation: Break zone = the period from the chart bar that breaks an active level through the configured duration after that bar's opening time, inclusive. A later break replaces the zone and resets its signal count and reaction state. If both high and low levels break on the same bar, process longer-timeframe highs, longer-timeframe lows, shorter-timeframe highs, then shorter-timeframe lows; the last break determines the zone's side. A zone exists only when zones are enabled.
Calculation: Signal state = count signals per zone and track whether a signal is active. A signal becomes active at the signal bar's close and remains active until its stop is hit or the zone ends or is replaced. Reset the signal count and reaction state when the zone ends or is replaced. Do not issue a signal on a bar that hits the active signal's stop. In candle mode, after a short signal wait until a subsequent eligible bar's high exceeds the first bearish candle's high; after a long signal wait until a subsequent eligible bar's low falls below the first bullish candle's low. Check this sweep only while no signal is active, and resume looking for candles on the following eligible bar.
Calculation: RSI trigger = calculate RSI on a lower timeframe than the chart and test every completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar against the preceding lower-timeframe RSI value, including the previous chart bar's last sample. If the RSI timeframe equals the chart timeframe, use chart-bar RSI; if it is higher, use the last completed higher-timeframe RSI.
Long: The current break zone is a low-break zone AND no signal is active AND no stop was hit on this bar AND the zone's signal count is below its limit AND ((RSI mode is selected AND RSI crosses above the oversold threshold) OR (candle mode is selected AND no sweep is pending AND close > open AND close > the high of the first candle in the current consecutive sequence of candles with close > open)).
Short: The current break zone is a high-break zone AND no signal is active AND no stop was hit on this bar AND the zone's signal count is below its limit AND ((RSI mode is selected AND RSI crosses below the overbought threshold) OR (candle mode is selected AND no sweep is pending AND close < open AND close < the low of the first candle in the current consecutive sequence of candles with close < open)).
Exit: long, the signal is active AND the chart bar is after the entry bar AND low <= entry price multiplied by one minus the stop-loss percentage.
Exit: short, the signal is active AND the chart bar is after the entry bar AND high >= entry price multiplied by one plus the stop-loss percentage.
