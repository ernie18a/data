Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a value with the specified number of preceding and subsequent bars; for a low, it is less than or equal to every preceding value and strictly less than every subsequent value; reverse the inequalities for a high. A pivot becomes available only after its subsequent bars have closed. Scan several increasing pivot strengths, using equal preceding and subsequent strengths for both CCI and price.
Calculation: Matched price structure = for each CCI endpoint, search within a matching radius for confirmed price pivots of any scanned strength. Require the second price pivot to follow the first, a lower second low for bullish structure or a higher second high for bearish structure, and an absolute difference between price-pivot span and CCI-pivot span divided by CCI-pivot span no greater than a tolerance. No intervening confirmed price pivot may be lower than the second bullish low or higher than the second bearish high. Select the eligible pair minimizing the sum of endpoint distances from their CCI pivots plus a weighted span difference; break ties in search order, from the most recent candidate at each endpoint.
Calculation: Confirmed divergence = maintain a separate first CCI pivot for each strength and direction. Initialize it from a low at or below the negative extreme or a high at or above the positive extreme. Clear a bullish first pivot if current CCI reaches the positive extreme, and clear a bearish first pivot if current CCI reaches the negative extreme. On each later same-strength pivot, confirm a bullish divergence when its distance from the first is positive and within the maximum span, its CCI low is higher, and matched bullish price structure exists; reverse the CCI comparison and price structure for bearish divergence. After a qualifying pair, clear its first pivot even if the divergence is suppressed. Otherwise, if the maximum span is exceeded, replace the first pivot with the new pivot only if the latter is extreme; also replace it when the new pivot is extreme and at least as low as the stored bullish low or at least as high as the stored bearish high. Suppress a divergence if a recently recorded same-direction divergence has both matched price endpoints within a deduplication distance. Process strengths from shorter to longer, bullish before bearish at each strength, and accept at most the first unsuppressed divergence per bar.
Calculation: Volume level = on a confirmed divergence, retire all untriggered levels from the preceding setup. Search the inclusive CCI-pivot span for candles whose volume divided by its same-bar volume simple moving average is at least a threshold; select the greatest-volume candle, breaking ties toward the most recent. If none qualifies, repeat within a radius around the second CCI pivot, using only bars available at confirmation and within the search lookback. Create one level from the selected candle, if any. During the subsequent candidate-search window, add each qualifying current candle until the maximum candidates per divergence is reached. Each level is the source candle's high for bullish setups or low for bearish setups; it remains armed through its creation bar plus the breakout window, unless retired or triggered, and may trigger only after its source bar. Retain only a bounded number of the most recently created levels.
Calculation: Signal stop = for a bullish breakout, the low of the most recent source candle among bullish levels breaking on the signal bar minus a multiple of signal-bar ATR; for a bearish breakout, its high plus that ATR multiple. The associated entry price is the next bar's open.
Long: A bullish divergence is confirmed.
Short: A bearish divergence is confirmed.
Long: An armed bullish volume level exists AND the confirmed close is above that level AND the current bar is after its source bar AND no later than its expiry; mark each broken level triggered.
Short: An armed bearish volume level exists AND the confirmed close is below that level AND the current bar is after its source bar AND no later than its expiry; mark each broken level triggered.
Exit: long, low <= signal stop.
Exit: short, high >= signal stop.
