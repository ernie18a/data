Calculation: Trend = bullish when short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND close > long EMA; bearish when short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND close < long EMA; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Structure = bullish break when close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high; bullish change when latest confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high AND close > latest confirmed pivot high; bearish break when close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low; bearish change when latest confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low AND close < latest confirmed pivot low. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks and become available after the right lookback completes.
Calculation: Pressure = let range = high - low, close position = (close - low) / range, lower rejection = (min(open, close) - low) / range, and upper rejection = (high - max(open, close)) / range; when range = 0 use close position = 0.5 and both rejections = 0. Raw buy pressure is capped at 100 and equals the volume weight times the sum of weighted close position, weighted lower rejection, and a fixed contribution if close > open AND abs(close - open) > short ATR times a multiplier. Raw sell pressure is analogous using 1 - close position, upper rejection, and close < open. Volume weight is higher when volume > its SMA. Buy pressure is an EMA of 100 times raw buy pressure divided by the sum of raw pressures, using 50 when that sum is zero; sell pressure = 100 - buy pressure; delta = buy pressure - sell pressure; cumulative delta = EMA of delta.
Calculation: Order flow = bullish when buy pressure > its threshold AND delta > its positive threshold AND cumulative delta > 0; bearish when sell pressure > its threshold AND delta < its negative threshold AND cumulative delta < 0; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bias = on each of three ordered timeframes, test close > EMA; on the middle timeframe also require RSI > its midpoint. Count true tests as bullish and all remaining tests as bearish; bullish bias requires a bullish majority and bearish bias requires a bearish majority. Use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe; a timeframe equal to the chart uses the chart bar. Confidence is the rounded percentage of the larger count out of the three tests.
Calculation: Confidence score = rounded, bounded percentage of the weighted mean of these factors: trend factor is high for bullish or bearish trend and low otherwise; momentum factor is high inside either of two separate RSI ranges, intermediate inside a third range if neither first range applies, and low otherwise; MACD factor is high when MACD line > signal AND histogram > 0 OR MACD line < signal AND histogram < 0, low when neither applies AND absolute histogram is falling, and intermediate otherwise; volume factor is high when volume > volume SMA times the spike multiplier, intermediate when volume > volume SMA, and low otherwise; structure factor is high for any bullish or bearish structure event and low otherwise; order-flow factor is high for bullish or bearish order flow and low otherwise; multi-timeframe factor is selected by descending confidence thresholds; session factor is high in either active UTC session, intermediate in the other defined UTC session, and low otherwise, with active sessions taking precedence; zone factor is high when close < the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low AND trend is bullish OR close > that midpoint AND trend is bearish, and low otherwise. Each factor level, weight, range, threshold, session boundary, and lookback is a parameter.
Calculation: Stop and target = at entry close, initial stop distance is entry-bar ATR times a stop multiplier and target distance is that stop distance times a reward-to-risk multiplier. For a long, stop = entry price - initial stop distance and target = entry price + target distance; for a short, reverse the signs. If trailing is enabled, on every bar including the entry bar replace the long stop by the greater of its previous value and close - current ATR times the stop multiplier, or the short stop by the lesser of its previous value and close + current ATR times the stop multiplier. If breakeven is enabled, after the trailing update move the long stop up to at least entry price when close >= entry price + current ATR times the stop multiplier; move the short stop down to at most entry price when close <= entry price - current ATR times the stop multiplier.
Long: Bullish break OR bullish change.
Short: Bearish break OR bearish change.
Long: Trend = bullish.
Short: Trend = bearish.
Long: Order flow = bullish.
Short: Order flow = bearish.
Long: Multi-timeframe bias = bullish.
Short: Multi-timeframe bias = bearish.
Long: Delta > positive label threshold OR cumulative delta > 0.
Short: Delta < negative label threshold OR cumulative delta < 0.
Long: Previous close < previous open AND close > previous high AND close > open AND abs(close - open) > short ATR times a multiplier AND volume > volume SMA.
Short: Previous close > previous open AND close < previous low AND close < open AND abs(close - open) > short ATR times a multiplier AND volume > volume SMA.
Long: Low > high two bars earlier AND low - high two bars earlier > ATR times a gap multiplier.
Short: High < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier - high > ATR times a gap multiplier.
Long: Trend = bullish AND RSI > lower long threshold AND RSI < upper long threshold AND MACD line > signal AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND volume > volume SMA times a multiplier AND ATR / close is within the normal volatility bounds inclusive AND (session filter disabled OR UTC time is in either active session) AND buy pressure > sell pressure AND confidence score >= the selected mode threshold AND (multi-timeframe filter disabled OR multi-timeframe bias = bullish) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR order flow = bullish OR order flow = neutral AND mode is not conservative) AND (zone filter disabled OR close < midpoint of rolling highest high and lowest low) AND smoothed stochastic K > stochastic D AND K < its upper threshold.
Short: Trend = bearish AND RSI < upper short threshold AND RSI > lower short threshold AND MACD line < signal AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND volume > volume SMA times a multiplier AND ATR / close is within the normal volatility bounds inclusive AND (session filter disabled OR UTC time is in either active session) AND sell pressure > buy pressure AND confidence score >= the selected mode threshold AND (multi-timeframe filter disabled OR multi-timeframe bias = bearish) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR order flow = bearish OR order flow = neutral AND mode is not conservative) AND (zone filter disabled OR close > midpoint of rolling highest high and lowest low) AND smoothed stochastic K < stochastic D AND K > its lower threshold.
Exit: long, low <= updated stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= updated stop OR low <= target.
