Calculation: OBV MACD = Form OBV as the cumulative sum of sign(close change) × volume. Subtract its shorter-lookback SMA; divide by the rolling standard deviation of that difference and multiply by the rolling standard deviation of high − low (use zero if the divisor is zero). Add the result to the high when positive, otherwise to the low. Apply a shorter-lookback DEMA to this series and subtract a longer-lookback EMA of close.
Calculation: OBV MACD direction state = Track an adaptive step line of OBV MACD: on each bar, set it to OBV MACD if OBV MACD is above or below the prior line by more than the cumulative mean absolute change from that prior line; otherwise keep the prior line. A rising line sets the state bullish, a falling line sets it bearish, and an unchanged line retains the prior state.
Calculation: Net signal count = Number of bullish signals minus bearish signals across the four close-versus-EMA comparisons, RSI, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, Supertrend direction, and OBV MACD direction; RSI and Bollinger Bands contribute no signal outside their stated thresholds.
Long: close > short-lookback EMA OR close > medium-short-lookback EMA OR close > medium-long-lookback EMA OR close > long-lookback EMA OR RSI <= 30 OR close <= lower Bollinger Band OR close > VWAP OR Supertrend direction is bullish OR Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish OR OBV MACD direction state is bullish OR Net signal count >= 1
Short: close <= short-lookback EMA OR close <= medium-short-lookback EMA OR close <= medium-long-lookback EMA OR close <= long-lookback EMA OR RSI >= 70 OR close >= upper Bollinger Band OR close <= VWAP OR Supertrend direction is bearish OR Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish OR OBV MACD direction state is bearish OR Net signal count <= -1
