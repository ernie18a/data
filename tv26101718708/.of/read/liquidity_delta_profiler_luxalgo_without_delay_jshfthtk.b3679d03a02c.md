Calculation: Liquidity zones = On confirmation of a pivot high, create a resistance zone with top at the pivot high and bottom at the lower of the pivot candle's body top and pivot high minus a minimum width based on ATR at the pivot. On confirmation of a pivot low, create a support zone with bottom at the pivot low and top at the higher of the pivot candle's body bottom and pivot low plus that minimum width. Confirm pivots using bars before and after the pivot. Optionally filter overlaps separately by zone type: inspect existing unswept zones from oldest to newest; when a new zone overlaps one, remove the existing zone if the new resistance top is higher or the new support bottom is lower; otherwise discard the new zone and stop checking. Retain at most a configured number of zones per type, removing the oldest when exceeded. A zone is swept when high exceeds a resistance top or low falls below a support bottom; stop updating it after that bar. Each zone can signal only once.
Calculation: Zone delta = Divide each zone into four equal price intervals from bottom to top. Bar delta is volume times (close minus open) divided by (high minus low), or zero when high equals low. Starting on the zone's confirmation bar and including its sweep bar, add to each interval's accumulated delta the bar delta multiplied by the positive length of the bar's high-low overlap with that interval divided by the bar's high-low range. The outer delta is the accumulated delta of the highest resistance interval or lowest support interval. Total absolute delta is the sum of the absolute accumulated interval deltas. Outer ratio is absolute outer delta divided by total absolute delta plus a small positive constant. Bar ratio is absolute bar delta divided by volume plus that constant.
Long: An unswept-before-this-bar support zone has not signaled AND total absolute delta > 0 AND (the bar overlaps the zone by a positive price range OR low < zone bottom) AND ((low < zone bottom AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > absorption threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer ratio < exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > divergence threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND bar ratio > rejection threshold)); the exhaustion threshold is lower than the absorption threshold, which is lower than the divergence threshold.
Short: An unswept-before-this-bar resistance zone has not signaled AND total absolute delta > 0 AND (the bar overlaps the zone by a positive price range OR high > zone top) AND ((high > zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > absorption threshold) OR (high > zone top AND outer ratio < exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > divergence threshold) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND bar ratio > rejection threshold)); the exhaustion threshold is lower than the absorption threshold, which is lower than the divergence threshold.
