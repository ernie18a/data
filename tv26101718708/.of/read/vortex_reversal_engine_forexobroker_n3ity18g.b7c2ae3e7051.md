Calculation: Recent lower-band touch = at least one bar from the current bar through the configured number of bars ago has low <= its own lower Bollinger Band.
Calculation: Recent upper-band touch = at least one bar from the current bar through the configured number of bars ago has high >= its own upper Bollinger Band.
Calculation: Recent oversold RSI = at least one bar from the current bar through the same number of bars ago as the band-touch lookback has RSI below the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Recent overbought RSI = at least one bar from the current bar through the same number of bars ago as the band-touch lookback has RSI above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Exit levels = signal-bar close plus or minus signal-bar ATR multiplied by the respective target or stop multiplier; hold these levels fixed after the signal. The second target uses a separate multiplier.
Calculation: Trailing distance = signal-bar ATR multiplied by the trailing multiplier, rounded to the nearest price tick. Activate the long trailing stop when price reaches actual entry price plus this distance, then set it to the highest price since activation minus this distance; activate the short trailing stop when price reaches actual entry price minus this distance, then set it to the lowest price since activation plus this distance.
Long: close < Bollinger Band basis.
Short: close > Bollinger Band basis.
Long: RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI <= overbought threshold.
Short: RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: position side = flat AND recent lower-band touch AND recent oversold RSI.
Short: position side = flat AND recent upper-band touch AND recent overbought RSI AND NOT (recent lower-band touch AND recent oversold RSI).
Long: position side = flat AND ADX < range threshold AND recent lower-band touch AND recent oversold RSI AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close < Bollinger Band basis AND RSI > previous RSI AND RSI < bullish recovery ceiling AND close > open AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar opening UTC time is within the selected session) AND bars since each of the most recent long and short entry signals >= cooldown; evaluate at bar close.
Short: position side = flat AND ADX < range threshold AND recent upper-band touch AND recent overbought RSI AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close > Bollinger Band basis AND RSI < previous RSI AND RSI > bearish recovery floor AND close < open AND close < (high + low) / 2 AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar opening UTC time is within the selected session) AND bars since each of the most recent long and short entry signals >= cooldown; evaluate at bar close.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR high >= first target; in partial mode apply this to the first portion only.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR low <= first target; in partial mode apply this to the first portion only.
Exit: long, partial mode enabled AND (low <= fixed stop OR high >= second target OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND low <= trailing stop)); apply to the remaining portion.
Exit: short, partial mode enabled AND (high >= fixed stop OR low <= second target OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND high >= trailing stop)); apply to the remaining portion.
