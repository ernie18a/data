Calculation: Active levels = On each completed chart bar, first discard upper pivot candidates when high exceeds candidate price plus its percentage matching tolerance, and lower candidates when low falls below candidate price minus that tolerance. Then process existing active levels for breaks, followed by expiration at their maximum age. Finally process newly confirmed pivot highs, then pivot lows. A pivot is confirmed after the same number of bars on each side. For each pivot, if a same-side active level is within its percentage matching tolerance, leave that level unchanged. Otherwise, match the closest same-side candidate within the candidate's percentage tolerance and at least the minimum pivot-bar spacing away; create an upper level at the higher pivot price or a lower level at the lower pivot price, and remove the matched candidate. If none matches, store the pivot as a candidate. Limit candidate and active-level storage by removing the oldest when their respective capacities are exceeded. New levels become eligible for breaks starting on the next bar.
Calculation: Broken levels = When an active upper level is broken by high or close above its price plus an ATR buffer, or an active lower level is broken by low or close below its price minus an ATR buffer, remove it from active levels and store it as broken. Use the selected wick-or-close break trigger consistently. Limit broken-level storage by removing the oldest when capacity is exceeded. A broken level can produce only its first qualifying retest, starting after its break bar.
Long: An active upper level breaks.
Long: Low is at or below a broken upper level AND close is above that level AND it has not previously qualified for a retest.
Short: An active lower level breaks.
Short: High is at or above a broken lower level AND close is below that level AND it has not previously qualified for a retest.
