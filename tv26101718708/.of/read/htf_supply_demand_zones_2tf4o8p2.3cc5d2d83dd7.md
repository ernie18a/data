Calculation: Demand zone = On the first chart bar after a higher-timeframe bar completes, if that last completed bar closes above its open and its body is at least an impulse multiplier times its higher-timeframe ATR, select the most recent bearish higher-timeframe bar within a lookback preceding the impulse bar. The zone is either that bar's body or its full high-to-low range, according to a setting, provided its width exceeds a minimum-width parameter.
Calculation: Supply zone = On the first chart bar after a higher-timeframe bar completes, if that last completed bar closes below its open and its body is at least an impulse multiplier times its higher-timeframe ATR, select the most recent bullish higher-timeframe bar within a lookback preceding the impulse bar. The zone is either that bar's body or its full high-to-low range, according to the same setting, provided its width exceeds the minimum-width parameter.
Calculation: Active zones = Keep a separate creation-ordered list for each zone type; when a new zone exceeds the per-side count limit, remove the oldest zone, including broken zones in the count. After adding zones on each chart bar, mark a demand zone broken if the chart close is below its lower bound, or if the chart low is below its lower bound when the alternative break setting is selected. Mark a supply zone broken if the chart close is above its upper bound, or if the chart high is above its upper bound under the alternative break setting. A zone remains active until broken or removed.
Long: A demand zone is newly created AND it remains active after the current chart bar's break check.
Long: Chart low <= an active demand zone's upper bound AND chart high >= that zone's lower bound.
Short: A supply zone is newly created AND it remains active after the current chart bar's break check.
Short: Chart high >= an active supply zone's lower bound AND chart low <= that zone's upper bound.
