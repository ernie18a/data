Calculation: Trend line = Let A = WMA(ATR(1), lookback), H = highest high over lookback, and L = lowest low over lookback. On each bar, upper = prior-bar H − prior-bar A × multiplier; lower = prior-bar L + prior-bar A × multiplier. Set trend line to upper if close > upper AND close > lower; otherwise set it to lower if close < lower AND close < upper; otherwise retain its prior value.
Long: On the higher-timeframe completed bars, the short-term DEMA crosses above the long-term DEMA; use the uptrend DEMA pair when close > trend line, otherwise use the normal DEMA pair.
Exit: Long, a short-term DEMA crosses below the long-term DEMA OR close crosses below the trend line OR unrealized loss as a percentage of equity is greater than the stop-loss threshold AND less than the let-it-ride threshold.
