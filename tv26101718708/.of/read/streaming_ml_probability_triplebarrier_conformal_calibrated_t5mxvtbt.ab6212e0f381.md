Calculation: Features = bounded inputs comprising rolling z-scores of one-bar close change, longer-lookback close momentum, centered efficiency ratio, centered RSI, close-to-EMA distance divided by ATR, and rolling volume z-score.
Calculation: Training labels = for each confirmed bar with valid features, open a sample at close with symmetric upper and lower barriers offset by ATR; label it by the first barrier touched, resolving same-bar ties by whether close is at or above entry; discard timeouts. Skip opening samples when the close-minus-EMA spread's AR(1) half-life exceeds the vertical horizon. Train online logistic regression on resolved labels with uniqueness-weighted SGD and L2 regularization; retain recent resolved samples for Lorentzian k-NN, whose distance sums log(1 + absolute feature differences) and whose vote averages the nearest labels.
Calculation: Model probability = logistic probability fused with the k-NN vote in log-odds using a decorrelation shrink; shrink toward 0.5 according to resolved-sample count in the current regime; recalibrate through a monotone isotonic map fitted to resolved outcomes binned by prior probability.
Calculation: Probability band = model probability plus or minus the conformal quantile of recent absolute differences between resolved binary labels and their recorded probabilities; adapt the quantile using the online coverage-error update.
Calculation: Regime = volatile when normalized exponentially decayed absolute log returns exceed their threshold and the market is not trending; otherwise trending when ADX and efficiency exceed their thresholds, classified bullish when close is above its EMA and bearish otherwise; classify remaining bars as range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close above/below EMA on each higher timeframe; use the latest available bar with lookahead off, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Participation direction = sign of the change in cumulative volume delta over its lookback; each bar's delta is volume signed positive when close is at or above open and negative otherwise.
Calculation: Conviction = rounded weighted sum of model confidence, regime fit, higher-timeframe directional agreement, calibration quality from Brier score, and participation alignment; use the candidate direction, or the probability's leaning direction when there is no candidate.
Long: resolved-label count reaches its warm-up threshold AND lower probability band > 0.5 + signal margin AND recalibrated probability >= 0.5 AND conviction >= its floor AND regime is not volatile or bearish AND bullish higher-timeframe agreement reaches its minimum (when enabled) AND participation is not bearish (when enabled) AND minimum spacing since the prior signal has elapsed.
Short: resolved-label count reaches its warm-up threshold AND upper probability band < 0.5 - signal margin AND recalibrated probability <= 0.5 AND conviction >= its floor AND regime is not volatile or bullish AND bearish higher-timeframe agreement reaches its minimum (when enabled) AND participation is not bullish (when enabled) AND minimum spacing since the prior signal has elapsed.
