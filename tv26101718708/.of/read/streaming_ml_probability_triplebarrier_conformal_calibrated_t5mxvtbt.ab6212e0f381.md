Calculation: Features = six chart-bar values: rolling population z-scores of the one-bar close change, the momentum-period close change, and volume, each clipped to [-5, 5] and replaced by zero when unavailable or its standard deviation is zero; twice the efficiency ratio minus one, clipped to [-1, 1]; RSI divided by 100 minus 0.5; and (close minus its EMA) divided by ATR, clipped to [-4, 4] or zero when ATR is nonpositive. Replace unavailable efficiency and RSI features with zero. Efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Regime = trending when ADX and efficiency ratio both exceed their respective thresholds; a trending regime is up when close is above its regime EMA and down otherwise. A non-trending regime is volatile when the rolling z-score of the exponentially decayed sum of absolute log returns exceeds its threshold, and is range otherwise. Initialize that decayed sum at zero and update it as prior sum × exp(-decay parameter) + absolute log return, replacing an unavailable return or z-score with zero.
Calculation: Participation = the sign of the change over its lookback in cumulative signed volume, where signed volume is positive when close >= open and negative otherwise; replace unavailable volume with zero.
Calculation: Model state = initialize logistic weights, resolved counts, reliability-bin counts and sums to zero, and initialize conformal working alpha and recent miss rate to target alpha. On each closed chart bar, first calculate the current prediction from the state carried over from the previous bar. Once the bar index exceeds the longest feature lookback, open a training sample containing close, ATR, features, regime, current prediction, current conformal half-width and the current number of unresolved samples. Optionally skip opening when the valid AR(1) half-life of close minus its feature EMA exceeds the barrier horizon: estimate the AR coefficient as rolling covariance of the spread and its one-bar lag divided by rolling variance of the lag; the half-life is -log(2)/log(coefficient) only when the coefficient is between zero and one. Resolve samples newest first, including the newly opened sample, against entry close ± barrier-width multiplier × entry ATR. An upper touch gives outcome 1 and a lower touch gives 0; if both touch on the same bar, use 1 when that bar's close >= sample entry and 0 otherwise. Remove samples that reach the horizon without a touch without training. For every resolved outcome, recompute the logistic prediction from the sample features and current weights; update the bias by subtracting learning rate × error / (1 + the sample's recorded unresolved-sample count), and each feature weight by subtracting learning rate × (error × feature + L2 coefficient × weight) / (1 + that count); clip all weights to [-12, 12]. Append the features and outcome to a bounded recent-sample neighbor memory, append absolute outcome minus the prediction recorded at sample opening to a bounded conformal memory, update the reliability bin selected by that opening prediction, update the sum and count of squared prediction errors, and increment the overall and opening-regime resolved counts. A miss is 1 only when the recorded half-width exists and the absolute prediction error exceeds it, otherwise 0. Update working alpha by adding adaptation rate × (target alpha - miss), clipped to [0.01, 0.50], and update the recent miss rate by adding smoothing rate × (miss - recent miss rate).
Calculation: Prediction = sigmoid of the logistic bias plus the feature-weight dot product, with sigmoid input clipped to [-30, 30]. Optionally, when neighbor memory contains at least the configured number of samples, find the nearest samples by sum over features of log(1 + absolute feature difference), breaking ties by memory order; their mean outcome is the neighbor probability. Fuse by taking sigmoid of the decorrelation factor × (logit of logistic probability + logit of neighbor probability), clipping each probability to [0.000001, 0.999999] before its logit. Without an available or enabled neighbor vote, use the logistic probability. Optionally shrink the fused probability toward 0.5 by multiplying its deviation from 0.5 by min(1, current-regime resolved count / warm-up sample threshold).
Calculation: Conformal half-width = the linearly interpolated quantile of the bounded recent absolute-error memory at 1 - effective alpha, available only after the minimum conformal-memory count. Effective alpha is working alpha + proportional gain × (target alpha - recent miss rate), clipped to [0.01, 0.50]. The lower and upper bounds are prediction minus and plus the half-width, clipped to [0, 1].
Calculation: Recalibrated probability = optionally map the prediction through a monotone pool-adjacent-violators fit of outcome frequencies in equal-width prediction bins that each meet the minimum bin count. Weight each bin frequency by its count, merge adjacent decreasing blocks using weighted means, retain the first bin midpoint as each merged block's coordinate, and interpolate linearly between block coordinates with constant extrapolation. Use the prediction unchanged when recalibration is disabled or fewer than two eligible bins exist.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = for each configured higher timeframe, compare its close with its EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars; on a realtime chart bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Count the timeframes above and below their EMA separately; equality contributes to neither count. When higher-timeframe confluence is disabled, both counts are zero.
Calculation: Conviction = round(30 × confidence + 20 × regime fit + 20 × timeframe agreement + 20 × calibration quality + 10 × participation fit), evaluated for the candidate direction. Confidence is min(1, absolute prediction deviation from 0.5 / 0.5). Regime fit is 1 for a matching trend, 0.5 for range, and 0.2 otherwise. Timeframe agreement is the matching higher-timeframe count divided by the total number of configured higher timeframes, or 0.5 when confluence is disabled. Calibration quality is (0.25 - mean squared opening-prediction error) / 0.15 clipped to [0, 1], or zero before any outcomes resolve. Participation fit is 1 when participation matches the candidate direction, 0.6 when neutral, and 0.4 otherwise. Use the resolved sample updates from the current bar for calibration quality and warm-up counts, while using the prediction and band calculated before those updates.
Long: prediction >= 0.5 OR EMA of recalibrated probability >= 0.5.
Short: prediction < 0.5 OR EMA of recalibrated probability < 0.5.
Long: regime = up trend OR participation > 0.
Short: regime = down trend OR participation < 0.
Long: resolved sample count >= warm-up threshold AND conformal lower bound > 0.5 + signal margin AND recalibrated probability >= 0.5 AND (regime veto disabled OR regime is neither volatile nor down trend) AND (timeframe veto disabled OR timeframe confluence disabled OR bullish higher-timeframe agreement count >= minimum agreement) AND (conviction veto disabled OR long conviction >= minimum conviction) AND (participation veto disabled OR participation >= 0) AND bars since the most recent long or short signal >= minimum signal gap.
Short: resolved sample count >= warm-up threshold AND conformal upper bound < 0.5 - signal margin AND recalibrated probability <= 0.5 AND (regime veto disabled OR regime is neither volatile nor up trend) AND (timeframe veto disabled OR timeframe confluence disabled OR bearish higher-timeframe agreement count >= minimum agreement) AND (conviction veto disabled OR short conviction >= minimum conviction) AND (participation veto disabled OR participation <= 0) AND bars since the most recent long or short signal >= minimum signal gap.
