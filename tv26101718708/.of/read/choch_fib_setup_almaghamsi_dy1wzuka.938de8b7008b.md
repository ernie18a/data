Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the pivot bar higher/lower than the preceding and following lookback bars on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish after price breaks above the latest confirmed swing high while bias is neutral or bearish; bearish after price breaks below the latest confirmed swing low while bias is neutral or bullish; otherwise retain the prior bias, initially neutral
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement levels = for a bullish setup, impulse high − (impulse high − impulse low) × 0.5 and × 0.618; for a bearish setup, impulse low + (impulse high − impulse low) × 0.5 and × 0.618
Long: (close > latest confirmed swing high AND structure bias is neutral or bearish AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled or higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish)) OR (latest aligned setup is bullish AND close is between the 0.5 and 0.618 Fibonacci retracement levels)
Short: (close < latest confirmed swing low AND structure bias is neutral or bullish AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled or higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish)) OR (latest aligned setup is bearish AND close is between the 0.5 and 0.618 Fibonacci retracement levels)
