Calculation: Bullish gap = the most recently detected gap with current low > high two bars earlier and gap size >= a sensitivity-dependent fraction of current ATR; its bottom is that earlier high, its top is current low, and its age starts at zero. Before detecting a new gap on each bar, clear the stored gap if age > 0 and age <= the sensitivity-dependent lifetime and low <= its top and close > open and close > its bottom, regardless of entry method or filters. A new gap replaces any stored bullish gap.
Calculation: Bearish gap = the most recently detected gap with current high < low two bars earlier and gap size >= a sensitivity-dependent fraction of current ATR; its top is that earlier low, its bottom is current high, and its age starts at zero. Before detecting a new gap on each bar, clear the stored gap if age > 0 and age <= the sensitivity-dependent lifetime and high >= its bottom and close < open and close < its top, regardless of entry method or filters. A new gap replaces any stored bearish gap.
Calculation: Session eligibility = the bar opens within an enabled session on an enabled day in the configured timezone AND, if session-end closing is enabled, the bar closes strictly inside that session with more than one chart bar's duration remaining. For an overnight session, apply the configured day test to each timestamp's local calendar day.
Calculation: News blackout = the bar close time is >= an enabled event time minus the configured offset AND < that event time plus the same offset, using the configured timezone.
Calculation: Bullish setup = if the gap method is selected, the bullish gap is cleared on this bar; otherwise, the short-term EMA > the long-term EMA AND close > the short-term EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND this conjunction was false on the previous bar.
Calculation: Bearish setup = if the gap method is selected, the bearish gap is cleared on this bar; otherwise, the short-term EMA < the long-term EMA AND close < the short-term EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND this conjunction was false on the previous bar.
Calculation: Bullish signal = bullish setup AND either the least restrictive sensitivity is selected OR close > the trend-filter EMA AND either the intermediate sensitivity is selected OR the last completed higher-timeframe close > its higher-timeframe trend-filter EMA AND chart-timeframe ADX >= its threshold. The same sensitivity selects the gap lifetime, minimum gap size and EMA lengths; more restrictive settings use longer gap lifetimes, larger minimum gaps and longer entry EMA lengths.
Calculation: Bearish signal = bearish setup AND either the least restrictive sensitivity is selected OR close < the trend-filter EMA AND either the intermediate sensitivity is selected OR the last completed higher-timeframe close < its higher-timeframe trend-filter EMA AND chart-timeframe ADX >= its threshold; AND bullish signal is false.
Long: Bullish signal AND session eligibility AND news blackout is false AND position is flat or short.
Short: Bearish signal AND session eligibility AND news blackout is false AND position is flat or long.
Exit: long, bearish signal AND session eligibility AND news blackout is false.
Exit: short, bullish signal AND session eligibility AND news blackout is false.
Exit: long or short, session-end closing is enabled AND the bar opens inside an enabled session AND closes outside that same session OR news blackout is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance.
