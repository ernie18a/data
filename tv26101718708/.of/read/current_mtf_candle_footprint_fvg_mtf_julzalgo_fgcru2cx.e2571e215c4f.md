Calculation: Chart-bar buy and sell volume = Sum classified volume over all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar. Classify each lower-timeframe bar by close versus open; if equal, classify by close versus the previous lower-timeframe close. Assign its volume to buy if higher, sell if lower, and half to each if equal. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, let range = max(high − low, minimum price increment), body = close − open, upper wick = high − max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) − low, and total = max(abs(body) + upper wick + lower wick, minimum price increment). Buy strength = (close − low) / range × 0.55 + lower wick / total × 0.25 + max(body, 0) / range × 0.20; sell strength = (1 − (close − low) / range) × 0.55 + upper wick / total × 0.25 + max(−body, 0) / range × 0.20. Allocate chart-bar volume in proportion to the two strengths, or half to each if their sum is zero.
Calculation: Forming higher-timeframe buy and sell volume = Sum chart-bar buy and sell volume since the start of the still-forming higher-timeframe candle.
Calculation: Footprint node buy and sell volume = Divide the still-forming higher-timeframe candle's high-to-low range or open-to-close body into equal price rows. If the range is smaller than minimum price increment × max(row count, text-clamp parameter), expand it symmetrically to that size. For each completed lower-timeframe bar within the candle, split its classified buy and sell volume equally across all rows touched by its high-to-low range; if lower-timeframe bars are unavailable for a chart bar, use that chart bar's high, low, and estimated buy and sell volume. Sum the allocations per row.
Long: Close >= open of the still-forming higher-timeframe candle.
Short: Close < open of the still-forming higher-timeframe candle.
Long: Forming higher-timeframe buy volume >= forming higher-timeframe sell volume.
Short: Forming higher-timeframe buy volume < forming higher-timeframe sell volume.
Long: Footprint node buy volume >= footprint node sell volume.
Short: Footprint node buy volume < footprint node sell volume.
Long: A new selected-timeframe bar is detected AND the low of the last completed selected-timeframe bar minus the high of the third most recently completed selected-timeframe bar >= minimum gap expressed in price units. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe, use the last selected-timeframe bar returned within each chart bar.
Short: A new selected-timeframe bar is detected AND the low of the third most recently completed selected-timeframe bar minus the high of the last completed selected-timeframe bar >= minimum gap expressed in price units. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe, use the last selected-timeframe bar returned within each chart bar.
