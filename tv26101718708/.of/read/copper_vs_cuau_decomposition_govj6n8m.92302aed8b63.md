Calculation: Copper z-score = EMA-smoothed rolling z-score of copper closes from the higher timeframe’s last completed bars
Calculation: Copper/gold ratio z-score = EMA-smoothed rolling z-score of copper close divided by gold close from the same higher-timeframe bar, using the higher timeframe’s last completed bars
Calculation: Copper-minus-ratio spread = copper z-score minus copper/gold ratio z-score
Long: Copper-minus-ratio spread > 0 AND copper-minus-ratio spread > its value on the previous bar
