Calculation: RSI signal = RSI of closes on a configurable timeframe; on a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time, on a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar, and on the chart timeframe use the current bar. Evaluate the crossing on that timeframe before mapping it to the chart.
Calculation: Averaging threshold for the next order = base entry price multiplied by one plus the cumulative deviation, where cumulative deviation is the sum of the initial deviation and successive deviations multiplied by a configurable step multiplier for each preceding averaging order.
Calculation: Average entry price = quantity-weighted average fill price of the base entry and filled averaging orders; each averaging order's quote size equals the first averaging order's quote size multiplied by a configurable size multiplier raised to the number of preceding averaging orders, and its quantity is that quote size divided by the signal bar's close.
Calculation: Trailing minimum = lowest close observed while the short is open and the close is at or below the current average entry price reduced by the take-profit fraction; retain this minimum when the close is above that level and reset it when the position is flat.
Short: Within the configured backtest window, or the window filter is disabled, AND position is flat AND no trade is open AND RSI signal crosses below its threshold.
Short: Within the configured backtest window, or the window filter is disabled, AND position is short AND the number of averaging orders already triggered is below the configured limit AND close >= averaging threshold for the next order; trigger at most one averaging order per bar.
Exit: short, close >= average entry price multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction.
Exit: short, trailing minimum exists AND close >= trailing minimum multiplied by one plus the trailing fraction.
