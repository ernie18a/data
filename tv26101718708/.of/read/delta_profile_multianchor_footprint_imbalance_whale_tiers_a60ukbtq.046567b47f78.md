Calculation: Chart delta = buy volume minus sell volume; classify completed lower-timeframe bars by close versus open, assigning their volume to buys or sells and splitting unchanged bars equally; sum within each chart bar. If lower-timeframe volume is unavailable, split chart volume by the close's position within the high-low range.
Calculation: Pressure = chart delta standardized over a rolling window using either mean and standard deviation or median and median absolute deviation, then clipped to a fixed symmetric range.
Calculation: Timeframe flow = volume multiplied by ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low; standardize by rolling median and median absolute deviation and clip to a fixed symmetric range. For higher timeframes, use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Flow alignment = sum of the signs of chart-timeframe and progressively higher-timeframe flows, assigning zero to each flow inside a symmetric neutral band.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize the upward stop at close minus an ATR multiple; while upward, raise it to the greater of its previous value and close minus the ATR multiple, switching downward and resetting it to close plus the ATR multiple when close falls below it. While downward, lower it to the lesser of its previous value and close plus the ATR multiple, switching upward and resetting it to close minus the ATR multiple when close rises above it. ATR is calculated on the chart timeframe or on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of chart delta, optionally reset at each session boundary.
Long: Pressure crosses above a positive threshold AND pressure has reached or fallen below zero since the previous long pressure crossing AND the optional efficiency-ratio regime filter passes AND the optional flow-alignment filter is disabled or flow alignment is positive.
Short: Pressure crosses below a negative threshold AND pressure has reached or risen above zero since the previous short pressure crossing AND the optional efficiency-ratio regime filter passes AND the optional flow-alignment filter is disabled or flow alignment is negative.
Long: The trailing stop switches upward.
Short: The trailing stop switches downward.
Long: On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, timeframe flow rises above the positive neutral-band threshold AND its preceding chart-bar observation was not above that threshold.
Short: On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, timeframe flow falls below the negative neutral-band threshold AND its preceding chart-bar observation was not below that threshold.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND cumulative delta at that pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot low.
Short: A confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND cumulative delta at that pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot high.
Exit: long, low reaches the stop fixed at entry OR high reaches the farther profit target fixed at entry.
Exit: short, high reaches the stop fixed at entry OR low reaches the farther profit target fixed at entry.
