Calculation: ATR volatility filter = ATR is above its own same-length simple moving average times a lower bound and below it times an upper bound
Calculation: EMA compression = absolute difference between the shorter-lookback EMA and longer-lookback EMA is below ATR times a compression threshold
Calculation: Bullish structure = at least one confirmed pivot high and low exist, and close is above the latest recorded higher pivot low; a pivot is confirmed using equal left and right lookbacks
Calculation: Bearish structure = at least one confirmed pivot high and low exist, and close is below the latest recorded lower pivot high; a pivot is confirmed using equal left and right lookbacks
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low is below the prior lookback-period low, close is above that low, and close is above open
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high is above the prior lookback-period high, close is below that high, and close is below open
Calculation: Bullish breakout = close is above the prior 20-bar high, volume exceeds its longer-lookback simple moving average times a multiplier, and candle body exceeds its 20-bar simple moving average
Calculation: Bearish breakout = close is below the prior 20-bar low, volume exceeds its longer-lookback simple moving average times a multiplier, and candle body exceeds its 20-bar simple moving average
Calculation: Confirmed bullish breakout = bullish breakout and the current close has not fallen back below the breakout level on the following bar
Calculation: Confirmed bearish breakout = bearish breakout and the current close has not risen back above the breakout level on the following bar
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier and the middle bar closed above that earlier high; use this condition from the prior bar
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high is below the low two bars earlier and the middle bar closed below that earlier low; use this condition from the prior bar
Calculation: Bullish order block = prior candle closed below open, current candle closed above open, and current body exceeds 1.5 times the 20-bar average body
Calculation: Bearish order block = prior candle closed above open, current candle closed below open, and current body exceeds 1.5 times the 20-bar average body
Calculation: Long score = count of: close above both EMAs and shorter-lookback EMA above longer-lookback EMA; close above VWAP; bullish structure; volume spike; ATR volatility filter; ADX above threshold and no EMA compression; RSI above its long threshold; bullish sweep or confirmed bullish breakout or close above a prior confirmed pivot high; close above prior bar high; enabled higher-timeframe EMA alignment; bullish fair value gap or bullish order block
Calculation: Short score = count of: close below both EMAs and shorter-lookback EMA below longer-lookback EMA; close below VWAP; bearish structure; volume spike; ATR volatility filter; ADX above threshold and no EMA compression; RSI below its short threshold; bearish sweep or confirmed bearish breakout or close below a prior confirmed pivot low; close below prior bar low; enabled higher-timeframe EMA alignment; bearish fair value gap or bearish order block
Long: long score meets an enabled weak, medium, or strong signal threshold AND the bar is in an enabled London or New York session AND the long signal cooldown has elapsed AND no short signal is simultaneously active; OR bullish sweep; OR bullish break of structure (close above a confirmed pivot high); OR close above both EMAs and shorter-lookback EMA above longer-lookback EMA; OR this EMA trend condition became true on the current bar
Short: short score meets an enabled weak, medium, or strong signal threshold AND the bar is in an enabled London or New York session AND the short signal cooldown has elapsed AND no long signal is simultaneously active; OR bearish sweep; OR bearish break of structure (close below a confirmed pivot low); OR close below both EMAs and shorter-lookback EMA below longer-lookback EMA; OR this EMA trend condition became true on the current bar
