Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when close > shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA; bearish when close < shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding structure-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open; bearish sweep = high > highest high of the preceding structure-lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = a pivot high confirmed structure-lookback bars ago exists AND close > that pivot high; bearish break of structure = a pivot low confirmed structure-lookback bars ago exists AND close < that pivot low. A pivot uses the structure lookback on both sides and is available only after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago; bearish gap = high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND volume > its SMA times a volume multiplier AND absolute candle body > its SMA AND, if the fake-breakout filter is enabled, NOT (the previous bar was a bullish breakout AND close < the highest high of the breakout-lookback bars preceding the previous bar). Bearish breakout = close < lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND volume > its SMA times the volume multiplier AND absolute candle body > its SMA AND, if the fake-breakout filter is enabled, NOT (the previous bar was a bearish breakout AND close > the lowest low of the breakout-lookback bars preceding the previous bar). The body SMA includes the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish order block = previous close < previous open AND close > open AND absolute candle body > its SMA times an impulse multiplier; bearish order block = previous close > previous open AND close < open AND absolute candle body > its SMA times the impulse multiplier.
Calculation: Long score = sum of one point for each true condition: bullish trend direction; VWAP filter disabled OR close > cumulative volume-weighted average of typical price from the first available bar; volume > its SMA times the volume multiplier; ATR > its same-length SMA times a minimum multiplier AND ATR < that SMA times a maximum multiplier; ADX > its threshold AND absolute difference between the two trend EMAs >= ATR times a compression multiplier; RSI filter disabled OR RSI > its long threshold; enabled bullish sweep OR bullish breakout OR bullish break of structure; close > previous high; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; enabled bullish gap on the previous bar OR enabled bullish order block. The structure score point is always zero because its required pivot state is never initialized.
Calculation: Short score = sum of one point for each true condition: bearish trend direction; VWAP filter disabled OR close < cumulative volume-weighted average of typical price from the first available bar; volume > its SMA times the volume multiplier; ATR > its same-length SMA times a minimum multiplier AND ATR < that SMA times a maximum multiplier; ADX > its threshold AND absolute difference between the two trend EMAs >= ATR times a compression multiplier; RSI filter disabled OR RSI < its short threshold; enabled bearish sweep OR bearish breakout OR bearish break of structure; close < previous low; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; enabled bearish gap on the previous bar OR enabled bearish order block. The structure score point is always zero because its required pivot state is never initialized.
Calculation: Raw long signal = session filter disabled OR bar time is in either configured London-time or New York-time session, AND bars since the last final long signal >= cooldown, AND long score >= strong threshold OR medium signals enabled AND long score >= medium threshold OR weak signals enabled AND long score >= weak threshold. Group the session, cooldown, and score clauses with AND; thresholds satisfy strong > medium > weak. Initialize the last final long signal's bar index to zero. Raw short signal is defined symmetrically using short score and the last final short signal. A final long signal occurs when the raw long signal is true AND the raw short signal is false; a final short signal occurs when the raw short signal is true AND the raw long signal is false. Update each side's last signal index only on its final signal.
Long: Trend direction = bullish.
Short: Trend direction = bearish.
Long: Bullish sweeps enabled AND bullish sweep = true.
Short: Bearish sweeps enabled AND bearish sweep = true.
Long: Bullish gaps enabled AND bullish gap = true.
Short: Bearish gaps enabled AND bearish gap = true.
Long: Bullish break of structure = true.
Short: Bearish break of structure = true.
Long: Long score >= weak threshold.
Short: Short score >= weak threshold.
Long: Final long signal = true.
Short: Final short signal = true.
