Calculation: Composite = 100 × (average of the seven 1–5 tiers for RSI, EMA stack, MACD, ADX/DI, Ichimoku, Bollinger Bands, and OBV − 1) / 4; 5 denotes oversold and 1 overbought.
Calculation: RSI tier = 5 below the extreme-oversold threshold, 4 below the oversold threshold, 1 above the extreme-overbought threshold, 2 above the overbought threshold, otherwise 3.
Calculation: EMA-stack tier = 1 for a fully descending short-to-long EMA stack; 2 when the shortest EMA exceeds the next but the next is below the third; 3 when the first three are descending but the third is below the fourth; 4 when the first four are descending but the full stack is not; 5 for a fully ascending short-to-long stack; otherwise 3. The stack uses progressively longer EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: MACD tier = 1 when MACD is below zero, histogram below zero, and histogram falling; 2 when MACD is below zero and (histogram is nonnegative or rising); 3 on a MACD/signal cross at or across zero as specified by the cross direction; 5 when MACD and histogram are above zero and both are rising; 4 when MACD and histogram are above zero and (MACD exceeds its signal or histogram is rising); otherwise 3.
Calculation: ADX/DI tier = 1 when ADX is below the weak-trend cutoff and −DI > +DI; 2 when ADX is in the weak-trend band and −DI > +DI; 3 when ADX is in that band and +DI and −DI are within the configured equality tolerance; 4 when ADX is in the strong-trend band and +DI > −DI; 5 when ADX is above the strong-trend band and +DI > −DI; otherwise 3.
Calculation: Ichimoku tier = 1 when Tenkan < Kijun, close is below the displaced cloud, and close is below its value one displacement ago; 2 when Tenkan < Kijun and (close is in the cloud or is not below its value one displacement ago); 3 when Tenkan/Kijun cross in the cloud; 5 when Tenkan > Kijun, close is above the cloud and its value one displacement ago, and the current cloud is bullish and widening; 4 when the first three of those bullish conditions hold without the widening condition; otherwise 3. Use the last completed displaced cloud values.
Calculation: Bollinger tier = 1 when price is below the lower band or at/below it with the lower band or basis falling; 5 when price is above the upper band or at/above it with the upper band or basis rising; 2 between lower band and basis; 4 between basis and upper band; otherwise 3.
Calculation: OBV tier = 1 when OBV is below its moving average and falling over the short lookback; 2 when below its average and (rising over the short lookback or within the configured near-average tolerance); 3 when within that tolerance; 4 when above its average and rising over the short lookback; 5 when above its average, rising over the longer lookback, and its latest change exceeds the configured multiple of its average absolute change; otherwise 3. The longer rise lookback exceeds the short rise lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low/high = a low/high that is the extreme across the configured number of chart bars on both sides, recognized only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Support/resistance zone = an ATR-scaled band anchored at a confirmed swing low/high, respectively; its bounds extend mostly below a support anchor or above a resistance anchor, with a smaller portion across the anchor. A support zone is BUY-DIP when Composite at the pivot is at/above the oversold threshold; a resistance zone is SELL-RIP when Composite at the pivot is at/below the overbought threshold. Skip a new zone if an active same-side zone anchor is closer than the configured ATR-based deduplication distance.
Long: Composite >= oversold threshold OR Composite >= oversold threshold AND previous Composite < oversold threshold OR confirmed swing low AND Composite at pivot >= oversold threshold AND no active same-side zone anchor is within the configured deduplication distance OR active BUY-DIP support zone AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND more than two bars since the last zone touch.
Short: Composite <= overbought threshold OR Composite <= overbought threshold AND previous Composite > overbought threshold OR confirmed swing high AND Composite at pivot <= overbought threshold AND no active same-side zone anchor is within the configured deduplication distance OR active SELL-RIP resistance zone AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND more than two bars since the last zone touch.
Exit: long, close < support-zone bottom × (1 − invalidation offset)
Exit: short, close > resistance-zone top × (1 + invalidation offset)
