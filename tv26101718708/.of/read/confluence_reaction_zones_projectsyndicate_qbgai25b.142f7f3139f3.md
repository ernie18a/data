Calculation: Rising(x, length) = x is greater than every one of its preceding length values; falling is defined symmetrically.
Calculation: Signal levels = the following ordered classifications, taking the first matching level and otherwise level 3. All indicator sources and lengths are parameters. RSI: level 5 below the lowest RSI threshold, 4 below the next threshold, 1 above the highest threshold, 2 above the next-highest threshold. EMA stack: using six close EMAs ordered from shortest to longest, level 1 if all are strictly descending, 2 if the shortest is above the second and the second is below the third, 3 if the first three are strictly ascending and the third is below the fourth, 4 if the first four are strictly ascending but the full stack is not, and 5 if all are strictly ascending. MACD: level 1 if MACD is negative and its negative histogram is falling over a short lookback; 2 if MACD is negative and the histogram is nonnegative or rising over that lookback; 3 if MACD crosses its signal upward at or below zero or downward at or above zero; 5 if MACD and histogram are positive and MACD is rising over a longer lookback and histogram over the short lookback; 4 if MACD and histogram are positive and MACD is above its signal or histogram is rising over the short lookback. ADX: level 5 if ADX is above the high threshold and +DI exceeds -DI; 4 if ADX is between the middle and high thresholds inclusive and +DI exceeds -DI; 3 if ADX is at least the low threshold and below the middle threshold and the absolute DI difference is at most the tolerance; 2 if ADX is in that same interval and -DI exceeds +DI; 1 if ADX is below the low threshold and -DI exceeds +DI. Ichimoku: use undisplaced conversion and base lines, the cloud spans displaced backward by the displacement, and current close versus close displacement bars ago; level 1 if conversion is below base, close is below the cloud, and close is below its displaced value; 2 if conversion is below base and close is inside the cloud or is not below its displaced value; 3 if conversion crosses base inside the cloud; 5 if conversion is above base, close is above the cloud and its displaced value, and the current undisplaced leading span A exceeds span B with their difference increasing; 4 if those same conditions hold except for the undisplaced span condition. Bollinger: use close against bands formed from the selected source, moving-average type, lookback and deviation multiplier; level 1 if close is below the lower band or equals it while the lower band or basis is falling over a lookback; 5 if close is above the upper band or equals it while the upper band or basis is rising over that lookback; 2 if close is at least the lower band and below the basis; 4 if close is above the basis and at most the upper band. OBV: level 1 if OBV is below its selected moving average and falling over the short fall lookback; 2 if below its average and rising over the short rise lookback or at least average times one minus the near tolerance; 3 if its absolute distance from its average is at most the average's absolute value times the near tolerance, or at most the tolerance when the average is zero; 4 if above its average and rising over the short rise lookback; 5 if above its average, rising over the longer rise lookback, and its change exceeds a multiplier times the moving average of its absolute changes.
Calculation: Composite = clamp((mean of the seven signal levels minus 1) divided by 4, 0, 1) multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Pressure = rolling sum of per-bar buy volume divided by rolling sum of total volume; per-bar buy volume is volume times (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), or half the volume when high equals low. Use neutral pressure when rolling volume is zero.
Calculation: Directional zones = on confirmation of a pivot low or high with equal left and right strengths, use the composite at the pivot bar to classify support as long when it meets the upper composite threshold and resistance as short when it meets the lower composite threshold; other pivots are mixed. Create eligible directional and optionally mixed zones unless an active same-side anchor is closer than the current ATR times the separation parameter. Before creation, if the active-zone count meets the maximum, delete the oldest stored zone. The anchor is the pivot price; zone height is confirmation-bar ATR times a height multiplier. Support extends from anchor minus height to anchor plus height times an edge fraction; resistance extends from anchor minus height times the edge fraction to anchor plus height. Process support creation before resistance. A support becomes inactive when close is below its lower edge times one minus the invalidation offset; resistance becomes inactive when close is above its upper edge times one plus the offset. Retained inactive zones are deleted oldest first when their count exceeds the history limit.
Long: Any signal level is at least 4.
Short: Any signal level is at most 2.
Long: Composite is at least the upper composite threshold.
Short: Composite is at most the lower composite threshold.
Long: Pressure is at least neutral pressure.
Short: Pressure is below neutral pressure.
Long: A newly confirmed support pivot creates a long directional zone.
Short: A newly confirmed resistance pivot creates a short directional zone.
Long: Low is at or below an active long directional zone's upper edge AND high is at or above its lower edge.
Short: Low is at or below an active short directional zone's upper edge AND high is at or above its lower edge.
