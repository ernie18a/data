Calculation: Confirmed pivot = at each chart-bar close, test the high or low pivot candidate right-lookback bars ago against the left-lookback older bars and the more recent bars excluding the current bar; a high must be strictly greater than every tested high, and a low strictly less than every tested low. Store confirmed highs and lows separately, newest first.
Calculation: Direction-normalized prices = multiply pivot prices and closes by direction d, where d is positive for long and negative for short; a favorable extreme is a high for long and a low for short, and an adverse extreme is the opposite.
Calculation: Common filters = optional direction-normalized current close > direction-normalized midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the market-structure lookback AND optional direction-normalized close > direction-normalized SMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Impulse candidate = take the latest three adverse pivots and latest three favorable pivots in alternating chronological order as p0 through p5, starting with an adverse pivot; use direction-normalized prices. Define L1=p1-p0, L3=p3-p2, L5=p5-p4, R2=(p1-p2)/L1, R4=(p3-p4)/L3, risk=abs(p5-(p4-ATR times stop buffer)), and reward=target multiplier times L3.
Calculation: Correction candidate = require at least two confirmed pivots of each type; take the second-newest adverse pivot, second-newest favorable pivot, and newest adverse pivot in chronological order as A, B, C, using direction-normalized prices. Define L=B-A, E=(B-C)/L, risk=ATR times stop buffer, and reward=correction target multiplier times L.
Calculation: Candidate selection = after the warm-up of twice the sum of the pivot lookbacks, select the first valid enabled candidate in this order: long impulse, short impulse, long correction, short correction. A correction is eligible only if its pivot interval does not overlap an impulse among the retained most recent patterns. On acceptance, remove retained corrections overlapping an impulse, append the accepted pattern, and discard oldest patterns beyond the retention limit. Accept the selected candidate only if its starting pivot index differs from that of the last accepted pattern by more than the cooldown; do not fall through to another candidate if it fails this test.
Long: last completed higher-timeframe close > its close SMA, when the higher-timeframe bias display is enabled.
Short: last completed higher-timeframe close <= its close SMA, when the higher-timeframe bias display is enabled.
