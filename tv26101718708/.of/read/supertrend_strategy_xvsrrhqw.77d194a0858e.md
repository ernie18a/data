Calculation: Supertrend state = initialize state to bullish; calculate true range and either Wilder's ATR or the simple moving average of true range over the same lookback. Set the raw lower band to the selected price source minus an ATR multiplier times ATR, and the raw upper band to that source plus the same multiplier times ATR. Set the lower band to the maximum of its raw value and its previous value when the previous close exceeds the previous lower band; otherwise use its raw value. Set the upper band to the minimum of its raw value and its previous value when the previous close is below the previous upper band; otherwise use its raw value. On initialization use the current raw band in place of a missing previous band. Change bearish state to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper band; change bullish state to bearish when close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Money flow = the rolling sum of money-flow volume divided by the rolling sum of volume, where money-flow volume is zero when high equals low and otherwise is volume times (2 times the selected price source minus low minus high) divided by (high minus low).
Long: datetime is within the inclusive backtest date range AND bar time is within the entry session AND (regular trading is enabled AND ((Supertrend is enabled AND Supertrend state is bullish AND lower band is nonzero AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled AND previous Supertrend state is bearish)) OR (Supertrend-disabled trading is enabled AND at least one filter is enabled AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled))) AND (no EMA filter is enabled OR EMA filter is enabled AND selected EMA price source > EMA of selected EMA source) AND (no money-flow filter is enabled OR money-flow filter is enabled AND money flow > 0) OR contrarian trading is enabled AND ((Supertrend is enabled AND Supertrend state is bearish AND upper band is nonzero AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled AND previous Supertrend state is bullish)) OR (Supertrend-disabled trading is enabled AND at least one filter is enabled AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled))) AND (no EMA filter is enabled OR EMA filter is enabled AND selected EMA price source < EMA of selected EMA source) AND (no money-flow filter is enabled OR money-flow filter is enabled AND money flow < 0)).
Short: datetime is within the inclusive backtest date range AND bar time is within the entry session AND (regular trading is enabled AND ((Supertrend is enabled AND Supertrend state is bearish AND upper band is nonzero AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled AND previous Supertrend state is bullish)) OR (Supertrend-disabled trading is enabled AND at least one filter is enabled AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled))) AND (no EMA filter is enabled OR EMA filter is enabled AND selected EMA price source < EMA of selected EMA source) AND (no money-flow filter is enabled OR money-flow filter is enabled AND money flow < 0) OR contrarian trading is enabled AND ((Supertrend is enabled AND Supertrend state is bullish AND lower band is nonzero AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled AND previous Supertrend state is bearish)) OR (Supertrend-disabled trading is enabled AND at least one filter is enabled AND (enter immediately is enabled OR wait for trend change is enabled))) AND (no EMA filter is enabled OR EMA filter is enabled AND selected EMA price source > EMA of selected EMA source) AND (no money-flow filter is enabled OR money-flow filter is enabled AND money flow > 0)).
Exit: long, short-entry condition is true OR session exit is enabled AND bar time is outside the exit session OR percentage stop-loss and take-profit orders have been enabled and placed during the inclusive backtest date range AND (low <= entry price times (1 minus stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price times (1 plus take-profit fraction)).
Exit: short, long-entry condition is true OR session exit is enabled AND bar time is outside the exit session OR percentage stop-loss and take-profit orders have been enabled and placed during the inclusive backtest date range AND (high >= entry price times (1 plus stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price times (1 minus take-profit fraction)).
