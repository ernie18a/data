Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low over a configurable equal number of bars on each side; use only after the bars on both sides have completed. Each level can trigger a break once, until replaced by a new pivot.
Calculation: Order-block zone = at or before a confirmed swing pivot, select the most recent opposite-colored candle within a short fixed lookback. Require its high-low range to meet a configurable ATR-multiple minimum and, if enabled, its volume to meet a configurable multiple of the volume moving average. Zone edges are either the candle body (open and close) or full range (high and low), per configuration.
Calculation: Structure break = confirmed bar close beyond the latest unbroken swing level, optionally by a configurable ATR multiple. A downward break flips pending swing-low order blocks; an upward break flips pending swing-high order blocks.
Calculation: Breaker quality score = rounded weighted mean of six 0–100 scores: break displacement in ATR units (linear, capped at 2 ATR); source-candle range in ATR units (linear, capped at 3 ATR); retest penetration ratio scored linearly from 100 at the configurable ideal ratio to 0 at the farther of 0 or 1; confirming-candle rejecting-wick fraction; confirmation speed (100 × [1 − elapsed retest bars / retest-window bars], floored at 0); and distance from zone midpoint to break close in ATR units (linear, capped at 5 ATR). Weights are configurable and normalized by their sum; if all are zero, use the unweighted mean.
Long: A qualifying bearish-origin order-block candidate at a confirmed swing high has an upward structure break before candidate expiry AND price subsequently retests the zone within the retest window (low ≤ zone top) AND maximum penetration (zone top − lowest low since retest began) / zone height ≥ the configurable minimum AND a confirmed close > zone top + configurable confirmation ATR multiple AND breaker quality score ≥ configurable threshold, if quality filtering is enabled. Discard if, during retesting, close < zone bottom − confirmation ATR multiple or the retest window expires.
Short: A qualifying bullish-origin order-block candidate at a confirmed swing low has a downward structure break before candidate expiry AND price subsequently retests the zone within the retest window (high ≥ zone bottom) AND maximum penetration (highest high since retest began − zone bottom) / zone height ≥ the configurable minimum AND a confirmed close < zone bottom − configurable confirmation ATR multiple AND breaker quality score ≥ configurable threshold, if quality filtering is enabled. Discard if, during retesting, close > zone top + confirmation ATR multiple or the retest window expires.
Exit: Long, close < the bottom of its active bullish breaker zone − configurable invalidation ATR multiple.
Exit: Short, close > the top of its active bearish breaker zone + configurable invalidation ATR multiple.
