Calculation: Structure breaks = Confirm a pivot high or low after the same number of bars on each side. Replace the latest corresponding pivot and reset its broken flag when a new pivot confirms. An upward break occurs when close > latest pivot high + structure displacement buffer and that pivot is unbroken; a downward break occurs when close < latest pivot low - structure displacement buffer and that pivot is unbroken. The buffer is ATR multiplied by a configurable multiplier when the filter is enabled, otherwise zero. Mark the pivot broken after its first break. Structure bias is the direction of the latest break, with downward taking precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Breaker candidates = At each confirmed pivot high, select the most recent bullish candle at or before the pivot within the source lookback; at each confirmed pivot low, select the most recent bearish candle similarly. Accept it only if its ATR > 0 AND its high-low range >= a multiple of its ATR AND, if the volume filter is enabled, its volume SMA > 0 AND its volume >= a multiple of its volume SMA. Do not search for another candle if the selected candle fails these filters. The zone is the source candle's body or full high-low range, according to configuration. Maintain separate candidate lists for pivot highs and lows, adding a candidate only if its list is below the candidate cap. Process candidates newest first. Pending candidates expire when bars since the source candle > the pending expiry; otherwise a pivot-high candidate flips on any upward structure break and a pivot-low candidate flips on any downward structure break. Record the break's absolute close-to-pivot distance divided by current ATR and the absolute close-to-zone-midpoint distance divided by current ATR, using zero for either if ATR <= 0. A flipped candidate expires when bars since its flip > the retest window; otherwise a pivot-high candidate starts retesting when low <= zone top and a pivot-low candidate starts retesting when high >= zone bottom. Track the lowest low or highest high respectively from retest start. Retesting expires when bars since retest start > the retest window. Discard a pivot-high candidate if close < zone bottom - confirmation ATR buffer and a pivot-low candidate if close > zone top + confirmation ATR buffer. Pending, flipped, and retesting transitions can all occur on the same bar. Remove a candidate after its qualifying confirmation, even if it fails the quality filter.
Calculation: Penetration = For a pivot-high candidate, (zone top - lowest retest low) / zone height; for a pivot-low candidate, (highest retest high - zone bottom) / zone height; use 1 if zone height <= 0.
Calculation: Breaker quality = Round the weighted mean of six scores, using their unweighted mean if all weights are zero. Clip each score to 0–100: break strength = 100 times recorded break displacement divided by its scoring cap; source size = 100 times source range / source ATR divided by its scoring cap; retest precision = 100 times (1 - abs(penetration - ideal penetration) / max(ideal penetration, 1 - ideal penetration)); wick rejection = 100 times the confirmation candle's lower wick / range for a pivot-high candidate or upper wick / range for a pivot-low candidate, using zero if range is zero; speed = 100 times (1 - min(bars since retest start / retest window, 1)); distance = 100 times recorded midpoint distance divided by its scoring cap.
