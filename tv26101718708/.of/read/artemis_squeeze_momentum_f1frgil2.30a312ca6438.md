Calculation: Momentum = linear-regression smoothing of (close − average of the lookback channel midpoint and lookback SMA of (high + low)/2) / channel true range × 100; channel midpoint = (highest high + lowest low)/2, and channel true range includes the gap from prior close.
Calculation: Signal = simple moving average of Momentum over a shorter lookback than Momentum’s.
Calculation: Directional Flux = 100 × RMA((RMA(max(change(high),0), lookback) − RMA(max(−change(low),0), lookback)) / (RMA(max(change(high),0), lookback) + RMA(max(−change(low),0), lookback)), half that lookback); use Heiken Ashi OHLC instead when that option is enabled.
Calculation: Regular divergence anchors = at a Momentum/Signal cross in an extreme zone, store the current high and Signal for bearish comparison or current low and Signal for bullish comparison; compare only to the latest same-side anchor and reset that anchor after a passing divergence.
Calculation: Hidden divergence anchors = at any Momentum/Signal cross, store the current high and Signal for bearish comparison or current low and Signal for bullish comparison; compare only to the latest same-side anchor and reset that anchor after a passing divergence.
Calculation: Squeeze release = previous bar had sample standard deviation of close below ATR times the loose compression threshold, and the current bar does not; ATR is RMA of true range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish if Momentum > 0, bearish if Momentum < 0, otherwise neutral; Momentum and Directional Flux are calculated on each of four higher timeframes using its last completed bar.
Long: Momentum crosses above Signal OR (Momentum crosses above Signal AND Momentum < −threshold AND Directional Flux < 0 AND (volume > its moving average when volume confirmation is enabled and volume data is available)) OR regular bullish divergence (at a Momentum cross above Signal with Momentum below the negative extreme threshold, current low < prior regular bullish anchor low, and current Signal > prior anchor Signal) OR hidden bullish divergence (at a Momentum cross above Signal, current low > prior hidden bullish anchor low, and current Signal < prior anchor Signal) OR squeeze release with Momentum change over the predictor lookback > the positive slope threshold OR Momentum > 0 OR all four higher-timeframe biases are bullish OR composite trend-strength score ≥ the bullish threshold; when the smart divergence filter is enabled, divergence also requires momentum swing ≥ its minimum and price swing ≥ its minimum, and bullish divergence requires Directional Flux > 0 when flux confirmation is enabled.
Short: Momentum crosses below Signal OR (Momentum crosses below Signal AND Momentum > threshold AND Directional Flux > 0 AND (volume > its moving average when volume confirmation is enabled and volume data is available)) OR regular bearish divergence (at a Momentum cross below Signal with Momentum above the positive extreme threshold, current high > prior regular bearish anchor high, and current Signal < prior anchor Signal) OR hidden bearish divergence (at a Momentum cross below Signal, current high < prior hidden bearish anchor high, and current Signal > prior anchor Signal) OR squeeze release with Momentum change over the predictor lookback < the negative slope threshold OR Momentum < 0 OR all four higher-timeframe biases are bearish OR composite trend-strength score ≤ the bearish threshold; when the smart divergence filter is enabled, divergence also requires momentum swing ≥ its minimum and price swing ≥ its minimum, and bearish divergence requires Directional Flux < 0 when flux confirmation is enabled.
Exit: Long, Momentum crosses below Signal.
Exit: Short, Momentum crosses above Signal.
