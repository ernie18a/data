Calculation: Opening range high and low = For each of three independently configurable window durations, reset at each new day; take the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within both the regular session and the window starting at the regular-session open. Lock the range on the first bar outside that window if at least one window bar exists; retain it until the next day.
