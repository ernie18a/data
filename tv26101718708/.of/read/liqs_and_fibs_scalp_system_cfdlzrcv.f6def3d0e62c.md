Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure bias = On the higher timeframe, track the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low; bias becomes bullish when the still-forming bar closes above the latest pivot high, bearish when it closes below the latest pivot low, and otherwise retains its previous direction.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe structure break = Break above the latest confirmed pivot high or below the latest confirmed pivot low; use close beyond the pivot when body-break mode is enabled, otherwise use high above or low below.
Calculation: CHoCH = An upward structure break when the prior structure direction was bearish, or a downward structure break when it was bullish.
Calculation: Fibonacci reaction zone = The price range between the 0.318 and 0.618 retracements of the price range from the lowest low to the highest high between the pivot that caused a CHoCH and the CHoCH bar; reverse the range direction for a downward CHoCH.
Calculation: Pinbar = Upper wick or lower wick exceeds both the candle body by the configured multiple and ATR by the configured fraction.
Long: Upward structure break OR bullish higher-timeframe low sweep (low below a tracked higher-timeframe low and close above it) with a bullish pinbar OR price range overlaps the latest valid bullish Fibonacci reaction zone AND higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish AND close is above VWAP.
Short: Downward structure break OR bearish higher-timeframe high sweep (high above a tracked higher-timeframe high and close below it) with a bearish pinbar OR price range overlaps the latest valid bearish Fibonacci reaction zone AND higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish AND close is below VWAP.
