Calculation: Chart pivots = highs and lows confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each kind, replacing the previous level and resetting its broken state when a new pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Structure break = the first bar after a chart pivot is confirmed on which close, or optionally high for an upward break or low for a downward break, crosses strictly beyond that pivot. Process upward breaks before downward breaks on the same bar. Structure direction starts neutral and becomes the direction of each break; a change of character is a break opposite the direction immediately before it.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure direction = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and low using equal left and right pivot lengths. A close above the stored high sets direction bullish and clears that high; a close below the stored low sets direction bearish and clears that low. Process the high break before the low break. Direction persists between breaks and starts neutral.
Calculation: Sweep levels = highs and lows of the most recent retained completed higher-timeframe bars, maintained separately. A high level is swept on the first chart bar with high above it; a low level is swept on the first chart bar with low below it. Each level remains swept until it is discarded when the retained-level limit is exceeded.
Calculation: Reversal = for a swept high level, the first bar on or after the sweep with close below the level; for a swept low level, the first bar on or after the sweep with close above the level.
Calculation: Bullish pinbar = min(open, close) minus low is greater than a parameter times abs(close minus open) AND greater than another parameter times ATR. Bearish pinbar = high minus max(open, close) is greater than a parameter times abs(close minus open) AND greater than another parameter times ATR.
Calculation: Fibonacci setup = on a change of character, take the highest high and lowest low from the broken pivot's bar through the break bar inclusive. For an upward change, A is the low and B is the high; for a downward change, A is the high and B is the low. If the pivot bar index is not greater than zero, use only the break bar's high and low. Each zone consists of the two prices A + (B - A) times its lower and upper ratio parameters. The entry ratios are between zero and one, stop ratios are below zero, and six successively farther target ratio pairs are above one. Retain the configured number of most recent setups. A setup is valid if the optional bias filter is disabled, or if at creation its direction matches higher-timeframe structure direction AND its close is at or above VWAP for bullish setups or at or below VWAP for bearish setups.
Long: A chart pivot high is confirmed.
Short: A chart pivot low is confirmed.
Long: An upward structure break occurs.
Short: A downward structure break occurs.
Long: Low is below an unswept retained higher-timeframe low.
Short: High is above an unswept retained higher-timeframe high.
Long: A swept higher-timeframe low has its first reversal AND the reversal bar is a bullish pinbar.
Short: A swept higher-timeframe high has its first reversal AND the reversal bar is a bearish pinbar.
Long: After the sweep bar, low is at or below a swept higher-timeframe low AND close is above that level.
Short: After the sweep bar, high is at or above a swept higher-timeframe high AND close is below that level.
Long: The latest retained Fibonacci setup is bullish AND valid AND the current bar overlaps its entry zone AND the previous bar did not overlap a valid latest bullish entry zone.
Short: The latest retained Fibonacci setup is bearish AND valid AND the current bar overlaps its entry zone AND the previous bar did not overlap a valid latest bearish entry zone.
Exit: long, price overlaps the stop zone or a visible target zone of a retained bullish Fibonacci setup.
Exit: short, price overlaps the stop zone or a visible target zone of a retained bearish Fibonacci setup.
Calculation: Fibonacci target visibility = the first two target zones are visible from setup creation; the third and fourth become visible when high reaches the near edge of target two for a bullish setup or low reaches it for a bearish setup; the fifth and sixth become visible when the corresponding price reaches the near edge of target four after target two has been reached.
