Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on each side; confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Upper/lower trendline = straight line through the two most recent confirmed swing highs/lows, projected to the current bar
Calculation: Wedge width = upper trendline − lower trendline
Calculation: Wedge height = upper trendline − lower trendline at the earlier of the older of the two swing highs and the older of the two swing lows
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close
Long: the two latest swing highs and the two latest swing lows are each lower than their preceding swings AND both trendline slopes are negative AND the upper trendline slope is less than the lower trendline slope multiplied by the convergence strictness parameter AND wedge height ≥ ATR × minimum-height parameter AND current wedge width > 0 AND current wedge width < width at the start AND (width at the start − current width) / width at the start ≥ minimum-contraction parameter AND the trendlines' intersection is after the current bar (or they are parallel) AND elapsed bars from the start ≥ pivot-length parameter AND elapsed bars from the start ≤ maximum-duration parameter AND close > current upper trendline AND previous close ≤ upper trendline projected to the previous bar AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average × volume multiplier) AND (breakout-size filter disabled OR |close − open| ≥ ATR × minimum-breakout-size parameter) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA)
Short: the two latest swing highs and the two latest swing lows are each higher than their preceding swings AND both trendline slopes are positive AND the lower trendline slope is greater than the upper trendline slope multiplied by the convergence strictness parameter AND wedge height ≥ ATR × minimum-height parameter AND current wedge width > 0 AND current wedge width < width at the start AND (width at the start − current width) / width at the start ≥ minimum-contraction parameter AND the trendlines' intersection is after the current bar (or they are parallel) AND elapsed bars from the start ≥ pivot-length parameter AND elapsed bars from the start ≤ maximum-duration parameter AND close < current lower trendline AND previous close ≥ lower trendline projected to the previous bar AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average × volume multiplier) AND (breakout-size filter disabled OR |close − open| ≥ ATR × minimum-breakout-size parameter) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA)
Exit: long, close ≤ lower trendline at the entry signal OR close ≥ upper trendline at the entry signal + wedge height
Exit: short, close ≥ upper trendline at the entry signal OR close ≤ lower trendline at the entry signal − wedge height
