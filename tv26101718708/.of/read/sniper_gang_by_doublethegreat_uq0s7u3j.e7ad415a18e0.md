Calculation: Risk distance = ATR × stop multiplier
Calculation: Long target levels = entry price + Risk distance multiplied by increasing target multiples
Calculation: Short target levels = entry price − Risk distance multiplied by increasing target multiples
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND current position side is not long
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND current position side is not short
Exit: Long, high reaches any long target level
Exit: Short, low reaches any short target level
