Calculation: Estimated buying/selling/neutral volume = Sum lower-timeframe bar volume classified by close vs. open (up = buying, down = selling); for dojis use close vs. the preceding lower-timeframe close, otherwise inherit the last resolved direction, initially neutral. Use lower-timeframe bars completed within the confirmed chart bar; require valid source data and no more than 3,000 source bars.
Calculation: Net volume share = (estimated buying volume − estimated selling volume) / total classified volume, including neutral volume.
Calculation: Relative volume = current total classified volume / mean total classified volume over the 30 preceding chart bars; require all 30 bars to have usable source data.
Calculation: Prior ATR = ATR over 14 chart bars, shifted back one completed chart bar.
Calculation: Directional body response = sign(net volume share) × (chart close − chart open) / prior ATR.
Calculation: Rolling flow mean = volume-weighted mean of lower-timeframe HLC3 over the configured chart-bar window; require usable source data for at least 80% of the window. Use the last completed chart-bar value.
Calculation: Rolling pressure = (buying volume − selling volume) / total classified volume, summed across the configured chart-bar window.

Short: Confirmed chart bar, usable data, relative volume ≥ raw activity threshold, |net volume share| ≥ raw imbalance threshold, prior ATR > 0, net volume share < 0, directional body response ≥ drive threshold, and the bar is at least the configured spacing after the previous DRIVE or RESISTED mark.

Calculation: Initial risk = prior ATR at the entry signal × configured risk multiple, rounded up to a whole tick with a minimum of two ticks. Entry price = next chart bar open; initial stop = entry price − initial risk for long or + initial risk for short; target = entry price + initial risk × target multiple for long or − initial risk × target multiple for short, rounded outward to a whole tick.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, tightened only. After favorable excursion reaches the configured break-even trigger in risk units, candidate stop is entry price plus/minus the configured risk buffer, rounded toward the entry. After favorable excursion reaches the configured trailing trigger, candidate stop is the favorable high/low extreme minus/plus configured trailing distance in risk units, rounded toward the position. Updates apply from the next bar.
Exit: long, valid OHLC and open ≤ active stop OR low ≤ active stop OR open ≥ target OR high ≥ target.
Exit: short, valid OHLC and open ≥ active stop OR high ≥ active stop OR open ≤ target OR low ≤ target.
Exit: long, qualified short DRIVE on the confirmed bar, if opposite-drive exits are enabled.
Exit: short, qualified long DRIVE on the confirmed bar, if opposite-drive exits are enabled.
Exit: long, rolling flow mean and rolling pressure are usable, close < rolling flow mean, and rolling pressure ≤ −aligned-pressure threshold for the configured consecutive number of chart-bar closes, if flow exits are enabled.
Exit: short, rolling flow mean and rolling pressure are usable, close > rolling flow mean, and rolling pressure ≥ aligned-pressure threshold for the configured consecutive number of chart-bar closes, if flow exits are enabled.
Exit: long, confirmed chart bar and holding duration ≥ maximum holding bars, counting the entry fill bar as bar one.
Exit: short, confirmed chart bar and holding duration ≥ maximum holding bars, counting the entry fill bar as bar one.
