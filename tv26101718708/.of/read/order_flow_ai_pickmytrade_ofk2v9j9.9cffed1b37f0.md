Calculation: Intrabar direction = On each completed lower-timeframe bar, use +1 if close > open and -1 if close < open; for a doji use the sign of close minus the preceding lower-timeframe close, or retain the preceding resolved direction if equal or unavailable; start at 0. Sum lower-timeframe volume by direction within each completed chart bar as buying, selling and neutral volume. A chart bar is usable if it has at least one and no more than the permitted number of lower-timeframe bars, every intrabar HLC3 and volume is valid, no intrabar volume is negative, and total volume is positive.
Calculation: Pressure share = (buying volume - selling volume) / total volume. Relative volume = current total volume / mean total volume over the immediately preceding volume-baseline lookback, provided every bar in that lookback is usable. Aligned response = sign(pressure share) × (close - open) / previous chart bar ATR.
Calculation: Rolling flow mean = volume-weighted mean of lower-timeframe HLC3 over the rolling flow window, using all completed lower-timeframe bars within usable chart bars. Rolling pressure share = sum of buying volume minus selling volume over that window divided by sum of total volume. Both require the minimum usable-bar fraction and positive total volume.
Calculation: Raw pressure event = current bar usable AND relative volume >= raw activity threshold AND absolute pressure share >= raw imbalance threshold AND previous chart bar ATR > 0 AND the number of chart bars since the last raw pressure event of either direction or response type >= shared spacing threshold. The first event has no spacing restriction. A drive has aligned response >= drive threshold; a resisted event has aligned response <= resisted threshold, which is lower than the drive threshold.
Calculation: Continuation vote = For each raw pressure event with a valid rolling flow mean and share and high > low, freeze its pressure direction, close, previous-bar ATR and seven features: absolute pressure share; relative volume / (1 + relative volume); (clip(aligned response, -2, 2) + 2) / 4; pressure-side wick / candle range; pressure-aligned close location within the candle range; (clip(direction × (close - rolling flow mean) / previous-bar ATR, -3, 3) + 3) / 6; and clip((direction × rolling pressure share + 1) / 2, 0, 1). The pressure-side wick is high - max(open, close) for buying and min(open, close) - low for selling; aligned close location is (close - low) / range for buying and (high - close) / range for selling. Starting on the next chart bar, label the case continuation if the pressure-side barrier at a configured multiple of frozen ATR from frozen close is touched first, or opposition if the opposite barrier is touched first. Discard invalid-price cases, same-bar double touches and cases with no touch by the outcome deadline; a touch on the deadline counts. Before scoring each new event, resolve all earlier cases using the current bar and retain the latest configured number of resolved cases. Require at least the greater of the warm-up count and neighbour count and require both outcome classes. Select the configured number of nearest cases by mean log(1 + absolute feature difference), breaking equal-distance ties in favour of newer cases; require the farthest selected distance <= the distance ceiling. Give each neighbour weight 1 / max(distance floor, distance), calculate its weighted continuation fraction and effective count as squared sum of weights / sum of squared weights, and calculate the smoothed library base rate as (continuations + 1) / (library size + 2). The vote is base rate + (weighted continuation fraction - base rate) × effective count / (effective count + shrinkage). Otherwise the vote is unavailable.
Calculation: Qualified long drive = raw pressure event AND drive AND pressure share > 0 AND relative volume >= managed activity threshold AND absolute pressure share >= managed imbalance threshold AND rolling pressure share >= aligned-flow threshold AND close > rolling flow mean AND rolling flow mean > its value at the slope lookback AND absolute(close - rolling flow mean) / previous-bar ATR <= stretch threshold AND, if the optional vote gate is enabled, continuation vote >= vote threshold. Qualified short drive reverses the pressure, rolling-share, close-versus-mean and mean-slope comparisons. The managed activity and imbalance thresholds are additional to the raw thresholds.
Calculation: Managed state = Initially flat with both sides armed. An accepted managed entry schedules that side's position at the next chart bar open and freezes initial risk as the signal bar's previous-bar ATR × risk multiplier. Maintain only one open or pending position. Disarm the entered side; rearm it only after the configured number of consecutive usable flat bars with no pending entry and without that side's unspaced raw drive regime, and after the exit cooldown has elapsed. An unspaced raw drive regime satisfies the raw activity, imbalance, positive prior ATR and drive comparisons with the corresponding pressure sign, without the shared spacing test. Open, pending or unusable bars reset the quiet count; an exit bar cannot rearm or enter. A new managed entry also requires valid chart OHLC, the configured start time reached, the side enabled, no exit on the current bar, and the exit cooldown elapsed. The scheduled fill requires valid OHLC on the immediately following bar; otherwise emit an exit for the pending side.
Calculation: Protective levels = For an open position, initialize stop at entry price minus side × initial risk and target at entry price plus side × initial risk × target multiplier, where side is +1 for long and -1 for short. Track the most favourable high for long or low for short from the fill bar. After checking the levels active at the start of each valid bar, optionally tighten the stop to entry price plus side × initial risk × break-even buffer when favourable excursion reaches the break-even activation multiple of initial risk; optionally tighten it further to the favourable extreme minus side × initial risk × trailing-distance multiplier when excursion reaches the trailing activation multiple. Stop changes apply from the next bar. If both stop and target are touched, treat the stop as first.
Long: Raw pressure event AND drive AND pressure share > 0.
Long: Raw pressure event AND resisted AND pressure share < 0.
Long: Qualified long drive AND managed state permits a long entry.
Short: Raw pressure event AND drive AND pressure share < 0.
Short: Raw pressure event AND resisted AND pressure share > 0.
Short: Qualified short drive AND managed state permits a short entry.
Exit: long, pending long fill bar OHLC is invalid OR low <= active stop OR high >= active target OR optional opposite-drive exit is enabled AND qualified short drive OR optional flow exit is enabled AND close < rolling flow mean AND rolling pressure share <= negative aligned-flow threshold for the configured number of consecutive usable chart bars OR holding bars >= maximum holding period.
Exit: short, pending short fill bar OHLC is invalid OR high >= active stop OR low <= active target OR optional opposite-drive exit is enabled AND qualified long drive OR optional flow exit is enabled AND close > rolling flow mean AND rolling pressure share >= aligned-flow threshold for the configured number of consecutive usable chart bars OR holding bars >= maximum holding period.
