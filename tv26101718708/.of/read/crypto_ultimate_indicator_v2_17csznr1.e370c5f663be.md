Calculation: Primary average = a selectable moving average of close; its bearish state includes an unchanged average.
Calculation: Ribbon score = for five close EMAs ordered from shortest to longest, add +1 for each shorter EMA greater than the next longer EMA and -1 otherwise; also add +1 if close is greater than the shortest EMA and -1 otherwise.
Calculation: Volume delta = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low; cumulative volume delta is its running sum.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a value that is a local high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; emit its decision on the confirmation bar. Price, RSI, and cumulative-volume-delta pivots are tracked independently.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe score = weighted sum of three higher-timeframe direction scores, using the last completed bar of each higher timeframe. For the first two timeframes, each score is the sign of the sum of: +1 if Hull MA rose from the preceding completed bar, otherwise -1; +1 for bullish Supertrend, otherwise -1; and +1, -1, or zero when RSI is respectively above an upper threshold, below a lower threshold, or between them. The third score uses only Hull MA and RSI. The second timeframe is longer than the first; its weight is greater than the first weight, which is greater than the third weight. Hull MA, Supertrend, and RSI use the same respective parameters as their chart-timeframe counterparts.
Calculation: Bollinger width percentile = (current Bollinger Band width − lowest width over a lookback) / (highest width over that lookback − lowest width); protect a zero denominator with a small positive value. A squeeze release occurs when the prior percentile is below its threshold and the current percentile is at or above it.
Calculation: Regime score = weighted sum of capped scaled ADX, max(0, 100 − Choppiness Index), and scaled Bollinger width percentile. Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true ranges over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback), with a small positive denominator floor. The ADX and choppiness weights are equal and greater than the width weight. Trending and ranging are respectively above an upper and below a lower regime threshold.
Calculation: Directional confidence = sum of points for absolute higher-timeframe score, regime category, absolute ribbon score, absolute RSI distance from its midpoint, absolute volume delta relative to its moving average, squeeze release, same-direction cumulative-volume-delta divergence, and optionally same-direction liquidity sweep. Higher absolute higher-timeframe score gives proportionally more points up to a cap; trending scores more than intermediate regime and ranging scores zero; stronger ribbon and larger RSI distance score more; elevated relative absolute volume delta scores more than other volume; squeeze release, divergence, and sweep each add fixed points.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = bullish intervals from high two bars ago to current low when current low is greater; bearish intervals from current high to low two bars ago when current high is lower. Keep a configurable maximum number of the most recent gaps per direction; optionally remove a bullish gap when low reaches its lower bound and a bearish gap when high reaches its upper bound.
Calculation: Pivot-cluster levels = process confirmed high and low pivots in order; merge each into the first existing level within a configurable multiple of current ATR, replacing its price with the touch-count-weighted mean and incrementing its touch count; otherwise create a level with one touch. When the level capacity is exceeded, remove the least-touched level, breaking ties by oldest update.
Calculation: Volume-profile levels = divide the lookback high–low range into equal price bins and distribute each bar's volume equally across the bins touched by its range. Point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, resolving ties toward the lower bin. Starting there, add the larger-volume adjacent bin until the target volume fraction is reached, choosing the upper bin on ties; the outer bin edges are the value-area high and low.
Calculation: Entry filter = the directional confidence is at least its threshold AND, if higher-timeframe suppression is enabled, the higher-timeframe score is at least its positive threshold for long or at most its negative threshold for short AND, if the gap filter is enabled, close is outside every active opposing fair value gap AND, if the strict regime filter is enabled, regime score is not below its ranging threshold simultaneously with ADX below its threshold and Choppiness Index above its threshold AND, if the body filter is enabled, high is greater than low and absolute candle body divided by range is above its threshold AND the number of bars since either last confirmed entry is at least the spacing threshold AND, if pivot-cluster filtering is enabled, no level with sufficient touches lies between close and the directional ATR-distance limit inclusive AND, if volume-profile filtering is enabled, no point of control or value-area edge lies between close and the directional ATR-distance limit inclusive. The gap filter has effect only when gaps are being tracked.
Calculation: Entry confirmation = the complete directional entry condition including the entry filter holds on the current bar and, if confirmation is enabled, also held on the preceding bar; reset both directional confirmation counts and the last-entry bar after either confirmed entry. Initialize the last-entry bar to the first bar.
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR at the entry bar; initial stop distance = entry ATR multiplied by a selectable fixed multiplier or by a regime-dependent multiplier selected at entry. Each profit target equals entry price plus or minus entry ATR times its target multiplier; target multipliers increase from first to third.
Calculation: Effective stop = initial stop until the first target is reached; thereafter entry price if trailing is disabled, otherwise the more protective of entry price and the trailing stop. Initialize the trailing stop to the initial stop; after the first target, update it on every bar to the more protective of its previous value and high minus current ATR times a multiplier for long, or low plus current ATR times that multiplier for short. Check targets first, update the trailing stop second, and check the effective stop last, including on the entry bar.
Long: Primary average > its previous value.
Short: Primary average <= its previous value.
Long: Supertrend is bullish.
Short: Supertrend is bearish.
Long: Ribbon score >= its bullish threshold.
Short: Ribbon score <= its bearish threshold.
Long: RSI < its oversold threshold.
Short: RSI > its overbought threshold.
Long: Volume delta > zero.
Short: Volume delta <= zero.
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed AND the latest price pivot low < the previous price pivot low AND the latest independently confirmed RSI pivot low > the previous RSI pivot low.
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed AND the latest price pivot high > the previous price pivot high AND the latest independently confirmed RSI pivot high < the previous RSI pivot high.
Long: A cumulative-volume-delta pivot low is confirmed AND the latest independently confirmed price pivot low < its previous pivot low AND the latest cumulative-volume-delta pivot low > its previous pivot low.
Short: A cumulative-volume-delta pivot high is confirmed AND the latest independently confirmed price pivot high > its previous pivot high AND the latest cumulative-volume-delta pivot high < its previous pivot high.
Long: Low < the lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback AND close > that lowest low.
Short: High > the highest high over the preceding sweep lookback AND close < that highest high.
Long: Low > high two bars ago.
Short: High < low two bars ago.
Long: Higher-timeframe score >= its bullish threshold.
Short: Higher-timeframe score <= its bearish threshold.
Long: Entry confirmation holds for primary average > previous primary average AND RSI > midpoint AND volume delta > zero AND ribbon score >= its bullish threshold AND bullish Supertrend AND absolute distance from close to the lookback highest high plus an ATR multiple > a separate ATR-distance threshold AND the entry filter.
Short: Entry confirmation holds for primary average <= previous primary average AND RSI < midpoint AND volume delta < zero AND ribbon score <= its bearish threshold AND bearish Supertrend AND absolute distance from close to the lookback lowest low minus an ATR multiple > a separate ATR-distance threshold AND the entry filter.
Exit: long, high >= an unhit profit target; exit the corresponding configurable position fraction.
Exit: short, low <= an unhit profit target; exit the corresponding configurable position fraction.
Exit: long, low <= effective stop.
Exit: short, high >= effective stop.
