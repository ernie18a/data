Calculation: CRT candles = for each configured higher-than-chart source timeframe, C1 is the completed bar before C2, C2 is the last completed bar, and C3 is the bar still forming. A bullish CRT has C2.low < C1.low AND C1.low <= C2.close <= C1.high AND (dual sweeps are allowed OR C2.high <= C1.high). A bearish CRT has C2.high > C1.high AND C1.low <= C2.close <= C1.high AND (dual sweeps are allowed OR C2.low >= C1.low).
Calculation: Trend permission = manual bullish, manual bearish, both directions, or automatic. Automatic structure uses confirmed pivot highs and lows on a higher-than-chart timeframe: bullish when the latest two pivot highs and latest two pivot lows both rise, bearish when both fall, otherwise neutral. Pivot confirmation requires the configured number of bars on each side; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in realtime. An optional override uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar: a bullish rejection has low below the latest confirmed pivot low, close above it and above open, lower wick >= body times a threshold, and (close - low) / range >= a threshold; bearish rejection reverses these comparisons using the pivot high and upper wick. A rejection remains eligible for a configured number of higher-timeframe bars; the more recent eligible direction overrides structure, while equally recent opposite rejections give no override. Floor range and absolute body at a minimum price increment.
Calculation: Selected setup = at the start of a source C3 or the configured session, select the first trend-permitted CRT during the entry window, provided its developing C3 has not reached the opposite C1 extreme and the chart close has not crossed beyond the swept C2 extreme. If multiple candidates arise together, select the smallest C1 range, treating ranges within a minimum price increment as tied and preferring the shorter source timeframe; if both directions qualify for that source, select bullish. Keep it until its C3 ends, trend permission is lost, chart close crosses beyond the swept C2 extreme, or chart high reaches C1.high for bullish or chart low reaches C1.low for bearish. Clear selection at the start of each configured session, or each chart day when session filtering is disabled. Optionally prevent selection and entry after an entry in the same session or chart day.
Calculation: Price-action quality = over a configured chart-bar lookback, efficiency is absolute net close change divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes, or 1 if that sum is no greater than a minimum price increment; alternation ratio is the number of adjacent pairs of non-doji candles with opposite directions divided by the number of available pairs; two-sided-wick ratio is the number of sampled candles whose upper and lower wicks each reach a configured fraction of their range divided by the number of samples. Floor each range at a minimum price increment. Severe chop means efficiency <= its severe threshold AND alternation ratio >= its severe threshold AND two-sided-wick ratio >= its severe threshold. Choppy means severe chop OR (efficiency < its clean threshold AND (alternation ratio >= its chop threshold OR two-sided-wick ratio >= its chop threshold)); clean means not choppy.
Calculation: Directional structure break = bullish when chart close > the highest high of a configured number of preceding chart bars, bearish when close < the lowest low of those bars. Optionally require displacement on the break bar: range >= chart ATR times a threshold AND absolute body / range >= a threshold AND close > open for bullish or close < open for bearish; floor range at a minimum price increment.
Calculation: C2 break = bullish when chart high crosses above C2.high, or chart close crosses above it if close-break mode is selected; bearish when chart low crosses below C2.low, or close crosses below it if close-break mode is selected. A cross requires the preceding chart bar's corresponding value to be on or beyond the other side of the same level.
Calculation: Entry permission = the selected setup remains valid AND the chart bar is within the optional entry session AND its intraday chart timeframe is permitted AND the optional one-entry limit is not exhausted AND (severe-chop blocking is disabled OR price action is not severe chop) AND (the optional midpoint filter is disabled OR bullish close <= (C1.high + C1.low) / 2, or bearish close >= that midpoint). Optionally require the chart bar to be completed.
Calculation: FVG entry condition = after a C2 break during the entry window on a permitted chart timeframe, find the first directional chart-bar gap within a configured number of bars, including the break bar: bullish when current low > high two bars ago and the gap >= chart ATR times a threshold; bearish when current high < low two bars ago and the gap >= chart ATR times a threshold. Store the gap boundaries. On a later bar within a configured retest window, bullish requires low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND close >= gap bottom; bearish requires high >= gap bottom AND low <= gap top AND close <= gap top. Optionally also require close > open for bullish or close < open for bearish. Disarm the gap if a completed bar closes below its bottom for bullish or above its top for bearish, or if the retest window expires; while the original break remains within its search window, another qualifying gap may arm. Reset all stored entry-model state when the selected setup changes.
Calculation: Structure-retest condition = after the first directional structure break during the entry window on a permitted chart timeframe, store the broken preceding-high or preceding-low level. On a later chart bar within a configured retest window, bullish requires low <= stored level + current chart ATR times a tolerance AND close >= stored level AND close > open; bearish requires high >= stored level - current chart ATR times that tolerance AND close <= stored level AND close < open. After expiry, clear the stored break and allow a new break.
Calculation: Adaptive-retest condition = after a C2 break while price action is choppy but not severe, during the entry window on a permitted chart timeframe, store the C2 trigger. On a later chart bar within the configured retest window, apply the same directional retest comparisons as the structure-retest condition to that trigger. After expiry, clear the stored break and allow a new one.
Calculation: Turtle-soup shift condition = during the entry window on a permitted chart timeframe, record each bullish setup bar whose close < the lowest low of a configured number of preceding chart bars, or each bearish setup bar whose close > the highest high of those bars. Require a directional structure break on a later bar within a configured maximum number of bars after the most recent such record.
Long: bullish CRT = true.
Short: bearish CRT = true.
Long: selected setup direction = bullish AND entry permission = true AND ((entry model = FVG AND FVG entry condition = true) OR (entry model = adaptive AND ((price action = clean AND C2 break = bullish) OR adaptive-retest condition = true)) OR (entry model = exact C2 break AND C2 break = bullish) OR (entry model = aggressive structure break AND directional structure break = bullish) OR (entry model = turtle-soup shift AND turtle-soup shift condition = true) OR (entry model = structure retest AND structure-retest condition = true)).
Short: selected setup direction = bearish AND entry permission = true AND ((entry model = FVG AND FVG entry condition = true) OR (entry model = adaptive AND ((price action = clean AND C2 break = bearish) OR adaptive-retest condition = true)) OR (entry model = exact C2 break AND C2 break = bearish) OR (entry model = aggressive structure break AND directional structure break = bearish) OR (entry model = turtle-soup shift AND turtle-soup shift condition = true) OR (entry model = structure retest AND structure-retest condition = true)).
