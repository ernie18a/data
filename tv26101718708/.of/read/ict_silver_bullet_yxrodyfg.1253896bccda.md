Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when the still-forming higher-timeframe close is above both the previous higher-timeframe close and its own open; bearish when below both.
Calculation: Liquidity swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using a longer lookback.
Calculation: Local swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using a shorter lookback than the liquidity swings.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = the low two bars after a bar is above that bar’s high / the high two bars after a bar is below that bar’s low.
Long: Within an enabled session window, a liquidity swing low is swept (low < swing low) and reclaimed (close > swing low), the directional bias filter allows bullish trades, and price breaks above the latest eligible local swing high formed near the sweep (close > swing high when confirmation is by close, otherwise high > swing high); if FVG confluence is required, a bullish fair value gap exists; enter on that break bar’s close in MSS-close mode, or when a later bar reaches the gap (low <= gap top) in FVG-retest mode.
Short: Within an enabled session window, a liquidity swing high is swept (high > swing high) and rejected (close < swing high), the directional bias filter allows bearish trades, and price breaks below the latest eligible local swing low formed near the sweep (close < swing low when confirmation is by close, otherwise low < swing low); if FVG confluence is required, a bearish fair value gap exists; enter on that break bar’s close in MSS-close mode, or when a later bar reaches the gap (high >= gap bottom) in FVG-retest mode.
Exit: long, low <= sweep extreme OR high >= entry price + configured reward-to-risk multiple × abs(entry price − sweep extreme)
Exit: short, high >= sweep extreme OR low <= entry price − configured reward-to-risk multiple × abs(entry price − sweep extreme)
