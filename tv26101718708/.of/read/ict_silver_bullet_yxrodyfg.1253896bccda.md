Calculation: Liquidity pivots = confirmed chart-bar pivot highs and lows using the same left and right lookback; retain the most recent limited number of each. Mark a pivot taken on its first subsequent strict high or low breach, even if no setup is created.
Calculation: Setup = on an enabled New York-time session bar, create a bullish setup when low breaches an untaken liquidity pivot low and close is above it, or a bearish setup when high breaches an untaken liquidity pivot high and close is below it. The selected direction filter must allow the setup. Process highs before lows and newest pivots first. Reject a setup if any non-invalidated retained setup has a sweep level within a configurable fraction of the recent high-low range. Retain a limited number of setups, deleting the oldest when the limit is exceeded. Store the pivot price as sweep level and the sweep bar's low or high as sweep extreme.
Calculation: Market structure pivot = the most recent confirmed chart-bar pivot high for a bullish setup or pivot low for a bearish setup, with independently configurable left and right lookbacks, whose pivot bar precedes the current bar and is no earlier than a configurable number of bars before the sweep. Retain only a limited number of each pivot type.
Calculation: Fair value gap = the most recent gap within a configurable chart-bar lookback: bullish when a bar's low is above the high two bars earlier, with those prices as upper and lower boundaries; bearish when a bar's high is below the low two bars earlier, with those prices as lower and upper boundaries.
Calculation: Structure confirmation = a retained setup whose pre-confirmation bars have not breached its sweep extreme and whose age does not exceed the confirmation window, AND the current close or high is above its market structure pivot for a bullish setup, or the current close or low is below its market structure pivot for a bearish setup, AND a same-direction fair value gap exists if gap confluence is required. The sweep bar itself can confirm. Store the most recent gap, if present, and the sweep extreme as the stop. A confirmed setup without a gap cannot produce a retest entry.
Calculation: Retest entry price = the lesser of the bullish gap's upper boundary and the entry bar's close, or the greater of the bearish gap's lower boundary and the entry bar's close.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus the configured risk-reward multiplier times the absolute difference between entry price and stop for a long, or entry price minus that amount for a short. Check trade outcomes starting on the bar after entry; when stop and target are both reached on one bar, the stop takes priority.
Long: A bullish setup is created.
Short: A bearish setup is created.
Long: Bullish structure confirmation occurs.
Short: Bearish structure confirmation occurs.
Long: Bullish structure confirmation occurs AND entry mode is structure-close.
Short: Bearish structure confirmation occurs AND entry mode is structure-close.
Long: Entry mode is gap-retest AND a retained bullish setup has confirmed structure with a gap on an earlier bar AND low <= the stored gap's upper boundary AND the setup has not previously entered.
Short: Entry mode is gap-retest AND a retained bearish setup has confirmed structure with a gap on an earlier bar AND high >= the stored gap's lower boundary AND the setup has not previously entered.
Exit: long, low <= the setup's sweep extreme OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= the setup's sweep extreme OR low <= target.
