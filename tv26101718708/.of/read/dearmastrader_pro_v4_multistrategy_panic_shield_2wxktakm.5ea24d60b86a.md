Calculation: Source = a configurable price series derived from the backtest data; the default is close.
Calculation: Zero-lag EMA = 2 × EMA(source) − EMA(EMA(source)), using the same length for both EMAs.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = source − ATR × trailing multiplier on the first bar with a long position after a flat bar; thereafter, the maximum of its previous value and source − ATR × trailing multiplier. Short trailing stop = source + ATR × trailing multiplier on the first bar with a short position after a flat bar; thereafter, the minimum of its previous value and source + ATR × trailing multiplier. Reset when flat; retain the previous stop across a direct position reversal.
Calculation: A confirmed RSI pivot low or high is an RSI low or high with configurable numbers of bars to its left and right; identify it on the bar that completes the right side. Its associated source value is the source at the pivot bar.
Long: Direction permits long AND panic is off AND (trend filter is off OR source > trend EMA) AND (volume filter is off OR volume ≥ EMA(volume) × volume multiplier) AND ((zero-lag entry selected AND source crosses above zero-lag EMA + ATR × band multiplier AND zero-lag EMA > its previous value) OR (Bollinger entry selected AND source crosses above upper Bollinger Band) OR (RSI divergence entry selected AND a confirmed RSI pivot low > the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND its associated source < the preceding pivot's associated source) OR (MACD entry selected AND MACD line crosses above its signal line) OR (DCA entry selected AND source > macro EMA AND source crosses above zero-lag EMA AND RSI < overbought threshold)).
Short: Direction permits short AND panic is off AND (trend filter is off OR source < trend EMA) AND (volume filter is off OR volume ≥ EMA(volume) × volume multiplier) AND ((zero-lag entry selected AND source crosses below zero-lag EMA − ATR × band multiplier AND zero-lag EMA < its previous value) OR (Bollinger entry selected AND source crosses below lower Bollinger Band) OR (RSI divergence entry selected AND a confirmed RSI pivot high < the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high AND its associated source > the preceding pivot's associated source) OR (MACD entry selected AND MACD line crosses below its signal line) OR (DCA entry selected AND source < macro EMA AND source crosses below zero-lag EMA AND RSI > oversold threshold)).
Exit: long, panic is on OR (zero-lag exit selected AND source crosses below zero-lag EMA) OR (Bollinger exit selected AND trailing is enabled AND source crosses below long trailing stop) OR (RSI exit selected AND RSI crosses above overbought exit threshold) OR (MACD exit selected AND MACD line crosses below its signal line) OR (DCA exit selected AND (source crosses below the rolling lowest low including the current bar OR source crosses below macro EMA)).
Exit: short, panic is on OR (zero-lag exit selected AND source crosses above zero-lag EMA) OR (Bollinger exit selected AND trailing is enabled AND source crosses above short trailing stop) OR (RSI exit selected AND RSI crosses below oversold exit threshold) OR (MACD exit selected AND MACD line crosses above its signal line) OR (DCA exit selected AND (source crosses above the rolling highest high including the current bar OR source crosses above macro EMA)).
Exit: long, stop enabled AND low ≤ long stop OR target enabled AND high ≥ long target. The stop is the close of the latest long entry signal minus its ATR × stop multiplier, or that close × (1 − stop percentage); the target is that close plus its ATR × target multiplier, or that close × (1 + target percentage).
Exit: short, stop enabled AND high ≥ short stop OR target enabled AND low ≤ short target. The stop is the close of the latest short entry signal plus its ATR × stop multiplier, or that close × (1 + stop percentage); the target is that close minus its ATR × target multiplier, or that close × (1 − target percentage).
