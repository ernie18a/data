Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close.
Calculation: HTF RSI = RSI computed on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar (no lookahead).
Calculation: CooldownOk = bars elapsed since the most recent Long or Short signal of either side >= cooldown length (every emitted Long or Short signal resets the counter; if both fire on the same bar, both are emitted).
Calculation: Bullish divergence = evaluated on the bar that confirms an RSI pivot low (RSI lower than the L bars before and the L bars after it, so confirmed L bars later). The pivot low RSI is below a threshold between the oversold level and 50. The pivot bar's low is lower than the low stored at the previous RSI pivot low, AND the pivot RSI is higher than the previous pivot's RSI. The previous pivot values must exist. Every confirmed pivot low overwrites the stored previous values, whether or not it signals.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = mirror image: confirmed RSI pivot high (L bars each side), pivot RSI above a threshold between 50 and the overbought level, pivot bar's high > previous pivot-high price, AND pivot RSI < previous pivot-high RSI.
Calculation: Bullish failure swing = while RSI <= oversold, track L0 = RSI of the latest such bar (reset state each such bar). P = RSI of the first bar after the latest oversold bar (RSI > L0). Signal on the first later bar where RSI > P. Fires once per oversold episode.
Calculation: Bearish failure swing = while RSI >= overbought, track H0 = RSI of the latest such bar (reset state each such bar). Q = RSI of the first later bar with RSI < H0. Signal on the first later bar where RSI < Q. Fires once per overbought episode.
Long: CooldownOk AND (HTF RSI > midline 50, only if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled) AND (A OR B OR C OR D), where A = RSI <= oversold AND RSI has risen for k consecutive bars AND RSI > RSI[1] AND RSI > RSI[2] AND (volume > SMA of volume, only if the volume filter is enabled) AND (close > EMA of close, only if the trend filter is enabled); B = RSI crosses above oversold (RSI[1] <= oversold AND RSI > oversold) AND volume and trend filters are both disabled; C = Bullish divergence; D = Bullish failure swing
Short: CooldownOk AND (HTF RSI < midline 50, only if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled) AND (A OR B OR C OR D), where A = RSI >= overbought AND RSI has fallen for k consecutive bars AND RSI < RSI[1] AND RSI < RSI[2] AND (volume > SMA of volume, only if the volume filter is enabled) AND (close < EMA of close, only if the trend filter is enabled); B = RSI crosses below overbought (RSI[1] >= overbought AND RSI < overbought) AND volume and trend filters are both disabled; C = Bearish divergence; D = Bearish failure swing
