Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of selected bar price multiplied by volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each selected session, week, or month boundary.
Calculation: ATR = average true range, using true range until ATR is available and flooring the result at the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Pullback age for each direction = bars since low was at or below anchored VWAP minus ATR times pocket depth for bullish direction, or high was at or above anchored VWAP plus ATR times pocket depth for bearish direction; include the current bar.
Calculation: Direction for scoring = reclaim direction when a reclaim occurs; otherwise bullish if close is at or above VWAP and bearish if below.
Calculation: Reclaim score = clamp to its score range the sum of weighted components for directional close distance from VWAP in ATR units, pullback freshness, directional wick fraction, absolute body fraction, relative volume, directional VWAP slope alignment, and absolute close distance from VWAP in ATR units. Each continuous component is divided by its own normalization parameter and clamped between zero and one before weighting. Freshness is one minus pullback age divided by the pullback lookback, clamped between zero and one, or zero if no pullback exists. Wick and body fractions use high minus low floored at the minimum price increment. Relative volume is volume divided by its simple moving average, or one when that average is not positive; its contribution can instead be a fixed score. Slope alignment compares current VWAP with VWAP a specified number of bars earlier, using zero slope when unavailable, and uses separate aligned and unaligned weights.
Calculation: Pullback quality = the sum of the freshness, directional wick, and directional close-distance score components, scaled to the score range and clamped to that range.
Calculation: Reclaim state = on every ready reclaim, replace the active direction, bar, VWAP and ATR with those of that reclaim and reset both target-reached flags. The state remains active through the configured window, inclusive, until invalidation; a new ready reclaim replaces it. Each target is the stored VWAP plus or minus its own ATR multiple in the active direction. Mark a target reached when close reaches or exceeds it in the active direction; reaching the second target also marks the first reached. Evaluate invalidation before updating target flags on each bar.
Long: Cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND volume >= minimum volume AND bullish pullback age <= pullback lookback AND close > VWAP + ATR times reclaim buffer AND previous close <= current VWAP AND reclaim score >= ready threshold.
Short: Cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND volume >= minimum volume AND bearish pullback age <= pullback lookback AND close < VWAP - ATR times reclaim buffer AND previous close >= current VWAP AND reclaim score >= ready threshold.
Long: Cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND low <= VWAP - ATR times pocket depth AND pullback quality >= watch threshold AND no ready reclaim on the current bar.
Short: Cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND high >= VWAP + ATR times pocket depth AND low > VWAP - ATR times pocket depth AND pullback quality >= watch threshold AND no ready reclaim on the current bar.
Long: Active bullish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND second target was not previously reached AND close >= stored VWAP - stored ATR times invalidation multiplier AND first target has not been reached.
Short: Active bearish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND second target was not previously reached AND close <= stored VWAP + stored ATR times invalidation multiplier AND first target has not been reached.
Long: No active reclaim state within its window AND cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND either pullback pocket is breached AND pullback quality >= watch threshold AND close >= VWAP.
Short: No active reclaim state within its window AND cumulative anchored volume > 0 AND either pullback pocket is breached AND pullback quality >= watch threshold AND close < VWAP.
Exit: long, active bullish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND second target was not previously reached AND close < stored VWAP - stored ATR times invalidation multiplier.
Exit: short, active bearish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND second target was not previously reached AND close > stored VWAP + stored ATR times invalidation multiplier.
Exit: long, active bullish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND close >= either unreached target.
Exit: short, active bearish reclaim state AND current bar is within its window AND close <= either unreached target.
