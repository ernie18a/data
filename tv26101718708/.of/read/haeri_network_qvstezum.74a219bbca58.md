Calculation: A crossover of A above B means A > B on the current bar AND A <= B on the preceding bar; a crossunder reverses the inequalities. Strict crossing means the preceding inequality is also strict.
Calculation: Oscillator divergence = on the bar confirming an oscillator pivot, compare that pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. Require the number of bars since the preceding confirmation, measured from the previous bar, to lie within an inclusive minimum and maximum. Bullish divergence has a higher oscillator pivot low AND a lower price low at the pivot bars; bearish divergence has a lower oscillator pivot high AND a higher price high at the pivot bars. Pivot left and right strengths are parameters.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA((typical price - EMA(typical price)) / (scale × EMA(abs(typical price - EMA(typical price))))); the two inner EMAs have the same length. Its signal is an SMA of WaveTrend.
Calculation: Relative volatility index = 100 × EMA(upward deviation) / (EMA(upward deviation) + EMA(downward deviation)), where deviation is the standard deviation of close, upward deviation is zero when close does not rise and deviation otherwise, and downward deviation is zero when close rises and deviation otherwise. The two EMAs have the same length.
Calculation: OBV oscillator = OBV - EMA(OBV).
Calculation: ATR reversal state = initially up. Raw long stop is the rolling highest high minus a multiple of ATR; raw short stop is the rolling lowest low plus the same multiple of ATR. The long stop is max(raw long stop, previous long stop) when previous close > previous long stop, otherwise raw long stop. The short stop is min(raw short stop, previous short stop) when previous close < previous short stop, otherwise raw short stop. Initialize each previous stop to its current raw stop. Set state to up if close > previous short stop, otherwise down if close < previous long stop, otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Key levels = the latest confirmed close pivot high and low using a short right strength, plus the latest and two preceding confirmed close pivot highs and lows using a longer right strength; both use the same left strength. Each level retains its value until its next corresponding pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close < SMA(close) AND close > open AND current body size > EMA(body size) AND previous close < previous open AND previous body size < its EMA on that bar AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close AND (close > previous open OR open < previous close). Bearish engulfing reverses the trend, candle directions, and engulfing inequalities. Body size is abs(close - open).
Calculation: Directional score = sum of independently parameterized nonnegative weights for the true conditions in the corresponding bullish or bearish component list. Paired components share a weight; each divergence component remains true for a parameterized number of bars after confirmation. The lists are: close above/below a long EMA; shorter EMA rising/falling while longer EMA falling/rising; Supertrend in up/down direction; Supertrend reversing up/down; close above/below Parabolic SAR; SAR below close after previously being above previous close / SAR above close after previously being below current close; close above/below a longer HMA, with the bullish case additionally requiring the preceding HMA to be nonzero; HMA turning up/down at the preceding bar; bullish/bearish RSI divergence; RSI below oversold/above overbought; RSI above/below its midpoint while either of two preceding observations is strictly on the opposite side; bullish/bearish MACD-line divergence with the pivot below/above zero and the rolling maximum/minimum of the MACD line strictly below/above zero; MACD histogram turning up below zero/down above zero; MACD line strictly crossing above/below its signal while the signal is below/above zero; bullish/bearish WaveTrend divergence; WaveTrend strictly crossing above/below its signal while the signal is below a lower/above an upper threshold; bullish/bearish divergence of SMA-smoothed stochastic %K; smoothed stochastic %K strictly crossing above/below its SMA signal; close below the lower Bollinger band/open above the upper Bollinger band; ATR reversal state changing down-to-up/up-to-down; relative volatility index above a lower current threshold with its preceding value below a higher preceding threshold / below an upper current threshold with its preceding value above a lower preceding threshold; bullish/bearish relative volatility index divergence; a shorter HMA strictly crossing above/below any key level while close is above/below a parameterized earlier close; bullish/bearish OBV oscillator divergence; Chaikin money flow strictly crossing above/below zero; Chaikin money flow above/below zero; the longer HMA strictly crossing below/above session VWAP; the shorter EMA above/below session VWAP; bullish/bearish engulfing. MACD uses a shorter and longer close EMA and an EMA signal. Session VWAP uses typical price and resets at each session boundary.
Calculation: Score direction state = initially neutral. On each bar, set it to long if bullish score >= strong long threshold AND the preceding state is neutral or short; then set it to short if bearish score >= strong short threshold AND the resulting state is neutral or long. A long or short state-change signal occurs when the final state changes to that side.
Calculation: Stochastic CCI = SMA-smoothed stochastic of CCI, optionally followed by a second SMA; use the selected smoothed series.
Calculation: Short CCI reversal up/down = short-lookback CCI of close crosses above/below its EMA AND previous CCI is below a lower/above an upper threshold AND adjusted CCI equals unadjusted CCI. Adjusted CCI adds an adjustment to CCI for a bearish candle whose body exceeds a size threshold, subtracts it for a bullish candle whose body exceeds that threshold, and otherwise equals CCI; if the adjusted result is zero, use unadjusted CCI.
Calculation: HalfTrend state = initially up, with next direction down, maximum low initialized to previous low or current low if unavailable, and minimum high initialized to previous high or current high if unavailable. While next direction is down, update maximum low to the maximum of itself and the low at the lowest-low bar of the amplitude lookback; if SMA(high, amplitude) < maximum low AND close < previous low, set state down, next direction up, and minimum high to the high at the highest-high bar of that lookback. Otherwise update minimum high to the minimum of itself and that highest-bar high; if SMA(low, amplitude) > minimum high AND close > previous high, set state up, next direction down, and maximum low to the lowest-bar low. Up/down reversal means state changes to up/down; comparisons of bars since reversals require both reversals to have occurred.
Calculation: Long setup = long-lookback RSI of close + margin > EMA of that RSI OR (short CCI reversal up AND previous short-lookback CCI below a second lower threshold AND the latest HalfTrend up reversal occurred after the latest down reversal AND at least one bar ago) OR (previous short-lookback RSI below its lower threshold AND short CCI reversal up). The second CCI threshold is higher than the lower threshold in the short CCI reversal.
Calculation: Short setup = (long-lookback RSI of close - margin < EMA of that RSI AND short CCI reversal down AND previous short-lookback CCI above a second upper threshold AND the latest HalfTrend down reversal occurred after the latest up reversal AND at least one bar ago) OR (previous short-lookback RSI above its upper threshold AND short CCI reversal down). The second CCI threshold is higher than the upper threshold in the short CCI reversal.
Calculation: Exit stochastic RSI = stochastic of RSI of close, smoothed first with an EMA for %K and then with another EMA for %D.
Long: stochastic CCI < its oversold threshold.
Short: stochastic CCI > its overbought threshold.
Long: bullish score >= weak long threshold.
Short: bearish score >= weak short threshold.
Long: bullish score >= strong long threshold AND (alternation disabled OR score direction state changes to long) AND (trend filter disabled OR trend EMA > previous trend EMA).
Short: bearish score >= strong short threshold AND (alternation disabled OR score direction state changes to short) AND (trend filter disabled OR trend EMA < previous trend EMA).
Long: long setup AND (bearish score < weak short threshold OR exit stochastic RSI %K >= %D).
Short: short setup AND (bullish score < weak long threshold OR exit stochastic RSI %K <= %D).
Exit: long, bearish score >= weak short threshold AND exit stochastic RSI %K < %D AND long setup is false.
Exit: short, bullish score >= weak long threshold AND exit stochastic RSI %K > %D AND short setup is false.
Exit: long, close < entry price × (1 - stop fraction) AND previous close > previous bar's entry price × (1 - stop fraction).
Exit: short, close > entry price × (1 + stop fraction) AND previous close < previous bar's entry price × (1 + stop fraction).
