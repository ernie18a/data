Calculation: Structure pivots = independently for short-term internal and longer-term swing structure, use either symmetric high/low pivots confirmed after the same number of bars on each side, or leg transitions: compare the high and low from the lookback bars ago with the highest high and lowest low of the subsequent lookback bars; a high exceeding that highest high sets the leg state to high, otherwise a low below that lowest low sets it to low; emit a pivot only when the state changes. Keep the latest high and low pivots and mark each unbroken until its first break.
Calculation: Structure break = on a completed chart bar, the selected break source, either close or the corresponding wick extreme, exceeds the latest unbroken pivot high or falls below the latest unbroken pivot low. Set the corresponding structure direction upward or downward. A break in the existing direction is continuation; any other break is a change. A change is supported when the preceding direction is opposite and the latest opposite pivot is respectively a higher low or lower high formed after the broken pivot.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = the latest swing pivot low and high, overridden respectively by the lower of the latest two swing lows or the higher of the latest two swing highs when their distance is within an ATR multiple. Once established, an equal level remains in use until replaced by another equal level.
Calculation: Grab levels = confirmed symmetric pivot highs and lows; make each eligible only after confirmation, discard it after the maximum age or its first penetration, and retain only the most recently registered levels up to the pool limit. Measure penetration against the previous bar's ATR and rejection wick as a fraction of the current bar's range.
Calculation: Trendline = extend the line through the latest two swing pivot highs when the newer high is lower, or through the latest two swing pivot lows when the newer low is higher, provided their bar separation meets the minimum. Replace or remove the corresponding line whenever a new pivot of that side is confirmed; remove it on its first close beyond the line.
Calculation: Impulse range = after an upward swing structure break, set its low to the latest swing low, falling back to the rolling lowest low, and its high to the break bar's high; after a downward break, set its high to the latest swing high, falling back to the rolling highest high, and its low to the break bar's low. Until the next swing break, extend the high upward for an upward impulse or the low downward for a downward impulse. Retracement levels are measured from the impulse endpoint toward its origin.
Calculation: Displacement = a directional candle whose absolute body exceeds both a multiple of average absolute body and a multiple of ATR, and whose close is within the specified fraction of the range from the directional extreme. A recent event is one occurring on the current bar or within its configured lookback.
Calculation: Sweep = a low below the liquidity low with close above that level and above open, or a high above the liquidity high with close below that level and below open. Retain its bar and extreme for subsequent calculations.
Calculation: Imbalance zones = on the chart timeframe or the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, form a directional three-bar gap when the newest low exceeds the high two bars earlier or the newest high is below the low two bars earlier. Require gap size at least the larger configured ATR threshold and optionally require the intervening body to meet its ATR threshold. Alternatively form a directional adjacent-body gap when the wick ranges overlap, or an adjacent full-range gap; apply the same size floor. An inverse gap reverses the most recent three-bar gap when its selected mitigation condition is met; a double gap is the overlap of a new gap with the most recent opposite gap within the age limit. Remove zones on the selected close, wick, or midpoint mitigation, age, or retention limit. A higher-timeframe zone is evaluated only when a new completed source bar becomes available.
Calculation: Order-block zones = create a directional zone using one selected engine: a structure break with the latest opposite candle or the appropriate extreme between the broken pivot and break; an independent leg-pivot close break with the appropriate extreme strictly between pivot and break; or a confirmed symmetric volume pivot classified by the independent leg state. Extreme selection may swap high and low on bars whose range exceeds a multiple of ATR or cumulative mean true range. Select full candle, body, directional half, refined, or ATR-capped boundaries according to the engine; optionally require recent directional displacement. Reject invalid volume-pivot zones during confirmation. Remove or reverse a zone at its selected close, wick, or midpoint invalidation, and apply configured age, overlap, and retention rules. A zone interaction requires the current high-low range to overlap an active, non-breaker zone of the matching direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA. EMA alignment compares a shorter EMA with a longer EMA and close with a still longer EMA. Volume confirmation compares volume with a multiple of its moving average. RSI confirmation requires RSI inside the configured directional range.
Calculation: Directional score = sum configured weights for matching swing direction, internal direction, higher-timeframe direction or disabled higher-timeframe filter, current or recent swing continuation, current or recent ordinary swing change, recent directional sweep, matching order-block interaction, matching imbalance interaction, close in the directional impulse's configured retracement band, recent directional displacement, enabled volume confirmation, and enabled EMA alignment. A supported change receives the current-change contribution on its event bar but does not receive the ordinary-change contribution on subsequent bars.
Calculation: Planned entry = current close, the selected impulse retracement, or the midpoint of the latest active matching swing order block, falling back to the latest matching imbalance midpoint and then close. Planned stop = entry plus or minus ATR times the greater of the stop buffer and its minimum, or the latest matching order-block outer edge plus or minus the ATR buffer, or the more adverse of that edge and the current bar extreme combined with the most recent directional sweep extreme, plus or minus the ATR buffer. The zone method falls back to the structural method if no matching block exists.
Calculation: Planned targets = entry plus or minus successive configured risk multiples, where risk is the positive distance from planned entry to stop. For smart targets, substitute the latest internal pivot beyond entry, then the latest swing pivot beyond entry or equal-liquidity level beyond entry, then the directional impulse extension; for hybrid targets substitute only the first two. Replace a missing or wrong-side target with its risk-multiple target, and replace a second or third target that does not advance beyond its predecessor with its corresponding risk-multiple target.
Long: A newly confirmed internal or swing pivot high is greater than its preceding pivot high OR a newly confirmed internal or swing pivot low is greater than its preceding pivot low.
Short: A newly confirmed internal or swing pivot high is less than or equal to its preceding pivot high OR a newly confirmed internal or swing pivot low is less than or equal to its preceding pivot low.
Long: An internal or swing unbroken pivot high is broken upward.
Short: An internal or swing unbroken pivot low is broken downward.
Long: Close exceeds an active descending swing-high trendline.
Short: Close falls below an active ascending swing-low trendline.
Long: An eligible confirmed pivot low is penetrated AND both open and close are above it AND the previous close is at or above it AND penetration meets the configured previous-ATR minimum AND the lower wick meets the configured fraction of candle range.
Short: An eligible confirmed pivot high is penetrated AND both open and close are below it AND the previous close is at or below it AND penetration meets the configured previous-ATR minimum AND the upper wick meets the configured fraction of candle range.
Long: A bullish imbalance zone or bullish order-block zone is created.
Short: A bearish imbalance zone or bearish order-block zone is created.
Long: No planned trade is active AND the upward impulse retracement band contains close AND close is above open AND the selected strict or score conditions pass AND all enabled sweep, order-block, imbalance, displacement, and RSI filters pass AND planned risk is positive and no greater than its ATR limit AND the second target's reward divided by risk meets the minimum.
Short: No planned trade is active AND the downward impulse retracement band contains close AND close is below open AND the selected strict or score conditions pass AND all enabled sweep, order-block, imbalance, displacement, and RSI filters pass AND planned risk is positive and no greater than its ATR limit AND the second target's reward divided by risk meets the minimum AND no long setup is opened on the same bar.
Calculation: Strict conditions = matching swing direction AND a current or recent matching swing break AND recent matching displacement AND close in both the directional discount half and retracement band of the impulse AND interaction with a matching order block or imbalance AND a recent matching sweep AND matching higher-timeframe direction when that filter is enabled. Score conditions = directional score at or above its minimum. The recent swing-break lookback is longer than the recent sweep lookback, which is longer than the recent displacement lookback.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the planned stop OR high reaches the third planned target after the first and second targets have been reached OR a downward swing structure break occurs.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the planned stop OR low reaches the third planned target after the first and second targets have been reached OR an upward swing structure break occurs.
Calculation: Trade state = evaluate the stop before targets, including on the signal bar; then advance through targets in order, allowing multiple targets on one bar. If enabled, move the stop to planned entry after the first target, effective for stop checks from the next bar. An opposing swing structure break closes a trade still active after target processing.
