Calculation: Fast and slow ribbon averages = the mean of two configurable moving averages of close per ribbon; within each ribbon use a shorter and a longer length, and use faster lengths for the fast ribbon than for the slow ribbon. Multiply lengths by the common anchor multiplier when enabled. Each ribbon can use SMA, EMA, WMA, volume-weighted MA, smoothed MA, double EMA, triple EMA, Laguerre filter, Hull MA, zero-lag EMA, triangular MA, or SuperSmoother; use the same type within each ribbon.
Calculation: Laguerre filter = (L0 + 2*L1 + 2*L2 + L3)/6, where L0=(1-g)*close+g*previous L0, L1=-g*L0+previous L0+g*previous L1, L2=-g*L1+previous L1+g*previous L2, and L3=-g*L2+previous L2+g*previous L3; initialize previous values to zero. Use the lower gamma for the shorter ribbon MA and the higher gamma for the longer ribbon MA.
Calculation: SuperSmoother = c1*(close+previous close)/2+c2*previous SuperSmoother+c3*SuperSmoother two bars ago, where a=exp(-1.414*pi/length), c2=2*a*cos(1.414*pi/length), c3=-a*a, and c1=1-c2-c3; replace unavailable previous values with zero.
Calculation: Zero-lag EMA = 2*EMA(close, length)-EMA(EMA(close, length), length).
Calculation: Triangular MA = SMA(SMA(close, length), length).
Calculation: Ribbon expansion up = the shorter MA minus the longer MA is positive and greater than its previous value for two consecutive bars; ribbon expansion down = the difference is negative and less than its previous value for two consecutive bars. Apply the same definitions to the fast ribbon average minus the slow ribbon average.
Calculation: Channel midpoint = (highest high + lowest low)/2 over a common lookback.
Calculation: Lower and upper volatility bands = SMA(close) minus and plus a configurable fraction of EMA(true range), respectively.
Calculation: Long oscillator event = channel midpoint > its value a configurable number of bars ago AND close > lower volatility band AND close < channel midpoint AND RSI < a lower threshold AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar. Short oscillator event = channel midpoint < its value the same number of bars ago AND close < upper volatility band AND close > channel midpoint AND RSI > an upper threshold AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar.
Calculation: Long score = 2 on the first bar when both ribbons and their averages all have expansion up, increasing by 1 for each consecutive bar on which that remains true; otherwise 0. Add 1 when the long oscillator event occurs and 1 when parabolic SAR > high. Short score is calculated symmetrically using expansion down and the short oscillator event, but adds 1 when parabolic SAR < low. Do not carry these additions into the next bar's base score.
Calculation: Internal trade state = flat initially; when flat, set it to long if long score >= 3, otherwise set it to short if short score >= 3. Respect the enabled order sides, prioritize long when both qualify, and do not enter on the bar immediately after a close signal. Reset to flat after an internal close signal. In flip mode swap the resulting long and short entry actions; retain the original internal state for exit calculations.
Calculation: Effective high and low = high and low, or, when the candle-body option is enabled, max(open, close) and min(open, close). Running high and running low are the extremes of effective high and low respectively since the corresponding internal state began, including the current bar. The reference entry price is the close of the corresponding entry-signal bar.
Long: close > EMA(close).
Short: close <= EMA(close).
Long: fast ribbon expansion up OR slow ribbon expansion up.
Short: fast ribbon expansion down OR slow ribbon expansion down.
Long: internal trade state changes from flat to long AND long orders are enabled AND bar datetime is within the configured backtest period; in flip mode use the change from flat to short instead.
Short: internal trade state changes from flat to short AND short orders are enabled AND bar datetime is within the configured backtest period; in flip mode use the change from flat to long instead.
Exit: long, long orders enabled AND bar datetime within the configured backtest period AND internal state is long AND no long entry signal on this bar AND ((trailing stop enabled AND effective low <= running high*(1-trailing stop fraction)) OR (take profit enabled AND running high >= reference entry price*(1+profit target fraction) AND effective low <= running high*(1-min(trailing profit fraction, profit target fraction))) OR (stop loss enabled AND effective low <= reference entry price*(1-stop loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-trade exit enabled OR trailing stop and stop loss both disabled) AND previous fast ribbon average < previous slow ribbon average AND open < previous slow ribbon average AND this combined comparison was false on the preceding bar)).
Exit: short, short orders enabled AND bar datetime within the configured backtest period AND internal state is short AND no short entry signal on this bar AND ((trailing stop enabled AND effective high >= running low*(1+trailing stop fraction)) OR (take profit enabled AND running low <= reference entry price*(1-profit target fraction) AND effective high >= running low*(1+min(trailing profit fraction, profit target fraction))) OR (stop loss enabled AND effective high >= reference entry price*(1+stop loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-trade exit enabled OR trailing stop and stop loss both disabled) AND previous fast ribbon average > previous slow ribbon average AND open > previous slow ribbon average AND this combined comparison was false on the preceding bar)).
