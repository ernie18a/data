Calculation: Timeframe factors = For each configured timeframe, calculate on its own bars. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use its last completed bar within the chart bar. Let A be ATR, falling back to close times a small positive factor when ATR is not positive. Clamp means limit to [-1, 1]. Trend = clamp(weighted sum of sign(fast EMA − slow EMA), clamp((close − slow EMA)/(A × distance scale)), and clamp(linear-regression slope/A × slope sensitivity) × squared correlation of close with bar index). Trend quality = clamp((efficiency ratio − trend threshold)/(1 − trend threshold)) × sign determined by close >= slow EMA. Momentum = clamp(weighted sum of centered RSI and clamp(RSI change over a lookback/RSI-change scale)). Structure = clamp(weighted sum of close's normalized position in the rolling high-low range and swing break); swing break is +1 if close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high, otherwise −1 if close is below the latest confirmed pivot low, otherwise 0. Flow = clamp(weighted sum of centered MFI of typical price and EMA of ((close − low) − (high − close))/(high − low)); use zero for a zero range. Mean reversion = clamp(−(close − SMA(close))/(standard deviation of close × deviation scale)); use zero when the standard deviation is zero. Efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Regime = Trending if ADX exceeds its threshold AND efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; otherwise volatile if ATR exceeds its moving average times a volatility multiplier; otherwise ranging. A trending regime is up if close >= slow EMA and down if close < slow EMA.
Calculation: Factor accuracy multiplier = On each confirmed chart bar, record each chart-timeframe factor whose absolute value reaches its recording threshold, its sign and the close. After a fixed horizon, a record is a hit if the close change in its recorded direction is at least a threshold times the ATR at resolution. Also record every chart close and, after the same horizon, measure the proportions of upward and downward changes reaching the same ATR threshold. For each factor, subtract the direction-count-weighted base hit rate from its hit rate; multiply that difference by a sensitivity, add one, and clamp to configured minimum and maximum weights. Use one until the minimum sample count is reached or when accuracy weighting is disabled. Apply these chart-timeframe multipliers to all timeframes.
Calculation: Timeframe composite = Weighted average of trend, trend quality, momentum, structure, flow and mean reversion, with weights selected by regime and multiplied by their respective factor accuracy multipliers; multiply by one minus a volatility-damping coefficient times the ATR percentile rank, and clamp to [-1, 1].
Calculation: Horizon composite = Weighted sum of the configured timeframe composites for a fast, intermediate or slow horizon, optionally blended with chart-timeframe directional context, then clamped to [-1, 1]. The fast horizon emphasizes shorter timeframes, the intermediate horizon emphasizes middle timeframes, and the slow horizon emphasizes longer timeframes. With only the available backtest fields, the reproducible directional context is the enabled value component: clamp((close − session VWAP of typical price)/(rolling standard deviation of close × scale)), or zero if the standard deviation is zero. Omit unavailable external-symbol and external-input context components.
Calculation: Shift direction = Standardize each chart-bar log return using its rolling mean and standard deviation, using zero if the deviation is zero. Update an upper cumulative sum as max(0, previous upper sum + standardized return − slack) and a lower sum as min(0, previous lower sum + standardized return + slack). When either sum crosses its positive or negative detection threshold, reset both sums to zero and activate the shift for a fixed number of bars. During an active shift, direction is up when the upper sum >= absolute lower sum, otherwise down.
Long: Any timeframe factor or timeframe composite > positive directional-color threshold.
Short: Any timeframe factor or timeframe composite < negative directional-color threshold.
Long: Regime is trending AND close >= slow EMA on any configured timeframe.
Short: Regime is trending AND close < slow EMA on any configured timeframe.
Long: Any horizon composite > positive neutral-deadband threshold.
Short: Any horizon composite < negative neutral-deadband threshold.
Long: Shift is active AND upper cumulative sum >= absolute lower cumulative sum.
Short: Shift is active AND upper cumulative sum < absolute lower cumulative sum.
