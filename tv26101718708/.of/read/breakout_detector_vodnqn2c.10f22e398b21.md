Calculation: Upper and lower rails = EMA of, respectively, the highest high and lowest low over the rail lookback plus or minus ATR padding. Padding is ATR multiplied by a padding parameter; when adaptive padding is enabled, add the close standard deviation over the rail lookback divided by ATR times an adaptive scaling parameter to the padding parameter before multiplying by ATR. Use zero adaptive addition when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Breakout state = initially neutral. On each completed chart bar, reset to neutral if close is between the previous upper and lower rails inclusive. Then set to up on a qualifying upper breakout if the state is not already up, or to down on a qualifying lower breakout if the state is not already down. A qualifying breakout meets the corresponding close, candle-body, ATR and volume comparisons below; prior ATR and prior volume SMA must be available and positive.
Calculation: Directional volume delta = sum lower-timeframe bar volume when its close is above its open, negative volume when its close is below its open, and zero otherwise, over the completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, apply the same rule to the chart bar.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same lookback on each side, identified only after the right-hand bars have completed. Compare it with the most recent previous confirmed swing of the same kind among the retained swing history.
Calculation: Normalized rail slope = change in the midpoint of the upper and lower rails over a slope lookback divided by that lookback and current ATR; use zero when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger Bands lie strictly inside a Keltner channel using the same close SMA basis and calculation length, with channel width based on ATR, OR the compression score meets its threshold. Compression score is one minus the current Bollinger Bandwidth's position within its rolling minimum-to-maximum range, clamped to zero through one; use zero when that range is unavailable or zero.
Long: Close > previous upper rail AND close > open AND absolute candle body >= a displacement multiple of previous ATR AND volume / previous volume SMA >= a participation threshold AND previous ATR > 0 AND previous volume SMA > 0 AND breakout state before processing the current breakout != up.
Short: Close < previous lower rail AND close < open AND absolute candle body >= a displacement multiple of previous ATR AND volume / previous volume SMA >= a participation threshold AND previous ATR > 0 AND previous volume SMA > 0 AND breakout state before processing the current breakout != down.
Long: Breakout state = up.
Short: Breakout state = down.
Long: Confirmed swing high > previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low > previous confirmed swing low.
Short: Confirmed swing high <= previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low <= previous confirmed swing low.
Long: Directional volume delta >= 0.
Short: Directional volume delta < 0.
Long: Normalized rail slope > positive flow threshold OR normalized rail slope >= positive expansion threshold AND compression = false AND breakout state = neutral.
Short: Normalized rail slope < negative flow threshold OR normalized rail slope <= negative expansion threshold AND compression = false AND breakout state = neutral.
