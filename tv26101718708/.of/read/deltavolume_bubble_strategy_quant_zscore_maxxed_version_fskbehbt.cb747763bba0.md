Calculation: Signed volume delta = footprint buy volume minus sell volume, aggregated from lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar; use its absolute value for the delta magnitude.
Calculation: Delta Z-score = (absolute volume delta − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation, using the same lookback for both statistics.
Calculation: Entry volume extreme = delta Z-score ≥ threshold in adaptive mode, or absolute signed volume delta ≥ threshold in fixed mode.
Calculation: Absorption = absorption filter enabled AND the bubble extreme threshold is met AND absolute(close − open) < rolling mean of absolute(close − open) × absorption ratio; use the same lookback for both body calculations.
Calculation: Trend line = the configured EMA, WMA, KAMA, or non-repainting Nadaraya–Watson rational-quadratic regression of close, using its configured lookback/window; KAMA uses shorter fast and longer slow smoothing lengths.
Calculation: LNL chop = at least one active LNL stop level is below close AND at least one active LNL stop level is above close. Stop levels use a 13-period EMA of close and an 8-period EMA of true range scaled by configured ATR fractions. Each stop level's trend state switches bullish when close crosses above EMA + scaled ATR, bearish when close crosses below EMA − scaled ATR, and otherwise retains its prior state; its stop is EMA − scaled ATR in the bullish state and EMA + scaled ATR in the bearish state. Use primary and secondary levels, plus the additional three progressively tighter levels in Net mode.
Long: Entry volume extreme AND signed volume delta > 0 AND (trend filter disabled OR trend type is None OR close > trend line) AND NOT Absorption AND (LNL filter disabled OR NOT LNL chop)
Short: Entry volume extreme AND signed volume delta < 0 AND (trend filter disabled OR trend type is None OR close < trend line) AND NOT Absorption AND (LNL filter disabled OR NOT LNL chop)
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − (configured stop multiplier × 14-period ATR) OR high ≥ entry price + (configured stop multiplier × 14-period ATR × configured reward-to-risk ratio)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + (configured stop multiplier × 14-period ATR) OR low ≤ entry price − (configured stop multiplier × 14-period ATR × configured reward-to-risk ratio)
