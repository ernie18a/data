Calculation: Signed volume = either chart-bar volume when close >= open and negative chart-bar volume otherwise, or lower-timeframe volume delta: sum the volumes of the lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, assigning positive volume when a lower-timeframe close exceeds its open and negative volume when it is below its open; when close equals open, use the sign of its close change from the preceding lower-timeframe bar, retaining the preceding lower-timeframe bar's sign if the closes are equal. Use the lower-timeframe bars available within the chart bar, including any still forming.
Calculation: Spike = either the rolling Z-score of absolute signed volume >= a threshold, with Z-score set to zero when its rolling standard deviation is zero, or absolute signed volume >= a fixed threshold. The bubble and entry spike thresholds are independently configurable; fixed mode uses the same fixed threshold for both.
Calculation: Absorption = absorption detection enabled AND bubble spike true AND absolute chart-bar candle body < its rolling simple moving average multiplied by a ratio; the average uses the same lookback as the spike Z-score.
Calculation: Trend line = a selectable EMA, WMA, KAMA, or rational-quadratic Nadaraya-Watson regression of chart-bar close. For the regression, average available closes at offsets i from a configurable starting offset through window length minus one, weighted by (1 + i² / (relative weight² × window length²)) raised to the negative relative weight. KAMA uses configurable faster and slower smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Trailing levels = chart-bar close EMA plus or minus each selected multiple of an EMA of true range. For each multiple independently, set its state to bullish when close > EMA plus the offset, bearish when close < EMA minus the offset, and otherwise retain its previous state; initialize the state as neutral. Its level is EMA minus the offset in the bullish state, EMA plus the offset in the bearish state, and unavailable in the neutral state. Use a primary and a smaller secondary offset; the net setting additionally uses three successively smaller offsets.
Calculation: Chop = at least one available trailing level < close AND at least one available trailing level > close.
Long: Entry spike true AND signed volume > 0 AND (trend filter disabled OR no trend line selected OR close > trend line) AND absorption false AND (chop filter disabled OR chop false).
Short: Entry spike true AND signed volume < 0 AND (trend filter disabled OR no trend line selected OR close < trend line) AND absorption false AND (chop filter disabled OR chop false).
Long: Bubble spike true AND signed volume > 0 AND bullish bubbles enabled.
Short: Bubble spike true AND signed volume <= 0 AND bearish bubbles enabled.
Long: Shortest close VWMA > next-shorter close VWMA AND next-shorter close VWMA > next-longer close VWMA AND next-longer close VWMA > longest close VWMA AND close >= trailing-level close EMA.
Short: Shortest close VWMA < next-shorter close VWMA AND next-shorter close VWMA < next-longer close VWMA AND next-longer close VWMA < longest close VWMA AND close <= trailing-level close EMA.
Long: A selected trailing-level state is bullish AND its marking is enabled.
Short: A selected trailing-level state is bearish AND its marking is enabled.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR multiplied by a stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier and a reward-to-risk multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR multiplied by a stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier and a reward-to-risk multiplier.
