Calculation: Gann levels = Keep the latest confirmed pivot high and low whose pivot-bar range exceeds ATR × a minimum-range multiplier; use the high as anchor when close is below the midpoint of those pivots, otherwise use the low. For each enabled 45° increment from 45° to 360°, calculate (√anchor + direction × increment/360)², where direction is −1 for a high anchor and +1 for a low anchor.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish divergence = Compare a confirmed price pivot with the corresponding price value 2 × the divergence lookback bars earlier, and compare the current indicator pivot with the indicator value 2 × the divergence lookback bars earlier. Bullish divergence requires a lower price low and a higher Stochastic pivot below 40, or a higher MACD histogram pivot below zero. Bearish divergence requires a higher price high and a lower Stochastic pivot above 60, or a lower MACD histogram pivot above zero.
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR on the entry bar.
Long: close is within the configured percentage of any enabled Gann level AND (bullish divergence OR smoothed Stochastic is below 35) AND close > open AND close − open ≥ (high − low) × the minimum body ratio AND close > the EMA on the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: close is within the configured percentage of any enabled Gann level AND (bearish divergence OR smoothed Stochastic is above 65) AND close < open AND open − close ≥ (high − low) × the minimum body ratio AND close < the EMA on the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, price ≤ entry price − entry ATR × stop multiplier OR price ≥ entry price + entry ATR × target multiplier OR an enabled trailing stop is hit after price has risen by the current ATR × trailing multiplier from entry, with the stop that distance below the highest price since activation.
Exit: short, price ≥ entry price + entry ATR × stop multiplier OR price ≤ entry price − entry ATR × target multiplier OR an enabled trailing stop is hit after price has fallen by the current ATR × trailing multiplier from entry, with the stop that distance above the lowest price since activation.
