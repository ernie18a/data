Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a value at a bar that is a pivot high or low over equal left and right lookbacks, reported on the bar that completes the right lookback; otherwise unavailable.
Calculation: Gann levels = retain the latest confirmed price pivot high and low whose pivot-bar high minus low exceeds pivot-bar ATR times a minimum-range multiplier. If both exist and close is below their midpoint, use the high as anchor and direction −1; otherwise use the low as anchor and direction +1. For a positive anchor, each level is (sqrt(anchor) + direction × fraction)²; select the enabled cardinal fractions {2/8, 4/8, 6/8, 8/8} and ordinal fractions {1/8, 3/8, 5/8, 7/8}.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = stochastic of close, high and low, smoothed twice with successive SMAs using separately configurable lengths.
Calculation: Bullish stochastic divergence = confirmed price pivot low and confirmed smoothed-stochastic pivot low on the current bar AND current price pivot low < the price-pivot-low series at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current stochastic pivot low > smoothed stochastic at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current stochastic pivot low < a bullish pivot threshold. All pivots use the same divergence lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bearish stochastic divergence = confirmed price pivot high and confirmed smoothed-stochastic pivot high on the current bar AND current price pivot high > the price-pivot-high series at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current stochastic pivot high < smoothed stochastic at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current stochastic pivot high > a bearish pivot threshold. All pivots use the same divergence lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish MACD divergence = confirmed price pivot low and confirmed MACD histogram pivot low on the current bar AND current price pivot low < the price-pivot-low series at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current histogram pivot low > histogram at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current histogram pivot low < zero. All pivots use the same divergence lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bearish MACD divergence = confirmed price pivot high and confirmed MACD histogram pivot high on the current bar AND current price pivot high > the price-pivot-high series at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current histogram pivot high < histogram at twice the divergence lookback bars ago AND current histogram pivot high > zero. All pivots use the same divergence lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Trailing distance = current ATR times a trailing multiplier, rounded to the nearest price tick. After price moves favorably from the entry fill price by this distance, activate a trailing stop at the best price reached since activation minus the distance for a long position, or plus the distance for a short position; update the stop only in the favorable direction as price and distance change.
Long: (Gann proximity filter disabled OR absolute difference between close and any enabled Gann level <= close times a proximity tolerance) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish stochastic divergence OR bullish MACD divergence OR (fallback enabled AND smoothed stochastic < bullish fallback threshold)) AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND close − open >= (high − low) times a body-ratio threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: (Gann proximity filter disabled OR absolute difference between close and any enabled Gann level <= close times a proximity tolerance) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish stochastic divergence OR bearish MACD divergence OR (fallback enabled AND smoothed stochastic > bearish fallback threshold)) AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close < open AND open − close >= (high − low) times a body-ratio threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA).
Exit: long, (fixed exits enabled AND (low <= entry-signal close − entry-signal ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= entry-signal close + entry-signal ATR times target multiplier)) OR (trailing exit enabled AND trailing stop activated AND low <= trailing stop).
Exit: short, (fixed exits enabled AND (high >= entry-signal close + entry-signal ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= entry-signal close − entry-signal ATR times target multiplier)) OR (trailing exit enabled AND trailing stop activated AND high >= trailing stop).
