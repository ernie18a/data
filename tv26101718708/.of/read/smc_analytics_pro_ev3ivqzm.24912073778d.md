Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side. The swing pivot lookback is longer than the internal pivot lookback.
Calculation: For each pivot type and lookback, the latest confirmed pivot is updated when a pivot is confirmed and cleared after a close crosses it in its break direction. Crossings compare the current close and current pivot value with the previous close and previous pivot value.
Long: Close crosses above the latest uncleared internal pivot high OR the latest uncleared swing pivot high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest uncleared internal pivot low OR the latest uncleared swing pivot low.
Long: A swing pivot high is confirmed above the preceding uncleared swing pivot high OR a swing pivot low is confirmed above the preceding uncleared swing pivot low.
Short: A swing pivot high is confirmed at or below the preceding uncleared swing pivot high OR a swing pivot low is confirmed at or below the preceding uncleared swing pivot low.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago.
Long: The absolute difference between a newly confirmed swing pivot low and the preceding confirmed swing pivot low is at or below a price-distance threshold.
Short: The absolute difference between a newly confirmed swing pivot high and the preceding confirmed swing pivot high is at or below a price-distance threshold.
