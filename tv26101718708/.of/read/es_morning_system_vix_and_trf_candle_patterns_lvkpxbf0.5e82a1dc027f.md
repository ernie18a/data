Calculation: Twin range filter = For each of a shorter and a longer period, calculate EMA(EMA(abs(close − previous close), period), 2 × period − 1) × its multiplier; let r be their average. Starting with previous filter value 0, set filter to max(previous filter, close − r) when close > previous filter, otherwise min(previous filter, close + r). Set upper band to filter + r if that value is below the previous upper band OR previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Set lower band to filter − r if that value is above the previous lower band OR previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize missing previous values to 0. If the previous line equals the previous upper band, use the upper band when close <= upper band and otherwise the lower band; if the previous line equals the previous lower band, use the lower band when close >= lower band and otherwise the upper band; in all other cases use the upper band.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, resetting at its specified local-time anchor and including the anchor bar. Use separate overnight and session anchors.
Calculation: Long setup = Reset daily. Process confirmed chart bars only within the specified session. Treat a doji as the previous processed bar's direction, initially green. At each green-to-red transition start a red run; its candidate swing high is the maximum high of the preceding green run and the red run, and its swing low is the minimum low of the red run. At the next red-to-green transition confirm both swings. If both exceed their respective previous confirmed swings, store the red run's maximum high as the break level and its minimum low as the invalidation level and enter break-wait, unless dormant. Replace an existing setup when a later qualifying pair occurs. On each break-wait bar, including the setup bar, if high >= break level, retain the setup unless a long fires; otherwise, if low < invalidation level, cancel it. A long fire makes the setup dormant. A new qualifying VWAP touch releases dormancy before that bar's swing and break processing.
Calculation: Armed = Reset false daily and after a long fire. Set true on a confirmed session bar whose range contains either anchored VWAP when the immediately preceding chart bar's range did not contain either VWAP. Update the previous-contact state on every chart bar, including outside the session.
Long: long setup is in break-wait AND high >= its break level AND armed = true AND close > overnight anchored VWAP AND (session anchored VWAP is unavailable OR close > session anchored VWAP) AND the last completed higher-timeframe close > its twin range filter AND local time is within the specified signal window.
Long: current chart close > chart twin range filter.
Short: current chart close <= chart twin range filter.
Long: last completed higher-timeframe close > its twin range filter.
Short: last completed higher-timeframe close <= its twin range filter.
Long: previous bar's high > the highs immediately before and after it AND previous bar's high > the previous confirmed such swing high.
Short: previous bar's high > the highs immediately before and after it AND previous bar's high < the previous confirmed such swing high.
Long: previous bar's low < the lows immediately before and after it AND previous bar's low > the previous confirmed such swing low.
Short: previous bar's low < the lows immediately before and after it AND previous bar's low < the previous confirmed such swing low.
