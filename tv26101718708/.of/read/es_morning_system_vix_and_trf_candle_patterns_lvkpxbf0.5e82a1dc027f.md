Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) / cumulative volume, reset at the overnight-session start or regular-session open.
Calculation: Bullish Twin Range Filter = close > its recursive range-filter line. Build the range from the mean of two smoothed ranges, each equal to EMA(EMA(abs(close − prior close), period), 2 × period − 1) × multiplier, using a shorter and a longer period. The price filter holds its prior value unless close moves beyond it by the range; trailing upper and lower bands are updated from the filter ± range, and the line switches between those bands when close crosses them.
Calculation: Long candle-color swings = a swing high is the maximum high from a green run through the following red run; its paired swing low is the minimum low of that red run. Confirm the pair at the next red-to-green transition. Track the maximum high of red pullback candles as the long break level.
Calculation: Short candle-color swings = a swing low is the minimum low from a red run through the following green run; its paired swing high is the maximum high of that green run. Confirm the pair at the next green-to-red transition. Track the minimum low of green bounce candles as the short break level.
Long: During the regular session, after a confirmed bar touches either anchored VWAP, a confirmed higher swing high and higher swing low have formed; while waiting for the break, high ≥ the maximum high of the red pullback candles AND close > overnight VWAP AND (regular-session VWAP is unavailable OR close > regular-session VWAP) AND the underlying series' higher-timeframe Bullish Twin Range Filter is true AND time is within the signal window (15 minutes after the regular-session open through noon); after entry, require a new VWAP touch to re-arm, and cancel the pending setup if low < its higher swing low before the break.
Short: During the regular session, a confirmed lower swing low and lower swing high have formed; while waiting for the break, low ≤ the minimum low of the green bounce candles AND close < overnight VWAP AND the volatility-index series' higher-timeframe Bullish Twin Range Filter is true AND time is within the signal window (15 minutes after the regular-session open through noon); cancel the pending setup if high > its lower swing high before the break.
