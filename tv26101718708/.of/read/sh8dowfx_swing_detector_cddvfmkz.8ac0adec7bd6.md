Calculation: Swing range = On each higher-timeframe completed bar, detect pivot highs and lows using configurable numbers of preceding and following bars; a pivot high is at least as high as every preceding bar and higher than every following bar, and a pivot low is at most as low as every preceding bar and lower than every following bar. Process newly confirmed highs before lows. Store the first pivot as the last swing. When a pivot is opposite in type to the last swing, set the range origin to the last swing and the endpoint to the new pivot, then store the new pivot as the last swing. For a same-type pivot, replace the last swing only if the new high is higher or the new low is lower; also replace the endpoint if the existing range is respectively rising or falling. Keep the origin unchanged. No range exists until opposite-type pivots have been found.
Long: Swing range endpoint > swing range origin
Short: Swing range endpoint <= swing range origin
