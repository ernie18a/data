Calculation: Pivot state = For each independent lookback, start with a bearish leg. If the high N bars ago exceeds the highest high of the N subsequent bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low N bars ago is below the lowest low of the N subsequent bars, set it bullish. On a change to bearish, record that earlier high as the latest pivot high; on a change to bullish, record that earlier low as the latest pivot low. Each new pivot resets its crossed flag. Use separate shorter internal, longer swing, and equal-high/low lookbacks.
Calculation: Structure break = Close crosses above the latest pivot high or below the latest pivot low, comparing each bar's close with that bar's pivot level, AND that pivot has not already been crossed. Mark the pivot crossed and set the corresponding structure trend to bullish or bearish. Initialize each trend to neutral. For an internal break, additionally require the internal pivot level to differ from the corresponding swing pivot level; if the optional confluence filter is enabled, require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for an upward break, or < for a downward break. A change of character is a break opposite to the preceding structure trend.
Calculation: Order block = At each structure break, select the bar from the broken pivot bar inclusive to the break bar exclusive with the lowest parsed low for an upward break or highest parsed high for a downward break; choose the earliest on a tie. Parsed high and low equal the bar's high and low, except they are swapped when high - low is at least twice the selected volatility measure. The measure is ATR or cumulative true range divided by the zero-based chart bar index. Store the selected parsed high and low as a bullish or bearish block respectively. Maintain separate internal and swing lists, retaining at most the most recent bounded number of blocks, and remove a block when its mitigation condition occurs.
Calculation: Fair value gap threshold = Optionally, twice the cumulative sum, over chart bars, of the absolute value of (the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close - open) / (open * 100) on each new higher-timeframe bar, divided by the zero-based chart bar index; otherwise zero.
Long: An upward swing or internal structure break occurs.
Short: A downward swing or internal structure break occurs.
Long: A new swing pivot low is greater than or equal to the preceding swing pivot low, OR a new swing pivot high is greater than the preceding swing pivot high.
Short: A new swing pivot low is below the preceding swing pivot low, OR a new swing pivot high is less than or equal to the preceding swing pivot high.
Long: A new equal-low pivot differs from the preceding equal-low pivot by less than a threshold multiple of chart-timeframe ATR.
Short: A new equal-high pivot differs from the preceding equal-high pivot by less than a threshold multiple of chart-timeframe ATR.
Long: The high, or optionally the close, exceeds the parsed high of an active bearish internal or swing order block.
Short: The low, or optionally the close, falls below the parsed low of an active bullish internal or swing order block.
Long: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the still-forming higher-timeframe low and the last completed higher-timeframe close are both above the high of the higher-timeframe bar before the last completed bar AND the last completed higher-timeframe (close - open) / (open * 100) exceeds the fair value gap threshold.
Short: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the still-forming higher-timeframe high and the last completed higher-timeframe close are both below the low of the higher-timeframe bar before the last completed bar AND the negative of the last completed higher-timeframe (close - open) / (open * 100) exceeds the fair value gap threshold.
Long: The internal structure trend is bullish.
Short: The internal structure trend is bearish or neutral.
Long: A bullish internal change of character occurs AND volume exceeds its chart-timeframe simple moving average AND the shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the close exceeds its EMA on the last completed bar of a second, lower higher timeframe.
Short: A bearish internal change of character occurs AND volume exceeds its chart-timeframe simple moving average AND the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the close is below its EMA on the last completed bar of a second, lower higher timeframe.
Long: The shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: The shorter EMA is less than or equal to the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
