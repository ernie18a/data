Calculation: Relative strength = (current close / close from a lookback ago) / (current comparative-symbol close / its close from the same lookback ago) − 1
Calculation: Average volume = average volume over a lookback including the current bar
Calculation: Highest prior high = highest high over a lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Prior-close average = average close over several preceding bars, excluding the current bar
Long: close > shorter-term EMA AND volume > a threshold multiple of average volume OR close > longer-term EMA AND relative strength > 0 AND volume > a threshold multiple of average volume AND close > highest prior high OR close > shortest-term EMA AND volume > a higher threshold multiple of average volume
Exit: long, close < a threshold fraction of the shorter-term EMA OR close < a threshold fraction of the previous bar's close OR close > a threshold multiple of the prior-close average OR (close < shortest-term EMA AND close < shorter-term EMA)
