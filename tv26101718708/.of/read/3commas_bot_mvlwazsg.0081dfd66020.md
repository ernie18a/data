Calculation: Each of two independently selected moving averages uses close and may be EMA, SMA, HMA, WMA, DEMA, VWMA, session VWAP, T3, or EMA of Heikin-Ashi open. The first normally has a shorter length than the second. Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2. T3 uses a smoothing factor of 0.7. Session VWAP uses typical price and resets each trading session.
Calculation: At an entry signal, save long stop = lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR times the risk adjustment; short stop = highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR times the risk adjustment. Save long target = signal close + reward-to-risk ratio times (signal close - long stop); short target = signal close - reward-to-risk ratio times (short stop - signal close). Save the trailing trigger as signal close plus the configured trigger fraction of (target - signal close), even when the target exit is disabled.
Calculation: Initialize the trailing stop to the saved stop at each entry signal. If trailing is enabled, activate it when the trigger fraction equals zero or, for a long position, high >= the saved trigger, or, for a short position, low <= the saved trigger. Starting on the next confirmed bar after activation, update the long trailing stop to the greater of its previous value and long trailing source minus ATR times the trailing multiplier; update the short trailing stop to the lesser of its previous value and short trailing source plus ATR times the trailing multiplier. The source is selectable as previous close, previous open, or the current swing-lookback lowest low for long and highest high for short.
Long: The first moving average crosses above the second AND ATR is available AND the bar is confirmed AND datetime is within the configured date range AND the optional excluded session, interpreted in GMT-6, does not contain the bar open AND long trading is enabled AND (position is flat OR position is short AND reversal is enabled).
Short: The first moving average crosses below the second AND ATR is available AND the bar is confirmed AND datetime is within the configured date range AND the optional excluded session, interpreted in GMT-6, does not contain the bar open AND short trading is enabled AND (position is flat OR position is long AND reversal is enabled).
Long: Either moving average >= its previous-bar value.
Short: Either moving average < its previous-bar value.
Exit: long, low <= the saved long stop when trailing is disabled OR low <= the trailing stop when trailing is enabled OR high >= the saved long target when the target exit is enabled OR a permitted short entry occurs while reversal is enabled.
Exit: short, high >= the saved short stop when trailing is disabled OR high >= the trailing stop when trailing is enabled OR low <= the saved short target when the target exit is enabled OR a permitted long entry occurs while reversal is enabled.
