Calculation: Yearly VWAP = cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each calendar year; use typical price if cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot with the same lookback on each side, available only after the right-side bars close. Track the latest two confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = the latest confirmed swing high is above the preceding high AND close is above that high plus an ATR buffer AND that high differs from the last bullish broken high by more than a minimum price increment AND this entire condition was false on the preceding bar. Record the high when the break occurs. Bearish structure break is the mirror using swing lows, a lower latest low, close below the low minus the buffer, and the last bearish broken low.
Calculation: Directional volume = EMA(volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) / max(EMA(volume), 1), using the same EMA lookback in numerator and denominator.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Trail bands = EMA-smoothed edges around a smoothed trail. Smooth ATR and the absolute EMA-to-yearly-VWAP gap with the same lookback; cap the smoothed gap at an ATR multiple. The raw trail is the greater of EMA and yearly VWAP in a bullish stack, the lesser in a bearish stack, otherwise EMA when the chart is in the short-duration regime and the uncapped gap exceeds its cap, otherwise their midpoint. Smooth the raw trail with a separate EMA. Width is the greater of the smoothed ATR times a width multiplier plus capped gap times a gap multiplier, and an ATR-based floor; in the short-duration regime cap the gap multiplier and increase the floor. Prevent width from decreasing by more than a maximum fraction of its previous value per bar. EMA-smooth width and the smoothed trail with the same edge lookback, then EMA-smooth each edge with that lookback: resistance edges are trail plus width times the inner and outer edge multipliers; support edges are trail minus those amounts.
Calculation: Order block = on each directional structure break, search preceding bars up to the smaller of a maximum search limit and the greater of the search lookback and twice the pivot lookback. Eligible bullish candidates are bearish candles with body/range at least a minimum; eligible bearish candidates are bullish candles meeting the same test. Floor range at the minimum price increment. Select the lowest score, resolving ties in favor of the most recent candidate. Bullish score = 0.65 × abs(candidate high − latest swing high) + 0.20 × range + 0.15 × upper wick − 0.25 × body − 0.10 × max(break close − candidate high, 0). Bearish score mirrors this with candidate low, swing low, lower wick, and max(candidate low − break close, 0). The zone is the selected candle's high to low; clear the previous same-side zone if no candidate qualifies. Replace the zone on the next same-side break, and clear it when order blocks are disabled, its forward lifetime expires, or its midpoint is farther than a configured ATR multiple from close when distance hiding is enabled. A bullish zone becomes mitigated when close, or optionally low, falls below its bottom; a bearish zone becomes mitigated when close, or optionally high, rises above its top. Mitigation persists until replacement or clearing.
Calculation: Near a level = low ≤ level + ATR tolerance AND high ≥ level − ATR tolerance. Near a zone = high ≥ zone bottom − ATR tolerance AND low ≤ zone top + ATR tolerance; use half the tolerance for trail bands.
Long: At bar close, bullish structure break AND EMA > yearly VWAP AND close > EMA AND close > yearly VWAP AND ATR / close ≥ the activity threshold, with that threshold multiplied by a relaxation factor in the short-duration chart regime AND (location gate disabled OR near an unmitigated bullish order block OR near the latest swing high OR near the support band OR near yearly VWAP) AND (no prior A- or B-tier signal OR bars since the latest signal of either side ≥ cooldown) AND ((directional volume > its positive threshold AND relative volume ≥ its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA)) OR (B tier enabled AND ((directional volume > its positive threshold AND relative volume < its threshold) OR (directional volume > a fraction of its positive threshold AND relative volume ≥ its threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR B-tier higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA))).
Short: At bar close, bearish structure break AND EMA < yearly VWAP AND close < EMA AND close < yearly VWAP AND ATR / close ≥ the activity threshold, with that threshold multiplied by a relaxation factor in the short-duration chart regime AND (location gate disabled OR near an unmitigated bearish order block OR near the latest swing low OR near the resistance band OR near yearly VWAP) AND (no prior A- or B-tier signal OR bars since the latest signal of either side ≥ cooldown) AND ((directional volume < the negative of its threshold AND relative volume ≥ its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA)) OR (B tier enabled AND ((directional volume < the negative of its threshold AND relative volume < its threshold) OR (directional volume < the negative of a fraction of its threshold AND relative volume ≥ its threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR B-tier higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA))).
