Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low + minimum price increment), starting from zero. Treat the minimum price increment as a parameter.
Calculation: Flow rate = (cumulative volume delta − its value at the momentum lookback) / (absolute value of its value at that lookback + minimum price increment). Price rate = (close − close at the same lookback) / (close at that lookback + minimum price increment).
Calculation: Classifier features = rolling z-scores of flow rate, price rate minus flow rate, and the linear regression slope of cumulative volume delta. Use the same normalization window for all three; return zero for a feature when its rolling standard deviation is not positive.
Calculation: Classifier posteriors = three-class Gaussian naive Bayes probabilities for the current features. Classify each training bar as bullish when price rate and flow rate are both positive, bearish when both are negative, and divergent otherwise. Starting at the bar whose zero-based index equals the normalization window plus the momentum lookback plus two, add the previous bar's class and features to expanding class statistics before calculating the current posterior. For each class and feature, use the population mean and population variance; use mean zero for an empty class, standard deviation one for fewer than two observations, and otherwise floor variance at a small positive constant. Multiply the three Gaussian densities by the class's fraction of all training observations; divide each weighted likelihood by the sum of all three weighted likelihoods plus a small positive constant. Total training observations is the sum of the three class counts.
