Calculation: Modeled buy fraction = the Student-t or normal cumulative distribution function of the current close change divided by the rolling standard deviation of close changes; use zero as the standardized change when the standard deviation is not positive.
Calculation: Classified buy and sell volume = either modeled buy fraction times volume and its remainder, or, when enabled and available, the sums of volume from completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar whose close is respectively above or below their open; ignore lower-timeframe bars with equal open and close. Fall back to the modeled split when their combined volume is zero or no lower-timeframe bars are available.
Calculation: Toxicity = percentile rank over a rolling window of the selected raw toxicity. Bar-clock raw toxicity is the rolling average of absolute classified buy-minus-sell volume divided by the greater of the rolling average volume and one. Volume-clock raw toxicity accumulates classified buy volume, classified sell volume, and total chart-bar volume until accumulated total volume reaches the current bucket target, defined as a rolling average of volume multiplied by a bucket-size parameter; then record the absolute accumulated buy-minus-sell volume and reset all accumulators without carrying excess volume forward. Divide the average of the most recent recorded bucket imbalances, up to the toxicity-window length, by the current bucket target. Use bar-clock raw toxicity when volume-clock calculation is disabled, no bucket is recorded, or the bucket target is not positive.
Calculation: VSA volume measure = volume, or, when the time-of-day baseline is enabled, volume divided by the updated exponential average volume for the current time-of-day slot; initialize each slot average with its first available volume and use one when the updated average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: VSA close position = (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), or one-half when high equals low.
Calculation: Upthrust = volume > 0 AND high >= the highest high of the preceding extreme-lookback bars AND VSA close position <= the lower close-position threshold AND percentile rank of VSA volume measure over the VSA history window >= the high-volume threshold.
Calculation: Shakeout = volume > 0 AND low <= the lowest low of the preceding extreme-lookback bars AND VSA close position >= the upper close-position threshold AND percentile rank of VSA volume measure over the VSA history window >= the high-volume threshold.
Calculation: Stopping volume = volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND percentile rank of high minus low over the VSA history window >= the wide-spread threshold AND percentile rank of VSA volume measure over the VSA history window >= the very-high-volume threshold AND VSA close position >= the stopping close-position threshold.
Calculation: Buying climax = volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND VSA volume measure >= its highest value over the VSA history window including the current bar AND percentile rank of high minus low over the VSA history window >= the climax spread threshold.
Calculation: Selling climax = volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND VSA volume measure >= its highest value over the VSA history window including the current bar AND percentile rank of high minus low over the VSA history window >= the climax spread threshold.
Calculation: No demand = volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND percentile rank of high minus low over the VSA history window <= the narrow-spread threshold AND percentile rank of VSA volume measure over the VSA history window <= the low-volume threshold.
Calculation: No supply = volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND percentile rank of high minus low over the VSA history window <= the narrow-spread threshold AND percentile rank of VSA volume measure over the VSA history window <= the low-volume threshold.
Long: Toxicity crosses above the toxic threshold AND modeled buy fraction >= one-half.
Short: Toxicity crosses above the toxic threshold AND modeled buy fraction < one-half.
Long: Selling climax OR shakeout OR stopping volume OR no supply.
Short: Buying climax OR upthrust OR no demand AND NOT (selling climax OR shakeout OR stopping volume OR no supply) OR upthrust AND NOT buying climax AND NOT selling climax.
