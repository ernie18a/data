Calculation: Signed cumulative volume = cumulatively add volume when close > open, subtract volume when close < open, and add zero otherwise; reset to the current bar's signed volume at each calendar-day boundary.
Calculation: Volume composite = clamp the Hull moving average of a weighted sum of normalized money flow index of close, normalized percentile rank of signed cumulative volume, and clamped normalized z-score of on-balance volume rate of change, scaled to a symmetric range. Normalize money flow index and percentile rank around their midpoints; compute the z-score using a simple moving average and standard deviation of the rate of change.
Calculation: Confirmed composite pivot = a composite high or low with the configured number of bars on each side; evaluate it only when the right-side bars have completed. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed composite pivot of the same type, including pivots that did not produce a divergence. Measure their separation as the number of bars since the preceding pivot confirmation on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Bull zone = for each confirmed bullish divergence, retain the lowest low and lowest candle-body bottom over a lookback ending at the divergence pivot. Bear zone = for each confirmed bearish divergence, retain the highest high and highest candle-body top over a lookback ending at the divergence pivot. On each bar, including the creation bar, remove a zone if its age measured from the pivot exceeds the maximum age, or if its edge is breached: for a bull zone, close below the lowest low in body mode or low below it in wick mode; for a bear zone, close above the highest high in body mode or high above it in wick mode. In body mode with rejection enabled, require both the current and preceding close to breach the same edge. Test touches only against zones remaining after removal.
Long: A composite pivot low is confirmed AND its separation from the preceding composite pivot low is within the configured inclusive range AND ((its price low < the preceding pivot's price low AND its composite > the preceding pivot's composite) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its price low > the preceding pivot's price low AND its composite < the preceding pivot's composite)) AND (fair-value-gap filtering is disabled OR a bar within the configured lookback, including the current bar, has low > high two bars earlier).
Short: A composite pivot high is confirmed AND its separation from the preceding composite pivot high is within the configured inclusive range AND ((its price high > the preceding pivot's price high AND its composite < the preceding pivot's composite) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its price high < the preceding pivot's price high AND its composite > the preceding pivot's composite)) AND (fair-value-gap filtering is disabled OR a bar within the configured lookback, including the current bar, has high < low two bars earlier).
Long: Current high >= a remaining bull zone's lower boundary AND current low <= its upper boundary AND close > open.
Short: Current high >= a remaining bear zone's lower boundary AND current low <= its upper boundary AND close < open.
Long: The count of consecutive bars with volume composite > zero equals the configured streak length.
Short: The count of consecutive bars with volume composite < zero equals the configured streak length.
Long: Volume composite >= its exponential moving average.
Short: Volume composite < its exponential moving average.
Long: Volume composite >= zero OR volume composite > the positive composite threshold OR money flow index of close > its upper threshold OR percentile rank of signed cumulative volume > its upper threshold OR z-score of on-balance volume rate of change > its upper threshold.
Short: Volume composite < zero OR volume composite < the negative composite threshold OR money flow index of close < its lower threshold OR percentile rank of signed cumulative volume < its lower threshold OR z-score of on-balance volume rate of change < its lower threshold.
Exit: long, a bull zone is removed by its breach condition or maximum age.
Exit: short, a bear zone is removed by its breach condition or maximum age.
