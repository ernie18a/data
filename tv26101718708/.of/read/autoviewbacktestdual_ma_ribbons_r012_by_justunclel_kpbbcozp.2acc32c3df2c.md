Calculation: Each ribbon has a shorter-lookback and a longer-lookback moving average of chart-bar closes, using the same selected moving-average type within that ribbon; the fast ribbon uses shorter lookbacks than the slow ribbon. The anchor multiplier scales the lookbacks on the chart timeframe. For a Laguerre average, the shorter-lookback member uses a smaller gamma than the longer-lookback member.
Calculation: Ribbon spread = shorter-lookback moving average minus longer-lookback moving average. Ribbon midpoint = the mean of its two moving averages. Midpoint spread = fast ribbon midpoint minus slow ribbon midpoint.
Calculation: A spread is rising-positive when it is greater than zero and greater than its previous-bar value; it is falling-negative when it is less than zero and less than its previous-bar value.
Calculation: Effective high = high and effective low = low; in Renko mode, effective high = max(open, close) and effective low = min(open, close).
Calculation: Running high = maximum effective high since the internal trade state became long, including the current bar. Running low = minimum effective low since the internal trade state became short, including the current bar.
Long: Either ribbon's spread is rising-positive on the current and previous bars.
Short: Either ribbon's spread is falling-negative on the current and previous bars.
Long: Internal trade state is flat AND no close occurred on the previous bar AND the fast ribbon spread, slow ribbon spread, and midpoint spread are each rising-positive on the current and previous bars AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND long trading is enabled AND flip mode is disabled.
Short: Internal trade state is flat AND no close occurred on the previous bar AND the fast ribbon spread, slow ribbon spread, and midpoint spread are each falling-negative on the current and previous bars AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND short trading is enabled AND flip mode is disabled.
Long: Internal trade state is flat AND no close occurred on the previous bar AND the fast ribbon spread, slow ribbon spread, and midpoint spread are each falling-negative on the current and previous bars AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND flip mode is enabled.
Short: Internal trade state is flat AND no close occurred on the previous bar AND the fast ribbon spread, slow ribbon spread, and midpoint spread are each rising-positive on the current and previous bars AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND flip mode is enabled.
Calculation: The internal trade state becomes long on the rising-positive entry trigger and short on the falling-negative entry trigger, even in flip mode; it becomes flat after an internal close. Entry price is the close on the corresponding actual entry signal. Internal closes are suppressed on the entry-signal bar.
Exit: long, internal trade state is long AND trailing stop is enabled AND effective low <= running high * (1 - trailing-stop fraction).
Exit: long, internal trade state is long AND take profit is enabled AND running high >= entry price * (1 + profit-target fraction) AND effective low <= running high * (1 - trailing-profit fraction); trailing-profit fraction <= profit-target fraction.
Exit: long, internal trade state is long AND stop loss is enabled AND effective low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, internal trade state is long AND opposite-signal exit is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled AND previous-bar fast midpoint < previous-bar slow midpoint AND open < previous-bar slow midpoint AND this pair of comparisons was false on the previous bar.
Exit: short, internal trade state is short AND trailing stop is enabled AND effective high >= running low * (1 + trailing-stop fraction).
Exit: short, internal trade state is short AND take profit is enabled AND running low <= entry price * (1 - profit-target fraction) AND effective high >= running low * (1 + trailing-profit fraction); trailing-profit fraction <= profit-target fraction.
Exit: short, internal trade state is short AND stop loss is enabled AND effective high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, internal trade state is short AND opposite-signal exit is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled AND previous-bar fast midpoint > previous-bar slow midpoint AND open > previous-bar slow midpoint AND this pair of comparisons was false on the previous bar.
