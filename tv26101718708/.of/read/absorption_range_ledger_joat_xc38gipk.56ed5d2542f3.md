Calculation: Participation zones = On each bar with volume greater than its volume SMA multiplied by a participation threshold, find the oldest active zone whose range expanded on both sides by ATR multiplied by a tolerance overlaps the bar's high-low range. If none exists, create a zone with top = high, bottom = low, start = current bar, bull volume = volume × bull share, bear volume = volume × (1 − bull share), and equilibrium = ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) / max(volume, 1). Otherwise, update that first matching zone: top = max(old top, high), bottom = min(old bottom, low), add the bar's bull and bear volumes, and set equilibrium = (old equilibrium × old total volume + (high + low + close) / 3 × volume) / max(new total volume, 1). Bull share uses body position = (max(close, open) − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low; it is max(0.55, body position) when close >= open and min(0.45, body position) otherwise. After creation or merging, deactivate each zone when its age since creation exceeds the zone lifetime, or close > zone top AND its balance >= 0, or close < zone bottom AND its balance <= 0. Balance = (bull volume − bear volume) / total volume, or 0 when total volume <= 0. Select the remaining active zone with greatest absolute balance; break ties in favor of the most recently created zone. Its top, bottom, balance, and equilibrium are the dominant zone values.
Long: A dominant zone exists AND its balance > a positive balance threshold.
Short: A dominant zone exists AND its balance < the negative of the balance threshold.
Long: A dominant zone exists AND close > its top.
Short: A dominant zone exists AND close < its bottom.
Long: A dominant zone exists AND close > its equilibrium AND previous close <= the previous bar's dominant equilibrium.
Short: A dominant zone exists AND close < its equilibrium AND previous close >= the previous bar's dominant equilibrium.
