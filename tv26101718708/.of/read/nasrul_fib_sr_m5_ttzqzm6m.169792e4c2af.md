Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivots and leg = Process the last completed higher-timeframe bar once when a new higher-timeframe bar begins. Initialize the last pivot and swing low to its low, direction up, and active extreme to its high. Before updating the extreme, change an up leg to down if close < last swing low, or a down leg to up if close > last swing high; reset the extreme to that bar's low or high respectively. In an up leg, update the extreme and its bar whenever high >= extreme; confirm it as a swing high when extreme - low >= the selected deviation threshold, bars since the last pivot confirmation >= depth, and the absolute distance in bars between the extreme and last pivot confirmation >= backstep. In a down leg, apply the symmetric rules using low <= extreme and high - extreme >= threshold. The threshold is either higher-timeframe ATR times a multiplier or the extreme times a percentage. On confirmation, record the pivot and its extreme's time, set the last pivot confirmation bar to the current bar, reverse direction, and initialize the new extreme to the current low or high. Retain the latest confirmed swing high and low. Initially trend is choppy; after each confirmation, if two highs and two lows exist, trend is up when both latest pivots exceed their preceding same-type pivots, down when both are lower, choppy when both pairs are equal, and range otherwise; without two of each it is choppy.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Fibonacci levels = On each processed higher-timeframe bar, form a leg from the latest two confirmed pivot prices, extending its high with that bar's high when direction is up or its low with that bar's low when direction is down. With only one pivot, use that pivot and the current high for a low pivot in an up leg or the current low for a high pivot in a down leg; otherwise both ends equal the pivot. If leg size >= completed higher-timeframe ATR times a minimum-leg multiplier, set the Fibonacci origin to the high for an up leg or low for a down leg, and its other endpoint to the opposite extreme. Retain prior levels when the leg fails this test. On each chart bar, extend the stored leg high or low in the direction of the leg using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, and, if its size >= the still-forming higher-timeframe ATR times the multiplier, refresh the Fibonacci levels from that extended leg. The levels are the 50% and 61.8% interpolations from origin to opposite endpoint; otherwise retain the prior available levels.
Calculation: Level hit = For a selected level, either absolute chart close minus level <= chart ATR times a proximity multiplier, the chart candle body overlaps level plus or minus that tolerance, or the chart high-low range overlaps that interval, according to the selected confirmation mode.
Calculation: Patterns = Chart range = high - low, body = abs(close - open), upper wick = high - max(open, close), and lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Every pattern requires range >= chart ATR times a minimum-range multiplier. Bullish pin: lower wick >= 2 times body, upper wick <= 0.5 times body, and range > 0; bearish pin reverses the wicks. Bullish engulfing: close > open, prior close < prior open, close >= prior open, and open <= prior close; bearish engulfing reverses these inequalities. An inside bar has prior high < high two bars ago and prior low > low two bars ago. Bullish inside breakout: inside bar and close > high two bars ago and prior close <= that high; bearish: inside bar and close < low two bars ago and prior close >= that low. Bullish false breakout: inside bar, low < low two bars ago, and close strictly between the high and low two bars ago; bearish: inside bar, high > high two bars ago, and close strictly between those levels. Each pattern type can be enabled independently.
Calculation: Confluence = For each side, add one for a hit on either Fibonacci level, one for a hit on the latest swing low for long or high for short, one for simultaneous hits on the 50% Fibonacci level and swing low for long or the 61.8% Fibonacci level and swing high for short, and, when the moving-average filter is enabled, one for the shorter EMA above the longer EMA for long or below it for short. For a breakout setup also add one when close > latest swing high for long or close < latest swing low for short. If a valid reversal and breakout coexist, use the reversal score and threshold.
Calculation: Order levels = Select the first matching raw pattern in priority order pin, engulfing, false breakout, inside breakout, regardless of whether that pattern is enabled; otherwise select none. For market or trend-only mode, reference entry = signal close. For limit mode, reference entry = signal range midpoint for pin or engulfing, mother-bar range midpoint for false or inside breakout, and signal close otherwise. Long stop = signal low minus chart ATR times a padding multiplier for pin or engulfing; otherwise it is mother-bar low minus padding if the preceding bar is inside, or signal low minus padding. Short stop is symmetric using highs and adding padding. Long target = latest swing high if above reference entry, otherwise reference entry plus absolute entry-to-stop distance times the minimum reward-to-risk ratio; short target = latest swing low if below reference entry, otherwise reference entry minus that distance times the ratio. Fix stop and target when an order is submitted. A limit order remains pending until filled or its expiration in chart bars; a new same-side signal replaces it.
Long: Chart bar is completed AND choppy trading is enabled OR trend is not choppy, grouped as a single filter; AND trend-only entry mode is disabled OR shorter EMA > longer EMA, grouped as a single filter; AND confluence >= the applicable threshold AND ((reversal mode is enabled AND an enabled bullish pin, engulfing, or false breakout occurs AND a Fibonacci level or latest swing low is hit AND (reversal trend restriction is disabled OR countertrend is enabled OR trend is up OR trend is range)) OR (breakout mode is enabled AND (enabled bullish inside breakout occurs OR close > latest swing high AND prior close <= that same swing high AND chart range >= chart ATR times the minimum-range multiplier) AND (countertrend is enabled OR trend is up))).
Short: Chart bar is completed AND choppy trading is enabled OR trend is not choppy, grouped as a single filter; AND trend-only entry mode is disabled OR shorter EMA < longer EMA, grouped as a single filter; AND confluence >= the applicable threshold AND ((reversal mode is enabled AND an enabled bearish pin, engulfing, or false breakout occurs AND a Fibonacci level or latest swing high is hit AND (reversal trend restriction is disabled OR countertrend is enabled OR trend is down OR trend is range)) OR (breakout mode is enabled AND (enabled bearish inside breakout occurs OR close < latest swing low AND prior close >= that same swing low AND chart range >= chart ATR times the minimum-range multiplier) AND (countertrend is enabled OR trend is down))).
Calculation: Executable order = A directional setup can submit an order when position size = 0 AND absolute reference-entry-to-stop distance > 0 AND absolute target-to-reference-entry distance >= that risk distance times the minimum reward-to-risk ratio AND calculated risk-based quantity > 0. Market orders execute on the next available tick; limit orders execute when their limit is reached.
Exit: long, low <= fixed long stop OR high >= fixed long target.
Exit: short, high >= fixed short stop OR low <= fixed short target.
