Calculation: Chart ATR = average true range of chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Short/long moving averages = chart-timeframe exponential moving averages, with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: M30 ZigZag pivots = process higher-timeframe bars only when a new M30 bar begins, using the last completed M30 bar. Track alternating extremes; confirm a pivot after reversal exceeds either a percentage of the extreme or an ATR multiple, subject to minimum bars since the prior pivot and minimum spacing from its extreme. A close beyond the last opposing swing flips the leg direction.
Calculation: M30 trend = from the two latest confirmed swing highs and lows on completed M30 bars: up when both latest swings are higher, down when both are lower, choppy when neither is higher nor lower, otherwise range.
Calculation: M30 swing levels = latest confirmed swing high and low from completed M30 bars; a swing high becomes resistance and a swing low becomes support, with a level changing type after a completed M30 close crosses it.
Calculation: Active M30 Fibonacci 50% and 61.8% = interpolate those percentages from the active ZigZag leg, with Fib 0 at the leg high and Fib 100 at the leg low for an upward leg, reversed for a downward leg. Require leg size to meet a minimum M30 ATR multiple. Refresh using the still-forming M30 high or low as the active extreme; otherwise retain the last valid levels.
Calculation: Chart-bar level contact = within an ATR-scaled tolerance of a level by close distance, candle-body overlap, or high/low overlap, according to the selected confirmation mode.
Calculation: Bullish reversal patterns = (bar range meets an ATR-scaled minimum and lower wick is at least twice the body and upper wick at most half the body) OR (bar range meets the minimum, current bar bullish, prior bar bearish, current close at/above prior open and current open at/below prior close) OR (bar range meets the minimum, prior bar is inside the bar before it, current low breaks the mother-bar low and current close returns inside the mother-bar range).
Calculation: Bearish reversal patterns = (bar range meets an ATR-scaled minimum and upper wick is at least twice the body and lower wick at most half the body) OR (bar range meets the minimum, current bar bearish, prior bar bullish, current close at/below prior open and current open at/above prior close) OR (bar range meets the minimum, prior bar is inside the bar before it, current high breaks the mother-bar high and current close returns inside the mother-bar range).
Calculation: Bullish inside-bar breakout = bar range meets the ATR-scaled minimum, prior bar is inside the bar before it, current close is above the mother-bar high, and prior close was at/below it. Bearish inside-bar breakout = same inside-bar and range conditions, current close below the mother-bar low, and prior close was at/above it.
Calculation: Long confluence score = 1 if either Fib level is contacted + 1 if support is contacted + 1 if both Fib 50% and support are contacted + 1 if the short moving average is above the long moving average when MA confluence is enabled; for breakouts, add 1 if close is above M30 swing high.
Calculation: Short confluence score = 1 if either Fib level is contacted + 1 if resistance is contacted + 1 if both Fib 61.8% and resistance are contacted + 1 if the short moving average is below the long moving average when MA confluence is enabled; for breakouts, add 1 if close is below M30 swing low.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low minus an ATR-scaled pad, except an inside-bar breakout uses mother-bar low minus the pad. Short stop = signal-bar high plus the pad, except an inside-bar breakout uses mother-bar high plus the pad.
Calculation: Entry reference = signal-bar close in market or trend-only mode; in limit mode use the signal-bar midpoint for pin bars or engulfing bars, and the mother-bar midpoint for false breakouts or inside-bar breakouts.
Calculation: Long target = M30 swing high if above entry; otherwise entry plus risk distance times the minimum reward/risk ratio. Short target = M30 swing low if below entry; otherwise entry minus risk distance times the minimum reward/risk ratio.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, bullish reversal pattern AND contact with Fib 50%, Fib 61.8%, or support AND (reversal setup enabled) AND (M30 trend is up or range, or countertrend/reversal trend restriction is disabled), OR bullish inside-bar breakout or close crossing above M30 swing high from at/below it AND (breakout setup enabled) AND (M30 trend is up or countertrend trading is enabled); AND choppy M30 trend is allowed or trend is not choppy; AND short moving average is above long moving average if trend-only entry mode is selected; AND the applicable long confluence score meets its reversal or breakout minimum; AND risk is positive and target reward is at least risk times the minimum reward/risk ratio; AND position is flat for strategy entry.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, bearish reversal pattern AND contact with Fib 50%, Fib 61.8%, or resistance AND (reversal setup enabled) AND (M30 trend is down or range, or countertrend/reversal trend restriction is disabled), OR bearish inside-bar breakout or close crossing below M30 swing low from at/above it AND (breakout setup enabled) AND (M30 trend is down or countertrend trading is enabled); AND choppy M30 trend is allowed or trend is not choppy; AND short moving average is below long moving average if trend-only entry mode is selected; AND the applicable short confluence score meets its reversal or breakout minimum; AND risk is positive and target reward is at least risk times the minimum reward/risk ratio; AND position is flat for strategy entry.
Exit: Long, chart-bar low <= long stop OR chart-bar high >= long target.
Exit: Short, chart-bar high >= short stop OR chart-bar low <= short target.
