Calculation: Smoothed higher-timeframe MACD and signal = calculate MACD from the shorter- and longer-period moving averages of close and its signal using the same selectable moving-average type; request each from the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time. Apply a chart-timeframe SMA to each requested series, with length equal to the rounded ratio of higher to chart timeframe durations.
Calculation: Smoothed higher-timeframe trend average = calculate a selectable moving average of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time; apply a chart-timeframe SMA with length equal to the rounded ratio of higher to chart timeframe durations.
Long: Smoothed higher-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal AND smoothed higher-timeframe MACD < 0 AND smoothed higher-timeframe trend average > its previous chart-bar value AND (session filter disabled OR bar opening and closing times are within the configured session and selected weekdays).
Short: Smoothed higher-timeframe MACD crosses below its signal AND smoothed higher-timeframe MACD > 0 AND smoothed higher-timeframe trend average < its previous chart-bar value AND (session filter disabled OR bar opening and closing times are within the configured session and selected weekdays).
Exit: Long, smoothed higher-timeframe MACD crosses below its signal OR (session-end exit enabled AND session filter enabled AND bar opening or closing time is outside the configured session or selected weekdays).
Exit: Short, smoothed higher-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal OR (session-end exit enabled AND session filter enabled AND bar opening or closing time is outside the configured session or selected weekdays).
