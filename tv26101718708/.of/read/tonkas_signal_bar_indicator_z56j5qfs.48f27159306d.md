Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low of the most recently completed configured session, using chart bars whose opening times fall within that session in the configured timezone.
Long: low < low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND close > that low
Long: low < session low AND close > session low
Short: high > high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND close < that high
Short: high > session high AND close < session high
