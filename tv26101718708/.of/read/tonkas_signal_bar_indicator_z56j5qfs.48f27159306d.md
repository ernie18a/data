Calculation: Average body size = mean of absolute close-minus-open over a longer lookback
Calculation: Body fraction beyond level = the fraction of the candle body beyond a crossed level, clamped to [0,1]
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Long: An enabled positive manual level exists AND ((close > open AND close > level AND body fraction beyond level >= configured minimum) OR (previous candle touched the level within configured tick tolerance AND close > open AND close > level AND previous candle closed below its open AND close > previous candle body high)) AND current body size > average body size × configured displacement multiplier AND current body size >= candle range × configured minimum body fraction AND close > the highest high of the prior bars over the configured short lookback
Short: An enabled positive manual level exists AND ((close < open AND close < level AND body fraction beyond level >= configured minimum) OR (previous candle touched the level within configured tick tolerance AND close < open AND close < level AND previous candle closed above its open AND close < previous candle body low)) AND current body size > average body size × configured displacement multiplier AND current body size >= candle range × configured minimum body fraction AND close < the lowest low of the prior bars over the configured short lookback
