Calculation: Keltner Channel upper/lower bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × SMA(True Range, same lookback)
Long: for each of four configured higher timeframes, the still-forming bar’s open < close
Short: for each of four configured higher timeframes, the still-forming bar’s open ≥ close
Exit: long, chart-timeframe close < Keltner Channel upper band
Exit: short, chart-timeframe close > Keltner Channel lower band
