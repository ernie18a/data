Calculation: Close location value = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Flow score = (CLV weight × 100 × SMA of close location value + RSI weight × 2 × (close RSI − 50) + CMF weight × 100 × Chaikin Money Flow) / sum of weights; use 0 when the sum of weights is 0. Chaikin Money Flow is the lookback sum of close location value × volume divided by the lookback sum of volume; use 0 when that volume sum is 0.
Calculation: Pivot age = number of bars since the most recent confirmation of a pivot high or low, respectively; a pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-side bars. Use an effectively infinite age before the first confirmation.
Long: Flow score >= 0.
Short: Flow score < 0.
Long: Flow score crosses above the bullish threshold AND position side is not long AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= linearly interpolated volume percentile over the volume lookback) AND (pivot filter is disabled OR pivot-low age <= maximum pivot age) AND (exhaustion filter is disabled OR (close RSI < overbought threshold AND minimum flow score over the regime lookback < 0)).
Short: Short signals are enabled AND flow score crosses below the bearish threshold AND position side is not short AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= linearly interpolated volume percentile over the volume lookback) AND (pivot filter is disabled OR pivot-high age <= maximum pivot age) AND (exhaustion filter is disabled OR (close RSI > oversold threshold AND maximum flow score over the regime lookback > 0)).
Exit: long, flow score crosses below the bearish threshold AND (exhaustion filter is disabled OR (close RSI > oversold threshold AND maximum flow score over the regime lookback > 0)) AND (short signals are disabled OR (volume filter is enabled AND volume < linearly interpolated volume percentile over the volume lookback) OR (pivot filter is enabled AND pivot-high age > maximum pivot age)).
