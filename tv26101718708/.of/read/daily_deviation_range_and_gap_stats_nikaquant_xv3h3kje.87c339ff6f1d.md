Calculation: Session range = highest and lowest close of completed internal-timeframe bars (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as applicable) during the configured range window; midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2; effective range = actual range or, when normalization is enabled, the average of prior completed session ranges over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Deviation level prices = session range high + effective range × level multiplier (upper) and session range low − effective range × level multiplier (lower).
Calculation: Historical side-and-level revert rate = count of prior sessions that touched that side and later revisited the midpoint before extension end, divided by prior sessions that touched that side; also track the touch count.
Calculation: Gap price = close of the completed internal-timeframe bar at the configured gap time; the gap is open until a subsequent completed internal-timeframe bar in the configured fill window has high ≥ gap price ≥ low. Historical gap fill rate = filled prior gaps / prior gaps set.
Calculation: Remaining extension minutes = time from the current bar to the configured extension end.
Long: Extension active AND remaining extension minutes is not between 0 and the configured minimum AND ((close < session midpoint AND the lower deviation level selected as the furthest level already touched today is within 5% of the absolute close-to-midpoint distance AND its historical side-and-level touch count ≥ 3 AND its historical side-and-level revert rate ≥ the configured minimum) OR (gap is open AND gap price > close AND close > session midpoint AND close < gap price AND prior gap count ≥ 3 AND historical gap fill rate ≥ the configured minimum)).
Short: Extension active AND remaining extension minutes is not between 0 and the configured minimum AND ((close > session midpoint AND the upper deviation level selected as the furthest level already touched today is within 5% of the absolute close-to-midpoint distance AND its historical side-and-level touch count ≥ 3 AND its historical side-and-level revert rate ≥ the configured minimum) OR (gap is open AND gap price < close AND close < session midpoint AND gap price < close AND prior gap count ≥ 3 AND historical gap fill rate ≥ the configured minimum)).
