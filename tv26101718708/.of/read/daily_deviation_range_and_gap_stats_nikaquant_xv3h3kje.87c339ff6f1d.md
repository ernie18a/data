Calculation: Internal bars = completed bars of a configurable timeframe; this timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. When higher or equal, process the last completed internal bar once; when lower, process every completed internal bar within the chart bar in chronological order.
Calculation: Session range = highest and lowest internal-bar closes in the configured New York range window; midline = their average; actual range = their difference. Effective range = actual range, or, when normalization is enabled and a positive average is available, the average actual range over a configurable number of completed sessions. Effective high and low = midline plus and minus half the effective range. For each configured ordered level multiple, upper level = effective high + multiple × effective range and lower level = effective low − multiple × effective range.
Calculation: Session touches = for each level and side, record whether an internal-bar high reaches the upper level or an internal-bar low reaches the lower level during the configured extension window. Record a midline revisit if, after any touch including on the same internal bar, that bar's low ≤ midline ≤ high. At extension end, mark every level touched that session as reverted if any midline revisit occurred, including levels touched after the revisit. The selected level is the highest-index touched level on the side of the current chart close relative to the midline. Historical touch count and revert count are the counts for that same side and level over a configurable lookback of completed sessions; revert rate is their ratio expressed as a percentage and rounded to the nearest integer.
Calculation: Gap = close of the completed internal bar at a configured New York time. It remains active until replaced at the next trigger or filled by a subsequent internal bar whose low ≤ gap ≤ high during the configured gap-fill window. At each new trigger, record the preceding gap and whether it filled; historical gap fill rate = filled gaps divided by recorded gaps over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Time blocked = extension is active AND a positive minimum-remaining-time parameter is enabled AND remaining time from the chart bar's opening time to extension end is positive and less than that parameter.
Calculation: Fade stop = the next higher-index deviation level on the selected side, or the outermost level extended by a configurable range-multiple buffer when the selected level is outermost.
Calculation: Daily bias = rounded percentage of completed sessions in a configurable lookback whose final internal-bar close during the extension was above that session's midline; use a neutral percentage when no history exists.
Long: Chart close < midline AND effective range > 0.
Short: Chart close > midline AND effective range > 0.
Long: A lower level was touched this session AND no upper level was touched this session.
Short: An upper level was touched this session AND no lower level was touched this session.
Long: Daily bias ≥ an upper bias threshold.
Short: Daily bias ≤ a lower bias threshold.
Long: Gap is active AND chart close < gap.
Short: Gap is active AND chart close > gap.
Long: Chart close ≤ midline AND effective range > 0 AND a selected lower level exists AND absolute distance of chart close from midline divided by effective range ≥ selected level multiple × a configurable proximity factor AND historical touch count ≥ a configurable minimum AND revert rate ≥ a configurable threshold AND time blocked = false AND the qualified gap-target setup is absent.
Short: Chart close > midline AND effective range > 0 AND a selected upper level exists AND absolute distance of chart close from midline divided by effective range ≥ selected level multiple × a configurable proximity factor AND historical touch count ≥ a configurable minimum AND revert rate ≥ a configurable threshold AND time blocked = false AND the qualified gap-target setup is absent.
Calculation: Qualified gap-target setup = gap is active AND chart close is strictly between midline and gap AND historical recorded gap count ≥ a configurable minimum AND historical gap fill rate ≥ the fade revert-rate threshold AND time blocked = false.
Long: Qualified gap-target setup = true AND gap > chart close.
Short: Qualified gap-target setup = true AND gap < chart close.
Exit: long, fade position AND chart price ≥ session midline OR fade position AND chart price ≤ fade stop OR gap-target position AND chart price ≥ gap.
Exit: short, fade position AND chart price ≤ session midline OR fade position AND chart price ≥ fade stop OR gap-target position AND chart price ≤ gap.
