Calculation: Opening range = the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within either a configurable interval beginning at the daily session open or a configurable custom session; freeze the range when that interval ends. Opening price is the open of its first bar.
Calculation: ADR at opening = the simple moving average of daily high minus low, using the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar and frozen at the opening-range start.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = the EMA of close calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar, merged without lookahead.
Calculation: Directional strength = clamp to the score bounds the rounded sum of a base score and these components: a tiered score for absolute candle body divided by candle range; a tiered score for candle range divided by ATR; a tiered score for volume divided by its simple moving average; a score depending on whether both, one, or neither of the short- and longer-lookback close changes agree with the direction; a tiered score for directional penetration beyond the opening-range edge divided by ATR; an RSI agreement score; a higher-timeframe trend agreement score; and a score for close being on the directional side of the opening price. Each tier has its own configurable threshold and weight. Use a neutral body ratio when the candle range is zero, a neutral volume ratio when average volume is not positive, and zero for the range-to-ATR and penetration ratios when ATR is not positive. For the trend agreement component, count agreement when the optional trend filter is disabled or when close is on the directional side of the higher-timeframe trend.
Calculation: Initial risk = the absolute distance from entry close to the selected raw stop, clamped between configurable lower and upper multiples of entry-bar ATR. The raw stop is either entry close minus ATR times a multiplier for a long or plus it for a short, the opening-range midpoint, or the opposite opening-range edge. Initial stop is entry minus risk for a long and entry plus risk for a short; each of three independently parameterized targets is entry plus its risk multiple for a long and entry minus it for a short.
Calculation: Trade state = evaluate targets and the current stop only on bars after entry. A target is hit when high reaches or exceeds it for a long or low reaches or falls below it for a short; a stop is hit when low reaches or falls below it for a long or high reaches or exceeds it for a short. Track the highest numbered target hit; do not update that stage on a bar that also hits the pre-update stop. If break-even is enabled, move the stop to entry when target one is hit without a stop hit, and apply the new stop starting on the next bar.
Long: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trade window AND position is flat AND either the daily trade limit is disabled OR no trade has opened since the daily boundary AND either the close trigger is selected and close > opening-range high OR the wick trigger is selected and high > opening-range high AND low >= opening-range low AND either the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR close > higher-timeframe trend AND either the ADR filter is disabled OR opening-range width divided by ADR at opening is within its inclusive configured bounds AND directional strength >= its minimum AND ATR is available.
Short: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trade window AND position is flat AND either the daily trade limit is disabled OR no trade has opened since the daily boundary AND either the close trigger is selected and close < opening-range low OR the wick trigger is selected and low < opening-range low AND high <= opening-range high AND either the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR close < higher-timeframe trend AND either the ADR filter is disabled OR opening-range width divided by ADR at opening is within its inclusive configured bounds AND directional strength >= its minimum AND ATR is available.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has high >= the third target AND low > the current stop OR a bar after entry has low <= the current stop OR the trade window has just ended on a bar after entry.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has low <= the third target AND high < the current stop OR a bar after entry has high >= the current stop OR the trade window has just ended on a bar after entry.
