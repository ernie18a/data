Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 times volume, divided by cumulative volume; reset at the start of each selected session
Calculation: Reaction corridor = Session VWAP ± ATR × corridor-width parameter
Long: In selected session AND close > upper reaction corridor OR close > Session VWAP OR (a bullish cross above Session VWAP occurred reclaim-confirm-bars ago AND each of the last reclaim-confirm-bars closes was above its corresponding Session VWAP AND the minimum prior-side bars before the cross closed below their corresponding Session VWAP) OR (the minimum prior-side bars closed above their corresponding Session VWAP AND low ≤ lower reaction corridor AND close > Session VWAP AND close > open AND Session VWAP ≥ its value slope-lookback bars ago) OR (the minimum prior-side bars closed above their corresponding Session VWAP AND low ≤ Session VWAP AND close > Session VWAP AND close > open AND no bullish reclaim or bounce qualifies)
Short: In selected session AND close < lower reaction corridor OR close < Session VWAP OR (a bearish cross below Session VWAP occurred reclaim-confirm-bars ago AND each of the last reclaim-confirm-bars closes was below its corresponding Session VWAP AND the minimum prior-side bars before the cross closed above their corresponding Session VWAP) OR (the minimum prior-side bars closed below their corresponding Session VWAP AND high ≥ upper reaction corridor AND close < Session VWAP AND close < open AND Session VWAP ≤ its value slope-lookback bars ago) OR (the minimum prior-side bars closed below their corresponding Session VWAP AND high ≥ Session VWAP AND close < Session VWAP AND close < open AND no bearish reclaim or bounce qualifies)
