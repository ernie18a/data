Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each selected session; undefined when cumulative volume is zero or outside the session.
Calculation: Corridor half-width = a configurable multiple of the greater of ATR and a configurable positive price floor; use true range in place of ATR when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Prior above/below = every one of the specified number of immediately preceding closes is strictly above/below its corresponding session VWAP.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish reclaim = a close crosses strictly above/below session VWAP after prior below/above, followed by enough consecutive closes strictly on the new side to reach the specified confirmation count, including the crossing bar; evaluate true only on the final confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish bounce = in session AND bullish reclaim is false AND prior above AND low <= session VWAP - corridor half-width AND close > session VWAP AND close > open AND (slope filter disabled OR session VWAP >= its value at the specified lookback).
Calculation: Bearish bounce = in session AND bearish reclaim is false AND prior below AND high >= session VWAP + corridor half-width AND close < session VWAP AND close < open AND (slope filter disabled OR session VWAP <= its value at the specified lookback).
Calculation: Bullish reject = in session AND bullish reclaim is false AND bullish bounce is false AND prior above AND low <= session VWAP AND close > session VWAP AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish reject = in session AND bearish reclaim is false AND bearish bounce is false AND prior below AND high >= session VWAP AND close < session VWAP AND close < open.
Calculation: Accepted reaction = any bullish or bearish reclaim, bounce, or reject AND no reaction has previously been accepted OR the number of bars since the last accepted reaction is at least the cooldown; apply the cooldown to reactions of both directions together. Reclaims originate in session, and their consecutive confirmation closes must have defined session VWAP.
Calculation: Retained bias = the last in-session comparison of close with session VWAP, retained outside the session until the next in-session comparison; initially neutral.
Long: Accepted reaction AND (bullish reclaim OR bullish bounce OR bullish reject).
Short: Accepted reaction AND (bearish reclaim OR bearish bounce OR bearish reject).
Long: In session AND close > session VWAP OR outside session AND retained bias is above.
Short: In session AND close < session VWAP OR outside session AND retained bias is below.
