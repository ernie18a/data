Calculation: Body top = max(open, close); body bottom = min(open, close); range = high - low.
Calculation: ATR = ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Tick size is a configurable price increment.
Calculation: Wick minimum = max(configurable fraction of ATR, configurable number of ticks); opposite-wick maximum = max(configurable fraction of range, one tick); momentum threshold = max(configurable fraction of ATR, configurable number of ticks); level tolerance = max(configurable fraction of ATR, configurable number of ticks).
Calculation: Session levels = premarket high and low accumulated through the current bar while the premarket is open and frozen afterward; previous session high and low from either regular-session bars or all bars, rolled at the configured session boundary; previous regular-session close rolled at that boundary; current week high and low accumulated through the current bar; and high and low of the last completed overnight session. A running weekly level is eligible only after it has remained unchanged for a configurable number of chart bars; the same applies to a premarket level while the premarket is open. Frozen levels are eligible immediately.
Calculation: Camarilla inputs = high, low and close of the previous completed regular session or previous completed extended session. In automatic mode, use extended-session inputs if the previous postmarket or current premarket high exceeds the previous regular-session high, or its low falls below the previous regular-session low, by more than a configurable fraction of ATR; otherwise use regular-session inputs. Current premarket extremes include the current bar. The close can independently be forced to either session's close.
Calculation: Camarilla resistance levels = C + 1.1 × (H - L) / 4, C + 1.1 × (H - L) / 2, and C × H / L; Camarilla support levels = C - 1.1 × (H - L) / 4, C - 1.1 × (H - L) / 2, and 2 × C - C × H / L, where H, L and C are the Camarilla inputs.
Calculation: Long levels = eligible premarket low, previous session low, previous regular-session close, eligible current week low, optionally previous overnight low, optionally Camarilla support levels, and enabled positive custom price levels. Short levels = the corresponding highs, previous regular-session close, optionally Camarilla resistance levels, and enabled positive custom price levels.
Long: The chart bar is complete AND ATR is available AND range > 0 AND the bar is within the configured permitted session when session restriction is enabled AND low < the lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND body bottom >= high - a configurable fraction of range AND high - body top <= opposite-wick maximum AND body bottom - low >= wick minimum AND (previous-bar body containment is disabled OR body bottom >= previous low AND body top <= previous high) AND (hard prior-momentum filter is disabled OR previous close >= previous open OR previous open - previous close <= momentum threshold) AND (level confluence is optional OR at least one long level is >= low - level tolerance AND <= body bottom + level tolerance).
Short: The chart bar is complete AND ATR is available AND range > 0 AND the bar is within the configured permitted session when session restriction is enabled AND high > the highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND body top <= low + a configurable fraction of range AND body bottom - low <= opposite-wick maximum AND high - body top >= wick minimum AND (previous-bar body containment is disabled OR body bottom >= previous low AND body top <= previous high) AND (hard prior-momentum filter is disabled OR previous close <= previous open OR previous close - previous open <= momentum threshold) AND (level confluence is optional OR at least one short level is >= body top - level tolerance AND <= high + level tolerance).
