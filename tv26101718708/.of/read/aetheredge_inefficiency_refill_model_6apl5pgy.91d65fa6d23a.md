Calculation: Void oscillator = z-score of the one-bar log return against its rolling mean and standard deviation, multiplied by 1 when volume is below its rolling average and by a fade factor otherwise.
Calculation: Refill probability = sigmoid of a linear logistic model using six features: absolute void oscillator; volume shortfall (1 − volume / rolling average volume, clipped to [−1, 2]); absolute return z-score; efficiency ratio (absolute net close change / sum of absolute close changes over its lookback); ATR / its longer-term average; and absolute distance from close to the close a displacement lookback earlier, divided by ATR. Standardize features using online EWMA means and variances and clip standardized values to [−6, 6]. Initialize model weights at zero; on each void, save the standardized feature vector. When its origin is touched, train on label 1; if it remains untouched through the refill horizon, train on label 0. Update logistic weights online using the learning rate and L2 regularization. The probability used for a bar is computed before any training updates caused by resolutions on that bar.
Long: bar is completed AND warm-up has elapsed AND void oscillator crosses below the negative void threshold AND refill probability > minimum probability threshold.
Short: bar is completed AND warm-up has elapsed AND void oscillator crosses above the void threshold AND refill probability > minimum probability threshold.
