Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is the high or low of a bar that exceeds or falls below the corresponding values of the same number of bars on both sides; it becomes available after the right-side bars complete. The latest and previous pivots are tracked separately for highs and lows.
Calculation: Trend score = sum of weights for short EMA > medium EMA, medium EMA > long EMA, close > each of the three EMAs, each EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago, absolute short-to-medium EMA separation > ATR times a separation multiplier, and absolute medium-to-long EMA separation > ATR times a larger separation multiplier, capped at a maximum. The alignment weights are equal; the close and slope weights are equal and smaller; the separation weights are equal and smaller again.
Calculation: Structure score = an equal positive weight for each of latest pivot high > previous pivot high and latest pivot low > previous pivot low, minus the same weight for each of the corresponding less-than comparisons, capped symmetrically.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed pivot high. Bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish character change = latest confirmed pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND close > short EMA AND short EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago. Bearish character change = latest confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND close < short EMA AND short EMA < its value a slope-lookback ago.
Calculation: Long trend component = a tiered weight increasing as trend score crosses successively higher thresholds, plus positive structure score, plus equal weights for bullish structure break and bullish character change, plus an equal smaller weight for close > each EMA; cap the result. Short trend component = a tiered weight increasing as trend score crosses successively lower thresholds, plus the absolute value of negative structure score, plus equal weights for bearish structure break and bearish character change, plus an equal smaller weight for close < each EMA; cap the result. Corresponding long and short weights and caps are equal.
Calculation: Volatility component = a high, middle, or low weight according to whether ATR divided by close is above a higher threshold, above a lower threshold, or neither.
Calculation: Long final score = long trend component plus equal weights for RSI > its midpoint and MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > zero, plus volatility component and a smaller weight when volume > its SMA; cap the result. Short final score uses RSI < its midpoint and MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < zero, with the same weights, volatility component, volume condition, and cap.
Calculation: On every smart entry, set entry price to close, replace the previous entry and side, initialize the fixed stop at entry price adjusted by a stop-loss percentage against the position, set two profit targets at entry price adjusted by their respective profit percentages in favor of the position, and reset the trailing stop. On each bar after that initialization, including the entry bar, if trailing is enabled, set the long trailing stop to the maximum of its previous value and close minus an ATR multiple, or the short trailing stop to the minimum of its previous value and close plus that multiple. If break-even is enabled and close reaches the break-even trigger percentage in favor of the position, set the fixed stop to entry price. The active stop is the trailing stop when trailing is enabled and initialized; otherwise it is the fixed stop. Entry and side persist until another smart entry.
Long: close > latest confirmed pivot high.
Long: latest confirmed pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND close > short EMA AND short EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago.
Long: latest confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low > previous confirmed pivot low.
Long: trend score >= uptrend threshold.
Long: close > short EMA AND short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA.
Long: long final score >= buy-action threshold.
Long: close > short EMA AND short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > zero AND ADX > trend-strength threshold AND long final score >= signal threshold; this is a smart entry.
Short: close < latest confirmed pivot low.
Short: latest confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND close < short EMA AND short EMA < its value a slope-lookback ago.
Short: latest confirmed pivot high < previous confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low.
Short: trend score <= downtrend threshold.
Short: close < short EMA AND short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA.
Short: long final score < buy-action threshold AND short final score >= sell-action threshold.
Short: close < short EMA AND short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < zero AND ADX > trend-strength threshold AND short final score >= signal threshold; this is a smart entry.
Exit: long, close >= first profit target OR close >= second profit target OR close <= active stop.
Exit: short, close <= first profit target OR close <= second profit target OR close >= active stop.
