Calculation: Bar notation = [0] is the decision bar and [n] is n bars earlier; body high is max(open, close), body low is min(open, close), and body size is body high minus body low.
Calculation: Swing levels = Starting in the swing-high state, compare the bar a swing lookback ago with the intervening bars through the current bar. If its high is strictly above all their highs, set the state to swing-high; otherwise, if its low is strictly below all their lows, set the state to swing-low; otherwise retain the state. On a change to swing-high or swing-low, respectively record that older bar's high or low as the latest swing level of that type and mark it unused. A level becomes used on its first qualifying break. Swing range is the absolute difference between the latest high and low levels, or zero if either is unavailable.
Calculation: Order-block zone = At a qualifying swing break, scan bars between the swing bar and the break bar, excluding both, capped by a maximum scan lookback and including at least the preceding bar. Select the lowest low for a bullish block or highest high for a bearish block; optionally use body lows and highs for this selection and the zone edges. Resolve equal extremes in favor of the most recent bar. If the selected bar is not bearish for a bullish block or bullish for a bearish block, replace it with an immediately adjacent bar of the required direction within the scan, excluding the break bar; if both qualify, choose the more extreme low or high, resolving ties toward the more recent bar. The zone edges are that bar's high and low, or body high and body low when body measurement is selected.
Calculation: Zone state = A bullish zone arms when close is above its top; a bearish zone arms when close is below its bottom. Starting on a later bar, an armed bullish zone fills when low <= its bottom, and an armed bearish zone fills when high >= its top. An unfilled zone stays active until filled or removed by its per-family, per-direction zone limit. On creation, an order block is armed; replay bars from its anchor through the bar preceding its creation using the same arm and fill rules, and discard it as active if that replay filled it. With flipping enabled, the first fill of an active order block or chart-timeframe fair value gap reverses its direction and resets it to unarmed; its next fill removes it. Evaluate subsequent zone states on chart bars, including for higher-timeframe zones.
