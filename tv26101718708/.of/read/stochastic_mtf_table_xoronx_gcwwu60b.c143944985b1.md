Calculation: SlowStoch = SMA over a smoothing length of rawK, where rawK = 100*(close - lowest low over K lookback)/(highest high over K lookback - lowest low over K lookback); SlowStoch is also called %K. Compute it separately on each of five fixed timeframes, ranging from lower to higher than the chart timeframe, aggregated from the base bars. At each chart bar use the still-forming bar of each of those timeframes, not the last completed one.
Calculation: OB(tf) = SlowStoch(tf) >= upper level; OS(tf) = SlowStoch(tf) <= lower level, where the lower level is less than the upper level.
Calculation: Pivot low = a low that is the lowest over N bars on each side. It is confirmed N bars after it forms, with the same N on the left and right. Pivot high = a high that is the highest over N bars on each side, confirmed the same way.
Calculation: BullDiv(tf) event, evaluated on that timeframe's bars, fires on the bar a pivot low is confirmed when ALL of: a previous confirmed pivot low exists; the bar distance between the two pivots is > 0 and <= a maximum distance; the low at the new pivot < the low at the previous pivot; SlowStoch at the new pivot bar > SlowStoch at the previous pivot bar; AND, only if the optional zone filter is on, SlowStoch at either pivot bar is in OS. BearDiv(tf) event is the mirror image using pivot highs: the new pivot high > the previous pivot high; SlowStoch at the new pivot < SlowStoch at the previous pivot; the optional filter requires OB at either pivot.
Long: OB is true on all five timeframes now AND OB was not true on all five on the previous chart bar (first bar of the alignment); OR a BullDiv event is newly active on any one of the five timeframes. A BullDiv stays active for a fixed number of that timeframe's bars after its event; when BullDiv and BearDiv are both active, the more recent one wins.
Short: OS is true on all five timeframes now AND OS was not true on all five on the previous chart bar (first bar of the alignment); OR a BearDiv event is newly active on any one of the five timeframes. The activity window and tie rule are the same as for Long.
