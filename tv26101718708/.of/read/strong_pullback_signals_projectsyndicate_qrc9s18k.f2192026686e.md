Calculation: Bullish trend = shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND close > longer-period close EMA AND shorter-period EMA > its value a slope-lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish trend = shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND close < longer-period close EMA AND shorter-period EMA < its value a slope-lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Armed setup = on a bar with no armed setup and with cooldown disabled, no previous entry, or bars since the previous entry >= cooldown length, arm long when bullish trend AND close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND close > open AND abs(close - open) >= breakout-body multiplier × ATR; arm short when bearish trend AND close < lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND close < open AND abs(close - open) >= breakout-body multiplier × ATR. A setup can arm while a trade is active. Its age is bars since the breakout, and its swing extreme is the lowest low for long or highest high for short from the breakout bar through the current bar. After evaluating the current bar's entry, disarm if its limit was touched, age > maximum fill age, or its corresponding trend no longer holds. A newly armed setup has age zero.
Calculation: Pullback limit = pullback close EMA minus entry-depth multiplier × ATR for long, or plus entry-depth multiplier × ATR for short; recalculate on every bar. Entry price is min(open, pullback limit) for long and max(open, pullback limit) for short.
Calculation: Setup score = clamp(score scale × weighted mean of higher-timeframe alignment, session, trend separation, entry depth, momentum, and volume factors, 0, score scale), or zero if the sum of weights is zero. Alignment is a neutral fallback if the higher-timeframe filter is disabled or its EMA is unavailable; otherwise it is 1 if close is above the higher-timeframe EMA for long or below it for short, and 0 otherwise. Session is 0 if the session filter is enabled and the bar's exchange-timezone hour is in the weak-hour list, otherwise 1. Trend separation is min(abs(shorter-period EMA - longer-period EMA) / (positive ATR × separation normalization), 1), using a minimum price increment in place of nonpositive ATR. Entry depth is min(entry-depth multiplier / depth normalization, 1). Momentum is clamp((RSI - RSI midpoint) / momentum normalization, 0, 1) for long and clamp((RSI midpoint - RSI) / momentum normalization, 0, 1) for short. Volume is min(volume / volume SMA, 1) if volume SMA > 0, otherwise 0. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Entry risk = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), minimum-risk multiplier × entry-bar ATR), maximum-risk multiplier × entry-bar ATR), where raw stop is the setup's swing extreme minus stop-buffer multiplier × entry-bar ATR for long and plus it for short. Fix risk at entry. Stop = entry price minus risk for long and plus risk for short; final target = entry price plus final-target risk multiplier × risk for long and minus it for short.
Long: Armed long setup AND age >= minimum fill age AND low <= pullback limit AND no active trade AND no trade exited on this bar AND ATR is available AND close > longer-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR exchange-timezone hour is not in the weak-hour list) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= long threshold) AND (close confirmation disabled OR close > open) AND (score filter disabled OR setup score >= score threshold).
Short: Armed short setup AND age >= minimum fill age AND high >= pullback limit AND no active trade AND no trade exited on this bar AND ATR is available AND close < longer-period EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR exchange-timezone hour is not in the weak-hour list) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= short threshold) AND (close confirmation disabled OR close < open) AND (score filter disabled OR setup score >= score threshold).
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target).
