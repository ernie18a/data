Calculation: Trailing drawdown line = for a long, the favorable reference price minus the configured full drawdown distance; for a short, the reference price plus that distance. Initialize the reference from the entry price, adjusted in the favorable direction by full drawdown distance minus entry buffer distance. Update it to the most favorable of the prior reference and the selected trail price: intraday uses the anchor-bar close and then subsequent highs for longs or lows for shorts; bar-close uses each close; end-of-day uses the previous completed chart bar's close on the first chart bar of each new day, with the boundary determined by daily bars. Apply the optional lock as a ceiling for longs or a floor for shorts, then round toward safety to the nearest tick.
Exit: Long, position is active and not previously breached and bar ends by configured exit time AND (close <= trailing drawdown line OR (bar is after the entry bar AND low <= trailing drawdown line in force during the bar; in bar-close mode use the prior bar's line))
Exit: Short, position is active and not previously breached and bar ends by configured exit time AND (close >= trailing drawdown line OR (bar is after the entry bar AND high >= trailing drawdown line in force during the bar; in bar-close mode use the prior bar's line))
