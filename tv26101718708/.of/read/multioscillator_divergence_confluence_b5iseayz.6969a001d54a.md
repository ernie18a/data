Calculation: Oscillators = enabled RSI of close, MACD line of close using shorter and longer EMA lengths, SMA-smoothed stochastic of close/high/low, CCI of close, MFI of typical price, and EMA-smoothed OBV.
Calculation: Bullish lean count = number of enabled oscillators whose current value is greater than its value at the lean lookback while close is lower than at that lookback; bearish lean count = number whose current value is lower while close is higher.
Calculation: Pressure = EMA of a neutral midpoint plus the bullish lean count minus the bearish lean count, scaled by the full oscillator count and the distance from the midpoint to the pressure range endpoint.
Calculation: Regime = trending if ADX is at least its trend threshold; otherwise volatile if the percentile rank of ATR divided by close is at least its upper threshold; otherwise quiet if that percentile rank is at most its lower threshold; otherwise ranging.
Calculation: Pivot = a price low or high confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, using configured left-hand and right-hand pivot widths. Compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind; spacing is their bar distance. Evaluate decisions on the confirmation bar, using price, oscillator, EMA, and volume values at the pivot unless stated otherwise.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence count = enabled oscillators higher at the new low pivot than at the preceding low pivot when the new price low is lower; hidden bullish count = enabled oscillators lower when the new price low is higher. Regular bearish count = enabled oscillators lower at the new high pivot when the new price high is higher; hidden bearish count = enabled oscillators higher when the new price high is lower. Each count is zero unless pivot spacing is within its minimum and maximum bounds.
Calculation: Exhaustion = at least one enabled RSI, smoothed stochastic, CCI, or MFI is below its oscillator-specific lower threshold for bullish divergences or above its oscillator-specific upper threshold for bearish divergences.
Calculation: Divergence score = divergence count times its weight, plus weights for matching EMA context, exhaustion, optional above-average pivot volume, and matching regime, plus the rounded spacing-quality weight, minus a penalty if the enabled oscillator count is below its threshold; clamp to the score range. Spacing quality is the spacing weight times max(0, 1 - abs(spacing - midpoint of spacing bounds) / max(half their difference, a positive floor)). EMA context matches when pivot close is below EMA for regular bullish and hidden bearish, or above EMA for hidden bullish and regular bearish. Regime matches when the confirmation-bar regime is ranging or quiet for regular divergence, or trending for hidden divergence. Above-average volume means pivot volume exceeds its SMA at the pivot.
Long: Bullish lean count > bearish lean count AND histogram enabled.
Short: Bullish lean count <= bearish lean count AND histogram enabled.
Long: Pressure >= upper pressure threshold AND pressure background enabled.
Short: Pressure <= lower pressure threshold AND pressure background enabled AND pressure < upper pressure threshold.
Long: Pressure crosses above the upper setup threshold AND bullish lean count >= minimum setup count AND low minus the lowest low over the swing lookback <= ATR times the proximity multiplier AND exploratory setups or setup alerts enabled.
Short: Pressure crosses below the lower setup threshold AND bearish lean count >= minimum setup count AND highest high over the swing lookback minus high <= ATR times the proximity multiplier AND exploratory setups or setup alerts enabled.
Long: Enabled oscillator count >= minimum divergence count AND ((regular bullish divergence count >= minimum divergence count AND regular bullish score >= density-dependent minimum score) OR (hidden bullish divergence count >= minimum divergence count AND hidden bullish score >= density-dependent minimum score AND pivot close > pivot EMA)).
Short: Enabled oscillator count >= minimum divergence count AND ((regular bearish divergence count >= minimum divergence count AND regular bearish score >= density-dependent minimum score) OR (hidden bearish divergence count >= minimum divergence count AND hidden bearish score >= density-dependent minimum score AND pivot close < pivot EMA)).
