Calculation: Mean = a selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, VWMA, or HMA of a selected OHLC-derived source.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = mean plus or minus a selected deviation: standard deviation of the source multiplied by its coefficient, ATR multiplied by its coefficient, or the average of those two deviations.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = (min(open, close) - low) / max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) >= minimum wick-to-body ratio AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) <= maximum opposite-wick fraction.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = (high - max(open, close)) / max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) >= minimum wick-to-body ratio AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) <= maximum opposite-wick fraction.
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR / SMA of ATR, or 1 when the SMA of ATR is zero.
Long: (low <= lower band for touch mode OR (lowest low over the extreme lookback, including the current bar, <= current lower band AND close > lower band AND close > open) for reclaim mode OR (lowest low over the extreme lookback, including the current bar, <= current lower band AND bullish rejection AND close > open) for rejection mode) AND bars since the previous long signal >= minimum spacing, or no previous long signal exists AND (ADX <= maximum ADX if the ADX filter is enabled) AND (close >= EMA of close if the EMA regime filter is enabled and countertrend signals are disallowed) AND (volume > SMA of volume multiplied by its coefficient if the volume filter is enabled) AND (ATR expansion <= maximum expansion if the volatility filter is enabled) AND (RSI of the selected source <= oversold threshold if the RSI filter is enabled) AND (MFI of typical price <= oversold threshold if the MFI filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close >= higher-timeframe EMA of close if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled in same-side mode). Evaluate on closed chart bars; the higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, updating when the higher-timeframe bar completes, and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Short: (high >= upper band for touch mode OR (highest high over the extreme lookback, including the current bar, >= current upper band AND close < upper band AND close < open) for reclaim mode OR (highest high over the extreme lookback, including the current bar, >= current upper band AND bearish rejection AND close < open) for rejection mode) AND bars since the previous short signal >= minimum spacing, or no previous short signal exists AND (ADX <= maximum ADX if the ADX filter is enabled) AND (close <= EMA of close if the EMA regime filter is enabled and countertrend signals are disallowed) AND (volume > SMA of volume multiplied by its coefficient if the volume filter is enabled) AND (ATR expansion <= maximum expansion if the volatility filter is enabled) AND (RSI of the selected source >= overbought threshold if the RSI filter is enabled) AND (MFI of typical price >= overbought threshold if the MFI filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close <= higher-timeframe EMA of close if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled in same-side mode). Evaluate on closed chart bars; the higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, updating when the higher-timeframe bar completes, and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Long: close < lower band.
Short: close > upper band.

Short: higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA of close when higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, updating when the higher-timeframe bar completes, and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
