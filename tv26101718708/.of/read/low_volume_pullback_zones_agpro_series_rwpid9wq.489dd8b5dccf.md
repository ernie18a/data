Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow EMAs = EMAs of close with increasing lengths. Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Safe ATR = max(ATR, a positive price floor). Normalized slope = (medium EMA - medium EMA at the slope lookback) / safe ATR.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish trend = bar index > max(slow EMA length, impulse lookback, relative-volume length, ATR length) + max(slope lookback, ADX smoothing length) + warmup buffer AND fast EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > slow EMA AND close > medium EMA AND normalized slope >= minimum slope AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND +DI >= -DI. Confirmed bearish trend = same warmup condition AND fast EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < slow EMA AND close < medium EMA AND normalized slope <= -minimum slope AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND -DI >= +DI.
Calculation: Bullish trend direction = confirmed bullish trend OR (neither confirmed trend AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND normalized slope > -minimum slope AND +DI >= -DI multiplied by the watch DI factor). Bearish trend direction = confirmed bearish trend OR (neither confirmed trend AND bullish watch conditions are false AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND normalized slope < minimum slope AND -DI >= +DI multiplied by the watch DI factor).
Calculation: Pocket = when no pocket is active, start a bullish pocket if bullish trend direction AND (low <= fast EMA + lane tolerance * safe ATR OR low <= medium EMA + lane tolerance * safe ATR) AND close > slow EMA AND relative volume <= maximum starting relative volume AND close <= max(open, previous close); start a bearish pocket if bearish trend direction AND (high >= fast EMA - lane tolerance * safe ATR OR high >= medium EMA - lane tolerance * safe ATR) AND close < slow EMA AND relative volume <= maximum starting relative volume AND close >= min(open, previous close). At the start, fix anchor high as max(current high, highest high over the preceding impulse lookback) and anchor low as min(current low, lowest low over that lookback). Include the start and current bars in pocket age, highest high, lowest low, and average relative volume. Update these before evaluating decisions on each bar. End the pocket after a trigger, or after invalidation if no trigger occurs; do not start another on the same bar.
Calculation: Pocket depth = 100 * (anchor high - pocket lowest low) / max(anchor high - anchor low, positive price floor) for a bullish pocket; 100 * (pocket highest high - anchor low) / the same denominator for a bearish pocket. Dry-up score = 100 at or below preferred average relative volume, 0 at or above hard average relative volume, and linear between them. Depth score = 100 at or below preferred depth, 0 at or above hard depth, and linear between them. Preferred depth is below trigger depth or equal to it, and trigger depth is at or below hard depth. Preferred relative volume is below hard relative volume.
Calculation: Trend score = 0 when trend direction is neutral; otherwise 0.42 * clamp(100 * abs(normalized slope) / max(3 * minimum slope, slope floor), 0, 100) + 0.33 * clamp(100 * (ADX - minimum ADX) / max(minimum ADX, ADX floor), 0, 100) + 25 for a confirmed trend or 12 otherwise. Duration score = 55 * pocket age / minimum pocket age before minimum age, 100 from minimum through maximum pocket age, and 0 thereafter. Trigger level = pocket highest high + trigger buffer * safe ATR for a bullish pocket, or pocket lowest low - trigger buffer * safe ATR for a bearish pocket. Trigger pressure score = clamp(100 - 45 * distance / safe ATR, 0, 100), where distance is max(trigger level - close, 0) for a bullish pocket and max(close - trigger level, 0) for a bearish pocket. Quality score = 0.20 * trend score + 0.28 * dry-up score + 0.24 * depth score + 0.12 * duration score + 0.16 * trigger pressure score.
Calculation: Pocket invalidation = depth > hard depth OR pocket age > maximum pocket age OR, for a bullish pocket, close < pocket lowest low - invalidation buffer * safe ATR OR close < slow EMA; for a bearish pocket, close > pocket highest high + invalidation buffer * safe ATR OR close > slow EMA.
Calculation: Trigger = active pocket AND pocket age >= minimum pocket age AND dry-up score >= minimum dry-up score AND depth <= trigger depth AND quality score >= minimum trigger quality AND relative volume >= average pocket relative volume * recovery ratio AND, for a bullish pocket, close > trigger level AND close > open; for a bearish pocket, close < trigger level AND close < open.
Calculation: Watch = active pocket AND pocket age >= minimum pocket age AND dry-up score >= minimum dry-up score AND depth <= hard depth AND quality score >= minimum watch quality AND no trigger AND no pocket invalidation AND no prior watch in this pocket AND bars since the previous same-side watch >= cooldown, or no previous same-side watch.
Long: bullish trend direction.
Short: bearish trend direction.
Long: bullish pocket AND (trigger OR watch).
Short: bearish pocket AND (trigger OR watch).
