Calculation: DayMid = midpoint of the running high and running low since the start of the current day, including the current bar
Calculation: PDH/PDL/PWH/PWL = high/low of the last completed daily bar and of the last completed weekly bar (both higher timeframes than the chart)
Calculation: SwingHigh/SwingLow = most recent confirmed pivot high/low (extreme versus an equal number of bars on each side)
Calculation: HTFBullFVG = most recent bullish fair value gap (bar low > high two bars earlier; zone = [high two bars earlier, low]) found on any of several higher timeframes, computed on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, with the longest timeframe taking priority when several appear at once; a newer gap replaces the older one; the zone is discarded once close < zone bottom. HTFBearFVG = mirror (high < low two bars earlier; zone = [high, low two bars earlier]; discarded once close > zone top)
Calculation: AllowSignal = bar time-of-day is within one of the predefined key intraday windows AND number of Long/Short signals already issued on the current day < daily cap
Calculation: BullManip = on a bar where AllowSignal AND bars since the last manipulation bar of either direction >= cooldown AND no bullish setup is in progress AND (for any L in {PDL, PWL, SwingLow, previous bar's running day low}: low < L - small buffer AND close > L; OR the bar range overlaps HTFBullFVG). This bar starts a new BullSetup, discarding any BearSetup in progress, and stores ManipHigh/ManipLow = this bar's high/low
Calculation: BearManip = mirror of BullManip: on a bar where AllowSignal AND cooldown elapsed AND no bearish setup is in progress AND (for any L in {PDH, PWH, SwingHigh, previous bar's running day high}: high > L + small buffer AND close < L; OR the bar range overlaps HTFBearFVG). This bar starts a new BearSetup, discarding any BullSetup in progress, and stores ManipHigh/ManipLow. If both fire on the same bar, BearManip prevails
Calculation: BullSetup = ordered stages after the BullManip bar. A stage can complete on the same bar that completed the previous stage. The setup is abandoned when bars since the BullManip bar > max wait, when an opposite manipulation occurs, or once it has produced a Long. (1) Reference: the latest bearish bar (close < open), from the manip bar onward, with low >= ManipLow and high <= ManipHigh plus a small tolerance; RefOpen = its open. (2) Shift: a bar after (1) with close > RefOpen AND close > open. (3) Displacement: a bar after (2) with close > open AND |close-open| > multiplier * SMA(|close-open|, lookback) AND close > highest high of the previous N bars; DispHigh = its high. (4) FVG: a bar after (3) with low > high two bars earlier; FvgTop = low, FvgBot = high two bars earlier, FvgMid = their midpoint. (5) OTE touch: from the (4) bar onward, any bar whose range overlaps the band between DispHigh - R*a and DispHigh - R*b, where R = DispHigh - ManipLow and b > a are deep-retracement ratios of the range
Calculation: BearSetup = mirror of BullSetup with highs/lows and bullish/bearish swapped. (1) Reference: the latest bullish bar with high <= ManipHigh and low >= ManipLow minus a small tolerance. (2) Shift: close < RefOpen AND close < open. (3) Displacement: bearish bar, body > multiplier * average body AND close < lowest low of the previous N bars; DispLow = its low. (4) FVG: high < low two bars earlier; FvgTop = low two bars earlier, FvgBot = high. (5) OTE touch: bar range overlaps the band between DispLow + R*a and DispLow + R*b, where R = ManipHigh - DispLow
Long: BullSetup stages (1) to (5) are all complete AND AllowSignal AND ManipLow < DayMid AND current bar is bullish (close > open) AND close > FvgMid AND open >= FvgBot
Short: BearSetup stages (1) to (5) are all complete AND AllowSignal AND ManipHigh > DayMid AND current bar is bearish (close < open) AND close < FvgMid AND open <= FvgTop
