Calculation: Delta oscillator = EMA of the rolling sum of directional volume divided by rolling total volume, multiplied by 100; directional volume = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low. The rolling window is shorter than the EMA smoothing length.
Calculation: CMF = 100 × the rolling sum of (((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low)) × volume divided by rolling volume; the multiplier is 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Weis Wave direction = track the running close extreme in the current direction; reverse from up to down when close is at or below the extreme minus ATR × multiplier, and from down to up when close is at or above the extreme plus ATR × multiplier. Initialize up if the first close exceeds the prior close, otherwise down.
Calculation: Bull score = sum of the configured weights for each true condition: Delta oscillator > 0; Delta oscillator > its prior value; close ≥ open and relative volume ≥ its medium threshold; CMF > 5; CMF > its prior value; Weis Wave direction is up. Bear score = sum of the configured weights for each true condition: Delta oscillator < 0; Delta oscillator < its prior value; close < open and relative volume ≥ its medium threshold; CMF < −5; CMF < its prior value; Weis Wave direction is down. Relative volume = volume / average volume; the average uses a longer lookback than the Delta oscillator's rolling sum.
Long: close > open AND bull score ≥ the configured signal threshold AND this signal condition was false on the prior bar when first-occurrence-only is enabled
Long: At a confirmed local low of the Delta oscillator, price low is below the prior confirmed local low, Delta oscillator is above its prior confirmed local low, and the prior oscillator low pivot was 5–60 bars ago
Short: close < open AND bear score ≥ the configured signal threshold AND this signal condition was false on the prior bar when first-occurrence-only is enabled
Short: At a confirmed local high of the Delta oscillator, price high is above the prior confirmed local high, Delta oscillator is below its prior confirmed local high, and the prior oscillator high pivot was 5–60 bars ago
