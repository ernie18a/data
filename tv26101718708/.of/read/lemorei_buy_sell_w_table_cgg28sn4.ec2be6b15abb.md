Calculation: Opening-window bar count = the number of chart bars since the first bar inside the configured session; the opening window includes bars whose count is at most the configured limit.
Calculation: Opening-range high = the highest high of the first configured number of chart bars in the session, including the current bar until the range is complete; retain that high for the rest of the session.
Calculation: Session gap percentage = absolute value of (first session bar open minus preceding bar close) divided by preceding bar close, multiplied by 100; retain it throughout the session.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its SMA including the current bar.
Calculation: RSI average = configured SMA, EMA, RMA, or WMA of RSI.
Calculation: Position quantity and average entry = on each primary long signal, add max(1, floor(configured capital per entry / close)) shares at close and recalculate the share-weighted average entry; a buy-more reminder does not change the position. Clear both on exit.
Calculation: Stop = low minus ATR times the initial-stop multiplier on the first primary long signal; on each additional primary long signal, take the maximum of the existing stop and that bar's low minus ATR times the initial-stop multiplier. If trailing is enabled, on every bar with a position, including entry bars, also take the maximum of the stop and the fast EMA minus ATR times the trailing multiplier. Update the stop before testing exits.
