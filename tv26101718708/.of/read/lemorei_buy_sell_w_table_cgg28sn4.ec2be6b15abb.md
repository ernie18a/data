Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by the moving average of volume over a longer lookback
Calculation: Pullback dip = at least one close in the lookback was below the fast EMA
Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the initial opening interval, fixed once that interval ends
Calculation: Stop loss = on entry, entry-bar low minus an ATR multiple; while long, raise it to the greater of its prior value and fast EMA minus an ATR multiple
Long: Cooldown since the previous buy has elapsed AND price is at or above VWAP AND (MACD line crosses above signal line AND RSI is rising AND RSI is above its moving average AND (during the opening window, RSI moving average need not be rising; otherwise it is rising) AND (during the opening window, RSI is below its cap if enabled; otherwise it is below its cap if enabled) OR MACD line is above signal line AND the same RSI conditions AND price crosses above the fast EMA AND (a close in the pullback lookback was below the fast EMA if required) OR during the opening window, close is above VWAP and fast EMA AND the opening-range break condition holds if enabled AND EMA alignment holds if required AND MACD line is above signal line AND MACD histogram is rising if required AND the same opening-window RSI conditions AND relative volume meets its minimum AND the session gap is within its maximum if gap filtering is enabled OR relative volume meets its breakout threshold AND bar range is at least an ATR multiple AND close breaks above the prior-high lookback if required AND close is sufficiently near the bar high if required AND EMA alignment holds if required AND RSI is rising and above its moving average and below its cap if enabled AND MACD line and histogram are rising); for the first three alternatives, ADX is above its trade threshold or is below that threshold but rising on each of the required consecutive bars and price is at or above VWAP, except that the regime gate is bypassed during the opening window if configured; apply the configured green-candle requirement except where the opening window suppresses it; the breakout-volume alternative bypasses the regime gate if configured
Long: While long, buy-more reminders are enabled AND cooldowns since the last buy and last reminder have elapsed AND the first-three-alternatives regime/VWAP gate holds AND close is above the fast EMA AND MACD line is above signal line AND the applicable RSI conditions hold AND MACD histogram is above zero and rising if required AND ADX is rising if required AND no regular buy signal occurs
Exit: long, low is at or below the stop loss (or close is at or below it when close-triggered) OR current unrealized P/L is at or below the maximum-loss threshold OR unrealized P/L is positive AND fast EMA is falling AND close is below fast EMA OR MACD line crosses below signal line (only when profitable if configured)
