Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = the high a configurable number of chart bars ago, strictly greater than every other high from one bar ago through twice that number of bars ago; the current bar is excluded.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = the low a configurable number of chart bars ago, strictly lower than every other low from one bar ago through twice that number of bars ago; the current bar is excluded.
Long: A confirmed pivot low exists on the current bar.
Short: A confirmed pivot high exists on the current bar.
Long: Low < the lowest low over a configurable lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > that lowest low AND close > open.
Short: High > the highest high over a configurable lookback ending on the previous bar AND close < that highest high AND close < open.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close > its higher-timeframe simple moving average.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close <= its higher-timeframe simple moving average OR either value is unavailable.
