Calculation: Adaptive ATR trailing stop = initialize to close; each bar, calculate ATR, volume ratio = volume / short-term average volume, and efficiency ratio = abs(close − close 10 bars ago) / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over the last 10 bars. Trail distance = ATR × sensitivity × asset multiplier × volatility multiplier × clamped volume ratio × chop multiplier, where clamped volume ratio is limited to 0.8–1.2 and chop multiplier is 1 + (1 − efficiency ratio) × chop strength when adaptive chop is enabled, otherwise 1. Ratchet the stop: if current and previous close are above the previous stop, use the greater of the previous stop and current close minus trail distance; if both are below, use the lesser of the previous stop and current close plus trail distance; otherwise use current close minus trail distance when current close is above the previous stop, or current close plus trail distance otherwise.
Calculation: Long stop level = selected stop method applied to entry price (ATR distance, percentage distance, tick distance, recent swing low, volatility-scaled ATR distance, smart volatility-scaled ATR distance with a minimum percentage distance, or the widest of ATR, percentage, and swing distances), then subtract the configured buffers. Short stop level = corresponding selected method applied to entry price (ATR distance, percentage distance, tick distance, recent swing high, volatility-scaled ATR distance, smart volatility-scaled ATR distance with a minimum percentage distance, or the widest of ATR, percentage, and swing distances), then add the configured buffers.
Calculation: Profit target levels = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the initial risk distance multiplied by each enabled reward multiple; initial risk distance is the absolute difference between entry price and the unbuffered stop level.
Long: close crosses above the adaptive ATR trailing stop AND close is above the stop (and, if enabled, low is above it) AND the configured prior-bar direction confirmation passes AND, if enabled, the two-bar confirmation, minimum absolute percentage-change threshold, swing condition (close > 1.01 × the recent lowest close), ADX regime threshold, cooldown, volume threshold, RSI ceiling, close above Hull moving average, bullish SuperTrend, higher-timeframe EMA(9) > EMA(21) or equal, and no resistance-zone match all pass AND the previous signal was not long. The two-bar confirmation requires the upward cross two bars earlier and both intervening closes above their corresponding trailing stops. The higher-timeframe check uses the selected higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Short: close crosses below the adaptive ATR trailing stop AND close is below the stop (and, if enabled, high is below it) AND the configured prior-bar direction confirmation passes AND, if enabled, the two-bar confirmation, minimum absolute percentage-change threshold, swing condition (close < 0.99 × the recent highest close), ADX regime threshold, cooldown, volume threshold, RSI floor, close below Hull moving average, bearish SuperTrend, higher-timeframe EMA(9) < EMA(21) or equal, and no support-zone match all pass AND the previous signal was not short. The two-bar confirmation requires the downward cross two bars earlier and both intervening closes below their corresponding trailing stops. The higher-timeframe check uses the selected higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Exit: long, low <= long stop level OR high >= the farthest enabled profit target; check only from the bar after entry.
Exit: short, high >= short stop level OR low <= the farthest enabled profit target; check only from the bar after entry.
