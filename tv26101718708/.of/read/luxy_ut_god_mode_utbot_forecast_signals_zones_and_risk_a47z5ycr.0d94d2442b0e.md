Calculation: Adaptive trail = Let A be ATR of close-bar true ranges, V be volume divided by its volume SMA, and Q be ATR divided by its SMA, with Q = 1 when that SMA is not positive. Select a sensitivity multiplier based on Q's low, normal, or high regime; fixed mode uses a constant multiplier. Let efficiency be the absolute change in close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or 0 when unavailable. Distance = A × sensitivity × selected multiplier × clamp(V, lower bound, upper bound) × (1 + chop strength × (1 − efficiency)); the volume and chop factors can separately be disabled. Classic mode uses distance = A × sensitivity. Initialize the trail to close. Thereafter, if current and previous closes are both above the previous trail, trail = max(previous trail, close − distance); if both are below it, trail = min(previous trail, close + distance); otherwise trail = close − distance when close > previous trail, and close + distance otherwise.
Calculation: Resistance and support zones = Maintain separate capped lists of confirmed pivot-high and pivot-low prices, using equal left and right pivot lookbacks. On each bar first remove zones whose distance from close exceeds a percentage of close. Then register each newly confirmed pivot: if any existing zone is within current ATR × zone-width multiplier, replace the last matching zone by its average with the pivot; otherwise, if the list is full, remove the zone farthest from close, then append the pivot. A price is inside a zone when its distance from any zone in the relevant list is at most current ATR × zone-width multiplier.
Calculation: Entry stop and targets = At a filtered entry, set entry price to close and retain all stop calculations from that bar. Choose the stop distance by ATR multiple, entry-price percentage, fixed price increment, distance to the lowest low or highest high over a swing lookback, volatility-scaled ATR with a minimum entry-price percentage, or the widest of ATR, percentage, and swing distances. For a swing on the wrong side of entry or unavailable, substitute an ATR multiple. Add an ATR buffer and optional fixed price buffer beyond the resulting stop; if the resulting stop is on the wrong side, substitute an ATR-based minimum distance. Each target is entry plus the selected reward multiple of the unadjusted buffered stop distance for a long, or entry minus it for a short; the optional fixed price buffer is excluded from target risk. Retain the stop and targets until the next filtered entry.
