Calculation: Regression = linear-regression fitted value of close at the current bar.
Calculation: Price benchmark = session VWAP of close, or simple moving average of (high + low + close) / 3, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars where close > open, minus volume on bars where close < open; bars where close = open contribute nothing.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Daily eligibility = trades entered since the day-of-week change < trade limit AND consecutive losing closed trades since that change < loss-count limit AND daily realized P&L < profit limit AND abs(daily realized P&L) < loss limit AND current time is within the inclusive trading session AND the number of session bars counted since the day-of-week change > initial-bar skip count AND position side = flat. Reset the trade count, consecutive loss count, and realized P&L at each day-of-week change; add the most recently closed trade's profit when the closed-trade count changes, increment the loss count if that profit < 0, otherwise reset it to zero.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry-bar close minus ATR times its multiplier for a long, or plus that distance for a short; alternatively use the lower of that long stop and the entry-bar rolling lowest low, or the higher of that short stop and the entry-bar rolling highest high; alternatively use entry-bar close minus a fixed distance for a long, or plus it for a short. Entry price for these calculations is the signal bar's close.
Calculation: Initial risk = abs(entry price - initial stop). First and second targets = entry price plus the respective target multiple of initial risk for a long, or minus it for a short; the second target multiple is larger than the first.
Calculation: Current stop = initial stop until the first target exit, then entry price. If trailing is enabled, activate it when close - entry price >= trailing activation multiple times current risk for a long, or entry price - close >= that amount for a short, where current risk = abs(entry price - current stop). Once active, the trailing stop is the highest value of close - trailing distance multiple times current risk since activation for a long, or the lowest value of close + that distance for a short; use the trailing stop as the active stop while trailing is active, otherwise use the current stop. Update trailing before evaluating exits on each bar.
Long: Long trading enabled AND daily eligibility AND Regression > previous Regression AND close > Regression AND close > price benchmark AND price benchmark > previous price benchmark AND (CVD filter disabled OR (cumulative volume delta > its simple moving average AND cumulative volume delta > each of its two preceding values)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold)) AND (volume > volume simple moving average times spike multiplier OR (volume > previous volume AND previous volume > volume two bars ago)) AND close > open AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND high - low >= minimum range AND close >= high - (high - low) / 4.
Short: Short trading enabled AND daily eligibility AND Regression < previous Regression AND close < Regression AND close < price benchmark AND price benchmark < previous price benchmark AND (CVD filter disabled OR (cumulative volume delta < its simple moving average AND cumulative volume delta < each of its two preceding values)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold)) AND (volume > volume simple moving average times spike multiplier OR (volume > previous volume AND previous volume > volume two bars ago)) AND close < open AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND high - low >= minimum range AND close <= low + (high - low) / 4.
Exit: long, low <= active stop.
Exit: long, low > active stop AND high >= first target AND first target has not previously triggered; exit the configured fraction of the position, rounded down to whole units, and set the current stop to entry price.
Exit: long, low > active stop AND (high < first target OR first target has previously triggered) AND high >= second target.
Exit: long, low > active stop AND (high < first target OR first target has previously triggered) AND high < second target AND current time >= session end minus the configured exit lead time.
Exit: long, first target has triggered AND ((close < Regression AND Regression < previous Regression) OR close < price benchmark).
Exit: short, high >= active stop.
Exit: short, high < active stop AND low <= first target AND first target has not previously triggered; exit the configured fraction of the position, rounded down to whole units, and set the current stop to entry price.
Exit: short, high < active stop AND (low > first target OR first target has previously triggered) AND low <= second target.
Exit: short, high < active stop AND (low > first target OR first target has previously triggered) AND low > second target AND current time >= session end minus the configured exit lead time.
Exit: short, first target has triggered AND ((close > Regression AND Regression > previous Regression) OR close > price benchmark).
