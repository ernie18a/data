Calculation: Fast and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast EMA using a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Recent high/low = highest high/lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: EMA separation = absolute difference between fast and slow EMA divided by close, expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish candle = close above/below open, high above low, and close in the top/bottom 35% of the candle range.
Long: Confirmed bar AND no prior consecutive long signal AND ADX >= minimum AND (close > VWAP OR (close > previous close AND close > fast EMA)) AND bullish candle AND (EMA separation >= required minimum OR separation filter disabled) AND (adaptive noise filter disabled/bypassed by the active timeframe profile OR (close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA)) AND ((close > recent high AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > previous fast EMA AND RSI > buy threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND +DI > -DI) OR ((RSI crosses above buy threshold OR +DI crosses above -DI) AND close > fast EMA AND +DI > -DI AND RSI > 50)).
Short: Confirmed bar AND no prior consecutive short signal AND ADX >= minimum AND (close < VWAP OR (close < previous close AND close < fast EMA)) AND bearish candle AND (EMA separation >= required minimum OR separation filter disabled) AND (adaptive noise filter disabled/bypassed by the active timeframe profile OR (close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA)) AND ((close < recent low AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < previous fast EMA AND RSI < sell threshold AND RSI < previous RSI AND -DI > +DI) OR ((RSI crosses below sell threshold OR -DI crosses above +DI) AND close < fast EMA AND -DI > +DI AND RSI < 50)).
