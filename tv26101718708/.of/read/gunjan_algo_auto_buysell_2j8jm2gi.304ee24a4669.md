Calculation: EMA separation percentage = 100 × abs(shorter-period close EMA − longer-period close EMA) / close; use zero when close equals zero.
Calculation: Directional state = initially neutral; on each completed bar, change to long when the long condition below is met and the previous state is not long, or to short when the short condition below is met and the previous state is not short; otherwise retain the previous state. Calculate independently on the chart timeframe and each selected higher and lower timeframe, using that timeframe's parameter profile. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar; for lower timeframes use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Active long fraction = number of selected higher- and lower-timeframe directional states that are long divided by the number of those states that are long or short; use zero if none are active. Active short fraction is defined analogously.
Long: On a completed chart bar, previous chart-timeframe directional state is not long AND ADX >= its minimum threshold AND EMA separation filter is disabled OR EMA separation percentage >= its threshold AND adaptive noise filter is disabled or exempt for the chart timeframe OR close > shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND (close > session VWAP of typical price OR (close > previous close AND close > shorter-period EMA)) AND close > open AND high > low AND close >= high − (high − low) × candle-close fraction AND ((close > highest high of the preceding structure lookback AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA > its previous value AND RSI > long RSI threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND +DI > −DI) OR ((RSI crosses above long RSI threshold OR +DI crosses above −DI) AND close > shorter-period EMA AND +DI > −DI AND RSI > RSI midpoint)).
Short: On a completed chart bar, previous chart-timeframe directional state is not short AND ADX >= its minimum threshold AND EMA separation filter is disabled OR EMA separation percentage >= its threshold AND adaptive noise filter is disabled or exempt for the chart timeframe OR close < shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND (close < session VWAP of typical price OR (close < previous close AND close < shorter-period EMA)) AND close < open AND high > low AND close <= low + (high − low) × candle-close fraction AND ((close < lowest low of the preceding structure lookback AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA < its previous value AND RSI < short RSI threshold AND RSI < previous RSI AND −DI > +DI) OR ((RSI crosses below short RSI threshold OR −DI crosses above +DI) AND close < shorter-period EMA AND −DI > +DI AND RSI < RSI midpoint)).
Long: A selected higher- or lower-timeframe directional state equals long.
Short: A selected higher- or lower-timeframe directional state equals short.
Long: Active long fraction >= long-bias threshold.
Short: Active short fraction >= short-bias threshold.
