Calculation: Reference range = the high and low of a completed measuring session, available during its eligible subsequent session, or the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, available during the current higher-timeframe period. Maintain each range independently; require high > low and set height = high - low.
Calculation: Next upper band = [reference high + height × upper count, reference high + height × (upper count + 1)]. Next lower band = [reference low - height × (lower count + 1), reference low - height × lower count]. Reset both counts to zero when a new reference period begins; increment the corresponding count when its band qualifies, subject to a maximum count per side.
Long: Reference range is available AND upper count < maximum count AND open >= next upper band bottom AND open <= next upper band top AND close >= next upper band bottom AND close <= next upper band top.
Short: Reference range is available AND lower count < maximum count AND open >= next lower band bottom AND open <= next lower band top AND close >= next lower band bottom AND close <= next lower band top.
