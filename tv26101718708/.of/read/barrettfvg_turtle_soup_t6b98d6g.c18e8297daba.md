Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the configured pivot length of bars on each side; track confirmed levels until price trades beyond them, retaining no more than the configured level cap per timeframe.
Calculation: Turtle soup = on a confirmed bar, price trades above a tracked swing high and closes below it (short), or trades below a tracked swing low and closes above it (long); if multiple levels qualify, use the most recently formed. For higher-timeframe scans, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use the chart-timeframe bar for the chart scan.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = respectively, current low > high two chart bars earlier, or current high < low two chart bars earlier; zone is the interval between those prices, and its size must exceed the configured multiple of chart-timeframe ATR(14).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish IFVG = an existing bearish/bullish FVG zone, respectively, whose close crosses beyond its far boundary (close above the bearish zone top / below the bullish zone bottom); the inverted zone points in the opposite direction from the original FVG. Reject a new FVG or IFVG if its zone overlaps an existing zone with the same effective direction when overlap filtering is enabled.
Calculation: Delivery state = a run of consecutive bullish or bearish chart bars (close > open / close < open) at least the configured minimum length; its opening is the first bar's open. Bullish change occurs when close > the latest bearish run opening plus configured ATR(30) clearance; bearish change occurs when close < the latest bullish run opening minus that clearance. Track the direction and reclaimed opening of the latest change.
Calculation: Entry stop = the pending soup's most adverse extreme, extended on each chart bar while pending or held (lowest low for long, highest high for short), plus the configured tick buffer beyond that extreme; keep it at least one tick beyond entry. Risk = absolute distance from entry to stop. Target = entry plus/minus risk times the configured target multiple. Breakeven trigger = entry plus/minus the configured fraction of the entry-to-target distance.
Long: A tracked swing low is swept and rejected by a confirmed bar, then within the configured number of chart bars a qualifying bullish FVG or bullish IFVG forms with its entire zone above the swept level by at least the configured clearance; the setup has not been invalidated by a later confirmed close below its level on its source timeframe, is in an allowed session, and has not already produced an entry. If several soups qualify, use the most recent. When the delivery filter is enabled, enter only if delivery state is not bearish or close > the latest reclaimed opening; otherwise hold the signal and enter on the first such qualifying close within the configured hold window and an allowed session. Consume the soup after the qualifying gap/inversion, whether entered immediately or held.
Short: A tracked swing high is swept and rejected by a confirmed bar, then within the configured number of chart bars a qualifying bearish FVG or bearish IFVG forms with its entire zone below the swept level by at least the configured clearance; the setup has not been invalidated by a later confirmed close above its level on its source timeframe, is in an allowed session, and has not already produced an entry. If several soups qualify, use the most recent. When the delivery filter is enabled, enter only if delivery state is not bullish or close < the latest reclaimed opening; otherwise hold the signal and enter on the first such qualifying close within the configured hold window and an allowed session. Consume the soup after the qualifying gap/inversion, whether entered immediately or held.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= target; first move stop to entry if high >= breakeven trigger, then evaluate the stop, and if stop and target are both touched count the stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= target; first move stop to entry if low <= breakeven trigger, then evaluate the stop, and if stop and target are both touched count the stop.
