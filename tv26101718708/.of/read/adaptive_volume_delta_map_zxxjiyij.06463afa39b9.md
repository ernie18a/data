Calculation: Classified volume = For each completed lower-timeframe bar, or the chart bar when the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe, classify all its volume as buy if close > open, sell if close < open; otherwise classify it as buy if close > previous close, sell if close < previous close, and otherwise retain the previous classification, initially sell.
Calculation: Price-row volumes = Divide the chart bar's low-to-high range into consecutive equal-width price rows, starting at floor(low / row width) × row width. Row width is either a fixed parameter or the chart bar's high-low range divided by a row-count parameter minus one. Allocate each classified lower-timeframe bar's buy or sell volume to each row in proportion to the overlap of its low-to-high range with that row; allocate all its volume to a row if its low is above the row's lower boundary and its high is at or below the row's upper boundary.
Long: Sum of classified buy volume > sum of classified sell volume within the chart bar.
Short: Sum of classified buy volume <= sum of classified sell volume within the chart bar.
Long: Any price row except the lowest has buy volume > 0 AND its buy volume >= the sell volume in the row immediately below multiplied by an imbalance threshold.
Short: Any price row except the lowest and highest has sell volume > 0 AND its sell volume >= the buy volume in the row immediately above multiplied by an imbalance threshold.
