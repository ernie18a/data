Calculation: ATR = Average True Range; SMA = simple moving average; EMA = exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: DemandZone = created on a bar where close > open AND |close-open| >= a*ATR(previous bar) AND volume >= b*SMA(volume)(previous bar) (optional extra ANDs: close > previous bar high; previous bar close < previous bar open). The zone is the previous bar's [low, high] (or optionally its body [min(open,close), max(open,close)]), and its impulse volume is the impulse bar's volume. It is not created if it overlaps an active DemandZone. It is active from the next bar until a close < zone bottom, its age exceeds a max bar count, or it is evicted as the oldest when the active-zone cap is exceeded. It is consumed (usable once) only when it triggers an entry.
Calculation: SupplyZone = mirror of DemandZone: close < open AND |close-open| >= a*ATR(previous bar) AND volume >= b*SMA(volume)(previous bar) (optional extra ANDs: close < previous bar low; previous bar close > previous bar open). Same zone construction, overlap rule and lifetime, except it is invalidated by a close > zone top.
Calculation: LongRisk = signal-bar close - (zone bottom - k*ATR at signal bar); LongStop starts at (zone bottom - k*ATR at signal bar); LongTarget = signal close + R*LongRisk. For shorts: ShortStop = zone top + k*ATR at signal bar, ShortRisk = ShortStop - signal close, ShortTarget = signal close - R*ShortRisk. Stop and target levels are fixed at the signal bar. Entry fills on the next bar after the signal bar closes.
Long: position is flat AND an active unused DemandZone created on an earlier bar exists (check newest first) AND high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND close > open AND close > zone bottom AND LongRisk > 0 AND (optional) (min(open,close)-low)/(high-low) >= min wick fraction AND (optional) volume <= f*zone impulse volume AND (optional) close > EMA(close).
Short: position is flat AND an active unused SupplyZone created on an earlier bar exists (check newest first) AND high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND close < open AND close < zone top AND ShortRisk > 0 AND (optional) (high-max(open,close))/(high-low) >= min wick fraction AND (optional) volume <= f*zone impulse volume AND (optional) close < EMA(close).
Exit: long, low <= LongStop OR high >= LongTarget OR bars since entry >= max hold bars; additionally, once high >= entry price + be*LongRisk, LongStop is raised to the entry price (be = break-even trigger in R multiples).
Exit: short, high >= ShortStop OR low <= ShortTarget OR bars since entry >= max hold bars; additionally, once low <= entry price - be*ShortRisk, ShortStop is lowered to the entry price.
