Calculation: Active order-block zone = the immediately preceding candle’s full high/low range (or body, if selected), created by a bullish or bearish impulse; invalidate when a bar closes beyond the far edge, expire after the maximum age, remove the oldest when the zone count exceeds the maximum, and do not add overlapping zones of the same direction. Store the impulse bar’s volume and whether the zone has been used.
Long: close > open AND abs(close - open) >= prior-bar ATR × displacement multiplier AND volume >= prior-bar volume SMA × impulse-volume multiplier AND (close > prior-bar high if the prior-range-break filter is enabled) AND (prior close < prior open if the opposite-colour filter is enabled) AND long signals are allowed.
Short: close < open AND abs(close - open) >= prior-bar ATR × displacement multiplier AND volume >= prior-bar volume SMA × impulse-volume multiplier AND (close < prior-bar low if the prior-range-break filter is enabled) AND (prior close > prior open if the opposite-colour filter is enabled) AND short signals are allowed.
Long: position is flat AND an active bullish zone is touched (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) AND close > zone bottom AND zone is unused if first-touch is enabled AND (close > open if candle confirmation is enabled) AND (lower-wick length / bar range >= minimum wick percentage if rejection filter is enabled) AND (volume <= stored impulse volume × retest-volume multiplier if retest-volume filter is enabled) AND (close > EMA if trend filter is enabled) AND long signals are allowed.
Short: position is flat AND an active bearish zone is touched (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) AND close < zone top AND zone is unused if first-touch is enabled AND (close < open if candle confirmation is enabled) AND (upper-wick length / bar range >= minimum wick percentage if rejection filter is enabled) AND (volume <= stored impulse volume × retest-volume multiplier if retest-volume filter is enabled) AND (close < EMA if trend filter is enabled) AND short signals are allowed.
Exit: long, low <= stop (initially the signal bar’s zone bottom minus signal-bar ATR × stop-buffer multiplier; move to entry price after high >= average entry price + initial risk × break-even trigger) OR high >= signal close + initial risk × target R multiple OR elapsed bars since entry >= time-stop bars when the time stop is enabled.
Exit: short, high >= stop (initially the signal bar’s zone top plus signal-bar ATR × stop-buffer multiplier; move to entry price after low <= average entry price - initial risk × break-even trigger) OR low <= signal close - initial risk × target R multiple OR elapsed bars since entry >= time-stop bars when the time stop is enabled.
