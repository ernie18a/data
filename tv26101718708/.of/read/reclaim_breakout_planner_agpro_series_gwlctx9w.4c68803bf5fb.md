Calculation: ATR = Wilder's average true range; before ATR is available, use the current true range.
Calculation: Armed watch = On a bar after a pivot high or low is confirmed, compare the close and previous close with the latest confirmed pivot plus or minus the current ATR times the breakout threshold. Arm a bullish watch when close is above the upper threshold and previous close is at or below that same current threshold; arm a bearish watch when close is below the lower threshold and previous close is at or above that same current threshold. Store the pivot as the watch level and the current bar as the breakout bar. A new breakout replaces the previous watch, with bullish breakout taking priority if both occur. Cancel the watch when its age exceeds the retest window, when a bullish watch closes below its level minus current ATR times the pocket-width parameter, or when a bearish watch closes above its level plus that amount. Cancel it after an accepted reclaim.
Calculation: Reclaim candidate = An armed watch has age greater than zero and, on the same bar, its low is at or below the bullish watch level minus current ATR times the loss threshold and its close is at or above the level plus current ATR times the reclaim threshold, or its high is at or above the bearish watch level plus current ATR times the loss threshold and its close is at or below the level minus current ATR times the reclaim threshold.
Calculation: Acceptance score = Clamp the weighted sum of reclaim-distance, retest-depth, volume, trend, and wick scores to 0–100. Their respective weights are 0.28, 0.24, 0.18, 0.18, and 0.12. Normalize a value between a lower and upper bound by scaling linearly to 0–100 and clamping to that range. Reclaim-distance score normalizes the directional distance from close to watch level in ATR units between zero and the greater of the breakout threshold and the reclaim threshold plus 0.18. Retest-depth score rises linearly from zero at zero depth to 100 at 0.55 times the pocket-width parameter, then falls linearly to zero at 1.75 times that parameter; depth is the directional penetration of the watch level by the bar's low or high, in ATR units, floored at zero. Volume score normalizes volume divided by its simple moving average between 0.75 and 1.55; use a ratio of one if the average is unavailable or nonpositive, and a score of 55 if volume is unavailable. Trend score is 100 when close and the shorter EMA are both on the reclaim side of the longer EMA, 68 when only close is on that side, and 32 otherwise. Wick score is clamp(180 times the reclaim-side wick fraction plus 36 times the directional close-location fraction plus 20 times the absolute candle-body fraction, 0, 100); all fractions use the bar's high-low range.
Calculation: Active plan = When a reclaim candidate's acceptance score is at least the watch threshold, store its direction, watch level, bar, and ATR. Set its risk edge to the lesser of the reclaim bar's low and level minus ATR times the risk-buffer parameter for a bullish plan, or the greater of the reclaim bar's high and level plus ATR times the risk-buffer parameter for a bearish plan. Set risk distance to the greater of the absolute level-to-risk-edge distance and 0.20 times that ATR. Set the target to the level plus risk distance times the target-multiple parameter for a bullish plan, or minus that amount for a bearish plan. A new accepted reclaim replaces the active plan. Resolve the plan at the first risk-edge failure or target reach; expire it when its age exceeds the plan-expiry window or its resolved age exceeds the resolved-display window.
Long: Bullish armed watch is active.
Short: Bearish armed watch is active.
Long: Bullish reclaim candidate.
Short: Bearish reclaim candidate.
Long: Bullish active plan is unresolved AND close is at or above its level.
Short: Bearish active plan is unresolved AND close is at or below its level.
Exit: long, bullish active plan is unresolved AND its age is at most the plan-expiry window AND close is below its risk edge OR close is at or above its target.
Exit: short, bearish active plan is unresolved AND its age is at most the plan-expiry window AND close is above its risk edge OR close is at or below its target.
