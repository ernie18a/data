Calculation: Valuation weight = 1 in fixed-amount mode or when the valuation score is unavailable; otherwise clamp 1 − sensitivity × valuation score between the smaller and larger weight limits. The valuation score is log(close / SMA(close)) divided by the root mean square of that log distance over the same lookback; it is unavailable when the root mean square is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Schedule index = for a start-date anchor, the number of completed schedule periods since the trading date of the first chart bar on or after the configured start date. For month-based periods, use the anchor day of the month, clamped to the last day of shorter months. For a calendar anchor, count base calendar-period changes observed on or after the start date, starting at zero, and divide the count by the number of base periods per scheduled period, rounding down. The base calendar period is at least as long as the chart timeframe; its current, still-forming period identifies each bar. Missing scheduled dates advance to the next available bar.
Long: Bar time ≥ start date AND chart timeframe ≤ the supported base calendar period for a calendar anchor or ≤ the supported daily limit for a start-date anchor AND schedule index advances, or this is the first eligible bar under a start-date anchor AND the previous chart bar's valuation weight > 0. For end-of-period timing, require a previous scheduled boundary and attribute the entry to the previous chart bar; for start-of-period timing, attribute it to the current bar.
