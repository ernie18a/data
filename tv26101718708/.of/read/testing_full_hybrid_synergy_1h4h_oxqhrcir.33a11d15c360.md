Calculation: Short EMA and long EMA = exponential moving averages using shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively.
Calculation: MACD histogram = the MACD line (fast EMA minus slow EMA) minus its signal line (EMA of the MACD line).
Calculation: Reverse MACD price threshold = max(0, (fast smoothing factor × previous fast EMA − slow smoothing factor × previous slow EMA) ÷ (fast smoothing factor − slow smoothing factor)); each smoothing factor = 2 ÷ (lookback + 1).
Calculation: MACD signal-cross price threshold = max(0, N ÷ D), where a, b, c are the smoothing factors for the fast EMA, slow EMA, and signal EMA; F and S are the previous fast and slow EMAs of close; G is the previous signal EMA of the MACD line; N = Fac − Sbc − Fa + Sb + Sc + Gc − Fc − S − G + F; D = ac − bc − a + b.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of (the average of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback) and the simple moving average of close over that lookback, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Squeeze-off = the lower Bollinger Band is below the lower Keltner Channel and the upper Bollinger Band is above the upper Keltner Channel.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution = cumulative sum of volume × (2 × close − high − low) ÷ (high − low), with a zero contribution when high equals low.
Long: position is flat AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe long EMA AND higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram AND RSI is above its lower threshold AND RSI is below its upper threshold AND close > short EMA AND close > Reverse MACD price threshold AND close > MACD signal-cross price threshold AND Squeeze-off AND Squeeze momentum > 0 AND Squeeze momentum > previous Squeeze momentum AND Accumulation/distribution > previous Accumulation/distribution
Exit: Long, short EMA < long EMA OR RSI > exit threshold OR MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < previous MACD histogram OR close < MACD signal-cross price threshold OR Squeeze momentum < 0 AND Squeeze momentum < previous Squeeze momentum OR Accumulation/distribution < previous Accumulation/distribution OR high >= entry price × (1 + target-percentage parameter ÷ 100) OR low <= previous bar's close − (ATR × trailing-stop multiple)
