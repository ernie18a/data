Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed using the same number of chart bars on each side; evaluate it on the confirmation bar.
Long: A swing high is confirmed AND (no previous confirmed swing high exists OR its high > the previous confirmed swing high).
Long: A swing low is confirmed AND (no previous confirmed swing low exists OR its low > the previous confirmed swing low).
Short: A swing high is confirmed AND a previous confirmed swing high exists AND its high <= the previous confirmed swing high.
Short: A swing low is confirmed AND a previous confirmed swing low exists AND its low <= the previous confirmed swing low.
Long: Close > the latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND that swing high has not previously been broken since its confirmation.
Short: Close < the latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND that swing low has not previously been broken since its confirmation.
Long: Close > the high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND previous close <= that same high.
Short: Close < the low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND previous close >= that same low.
