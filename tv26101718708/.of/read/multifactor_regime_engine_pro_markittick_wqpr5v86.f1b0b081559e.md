Calculation: Let N be the core lookback; longer lookbacks are multiples of N. ATR and RSI use N; slow ATR uses a longer lookback. Fast and slow simple moving averages use N and a longer lookback, respectively. DMI uses N for both directional movement and ADX smoothing. Bollinger Bands use N. ROC uses N; its standard deviation uses N observations of ROC with a longer lookback. All calculations use chart bars. Treat unavailable prior state as bullish and unavailable prior bands as the current raw bands.
Calculation: Directional features = centered RSI scaled to [-1,1]; ADX capped at 1 after scaling, positive when +DI >= -DI and negative otherwise; close minus fast SMA divided by scaled ATR and clamped to [-1,1]; fast SMA minus slow SMA divided by ATR and clamped to [-1,1]; ROC divided by the scaled standard deviation of longer-lookback ROC and clamped to [-1,1]; and (number of rising closes minus number of non-rising closes) divided by N over the preceding N bars, excluding the current bar. Add a small positive constant to each denominator involving ATR or ROC standard deviation.
Calculation: Directional score = weighted sum of the directional features. Quality score = weighted sum of slow ATR divided by scaled ATR, capped at 1; volume divided by scaled volume SMA, capped at 1, or a neutral value when volume SMA is zero; Bollinger Band width divided by scaled ATR, capped and scaled to [0,1]; and absolute signed ADX feature. Add a small positive constant to ATR denominators. Bollinger position = (close - lower band) / band width, or the midpoint when band width is zero. Raw confidence = weighted sum of (directional score + 1) / 2, quality score, and the absolute distance of Bollinger position from its midpoint scaled by the full band width. Confidence = SMA of raw confidence, clamped to [0,1]; its smoothing length increases with N and decreases with prediction weight, subject to a minimum length.
Calculation: Adaptive upper and lower raw bands = (high + low) / 2 plus or minus ATR multiplied by a base band-width parameter and an affine decreasing function of confidence. If the preceding trend is bearish, upper band = minimum of the raw upper band and preceding upper band; otherwise upper band = raw upper band. If the preceding trend is bullish, lower band = maximum of the raw lower band and preceding lower band; otherwise lower band = raw lower band. Flip buffer = ATR multiplied by a positive factor that decreases as confidence increases.
Calculation: Regime shift = ATR > slow ATR multiplied by a volatility threshold AND the sign of centered RSI differs from its preceding value AND absolute centered RSI exceeds a threshold; substitute zero for unavailable preceding centered RSI. Trend starts bullish. If preceding trend is bullish, trend becomes bearish when close < lower band - flip buffer OR regime shift AND directional score < 0; otherwise it remains bullish. If preceding trend is bearish, trend becomes bullish when close > upper band + flip buffer OR regime shift AND directional score >= 0; otherwise it remains bearish.
Long: Trend is bullish.
Short: Trend is bearish.
Long: Preceding bar's trend is bullish AND trend two bars ago is bearish.
Short: Preceding bar's trend is bearish AND trend two bars ago is bullish.
Exit: long, preceding bar's trend is bullish AND trend two bars ago is bearish.
Exit: long, preceding bar's trend is bearish AND trend two bars ago is bullish.
