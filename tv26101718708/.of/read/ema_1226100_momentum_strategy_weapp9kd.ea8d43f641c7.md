Calculation: Long stop = the greater of entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) and each close × (1 − trailing-offset fraction) since entry for which trailing is enabled and close ≥ entry price × (1 + trailing-activation fraction).
Calculation: Short stop = the lesser of entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) and each close × (1 + trailing-offset fraction) since entry for which trailing is enabled and close ≤ entry price × (1 − trailing-activation fraction).
Long: Close > long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close > medium-term EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND RSI < upper entry threshold AND (short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA OR MACD line crosses above its signal line OR (previous RSI < oversold threshold AND current RSI > oversold threshold AND current RSI > previous RSI) OR close > highest high over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar OR (low ≤ short-term EMA × (1 + pullback fraction) AND close > short-term EMA AND close > open AND close − open > candle-body fraction × (high − low)) OR (previous close ≤ current lower Bollinger Band AND close > current lower Bollinger Band AND close > open AND close − open > candle-body fraction × (high − low))).
Short: Close < long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close < medium-term EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND RSI > lower entry threshold AND (short-term EMA crosses below medium-term EMA OR MACD line crosses below its signal line OR (previous RSI > overbought threshold AND current RSI < overbought threshold AND current RSI < previous RSI) OR close < lowest low over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar OR (high ≥ short-term EMA × (1 − pullback fraction) AND close < short-term EMA AND close < open AND open − close > candle-body fraction × (high − low)) OR (previous close ≥ current upper Bollinger Band AND close < current upper Bollinger Band AND close < open AND open − close > candle-body fraction × (high − low))).
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop active for the bar OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR short-term EMA crosses below medium-term EMA OR bars elapsed since the position first appeared ≥ maximum hold bars.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop active for the bar OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA OR bars elapsed since the position first appeared ≥ maximum hold bars.
