Calculation: Value-area overlap = intersection width of current and reference value areas divided by their union width.
Calculation: Normalized POC shift = (current profile POC − reference profile POC) / current value-area width.
Calculation: Signal bias = clamp(100 × [0.40 × clamp(normalized POC shift, −1, 1) + 0.25 × (2 × (1 − value-area overlap) − 1) + 0.20 × clamp((close − current POC) / current value-area width, −1, 1) + 0.15 × acceptance direction], −100, 100), where acceptance direction is +1 for upward acceptance, −1 for downward acceptance, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Session cumulative delta = cumulative sum within the session of volume for bars closing above their open, minus volume for bars closing below their open; unchanged bars contribute zero.
Long: Close > value-area high AND upward acceptance confirmation count reaches its configured threshold OR close is between value-area low and high AND the previous downward-outside count is > 0 and below its configured threshold OR close > value-area high OR signal bias > 0 OR signal regime is upward trend or upward breakout OR a confirmed pivot low is below the preceding pivot low while session cumulative delta at the pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot.
Short: Close < value-area low AND downward acceptance confirmation count reaches its configured threshold OR close is between value-area low and high AND the previous upward-outside count is > 0 and below its configured threshold OR close < value-area low OR signal bias < 0 OR signal regime is downward trend or downward breakout OR a confirmed pivot high is above the preceding pivot high while session cumulative delta at the pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot.
