Calculation: Baseline = KAMA, HMA, EMA, or ALMA of close over the selected lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed Z = optionally smooth a residual score with ALMA, HMA, or EMA. The residual is close minus baseline, its percentage of the absolute baseline, or log(close/baseline) multiplied by 100. The classical score is residual divided by its rolling standard deviation, without subtracting its mean. The modified score is 0.6745 times (residual minus its rolling median) divided by the rolling median of the series abs(residual minus its contemporaneous rolling median). A zero denominator gives a score of zero. Use the same lookback for baseline and residual statistics.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivots = highs or lows of smoothed Z confirmed by the selected numbers of bars on the left and right. Compare the two most recent pivots of the same type and the chart high or low at each pivot; evaluate divergence on the bar confirming the newer pivot. The pivot separation must not exceed the maximum divergence lookback.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed oscillator low pivot with lower price low and higher oscillator low than the preceding low pivot, AND, if the zone filter is enabled, the newer oscillator low is below the negative inner threshold. Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed oscillator high pivot with higher price high and lower oscillator high than the preceding high pivot, AND, if the zone filter is enabled, the newer oscillator high is above the positive inner threshold. Hidden bullish divergence = a confirmed oscillator low pivot with higher price low and lower oscillator low. Hidden bearish divergence = a confirmed oscillator high pivot with lower price high and higher oscillator high. Each divergence also requires pivot separation within the maximum divergence lookback and its divergence type to be enabled.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = EMA of the value clamped to [0, 1] of log(variance(close minus close at the selected lag) / variance(close minus previous close)) / (2 × log(selected lag)); use the same rolling variance lookback for both variances and floor each variance at a small positive constant.
Calculation: Bull and bear scores = weighted sums of their respective conditions, divided by the sum of all configured weights, including weights for disabled components. Bull conditions are: smoothed Z crosses above the negative outer threshold; regular OR hidden bullish divergence; baseline above its value at an earlier bar; enabled Hurst estimate below the mean-reversion threshold AND (regular bullish divergence OR the bullish Z crossover), OR enabled Hurst estimate above the higher trending threshold AND (hidden bullish divergence OR baseline above its earlier value AND smoothed Z below zero); enabled higher-timeframe confirmation AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Z below the negative inner threshold OR above zero AND below its value on the previous chart bar); enabled volume confirmation AND volume above zero AND its rolling Z-score above a positive threshold. Bear conditions are: smoothed Z crosses below the positive outer threshold; regular OR hidden bearish divergence; baseline below its value at the same earlier bar; enabled Hurst estimate below the mean-reversion threshold AND (regular bearish divergence OR the bearish Z crossover), OR enabled Hurst estimate above the trending threshold AND (hidden bearish divergence OR baseline below its earlier value AND smoothed Z above zero); enabled higher-timeframe confirmation AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Z above the positive inner threshold OR below zero AND above its value on the previous chart bar); the same enabled volume confirmation. Volume Z-score uses the baseline lookback. Higher-timeframe smoothed Z uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar, carried forward to chart bars. The outer Z threshold is greater than the inner threshold; both depend on the score method.
Long: Regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence.
Short: Regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence.
Long: Bull score is at least the lower signal threshold AND bull score > bear score.
Short: Bear score is at least the lower signal threshold AND bear score > bull score.
Long: Higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Z < negative inner threshold OR higher-timeframe smoothed Z > 0 AND higher-timeframe smoothed Z < its value on the previous chart bar).
Short: Higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Z > positive inner threshold OR higher-timeframe smoothed Z < 0 AND higher-timeframe smoothed Z > its value on the previous chart bar).
Long: Smoothed Z >= 0.
Short: Smoothed Z < 0.
Long: Oscillator-candle mode is selected AND the raw score computed using open <= the raw score computed using close.
Short: Oscillator-candle mode is selected AND the raw score computed using open > the raw score computed using close.
