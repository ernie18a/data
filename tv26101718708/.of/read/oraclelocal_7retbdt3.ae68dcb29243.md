Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by its moving average
Long: Position is flat AND close > EMA AND RSI > 55 AND relative volume >= 1 AND EMA > its value on the previous bar
Short: Position is flat AND close < EMA AND RSI < 45 AND relative volume >= 1 AND EMA < its value on the previous bar
Exit: Long, close < EMA OR (RSI < 50 AND previous-bar RSI >= 50)
Exit: Short, close > EMA OR (RSI > 50 AND previous-bar RSI <= 50)
