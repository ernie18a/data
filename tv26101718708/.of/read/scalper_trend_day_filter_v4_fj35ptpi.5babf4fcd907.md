Calculation: Session = bars whose opening times fall within the configured session; a new session starts on the first such bar after an out-of-session bar.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of session bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening window, starting with the first session bar. It is complete on the first session bar at or after the window's end.
Calculation: Long acceptance = opening range is complete AND every close in the acceptance lookback is above its high. Short acceptance = opening range is complete AND every close in the acceptance lookback is below its low.
Calculation: Long invalidation = since the session opened, long acceptance and close below the opening-range low have occurred on the same bar. Short invalidation = since the session opened, short acceptance and close above the opening-range high have occurred on the same bar. Reset both at each session open.
Calculation: Volume average and deviation = chart-bar volume SMA and population standard deviation over the same lookback, including the current bar. Relative volume = current volume / volume average, or zero when the average is not positive. Strong volume threshold = volume average + a deviation multiplier × deviation; use zero for unavailable deviation. Moderate volume threshold = volume average × a moderate-volume multiplier. Strong current volume = current volume >= strong volume threshold.
Calculation: Level-test volume = most recent chart-bar volume since the session opened for which the opening range was complete and absolute distance from close to the relevant opening-range boundary, divided by that boundary, was <= the retest tolerance; use zero before a test. Update it before evaluating the current bar. Level-volume score = two points if level-test volume >= the current strong volume threshold, otherwise one point if it >= the current moderate volume threshold, otherwise zero.
Calculation: ATR headroom = absolute difference between close and the current session's opening price / higher-timeframe ATR < an ATR-use threshold. Use the ATR of the higher-timeframe bar still forming, and require it to be available.
Calculation: Static bullish context = close > previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high. Static bearish context = close < previous completed higher-timeframe bar's low. Neutral context = neither static context is true. The higher timeframe for these boundaries is the same as for ATR.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of chart-bar typical price × volume / cumulative volume for session bars, reset at session open; unavailable if cumulative volume is zero. Previous-session VWAP is the final available session VWAP saved at the next session open.
Calculation: Session POC = midpoint of the fixed price bucket with the greatest accumulated session volume, choosing the lowest-index bucket on ties. At session open, set the grid range to a range multiplier × the previous completed higher-timeframe high-minus-low when available and positive, otherwise that multiplier × higher-timeframe ATR, using a fallback range if ATR is unavailable. Set the grid base to the previous higher-timeframe low minus a grid-offset fraction × grid range when that low is available, otherwise session-opening close minus a fallback fraction × grid range. Divide the range into a fixed number of equal buckets. For each session bar, add its entire volume to the bucket containing its typical price, clipping prices outside the grid to the nearest end bucket. Current-session POC includes the current bar; previous-session POC is the final POC saved at the next session open.
Calculation: Effective bullish context = static bullish context OR neutral context AND close > each available current-session VWAP, current-session POC, and previous-session POC. Effective bearish context = static bearish context OR neutral context AND close < each available one of those levels. An unavailable level imposes no restriction.
Calculation: Tape volumes = when tape analysis is enabled and more than one lower-timeframe bar exists inside the chart bar, compare each lower-timeframe close with the next lower-timeframe close: add the earlier bar's volume to bullish volume when its close is greater, bearish volume when it is lower, and absorbed volume when equal; exclude the final lower-timeframe bar's volume. Use completed lower-timeframe bars available within the current chart bar. If at most one lower-timeframe bar exists, assign the whole chart-bar volume to bullish, bearish, or absorbed volume according as chart close is above, below, or equal to chart open.
Calculation: Tape ratio = bullish volume / max(bearish volume + absorbed volume, minimum denominator) when bullish volume > bearish volume; negative bearish volume / max(bullish volume + absorbed volume, minimum denominator) when bearish volume > bullish volume; otherwise zero. When tape analysis is disabled, use a fixed positive ratio for an up candle, its negative for a down candle, and zero for a flat candle. Absorbed fraction = absorbed volume / total tape volume, or zero when total tape volume is zero; when tape analysis is disabled it is zero.
Calculation: Directional tape score = two points when tape ratio reaches the strong threshold in the direction being evaluated, otherwise one point when it reaches the weaker directional threshold, otherwise zero. The strong threshold is larger in magnitude than the weaker threshold.
Calculation: Directional opening-range score = one point for acceptance in that direction, plus its level-volume score, plus one point each for relative volume >= its minimum, ATR headroom, and static context in that direction.
Calculation: Directional breakout-quality score = directional tape score plus one point each for MACD line being above zero for long or below zero for short, RSI being >= its upper threshold for long or <= its lower threshold for short, directional level-test volume >= the current strong volume threshold, and close being above both available previous-session POC and previous-session VWAP for long or below both for short. An unavailable previous-session level imposes no restriction. MACD is the shorter-period EMA minus the longer-period EMA. Calculate MACD and RSI on a lower timeframe using the last completed lower-timeframe bar available within the chart bar; if that timeframe equals the chart timeframe, use the current chart bar. Substitute chart close for an unavailable MACD EMA and a neutral RSI value for unavailable RSI.
Calculation: Tight balance = the highest high minus the lowest low over a balance lookback ending at the preceding chart bar is positive and <= current close × a balance-width fraction. Balance high and low are those preceding-bar lookback extremes.
Long: In session AND static bullish context AND long opening-range score = the direct-signal score threshold AND long breakout-quality score >= the direct-signal quality threshold AND long tape score > zero AND previous-session POC is unavailable OR the preceding conditions hold AND previous-session POC is available AND it is not above close within the configured resistance-distance fraction of close.
Short: In session AND static bearish context AND short opening-range score = the direct-signal score threshold AND short breakout-quality score >= the direct-signal quality threshold AND short tape score > zero AND previous-session POC is unavailable OR the preceding conditions hold AND previous-session POC is available AND it is not below close within the configured support-distance fraction of close.
Long: In session AND long acceptance AND long invalidation is false AND close >= opening-range high AND (close − opening-range high) / opening-range high <= retest tolerance AND (strong current volume OR absorbed fraction >= absorption threshold) AND relative volume >= its minimum AND ATR headroom AND static bearish context is false AND long breakout-quality score >= the retest quality threshold AND long opening-range score excluding its static-context point >= the retest opening-range threshold AND effective bullish context AND (long tape score > zero OR absorbed fraction >= absorption threshold).
Short: In session AND short acceptance AND short invalidation is false AND close <= opening-range low AND (opening-range low − close) / opening-range low <= retest tolerance AND (strong current volume OR absorbed fraction >= absorption threshold) AND relative volume >= its minimum AND ATR headroom AND static bullish context is false AND short breakout-quality score >= the retest quality threshold AND short opening-range score excluding its static-context point >= the retest opening-range threshold AND effective bearish context AND (short tape score > zero OR absorbed fraction >= absorption threshold).
Long: In session AND (short acceptance AND short invalidation is false OR long acceptance AND long invalidation is false) AND tight balance AND close > balance high AND strong current volume AND relative volume >= its minimum AND ATR headroom AND static bearish context is false AND long breakout-quality score >= the balance-break quality threshold AND effective bullish context.
Short: In session AND (long acceptance AND long invalidation is false OR short acceptance AND short invalidation is false) AND tight balance AND close < balance low AND strong current volume AND relative volume >= its minimum AND ATR headroom AND static bullish context is false AND short breakout-quality score >= the balance-break quality threshold AND effective bearish context.
