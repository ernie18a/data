Calculation: Cluster bounds = highest high and lowest low over the cluster lookback, including the current bar. When their difference is at most ATR multiplied by the tightness parameter and contraction is disabled or ATR is less than ATR at the contraction lookback, replace the stored upper and lower bounds with these values and activate the bounds. Otherwise retain the previous bounds; once activated, they remain active.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 0.5.
Calculation: Entry state = on an entry signal while flat, store the close as entry price, set the trade-bar count to zero and phase 2 to false. At the end of every calculation while the reported position is flat, clear the entry price and reset the trade-bar count and phase. Because orders are processed on the close, this reset also occurs on the entry-signal calculation; consequently the entry-price-dependent exits below have undefined levels unless the entry price is retained by the execution environment.
Long: Position is flat AND cluster bounds are active AND close > stored upper bound + ATR multiplied by breakout buffer AND previous close <= previous stored upper bound + previous ATR multiplied by breakout buffer AND session restriction is disabled or the bar is in the configured session AND entries today < daily entry limit AND bars since the last fully closed position >= cooldown or no prior exit exists AND volume confirmation is disabled or volume is unavailable or volume <= 0 or volume > volume SMA multiplied by volume parameter AND strong-close filter is disabled or close position >= strong-close threshold AND trend filter is disabled or close > EMA AND ADX > ADX threshold.
Short: Position is flat AND cluster bounds are active AND close < stored lower bound - ATR multiplied by breakout buffer AND previous close >= previous stored lower bound - previous ATR multiplied by breakout buffer AND session restriction is disabled or the bar is in the configured session AND entries today < daily entry limit AND bars since the last fully closed position >= cooldown or no prior exit exists AND volume confirmation is disabled or volume is unavailable or volume <= 0 or volume > volume SMA multiplied by volume parameter AND strong-close filter is disabled or close position <= 1 - strong-close threshold AND trend filter is disabled or close < EMA AND ADX > ADX threshold.
Exit: Long, phase 2 is false AND close >= entry price multiplied by (1 + first target percentage); close half and set phase 2 true.
Exit: Short, phase 2 is false AND close <= entry price multiplied by (1 - first target percentage); close half and set phase 2 true.
Exit: Long, phase 2 is false AND low <= entry price multiplied by (1 - initial stop percentage) OR phase 2 is false AND high >= entry price multiplied by (1 + first target percentage) OR phase 2 is true AND low <= entry price multiplied by (1 + remaining-position stop percentage) OR phase 2 is true AND high >= entry price multiplied by (1 + first target percentage multiplied by second target multiplier); close the full remaining position with stop or limit orders.
Exit: Short, phase 2 is false AND high >= entry price multiplied by (1 + initial stop percentage) OR phase 2 is false AND low <= entry price multiplied by (1 - first target percentage) OR phase 2 is true AND high >= entry price multiplied by (1 - remaining-position stop percentage) OR phase 2 is true AND low <= entry price multiplied by (1 - first target percentage multiplied by second target multiplier); close the full remaining position with stop or limit orders.
Exit: Long or short, maximum-hold exit is enabled AND trade-bar count >= maximum hold, where the count increments on each calculation with an open position.
Exit: Long or short, session-end exit is enabled AND the bar is outside the configured session.
