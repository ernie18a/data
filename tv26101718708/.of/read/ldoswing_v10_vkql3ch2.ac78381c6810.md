Calculation: Swing-bar indexing = bar 0 is the last completed bar on the selected swing timeframe; for a lower timeframe, evaluate each completed lower-timeframe bar, and for a higher timeframe, use only the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: (5-bar layout: close[4] < open[4] AND close[3] < open[3] AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[0] > open[0]) OR (4-bar layout: close[3] < open[3] AND close[2] < open[2] AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[0] > open[0]) OR (3-bar layout: close[2] < open[2] AND close[0] > open[0]); when the structure-lookback filter is enabled, also require the layout's pivot low to be lower than the lows of the preceding lookback bars and the moving-away bars.
Short: (5-bar layout: close[4] > open[4] AND close[3] > open[3] AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[0] < open[0]) OR (4-bar layout: close[3] > open[3] AND close[2] > open[2] AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[0] < open[0]) OR (3-bar layout: close[2] > open[2] AND close[0] < open[0]); when the structure-lookback filter is enabled, also require the layout's pivot high to be higher than the highs of the preceding lookback bars and the moving-away bars.
Exit: long, low < each active swing-low level (or close < each level when close-based invalidation is selected)
Exit: short, high > each active swing-high level (or close > each level when close-based invalidation is selected)
