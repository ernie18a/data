Calculation: Active liquidity walls = On each completed chart bar, first process existing walls: remove an upper wall if the selected price high reaches or exceeds its price, and remove a lower wall if the selected price low reaches or falls below its price; the selected high and low are either the bar's high and low or the maximum and minimum of open and close. Otherwise multiply its intensity by an exponential half-life decay factor, and remove it if the decayed intensity is below a parameter times the volume SMA. Then, if volume and its SMA are positive, add upper and lower walls for each leverage tier at HLC3 × (1 ± (1 / leverage − maintenance margin fraction)), with intensity equal to volume × (base coefficient + relative-volume coefficient × min(volume / volume SMA, relative-volume cap)) × tier weight. Add an upper wall at each confirmed pivot high and a lower wall at each confirmed pivot low, with intensity equal to the pivot bar's volume × stop-liquidity weight; use equal left and right pivot lookbacks. For each added wall, merge it into the first existing same-side wall within an ATR-multiple distance by adding intensity without changing the existing price; otherwise append it. After additions, remove the weakest walls until the active-wall limit is met. Start with no walls.
Calculation: Liquidity imbalance = Sum intensities of active lower walls below close and within a maximum percentage distance of close, divided by the sum of those intensities and the intensities of active upper walls above close and within the same distance; use a neutral value when the denominator is zero.
Long: Liquidity imbalance <= upper-pool dominance threshold
Short: Liquidity imbalance >= lower-pool dominance threshold
