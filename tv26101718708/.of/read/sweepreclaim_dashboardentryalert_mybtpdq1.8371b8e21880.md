Calculation: Levels = the high and low of the most recently completed regular session, and the high and low of the current overnight session using chart bars so far, or of the most recently completed overnight session when outside it. Sessions, days and time zone are configurable.
Calculation: Active levels = available levels, except that when the regular-session and overnight highs differ by no more than a configurable percentage of current close, exclude the lower high; similarly, when the lows differ by no more than that tolerance, exclude the higher low. Equal levels remain active.
Calculation: Sweep state = track each level separately, updating it only on completed chart bars while the level is active. Reset all states at the start of each regular session. From idle, start an upward sweep when previous close < current level AND high > current level; otherwise start a downward sweep when previous close > current level AND low < current level. Set the sweep extreme to that bar's high or low, elapsed bars to zero, and beyond-close count to zero. On each later active bar, increment elapsed bars and update the extreme with the highest high or lowest low since the sweep began. Count each close >= the current level for an upward sweep or <= the current level for a downward sweep, including the starting bar. Cancel the sweep if a beyond close makes the count exceed its maximum or occurs when elapsed bars >= the maximum. Otherwise, the first close on the opposite side is a reclaim; evaluate it and then reset the state even if the decision fails. Use the current level on every bar, even if it changes during the sweep.
Calculation: Reclaim risk = absolute difference between reclaim close and stop, where stop is the sweep extreme plus a configurable percentage of reclaim close for an upward sweep, or minus that percentage for a downward sweep. The target is the nearest active level strictly below the reclaimed level for an upward sweep or strictly above it for a downward sweep; previously swept levels remain eligible.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or one half when high equals low.
Long: For any active level, close >= level.
Short: For any active level, close < level.
Long: A downward sweep reclaims an active level AND beyond-close count <= its maximum AND elapsed bars <= its maximum AND (level - sweep extreme) / level is between the configured minimum and maximum sweep-depth fractions inclusive AND close position >= the configured rejection fraction AND (the candle-direction filter is disabled OR close > open) AND the bar is in either configured trading window AND reclaim risk > 0 AND a target exists AND abs(target - close) / reclaim risk >= the configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Short: An upward sweep reclaims an active level AND beyond-close count <= its maximum AND elapsed bars <= its maximum AND (sweep extreme - level) / level is between the configured minimum and maximum sweep-depth fractions inclusive AND close position <= one minus the configured rejection fraction AND (the candle-direction filter is disabled OR close < open) AND the bar is in either configured trading window AND reclaim risk > 0 AND a target exists AND abs(target - close) / reclaim risk >= the configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Calculation: Signal trade levels = for the latest valid sweep-reclaim signal, fix entry at signal close, stop at reclaim risk's stop, first target at entry plus or minus a configurable multiple of reclaim risk in the trade direction, and final target at reclaim risk's target. Replace these levels on each newer valid signal; evaluate hits starting on the signal bar.
Exit: Long, low <= signal trade stop OR high >= signal trade final target.
Exit: Short, high >= signal trade stop OR low <= signal trade final target.
