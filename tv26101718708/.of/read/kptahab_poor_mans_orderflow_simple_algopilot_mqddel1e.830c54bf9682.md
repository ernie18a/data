Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > a multiplier times the simple moving average of volume over the preceding volume-lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = body ratio < absorption threshold AND volume spike AND close < open.
Calculation: Bearish absorption = body ratio < absorption threshold AND volume spike AND close > open.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = body ratio > impulse threshold AND volume spike AND high - low > a multiplier times the simple moving average of high - low over the same preceding volume-lookback bars AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish impulse = body ratio > impulse threshold AND volume spike AND high - low > a multiplier times the simple moving average of high - low over the same preceding volume-lookback bars AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish cluster = count of bullish absorptions over the cluster lookback, including the current bar, >= cluster threshold.
Calculation: Bearish cluster = count of bearish absorptions over the cluster lookback, including the current bar, >= cluster threshold.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = a bullish absorption exists within the preceding entry-lookback bars AND previous close <= high of the most recent such absorption AND close > open AND close > that absorption's high.
Calculation: Bearish breakout = a bearish absorption exists within the preceding entry-lookback bars AND previous close >= low of the most recent such absorption AND close < open AND close < that absorption's low.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price times (1 - stop percentage) in fixed mode; in trailing mode, initialize at that value and on each bar take the maximum of close times (1 - stop percentage) and the previous stop, first multiplying the previous stop by current average entry price / previous average entry price if the average entry changes. If breakeven is enabled and the preceding bar's high strictly exceeded the current first target during this position, raise the stop to at least the current average entry price.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price times (1 + stop percentage) in fixed mode; in trailing mode, initialize at that value and on each bar take the minimum of close times (1 + stop percentage) and the previous stop, first multiplying the previous stop by current average entry price / previous average entry price if the average entry changes. If breakeven is enabled and the preceding bar's low strictly fell below the current first target during this position, lower the stop to at most the current average entry price.
Long: Bullish breakout OR bullish impulse.
Short: Bearish breakout OR bearish impulse.
Long: Position side = long AND loss repurchase enabled AND close < average entry price times (1 - repurchase percentage) AND bar close is within the enabled date range AND bar is in the enabled session and closes before its end AND no session close or signal-based close is requested.
Short: Position side = short AND loss repurchase enabled AND close > average entry price times (1 + repurchase percentage) AND bar close is within the enabled date range AND bar is in the enabled session and closes before its end AND no session close or signal-based close is requested.
Exit: long, signal closing enabled AND ((impulse close mode AND bearish impulse) OR (cluster-plus-impulse close mode AND bearish cluster AND bearish impulse) OR (confirmed-opposite-entry close mode AND bearish cluster AND bearish breakout) OR (reversal mode AND selected short entry signal)).
Exit: short, signal closing enabled AND ((impulse close mode AND bullish impulse) OR (cluster-plus-impulse close mode AND bullish cluster AND bullish impulse) OR (confirmed-opposite-entry close mode AND bullish cluster AND bullish breakout) OR (reversal mode AND selected long entry signal)).
Calculation: Selected long entry signal = bullish breakout in direct or cluster-and-signal mode; bullish cluster AND bullish breakout in cluster-confirmed mode; bullish impulse in impulse mode; bullish cluster AND bullish impulse in cluster-plus-impulse mode.
Calculation: Selected short entry signal = bearish breakout in direct or cluster-and-signal mode; bearish cluster AND bearish breakout in cluster-confirmed mode; bearish impulse in impulse mode; bearish cluster AND bearish impulse in cluster-plus-impulse mode.
Exit: long, high >= average entry price times (1 + enabled target percentage), for each enabled partial target and the final target.
Exit: short, low <= average entry price / (1 + enabled target percentage), for each enabled partial target and the final target.
Exit: long, stop enabled AND (low <= long stop OR close < long stop).
Exit: short, stop enabled AND (high >= short stop OR close > short stop).
Exit: long, session enabled AND (bar is outside the selected session OR bar is inside the session AND bar close is at or after the session end).
Exit: short, session enabled AND (bar is outside the selected session OR bar is inside the session AND bar close is at or after the session end).
