Calculation: True Strength Index = 100 × double-smoothed close change / double-smoothed absolute close change; the first smoothing uses the shorter lookback and the second the longer lookback
Calculation: Pivotal levels = divide the range between the selected range high and range low into eight equal steps; the range is either a rolling chart-bar range or the previous completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Strong high/low = confirmed local pivot high/low that is also the highest/lowest value over a longer lookback
Calculation: Support/resistance blocks = zones centered on confirmed local pivot lows/highs, respectively, created only when the corresponding bullish/bearish setup passes; reject when price touches the zone and closes bullish above its bottom / bearish below its top
Calculation: Buy score = sum of weighted points for: buy context; bullish moving-average confirmation; price above both 200-period averages; price above all three longer-period averages; bullish stochastic; bullish structure; bullish candle; bullish sweep; discount; optional higher-timeframe bullish bias; near support block; support-block rejection; near S2 or another buy pivotal level; oversold extreme; bullish setup
Calculation: Sell score = sum of weighted points for: sell context; bearish moving-average confirmation; price below both 200-period averages; price below all three longer-period averages; bearish stochastic; bearish structure; bearish candle; bearish sweep; premium; optional higher-timeframe bearish bias; near resistance block; resistance-block rejection; near R6 or another sell pivotal level; overbought extreme; bearish setup
Long: Buy score >= its threshold AND (near a strong low OR bullish sweep OR support-block rejection OR near support block OR in a buy pivotal zone) AND (higher-timeframe bullish bias if enabled) AND NOT (near a strong high OR bearish sweep OR resistance-block rejection OR near resistance block OR in a sell pivotal zone) AND (first bar meeting these conditions if first-signal-only is enabled)
Short: Sell score >= its threshold AND (near a strong high OR bearish sweep OR resistance-block rejection OR near resistance block OR in a sell pivotal zone) AND (higher-timeframe bearish bias if enabled) AND NOT (near a strong low OR bullish sweep OR support-block rejection OR near support block OR in a buy pivotal zone) AND (first bar meeting these conditions if first-signal-only is enabled)
