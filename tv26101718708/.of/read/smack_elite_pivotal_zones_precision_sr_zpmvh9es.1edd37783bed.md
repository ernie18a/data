Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is a high or low preceded by the specified number of lower highs or higher lows and followed by the specified number of lower or higher values; it becomes available on the confirming bar. A strong pivot is also the highest high or lowest low over its lookback ending on the pivot bar. Retain the latest confirmed strong high and low.
Calculation: Pivot-zone bounds are either the highest and lowest chart-bar highs and lows over a lookback, or the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; optionally use candle-body extremes instead. Divide their range into equal steps, with a minimum step equal to a price-increment parameter. Support levels are the lower bound and the next three levels above it; resistance levels are the upper bound and the next three levels below it. The oversold and overbought extremes are respectively one step below the lower bound and one step above the upper bound. A level is near when its distance from close is at most a signal-distance multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Maintain a limited oldest-first list of support and resistance blocks. On confirmation of a separate pivot low or high, create the corresponding block centered on that pivot and extending an ATR multiple in each direction, provided the corresponding optional setup filters pass, the same-type creation cooldown has elapsed, and its center is farther than a spacing multiple of current ATR from every existing block center. Process resistance before support. Remove the oldest block when capacity is reached. A support block becomes resistance when close is below its bottom; a resistance block becomes support when close is above its top. A block is near when close is within a confirmation-distance multiple of ATR from its center OR the bar's high-low range overlaps the block. A support rejection is low <= block top AND close > block bottom AND close > open; a resistance rejection is high >= block bottom AND close < block top AND close < open. Evaluate proximity and rejection after updating block type.
Calculation: Bullish setup = chart timeframe and date within their respective optional allowed ranges AND the bar is confirmed if that option is enabled AND each enabled momentum filter passes: TSI > its signal EMA AND TSI > its previous value; RSI > its buy threshold AND RSI >= its previous value; close > HMA AND HMA > its previous value; CCI of typical price > its lower threshold AND CCI > its previous value. Bearish setup reverses the inequalities, using the RSI sell threshold and CCI upper threshold. TSI is 100 times the short-then-long EMA of close change divided by the same smoothing of absolute close change, or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Bullish structure is retained after close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high OR the latest confirmed pivot low is higher than its predecessor; otherwise bearish structure is retained after close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low OR the latest confirmed pivot high is not higher than its predecessor. Bullish takes precedence when both apply. Structure starts neutral.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < previous bar's latest strong low AND close > that low; bearish sweep = high > previous bar's latest strong high AND close < that high.
Calculation: Buy signal context = close within the signal-distance ATR multiple of the latest strong low OR bullish sweep OR support rejection OR near support block OR near any support level OR close <= oversold extreme. Sell signal context = close within the signal-distance ATR multiple of the latest strong high OR bearish sweep OR resistance rejection OR near resistance block OR near any resistance level OR close >= overbought extreme.
Calculation: Buy score context = close within the separate, wider score-distance ATR multiple of the latest strong low OR a strong low was confirmed within a freshness lookback OR bullish sweep OR near support block OR near any support level OR close <= oversold extreme. Sell score context is the corresponding strong-high, bearish-sweep, resistance and overbought condition.
Calculation: Use a shorter and a longer length for DEMA and EMA, and the longer length for SMA, all on the selected price source. Bullish MA confirmation = close above all five averages OR close crosses above either shorter average OR any of the five averages rises from its previous value. Bearish MA confirmation reverses these comparisons. Higher-timeframe bullish or bearish bias requires the higher-timeframe close to be above or below all five averages calculated on higher-timeframe close; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time. When the bias filter is disabled, both biases pass.
Calculation: Fast and slow stochastic are SMAs of stochastic calculated with respectively shorter and longer lookbacks and a common smoothing length.
Calculation: Buy score is the capped sum of configurable weights awarded independently for: buy score context; bullish MA confirmation; close above both shorter averages; close above all three longer averages; fast stochastic > slow stochastic AND fast stochastic > its previous value; bullish structure; close > open; bullish sweep; latest strong high > latest strong low AND close below their midpoint; bullish higher-timeframe bias passing; near support block; support rejection; proximity to the third support level, otherwise proximity to any other support level; close <= oversold extreme; bullish setup. Sell score uses the corresponding opposite conditions, the third resistance level below the upper bound, and the other resistance levels. The proximity-to-third-level and proximity-to-other-level weights are mutually exclusive. Cap each score at its configured maximum.
Long: A support-block pivot is confirmed AND bullish setup passes when precise filtering is enabled AND the support-block cooldown and spacing conditions pass.
Short: A resistance-block pivot is confirmed AND bearish setup passes when precise filtering is enabled AND the resistance-block cooldown and spacing conditions pass.
Long: Buy score >= its threshold AND buy signal context.
Short: Sell score >= its threshold AND sell signal context.
Long: Buy score > sell score.
Short: Sell score > buy score.
Long: Bullish setup.
Short: Bearish setup AND bullish setup is false.
Long: Fast stochastic > slow stochastic AND fast stochastic > its previous value.
Short: Fast stochastic < slow stochastic AND fast stochastic < its previous value.
Long: TSI > its signal EMA AND TSI > its previous value.
Short: TSI < its signal EMA AND TSI < its previous value.
Long: Close is within the signal-distance ATR multiple of the third support level.
Short: Close is within the signal-distance ATR multiple of the third resistance level below the upper bound AND close is not within that distance of the third support level.
