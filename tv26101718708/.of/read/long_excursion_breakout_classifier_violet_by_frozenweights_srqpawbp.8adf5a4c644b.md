Calculation: Breakout score = sigmoid of a linear combination of two convolutional filter banks, including their biases. Normalize each sampled open, high, low, and close by subtracting the current close and dividing by the current Wilder ATR; normalize each sampled volume by dividing by the current volume SMA and subtracting one. The near bank samples consecutive bars ending at the current bar; the far bank samples bars at a fixed stride over a longer lookback, also ending at the current bar. Each filter is a weighted sum of its normalized samples plus a bias, with no activation before the linear head. Filter weights, head weights, biases, lookbacks, stride, ATR length, and volume SMA length are parameters.
Calculation: Effective threshold = a fixed threshold when calibration is disabled; otherwise the linearly interpolated percentile of breakout scores over the calibration window including the current score, available only after at least the calibration-window length of finite scores has been produced.
Long: Sufficient history is available AND current ATR > 0 AND current volume SMA > 0 AND breakout score > effective threshold
