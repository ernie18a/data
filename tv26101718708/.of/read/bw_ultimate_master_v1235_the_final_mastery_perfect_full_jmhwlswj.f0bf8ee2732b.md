Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest over a symmetric number of chart bars before and after it; recognize it on the confirmation bar and retain the latest confirmed value.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator = short-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2 minus long-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Acceleration Oscillator = Awesome Oscillator minus its SMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe directional alignment = on each of three successively higher timeframes, assign its timeframe weight to the bullish score if Awesome Oscillator > 0 or to the bearish score if Awesome Oscillator < 0; the longest timeframe has the largest weight and the other two have equal smaller weights. Bullish alignment requires the bullish score > the bearish score and >= the alignment threshold; bearish alignment reverses the scores. Higher-timeframe calculations use the still-forming bar in real time and the last completed bar on historical chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish price action = (close > open AND close >= max(previous open, previous close) AND open <= min(previous open, previous close)) OR (min(close, open) - low >= minimum lower-wick-to-body ratio × max(abs(close - open), minimum body size) AND high - max(close, open) <= maximum upper-wick-to-body ratio × max(abs(close - open), minimum body size)).
Calculation: Bearish price action = (close < open AND close <= min(previous open, previous close) AND open >= max(previous open, previous close)) OR (high - max(close, open) >= minimum upper-wick-to-body ratio × max(abs(close - open), minimum body size) AND min(close, open) - low <= maximum lower-wick-to-body ratio × max(abs(close - open), minimum body size)).
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = the count of true conditions among: close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high; bullish price action; low <= EMA of close AND close > EMA; bullish higher-timeframe alignment AND chart-timeframe Acceleration Oscillator > its previous value.
Calculation: Bearish confluence count = the count of true conditions among: close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low; bearish price action; high >= EMA of close AND close < EMA; bearish higher-timeframe alignment AND chart-timeframe Acceleration Oscillator < its previous value.
Calculation: Bullish reversal retest = after low <= the lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars, keep a sweep active through the sweep-expiry number of bars, restarting its age on each new sweep; if while active close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high, clear the sweep and set a pending retest level to the average of close and the lowest low over the sweep lookback including the current bar. Replace the level on another qualifying break; the pending retest has no expiry. The retest is true when pending AND low <= the level AND close > open; clear it immediately when true, even if the signal cooldown blocks the entry.
Calculation: Bearish reversal retest = after high >= the highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback bars, keep a sweep active through the sweep-expiry number of bars, restarting its age on each new sweep; if while active close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low, clear the sweep and set a pending retest level to the average of close and the highest high over the sweep lookback including the current bar. Replace the level on another qualifying break; the pending retest has no expiry. The retest is true when pending AND high >= the level AND close < open; clear it immediately when true, even if the signal cooldown blocks the entry.
Calculation: Signal cooldown is clear when bars since the last long or short confluence/retest signal > cooldown bars OR abs(close - that signal's close) > ATR × cooldown-distance multiplier; before any such signal the distance condition is true. Evaluate both directions before updating the shared last-signal bar and close.
Long: (bullish confluence count >= confluence threshold AND RSI <= upper RSI threshold OR bullish reversal retest) AND signal cooldown is clear.
Short: (bearish confluence count >= confluence threshold AND RSI >= lower RSI threshold OR bearish reversal retest) AND signal cooldown is clear.
Long: close > the open of the first chart bar crossing the configured daily session-start time in the session timezone.
Short: close <= the open of the first chart bar crossing the configured daily session-start time in the session timezone.
Long: Awesome Oscillator > 0 on any displayed timeframe.
Short: Awesome Oscillator <= 0 on any displayed timeframe.
Long: Acceleration Oscillator < 0 AND Acceleration Oscillator >= its previous value on any displayed timeframe OR Acceleration Oscillator >= 0 AND Acceleration Oscillator > its previous value on any displayed timeframe.
Short: Acceleration Oscillator >= 0 AND Acceleration Oscillator <= its previous value on any displayed timeframe OR Acceleration Oscillator < 0 AND Acceleration Oscillator < its previous value on any displayed timeframe.
Long: bullish higher-timeframe alignment AND the spread between the maximum and minimum of three SMMAs of midpoint price with different lookbacks >= ATR × sleeping threshold.
Short: bearish higher-timeframe alignment AND the spread between the maximum and minimum of three SMMAs of midpoint price with different lookbacks >= ATR × sleeping threshold.
Calculation: Displayed-timeframe oscillator values use the still-forming bar in real time and the last completed bar on historical chart bars when the displayed timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe; when it is lower, use the last available intrabar.
