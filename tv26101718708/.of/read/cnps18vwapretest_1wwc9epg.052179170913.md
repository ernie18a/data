Calculation: VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each trading session; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe VWAP = VWAP calculated from higher-timeframe bars; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Long: The most recent close crossover above VWAP occurred no more than a configurable number of bars ago AND at least one low from that crossover bar through the current bar is <= current VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND (rejection filter disabled OR min(open, close) - low > configurable ratio × abs(close - open)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configurable factor × SMA of volume) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > configurable factor × ATR) AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP).
Short: The most recent close crossunder below VWAP occurred no more than a configurable number of bars ago AND at least one high from that crossunder bar through the current bar is >= current VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND (rejection filter disabled OR high - max(open, close) > configurable ratio × abs(close - open)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configurable factor × SMA of volume) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > configurable factor × ATR) AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP).
Exit: long, low <= entry price - configurable stop distance OR high >= entry price + configurable profit distance OR bar open time < configurable session end time AND bar close time >= configurable session end time.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + configurable stop distance OR low <= entry price - configurable profit distance OR bar open time < configurable session end time AND bar close time >= configurable session end time.
