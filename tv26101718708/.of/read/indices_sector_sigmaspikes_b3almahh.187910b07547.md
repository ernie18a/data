Calculation: Sigma = screened bar return divided by the population standard deviation of returns over a lookback ending on the preceding screened bar; return = (close - previous close) / previous close. The screening timeframe may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. Select either the current screened bar or the preceding screened bar. For a higher timeframe, the current bar is still forming in real time and is the last completed bar on historical chart bars; for a lower timeframe, select the last available screened bar within the chart bar.
