Calculation: Fractal high = on the chart timeframe or a selectable higher or lower timeframe, let the candidate be the bar N bars before the evaluation bar. Its high must exceed the highs of all N subsequent bars. For at least one k from 0 through 4, its high must also be greater than or equal to the highs of the immediately preceding k bars and greater than the highs of the N bars preceding those k bars. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; emit each candidate only once per timeframe.
Calculation: Fractal low = on the chart timeframe or a selectable higher or lower timeframe, let the candidate be the bar N bars before the evaluation bar. Its low must be below the lows of all N subsequent bars. For at least one k from 0 through 4, its low must also be less than or equal to the lows of the immediately preceding k bars and less than the lows of the N bars preceding those k bars. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; emit each candidate only once per timeframe.
Calculation: Resistance levels = the greater of open and close of each confirmed fractal-high candidate on a selectable timeframe; retain a limited number of the most recent levels. A level becomes permanently broken when a chart-bar close exceeds it, including on the bar it is created.
Calculation: Support levels = the lesser of open and close of each confirmed fractal-low candidate on the same selectable timeframe and with the same lookback as the resistance levels; retain a limited number of the most recent levels. A level becomes permanently broken when a chart-bar close falls below it, including on the bar it is created.
Long: A fractal low is confirmed.
Short: A fractal high is confirmed.
Long: Chart-bar low <= an unbroken support level AND chart-bar high >= that level.
Short: Chart-bar low <= an unbroken resistance level AND chart-bar high >= that level.
