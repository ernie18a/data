Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the configured opening session on the chart timeframe; use only after that session ends and reset each trading day
Calculation: Prior-day OHLC = high, low, and close of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Calculation: Pivot resistance levels = with P = (prior-day high + prior-day low + prior-day close) / 3 and range = prior-day high − prior-day low: R1 = 2P − prior-day low; R2 = P + range; R3 = 2P + prior-day high − 2 × prior-day low; R4 = 3P + prior-day high − 3 × prior-day low; R5 = 4P + prior-day high − 4 × prior-day low
Calculation: Half-pivot levels = R0.5 is halfway between the opening range high and R1; R1.5, R2.5, R3.5, and R4.5 are halfway between each adjacent pair R1–R2, R2–R3, R3–R4, and R4–R5
Calculation: Initial stop = the lower of entry price and either (entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage)) or the low of the previous chart-timeframe bar at setup, according to the selected stop type
Calculation: Trailing stop = reset to 0 on entry; while long, update from its prior value to the first qualifying level in this priority order: R4 if high > R5 and R4 > prior trailing stop; R3.5 if high > R4.5 and R3.5 > prior trailing stop; R3 if high > R4 and R3 > prior trailing stop; R2.5 if high > R3.5 and R2.5 > prior trailing stop; R2 if high > R3 and R2 > prior trailing stop; R1.5 if high > R2.5 and R1.5 > prior trailing stop; R1 if high > R2 and R1 > prior trailing stop; R0.5 if high > R1.5 and R0.5 > prior trailing stop; opening range high if high > R0.5 and opening range high > prior trailing stop; otherwise retain prior trailing stop
Calculation: Active stop = greater of initial stop and trailing stop
Long: flat AND opening range is complete AND open < opening range high AND high > opening range high AND R1 > opening range high AND entries today < configured daily maximum AND current time > configured backtest start AND current time < configured backtest end
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR chart-bar close time = close time of its higher-timeframe daily bar
