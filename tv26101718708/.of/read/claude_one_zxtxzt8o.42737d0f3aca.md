Calculation: ChandelierLong = if previous close > previous ChandelierLong then max(raw, previous ChandelierLong) else raw, where raw = highest high over N bars - m * ATR(N)
Calculation: ChandelierShort = if previous close < previous ChandelierShort then min(raw, previous ChandelierShort) else raw, where raw = lowest low over N bars + m * ATR(N)
Long: close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > trend EMA (fast EMA has a shorter period than trend EMA) AND low <= fast EMA + k * ATR AND lowest RSI over the last L bars <= RSI oversold level AND RSI crosses above RSI long trigger level (oversold level is below the long trigger level) AND close > open AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND bar time-of-day (UTC) is within the London+NY session window AND no open position
Short: close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < trend EMA (fast EMA has a shorter period than trend EMA) AND high >= fast EMA - k * ATR AND highest RSI over the last L bars >= RSI overbought level AND RSI crosses below RSI short trigger level (overbought level is above the short trigger level) AND close < open AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND bar time-of-day (UTC) is within the London+NY session window AND no open position
Exit: long, low <= max(entry price - s * ATR, ChandelierLong) OR RSI crosses below its neutral midline OR bar hour (UTC) >= force-flat hour
Exit: short, high >= min(entry price + s * ATR, ChandelierShort) OR RSI crosses above its neutral midline OR bar hour (UTC) >= force-flat hour
