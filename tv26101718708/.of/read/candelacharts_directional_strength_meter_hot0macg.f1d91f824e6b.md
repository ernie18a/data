Calculation: Signed normalization of x = x divided by its rolling highest value if x > 0, x divided by the absolute value of its rolling lowest value if x < 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Directional oscillator = equal-weight average of enabled components, or 0 if none are enabled; optionally apply EMA smoothing. Components are: (RSI(close) − 50) / 20; (MFI(close, volume) − 50) / 20; signed-normalized MACD line of close, with its slow length twice its fast length and normalization lookback equal to the fast length; CCI(close) / 100; signed-normalized TSI(close), with its long length twice its short length and normalization lookback equal to the short length; (100 × U / (U + D) − 50) / 20, where U and D are EMAs of close's rolling standard deviation on bars with rising and non-rising close respectively, and zero otherwise, using the standard-deviation length for both EMAs; (SMA of stochastic − 50) / 50, where stochastic is 100 × (high − rolling lowest close) / (rolling highest low − rolling lowest close); (Williams %R + 50) / 50; signed-normalized ROC(close); signed-normalized momentum(close); and CMO(close) / 100. ROC and momentum normalization lookbacks equal their respective indicator lengths. RSI and MFI use their own lengths; the other component lengths are multiplied by a common overall-length parameter.
Calculation: Environmental quality = signed normalization of the equal-weight average of enabled quality components, or of 0 if none are enabled, over a separate normalization lookback. For each component x, let R(x) = (x / EMA(x) − 1) × (1 + rolling standard deviation of x), using that component's length for its EMA and standard deviation. The components are R(ATR) − 1; R(ADX), with both DMI and ADX smoothing lengths equal to the ADX length multiplied by the overall-length parameter; R(absolute linear-regression slope of close) − 1, where slope is the difference between the current regression line's fitted values at offsets 0 and 1; and R(absolute correlation of close with bar index) − 1.
Calculation: Alert source = the directional oscillator or a selected moving average of it; the moving average can be SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, volume-weighted MA, HMA, or ALMA.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = a pivot high or low of the directional oscillator identified after the configured number of right-hand bars, using configured left- and right-hand pivot lengths. Compare consecutive confirmed pivots of the same type; their separation filter is the number of bars since the preceding pivot's confirmation as measured on the preceding bar.
Long: Directional oscillator > 0.
Short: Directional oscillator < 0.
Long: Alert source crosses above the buy threshold AND environmental quality >= its threshold.
Short: Alert source crosses below the sell threshold AND environmental quality >= its threshold.
Long: A confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than the preceding oscillator pivot low AND the price low at that pivot is lower than the price low at the preceding oscillator pivot low AND the pivot separation filter is within its inclusive lower and upper bounds AND, if the optional extreme filter is enabled, the absolute value of either oscillator pivot is > 1.
Short: A confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than the preceding oscillator pivot high AND the price high at that pivot is higher than the price high at the preceding oscillator pivot high AND the pivot separation filter is within its inclusive lower and upper bounds AND, if the optional extreme filter is enabled, the absolute value of either oscillator pivot is > 1.
