Calculation: Time-of-day statistics = For each chart-bar time-of-day bucket, retain a configurable number of the most recent volumes and nonzero fair value gap heights separately. Add the current bar's volume and, if present, its gap height before evaluating a new gap. Gap height is low minus high two bars earlier for an upward gap, or low two bars earlier minus high for a downward gap. For a new gap, use the mean and population standard deviation of each series in the previous bar's time-of-day bucket; use zero for an unavailable mean or standard deviation. Each z-score is (observation minus mean) divided by standard deviation, or zero when the standard deviation is zero. The volume observation is the previous bar's volume; the gap observation is the current gap height.
Calculation: Gap filter = The volume filter is disabled OR the previous bar's volume is greater than zero AND its volume z-score is at least the configurable volume threshold. The gap filter is disabled OR the gap height is greater than zero AND its gap z-score is at least the configurable gap threshold. Both filters must pass.
Calculation: Active gaps = Create a bullish gap from the high two bars earlier to the current low, or a bearish gap from the current high to the low two bars earlier, when the corresponding gap decision passes. Initialize its remaining volume to the previous bar's volume. After each creation, if the tracked count exceeds the configured maximum by more than two, remove oldest gaps until the count equals the maximum. On each bar, expire gaps whose age reaches the configured maximum; otherwise, for each gap with positive overlap between its current price interval and the bar's low-to-high interval, subtract bar volume multiplied by overlap height divided by bar range from remaining volume, floored at zero. For a bullish gap, lower its top to the lesser of its current top and the bar low; for a bearish gap, raise its bottom to the greater of its current bottom and the bar high. Remove a gap when its top is at or below its bottom, its remaining volume is at or below a configurable negligible-volume threshold, a bullish gap's bar low is below its original bottom, or a bearish gap's bar high is above its original top.
Calculation: Gap profile = Divide the range from the lowest original bottom to the highest original top of active gaps into a configurable number of equal price bins. For each bin, separately sum bullish and bearish remaining gap volumes weighted by the fraction of each gap's current height overlapping that bin. Optionally smooth each side independently across bins using a normalized Epanechnikov kernel with configurable bandwidth: weight is 0.75 times (1 minus squared bin distance divided by squared bandwidth) within the bandwidth and zero outside it; use the unsmoothed values when bandwidth is zero.
Long: Current low > high two bars earlier AND gap filter passes.
Short: Current high < low two bars earlier AND gap filter passes.
Long: A gap profile bin's bullish volume + bearish volume > 0 AND bullish volume >= bearish volume.
Short: A gap profile bin's bullish volume + bearish volume > 0 AND bearish volume > bullish volume.
