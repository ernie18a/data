Calculation: Yesterday’s high = high of the last completed daily bar; the daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish hammer = close > open AND open − low > 2 × absolute(close − open) AND high − close < 2 × absolute(close − open)
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND close > prior close AND open < prior open AND close > prior open
Calculation: Bullish momentum candle = close > open AND absolute(close − open) > the short-lookback average of absolute(close − open) AND close is within the upper 20% of the bar’s range
Calculation: Recent EMA/VWAP crossover = the short-term EMA of close crossed from at-or-below to above session VWAP within the recent short lookback
Calculation: VWAP at entry = session VWAP on the long-signal bar, held for the position
Long: Recent EMA/VWAP crossover AND low is within the VWAP retest buffer AND close > session VWAP AND close > yesterday’s high AND (bullish hammer OR bullish engulfing OR bullish momentum candle) AND time is within the US morning session (09:30–12:30 EST)
Exit: long, high reaches the take-profit level based on the long-signal bar’s close OR low reaches the stop level below VWAP at entry OR time is 13:00 EST
