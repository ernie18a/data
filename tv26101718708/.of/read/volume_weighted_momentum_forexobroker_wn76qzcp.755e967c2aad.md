Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum = sum((close − close N bars ago) × volume) over the averaging window / sum(volume) over that window
Long: in the configured session if session restriction is enabled AND (ATR ≤ 0 OR |volume-weighted momentum| ≥ minimum magnitude × ATR) AND (zero-cross-only mode is enabled AND volume-weighted momentum crosses above zero OR zero-cross-only mode is disabled AND volume-weighted momentum > 0 AND close crosses above its EMA) AND position is not long AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed
Short: in the configured session if session restriction is enabled AND (ATR ≤ 0 OR |volume-weighted momentum| ≥ minimum magnitude × ATR) AND (zero-cross-only mode is enabled AND volume-weighted momentum crosses below zero OR zero-cross-only mode is disabled AND volume-weighted momentum < 0 AND close crosses below its EMA) AND position is not short AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed
