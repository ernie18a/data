Calculation: VWM = sum(ROC*volume over a window) / sum(volume over the same window), where ROC = close - close N bars earlier; VWM = 0 if the volume sum is 0.
Long: trigger (one of two selectable alternatives: A = VWM crosses above 0; B = VWM > 0 AND close crosses above EMA of close) AND (ATR is 0 OR abs(VWM) >= k * ATR, with k >= 0 optional) AND position is not already long (flat or short, tracked from previous Long/Short signals) AND bars since the last Long or Short signal >= cooldown; evaluated on completed bars only.
Short: trigger (one of two selectable alternatives: A = VWM crosses below 0; B = VWM < 0 AND close crosses below EMA of close, same alternative as chosen for Long) AND (ATR is 0 OR abs(VWM) >= k * ATR, with k >= 0 optional) AND position is not already short (flat or long, tracked from previous Long/Short signals) AND bars since the last Long or Short signal >= cooldown; evaluated on completed bars only.
