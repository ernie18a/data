Calculation: Pivot pools = Separate chronological pools of confirmed pivot highs and lows, each capped at a configurable number of pivots. A pivot uses configurable left and right bar counts and is added on its confirmation bar only if its price is positive and ATR is available and positive. Store its pivot-bar price, bar index, volume (use 1 if missing or nonpositive), and upper wick for a high or lower wick for a low.
Calculation: Zones = Maintain resistance zones from high pivots and support zones from low pivots. When either pool receives a pivot, rebuild both sides using the current ATR and volume SMA. For each pool in chronological order, take each unused pivot as a seed; add later unused pivots within a configurable ATR multiple of the seed price only if each is at least a configurable number of bars from every existing member. Mark accepted members used. Keep clusters with at least a configurable minimum member count. Each member's weight is (1 + configurable volume coefficient × pivot volume / current volume SMA + configurable wick coefficient × pivot wick / current ATR) / (1 + max(1, current bar index - pivot bar index) / configurable age scale); use volume ratio 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. The center is the weighted mean of member prices, strength is the sum of weights, and the upper and lower boundaries are the center plus and minus a configurable ATR multiple. Select the strongest configurable number of zones per side, breaking ties by cluster order. Exclude a selected zone if a previously archived broken zone on the same side has a center within a configurable fraction of the clustering tolerance. A selected zone inherits the last-touch state of the first previous same-side zone within the clustering tolerance; otherwise its last-touch bar is its latest member's pivot bar. A zone is valid when its boundaries are positive and its lower boundary is below its upper boundary.
Calculation: Zone lifecycle = Before rebuilding zones on each bar, mark each live resistance broken when close exceeds its upper boundary and each live support broken when close falls below its lower boundary. A live break additionally requires the previous close to be at or below the resistance upper boundary or at or above the support lower boundary, respectively. Archive broken zones with their break bar and live-break status. For an archive entry on the same side within a configurable fraction of the current clustering tolerance, keep the existing entry; if it was a dropped zone, update it to broken with the new break bar and live-break status. Otherwise append the zone, retaining only a configurable maximum number of archive entries. After break processing, update each surviving zone's last-touch bar when high is at least its lower boundary AND low is at most its upper boundary. On a rebuild, archive each previously live zone that has no newly selected same-side zone within the current clustering tolerance as dropped, using the same archive deduplication rule.
Calculation: Test cooldown = Process valid live support zones in strength order, then valid live resistance zones in strength order, on every bar. A zone passes if no recorded successful support or resistance test among the most recent configurable maximum number of test records has a center within the current clustering tolerance AND occurred fewer than the configurable cooldown bars ago. On the first qualifying test per side, record its center and current bar, even if its marking is disabled.
Long: A valid live support zone has low <= its upper boundary AND close >= its lower boundary AND close >= open AND passes the test cooldown.
Short: A valid live resistance zone has high >= its lower boundary AND close <= its upper boundary AND close <= open AND passes the test cooldown.
Long: An archived broken resistance zone has current bar index - break bar index > 0 AND <= the configurable retest window AND low <= its upper boundary AND close >= its lower boundary AND close >= open.
Short: An archived broken support zone has current bar index - break bar index > 0 AND <= the configurable retest window AND high >= its lower boundary AND close <= its upper boundary AND close <= open.
Long: A valid live support zone has low <= its upper boundary AND close > its lower boundary.
Short: A valid live resistance zone has high >= its lower boundary AND close < its upper boundary.
Long: A live resistance zone has close > its upper boundary AND previous close <= its upper boundary AND its live break is retained in the broken-zone archive.
Short: A live support zone has close < its lower boundary AND previous close >= its lower boundary AND its live break is retained in the broken-zone archive.
