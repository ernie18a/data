Calculation: active support zone = cluster of confirmed pivot lows; active resistance zone = cluster of confirmed pivot highs. Confirm pivots using configurable left and right chart-bar widths. Rebuild clusters from the retained pivot history when a new pivot confirms: visit pivots chronologically, seed each cluster with the oldest unassigned pivot, and add later unassigned pivots within a configurable ATR-relative tolerance of the seed, provided each is at least a configurable bar spacing from every cluster member. Require a configurable minimum touch count. Set the zone center to the members’ weighted mean pivot price, with each weight increasing with pivot volume relative to a long-term volume average and rejection-wick size relative to long-term ATR, and decreasing with pivot age. Set zone boundaries at the center plus or minus a configurable ATR fraction. Keep the highest-strength zones per side up to a configurable limit; exclude previously broken zones. A zone is active until a chart-bar close passes its far boundary: resistance breaks when close > top; support breaks when close < bottom.
