Calculation: Analysis bars = bars aggregated to the analysis timeframe; relative to the chart timeframe it may be higher, equal, or lower. Use the last completed analysis bar; when it is lower, use the last completed analysis bar within each chart bar. All EMAs, ATR, trend age, swing counts, and stock types below use analysis bars.
Calculation: EMA ribbon = EMAs of close with fast, medium, slow, filter, and major lookbacks, respectively; their lookbacks increase in that order, except the slow and filter lookbacks may be equal.
Calculation: Bullish regime = fast EMA >= medium EMA AND medium EMA >= slow EMA AND slow EMA >= filter EMA AND filter EMA >= major EMA, OR close > major EMA AND (fast EMA >= medium EMA AND medium EMA >= slow EMA AND slow EMA >= filter EMA, OR close >= filter EMA).
Calculation: Bearish regime = bullish regime is false AND (close < major EMA OR close < filter EMA).
Calculation: Regime = bullish when the bullish regime condition holds, bearish when the bearish regime condition holds, and neutral otherwise. Trend age is the number of analysis bars since the regime last changed.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed after the configured number of right-hand analysis bars, using configured left-hand and right-hand pivot lookbacks. Swing count resets when the regime changes and increments on each confirmed swing high in a bullish regime or confirmed swing low in a bearish regime.
Calculation: Chase risk = trend age >= its threshold AND regime is non-neutral, OR swing count >= its threshold, OR the selected extension measure exceeds its threshold. Extension is close minus slow EMA in a bullish regime or slow EMA minus close in a bearish regime, divided by ATR for the ATR method or by slow EMA for the percentage method.
Calculation: Stock type = 1 if close > major EMA AND no close was below major EMA within the reversal lookback; otherwise 2 if close > filter EMA AND a close was below major EMA within that lookback; otherwise 4 if close < filter EMA AND a new low occurred within the new-low lookback; otherwise 3 if close < filter EMA; otherwise 0. A new low occurs when low is below the lowest low of the preceding new-low lookback bars. A past event is within a lookback when its bars-since count is <= that lookback.
Long: Bullish regime AND close >= filter EMA AND close >= major EMA AND stock type != 3 AND stock type != 4 AND consecutive analysis-bar closes below filter EMA < the cut threshold AND chase risk is false.
Short: Bearish regime AND close < filter EMA AND close >= major EMA AND stock type != 3 AND stock type != 4 AND consecutive analysis-bar closes below filter EMA < the cut threshold AND chase risk is false.
Long: Bullish regime AND analysis-bar close >= filter EMA AND chart-bar low <= analysis-bar fast EMA AND chart-bar high >= analysis-bar fast EMA.
Long: A swing low is confirmed on an analysis bar.
Short: A swing high is confirmed on an analysis bar.
Exit: long, consecutive analysis-bar closes below filter EMA >= the cut threshold.
