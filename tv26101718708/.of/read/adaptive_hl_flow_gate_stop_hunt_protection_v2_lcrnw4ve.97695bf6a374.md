Calculation: Hybrid volatility = blend of average true range and Parkinson volatility from high/low ranges.
Calculation: Adaptive trail direction = bullish until close falls below the prior upper trail, bearish until close rises above the prior lower trail; trail distance is hybrid volatility multiplied by a factor adjusted for volatility, efficiency ratio, and ADX.
Calculation: Flow gates = smooth the rolling highest high and lowest low using a longer structure lookback and shorter smoothing lookback; form a midpoint and gates on either side using the greater of the structure range or twice hybrid volatility.
Calculation: Gate direction = bullish when close is above the upper flow gate, bearish when close is below the lower flow gate, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Momentum direction = bullish when MFI (if volume is available, otherwise RSI) is above 50 plus a deadband; bearish when below 50 minus the deadband, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend strength direction = bullish when positive directional index exceeds negative directional index and ADX meets its minimum; bearish for the reverse; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Candle pressure direction = direction of an EMA of candle close-position pressure weighted by volume relative to average volume; neutral inside a pressure deadband.
Calculation: Volume pulse direction = candle direction when volume exceeds its average times a pulse factor; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Sweep direction = bullish for a qualified sell-side sweep and bearish for a qualified buy-side sweep. A buy-side sweep requires a high above the prior rolling high, a close back below that high, and a sufficiently large upper wick; a sell-side sweep is the inverse using the prior rolling low and lower wick. Qualification also requires sweep strength and volume/range conditions.
Calculation: Liquidity-memory direction = bullish when decayed active sell-side sweep risk exceeds active buy-side risk; bearish when active buy-side risk exceeds active sell-side risk; otherwise neutral. Sweep risk decays over a bounded number of bars after the sweep.
Calculation: Expansion direction = candle direction when volatility was compressed on the prior bar and current volatility expands beyond its average range; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe slope direction = direction of the EMA slope on a higher timeframe, using the bar returned by lookahead-off security data (the current higher-timeframe bar while forming).
Calculation: Middle-timeframe slope direction = direction of the EMA slope on a middle timeframe, higher than the chart timeframe and between chart and higher timeframe, using the bar returned by lookahead-off security data (the current middle-timeframe bar while forming).
Calculation: VWAP direction = bullish when intraday close is above session VWAP, bearish when below, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Directional flow score = weighted sum of adaptive trail direction, gate direction, momentum direction, trend strength direction, candle pressure direction, volume pulse direction, sweep direction, liquidity-memory direction, expansion direction, higher-timeframe slope direction, middle-timeframe slope direction, and VWAP direction; higher- and middle-timeframe and VWAP terms are included only when enabled.
Calculation: Chop = efficiency ratio is below its minimum OR ADX is below its minimum.
Calculation: Active buy-side block = a buy-side sweep is still within its risk-decay window, decayed risk meets the block threshold, and close has not reclaimed above the sweep high. Active sell-side block is the inverse.
Calculation: Weak-body flip = body-to-range ratio is below its minimum AND either wick-to-range ratio meets the wick minimum.
Calculation: Entry stretch = absolute close-to-trail distance divided by hybrid volatility exceeds its maximum.
Calculation: Large gap = absolute difference between current open and prior close exceeds hybrid volatility times the gap limit.
Calculation: Long firewall = chop OR active buy-side block OR weak-body flip OR entry stretch OR opposing higher-timeframe slope (when enabled) OR opposing middle-timeframe slope (when enabled) OR large gap.
Calculation: Short firewall = chop OR active sell-side block OR weak-body flip OR entry stretch OR opposing higher-timeframe slope (when enabled) OR opposing middle-timeframe slope (when enabled) OR large gap.
Calculation: Long retest = flow direction is bullish; enough bars have elapsed since its last change; low touches or crosses the upper gate; close finishes above the upper gate and trail; momentum direction is not bearish; and no active buy-side block.
Calculation: Short retest = flow direction is bearish; enough bars have elapsed since its last change; high touches or crosses the lower gate; close finishes below the lower gate and trail; momentum direction is not bullish; and no active sell-side block.
Calculation: Buy-side sweep reclaim = during the active buy-side sweep window, close rises above that sweep's high. Sell-side sweep reclaim is the inverse: close falls below the active sell-side sweep's low.
Long: (directional flow score >= bullish threshold AND prior flow direction is not bullish AND flip cooldown has elapsed AND NOT long firewall) OR long retest OR (flow direction is bullish AND close > trail AND close > upper flow gate AND bar index is divisible by continuation-dot spacing AND NOT active buy-side block) OR buy-side sweep reclaim.
Short: (directional flow score <= negative bearish threshold AND prior flow direction is not bearish AND flip cooldown has elapsed AND NOT short firewall) OR short retest OR (flow direction is bearish AND close < trail AND close < lower flow gate AND bar index is divisible by continuation-dot spacing AND NOT active sell-side block) OR sell-side sweep reclaim.
