Calculation: Hybrid volatility = a weighted blend of ATR and close × square root of SMA(log(high / low)²) / (4 × log(2)); use zero for the logarithm when low is nonpositive.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive trail = source ± hybrid volatility × base multiplier × clamped(hybrid volatility / its longer SMA) × clamped(constant − efficiency ratio × coefficient) × ADX factor. The ADX factor changes at lower and upper ADX thresholds. Maintain a lower trail as the maximum of its current basic lower level and its previous value while the previous close is above that previous value; otherwise reset it to the basic lower level. Maintain an upper trail symmetrically using the minimum while the previous close is below it. Initialize direction bullish; switch from bearish to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper trail, and from bullish to bearish when close falls below the previous lower trail. The active trail is the lower trail in bullish direction and the upper trail in bearish direction.
Calculation: Flow gates = smooth the rolling highest high and lowest low separately with the selected moving-average type. Their midpoint is the average of the two smoothed values; gate width is the greater of their difference and a multiple of hybrid volatility. Upper and lower gates are midpoint plus and minus gate width × tightness. Gate direction is bullish above the upper gate, bearish below the lower gate, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Candle fractions = body, upper wick, and lower wick divided by high − low; for a zero-range bar use body fraction 1 and wick fractions 0. Close position is (close − low) / (high − low), or its midpoint for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Volume is available when current volume and its SMA are positive. Volume pulse occurs when available volume exceeds its SMA × a pulse multiplier. Pressure is an EMA of signed close position × clamped(volume / volume SMA), using a neutral volume weight when volume is unavailable; signed close position maps the candle range linearly from negative to positive. Pressure direction is bullish above its positive threshold, bearish below its negative threshold, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: A buy-side sweep candidate occurs when high exceeds the highest high of a preceding lookback, upper-wick fraction meets its threshold, optional sweep-volume confirmation passes, and either close is below open or both close is below that prior high and candle range exceeds its SMA × a range multiplier. A sell-side candidate reverses high, low, wick, and price comparisons. Sweep-volume confirmation passes when it is disabled, volume is unavailable, or volume exceeds its SMA × the sweep-volume multiplier.
Calculation: Sweep strength = clamped weighted sum of the relevant wick fraction, range-expansion flag, sweep-volume-confirmation flag, and volume-pulse flag. A candidate becomes a sweep when its strength meets the smaller of a strength cap and the risk threshold plus the optional cluster add-on. The cluster add-on applies to every current candidate when enabled because the current candidate makes bars since a candidate zero.
Calculation: For each sweep side, store the latest sweep's bar, extreme, swept prior high or low, and strength. It remains active for the configured blocking lookback. Its active risk is stored strength multiplied by a linear decay from full strength on the sweep bar to zero at the end of that lookback. A buy-side sweep is reclaimed when close exceeds its stored high; a sell-side sweep is reclaimed when close falls below its stored low. Long blocking occurs when the latest buy-side sweep is active, its active risk meets the risk threshold, and it is not reclaimed; short blocking is symmetric for the sell-side sweep.
Calculation: Momentum is MFI of typical price when volume is available and RSI of close otherwise. Momentum direction is bullish above its upper deadband, bearish below its lower deadband, and neutral otherwise. DMI direction follows the larger directional indicator only when ADX meets its minimum; otherwise it is neutral.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe and middle-timeframe directions are the signs of the changes in their respective EMA of close, with the same EMA lookback. Both use higher timeframes than the chart, with the middle timeframe no higher than the other higher timeframe; evaluate the bar still forming and compare its EMA with the preceding completed bar's EMA. Session VWAP direction follows close above or below session VWAP on intraday charts and is neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Expansion occurs when the previous bar's hybrid volatility divided by its compression-lookback SMA is below a compression threshold, the current ratio exceeds an expansion threshold, and current candle range exceeds its sweep-lookback SMA. Expansion direction follows the candle body. Sweep direction is bullish for a sell-side sweep and bearish for a buy-side sweep, with sell-side precedence if both occur. Sweep-memory direction follows the side with greater active risk: sell-side risk is bullish and buy-side risk bearish.
Calculation: Flow score = clamped weighted sum of adaptive-trail direction, gate direction, momentum direction, DMI direction, pressure direction, candle direction on a volume pulse, sweep direction, sweep-memory direction, expansion direction, higher-timeframe direction, middle-timeframe direction, and session-VWAP direction. Optional components contribute zero when disabled.
Calculation: The long firewall is active when enabled chop filtering finds efficiency ratio or ADX below its minimum, long sweep blocking is active, enabled body filtering finds body fraction below its minimum and either wick fraction at or above its threshold, enabled stretch filtering finds absolute close-to-trail distance divided by hybrid volatility above its maximum, an enabled higher-timeframe or middle-timeframe conflict is bearish, or an enabled gap filter finds absolute open-to-previous-close gap above a hybrid-volatility multiple. The short firewall uses the same conditions with short sweep blocking and bullish timeframe conflicts. Treat zero hybrid volatility as zero trail distance; the gap filter requires positive hybrid volatility.
Calculation: Flow state starts neutral. On each confirmed chart bar, choose bullish when flow score meets its positive threshold and the long firewall is inactive; otherwise choose bearish when score meets its negative threshold and the short firewall is inactive; otherwise choose neutral when neutral resets are enabled and absolute score is at or below the reset threshold; otherwise retain the previous state. Apply a changed directional state only after the minimum interval since the last state change; apply a neutral change immediately. Every state change resets that interval.
Calculation: Entry quality = clamped weighted sum of absolute flow score, higher-timeframe alignment, middle-timeframe alignment, nonneutral flow, expansion, and sufficiently large absolute pressure, minus weighted penalties for chop, weak body, maximum active sweep risk, timeframe misalignment, stretch blocking, high manipulation index, and large gap. Alignment means the relevant timeframe is disabled, neutral, or agrees with flow. Manipulation index is the clamped weighted sum of the larger wick fraction, range expansion, volume pulse, and the presence of either sweep.
Calculation: Screener score = clamped weighted sum of close direction relative to a selected moving average, gate direction, and RSI direction relative to upper and lower momentum deadbands. Calculate this on the chart timeframe or on another aggregated timeframe using its still-forming bar; the other timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Flow state changes to bullish AND entry quality meets its minimum when quality filtering is enabled.
Short: Flow state changes to bearish AND entry quality meets its minimum when quality filtering is enabled.
Long: Flow state is bullish AND bars since the last flow-state change meet the retest delay AND low <= upper gate AND close > upper gate AND close > adaptive trail AND momentum direction >= neutral AND long sweep blocking is inactive.
Short: Flow state is bearish AND bars since the last flow-state change meet the retest delay AND high >= lower gate AND close < lower gate AND close < adaptive trail AND momentum direction <= neutral AND short sweep blocking is inactive.
Long: Flow state is bullish AND close > adaptive trail AND close > upper gate AND bar index modulo continuation spacing = 0 AND long sweep blocking is inactive.
Short: Flow state is bearish AND close < adaptive trail AND close < lower gate AND bar index modulo continuation spacing = 0 AND short sweep blocking is inactive.
Long: Sell-side sweep occurs.
Short: Buy-side sweep occurs.
Long: Latest buy-side sweep is active AND close > its stored high AND the preceding bar was not above the then-current stored buy-side sweep high.
Short: Latest sell-side sweep is active AND close < its stored low AND the preceding bar was not below the then-current stored sell-side sweep low.
Long: Latest sell-side sweep is active AND close >= its stored low AND low <= its stored swept level AND close > that level.
Short: Latest buy-side sweep is active AND close <= its stored high AND high >= its stored swept level AND close < that level.
Long: Flow state is bullish AND entry quality meets the coach threshold AND long sweep blocking is inactive.
Short: Flow state is bearish AND entry quality meets the coach threshold AND short sweep blocking is inactive.
Long: Screener score >= its positive direction threshold.
Short: Screener score <= its negative direction threshold.
