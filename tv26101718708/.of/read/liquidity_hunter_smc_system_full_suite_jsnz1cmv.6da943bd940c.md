Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side. Track the latest confirmed swing of each side and a separate bounded, age-limited list of confirmed swings of each side; add newly confirmed swings before removing expired swings, and remove the oldest when the list exceeds its capacity.
Calculation: High sweep = high > a tracked swing high AND close < that swing high; low sweep = low < a tracked swing low AND close > that swing low. On each bar remove only the most recently added qualifying swing from each list.
Calculation: Upward structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high AND, if displacement is required, close > open AND absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by a threshold. Downward structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low AND, if displacement is required, close < open AND absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by a threshold. Update structure direction to the direction of the latest structure break before evaluating sweeps; initialize it as neutral.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Confirmed long reversal = upward structure break AND a low sweep armed while structure direction was neutral or down, no more than the sweep timeout bars ago, without an intervening downward structure break AND, if higher-timeframe bias is enabled, higher-timeframe close > its SMA. Confirmed short reversal = downward structure break AND a high sweep armed while structure direction was neutral or up, no more than the sweep timeout bars ago, without an intervening upward structure break AND, if higher-timeframe bias is enabled, higher-timeframe close < its SMA. Each new qualifying sweep resets its side's timeout; an opposite structure break disarms it. Higher-timeframe close and SMA use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Order block = high and low of the most recent opposite-colored candle within a lookback preceding a confirmed reversal. If fair-value-gap confluence is enabled, freeze that order block when the matching fair value gap occurs on the reversal bar or during the subsequent gap window; the gap check precedes expiration, allowing a gap on the first bar beyond the window. Otherwise freeze it on the reversal bar. A newer confirmed reversal replaces the pending gap check or frozen block for its side. An opposite structure break cancels the pending check, armed block and touched state.
Calculation: Retest confirmation = a frozen order block overlaps a bar, high >= block low AND low <= block high, within the touch timeout after freezing, including the freeze bar; retain the first touch bar's high and low. Within the confirmation timeout after that touch, including the touch bar, apply the selected mode: no additional condition; bullish rejection is close > open AND min(open, close) - low > absolute body multiplied by a wick ratio, bearish rejection is close < open AND high - max(open, close) > absolute body multiplied by that ratio; bullish engulfing is close > open AND previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open, bearish engulfing is close < open AND previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open; close-through is close > block high for bullish or close < block low for bearish; touch-bar break is close > touch-bar high for bullish or close < touch-bar low for bearish. Consume the touched state on its first confirmation, even if no order is placed.
Calculation: Initial stop = on the entry-signal bar, for a long, the selected order-block low minus an ATR buffer, close minus an ATR multiple, or close reduced by a percentage; for a short, the selected order-block high plus an ATR buffer, close plus an ATR multiple, or close increased by a percentage. In order-block mode use the frozen block when retests are required, otherwise the latest same-side order block; fall back to the ATR stop if unavailable. Preserve this stop when the order is submitted.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until the breakeven option is enabled AND a bar's high reaches entry price + (entry price - initial stop) for a long, or its low reaches entry price - (initial stop - entry price) for a short; thereafter use entry price. The script records entry price as the close of the bar on which the new position is first observed.
Calculation: Target = recompute on every bar using that bar's close, ATR, selected stop calculation and remaining liquidity pools. In percentage-stop mode use close increased by the target percentage for a long or decreased by it for a short. Otherwise use the nearest tracked high strictly above close for a long or low strictly below close for a short when liquidity targeting is selected and such a pool exists; otherwise use close plus a risk multiple of (close - current calculated long stop), or close minus a risk multiple of (current calculated short stop). Submit updated stop and target orders while the position is open.
Long: low sweep.
Short: high sweep.
Long: bullish fair value gap.
Short: bearish fair value gap.
Long: upward structure break.
Short: downward structure break.
Long: confirmed long reversal AND retest confirmation if retests are required.
Short: confirmed short reversal AND retest confirmation if retests are required.
Exit: long, price <= active stop OR price >= target.
Exit: short, price >= active stop OR price <= target.
