Calculation: Bar notation = O, H, L, C and V are open, high, low, close and volume; subscript 0 is the current chart bar and subscript i is i chart bars ago. Body B = abs(C - O), upper wick U = H - max(O, C), lower wick D = min(O, C) - L, midpoint M = (O + C) / 2, and average body A = SMA(B) on the current bar.
Calculation: Developing volume profile = At the start of each daily session, create equal-width price bins spanning the session open plus and minus a multiple of daily ATR. Daily ATR uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming; fall back to chart ATR if unavailable. For each completed chart bar in the session, distribute its volume equally among all bins touched by its low-to-high range, clipping prices to the outer bins. POC is the center of the highest-volume bin, with ties going to the lowest bin. Starting at POC, expand the value area one adjacent bin at a time toward the higher-volume neighbor, choosing the upper neighbor on ties, until a specified fraction of session volume is included; VAL and VAH are the centers of its lowest and highest bins. Mean bin volume is the mean over nonempty bins. Current-bin volume is the volume of the bin containing the current close. Previous POC is the final POC of the preceding session; it is untouched only until a current-session bar's low-to-high range includes it.
Calculation: Delta = Either sum the volume of completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar with positive sign when their close exceeds their open, negative sign when their close is below their open, and zero otherwise; or use chart volume multiplied by (2 × close - high - low) / (high - low), with zero when high equals low. Cumulative delta is the sum of delta since the start of the daily session.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of higher-timeframe closes and its preceding higher-timeframe value; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming for its current close and EMA, and the last completed higher-timeframe bar for the preceding EMA.
Long: After the warmup period AND an enabled bullish reversal pattern matches AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional reversal-location filter passes AND the optional reversal-order-flow filter passes. Bullish reversal patterns are: (B0 > 0 AND D0 >= large-wick multiple × B0 AND U0 <= small-wick multiple × B0) OR (B0 > 0 AND U0 >= large-wick multiple × B0 AND D0 <= small-wick multiple × B0) OR (C1 < O1 AND C0 > O0 AND C0 >= O1 AND O0 <= C1) OR (C2 < O2 AND B2 > A0 AND B1 < small-body multiple × B2 AND C0 > O0 AND C0 > M2) OR (C1 < O1 AND O0 < C1 AND C0 > M1 AND C0 < O1) OR (C0 > O0 AND C1 > O1 AND C2 > O2 AND C0 > C1 AND C1 > C2 AND O0 <= C1 AND O0 >= O1 AND O1 <= C2 AND O1 >= O2 AND B0 > tall-body multiple × A0) OR (C3 < O3 AND C2 < O2 AND C1 < O1 AND C2 < C3 AND C1 < C2 AND C0 > O0 AND O0 < C1 AND C0 > O3) OR (C2 < O2 AND B1 <= doji fraction × (H1 - L1) AND H1 < L2 AND L0 > H1 AND C0 > O0). The lower-wick pattern also has a separately selectable variant requiring C0 < O0.
Short: After the warmup period AND an enabled bearish reversal pattern matches AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional reversal-location filter passes AND the optional reversal-order-flow filter passes. Bearish reversal patterns are: (B0 > 0 AND U0 >= large-wick multiple × B0 AND D0 <= small-wick multiple × B0) OR (C1 > O1 AND C0 < O0 AND O0 >= C1 AND C0 <= O1) OR (C2 > O2 AND B2 > A0 AND B1 < small-body multiple × B2 AND C0 < O0 AND C0 < M2) OR (C1 > O1 AND O0 > C1 AND C0 < M1 AND C0 > O1) OR (C0 < O0 AND C1 < O1 AND C2 < O2 AND C0 < C1 AND C1 < C2 AND O0 >= C1 AND O0 <= O1 AND O1 >= C2 AND O1 <= O2 AND B0 > tall-body multiple × A0) OR (C3 > O3 AND C2 > O2 AND C1 > O1 AND C2 > C3 AND C1 > C2 AND C0 < O0 AND O0 > C1 AND C0 < O3) OR (C2 > O2 AND B1 <= doji fraction × (H1 - L1) AND L1 > H2 AND H0 < L1 AND C0 < O0).
Long: After the warmup period AND the bullish harami pattern is enabled AND O2 > C2 AND max(O1, C1) < O2 AND min(O1, C1) > C2 AND B1 < B2 AND C0 > H1 AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional harami-location filter passes AND the optional reversal-order-flow filter passes.
Short: After the warmup period AND the bearish harami pattern is enabled AND C2 > O2 AND max(O1, C1) < C2 AND min(O1, C1) > O2 AND B1 < B2 AND C0 < L1 AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional harami-location filter passes AND the optional reversal-order-flow filter passes.
Long: After the warmup period AND an enabled bullish continuation pattern matches AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional continuation-location filter passes AND, if the order-flow filter is enabled, delta > 0 AND, for the marubozu pattern, volume > volume SMA × spike multiple. Bullish continuation patterns are: L0 > H1 OR (C0 > O0 AND U0 <= marubozu-wick multiple × B0 AND D0 <= marubozu-wick multiple × B0 AND B0 > A0) OR (L1 > H2 AND C1 > O1 AND C0 > O0 AND L0 > L1) OR (C4 > O4 AND B4 > drive-body multiple × A0 AND max(B1, B2, B3) < pullback-body multiple × B4 AND C1 < C4 AND min(L1, L2, L3) > L4 AND max(H1, H2, H3) < H4 AND C0 > O0 AND C0 > H4 AND B0 > A0).
Short: After the warmup period AND an enabled bearish continuation pattern matches AND the optional regime filter passes AND the optional higher-timeframe filter passes AND the optional continuation-location filter passes AND, if the order-flow filter is enabled, delta < 0 AND, for the marubozu pattern, volume > volume SMA × spike multiple. Bearish continuation patterns are: H0 < L1 OR (C0 < O0 AND U0 <= marubozu-wick multiple × B0 AND D0 <= marubozu-wick multiple × B0 AND B0 > A0) OR (H1 < L2 AND C1 < O1 AND C0 < O0 AND H0 < H1) OR (C4 < O4 AND B4 > drive-body multiple × A0 AND max(B1, B2, B3) < pullback-body multiple × B4 AND C1 > C4 AND max(H1, H2, H3) < H4 AND min(L1, L2, L3) > L4 AND C0 < O0 AND C0 < L4 AND B0 > A0).
Calculation: Warmup period = A number of chart bars greater than the larger of the average-body and divergence lookbacks plus twice the swing-pivot length plus a buffer.
Calculation: Regime filter = Optionally require ATR percentile rank over its lookback to be below a threshold for calm, or at least the threshold for panic.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe filter = For long, higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND optionally current higher-timeframe EMA >= preceding higher-timeframe EMA; for short, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND optionally current higher-timeframe EMA <= preceding higher-timeframe EMA.
Calculation: Reversal-location filter = For long, abs(close - VAL) <= proximity multiple × chart ATR OR close < VAL OR (previous POC remains untouched AND abs(close - previous POC) <= proximity multiple × chart ATR) OR a bullish sweep OR (current-bin volume > high-volume multiple × mean bin volume AND close <= POC). For short, abs(close - VAH) <= proximity multiple × chart ATR OR close > VAH OR (previous POC remains untouched AND abs(close - previous POC) <= proximity multiple × chart ATR) OR a bearish sweep OR (current-bin volume > high-volume multiple × mean bin volume AND close >= POC). Optionally also require the corresponding sweep.
Calculation: Sweep = A bullish sweep has low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND volume > volume SMA × spike multiple; a bearish sweep has high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND volume > volume SMA × spike multiple. A pivot is confirmed only after the configured number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Harami-location filter = On the preceding chart bar, abs(close - POC) <= proximity multiple × chart ATR OR current-bin volume > high-volume multiple × mean bin volume.
Calculation: Continuation-location filter = For long, (current-bin volume < low-volume multiple × mean bin volume OR close > VAH) AND NOT (current-bin volume > high-volume multiple × mean bin volume AND close < VAH). For short, (current-bin volume < low-volume multiple × mean bin volume OR close < VAL) AND NOT (current-bin volume > high-volume multiple × mean bin volume AND close > VAL). Mean bin volume must be positive for either volume-node comparison.
Calculation: Reversal-order-flow filter = For long, delta > 0 OR (low <= lowest low over the divergence lookback AND cumulative delta > cumulative delta that many chart bars ago); for short, delta < 0 OR (high >= highest high over the divergence lookback AND cumulative delta < cumulative delta that many chart bars ago).
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + target multiple × ATR at entry OR low <= entry price - stop multiple × ATR at entry OR bars since entry >= holding horizon.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= entry price - target multiple × ATR at entry OR high >= entry price + stop multiple × ATR at entry OR bars since entry >= holding horizon.
