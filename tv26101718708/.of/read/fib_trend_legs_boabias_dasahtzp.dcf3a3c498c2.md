Calculation: ALMA SuperTrend = ALMA of close plus and minus a parameter times the standard deviation of close. For each bar, retain the previous upper band unless the new upper band is lower or the previous close exceeded the previous upper band; retain the previous lower band unless the new lower band is higher or the previous close was below the previous lower band. Initialize direction as bearish while the previous standard deviation is unavailable. If the previous SuperTrend was the previous upper band, direction becomes bullish when close exceeds the current upper band and bearish otherwise; otherwise direction becomes bearish when close is below the current lower band and bullish otherwise. SuperTrend is the lower band in the bullish direction and the upper band in the bearish direction.
Calculation: Market structure = Maintain trend, continuation level, protective level, running extreme, and a reference bar. Initialize trend as neutral, continuation level as the first high, protective level as the first low, and reference bar as the first bar. Maintain outside-bar bounds initialized to the first high and low: when high exceeds the upper bound, set both bounds to the current high and low and flag an upward break; then, when low is below the lower bound, set both bounds to the current high and low and flag a downward break. Maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and low using equal left and right pivot lengths; discard the latest pivot high when current high exceeds it and the latest pivot low when current low falls below it. A swing high or low is the corresponding extreme from the current bar back to but excluding the reference bar, capped by a lookback parameter; ties select the oldest bar. Process each bar in this order. In neutral trend, first test a downside sweep of the protective level, then an upside sweep of the continuation level; a sweep requires the wick to reach or exceed the level and the close to remain on its original side, including equality, and moves that level to the wick. If neither sweep occurs, close at or below the protective level sets bearish trend, moves the old continuation level to the protective level, clears the continuation level, and sets the running extreme to low; otherwise close at or above the continuation level sets bullish trend, clears the continuation level, and sets the running extreme to high. Either transition sets the reference bar to the current bar. In bearish trend, update the running extreme to the lowest low and remember its latest bar. In adjusted-points mode, on bars whose index is divisible by the pivot length, if the continuation level exists and the latest pivot high is below the protective level, replace the protective level with that pivot high and the reference and extreme bars with its bar. If the continuation level is absent, an upward outside-bound break with both current and previous candles bullish sets it to the running extreme and the reference bar to the extreme bar. Next, a downside sweep of the continuation level moves it to the current low; otherwise close at or below that level clears it and sets the protective level and reference bar to the swing high and its bar. Then an upside sweep of the protective level moves it to the current high; otherwise close at or above it changes trend to bullish, sets the protective level to the continuation level if present or the swing low otherwise, clears the continuation level, and sets the running extreme and reference bar to the current high and bar. In bullish trend, mirror these operations: track the highest high; in adjusted-points mode replace the protective level with the latest pivot low when it is higher; establish an absent continuation level after a downward outside-bound break and two bearish candles; process an upside continuation-level sweep or close at or above that level, setting the protective level to the swing low on a break; then process a downside protective-level sweep or close at or below it, switching to bearish trend and setting the protective level to the continuation level if present or the swing high otherwise. Sweeps are evaluated only when sweep detection is enabled; continuation and protective blocks are independent and run sequentially, including on a bar that leaves neutral trend.
