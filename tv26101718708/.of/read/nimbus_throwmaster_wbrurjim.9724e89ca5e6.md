Calculation: Tenkan = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Kijun = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Span A = (Tenkan + Kijun) / 2.
Calculation: Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the cloud lookback.
Calculation: Cloud top and cloud bottom = respectively the maximum and minimum of Span A and Span B from displacement minus one bars ago.
Calculation: Nearest forward twist = the largest offset n from zero through displacement minus two for which (Span A[n] > Span B[n]) differs from (Span A[n+1] > Span B[n+1]); it exists only when the bar index exceeds displacement plus the Span B lookback plus two.
Long: Tenkan crosses above Kijun.
Short: Tenkan crosses below Kijun.
Long: Close crosses above the cloud top AND Tenkan > Kijun AND Span A > Span B AND close > close from displacement minus one bars ago.
Short: Close crosses below the cloud bottom AND Tenkan <= Kijun AND Span A <= Span B AND close <= close from displacement minus one bars ago.
Long: Close > cloud top.
Short: Close < cloud bottom.
Long: Tenkan > Kijun.
Short: Tenkan <= Kijun.
Long: Span A > Span B.
Short: Span A <= Span B.
Long: Nearest forward twist exists AND Span A at its offset > Span B at its offset.
Short: Nearest forward twist exists AND Span A at its offset <= Span B at its offset.
