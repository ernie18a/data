Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars earlier AND low - high two bars earlier >= minimum gap size.
Calculation: Bearish gap = high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier - high >= minimum gap size.
Calculation: Adjusted gap zone = start with the interval between the first and third bars' gap-facing wicks; for each adjacent pair of bars, if their bodies do not overlap by more than the minimum price increment in the gap direction, replace that side's outer wick boundary with the corresponding outer body boundary.
Calculation: Opening-gap exclusion = during a parent period containing the configured New York reopen, form the interval between the reopen bar's open and the preceding traded bar's close if its size is at least the minimum price increment; exclude a gap if its adjusted zone overlaps this interval by at least the minimum price increment.
Long: Chart timeframe equals the configured macro timeframe AND bar time is inside an enabled macro window AND close >= open.
Short: Chart timeframe equals the configured macro timeframe AND bar time is inside an enabled macro window AND close < open.
Long: Bullish gap on the last completed detection bar AND that bar and the preceding detection bar belong to the same higher parent period AND no earlier bullish or bearish gap in that parent period passed the opening-gap exclusion AND the current gap passes the opening-gap exclusion. The detection timeframe is the chart timeframe or lower; optional additional detection timeframes are higher than the chart timeframe and use the last completed bar.
Short: Bearish gap on the last completed detection bar AND that bar and the preceding detection bar belong to the same higher parent period AND no earlier bullish or bearish gap in that parent period passed the opening-gap exclusion AND the current gap passes the opening-gap exclusion. The detection timeframe is the chart timeframe or lower; optional additional detection timeframes are higher than the chart timeframe and use the last completed bar.
Long: Bullish gap on the last completed session detection bar AND that bar is within a configured weekday session AND no earlier bullish or bearish gap has occurred in that session. The session detection timeframe is the chart timeframe or lower.
Short: Bearish gap on the last completed session detection bar AND that bar is within a configured weekday session AND no earlier bullish or bearish gap has occurred in that session. The session detection timeframe is the chart timeframe or lower.
