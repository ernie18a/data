Calculation: All calculations use the configurable scan timeframe. If it is higher than the chart timeframe, use its last completed bar; if lower, use its last completed intrabar within the chart bar. If equal, use the chart bar.
Calculation: Short and long EMA slopes = current EMA minus its EMA a configurable number of scan bars earlier; the short EMA has a shorter lookback than the long EMA.
Calculation: Buying pressure = SMA of volume times (close minus open if close > open, otherwise a configurable fraction of the lower wick); selling pressure = SMA of volume times (open minus close if close < open, otherwise the same fraction of the upper wick). Both use the same lookback.
Calculation: Range-weighted average = sum of close times weight divided by sum of weight over a lookback, where weight = absolute change in close divided by the previous close, or zero when the previous close is zero. It is unavailable when the weight sum is zero. Range oscillator = close minus this average divided by a longer-lookback ATR times a configurable multiplier, or zero when the average is unavailable or the ATR is zero.
Calculation: Bull evidence = weighted sum of these true conditions: close > short EMA, close > long EMA, short EMA slope > 0, long EMA slope > 0, session VWAP unavailable OR close > session VWAP, buying pressure > selling pressure, buying pressure > previous buying pressure, close > range-weighted average AND range oscillator > 0, close > range-weighted average plus the oscillator ATR times its multiplier, positive DI > negative DI, RSI >= a bullish threshold. Use the same high weight for both close-versus-EMA conditions, the VWAP condition, pressure dominance, and the range oscillator condition; the same intermediate weight for both slope conditions; and the same low weight for the remaining conditions. High weight > intermediate weight > low weight. If previous buying pressure is unavailable, use current buying pressure.
Calculation: Bear evidence = the corresponding weighted sum with close < each EMA, both slopes < 0, session VWAP unavailable OR close < session VWAP, selling pressure > buying pressure, selling pressure > previous selling pressure, close < range-weighted average AND range oscillator < 0, close < range-weighted average minus the oscillator ATR times its multiplier, negative DI > positive DI, and RSI <= a bearish threshold. Use the same corresponding weights and lookbacks as bull evidence; the bearish RSI threshold is lower than the bullish threshold. If previous selling pressure is unavailable, use current selling pressure.
Calculation: Bull state = close > short EMA AND close > long EMA AND short EMA > long EMA AND both EMA slopes > 0 AND (session VWAP unavailable OR close > session VWAP) AND (buying pressure > selling pressure OR (close > range-weighted average AND range oscillator > 0)).
Calculation: Bear state = close < short EMA AND close < long EMA AND short EMA < long EMA AND both EMA slopes < 0 AND (session VWAP unavailable OR close < session VWAP) AND (selling pressure > buying pressure OR (close < range-weighted average AND range oscillator < 0)).
Long: Bull state OR (NOT bear state AND ((close > short EMA AND short EMA slope > 0 AND (session VWAP unavailable OR close > session VWAP) AND buying pressure > selling pressure AND range oscillator >= 0 AND bull evidence >= bear evidence plus a smaller evidence margin) OR bull evidence >= bear evidence plus a larger evidence margin)).
Short: Bear state OR (NOT bull state AND ((close < short EMA AND short EMA slope < 0 AND (session VWAP unavailable OR close < session VWAP) AND selling pressure > buying pressure AND range oscillator <= 0 AND bear evidence >= bull evidence plus the smaller evidence margin) OR bear evidence >= bull evidence plus the larger evidence margin)).
