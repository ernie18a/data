Calculation: Short-term EMA = exponential moving average with a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA
Calculation: Long-term EMA = exponential moving average with a longer lookback than the short-term EMA
Calculation: EMA slope = current EMA minus its value at an earlier bar; use the same lookback for both EMAs
Calculation: Buy pressure = moving average of volume-weighted bullish candle bodies, or 0.35 times volume-weighted lower wicks when the candle is not bullish
Calculation: Sell pressure = moving average of volume-weighted bearish candle bodies, or 0.35 times volume-weighted upper wicks when the candle is not bearish; use the same lookback as buy pressure
Calculation: Range oscillator = 100 times (close minus the weighted average of recent closes) divided by ATR; weight each close by the absolute percentage change from the preceding close
Calculation: Bull evidence = weighted sum of bullish conditions: close above each EMA, each EMA slope positive, close above VWAP or VWAP unavailable, buy pressure exceeds sell pressure, buy pressure is rising, range oscillator is positive and close is above its weighted average, +DI exceeds -DI, and RSI exceeds its bullish threshold
Calculation: Bear evidence = weighted sum of bearish conditions: close below each EMA, each EMA slope negative, close below VWAP or VWAP unavailable, sell pressure exceeds buy pressure, sell pressure is rising, range oscillator is negative and close is below its weighted average, -DI exceeds +DI, and RSI is below its bearish threshold
Long: close above the short-term EMA AND both EMA slopes positive AND close above VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND (buy pressure exceeds sell pressure OR range oscillator is bullish); OR close above the short-term EMA AND short-term EMA slope positive AND close above VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND buy pressure exceeds sell pressure AND range oscillator is nonnegative AND bull evidence exceeds bear evidence by a small margin; OR bull evidence exceeds bear evidence by a larger margin
Short: close below both EMAs AND short-term EMA below long-term EMA AND both EMA slopes negative AND close below VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND (sell pressure exceeds buy pressure OR range oscillator is bearish); OR close below the short-term EMA AND short-term EMA slope negative AND close below VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND sell pressure exceeds buy pressure AND range oscillator is nonpositive AND bear evidence exceeds bull evidence by a small margin; OR bear evidence exceeds bull evidence by a larger margin
