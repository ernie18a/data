Calculation: Session-slot volume statistics = Assign each in-session bar to floor(elapsed minutes since exchange-local session start divided by chart-bar duration), with elapsed minutes wrapping at midnight. For each slot, maintain a count n, mean m, and variance v using prior completed bars with nonnegative, available volume. Initialize n, m, and v to zero. After evaluating the current bar, update its slot: alpha = 1/(n+1) if n is less than the memory parameter, otherwise 2/(memory parameter+1); delta = volume-m; if n=0 set m=volume and v=0, otherwise set m=m+alpha*delta and v=max(0,(1-alpha)*(v+alpha*delta^2)); increment n, capped at 1000000.
Calculation: Volume baseline readiness = Current bar is in session and has nonnegative, available volume AND its slot's prior count is at least the minimum sample parameter AND its prior mean is positive AND max(sqrt(prior variance), prior mean multiplied by the dispersion-floor fraction) is positive.
Calculation: Volume Z = Clamp (current volume minus its slot's prior mean) divided by max(sqrt(prior variance), prior mean multiplied by the dispersion-floor fraction) to [-5,5]; available only when the volume baseline is ready.
Calculation: Upper rail = Highest high over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Lower rail = Lowest low over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout tracking state = Initially inactive. On a completed bar, if inactive and a confirmed long or short breakout occurs, set its direction to that breakout's direction, freeze its corresponding rail, and set age to zero. Otherwise, if active, increment age; clear the state when a long breakout's close is at or below its frozen rail, a short breakout's close is at or above its frozen rail, or age exceeds the tracking-length parameter. A state cleared on a bar cannot be replaced until a subsequent bar. A confirmed long breakout requires a ready volume baseline, available rails and positive ATR, close above the upper rail, previous close at or below the current upper rail, (close minus upper rail)/ATR at least the minimum breakout-distance parameter, and volume Z at least the breakout-volume threshold. A confirmed short breakout requires a ready volume baseline, available rails and positive ATR, close below the lower rail, previous close at or above the current lower rail, (lower rail minus close)/ATR at least the minimum breakout-distance parameter, and volume Z at least the breakout-volume threshold.
Long: Confirmed long breakout.
Short: Confirmed short breakout.
Long: Close above upper rail AND both rails and ATR are available AND ATR > 0 AND volume baseline is ready AND volume Z > 0.
Short: Close below lower rail AND both rails and ATR are available AND ATR > 0 AND volume baseline is ready AND volume Z > 0.
Long: Breakout tracking state is long AND close > frozen rail AND (volume baseline is not ready OR volume Z > decay threshold).
Short: Breakout tracking state is short AND close < frozen rail AND (volume baseline is not ready OR volume Z > decay threshold).
