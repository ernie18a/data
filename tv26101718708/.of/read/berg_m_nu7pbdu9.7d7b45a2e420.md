Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a chart bar whose high is greater than the highs of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; known after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a chart bar whose low is less than the lows of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; known after the bars after it complete
Calculation: RS line = stock close divided by comparative instrument close
Long: confirmed pivot low
Long: RS line equals its highest value over its lookback, AND stock high is below its highest value over that same lookback when the before-price mode is selected
Short: confirmed pivot high
Short: RS line equals its lowest value over its lookback, AND stock low is above its lowest value over that same lookback when the before-price mode is selected
