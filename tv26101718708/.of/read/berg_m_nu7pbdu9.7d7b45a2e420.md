Calculation: Moving averages = six SMAs of close with strictly increasing lengths and one EMA of close with a length between the shortest and second-shortest SMA lengths; calculate them on the chart timeframe or another selected timeframe, using the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Close > any one of the moving averages.
Short: Close < any one of the moving averages.
Long: Shortest SMA > EMA OR EMA > second-shortest SMA OR second-shortest SMA > second-longest SMA.
Short: Shortest SMA <= EMA OR EMA <= second-shortest SMA OR second-shortest SMA <= second-longest SMA.
