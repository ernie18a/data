Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same number of bars on each side, available on its confirmation bar; retain the latest and preceding confirmed swing of each kind.
Calculation: Directional break = close above the latest confirmed swing high for an upward break, or below the latest confirmed swing low for a downward break. Each swing can produce at most one break; a newly confirmed swing resets its side. Optionally require close above the EMA for an upward break or below it for a downward break.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = low below the latest confirmed swing low AND close above it for an upward sweep, or high above the latest confirmed swing high AND close below it for a downward sweep. Each swing can produce at most one sweep; a newly confirmed swing resets its side. Optionally apply the same EMA alignment as for directional breaks.
Calculation: Structure direction = the direction of the most recent directional break, initially neutral. Classify a break as a continuation if its direction equals the preceding structure direction; otherwise classify it as a change of character.
Calculation: Directional gap = low above the high two bars earlier for an upward gap, or high below the low two bars earlier for a downward gap.
Calculation: Smart money score = round and clamp to a bounded score range the midpoint plus a normalized weighted sum of structure direction, decaying continuation-break direction, decaying change-of-character direction, decaying sweep direction, decaying gap direction, live order-block direction containing close, EMA direction, and swing-range location. Encode upward and downward directions as positive and negative unit values and neutral as zero. Each event factor starts at zero, becomes its event direction on an event, and otherwise decays toward zero each bar; continuation and change-of-character factors share a decay rate slower than the shared sweep and gap decay rate. Use equal weights for structure, continuation and EMA, each twice the equal weight of change of character, sweep, gap and order block. Swing-range location is a positive half-unit when close is below the midpoint of the latest valid swing range, a negative half-unit when above it, and zero otherwise; give it the same weight as change of character. Normalize by the maximum possible weighted sum. EMA direction is positive when close is above the EMA, negative when below, and zero when equal. Create an upward order block on an upward break from the high and low of the most recent bearish candle in a configurable preceding lookback; create a downward block analogously from the most recent bullish candle. An upward block is invalid once close is below its low; a downward block is invalid once close is above its high. Retain a configurable maximum number of blocks, removing the oldest first, and use the most recently created non-invalid block containing close. When the corresponding display feature is disabled, omit its sweep, gap, order-block or swing-range contribution.
Long: A confirmed swing high exceeds the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low exceeds the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A confirmed swing high is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed swing low OR the first swing high or first swing low is confirmed.
Long: Structure direction is upward OR an upward liquidity sweep occurs OR an upward directional gap occurs OR the smart money score is at or above its bullish threshold.
Short: Structure direction is downward OR a downward liquidity sweep occurs OR a downward directional gap occurs OR the smart money score is at or below its bearish threshold.
