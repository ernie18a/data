Calculation: Pivot bar = a bar whose high (for pivot high) is the highest, or whose low (for pivot low) is the lowest, versus the L bars before it and the R bars after it. It is confirmed only on the bar R bars after the pivot bar, so the signal is evaluated on that confirming bar using the pivot bar's values.
Calculation: Supply zone = created at a confirmed pivot high; top = pivot bar high; height = max(max(pivot bar high - max(pivot bar open, pivot bar close), minimum tick floor), k * ATR at pivot bar); bottom = top - height. It stays active until broken.
Calculation: Demand zone = created at a confirmed pivot low; bottom = pivot bar low; height = max(max(min(pivot bar open, pivot bar close) - pivot bar low, minimum tick floor), k * ATR at pivot bar); top = bottom + height. It stays active until broken.
Long: (pivot low confirmed on the current bar AND, if the optional filters are used, pivot bar range >= k1 * ATR AND pivot bar RSI <= oversold level AND pivot bar volume >= k2 * average volume AND pivot bar close >= long-term EMA) OR (an active demand zone exists AND current high >= its bottom AND current low <= its top AND current close >= its bottom)
Short: (pivot high confirmed on the current bar AND, if the optional filters are used, pivot bar range >= k1 * ATR AND pivot bar RSI >= overbought level AND pivot bar volume >= k2 * average volume AND pivot bar close <= long-term EMA) OR (an active supply zone exists AND current high >= its bottom AND current low <= its top AND current close <= its top)
Exit: long, close < bottom of the active demand zone (zone broken)
Exit: short, close > top of the active supply zone (zone broken)
