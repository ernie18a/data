Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a bar whose high or low is a pivot over configurable numbers of bars to its left and right; recognize it only after the right-hand bars have elapsed.
Calculation: Bullish setup = when a confirmed pivot low is below the preceding pivot low and the latest confirmed pivot high occurred strictly between those two lows, store that high as the break level and its bar's high and low, or alternatively its body boundaries, as the zone. Replace any earlier bullish setup; expire it after a configurable maximum number of chart bars. The break may occur on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bearish setup = when a confirmed pivot high is above the preceding pivot high and the latest confirmed pivot low occurred strictly between those two highs, store that low as the break level and its bar's high and low, or alternatively its body boundaries, as the zone. Replace any earlier bearish setup; expire it after a configurable maximum number of chart bars. The break may occur on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish gap has bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low; a bearish gap has bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago. Keep only a configurable maximum number of the most recently created gaps. An unflipped bullish gap flips bearish when close < bottom; an unflipped bearish gap flips bullish when close > top. Remove a flipped bullish gap when close < bottom and a flipped bearish gap when close > top.
Calculation: Breaker = the zone of a setup when its break condition occurs. Keep only a configurable maximum number of the most recently created breakers per direction. A breaker is invalidated when a bullish breaker's close < zone bottom or a bearish breaker's close > zone top; only its first overlap with a bar can be a retest, including on its creation bar.
Long: Close >= open on a selected chart timeframe, higher timeframe, or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Short: Close < open on a selected chart timeframe, higher timeframe, or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Long: Shorter-period close EMA >= longer-period close EMA on a selected chart timeframe, higher timeframe, or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Short: Shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA on a selected chart timeframe, higher timeframe, or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Long: Bullish setup is active AND close > its break level; consume the setup.
Short: Bearish setup is active AND close < its break level; consume the setup.
Long: An uninvalidated bullish breaker has not previously been retested AND high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top.
Short: An uninvalidated bearish breaker has not previously been retested AND high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top.
Long: Low > high two bars ago.
Short: High < low two bars ago.
Long: An unflipped bearish fair value gap exists AND close > its top.
Short: An unflipped bullish fair value gap exists AND close < its bottom.
