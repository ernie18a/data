Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = a bearish FVG followed by a close above its upper boundary; bearish IFVG = a bullish FVG followed by a close below its lower boundary
Calculation: New low/high = low/high is at or below/above the lowest/highest value in the preceding configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: FX risk score = sum of daily close-minus-open directions weighted by currency risk weights for EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD, AUDJPY, and NZDJPY; directions use the daily bar still forming
