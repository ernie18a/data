Calculation: Pivot length = either a fixed length or round(clamp(base length / max(ratio floor, short-term ATR / long-term ATR), minimum length, maximum length)); use a separate right-side confirmation length. Start processing pivots and signals after a warmup of max(warmup bars, risk ATR length + warmup offset).
Calculation: Liquidity pools = process confirmed pivot highs and lows separately. Match each pivot to the first active same-side pool whose level is within the current short-term ATR times an equality multiplier and whose last pivot touch is within the maximum lookback; otherwise create a pool. A pool's level is the arithmetic mean of its pivot prices, its bounds are the level plus and minus half the tolerance at its latest pivot update, and its last-touch index is the pivot bar's index. Initialize cumulative volume with the pivot bar's volume; add each matched pivot bar's volume. If volume weighting is enabled, also add each later bar's volume when its range intersects the pool's bounds. On the first eligible sweep, make the pool inactive regardless of whether it generates a signal. Optionally remove inactive pools immediately; otherwise retain them. When the pool count exceeds its limit, remove one pool per bar: the oldest inactive pool, or, if none, the active pool with the lowest strength.
Calculation: Pool strength = clamp(touch component + recency component + volume component + higher-timeframe bonus, minimum score, maximum score). The touch component is min(touch cap, (pivot touch count - 1) times touch weight); recency is recency weight times exponential half-life decay based on bars since the last pivot touch. If enabled and the cached median cumulative volume is positive, volume component is min(volume cap, volume weight times natural logarithm of max(1, pool cumulative volume / cached median)); otherwise it is zero. The median includes active and inactive pools, is initialized when a minimum pool count is reached, and is refreshed at a fixed bar interval before the current bar's volume accumulation. The bonus is set at pool creation or a later matching pivot when the same-side confirmed pivot from the last completed higher-timeframe bar is within current tolerance times a confluence multiplier; once set it persists.
Calculation: Sweep signal = an active pool is at least its right-confirmation length plus one bar past its last pivot touch AND its strength is at least the signal threshold AND its outer bound is pierced; optionally require the close to return across its center. Evaluate every pool on each completed chart bar after pivot processing and volume accumulation.
Calculation: Trade state = enter at the signal bar's close when flat or when the current position's stop or final target is touched on that bar; if both directions signal, enter long. Signal markings are generated even while a position is locked. At entry, risk distance is either risk ATR times a stop multiplier or the distance from close to the signal bar's adverse extreme plus an ATR buffer; use the ATR distance if the latter is nonpositive or below a minimum ATR fraction. The initial stop is entry minus risk distance for a long or entry plus risk distance for a short. The first and final targets are entry plus or minus risk distance times their respective target multipliers. If enabled, after the first target is touched without the stop also being touched, move the stop to entry starting with the next bar. Do not test stops or targets on the entry bar; if the stop and final target are both touched, the stop takes priority.
Long: Low < lower bound of a low-pivot pool AND the sweep signal conditions hold AND (close > pool level OR close-back confirmation is disabled).
Short: High > upper bound of a high-pivot pool AND the sweep signal conditions hold AND (close < pool level OR close-back confirmation is disabled).
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target.
