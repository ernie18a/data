Calculation: Braid averages = three averages of the same selectable type: shortest-lookback close average, intermediate-lookback open average, and longest-lookback close average. Selectable types are EMA, DEMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, SMA, SMMA, HMA, LSMA, Kijun, McGinley, and RMA. For SMMA, use the SMA when the previous WMA is unavailable; otherwise use (previous WMA × (lookback − 1) + current source) / lookback. Kijun is the midpoint of the lookback high and low, regardless of source.
Calculation: Braid spread = maximum of the three braid averages minus their minimum.
Calculation: Custom ADX = SMA of DX over the directional-movement lookback. True range is the maximum of high − low, absolute high − previous close, and absolute low − previous close. Positive movement is max(high − previous high, 0) when high − previous high > previous low − low, otherwise 0; negative movement is max(previous low − low, 0) when previous low − low > high − previous high, otherwise 0. Replace unavailable previous OHLC values with 0. For each of true range and the two movements, recursively smooth as previous smoothed value − previous smoothed value / lookback + current value, initializing the previous smoothed value to 0. Positive and negative DI are their respective smoothed movements divided by smoothed true range, multiplied by 100. DX = 100 × absolute(positive DI − negative DI) / (positive DI + negative DI).
Long: (high + low + close) / 3 >= EMA of close.
Short: (high + low + close) / 3 < EMA of close.
Long: datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND position side is not long AND (high + low + close) / 3 >= EMA of close AND shortest-lookback braid average > intermediate-lookback braid average AND braid spread > ATR × separation parameter AND custom ADX > threshold AND previous custom ADX > custom ADX two bars ago + slope parameter AND current custom ADX > previous custom ADX + slope parameter.
Short: datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND position side is not short AND (high + low + close) / 3 < EMA of close AND intermediate-lookback braid average > shortest-lookback braid average AND braid spread > ATR × separation parameter AND custom ADX > threshold AND previous custom ADX > custom ADX two bars ago + slope parameter AND current custom ADX > previous custom ADX + slope parameter.
Exit: long, datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND high >= entry price × (1 + profit-target fraction), for each of four independently parameterized targets; the first three close their respective configured position fractions and the fourth closes the remainder.
Exit: short, datetime > optional start datetime AND datetime < optional end datetime AND low <= entry price × (1 − profit-target fraction), for each of four independently parameterized targets; the first three close their respective configured position fractions and the fourth closes the remainder.
