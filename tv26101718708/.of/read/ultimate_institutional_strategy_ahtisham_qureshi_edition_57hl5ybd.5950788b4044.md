Calculation: Fast average = TEMA of close; medium average = LSMA of close; long average = EMA of close, with fast, medium, and long lookbacks in increasing order.
Calculation: Reversal average = EMA of close.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = the most recent high or low with the specified number of bars on each side confirming it; update it on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish retest level = long average when close last crossed above it; bearish retest level = long average when close last crossed below it. Retest levels persist until replaced by another cross in the same direction.
Calculation: Zone score = mean of 100 × (close − average) / average for the fast, medium, and long averages.
Calculation: Overbought zone = zone score above the extreme oversold threshold, oversold threshold, neutral-low threshold, and neutral-high threshold; preserve that ordered classification even when the thresholds overlap.
Calculation: Long-average test count = increment when absolute distance from close to long average is below an ATR multiple and close differs from the last counted close; reset to zero when the distance exceeds a separate ATR multiple. Keep the last counted close across resets.
Calculation: Directional index = 100 × RMA(max(high − previous high, 0)) / RMA(true range) for positive direction, and 100 × RMA(max(previous low − low, 0)) / RMA(true range) for negative direction. ADX = RMA(100 × absolute difference of the two directional indices / their sum).
Calculation: Bullish consensus score = number of true conditions among RSI above its midpoint; smoothed stochastic K above its midpoint OR above its smoothed D; stochastic RSI above its midpoint; MACD line above signal line; Williams %R above its midpoint; CCI above zero. Bearish consensus score reverses every comparison.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute value of the mean of the fast, medium, and long average changes over respectively short, intermediate, and long lookbacks, divided by ATR.
Calculation: Aligned bullish trend = fast, medium, and long averages each greater than their previous values; aligned bearish trend = each less than or equal to its previous value. Trend duration counts consecutive bars of the current aligned trend and resets on loss of alignment; a new aligned trend starts at one. Trend exhaustion = duration above a threshold AND (trend strength below a threshold OR, when divergence is enabled, close has moved in the trend direction over a lookback while RSI has moved in the opposite direction over that lookback).
Calculation: Ideal long candidate = (low below confirmed pivot low AND close above previous high AND previous high minus low above an ATR multiple AND close above long average AND volume above an SMA of volume times a multiplier) OR (close inside the tolerance band around the bullish retest level AND close above open AND fast average above medium average AND volume above the same volume threshold) OR (zone score at or below the oversold threshold AND close above long average AND fast average above medium average AND volume above the same volume threshold).
Calculation: Ideal short candidate = (close inside the tolerance band around the bearish retest level AND close below open AND fast average below medium average AND volume above the original volume threshold) OR (high below confirmed pivot high AND high above previous high AND zone in the overbought zone AND fast average below medium average) OR (long-average test count at or above its threshold AND close crosses below long average AND fast average below medium average).
Calculation: Reversal long candidate = close above reversal average AND reversal average above its previous value AND (ADX above its threshold OR positive directional index above negative directional index) AND bullish consensus score at or above its threshold AND volume above an SMA of volume times the reversal multiplier. Reversal short candidate reverses these price, average, directional-index, and consensus comparisons.
Long: Low below confirmed pivot low AND close above previous high AND previous high minus low above an ATR multiple.
Short: High above confirmed pivot high AND close below previous low AND high minus previous low above an ATR multiple.
Long: Aligned bullish trend.
Short: Aligned bearish trend.
Long: Position is flat AND ideal conditions enabled AND ideal long candidate AND ideal short candidate is false.
Short: Position is flat AND ideal conditions enabled AND ideal short candidate.
Long: Position is flat AND no enabled ideal candidate AND reversals and active-hours switch enabled AND reversal long candidate.
Short: Position is flat AND no enabled ideal candidate AND reversals and active-hours switch enabled AND reversal long candidate is false AND reversal short candidate.
Long: No enabled ideal or reversal candidate takes priority AND fast average crosses above medium average AND volume above the original volume threshold AND ((alignment required AND aligned bullish trend) OR (alignment not required AND fast average above medium average AND close above long average) OR (exhaustion filter enabled AND trend exhaustion AND current aligned trend is bearish)).
Short: No enabled ideal or reversal candidate takes priority AND fast average crosses below medium average AND volume above the original volume threshold AND ((alignment required AND aligned bearish trend) OR (alignment not required AND fast average below medium average AND close below long average) OR (exhaustion filter enabled AND trend exhaustion AND current aligned trend is bullish)).
Exit: Long, low reaches entry price minus the current ATR times the stop multiplier OR high reaches entry price plus the current ATR times the profit multiplier OR, after price has advanced from entry by the trailing activation distance, low reaches the highest price since activation minus the trailing distance OR bars since entry reaches the maximum. The trailing activation distance and trailing distance are each the current ATR multiple interpreted as a number of minimum price ticks.
Exit: Short, high reaches entry price plus the current ATR times the stop multiplier OR low reaches entry price minus the current ATR times the profit multiplier OR, after price has declined from entry by the trailing activation distance, high reaches the lowest price since activation plus the trailing distance OR bars since entry reaches the maximum. The trailing activation distance and trailing distance are each the current ATR multiple interpreted as a number of minimum price ticks.
