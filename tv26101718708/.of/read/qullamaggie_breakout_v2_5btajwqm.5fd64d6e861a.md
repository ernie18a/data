Calculation: Swing high = high of the most recent confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high, whose high is at least as high as the highs over equal lookbacks on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars have closed.
Calculation: Swing low = low of the most recent confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low, whose low is at least as low as the lows over equal lookbacks on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars have closed.
Calculation: Average range percentage = SMA of 100 × (high − low) / abs(low) on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; on historical bars use the final higher-timeframe value from the start of that bar.
Calculation: Moving averages = SMAs or EMAs of close on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; on historical bars use the final higher-timeframe values from the start of that bar. The entry-filter average has a longer lookback than either selectable trailing average; the selectable trailing averages have shorter and longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Initial stop = latest confirmed swing low while flat; while long, update it to the latest confirmed swing low if entry price > previous bar's latest confirmed swing low, otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Moving-average stop = low at the most recent crossunder of low below the selected trailing average since entering the long position. Evaluate the cross on either the chart timeframe or the moving-average higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use its still-forming bar and, on historical bars, its final value from the start of that bar.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop if initial stop > moving-average stop; otherwise moving-average stop if previous chart-bar close > moving-average stop AND previous chart-bar close > selected trailing average; otherwise initial stop. If the moving-average stop is unavailable, use the initial stop.
Long: A previously placed buy stop at the latest confirmed swing high is reached by price AND, when the order was placed or updated, time was inside the configured backtest window AND either the moving-average filter was disabled OR the swing high > the longer entry-filter moving average AND either the range filter was disabled OR the swing high − the previous chart bar's initial stop < a configurable multiple of average range percentage.
Exit: long, price reaches the previously placed active sell stop.
