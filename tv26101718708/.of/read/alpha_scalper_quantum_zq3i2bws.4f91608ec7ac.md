Calculation: Range-normalized oscillator = For a selectable OHLC-derived source, find its rolling highest and lowest values. Let range be their difference. Momentum equals range if the rolling high increased from the previous bar; otherwise it equals negative range if the rolling low decreased; otherwise it equals the source change. Optionally multiply momentum and its absolute value by volume. The oscillator is its midpoint plus half its full scale multiplied by Wilder-smoothed momentum divided by Wilder-smoothed absolute momentum; use zero for the ratio when the denominator is zero or unavailable. Calculate on the chart timeframe or, optionally, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Macro averages = Four independently selected SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, or HMA calculations of close, ordered from shortest to longest lookback. Use a higher timeframe: with confirmation enabled use its last completed bar; otherwise use its forming bar. Historical lookahead-on values use the final value of the higher-timeframe bar throughout that bar.
Calculation: Bull trend = Higher-timeframe close > shortest macro average AND shortest macro average > second-shortest macro average > second-longest macro average > longest macro average. Use the same higher-timeframe bar selection as the macro averages.
Calculation: Bear trend = Higher-timeframe close < shortest macro average AND shortest macro average < second-shortest macro average < second-longest macro average < longest macro average. Use the same higher-timeframe bar selection as the macro averages.
Calculation: Oscillator states = Evaluate signals using the states from the previous chart bar, then update them in this order. At or below the lower threshold, set lower-extreme true and upper-extreme, shallow-bull, and medium-bull false. At or above the upper threshold, set upper-extreme true and lower-extreme, shallow-bear, and medium-bear false. On a cross below the upper threshold set shallow-bull true; at or below the midpoint or at or above the upper threshold set it false. On a cross above the lower threshold set shallow-bear true; at or above the midpoint or at or below the lower threshold set it false. If upper-extreme is true and the oscillator crosses below the midpoint, set medium-bull true. If lower-extreme is true and the oscillator crosses above the midpoint, set medium-bear true. At either extreme set both medium states false. After a micro long signal, reset lower-extreme, shallow-bull, and medium-bull; after a micro short signal, reset upper-extreme, shallow-bear, and medium-bear. Initialize all states false.
Calculation: Divergence reference = On the chart timeframe, confirm oscillator and source pivots of the same kind on the same bar, using equal left and right lookbacks. Accept a pair for storage only when the oscillator pivot's absolute distance from its midpoint is at least a strength threshold and its pivot bar is at least a minimum distance from the previously accepted pair of that kind. Store the two most recent accepted high pairs and low pairs separately. Update storage before testing divergence; compare the current confirmed pivot pair with the older of the two stored pairs, even when the current pair was not accepted for storage.
Calculation: Active candle direction = Start neutral. A new bullish macro alignment sets bullish; otherwise a micro long sets bullish. Then a new bearish macro alignment sets bearish; otherwise a micro short sets bearish. Each new signal resets the failure count. Set neutral when neither macro trend holds. For bullish direction, increment the failure count when close is at or below its chart-timeframe EMA OR that EMA is not rising; otherwise reset the count. For bearish direction, increment it when close is at or above the EMA OR that EMA is not falling; otherwise reset the count. Set neutral when the count reaches the consecutive-failure limit.
Long: The shortest macro average > second-shortest > second-longest > longest AND this alignment was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: The shortest macro average < second-shortest < second-longest < longest AND this alignment was false on the previous chart bar.
Long: Bull trend AND ((lower-extreme OR shallow-bull) AND oscillator crosses above the upper threshold OR medium-bull AND oscillator crosses above the midpoint).
Short: Bear trend AND ((upper-extreme OR shallow-bear) AND oscillator crosses below the lower threshold OR medium-bear AND oscillator crosses below the midpoint).
Long: A confirmed source low pivot < the divergence reference source low AND the corresponding oscillator low pivot > the divergence reference oscillator low AND the current oscillator pivot's absolute distance from the midpoint >= the strength threshold.
Short: A confirmed source high pivot > the divergence reference source high AND the corresponding oscillator high pivot < the divergence reference oscillator high AND the current oscillator pivot's absolute distance from the midpoint >= the strength threshold.
Long: Bull trend AND a confirmed source low pivot > the divergence reference source low AND the corresponding oscillator low pivot < the divergence reference oscillator low AND the current oscillator pivot's absolute distance from the midpoint >= the strength threshold.
Short: Bear trend AND a confirmed source high pivot < the divergence reference source high AND the corresponding oscillator high pivot > the divergence reference oscillator high AND the current oscillator pivot's absolute distance from the midpoint >= the strength threshold.
Long: Oscillator > upper threshold OR bull trend OR active candle direction is bullish.
Short: Oscillator < lower threshold OR bear trend OR active candle direction is bearish.
