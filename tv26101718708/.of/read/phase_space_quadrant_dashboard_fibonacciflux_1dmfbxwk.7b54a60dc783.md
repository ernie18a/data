Calculation: Phase points = for each of four progressively longer timeframes, calculate x = (RSI of the chosen price source - 50) / 50 and y = (SMA-smoothed Stochastic %K - 50) / 50. The shortest timeframe is the chart timeframe and the other three are higher. Use either the last completed bar of each timeframe or its still-forming bar, consistently across timeframes.
Calculation: Rotation = for each timeframe, calculate the change in atan2(y, x) from the preceding bar of that timeframe, wrapped into (-pi, pi]; if the preceding angle is unavailable, use a change of zero. Rotation is the negative sign of that change.
Calculation: Phase-lock scores = normalize the four nonnegative timeframe weights to sum to one. Calculate the weighted centroid of the phase points and the square root of their weighted mean squared Euclidean distance from that centroid. Tightness is 1 minus this dispersion divided by a reference parameter, clipped to [0, 1]. Magnitude is the centroid's Euclidean distance from the origin, capped at 1. Direction is the weighted mean of the rotations. The bull score is the cube root of tightness times magnitude times max(direction, 0); the bear score uses max(-direction, 0) instead. Scores are unavailable until all phase points and rotations are available.
Long: Bull score >= score threshold AND (previous chart bar's bull score < score threshold OR previous bull score is unavailable).
Short: Bear score >= score threshold AND (previous chart bar's bear score < score threshold OR previous bear score is unavailable).
