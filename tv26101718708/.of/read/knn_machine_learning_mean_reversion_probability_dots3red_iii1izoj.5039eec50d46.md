Calculation: Basis = selected moving average of close
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: P(reversion from above/below) = For each of five features—percent distance from Basis, position relative to Basis within Bollinger Bands, RSI minus 50, candle body divided by candle range, and negative volume divided by its prior-bars-only moving average—compute a rolling Z-score using prior bars only. Compare the current feature vector with historical vectors in the lookback, sampled every reversion-window bars, using Minkowski distance. For each candidate, label whether price extended on that side of Basis touched Basis within the following reversion window; non-reversions count toward the side indicated by the distance feature. Among the K nearest candidates, weight each by exp(−distance^bandwidth / (2 × median_K_distance²)); for each side, probability is the weighted reversion labels divided by the total weights for that side.
Long: P(reversion from below) > threshold AND prior P(reversion from below) <= threshold AND close < Basis AND close < Basis − gate multiplier × ATR AND no long signal since close last crossed above Basis
Short: P(reversion from above) > threshold AND prior P(reversion from above) <= threshold AND close > Basis AND close > Basis + gate multiplier × ATR AND no short signal since close last crossed below Basis
