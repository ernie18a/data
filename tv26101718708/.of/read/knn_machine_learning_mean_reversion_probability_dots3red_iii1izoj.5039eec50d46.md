Calculation: Basis = selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA of close.
Calculation: Features = [(close − basis) / max(basis, 0.000001) × 100, (close − basis) / max(Bollinger multiplier × standard deviation of close over the basis length, 0.000001), RSI of close − 50, abs(close − open) / max(high − low, 0.000001), −volume / max(SMA of preceding volumes, 1)]. Normalize each feature using its mean and standard deviation over the preceding lookback window: (current feature − mean) / max(standard deviation, 0.000001).
Calculation: Reversion label at a bar = 1 if close a reversion window earlier was above its basis and any subsequent low through the current bar was at or below its corresponding basis; −1 if that earlier close was below its basis and any subsequent high through the current bar was at or above its corresponding basis; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Reversion probabilities = initially 0 for each side. After the bar index exceeds the lookback window plus the reversion window, sample offsets from one reversion window through lookback window plus reversion window in steps of one reversion window. For each sample, calculate Minkowski distance between the current and sampled normalized feature vectors and use the reversion label at the sampled bar. If at least K samples exist, sort samples by ascending distance, select K, and set sigma to max(0.000001, the distance at zero-based sorted index max(1, floor(K / 2))). Each selected sample has weight exp(−distance^bandwidth / (2 × sigma²)). For the above probability, divide the sum of weights with label 1 by the sum of weights with label 1 or with label 0 and normalized distance feature above 0 at the bar offset equal to that sample's zero-based original array index. For the below probability, divide the sum of weights with label −1 by the sum of weights with label −1 or with label 0 and that indexed normalized distance feature at or below 0. A zero denominator gives probability 0. If fewer than K samples exist, retain the previous probabilities.
Calculation: Signal direction = initially 0; on each bar, set it to −1 when the short signal condition is met and its prior value is at least 0, or to 1 when the long signal condition is met and its prior value is at most 0. After signals are evaluated, reset it to 0 if it is 1 and close is above basis, or if it is −1 and close is below basis.
Long: Below reversion probability crosses above the probability threshold AND close < basis AND (extension gate is disabled OR close < basis − gate multiplier × ATR) AND prior signal direction <= 0.
Short: Above reversion probability crosses above the probability threshold AND close > basis AND (extension gate is disabled OR close > basis + gate multiplier × ATR) AND prior signal direction >= 0.
Long: Close < basis AND below reversion probability > 0.7 × probability threshold.
Short: Close >= basis AND above reversion probability > 0.7 × probability threshold.
