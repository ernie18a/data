Calculation: Hull oscillator = for length N, let S=floor(N/2), H=floor(sqrt(N)), and D(k)=k*(k+1)/2. Start an array with H zeros; for i from 0 through N-1 prepend 2*max(S-i,0)/D(S)-(N-i)/D(N); then prepend H-1 zeros. For each i from H through array length-1, prepend to a coefficient array the sum over j=i-H through i-1 of array[j]*(i-j)/D(H). With L=coefficient array length-1, oscillator=sum over i=0 through L of coefficient[i]*(source[i]-source[L-i]), where source is a selected price series.
Calculation: Hull state = initially 0; on each bar set it to 0 if the Hull oscillator crosses either its positive or negative threshold, otherwise set it to -1 if the oscillator is decreasing and above the positive threshold, or to 1 if the oscillator is increasing and below the negative threshold; otherwise retain its previous value. The positive threshold is a multiplier times cumulative absolute oscillator divided by the zero-based bar index; the negative threshold is its negation.
Calculation: HalfTrend state = initialize trend and next-trend to 0, maximum low to previous low or current low if unavailable, and minimum high to previous high or current high if unavailable. On each bar calculate the lowest low and highest high over the same lookback and the simple moving averages of low and high over that lookback. If next-trend is 1, update maximum low to the greater of itself and the lowest low; if average high < maximum low AND close < previous low, set trend to 1, next-trend to 0, and minimum high to the highest high. Otherwise, update minimum high to the lesser of itself and the highest high; if average low > minimum high AND close > previous high, set trend to 0, next-trend to 1, and maximum low to the lowest low. Use current low or high when the corresponding previous value is unavailable.
Calculation: Alternative ADX = calculate true range as max(high-low, abs(high-previous close), abs(low-previous close)); positive directional movement is max(high-previous high,0) when high-previous high > previous low-low, otherwise 0; negative directional movement is max(previous low-low,0) when previous low-low > high-previous high, otherwise 0. Replace unavailable previous prices with 0. For each series initialize its smoothed value to 0 and update it as previous smoothed value minus previous smoothed value divided by the lookback plus the current value. Positive and negative directional indices are their respective smoothed movements divided by smoothed true range and multiplied by 100. Alternative ADX is the simple moving average over that lookback of 100*abs(positive index-negative index)/(positive index+negative index).
Long: Hull state = 1 AND Hull state > previous Hull state.
Short: Hull state = -1 AND Hull state < previous Hull state.
Long: HalfTrend state = 0 AND previous HalfTrend state = 1 AND ATR is available.
Short: HalfTrend state = 1 AND previous HalfTrend state = 0 AND ATR is available.
Long: HalfTrend state = 0 AND previous HalfTrend state = 1 AND ATR is available AND bars since Hull state became 1 from a lower state <= maximum lookback AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected EMA source > its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR selected standard ADX or alternative ADX > threshold) AND (start-date filter disabled OR datetime >= start date) AND (end-date filter disabled OR datetime <= end date) AND (time-window filter disabled OR bar opening time in selected time zone is within selected window) AND long trading is enabled AND position side != long.
Short: HalfTrend state = 1 AND previous HalfTrend state = 0 AND ATR is available AND bars since Hull state became -1 from a higher state <= maximum lookback AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected EMA source < its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR selected standard ADX or alternative ADX > threshold) AND (start-date filter disabled OR datetime >= start date) AND (end-date filter disabled OR datetime <= end date) AND (time-window filter disabled OR bar opening time in selected time zone is within selected window) AND short trading is enabled AND position side != short.
Exit: long, close < entry price*(1-stop-loss fraction) AND (start-date filter disabled OR datetime >= start date) AND (end-date filter disabled OR datetime <= end date) AND (time-window filter disabled OR bar opening time in selected time zone is within selected window).
Exit: short, close > entry price*(1+stop-loss fraction) AND (start-date filter disabled OR datetime >= start date) AND (end-date filter disabled OR datetime <= end date) AND (time-window filter disabled OR bar opening time in selected time zone is within selected window).
Exit: long, high >= entry price plus a selected profit-target fraction of entry price, rounded to the nearest price tick; apply each of four independently configured targets to its configured position fraction, with the final target closing the remainder, after the target order has been placed within the enabled date and time window.
Exit: short, low <= entry price minus a selected profit-target fraction of entry price, rounded to the nearest price tick; apply each of four independently configured targets to its configured position fraction, with the final target closing the remainder, after the target order has been placed within the enabled date and time window.
