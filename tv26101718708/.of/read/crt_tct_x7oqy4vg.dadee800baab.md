Calculation: Structure levels = the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low on the selected structure timeframe, using equal left and right pivot lengths. Use the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe's last completed bar or a lower timeframe's last completed intrabar. Carry each sampled pivot value forward between structure bars; on each chart bar, restore any available sampled pivot before testing breaks. After an upward break clear the high level; after a downward break clear the low level.
Calculation: Structure bias = initially neutral; on each confirmed chart bar set it bullish when the structure-timeframe close exceeds the structure high level, and bearish when that close is below the structure low level. Process the upward break before the downward break. Use the last completed bar for a higher structure timeframe and the last completed intrabar for a lower structure timeframe.
Calculation: Sweep pivot low and high = the most recent confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low and high, each using equal left and right lengths. The sweep rolling lookback equals that pivot length.
Calculation: Bullish sweep bar = low < sweep pivot low AND open > sweep pivot low AND close > sweep pivot low AND low = lowest low over the sweep rolling lookback including that bar AND lowest close over the same lookback >= sweep pivot low.
Calculation: Bearish sweep bar = high > sweep pivot high AND open < sweep pivot high AND close < sweep pivot high AND high = highest high over the sweep rolling lookback including that bar AND highest close over the same lookback <= sweep pivot high.
Long: Structure-timeframe close > available structure high level on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: Structure-timeframe close < available structure low level on a confirmed chart bar.
Long: Structure bias = bullish.
Short: Structure bias = bearish.
Long: A bullish sweep bar occurred a confirmation delay ago AND each subsequent chart bar through the current bar closed above its own contemporaneous sweep pivot low AND chart bar index >= the previous bullish sweep's bar index plus the sweep cooldown, using zero as the initial previous index.
Short: A bearish sweep bar occurred a confirmation delay ago AND each subsequent chart bar through the current bar closed below its own contemporaneous sweep pivot high AND chart bar index >= the previous bearish sweep's bar index plus the sweep cooldown, using zero as the initial previous index.
