Calculation: HalfTrend baseline = Initialize trend to up, next trend to up, tracked low to the first low, tracked high to the first high, and both up and down baselines to zero. On each bar, calculate the highest high, lowest low, SMA of highs, and SMA of lows over the same amplitude lookback. If next trend is down, update tracked low to the maximum of tracked low and lowest low; if SMA of highs < tracked low AND close < previous low, set trend to down, next trend to up, and tracked high to highest high. Otherwise, update tracked high to the minimum of tracked high and highest high; if SMA of lows > tracked high AND close > previous high, set trend to up, next trend to down, and tracked low to lowest low. On an uptrend change, set the up baseline to the previous down baseline; on other uptrend bars, set it to the maximum of tracked low and previous up baseline. On a downtrend change, set the down baseline to the previous up baseline; on other downtrend bars, set it to the minimum of tracked high and previous down baseline. The active baseline is the up baseline in an uptrend and the down baseline otherwise.
Calculation: Band regime = Upper band is HalfTrend baseline plus an adjustable multiplier times ATR divided by two; lower band is HalfTrend baseline minus the same amount. Initialize regime to down. Set it to up when close crosses above the upper band, meaning current close > current upper band AND previous close <= previous upper band. Set it to down when close crosses below the lower band, meaning current close < current lower band AND previous close >= previous lower band. Otherwise retain the previous regime; if both crossings occur, down takes precedence.
Calculation: Pending fractal levels = A top fractal is confirmed when the high two bars ago is strictly greater than the highs of each of its two preceding and two following bars; a bottom fractal is confirmed when the low two bars ago is strictly less than the lows of each of those four bars. On each bar, replace the pending top with the newly confirmed top price if the band regime is up, and replace the pending bottom with the newly confirmed bottom price if the regime is down. Clear both pending levels on every regime change, after registering any fractals on that bar. Clear a pending level after its corresponding breakout decision.
Long: Band regime = up
Short: Band regime = down
Long: Band regime = up AND pending top fractal exists AND close > pending top fractal
Short: Band regime = down AND pending bottom fractal exists AND close < pending bottom fractal
