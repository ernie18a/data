Calculation: Adaptive flow = Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average of a selectable OHLC-derived price source, initialized to that source; when the optional noise filter is enabled, retain the previous value unless the absolute change to the candidate KAMA exceeds ATR times a threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive trend and stop = Initialize trend bullish and stop to adaptive flow minus ATR times a multiplier. In ATR trailing or Supertrend mode, a bullish trend flips bearish when flow < previous stop and sets stop to flow plus ATR times multiplier; otherwise stop is the maximum of previous stop and flow minus ATR times multiplier. A bearish trend flips bullish when flow > previous stop and sets stop to flow minus ATR times multiplier; otherwise stop is the minimum of previous stop and flow plus ATR times multiplier. In Chandelier mode, use the rolling highest flow minus ATR times multiplier as the bullish candidate and rolling lowest flow plus ATR times multiplier as the bearish candidate. In Donchian mode, use the rolling lowest flow as the bullish candidate and rolling highest flow as the bearish candidate; both windows include the current bar. In volatility pivot mode, use the latest confirmed flow pivot low minus ATR times a buffer as the bullish candidate and the latest confirmed flow pivot high plus ATR times a buffer as the bearish candidate; if the relevant pivot is unavailable, use flow minus or plus ATR times multiplier respectively. For these three modes, bullish trend flips bearish when flow < bullish candidate and sets stop to the bearish candidate; otherwise stop is the maximum of the previous stop and bullish candidate. Bearish trend flips bullish when flow > bearish candidate and sets stop to the bullish candidate; otherwise stop is the minimum of the previous stop and bearish candidate.
Calculation: Structure state = Initialize direction neutral and both directional BOS counts to zero. On each bar, compare close first with the previously stored confirmed price pivot high, then, only if that comparison fails, with the previously stored confirmed price pivot low. Above the high, if direction is bullish, increment the bullish BOS count; otherwise, if the bearish BOS count is at least the required count, change direction to bullish and reset both counts. Below the low, if direction is bearish, increment the bearish BOS count; otherwise, if the bullish BOS count is at least the required count, change direction to bearish and reset both counts. After these comparisons, store any newly confirmed pivot highs and lows. Pivot confirmation uses configurable left and right bar counts.
Calculation: Session VWAP = Cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting on each new calendar day in the selected timezone or on entry into a selected custom session; in custom mode accumulate only inside the session.
Long: Adaptive trend changes from bearish to bullish AND selected price source > EMA of that source on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the selected session) AND bars since the previous filtered bullish reversal signal >= cooldown; for a higher timeframe EMA, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar until the current higher-timeframe bar completes.
Short: Adaptive trend changes from bullish to bearish AND selected price source < EMA of that source on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the selected session) AND bars since the previous filtered bearish reversal signal >= cooldown; for a higher timeframe EMA, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar until the current higher-timeframe bar completes.
Long: Adaptive trend = bullish.
Short: Adaptive trend = bearish.
Long: Close > previously stored confirmed price pivot high AND (structure direction = bullish OR bearish BOS count >= required count).
Short: Close <= previously stored confirmed price pivot high OR no stored high exists AND close < previously stored confirmed price pivot low AND (structure direction = bearish OR bullish BOS count >= required count).
Long: Close > latest confirmed price pivot high.
Short: Close < latest confirmed price pivot low.
Long: Selected price source > EMA of that source on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar until the current higher-timeframe bar completes.
Short: Selected price source <= EMA of that source on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar until the current higher-timeframe bar completes.

Short: Close <= session VWAP.
