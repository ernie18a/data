Short: position side = flat AND (date filter disabled OR chart bar time >= start time AND chart bar time <= end time) AND (RSI of the last completed bar on the configured RSI timeframe > threshold OR RSI of that bar < threshold, according to the configured comparison) AND (the last completed Heikin Ashi bar on the configured Heikin Ashi timeframe and at least the configured number of consecutive bars ending with it have close > open OR all have close < open, according to the configured direction). Each configured timeframe may be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed constituent bar in the chart bar.
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage)
