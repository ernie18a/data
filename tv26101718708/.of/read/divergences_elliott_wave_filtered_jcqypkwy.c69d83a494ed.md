Calculation: Divergence pivots = confirmed chart-bar low or high pivots using configurable left and right spans. Store a limited number of each, newest first, together with RSI, smoothed stochastic RSI, MFI of typical price, and normalized ROC at the pivot. Normalized ROC = midpoint + scale × ROC / rolling standard deviation of ROC, or midpoint if the deviation is zero. Stochastic RSI is the SMA of the stochastic of RSI, using the same lookback for its RSI and stochastic stages. Evaluate a new pivot on its confirmation bar, comparing it with stored same-kind pivots newest first; stop at the first qualifying pair. Process low pivots before high pivots on a bar, and store each new pivot after its comparisons.
Calculation: Concordance = the number of enabled oscillators whose values at the two price pivots satisfy the specified oscillator comparison. For regular divergence, RSI, stochastic RSI, and MFI also require their first-pivot value to be beyond their respective oversold threshold for bullish divergence or overbought threshold for bearish divergence; normalized ROC has no zone requirement. Hidden divergence has no zone requirement.
Calculation: Slope clearance = for each intermediate bar between the pivots, subtract the straight line joining their prices from that bar's low for bullish divergence or high for bearish divergence. Ignore intermediate bars outside the available historical indexing limit. Clearance holds if no deviation changes sign from its immediately preceding deviation when the preceding deviation is nonzero; a change to zero counts as a sign change.
Calculation: Wave swings = three independent chart-bar swing sequences, using successively longer pivot-span ranges, larger minimum percentage changes, and longer proximity limits for small, medium, and large degrees. At each bar, scan all left/right span combinations for newly confirmed high and low pivots later than the sequence's last stored bar; choose the highest high and lowest low. Process both in pivot-bar order, high first on a tie. Accept each only if its percentage distance from the last stored swing meets the degree's minimum. If the last two accepted swings have the same type, retain only the more extreme one, retaining the older on equality. Limit stored swings and update the last stored bar after processing.
Calculation: Wave classification = classify each degree's latest swings in the following priority order, using prices p0 through p5 in chronological order and absolute leg amplitudes a1=|p1-p0|, a3=|p3-p2|, a5=|p5-p4|. Impulse: six swings, p2 remains beyond p0 in the direction of p1, a3 is not strictly shorter than both a1 and a5, the p3-p4 price interval lies strictly beyond the p0-p1 interval in the direction of p1, and p3 extends beyond p1; its quality is a weighted sum of third-leg speed relative to first and fifth leg speeds, parallelism of the p0-p4 and p1-p3 channels normalized by the largest impulse amplitude, and the absolute difference between correction-leg speeds divided by their sum. Diagonal: the same p2 and a3 tests, overlap of those two price intervals, and unequal p1-p3 and p2-p4 slopes; use a fixed quality. Accept impulse or diagonal only when its quality reaches the minimum. Otherwise classify W3 if the last four swings' first and third legs have the same direction and the third amplitude exceeds a configured multiple of the first; quality is their amplitude ratio divided by a scale and clamped. Otherwise classify W2 if the latest swing retraces the preceding leg without passing its origin and its retracement ratio is within configured bounds; quality is a clamped function decreasing with distance from a target retracement. Otherwise classify W4 if the first and third legs of the latest five swings share direction, the fourth retraces the third without passing its origin, its retracement ratio is within configured bounds, and the third-to-fourth interval does not overlap the first leg; use a similarly clamped retracement quality. Otherwise classify corrective C if the second leg of the latest four swings retraces less than a configured fraction of the first and the third leg is nonzero; use fixed quality. Otherwise classify triangle E if the latest five swing types alternate and their latest two highs fall while their latest two lows rise; use fixed quality. Otherwise classify neutral with zero quality. Trend is the first impulse or diagonal leg's direction, W3's third-leg direction, W2's first-leg direction, W4's first-leg direction, or the opposite of corrective C's last-leg direction; for triangle or neutral use the direction of the latest swing leg when available.
Calculation: Wave context = at the divergence confirmation bar, retain each degree's classification, quality, and trend only if its latest swing bar is within that degree's proximity limit of the second divergence pivot; otherwise use neutral, zero quality, and no trend. Optionally require the configured allowed classifications for the divergence type to pass on any, a selected, or all degrees, including neutral if configured. Assign each retained classification a configurable regular-divergence base score or hidden-divergence base score; hidden diagonal uses the hidden impulse score and hidden triangle uses neutral. Pull each score toward neutral by multiplying its deviation from neutral by the greater of its quality and a quality floor. Combine the three adjusted scores with degree weights, add the configured agreement bonus when two or three unadjusted scores exceed a high threshold, or the applicable conflict penalty when the small-degree score exceeds a stronger threshold and one or both other scores fall below a low threshold; clamp the result. Trend probability uses medium trend if present, otherwise large trend: assign the favorable value when a regular divergence opposes trend or a hidden divergence agrees with trend, and the unfavorable value otherwise; use neutral if neither trend exists. When medium and large trends both exist and agree, adjust that probability upward for favorable alignment and downward otherwise, then clamp.
Calculation: Hurst component = over a configured close window, calculate consecutive log returns in reverse chronological order, subtract their mean, and divide the range of their cumulative deviations by the standard deviation of close; take the logarithm of that ratio divided by the logarithm of the window length and clamp to the unit interval, using the neutral value when unavailable or the ratio is nonpositive. Convert it to a clamped probability decreasing linearly with Hurst using a configurable weight. Use this same conversion for regular and hidden divergences.
Calculation: Entropy component = histogram the same window's log returns into equal-width bins between their minimum and maximum; divide Shannon entropy by the logarithm of the bin count, using zero when unavailable or the range is zero, and clamp as a probability.
Calculation: Extension component = for bullish divergence select the highest stored high pivot between P1 and P2 and the highest stored high pivot before P1; for bearish select the lowest stored low pivots in those intervals. Divide the absolute P2-to-intermediate-pivot distance normalized by the intermediate price by the absolute P1-to-prior-pivot distance normalized by the prior price. Use a fixed fallback if either pivot is missing or the denominator is zero; apply a configured linear transformation and clamp as a probability.
Calculation: Posterior = logistic(prior log-odds + weighted sum of logit probabilities for concordance divided by enabled oscillator count, absolute RSI change between pivots divided by the current rolling RSI standard deviation and a configurable scale, Hurst component, entropy component, wave context, extension component, and trend probability). Use zero RSI change when RSI is disabled, zero standardized change when its deviation is nonpositive, and clamp each probability before its logit. All components are evaluated on the pivot confirmation bar except oscillator values explicitly sampled at pivots.
Long: A newly confirmed low pivot P2 has an earlier stored low pivot P1 with their bar distance within configured minimum and optional maximum limits AND the distance from the preceding emitted bullish P2 meets the bullish cooldown AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND P2 low < P1 low AND concordance of oscillator P2 > P1 is at least the minimum) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND P2 low > P1 low AND concordance of oscillator P2 < P1 is at least the minimum)) AND (slope clearance is disabled OR slope clearance holds) AND the optional wave classification filter passes AND posterior >= minimum score.
Short: A newly confirmed high pivot P2 has an earlier stored high pivot P1 with their bar distance within configured minimum and optional maximum limits AND the distance from the preceding emitted bearish P2 meets the bearish cooldown AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND P2 high > P1 high AND concordance of oscillator P2 < P1 is at least the minimum) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND P2 high < P1 high AND concordance of oscillator P2 > P1 is at least the minimum)) AND (slope clearance is disabled OR slope clearance holds) AND the optional wave classification filter passes AND posterior >= minimum score.
