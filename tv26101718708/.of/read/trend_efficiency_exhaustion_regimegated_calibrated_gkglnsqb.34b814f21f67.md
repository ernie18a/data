Calculation: Fast and slow efficiency ratios = absolute net price change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that lookback; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Internal trend regime = trend when the average of min(1, slow efficiency ratio / its normalization constant) and min(1, ADX / its normalization constant) meets the trend threshold; chaos overrides this when the volatility-cluster intensity percentile meets its threshold. Volatility-cluster intensity decays by a fixed fraction each bar and jumps when true range exceeds its moving average by a threshold multiple.
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of q-bar price changes divided by q times the variance of one-bar price changes.
Calculation: Mean-reversion half-life = -ln(2) / ln(1 + beta), where beta is the regression slope of one-bar price change on the prior price; defined only for -1 < beta < 0.
Calculation: Completed-leg displacement percentile = fraction of recent completed positive leg displacements that are no greater than the current leg displacement; require at least five completed legs. A confirmed pivot high starts a down leg and a confirmed pivot low starts an up leg; displacement is the current source price's distance from the latest opposite pivot, measured in the leg's direction.
Calculation: Divergence quality at a pivot = 0.5 × min(1, positive efficiency-ratio drop / its normalization constant) + 0.3 × min(1, bars since the prior same-side pivot / leg-memory lookback) + 0.2 if volume is lower than at the prior same-side pivot (otherwise 0). The drop is prior pivot fast efficiency ratio minus current pivot fast efficiency ratio; the pivot must make a more extreme price high or low.
