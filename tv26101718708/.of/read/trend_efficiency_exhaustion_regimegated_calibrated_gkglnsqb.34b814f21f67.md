Calculation: Source = a configurable price series derived from the chart bar; use close by default.
Calculation: Fast and slow efficiency ratios = absolute source change over the respective lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback, or zero if the sum is zero. The fast lookback is shorter. Optionally set each lookback to a rounded, bounded multiple of the Ehlers homodyne dominant cycle of hl2, using a smaller multiple for the fast lookback; use the fixed lookbacks until the cycle is available.
Calculation: Leg state and displacement percentile = confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot widths. At each confirmed high, record its positive distance from the latest pivot low, set the leg direction to down, and set the anchor to that high. At each confirmed low, record its positive distance from the latest pivot high, set the leg direction to up, and set the anchor to that low. Process a high before a low if both confirm together. Retain a bounded history of completed distances. Current displacement is source minus anchor for an up leg, or anchor minus source for a down leg. Its percentile is the fraction of retained distances less than or equal to current displacement; it is unavailable until the minimum history size is reached.
Calculation: Divergence quality = at each confirmed pivot, compare it with the previous confirmed pivot of the same type. Score 0.5 times the capped ratio of the positive decline in fast efficiency at the pivot to a scale parameter, but only if the new high is higher or the new low is lower; add 0.3 times the capped ratio of bars between the pivots to the completed-leg history capacity; add 0.2 if pivot volume declined. Each capped ratio is capped at one. For an up leg use the latest high-pivot score; for a down leg use the latest low-pivot score.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster percentile = percentile rank over a rolling lookback of an intensity initialized to zero and updated each bar as prior intensity times exponential decay plus a fixed jump when true range exceeds a multiple of its moving average.
Calculation: Trend score = the mean of slow efficiency divided by its normalization parameter and ADX divided by its normalization parameter, each capped at one; use zero for unavailable ADX.
Calculation: Variance ratio = for a window of N one-bar source changes and a shorter aggregation period q, let mu be their mean, A be the sum of squared deviations from mu divided by N-1, and C be the sum over all overlapping q-bar changes of their squared deviations from q times mu, divided by q times (N-q+1) times (1-q/N). The ratio is C divided by q times A; it is unavailable when the denominator is nonpositive.
Calculation: Mean-reversion half-life = negative log(2) divided by log(1+b), where b is the rolling regression slope of one-bar source change on the previous source; it is unavailable unless -1 < b < 0.
Calculation: Exhaustion eligibility = the stretched-displacement threshold is reached AND fast efficiency is below its value at the rollover lookback AND either the regime veto is disabled OR volatility-cluster percentile is below the chaos threshold AND trend score is at least the trend threshold AND either the variance-ratio gate is disabled OR the variance ratio is unavailable OR it is at least its trend floor AND either the half-life gate is disabled OR half-life is available and no greater than an evaluation-horizon multiple AND either the divergence gate is disabled OR divergence quality is available and at least its threshold. Treat the external volatility-series gate as disabled because that series is absent from the backtest data.
Calculation: Exhaustion cooldown = bars since the last exhaustion event in either direction; ignition cooldown = bars since the last ignition event in either direction. Each is initially unrestricted. An exhaustion event takes priority over an ignition event on the same bar.
Long: Leg direction is down AND exhaustion eligibility is true AND exhaustion cooldown is at least its minimum.
Short: Leg direction is up AND exhaustion eligibility is true AND exhaustion cooldown is at least its minimum.
Long: Ignition is enabled AND leg direction is up AND fast efficiency crosses above its ignition threshold AND displacement percentile is at most its young-leg threshold AND ignition cooldown is at least its minimum AND no exhaustion event fires on this bar AND either the regime veto is disabled OR volatility-cluster percentile is unavailable or below the chaos threshold.
Short: Ignition is enabled AND leg direction is down AND fast efficiency crosses above its ignition threshold AND displacement percentile is at most its young-leg threshold AND ignition cooldown is at least its minimum AND no exhaustion event fires on this bar AND either the regime veto is disabled OR volatility-cluster percentile is unavailable or below the chaos threshold.
