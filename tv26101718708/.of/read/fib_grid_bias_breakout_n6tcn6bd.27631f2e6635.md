Calculation: Grid levels = For each enabled level coefficient, calculate anchor high minus the coefficient times the anchor span. In manual mode, use the configured anchor high and low. In automatic mode, use the running high and low of chart bars in the configured overnight session while it is building; on the first bar after that session, the anchors are unavailable; afterward use the frozen session high and low, padded symmetrically around their midpoint to a configured minimum span. Automatic anchors are unavailable during the configured weekend blackout. If no frozen range is available during the holding period, use the configured manual anchors. At initialization, recover the most recent contiguous run of historical bars whose New York hour is at or after the session start or within the midnight hour, scanning backward up to a configured limit; use its high and low as the initial frozen range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = Shorter-period and longer-period EMAs of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, with its values carried forward between completions; their periods match the corresponding chart-timeframe EMAs.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close <= longer-period EMA of close.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND their difference > close times a configured percentage AND shorter-period EMA > its previous value AND higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA > higher-timeframe longer-period EMA.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND their difference > close times a configured percentage AND shorter-period EMA < its previous value AND higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA < higher-timeframe longer-period EMA.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND their difference > close times a configured percentage AND shorter-period EMA > its previous value AND higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA > higher-timeframe longer-period EMA AND previous close < any current enabled grid level AND current close > that same current grid level AND bars since the most recent long or short breakout >= configured cooldown, or no prior breakout exists.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND their difference > close times a configured percentage AND shorter-period EMA < its previous value AND higher-timeframe shorter-period EMA < higher-timeframe longer-period EMA AND previous close > any current enabled grid level AND current close < that same current grid level AND bars since the most recent long or short breakout >= configured cooldown, or no prior breakout exists.
