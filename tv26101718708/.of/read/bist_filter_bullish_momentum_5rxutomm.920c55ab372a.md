Calculation: RelativeVolume = volume / SMA(volume, N)
Calculation: CMF = sum(MFM * volume, N) / sum(volume, N), where MFM = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), and MFM = 0 when high == low
Calculation: StochRSI_K = SMA(stochastic of RSI over a lookback, smoothing); StochRSI_D = SMA(StochRSI_K, same smoothing length)
Calculation: IchimokuConversion = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the shortest of the Ichimoku lookbacks
Long: RelativeVolume > threshold above 1 AND RSI > its neutral midline AND ADX > trend-strength threshold AND +DI > -DI AND close > EMA(short) AND close > EMA(medium) AND close > EMA(very long) AND MACD line > MACD signal line AND Parabolic SAR < close AND MFI (on typical price hlc3) > its neutral midline AND CMF > positive threshold AND Hull MA (short lookback) < close AND StochRSI_K >= StochRSI_D AND IchimokuConversion < close AND EMA(fast) < EMA(mid) < EMA(slow) AND EMA(slowest) < EMA(slow) AND SMA(fast) < SMA(mid) < SMA(slow) AND SMA(slowest) < SMA(slow) (fast < mid < slow < slowest lookbacks; the same relationship in both the EMA and SMA sets; the EMA(very long) in the close-above condition is the same as EMA(slowest)); all computed on the chart timeframe using the current bar
