Calculation: Accumulation range = in adaptive mode, select a whole-bar candidate window whose later bars stay within the earlier seed bars and whose width is no greater than the rolling percentile cutoff of prior candidate-window widths; in opening-range mode, use the high and low of the selected fixed opening window.
Calculation: RTH ATR = Wilder ATR calculated from regular-session true ranges; use the value known before the current bar for sweep qualification and latch the value at reclaim for distribution confirmation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe accumulation range = the high and low of the first four completed bars in that period's calendar container; evaluate the period using its last completed bar.
Long: Confirmed regular-session bar closes above the accumulation high after a sweep below the accumulation low and a subsequent close back inside the range, within the configured sweep and reclaim deadlines, with no opposite-boundary breach or both-boundary sweep; if selective volatility confirmation is enabled, the sweep depth is at least the configured fraction of prior RTH ATR and the confirming bar closes up with body at least the configured fraction of reclaim-latched RTH ATR and at least the configured fraction of its range. OR, on a completed higher-timeframe bar, the same sequence closes above that period's accumulation high after a sweep below its accumulation low and reclaim, with no ambiguity.
Short: Confirmed regular-session bar closes below the accumulation low after a sweep above the accumulation high and a subsequent close back inside the range, within the configured sweep and reclaim deadlines, with no opposite-boundary breach or both-boundary sweep; if selective volatility confirmation is enabled, the sweep depth is at least the configured fraction of prior RTH ATR and the confirming bar closes down with body at least the configured fraction of reclaim-latched RTH ATR and at least the configured fraction of its range. OR, on a completed higher-timeframe bar, the same sequence closes below that period's accumulation low after a sweep above its accumulation high and reclaim, with no ambiguity.
