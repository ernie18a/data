Calculation: Session = the configured regular trading session identified from datetime; evaluate intraday calculations on completed session bars, resetting the cycle at each new session.
Calculation: Intraday balance = either the high and low of the initial session window, provided it contains at least the minimum number of bars and has positive width, or the first eligible adaptive window. An adaptive window has a seed followed by a shorter containment segment whose highs and lows remain within the seed high and low; its width must be positive and no greater than a configured nearest-rank percentile of a baseline of earlier same-length window widths. Keep recent window widths pending until they cannot overlap the candidate; flush pending widths at session end or a gap. After an adaptive cycle ends, wait for a close outside its balance, then begin looking for a new candidate strictly after that bar. The opening-window model permits one cycle per session.
Calculation: Intraday directional resolution = after a balance is set, wait until a subsequent bar breaches exactly one boundary within the sweep deadline. If the selective profile is used, the breach depth must also be at least a configured multiple of the preceding session-bar Wilder ATR. A breach of both boundaries ends the cycle unresolved. Starting with the sweep bar, require a close inside or on the balance within the reclaim deadline; before reclaim, a breach of the opposite boundary ends the cycle unresolved. Starting on the bar after reclaim, end the cycle unresolved if both boundaries are breached on one bar or if the close is beyond the swept boundary. Otherwise, a close beyond the opposite boundary resolves the cycle in that direction; under the selective profile, the bar must also have a body in that direction, at least a configured multiple of the preceding session-bar Wilder ATR captured at reclaim, and at least a configured fraction of its own range. If this body filter fails, keep waiting. End an unresolved cycle at the applicable deadline or session end.
Calculation: Calendar directional resolution = independently for each configured higher timeframe, use its last completed bar and reset at the start of its configured longer calendar container. The high and low of the initial configured number of higher-timeframe bars form the balance if its width is positive. Thereafter, a breach of both boundaries ends the container unresolved; a breach of exactly one starts a sweep. Starting with the sweep bar, require a close inside or on the balance; before reclaim, a breach of the opposite boundary ends the container unresolved. Starting on the higher-timeframe bar after reclaim, a bar breaching both boundaries or closing beyond the swept boundary ends the container unresolved; a close beyond the opposite boundary resolves the container in that direction. Retain the resolution until the container resets.
Long: Intraday directional resolution equals upward OR calendar directional resolution equals upward.
Short: Intraday directional resolution equals downward OR calendar directional resolution equals downward.
