Calculation: Close-based Supertrend = Start with upper and lower bands equal to close plus and minus an ATR multiple. Carry the previous lower band forward unless the new lower band is higher or the previous close is below the previous lower band; carry the previous upper band forward unless the new upper band is lower or the previous close is above the previous upper band. Initialize the direction as down. If the previous Supertrend equals the previous upper band, switch up when close exceeds the current upper band; otherwise remain down. If it does not, switch down when close is below the current lower band; otherwise remain up. The Supertrend is the lower band in an up direction and the upper band in a down direction.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI divergence = A price high and RSI high, or a price low and RSI low, are both confirmed as pivots on the same bar, using equal left and right lookbacks. Compare them with the preceding simultaneously confirmed pair of the same type. Bearish divergence has a higher price high and lower RSI high; bullish divergence has a lower price low and higher RSI low. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Qualified crossover = Close crosses above the close-based Supertrend, the UTC bar hour is within either configured session, higher-timeframe close is above its EMA, ADX is above its threshold, and either RSI is below its overbought threshold or no bearish RSI divergence was confirmed within the configured recent-bar window. The higher-timeframe calculation uses the last completed bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Qualified crossunder = Close crosses below the close-based Supertrend, the UTC bar hour is within either configured session, higher-timeframe close is below its EMA, ADX is above its threshold, and either RSI is above its oversold threshold or no bullish RSI divergence was confirmed within the configured recent-bar window. The higher-timeframe calculation uses the last completed bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Retest zone = On a qualified crossover or crossunder with ADX above the additional retest threshold, replace any pending zone with a zone between that bar's close and that close minus or plus an ATR multiple, respectively. Keep it pending until the first retest, a replacement, expiration after the configured maximum wait, or an earlier close below the current rolling lowest low minus an ATR buffer for a bullish zone or above the current rolling highest high plus an ATR buffer for a bearish zone. Calculate a retest before applying expiration or invalidation on the same bar.
Calculation: Range filter = Smooth the absolute change in close with an EMA, smooth that result with a second EMA whose length is derived from the first length, and multiply by a range multiplier. Initialize the filter at close; thereafter, if close is above the previous filter, use the greater of the previous filter and close minus the smoothed range; otherwise use the lesser of the previous filter and close plus the smoothed range. Compute a longer-lookback, larger-multiplier filter and a shorter-lookback, smaller-multiplier filter.
Calculation: Structure swing = For each of a longer swing lookback and a shorter internal lookback, compare the high and low that many bars ago with the highest high and lowest low of the intervening bars. Set the swing state to high if that old high exceeds the intervening highest high, otherwise set it to low if that old low is below the intervening lowest low, otherwise retain the state. A state change confirms the corresponding old high or low as the latest swing level. Each newly confirmed level can be broken once.
Calculation: Internal trend = Set the trend up when close crosses above an unbroken internal swing high different from the latest longer-lookback swing high; set it down when close crosses below an unbroken internal swing low different from the latest longer-lookback swing low. If the optional confluence filter is enabled, additionally require high minus max(close, open) to be greater than min(close, open minus low) for an upward break, or less than it for a downward break.
Calculation: Fair value gap threshold = If automatic thresholding is enabled, take the cumulative sum, across chart bars, of the absolute percentage change from open to close of the previous gap-timeframe bar on each new gap-timeframe bar, divided by the chart bar index and multiplied by a threshold factor; otherwise use zero. For a higher gap timeframe, historical requests use the last completed bar and real-time requests use the still-forming bar; for a lower gap timeframe, use the last available intrabar. The previous and two-bars-ago references are relative to the requested gap timeframe.

Short: Close crosses below the close-based Supertrend.
Long: Close is above the close-based Supertrend.
Short: Close is at or below the close-based Supertrend.
Long: Bullish RSI divergence is confirmed.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence is confirmed.
Long: A bullish retest zone is pending AND at least one full bar has elapsed since its creation AND high is greater than low AND abs(close minus open) divided by high minus low is at least the minimum body ratio AND low is at or below the zone top AND close is at or above the zone bottom AND close is above open.
Short: A bearish retest zone is pending AND at least one full bar has elapsed since its creation AND high is greater than low AND abs(close minus open) divided by high minus low is at least the minimum body ratio AND high is at or above the zone bottom AND close is at or below the zone top AND close is below open.
Long: The longer range filter is at or below the shorter range filter.
Short: The longer range filter is above the shorter range filter.
Long: A longer-lookback swing high is confirmed above the preceding swing high OR a longer-lookback swing low is confirmed at or above the preceding swing low.
Short: A longer-lookback swing high is confirmed at or below the preceding swing high OR a longer-lookback swing low is confirmed below the preceding swing low.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken longer-lookback swing high OR an eligible internal swing high.
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken longer-lookback swing low OR an eligible internal swing low.
Long: Internal trend is up.
Short: Internal trend is down or uninitialized.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its requested low is above the high two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous gap-timeframe close is above that high AND the previous gap-timeframe candle's percentage change is above the fair value gap threshold.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its requested high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the previous gap-timeframe close is below that low AND the negative of the previous gap-timeframe candle's percentage change is above the fair value gap threshold.
