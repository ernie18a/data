Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA and higher-timeframe EMA > its value on the prior higher-timeframe bar; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Pullback from latest higher-timeframe swing high = higher-timeframe trend is up and higher-timeframe close < latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high; pullback from latest higher-timeframe swing low = higher-timeframe trend is down and higher-timeframe close > latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming; pivots use the swing lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = current low <= the prior bar’s rolling lowest low over the divergence lookback AND current low < prior low AND current RSI > the prior bar’s rolling lowest RSI over that lookback AND current RSI < oversold threshold; bearish RSI divergence = current high >= the prior bar’s rolling highest high over the divergence lookback AND current high > prior high AND current RSI < the prior bar’s rolling highest RSI over that lookback AND current RSI > overbought threshold.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current close > current open AND prior close < prior open AND current close > prior open AND current open < prior close; bearish engulfing = current close < current open AND prior close > prior open AND current close < prior open AND current open > prior close.
Calculation: Swing-low bullish engulfing = bullish engulfing on a confirmed pivot-low bar or within the next two bars; swing-high bearish engulfing = bearish engulfing on a confirmed pivot-high bar or within the next two bars. Pivots use the swing lookback on both sides.
Long: position is flat AND higher-timeframe trend is up AND chart close > higher-timeframe EMA AND pullback from latest higher-timeframe swing high AND (RSI < oversold threshold OR RSI < 40) AND (bullish RSI divergence OR (RSI > prior RSI AND prior RSI < oversold threshold)) AND swing-low bullish engulfing
Short: position is flat AND higher-timeframe trend is down AND chart close < higher-timeframe EMA AND pullback from latest higher-timeframe swing low AND (RSI > overbought threshold OR RSI > 60) AND (bearish RSI divergence OR (RSI < prior RSI AND prior RSI > overbought threshold)) AND swing-high bearish engulfing
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price × (1 - take-profit percentage)
