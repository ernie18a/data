Calculation: Fair value = VWMA of close, falling back to EMA of close with the same lookback when cumulative volume is zero or VWMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA when cumulative volume and the SMA are positive; otherwise 1. Missing volume is treated as zero.
Calculation: Extension = (close - fair value) / ATR when ATR is positive; otherwise 0. Treat an unavailable numerator as 0.
Calculation: Herding = EMA of bar direction multiplied by relative volume capped at a threshold, clamped to [-1, 1]; replace an unavailable result with 0. Bar direction is 1 when close > open, -1 when close < open, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Range position = 2 * (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) - 1 over a lookback including the current bar; use 0 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Crowd emotion = EMA of the weighted average of five components, mapped from [-1, 1] to the index scale. The components are extension divided by its cap and clamped to [-1, 1]; RSI of close centered and scaled to [-1, 1] and clamped; herding; EMA of (close - previous close) / ATR, divided by a scaling parameter and clamped to [-1, 1]; and range position. Use 0 for thrust when ATR is not positive or the price change is unavailable, the RSI midpoint when RSI is unavailable, the index midpoint when the final EMA is unavailable, and a weighted average of 0 when the sum of weights is 0.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= an upper close-position threshold AND (min(open, close) - low >= a fraction of (high - low) OR close > open).
Calculation: Bearish rejection = high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) <= a lower close-position threshold AND (high - max(open, close) >= a fraction of (high - low) OR close < open).
Calculation: Volume climax = cumulative volume > 0 AND relative volume >= a climax threshold.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < lowest low over a swing lookback ending on the previous chart bar AND close > that lowest low; alternatively, on an intraday chart, low < the low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar AND close > that low.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high > highest high over a swing lookback ending on the previous chart bar AND close < that highest high; alternatively, on an intraday chart, high > the high of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar AND close < that high.
Calculation: Sellers trapped = bullish sweep AND min(open, close) - low >= ATR multiplied by a wick threshold.
Calculation: Buyers trapped = bearish sweep AND high - max(open, close) >= ATR multiplied by a wick threshold.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = lowest low over a short lookback <= lowest low over a longer divergence lookback AND lowest crowd emotion over the short lookback > lowest crowd emotion over the longer lookback + a divergence threshold; all windows include the current bar.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = highest high over a short lookback >= highest high over a longer divergence lookback AND highest crowd emotion over the short lookback < highest crowd emotion over the longer lookback - a divergence threshold; all windows include the current bar.
Calculation: Uptrend = shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close AND close > fair value.
Calculation: Downtrend = shorter EMA of close < longer EMA of close AND close < fair value.
Calculation: Long reversal quality = count of true conditions: lowest crowd emotion over the emotion lookback including the current bar <= panic threshold; volume climax on the current or either of the two preceding bars; bullish rejection; sellers trapped on the current or previous bar; bullish divergence.
Calculation: Short reversal quality = count of true conditions: highest crowd emotion over the emotion lookback including the current bar >= euphoria threshold; volume climax on the current or either of the two preceding bars; bearish rejection; buyers trapped on the current or previous bar; bearish divergence.
Calculation: Long trap quality = count of true conditions: the current bar sweeps the last completed higher-timeframe daily low on an intraday chart; the current bar sweeps the preceding swing low; bullish rejection; volume climax; crowd emotion <= fear threshold. A sweep requires a breach and a close back across the level.
Calculation: Short trap quality = count of true conditions: the current bar sweeps the last completed higher-timeframe daily high on an intraday chart; the current bar sweeps the preceding swing high; bearish rejection; volume climax; crowd emotion >= greed threshold. A sweep requires a breach and a close back across the level.
Calculation: Long herd quality = count of true conditions: uptrend; relative volume >= its neutral level; herding >= a positive threshold; extension < a positive threshold; range position > 0.
Calculation: Short herd quality = count of true conditions: downtrend; relative volume >= its neutral level; herding <= a negative threshold; extension > a negative threshold; range position < 0.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = bars elapsed since the last emitted signal > cooldown. Evaluate signals on completed chart bars, using this priority when several conditions coincide: long reversal, short reversal, long trap, short trap, long herd, short herd. Only the highest-priority eligible condition emits a signal and resets the cooldown. Each signal family can be independently enabled.
Long: Signal eligibility AND reversal family enabled AND lowest crowd emotion over the emotion lookback including the current bar <= fear threshold AND crowd emotion > previous crowd emotion AND long reversal quality >= its minimum.
Short: Signal eligibility AND reversal family enabled AND long reversal condition is false AND highest crowd emotion over the emotion lookback including the current bar >= greed threshold AND crowd emotion < previous crowd emotion AND short reversal quality >= its minimum.
Long: Signal eligibility AND trap family enabled AND both reversal conditions are false AND sellers trapped AND bullish rejection AND long trap quality >= its minimum.
Short: Signal eligibility AND trap family enabled AND both reversal conditions and the long trap condition are false AND buyers trapped AND bearish rejection AND short trap quality >= its minimum.
Long: Signal eligibility AND herd family enabled AND both reversal and both trap conditions are false AND uptrend AND crowd emotion crosses above its midpoint AND long herd quality >= its minimum.
Short: Signal eligibility AND herd family enabled AND both reversal and both trap conditions and the long herd condition are false AND downtrend AND crowd emotion crosses below its midpoint AND short herd quality >= its minimum.
Calculation: Entry stop = signal-bar low minus signal-bar ATR multiplied by a stop-buffer parameter for a long position, or signal-bar high plus signal-bar ATR multiplied by the stop-buffer parameter for a short position. Entry price is the signal-bar close. Target = entry price plus the absolute entry-to-stop distance multiplied by a target parameter for a long position, or entry price minus that distance multiplied by the target parameter for a short position. Track only one position at a time; after an exit, a signal on the same bar can open a new position.
Exit: Long, a completed bar after the entry bar has low <= entry stop OR high >= target; the stop takes precedence if both are reached.
Exit: Short, a completed bar after the entry bar has high >= entry stop OR low <= target; the stop takes precedence if both are reached.
