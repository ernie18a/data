Calculation: Breakout levels = highest high and lowest low over the channel lookback ending on the previous bar, respectively plus and minus the current ATR times an entry-buffer multiplier.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = current ATR divided by its simple moving average; use 1 when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Volatility filter = optionally require the volatility ratio to be at least a high threshold, at least a lower threshold, or at most an upper threshold.
Calculation: Target and stop distances = the greater of a minimum price increment, current ATR times the respective target or stop multiplier, and channel width times the respective target or stop width multiplier. Channel width is the upper breakout level minus the lower breakout level. Optionally increase the ATR multipliers on sufficiently low chart timeframes.
Calculation: Target and initial stop = freeze the target and stop distances at entry; add and subtract them respectively from the long entry price, and subtract and add them respectively from the short entry price. Entry price is the corresponding breakout level.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after the target is first touched, if trailing was selected at entry or automatic mode was selected and the entry-bar volatility ratio was at least its threshold, update the long stop to the greater of its previous value and current close minus the current stop distance; update the short stop to the lesser of its previous value and current close plus the current stop distance. Otherwise retain the initial stop.
Calculation: Volume climax = volume and volume-spread simple moving averages are positive AND (volume is at least its average times a climax multiplier OR volume times (high minus low) is at least its own average times the climax multiplier).
Calculation: Rising volume = volume and volume-spread simple moving averages are positive AND volume climax is false AND volume is at least its average times a rising-volume multiplier.
Long: Position is flat AND channel width is greater than the minimum price increment AND the volatility filter passes AND optionally close is above EMA AND either (confirmed-close mode AND close is at least the upper breakout level AND previous close is below the previous upper breakout level) OR (wick-touch mode AND high is at least the upper breakout level AND previous high is below the previous upper breakout level).
Short: Position is flat AND channel width is greater than the minimum price increment AND the volatility filter passes AND optionally close is below EMA AND either (confirmed-close mode AND close is at most the lower breakout level AND previous close is above the previous lower breakout level) OR (wick-touch mode AND low is at most the lower breakout level AND previous low is above the previous lower breakout level) AND no long entry occurs on the same bar.
Long: Close is at least open AND (volume climax OR rising volume).
Short: Close is below open AND (volume climax OR rising volume).
Exit: Long, bar is after the entry bar AND target has not previously been touched AND high is at least the target AND either conservative stop-first mode is disabled OR low is above the stop before its current-bar trailing update; exit half the position and retain the trade state until the stop is hit.
Exit: Short, bar is after the entry bar AND target has not previously been touched AND low is at most the target AND either conservative stop-first mode is disabled OR high is below the stop before its current-bar trailing update; exit half the position and retain the trade state until the stop is hit.
Exit: Long, bar is after the entry bar AND low is at most the stop; in conservative stop-first mode, test the existing stop before the target when both are touched, otherwise process the target and current-bar trailing update before testing the stop.
Exit: Short, bar is after the entry bar AND high is at least the stop; in conservative stop-first mode, test the existing stop before the target when both are touched, otherwise process the target and current-bar trailing update before testing the stop.
