Calculation: Opening reference = On the chart timeframe, collect either all bars in the selected opening session or the first selected number of bars in each calendar period; for the higher-calendar-period fallback, collect the first selected number of chart bars in each selected higher calendar period. Lock the reference on the first bar after collection ends: H = maximum high, L = minimum low, C = last close, V = mean volume, and R = H - L. Use the ATR calculated on the lock bar, falling back to R if unavailable, as A. The reference remains in force until the next reference locks.
Calculation: Drive quality = Let N(x,a,b) = clamp(100 × (x-a)/(b-a), 0, 100). Let direction d = 1 if C >= (H+L)/2, otherwise -1. Let location = (C-L)/R for d = 1, otherwise (H-C)/R. Let relative volume = V divided by the chart-bar volume SMA on the lock bar, or 1 if that SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Drive quality = clamp(0.44 × N(R/A,0.45,1.70) + 0.36 × clamp(100 × location,0,100) + 0.20 × N(relative volume,0.70,1.50),0,100).
Calculation: Pullback plan = For d = 1, zone top = H - R × shallow retracement, zone bottom = H - R × deep retracement, trigger = H + A × trigger buffer, failure = zone bottom - A × failure buffer, and target = H + R × target multiple. For d = -1, zone top = L + R × deep retracement, zone bottom = L + R × shallow retracement, trigger = L - A × trigger buffer, failure = zone top + A × failure buffer, and target = L - R × target multiple. Shallow retracement is less than ideal retracement, which is less than deep retracement. Zone midpoint is the average of its boundaries.
Calculation: Plan tracking = A plan is armed when drive quality reaches its sensitivity-adjusted drive threshold. Track it after the reference locks, within the configured tracking session where applicable, until the next reference locks, its memory limit is exceeded, it is invalidated, or it expires. A zone touch occurs when high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top. Record the first touch bar and the greatest depth on bars that touch the zone, where depth = (H-low)/R for d = 1 and (high-L)/R for d = -1. Invalidation occurs when low < failure for d = 1 or high > failure for d = -1 in realtime mode; otherwise use close < failure for d = 1 or close > failure for d = -1. The plan expires when bars since lock exceed the review limit and continuation has not previously occurred, provided it is not invalidated. Evaluate expiration after the current bar's continuation decision.
Calculation: Pullback score = Let P(x,a,b,c) = N(x,a,b) if x <= b, otherwise N(c-x,0,c-b). Clamp the greatest recorded depth to [0,2]. Recovery = (close - zone bottom)/(zone top - zone bottom) for d = 1, otherwise (zone top - close)/(zone top - zone bottom). Live relative volume = volume divided by its chart-bar SMA, or 1 if unavailable or nonpositive. Volatility fit = chart-bar ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA, or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Room = (target-close)/A and risk = (close-failure)/A for d = 1; room = (close-target)/A and risk = (failure-close)/A for d = -1. Risk reward = clamp(room/max(risk,0.10),0,9.99). After a zone touch, score = clamp(0.18 × drive quality + 0.30 × P(depth,shallow retracement,ideal retracement,deep retracement+0.18) + 0.27 × clamp(100 × recovery,0,100) + 0.10 × N(live relative volume,0.65,1.35) + 0.05 × P(volatility fit,0.60,1.00,1.85) + 0.10 × N(risk reward,0.75,2.00),0,100).
Long: A reference locks AND C >= (H+L)/2.
Short: A reference locks AND C < (H+L)/2.
Long: Plan tracking is active AND d = 1 AND the zone has been touched AND the plan is not invalidated on this bar AND ((close > trigger AND pullback score >= alert threshold AND continuation has not previously occurred) OR (close >= zone midpoint AND pullback score >= sensitivity-adjusted constructive threshold AND constructive has not previously occurred AND either volume confirmation is disabled or live relative volume >= its confirmation threshold AND either strict confirmation is disabled or risk reward >= its confirmation threshold)).
Short: Plan tracking is active AND d = -1 AND the zone has been touched AND the plan is not invalidated on this bar AND ((close < trigger AND pullback score >= alert threshold AND continuation has not previously occurred) OR (close <= zone midpoint AND pullback score >= sensitivity-adjusted constructive threshold AND constructive has not previously occurred AND either volume confirmation is disabled or live relative volume >= its confirmation threshold AND either strict confirmation is disabled or risk reward >= its confirmation threshold)).
Exit: long, plan tracking is active AND d = 1 AND the plan is invalidated on this bar.
Exit: short, plan tracking is active AND d = -1 AND the plan is invalidated on this bar.
