Calculation: Bullish and bearish displacement score = round(100 × (0.30 × min(abs(close − open) / ATR, 3) / 3 + 0.30 × min(volume / safe average volume, 3) / 3 + 0.20 × min(max(displacement / ATR, 0), 3) / 3 + 0.20 × trend alignment)), where average volume is the SMA of volume, safe average volume is 1 when that SMA is zero and otherwise the SMA, bullish displacement is close minus the highest high of the preceding displacement lookback, bearish displacement is the lowest low of the preceding displacement lookback minus close, and trend alignment is 1 when bullish close > EMA of close or bearish close < EMA of close, otherwise 0. The ATR lookback is shorter than the volume SMA lookback, which is shorter than the EMA lookback.
Long: Close > highest high of the preceding displacement lookback AND close > open AND bullish displacement score >= minimum score AND at least one preceding bearish candle exists within the origin lookback, excluding the first bar of the dataset.
Short: Close < lowest low of the preceding displacement lookback AND close < open AND bearish displacement score >= minimum score AND at least one preceding bullish candle exists within the origin lookback, excluding the first bar of the dataset.
