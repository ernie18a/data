Calculation: Cascade score = min(100, 30 × the larger of the bullish and bearish level counts + 10 when that larger count is 3 and LTF RSI > 55 or < 45), rounded to an integer; each level count is the number of its three conditions that are true: recent same-direction LTF trigger, MTF state, and HTF state.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an LTF close crosses above its EMA and LTF RSI > 50 within the configured window bars, and the higher-than-chart MTF last completed bar closes above its EMA and its EMA is greater than its value 3 MTF bars earlier, and the higher-than-chart HTF last completed bar closes above its EMA and its EMA is greater than its value 3 HTF bars earlier, and this bullish cascade was false on the prior chart bar, and cascade score ≥ configured threshold, and chart bars since the last signal ≥ configured cooldown.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an LTF close crosses below its EMA and LTF RSI < 50 within the configured window bars, and the higher-than-chart MTF last completed bar closes below its EMA and its EMA is less than its value 3 MTF bars earlier, and the higher-than-chart HTF last completed bar closes below its EMA and its EMA is less than its value 3 HTF bars earlier, and this bearish cascade was false on the prior chart bar, and cascade score ≥ configured threshold, and chart bars since the last signal ≥ configured cooldown.
