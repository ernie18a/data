Calculation: Dual-WMA Momentum Oscillator = (shorter-lookback WMA of OHLC4 − longer-lookback WMA of OHLC4) / longer-lookback WMA of OHLC4 × 100, calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar
Calculation: Momentum histogram = Dual-WMA Momentum Oscillator − EMA of the Dual-WMA Momentum Oscillator, calculated on the same higher timeframe using its still-forming bar
Long: Dual-WMA Momentum Oscillator > 0
Long: Momentum histogram >= 0
Short: Dual-WMA Momentum Oscillator < 0
Short: Momentum histogram < 0
