Calculation: Active pivot high = the latest confirmed pivot high; a pivot is a bar whose high is at least as high as every high in an equal-length preceding window and strictly higher than every high in the following window. Confirm it after the following window completes. Replace the active pivot when a new one is confirmed; mark it unbroken until its first upward break.
Calculation: Active pivot low = the latest confirmed pivot low; a pivot is a bar whose low is at most as low as every low in an equal-length preceding window and strictly lower than every low in the following window. Confirm it after the following window completes. Replace the active pivot when a new one is confirmed; mark it unbroken until its first downward break. Use the same window length as for pivot highs.
Long: Active pivot high is unbroken AND close > active pivot high, or, when wick breaks are selected, high > active pivot high.
Short: Active pivot low is unbroken AND close < active pivot low, or, when wick breaks are selected, low < active pivot low.
