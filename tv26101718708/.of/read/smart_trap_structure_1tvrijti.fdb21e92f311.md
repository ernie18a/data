Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar low lower than the lows of the configured equal number of bars before and after it; use after the bars after it are complete.
Calculation: Upward structure break = the current close is greater than the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high when close-based breaks are enabled, otherwise the current high is greater than it.
Calculation: Downward structure break = the current close is lower than the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low when close-based breaks are enabled, otherwise the current low is lower than it.
