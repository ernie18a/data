Calculation: Price sources = either high and low, or respectively the greater and lesser of open and close.
Calculation: ZigZag pivots = track the running high or low using the price sources. Confirm an extreme when a later bar's opposite extreme reverses by at least a threshold equal to either a percentage of the running extreme or a multiple of ATR. An extreme and its confirming counter-extreme cannot originate on the same bar. After confirmation, alternate directions and start the new running extreme at the counter-extreme. Initially track both extremes; confirm whichever reversal qualifies, choosing the larger price move if both qualify.
Calculation: Impulse = after two confirmed pivots, initialize direction from the first to the second, origin at the first and extreme at the second. On a confirmed pivot in its direction, extend the extreme only if the pivot exceeds it. On an opposite pivot, reverse direction and set the old extreme as origin and the pivot as extreme if its absolute distance from the extreme divided by the origin-to-extreme distance reaches the degree's retracement threshold; otherwise retain the impulse. Independently, if the running ZigZag leg opposes the impulse and passes its origin, reverse immediately, using the old extreme as origin and the running extreme as the new extreme. While the running leg agrees, extend the impulse extreme whenever it exceeds the existing extreme. The internal degree can also reverse when an opposite confirmed pivot breaks the preceding retained pullback; the external degree uses retracement alone and has a higher retracement threshold.
Calculation: External retracement = absolute distance from the external impulse extreme to the most adverse price source since that extreme, divided by the absolute distance from its origin to its extreme. Reset the adverse price to the extreme when the extreme occurs on the current bar; otherwise initialize it to the more adverse of the current price source and the running ZigZag extreme, then update it with each bar's adverse price source.
Calculation: Sweep = watch a limited, oldest-first-evicted list of confirmed ZigZag pivots. A high pivot is pierced when high exceeds it; a low pivot is pierced when low falls below it. From the first piercing bar through the permitted close-back window, the first close strictly back inside confirms a sweep; if no close back occurs by the window limit, the level is broken. Record the most extreme high or low and the corresponding maximum or minimum chart-timeframe MACD line across the piercing-to-confirmation bars. If several sweeps confirm on one bar, the newest watched level determines the counter-trend setup.
Calculation: Counter-trend setup = the latest qualifying sweep arms the opposite direction through a trigger window, including the sweep bar. Optionally require its recorded MACD extreme to be lower than the MACD at the swept high pivot, or higher than the MACD at the swept low pivot. Expire the setup when its window is exceeded or price strictly exceeds its recorded sweep extreme. Replace it on a new qualifying sweep and consume it after a same-direction signal.
Calculation: Trend pullback setup = external retracement is at least the shallower pullback threshold and below the deeper threshold, and the adverse price source reached the shallow band edge within a trigger window. The edge is the external extreme minus direction times the shallower threshold times the absolute origin-to-extreme distance. Reset the last touch on a new external impulse. A signal in the external impulse's direction consumes its current extreme bar; a changed extreme permits another signal. The thresholds can be set independently or taken from the internal and external impulse thresholds.
Calculation: Slow MACD = MACD on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, or on the chart timeframe using the current completed bar. Its bullish filter is selectable as line above zero, line above signal, or both; its bearish filter uses the corresponding strict less-than comparisons. Chart-timeframe MACD uses the current completed bar.
Long: A confirmed ZigZag low is greater than or equal to the previous confirmed ZigZag low.
Short: A confirmed ZigZag high is less than or equal to the previous confirmed ZigZag high.
Long: A confirmed ZigZag high is greater than the previous confirmed ZigZag high.
Short: A confirmed ZigZag low is less than the previous confirmed ZigZag low.
Long: Internal impulse direction equals up.
Short: Internal impulse direction equals down.
Long: A low pivot sweep is confirmed.
Short: A high pivot sweep is confirmed.
Long: External impulse direction equals up AND the trend pullback setup is active AND the current external extreme has not been consumed AND the slow MACD bullish filter holds AND the chart-timeframe MACD line is above its signal.
Short: External impulse direction equals down AND the trend pullback setup is active AND the current external extreme has not been consumed AND the slow MACD bearish filter holds AND the chart-timeframe MACD line is below its signal.
Long: A low-sweep counter-trend setup is active AND close is above the swept level AND the chart-timeframe MACD line is above its signal.
Short: A high-sweep counter-trend setup is active AND close is below the swept level AND the chart-timeframe MACD line is below its signal.
