Calculation: Volume imbalance = (rolling volume of bars closing above the rolling volume-weighted average close minus rolling volume of bars closing below it) / their combined volume.
Calculation: Normalized directional score = count of qualifying factors divided by the maximum raw score; long factors are short-lookback SMA(close) > long-lookback SMA(close), positive volume imbalance above its threshold, RSI above its threshold, and ATR above its smoothed threshold; short factors reverse the SMA and imbalance comparisons, use RSI below its threshold, and share the ATR factor.
Calculation: Normalized momentum = min(1, absolute difference between short- and long-lookback EMA(close) divided by the rolling high-percentile value of that absolute difference).
Long: Flat position AND within configured trading session AND ATR is not below its rolling low-percentile threshold AND normalized long score >= configured confidence threshold AND normalized momentum > configured momentum threshold AND short-lookback EMA(close) has not crossed below long-lookback EMA(close) AND RSI <= configured long ceiling AND ATR is not above its 200-bar mean plus three standard deviations.
Short: Flat position AND within configured trading session AND ATR is not below its rolling low-percentile threshold AND normalized short score >= configured confidence threshold AND normalized momentum > configured momentum threshold AND short-lookback EMA(close) has not crossed above long-lookback EMA(close) AND RSI >= configured short floor AND ATR is not above its 200-bar mean plus three standard deviations.
Exit: Long, price <= entry-signal bar close minus ATR-based stop distance OR price reaches the long trailing stop (trail distance is ATR-based and the trail is activated from the entry-signal bar close) OR price >= average entry price plus an ATR-based scale-out distance OR short-lookback EMA(close) crosses below long-lookback EMA(close) OR ATR exceeds its 200-bar mean plus three standard deviations OR time is 15:50 or later and before 17:00.
Exit: Short, price >= entry-signal bar close plus ATR-based stop distance OR price reaches the short trailing stop (trail distance is ATR-based and the trail is activated from the entry-signal bar close) OR price <= average entry price minus an ATR-based scale-out distance OR short-lookback EMA(close) crosses above long-lookback EMA(close) OR ATR exceeds its 200-bar mean plus three standard deviations OR time is 15:50 or later and before 17:00.
