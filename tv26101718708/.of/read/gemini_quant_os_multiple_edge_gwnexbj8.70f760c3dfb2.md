Calculation: Breakout high = highest high of the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Breakout low = lowest low of the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a higher-timeframe bar still forming
Long: Close crosses above breakout high AND shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI is within the inclusive lower and upper bounds AND volume > its moving average × volume multiplier
Short: Close crosses below breakout low AND shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI is within the inclusive lower and upper bounds AND volume > its moving average × volume multiplier
