Calculation: Bullish OB = On each configured OB timeframe (which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe), using completed source bars with lookahead off, when the 4-bar percentage change in open crosses above the positive sensitivity threshold and more than five source bars have passed since the prior accepted bullish cross, take the high and low of the nearest red candle 4–15 bars back as the zone; reject it if, from that candle through the current source bar, the selected mitigation price (close or wick) fell below the candle low. The zone remains active until the selected chart-timeframe mitigation price (prior close or current low) falls below its top minus the configured penetration fraction of its height.
Calculation: Bearish OB = On each configured OB timeframe (which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe), using completed source bars with lookahead off, when the 4-bar percentage change in open crosses below the negative sensitivity threshold and more than five source bars have passed since the prior accepted bearish cross, take the high and low of the nearest green candle 4–15 bars back as the zone; reject it if, from that candle through the current source bar, the selected mitigation price (close or wick) rose above the candle high. The zone remains active until the selected chart-timeframe mitigation price (prior close or current high) rises above its bottom plus the configured penetration fraction of its height.
Long: Current low < top of any active bullish OB
Short: Current high > bottom of any active bearish OB
