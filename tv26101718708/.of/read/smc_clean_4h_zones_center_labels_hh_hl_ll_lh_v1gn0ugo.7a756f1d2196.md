Calculation: ATR = Average True Range
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) among the N bars on each side; it is only known N bars after it occurs. LastPivotHigh and LastPivotLow = the price of the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low
Calculation: BullBreak = close > LastPivotHigh AND previous close <= LastPivotHigh. BearBreak = close < LastPivotLow AND previous close >= LastPivotLow
Calculation: Trend = +1 after the latest BullBreak, -1 after the latest BearBreak, 0 initially, taken as of before the current bar. BullCHOCH = BullBreak AND Trend = -1. BearCHOCH = BearBreak AND Trend = +1
Calculation: BullFVG zone = created when low > high of 2 bars ago AND (low - high 2 bars ago) >= a fixed fraction of ATR; top = low, bottom = high 2 bars ago, mid = (top+bottom)/2; a newer one replaces the older; the zone is active from creation until a close < its bottom. BearFVG zone = created when high < low of 2 bars ago AND (low 2 bars ago - high) >= the same fraction of ATR; top = low 2 bars ago, bottom = high; mid = (top+bottom)/2; a newer one replaces the older; the zone is active from creation until a close > its top
Calculation: BullOB zone = on a BullBreak bar, take the nearest earlier bearish candle (close < open) within a fixed lookback; zone = that candle's high (top) to low (bottom); a newer one replaces the older; no zone if none is found; active from creation until a close < its bottom. BearOB zone = on a BearBreak bar, take the nearest earlier bullish candle (close > open) within the same lookback; zone = that candle's high (top) to low (bottom); a newer one replaces the older; no zone if none is found; active from creation until a close > its top
Calculation: Demand zone = on the first chart bar of each new higher-timeframe bar, if the last completed higher-timeframe bar confirms a pivot low (N higher-timeframe bars on each side, same N as above), then take the higher-timeframe bar N bars before that last completed bar: top = max(its open, its close), bottom = its low; it replaces the previous Demand zone and persists until replaced. Supply zone = same timing, with a pivot high on the higher timeframe: top = that bar's high, bottom = min(its open, its close); it replaces the previous Supply zone and persists until replaced
Long: (on a confirmed bar) [active BullFVG AND low <= BullFVG mid AND close > BullFVG mid] OR [active BullOB AND low <= BullOB top AND close > BullOB bottom] OR [Demand zone exists AND low <= Demand top AND close > Demand bottom] OR BullCHOCH OR [active BearFVG AND close > BearFVG top]
Short: (on a confirmed bar) [active BearFVG AND high >= BearFVG mid AND close < BearFVG mid] OR [active BearOB AND high >= BearOB bottom AND close < BearOB top] OR [Supply zone exists AND high >= Supply bottom AND close < Supply top] OR BearCHOCH OR [active BullFVG AND close < BullFVG bottom]
Exit: long, Short condition is true OR low <= entry price - ATR at entry bar * stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry bar * target multiplier (target multiplier > stop multiplier); entry price = close of the signal bar
Exit: short, Long condition is true OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry bar * stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR at entry bar * target multiplier (target multiplier > stop multiplier); entry price = close of the signal bar
