Calculation: OTE zone = after price sweeps the prior higher-timeframe candle’s high without sweeping its low (long), or its low without sweeping its high (short), within the opening sweep window, use that prior candle’s range to set the retracement zone from 50% to the configured deeper retracement level; use the still-forming signal-timeframe candle for sweep detection and the last completed candle for its high, low, and open
Calculation: Swing high = a completed higher-timeframe candle’s high is strictly above the highs of the immediately preceding and following candles; Swing low = its low is strictly below the lows of the immediately preceding and following candles. Evaluate the four most recent such levels, from candles offset 2 through 5
Calculation: Long stop = prior candle low, or below the deep OTE boundary by the configured range buffer; short stop = prior candle high, or above the deep OTE boundary by the configured range buffer
Calculation: Long target = prior candle high, nearest available swing high above the sweep, or entry price plus configured risk-reward multiple of entry-to-stop risk; short target = prior candle low, nearest available swing low below the sweep, or entry price minus configured risk-reward multiple of stop-to-entry risk
Long: (within sweep window, current high > prior higher-timeframe candle high, and current low >= prior higher-timeframe candle low; then low <= upper OTE boundary and close >= deep OTE boundary for the first consecutive bar) AND within configured session hours AND prior candle range <= configured maximum when range filter enabled AND no evaluated swing high above the prior high was exceeded when swing filter enabled AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (DR filter disabled OR NY DR bias is not confirmed and active OR confirmed NY DR bias is bullish) AND (pre-gate blocking disabled OR current time is past the configured gate)
Short: (within sweep window, current low < prior higher-timeframe candle low, and current high <= prior higher-timeframe candle high; then high >= lower OTE boundary and close <= upper OTE boundary for the first consecutive bar) AND within configured session hours AND prior candle range <= configured maximum when range filter enabled AND no evaluated swing low below the prior low was exceeded when swing filter enabled AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (DR filter disabled OR NY DR bias is not confirmed and active OR confirmed NY DR bias is bearish) AND (pre-gate blocking disabled OR current time is past the configured gate)
Long: during the NY DR extension window, the configured number of consecutive closes are above the completed NY DR high; OR on any monitored timeframe, the still-forming bar breaks the last completed bar’s high but not its low, or breaks both and closes at or above its open
Short: during the NY DR extension window, the configured number of consecutive closes are below the completed NY DR low; OR on any monitored timeframe, the still-forming bar breaks the last completed bar’s low but not its high, or breaks both and closes below its open
Exit: long, high >= target OR low <= stop; if both occur on the same bar, target takes precedence
Exit: short, low <= target OR high >= stop; if both occur on the same bar, target takes precedence
