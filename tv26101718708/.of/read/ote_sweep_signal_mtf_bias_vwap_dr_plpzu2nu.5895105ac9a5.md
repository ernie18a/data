Calculation: Sweep setup = On each new higher-timeframe signal bar, reset the setup and signal-used flag. During a configurable window measured from that bar's open, a chart bar whose high exceeds the last completed signal bar's high and whose low is at or above its low locks a long setup; a chart bar whose low is below that low and whose high is at or below that high locks a short setup. An opposite sweep replaces the locked setup until a signal is used; another sweep in the already locked direction does not update it. At the locking bar, retain the prior signal bar's high and low, the forming signal bar's open, the sweep bar's high or low, and the filter results. A signal is used when an in-session setup first enters its zone, even if another filter blocks it.
Calculation: Sweep zones = For the locked prior high H, low L and range R = H - L, the long zone runs from H - R × deep retracement fraction to H - R / 2; the short zone runs from L + R / 2 to L + R × deep retracement fraction. The deep retracement fraction exceeds the midpoint fraction.
Calculation: Signal-timeframe swing levels = Among a configurable set of recent completed higher-timeframe signal bars before the immediately preceding bar, retain a high strictly greater than both adjacent bars' highs or a low strictly lower than both adjacent bars' lows. Evaluate these levels at the sweep bar. A long swing breach exists if a swing high is strictly between the prior high and the sweep bar high; a short swing breach exists if a swing low is strictly between the sweep bar low and the prior low.
Calculation: Dealing range = During a configurable New York build session, accumulate the high and low and freeze them when the session ends. On chart timeframes below a configurable higher timeframe, use that higher timeframe's still-forming bar high and low; otherwise use chart bars. Reset the frozen range and daily bias on each new trading day. During the subsequent configurable extension session, count consecutive chart closes strictly above the frozen high or strictly below the frozen low, resetting the opposing count and resetting both counts on an inside close. The first count to reach the confirmation threshold locks the corresponding daily bias until the next trading day. The optional time gate controls when the locked bias filters entries, not when confirmation is counted.
Calculation: Trade levels = At an OTE entry, retain the locked setup's levels. The long entry reference is the long zone's upper edge and the short entry reference is the short zone's lower edge. Select the long stop as the prior low or the long zone's lower edge minus a configurable fraction of R; select the short stop as the prior high or the short zone's upper edge plus that fraction of R. Select the long target as the prior high, the nearest retained swing high above the sweep bar high, or entry reference plus a configurable multiple of entry reference minus stop; select the short target as the prior low, the nearest retained swing low below the sweep bar low, or entry reference minus that multiple of stop minus entry reference. If the selected swing target does not exist, use the prior high or low respectively.
Long: A long sweep setup is active AND the current bar's low <= the long zone upper edge AND its close >= the long zone lower edge AND this combined zone condition was false on the preceding chart bar AND the optional session filter passes AND the optional maximum-range and swing-breach filters passed at the sweep AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (daily-bias filter disabled OR confirmed bias is not yet active under the optional time gate OR daily bias is long) AND (block-before-gate disabled OR the optional time gate has passed).
Short: A short sweep setup is active AND the current bar's high >= the short zone lower edge AND its close <= the short zone upper edge AND this combined zone condition was false on the preceding chart bar AND the optional session filter passes AND the optional maximum-range and swing-breach filters passed at the sweep AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (daily-bias filter disabled OR confirmed bias is not yet active under the optional time gate OR daily bias is short) AND (block-before-gate disabled OR the optional time gate has passed).
Long: During the dealing-range extension session, the daily bias is not yet confirmed AND the required number of consecutive chart closes exceeds the frozen dealing-range high.
Short: During the dealing-range extension session, the daily bias is not yet confirmed AND the required number of consecutive chart closes is below the frozen dealing-range low.
Long: For each configured bias timeframe, the current bar's high > the preceding completed bar's high AND (its low >= the preceding low OR its close >= its open). Use the still-forming bar for a higher timeframe and the first completed intrabar for a lower timeframe.
Short: For each configured bias timeframe, the current bar's low < the preceding completed bar's low AND (its high <= the preceding high OR its close < its open). Use the still-forming bar for a higher timeframe and the first completed intrabar for a lower timeframe.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= retained long target OR low <= retained long stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= retained short target OR high >= retained short stop.
