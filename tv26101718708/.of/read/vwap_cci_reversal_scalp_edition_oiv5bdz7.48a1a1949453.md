Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume since the start of the anchor period; use typical price when cumulative volume is zero. Use a shorter anchor period for the bands and a longer anchor period for the optional bias filter.
Calculation: Anchored deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative sum of typical price squared × volume divided by cumulative volume − anchored VWAP squared); use zero when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Bands = short-anchor VWAP ± multiplier × anchored deviation; use an inner, a middle, and an outer multiplier in increasing order.
Calculation: Smoothed CCI = EMA of CCI calculated from a configurable price source, defaulting to (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Recent touch = on the chart timeframe, or optionally on the last completed lower-timeframe bar when the chart timeframe is higher, at least one bar from the current bar through the configured lookback bars ago has its close between the low minus a configurable price tolerance and the high plus that tolerance of the bar a configured shift earlier; optionally replace the close with the range between the current and previous closes and require the two ranges to overlap.
Long: Smoothed CCI crosses above a negative threshold AND ((wide-zone mode is disabled AND low <= lower middle band + buffer × anchored deviation AND low >= lower outer band − buffer × anchored deviation) OR (wide-zone mode is enabled AND close < lower inner band)) AND (weekly-bias filter is disabled OR close > longer-anchor VWAP) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × multiplier) AND (candle filter is disabled OR close > open) AND (extreme filter is disabled OR lowest smoothed CCI over the configured lookback < a more negative threshold than the crossover threshold) AND (touch filter is disabled OR recent touch is true).
Short: Smoothed CCI crosses below a positive threshold AND ((wide-zone mode is disabled AND high >= upper middle band − buffer × anchored deviation AND high <= upper outer band + buffer × anchored deviation) OR (wide-zone mode is enabled AND close > upper inner band)) AND (weekly-bias filter is disabled OR close < longer-anchor VWAP) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × multiplier) AND (candle filter is disabled OR close < open) AND (extreme filter is disabled OR highest smoothed CCI over the configured lookback > a more positive threshold than the crossover threshold) AND (touch filter is disabled OR recent touch is true).
Exit: long, low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × profit-target multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × profit-target multiplier.
