Calculation: Confirmed pivots = A high pivot is higher than every high in its preceding left window and at least as high as every high in its following right window; a low pivot is lower than every low in its preceding left window and at most as low as every low in its following right window. Confirm each pivot after its right window completes. Process simultaneous high and low confirmations in that order. Maintain an alternating sequence: append an opposite-side pivot; replace the latest same-side pivot only if the new one is more extreme. Let A, B, C be the third-latest, second-latest and latest pivots, and A2 the fifth-latest pivot.
Calculation: Swing = B − A; retracement = (B − C) / swing. Primary retracement levels are B − shallow ratio × swing and B − deep ratio × swing. Secondary retracement levels use B − A2 in place of swing. The deep ratio exceeds the shallow ratio.
Calculation: Confluence = A2 exists AND B − A2 has the same sign as swing AND the smallest absolute difference between any primary retracement level and any secondary retracement level is at most a tolerance multiple of current ATR.
Calculation: Tracked levels = At each entry signal, fix the stop at C minus a buffer multiple of signal-bar ATR for a long, or C plus that buffer for a short; fix the three objectives at C plus swing multiplied by a first, second or third objective multiplier, respectively, in increasing order. Replace these levels and reset their hit flags on every new signal. Evaluate their hits starting on the next bar; a stop hit takes priority over all objectives on the same bar. A stop or third-objective hit closes the tracked setup; the first two objectives are recorded only once each.
Long: A, B, C are low, high, low AND swing > 0 AND retracement >= shallow ratio AND retracement <= maximum retracement AND absolute swing >= minimum swing multiple × ATR AND (displaced SMA filter disabled OR close > SMA of close displaced backward by the configured number of bars) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close > selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA or VWMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close unavailable OR close > last completed higher-timeframe close) AND (confluence filter disabled OR confluence exists) AND C's pivot bar differs from that of the last fired signal AND the chart bar is closed AND (signal lock disabled OR this is not the last chart bar).
Short: A, B, C are high, low, high AND swing < 0 AND retracement >= shallow ratio AND retracement <= maximum retracement AND absolute swing >= minimum swing multiple × ATR AND (displaced SMA filter disabled OR close < SMA of close displaced backward by the configured number of bars) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close < selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA or VWMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close unavailable OR close < last completed higher-timeframe close) AND (confluence filter disabled OR confluence exists) AND C's pivot bar differs from that of the last fired signal AND the chart bar is closed AND (signal lock disabled OR this is not the last chart bar).
Long: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close available AND close > last completed higher-timeframe close.
Short: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close available AND close <= last completed higher-timeframe close.
Exit: long, tracked setup open AND low <= tracked stop.
Exit: short, tracked setup open AND high >= tracked stop.
Exit: long, tracked stop not hit AND low > tracked stop AND high >= a tracked objective AND that objective has not previously been hit.
Exit: short, tracked stop not hit AND high < tracked stop AND low <= a tracked objective AND that objective has not previously been hit.
Exit: long, tracked setup open after evaluating the current bar's stop and objectives AND a short signal fires.
Exit: short, tracked setup open after evaluating the current bar's stop and objectives AND a long signal fires.
