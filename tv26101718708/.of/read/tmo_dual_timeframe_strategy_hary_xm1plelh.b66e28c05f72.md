Calculation: TMO(tf) = on bars of timeframe tf, s = sum over k=0..N of sign(close - open[k]) (current close vs the opens of the current and previous N bars; sign is +1, -1 or 0); TMO = moving average of (moving average of s). Value is read at each chart bar from the bar of tf still forming (not only last completed), and the previous value is the same series at the previous chart bar.
Long: TMO(higher timeframe) > 0 AND TMO(entry timeframe, lower than the higher timeframe) crosses above 0 (previous value <= 0 and current value > 0)
Short: TMO(higher timeframe) < 0 AND TMO(entry timeframe, lower than the higher timeframe) crosses below 0 (previous value >= 0 and current value < 0)
Exit: long, TMO(higher timeframe) < 0 AND TMO(entry timeframe) crosses below 0 (the Short condition) OR low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance (distances are a percent of entry price, or a fixed price distance)
Exit: short, TMO(higher timeframe) > 0 AND TMO(entry timeframe) crosses above 0 (the Long condition) OR high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance (distances are a percent of entry price, or a fixed price distance)
