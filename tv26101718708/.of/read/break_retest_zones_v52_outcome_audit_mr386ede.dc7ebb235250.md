Calculation: Zone = On confirmation of a swing low or swing high, create a zone from the pivot bar's low to the lesser of its high and low plus an ATR multiple, or from the greater of its low and high minus an ATR multiple to its high, respectively. Use ATR on the confirmation bar. Reject a new zone that overlaps any existing zone. Limit zones separately by pivot type; when full, remove the oldest live zone of that type, or the oldest zone of that type if none is live. Initialize its touch count to one. Process swing lows before swing highs.
Calculation: Zone state = A new zone is live unless its creation-bar close is outside it, in which case it is armed in the direction of that close. A live zone becomes armed long when close crosses above its top, or armed short when close crosses below its bottom. Count a further touch while live when the bar overlaps the zone, closes outside it, and a minimum separation from the previous counted touch has elapsed. An armed zone fails when close crosses its opposite edge; revive it after the failure cooldown. Return it to live when its retest window expires or, if enabled, close moves away beyond an ATR multiple. Remove it after a confirmed entry. Apply failure, expiry, and runaway checks before entry; process zones newest first.
Calculation: Breakout record = At arming, save breakout volume divided by its volume SMA, using one if the SMA is not positive; save whether the breakout body is at least an ATR multiple, whether a directional sweep occurred within the sweep lookback age, and whether a directional fair value gap occurred. A long sweep is low below the lowest low of the preceding lookback and close above that low; a short sweep reverses these comparisons using the highest high. A bullish fair value gap is low above the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap is high below the low two bars earlier. While armed, accumulate volume and directional fair value gaps only on bars that do not fail, expire, run away, or satisfy the basic retest condition.
Calculation: Confluence score = Add two points if a previous completed higher-timeframe high or low from either of two higher timeframes lies within an ATR tolerance of the zone. Add one point each if breakout volume ratio meets its threshold; average accumulated pullback volume divided by the current volume SMA is below the breakout volume ratio; an enabled directional sweep was recorded; an enabled displacement was recorded; or an enabled directional fair value gap was recorded. If there is no accumulated pullback volume or the current volume SMA is not positive, use the breakout volume ratio as the pullback ratio. Compare the score with the selected grade threshold.
Calculation: Stop and risk = For a long entry, stop is the zone bottom minus a configurable price offset; for a short entry, stop is the zone top plus that offset. Risk is the absolute difference between entry price and stop. Entry price is the retest bar's close.
Long: A newly created zone has close above its top OR a live zone has close above its top AND previous close at or below its top.
Short: A newly created zone has close below its bottom OR a live zone has close below its bottom AND previous close at or above its bottom.
Long: Zone is armed long AND bars since arming exceed the minimum gap AND do not exceed the retest window AND close is at or above the zone bottom AND the enabled runaway limit is not exceeded AND low is at or below the zone top AND close is above the zone top AND touch count meets its minimum AND the enabled session filter passes AND confluence score meets the selected threshold AND the enabled room filter passes: the previous completed higher-timeframe high is unavailable, at or below close, or at least a risk multiple above close.
Short: Zone is armed short AND bars since arming exceed the minimum gap AND do not exceed the retest window AND close is at or below the zone top AND the enabled runaway limit is not exceeded AND high is at or above the zone bottom AND close is below the zone bottom AND touch count meets its minimum AND the enabled session filter passes AND confluence score meets the selected threshold AND the enabled room filter passes: the previous completed higher-timeframe low is unavailable, at or above close, or at least a risk multiple below close.
Long: Previous completed higher-timeframe close is above its open.
Short: Previous completed higher-timeframe close is below its open.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low is at or below the entry's stop OR high is at or above entry price plus a target multiple of entry risk; stop takes precedence if both occur.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high is at or above the entry's stop OR low is at or below entry price minus a target multiple of entry risk; stop takes precedence if both occur.
