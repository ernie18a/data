Calculation: Liquidity grid = Over the lookback bars, set the lower bound to the minimum of low − ATR and the upper bound to the maximum of high + ATR; divide the range into 26 equal steps. Level i = lower bound + i × step, for i = 0…25.
Calculation: Level volume = Sum volume over the current and lookback previous bars where the bar’s (high + low) / 2 is within half a step of the level.
Calculation: Trend midline = EMA of SMA(close, N), using the same lookback N for both averages.
Long: For any grid level i = 1…25, close > level AND level volume > configured minimum percentage of the maximum level volume AND the level is not the POC when POC display is enabled AND abs(hl2 − level) > 2 × step AND abs(close − level) > 2 × step AND abs(hlc3 − level) > 2 × step AND abs(high − level) > 2 × step AND abs(low − level) > 2 × step AND abs(open − level) > 2 × step AND (trend line display is disabled OR abs(trend midline − level) > step).
Short: For any grid level i = 1…25, close <= level AND level volume > configured minimum percentage of the maximum level volume AND the level is not the POC when POC display is enabled AND abs(hl2 − level) > 2 × step AND abs(close − level) > 2 × step AND abs(hlc3 − level) > 2 × step AND abs(high − level) > 2 × step AND abs(low − level) > 2 × step AND abs(open − level) > 2 × step AND (trend line display is disabled OR abs(trend midline − level) > step).
