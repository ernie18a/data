Calculation: Velocity = (close − close from the velocity lookback bars ago) / ATR; when ATR normalization is disabled, use the unnormalized price change
Calculation: Velocity z-score = (Velocity − its moving average over the z-score window) / its standard deviation over that window; use zero when the standard deviation is zero
Calculation: Dynamic threshold = in classic mode, the fixed z-score threshold; otherwise, the fixed threshold when adaptation is disabled, or the greater of a fraction of the fixed threshold and the selected percentile of absolute z-scores over a longer window
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / its moving average over the volume window; use 1 when that average is zero
Calculation: Acceleration = current Velocity − previous-bar Velocity
Calculation: Stretch = ATR distance between close and the baseline EMA
Calculation: Lower-wick ratio = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low); use zero when the range is zero
Calculation: Upper-wick ratio = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low); use zero when the range is zero
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 when the range is zero
Long: (classic mode AND Velocity z-score ≤ −fixed threshold AND Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold AND Acceleration > 0 AND previous-bar Acceleration ≤ 0) OR (nonclassic mode AND (minimum Velocity z-score over the short flip window ≤ −Dynamic threshold AND (Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold OR Volume ratio ≤ volume dry-up threshold) AND Acceleration > 0 AND previous-bar Acceleration ≤ 0 OR Velocity z-score ≤ −Dynamic threshold times hard-climax multiplier AND Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold OR minimum Velocity z-score over the short flip window ≤ −Dynamic threshold AND Lower-wick ratio ≥ wick threshold AND Close location ≥ 0.5 OR low ≤ the lowest low over the divergence lookback AND Velocity z-score > the lowest Velocity z-score over that lookback ending on the previous bar AND Velocity z-score ≤ −fraction of Dynamic threshold AND close > open) AND (stretch filter disabled OR baseline EMA − close ≥ stretch threshold times ATR)) AND bars since the previous long signal > cooldown
Short: (classic mode AND Velocity z-score ≥ fixed threshold AND Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold AND Acceleration < 0 AND previous-bar Acceleration ≥ 0) OR (nonclassic mode AND (maximum Velocity z-score over the short flip window ≥ Dynamic threshold AND (Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold OR Volume ratio ≤ volume dry-up threshold) AND Acceleration < 0 AND previous-bar Acceleration ≥ 0 OR Velocity z-score ≥ Dynamic threshold times hard-climax multiplier AND Volume ratio ≥ volume-surge threshold OR maximum Velocity z-score over the short flip window ≥ Dynamic threshold AND Upper-wick ratio ≥ wick threshold AND Close location ≤ 0.5 OR high ≥ the highest high over the divergence lookback AND Velocity z-score < the highest Velocity z-score over that lookback ending on the previous bar AND Velocity z-score ≥ fraction of Dynamic threshold AND close < open) AND (stretch filter disabled OR close − baseline EMA ≥ stretch threshold times ATR)) AND bars since the previous short signal > cooldown
