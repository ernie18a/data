Calculation: Regime = Initially range. On each confirmed bar, once the bar index reaches the training-window length plus the feature lookback, train immediately if no centroids exist and thereafter at the specified retraining interval. Form four features: absolute difference between a short EMA and a long EMA divided by ATR; close standard deviation divided by max(close, a small positive floor), scaled by a constant; the feature-lookback highest high minus lowest low divided by ATR; and the absolute change in the short EMA over the feature lookback divided by ATR times that lookback. Floor each ATR denominator at a small positive value. Min-max normalize each feature over its own trailing training window, using one-half when its range is zero. Initialize centroids from evenly spaced offsets across the training window, including the current bar and capped at the oldest bar in the window; replace unavailable normalized values with one-half. For a specified number of iterations, assign each historical normalized vector to its nearest centroid by Euclidean distance, breaking ties in favor of the first centroid, and replace each nonempty centroid with the mean of its assigned vectors; leave empty centroids unchanged. Classify each centroid as trend if its first plus fourth coordinates are at least both its second coordinate and one minus its third minus its first; otherwise classify it as volatile if its second coordinate is at least one minus its third minus its first; otherwise classify it as range. On each confirmed bar with trained centroids, use the classification of the nearest centroid, breaking ties in favor of the first.
Calculation: Adaptive channel = For the range regime, midpoint is the average of the highest high and lowest low over the range-channel lookback, and half-width is the greater of a fraction of half that high-low range and an ATR multiple. For the trend regime, midpoint is the current endpoint of the close linear regression over the trend-channel lookback, and half-width is the greater of the root mean square of the regression residuals and a separate ATR multiple. For the volatile regime, midpoint is the close EMA over the volatile-channel lookback, and half-width is a separate ATR multiple. Upper and lower boundaries are midpoint plus and minus half-width, respectively. Each regime has its own channel lookback and width parameter.
Calculation: Channel position = (close minus lower boundary) divided by (upper boundary minus lower boundary), or one-half if either boundary is unavailable or the channel width is nonpositive.
Long: Close exceeds the upper boundary plus a breakout ATR multiple for exactly the specified number of consecutive bars, with the condition false on the preceding bar.
Short: Close is below the lower boundary minus the breakout ATR multiple for exactly the specified number of consecutive bars, with the condition false on the preceding bar.
Long: Channel position exceeds an upper-position threshold.
Short: Channel position is below a lower-position threshold.
