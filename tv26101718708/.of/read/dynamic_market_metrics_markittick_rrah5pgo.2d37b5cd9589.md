Calculation: ATR = simple moving average of true range, using prior chart bars.
Calculation: AlphaTrend = if the prior-bar Money Flow Index is at least 50, the greater of the prior AlphaTrend and prior low minus ATR times a multiplier; otherwise, the lesser of the prior AlphaTrend and prior high plus ATR times a multiplier. Initialize AlphaTrend at 0.
Long: (AlphaTrend[1] > AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] <= AlphaTrend[4] OR (absolute(close[1] − close[2]) > ATR[1] × breakout sensitivity AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] <= AlphaTrend[2])) AND bars since the preceding long signal > bars since the preceding short signal; OR the same condition evaluated on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, on the chart bar one bar after that higher-timeframe period begins.
Short: (AlphaTrend[1] < AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] >= AlphaTrend[4] OR (absolute(close[1] − close[2]) > ATR[1] × breakout sensitivity AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] >= AlphaTrend[2])) AND bars since the preceding short signal > bars since the preceding long signal; OR the same condition evaluated on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, on the chart bar one bar after that higher-timeframe period begins.
Exit: Long, bars since the preceding long signal < bars since the preceding short signal AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1].
Exit: Short, bars since the preceding short signal < bars since the preceding long signal AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1].
