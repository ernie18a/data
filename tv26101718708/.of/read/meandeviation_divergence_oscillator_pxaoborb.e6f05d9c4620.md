Calculation: Mean-deviation oscillator = (price source − SMA of price source) / (deviation-scale parameter × mean absolute deviation of price source over the same lookback); use zero when mean absolute deviation is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low surrounded on each side by a parameterized equal number of chart bars with no higher high or lower low, respectively; evaluate it on the bar that completes the right-hand window. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, using oscillator values at the pivot bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe divergence state = compute the mean-deviation oscillator, high and low on a higher timeframe; forward-fill their last completed values onto chart bars, then find confirmed pivots in those forward-filled high and low series using a separate equal left and right chart-bar window. A bullish event occurs when a confirmed low pivot is below the preceding low pivot AND its oscillator value is above the preceding pivot's oscillator value. A bearish event occurs when a confirmed high pivot is above the preceding high pivot AND its oscillator value is below the preceding pivot's oscillator value. Initially both states are false; a bullish event sets the bullish state true, otherwise a bearish event sets it false; a bearish event sets the bearish state true, otherwise a bullish event sets it false. Retain each state between events.
Long: Mean-deviation oscillator >= 0.
Short: Mean-deviation oscillator < 0.
Long: A confirmed low pivot is within the maximum pivot-separation parameter of the preceding low pivot AND ((its low is lower AND its oscillator is higher) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its low is higher AND its oscillator is lower)).
Short: A confirmed high pivot is within the maximum pivot-separation parameter of the preceding high pivot AND ((its high is higher AND its oscillator is lower) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its high is lower AND its oscillator is higher)).
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish divergence state is true.
Short: Higher-timeframe bullish divergence state is false AND higher-timeframe bearish divergence state is true.
