Calculation: Pivot = a high or low confirmed by the specified numbers of bars on its left and right; evaluate pivot-bar volume and close position at the pivot, and emit decisions no earlier than confirmation. Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or a neutral midpoint when high = low. Bar move = (close − open) / open, or zero when open is not positive. Volume average = SMA of volume. Prior trend is up when the current close SMA exceeds its value a lookback ago; otherwise it is down.
Calculation: Cycle state = initially idle; maintain support, resistance, the bar of the latest phase transition, whether the cycle began at a buying climax, whether it is a markup subcycle, and whether markup and markdown are active. Process the following transitions in order on each bar, allowing a later transition to occur on the same bar as an earlier transition. Within each transition group, use the first matching branch unless stated otherwise. A phase times out when bars since its latest transition exceed the timeout parameter. A reset returns to idle and clears support, resistance and any pending climax. Track the highest close while markup is active, updating it before testing for markup failure.
Calculation: Idle transitions = if markup is active and close falls below its highest close by the larger markup-failure percentage, activate markdown and enter markdown. Otherwise, a confirmed pivot low with climax volume, sufficiently high pivot close position and down prior trend starts a selling-climax phase, sets support to the pivot low and stores a pending bullish climax. Otherwise, a confirmed pivot high with climax volume, sufficiently low pivot close position and up prior trend starts a buying-climax phase, sets resistance to the pivot high and stores a pending bearish climax; mark it as a subcycle if markup is active. Otherwise, a pivot low with down prior trend, volume above a pre-climax fraction of the climax threshold but not above the climax threshold, and pivot close position above the looser bullish threshold produces a bullish preliminary event; the corresponding pivot high, up prior trend and pivot close position below the looser bearish threshold produce a bearish preliminary event.
Calculation: Climax and reaction transitions = in selling-climax phase, a confirmed pivot high whose percentage distance above support, measured from support, reaches the minimum range sets resistance and enters reaction; confirm the pending bullish climax and produce a bullish reaction. In buying-climax phase, first raise resistance to any higher confirmed pivot high; relocate the pending bearish climax to that pivot only if its volume is climax volume and its close position meets the ordinary bearish climax threshold. A confirmed pivot low whose percentage distance below resistance, measured from resistance, reaches the minimum range sets support and enters reaction; confirm the pending bearish climax and produce a bearish reaction. A positive range smaller than the minimum resets the cycle. A climax phase also resets on timeout. On reaction confirmation clear both active trend flags and the markup peak, except when the buying-climax cycle is a markup subcycle.
Calculation: Reaction transitions = for a buying-climax cycle, a low-volume confirmed pivot high between resistance minus the wider secondary-test tolerance and resistance plus the breakout tolerance enters secondary test and produces a bearish test. Otherwise, close above that upper boundary with bar move at least the positive move threshold and volume above the strong-volume threshold enters strength and produces a bullish event; otherwise close below support by the failure tolerance enters weakness and produces a bearish event. For a selling-climax cycle, a low-volume confirmed pivot low between support minus breakout tolerance and support plus the narrower secondary-test tolerance enters secondary test and produces a bullish test. Otherwise, close above resistance with bar move at least the positive move threshold and strong volume enters strength and produces a bullish event; otherwise close below support by the failure tolerance resets the cycle. Reaction resets on timeout.
Calculation: Secondary-test transitions = if low is between support minus breakout tolerance and support, close is at least support less the recovery allowance, and close position meets the stricter bullish reversal threshold, enter spring and produce a bullish event. Otherwise, if low is below support minus breakout tolerance and close position meets the looser bullish reversal threshold, set support to low, enter spring and produce a bullish event. Otherwise, if low is below that boundary, enter weakness and produce a bearish event only for a buying-climax cycle with bar move at most the negative move threshold and strong volume; reset in all other cases. Otherwise, for a buying-climax cycle, if high is between resistance and resistance plus breakout tolerance, close is at most resistance plus the rejection allowance, and close position meets the bearish rejection threshold, enter upthrust and produce a bearish event; if high exceeds that upper boundary, reset. Secondary test resets on timeout.
Calculation: Spring and retest transitions = in spring, a low-volume confirmed pivot low at or above support less the retest allowance enters retest and produces a bullish event. Independently, in spring, close above resistance with bar move at least the positive move threshold and strong volume enters strength and produces a bullish event, even if a retest also occurred on that bar. In retest, the same breakout condition enters strength and produces a bullish event. Spring and retest reset on timeout.
Calculation: Strength and support transitions = in strength, a low-volume confirmed pivot low above support enters last support and produces a bullish event; otherwise close below support by the failure tolerance resets. In last support, close above resistance enters markup, activates markup and deactivates markdown, producing a bullish markup event only if markup was not already active. Otherwise, a low-volume confirmed pivot low above support raises support to that pivot, refreshes the phase timer and produces a bullish event; otherwise close below support by the failure tolerance resets. These phases reset on timeout.
Calculation: Markup transitions = if close falls below the highest close during active markup by the larger markup-failure percentage, deactivate markup, activate markdown, enter markdown and produce a bearish event. Otherwise, a confirmed pivot high with climax volume, pivot close position below the stricter subcycle bearish threshold, and pivot high within the peak-proximity allowance of the markup peak starts a buying-climax subcycle and stores a pending bearish climax. Otherwise, a low-volume confirmed pivot low above support raises support and produces a bullish event. Markup phase resets on timeout while its active flag and peak persist. The same markup-failure condition applies in idle while markup remains active.
Calculation: Weakness and supply transitions = during weakness or last supply, track the lowest close since weakness began; reset if close rises above that low by the distribution-invalidation percentage. In upthrust, close below support with bar move at most the negative move threshold and strong volume enters weakness and produces a bearish event. In weakness, a low-volume confirmed pivot high below resistance enters last supply and produces a bearish event. In last supply, each low-volume confirmed pivot high below resistance refreshes the timer and produces a bearish event; independently, close below support with strong volume enters markdown, activates markdown, deactivates markup and produces a bearish event. Each of these phases resets on timeout.
Calculation: Markdown transitions = track the lowest close since markdown began. A confirmed pivot high whose pivot bar is after markdown began and whose price exceeds that lowest close by the rally threshold produces a bullish reaction and deactivates markdown. If its pivot volume exceeds its volume average and its pivot close position meets the ordinary bearish climax threshold, start a buying-climax phase at that pivot and store a pending bearish climax; otherwise reset. Markdown resets and deactivates on timeout.
Calculation: Directional phase conditions = the selling-climax cycle through last support is bullish; the buying-climax cycle from spring through last support is bullish; upthrust through last supply is bearish. Markup is bullish while active outside a range, except that a close below its peak by the smaller distribution-shading percentage is bearish when the phase is markup or idle. Markdown is bearish while active outside a range. A bullish or bearish trend-line condition also occurs when the corresponding trend is active outside a range and the current high reaches or exceeds the highest high, or the current low reaches or falls below the lowest low, respectively, since that uninterrupted outside-range trend began. Treat a missing prior extreme as satisfied.
Long: A bullish preliminary, confirmed climax, reaction, test, spring, strength, last-support or markup event occurs OR a bullish directional phase condition holds.
Short: A bearish preliminary, confirmed climax, reaction, test, upthrust, weakness, last-supply or markdown event occurs OR a bearish directional phase condition holds.
