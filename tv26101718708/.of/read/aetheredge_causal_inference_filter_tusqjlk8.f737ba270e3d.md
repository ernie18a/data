Calculation: Normalized momentum, volatility, volume anomaly, and range expansion = respectively, EMA(close, short length) minus EMA(close, twice that length) divided by ATR(14); ATR(short length) divided by its longer-term average minus 1; volume divided by its average minus 1; and (high − low) divided by its average minus 1. For each, subtract its rolling mean, divide by its rolling standard deviation, and clip to [−3, 3].
Calculation: Driver causal lift = max(0, abs(rolling correlation of the lagged normalized driver with one-bar return) − 0.5 × abs(rolling correlation of lagged one-bar return with one-bar return)); use the same lag and rolling window for all drivers.
Calculation: Forecast = tanh output of a feedforward network with the four normalized drivers as inputs and one tanh hidden layer, trained by gradient descent to predict clipped forward return (close − close[prediction horizon]) / ATR(14), using a replay buffer of prior driver values and realized targets.
Calculation: Driver importance = absolute change in network forecast when that driver is set to zero. Causal confidence = clipped to [0, 1] average of (top driver's causal lift divided by twice the minimum lift threshold, capped at 1) and ((top driver's importance share − 0.25) / 0.5, clipped to [0, 1]); smooth this confidence with an EMA.
Long: forecast > 0.05 AND smoothed causal confidence >= confidence threshold AND bar is confirmed AND bar is within the configured training horizon AND (one-signal-per-directional-shift is disabled OR direction differs from the previous qualifying direction)
Short: forecast < −0.05 AND smoothed causal confidence >= confidence threshold AND bar is confirmed AND bar is within the configured training horizon AND (one-signal-per-directional-shift is disabled OR direction differs from the previous qualifying direction)
