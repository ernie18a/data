Calculation: Drivers = For each raw driver, subtract its rolling SMA, divide by its rolling standard deviation, and clamp to [-3, 3]; use zero when the standard deviation is zero. Raw momentum is (shorter EMA of close minus longer EMA of close) / ATR. Raw volatility is ATR / its rolling SMA minus 1. Raw volume is volume / its rolling SMA minus 1. Raw range expansion is (high - low) / its rolling SMA minus 1. Replace any zero divisor with 1.
Calculation: Forecast = A four-input, single-hidden-layer tanh neural network with a tanh output. Initialize both weight layers with the seeded pseudorandom sequence fract(sin((abs(seed % 2147483647) + 1) * 12.9898 + 78.233) * 43758.5453), incrementing the seed by 2654435761 before each weight and scaling each result as (random - 0.5) * 0.6; use separate seed-derived starting seeds for the two layers and initialize biases to zero. On each confirmed bar within the final training-horizon bars of the dataset, append the preceding bar's drivers and the current target, clamp((close - close[outcome lookback]) / ATR, -1, 1), to a finite FIFO replay buffer; replace zero ATR with 1. Perform the configured number of stochastic gradient updates before forecasting the current drivers. For update step s, select floor(random((bar index * 7919 + s * 104729 + initialization seed) % 2147483647) * buffer size) % buffer size. Backpropagate squared-error gradients with output error clamp(prediction - target, -3, 3) * (1 - prediction²), using the configured learning rate and the pre-update output weights for hidden gradients. Skip buffer insertion until preceding drivers exist. The forecast is the network output for the current drivers.
Calculation: Confidence = For each driver, compute its importance as the absolute difference between the forecast and the forecast with that driver set to zero. Select the greatest importance, breaking ties in driver order: momentum, volatility, volume, range. For that driver, lift is max(0, abs(rolling correlation of the lagged driver and one-bar return) - 0.5 * abs(rolling correlation of lagged and current one-bar returns)); replace unavailable correlations with zero. Concentration is the selected importance divided by total importance, or zero when total importance is zero. Raw confidence is clamp(0.5 * min(1, lift / max(0.01, 2 * lift threshold)) + 0.5 * min(1, (concentration - 0.25) / 0.5), 0, 1). Confidence is the EMA of raw confidence, initialized to its first value, calculated within the training horizon.
Calculation: Previous qualified direction = The direction of the most recent confirmed bar within the training horizon whose confidence was at least the confidence threshold and whose forecast was outside the symmetric forecast threshold; initially unset. Update it on every such bar, including when repeated-direction suppression is enabled.
Long: bar is confirmed AND bar is within the training horizon AND confidence >= confidence threshold AND forecast > positive forecast threshold AND (repeated-direction suppression is disabled OR previous qualified direction is unset OR previous qualified direction is short)
Short: bar is confirmed AND bar is within the training horizon AND confidence >= confidence threshold AND forecast < negative forecast threshold AND (repeated-direction suppression is disabled OR previous qualified direction is unset OR previous qualified direction is long)
Long: confidence >= confidence threshold AND forecast > positive forecast threshold
Short: confidence >= confidence threshold AND forecast < negative forecast threshold
Long: glow is enabled AND bar is within the training horizon AND confidence > 1.2 * confidence threshold AND forecast >= negative forecast threshold
