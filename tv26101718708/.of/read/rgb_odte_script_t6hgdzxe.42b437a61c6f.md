Calculation: Range distance = average body range multiplied by a buffer parameter, optionally divided by two.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price multiplied by effective volume divided by cumulative effective volume, resetting at each calendar-day boundary; typical price is (high + low + close) / 3, and effective volume is volume when positive and 1 otherwise.
