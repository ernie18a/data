Calculation: Volume delta = +volume when close > open; -volume when close < open; +0.5 × volume when close = open and close is at or above the bar midpoint; otherwise -0.5 × volume; optionally divide by the 20-bar average volume when normalization is enabled
Long: Volume delta >= 0 OR Volume delta crosses above its SMA
Short: Volume delta < 0 OR Volume delta crosses below its SMA
