Calculation: Structure series = higher-timeframe highs and lows mapped onto chart bars with gaps filled, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Find confirmed pivot highs and lows on these mapped chart-bar series using configurable left and right chart-bar counts; retain the latest confirmed value of each.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = chart high > latest pivot high × (1 + break buffer) AND the same comparison was false on the preceding chart bar. Bearish structure break = chart low < latest pivot low × (1 - break buffer) AND the same comparison was false on the preceding chart bar. Evaluate each preceding comparison using the pivot available on that preceding bar.
Calculation: MACD profiles = two MACDs calculated on a chosen price source, normally close. Each profile uses its own fast, slow, and signal lengths; the fusion profile uses shorter lengths than the primary profile. The source is either the chart-bar price or the last completed higher-timeframe price mapped onto chart bars with gaps filled; calculate the MACDs on that mapped chart-bar series. A profile is bullish when its MACD line > its signal line AND its MACD line > 0 AND its histogram > its preceding chart-bar histogram. A profile is bearish when the corresponding comparisons are <. Combined momentum is the primary profile's state AND, when fusion is enabled, the fusion profile's same-direction state.
Calculation: ATR = chart-timeframe ATR or ATR calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar and mapped onto chart bars with gaps filled. When unavailable, use chart-timeframe ATR for calculations other than the entry readiness check.
Calculation: Quality score = the mean of five components: structure component is 1 for a bullish structure break, otherwise 0 for a bearish break, otherwise 0.5; momentum component is 1 for bullish combined momentum, otherwise 0 for bearish combined momentum, otherwise 0.5; trend component is 1 when close > shorter trend EMA, 0 when close < it, otherwise 0.5; volatility component is clamp(ATR / chart-bar SMA of ATR, 0, 1.5) / 1.5 when that SMA > 0, otherwise 1; volume component is clamp((volume / volume SMA) / volume threshold, 0, 1.5) / 1.5 when the volume filter is enabled, otherwise 1. Use volume intensity 1 when the volume SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = ATR is available AND ATR ≥ minimum ATR threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold) AND (quality filter disabled OR quality score ≥ quality threshold) AND entries since the start of the current UTC day < daily entry limit AND (no previous entry OR chart bars since the last entry ≥ cooldown). Count entries when the position changes to a new long or short.
Calculation: Long setup = bullish structure break AND bullish combined momentum AND close > longer directional EMA AND close > shorter trend EMA AND entry eligibility AND (impulse filter disabled OR shorter impulse EMA > longer impulse EMA). All EMAs use the chosen chart-bar price source.
Calculation: Short setup = bearish structure break AND bearish combined momentum AND close < longer directional EMA AND close < shorter trend EMA AND entry eligibility AND (impulse filter disabled OR shorter impulse EMA ≤ longer impulse EMA) AND long-only mode disabled.
Calculation: Long trailing level = highest chart-bar high since the long position began minus current ATR × trailing distance, when highest high since entry - entry price ≥ current ATR × trailing activation; otherwise unavailable. Short trailing level = lowest chart-bar low since the short position began plus current ATR × trailing distance, when entry price - lowest low since entry ≥ current ATR × trailing activation; otherwise unavailable. Recalculate on each bar.
Long: bullish structure break
Short: bearish structure break
Long: long setup AND position side ≤ 0
Short: short setup AND position side ≥ 0
Exit: long, short setup
Exit: short, long setup
Exit: long, low ≤ max(close - current ATR × stop multiplier, long trailing level when available) OR high ≥ close + current ATR × target multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ min(close + current ATR × stop multiplier, short trailing level when available) OR low ≤ close - current ATR × target multiplier
