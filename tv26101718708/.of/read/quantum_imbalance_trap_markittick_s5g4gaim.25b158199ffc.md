Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / the long-lookback simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Delayed swing low/high = lowest low/high over the long lookback, shifted back four bars
Long: The previous completed chart bar is a bullish imbalance (close > open, body-to-range ratio ≥ its threshold, volume ≥ long-lookback volume average × its multiplier, and short-lookback close SMA > longer-lookback close SMA); the bar before it is not a bullish imbalance; AND (liquidity sweep is not required OR the minimum low of the last three completed bars is below the delayed swing low and the previous bar closes above it); AND (RSI divergence is not required OR the previous bar makes a lower low than the bar one long lookback earlier while its 14-period RSI is higher); AND (higher-timeframe alignment is not required OR the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its 20-period close SMA); AND (session filter is disabled OR the previous bar falls within a selected session). The longer momentum SMA lookback is twice the long volume-average lookback.
Short: The previous completed chart bar is a bearish imbalance (close < open, body-to-range ratio ≥ its threshold, volume ≥ long-lookback volume average × its multiplier, and short-lookback close SMA < longer-lookback close SMA); the bar before it is not a bearish imbalance; AND (liquidity sweep is not required OR the maximum high of the last three completed bars is above the delayed swing high and the previous bar closes below it); AND (RSI divergence is not required OR the previous bar makes a higher high than the bar one long lookback earlier while its 14-period RSI is lower); AND (higher-timeframe alignment is not required OR the last completed higher-timeframe close is below its 20-period close SMA); AND (session filter is disabled OR the previous bar falls within a selected session). The longer momentum SMA lookback is twice the long volume-average lookback.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR at the signal bar × stop multiplier (when dynamic re-entry zone is enabled)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR at the signal bar × stop multiplier (when dynamic re-entry zone is enabled)
