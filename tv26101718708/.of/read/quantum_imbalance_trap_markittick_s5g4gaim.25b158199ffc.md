Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, imbalance lookback) when that SMA is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = close > open AND body ratio >= threshold AND volume ratio >= multiplier AND short-period SMA(close) > long-period SMA(close); the long period is twice the imbalance lookback.
Calculation: Bearish imbalance = close < open AND body ratio >= threshold AND volume ratio >= multiplier AND short-period SMA(close) < long-period SMA(close); the long period is twice the imbalance lookback.
Long: Bullish imbalance on the previous bar AND no bullish imbalance two bars ago AND (liquidity filter disabled OR (minimum low of the previous three bars < lowest low over the imbalance lookback ending four bars ago AND previous close > that lowest low)) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (previous low < low one imbalance lookback before the previous bar AND previous RSI > RSI one imbalance lookback before the previous bar)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its SMA(close)) AND (session filter disabled OR previous bar is in an enabled UTC session).
Short: Bearish imbalance on the previous bar AND no bearish imbalance two bars ago AND (liquidity filter disabled OR (maximum high of the previous three bars > highest high over the imbalance lookback ending four bars ago AND previous close < that highest high)) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (previous high > high one imbalance lookback before the previous bar AND previous RSI < RSI one imbalance lookback before the previous bar)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its SMA(close)) AND (session filter disabled OR previous bar is in an enabled UTC session).
Exit: long, dynamic re-entry zone enabled AND low <= entry price - stop multiplier * ATR on the bar preceding the latest long signal.
Exit: short, dynamic re-entry zone enabled AND high >= entry price + stop multiplier * ATR on the bar preceding the latest short signal.
