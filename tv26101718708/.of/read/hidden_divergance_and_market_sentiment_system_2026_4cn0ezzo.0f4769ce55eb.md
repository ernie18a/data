Calculation: Composite momentum = round(0.40 × RSI + 0.35 × clamped(50 + MACD histogram / close × 10000, 0, 100) + 0.25 × clamped(50 + volume oscillator, 0, 100)), where volume oscillator = (shorter-period EMA(volume) − longer-period EMA(volume)) / longer-period EMA(volume) × 100.
Calculation: Normalized timeframe score = round((score + 8) / 16 × 100), where score sums: +1 if close > open, −1 if close < open; +1 if (close − low)/(high − low) ≥ 0.65, −1 if ≤ 0.35 (use 0.5 when high = low); +1 if short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs are strictly ascending, −1 if strictly descending; +1 if RSI > 58, −1 if RSI < 42; +1 if MACD histogram > 0, −1 if < 0; +1 if close > open and volume > 1.5 × 20-bar SMA(volume), −1 if close < open and volume > 1.5 × 20-bar SMA(volume); +1 if stochastic > 65, −1 if < 35; +1 if close > prior close > close two bars ago, −1 if close < prior close < close two bars ago. All unspecified cases contribute 0. Calculate on each of four sentiment timeframes; use the last completed bar when the timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, and the current bar when it is equal or lower.
Calculation: Weighted sentiment = round(0.10 × shortest-timeframe score + 0.20 × next-shortest score + 0.30 × next-longest score + 0.40 × longest-timeframe score).
Calculation: Bias points = sum 1 for each true condition: weekly close > weekly open; weekly close > weekly 21-period EMA; daily close > daily open; daily close > daily 21-period EMA; daily close > daily 55-period EMA; daily close > daily 200-period EMA; daily 21-period EMA > daily 55-period EMA; and recovery, where (daily close − lowest daily low over 20 bars) / max(highest daily high over 20 bars − lowest daily low over 20 bars, 1) > 0.5 and daily close > daily 21-period EMA. Weekly and daily calculations use the last completed bar when higher than the chart timeframe, otherwise the current bar.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot highs and lows = highs and lows with a five-bar left and right pivot lookback; associate each pivot with the Composite momentum value at that pivot. Compare the two most recent confirmed pivots of the same type.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = latest pivot low < previous pivot low and Composite momentum at latest pivot low > Composite momentum at previous pivot low. Bearish divergence = latest pivot high > previous pivot high and Composite momentum at latest pivot high < Composite momentum at previous pivot high.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = latest pivot low > previous pivot low and Composite momentum at latest pivot low < Composite momentum at previous pivot low. Hidden bearish divergence = latest pivot high < previous pivot high and Composite momentum at latest pivot high > Composite momentum at previous pivot high.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation count = number of true conditions: RSI < 50 and RSI > its value 10 bars ago; MACD histogram < 0 and > its value 10 bars ago; volume oscillator < 0 and > its value 10 bars ago. Bearish confirmation count = number of true conditions: RSI > 50 and RSI < its value 10 bars ago; MACD histogram > 0 and < its value 10 bars ago; volume oscillator > 0 and < its value 10 bars ago.
Calculation: Short ban = recovery and weekly close > weekly 21-period EMA and bias points ≥ 5.
Long: Bullish divergence and bullish confirmation count ≥ 2 OR hidden bullish divergence OR all three shortest timeframe scores ≥ 75 and at least one was < 75 on the prior bar and bars since the previous bullish signal ≥ cooldown OR all four timeframe scores ≥ 75 and at least one was < 75 on the prior bar and bars since the previous bullish signal ≥ cooldown OR bias points ≥ 5 and weighted sentiment ≥ 55.
Short: Bearish divergence and bearish confirmation count ≥ 2 OR hidden bearish divergence OR all three shortest timeframe scores ≤ 25 and at least one was > 25 on the prior bar and bars since the previous bearish signal ≥ cooldown OR all four timeframe scores ≤ 25 and at least one was > 25 on the prior bar and bars since the previous bearish signal ≥ cooldown OR bias points ≤ 3 and weighted sentiment ≤ 45 and short ban is false.
Exit: Long, false
