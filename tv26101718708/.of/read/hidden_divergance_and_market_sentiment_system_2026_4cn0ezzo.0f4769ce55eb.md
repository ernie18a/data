Calculation: Composite = round(weighted sum of chart-timeframe RSI, clamped normalized MACD histogram, and clamped normalized volume oscillator); normalized MACD histogram = clamp(MACD histogram / close × scale + midpoint, lower bound, upper bound); volume oscillator = (shorter-volume EMA − longer-volume EMA) / longer-volume EMA × scale; normalized volume oscillator = clamp(volume oscillator + midpoint, lower bound, upper bound); RSI weight > MACD weight > volume weight.
Calculation: Divergences = compare the two most recent confirmed price pivots of the same type and composite values at those pivot bars; pivot confirmation uses equal left and right lookbacks. Regular bullish: newer pivot low < older pivot low AND newer composite > older composite. Regular bearish: newer pivot high > older pivot high AND newer composite < older composite. Hidden bullish: newer pivot low > older pivot low AND newer composite < older composite. Hidden bearish: newer pivot high < older pivot high AND newer composite > older composite. Each divergence persists until its next same-type price pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation count = number of conditions satisfied: RSI < midpoint AND RSI > RSI twice the pivot lookback ago; MACD histogram < zero AND MACD histogram > MACD histogram twice the pivot lookback ago; volume oscillator < zero AND volume oscillator > volume oscillator twice the pivot lookback ago. Bearish confirmation count reverses all comparison directions.
Calculation: Timeframe score = normalize and round the sum of eight components, each scoring +1, −1, or zero: close versus open; close's position in the high-low range versus upper and lower thresholds, using the midpoint when high equals low; short, medium, and long close EMAs in ascending or descending order; RSI versus upper and lower thresholds; MACD histogram versus zero; close versus open when volume > a multiple of its SMA; stochastic versus upper and lower thresholds; and close versus the two preceding closes in strictly ascending or descending order. Each component scores +1 for its bullish comparison, −1 for its bearish comparison, and zero otherwise. Normalize the minimum possible sum to the lower score bound and the maximum possible sum to the upper score bound.
Calculation: Sentiment = rounded weighted sum of four timeframe scores, with weights increasing with timeframe. The four configured timeframes increase in length. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar; for the chart timeframe use the current bar.
Calculation: Bias = count of satisfied conditions: longer-timeframe close > open; longer-timeframe close > its close EMA; shorter-timeframe close > open; shorter-timeframe close > each of its short, medium, and long close EMAs; shorter-timeframe short EMA > medium EMA; recovery = shorter-timeframe close's position above the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a chart-bar lookback of the merged shorter-timeframe series AND shorter-timeframe close > its short EMA. The longer bias timeframe is longer than the shorter bias timeframe, which is the longest sentiment timeframe. Apply the same higher-, lower-, and chart-timeframe bar-selection rules as sentiment. Calculate range position as (close − lowest low) / max(highest high − lowest low, minimum denominator).
Long: Regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence.
Short: Regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence.
Long: Regular bullish divergence AND bullish confirmation count >= confirmation threshold.
Short: Regular bearish divergence AND bearish confirmation count >= confirmation threshold.
Long: Composite >= bullish threshold OR composite <= lower extreme threshold.
Short: Composite <= bearish threshold OR composite >= upper extreme threshold.
Long: Any timeframe score >= bullish score threshold OR sentiment >= bullish score threshold.
Short: Any timeframe score <= bearish score threshold OR sentiment <= bearish score threshold.
Long: Bias >= bullish bias threshold.
Short: Bias <= bearish bias threshold.
Long: The three shortest timeframe scores >= strong bullish threshold AND they were not all >= that threshold on the preceding chart bar AND the longest timeframe score < strong bullish threshold AND bars since the last bullish core or all-timeframe event >= cooldown; OR all four timeframe scores >= strong bullish threshold AND they were not all >= that threshold on the preceding chart bar AND bars since the last bullish core or all-timeframe event >= cooldown.
Short: The three shortest timeframe scores <= strong bearish threshold AND they were not all <= that threshold on the preceding chart bar AND the longest timeframe score > strong bearish threshold AND bars since the last bearish core or all-timeframe event >= cooldown; OR all four timeframe scores <= strong bearish threshold AND they were not all <= that threshold on the preceding chart bar AND bars since the last bearish core or all-timeframe event >= cooldown.
