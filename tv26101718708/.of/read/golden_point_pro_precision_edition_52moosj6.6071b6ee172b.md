Calculation: Directional score = round(100 × earned weight / enabled weight), where momentum always has weight 10 and enabled EMA, VWAP, RSI, ADX/DI, volume, and ATR components have weights 20, 20, 15, 15, 10, and 10 respectively. For the long score, award each enabled weight when its corresponding condition is true: short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA; close > session VWAP of typical price; RSI >= long RSI threshold; ADX >= strength threshold AND +DI > -DI; volume SMA > 0 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier; ATR SMA > 0 AND ATR >= ATR SMA × ATR multiplier. Award momentum weight when close > previous close AND close > open. For the short score, reverse the EMA, VWAP, RSI, DI, and momentum comparisons, using the short RSI threshold; volume and ATR conditions are unchanged. Enabled weight includes momentum and all enabled component weights whether or not their conditions are true.
Calculation: Last signal = initially none; after each completed bar, set to long if the long signal conditions are met and the previous last signal is not long; otherwise set to short if the short signal conditions are met and the previous last signal is not short; otherwise retain its previous value.

Short: Close < previous close AND close < open AND short directional score >= score threshold AND (EMA filter disabled OR (short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA)) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (sideways filter disabled OR ((ATR > 0 AND abs(short EMA - long EMA) / ATR >= spread threshold OR ATR <= 0 AND 0 >= spread threshold) AND ADX >= sideways ADX threshold)) AND (trend-entry filter disabled OR (highest high over entry lookback including current bar >= current short EMA AND close < short EMA)) AND previous last signal != short

Short: Short EMA < long EMA AND (session VWAP of typical price unavailable OR close < session VWAP of typical price)
