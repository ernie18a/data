Calculation: Zone = ATR multiplied by a zone-width parameter.
Calculation: Support and resistance = independently track the latest confirmed pivot low and pivot high, using equal numbers of bars on either side for confirmation. When a pivot is confirmed, compare its price with the corresponding stored level using the zone at the pivot bar. If the distance is within that zone, replace the level with the touch-count-weighted average of the stored level and pivot and increment its touch count; otherwise replace the level with the pivot, set its count to one, and set its last-touch bar to the pivot bar. Then, if the current bar overlaps the level plus or minus the current zone and the minimum touch separation has elapsed since its last-touch bar, increment the count and set the last-touch bar to the current bar. A level is valid when its count reaches the minimum touch count. A matching pivot does not update the last-touch bar.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick >= rejection ratio multiplied by max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) AND lower wick > upper wick; lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close).
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND upper wick >= rejection ratio multiplied by max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) AND upper wick > lower wick.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open.
Calculation: Breakout retest = when resistance is valid AND close > resistance + zone multiplied by a breakout buffer AND previous close <= that same current-bar threshold, remember the current resistance and bar, replacing any earlier remembered breakout. Expire it when its age exceeds the retest limit. A retest occurs after the breakout bar AND before expiration AND low <= remembered resistance + current zone AND high >= remembered resistance - current zone AND close >= remembered resistance AND (bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing). Clear the remembered breakout whenever this retest occurs, even if no entry is permitted.
Calculation: Resistance warning = valid resistance AND ((high >= resistance - zone AND low <= resistance + zone AND (bearish rejection OR bearish engulfing)) OR (high > resistance + zone multiplied by the breakout buffer AND close < resistance)).
Calculation: Trade state = process each bar in this order: update levels and breakout retest; evaluate normal entry; evaluate resistance warning; evaluate exit; expire re-entry eligibility; evaluate re-entry. On normal entry, record the current close as entry price, the lowest low of the preceding exit-lookback completed bars as frozen low, and the remembered breakout resistance as setup level if a breakout retest qualified; otherwise record the current support as setup level. Reset warning protection. A resistance warning while in a trade activates warning protection on that bar if the warning cooldown has elapsed since the last accepted warning, including warnings from previous trades; record the first activation bar. On exit, save the setup level and arm re-entry for a limited number of bars, expiring it when its age exceeds that limit. On re-entry, retain the saved setup level, record a new entry price and frozen low, and reset warning protection. Record the bar of every normal entry and re-entry for the normal-entry cooldown.
Long: No open trade AND re-entry is not armed AND the normal-entry cooldown has elapsed since the last normal entry or re-entry AND ((support is valid AND low <= support + zone AND high >= support - zone AND ((low < support - zone multiplied by a reclaim buffer AND close > support) OR bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing)) OR breakout retest).
Long: No open trade AND re-entry is armed AND current bar is after the exit bar AND re-entry age <= its limit AND close > saved setup level + current ATR multiplied by a re-entry buffer AND previous close <= that same current-bar threshold AND (bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing OR (close > open AND close > previous close)).
Exit: long, warning protection is inactive AND current bar is after the entry bar AND close < frozen low.
Exit: long, warning protection is active AND current bar is after its activation bar AND close < lowest low of the preceding exit-lookback completed bars.
