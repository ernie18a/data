Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source over the selected session, anchored period, or rolling window
Calculation: Outer deviation bands = VWAP plus or minus the configured multiple of the volume-weighted standard deviation of the selected price source over the same period
Long: More than the configured cooldown length of bars since the previous signal AND ((Aggressive model AND low < lower outer deviation band AND close > lower outer deviation band) OR (Balanced model AND the previous bar's low < its lower outer deviation band AND its close > its lower outer deviation band AND close > previous bar's high) OR (Conservative model AND the bar two bars ago's low < its lower outer deviation band AND its close > its lower outer deviation band AND close > the highest high of the current and previous bars AND (RSI is above its oversold threshold if the RSI filter is enabled))
Short: More than the configured cooldown length of bars since the previous signal AND ((Aggressive model AND high > upper outer deviation band AND close < upper outer deviation band) OR (Balanced model AND the previous bar's high > its upper outer deviation band AND its close < its upper outer deviation band AND close < previous bar's low) OR (Conservative model AND the bar two bars ago's high > its upper outer deviation band AND its close < its upper outer deviation band AND close < the lowest low of the current and previous bars AND (RSI is below its overbought threshold if the RSI filter is enabled)))
