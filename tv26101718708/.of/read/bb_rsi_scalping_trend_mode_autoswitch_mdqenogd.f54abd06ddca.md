Calculation: Bollinger width = upper Bollinger Band minus lower Bollinger Band; the basis is the close SMA.
Calculation: Dynamic RSI lower threshold = lowest RSI over its lookback plus a buffer; dynamic RSI upper threshold = highest RSI over the same lookback minus a buffer.
Calculation: Bullish MACD histogram inflection = histogram > previous histogram AND previous histogram < histogram two bars ago; bearish inflection reverses both inequalities. A recent inflection occurred within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Recent strong volume = volume exceeded its SMA times a multiplier within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar, AND, if increasing volume is required, that bar's volume exceeded its previous bar's volume.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND its RSI is higher than RSI at the preceding pivot AND the pivots are no more than a configurable number of bars apart. A pivot is confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side. Bearish divergence uses a higher pivot high and lower RSI.
Calculation: Effective entry mode = selected conservative, balanced, or aggressive mode; in automatic mode, aggressive when Bollinger width > its SMA AND ATR > its SMA AND volume > volume SMA times the automatic-volume multiplier, conservative when Bollinger width <= its SMA AND volume <= volume SMA times that multiplier, and balanced otherwise.
Calculation: Long continuation score = count of these true conditions: low <= Bollinger basis, close > basis, close > open, RSI > RSI midpoint, MACD histogram > previous histogram, MACD line >= signal line, recent strong volume. Short continuation score = count of the corresponding conditions high >= basis, close < basis, close < open, RSI < midpoint, histogram < previous histogram, MACD line <= signal line, and recent strong volume.
Calculation: Long trend confirmation = recent bullish histogram inflection AND recent strong volume if the long pullback conditions AND the require-confirmation setting are true; otherwise true. Long pullback conditions are ADX >= trend threshold AND +DI > -DI AND RSI between a lower bound and a long pullback threshold inclusive AND close >= basis minus a fraction of Bollinger width AND close > open AND bullish histogram inflection. Short trend confirmation is defined symmetrically using -DI > +DI, RSI between a short pullback threshold and an upper bound inclusive, close <= basis plus the same fraction of width, close < open, and bearish inflection.
Calculation: Long continuation condition = low <= basis AND close > basis AND close > open AND RSI > RSI midpoint AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD line >= signal line AND recent strong volume. Short continuation condition = high >= basis AND close < basis AND close < open AND RSI < RSI midpoint AND histogram < previous histogram AND MACD line <= signal line AND recent strong volume.
Calculation: Long reversal condition = (effective mode is conservative AND ((low < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI <= dynamic lower threshold AND recent bullish histogram inflection AND recent strong volume AND close > lower band AND close > previous close) OR (bullish RSI divergence AND pivot low < lower band at the pivot AND pivot RSI <= dynamic lower threshold at the pivot AND recent bullish histogram inflection AND recent strong volume))) OR (effective mode is balanced AND low <= lower band plus a fraction of Bollinger width AND RSI <= dynamic lower threshold plus a buffer AND close > open) OR (effective mode is aggressive AND low <= lower band plus that fraction of width AND RSI <= dynamic lower threshold plus that buffer). Short reversal condition reverses the price, RSI, candle, divergence, and histogram directions, using the upper band and dynamic upper threshold.
Calculation: Entry trade type = trend when trend mode is enabled AND ADX >= trend threshold AND the respective trend confirmation is true; otherwise continuation when continuation mode is enabled AND the respective continuation condition is true; otherwise reversal. Store the entry bar's ADX, ATR, time, and entry price; entry price is selectable as the entry bar's low for long or high for short, the respective outer Bollinger Band, or close. Evaluate both entry signals before updating position; if both are true, short state takes precedence.
Calculation: Initial long invalidation = lowest low over an initial lookback at entry minus a configurable price buffer; initial short invalidation = highest high plus that buffer. Dynamic long invalidation starts at the lowest low over a separate lookback at entry minus the buffer and, if dynamic updating is enabled, becomes the maximum of its previous value and the current rolling lowest low minus the buffer. Dynamic short invalidation starts at the rolling highest high plus the buffer and, if updating is enabled, becomes the minimum of its previous value and the current rolling highest high plus the buffer. Update before testing exits, including on the entry bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop activates when high >= entry price plus current ATR times an activation multiplier; once active it is the maximum since activation of high minus current ATR times a stop multiplier. Short trailing stop activates when low <= entry price minus current ATR times the activation multiplier; once active it is the minimum since activation of low plus current ATR times the stop multiplier. Update before testing exits.
Calculation: Long break-even stop activates when high >= entry price plus current ATR times a multiplier; short break-even stop activates when low <= entry price minus current ATR times that multiplier. Once activated, its level is entry price.
Calculation: Take-profit levels are entry price plus, for long, or minus, for short, entry-bar ATR times each of three increasing multipliers. Mark every newly reached level as hit before evaluating full and partial exits.
Long: Position is flat AND the optional session filter permits the bar AND ((trend mode enabled AND ADX >= trend threshold AND long trend confirmation is true) OR trend mode disabled OR (ADX < strong-trend threshold AND (long reversal condition OR (continuation mode enabled AND long continuation condition)))).
Short: Position is flat AND the optional session filter permits the bar AND ((trend mode enabled AND ADX >= trend threshold AND short trend confirmation is true) OR trend mode disabled OR (ADX < strong-trend threshold AND (short reversal condition OR (continuation mode enabled AND short continuation condition)))).
Long: Position is flat AND low <= lower Bollinger Band plus a fraction of Bollinger width AND RSI <= dynamic lower threshold plus the near-entry buffer AND bars since the previous such long marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Short: Position is flat AND high >= upper Bollinger Band minus a fraction of Bollinger width AND RSI >= dynamic upper threshold minus the near-entry buffer AND bars since the previous such short marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Long: Position is flat AND continuation mode is enabled AND long continuation condition is false AND long continuation score >= warning threshold AND bars since the previous such long marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Short: Position is flat AND continuation mode is enabled AND short continuation condition is false AND short continuation score >= warning threshold AND bars since the previous such short marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Long: Position is flat AND trend mode is enabled AND ADX >= trend threshold AND +DI > -DI AND long trend confirmation is false AND RSI <= long pullback threshold AND bars since the previous such long marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Short: Position is flat AND trend mode is enabled AND ADX >= trend threshold AND -DI > +DI AND short trend confirmation is false AND RSI >= short pullback threshold AND bars since the previous such short marking >= its cooldown, or no previous marking exists.
Exit: long, (trade type is trend AND trend mode enabled AND (ADX < entry ADX minus a weakening threshold OR (-DI > +DI AND ADX > reversal threshold))) OR (trade type is reversal AND ((selected exit mode is momentum AND ((enabled bearish histogram inflection) OR (enabled MACD line cross below signal line) OR (enabled RSI cross below midpoint))) OR (selected exit mode is first retrace AND close < open) OR (selected exit mode is retrace with MACD weakness AND close < open AND histogram < previous histogram))) OR (trade type is continuation AND ((selected exit mode is momentum AND ((enabled bearish histogram inflection) OR (enabled MACD line cross below signal line) OR (enabled RSI cross below midpoint))) OR (selected exit mode is MACD cross AND MACD line crosses below signal line) OR (selected exit mode is basis break AND close < basis AND histogram < previous histogram))) OR low <= initial long invalidation OR low <= dynamic long invalidation OR (trailing stop enabled AND activated AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled AND activated AND low <= entry price) OR (take profit enabled AND ((third level newly reached AND second and first levels have been hit) OR (third level newly reached AND first level has not been hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level has been hit))) OR (maximum holding time enabled AND bar time >= entry time plus maximum holding time).
Exit: short, (trade type is trend AND trend mode enabled AND (ADX < entry ADX minus a weakening threshold OR (+DI > -DI AND ADX > reversal threshold))) OR (trade type is reversal AND ((selected exit mode is momentum AND ((enabled bullish histogram inflection) OR (enabled MACD line cross above signal line) OR (enabled RSI cross above midpoint))) OR (selected exit mode is first retrace AND close > open) OR (selected exit mode is retrace with MACD weakness AND close > open AND histogram > previous histogram))) OR (trade type is continuation AND ((selected exit mode is momentum AND ((enabled bullish histogram inflection) OR (enabled MACD line cross above signal line) OR (enabled RSI cross above midpoint))) OR (selected exit mode is MACD cross AND MACD line crosses above signal line) OR (selected exit mode is basis break AND close > basis AND histogram > previous histogram))) OR high >= initial short invalidation OR high >= dynamic short invalidation OR (trailing stop enabled AND activated AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled AND activated AND high >= entry price) OR (take profit enabled AND ((third level newly reached AND second and first levels have been hit) OR (third level newly reached AND first level has not been hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level has been hit))) OR (maximum holding time enabled AND bar time >= entry time plus maximum holding time).
Exit: long, take profit enabled AND no full exit AND ((first level newly reached AND second level has not been hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level has been hit AND third level has not been hit)).
Exit: short, take profit enabled AND no full exit AND ((first level newly reached AND second level has not been hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level has been hit AND third level has not been hit)).
Exit: long, no full or partial exit AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: short, no full or partial exit AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
