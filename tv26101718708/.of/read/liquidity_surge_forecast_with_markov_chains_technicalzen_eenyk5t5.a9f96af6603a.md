Calculation: Momentum forecast = centered (MFI or RSI) after a Hull moving average, projected forward by its slope over the slope lookback and forecast horizon, with exponential slope decay and output clamped to ±100; use the middle momentum layer.
Calculation: Price-current forecast = the middle layer of either the Hull moving average of VWMA(close) slope, expressed as a scaled percentage change over 2 bars, or signed ADX (ADX positive when +DI ≥ −DI); project its value by its slope over the slope lookback and forecast horizon with exponential decay, clamped to ±100. The middle layer uses a shorter lookback than the longest layer.
Calculation: Angle regime consensus = for close, high, and low, classify each as up when its normalized slope angle over both short and long lookbacks exceeds the trend threshold, down when both are below its negative, sideways when both absolute angles are below the sideways threshold, otherwise neutral; consensus up/down requires that vote count to exceed each other category's count. Short lookback is shorter than long lookback.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low)) ÷ log10(lookback).
Calculation: Markov next-state probabilities = estimate Bear, Sideways, and Bull probabilities from transitions conditioned on the prior two regimes, using separate counts for current-regime duration below versus at least a duration threshold; decay counts each bar, add one to each state count, then normalize. A prediction is confident only when the decayed context count reaches a minimum; its vote is the highest-probability state, ties resolved Bull, then Bear, then Sideways.
Long: Choppiness Index is below its threshold AND (angle regime consensus is up or Markov confidently predicts Bull from a current Sideways regime with Bull probability at least the hatchling threshold) AND ((Momentum forecast > 0 AND Price-current forecast > 0 AND Markov is not confident or does not vote Bear) OR (current regime is Sideways AND Markov confidently predicts Bull with Bull probability at least the hatchling threshold)).
Short: Choppiness Index is below its threshold AND (angle regime consensus is down or Markov confidently predicts Bear from a current Sideways regime with Bear probability at least the hatchling threshold) AND ((Momentum forecast < 0 AND Price-current forecast < 0 AND Markov is not confident or does not vote Bull) OR (current regime is Sideways AND Markov confidently predicts Bear with Bear probability at least the hatchling threshold)).
