Calculation: High pool and low pool = respectively the average of the latest two confirmed pivot highs or pivot lows. A pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks; confirm it only after the right-hand bars complete. A pool is active when the absolute difference between its two pivots, divided by the earlier pivot, is at most the equal-level tolerance.
Calculation: Acceptance score = volume weight × volume score + (1 − volume weight) × ATR score. Each component score is clamp((expansion ratio − 1) / max(expansion multiplier − 1, epsilon), 0, 1). The volume expansion ratio is volume / SMA(volume); the ATR expansion ratio is ATR / SMA(ATR). Use the same lookback for ATR and both SMAs. If a denominator is not positive, use an expansion ratio of 1.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA bias = EMA of higher-timeframe closes with lookahead off, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, compared with its value on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Last signaled pool = the pool level recorded at the most recent long or short decision, tracked separately by direction. Update it on each respective decision, including when the resulting zone is subsequently invalidated or removed.
Calculation: Active zones = zones created by long and short decisions, centered on their respective pool levels with boundaries at the pool level plus and minus half the ATR on the decision bar. Before creating each zone, if the number of valid zones is at the configured cap, remove the oldest valid zone. Process a long zone before a short zone when both occur on the same bar. After creating zones, invalidate each valid zone whose exit condition is met; invalidated zones no longer count toward the cap.
Long: The low pool is active AND (close is at or below the low pool reduced by the sweep margin when close confirmation is enabled OR low is at or below that threshold when close confirmation is disabled) AND acceptance score is at least the minimum score AND (higher-timeframe bias filtering is disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA bias is nonnegative) AND (no long pool has previously been signaled OR the absolute difference between the low pool and the last signaled long pool divided by the last signaled long pool exceeds the equal-level tolerance).
Short: The high pool is active AND (close is at or above the high pool increased by the sweep margin when close confirmation is enabled OR high is at or above that threshold when close confirmation is disabled) AND acceptance score is at least the minimum score AND (higher-timeframe bias filtering is disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA bias is nonpositive) AND (no short pool has previously been signaled OR the absolute difference between the high pool and the last signaled short pool divided by the last signaled short pool exceeds the equal-level tolerance).
Exit: long, close is below a valid long zone's lower boundary reduced by the invalidation offset.
Exit: short, close is above a valid short zone's upper boundary increased by the invalidation offset.
