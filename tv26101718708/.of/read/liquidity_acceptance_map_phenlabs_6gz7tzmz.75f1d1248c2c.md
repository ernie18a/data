Calculation: Equal-high/low pool = the average of the latest two confirmed swing highs/lows whose difference as a percentage of the earlier pivot is within the equal-level tolerance; each pivot uses a balanced left/right lookback of half the swing lookback, with at least two bars on each side.
Calculation: Acceptance score = volume score × volume weight + ATR score × (1 − volume weight), where each score is clamp(((current value / its simple moving average − 1) / (its expansion multiplier − 1)) × 100, 0, 100); use volume and ATR simple moving averages with the same lookback.
Long: confirmed bar low (or close when close-beyond-sweep is required) ≤ equal-low pool × (1 − sweep margin) AND acceptance score ≥ minimum score AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA is not falling) AND the pool level differs from the last triggered bullish pool by more than the equal-level tolerance.
Short: confirmed bar high (or close when close-beyond-sweep is required) ≥ equal-high pool × (1 + sweep margin) AND acceptance score ≥ minimum score AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA is not rising) AND the pool level differs from the last triggered bearish pool by more than the equal-level tolerance. The HTF EMA uses the latest available higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off; it may still be forming in real time.
