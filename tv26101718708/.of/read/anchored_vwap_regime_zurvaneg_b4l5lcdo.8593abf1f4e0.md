Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of the configured price source since the configured anchor reset; use equal bar weights if volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Effective deviation = greater of the anchored weighted standard deviation and a configurable ATR-based floor.
Calculation: Acceptance gate = +1 after the configured number of consecutive mature-bar closes above Anchored VWAP plus the configured dead-zone width; −1 after that many closes below Anchored VWAP minus the dead-zone width; otherwise retain the prior gate. Dead-zone width is a configurable multiple of Effective deviation.
Calculation: Fast and slow exponential VWAP = EMA(configured price source × bar weight) / EMA(bar weight), using shorter and longer lookbacks respectively; spread = (Fast exponential VWAP − Slow exponential VWAP) / ATR.
Calculation: Rotation count = number of changes between nonzero sides of mature closes relative to Anchored VWAP ± the dead zone, counted over a configurable recent-bar window clipped to the current anchor.
Calculation: Candidate regime = Balance if rotation count reaches the balance threshold; otherwise Trend Up if the current regime is Trend Up, the gate is not −1, and spread is above the lower spread threshold, or if the gate is +1, spread reaches the entry threshold, and rotation count is at or below its maximum; otherwise Trend Down by the symmetric conditions (gate is not +1 and spread below the negative lower threshold for an existing Trend Down; or gate is −1, spread at or below the negative entry threshold, and rotation count at or below its maximum); otherwise Transition. The resumption entry threshold is lower than the new-trend entry threshold. Evaluate only after the anchor matures.
Calculation: Confirmed regime = candidate regime persists for the configured number of consecutive closed bars.
Calculation: Saturation reversal = after a mature bar closes at or beyond the configurable positive or negative saturation distance from Anchored VWAP, the next bar closes back inside that distance; direction is opposite the preceding saturated side.
Long: Confirmed regime is Trend Up OR Saturation reversal follows a close below the negative saturation distance and closes back inside it (unless the optional trend filter is enabled and Confirmed regime is Trend Down).
Short: Confirmed regime is Trend Down OR Saturation reversal follows a close above the positive saturation distance and closes back inside it (unless the optional trend filter is enabled and Confirmed regime is Trend Up).
