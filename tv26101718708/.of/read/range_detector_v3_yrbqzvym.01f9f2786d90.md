Calculation: Range factor count = number of true conditions among ADX < ceiling; choppiness index > floor; normalized Bollinger bandwidth < ceiling; Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band; absolute ATR-normalized linear-regression slope < ceiling. Use the same length for ADX directional movement and smoothing, and the same length for Bollinger bands and Keltner channel. The Keltner center is the SMA of close and its half-width is an ATR multiple. Normalized Bollinger bandwidth = (current bandwidth − lowest bandwidth over a lookback) / (highest bandwidth over that lookback − lowest bandwidth over that lookback), or 0.5 if the extremes are equal; bandwidth = band width / SMA of close, or 0 if that SMA is zero. ATR-normalized slope = (current linear-regression endpoint − preceding point on the same fitted line) / ATR, or 0 if ATR is zero.
Calculation: Range state and rails = start idle. On a bar with range factor count >= required count, start a candidate with its first bar at the current bar and rails at its high and low. On each subsequent candidate bar, discard the candidate if the factor count < required count; otherwise extend the rails to the highest high and lowest low since the first candidate bar. Confirm it when consecutive qualifying bars >= confirmation count AND current bar index − first candidate bar index >= minimum range age. Starting on the next bar, before checking for a breakout, raise the upper rail to the current high if high > upper rail AND high − upper rail < an ATR multiple; lower the lower rail to the current low if low < lower rail AND lower rail − low < the same ATR multiple. A breakout ends the range; otherwise end it after checking for a breakout when its age > maximum range age. After a breakout, spend the next bar resetting to idle; a new candidate can start on the following bar. Evaluate state changes on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA of volume when that SMA > 0, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Latest breakout = direction, bar index, and updated rail price of the most recent breakout; retain these until another breakout replaces them.
Long: Confirmed range active at the start of the bar AND high > updated upper rail AND close > updated upper rail AND absolute value of close − open >= breakout ATR multiple AND relative volume >= participation threshold AND close > open.
Short: Confirmed range active at the start of the bar AND low < updated lower rail AND close < updated lower rail AND absolute value of close − open >= breakout ATR multiple AND relative volume >= participation threshold AND close < open.
Long: Latest breakout direction = up AND current bar index − breakout bar index >= 2 AND current bar index − breakout bar index <= retest lookback AND low <= breakout rail price AND close > breakout rail price AND close > open.
Short: Latest breakout direction = down AND current bar index − breakout bar index >= 2 AND current bar index − breakout bar index <= retest lookback AND high >= breakout rail price AND close < breakout rail price AND close < open.
