Calculation: Candle geometry = range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body ratio and wick ratios = their respective sizes divided by range; bullish close location = (close - low) / range; bearish close location = (high - close) / range. A zero denominator produces zero. The minimum price increment is a parameter because it is not supplied in the data.
Calculation: Participation = volume is usable when volume and its moving average are available and positive; effective volume = volume when usable, otherwise 1; relative volume = volume / its moving average when usable, otherwise 1; participation expansion = volume is unusable OR relative volume >= its threshold.
Calculation: Structural center = volume-weighted moving average of (high + low + close) / 3 over a long lookback, falling back to its EMA over the same lookback when unavailable; structural deviation = max(standard deviation of that price over the same lookback, or ATR when unavailable, ATR times a floor multiplier).
Calculation: Directional evidence = weighted sum of participation bias, center slope and structural displacement. Participation bias = clamp(EMA(effective volume times (2 times bullish close location - 1)) / EMA(effective volume), -1, 1), using the same lookback for both EMAs; center slope = clamp((structural center - previous structural center) / ATR, -1, 1); structural displacement = clamp((close - structural center) / structural deviation, -1, 1). A zero denominator produces zero. Participation bias has the largest weight, center slope the next largest, and displacement the smallest.
Calculation: Dynamic control = structural center + structural deviation times participation bias times a multiplier; control half-width = max(structural deviation times its multiplier, ATR times its multiplier); value half-width = max(structural deviation times its multiplier, ATR times its multiplier); value bounds = structural center plus and minus value half-width. Session control and its upper and lower bounds are dynamic control and dynamic control plus and minus control half-width, frozen at the first completed chart bar of each calendar session; initialize them on the first available bar.
Calculation: Participation envelope = the effective-volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 since the current calendar session began, plus and minus max(its effective-volume-weighted standard deviation times a multiplier, ATR times a floor multiplier). Include the current completed bar and reset the sums at each new session.
Calculation: Upper and lower references = maintain separate latest confirmed pivot-high and pivot-low clusters. A pivot uses equal numbers of bars on each side and becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. When a pivot is confirmed, merge it into its side's existing reference if their absolute difference is <= ATR at the pivot bar times a clustering multiplier; the merged reference is the confirmation-count-weighted mean and its count increases. Otherwise replace the reference with the pivot and reset its count. On either update, set its age origin to the pivot bar. A reference is active while its age is <= the maximum age.
Calculation: Bullish channel = directional evidence >= its positive threshold AND (EMA of typical price over a short lookback - its previous value) / ATR >= a negative slope threshold AND close >= structural center. Bearish channel = directional evidence <= the corresponding negative threshold AND the normalized EMA slope <= the corresponding positive slope threshold AND close <= structural center.
Calculation: Bullish acceptance = close > open AND close > upper value bound AND close > upper session control bound AND directional evidence >= its positive acceptance threshold AND body ratio >= its acceptance threshold AND bullish close location >= its acceptance threshold AND participation expansion. Bearish acceptance reverses price comparisons, uses the lower bounds, corresponding negative evidence threshold and bearish close location.
Calculation: Bullish reclaim = close > open AND low <= lower session control bound AND close > upper session control bound AND directional evidence >= its positive reclaim threshold AND lower wick ratio > upper wick ratio AND body ratio >= its reclaim threshold. Bearish reclaim reverses price comparisons, uses the corresponding negative evidence threshold and requires upper wick ratio > lower wick ratio.
Calculation: Bullish micro break = close > open AND close > highest high over the preceding short lookback; bearish micro break = close < open AND close < lowest low over that lookback. Bullish liquidity turn = low < lowest low over a longer preceding lookback AND close > that lowest low AND bullish micro break AND directional evidence >= its positive liquidity threshold AND close > session control AND participation expansion. Bearish liquidity turn reverses high/low and price comparisons and uses the corresponding negative evidence threshold.
Calculation: Pressure balance = (simple moving average of effective volume times bullish close location - simple moving average of effective volume times bearish close location) / their sum, or zero when the sum is not positive; pressure shift = current balance - previous balance, or zero if the previous balance is unavailable. Both averages use the same lookback.
Calculation: Behavior line = adaptive moving average of close initialized to the first close; its smoothing coefficient is the square of slow coefficient + efficiency times (fast coefficient - slow coefficient), where fast coefficient > slow coefficient and efficiency = min(abs(close - close at the behavior lookback) / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, 1), or zero when the denominator is not positive. Missing lagged close defaults to current close; an unavailable travel sum defaults to zero.
Calculation: Bullish pressure sweep = low < lowest low over a preceding pressure lookback AND close > that low AND bullish close location >= its sweep threshold. Bullish rejection = lower wick >= max(body, minimum price increment) times a rejection multiplier AND bullish close location >= its rejection threshold. Bullish initiative = participation expansion AND close > open AND body >= ATR times an initiative multiplier AND bullish close location >= its initiative threshold. Bearish versions reverse high/low and price comparisons and use upper wick and bearish close location.
Calculation: Bullish support score = sum of separate parameter weights awarded when, respectively: pressure balance >= its positive threshold; pressure shift >= its positive threshold; bullish pressure sweep; bullish rejection; bullish initiative; close > behavior line AND behavior line > its previous value; EMA of close > its value a fixed number of bars earlier; bullish micro break; bullish channel. Bearish support score uses corresponding negative pressure thresholds, bearish events, close < behavior line AND behavior line < its previous value, a declining close EMA, bearish micro break and bearish channel. Corresponding bullish and bearish weights match; the pressure-balance weight is largest, followed by sweep, micro break, initiative, pressure shift and channel, rejection and behavior, then trend slope, with equal weights within the stated pairs. Unavailable prior behavior or trend values default to the current value.
Calculation: Bullish support = chart timeframe is shorter than the session timeframe AND bullish support score >= its threshold AND bullish support score >= bearish support score + a margin AND (bullish pressure sweep OR bullish initiative OR (bullish rejection AND pressure shift > 0)). Bearish support uses the corresponding bearish score and events and pressure shift < 0.
Calculation: Bullish votes = twice the count of bullish acceptance, bullish reclaim and bullish liquidity turn that hold, plus the count of bullish support, bullish channel and directional evidence >= its positive vote threshold that hold. Bearish votes use the corresponding bearish conditions and negative evidence threshold.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = close > open AND bearish channel is false AND NOT (upper reference is active AND high >= upper reference AND close < upper reference) AND NOT (high >= upper participation envelope AND close < upper participation envelope AND upper wick ratio >= its envelope rejection threshold) AND (upper reference is inactive OR close > upper reference OR upper reference - close >= ATR times a room multiplier) AND ((bullish acceptance AND directional evidence >= its stronger positive threshold AND body ratio >= its stronger threshold) OR (bullish votes >= the higher vote threshold AND (bullish acceptance OR bullish reclaim OR bullish liquidity turn)) OR (bullish votes >= the lower vote threshold AND (bullish acceptance OR bullish reclaim OR bullish liquidity turn) AND bullish support)).
Calculation: Bearish candidate = close < open AND bullish channel is false AND NOT (lower reference is active AND low <= lower reference AND close > lower reference) AND NOT (low <= lower participation envelope AND close > lower participation envelope AND lower wick ratio >= its envelope rejection threshold) AND (lower reference is inactive OR close < lower reference OR close - lower reference >= ATR times the room multiplier) AND ((bearish acceptance AND directional evidence <= its stronger negative threshold AND body ratio >= its stronger threshold) OR (bearish votes >= the higher vote threshold AND (bearish acceptance OR bearish reclaim OR bearish liquidity turn)) OR (bearish votes >= the lower vote threshold AND (bearish acceptance OR bearish reclaim OR bearish liquidity turn) AND bearish support)).
Calculation: Last signal direction = initialize to neutral; after each completed bar with a routed buy or sale, set it to that signal's direction. A newly bullish channel has routing priority when the last signal was a sale; otherwise a newly bearish channel has priority when the last signal was a buy. If neither priority applies, route a candidate only when the last signal is neutral or opposite; if both candidates hold, retain only the one matching the sign of directional evidence, or neither when evidence is zero.
Long: bullish channel.
Short: bearish channel.
Long: last signal direction = sale AND bullish channel is true AND bullish channel was false on the preceding bar OR bullish candidate is routed as a buy.
Short: last signal direction = buy AND bearish channel is true AND bearish channel was false on the preceding bar OR bearish candidate is routed as a sale.
