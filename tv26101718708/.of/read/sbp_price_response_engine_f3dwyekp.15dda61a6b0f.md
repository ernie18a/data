Calculation: Confirmed upper/lower reference = most recently confirmed pivot high/low, merging a new pivot into the existing reference when within an ATR-scaled clustering distance; merged price is strength-weighted, and each reference is usable only while within its maximum age.
Calculation: Directional evidence = 0.45 × clamped participation bias + 0.30 × clamped normalized slope of the long-lookback volume-weighted price center (EMA fallback when unavailable) + 0.25 × clamped normalized close displacement from that center. Participation bias is the EMA of volume-weighted close location signed around the candle midpoint, divided by EMA volume.
Calculation: Bull/bear channel = directional evidence is respectively positive/negative beyond its threshold, the short-lookback EMA of typical price has no opposing normalized slope beyond its tolerance, and close is respectively above/below the long-lookback volume-weighted price center. Channel start = channel is true on this completed bar and false on the prior bar.
Calculation: Bull/bear acceptance = respectively bullish/bearish candle closes beyond both the long-lookback value-area boundary and session control boundary; directional evidence, body-to-range ratio, close location, and relative volume meet their directional qualification thresholds. Value area uses the long-lookback center and deviation; session control is set from the first completed bar of each daily session.
Calculation: Bull/bear reclaim = respectively bullish/bearish candle reaches through the relevant session control boundary and closes beyond the opposite boundary; directional evidence, dominant opposing wick, and body-to-range ratio meet their directional thresholds.
Calculation: Bull/bear liquidity turn = respectively, the bar sweeps the prior short-lookback low/high and closes back beyond it, closes beyond the prior shorter micro-lookback high/low in the directional candle, directional evidence meets its directional threshold, close is beyond session control in that direction, and relative volume qualifies.
Calculation: Bull/bear support = intraday chart and respective directional score meets its minimum and exceeds the opposite score by a margin, with at least one of a qualifying pressure sweep, initiative candle, or rejection plus pressure shift in the same direction. Each score sums weighted points for directional pressure balance, pressure shift, pressure sweep, wick rejection, initiative candle, adaptive behavior-line direction, trend-EMA slope, micro-break, and directional channel; the trend EMA lookback is shorter than the pressure lookback, and the adaptive behavior line uses an efficiency-adjusted smoothing constant.
Calculation: Bull/bear votes = 2 points for each respective acceptance, reclaim, and liquidity turn; 1 point for respective support, channel, and directional evidence beyond its vote threshold.
Calculation: Strong bull/bear acceptance = respective acceptance with stronger same-direction evidence and body-to-range ratio thresholds.
Calculation: Bull/bear candidate = completed bar and respectively bullish/bearish candle; opposite channel is false; respective opposing reference rejection and adaptive session-envelope rejection are false; there is room to the relevant opposing reference (no reference, close already beyond it, or separation of at least an ATR-scaled minimum); and strong respective acceptance, or a respective core event (acceptance, reclaim, or liquidity turn) with at least the vote minimum, or a respective core event with a lower vote minimum plus respective support.
Long: bull channel starts and last signal direction is short; OR last signal direction is neutral or short and bull candidate is true and bear candidate is false; OR bull candidate is true while last signal direction is long.
Short: bear channel starts and last signal direction is long; OR last signal direction is neutral or long and bear candidate is true and bull candidate is false; OR bear candidate is true while last signal direction is short. If both candidates are true, retain only the candidate matching the sign of directional evidence; suppress both if evidence is zero. Signal direction starts neutral and alternates after each signal; a channel-start reversal takes precedence.
