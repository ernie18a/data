Calculation: Pattern skeleton = Select the oldest and newest closes in a fixed-length window or a window whose length is the rounded Ehlers homodyne dominant cycle of (high + low) / 2 multiplied by a configurable factor and clamped between configurable bounds. Repeatedly insert the unselected close with the largest absolute vertical distance from the straight line joining its two neighboring selected points until the configured number of points is selected. Normalize their prices to [0, 1] using their minimum and maximum; pattern height is their unnormalized maximum minus minimum.
Calculation: Pattern family and fit = Compare the normalized skeleton with configurable double-top, double-bottom, head-and-shoulders, inverse-head-and-shoulders, triple-top, and triple-bottom templates. Template fit is max(0, 1 - RMSE / tolerance); select the highest fit if it meets the minimum. Separately fit least-squares lines to the skeleton's interior local highs and lows when each has at least two points and both line R² values meet the minimum. Divide each slope by ATR and calculate convergence as the distance between the lines at the latest interior extreme divided by their distance at the earliest, or 1 if the initial distance is nonpositive. In order, classify as broadening if convergence exceeds its upper threshold; ascending triangle if the high line is flat and the low slope is positive; descending triangle if the low line is flat and the high slope is negative; symmetric triangle if the high slope is negative and the low slope is positive; rising wedge or ascending channel if both slopes are positive, according to whether convergence is below the wedge threshold; falling wedge or descending channel if both slopes are negative, using the same test. Flat means absolute ATR-normalized slope below a threshold. Geometric fit is the smaller R² multiplied by a penalty when either line has only two points. Replace the template family only when a geometric family exists and its fit is greater. The family direction is long for double bottom, inverse head-and-shoulders, triple bottom, ascending triangle, falling wedge and ascending channel; short for double top, head-and-shoulders, triple top, descending triangle, rising wedge and descending channel; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Analog memory = On every accepted directional emission with positive ATR, save its normalized skeleton, close, ATR, direction and bar index. After the configured resolution horizon, append its skeleton and (resolution close - emission close) / emission ATR to a bounded FIFO memory. Resolve pending emissions after evaluating the current bar's emission, so resolutions on the current bar are unavailable to that bar's match. Neutral emissions update the emission cooldown but do not enter memory.
Calculation: Analog direction, confidence and motif = When analog matching is enabled and memory has at least the required number of resolved emissions, rank memory skeletons by dynamic time warping distance using absolute point differences and normalize accumulated distance by twice the skeleton length. Select the configured number of nearest neighbors, breaking distance ties by memory order. Analog direction is the sign of their mean signed forward move; confidence is the fraction whose forward move has that direction and absolute magnitude at least the configured ATR-multiple threshold. Motif is max(0, 1 - nearest distance / mean distance over the entire memory), undefined when the mean distance is zero. Otherwise use family direction and family fit as direction and confidence.
Calculation: Liquidity context = Confirm swing highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks. Maintain the latest two confirmed highs and lows; when consecutive highs or lows differ by no more than an ATR-multiple tolerance, retain their higher high or lower low respectively as an equal-level pool until replaced. A bullish sweep is a low below the retained low pool followed by a close above it, or a low below the latest confirmed swing low followed by a close above it and above the open. A bearish sweep is a high above the retained high pool followed by a close below it, or a high above the latest confirmed swing high followed by a close below it and below the open. A directional sweep is recent when its latest occurrence is within the configured sweep lookback. Dealing-range position is (close - latest confirmed swing low) / (latest confirmed swing high - latest confirmed swing low), undefined if either pivot is unavailable or the range is nonpositive.
Calculation: Setup score = Compute true range / ATR, absolute candle body / high-low range, and the volume z-score over a configurable lookback; use zero for an undefined volume z-score or zero-range candle body ratio. Normalize and cap displacement and body ratios at 1, and clamp the shifted and scaled volume z-score to [0, 1]. Quality is their configurable weighted sum plus a weight for a recent directional sweep. The setup score is the weighted average of confidence, optional quality, recent directional sweep and favorable location, where favorable location means below the dealing-range midpoint for a long or above it for a short. Count a sweep only when the context layer is enabled. Grade is assigned by ordered score thresholds; the minimum grade and the quality contribution are configurable.
Calculation: Emission eligibility = A family exists, pattern height is at least a configured ATR multiple, and at least the configured cooldown has elapsed since any accepted emission. If the optional regime veto is enabled, reject reversal families in a trend regime, continuation families in a choppy regime, and optionally neutral families in a choppy regime. Reversal families are the six template families and both wedges; continuation families are both triangles with horizontal sides and both channels. Regime is determined by whether Kaufman's efficiency ratio is above the trend threshold or below the lower chop threshold. If mature analog matching and the optional motif gate are enabled, motif must meet its minimum. If the context layer and location gate are enabled and dealing-range position is defined, reject longs above the upper location threshold and shorts below its complementary lower threshold. If the context layer and grade gate are enabled, grade must meet the selected minimum. An accepted emission updates cooldown even when its direction is neutral.
Long: Emission eligibility = true AND analog direction, or family direction when analog memory is not mature or matching is disabled, > 0
Short: Emission eligibility = true AND analog direction, or family direction when analog memory is not mature or matching is disabled, < 0
