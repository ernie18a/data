Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its simple moving average, or zero when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Store the latest two confirmed highs and lows and the RSI at each pivot bar.
Calculation: Structure direction = bullish when the latest two swing highs and latest two swing lows are both rising, bearish when both are falling; otherwise retain the previous direction, initially neutral. Update it before evaluating breaks on the same bar.
Calculation: Bullish break = close > latest swing high AND previous close <= the current latest swing high. Bearish break = close < latest swing low AND previous close >= the current latest swing low.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest swing low AND close > latest swing low AND relative volume >= spike threshold. Bearish sweep = high > latest swing high AND close < latest swing high AND relative volume >= spike threshold.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = bullish break AND structure direction is bullish. Bearish structure break = bearish break AND structure direction is bearish.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions among close > daily-anchored HLC3 VWAP, short EMA > medium EMA, bullish sweep within the recent lookback, bullish structure break within the recent lookback, relative volume >= spike threshold, and close > the upper daily VWAP standard-deviation band. Bearish score = the analogous count using close below VWAP, short EMA < medium EMA, bearish sweep, bearish structure break, the same volume spike, and close below the lower band. The recent lookback includes the current bar.
Calculation: Flow = a scale factor multiplied by (bullish score - bearish score) / (bullish score + bearish score), or zero if their sum is zero. Bullish and bearish percentages are their respective scores divided by the sum, or equal neutral percentages if the sum is zero.
Calculation: Trend strength = capped ADX contribution + EMA contribution + capped VWAP-distance contribution + native-volume contribution. The ADX contribution is proportional to ADX up to its cap; the EMA contribution is full for a strictly ordered short/medium/long EMA stack in either direction, half when the short and medium EMAs differ without a full stack, and zero otherwise; the VWAP contribution is proportional to absolute close-to-daily-VWAP distance divided by ATR, capped at its maximum, using a unit denominator when ATR is zero; the volume contribution is full when relative volume reaches the spike threshold, half when relative volume exceeds its average level, and zero otherwise. Calculate all components in the timeframe being evaluated.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = latest confirmed swing high > preceding swing high AND RSI at the latest high < RSI at the preceding high. Bullish RSI divergence = latest confirmed swing low < preceding swing low AND RSI at the latest low > RSI at the preceding low.
