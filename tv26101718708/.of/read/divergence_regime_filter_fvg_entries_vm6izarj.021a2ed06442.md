Calculation: Oscillator = selected RSI, MACD histogram, CCI, Stoch RSI, or MFI calculated from chart bars
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = an oscillator pivot low confirmed after the right-side bars, where the price low at that pivot is lower than at the preceding oscillator pivot low by at least a threshold times ATR, and the oscillator pivot is higher by at least a threshold times its own 100-bar standard deviation; pivot spacing is within minimum and maximum limits
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = an oscillator pivot high confirmed after the right-side bars, where the price high at that pivot is higher than at the preceding oscillator pivot high by at least a threshold times ATR, and the oscillator pivot is lower by at least a threshold times its own 100-bar standard deviation; pivot spacing is within minimum and maximum limits
Calculation: Strong EMA slope = the absolute difference between the trend EMA and its value at the slope lookback, divided by ATR, is at least a sensitivity multiple of its own 100-bar average absolute value
Calculation: Bearish trend regime = close below the trend EMA, negative EMA slope, strong EMA slope, and recent bullish-divergence count at least the minimum and greater than the recent bearish-divergence count
Calculation: Bullish trend regime = close above the trend EMA, positive EMA slope, strong EMA slope, and recent bearish-divergence count at least the minimum and greater than the recent bullish-divergence count
Calculation: Bullish displacement FVG = current low above the high two bars earlier, the middle candle closes above its open, the gap is at least a threshold times ATR, and the middle candle body is at least a threshold times ATR
Calculation: Bearish displacement FVG = current high below the low two bars earlier, the middle candle closes below its open, the gap is at least a threshold times ATR, and the middle candle body is at least a threshold times ATR
Long: regular bullish divergence confirms during a bearish trend regime OR a pending bullish-divergence setup is within its confirmation window, its pivot has not been broken by a close below it, and a bullish displacement FVG forms
Short: regular bearish divergence confirms during a bullish trend regime OR a pending bearish-divergence setup is within its confirmation window, its pivot has not been broken by a close above it, and a bearish displacement FVG forms
