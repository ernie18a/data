Calculation: Oscillator = one selected from RSI of close, MACD histogram of close, CCI of (high + low + close) / 3, SMA-smoothed stochastic of RSI of close, or MFI of (high + low + close) / 3 and volume. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = on the confirmation bar of an oscillator pivot low, compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed oscillator pivot low. The pivot bars are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum pivot spacing; previous pivot-bar low minus current pivot-bar low is at least a threshold multiple of ATR on the current pivot bar; and current pivot oscillator minus previous pivot oscillator is at least a threshold multiple of oscillator standard deviation on the confirmation bar. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-side bars; its oscillator value is no greater than the specified number of preceding oscillator values and is less than all specified subsequent oscillator values.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = on the confirmation bar of an oscillator pivot high, compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed oscillator pivot high. The pivot bars are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum pivot spacing; current pivot-bar high minus previous pivot-bar high is at least a threshold multiple of ATR on the current pivot bar; and previous pivot oscillator minus current pivot oscillator is at least a threshold multiple of oscillator standard deviation on the confirmation bar. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-side bars; its oscillator value is no less than the specified number of preceding oscillator values and is greater than all specified subsequent oscillator values.
Calculation: Normalized slope = (close EMA minus the same EMA a slope-lookback number of bars earlier) / current ATR. Average slope magnitude = SMA of the absolute normalized slope over its averaging lookback.
Calculation: Bullish trend regime = close > close EMA AND normalized slope > 0 AND absolute normalized slope >= a threshold multiple of average slope magnitude AND bearish divergences confirmed in the divergence-memory lookback, including the current bar, >= minimum divergence count AND bearish divergence count > bullish divergence count over that same lookback.
Calculation: Bearish trend regime = close < close EMA AND normalized slope < 0 AND absolute normalized slope >= a threshold multiple of average slope magnitude AND bullish divergences confirmed in the divergence-memory lookback, including the current bar, >= minimum divergence count AND bullish divergence count > bearish divergence count over that same lookback.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > previous open AND current low minus high two bars earlier >= a threshold multiple of current ATR AND absolute previous candle body >= a threshold multiple of current ATR.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < previous open AND low two bars earlier minus current high >= a threshold multiple of current ATR AND absolute previous candle body >= a threshold multiple of current ATR.
Calculation: Long setup = a bullish divergence confirmed in a bearish trend regime. Record its confirmation bar and pivot-bar low; replace any existing long setup whenever a new one occurs. Cancel it before evaluating confirmation if its age exceeds the FVG window or, when pivot-break cancellation is enabled, close < its recorded pivot-bar low. After a bullish FVG confirms it, clear it. Process these state updates even when FVG confirmation is disabled.
Calculation: Short setup = a bearish divergence confirmed in a bullish trend regime. Record its confirmation bar and pivot-bar high; replace any existing short setup whenever a new one occurs. Cancel it before evaluating confirmation if its age exceeds the FVG window or, when pivot-break cancellation is enabled, close > its recorded pivot-bar high. After a bearish FVG confirms it, clear it. Process these state updates even when FVG confirmation is disabled.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Long: Bullish divergence AND bearish trend regime.
Long: FVG confirmation enabled AND long setup active AND setup age >= one bar AND setup age <= FVG window AND bullish FVG.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Short: Bearish divergence AND bullish trend regime.
Short: FVG confirmation enabled AND short setup active AND setup age >= one bar AND setup age <= FVG window AND bearish FVG.
