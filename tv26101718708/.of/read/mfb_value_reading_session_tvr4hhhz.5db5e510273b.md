Calculation: Previous session profile = Divide the previous completed configured session's chart-bar high-to-low range into equal-width price rows. Assign each completed lower-timeframe bar's volume to the row containing its close, capping the row index at the highest row. Ignore bars with missing close or volume. The point of control is the midpoint of the highest-volume row, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Starting at that row, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time toward the greater volume, choosing the upper row on a tie, until the included volume reaches the configured fraction of total volume or all rows are included. Value area high and low are the upper and lower boundaries of the included rows; value area midpoint is their average. The profile is valid only when the session range and maximum row volume are positive.
Long: Current chart bar is within the configured session AND previous session profile is valid AND the first chart bar's open in the current session > previous session value area high AND previous session point of control > previous session value area midpoint
