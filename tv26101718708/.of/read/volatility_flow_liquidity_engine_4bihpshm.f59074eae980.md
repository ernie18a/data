Calculation: Price-path direction score = Up score minus down score. Up score is twice the indicator that close exceeds the highest high of the preceding trigger lookback, plus the indicator that close is at least as far above low as below high, plus the indicator that the selected price source has not fallen over the direction lookback. Down score is twice the indicator that close is below the lowest low of the preceding trigger lookback, plus the indicators that close is closer to low than high and that the selected price source has fallen over the direction lookback.
Calculation: Price-only pressure = The weighted mean of ATR compression percentile, absolute log-return acceleration percentile, and trend inefficiency, with acceleration weighted one and a half times as heavily as each other component. ATR compression is 100 minus the percentile rank of ATR. Trend inefficiency is 100 times one minus the absolute price-source change over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price-source changes over that lookback; use zero for the efficiency ratio when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Order-flow bias = Classify each bar's buy and sell volume by the selected method: split volume by the standard normal CDF of close change divided by its rolling standard deviation; or split by close location in the high-low range; or sum completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, assigning their volume to buy when close exceeds open, sell when close is below open, and half to each when equal. Use candle geometry if lower-timeframe data is unavailable. Substitute unit volume for zero or missing volume. Standardize buy minus sell volume using its rolling mean and standard deviation, using zero when the deviation is zero. Bias is positive when the standardized value reaches the positive threshold and buy volume exceeds sell volume, negative when it reaches the negative threshold and sell volume exceeds buy volume, and otherwise zero.
Calculation: Liquidity imbalance = The rolling sum of volume times (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), divided by rolling volume; use zero for a zero range or denominator. When volume is disabled, use unit volume.
Calculation: Anchored fair value = The volume-weighted mean of typical price since the chosen anchor, using unit weights when volume is disabled. The anchor resets at the confirmation bar of a symmetric high or low pivot, at a new session, week or month, or at a positive-volume bar matching the rolling highest volume, according to the selected anchor method.

Short: Price-path direction score < 0
Long: Percentile rank of price-only pressure >= threshold AND close > EMA of close
Short: Percentile rank of price-only pressure >= threshold AND close <= EMA of close
Long: Order-flow bias > 0
Short: Order-flow bias < 0

Short: Liquidity imbalance < negative threshold
