Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low confirmed after its right-side bars; evaluate all swing decisions on the confirmation bar. Compare each pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type.
Calculation: Structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high or below the latest confirmed swing low, using the current swing level for both current and preceding close, and that particular swing has not previously been broken. Structure state starts neutral and persists as the direction of the latest break; if both breaks occur on a bar, the bearish state takes precedence.
Calculation: Trend state = Supertrend direction agrees with the ordering of a shorter-period and longer-period close EMA. Bias starts neutral; set it bullish on a bullish structure break with bullish trend state or a bullish EMA crossover while structure and trend state are bullish; set it bearish symmetrically. While bias is neutral, also initialize it when structure and trend state agree. Otherwise retain bias; a bullish candidate takes precedence if both occur.
Calculation: Momentum = (shorter-period close EMA minus longer-period close EMA) divided by ATR, or zero when ATR is not positive. Momentum signal = EMA of momentum. Bullish momentum requires the shorter EMA above the longer EMA AND momentum above its signal AND momentum above zero; bearish momentum reverses all comparisons.
Calculation: Order block = on a structure break or an enabled first bar of directional displacement, search preceding bars from nearest to oldest within a lookback for an opposite-direction candle whose range is at least a specified multiple of its contemporaneous ATR, whose body is at least a specified fraction of its range, and whose volume is at least a specified multiple of its contemporaneous volume SMA when that filter is enabled and the SMA exists. Displacement requires a directional candle, body at least a specified multiple of current ATR, and close beyond the preceding high for bullish or low for bearish displacement. Define the candidate zone by its candle's high and low or body edges, as selected. Reject a candidate if any of the most recent bars between it and the trigger, up to the scan limit, closes or wicks beyond its far edge according to the selected mitigation method; continue searching. Add the first eligible candidate unless an active same-direction zone has both edges within a specified price tolerance. Retain at most the specified number of zones, removing the oldest on overflow, including inactive retained zones. On subsequent bars deactivate a zone when its age since creation exceeds the maximum or the selected close or wick crosses its far edge; remove it if expired or deletion of mitigated zones is enabled. A touch requires an active same-direction zone created before the current bar and high at or above its bottom AND low at or below its top. Update existing zones before creating zones and testing touches.
Calculation: Discount ceiling = rolling lowest low plus a specified fraction of the rolling high-low range; premium floor = rolling highest high minus a specified fraction of that range. Floor the range at the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = the sum of weights for matching bias, matching structure state, matching momentum, matching order-block touch, and matching discount or premium location, respectively; their weight ratio is 25:15:15:25:20. A directional setup requires matching bias AND structure state AND score at least the threshold AND each enabled requirement among matching touch, location, and momentum. A signal requires the setup to be true now and false on the preceding bar AND the elapsed bars since the last signal of the same direction to be at least the cooldown, or no such prior signal.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = the midpoint of two consecutive confirmed highs or lows whose absolute difference is at most a specified multiple of ATR at the newer pivot. Maintain pools in creation order up to a maximum, removing the oldest on overflow. A pool may be swept only after its creation bar and only once.
