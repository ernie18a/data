Calculation: Swing low = lowest low over a lookback including the current bar. Near low means low <= swing low + a configurable ATR multiple.
Calculation: Signed volume = -volume when close < open, otherwise volume. Cumulative signed volume resets to signed volume on the first bar of each configured trading session and otherwise adds signed volume each bar.
Calculation: A confirmed pivot low is a low with the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. For divergence and higher-low calculations, retain the two most recently confirmed price pivots and the RSI and cumulative signed volume at their pivot bars.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed pivot lows using a separate liquidity lookback. Retain at most a configurable number of the newest pools; remove pools older than a configurable age measured from their pivot bars. On each bar, process pools from oldest to newest: remove a pool if close < its level; otherwise, if low < its level, record its level and current bar as the latest sweep, and remove it if close > its level. A sweep event occurs if any pool was removed by that last condition OR if the latest recorded sweep is within the reclaim window AND close > its level AND previous close <= its level.
Calculation: Selling climax = close < open AND volume > a configurable multiple of volume SMA AND low <= swing low.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = weighted sum of these conditions: a sweep event within the watch window and bullish cumulative-signed-volume divergence within the watch window each have weight 3; bullish RSI divergence within the watch window, current sell-volume dry-up, absorption within the watch window, and a higher pivot low within the watch window each have weight 2; a selling climax within the watch window and confirmed wick rejection within the watch window each have weight 1. Bullish divergence occurs on pivot confirmation when the latest pivot low < the preceding pivot low AND the corresponding latest indicator value > its preceding value. Higher pivot low occurs on pivot confirmation when the latest pivot low > the preceding pivot low. Sell-volume dry-up means near low AND short SMA of down volume < a configurable multiple of long SMA of down volume AND long SMA of down volume > 0; down volume is volume on bars with close < open and zero otherwise. Absorption means near low AND volume > a configurable multiple of volume SMA AND high - low < a configurable multiple of ATR AND close >= (high + low) / 2. Wick rejection means the preceding bar was near low AND its lower wick > a configurable multiple of the greater of its body size and a configurable minimum price increment AND its close >= its midpoint AND current close > preceding high. All watch windows include the event bar.
Calculation: Gates pass when (the number of bars since low <= swing low is at least a configurable holding period OR a sweep event occurred within the reclaim window) AND close > EMA AND EMA minus its value a configurable number of bars ago >= a negative configurable multiple of ATR AND short SMA of signed volume > 0. The signed-volume SMA, down-volume short SMA, EMA comparison lag, and accumulation breakout lookback use the same period.
Calculation: A new exhaustion occurs when exhaustion score >= its threshold AND gates pass AND near low AND either no earlier new exhaustion exists OR more than one watch window has elapsed since the previous new exhaustion. On each new exhaustion, store the current swing low; clear the stored low when a later or current bar has low < the stored low.
Calculation: Accumulation condition = bars since the latest new exhaustion > 0 AND bars since that exhaustion <= twice the watch window AND close > the highest high over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > EMA AND short SMA of signed volume > 0.
Long: Selling climax.
Long: Sweep event.
Long: New exhaustion.
Long: Exhaustion score >= its threshold minus the weight of a sweep event AND near low AND gates do not pass AND the preceding bar did not have an early warning; an early warning is this same condition evaluated recursively on each bar.
Long: Accumulation condition AND the preceding bar did not satisfy the accumulation condition.
Exit: long, stored exhaustion low exists AND low < stored exhaustion low.
