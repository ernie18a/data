Calculation: MACD line = EMA of close with a shorter lookback minus EMA of close with a longer lookback; its upper band is SMA of the MACD line plus a configurable multiple of its standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Baseline channel = a configurable moving average of a configurable price source plus or minus a configurable multiple of the EMA of either true range or high minus low; the moving average and range EMA use the same lookback. The baseline is the same type of moving average of close with that lookback.
Calculation: Session buying and selling volume = sums, from the most recent bar whose opening time falls within a configurable weekday session-start window through the previous bar, of volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and volume × (high − close) / (high − low), respectively. Buying and selling strength are their respective shares of their combined total, expressed as percentages and rounded to the nearest integer. Undefined values do not satisfy comparisons.
Calculation: SSL line = a moving average of high when the SSL state is negative, otherwise the same type and length of moving average of low. Set the state to positive when close is above the high average, negative when close is below the low average, and otherwise retain its previous state. Use independently configured moving averages and lengths for the continuation and exit SSL lines.
Calculation: Smoothed true range = a configurable moving average of true range. Continuation distance = smoothed true range multiplied by a configurable factor.
Long: Within the configured date range AND the long direction is enabled AND MACD line > 0 AND MACD line >= its upper band AND at least one bar in the configured inclusive lookback has MACD line > 0 AND MACD line < its upper band AND the configurable price source > baseline AND close > upper baseline channel AND at least one bar in the configured inclusive pullback lookback has close <= upper baseline channel AND rounded buying strength > configurable threshold AND either the ADX filter is disabled or ADX > its configurable threshold.
Short: Within the configured date range AND the short direction is enabled AND MACD line < 0 AND MACD line < its upper band AND at least one bar in the configured inclusive lookback has MACD line < 0 AND MACD line >= its upper band AND the configurable price source < baseline AND close < lower baseline channel AND at least one bar in the configured inclusive pullback lookback has close >= lower baseline channel AND rounded selling strength > configurable threshold AND either the ADX filter is disabled or ADX > its configurable threshold.
Long: Close crosses above the upper baseline channel.
Short: Close crosses below the lower baseline channel.
Long: Close > baseline AND close > continuation SSL line AND close − continuation distance < continuation SSL line.
Short: Close < baseline AND close < continuation SSL line AND close + continuation distance > continuation SSL line.
Exit: short, close crosses above the exit SSL line.
Exit: long, close crosses below the exit SSL line.
Exit: long, within the configured date range AND close < entry price × (1 − configurable stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, within the configured date range AND close > entry price × (1 + configurable stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, within the configured date range AND high >= entry price × (1 + a configured profit-target fraction); apply each of the four independently configured target fractions and its configured position fraction.
Exit: short, within the configured date range AND low <= entry price × (1 − a configured profit-target fraction); apply each of the four independently configured target fractions and its configured position fraction.
