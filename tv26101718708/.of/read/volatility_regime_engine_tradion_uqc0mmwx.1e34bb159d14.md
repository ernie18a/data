Calculation: Rolling normalization = 100 × (value − rolling minimum) / (rolling maximum − rolling minimum), clipped to [0, 100]; use the midpoint when the range is zero.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its simple moving average, or 1 when that average is not positive; compute both a longer-term and a shorter-term version. Normalize the longer-term ratio over its normalization lookback and the shorter-term ratio over its baseline lookback.
Calculation: Bollinger bandwidth = (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band) / absolute value of the Bollinger middle band, or 0 when the middle band is zero. Normalize it over longer and shorter lookbacks. Bollinger bands use a simple moving average of close and a multiple of the standard deviation of close; Keltner bands use an EMA of close plus or minus a multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Historical volatility = standard deviation of log returns multiplied by a fixed annualization factor; use the current close in place of a nonpositive previous close. Normalize over its lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume, or 0 when the average is not positive. Its score is a descending step function of relative volume with four thresholds and four corresponding scores; below the lowest threshold use relative volume multiplied by a fixed factor, floored at 0. The highest threshold exceeds the confirmed-volume threshold, which exceeds the unit-volume threshold, which exceeds the lowest threshold.
Calculation: Directional primitives = bullish trend is close > shorter EMA of close AND shorter EMA > longer EMA; bearish trend is close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA. Bullish direction additionally requires rate of change > 0; bearish direction requires rate of change < 0. Bullish momentum acceleration is rate of change > its previous value; bearish momentum acceleration is rate of change < its previous value. Body-to-ATR is absolute close − open divided by longer-term ATR, or 0 when ATR is not positive. Bullish impulse is close > open AND close > shorter EMA AND body-to-ATR >= impulse threshold; bearish impulse reverses the price comparisons. A strong impulse additionally closes beyond the previous high for bullish or previous low for bearish.
Calculation: Recent directional breakout = at least one breakout in the current bar or the breakout-memory lookback. A bullish breakout is close > the highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND close > shorter EMA AND shorter EMA > its previous value; bearish uses close < the lowest prior low AND close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < its previous value.
Calculation: Confirmed volatility score = clipped weighted sum of normalized longer-term ATR ratio, normalized longer-lookback Bollinger bandwidth, normalized historical volatility, relative-volume score, and volatility acceleration score. The acceleration score is its highest level when both ATR ratio and bandwidth increase faster than on the preceding bar, its middle level when both increase otherwise, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Fast volatility score = clipped weighted sum of early ATR score, early bandwidth score, impulse score, momentum score, breakout score, and relative-volume score. Early ATR score is the maximum of normalized shorter-term ATR ratio and fixed floors triggered respectively by longer-term ATR ratio turning upward, longer-term ATR ratio accelerating upward, and shorter-term ATR ratio rising. Early bandwidth score is the maximum of normalized shorter-lookback bandwidth and fixed floors triggered by bandwidth turning upward or accelerating upward. Impulse score takes descending levels for a strong directional impulse, another directional impulse, or body-to-ATR above a separate threshold, otherwise zero. Momentum score takes its highest level when either directional momentum acceleration holds, a lower level when absolute rate of change increases otherwise, and zero otherwise. Breakout score takes its highest level on a current directional breakout, a lower level on a recent directional breakout otherwise, and zero otherwise. The fast signal is an EMA of this score.
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger bands inside Keltner bands OR normalized longer-term ATR ratio and normalized longer-lookback bandwidth both below their respective compression thresholds OR confirmed volatility score below its compression threshold. Compression exit occurs when compression ends after at least a specified number of consecutive compression bars.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish setup scores = separately sum fixed weights for close on the respective side of the shorter EMA, shorter EMA on the respective side of the longer EMA, shorter EMA sloping in the respective direction, rate of change having the respective sign, respective momentum acceleration, respective impulse, and a current respective breakout or, at a lower weight, a recent respective breakout. Add further weights when fast volatility score >= the early threshold AND respective direction or momentum acceleration holds; when relative volume >= the fast-breakout threshold AND the candle closes in the respective direction or unchanged; and when both ATR ratio and bandwidth rise AND respective momentum acceleration holds. Clip each score to [0, 100]. Bullish dominance means bullish setup minus bearish setup >= the dominance threshold; bearish dominance means that difference <= the negative dominance threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe volatility score = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, calculate normalized ATR ratio, normalized Bollinger bandwidth, normalized historical volatility, and a relative-volume step score; combine them with fixed weights and clip to [0, 100]. Its relative-volume score has descending levels for relative volume >= the highest threshold, >= the confirmed-volume threshold, >= unit volume, and below unit volume. When higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled, exclude its risk reduction and quality modifier.
Calculation: False-expansion risk points = floor at zero the sum of fixed penalties for relative volume below its risk threshold, ATR ratio not rising, bandwidth not rising, body-to-ATR below its risk threshold, confirmed volatility score below its compression threshold, and neither breakout being recent, minus fixed reductions for higher-timeframe score >= the expansion threshold, absolute setup-score difference >= the strong-dominance threshold, either directional trend aligned with its dominance, and an aligned trend and dominance also having a recent breakout in that direction. Very-high risk and high risk use distinct thresholds, with the very-high threshold higher.
Calculation: Directional ignition points = one point each for ATR ratio rising, bandwidth rising, the respective impulse, respective momentum acceleration, recent respective breakout, fast volatility score >= the early threshold, close on the respective side of the shorter EMA, and compression exit.
Calculation: Release quality = for each direction, sum fixed points for fast volatility score >= fast-breakout threshold, confirmed volatility score >= expansion threshold, ATR ratio accelerating upward, bandwidth accelerating upward, relative volume >= fast-breakout threshold, recent respective breakout, respective direction, and respective dominance; the first two conditions carry greater weights. Scale and cap each directional total, select the score of the higher setup score or the greater quality score on a setup tie, add a fixed bonus if the last completed higher-timeframe score >= expansion threshold or subtract a fixed penalty if it < compression threshold, and clip to [0, 100]. Apply no higher-timeframe adjustment when confirmation is disabled.
Calculation: Cycle state = process each confirmed chart bar sequentially, initially neutral, with no previous ignition or directional signal, no continuation fired, and no macro lock. First clear a bullish macro lock when bearish direction AND bearish dominance hold, or clear a bearish lock when bullish direction AND bullish dominance hold. Reset the cycle to neutral and clear its continuation flag when fast and confirmed scores remain below their separate reset thresholds for the required consecutive bars, neither breakout is recent, and the minimum age since ignition has elapsed. Next, a bullish invalidation occurs in a bearish cycle when bullish setup >= reversal threshold AND bullish dominance AND recent bullish breakout AND fast score >= early threshold AND risk is below very high; bearish invalidation is symmetric. An invalidation resets the cycle and continuation flag and prevents arming on that bar. Then arm a neutral cycle in a direction when its signal cooldown has elapsed AND fast score >= early threshold AND its setup >= arming threshold AND its dominance holds AND its momentum acceleration OR recent breakout OR direction holds. Then ignition can occur on the same bar: the armed direction's cooldown must have elapsed AND its dominance must hold AND risk must be below very high AND either (its ignition points >= ignition threshold AND (its breakout is recent OR compression exit occurs OR fast score crosses above the early threshold)) OR (fast score >= fast-breakout threshold AND its setup >= fast-ignition threshold AND relative volume >= fast-breakout threshold AND its breakout is recent). Ignition records its bar and the direction's signal bar and clears the continuation flag. An ignited cycle releases when confirmed score >= expansion threshold AND its setup >= opposite setup AND its direction OR recent breakout holds; a release immediately advances to expansion on that bar. A directional cycle advances to extreme when confirmed score crosses above the extreme threshold. A continuation sets its fired flag and locks its direction; the lock persists through resets until the opposite directional trend and dominance coincide. Evaluate reset, invalidation, arming, ignition, release, continuation, expansion, and extreme in that order; preserve cycle direction until reset or invalidation.

Short: Bearish direction AND bullish direction is false OR bearish dominance AND bullish direction and bullish dominance are false OR recent bearish breakout AND recent bullish breakout is false OR bearish cycle direction OR bearish ignition points > bullish ignition points.
