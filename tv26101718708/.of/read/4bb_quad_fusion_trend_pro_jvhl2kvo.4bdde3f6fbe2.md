Calculation: Bollinger Bands = simple moving average of close ± multiplier × standard deviation of close over the same lookback; use the last completed bar on each referenced timeframe (daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour), whether that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Upper trendline state = reset to 0 when a confirmed pivot high occurs; otherwise set to 1 when close is above the projected upper line, else retain the prior state. The line resets to the latest confirmed pivot high and otherwise declines each bar by ATR over the pivot lookback, multiplied by a slope factor.
Calculation: Lower trendline state = reset to 0 when a confirmed pivot low occurs; otherwise set to 1 when close is below the projected lower line, else retain the prior state. The line resets to the latest confirmed pivot low and otherwise rises each bar by ATR over the pivot lookback, multiplied by a slope factor.
Long: Upper trendline state changes from 0 to 1 OR (Monday or Tuesday AND the last completed weekly bar closes above its open AND its body is at least 60% of its high-low range) OR (bullish Bollinger event on at least one of the daily, 4-hour, or 1-hour bars AND no bearish Bollinger event on those bars) OR (bullish Bollinger event on at least one of those bars AND no bearish Bollinger event on those bars)
Short: Lower trendline state changes from 0 to 1 OR (Monday or Tuesday AND (the last completed weekly bar closes below its open OR its upper wick is at least 1.5 times its body)) OR (bearish Bollinger event on at least one of the daily, 4-hour, or 1-hour bars AND no bullish Bollinger event on those bars) OR (bearish Bollinger event on at least one of those bars AND no bullish Bollinger event on those bars)
