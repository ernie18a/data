Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars to its left and confirmed by the specified number of bars to its right; use it only from its confirmation bar onward. Resistance/support is the latest confirmed pivot high/low; previous resistance/support is the preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Resistance/support zone = the corresponding pivot level multiplied by one plus or minus a zone-tolerance fraction.
Calculation: Volume delta = the rolling sum of volume on bars with close >= open minus the rolling sum of volume on bars with close < open, over the same lookback as the volume SMA.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; range = high - low.
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = lower wick >= body times a wick-ratio parameter AND lower wick >= twice the upper wick AND body > 0 AND range > 0; bearish pin bar reverses upper and lower wick.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close <= previous open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close AND body >= previous body times a minimum-body-ratio parameter; bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close >= previous open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close AND body >= previous body times the same parameter.
Calculation: Long stop and targets = fixed levels calculated on the signal bar from its close and ATR. Stop is support times one minus a buffer fraction if pivot stops are enabled and support < signal close, otherwise signal close minus ATR times a stop multiplier. First target is resistance if pivot targets are enabled and resistance > signal close, otherwise signal close plus ATR times a first-target multiplier. Second target is previous resistance if pivot targets are enabled and previous resistance > first target, otherwise signal close plus ATR times a second-target multiplier; if the selected second target <= first target, use that same ATR fallback.
Calculation: Short stop and targets = fixed levels calculated on the signal bar from its close and ATR. Stop is resistance times one plus a buffer fraction if pivot stops are enabled and resistance > signal close, otherwise signal close plus ATR times the stop multiplier. First target is support if pivot targets are enabled and support < signal close, otherwise signal close minus ATR times the first-target multiplier. Second target is previous support if pivot targets are enabled and previous support < first target, otherwise signal close minus ATR times the second-target multiplier; if the selected second target >= first target, use that same ATR fallback.
Long: ((pin bars enabled AND bullish pin bar AND support-zone lower bound <= low AND low <= support-zone upper bound) OR (engulfing enabled AND bullish engulfing AND support-zone lower bound <= low AND low <= support-zone upper bound) OR (breakouts enabled AND close > resistance-zone upper bound AND previous close <= previous resistance-zone upper bound)) AND (EMA filter disabled OR (shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND abs(shorter-period EMA - longer-period EMA) / longer-period EMA >= minimum EMA separation fraction AND close > shorter-period EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < upper RSI threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume > volume SMA times a volume threshold AND volume delta > 0)) AND (strength filter disabled OR (body >= ATR times a minimum-body multiplier AND body / range >= minimum body fraction AND range > 0 AND volume > volume SMA times a spike multiplier AND (confirmation disabled OR previous close > previous open))).
Short: ((pin bars enabled AND bearish pin bar AND resistance-zone lower bound <= high AND high <= resistance-zone upper bound) OR (engulfing enabled AND bearish engulfing AND resistance-zone lower bound <= high AND high <= resistance-zone upper bound) OR (breakouts enabled AND close < support-zone lower bound AND previous close >= previous support-zone lower bound)) AND (EMA filter disabled OR (shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND abs(shorter-period EMA - longer-period EMA) / longer-period EMA >= minimum EMA separation fraction AND close < shorter-period EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > lower RSI threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close <= session VWAP of typical price) AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume > volume SMA times a volume threshold AND volume delta < 0)) AND (strength filter disabled OR (body >= ATR times a minimum-body multiplier AND body / range >= minimum body fraction AND range > 0 AND volume > volume SMA times a spike multiplier AND (confirmation disabled OR previous close < previous open))).
Long: shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND abs(shorter-period EMA - longer-period EMA) / longer-period EMA >= minimum EMA separation fraction.
Short: shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND abs(shorter-period EMA - longer-period EMA) / longer-period EMA >= minimum EMA separation fraction.
Long: RSI < lower RSI threshold.
Short: RSI > upper RSI threshold.
Long: close > session VWAP of typical price.
Short: close <= session VWAP of typical price.
Long: volume delta > 0.
Short: volume delta <= 0.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long first target for the configured partial position OR high >= long second target for the remaining position.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short first target for the configured partial position OR low <= short second target for the remaining position.
