Calculation: FairPrice = midpoint of the candle body, (max(open,close)+min(open,close))/2, of the chart-timeframe bar containing 09:29 New York time on the current day; held fixed for the rest of that day
Calculation: Band = a fixed stretch distance in price points; UpperBand = FairPrice + Band, LowerBand = FairPrice - Band
Calculation: InWindow = bar's New York time is between 09:29 and a later session end time (inclusive) on the same day AND FairPrice is already captured for the day AND no Long or Short signal has already fired earlier that day
Long: InWindow AND close <= LowerBand AND close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous close AND bar is completed AND (optional filters, each applied only if enabled: close > LowerBand; volume > simple moving average of volume over a lookback including the current bar; RSI(close) < a low threshold; abs(first open of the day - prior day's last close at or after 15:55 New York time) <= gap threshold; 09:29 bar's body low >= FairPrice - Band AND body high <= FairPrice + Band)
Short: InWindow AND close >= UpperBand AND close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous close AND bar is completed AND (optional filters, each applied only if enabled: close < UpperBand; volume > simple moving average of volume over a lookback including the current bar; RSI(close) > 100 - the same low threshold used for Long; abs(first open of the day - prior day's last close at or after 15:55 New York time) <= gap threshold; 09:29 bar's body low >= FairPrice - Band AND body high <= FairPrice + Band)
