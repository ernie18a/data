Calculation: Upper and lower liquidity levels = Maintain separate ordered sets. For each of a short, medium, and long lookback, calculate the rolling highest high and lowest low. On the first available value, create the corresponding upper or lower level. Subsequently create an upper level when its rolling high exceeds its value a comparison lookback ago and equals its previous-bar value; create a lower level when its rolling low is below its value a comparison lookback ago and equals its previous-bar value. Round each new level to a configurable price increment. If an existing same-side level is within a configurable merge tolerance, retain the nearest existing price and reset its birth bar; if two are equally near, choose the level at the insertion position in descending upper-price or ascending lower-price order. Otherwise insert the new level with the current birth bar. On each completed bar, evaluate decisions using the sets from the preceding bar, then remove upper levels at or below the current high, or current close under the alternative removal setting, and lower levels at or above the current low, or current close under that setting. At a configurable periodic cleanup interval, also remove levels whose age exceeds a configurable maximum. Then add or refresh the current bar's new levels in short, medium, long order, separately for each side.
Calculation: Upper body crossings = Upper levels strictly between open and close when close > open; otherwise an empty set.
Calculation: Lower body crossings = Lower levels strictly between close and open when close < open; otherwise an empty set.
Calculation: Upper mass liquidation = Upper body crossing count is at least a configurable threshold AND (open + close) / 2 > the lowest upper body crossing.
Calculation: Lower mass liquidation = Lower body crossing count is at least a configurable threshold AND (open + close) / 2 < the highest lower body crossing.
Long: Lower mass liquidation = true
Short: Upper mass liquidation = true
Long: At least one lower liquidity level < min(open, close) AND that level >= low AND upper mass liquidation = false AND lower mass liquidation = false
Short: At least one upper liquidity level > max(open, close) AND that level <= high AND upper mass liquidation = false AND lower mass liquidation = false
