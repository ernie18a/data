Calculation: Active upper and lower liquidity levels = rolling highest highs and lowest lows over fast, medium, and slow lookbacks; add a level when its rolling extreme exceeds (upper) or falls below (lower) its value 3 bars earlier and equals its prior-bar value; merge levels within 2 minimum price increments; remove mitigated or expired levels.
Long: Confirmed bar AND ((low < min(open, close) AND an active lower level is >= low and < min(open, close) AND no mass liquidation) OR (close > open AND at least 2 active upper levels are strictly between open and close AND (open + close) / 2 is above the lowest crossed level))
Short: Confirmed bar AND ((high > max(open, close) AND an active upper level is > max(open, close) and <= high AND no mass liquidation) OR (close < open AND at least 2 active lower levels are strictly between close and open AND (open + close) / 2 is below the highest crossed level))
Exit: short, Confirmed bar AND close > open AND at least 2 active upper levels are strictly between open and close AND (open + close) / 2 is above the lowest crossed level
Exit: long, Confirmed bar AND close < open AND at least 2 active lower levels are strictly between close and open AND (open + close) / 2 is below the highest crossed level
