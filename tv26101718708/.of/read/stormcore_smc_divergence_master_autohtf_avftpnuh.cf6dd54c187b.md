Calculation: SuperTrend state = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, smooth true range with SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, or WMA(true range × volume) / WMA(volume); form basic upper and lower bands as (high + low) / 2 plus and minus a multiplier times the smoothed true range. Initialize the state bullish and bands to their basic values. While bearish, count consecutive closes above the retained upper band; on reaching the confirmation count, switch bullish and reset the lower band to its basic value. While bullish, count consecutive closes below the retained lower band; on reaching the confirmation count, switch bearish and reset the upper band to its basic value. Otherwise, raise the bullish lower band to the greater of its prior value and its basic value, or lower the bearish upper band to the lesser of its prior value and its basic value. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. Compare the resulting state on consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: Structure setup = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, track the latest confirmed pivot high and low and a direction initialized bullish. Switch bearish when the close is below the latest pivot low while bullish; switch bullish when the close is above the latest pivot high while bearish. At a bearish switch, find the most recent prior bearish fair value gap within a lookback, where high[i] < low[i+2] AND close[i+1] < open[i+1], and the most recent bullish candle within that lookback whose time is no later than the latest pivot high. At a bullish switch, find the most recent prior bullish fair value gap, where low[i] > high[i+2] AND close[i+1] > open[i+1], and the most recent bearish candle within the lookback whose time is no later than the latest pivot low. A setup exists only if both searches succeed; emit it only if its order-block candle time differs from the previous emitted setup.
Calculation: Divergence = Use close or high/low as the pivot source, with equal left and right pivot periods; evaluate on the confirmation bar. Store the most recent configured maximum number of confirmed highs and lows. For each enabled oscillator, scan prior same-side pivots newest first, stopping beyond the maximum separation and requiring a separation greater than a minimum. Compare the oscillator at the new pivot with its value at the prior pivot; compare close, or (high + low) / 2 in high/low mode, at the new pivot with the stored prior pivot price. Between the pivots, both the comparison price series and oscillator must stay on or above their respective endpoint-to-endpoint straight lines for low pivots, or on or below them for high pivots. For each oscillator, stop at its first qualifying prior pivot, testing regular before hidden divergence. Count oscillators by divergence type and identical prior pivot; require the count to reach the minimum. Oscillators are MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, SMA-smoothed stochastic, CCI of close, close momentum, OBV, short-period VWMA(close) minus long-period VWMA(close), Chaikin money flow using a denominator of one when high equals low, and MFI of close; MACD uses a shorter and longer EMA plus a signal EMA. An external source is usable only if it can be derived from the permitted data. Automatic pivot-period mode disables divergence on intraday charts.
Long: SuperTrend state changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: SuperTrend state changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: A bullish structure setup exists.
Short: A bearish structure setup exists.
Long: A confirmed low-pivot divergence has comparison price < prior pivot price AND oscillator > its value at the prior pivot, OR comparison price > prior pivot price AND oscillator < its value at the prior pivot.
Short: A confirmed high-pivot divergence has comparison price > prior pivot price AND oscillator < its value at the prior pivot, OR comparison price < prior pivot price AND oscillator > its value at the prior pivot.
