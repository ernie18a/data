Calculation: Weighted moving average = over the range lookback, average each close weighted by its absolute change from the preceding close divided by that preceding close; use the current close if total weight is zero.
Calculation: Range active = the maximum absolute distance of closes over the range lookback from the weighted moving average is no greater than ATR times the adaptive factor; the adaptive factor is the base multiplier times the ratio of recent close-change volatility to its average, clamped to its configured bounds, or the base multiplier when adaptation is disabled. On the first active bar, set range top and bottom to the weighted moving average plus and minus ATR times the adaptive factor; retain them while the range remains active.
Calculation: Active bullish breakout = becomes active on a long breakout condition and remains active until the range is inactive and close is at or below the lower main regression band, or at or above the upper main regression band multiplied by the reset factor.
Calculation: Active bearish breakout = becomes active on a short breakout condition and remains active until the range is inactive and close is at or above the upper main regression band, or at or below the lower main regression band multiplied by the reset factor.
Long: (close crosses above the upper main linear regression channel band OR close crosses above the range top while the range is active OR close crosses above a resistance level whose strength meets the signal threshold) AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND volume confirmation is disabled or current volume meets its configured multiple of average volume; OR active bullish breakout AND close is at or below the main channel midline AND above the lower main regression band.
Short: (close crosses below the lower main linear regression channel band OR close crosses below the range bottom while the range is active OR close crosses below a support level whose strength meets the signal threshold) AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND volume confirmation is disabled or current volume meets its configured multiple of average volume; OR active bearish breakout AND close is at or above the main channel midline AND below the upper main regression band.
