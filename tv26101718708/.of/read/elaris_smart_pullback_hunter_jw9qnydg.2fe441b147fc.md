Calculation: Fast, mid, and slow EMA = EMAs of chart-timeframe close with progressively longer lengths.
Calculation: EMA slope = 100 × (fast EMA − fast EMA at the slope lookback) / fast EMA at the slope lookback; use 0 if that prior EMA is 0.
Calculation: Bullish trend = fast EMA > mid EMA AND mid EMA > slow EMA AND EMA slope >= minimum positive slope AND (structure filter disabled OR low > lowest low over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar).
Calculation: Bearish trend = fast EMA < mid EMA AND mid EMA < slow EMA AND EMA slope <= negative minimum slope AND (structure filter disabled OR high < highest high over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar).
Calculation: Long pullback = bullish trend AND ((fast pullback enabled AND low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA) OR (mid pullback enabled AND low <= mid EMA AND close > mid EMA) OR (low < mid EMA AND close > mid EMA AND 100 × |low − mid EMA| / mid EMA <= maximum deep-pullback percentage)).
Calculation: Short pullback = bearish trend AND ((fast pullback enabled AND high >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA) OR (mid pullback enabled AND high >= mid EMA AND close < mid EMA) OR (high > mid EMA AND close < mid EMA AND 100 × |high − mid EMA| / mid EMA <= maximum deep-pullback percentage)).
Calculation: Body percentage = 100 × |close − open| / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Lower wick percentage = 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Upper wick percentage = 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish alignment = fast EMA > mid EMA AND mid EMA > slow EMA, calculated on higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one that completes with the current chart bar; EMA lengths match their chart-timeframe counterparts.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish alignment = fast EMA < mid EMA AND mid EMA < slow EMA, calculated on higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one that completes with the current chart bar; EMA lengths match their chart-timeframe counterparts.
Calculation: Long signal score = min(100, 45 + 20 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA + 15 if EMA slope >= minimum positive slope + 10 if structure filter disabled OR low > lowest low over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar + pullback points). Pullback points are 20 if low <= mid EMA AND close > mid EMA; otherwise 14 if low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA; otherwise 12 if low < mid EMA AND close > mid EMA AND 100 × |low − mid EMA| / mid EMA <= maximum deep-pullback percentage; otherwise 0. The base 45 applies only when the long confirmation and higher-timeframe conditions below are satisfied.
Calculation: Short signal score = min(100, 45 + 20 if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA + 15 if EMA slope <= negative minimum slope + 10 if structure filter disabled OR high < highest high over the pullback lookback ending on the previous bar + pullback points). Pullback points are 20 if high >= mid EMA AND close < mid EMA; otherwise 14 if high >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA; otherwise 12 if high > mid EMA AND close < mid EMA AND 100 × |high − mid EMA| / mid EMA <= maximum deep-pullback percentage; otherwise 0. The base 45 applies only when the short confirmation and higher-timeframe conditions below are satisfied.
Long: Bullish trend = true.
Short: Bearish trend = true.
Long: A long pullback occurred within the maximum pullback age, including the current bar.
Short: A short pullback occurred within the maximum pullback age, including the current bar.
Long: A long pullback occurred within the maximum pullback age, including the current bar AND close > open AND (close-back filter disabled OR close > fast EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick percentage >= minimum rejection wick percentage) AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish alignment = true) AND long signal score >= configured minimum score, or >= 90 when the A+ only option is enabled.
Short: A short pullback occurred within the maximum pullback age, including the current bar AND close < open AND (close-back filter disabled OR close < fast EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick percentage >= minimum rejection wick percentage) AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish alignment = true) AND short signal score >= configured minimum score, or >= 90 when the A+ only option is enabled.
