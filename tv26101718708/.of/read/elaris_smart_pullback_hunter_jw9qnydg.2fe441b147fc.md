Calculation: Fast EMA slope % = (current fast EMA − fast EMA slope-lookback bars ago) / fast EMA slope-lookback bars ago × 100; if the earlier EMA is zero, use 0
Calculation: Long signal score = capped-at-100 weighted sum of: bullish EMA stack, positive slope, bullish structure (or true when structure filter is disabled), pullback type (mid-EMA touch takes precedence over fast-EMA touch, which takes precedence over deep pullback), volume confirmation (or true when disabled), bullish candle with sufficient body, and higher-timeframe EMA alignment (or true when disabled)
Calculation: Short signal score = capped-at-100 weighted sum of: bearish EMA stack, negative slope, bearish structure (or true when structure filter is disabled), pullback type (mid-EMA touch takes precedence over fast-EMA touch, which takes precedence over deep pullback), volume confirmation (or true when disabled), bearish candle with sufficient body, and higher-timeframe EMA alignment (or true when disabled)
Long: fast EMA > mid EMA AND mid EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA slope % ≥ positive minimum slope AND (structure filter disabled OR current low > the prior bar's lowest low over the pullback lookback) on a bar in the current or previous maximum pullback-age bars where (enabled fast-EMA pullback: low ≤ fast EMA AND close > fast EMA OR enabled mid-EMA pullback: low ≤ mid EMA AND close > mid EMA OR deep pullback: low < mid EMA AND close > mid EMA AND distance from low to mid EMA ≤ configured maximum percentage) AND current bar is confirmed AND close > open AND (close-back-above-fast-EMA requirement disabled OR close > fast EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × configured multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick as a percentage of bar range ≥ configured minimum) AND body as a percentage of bar range ≥ configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA > mid EMA AND mid EMA > slow EMA; use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming) AND (score meets configured minimum, or configured A+ threshold when A+ only is enabled)
Short: fast EMA < mid EMA AND mid EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA slope % ≤ negative minimum slope AND (structure filter disabled OR current high < the prior bar's highest high over the pullback lookback) on a bar in the current or previous maximum pullback-age bars where (enabled fast-EMA pullback: high ≥ fast EMA AND close < fast EMA OR enabled mid-EMA pullback: high ≥ mid EMA AND close < mid EMA OR deep pullback: high > mid EMA AND close < mid EMA AND distance from high to mid EMA ≤ configured maximum percentage) AND current bar is confirmed AND close < open AND (close-back-below-fast-EMA requirement disabled OR close < fast EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × configured multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick as a percentage of bar range ≥ configured minimum) AND body as a percentage of bar range ≥ configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA < mid EMA AND mid EMA < slow EMA; use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming) AND (score meets configured minimum, or configured A+ threshold when A+ only is enabled)
