Calculation: FracDiffClose = sum over k=0..n of w_k * close[k], with w_0 = 1 and w_k = -w_(k-1) * (d - k + 1) / k for a fractional order d between 0 and 1; weights stop at the first k where |w_k| falls below a small threshold or k reaches a maximum window
Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close
Calculation: PivotLow(RSI) = RSI value R bars ago is the lowest RSI over the L bars before it and the R bars after it, confirmed on the current bar. PivotHigh(RSI) is the mirror case with highest.
Long: FracDiffClose crosses above its simple moving average over a lookback (previous FracDiffClose <= previous average AND current FracDiffClose > current average) OR (PivotLow(RSI) is confirmed on the current bar AND RSI at this pivot > RSI at the previous confirmed RSI pivot low AND the number of bars since the previous pivot low was confirmed is between a minimum and a maximum AND low at this pivot bar < low at the previous pivot low bar)
Short: FracDiffClose crosses below its simple moving average over the same lookback (previous FracDiffClose >= previous average AND current FracDiffClose < current average) OR (PivotHigh(RSI) is confirmed on the current bar AND RSI at this pivot < RSI at the previous confirmed RSI pivot high AND the number of bars since the previous pivot high was confirmed is between the same minimum and maximum AND high at this pivot bar > high at the previous pivot high bar)
