Calculation: Normalized candle open and close = apply the same rolling Z-score and sigmoid normalization to the differenced open and close. Differenced close is the fractional difference of close, using weights w₀=1 and wₖ=−wₖ₋₁(d−k+1)/k until the weight threshold or maximum lag count is reached; differenced open = differenced close − (close−open). When differencing is disabled or not warmed up, use raw open and close. For each value, normalize as 100/(1+exp(−z/(scale/2))), where z=(value−rolling mean of differenced close)/max(rolling standard deviation of differenced close, 0.0001).
Long: Normalized candle close >= normalized candle open OR normalized candle close crosses above 50 OR a confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the previous RSI pivot low while the corresponding price low is lower than the previous price pivot low, with pivot separation within the configured bounds
Short: Normalized candle close < normalized candle open OR normalized candle close crosses below 50 OR a confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the previous RSI pivot high while the corresponding price high is higher than the previous price pivot high, with pivot separation within the configured bounds
