Calculation: Requested-timeframe value = evaluate the indicator on its requested timeframe; for a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar in each chart bar.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = simple moving average of 100 × (RSI of the selected price source − lowest RSI) / (highest RSI − lowest RSI), using a stochastic lookback and a separate smoothing length; calculate RSI and K on the same requested timeframe and apply requested-timeframe value mapping.
Long: No open position AND bar datetime is after the backtest start and before the backtest end AND both the shorter-timeframe and longer-timeframe Supertrends indicate an uptrend AND Stochastic RSI K crossed above its lower threshold on at least one of the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Short: No open position AND bar datetime is after the backtest start and before the backtest end AND both the shorter-timeframe and longer-timeframe Supertrends indicate a downtrend AND Stochastic RSI K crossed below its upper threshold on at least one of the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Exit: long, close < shorter-timeframe Supertrend line.
Exit: short, close > shorter-timeframe Supertrend line.
Calculation: Both Supertrends use the same ATR length and factor and apply requested-timeframe value mapping; their requested timeframes may each be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and the longer timeframe is longer than the shorter timeframe. The Stochastic RSI requested timeframe may also be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
