Calculation: Averaging ladder = For averaging order k, the deviation above the base entry is the sum of the first k steps of a geometric progression starting at the first deviation and multiplied by the deviation step multiplier; its quote size is the first averaging-order size multiplied by the order-size multiplier to the power k−1. Fill at the chart bar close, at most one order per bar, in ascending order, including on the base-entry bar.
Calculation: Average entry = Sum of the base and filled averaging-order quote sizes divided by the sum of each quote size divided by its fill price; include any averaging order filled on the current bar before evaluating exit.
Short: Within the optional inclusive date window AND flat AND RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below its threshold AND RSI on the preceding higher-timeframe bar was at or above the threshold; forward-fill this higher-timeframe crossunder result until the next higher-timeframe bar completes.
Short: Within the optional inclusive date window AND short position is open AND the number of filled averaging orders is less than the configured maximum AND close is at or above the base entry multiplied by one plus the next averaging ladder deviation.
Exit: short, within the optional inclusive date window AND close is at or below average entry multiplied by one minus the take-profit fraction.
