Calculation: DCX = 100 × sqrt((DX / 100) × ((1 − convexity weight) × efficiency + convexity weight × (convexity + 1) / 2)), where DX = 100 × abs(+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI); +DI and −DI are smoothed positive and negative directional movement divided by smoothed true range; efficiency = clamp(abs(close − close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close change), lookback), efficiency floor, 1); convexity = (smoothed positive second close change − smoothed negative second close change) / their sum. Use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: AVE upper/lower = midpoint ± adaptive multiplier × blended volatility; midpoint is the selected ALMA, HMA, or EMA of hlc3; blended volatility = w × TR percentile + (1 − w) × ATR, where w = clamp((stdev(TR) / EMA(TR)) / volatility reference, 0, 1); adaptive multiplier = base multiplier × (1 + regime adaptation × (2 × clamp(DCX / 100, 0, 1) − 1)). Use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Initial long stop = min(AVE lower band, entry price − stop multiplier × Weekly ATR); initial short stop = max(AVE upper band, entry price + stop multiplier × Weekly ATR). Weekly ATR uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + target R multiple × abs(entry price − initial long stop); short target = entry price − target R multiple × abs(entry price − initial short stop).
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(AVE lower band, average entry price − Weekly ATR); short trailing stop = min(AVE upper band, average entry price + Weekly ATR). Weekly ATR uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: chart-timeframe close > AVE upper band AND DCX > threshold AND DCX is rising over its lookback AND +DI > −DI AND DXY daily close < its daily EMA. DXY uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar when the chart timeframe is below daily; omit the DXY condition when its filter is disabled.
Short: chart-timeframe close < AVE lower band AND DCX > threshold AND DCX is falling over its lookback AND −DI > +DI AND DXY daily close > its daily EMA. DXY uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar when the chart timeframe is below daily; omit the DXY condition when its filter is disabled.
Exit: long, low ≤ initial long stop OR high ≥ long target OR (trailing enabled AND low ≤ long trailing stop)
Exit: short, high ≥ initial short stop OR low ≤ short target OR (trailing enabled AND high ≥ short trailing stop)
