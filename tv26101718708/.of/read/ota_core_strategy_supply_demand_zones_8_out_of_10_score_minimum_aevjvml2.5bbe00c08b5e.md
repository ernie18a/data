Calculation: Candidate zone = scan offsets from the minimum leg length through lookback minus maximum base length minus minimum leg length minus two, in ascending order; for each offset scan base lengths from shortest to longest and leg lengths from shortest to the minimum leg length, retaining only candidates whose base ends inside the lookback. The leg starts at the offset and the base immediately precedes it. Each leg bar must close above its open for demand or below its open for supply, and its absolute body divided by its positive close must be at least the minimum leg-move fraction. The average base body must be less than the average leg body divided by the leg-to-base body ratio, and every base body must be less than a separate fraction of the average leg body. Demand proximal and distal are the maximum base body high and minimum base low; supply proximal and distal are the minimum base body low and maximum base high.
Calculation: Zone tests = count of bars from the bar after the leg's most recent bar through the current bar inclusive whose low is at or below the zone top and whose high is at or above the zone bottom. A demand zone is mitigated if any of those bars closes below its distal; a supply zone is mitigated if any closes above its distal.
Calculation: Zone score = sum of points for base length at or below each of two increasing length thresholds; average leg body divided by average base body at or above each of two decreasing ratio thresholds, using zero if the average base body is zero; zero or one zone test; the most recent leg bar's close position within its range at or above a threshold for demand or at or below a lower threshold for supply; that leg bar's volume above a multiple of the average volume over a preceding window ending immediately before the leg; and the sum of leg ranges above a multiple of current-bar ATR times leg length. The two base-length cases, two body-ratio cases, and two test-count cases each award mutually exclusive point amounts, with the more favorable case worth more. The close-position point requires a positive leg-bar range, the volume point a positive average volume, and the range point a positive ATR. Cap the sum at the maximum score.
Calculation: Eligible zones = candidate zones with score at least the minimum score, with mitigated zones included only if the corresponding option is enabled, and with tested zones included only if the fresh-only option is disabled. Retain the first configured maximum number of eligible zones per side in scan order.
Calculation: Rolling demand proximal = highest open or close over a rolling window including the current bar; rolling demand distal = lowest low over that window. Rolling supply proximal = lowest open or close over the same window; rolling supply distal = highest high over that window.
Long: The count of eligible demand zones is greater than zero.
Short: The count of eligible supply zones is greater than zero.
Long: Close is at or below rolling demand proximal AND close is at or above rolling demand distal.
Short: Close is at or above rolling supply proximal AND close is at or below rolling supply distal.
Long: Close is outside the rolling demand zone AND close is at or below rolling demand proximal times one plus the approach fraction AND close is at or above rolling demand distal AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these approach conditions using its own rolling levels.
Short: Close is outside the rolling supply zone AND close is at or above rolling supply proximal times one minus the approach fraction AND close is at or below rolling supply distal AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these approach conditions using its own rolling levels.
Exit: long, close is below the distal of the associated demand zone.
Exit: short, close is above the distal of the associated supply zone.
