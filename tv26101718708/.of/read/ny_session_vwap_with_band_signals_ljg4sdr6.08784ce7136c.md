Calculation: SessionVWAP = cumulative sum(typical price * volume) / cumulative sum(volume), typical price = (high+low+close)/3, accumulated from the first bar of the New York regular session each day and reset at the next session open; undefined outside the session.
Calculation: Band = SessionVWAP +/- k * volume-weighted standard deviation, where the deviation = sqrt(cumulative sum(volume * (typical price - current SessionVWAP)^2) / cumulative sum(volume)) over the same session accumulation; Upper = SessionVWAP + k*deviation, Lower = SessionVWAP - k*deviation.
Calculation: ClosePos = (close - low) / (high - low), taken as 0.5 when high = low.
Long: bar time (New York time) is after a fixed delay from the regular session open and before the session close AND low - P <= SessionVWAP <= high + P (P = proximity distance in price units) AND close > open AND close > Upper AND ClosePos >= T (T = close-position threshold, a fraction near the high) AND low > Lower
Short: bar time (New York time) is after a fixed delay from the regular session open and before the session close AND low - P <= SessionVWAP <= high + P (P = proximity distance in price units) AND close < open AND close < Lower AND ClosePos <= 1 - T AND high < Upper
