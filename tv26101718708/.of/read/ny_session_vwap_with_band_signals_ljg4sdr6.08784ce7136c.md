Calculation: NY-session VWAP = cumulative sum(typical price × volume) since 09:30 ET ÷ cumulative volume; typical price = (high + low + close) ÷ 3; reset at 09:30 ET.
Calculation: VWAP bands = VWAP ± band-deviation parameter × standard deviation, where standard deviation = square root of cumulative sum((typical price − current VWAP)² × volume) ÷ cumulative volume; update the cumulative sum each bar using that bar’s typical price and current VWAP.
Long: bar time is from 09:30 ET plus the configured delay through 16:00 ET AND VWAP is available AND (absolute value of low − VWAP ≤ configured proximity distance OR absolute value of high − VWAP ≤ configured proximity distance OR low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP) AND close > upper VWAP band AND close > open AND (high = low OR 100 × (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≥ configured close-percentage threshold) AND low > lower VWAP band
Short: bar time is from 09:30 ET plus the configured delay through 16:00 ET AND VWAP is available AND (absolute value of low − VWAP ≤ configured proximity distance OR absolute value of high − VWAP ≤ configured proximity distance OR low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP) AND close < lower VWAP band AND close < open AND (high = low OR 100 × (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≤ 100 − configured close-percentage threshold) AND high < upper VWAP band
