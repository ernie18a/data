Calculation: Prior configured-timeframe high = high of the last completed bar on that timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Prior configured-timeframe low = low of the last completed bar on that timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe
Long: For any configured timeframe, close > its prior configured-timeframe high AND previous chart-bar close <= that high
Short: For any configured timeframe, close < its prior configured-timeframe low AND previous chart-bar close >= that low
