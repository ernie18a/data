Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars, using configurable left-hand and right-hand bar counts; retain the latest two confirmed pivots of each kind and their pivot-bar indices.
Calculation: Liquidity high/low = the high/low of the previous completed exchange day, the previous completed selected session, or the first configurable number of chart bars in the current trading session; the last choice is available only after that range is complete.
Calculation: Sweep distance = maximum of a configurable price distance and ATR multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low breaches liquidity low by more than the sweep distance AND close returns above the current liquidity low within a configurable number of bars; each new breach resets the breach bar. Bearish sweep = high breaches liquidity high by more than the sweep distance AND close returns below the current liquidity high within a configurable number of bars; each new breach resets the breach bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high. Bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low. Store the swing level at each respective break.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = selected pattern is double bottom AND a new confirmed swing low is separated from the preceding swing low by at least a configurable number of bars AND their absolute price difference <= ATR times a configurable factor; OR selected pattern is range breakout AND the highest high minus lowest low of a configurable number of preceding bars <= ATR times a configurable factor AND close > that highest high; OR selected pattern is wedge AND the latest swing high < preceding swing high AND latest swing low > preceding swing low AND close > latest swing high. Bearish pattern = selected pattern is double top AND a new confirmed swing high is separated from the preceding swing high by at least a configurable number of bars AND their absolute price difference <= ATR times a configurable factor; OR selected pattern is range breakout AND the highest high minus lowest low of a configurable number of preceding bars <= ATR times a configurable factor AND close < that lowest low; OR selected pattern is wedge AND the latest swing high < preceding swing high AND latest swing low > preceding swing low AND close < latest swing low.
Calculation: Bullish EMA break = close crosses above the short EMA AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, short EMA > previous short EMA. Bearish EMA break = close crosses below the short EMA AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, short EMA < previous short EMA.
Calculation: Bullish qualification = bullish structure break occurs within a configurable number of bars after the latest bullish EMA break, or bullish EMA break occurs within that number of bars after the latest bullish structure break, according to the selected ordering; the events may occur on the same bar. If structure retest is required, a low <= stored bullish break level AND close > that level must already have occurred within a configurable number of bars after the latest bullish break, including the break bar. Bearish qualification is the corresponding bearish ordering, with retest high >= stored bearish break level AND close < that level. A retest after the base event does not itself create a qualification.
Calculation: Initial long stop = latest confirmed swing low minus a configurable price buffer, entry close minus ATR times a configurable factor, or entry close minus a configurable price distance, according to the selected stop method. Initial short stop = latest confirmed swing high plus the buffer, entry close plus ATR times the factor, or entry close plus the distance. Initial risk = absolute difference between entry close and initial stop.
Calculation: Long effective stop = initial long stop before the first target; afterward, maximum of initial long stop and entry price, additionally maximized with either the highest close minus ATR times a configurable factor observed since the first target, recalculated each bar, or the current short EMA when the corresponding trailing mode is selected. Short effective stop = initial short stop before the first target; afterward, minimum of initial short stop and entry price, additionally minimized with either the lowest close plus ATR times a configurable factor observed since the first target, recalculated each bar, or the current short EMA when the corresponding trailing mode is selected.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Bullish pattern.
Short: Bearish pattern.
Long: A bullish qualification occurred within its configurable validity window AND either a bullish sweep occurred within its configurable validity window OR no bullish sweep occurred within that window AND a bullish pattern occurred within its configurable validity window AND the selected entry mode is breakout close, or low <= short EMA AND close > short EMA for EMA retest mode, or low <= stored bullish break level AND close > that level for structure retest mode AND, if enabled, close > medium EMA AND, if enabled, close > long EMA AND, if enabled, short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND, if enabled, volume > volume SMA AND, if enabled, ATR > ATR SMA times a configurable factor AND, if enabled, the bar is in the selected trading session AND, if one-trade-at-a-time is enabled, position is flat AND initial risk > a configurable minimum price increment.
Short: A bearish qualification occurred within its configurable validity window AND either a bearish sweep occurred within its configurable validity window OR no bearish sweep occurred within that window AND a bearish pattern occurred within its configurable validity window AND the selected entry mode is breakout close, or high >= short EMA AND close < short EMA for EMA retest mode, or high >= stored bearish break level AND close < that level for structure retest mode AND, if enabled, close < medium EMA AND, if enabled, close < long EMA AND, if enabled, short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND, if enabled, volume > volume SMA AND, if enabled, ATR > ATR SMA times a configurable factor AND, if enabled, the bar is in the selected trading session AND, if one-trade-at-a-time is enabled, position is flat AND initial risk > a configurable minimum price increment.
Exit: Long, close >= entry price + initial risk AND first target has not previously been reached; exit a configurable fraction of the position.
Exit: Short, close <= entry price - initial risk AND first target has not previously been reached; exit a configurable fraction of the position.
Exit: Long, close <= long effective stop OR first target has been reached AND close >= entry price + a selected larger multiple of initial risk for a fixed target, or close >= the liquidity high captured at entry for the opposite-liquidity target.
Exit: Short, close >= short effective stop OR first target has been reached AND close <= entry price - a selected larger multiple of initial risk for a fixed target, or close <= the liquidity low captured at entry for the opposite-liquidity target.
