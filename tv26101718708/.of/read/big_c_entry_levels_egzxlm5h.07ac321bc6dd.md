Calculation: Opening-range levels = the running high and low during the opening-range session, reset each day and available after that session ends.
Calculation: Premarket levels = the running high and low during the premarket session, reset each day.
Calculation: Current-day open = the open of the first chart bar of the current day.
Calculation: Prior-period levels = the open, high, or low of the last completed daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly bar, as applicable. These periods are higher than the intraday chart timeframe.
Calculation: Historical extreme = the highest high or lowest low over a rolling lookback of monthly bars, including the current monthly bar; use the running chart-bar extreme if monthly data is unavailable. The monthly timeframe is higher than an intraday chart timeframe. Historical bars use the final value of the monthly bar even before it completes; the live bar uses the forming monthly bar.
Calculation: Break buffer = ATR multiplied by a nonnegative parameter; if ATR is unavailable, use close multiplied by a fallback parameter instead of ATR.
Calculation: Break cooldown = bars elapsed since the last signal for the same level and direction, with the last-signal bar initialized to bar index zero.
Long: An enabled level is the completed opening-range high, premarket high, current-day open, prior-day open, prior-day high, prior-week high, prior-month high, prior-quarter high, prior-year high, or historical highest high AND the level is available AND the session filter is disabled OR the current bar is in the regular session AND break cooldown is at least the cooldown parameter AND, according to the selected break mode, either high >= current level AND previous high < current level, OR close > current level AND previous close <= current level, OR close > current level + break buffer AND previous close <= current level.
Short: An enabled level is the completed opening-range low, premarket low, current-day open, prior-day open, prior-day low, prior-week low, prior-month low, prior-quarter low, prior-year low, or historical lowest low AND the level is available AND the session filter is disabled OR the current bar is in the regular session AND break cooldown is at least the cooldown parameter AND, according to the selected break mode, either low <= current level AND previous low > current level, OR close < current level AND previous close >= current level, OR close < current level - break buffer AND previous close >= current level.
