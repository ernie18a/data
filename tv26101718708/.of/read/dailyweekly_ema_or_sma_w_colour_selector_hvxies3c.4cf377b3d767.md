Short: An EMA or SMA of higher-timeframe closes in one slot is less than the EMA or SMA in the next slot; both slots are displayed and have independently configurable lengths and types.

Long: Close crosses above a selected EMA or SMA of higher-timeframe closes.
Short: Close crosses below a selected EMA or SMA of higher-timeframe closes.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA or SMA = EMA or SMA of higher-timeframe closes, using either the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the last completed higher-timeframe bar, according to the selected lookahead mode.
