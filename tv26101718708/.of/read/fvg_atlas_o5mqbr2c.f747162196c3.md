Calculation: Gap zones = For each chart timeframe or higher timeframe, a bullish fair value gap has bottom = high of the first of three consecutive bars and top = low of the third, when top > bottom; a bearish gap has bottom = high of the third bar and top = low of the first, when top > bottom. Require gap size >= minimum gap size. Detect either using the still-forming third bar or, at the start of the next timeframe bar, the last completed third bar.
Calculation: Auxiliary zones = At the start of a new chart or higher timeframe bar, use the last two completed bars. A bullish volume imbalance has bottom = max(open, close) of the older bar and top = min(open, close) of the newer bar; a bearish one has bottom = max(open, close) of the newer bar and top = min(open, close) of the older bar. Require top > bottom and overlapping wick ranges, including boundary contact. A bullish opening gap has bottom = older high and top = newer low; a bearish one has bottom = newer high and top = older low. Require top > bottom. Require zone size >= minimum gap size.
Calculation: Active zone = A confirmed gap or auxiliary zone remains active until the first selected invalidation: bullish low <= top or bearish high >= bottom for touch; bullish low <= midpoint or bearish high >= midpoint for midpoint; bullish low <= bottom or bearish high >= top for wick sweep; bullish close <= bottom or bearish close >= top for close beyond. Evaluate invalidation on chart bars, starting on the confirmation bar; retain only the configured maximum number of active zones per timeframe and type, evicting the oldest first.
Calculation: First-touch rejection = On an active zone's first near-edge touch, start an observation only if chart high >= zone bottom AND chart low <= zone top. Include the touch bar in the confirmation window. Stop after the first qualifying rejection, a bullish close <= zone bottom or bearish close >= zone top, or expiry of the window; invalidation of the zone does not stop the observation.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is detected.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is detected.
Long: A bullish volume imbalance or bullish opening gap is confirmed.
Short: A bearish volume imbalance or bearish opening gap is confirmed.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is confirmed AND its price range overlaps by a positive amount with a previously confirmed bearish fair value gap from the same timeframe within the pairing window, including an invalidated gap.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is confirmed AND its price range overlaps by a positive amount with a previously confirmed bullish fair value gap from the same timeframe within the pairing window, including an invalidated gap.
Long: Active confirmed bullish fair value gaps from at least the required number of distinct chart or higher timeframes overlap by a positive amount.
Short: Active confirmed bearish fair value gaps from at least the required number of distinct chart or higher timeframes overlap by a positive amount.
Long: A bearish fair value gap is invalidated.
Short: A bullish fair value gap is invalidated.
Long: A bullish zone has a pending first-touch observation AND chart close > zone top within the confirmation window.
Short: A bearish zone has a pending first-touch observation AND chart close < zone bottom within the confirmation window.
