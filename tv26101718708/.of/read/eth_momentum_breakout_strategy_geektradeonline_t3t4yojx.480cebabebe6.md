Calculation: Squeeze = lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel AND upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel; the Keltner Channel uses an SMA of close and ATR with the same lookback.
Calculation: Recent squeeze = the current bar is outside a squeeze AND the immediately preceding squeeze lasted at least the minimum duration AND the number of bars since its release is at most the maximum delay.
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression endpoint of close minus its SMA, using the same lookback for both.
Calculation: Regime filter = enabled higher-timeframe ADX is between its minimum and maximum inclusive AND enabled absolute percentage distance of chart close from the higher-timeframe long-term EMA is at most its maximum AND enabled higher-timeframe ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA is between its minimum and maximum inclusive. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for these calculations.
Calculation: Trend filter = compare chart close with the EMA plus or minus an ATR buffer on the selected trend timeframe; compare that EMA with its value a specified number of trend bars earlier when slope confirmation is enabled. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when the trend timeframe is higher, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar when it is lower.
Calculation: Entry regime = at the signal bar, hot if ATR percentile rank exceeds its upper threshold AND directional distance of close from the trend EMA in ATR units exceeds its upper threshold; otherwise cold if ATR percentile rank is below its lower threshold OR directional distance is below its lower threshold; otherwise normal. Directional distance is close minus EMA for long and EMA minus close for short. Lock the regime at entry; scale stop, profit-target, and trailing-distance multipliers up for hot and down for cold. If adaptive parameters are disabled, use normal.
Calculation: Partial filled = the absolute position size falls below a specified fraction of its initial size after the partial-profit order executes; retain this state until flat.
Calculation: Long stop = initialize at signal close minus entry ATR times the regime-scaled stop multiplier; on each position bar, take the maximum of the prior stop, entry price plus an entry-ATR offset once close minus entry price reaches the breakeven activation distance, and, if trailing is enabled, close minus current ATR times the regime-scaled trail multiplier. After partial filled, use the tighter trail multiplier and, if runner mode is enabled, also include the lowest low over the structure lookback in the maximum.
Calculation: Short stop = initialize at signal close plus entry ATR times the regime-scaled stop multiplier; on each position bar, take the minimum of the prior stop, entry price minus an entry-ATR offset once entry price minus close reaches the breakeven activation distance, and, if trailing is enabled, close plus current ATR times the regime-scaled trail multiplier. After partial filled, use the tighter trail multiplier and, if runner mode is enabled, also include the highest high over the structure lookback in the minimum.
Long: Position is flat AND long direction is enabled AND regime filter passes AND recent squeeze AND momentum > positive threshold AND momentum > previous momentum AND volume > volume SMA times spike multiplier AND close > upper Bollinger Band plus ATR breakout margin AND absolute candle body divided by range >= minimum body ratio, treating a zero range as zero AND enabled trend filter has close > trend EMA plus ATR buffer AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, trend EMA > its earlier value AND enabled RSI filter has RSI < upper threshold AND ATR divided by close >= minimum volatility fraction AND bars since full exit >= cooldown, reduced after a partially filled trade when smart cooldown is enabled AND enabled session filter admits the bar.
Short: Position is flat AND short direction is enabled AND regime filter passes AND recent squeeze AND momentum < negative threshold AND momentum < previous momentum AND volume > volume SMA times spike multiplier AND close < lower Bollinger Band minus ATR breakout margin AND absolute candle body divided by range >= minimum body ratio, treating a zero range as zero AND enabled trend filter has close < trend EMA minus ATR buffer AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, trend EMA < its earlier value AND enabled RSI filter has RSI > lower threshold AND ATR divided by close >= minimum volatility fraction AND bars since full exit >= cooldown, reduced after a partially filled trade when smart cooldown is enabled AND enabled session filter admits the bar.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= signal close plus entry ATR times the regime-scaled final profit multiplier AND runner mode with partial filled is not active.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= signal close minus entry ATR times the regime-scaled final profit multiplier AND runner mode with partial filled is not active.
Exit: Long, partial profit is enabled AND partial is not filled AND high >= signal close plus entry ATR times the regime-scaled first profit multiplier; exit the regime-selected fraction of the position.
Exit: Short, partial profit is enabled AND partial is not filled AND low <= signal close minus entry ATR times the regime-scaled first profit multiplier; exit the regime-selected fraction of the position.
Exit: Long, failed-breakout exit is enabled AND bars in trade are within the configured early-trade window AND close < the selected reference: upper Bollinger Band, Bollinger basis, or signal close.
Exit: Short, failed-breakout exit is enabled AND bars in trade are within the configured early-trade window AND close > the selected reference: lower Bollinger Band, Bollinger basis, or signal close.
Exit: Long, timeout is enabled AND bars in trade >= timeout AND partial is not filled OR stagnation exit is enabled AND bars in trade >= stagnation threshold AND maximum of zero and close minus entry price since entry < entry ATR times the stagnation progress multiplier.
Exit: Short, timeout is enabled AND bars in trade >= timeout AND partial is not filled OR stagnation exit is enabled AND bars in trade >= stagnation threshold AND maximum of zero and entry price minus close since entry < entry ATR times the stagnation progress multiplier.
