Calculation: Valid recent squeeze = Bollinger Bands are inside Keltner Channels for at least the minimum squeeze duration, then the squeeze releases within the maximum wait
Calculation: Breakout momentum = linear regression of close minus its simple moving average, using the same lookback; rising momentum is above its threshold and greater than its prior value, falling momentum is below the negative threshold and less than its prior value
Calculation: Daily volatility ratio = daily ATR divided by its longer-lookback average
Calculation: Trend confirmation = on the lower timeframe, close is above (below) the EMA by more than an ATR buffer, and the EMA is above (below) its value from a longer lookback; use the latest available lower-timeframe bar
Calculation: Adaptive regime = hot when ATR percentile rank is above its threshold and directional distance from the EMA, in ATR units, is above its threshold; cold when ATR percentile rank is below its threshold or that distance is below its threshold; otherwise normal
Long: position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed AND valid recent squeeze AND breakout momentum is rising AND volume > its moving average × spike threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band + ATR × breakout margin AND candle body / candle range ≥ minimum body fraction AND trend confirmation is bullish AND RSI < its upper limit AND ATR / close ≥ minimum volatility fraction AND absolute distance from close to the higher-timeframe EMA / EMA ≤ maximum distance AND higher-timeframe volatility ratio is within its minimum and maximum AND higher-timeframe ADX is within its minimum and maximum, if enabled
Short: position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed AND valid recent squeeze AND breakout momentum is falling AND volume > its moving average × spike threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band − ATR × breakout margin AND candle body / candle range ≥ minimum body fraction AND trend confirmation is bearish AND RSI > its lower limit AND ATR / close ≥ minimum volatility fraction AND absolute distance from close to the higher-timeframe EMA / EMA ≤ maximum distance AND higher-timeframe volatility ratio is within its minimum and maximum AND higher-timeframe ADX is within its minimum and maximum, if enabled
Calculation: Long stop = ratchet upward to the maximum of the prior stop, close − current ATR × trailing distance, the initial stop (entry price − entry ATR × regime-scaled stop distance), and, after favorable movement reaches its threshold, entry price + entry ATR × breakeven offset; after partial take-profit in runner mode, also use the higher of these and the recent N-bar low
Calculation: Short stop = ratchet downward to the minimum of the prior stop, close + current ATR × trailing distance, the initial stop (entry price + entry ATR × regime-scaled stop distance), and, after favorable movement reaches its threshold, entry price − entry ATR × breakeven offset; after partial take-profit in runner mode, also use the lower of these and the recent N-bar high
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × regime-scaled partial target (partial exit) OR (runner mode is off or partial target has not filled) AND high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × regime-scaled final target OR bars in trade ≥ timeout and partial target has not filled OR bars in trade ≥ regime-dependent stagnation duration AND maximum favorable close excursion < entry ATR × stagnation threshold OR, if enabled, within the failed-breakout window, close < the selected breakout reference
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × regime-scaled partial target (partial exit) OR (runner mode is off or partial target has not filled) AND low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × regime-scaled final target OR bars in trade ≥ timeout and partial target has not filled OR bars in trade ≥ regime-dependent stagnation duration AND maximum favorable close excursion < entry ATR × stagnation threshold OR, if enabled, within the failed-breakout window, close > the selected breakout reference
