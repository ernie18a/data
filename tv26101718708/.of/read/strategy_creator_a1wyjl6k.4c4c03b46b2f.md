Calculation: Positive MACD histogram average = cumulative sum of strictly positive chart-timeframe MACD histogram values divided by their count, including the current bar; use zero until one occurs.
Calculation: Negative MACD histogram average = cumulative sum of strictly negative chart-timeframe MACD histogram values divided by their count, including the current bar; use zero until one occurs.
Long: datetime is within the inclusive backtest window AND position is flat AND the number of consecutive previously closed long trades is less than the configured limit AND (EMA filter disabled OR (one-EMA mode AND close > short EMA) OR (two-EMA mode AND short EMA > medium EMA) OR (three-EMA mode AND short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < long RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (stochastic line-comparison mode AND smoothed %K > %D) OR (stochastic threshold mode AND smoothed %K < long stochastic threshold)) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (negative-average adjustment enabled AND MACD histogram < negative MACD histogram average - negative offset) OR (negative-average adjustment disabled AND MACD histogram < negative offset)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold).
Short: datetime is within the inclusive backtest window AND position is flat AND the number of consecutive previously closed short trades is less than the configured limit AND (EMA filter disabled OR (one-EMA mode AND close < short EMA) OR (two-EMA mode AND short EMA < medium EMA) OR (three-EMA mode AND short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > short RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (stochastic line-comparison mode AND smoothed %K < %D) OR (stochastic threshold mode AND smoothed %K > short stochastic threshold)) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (positive-average adjustment enabled AND MACD histogram > positive MACD histogram average + positive offset) OR (positive-average adjustment disabled AND MACD histogram > positive offset)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold).
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 - long stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + long take-profit fraction).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + short stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 - short take-profit fraction).
