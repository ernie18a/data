Calculation: MACD histogram = fast moving average of close minus slow moving average of close, minus a signal moving average of that difference; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback
Calculation: Positive and negative histogram averages = running averages of histogram values greater than zero and less than zero, respectively
Long: position side = flat AND datetime is within the configured test period AND (EMA filter disabled OR (one-EMA mode: close > fast EMA OR two-EMA mode: fast EMA > middle EMA OR three-EMA mode: fast EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > slow EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close) < long RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (crossover mode: %K > %D OR value mode: %K < long %K threshold)) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (negative-average mode: MACD histogram < negative histogram average minus configured offset OR absolute-threshold mode: MACD histogram < configured negative threshold)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > configured strength threshold)
Short: position side = flat AND datetime is within the configured test period AND (EMA filter disabled OR (one-EMA mode: close < fast EMA OR two-EMA mode: fast EMA < middle EMA OR three-EMA mode: fast EMA < middle EMA AND middle EMA < slow EMA)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close) > short RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (crossover mode: %K < %D OR value mode: %K > short %K threshold)) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (positive-average mode: MACD histogram > positive histogram average plus configured offset OR absolute-threshold mode: MACD histogram > configured positive threshold)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > configured strength threshold)
Exit: long, low <= entry price * (1 - configured long stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + configured long take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, high >= entry price * (1 + configured short stop-loss percentage) OR low <= entry price * (1 - configured short take-profit percentage)
