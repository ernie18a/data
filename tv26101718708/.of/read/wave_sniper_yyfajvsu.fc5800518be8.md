Calculation: Chart zigzag pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right strengths; optionally adapt strength by multiplying the base strength by chart-timeframe and wave-degree scales and by twice ATR divided by the corresponding high-low range, then smooth, round, and clamp it. Confirm pivots only after the required right-hand bars have elapsed. Keep alternating pivots, replacing a consecutive same-side pivot only when it is more extreme; process highs before lows. Retain pivots within the configured lookback and history limit.
Calculation: Wave state = classify the most recent six zigzag pivots, or all available pivots if fewer than six, as follows. Try five, four, three, two, then one impulse legs in that order, taking the first valid state. Adjacent pivot sides must alternate; direction is the sign of the final pivot minus the first pivot. For two or more legs, leg 2 must retrace at least a lower fraction and less than an upper fraction of leg 1 without crossing the first pivot. For three or more legs, leg 3 must be at least a configured multiple of leg 1. For four or more legs, pivot 4 must remain beyond pivot 1 in the impulse direction, allowing a configured overlap proportional to leg 1. A five-leg impulse is truncated when pivot 5 does not exceed pivot 3 in the impulse direction. If no impulse state is valid, test corrective C, B, then A: alternating pivot sides and respectively C at least a minimum fraction of A with B within a retracement range, B at least a minimum fraction of A, or two opposite-side pivots. Corrective direction is the sign of the first pivot minus the last corrective pivot.
Calculation: Flat = the latest four chart zigzag pivots O, A, B, C have alternating sides, with A and C on the same side. Let a=abs(A-O), b=abs(B-A), c=abs(C-B). A regular flat has b/a and c/a within their respective regular ranges; otherwise an expanded flat has both ratios within their expanded ranges; otherwise a running flat has b/a above its threshold and c/a below its threshold. Apply the configured tolerance to the regular and expanded bounds and to the running C bound; increase that tolerance when the current chart wave state is a truncated five-leg impulse. Record the first applicable type. Do not record a flat if its A, B, or C pivot bar is already used in the corresponding role by a retained flat; retain only the configured number and lookback of flats. Evaluate on the confirmation bar, not retrospectively on C.
Calculation: Pullback = the latest four chart zigzag pivots O, A, B, C have alternating sides, with A and C on the same side, and abs(abs(C-B)/abs(A-O)-1) is at most the pullback tolerance. When O is a high, C must differ from O multiplied by one minus the tolerance; when O is a low, C must differ from O multiplied by one plus the tolerance. Do not record a pullback if its A, B, or C pivot bar is already used in the corresponding role by a retained pullback; retain only the configured number and lookback of pullbacks, and remove one when close crosses its origin in the direction from O to A. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Projected C context = use a latched O, A, B when a newly confirmed third chart pivot alternates with the preceding two and abs(B-A)/abs(A-O) is at least a minimum fraction; clear the latch when a later alternating C completes or close crosses O opposite the direction from O to A. Otherwise use the latest two opposite-side pivots as A and B only when four alternating pivots do not already complete an ABC, with the preceding pivot as O if available; otherwise use a corrective B wave state. Let the projected C direction be the sign of A-O, or B-A when O is unavailable, and its length base be abs(A-O), or abs(B-A) when O is unavailable. Project C levels from B in that direction using an equal-length, a larger, and a still larger extension multiplier.
Long: A new regular or expanded flat has C at a low OR a new running flat has C at a high.
Short: A new regular or expanded flat has C at a high OR a new running flat has C at a low.
Long: A new pullback has C at a low.
Short: A new pullback has C at a high.
Long: The chart wave state becomes a valid five-leg impulse with positive direction OR a valid truncated five-leg impulse with negative direction is newly present.
Short: The chart wave state becomes a valid five-leg impulse with negative direction OR a valid truncated five-leg impulse with positive direction is newly present.
Long: A projected C context exists AND its projected C direction is negative AND the distance from close to the nearest projected C level is at most a configured multiple of ATR.
Short: A projected C context exists AND its projected C direction is positive AND the distance from close to the nearest projected C level is at most a configured multiple of ATR.
