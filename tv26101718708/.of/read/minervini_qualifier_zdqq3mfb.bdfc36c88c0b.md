Calculation: Short-, medium-, long-, and longest-lookback simple moving averages = simple moving averages of daily close, with progressively longer lookbacks. Use daily bars, which are higher timeframe than intraday charts, equal to daily charts, and lower than charts above daily; use the current daily bar while it is forming, otherwise the latest completed daily bar.
Calculation: Rolling low and rolling high = lowest and highest daily close over the configured year-long lookback.
Calculation: Longest moving average rising = longest-lookback simple moving average rising over a longer lookback.
Long: Daily close crosses above short-lookback simple moving average AND daily close is above long- and longest-lookback simple moving averages AND long-lookback simple moving average is above longest-lookback simple moving average AND longest-lookback simple moving average is rising AND medium-lookback simple moving average is above long- and longest-lookback simple moving averages AND daily close is at least the configured percentage above the rolling low AND daily close is at least the configured fraction of the rolling high.
