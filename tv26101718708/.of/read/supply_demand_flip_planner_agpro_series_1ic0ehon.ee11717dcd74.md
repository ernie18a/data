Calculation: Source zones = On pivot confirmation, replace the corresponding active source zone: a supply zone extends downward from the pivot high by ATR at the pivot times a depth multiplier; a demand zone extends upward from the pivot low by the same amount. Measure age from the pivot bar. Deactivate a source zone when its age exceeds the maximum source-zone age or it breaks. If a pivot-bar ATR is unavailable, use the current ATR.
Calculation: Active flip = When close exceeds an active supply zone's top plus current ATR times the break multiplier, replace the active flip with that zone in the long direction. When close falls below an active demand zone's bottom minus current ATR times the break multiplier, replace it with that zone in the short direction. If both occur on the same bar, the short flip takes precedence. Store the flip's bounds, direction, break bar, and source quality: clamp(zone width / current ATR / source-quality scale, 0, 1). Expire a flip when its age exceeds the retest window and it has no active acceptance. Clear it after an invalidation. A retest occurs when high >= flip bottom AND low <= flip top. Retest recency is measured from the most recent retest, including retests of previous flips.
Calculation: Acceptance score = Clamp the sum of weighted components to the score range. Each component is clamped to its permitted range before weighting: break strength is (close - flip top) / ATR for a long flip or (flip bottom - close) / ATR for a short flip; retest depth is (flip top - low) / zone width for long or (high - flip bottom) / zone width for short; wick response is (min(open, close) - low) / bar range for long or (high - max(open, close)) / bar range for short; body ratio is abs(close - open) / bar range; freshness is 1 - flip age / retest window; relative volume is volume / SMA(volume), or a neutral value if that average is not positive. Scale break strength, retest depth, wick response, body ratio, and relative volume by their respective normalization parameters. Add weighted source quality and freshness. If relative-volume scoring is disabled, substitute a fixed volume contribution. Use a positive minimum denominator for ATR, zone width, and bar range.
Calculation: Active acceptance = Set on every bar where an active flip has a recent retest, close is beyond its acceptance edge by current ATR times the acceptance multiplier, and acceptance score >= the required score adjusted for the selected confirmation mode. The acceptance edge is the top for a long flip and the bottom for a short flip. Reset the acceptance age on each such bar; clear acceptance when its age exceeds the retest window, a new flip is locked, or the flip is invalidated.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND a pivot low is not confirmed on the same bar.
Long: Close > active supply-zone top + current ATR times the break multiplier AND no demand-zone break occurs on the same bar.
Short: Close < active demand-zone bottom - current ATR times the break multiplier.
Long: Active flip direction = long AND active flip exists.
Short: Active flip direction = short AND active flip exists.
Long: Active flip direction = long AND active flip exists AND bars since most recent retest <= retest-recency limit AND close > flip top + current ATR times the acceptance multiplier AND acceptance score >= adjusted required score.
Short: Active flip direction = short AND active flip exists AND bars since most recent retest <= retest-recency limit AND close < flip bottom - current ATR times the acceptance multiplier AND acceptance score >= adjusted required score.
Exit: long, active flip direction = long AND active flip exists AND close < flip bottom - current ATR times the invalidation multiplier.
Exit: short, active flip direction = short AND active flip exists AND close > flip top + current ATR times the invalidation multiplier.
