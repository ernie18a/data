Calculation: Structure = On either the chart timeframe or the higher structure timeframe, find the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lookbacks. Use ATR from that timeframe. A higher high or higher low exceeds its preceding pivot by more than ATR times the swing buffer; a lower high or lower low is below its preceding pivot by more than that buffer. An upward break occurs when the previous bar's close exceeds the latest pivot high plus current ATR times the break buffer; a downward break occurs when the previous bar's close is below the latest pivot low minus that buffer. The bullish score is 3 for a higher low, plus 2 for a higher high, plus 3 for an upward break, plus 1 if the previous close is above the midpoint of the latest pivot high and low. The bearish score is 3 for a lower high, plus 2 for a lower low, plus 3 for a downward break, plus 1 if the previous close is below that midpoint. Structure direction is bullish if the bullish score is at least 5 and at least 2 greater than the bearish score; otherwise it is bearish if the bearish score is at least 5 and at least 2 greater than the bullish score; otherwise it is neutral. The higher-timeframe calculation uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Daily context = On the daily timeframe, using completed daily bars, award one bullish point for each of the previous daily close being above its open, above its high-low midpoint, above the close a lookback before it, and above its EMA; award bearish points for the corresponding below comparisons. Context is bullish if bullish points are at least 3 and exceed bearish points, bearish if bearish points are at least 3 and exceed bullish points, and otherwise neutral. When disabled, context is neutral. The daily timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe according to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Session context = Compare the chart close with the midpoint of the most recently completed reference session's high and low: above is bullish, below is bearish, and equality or an unavailable session is neutral. When disabled, context is neutral.
Calculation: Master scores = Bullish score is 5 for bullish higher-timeframe structure, plus 3 for bullish chart-timeframe structure, plus 2 for bullish daily context, plus 1 for bullish session context. Bearish score uses the corresponding bearish conditions and weights.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish reversal = Higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND chart-timeframe structure has a higher low AND an upward break AND (chart close is above the latest higher-timeframe pivot high OR chart close is above the previous session high OR daily context is not bearish).
Calculation: Confirmed bearish reversal = Higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND chart-timeframe structure has a lower high AND a downward break AND (chart close is below the latest higher-timeframe pivot low OR chart close is below the previous session low OR daily context is not bullish).
Calculation: Reversal warning = (Higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a higher low OR chart-timeframe structure is bullish OR chart close is above the previous session high) AND confirmed bullish reversal is false) OR (higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a lower high OR chart-timeframe structure is bearish OR chart close is below the previous session low) AND confirmed bearish reversal is false).
Long: Higher-timeframe structure is bullish OR chart-timeframe structure is bullish OR daily context is bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe structure is bearish OR chart-timeframe structure is bearish OR daily context is bearish.
Long: Higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a higher low OR chart-timeframe structure is bullish OR chart close is above the previous session high).
Short: Higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a lower high OR chart-timeframe structure is bearish OR chart close is below the previous session low).
Long: Confirmed bullish reversal is true OR (confirmed bearish reversal is false AND reversal warning is false AND bullish master score is at least 6 AND bullish master score is at least 2 greater than bearish master score).
Short: Confirmed bullish reversal is false AND (confirmed bearish reversal is true OR (reversal warning is false AND bearish master score is at least 6 AND bearish master score is at least 2 greater than bullish master score)).
