Calculation: Baselines = EMAs of chart-timeframe close using short, medium, and long lengths, respectively. Higher-timeframe baselines use the same respective lengths and the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume since the start of the exchange day; use close if cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Structure state = initially 0; set to +1 when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, or -1 when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. A pivot uses equal numbers of bars on either side and is available only after confirmation. A break counts only if the pivot bar is within the structure-memory lookback. Compare both current and previous closes with the current pivot level; retain the state until the next qualifying break.
Calculation: Opening range = the running high and low during the configured opening window, reset each exchange day. Opening-range acceptance contributes only when tracking is enabled and the range exists; acceptance is close above the running high or below the running low.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of: +1 or -1 for each of short versus medium and medium versus long chart-timeframe baselines; +1 or -1 for each corresponding comparison of higher-timeframe baselines; +1 or -1 for close above or below daily VWAP; +1 or -1 when (short baseline minus its value at the slope lookback) / ATR is above a positive slope threshold or below its negative, using 0 if ATR is zero; structure state; +1 or -1 when respectively +DI exceeds -DI or -DI exceeds +DI AND ADX is at least its threshold; +1 or -1 for respectively a bullish or bearish candle whose absolute body exceeds its body-size SMA multiplied by a body threshold AND whose volume divided by its volume SMA is at least a relative-volume threshold; +1 or -1 for opening-range acceptance above or below the range. Each comparison contributes 0 when neither direction applies. Use a relative volume of 1 if its volume SMA is not positive. Clamp the sum to symmetric positive and negative score limits.
Calculation: Squeeze = the upper EMA-centered Bollinger band is below the upper Keltner band AND the lower Bollinger band is above the lower Keltner band. Bollinger deviation is the standard deviation of close; Keltner bands use an EMA of close plus or minus a multiplier of the EMA of true range.
Calculation: Expansion = Bollinger width, defined as (upper band - lower band) / basis or 0 if the basis is zero, has a positive SMA AND exceeds that SMA multiplied by an expansion threshold AND exceeds its previous-bar value, using 0 when the previous width is unavailable.
Long: short chart-timeframe baseline > medium chart-timeframe baseline AND medium chart-timeframe baseline > long chart-timeframe baseline AND short higher-timeframe baseline > medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline > long higher-timeframe baseline.
Short: short chart-timeframe baseline < medium chart-timeframe baseline AND medium chart-timeframe baseline < long chart-timeframe baseline AND short higher-timeframe baseline < medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline < long higher-timeframe baseline.
Long: close > long chart-timeframe baseline.
Short: close < long chart-timeframe baseline.
Long: short higher-timeframe baseline > medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline > long higher-timeframe baseline.
Short: short higher-timeframe baseline < medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline < long higher-timeframe baseline.
Long: structure state > 0.
Short: structure state < 0.
Long: close > daily VWAP.
Short: close < daily VWAP.
Long: directional score >= positive bias threshold OR (squeeze = false AND directional score > 0).
Short: directional score <= negative bias threshold OR (squeeze = false AND directional score < 0).
Long: squeeze = false AND directional score >= positive trend threshold AND short higher-timeframe baseline > medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline > long higher-timeframe baseline AND close > daily VWAP.
Short: squeeze = false AND directional score <= negative trend threshold AND short higher-timeframe baseline < medium higher-timeframe baseline AND medium higher-timeframe baseline < long higher-timeframe baseline AND close < daily VWAP.
Long: expansion = true AND +DI > -DI AND ADX >= its threshold AND directional score >= positive expansion-score threshold.
Short: expansion = true AND -DI > +DI AND ADX >= its threshold AND directional score <= negative expansion-score threshold.
