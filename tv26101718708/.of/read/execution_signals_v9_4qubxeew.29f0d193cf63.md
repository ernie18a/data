Calculation: Failure high = bar high > previous bar high AND bar close <= previous bar high AND bar high > max(bar open, bar close).
Calculation: Failure low = bar low < previous bar low AND bar close >= previous bar low AND bar low < min(bar open, bar close).
Calculation: Inside bar = bar high < previous bar high AND bar low > previous bar low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe signal = the last completed higher-timeframe bar is an enabled failure high, failure low, or inside bar; its opening time and the preceding higher-timeframe bar's opening time are separated by no more than a configurable multiple of the higher timeframe, and the chart bar's opening time and the signal bar's opening time are separated by no more than the same limit. Evaluate only when a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Calculation: Armed higher-timeframe signal = the latest higher-timeframe signal detected while flat and outside the no-entry window; it expires when another higher-timeframe bar begins while flat, when its high or low is first broken by a chart-bar close, or at the forced-close time.
Calculation: Nested bias = direction of the first chart-bar close above the armed higher-timeframe signal high or below its low, retained for a configurable number of chart bars. Store the opposite extreme of that higher-timeframe signal. A new bias can be set only when no bias is active; it expires on its countdown, on the first break of an armed chart-timeframe setup, or at the forced-close time.
Calculation: Armed chart-timeframe setup = the latest enabled chart-timeframe failure high, failure low, or inside bar detected while a nested bias is active and trading is allowed. If direction matching is enabled, a long bias accepts only failure lows or inside bars and a short bias accepts only failure highs or inside bars. A newer qualifying bar replaces the setup; it expires after a configurable number of chart bars, on its first break, or at the forced-close time. Check for a break before arming the current bar.
Calculation: Prior regular-session high and low = extrema of chart bars opening during the previous accumulated New York regular session; deploy them at the next evening opening or the first available new-calendar-day bar before the regular-session close. The regular session runs from 09:30 inclusive to 16:15 exclusive New York time.
Calculation: Direction filter = when the optional prior-session filter is disabled or prior-session levels are unavailable, both directions are allowed; otherwise a close above the prior-session high allows only long, a close below the prior-session low allows only short, and a close inside or equal to the range allows both directions or neither according to the inside-range setting.
Calculation: Stop distance = for a long, entry close minus the lesser of the selected raw stop and entry close minus the minimum stop distance; for a short, the greater of the selected raw stop and entry close plus the minimum stop distance minus entry close. The raw stop is selectable as the previous chart bar's low or high, the minimum low or maximum high of the current and previous chart bars, the opposite extreme of the higher-timeframe signal, or, in nested mode, the opposite extreme of the armed chart-timeframe setup. The chart-timeframe setup option falls back to the previous chart bar in direct mode.
Long: Enabled failure low on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the higher-timeframe signal gap checks pass AND a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Short: Enabled failure high on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the higher-timeframe signal gap checks pass AND a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Long: Flat AND chart-bar opening time is outside 15:59 inclusive to 18:00 exclusive New York time AND direction filter allows long AND optional maximum stop distance is disabled OR flat AND chart-bar opening time is outside 15:59 inclusive to 18:00 exclusive New York time AND direction filter allows long AND stop distance <= maximum stop distance; AND either direct mode with an armed higher-timeframe signal AND close > its high, or nested mode with an active long bias AND an armed chart-timeframe setup AND close > its high.
Short: Flat AND chart-bar opening time is outside 15:59 inclusive to 18:00 exclusive New York time AND direction filter allows short AND optional maximum stop distance is disabled OR flat AND chart-bar opening time is outside 15:59 inclusive to 18:00 exclusive New York time AND direction filter allows short AND stop distance <= maximum stop distance; AND either direct mode with an armed higher-timeframe signal AND close < its low, or nested mode with an active short bias AND an armed chart-timeframe setup AND close < its low.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - entry stop distance OR high >= entry price + entry stop distance * reward multiple OR current chart-bar opening time is at or after 15:59 New York time AND previous chart-bar opening time was before 15:59.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + entry stop distance OR low <= entry price - entry stop distance * reward multiple OR current chart-bar opening time is at or after 15:59 New York time AND previous chart-bar opening time was before 15:59.
