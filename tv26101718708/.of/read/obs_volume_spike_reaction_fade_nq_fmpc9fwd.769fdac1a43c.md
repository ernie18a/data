Calculation: Reaction bar = a bar whose volume is at least its volume SMA including the current bar times the regular-session multiplier during the regular session, or the other-session multiplier otherwise; whose high minus low is at least the minimum range; whose range, if the optional overshoot filter is enabled, is at least a multiplier times the positive average range of preceding bars; and for which every volume in the preceding cluster lookback is below its corresponding volume SMA times the multiplier selected by the current bar's session. Its time must pass the enabled session, news-window, and session-boundary exclusion filters. If the body filter is enabled, absolute close minus open divided by range must be above the upper threshold or below the lower threshold, with the ratio treated as zero when range is zero. If the regime filter is enabled, close must be at least its long-lookback SMA or that SMA must be greater than at least one of its values in the preceding shorter lookback.
Calculation: Each qualifying reaction bar defines a long entry limit below its low and a short entry limit above its high by an entry-offset multiple of its range. For each side, the optional static-level filter passes when the entry limit is farther than a tolerance from every fixed level in avoid mode, within the tolerance of any fixed level in boost mode, or unconditionally in off mode. A new signal for a side replaces that side's pending limit and stored reaction-bar levels. A pending limit becomes eligible after its signal bar and expires when its age in chart bars exceeds the expiry parameter. The first filled side cancels the other pending limit; a reaction bar can produce at most one trade.
Calculation: For each side, the stored stop is beyond the reaction-bar extreme by a stop multiple of the range, and the stored target is inside that extreme by a target multiple of the range. The stop multiple exceeds the entry-offset multiple. Preset selection determines whether these multiples are fixed parameters or custom parameters.
Long: A reaction bar occurs AND the long leg is enabled AND its static-level filter passes.
Long: An eligible long limit remains pending AND low is at or below that limit.
Short: A reaction bar occurs AND the short leg is enabled AND its static-level filter passes.
Short: An eligible short limit remains pending AND high is at or above that limit.
Exit: long, low is at or below the stored long stop OR high is at or above the stored long target.
Exit: short, high is at or above the stored short stop OR low is at or below the stored short target.
