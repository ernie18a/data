Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × absolute value of (close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic K = simple moving average of stochastic %K calculated from close, high and low; stochastic D = simple moving average of smoothed stochastic K.
Calculation: Timing EMA gap = shorter-period close EMA minus longer-period close EMA, using a separate configurable EMA pair from the signal EMA pair.
Long: In scalping mode, close > open AND candle body percentage >= minimum AND (shorter-period signal EMA crosses above longer-period signal EMA OR RSI crosses above its oversold threshold OR smoothed stochastic K crosses above D while K < its oversold threshold) AND, if enabled, volume > its simple moving average × volume multiplier AND, if enabled, close > long-term trend EMA AND, if enabled, the selected timing condition is met: timing EMA gap crosses above zero, timing EMA gap < 0 AND timing EMA gap > its previous value AND absolute timing EMA gap < ATR × convergence multiplier, or either condition.
