Calculation: Projected trend bands = EMA of (lowest low over the projection lookback + SMA of RSI(close) × max(highest high − lowest low over the same lookback, epsilon) / RSI scale), plus and minus a multiplier times the SMA of absolute candle body size. Initialize trend as bearish; on each completed bar set it bullish if close is above the upper band, bearish if close is below the lower band, and otherwise retain its previous state.
Calculation: Readiness = bar count at least the maximum of the long volume SMA lookback, projection range lookback, strength lookback, and candle-body SMA lookback.
Calculation: Elevated volume = short volume SMA / max(long volume SMA, epsilon) at least the high-volume threshold, or at least the extreme-volume threshold.
Calculation: Armed box = start idle; after readiness, start building when elevated volume changes from false to true and bars since the last resolution or cancellation exceed the cooldown. Initialize the box to that bar's high and low. On each subsequent elevated-volume bar, extend the box to include its high and low if the build count is below its limit; otherwise cancel it and start the cooldown. On the first subsequent bar without elevated volume, freeze the box and arm it with age zero. On later armed bars increment its age and cancel it if age exceeds its limit. After either breakout, resolve the box and start the cooldown. Evaluate breakouts after updating the box state, including on the bar that first arms it.
Calculation: Active risk levels = on each box breakout, replace any previous active risk map with one based on the breakout close; risk distance is either ATR at breakout multiplied by a stop multiplier or breakout close multiplied by a percentage multiplier. The stop is entry minus risk distance for a long and entry plus risk distance for a short; the final target is entry plus risk distance times a reward multiplier for a long and entry minus that amount for a short. Deactivate the map when its stop or final target is hit or its age exceeds its maximum; do not test it on the breakout bar.
Long: Trend state is bullish.
Short: Trend state is bearish.
Long: Readiness AND box is armed AND close > box top AND previous close <= box top.
Short: Readiness AND box is armed AND close < box bottom AND previous close >= box bottom.
Exit: long, active long risk map AND no new box breakout on this bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, active short risk map AND no new box breakout on this bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target).
