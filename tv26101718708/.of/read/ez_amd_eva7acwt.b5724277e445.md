Calculation: Support and resistance zones = On the chart timeframe, confirm a pivot low or high using equal numbers of bars on each side. A new pivot replaces the previous zone on that side. Support bottom is the pivot low; support top is the greater of the pivot bar's body low and its low plus a configurable multiple of ATR at the pivot. Resistance top is the pivot high; resistance bottom is the lesser of the pivot bar's body high and its high minus that multiple of ATR. Remove support when close is below its bottom and resistance when close is above its top, except on a bar that creates a new pivot on that side. Each zone is identified by its pivot bar.
Calculation: Reaction measures = Range is max(high − low, minimum price increment); body is abs(close − open); close location is (close − low) / range; lower wick is min(open, close) − low; upper wick is high − max(open, close). Signal ATR is either rolling ATR or session-adaptive ATR: for each configured session independently, initialize it to the first session bar's true range and update it on each subsequent bar in that session as previous value + (true range − previous value) / ATR length; use rolling ATR outside those sessions or before the relevant session value exists.
Calculation: Bullish reaction = (close > open AND lower wick >= body × wick threshold AND close location >= close-location threshold) OR (close > open AND range >= signal ATR × displacement threshold AND body / range >= body threshold) OR close > highest high of the preceding microstructure lookback bars OR (relative-volume reactions enabled AND volume >= its moving average × volume threshold AND close > open AND close location >= close-location threshold).
Calculation: Bearish reaction = (close < open AND upper wick >= body × wick threshold AND close location <= 1 − close-location threshold) OR (close < open AND range >= signal ATR × displacement threshold AND body / range >= body threshold) OR close < lowest low of the preceding microstructure lookback bars OR (relative-volume reactions enabled AND volume >= its moving average × volume threshold AND close < open AND close location <= 1 − close-location threshold).
Calculation: Support defense = close > support top AND ((low < support bottom AND bullish reaction) OR (high >= support bottom AND low <= support top AND previous close > support top AND close > open) OR (previous close <= support top AND bullish reaction) OR (high >= support bottom AND low <= support top AND bullish reaction)).
Calculation: Resistance defense = close < resistance bottom AND ((high > resistance top AND bearish reaction) OR (high >= resistance bottom AND low <= resistance top AND previous close < resistance bottom AND close < open) OR (previous close >= resistance bottom AND bearish reaction) OR (high >= resistance bottom AND low <= resistance top AND bearish reaction)).
Calculation: First defense = A zone's first qualifying defense since its creation; reset the count when that zone is replaced or removed. Calculate first support and resistance defenses independently, including on bars where both occur.
Long: First support defense AND no first resistance defense on the same bar.
Short: First resistance defense AND no first support defense on the same bar.
