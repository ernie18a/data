Calculation: Support zone = After a confirmed pivot low, bottom is the pivot bar’s low and top is the greater of its lower open/close and bottom plus ATR padding; it is replaced by a newer confirmed pivot low and cleared when a bar closes below its bottom.
Calculation: Resistance zone = After a confirmed pivot high, top is the pivot bar’s high and bottom is the lesser of its higher open/close and top minus ATR padding; it is replaced by a newer confirmed pivot high and cleared when a bar closes above its top.
Calculation: Bullish reaction = Any of: bullish rejection (close > open, lower wick ≥ body × rejection ratio, close location ≥ strong-close threshold); bullish displacement (close > open, range ≥ ATR × displacement threshold, body/range ≥ displacement threshold); close above the highest high of the preceding microstructure lookback; or, when enabled, bullish high-relative-volume reaction.
Calculation: Bearish reaction = Any of: bearish rejection (close < open, upper wick ≥ body × rejection ratio, close location ≤ 1 − strong-close threshold); bearish displacement (close < open, range ≥ ATR × displacement threshold, body/range ≥ displacement threshold); close below the lowest low of the preceding microstructure lookback; or, when enabled, bearish high-relative-volume reaction.
Long: On a confirmed bar of a 1-minute chart, an active support zone is touched (high ≥ zone bottom AND low ≤ zone top) AND (low < zone bottom AND close > zone top AND bullish reaction OR close > zone top AND previous close > zone top AND close > open OR close > zone top AND previous close ≤ zone top AND bullish reaction OR close > zone top AND bullish rejection OR close > zone top AND (bullish displacement OR bullish microstructure shift OR bullish high-relative-volume reaction)); this is the first confirmed support defense since the zone was created or invalidated AND no resistance-zone defense qualifies on the same bar.
Short: On a confirmed bar of a 1-minute chart, an active resistance zone is touched (high ≥ zone bottom AND low ≤ zone top) AND (high > zone top AND close < zone bottom AND bearish reaction OR close < zone bottom AND previous close < zone bottom AND close < open OR close < zone bottom AND previous close ≥ zone bottom AND bearish reaction OR close < zone bottom AND bearish rejection OR close < zone bottom AND (bearish displacement OR bearish microstructure shift OR bearish high-relative-volume reaction)); this is the first confirmed resistance defense since the zone was created or invalidated AND no support-zone defense qualifies on the same bar.
Exit: Long, close < the support-zone bottom that generated the active long signal OR that support zone is replaced.
Exit: Short, close > the resistance-zone top that generated the active short signal OR that resistance zone is replaced.
