Calculation: Trend bands = basis is SMA of EMA of close using the same lookback; deviation is EMA of absolute difference between close and basis using a longer lookback, multiplied by a multiplier. Upper and lower bands are basis plus and minus deviation. Trend starts neutral, becomes bullish when close > upper band, becomes bearish when close < lower band, and otherwise retains its previous state.
Calculation: Liquidity zones = at each confirmed pivot high or low, create a supply or demand zone if pivot-bar volume > the confirmation-bar volume SMA multiplied by a threshold. The supply upper boundary is the pivot high; the demand lower boundary is the pivot low. Retain the configured maximum number of zones, removing the oldest first; remove a zone from consideration after its boundary is swept.
Calculation: Sweep levels = highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar. Rejection fractions are (high - max(open, close)) / max(minimum price increment, high - low) and (min(open, close) - low) / max(minimum price increment, high - low).
Calculation: Trend sweep levels = in a bearish trend, store each previous-bar local high below the current upper trend band; in a bullish trend, store each previous-bar local low above the current lower trend band. Remove a stored high when high > that level or upper band < that level; remove a stored low when low < that level or lower band > that level. Retain the configured maximum number of each, removing the oldest first.
Calculation: Structural zones = create a supply zone at each confirmed pivot high and a demand zone at each confirmed pivot low, using a separate symmetric pivot lookback. If both pivots confirm on the same bar, create only the supply zone. Supply top is the pivot high and bottom is top minus confirmation-bar ATR times a width multiplier; demand bottom is the pivot low and top is bottom plus that width. Reject a new zone if its midpoint is within a multiple of confirmation-bar ATR of an existing same-side zone midpoint. Retain the configured number of each side, removing the oldest first, and remove a zone when its boundary is broken.
Calculation: RSI divergence = compare consecutive confirmed symmetric pivot highs or lows of RSI and the corresponding price highs or lows at those RSI pivot bars. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Divergence trailing stop = a bullish divergence makes the long stop pending and cancels the short pending stop; a bearish divergence does the reverse. When no stop is active, activate the pending long stop on RSI crossing above its midpoint at low minus ATR times a multiplier, or the pending short stop on RSI crossing below its midpoint at high plus ATR times the multiplier. While active, if RSI is at or above its midpoint and below overbought, update the stop to max(previous stop, low minus ATR times multiplier); if RSI is below its midpoint and above oversold, update it to min(previous stop, high plus ATR times multiplier). Deactivate when RSI reaches either extreme or close crosses beyond the updated stop for the applicable RSI region.

Calculation: Sweep EMA = EMA of close on the chart timeframe, or on a selected higher timeframe using its last completed bar on historical bars and its still-forming bar in real time, or on a selected lower timeframe using its last intrabar.

Store or replace the downside breakout level when a sweep pivot low confirms and close < that pivot; clear it after this decision.

Store or replace the upside breakout level when a sweep pivot high confirms and close > that pivot; clear it after this decision.
