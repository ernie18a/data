Calculation: Bull swing = the latest confirmed swing high occurred after the latest confirmed swing low; bear swing = the latest confirmed swing low occurred after the latest confirmed swing high. A pivot is confirmed using equal left and right lookback lengths.
Calculation: Active swing = a bull or bear swing whose terminal pivot is within the maximum swing age and whose leg height exceeds 0.35 ATR; ATR uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Retest pocket = for a bull swing, the band from terminal price minus leg height times shallow depth to terminal price minus leg height times deep depth; for a bear swing, the band from terminal price plus leg height times deep depth to terminal price plus leg height times shallow depth.
Calculation: Pocket touch = active swing AND bar range overlaps the retest pocket. Recently touched = a pocket touch occurred within the response window, including the current bar.
Calculation: Defense loss = bull swing AND close below origin price minus ATR times defense buffer, OR bear swing AND close above origin price plus ATR times defense buffer.
Calculation: Pocket holding = recently touched AND NOT defense loss AND (bull swing AND close at or above pocket midpoint OR bear swing AND close at or below pocket midpoint).
Calculation: Retest quality = clamp(0.22 × swing clarity score + 0.26 × depth score + 0.20 × rejection score + 0.20 × close response score + 0.12 × relative-volume score − sensitivity penalty, 0, 100). Swing clarity combines leg-height-in-ATR and leg-duration scores (68% and 32%); depth score peaks at ideal depth within the pocket; rejection score uses the direction-side wick and close location; close-response score uses close relative to pocket midpoint and close location; relative-volume score normalizes volume divided by its chart-timeframe moving average. Each component score is clamped to 0–100.
Calculation: Clean target room = target distance divided by ATR is at least the minimum target-room threshold; target guide is beyond the swing terminal by leg height times target extension, in the swing direction.
Long: active bull swing AND (pocket touch OR (recently touched AND NOT defense loss AND (pocket holding AND clean target room AND retest quality at least the valid threshold OR retest quality at least the watch threshold)))
Short: active bear swing AND (pocket touch OR (recently touched AND NOT defense loss AND (pocket holding AND clean target room AND retest quality at least the valid threshold OR retest quality at least the watch threshold)))
