Calculation: Session = configurable time-of-day interval evaluated using the bar datetime and the instrument's exchange timezone; the session filter passes when disabled or the bar opens inside the interval.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on a higher timeframe, close, EMA of close, and ADX with the same length for directional-movement and ADX smoothing; historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while a real-time bar uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming. The long trend gate passes when disabled or higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND the optional ADX filter passes; the short gate uses close < EMA. The ADX filter passes when disabled or ADX >= its threshold.
Calculation: Momentum = EMA of close minus close a configurable number of bars earlier, with a separately configurable smoothing length.
Calculation: Q-trend source = close or a configurable OHLC-derived source, optionally smoothed with an EMA. Its rolling highest and lowest values define a midpoint and range. Initialize the trend line to the rolling midpoint; after the trend lookback, start each bar with the previous trend line. The offset is a multiplier times the previous bar's ATR. An up event occurs when source crosses above the starting line plus offset OR source > that level; a down event occurs when source crosses below the starting line minus offset OR source < that level. In the alternate signal mode, use a cross in either direction in place of each directional cross. Update the line to the starting line unchanged if either event occurs and that line differs from the previous line; otherwise add the offset for an up event, subtract it for a down event, or retain the previous line. State becomes long on an up event, otherwise short on a down event, otherwise retains its previous state. A fresh event is an event whose direction differs from the previous state. A strong up event additionally requires at least one bar in a configurable recent window to have open >= its rolling low AND open < rolling low + one eighth of its rolling range; a strong down event requires open <= rolling high AND open > rolling high - one eighth of its rolling range. The strong-only option restricts scored triggers to strong fresh events.
Calculation: UT source = close or Heikin Ashi close, defined as (open + high + low + close) / 4. UT distance = a multiplier times chart-timeframe ATR. Let previous trail default to zero: if current and previous UT source are both > previous trail, current trail = max(previous trail, current source - distance); if both are < previous trail, current trail = min(previous trail, current source + distance); otherwise current trail = current source - distance when current source > previous trail, and current source + distance otherwise.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each exchange-calendar day boundary.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the first configurable number of chart bars after each exchange-calendar day boundary; the range is complete once that many bars have elapsed.
Calculation: Delta = either volume × (close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), or signed volume according to close >= previous close, or signed volume according to close >= open. The selected comparison assigns positive volume when true and negative volume otherwise. Strong positive delta requires delta > 0 AND absolute delta >= max(a configurable linear-interpolated percentile of absolute delta over a configurable rolling lookback, a fixed minimum); strong negative delta substitutes delta < 0.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > SMA of volume × a configurable multiplier.
Calculation: Zones = for each of two pivot configurations, one with longer left and right lookbacks than the other, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low. On the confirmation bar, set supply bounds to pivot high and pivot high - current ATR × a depth multiplier; set demand bounds to pivot low and pivot low + current ATR × that multiplier. A bar overlaps a zone when low <= its upper bound AND high >= its lower bound. Zones are created only when zone display is enabled and persist until replaced by a newer pivot of the same type and configuration.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of one point for each satisfied condition: corresponding higher-timeframe trend gate; fast DEMA above slow DEMA for long or below for short; corresponding Q-trend state; UT source above trail for long or below for short; overlap with either demand zone for long or supply zone for short; corresponding strong delta; disabled VWAP confirmation or close above session VWAP for long or below for short; disabled opening-range confirmation or completed opening range AND close above its high for long or below its low for short; momentum > 0 for long or < 0 for short. Fast DEMA uses a shorter lookback than slow DEMA.
Calculation: Scored long signal = session filter passes AND no prior scored long signal exists or bars since it > cooldown AND (fresh Q-trend up event satisfying the optional strong-only filter OR UT source crosses above its trail OR fast DEMA crosses above slow DEMA) AND long score >= threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume spike) AND long trend gate passes. Scored short signal uses the opposite events and score, its own cooldown, and the short trend gate. Update each cooldown on its scored signal, including when the subsequent RSI filter fails.
Calculation: Sentiment = mean of eleven components: piecewise-linearly rescaled RSI, smoothed stochastic, smoothed stochastic RSI, CCI and bull-bear power; normalized moving-average position, VWAP-band position, Bollinger-band position, supertrend position and pivot market structure; and close-versus-bar-index correlation rescaled from [-1, 1] to [0, 100]. Each piecewise rescaling maps its ordered input breakpoints to ordered output levels from 0 to 100, with extremes capped where specified below. RSI breakpoints are its lower, middle and upper thresholds mapped to the lower quartile, midpoint and upper quartile, with its endpoints mapped to 0 and 100. Stochastic and stochastic RSI use the same mapping with their respective lower, middle and upper thresholds. CCI maps negative extreme, negative threshold, zero, positive threshold and positive extreme to 0, lower quartile, midpoint, upper quartile and 100, capped outside the extremes. Bull-bear power is high + low - 2 × EMA(close); map a configurable multiple of its lower Bollinger band, the lower band, zero, the upper band and the same multiple of the upper band to 0, lower quartile, midpoint, upper quartile and 100, capped outside the extremes. For each normalized position component, maintain a state that changes to bullish on its buy comparison and bearish on its sell comparison, otherwise persists; on a bullish state change reset the running maximum close, on a bearish state change reset the running minimum close, otherwise update the applicable maximum and minimum with close. Normalize close between those extrema, using the midpoint if equal, then smooth with an SMA and scale to 0–100. The comparisons are close versus SMA, close versus session VWAP plus or minus a multiple of close standard deviation, close versus Bollinger bands, and close versus supertrend respectively. For market structure, the buy comparison is the first close above the latest confirmed pivot high and the sell comparison is the first close below the latest confirmed pivot low; reset the respective first-break flag when a new pivot is confirmed. Substitute the midpoint for an unavailable normalized VWAP component.
Long: Fast DEMA crosses above slow DEMA AND session filter passes.
Short: Fast DEMA crosses below slow DEMA AND session filter passes.
Long: Momentum crosses above zero.
Short: Momentum crosses below zero.
Long: Q-trend fresh up event.
Short: Q-trend fresh down event.
Long: UT source crosses above UT trail AND UT source > UT trail.
Short: UT source crosses below UT trail AND UT source < UT trail.
Long: Bar overlaps either demand zone AND strong positive delta AND (bubble volume filter disabled OR volume spike).
Short: Bar overlaps either supply zone AND strong negative delta AND (bubble volume filter disabled OR volume spike).
Long: Scored long signal AND RSI < upper RSI threshold.
Short: Scored short signal AND RSI > lower RSI threshold.
Long: Sentiment > upper sentiment threshold.
Short: Sentiment < lower sentiment threshold.
Exit: long, scored short signal AND RSI > lower RSI threshold.
Exit: short, scored long signal AND RSI < upper RSI threshold.
Exit: long, low <= previous bar's close - previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= previous bar's close + previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= previous bar's close + previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= previous bar's close - previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk multiplier.
