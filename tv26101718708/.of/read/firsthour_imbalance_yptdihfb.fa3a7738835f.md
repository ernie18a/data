Calculation: Session range = for each configured session independently, accumulate the highest high and lowest low of all chart bars whose opening times fall within its configured opening window; retain these extremes across successive session occurrences without resetting them, and freeze them when the opening window ends.
Calculation: Boundary confirmation count = for each session and boundary independently, reset to zero when the opening window ends; while the range is frozen and the session is active, increment when the bar reaches the boundary by the configured sweep depth and closes back inside it, reset to zero when it reaches that depth but does not close back inside, and otherwise leave unchanged. A boundary can signal only once per opening-window completion; reaching the next opening window resets its eligibility when that window ends.
Long: The session opening window has ended AND the session is active AND its low boundary has not yet signaled since the window ended AND low <= session range low - sweep depth AND close > session range low AND low-boundary confirmation count >= required count AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe close) AND (displacement filter disabled OR close - session range low >= ATR × displacement multiplier).
Short: The session opening window has ended AND the session is active AND its high boundary has not yet signaled since the window ended AND high >= session range high + sweep depth AND close < session range high AND high-boundary confirmation count >= required count AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe close) AND (displacement filter disabled OR session range high - close >= ATR × displacement multiplier).
Calculation: Sweep depth = zero when the depth filter is disabled; otherwise a configured price distance equivalent to a configured number of minimum price ticks.
