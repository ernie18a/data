Calculation: Low-sweep = low < any of the most recently completed UTC session low, previous UTC day low, most recently confirmed symmetric pivot low, or floor(close / round-number increment) × increment, AND close > that same level, AND min(open, close) − low > (high − low) × wick-ratio threshold / 2. A symmetric pivot is confirmed only after its right-hand lookback bars have elapsed.
Calculation: High-sweep = high > any of the most recently completed UTC session high, previous UTC day high, most recently confirmed symmetric pivot high, or ceil(close / round-number increment) × increment, AND close < that same level, AND high − max(open, close) > (high − low) × wick-ratio threshold / 2.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body-ratio threshold AND high − low > ATR × range multiplier AND a low-sweep occurred on the current bar or within the preceding sweep-window bars. The body ratio is zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body-ratio threshold AND high − low > ATR × range multiplier AND a high-sweep occurred on the current bar or within the preceding sweep-window bars. The body ratio is zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish bias = (short-term EMA > medium-term EMA AND medium-term EMA > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > its value at the slope lookback) OR (the latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low are each greater than their respective previous confirmed pivots AND close > long-term EMA). All EMAs and pivots use chart bars; the structure pivot lookback is independent of the sweep pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bearish bias = (short-term EMA < medium-term EMA AND medium-term EMA < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < its value at the slope lookback) OR (the latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low are each less than their respective previous confirmed pivots AND close < long-term EMA). All EMAs and pivots use chart bars; the structure pivot lookback is independent of the sweep pivot lookback.
Calculation: Zone score = liquidity weight × liquidity quality + displacement weight + inefficiency weight + session weight if the current UTC hour is in either preferred session + bias weight if the matching directional bias holds. Liquidity quality is the higher quality value only if the current bar sweeps the relevant completed session or previous-day level; otherwise it is the lower quality value, even when the preceding sweep did so.
Calculation: Bullish zone = on bullish displacement outside the excluded UTC hours, if the minimum spacing from the last created zone of either direction has elapsed and the bullish zone score meets the drawing threshold, use [high two bars ago, current low] when the configured zone type permits gaps AND current low > high two bars ago AND (current low − high two bars ago) / current close exceeds the gap-size threshold; otherwise use [previous low, previous high] when the configured type permits order blocks AND previous close < previous open. Prefer the gap when both qualify. Create no zone if neither qualifies.
Calculation: Bearish zone = on bearish displacement outside the excluded UTC hours, if the minimum spacing from the last created zone of either direction has elapsed and the bearish zone score meets the drawing threshold, use [current high, low two bars ago] when the configured zone type permits gaps AND current high < low two bars ago AND (low two bars ago − current high) / current close exceeds the gap-size threshold; otherwise use [previous low, previous high] when the configured type permits order blocks AND previous close > previous open. Prefer the gap when both qualify. Create no zone if neither qualifies. Process bullish creation before bearish creation.
Calculation: Active candidate zones = newly created zones, including on their creation bar, until their first retest, expiry, mitigation, invalidation, or removal as the oldest zone when the maximum stored-zone count is exceeded. Before checking a retest, expire a zone when its age exceeds the expiry threshold; mitigate a bullish zone when low < zone top − zone height × mitigation fraction or a bearish zone when high > zone bottom + zone height × mitigation fraction; invalidate a bullish zone when close < zone bottom and its age exceeds the invalidation delay, or a bearish zone when close > zone top and its age exceeds that delay. Keep expired, mitigated, invalidated, and retested zones in the stored-zone count; trim oldest zones after processing the current bar.
Long: Low-sweep outside the excluded UTC hours OR bullish displacement OR an active bullish candidate zone has low <= zone top AND low >= zone bottom AND close > zone top.
Short: High-sweep outside the excluded UTC hours OR bearish displacement OR an active bearish candidate zone has high >= zone bottom AND high <= zone top AND close < zone bottom.
