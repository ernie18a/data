Calculation: Trend moving average = selected SMA or EMA of chart-timeframe close; the higher-timeframe version uses the same MA type and a longer lookback on a higher timeframe, using that timeframe's bar still forming (historical lookahead enabled).
Calculation: Average volume = SMA of chart-timeframe volume. A volume surge is volume greater than average volume times a configurable multiplier.
Calculation: Near support = chart low within a configurable percentage tolerance of the latest confirmed pivot low; near resistance = chart high within that tolerance of the latest confirmed pivot high. Pivot left and right lookbacks are equal.
Long: bullish average strength ≥ minimum average strength AND bearish average strength < minimum average strength AND close > trend moving average AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > average volume × volume multiplier) AND (support confirmation disabled OR near support) AND (follow-through disabled OR close > open when candle confirmation is selected OR close > prior close when previous-close confirmation is selected) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe moving average).
Short: bearish average strength ≥ minimum average strength AND bullish average strength < minimum average strength AND close < trend moving average AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > average volume × volume multiplier) AND (resistance confirmation disabled OR near resistance) AND (follow-through disabled OR close < open when candle confirmation is selected OR close < prior close when previous-close confirmation is selected) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe moving average).
Exit: Long, short-entry conditions are true OR low ≤ trailing stop OR high ≥ any enabled take-profit level above entry price. The trailing stop is a configurable percentage below the best high since entry when trailing from entry is enabled, or until a take-profit is hit otherwise.
Exit: Short, long-entry conditions are true OR high ≥ trailing stop OR low ≤ any enabled take-profit level below entry price. The trailing stop is a configurable percentage above the best low since entry when trailing from entry is enabled, or until a take-profit is hit otherwise.
