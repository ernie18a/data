Calculation: For each direction d, transform prices to O[i]=d*open[i], C[i]=d*close[i], H[i]=max(d*high[i],d*low[i]), L[i]=min(d*high[i],d*low[i]), where d=1 for bullish and d=-1 for bearish, and i is the number of chart bars ago. Define body B[i]=abs(C[i]-O[i]), range R[i]=H[i]-L[i], upper shadow U[i]=H[i]-max(O[i],C[i]), and lower shadow D[i]=min(O[i],C[i])-L[i]. A positive candle has C[i]>O[i]; a negative candle has C[i]<O[i].
Calculation: A small body at i has B[i]<ATR[i]*small-body multiplier when the ATR filter is enabled, otherwise B[i]<R[i]*the pattern's small-body fraction. A long body at i has B[i]>ATR[i]*long-body multiplier when the ATR filter is enabled, otherwise B[i]>R[i]*the long-body fraction. A doji is a small body with U[i]>0 OR D[i]>0. A marubozu has B[i]>=R[i]*(1-marubozu tolerance). An inside bar at i has H[i]<H[i+1] AND L[i]>L[i+1].

Calculation: Directional average strength = the mean of the configured strengths of all occurring patterns in that direction whose individual strength is at least the individual-strength threshold; use zero if none qualify. Directional count = the number of those qualifying occurrences. The duplicated marubozu occurrence has the same strength for both copies. Each named occurrence has its own fixed strength parameter.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over equal numbers of bars before and after it. Use only the pivot confirmed on the immediately preceding chart bar for support and resistance comparisons.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe moving average = the selected SMA or EMA of higher-timeframe closes. On historical chart bars, the source uses the final value of the enclosing higher-timeframe bar before that bar has completed; on the current bar it uses the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long trailing base = entry signal close until trailing is enabled; then the maximum of that base and each subsequent chart-bar high while long. Short trailing base = entry signal close until trailing is enabled; then the minimum of that base and each subsequent chart-bar low while short. Trailing is enabled from entry or after a take-profit fill, according to the selected setting.
