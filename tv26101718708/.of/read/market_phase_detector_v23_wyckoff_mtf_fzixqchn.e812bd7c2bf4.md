Calculation: Trend score = clamp to [-100,100] the sum of: clamp(10 × (close − fast EMA) / ATR, −18,18); clamp(12 × (fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR, −22,22); clamp(15 × (fast EMA − its value 5 bars earlier) / ATR, −18,18); clamp(18 × (slow EMA − its value 5 bars earlier) / ATR, −16,16); clamp(2 × (close − close at the longer momentum lookback) / ATR, −14,14); and clamp(30 × average of the linear-regression slopes of high and low over the structure lookback / ATR, −12,12). The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback. Use a 14-bar ATR.
Calculation: Trading range = absolute(close − close at the range lookback) / sum of absolute close changes over that lookback is below the efficiency threshold. Range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Accumulation pressure = points for each true condition: trading range (20); CMF at or above its bullish threshold (20), otherwise CMF above zero (10); rising OBV slope (15); rising A/D slope (10); up-volume average greater than down-volume average (10); recent downside sweep (5); confirmed Spring (10); bullish structural recovery (10). Distribution pressure uses the corresponding bearish conditions: trading range (20); CMF at or below its bearish threshold (20), otherwise CMF below zero (10); falling OBV slope (15); falling A/D slope (10); down-volume average greater than up-volume average (10); recent upside sweep (5); confirmed UTAD (10); bearish structural damage (10). Clamp each score to 0–100. CMF uses the money-flow multiplier ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), weighted by volume over its lookback. OBV adds volume when close rises and subtracts it when close falls; A/D cumulatively sums the money-flow multiplier times volume. Their slopes are the current minus prior one-bar linear-regression values over the same lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed Spring = a downside sweep occurred within the sweep-memory lookback AND trading range AND close above range midpoint AND close above fast EMA AND high-regression slope below zero AND CMF above zero AND OBV slope above zero. Confirmed UTAD = an upside sweep occurred within the sweep-memory lookback AND trading range AND close below range midpoint AND close below fast EMA AND high-regression slope below zero AND CMF below zero AND OBV slope below zero.
Calculation: SOS = close above prior range high AND relative volume (volume / its average over the volume lookback) at or above the breakout threshold AND CMF above zero. SOW = close below prior range low AND relative volume at or above the breakout threshold AND CMF below zero.
Calculation: Market phase = first require the same raw phase for the phase-confirmation lookback; otherwise phase is neutral. If trading range: accumulation pressure is dominant when it reaches the phase threshold and exceeds distribution pressure by the required margin; distribution pressure is dominant under the converse condition. With dominant accumulation pressure, phase is Re-Accumulation if trend score meets its minimum, else Re-Distribution if trend score meets its maximum and bullish structural recovery is false, else Accumulation. With dominant distribution pressure, phase is Re-Accumulation if trend score meets the strong-bull gate and bearish structural damage and SOW are both false; else Re-Distribution if trend score meets its maximum; else Distribution. With neither dominant, phase is Re-Accumulation if trend score meets the strong-bull gate, Re-Distribution if it meets the strong-bear gate, else Neutral. Outside a trading range, phase is Markup if trend score meets the strong-trend threshold, Markdown if it meets the negative threshold, else Neutral. Bullish structural recovery = close above range midpoint AND close above fast EMA AND low-regression slope above zero. Bearish structural damage = close below range midpoint AND close below fast EMA AND high-regression slope below zero. A downside sweep = low below prior range low AND close above it; an upside sweep = high above prior range high AND close below it. Use the same range lookback for range and efficiency; the phase uses the longer trend and pressure conditions defined above.
Long: phase changed to Accumulation OR Markup OR Re-Accumulation OR confirmed Spring OR SOS.
Short: phase changed to Distribution OR Markdown OR Re-Distribution OR confirmed UTAD OR SOW.
