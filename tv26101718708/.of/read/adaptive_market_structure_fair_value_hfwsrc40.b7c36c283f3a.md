Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive pivot candidate = a high strictly above all highs, or a low strictly below all lows, in a preceding lookback. At creation, assign its required confirmation bars by linearly interpolating from the maximum to the minimum confirmation length using the EMA-smoothed close efficiency ratio, rounding to the nearest integer and clamping to those limits. Before the efficiency-ratio lookback is available, use zero as the unsmoothed ratio.
Calculation: Confirmed adaptive pivot = a candidate that survives its assigned number of subsequent bars without a strictly higher high for a high candidate or a strictly lower low for a low candidate. Confirm it on the final surviving bar; equal prices do not invalidate it. A confirmed high or low becomes broken when a completed bar closes strictly above or below it, respectively. Retain at most the most recent configured number of confirmed pivots per side.
Calculation: Adaptive KAMA = recursively update the previous KAMA toward close using the square of the linear interpolation between a slow smoothing constant and a faster smoothing constant, weighted by the EMA-smoothed close efficiency ratio; initialize with close. The slow constant is 2 divided by one plus the slow length.
Calculation: KAMA state = classify the smoothed close efficiency ratio into range, transition, directional, or high-efficiency bands using increasing thresholds. Outside the range band, assign the band a bullish or bearish direction according to the sign of the linear-regression slope of adaptive KAMA multiplied by the smoothed efficiency ratio; use range if that product is zero. If the optional acceptance filter is enabled, retain the previous state when moving from range to a direction or reversing direction unless close exceeds the highest close since the previous state began for a bullish change, or falls below the lowest close since it began for a bearish change. Accept same-direction band changes and changes to range immediately. Reset the state’s close extrema on every accepted state change; otherwise update them with the current close.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected price source from the session’s first bar through the current bar, reset at each session start; unavailable outside the session or when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: VWAP displacement strength = change in session VWAP over a lookback divided by the square root of that lookback and by the greater of an ATR-based noise floor and a minimum price-increment-based noise floor. Use the configured lookback for the chart timeframe.
Calculation: VWAP trend state = before both the VWAP efficiency-ratio EMA and displacement-strength standard-deviation warm-ups are complete, bullish when displacement strength is positive and bearish when negative. After both warm-ups, bullish when displacement strength exceeds a positive multiple of its rolling standard deviation and smoothed VWAP efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; bearish when displacement strength is below the negative of that multiple and smoothed VWAP efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold. Otherwise neutral.

Short: Current high is an adaptive pivot high candidate.

Short: An adaptive pivot high becomes confirmed on the current bar.

Short: The completed bar closes below an unbroken confirmed adaptive pivot low whose recency rank is within the configured structural depth.

Short: KAMA state is bearish.

Short: KAMA linear-regression slope is negative.
