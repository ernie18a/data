Calculation: Donchian midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over its lookback. Use a short lookback for the conversion line, a longer lookback for the base line, and a still longer lookback for span B. In classic mode use the configured lookbacks. In adaptive mode, for each base lookback P, use round(P × (1 + strength × (1 − 2 × volatility ratio))), clamped between max(round(P × (1 − strength)), minimum lookback) and round(P × (1 + strength)); volatility ratio is the ATR's position within its rolling minimum-to-maximum range, clamped to [0, 1], or the midpoint of that range when the range is zero.
Calculation: Cloud = span A, the mean of the conversion and base lines, and span B, the longest-lookback Donchian midpoint. The current cloud top and bottom are the maximum and minimum of those spans displaced back by the configured displacement minus one bar; use the current span when its displaced value is unavailable. Cloud thickness is top minus bottom; a thin cloud has thickness below a configured fraction of its rolling average thickness.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume is unavailable or nonpositive, OR volume exceeds its moving average multiplied by a confirmation multiplier. Strong breakout volume uses the same rule with a separately configured multiplier.
Calculation: Momentum = EMA of the clamped weighted sum of normalized conversion-minus-base spread, its change over a lookback, and the difference between the changes of undisplaced spans A and B over another lookback. Normalize spread and span changes by ATR, using zero when ATR is nonpositive. The spread-change and cloud-change lookbacks are separately configurable.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of configured points for: close beyond the cloud in the direction, or fewer points for close inside the cloud including its boundaries; conversion line beyond base line in the direction; source above or below its value at the displacement lookback in the direction; change in span A greater than change in span B for long, or less than or equal for short, over a separate lookback; cloud not thin, or fewer points if thin; volume confirmation; momentum beyond a directional strength threshold, or fewer points when merely on the directional side of zero; and momentum on the directional side of zero and moving further in that direction. The conversion/base and source components have equal weights; the cloud-slope, volume and momentum-acceleration components have equal weights; the full momentum-alignment weight is greater than each of those, and its partial weight is half its full weight. Cap the total at the score maximum.
Calculation: Warmup = bar index is at least the greater of the longest base Donchian lookback plus displacement and a configured minimum warmup.
Calculation: Breakout up = warmup AND close > current cloud top AND previous close <= previous cloud top AND strong breakout volume. Breakout down = warmup AND close < current cloud bottom AND previous close >= previous cloud bottom AND strong breakout volume. Initialize the most recent breakout bar index for each direction to zero and update it on every corresponding breakout.
Calculation: Entry stop = fixed at the entry bar: for a long, cloud bottom minus an ATR buffer, base line minus an ATR buffer, or entry price minus an ATR multiple; for a short, cloud top plus an ATR buffer, base line plus an ATR buffer, or entry price plus an ATR multiple. Risk distance = max(abs(entry price − entry stop), ATR × minimum-risk multiplier), evaluated at entry. The final target is entry price plus risk distance times the final-target multiplier for a long, or minus that amount for a short. Each new entry signal resets these levels and their hit state; if both directions signal on the same bar, the short levels replace the long levels. Check the entry bar as well as subsequent bars.
Long: Warmup AND conversion line crosses above base line AND directional long score >= minimum score AND (cloud filter disabled OR cloud is not thin) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume confirmation) AND (source confirmation disabled OR source > source at the displacement lookback).
Short: Warmup AND conversion line crosses below base line AND directional short score >= minimum score AND (cloud filter disabled OR cloud is not thin) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume confirmation) AND (source confirmation disabled OR source < source at the displacement lookback).
Long: Breakout up.
Short: Breakout down.
Long: Warmup AND bars since the most recent breakout up > minimum retest delay AND bars since that breakout <= maximum retest lookback AND low <= current cloud top AND close > current cloud top.
Short: Warmup AND bars since the most recent breakout down > minimum retest delay AND bars since that breakout <= maximum retest lookback AND high >= current cloud bottom AND close < current cloud bottom.
Long: Undisplaced span A > undisplaced span B.
Short: Undisplaced span A <= undisplaced span B.
Long: Close > current cloud top.
Short: Close < current cloud bottom.
Long: Momentum > 0.
Short: Momentum < 0.
Long: On a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, close > the maximum of undisplaced classic span A and span B AND classic conversion line > classic base line.
Short: On a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, close < the minimum of undisplaced classic span A and span B AND classic conversion line < classic base line.
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= final target.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= final target.
