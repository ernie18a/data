Calculation: Supertrend = standard Supertrend (ATR-based band, ATR length and factor as parameters); bullish when price is above the Supertrend line, bearish when below
Calculation: ADX = standard ADX with DI (Wilder)
Calculation: HTFHigh = highest high over the last N completed bars of a higher timeframe (last completed bar only, not the forming one); HTFLow = lowest low over the same bars; Range = HTFHigh - HTFLow
Calculation: BullZone = close between HTFHigh - Range*retraceMax and HTFHigh - Range*retraceMin (retraceMin < retraceMax); BearZone = close between HTFLow + Range*retraceMin and HTFLow + Range*retraceMax; each bar is evaluated with the HTFHigh/HTFLow/Range values known at that bar
Calculation: Donchian = highest high and lowest low of the previous N chart bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Keltner = EMA(close) +/- multiplier * ATR (upper/lower)
Long: bar is completed AND Supertrend is bullish AND ADX > threshold AND ATR > SMA of ATR (SMA length longer than the ATR length) AND ATR/close*100 >= minimum percent AND volume > SMA(volume)*multiplier (multiplier > 1) AND close > open AND volume > previous bar volume AND BullZone was true on the current bar or any of the last K bars AND close > previous bar high AND (close > Donchian high OR (fast EMA(close) > slow EMA(close) AND lowest low of the last M bars including the current <= fast EMA) OR close > Keltner upper) AND the same full condition was not true on the previous bar
Short: bar is completed AND Supertrend is bearish AND ADX > threshold AND ATR > SMA of ATR (SMA length longer than the ATR length) AND ATR/close*100 >= minimum percent AND volume > SMA(volume)*multiplier (multiplier > 1) AND close < open AND volume > previous bar volume AND BearZone was true on the current bar or any of the last K bars AND close < previous bar low AND (close < Donchian low OR (fast EMA(close) < slow EMA(close) AND highest high of the last M bars including the current >= fast EMA) OR close < Keltner lower) AND the same full condition was not true on the previous bar
