Calculation: Average range = simple moving average of high minus low over a lookback.
Calculation: Bullish vacuum quality = rounded 0–100 score: 30% of capped range-expansion score + 28% of capped body-to-range score + 18% of capped volume-participation score + 24% of capped bullish close-drive score. Each component is its measured ratio divided by its minimum threshold, multiplied by 100, and capped at 100; volume participation is 1 when average volume is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bearish vacuum quality = same score, using bearish close drive.
Long: close > open AND (high − low) / average range ≥ minimum displacement range AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body-to-range ratio AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ minimum close drive AND bullish vacuum quality ≥ minimum vacuum quality AND no prior qualified vacuum exists OR bars since the prior qualified vacuum ≥ cooldown.
Short: close < open AND (high − low) / average range ≥ minimum displacement range AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body-to-range ratio AND (high − close) / (high − low) ≥ minimum close drive AND bearish vacuum quality ≥ minimum vacuum quality AND no prior qualified vacuum exists OR bars since the prior qualified vacuum ≥ cooldown.
