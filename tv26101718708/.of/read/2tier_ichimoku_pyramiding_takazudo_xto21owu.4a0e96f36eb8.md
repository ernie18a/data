Calculation: Ichimoku lines = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the lookback; Tenkan and Kijun use shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively. Senkou A is their midpoint; Senkou B is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback. Use values from the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish alignment = base-timeframe Senkou A > Senkou B, close > close from the Chikou offset ago, Tenkan > Kijun, close > the cloud at the Chikou offset, and close > both the base-timeframe and highest-timeframe cloud tops. Bearish alignment reverses all comparisons. Cloud top/bottom are max/min of the Senkou lines, offset to the cloud position used by the strategy.
Calculation: Continuous bullish/bearish = bullish/bearish alignment holds for the configured number of consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: MACD direction = MACD line > signal line; down direction = MACD line < signal line. Use standard MACD with fast, slow, and signal lookbacks, where fast is shorter than slow.
Calculation: Range high/low = highest/lowest candle-body edge among prior chart bars, excluding the most recent bars as in the strategy; range high uses max(open, close), range low uses min(open, close).
Calculation: Entry padding = ATR over a lookback multiplied by a factor.
Calculation: Long stop = minimum of current low minus ATR-based stop distance, midpoint of base-timeframe cloud top and current low minus ATR-based stop distance, and lowest prior candle-body low over a lookback. Ratchet this stop upward while long.
Calculation: Short stop = maximum of current high plus ATR-based stop distance, midpoint of base-timeframe cloud bottom and current high plus ATR-based stop distance, and highest prior candle-body high over a lookback. Ratchet this stop downward while short.
Calculation: Extra-entry long limit = lowest candle-body low over a shorter recent lookback, excluding the latest bar, plus entry padding. Extra-entry short limit = highest candle-body high over that lookback, excluding the latest bar, minus entry padding.
Calculation: Initial long target = range high + (current hl2 − extra-entry long limit). Initial short target = range low − (extra-entry short limit − current hl2). Extra-entry long target = range high + entry padding; extra-entry short target = range low − entry padding.
Long: Continuous bullish AND ((first entry in the current trend AND bullish alignment AND, since trend change, MACD was down and then turned up) OR (after a range breakout, close > range high AND close > base-timeframe cloud top AND MACD is down AND close > range high)) AND high >= range high + entry padding.
Short: Continuous bearish AND ((first entry in the current trend AND bearish alignment AND, since trend change, MACD was up and then turned down) OR (after a range breakout, close < range low AND close < base-timeframe cloud bottom AND MACD is up AND close < range low)) AND low <= range low − entry padding.
Long: In a long position, a prior take-profit has triggered AND bullish alignment AND extra-entry long limit > previous entry price AND current candle is bullish (close > open, or an up-doji with body < one-seventh of range and body in the upper third) AND low <= extra-entry long limit.
Short: In a short position, a prior take-profit has triggered AND bearish alignment AND extra-entry short limit < previous entry price AND current candle is bearish (close < open, or a down-doji with body < one-seventh of range and body in the lower third) AND high >= extra-entry short limit.
Exit: Long, low <= ratcheting long stop OR high >= initial long target OR high >= extra-entry long target.
Exit: Short, high >= ratcheting short stop OR low <= initial short target OR low <= extra-entry short target.
