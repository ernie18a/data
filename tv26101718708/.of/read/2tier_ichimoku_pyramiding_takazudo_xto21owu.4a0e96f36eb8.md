Calculation: Higher-timeframe Ichimoku series = unsmoothed Ichimoku values from the last completed bar of each of two higher timeframes, forward-filled onto chart bars; the second timeframe is higher than the first. If the second timeframe is disabled or unavailable, use chart-timeframe values for it. The conversion, base, and leading-span-B periods are respectively short, intermediate, and long. For each timeframe, the current cloud bounds are the maximum and minimum of its leading spans shifted backward by the configured forward displacement expressed in chart bars. The lagging-cloud bounds use an additional backward lagging displacement. Use the first higher timeframe for conversion, base, and lagging comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish state = first higher-timeframe leading span A > leading span B AND close > close at the lagging displacement AND conversion line > base line AND close > upper lagging-cloud bound AND close > upper current-cloud bound of each timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish state = first higher-timeframe leading span A <= leading span B AND close < close at the lagging displacement AND conversion line < base line AND close < lower lagging-cloud bound AND close < lower current-cloud bound of each timeframe.
Calculation: Continuous bullish or bearish state = the corresponding state holds on every bar of a configurable consecutive-bar lookback.
Calculation: Range high and range low = respectively the highest candle-body top and lowest candle-body bottom over a configurable lookback ending before the most recent bars. Recent high and recent low use a separate lookback of preceding candle bodies. Extra-entry high and low use another lookback of preceding candle bodies that excludes the most recent bar.
Calculation: Entry padding = an ATR multiplied by its configurable multiplier. Extra-entry long price = extra-entry low + entry padding. Extra-entry short price = extra-entry high - entry padding. The entry-padding ATR and trailing-stop ATR have independently configurable periods.
Calculation: Trend regime = bullish when close is above both current clouds, bearish when below both, and neutral otherwise; retain the prior regime temporarily after leaving either directional regime. A regime change resets the first-entry flag and range-break flags. A position sets the first-entry flag false; closing the position resets the range-break detection and confirmation flags.
Calculation: First-entry confirmation = while the first-entry flag is true and the bullish state holds, remember a MACD line <= its signal line and then confirm when the MACD line > its signal line; for the bearish state, remember MACD line > signal line and then confirm when MACD line <= signal line. Clear confirmation whenever either directional state changes or the first-entry flag becomes false. Reset the remembered reversal on a regime change. The MACD fast period is shorter than its slow period.
Calculation: Later-entry confirmation = while flat and the first-entry flag is false, remember a continuous bullish state with close > range high or a continuous bearish state with close < range low. After detection, set resistance to the current range high when close > the first cloud's upper bound, or to the current range low when close < its lower bound; confirm on MACD line <= signal line in the former case or MACD line > signal line in the latter. Keep confirmation until reset by a regime change or position closure.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = while flat with a long entry decision, initialize to the minimum of low - trailing ATR multiplied by its multiplier, the average of that value and the first cloud's upper bound, and recent low; while long, update to the maximum of its previous value and that same minimum. Short trailing stop = while flat with a short entry decision, initialize to the maximum of high + trailing ATR multiplied by its multiplier, the average of that value and the first cloud's lower bound, and recent high; while short, update to the minimum of its previous value and that same maximum.
Calculation: Initial long target = range high + candle midpoint - extra-entry long price; initial short target = range low - extra-entry short price + candle midpoint. Fix each target at its last value while flat. Extra-entry long target = range high + entry padding; extra-entry short target = range low - entry padding.
Long: First higher-timeframe leading span A > leading span B OR enabled second higher-timeframe leading span A > leading span B.
Short: First higher-timeframe leading span A <= leading span B OR enabled second higher-timeframe leading span A <= leading span B.
Long: Continuous bullish state.
Short: Continuous bearish state.
Long: First-entry confirmation changes from false to true AND bullish state.
Short: First-entry confirmation changes from false to true AND bullish state is false.
Long: Later-entry range-break detection changes from false to true AND bullish state.
Short: Later-entry range-break detection changes from false to true AND bullish state is false.
Long: Flat AND continuous bullish state AND ((first-entry flag AND first-entry confirmation) OR (later-entry confirmation AND close > resistance)) AND a pending buy stop at range high + entry padding is reached; cancel the pending order when continuous bullish state is false.
Short: Flat AND continuous bearish state AND ((first-entry flag AND first-entry confirmation) OR (later-entry confirmation AND close < resistance)) AND a pending sell stop at range low - entry padding is reached; cancel the pending order when continuous bearish state is false.
Long: Long position AND a partial take-profit has reduced the position below its initial size AND bullish state AND extra-entry long price > previous submitted entry reference AND (close > open OR (absolute candle body < candle range / 7 AND candle-body top > low + 2 * candle range / 3)) AND a buy limit at extra-entry long price is reached. The initial reference is the range high when the initial order is submitted; update it to the extra-entry long price when an extra order is submitted. Cancel the extra order when its readiness conditions or continuous bullish state are false.
Short: Short position AND a partial take-profit has reduced the position below its initial size AND bearish state AND extra-entry short price < previous submitted entry reference AND (close < open OR (absolute candle body < candle range / 7 AND candle-body bottom < high - 2 * candle range / 3)) AND a sell limit at extra-entry short price is reached. The initial reference is the range low when the initial order is submitted; update it to the extra-entry short price when an extra order is submitted. Cancel the extra order when its readiness conditions or continuous bearish state are false.
Exit: Long, low <= long trailing stop.
Exit: Short, high >= short trailing stop.
Exit: Long initial entry, high >= initial long target; exit half the initial quantity.
Exit: Short initial entry, low <= initial short target; exit half the initial quantity.
Exit: Long extra entry, high >= extra-entry long target.
Exit: Short extra entry, low <= extra-entry short target.
