Calculation: Exhaustion(bars) = for a bar: range = high - low; body = abs(close - open), replaced by a tiny positive value if 0; upperWick = high - max(open, close); lowerWick = min(open, close) - low; bigRange = range > SMA(range, lookback) * rangeMultiplier; volSpike = volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * volMultiplier (this volume check can be switched off, then always true); UpperExhaustion = bigRange AND volSpike AND upperWick > body * wickRatio AND upperWick > lowerWick; LowerExhaustion = bigRange AND volSpike AND lowerWick > body * wickRatio AND lowerWick > upperWick
Long: LowerExhaustion is true on the current chart-timeframe bar OR LowerExhaustion is true on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart
Short: UpperExhaustion is true on the current chart-timeframe bar OR UpperExhaustion is true on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart
