Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow bands = exponential moving averages of the signal source, using progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Multi-scale vote = mean(sign of each band’s one-bar change divided by ATR) × clamp(mean absolute normalized change, 0, 1).
Calculation: Standardized log return = (one-bar change in log signal source − its moving average) / its moving standard deviation.
Calculation: Upward and downward CUSUM = respectively max(0, prior upward CUSUM + standardized log return − drift) and min(0, prior downward CUSUM + standardized log return + drift); reset the respective accumulator to zero when it crosses its positive or negative detection threshold.
Calculation: CUSUM vote = +1 on an upward threshold crossing, −1 on a downward threshold crossing, otherwise the prior vote multiplied by a decay factor.
Calculation: Conformal residual = signal source − (prior signal source + moving average of the signal source’s one-bar change); conformal radius = empirical (1 − miscoverage) quantile of the rolling absolute residuals; anomaly direction = sign(residual) when absolute residual / radius > 1, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Conformal confidence = clamp(1 − (absolute residual / conformal radius − 1), 0, 1); use 0 for the ratio when the radius is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Permutation entropy = Shannon entropy of ordinal patterns in the rolling signal-source window, normalized by log of the factorial of the embedding dimension.
Calculation: Adaptive momentum vote = clamp(1.5 × (smoothed RSI − 50) / 50, −1, 1); RSI smoothing uses an exponential moving average. RSI lookback is shorter than its volatility reference lookback. Adaptive bounds are 50 ± 20 × clamp(standard deviation of one-bar source changes / its moving average, 0.5, 2), with the upper and lower bounds clamped to 55–90 and 10–45 respectively.
Calculation: Weighted net vote = clamp(weighted sum of multi-scale vote, CUSUM vote, momentum vote, and half-weighted anomaly direction divided by the sum of their weights, multiplied by (0.85 + 0.3 × entropy-gate strength) × conformal confidence floor × entropy predictability factor, −1, 1). Conformal confidence floor = clamp(conformal confidence, 0.35, 1); entropy predictability factor = clamp(0.6 + 0.4 × (1 − permutation entropy), 0.5, 1), multiplied by 0.5 when permutation entropy exceeds the chaos threshold.
Calculation: Agreement count = number of nonzero weighted votes among multi-scale, CUSUM, momentum, and anomaly votes whose sign matches the net-vote sign, plus one when nonzero multi-scale alignment has that sign.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = sign of the 20-period EMA minus the 50-period EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: upward CUSUM crosses its positive detection threshold OR net vote ≥ consensus threshold AND agreement count ≥ minimum agreeing mechanisms AND permutation entropy ≤ chaos threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is zero or positive) AND cooldown since the last consensus signal has elapsed.
Short: downward CUSUM crosses its negative detection threshold OR net vote ≤ −consensus threshold AND agreement count ≥ minimum agreeing mechanisms AND permutation entropy ≤ chaos threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is zero or negative) AND cooldown since the last consensus signal has elapsed.
