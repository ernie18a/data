Calculation: Source = a configurable price series from the input bar fields; default to close. A zero divisor produces zero.
Calculation: Scale alignment and vote = calculate three EMAs of source with short, intermediate, and long lengths. Divide each one-bar EMA change by ATR; alignment is the mean of the three slope signs, and scale vote is alignment times the mean absolute slope clamped to [0, 1].
Calculation: Change-point accumulators and vote = standardize the one-bar log return using its rolling mean and standard deviation. Starting from zero, update the positive accumulator to max(0, previous positive accumulator + standardized return − drift) and the negative accumulator to min(0, previous negative accumulator + standardized return + drift). Evaluate their threshold comparisons before resetting each triggered accumulator to zero. The vote is +1 on an upward trigger, otherwise −1 on a downward trigger, otherwise the previous vote multiplied by 0.8; start the vote at zero.
Calculation: Conformal score and anomaly direction = residual is source minus the sum of the previous source and the SMA of source changes; substitute zero for unavailable previous source or drift. Keep a rolling calibration window of absolute residuals including the current residual. Once at least 20 residuals exist, sort them and select index floor((1 − miscoverage) × (count − 1)) as the radius. Ratio is absolute residual divided by radius, or zero if the radius is unavailable or zero. Conformal score is clamp(2 − ratio, 0, 1); anomaly direction is the sign of the residual if ratio > 1, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Entropy = for each offset in the entropy window, take an embedding-length sequence of source values from oldest to newest, substituting zero for unavailable values. For each position i, count later values strictly smaller than its value; sum each count multiplied by the product of (embedding length − j) for preceding positions j. Count codes in [0, embedding length factorial), form their relative frequencies, and divide their Shannon entropy by the logarithm of embedding length factorial.
Calculation: Momentum vote = clamp(1.5 × (EMA(RSI(source)) − 50) / 50, −1, 1).
Calculation: Net vote and agreement = weight the scale, change-point, and momentum votes separately; weight anomaly direction by half its gate weight. Divide their sum by the sum of those four weights. Multiply by (0.85 + 0.3 × entropy weight), clamp(conformal score, 0.35, 1), and clamp(0.6 + 0.4 × clamp(1 − entropy, 0, 1), 0.5, 1); multiply by 0.5 if entropy exceeds its gate threshold, then clamp to [−1, 1]. Agreement is the count of the four weighted votes whose signs match the nonzero net vote sign, plus one if the nonzero scale alignment has that sign.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = sign of the shorter-length EMA of higher-timeframe close minus the longer-length EMA of higher-timeframe close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Long: Positive change-point accumulator > change-point threshold.
Short: Negative change-point accumulator < −change-point threshold.
Long: Net vote >= consensus threshold AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND entropy <= gate threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe trend >= 0) AND bars since the last consensus long or short >= cooldown.
Short: Net vote <= −consensus threshold AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND entropy <= gate threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe trend <= 0) AND bars since the last consensus long or short >= cooldown.
Long: Long-length EMA of source > its previous value OR intermediate-length EMA of source > its previous value.
Short: Long-length EMA of source <= its previous value OR intermediate-length EMA of source <= its previous value.
Long: Net vote > 0 OR scale vote > 0 OR change-point vote > 0 OR momentum vote > 0.
Short: Net vote <= 0 OR scale vote <= 0 OR change-point vote <= 0 OR momentum vote <= 0.
Long: Higher-timeframe confirmation enabled AND higher-timeframe trend > 0.
Short: Higher-timeframe confirmation enabled AND higher-timeframe trend < 0.
