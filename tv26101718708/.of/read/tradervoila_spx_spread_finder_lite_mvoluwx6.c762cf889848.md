Calculation: Support = most recently confirmed pivot low, using equal left and right lookbacks; replace the stored support only when the new pivot differs from it by at least the minimum level separation. Resistance is defined analogously using pivot highs and its own stored level.
Calculation: Bullish setup = (close > support AND close - support <= pivot zone thickness OR prior-period levels enabled AND close > prior-period low AND close - prior-period low <= prior-period zone thickness) AND (MA filter disabled OR short-lookback SMA > long-lookback SMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP of close) AND (VWAP slope filter disabled OR VWAP of close > its value at the slope lookback) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= bullish threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session AND elapsed minutes since the first in-session bar >= opening delay AND (cutoff disabled OR bar time < cutoff in America/New_York)).
Calculation: Bearish setup = (close < resistance AND resistance - close <= pivot zone thickness OR prior-period levels enabled AND close < prior-period high AND prior-period high - close <= prior-period zone thickness) AND (MA filter disabled OR short-lookback SMA < long-lookback SMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP of close) AND (VWAP slope filter disabled OR VWAP of close < its value at the slope lookback) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= bearish threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session AND elapsed minutes since the first in-session bar >= opening delay AND (cutoff disabled OR bar time < cutoff in America/New_York)).
Calculation: Prior-period high and low are from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The SMAs use higher-timeframe closes: the last completed bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time. VWAP resets at each daily boundary. All other calculations use chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Bullish setup is true AND bullish setup on the preceding bar is false AND bars since the most recent emitted long or short signal > cooldown, with the initial last-signal bar index set to a negative offset.
Short: Bearish setup is true AND bearish setup on the preceding bar is false AND bars since the most recent emitted long or short signal > cooldown, with the initial last-signal bar index set to the same negative offset.
