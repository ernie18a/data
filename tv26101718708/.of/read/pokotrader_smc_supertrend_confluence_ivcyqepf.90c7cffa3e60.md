Calculation: Structure pivots = update the latest pivot high when a new bearish leg begins and the latest pivot low when a new bullish leg begins; a pivot is confirmed when the high (or low) N bars back exceeds the highest (or lowest) of the N more recent bars. Use the shorter lookback for internal structure and the longer lookback for swing structure.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = on a new bar of the configured FVG timeframe, the still-forming bar's low is above the high two bars back, the last completed bar's close is above that high, and its positive open-to-close return exceeds the enabled auto threshold. Bearish fair value gap = the inverse: still-forming bar's high below the low two bars back, last completed close below that low, and the absolute negative return exceeds the threshold. If this timeframe differs from the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower; use the still-forming bar and completed prior bars as specified.
Calculation: Active internal order block = on a bullish structure break, the price range of the bar with the lowest parsed low between the pivot and break; on a bearish break, the range of the bar with the highest parsed high in that interval. A bar is parsed by swapping its high and low when its range is at least twice the volatility measure. Keep the block active until mitigated; a tap occurs when the chart bar's range overlaps the block's price range.
Long: Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish OR close crosses above the latest unbroken internal or swing pivot high OR a bullish fair value gap forms OR Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish AND (a bullish internal or swing structure break occurred within the configured lookback OR the bar taps an active bullish internal order block OR a bullish fair value gap forms).
Short: Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish OR close crosses below the latest unbroken internal or swing pivot low OR a bearish fair value gap forms OR Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish AND (a bearish internal or swing structure break occurred within the configured lookback OR the bar taps an active bearish internal order block OR a bearish fair value gap forms).
