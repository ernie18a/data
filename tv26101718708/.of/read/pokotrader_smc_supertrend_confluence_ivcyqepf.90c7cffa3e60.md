Calculation: Supertrend state = Initialize bullish. Set raw lower band to source minus ATR multiplier times ATR, and raw upper band to source plus ATR multiplier times ATR; source defaults to (high + low) / 2, and ATR may use Wilder smoothing or a simple moving average of true range. Carry the lower band forward as the maximum of its raw value and previous lower band when previous close is above the previous lower band; otherwise use its raw value. Carry the upper band forward as the minimum of its raw value and previous upper band when previous close is below the previous upper band; otherwise use its raw value. Change bearish state to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper band; change bullish state to bearish when close falls below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the state.
Calculation: Structure pivots = Maintain separate internal and swing legs, using a shorter lookback for internal structure. Initialize each leg bearish. A bar whose high lookback bars ago exceeds every high in the intervening bars starts a bearish leg; otherwise, if its low lookback bars ago is below every low in the intervening bars, it starts a bullish leg. When the leg changes, record that earlier bar's high as the latest high pivot for a bearish leg or its low as the latest low pivot for a bullish leg, and mark that pivot unbroken.
Calculation: Structure break = A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest unbroken high pivot; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest unbroken low pivot. Mark the pivot broken after the break. For an internal break, also require its pivot to differ from the corresponding swing pivot; if the optional internal filter is enabled, require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for a bullish break, or high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low) for a bearish break. Maintain separate internal and swing structure states, initialized neutral and set to the direction of their latest break.
Calculation: Internal order block = On an internal structure break, examine bars from the broken pivot bar through the bar before the break. For each bar, swap its high and low when its range is at least twice the chosen volatility measure; the measure is ATR or cumulative true range divided by bar index. For a bullish break select the first bar with the minimum resulting low; for a bearish break select the first bar with the maximum resulting high. Store its resulting high and low and the break direction. Retain the most recent configured number of active blocks for tap checks. Remove a bullish block when low, or optionally close, falls below its stored low; remove a bearish block when high, or optionally close, exceeds its stored high. A tap occurs when current low <= stored high AND current high >= stored low.
Calculation: Fair value gap = At the start of a new gap-timeframe bar, compare its high and low with the high and low two gap-timeframe bars earlier and the last completed gap-timeframe bar's open and close. The gap timeframe can equal or be higher than the chart timeframe. Its current high and low are from the bar still forming in real time; historical lookahead uses that bar's final high and low. Let delta = (last completed close - last completed open) / (last completed open * 100). The optional threshold is twice the cumulative sum of absolute delta observed at gap-timeframe changes divided by the current chart bar index; otherwise it is zero. A bullish gap requires current low > the high two gap bars earlier AND last completed close > that high AND delta > threshold. A bearish gap requires current high < the low two gap bars earlier AND last completed close < that low AND -delta > threshold.
