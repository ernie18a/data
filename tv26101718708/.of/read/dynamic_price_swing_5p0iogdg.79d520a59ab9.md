Calculation: Prior channel high and low = respectively the maximum of the highest high-source values and minimum of the lowest low-source values over two independently selected lookbacks, evaluated on the previous bar. The high and low sources are selectable price fields. Channel midpoint = (prior channel high + prior channel low) / 2.
Calculation: Range width = EMA of EMA of absolute close-to-close change, with the second EMA length equal to twice the first length minus 1, multiplied by the channel price-lookback parameter. Range filter = previous range filter initialized to 0, raised to high source minus range width when high source is above the previous filter and that difference exceeds it, or lowered to high source plus range width when high source is at or below the previous filter and that sum is below it; otherwise retain the previous filter. Upward range trend = high source > range filter AND high source != previous high source AND the last nonzero change in range filter > 0. Downward range trend = high source < range filter AND high source != previous high source AND the last nonzero change in range filter < 0.
Calculation: Momentum line = (fast normalized average - slow normalized average) * 100, where fast normalized average = (shorter-period selected moving average of close - highest high over that period) / (highest high - lowest low over that period), and slow normalized average = (longer-period selected moving average of close - lowest low over that period) / (highest high - lowest low over that period). The selected moving average type is EMA, SMA, VWMA, or WMA. Momentum signal = selected moving average of momentum line over the channel trend-lookback period.
Calculation: Volume flow = sum of volume on bars with close > open minus sum of volume on all other bars over the channel price-lookback period. Volume divergence = (volume flow > 0 AND previous volume flow > 0 AND close < open AND volume >= average volume of bars with close <= open over that lookback) OR (volume flow < 0 AND previous volume flow < 0 AND close > open AND volume >= average volume of bars with close > open over that lookback).
Calculation: Triple-smoothed price = three successive EMAs of (2 * close + high + low) / 4 using the same length. Fast price momentum = 10 * 100 * (triple-smoothed price - previous triple-smoothed price) / previous triple-smoothed price. Slow price momentum = 10 * ALMA of the SMA of that percentage change, with independently selected SMA and ALMA parameters.
Calculation: Moving-average upper and lower bands = channel midpoint multiplied by 1 plus or minus the selected percentage. Select one of three independently parameterized percentage bands.
Calculation: Fibonacci upper and lower bands = channel midpoint plus or minus ATR over the channel price-lookback period multiplied by the selected Fibonacci multiplier. Use two independently selected multipliers, called first and second.
Calculation: Buy condition = within the selected backtest date window AND one selected strategy's condition: price channel: (low < prior channel low AND upward range trend) OR (fast price momentum > slow price momentum AND fast price momentum < 0 AND upward range trend); moving average: low < selected lower band AND (upward range trend OR momentum line crosses above momentum signal); Fibonacci: (current low and each of the previous three lows <= current second lower Fibonacci band) OR (low <= second lower Fibonacci band AND volume divergence) OR (low <= first lower Fibonacci band AND momentum line crosses above momentum signal); peaks and valleys: low < prior channel low AND (momentum line crosses above momentum signal OR volume divergence).
Calculation: Sell condition = within the selected backtest date window AND one selected strategy's condition: price channel: (high >= prior channel high AND downward range trend) OR (fast price momentum < slow price momentum AND fast price momentum > 0 AND downward range trend) OR ((previous high - previous close) / previous close > selected knife percentage); moving average: high > selected upper band AND (downward range trend OR momentum line crosses below momentum signal); Fibonacci: (previous high >= current first upper Fibonacci band AND high >= current second upper Fibonacci band) OR (previous high >= current first upper Fibonacci band AND close < current second upper Fibonacci band) OR (high >= first upper Fibonacci band AND volume divergence) OR high > second upper Fibonacci band; peaks and valleys: high >= prior channel high AND (momentum line crosses below momentum signal OR volume divergence).
Long: Buy condition AND position side = flat AND selected trade mode permits long entries.
Short: Sell condition AND position side = flat AND selected trade mode permits short entries AND (trade mode is short-only OR previous bar's position side = flat).
Exit: long, Sell condition OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + profit-target fraction).
Exit: short, Buy condition OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price * (1 - profit-target fraction).
