Calculation: TRIN = (advancing issues / declining issues) / (advancing volume / declining volume)
Calculation: Dynamic TRIN threshold = TRIN's simple moving average + multiplier × TRIN's standard deviation, both using the same lookback
Calculation: Long stop price = entry price − multiplier × standard deviation of chart-timeframe closes over a lookback
Long: TRIN from the higher-timeframe bar still forming > dynamic TRIN threshold calculated from higher-timeframe bars
Exit: long, chart-timeframe close > previous chart-timeframe bar high OR chart-timeframe low ≤ long stop price
