Calculation: Accumulation = On confirmed bars with the engine enabled, when idle and at least three bars after the last setup ended, start if ATR > 0 AND the highest high minus lowest low over the range lookback <= the compression multiple × ATR. Set the start bar to the later of the lookback's first bar and the bar after the last setup ended; initialize the range high and low to the lookback's highest high and lowest low. On each subsequent bar, first test the close against the existing range. If it is outside, end the accumulation: accept a breakout only if bars since the start >= the minimum accumulation bars AND the optional higher-timeframe bias agrees; otherwise discard it. If the close is inside or on the range, expand the range using that bar's high and low, then discard it if its height > the abandon multiple × the compression multiple × current ATR OR bars since the start > the maximum accumulation bars. A discarded setup ends on the current bar. The setup machine continues while a position is open and pauses while the engine is disabled.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = Compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA on that same completed higher-timeframe bar; above is long, below is short, and equality or unavailable values agree with neither direction.
Calculation: Setup profile = At an accepted breakout, divide the accumulated range into equal price rows. Profile the bars from the accumulation start through the bar before breakout, assigning each bar's positive volume, or a weight of one if volume is missing or nonpositive, equally across all rows touched by its low-to-high span; clamp row indices to the profile. POC is the midpoint of the first lowest-index row with maximum weight, or the middle row if total weight is zero. Starting at the POC row, add the heavier adjacent row until the selected value-area percentage of total weight is reached or no rows remain; choose the upper row on ties. The value-area low and high are the outer edges of the included rows. Freeze these levels and the accumulation range at breakout.
Calculation: Pullback setup = After an accepted breakout, wait at least one bar for a long setup's low <= POC + tolerance × range height or a short setup's high >= POC - tolerance × range height. Discard the setup if bars since breakout > the maximum wait OR, for a long setup, close < value-area low OR, for a short setup, close > value-area high. After a touch, the entry trigger may occur on the same bar or a later bar; continue applying the same invalidation and timeout. If the trigger occurs while entry is not ready, discard the setup. An accepted breakout records the breakout bar as the last setup end; a discarded setup records the discard bar as the last setup end.
Calculation: Entry readiness = The engine is enabled AND the bar is confirmed AND no position is open AND no position exited on this bar AND bars since the last entry >= the entry cooldown AND either the optional post-exit cooldown is disabled or bars since the last exit >= the cooldown assigned to that exit AND either end-of-day flattening is disabled or the bar's New York opening time is before the configured flatten time. The post-exit cooldown is selected by profitable target/stop exit, nonprofitable target/stop exit, or time/end-of-day exit.
Calculation: Target and stop = At entry, freeze separate target and stop distances as either separate percentages of entry price or separate multiples of entry-bar chart ATR. For a long, target = entry price + target distance and stop = entry price - stop distance; for a short, target = entry price - target distance and stop = entry price + stop distance. Exit checks begin on the bar after entry; if several exits coincide, apply stop first, then target, then time limit, then end-of-day. Target and stop exits use their respective level as the exit price; time and end-of-day exits use close.
Long: Accepted upward breakout AND pullback setup touched and not discarded AND close > POC AND close > open AND entry readiness = true.
Short: Accepted downward breakout AND pullback setup touched and not discarded AND close < POC AND close < open AND entry readiness = true.
Exit: long, bars since entry >= 1 AND (low <= stop OR high >= target OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade OR (end-of-day flattening enabled AND bar's New York opening time >= configured flatten time)).
Exit: short, bars since entry >= 1 AND (high >= stop OR low <= target OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade OR (end-of-day flattening enabled AND bar's New York opening time >= configured flatten time)).
