Calculation: Accumulation range = Open when the highest high minus lowest low over the range lookback is at most the compression multiple times chart-timeframe ATR; initialize its high and low to that lookback range, then extend them with each bar's high and low until breakout or abandonment.
Calculation: Setup volume profile levels = Over the accumulation bars before the breakout bar, divide the accumulation price range into equal rows; distribute each bar's volume equally across the rows spanned by its low and high. POC is the midpoint of the row with greatest volume. Starting at the POC row, expand the value area toward the adjacent row with more volume until it contains the configured share of total volume; VAL and VAH are its lower and upper boundaries.
Calculation: HTF bias = Prior completed higher-timeframe close compared with the EMA of closes on that same higher timeframe; bullish if close is above EMA, bearish if below.
Calculation: Take-profit and stop-loss levels = From entry price, use configured percentages of entry price or configured multiples of chart-timeframe ATR; for a long, TP is above entry and SL below, and for a short, TP is below entry and SL above.
Long: On a confirmed bar, while flat and entry/cooldown/session gates allow entry, an active accumulation range closes above its high after the minimum accumulation duration, remains within abandonment limits, and (if the HTF filter is enabled) has bullish HTF bias; then, within the pullback wait limit, price touches the POC zone (low <= POC + tolerance) without closing below VAL, and a confirmed bar closes above POC and above its open, without closing below VAL; entry is at that close.
Short: On a confirmed bar, while flat and entry/cooldown/session gates allow entry, an active accumulation range closes below its low after the minimum accumulation duration, remains within abandonment limits, and (if the HTF filter is enabled) has bearish HTF bias; then, within the pullback wait limit, price touches the POC zone (high >= POC - tolerance) without closing above VAH, and a confirmed bar closes below POC and below its open, without closing above VAH; entry is at that close.
Exit: long, low <= stop-loss level OR high >= take-profit level OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration OR confirmed bar time reaches the configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
Exit: short, high >= stop-loss level OR low <= take-profit level OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration OR confirmed bar time reaches the configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
