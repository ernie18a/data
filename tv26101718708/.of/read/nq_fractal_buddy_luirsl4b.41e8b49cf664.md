Calculation: Bullish C2 = On a higher timeframe, for the last completed bar, its low is below the prior bar’s low and its close is above the prior low, but it did not also exceed the prior high.
Calculation: Bearish C2 = On a higher timeframe, for the last completed bar, its high is above the prior bar’s high and its close is below the prior high, but it did not also fall below the prior low.
Calculation: Bullish C3 = On a higher timeframe, for the last completed bar, the prior bar is bearish; the current low is at or above the prior low; the current open is at or below the prior body bottom; the current close is above the prior body top; the prior bar was not a bullish C2 against the bar before it; and the current bar is neither a bullish nor bearish C2.
Calculation: Bearish C3 = On a higher timeframe, for the last completed bar, the prior bar is bullish; the current high is at or below the prior high; the current open is at or above the prior body top; the current close is below the prior body bottom; the prior bar was not a bearish C2 against the bar before it; and the current bar is neither a bullish nor bearish C2.
Calculation: Active CISD level = On the selected CISD timeframe, track a delivery bias from candle direction (up when close > open, down when close < open; initialize from the most recent non-doji direction). For up bias, the level is the lowest open in the current uninterrupted run of up candles; for down bias, it is the highest open in the current uninterrupted run of down candles. When price makes a new high under up bias or a new low under down bias, reset the level to the corresponding extreme open in the current or immediately preceding uninterrupted run of that bias. After a confirmed close through the level, switch bias to the new direction and reset the level from that direction’s current uninterrupted run. Use the last completed bar of the selected CISD timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe itself).
Long: Bullish C2 OR Bullish C3 OR (CISD bias is down AND close > Active CISD level)
Short: Bearish C2 OR Bearish C3 OR (CISD bias is up AND close < Active CISD level)
