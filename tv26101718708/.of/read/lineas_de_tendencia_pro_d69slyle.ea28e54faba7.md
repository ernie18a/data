Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is the lowest within the lookback on each side; confirmed after the bars on the right are complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is the highest within the lookback on each side; confirmed after the bars on the right are complete.
Calculation: Rising support line = the line through the latest two confirmed swing lows, projected to the current bar; valid when the newer low is higher than the older low.
Calculation: Falling resistance line = the line through the latest two confirmed swing highs, projected to the current bar; valid when the newer high is lower than the older high.
Calculation: Strong volume = volume > moving average of volume over a lookback × a multiplier.
Calculation: Near line = absolute difference between close and the projected line < projected line × a tolerance percentage / 100.
Long: Rising support line is valid AND near line at the rising support line AND strong volume AND close > open
Short: Falling resistance line is valid AND near line at the falling resistance line AND strong volume AND close < open
