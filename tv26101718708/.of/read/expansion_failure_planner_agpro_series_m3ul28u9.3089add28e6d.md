Calculation: Prior range = the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending on the preceding bar; its midpoint is their average. The range is valid when its high is greater than its low.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average; use a neutral ratio if the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Relative volume = volume divided by its simple moving average; use a neutral ratio if the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), using a positive minimum denominator.
Calculation: Upside attempt = valid prior range AND (current bar range / ATR >= sensitivity-adjusted expansion threshold OR ATR ratio >= volatility expansion threshold) AND high > prior range high + ATR × sensitivity-adjusted edge buffer AND close location >= sensitivity-adjusted close-location threshold. Downside attempt = valid prior range AND the same expansion test AND low < prior range low - ATR × sensitivity-adjusted edge buffer AND close location <= the complement of the same close-location threshold. If both attempts occur, select upside when close >= prior range midpoint; otherwise select downside. Apply the configured side restriction and, except in realtime mode, evaluate attempts on closed bars.
Calculation: Active attempt = the most recent attempt, replaced immediately by every new attempt. Store its direction, bar index, range edge, range midpoint, ATR, bar range / ATR, relative volume, and extreme high for upside or extreme low for downside. Update its best extreme with each subsequent bar's high for upside or low for downside, including after its evaluation window expires.
Calculation: Progress = distance from the stored edge to the best extreme in the attempt direction / stored ATR. Inside depth = distance from the stored edge to close opposite the attempt direction / stored ATR. Edge distance = negative inside depth. Attempt age = current bar index minus attempt bar index.
Calculation: High normalization(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / max(strong - weak, positive minimum), 0, 1). Low normalization(x, strong, weak) = clamp((weak - x) / max(weak - strong, positive minimum), 0, 1).
Calculation: Failure score = weighted sum of five components, scaled to the score range: high normalization of stored expansion size between lower and higher multiples of the sensitivity-adjusted expansion threshold; high normalization of max(inside depth, 0) between zero and a positive floor or a multiple of the required travel threshold, whichever is greater; low normalization of progress between a smaller multiple of the required travel threshold and the required travel threshold; high normalization of the greater of current and stored relative volume between a lower volume reference and a multiple of the volume-support threshold; and high normalization of max(inside depth, 0) between the sensitivity-adjusted re-entry buffer and the greater of the required travel threshold or that buffer plus a positive offset. Use separate component weights whose sum is one and clamp the result to the score range.
Calculation: Ready failure = attempt age <= evaluation window AND inside depth >= sensitivity-adjusted re-entry buffer AND progress < required travel threshold AND failure score >= ready threshold AND the selected confirmation condition holds. Realtime confirmation has no additional condition; close confirmation requires a closed bar; volume confirmation also requires current OR stored relative volume >= volume-support threshold; strict confirmation additionally requires close below the context EMA for an upside attempt or above it for a downside attempt.
Calculation: Watch failure = attempt age <= evaluation window AND inside depth > 0 AND progress < required travel threshold AND failure score >= watch threshold AND ready failure is false. The watch threshold is lower than the ready threshold; watch does not require the selected confirmation condition.
Long: Downside attempt.
Short: Upside attempt.
Long: Active attempt direction = downside AND (ready failure OR watch failure).
Short: Active attempt direction = upside AND (ready failure OR watch failure).
