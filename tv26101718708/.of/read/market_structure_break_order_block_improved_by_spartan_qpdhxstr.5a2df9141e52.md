Calculation: Swing points = Initialize swing trend as up. On each bar, set it down if it is up and low is at or below the lowest low over the swing lookback; otherwise set it up if it is down and high is at or above the highest high over that lookback. On a change to up, record the lowest low over the bars since the most recent prior bar satisfying the high condition; on a change to down, record the highest high over the bars since the most recent prior bar satisfying the low condition. Use a lookback of at least one bar. Record the most recent bar index at which each recorded extreme occurred. Let H0 and H1 be the latest and preceding recorded swing highs, and L0 and L1 the latest and preceding recorded swing lows.
Calculation: Market structure = Initialize direction as bullish. At each confirmed bar, change from bearish to bullish when H0 > H1 AND H0 > H1 + abs(H1 - L0) * break fraction; change from bullish to bearish when L0 < L1 AND L0 < L1 - abs(H0 - L1) * break fraction. Either change additionally requires both L0 and H0 to differ from their values at the preceding market structure change. Before a preceding change exists, no change is confirmed.
Calculation: Zones = At a bullish structure change, create a bullish order block from the high and low of the last bearish candle between H1 and L0, inclusive, and a bullish breaker or mitigation block from the last bullish candle between the bar one swing lookback before L1 and H1, inclusive. At a bearish change, create a bearish order block from the last bullish candle between L1 and H0, inclusive, and a bearish breaker or mitigation block from the last bearish candle between the bar one swing lookback before H1 and L1, inclusive. Create each zone only if its search interval is valid and contains a qualifying candle. Keep a separate capped list for each zone type, removing the oldest when its cap is exceeded. Remove bullish order blocks and bearish breaker or mitigation blocks when close < zone low; remove bearish order blocks and bullish breaker or mitigation blocks when close > zone high. Apply removal on the creation bar as well. Initialize each zone's previous-inside state from whether the creation-bar close is within its inclusive range; initialize its tap count and wick-tested state to zero and false.
Calculation: Zone tap = Close is within the inclusive zone range AND the preceding inside state is false; then increment the zone's tap count and update its inside state on every surviving bar. A first tap additionally requires the prior tap count to be zero.
Calculation: First wick test = For a bullish order block or bearish breaker or mitigation block, (low >= zone low AND low <= zone high AND close > zone high) OR (low < zone low AND close >= zone low). For a bearish order block or bullish breaker or mitigation block, (high >= zone low AND high <= zone high AND close < zone low) OR (high > zone high AND close <= zone high). Require the zone to survive the bar and its wick-tested state to be false, then set that state true.
Calculation: Confluence = A surviving order block and a surviving breaker or mitigation block of the same direction overlap inclusively AND that order block has not previously recorded confluence; then mark the order block as having recorded confluence.
Calculation: Failed break = Store the latest structure-change bar, direction, preceding swing high for a bullish change or preceding swing low for a bearish change, and the corresponding break range abs(H1 - L0) or abs(H0 - L1). Replace these values at every subsequent structure change. A failure occurs at most once for each stored change, after at least one bar and no later than the failure lookback: for a bullish change, close < stored level AND stored level - close >= stored range * reversal fraction; for a bearish change, close > stored level AND close - stored level >= stored range * reversal fraction.
Long: Market structure changes to bullish.
Short: Market structure changes to bearish.
Long: The latest bearish structure break fails.
Short: The latest bullish structure break fails.
Long: A surviving bullish zone has a zone tap OR a first wick test.
Short: A surviving bearish zone has a zone tap OR a first wick test.
Long: Bullish confluence occurs.
Short: Bearish confluence occurs.
