Calculation: RSI price bands = For length n and RSI threshold q, let gain be EMA(max(close - previous close, 0), 2n - 1), loss be EMA(max(previous close - close, 0), 2n - 1), and x = (n - 1) × (loss × q / (100 - q) - gain). The band is close + x if x >= 0, otherwise close + x × (100 - q) / q. Use overbought and oversold thresholds for the upper and lower bands. The midpoint is their average. Calculate entry bands and longer-length take-profit bands separately.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA((typical price - EMA(typical price, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(typical price - EMA(typical price, channel length)), channel length)), average length).
Calculation: Pivot-based trend = Confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; prefer the high if both occur. At each confirmed pivot, update the center to (2 × previous center + pivot) / 3, initializing it to the first pivot. Form raw lower and upper bands as center minus and plus an ATR multiple. If previous close > previous trailing lower band, the lower band is the maximum of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. If previous close < previous trailing upper band, the upper band is the minimum of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. The trend is bullish if close > previous upper band, bearish if close < previous lower band, and otherwise unchanged; initialize to neutral and substitute zero for unavailable previous bands.
Calculation: Classic trend = Form raw lower and upper bands as the selected price source minus and plus an ATR multiple. Trail each band using the same recurrence and unavailable-value handling as the pivot-based trend. Initialize trend bullish; switch from bearish to bullish when close > previous upper band, or from bullish to bearish when close < previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend.
Calculation: Chandelier trend = Raw long stop is the highest close over its lookback minus an ATR multiple; raw short stop is the lowest close over the same lookback plus that multiple. Use the current raw stop when its previous value is unavailable. If previous close > previous long stop, replace the long stop with the maximum of its raw and previous values. If previous close < previous short stop, replace the short stop with the minimum of its raw and previous values. Initialize trend bullish; set it bullish when close > previous short stop, bearish when close < previous long stop, otherwise leave it unchanged.
Calculation: Twin-range trend = For each of two independently parameterized ranges, smooth the absolute change in the selected price source with an EMA of its range period and then an EMA of twice that period minus one, and multiply by its range multiplier. Average the two ranges. Starting with zero as the unavailable previous filter, set the filter to max(previous filter, source - average range) when source > previous filter, otherwise min(previous filter, source + average range). Track the most recent nonzero filter movement. The bullish condition is source > filter AND the most recent movement was upward; the bearish condition is source < filter AND the most recent movement was downward. Retain the last bullish or bearish condition until the opposite condition occurs; initialize neutral.
Calculation: Directional component events = A bullish or bearish event occurs when the pivot-based, classic, or Chandelier trend changes from the opposite direction to that direction, or when the corresponding twin-range condition holds after its last retained condition was opposite. Count every event over the configurable window, including repeated events from the same component.
Calculation: Entry stop = For a grand entry, the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over the stop lookback ending on the bar before entry. For a trend entry, entry close minus an ATR multiple for a long or plus it for a short. Fix this level at entry.
Calculation: Trailing stop = On a partial take-profit bar, initialize to close minus an ATR multiple for a long or close plus an ATR multiple for a short. On each subsequent bar, update it before testing exits to the maximum of its previous value and close minus the ATR multiple for a long, or the minimum of its previous value and close plus the ATR multiple for a short. The trailing ATR uses the configured true-range smoothing method.
Long: Previous position state = flat AND external-reference filtering = disabled AND bullish component events in the signal window >= required count AND bullish component events on the current bar >= 1 AND (entry-band filter disabled OR at least one low <= its contemporaneous lower entry RSI band within its lookback) AND (midpoint filter disabled OR high < current entry RSI midpoint) AND (WaveTrend filter disabled OR at least one WaveTrend <= the higher oversold threshold within its lookback).
Short: Previous position state = flat AND external-reference filtering = disabled AND bearish component events in the signal window >= required count AND bearish component events on the current bar >= 1 AND (entry-band filter disabled OR at least one high >= its contemporaneous upper entry RSI band within its lookback) AND (midpoint filter disabled OR low > current entry RSI midpoint) AND (WaveTrend filter disabled OR at least one WaveTrend >= the lower overbought threshold within its lookback).
Long: Previous position state = flat AND external-reference filtering = disabled AND trend-long enabled AND (local EMA filter disabled OR close > local close EMA) AND low <= lower entry RSI band AND (trend WaveTrend filter disabled OR at least one WaveTrend <= the higher oversold threshold within its lookback) AND grand-long condition = false.
Short: Previous position state = flat AND trend-short enabled AND (local EMA filter disabled OR close < local close EMA) AND high >= upper entry RSI band AND (trend WaveTrend filter disabled OR at least one WaveTrend >= the lower overbought threshold within its lookback) AND grand-short condition = false.
Exit: long, previous position state = before take-profit AND (low <= entry stop in wick mode OR close <= entry stop in close mode).
Exit: short, previous position state = before take-profit AND (high >= entry stop in wick mode OR close >= entry stop in close mode).
Exit: long, previous position state = before take-profit AND stop condition = false AND (high >= current upper take-profit RSI band in wick mode OR close >= current upper take-profit RSI band in close mode); close the configured fraction and enter the trailing state if a remainder exists.
Exit: short, previous position state = before take-profit AND stop condition = false AND (low <= current lower take-profit RSI band in wick mode OR close <= current lower take-profit RSI band in close mode); close the configured fraction and enter the trailing state if a remainder exists.
Exit: long, previous position state = trailing AND ((low <= entry price in stop wick mode OR close <= entry price in stop close mode) OR (low <= current trailing stop in trailing wick mode OR close <= current trailing stop in trailing close mode)).
Exit: short, previous position state = trailing AND ((high >= entry price in stop wick mode OR close >= entry price in stop close mode) OR (high >= current trailing stop in trailing wick mode OR close >= current trailing stop in trailing close mode)).
