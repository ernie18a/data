Calculation: Twin Range Filter flip = bullish when close is above a rising range-filter line after the prior short condition; bearish when close is below a falling line after the prior long condition; the line uses the average of fast- and slow-period smoothed ranges
Calculation: Entry RSI bands = upper and lower close-based bands derived from smoothed gains and losses at configurable overbought and oversold levels
Calculation: TP RSI bands = the same RSI-band calculation using a longer lookback than the entry RSI bands
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of (HLC3 − EMA(HLC3)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3))))
Calculation: Reference trend = reference-symbol close above its EMA on both selected higher timeframes for bullish, or below its EMA on both for bearish; use the last completed bar on each timeframe
Calculation: Grand long stop = lowest low across the preceding lookback bars; Grand short stop = highest high across the preceding lookback bars
Calculation: Trend long stop = entry price − ATR × stop multiplier; Trend short stop = entry price + ATR × stop multiplier
Calculation: Trailing stop = highest close since TP1 − ATR × trailing multiplier for longs; lowest close since TP1 + ATR × trailing multiplier for shorts, updated only in the favorable direction

Exit: Long, before TP1: stop trigger price ≤ fixed stop level OR TP1 trigger price ≥ current TP RSI upper band; after partial TP1: stop trigger price ≤ entry price OR trailing-stop trigger price ≤ trailing stop
