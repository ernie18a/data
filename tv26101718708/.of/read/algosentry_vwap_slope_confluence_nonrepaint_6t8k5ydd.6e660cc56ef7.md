Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at each selected session, week, or month boundary; its upper and lower bands are VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of its volume-weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: Signed slope strength of a series = compute the change in its linear-regression endpoint from the preceding bar, divided by ATR; use zero when ATR is zero. Z-score the absolute normalized slope against its rolling mean and standard deviation, using zero when that standard deviation is zero. Award one vote if this z-score exceeds (0.25 times its rolling mean + 0.10 times its rolling standard deviation) times an ease factor; one if the efficiency-ratio filter is disabled or the efficiency ratio is at least its rolling mean times the ease factor; and one if the R-squared filter is disabled or R-squared is at least its rolling mean times the ease factor. Efficiency ratio is absolute net change divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar changes, or zero when the denominator is zero. R-squared is squared correlation of the series with bar index, or zero when unavailable. Use a shorter efficiency-ratio lookback than the regression lookback, an R-squared lookback equal to the regression lookback, and a longer rolling lookback for the means and standard deviations. Strength is the sign of the normalized slope if votes reach the required count, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Retest direction = direction of the most recent crossover of the fast close EMA and anchored VWAP. A qualifying long retest requires direction positive, open above VWAP, and either low at or below VWAP or the optional upper-band test enabled and low at or below the upper band; a qualifying short retest requires direction negative, open below VWAP, and either high at or above VWAP or the optional lower-band test enabled and high at or above the lower band. If rejection is enabled, long also requires close above VWAP and short requires close below VWAP. Either retest requires more than the cooldown number of bars since the last qualifying retest that passed the volume filter, and the volume filter disabled, its volume average unavailable, or volume greater than its average times a multiplier. Both directions share the cooldown; update it on every qualifying retest even if retest markings are disabled.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe confirmation = count completed lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar whose signed slope strength is positive and separately count those whose strength is negative, independently for lower-timeframe session VWAP and the slower close EMA. A direction is confirmed when its count reaches the required count. Use lower-timeframe data through the last completed lower-timeframe bar, with independently configured slope parameters.
Calculation: Chart slope direction = signed slope strength of anchored VWAP or the slower close EMA; VWAP additionally requires the number of bars since the anchor reset to reach the warmup. Each series' slope direction is zero when its slope display is disabled.
Calculation: Confluence scores = add 25 to the bullish or bearish score for each chart VWAP and slower EMA slope with the corresponding sign, and, when lower-timeframe confirmation is enabled, add 20 for each corresponding lower-timeframe VWAP and EMA confirmation. If the volume filter is enabled and passes, add 10 to the larger score; add nothing on a tie. Confluence is the larger score divided by 50 plus 40 if lower-timeframe confirmation is enabled plus 10 if the volume filter is enabled, multiplied by 100 and rounded.
Calculation: Locked direction = on every qualifying retest, arm its direction, clear its used flag and clear its continuous-confirmation lock. For that direction, confirmation requires lower-timeframe confirmation enabled, both chart slope directions matching the retest, and each lower-timeframe series confirming that direction; if same-direction confirmation is disabled, each lower-timeframe series may instead confirm either direction. A lock occurs while armed when confirmation is true and its continuous-confirmation lock is clear, provided first-only mode is disabled or the used flag is clear. Set the continuous-confirmation lock when confirmation first becomes true, clear it when confirmation becomes false, and set the used flag on a lock in first-only mode. A new retest can arm and lock on the same bar.
Long: Fast close EMA crosses above anchored VWAP.
Short: Fast close EMA crosses below anchored VWAP.
Long: Qualifying long retest.
Short: Qualifying short retest.
Long: Locked direction is positive.
Short: Locked direction is negative.
Long: Chart VWAP slope direction > 0 OR chart slower EMA slope direction > 0.
Short: Chart VWAP slope direction < 0 OR chart slower EMA slope direction < 0.
Long: Close >= anchored VWAP.
Short: Close < anchored VWAP.
Long: Confluence candle coloring enabled AND confluence >= its threshold AND bullish score > bearish score.
Short: Confluence candle coloring enabled AND confluence >= its threshold AND bearish score > bullish score.
Long: Bullish score > bearish score.
Short: Bearish score > bullish score.
Exit: long, a positive locked direction is active AND fast close EMA crosses below anchored VWAP.
Exit: short, a negative locked direction is active AND fast close EMA crosses above anchored VWAP.
