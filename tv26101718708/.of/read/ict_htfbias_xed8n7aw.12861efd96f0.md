Calculation: Zones = For each enabled timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, use its last completed bar. A bullish fair value gap spans the high two bars earlier to the current low when the current low is greater; a bearish gap spans the current high to the low two bars earlier when the current high is lower. A confirmed pivot high or low has a parameterized number of bars on each side. When the close is above the latest confirmed pivot high, a bullish order block spans the low to high of the most recent bearish candle within a parameterized prior-bar lookback. When the close is below the latest confirmed pivot low, a bearish order block spans the low to high of the most recent bullish candle within that lookback; the bullish block takes precedence if both conditions hold. Keep separate newest-first lists by timeframe and zone type, discard duplicate zones with the same direction, anchor time and bounds within one price tick, and retain at most the larger of the display and calculation depths. On each completed timeframe bar, remove bullish zones if its close is below their lower bound and bearish zones if its close is above their upper bound.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = Among the most recent order blocks within their calculation depth, use the direction of the newest one containing the chart close, including its bounds. Otherwise use the direction of the newest fair value gap containing the chart close within its calculation depth. Otherwise use the direction of the newest order block within its calculation depth, or, if none exists, the newest fair value gap within its calculation depth; use zero if neither exists. Represent bullish as positive and bearish as negative.
Calculation: Global bias = Sum the enabled timeframe biases weighted by their row ranks, with weights increasing by one from the first row to the last; exclude timeframes lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: A newly completed bullish fair value gap or bullish order block exists.
Short: A newly completed bearish fair value gap or bearish order block exists.
Long: The chart close is within the newest containing bullish zone of either type among that type's zones within its calculation depth.
Short: The chart close is within the newest containing bearish zone of either type among that type's zones within its calculation depth.
Long: Timeframe bias > 0.
Short: Timeframe bias < 0.
Long: Global bias > 0.
Short: Global bias < 0.
