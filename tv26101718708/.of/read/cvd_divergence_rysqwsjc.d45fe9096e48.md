Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) = continuously sum volume from lower-timeframe bars, adding volume when close > open, subtracting it when close < open, and adding zero when close = open; use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar as they accrue.
Calculation: CVD pivot low/high = a local low/high with the configured number of chart bars on each side; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Long: A confirmed CVD pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed CVD pivot low AND its corresponding chart-bar low is lower than the previous pivot's low AND the pivot confirmations are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation OR a confirmed CVD pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed CVD pivot low AND its corresponding chart-bar low is higher than the previous pivot's low AND the pivot confirmations are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation.
Short: A confirmed CVD pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed CVD pivot high AND its corresponding chart-bar high is higher than the previous pivot's high AND the pivot confirmations are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation OR a confirmed CVD pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed CVD pivot high AND its corresponding chart-bar high is lower than the previous pivot's high AND the pivot confirmations are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation.
