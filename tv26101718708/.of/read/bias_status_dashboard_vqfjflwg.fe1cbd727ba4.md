Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = smoothed stochastic of RSI; Stochastic RSI D = smoothed moving average of K
Calculation: Single-timeframe bias = bullish if at least two of these are bullish and bullish count exceeds bearish count; bearish if at least two are bearish and bearish count exceeds bullish count; otherwise neutral: fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA / fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA, RSI > 50 / RSI < 50, Stochastic RSI K > D and K > 50 / K < D and K < 50
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bias = bullish if bullish single-timeframe bias count is at least the minimum agreement and exceeds bearish count; bearish if bearish count is at least the minimum agreement and exceeds bullish count; otherwise neutral. Evaluate each of the eight configured timeframes using its most recent available bar; each timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Structure direction = 1 when close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high; -1 when close is below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the previous direction (if both conditions hold, use 1). A pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bull score = 1 each for bullish structure direction, bullish EMA stack, strong ADX with +DI > -DI, bullish Stochastic RSI, RSI > 50, and bullish multi-timeframe bias; plus 1 each when ATR is expanding or normal, volume is above its moving average, and a configured session is active.
Calculation: Bear score = 1 each for bearish structure direction, bearish EMA stack, strong ADX with -DI > +DI, bearish Stochastic RSI, RSI < 50, and bearish multi-timeframe bias; plus 1 each when ATR is expanding or normal, volume is above its moving average, and a configured session is active.
Calculation: Final score = maximum of bull score and bear score
Long: Bullish EMA stack OR RSI > 50 OR Stochastic RSI K > D and K > 50 OR bullish single-timeframe bias OR bullish multi-timeframe bias OR bull score > bear score and final score >= 6
Short: Bearish EMA stack OR RSI < 50 OR Stochastic RSI K < D and K < 50 OR bearish single-timeframe bias OR bearish multi-timeframe bias OR bear score > bull score and final score >= 6
