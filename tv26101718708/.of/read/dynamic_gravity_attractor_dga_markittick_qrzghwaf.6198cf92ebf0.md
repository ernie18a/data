Calculation: Gravity zones = On each confirmed bar, detect a pivot high or low as the unique highest high or lowest low in a symmetric window of bars. Process a high before a low if both occur. Assign each pivot the SMA of volume ending at the pivot bar. Match it to the nearest existing zone within a parameterized fraction of current ATR, choosing the most recently created zone on a distance tie; include expired zones in matching. If no zone matches, create one with the pivot price, assigned volume, one touch, and current bar as its update bar. Otherwise, update its price to the volume-weighted average of its old price and the pivot price, add the assigned volume, increment touches, and set its update bar to the current bar. A zone is active when its last update is within the history lookback and its touches meet the minimum. Calculate maximum volume and maximum touches across all zones updated within the lookback, including those below the touch minimum. Each active zone's mass is the weighted average of its volume divided by maximum volume and its touches divided by maximum touches, with each component set to zero if its maximum is zero. Its gravity is a constant times mass divided by the square of the greater of its absolute distance from close and a parameterized fraction of ATR.
Calculation: Live net force = Sum gravity for all active zones priced at or below close, minus sum gravity for all active zones priced above close.
Calculation: Ranked net force = Sum gravity for the highest-gravity active zones, up to a parameterized zone count, priced at or below close, minus sum gravity for those priced above close. Preserve zone creation order when gravity values tie.
Long: Live net force > 0 OR ranked net force > 0 OR previous bar's ranked net force > 0 AND the ranked net force on the bar before that <= 0
Short: Live net force < 0 OR ranked net force < 0 OR previous bar's ranked net force < 0 AND the ranked net force on the bar before that >= 0
