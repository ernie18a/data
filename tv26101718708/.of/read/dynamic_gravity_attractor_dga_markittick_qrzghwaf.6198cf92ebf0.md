Calculation: Live net force = sum of gravity for every active pivot cluster at or below close minus gravity for every active cluster above close; each cluster’s gravity = G × mass / max(abs(cluster price − close), 1% of ATR(14))². A pivot high/low is a strict local high/low over an equal parameterized number of bars on each side and is registered when confirmed. Assign it the 5-bar average volume from its pivot bar; merge it into the nearest existing cluster within a parameterized ATR(14)-based radius, updating cluster price by volume-weighted average, cumulative volume, touch count, and last-update bar; otherwise create a cluster. Active clusters were updated within a parameterized lookback and meet a parameterized minimum touch count. Cluster mass is the weighted sum of its volume and touch count, each normalized by the maximum among active clusters; normalize the two weights by their sum.
