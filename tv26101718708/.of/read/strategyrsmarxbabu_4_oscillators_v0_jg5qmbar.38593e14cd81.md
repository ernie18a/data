Calculation: MFI = RSI of the rolling sum of volume × source on bars where source increased versus the rolling sum of volume × source on bars where source decreased; unchanged bars contribute to neither sum
Long: RSI > overbought level AND Stochastic RSI > overbought level AND MFI > overbought level AND Williams %R > overbought level
Short: RSI < oversold level AND Stochastic RSI < oversold level AND MFI < oversold level AND Williams %R < oversold level
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance in ticks OR low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance in ticks
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance in ticks OR high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance in ticks
