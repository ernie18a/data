Calculation: Completed-period liquidity levels = the high and low of each of several completed higher-timeframe periods, from shorter to longer periods.
Calculation: Swing liquidity levels = confirmed higher-timeframe pivot highs and lows, each more extreme than the bars in its equal-length left and right confirmation windows; retain confirmed levels within a configurable time lookback.
Calculation: Session liquidity levels = the running high and low of each configurable session, retained after the session ends until that session next starts. Update them with the current chart bar before evaluating decisions.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = chart-bar low < any enabled completed-period low, swing low, or session low AND chart-bar close > that same low. Bearish sweep = chart-bar high > any enabled completed-period high, swing high, or session high AND chart-bar close < that same high.
Calculation: Simple bullish FVG = a bar's low > the high two bars earlier; its zone is from that earlier high to the bar's low. Simple bearish FVG = a bar's high < the low two bars earlier; its zone is from the bar's high to that earlier low. For event detection, use the last completed bar of a configurable timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and emit each direction at most once per source bar.
Calculation: Displayed FVG = on the first chart bar of a new configurable source-timeframe bar, compare that source bar's low or high with the high or low two source bars earlier. A bullish zone requires current low > earlier high AND previous source-bar close > earlier high AND (previous close - previous open) / (previous open * 100) > threshold; its bounds are earlier high and current low. A bearish zone requires current high < earlier low AND previous close < earlier low AND the negative of that return > threshold; its bounds are current high and earlier low. The source timeframe may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; the current source bar is still forming, while the previous and earlier bars are completed. The threshold is either zero or twice the cumulative sum of the absolute previous-source-bar returns sampled at source-timeframe changes, divided by the chart bar index, with a minimum denominator of one. Retain zones up to a configurable count; optionally delete bullish zones when chart low < zone bottom and bearish zones when chart high > zone top before checking taps.
Calculation: Displayed FVG tap = chart high >= zone bottom AND chart low <= zone top AND chart bar index > the zone's creation bar index. Depending on the configured tap mode, delete the zone on a tap or retain it; retained zones may be eligible only for their first tap or for repeated taps.
Calculation: Bias = for each of several ordered timeframes, compare its last completed close with its same-length EMA of close. The timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Bullish bias means a configurable majority of comparisons are close > EMA; bearish bias means the same majority are close <= EMA.
Calculation: Tracked FVG tap = the first chart bar whose time is at least one source-bar duration after a simple FVG's source-bar opening time AND whose high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top, provided the zone's source-bar opening time remains within a configurable time lookback. Use a configurable source timeframe longer than the simple-FVG confirmation timeframe; it may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Structure swing = at each of two chart-bar lookbacks, one shorter than the other, maintain a state that becomes high when the high at the lookback offset > the highest high of the bars since then, or otherwise becomes low when the low at that offset < the lowest low of the bars since then. Record a new swing high or low only when the state changes, and retain its price and bar index.
Calculation: Order block = after chart close crosses above a retained structure swing high or below a retained structure swing low, examine opposite-colored candles from the corresponding most recent swing low or high through the crossing bar, using the same swing lookback. A bullish candidate is bearish and a bearish candidate is bullish, at least three bars old. Its zone is its high and low. It must produce a same-direction simple FVG within a configurable number of bars after the candidate. In strict mode the FVG must occur exactly two bars later, and a local low for a bullish candidate or local high for a bearish candidate must occur after the candidate and before the crossing bar, followed by a close beyond the broken structure level. In session-owned mode the zone must overlap a buffered session low for a bullish candidate or buffered session high for a bearish candidate; the buffer is the maximum of configurable multiples of zone height, that session's range, and chart ATR. Optionally require a session high above a bullish zone or session low below a bearish zone. Reject a candidate if any bar after its first qualifying FVG through the crossing bar meets the configured mitigation condition. Retain qualifying zones, deduplicated by candidate bar, up to a configurable count.
Calculation: Order-block mitigation = either chart high >= zone bottom AND chart low <= zone top, OR chart close >= zone bottom AND chart close <= zone top, OR bullish chart low < zone bottom and bearish chart high > zone top, according to the configured mitigation mode. Remove every mitigated order block. A mitigated block is golden only if, at mitigation time, it overlaps the corresponding buffered session level and satisfies the optional opposite-session-liquidity target condition.
Long: Chart-bar low < an enabled liquidity low AND chart-bar close > that same low.
Short: Chart-bar high > an enabled liquidity high AND chart-bar close < that same high.
Long: A bullish sweep occurred within a configurable time window AND a new bullish simple FVG event occurs on the confirmation timeframe AND that sweep has not already been used for a confirmed entry. A new bullish sweep resets the window.
Short: A bearish sweep occurred within a configurable time window AND a new bearish simple FVG event occurs on the confirmation timeframe AND that sweep has not already been used for a confirmed entry. A new bearish sweep resets the window.
Long: A bullish displayed FVG forms.
Short: A bearish displayed FVG forms.
Long: A bullish displayed FVG tap occurs.
Short: A bearish displayed FVG tap occurs.
Long: A bullish simple FVG event occurs on either configurable event timeframe.
Short: A bearish simple FVG event occurs on either configurable event timeframe.
Long: A bullish tracked FVG tap occurs AND bias is bullish.
Short: A bearish tracked FVG tap occurs AND bias is bearish.
Long: The last completed close of any bias timeframe > its EMA.
Short: The last completed close of any bias timeframe <= its EMA.
Long: Bias is bullish.
Short: Bias is bearish.
Long: A bullish order block is mitigated AND is golden at mitigation time.
Short: A bearish order block is mitigated AND is golden at mitigation time.
