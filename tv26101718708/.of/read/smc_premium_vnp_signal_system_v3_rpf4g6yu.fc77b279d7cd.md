Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal numbers of bars on each side; update the stored level and activate it only when a newly confirmed pivot differs from the preceding confirmed pivot. Deactivate that side after its entry if structure display is enabled.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. Its short and medium lengths correspond to the chart-timeframe short and medium EMA lengths; the chart also uses a longer EMA.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = sum of configurable weights for each true condition: bullish trend; ADX above its threshold; positive DI above negative DI; RSI between its lower and upper bullish thresholds; MACD line above signal line AND histogram above zero; volume above its moving average times a multiplier; ATR above its moving average times a multiplier; low below the lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close above that low; current low above the high two bars ago AND previous close above that high; close above open AND candle body above ATR times a multiplier. Bullish trend and bullish MACD have equal highest weights; ADX, DI, RSI, volume, sweep and gap have equal intermediate weights; ATR expansion and candle body have equal lowest weights.
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = sum of configurable weights for each true condition: bearish trend; ADX above its threshold; negative DI above positive DI; RSI between its lower and upper bearish thresholds; MACD line below signal line AND histogram below zero; volume above its moving average times a multiplier; ATR above its moving average times a multiplier; high above the highest high of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close below that high; current high below the low two bars ago AND previous close below that low; close below open AND candle body above ATR times a multiplier. Use the corresponding bullish-score weights.
Calculation: Bullish trend = chart short EMA above chart medium EMA AND close above chart long EMA AND higher-timeframe short EMA above higher-timeframe medium EMA AND chart close above higher-timeframe medium EMA.
Calculation: Bearish trend = chart short EMA below chart medium EMA AND close below chart long EMA AND higher-timeframe short EMA below higher-timeframe medium EMA AND chart close below higher-timeframe medium EMA.
Calculation: Entry risk distance = absolute difference between entry close and entry-bar low minus adaptive ATR for a long, or entry-bar high plus adaptive ATR for a short. Adaptive ATR = entry-bar ATR times a base multiplier adjusted upward when entry-bar ADX exceeds an upper threshold, unchanged when ADX exceeds a lower threshold, and adjusted downward otherwise.
Long: No position AND active confirmed swing high exists AND selected high or close is above that swing high AND bullish confluence score is at least its threshold AND bullish trend is true AND ADX is above its threshold.
Short: No position AND active confirmed swing low exists AND selected low or close is below that swing low AND bearish confluence score is at least its threshold AND bearish trend is true AND ADX is above its threshold.
Long: Current low is above the high two bars ago AND previous close is above that high.
Short: Current high is below the low two bars ago AND previous close is below that low.
Long: Previous close is below previous open AND close is above previous high AND close minus open is above ATR times a multiplier.
Short: Previous close is above previous open AND close is below previous low AND open minus close is above ATR times a multiplier.
Long: Higher-timeframe short EMA is above higher-timeframe medium EMA AND chart close is above higher-timeframe medium EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe short EMA is below higher-timeframe medium EMA AND chart close is below higher-timeframe medium EMA.
Exit: long, high is at least entry price plus entry risk distance times the selected profit-target multiplier OR selected close or low is at most entry price minus entry risk distance.
Exit: short, low is at most entry price minus entry risk distance times the selected profit-target multiplier OR selected close or high is at least entry price plus entry risk distance.
