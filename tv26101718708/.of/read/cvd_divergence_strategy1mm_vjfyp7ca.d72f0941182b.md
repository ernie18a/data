Calculation: CVD = running cumulative sum over all bars of delta, where delta = (bullPower - bearPower) / (bullPower + bearPower) * volume. Notation: o, h, l, c = current open, high, low, close; pc = previous bar close; R = h - l. bullPower: if c<o: (pc<o ? max(h-pc, c-l) : max(h-o, c-l)); if c>o: (pc>o ? R : max(o-pc, R)); if c==o: (h-c > c-l ? (pc<o ? max(h-pc, c-l) : h-o) : h-c < c-l ? (pc>o ? R : max(o-pc, R)) : (pc>o ? max(h-o, c-l) : pc<o ? max(o-pc, R) : R)). bearPower: if c<o: (pc>o ? max(pc-o, R) : R); if c>o: (pc>o ? max(pc-l, h-c) : max(o-l, h-c)); if c==o: (h-c > c-l ? (pc>o ? max(pc-o, R) : R) : h-c < c-l ? (pc>o ? max(pc-l, h-c) : o-l) : (pc>o ? max(pc-o, R) : pc<o ? max(o-l, h-c) : R)).
Calculation: CVD pivot low confirmed = on the current bar, the CVD value R bars ago is the lowest CVD among the L bars before it and the R bars after it (R = right lookback, L = left lookback). The pivot bar is the bar R bars ago. CVD pivot high confirmed = same with highest. Previous pivot = the pivot bar of the most recent earlier confirmation of the same type (low or high). Gap = number of bars between the previous confirmation and the current bar, minus 1; gap-OK = gap is between a minimum lookback and a maximum lookback (inclusive).
Long: CVD pivot low confirmed on current bar AND gap-OK for pivot lows AND ((CVD at pivot > CVD at previous pivot AND low at pivot bar < low at previous pivot bar) OR (CVD at pivot < CVD at previous pivot AND low at pivot bar > low at previous pivot bar))
Short: CVD pivot high confirmed on current bar AND gap-OK for pivot highs AND ((CVD at pivot < CVD at previous pivot AND high at pivot bar > high at previous pivot bar) OR (CVD at pivot > CVD at previous pivot AND high at pivot bar < high at previous pivot bar))
Exit: long, Short condition is true OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percent) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percent)
Exit: short, Long condition is true OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percent) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percent)
