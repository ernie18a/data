Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = For a selectable price source derived from the bar fields, accumulate volume, source × volume, and source² × volume from the start of the current anchor period. VWAP is the second sum divided by the first; deviation is the square root of the greater of zero and the third sum divided by the first minus VWAP². Reset the sums at each anchor-period boundary. The anchor period is a higher timeframe and uses its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Cluster classification = Separately for ATR and raw volume, retain a rolling window using the same lookback. At each shared recalculation interval, once the window is full and the respective clustering is enabled, initialize three centroids evenly between the window minimum and maximum. For a selectable number of iterations, assign each observation to its nearest centroid, breaking ties toward the lower-index centroid, and replace each nonempty centroid with its assigned observations' mean. Retain the last centroids between recalculations. Classify the current reading by its nearest centroid, breaking ties toward the lower index. Confidence is 100 × (1 − nearest distance / second-nearest distance), or 100 if the second-nearest distance is zero. A classification is ready after a valid clustering pass.
Calculation: Effective band distance = A selectable deviation multiplier multiplied by the regime-specific scale when adaptive width is enabled and ATR clustering is ready and its confidence is at least the confidence threshold; otherwise use the unscaled multiplier. Upper and lower bands are anchored VWAP plus and minus deviation × effective band distance. The cross buffer is deviation × a selectable buffer multiplier.
Calculation: Active mode = The selected mean-reversion or breakout mode; in automatic mode use breakout when ATR clustering is ready, confidence is at least its threshold, and the current ATR belongs to the highest cluster; otherwise use mean reversion.
Calculation: Crossover and crossunder = Current close is respectively above or below the current threshold AND previous close is respectively at or below or at or above the previous bar's threshold.
Long: ((active mode = mean reversion AND close crosses over lower band + cross buffer) OR (active mode = breakout AND close crosses over upper band + cross buffer)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume clustering not ready OR current volume belongs to the middle or highest cluster) AND (no earlier signal exists OR bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown).
Short: ((active mode = mean reversion AND close crosses under upper band − cross buffer) OR (active mode = breakout AND close crosses under lower band − cross buffer)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume clustering not ready OR current volume belongs to the middle or highest cluster) AND (no earlier signal exists OR bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown).
Exit: long, short decision AND NOT long decision.
Exit: short, long decision AND NOT short decision.
