Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of (configured price source × volume) since the start of the configured anchor period, divided by cumulative volume
Calculation: Anchored VWAP standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted mean of squared source prices minus the square of Anchored VWAP; use zero if variance is not positive
Calculation: Band 2 = Anchored VWAP ± standard deviation × configured Band 2 multiplier × active band scale; active band scale uses the confident volatility regime's scale when adaptive width is enabled, otherwise 1
Calculation: Volatility regime = classify current ATR against three ascending centroids from k-means clustering of the rolling ATR lookback (low, medium, high); recluster every configured interval. Regime is trusted only when the lookback is full, clustering has run, and confidence meets the configured minimum; confidence = (1 − distance to nearest centroid / distance to second-nearest centroid) × 100, or 100 if the second distance is zero
Calculation: Volume regime = classify current volume against three ascending centroids from the same k-means procedure applied to the rolling volume lookback (low, normal, high); recluster at the configured interval
Long: In Mean Reversion mode, close crosses above (lower Band 2 + configured standard-deviation buffer); in Breakout mode, close crosses above (upper Band 2 + configured standard-deviation buffer); in Auto mode, use Breakout when the trusted volatility regime is high, otherwise use Mean Reversion; AND, if volume confirmation is enabled and its lookback is ready, volume regime is normal or high; AND, if bar-close confirmation is enabled, the bar is complete; AND, no signal has occurred within the configured cooldown
Short: In Mean Reversion mode, close crosses below (upper Band 2 − configured standard-deviation buffer); in Breakout mode, close crosses below (lower Band 2 − configured standard-deviation buffer); in Auto mode, use Breakout when the trusted volatility regime is high, otherwise use Mean Reversion; AND, if volume confirmation is enabled and its lookback is ready, volume regime is normal or high; AND, if bar-close confirmation is enabled, the bar is complete; AND, no signal has occurred within the configured cooldown
Exit: long, Short condition
Exit: short, Long condition
