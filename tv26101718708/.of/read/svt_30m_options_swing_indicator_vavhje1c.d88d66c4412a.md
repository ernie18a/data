Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = values calculated on each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on the current real-time bar. A previous higher-timeframe value referenced on a chart bar is the value displayed on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the channel index over an averaging lookback; channel index = (typical price − EMA of typical price over a channel lookback) / (scaling factor × EMA of the absolute difference between typical price and that EMA over the channel lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero. Its signal is an SMA of WaveTrend.
Calculation: Bull score = count of true conditions: RSI above its midpoint; shorter-lookback RSI above longer-lookback RSI; WaveTrend above its signal; CCI above zero; ADX at or above its threshold AND positive DI above negative DI; MACD above its signal AND histogram above its previous chart-bar value; close above both short and medium EMAs. Bear score reverses each directional comparison, retaining the same ADX threshold.
Calculation: Recent bullish MACD cross = chart bars since MACD crossed above its signal are at most the cross lookback; recent bearish cross is defined symmetrically. A cross requires the previous MACD to be on or beyond the opposite side of its signal.
Calculation: Bull momentum = MACD above its signal AND histogram above its previous value; bear momentum = MACD below its signal AND histogram below its previous value.
Calculation: Bull pullback = at least one bar in the pullback lookback, including the current bar, has low at or below its short EMA OR low at or below its medium EMA OR RSI below its lower pullback threshold. Bear pullback = at least one such bar has high at or above its short EMA OR high at or above its medium EMA OR RSI above its upper pullback threshold.
Calculation: Longer-timeframe bull bias = longer-timeframe close above its medium EMA AND RSI above its midpoint; bear bias reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Shorter-timeframe bull trend = shorter-timeframe close above its short EMA AND RSI above its midpoint AND MACD above its signal; bear trend reverses all comparisons. Strong bull trend = shorter-timeframe close above its medium EMA AND short EMA above medium EMA AND RSI above its midpoint AND MACD above its signal; strong bear trend reverses all comparisons. Bull recovery = shorter-timeframe MACD histogram above its previous chart-bar value AND RSI above a lower recovery threshold AND close above its short EMA; bear recovery = histogram below its previous chart-bar value AND RSI below an upper recovery threshold AND close below its short EMA.
Calculation: Strong longer-timeframe bull bias = close above medium EMA AND medium EMA above long EMA AND RSI above an upper strong-bias threshold; strong bear bias = close below medium EMA AND medium EMA below long EMA AND RSI below a lower strong-bias threshold.
Calculation: Chart bull trend = close above short EMA AND short EMA at or above medium EMA; chart bear trend = close below short EMA AND short EMA at or below medium EMA.
Calculation: Chop = ADX below its chop threshold AND absolute difference between short and medium EMAs below an ATR multiple AND highest high minus lowest low over the chop lookback below an ATR multiple AND close strictly between that highest high and lowest low. Chop permission = chop filter disabled OR chop is false OR close above the previous bar's lookback highest high AND bull momentum AND RSI above its midpoint OR close below the previous bar's lookback lowest low AND bear momentum AND RSI below its midpoint.
Calculation: Bull setup = chart bull trend AND recent bullish MACD cross AND bull score at or above its mode-dependent threshold AND close at or below short EMA plus an ATR multiple AND chop permission AND ((bull pullback AND bull momentum AND close above short EMA AND close above previous close AND close above open AND, in restrictive mode, longer-timeframe bull bias AND (shorter-timeframe bull trend OR bull recovery), otherwise longer-timeframe bull bias OR shorter-timeframe bull trend OR bull recovery) OR (strong longer-timeframe bull bias AND strong shorter-timeframe bull trend)). Bear setup reverses the directional comparisons and uses bear conditions; its extension limit is close at or above short EMA minus the ATR multiple.
Calculation: Entry permission = bars since the last entry exceed the entry cooldown OR there has been no entry, AND bars since the last detected flat exit exceed the exit cooldown OR there has been no detected flat exit. Parenthesize each OR group before applying AND.
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR on the entry signal bar. Highest high and lowest low since entry include the entry bar and all subsequent bars while the position is open. Long stop = entry price minus an entry-ATR multiple until highest high since entry reaches entry price plus a profit-trigger multiple of entry ATR; thereafter the greater of entry price plus a break-even multiple of entry ATR and highest high since entry minus a trailing multiple of entry ATR. Short stop = entry price plus an entry-ATR multiple until lowest low since entry reaches entry price minus a profit-trigger multiple of entry ATR; thereafter the lesser of entry price minus a break-even multiple of entry ATR and lowest low since entry plus a trailing multiple of entry ATR.

Short: Longer-timeframe bear bias.
Long: Strong shorter-timeframe bull trend OR shorter-timeframe bull recovery.
Short: Strong shorter-timeframe bear trend OR shorter-timeframe bear recovery.
Long: Chart bull trend.
Short: Chart bear trend.
Long: Recent bullish MACD cross.
Short: Recent bearish MACD cross AND recent bullish MACD cross is false.
Long: Bull pullback.
Short: Bear pullback AND bull pullback is false.
Long: Bull score at or above its mode-dependent threshold.
Short: Bear score at or above its mode-dependent threshold AND bull score below its mode-dependent threshold.
Long: Position is flat AND bull setup.
Short: Position is flat AND bear setup AND bull setup is false.
Long: Chart bar is complete AND position is flat or short AND bull setup AND entry permission AND strong shorter-timeframe bear trend is false.
Short: Chart bar is complete AND position is flat or long AND bear setup AND entry permission AND strong shorter-timeframe bull trend is false.
Exit: long, short entry conditions are met.
Exit: short, long entry conditions are met.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below the active long stop.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds the active short stop.
Exit: long, bars since entry at or above minimum hold AND ((MACD crosses below its signal AND RSI below its RSI SMA AND close below short EMA AND bear score at or above its exit threshold) OR strong shorter-timeframe bear trend OR (close below medium EMA AND MACD below its signal AND RSI below its lower exit threshold)).
Exit: short, bars since entry at or above minimum hold AND ((MACD crosses above its signal AND RSI above its RSI SMA AND close above short EMA AND bull score at or above its exit threshold) OR strong shorter-timeframe bull trend OR (close above medium EMA AND MACD above its signal AND RSI above its upper exit threshold)).
Exit: long, no-progress protection enabled AND bars since entry at or above its check period AND highest high since entry below entry price plus a minimum-move multiple of entry ATR AND strong shorter-timeframe bull trend is false.
Exit: short, no-progress protection enabled AND bars since entry at or above its check period AND lowest low since entry above entry price minus a minimum-move multiple of entry ATR AND strong shorter-timeframe bear trend is false.
Exit: long, bars since entry at or above maximum hold.
Exit: short, bars since entry at or above maximum hold.
