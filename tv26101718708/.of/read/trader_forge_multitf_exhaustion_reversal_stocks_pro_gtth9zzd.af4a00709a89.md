Calculation: Stoch RSI %K = smoothed stochastic of RSI; calculate on the chart timeframe and on two successively higher timeframes, using each timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Calculation: Donchian upper/lower = highest high/lowest low over the lookback on the chart timeframe, including the current still-forming bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe, using the current still-forming bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current chart-timeframe volume divided by its moving-average volume, using the current still-forming bar.
Long: Within the configured allowed session AND outside the configured opening avoidance window AND relative volume meets its minimum AND (current low is within the configured ATR tolerance of the Donchian lower band AND absolute close-to-open body is at most the configured ATR fraction AND chart-timeframe and both higher-timeframe Stoch RSI %K values are at or below the oversold threshold OR the preceding bar met those conditions and current close is above the preceding bar’s high)
Short: Within the configured allowed session AND outside the configured opening avoidance window AND relative volume meets its minimum AND (current high is within the configured ATR tolerance of the Donchian upper band AND absolute close-to-open body is at most the configured ATR fraction AND chart-timeframe and both higher-timeframe Stoch RSI %K values are at or above the overbought threshold OR the preceding bar met those conditions and current close is below the preceding bar’s low)
