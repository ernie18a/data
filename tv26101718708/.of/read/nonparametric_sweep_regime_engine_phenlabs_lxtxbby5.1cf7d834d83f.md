Calculation: Active liquidity levels = The latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, tracked separately. A pivot has equal numbers of bars on each side; confirm it only after the right-side bars complete. For a pivot high, its high is at least as high as every left-side high and strictly higher than every right-side high; reverse the inequalities for a pivot low. A newly confirmed pivot replaces and activates its corresponding level before evaluating the current bar. Deactivate an active low after any bar with low below and close above it; deactivate an active high after any bar with high above and close below it, even if the other signal filters fail.
Calculation: Bar span = max(high - low, minimum price increment), with minimum price increment supplied as a parameter.
Calculation: Lower wick ratio = max(min(open, close) - low, 0) / bar span; upper wick ratio = max(high - max(open, close), 0) / bar span.
Calculation: Trend expansion = abs(shorter-period EMA(close) - longer-period EMA(close)) / ATR; undefined when ATR is unavailable or not positive.
Calculation: Percentile rank = 100 times the fraction of values in the rolling lookback, including the current bar, that are less than or equal to the current value. Use the same lookback for volume, both wick ratios, and trend expansion.
Calculation: Long score = clamp(0.35 × volume percentile rank + 0.35 × lower wick percentile rank + 0.30 × 100 × max((close - low) / bar span, 0), 0, 100).
Calculation: Short score = clamp(0.35 × volume percentile rank + 0.35 × upper wick percentile rank + 0.30 × 100 × max((high - close) / bar span, 0), 0, 100).
Long: Active pivot low exists AND low < active pivot low AND close > active pivot low AND ATR and all four percentile ranks are available AND trend expansion percentile rank <= maximum trend rank threshold AND volume percentile rank >= minimum volume rank threshold AND long score >= minimum score threshold AND (directional candle requirement is disabled OR close > open).
Short: Active pivot high exists AND high > active pivot high AND close < active pivot high AND ATR and all four percentile ranks are available AND trend expansion percentile rank <= maximum trend rank threshold AND volume percentile rank >= minimum volume rank threshold AND short score >= minimum score threshold AND (directional candle requirement is disabled OR close < open).
