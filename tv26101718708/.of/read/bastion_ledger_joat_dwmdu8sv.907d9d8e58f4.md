Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed after the specified numbers of bars on its left and right; retain the most recently confirmed value of each, updating it before evaluating structure breaks.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish after close > the latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high; bearish after close < the latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low. Retain the last bias until another break; if both breaks occur on the same bar, bearish takes precedence.
Calculation: Zone candidates = at a confirmed swing low, a demand zone extends from the pivot low minus an ATR padding to the lower of the pivot bar's open and close plus a separate ATR padding; at a confirmed swing high, a supply zone extends from the higher of the pivot bar's open and close minus an ATR padding to the pivot high plus a separate ATR padding. Use ATR at the pivot bar. Optionally require pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA to be at least a threshold; use 1 as the ratio when that SMA is not positive. On a bar with positive volume SMA, volume divided by its SMA at least an impulse threshold, AND absolute candle body divided by the greater of its range and a minimum price increment at least an efficiency threshold, create an impulse candidate: if close >= open, its demand zone extends from low minus an ATR padding to the higher of open and close plus a separate ATR padding; otherwise its supply zone extends from the lower of open and close minus an ATR padding to high plus a separate ATR padding. Use current-bar ATR. Each padding has its own adjustable multiplier. Sort each candidate's bounds and expand them equally if necessary to reach a minimum height expressed in price increments.
Calculation: Stored zones = process confirmed pivot-high, pivot-low, then impulse candidates in that order. Reject a candidate if any non-archived zone of the same type overlaps it or lies within a tolerance equal to current ATR times a multiplier. Otherwise store it with its pivot bar or impulse bar as its start. After each insertion, if the storage limit is exceeded, remove the oldest archived zone, or the oldest zone if none is archived. After processing candidates, archive each zone whose age from its start exceeds the zone lifetime; remove archived zones after their age exceeds the lifetime plus a grace period. Existing zones remain eligible for overlap checks regardless of breaks, sweeps, or retests until archived.
Long: A demand zone candidate passes the volume and efficiency conditions applicable to its source AND is stored.
Short: A supply zone candidate passes the volume and efficiency conditions applicable to its source AND is stored.
Long: Structure bias = bullish.
Short: Structure bias = bearish.
