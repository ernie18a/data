Calculation: Smoothed multi-timeframe K, D, J = For each of three selected timeframes, calculate RSV = 100 × (close − lowest low over the selected lookback) / (highest high over the selected lookback − lowest low over the selected lookback); K = 3-bar SMA of RSV, D = 3-bar SMA of K, J = 3K − 2D. The lookback is selected as volatility-based (using that timeframe’s ATR divided by close, with either direct or reversed mapping) or fixed. Weight each timeframe’s K, D, and J, then EMA-smooth each weighted average. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar; for lower timeframes use the last intrabar returned within the chart bar; for the chart timeframe use its current bar.
Calculation: Smoothed total = EMA of the mean of the three weighted K, D, and J values.
Calculation: Confirmed price and oscillator pivots = pivot highs and lows, confirmed using bars on both sides; retain the high or low, and the smoothed J value, respectively, on the pivot confirmation bar. Previous pivot references use the prior confirmed pivot.
Long: Smoothed J < oversold threshold AND current smoothed K > current smoothed D AND previous smoothed K <= previous smoothed D OR Smoothed J is between the oversold and mild-buy thresholds AND current smoothed K > current smoothed D AND previous smoothed K <= previous smoothed D OR current smoothed K > previous smoothed K AND current smoothed D < previous smoothed D AND absolute difference between smoothed K and D < anticipation threshold AND smoothed total < anticipation threshold OR current low < low recorded at the previous confirmed price pivot low AND current smoothed J > smoothed J recorded at the previous confirmed oscillator pivot low OR a bullish divergence potential was true one bar earlier.
Short: Smoothed J > overbought threshold AND current smoothed K < current smoothed D AND previous smoothed K >= previous smoothed D OR Smoothed J is between the mild-sell and overbought thresholds AND current smoothed K < current smoothed D AND previous smoothed K >= previous smoothed D OR current smoothed K < previous smoothed K AND current smoothed D > previous smoothed D AND absolute difference between smoothed K and D < anticipation threshold AND smoothed total > anticipation threshold OR current high > high recorded at the previous confirmed price pivot high AND current smoothed J < smoothed J recorded at the previous confirmed oscillator pivot high OR a bearish divergence potential was true one bar earlier.
Exit: long, short-entry order condition is true OR long trailing exit order condition is true (anticipation or short-entry signal).
Exit: short, long-entry order condition is true OR short trailing exit order condition is true (anticipation or long-entry signal).
