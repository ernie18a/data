Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot = a high/low exceeding the highs/lows in the specified number of bars on both sides; confirm it only after the right-side bars complete. Keep alternating pivots; replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type.
Calculation: ABCD points = the latest three alternating confirmed pivots A, B, C.
Calculation: AB length = absolute difference between A and B prices; BC retracement = absolute difference between B and C prices divided by AB length.
Calculation: D entry zone = projected D price ± ATR times a zone-width parameter; projected D is C minus AB length for bullish patterns and C plus AB length for bearish patterns.
Long: A is a high, B is a low, C is a high below A, AB length ≥ ATR times a minimum-size parameter, BC retracement is within the configured minimum and maximum, and the bar's high ≥ the lower D-zone boundary and its low ≤ the upper D-zone boundary
Short: A is a low, B is a high, C is a low above A, AB length ≥ ATR times a minimum-size parameter, BC retracement is within the configured minimum and maximum, and the bar's high ≥ the lower D-zone boundary and its low ≤ the upper D-zone boundary
Exit: long, low ≤ the lower D-zone boundary OR high ≥ C OR high ≥ A
Exit: short, high ≥ the upper D-zone boundary OR low ≤ C OR low ≤ A
