Calculation: Momentum = EMA(SMA((high + low) / 2, short lookback) - EMA((high + low) / 2, long lookback), smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Resistance line = straight line through the two most recent consecutive confirmed pivot highs. A pivot high has a specified number of bars on each side. The second high must be lower than the first, and their bar separation must be at least the minimum pivot separation. Replace the previous line whenever a new pivot high is confirmed, even if the new pair is invalid. On confirmation, mark the line broken if any close from the second pivot bar through the confirmation bar is above the line. Otherwise, mark it broken at its first buffered upward break, even if entry filters reject that break. Its age is the number of bars since the second pivot.
Calculation: Support line = straight line through the two most recent consecutive confirmed pivot lows. A pivot low has a specified number of bars on each side. The second low must be higher than the first, and their bar separation must be at least the minimum pivot separation. Replace the previous line whenever a new pivot low is confirmed, even if the new pair is invalid. On confirmation, mark the line broken if any close from the second pivot bar through the confirmation bar is below the line. Otherwise, mark it broken at its first buffered downward break, even if entry filters reject that break. Its age is the number of bars since the second pivot.
Calculation: Long signal = valid unbroken resistance line age <= maximum age AND close > current resistance line + ATR * break buffer AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum > previous momentum) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier).
Calculation: Short signal = valid unbroken support line age <= maximum age AND close < current support line - ATR * break buffer AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum < previous momentum) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier).
Calculation: Initial risk distance = at the entry signal close, the greater of an ATR-based minimum distance and the distance from close to the selected stop reference. The ATR stop reference is close minus an ATR multiple for a long or close plus an ATR multiple for a short. The swing stop reference is the lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR padding for a long or the highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR padding for a short. Fill entry at the next bar open and use its fill price as entry price.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus initial risk distance on the entry bar. On subsequent bars, if break-even is enabled and high reaches entry price plus the break-even multiple of initial risk distance, raise the stop to at least entry price. If trailing is enabled and high reaches entry price plus the trail-start multiple of initial risk distance, activate trailing for the rest of the position and raise the stop each bar to at least close minus an ATR multiple. Stop changes calculated at bar close apply to subsequent bars.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus initial risk distance on the entry bar. On subsequent bars, if break-even is enabled and low reaches entry price minus the break-even multiple of initial risk distance, lower the stop to at most entry price. If trailing is enabled and low reaches entry price minus the trail-start multiple of initial risk distance, activate trailing for the rest of the position and lower the stop each bar to at most close plus an ATR multiple. Stop changes calculated at bar close apply to subsequent bars.
Long: Long entries enabled AND long signal.
Short: Short entries enabled AND short signal.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + target multiple * initial risk distance) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= maximum bars) OR (reversal enabled AND short entries enabled AND short signal).
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price - target multiple * initial risk distance) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= maximum bars) OR (reversal enabled AND long entries enabled AND long signal).
Calculation: Take profit exits the configured fraction of the position; the remaining position retains its stop. Stop and target are active after the entry fill; when both are reached within one bar, resolve their order using the assumed intrabar price path. Time exits and reversals are submitted at bar close and fill at the next bar open. An entry signal opens a position only when flat, or when reversal is enabled and the position is opposite; signal markings occur regardless of position.
