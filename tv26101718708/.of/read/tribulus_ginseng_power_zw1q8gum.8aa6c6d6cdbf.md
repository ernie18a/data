Calculation: Crossover and crossunder = current value is respectively above or below the current reference AND the previous value is respectively at or below or at or above the previous reference.
Calculation: Paired event(A, B, window) = maintain a pending event type and elapsed bars, initially none and zero. Increment elapsed on each bar while an event is pending; clear the pending event if elapsed exceeds the window. Process A, then B: each event emits a match if the other type is pending, otherwise becomes pending; clear the pending event on a match, and reset elapsed whenever an event is processed. The result for the bar is the result of the last event processed, or false if neither occurs.
Calculation: Swing-range events = over a short lookback and a twice-as-long lookback, find the highest high and lowest low. A valid range has equal highs across the two lookbacks AND equal lows across the two lookbacks AND either the longer-window low or high occurred at the oldest bar of the short lookback. A confirmed range occurs on a closed bar when the range is valid AND the previous bar's valid range was at least a short-lookback number of bars ago. On the first bar of each consecutive confirmed-range run, save its longer-window high and low. Maintain a direction, initially undefined: set it positive when close crosses above the saved high, otherwise negative when close crosses below the saved low. The bullish event is a change to positive; the bearish event is a change to negative, treating the previous undefined direction as negative.
Calculation: Pivot-break events = confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. At each confirmed pivot save its current slope: choose ATR divided by pivot lookback times a multiplier, close standard deviation divided by pivot lookback times a multiplier, or the absolute linear-regression slope of close against bar index divided by two times a multiplier. Maintain an upper line initialized to zero and reset to each confirmed pivot high, otherwise subtract its saved high-pivot slope each bar; maintain a lower line initialized to zero and reset to each confirmed pivot low, otherwise add its saved low-pivot slope each bar. For each line, maintain a flag initially zero, reset it to zero on its corresponding pivot, and otherwise set it to one when close exceeds upper line minus high-pivot slope times pivot lookback, or close falls below lower line plus low-pivot slope times pivot lookback, respectively. The bullish or bearish event occurs when the corresponding flag changes from zero to one.
Calculation: Structure events = separately pair bullish swing-range and bullish pivot-break events, and bearish swing-range and bearish pivot-break events, using the same pairing window.
Calculation: Kernel bands = the causal Gaussian weighted average of close over a fixed window, with weight exp(-lag squared / (2 × bandwidth squared)) and missing closes replaced by zero, plus or minus a multiplier times the simple moving average of absolute close-to-kernel deviations over a window one bar shorter than the kernel window.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize the previous stop to zero; let distance be ATR times a multiplier. If current and previous close are both above the previous stop, use max(previous stop, close minus distance); if both are below it, use min(previous stop, close plus distance); otherwise use close minus distance when close is above the previous stop, and close plus distance otherwise. Maintain a direction initially neutral, changing to positive when close is above the previous stop AND previous close is at or below that stop, or negative when close is below the previous stop AND previous close is at or above that stop.
Calculation: Reversal events = maintain the most recent kernel-band crossing direction, initially neutral: positive on a close crossunder of the lower band and negative on a close crossover of the upper band. A positive reversal occurs when the lower-band crossunder occurs with positive trailing-stop direction OR trailing-stop direction changes to positive while the most recent band crossing is positive. A negative reversal is the symmetric upper-band and negative-direction condition. Require ADX to be at least its threshold if the ADX filter is enabled. If the liquidity filter is enabled, also require a low below the preceding lookback's lowest low followed by a close above that low within the liquidity memory window for a positive reversal, or a high above the preceding lookback's highest high followed by a close below that high within that window for a negative reversal.
Calculation: Phase events = maintain a phase initially zero. On any bar where volume exceeds its threshold AND close differs from its EMA, set phase to one before processing reversals. In phase one, a negative reversal emits a positive phase event and a positive reversal emits a negative phase event, with negative reversal taking priority; either changes phase to two. In phase two, a positive reversal emits a positive phase event and a negative reversal emits a negative phase event, with positive reversal taking priority; either resets phase to zero.
Calculation: Inverted local events = pair positive phase events with bullish structure events, and separately pair negative phase events with bearish structure events, using a second pairing window. The negative pair is the local long event and the positive pair is the local short event.
Long: local long event AND (RSI filter disabled OR lowest RSI over its lookback >= lower threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram >= 0 OR MACD histogram >= its previous value) AND (ATR filter disabled OR the largest bearish candle body over its lookback <= current ATR times its multiplier).
Short: local short event AND (RSI filter disabled OR highest RSI over its lookback <= upper threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram <= 0 OR MACD histogram <= its previous value) AND (ATR filter disabled OR the largest bullish candle body over its lookback <= current ATR times its multiplier).
Exit: long, short condition is true.
