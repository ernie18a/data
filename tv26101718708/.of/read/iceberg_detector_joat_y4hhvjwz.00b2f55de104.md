Calculation: Test = On each completed bar, let upper wick = high − max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) − low, body = max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment), rolling high = highest high and rolling low = lowest low over the extremum lookback including the current bar. A resistance test requires upper wick > body × wick ratio, high ≥ rolling high − (rolling high − rolling low) × extremum-band fraction, and volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier. A support test requires lower wick > body × wick ratio, low ≤ rolling low + (rolling high − rolling low) × extremum-band fraction, and volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier. Volume ratio is volume divided by its simple moving average, or zero if that average is not positive. If both tests qualify, retain only the resistance test when upper wick ≥ lower wick; otherwise retain only the support test. The test level is high for resistance and low for support. A resistance test is rejected when close ≤ open; a support test is rejected when close ≥ open.
Calculation: Iceberg state = Keep tests whose age in bars is ≤ the detection window. On each test, first match it to the oldest active iceberg of the same side whose fixed level differs from the test level by ≤ current ATR × cluster tolerance. If none matches, collect all retained tests of the same side whose levels differ from the current test level by ≤ current ATR × cluster tolerance. Create an active iceberg when their count ≥ minimum tests AND their rejected fraction ≥ rejection threshold; its fixed level is their mean level. After creation, remove the oldest active icebergs until their count ≤ the active-iceberg cap. Then remove every resistance iceberg when close ≥ its level + current ATR × break multiplier and every support iceberg when close ≤ its level − current ATR × break multiplier. Perform test matching and creation before removals on each completed bar.
Long: A support test creates a support iceberg OR a support test matches an active support iceberg OR close ≥ an active resistance iceberg's level + current ATR × break multiplier.
Short: A resistance test creates a resistance iceberg OR a resistance test matches an active resistance iceberg OR close ≤ an active support iceberg's level − current ATR × break multiplier.
