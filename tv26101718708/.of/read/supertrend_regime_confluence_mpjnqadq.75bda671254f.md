Calculation: Regime = Volatile when ATR / its longer-lookback SMA exceeds a threshold; Ranging when ADX is below its threshold and ATR / its longer-lookback SMA is below a threshold; otherwise Trending. ADX uses the same lookback for its directional movement and smoothing.
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = ATR-based upper and lower bands around bar midpoint, using a multiplier adjusted by regime (wider in Volatile, tighter in Ranging, base multiplier in Trending). In an uptrend, the active lower band ratchets upward; a close below it flips direction down and resets the band to the upper band. In a downtrend, the active upper band ratchets downward; a close above it flips direction up and resets the band to the lower band.
Calculation: Signal score = capped, rounded sum of tiered points for (1) volume relative to its moving average, (2) directional close-to-band distance in ATR units, (3) directional alignment with a longer-lookback EMA and close-to-EMA distance in ATR units, (4) regime quality, and (5) prior close-to-prior-band distance in current ATR units. Higher volume, displacement, EMA alignment and distance, cleaner regime, and greater prior distance receive higher points.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × configured multiplier, or close × configured percentage, or absolute distance between close and the SuperTrend band, according to the selected stop mode.
Calculation: Profit target distance = stop distance × configured reward-to-risk multiple, or close × configured percentage, according to the selected target mode; no target in None mode.
Long: SuperTrend direction flips up AND bar is confirmed AND datetime is within the configured backtest window AND current bar index minus last entry bar exceeds the configured cooldown AND signal score meets its minimum AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA) AND (ranging filter is disabled OR regime is not Ranging) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND long entries are enabled.
Short: SuperTrend direction flips down AND bar is confirmed AND datetime is within the configured backtest window AND current bar index minus last entry bar exceeds the configured cooldown AND signal score meets its minimum AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA) AND (ranging filter is disabled OR regime is not Ranging) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND short entries are enabled.
Exit: Long, low <= entry close − stop distance OR (target mode is enabled AND high >= entry close + target distance) OR (stop mode is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend direction flips down).
Exit: Short, high >= entry close + stop distance OR (target mode is enabled AND low <= entry close − target distance) OR (stop mode is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend direction flips up).
