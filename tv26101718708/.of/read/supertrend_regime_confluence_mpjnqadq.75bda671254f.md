Calculation: Regime = volatile if ATR divided by its moving average exceeds an upper threshold; otherwise ranging if ADX is below its threshold AND the ATR ratio is below a lower threshold; otherwise trending. Use the same lookback for both ADX smoothing stages.
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = initialize direction as up. Set the multiplier to the base multiplier, optionally scaled upward linearly with the ATR ratio in the volatile regime or scaled downward in the ranging regime, and bounded by lower and upper multiples of the base multiplier. Set upper and lower bases to the selected price source plus or minus multiplier × ATR. In an up direction, set the band to the greater of the lower base and previous band; if close is below it, switch direction to down and set the band to the upper base. In a down direction, set the band to the lesser of the upper base and previous band; if close is above it, switch direction to up and set the band to the lower base. Initialize a missing previous band to the current base for the current direction.
Calculation: Signal score = sum five independently parameterized point schedules, capped at a maximum score: volume divided by its moving average, with points increasing across three ordered thresholds; directional displacement from the current SuperTrend band divided by current ATR, with points increasing across four ordered thresholds; EMA alignment, with the highest points when aligned and absolute close-to-EMA distance divided by ATR exceeds an upper threshold, fewer when otherwise aligned, fewer when unaligned and the distance is below a lower threshold, and the fewest otherwise; regime quality, with most points for trending, fewer for volatile and fewest for ranging; absolute previous close-to-previous-band distance divided by current ATR, with points increasing across three ordered thresholds. For long displacement use close minus band; for short displacement use band minus close. Use a fallback ATR when ATR is unavailable and a volume ratio of one when average volume is not positive.
Calculation: Entry-qualified flip = SuperTrend direction changes AND datetime is within the configured window AND bars since the last executed entry exceed the cooldown, counting from the initial bar before any entry AND signal score meets its threshold AND, if enabled, the regime is not ranging AND, if enabled, volume exceeds its moving average AND, if enabled, close is above the trend EMA for a long or below it for a short AND the corresponding direction is enabled.
Calculation: Stop distance = at the entry signal bar, ATR × a multiplier, or close × a percentage, or absolute close-to-SuperTrend-band distance, according to the selected stop mode. Target distance = stop distance × a reward-to-risk multiplier, or signal-bar close × a percentage, according to the selected target mode.
Calculation: Trailing stop = optionally activate after price moves favorably from the entry order price by the stop distance; thereafter track the most favorable price since activation less an ATR-based offset for a long, or plus that offset for a short, with the offset fixed from the entry signal bar.
Long: SuperTrend direction changes from down to up.
Short: SuperTrend direction changes from up to down.
Exit: long, low reaches entry signal-bar close minus stop distance OR, if a target is enabled, high reaches entry signal-bar close plus target distance OR, if trailing is enabled and activated, low reaches the trailing stop OR, in SuperTrend stop mode, SuperTrend direction changes from up to down OR an entry-qualified flip to down occurs.
Exit: short, high reaches entry signal-bar close plus stop distance OR, if a target is enabled, low reaches entry signal-bar close minus target distance OR, if trailing is enabled and activated, high reaches the trailing stop OR, in SuperTrend stop mode, SuperTrend direction changes from down to up OR an entry-qualified flip to up occurs.
