Calculation: Permutation entropy = normalized Shannon entropy of three-point ordinal patterns, computed over the same lookback at three spacings (base delay, twice the delay, four times the delay); use the minimum entropy across spacings.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = slope of log mean absolute price change against log lag for lags 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16 over a common lookback, clamped to [0,1].
Calculation: Predictability = blend of (1 − minimum permutation entropy) and 2 × |Hurst − 0.5|; rank each component against its own longer lookback, then blend the ranks with the same blend weight.
Calculation: Gate = enough entropy patterns have accumulated AND predictability rank ≥ its threshold AND predictability ≥ its floor.
Calculation: Expert votes = sign(shorter-lookback EMA − longer-lookback EMA); sign(close − session VWAP); sign(smoothed order-flow delta); RSI vote (+1 below its lower threshold, −1 above its upper threshold, otherwise 0); band vote (+1 below the lower deviation threshold, −1 above the upper deviation threshold, otherwise 0); range vote (+1 below the lower range-position threshold, −1 above the upper range-position threshold, otherwise 0). Order-flow delta is the sum of signed volume (sign(lower-timeframe close change) × lower-timeframe volume) from completed bars of a lower timeframe, falling back to bar-shape signed volume if unavailable; smooth it with an EMA.
Calculation: Routed committee vote = weighted mean of the six expert votes, where EMA, VWAP, and order-flow votes have trend affinity max(0, min(1, 2 × (Hurst − 0.5))) and RSI, band, and range votes have reversion affinity max(0, min(1, 2 × (0.5 − Hurst))). Update each active expert's weight each confirmed bar using its prior vote and the latest close change: multiply by exp(−learning rate × loss), with loss 0 for a matching nonzero vote, 1 for a mismatching nonzero vote, and 0.5 for a zero vote; normalize weights, then blend them toward equal weights. Divide the affinity-weighted vote sum by the affinity-weighted weight sum. If the distribution-shift score (|shorter-lookback mean return − longer-lookback mean return| + |shorter-lookback return deviation − longer-lookback return deviation|) / longer-lookback return deviation exceeds its threshold, multiply the vote by 0.4. Set the vote to zero when the gate is closed.
Long: flat AND Gate is open AND routed committee vote ≥ its positive threshold.
Short: flat AND Gate is open AND routed committee vote ≤ the negative of its positive threshold.
Exit: long, routed committee vote ≤ 0 OR Gate is closed OR routed committee vote ≤ the negative of its positive threshold.
Exit: short, routed committee vote ≥ 0 OR Gate is closed OR routed committee vote ≥ its positive threshold.
