Calculation: Price = selected available price-field series, defaulting to close; volume = selected available volume-field series, defaulting to volume. Evaluate on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Entropy predictability = 1 minus the minimum normalized permutation entropy of three-point price patterns over a rolling pattern window, using base sample spacing, twice that spacing, and four times that spacing. Classify ties with earlier samples ordered before later samples; normalize Shannon entropy by the logarithm of the six possible patterns. Begin collecting patterns only when the longest spacing has sufficient history.
Calculation: Hurst = the least-squares slope of log(SMA(abs(price - price lagged by k), Hurst lookback)) against log(k), for k in 1, 2, 4, 8, 16; clamp to [0,1], or use 0.5 when unavailable. Trend affinity = max(0, 2 × (Hurst - 0.5)); reversion affinity = max(0, 2 × (0.5 - Hurst)); structure = max(trend affinity, reversion affinity).
Calculation: Predictability gate = sufficient collected patterns AND (gate disabled OR (weighted average of rolling percentile ranks of entropy predictability and structure >= gate percentile AND the same weighted average of their unranked values >= absolute floor)). The required pattern count is the smaller of the pattern window and the warmup parameter.
Calculation: Distribution shift = shift guard enabled AND (abs(short-window mean price change - long-window mean price change) + abs(short-window standard deviation of price change - long-window standard deviation of price change)) / long-window standard deviation of price change > shift threshold; use zero for the ratio when the long-window standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Expert votes = sign(short EMA of price - long EMA of price); sign(price - session VWAP of typical price); sign(EMA of signed volume flow); -1 when RSI exceeds its upper threshold, +1 when below its lower threshold, otherwise 0; -1 when the price's rolling standard-deviation z-score exceeds its upper threshold, +1 when below its lower threshold, otherwise 0; and -1 when (price - rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high - rolling lowest low) exceeds its upper threshold, +1 when below its lower threshold, otherwise 0. Use zero z-score when standard deviation is zero and the midpoint for range position when the range is zero. Signed volume flow is the sum of sign(lower-timeframe close - previous lower-timeframe close) × lower-timeframe volume over all completed lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar; when unavailable or disabled, use volume × (2 × close - high - low) / (high - low), or zero for a zero-range bar. Replace unavailable votes with zero. Optional enabled external experts use available input-field series divided by a scale and clamped to [-1,1].
Calculation: Committee = weighted mean of active expert votes, with each weight multiplied by its affinity: trend affinity for the first three votes, reversion affinity for the next three, and the selected trend, reversion, or maximum affinity for each external expert. If routing is disabled, use affinity 1. Initialize active expert weights equally. On each completed bar, before computing the committee, if the gate is open, distribution shift is false, and learning is enabled, multiply each weight by exp(-learning rate × loss), normalize weights, then shrink each toward equal weight; loss is 0.5 for a zero previous vote, 0 when the previous vote's sign equals the current price-change sign, otherwise 1. Save current votes for the next bar even when learning is frozen. If learning is disabled, use equal weights. Use zero when the affinity-weight sum is zero; multiply the result by the shift-discount factor during a distribution shift; set it to zero when the gate is closed.
Long: predictability gate open AND committee >= positive entry threshold.
Short: predictability gate open AND committee <= negative entry threshold.
Long: position side is flat or short AND predictability gate open AND committee >= positive entry threshold.
Short: position side is flat or long AND predictability gate open AND committee <= negative entry threshold.
Exit: long, committee <= 0 OR predictability gate closed.
Exit: short, committee >= 0 OR predictability gate closed.
Exit: long, at least one bar since entry AND entry-bar ATR > 0 AND (low <= entry price - stop multiplier × entry-bar ATR OR high >= entry price + target multiplier × entry-bar ATR OR bars since entry >= time limit).
Exit: short, at least one bar since entry AND entry-bar ATR > 0 AND (high >= entry price + stop multiplier × entry-bar ATR OR low <= entry price - target multiplier × entry-bar ATR OR bars since entry >= time limit).
