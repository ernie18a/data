Calculation: RSI series = three chart-timeframe RSIs of a selected price source, with independently configurable lengths; a fourth RSI uses its own source and length and either the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. For the fourth RSI, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; on the chart timeframe, use the previous bar in completed-bar mode or the current bar in forming-bar mode.
Calculation: Oversold alignment = all three chart-timeframe RSIs are at or below a shared oversold threshold AND, if fourth-RSI confirmation is required, the fourth RSI is at or below its oversold threshold AND, if turning is required, the fourth RSI is above its previous chart-bar value. Its threshold can be shared or separately configured.
Calculation: Overbought alignment = all three chart-timeframe RSIs are at or above a shared overbought threshold AND, if fourth-RSI confirmation is required, the fourth RSI is at or above its overbought threshold AND, if turning is required, the fourth RSI is below its previous chart-bar value. Its threshold can be shared or separately configured.
Calculation: RSI pivot = an RSI pivot high or low with configurable left and right lookbacks, confirmed after the right lookback has elapsed. Compare consecutive pivots of the same type on the same RSI; the number of chart bars between their confirmation bars minus one must be within the inclusive configured range. Compare price highs or lows at the RSI pivot bars, without requiring price pivots. Apply this independently to each enabled RSI, including the optional fourth RSI.
Long: Oversold alignment AND, if first-alignment-only is selected, oversold alignment was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: Overbought alignment AND, if first-alignment-only is selected, overbought alignment was false on the previous chart bar.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its RSI is above the preceding pivot-low RSI AND its price low is below the preceding pivot-low price low.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its RSI is below the preceding pivot-low RSI AND its price low is above the preceding pivot-low price low.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its RSI is below the preceding pivot-high RSI AND its price high is above the preceding pivot-high price high.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its RSI is above the preceding pivot-high RSI AND its price high is below the preceding pivot-high price high.
