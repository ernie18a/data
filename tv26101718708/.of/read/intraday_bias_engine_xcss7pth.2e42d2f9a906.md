Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of bars in the opening-range session; available after that session ends.
Calculation: Daily anchors = previous completed daily bar's high, low, and close, and current still-forming daily bar's open; daily ATR uses completed daily bars. Daily bars are higher timeframe bars.
Calculation: CPR = pivot (previous daily high + low + close)/3; base = (previous daily high + low)/2; top = 2 × pivot − base; CPR bias is bullish when close is above both top and base, bearish when below both, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close)/3 × volume divided by cumulative volume since the start of the current day, using chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Market structure = bullish when the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows are both rising; bearish when both are falling; otherwise neutral. Pivots use equal left/right lookbacks.
Calculation: Pressure = clamp to 0–100 a score starting neutral, adjusted bullish or bearish by close relative to VWAP, cumulative volume delta, momentum, relative volume with candle direction, and close relative to daily open. Volume delta per bar = volume × (2 × close − high − low)/(high − low), or zero when high equals low; cumulative volume delta resets each day. Relative volume compares volume with its moving average. Momentum is rate of change; its contribution is capped at its scale.
Calculation: Bull, bear, and sideways probabilities = each category's points divided by total points. Bull points add capped positive gap size relative to the significant-gap threshold, capped positive VWAP distance, one point for close above opening-range high, one for close above CPR, one for bullish structure, capped positive momentum, pressure above neutral scaled to its range, and capped positive distance of close above daily open relative to half daily ATR. Bear points use the corresponding negative conditions. Sideways points add points when opening-range width relative to daily ATR is below its narrow threshold, relative volume is below its low-volume threshold, absolute VWAP distance is below its proximity threshold, and chart-timeframe ATR is below its moving average times its compression factor.
Calculation: Confidence = rounded, clamped weighted combination of the dominant bull/bear/sideways probability and opening-range clarity; clarity is the opening-range-width/daily-ATR ratio's absolute distance from its narrow threshold, scaled by its wide threshold, or a default when unavailable.
Long: After the opening-range session ends AND bull probability > its confirmation threshold AND confidence >= its strong threshold OR close crosses above opening-range high OR close crosses above VWAP.
Short: After the opening-range session ends AND bear probability > its confirmation threshold AND confidence >= its strong threshold OR close crosses below opening-range low OR close crosses below VWAP.
