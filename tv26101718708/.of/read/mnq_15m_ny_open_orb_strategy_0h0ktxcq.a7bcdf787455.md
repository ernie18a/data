Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of the bars in the opening-range session, finalized when that session ends
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of the bars in the opening-range session, finalized when that session ends
Long: opening range is locked AND current bar is in the trade session AND position is flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit is disabled OR no trade has occurred today) AND long entries are enabled AND (close > opening-range high if close-break mode is enabled, otherwise high > opening-range high)
Short: opening range is locked AND current bar is in the trade session AND position is flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit is disabled OR no trade has occurred today) AND short entries are enabled AND (close < opening-range low if close-break mode is enabled, otherwise low < opening-range low)
Exit: long, bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR bar low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR (highest high since entry >= entry price + trailing activation distance AND bar low <= highest high since entry - trailing stop distance) OR (end-of-day close is enabled AND current bar is outside the trade session AND previous bar was inside it)
Exit: short, bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR bar high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR (lowest low since entry <= entry price - trailing activation distance AND bar high >= lowest low since entry + trailing stop distance) OR (end-of-day close is enabled AND current bar is outside the trade session AND previous bar was inside it)
