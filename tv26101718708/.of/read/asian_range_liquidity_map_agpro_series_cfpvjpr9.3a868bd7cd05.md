Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session in the configured timezone; width = highest high - lowest low. At the first bar outside the session, freeze the range and its validity until the next session ends. The range is valid if the ATR filter is enabled and higher-timeframe ATR is available and positive and width / ATR is between the configured minimum and maximum inclusive; otherwise it is valid if width > 0. ATR uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars, including the higher-timeframe bar closing on the current chart bar; on a live bar it uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Sweep = the first chart bar from session end through the configured sweep-window duration inclusive where high > frozen session high OR low < frozen session low, provided the frozen range is valid and the chart timeframe is within the configured intraday limits. If both sides are broken, select the high sweep when high - session high >= session low - low; otherwise select the low sweep. Retain the selected side and range until the next session ends.
Calculation: Reaction statistics = for each sweep, after the configured number of chart bars, classify the following bars over that lookback, excluding the sweep bar. For a high sweep, reversal means lowest close <= swept high - reversal threshold * frozen width; continuation means current close > swept high AND lowest low >= swept high - continuation threshold * frozen width. For a low sweep, reversal means highest close >= swept low + reversal threshold * frozen width; continuation means current close < swept low AND highest high <= swept low + continuation threshold * frozen width. Reversal takes precedence over continuation; otherwise the reaction is mixed. Keep the configured number of most recently classified sweeps across both sides, and count reversals and continuations separately for each side. Discard an unclassified sweep if the next session ends first.
Long: A high sweep occurs.
Short: A low sweep occurs.
Long: Chart timeframe is within the configured intraday limits AND the retained sweep is high AND its historical continuation count > its historical reversal count.
Short: Chart timeframe is within the configured intraday limits AND the retained sweep is high AND its historical reversal count > its historical continuation count.
Long: Chart timeframe is within the configured intraday limits AND the retained sweep is low AND its historical reversal count > its historical continuation count.
Short: Chart timeframe is within the configured intraday limits AND the retained sweep is low AND its historical continuation count > its historical reversal count.
