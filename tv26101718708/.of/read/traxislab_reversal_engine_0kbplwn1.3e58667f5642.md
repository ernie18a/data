Calculation: ATR used = ATR, or close multiplied by a fallback fraction when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: EMA slope = (short EMA of close minus its value a specified number of chart bars ago) / ATR used.
Calculation: Relative vector force = volume × (high − low) / SMA of volume × (high − low), using a specified lookback; use zero when the SMA is not positive. Body ratio = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = high or low of a bar that is respectively the highest or lowest over a specified number of preceding bars and a shorter specified number of subsequent bars. The latest confirmed pivot of each kind replaces the preceding one; clear a pivot after its corresponding structure break. Bullish structure break = close > latest pivot high AND previous close ≤ that same pivot high. Bearish structure break = close < latest pivot low AND previous close ≥ that same pivot low. A change of character is a structure break opposite to the direction of the preceding structure break.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe direction = for each enabled timeframe, sum +1 or −1 respectively when its short EMA is above or below its medium EMA, its close is above or below its long EMA, and its RSI is above an upper or below a lower threshold; otherwise add zero for each comparison. Direction is bullish when the sum reaches a positive threshold, bearish when it reaches the corresponding negative threshold, and neutral otherwise. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on a realtime chart bar, and the last available lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. Count bullish and bearish timeframes separately.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × max(volume, minimum volume) divided by cumulative sum of max(volume, minimum volume), resetting at each specified session boundary; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3. Use the current still-forming session.
Calculation: Long score = sum of configurable component weights, capped at a maximum: if the trend filter is enabled, add separate weights for short EMA > medium EMA, close > long EMA, and enabled slope filter AND EMA slope > positive slope threshold; otherwise add a fixed weight. Add the larger or smaller RSI weight when RSI is below the oversold threshold or, otherwise, a higher threshold. Add the larger or smaller stochastic %K weight when it is below a lower or, otherwise, a higher threshold. Add the change-of-character weight or, otherwise, the bullish structure-break weight. Add the bullish-engulfing weight or, otherwise, the bullish-hammer weight or, otherwise, the bullish large-body weight. If the volume filter is enabled, add its weight when volume > its SMA × volume multiplier; otherwise add a fixed weight. Add weights when close is within the lower fraction of the range between the recent lowest low and highest high, when enabled ADX > its threshold, when close > session VWAP AND close − session VWAP < ATR used × proximity multiplier, and when relative vector force ≥ vector threshold AND body ratio ≥ body threshold AND close > open. If the ADX filter is disabled, add its fixed weight. If the multi-timeframe bonus is enabled, add a weight per bullish timeframe. Bullish engulfing requires close > open, previous close < previous open, close > previous open, and open < previous close. Bullish hammer requires close > open, lower wick > body × wick multiplier, and upper wick < body × smaller wick multiplier. Bullish large body requires close > open and body ratio > large-body threshold.
Calculation: Short score = the corresponding short-side sum, capped at the same maximum: reverse EMA, slope, structure, candle and VWAP directions; award RSI and stochastic weights above their upper thresholds, with the larger weight at the more extreme threshold; award the range weight in the upper fraction of the recent high-low range; use bearish vector force with close ≤ open; and use the bearish multi-timeframe count. Apply the same volume and ADX terms and disabled-filter weights. Bearish engulfing requires close < open, previous close > previous open, close < previous open, and open > previous close. Bearish shooting star requires close < open, upper wick > body × wick multiplier, and lower wick < body × smaller wick multiplier. Bearish large body requires close < open and body ratio > large-body threshold.
Calculation: Entry qualification for each side = its score ≥ entry threshold AND bars since its last actual signal > side cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND (multi-timeframe filter disabled OR its directional timeframe count ≥ minimum count) AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA slope exceeds the threshold in that side's direction). Entry threshold is selected by signal mode or a manual override.
Calculation: Signal state = remember the last actual signal side, bar, entry close, and entry-bar ATR used. Before evaluating signals on each later bar, clear the active side when its stop or first target is reached or its maximum age is exceeded. Calculate the opposite-side lock from the active side and its age before clearing it; an opposite signal is locked while that age is less than the lock duration. A same-side signal is blocked while that side remains active. The stop is entry price minus entry-bar ATR used × stop multiplier for a long and plus that distance for a short; the first target is entry price plus or minus that distance × first reward-to-risk multiplier, respectively.
Calculation: Trailing level = after a long signal set close − ATR used × trail multiplier, or after a short signal set close + ATR used × trail multiplier. While direction is long and close > level, raise the level to the maximum of its previous value and close − ATR used × trail multiplier; while direction is short and close < level, lower it to the minimum of its previous value and close + ATR used × trail multiplier. If long direction and close < level, switch to short and set level to close + ATR used × trail multiplier; if short direction and close > level, switch to long and set level to close − ATR used × trail multiplier. Process long update, short update, long reversal, then short reversal in that order.

Short: short entry qualification AND (long entry qualification is false OR short score > long score) AND absolute(long score − short score) ≥ minimum score gap AND opposite-side lock is false AND active side ≠ short.
Long: bars since either pending warning > pending cooldown AND bars since last actual long signal > side cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND opposite-side lock is false AND active side ≠ long AND (multi-timeframe filter disabled OR bullish timeframe count ≥ minimum count) AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA slope > positive slope threshold) AND long score ≥ rounded(entry threshold × pending fraction) AND long score < entry threshold AND long score > short score AND absolute(long score − short score) ≥ minimum score gap AND actual long signal is false.
Short: bars since either pending warning > pending cooldown AND bars since last actual short signal > side cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ threshold) AND opposite-side lock is false AND active side ≠ short AND (multi-timeframe filter disabled OR bearish timeframe count ≥ minimum count) AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA slope < negative slope threshold) AND short score ≥ rounded(entry threshold × pending fraction) AND short score < entry threshold AND short score > long score AND absolute(long score − short score) ≥ minimum score gap AND actual short signal is false.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − entry-bar ATR used × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + entry-bar ATR used × stop multiplier × first reward-to-risk multiplier.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + entry-bar ATR used × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − entry-bar ATR used × stop multiplier × first reward-to-risk multiplier.
