Calculation: Selected support and resistance zones = From confirmed pivot lows and highs in the lookback, separately cluster pivots within the merge threshold and use pivot-score-weighted centers with bounds spanning cluster prices. Pivot scores incorporate relative volume and recency. Search below and above price independently, expanding through distance tiers until a cluster meets minimum touch and quality thresholds; quality combines touch count, post-pivot reaction, relative volume, recency, and accumulated pivot score, with the threshold adjusted by ATR as a percentage of close. Within each active range, select the cluster with the highest accumulated pivot score plus proximity bonus. Expand its bounds by the larger of percentage padding or ATR padding. Use the prior bar's selected zones for break triggers.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the prior resistance zone's upper bound plus the breakout buffer; OR, while waiting for a resistance retest within the retest window, low reaches the former resistance upper bound within tolerance and close finishes above that bound; OR, while waiting for that retest, close falls below the former resistance lower bound beyond the failed-break buffer.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the prior support zone's lower bound minus the breakout buffer; OR, while waiting for a support rebound within the retest window, high reaches the former support lower bound within tolerance and close finishes below that bound; OR, while waiting for that rebound, close rises above the former support upper bound beyond the failed-break buffer.
