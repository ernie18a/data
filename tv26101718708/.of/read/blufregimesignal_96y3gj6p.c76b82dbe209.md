Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Regime = initialize as caution. From range, when efficiency ratio is at least the range-exit threshold, change to trend if it exceeds the trend-entry threshold, otherwise caution. From trend, when efficiency ratio is at most the trend-exit threshold, change to range if it is below the range-entry threshold, otherwise caution. From caution, change to range below the range-entry threshold or to trend above the trend-entry threshold.
Calculation: Directional confluence count = number of satisfied checks: efficiency ratio at least the trend-entry threshold; shorter HMA above the longer HMA for long or below it for short; RSI at least the bullish threshold for long or at most the bearish threshold for short; MACD histogram positive and rising for two consecutive bars for long or negative and falling for two consecutive bars for short; close above session VWAP for long or below it for short. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA. Session VWAP is the cumulative volume-weighted average of the selected price source, reset at each new session, with close as fallback when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Minimum confluence count = three for the weak tier, four for the strong tier, or five for the prime tier; the prime-only setting also requires five.
Calculation: Flip state and stop = initialize bullish with no stop. In bullish state, compare close with the previous stop; if close is below it, change to bearish and set the stop to close plus ATR times a multiplier. Otherwise set the stop to the greater of its previous value and close minus ATR times the multiplier, using the latter if no stop exists. In bearish state, if close is above the previous stop, change to bullish and set the stop to close minus ATR times the multiplier. Otherwise set the stop to the lesser of its previous value and close plus ATR times the multiplier, using the latter if no stop exists.
Calculation: Shift cooldown = bars since the last accepted upward trend shift, downward trend shift, or range shift. A range shift is accepted when the regime changes to range and the cooldown is at least its threshold. Initialize the cooldown as already satisfied; reset it on each accepted shift.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe HMA = HMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime.
Long: Flip state changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Flip state changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: Shorter HMA of close > longer HMA of close.
Short: Shorter HMA of close < longer HMA of close.
Long: Close > higher-timeframe HMA.
Short: Close < higher-timeframe HMA.
Long: Regime changes to trend AND shift cooldown is at least its threshold AND close > session VWAP AND long confluence count is at least the selected minimum AND bars since the last accepted upward trend shift are at least the same-direction lockout AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR filter is aligned AND close > higher-timeframe HMA OR filter is reversal AND close < higher-timeframe HMA).
Short: Regime changes to trend AND shift cooldown is at least its threshold AND close <= session VWAP AND short confluence count is at least the selected minimum AND bars since the last accepted downward trend shift are at least the same-direction lockout AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR filter is aligned AND close < higher-timeframe HMA OR filter is reversal AND close > higher-timeframe HMA).
Long: Capture mode is enabled AND regime changes to trend AND shift cooldown is at least its threshold AND long confluence count >= short confluence count.
Short: Capture mode is enabled AND regime changes to trend AND shift cooldown is at least its threshold AND long confluence count < short confluence count.
