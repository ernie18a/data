Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored (reset each day) cumulative sum(typical price*volume)/cumulative sum(volume), typical price=(high+low+close)/3
Calculation: SD = session-anchored sqrt(cumulative sum(volume*(typical price-current VWAP)^2)/cumulative volume); if that is 0 use (high-low)/2
Calculation: Setup = the first bar in a session, at or after a fixed time of day, while flat with no pending setup, where low <= VWAP-2*SD (setup direction long) or high >= VWAP+2*SD (setup direction short); if both are true, direction is long if close<VWAP else short. That bar is the touch bar. The setup is pending only on bars 1..N after the touch bar (the touch bar itself is excluded). It is cancelled by an entry, by window expiry, by a session reset, or by any pending-window bar without an entry where (long direction) high >= VWAP - small distance, or (short direction) low <= VWAP + small distance
Calculation: Body=|close-open|; Range=high-low; Doji = Range>0 AND Body/Range <= small fraction; Hammer = Range>0 AND lower wick/Range >= large fraction AND upper wick/Range <= small fraction (large fraction > small fraction); InvHammer = same with upper and lower wicks swapped (upper wick = high-max(open,close), lower wick = min(open,close)-low)
Calculation: StopLevel = fixed at entry; long = lowest low of the last M bars including the entry bar minus a fixed buffer; short = highest high of the last M bars including the entry bar plus a fixed buffer
Long: pending setup with direction long AND close<VWAP AND close>previous close AND ((close>open AND Body>previous Body) OR (close>open AND previous close>previous open AND Body+previous Body > Body of the bar 2 bars ago)) AND NOT Doji AND |close-VWAP| >= m*SD AND NOT InvHammer; enter at the bar close, and no exit is evaluated on the entry bar or on a bar where an exit occurred
Short: pending setup with direction short AND close>VWAP AND close<previous close AND ((close<open AND Body>previous Body) OR (close<open AND previous close<previous open AND Body+previous Body > Body of the bar 2 bars ago)) AND NOT Doji AND |close-VWAP| >= m*SD AND NOT Hammer; enter at the bar close, and no exit is evaluated on the entry bar or on a bar where an exit occurred
Exit: long, low <= StopLevel (checked first) OR high >= current VWAP - small distance
Exit: short, high >= StopLevel (checked first) OR low <= current VWAP + small distance
