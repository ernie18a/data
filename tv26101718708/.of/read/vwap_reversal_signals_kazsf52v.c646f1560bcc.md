Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting both sums at each new calendar day; use close if cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Session standard deviation = square root of cumulative sum of volume × (typical price − current session VWAP)² divided by cumulative volume; if that sum or cumulative volume is zero, use (high − low) / 2.
Calculation: Upper and lower 2σ bands = session VWAP ± 2 × session standard deviation.
Calculation: Candle body = absolute close − open; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; range = high − low.
Calculation: Doji = range > 0 AND body / range ≤ 0.10; otherwise not a doji.
Calculation: Inverted hammer = range > 0 AND upper wick / range ≥ configured dominant-wick percentage AND lower wick / range ≤ configured opposite-wick percentage. Hammer uses the same conditions with lower and upper wick exchanged.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the configured lookback, including the current bar, − configured tick buffer. Short stop = highest high over that lookback + configured tick buffer.
Long: While flat, after the configured New York start time, the first fresh touch of the lower 2σ band without a simultaneous upper-band touch arms a long setup (if both bands are touched, arm long when close < session VWAP); on a later bar within the configured expiry, enter if close < session VWAP AND (close > open AND current body > previous body OR current and previous bars both close above their opens AND current body + previous body > body two bars ago) AND close > previous close AND (range = 0 OR body / range > 0.10) AND abs(close − session VWAP) ≥ configured minimum standard-deviation distance × session standard deviation AND the bar is not an inverted hammer. Do not enter if price reaches the configured VWAP proximity during the arm window; discard the setup after that, or when it expires.
Short: While flat, after the configured New York start time, the first fresh touch of the upper 2σ band without a simultaneous lower-band touch arms a short setup (if both bands are touched, arm short when close ≥ session VWAP); on a later bar within the configured expiry, enter if close > session VWAP AND (close < open AND current body > previous body OR current and previous bars both close below their opens AND current body + previous body > body two bars ago) AND close < previous close AND (range = 0 OR body / range > 0.10) AND abs(close − session VWAP) ≥ configured minimum standard-deviation distance × session standard deviation AND the bar is not a hammer. Do not enter if price reaches the configured VWAP proximity during the arm window; discard the setup after that, or when it expires.
Exit: Long, low ≤ the long stop OR high ≥ session VWAP − configured proximity ticks × minimum tick size.
Exit: Short, high ≥ the short stop OR low ≤ session VWAP + configured proximity ticks × minimum tick size.
