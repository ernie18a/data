Calculation: Shorter-lookback EMA = exponential moving average of close over a shorter lookback on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Longer-lookback EMA = exponential moving average of close over a longer lookback on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Money flow = simple moving average of ((close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)) × multiplier over a lookback on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming
Long: Shorter-lookback EMA > Longer-lookback EMA AND Money flow > 0
Short: Shorter-lookback EMA < Longer-lookback EMA AND Money flow < 0
