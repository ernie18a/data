Calculation: Active leg = a configured positive-weight asset with a present chart-timeframe bar, positive volume and high > low, and, when the optional session filter is enabled, a bar within the configured session. Update each asset's volume baseline only on eligible positive-volume bars: initialize it to volume, then use (previous baseline × (baseline length − 1) + volume) / baseline length. Carry it forward between updates.
Calculation: Leg flow = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) × min(volume / updated volume baseline, relative-volume cap); negate the flow of the inverse hedge leg.
Calculation: Composite flow = sum of active leg flow × leg weight divided by sum of active weights. Smooth it with an EMA initialized to the first available composite flow and updated only when composite flow is available. Standardized flow = (smoothed flow − mean) / population standard deviation of the latest available smoothed-flow observations, including the current one; require the minimum warm-up count and a positive standard deviation. Carry the smoothed flow across unavailable bars, but add observations to the normalization window only when composite flow is available.
Calculation: Display flow = standardized flow when normalization is enabled, otherwise smoothed flow. Display flow is unavailable outside the configured session when histogram suppression is enabled.
Calculation: Directional breadth = sum of active weights whose leg flow is strictly positive or strictly negative, respectively, divided by total active weight.
Calculation: Bullish RSI filter = filter disabled OR RSI below its lower band OR (RSI rising AND previous RSI below its lower band) OR (RSI above its midpoint AND below its upper band). Bearish RSI filter = filter disabled OR RSI above its upper band OR (RSI falling AND previous RSI above its upper band) OR (RSI below its midpoint AND above its lower band). Calculate RSI on the configured filter asset's own chart-timeframe bars and carry its last available value across gaps.
Calculation: Bullish agreement state = standardized flow > 0 AND standardized flow > previous chart bar's standardized flow AND bullish RSI filter AND positive directional breadth >= breadth threshold AND active leg count >= coverage threshold AND current bar within the configured session. Bearish agreement state = standardized flow < 0 AND standardized flow < previous chart bar's standardized flow AND bearish RSI filter AND negative directional breadth >= breadth threshold AND active leg count >= coverage threshold AND current bar within the configured session.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low with the configured number of bars on each side satisfying the price-pivot rule; evaluate it only after the right-side bars have closed. Store the price and standardized flow of each confirmed high and low separately whenever its pivot bar has sufficient active legs and available standardized flow, regardless of whether a divergence is shown.
Calculation: Flow strength = absolute standardized flow divided by its chart-bar simple moving average over the strength lookback, when that average is positive.
Calculation: Pressure cloud = separately EMA-smooth the active-weight-normalized sums of positive leg flow and of the absolute value of negative leg flow, initializing each EMA with its first available input and updating only when that input is available.
