Calculation: Per-asset flow = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) × min(volume / trailing RMA volume, cap); exclude invalid or out-of-session bars, and negate the inverse-hedge asset’s flow
Calculation: Composite flow = weighted mean of available per-asset flows
Calculation: Normalized composite flow = z-score of the EMA-smoothed composite flow over a rolling lookback; require at least 10 values and positive standard deviation. Signals use this normalized value even when the display uses raw flow
Calculation: Directional breadth = active weight of assets whose flow has the specified sign / total active weight
Calculation: Filter RSI = RSI of the configured source asset on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot high/low = a high/low with the configured equal left- and right-side strength; evaluate its normalized composite flow and RSI at the pivot bar
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, active asset count ≥ configured minimum AND during the configured regular session AND (normalized composite flow crosses above 0 AND bullish RSI filter OR normalized composite flow > 0 AND normalized composite flow > its prior value AND bullish RSI filter AND bullish breadth ≥ configured minimum AND this bullish agreement has just begun AND bullish cooldown elapsed OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the prior confirmed pivot low AND normalized composite flow at the new pivot exceeds its value at the prior pivot low by at least the configured minimum disagreement AND the pivot meets the configured session and optional RSI confirmation)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, active asset count ≥ configured minimum AND during the configured regular session AND (normalized composite flow crosses below 0 AND bearish RSI filter OR normalized composite flow < 0 AND normalized composite flow < its prior value AND bearish RSI filter AND bearish breadth ≥ configured minimum AND this bearish agreement has just begun AND bearish cooldown elapsed OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the prior confirmed pivot high AND normalized composite flow at the prior pivot high exceeds its value at the new pivot by at least the configured minimum disagreement AND the pivot meets the configured session and optional RSI confirmation)
