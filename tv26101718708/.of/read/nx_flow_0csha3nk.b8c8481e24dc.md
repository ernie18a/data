Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = high or low at a candidate bar that qualifies as a pivot against configurable numbers of bars to its left and right; it becomes available only after the right-hand bars have completed. Retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low separately.
Calculation: SuperTrend direction = initially bullish. Form basic upper and lower bands from a configurable price source plus and minus an ATR multiple. Set the upper band to its basic value when it is below the previous upper band or the previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Set the lower band to its basic value when it is above the previous lower band or the previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize each band with its basic value. Switch from bullish to bearish when close is below the current lower band, and from bearish to bullish when close is above the current upper band; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = compare the higher-timeframe close with its EMA. With lookahead disabled, historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, made available at its closing chart bar; a live chart bar can use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Volume-spread direction = when volume divided by its SMA exceeds a threshold AND high minus low exceeds its SMA, bullish if close > open and bearish if close <= open; otherwise neutral. The two SMAs use the same lookback.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise; optionally reset at each daily session boundary.
Calculation: Volume-profile point of control = divide the highest high to lowest low over a lookback into equal price bins, assign each bar's volume to the bin containing its typical price, and take the midpoint of the highest-volume bin; resolve ties in favor of the lowest bin. Compute on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Pivot midpoint = midpoint of the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of these seven true conditions: low > high two bars ago; close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND volume-spread direction is not bearish; SuperTrend direction is bullish; close > pivot midpoint; cumulative volume delta > 0; close > volume-profile point of control. A crossover requires current close > current pivot high AND previous close <= previous pivot high.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of these seven true conditions: high < low two bars ago; close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low; higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND volume-spread direction is not bullish; SuperTrend direction is bearish; close < pivot midpoint; cumulative volume delta < 0; close < volume-profile point of control. A crossunder requires current close < current pivot low AND previous close >= previous pivot low.
Long: low > high two bars ago
Short: high < low two bars ago
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low
Long: SuperTrend direction is bullish
Short: SuperTrend direction is bearish
Long: close >= open
Short: close < open
Long: bullish score >= sensitivity threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (body filter disabled OR absolute value of close minus open > ATR multiplied by a threshold)
Short: bearish score >= sensitivity threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (body filter disabled OR absolute value of close minus open > ATR multiplied by a threshold)
