Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative volume, adding volume when close > open and subtracting volume when close < open; reset according to the configured reset period
Calculation: Volume profile point of control = midpoint of the equal-width price bin with the greatest summed volume, using typical price (high + low + close) / 3 to assign volume across the configured lookback
Calculation: Fibonacci 0.5 level = midpoint between the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low
Calculation: Smart bias = bullish when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and VSA is not bearish; bearish when the close is below its EMA and VSA is not bullish; otherwise neutral. VSA is bullish or bearish when current-timeframe volume exceeds its average by the configured relative-volume threshold, the high-low spread exceeds its average, and close is above or below open, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of bullish FVG, bullish CHoCH, bullish smart bias, bullish SuperTrend direction, close above Fibonacci 0.5, positive cumulative volume delta, and close above volume profile point of control
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = count of bearish FVG, bearish CHoCH, bearish smart bias, bearish SuperTrend direction, close below Fibonacci 0.5, negative cumulative volume delta, and close below volume profile point of control
Long: low > high two bars earlier OR close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high OR bullish confluence score >= configured threshold AND (volume > its configured moving average, if enabled) AND (absolute(close - open) > half the configured ATR, if enabled)
Short: high < low two bars earlier OR close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low OR bearish confluence score >= configured threshold AND (volume > its configured moving average, if enabled) AND (absolute(close - open) > half the configured ATR, if enabled)
