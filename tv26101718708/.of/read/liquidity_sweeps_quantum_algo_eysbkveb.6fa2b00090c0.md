Calculation: Liquidity levels = On each chart bar, add each newly confirmed pivot high and pivot low to its respective side before evaluating sweeps. A pivot has the same configurable number of bars on each side; a pivot high is at least as high as all preceding bars and higher than all following bars in that window, and a pivot low is at most as low as all preceding bars and lower than all following bars. Keep a configurable maximum number of the most recently confirmed levels per side, counting retired levels if the option to retain them is enabled; discard the oldest when the limit is exceeded. A level is active until it is swept or, if clean-break retirement is enabled, until a high level has high > level AND close >= level or a low level has low < level AND close <= level. A rejected breach that fails volume confirmation leaves the level active. Retired levels are retained or removed according to the retention option.
Long: Low < any active pivot-low level AND close > that level AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > configurable multiplier × volume SMA, with an unavailable SMA treated as zero).
Short: High > any active pivot-high level AND close < that level AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > configurable multiplier × volume SMA, with an unavailable SMA treated as zero).
