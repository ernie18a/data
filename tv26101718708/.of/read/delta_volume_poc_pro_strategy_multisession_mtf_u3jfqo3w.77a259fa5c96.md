Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of chart-timeframe closes over a long lookback
Long: On the configured signal timeframe’s current bar (same as chart by default; if higher, use the still-forming bar; if lower, use the last lower-timeframe bar mapped to the chart bar), low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close > chart-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is within an enabled session)
Short: On the configured signal timeframe’s current bar (same as chart by default; if higher, use the still-forming bar; if lower, use the last lower-timeframe bar mapped to the chart bar), high > highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close < chart-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is within an enabled session)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR (trailing stop enabled AND highest high since trailing activation >= entry price × (1 + trailing activation fraction) AND low <= highest high since activation − entry price × trailing offset fraction)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR (trailing stop enabled AND lowest low since trailing activation <= entry price × (1 − trailing activation fraction) AND high >= lowest low since activation + entry price × trailing offset fraction)
