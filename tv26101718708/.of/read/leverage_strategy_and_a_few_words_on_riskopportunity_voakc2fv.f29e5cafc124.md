Short: close crosses below the low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND datetime is within the allowed trading date range AND account equity is above the margin threshold

Exit: Short, close crosses above the high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar OR (the downward breakout is more recent than the upward breakout AND risk management is enabled AND (high crosses above entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low crosses below entry price minus take-profit distance)) OR account equity is at or below the margin threshold
