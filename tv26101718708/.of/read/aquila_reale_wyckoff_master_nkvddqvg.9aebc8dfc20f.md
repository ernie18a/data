Calculation: Range high and range low = maximum of the most recent confirmed pivot highs and minimum of the most recent confirmed pivot lows, respectively; retain a fixed number of each. Until a pivot of a side exists, use that side's highest high or lowest low over the range lookback. If range high <= range low, use the lookback high and low for both. Pivots are confirmed only after the specified number of bars to their right. Range size = range high - range low; range midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = higher-timeframe close and its shorter- and longer-period EMAs, with lookahead off: use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, becoming available on its final chart bar; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Volume ratio = average volume of bullish bars divided by average volume of bearish bars over the range lookback, with either average set to zero when its bar count is zero and a small positive constant added to the denominator. Bullish means close > open; bearish means close < open.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low + a small positive constant). High volume and low volume mean volume respectively above and below separate multiples of the volume SMA; the high-volume multiple is greater. A bearish or bullish climax exists when a bar in a separate recent lookback has the corresponding candle direction and volume above a higher climax multiple of its own volume SMA.
Calculation: Accumulation trend score = one point each for higher-timeframe close < shorter EMA, close < longer EMA, and shorter EMA < longer EMA. Distribution trend score = one point for each opposite comparison.
Calculation: Accumulation structure score = two points if the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding pivot low, plus one if the latest confirmed pivot high > the preceding pivot high. Distribution structure score = one point if the latest pivot low < the preceding pivot low, plus two if the latest pivot high < the preceding pivot high. A comparison contributes zero until both pivots exist.
Calculation: Accumulation volume score = two points if volume ratio > an upper threshold, otherwise one if > a lower threshold, plus two if a bearish climax exists AND close < range low + a range-size fraction. Distribution volume score = two points if volume ratio < a lower threshold, otherwise one if < an upper threshold, plus two if a bullish climax exists AND close > range high - the same range-size fraction.
Calculation: Accumulation VSA score = one point for each of: bearish AND high volume AND body fraction > a threshold AND close > low + half of (high - low + a small positive constant) AND close < range midpoint; bearish AND low volume AND body fraction < a smaller threshold AND close < range low + the volume-score range fraction; low < range low + a smaller range-size fraction AND low > range low AND low volume; bullish AND high volume AND body fraction > a larger threshold AND close > range midpoint.
Calculation: Distribution VSA score = one point for each of: bullish AND low volume AND body fraction < the small VSA threshold AND close > range high - the volume-score range fraction; high > range high - the smaller VSA range fraction AND high < range high AND low volume; bearish AND high volume AND body fraction > the large VSA threshold AND close < range midpoint.
Calculation: Price position = (close - range low) / (range size + a small positive constant). Accumulation position score = two points if price position < a lower threshold, otherwise one if < a higher threshold. Distribution position score = two points if price position > a higher threshold, otherwise one if > a lower threshold.
Calculation: Accumulation total and distribution total = sums of their respective trend, structure, volume, VSA, and position scores.
Long: Accumulation trend score > distribution trend score OR accumulation structure score > distribution structure score OR accumulation volume score > distribution volume score OR accumulation VSA score > distribution VSA score OR accumulation position score > distribution position score OR accumulation total > distribution total.
Short: Distribution trend score > accumulation trend score OR distribution structure score > accumulation structure score OR distribution volume score > accumulation volume score OR distribution VSA score > accumulation VSA score OR distribution position score > accumulation position score OR distribution total > accumulation total.
Long: Accumulation total >= mature threshold AND accumulation total > distribution total + required score lead.
Short: Distribution total >= mature threshold AND distribution total > accumulation total + required score lead.
Long: Accumulation total >= weaker threshold AND accumulation total > distribution total.
Short: Distribution total >= weaker threshold AND distribution total > accumulation total.
Long: Accumulation total = distribution total AND close < range midpoint AND forced display is enabled AND automatic direction is selected.
Short: Accumulation total = distribution total AND close > range midpoint AND forced display is enabled AND automatic direction is selected.
