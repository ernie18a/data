Calculation: Accumulation score = HTF trend points (1 each if higher-timeframe last completed close < fast EMA, close < slow EMA, and fast EMA < slow EMA; fast EMA has shorter lookback) + structure points (2 if latest confirmed pivot low > preceding pivot low; 1 if latest confirmed pivot high > preceding pivot high) + volume points (2 if average volume on up bars / average volume on down bars over the range lookback > 1.2, else 1 if > 1.05; add 2 if any of the latest 11 bars has down-bar volume > climax multiple × volume moving average and current close is in the bottom 30% of the range) + VSA points (1 each for: down candle, high volume, body/range > 0.5, close in upper half of candle, and close below range midpoint; down candle, low volume, body/range < 0.4, and close in bottom 30% of range; low-volume test with high in top 5% below range high) + position points (2 if close is in bottom 25% of range, else 1 if below 40%). Range high/low are the highest/lowest of the latest confirmed pivot highs/lows, using up to five of each; if unavailable, use the range lookback highest high/lowest low. If range size is nonpositive, use the lookback extremes.
Calculation: Distribution score = HTF trend points (1 each if higher-timeframe last completed close > fast EMA, close > slow EMA, and fast EMA > slow EMA; fast EMA has shorter lookback) + structure points (2 if latest confirmed pivot high < preceding confirmed pivot high; 1 if latest confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low) + volume points (2 if average volume on up bars / average volume on down bars over the range lookback < 0.83, else 1 if < 0.95; add 2 if any of the latest 11 bars has up-bar volume > climax multiple × volume moving average and current close is in the top 30% of the range) + VSA points (1 each for: up candle, high volume, body/range < 0.4, and close in top 30% of range; low-volume test with low in bottom 5% above range low; up candle, high volume, body/range > 0.7, and close above range midpoint) + position points (2 if close is in top 25% of range, else 1 if above 60%). High/low volume means volume above/below its moving average times the respective high/low volume threshold. The volume moving average and volume averages use the chart timeframe.
Long: accumulation score >= trade threshold AND accumulation score > distribution score + 2 OR accumulation score >= weak threshold AND accumulation score > distribution score OR forced direction is LONG OR direction is AUTO AND (accumulation detected OR (distribution not detected AND accumulation score > distribution score) OR force-show enabled AND close < range midpoint AND not auto-short). Accumulation detected means accumulation score >= trade threshold AND accumulation score > distribution score + 2; auto-short means distribution detected OR (accumulation not detected AND distribution score > accumulation score).
Short: distribution score >= trade threshold AND distribution score > accumulation score + 2 OR distribution score >= weak threshold AND distribution score > accumulation score OR forced direction is SHORT OR direction is AUTO AND (distribution detected OR (accumulation not detected AND distribution score > accumulation score) OR force-show enabled AND close > range midpoint AND not auto-long). Distribution detected means distribution score >= trade threshold AND distribution score > accumulation score + 2; auto-long means accumulation detected OR (distribution not detected AND accumulation score > distribution score).
