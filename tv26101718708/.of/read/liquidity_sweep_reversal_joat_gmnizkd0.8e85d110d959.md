Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured lookback bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars on the right complete. Keep the latest configured number of levels per side; discard levels older than the configured maximum age and remove a level when swept.
Calculation: Bullish sweep/reclaim = select the highest active swing low below the bar’s low; require low < level, high >= level, and level − low within the configured minimum and maximum ATR depth (a maximum of zero disables the cap). A close above the level reclaims it on that bar; otherwise, if the reclaim window is positive, retain the level and the lowest low reached, and signal if a later close above the level occurs within the window.
Calculation: Bearish sweep/reclaim = select the lowest active swing high above the bar’s high; require high > level, low <= level, and high − level within the configured minimum and maximum ATR depth (a maximum of zero disables the cap). A close below the level reclaims it on that bar; otherwise, if the reclaim window is positive, retain the level and the highest high reached, and signal if a later close below the level occurs within the window.
Calculation: Volume-spike filter = current volume >= its simple moving average over the configured lookback × configured multiplier; pass if volume or its average is unavailable or the average is <= 0. Apply only when enabled.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Apply the trend filter only when enabled.
Calculation: Stop and targets = entry is the reclaim bar close. Raw long stop = lowest low of the bullish sweep/reclaim − ATR buffer; raw short stop = highest high of the bearish sweep/reclaim + ATR buffer. Risk = clamp the absolute entry-to-raw-stop distance between the configured minimum-risk and maximum-risk ATR multiples. Long stop = entry − risk; short stop = entry + risk. Long targets = entry + risk × each configured target multiple; short targets = entry − risk × each configured target multiple. Target multiples are ordered TP1 < TP2 < TP3.
Long: bullish sweep/reclaim AND ATR is available AND (volume-spike filter is disabled OR volume-spike filter passes) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA is unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND long direction is allowed AND a bearish sweep/reclaim does not also occur on this bar AND cooldown bars since the last entry >= configured cooldown AND no trade is active AND no trade exits on this bar.
Short: bearish sweep/reclaim AND ATR is available AND (volume-spike filter is disabled OR volume-spike filter passes) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA is unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND short direction is allowed AND a bullish sweep/reclaim does not also occur on this bar AND cooldown bars since the last entry >= configured cooldown AND no trade is active AND no trade exits on this bar.
Exit: Long, high >= TP3 OR low <= stop OR bars since entry >= configured timeout bars, resolving same-bar stop/target hits according to the configured priority; if a target stage (TP1 or TP2) was reached before a stop, close at the highest reached target unless TP3 is reached.
Exit: Short, low <= TP3 OR high >= stop OR bars since entry >= configured timeout bars, resolving same-bar stop/target hits according to the configured priority; if a target stage (TP1 or TP2) was reached before a stop, close at the highest reached target unless TP3 is reached.
