Calculation: Tracked levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows with the same number of bars on each side; add each level on its confirmation bar, retain at most a configurable number per side by removing the oldest, and remove levels when their age from the pivot bar exceeds a configurable limit.
Calculation: Reclaim = process each side independently on every bar, including while a trade is active. If a level is pending, update its extreme with the current low for a low level or high for a high level; confirm its reclaim if close is strictly above the low level or strictly below the high level. Otherwise, expire it when its age since the sweep is at least the reclaim window. If no level remains pending and no reclaim was confirmed on this bar, select the highest tracked low strictly above the current low, or the lowest tracked high strictly below the current high. Test only that selected level: penetration must be at least a configurable multiple of the current ATR and, unless the maximum is disabled, at most another configurable multiple of the current ATR; the bar's high must reach the low level or its low must reach the high level. If these tests pass, remove the level from tracking. Confirm the reclaim immediately if close is strictly back across the level; otherwise hold it pending if the reclaim window is greater than zero. A pending reclaim can confirm through the final bar of the window; its extreme includes every bar from the sweep through confirmation. A consumed level stays removed even if the reclaim expires or a signal is filtered out.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of close on the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar with historical lookahead disabled.
Calculation: Risk = at entry, clamp the absolute distance from entry price to the reclaimed sweep extreme minus the ATR buffer for a long or plus the ATR buffer for a short between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of entry-bar ATR. The stop is entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short; the final target is entry price plus a configurable risk multiple for a long or minus it for a short. Keep these levels fixed until exit.
Long: A low-level reclaim is confirmed AND ATR is available AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume or its SMA is unavailable OR volume SMA <= 0 OR current volume >= its SMA times the configured multiple) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe trend) AND long direction is enabled AND (dual-side blocking is disabled OR no high-level reclaim is confirmed on this bar) AND the number of bars since the last actual entry is at least the cooldown or no entry has occurred AND the corresponding short conditions through the cooldown filter are not simultaneously satisfied AND no trade is active AND no trade exited on this bar.
Short: A high-level reclaim is confirmed AND ATR is available AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume or its SMA is unavailable OR volume SMA <= 0 OR current volume >= its SMA times the configured multiple) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe trend) AND short direction is enabled AND (dual-side blocking is disabled OR no low-level reclaim is confirmed on this bar) AND the number of bars since the last actual entry is at least the cooldown or no entry has occurred AND the corresponding long conditions through the cooldown filter are not simultaneously satisfied AND no trade is active AND no trade exited on this bar.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target OR bars since entry >= trade timeout).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target OR bars since entry >= trade timeout).
