Calculation: LVN zone = split the price range of the current session (volume profile over the chart-timeframe bars since the session start) into equal-height price bins; allocate each bar's volume to the bins its high-low range overlaps, proportional to overlap; a bin is a low-volume node if its volume is above a tiny minimum fraction of the maximum bin volume AND strictly lower than every bin within N bins above and N bins below; adjacent low-volume bins merge into one zone, which is widened by a few bins on each side; the top and bottom edges of the profile range also count as zones. A zone is support if its midpoint is below the current close, resistance if above.
Calculation: average volume = simple moving average of volume over a lookback
Long: (support zone AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top) OR (resistance zone AND previous close <= zone top AND close > zone top AND volume > average volume)
Short: (resistance zone AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom) OR (support zone AND previous close >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND volume > average volume)
