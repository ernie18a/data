Calculation: Asia range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured Asia session in America/New_York, including both endpoints; reset at the start of each Asia session.
Calculation: Daily bias = the direction of the first confirmed chart bar after the Asia session completes and within the bias scan window whose close is outside the Asia range, provided the optional range-size gate passes; reset at the next Asia session. The scan starts at the end of the Asia session or at a configured later time and ends at a configured time, inclusive. The range-size gate passes when the Asia high minus the Asia low is between its configured minimum and maximum, inclusive.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within a configured London opening window in America/New_York, including its start and excluding its end; reset at the next Asia session.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = at the daily-bias bar, each enabled higher timeframe's last completed close is above its EMA for a bullish bias, or less than or equal to its EMA for a bearish bias. The two higher timeframes use the same EMA length; one is longer than the other. Preserve this result until the next Asia session.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish zone from the high two chart bars ago to the current low when current low minus that high exceeds the minimum gap size, or a bearish zone from the current high to the low two chart bars ago when that low minus current high exceeds the minimum gap size. The minimum may be an absolute amount or a percentage of current close. A gap is mitigated only when its age exceeds one bar and, according to the selected mode, the bar overlaps the zone, or its low for a bullish gap or high for a bearish gap reaches the midpoint, or its low for a bullish gap reaches the bottom or its high for a bearish gap reaches the top. Remove gaps after the configured maximum age.
Calculation: Balanced price range = the overlap of a newly formed fair value gap and an opposite-direction unmitigated gap, or, if no eligible live match is found, a recently mitigated opposite-direction gap retained in a bounded, age-limited memory. The overlap size must be at least the configured minimum, expressed as an absolute amount or a percentage of current close. Search newest eligible gaps first, create at most one range per new gap, reject an overlap that intersects an existing unmitigated balanced price range, and consume a matched memory gap. A range's direction is that of the new gap. After its age exceeds two bars, mitigate a bullish range when close is below its bottom or a bearish range when close is above its top; remove it after the configured maximum age.

Short: A bearish fair value gap forms on a confirmed bar.

Short: Daily bias = bearish AND a bearish fair value gap was unmitigated at the start of the bar AND its age > one bar AND high <= its top AND high >= its bottom multiplied by a configured factor below one.
