Calculation: Timeframe score = For each of five ordered timeframes, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the last lower-timeframe bar completed within the chart bar, or the chart bar when the timeframes match. On that timeframe, regress log(hlc3) over a lookback; divide the difference between log(high) for close >= open, otherwise log(low), and the regression endpoint by the rolling standard deviation of log(hlc3) minus its own regression endpoint, floored at a small positive value. Clamp this z-score symmetrically, apply a sigmoid scaled to the oscillator range, average it with RSI of close, and clamp the result to that range. Use progressively shorter regression lookbacks and longer RSI lookbacks as the timeframes increase.
Calculation: Raw score = Weighted average of available timeframe scores, renormalizing their weights when a score is unavailable. The two middle-longer timeframes have equal highest weights; the shortest and longest have equal lowest weights; the remaining timeframe has an intermediate weight.
Calculation: Kinetic score = On chart bars, calculate volume delta as volume * ((close - low) - (high - close)) / max(high - low, a small positive value). Standardize it using its rolling SMA and standard deviation, with the deviation floored at a small positive value; multiply by a positive factor and clamp symmetrically to obtain the volume bonus. Calculate volume z-score similarly over a longer lookback. When volume z-score >= its threshold, set the climax adjustment to a positive fraction of (raw score - oscillator midpoint); otherwise set it to zero. Calculate volatility as (rolling highest high - rolling lowest low) / close, or zero if close <= zero; normalize it to the oscillator range using its rolling minimum and maximum over a longer lookback, or use the midpoint when they are equal. Set the squeeze factor to a positive fraction below one when normalized volatility <= its threshold, otherwise one. Add raw score, volume bonus, and climax adjustment, replacing unavailable components with zero; subtract the oscillator midpoint, multiply by the squeeze factor, add the midpoint, clamp to the oscillator range, and apply a short WMA.
Long: Kinetic score < oscillator midpoint
Short: Kinetic score >= oscillator midpoint
