Calculation: SwingHigh/SwingLow = most recent confirmed pivot: the high (low) of the bar N bars ago, once it is the highest high (lowest low) of the 2N+1 bars ending at the current bar; Mid = (SwingHigh + SwingLow)/2
Calculation: TrendEMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: BullZone = on a bar (only when flat or no zone exists) where close[1] < open[1] AND high[1] < low[3] AND close > SwingLow AND lowest low of the last M bars including the current bar < low - tol*close AND close < Mid, set zoneHigh = high[1], zoneLow = low[1] (replaces any existing zone). The zone stays active until close < zoneLow, which deletes it
Calculation: BearZone = on a bar (only when flat or no zone exists) where close[1] > open[1] AND low[1] > high[3] AND close < SwingHigh AND highest high of the last M bars including the current bar > high + tol*close AND close > Mid, set zoneHigh = high[1], zoneLow = low[1] (replaces any existing zone). The zone stays active until close > zoneHigh, which deletes it
Long: flat AND BullZone active AND low <= zoneHigh + small buffer proportional to close AND close > TrendEMA (the signal bar's close is the entry reference; if the TrendEMA check fails the signal is dropped for that bar)
Short: flat AND BearZone active AND high >= zoneLow - small buffer proportional to close AND close < TrendEMA (the signal bar's close is the entry reference; if the TrendEMA check fails the signal is dropped for that bar)
Exit: long, low <= zoneLow at entry (stop) OR high >= entry + R*(entry - zoneLow at entry) (target, R is a fixed reward-to-risk multiple)
Exit: short, high >= zoneHigh at entry (stop) OR low <= entry - R*(zoneHigh at entry - entry) (target, R is a fixed reward-to-risk multiple)
