Calculation: Rolling volume profile levels = Over a lookback window, divide the range from its lowest low to highest high into price bins; distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the bins spanned by its high–low range. Calculate levels on the chart timeframe or an optional higher timeframe, using that timeframe’s current bar while it is forming.
Calculation: POC = midpoint of the bin with the greatest accumulated volume.
Calculation: VAH and VAL = upper and lower boundaries of the value area, expanded from the POC by adding the larger adjacent bin at each step until the target share of total volume is covered.
Calculation: HVP levels = Up to six highest-volume bins that are local maxima (volume greater than the lower neighbor and at least the upper neighbor) and exceed average bin volume times a threshold.
Calculation: LVP levels = Up to six lowest-volume bins that are local minima (volume less than the lower neighbor and at most the upper neighbor) and are below average bin volume times a threshold.
Long: Close > POC AND previous close <= POC OR Close > VAH AND previous close <= VAH OR Close > VAL AND previous close <= VAL OR Close crosses above any HVP or LVP level OR, within a configurable bar window after an upward close cross of POC, VAH, or VAL, low <= that level AND high >= that level AND close > that level.
Short: Close < POC AND previous close >= POC OR Close < VAH AND previous close >= VAH OR Close < VAL AND previous close >= VAL OR Close crosses below any HVP or LVP level OR, within a configurable bar window after a downward close cross of POC, VAH, or VAL, low <= that level AND high >= that level AND close < that level.
