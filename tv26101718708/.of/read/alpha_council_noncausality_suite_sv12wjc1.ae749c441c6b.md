Calculation: Upper/lower Whittaker bands = asymmetric least-squares smooths of bar highs/lows, using longer smoothing than the inner body-top/body-bottom bands
Calculation: Polynomial regression channel = least-squares polynomial fit to closes over a rolling lookback, with outer bands at a multiple of the fit residual standard deviation
Calculation: NW envelope = Gaussian-kernel regression of closes, plus/minus a multiple of the Gaussian-kernel regression of absolute close-to-regression deviations
Calculation: WESO = normalized close position within the four Whittaker bands, multiplied by 0.3 + 0.7 × the inner-band width / outer-band width, then EMA-smoothed
Calculation: Lead sine = sine of the Hilbert phase plus 45 degrees; Hilbert phase uses the NW regression, the midpoint of the four Whittaker bands, and the NW regression two bars earlier
Calculation: Bullish/bearish pulse = capped recursive count of bullish/bearish candles closing in the top/bottom pulse-conviction portion of their range; otherwise the prior pulse decays by a factor
Calculation: Extreme = high reaches the upper outer Whittaker band OR (if enabled) the upper NW envelope OR (if enabled) the upper polynomial regression band; for a lower extreme, use low and the corresponding lower bands
Calculation: ACV clearance, short = an upper extreme has occurred since close last fell below the 24-bar close mean, and there is no bullish cascade (ATR(3)/ATR(50) > 1.5 AND HP-filter slope > 0), no NW slope or acceleration exceeding its historical maximum times the bound multiplier, no pulse lockdown dominated by bullish pulse, and no positive ATR(3) second difference unless absorption is present (volume > 1.5 × 50-bar average volume AND candle body/range < 0.3); also require close < prior bar open (or prior low in strict mode), true range <= 1.5 × prior true range, and, if the upper extreme lasted beyond the time limit, the close below prior open (or prior low in strict mode)
Calculation: ACV clearance, long = a lower extreme has occurred since close last rose above the 24-bar close mean, and there is no bearish cascade (ATR(3)/ATR(50) > 1.5 AND HP-filter slope < 0), no NW slope or acceleration exceeding its historical maximum times the bound multiplier, no pulse lockdown dominated by bearish pulse, and no positive ATR(3) second difference unless absorption is present; also require close > prior bar open (or prior high in strict mode), true range <= 1.5 × prior true range, and, if the lower extreme lasted beyond the time limit, the close above prior open (or prior high in strict mode)
Long: low reaches the lower outer Whittaker band OR enabled lower NW envelope OR enabled lower polynomial regression band, AND an enabled London-session sweep of the Asia session low (low < Asia low AND close > Asia low) for the AMD marker; OR WESO was <= -0.5 and the next value is higher while the lower outer Whittaker band rises or WESO rises; OR sine was <= lead sine and is now > lead sine; OR lead sine crosses above 0.95; OR ACV clearance, long AND a bullish candle AND any enabled long trigger: CVD, OBV, MACD, or Aroon institutional consensus reaches 75% of valid volume-anomaly anchors (at least one valid anchor); RSI(24) relative to those anchors crosses from below -15 to >= -15; Stoch RSI(24) %K crosses above %D while %K < 20; or, if VWAP trigger is enabled, after arming, close crosses above the cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 since arming
