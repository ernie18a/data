Calculation: Evaluation window = the rolling chart-bar window ending at the current bar. Recalculate all fitted values across that window on each bar; values for earlier bars within it can change.
Calculation: Asymmetric Whittaker bounds = separately fit highs, candle body tops, candle body bottoms, and lows over the evaluation window by iterative weighted least squares with a second-difference smoothness penalty. Start with unit weights; after each fit use weight p where the observed value exceeds its fit and 1-p otherwise. Use an upper-tail p for highs and body tops and a lower-tail p for body bottoms.
Calculation: Normalized envelope position = clamp((close - mean of the four Whittaker bounds) / ((upper high bound - lower low bound) / 2), -1, 1) multiplied by a positive affine function of clamp((upper body bound - lower body bound) / (upper high bound - lower low bound), 0, 1).
Calculation: Gaussian kernel = normalized Gaussian-weighted mean of closes within a bandwidth-dependent radius on either side of each point in the evaluation window. Its envelope is the kernel plus or minus a multiplier times the same Gaussian smoothing of absolute close-to-kernel deviations.
Calculation: Polynomial channel = polynomial least-squares fit to the most recent, shorter window of closes, plus or minus a multiplier times the root mean squared fit residual.
Calculation: Upper extreme = high at or above the upper high Whittaker bound, enabled upper Gaussian envelope, or enabled upper polynomial channel. Lower extreme = low at or below the lower low Whittaker bound, enabled lower Gaussian envelope, or enabled lower polynomial channel.
Calculation: Reversal phase = atan2 of a multiplier times the difference between the Gaussian kernel at the current and an earlier bar, and the difference between the current kernel and mean of the four current Whittaker bounds. Phase sine = sine of this phase; lead sine = sine of the phase plus a phase offset.
Calculation: Signed candle volume = cumulative volume added on close above open, subtracted on close below open, and unchanged otherwise.
Calculation: Anomaly anchors = a fixed-length queue of values captured when the volume z-score exceeds a positive threshold. An anchor is valid when its signed candle volume is nonzero. For each valid anchor, compare the current indicator with its value at that anchor; consensus requires at least one valid anchor and a sufficient fraction of valid anchors to vote.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative typical-price times volume divided by cumulative volume, starting anew whenever the corresponding armed condition changes from false to true. Use the chart timeframe or the last completed bar of the selected higher or lower timeframe for its close, typical price, and volume.
Calculation: Pulse = separate bullish and bearish running values: increment the corresponding value up to a cap when the candle has the corresponding direction and closes in its extreme portion of the range; otherwise multiply it by a decay factor. Lock when the larger pulse divided by the cap reaches an upper threshold and unlock when it falls to a lower threshold.
Calculation: Kernel lockdown = absolute one-bar Gaussian-kernel slope or absolute change in that slope exceeds a multiplier times its respective maximum over the older portion of the evaluation window.
Calculation: Armed short state = an upper extreme has occurred since the last close below the close SMA, the short-ATR to long-ATR ratio is not above its threshold with a positive Hodrick-Prescott trend slope, kernel lockdown is false, the pulse is not locked with bullish pulse dominant, and ATR acceleration is not positive unless enabled absorption is present. Armed long state = the symmetric lower-extreme condition, reset by close above the SMA, with negative trend slope and bearish pulse dominance. ATR acceleration is the second difference of the short ATR; absorption means volume exceeds a multiple of its longer SMA and candle body divided by range is below a threshold. Recompute these states sequentially from the oldest bar in each evaluation window.
