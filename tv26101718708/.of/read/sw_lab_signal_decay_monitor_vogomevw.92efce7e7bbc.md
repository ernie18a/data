Long: after at least max(ATR slow lookback, twice trend SMA lookback) bars AND (fast SMA crosses above slow SMA OR RSI crosses above its oversold level OR MACD line crosses above its signal line OR close <= lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < its lower threshold OR close > the previous bar’s highest high over the breakout lookback AND volume > its moving average × the volume threshold OR price z-score < the negative deviation threshold)
