Calculation: Screener values = calculate gap, first-bar range, inside-range and breakout comparisons on the selected screener timeframe, then merge them onto chart bars; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe and the last intrabar for a lower timeframe. References to previous screener values are offsets in chart bars after merging.
Calculation: First-bar range = high and low of the screener timeframe bar at the start of the current day.
Long: Chart bar is the first of the day AND screener low > previous screener bar high.
Short: Chart bar is the first of the day AND screener high < previous screener bar low.
Long: Chart bar is the third or fourth of the day AND the first chart bar's screener low > its previous screener bar high OR the first chart bar's screener high < its previous screener bar low, AND every intervening chart bar's screener high < first-bar range high AND screener low > first-bar range low, AND current screener close > first-bar range high.
Short: Chart bar is the third of the day OR chart bar is the fourth of the day AND the instrument occupies one of the first two screener slots, AND the first chart bar's screener low > its previous screener bar high OR the first chart bar's screener high < its previous screener bar low, AND every intervening chart bar's screener high < first-bar range high AND screener low > first-bar range low, AND current screener close < first-bar range low.
