Calculation: Effective volume = volume, or 1 when volume is missing or zero.
Calculation: Buy and sell volume = choose one method: (1) sum effective volume separately over lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar whose close is above or below their open, splitting unchanged bars equally; use the lower-timeframe bars available in the forming chart bar; if there are none, use method 3; (2) multiply effective volume by the standard normal CDF of close change divided by the rolling standard deviation of close changes for buy volume, and by one minus that probability for sell volume; use a standardized change of zero if the standard deviation is not positive; (3) multiply effective volume by (close − low)/(high − low) for buy volume and by (high − close)/(high − low) for sell volume; split equally when high equals low.
Calculation: Raw flow = buy volume − sell volume; normalized flow = raw flow / (buy volume + sell volume), or zero if the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Standardized flow = choose either the rolling z-score of raw flow or (raw flow − rolling median of raw flow) / (MAD scaling factor × rolling median of the series absolute value of raw flow minus its contemporaneous rolling median); use zero when the relevant denominator is too small, and clip the result to symmetric upper and lower bounds.
Calculation: Buy intensity and sell intensity = initialize both to zero; on each bar multiply each previous intensity by an exponential decay factor and add an excitation factor times, respectively, normalized flow when it exceeds a positive event threshold or minus normalized flow when it is below the negative event threshold; otherwise add zero.
Calculation: Cascade strength = rolling percentile rank of buy intensity + sell intensity, substituting the midpoint of the percentile scale when unavailable.
Calculation: Buy cascade = buy intensity > sell intensity AND cascade strength > cascade threshold AND standardized flow > positive flow threshold. Sell cascade = sell intensity > buy intensity AND cascade strength > cascade threshold AND standardized flow < negative flow threshold. These conditions use only the chart instrument; cross-asset confirmation is disabled.
Calculation: Consensus = mean of short-, medium-, and long-horizon EMAs of standardized flow. Buy pressure = midpoint of the pressure scale × (1 + scaled standardized flow / (1 + absolute value of scaled standardized flow)); sell pressure = maximum of the pressure scale − buy pressure.
Calculation: Flow bias = positive if consensus > positive consensus threshold OR buy pressure ≥ pressure threshold; otherwise negative if consensus < negative consensus threshold OR sell pressure ≥ pressure threshold; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Expected move = raw flow × maximum of zero and the rolling regression slope of close change on raw flow; use zero slope when the flow standard deviation is zero or correlation is unavailable.
Calculation: Relative range = true range / ATR, or one if ATR is not positive. Relative volume = effective volume / its rolling SMA, or one if the SMA is not positive. Liquidity stress = mean of the rolling percentile ranks of: rolling sum of absolute raw flow divided by rolling sum of total buy and sell volume; relative range divided by relative volume floored at a positive value; and absolute close return divided by close × effective volume. Use zero for either ratio with a nonpositive denominator and the percentile midpoint for an unavailable rank.
Calculation: Regime scores = trend: weighted absolute consensus + alignment bonus when all three consensus EMAs have the same nonzero sign + weighted absolute normalized intensity difference when its sign agrees with consensus + weighted capped absolute expected move / ATR; squeeze: squeeze bonus when Bollinger width < Keltner width + weighted positive part of a range threshold − relative range; blast: weighted cascade strength + release bonus when squeeze is absent now but present a fixed number of bars ago + weighted absolute normalized intensity difference; reversion: divergence bonus when the sign of close change over a lookback differs from consensus and absolute consensus exceeds a threshold + weighted absolute (close − session VWAP)/ATR + weighted positive RSI extremity; shock: weighted liquidity stress + weighted positive part of relative range − a range threshold. Normalized intensity difference is (buy intensity − sell intensity)/(buy intensity + sell intensity), or zero when the sum is not positive. RSI extremity is the greater of RSI minus its upper extreme and its lower extreme minus RSI, divided by an RSI scaling factor. Use zero for ATR-normalized terms when ATR is not positive. The dominant regime has the highest score, with ties resolved in the order trend, squeeze, blast, reversion, shock.
Calculation: Volume measure = volume, or optionally volume divided by the current exponentially updated volume average for its time-of-day slot; initialize each slot average with its first available volume and use a ratio of one when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.

Short: Sell cascade is true AND sell cascade was false on the previous bar.
Long: Standardized flow ≥ 0.
Short: Standardized flow < 0.
Long: Buy intensity > sell intensity.
Short: Sell intensity > buy intensity.

Short: Expected move < 0.

Short: Buy cascade is false AND sell cascade is false AND dominant regime is reversion AND flow bias > 0.

Short: Volume > 0 AND high ≥ the highest high over the preceding extreme lookback AND (close − low)/(high − low) ≤ lower close-location threshold AND rolling percentile rank of volume measure ≥ volume threshold; use the midpoint for close location when high equals low.
Long: Volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND rolling percentile rank of high − low ≥ spread threshold AND rolling percentile rank of volume measure ≥ high volume threshold AND (close − low)/(high − low) ≥ stopping close-location threshold; use the midpoint for close location when high equals low.
