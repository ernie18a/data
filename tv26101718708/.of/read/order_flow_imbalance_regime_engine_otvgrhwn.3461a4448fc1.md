Calculation: Signed order-flow imbalance = buy volume − sell volume. Split volume by the selected method: lower-timeframe tick rule (buy when a lower-timeframe bar closes above its open, sell when below, split evenly when equal; use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including a still-forming one if present); bulk classification (split by the normal CDF of the chart-bar price change divided by its rolling standard deviation); or candle geometry (split by the close’s position in the chart-bar range). If the split is empty, use candle geometry. Replace missing or zero volume with 1.
Calculation: Standardized imbalance = signed order-flow imbalance standardized over its lookback using either median and 1.4826 × median absolute deviation, or mean and standard deviation, then clipped to [−4, 4].
Calculation: Cascade direction = buy excitation − sell excitation; each excitation is its prior value times exp(−decay rate), plus excitation gain times the positive buy or sell imbalance when its absolute value exceeds the event threshold. Cascade strength = percentile rank of the sum of both excitations over its rolling window.
Calculation: Cross-asset confirmation = the sign of the prior completed bar’s standardized geometry imbalance on the selected correlated instrument and the current standardized imbalance agree, and the prior value’s absolute value exceeds 0.5. The reference uses the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar.
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when the range is zero. Range percentile and volume percentile = current chart-bar range and selected volume measure’s respective percentile ranks over their rolling lookbacks. Selected volume measure is raw volume, or the time-of-day-adjusted volume measure when enabled.
Long: First bar where cascade direction > 0 AND cascade strength > 60 AND standardized imbalance > 0.5 AND (cross-asset confirmation is disabled OR cross-asset confirmation is true); evaluate on the confirmed chart bar when signal confirmation is enabled. OR volume > 0 AND low <= the lowest low of prior bars over the new-extreme lookback AND close position >= 0.65 AND volume percentile >= 60. OR volume > 0 AND close < prior close AND range percentile >= 60 AND volume percentile >= 80 AND close position >= 0.45. OR volume > 0 AND close < prior close AND selected volume measure >= its highest value over the rolling lookback AND range percentile >= 70.
Short: First bar where cascade direction < 0 AND cascade strength > 60 AND standardized imbalance < −0.5 AND (cross-asset confirmation is disabled OR cross-asset confirmation is true); evaluate on the confirmed chart bar when signal confirmation is enabled. OR volume > 0 AND high >= the highest high of prior bars over the new-extreme lookback AND close position <= 0.35 AND volume percentile >= 60. OR volume > 0 AND close > prior close AND selected volume measure >= its highest value over the rolling lookback AND range percentile >= 70.
