Long: On a newly completed higher-timeframe or chart-timeframe bar, its low > the high of the first of the three most recently completed bars AND, if displacement filtering is enabled, the middle bar's body-to-range ratio >= its threshold AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, the middle bar's range >= a multiple of ATR on the last completed pattern bar AND, if trend filtering is enabled, the last completed pattern bar's close > its EMA for aligned filtering or <= its EMA for countertrend filtering AND, if session filtering applies, the middle bar's opening time is within the configured session.
Short: On a newly completed higher-timeframe or chart-timeframe bar, its high < the low of the first of the three most recently completed bars AND, if displacement filtering is enabled, the middle bar's body-to-range ratio >= its threshold AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, the middle bar's range >= a multiple of ATR on the last completed pattern bar AND, if trend filtering is enabled, the last completed pattern bar's close <= its EMA for aligned filtering or > its EMA for countertrend filtering AND, if session filtering applies, the middle bar's opening time is within the configured session.
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low, or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Session filtering applies when enabled and the pattern timeframe is below daily.
