Calculation: Upper drift = average of the most recent N confirmed pivot highs, where a pivot high is confirmed using equal lookback on each side
Calculation: Lower drift = average of the most recent N confirmed pivot lows, where a pivot low is confirmed using equal lookback on each side
Long: Close crosses above upper drift (current close > current upper drift AND previous close <= previous upper drift)
Short: Close crosses below lower drift (current close < current lower drift AND previous close >= previous lower drift)
