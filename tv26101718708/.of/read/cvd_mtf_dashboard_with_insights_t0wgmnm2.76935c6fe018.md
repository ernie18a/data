Calculation: Estimated volume delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick size) − 1), calculated on each configured analysis timeframe; use the requested bar as returned (the current, still-forming bar when the analysis timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta (CVD) = cumulative sum of estimated volume delta.
Calculation: Buyer share = rounded percentage of the bar range below the close, clamped to 0–100; use minimum tick size when the bar range is zero.
Calculation: ADX = Wilder-smoothed directional movement index.
Calculation: Bullish CVD direction = CVD is strictly greater than each of its previous three bars; bearish CVD direction = CVD is strictly less than each of its previous three bars.
Calculation: Bullish CVD momentum = the EMA of CVD is strictly greater than each of its previous two bars; bearish CVD momentum = it is strictly less than each of its previous two bars. Use a longer smoothing length for CVD momentum than the short lookback used to assess CVD direction.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = close is above each of its previous divergence-lookback bars and CVD is not above each of its previous bars over the same lookback; bearish divergence = close is below each of its previous bars and CVD is not below each of its previous bars over that same lookback.
Calculation: Bull score = number of enabled analysis timeframes with bullish CVD direction; bear score = number with bearish CVD direction. Weighted bull and bear scores sum those same timeframe directions using increasing weights in configured timeframe order. Alignment score = the greater of bull and bear scores; dominant direction is whichever weighted score is greater.
Calculation: Average buyer share and average ADX = arithmetic means across enabled analysis timeframes. Fresh count = enabled timeframes whose most recent change into or out of bullish/bearish CVD direction occurred within the configured freshness limit. Build count = enabled timeframes with bullish CVD momentum or ADX at or above the balanced-regime threshold; slow count = enabled timeframes with bearish CVD momentum. Divergence count = enabled timeframes with either divergence.
Calculation: Strong bull trend = at least one enabled timeframe, weighted bull score at least 6, average ADX at least 22, and fresh count at least 2; strong bear trend uses weighted bear score instead. Active bull trend = at least one enabled timeframe, dominant bull direction, bull score at least min(2, enabled timeframe count), average ADX at least 18, and build count at least min(2, enabled timeframe count); active bear trend uses bear direction and score instead.
Calculation: Early bull expansion = the first two enabled analysis timeframes both have bullish CVD direction, bull direction is dominant, average ADX is at least 18, and build count is at least 2; early bear expansion uses bearish direction instead. Transition expansion = at least one enabled timeframe, average ADX at least 18, build count at least min(2, enabled timeframe count), fresh count at least 1, and alignment score at least min(2, enabled timeframe count).
Calculation: Bullish flow = buyer share at least 58% and bullish CVD direction; bearish flow = buyer share at most 42% and bearish CVD direction.
Long: bullish flow OR bullish divergence OR bull score >= (enabled timeframe count >= 3 ? 3 : enabled timeframe count == 2 ? 2 : 1) OR bull score < (enabled timeframe count >= 3 ? 3 : enabled timeframe count == 2 ? 2 : 1) AND the first two enabled analysis timeframes both have bullish CVD direction AND dominant direction is bullish OR strong bull trend OR active bull trend OR early bull expansion OR transition expansion AND dominant direction is bullish.
Short: bearish flow OR bearish divergence OR bear score >= (enabled timeframe count >= 3 ? 3 : enabled timeframe count == 2 ? 2 : 1) OR bear score < (enabled timeframe count >= 3 ? 3 : enabled timeframe count == 2 ? 2 : 1) AND the first two enabled analysis timeframes both have bearish CVD direction AND dominant direction is bearish OR strong bear trend OR active bear trend OR early bear expansion OR transition expansion AND dominant direction is bearish.
