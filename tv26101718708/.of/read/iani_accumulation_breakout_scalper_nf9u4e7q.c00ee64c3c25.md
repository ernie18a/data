Calculation: Confirmed pivots = Pivot highs and lows of either close or, respectively, high and low, using equal numbers of bars on each side; recognize each pivot only when the right-hand bars have completed.
Calculation: Resistance and support = Lines through the two latest confirmed pivot highs and the two latest confirmed pivot lows, respectively, extrapolated to the evaluated bar. For each line, count touches over a lookback from oldest to newest: count a bar when its selected pivot source is within a touch tolerance of the line at that bar and it is at least a minimum separation from the last counted touch.
Calculation: Accumulation = Close strictly between resistance and support AND each line has at least the minimum number of touches. Arm on accumulation and remain armed until either breakout. An upward breakout occurs when armed AND close crosses above resistance plus a breakout buffer; a downward breakout occurs when armed AND close crosses below support minus the buffer. Each breakout replaces any previous breakout state. The state expires when its age exceeds a maximum wait or its corresponding retest occurs. Update accumulation, breakout, expiration, and retest states in that order on each bar.
Calculation: Pattern pivots = Chronological confirmed pivots, processing a high before a low if both are confirmed on the same bar. Append a pivot of the opposite type; for consecutive pivots of the same type, replace the last pivot only if the new high is at least as high or the new low is at least as low.
Calculation: Pattern setup = On a change to the pattern pivots, examine their latest points. A head-and-shoulders setup has five alternating high-low-high-low-high pivots, shoulders within a tolerance, the middle high more than that tolerance above both shoulders, and at least a minimum number of bars between the first and last pivots. Its inverse has low-high-low-high-low pivots and the middle low more than the tolerance below both shoulders. A double top has three high-low-high pivots with the highs within the same tolerance and at least the minimum span; a double bottom has low-high-low pivots with the lows within tolerance and the same minimum span. Create a setup only when its last pivot's bar differs from the last created setup of that type; a new setup of the same type replaces the active one. Head-and-shoulders necklines extend the line through the second and fourth pivots; double-pattern necklines are horizontal at the second pivot. Each setup stays active through a maximum number of bars after its detection, until replaced or its neckline break occurs.
Long: Armed AND close crosses above resistance plus the breakout buffer.
Short: Armed AND close crosses below support minus the breakout buffer.
Long: Upward breakout state is active AND low <= resistance plus the retest tolerance AND close > resistance AND, if the optional EMA filter is enabled, the shorter close EMA > the longer close EMA OR close > the longer close EMA.
Short: Downward breakout state is active AND high >= support minus the retest tolerance AND close < support AND, if the optional EMA filter is enabled, the shorter close EMA < the longer close EMA OR close < the longer close EMA.
Long: Upward breakout state remains active after retest processing.
Short: Downward breakout state remains active after retest processing.
Long: A new inverse head-and-shoulders OR double-bottom setup is detected.
Short: A new head-and-shoulders OR double-top setup is detected.
Long: An inverse head-and-shoulders OR double-bottom setup is active AND the current bar is later than its last pivot AND close > its current neckline plus a neckline breakout buffer.
Short: A head-and-shoulders OR double-top setup is active AND the current bar is later than its last pivot AND close < its current neckline minus the neckline breakout buffer.
