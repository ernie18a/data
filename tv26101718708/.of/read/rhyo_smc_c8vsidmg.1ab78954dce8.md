Calculation: Structure pivots = Maintain separate swing and shorter-lookback internal legs, initially bearish. On each bar, if the high N bars ago exceeds every high in the subsequent N bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low N bars ago is below every low in the subsequent N bars, set the leg bullish. On a change to bearish, record that historical high as the latest high pivot; on a change to bullish, record that historical low as the latest low pivot. Each new high pivot resets its own crossed flag. For each scale, maintain a trend bias initially neutral; a qualifying upward or downward cross of its latest high pivot sets its crossed flag and its bias bullish or bearish, respectively.
Calculation: Equal-pivot events = Apply the structure-pivot leg procedure with a separate lookback. An equal-low event occurs when a newly confirmed low pivot differs from the preceding low pivot by less than a threshold times ATR; an equal-high event is defined symmetrically. Compare using ATR on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: CISD levels = Initialize the structure top and bottom to zero. A previous bullish candle starts a bearish pullback if one is not already active, with its open and bar index as the candidate top and index; a previous bearish candle similarly starts a bullish pullback with its open and index as the candidate bottom and index. During a bullish pullback, replace the candidate bottom and index with the current open and index if the open is lower than the candidate, or if the current candle is bearish and its open is higher than the candidate. During a bearish pullback, replace the candidate top and index if the open is higher than the candidate, or if the current candle is bullish and its open is lower than the candidate. If the current low is below the structure bottom, set the bottom to that low; if a bearish pullback is active and its candidate index differs from the current index, set the structure top to the greater high at the candidate index and the following bar, end that pullback, and create a bullish level at the candidate top; otherwise, if the previous candle is bullish and the current candle bearish, set the structure top to the previous high, end the pullback, and create a bullish level at the candidate top. Then, if the current high is above the structure top, set the top to that high; if a bullish pullback is active and its candidate index differs from the current index, set the structure bottom to the lesser low at the candidate index and the following bar, end that pullback, and create a bearish level at the candidate bottom; otherwise, if the previous candle is bearish and the current candle bullish, set the structure bottom to the previous low, end the pullback, and create a bearish level at the candidate bottom. Keep separate ordered lists of bullish and bearish levels, each initially empty. Unless old levels are retained, remove the oldest level when its list contains more than one. After level creation and removal, process the oldest unfinished bearish level: if close is below it, complete it and append a bullish level at the current candidate top. Then process the oldest unfinished bullish level: if close is above it, complete it and append a bearish level at the current candidate bottom. A level without a defined price cannot trigger a comparison.
Long: A swing or internal close crosses above its latest high pivot AND that high pivot has not already been crossed AND, for internal structure, its high pivot differs from the swing high pivot AND, if the optional confluence filter is enabled, the upper wick is longer than the lower wick.
Short: A swing or internal close crosses below its latest high pivot AND that high pivot has not already been crossed AND, for internal structure, its latest low pivot differs from the swing low pivot AND, if the optional confluence filter is enabled, the upper wick is shorter than the lower wick.
Long: A new swing low pivot is confirmed AND its price is below, equal to, or above the preceding swing low pivot.
Short: A new swing high pivot is confirmed AND its price is below, equal to, or above the preceding swing high pivot.
Long: An equal-low event occurs.
Short: An equal-high event occurs.
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed using equal numbers of bars on both sides AND its price is below the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND RSI at the new pivot is above RSI at the preceding pivot.
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed using equal numbers of bars on both sides AND its price is above the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND RSI at the new pivot is below RSI at the preceding pivot.
Long: A bullish CISD level is created OR close is above the oldest unfinished bullish CISD level.
Short: A bearish CISD level is created OR close is below the oldest unfinished bearish CISD level.
Long: Low minus the high two bars ago is greater than ATR AND low is greater than the high two bars ago AND the previous close is greater than the high two bars ago.
Short: The low two bars ago minus high is greater than ATR AND high is below the low two bars ago AND the previous close is below the low two bars ago.
