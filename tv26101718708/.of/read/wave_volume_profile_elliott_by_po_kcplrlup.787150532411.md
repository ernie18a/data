Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed using configurable numbers of left and right bars; on simultaneous confirmation keep only the high. Maintain alternating pivots, replacing consecutive pivots of the same type only when the new high is higher or the new low is lower.
Calculation: Pullback threshold = configurable minimum, or, when adaptive mode is enabled and enough qualifying observations exist, a configurable fraction of the upper median of the most recent bounded history of positive high-to-low pivot drops below a configurable maximum, clamped to configurable bounds. Record a drop when a low is appended after a high, before testing the new pattern.
Calculation: ABC pattern = the latest five alternating pivots are low, impulse high, A low, B high, C low; (impulse high − A low) / impulse high >= pullback threshold; (B high − A low) / (impulse high − A low) <= maximum retracement; B high < impulse high; and no earlier high in the configured pivot lookback through the first low is >= impulse high. If deep C is disallowed, C low >= A low multiplied by a configurable factor. Detect only when the cooldown counter, decremented at the start of each bar, is zero; reset it on detection.
Calculation: Active structure = on ABC detection, discard the previous structure and any pending pattern. In static mode, activate the new structure immediately and end its profile at the C pivot bar. In dynamic mode, keep it pending; invalidate it if close < C low − ATR × invalidation multiplier; otherwise activate it on the first bar where close > B high + ATR × breakout multiplier AND volume / SMA(volume) >= breakout volume ratio, OR a newly confirmed pivot low has a pivot bar later than C and price > C low, OR pending age >= time cap. End the dynamic profile on that activation bar and reset cooldown. A later ABC detection replaces the active or pending structure. After processing entries and exits, deactivate the active structure if no strategy position is open AND (high >= final target OR low <= C low).
Calculation: Profile point of control = divide the price range from the chosen start pivot, either impulse high or A low, through the profile end bar inclusive into equal price rows. Allocate each bar's volume across rows in proportion to overlap with its high-low range; allocate all volume to its low's row when high equals low. The point of control is the midpoint of the row with greatest allocated volume, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Freeze it when the structure activates.
Calculation: Targets = point of control + risk × target multiplier, where risk = point of control − C low if positive, otherwise C low × a configurable fallback fraction. Use three target multipliers ordered from smallest to largest.
Long: An active structure exists AND the current bar is later than the C pivot bar AND close has remained above the point of control for at least the shorter configured number of consecutive bars since activation AND the shorter-count entry has not previously fired for this structure.
Long: An active structure exists AND the current bar is later than the C pivot bar AND close has remained above the point of control for at least the longer configured number of consecutive bars since activation AND the longer-count entry has not previously fired for this structure.
Long: An active structure exists AND the current bar is later than the C pivot bar AND close has remained above the point of control for at least the shorter configured number of consecutive bars since activation AND volume / SMA(volume) >= the entry volume ratio AND the volume-confirmed entry has not previously fired for this structure.
Exit: long, a new ABC pattern is detected.
Exit: long, low <= C low.
Exit: long, high >= the largest target.
Exit: long, for the hold-with-trailing-stop alternative, the smallest target has been reached since entry AND low <= the highest price since entry minus current ATR × trailing multiplier.
Exit: long, for the scale-out alternative, high >= the smallest target AND the first portion remains open.
Exit: long, for the scale-out alternative, high >= the middle target AND the second portion remains open.
Exit: long, for the scale-out alternative, the first two portions have been closed AND low <= the highest price since entry minus current ATR × trailing multiplier.
Exit: long, for the breakeven alternative, the smallest target has been reached since entry AND low <= entry price.
Exit: long, for the separate trend-following evaluation, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND close < EMA(close).
