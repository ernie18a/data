Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; update the latest swing when its right-hand bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars ago, with bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low. Bearish gap = current high < low two bars ago, with bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago. Track each direction separately, discarding its oldest unconverted gap when its configurable capacity is exceeded. On each bar, including the creation bar, a gap is intersected when high >= bottom AND low <= top. An intersected bullish gap converts to a bearish inverted gap if close < bottom, is discarded if close > top, and otherwise is marked touched. An intersected bearish gap converts to a bullish inverted gap if close > top, is discarded if close < bottom, and otherwise is marked touched. A converted gap retains whether it was touched before conversion and has midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish inverted-gap contact = low <= midpoint if previously touched, otherwise low <= top, AND high >= bottom. Bearish inverted-gap contact = high >= midpoint if previously touched, otherwise high >= bottom, AND low <= top. Check contact on the conversion bar and every subsequent bar before removing a bullish inverted gap when close > top or a bearish inverted gap when close < bottom.
Calculation: Delivery open = the most recent open of a bar of the specified direction with high > low AND absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= a configurable threshold. Update the corresponding delivery open before testing the current bar; clear the bullish delivery open after close < it and the bearish delivery open after close > it.
Calculation: Nearest upper/lower liquidity = respectively the nearest confirmed swing high above close and nearest confirmed swing low below close among a fixed-capacity history of the most recent confirmed swings of each type.
Long: On a newly completed higher-timeframe bar, its low > the high two higher-timeframe bars earlier; use completed higher-timeframe bars.
Short: On a newly completed higher-timeframe bar, its high < the low two higher-timeframe bars earlier; use completed higher-timeframe bars.
Long: Current low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low.
Short: Current high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high.
Long: Close > bearish delivery open.
Short: Close < bullish delivery open.
Long: A bearish gap converts to a bullish inverted gap.
Short: A bullish gap converts to a bearish inverted gap.
Long: A bullish inverted-gap contact is present.
Short: A bearish inverted-gap contact is present.
Long: A bullish sweep occurred within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar, AND a bullish inverted gap converts or is contacted AND the bearish delivery open is crossed when the delivery filter is enabled AND, when the liquidity filter is enabled and both nearest liquidity levels exist, close - nearest lower liquidity >= nearest upper liquidity - close AND, when the wick filter is enabled, min(open, close) - low <= ATR multiplied by a configurable factor.
Short: A bearish sweep occurred within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar, AND a bearish inverted gap converts or is contacted AND the bullish delivery open is crossed when the delivery filter is enabled AND, when the liquidity filter is enabled and both nearest liquidity levels exist, nearest upper liquidity - close >= close - nearest lower liquidity AND, when the wick filter is enabled, high - max(open, close) <= ATR multiplied by a configurable factor.
