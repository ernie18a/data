Calculation: Bullish FVG = current-timeframe high two bars ago < current-timeframe low; bearish FVG = low two bars ago > high.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = a tracked bullish FVG is intersected and a bar closes below its bottom; bearish IFVG = a tracked bearish FVG is intersected and a bar closes above its top. A zone remains active until a bar closes beyond its far edge. For a previously touched zone, the bullish entry ceiling or bearish entry floor is the zone midpoint; otherwise it is the near edge.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG zone touch = low <= its entry ceiling AND high >= its bottom; bearish IFVG zone touch = high >= its entry floor AND low <= its top.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar's high/low is the maximum/minimum over an equal swing lookback on both sides; it becomes available after the bars on its right complete. Keep the latest confirmed pivot high and low.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot; bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot. A sweep is recent while the number of bars since it occurred <= the sweep-valid lookback.
Calculation: Delivery bar = high > low AND absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= 0.3. Track the open of the latest delivery bar closing above its open and the latest delivery bar closing below its open.
Calculation: Bullish CISD = close > the tracked bearish delivery open; bearish CISD = close < the tracked bullish delivery open. Clear the corresponding tracked open when its CISD occurs.
Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = current low < the lowest low over the prior SMT lookback bars AND the comparison instrument's current low > its lowest low over the prior same-length lookback. Bearish SMT divergence = current high > the highest high over the prior lookback bars AND the comparison instrument's current high < its highest high over the prior same-length lookback. Use same-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Data-wick block for longs = lower wick > ATR(20) * wick multiplier; for shorts = upper wick > ATR(20) * wick multiplier. Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Nearest liquidity above/below = nearest confirmed pivot high above close / pivot low below close, among the five most recently confirmed pivots of that type. Opposing liquidity for a long = both sides exist AND distance to the lower level < distance to the upper; for a short = both sides exist AND distance to the upper level < distance to the lower.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish FVG = high two bars ago < low; bearish FVG = low two bars ago > high. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; update bias to bullish or bearish when a new higher-timeframe bar is detected and its corresponding FVG forms.
Long: Bullish FVG forms OR bullish IFVG forms OR price touches an active bullish IFVG zone OR bullish sweep occurs OR bullish CISD occurs OR bullish SMT divergence occurs OR (bullish higher-timeframe FVG forms and is displayed) OR (recent bullish sweep AND (bullish IFVG forms OR price touches an active bullish IFVG zone) AND (CISD requirement disabled OR bullish CISD) AND (SMT requirement disabled OR bullish SMT divergence) AND NOT (DOL filter enabled AND opposing liquidity for a long) AND NOT (wick filter enabled AND data-wick block for longs)).
Short: Bearish FVG forms OR bearish IFVG forms OR price touches an active bearish IFVG zone OR bearish sweep occurs OR bearish CISD occurs OR bearish SMT divergence occurs OR (bearish higher-timeframe FVG forms and is displayed) OR (recent bearish sweep AND (bearish IFVG forms OR price touches an active bearish IFVG zone) AND (CISD requirement disabled OR bearish CISD) AND (SMT requirement disabled OR bearish SMT divergence) AND NOT (DOL filter enabled AND opposing liquidity for a short) AND NOT (wick filter enabled AND data-wick block for shorts)).
