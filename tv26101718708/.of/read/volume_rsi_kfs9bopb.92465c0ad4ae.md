Calculation: Volume power = Let directional strength be (close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low. Let adjusted volume be volume raised to (1 - volume boost) when volume is positive, otherwise 0, multiplied by volume factor, plus (1 - volume factor). Let raw power be directional strength multiplied by adjusted volume. Divide raw power by the RMA of its absolute value over a scaling-days parameter multiplied by max(1, round(23400 / chart-bar duration in seconds)); multiply by 100, or use 0 if the RMA is not positive. Clamp the result to [-100, 100], apply an RMA, then optionally apply SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, WMA, or no smoothing. For HMA, use WMA(2 × WMA(input, floor(length / 2)) - WMA(input, length), floor(sqrt(length))).
