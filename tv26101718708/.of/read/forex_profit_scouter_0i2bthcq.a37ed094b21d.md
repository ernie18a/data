Calculation: Reference ranges = high and low of each enabled last-completed higher-timeframe bar, using independently configured durations and session anchors, plus the high and low accumulated over a configured intraday window. Reset a range’s sweep state whenever either boundary changes.
Calculation: Sweep state = for each range, record whether low has fallen below its low or high has risen above its high, and the most extreme low or high since the range changed. Invalidate that side for the range when its sweep depth exceeds a configurable fraction of the range width. At each new extreme, record the open of the latest run of consecutive bearish candles for a low sweep or bullish candles for a high sweep; a run starts when the preceding candle is not of the same direction.
Calculation: Delayed confirmation = when a range changes, arm each valid swept side if the preceding chart bar closed strictly inside the old range; retain the old range and sweep extreme. Disarm a low-sweep setup if a subsequent low falls below its retained extreme, or a high-sweep setup if a subsequent high rises above its retained extreme. Disarm on its first signal and on the configured intraday range’s daily reset.
Calculation: Regime = either disabled, chart-timeframe short EMA greater than long EMA for upward and otherwise downward, or ADX on the last completed higher-timeframe bar at least a threshold AND its short EMA greater than its medium EMA greater than its long EMA for upward, with the reverse EMA ordering for downward. In ADX mode, neither direction applies when these conditions are unmet.
Calculation: Risk distance = for a range-reclaim setup, the greater of the minimum stop distance and the distance from entry close to the sweep extreme plus a buffer; for an opening-range breakout, the greater of the minimum stop distance and the absolute distance from entry close to the opening-range midpoint. Buffer and stop limits are configured price distances or fractions of ATR on the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Targets = for a range-reclaim trade, first target is the range midpoint, second is the opposite boundary, and third is the opposite boundary plus a configurable range-width extension in the trade direction. If the midpoint’s positive distance in risk units is below a threshold and the configured action is to shift targets, use the opposite boundary, then one and two extension distances beyond it. For an opening-range breakout, targets are entry plus separate configured risk multiples in the trade direction. Store risk and targets at entry.
Calculation: Active stop = entry minus risk distance in the position direction initially; after the first target optionally set it to entry; after the second target optionally set it to the first target, otherwise optionally move it to at least breakeven. In trailing mode after the second target, tighten it each bar to the more protective of the existing stop and close minus a configured multiple of chart ATR in the position direction. Apply stop updates only after testing the current bar’s old stop and targets.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = chart bar is closed AND weekday AND time is within the configured trading window AND outside the optional news window AND no position is open or closed on this bar AND daily trade and net-loss counts are below their limits AND current high minus low is no greater than a multiple of preceding-bar chart ATR AND optional current chart ATR is no greater than a multiple of its longer simple moving average AND risk distance is no greater than the maximum stop. Count losses from completed trade results after configured trading costs; reset daily counts on the higher-timeframe daily boundary.
Calculation: Candidate selection = evaluate all enabled ranges and the opening range independently, consume each side’s first raw signal even if a later filter rejects it, and select the first regime-allowed candidate in configured range priority followed by opening range; if both directions occur in one model, select long. Apply remaining eligibility filters only to that selected candidate.
Long: shortest chart EMA > longest chart EMA.
Short: shortest chart EMA <= longest chart EMA.
Long: entry eligibility AND selected low-sweep candidate AND regime is not downward AND either fast or candle-delivery mode has a valid low sweep AND close > open AND close > range low AND, in candle-delivery mode, close > the recorded bearish-run open when available, OR delayed-confirmation mode has an armed low sweep AND close > retained range low AND close < retained range midpoint; AND midpoint > close AND (opposite range high - close) / risk distance >= configured minimum AND either midpoint distance / risk distance >= configured minimum or the configured action is to shift targets AND optional close <= last completed higher-timeframe daily range midpoint.
Short: entry eligibility AND selected high-sweep candidate AND regime is not upward AND either fast or candle-delivery mode has a valid high sweep AND close < open AND close < range high AND, in candle-delivery mode, close < the recorded bullish-run open when available, OR delayed-confirmation mode has an armed high sweep AND close < retained range high AND close > retained range midpoint; AND midpoint < close AND (close - opposite range low) / risk distance >= configured minimum AND either midpoint distance / risk distance >= configured minimum or the configured action is to shift targets AND optional close >= last completed higher-timeframe daily range midpoint.
Long: entry eligibility AND selected opening-range candidate AND close > completed opening-range high AND no earlier raw upward breakout signal since the daily reset AND regime is disabled or upward AND optional close <= last completed higher-timeframe daily range midpoint.
Short: entry eligibility AND selected opening-range candidate AND close < completed opening-range low AND no earlier raw downward breakout signal since the daily reset AND regime is disabled or downward AND optional close >= last completed higher-timeframe daily range midpoint.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= active stop OR fixed-target mode AND high >= third target OR time is within the configured flattening window OR a new higher-timeframe day begins.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= active stop OR fixed-target mode AND low <= third target OR time is within the configured flattening window OR a new higher-timeframe day begins.
Exit: long, after the entry bar and if no full exit occurs, high >= first target exits one third if not already exited AND high >= second target exits another third if the first target has been reached.
Exit: short, after the entry bar and if no full exit occurs, low <= first target exits one third if not already exited AND low <= second target exits another third if the first target has been reached.
