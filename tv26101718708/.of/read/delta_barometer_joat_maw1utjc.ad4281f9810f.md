Calculation: Volatility regime = low if ATR / SMA(ATR) < low threshold; high if the ratio > high threshold; medium otherwise. Use a ratio of 1 when the ATR SMA is not positive. Select a separate ATR multiplier for each regime.
Calculation: ATR trail and direction = start with direction up. On each bar, set the up candidate to close − ATR × regime multiplier and the down candidate to close + ATR × regime multiplier. If the previous direction was up, replace the up candidate with the maximum of it and the previous trail; otherwise replace the down candidate with the minimum of it and the previous trail. If the previous direction was up, use the up candidate and switch direction to down and use the down candidate if close < that candidate. If the previous direction was down, use the down candidate and switch direction to up and use the up candidate if close > that candidate. On initialization, use the unratcheted candidates.
Calculation: Volume component = clamp((volume / EMA(volume) − 1) / volume scaling factor, 0, 1), using a volume ratio of 1 when the EMA is not positive.
Calculation: Trend strength = weighted sum of clamp(abs(RSI − RSI from a lookback number of bars ago) / RSI scaling factor, 0, 1), the volume component, and clamp((ATR / shorter-period ATR − 1) / ATR scaling factor, 0, 1). Use an ATR ratio of 1 when the shorter-period ATR is not positive. Normalize the weights to sum to 1; the RSI weight is greater than the volume weight, which is greater than the ATR weight.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of volume when close >= open and negative volume otherwise.
Calculation: Pressure = weighted sum of abs(signed volume / volume), the volume component, and clamp(abs(close − open) / (high − low), 0, 1). Signed volume is volume when close >= open and negative volume otherwise. Use 0 for the signed-volume ratio when volume is not positive and 0 for the body component when high − low is not positive. Normalize the weights to sum to 1; the signed-volume weight is greater than the volume weight, which is greater than the body weight.
Calculation: A-long = A enabled AND A's selected regime includes the current regime AND previous trail direction = down AND close > current ATR trail AND trend strength >= its threshold. A-short = A enabled AND A's selected regime includes the current regime AND previous trail direction = up AND close < current ATR trail AND trend strength >= its threshold. Each engine's selectable regime set is all, low only, medium and high, or high only.
Calculation: B-long = B enabled AND B's selected regime includes the current regime AND cumulative delta crosses above its SMA AND pressure >= its threshold AND RSI < the overbought threshold. B-short = B enabled AND B's selected regime includes the current regime AND cumulative delta crosses below its SMA AND pressure >= its threshold AND RSI > the oversold threshold. A crossover requires the current value to be strictly above the SMA and the previous value to be at or below its previous SMA; a crossunder is the converse.
Long: Bars since the most recent confirmed long or short signal >= cooldown AND ((mode = both AND A-long AND B-long) OR (mode = priority A AND (A-long OR (B-long AND A-short = false))) OR (mode = priority B AND (B-long OR (A-long AND B-short = false))) OR (mode = either AND (A-long OR B-long))).
Short: Bars since the most recent confirmed long or short signal >= cooldown AND ((mode = both AND A-short AND B-short) OR (mode = priority A AND (A-short OR (B-short AND A-long = false))) OR (mode = priority B AND (B-short OR (A-short AND B-long = false))) OR (mode = either AND (A-short OR B-short))).
Long: Trend strength > the upper strength threshold AND pressure > the upper pressure threshold OR trend strength > the lower strength threshold OR pressure > the lower pressure threshold; each upper threshold is greater than its corresponding lower threshold.
Long: ATR trail direction = up OR cumulative delta > its SMA.
Short: ATR trail direction = down OR cumulative delta <= its SMA.
Calculation: Entry stop = for a long, min(ATR trail at entry, entry price − entry ATR × stop factor); for a short, max(ATR trail at entry, entry price + entry ATR × stop factor). If the trail is unavailable, use entry price reduced by the stop percentage for a long or increased by it for a short. Entry target = entry price plus entry ATR × target multiplier for a long, or minus that amount for a short. Fix both levels at the confirmed entry bar's close; a new confirmed signal replaces the tracked side and levels before checking that bar's high and low.
Exit: long, high >= entry target OR low <= entry stop.
Exit: short, low <= entry target OR high >= entry stop.
