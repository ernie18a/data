Calculation: +DI / −DI = For each bar, calculate true range as max(high−low, abs(high−previous close), abs(low−previous close)); +DM is the positive high change when it exceeds the positive low decrease, otherwise 0; −DM is the positive low decrease when it exceeds the high change, otherwise 0. Apply Wilder RMA with the same lookback to true range and each DM; divide each smoothed DM by smoothed true range and multiply by 100.
Calculation: ADX = Simple moving average, using the same lookback as the DI calculations, of 100 × abs(+DI−−DI) / max(+DI+−DI, 0.000001).
Calculation: MTF participation = For each of three aggregated timeframes (higher, intermediate, and lower than the chart timeframe), assign strong if ADX ≥ threshold, building if 20 ≤ ADX < threshold, and weak if ADX < 20. Use the most recent available bar for each timeframe, including a still-forming bar where available.
Long: +DI > −DI on the chart timeframe OR +DI > −DI on any of the three aggregated timeframes OR all three aggregated timeframes have +DI > −DI and (all three have strong participation OR participation is nondecreasing from lower to higher timeframe with at least one strong timeframe OR at most one strong timeframe and the nondecreasing-participation condition is false)
Short: −DI > +DI on the chart timeframe OR −DI > +DI on any of the three aggregated timeframes OR all three aggregated timeframes have −DI > +DI and (all three have strong participation OR participation is nondecreasing from lower to higher timeframe with at least one strong timeframe OR at most one strong timeframe and the nondecreasing-participation condition is false)
Exit: Long, no exit decision
Exit: Short, no exit decision
