Calculation: DI and ADX = On each timeframe, true range is the maximum of high minus low, absolute high minus previous close, and absolute low minus previous close. Up movement is high minus previous high; down movement is previous low minus low. Positive directional movement is max(up movement, 0) when up movement > down movement, otherwise 0; negative directional movement is max(down movement, 0) when down movement > up movement, otherwise 0. Replace missing previous prices with 0. Positive and negative DI are 100 times their respective Wilder-smoothed directional movements divided by Wilder-smoothed true range, all using the same length. DX is 100 times the absolute DI difference divided by the greater of their sum and a small positive floor. ADX is the simple moving average of DX using that same length.
Calculation: MTF values = Calculate DI and ADX with the same length on three independently selected timeframes ordered longest, middle, and shortest. Each may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar.
Calculation: Participation = 2 if ADX >= the upper participation threshold, 1 if ADX < the upper threshold AND ADX >= the lower participation threshold, otherwise 0. Use the same thresholds across the three timeframes; the upper threshold is higher than the lower threshold.
Calculation: Directional state = Full bullish or bearish when all three timeframes have the respective DI direction AND all three have participation 2. Building bullish or bearish when all three have the respective DI direction AND shortest-timeframe participation >= middle-timeframe participation AND middle-timeframe participation >= longest-timeframe participation AND at least one has participation 2 AND the state is not full. Weak bullish or bearish when all three have the respective DI direction AND at most one has participation 2 AND the state is not building. Otherwise the state is mixed.
Long: Chart-timeframe positive DI > negative DI.
Short: Chart-timeframe positive DI <= negative DI.
Long: Positive DI > negative DI on any one of the three MTF timeframes.
Short: Negative DI > positive DI on any one of the three MTF timeframes.
Long: Directional state is full bullish OR building bullish OR weak bullish.
Short: Directional state is full bearish OR building bearish OR weak bearish.
Long: Chart bar is complete AND directional state differs from the previous chart bar AND directional state is full bullish OR building bullish OR weak bullish; when strict alerts are enabled, directional state must be full bullish.
Short: Chart bar is complete AND directional state differs from the previous chart bar AND directional state is full bearish OR building bearish OR weak bearish; when strict alerts are enabled, directional state must be full bearish.
