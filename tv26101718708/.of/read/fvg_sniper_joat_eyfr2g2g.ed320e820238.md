Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish if current low > high two bars ago, with top=current low and bottom=high two bars ago; bearish if current high < low two bars ago, with top=low two bars ago and bottom=current high. Keep only gaps whose height is at least the minimum ATR multiple and whose middle-bar absolute body/range is at least the minimum ratio; use a minimum tick for zero ranges.
Calculation: Gap grade = 10 × min(1, gap height/ATR ÷ 1 × 0.40 + middle-bar range/ATR ÷ 2 × 0.35 + relative volume × 0.25), capped at 10; relative volume = min(previous-bar volume/current 20-bar volume SMA, 2)/2 when current volume and SMA are positive, otherwise 0.5.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA, using the latest value available with lookahead off (the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically); bullish when close > EMA, bearish otherwise. If the bias filter is disabled, it imposes no constraint.
Long: A qualified bullish gap at least one bar old is unmitigated, uninverted, and has not previously triggered a rejection; current low is within [gap bottom, gap top], close > gap bottom, grade ≥ minimum, higher-timeframe bias is bullish or the filter is disabled, and (if required) close > open; OR a qualified bearish gap closes above its top, grade ≥ minimum, and higher-timeframe bias is bullish or the filter is disabled. Enter at close only when no trade is active and ATR is available; if long and short signals coincide, take the long.
Short: A qualified bearish gap at least one bar old is unmitigated, uninverted, and has not previously triggered a rejection; current high is within [gap bottom, gap top], close < gap top, grade ≥ minimum, higher-timeframe bias is bearish or the filter is disabled, and (if required) close < open; OR a qualified bullish gap closes below its bottom, grade ≥ minimum, and higher-timeframe bias is bearish or the filter is disabled. Enter at close only when no trade is active, ATR is available, and no long is taken on the same bar.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, if low ≤ stop, or high ≥ third target, or elapsed bars since entry ≥ maximum trade duration; stop takes priority if stop and target are both touched.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, if high ≥ stop, or low ≤ third target, or elapsed bars since entry ≥ maximum trade duration; stop takes priority if stop and target are both touched.
