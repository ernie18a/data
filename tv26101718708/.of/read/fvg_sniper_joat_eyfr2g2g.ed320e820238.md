Calculation: Qualified gap = on its creation bar, a bullish gap has bottom = high two bars earlier and top = current low, with top > bottom; a bearish gap has bottom = current high and top = low two bars earlier, with top > bottom. Its height divided by current ATR must be at least a threshold, and the absolute body of the preceding bar divided by its range must be at least another threshold. Store its boundaries, direction, creation bar and grade. Track gaps in creation order, remove the oldest when their count exceeds a limit, and remove a gap after processing it on the first bar its age exceeds a lifetime limit.
Calculation: Gap grade = at creation, 10 times the sum of 0.40 × min(gap height / ATR, 1), 0.35 × min(preceding bar range / ATR / 2, 1), and 0.25 × volume score, capped at 10. Volume score = min(preceding bar volume / current volume SMA, 2) / 2 when current volume and its SMA are positive; otherwise it is 0.5.
Calculation: Gap state = initially unmitigated, uninverted and unreacted. On each subsequent bar, a bullish gap becomes mitigated when low <= bottom and inverted when close < bottom; a bearish gap becomes mitigated when high >= top and inverted when close > top. Inversion occurs only once per gap. A gap becomes reacted when it produces a qualifying rejection, even if a trade is already active.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the higher-timeframe close with its EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with no lookahead. Bias is bullish when close > EMA and bearish otherwise; an unavailable bias is neutral.
Calculation: Signal gap = when multiple qualifying rejection or inversion signals occur on a bar, process retained gaps newest first and use the boundaries of the last qualifying gap processed, regardless of signal direction. Long has priority if both directions signal. Entry is at close; a new trade requires no active trade and available ATR. Process entries before exits, so an exit bar cannot also open a new trade.
Calculation: Trade risk = at entry, choose either ATR multiplied by a stop-distance parameter or the absolute distance from entry to the signal gap's bottom minus an ATR buffer for a long, or its top plus an ATR buffer for a short. Clamp that distance between minimum and maximum ATR multiples. The long stop is entry minus risk and its final target is entry plus risk times a target multiple; the short stop and target are entry plus risk and entry minus risk times that multiple. Fix these levels at entry.
Long: A retained bearish gap becomes inverted on this bar AND inversion signals are enabled.
Short: A retained bullish gap becomes inverted on this bar AND inversion signals are enabled.
Long: Rejection signals are enabled AND a retained bullish gap is unmitigated, uninverted and unreacted after the current bar's state update AND its age is at least one bar AND low <= gap top AND low >= gap bottom AND close > gap bottom AND gap grade >= minimum grade AND (directional-close filter is disabled OR close > open) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR bias is bullish OR bias is neutral).
Short: Rejection signals are enabled AND a retained bearish gap is unmitigated, uninverted and unreacted after the current bar's state update AND its age is at least one bar AND high >= gap bottom AND high <= gap top AND close < gap top AND gap grade >= minimum grade AND (directional-close filter is disabled OR close < open) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR bias is bearish OR bias is neutral).
Long: A retained bearish gap becomes inverted on this bar AND inversion signals are enabled AND gap grade >= minimum grade AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR bias is bullish OR bias is neutral).
Short: A retained bullish gap becomes inverted on this bar AND inversion signals are enabled AND gap grade >= minimum grade AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR bias is bearish OR bias is neutral).
Long: A retained uninverted bullish gap has close >= its bottom AND close <= its top AND no long or short signal occurs on the bar.
Short: A retained uninverted bearish gap has close >= its bottom AND close <= its top AND no long signal occurs on the bar AND close is not inside a retained uninverted bullish gap.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target OR elapsed bars >= maximum trade duration).
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target OR elapsed bars >= maximum trade duration).
