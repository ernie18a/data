Calculation: Momentum = selected RSI of close, ROC of close, MFI of typical price, smoothed stochastic of close against high and low, or their composite. The stochastic is a simple moving average of stochastic %K; the composite is the mean of RSI, normalized ROC, MFI and smoothed stochastic. All four use the same momentum lookback. Normalized ROC = clamp(50 + 50 × ROC / (2 × standard deviation of ROC + 0.0001), 0, 100), using a separate normalization lookback. For the ROC selection, use raw ROC as momentum and normalized ROC as its oscillator-scale value; for all other selections, the oscillator-scale value equals momentum.
Calculation: Peak and trough = the highest and lowest momentum in the rolling peak lookback, including the current bar; use the most recent occurrence on a tie. Their anchor prices are the high and low, respectively, of their occurrence bars. Their oscillator-scale values and volumes are taken from those same bars.
Calculation: Pullback threshold = selected fixed percentage, optionally multiplied by clamp((ATR / close × 100) / (simple moving average of ATR / close × 100 + 0.0001), 0.6, 1.8), using separate ATR and volatility-average lookbacks.
Calculation: Strength = round(extremity + pullback score + volume score + divergence score). For a peak, extremity = clamp((peak oscillator-scale value - 50) × 40 / 50, 0, 40) and actual pullback = (peak momentum - current momentum) / (peak momentum + 0.0001) × 100. For a trough, extremity = clamp((50 - trough oscillator-scale value) × 40 / 50, 0, 40) and actual pullback = (current momentum - trough momentum) / (abs(trough momentum) + 0.0001) × 100. Pullback score = clamp((actual pullback - pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20). Volume score = clamp((extreme-bar volume / (current bar's volume simple moving average + 0.0001) - 1) × 20, 0, 20). Divergence score is 20 when the optional divergence bonus is enabled and divergence is present, otherwise 0. Peak divergence requires the peak anchor to be higher and peak momentum lower than at the previous qualifying peak saturation; trough divergence requires the trough anchor to be lower and trough momentum higher than at the previous qualifying trough saturation.
Calculation: Qualifying saturation = the corresponding saturation decision below; evaluate both sides using state from before either decision on the current completed bar. After evaluation, record each qualifying extreme and its bar, and record the current bar as the latest saturation bar even when no zone is created. The cooldown is shared by both sides.
Calculation: Active zones = on a qualifying saturation, create a support zone from the trough anchor down by a multiple of current ATR, or a resistance zone from the peak anchor up by that amount, unless an existing same-side zone's anchor is within a specified multiple of current ATR. Before creating a zone at the active-zone limit, remove the oldest active zone. Process resistance creation before support creation. Then remove support zones when close is below their lower edge reduced by an invalidation percentage, and resistance zones when close is above their upper edge increased by that percentage. Zone boundaries remain fixed at creation values.
Calculation: Zone direction = after zone maintenance, if the bar overlaps one or more active zones, use the side of the most recently created overlapping zone. Otherwise use the side of the active zone with the closest anchor to close, provided that distance is less than a specified multiple of an ATR with its own lookback; ties select the oldest zone.
Long: On a completed bar, momentum >= trough momentum + abs(trough momentum) × pullback threshold / 100 AND the trough bar differs from the last qualifying trough bar AND bars since the latest qualifying saturation >= cooldown AND (absolute-extreme filter is disabled OR trough oscillator-scale value <= oversold threshold) AND (volume filter is disabled OR trough-bar volume > current volume simple moving average × volume multiplier) AND trough age >= confirmation bars AND trough strength >= minimum strength.
Short: On a completed bar, momentum <= peak momentum × (1 - pullback threshold / 100) AND the peak bar differs from the last qualifying peak bar AND bars since the latest qualifying saturation >= cooldown AND (absolute-extreme filter is disabled OR peak oscillator-scale value >= overbought threshold) AND (volume filter is disabled OR peak-bar volume > current volume simple moving average × volume multiplier) AND peak age >= confirmation bars AND peak strength >= minimum strength.
Long: Zone direction = support.
Short: Zone direction = resistance.
Long: Current oscillator-scale momentum < oversold threshold.
Short: Current oscillator-scale momentum > overbought threshold.
