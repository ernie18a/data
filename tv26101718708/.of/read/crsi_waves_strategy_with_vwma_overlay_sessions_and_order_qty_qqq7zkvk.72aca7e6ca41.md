Calculation: Cyclic RSI = let the dominant cycle be a parameter, RSI length be half that cycle, smoothing coefficient be 2/(vibration+1), and lag be (vibration-1)/2 bars; compute RSI from close using Wilder-smoothed gains and losses, returning the RSI maximum when smoothed loss is zero and zero when only smoothed gain is zero; cyclic RSI = smoothing coefficient × (2 × RSI − lagged RSI) + (1 − smoothing coefficient) × previous cyclic RSI, with a missing previous value replaced by zero.
Calculation: Cyclic RSI bands = over a memory twice the dominant cycle, initialize both extrema to zero and scan cyclic RSI from current to oldest, updating the maximum if the value is greater than the maximum, otherwise updating the minimum if it is less than the minimum. Divide the range into equal steps. The lower band is the first step upward from the minimum for which the fraction of memory values strictly below the step reaches the leveling fraction; the upper band is the first step downward from the maximum for which the fraction of memory values greater than or equal to the step reaches that fraction. Initialize either band to zero if no step qualifies.
Calculation: Cyclic RSI top = cyclic RSI on the previous bar is greater than on the bar before it AND current cyclic RSI is lower than on the previous bar; optionally also require the value two bars ago to be greater than the value three bars ago. Cyclic RSI bottom reverses all these inequalities. Tops and bottoms are detected on the current bar.
Calculation: Wave candidates = A is a cyclic RSI bottom whose previous-bar cyclic RSI is below its lower band; B is a cyclic RSI top whose previous-bar cyclic RSI is below its WMA OR below the RSI midpoint; C is A OR a cyclic RSI bottom whose previous-bar cyclic RSI is below its WMA. Starting with A, construct AB, ABC, ABCD, ABCDE, ABCDEF and ABCDEFG by requiring respectively B, C, B, C, B and C now and the preceding sequence on an earlier bar within a wave-search lookback; every bar from that earlier bar through the previous bar must have cyclic RSI below its WMA. Wave A is candidate A AND neither ABC nor ABCDE; wave C is ABC, wave E is ABCDE, and wave G is ABCDEFG.
Calculation: Cyclic RSI divergence lows = candidate A OR candidate C. Divergence highs = a cyclic RSI top AND either previous-bar cyclic RSI is at or above its upper band OR previous-bar cyclic RSI is below its upper band AND current cyclic RSI is above its WMA. Compare current cyclic RSI and close with their values on the most recent earlier qualifying low or high within a divergence lookback; use detection-bar values.
Calculation: Signal suppression = accept a raw buy or sell signal only when no previously accepted signal of the same side occurred within its suppression lookback; update this history even outside the permitted sessions and even when order size is zero.
Calculation: Buy size = maximum order size multiplied by one minus the lower-band value normalized and clamped between its lower and upper sizing thresholds; replace it with maximum order size if wave E or G occurred within the inclusive recent-wave lookback. Set it to zero if below one third of maximum order size, then round. Sell size = maximum order size multiplied by the upper-band value normalized and clamped between its lower and upper sizing thresholds; replace it with one and a half times maximum order size if cyclic RSI exceeds the extreme upper threshold. Set it to zero if below one third of maximum order size, then round.
Long: Close > volume-weighted moving average of high, or its ordinary WMA when volume weighting is disabled.
Short: Close < volume-weighted moving average of low, or its ordinary WMA when volume weighting is disabled.
Long: Cyclic RSI >= its WMA.
Short: Cyclic RSI < its WMA.
Long: A divergence low occurs AND an earlier divergence low exists within the divergence lookback AND ((cyclic RSI > its earlier value AND close <= its earlier value) OR (cyclic RSI <= its earlier value AND close > its earlier value)).
Short: A divergence high occurs AND an earlier divergence high exists within the divergence lookback AND ((cyclic RSI <= its earlier value AND close > its earlier value) OR (cyclic RSI > its earlier value AND close <= its earlier value)).
Long: An accepted raw buy signal AND buy size > 0 AND sessions are disabled OR an accepted raw buy signal AND buy size > 0 AND the bar opening time is in either enabled session; raw buy is cyclic RSI crossing from below to at or above its WMA OR wave A or C with regular bullish divergence OR wave E with the longer top/bottom detector setting OR wave G with the shorter setting; regular bullish divergence requires a divergence low, an earlier divergence low within the divergence lookback, current cyclic RSI > its earlier value, and current close <= its earlier value.
Short: An accepted raw sell signal AND sell size > 0 AND sessions are disabled OR an accepted raw sell signal AND sell size > 0 AND the bar opening time is in either enabled session; raw sell is upper band > its sell threshold AND (a cyclic RSI top with previous-bar cyclic RSI >= its upper band OR current cyclic RSI < previous cyclic RSI AND cyclic RSI was >= its upper band on the current bar, previous bar, or two bars ago).
Exit: long OR short, session-end closing is enabled for either session AND the bar opening hour equals that session's stop hour AND its minute is at or after that session's stop minute.
