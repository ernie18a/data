Calculation: cRSI = cyclically smoothed RSI of close: apply Wilder-smoothed gains and losses over half the dominant cycle to calculate RSI, phase-adjust it by half the vibration length, then smooth it recursively with weight 2/(vibration length + 1)
Calculation: Adaptive upper and lower cRSI bands = upper and lower percentile levels over twice the dominant-cycle lookback, each selected at the configured leveling percentile
Calculation: cRSI top = cRSI turns down after rising for the configured 2- or 3-bar lookback; cRSI bottom = cRSI turns up after falling for that lookback
Calculation: Strong impulse = cRSI top and cRSI was at or above the upper band on the preceding bar; weak impulse = cRSI top, cRSI was below the upper band on the preceding bar, and current cRSI is above its weighted moving average
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a cRSI low (a bottom below the lower band, or a bottom below the weighted moving average), cRSI is higher than at the most recent prior such low within 50 bars while close is not higher; hidden bullish divergence = at that low, cRSI is not higher while close is higher
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a strong or weak impulse high, cRSI is not higher than at the most recent prior such high within 50 bars while close is higher; hidden bearish divergence = at that high, cRSI is higher while close is not higher
Long: cRSI crosses above its weighted moving average OR (cRSI bottom below the lower band AND bullish divergence) OR (cRSI bottom below its weighted moving average AND bullish divergence) OR (cRSI bottom below the lower band AND a prior A-B-C sequence in the current bearish phase AND bullish divergence) OR (cRSI bottom below its weighted moving average AND a prior A-B-C sequence in the current bearish phase AND bullish divergence) OR (cRSI bottom below the lower band AND prior A-B-C-D-E sequence in the current bearish phase AND configured top/bottom lookback is 3) OR (cRSI bottom below its weighted moving average AND prior A-B-C-D-E sequence in the current bearish phase AND configured top/bottom lookback is 3) OR (cRSI bottom below the lower band AND prior A-B-C-D-E-F-G sequence in the current bearish phase AND configured top/bottom lookback is 2) OR (cRSI bottom below its weighted moving average AND prior A-B-C-D-E-F-G sequence in the current bearish phase AND configured top/bottom lookback is 2) OR strong impulse OR weak impulse; AND the signal is not duplicated within the configured recent-signal lookback; AND new trades are permitted by the configured sessions; AND calculated buy quantity is at least 1
Short: (strong impulse OR (cRSI at or above the upper band AND cRSI is falling) OR (cRSI was at or above the upper band one bar ago AND cRSI is falling) OR (cRSI was at or above the upper band two bars ago AND cRSI is falling)) AND upper band is above its configured high level; AND the signal is not duplicated within the configured recent-signal lookback; AND new trades are permitted by the configured sessions; AND calculated sell quantity is at least 1 OR bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence OR cRSI crosses below its weighted moving average
Exit: long or short, configured close-all option is enabled and the bar time is at or after the configured stop time of either session
Calculation: Session permission = current bar time is within either configured trading session, or session limiting is disabled
Calculation: Weighted moving average = weighted moving average of cRSI over its configured lookback
