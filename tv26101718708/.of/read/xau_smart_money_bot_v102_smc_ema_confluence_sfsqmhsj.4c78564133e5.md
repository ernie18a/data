Calculation: ATR = Average True Range, same length everywhere; OBV = cumulative sum of volume signed by close-to-close direction (+volume if close > previous close, -volume if lower, 0 if equal); body = abs(close - open); range = high - low
Calculation: HTF-bar = bar of a timeframe equal to or higher than the chart, the one containing the current chart bar (still forming, not the last completed bar); HTF-1 and HTF-2 = the one and two HTF bars before it
Calculation: EMA fast/slow = EMA with a short period / EMA with a longer period, on chart-timeframe close
Calculation: LongScore = count of true among: (1) EMA fast > EMA slow; (2) low < lowest low of previous M bars (current bar excluded) AND close > low + 0.5*range; (3) close > open AND body > k1*ATR; (4) HTF-bar, HTF-1 and HTF-2 all have close > open AND HTF-bar low > HTF-2 high; (5) HTF-1 close < open AND HTF-bar close > HTF-1 high; (6) OBV > OBV one bar earlier; (7) ATR > SMA of ATR over a longer lookback than the ATR length; (8) abs(close - (SwingHigh - 0.618*(SwingHigh - SwingLow))) < fixed price tolerance, where SwingHigh/SwingLow = highest high / lowest low over N1 bars including current; (9) low <= EMA fast AND close > EMA fast; (10) high > previous high AND previous high > high two bars ago AND close > EMA slow; (11) abs(low - previous low) < fixed price tolerance AND close > low; (12) close < (highest high + lowest low)/2 over N2 bars (N2 longer than N1); (13) close > open AND body > k2*ATR (k2 smaller than k1)
Calculation: ShortScore = count of true among: (1) EMA fast < EMA slow; (2) high > highest high of previous M bars (current excluded) AND close < high - 0.5*range; (3) close < open AND body > k1*ATR; (4) HTF-bar, HTF-1 and HTF-2 all have close < open AND HTF-bar high < HTF-2 low; (5) HTF-1 close > open AND HTF-bar close < HTF-1 low; (6) OBV < OBV one bar earlier; (7) ATR > SMA of ATR over a longer lookback than the ATR length; (8) abs(close - (SwingLow + 0.618*(SwingHigh - SwingLow))) < fixed price tolerance, same SwingHigh/SwingLow as above; (9) high >= EMA fast AND close < EMA fast; (10) low < previous low AND previous low < low two bars ago AND close < EMA slow; (11) abs(high - previous high) < fixed price tolerance AND close < high; (12) close > (highest high + lowest low)/2 over N2 bars; (13) close < open AND body > k2*ATR
Calculation: SessionOK = bar time, in a fixed local timezone, is NOT inside the early-morning (Asian session) hour window
Calculation: BiasBull / BiasBear = EMA fast > / < EMA slow (same periods as above) computed on a fixed timeframe equal to or lower than the chart timeframe, evaluated at the chart bar's close
Calculation: LongEntry = midpoint (high+low)/2 of the previous bar if it is bearish (close < open), otherwise of the bar two bars back (any color)
Calculation: ShortEntry = midpoint (high+low)/2 of the previous bar if it is bullish (close > open), otherwise of the bar two bars back (any color)
Long: (LongScore >= threshold, which is about 60% of the 13 conditions, AND SessionOK AND BiasBull) is true on this bar AND was false on the previous bar (first bar of the condition only); enter long as a limit order at LongEntry
Short: (ShortScore >= threshold, same as for long, AND SessionOK AND BiasBear) is true on this bar AND was false on the previous bar (first bar of the condition only); enter short as a limit order at ShortEntry
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance (fixed price distances, target distance larger than stop distance, about 3x)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance (same fixed distances)
