Calculation: Positive-volume index = start at 1000; when volume is greater than the prior bar's volume, add the prior index multiplied by the close percentage change; otherwise keep the prior index. Retail oscillator = 100 × (positive-volume index − its EMA) / max(rolling highest EMA − rolling lowest EMA, 1).
Calculation: Negative-volume index = start at 1000; when volume is less than the prior bar's volume, add the prior index multiplied by the close percentage change; otherwise keep the prior index. Whale oscillator = 100 × (negative-volume index − its EMA) / max(rolling highest EMA − rolling lowest EMA, 1).
Calculation: Fibonacci 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, and 61.8% levels = rolling highest high minus (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low) multiplied by 0.236, 0.382, 0.500, and 0.618, respectively.
Long: At least the configured threshold number of these four conditions are true, and fewer than the threshold were true on the prior bar: short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA; whale oscillator > retail oscillator OR (whale oscillator > its prior-bar value AND whale oscillator > 0); close <= Fibonacci 38.2% level AND close >= Fibonacci 61.8% level; MACD histogram > its prior-bar value.
Exit: long, at least the configured threshold number of these four conditions are true, and fewer than the threshold were true on the prior bar: short-term EMA < medium-term EMA; whale oscillator < retail oscillator OR (whale oscillator < its prior-bar value AND retail oscillator > its prior-bar value); close >= Fibonacci 23.6% level; MACD histogram < its prior-bar value.
Exit: long, close crosses below the medium-term EMA (current close < EMA AND prior-bar close >= prior-bar EMA).
