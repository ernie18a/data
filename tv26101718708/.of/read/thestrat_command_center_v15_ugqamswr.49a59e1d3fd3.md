Calculation: Failed downside bar = a completed bar whose low is below the previous bar's low, high is at or below the previous bar's high, and close is above its open.
Calculation: Failed upside bar = a completed bar whose high is above the previous bar's high, low is at or above the previous bar's low, and close is below its open.
Calculation: Structural entry levels = the preceding completed chart bar's high plus a configurable tick offset for long and its low minus that offset for short; use a minimum-tick offset for long after a failed downside bar and for short after a failed upside bar.
Calculation: Stop = at entry, select the setup bar's opposite extreme beyond which the corresponding entry offset is applied, a configurable distance from entry, or a configured price; freeze the selected level at entry.
Calculation: Broadening range = on a higher timeframe, search backward through completed bars for the most recent reference bar whose subsequent completed bars collectively have a higher high and a lower low than the reference; the range is the highest high and lowest low of those subsequent bars.
Calculation: Gap = on a higher timeframe, a completed bar's low above the high two bars earlier defines a bullish gap between those prices; its high below the low two bars earlier defines a bearish gap. Retire a bullish gap when chart low reaches its selected near edge, midpoint, or far edge; retire a bearish gap when chart high reaches the corresponding edge or midpoint. Exclude a gap from targets if a later completed higher-timeframe bar overlaps it and closes at or below the upper edge of a bullish gap or at or above the lower edge of a bearish gap. Retain only a configurable recent history of gaps.
Calculation: Structured target = select a configured eligible objective, or the nearest enabled eligible objective in the trade direction: broadening-range boundary or midpoint; one of a configurable number of most recent confirmed higher-timeframe swing highs for long or swing lows for short; an eligible gap; previous completed session high for long or low for short; or a zone inside the broadening range defined by configurable retracement fractions measured down from its high when the last completed higher-timeframe bar closes at or above its open, otherwise up from its low. Swing pivots require configurable numbers of bars on each side and are used only after confirmation. For a gap or zone, use the near edge if entry is outside it or the far edge if entry is inside it. Require the selected target to be strictly beyond entry in the trade direction.
Calculation: Target = at entry, select a configured multiple of the entry-to-stop distance, a configurable distance from entry, a configured price, or the structured target; freeze the selected level at entry.
Long: The completed chart bar is a failed downside bar.
Short: The completed chart bar is a failed upside bar.
Long: No active position AND no trade or outside setup was resolved on the current bar AND chart high is at or above the long structural entry level AND chart low is above the short structural entry level AND the long stop is below entry AND the long target is above entry AND, if continuity filtering is enabled and at least one timeframe is required, every required higher timeframe's still-forming bar has price above its open.
Short: No active position AND no trade or outside setup was resolved on the current bar AND chart low is at or below the short structural entry level AND chart high is below the long structural entry level AND the short stop is above entry AND the short target is below entry AND, if continuity filtering is enabled and at least one timeframe is required, every required higher timeframe's still-forming bar has price below its open.
Long: A configured long price is armed from a later chart bar AND no active position exists AND no trade was resolved on the current bar AND chart low is at or below that price AND chart high is at or above it AND the selected stop is below that price AND the selected target is above it AND the configured trade has not already completed.
Short: A configured short price is armed from a later chart bar AND no active position exists AND no trade was resolved on the current bar AND chart low is at or below that price AND chart high is at or above it AND the selected stop is above that price AND the selected target is below it AND the configured trade has not already completed.
Exit: long, chart low is at or below the frozen stop OR chart high is at or above the frozen target OR the configured maximum holding period has elapsed at a completed bar's close.
Exit: short, chart high is at or above the frozen stop OR chart low is at or below the frozen target OR the configured maximum holding period has elapsed at a completed bar's close.
Calculation: Exit execution = stop and target touches take precedence over a timed exit; a touch on the entry bar or both stop and target touched on the same historical bar closes the trade at an indeterminate price.
