Calculation: Zone score = 10 × weighted average of five 0–1 scores: (1 − ADX percentile rank), Bollinger width’s position inverted between its rolling low and high, (1 − percentile rank of the N-bar high-low range), (1 − volume percentile rank), and RSI balance max(0, 1 − |RSI − 50|/25); percentile ranks use the same rolling lookback, and each component uses its own configurable weight.
Calculation: Active zone = at least the configured minimum number of consecutive completed bars with zone score ≥ the minimum score; zone high/low are the highest high/lowest low since the run began. Extend the zone while score ≥ minimum score, high ≤ buy-stop, and low ≥ sell-stop, until its maximum duration.
Calculation: Buy-stop = zone high + buffer; sell-stop = zone low − buffer, where buffer is a configurable fraction of zone height, with a minimum of two ticks.
Calculation: Zone bias = compare bull and bear scores, each summing: range position and its inverse (weight 30), fraction of zone bars spent in upper and lower halves (25), fraction of top and bottom edge touches (25), and positive and negative RSI deviation from 50, capped at 25 points (20). Bias is up when bull score ≥ bear score; otherwise down.
Calculation: Trade risk = distance from entry to the opposite zone edge, zone midpoint, or configurable ATR multiple, according to the selected stop basis; minimum risk is 0.25 ATR. Stop-loss is one risk distance against the position. Targets are configurable risk multiples in the position direction.
Long: Zone bias is up OR an active zone’s first upward breakout occurs (close > buy-stop, or high > buy-stop when wick confirmation is selected); zone score met the minimum and the zone was not extended on this bar.
Short: Zone bias is down OR an active zone’s first downward breakout occurs (close < sell-stop, or low < sell-stop when wick confirmation is selected); zone score met the minimum and the zone was not extended on this bar.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop-loss OR high ≥ selected win target (TP1, TP2, or TP3) OR bars since entry ≥ maximum tracking duration.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop-loss OR low ≤ selected win target (TP1, TP2, or TP3) OR bars since entry ≥ maximum tracking duration.
