Calculation: Compression score = 10 times the weighted average of five components: one minus the percentile rank of ADX; inverse min-max normalization of Bollinger Band width over the percentile window, or zero if its range is zero; one minus the percentile rank of the high-low range over the Bollinger lookback; one minus the percentile rank of volume, or 0.5 when volume is not positive; and clamp(1 - abs(RSI - 50) / 25, 0, 1). Clamp each component and the final score to their valid ranges; use zero if the sum of weights is zero. Bollinger Band width is twice the Bollinger standard-deviation offset divided by the close SMA, or zero when that SMA is zero.
Calculation: Zone = after the minimum number of consecutive bars with compression score at least the threshold, set the zone high and low to the highest high and lowest low of those bars. On each subsequent bar, if its score remains at least the threshold, its high is at or below the previous buy stop, its low is at or above the previous sell stop, and its age from the first zone bar is less than the maximum zone age, extend the zone high and low with that bar. Otherwise test the previous stops for a breakout; retire the zone after a breakout or when its age reaches the maximum. Start a new search on the following bar.
Calculation: Zone stops = zone high plus buffer and zone low minus buffer, where buffer is the greater of a fraction of zone height and a minimum price increment buffer.
Calculation: Directional probability = for the qualifying zone bar and each extended zone bar, calculate position = (close - zone low) / max(zone height, minimum price increment), upper-half fraction = upper-half closes / max(all counted closes, 1), and top-touch fraction = top touches / max(all counted touches, 1). Count touches and closes only on extended bars, after updating the zone: a top touch has high at least zone high minus a fraction of zone height; a bottom touch has low at most zone low plus that fraction; an upper-half close has position greater than 0.5. Let momentum = clamp((RSI - 50) / 25, -1, 1). Bull points are weighted position, upper-half fraction, top-touch fraction, and positive momentum; bear points use their complements and negative momentum magnitude. Probability is the rounded percentage of bull points in total points, or 50 if total points are not positive.
Calculation: Trade risk = at breakout, the greater of an ATR fraction and the selected distance from entry to the opposite zone edge, the zone midpoint, or an ATR multiple. The entry price is the corresponding zone stop. Freeze risk at entry.
Long: Active zone AND directional probability >= 50.
Short: Active zone AND directional probability < 50.
Long: Active zone AND extension conditions fail AND selected close or high > previous buy stop.
Short: Active zone AND extension conditions fail AND selected close or low < previous sell stop AND the selected upward breakout condition is false.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + trade risk times the selected profit-target multiple OR low <= entry price - trade risk OR bars since entry >= maximum tracking bars.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - trade risk times the selected profit-target multiple OR high >= entry price + trade risk OR bars since entry >= maximum tracking bars.
