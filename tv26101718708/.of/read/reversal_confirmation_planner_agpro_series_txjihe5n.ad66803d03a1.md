Calculation: Common measures = ATR is the chart-timeframe average true range, falling back to true range if unavailable; floor ATR and high-low at a small positive price increment. Lower wick = min(open, close)-low; upper wick = high-max(open, close); wick ratios and body ratio divide the respective wick or abs(close-open) by the floored range. Close location = clamp((close-low)/range, 0, 1). Clamp all scores to their allowed range. Use a shorter and a longer EMA of close. Momentum turn = (shorter EMA minus its value at a momentum lookback)/ATR, using the current EMA if its historical value is unavailable. Volume ratio = volume divided by its moving average, or neutral if the average is nonpositive; volume slope = volume ratio minus its value at a shorter volume-slope lookback, using the current ratio if unavailable. Volume score = clamp(an affine function of volume ratio plus a weighted volume slope). The latest swing high and low are the most recent confirmed symmetric pivot high and low, respectively.
Calculation: Reaction candidates = A bullish sweep requires low below the latest swing low by more than an ATR-scaled minimum depth, close above that swing low, and close location at or above a sensitivity-dependent threshold. A bearish sweep mirrors these comparisons at the latest swing high. A bullish rejection requires rejection-only reactions enabled, (close below its momentum-lookback value OR close below the longer EMA), lower wick ratio at or above a sensitivity-dependent threshold, close location at or above its threshold, and close above open. A bearish rejection mirrors these comparisons using the upper wick. A bullish or bearish reaction is its corresponding sweep OR rejection. Sensitivity also sets the ideal sweep depth used in scoring.
Calculation: Reaction score = For each side, sweep score is the weighted sum of normalized sweep depth and directional close location; normalize sweep depth linearly between minimum and ideal depth and clamp it. Rejection score is the weighted sum of the directional wick ratio, directional close location, and body ratio when the candle closes in the reaction direction. Extension score is the clamped ATR-normalized distance from the longer EMA to the reaction-side extreme. Reaction score is the clamped weighted sum of the greater applicable sweep or rejection score, extension score, and volume score. If both sides react, select the higher reaction score, with a tie selecting bullish; otherwise select the reacting side.
Calculation: Active setup = Start the selected reaction when its score reaches the minimum and there is no active setup, the setup has failed or expired, the candidate is opposite and exceeds the active reaction score by an opposite-side replacement margin, OR the setup has never confirmed, the candidate exceeds its reaction score by a larger replacement margin, and its age exceeds a minimum replacement age. On replacement, reset all setup states. Freeze the reaction score and bar, confirmation line at reaction high plus an ATR buffer for bullish or reaction low minus that buffer for bearish, invalidation at reaction low minus an ATR buffer for bullish or reaction high plus that buffer for bearish, and target at the confirmation line plus or minus a multiple of the confirmation-to-invalidation distance. The reaction reference is the swept swing when a sweep exists; otherwise it is the reaction low plus a fraction of the lower wick for bullish or reaction high minus a fraction of the upper wick for bearish. Mark failure when close crosses beyond invalidation. Mark expiry when an unconfirmed, nonfailed setup age exceeds the confirmation window. After failure, expiry, or first target touch, deactivate the setup when the age of its first terminal event exceeds the terminal hold period; a target touch replaces the terminal-event bar. Evaluate replacement before these state updates on each bar.
Calculation: Confirmation score = Confirmation progress is the clamped directional distance from the frozen reference to close divided by the absolute reference-to-confirmation-line distance. Directional momentum score is a clamped affine function of momentum turn, with its sign reversed for bearish setups, plus one adjustment when close is on the directional side of the shorter EMA and another otherwise. Room is the lesser of absolute target-to-close distance and the distance to the latest opposite swing if that swing is ahead of close; otherwise it is target-to-close distance. Room ratio divides room by the frozen confirmation-to-invalidation distance. Room score is a clamped affine normalization of room ratio against preferred room. Confirmation score is the clamped weighted sum of frozen reaction score, confirmation progress, directional momentum score, volume score, and room score.
Calculation: Setup stages = A setup confirms when it was not previously confirmed, failed, or expired, close is beyond its confirmation line in the setup direction, and confirmation score reaches the confirmation threshold. Compute confirmation and expiry from the states at the start of the bar, then apply failure, expiry, and confirmation in that order. After confirmation, mark target touched on the first bar whose high reaches the bullish target or low reaches the bearish target, provided the setup has not failed. Stage priority is failed, expired, target touched, confirmed, confirming, building, reaction. Confirming requires close beyond the confirmation line and score at or above the watch threshold; building requires score at or above the watch threshold. The watch threshold is lower than the confirmation threshold. Evaluate decisions on completed chart bars.
Long: A selected bullish reaction starts an active setup.
Short: A selected bearish reaction starts an active setup.
Long: An active bullish setup has stage confirming, confirmed, building, or target touched.
Short: An active bearish setup has stage confirming, confirmed, building, or target touched.
