Calculation: Swing state for a lookback = initialize state to high; on each bar, if the high at the lookback offset is greater than the highest high of the intervening bars, set state to high; otherwise, if the low at that offset is less than the lowest low of the intervening bars, set state to low; otherwise retain state. A change to high confirms that offset bar as a swing high; a change to low confirms it as a swing low. Calculate independently for a longer and a shorter lookback; retain the latest confirmed high and low from each.
Calculation: Structure = initialize bearish. The first close above each newly confirmed longer-lookback swing high sets structure bullish and marks that high crossed; the first close below each newly confirmed longer-lookback swing low sets structure bearish and marks that low crossed. Reset the corresponding crossed flag when a new swing is confirmed. On a structure change, reset the running high and low to the current high and low and clear both shorter-swing crossed flags. After evaluating each bar's structure events, update the running high and low with that bar's high and low and retain their bar indices.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = structure is bullish AND low is below the latest shorter-lookback swing low AND that low differs from the latest longer-lookback swing low AND that shorter swing has not been crossed since the last reset, followed by close above the running high. Mark the shorter swing crossed on the low breach and clear that flag on the close break; both events may occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Bearish structure break = structure is bearish AND high is above the latest shorter-lookback swing high AND that high differs from the latest longer-lookback swing high AND that shorter swing has not been crossed since the last reset, followed by close below the running low. Mark the shorter swing crossed on the high breach and clear that flag on the close break; both events may occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = structure is bearish AND low is below the preceding running low AND close is above that running low AND the running low was established more than a minimum number of bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = structure is bullish AND high is above the preceding running high AND close is below that running high AND the running high was established more than a minimum number of bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = highest high over the recent lookback is greater than highest high over the preceding, twice-as-long lookback AND highest RSI over the recent lookback is less than highest RSI over that preceding lookback.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = lowest low over the recent lookback is less than lowest low over the preceding, twice-as-long lookback AND lowest RSI over the recent lookback is greater than lowest RSI over that preceding lookback.
Calculation: Shooting star = upper wick is greater than a multiple of candle body AND lower wick is less than another multiple of candle body AND high minus low is greater than a multiple of scaled ATR; candle body is absolute close minus open, upper wick is high minus the greater of open and close, and lower wick is the lesser of open and close minus low.
Calculation: Hammer = lower wick is greater than a multiple of candle body AND upper wick is less than another multiple of candle body AND high minus low is greater than a multiple of scaled ATR.
Calculation: Scaled ATR = ATR multiplied by its scale parameter.
Calculation: VWAP distance = round the percentage difference between close and session VWAP of typical price to the specified precision.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA and slower EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Long filters = each enabled filter passes: RSI is above its long minimum and below its hard maximum and either below its high threshold or rising for two consecutive bars without bearish RSI divergence; volume is above a multiple of its SMA; fast EMA is above slow EMA and close is above long-term EMA; ADX is above its minimum; close is above VWAP and rounded VWAP distance is at or below its hard maximum; higher-timeframe fast EMA is above its slower EMA; MACD histogram is above zero; and bar time is in either enabled session. The long direction must be enabled.
Calculation: Short filters = each enabled filter passes: RSI is below its short maximum and above its oversold minimum; volume is above a multiple of its SMA; fast EMA is below slow EMA and close is below long-term EMA; ADX is above its minimum; close is below VWAP; higher-timeframe fast EMA is below its slower EMA; MACD histogram is below zero; and bar time is in either enabled session. The short direction must be enabled.
Calculation: Long score = count the passing long RSI, volume, EMA, ADX, VWAP, higher-timeframe and MACD filters plus bullish structure; disabled filters count as passing.
Calculation: Exit signal count = count bearish RSI divergence, shooting star, volume above an exhaustion multiple of its SMA with close below open, MACD histogram below zero after being above zero on the preceding bar, first close below an uncrossed longer-lookback swing low, close below VWAP while long, higher-timeframe fast EMA at or below its slower EMA while long, RSI at or above its hard maximum, and rounded VWAP distance above its hard maximum.

Long: Bullish sweep AND long filters AND position is flat AND either the candle filter is disabled OR candle body is greater than zero AND lower wick divided by candle body is at least its minimum.
Short: Bearish structure break AND short filters AND position is flat.
Short: Bearish sweep AND short filters AND position is flat AND either the candle filter is disabled OR candle body is greater than zero AND upper wick divided by candle body is at least its minimum.

Short: A longer-lookback swing high is confirmed.

Short: Structure changes to bearish.

Short: Bearish structure break OR bearish sweep OR bearish RSI divergence OR shooting star OR rounded VWAP distance is above its hard maximum.

Short: Higher-timeframe fast EMA is at or below its slower EMA OR MACD histogram is at or below zero OR close is at or below VWAP.

Exit: long, low is at or below entry price minus the stop multiplier times scaled ATR fixed at entry OR high is at or above entry price plus the final target multiplier times scaled ATR fixed at entry; exit the configured partial quantity when partial profit taking is enabled, otherwise the full position.
Exit: short, high is at or above entry price plus the stop multiplier times scaled ATR fixed at entry OR low is at or below entry price minus the final target multiplier times scaled ATR fixed at entry; exit the configured partial quantity when partial profit taking is enabled, otherwise the full position.

Exit: long, rounded VWAP distance is above its lower extension threshold AND bearish RSI divergence OR bearish RSI divergence AND RSI is above its high threshold OR volume exceeds its exhaustion multiple of volume SMA AND close is below open AND rounded percentage profit from entry is above its gain threshold OR shooting star AND RSI is above its reversal threshold OR close is below VWAP OR higher-timeframe fast EMA is at or below its slower EMA OR MACD histogram is below zero after being above zero on the preceding bar AND rounded percentage profit from entry is above its MACD gain threshold; exit half.
Exit: long, structure is bearish AND none of the preceding sell or half-sell conditions applies AND no hold condition applies; hold conditions are positive rounded profit before the first target, first target reached, bullish structure with long filters and RSI below its hold threshold without bearish RSI divergence, bullish structure with RSI at or above its hold threshold and rising for two consecutive bars without bearish RSI divergence, or a doji with rounded profit above its gain threshold. A doji has body below a multiple of its range and range above a multiple of scaled ATR.
