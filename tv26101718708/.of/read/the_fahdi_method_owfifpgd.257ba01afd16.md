Calculation: Bullish structure break = after a bullish change of character, price sweeps below the latest short-term swing low, then closes above the highest high since the change of character
Calculation: Bearish structure break = after a bearish change of character, price sweeps above the latest short-term swing high, then closes below the lowest low since the change of character
Calculation: Bullish sweep = during bearish structure bias, low breaks below the lowest low since the bias began and close finishes above it
Calculation: Bearish sweep = during bullish structure bias, high breaks above the highest high since the bias began and close finishes below it
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = price makes a lower low over the recent lookback while RSI makes a higher low than over the preceding, equally long lookback
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher high over the recent lookback while RSI makes a lower high than over the preceding, equally long lookback
Calculation: Hammer = lower wick is more than twice the candle body, upper wick is less than half the body, and range exceeds half the ATR
Calculation: Shooting star = upper wick is more than twice the candle body, lower wick is less than half the body, and range exceeds half the ATR
Long: (bullish structure break OR (bullish sweep AND bearish structure bias AND lower wick / candle body ≥ minimum wick/body ratio) OR bullish RSI divergence OR hammer) AND RSI > long minimum AND RSI < hard-block level AND (RSI < 75 OR RSI rises for three consecutive bars AND no bearish RSI divergence) AND volume > volume moving average AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND ADX > minimum AND close > VWAP AND close is not above VWAP by more than the hard-extension threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND MACD histogram > 0
Short: (bearish structure break OR (bearish sweep AND bullish structure bias AND upper wick / candle body ≥ minimum wick/body ratio) OR bearish RSI divergence OR shooting star) AND RSI < short maximum AND RSI > oversold level AND volume > volume moving average AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND ADX > minimum AND close < VWAP AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND MACD histogram < 0
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss ATR multiple × ATR OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit ATR multiple × ATR OR (partial target enabled AND high ≥ entry price + 2 × ATR) OR bearish change of character

Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss ATR multiple × ATR OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit ATR multiple × ATR OR (partial target enabled AND low ≤ entry price − 2 × ATR) OR bullish change of character
