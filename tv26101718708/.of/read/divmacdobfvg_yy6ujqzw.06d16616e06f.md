Calculation: MACD histogram = fast EMA minus slow EMA, minus the simple moving average of that EMA difference.
Calculation: Confirmed histogram trough = previous bar’s histogram is below zero and below the histogram two bars earlier, current histogram is above the previous value, and the last completed lower-timeframe histogram is above its preceding value.
Calculation: Confirmed histogram peak = previous bar’s histogram is above zero and above the histogram two bars earlier, current histogram is below the previous value, and the last completed lower-timeframe histogram is below its preceding value.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed histogram pivot low is higher than the prior histogram pivot low while its corresponding price low is lower, and the current histogram pivot low is below zero; compare pivots only when their separation is within the configured minimum and maximum bar distances.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed histogram pivot high is lower than the prior histogram pivot high while its corresponding price high is higher, and the current histogram pivot high is above zero; compare pivots only when their separation is within the configured minimum and maximum bar distances.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high-to-low range of the lowest-low candle between the last confirmed price pivot high and a close above that pivot high; valid until a close falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Bearish order block = the high-to-low range of the highest-high candle between the last confirmed price pivot low and a close below that pivot low; valid until a close rises above its top.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier, the intervening candle closes above that earlier high, and the gap exceeds the configured ATR multiple; valid until a close falls below the gap bottom.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high is below the low two bars earlier, the intervening candle closes below that earlier low, and the gap exceeds the configured ATR multiple; valid until a close rises above the gap top.
Long: regular bullish divergence OR confirmed histogram trough OR bullish order block forms OR bullish fair value gap forms
Long: (regular bullish divergence OR confirmed histogram trough) AND price is inside or near a valid bullish order block or bullish fair value gap AND (MACD histogram is below zero and above its previous value OR a limit order reaches the zone’s upper edge)
Short: regular bearish divergence OR confirmed histogram peak OR bearish order block forms OR bearish fair value gap forms
Short: (regular bearish divergence OR confirmed histogram peak) AND price is inside or near a valid bearish order block or bearish fair value gap AND (MACD histogram is above zero and below its previous value OR a limit order reaches the zone’s lower edge)
Exit: long, close below the bullish order block bottom OR price reaches the first bearish order block or bearish fair value gap above OR confirmed histogram peak OR regular bearish divergence OR MACD histogram turns down below zero after being above zero OR the configured risk/reward target is reached
Exit: short, close above the bearish order block top OR price reaches the first bullish order block or bullish fair value gap below OR confirmed histogram trough OR regular bullish divergence OR MACD histogram turns up above zero after being below zero OR the configured risk/reward target is reached
