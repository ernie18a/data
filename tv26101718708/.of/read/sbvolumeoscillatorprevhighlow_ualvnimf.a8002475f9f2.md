Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (short-term EMA(volume) − long-term EMA(volume)) / long-term EMA(volume), with the short-term lookback shorter than the long-term lookback
Calculation: Positive peak = highest volume oscillator value during the most recently completed above-zero interval
Calculation: Negative trough = lowest volume oscillator value during the most recently completed below-zero interval
Long: Volume oscillator crosses above or below zero AND Positive peak ≤ absolute value of Negative trough
Short: Volume oscillator crosses above or below zero AND Positive peak > absolute value of Negative trough
