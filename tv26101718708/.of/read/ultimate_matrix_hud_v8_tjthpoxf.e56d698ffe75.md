Calculation: Long score = trend weight when the higher-timeframe fast EMA is above the higher-timeframe slow EMA and chart close is above the higher-timeframe fast EMA, plus momentum weight when RSI is above its midpoint and below its upper bound, plus volume weight when volume is above its SMA multiplied by a volume multiplier and close is above open; otherwise each contribution is zero. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in live calculations; with historical lookahead disabled, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar until the current one closes. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Short score = trend weight when the higher-timeframe fast EMA is below the higher-timeframe slow EMA and chart close is below the higher-timeframe fast EMA, plus momentum weight when RSI is below its midpoint and above its lower bound, plus volume weight when volume is above its SMA multiplied by a volume multiplier and close is below open; otherwise each contribution is zero. Use the same higher-timeframe bar and EMA lookbacks as the long score.
Calculation: Score parameters = the trend weight is greater than the equal momentum and volume weights; the strong threshold is greater than the weak threshold.
Long: Long score >= strong threshold OR (long score < strong threshold AND short score < strong threshold AND long score >= weak threshold AND long score > short score)
Short: (long score < strong threshold AND short score >= strong threshold) OR (long score < strong threshold AND short score < strong threshold AND (long score >= weak threshold OR short score >= weak threshold) AND short score >= long score)
Long: Long score - short score > positive net-score threshold
Short: Long score - short score < negative net-score threshold of the same magnitude
