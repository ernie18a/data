Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression endpoint of close minus the average of (1) the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the lookback and (2) the simple moving average of close over the same lookback, using that lookback for the regression.
Calculation: Prior range boundaries = previous bar's high-low midpoint plus and minus its high-low range divided by a configurable divisor.
Calculation: Moving averages = independently configurable moving averages of close with longer and shorter lookbacks; each can use the chart timeframe or another timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. A disabled moving average equals zero.
Long: datetime is strictly inside the enabled date boundaries AND position side is not long AND close > Parabolic SAR AND previous close <= previous Parabolic SAR AND close > longer moving average AND close > shorter moving average AND shorter moving average > longer moving average AND squeeze momentum > 0 AND (close > previous high-low midpoint OR (high - low > simple moving average of high - low AND close > upper prior range boundary AND volume > simple moving average of volume) OR (high > previous high AND high - low < simple moving average of high - low divided by a threshold AND volume < simple moving average of volume) OR (low < previous low AND high - low < simple moving average of high - low divided by the same threshold AND volume > simple moving average of volume)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold).
Short: datetime is strictly inside the enabled date boundaries AND position side is not short AND close <= Parabolic SAR AND previous close > previous Parabolic SAR AND close < longer moving average AND close < shorter moving average AND shorter moving average < longer moving average AND squeeze momentum < 0 AND ((high - low > simple moving average of high - low AND close < lower prior range boundary AND volume > simple moving average of volume) OR close < previous high-low midpoint) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold).
Exit: long, close < entry price multiplied by one minus the stop-loss percentage AND datetime is strictly inside the enabled date boundaries.
Exit: short, close > entry price multiplied by one plus the stop-loss percentage AND datetime is strictly inside the enabled date boundaries.
Exit: long, high >= entry price multiplied by one plus any of four take-profit percentages.
Exit: short, low <= entry price multiplied by one minus any of four take-profit percentages.
