Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high two bars earlier AND low minus that earlier high >= minimum gap size; its zone is from that earlier high to current low. Bearish FVG = high < low two bars earlier AND that earlier low minus high >= minimum gap size; its zone is from current high to that earlier low.
Calculation: Active FVG = the most recent FVG of the specified direction occurred no more than the lookback number of bars ago on its own timeframe. Alignment score = number of configured timeframes with an active FVG of that direction. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for lower timeframes use the last available intrabar. The configured timeframes span lower through higher timeframes; their relationship to the chart timeframe depends on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Retest = an active FVG on the designated zone timeframe AND chart close is within the most recent same-direction FVG zone, inclusive. Invalidation = that FVG is active AND chart close is below the bullish zone's lower boundary or above the bearish zone's upper boundary, respectively. A new event means the condition is true on the current chart bar and false on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Sequence = same-direction FVGs are active on the designated shorter and longer sequence timeframes AND bars since the shorter-timeframe FVG >= bars since the longer-timeframe FVG, with each count measured in its own timeframe's bars. The designated zone timeframe is the longer sequence timeframe.
Calculation: Swing high or low = the most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high or low, using equal numbers of bars on each side. Structure direction starts neutral, becomes bullish when close crosses above the current swing high, and becomes bearish when close crosses below the current swing low; compare the previous close with the current swing level. A bullish or bearish change of character is the corresponding crossing when the prior structure direction was opposite.
Calculation: Session sweeps = during the configured London session, record whether high exceeds the previous completed Asia session high and whether low falls below the previous completed Asia session low; reset both records at the start of each London session. The session filter passes long unless the current bar is in the configured New York session and only the high was swept; it passes short unless the current bar is in that session and only the low was swept. The filter can be disabled.
Calculation: Entry-used state = separately for each direction, reset to false when the latest zone's upper boundary changes from a previously available value or a new same-direction invalidation occurs; set to true on a same-direction entry. Apply resets before testing entries.
Long: A bullish FVG forms on the chart timeframe.
Short: A bearish FVG forms on the chart timeframe.
Long: A new bullish retest AND bullish alignment score >= threshold AND bullish alignment score > bearish alignment score AND the configured entry-time filter passes or is disabled AND the session filter passes or is disabled AND structure direction is neutral or bullish or the structure filter is disabled AND bullish entry-used state is false.
Short: A new bearish retest AND bearish alignment score >= threshold AND bearish alignment score > bullish alignment score AND the configured entry-time filter passes or is disabled AND the session filter passes or is disabled AND structure direction is neutral or bearish or the structure filter is disabled AND bearish entry-used state is false.
Long: Bullish sequence AND bullish alignment score >= threshold AND bullish alignment score > bearish alignment score AND bullish retest is false AND the configured entry-time filter passes or is disabled AND the combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar AND bullish entry-used state is false after processing entries.
Short: Bearish sequence AND bearish alignment score >= threshold AND bearish alignment score > bullish alignment score AND bearish retest is false AND the configured entry-time filter passes or is disabled AND the combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar AND bearish entry-used state is false after processing entries.
Long: Low < swing low AND close > swing low AND the same condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Short: High > swing high AND close < swing high AND the same condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Long: Close > swing high AND previous close <= current swing high.
Short: Close < swing low AND previous close >= current swing low.
Long: A bullish FVG occurred within a short displacement lookback on either of two designated higher timeframes AND a bullish FVG is active on either of two still higher timeframes AND this combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Short: A bearish FVG occurred within a short displacement lookback on either of two designated higher timeframes AND a bearish FVG is active on either of two still higher timeframes AND this combined condition was false on the preceding chart bar.
Long: The London low sweep record is true AND the London high sweep record is false.
Short: The London high sweep record is true AND the London low sweep record is false.
Exit: long, a new bullish FVG invalidation occurs.
Exit: short, a new bearish FVG invalidation occurs.
