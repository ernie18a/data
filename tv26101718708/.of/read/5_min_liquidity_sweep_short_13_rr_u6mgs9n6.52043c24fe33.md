Calculation: Current day's running high = maximum high of chart-timeframe bars from the start of the current day through the current bar (higher timeframe, still forming)
Calculation: Short stop = high of the entry signal bar, frozen at entry
Calculation: Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × target multiple, frozen at entry
Short: previous close > previous open AND previous high >= previous bar's current day's running high AND current close < previous open AND current high > previous high AND current high > current close AND position is flat
Exit: Short, current high >= short stop OR current low <= short target
