Calculation: Premarket high = highest high during the premarket session on lower-timeframe bars, including the current/forming bar
Calculation: Premarket low = lowest low during the premarket session on lower-timeframe bars, including the current/forming bar
Calculation: Previous day's high = high of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Calculation: Previous day's low = low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Long: close > premarket high AND close > previous day's high
Short: close < premarket low AND close < previous day's low
