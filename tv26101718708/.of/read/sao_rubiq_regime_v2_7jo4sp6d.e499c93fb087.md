Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = the high or low of a bar that satisfies the centered pivot test with equal left and right lookbacks; evaluate it only when the right-hand bars have completed. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low on the same chart bar. Compare each pivot with the previous pivot of the same type before updating that previous pivot.
Calculation: Pivot level match = maintain one shared FIFO list of levels for highs and lows. For each confirmed pivot, select the closest existing level within a tolerance equal to a multiplier of current chart-bar ATR; break equal-distance ties in favor of the oldest level. A matched level retains its original price and first pivot bar and increments its touch count. Otherwise add a level at the pivot price with one touch, removing the oldest level first if the capacity is reached. A bounce occurs when the resulting touch count is at least two and the current pivot bar minus the level's first pivot bar is at most the maximum age. If both pivot types bounce on the same chart bar, only the low bounce is retained.
Calculation: Effective trend = maintain the latest high and low pivot classifications and a persistent trend initially unknown. A high is higher if strictly above the previous high and lower otherwise; a low is lower if strictly below the previous low and higher otherwise. The first high and low have unclassified labels. On any new pivot, set the trend to up if the latest high is higher and latest low is higher, down if the latest high is lower and latest low is lower, and range otherwise if both labels exist. A bounce sets a reversal in the direction of the bouncing low or high for a configured number of chart bars, including the current bar; a new bounce resets the duration. When the underlying trend changes to up or down, cancel the reversal. While a reversal remains active it overrides the underlying trend.
Calculation: Stress = normalized realized volatility exceeds its threshold OR ATR deviation exceeds its threshold. Realized volatility is the standard deviation of close-to-close log returns over a short lookback multiplied by the square root of that lookback; normalize it by its rolling median over a longer lookback. ATR deviation is ATR minus its rolling median, divided by its rolling standard deviation, using the same longer lookback. Treat an unavailable normalized value as zero.
Calculation: RSI agreement = count of RSI values across a set of successively longer timeframes that strictly exceed their respective bullish thresholds, or strictly fall below their respective bearish thresholds. Use the same RSI length on all timeframes; thresholds are specific to each timeframe and direction. For a higher timeframe use its last completed bar, for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, and for the chart timeframe use the chart bar. Substitute zero for unavailable bullish RSI and the neutral RSI midpoint for unavailable bearish RSI.
Calculation: Committed regime = on each chart bar classify the raw regime as stress if stress is true, otherwise bullish if effective trend is up and bullish RSI agreement meets its minimum, otherwise bearish if effective trend is down and bearish RSI agreement meets its minimum, otherwise other. Initially the committed regime is other; replace it with the current raw regime only after that same raw regime persists for the configured number of consecutive chart bars, retaining the previous committed regime until then.
Long: Committed regime = bullish
Short: Committed regime = bearish
Long: A pivot high is confirmed AND a previous pivot high exists AND the pivot high > the previous pivot high
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND (no previous pivot high exists OR the pivot high <= the previous pivot high)
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND (no previous pivot low exists OR the pivot low >= the previous pivot low)
Short: A pivot low is confirmed AND a previous pivot low exists AND the pivot low < the previous pivot low
Long: A confirmed pivot low produces a bounce
Short: A confirmed pivot high produces a bounce AND no confirmed pivot low on the same chart bar produces a bounce
