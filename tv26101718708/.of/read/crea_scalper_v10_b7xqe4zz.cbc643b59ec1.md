Calculation: Use fast, middle, and slow close EMAs in increasing order of lookback. A cross above means the fast EMA is above the middle EMA and was at or below it on the previous bar; a cross below is the reverse.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = simple moving average of stochastic %K calculated from close, high, and low. Body = absolute close minus open; lower wick = minimum of open and close minus low; upper wick = high minus maximum of open and close.
Calculation: Daily VWAP and its deviation = the volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of typical price, accumulated from the start of the day through the current bar; if cumulative volume is zero, use typical price as the mean and zero as the deviation.
Calculation: Delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low. Cumulative delta resets to zero on the first bar of each day and adds delta on subsequent bars.
Calculation: Long candle = (close > open AND close > previous close AND open < previous close) OR (lower wick > a multiple of body AND lower wick > a multiple of upper wick) OR (close > open AND body > a multiple of the simple moving average of body). Short candle = (close < open AND open >= previous close AND close < previous close) OR (upper wick > a multiple of body AND upper wick > a multiple of lower wick) OR (close < open AND body > a multiple of the simple moving average of body). Each pattern can be independently enabled.
Calculation: Long divergence and short divergence are always false: each confirmed price pivot replaces the stored price pivot before being compared strictly against that same pivot.
Calculation: Long score = count of enabled true conditions: (1) fast EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA; (2) RSI > its bullish threshold AND MACD histogram > zero AND, optionally, histogram > previous histogram; (3) volume > a multiple of its simple moving average AND, optionally, close > daily VWAP; (4) smoothed stochastic was <= its oversold threshold within the memory window AND is now > that threshold AND < its upper recovery limit AND close > middle EMA; (5) long candle; (6) long divergence; (7) fast EMA crossed above middle EMA within the freshness window AND middle EMA > slow EMA. The cross layer can be disabled.
Calculation: Short score = count of enabled true conditions: (1) close < fast EMA AND close < middle EMA; (2) RSI < its bearish threshold AND MACD histogram < zero AND, optionally, histogram < previous histogram; (3) volume > a multiple of its simple moving average AND, optionally, close < daily VWAP; (4) smoothed stochastic was >= its overbought threshold within the memory window AND is now < that threshold AND > its lower recovery limit AND close < middle EMA; (5) short candle; (6) short divergence; (7) fast EMA crossed below middle EMA within the freshness window AND middle EMA < slow EMA. The cross layer can be disabled.
Calculation: Long exhaustion = enabled AND (RSI >= upper exhaustion threshold OR smoothed stochastic >= upper exhaustion threshold OR close >= daily VWAP plus a deviation multiple OR short divergence). Short exhaustion = enabled AND (RSI <= lower exhaustion threshold OR smoothed stochastic <= lower exhaustion threshold OR close <= daily VWAP minus a deviation multiple OR long divergence).
Calculation: Volatility pass = volatility filter disabled OR ATR divided by its simple moving average >= lower threshold AND <= upper threshold. Higher-timeframe long pass = filter disabled OR fast higher-timeframe close EMA > slow higher-timeframe close EMA. Higher-timeframe short pass = filter disabled OR strict short filtering disabled OR fast higher-timeframe close EMA < slow higher-timeframe close EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Long and short states are tracked separately. Initially idle. On each bar, in order: reset setup to idle if its score < score threshold minus one; enter setup from idle if its score >= threshold AND its exhaustion is false; while in setup remember whether smoothed stochastic is below its midpoint for long or above its midpoint for short; arm if score >= threshold AND bars since setup began >= setup duration AND optional remembered pullback is satisfied; reset armed to idle if bars since arming > expiry; reset a previously triggered state to idle. A final signal sets its side to triggered after both final signals are evaluated. State updates occur even when the state-machine gate is disabled.
Calculation: Common long gates = long exhaustion is false AND close <= daily VWAP plus the outer-band deviation multiple AND volatility pass AND higher-timeframe long pass AND bars since the most recent final signal of either side >= cooldown. Common short gates = short exhaustion is false AND close >= daily VWAP minus the outer-band deviation multiple AND volatility pass AND higher-timeframe short pass AND the same cooldown. Initialize the last-signal bar to a bar index before the start of the data.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above middle EMA AND middle EMA > slow EMA.
Short: Fast EMA crosses below middle EMA AND middle EMA < slow EMA.
Long: High > highest high of the preceding lookback bars.
Short: Low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars.
Long: Low > high two bars earlier AND low minus that earlier high > an ATR multiple.
Short: High < low two bars earlier AND that earlier low minus high > an ATR multiple.
Long: Delta > zero.
Short: Delta <= zero.
Long: Cumulative delta > zero.
Short: Cumulative delta <= zero.
Long: Positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator.
Short: Positive directional indicator <= negative directional indicator.
Long: Money flow index <= oversold threshold OR money flow index > its midpoint AND money flow index < overbought threshold.
Short: Money flow index >= overbought threshold OR money flow index <= its midpoint AND money flow index > oversold threshold.
Long: (Long score = score threshold minus one OR long state = armed) AND volatility pass AND long exhaustion is false.
Short: (Short score = score threshold minus one OR short state = armed) AND volatility pass AND short exhaustion is false.
Long: Common long gates AND ((fast path enabled AND cross layer enabled AND fast EMA crossed above middle EMA within the freshness window AND middle EMA > slow EMA AND long score excluding the cross layer >= fast-path confirmation threshold) OR ((state machine disabled OR long state = armed AND long candle) AND long score >= score threshold AND short score < score threshold AND (structure gate disabled OR high > highest high of preceding lookback bars))).
Short: Common short gates AND ((fast path enabled AND cross layer enabled AND fast EMA crossed below middle EMA within the freshness window AND middle EMA < slow EMA AND short score excluding the cross layer >= fast-path confirmation threshold) OR ((state machine disabled OR short state = armed AND short candle) AND short score >= score threshold AND long score < score threshold AND (structure gate disabled OR low < lowest low of preceding lookback bars))).
Exit: long, high >= entry price plus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the base stop multiplier and first target multiplier OR low <= entry price minus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the base stop multiplier.
Exit: short, low <= entry price minus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the base stop multiplier and first target multiplier OR high >= entry price plus the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the base stop multiplier.
