Calculation: Bullish confirmation count = number of true conditions among: EMA fast > EMA medium > EMA slow and close > EMA fast; RSI > its bullish threshold and MACD histogram > 0 and (if enabled) greater than its prior-bar value; volume > its moving average × multiplier and (if enabled) close > session VWAP; stochastic recently visited oversold and is now above oversold but below its recovery cap, with close > EMA medium; bullish engulfing candle OR bullish pinbar OR bullish momentum candle; bullish RSI divergence; fresh bullish EMA-fast/EMA-medium cross with EMA-medium > EMA-slow.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation count = number of true conditions among: EMA fast < EMA medium < EMA slow and close < EMA fast and close < EMA medium; RSI < its bearish threshold and MACD histogram < 0 and (if enabled) less than its prior-bar value; volume > its moving average × multiplier and (if enabled) close < session VWAP; stochastic recently visited overbought and is now below overbought but above its recovery cap, with close < EMA medium; bearish engulfing candle OR bearish pinbar OR bearish momentum candle; bearish RSI divergence; fresh bearish EMA-fast/EMA-medium cross with EMA-medium < EMA-slow.
Calculation: Fresh EMA cross = EMA fast crossed EMA medium within the configured freshness window; bullish cross is a cross above and bearish cross is a cross below.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while RSI at that pivot is higher than RSI at the preceding price pivot low; bearish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high while RSI at that pivot is lower than RSI at the preceding price pivot high.
Calculation: Bullish candle trigger = close > open and close > prior close and open < prior close, OR lower wick > 2 × body and > 2 × upper wick, OR close > open and body > its moving average × multiplier; bearish candle trigger = close < open and open >= prior close and close < prior close, OR upper wick > 2 × body and > 2 × lower wick, OR close < open and body > its moving average × multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = current high > highest high of prior lookback bars; bearish BOS = current low < lowest low of prior lookback bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA fast > EMA slow for bullish trend or EMA fast < EMA slow for bearish trend, calculated on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
Calculation: Exhaustion block = for longs, RSI >= its overbought limit OR stochastic >= its upper limit OR close >= session VWAP + configured standard-deviation multiple × session VWAP standard deviation OR bearish RSI divergence; for shorts, RSI <= its oversold limit OR stochastic <= its lower limit OR close <= session VWAP - configured standard-deviation multiple × session VWAP standard deviation OR bullish RSI divergence.
