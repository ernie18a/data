Calculation: Sweep bars = chart-timeframe bars or higher-timeframe bars; evaluate each last completed sweep bar once when a new sweep bar begins.
Calculation: Active levels = confirmed sweep-bar pivot highs and lows, tracked separately. A pivot has a parameterized equal number of bars on each side: its high is strictly greater than all earlier-side highs and greater than or equal to all later-side highs; its low is strictly less than all earlier-side lows and less than or equal to all later-side lows. Add newly confirmed pivots before testing sweeps, retaining only the most recently confirmed parameterized maximum number per side. Remove a high level whenever a sweep bar's high exceeds it by more than the configured penetration; remove a low level whenever a sweep bar's low falls below it by more than the configured penetration, whether or not the other decision conditions pass. Process high levels before low levels.
Calculation: Penetration = zero when the penetration filter is disabled; otherwise a parameterized multiple of the sweep bar's ATR, using zero if ATR is unavailable.
Long: The last completed sweep bar's low is below an active low level minus penetration AND either close-back is disabled or its close is above that level AND either displacement rejection is disabled, or its close is at or above the previous sweep bar's low for loose rejection or at or above the previous sweep bar's high-low midpoint for strict rejection AND either the session filter is disabled or the sweep bar's opening time is inside a configured session.
Short: The last completed sweep bar's high is above an active high level plus penetration AND either close-back is disabled or its close is below that level AND either displacement rejection is disabled, or its close is at or below the previous sweep bar's high for loose rejection or at or below the previous sweep bar's high-low midpoint for strict rejection AND either the session filter is disabled or the sweep bar's opening time is inside a configured session.
