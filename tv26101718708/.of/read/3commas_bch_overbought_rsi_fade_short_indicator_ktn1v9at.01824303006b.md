Calculation: RSI crossunder = RSI on the selected timeframe is below the threshold AND its previous bar's RSI is at or above the threshold. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical data and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar available in each chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Averaging ladder = for the next unfilled order k, its deviation is the sum of the first k steps, starting with the first deviation and multiplying each subsequent step by the step multiplier. Its quote size is the first averaging-order size multiplied by the size multiplier to the power k−1. Fill at the chart close, at most one order per chart bar.
Calculation: Average entry = total quote size of the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order's quote size divided by its fill price. The base entry fills at the signal bar's close. Process the base entry, averaging order, and exit in that order on each bar.
Calculation: Trailing minimum = after entry, arm the trail when close is at or below average entry multiplied by one minus the take-profit fraction. While armed, update the minimum only on bars whose close is at or below that bar's take-profit price, using the lowest such close since arming. Reset it on exit.
Short: Position is flat AND RSI crossunder is true AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR position is flat AND RSI crossunder is true AND bar datetime is within the active window.
Short: Position is short AND filled averaging-order count is below the configured maximum AND close is at or above base entry multiplied by one plus the next averaging-ladder deviation AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR position is short AND filled averaging-order count is below the configured maximum AND close is at or above base entry multiplied by one plus the next averaging-ladder deviation AND bar datetime is within the active window.
Exit: short, close is at or above average entry multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR close is at or above average entry multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction AND bar datetime is within the active window.
Exit: short, close is below the stop-loss price AND the trail is armed AND close is at or above trailing minimum multiplied by one plus the trailing fraction AND the optional active-window filter is disabled OR close is below the stop-loss price AND the trail is armed AND close is at or above trailing minimum multiplied by one plus the trailing fraction AND bar datetime is within the active window.
