Calculation: Fast range position = EMA of 100 × (close − highest high over a short lookback) ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback); use a shorter lookback and faster smoothing than the slow range position.
Calculation: Slow range position = EMA of 100 × (close − highest high over a longer lookback) ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback).
Calculation: Trend score (up) = count of these conditions: long EMA slope over a lookback is positive by at least an ATR-scaled threshold; consecutive closes above the long EMA meet a minimum; ADX meets a minimum and +DI > −DI; the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and that EMA is rising; slow range position > −50.
Calculation: Trend score (down) = count of these conditions: long EMA slope over a lookback is negative by at least an ATR-scaled threshold; consecutive closes below the long EMA meet a minimum; ADX meets a minimum and −DI > +DI; the last completed higher-timeframe close is below its EMA and that EMA is falling; slow range position < −50.
Calculation: Uptrend = close > long EMA and trend score (up) meets the minimum.
Calculation: Downtrend = close < long EMA and trend score (down) meets the minimum.
Calculation: Large counter-candle block (long) = largest bearish candle body over a recent lookback is at least an ATR-scaled threshold.
Calculation: Large counter-candle block (short) = largest bullish candle body over a recent lookback is at least an ATR-scaled threshold.
Long: Uptrend AND fast range position crosses above its lower threshold after being at or below it AND an eligible bullish candle (close > open) occurs within the confirmation window while uptrend persists and fast range position remains above the lower threshold AND no large counter-candle block (long) AND the long signal is armed AND cooldown and opposite-side lock have expired; OR fast and slow range positions both remain at the lower extreme for the minimum stretch, then leave that extreme while ADX is below its maximum, and within the confirmation window close > the high of the first bar outside the extreme AND no large counter-candle block (long) AND cooldown and opposite-side lock have expired.
Short: Downtrend AND fast range position crosses below its upper threshold after being at or above it AND an eligible bearish candle (close < open) occurs within the confirmation window while downtrend persists and fast range position remains below the upper threshold AND no large counter-candle block (short) AND the short signal is armed AND cooldown and opposite-side lock have expired; OR fast and slow range positions both remain at the upper extreme for the minimum stretch, then leave that extreme while ADX is below its maximum, and within the confirmation window close < the low of the first bar outside the extreme AND no large counter-candle block (short) AND cooldown and opposite-side lock have expired.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR (first target has been reached AND (low <= entry price OR high >= second target)).
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR (first target has been reached AND (high >= entry price OR low <= second target)).
