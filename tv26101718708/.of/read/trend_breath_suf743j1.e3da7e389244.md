Calculation: Prices = either chart-timeframe OHLC or Heikin Ashi OHLC; use the same choice for every calculation. For Heikin Ashi, close = (open + high + low + close) / 4, open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close) / 2, high = max(high, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close), and low = min(low, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close).
Calculation: Range oscillator = EMA of 100 × (close − highest high) / (highest high − lowest low) over a lookback; use −50 when highest high equals lowest low. The fast oscillator has a shorter range lookback and a longer EMA smoothing period than the slow oscillator.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = higher-timeframe close and its EMA and previous EMA, using higher-timeframe bars with lookahead off, then shifting the resulting chart-timeframe series by one chart bar. Thus the values are from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with a one-chart-bar delay when a higher-timeframe bar completes. The higher-timeframe EMA has a shorter period than the chart-timeframe trend EMA.
Calculation: Trend score = count of five conditions: ATR-normalized change in the trend EMA over a lookback is at least a positive slope threshold for long or at most its negative for short; consecutive closes above the trend EMA for long or below it for short reach a persistence threshold; ADX reaches a strength threshold and +DI > −DI for long or −DI > +DI for short; higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA > its previous value for long, or close < EMA < its previous value for short; slow oscillator > −50 for long or < −50 for short. When ATR is zero, normalized EMA change is zero.
Calculation: Trend qualification = close > trend EMA AND long trend score >= score threshold for long, or close < trend EMA AND short trend score >= score threshold for short.
Calculation: Counter-candle block = highest max(open − close, 0) over a lookback >= current ATR × a threshold for long, or highest max(close − open, 0) over that lookback >= current ATR × the same threshold for short.
Calculation: Pullback candidate = for long, fast oscillator crosses above the lower pullback threshold while long trend qualifies and the long side is armed; for short, fast oscillator crosses below the upper pullback threshold while short trend qualifies and the short side is armed. Both sides start armed; the long side rearms when fast oscillator >= upper pullback threshold, and the short side rearms when fast oscillator <= lower pullback threshold. A candidate remains pending through a confirmation window including its creation bar; a new candidate replaces the prior candidate of that side. Cancel it before testing confirmation if the window has expired, its trend no longer qualifies, or the oscillator returns to or beyond the crossed threshold. Otherwise consume it on the first bullish candle without a long counter-candle block, or bearish candle without a short counter-candle block, respectively, even if a later signal filter rejects it. Any accepted signal disarms its side.
Calculation: Exhaustion candidate = both oscillators remain at or above an upper extreme threshold for a minimum consecutive duration, then at least one falls below it while ADX < a maximum; create a pending short candidate with the exit bar's low as trigger. For long, both oscillators remain at or below a lower extreme threshold for the minimum duration, then at least one rises above it while ADX < the maximum; use the exit bar's high as trigger. Store the highest high or lowest low, respectively, of the completed extreme run. Starting on the next bar, cancel a pending candidate before confirmation if its confirmation window has expired or both oscillators have returned to its extreme zone; otherwise consume it on the first close below the short trigger without a short counter-candle block or above the long trigger without a long counter-candle block, even if a later signal filter rejects it. A new extreme-zone exit replaces any pending candidate of that side after that bar's pending-candidate check.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = bars since the last accepted same-side signal > same-side cooldown AND bars since the last accepted opposite-side signal > opposite-side lock; a missing prior signal satisfies its respective condition. Evaluate both sides using the prior accepted signals; reject both if both raw sides qualify on the same bar. Only accepted signals update cooldowns and disarm a side.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a pullback long, min(lowest low over a swing lookback − ATR × stop buffer, entry price − ATR × minimum stop distance); for an exhaustion long, replace lowest low with the stored extreme-run low. For a pullback short, max(highest high over the swing lookback + ATR × stop buffer, entry price + ATR × minimum stop distance); for an exhaustion short, replace highest high with the stored extreme-run high. If both candidate types confirm on one side, use the pullback stop. Risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop; first and second targets are entry price plus risk × their respective reward multiples for long, or minus those amounts for short; the second multiple is larger.
Long: (long pullback candidate confirms OR long exhaustion candidate confirms) AND long signal eligibility AND NOT simultaneously qualifying short raw signal.
Short: (short pullback candidate confirms OR short exhaustion candidate confirms) AND short signal eligibility AND NOT simultaneously qualifying long raw signal.
Exit: long, after the signal bar AND before the first target has been reached AND low <= initial stop, OR after the signal bar AND before the first target has been reached AND low > initial stop AND high >= second target, OR after a prior bar reached the first target without reaching the second target AND (low <= entry price OR high >= second target).
Exit: short, after the signal bar AND before the first target has been reached AND high >= initial stop, OR after the signal bar AND before the first target has been reached AND high < initial stop AND low <= second target, OR after a prior bar reached the first target without reaching the second target AND (high >= entry price OR low <= second target).
