Calculation: Confirmed swing high and low = pivot high and pivot low with equal left and right strengths, available only on confirmation; retain the most recently confirmed value of each, including pivots confirmed on the current bar.
Calculation: Structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed structure swing high for an upward break, or below the latest confirmed structure swing low for a downward break; a cross compares the current close and level with their respective previous-bar values.
Calculation: Structure trend = direction of the most recent structure break, initially neutral; if both breaks occur on one bar, the downward break sets the final trend.
Calculation: Order block = on a structure break, take the high and low of the most recent opposite-direction candle within a lookback ending at the previous bar; a bullish break creates a demand block from a down-close candle, and a bearish break creates a supply block from an up-close candle. If both breaks occur, create only a demand block. A block is active from its creation bar through its first bar whose low is at or below its high and whose high is at or above its low; remove it after evaluating that bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = the previous bar's low is below the latest confirmed sweep swing low AND the current close is above that level AND the current close is above the current open AND the current close is above the previous close AND the current close minus the previous low is greater than ATR multiplied by a threshold AND, if the volume filter is enabled, current volume is greater than its SMA.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = the previous bar's high is above the latest confirmed sweep swing high AND the current close is below that level AND the current close is below the current open AND the current close is below the previous close AND the previous high minus the current close is greater than ATR multiplied by a threshold AND, if the volume filter is enabled, current volume is greater than its SMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep direction = on a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar's bullish sweep as +1, otherwise its bearish sweep as -1, otherwise 0; bullish takes precedence if both occur. Calculate independently for each of two higher timeframes.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed structure swing high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed structure swing low.
Long: An active demand block is touched AND structure trend is bullish AND close is greater than open AND, if the higher-timeframe bias filter is enabled, the shorter-period EMA of close is greater than the longer-period EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND, if the chop filter is enabled, the absolute difference between the latest confirmed structure swing high and low is greater than ATR multiplied by a threshold; use zero for this difference if either swing is unavailable.
Short: An active supply block is touched AND structure trend is bearish AND close is less than open AND, if the higher-timeframe bias filter is enabled, the shorter-period EMA of close is less than the longer-period EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND, if the chop filter is enabled, the absolute difference between the latest confirmed structure swing high and low is greater than ATR multiplied by a threshold; use zero for this difference if either swing is unavailable.
Long: Bullish sweep on the chart timeframe.
Short: Bearish sweep on the chart timeframe.
Long: Higher-timeframe sweep direction is +1 AND its value on the preceding chart bar is different or unavailable.
Short: Higher-timeframe sweep direction is -1 AND its value on the preceding chart bar is different or unavailable.
