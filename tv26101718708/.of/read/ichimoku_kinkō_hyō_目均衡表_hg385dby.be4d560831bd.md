Calculation: Tenkan-Sen and Kijun-Sen = each is the average of (1) the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback and (2) the same midpoint over a shorter lookback; Tenkan uses a shorter lookback than Kijun. The input price may be a configured OHLC-derived source, optionally smoothed by a 4-bar symmetrically weighted moving average.
Calculation: Present Kumo = the maximum and minimum, respectively, of Senkou A and Senkou B calculated the configured offset bars earlier; Senkou A is the average of Tenkan-Sen and Kijun-Sen, and Senkou B is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Chikou breakout = input price > the highest high, or < the lowest low, over a configured lookback, with that high/low range taken from the configured Chikou offset bars earlier.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = the configured volume oscillator (TFS, OBV, Klinger, cumulative-volume, or volume-zone); compare it with a configured breakout threshold. For the bearish filter, the comparison is oscillator < threshold; for the bullish filter, oscillator > threshold.
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR over a shorter lookback > ATR over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Flat-market block = ADX-like trend strength over a configured lookback < its configured threshold AND Kumo thickness as a percentage of price < its configured threshold.
Long: Warm-up complete AND flat-market block is false AND ((Tenkan-Sen = Kijun-Sen AND previous Tenkan-Sen < previous Kijun-Sen) OR every enabled bullish filter is true) AND bullish condition was false on the previous bar AND current regime is not long. Bullish filters: volume oscillator > threshold; ATR expansion; Tenkan-Sen > Kijun-Sen; input price > input price at the prior Chikou-offset bar; current input price > input price at the Chikou-offset bar; input price > top of Present Kumo; input price > Tenkan-Sen; Chikou breakout is upward.
Short: Warm-up complete AND flat-market block is false AND ((Tenkan-Sen = Kijun-Sen AND previous Tenkan-Sen > previous Kijun-Sen) OR every enabled bearish filter is true) AND bearish condition was false on the previous bar AND current regime is not short. Bearish filters: volume oscillator < threshold; ATR expansion; Tenkan-Sen < Kijun-Sen; input price < input price at the prior Chikou-offset bar; current input price < input price at the Chikou-offset bar; input price < top of Present Kumo; input price < Tenkan-Sen; Chikou breakout is downward.
Long: Confirmed swing low, where the low is lower than the lows on the configured number of bars on both sides.
Short: Confirmed swing high, where the high is higher than the highs on the configured number of bars on both sides.
