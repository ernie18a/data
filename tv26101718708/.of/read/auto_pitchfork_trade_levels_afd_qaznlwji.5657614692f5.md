Calculation: Confirmed pivots = For each enabled layer, independently use its swing length; the optional second layer uses a longer swing length. A pivot high or low is confirmed after that many chart bars on each side. Require its high or low to be strictly more extreme than all neighbors, or, under the latest-equal option, allow equality only with earlier neighbors. Reject a bar that qualifies as both a high and a low. Retain alternating pivots within a lookback horizon and a maximum count; when consecutive pivots have the same type, retain the more extreme one, or the later equal one under the latest-equal option. Evaluate a candidate only when a new pivot is retained. Its newest pivot is P2; choose P1 and P0 from the retained alternating sequence, either using the latest triple or searching P1 and then P0 newest first for the first valid triple.
Calculation: Fork geometry = Let s be +1 for low-high-low pivots and -1 for high-low-high pivots. Set the origin to P0 for the standard variant, to P0's bar and the midpoint of P0 and P1 prices for Schiff, or to the midpoint of P0 and P1 in both bar index and price for modified Schiff. The median is the line through the origin and the midpoint of P1 and P2. The upper and lower parallels are the lines through P1 and P2 parallel to the median, ordered by price. Each warning line is one additional median-to-parallel distance outside its corresponding parallel. Width is the upper-minus-lower parallel distance.
Calculation: Valid triple = At the confirmation close, chart-timeframe volatility is the simple average of true range over its lookback and is positive; both s*(P1 price - P0 price) and s*(P1 price - P2 price) are at least a minimum volatility multiple; both pivot-to-pivot bar distances are at least the layer's swing length; s*(P2 price - P0 price) > 0; fork width is between its minimum and maximum volatility multiples inclusive; and the confirmation close is between the warning lines expanded outward by a volatility allowance, inclusive. For each inclusive P0-to-P1 and P1-to-P2 interval, require valid price data and require the interval's same-side extreme not to exceed its respective P0 or P2 extreme, and its opposite-side extreme not to exceed P1, by more than a volatility tolerance. Same-side means low for s=+1 and high for s=-1; opposite-side means the other extreme.
Calculation: Setup levels = Freeze volatility and fork geometry at formation. Round the formation close to the nearest configured price increment for entry. The geometric risk is the P1-to-P2 price distance capped by a multiple of the simple average of true range: on intraday charts use the last completed higher-timeframe bar's average; otherwise use the chart-timeframe average. Set the raw stop beyond P2 by a frozen-volatility multiple; ensure its entry distance is at least a configured fraction of geometric risk, then round it away from entry to the price grid and at least one increment from entry. For the fixed-target alternatives, set an ordered ladder of targets at increasing multiples of either the entry-to-stop distance or frozen volatility, rounding each away from entry to the grid. For the sloped-target alternatives, use the median alone, or the median followed by the parallel and warning line on the target side; retain only lines strictly beyond entry at formation and evaluate retained lines at each subsequent bar. A setup tracks levels only when the price increment and risk cap are available and at least one target is retained.
Long: A newly confirmed low pivot is retained AND a valid low-high-low triple is selected.
Short: A newly confirmed high pivot is retained AND a valid high-low-high triple is selected.
Exit: long, a later bar belongs to a tracking setup AND (low <= stop + half a price increment OR high >= the last retained target at that bar - half a price increment).
Exit: short, a later bar belongs to a tracking setup AND (high >= stop - half a price increment OR low <= the last retained target at that bar + half a price increment).
Exit: long, a later bar belongs to a tracking setup AND (close > upper warning line + frozen-volatility allowance OR close < lower warning line - frozen-volatility allowance OR bars since formation >= maximum age OR a new valid fork forms in the same layer).
Exit: short, a later bar belongs to a tracking setup AND (close > upper warning line + frozen-volatility allowance OR close < lower warning line - frozen-volatility allowance OR bars since formation >= maximum age OR a new valid fork forms in the same layer).
