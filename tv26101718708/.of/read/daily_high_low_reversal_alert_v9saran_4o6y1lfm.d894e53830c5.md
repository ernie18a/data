Calculation: Retained levels = At the first available chart bar in the premarket window, or the market-open fallback window if no premarket bar was available, store the high and low returned by a higher-timeframe daily bar. Use the last completed daily bar on historical bars and the still-forming daily bar in real time. Retain only the most recent parameterized number of stored high-low pairs and only through a parameterized number of chart bars after each pair was stored.
Calculation: Level proximity = A retained high is positive AND abs(chart-bar high - retained high) / retained high <= a tolerance parameter, OR a retained low is positive AND abs(chart-bar low - retained low) / retained low <= the same tolerance parameter.
Calculation: Candle measurements = Range is high - low; body high is max(open, close); body low is min(open, close); body size is body high - body low; upper wick is high - body high; lower wick is body low - low.
Long: Chart-bar opening time is within the parameterized market session AND level proximity AND range > 0 AND ((lower wick >= range times a wick threshold AND body size <= range times a body threshold AND upper wick <= range times an opposite-wick threshold AND body low >= low + range times the same wick threshold) OR (close >= high times a near-high factor AND lower wick >= range times a smaller wick threshold AND upper wick <= range times the same opposite-wick threshold)).
Long: Chart-bar opening time is within the parameterized market session AND level proximity AND range > 0 AND ((upper wick >= range times the wick threshold AND body size <= range times the body threshold AND lower wick <= range times the opposite-wick threshold AND body high <= high - range times the same wick threshold) OR (open <= low times a near-low factor AND upper wick >= range times the smaller wick threshold AND lower wick <= range times the same opposite-wick threshold)).
