Calculation: Daily high/low levels = the daily bar’s high and low captured at the first bar between 09:00 and 09:30 ET, or, if not captured then, between 09:30 and 09:32 ET; use the higher-timeframe daily bar while it is still forming. A level is eligible while its configured line-extension period is active and it remains among the configured number of most recent daily levels.
Calculation: Near a daily level = the current bar’s high is within the configured percentage tolerance of any eligible daily high, OR its low is within that tolerance of any eligible daily low; proximity is absolute difference divided by level, expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Hammer = during 09:30–16:00 ET, either (high > low, lower wick ≥ 45% of range, body ≤ 40% of range, upper wick ≤ 20% of range, and body low ≥ low + 45% of range) OR (close ≥ 99.9% of high, lower wick ≥ 40% of range, and upper wick ≤ 20% of range). Body is the interval between open and close; lower wick is body low − low; upper wick is high − body high; range is high − low.
Calculation: Inverted hammer = during 09:30–16:00 ET, either (high > low, upper wick ≥ 45% of range, body ≤ 40% of range, lower wick ≤ 20% of range, and body high ≤ high − 45% of range) OR (open ≤ 100.1% of low, upper wick ≥ 40% of range, and lower wick ≤ 20% of range), using the body, wick, and range definitions above.
Long: Near a daily level AND (Hammer OR Inverted hammer)
