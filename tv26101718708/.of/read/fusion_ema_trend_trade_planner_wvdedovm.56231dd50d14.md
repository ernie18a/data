Calculation: EMA structure = EMAs of close with successively longer lookbacks; select the shortest two, three, or four. Bullish structure means each selected EMA is greater than the next; bearish structure means each is less than the next. Full bullish or bearish structure uses all four EMAs.
Calculation: Basis = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3, optionally smoothed again with an EMA of the same length. Cloud reference = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3 with a shorter lookback than the basis. Cloud upper and lower edges are the maximum and minimum of the basis and cloud reference.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure = the same selected EMA structure calculated on higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical data and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Ribbon expansion = absolute difference between the shortest-lookback and longest-lookback selected EMAs.
Calculation: Long reference = optionally the average of the selected EMAs, the basis, the cloud upper edge, or the maximum selected EMA. Short reference = respectively the same average, the basis, the cloud lower edge, or the minimum selected EMA. Tolerance = ATR multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Bullish flip = bullish structure AND previous bar is not bullish structure. Bearish flip = bearish structure AND previous bar is not bearish structure.
Calculation: Long quality = bullish structure AND close > open AND close > basis AND ribbon expansion > ATR multiplied by a configurable factor AND, optionally, higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND, optionally, ADX >= a configurable threshold. Short quality = bearish structure AND close < open AND close < basis AND ribbon expansion > ATR multiplied by a configurable factor AND, optionally, higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND, optionally, ADX >= a configurable threshold.
Calculation: Long reclaim = low <= long reference + tolerance AND close > long reference AND close > open. Short reclaim = high >= short reference - tolerance AND close < short reference AND close < open.
Calculation: Pending retest = the most recent bullish or bearish flip, replaced by any subsequent flip, and expired when its age exceeds a configurable number of bars; clear it after a same-direction trade-plan entry. A retest is eligible only after the flip bar.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR at entry multiplied by a configurable factor OR entry price multiplied by a configurable percentage. Stop = entry price minus stop distance for a long, plus stop distance for a short. Each of three targets = entry price plus the stop distance multiplied by its configurable reward-to-risk factor for a long, minus that amount for a short; the target factors are ordered from nearest to farthest.
Long: Bullish structure.
Short: Bearish structure.
Long: Basis > previous basis.
Short: Basis <= previous basis.
Long: Higher-timeframe structure is bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe structure is bearish.
Long: Bullish flip AND entry mode is trend flip AND, if the quality filter is enabled, long quality AND, unless same-direction re-entry or historical plans are enabled, the active trade-plan direction is not long.
Short: Bearish flip AND entry mode is trend flip AND, if the quality filter is enabled, short quality AND, unless same-direction re-entry or historical plans are enabled, the active trade-plan direction is not short.
Long: Bullish structure AND long reclaim AND (entry mode includes retest AND pending retest is bullish and eligible OR entry mode includes pullback AND current bar is not a bullish flip) AND, if the quality filter is enabled, long quality AND, unless same-direction re-entry or historical plans are enabled, the active trade-plan direction is not long.
Short: Bearish structure AND short reclaim AND (entry mode includes retest AND pending retest is bearish and eligible OR entry mode includes pullback AND current bar is not a bearish flip) AND, if the quality filter is enabled, short quality AND, unless same-direction re-entry or historical plans are enabled, the active trade-plan direction is not short.
Exit: long, previous bar has bullish structure AND current bar does not have bullish structure OR previous bar has full bullish structure AND current bar does not have full bullish structure OR bearish flip; select one of these three exit modes.
Exit: short, previous bar has bearish structure AND current bar does not have bearish structure OR previous bar has full bearish structure AND current bar does not have full bearish structure OR bullish flip; select one of these three exit modes.
Exit: long, bar is after the trade-plan entry bar AND low <= stop OR bar is after the trade-plan entry bar AND high >= any target; stop takes priority if both occur on the same bar.
Exit: short, bar is after the trade-plan entry bar AND high >= stop OR bar is after the trade-plan entry bar AND low <= any target; stop takes priority if both occur on the same bar.
