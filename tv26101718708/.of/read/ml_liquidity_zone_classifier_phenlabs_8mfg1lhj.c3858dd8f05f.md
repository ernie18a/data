Calculation: Features = [min((volume / volume SMA, or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive) / 3, 1), 1 - min(distance from close to the nearest OHLC level of the last completed higher-timeframe bar / ATR / 3, 1), min(abs(close - open) / ATR / 3, 1)]. Use 0.5 for the second feature when its distance or ATR is unavailable or ATR is nonpositive, and 0 for the third when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Confidence = percentage of positive labels among the nearest training samples by Euclidean distance between feature vectors; use the available sample count if smaller than the neighbor count, break distance ties by oldest sample first, and use 50 when no samples exist. On each completed chart bar, before scoring, add the features from the evaluation-lookback bar with a positive label if abs(current close - that bar's close) > that bar's ATR multiplied by the label threshold, otherwise a negative label. Add samples only after the bar index exceeds the sum of the evaluation lookback, ATR length, and volume SMA length and when the historical features and positive ATR are available; retain only the most recent training-window samples.
Calculation: Zone eligibility = confidence >= minimum confidence AND bars since the last accepted zone >= minimum spacing, or no zone has yet been accepted, AND the proposed zone does not overlap any retained zone after expanding the proposed zone by the current ATR multiplied by the merge-distance parameter. Before the overlap test, clamp a zone taller than the current ATR multiplied by the maximum-height parameter symmetrically around its midpoint; do not clamp if that maximum height is nonpositive. Before testing candidates on each bar, optionally remove retained bullish zones when close < zone bottom and bearish zones when close > zone top. After accepting a zone, retain only the most recent maximum number of zones. Test candidates in order: bullish order block, bearish order block, equal high, equal low, volume node; accept only the first eligible candidate.
