Calculation: Pool tolerance = ATR multiplied by a tolerance parameter; sweep depth = ATR multiplied by a separate depth parameter.
Calculation: High and low pivot clusters = maintain one cluster per side. Confirm a pivot after an equal number of bars on either side. If a new pivot is farther than a multiple of the current pool tolerance from its cluster, replace the cluster with that pivot, set its touch count to one, and record the pivot bar. Otherwise update the cluster to the touch-count-weighted average with the new pivot and increment its touch count. A cluster qualifies when its touch count and age since its first pivot bar meet their respective minima.
Calculation: Equal-high and equal-low candidates = the highest high and lowest low over a lookback including the current bar, respectively, provided the number of highs or lows within the current pool tolerance of that extreme meets a minimum.
Calculation: Upper pool = the qualified high-pivot cluster or equal-high candidate closest to and strictly above the current close. Lower pool = the qualified low-pivot cluster or equal-low candidate closest to and strictly below the current close. A pool is unavailable if neither candidate qualifies on the required side.
Calculation: Lower sweep = lower pool is available AND low < lower pool − sweep depth. Upper sweep = upper pool is available AND high > upper pool + sweep depth. Store the bar and pool level of the most recent sweep separately for each side, updating them before evaluating the current bar. A sweep is recent when its age is between zero and the reclaim-window parameter inclusive.
Calculation: Bull trigger = close > open AND absolute close−open >= a multiple of ATR AND (close−low)/(high−low) > a threshold. Bear trigger = close < open AND absolute close−open >= the same multiple of ATR AND (high−close)/(high−low) > the same threshold.
Calculation: Lower zone = lower pool is available AND low <= lower pool + pool tolerance. Upper zone = upper pool is available AND high >= upper pool − pool tolerance.
Calculation: Long primary setup = an enabled same-bar setup with lower sweep AND close > lower pool AND bull trigger, OR an enabled delayed setup with a recent lower sweep at least one bar old AND close > the stored lower-sweep level AND low <= that level + pool tolerance AND bull trigger.
Calculation: Short primary setup = an enabled same-bar setup with upper sweep AND close < upper pool AND bear trigger, OR an enabled delayed setup with a recent upper sweep at least one bar old AND close < the stored upper-sweep level AND high >= that level − pool tolerance AND bear trigger.
Calculation: Long secondary setup = an enabled absorption setup with lower zone AND (min(open, close)−low)/(high−low) > a wick threshold AND absolute close−open/(high−low) < a body threshold AND close > open AND volume >= a multiple of its SMA AND close > lower pool, OR an enabled gap setup with low > high two bars ago AND high two bars ago <= lower pool + pool tolerance AND low >= lower pool − pool tolerance AND lower pool is available AND bull trigger.
Calculation: Short secondary setup = an enabled absorption setup with upper zone AND (high−max(open, close))/(high−low) > the wick threshold AND absolute close−open/(high−low) < the body threshold AND close < open AND volume >= the same multiple of its SMA AND close < upper pool, OR an enabled gap setup with high < low two bars ago AND high <= upper pool + pool tolerance AND low two bars ago >= upper pool − pool tolerance AND upper pool is available AND bear trigger.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA; bearish bias = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA. Calculate all three from the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = a scale constant multiplied by log10(sum of true range over the lookback divided by highest high minus lowest low over that lookback), divided by log10 of the lookback; use a neutral fallback when the high-low difference is zero. Chop passes when blocking is disabled OR the index is below its threshold.
Calculation: Directional score = primary setup weight if a primary setup is true, otherwise secondary setup weight if a secondary setup is true, otherwise zero; add one point for each of: corresponding pool available; enabled higher-timeframe bias matching the direction; volume layer disabled OR volume >= its SMA multiplied by its threshold; close above the structure EMA OR RSI above its long structural threshold for long, with the comparisons reversed and a separate short threshold for short; RSI below its long exhaustion threshold AND a recent lower sweep for long, or RSI above its short exhaustion threshold AND a recent upper sweep for short; chop passes; corresponding pool zone. The primary weight exceeds the secondary weight. The long exhaustion RSI threshold is below the long structural threshold, and the short exhaustion threshold is above the short structural threshold.
Long: Lower sweep.
Short: Upper sweep.
Long: Long primary setup OR long secondary setup.
Short: Short primary setup OR short secondary setup.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish bias.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish bias.
Long: Long directional score >= score threshold.
Short: Short directional score >= score threshold AND long directional score < score threshold.
Long: Lower sweep is recent.
Short: Upper sweep is recent AND lower sweep is not recent.
