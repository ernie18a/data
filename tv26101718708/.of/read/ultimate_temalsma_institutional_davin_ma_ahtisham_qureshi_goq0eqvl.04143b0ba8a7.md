Calculation: Institutional buy zone = lowest low over the zone lookback through the current bar to that low plus ATR times a zone-width multiplier.
Calculation: Institutional sell zone = highest high over the same lookback through the current bar minus ATR times the zone-width multiplier to that high.
Calculation: Zone score = mean of the percentage differences of close from TEMA, LSMA, and the long-term EMA.
Calculation: Bullish alignment = TEMA, LSMA, and long-term EMA each greater than their previous-bar values. Bearish alignment = TEMA, LSMA, and long-term EMA each less than or equal to their previous-bar values.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute value of the mean of the TEMA, LSMA, and long-term EMA changes over respectively short, medium, and long lookbacks, divided by ATR.
Calculation: Trend duration = consecutive bars of bullish alignment or bearish alignment; reset to zero on any unaligned bar. The trend direction is the alignment direction.
Calculation: Exhaustion = trend duration greater than twice a duration threshold AND (trend strength below its threshold OR, when divergence is enabled, bullish trend with close above and RSI below their values at a divergence lookback, or bearish trend with close below and RSI above their values at that lookback).
Calculation: Bullish retest level = long-term EMA value at the most recent close crossover above that EMA. Bearish retest level = long-term EMA value at the most recent close crossunder below that EMA.
Calculation: EMA test count = count of bars whose close is within an ATR-scaled test distance of the long-term EMA and differs from the last counted close; reset the count to zero whenever the absolute distance from the EMA exceeds a separate ATR-scaled reset distance. Preserve the last counted close across resets.
Calculation: Reversal directional movement = Wilder smoothing of max(high change, 0) and of max(-low change, 0), separately. Divide each by Wilder-smoothed true range and multiply by 100 to obtain positive and negative directional indicators. Reversal ADX = Wilder smoothing of 100 times their absolute difference divided by their sum.
Calculation: Bullish consensus count = number of true conditions among RSI above its midpoint; smoothed stochastic K above its midpoint OR above its signal; stochastic RSI above its midpoint; MACD line above its signal; Williams %R above its midpoint; CCI above zero. Bearish consensus count uses the corresponding below comparisons. Stochastic K is an SMA of stochastic; its signal is a further SMA. Stochastic RSI is RSI normalized between its rolling minimum and maximum. CCI uses close as its source.
Calculation: Signal priority = enabled institutional buy zone first, then enabled institutional sell zone, then enabled ideal sell, enabled ideal buy, enabled reversal long, enabled reversal short, enabled moving-average pullback buy, enabled moving-average exit marking, and finally original crossover signals. Ideal, reversal, and pullback entries require a flat position; the moving-average exit marking requires a long position. Institutional zone signals have priority even while a position is open. If an ideal sell and ideal buy both occur, the sell overrides the buy.
Long: Position is flat AND the enabled institutional buy zone contains close.
Short: Position is flat AND the enabled institutional buy zone does not contain close AND the enabled institutional sell zone contains close.
Long: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND an enabled ideal buy applies: (low below the latest confirmed pivot low AND close above previous high AND previous high minus low greater than an ATR-scaled sweep threshold AND close above long-term EMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier) OR (close strictly within a percentage tolerance of the bullish retest level AND close above open AND TEMA above LSMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier) OR (zone score at or below the oversold threshold AND close above long-term EMA AND TEMA above LSMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier). A pivot is confirmed after equally many left and right bars.
Short: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND an enabled ideal sell applies: (close strictly within a percentage tolerance of the bearish retest level AND close below open AND TEMA below LSMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier) OR (high below the latest confirmed pivot high AND high above previous high AND zone score in the overbought or extreme-overbought category AND TEMA below LSMA) OR (EMA test count at least its threshold AND close below long-term EMA AND TEMA below LSMA AND close crosses below long-term EMA). Assign the zone categories by testing zone score in order against the negative extreme-oversold threshold, negative oversold threshold, lower neutral boundary, upper neutral boundary, and overbought threshold; scores above the last boundary are extreme overbought.
Long: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND reversals and their active-hours switch are enabled AND close above a reversal EMA AND that EMA rising AND (reversal ADX above its threshold OR positive directional indicator above negative directional indicator) AND bullish consensus count at least its threshold AND volume above its SMA times the reversal volume multiplier.
Short: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND reversals and their active-hours switch are enabled AND close below the reversal EMA AND that EMA falling AND (reversal ADX above its threshold OR negative directional indicator above positive directional indicator) AND bearish consensus count at least its threshold AND volume above its SMA times the reversal volume multiplier.
Long: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND the moving-average strategy is enabled AND ((close above the longer close SMA AND close below the shorter close SMA) OR (dip buying is enabled AND percentage decline from the rolling highest high to close exceeds the dip threshold)).
Exit: long, no higher-priority signal applies AND the moving-average strategy is enabled AND ((close above the shorter close SMA AND (lower-close filter disabled OR close below previous low)) OR (stored buy price minus close, divided by close, exceeds the stop threshold)). The stored buy price starts at zero and is replaced by the current open when the preceding bar met the moving-average buy condition.
Long: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND TEMA crosses above LSMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier AND ((alignment filter enabled AND bullish alignment) OR (alignment filter disabled AND TEMA above LSMA AND close above long-term EMA) OR (exhaustion filter enabled AND exhaustion AND bearish trend)).
Short: Position is flat AND no higher-priority signal applies AND TEMA crosses below LSMA AND volume above its SMA times a multiplier AND ((alignment filter enabled AND bearish alignment) OR (alignment filter disabled AND TEMA below LSMA AND close below long-term EMA) OR (exhaustion filter enabled AND exhaustion AND bullish trend)).
Exit: long, low reaches entry price minus current ATR times the stop multiplier OR high reaches entry price plus current ATR times the target multiplier OR an activated trailing stop is reached. Increase the stop and target multipliers by their respective institutional scaling factors when the current bar's highest-priority signal source is institutional.
Exit: short, high reaches entry price plus current ATR times the stop multiplier OR low reaches entry price minus current ATR times the target multiplier OR an activated trailing stop is reached. Increase the stop and target multipliers by their respective institutional scaling factors when the current bar's highest-priority signal source is institutional.
Calculation: Trailing stop = activate after favorable movement from entry by the current ATR times a trailing multiplier measured in minimum price ticks; once activated, trail the most favorable price by the same tick distance. The activation and offset arguments are recalculated from current ATR each bar.
Exit: long OR short, bars elapsed since the entry bar at least the maximum holding-period threshold.
