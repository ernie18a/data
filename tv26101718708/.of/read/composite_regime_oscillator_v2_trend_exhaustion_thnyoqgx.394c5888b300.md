Calculation: Scale(x, a, b) = 100 × clamp((x − a) / (b − a), 0, 1); return 50 if a = b. Each use has its own lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Let H and L be the highest high and lowest low over a range lookback, and D the highest high over a separate drawdown lookback. Let A = short-period ATR / long-period ATR, Q = (D − close) / D, R = (close − L) / L, T = (close − EMA of close) / short-period ATR, P = (close − L) / (H − L), U = (H − close) / H, and V = volume / SMA of volume. A, Q, R, T, U, and V are undefined when their denominators are zero; P is 0.5 when H = L.
Calculation: Regime = weighted average of Scale(A), Scale(Q), and Scale(R); use 50 if A, Q, or R is undefined. Stretch = weighted average of Scale(T) and Scale(P); use 50 if T is undefined. Location = weighted average of Scale(P), Scale(R), and 100 − Scale(U); use 50 if R or U is undefined. Overreaction = weighted average of Scale(RSI of close) and Scale(T); use 50 if RSI is undefined. Pressure = weighted average of Scale(V) and Scale(A); use 50 if V is undefined. Each component has independent weights and scale bounds; otherwise undefined inputs propagate.
Calculation: Oscillator = EMA of the weighted average of Regime, Stretch, Location, Overreaction, and Pressure; use 50 for the average if its weights sum to zero. Bullish reversal strength = EMA of a weighted average of 100 − Location, 100 − Overreaction, Regime, and Pressure. Bearish reversal strength = EMA of a weighted average of Location, Overreaction, 100 − Regime, and Pressure. The three EMAs use the same smoothing length; each average has its own weights.
Long: Oscillator crosses above the upper extreme threshold OR oscillator < lower extreme threshold AND oscillator > its highest value over a reversal lookback of preceding bars.
Short: Oscillator crosses below the lower extreme threshold OR oscillator >= upper extreme threshold AND oscillator < its lowest value over a reversal lookback of preceding bars.
Long: Oscillator < lower extreme threshold.
Short: Oscillator >= upper extreme threshold.
Long: Oscillator < lower extreme threshold AND bullish reversal strength > its highest value over a warning lookback of preceding bars.
Short: Oscillator >= upper extreme threshold AND bearish reversal strength > its highest value over a warning lookback of preceding bars.
Exit: long, oscillator >= upper momentum threshold AND oscillator < its lowest value over an exhaustion lookback of preceding bars.
