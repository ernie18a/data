Calculation: PDH, PDL = high and low of the last completed bar of a higher (daily) timeframe than the chart
Calculation: ON_H, ON_L = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the overnight session (the session before the regular session), accumulated from that session's start and held through the following regular session
Calculation: OR_H, OR_L = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the initial window of the current regular session; usable only from the first bar at or after the window end. Prev_OR_H, Prev_OR_L = the same values from the preceding regular session
Calculation: LongSweepLevel = on any bar inside the regular session where low < PDL OR low < ON_L OR low < OR_L OR low < Prev_OR_L (each level only if it exists), the level broken by that bar; if several are broken on the same bar, priority is Prev_OR_L, then OR_L, then ON_L, then PDL. It stays the most recent such level and is cleared when an entry is taken
Calculation: ShortSweepLevel = mirror of LongSweepLevel: high > PDH OR ON_H OR OR_H OR Prev_OR_H, with priority Prev_OR_H, then OR_H, then ON_H, then PDH
Calculation: BullDisplacement = close > open AND |close-open| >= ATR * a fraction AND |close-open| / max(high-low, mintick) >= a fraction AND close > previous bar high. BearDisplacement = close < open AND the same two body-size conditions AND close < previous bar low
Calculation: BullFVG = low > high of 2 bars ago. BearFVG = high < low of 2 bars ago
Long: bar closed AND close > LongSweepLevel AND the bar that set LongSweepLevel is the current bar or at most a fixed confirmation-window number of bars earlier AND BullDisplacement AND bars since the last entry >= a cooldown AND entries in the current regular session < a daily maximum AND no open position; optionally AND fast EMA(close) > slow EMA(close) (fast length shorter than slow), optionally AND BullFVG
Short: bar closed AND close < ShortSweepLevel AND the bar that set ShortSweepLevel is the current bar or at most a fixed confirmation-window number of bars earlier AND BearDisplacement AND bars since the last entry >= a cooldown AND entries in the current regular session < a daily maximum AND no open position; optionally AND fast EMA(close) < slow EMA(close), optionally AND BearFVG
Exit: long, low <= entry-bar low OR high >= entry price + R * (entry price - entry-bar low), where R is a reward-to-risk multiple; checked from the entry bar onward
Exit: short, high >= entry-bar high OR low <= entry price - R * (entry-bar high - entry price), where R is a reward-to-risk multiple; checked from the entry bar onward
