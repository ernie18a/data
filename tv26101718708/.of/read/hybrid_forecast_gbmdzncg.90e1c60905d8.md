Calculation: Gaussian baseline = normalized weighted average of current and preceding closes, with weight exp(-lag² / (2 × bandwidth²)); substitute the current close for unavailable historical closes. Baseline slope is its change from the previous bar, or zero on the first bar.
Calculation: Filtered velocity = initialize filtered price to the first close and velocity to zero; on each later bar, predict price as previous filtered price plus previous velocity, calculate residual as close minus predicted price, add a price-gain multiple of residual to predicted price, and add a velocity-gain multiple of residual to previous velocity.
Calculation: Trend fit = clamp(1 − standard deviation of (close − rolling linear-regression endpoint) / standard deviation of close, 0, 1), or a neutral fallback when close standard deviation is unavailable or zero. Each historical residual uses the regression endpoint calculated on its own bar.
Calculation: Signed efficiency = close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, with a small positive denominator floor. Calculate it with shorter and longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Trend quality = clamp(weighted sum of trend fit and absolute longer-lookback signed efficiency, 0, 1).
Calculation: Relative ATR = ATR / its longer-lookback SMA, with a neutral fallback if the SMA is zero. Stretch = absolute difference between close and Gaussian baseline / ATR, or zero if ATR is zero. Trend strength = weighted sum of absolute filtered velocity / ATR, absolute baseline slope / ATR, and trend quality; apply a small positive denominator floor. Direction score = weighted sum of filtered velocity / ATR and baseline slope / ATR. Regime direction is positive, negative, or zero according to whether direction score exceeds a positive threshold, falls below its negative, or lies between them. Regime persistence is the consecutive number of completed bars with the same nonzero regime direction.
Calculation: Market state = trend when trend quality is at least its trend threshold AND stretch is at most its maximum; otherwise chop when trend quality is below its chop threshold OR stretch exceeds its maximum; otherwise transition. Update the retained market state only after the raw state has remained unchanged for the required number of completed bars; initialize it to transition.
Calculation: Breakout high and breakout low = respectively the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending on the preceding bar. A bullish reclaim is a close crossing above the Gaussian baseline or breakout low within a specified number of bars after crossing below that same series, AND (close − low) / (high − low) above a threshold. A bearish reclaim is the symmetric crossing below the baseline or breakout high after crossing above it, AND (high − close) / (high − low) above a threshold. Evaluate crossings against each series' value on both the current and preceding bar; floor the range denominator at a small positive value.
Calculation: Memory features = (1) a scaled and shifted tanh of filtered velocity / ATR; (2) a clamped, scaled and shifted ATR deviation from its longer-lookback SMA divided by its longer-lookback standard deviation; (3) clamped trend quality minus a multiple of feature 2's deviation from its midpoint; (4) clamped, scaled and shifted (close − Gaussian baseline) / ATR; (5) clamped, scaled and shifted (close − ALMA of close) / ATR. Use zero for each ATR-based ratio when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Forecast memory = for every emitted entry, retain its direction, entry close, entry features and due bar. Through the due bar, track maximum nonnegative adverse log excursion using low for a long and high for a short. At the due bar, store those features, direction, adverse excursion, log(due close / entry close), and completion bar; remove the oldest record when capacity is exceeded. Update each feature's scale by exponentially smoothing its absolute deviation from the feature midpoint, subject to a positive floor; update the adverse-excursion floor by exponentially smoothing completed adverse excursions. Initialize both from positive constants.
Calculation: Neighbors = up to the configured count of completed memory records with the current regime direction and smallest sum of squared feature differences divided by the corresponding squared feature scales; prefer older records on distance ties. Weight each selected record by an exponential age penalty divided by distance plus a positive offset. Calculate weighted mean terminal log return, weighted mean adverse excursion, weighted terminal-return standard deviation, unweighted mean distance, and agreement: the fraction of neighbors whose terminal-return sign matches the weighted mean's sign, or the larger positive or negative fraction if the mean is zero. With no neighbors, use zero for the means, deviation and agreement.
Calculation: Memory confidence = the clamped geometric mean of clamped neighbor-count coverage, memory-size coverage, one minus clamped terminal-return deviation / its exponentially smoothed reference, one minus clamped mean distance / its exponentially smoothed reference, and agreement; floor each factor at a small positive value. Update the positive references on each bar from available positive deviation and available mean distance. Memory is ready when total stored samples and selected neighbors each meet their minimum counts.
Calculation: Live confidence = a clamped weighted sum of normalized trend strength, inverse normalized stretch, normalized absolute shorter-lookback signed efficiency, and a clamped weighted sum of trend quality and that absolute efficiency. Setup confidence is live confidence in live-only mode; otherwise it is an equal-weight average of live and memory confidence when memory is ready, or an interpolation from live to memory confidence by clamped memory fill when it is not. Apply the configured session-open confidence deduction using datetime when that session rule applies, then clamp to the valid range.
Calculation: Memory veto = memory is ready AND (absolute weighted mean terminal return is positive AND terminal-return deviation / its absolute mean exceeds a dispersion threshold OR agreement is below its threshold). The relaxed hybrid mode uses a higher dispersion threshold and a lower agreement threshold. The veto applies even in live-only mode.
Calculation: Entry filters = completed chart bar AND trend strength above its threshold AND stretch at most its maximum AND absolute shorter-lookback signed efficiency at least its minimum AND relative ATR within its permitted bounds AND nonzero regime direction persists for at least its minimum bars AND absolute filtered velocity exceeds an ATR multiple AND absolute one-bar close change / ATR is at least its threshold AND current high-low range / its SMA is at least its threshold AND (volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume / its SMA is at least its threshold) AND memory veto is false AND setup confidence is at least its mode-adjusted threshold AND bars since the last emitted entry are at least the cooldown AND active forecasts are below the total limit AND active forecasts in the proposed direction are below the per-direction limit. Remove forecasts from the active counts on their due bar before evaluating entries. Frequency profiles adjust the thresholds and persistence and cooldown lengths consistently: conservative is more restrictive and active is less restrictive than balanced.
Calculation: Stop distance = when hybrid memory use is enabled, memory is ready, memory confidence meets the adaptive-blend threshold when adaptive blending is enabled, relative ATR is permitted, and memory veto is false, use the greater of weighted mean adverse excursion and a fraction of the smoothed adverse-excursion floor; otherwise use the greatest of a fraction of the clamped ATR-based live forecast zone width, the smoothed adverse-excursion floor, and a minimum zone width. The live zone width is proportional to ATR / close times the square root of the forecast horizon. Fix the long stop at entry close × exp(−stop distance) and the short stop at entry close × exp(stop distance); for nonpositive entry close use the entry close.
Long: Entry filters hold AND regime direction is positive AND ((continuations are enabled AND previous regime direction is positive AND close > breakout high AND close > Gaussian baseline AND (strict market filtering is disabled OR retained market state is trend)) OR ((reversals are enabled AND previous regime direction is nonpositive AND close > Gaussian baseline AND close > ALMA) OR (reclaims are enabled AND bullish reclaim)) AND (strict market filtering is disabled OR retained market state is chop)).
Short: Entry filters hold AND regime direction is negative AND ((continuations are enabled AND previous regime direction is negative AND close < breakout low AND close < Gaussian baseline AND (strict market filtering is disabled OR retained market state is trend)) OR ((reversals are enabled AND previous regime direction is nonnegative AND close < Gaussian baseline AND close < ALMA) OR (reclaims are enabled AND bearish reclaim)) AND (strict market filtering is disabled OR retained market state is chop)).
Long: Retained market state is trend AND regime direction > 0.
Short: Retained market state is trend AND regime direction <= 0.
Exit: long, low <= fixed long stop while the entry's forecast remains active through its due bar.
Exit: short, high >= fixed short stop while the entry's forecast remains active through its due bar.
