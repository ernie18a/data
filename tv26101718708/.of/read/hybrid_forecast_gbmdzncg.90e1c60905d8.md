Calculation: Baseline = causal Gaussian-weighted average of the current and prior closes
Calculation: Direction = sign of a weighted sum of normalized smoothed-price velocity and baseline slope; classify as bullish, bearish, or neutral using a small directional threshold
Calculation: Trend strength = weighted sum of absolute normalized smoothed-price velocity, absolute normalized baseline slope, and blended trend quality
Calculation: Blended trend quality = weighted blend of price-fit quality and absolute long-lookback efficiency ratio, adjusted for relative volatility
Calculation: Fast efficiency ratio = close change over a shorter lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Long-lookback efficiency ratio = close change over a longer lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Stretch = absolute distance from baseline divided by ATR
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback average
Calculation: Trend regime = trend when blended trend quality meets the continuation threshold and stretch is within its limit; chop when blended trend quality is below its chop threshold or stretch exceeds its limit; otherwise transition. Update the regime only after the raw regime remains stable for its persistence threshold
Calculation: Bullish reversal trigger = Direction turns bullish from bearish or neutral, and close is above both Baseline and ALMA
Calculation: Bearish reversal trigger = Direction turns bearish from bullish or neutral, and close is below both Baseline and ALMA
Calculation: Bullish reclaim trigger = close crosses above Baseline after crossing below it within the recent-bar limit, or close crosses above the prior short-lookback low after crossing below it within that limit; in either case, close is in the upper portion of the bar
Calculation: Bearish reclaim trigger = close crosses below Baseline after crossing above it within the recent-bar limit, or close crosses below the prior short-lookback high after crossing above it within that limit; in either case, close is in the lower portion of the bar
Calculation: Bullish continuation trigger = Direction is bullish on this and the prior bar, close exceeds the prior short-lookback high, and close is above Baseline
Calculation: Bearish continuation trigger = Direction is bearish on this and the prior bar, close is below the prior short-lookback low, and close is below Baseline
Calculation: Gate = trend strength exceeds its threshold, stretch is within its limit, and absolute fast efficiency ratio meets its minimum
Calculation: Memory confidence = confidence from the nearest stored setups with the same Direction, weighted by recency and inverse distance; derive agreement from the fraction of neighbors with the prevailing forecast sign
Calculation: Setup confidence = blended live setup confidence and Memory confidence, with the blend determined by memory availability and confidence
Calculation: Invalidation price = close adjusted against Direction by the larger of predicted adverse excursion and its adaptive floor when memory is active; otherwise adjust by the larger of a fraction of forecast volatility zone, the adaptive floor, and the minimum zone
Long: enabled continuation trigger or enabled reversal or reclaim trigger in the allowed regime, AND confirmed bar, AND (strict regime filter disabled OR regime is not transition), AND Gate, AND Direction persistence meets its threshold, AND cooldown since the prior signal has elapsed, AND absolute smoothed-price velocity exceeds its ATR-based minimum, AND absolute close change divided by ATR meets its minimum, AND bar range divided by its short-lookback average meets its minimum, AND (volume unavailable OR volume divided by its short-lookback average meets its minimum), AND memory forecast dispersion is within its limit, AND memory agreement meets its minimum, AND Setup confidence meets its minimum, AND active setup limits permit another signal. With strict regime filtering enabled, continuation requires trend regime and reversal or reclaim requires chop regime with bullish Direction.
