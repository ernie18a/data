Calculation: Grid reference for a gap = initialize to close; on each subsequent bar, set it to high if high >= previous reference + gap, otherwise set it to low if low <= previous reference - gap, otherwise retain the previous reference. Use independent gaps and references for entries, profit taking, and stop loss.
Calculation: Confirmed fractal high or low = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side; retain its pivot price after confirmation. A past fractal is selected by its occurrence number.
Calculation: Reversal fractal = bullish when a pivot low with shorter left and zero right bars or longer left and one right bar is confirmed AND close > open AND previous close < previous open, OR a pivot low with longer left and one right bar is confirmed AND low > previous low AND close < open AND previous close < previous open; bearish is the mirror image using pivot highs, high < previous high, and opposite candle directions.
Calculation: Pin bar = bullish when a current-bar pivot low is confirmed AND close differs from open AND lower wick > sensitivity × absolute candle body AND lower wick > upper wick; bearish uses a current-bar pivot high, upper wick, and upper wick > lower wick.
Calculation: Modified HMA = WMA(3 × WMA(close, one-third of half-length) - WMA(close, one-half of half-length) - WMA(close, half-length), half-length). Compare it with HMA of the high-low midpoint using the full length.
Calculation: TRIX slope series = change in the triple EMA of log(close), multiplied by a positive scaling factor.
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud boundaries = maximum and minimum of the two standard Ichimoku leading spans shifted back by the displacement minus one bar. The conversion line uses a shorter lookback than the base line; the second leading span uses a longer lookback than the base line.
Calculation: Fractal trendline level = extrapolate the line between the latest two confirmed fractal highs or lows to the current bar; determine the horizontal offsets with the source's bounded pivot search and its asymmetric equal-high or equal-low comparisons.
Calculation: Segment condition = for lows, require low > previous low; search within a maximum lookback for low[1] > low[i+2], counting iterations as a; then search the same maximum range starting at i=a for low[1+a] > low[i+2], counting iterations as b; then search the same maximum range starting at i=b for low[1+a+b] > low[i+2+a]. The high condition reverses every price inequality. All three searches must succeed.
Calculation: Filter timeframe = chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe use either its still-forming bar or its last completed bar. Chart-only filters use the current or preceding chart bar, respectively. Each of two selected filters can have its own timeframe, and both selected filters must pass.
Long: Within the selected date interval AND after any enabled trigger level has been crossed by high or low AND before either enabled border has been crossed by high or low AND the selected long exit condition is false AND each selected long filter passes AND at least one selected entry condition holds: entry grid reference increases for trend-following or decreases for countertrend; OR low < lower Bollinger Band; OR bullish pin bar; OR high crosses above the latest confirmed fractal high; OR bullish reversal fractal. The same selected filters apply to both entry choices.
Short: Within the selected date interval AND after any enabled trigger level has been crossed by high or low AND before either enabled border has been crossed by high or low AND the selected short exit condition is false AND each selected short filter passes AND at least one selected entry condition holds: entry grid reference decreases for trend-following or increases for countertrend; OR high > upper Bollinger Band; OR bearish pin bar; OR low crosses below the latest confirmed fractal low; OR bearish reversal fractal. The same selected filters apply to both entry choices.
