Calculation: ATR = Wilder-smoothed true range over a lookback, falling back to high minus low until available, and floored at a positive price increment.
Calculation: Candle range = high minus low, floored at a positive price increment.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND absolute(close − open) / ATR >= body threshold AND candle range / ATR >= range threshold AND (close − low) / candle range >= close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND absolute(close − open) / ATR >= body threshold AND candle range / ATR >= range threshold AND (high − close) / candle range >= close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Active displacement = the most recent completed bar satisfying bullish or bearish displacement; replace the previous active displacement whenever a new one occurs. Its direction remains active until replaced.
Calculation: Shelf = for a bullish active displacement, upper boundary is min(anchor close, anchor high − anchor ATR × shelf-depth parameter) and lower boundary is max(anchor open, anchor low + anchor ATR × shelf-depth parameter); for a bearish active displacement, upper boundary is min(anchor open, anchor high − anchor ATR × shelf-depth parameter) and lower boundary is max(anchor close, anchor low + anchor ATR × shelf-depth parameter).
Long: Active displacement direction is bullish.
Short: Active displacement direction is bearish.
Exit: long, active displacement direction is bullish AND bars since the active displacement <= failure-window parameter AND close < shelf lower boundary AND the shelf has not previously failed since the active displacement.
Exit: short, active displacement direction is bearish AND bars since the active displacement <= failure-window parameter AND close > shelf upper boundary AND the shelf has not previously failed since the active displacement.
