Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the highs/lows of an equal-length lookback on both sides; confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Forming-window high/low = highest high/lowest low over an equal-length window of candidate bars; use the current forming bar and preceding bars in real-time mode, or the preceding completed bars in after-close mode.
Long: At a new confirmed chart swing low, chart low is lower than at the preceding confirmed chart swing low AND paired-symbol low at the same pivot is not lower than at the preceding chart pivot, OR paired-symbol low at a new confirmed paired-symbol swing low is lower than at its preceding confirmed swing low AND chart low over the candidate window is not lower than chart low at the preceding paired-symbol pivot, OR chart forming-window low is below the latest confirmed chart swing low AND paired-symbol forming-window low is not below paired-symbol low at that chart pivot, OR paired-symbol forming-window low is below its latest confirmed swing low AND chart forming-window low is not below chart low at that paired-symbol pivot.
Short: At a new confirmed chart swing high, chart high is higher than at the preceding confirmed chart swing high AND paired-symbol high at the same pivot is not higher than at the preceding chart pivot, OR paired-symbol high at a new confirmed paired-symbol swing high is higher than at its preceding confirmed swing high AND chart high over the candidate window is not higher than chart high at the preceding paired-symbol pivot, OR chart forming-window high is above the latest confirmed chart swing high AND paired-symbol forming-window high is not above paired-symbol high at that chart pivot, OR paired-symbol forming-window high is above its latest confirmed swing high AND chart forming-window high is not above chart high at that paired-symbol pivot.
