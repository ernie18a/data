Calculation: Volume mass = clamp(volume / SMA(volume), 0.1, 5); use 1 if the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Candle measures = body percentage = 100 * abs(close - open) / close; range percentage = 100 * (high - low) / close; ATR percentage = 100 * ATR / close; use zero for each when close is zero. Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low. Range expansion = clamp(range percentage / ATR percentage, 0.25, 4), or 1 when ATR percentage is zero.
Calculation: Raw energy = volume mass * (0.6 * body percentage + 0.4 * range percentage) * range expansion * (1 + 0.35 * abs(close location - 0.5) * 2). Energy score = 100 * (raw energy - rolling minimum raw energy) / (rolling maximum raw energy - rolling minimum raw energy), clamped to [0, 100]; use zero when the rolling range is zero. Critical energy = SMA(raw energy) + sensitivity * standard deviation(raw energy).
Calculation: Force score = 100 * smoothed force / rolling maximum of abs(smoothed force), clamped to [-100, 100], or zero when that maximum is zero. Smoothed force = EMA(volume mass * (0.65 * signed body percentage + 0.2 * signed range percentage + 0.15 * (2 * close location - 1) * ATR percentage)). Signed body percentage = 100 * (close - open) / close, or zero when close is zero; signed range percentage is positive when close >= open and negative otherwise. Use the same normalization lookback as the energy score.
Calculation: Bull impulse = raw energy > critical energy AND energy score >= minimum energy threshold AND force score >= positive force threshold. Bear impulse = raw energy > critical energy AND energy score >= minimum energy threshold AND force score <= negative force threshold. Critical event = raw energy > critical energy AND energy score >= minimum energy threshold.
Calculation: Bull burst = force score >= positive burst threshold AND energy score < minimum energy threshold. Bear burst = force score <= negative burst threshold AND energy score < minimum energy threshold.
Calculation: Charging = raw energy <= critical energy OR energy score < minimum energy threshold, AND ATR percentage < its SMA * compression factor, AND (volume mass > volume threshold OR volume mass > each of its preceding volume-rise lookback values), AND (abs(force score) > positive force threshold OR abs(force score) > each of its preceding force-rise lookback values), AND energy score >= charging minimum AND energy score <= charging maximum. Parenthesize the first OR as one condition.
Calculation: Bull memory = bars since the most recent bull impulse <= memory lookback AND either no bear impulse has occurred or the bull impulse is more recent than the bear impulse. Bear memory is symmetric. Bull recovery = bear memory AND force score >= positive burst threshold. Bear recovery = bull memory AND force score <= negative burst threshold.
Calculation: Directional grade score = clamp(0.35 * energy score + 0.35 * max(directional force score, 0) + volume bonus when volume mass > 1 + expansion bonus when range expansion > 1 + trend bonus when the direction's trend comparison passes + recovery bonus when the direction's recovery condition passes + charging bonus when charging AND directional force score > 0, 0, 100). Directional force score is force score for long and its negation for short. The trend comparison is short-lookback EMA(close) >= long-lookback EMA(close) for long and <= for short. A grade filter passes when the grade score >= the selected grade cutoff; selecting all grades also permits scores below the lowest cutoff.
Calculation: Long overlay = (bull impulse OR, when the selected mode includes bursts and recoveries, bull burst OR bull recovery OR, when the selected mode also includes charging, charging AND force score > 0) AND the long grade filter passes AND, if enabled, the long trend comparison passes AND, if enabled, the higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) >= its long-lookback EMA(close) AND, if enabled, the bar opens within the selected session AND bars since the preceding long overlay > overlay cooldown or no preceding long overlay exists. Higher-timeframe EMAs use the higher-timeframe bar still forming as of the chart bar; on historical bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar until the higher-timeframe bar closes.
Calculation: Short overlay = (bear impulse OR, when the selected mode includes bursts and recoveries, bear burst OR bear recovery OR, when the selected mode also includes charging, charging AND force score < 0) AND the short grade filter passes AND, if enabled, the short trend comparison passes AND, if enabled, the higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA(close) <= its long-lookback EMA(close) AND, if enabled, the bar opens within the selected session AND bars since the preceding short overlay > overlay cooldown or no preceding short overlay exists. Use the same higher-timeframe bar convention as the long overlay.
Calculation: Possible short trap = low < lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback AND close > that lowest low AND high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= rejection threshold. Possible long trap = high > highest high over the preceding sweep lookback AND close < that highest high AND high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= rejection threshold. Confirmed short trap = possible short trap on the preceding bar AND close > preceding high. Confirmed long trap = possible long trap on the preceding bar AND close < preceding low.
Calculation: Tracked entry = close of the latest enabled long or short overlay; a new tracked overlay replaces any unresolved tracked entry before that bar's exit check. Capture ATR on the entry bar. Long take-profit and stop levels are entry multiplied by 1 plus the take-profit percentage and 1 minus the stop percentage, respectively, or entry plus the take-profit ATR multiple and minus the stop ATR multiple, respectively. Short levels reverse those signs. Use percentages as fractions and the captured entry-bar ATR for the ATR mode.
Long: force score >= 0.
Short: force score < 0.
Long: bull impulse OR bull burst OR charging AND force score > 0 OR bull recovery OR bull memory AND no critical event AND no bull burst AND no bear burst AND no charging AND bars since the most recent critical event > aftershock lookback.
Short: bear impulse OR bear burst OR charging AND force score < 0 OR bear recovery OR bear memory AND no critical event AND no bull burst AND no bear burst AND no charging AND bars since the most recent critical event > aftershock lookback.
Long: (possible short trap in possible mode OR confirmed short trap in confirmed mode) AND (bars since the preceding short-trap signal > trap cooldown OR no preceding short-trap signal exists).
Short: (possible long trap in possible mode OR confirmed long trap in confirmed mode) AND (bars since the preceding long-trap signal > trap cooldown OR no preceding long-trap signal exists).
Long: long overlay.
Short: short overlay.
Exit: long, bar index > tracked entry bar index AND (high >= long take-profit level OR low <= long stop level).
Exit: short, bar index > tracked entry bar index AND (low <= short take-profit level OR high >= short stop level).
