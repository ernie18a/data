Calculation: Trend = bullish when shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close AND close > shorter EMA; bearish when shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < shorter EMA; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR / close when close != 0; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > volume SMA × a multiplier OR (volume − volume SMA) / volume standard deviation > a threshold; use 0 for the standardized value when standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(a × (close − low) / range + b × body direction + c × body power × (body direction − 0.5) + d, 0, 1), where range = max(high − low, a positive price floor), body direction = 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, otherwise 0.5, and body power = abs(close − open) / range.
Calculation: Pressure delta = rolling sum of volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1) divided by rolling sum of volume, expressed as a percentage; use 0 when the denominator is 0. Cumulative volume delta is the cumulative sum of volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1); its signal is an EMA of that cumulative sum.
Calculation: Auction distance = (close − session VWAP of typical price) / ATR when ATR > 0; otherwise 0. Bullish auction = auction distance < negative band threshold AND close > open; bearish auction = auction distance > positive band threshold AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = lower wick > max(abs(close − open), positive price floor) × wick multiplier AND close > open AND close > low + range × upper close-position threshold. Bearish rejection = upper wick > max(abs(close − open), positive price floor) × wick multiplier AND close < open AND close < low + range × lower close-position threshold. Range uses the same positive price floor as estimated buy share.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding structure lookback AND close > that lowest low AND close > open. Bearish sweep = high > highest high of the preceding structure lookback AND close < that highest high AND close < open.
Calculation: Compression = (highest high − lowest low over a lookback including the current bar) / close × 100 < ATR percentage × a multiplier AND abs(shorter EMA − longer EMA) < ATR × another multiplier; use 0 for the first percentage when close = 0. Bullish expansion = previous bar was compressed AND close > highest high of a preceding breakout lookback AND volume expansion. Bearish expansion = previous bar was compressed AND close < lowest low of that preceding breakout lookback AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when trend is bullish AND close >= session VWAP; bearish when trend is bearish AND close <= session VWAP; otherwise flat. Initialize bullish-hold, bullish-to-bearish, bearish-hold, bearish-to-bullish, flat-to-bullish and flat-to-bearish counts with equal positive pseudocounts. On each completed bar, increment the matching count for the previous-to-current regime transition; transitions to flat do not increment a count. Bullish continuation = bullish-hold / (bullish-hold + bullish-to-bearish); bearish continuation = bearish-hold / (bearish-hold + bearish-to-bullish); flat-break strength = max(flat-to-bullish, flat-to-bearish) / (flat-to-bullish + flat-to-bearish).
Calculation: Long score = capped weighted sum of bullish trend; close >= session VWAP; ATR percentage >= minimum; ADX > threshold AND positive DI > negative DI; RSI > midpoint AND RSI >= previous RSI; volume expansion; pressure delta > positive strong threshold, otherwise pressure delta > 0 with a smaller weight; cumulative volume delta > its signal AND signal >= previous signal; bullish auction; bullish rejection; bullish sweep; bullish expansion; and bullish continuation > threshold, otherwise flat-break strength > threshold AND regime is bullish with a smaller weight. Each true component contributes its assigned weight; cap the sum at a maximum.
Calculation: Short score = capped weighted sum of bearish trend; close <= session VWAP; ATR percentage >= minimum; ADX > threshold AND negative DI > positive DI; RSI < midpoint AND RSI <= previous RSI; volume expansion; pressure delta < negative strong threshold, otherwise pressure delta < 0 with a smaller weight; cumulative volume delta < its signal AND signal <= previous signal; bearish auction; bearish rejection; bearish sweep; bearish expansion; and bearish continuation > threshold, otherwise flat-break strength > threshold AND regime is bearish with a smaller weight. Use the corresponding long-score weights and the same cap.
Long: Long score > short score + score separation.
Short: Short score > long score + score separation.
Long: ATR percentage >= minimum AND (trend filter disabled OR trend is bullish) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND long score >= setup threshold AND long score > short score + score separation AND (no previous setup signal OR bars since either setup signal > cooldown).
Short: ATR percentage >= minimum AND (trend filter disabled OR trend is bearish) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND short score >= setup threshold AND short score > long score + score separation AND (no previous setup signal OR bars since either setup signal > cooldown).
Long: Bullish sweep OR bullish expansion OR bullish auction.
Short: Bearish sweep OR bearish expansion OR bearish auction.
Long: Pressure delta >= 0.
Short: Pressure delta < 0.
Long: Cumulative volume delta > its signal AND signal >= previous signal.
Short: Cumulative volume delta < its signal AND signal <= previous signal.
Long: Close >= session VWAP.
Short: Close < session VWAP.
