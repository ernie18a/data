Calculation: Opening range = the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall in the configured opening-range window, accumulated until the window ends; reset at each exchange-day boundary. Its midpoint is (high + low) / 2, its width is high - low, and its average volume is the mean volume of those chart bars.
Calculation: Confirmation close = if higher-timeframe confirmation is selected and the chart timeframe is lower than the configured confirmation timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe close, available for a decision only on the first chart bar after that higher-timeframe bar completes; otherwise the current chart-bar close.
Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR calculated on daily bars using the last completed daily bar; the daily timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Breakout state = reset all breakout, failure, and retest flags and the active direction at each exchange-day boundary. A breakout decision sets its side's breakout flag and makes that side active. A failed-breakout decision sets its side's failure flag and clears the active direction. A retest decision sets its side's retest flag. Flags remain set until the next reset.
Long: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trading window AND no long breakout has occurred this day AND confirmation close > opening-range high AND (volume filter disabled OR chart-bar volume >= volume multiplier * opening-range average volume) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR chart close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (EMA filter disabled OR chart close > EMA of chart closes) AND (range filter disabled OR opening-range width >= minimum multiplier * daily ATR AND opening-range width <= maximum multiplier * daily ATR) AND (one-breakout-per-day option disabled OR neither side has had a breakout this day).
Short: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trading window AND no short breakout has occurred this day AND confirmation close < opening-range low AND (volume filter disabled OR chart-bar volume >= volume multiplier * opening-range average volume) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR chart close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (EMA filter disabled OR chart close < EMA of chart closes) AND (range filter disabled OR opening-range width >= minimum multiplier * daily ATR AND opening-range width <= maximum multiplier * daily ATR) AND (one-breakout-per-day option disabled OR neither side has had a breakout this day) AND no long breakout decision occurs on this bar.
Long: Retests enabled AND opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trading window AND a long breakout has occurred this day AND that breakout has not failed AND no long retest has occurred this day AND no long breakout decision occurs on this bar AND low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high.
Short: Retests enabled AND opening range is complete AND chart bar is in the trading window AND a short breakout has occurred this day AND that breakout has not failed AND no short retest has occurred this day AND no short breakout decision occurs on this bar AND high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low.
Short: Failed-breakout signals enabled AND opening range is complete AND a long breakout has occurred this day AND that breakout has not failed AND the active direction is long AND confirmation close < opening-range high AND confirmation close > opening-range low.
Long: Failed-breakout signals enabled AND opening range is complete AND a short breakout has occurred this day AND that breakout has not failed AND the active direction is short AND confirmation close < opening-range high AND confirmation close > opening-range low.
Long: Opening range is complete AND close > opening-range midpoint.
Short: Opening range is complete AND close <= opening-range midpoint.
Long: Opening range is complete AND (no previous opening-range midpoint is available OR opening-range midpoint >= most recent previous opening-range midpoint).
Short: Opening range is complete AND opening-range midpoint < most recent previous opening-range midpoint.
