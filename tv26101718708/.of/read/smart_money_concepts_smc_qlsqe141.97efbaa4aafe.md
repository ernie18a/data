Calculation: SwingHigh = the high of the most recent confirmed pivot high on the chart timeframe (a bar whose high is the highest of the same number of bars on each side; confirmed only after the right-side bars have completed). It counts as unbroken from confirmation until the first bar whose close is above it; a newly confirmed pivot high replaces it and resets it to unbroken.
Calculation: SwingLow = the low of the most recent confirmed pivot low on the chart timeframe (a bar whose low is the lowest of the same number of bars on each side; confirmed only after the right-side bars have completed). It counts as unbroken from confirmation until the first bar whose close is below it; a newly confirmed pivot low replaces it and resets it to unbroken.
Long: (close > SwingHigh AND SwingHigh is unbroken) OR (high of 2 bars ago < current low AND close of previous bar > open of previous bar)
Short: (close < SwingLow AND SwingLow is unbroken) OR (low of 2 bars ago > current high AND close of previous bar < open of previous bar)
