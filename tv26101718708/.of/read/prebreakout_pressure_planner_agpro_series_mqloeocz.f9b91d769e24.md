Calculation: Clamp(x) = max(0, min(100, x)); all weights, thresholds, lookbacks, offsets, and multipliers below are parameters.
Calculation: Range = highest high and lowest low over the range lookback ending at the previous bar; it is valid when the upper boundary exceeds the lower boundary. If invalid, use the current high and low as boundaries. Range height is the boundary difference, floored at a positive price-precision parameter. ATR is floored at that parameter and falls back to true range when unavailable.
Calculation: Range close location = clamp((close - lower boundary) / range height, 0, 1). Candle close location = clamp((close - low) / max(high - low, price-precision parameter), 0, 1). Body efficiency = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, price-precision parameter).
Calculation: Compression score = Clamp(100 - (range height / ATR - compression floor) / max(compression limit - compression floor, denominator floor) * 100).
Calculation: Bull slope score = Clamp(slope midpoint + linear regression slope of lows / ATR * slope multiplier); bear slope score = Clamp(slope midpoint - linear regression slope of highs / ATR * slope multiplier). Use the same slope lookback for both and substitute zero for an unavailable slope.
Calculation: Bull candle score = body efficiency * body weight + candle close location * close weight when close >= open; otherwise candle close location * opposite-candle weight. Bear candle score = body efficiency * body weight + (1 - candle close location) * close weight when close <= open; otherwise (1 - candle close location) * opposite-candle weight.
Calculation: Bull directional score = weighted sum of bull slope score, 100 * range close location, and bull candle score. Bear directional score = the same weighted sum of bear slope score, 100 * (1 - range close location), and bear candle score. The active direction is bullish when the bull score >= the bear score and bearish otherwise; its directional, candle, and range-close scores are those of the active direction.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume) when the SMA is positive, otherwise 1; substitute current volume for an unavailable SMA. Relative-volume change = current relative volume minus relative volume at a prior lookback, substituting current relative volume if unavailable. Volume score = Clamp(clamp((relative volume - volume floor) / max(volume support threshold - volume floor, denominator floor) * volume multiplier, 0, volume cap) + clamp(relative-volume change * change multiplier, negative change cap, positive change cap) + volume offset).
Calculation: Pressure score = Clamp(weighted sum of compression score, active directional score, volume score, active candle score, and active range-close score).
Calculation: Upper probe = valid range AND high > upper boundary + ATR * corridor multiplier AND close <= upper boundary. Lower probe = valid range AND low < lower boundary - ATR * corridor multiplier AND close >= lower boundary.
Calculation: Fakeout score = Clamp(weighted sum of (100 - active candle score), (100 - volume score), a wide-range penalty when range height / ATR > compression limit, and a probe penalty when either probe is true).
Long: Bull directional score - bear directional score >= side separation threshold.
Short: Bear directional score - bull directional score >= side separation threshold.
Long: Valid range AND lower boundary <= close AND close <= upper boundary AND (neither probe is true OR fakeout score < fakeout threshold) AND bull directional score - bear directional score >= side separation threshold AND pressure score >= pressure threshold.
Short: Valid range AND lower boundary <= close AND close <= upper boundary AND (neither probe is true OR fakeout score < fakeout threshold) AND bear directional score - bull directional score >= side separation threshold AND pressure score >= pressure threshold.
Long: Valid range AND close > upper boundary + ATR * corridor multiplier AND pressure score >= watch threshold.
Short: Valid range AND close < lower boundary - ATR * corridor multiplier AND pressure score >= watch threshold.
