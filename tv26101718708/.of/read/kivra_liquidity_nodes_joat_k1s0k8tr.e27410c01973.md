Calculation: Profile = Divide the lookback's lowest low to highest high into equal price rows, using at least one minimum price increment for the range. If mean lookback volume is positive and available, use each bar's volume; otherwise use its high-low range, floored at one minimum price increment, divided by the minimum price increment. Replace nonpositive bar volume with one. Allocate each bar's volume to each row in proportion to their price overlap divided by the bar's high-low range, floored at one minimum price increment. Smooth each row's allocated volume using the weighted average of its lower neighbor, itself, and its upper neighbor, with the center weighted twice as much as either neighbor; repeat the edge row for a missing neighbor.
Calculation: Profile bias share = For each lookback bar, clamp its close's fractional position within its high-low range to zero through one, with the range floored at one minimum price increment. Estimate its bullish share as an upward-candle base plus an upward-candle coefficient times that position when close >= open, otherwise as a downward-candle base plus a downward-candle coefficient times that position; clamp the result to zero through one. Allocate bullish and bearish volume to rows using that share and its complement, respectively, and smooth them as in Profile. Divide summed smoothed bullish volume by the greater of the minimum price increment and summed smoothed bullish plus bearish volume, and clamp to zero through one.
Calculation: Point of control = The midpoint of the first price row with the highest positive smoothed Profile volume; use the first row if none is positive.
Long: Profile bias share > upper bias threshold
Short: Profile bias share < lower bias threshold
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the point of control, comparing the current close and point of control with their respective previous-bar values.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the point of control, comparing the current close and point of control with their respective previous-bar values.
