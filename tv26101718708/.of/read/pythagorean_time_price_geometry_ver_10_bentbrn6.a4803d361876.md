Calculation: Anchor swing = Select either manual or automatic mode. In manual mode, on each bar use the high and low at their respective positive bar offsets if the high exceeds the low; otherwise retain the previous swing. In automatic mode, store confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks; when a new pivot of either kind is stored, remove stored pivots of that kind older than the maximum history. For each stored high-low pair in oldest-first order, require different bar indices, positive range R at least ATR times the minimum-range multiplier, duration D within the search radius of the expected duration, and R/D no greater than the ideal ratio times the maximum-ratio multiplier, where ideal ratio = ATR times the minimum-range multiplier / expected duration. If a swing exists, also require R at least its range times the stability threshold OR the high above its high plus its range times the boundary threshold OR the low below its low minus its range times the boundary threshold; reject the pair if both pivot indices are less than the base period away from their corresponding existing indices. Score each eligible pair as 1/(abs(R/D - ideal ratio)/(ideal ratio × 0.1 + 0.0001) + 0.1) + 1/(abs(D - expected duration)/search radius + 0.1) + R/(ATR × minimum-range multiplier + 0.0001). Initialize the best score to -1 on each bar. Replace the best pair when its score exceeds 1.75 times the best score AND either no swing exists OR its score exceeds 2.5 times the best score and both pivot indices are more than twice the base period away from their corresponding existing indices. If the best score is positive and the current bar is at or beyond the swing lock, assign the best pair and lock it for the expected duration. The pivot lookback and search radius are shorter than the base period; maximum history is longer than the base period; expected duration is derived from the base period.
Calculation: Grid = For a valid anchor swing, let R be its high minus its low and D the absolute difference between their bar indices. Cell height = R × selected Pythagorean triple's second leg / first leg × scale. Grid origin is either negative cell height or max(min(swing low, lowest low over a lookback) - 2 × cell height, 0), according to the selected origin mode. Bottom = origin + truncate((close - origin)/cell height) × cell height; top = bottom + cell height; center = bottom + cell height/2.
Calculation: Time node = For either the base or doubled swing duration L, let P = round(sqrt(2) × L) and offset = (current bar index - earlier anchor bar index) remainder P. A node occurs when P > 0 AND offset equals round(sqrt(1.25) × L) remainder P OR round(L) remainder P OR P OR truncate(L) remainder P.
Long: A valid anchor swing exists AND close > grid center.
Short: A valid anchor swing exists AND close <= grid center.
Long: A valid anchor swing exists AND close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR × minimum-body multiplier AND (time node OR abs(close - grid bottom) < cell height × grid tolerance).
Short: A valid anchor swing exists AND close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR × minimum-body multiplier AND (time node OR abs(close - grid top) < cell height × grid tolerance).
