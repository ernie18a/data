Calculation: Bull/Bear score = 3-bar EMA of the weighted average of eight directional scores, scaled 0–100: WaveTrend (oscillator versus its signal and zero/oversold levels), fast/slow/baseline EMA alignment, close versus VWAP and ATR, RSI regime, MACD histogram sign and slope, ADX regime, volume versus its moving average with candle direction, and squeeze state with momentum direction; use separate bull and bear factor scores.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA versus slow EMA on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Squeeze release = Bollinger Bands were inside Keltner Channels on the prior bar and are no longer inside on the current bar; squeeze momentum is linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint of the 20-bar high/low range and the 20-bar close SMA, over 20 bars.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price − ATR × configurable stop multiple; initial short stop = entry price + ATR × configurable stop multiple. Targets are on the profit side at configurable risk/reward multiples of the initial risk. After target 1, if trailing is enabled, move the stop to breakeven; after target 2 move it to target 1; after target 3 move it to target 2. Also after target 1, trail at 0.5 ATR from the bar low for longs or bar high for shorts; keep the tighter stop.
Long: (fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA was below slow EMA on the prior bar) OR (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bull score ≥ configurable threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (squeeze release AND squeeze momentum > 0).
Short: (fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA was above slow EMA on the prior bar) OR (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bear score ≥ configurable threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA) OR (squeeze release AND squeeze momentum < 0).
Exit: Long, low ≤ current long stop.
Exit: Short, high ≥ current short stop.
