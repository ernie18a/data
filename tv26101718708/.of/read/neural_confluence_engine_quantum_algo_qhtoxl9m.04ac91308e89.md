Calculation: Fast, slow, and baseline EMAs are EMAs of close with respectively longer lookbacks; higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs use the same respective lookbacks and the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Squeeze is active when the lower Bollinger Band is above the lower Keltner Channel and the upper Bollinger Band is below the upper Keltner Channel. A squeeze releases when it was active on the previous bar and either Bollinger Band is outside its corresponding Keltner Channel on the current bar. Both envelopes use the same close SMA as their center; Bollinger width is a standard-deviation multiple and Keltner width is an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum is the endpoint of a linear regression of close minus half the SMA of the sum of the rolling high-low midpoint and the close SMA. The rolling high, low, close SMA, and regression use the squeeze lookback; the outer SMA uses a shorter lookback.
Calculation: WaveTrend oscillator is EMA((HLC3 - EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)) / (scale × EMA(abs(HLC3 - EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)), channel lookback)), average lookback); its signal is a shorter SMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Directional score is an EMA of the weighted average of eight factors, scaled to a percentage. For bullish and bearish scores respectively, assign factors as follows, evaluating each case in order: WaveTrend: oscillator above/below its signal gives the highest factor when below the lower/above the upper oscillator threshold, the next factor when above/below zero, otherwise a lower positive factor; otherwise zero. EMA alignment: fast above/below slow and slow above/below baseline gives the highest factor, fast above/below slow alone gives a lower factor, otherwise zero. VWAP: close above/below VWAP by more than an ATR gives the highest factor, above/below VWAP otherwise gives a lower factor, otherwise zero. RSI: above the upper/below the lower threshold gives the highest factor, above/below the midpoint otherwise gives a lower factor, otherwise zero. MACD histogram: positive/negative and rising/falling gives the highest factor, positive/negative otherwise gives a lower factor, otherwise zero. ADX: above an upper threshold gives the highest factor, above a lower threshold gives a lower factor, otherwise a still lower positive factor; this factor is the same for both directions. Volume: volume above its SMA and close above/below open gives the highest factor; volume above a fraction of its SMA otherwise gives a lower factor; otherwise zero. Squeeze: release with positive/negative momentum gives the highest factor, an active squeeze otherwise gives a lower positive factor, otherwise zero. Use separate fixed weights for the eight factors.
Calculation: Entry risk is ATR on the entry bar multiplied by a stop multiplier. For a long/short, the initial stop is entry price minus/plus entry risk; each of three profit targets is entry price plus/minus entry risk multiplied by its respective reward multiple, ordered from nearest to farthest.
Calculation: For each trade, initialize the trailing stop to the initial stop. Before testing the stop on each bar, including the entry bar, update it if trailing is enabled: a first-target hit raises/lowers it to at least/at most entry price; a second-target hit raises/lowers it to at least/at most the first target; a third-target hit raises/lowers it to at least/at most the second target. After the first target has been hit, also raise/lower it to at least/at most the current low/high minus/plus an ATR multiple. Target hits use high for longs and low for shorts and remain recorded until a new entry or stop hit. A new entry resets all targets, hit states, and the stop before testing that bar.
Long: Fast EMA > slow EMA AND previous fast EMA < previous slow EMA.
Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA AND previous fast EMA > previous slow EMA.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bullish directional score >= score threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA).
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bearish directional score >= score threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA).
Long: Squeeze releases AND squeeze momentum > zero.
Short: Squeeze releases AND squeeze momentum < zero.
Exit: long, high >= any profit target not previously hit.
Exit: short, low <= any profit target not previously hit.
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, high >= trailing stop.
