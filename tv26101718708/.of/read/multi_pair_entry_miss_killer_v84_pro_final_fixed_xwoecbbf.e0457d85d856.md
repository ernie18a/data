Calculation: Prior-day high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low so far in the configured session on the current day, resetting at each new day; include the current bar before testing a sweep.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below AND close above any of prior-day low, session low, or the lowest low of a longer lookback excluding the current bar. Bearish sweep = high above AND close below any of prior-day high, session high, or the highest high of the same lookback excluding the current bar. A recent sweep occurred within the configured number of bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close above the highest high of a shorter lookback excluding the current bar. Bearish structure break = close below the lowest low of that lookback. Bullish displacement = close above open AND candle body greater than an ATR multiple; bearish displacement = close below open AND candle body greater than the same ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low above the high two bars earlier; bearish gap = high below the low two bars earlier. Bullish block = previous close below previous open AND close above previous high; bearish block = previous close above previous open AND close below previous low.
Calculation: Bullish zone = the most recent bullish gap's range from the high two bars earlier to the current low, or the previous bar's low-to-high range when a bullish block occurs; a block takes precedence if both occur. Bearish zone = the most recent bearish gap's range from the current high to the low two bars earlier, or the previous bar's low-to-high range when a bearish block occurs; a block takes precedence if both occur. Retain each side's zone until replaced. Bullish full retest = close within the bullish zone; bullish shallow retest = low at or below its upper bound AND close above its lower bound. Bearish full retest = close within the bearish zone; bearish shallow retest = high at or above its lower bound AND close below its upper bound.
Calculation: Directional trend score = 2 when chart close is on the directional side of its EMA AND the close of the first higher-timeframe bar is on that side of its same-length EMA AND at least one of the next two successively higher-timeframe closes is on that side of its same-length EMA; otherwise 1 when only the chart and first higher-timeframe tests pass, otherwise 0. For each higher timeframe use its last completed bar on historical chart bars; a realtime chart bar uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Equality fails the directional test.
Calculation: Bullish score = bullish trend score + 2 for a recent bullish sweep + 2 for a bullish structure break + 1 for a bullish gap or block + 2 for a bullish full or shallow retest + 1 when the bar is in the configured trading session in Asia/Karachi time + 1 when close is at or below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback + 1 when volume is at or below a multiple of its SMA OR close is at or above open + 1 for neutral manual correlation. Bearish score = bearish trend score + 2 for a recent bearish sweep + 2 for a bearish structure break + 1 for a bearish gap or block + 2 for a bearish full or shallow retest + 1 for the same session + 1 when close is above the same midpoint + 1 when volume is at or below the same SMA multiple OR close is at or below open + 1 for neutral manual correlation. Use automatic volume proxy and neutral correlation for data-only reproduction.
Calculation: Bullish model = recent bullish sweep AND bullish structure break AND close at or below the bullish zone midpoint plus an ATR multiple AND ((bullish displacement AND (bullish full or shallow retest OR bullish gap OR bullish block)) OR (bullish gap AND bullish block AND (bullish full or shallow retest))). Bearish model = recent bearish sweep AND bearish structure break AND close at or above the bearish zone midpoint minus the same ATR multiple AND ((bearish displacement AND (bearish full or shallow retest OR bearish gap OR bearish block)) OR (bearish gap AND bearish block AND (bearish full or shallow retest))).
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of entry-bar low and the lowest low of the structure lookback preceding entry, minus an ATR multiple measured at entry. Short stop = the higher of entry-bar high and the highest high of the structure lookback preceding entry, plus the same ATR multiple. Final target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a configured reward-to-risk multiple times the entry-to-stop distance; retain stop and target from entry.
Long: A confirmed bar has low below AND close above prior-day low OR session low OR the preceding longer-lookback low.
Short: A confirmed bar has high above AND close below prior-day high OR session high OR the preceding longer-lookback high.
Long: Chart close is above its EMA OR a higher-timeframe close is above its same-length EMA, using the higher-timeframe bar as specified for the trend score.
Short: Chart close is below its EMA OR a higher-timeframe close is at or below its same-length EMA, using the higher-timeframe bar as specified for the trend score.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe daily close is above its open OR close is above open OR bullish structure break OR bullish gap OR bullish block OR volume is above its SMA multiple AND close is above open OR bullish score is above bearish score.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe daily close is below its open OR close is below open OR bearish structure break OR bearish gap OR bearish block OR volume is above its SMA multiple AND close is below open OR bearish score is above bullish score.
Long: On a confirmed bar, no trade is active AND bars since the last entry are greater than the cooldown, or no entry has occurred yet, AND current-day entry count is below its limit AND bullish score is at least the threshold AND bullish model is true AND bullish score is at least bearish score.
Short: On a confirmed bar, no trade is active AND bars since the last entry are greater than the cooldown, or no entry has occurred yet, AND current-day entry count is below its limit AND bearish score is at least the threshold AND bearish model is true AND bearish score is greater than bullish score.
Exit: long, high is at or above the final target OR low is at or below the long stop OR bars since entry are greater than the expiry limit OR a new day begins.
Exit: short, low is at or below the final target OR high is at or above the short stop OR bars since entry are greater than the expiry limit OR a new day begins.
