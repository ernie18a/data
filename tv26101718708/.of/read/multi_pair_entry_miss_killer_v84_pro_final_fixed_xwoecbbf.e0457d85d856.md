Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = current bar closes above at least one of the previous day’s low, the current day’s Asia-session low, or the lowest low of the preceding 20 bars, after its low fell below that level; bearish liquidity sweep = current bar closes below at least one of the previous day’s high, the current day’s Asia-session high, or the highest high of the preceding 20 bars, after its high rose above that level. Sweep conditions require a completed chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish sweep = corresponding liquidity sweep occurred within the preceding 20 chart-timeframe bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish MSS = close above the highest high of the preceding 10 chart-timeframe bars; bearish MSS = close below the lowest low of the preceding 10 chart-timeframe bars. Bullish displacement = completed bar closes above its open and absolute close-open distance exceeds 0.45 ATR; bearish displacement = completed bar closes below its open and absolute close-open distance exceeds 0.45 ATR. ATR uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish CISD = recent bullish sweep AND bullish MSS AND bullish displacement; bearish CISD = recent bearish sweep AND bearish MSS AND bearish displacement.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = completed bar low above the high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = completed bar high below the low two bars earlier. Bullish order block = prior bar closes below its open AND current close above prior high; bearish order block = prior bar closes above its open AND current close below prior low.
Calculation: Bullish POI bounds = bounds of the latest bullish FVG or bullish order block, updated when either occurs; bearish POI bounds = bounds of the latest bearish FVG or bearish order block, updated when either occurs. An FVG’s bounds are the two-bar-ago high and current low for bullish, and current high and two-bar-ago low for bearish. An order block’s bounds are the prior bar’s low and high. Bullish Unicorn = bullish FVG AND bullish order block on the same bar; bearish Unicorn = bearish FVG AND bearish order block on the same bar.
Calculation: Bullish retest = close is within the bullish POI bounds; shallow bullish retest = low at or below the bullish POI top AND close above its bottom. Bearish retest = close is within the bearish POI bounds; shallow bearish retest = high at or above the bearish POI bottom AND close below its top.
Calculation: Too-far bullish = close exceeds the midpoint of the bullish POI bounds by more than 2 ATR; too-far bearish = close is below the midpoint of the bearish POI bounds by more than 2 ATR. ATR uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish entry model = any of: (inside configured silver-time session AND recent bullish sweep AND bullish CISD AND (bullish FVG OR bullish order block) AND (bullish retest OR shallow bullish retest OR bullish displacement model) AND NOT too-far bullish); (recent bullish sweep AND (bullish MSS OR bullish CISD) AND bullish Unicorn AND (bullish retest OR shallow bullish retest) AND NOT too-far bullish); (recent bullish sweep AND bullish CISD AND bullish displacement AND (bullish FVG OR bullish order block) AND NOT bullish retest AND NOT too-far bullish); (recent bullish sweep AND bullish CISD AND (bullish retest OR shallow bullish retest) AND NOT too-far bullish); or (inside configured trading session AND recent bullish sweep AND bullish CISD AND (bullish retest OR shallow bullish retest OR bullish displacement model) AND NOT too-far bullish). Bullish displacement model = recent bullish sweep AND bullish CISD AND bullish displacement AND (bullish FVG OR bullish order block) AND NOT bullish retest AND NOT too-far bullish.
Calculation: Bearish entry model = the corresponding bearish conditions: replace bullish with bearish throughout the bullish entry model definition, including POI, sweep, CISD, displacement, retests, Unicorn, and too-far conditions.
Calculation: Chart trend = close above the chart-timeframe EMA, or below it. Higher-timeframe trend = close above that timeframe’s EMA, or below it, calculated on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed bar. Short-term EMA lookback is the same for chart and higher-timeframe trends. Bullish trend alignment = chart and lower of the two requested higher-timeframe trends are bullish; bearish trend alignment = both are bearish. Soft bullish alignment = bullish trend alignment AND (higher-timeframe trend from the first requested higher timeframe is bullish OR trend from the second is not bearish). Soft bearish alignment = bearish trend alignment AND (first requested higher-timeframe trend is bearish OR second is not bullish).
Calculation: Bullish score = 2 for soft bullish alignment, otherwise 1 for bullish trend alignment, otherwise 0; plus 2 for recent bullish sweep; plus 2 for bullish CISD or bullish MSS; plus 1 for bullish FVG or bullish order block; plus 2 for bullish retest or shallow bullish retest; plus 1 inside configured trading session; plus 1 when close is below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low of the preceding 50 chart-timeframe bars; plus 1 when configured footprint mode is bullish delta, neutral, or automatic mode has no volume spike or a neutral spike; plus 1 when configured SMT state is bullish or neutral. Automatic volume spike means volume exceeds its 20-bar average by the configured multiplier; automatic bullish delta means a spike with close above open.
Calculation: Bearish score = 2 for soft bearish alignment, otherwise 1 for bearish trend alignment, otherwise 0; plus 2 for recent bearish sweep; plus 2 for bearish CISD or bearish MSS; plus 1 for bearish FVG or bearish order block; plus 2 for bearish retest or shallow bearish retest; plus 1 inside configured trading session; plus 1 when close is above the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low of the preceding 50 chart-timeframe bars; plus 1 when configured footprint mode is bearish delta, neutral, or automatic mode has no volume spike or a neutral spike; plus 1 when configured SMT state is bearish or neutral. Automatic bearish delta means a volume spike with close below open.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the entry bar low and the lowest low of the preceding 10 chart-timeframe bars, minus 0.2 ATR. Short stop = the higher of the entry bar high and the highest high of the preceding 10 chart-timeframe bars, plus 0.2 ATR. Initial risk = absolute distance from entry price to stop. Third target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) initial risk multiplied by configured third-target risk/reward ratio.
Long: bullish liquidity sweep OR (bar is confirmed AND no position is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND daily trade limit is not reached AND news block is off AND bullish score meets configured minimum score AND bullish entry model AND bullish score is greater than or equal to bearish score).
Short: bearish liquidity sweep OR (bar is confirmed AND no position is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND daily trade limit is not reached AND news block is off AND bearish score meets configured minimum score AND bearish entry model AND bearish score is greater than bearish score).
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= third target OR bars since entry exceed configured setup expiry OR a new day begins.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= third target OR bars since entry exceed configured setup expiry OR a new day begins.
