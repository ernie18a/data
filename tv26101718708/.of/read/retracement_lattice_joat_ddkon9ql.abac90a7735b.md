Calculation: Swing anchors = Initialize the high and low to the highest high and lowest low over a fallback lookback and record their bar indices. Thereafter, independently replace each anchor when a pivot is confirmed: its high or low must be strictly greater or strictly less, respectively, than every corresponding value in an equal number of bars before and after it. If pivot volume validation is enabled, the pivot bar's volume must exceed its volume SMA multiplied by a threshold. Record the pivot bar's index. On each bar, set the swing direction to down if the low anchor is later than the high anchor, otherwise up; override it to up if close exceeds the high anchor, then override it to down if close is below the low anchor. Optionally reverse the resulting direction.
Calculation: Retracement level at ratio r = For an up swing, high anchor minus r times the anchor span; for a down swing, low anchor plus r times the anchor span. The anchor span is the greater of high anchor minus low anchor and an adjustable minimum price increment. The pocket is the interval between the retracement levels at ratios 0.618 and 0.786; its midpoint is the level at ratio 0.705.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = Down if the close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below its open, otherwise up; optionally reverse it using the same reversal setting as the swing direction.
Calculation: Pocket distance = The distance from close to the nearest pocket boundary if close is outside the pocket, otherwise zero.
Long: Swing direction = up AND close is inside the pocket AND close >= pocket midpoint AND close > open AND (trend filter disabled OR close >= close EMA) AND (higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe grid disabled OR higher-timeframe direction = swing direction) AND (volume sponsorship disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by its threshold).
Short: Swing direction = down AND close is inside the pocket AND close <= pocket midpoint AND close < open AND (trend filter disabled OR close <= close EMA) AND (higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe grid disabled OR higher-timeframe direction = swing direction) AND (volume sponsorship disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by its threshold).
Long: Swing direction = up AND close crosses above the pocket midpoint AND pocket distance <= ATR multiplied by a response-buffer factor AND (trend filter disabled OR close >= close EMA).
Short: Swing direction = down AND close crosses below the pocket midpoint AND pocket distance <= ATR multiplied by a response-buffer factor AND (trend filter disabled OR close <= close EMA).
