Calculation: Direction = Let fast, base, and slow be close EMAs with increasing lookbacks. A bullish-trend touch occurs when fast > base > slow, (base - base from a slope-lookback number of bars ago) / ATR >= the slope threshold, low <= fast + ATR × reach, and close < fast + ATR × close-reach. A bearish-trend touch reverses these inequalities and signs. If a touch occurred within the failure window, direction is short for the most recent bullish-trend touch or long for the most recent bearish-trend touch; a tie selects short. Otherwise direction is zero. The slope threshold and reach depend on sensitivity. If the earlier base EMA is unavailable, use the current base EMA.
Calculation: Directional measures = Let d = +1 for long and -1 for short. Control rail = base EMA + d × ATR × control buffer; invalidation rail = fast EMA - d × ATR × invalidation buffer. Control break = d × (close - control rail) / ATR. Depth = max(window high - fast EMA, 0) / ATR for long, or max(fast EMA - window low, 0) / ATR for short; the window uses the failure-window lookback and includes the current bar. Follow-through = (close - prior-window high) / ATR for long, or (prior-window low - close) / ATR for short; the follow-through window excludes the current bar. Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, a small positive price increment); directional close location is close location for long and 1 - close location for short. Target room = max(d × (prior structure extreme - close) / ATR, 0), using the prior structure-window high for long or low for short; if the extreme is unavailable or its directional distance is below the minimum room threshold, use the fallback room. The structure window excludes the current bar.
Calculation: Failure score = Round the weighted sum of depth, control-break, directional-close, volume, and follow-through scores, with respective fixed weights 0.23, 0.27, 0.20, 0.15, and 0.15. Clamp each component to 0–100. Depth score rises linearly from 0 at minimum depth to 100 at ideal depth, then falls linearly to 0 at excessive depth. Control-break and follow-through scores rise linearly from 0 at zero to 100 at their respective sensitivity-dependent thresholds. Directional-close score is 100 × directional close location / its sensitivity-dependent threshold, multiplied by 0.55 if d × (close - open) <= 0. Volume score is 100 × volume ratio / its sensitivity-dependent threshold.
