Calculation: Pre-market high (PMH) = highest high from 04:00 through 09:30 Eastern time on the current trading day; reset each trading day
Calculation: Pre-market low (PML) = lowest low from 04:00 through 09:30 Eastern time on the current trading day; reset each trading day
Long: Close crosses above PMH (current close > current PMH AND previous close ≤ previous PMH)
Short: Close crosses below PMH (current close < current PMH AND previous close ≥ previous PMH)
Long: Close crosses above PML (current close > current PML AND previous close ≤ previous PML)
Short: Close crosses below PML (current close < current PML AND previous close ≥ previous PML)
