Calculation: Williams %R = 100 × (close − highest high over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback)
Calculation: Fast smoothed Williams %R = weighted moving average of Williams %R over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Slow smoothed Williams %R = exponential moving average of Williams %R over a longer lookback
Long: close > EMA AND MACD histogram > 0 AND fast smoothed Williams %R > slow smoothed Williams %R
Short: close < EMA AND MACD histogram < 0 AND fast smoothed Williams %R < slow smoothed Williams %R
Exit: long, MACD histogram < 0 AND fast smoothed Williams %R < slow smoothed Williams %R AND fast smoothed Williams %R < -20
Exit: short, MACD histogram > 0 AND fast smoothed Williams %R > slow smoothed Williams %R AND fast smoothed Williams %R > -80
