Calculation: Context-series timeframe = chart timeframe by default; otherwise the selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. The script does not offset requests to completed bars, so a still-forming source bar may be used.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that remains the highest or lowest across the pivot lookback bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a new context-series pivot high or chart-series pivot low, with the latest context-series pivot high higher than the previous one, the latest chart-series pivot low higher than the previous one, and the latest context pivot high and chart pivot low within the allowed time separation. Apply the enabled filters: context close above its trend EMA, and/or context close above the highest prior context high over the push lookback.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a new context-series pivot low or chart-series pivot high, with the latest context-series pivot low lower than the previous one, the latest chart-series pivot high lower than the previous one, and the latest context pivot low and chart pivot high within the allowed time separation. Apply the enabled filters: context close below its trend EMA, and/or context close below the lowest prior context low over the push lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence active = a bullish divergence event occurred within the hold period; bearish divergence active = a bearish divergence event occurred within the hold period. A new event is allowed only if more than the minimum signal gap has elapsed since the previous event of that direction.
Calculation: Context-series impulse = current context high minus low is greater than its EMA of high-minus-low times the impulse threshold.
Calculation: Bullish lead = context close below the lowest prior context low over the lead lookback AND context-series impulse AND chart close above the lowest prior chart low over the lead lookback.
Calculation: Bearish lead = context close above the highest prior context high over the lead lookback AND context-series impulse AND chart close below the highest prior chart high over the lead lookback.
Calculation: Bullish score = one point if context close is below its trend EMA, plus one if bullish divergence is active, plus one if bullish lead. Bearish score = one point if context close is above its trend EMA, plus one if bearish divergence is active, plus one if bearish lead.
Long: bullish divergence event OR (bullish divergence active AND NOT bearish divergence active) OR bullish lead OR bullish score greater than bearish score
Short: bearish divergence event OR (bearish divergence active AND NOT bullish divergence active) OR bearish lead OR bearish score greater than bullish score
