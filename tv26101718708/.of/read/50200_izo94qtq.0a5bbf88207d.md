Calculation: WeeklyMACD = standard MACD line and signal line computed on bars of a higher timeframe than the chart (weekly), using the value of the higher-timeframe bar still forming at the current chart bar
Calculation: HA = Heikin-Ashi candle (open, close) computed on the chart timeframe
Long: fast SMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above slow SMA(close, longer lookback)
Long: chart-timeframe MACD line crosses above its signal line AND ((fast SMA > slow SMA AND WeeklyMACD line < WeeklyMACD signal) OR (fast SMA < slow SMA AND WeeklyMACD line > WeeklyMACD signal))
Long: WeeklyMACD line crosses above WeeklyMACD signal
Long: position side is long AND close < entry price AND HA close > HA open AND previous HA close < previous HA open AND close < reference price * (1 - fixed percentage), where reference price = price of the last add since entry if any add exists, otherwise entry price
Short: fast SMA crosses below slow SMA
Short: WeeklyMACD line crosses below WeeklyMACD signal
Short: position side is short AND close > entry price AND HA close < HA open AND previous HA close > previous HA open AND close > reference price * (1 + fixed percentage), where reference price = price of the last add since entry if any add exists, otherwise entry price
Exit: short, fast SMA crosses above slow SMA
Exit: long, fast SMA crosses below slow SMA
Exit: short, WeeklyMACD line crosses above WeeklyMACD signal
Exit: long, WeeklyMACD line crosses below WeeklyMACD signal
