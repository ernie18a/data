Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side, confirmed only after the right-hand bars have elapsed; compare each pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind. Use chart-timeframe bars or higher-timeframe bars; for the higher timeframe, use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time.
Long: RSI on chart-timeframe bars or higher-timeframe bars > bullish threshold; for the higher timeframe, use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time.
Short: RSI on chart-timeframe bars or higher-timeframe bars <= bearish threshold; for the higher timeframe, use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed.
Long: A pivot high is confirmed AND its value > the preceding pivot high OR a pivot high is confirmed AND no preceding pivot high exists.
Short: A pivot low is confirmed AND its value < the preceding pivot low OR a pivot low is confirmed AND no preceding pivot low exists.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its value >= the preceding pivot low.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its value <= the preceding pivot high.
