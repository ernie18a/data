Calculation: Source bars = chart-timeframe bars or bars aggregated to a higher or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish gap = a source bar whose low exceeds the high two source bars earlier, whose preceding source bar closed above that earlier high, and whose gap width divided by that earlier high exceeds a threshold; its lower and upper bounds are that earlier high and the current low.
Calculation: Bearish gap = a source bar whose high is below the low two source bars earlier, whose preceding source bar closed below that earlier low, and whose gap width divided by the current high exceeds a threshold; its lower and upper bounds are the current high and that earlier low.
Calculation: Live gaps = track gaps separately by source timeframe. Merge a new gap into an existing live gap of the same direction when their overlap exceeds a threshold fraction of the smaller gap's height; replace the existing bounds with their union. Otherwise add the new gap once per source bar. On each chart bar, after detecting gaps, remove a bullish live gap when the latest source close is at or below its lower bound, or a bearish live gap when the latest source close is at or above its upper bound; alternatively use source low and high respectively instead of close.
Long: A new bullish gap is added.
Long: A live bearish gap is removed because the latest source close or, under the high/low alternative, source high is at or above its upper bound.
Long: Chart low is below a live bullish gap's upper bound AND chart high is above its lower bound AND the current chart bar is later than the gap's creation bar AND no earlier chart bar has touched that gap.
Short: A new bearish gap is added.
Short: A live bullish gap is removed because the latest source close or, under the high/low alternative, source low is at or below its lower bound.
Short: Chart low is below a live bearish gap's upper bound AND chart high is above its lower bound AND the current chart bar is later than the gap's creation bar AND no earlier chart bar has touched that gap.
