Calculation: Long initial stop = lowest low over a configurable chart-timeframe lookback minus ATR times a configurable buffer, or VWAP at entry, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: Short initial stop = highest high over a configurable chart-timeframe lookback plus ATR times a configurable buffer, or VWAP at entry, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: Initial risk = distance from entry price to the selected initial stop
Calculation: Long first target = entry price + initial risk × configured risk multiple
Calculation: Short first target = entry price − initial risk × configured risk multiple
Calculation: Long active stop = selected base stop; after the first target is hit and trailing is enabled, the maximum of entry price, chart-timeframe fast EMA, and selected base stop
Calculation: Short active stop = selected base stop; after the first target is hit and trailing is enabled, the minimum of entry price, chart-timeframe fast EMA, and selected base stop
Long: long entries enabled AND flat AND configured cooldown elapsed since the prior close AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA) AND (additional higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND (entry-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR volume / its chart-timeframe moving average over a configurable lookback ≥ configured threshold) AND low ≤ selected pullback EMA (fast or slow, according to configuration) AND close > fast EMA AND previous close ≤ current fast EMA
Short: short entries enabled AND flat AND configured cooldown elapsed since the prior close AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA) AND (additional higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND (entry-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR volume / its chart-timeframe moving average over a configurable lookback ≥ configured threshold) AND high ≥ selected pullback EMA (fast or slow, according to configuration) AND close < fast EMA AND previous close ≥ current fast EMA
Exit: long, low ≤ long active stop OR high ≥ long first target OR (first target not hit AND configured time-stop bar count reached AND close ≤ entry price)
Exit: short, high ≥ short active stop OR low ≤ short first target OR (first target not hit AND configured time-stop bar count reached AND close ≥ entry price)
