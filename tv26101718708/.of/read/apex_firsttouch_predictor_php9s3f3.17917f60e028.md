Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot whose high or low is respectively the highest or lowest over equal numbers of preceding and following bars; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each type.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on each of two higher timeframes, use the last completed bar and its confirmed swing levels; start at zero, set to +1 when close exceeds the latest swing high, set to -1 when close falls below the latest swing low, and otherwise retain the previous bias. Apply the low-break update after the high-break update. The regime timeframe is higher than the bias timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range position = (chart close - range midpoint) / (range width / 2), or zero if the range width is not positive; range high and low are the rolling highest high and lowest low on the bias higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Zones = retain up to a configurable maximum number of the most recently created zones separately for each of the four types; do not remove zones when touched. A bullish gap zone spans the high two bars ago to the current low when current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open; a bearish gap zone spans current high to the low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open. A bullish order-block zone spans the low to high of the most recent bearish candle within a lookback when close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high; a bearish order-block zone spans the low to high of the most recent bullish candle within a lookback when close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low. Create an order-block zone only when the respective candle exists. Zone midpoint is the average of its bounds.
Calculation: Liquidity difference = upper liquidity minus lower liquidity. For each side, cap at one a weighted sum of: the equal-extreme score, the previous completed daily bar's high or low proximity score, the zone-count score, and the nearest-zone proximity score. Count prior chart bars, excluding the current bar, whose high or low is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the rolling highest high or lowest low including the current bar; the equal-extreme score is the count less an offset, floored at zero, divided by a scale and capped at one. The daily proximity score is max(0, 1 - distance / an ATR-scaled distance) only when the previous daily high is above close or previous daily low is below close, respectively; otherwise it is zero. The daily bar is on a higher timeframe than the chart and is the last completed bar. The zone-count score is the number of retained zone midpoints strictly on that side of close plus one if any exists, divided by a scale and capped at one. The nearest-zone proximity score is max(0, 1 - distance to the nearest zone midpoint on that side / an ATR-scaled distance), or zero if no such zone exists.
Calculation: Signed candle average = sum of (close - open) / (high - low) over a lookback, treating zero-range bars as zero, divided by the lookback, multiplied by a scale and clamped to [-1, 1]. Use a longer lookback for momentum and a shorter lookback for displacement.
Calculation: Directional score = weighted sum of: a weighted combination of the two higher-timeframe biases; the negative of higher-timeframe range position clamped to [-1, 1]; liquidity difference; momentum multiplied by a session strength selected from configurable New York local-time intervals; displacement; and momentum multiplied by volatility expansion. Volatility expansion is (ATR / its longer-period simple moving average - 1) multiplied by a scale and clamped to [0, 1]. If the optional liquidity override is enabled and zones exist on both sides, floor the score at a positive level when liquidity difference >= a positive threshold AND distance to the upper zone <= a multiple of distance to the lower zone; cap the score at the corresponding negative level when liquidity difference <= the negative threshold AND distance to the lower zone <= that multiple of distance to the upper zone.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open
Short: high < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open
Long: close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND at least one bearish candle exists within the order-block lookback
Short: close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND at least one bullish candle exists within the order-block lookback
Long: directional score > 0
Short: directional score < 0
Long: directional score crosses above a positive high-confidence threshold
Short: directional score crosses below the negative high-confidence threshold
Long: bias of the bias higher timeframe > 0
Short: bias of the bias higher timeframe < 0
Long: higher-timeframe range position < a negative discount threshold
Short: higher-timeframe range position > a positive premium threshold
Long: liquidity difference > a positive liquidity threshold
Short: liquidity difference < the negative liquidity threshold
