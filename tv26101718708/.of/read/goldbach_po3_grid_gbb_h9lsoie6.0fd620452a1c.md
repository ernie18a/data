Calculation: Range = Select one source: (1) the highest high and lowest low over a chart-bar lookback obtained by multiplying a higher-timeframe lookback by the ratio of higher-timeframe duration to chart-timeframe duration and rounding to the nearest integer; if that lookback exceeds the platform history limit, use the highest high and lowest low over the higher-timeframe lookback, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; accept a new range only when the high and low occur on different bars; (2) the most recent confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed by the same number of bars on either side, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; or (3) selected positive manual bounds with high greater than low. Retain the previous valid range when the new range is invalid.
Calculation: Grid level at fraction p = range low + p × (range high − range low).
Calculation: Cycle state = At each selected calendar-period boundary or selected session start, reset the state to accumulation, bias to zero, and cycle open to that chart bar's open. On each bar, if accumulation and abs(close − cycle open) > accumulation threshold × ATR, change to manipulation and record whether close is above or below cycle open. While in manipulation, if close is on the opposite side of cycle open, abs(close − cycle open) ≥ displacement threshold × ATR, and the optional fair-value-gap filter passes, change to distribution, set bias long if the recorded side was below and short if above, and store abs(close − cycle open) / ATR. The bullish fair-value-gap filter is low > high two bars ago OR prior low > high three bars ago; the bearish filter is high < low two bars ago OR prior high < low three bars ago. Use the bullish filter for long bias and bearish filter for short bias. State persists until the next reset.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = A grid level is tapped when low ≤ level AND high ≥ level. Strict permits fractions 0.11 and 0.17 and their complements, requires cycle bias to match the level's direction, stored displacement ratio ≥ the strict threshold, and higher-timeframe close relative to its SMA to match the direction. Standard additionally permits fractions 0.41 and 0.47 and their complements, requires matching cycle bias, and optionally requires the same higher-timeframe filter. Loose additionally permits fraction 0.03 and its complement and requires cycle state to be manipulation or distribution, with no bias agreement required. For the higher-timeframe filter, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar: long requires close > SMA; short requires close ≤ SMA. All presets require at least the cooldown number of chart bars since the preceding signal of either direction. Evaluate eligible tapped levels from lowest fraction to highest and emit only the first signal.
Long: Cycle state changes to distribution AND cycle bias = long.
Short: Cycle state changes to distribution AND cycle bias = short.
Long: A permitted grid level below the range midpoint is tapped AND signal eligibility conditions hold.
Short: A permitted grid level above the range midpoint is tapped AND signal eligibility conditions hold.
