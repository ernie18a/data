Calculation: Dealing range = highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback on a higher timeframe, using completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Goldbach trigger levels = range low + (range high − range low) × 3%, 11%, 17%, 41%, 47%, 50%, 53%, 59%, 83%, 89%, or 97%; the 29%, 35%, 65%, 71%, 0%, and 100% levels do not trigger entries.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier, or previous low > high three bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier, or previous high < low three bars earlier.
Calculation: PO3 bias = bullish when price first moves below the cycle open by more than an ATR-based accumulation threshold, then closes above the open by at least an ATR-based displacement threshold (and a bullish fair value gap occurs within the recent-bar confirmation window if required); bearish is the inverse. PO3 phase is manipulation after the first threshold move and distribution after the reversal and displacement are confirmed.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close compared with its simple moving average, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: bar low ≤ any qualifying Goldbach trigger level below 50% ≤ bar high AND PO3 bias is bullish; Strict uses only 11%, 17%, 83%, and 89% triggers, requires displacement ≥ 1.5 × ATR and bullish higher-timeframe trend; Standard uses 11%, 17%, 41%, 47%, 53%, 59%, 83%, and 89% triggers and may require bullish higher-timeframe trend; Loose uses all trigger levels, including 3% and 97%, and requires PO3 phase to be manipulation or distribution.
Short: bar low ≤ any qualifying Goldbach trigger level above 50% ≤ bar high AND PO3 bias is bearish; Strict uses only 11%, 17%, 83%, and 89% triggers, requires displacement ≥ 1.5 × ATR and bearish higher-timeframe trend; Standard uses 11%, 17%, 41%, 47%, 53%, 59%, 83%, and 89% triggers and may require bearish higher-timeframe trend; Loose uses all trigger levels, including 3% and 97%, and requires PO3 phase to be manipulation or distribution.
