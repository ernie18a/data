Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a chart bar whose high is greater than or equal to the highs of a configurable number of preceding bars and greater than the highs of a configurable number of following bars, or whose low is less than or equal to the lows of those preceding bars and less than the lows of those following bars. Evaluate the pivot when the last following bar closes; the preceding and following counts can differ.
Calculation: Pivot candle proportions = for the confirmed pivot bar, range = high - low, body = abs(close - open) / range, upper shadow = (high - max(open, close)) / range, and lower shadow = (min(open, close) - low) / range; proportions are zero when range is zero.
Long: A pivot high is confirmed AND (no previous pivot high exists OR pivot high > previous pivot high)
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND (no previous pivot low exists OR pivot low > previous pivot low)
Long: A pivot high or low is confirmed AND pivot close > pivot open AND pivot range > 0 AND pivot body <= maximum body proportion AND ((pivot lower shadow >= minimum long-shadow proportion AND pivot upper shadow <= maximum short-shadow proportion) OR (pivot upper shadow >= minimum long-shadow proportion AND pivot lower shadow <= maximum short-shadow proportion))
Long: A pivot high or low is confirmed AND pivot close > pivot open AND preceding candle close < preceding candle open AND pivot close > preceding candle open AND pivot open < preceding candle close
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND a previous pivot high exists AND pivot high <= previous pivot high
Short: A pivot low is confirmed AND a previous pivot low exists AND pivot low <= previous pivot low
Short: A pivot high or low is confirmed AND pivot close < pivot open AND pivot range > 0 AND pivot body <= maximum body proportion AND ((pivot lower shadow >= minimum long-shadow proportion AND pivot upper shadow <= maximum short-shadow proportion) OR (pivot upper shadow >= minimum long-shadow proportion AND pivot lower shadow <= maximum short-shadow proportion))
Short: A pivot high or low is confirmed AND pivot close < pivot open AND preceding candle close > preceding candle open AND pivot close < preceding candle open AND pivot open > preceding candle close
