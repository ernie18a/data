Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the opening session so far; retain the final values until the next opening session. Its midpoint is the average of its high and low, and its width is high minus low.
Calculation: Opening-range bias = compare the current opening-range midpoint at the end of the opening session with the midpoint immediately before that session began; positive if greater, negative if less, otherwise neutral. Retain this bias until the next opening session ends.
Calculation: Long readiness = true at the start of each opening session; set false after a long signal and true again when close is below the opening-range midpoint.
Calculation: Short readiness = true at the start of each opening session; set false after a short signal and true again when close is above the opening-range midpoint.
Long: Long readiness = true AND ((bias filter disabled OR opening-range bias is not negative) AND close crosses above the opening-range high OR bias filter enabled AND opening-range bias is negative AND close crosses above the opening-range high plus a configurable fraction of the opening-range width).
Short: Short readiness = true AND ((bias filter disabled OR opening-range bias is not positive) AND close crosses below the opening-range low OR bias filter enabled AND opening-range bias is positive AND close crosses below the opening-range low minus a configurable fraction of the opening-range width).
