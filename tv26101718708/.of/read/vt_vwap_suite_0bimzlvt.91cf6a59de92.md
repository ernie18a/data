Calculation: V = anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(hlc3*volume) / cumulative sum(volume) over chart-timeframe bars since the start of the current anchor period (anchor is a calendar day, week, month, or custom session; resets at each new period; one anchor is selected)
Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on chart timeframe
Calculation: closeLoc = (close-low)/(high-low); bodyRatio = abs(close-open)/(high-low); if high=low, closeLoc=0.5 and bodyRatio=0
Calculation: volOK = volume >= simple moving average of volume (medium lookback), or true if that average is unavailable or zero
Long: (evaluated on a completed bar) low <= V AND close > V AND close > open AND closeLoc >= upper threshold AND bodyRatio >= body threshold AND abs(close-V)/ATR >= distance threshold AND volOK AND the current bar is not the first bar of the anchor period AND V > V of the previous bar AND bars since the last Long or Short signal > cooldown length (or no prior signal)
Short: (evaluated on a completed bar) high >= V AND close < V AND close < open AND closeLoc <= lower threshold (mirror of the upper threshold, i.e. 1 - upper threshold is not required, only a low-end cutoff) AND bodyRatio >= body threshold AND abs(close-V)/ATR >= distance threshold AND volOK AND the current bar is not the first bar of the anchor period AND V < V of the previous bar AND bars since the last Long or Short signal > cooldown length (or no prior signal)
