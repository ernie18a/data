Calculation: Candidate resistance = highest high over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Candidate support = lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Directional efficiency = absolute net close change over the lookback bars divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over those bars
Calculation: Frozen ATR = ATR from the bar before range activation
Calculation: Volume baseline = average volume over the baseline lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / volume baseline
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / candle spread
Long: At bar close, an active, unexpired range is in range state, and (after the sweep cooldown, low < support - boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND low >= support - maximum sweep depth × frozen ATR AND high <= resistance + boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close > support AND close < resistance AND close location >= strong-close threshold AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available) OR a prior spring sweep is within its test window and low > sweep low AND low <= support + half frozen ATR AND close > support AND close < resistance AND close location >= test close-location threshold AND candle spread <= frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR relative volume <= test-volume threshold AND current volume < sweep-bar volume) OR close > resistance + boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close > open AND close location >= breakout close-location threshold AND candle spread >= breakout spread threshold × frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available AND relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold) OR after an upward breakout, within its retest window and before any prior retest, low is within half frozen ATR of resistance AND close > resistance + boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close location >= retest close-location threshold AND candle spread <= frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available AND relative volume <= test-volume threshold))
Short: At bar close, an active, unexpired range is in range state, and (after the sweep cooldown, high > resistance + boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND high <= resistance + maximum sweep depth × frozen ATR AND low >= support - boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close < resistance AND close > support AND close location <= weak-close threshold AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available) OR a prior upthrust sweep is within its test window and high < sweep high AND high >= resistance - half frozen ATR AND close < resistance AND close > support AND close location <= test close-location threshold AND candle spread <= frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR relative volume <= test-volume threshold AND current volume < sweep-bar volume) OR close < support - boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close < open AND close location <= breakout close-location threshold AND candle spread >= breakout spread threshold × frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available AND relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold) OR after a downward breakout, within its retest window and before any prior retest, high is within half frozen ATR of support AND close < support - boundary buffer × frozen ATR AND close location <= retest close-location threshold AND candle spread <= frozen ATR AND (price-only mode OR current and baseline volume are positive and available AND relative volume <= test-volume threshold))
Exit: Long, after an upward breakout, close < resistance - half frozen ATR
Exit: Short, after a downward breakout, close > support + half frozen ATR
Long and Short filters: The active range is either manually activated with valid support below resistance once prior-bar ATR is available, or automatically detected when candidate width is between minimum and maximum multiples of prior-bar ATR, directional efficiency is at most its threshold, and both boundaries have at least the required distinct visits. In automatic detection, boundary zones use tolerance = min(20% of candidate width, max(0.35 × prior-bar ATR, 5% of candidate width)); a new visit requires the configured consecutive bars outside that zone. Freeze support, resistance, and prior-bar ATR at activation. End tracking after the configured range age, on an unqualified close more than one frozen ATR beyond either boundary, or on breakout failure. Tests must occur after their sweep and before the sweep test window expires; a new accepted sweep replaces the pending test. A breakout needs no prior sweep. Commit signals at bar close. Parameters are configurable; use longer lookbacks for longer-term calculations.
