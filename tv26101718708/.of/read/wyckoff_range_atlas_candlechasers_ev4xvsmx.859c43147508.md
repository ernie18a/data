Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low), or zero when high = low.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over the preceding volume-lookback bars, excluding the current bar; it is available only when current volume and the average are positive.
Calculation: Candidate range = highest high and lowest low over the preceding range-lookback bars; its width is their difference. Directional efficiency = absolute difference between the preceding close and the close one range-lookback earlier, divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over those preceding bars; use one if that sum is zero.
Calculation: Active range = one range at a time, with resistance, support and prior-bar ATR frozen at discovery. When searching, discover a range if more than range-lookback plus volume-lookback bars have elapsed, prior-bar ATR is positive, candidate width is between its minimum and maximum ATR multiples, directional efficiency is at most its maximum, and each boundary has at least the minimum number of distinct visits. A boundary zone has width equal to the smaller of a range-width cap and the larger of an ATR multiple and a range-width multiple. Scan the discovery bars oldest to newest; a top visit occurs when high enters the resistance zone and a bottom visit when low enters the support zone, with consecutive visits counted only after the specified number of consecutive bars outside the respective zone. The detection bar can also generate events. Expire the range before processing events when its age exceeds the maximum; discovery can occur on that same bar. An unqualified close beyond resistance plus frozen ATR or below support minus frozen ATR ends the range after event processing; discovery resumes on a later bar.
Calculation: Boundary buffer = frozen ATR multiplied by the boundary-buffer parameter. Volume qualification = price-only mode OR relative volume is available. Quiet sweep-test volume = price-only mode OR relative volume is available AND relative volume is at most its test maximum AND volume is below the sweep bar's volume. Quiet retest volume = price-only mode OR relative volume is available AND relative volume is at most its test maximum. Breakout volume = price-only mode OR relative volume is available AND relative volume is at least its breakout minimum.
Calculation: Pending sweep = the most recent accepted spring or upthrust in the active unbroken range; a new sweep replaces it. Sweeps require the specified cooldown since the preceding sweep. After the sweep bar, clear the pending sweep after its first matching test, or if its extreme is breached or its test window expires; evaluate the test before clearing it for an extreme breach. A qualified breakout also clears it.
Calculation: Breakout tracking = after a qualified upward or downward breakout, retain its direction and bar until range expiry or breakout failure. Starting on the following bar, allow at most one matching retest within the retest window. Evaluate a retest before checking failure; failure ends tracking and discovery resumes on a later bar.
Long: Active unbroken range AND sweep cooldown satisfied AND volume qualification AND low < support − boundary buffer AND low >= support − maximum sweep-depth multiple of frozen ATR AND high <= resistance + boundary buffer AND support < close < resistance AND close location >= spring threshold.
Short: Active unbroken range AND sweep cooldown satisfied AND volume qualification AND high > resistance + boundary buffer AND high <= resistance + maximum sweep-depth multiple of frozen ATR AND low >= support − boundary buffer AND support < close < resistance AND close location <= upthrust threshold.
Long: Active unbroken range AND pending spring AND current bar is after the sweep bar and within its test window AND low > spring low AND low <= support + test-distance multiple of frozen ATR AND support < close < resistance AND close location >= spring-test threshold AND high − low <= frozen ATR AND quiet sweep-test volume.
Short: Active unbroken range AND pending upthrust AND current bar is after the sweep bar and within its test window AND high < upthrust high AND high >= resistance − test-distance multiple of frozen ATR AND support < close < resistance AND close location <= upthrust-test threshold AND high − low <= frozen ATR AND quiet sweep-test volume.
Long: Active unbroken range AND close > resistance + boundary buffer AND close > open AND close location >= upward-breakout threshold AND high − low >= breakout-spread multiple of frozen ATR AND breakout volume.
Short: Active unbroken range AND close < support − boundary buffer AND close < open AND close location <= downward-breakout threshold AND high − low >= breakout-spread multiple of frozen ATR AND breakout volume.
Long: Upward breakout tracking AND no prior retest AND current bar is after the breakout bar and within its retest window AND low is within the retest-distance multiple of frozen ATR on either side of resistance AND close > resistance + boundary buffer AND close location >= spring-test threshold AND high − low <= frozen ATR AND quiet retest volume.
Short: Downward breakout tracking AND no prior retest AND current bar is after the breakout bar and within its retest window AND high is within the retest-distance multiple of frozen ATR on either side of support AND close < support − boundary buffer AND close location <= upthrust-test threshold AND high − low <= frozen ATR AND quiet retest volume.
Exit: long, upward breakout tracking AND current bar is after the breakout bar AND close < resistance − failure-distance multiple of frozen ATR.
Exit: short, downward breakout tracking AND current bar is after the breakout bar AND close > support + failure-distance multiple of frozen ATR.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND that EMA > the EMA on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND that EMA < the EMA on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
