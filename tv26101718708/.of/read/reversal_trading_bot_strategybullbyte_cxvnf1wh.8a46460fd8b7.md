Calculation: Bullish divergence = the lowest low over the lookback window ending one completed bar ago is lower than the lowest low over the same-length window ending two completed bars ago AND the lowest RSI over the window ending two completed bars ago is higher than the lowest RSI over the same-length window ending three completed bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = the highest high over the lookback window ending one completed bar ago is higher than the highest high over the same-length window ending two completed bars ago AND the highest RSI over the window ending two completed bars ago is lower than the highest RSI over the same-length window ending three completed bars ago.
Long: bullish divergence AND (if enabled, previous completed bar volume ≥ configured multiple of its volume SMA) AND (if enabled, previous completed bar ADX > threshold AND DI+ > DI−) AND (if enabled, previous completed bar close ≤ lower Bollinger Band) AND (if enabled, fast RSI crosses above slow RSI on the previous completed bar; fast RSI uses a shorter lookback than slow RSI).
Short: bearish divergence AND (if enabled, previous completed bar volume ≥ configured multiple of its volume SMA) AND (if enabled, previous completed bar ADX > threshold AND DI− > DI+) AND (if enabled, previous completed bar close ≥ upper Bollinger Band) AND (if enabled, fast RSI crosses below slow RSI on the previous completed bar; fast RSI uses a shorter lookback than slow RSI).
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage)
