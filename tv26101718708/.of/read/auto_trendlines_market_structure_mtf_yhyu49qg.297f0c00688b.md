Calculation: Pivot lookback = a fixed parameter or, in adaptive mode, the truncated integer ATR / close multiplied by a scaling parameter, clamped between minimum and maximum lookback parameters. A pivot high or low is confirmed after this many bars on each side; use the lookback calculated on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Resistance and support lines = straight lines through the two most recently confirmed pivot highs and lows, respectively. On each new same-side pivot, replace the corresponding line and validate it against a parameterized number of preceding same-side pivots: reject resistance if any checked high exceeds its extrapolated line by more than an ATR multiple, and reject support if any checked low falls below its extrapolated line by more than that multiple. Store each line's slope divided by ATR at replacement. A valid line remains active until replaced or until close exceeds resistance plus an ATR multiple or falls below support minus an ATR multiple, respectively. Process replacement before breaks and breaks before trend classification.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe line state = apply the same pivot, replacement and break calculations independently to bars of each selected higher timeframe and use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. For higher-timeframe classification, treat a line as active when its stored normalized slope exists and it has not been invalidated, even if its pivot validation failed.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Both resistance and support are active AND both stored normalized slopes are above a positive slope threshold, OR exactly one line is active AND its stored normalized slope is above the positive slope threshold.
Short: Both resistance and support are active AND both stored normalized slopes are below the negative slope threshold, OR exactly one line is active AND its stored normalized slope is below the negative slope threshold.
Long: For any selected higher timeframe, both resistance and support are active AND both stored normalized slopes are above the positive slope threshold, OR exactly one line is active AND its stored normalized slope is above the positive slope threshold.
Short: For any selected higher timeframe, both resistance and support are active AND both stored normalized slopes are below the negative slope threshold, OR exactly one line is active AND its stored normalized slope is below the negative slope threshold.
Long: Close is above active resistance plus the break ATR multiple AND the preceding bar did not break resistance.
Short: Close is below active support minus the break ATR multiple AND the preceding bar did not break support.
