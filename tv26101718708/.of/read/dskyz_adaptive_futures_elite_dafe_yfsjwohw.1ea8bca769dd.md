Calculation: Adaptive fast and slow SMAs = simple moving averages of close using, respectively, shorter and longer lookbacks; each lookback is its base lookback divided by (1 + ATR/close), rounded and floored at one.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior bar closes below its open, current bar closes above its open, current open is below prior close, current close is above prior open, and current body exceeds a fraction of the prior body. Bearish engulfing is the inverse.
Calculation: Recent bullish pattern = a bullish engulfing occurred within the configured lookback and current volume exceeds its short-lookback volume SMA or current bar range exceeds its short-lookback range SMA. Recent bearish pattern uses the same strength test.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = compare shorter-lookback and longer-lookback SMAs of close on a higher-timeframe bar; use the developing bar in real time and the last completed bar historically.
Long: (if pattern priority: recent bullish pattern OR (NOT recent bearish pattern AND (close > adaptive fast SMA AND adaptive fast SMA > adaptive slow SMA AND close > prior close AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is not bearish) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses above its oversold level AND RSI at its configured lookback is below an upper bound))); OR (if MA priority: (close > adaptive fast SMA AND adaptive fast SMA > adaptive slow SMA AND close > prior close AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is not bearish) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses above its oversold level AND RSI at its configured lookback is below an upper bound)) OR (NOT the corresponding short MA condition AND recent bullish pattern)); AND NOT (recent bullish pattern AND recent bearish pattern) AND volume >= minimum volume AND volume > a multiplier times its short-lookback SMA AND bar hour is within the configured trading window AND ATR/close <= volatility threshold AND position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed AND long condition is true while short condition is false.
Short: (if pattern priority: recent bearish pattern OR (NOT recent bullish pattern AND (close < adaptive fast SMA AND adaptive fast SMA < adaptive slow SMA AND close < prior close AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is not bullish) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses below its overbought level AND RSI at its configured lookback is above a lower bound))); OR (if MA priority: (close < adaptive fast SMA AND adaptive fast SMA < adaptive slow SMA AND close < prior close AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is not bullish) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses below its overbought level AND RSI at its configured lookback is above a lower bound)) OR (NOT the corresponding long MA condition AND recent bearish pattern)); AND NOT (recent bullish pattern AND recent bearish pattern) AND volume >= minimum volume AND volume > a multiplier times its short-lookback SMA AND bar hour is within the configured trading window AND ATR/close <= volatility threshold AND position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed AND short condition is true while long condition is false.
Exit: Long, low <= average entry price - ATR × fixed-stop multiplier OR high >= average entry price + ATR × profit-target multiplier OR (after the trailing stop activates when price reaches average entry price + ATR × adjusted trail-points multiplier, low <= highest high since activation - ATR × trail-offset multiplier).
Exit: Short, high >= average entry price + ATR × fixed-stop multiplier OR low <= average entry price - ATR × profit-target multiplier OR (after the trailing stop activates when price reaches average entry price - ATR × adjusted trail-points multiplier, high >= lowest low since activation + ATR × trail-offset multiplier).
