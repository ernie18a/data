Calculation: Volatility = ATR / close, with zero substituted when ATR / close is unavailable.
Calculation: Adaptive short and long SMAs = SMAs of close with respective lookbacks max(1, round(base lookback / (1 + volatility))); use the respective base lookbacks while ATR is unavailable. The short base lookback is shorter than the long base lookback.
Calculation: Trend SMAs = short- and long-lookback SMAs of close using the corresponding adaptive SMA base lookbacks on the configured other timeframe. On a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; on a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = previous close < previous open AND close > open AND open < previous close AND close > previous open AND close - open > a proportion of previous open - previous close. Bearish engulfing = previous close > previous open AND close < open AND open > previous close AND close < previous open AND open - close > the same proportion of previous close - previous open.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = a bullish engulfing occurred within the pattern lookback, including the current bar, AND (volume > volume SMA OR high - low > SMA of high - low). Bearish pattern = a bearish engulfing occurred within the same lookback AND the same current-bar volume or range comparison. The volume and range SMAs use the same lookback.
Calculation: Long MA condition = close > adaptive short SMA AND adaptive short SMA > adaptive long SMA AND close > previous close AND (trend filter disabled OR short trend SMA >= long trend SMA) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses above the lower RSI threshold AND RSI at the RSI lookback < an upper RSI limit). Short MA condition = close < adaptive short SMA AND adaptive short SMA < adaptive long SMA AND close < previous close AND (trend filter disabled OR short trend SMA <= long trend SMA) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI crosses below the upper RSI threshold AND RSI at the same RSI lookback > a lower RSI limit).
Calculation: Long candidate = (pattern priority selected AND (patterns enabled AND bullish pattern OR NOT (patterns enabled AND bearish pattern) AND long MA condition) OR MA priority selected AND (long MA condition OR NOT short MA condition AND patterns enabled AND bullish pattern)) AND volume >= minimum volume AND volume > a multiple of volume SMA AND hour >= start hour AND hour <= end hour AND volatility <= maximum volatility AND NOT (bullish pattern AND bearish pattern). The volume SMA uses the pattern-strength volume SMA lookback. Short candidate = (pattern priority selected AND (patterns enabled AND bearish pattern OR NOT (patterns enabled AND bullish pattern) AND short MA condition) OR MA priority selected AND (short MA condition OR NOT long MA condition AND patterns enabled AND bearish pattern)) AND the same volume, hour, volatility, and pattern-conflict filters.
Calculation: Trailing activation distance = current ATR multiplied by a profit-dependent factor: use the smallest factor when current close profit exceeds a higher ATR multiple, an intermediate factor when it exceeds a lower ATR multiple, and the configurable base factor otherwise. Trailing offset = current ATR multiplied by the offset factor. Both distances are interpreted as ticks; activation and offset orders are updated on each calculation.
Long: Position is flat AND long candidate AND NOT short candidate AND (no previous exit time OR elapsed time since the bar on which the position became flat >= cooldown).
Short: Position is flat AND short candidate AND NOT long candidate AND (no previous exit time OR elapsed time since the bar on which the position became flat >= cooldown).
Long: Position is flat AND long candidate AND (no previous long marking OR bars since previous long marking >= minimum marking spacing).
Short: Position is flat AND short candidate AND (no previous short marking OR bars since previous short marking >= minimum marking spacing).
Long: DCA enabled AND position is long AND long entry count < maximum entry count AND long MA condition AND RSI < upper RSI limit AND close <= initial long signal close - long entry count * ATR at initial long signal * DCA multiplier.
Short: DCA enabled AND position is short AND short entry count < maximum entry count AND short MA condition AND RSI > lower RSI limit AND close >= initial short signal close + short entry count * ATR at initial short signal * DCA multiplier.
Exit: Long, low <= average position entry price - current ATR * fixed stop multiplier.
Exit: Short, high >= average position entry price + current ATR * fixed stop multiplier.
Exit: Long, bar index differs from initial signal bar index AND (high >= average position entry price + current ATR * profit target multiplier OR activated trailing stop is reached). The trailing stop activates when high reaches average position entry price + trailing activation distance and, after activation, follows the highest price reached at the trailing offset below it.
Exit: Short, bar index differs from initial signal bar index AND (low <= average position entry price - current ATR * profit target multiplier OR activated trailing stop is reached). The trailing stop activates when low reaches average position entry price - trailing activation distance and, after activation, follows the lowest price reached at the trailing offset above it.
