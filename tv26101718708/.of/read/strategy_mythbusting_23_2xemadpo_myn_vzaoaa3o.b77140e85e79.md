Calculation: DPO = close minus the close SMA from floor(period / 2) + 1 bars ago; alternatively, close from floor(period / 2) + 1 bars ago minus the current close SMA.
Calculation: Alternate ADX = for each bar, true range is the maximum of high − low, abs(high − previous close), and abs(low − previous close); positive directional movement is max(high − previous high, 0) if high − previous high > previous low − low, otherwise 0; negative directional movement is max(previous low − low, 0) if previous low − low > high − previous high, otherwise 0. Replace missing previous OHLC with 0. Smooth each series recursively as previous smoothed value − previous smoothed value / lookback + current value, starting from 0. Positive and negative DI are the corresponding smoothed movements divided by smoothed true range; alternate ADX is the SMA of 100 × abs(positive DI − negative DI) / (positive DI + negative DI), using the same lookback.
Calculation: Signal price = close on the latest qualifying long or short entry signal, calculated before applying the date and time filters.
Calculation: Long stop = at the latest qualifying long entry signal before date and time filters, select either signal-bar close × (1 − stop percentage), the lowest low over the stop lookback including the signal bar, or signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = at the latest qualifying short entry signal before date and time filters, select either signal-bar close × (1 + stop percentage), the highest high over the stop lookback including the signal bar, or signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = select either the latest qualifying long signal-bar close × (1 + target percentage), signal price + (signal price − long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio, or the latest qualifying long signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR × target multiplier. Qualifying signals are evaluated before date and time filters.
Calculation: Short target = select either the latest qualifying short signal-bar close × (1 − target percentage), signal price + (signal price − short stop) × reward-to-risk ratio, or the latest qualifying short signal-bar close − signal-bar ATR × target multiplier. Qualifying signals are evaluated before date and time filters.
Long: Shorter-period close EMA crosses above longer-period close EMA AND DPO > 0 on the current and each of the previous two bars AND position side is not long AND, if enabled, close > filter EMA AND, if enabled, selected ADX > threshold AND datetime is within each enabled date boundary and time-of-day window.
Short: Shorter-period close EMA crosses below longer-period close EMA AND DPO < 0 on the current and each of the previous two bars AND position side is not short AND, if enabled, close < filter EMA AND, if enabled, selected ADX > threshold AND datetime is within each enabled date boundary and time-of-day window.
Exit: long, datetime is within each enabled date boundary and time-of-day window AND ((stop is enabled AND close <= long stop) OR (take profit is enabled AND close >= long target)).
Exit: short, datetime is within each enabled date boundary and time-of-day window AND ((stop is enabled AND close >= short stop) OR (take profit is enabled AND close <= short target)).
