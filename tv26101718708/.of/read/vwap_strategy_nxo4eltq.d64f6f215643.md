Calculation: NY-session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of bar HLC3 since the regular session opens; reset at each session open
Calculation: Fast, middle, and slow EMAs = EMAs of close, with fast period shorter than middle and middle shorter than slow
Calculation: Reclaim state = becomes active after the configured bullish or bearish reclaim of NY-session VWAP; resets at session open or after two consecutive closes on the opposite side of VWAP
Calculation: VWAP cross count = number of price crosses of NY-session VWAP during the current regular session, using either close crosses or wick-spanning crosses as configured
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR close crosses above NY-session VWAP OR NY-session VWAP crosses above the 18:00-anchored VWAP OR in the trade window, reclaim state is active, VWAP cross count is below its configured limit, fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA, each EMA is rising, low <= NY-session VWAP + configured touch buffer, close > NY-session VWAP, and volume is greater than the prior bar's volume when volume confirmation is enabled OR after such a hold candle, price reaches its high plus the configured entry offset while the setup remains valid and its stop risk is within the configured maximum
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR close crosses below NY-session VWAP OR NY-session VWAP crosses below the 18:00-anchored VWAP OR in the trade window, reclaim state is active, VWAP cross count is below its configured limit, fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA, each EMA is falling, high >= NY-session VWAP - configured touch buffer, close < NY-session VWAP, and volume is greater than the prior bar's volume when volume confirmation is enabled OR after such a hold candle, price reaches its low minus the configured entry offset while the setup remains valid and its stop risk is within the configured maximum
Exit: long, price <= setup-candle low minus configured stop buffer OR price >= entry price plus configured profit target
Exit: short, price >= setup-candle high plus configured stop buffer OR price <= entry price minus configured profit target
