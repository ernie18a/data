Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of its designated session and held constant outside that session. Use separate regular-session and extended-session VWAPs.
Calculation: VWAP cross count = number of regular-session close crossings of regular-session VWAP since the session began, excluding its first bar. In the alternative wick mode, count only crossings whose previous close is strictly on the opposite side and whose current low and high span the VWAP. Reset the count at the session start.
Calculation: Long reclaim state = false at the regular-session start or after two consecutive regular-session closes below regular-session VWAP; otherwise set true when a regular-session close is above VWAP and either the preceding regular-session close is above its VWAP or, in the alternative reclaim mode, the preceding close is at or below its VWAP. Retain the state otherwise. Short reclaim state is the inverse.
Calculation: Long hold = within the trade window and regular session AND VWAP cross count below its limit AND long reclaim state is true AND short-period EMA > medium-period EMA > long-period EMA AND each EMA > its preceding value AND low <= regular-session VWAP + touch buffer AND close > regular-session VWAP AND, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume > preceding volume. Short hold reverses the EMA comparisons, uses short reclaim state, high >= regular-session VWAP - touch buffer, and close < regular-session VWAP.
Calculation: Pending setup = the latest hold bar of its side since the regular-session start, replaced by each subsequent hold of that side. Clear it at the regular-session start, or when the corresponding reclaim state is false, the corresponding EMA ordering or rising/falling condition fails, the trade window ends, the VWAP cross count reaches its limit, or two consecutive regular-session closes are on the opposite side of regular-session VWAP. A long setup entry stop is its high + minimum price increment and its protective stop is its low - stop buffer; a short setup entry stop is its low - minimum price increment and its protective stop is its high + stop buffer. Setup values and protective stops continue to update or clear under these rules while a position is open.
Long: During the regular session, short-period EMA crosses above long-period EMA.
Short: During the regular session, short-period EMA crosses below long-period EMA.
Long: During the regular session after its first bar, close crosses above regular-session VWAP AND, in the alternative wick mode, preceding close < preceding VWAP AND low <= VWAP AND high >= VWAP.
Short: During the regular session after its first bar, close crosses below regular-session VWAP AND, in the alternative wick mode, preceding close > preceding VWAP AND low <= VWAP AND high >= VWAP.
Long: During the regular session after its first bar, regular-session VWAP crosses above extended-session VWAP.
Short: During the regular session after its first bar, regular-session VWAP crosses below extended-session VWAP.
Long: Long hold is true.
Short: Short hold is true.
Long: Position is flat AND within the trade window AND VWAP cross count is below its limit AND a long pending setup exists AND its entry stop > its protective stop AND their difference, rounded to minimum price increments, <= maximum risk AND price reaches the entry stop after the order is placed.
Short: Position is flat AND within the trade window AND VWAP cross count is below its limit AND a short pending setup exists AND its entry stop < its protective stop AND their difference, rounded to minimum price increments, <= maximum risk AND price reaches the entry stop after the order is placed.
Exit: long, price <= current long setup protective stop OR price >= entry price + profit target distance.
Exit: short, price >= current short setup protective stop OR price <= entry price - profit target distance.
