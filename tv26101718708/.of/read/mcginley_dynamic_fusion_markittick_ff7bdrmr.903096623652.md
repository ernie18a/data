Calculation: McGinley Dynamic = Initialize with the simple moving average of the selected price source over its lookback. Thereafter, let denominator = constant × lookback × (current source / previous dynamic)^4; if the previous dynamic is zero or the denominator is zero or unavailable, retain the previous dynamic; otherwise use previous dynamic + (current source − previous dynamic) / denominator. Calculate fast and slow dynamics with shorter and longer lookbacks respectively and the same constant.
Calculation: Take-profit level = The close of the bar preceding the latest directional crossover signal, plus for a long signal or minus for a short signal an independent multiplier × ATR on the signal bar. Calculate three levels with independent multipliers and retain them until the next directional crossover signal.
Long: Fast McGinley Dynamic > slow McGinley Dynamic.
Short: Fast McGinley Dynamic <= slow McGinley Dynamic.
Long: Fast McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar > slow McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar AND fast McGinley Dynamic two bars ago <= slow McGinley Dynamic two bars ago AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected price source > fast McGinley Dynamic on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (no previous qualifying long crossover signal OR bars since that signal >= cooldown).
Short: Fast McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar < slow McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar AND fast McGinley Dynamic two bars ago >= slow McGinley Dynamic two bars ago AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected price source < fast McGinley Dynamic on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (no previous qualifying short crossover signal OR bars since that signal >= cooldown).
Exit: long, fast McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar < slow McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar AND fast McGinley Dynamic two bars ago >= slow McGinley Dynamic two bars ago AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected price source < fast McGinley Dynamic on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (no previous qualifying short crossover signal OR bars since that signal >= cooldown).
Exit: short, fast McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar > slow McGinley Dynamic on the previous bar AND fast McGinley Dynamic two bars ago <= slow McGinley Dynamic two bars ago AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected price source > fast McGinley Dynamic on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (no previous qualifying long crossover signal OR bars since that signal >= cooldown).
Exit: long, latest directional crossover signal was long AND high >= any of its three take-profit levels.
Exit: short, latest directional crossover signal was short AND low <= any of its three take-profit levels.
