Calculation: Confirmed swing low = the bar N bars ago has the lowest low among itself and the N bars before and after it; confirm only after those N later bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = the bar N bars ago has the highest high among itself and the N bars before and after it; confirm only after those N later bars complete.
Calculation: Kept pivot = a confirmed swing high or low whose price is at least the minimum swing-size ATR multiple times ATR(14) at the pivot bar away from the previous kept pivot; keep the first pivot.
Long: kept swing low price < previous kept swing low price AND OBV at the swing low > OBV at the previous kept swing low
Short: kept swing high price > previous kept swing high price AND OBV at the swing high < OBV at the previous kept swing high
