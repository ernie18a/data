Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low at least as extreme as the highs/lows over an equal parameterized number of bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier AND the gap size >= a parameterized multiple of current ATR. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND the gap size >= the same multiple of current ATR.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; track the latest confirmed structure pivot high and low separately, each unbroken until the first close beyond it. A first close above an unbroken high sets state bullish if state is neutral or bullish, or if state is bearish and bullish displacement is present. A first close below an unbroken low sets state bearish if state is neutral or bearish, or if state is bullish and bearish displacement is present. Mark the pivot broken even when displacement is absent.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = a bullish candle with body >= a parameterized multiple of ATR occurred within a parameterized recent bar window AND, if the FVG requirement is enabled, a bullish FVG occurred within that same window. Bearish displacement is symmetric. The large candle and FVG need not occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed swing pivot highs and lows, last completed higher-timeframe day and week highs and lows, and highs and lows of the most recently completed parameterized intraday sessions. Replace each day, week, or session pool when its next corresponding period completes. For swing pools, merge a newly confirmed pivot into the first unbreached same-side pool within a parameterized ATR tolerance, retaining the higher high or lower low; otherwise add a pool and discard the oldest if the parameterized maximum is exceeded. Scan pools before adding new pools on each bar.
Calculation: Liquidity raid = after a pool's creation bar, price first exceeds a high pool by more than a parameterized penetration or falls below a low pool by more than that penetration, then closes back across the pool within a parameterized window including the breach bar. Remove the pool on reclaim or when the window expires.
Calculation: OTE leg = on a bullish structure shift, origin is the lowest low from the broken pivot high bar through the shift bar, subject to a maximum lookback, and endpoint is the shift bar high; on a bearish shift, origin is the highest high from the broken pivot low bar through the shift bar, subject to the same maximum lookback, and endpoint is the shift bar low. Before the first retracement, extend the endpoint to each new high or low. The retracement threshold is a parameterized fraction of the distance from endpoint toward origin. Replace the leg on a new shift and remove it when close crosses beyond its origin or its parameterized lifetime expires; evaluate a first touch before invalidation on each bar.
Long: An unbroken latest confirmed structure pivot high is exceeded by close AND the prior structure state is bearish AND bullish displacement is present.
Short: An unbroken latest confirmed structure pivot low is breached by close AND the prior structure state is bullish AND bearish displacement is present.
Long: An unbroken latest confirmed structure pivot high is exceeded by close AND the prior structure state is bullish.
Short: An unbroken latest confirmed structure pivot low is breached by close AND the prior structure state is bearish.
Long: Bullish FVG forms.
Short: Bearish FVG forms.
Long: After a bearish FVG forms, close first exceeds its upper boundary before that zone is removed.
Short: After a bullish FVG forms, close first falls below its lower boundary before that zone is removed.
Long: A low liquidity pool is raided.
Short: A high liquidity pool is raided.
Long: After a bullish structure shift and after its formation bar, low first reaches or falls below the current OTE retracement threshold before the leg is removed.
Short: After a bearish structure shift and after its formation bar, high first reaches or exceeds the current OTE retracement threshold before the leg is removed.
