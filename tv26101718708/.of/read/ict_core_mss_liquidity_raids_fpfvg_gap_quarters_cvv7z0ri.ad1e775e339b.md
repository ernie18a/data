Calculation: ATR14 = 14-bar average true range.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier by at least a configurable fraction of ATR14; bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least a configurable fraction of ATR14.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after an equal configurable number of bars on its right; use the latest unbroken pivot.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = prior completed day/week highs and lows, completed Asia/London session highs and lows, confirmed swing highs/lows, and relative equal highs/lows formed when swing levels are within a configurable ATR14 multiple.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = a bullish candle whose body is at least a configurable multiple of ATR14; bearish displacement is the corresponding bearish candle. Displacement and same-direction FVG must both have occurred within a configurable short lookback.
Long: Bullish FVG occurs OR a confirmed swing high is closed above while prior structure trend is bearish and bullish displacement plus bullish FVG occurred within the short lookback OR a confirmed swing high is closed above while prior structure trend is bullish (continuation break) OR a sell-side liquidity level is breached by at least a configurable tick amount and price closes above it within a configurable reclaim window OR, after bullish MSS, price first reaches the evolving bullish OTE threshold (62% retracement from the displacement-leg high toward its low) before the leg origin is closed below or the setup expires OR a bullish order block is marked at bullish MSS when an opposing bearish candle is found within the configurable search range before the displacement candle.
Short: Bearish FVG occurs OR a confirmed swing low is closed below while prior structure trend is bullish and bearish displacement plus bearish FVG occurred within the short lookback OR a confirmed swing low is closed below while prior structure trend is bearish (continuation break) OR a buy-side liquidity level is breached by at least a configurable tick amount and price closes below it within a configurable reclaim window OR, after bearish MSS, price first reaches the evolving bearish OTE threshold (62% retracement from the displacement-leg low toward its high) before the leg origin is closed above or the setup expires OR a bearish order block is marked at bearish MSS when an opposing bullish candle is found within the configurable search range before the displacement candle.
