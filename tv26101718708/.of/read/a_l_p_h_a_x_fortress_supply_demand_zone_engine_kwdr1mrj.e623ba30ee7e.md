Calculation: Explosive bar = high - low > ATR × move threshold AND abs(close - open) > (high - low) × body threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier).
Calculation: Equilibrium = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the dealing-range lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA(bull volume) - EMA(bear volume), using the same smoothing length; bull volume = volume × (close - low) / (high - low), or volume / 2 if high = low; bear volume = volume - bull volume.
Calculation: Chart zone = on each bar after an explosive bar, create a demand zone if that preceding bar closed above its open, or a supply zone if it closed below its open. Initialize base high and low from the bar immediately before the explosive bar; extend them over consecutive preceding bars up to the maximum base length while each bar's body is less than half the explosive bar's ATR and that bar is not explosive. Demand boundaries are max(base high, explosive bar open) and base low; supply boundaries are base high and min(base low, explosive bar open). Keep at most the configured number of zones per side, removing the oldest first. Before checking signals, remove demand zones when close < zone bottom in close mitigation mode or low < zone bottom in wick mitigation mode; remove supply zones when close > zone top or high > zone top respectively. A zone is tested only when it produces a standard rejection signal; it is used when it produces either signal; a sweep can occur only once per zone. Evaluate zones newest first, with standard rejection before sweep for each zone and at most one signal per side per bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe zone = use the last completed higher-timeframe bar's detection result: an explosive directional bar immediately preceding that higher-timeframe bar creates a demand zone from the low of the bar before the explosive bar to max(its high, explosive bar open), or a supply zone from min(its low, explosive bar open) to its high. Keep the latest zone per side while the overlay is enabled; remove demand when chart close < its bottom and supply when chart close > its top.
Calculation: Zone grade = at chart-zone creation, score min(18 × explosive bar range / its ATR, 30) + (min(13 × explosive bar volume / its volume SMA, 20) if volume confirmation is enabled, otherwise 10) + 20 if the VWAP boost is enabled and current session VWAP lies between zone bottom - current ATR × VWAP tolerance and zone top + current ATR × VWAP tolerance + 20 if an enabled, active same-side higher-timeframe zone overlaps the chart zone + 15 if current close is below equilibrium for demand or above equilibrium for supply + 5. If the explosive bar's volume SMA is not positive, use a volume ratio of 1. Rank the score against the configured grade thresholds.
Long: Previous bar is explosive AND previous close > previous open.
Short: Previous bar is explosive AND previous close < previous open.
Long: Trend filter disabled OR close > trend EMA.
Short: Trend filter enabled AND close < trend EMA.
Long: Close < equilibrium.
Short: Close > equilibrium.
Long: Volume delta > 0.
Short: Volume delta < 0.
Long: Signals enabled AND current bar is in the configured session if the session filter is enabled AND bars since the last signal >= cooldown AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (premium/discount filter disabled OR close < equilibrium) AND (volume delta filter disabled OR volume delta > 0) AND an active demand zone has grade >= minimum AND (one-signal-per-zone disabled OR zone unused) AND ((fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone untested) AND low <= zone top AND low >= zone bottom - ATR × touch tolerance AND close > open AND close > zone bottom AND high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= rejection-wick threshold OR sweep signals enabled AND zone has not swept AND low < zone bottom - ATR × sweep depth AND close > zone bottom).
Short: Signals enabled AND current bar is in the configured session if the session filter is enabled AND bars since the last signal >= cooldown AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (premium/discount filter disabled OR close > equilibrium) AND (volume delta filter disabled OR volume delta < 0) AND an active supply zone has grade >= minimum AND (one-signal-per-zone disabled OR zone unused) AND ((fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone untested) AND high >= zone bottom AND high <= zone top + ATR × touch tolerance AND close < open AND close < zone top AND high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= rejection-wick threshold OR sweep signals enabled AND zone has not swept AND high > zone top + ATR × sweep depth AND close < zone top).

Exit: short, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR × target multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier.
