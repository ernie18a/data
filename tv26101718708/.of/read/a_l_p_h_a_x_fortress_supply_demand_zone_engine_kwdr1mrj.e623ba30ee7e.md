Calculation: Explosive candle = high − low > ATR × move-strength parameter AND |close − open| > (high − low) × minimum-body parameter AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > longer-lookback average volume × volume multiplier).
Calculation: Demand zone = after an explosive bullish candle, the range from the low of its base candles to the greater of their highest high and the departure candle’s open; supply zone = after an explosive bearish candle, the range from the lesser of the base candles’ lowest low and the departure candle’s open to their highest high. Base candles are consecutive preceding candles, up to the base-count parameter, with body < 0.5 × ATR and not explosive; the immediate preceding candle is included. Register the zone on the following chart bar.
Calculation: Zone grade = move-strength score (min(departure range / departure ATR × 18, 30)) + volume score (min(departure volume / longer-lookback average volume × 13, 20) when volume confirmation is enabled, otherwise 10) + 20 if session VWAP is within the configured ATR tolerance of the zone + 20 if the zone overlaps an active same-side higher-timeframe zone + 15 if supply forms in premium or demand forms in discount + 5. Grades: A+ at 80+, A at 65–<80, B at 50–<65, otherwise C. Session VWAP is anchored to the session’s first chart bar. Higher-timeframe zones use bars aggregated to a higher timeframe and the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: ADX = Wilder-smoothed directional movement and true range, converted to +DI and −DI, then Wilder-smoothed DX.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of estimated bull volume minus EMA of estimated bear volume, where estimated bull volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), estimated bear volume = volume − estimated bull volume; when high = low, estimated bull volume = volume / 2. The EMA lookback is the volume-delta lookback.
Calculation: Dealing-range equilibrium = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the dealing-range lookback; discount = close < equilibrium; premium = close > equilibrium.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, signals enabled, within the active session if session filtering is enabled, cooldown elapsed, close > trend EMA if trend filtering is enabled, ADX >= minimum if ADX filtering is enabled, close < dealing-range equilibrium if premium/discount filtering is enabled, and volume delta > 0 if volume-delta filtering is enabled; AND an active demand zone meets the minimum grade, is unused if one-signal-per-zone is enabled, and (for standard rejection, is fresh if fresh-zones-only is enabled, low <= zone top, low >= zone bottom − 0.05 × ATR, close > open, close > zone bottom, and (min(close, open) − low) / (high − low) >= minimum wick fraction; OR, for a sweep, sweeps are enabled, the zone has not already produced a sweep, low < zone bottom − ATR × minimum sweep-depth parameter, and close > zone bottom). A signal marks the zone used; a standard rejection also marks it tested.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, signals enabled, within the active session if session filtering is enabled, cooldown elapsed, close < trend EMA if trend filtering is enabled, ADX >= minimum if ADX filtering is enabled, close > dealing-range equilibrium if premium/discount filtering is enabled, and volume delta < 0 if volume-delta filtering is enabled; AND an active supply zone meets the minimum grade, is unused if one-signal-per-zone is enabled, and (for standard rejection, is fresh if fresh-zones-only is enabled, high >= zone bottom, high <= zone top + 0.05 × ATR, close < open, close < zone top, and (high − max(close, open)) / (high − low) >= minimum wick fraction; OR, for a sweep, sweeps are enabled, the zone has not already produced a sweep, high > zone top + ATR × minimum sweep-depth parameter, and close < zone top). A signal marks the zone used; a standard rejection also marks it tested.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × target parameter OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop parameter; if both occur on the same bar, target takes priority.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × target parameter OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop parameter; if both occur on the same bar, target takes priority.
