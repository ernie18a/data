Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot with the same configurable number of bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have completed.
Calculation: Structure direction = bullish when the latest confirmed swing high and low are each above their respective preceding confirmed swings; bearish when both are below; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Recovery plan = start a bullish plan when structure direction is bullish and close is below the latest confirmed swing low by more than a damage threshold times ATR; start a bearish plan when structure direction is bearish and close is above the latest confirmed swing high by more than that threshold times ATR. A new plan can start only when there is no plan, the current plan has failed, its expiry cooldown has ended, or its age exceeds a multiple of the recovery window. At creation, store the broken swing as the rail, the bar's low for a bullish plan or high for a bearish plan as the damage extreme, and the bar index as the start. Age is current bar index minus start index. An unrecovered plan expires when age exceeds a multiple of the recovery window; its cooldown ends after an additional recovery window. A plan recovers on the first bar whose close exceeds the rail plus an ATR-scaled recovery buffer for a bullish plan, or falls below the rail minus that buffer for a bearish plan, provided the plan has not expired or failed. A recovered plan fails when close falls below the damage extreme minus an ATR-scaled risk buffer for a bullish plan, or rises above the damage extreme plus that buffer for a bearish plan. Recalculate the buffers using current ATR.
Calculation: Recovery proof = on a recovery event, if no proof is open, store the recovery close, current ATR and plan direction; close the proof when close reaches its fixed ATR-scaled target or stop, or its age exceeds a configurable bar limit. Test the stop before the target. The target and stop use separate ATR multiples fixed at recovery.
Long: Structure direction is bullish AND no recovery plan exists.
Long: Recovery plan direction is bullish AND the plan has not failed.
Short: Structure direction is bearish AND no recovery plan exists.
Short: Recovery plan direction is bearish AND the plan has not failed.
Exit: long, the bullish plan has recovered AND close is below its damage extreme minus the current ATR-scaled risk buffer.
Exit: short, the bearish plan has recovered AND close is above its damage extreme plus the current ATR-scaled risk buffer.
Exit: long, a bullish recovery proof is open AND (close is at or below its fixed stop OR close is at or above its fixed target OR proof age exceeds its bar limit).
Exit: short, a bearish recovery proof is open AND (close is at or above its fixed stop OR close is at or below its fixed target OR proof age exceeds its bar limit).
