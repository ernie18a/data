Calculation: Return skew = on the chart timeframe, or on the lower timeframe using its last completed bar, calculate returns as either log(source / previous source) or source minus previous source; at each bar subtract the rolling mean return from that bar's return, then divide the rolling mean of cubed deviations by the rolling mean of squared deviations raised to the power 3/2; use zero if the denominator is zero. For the lower timeframe use close as the source and sample the resulting skew at each chart bar.
Calculation: Normalized skew = standardize return skew using either rolling mean and standard deviation or rolling median and median absolute deviation scaled to standard-deviation units; use either one normalization window or the median of the standardized values from shorter, medium, and longer windows, where the shorter and longer windows are respectively half and twice the medium window.
Long: A price low pivot is confirmed after equal numbers of bars on either side AND its distance from the preceding confirmed low pivot is at most the maximum pivot spacing AND ((its low is below the preceding pivot's low AND its normalized skew at the pivot is above the preceding pivot's normalized skew) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its low is above the preceding pivot's low AND its normalized skew at the pivot is below the preceding pivot's normalized skew)).
Short: A price high pivot is confirmed after equal numbers of bars on either side AND its distance from the preceding confirmed high pivot is at most the maximum pivot spacing AND ((its high is above the preceding pivot's high AND its normalized skew at the pivot is below the preceding pivot's normalized skew) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND its high is below the preceding pivot's high AND its normalized skew at the pivot is above the preceding pivot's normalized skew)).
