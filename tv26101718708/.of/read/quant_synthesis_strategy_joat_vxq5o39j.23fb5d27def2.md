Calculation: normalized EMA spread = (shorter-lookback EMA of close − longer-lookback EMA of close) / ATR
Calculation: trend regime = ADX ≥ configured trend threshold AND absolute normalized EMA spread ≥ configured spread threshold
Calculation: balance regime = ADX < configured balance threshold OR ATR / close < its longer-lookback moving average × configured ratio
Calculation: expansion regime = ATR / close > its longer-lookback moving average × configured ratio
Calculation: confirmed bullish structure = the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows each rise; confirmed bearish structure = each falls. Pivots are confirmed after the configured right-side bars.
Calculation: higher-timeframe bullish bias = fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe bearish bias is the inverse. Use higher-timeframe bars and the last completed bar.
Calculation: normalized trend slope = (current linear-regression value of close − its prior-bar value) / ATR
Calculation: momentum score = clamp(normalized EMA spread × configured weight + normalized trend slope × configured weight + EMA of normalized EMA spread plus normalized trend slope × configured weight + divergence bias × configured weight, configured lower bound, configured upper bound). Divergence bias is positive when a confirmed lower pivot low has a higher oscillator value than the prior confirmed pivot low; negative when a confirmed higher pivot high has a lower oscillator value than the prior confirmed pivot high; otherwise zero. The oscillator is normalized EMA spread + normalized trend slope + normalized EMA-spread signal EMA.
Calculation: breakout long = close > highest high over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar; breakout short = close < lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: volume z-score = (volume − its moving average) / its standard deviation; directional volume = volume × (close − open) / moving average of volume × absolute close-open change; volume score = clamp(volume z-score × configured weight + directional volume × configured weight, configured lower bound, configured upper bound).
Calculation: opening range = highest high and lowest low over the configured initial session bars; opening-range midpoint = their average. Session long context = close > opening-range high AND close > midpoint; session short context = close < opening-range low AND close < midpoint. Session bias long = close > session open AND close > midpoint; session bias short = close < session open AND close < midpoint. Use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: long confluence score = configured regime points (trend or expansion with positive normalized EMA spread) + configured bias points (bullish higher-timeframe bias and bullish structure) + positive momentum score capped at its configured maximum + configured volume points when volume z-score exceeds its configured threshold and close > open + configured session points when session bias is long OR (session long context AND breakout long).
Calculation: short confluence score = configured regime points (trend or expansion with negative normalized EMA spread) + configured bias points (bearish higher-timeframe bias and bearish structure) + absolute negative momentum score capped at its configured maximum + configured volume points when volume z-score exceeds its configured threshold and close < open + configured session points when session bias is short OR (session short context AND breakout short).
Long: bar is confirmed AND long confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND long confluence score > short confluence score AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND cooldown elapsed AND session entry limit not reached AND position is flat or short
Short: bar is confirmed AND short confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND short confluence score > long confluence score AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND cooldown elapsed AND session entry limit not reached AND position is flat or long
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum OR confirmed bearish structure OR bearish higher-timeframe bias OR balance regime OR session ends
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum OR confirmed bullish structure OR bullish higher-timeframe bias OR balance regime OR session ends
