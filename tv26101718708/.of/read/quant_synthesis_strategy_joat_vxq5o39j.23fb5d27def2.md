Calculation: EMA spread = shorter-lookback EMA of close minus longer-lookback EMA of close; normalized spread = EMA spread / ATR.
Calculation: Normalized slope = (linear regression of close at offset 0 minus the same regression at offset 1) / ATR; normalized signal = EMA of EMA spread / ATR; trend pulse = EMA of normalized spread + normalized slope.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low extremum with specified numbers of preceding and following bars; recognize it only after the following bars have completed. Bullish structure = latest confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low > preceding confirmed pivot low; bearish structure reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Pivot oscillator = normalized spread + normalized slope + normalized signal at the pivot bar. Bullish divergence = newly confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low AND its pivot oscillator > the preceding pivot low's oscillator; bearish divergence = newly confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high AND its pivot oscillator < the preceding pivot high's oscillator. Divergence bias = positive weight for bullish divergence, negative weight for bearish divergence, otherwise zero; bullish takes precedence if both occur.
Calculation: Momentum = clamp(weighted sum of normalized spread, normalized slope, trend pulse, and divergence bias, negative limit, positive limit), with positive weights.
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR / close; trend regime = ADX >= threshold AND absolute normalized spread >= threshold; balance regime = ADX < threshold OR ATR percentage < its SMA multiplied by a threshold; expansion regime = ATR percentage > its SMA multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND shorter-lookback EMA > its value on the preceding chart bar; bearish bias reverses both comparisons. Both EMAs use higher-timeframe bars with lookahead off: the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, becoming available on the chart bar that completes it; on a realtime bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low of the first specified number of chart bars in each configured session; opening midpoint = their average. The range is ready from the last of those bars until the session ends. Session open = open of the first session bar.
Calculation: Long session permission = session filter disabled OR range ready AND close > opening range high AND close > opening midpoint; short session permission reverses the price comparisons. Long session bias = range ready AND close > session open AND close > opening midpoint; short session bias reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - SMA of volume) / standard deviation of volume, or zero when the standard deviation is zero. Directional volume = volume × (close - open) / SMA of volume × absolute(close - open), with the denominator floored at a minimum positive price-volume scale. Volume score = clamp(weighted sum of volume z-score and directional volume, negative limit, positive limit), with positive weights.
Calculation: Long confluence = sum of: trend points if trend regime AND normalized spread > 0, otherwise fewer expansion points if expansion regime AND normalized spread > 0; higher-timeframe bias points if the bias filter is enabled AND bullish bias holds, or fixed partial points if the filter is disabled; structure points if bullish structure; positive momentum capped at its limit; volume score capped at its upper limit if volume z-score > surprise threshold AND close > open; session points if long session bias OR long session permission AND close > the highest high of a specified lookback ending on the previous bar. Unmet components contribute zero; the volume component can be negative.
Calculation: Short confluence = sum of: trend points if trend regime AND normalized spread < 0, otherwise fewer expansion points if expansion regime AND normalized spread < 0; higher-timeframe bias points if the bias filter is enabled AND bearish bias holds, or fixed partial points if the filter is disabled; structure points if bearish structure; absolute negative momentum capped at its limit; absolute volume score capped at its limit if volume z-score > surprise threshold AND close < open; session points if short session bias OR short session permission AND close < the lowest low of a specified lookback ending on the previous bar. Unmet components contribute zero. Corresponding long and short components use the same parameters.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = bars since the last entry decision >= cooldown, or no previous entry decision; entries since the current session began < session entry limit. Reset the session entry count on the first session bar and increment it on every entry decision, including outside the session.
Calculation: Stop distance = maximum of current ATR × stop multiplier and a minimum price distance; target distance = maximum of current ATR × target multiplier and a minimum price distance. Recalculate both distances on each chart bar.
Long: shorter-lookback EMA of close > longer-lookback EMA of close.
Short: shorter-lookback EMA of close <= longer-lookback EMA of close.
Long: higher-timeframe bullish bias.
Short: higher-timeframe bearish bias.
Long: dashboard enabled AND long confluence >= confluence threshold.
Short: dashboard enabled AND short confluence >= confluence threshold.
Long: long confluence >= confluence threshold AND long confluence > short confluence AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND entry eligibility AND position side is flat or short.
Short: short confluence >= confluence threshold AND short confluence > long confluence AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND entry eligibility AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance.
Exit: long, bars since the position was first observed after a flat position >= maximum holding bars OR bearish structure OR higher-timeframe bearish bias OR balance regime OR current bar outside the session AND previous bar inside the session.
Exit: short, bars since the position was first observed after a flat position >= maximum holding bars OR bullish structure OR higher-timeframe bullish bias OR balance regime OR current bar outside the session AND previous bar inside the session.
