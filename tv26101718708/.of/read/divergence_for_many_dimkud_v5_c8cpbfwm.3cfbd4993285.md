Calculation: Divergence indicator = any enabled one of MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI of close, SMA-smoothed stochastic of close, CCI of close, momentum of close, OBV, short-period VWMA of close minus long-period VWMA of close, Chaikin money flow, MFI of close, negative Williams Vix Fix, stochastic RSI, SMI ergodic oscillator, volume-weighted MACD histogram, bull bear power, smoothed balance of power, smoothed relative volatility index, or logistic dominance. An external indicator is reproducible only if it is itself calculated from the permitted data.
Calculation: Negative Williams Vix Fix = -100 * (highest close over the lookback - low) / highest close over the lookback.
Calculation: Volume-weighted MACD histogram = D - EMA(D), where D is EMA(volume * close) / EMA(volume) with a shorter period minus the same ratio with a longer period.
Calculation: Bull bear power = high + low - 2 * EMA(close).
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power = TEMA(EMA(B)), where R = high - low if high != low, otherwise R = 0.01, and B = ((high - open) + (close - low) + max(close - open, 0) - (open - low) - (high - close) - max(open - close, 0)) / (3 * R).
Calculation: Smoothed relative volatility index = a selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of 100 * U / (U + D), where U is the EMA of close standard deviation on bars with positive close change and zero otherwise, and D is the EMA of close standard deviation on bars with nonpositive close change and zero otherwise; U and D have the same EMA period.
Calculation: Logistic dominance = sign(mean(A)) * standard deviation(A), where H is the highest high over the map lookback, J is the highest high over the dominance lookback, r = -g(-close) - g(close), g(s) = (close - close[dominance lookback]) / close[dominance lookback] * s / J * (1 - s / J), m = r * close / H * (1 - close / H), and A contains m and, for each historical offset from zero through the iteration count minus two, r * abs(m[offset]) * (1 - m[offset]).
Calculation: Regular divergence = for an enabled divergence indicator I, select a confirmed price pivot among the most recent configured number of pivots and within the maximum bar lookback. Pivots use close or high/low with equal left and right pivot periods. Let L be bars since the actual pivot, S be zero for no confirmation or one otherwise, and require L > 5. For every offset y from S + 1 through L - 1, compare I[y] and close[y] with their respective lines starting at the value at S minus one slope and decreasing by one slope per iteration, where slope = (value[S] - value[L]) / (L - S). Bullish divergence requires I[S] > I[L], the selected close or low at S < the pivot low, and all intermediate indicator and close values >= their respective lines. Bearish divergence requires I[S] < I[L], the selected close or high at S > the pivot high, and all intermediate indicator and close values <= their respective lines. When confirmation is enabled, bullish divergence also requires I > I[1] OR close > close[1], and bearish divergence requires I < I[1] OR close < close[1].
Calculation: Optional entry filters = each enabled filter must pass: short-period smoothed true range < or > long-period smoothed true range as selected; short-period smoothed volume < or > long-period smoothed volume as selected; selected Keltner or Bollinger channel condition, using the current band for both current and previous-bar comparisons; each of two independently configured RSI ranges; CCI range; Chande momentum oscillator range; MFI range; MFI rising above every prior value or falling below every prior value over the selected lookback, according to the enabled alternatives; and Supertrend direction, optionally inverted. The channel alternatives for a long are low < lower band, low[1] < current lower band AND close > current lower band, close < lower band, or close[1] < current lower band AND close > current lower band; for a short substitute high and upper band and reverse the inequalities. RSI, CCI, and Chande momentum ranges include their endpoints; MFI ranges exclude them. For each RSI, CCI, Chande momentum, and MFI range filter, enabling either side disables the other side unless its own range is also enabled. Compute RSI, CCI, and Chande momentum on the selected higher or lower timeframe using the last completed requested bar, with the historical result shifted by one chart bar; compute MFI on the selected higher or lower timeframe using the bar still forming. Evaluate the MFI rising/falling test in the requested timeframe using that timeframe's still-forming MFI. Chande momentum uses the selected OHLC-derived source. Supertrend uses the midpoint of high and low and ATR: its lower band is the greater of the current raw lower band and previous lower band when previous close was above the previous lower band, otherwise the current raw lower band; its upper band is the lesser of the current raw upper band and previous upper band when previous close was below the previous upper band, otherwise the current raw upper band. Its direction starts up, switches up when close exceeds the previous upper band, and switches down when close falls below the previous lower band.
Long: bullish regular divergence.
Short: bearish regular divergence.
Long: bullish regular divergence AND all enabled long entry filters pass AND datetime is within the configured testing interval AND the most recently closed trade's entry bar is not the current bar.
Short: bearish regular divergence AND all enabled short entry filters pass AND datetime is within the configured testing interval AND the most recently closed trade's entry bar is not the current bar.
Exit: short, opposite-signal exit enabled AND bullish regular divergence AND all enabled long entry filters pass.
Exit: long, opposite-signal exit enabled AND bearish regular divergence AND all enabled short entry filters pass.
Exit: long, low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction).
Exit: short, high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction).
