Calculation: Rejection-block candidates = On the first confirmed chart bar of each new higher-timeframe bar, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and its ATR. Body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. A bearish candidate has body > 0, upper wick > a body-ratio parameter × body, and either ATR is unavailable or upper wick > an ATR-multiple parameter × ATR. A bullish candidate uses the same conditions with lower wick. If both qualify, select the bearish candidate for its direction and boundaries, but mark it bullish for subsequent invalidation. Bearish boundaries are high and max(open, close); bullish boundaries are min(open, close) and low. Reject a candidate if its top or bottom differs from the corresponding boundary of any retained block by less than a proximity parameter. Retain accepted blocks in creation order up to a maximum count, removing the oldest first. When mitigation is enabled, remove a bullish-marked block if chart low < its bottom and otherwise remove a bearish block if chart high > its top; perform this removal after candidate detection on each bar.
Calculation: Profile levels = For the selected session or weekly period, accumulate chart-bar highs, lows, and volumes until the period ends. If the bar count exceeds a minimum parameter and the range is positive, divide the range into a parameterized number of equal price bins. Distribute each bar's volume equally among all bins from its low bin through its high bin, inclusive. The point of control is the center of the lowest-index bin with maximum volume. Starting there, expand the value area one adjacent bin at a time toward the greater adjacent volume, preferring the upper bin on a tie, until a parameterized fraction of total volume is included or both adjacent volumes are zero. The value-area levels are the upper edge of the highest included bin and the lower edge of the lowest included bin. Use the most recently completed valid profile.
Calculation: Structural midpoint = Sort all available enabled levels ascending and select the element at zero-based index floor(count / 2). Levels comprise: for the active configured session, if its running high exceeds its running low, its running pivot (high + low + latest close) / 3 if enabled and enabled symmetric pivot plus/minus parameterized range multiples; for the last completed higher-timeframe bar, if enabled and its high exceeds its low, its pivot and enabled symmetric pivot plus/minus the same range multiples; and the latest profile's point of control and value-area levels if the profile is enabled. The session range and higher-timeframe range are their respective high minus low. Only the configured subset of pivot multiples used by the bias is included. The midpoint is unavailable when there are no levels.
Long: A bullish rejection-block candidate is accepted AND no bearish candidate qualifies on the same bar.
Short: A bearish rejection-block candidate is accepted.
Long: Structural midpoint is available AND close > structural midpoint.
Short: Structural midpoint is available AND close <= structural midpoint.
