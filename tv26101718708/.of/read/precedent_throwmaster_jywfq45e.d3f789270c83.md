Calculation: Structural levels = Maintain a capacity-limited, oldest-first list of prices. Add each newly confirmed external pivot high or low; each bullish or bearish fair value gap midpoint when the intervening candle has the corresponding direction; the previous candle's low after a bullish displacement or high after a bearish displacement; and the last completed higher-timeframe period's high and low at the start of each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other. A bullish displacement is a bullish candle with body at least its rolling body-size percentile, preceded by a bearish candle and closing above the latest confirmed internal pivot high; bearish displacement is the mirror. Internal pivots use a shorter symmetric lookback than external pivots. Merge each new price into the nearest existing level within an ATR-based tolerance, choosing the later level on a distance tie; otherwise append it. When a pivot merges, replace the level price with the average of its previous price and the pivot price. Retain levels until removed by the capacity limit.
Calculation: External pivot = A high or low that is extreme over equal numbers of bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars have completed. A new pivot replaces the corresponding pending structure-break level before that bar's events are evaluated.
Calculation: Flow = Cumulative sum of (close minus open) divided by the greater of bar range and minimum price increment, multiplied by volume when its rolling average is positive and available; otherwise use the ratio without volume.
Calculation: Upward sweep = The nearest structural level below close within an ATR-based distance exists AND low is below that level AND the lower wick is at least its rolling percentile threshold. Downward sweep is the mirror using the nearest level above close, high and upper wick.
Calculation: Upward divergence = A new external pivot low confirms AND its price is below the preceding external pivot low AND flow at the new pivot is above flow at the preceding pivot. Downward divergence is the mirror at external pivot highs.
Calculation: Upward shift = Close is above the pending external pivot high. Downward shift = Close is below the pending external pivot low. Clear each corresponding pending level whenever its shift condition occurs, including when another event takes priority or no event is recorded.
Calculation: Climax = If rolling average volume is positive and available and its percentile threshold is available, volume is at least its rolling percentile threshold AND range is at least its rolling percentile threshold; otherwise range is at least its rolling percentile threshold AND body size is at least its rolling percentile threshold.
Calculation: Squeeze release = Bollinger Bands were strictly inside Keltner Channels for at least a threshold number of consecutive preceding bars AND are no longer strictly inside. Both channels use the same close SMA as their center; Bollinger width is a standard-deviation multiple and Keltner width is an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Upward rejection = Close is within the ATR-based merge tolerance of a structural level AND either a bullish engulfing candle or a bullish pin candle occurs. A bullish engulfing candle closes above open, follows a bearish candle, closes at or above the previous open and opens at or below the previous close. A bullish pin candle has a lower wick at least its rolling percentile threshold and greater than a body-size multiple, and closes above the bar midpoint. Downward rejection mirrors these conditions using a bearish engulfing or bearish pin candle.
Calculation: Selected event = Evaluate raw conditions in this priority order and select only the first true condition: upward sweep, downward sweep, upward divergence, downward divergence, upward shift, downward shift, climax, squeeze release, upward rejection, downward rejection. A climax is upward when close is above open and downward otherwise. A squeeze release is upward when close is above the channel center and downward otherwise. Selection occurs before the spacing filter, so a selected event that fails spacing does not fall through to another condition.
Calculation: Recorded event = The selected event occurs on a completed chart bar AND the calibration lookback has elapsed AND the required efficiency-ratio rank and upper-wick percentile are available AND ATR is positive AND at least the outcome-horizon number of bars have elapsed since the last recorded event of the same class, regardless of direction. Update the last-recorded bar only when an event passes these conditions.
Long: Recorded event direction = upward
Short: Recorded event direction = downward
