Calculation: UT trailing stop = On each chart bar, let p be the prior stop (0 if unavailable), x be close, x_prev be prior close, and d be key × ATR(period). Set the stop to max(p, x − d) if x > p AND x_prev > p; min(p, x + d) if x < p AND x_prev < p; x − d if x > p; otherwise x + d. Compute separate stops for buy and sell using their independently configured key and ATR-period parameters.
Long: (On the previous chart bar, the shorter-lookback EMA of close crossed above the medium-lookback EMA of close OR the longer-lookback EMA of close) AND previous close < previous open AND current high > previous high AND current close > current open OR current close crossed above the buy UT trailing stop.
Short: (On the previous chart bar, the shorter-lookback EMA of close crossed below the medium-lookback EMA of close OR the longer-lookback EMA of close) AND previous close > previous open AND current low < previous low AND current close < current open OR current close crossed below the sell UT trailing stop.
