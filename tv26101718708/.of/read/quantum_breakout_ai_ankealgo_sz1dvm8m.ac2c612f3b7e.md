Calculation: Compression range = the highest high minus the lowest low over a short lookback ending at the current bar; compression holds when this range is less than ATR multiplied by a range multiplier.
Calculation: Tracked range = on a compressed bar with no tracked range, initialize its upper and lower boundaries to the highest high and lowest low over the compression lookback, its start to the first bar of that lookback, and its count to the lookback length. On each subsequent compressed bar, if high is at most the upper boundary plus an ATR tolerance and low is at least the lower boundary minus that tolerance, expand the boundaries to include the bar and increment the count; otherwise discard the range. Discard the range on a noncompressed bar. Do not restart a discarded range until a later bar.
Calculation: Live zone = when the tracked range count first equals the minimum zone duration, create a zone with the tracked boundaries and start, replacing any previous live zone. Its boundaries remain fixed. At each bar close, check for a breakout before updating the tracked range; a breakout ends the live zone. After that check, a noncompressed bar also ends the live zone if the discarded tracked range had reached the minimum duration.
Calculation: Trade levels = at breakout, set entry price to the breached zone boundary and freeze the stop at the opposite boundary beyond an ATR buffer calculated on the breakout bar. Risk is the distance between entry and stop. Set three progressively more distant profit targets at entry plus risk multiples for a long, or entry minus risk multiples for a short. Each target closes its configured fraction of the original position once, capped at the remaining position; the fractions sum to the original position.
Long: At bar close, a live zone exists AND close > its upper boundary.
Short: At bar close, a live zone exists AND close < its lower boundary.
Exit: long, low <= the frozen long stop OR a new zone forms after the entry bar OR another zone breaks out while the position remains open.
Exit: short, high >= the frozen short stop OR a new zone forms after the entry bar OR another zone breaks out while the position remains open.
Exit: long, high >= an enabled, not-yet-closed profit target; close its configured fraction.
Exit: short, low <= an enabled, not-yet-closed profit target; close its configured fraction.
Calculation: Same-bar execution = check breakout and replace any prior trade first, then evaluate the new trade's stop and targets on the breakout bar. For partial-position accounting, process reached enabled targets in order from nearest to farthest before applying the stop to the remaining position. A newly formed zone closes the active trade only after its breakout bar.
