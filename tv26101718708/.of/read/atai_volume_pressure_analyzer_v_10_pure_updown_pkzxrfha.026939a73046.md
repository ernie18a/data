Calculation: Directional volume = either geometry estimates, with buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and sell volume = volume × (high − close) / (high − low), splitting volume equally when high = low; or sums of classified volume from all lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the still-forming lower-timeframe bar. Classify a lower-timeframe bar as up when close > open, down when close < open; when close = open, compare close with the previous lower-timeframe close, retaining the previous classification when equal. Buy and sell volume are the respective up and down sums.
Calculation: Volume delta = buy volume − sell volume. Segment deltas are sums of volume delta over the newer and older portions of a chart-timeframe lookback, excluding the midpoint bar; the newer portion includes the current bar. Ranked-bar deltas are volume deltas at each of the highest buy-volume and highest sell-volume bars in the lookback.
Calculation: Wing angles = atan((later price − earlier price) / number of bars between them / ATR) in degrees, calculated separately for highs and lows between the oldest and midpoint bars and between the midpoint and current bars of the same lookback. With the optional offset, use prices at the oldest and midpoint offsets reduced by the offset parameter; the current endpoint for the angle remains the current high or low.
Calculation: Midpoint angle = average of the oriented angles from the midpoint-to-oldest vector to the midpoint-to-current vector for highs and lows, each vector using horizontal bar distance and vertical price difference divided by ATR over the same lookback. Measure each angle counterclockwise in degrees from 0 inclusive to 360 exclusive.
Calculation: Volume spike = directional volume / max(SMA of that directional volume, epsilon) >= ratio threshold OR (directional volume − its SMA) / max(standard deviation of that directional volume, epsilon) >= z-score threshold; SMA and standard deviation use the same lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator vote count = number of enabled oscillators meeting their respective overbought or oversold thresholds on the same bar: RSI of close; stochastic with raw close position in the high-low range smoothed by an SMA for K and another SMA of equal length for D, both K and D meeting the threshold; Williams %R; CCI and MFI of typical price; DeMarker as SMA(max(high − previous high, 0)) / max(SMA(max(high − previous high, 0)) + SMA(max(previous low − low, 0)), epsilon); and stochastic RSI as (RSI − lowest RSI) / max(highest RSI − lowest RSI, epsilon). The stochastic RSI uses the same RSI series as the RSI vote. Windowed vote count = maximum same-bar vote count across the current bar and a configurable number of preceding bars.
Long: Current volume delta > 0 OR either segment delta > 0 OR any ranked-bar delta > 0.
Short: Current volume delta < 0 OR either segment delta < 0 OR any ranked-bar delta < 0.
Long: Either wing's high angle or low angle > 0 AND its absolute angle >= the near-zero threshold.
Short: Either wing's high angle or low angle < 0 AND its absolute angle >= the near-zero threshold.
Long: Midpoint angle > 180 degrees.
Short: Midpoint angle < 180 degrees.
Long: Buy-volume spike = true AND windowed oversold vote count >= minimum votes AND (sell-volume spike = false OR windowed overbought vote count < minimum votes OR windowed oversold vote count > windowed overbought vote count).
Short: Sell-volume spike = true AND windowed overbought vote count >= minimum votes AND (buy-volume spike = false OR windowed oversold vote count < minimum votes OR windowed overbought vote count >= windowed oversold vote count).
