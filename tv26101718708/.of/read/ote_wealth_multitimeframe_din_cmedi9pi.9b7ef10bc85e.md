Calculation: Confirmed impulse = On each configured timeframe, confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. For a new pivot high, choose the lowest retained pivot low strictly between the preceding pivot high and the new high, or the most recent earlier pivot low if none exists; optionally always choose the most recent earlier pivot low. For a new pivot low, choose the highest retained pivot high strictly between the preceding pivot low and the new low, or the most recent earlier pivot high if none exists; optionally always choose the most recent earlier pivot high. If there is no preceding same-side pivot, use the configured lookback as the beginning of the search interval. Retain a bounded history of confirmed pivots on each side. Process a pivot high before a pivot low when both confirm together.
Calculation: Valid impulse = The endpoint is higher than the origin for an upward impulse or lower for a downward impulse AND the distance between their pivot bars is at least the minimum impulse length and at most the lookback AND the endpoint confirmation delay is at most the lookback AND, if the structure filter is selected, the endpoint breaks the preceding same-side pivot or no preceding same-side pivot exists AND, if the displacement filter is selected, the impulse price range is at least a multiple of ATR at confirmation, unless ATR is unavailable or the multiplier is nonpositive. Either, both, or neither filter may be selected.
Calculation: Active zone = The most recently registered valid impulse for a configured timeframe, replaced by the next valid impulse and removed when invalidated. A zone is registered when its direction, endpoint time, high, or low differs from the last registered zone for that timeframe. Evaluate impulses on the chart timeframe or on a configured higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use either its forming bar or, with confirmation selected, its last completed bar; historical lookahead-off data is released at the higher bar's close. For a lower timeframe, use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, optionally shifted by one lower-timeframe bar. A newly registered zone is active immediately.
Calculation: Retracement level = For an upward impulse, impulse high minus impulse range multiplied by the selected retracement fraction; for a downward impulse, impulse low plus impulse range multiplied by that fraction. The zone uses a shallower and a deeper retracement level; the core uses either the shallower zone level or another configurable level and a further configurable level.
Calculation: Zone overlap = Chart-bar high is at least the lower boundary AND chart-bar low is at most the upper boundary. A zone or core entry occurs on the first overlapping bar after a non-overlapping bar, or on the zone's registration bar if it overlaps.
Long: A new valid upward impulse registers an active zone.
Short: A new valid downward impulse registers an active zone.
Long: An active upward-impulse zone is entered OR its core is entered.
Short: An active downward-impulse zone is entered OR its core is entered.
Calculation: Zone invalidation = For an upward-impulse zone, chart-bar close or, if wick invalidation is selected, low is below the deeper zone level; for a downward-impulse zone, chart-bar close or, if wick invalidation is selected, high is above the deeper zone level. Evaluate zone and core entries before invalidation on the same bar.
