Long: Position is flat AND no trade is open AND (date filter is disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured date window) AND RSI of close is below its threshold; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, and for the chart timeframe use the current bar.
Long: Position is long AND (date filter is disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured date window) AND a safety-order rung remains unfilled AND close is at or below the original base entry price reduced by the next rung's percentage deviation. Fill rungs in configured order, at most one per chart bar; each rung has its own deviation and cash size.
Exit: long, close is at or above the position's quantity-weighted average entry price increased by the take-profit percentage.
