Calculation: Timeframe bias = compare each timeframe's close with its shorter-period EMA and compare that EMA with its longer-period EMA. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use its last intrabar within the chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Alignment count = number of selected timeframes whose bias is bullish or bearish, respectively. The selected timeframes are ordered from longer to shorter and may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Linear regression slope = current linear regression estimate of close minus the estimate at the preceding point of the same fitted regression.
Calculation: Choppiness index = 100 times log10 of the sum of true range over the lookback divided by the highest high minus the lowest low over that lookback, divided by log10 of the lookback; floor the high-low range at a small positive value.
Calculation: Chandelier direction = calculate a long stop as the lookback's highest high minus an ATR multiple and a short stop as its lowest low plus the same ATR multiple. Unless the previous close was below the previous long stop, raise the long stop to at least its previous value; unless the previous close was above the previous short stop, lower the short stop to at most its previous value. Set direction bullish if close is above the previous short stop, bearish if close is below the previous long stop, otherwise retain the previous direction. Initialize direction bullish, and substitute the current low or high for an unavailable previous long or short stop respectively when updating stops.
Calculation: Volume delta slope = rolling sum of volume assigned positive when close is at least open and negative otherwise, minus that rolling sum a slope-lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close above open AND candle body greater than an ATR multiple; bearish displacement = close below open AND candle body greater than the same ATR multiple.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = the previous completed higher-timeframe period's low and high and the latest confirmed major and minor pivot lows and highs. A pivot requires the same number of bars on each side; the major pivot lookback is longer than the minor pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bullish sweep of a level = low below the level AND close above the level AND close above open AND (level minus low) divided by the candle's high-low range is at least a wick-ratio threshold; bearish sweep = high above the level AND close below the level AND close below open AND (high minus level) divided by the candle's high-low range is at least the same threshold. Floor the range at a small positive value. A major sweep uses the previous higher-timeframe level or major pivot; any sweep also includes the minor pivot.
Calculation: Recent bullish macro touch = low at or below the previous higher-timeframe low or latest major pivot low within a lookback including the current bar; recent bearish macro touch = high at or above the previous higher-timeframe high or latest major pivot high within the same lookback.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = bars since the last same-side strong, regular, weak, or chop event must exceed a gap. A same-side event updates its timestamp and resets the opposite timestamp; process bullish events before bearish events. A chop event is any same-side sweep AND choppiness above its threshold AND no same-side displacement AND same-side cooldown satisfied. Swing events have separate same-side cooldowns and update their timestamps whenever their conditions occur.
Long: A selected timeframe's close > its shorter EMA AND its shorter EMA > its longer EMA.
Short: A selected timeframe's close < its shorter EMA AND its shorter EMA < its longer EMA.
Long: Recent bullish macro touch AND close > open AND linear regression slope > 0 AND (Chandelier direction is bullish OR bullish displacement) AND bullish swing cooldown is satisfied.
Short: Recent bearish macro touch AND close < open AND linear regression slope < 0 AND (Chandelier direction is bearish OR bearish displacement) AND bearish swing cooldown is satisfied AND the bullish swing condition is false.
Long: Major bullish sweep AND linear regression slope > 0 AND Chandelier direction is bullish AND volume delta slope > 0 AND (session filter is disabled OR current bar is in an active session) AND bullish signal cooldown is satisfied AND (choppiness index <= threshold OR bullish displacement).
Short: Major bearish sweep AND linear regression slope < 0 AND Chandelier direction is bearish AND volume delta slope < 0 AND (session filter is disabled OR current bar is in an active session) AND bearish signal cooldown is satisfied AND (choppiness index <= threshold OR bearish displacement).
Long: (Any bullish sweep OR (bullish alignment count >= alignment threshold AND Chandelier direction is bullish AND choppiness index <= threshold AND bullish displacement AND (previous close < previous open OR low <= previous low))) AND linear regression slope > 0 AND Chandelier direction is bullish AND (session filter is disabled OR current bar is in an active session) AND bullish signal cooldown is satisfied AND (choppiness index <= threshold OR bullish displacement).
Short: (Any bearish sweep OR (bearish alignment count >= alignment threshold AND Chandelier direction is bearish AND choppiness index <= threshold AND bearish displacement AND (previous close > previous open OR high >= previous high))) AND linear regression slope < 0 AND Chandelier direction is bearish AND (session filter is disabled OR current bar is in an active session) AND bearish signal cooldown is satisfied AND (choppiness index <= threshold OR bearish displacement).
Long: Any bullish sweep AND (linear regression slope > 0 OR Chandelier direction is bullish) AND choppiness index <= threshold AND bullish signal cooldown is satisfied AND neither swing condition nor either side's strong or regular condition is true.
Short: Any bearish sweep AND (linear regression slope < 0 OR Chandelier direction is bearish) AND choppiness index <= threshold AND bearish signal cooldown is satisfied AND neither swing condition nor either side's strong or regular condition nor the weak bullish condition is true.
