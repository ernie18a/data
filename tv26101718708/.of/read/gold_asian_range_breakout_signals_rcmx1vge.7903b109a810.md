Calculation: Session high and session low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured Asian session on the current day in the configured timezone; reset at each new day and retain after the session ends.
Calculation: Upper breakout level = session high + breakout buffer multiplier × chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Lower breakout level = session low − breakout buffer multiplier × chart-timeframe ATR.
Long: Bar opening time is within the configured London session AND the Asian session has ended AND session high − session low >= minimum range multiplier × ATR AND session high − session low <= maximum range multiplier × ATR AND the current weekday is not configured to be skipped AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND close > upper breakout level AND previous close <= previous upper breakout level.
Short: Bar opening time is within the configured London session AND the Asian session has ended AND session high − session low >= minimum range multiplier × ATR AND session high − session low <= maximum range multiplier × ATR AND the current weekday is not configured to be skipped AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND close < lower breakout level AND previous close >= previous lower breakout level.
