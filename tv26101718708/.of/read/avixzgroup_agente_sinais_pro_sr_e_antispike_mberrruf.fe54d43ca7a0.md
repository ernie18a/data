Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that is the highest/lowest across the pivot lookback on both sides; detect on the higher-timeframe bar still forming, after the right-side bars are available.
Calculation: Resistance/support levels = add each detected pivot high/low (only if the optional creation-volume filter is disabled or pivot-bar volume ≥ its longer-lookback volume average × multiplier); merge with the first existing level within the grouping percentage, keeping the higher resistance or lower support, otherwise add a level. At each daily boundary, snapshot the levels if daily caching is enabled.
Calculation: Active resistance/support = the nearest cached (or current, if caching is disabled) resistance above close / support below close.
Short: Active resistance exists AND high ≥ active resistance AND close ≤ active resistance × (1 + anti-spike percentage) AND (optional live-volume filter is disabled OR volume ≥ chart-timeframe volume average × multiplier).
Long: Active support exists AND low ≤ active support AND close ≥ active support × (1 − anti-spike percentage) AND (optional live-volume filter is disabled OR volume ≥ chart-timeframe volume average × multiplier).
