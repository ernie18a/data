Calculation: Hourly pump score = mean over the selected historical period of max(hourly close / hourly open − 1, 0) × hourly volume / its moving average, grouped by hour; use completed 1-hour bars.
Calculation: Hourly dump score = mean over the selected historical period of max(1 − hourly close / hourly open, 0) × hourly volume / its moving average, grouped by hour; use completed 1-hour bars.
Calculation: Daily pump score = mean over the selected historical period of max(daily close / daily open − 1, 0) × daily volume / its moving average, grouped by weekday; use completed 1-day bars.
Calculation: Daily dump score = mean over the selected historical period of max(1 − daily close / daily open, 0) × daily volume / its moving average, grouped by weekday; use completed 1-day bars.
Calculation: Pump threshold = mean of the available hourly pump scores × an extreme multiplier; separately, mean of the available daily pump scores × the same multiplier.
Calculation: Dump threshold = mean of the available hourly dump scores × an extreme multiplier; separately, mean of the available daily dump scores × the same multiplier.
Long: current hour's hourly pump score > hourly pump threshold OR current weekday's daily pump score > daily pump threshold OR, on a 1-hour chart, the next hour's pump score > hourly pump threshold AND (the next hour's dump score ≤ hourly dump threshold OR the next hour's pump score ≥ next hour's dump score) OR, on a 1-day chart, the next weekday's pump score > daily pump threshold AND (the next weekday's dump score ≤ daily dump threshold OR the next weekday's pump score ≥ next weekday's dump score)
Short: current hour's hourly dump score > hourly dump threshold OR current weekday's daily dump score > daily dump threshold OR, on a 1-hour chart, the next hour's dump score > hourly dump threshold AND (the next hour's pump score ≤ hourly pump threshold OR the next hour's dump score > next hour's pump score) OR, on a 1-day chart, the next weekday's dump score > daily dump threshold AND (the next weekday's pump score ≤ daily pump threshold OR the next weekday's dump score > next weekday's pump score)
