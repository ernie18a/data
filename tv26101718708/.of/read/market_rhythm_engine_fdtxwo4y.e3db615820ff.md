Calculation: Bucket profiles = For each hour-of-day bucket or weekday bucket in the selected timezone, average positive returns and negative-return magnitudes separately over completed bars in the selected historical period whose opening times fall in that bucket. Return = 100 × (close − open) / open, or 0 if open is 0. Weight each return magnitude by volume divided by its simple moving average, or by 1 if that average is unavailable or not positive. A bucket average is unavailable until its sample count reaches the selected minimum. The historical period starts at a selected fixed timestamp, a selected duration before the backtest end, or the beginning of loaded history. Hour buckets use hourly bars and weekday buckets use daily bars; these bars may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and profiles use completed bars only.
Calculation: Profile baseline = Mean of the available bucket averages for the same return direction and bucket type.
Long: The current hour-of-day or weekday bucket's positive-return average > its positive-return baseline × threshold.
Short: The current hour-of-day or weekday bucket's negative-return average > its negative-return baseline × threshold.
Long: Chart bars match the hour or day bucket duration AND the preceding chart bar's profile for the current bucket has positive-return average > its positive-return baseline × threshold AND (negative-return average ≤ its negative-return baseline × threshold OR negative-return average or baseline is unavailable OR positive-return average ≥ negative-return average).
Short: Chart bars match the hour or day bucket duration AND the preceding chart bar's profile for the current bucket has negative-return average > its negative-return baseline × threshold AND (positive-return average ≤ its positive-return baseline × threshold OR positive-return average or baseline is unavailable OR negative-return average > positive-return average).
