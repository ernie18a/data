Calculation: Signal state = initially neutral; change to long on a main long signal and short on a main short signal, and retain that state until the opposite main signal. Record the close and ATR on each change.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = on each enabled higher reference timeframe, the shorter EMA of close is above the longer EMA and chart close is above that timeframe's session VWAP for bullish alignment; reverse both comparisons for bearish alignment. Require alignment on every enabled reference timeframe. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = SMA of the stochastic of close over its lookback. Use four instances with progressively longer lookbacks: fast, medium, slow and trend. The trend instance uses its configured timeframe: use the last completed bar if it is higher than the chart timeframe on historical bars, the still-forming bar if higher in real time, or the last available intrabar if lower. Compare its previous value with the value on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Bull and bear scores = counts of the respective true conditions: close above or below session VWAP; RSI above or below its midpoint; MACD line above or below its signal line; shorter EMA above or below longer EMA; ADX above its strength threshold AND close above or below shorter EMA; volume above its SMA AND close above or below open; and other-timeframe RSI above or below its midpoint. For the other-timeframe RSI, use the last completed bar if its timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe on historical bars, the still-forming bar if higher in real time, or the last available intrabar if lower. Active score is the bull score in long signal state, the bear score in short signal state, and the greater score in neutral state.
Calculation: Quad reversal state = when all four smoothed stochastics first become oversold, record the current low and fast stochastic and activate the bullish stage; when all first become overbought, record the current high and fast stochastic and activate the bearish stage. Activating either stage deactivates the other. After the bullish stage, once all four are no longer oversold, set bullish reversal when low is at or below the recorded low, fast stochastic is above its recorded value and its previous value, and fast stochastic is at or below the oversold threshold plus an offset. For bearish reversal, reverse the price and stochastic comparisons and use the overbought threshold minus an offset. A reversal stays set until reset by a main signal or a new stage of the same direction; a main signal resets both stages and both reversals.
Calculation: Structure oscillator = identify a short-term pivot high when the previous high is greater than the high before it and greater than or equal to the current high; define pivot lows symmetrically with less-than comparisons. At each pivot, replace the latest corresponding level and reset its crossed flag. A bullish or bearish break occurs on the first close strictly above the latest high level or below the latest low level, respectively. For intermediate levels, copy the latest short-term pivot level on the first bar of each consecutive run of corresponding short-term pivots and apply the same first-break rule. For long-term levels, copy the latest intermediate level on the first bar of each consecutive run of short-term pivots for which an intermediate level exists, and apply the same first-break rule. Process each level and its break before the next level. For each term, maintain a direction initially neutral, changing to bullish on its bullish break or bearish on its bearish break, with bullish taking precedence if both occur. On a bullish direction change reset its running maximum close to current close; on a bearish direction change reset its running minimum close to current close; otherwise update each extreme except that the opposite extreme is retained on a direction change. Normalize as 100 times the SMA of (close minus running minimum) divided by (running maximum minus running minimum). The structure oscillator is the weighted average of the three normalized values, replacing unavailable values with zero; the cycle oscillator is structure oscillator minus its EMA plus the midpoint of the oscillator scale.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = initialize the previous stop to zero. If current and previous close are both above the previous stop, use the greater of the previous stop and close minus ATR times a multiplier. If both are below it, use the lesser of the previous stop and close plus ATR times the multiplier. Otherwise use close minus that distance when close is above the previous stop, and close plus that distance otherwise.
Long: Shorter EMA crosses above longer EMA AND signal state was neutral or short AND higher-timeframe bullish alignment is true if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.
Short: Shorter EMA crosses below longer EMA AND signal state was neutral or long AND higher-timeframe bearish alignment is true if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.
Long: Close crosses above the ATR trailing stop.
Short: Close crosses below the ATR trailing stop.
Long: The sign of structure oscillator minus its midpoint increases OR the sign of cycle oscillator minus its midpoint increases.
Short: The sign of structure oscillator minus its midpoint decreases OR the sign of cycle oscillator minus its midpoint decreases.
Long: Trend smoothed stochastic is at or above its bullish trend threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is at or below the oversold threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is above its previous value.
Short: Trend smoothed stochastic is at or below its bearish trend threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is at or above the overbought threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is below its previous value.
Long: Bullish quad reversal state is set.
Short: Bearish quad reversal state is set.
Long: Trend smoothed stochastic is at or above its strong bullish threshold AND medium and slow smoothed stochastics are each at or above their midpoint AND fast smoothed stochastic is at or below the oversold threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is above its previous value.
Short: Trend smoothed stochastic is at or below its strong bearish threshold AND medium and slow smoothed stochastics are each at or below their midpoint AND fast smoothed stochastic is at or above the overbought threshold AND fast smoothed stochastic is below its previous value.
Long: Signal state is long AND active score is at or above the higher setup threshold AND bullish quad reversal is set OR signal state is long AND active score is at or above the higher setup threshold AND the hidden bullish stochastic conditions hold OR signal state is long AND active score is at or above the higher setup threshold AND the bullish stochastic flag-entry conditions hold; in each case structure oscillator must be at or above its midpoint AND close must be above session VWAP AND higher-timeframe bullish alignment must hold if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.

Exit: short, fast smoothed stochastic is at or below the oversold threshold AND trend smoothed stochastic is above its previous chart-bar value AND close is above the longer EMA.
