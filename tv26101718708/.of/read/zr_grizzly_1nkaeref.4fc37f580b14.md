Calculation: Signal bars = chart bars, or bars from a higher signal timeframe using its last completed bar. On each signal bar, swing high and swing low are the highest high and lowest low over the same lookback, including that bar; range = swing high - swing low.
Calculation: Long retracement level = swing high - range × retracement fraction; short retracement level = swing low + range × retracement fraction. Use two fractions, one larger than the other.
Calculation: Long sniper = signal-bar range > 0 AND signal-bar low <= either current long retracement level AND preceding signal-bar low > that same current level. Short sniper = signal-bar range > 0 AND signal-bar high >= either current short retracement level AND preceding signal-bar high < that same current level. For either sniper, optionally require chart close to be respectively above or below an EMA of close on a reference timeframe; use the last completed bar if the reference timeframe is higher, or the last intrabar if it is lower. Optionally also require the corresponding sniper on an intermediate timeframe higher than the chart and lower than the signal timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: For every long sniper, independently record the chart-bar low and the signal-timeframe swing high. Long confirmation level = recorded low + (recorded swing high - recorded low) × confirmation fraction. For every short sniper, independently record the chart-bar high and the signal-timeframe swing low. Short confirmation level = recorded high - (recorded high - recorded swing low) × confirmation fraction. Use two confirmation fractions, one larger than the other and both smaller than both retracement fractions. Each level can trigger only once per recorded sniper, starting on the following chart bar; retain each record until both its levels have triggered.
Calculation: Long confirmation = recorded swing high > recorded low AND chart close >= an untriggered long confirmation level AND previous chart close < that level. Short confirmation = recorded high > recorded swing low AND chart close <= an untriggered short confirmation level AND previous chart close > that level.
Calculation: Entry reference price = chart close on the entry signal bar. Fix the stop and target at that bar. For a long, select either swing high - range × stop fraction or entry reference price × (1 - fixed-stop fraction); if the selected Fibonacci stop is unavailable or >= entry reference price, use the fixed stop. For a short, select either swing low + range × stop fraction or entry reference price × (1 + fixed-stop fraction); if the selected Fibonacci stop is unavailable or <= entry reference price, use the fixed stop. Use either of two stop fractions, both larger than the retracement fractions, with one larger than the other. Long target = entry reference price + reward-to-risk ratio × (entry reference price - long stop); short target = entry reference price - reward-to-risk ratio × (short stop - entry reference price).
Long: Long confirmation at the larger confirmation fraction.
Short: Short confirmation at the larger confirmation fraction.
Long: Selected long sniper OR selected long confirmation at either selected confirmation fraction, AND position is flat AND bars since the last entry >= minimum entry spacing, or no entry has occurred.
Short: Selected short sniper OR selected short confirmation at either selected confirmation fraction, AND position is flat AND bars since the last entry >= minimum entry spacing, or no entry has occurred.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
