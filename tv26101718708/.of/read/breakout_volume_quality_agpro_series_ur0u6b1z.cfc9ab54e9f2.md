Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Body efficiency = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Defended resistance/support = the average price of confirmed pivot highs/lows, respectively, grouped when each new pivot is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the current level; each level must have at least the minimum number of touches and its latest pivot must be within the maximum age. A pivot is confirmed using the same number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Breakout score = 30 × clamp((relative volume - 1) / (extreme relative volume - 1), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp(body efficiency / minimum body efficiency, 0, 1) + 15 × clamp((directional close location - 0.50) / (minimum close location - 0.50), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp(ATR-scaled close distance beyond the broken level / full break distance, 0, 1) + 10 × clamp(candle range in ATR / full expansion, 0, 1) + 5 × clamp((preferred maximum range width in ATR - defended range width in ATR) / preferred maximum range width in ATR, 0, 1). If both live levels do not form a range, use 2.5 for the final component. For bearish breaks, directional close location is 1 - close location; for bullish breaks, it is close location.
Long: Close crosses above defended resistance plus an ATR-scaled break buffer, the prior close was at or below that threshold, the bar is complete, and the event cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Close crosses below defended support minus an ATR-scaled break buffer, the prior close was at or above that threshold, the bar is complete, and the event cooldown has elapsed.
Long: The long breakout condition holds, relative volume meets its confirmation threshold (when volume is required), body efficiency meets its minimum, close location meets its directional minimum, and breakout score meets its minimum confirmation score.
Short: The short breakout condition holds, relative volume meets its confirmation threshold (when volume is required), body efficiency meets its minimum, 1 - close location meets its directional minimum, and breakout score meets its minimum confirmation score.
Long: The long breakout condition holds and the confirmed-break conditions do not.
Short: The short breakout condition holds and the confirmed-break conditions do not.
Long: A live defended resistance exists, high is within an ATR-scaled proximity below it, close is no farther above it than the ATR-scaled break buffer, relative volume meets the approach threshold, body efficiency meets 65% of its minimum, close location is at least 0.58, and there is no bullish break.
Short: A live defended support exists, low is within an ATR-scaled proximity above it, close is no farther below it than the ATR-scaled break buffer, relative volume meets the approach threshold, body efficiency meets 65% of its minimum, close location is at most 0.42, and there is no bearish break.
Long: The bar is complete, the build-up cooldown has elapsed, a live defended resistance exists, the highest high minus lowest low over the build-up lookback is within the maximum ATR-scaled width, relative volume meets the build-up threshold, close is at or below resistance plus the ATR-scaled break buffer, and high is within the ATR-scaled proximity below resistance.
Short: The bar is complete, the build-up cooldown has elapsed, a live defended support exists, the highest high minus lowest low over the build-up lookback is within the maximum ATR-scaled width, relative volume meets the build-up threshold, close is at or above support minus the ATR-scaled break buffer, and low is within the ATR-scaled proximity above support.
Long: Relative volume meets its confirmation threshold, body efficiency meets its minimum, and close location meets its directional minimum.
Short: Relative volume meets its confirmation threshold, body efficiency meets its minimum, and close location is at most 1 minus its directional minimum.
Long: Relative volume meets its confirmation threshold, body efficiency meets 65% of its minimum, and close location is at least 0.58.
Short: Relative volume meets its confirmation threshold, body efficiency meets 65% of its minimum, and close location is at most 0.42.
Exit: Long, after a confirmed bullish breakout, close falls below the broken resistance by the ATR-scaled failure amount.
Exit: Short, after a confirmed bearish breakout, close rises above the broken support by the ATR-scaled failure amount.
