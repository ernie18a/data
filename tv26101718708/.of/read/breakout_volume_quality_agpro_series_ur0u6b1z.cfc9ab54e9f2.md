Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR, a positive price floor); safe range = max(high - low, the same floor); relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), with the SMA replaced by 1 when unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Body efficiency = abs(close - open) / safe range; close location = (close - low) / safe range.
Calculation: Defended levels = maintain resistance and support independently from confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. On confirmation, compare the pivot with the existing level using an ATR-scaled tolerance measured at the pivot bar. If no level exists or the distance exceeds the tolerance, replace the level with the pivot and reset touches to one; otherwise update the level to the touch-count-weighted mean of the level and pivot and increment touches. A level is eligible when touches reach the minimum and its latest pivot is no older than the maximum age. A recorded confirmed breakout clears its corresponding level and touches.
Calculation: Breakout trigger = resistance + breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR for a long, or support - breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR for a short. A breakout requires an eligible corresponding level, the current close beyond the current trigger, the previous close on or inside that same current trigger, and more than the event cooldown since the last recorded event. If both directions trigger, select long.
Calculation: Breakout score = 30 × clamp((relative volume - 1) / max(full-score relative volume - 1, 0.01), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp(body efficiency / max(minimum body efficiency, 0.01), 0, 1) + 15 × clamp((directional close location - 0.5) / max(minimum directional close location - 0.5, 0.01), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp(break distance in safe ATR / max(full-score break distance, 0.01), 0, 1) + 10 × clamp(safe range / safe ATR / max(full-score expansion, 0.01), 0, 1) + structure score. Directional close location is close location for long and 1 - close location for short; break distance is the distance from the level to the close in the breakout direction. Structure score is 5 × clamp((preferred range width - (resistance - support) / safe ATR) / max(preferred range width, 0.01), 0, 1) when both levels are eligible and resistance exceeds support, otherwise 2.5.
Calculation: Confirmed breakout = breakout trigger AND (relative-volume requirement disabled OR relative volume >= confirmation threshold) AND body efficiency >= minimum AND directional close location >= minimum AND breakout score >= confirmation threshold. Weak breakout = breakout trigger AND confirmed breakout is false.
Calculation: Recorded event = confirmed breakout allowed by the chart-timeframe display guard OR weak breakout with breakout score >= weak-event threshold when weak-event display is enabled and allowed by that guard. Each recorded event resets the shared event cooldown; a recorded confirmed breakout also starts or replaces an active retest with its side, level and bar. The guard restricts confirmed events to scores at or above its score threshold on chart timeframes below its cutoff and suppresses weak events there.
Calculation: Active retest = the latest recorded confirmed breakout, until its first hold, failure, expiry, or replacement by another recorded confirmed breakout. Evaluate starting on the next bar; the band is the saved level plus or minus the current safe ATR multiplied by the band-width parameter. A hold is eligible through the retest-window bar; failure is evaluated before expiry even on the first bar after the window. A hold or failure ends the retest.
Long: Confirmed long breakout.
Short: Confirmed short breakout.
Long: Weak long breakout AND breakout score >= weak-event threshold AND weak events are enabled AND the chart-timeframe display guard permits weak events.
Short: Weak short breakout AND breakout score >= weak-event threshold AND weak events are enabled AND the chart-timeframe display guard permits weak events.
Long: Active long retest AND bars since breakout <= retest window AND low <= band upper edge AND high >= band lower edge AND close >= saved level AND close >= open.
Short: Active short retest AND bars since breakout <= retest window AND low <= band upper edge AND high >= band lower edge AND close <= saved level AND close <= open.
Exit: long, active long retest AND close < saved level - failure multiplier × safe ATR.
Exit: short, active short retest AND close > saved level + failure multiplier × safe ATR.
Long: Eligible resistance AND high >= resistance - approach multiplier × safe ATR AND close <= resistance + breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR AND relative volume >= approach threshold AND body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency × 0.65 AND close location >= 0.58 AND no long breakout trigger AND more than the shared approach cooldown since either approach.
Short: Eligible support AND low <= support + approach multiplier × safe ATR AND close >= support - breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR AND relative volume >= approach threshold AND body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency × 0.65 AND close location <= 0.42 AND no short breakout trigger AND more than the shared approach cooldown since either approach.
Long: Eligible resistance AND (highest high - lowest low over the buildup lookback) / safe ATR <= maximum buildup width AND relative volume >= buildup threshold AND close <= resistance + breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR AND high >= resistance - approach multiplier × safe ATR AND more than the shared buildup cooldown since either buildup.
Short: Eligible support AND (highest high - lowest low over the buildup lookback) / safe ATR <= maximum buildup width AND relative volume >= buildup threshold AND close >= support - breakout-buffer multiplier × safe ATR AND low <= support + approach multiplier × safe ATR AND more than the shared buildup cooldown since either buildup AND no simultaneous long buildup.
Long: Relative volume >= confirmation threshold AND ((body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency AND close location >= minimum directional close location) OR (body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency × 0.65 AND close location >= 0.58 AND no strong bearish pressure)).
Short: Relative volume >= confirmation threshold AND ((body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency AND close location <= 1 - minimum directional close location AND no strong bullish pressure) OR (body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency × 0.65 AND close location <= 0.42 AND no strong bullish or bearish pressure AND no soft bullish pressure)).
