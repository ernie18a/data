Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of (source minus its channel-length EMA) divided by 0.015 times the channel-length EMA of the absolute source deviation; the fast line is an average-length EMA of that result, and the slow line is an SMA of the fast line. WaveTrend spread = fast line minus slow line.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA of multiplier times (close minus open) divided by (high minus low), minus an offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = SMA-smoothed stochastic of RSI, optionally using the logarithm of the source; the divergence series is either the smoothed stochastic or its average with a further SMA of itself.
Calculation: A confirmed oscillator fractal occurs two bars after a pivot whose oscillator value is strictly above, for a top, or strictly below, for a bottom, each of the two values on either side. A threshold-qualified top has pivot oscillator at or above its upper threshold; a threshold-qualified bottom has pivot oscillator at or below its lower threshold. Compare each pivot with the most recent prior qualifying fractal of the same type. Regular bullish divergence requires a bottom with a lower price low and a higher oscillator; hidden bullish divergence requires a bottom with a higher price low and a lower oscillator. Regular bearish divergence requires a top with a higher price high and a lower oscillator; hidden bearish divergence requires a top with a lower price high and a higher oscillator. Evaluate on the confirmation bar using prices at the pivots.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe WaveTrend spread = WaveTrend spread calculated using the higher-timeframe source and the script's higher-timeframe series mapping, using the last completed higher-timeframe values.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candle = Heikin-Ashi candle calculated on aggregated higher-timeframe bars. The source uses lookahead: on historical bars it uses the final candle of the higher-timeframe interval containing the chart bar, including data after that chart bar; in real time it uses the still-forming candle.
Long: WaveTrend fast line > slow line.
Short: WaveTrend fast line < slow line.

Exit: short, WaveTrend fast line >= slow line OR regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend slow line at either of two lower-threshold levels OR regular bullish divergence in RSI with a lower threshold OR regular bullish divergence in Stochastic RSI without a threshold.
