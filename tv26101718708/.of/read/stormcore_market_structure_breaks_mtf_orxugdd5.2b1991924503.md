Calculation: Active swing high/low = most recent confirmed pivot high/low on each higher timeframe, confirmed when its high/low is the extreme across the pivot lookback before and after it; clear that side's level after a BOS through it.
Long: On a higher-timeframe bar, close > active swing high AND previous higher-timeframe close <= active swing high AND (up-close body >= ATR × displacement factor OR displacement filter disabled) AND (volume > volume SMA × volume factor OR volume filter disabled) OR, on a chart-timeframe bar, low < active higher-timeframe swing low AND close >= active higher-timeframe swing low AND NOT (previous chart-timeframe bar low < that level AND previous close >= that level).
Short: On a higher-timeframe bar, close < active swing low AND previous higher-timeframe close >= active swing low AND (down-close body >= ATR × displacement factor OR displacement filter disabled) AND (volume > volume SMA × volume factor OR volume filter disabled) OR, on a chart-timeframe bar, high > active higher-timeframe swing high AND close <= active higher-timeframe swing high AND NOT (previous chart-timeframe bar high > that level AND previous close <= that level).
