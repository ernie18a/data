Calculation: Active swing high and low = On each higher timeframe, use the last completed bar. A swing high or low is a pivot confirmed by equal numbers of bars on both sides. Replace the corresponding active level when a new pivot is confirmed; clear it after a filtered close crossover above the active high or crossunder below the active low, respectively. Compare the previous close with the current active level. Calculate each higher timeframe independently.
Calculation: Filtered bullish break = Higher-timeframe close > active swing high AND previous higher-timeframe close <= active swing high AND (displacement filter disabled OR ATR unavailable OR close - open >= ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by a threshold).
Calculation: Filtered bearish break = Higher-timeframe close < active swing low AND previous higher-timeframe close >= active swing low AND (displacement filter disabled OR ATR unavailable OR open - close >= ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA unavailable OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by a threshold).
Calculation: Visible break = A filtered break on the last completed higher-timeframe bar that was not present on the previous chart bar AND, for each shorter higher timeframe, its break level differs by more than a price tolerance from every available bullish and bearish break level on longer higher timeframes. A break level is available only on the higher-timeframe bar where its filtered break occurs.
Long: Visible bullish break on any higher timeframe.
Short: Visible bearish break on any higher timeframe.
Long: Chart low < active swing low of any higher timeframe AND chart close >= that level AND (previous chart low >= that same current level OR previous chart close < that same current level).
Short: Chart high > active swing high of any higher timeframe AND chart close <= that level AND (previous chart high <= that same current level OR previous chart close > that same current level).
Long: Chart volume > chart volume SMA multiplied by a threshold AND chart close >= chart open.
Short: Chart volume > chart volume SMA multiplied by a threshold AND chart close < chart open.
