Calculation: Comparison = a selected ATR, moving average, RSI, CCI, Bollinger Band width, ADX, MFI, or momentum compared with a selected indicator from the same set or a constant, using >, <, >=, <=, =, !=, crosses above, or crosses below; each moving average independently uses EMA, SMA, RMA, or WMA and an independent length, but both use the first moving average's source.
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band, calculated using the middle Bollinger Band of the selected source as the input series.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = while long, the maximum of the previous long trailing stop and current low minus the selected absolute distance or current low multiplied by one minus the selected percentage; reset to zero when not long.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = while short, the minimum of the previous short trailing stop and current high plus the selected absolute distance or current high multiplied by one plus the selected percentage; reset to 99999 when not short.
Long: Date filter disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive selected date range, AND all or any active long-designated comparisons are true according to the selected connector; with no active comparisons, AND is true and OR is false.
Short: Bidirectional mode is enabled AND (date filter disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive selected date range) AND all or any active short-designated comparisons are true according to the selected connector; with no active comparisons, AND is true and OR is false.
Exit: long, (date filter disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive selected date range) AND all or any active short-designated comparisons are true according to the selected connector; with no active comparisons, AND is true and OR is false.
Exit: short, (date filter disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive selected date range) AND all or any active long-designated comparisons are true according to the selected connector; with no active comparisons, AND is true and OR is false.
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND low reaches the active long trailing stop OR trailing stop disabled AND fixed stop enabled AND low reaches entry price minus the selected absolute stop distance or entry price multiplied by one minus the selected stop percentage OR take profit enabled AND high reaches entry price plus the selected absolute profit distance or entry price multiplied by one plus the selected profit percentage.
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND high reaches the active short trailing stop OR trailing stop disabled AND fixed stop enabled AND high reaches entry price plus the selected absolute stop distance or entry price multiplied by one plus the selected stop percentage OR take profit enabled AND low reaches entry price minus the selected absolute profit distance or entry price multiplied by one minus the selected profit percentage.
