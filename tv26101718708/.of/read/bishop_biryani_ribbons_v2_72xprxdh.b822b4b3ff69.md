Calculation: Moving averages = selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, ALMA, T3 or VWMA of a selectable price source; use the same type and source for all chart-timeframe averages, with independently selectable lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish trend = short-lookback moving average > medium-lookback moving average AND medium-lookback moving average > long-lookback moving average AND close > long-lookback moving average AND (choppiness filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold OR (early-trend option enabled AND ADX - ADX a configurable number of bars ago >= configurable increase)); in the two-average configuration omit the medium-lookback comparison.
Calculation: Bearish trend = short-lookback moving average < medium-lookback moving average AND medium-lookback moving average < long-lookback moving average AND close < long-lookback moving average AND (choppiness filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold OR (early-trend option enabled AND ADX - ADX a configurable number of bars ago >= configurable increase)); in the two-average configuration omit the medium-lookback comparison.
Calculation: Up pullback candidate = low <= selected touch moving average + configurable ATR multiple AND close > selected hold moving average AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish trend OR selected cloud's first moving average > its second moving average, according to the selected filter) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume < SMA of volume).
Calculation: Down pullback candidate = high >= selected touch moving average - configurable ATR multiple AND close < selected hold moving average AND (trend filter disabled OR bearish trend OR selected cloud's first moving average <= its second moving average, according to the selected filter) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume < SMA of volume).
Long: Bullish trend on the chart timeframe OR bullish trend calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Short: Bearish trend on the chart timeframe OR bearish trend calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Selected moving average > its value a configurable number of bars ago AND ATR > 0.
Short: Selected moving average < its value a configurable number of bars ago AND ATR > 0.
Long: Selected cloud's first moving average > its second moving average.
Short: Selected cloud's first moving average <= its second moving average.
Long: Close > selected reference moving average AND ATR > 0.
Short: Close < selected reference moving average AND ATR > 0.
Long: Selected cloud's first moving average crosses above its second moving average.
Short: Selected cloud's first moving average crosses below its second moving average.
Long: Up pullback candidate is true AND previous bar's up pullback candidate is false AND bars since the previous up pullback are at least the configurable minimum AND (first-pullback filter disabled OR no up pullback has occurred since the selected up-trend filter last became true; when the trend filter is disabled, reset on the start of a bullish trend).
Short: Down pullback candidate is true AND previous bar's down pullback candidate is false AND bars since the previous down pullback are at least the configurable minimum AND (first-pullback filter disabled OR no down pullback has occurred since the selected down-trend filter last became true; when the trend filter is disabled, reset on the start of a bearish trend).
