Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low of chart-timeframe bars during the session’s initial opening-range interval, finalized when that interval ends
Calculation: Lower-timeframe VWAP = VWAP of lower-timeframe bars, using the current bar, which may still be forming
Calculation: Lower-timeframe RSI = RSI of lower-timeframe closes, using the current bar, which may still be forming
Long: Opening range is finalized AND close crosses above opening-range high OR close crosses above lower-timeframe VWAP AND lower-timeframe RSI > 50 OR lower-timeframe RSI crosses above 50 AND close > lower-timeframe VWAP
Short: Opening range is finalized AND close crosses below opening-range low OR close crosses below lower-timeframe VWAP AND lower-timeframe RSI < 50
