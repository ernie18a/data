Calculation: Zones = On the first chart bar of each new pivot period, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar's O, H, L, C and ATR. Set width w to a chosen fraction of that chart bar's close, a multiple of that ATR, or a fixed distance; hold w and all levels until the next period. Set threshold d to a fraction of w. For each level x, its bottom B=x-w and top T=x+w. In descending named order index the levels R4, R3, R2, R1, P, S1, S2, S3, S4 as 0 through 8. Choose one level formula: Classic: P=(H+L+C)/3, R1=2P-L, S1=2P-H, R2=P+H-L, S2=P-H+L, R3=H+2(P-L), S3=L-2(H-P), R4=H+3(P-L), S4=L-3(H-P). Fibonacci: P=(H+L+C)/3 and Rn= P+kn(H-L), Sn=P-kn(H-L), with increasing conventional Fibonacci multipliers kn. Extended Woodie: P=(H+L+2C)/4, R1=2P-L, S1=2P-H, R2=P+H-L, S2=P-H+L, R3=R1+H-L, S3=S1-H+L, R4=R2+H-L, S4=S2-H+L. Custom Camarilla: P=(H+L+C)/3 and Rn=C+kn(H-L), Sn=C-kn(H-L), with increasing configurable multipliers kn. DM: X=H+2L+C if C<O, 2H+L+C if C>O, otherwise H+L+2C; P=X/4, R1=X/2-L, S1=X/2-H, R2=X-S1, S2=X-R1, R3=P+H-L, S3=P-H+L, R4=R1+H-L, S4=S1-H+L.
Calculation: Gap and directional state = Initially gap g is the gap between R1 and P. On a new pivot period clear both directional states and initialize g from the current close: g=0 if close>T0; otherwise g=i for the first i from 1 through 8 with close>Ti AND close<B(i-1); otherwise g=9 if close>B8, else g=5. Then process the same bar using previous close p and current close c. For 1<=g<=8 apply the first matching rule: if B(g-1)-d<=p<=T(g-1)+d AND c<=T(g-1)+d, set short state to whether zone g-1 is enabled and clear long state; else if Bg-d<=p<=Tg+d AND c>=Bg-d, set long state to whether zone g is enabled and clear short state; else if p>T(g-1)+d AND c>B(g-1)-d, set long state to whether zone g-1 is enabled, clear short state, then recompute g from p; else if p<Bg-d AND c<Tg+d, set short state and clear long state even if zone g is disabled, then recompute g from p. Otherwise retain the previous states. For g=0 or 9 clear both states and recompute g from p. To recompute g from a price q: return 0 if q>T0+d, 9 if q<B8-d, otherwise the first i from 1 through 8 with q<=B(i-1)-d AND q>=Ti+d, or 4 if none. When a rule sets a long state, its stop-zone reference is the relevant zone bottom; when it sets a short state, its reference is the relevant zone top. Retain that reference until another rule changes it.
Calculation: Near zone = For either zone adjacent to the updated gap, or the outermost zone when g is 0 or 9, current open is between its bottom and top inclusive OR current close is below its bottom AND current open is above its top.
Calculation: Body range filter = Absolute current close minus open is between configurable minimum and maximum inclusive; each bound is either a fraction of current close, a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR, or a fixed price distance.
Calculation: Bullish engulf = Current close>open AND at least one of the preceding two bars has close<open AND body range filter holds AND either body-only mode has current open<=previous close+allowed price gap AND current close>previous open if the previous bar is bearish, otherwise current close>previous high; OR full-range mode has current low<previous low AND current high>previous high.
Calculation: Bearish engulf = Current close<open AND at least one of the preceding two bars has close>open AND body range filter holds AND either body-only mode has current open>=previous close-allowed price gap AND current close<previous open if the previous bar is bullish, otherwise current close<previous low; OR full-range mode has current low<previous low AND current high>previous high.
Calculation: Sweep and reversal = The previous bar has nonzero range, body/range at most a maximum, longer wick/range at least a minimum, and longer wick/body at least a minimum. For bullish reversal its lower wick is longer than its upper wick, its low is below the lowest low of a lookback ending two bars ago, and current close>open AND current close>previous high. For bearish reversal its upper wick is at least as long as its lower wick, its high is above the highest high of a lookback ending two bars ago, and current close<open AND current close<previous low. The current bar also passes the body range filter.
Calculation: Trade counter = Count initial entries while flat, excluding additional entries; reset on the first bar of each trading day and, when enabled on sufficiently short chart timeframes, on bars whose intervals contain each configured session start shifted earlier by one chart-bar duration. A no-trade window, when enabled on sufficiently short chart timeframes, includes chart bar opening times between the configured New York start and end times, each shifted earlier by one chart-bar duration, inclusive.
Calculation: Initial stop = At each entry signal use the signal close as reference and freeze the selected stop: long low minus buffer and short high plus buffer; or the lowest low minus buffer and highest high plus buffer over their respective lookbacks including the signal bar; or the directional state's stop-zone reference minus buffer for long and plus buffer for short; or signal close minus/plus a fixed distance; or signal close minus/plus a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR. For each entry separately, risk is signal close minus long stop or short stop minus signal close; its main target is signal close plus or minus risk times its selected reward ratio, and its partial target uses a separate reward ratio.
Long: Bullish directional state = true.
Short: Bearish directional state = true.
Long: Current-timeframe EMA confirmation is enabled AND close>the configured-source chart-timeframe EMA at its configured bar offset.
Short: Current-timeframe EMA confirmation is enabled AND close<=the configured-source chart-timeframe EMA at its configured bar offset.
Long: Higher-timeframe EMA confirmation is enabled AND close>the configured-source EMA on the higher timeframe at its configured bar offset, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the still-forming bar in realtime.
Short: Higher-timeframe EMA confirmation is enabled AND close<=the configured-source EMA on the higher timeframe at its configured bar offset, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the still-forming bar in realtime.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND (bullish engulf OR bullish sweep and reversal) AND bullish directional state=true AND near zone AND (current-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close>configured-source chart-timeframe EMA at its configured offset OR bullish sweep and reversal) AND trade counter<maximum AND (RSI filter disabled OR close RSI<=upper threshold) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR normalized Bollinger bandwidth>=threshold) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close>configured-source higher-timeframe EMA at its configured offset, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the still-forming bar in realtime) AND no-trade window inactive.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND (bearish engulf OR bearish sweep and reversal) AND bearish directional state=true AND near zone AND (current-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close<configured-source chart-timeframe EMA at its configured offset OR bearish sweep and reversal) AND trade counter<maximum AND (RSI filter disabled OR close RSI>=lower threshold) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR normalized Bollinger bandwidth>=threshold) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close<configured-source higher-timeframe EMA at its configured offset, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the still-forming bar in realtime) AND no-trade window inactive.
Long: Long signal conditions hold AND recorded entries<maximum positions AND bars since last entry>minimum spacing AND initial stop differs from signal close AND (position is flat OR additional entry enabled AND position is long).
Short: Short signal conditions hold AND recorded entries<maximum positions AND bars since last entry>minimum spacing AND initial stop differs from signal close AND (position is flat OR additional entry enabled AND position is short).
Exit: long, low<=the entry's initial stop OR high>=the entry's main target.
Exit: short, high>=the entry's initial stop OR low<=the entry's main target.
Exit: long, partial exit enabled AND main reward ratio>partial reward ratio AND high>=the entry's partial target; exit the configured fraction.
Exit: short, partial exit enabled AND main reward ratio>partial reward ratio AND low<=the entry's partial target; exit the configured fraction.
Exit: long, breakeven enabled AND main reward ratio>partial reward ratio AND a bar since entry reached high>=partial target AND a subsequent bar has low<=the entry signal close; cancel the original stop and partial order after the trigger and retain the main target.
Exit: short, breakeven enabled AND main reward ratio>partial reward ratio AND a bar since entry reached low<=partial target AND a subsequent bar has high>=the entry signal close; cancel the original stop and partial order after the trigger and retain the main target.
Exit: long OR short, scheduled closing enabled AND the chart bar interval contains the configured New York end time shifted earlier by one chart-bar duration plus one minute AND (daily closing enabled AND chart timeframe is at most the configured short-timeframe cutoff OR weekly closing enabled AND current New York weekday is Friday).
