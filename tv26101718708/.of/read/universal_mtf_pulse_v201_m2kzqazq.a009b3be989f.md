Calculation: ATR-normalized momentum = (chart-timeframe close − close a shorter lookback earlier) / chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Relative volume = bar volume / mean volume over a longer preceding lookback, excluding the measured bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe momentum crosses from nonpositive to positive and exceeds the flat-band threshold on a confirmed bar OR chart-timeframe momentum is at least the strong-move threshold AND relative volume is at least the elevated-activity threshold OR, on any compared source timeframe (higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe), the latest completed source bar closes above its EMA and that EMA is above its value a longer lookback earlier.
Short: Chart-timeframe momentum crosses from nonnegative to negative and exceeds the flat-band threshold in magnitude on a confirmed bar OR chart-timeframe momentum is at most the negative strong-move threshold AND relative volume is at least the elevated-activity threshold OR, on any compared source timeframe (higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe), the latest completed source bar closes below its EMA and that EMA is below its value a longer lookback earlier.
