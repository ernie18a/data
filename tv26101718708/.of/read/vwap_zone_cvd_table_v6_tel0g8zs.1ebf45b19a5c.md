Calculation: Anchored VWAP = sum of typical price × volume divided by sum of volume, reset at each selected session, week, or month; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Anchored volume-weighted standard deviation = square root of max(sum of typical price² × volume / sum of volume − anchored VWAP², 0), using the same anchor as VWAP.
Calculation: Zones = the intervals centered on anchored VWAP and on anchored VWAP ± each of two standard-deviation multiples, with the second multiple larger than the first; each interval extends above and below its center by its zone-specific ATR multiple.
Calculation: Zone buy percentage = 100 × accumulated buy volume / (accumulated buy volume + accumulated sell volume), or 50 when the total is zero. For each zone independently, on every chart bar whose close is inside the zone, inclusive, and whose volume is at least the minimum threshold, add the selected timeframe's volume to buy volume if its close is above the midpoint of its high and low, or to sell volume if below; otherwise add nothing. Use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar for a lower timeframe, or the chart bar itself when timeframes are equal. Replace unavailable selected-timeframe volume with zero. Optionally reset accumulators before adding the current bar when the VWAP anchor changes or the selected-timeframe bar starts a new day; otherwise accumulate from the beginning.
Long: Any zone's buy percentage >= the bullish threshold.
Short: Any zone's buy percentage < the bullish threshold AND that zone's buy percentage <= 100 − the bullish threshold.
