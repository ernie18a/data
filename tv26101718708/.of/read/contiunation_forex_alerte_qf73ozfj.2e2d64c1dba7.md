Calculation: Fair value gap = on the lower timeframe, a bullish gap forms when the latest completed bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap forms when its high is below the low two bars earlier. Use completed bars.
Calculation: FVG trigger = for a bullish gap, the latest completed bar’s low; for a bearish gap, the low two bars earlier.
Long: Bullish fair value gap forms OR an unwicked bullish gap’s trigger is touched (chart low <= its trigger).
Short: Bearish fair value gap forms OR an unwicked bearish gap’s trigger is touched (chart high >= its trigger).
