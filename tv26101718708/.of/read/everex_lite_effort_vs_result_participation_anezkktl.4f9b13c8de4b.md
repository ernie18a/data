Calculation: Participation = volume, or 1 when volume is unavailable
Calculation: Normalized effort = 100 when volume is unavailable; otherwise map volume divided by its moving average over a normalization lookback to 100 if >1.50, 90 if >1.20, 80 if >1.00, 70 if >0.80, 60 if >0.60, 50 if >0.40, 25 if >0.20, else 10. The average is SMA or uses the selected ROF moving-average type.
Calculation: Price strength = the mean of (200 × (close − low) / max(high − low, ε) − 100), (100 × (close − open) / max(high − low, ε)), 200 × (close − lowest low over the current and previous bar) / max(highest high − lowest low over those bars, ε) − 100, 100 × (close − open) / its normalization-lookback moving average of absolute values × its sign, 100 × (close − previous close) / max(highest high − lowest low over the current and previous bar, ε), and 100 × (close − previous close) / its normalization-lookback moving average of absolute values × its sign. For the two moving averages of absolute values, use SMA or the selected ROF moving-average type; ε is a small positive constant.
Calculation: Bar flow = price strength × normalized effort / 100
Calculation: Smoothed Rate of Flow = WMA over a smoothing lookback of 2 × (100 − 100 / (1 + positive-flow average / max(negative-flow average, ε))) − 100. Positive-flow average and negative-flow average are moving averages over the same flow lookback and use the selected ROF moving-average type; positive flow is max(bar flow, 0), and negative flow is −min(bar flow, 0). ε is a small positive constant.
Long: Smoothed Rate of Flow >= 0
Short: Smoothed Rate of Flow < 0
