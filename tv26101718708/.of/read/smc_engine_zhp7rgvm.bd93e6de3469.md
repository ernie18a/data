Calculation: Open-based session range high/low = highest/lowest chart-bar open during the selected session
Calculation: Spread anomaly = chart-bar high−low exceeds the longer-lookback average chart-bar high−low multiplied by a multiplier
Calculation: Signed volume pressure = sum lower-timeframe bar volume, positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and zero when close = open; use lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, evaluated when they are complete
Long: (Spread anomaly AND high > open-based session range high AND close <= open-based session range high AND signed volume pressure < 0) OR (Spread anomaly AND low < open-based session range low AND close >= open-based session range low AND signed volume pressure > 0) OR (Spread anomaly AND signed volume pressure > 0 AND close > open) OR (NOT Spread anomaly AND close > open-based session range high AND signed volume pressure > 0)
