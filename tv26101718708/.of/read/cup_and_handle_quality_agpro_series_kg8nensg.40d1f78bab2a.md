Calculation: Pivot stream = Confirm high and low pivots using equal numbers of preceding and following chart bars. Process a high before a low if both confirm on the same bar. Keep at most the latest 50 alternating pivots; replace the latest pivot with a newly confirmed pivot of the same kind only when the new high is at least as high or the new low is at least as low.
Calculation: Candidate = Scan the pivot stream from newest to oldest, testing at each scan position first a high-low-high-low sequence and then, if absent, a high-low-high sequence. For the latter, use the lowest low from the right rim through the current bar, limited to a maximum lookback; on ties use the earliest bar. Select the first sequence passing all structural comparisons. Let L, B, R and H be the left rim high, cup bottom low, right rim high and handle low; let D = (L + R) / 2 - B, P = max(L, R), C = bars from L to R, A = bars from L to B, Z = bars from B to R, T = bars from R to H, V = abs(L - R) / D and Q = (P - H) / D. The pivot bars must be strictly ordered L, B, R, H; C must be between its minimum and maximum; D must exceed ATR times its depth threshold; V must be at most its threshold; T must be between its minimum and max(its minimum, round(C times a handle-duration multiplier)); Q must be between its minimum and maximum; and H must exceed B plus D times a bottom-clearance fraction.
Calculation: Scores = Clamp(x) limits x to [0, 1]. Rim score = 100 × (1 - clamp(V / maximum rim variance)); time score = 100 × clamp(min(A, Z) / max(A, Z)); handle depth score = 100 × (1 - clamp(abs(Q - handle depth midpoint) / max(handle depth half-width, minimum width))); handle time score = 100 × (1 - clamp(T / max(C × handle-duration multiplier, 1))); symmetry = 0.50 × rim score + 0.50 × time score; handle quality = 0.66 × handle depth score + 0.34 × handle time score; setup score = 0.55 × symmetry + 0.45 × handle quality. Breakout distance score = 100 × clamp((close - P) / max(ATR, minimum price increment)); volume score = 100 × clamp((volume / SMA(volume) - 0.80) / 0.90), using a ratio of 1 when the volume average is not positive; body score = 100 × clamp(1.25 × abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)); close score = 100 × clamp((close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)); breakout quality = 0.35 × breakout distance score + 0.25 × volume score + 0.20 × body score + 0.20 × close score; pattern score = 0.33 × symmetry + 0.27 × handle quality + 0.40 × breakout quality.
Long: A candidate exists AND setup score >= minimum setup score.
Long: A candidate exists AND bars since its handle low <= max(pivot span × freshness multiplier, minimum handle bars + pivot span + freshness allowance) AND close > P AND breakout quality >= minimum breakout quality AND pattern score >= minimum pattern score AND the candidate's four pivot-bar indices differ from those of the most recently confirmed breakout.
