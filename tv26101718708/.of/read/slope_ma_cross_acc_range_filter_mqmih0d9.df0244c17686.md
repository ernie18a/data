Calculation: MA = selected ALMA, EMA, VWMA, or Hull MA of close
Calculation: Slope = MA minus MA at the slope lookback
Calculation: Acceleration = slope minus previous bar's slope
Calculation: Slope angle = degrees(atan(100 × slope / MA at the slope lookback × sensitivity multiplier))
Calculation: Long stop = entry price × (1 − stop percentage), or, if shrinking is enabled, the greater of that value and the highest qualifying shrinking-stop candidate since entry; a candidate is max((high + low + close) / 3 × (1 − stop percentage × tightening multiplier), entry price × (1 − stop percentage)) on a bar where (high − entry price) / entry price >= minimum profit percentage AND, if the momentum trigger is enabled, slope > 0 AND slope < previous slope
Calculation: Short stop = entry price × (1 + stop percentage), or, if shrinking is enabled, the lesser of that value and the lowest qualifying shrinking-stop candidate since entry; a candidate is min((high + low + close) / 3 × (1 + stop percentage × tightening multiplier), entry price × (1 + stop percentage)) on a bar where (entry price − low) / entry price >= minimum profit percentage AND, if the momentum trigger is enabled, slope < 0 AND slope > previous slope
Calculation: Trailing distance = max(1, round(entry price × trailing percentage / minimum price increment)) × minimum price increment
Long: Position is flat AND (enabled slope-entry AND slope crosses above zero OR enabled price-entry AND close crosses above MA OR enabled acceleration-entry AND acceleration crosses above zero OR enabled re-entry AND slope > 0 AND close > MA AND close crosses above previous bar's high) AND (range filter disabled OR slope angle >= minimum angle)
Short: Position is flat AND long entry condition is false AND (enabled slope-entry AND slope crosses below zero OR enabled price-entry AND close crosses below MA OR enabled acceleration-entry AND acceleration crosses below zero OR enabled re-entry AND slope < 0 AND close < MA AND close crosses below previous bar's low) AND (range filter disabled OR slope angle <= negative minimum angle)
Exit: Long, slope crosses below zero OR close crosses below MA OR enabled acceleration-exit AND acceleration crosses below zero
Exit: Short, slope crosses above zero OR close crosses above MA OR enabled acceleration-exit AND acceleration crosses above zero
Exit: Long, enabled stop-loss AND low <= long stop active for the bar OR enabled take-profit AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR enabled trailing stop AND high reaches entry price × (1 + trailing activation percentage) AND price subsequently falls by the trailing distance from its highest price since activation
Exit: Short, enabled stop-loss AND high >= short stop active for the bar OR enabled take-profit AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR enabled trailing stop AND low reaches entry price × (1 − trailing activation percentage) AND price subsequently rises by the trailing distance from its lowest price since activation
