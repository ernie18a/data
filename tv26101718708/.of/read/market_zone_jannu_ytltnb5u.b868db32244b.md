Calculation: Impulse = scan lookbacks from one bar through the maximum impulse lookback; select the first lookback where the absolute change from the earlier close to the current close exceeds an impulse multiplier times ATR at the earlier bar AND the count of close-to-close changes in the direction of the move is at least the rounded product of the lookback and a directional-consistency fraction. Unchanged closes count as downward changes.
Calculation: Qualified base = within the base lookback immediately preceding the selected impulse's starting bar, at least one bar has high minus low below a tightness multiplier times ATR at that bar.
Calculation: POI cooldown = bars elapsed since the most recent long or short POI decision; before the first such decision, cooldown is inactive.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND current low minus high two bars ago >= a gap multiplier times current ATR AND previous bar's range >= a candle-range multiplier times current ATR AND previous bar's session VWAP > high two bars ago AND previous bar's volume > a volume multiplier times current EMA of volume AND previous bar's range > 0 AND previous bar's absolute candle body / range > a body-fraction threshold.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago minus current high >= a gap multiplier times current ATR AND previous bar's range >= a candle-range multiplier times current ATR AND previous bar's session VWAP < low two bars ago AND previous bar's volume > a volume multiplier times current EMA of volume AND previous bar's range > 0 AND previous bar's absolute candle body / range > a body-fraction threshold.
Long: Selected impulse's current close > its starting close AND qualified base exists AND POI cooldown is inactive OR POI cooldown >= cooldown length.
Short: Selected impulse's current close < its starting close AND qualified base exists AND POI cooldown is inactive OR POI cooldown >= cooldown length.
