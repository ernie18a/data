Calculation: FVG candle body proportion = absolute(close − open) / (high − low)
Calculation: FVG volume ratio = middle-candle volume / current EMA of volume, using a longer lookback than the chart’s ATR period
Calculation: Directional impulse = for a lookback of 1 through the configured maximum, current close minus the close that many bars earlier exceeds ATR at that earlier bar times the impulse multiple in the same direction, and at least the rounded 60% of bars in the move close directionally; use the shortest qualifying lookback
Calculation: POI base candle = the nearest candle before the impulse whose high − low is below ATR on that candle times the base tightness multiple
Long: regular-session open > prior close AND open − prior close > ATR × gap threshold OR low > high two bars earlier AND middle-candle range ≥ current ATR × FVG candle threshold AND low − high two bars earlier ≥ current ATR × FVG gap threshold AND middle-candle VWAP > high two bars earlier AND FVG volume ratio > volume threshold AND FVG candle body proportion > body threshold OR bullish directional impulse AND a POI base candle exists AND no POI was detected within the cooldown
Short: regular-session open < prior close AND prior close − open > ATR × gap threshold OR high < low two bars earlier AND middle-candle range ≥ current ATR × FVG candle threshold AND low two bars earlier − high ≥ current ATR × FVG gap threshold AND middle-candle VWAP < low two bars earlier AND FVG volume ratio > volume threshold AND FVG candle body proportion > body threshold OR bearish directional impulse AND a POI base candle exists AND no POI was detected within the cooldown
