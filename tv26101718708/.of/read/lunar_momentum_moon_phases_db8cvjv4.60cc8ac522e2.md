Calculation: Lunar momentum = sin(2π × lunar phase fraction), where lunar phase fraction is the positive remainder of (bar-opening datetime in days − reference new-moon datetime in days) divided by the synodic month length, divided by the synodic month length.
Calculation: Moon phase bucket = floor((lunar phase fraction + half a bucket width) × number of phase buckets) modulo the number of phase buckets; a phase begins when its bucket differs from the preceding bar’s bucket. The buckets, in order, are new moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon, waning gibbous, last quarter, and waning crescent.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish state = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend EMA of close AND higher-timeframe fast EMA of close > higher-timeframe slow EMA of close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on its closing chart bar, use that newly completed bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish state = higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe trend EMA of close AND higher-timeframe fast EMA of close < higher-timeframe slow EMA of close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on its closing chart bar, use that newly completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish momentum score = sum of the respective weights for each true condition: close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its previous value; close > slow EMA; RSI ≥ bullish RSI threshold; RSI > bullish RSI threshold + RSI offset; MACD histogram > 0 AND histogram ≥ its previous value; close > session VWAP of typical price; relative volume ≥ its threshold AND close > open AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ close-location threshold AND abs(close − open) ≥ ATR × body-size multiplier; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state. Clamp the sum to the score bounds. Relative volume is volume divided by max(SMA of volume, volume floor).
Calculation: Bearish momentum score = sum of the respective weights for each true condition: close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < its previous value; close < slow EMA; RSI ≤ bearish RSI threshold; RSI < bearish RSI threshold − RSI offset; MACD histogram < 0 AND histogram ≤ its previous value; close < session VWAP of typical price; relative volume ≥ its threshold AND close < open AND (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ close-location threshold AND abs(close − open) ≥ ATR × body-size multiplier; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish state. Clamp the sum to the score bounds. The corresponding bullish and bearish conditions use the same weights and lookbacks.
Calculation: Long confluence = bullish momentum score × momentum weight + max(lunar momentum, 0) × lunar-score scale × lunar weight + phase bonus if a new moon or first quarter begins AND lunar momentum > 0; otherwise omit the bonus. Clamp to the score bounds.
Calculation: Short confluence = bearish momentum score × momentum weight + max(−lunar momentum, 0) × lunar-score scale × lunar weight + phase bonus if a full moon or last quarter begins AND lunar momentum < 0; otherwise omit the bonus. Clamp to the score bounds.
Calculation: Long signal = long direction enabled AND bars since the previous long signal > cooldown, or no previous long signal exists; AND lunar momentum > lunar bias threshold AND bullish momentum score ≥ minimum momentum score AND long confluence ≥ minimum confluence score AND (close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND close > open OR lowest low over the current pullback-lookback bars ≤ fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND close > previous close) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state). Evaluate on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Short signal = short direction enabled AND bars since the previous short signal > cooldown, or no previous short signal exists; AND lunar momentum < −lunar bias threshold AND bearish momentum score ≥ minimum momentum score AND short confluence ≥ minimum confluence score AND (close < lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND close < open OR highest high over the current pullback-lookback bars ≥ fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND close < previous close) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish state). Evaluate on completed chart bars.
Calculation: Active trade levels = on each long signal, replace the active side with long, set entry to close, stop to the lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR × stop-buffer multiplier, and target to entry + max(entry − stop, minimum risk) × target risk multiplier. On each short signal, replace the active side with short, set entry to close, stop to the highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR × stop-buffer multiplier, and target to entry − max(stop − entry, minimum risk) × target risk multiplier. Minimum risk is a multiple of the minimum price increment. If both signals occur, process long then short. Evaluate exits after replacing these levels, including on the signal bar, and clear the active side and levels after an exit.
Long: Long signal.
Short: Short signal.
Long: Fast EMA ≥ slow EMA.
Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA.
Long: Close ≥ session VWAP of typical price.
Short: Close < session VWAP of typical price.
Long: Lunar momentum > lunar bias threshold.
Short: Lunar momentum < −lunar bias threshold.
Long: Bullish momentum score ≥ minimum momentum score.
Short: Bearish momentum score ≥ minimum momentum score.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish state.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish state.
Long: A new moon begins.
Short: A full moon begins.
Long: A displayed moon phase begins AND lunar momentum ≥ 0.
Short: A displayed moon phase begins AND lunar momentum < 0.
Exit: long, active side = long AND (low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ active target OR short signal).
Exit: short, active side = short AND (high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ active target OR long signal).
