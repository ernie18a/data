Calculation: HTF structure = computed separately on each of two timeframes that are both higher than the chart timeframe, using only the last completed higher-timeframe bar (never the forming bar), with the same swing lookback N (in higher-timeframe bars) for both. Swing high = a higher-timeframe bar whose high is the highest within N bars on each side (confirmed N bars later); swing low = the mirror case with lows. SwingHigh/SwingLow = the most recent confirmed, not-yet-broken swing high/low. On each completed higher-timeframe bar, after updating the swings: if SwingHigh exists and the bar close > SwingHigh then BullBreak, dir = up, and SwingHigh is cleared; else if SwingLow exists and the bar close < SwingLow then BearBreak, dir = down, and SwingLow is cleared. dir keeps the last break direction (none before the first break).
Long: on either higher timeframe, BullBreak occurs on its newly completed bar (a continuation break or a reversal break both count)
Short: on either higher timeframe, BearBreak occurs on its newly completed bar (a continuation break or a reversal break both count)
Long: dir of the first higher timeframe = up AND dir of the second higher timeframe = up, and at least one of the two dir values has just changed
Short: dir of the first higher timeframe = down AND dir of the second higher timeframe = down, and at least one of the two dir values has just changed
