Calculation: Momentum = selectable RSI of close, ROC of close, MFI of typical price, or their composite; all three use the same momentum lookback. Normalized ROC = clamp(50 + 50 × ROC / (2 × standard deviation of ROC over a separate lookback + 0.0001), 0, 100). Composite = (RSI + normalized ROC + MFI) / 3. Extreme-space momentum = normalized ROC when ROC is selected; otherwise it equals the selected momentum.
Calculation: Peak and trough = highest and lowest momentum, respectively, over the same rolling lookback, including the current bar; use the most recent bar when extremes tie. Their anchors are the high at the peak and low at the trough. Their extreme-space momentum and volume are taken from those respective bars.
Calculation: Pullback threshold = base percentage, optionally multiplied by clamp((ATR / close × 100) / (SMA of ATR / close × 100 + 0.0001), 0.6, 1.8), where the SMA has its own lookback.
Calculation: Bear strength = round(clamp((peak extreme-space momentum − 50) × 40 / 50, 0, 40) + clamp(((peak momentum − current momentum) / (peak momentum + 0.0001) × 100 − pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((peak volume / (current volume SMA + 0.0001) − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + divergence bonus). The bonus is 20 when enabled and the peak anchor is above, and peak momentum below, those of the previous bear saturation; otherwise it is 0. The volume SMA uses a separate lookback.
Calculation: Bull strength = round(clamp((50 − trough extreme-space momentum) × 40 / 50, 0, 40) + clamp(((current momentum − trough momentum) / (abs(trough momentum) + 0.0001) × 100 − pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((trough volume / (current volume SMA + 0.0001) − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + divergence bonus). The bonus is 20 when enabled and the trough anchor is below, and trough momentum above, those of the previous bull saturation; otherwise it is 0. The volume SMA uses the same lookback as in bear strength.
Calculation: Bear saturation = current momentum <= peak momentum × (1 − pullback threshold / 100) AND peak bar differs from the peak bar of the previous bear saturation AND bars since either saturation >= cooldown AND (absolute-extreme filter disabled OR peak extreme-space momentum >= upper extreme threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR peak volume > current volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND peak age >= confirmation bars AND bear strength >= minimum strength. Evaluate on completed bars; calculate both saturation conditions before updating either side's previous saturation and the shared cooldown.
Calculation: Bull saturation = current momentum >= trough momentum + abs(trough momentum) × pullback threshold / 100 AND trough bar differs from the trough bar of the previous bull saturation AND bars since either saturation >= cooldown AND (absolute-extreme filter disabled OR trough extreme-space momentum <= lower extreme threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR trough volume > current volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND trough age >= confirmation bars AND bull strength >= minimum strength. Evaluate on completed bars; calculate both saturation conditions before updating either side's previous saturation and the shared cooldown.
Calculation: Zones = on each saturation create a zone centered on its peak or trough anchor, with upper and lower boundaries equal to anchor ± ATR at creation × width multiplier / 2. Process bear creation before bull creation; before each creation, if the active-zone count >= the maximum, remove the oldest active zone. On each completed bar, process active zones in creation order, including newly created zones: a touch occurs when low <= upper boundary AND high >= lower boundary AND more than 2 bars have elapsed since the last touch of any zone; update that shared touch time immediately. After checking each zone's touch, invalidate a bull zone when close < lower boundary × (1 − invalidation percentage / 100), or a bear zone when close > upper boundary × (1 + invalidation percentage / 100). Invalid zones cannot be touched again.
Long: Bull saturation
Short: Bear saturation
Long: A bull zone touch occurs
Short: A bear zone touch occurs
Long: Extreme-space momentum < lower extreme threshold
Short: Extreme-space momentum > upper extreme threshold
Long: Active-zone count > 0 AND the peak bar of the previous bear saturation <= the trough bar of the previous bull saturation
Short: Active-zone count > 0 AND the peak bar of the previous bear saturation > the trough bar of the previous bull saturation
