Calculation: Impulse histogram = displacement minus its shorter-lookback SMA; displacement is zero-lag EMA of typical price minus the smoothed moving average of high when the former is greater, minus the smoothed moving average of low when the former is lower, and zero otherwise. The high, low, and zero-lag averages use the same lookback. The zero-lag EMA is twice the EMA of typical price minus the EMA of that EMA, using the same lookback. Each smoothed moving average is initialized with an SMA and subsequently equals (previous value × (lookback − 1) + current source) / lookback.
Long: (primary EMA of close > its previous value in single-EMA mode OR primary EMA of close > secondary EMA of close in two-EMA mode) AND (flat detection is disabled OR impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0).
Short: (primary EMA of close < its previous value in single-EMA mode OR primary EMA of close < secondary EMA of close in two-EMA mode) AND (flat detection is disabled OR impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0).
Long: On the chart timeframe, the long condition holds AND (in single-EMA mode the previous primary EMA <= its value before that OR in two-EMA mode the previous primary EMA <= the previous secondary EMA).
Short: On the chart timeframe, the short condition holds AND (in single-EMA mode the previous primary EMA >= its value before that OR in two-EMA mode the previous primary EMA >= the previous secondary EMA).
