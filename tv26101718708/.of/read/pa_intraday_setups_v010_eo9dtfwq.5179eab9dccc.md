Calculation: All decisions use completed chart bars. A bar is active if it belongs to the configured session; if session filtering is disabled, every bar is active and sessions reset daily. Previous-session high, low, and close use the last completed session; session open is from the current session. A daily session is a higher timeframe than the chart and uses its last completed bar for previous-session values.
Calculation: Bar range = max(high − low, a positive minimum price increment); body fraction = abs(close − open) / bar range; upper tail = high − max(open, close); lower tail = min(open, close) − low. A bullish bar has close > open; a bearish bar has close < open. A strong bullish bar is bullish, has body fraction at least the strong-body threshold, and upper tail / bar range at most the tail threshold; a strong bearish bar is defined symmetrically using the lower tail. A doji has body fraction at most the doji threshold.
Calculation: A bullish reversal bar is bullish, closes at or above 60% of its range measured from the low, and is strong bullish OR has lower tail at least 30% of its range OR closes above the preceding high. A bearish reversal bar is symmetric.
Calculation: Prior range high and low = highest high and lowest low over the range lookback excluding the current bar; prior range width = max(prior range high − prior range low, a positive minimum price increment). Overlap fraction = the fraction of bars in the range lookback whose high is at least the preceding low and whose low is at most the preceding high. Range efficiency = abs(close − close one range lookback ago) / max(highest high − lowest low over the range lookback including the current bar, a positive minimum price increment).
Calculation: Uptrend = EMA > EMA one trend lookback ago AND close − close one trend lookback ago >= ATR multiplied by the minimum trend-move threshold AND the fraction of closes above their contemporaneous EMA over the trend lookback >= the trend-side threshold AND range efficiency >= the efficiency threshold. Downtrend is symmetric. The trend and range lookbacks are independently configurable.
Calculation: Range context = prior range width <= ATR multiplied by the range-width threshold AND overlap fraction >= the range-overlap threshold AND range efficiency <= 0.45. Wide up channel = uptrend AND prior range width >= ATR multiplied by the wide-channel width threshold AND overlap fraction >= the wide-channel overlap threshold; wide down channel is symmetric. Always-long context = uptrend AND close > EMA AND range context is false; always-short context is symmetric.
Calculation: A bar touches a level within a buffer when low <= level + buffer AND high >= level − buffer. Key-level touch = the current bar touches previous-session high, low, or close, current-session open, or prior range high or low within the key ATR buffer, OR touches the current EMA within the EMA ATR buffer.
Calculation: A confirmed pivot low is a low surrounded on both sides by the configured number of bars with no lower low; a confirmed pivot high is symmetric. The pivot is available only after the right-hand bars have completed. For the three latest confirmed pivot lows, ordered newest to oldest, a bottom structure exists on a new pivot confirmation if the newest two differ by at most a configured ATR fraction, OR all three descend from oldest to newest, OR the middle is below both others and the newest and oldest differ by at most a separate configured ATR fraction. Top structure is symmetric. A descending three-pivot sequence and an ascending three-pivot sequence are the respective wedge structures. Record the pivot bar of the latest confirmed bottom or top structure; clear the corresponding record when its key-level reversal decision occurs.
Calculation: A two-leg down pullback = low < low two bars ago AND preceding high > high two bars ago; a two-leg up pullback is symmetric. Impulse midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the impulse lookback including the current bar.
Calculation: An initial upside breakout = active bar AND close > prior range high AND open <= prior range high AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold AND prior range width >= ATR multiplied by the breakout-width threshold. An initial downside breakout is symmetric. For each direction, record the latest initial breakout bar and its prior range extreme, replacing any earlier record. A break-fail-break becomes eligible when a later close crosses back to the other side of that recorded level; it expires after the breakout window and is consumed on its first qualifying re-break.
Calculation: A confirmed upside breakout = initial upside breakout AND strong bullish bar AND not doji; downside is symmetric. Track only the latest confirmed breakout in either direction and its recorded range extreme. Its immediately following bar must be strong in the breakout direction, not a doji, and close farther in that direction than the breakout bar; otherwise discard it. Expire it after the trapped-pullback window. Its first qualifying retest consumes the retest opportunity, while the breakout remains available for exit decisions until replaced or expired.
Calculation: Always-in state resets at each new session, changes to long when always-long context occurs, changes to short when always-short context occurs, and resets to neutral when neither always-in context holds and range context holds. On each state change, reset both EMA-pullback-used flags. Set the relevant flag after its first EMA-pullback decision.
Long: Always-long context AND preceding bar did not have always-long context.
Short: Always-short context AND preceding bar did not have always-short context.
Long: Active bar AND preceding bar was in an uptrend AND current bar is not in a wide up channel AND two-leg down pullback AND bullish bar AND no strong bearish bar among the current and preceding three bars.
Short: Active bar AND preceding bar was in a downtrend AND current bar is not in a wide down channel AND two-leg up pullback AND bearish bar AND no strong bullish bar among the current and preceding three bars.
Long: A recorded upside break-fail-break is eligible and unexpired AND close > its recorded level AND strong bullish bar.
Short: A recorded downside break-fail-break is eligible and unexpired AND close < its recorded level AND strong bearish bar.
Long: Active bar AND key-level touch AND ((latest bottom structure pivot is within the structure-confirmation window AND its pivot bar precedes the current bar AND (strong bullish bar OR bullish reversal bar) AND close > preceding high) OR ((preceding bar is an inside bar OR outside bar) AND bullish reversal bar AND close > preceding high) OR the trend-pullback long condition).
Short: Active bar AND key-level touch AND ((latest top structure pivot is within the structure-confirmation window AND its pivot bar precedes the current bar AND (strong bearish bar OR bearish reversal bar) AND close < preceding low) OR ((preceding bar is an inside bar OR outside bar) AND bearish reversal bar AND close < preceding low) OR the trend-pullback short condition).
Long: Active bar AND range context AND low <= prior range low + key ATR buffer AND (bullish reversal bar OR close > prior range low).
Short: Active bar AND range context AND high >= prior range high − key ATR buffer AND (bearish reversal bar OR close < prior range high).
Long: Active bar AND wide down channel on the pivot bar AND a descending three-pivot wedge is confirmed on the current bar AND key-level touch.
Short: Active bar AND wide up channel on the pivot bar AND an ascending three-pivot wedge is confirmed on the current bar AND key-level touch.
Long: Active bar AND uptrend AND range context is false AND current bar touches impulse midpoint within the key ATR buffer AND (two-leg down pullback OR current consecutive bearish-bar streak <= three bars) AND current consecutive strong-bearish-bar streak < three bars.
Short: Active bar AND downtrend AND range context is false AND current bar touches impulse midpoint within the key ATR buffer AND (two-leg up pullback OR current consecutive bullish-bar streak <= three bars) AND current consecutive strong-bullish-bar streak < three bars.
Long: An unexpired confirmed upside breakout has valid follow-through AND its retest opportunity is unused AND the current bar is later than the follow-through bar AND touches the recorded level within the key ATR buffer AND close > that level AND current bar is not a doji.
Short: An unexpired confirmed downside breakout has valid follow-through AND its retest opportunity is unused AND the current bar is later than the follow-through bar AND touches the recorded level within the key ATR buffer AND close < that level AND current bar is not a doji.
Long: Active bar AND always-long context AND a preceding most recent strong bullish bar in always-long context exists AND low <= its low AND close > its low AND the current and preceding two bars are not all bearish.
Short: Active bar AND always-short context AND a preceding most recent strong bearish bar in always-short context exists AND high >= its high AND close < its high AND the current and preceding two bars are not all bullish.
Long: Active bar AND preceding bar had always-long context AND current and preceding bars are bearish AND the bar before them is not bearish.
Short: Active bar AND preceding bar had always-short context AND current and preceding bars are bullish AND the bar before them is not bullish.
Long: Active bar AND always-in state is long AND its EMA-pullback-used flag is false AND low <= EMA + EMA ATR buffer AND close >= EMA − EMA ATR buffer AND current bar is not strong bearish.
Short: Active bar AND always-in state is short AND its EMA-pullback-used flag is false AND high >= EMA − EMA ATR buffer AND close <= EMA + EMA ATR buffer AND current bar is not strong bullish.
Long: Active bar AND always-long context AND bullish bar AND close >= low + 50% of bar range AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold.
Short: Active bar AND always-short context AND bearish bar AND close <= high − 50% of bar range AND body fraction >= the strong-body threshold.
Exit: long, preceding bar was in an uptrend AND current bar is strong bearish AND close < current impulse midpoint.
Exit: short, preceding bar was in a downtrend AND current bar is strong bullish AND close > current impulse midpoint.
Exit: long, an unexpired confirmed upside breakout has valid follow-through AND close < its recorded level AND current bar is strong bearish.
Exit: short, an unexpired confirmed downside breakout has valid follow-through AND close > its recorded level AND current bar is strong bullish.
Exit: long, preceding bar is strong bearish AND its close < its EMA AND current bar is bearish AND close < EMA AND close < preceding close.
Exit: short, preceding bar is strong bullish AND its close > its EMA AND current bar is bullish AND close > EMA AND close > preceding close.
Exit: long, uptrend AND current and preceding two bars are bearish.
Exit: short, downtrend AND current and preceding two bars are bullish.
