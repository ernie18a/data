Calculation: Band basis = SMA of close; upper and lower bands = basis plus and minus a configurable multiple of the standard deviation of close over the same lookback; band width = upper band minus lower band.
Calculation: Dynamic oversold threshold = lowest RSI over its lookback plus a buffer; dynamic overbought threshold = highest RSI over the same lookback minus a buffer.
Calculation: Bullish histogram inflection = MACD histogram > previous histogram AND previous histogram < histogram two bars ago; bearish histogram inflection = MACD histogram < previous histogram AND previous histogram > histogram two bars ago.
Calculation: Recent bullish or bearish inflection = the corresponding histogram inflection occurred within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar. Recent strong volume = volume exceeded a configurable multiple of its SMA within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar; optionally also require that bar's volume > its previous volume.
Calculation: Automatic entry mode = aggressive when band width > its SMA AND ATR > its SMA AND volume > a separate configurable multiple of its SMA; conservative when band width <= its SMA AND volume <= that multiple of its SMA; balanced otherwise. Entry mode may instead be selected directly.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND its RSI is higher than the RSI at that preceding pivot AND the pivots are at most a configurable number of bars apart. Bearish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND its RSI is lower than the RSI at that preceding pivot AND the pivots are at most a configurable number of bars apart. A pivot uses equal configurable numbers of bars before and after it and is evaluated when confirmed.
Calculation: Long continuation score = count of true conditions among low <= band basis, close > band basis, close > open, RSI > RSI midpoint, MACD histogram > previous histogram, MACD line >= signal line, and recent strong volume. Short continuation score = count of true conditions among high >= band basis, close < band basis, close < open, RSI < RSI midpoint, MACD histogram < previous histogram, MACD line <= signal line, and recent strong volume.
Calculation: Initial long invalidation = lowest low over its lookback at entry minus a configurable price buffer; initial short invalidation = highest high over its lookback at entry plus that buffer. Dynamic long invalidation starts at the lowest low over a separate lookback at entry minus the buffer; dynamic short invalidation starts at the highest high over that lookback at entry plus the buffer. If dynamic updating is enabled, on every holding bar including the entry bar, raise the long level to the maximum of its previous value and the current lookback low minus the buffer, or lower the short level to the minimum of its previous value and the current lookback high plus the buffer; otherwise retain the entry-bar level.
Calculation: Long trailing stop activates when high >= entry price + current ATR times a configurable activation multiple; from activation onward it is the maximum of high minus current ATR times a configurable stop multiple, evaluated on each bar since activation. Short trailing stop activates when low <= entry price - current ATR times the activation multiple; from activation onward it is the minimum of low plus current ATR times the stop multiple, evaluated on each bar since activation. Activation and stop updates occur before exit comparisons on each bar.
Calculation: Long break-even stop activates when high >= entry price + current ATR times a configurable multiple; short break-even stop activates when low <= entry price - current ATR times that multiple. Once activated, the stop remains at entry price; activation occurs before exit comparisons on each bar.
Calculation: Each long take-profit level = entry price + entry-bar ATR times its configurable multiple; each short take-profit level = entry price - entry-bar ATR times its configurable multiple. Track three separately configurable levels and mark every previously unhit level reached by the current bar as hit before evaluating take-profit exits.
Long: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = conservative AND ((low < lower band AND RSI <= dynamic oversold threshold AND recent bullish inflection AND recent strong volume AND close > lower band AND close > previous close) OR (bullish divergence AND pivot low < lower band at the pivot AND RSI at the pivot <= dynamic oversold threshold at the pivot AND recent bullish inflection AND recent strong volume)).
Short: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = conservative AND ((high > upper band AND RSI >= dynamic overbought threshold AND recent bearish inflection AND recent strong volume AND close < upper band AND close < previous close) OR (bearish divergence AND pivot high > upper band at the pivot AND RSI at the pivot >= dynamic overbought threshold at the pivot AND recent bearish inflection AND recent strong volume)).
Long: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = balanced AND low <= lower band + band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI <= dynamic oversold threshold + a configurable buffer AND close > open.
Short: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = balanced AND high >= upper band - band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI >= dynamic overbought threshold - a configurable buffer AND close < open.
Long: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = aggressive AND low <= lower band + band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI <= dynamic oversold threshold + a configurable buffer.
Short: No position AND optional session filter passes AND entry mode = aggressive AND high >= upper band - band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI >= dynamic overbought threshold - a configurable buffer.
Long: Continuation enabled AND low <= band basis AND close > band basis AND close > open AND RSI > RSI midpoint AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD line >= signal line AND recent strong volume.
Short: Continuation enabled AND high >= band basis AND close < band basis AND close < open AND RSI < RSI midpoint AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND MACD line <= signal line AND recent strong volume.
Long: No position AND low <= lower band + band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI <= dynamic oversold threshold + a configurable buffer AND bars since the previous such long marking >= a configurable cooldown, or no previous such marking exists.
Short: No position AND high >= upper band - band width times a configurable fraction AND RSI >= dynamic overbought threshold - a configurable buffer AND bars since the previous such short marking >= a configurable cooldown, or no previous such marking exists.
Long: No position AND continuation enabled AND long continuation score < its maximum AND long continuation score >= a configurable threshold AND bars since the previous such long continuation warning >= a configurable cooldown, or no previous such warning exists.
Short: No position AND continuation enabled AND short continuation score < its maximum AND short continuation score >= a configurable threshold AND bars since the previous such short continuation warning >= a configurable cooldown, or no previous such warning exists.
Exit: Long, momentum-reversal exit mode selected AND (bearish histogram inflection enabled AND bearish histogram inflection OR MACD-cross exit enabled AND MACD line crosses below signal line OR RSI-midpoint exit enabled AND RSI crosses below its midpoint).
Exit: Short, momentum-reversal exit mode selected AND (bullish histogram inflection enabled AND bullish histogram inflection OR MACD-cross exit enabled AND MACD line crosses above signal line OR RSI-midpoint exit enabled AND RSI crosses above its midpoint).
Exit: Long, reversal entry AND first-retrace exit mode selected AND close < open.
Exit: Short, reversal entry AND first-retrace exit mode selected AND close > open.
Exit: Long, reversal entry AND retrace-with-MACD-weakness exit mode selected AND close < open AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: Short, reversal entry AND retrace-with-MACD-weakness exit mode selected AND close > open AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
Exit: Long, continuation entry AND basis-break exit mode selected AND close < band basis AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: Short, continuation entry AND basis-break exit mode selected AND close > band basis AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
Exit: Long, continuation entry AND MACD-cross-only exit mode selected AND MACD line crosses below signal line.
Exit: Short, continuation entry AND MACD-cross-only exit mode selected AND MACD line crosses above signal line.
Exit: Long, low <= initial long invalidation OR low <= dynamic long invalidation.
Exit: Short, high >= initial short invalidation OR high >= dynamic short invalidation.
Exit: Long, trailing stop enabled AND long trailing stop activated AND low <= long trailing stop.
Exit: Short, trailing stop enabled AND short trailing stop activated AND high >= short trailing stop.
Exit: Long, break-even stop enabled AND long break-even stop activated AND low <= entry price.
Exit: Short, break-even stop enabled AND short break-even stop activated AND high >= entry price.
Exit: Long, take profit enabled AND ((third level newly reached AND second and first levels are marked hit) OR (third level newly reached AND first level is not marked hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level is marked hit)).
Exit: Short, take profit enabled AND ((third level newly reached AND second and first levels are marked hit) OR (third level newly reached AND first level is not marked hit) OR (second level newly reached AND first level is marked hit)).
Exit: Long, maximum holding time enabled AND current bar datetime >= entry bar datetime + configurable holding duration.
Exit: Short, maximum holding time enabled AND current bar datetime >= entry bar datetime + configurable holding duration.
Exit: Long, no full exit AND no partial take-profit exit AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: Short, no full exit AND no partial take-profit exit AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
