Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR AND abs(close - open) >= average candle body AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= minimum body fraction AND (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= minimum close-strength fraction.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick >= max(abs(close - open), minimum tick) × minimum rejection-wick/body ratio AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= 0.65.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND upper wick >= max(abs(close - open), minimum tick) × minimum rejection-wick/body ratio AND (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= 0.65.
Calculation: Internal swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the configured right-side bars; external swing high/low = same, using a longer lookback than internal swings and accepted only if its range from the opposing active swing is at least ATR × minimum range and its pivot is at least the minimum spacing from the prior accepted pivot of that side.
Calculation: External bullish/bearish structure sequence = the latest accepted external high is higher/not higher than the previous accepted high AND the latest accepted external low is not lower/lower than the previous accepted low.
Calculation: External bullish/bearish break = close crosses above the active external swing high plus / below the active external swing low minus ATR × break buffer; the pivot is old enough, at least the minimum fraction of the candle body lies beyond the swing, and the candle meets minimum body ATR, body fraction, and close-strength thresholds. A break is blocked in congestion when enabled; only one break per active swing is allowed.
Calculation: Congestion = the range of the configured recent bars is at most ATR × maximum range AND the required number of consecutive candle pairs overlap.
Long: Bullish displacement OR bullish rejection OR a newly accepted internal/external swing high above its previous accepted high OR a newly accepted internal/external swing low at or above its previous accepted low OR an external bullish break.
Short: Bearish displacement OR bearish rejection OR a newly accepted internal/external swing high at or below its previous accepted high OR a newly accepted internal/external swing low below its previous accepted low OR an external bearish break.
