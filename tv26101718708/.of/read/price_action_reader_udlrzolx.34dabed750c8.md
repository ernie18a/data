Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; bullish close strength = (close - low) / range; bearish close strength = (high - close) / range.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND body >= ATR times a threshold AND body >= SMA of body AND body / range >= a threshold AND bullish close strength >= a threshold. Bearish displacement uses close < open and bearish close strength with the same thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick >= body times a threshold AND bullish close strength >= a threshold. Bearish rejection uses close < open, upper wick and bearish close strength, with the same respective thresholds.
Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after their right-hand bars. Internal pivots use shorter left and right lookbacks than external pivots. An external pivot is accepted if its distance from the latest accepted opposite external pivot is at least an ATR multiple measured at the pivot, or no opposite pivot exists, AND its distance in bars from the previous accepted same-side external pivot is at least a threshold, or no previous same-side pivot exists. Process accepted highs before accepted lows on the same bar.
Calculation: Structure trend and protected levels = initially neutral. When neutral, set bullish if the latest accepted external high > its predecessor AND the latest accepted external low >= its predecessor; otherwise set bearish if the latest high <= its predecessor AND the latest low < its predecessor. On becoming bullish, protect the latest accepted low; on becoming bearish, protect the latest accepted high. Each bullish primary break sets the trend bullish and protects the latest accepted low; each bearish primary break sets the trend bearish and protects the latest accepted high, even if the break does not qualify as a displayed structure event.
Calculation: Congestion = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback <= ATR times a threshold AND the count of overlapping consecutive bar ranges among the preceding lookback bars >= a threshold; congestion detection can be disabled.
Calculation: Bullish primary break = close > latest accepted external high + ATR buffer AND previous close <= that same high + current ATR buffer AND the pivot is at least a minimum age AND it has not already had a bullish primary break AND (close - pivot high) / body >= a threshold AND close > open AND body >= ATR times a threshold AND body / range >= a threshold AND bullish close strength >= a threshold AND congestion is absent if congestion blocking is enabled. Bearish primary break reverses the inequalities around the latest accepted external low, uses (pivot low - close) / body and bearish close strength, and uses the corresponding same thresholds. A newly accepted same-side pivot resets its broken flag.
Calculation: Bullish structure event = bullish primary break AND (structure trend before the break is not bearish OR the protected high does not exist OR close > protected high + current ATR buffer OR protected-level checking is disabled). Bearish structure event = bearish primary break AND (structure trend before the break is not bullish OR the protected low does not exist OR close < protected low - current ATR buffer OR protected-level checking is disabled).
Calculation: Supply zone = a confirmed symmetric pivot high whose pivot bar range >= zone ATR at the pivot times a threshold, with top at the pivot high and bottom at the greater of the pivot bar's lower body edge and top minus zone ATR at the pivot times a width threshold; the lowest low from the pivot through its confirmation bar must be <= bottom minus zone ATR at the pivot times a departure threshold. Demand zones mirror this construction at pivot lows, with bottom at the pivot low, top at the lesser of the upper body edge and bottom plus the width allowance, and the highest high from pivot through confirmation >= top plus the departure allowance. If the optional structure filter is enabled, require a same-direction bearish event for supply or bullish event for demand within the specified preceding window including the current bar. Reject a new zone overlapping an existing same-side zone; keep at most the configured number of zones per side, removing the oldest first.
Calculation: Active zones = create eligible zones before testing breaks or retests on each bar. A supply zone breaks when close > its top + current ATR times a buffer AND bullish displacement; a demand zone breaks when close < its bottom - current ATR times the same buffer AND bearish displacement. Remove a broken zone immediately. Otherwise, after its creation bar, count each bar whose high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top as a retest, and remove the zone when its count exceeds the maximum. Nearest zone on each side minimizes distance from close to the zone interval, with ties favoring the newest zone.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = for each of two higher timeframes, compare the close and EMA of its last completed bar with the EMA of the preceding completed bar; bullish requires close > EMA AND EMA >= preceding EMA, and bearish requires close < EMA AND EMA <= preceding EMA. Count bullish and bearish timeframes separately.
