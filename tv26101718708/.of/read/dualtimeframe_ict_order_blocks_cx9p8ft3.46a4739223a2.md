Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low with the same lookback on both sides; it becomes available after the right-hand lookback has completed. Each newly confirmed swing replaces the previous swing of the same kind and can be broken only once.
Calculation: Order-block candidate = on the first close beyond the latest unbroken confirmed swing, search the bars strictly between the swing and the break, up to a maximum search lookback. For a bullish block select the lowest-low bar; for a bearish block select the highest-high bar. If the optional liquidity filter is enabled, only select bars whose low is below, or whose high is above respectively, the latest opposing confirmed swing that was already confirmed by that candidate bar. Break ties in favor of the most recent candidate.
Calculation: Order-block bounds = the candidate bar's high and low. In optional multi-candle mode, use the highest high and lowest low from the candidate back through the broken swing bar. If size restriction is enabled and that range exceeds the ATR-based limit, use the candidate plus its consecutive earlier bearish-or-doji candles for a bullish block, or bullish-or-doji candles for a bearish block, stopping at the swing bar; if that range still exceeds the limit, use the candidate alone. Reject the block if its final height exceeds the limit. ATR is measured on the break bar.
Calculation: Active order block = an accepted block retained among the most recent configured number of blocks of its original direction. A bullish block becomes a bearish breaker when low, or optionally the lower of open and close, falls below its bottom; a bearish block becomes a bullish breaker when high, or optionally the higher of open and close, rises above its top. Remove a bullish-origin breaker when a later high exceeds its top, and a bearish-origin breaker when a later low falls below its bottom. Calculate blocks on the chart timeframe or on a configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar. Optional combining merges overlapping retained blocks of the same effective direction and timeframe into their union; a combined block is broken if the current chart low is below its bullish bottom or the current chart high is above its bearish top.
Long: Close exceeds the latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND a bullish order-block candidate exists AND its final height satisfies the optional ATR limit AND, if fair-value-gap confirmation is enabled, low exceeds the high two bars earlier.
Short: Close falls below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND a bearish order-block candidate exists AND its final height satisfies the optional ATR limit AND, if fair-value-gap confirmation is enabled, high is below the low two bars earlier.
Long: A bearish-origin block is active AND high, or optionally the higher of open and close, exceeds its top.
Short: A bullish-origin block is active AND low, or optionally the lower of open and close, falls below its bottom.
Long: A primary-timeframe bullish block is active AND its height is positive AND chart-bar time is later than block creation AND chart low is at or below bottom plus 75% of block height AND chart high is at or above bottom plus 25% of block height AND no earlier chart bar after creation has met both overlap comparisons for that block.
Short: A primary-timeframe bearish block is active AND its height is positive AND chart-bar time is later than block creation AND chart low is at or below bottom plus 75% of block height AND chart high is at or above bottom plus 25% of block height AND no earlier chart bar after creation has met both overlap comparisons for that block.
