Calculation: Heikin-Ashi OHLC = HA close (open + high + low + close)/4; HA open (previous HA open + previous HA close)/2; HA high max(high, HA open, HA close); HA low min(low, HA open, HA close). For higher-timeframe bars, aggregate the backtest bars first and use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in backtests.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = HA high/low that is the maximum/minimum across equal left and right lookbacks; a pivot becomes available after the right-side bars complete. Retain the latest confirmed swing high and low.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = HA low < latest swing low AND HA close > latest swing low. Bearish sweep = HA high > latest swing high AND HA close < latest swing high.
Calculation: Bullish CHoC = after a bullish sweep, HA close exceeds the latest swing high captured at that sweep, before HA close falls below the latest swing low. Bearish CHoC = after a bearish sweep, HA close falls below the latest swing low captured at that sweep, before HA close exceeds the latest swing high. Clear the pending sweep on CHoC or invalidation.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the HA high-to-low range of the most recent bearish HA candle within the search lookback before a bullish CHoC. Bearish order-block zone = the range of the most recent bullish HA candle within the search lookback before a bearish CHoC. Keep the latest active zone of each direction.
Calculation: Bullish breaker zone = an active bullish order-block zone after close falls below its bottom; bearish breaker zone = an active bearish order-block zone after close rises above its top. Retain each breaker until close falls below a bullish breaker's bottom or rises above a bearish breaker's top. Use chart close for these tests, including for higher-timeframe zones.
Calculation: Price in bullish higher-timeframe zone = low is within an active bullish higher-timeframe order-block or breaker zone. Price in bearish higher-timeframe zone = high is within an active bearish higher-timeframe order-block or breaker zone.
Long: bullish sweep OR bullish CHoC OR bearish order-block becomes a breaker OR higher-timeframe bullish CHoC OR higher-timeframe bearish order-block becomes a breaker OR higher-timeframe direction is bullish AND price is in a bullish higher-timeframe zone
Short: bearish sweep OR bearish CHoC OR bullish order-block becomes a breaker OR higher-timeframe bearish CHoC OR higher-timeframe bullish order-block becomes a breaker OR higher-timeframe direction is bearish AND price is in a bearish higher-timeframe zone
