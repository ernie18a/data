Calculation: Period values = open, high and low of a forming bar on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; use the chart bar's close as the current price.
Calculation: Upper wick = period high − max(period open, current price).
Calculation: Lower wick = min(period open, current price) − period low.
Calculation: Elapsed fraction = elapsed time divided by the duration of the current period, clamped between zero and one.
Long: Current price >= period open.
Short: Current price < period open.
