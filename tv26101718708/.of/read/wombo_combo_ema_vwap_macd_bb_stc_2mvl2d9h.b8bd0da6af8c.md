Calculation: Price = selected OHLC price source.
Calculation: MACD line = EMA of price with shorter lookback minus EMA of price with longer lookback; MACD signal = SMA of the MACD line; MACD line and signal use the same historical offset when tested.
Calculation: STC = clamp to 0–100 the EMA of a stochastic normalization of the EMA of a stochastic normalization of the difference between a shorter-lookback EMA and a longer-lookback EMA of close. Both stochastic normalizations use the same lookback. Each normalization uses its input as high, low, and close; carry forward the last defined result when undefined, or use zero if none exists.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of close multiplied by one minus the trailing-loss fraction and the preceding long trailing stop; reset to zero when not long.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = minimum of close multiplied by one plus the trailing-loss fraction and the preceding short trailing stop; reset to a large positive sentinel when not short.
Long: price > VWAP
Short: price <= VWAP
Long: long-direction mode enabled AND (STC filter disabled OR STC crosses above its lower threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR offset price > current EMA of price) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR offset price > current VWAP) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (MACD line crosses above MACD signal AND MACD line < 0)) AND (Bollinger Band filter disabled OR offset price crosses below current lower Bollinger Band)
Short: short-direction mode enabled AND (STC filter disabled OR STC crosses below its upper threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR offset price < current EMA of price) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR offset price < current VWAP) AND (MACD filter disabled OR (MACD line crosses below MACD signal AND MACD signal > 0)) AND (Bollinger Band filter disabled OR offset price crosses above current upper Bollinger Band)
Exit: long, datetime within configured backtest window AND (low <= entry price multiplied by one minus stop-loss fraction OR high >= entry price multiplied by one plus take-profit fraction OR (trailing stop enabled AND low <= previously calculated long trailing stop))
Exit: short, datetime within configured backtest window AND (high >= entry price multiplied by one plus stop-loss fraction OR low <= entry price multiplied by one minus take-profit fraction OR (trailing stop enabled AND high >= previously calculated short trailing stop))
