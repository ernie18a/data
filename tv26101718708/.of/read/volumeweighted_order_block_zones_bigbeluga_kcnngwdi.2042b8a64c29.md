Calculation: Volume strength = 100 minus clamp(truncate_toward_zero(96 - 12 × pivot-bar volume / pivot-bar volume SMA), 40, 92); use a ratio of 1 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Active order blocks = Maintain an ordered collection of bullish and bearish zones, each bounded by the high and low of its pivot bar. A confirmed pivot uses equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot. When a pivot's volume strength meets the minimum, replace the pending pivot of that side and fix its displacement level at pivot low + confirmation-bar ATR × displacement multiplier for a low, or pivot high - confirmation-bar ATR × displacement multiplier for a high. If the highest high since the low pivot reaches its level, or the lowest low since the high pivot reaches its level, find the first such bar after the pivot through confirmation and create the corresponding zone only if no low below the bullish zone bottom, or high above the bearish zone top, occurs from that bar through confirmation. Otherwise, create the pending bullish zone when close crosses above its fixed level, or the pending bearish zone when close crosses below its fixed level. On creation, remove overlapping zones of the same side, append the new zone, and remove the oldest zone if the maximum collection size is exceeded. On each bar, process newly confirmed pivots and their immediate zone creation, then process zones newest first: remove bullish zones when low < zone bottom and bearish zones when high > zone top, otherwise evaluate their retests. Create zones from pending close crossovers only after retests, so those zones can first signal on the next bar. Track the last retest bar separately for each side, initially bar index zero; update it immediately on a retest, including when several zones qualify on the same bar.
Long: A bullish pivot low is confirmed AND its volume strength >= minimum strength.
Short: A bearish pivot high is confirmed AND its volume strength >= minimum strength.
Long: An active bullish order block remains valid AND ((previous low <= zone top AND low > zone top) OR (previous close <= zone top AND close > zone top)) AND bars since the last bullish retest > cooldown.
Short: An active bearish order block remains valid AND ((previous high >= zone bottom AND high < zone bottom) OR (previous close >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom)) AND bars since the last bearish retest > cooldown.
