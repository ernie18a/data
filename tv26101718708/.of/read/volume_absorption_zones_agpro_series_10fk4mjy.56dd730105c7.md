Calculation: Safe range = max(high - low, minimum price increment parameter).
Calculation: Effort ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or 0 if the SMA is not positive. Volume z-score = (volume - SMA(volume)) / population standard deviation of volume over the same lookback, or 0 if the standard deviation is not positive.
Calculation: Spread ratio = safe range / SMA(safe range), or 1 if the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / safe range.
Calculation: Effort score = clamp(70 * (effort ratio - 1) / (effort threshold - 1) + 30 * clamp(volume z-score, 0, 3) / 3, 0, 100).
Calculation: Compression score = clamp(100 * (1 - spread ratio) / (1 - maximum spread ratio), 0, 100).
Calculation: Bullish quality = 0.38 * effort score + 0.34 * compression score + 0.28 * clamp(200 * (close location - 0.5), 0, 100). Bearish quality = 0.38 * effort score + 0.34 * compression score + 0.28 * clamp(200 * (0.5 - close location), 0, 100).
Calculation: Each absorption signal creates a zone with its side, birth bar, high as top, low as bottom, and initial status active. Its invalidation is max(bottom - safe ATR * ATR buffer, minimum price increment parameter, bottom * 0.85) for a bullish zone and top + safe ATR * ATR buffer for a bearish zone. Safe ATR is the greater of the minimum price increment parameter and ATR at birth, falling back to safe range if ATR is unavailable. Before updating zones, remove those older than the zone-memory limit. On each later bar, process remaining zones oldest first: a bullish zone fails when close < invalidation; a bearish zone fails when close > invalidation. A nonfailed zone becomes defended on its first qualifying defense; a defended zone can subsequently fail. After updates, add new zones and remove the oldest zones above the zone-count limit. Also remove an older zone when its visible badge collides with a newer visible badge: their rounded horizontal box centers differ by at most the badge-spacing parameter and their vertical centers differ by less than the current safe ATR times the badge-distance parameter. Active boxes extend from birth to the current bar plus the extension parameter; defended or failed boxes end at the earlier of the resolution bar plus the resolved-tail parameter and the birth bar plus the extension parameter. Apply the platform's forward-coordinate cap when calculating centers.
Long: Volume and safe-range SMAs are available AND effort ratio >= effort threshold AND spread ratio <= maximum spread ratio AND close location >= strong-close threshold AND (min(open, close) - low) / safe range >= minimum wick-share threshold AND bullish quality >= minimum quality threshold.
Short: Volume and safe-range SMAs are available AND effort ratio >= effort threshold AND spread ratio <= maximum spread ratio AND close location <= 1 - strong-close threshold AND (high - max(open, close)) / safe range >= minimum wick-share threshold AND bearish quality >= minimum quality threshold.
Long: A retained bullish zone is active and older than its birth bar AND close >= its invalidation AND high >= its bottom AND low <= its top AND close > its top AND close > open AND bars since the last printed defense or failure event >= the event cooldown, or no event has been printed.
Short: A retained bearish zone is active and older than its birth bar AND close <= its invalidation AND high >= its bottom AND low <= its top AND close < its bottom AND close < open AND bars since the last printed defense or failure event >= the event cooldown, or no event has been printed.
