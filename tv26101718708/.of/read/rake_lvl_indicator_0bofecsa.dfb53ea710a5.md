Calculation: Opening print = open of the chart bar at the regular-session start; retain it until the next regular-session start.
Calculation: Active levels = maintain a persistent set of opens of the longest higher-timeframe bars. During the regular session, add the last completed bar's open when its close and the preceding bar's close are on opposite sides of their respective opens, unless an existing level differs from that open by less than a configurable percentage of that open. After adding levels, remove each level when the last completed bar's close and its preceding bar's close are strictly on opposite sides of the level.
Calculation: Near reference = absolute difference between chart close and either the opening print or any active level is at most a configurable percentage of that reference.
Long: Chart close > chart EMA AND shorter higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA AND longer higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA; all three EMAs use the same length.
Short: Chart close < chart EMA AND shorter higher-timeframe last completed close < its EMA AND longer higher-timeframe last completed close < its EMA; all three EMAs use the same length.
Long: The preceding bar of the last completed daily higher-timeframe bar has close < open AND the last completed daily bar's open > that preceding bar's open.
Short: The preceding bar of the last completed daily higher-timeframe bar has close > open AND the last completed daily bar's open < that preceding bar's open.
Long: Chart high > previous chart high AND previous chart high <= high two chart bars ago AND chart close > chart EMA AND near reference AND, if the configurable alignment filter is enabled, shorter higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA AND longer higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA; all three EMAs use the same length.
Short: Chart low < previous chart low AND previous chart low >= low two chart bars ago AND chart close < chart EMA AND near reference AND, if the configurable alignment filter is enabled, shorter higher-timeframe last completed close < its EMA AND longer higher-timeframe last completed close < its EMA; all three EMAs use the same length.
