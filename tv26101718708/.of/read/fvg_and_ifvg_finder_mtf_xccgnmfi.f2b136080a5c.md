Calculation: Bullish gap = low of the previous bar > high three bars ago; bottom = high three bars ago; top = low of the previous bar. On a higher timeframe, evaluate these offsets relative to the last completed higher-timeframe bar when its value becomes available on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish gap = high of the previous bar < low three bars ago; bottom = high of the previous bar; top = low three bars ago. On a higher timeframe, evaluate these offsets relative to the last completed higher-timeframe bar when its value becomes available on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Active gap = a newly detected gap whose first pattern bar can be mapped to a chart bar within a maximum lookback, retained until its age from that chart bar exceeds the maximum lookback or it is filled. A bullish gap is filled when chart close <= bottom or, in wick-fill mode, chart low <= bottom. A bearish gap is filled when chart close >= top or, in wick-fill mode, chart high >= top.
Long: A new bullish gap is detected on the chart timeframe OR a new bullish gap is detected on a higher timeframe OR an active bearish gap is filled.
Short: A new bearish gap is detected on the chart timeframe OR a new bearish gap is detected on a higher timeframe OR an active bullish gap is filled.
