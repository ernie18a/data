Calculation: Bullish FVG = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher timeframe, the low of the previous completed bar is greater than the high of the third-previous completed bar; zone bottom = that high and zone top = that low.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher timeframe, the high of the previous completed bar is less than the low of the third-previous completed bar; zone bottom = that high and zone top = that low.
Long: Bullish FVG forms OR close >= the top of an active bearish FVG
Short: Bearish FVG forms OR close <= the bottom of an active bullish FVG
