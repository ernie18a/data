Calculation: Fibonacci borders = for each of two independently parameterized lookbacks, let H be the highest high and L the lowest low; the borders are H, H - 0.236 × (H - L), H - 0.382 × (H - L), H - 0.618 × (H - L), H - 0.764 × (H - L), and L. Optionally use the preceding bar's H and L. For the second entry only, its lowest border is the first lookback's L.
Calculation: Directional indicators = standard +DI, -DI and ADX with independently configurable DI length and ADX smoothing for each zone; optionally calculate directional movement from the preceding bar's high and low changes while retaining current-bar true range.
Calculation: Source = a configurable price series for each zone; optionally use its preceding bar's value. The second zone's stop-loss source is the first zone's current source when this option is off.
Calculation: Zone entry trigger = the zone's source crosses its selected Fibonacci border in either direction, upward, or downward, according to its selected mode. Optionally include high and low crossing in the selected direction for the first zone; for the second zone, optionally include high >= border for upward mode, low <= border for downward mode, or either inequality for either-direction mode.
Calculation: Zone profit trigger = the zone's source crosses its selected upper or central Fibonacci border in either direction, upward, or downward, according to its selected mode; optionally include high crossing for upward mode, low crossing for downward mode, or either high or low crossing for either-direction mode.
Calculation: Zone stop trigger = the zone's stop-loss source crosses its selected Fibonacci border in either direction, upward, or downward, according to its selected mode; optionally include high crossing for either-direction or upward mode, or low crossing for downward mode.
Calculation: Safety threshold = initial entry source × (1 - deviation × n) when step scale equals 1; otherwise initial entry source × (1 - deviation × (step scale^n - 1) / (step scale - 1)), where n starts at 1 and increases after each safety order.
Calculation: Profit level = the active zone's selected upper or central Fibonacci border, or average position entry price × (1 + target return) when percentage target mode is selected.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after any enabled zone's profit trigger or high >= profit level, initialize the trailing state; on that bar and subsequent bars set the stop to the greater of its previous value and (high if either zone has faster exit enabled and its profit trigger occurs, otherwise close) × (1 - active zone's trailing deviation). Reset the state and stop after exit.
Long: Position is flat AND datetime is within the configured inclusive interval AND optional volume >= minimum volume AND optional volume >= its SMA AND optional RSI of the first zone's source < maximum RSI AND optional highest volume over its lookback > its previous value AND optional ranged-volume source > open AND ((first zone is enabled AND its entry trigger occurs AND its ADX, +DI and -DI are each within their respective inclusive bounds AND optionally +DI > -DI) OR (second zone is enabled AND its entry trigger occurs AND its ADX, +DI and -DI are each within their respective inclusive bounds AND optionally +DI > -DI)). For the ranged-volume source and open, optionally use Heikin-Ashi values. The first qualifying zone has priority for the buy marking; the actual position is attributed to the first enabled zone whose entry trigger and ADX bounds pass and whose unshifted source > 0, otherwise to the second such zone.
Long: Position is long AND safety-order count is below its configured maximum AND trailing state is inactive AND low <= safety threshold.
Exit: long, the active zone's optional stop trigger occurs.
Exit: long, the active zone's optional +DI crosses above -DI AND previous close < previous open.
Exit: long, the active zone's optional +DI crosses below -DI AND previous close > previous open.
Exit: long, trailing deviation = 0 AND (high >= profit level OR either enabled zone's profit trigger occurs).
Exit: long, trailing deviation > 0 AND trailing state was already active before this bar AND ((either zone's faster exit is enabled AND high <= trailing stop) OR (neither zone's faster exit is enabled AND close <= trailing stop)).
