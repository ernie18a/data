Calculation: Impulse histogram = D - SMA(D), where D is zero-lag EMA of (high + low + close) / 3 minus smoothed moving average of high when the zero-lag EMA is above that average, minus smoothed moving average of low when it is below that average, and zero otherwise. The zero-lag EMA is 2 × EMA(source) - EMA(EMA(source)); both EMA lengths and the high and low smoothed moving average lengths are equal. The SMA of D uses a shorter length. Each smoothed moving average is initialized with an SMA and subsequently equals (previous average × (length - 1) + current source) / length.
Long: (Impulse filtering disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)) AND (EMA of close > previous EMA of close in the single-EMA mode OR first EMA of close > second EMA of close in the two-EMA mode).
Short: (Impulse filtering disabled OR (impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)) AND (EMA of close < previous EMA of close in the single-EMA mode OR first EMA of close < second EMA of close in the two-EMA mode).
Calculation: Apply each directional condition independently on the chart timeframe and on each selected higher or lower timeframe. The two EMAs have independently configurable lengths, with the first normally shorter than the second. For historical higher-timeframe requests use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; for a live higher-timeframe request use the still-forming bar. For lower-timeframe requests use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
