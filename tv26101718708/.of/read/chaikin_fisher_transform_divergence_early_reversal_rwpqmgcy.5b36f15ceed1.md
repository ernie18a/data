Calculation: Accumulation/distribution line = cumulative sum of volume × (((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low)); use a zero multiplier when high = low
Calculation: Chaikin oscillator = short-lookback EMA of the accumulation/distribution line − long-lookback EMA of the accumulation/distribution line
Calculation: Fisher line = recursively smooth the Chaikin oscillator normalized to [−0.5, 0.5] by its rolling low and high over a lookback, clamp the smoothed normalized value to [−0.999, 0.999], then apply 0.5 × ln((1 + value) / (1 − value)) and add half the prior Fisher line
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a local low/high with the specified number of bars on each side; confirm only after the right-side bars complete
Long: Fisher line crosses above its one-bar lag OR at a confirmed Fisher pivot low the corresponding price low is below that of the preceding confirmed Fisher pivot low while the Fisher pivot value is higher, with pivot separation no greater than the maximum OR the prior bar's Fisher line is at or below the oversold level, the Fisher line on two bars ago is at or above the prior bar's value, the current Fisher line is above the prior bar's value, and the minimum same-direction early-signal cooldown has elapsed
Short: Fisher line crosses below its one-bar lag OR at a confirmed Fisher pivot high the corresponding price high is above that of the preceding confirmed Fisher pivot high while the Fisher pivot value is lower, with pivot separation no greater than the maximum OR the prior bar's Fisher line is at or above the overbought level, the Fisher line on two bars ago is at or below the prior bar's value, the current Fisher line is below the prior bar's value, and the minimum same-direction early-signal cooldown has elapsed
