Calculation: VWAP = cumulative(volume * (high+low+close)/3) / cumulative(volume), reset at the start of each trading day
Calculation: StopDist = stop multiplier * ATR, taken at the entry signal bar; entry price = that bar's close
Calculation: TrailStop = for long, running maximum since entry of (close - trail multiplier * ATR); for short, running minimum since entry of (close + trail multiplier * ATR); trail multiplier is smaller than the stop multiplier
Long: position is flat AND fast EMA(close) > slow EMA(close) AND close > fast EMA AND higher-timeframe (still-forming bar) fast EMA(close) > higher-timeframe slow EMA(close) AND MACD line crosses above signal line (MACD > signal on this bar and MACD <= signal on the previous bar) AND RSI < overbought threshold AND RSI > a lower bound below the midline AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND high > previous bar high AND volume > a factor above 1 * SMA(volume) AND ATR > a factor below 1 * SMA(ATR) AND bar time-of-day is inside the morning entry window
Short: position is flat AND fast EMA(close) < slow EMA(close) AND close < fast EMA AND higher-timeframe (still-forming bar) fast EMA(close) < higher-timeframe slow EMA(close) AND MACD line crosses below signal line (MACD < signal on this bar and MACD >= signal on the previous bar) AND RSI > oversold threshold AND RSI < an upper bound above the midline AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND low < previous bar low AND volume > a factor above 1 * SMA(volume) AND ATR > a factor below 1 * SMA(ATR) AND bar time-of-day is inside the morning entry window
Exit: long, low <= entry price - StopDist OR high >= entry price + reward ratio * StopDist OR close <= TrailStop OR bar time-of-day is inside the end-of-day close window
Exit: short, high >= entry price + StopDist OR low <= entry price - reward ratio * StopDist OR close >= TrailStop OR bar time-of-day is inside the end-of-day close window
