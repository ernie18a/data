Calculation: Bullish BOS = close crosses above the tracked last pivot low delayed by the BOS swing lookback; bearish BOS = close crosses below the tracked last pivot high delayed by the BOS swing lookback. Track pivot lows and highs confirmed using equal left and right BOS swing lookbacks.
Calculation: RSI = standard relative strength index of close. Bullish RSI divergence = low <= lowest low over the divergence lookback AND RSI > the prior bar's lowest RSI over that lookback. Bearish RSI divergence = high >= highest high over the divergence lookback AND RSI < the prior bar's highest RSI over that lookback.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > simple moving average of volume over the volume lookback × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = (lower wick >= body × 2 AND upper wick <= body × 0.5 AND close > open) OR (prior close < prior open AND close > open AND close >= prior open AND open <= prior close) OR (close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND prior body < prior-bar 14-bar average body × 0.3 AND close > open AND close > midpoint of the candle two bars ago). Bearish candle pattern = (upper wick >= body × 2 AND lower wick <= body × 0.5 AND close < open) OR (prior close > prior open AND close < open AND close <= prior open AND open >= prior close) OR (close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND prior body < prior-bar 14-bar average body × 0.3 AND close < open AND close < midpoint of the candle two bars ago). Each pattern group is enabled by its corresponding setting.
Calculation: Bullish trendline break = close crosses above the projection from the tracked last pivot high; bearish trendline break = close crosses below the projection from the tracked last pivot low. Track pivots using equal left and right trendline pivot lookbacks. For each tracked pivot, slope = (current tracked pivot value - its prior-bar tracked value) / max(current bar index - tracked pivot bar index, 1); projection = tracked pivot value + slope × (current bar index - tracked pivot bar index).
Calculation: Bullish score = bullish BOS + bullish RSI divergence + volume spike + bullish candle pattern + bullish trendline break, counting each true condition as 1. Bearish score = bearish BOS + bearish RSI divergence + volume spike + bearish candle pattern + bearish trendline break, counting each true condition as 1.
Long: low <= lowest low over the divergence lookback AND RSI > the prior bar's lowest RSI over that lookback
Long: bullish score >= minimum signals
Short: high >= highest high over the divergence lookback AND RSI < the prior bar's highest RSI over that lookback
Short: bearish score >= minimum signals
