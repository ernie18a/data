Calculation: Moving averages = four enabled moving averages of a selected price source, with strictly increasing lookbacks: shortest, short, long, and longest; use the same selected moving-average type throughout.
Calculation: Normalized slope = (current longest moving average − longest moving average a slope lookback ago) / current ATR; undefined when ATR is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Angle = (current longest moving average − longest moving average a slope lookback ago) / current longest moving average; undefined when the current longest moving average is zero or the earlier value is unavailable.
Calculation: Distance score = weighted sum of the absolute differences between each adjacent pair of moving averages, divided by ATR; use a separate fixed weight for each pair and substitute zero for an unavailable pairwise distance.
Calculation: Alignment count = number of enabled monitored timeframes whose normalized slope exceeds the positive slope threshold for long alignment, or is below its negative for short alignment. For each timeframe, request the longest moving average and ATR from that timeframe, then calculate the slope lookback in chart bars on the returned moving-average series. On historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on a forming chart bar use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the latest available lower-timeframe bar. A missing slope counts as zero.
Calculation: Table state = apply the moving averages, ATR, normalized slope, angle, and distance score entirely within each enabled monitored timeframe. On historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on a forming chart bar use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the latest available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trailing distance = current ATR multiplied by a trailing multiplier, rounded to the nearest price tick with a minimum of one tick. A long trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price plus the trailing distance and then follows the highest price reached since activation at that distance; a short trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price minus the trailing distance and then follows the lowest price reached since activation at that distance. Update the distance from current ATR while the order is active.
Long: Chart short moving average > chart long moving average AND chart long moving average > chart longest moving average AND close > chart shortest moving average AND chart normalized slope > slope threshold AND chart angle > angle threshold AND chart distance score > distance threshold AND long alignment count >= minimum alignment count AND the same combined condition was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: Chart short moving average < chart long moving average AND chart long moving average < chart longest moving average AND close < chart shortest moving average AND chart normalized slope < negative slope threshold AND chart angle < negative angle threshold AND chart distance score > distance threshold AND short alignment count >= minimum alignment count AND the same combined condition was false on the previous chart bar.
Long: An enabled table state's short moving average > its long moving average AND its long moving average > its longest moving average AND its close > its shortest moving average AND its normalized slope > slope threshold AND its angle > angle threshold AND its distance score > distance threshold.
Short: An enabled table state's short moving average < its long moving average AND its long moving average < its longest moving average AND its close < its shortest moving average AND its normalized slope < negative slope threshold AND its angle < negative angle threshold AND its distance score > distance threshold.
Long: Chart short moving average >= chart long moving average.
Short: Chart short moving average < chart long moving average.
Exit: long, the chart short-entry condition becomes true.
Exit: short, the chart long-entry condition becomes true.
Exit: long, ATR stop enabled AND low <= entry price − current ATR × stop multiplier.
Exit: short, ATR stop enabled AND high >= entry price + current ATR × stop multiplier.
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND trailing stop activated AND low <= active trailing stop.
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND trailing stop activated AND high >= active trailing stop.
