Calculation: Rejection event = On a higher timeframe bar, use the last completed bar. A lower-wick rejection has a lower wick at least the required fraction of the high-low range and a close at least the required ATR distance above the low; an upper-wick rejection has an upper wick at least that fraction and a close at least that ATR distance below the high.
Calculation: Rejection cluster = Group rejection wick tips within the required ATR-based tolerance; each completed higher-timeframe bar counts at most once per cluster. The cluster level is the average of its rejection prices, and its type is demand if it has lower-wick rejections only, supply if it has upper-wick rejections only, or both if it has both.
Long: A rejection cluster has at least the required number of separate events AND has lower-wick rejections AND has no upper-wick rejections.
Short: A rejection cluster has at least the required number of separate events AND has upper-wick rejections AND has no lower-wick rejections.
