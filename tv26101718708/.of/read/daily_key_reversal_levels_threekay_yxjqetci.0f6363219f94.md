Calculation: Source bars = bars aggregated to a source timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. On historical chart bars use the last completed source bar; on a real-time chart bar use the source bar still forming. If the timeframes are equal, use the chart bar.
Calculation: Rejection measures = source-bar range is max(high − low, minimum price increment); upper wick is high − max(open, close); lower wick is min(open, close) − low. A supply rejection has upper wick >= range × minimum wick fraction AND high − close >= source ATR × minimum reaction multiple. A demand rejection has lower wick >= range × minimum wick fraction AND close − low >= source ATR × minimum reaction multiple.
Calculation: Rejection zones = process source bars chronologically over the configured history, starting when source ATR is available. Each zone stores its center, radius, total hits, supply hits, demand hits, and last-hit time. For every existing zone, a supply hit occurs when the high is within its radius of its center AND close < center AND the bar is a supply rejection; a demand hit occurs when the low is within its radius of its center AND close > center AND the bar is a demand rejection. If either occurs and the zone has not already been hit on this bar, increment total hits once and each applicable directional hit count, update the center to the hit-count-weighted average of its previous center and the rejected tip, and record the bar time. If both occur, use the tip with the larger wick, choosing the high on a tie. Next, if the bar has either rejection, select its seed tip and direction using the larger wick when both occur, choosing supply on a tie. Find the nearest zone whose center is within max(zone radius, source ATR × clustering multiple) of the seed tip. If one exists and has not already been hit on this bar, update its center and counts with the seed and reduce its radius to min(previous radius, source ATR × clustering multiple). If no zone matches, create one at the seed tip with that ATR-scaled radius and one hit in the selected direction. When the zone database is full, remove the oldest zone before adding a new one.
Calculation: Selected zones = zones with total hits >= required hits, ranked by descending score, retaining up to the display limit; score = min(total hits, hit-count cap) × hit weight − abs(source close − center) / max(source ATR, minimum price increment). Break equal scores by earlier database order.
Long: A selected zone has demand hits > 0 AND supply hits = 0.
Short: A selected zone has supply hits > 0 AND demand hits = 0.
