Calculation: Body = absolute value of close minus open; use a small positive substitute when zero. Range = high minus low; use a small positive substitute when zero. Lower wick = minimum of open and close minus low. Upper wick = high minus maximum of open and close.
Calculation: Signal cooldown passes when there has been no previous fired institutional or scalping signal, or the number of bars since the last such signal exceeds the cooldown parameter. Evaluate all signals on a bar before updating the last signal bar.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe score = rounded weighted sum of RSI and an EMA score. The EMA score takes a bullish, bearish, or neutral value according to whether the fast EMA is above, below, or equal to the slow EMA. Calculate each indicator on the selected higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Signal cooldown passes AND ATR divided by close is at least the volatility threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times the institutional volume multiplier AND close > fast institutional EMA AND fast institutional EMA > slow institutional EMA AND ((lower wick > body times the wick multiplier AND low < previous low AND close > low plus range times the sweep threshold) OR (volume equals its highest value over the volume lookback AND body < range times the absorption threshold AND close > open AND close > fast institutional EMA)) AND ((close > open AND close > previous high) OR close > high minus range times the close-location threshold).
Short: Signal cooldown passes AND ATR divided by close is at least the volatility threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times the institutional volume multiplier AND close < fast institutional EMA AND fast institutional EMA < slow institutional EMA AND ((upper wick > body times the wick multiplier AND high > previous high AND close < high minus range times the sweep threshold) OR (volume equals its highest value over the volume lookback AND body < range times the absorption threshold AND close < open AND close < fast institutional EMA)) AND ((close < open AND close < previous low) OR close < low plus range times the close-location threshold).
Long: Signal cooldown passes AND ATR divided by close is at least the volatility threshold AND ADX is at least the trend threshold AND fast scalping EMA > slow scalping EMA AND low <= fast scalping EMA AND close > fast scalping EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND volume > its moving average times the scalping volume multiplier.
Short: Signal cooldown passes AND ATR divided by close is at least the volatility threshold AND ADX is at least the trend threshold AND fast scalping EMA < slow scalping EMA AND high >= fast scalping EMA AND close < fast scalping EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND volume > its moving average times the scalping volume multiplier.
Long: Volume > its moving average times the accumulation volume multiplier AND close > fast institutional EMA AND fast institutional EMA > slow institutional EMA.
Short: Volume > its moving average times the accumulation volume multiplier AND close < fast institutional EMA AND fast institutional EMA < slow institutional EMA.
Long: Lower wick > body times the trap wick multiplier AND volume equals its highest value over the volume lookback AND close > open AND neither the accumulation nor distribution condition holds.
Short: Upper wick > body times the trap wick multiplier AND volume equals its highest value over the volume lookback AND close < open AND neither the accumulation nor distribution condition holds.
Long: Multi-timeframe score >= the bullish threshold.
Short: Multi-timeframe score <= the complement of the bullish threshold on the score's scale.
