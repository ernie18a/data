Calculation: Signal = crossover of the shorter-period EMA of close above the longer-period EMA of close for long, and crossunder for short. Optionally reverse its direction and delay it by a selected number of bars. If re-entry is enabled, retain the latest nonzero signal and repeat it when flat; suppress a repeated same-side signal while in a position unless reopening is enabled. Suppress signals during the configured cooldown unless the retained signal direction changes.
Calculation: Entry filters = all enabled filters must pass. Long filters are ADX below its threshold; MACD histogram above zero; close above open; volume above previous volume; SMA of close above every one of its preceding lookback values; high at least the highest high over its lookback including the current bar; close below previous close increased by the maximum percentage; close above previous close increased by the minimum percentage; RSI at or below its upper threshold; and close above the selected moving average. Short filters use the same ADX and volume conditions, MACD histogram below zero, close below open, SMA below every one of its preceding lookback values, low at most the lowest low over its lookback including the current bar, close above previous close decreased by the maximum percentage, close below previous close decreased by the minimum percentage, RSI at or above its lower threshold, and close below the selected moving average. If filter inversion is enabled, use the short filters for both directions.
Calculation: Price source = selected close, OHLC average, high-low-close-close average, high-low-close average, high-low average, or side-dependent high or low.
Calculation: Volatility distance = selected ATR multiplied by its factor, standard deviation of close multiplied by its factor, price source multiplied by a percentage, or a fixed price distance. Stop and target use independently selected distances and lookbacks.
Calculation: Stop = selected stop calculated for the position side. Static volatility: price source minus the stop distance for long or plus it for short at entry. Trailing: running maximum of price source minus stop distance for long or running minimum of price source plus stop distance for short. Fast trailing: initialize at source minus or plus stop distance; on a new higher high for long add the positive part of the change in OHLC average, or on a new lower low for short add the negative part. Volatility stop: initialize at source minus or plus stop distance; thereafter use the running maximum source minus current distance, never decreasing, for long, or the running minimum source plus current distance, never increasing, for short. Chandelier: highest high since entry minus current distance, never decreasing, for long; lowest low since entry plus current distance, never increasing, for short. Activator: initialize at source minus or plus stop distance; thereafter add the sign of close minus the previous stop, multiplied by the entry-bar stop distance divided by its factor and lookback, and by bars since entry. Moving-average stop: initialize at source minus or plus stop distance; thereafter add the sign of previous close minus previous stop multiplied by the absolute change in the selected moving average of close. SAR stop: initialize from the previous low and entry high for long or previous high and entry low for short, constrain it by the low or high two bars ago, and thereafter advance it toward the most favorable high or low using an acceleration factor that increases on new extremes and is capped; continue applying the two-bar constraint. High-low stop: lowest or highest price source over its lookback at entry for long or short. If the entry-bar stop lies strictly inside that bar's range, replace it with the entry-bar low for long or high for short. Optionally multiply the stop by one minus a percentage for long or one plus that percentage for short. Optionally move the stop to entry after the configured count of profitable closes in the latest count-plus-one bars, or after the selected enabled intermediate target has been reached; optionally move it to the previous enabled target after a later enabled target is reached.
Calculation: Target = at entry, price source plus target volatility distance for long or minus it for short; or the highest or lowest price source over its target lookback for long or short; or entry plus the entry-to-stop distance multiplied by the reward-to-risk factor for long, or entry minus that distance multiplied by the factor for short. The reward-to-risk target may instead be recalculated as the stop changes. Intermediate target and stop levels divide the distance from entry to the applicable final level into six equal parts; enabled levels can close part of the position.
Long: Long signal AND long entries enabled AND permitted date, weekday, and intersection of enabled sessions AND all applicable entry filters pass AND sufficient bars exist for the configured stop and volatility lookbacks AND, if waiting for the current deal is enabled, position is flat.
Short: Short signal AND short entries enabled AND permitted date, weekday, and intersection of enabled sessions AND all applicable entry filters pass AND sufficient bars exist for the configured stop and volatility lookbacks AND, if waiting for the current deal is enabled, position is flat.
Exit: long, low at or below an enabled stop or intermediate stop level OR high at or above an enabled target or intermediate target level OR the count of closes below entry in the latest configured count-plus-one bars is at least the configured count OR a short entry is accepted while waiting for the current deal is disabled OR session-end closing is enabled and the time filter fails.
Exit: short, high at or above an enabled stop or intermediate stop level OR low at or below an enabled target or intermediate target level OR the count of closes above entry in the latest configured count-plus-one bars is at least the configured count OR a long entry is accepted while waiting for the current deal is disabled OR session-end closing is enabled and the time filter fails.
