Calculation: Profile = split the high-low range of the included chart bars into a parameterized number of equal price rows; distribute each bar's volume equally among every row touched by its low-to-high range, substituting a unit volume when volume is missing or nonpositive. The VPOC is the center of the highest-volume row, choosing the lowest row on ties. Starting at that row, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time toward the greater row volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until a parameterized fraction of total volume is included. The value-area low and high are the outer edges of the included rows.
Calculation: Session and weekly profiles = profiles of chart bars in the selected session, or all chart bars if the session filter is disabled. Reset at the start of each higher-timeframe session or week; calculate a completed profile only if it has more than a parameterized minimum number of bars and a positive range. Use the most recently completed higher-timeframe session and week profiles until replaced.
Calculation: Profile position = (VPOC - profile low) / (profile high - profile low); the upper position threshold is greater than the lower position threshold.
Calculation: VPOC trend = compare consecutive VPOCs among up to a parameterized number of the most recent completed higher-timeframe session profiles. Require at least one comparison; the rising or falling count must reach either all comparisons in strict mode or a parameterized fraction of comparisons rounded upward, with a minimum of one, otherwise.
Calculation: Weekly rejection = reset both directional rejection states at each new higher-timeframe week. For an upward rejection, first record a touch when the previous chart close is above the completed weekly value-area high and the current low is at or below that high plus an ATR buffer; on that bar or any subsequent bar in the week, record rejection when close is above that high and above open and high minus low is at least a parameterized ATR multiple. Mirror the inequalities around the completed weekly value-area low for a downward rejection. The touch and rejection states persist until the next week.
Calculation: Reference edges = select the completed higher-timeframe session value-area edges, the completed higher-timeframe weekly value-area edges, or the wider available pair: maximum of their highs and minimum of their lows, using the available profile if only one exists.
Calculation: Impulse and balance = on each confirmed chart bar in the selected session, while flat and below the session signal limit, find the highest high and lowest low over a parameterized impulse lookback including the current bar. An upward impulse has range at least a parameterized ATR multiple, close at or above a parameterized upper fraction of that range, low of the range below the upper reference edge and close above that edge; a downward impulse mirrors these comparisons using a lower fraction and the lower reference edge. Apply the optional weekly location and weekly opening filters at impulse detection. Prefer the upward impulse if both qualify. Store the impulse range and initialize balance high, low and bar count from the impulse bar. On each later eligible bar, evaluate entries using the balance bounds and count from before that bar; if no entry fires, incorporate its high and low and increment the count. Discard the setup if the updated balance height exceeds a parameterized ATR multiple, its count exceeds a parameterized maximum, or close crosses beyond the stored impulse origin against its direction. After a discard, the same bar may start a new impulse.
Calculation: Micro profile = profile of the most recent chart bars including the current bar, with lookback equal to the balance count plus the impulse lookback, capped at a parameterized maximum, and with a parameterized row count smaller than the session profile row count. Its profile position determines its directional shape.
Calculation: Single-print count = divide the running higher-timeframe session's range through the current bar into the session profile's price rows; count rows intersecting the stored impulse range that were touched by at least one and at most a parameterized number of included session bars.
Calculation: Entry context = on a confirmed chart bar in the selected session, require a stored impulse in the same direction, a balance count at least its parameterized minimum, a flat position, and fewer than the parameterized maximum signals in the current higher-timeframe session. Optionally require close outside the completed weekly value area in the trade direction and, nested within that filter, the current week's opening price outside it in the same direction; optionally require the corresponding weekly rejection, corresponding VPOC trend, and a single-print count at least its parameterized minimum. Require close beyond the corresponding reference edge and micro-profile position at or beyond its directional threshold.
Calculation: Risk levels = at entry use the trigger bar's close as entry price. For a long, set the initial stop to the balance low, impulse low, or the lower of the upper reference edge and balance low, according to the selected stop mode, minus a parameterized ATR buffer; mirror for a short. Initial risk is the greater of the entry-to-stop distance and a parameterized minimum price increment. Set two targets at parameterized risk multiples in the trade direction. On every eligible management bar including the entry bar, first record the first target when touched and optionally move the stop to entry; then check the second target, stop, and time stop in that order. The time stop applies only before the first target has been touched. Management is evaluated only on confirmed chart bars in the selected session.
Long: The completed higher-timeframe session profile position >= the upper position threshold.
Short: The completed higher-timeframe session profile position <= the lower position threshold.
Long: The current higher-timeframe week's opening price > the completed weekly value-area high.
Short: The current higher-timeframe week's opening price < the completed weekly value-area low.
Long: Close > the completed weekly value-area high.
Short: Close < the completed weekly value-area low.
Long: The completed higher-timeframe session VPOC trend is rising.
Short: The completed higher-timeframe session VPOC trend is falling.
Long: Entry context is satisfied AND (breakout is enabled AND close > balance high AND close > open OR break-in is enabled AND low <= balance low + a parameterized fraction of balance height AND close > balance midpoint AND close > open).
Short: Entry context is satisfied AND (breakout is enabled AND close < balance low AND close < open OR break-in is enabled AND high >= balance high - a parameterized fraction of balance height AND close < balance midpoint AND close < open).
Exit: long, high >= first target AND first target has not previously been touched.
Exit: short, low <= first target AND first target has not previously been touched.
Exit: long, high >= second target OR low <= current stop OR bars since entry >= the enabled time-stop limit AND first target has not been touched.
Exit: short, low <= second target OR high >= current stop OR bars since entry >= the enabled time-stop limit AND first target has not been touched.
