Calculation: Attention score and conviction = For each bar, form the state vector ((close − previous close) / ATR, (high − low) / ATR − 1, (volume − volume SMA) / volume standard deviation), using zero for each component when its denominator is unavailable or nonpositive and the current close when the previous close is unavailable. For each lag k from 1 through the attention lookback N, replace unavailable historical state components with zero, calculate squared Euclidean distance d_k from the current state, weight w_k = exp(−d_k / temperature − max_j(−d_j / temperature)), and outcome o_k = (close[k−1] − close[k]) / ATR[k], or zero if historical ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Let p_k = w_k / sum(w), forecast = sum(p_k × o_k), and conviction = clamp(1 + sum(p_k × ln(p_k)) / ln(N), 0, 1), excluding probabilities at or below a small numerical cutoff from the entropy sum. Before bar index N + 2, forecast = 0 and conviction = 0. The attention score is the EMA of forecast.
Calculation: Risk ledger = Reset daily R and daily signal count at each new exchange day. Maintain one simulated trade independently of the last signal direction; open it at a signal close only if no simulated trade is open. Fix its stop distance at entry as an ATR multiple and its target distance as that stop distance times the reward-to-risk parameter. On later confirmed bars, resolve stop before target: add −1 R for a stop or reward-to-risk R for a target to daily and cumulative R, then close the simulated trade. Track the maximum cumulative R, initially zero. Update the ledger before evaluating signals. The last signal direction starts neutral and changes on every signal; simulated exits do not reset it. The last signal bar index starts at zero.
Long: Attention score crosses above the positive score threshold AND conviction > conviction floor AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR close > EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the selected exchange-time session) AND (news filter disabled OR bar time is outside both exchange-time news windows) AND daily R > negative daily-loss limit divided by risk per trade AND (drawdown lock disabled OR peak cumulative R − cumulative R < maximum-drawdown limit divided by risk per trade) AND daily signal count < daily signal limit AND last signal direction is neutral or short AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND bar is confirmed.
Short: Attention score crosses below the negative score threshold AND conviction > conviction floor AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR close < EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the selected exchange-time session) AND (news filter disabled OR bar time is outside both exchange-time news windows) AND daily R > negative daily-loss limit divided by risk per trade AND (drawdown lock disabled OR peak cumulative R − cumulative R < maximum-drawdown limit divided by risk per trade) AND daily signal count < daily signal limit AND last signal direction is neutral or long AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND bar is confirmed.
