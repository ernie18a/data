Calculation: ATR = Average True Range (Wilder smoothing) over a fixed lookback on the same timeframe as the bars being evaluated.
Long: on a completed bar, low > high of 2 bars earlier AND (low - high of 2 bars earlier) >= a fraction of ATR (a size filter; the fraction is below 1) OR on a higher timeframe than the chart, using its last completed bar, the most recent bar satisfying the same condition (evaluated with that timeframe's own bars and ATR) is more recent than the most recent bearish gap condition on that timeframe.
Short: on a completed bar, high < low of 2 bars earlier AND (low of 2 bars earlier - high) >= a fraction of ATR (a size filter; the fraction is below 1) OR on a higher timeframe than the chart, using its last completed bar, the most recent bar satisfying the same condition (evaluated with that timeframe's own bars and ATR) is more recent than the most recent bullish gap condition on that timeframe.
