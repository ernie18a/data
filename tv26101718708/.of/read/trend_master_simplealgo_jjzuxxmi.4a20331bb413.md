Calculation: Efficiency = absolute change in close over the slow ribbon lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero. Smooth with an EMA and substitute zero when the smoothed value is unavailable.
Calculation: Ribbon averages = separate recursive EMAs of close, initialized at the first close. Their fast and slow base lengths use the same optional adaptive multiplier: a fixed offset minus smoothed efficiency when adaptation is enabled, otherwise one. Apply separate minimum lengths; on each bar use alpha = 2 / (current length + 1). The fast base length is shorter than the slow base length.
Calculation: Trend direction = up when the fast ribbon average is above the slow ribbon average, otherwise down. Trend age is the number of bars since the most recent change in direction, initially counted from the first bar. Ribbon top and bottom are the greater and lesser ribbon averages. Chop count is the number of direction changes in a rolling lookback.
Calculation: Registered direction = initially neither direction; on each bar where ATR is available, trend age is at least the confirmation period, direction differs from registered direction, and close is beyond the ribbon in the direction of the trend, set it to the current direction. Update it even when the chop count exceeds its limit or trend-start signals are disabled.
Calculation: Trend strength = an EMA of a weighted sum of ribbon separation, smoothed efficiency, and optionally directional volume, clamped before smoothing to the interval from zero to one. Ribbon separation is absolute ribbon width divided by ATR, capped at a separation limit and divided by that limit, or zero if ATR is zero. Directional volume is the rolling sum of volume on bars with close above open and negative volume on all other bars, divided by the rolling volume sum; reverse its sign in a downtrend and clamp it to the interval from zero to one. Use directional volume only when enabled and its rolling mean is available and positive; otherwise use the separate two-component weights. Scale smoothed efficiency before applying its weight.
Calculation: Pullback state = initially inactive. On bars with ATR available, clear it on a direction change. Otherwise, when trend age meets its minimum and registered direction equals current direction, set it active if price reaches the specified fraction of ribbon width from the outer edge toward the inner edge and close is not beyond the outer edge in the trend direction. If it was active and close is beyond that outer edge, clear it on that bar whether or not the remaining pullback signal conditions pass. Track the bar of the most recent emitted pullback signal in either direction; initialize it sufficiently far in the past to allow the first signal.
Long: fast ribbon average > slow ribbon average.
Short: fast ribbon average <= slow ribbon average.
Long: ATR available AND direction is up AND trend age >= confirmation period AND registered direction before this bar differs from up AND close > ribbon top AND chop count <= maximum.
Short: ATR available AND direction is down AND trend age >= confirmation period AND registered direction before this bar differs from down AND close < ribbon bottom AND chop count <= maximum.
Long: ATR available AND direction is up AND direction has not changed on this bar AND trend age >= minimum pullback age AND registered direction = up AND pullback state was active before this bar AND close > ribbon top AND close > open AND chop count <= maximum AND bars since the last emitted pullback signal >= minimum spacing AND trend strength >= minimum strength.
Short: ATR available AND direction is down AND direction has not changed on this bar AND trend age >= minimum pullback age AND registered direction = down AND pullback state was active before this bar AND close < ribbon bottom AND close < open AND chop count <= maximum AND bars since the last emitted pullback signal >= minimum spacing AND trend strength >= minimum strength.
