Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the slow lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; smooth it with an exponential moving average.
Calculation: Adaptive fast and slow exponential averages = recursive exponential averages of close using their respective base lengths multiplied by a factor that decreases as the smoothed efficiency ratio increases; the fast base length is shorter than the slow base length.
Calculation: Smoothed trend-strength score = a smoothed blend of the fast/slow average separation normalized by ATR, smoothed efficiency, and, when enabled and volume exists, directional volume support over a lookback; clamp the score to 0–1. Steady strength means the score meets its steady threshold.
