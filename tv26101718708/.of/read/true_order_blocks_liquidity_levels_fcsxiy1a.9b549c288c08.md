Calculation: Non-inside bar = a bar whose high is above the current mother bar’s high or whose low is below its low; otherwise it is inside and does not replace the mother bar. The first bar initializes the mother bar, and each non-inside bar becomes the next mother bar.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = on three successive non-inside bars (first, middle, third), third low > first high; let top = max(first high, middle low) and bottom = min(third low, middle high); the FVG is valid when bottom > top.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = on three successive non-inside bars (first, middle, third), third high < first low; let bottom = min(middle high, first low) and top = max(middle low, third high); the FVG is valid when bottom > top.
Calculation: Bullish order block formation = track a bullish candidate when a non-inside bar’s low is below the preceding non-inside bar’s low. For each three-bar non-inside sequence, test the first and middle bars against their respective preceding non-inside bars. If a bullish FVG exists while a bullish candidate is active, form a bullish order block unless the middle bar swept both the first bar’s high and low; if both first and middle bars swept their respective preceding lows, use the middle bar as the block, otherwise use the first bar. Cancel both candidates after forming a block or when the middle bar swept both sides of the first bar. If no block forms, set the bullish candidate when the first or middle bar swept its respective preceding low. Candidates persist until canceled or consumed.
Calculation: Bearish order block formation = track a bearish candidate when a non-inside bar’s high is above the preceding non-inside bar’s high. For each three-bar non-inside sequence, test the first and middle bars against their respective preceding non-inside bars. If a bearish FVG exists while a bearish candidate is active, form a bearish order block unless the middle bar swept both the first bar’s high and low; if both first and middle bars swept their respective preceding highs, use the middle bar as the block, otherwise use the first bar. Cancel both candidates after forming a block or when the middle bar swept both sides of the first bar. If no block forms, set the bearish candidate when the first or middle bar swept its respective preceding high. Candidates persist until canceled or consumed.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the normalized channel index, where the channel index is (HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel length)), channel length)); apply the average length EMA to that index. For each enabled selected timeframe, use its current value (including a still-forming bar) when confirmation is off, or its last completed bar’s value when confirmation is on. Selected timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured.
Long: Bullish FVG is valid.
Long: Bullish order block formation occurs.
Long: At least one trend-filter timeframe is enabled, the trend filter is enabled, and WaveTrend > 0 on every enabled selected timeframe.
Short: Bearish FVG is valid.
Short: Bearish order block formation occurs.
Short: At least one trend-filter timeframe is enabled, the trend filter is enabled, and WaveTrend < 0 on every enabled selected timeframe.
