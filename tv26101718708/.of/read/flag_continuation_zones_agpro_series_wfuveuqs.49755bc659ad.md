Calculation: Use chart-timeframe bars. ATR is floored at a positive price epsilon, falling back to true range when unavailable. A pole move is close minus close at the pole lookback; pole strength is its absolute value divided by ATR; pole efficiency is its absolute value divided by the sum of true ranges over the pole lookback. Bull trend means close > long-term EMA OR short-term EMA > long-term EMA; bear trend reverses both comparisons. The short-term EMA length is a fraction of the long-term EMA length, subject to a minimum length.
Calculation: A bull pole requires sufficient history, pole move > ATR times the minimum pole strength, pole efficiency >= its minimum, and bull trend; a bear pole reverses the pole-move and trend conditions. On each bar, expire an existing candidate when its age exceeds the flag window plus an extension equal to the greater of a minimum extension and a fraction of the flag window. Lock a new pole when there is no candidate, its direction opposes the candidate, or its strength exceeds the same-direction candidate's strength by an upgrade factor. Store the pole's bar, starting close, ending close, move, strength, and efficiency. Preserve the setup-printed flag on same-direction upgrades; otherwise clear it. Clear the candidate and setup-printed flag after a breakout. Process expiration and pole locking before evaluating the current bar's flag.
Calculation: Candidate age is bars since the locked pole. Flag high and flag low are the highest high and lowest low over the flag window ending on the previous bar, even if that window includes bars before the pole. Flag height is their difference floored at a positive price epsilon; pole size is the absolute locked pole move floored at that epsilon. Bull depth is (pole ending close - flag low) / pole size; bear depth is (flag high - pole ending close) / pole size. Flag range is flag height / pole size. Flag slope is the difference between the current linear-regression endpoint of close over the flag window and that regression's value one bar before its endpoint.
Calculation: Volume passes when volume confirmation is disabled OR volume or its moving average is unavailable OR its moving average <= zero OR volume >= its moving average times a minimum volume multiple.
Calculation: Quality is the rounded, bounded sum of: bounded pole strength / minimum pole strength times a pole weight; bounded pole efficiency / minimum pole efficiency times an efficiency weight; bounded (1 - depth) / maximum depth times a depth weight; bounded (1 - flag range) / maximum flag range times a range weight; a compression-pass or compression-fail score; a current trend-pass or trend-fail score; a volume-pass score or zero; and bounded favorable breakout distance divided by ATR times a breakout weight. Favorable breakout distance is close - flag high for a bull candidate and flag low - close for a bear candidate. Each component has its own bounds.
Calculation: A qualified bull setup requires a bull candidate with age >= minimum flag bars and <= flag window plus extension, depth between its negative lower bound and maximum depth inclusive, flag range <= maximum range, flag slope <= ATR times a compression limit, close > pole starting close + pole size times a position fraction, and quality >= minimum quality. A qualified bear setup uses bear depth, flag slope >= negative ATR times the compression limit, close < pole starting close - pole size times the position fraction, and the same remaining conditions.
Calculation: A bull breakout requires a qualified bull setup AND close > flag high + ATR times the breakout buffer AND close > open AND volume passes AND bars since the last breakout of either direction > breakout cooldown, or no previous breakout. A bear breakout requires a qualified bear setup AND close < flag low - ATR times the breakout buffer AND close < open AND volume passes AND the same shared breakout cooldown. Record every breakout bar.
Calculation: A setup event requires a qualified setup AND no setup event printed for the current candidate AND bars since the last setup event of either direction > setup cooldown, or no previous setup event AND no breakout on the current bar. Record the setup bar and mark the candidate as having printed a setup.
Long: A new bull pole is locked OR an active bull candidate has age >= one bar and <= flag window plus extension OR a bull setup event occurs OR a bull breakout occurs.
Short: A new bear pole is locked OR an active bear candidate has age >= one bar and <= flag window plus extension OR a bear setup event occurs OR a bear breakout occurs.
