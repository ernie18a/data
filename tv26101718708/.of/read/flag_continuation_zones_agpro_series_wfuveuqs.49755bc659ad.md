Calculation: Pole move = close − close[long pole lookback]; pole ATR multiple = absolute pole move / ATR; pole efficiency = absolute pole move / sum of true range over the long pole lookback.
Calculation: Trend context = bullish when close > long-term EMA or short-term EMA > long-term EMA; bearish when close < long-term EMA or short-term EMA < long-term EMA. The short-term EMA lookback is half the long-term EMA lookback, rounded to an integer and at least 5 bars.
Calculation: Bullish pole = pole move > ATR × minimum pole ATR multiple AND pole efficiency ≥ minimum pole efficiency AND bullish trend context. Bearish pole = pole move < −ATR × minimum pole ATR multiple AND pole efficiency ≥ minimum pole efficiency AND bearish trend context.
Calculation: Candidate pole = latest qualifying pole when no candidate exists, the new pole is opposite the candidate, the candidate expired, or a same-side pole has a pole ATR multiple > 1.14 × the candidate's; a same-side upgrade replaces the candidate. Candidate expires when bars since its pole > flag window + max(8, half the flag window rounded). It is active from 1 through that expiry limit bars after the pole; the flag window starts at minimum flag bars after the pole.
Calculation: Prior flag high/low = highest high/lowest low over the flag window, excluding the current bar. Pole size = absolute candidate pole move. Flag depth = (candidate pole end − prior flag low)/pole size for bullish candidates; (prior flag high − candidate pole end)/pole size for bearish candidates. Flag range = (prior flag high − prior flag low)/pole size. Flag slope = linear regression slope of close over the flag window.
Calculation: Flag quality = round(clamp(pole score + efficiency score + depth score + range score + compression score + trend score + volume score + breakout score, 0, 100)); pole score = clamp((candidate pole ATR multiple / minimum pole ATR multiple) × 28, 0, 30); efficiency score = clamp(candidate pole efficiency / max(minimum pole efficiency, 0.01) × 15, 0, 18); depth score = clamp((1 − flag depth / maximum flag depth) × 18, 0, 20); range score = clamp((1 − flag range / maximum flag range) × 14, 0, 15); compression score = 10 if compression qualifies, otherwise 3; trend score = 10 if trend context qualifies, otherwise 4; volume score = 7 if volume qualifies, otherwise 0; breakout score = clamp(breakout distance in ATRs × 8, 0, 8), where bullish distance = (close − prior flag high)/ATR and bearish distance = (prior flag low − close)/ATR.
Long: bullish candidate is in its flag window AND flag depth ≥ −0.05 AND flag depth ≤ maximum flag depth AND flag range ≤ maximum flag range AND flag slope ≤ 0.24 × ATR AND close > candidate pole start + 0.10 × pole size AND flag quality ≥ minimum setup quality AND the candidate has not printed a setup AND setup cooldown elapsed AND no bullish or bearish breakout qualifies.
Long: bullish setup conditions qualify OR (bullish setup conditions qualify AND close > prior flag high + ATR × breakout buffer AND close > open AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume data/average is unavailable OR volume ≥ volume average × minimum volume multiple) AND breakout cooldown elapsed).
Short: bearish candidate is in its flag window AND flag depth ≥ −0.05 AND flag depth ≤ maximum flag depth AND flag range ≤ maximum flag range AND flag slope ≥ −0.24 × ATR AND close < candidate pole start − 0.10 × pole size AND flag quality ≥ minimum setup quality AND the candidate has not printed a setup AND setup cooldown elapsed AND no bullish or bearish breakout qualifies.
Short: bearish setup conditions qualify OR (bearish setup conditions qualify AND close < prior flag low − ATR × breakout buffer AND close < open AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume data/average is unavailable OR volume ≥ volume average × minimum volume multiple) AND breakout cooldown elapsed).
