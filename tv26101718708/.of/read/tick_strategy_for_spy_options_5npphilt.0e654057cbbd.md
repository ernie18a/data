Calculation: RSI average = the mean of RSI and its moving average
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the entry-bar stop (low of the preceding bar minus a fraction of higher-timeframe daily ATR, using the last completed daily bar) and the current stop (low of the preceding bar minus a fraction of higher-timeframe daily ATR, using the last completed daily bar)
Calculation: Short stop = the higher of the entry-bar stop (high of the preceding bar plus a fraction of higher-timeframe daily ATR, using the last completed daily bar) and the current stop (high of the preceding bar plus a fraction of higher-timeframe daily ATR, using the last completed daily bar)
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a TICK pivot low, price makes a lower low and TICK makes a higher low than at the preceding TICK pivot low, with the pivots separated by a permitted lookback range
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = at a TICK pivot low, price makes a higher low and TICK makes a lower low than at the preceding TICK pivot low, with the pivots separated by a permitted lookback range
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = at a TICK pivot high, price makes a higher high and TICK makes a lower high than at the preceding TICK pivot high, with the pivots separated by a permitted lookback range
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = at a TICK pivot high, price makes a lower high and TICK makes a higher high than at the preceding TICK pivot high, with the pivots separated by a permitted lookback range
Long: TICK HMA crosses above its EMA smoothing AND RSI average > 50 AND RSI < its upper threshold around 50 AND VIX > its entry threshold AND fast TICK EMA > 0 AND close > price EMA; OR no open trades AND fewer than two bars have elapsed since those TICK, RSI, VIX, and fast TICK EMA conditions were true AND close > price EMA; OR TICK HMA crosses above its EMA smoothing; OR TICK HMA two bars ago >= TICK HMA one bar ago AND current TICK HMA > TICK HMA one bar ago; OR regular bullish divergence; OR hidden bullish divergence
Short: TICK HMA crosses below its EMA smoothing AND RSI average < 50 AND RSI > its lower threshold around 50 AND VIX > its entry threshold AND fast TICK EMA < 0 AND close < price EMA; OR TICK HMA crosses below its EMA smoothing; OR TICK HMA two bars ago <= TICK HMA one bar ago AND current TICK HMA < TICK HMA one bar ago; OR regular bearish divergence; OR hidden bearish divergence
Exit: long, low <= Long stop OR (held longer than the minimum hold AND RSI average is falling AND TICK HMA < its EMA smoothing) OR (held longer than the minimum hold AND regular bearish divergence) OR end-of-day close time
Exit: short, high >= Short stop OR (held longer than the minimum hold AND RSI average is rising AND TICK HMA > its EMA smoothing) OR (held longer than the minimum hold AND regular bullish divergence) OR end-of-day close time
