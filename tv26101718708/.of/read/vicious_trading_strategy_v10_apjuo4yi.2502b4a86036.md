Calculation: Trend score = 16 plus the count of bullish factor conditions minus the count of bearish factor conditions defined in the source.
Calculation: Supertrend direction = +1 when close is above the prior upper trailing band, −1 when close is below the prior lower trailing band, otherwise retain the prior direction; trailing bands use hl2 ± (ATR × multiplier) and carry forward using the source’s recursive rules.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sigma trend = state that turns up when close is above the higher-timeframe basis + standard deviation × tolerance, turns down when close is below the higher-timeframe basis − standard deviation × tolerance, and otherwise retains its prior state. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe sigma trend = state that turns up when close is above the chart-timeframe basis + standard deviation × tolerance, turns down when close is below the chart-timeframe basis − standard deviation × tolerance, and otherwise retains its prior state.
Calculation: Sigma buy-zone entry = prior chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe sigma trends were both up, and the current chart-timeframe trend is down while the higher-timeframe trend remains up.
Calculation: Sigma sell-zone entry = prior chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe sigma trends were both down, and the current chart-timeframe trend is up while the higher-timeframe trend remains down.
Calculation: Sigma trend-up start = current sigma trend is up and its prior value was down; use the higher-timeframe trend when higher-timeframe referencing is enabled, otherwise use the chart-timeframe trend.
Calculation: Sigma trend-down start = current sigma trend is down and its prior value was up; use the higher-timeframe trend when higher-timeframe referencing is enabled, otherwise use the chart-timeframe trend.
Calculation: RSI uptrend condition = 38-period EMA > 62-period EMA AND close > 200-period SMA AND close < 5-period SMA AND 2-period RSI < 10.
Calculation: RSI downtrend condition = close < 200-period SMA AND close > 5-period SMA AND 2-period RSI > 90 AND 38-period EMA < 62-period EMA.
Long: Trend score crosses above 24 OR crosses above 28 OR crosses above its 15-period EMA OR Supertrend direction changes from −1 to +1 OR sigma trend-up start OR sigma buy-zone entry.
Short: Supertrend direction does not change from −1 to +1 AND sigma trend-up start is false AND sigma buy-zone entry is false AND 38-period EMA < 62-period EMA is false AND RSI uptrend condition is false AND ((Trend score < 20 AND Trend score crosses below 19) OR Supertrend direction changes from +1 to −1 OR sigma trend-down start OR sigma sell-zone entry).
Exit: Long, Trend score crosses below 20 OR Supertrend direction changes from +1 to −1 OR RSI downtrend condition OR sigma sell-zone entry.
Exit: Short, Trend score crosses above 19 OR Supertrend direction changes from −1 to +1 OR sigma trend-up start OR sigma buy-zone entry OR 38-period EMA ≥ 62-period EMA OR RSI uptrend condition OR Trend score crosses above its 15-period EMA.
