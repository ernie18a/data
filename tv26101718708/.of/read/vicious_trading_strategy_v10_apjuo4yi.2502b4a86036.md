Calculation: Supertrend bands = midpoint of high and low minus or plus an ATR multiple. The trailing lower band is the greater of its previous value and the current lower band when the previous close is above the previous trailing lower band; otherwise it is the current lower band. The trailing upper band is the lesser of its previous value and the current upper band when the previous close is below the previous trailing upper band; otherwise it is the current upper band. The trend is up when close is above the previous trailing upper band, down when close is below the previous trailing lower band, and otherwise retains its previous state; initialize it as up.
Calculation: Sigma trend = up when the chart-timeframe source is above its selected moving average plus a standard-deviation multiple, down when it is below the moving average minus that multiple, and otherwise retains its previous state. The reference sigma trend uses the chart-timeframe source against a separately parameterized moving average and standard deviation of the higher-timeframe source, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: Supertrend changes from down to up.
Long: The selected sigma trend changes from down to up; select either the chart-timeframe or reference sigma trend.
Long: Chart-timeframe sigma trend changes from up to down AND reference sigma trend remains up.
Short: Supertrend changes from up to down AND Supertrend does not change from down to up AND the selected sigma trend does not change from down to up AND the buy-zone condition is false AND the shorter close EMA is below the longer close EMA AND the RSI uptrend condition is false.
Short: The selected sigma trend changes from up to down AND Supertrend does not change from down to up AND the selected sigma trend does not change from down to up AND the buy-zone condition is false AND the shorter close EMA is below the longer close EMA AND the RSI uptrend condition is false.
Short: Chart-timeframe sigma trend changes from down to up AND reference sigma trend remains down AND Supertrend does not change from down to up AND the selected sigma trend does not change from down to up AND the buy-zone condition is false AND the shorter close EMA is below the longer close EMA AND the RSI uptrend condition is false.
Exit: long, Supertrend changes from up to down.
Exit: long, close is below the longer close SMA AND close is above the shorter close SMA AND source RSI is above its upper threshold AND the shorter close EMA is below the longer close EMA.
Exit: long, chart-timeframe sigma trend changes from down to up AND reference sigma trend remains down.
Exit: short, Supertrend changes from down to up OR the selected sigma trend changes from down to up OR chart-timeframe sigma trend changes from up to down AND reference sigma trend remains up OR the shorter close EMA is greater than or equal to the longer close EMA OR the shorter close EMA is above the longer close EMA AND close is above the longer close SMA AND close is below the shorter close SMA AND source RSI is below its lower threshold.
