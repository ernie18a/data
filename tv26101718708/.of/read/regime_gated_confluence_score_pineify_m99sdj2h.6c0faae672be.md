Calculation: Trend factor = clip to [-1, 1] a weighted sum of (shorter EMA of close - longer EMA of close) / ATR and (shorter EMA - shorter EMA at the slope lookback) / (ATR * sqrt(slope lookback)); the spread has the larger weight. Trend strength = absolute EMA spread / ATR.
Calculation: Momentum factor = clip to [-1, 1] (RSI of close - RSI midpoint) / momentum scaling parameter.
Calculation: Volume factor = clip to [-1, 1] the EMA of volume pulse. Volume is available when the rolling fraction of bars with positive, nonmissing volume meets the minimum coverage and the rolling mean of volume, treating invalid volume as zero, is positive. Volume pulse is zero when volume is unavailable; otherwise it is the clipped close location (2 * close - high - low) / (high - low), or zero for a zero-range bar, multiplied by relative volume clipped between zero and its cap and divided by that cap.
Calculation: Path efficiency = absolute change in close over the regime lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, clipped to [0, 1]. Volatility ratio = ATR / its SMA over the regime lookback.
Calculation: Regime = volatile if volatility ratio reaches the volatility entry threshold, or if the previous regime was volatile and the ratio remains above the lower volatility hold threshold. Otherwise, trend if both trend strength and path efficiency reach their respective trend entry thresholds, or if the previous regime was trend and both reach their respective lower trend hold thresholds. Otherwise, range if trend strength or path efficiency is at or below its respective range threshold; otherwise transition. Reset to transition when required inputs are unavailable. Trend and volatility hold thresholds must not exceed their respective entry thresholds.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of the trend factor, momentum factor and volume factor multiplied by their regime-specific weights, normalized by the sum of active weights. Negate the momentum factor in the range regime. Omit the volume factor and its weight when volume is unavailable. Multiply the sum by a score scaling parameter, an agreement gate and a volatility gate. Agreement is the absolute weighted sum divided by the sum of absolute weighted contributions, or zero when the latter is negligible; clip agreement to [0, 1]. The agreement gate is a positive baseline plus the remaining fraction multiplied by agreement. In the volatile regime, the volatility gate is the volatility entry threshold divided by the volatility ratio, clipped between a positive floor and 1; otherwise it is 1. Calculate the score only when the regime inputs, trend factor and momentum factor are available and the longer EMA length exceeds the shorter EMA length.
Long: On a completed bar, confluence score crosses above the positive directional threshold.
Short: On a completed bar, confluence score crosses below the negative directional threshold.
