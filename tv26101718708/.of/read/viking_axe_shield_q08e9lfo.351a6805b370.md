Calculation: Reclaim = close > shorter-term EMA AND close was <= that EMA a lookback ago, OR close > longer-term EMA AND close was <= that EMA a lookback ago.
Calculation: Loss = close < shorter-term EMA AND close was >= that EMA a lookback ago, OR close < longer-term EMA AND close was >= that EMA a lookback ago. The EMAs and lookback are the same as for Reclaim.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = shorter-term SMA > long-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA was <= long-term SMA a lookback ago, OR shorter-term SMA <= long-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA is rising over a lookback AND their percentage separation is below a threshold, OR close > shorter-term SMA AND close <= long-term SMA AND close is within a percentage threshold of long-term SMA.
Calculation: Bearish breakout = shorter-term SMA <= long-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA was > long-term SMA a lookback ago, OR shorter-term SMA > long-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA is falling over a lookback AND their percentage separation is below a threshold. The SMAs and corresponding lookbacks are the same as for Bullish breakout.
Calculation: Bullish early trend = shortest-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND longer-term EMA > shorter-term SMA AND longer-term EMA was <= shorter-term SMA a lookback ago, OR shortest-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND longer-term EMA <= shorter-term SMA AND longer-term EMA is rising over a lookback AND its percentage separation from shorter-term SMA is below a threshold.
Calculation: Bearish early trend = shortest-term EMA <= longer-term EMA AND longer-term EMA <= shorter-term SMA AND longer-term EMA was > shorter-term SMA a lookback ago. The averages and comparison lookback are the same as for Bullish early trend.
Calculation: Bullish continuation = shortest-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND longer-term EMA > shorter-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA > long-term SMA, OR close > longer-term EMA AND the percentage distance from close to longer-term EMA is between a lower and an upper threshold. The averages are the same as for the preceding calculations.
Calculation: Bearish continuation = shortest-term EMA < longer-term EMA AND longer-term EMA < shorter-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA < long-term SMA.
Long: Reclaim = true OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = true) OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = false AND Bearish breakout = false AND Bullish early trend = true) OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = false AND Bearish breakout = false AND Bullish early trend = false AND Bearish early trend = false AND Bullish continuation = true).
Short: Reclaim = false AND Loss = true OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = false AND Bearish breakout = true) OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = false AND Bearish breakout = false AND Bullish early trend = false AND Bearish early trend = true) OR (Reclaim = false AND Loss = false AND Bullish breakout = false AND Bearish breakout = false AND Bullish early trend = false AND Bearish early trend = false AND Bullish continuation = false AND Bearish continuation = true).
