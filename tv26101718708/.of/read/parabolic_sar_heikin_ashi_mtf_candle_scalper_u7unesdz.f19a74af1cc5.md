Calculation: Parabolic SAR = Parabolic SAR calculated on chart bars from higher-timeframe Heikin Ashi OHLC
Long: previous chart bar’s higher-timeframe Heikin Ashi close > current Parabolic SAR AND current bar is within the entry session
Short: previous chart bar’s higher-timeframe Heikin Ashi close < current Parabolic SAR AND current bar is within the entry session
