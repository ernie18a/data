Calculation: Phase volume = volume when smoothing length is one; otherwise the simple moving average of volume over the smoothing length.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low at a bar that is respectively at least as high or at most as low as every high or low in the specified number of preceding bars, and strictly higher or lower than every high or low in the specified number of following bars. Recognize it on the last following bar. For each side, compare with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of that side whose comparison volume was available, regardless of whether it produced a signal.
Calculation: Pivot comparison volume = either volume at the pivot bar or the simple moving average of volume ending at the pivot bar.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its low is below the previous pivot low AND (previous pivot low - current pivot low) / previous pivot low meets or exceeds the minimum price difference AND its comparison volume is below the previous pivot's comparison volume AND (previous pivot volume - current pivot volume) / previous pivot volume meets or exceeds the minimum volume difference AND the distance between pivot bars is positive and no greater than the maximum pivot distance.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its high is above the previous pivot high AND (current pivot high - previous pivot high) / previous pivot high meets or exceeds the minimum price difference AND its comparison volume is below the previous pivot's comparison volume AND (previous pivot volume - current pivot volume) / previous pivot volume meets or exceeds the minimum volume difference AND the distance between pivot bars is positive and no greater than the maximum pivot distance.
Long: The close and phase-volume lookback references are available AND (close >= close at the price lookback OR (close < close at the price lookback AND phase volume < phase volume at the volume lookback)).
Short: The close and phase-volume lookback references are available AND (close < close at the price lookback AND phase volume >= phase volume at the volume lookback OR close >= close at the price lookback AND phase volume < phase volume at the volume lookback).
Long: Either lookback reference is unavailable AND (close >= open OR (close < open AND previous volume is available AND volume < previous volume)).
Short: Either lookback reference is unavailable AND ((close < open AND (previous volume is unavailable OR volume >= previous volume)) OR (close >= open AND previous volume is available AND volume < previous volume)).
Long: Close >= exponential moving average of close.
Short: Close < exponential moving average of close.
