Calculation: Watched levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; maintain separate high and low lists. On confirmation, add a level only if its distance from every existing level of the same kind is at least an ATR multiple, using ATR on the confirmation bar. Process additions before sweeps and expiry. On each bar, remove every level satisfying its sweep condition; otherwise remove it when its age from the pivot bar exceeds the maximum age. Check sweeps before expiry.
Calculation: High sweep = high > a watched high AND close < that level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR (body > 0 AND upper wick / body >= wick multiplier) OR (body = 0 AND upper wick > 0)); body = abs(close - open), upper wick = high - max(open, close).
Calculation: Low sweep = low < a watched low AND close > that level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR (body > 0 AND lower wick / body >= wick multiplier) OR (body = 0 AND lower wick > 0)); body = abs(close - open), lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Pending sweep = while flat and confirmation is enabled, first evaluate and clear the existing pending directions, then store the current bar's sweeps for the next evaluation while flat. Store one shared wick and midpoint: process the low sweep first and the high sweep second, so a simultaneous high sweep overwrites the shared values; midpoint = (stored wick + sweep-bar close) / 2. A pending sweep remains unchanged while a position is open.
Calculation: Selected wick = the current sweep bar's low for a low sweep or high for a high sweep when confirmation is disabled; otherwise the pending sweep's shared wick. If both directions qualify on the same bar, use the short candidate's selected wick for the long candidate and give the long candidate priority.
Calculation: Initial stop = selected wick minus an ATR multiple for a long candidate, or selected wick plus an ATR multiple for a short candidate; use ATR on the decision bar. Risk = decision-bar close minus initial stop for a long candidate, or initial stop minus decision-bar close for a short candidate. Target = decision-bar close plus risk × reward-to-risk multiplier for a long, or decision-bar close minus risk × reward-to-risk multiplier for a short. Fix these levels at the decision bar; submit a market entry for the next available tick.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until breakeven is enabled and, on a completed bar with an open position, the long high reaches the decision-bar close plus a configured fraction of the distance to target, or the short low reaches the decision-bar close minus that fraction of the distance to target; thereafter use the decision-bar close as the stop, effective on the next available tick.
Long: Position is flat AND longs are enabled AND (session filter disabled OR decision bar is in the configured exchange-time session) AND ((confirmation disabled AND low sweep) OR (confirmation enabled AND pending low sweep AND close > pending midpoint)) AND risk >= ATR × minimum-risk multiplier.
Short: Position is flat AND shorts are enabled AND (session filter disabled OR decision bar is in the configured exchange-time session) AND ((confirmation disabled AND high sweep) OR (confirmation enabled AND pending high sweep AND close < pending midpoint)) AND no long candidate qualifies before the risk check AND risk >= ATR × minimum-risk multiplier.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= target.
