Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × sign × rate, where sign is +1 if close >= open and -1 otherwise; rate is 0.5 × (upper wick + lower wick + 2 × absolute body) / (upper wick + lower wick + absolute body), or 0.5 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative-delta oscillator = cumulative sum of estimated delta; optionally use the close of Heikin-Ashi candles constructed from raw cumulative-delta candles whose open is the previous cumulative sum, close is the current cumulative sum, high is their maximum, and low is their minimum. Heikin-Ashi close is the mean of those four values; Heikin-Ashi open is the mean of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, initialized to the mean of the raw open and close.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot = a confirmed pivot high or low of the oscillator using configurable left and right lookbacks; evaluate it on the confirmation bar and pair its oscillator value with the price high or low on the oscillator pivot bar. Compare consecutive pivots of the same kind; price itself need not form a pivot.
Calculation: Divergence strength = min(100, 50 × (absolute price change / absolute previous pivot price + absolute oscillator change / absolute previous oscillator pivot)); use zero for either term whose denominator is zero.
Calculation: Divergence grade = grade the boosted composite score against configurable upper and lower thresholds. Boosted score is 100 × (base score / 100) raised to a configurable power. Base score is the raw score, or optionally clamp(50 + 50 × (raw score - rolling mean of raw score) / max(rolling standard deviation of raw score × adaptive multiplier, adaptive floor), 0, 100). Use the current bar in rolling calculations and substitute the current raw score for an unavailable mean and zero for an unavailable standard deviation.
Calculation: Chart divergence raw score = normalized weighted average of strength, delta momentum, volume, trend, higher-timeframe agreement, and pivot-spacing scores. Each component is scaled from 0 to 100. Strength score is 100 × clamp(strength / max(SMA of strength, minimum strength) × strength normalization factor, 0, 1), with unavailable SMA replaced by zero. Delta momentum is the change over a configurable lag in EMA of estimated delta, evaluated at the pivot; its score is 100 × clamp((1 + directional momentum / max(SMA of absolute momentum, positive floor)) / 2, 0, 1), with momentum and its scale evaluated at the pivot and direction positive for bullish and negative for bearish. Volume score at the pivot is 100 × clamp(volume / max(SMA of volume, positive floor) / volume normalization factor, 0, 1). Bullish trend score is 100 if pivot close > slower EMA of close AND faster EMA >= slower EMA, otherwise zero; bearish trend score is 100 if pivot close < slower EMA AND faster EMA <= slower EMA, otherwise zero. Higher-timeframe agreement is 100 if a same-side qualifying higher-timeframe divergence occurs on the current chart bar, zero otherwise, or a neutral score if higher-timeframe analysis is disabled. Pivot-spacing score is 100 × clamp(bars between the two oscillator pivots / (spacing factor × (left pivot lookback + right pivot lookback)), 0, 1). Calculate the raw score on every chart bar, separately for each side, using the latest applicable pivot pair and the candidate pivot offset for price, momentum, volume, and trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe divergence raw score = normalized weighted average of strength, trend, and volume scores. Compute the oscillator and pivots on higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and emit a candidate only on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar. Strength is normalized as for chart divergence using the rolling average of higher-timeframe pivot-event strength values sampled on chart bars. At the oscillator pivot, bullish trend score is 100 if close > EMA of close, otherwise zero; bearish trend score is 100 if close < EMA, otherwise zero. Volume score uses the chart divergence volume-score formula at the higher-timeframe pivot. Compute the higher-timeframe raw score and its adaptive grade on chart bars with independent grade thresholds.
Long: A chart-timeframe oscillator low pivot is confirmed AND its price low < the preceding oscillator-low pivot's price low AND its oscillator value > the preceding oscillator-low pivot's oscillator value AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND chart divergence grade >= the selected minimum grade AND bars since the previous qualifying regular bullish chart divergence >= the regular-divergence cooldown.
Long: A chart-timeframe oscillator low pivot is confirmed AND its price low > the preceding oscillator-low pivot's price low AND its oscillator value < the preceding oscillator-low pivot's oscillator value AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND chart divergence grade >= the selected minimum grade AND bars since the previous qualifying hidden bullish chart divergence >= the hidden-divergence cooldown.
Short: A chart-timeframe oscillator high pivot is confirmed AND its price high > the preceding oscillator-high pivot's price high AND its oscillator value < the preceding oscillator-high pivot's oscillator value AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND chart divergence grade >= the selected minimum grade AND bars since the previous qualifying regular bearish chart divergence >= the regular-divergence cooldown.
Short: A chart-timeframe oscillator high pivot is confirmed AND its price high < the preceding oscillator-high pivot's price high AND its oscillator value > the preceding oscillator-high pivot's oscillator value AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND chart divergence grade >= the selected minimum grade AND bars since the previous qualifying hidden bearish chart divergence >= the hidden-divergence cooldown.
Long: On the first chart bar after a completed higher-timeframe bar, a higher-timeframe oscillator low pivot is confirmed AND ((its price low < the preceding oscillator-low pivot's price low AND its oscillator value > the preceding oscillator-low pivot's oscillator value) OR (its price low > the preceding oscillator-low pivot's price low AND its oscillator value < the preceding oscillator-low pivot's oscillator value)) AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND higher-timeframe divergence grade >= its selected minimum grade AND chart bars since the previous qualifying bullish higher-timeframe divergence >= the higher-timeframe cooldown.
Short: On the first chart bar after a completed higher-timeframe bar, a higher-timeframe oscillator high pivot is confirmed AND ((its price high > the preceding oscillator-high pivot's price high AND its oscillator value < the preceding oscillator-high pivot's oscillator value) OR (its price high < the preceding oscillator-high pivot's price high AND its oscillator value > the preceding oscillator-high pivot's oscillator value)) AND optional minimum divergence strength is met AND higher-timeframe divergence grade >= its selected minimum grade AND chart bars since the previous qualifying bearish higher-timeframe divergence >= the higher-timeframe cooldown.
Long: Estimated delta < -SMA of absolute estimated delta × delta multiplier AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND max(high - low, positive range floor) <= SMA of high - low × range multiplier AND absolute body / max(high - low, positive range floor) <= maximum body ratio AND, if opposite-close filtering is enabled, close >= open AND bars since previous bullish absorption >= absorption cooldown AND, if divergence confluence is enabled, bars since a confirmed bullish chart divergence with optional strength filter and divergence grade >= the absorption minimum grade <= confluence lookback; the confluence divergence ignores divergence display toggles, the main grade filter, and divergence cooldown.
Short: Estimated delta > SMA of absolute estimated delta × delta multiplier AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND max(high - low, positive range floor) <= SMA of high - low × range multiplier AND absolute body / max(high - low, positive range floor) <= maximum body ratio AND, if opposite-close filtering is enabled, close <= open AND bars since previous bearish absorption >= absorption cooldown AND, if divergence confluence is enabled, bars since a confirmed bearish chart divergence with optional strength filter and divergence grade >= the absorption minimum grade <= confluence lookback; the confluence divergence ignores divergence display toggles, the main grade filter, and divergence cooldown.

Short: Oscillator Z-score > positive exhaustion threshold.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price × (1 - stop fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + target fraction); if both occur on the same bar, the stop is first.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= entry price × (1 + stop fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 - target fraction); if both occur on the same bar, the stop is first.
