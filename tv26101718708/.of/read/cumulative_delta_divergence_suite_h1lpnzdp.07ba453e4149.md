Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × 0.5 × (upper wick + lower wick + 2 × candle body) ÷ (upper wick + lower wick + candle body), positive when close ≥ open and negative otherwise.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of estimated delta; CDV oscillator = cumulative-delta close, optionally transformed into Heikin-Ashi candles.
