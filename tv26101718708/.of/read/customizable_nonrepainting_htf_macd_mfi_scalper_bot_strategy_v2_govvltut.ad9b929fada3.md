Calculation: Higher-timeframe input = last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars; on a still-forming realtime chart bar use the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Money flow index = 100 - 100 / (1 + positive flow / negative flow), where positive and negative flows are rolling sums over chart bars of higher-timeframe typical price multiplied by higher-timeframe volume, included respectively when higher-timeframe typical price rises or falls from its preceding chart-bar value.
Calculation: ATR filter = Wilder smoothing over chart bars of max(higher-timeframe high - higher-timeframe low, abs(higher-timeframe high - previous chart bar's higher-timeframe close), abs(higher-timeframe low - previous chart bar's higher-timeframe close)); initialize with the first available true range.
Calculation: Moving-average filter = selected moving average of close calculated on the filter timeframe; on historical bars its completed higher-timeframe value is made available from the start of that timeframe bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe close EMA > higher-timeframe close SMA, both calculated over chart bars with the same lookback AND (MACD crosses above its signal line OR money flow index crosses above its lower threshold) AND ATR filter > minimum ATR AND (moving-average filter disabled OR chart close >= moving-average filter) AND (RSI filter disabled OR higher-timeframe RSI >= lower RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe RSI <= upper RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe RSI > its previous chart-bar value) AND datetime is within the configured backtest period. Calculate MACD and its signal over chart bars from the higher-timeframe source, using a shorter and a longer moving-average lookback; each oscillator and signal moving-average type is independently SMA or EMA.
Long: Money flow highlighting enabled AND money flow index < lower threshold.
Short: Money flow highlighting enabled AND money flow index > upper threshold.
Exit: long, close < high of the most recent chart bar satisfying the unfiltered entry signal minus an ATR multiple OR close < entry price multiplied by one minus the fixed loss fraction, within the configured backtest period. The unfiltered entry signal is the entry condition before the outer EMA-versus-SMA trend condition and date restriction.
Exit: long, after close > entry price multiplied by one plus the profit fraction within the configured backtest period, a resting profit order is submitted: if trailing is disabled, exit when price reaches the profit target fixed from the signal-bar close; if enabled, activate at that target and exit when price falls from the highest price since activation by the target multiplied by the trailing deviation fraction.
Exit: long, after close > entry price multiplied by one plus the profit fraction within the configured backtest period, a second trailing order is submitted; activate when price rises above entry price by a configured multiple of the high of the most recent chart bar satisfying the unfiltered entry signal, and exit when price falls from the highest price since activation by the same amount.
