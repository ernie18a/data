Calculation: Consolidation = When no consolidation is active, examine a window of the minimum number of bars. The oldest bar is the base; its high and low are the fixed upper and lower boundaries. Start a consolidation if every bar's open and close lie within the boundaries plus or minus a margin equal to a fraction of current ATR, and, if the optional size filter is enabled, no bar's high-low range exceeds a multiple of its preceding bar's ATR; an unavailable or nonpositive preceding ATR does not reject that bar. Starting and breakout cannot occur on the same bar. On subsequent bars, end the consolidation on the first close outside either boundary. If no breakout occurs, end it when the elapsed bar count from the base reaches the optional maximum; a breakout on that bar takes precedence. Search for a new consolidation starting on the next bar.
Calculation: Directional score = Sum independently configurable weights for close above the trend EMA, the separate slope EMA above its previous value, and RSI above its neutral midpoint for long; use the corresponding below comparisons for short. The two EMA periods are independently configurable.
Calculation: Breakout score = Directional score plus independently configurable weights for each true condition: absolute candle body greater than a multiple of its SMA; ADX greater than its threshold; volume greater than a multiple of its SMA; ATR positive AND consolidation width divided by ATR below its threshold; elapsed bars from the base greater than or equal to the minimum consolidation length plus a configurable duration premium. Body and volume SMAs include the current bar. ADX uses the same period for directional movement and smoothing. The consolidation ATR is shared by the size filter, margin, and tightness calculation; the tester ATR has an independent period.
Long: Close above the upper boundary of an active consolidation AND long breakout score greater than or equal to the minimum score.
Short: Close below the lower boundary of an active consolidation AND short breakout score greater than or equal to the minimum score.
Long: Close above the upper boundary of an active consolidation AND the optional removal of boxes without a qualifying signal is disabled.
Short: Close below the lower boundary of an active consolidation AND the optional removal of boxes without a qualifying signal is disabled.
Long: Close above the trend EMA OR the slope EMA above its previous value OR RSI above its neutral midpoint.
Short: Close below the trend EMA OR the slope EMA below its previous value OR RSI below its neutral midpoint.
Long: Long directional score greater than short directional score AND (absolute candle body greater than a multiple of its SMA OR ADX greater than its threshold OR volume greater than a multiple of its SMA OR an active consolidation has positive ATR AND width divided by ATR below its threshold OR an active consolidation has elapsed bars from its base greater than or equal to the minimum consolidation length plus the duration premium).
Short: Short directional score greater than long directional score AND (absolute candle body greater than a multiple of its SMA OR ADX greater than its threshold OR volume greater than a multiple of its SMA OR an active consolidation has positive ATR AND width divided by ATR below its threshold OR an active consolidation has elapsed bars from its base greater than or equal to the minimum consolidation length plus the duration premium).
Calculation: Tester position = Open at the bar's open after a qualifying breakout signal on the preceding bar, using the tester ATR from that signal bar. Optionally block entry while any tester position remains open, and optionally block entry if the distance from the current open to any open entry of the same side is less than a multiple of that ATR. Process exits before entries. Fix take-profit and stop-loss levels at entry price plus or minus their respective multiples of that ATR; reverse the signs for shorts. Evaluate exits only after the entry bar, with stop-loss taking priority if both levels are touched.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low less than or equal to the fixed stop-loss OR high greater than or equal to the fixed take-profit OR bars elapsed since entry greater than the maximum holding period).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high greater than or equal to the fixed stop-loss OR low less than or equal to the fixed take-profit OR bars elapsed since entry greater than the maximum holding period).
