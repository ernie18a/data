Calculation: Liquidity pools = Asia and London session highs and lows, previous completed day and week highs and lows, and confirmed swing highs and lows within an ATR-based tolerance of an earlier confirmed swing of the same type
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, with gap size at least the minimum; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, with gap size at least the minimum
Calculation: Pool raid = price trades beyond an untouched pool and closes back inside within the reclaim window; track the most extreme price beyond the pool during that window
Calculation: Bearish MSS level = the greater of the last confirmed swing low and the lowest low over the lookback bars before the raid bar; bullish MSS level = the lesser of the last confirmed swing high and the highest high over those bars
Calculation: Entry price = the selected edge of the post-raid FVG or its midpoint
Calculation: Stop = beyond the most extreme raid price by the tick buffer; target = nearest untouched opposite-side pool that meets the minimum reward-to-risk, or the configured fixed reward-to-risk target if none qualifies
Long: close > last unbroken confirmed swing high OR sell-side pool raid during the NY AM window AND higher-timeframe bias allows longs OR bullish MSS (close > bullish MSS level AND close > open AND candle body ≥ ATR multiple) after a sell-side pool raid during the NY AM window AND higher-timeframe bias allows longs OR bullish FVG formed on or after the raid within the permitted MSS-to-FVG window AND a working buy limit is touched (low ≤ entry price) on a later bar, while within the NY AM window, below the daily trade limit, with no open position, and before order expiry or invalidation
Short: close < last unbroken confirmed swing low OR buy-side pool raid during the NY AM window AND higher-timeframe bias allows shorts OR bearish MSS (close < bearish MSS level AND close < open AND candle body ≥ ATR multiple) after a buy-side pool raid during the NY AM window AND higher-timeframe bias allows shorts OR bearish FVG formed on or after the raid within the permitted MSS-to-FVG window AND a working sell limit is touched (high ≥ entry price) on a later bar, while within the NY AM window, below the daily trade limit, with no open position, and before order expiry or invalidation
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target OR within the configured end-of-day flatten window
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target OR within the configured end-of-day flatten window
