Calculation: Liquidity pools = Enabled high and low pools from each completed configured session; the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe day and week, added at the start of the next respective period; and equal confirmed pivot highs or lows. Confirm pivots using equal numbers of bars on both sides. Add an equal-high pool at the higher of two successive confirmed pivot highs, or an equal-low pool at the lower of two successive confirmed pivot lows, when their difference is at most an ATR multiple and the earlier pivot has not been crossed by a close. Retain only the most recently created pools up to a configured limit. A pool can be breached only after its creation bar. Remove an unbreached pool after a configured age; remove a breached pool when reclaimed or when its reclaim window expires. Pending breached pools remain eligible as targets.
Calculation: Raid = A high pool is breached when high exceeds it and reclaimed when close is below it; a low pool is breached when low is below it and reclaimed when close is above it. Reclaim may occur on the breach bar or within a configured number of subsequent bars; on the last permitted bar, test reclaim before expiry. Record the highest high or lowest low from breach through reclaim. If multiple pools are reclaimed on the same bar, process them in creation order and combine their extremes in that order, using the high-pool maximum or low-pool minimum for each pool processed.
Calculation: Bullish gap = Low exceeds the high two bars earlier AND the difference is at least a configured price increment; its lower boundary is that earlier high and its upper boundary is the current low. Bearish gap = High is below the low two bars earlier AND the difference is at least a configured price increment; its lower boundary is the current high and its upper boundary is that earlier low.
Calculation: Structure = The latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low are each eligible until close crosses them or a newer pivot of the same kind is confirmed. A close above an eligible pivot high sets structure bullish; a close below an eligible pivot low sets structure bearish. If both occur on one bar, process the high first and the low second.
Calculation: Setup = Maintain separate long and short states, processed short first. During the configured trade window, a low-pool raid starts or replaces a long setup waiting for a shift; a high-pool raid starts or replaces a short setup waiting for a shift. A setup already waiting for a gap or with a working order cannot be replaced. If enabled, require the last completed higher-timeframe close to exceed its EMA for a long setup, or to be at or below its EMA for a short setup. At the raid bar, the long shift reference is the lower of the latest confirmed pivot high and the highest high over a configured lookback ending on the preceding bar; the short reference is the higher of the latest confirmed pivot low and the lowest low over that lookback. Use the lookback value alone if the pivot is unavailable. The lookback value must exist. Track the lowest low for a long setup or highest high for a short setup from the raid extreme through order placement. Within a configured number of bars after the raid, a long shift requires close above the reference, close above open, and candle body at least a configured ATR multiple; a short shift requires close below the reference, close below open, and the same body requirement. While waiting for the shift or a gap, retain the latest same-direction gap whose first candle is at or after the raid bar. Once shifted, place an order immediately if such a gap exists, including a gap found before or on the shift bar; otherwise wait a configured number of bars after the shift for one. Cancel a setup still waiting for a shift or gap when the trade window ends.
Calculation: Order = At placement, use the bullish gap upper boundary or bearish gap lower boundary, or alternatively the gap midpoint, as the limit price. Set the long stop below the tracked low and the short stop above the tracked high by a configured price buffer. Require positive entry-to-stop risk, no open position, and fewer than the configured maximum fills since the trade window began; otherwise discard the setup. Set the target to a configured risk multiple from entry, or to the nearest eligible opposite-side pool beyond entry whose distance from entry is at least a configured minimum risk multiple; if no such pool exists, use the fixed-risk target. Freeze entry, stop and target at placement. Starting on the next bar, fill a long order when low is at or below entry, or a short order when high is at or above entry, provided no position is open. Test fill before cancellation. If unfilled, cancel when the order age exceeds its configured expiry, the close is beyond the stop, the trade window has ended, or a position is open. Process short orders before long orders.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close <= its EMA.
Long: Close > an eligible confirmed pivot high.
Short: Close < an eligible confirmed pivot low.
Long: Bullish gap conditions are met AND gap display is enabled.
Short: Bearish gap conditions are met AND gap display is enabled.
Long: A low-pool raid is reclaimed during the trade window AND the long setup may start under the setup conditions.
Short: A high-pool raid is reclaimed during the trade window AND the short setup may start under the setup conditions.
Long: A long setup meets its shift conditions.
Short: A short setup meets its shift conditions.
Long: A long limit order is placed under the order conditions.
Short: A short limit order is placed under the order conditions.
Long: A long limit order fills under the order conditions.
Short: A short limit order fills under the order conditions.
Exit: Long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= target OR bar is in the configured flatten window).
Exit: Short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= target OR bar is in the configured flatten window).
Calculation: Exit price = Stop if the stop is reached, otherwise target if the target is reached, otherwise close; test the stop before the target when both are reached on the same bar.
