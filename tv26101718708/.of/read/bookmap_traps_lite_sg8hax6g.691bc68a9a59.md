Calculation: Lower-timeframe adjusted volume = (lower-timeframe volume / lower-timeframe range) × clamp((volume / range) / its 20-bar lower-timeframe SMA, 0.5, 2.5); use lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including a still-forming intrabar when available.
Calculation: Upper-wick trap volume = sum over lower-timeframe bars of adjusted volume × overlap with the chart bar’s upper wick / lower-timeframe range × clamp((lower-timeframe close − low) / range, 0.05, 0.95); require the upper wick to be at least the configured percentage of chart-bar range, and, when deep-wick mode is on, count only overlap midpoints in the outer half of the wick.
Calculation: Lower-wick trap volume = sum over lower-timeframe bars of adjusted volume × overlap with the chart bar’s lower wick / lower-timeframe range × (1 − clamp((lower-timeframe close − low) / range, 0.05, 0.95)); require the lower wick to be at least the configured percentage of chart-bar range, and, when deep-wick mode is on, count only overlap midpoints in the outer half of the wick.
Calculation: ML-adjusted trap volume = trap volume × 2 × the success fraction among the nearest historical trap patterns, when ML scoring is enabled and at least the configured minimum number of similar samples exists; otherwise use trap volume. Patterns are nearest by squared distance across normalized chart-bar body/ATR, upper-wick/range, lower-wick/range, close-position/range, range/ATR, upper-wick trap volume, and lower-wick trap volume. Use the configured rolling training window and neighbor count; label prior upper-wick traps successful when the next chart bar closes below the trap bar’s close, and prior lower-wick traps successful when it closes above.
Long: ML-adjusted lower-wick trap volume ≥ configured minimum trap volume.
Short: ML-adjusted upper-wick trap volume ≥ configured minimum trap volume.
