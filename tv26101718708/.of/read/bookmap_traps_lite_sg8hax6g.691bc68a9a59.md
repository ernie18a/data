Calculation: Wick trap volume = For a chart bar with positive range and a wick at least the minimum fraction of its range, sum contributions from all available completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the latest completed lower-timeframe bar while the chart bar is forming. For each lower-timeframe bar, let density = volume / (high - low), or 0 if its range is zero; ignore nonpositive density. Multiply density by density / its lower-timeframe SMA, clamped to the configured minimum and maximum, or by 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Multiply by the fraction of the lower-timeframe bar's range overlapping the chart bar's wick and by the directional strength: clamp (lower-timeframe close - low) / (high - low) to the configured bounds for the upper wick, and use 1 minus that strength for the lower wick. If deep-wick filtering is enabled, include an overlap only when its midpoint is strictly beyond the wick midpoint toward the extremity.
Calculation: Adjusted trap volume = When ML scoring is disabled or insufficient samples exist, use wick trap volume. Otherwise multiply it by twice the success rate among the nearest eligible historical traps of the same wick side. Use up to the configured number of neighbors and require the configured minimum number of both training bars and eligible neighbors. The training window contains the most recent configured number of chart bars with available features, each labeled on the following bar: an upper-wick trap succeeds if the following close is below its close; a lower-wick trap succeeds if the following close is above its close; equal closes fail. A historical bar is eligible for a side if its unadjusted trap volume for that side was positive. Rank by squared Euclidean distance across signed body / ATR, upper wick / range, lower wick / range, (close - low) / range, range / ATR, upper-wick trap volume, and lower-wick trap volume; divide each feature difference by that feature's training-window range, floored at a small positive constant. For zero chart-bar range use 0 for wick ratios and 0.5 for close position; use 1 when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Training begins after the initial chart bars, with each feature vector added only after its following bar is available.
Calculation: Display threshold = The linear-interpolation percentile corresponding to the configured top fraction of adjusted trap volumes in the latest bounded cache of qualifying traps from both wick sides. Add both current-bar traps that meet the minimum volume before calculating the threshold, insert the upper-wick trap first, and discard the oldest entries when the cache exceeds its capacity. Recalculate the threshold on every chart bar; historical markings can consequently change visibility.
Long: The chart bar is within the configured lookback from the dataset's last bar AND lower-wick adjusted trap volume >= minimum trap volume AND lower-wick adjusted trap volume >= display threshold.
Short: The chart bar is within the configured lookback from the dataset's last bar AND upper-wick adjusted trap volume >= minimum trap volume AND upper-wick adjusted trap volume >= display threshold.
