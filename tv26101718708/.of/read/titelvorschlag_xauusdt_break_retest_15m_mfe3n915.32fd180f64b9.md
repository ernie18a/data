Calculation: Levels = Previous UTC day's high and low, calculated from chart bars in the last completed higher-timeframe daily period; and the current UTC day's high and low accumulated from chart bars during a configurable UTC range beginning at midnight, available only after that range ends. Each source can be enabled or disabled. A range level is inactive when its distance from the corresponding previous-day level divided by the range level is below a configurable threshold and the previous-day source is enabled. Range levels are also inactive at or after the configured session end. Each level becomes inactive when selected for a break and is reactivated at the start of the next UTC day.
Calculation: Break = On a confirmed chart bar, while flat and without a pending setup, select the active high level for which close > level + break multiplier × ATR, or active low level for which close < level - break multiplier × ATR, with the smallest absolute distance from close. Resolve equal distances in this order: previous-day high, range high, previous-day low, range low. Store the selected level, direction, bar index, and ATR as a pending setup and consume the selected level. A high-level break has long direction; a low-level break has short direction.
Calculation: Pending setup = A setup expires when the number of bars since its break exceeds the retest window. Starting on the bar after the break, a long setup is touched when low <= its stored level; a short setup is touched when high >= its stored level. The first touch consumes the setup even if it is outside the session or fails the fee filter. On a bar without a touch, cancel a long setup when close < stored level - invalidation multiplier × stored ATR, or a short setup when close > stored level + invalidation multiplier × stored ATR. Preserve pending setups across UTC day changes. Do not search for a new break on a bar that begins with a position or pending setup, even if it exits or is cancelled on that bar.
Calculation: Risk distance = Stop multiplier × ATR stored at the selected break. Entry price = stored level. Round-trip fee in risk units = (entry fee rate + exit fee rate) × entry price / risk distance.
Long: Selected break direction = long.
Short: Selected break direction = short.
Long: Pending setup direction = long AND bars since break > 0 AND bars since break <= retest window AND low <= stored level AND bar opening time in UTC >= session start AND bar opening time in UTC < session end AND round-trip fee in risk units <= maximum fee threshold.
Short: Pending setup direction = short AND bars since break > 0 AND bars since break <= retest window AND high >= stored level AND bar opening time in UTC >= session start AND bar opening time in UTC < session end AND round-trip fee in risk units <= maximum fee threshold.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - risk distance OR (bar index > entry bar index AND high >= entry price + take-profit multiplier × risk distance).
Exit: short, high >= entry price + risk distance OR (bar index > entry bar index AND low <= entry price - take-profit multiplier × risk distance).
