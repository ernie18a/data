Calculation: OR high / OR low = highest high / lowest low of the bars in a fixed-length opening period at the start of the session, locked once that period completes
Long: (breakout) no breakout has occurred yet today AND the bar is after the opening period completes and before a later cutoff time AND close > OR high AND previous close <= OR high; OR (retest) a long breakout occurred earlier today or on this bar AND no retest has fired since that breakout AND low <= OR high AND low >= OR low (fires once per day, no time cutoff, may occur on the breakout bar itself)
Short: (breakout) no breakout has occurred yet today AND the bar is after the opening period completes and before a later cutoff time AND close < OR low AND previous close >= OR low; OR (retest) a short breakout occurred earlier today or on this bar AND no retest has fired since that breakout AND high >= OR low AND high <= OR high (fires once per day, no time cutoff, may occur on the breakout bar itself)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target distance (two targets, the second distance larger than the first; entry price = close of the breakout bar)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - target distance (two targets, the second distance larger than the first; entry price = close of the breakout bar)
