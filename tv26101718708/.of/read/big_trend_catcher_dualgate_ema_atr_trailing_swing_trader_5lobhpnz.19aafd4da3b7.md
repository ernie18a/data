Long: (a basic impulse occurred within the configured bar limit and has not since been invalidated by close crossing below the short-term EMA) AND close > long-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND flat position, where a basic impulse is (close crossing above the short-term EMA OR open > previous bar high) AND volume > its simple moving average × a configured factor OR (long position AND close is above entry price by more than a configured gain threshold AND close > long-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND open trade count < configured limit) OR (long position AND low crosses below the short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA)
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = highest(high − configured ATR multiple × ATR, over a configured lookback) when close > previous stop, ratcheted upward to at least the previous stop; otherwise low − configured ATR multiple × ATR
Exit: long, close < ATR trailing stop OR close crosses below long-term EMA
