Evaluate on confirmed underlying bars by default, using completed daily data for ATR, moving averages, liquidity levels, and realized volatility. Buy calls for bullish signals and puts for bearish signals; hold at most one position. Treat all lookbacks, thresholds, score weights, windows, buffers, and risk multiples as parameters.

Build daily trend from the ordering of fast and slow exponential averages and the slow average's direction. Track confirmed swing highs and lows, prior internal highs and lows, session VWAP, daily open, time-of-day normalized relative volume, directional candle displacement, and whether daily realized volatility is expanding. Calculate expected-move bands around the daily open from realized volatility. Compare the volatility index with its own Bollinger upper band and middle line.

Reversal: Arm a bullish setup when a bar penetrates and closes back above a prior major daily low, tactical daily low, or lower expected-move band; mirror for bearish setups. If both sides sweep, use the candle direction to choose a side. Store the sweep extreme and level types, replace any opposite armed setup, and expire or invalidate the setup after its confirmation window or a buffered close beyond the stored extreme. Starting on the next bar, score the setup using sweep significance and confluence, daily trend alignment, a directional swing or internal structure break, close beyond the daily open or session VWAP, strong relative volume, directional displacement, expanding realized volatility, and the volatility-index modifier: above its upper band for calls or below its middle line for puts. Require the applicable score threshold, with a higher threshold and optional major-or-confluent-sweep requirement when countertrend; optionally require a swing break. Trigger only when validity first becomes true.

Continuation: Require price on the directional side of a rising or falling daily fast average, a fresh close across the prior continuation-window extreme, a same-direction swing or internal structure break, and limited extension from the fast average. Score the regime, directional swing sequence and breaks, displacement, strong volume, expanding realized volatility, and, for calls only, a volatility index below its middle band. Trigger when the score reaches its threshold and validity first becomes true.

Transition: Arm on a close crossing the daily fast average, cancel the opposite arm and qualification, and expire the arm after its window. Qualify while armed when price remains on the correct side of the fast average, its slope is not excessively adverse, the opposing daily trend is absent, a directional structure break occurred recently, extension is limited, and a score for regime, recent structure, displacement, volume, and volatility expansion reaches its threshold. Retain qualification for a separate execution window; during that window it can trigger whenever transition risk is valid, even if qualification conditions no longer hold.

When flat and past the post-exit cooldown, route each direction in reversal, transition, then continuation priority. A higher-priority raw signal suppresses lower-priority routes even if its own risk is invalid; calls take precedence if both directions can enter. Reversal stops lie beyond the saved sweep extreme with an ATR buffer. Continuation stops use the tighter of the buffered breakout level and the latest opposite swing. Transition stops use the tightest of the buffered transition-window extreme, fast daily average, and latest opposite swing. Reject an entry unless its stop is on the correct side of the close and initial risk is positive and within the ATR risk cap. Record entry, stop, risk, target, session, and running favorable extreme; clear the selected side's sweep and transition states.

From the next bar onward, sell the held option on the first applicable exit in this order: underlying touches the initial stop; a confirmed opposite swing break closes beyond the most protective locked swing with an ATR buffer; after the minimum holding sessions, an enabled activated trail is touched; or, after that holding period, an enabled fixed risk-multiple target is touched. Update the locked swing only in the protective direction. Activate the runner when a confirmed close reaches the required favorable initial-risk multiple. Its trail uses the favorable extreme less or plus an ATR distance, optionally tightened by the buffered locked swing. Evaluate exits using the trail and favorable extreme carried into the bar, then update the extreme and runner state.
