After the configured start time, operate only when the chart instrument's base asset differs from the selected portfolio base currency and the two configured allocation fractions sum to one. Use equal allocations if equal composition is selected; otherwise use the configured fractions. Evaluate only on bars whose timeframe matches the chosen rebalance frequency; for daily, weekly, or monthly frequencies, also require the bar to open at midnight in the exchange timezone. Price the base currency in the investment currency, using one when they are the same, and price the chart's base asset using the selected exchange's asset/base pair multiplied by the base-currency price.

At the first eligible bar while the tracked balance is zero, allocate the initial investment between the two assets at their target fractions and submit a long limit entry at the chart close for the calculated main-asset quantity. On subsequent eligible bars, value the existing quantities at current external prices. In periodic mode, rebalance whenever the absolute difference between the target and current main-asset values meets the minimum trade value. In threshold mode, additionally require either asset's current portfolio fraction to reach or exceed its target plus the configured deviation, or reach or fall below its target minus that deviation. On a rebalance, reset both tracked quantities to their target fractions of the current total value. Submit a long limit entry at the chart close for an increase in the main-asset quantity, or a short limit entry for a decrease, using the absolute quantity change. Otherwise submit no order.
