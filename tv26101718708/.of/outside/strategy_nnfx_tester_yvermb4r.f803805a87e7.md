On each bar, determine bullish and bearish states from a configurable first confirmation indicator and a configurable second confirmation indicator; each confirmation may be disabled. Long signals require every enabled confirmation to be bullish, and short signals require every enabled confirmation to be bearish. The selected indicators compare trend or momentum using their corresponding directional rules. Optionally require price above or below a configurable moving-average baseline. With the baseline enabled, initial entries also require the baseline to be within a configurable ATR distance of price; optionally require the baseline condition to have held for exactly one prior bar and limit entries to a short confirmation streak. Continuation entries require price and the continuation trend line on the same side of the baseline, the relevant ATR proximity condition, directional confirmations, and a baseline-side streak longer than the time since the previous exit. For either entry type, optionally require the candle to move in the trade direction, block entries when the selected volatility filter indicates unsuitable conditions, and block entries when the external volatility index is below its configured floor. Enter only while flat and within the configured test dates. Optionally exit a long or short when the selected exit signal turns against that position, or when price crosses the baseline against it; the exit signal can use the first confirmation, second confirmation, or an exit trend channel. Independently place an ATR-based stop loss on every position and an ATR-based take profit on the first position tranche; an optional single-position mode uses one tranche, while the default mode splits the entry into two tranches and leaves the second tranche with its stop loss only.
