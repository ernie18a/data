Use an external net-liquidity series and the daily close of the selected reference index to calculate fair value as net liquidity divided by one billion and a configurable scale, minus a configurable offset. Subtract fair value from the reference index close. Trade only when net liquidity is positive, and identify signals when this spread crosses above the overbought boundary or below the oversold boundary. Begin trading on or after the configured start date. In long/short mode, trade the overbought cross short and the oversold cross long; reverse those directions when inverse mode is enabled. In short-only mode, enter short on the overbought cross and exit on the oversold cross; inverse mode enters long on the overbought cross and exits on the oversold cross. In long-only mode, enter long on the oversold cross and exit on the overbought cross; inverse mode enters short on the oversold cross and exits on the overbought cross. When a close date is enabled, close the position on that date in the single-direction modes. In long/short mode, the close-date condition instead triggers a short entry through the same branch as the oversold signal.
