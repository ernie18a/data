For each asset, calculate a sidewaysness score from the Choppiness Index and an inverse ADX component, then rank assets from lowest score to highest. Consider directional trades only among the three lowest-scoring assets; go long when positive directional movement is at least negative directional movement, and short when negative directional movement is greater. Prefer assets scoring 38% or lower; avoid directional breakout trades at 55% or higher. The indicator provides no exit rule.
