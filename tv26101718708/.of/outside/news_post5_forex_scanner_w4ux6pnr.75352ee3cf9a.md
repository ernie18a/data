Once the post-news observation window is complete, set each pair’s direction from the move between the event bar’s open and the close at the end of that window: positive means long, negative means short, and zero means flat. Score the signal using the move’s size relative to volatility, elevated volatility, and whether price confirms the move by holding beyond the post-window close and aligning with VWAP, the Bollinger basis, and medium- and higher-timeframe EMA trends; reward an expanding Bollinger band when price is aligned. Rank pairs by score and show those meeting the chosen minimum. This is a directional scanner; it defines no exit rule.
