At the start of each session, set the opening range to the high and low of its first 15-minute period. During the configured trading window, signal long when a completed 15-minute candle closes above the range high, and signal short when one closes below the range low; wick breaches alone do not qualify. If enabled, require breakout volume to exceed the opening-range average by a chosen multiplier, align price with session VWAP and an EMA (above for long, below for short), and require the range width to fall within chosen bounds relative to daily ATR. Limit signals to one per direction per session, or to the first breakout overall, if configured. After a breakout, signal a retest in that direction when price touches or crosses the broken boundary and closes back beyond it, provided that breakout has not failed or already produced a retest. If price later closes back inside the range after a breakout, mark that breakout as failed and exit its active direction; do not automatically open the opposite direction. Optionally plot successive profit targets beyond the breakout boundary, spaced by a chosen fraction of the opening-range width, advancing each time price reaches the current target.
