On each fully closed 4-hour candle, evaluate each currency pair. A signal candle must be bullish or bearish and qualify as a directional outside bar, engulfing candle, or railroad pattern. Reject it if its range is zero, it touches at least two Bollinger Band lines, or its close fails to pass the configured retracement threshold within its own range (upper portion for bullish candles, lower portion for bearish candles). For a bullish signal, classify as continuation buy if it closes above the Bollinger middle band by the configured pip buffer; otherwise classify as reversal buy if the entire candle stays below the middle band and the lower outer band was touched or breached by the signal candle or a candle in the configured recent lookback. For a bearish signal, classify as continuation sell if it closes below the middle band by the configured pip buffer; otherwise classify as reversal sell if the entire candle stays above the middle band and the upper outer band was touched or breached within that lookback. If none of these conditions is met, report no setup.
