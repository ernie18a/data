Trade long only. Enter when the short moving average crosses above the medium moving average, or after price closes above a medium or longer moving average for three consecutive bars following a close below it. Allow entries only while the external volatility index is below its configured ceiling, unless the filter is disabled. Close the position immediately if the enabled volatility filter rises above that ceiling, or if the short moving average crosses below the medium one. Manage the long with a ratcheting stop: start at a configured loss distance below entry; after profit reaches the first configured threshold, move the stop to a smaller configured loss below entry; after profit reaches a higher configured threshold, trail the highest price reached by a configured percentage. The stop may only move upward.
