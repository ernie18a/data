For each selected higher timeframe, scan completed bars backward within a configurable lookback for the most recent sweep. A bullish sweep has a low below the preceding bar’s low, a high above that low, and a close above it. A bearish sweep has a high above the preceding bar’s high and a close below it. If both occur on the same bar, treat it as bullish. A bullish sweep contributes a bullish bias when the current higher-timeframe close is at or above the sweep close and a bearish bias otherwise. A bearish sweep contributes a bearish bias when the current close is at or below the sweep close and a bullish bias otherwise. No sweep is neutral. Use the configured regular-hours-only or full-session data, with the source’s higher-timeframe alignment: daily data uses lookahead, while the other timeframes do not.

Count bullish and bearish biases across the selected higher timeframes. Round the external market-breadth index’s daily close to an integer, substituting a neutral value when unavailable. A sufficient bullish majority indicates long unless breadth exceeds its upper threshold; a sufficient bearish majority indicates short unless breadth falls below its lower threshold. A blocked majority is flagged as questionable; otherwise report mixed or no trade.

For entry signals, build the opening-range high and low from the configured opening session. Reset both breakout states at the regular-session open. After the opening range ends, a close above its high activates the bullish breakout and clears the bearish breakout; a close below its low does the reverse. During the configured entry window, signal long when the bullish state is active, the low is no higher than the opening-range high plus a tick-based tolerance, the close is above that high, the low is below the previous bar’s low, and both the high and close are above the previous low. Signal short when the bearish state is active, the high is no lower than the opening-range low minus the tolerance, the close is below that low, the high is above the previous bar’s high, and the close is below the previous high. Require the corresponding unblocked higher-timeframe majority and, if enabled, current volume above its moving average. Entry signaling must also be enabled. No exit rule is specified.
