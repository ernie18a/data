Build liquidation levels from bars with unusually high volume, weighted by volume and range. Project long liquidation levels below each trigger price and short liquidation levels above it at leverage-based distances; combine nearby projections into ranked walls. Treat a strong, untested wall as a potential magnet: when price is moving toward the nearest such wall and market structure or momentum agrees, take a position toward it and target the wall, then the next level beyond. Alternatively, on the first touch of a dense, untested wall, fade toward the mean if price arrives overextended or losing momentum. A decisive close through the wall invalidates the fade; stand aside or take a position in the breakout direction. Avoid trading toward weak or already-consumed walls, or when no strong wall is nearby.
