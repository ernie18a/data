Determine trend from price relative to the Ichimoku cloud, cloud orientation, equilibrium slope, and trend strength. In a clear uptrend, enter long when price reaches the lower discount area and closes bullish, provided higher-timeframe trend and optional lower-timeframe momentum agree and the setup meets a minimum reward-to-risk test. Place the stop beyond the cloud and target the nearest unswept equal-high pool, or the upper channel boundary if none exists. In a clear downtrend, apply the mirrored rules: enter short from the upper premium area on a bearish close with timeframe confirmation, stop beyond the cloud, and target the nearest unswept equal-low pool or lower channel boundary. Allow only one active setup; exit at its stop or target, or when trend reverses, closing as a win or loss according to whether price is favorable relative to entry. Also flag a buy or sell when price first enters the corresponding extreme zone during its matching trend. No option decisions.
