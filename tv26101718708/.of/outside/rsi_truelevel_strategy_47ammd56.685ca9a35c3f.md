Calculate RSI and upper/lower volatility bands around a linear regression of price. Enter long when RSI crosses above its oversold threshold or price crosses above the selected lower band. When short trading is enabled, enter short when RSI crosses below its overbought threshold or price crosses below the selected upper band. Exit a long when RSI crosses below its overbought threshold or price crosses below the selected upper band. Exit a short when RSI crosses above its oversold threshold or price crosses above the selected lower band.
