Compare year-over-year GDP growth and core CPI inflation with their respective sustainable-rate thresholds. If both are above threshold, favor commodities, cyclical value, and industrials; buy oil and gas exposure; sell information technology and basic materials exposure. If growth is below and inflation above, favor cash, defensive value, and utilities; buy pharmaceuticals and consumer staples; sell oil and gas exposure. If growth is above and inflation below, favor stocks, cyclical growth, and telecoms; buy information technology and basic materials; sell consumer discretionary exposure. If both are below threshold, favor bonds, defensive growth, and financials; buy consumer discretionary exposure; sell pharmaceuticals and consumer staples.
