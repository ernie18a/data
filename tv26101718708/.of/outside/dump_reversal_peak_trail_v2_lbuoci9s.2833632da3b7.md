On confirmed bars, arm a long setup when a bearish candle's absolute percentage change between the selected price sources exceeds the anomaly threshold, does not exceed the enabled dump cap, and its low breaks the enabled prior daily-low lookback. Permit setups while flat or below the open-trade limit, except during the cooldown after a position closes. A new dump replaces the setup and cancels pending entries. Block entry placement when the close is below and sufficiently close to the prior daily-lookback high, or when the optional external Bitcoin trend filter is enabled and its latest hourly close is not above its close a configured number of hours earlier.

For structure-based entries, remember the dump candle's high and the lowest low since arming. While waiting, a lower low on a bar whose absolute open-to-close change is within the enabled dump cap replaces both references and cancels pending entries. Choose one entry mode: enter at market on a close above the reference high; enter at market when a configured number of consecutive closes are above it; on an upward close crossover of the high place a limit buy at the high plus an ATR offset; or immediately place a stop buy at the high plus an ATR buffer. In automatic mode, on an upward crossover place the ATR-offset limit; otherwise enter at market after the sensitivity-dependent number of closes above the high. If the automatic limit expires, place an ATR-buffered stop if entry filters and position eligibility still pass. Cancel unfilled limit and stop orders at their configured expiry. Pending orders are not re-filtered before filling.

In rebound mode, instead track the lowest low since the dump and enter at market on a bullish candle when the configured recent high-low range is sufficiently flat and the close has rebounded sufficiently from that low, subject to the entry filters and position eligibility. Clear setup state when a position opens or an add fills; cooldown also clears waiting setups. Adds reset the profit peak.

For an open long, track the maximum close-based percentage profit relative to the current weighted average entry. Once that peak reaches the profit activation threshold, close the position when current profit falls from the peak by the configured percentage-point amount. Optionally maintain a stop-loss order at a configured percentage below average entry. Optionally close when the position has been open for the configured duration and the current close is below average entry; the timer starts at initial entry and does not reset on adds. After a full close, start the cooldown and cancel pending entries.
