Use the traded instrument’s OHLCV data, its higher-timeframe data, and a benchmark’s close on the trading timeframe. Treat all lookbacks, weights, thresholds, multipliers, cooldowns, and optional filters as configurable parameters.

Compute fast, main, base, and long EMAs; RSI; MACD and its histogram; stochastic oscillator and signal; ATR; relative volume; OBV and its average; Chaikin money flow; money flow index; VWAP; Bollinger-band width and its average; rolling range and swing extremes; and the previous day’s pivot. Higher-timeframe support means its close is above its main EMA and that EMA is at least its base EMA. Relative leadership means the instrument-to-benchmark close ratio is above its rising EMA and instrument RSI is above its bullish floor; a leadership breakout occurs when that ratio exceeds its prior rolling high.

Define a healthy trend as close above a rising or flat main EMA that is above the base EMA. A structure breakout is close above the preceding rolling high; structure support is close above a rising or flat rolling-range midpoint. Strong participation requires sufficient relative volume and either OBV above its average or positive money flow. A strong candle is bullish, with sufficient body size and close position within its range. A top-grade candle additionally has sufficient relative volume and closes above the main EMA; a lesser-grade candle meets lower bullish body and close-position requirements and closes at or above that EMA.

A liquidity reclaim is a sweep below the previous swing low that closes back above it with a sufficient lower wick, or an upward cross of close over VWAP, or over the main EMA. If VWAP is disabled, use the main EMA for that reclaim. A trap is any of: a bearish, upper-wick rejection above the previous range high on insufficient volume; a high-volume bearish candle with weak money flow index and negative money flow; or a sweep above the previous swing high closing below it with a sufficient upper wick. Price is overextended if it exceeds the main EMA by a configured ATR distance or RSI reaches its exhaustion threshold. A bullish phase is any of: compressed volatility with close at or above the range midpoint and acceptable money flow; expanding Bollinger width with close above its basis, sufficient relative volume, a structure breakout and strong participation; or a healthy trend with close at or above the fast EMA, nonnegative MACD histogram and RSI above its bullish floor.

Calculate a capped weighted structure score from: close above main EMA; fast EMA above main; main above base; base above long; healthy trend; structure support; structure breakout; close above range midpoint; higher-timeframe support; RSI above its bullish floor; RSI at or above its strength threshold; positive MACD histogram; nondecreasing histogram; relative leadership; leadership breakout; absence of a trap; and close above the main EMA without overextension. Structure qualifies when its score reaches its threshold and healthy trend, higher-timeframe support, and absence of a trap all hold.

Calculate a capped weighted timing score from: close above main EMA; close above VWAP, or above main EMA when VWAP is disabled; liquidity reclaim; close above the previous-day pivot; strong candle; top- or lesser-grade candle, with the top grade receiving its own weight; stochastic above or crossing above its signal; a bullish candle closing sufficiently high in its range; sufficient relative volume; OBV above its average; positive money flow; being within the optional session; a bullish phase; and absence of a trap. Timing qualifies when its score reaches its threshold, the optional session filter passes, a strong candle and absence of a trap hold, and the optional bar-close confirmation passes.

Count qualifying layers: structure score at threshold; timing score at threshold; healthy trend with main EMA above base; strong but not overheated RSI with positive MACD histogram; nondecreasing histogram with MACD at or above its signal; sufficient relative volume and strong participation; positive money flow or sufficiently strong money flow index; relative leadership; higher-timeframe support; strong top-grade candle; no trap or high-volume distribution with money flow above its allowed floor; and a bullish phase with close above main EMA and no overextension.

On an optionally confirmed bar, enter long when either the layer count reaches the configured strongest-setup requirement, or structure and timing both qualify and the layer count reaches the lower combined-setup requirement. The latter entry is disabled in the strongest-only display mode. Respect the major-signal cooldown and, if repeat-entry blocking is enabled, require no active trade. On entry, record close and a fixed stop equal to the lower of close minus an ATR multiple and the current rolling swing low minus an ATR buffer. Mark the trade active.

While active, exit on an optionally confirmed bar when close falls below that fixed stop, or close is below the base EMA with negative MACD histogram, or RSI is below its bullish floor while close is below the main EMA. Emit an exit only on the transition into this exit condition; clear the active trade and restart the major-signal cooldown. Profit targets are displayed but do not trigger exits. No short or options decisions are specified.
