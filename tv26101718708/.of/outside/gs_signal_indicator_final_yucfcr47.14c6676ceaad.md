Long entries occur only on confirmed bars when no trade is active and the buy cooldown has elapsed. A is triggered when the required count of bullish-quality layers is met. B is triggered when both the trend/setup score and execution/timing score meet their configured buy thresholds and the required near-complete layer count is met. The setup score combines price and moving-average alignment, range support or breakout, higher-timeframe bullish alignment, positive momentum, relative strength versus a benchmark, and acceptable risk. The timing score combines price position relative to moving averages and VWAP, liquidity or moving-average reclaim, a strong bullish candle, oscillator confirmation, volume and buying-flow participation, session eligibility, bullish market phase, and absence of trap conditions. Layers assess these same factors plus RSI and MACD strength, volume participation, money flow, benchmark leadership, higher-timeframe support, candle strength, distribution risk, and overextension. While a trade is active, issue a warning for trap or distribution conditions, overextension, fading momentum, or loss of the main moving average. Exit the long if price closes below its stored stop, closes below the base moving average with negative MACD momentum, or has weak RSI while below the main moving average. The initial stop is the lower of an ATR-based stop and a buffered recent swing low; targets are derived from the entry risk. No short-entry or options logic is present.
