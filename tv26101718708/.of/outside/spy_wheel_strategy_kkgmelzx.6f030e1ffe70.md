On daily bars, buy the configured share quantity when the start date is crossed, and record the fill price as cost basis. While holding, count consecutive closes below a configurable long-term simple moving average; at the configured count, close the position, cancel orders, and enter an emergency-exit state. From that state, re-enter long when the close rises above that average.

Otherwise, while holding, calculate a covered-call strike by rounding up the close increased by a configurable percentage. Use the high-volatility percentage when the external volatility index is available and at or above its threshold; otherwise use the normal percentage. In high volatility, compare the close with the newly calculated strike; otherwise compare it with the previous bar's stored strike. If the close exceeds the applicable strike and that strike is at least the current cost basis, close the shares as assigned. Otherwise, store the new strike only if it is at least cost basis; clear it if it is lower. The high-volatility same-bar comparison cannot trigger assignment when its strike percentage is positive.

After the initial entry, whenever flat outside the emergency-exit state, either re-enter long immediately or use the optional cash-secured-put mode. In that mode, cancel the previous entry order each bar and place a share buy limit at the open reduced by a configurable percentage and rounded down, but only if the close exceeds both configurable long-term simple and short-term exponential moving averages, RSI is outside a configurable inclusive blocked interval or that interval is disabled, the external volatility index is available and below its threshold, the close is at least VWAP if that filter is enabled, and the manual macro-event block is off. This limit order simulates put re-entry; the code does not place an option order. Orders are processed on bar close. A short-term average breach only produces a hedge alert; it does not place a protective-put order.
