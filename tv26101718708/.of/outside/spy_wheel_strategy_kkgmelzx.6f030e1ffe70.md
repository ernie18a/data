Enter long at the start, then hold shares and sell covered calls each eligible trading day. Set the call strike above the close, using a wider offset in elevated volatility; do not sell if the strike would be below share cost basis. Treat shares as assigned only when the daily close exceeds the applicable strike, and re-enter at the next open. Alternatively, when put-based re-entry is enabled, sell a cash-secured put only when price is above both long- and short-term moving averages, RSI is outside its blocked band, volatility is below its limit, any enabled VWAP condition passes, and no macro event block is active; set its strike below the open and re-enter on assignment. While holding shares, exit at the next open after consecutive closes below the long-term moving average; re-enter when price closes back above it.
