Track swing highs and lows and fair value gaps (three-candle wick gaps). A bullish setup begins when price wicks below a tracked swing low and closes back above it; a bearish setup begins when it wicks above a swing high and closes back below it. Record the opening price of the first candle in the consecutive candle series that ran into the extreme. Within the configured confirmation window, confirm bullish direction when a candle closes above that price, or bearish direction when it closes below it, subject to the configured candle-body displacement filter and any enabled session restriction. Score each confirmed setup with one base point, plus one point for each enabled and satisfied condition: correlated-market divergence at the sweep (the traded market sweeps while the correlated market does not), a same-direction inversion-gap retest within the lookback, a same-direction close through the latest opposing swing, alignment with the appropriate side of the recent range midpoint, alignment with the latest higher-timeframe gap direction, a sweep of equal swing levels, and occurrence during the configured session. Signal long or short when the score meets the configured minimum. For an entry, require a same-direction inversion-gap retest that rejects the zone edge—bullish: price reaches the former bearish gap and closes above its upper edge; bearish: price reaches the former bullish gap and closes below its lower edge—and that occurs within the configured lookback after a confirmed same-direction setup; apply any enabled session restriction. The displayed invalidation and risk-multiple target are levels, not exit signals.
