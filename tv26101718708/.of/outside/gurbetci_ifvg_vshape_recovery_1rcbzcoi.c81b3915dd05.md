On each bar close, track confirmed swing highs and lows. A bullish sweep occurs when the low falls below the latest swing low and the close returns above it; a bearish sweep is the inverse. Consume the swept swing. For a bullish sweep, set the confirmation level to the open of the oldest candle in the consecutive bearish run ending at the sweep, starting with the previous candle if the sweep candle is bullish; if there is no such run, use the starting candle's open. Reverse candle directions for a bearish sweep. Limit the scan to a configurable number of bars. Replace any pending same-direction sweep with the latest one and expire it after a configurable window. Confirm a bullish reversal when the close exceeds its level and the candle body meets the configurable ATR displacement threshold; reverse for bearish. If the optional session filter is enabled, confirmation must occur during the configured New York session.

Score each confirmed reversal starting with one point. Add one point for each applicable condition: a same-direction divergence, inverted-gap retest, or market-structure shift recorded no earlier than the sweep bar minus a configurable lookback; close in discount for bullish or premium for bearish relative to the midpoint of a configurable recent high-low range; matching higher-timeframe gap bias; the swept swing matching the preceding same-type pivot within an ATR tolerance; and the configured session being active. Apply each optional component only when enabled. A bullish divergence occurs at a bullish sweep if the correlated instrument's current low exceeds its lowest low over the pivot window recorded when the swept pivot was confirmed; reverse highs and comparisons for bearish divergence. For market structure, record a bullish shift when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high, or a bearish shift when it crosses below the latest confirmed swing low, consuming that structure level. For higher-timeframe bias, retain the direction of the most recent three-candle gap, including the developing higher-timeframe candle.

Track a bounded set of three-candle gaps meeting a configurable ATR width. Invert a bullish gap when price breaks below its lower edge, and a bearish gap when price breaks above its upper edge, using the configured close or wick rule. From the next bar onward, record the first bullish retest when an inverted bearish gap is touched at its upper edge and closes above it; record the first bearish retest when an inverted bullish gap is touched at its lower edge and closes below it. Remove an inverted gap when the close breaks through its opposite edge. Signal long or short on the corresponding confirmed reversal when its score reaches the configurable minimum. The separate retest entry signal does not depend on the correlated instrument or the score.
