This is a long-only stock decision framework; it gives no short or options signals. Build a weighted overall quality score from technical leadership versus a benchmark, strength of the relative-strength line, proximity to the 52-week high, volume versus average, accumulation versus distribution, breakout-pivot status, trend structure, price confirmation, extension risk, momentum reset or rollover, pullback and divergence timing, invalidation, and projected upside. Give greatest influence to leadership and price confirmation, with wave-style timing and projected upside as supporting factors. Mark price confirmed only when price makes a higher low, closes above its fast trend average and the prior bar’s high, and forms a bullish candle. A buy trigger requires a strong overall score, confirmed price, no break below the structural low, and no excessive extension; an optional technical-quality filter can further restrict triggers. A buy watch is issued when the score reaches the watch range below trigger strength and the setup is not invalidated. A trim/caution signal applies at a sufficient score when momentum rolls over or price is excessively extended, unless a higher-priority state applies. Otherwise, wait at middling scores and issue no trade at weak scores. A close below the structural low overrides every other state and marks the setup invalid.
