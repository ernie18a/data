On each bar, compare the instrument with a configurable benchmark on the same timeframe. Calculate their lookback returns and the instrument-to-benchmark price ratio. Classify relative strength by whether the instrument outperforms and whether the ratio exceeds its moving average; also check whether the ratio is near its lookback high.

Build a technical leadership score from relative strength, proximity of the ratio to its high, distance from the instrument's daily annual high, current volume relative to average volume, the balance of above-average-volume rising and falling bars, and price relative to the preceding base-period high. Classify a breakout as a close above that high with strong volume; otherwise distinguish proximity below it and a quiet move above it. Apply configurable points to each condition, using the first matching condition in that order for the base component.

Build a timing score from moving-average trend alignment; a close within a configurable retracement zone of the latest confirmed low-to-high impulse while above its low and in a basic bullish trend; recent bullish RSI divergence between confirmed pivot lows; a recent stochastic reset; a lower pivot low followed by a close above that low in a basic bullish trend; and price confirmation. A stochastic reset requires a recent oversold reading, an upward stochastic crossover or both smoothed lines rising, and the faster line below a configurable ceiling. Price confirmation requires the latest confirmed pivot low to exceed the previous one, a close above the fast exponential average and previous bar's high, and a bullish candle.

Calculate a weighted aggregate of the leadership and normalized timing scores plus graded components for relative strength, distance from the annual high, volume, accumulation versus distribution, base position, distance from the fast exponential average in average-true-range units, moving-average trend, stochastic state, price confirmation, invalidation, and positive projected upside. Use configurable component grades and weights. For overlapping grades, evaluate base position as breakout, near base, then quiet move above base; evaluate extension as discount, normal, extended, stretched, then broken; and evaluate stochastic as recent reset, faster line above slower line, then overbought downward crossover. The projected target is an extension of the latest confirmed impulse and is active only when the timing score reaches its threshold and the setup is not invalidated.

Apply action precedence on every bar: if the close is below the low of the latest confirmed impulse, exit or invalidate the long setup. Otherwise, signal a long entry when the aggregate reaches its entry threshold, price is confirmed, and extension is not stretched; optionally also require the leadership score to pass its threshold, price to be sufficiently near its annual high, and either strong relative strength or a ratio near its high. Otherwise, mark a watch when the aggregate is at least its watch threshold but below its entry threshold. Otherwise, signal trim or caution when the aggregate reaches its caution threshold and either the stochastic rolls down from overbought or price is stretched. Otherwise wait or report no trade according to the aggregate. Watch takes precedence over trim, and a score above the entry threshold that fails entry confirmation can still produce trim or wait. Alerts fire when the underlying entry, watch, trim, or invalidation condition becomes true, independently of the displayed action precedence.
