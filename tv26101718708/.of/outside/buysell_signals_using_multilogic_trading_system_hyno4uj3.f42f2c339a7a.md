On each confirmed bar, generate a long signal if any enabled engine qualifies: (1) Trend: price and fast, medium, and slow moving averages are bullishly aligned; enough of six bullish confirmations agree (alignment, RSI in its bullish range, bullish MACD, price above the Bollinger basis, high volume with bullish OBV, or a bullish close above the prior swing high); higher-timeframe trend and session VWAP agree when those filters are enabled. Short signals use the inverse alignment and confirmations. (2) Momentum: price is on the appropriate side of the fast average; RSI, MACD histogram and its change, OBV, volume when available, higher-timeframe trend, and VWAP all support that direction. (3) Breakout: the prior market qualifies as a range; price closes beyond its prior boundary by an ATR-based buffer, the previous close was not already beyond it, the candle closes strongly toward its breakout side, RSI and MACD confirm direction, volume confirms when required and available, and VWAP agrees when enabled. (4) Optional mean reversion: in a sideways market with a sufficiently wide range, price tests and rejects the corresponding outer range zone with a directional candle and rejection wick; RSI is extreme and turning in the reversal direction, and volume permission is met. Emit signals only after sufficient history and cooldown, optionally only on a fresh setup, and subject to the active-trade exit gate; if long and short qualify together, choose the side with the higher confluence score, otherwise emit neither. On a long, set a stop by the selected structure-plus-ATR, ATR, or percentage method and a target by the selected risk-reward, ATR, or percentage method; reverse these calculations for a short. Exit the active trade when price touches its stop or target. Consider trades only at confirmed candle close.
