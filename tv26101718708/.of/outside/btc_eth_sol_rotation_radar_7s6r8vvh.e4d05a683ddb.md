On each chart-timeframe bar, compare BTC, ETH and SOL using their respective close and volume series. For each asset, calculate percentage returns over configurable fast, medium and slow lookbacks; acceleration is the fast return minus its value a configurable number of bars earlier, substituting zero for a missing prior value. For each return and acceleration, assign 100 if strictly greater than both other assets, 0 if strictly less than both, and 50 otherwise, including ties.

Calculate RSI, fast and slow EMAs, volume divided by its moving average, and percentage distance of close above the slow EMA, using configurable periods. Substitute a volume ratio of 1 if its denominator is zero or unavailable, and an EMA distance of 0 if the slow EMA is zero or unavailable. Score RSI as follows: at or below 30, 15; below 50, 15 + 1.75 × (RSI − 30); through 68, 50 + 2.5 × (RSI − 50); through the configurable overbought threshold, 95 − 2 × (RSI − 68); above that threshold, clamp 81 − 4 × (RSI − threshold) to 0–81. Score trend as 100 when close > fast EMA > slow EMA, otherwise 65 when close > slow EMA, otherwise 45 when fast EMA > slow EMA, otherwise 10. For volume, clamp (volume ratio − 0.70) / 0.90 × 100 to 0–100; use that value when fast return is nonnegative and its complement to 100 otherwise.

Take the configurable weighted average of the four relative scores and the RSI, trend and volume scores, using 50 if total weight is zero. If exhaustion penalties are enabled, subtract the configurable maximum penalty multiplied by the greater of: the RSI excess above its configurable overbought threshold divided by the remaining distance to 100, and the EMA extension excess above its configurable limit divided by that limit. Clamp each excess ratio to 0–1 and use zero when its threshold is not exceeded. Clamp the resulting score to 0–100.

Select the highest-scoring asset as leader, resolving ties in BTC, ETH, SOL order. If defensive cash is enabled, the leader's score is below the configurable cash threshold, and all three closes are below their slow EMAs, choose cash as the destination; otherwise choose the leader. Determine the holding from the configured manual selection, or automatically from the chart ticker by checking for BTC, then ETH, then SOL, and otherwise treating it as cash. The holding score is its asset score, or zero for cash.

Generate a rotation signal only when the destination differs from the holding, the configured candle-close confirmation is satisfied, and more than the configured cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the last rotation signal. For an asset destination, also require the leader's score to meet the configurable minimum and exceed the holding score by at least the configurable advantage. For a cash destination, the defensive cash conditions alone satisfy these score and advantage tests. On a signal, record the bar and rotate from the holding into the destination; a cash destination exits the asset position. Otherwise retain the holding. The holding is not updated by signals: it remains determined by the manual selection or chart ticker. The source compares the destination label 'CASH' case-sensitively with the holding label 'Cash', so a cash holding does not prevent repeated cash signals after cooldown.
