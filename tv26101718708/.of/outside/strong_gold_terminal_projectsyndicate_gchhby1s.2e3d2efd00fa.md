Trade a level break or rejection in the direction of aligned multi-timeframe pressure and trend regime, targeting the next level when there is sufficient room and daily range remains. A session high or low break also favors continuation early in a session, especially on a historically wide weekday with a supportive macro backdrop. Late in a session, when the daily range is spent and macro is neutral, favor fading the move toward the daily open. Stand aside when pressure and price disagree, timeframes are split, the regime is ranging or compressed with neutral pressure, levels are too close or too distant, the daily range is spent late in the session, or macro opposes the trade. Assess gold's macro backdrop from daily moves in the dollar, yields, silver and volatility: falling dollar and yields and rising silver and volatility favor gold; opposite moves oppose it. Combine their scaled contributions to classify the backdrop as supportive, adverse or neutral.
