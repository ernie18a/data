On each bar, form a pressure score from ATR compression, the percentile rank of absolute return acceleration, price-path inefficiency, and, when enabled, percentile ranks of the absolute difference between the external volatility index and forecast realized volatility and of the change in the derivative-to-underlying basis. Use a higher weight for acceleration and normalize by the enabled weights. Forecast realized volatility with a rolling regression on fast/slow exponential averages of lagged returns and squared returns; use trailing realized volatility until the forecast is ready. Require valid data for every enabled external feed.

A release candidate occurs when pressure has reached its high percentile threshold within the memory window and the current high-low range, normalized by ATR and mapped to a bounded expansion score, reaches the release threshold. Require candidates on consecutive bars; evaluate an event only on the exact confirmation bar and only if enough bars have elapsed since the previous event. Determine direction by scoring an upside or downside break of the previous rolling high or low with greater weight than the close's position within the current range and the sign of the price change over the direction lookback. Choose long on a tie.

Optional filters can require agreement with the displaced Ichimoku cloud bias on the chart and higher timeframes, reject a statistically significant low variance-ratio pinning regime, or reject opposing reconstructed order flow. The order-flow filter permits neutral flow: classify buy and sell volume using standardized close changes and a normal CDF, candle geometry, or lower-timeframe candle direction; standardize signed volume over a rolling window and assign a direction only when both its z-score and raw imbalance agree. Each filter can exempt expiry-session events. Higher-timeframe cloud reads can use the last closed bars. By default these filters are informational and do not block events.

Record the event and start its cooldown before applying the final trade gates, even if a gate blocks the trade. When the liquidity gate is enabled, suppress a trade in stressed liquidity: estimate spread from OHLC, rank it against history, and, if volume is available, combine its percentile with the percentiles of range-to-dollar-volume illiquidity, signed-volume impact slope and dollar-volume depth. Classify liquidity as stressed when the resulting liquidity score is below its threshold or the current illiquidity ratio has an extreme positive rolling z-score. Without volume, use spread alone. The selected spread estimator may condition its high-low estimates on the path-volatility forecast.

When the location gate is enabled, suppress a long if the nearest level above the close is closer than the ATR-based clearance buffer, and suppress a short if the nearest level below is too close. Levels are the preceding session's volume-profile point of control and value-area boundaries, still-untested prior points of control, and anchored VWAP. Build each session profile by distributing bar volume across price bins, expanding the value area from the busiest bin toward the larger neighboring bin; retire an untested point of control when price trades through it. Use unit weights when volume is unavailable. Anchor VWAP at the configured swing, period boundary, or highest-volume event.

Emit a long or short only when the event passes the enabled final gates and signal display is enabled. Otherwise emit no trade decision. There is no exit rule or options trade rule.
