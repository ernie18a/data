On each chart-timeframe bar, calculate the traded instrument's EMA, ATR, MACD histogram, smoothed stochastic and its signal average, volume average, and ADX. The stochastic calculation uses high as its source, low as its highest-value input, and close as its lowest-value input; use the same calculation for both external instruments.

A long setup requires: close above a rising EMA; close within the configured ATR distance of the EMA; the lower of the stochastic and its signal within the bullish pullback zone; the stochastic crossing above its signal; a rising MACD histogram; and a bullish candle. A short setup requires the symmetric conditions, using the higher stochastic value within the bearish pullback zone, a downward crossover, falling histogram, and bearish candle. If enabled, also require volume above its average times the configured multiplier and ADX at or above its threshold.

If external confirmation is enabled, both configured external instruments must have closes above rising EMAs for a long or below falling EMAs for a short. If momentum confirmation is also enabled, each external instrument's stochastic must be above its signal for a long or below it for a short.

Signal a long or short only when its complete setup is true and was not true on the previous bar. A signal sets or replaces the current direction, even if already in a trade. Set the long stop to the lowest low over the configured swing lookback including the signal bar, minus an ATR buffer; set the short stop to the corresponding highest high plus the buffer.

After processing entry signals on each bar, exit a long if the low reaches its stop, or, if stochastic exits are enabled, the stochastic is at or above the configured upper exit threshold and crosses below its signal. Exit a short if the high reaches its stop, or, if stochastic exits are enabled, the stochastic is at or below the configured lower exit threshold and crosses above its signal. Stop checks also apply on the entry bar.
