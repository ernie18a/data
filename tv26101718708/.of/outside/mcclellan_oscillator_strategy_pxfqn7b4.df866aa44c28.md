Trade long or flat; never open a short. Build a breadth oscillator from weighted market advance/decline counts, using either their ratio-adjusted series or their raw difference, then subtract a slower EMA from a faster EMA. While flat, enter long if any of these signals holds: the oscillator’s normalized position within its recent range is above a configurable extreme threshold now or on the prior bar; its fast EMA is above or crosses above its slow EMA; a separate market-breadth measure reaches its lower range and a volatility-skew index’s moving average is rising; or a small-cap ETF is above its moving average and that skew average is rising. Suppress all entries during October of every fourth year, using the cycle anchored to 2004.

While long, close the position during October 10–17 of every fourth year on the same cycle, or when all of these conditions hold: front-month volatility futures’ premium over spot volatility is below a configurable threshold; the recent average rate of change of a selected percentage-of-stocks measure is negative; the oscillator’s fast EMA is below or crosses below its slow EMA; and either the normalized range position of a volatility-of-volatility index is high, or that of a volatility ETF is high while the fast oscillator EMA is falling. Also require that the volatility-skew index’s normalized range position is not above its configured extreme threshold on either of the two latest bars, and that the small-cap ETF is not above its moving average.
