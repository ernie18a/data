Select the calculation series by priority: use Heikin-Ashi bars of the traded instrument if enabled; otherwise use the S&P 500 if enabled; otherwise use the Nasdaq if enabled; otherwise use the traded instrument’s bars. For each bar, calculate the average of its open, high, low, and close, then calculate three configurable moving averages of that average. Enter long when the fastest average is above the middle average and the middle is above the slowest. Enter short when the fastest is below the middle and the middle is below the slowest. Close a long on the short alignment and close a short on the long alignment. Long and short entry switches independently gate both that side’s entries and its exits.
