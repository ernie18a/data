Enter long when the chart close crosses above the previous completed 4-hour high, and enter short when it crosses below the previous completed 4-hour low. Allow entries only within the configured date window and, if margin protection is enabled, while account equity is above the configured margin threshold. Close a long on a short breakout, or when enabled risk management detects its stop or target being crossed; close a short on a long breakout, or when enabled risk management detects its stop or target being crossed. Close all positions when account equity reaches or falls below the margin threshold.
