Use daily, weekly, and monthly RSI for the traded instrument, and compare its daily return over a lookback with the benchmark’s return over the same lookback. A setup occurs when either daily RSI crosses above the upper trend threshold while weekly RSI is above it, or weekly RSI crosses above it while daily RSI is above it; monthly RSI must also be above that threshold or unavailable, relative return must be positive, and, if enabled, the benchmark must close above its daily EMA. Record the setup bar’s high and low, replacing any earlier setup. Within the following confirmation window, enter long at the close if a bar’s high exceeds the setup high and the distance from that high to the setup low is within the maximum initial risk. Cancel the setup if the breakout exceeds the risk limit, or when the window expires. Entry is allowed when flat, or once after a partial profit exit while still long. Set the stop to the setup low on a new position; on an additional entry, never lower the existing stop. While long, raise the stop to the current bar’s low if that low is higher and either daily RSI is below its support threshold, or daily RSI is below the upper trend threshold and relative return is negative, except that the latter condition is ignored inside the support buffer above the support threshold. Maintain a protective stop order at a configured percentage below the stop. When the close falls below the stop, place another stop order at that bar’s low; retain it until triggered or until a subsequent close rises above the stop, which cancels it. When weekly RSI reaches the profit threshold and the close falls below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars, close a configured portion of the long position once; an additional entry resets eligibility for another partial exit. Process market entries and partial exits at bar close. The pending confirmation low persists across flat periods unless a later close above the current stop clears it.
