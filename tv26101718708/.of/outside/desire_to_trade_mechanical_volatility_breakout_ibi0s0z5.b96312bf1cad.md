Trade long when the traded instrument breaks above the high of a recent completed lookback range, and short when enabled and it breaks below the range low. Require the range width to fall within configured bounds relative to ATR, the instrument to be on the matching side of its trend average, the corresponding external market benchmark to be on the matching side of its trend average, volatility to exceed its own recent average, and volume to exceed its recent average by a configured ratio. Each filter, direction, and date restriction can be enabled or disabled. Enter on a closing breakout or place a stop entry at the range boundary while flat; cancel pending entries when setup conditions fail. Set the protective stop at the opposite range boundary. Exit using a selectable policy: full exit at a configured risk multiple; partial exit at a configured risk multiple with the remainder protected by a trail; or trail the whole position. The trail follows a selectable trend average or ATR-based level, ratcheting only in the favorable direction and never beyond the range stop. Optionally close a long below the instrument's trend average or a short above it, and optionally close on a qualifying opposite breakout.
