Enter long when enabled and flat, provided the close breaks above the current day's opening-range high (if that filter is enabled), the fast moving average is above the slow one (if enabled), momentum exceeds its long threshold (if enabled), trend strength exceeds its minimum (if enabled), the external volatility index is below its maximum (if enabled), and the higher-timeframe close is above its moving average (if enabled). Enter short under the mirrored conditions: close breaks below the opening-range low, fast average below slow, momentum below its short threshold, and higher-timeframe close below its average, with the same strength and volatility filters. Do not repeat the last entry direction, even after the position closes. On entry, set an ATR-based stop; if trailing is enabled, tighten it as price moves favorably using the current close and ATR. Exit a long when the close falls below its stop, or a short when the close rises above its stop.
