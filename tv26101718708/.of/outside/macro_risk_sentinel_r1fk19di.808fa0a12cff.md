Track the high-yield bond to intermediate Treasury price ratio and its short-term rate of change, plus the short-term to medium-term volatility index ratio. Enter a risk-off lockdown when credit deterioration exceeds its severe threshold or the volatility ratio exceeds its panic threshold; treat lesser credit deterioration or volatility inversion as caution unless lockdown applies. While locked, stay risk-off until the traded instrument closes above its recovery moving average and the volatility ratio returns below parity, or the maximum lockdown period expires. On release, allow risk-on exposure; the indicator does not define a short-entry rule.
