On each bar, calculate a signed reversal score for the traded instrument. Positive scores favor shorts and negative scores favor longs. Add these components:
- Count the configured standard-deviation bands crossed above or below an EMA, and add a stepped RSI score measured from its midpoint.
- Estimate volume delta from the close's position within the bar. Score consecutive bars where positive delta accompanies a bearish candle or negative delta accompanies a bullish candle, increasing the magnitude with the streak.
- Build daily and weekly volume profiles by distributing each bar's volume uniformly across sampled prices between its low and high, rounded to price bins. At each period change, expand the prior period's value area from its highest-volume bin toward the larger adjacent bin until the configured volume fraction is covered. Score closes near the prior value-area boundaries when the open approaches from outside, with separate daily and weekly weights.
- Score absorption when volume and candle-body delta exceed their thresholds, the body is sufficiently small, the close is near the appropriate end of the bar and on the corresponding side of a long EMA, and the bar overlaps the corresponding outer VWAP deviation zone. Scale the score by bounded delta intensity.
- Add extreme stochastic, commodity channel index and normalized MACD histogram scores, including the specified oscillator-cross and one-bar price-versus-MACD divergence adjustments. Add a directional score for sufficiently strong ADX.
- Score approaches to prior daily and weekly highs, lows, closes, pivot levels and nearby round prices, plus entries into the configured Fibonacci retracement zone of the latest confirmed swing. Score RSI divergence only on the bar that confirms a new swing.
- If a correlated instrument is configured, independently track its confirmed zigzag highs and lows. When the traded instrument confirms a higher swing high while the correlated instrument's latest two previously confirmed highs decrease, add the bearish divergence weight; when it confirms a lower swing low while the correlated instrument's latest two previously confirmed lows increase, add the bullish divergence weight. Evaluate this comparison before updating the correlated instrument's pivots on the current bar. With no configured correlated instrument, this component is zero.
