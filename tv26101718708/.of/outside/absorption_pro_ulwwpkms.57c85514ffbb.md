Build a composite score from price and volume signals: volatility-band and momentum extremes; buying or selling pressure inferred from candle range, close location, and volume, including consecutive pressure streaks; prior daily and weekly value-area boundaries; absorption near volume-weighted average-price deviation bands; oscillator extremes and divergences; trend strength and direction; prior-session pivots, highs, lows, closes, round-number levels, and swing retracement zones; and, when a correlated instrument is configured, divergences between its swing highs or lows and the traded instrument. Combine the score across the selected chart timeframe and any configured higher timeframes. Adjust its magnitude according to the trend moving-average slope and direction, the traded price's distance from a smoothed average when aligned with the score, and trend strength. Choose long and short score thresholds separately according to whether price is above or below the long-term exponential moving average. Go long whenever the adjusted score is below the selected long threshold; go short whenever it is above the selected short threshold. On an opposite-direction signal, close all open positions, then enter in the new direction. Add another position on each qualifying signal in the current direction. Attach a fixed-distance stop loss and take profit to each entry; close positions when either is reached.
