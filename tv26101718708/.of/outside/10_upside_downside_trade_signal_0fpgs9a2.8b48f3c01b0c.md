Buy calls when a new bullish signal appears: price is above both moving averages, the faster average is above the slower one, momentum is bullish but not overextended, and price breaks above its recent range; require bullish MACD and above-average volume when those confirmations are enabled. Also require broader market direction to agree and the option spread to be acceptable. Buy puts when a new bearish signal appears: price is below both moving averages, the faster average is below the slower one, momentum is bearish but not overextended, and price breaks below its recent range; require bearish MACD and above-average volume when enabled. Also require weakness to be confirmed and the option spread to be acceptable. The indicator displays a target and stop for each signal, but does not define exit decision logic.
