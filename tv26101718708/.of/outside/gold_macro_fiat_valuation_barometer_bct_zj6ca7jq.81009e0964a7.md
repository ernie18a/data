For gold, build a smoothed macro score from real yields, gold relative to money supply, gold trends across major currencies, the gold/silver ratio, and gold’s deviation from a money-supply-and-yield fair-value estimate. Favor long-term accumulation when the score is favorable, hold when constructive, use caution when neutral, and trim when unfavorable; signal regime entries or exits when the score crosses into the favorable or unfavorable regime. For tactical timing, buy when gold is substantially below fair value and turns upward; trim when substantially above fair value and turns downward. The indicator gives no short-entry rule.
