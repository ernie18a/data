Trade gold using the chart close and the previous completed observations of daily 10-year real yield, weekly M2, silver in USD, and gold in EUR, JPY, GBP and AUD. Forward-fill external feeds; use fixed fallback values for missing real yield and missing or nonpositive M2 or silver. Calibrate rolling windows by chart timeframe.

Compute a valuation spread as gold price minus M2 multiplied by the rolling median of the gold-to-M2 ratio and by an exponential adjustment that decreases as real yield rises above a neutral benchmark. If the median is unavailable, use the current ratio. Divide the spread by its rolling standard deviation, using a fixed positive fallback when that deviation is nonpositive.

For tactical signals, buy when the standardized spread is at or below the discount threshold and rises from its prior value. Trim when it is at or above the extension threshold and falls from its prior value. Independently latch each signal after it fires: rearm the buy after the spread rises above a near-fair-value threshold, and rearm the trim after it falls below another near-fair-value threshold. The tactical display also favors buying in discount, staggered purchases around fair value, patience at a slight premium, and waiting for a dip when extended.

For the macro score, sum weighted factor scores and smooth the total with an exponential moving average. Each factor receives its full weight for favorable conditions, zero for unfavorable conditions, and half otherwise: real yield is favorable when below its low threshold or its moving average, and unfavorable when above both its high threshold and moving average; the rolling percentile of gold divided by M2 is favorable when low and unfavorable when high; breadth is favorable when gold is above its trend EMA in at least a specified majority of the five currencies and unfavorable when at most a specified small count is above; the gold-to-silver ratio is favorable when high, or moderately high and falling, and unfavorable when low; the standardized valuation spread is favorable in discount and unfavorable when extended. Apply the favorable test before the unfavorable test for each factor.

Accumulate when the smoothed score crosses above its upper regime threshold, and trim or de-risk when it crosses below its lower regime threshold. The ongoing regime favors accumulation above the upper threshold, holding and routine purchases in the next band, caution in the next, and trimming below the lower threshold. On weekly charts, the displayed guidance also favors staggered accumulation above a separate score threshold.
