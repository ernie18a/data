Only open a position when flat, and evaluate entries at bar close. Generate four directional events from trend flips in a pivot-centered ATR trend band, a classic ATR Supertrend, a Chandelier volatility stop, and a dual-smoothed range filter. A Grand long or short requires the configurable minimum number of same-direction events within a recent bar window, with at least one such event on the current bar. Also require a recent touch of the corresponding adaptive RSI support or resistance band and a recent WaveTrend visit to the corresponding oversold or overbought zone when those filters are enabled; an optional RSI-midline filter requires a long below the midline or a short above it. An optional macro filter permits Grand entries only when an external reference instrument is above its EMA on both selected timeframes for longs, or below on both for shorts. Trend entries require the macro direction, a touch of the current instrument’s adaptive RSI entry band, and, when enabled, confirmation that the current close is on the matching side of its EMA and that WaveTrend recently visited the matching extreme zone. Suppress a Trend entry if a Grand entry fires in the same direction on that bar. Take the long or short at the signal close; allow no additional entry while a position is active. Set Grand stops at the recent swing low for longs or swing high for shorts; set Trend stops at entry plus or minus an ATR-based distance. For an active long, exit at the stop if hit, otherwise take profit when price reaches the dynamically recalculated upper RSI target band; for a short, use the stop first, otherwise the lower RSI target band. Stop and target hits may be determined by wick or close according to their configured trigger modes. If the target closes the whole position, return to flat. If it closes only part, move the remaining stop to entry and trail a stop behind favorable price movement by an ATR-based distance; exit the remainder at breakeven if that level is hit, otherwise at the trailing stop if hit. In the trailing phase, check breakeven before the trailing stop.
