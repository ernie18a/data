Use configurable periods, thresholds, multipliers, lookbacks and switches throughout. Process chart bars sequentially, allowing one position at a time. Use the chart's OHLC data for all local calculations.

For the external trend filter, compare the last completed close of a configurable reference instrument with its EMA on each of two configurable timeframes. Bullish requires both closes above their EMAs; bearish requires both below. When the filter is disabled, treat both directions as permitted.

Calculate four local directional reversal events: pivot-point SuperTrend, classic SuperTrend, Chandelier Exit using closing-price extremes, and Twin Range Filter. Count every same-direction event over the configured signal window, including repeated events from the same component; require the configured minimum count and at least one event on the current bar. For a confluence long, optionally also require a recent low at or below the corresponding RSI-derived lower entry band, the current high below the RSI-band midpoint, and a recent WaveTrend visit at or below the less extreme oversold threshold. Reverse these comparisons for a confluence short. If enabled for confluence entries, additionally require the corresponding external bullish or bearish condition.

A trend long requires the external bullish condition, a current low at or below the lower RSI entry band, and, when enabled, close above the local EMA and a recent WaveTrend oversold visit. A trend short requires a current high at or above the upper RSI entry band and, when enabled, close below the local EMA and a recent WaveTrend overbought visit. The trend short does not require the external bearish condition. Either trend direction can be independently disabled. Suppress a trend entry when a same-direction confluence entry occurs. When flat, resolve simultaneous entries in this order: confluence long, confluence short, trend long, trend short. Enter at close.

For confluence entries, fix the stop at the lowest prior low for a long or highest prior high for a short over the configured lookback, excluding the entry bar. For trend entries, fix it at entry minus or plus a configured multiple of Wilder ATR. On subsequent bars, update the target to the current upper RSI-derived profit band for longs or lower band for shorts. Test the fixed stop before the target, using independently configured wick or close triggers. A stop closes the position. A target hit closes the configured fraction; if anything remains, set its stop to entry and initialize a trailing level at close minus or plus a configured multiple of smoothed true range.

On each subsequent trailing bar, first ratchet that level upward for a long or downward for a short using the current close and volatility distance. Then test the entry-price stop before the trailing level, with their respective configured wick or close triggers; either hit closes the remainder. Use the touched level as the exit price for wick triggers and the close for close triggers. Do not evaluate exits on the entry bar or a trailing exit on the partial-target bar, and do not enter again on an exit bar.
