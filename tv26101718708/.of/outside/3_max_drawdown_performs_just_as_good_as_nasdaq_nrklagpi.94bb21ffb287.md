Go long when either (1) the broad-market ETF’s daily lows stay above its medium-term average for four days, the Nasdaq ETF’s lows are above its short- and medium-term averages, it has not fallen for three consecutive days, and broad-market trend strength is above a threshold; or (2) Bitcoin is above its short-term average, holding above its medium-term average, rising, and the broad-market ETF is above its short-term average. Block entries after an extended period without a broad-market all-time high unless price is sufficiently above its long-term average. After a major drawdown from the broad-market all-time high, hold any open position until that prior high is recovered. While holding, exit a losing position if the Nasdaq ETF closes below its medium-term average while the broad-market ETF is below its short-term average. Exit a profitable position if the broad-market short average is below its lagged long average while both markets are weak, or if the Nasdaq ETF falls below both its entry-based loss threshold and its medium-term average. Enter only when flat; do not open short positions.
