On each bar, look for a long setup when a recently confirmed swing low is below the preceding swing low and the current close breaks above the upper edge of a recent bearish fair-value gap. A bearish gap exists when the current high is below the low two bars earlier. For a short setup, require a recently confirmed swing high above the preceding swing high and a close below the lower edge of a recent bullish gap, formed when the current low is above the high two bars earlier. Confirm a swing using the highs or lows immediately on either side of the swing bar, with the confirmation delayed by one additional bar. The inversion candle must have a range above a configured multiple of ATR and a body above a configured fraction of its range. If multiple qualifying gaps are inverted, use the most recent one's broken edge as the entry level.

Permit signals during the configured core New York session, or during the configured extended session only when ATR exceeds a configured multiple of its moving average. Score each direction using weighted points for price inside a retained higher-timeframe gap of that direction, a recent corresponding swing sweep, being in the permitted session, confirmation from the external index, and sufficient stacked higher highs or lower lows in a lookback. Retain the most recently detected higher-timeframe gap of each direction, updating it from the highest-priority available timeframe in daily, four-hour, then hourly order; use the higher-timeframe data with lookahead enabled. External-index confirmation scores a point when its filter is disabled or when its current close is within a configured percentage of either its previous daily high or low. Convert the score to a grade using configured cutoffs and emit the long or short signal only if its grade meets the selected minimum. Thus the external index affects decisions through the grade, rather than acting as a mandatory filter.

Suppress repeated signals independently by direction until the corresponding flag resets. Reset the long flag on a new all-time-since-initialization high that also exceeds the previous bar's high, and the short flag on the analogous new low; reset both whenever price is outside both retained higher-timeframe gaps. Set the relevant flag when a signal passes the grade requirement.

For each signal, mark the latest corresponding swing-sweep level as the stop. Choose the nearest qualifying target in the trade direction at least one stop distance from entry, considering the relevant edges of current bullish and bearish gaps across the monitored intraday and daily timeframes, session extremes, the previous New York session extreme, and the previous daily extreme. A signal can occur without a target; these levels are displayed guidance and do not themselves trigger an exit.
