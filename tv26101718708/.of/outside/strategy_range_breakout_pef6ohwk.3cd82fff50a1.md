At each confirmed bar, go long when the close crosses above the opening-range high or previous-day high, the candle is bullish, and—unless momentum/volume filters are disabled—RSI is above its bullish threshold and current volume exceeds the configured multiple of the volume-weighted average close. Go short when the close crosses below the opening-range low or previous-day low, the candle is bearish, and—unless those filters are disabled—RSI is below its bearish threshold and current volume exceeds that same volume-weighted-average-close test. The opening range is the high and low formed during the configured opening interval. For a long, set the stop below the recent-period low by an ATR-based offset; for a short, set it above the recent-period high by an ATR-based offset. Set the profit target at the configured risk-to-reward multiple of the entry-to-stop distance, and exit on a bar close reaching the target or stop. When the market is not configured as always open, close all positions at or after the configured end-of-day time. The code makes the ATR stop and resulting target unavailable when its stop-line display option is off.
