Enter only when flat, inside the enabled trading session, and the equity guard is inactive. Evaluate long and short separately; a direction needs a trigger, every enabled directional gate, and a weighted confluence score at or above its threshold.

Triggers are: a fast/slow EMA crossover in the direction of the prevailing long-term EMA trend, with strong ADX and high volume; a close beyond the preceding price-channel extreme, on high volume and on the corresponding side of the long-term EMA; or an enabled Donchian breakout crossing the preceding channel boundary, with the same volume and EMA conditions. All require the volume floor. The coded mean-reversion triggers also require RSI to cross a threshold while remaining strictly beyond that same threshold, so they cannot fire.

The directional gates require, when enabled: higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs and close aligned with the direction; sufficient adjacent Gaussian kernel ribbon bands ordered in that direction; close on the directional side of daily-reset cumulative volume-weighted typical price; a current directional smart-money event; and close on the directional side of the Donchian midpoint. Smart-money events are an opposing-candle reversal with a small terminal wick, a sufficiently large three-bar gap, a sweep and rejection of the prior liquidity extreme, or a close crossing the latest confirmed swing extreme. Optionally block a direction when the online model strongly favors the opposite direction, and block longs in extreme volatility-index fear.

Build each directional score by summing configurable weights for the corresponding higher-timeframe, ribbon, volume-weighted price, smart-money and Donchian gates, strong ADX, directional slope of the fastest kernel, and a directional online-model vote. Disabled gates count as passing and receive their weights. Add the breadth weight to longs when the daily equity-index proxy is above its daily EMA, or breadth is disabled; add it to shorts unconditionally. Add the volatility-index weight to longs when sentiment is neutral or greed and its daily rate of change is not a panic spike; add it to shorts when sentiment is fear or there is a panic spike. The volatility-index outlook based on its moving average is display-only.

The online model is a logistic linear predictor using normalized RSI, rate of change, distance from a long EMA, ADX, fastest kernel slope, ribbon ordering difference and relative volume. On confirmed bars, train its coefficients and bias with decayed gradient updates against whether price rose over the prediction horizon, using the features from that horizon ago. Scale its vote weight by calibrated past directional accuracy. Kernel bands are endpoint Gaussian weighted averages with distinct bandwidths, optionally scaled by current normalized ATR relative to its average.

The equity guard activates when consecutive closed-trade losses or peak-equity drawdown reaches its limit. It clears when both are below their limits or its countdown expires; if a limit remains breached, it reactivates on the next calculation.

At entry, optionally scale quantity by directional score relative to the sum of nominal weights and by the daily volatility-index sentiment category; cap the latter multiplier during a panic spike. Place a partial profit limit and a common stop at entry, using an ATR-based stop selected by the ATR-percentile and ADX regime or, optionally, the opposite Donchian boundary. For the remainder, use either a trailing stop activated after a fraction of the regime profit distance with an ATR offset, or a fixed regime-based profit limit. These exit orders are set at entry. No separate adverse-move exit is executed.
