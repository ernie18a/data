For each mapped ticker, configure its call or put contract, directional trigger, confirmation level, invalidation levels and targets. On a confirmed regular-session bar, signal buying the mapped call when the underlying closes across either bullish level from below, or buying the mapped put when it closes across either bearish level from above. Require the configured cooldown since the last signal or target event. If enabled, require close above session VWAP and above the fast EMA with the fast EMA at least the slow EMA for calls; reverse these comparisons for puts. If enabled, also require volume divided by its moving average to meet the minimum. Signal invalidation when a confirmed regular-session close is below either invalidation level for calls or above it for puts. Signal a target when the current high reaches a call target while the preceding high was below it, or the current low reaches a put target while the preceding low was above it. Invalidation and target events also reset the entry-signal cooldown. The code does not enforce the narrative's market, Bitcoin, opening-range, support-hold or bounce-rejection conditions, and does not specify automatic option exits at targets or invalidation.
