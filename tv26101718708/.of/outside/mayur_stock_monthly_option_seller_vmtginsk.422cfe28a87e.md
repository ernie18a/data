On each instrument, score trend on daily, weekly, and monthly horizons. Each horizon combines normalized fast-versus-slow EMA spread, RSI momentum relative to neutral, price-versus-VWAP bias when available, and Supertrend direction; average the three horizon scores. In a clear positive regime, sell a put; in a clear negative regime, sell a call; otherwise sell both as a strangle. Place short strikes beyond the expected move estimated from annualized historical volatility and time to expiry. Estimate each option’s premium yield and probability of expiring in the money with a zero-rate Black–Scholes model. Flag the recommendation as weak when fewer than two horizons agree with a clear trend, risky when the active leg’s in-the-money probability exceeds its risk limit, thin when its yield is below the minimum target, and acceptable otherwise. For a strangle, assess the put leg. Exit or roll when price approaches the short put from above or short call from below by the configured expected-move buffer, or when a positive regime closes below the prior swing low or a negative regime closes above the prior swing high.
