On the chart timeframe and two configured higher timeframes, calculate a weighted trend score from four votes: fast minus slow EMA divided by ATR; RSI's deviation from its midpoint divided by a momentum scale; close minus VWAP divided by ATR on intraday timeframes, otherwise zero; and supertrend direction as positive for up and negative for down. Clamp the first three votes and the final weighted average to the unit interval around zero. Treat unavailable EMA, RSI, and VWAP votes as zero, but retain their weights in the denominator. If all weights are zero, use a denominator of one. Higher-timeframe scores optionally use the last closed bar. Average the three scores equally.

Above the positive range threshold, recommend selling a put; below the negative threshold, recommend selling a call; otherwise recommend selling both as a strangle. Estimate annualized realized volatility from the standard deviation of log returns, and estimate the move to the configured expiry as spot times volatility times the square root of time in years. Set the put and call strikes below and above current spot by a configured multiple of that move. Recalculate these levels on each bar.

Separately qualify the recommendation: wait if the active leg's estimated yield is unavailable. For a directional recommendation, flag a weak trend if fewer than two timeframe scores have the same sign as the overall score. Otherwise flag excessive risk if the active leg's estimated probability of expiring in the money exceeds its cap, or insufficient yield if its estimated premium divided by spot is below the minimum; otherwise mark the sale acceptable. Estimate premium and probability using zero-rate Black-Scholes with realized volatility; use the call leg for a bearish recommendation and the put leg for a bullish recommendation or strangle. The upper yield target does not restrict acceptance. The recommendation itself is displayed even when the verdict is not acceptable.

Signal exit or roll when an applicable put has spot at or below its current strike plus a configured fraction of the expected move, or an applicable call has spot at or above its current strike minus that fraction. The put applies when the overall score is at least the negative threshold, and the call applies when it is at most the positive threshold. Also signal exit or roll for a bullish regime if close falls below the lowest low of the configured preceding-bar lookback, or for a bearish regime if close rises above the highest high of that lookback. There is no trend-break signal inside the range. Recommendation-change and newly triggered exit signals alert on confirmed bar close.
