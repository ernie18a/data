On each bar, evaluate long and short setups independently. The setups are: rejection of a reference range extreme; rejection of a substantial swing's retracement zone; a confirmed break of recent structure or the opening range, or a subsequent confirmed retest; first confirmed defense of a qualified order block; a trend-aligned moving-average retest; a high-volume liquidity sweep and rejection with supporting order flow, a key-level match, or clustered sweeps; an opening or scheduled-data drive; momentum after a volatility squeeze releases; rejection of a valid fair-value gap; and a retest of a previously violated gap from the opposite side. Apply each setup's own confidence threshold and any applicable higher-timeframe opposition filter. Fair-value-gap entries also require the close to move in the entry direction from the previous bar. Opening-drive volume and candle strength adjust its confidence threshold rather than being mandatory conditions.

Calculate setup-specific confidence from the relevant combination of directional strength, distance from the mean, position within price bands and range, support or resistance rejection, realized volatility, candle quality, liquidity, moving averages, RSI, higher-timeframe trend and recent opposing structure. Adjust for session and market regime. Directional strength is a standardized composite of the traded instrument's trend, momentum, structure, volume and order flow, plus market breadth, volatility futures and term structure, technology-sector relative performance, broad-market relative strength, and divergence from S&P futures. Use QQQ price levels translated into the traded instrument's price scale among potential support, resistance and profit targets.

Issue an entry only when its setup and filters pass, a structural stop and buffered target exist, and the target offers at least the required profit from the current close. Select targets from the applicable nearby structural levels and place stops beyond the setup's invalidation level with a volatility- and level-sensitive buffer. Track each ticket separately. Exit a long when its target is reached by the bar high or, after the entry bar, its stop is reached by the bar low; reverse high and low for a short. A target can be hit on the entry bar. If both target and stop are hit on the same bar, record the target outcome.
