Score the instrument’s close as bullish or bearish relative to each of its fast, medium, and slow moving averages; sum the three signs to estimate positioning. Track consecutive bars at a fully bullish or fully bearish score and the score’s change over a short lookback. Use daily volatility data from an external volatility index. Signal long on a fast-average reclaim after a recent fully bearish score when either (a) the prior extreme run meets the longer-duration criterion, or (b) it meets the shorter-duration criterion and the score has rapidly improved; for the shorter-duration setup, require a strong bullish candle when that filter is enabled. Also signal long on a rapid score improvement and fast-average reclaim after a strongly bearish prior score, without requiring a sustained extreme run. All long signals require volatility above the configured long threshold when filtering is enabled. Apply the mirrored rules for shorts after fully or strongly bullish scores, using a fast-average break, rapid score deterioration where required, a strong bearish candle for the shorter-duration setup when enabled, and volatility below the configured short threshold. The framework defines no exit signal.
