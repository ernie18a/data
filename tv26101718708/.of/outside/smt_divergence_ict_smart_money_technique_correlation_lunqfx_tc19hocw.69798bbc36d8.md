On each bar, compare the traded instrument with a selected partner on the same timeframe. Calculate rolling close-to-close correlation; treat the pair as inverse when the current correlation is negative. Optionally require the absolute correlation to meet a minimum threshold at the confirmation bar.

Confirm swing highs and lows using the configured number of bars on each side. At each confirmed swing, record the traded price and the partner's extreme over the window centered on the swing: use the partner's high at a swing high and low at a swing low for a direct pair; reverse these for an inverse pair. Compare each swing only with the immediately preceding swing of the same type, and only if both were recorded in the same correlation mode. Update the stored swing even when no signal occurs.

At a swing high, issue a bearish short cue when exactly one of these is true: the traded high is strictly higher than its preceding swing high; the partner extreme confirms a higher high in direct mode or a lower low in inverse mode. At a swing low, issue a bullish long cue when exactly one of these is true: the traded low is strictly lower than its preceding swing low; the partner extreme confirms a lower low in direct mode or a higher high in inverse mode. Equality counts as failure to make a new extreme. For either cue, the absolute change in the partner extreme must meet the configured minimum multiple of its average bar range measured at confirmation, and that average range must be positive. Apply the optional correlation gate at confirmation. These are reversal cues; no entry execution or exit rule is specified.
