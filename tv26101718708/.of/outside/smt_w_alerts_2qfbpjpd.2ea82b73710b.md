Track confirmed swing highs and lows on the traded instrument and compare each with the prior swing of the same type. Around each traded-instrument pivot, find the corresponding comparison assets’ highest highs or lowest lows within a tolerance window. Only compare successive pivots no more than the configured maximum spacing apart. If the traded instrument’s highs move in the opposite direction from either comparison asset’s highs (one makes a higher high while the other makes a lower high, or vice versa), signal bearish. If their lows move in opposite directions (one makes a lower low while the other makes a higher low, or vice versa), signal bullish. These are divergence alerts; the script does not specify trade entries, exits, or orders.
