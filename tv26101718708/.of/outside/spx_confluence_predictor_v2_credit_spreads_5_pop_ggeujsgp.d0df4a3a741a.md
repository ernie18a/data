On each chart bar, calculate a directional score as the weighted average of available components, clamping each component to the range from bearish to bullish. Exclude disabled or unavailable components from both the numerator and denominator; use a neutral score if no weight remains. The components are: daily anchored VWAP price location plus VWAP slope; smoothed NYSE TICK normalized by a scale; a rolling z-score of NYSE up-volume minus down-volume; NYSE advance/decline normalized by a scale; a rolling z-score of the change in daily cumulative signed volume, where each bar's entire volume is signed by close versus open; MACD histogram normalized by ATR; centered and scaled money flow index; and a tiered contrarian RSI score for oversold and overbought levels. Include the RSI component only when VIX is below its regime threshold, or when VIX is disabled or unavailable. Treat unavailable component calculations as neutral when their component is otherwise counted.

Set the short put strike by rounding price minus a configured fixed or ATR-based buffer down to the strike increment; buy a put one configured spread width lower. Set the short call strike by rounding price plus the buffer up to the strike increment; buy a call one spread width higher. Estimate the expected move from a supplied value, or from ATR times the square root of the configured number of bars to expiry. Set directional drift to the score times a configured drift factor times the expected move. Estimate put survival probability as the standard normal CDF of (price plus drift minus short put strike) divided by expected move; estimate call survival probability as the CDF of (short call strike minus price minus drift) divided by expected move.

A put credit spread qualifies when the score is strictly above the negative directional threshold and its estimated probability meets the minimum. A call credit spread qualifies when the score is strictly below the positive threshold and its probability meets the minimum. Recommend both as a synthetic iron condor if both qualify, the qualifying vertical if only one qualifies, and waiting if neither qualifies. Separately, issue a put spread signal when the score crosses above the positive threshold and put probability meets the minimum; issue a call spread signal when the score crosses below the negative threshold and call probability meets the minimum.
