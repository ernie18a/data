Clamp each component to a bounded bearish-to-bullish range and omit unavailable or disabled components from the weighted average. Classify scores above a configurable positive threshold as bullish, below its negative as bearish, and otherwise neutral. For option recommendations, a bullish bias enables a put credit spread, a bearish bias enables a call credit spread, and neutral enables both sides. Place each short strike a configurable distance from market price, using ATR or a point offset; round put strikes down and call strikes up to the nearest five-point increment, and place the long leg five points farther out. Estimate each spread's probability of profit from a normal distribution using the distance from price to its short strike, expected move (manual input or ATR-based estimate), and a drift adjusted by the directional score. Recommend each enabled side only when its estimated probability meets the configurable minimum; recommend an iron condor if both qualify, the single qualifying spread if only one qualifies, and otherwise wait. Emit a bullish put-spread signal when the score crosses above the positive threshold and its probability passes the minimum; emit a bearish call-spread signal when the score crosses below the negative threshold and its probability passes the minimum.
