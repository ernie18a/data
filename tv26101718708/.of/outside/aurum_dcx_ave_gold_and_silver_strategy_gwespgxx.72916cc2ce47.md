Enter long when the traded instrument closes above its adaptive volatility envelope’s upper band, directional strength exceeds its threshold and is rising, positive directional movement leads negative directional movement, and—if enabled—the dollar index is below its trend average. Enter short when price closes below the lower band, directional strength exceeds its threshold and is falling, negative directional movement leads positive directional movement, and—if enabled—the dollar index is above its trend average. Set an initial stop using the wider of the relevant envelope boundary and a distance based on weekly volatility; set the target at a fixed multiple of the initial risk. Optionally trail the stop using the envelope and weekly volatility. Close or reverse positions when an opposing entry signal occurs.
