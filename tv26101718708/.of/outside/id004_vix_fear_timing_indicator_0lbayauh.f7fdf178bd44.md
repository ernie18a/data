Use a staged long-entry sequence driven by the traded instrument and a volatility index on the chart timeframe. Parameterize all lookbacks, thresholds, score weights, delays, and timing windows; select timeframe-specific settings and adjust required percentage declines by the chosen signal strictness.

Define volatility stress as an elevated index level, elevated rolling percentile, sharp percentile jump, or rare extreme. Strong stress means a rare extreme, an elevated level together with an elevated percentile, or a sharp jump together with either elevation. Fear cooling means basic stress and a falling index or percentile, or a rare extreme. For the final stage, cooling also passes if the percentile has fallen sufficiently from a recent very high peak, a rare extreme coincides with fear cooling, or the percentile is falling while still elevated after a recent very high peak. Missing index data passes the index gates, but contributes no index score.

Block entries during a fast, unusually high-volume decline with price below the fast exponential average. Also block entries when higher-timeframe price and the shorter higher-timeframe exponential average are below the longer average and higher-timeframe RSI is weak and not rising. Override this regime block only during a rare index extreme when higher-timeframe RSI or MACD histogram is improving and local price shows a structure shift, bullish candle, or close above the fast average.

Start a sequence with a long signal only after the post-reset cooldown, when price has declined sufficiently from its recent high, RSI was recently oversold and is now rising through or above a recovery level, and price closes above the fast average or the previous high. With the index filter enabled, require basic stress in the standard mode or strong stress in the strict mode, and require a minimum weighted quality score. Score the required setup conditions plus RSI reclaim, lower Bollinger-band reclaim, support reclaim, bullish RSI divergence, lower-wick rejection, improving MACD histogram, bullish structure shift, volume confirmation, strong index stress, rare index extremes, and fear cooling. Disable both this score gate and the index gate when the index filter is off. Record the signal close and bar.

For the first additional long, wait until the low reaches a specified percentage below the initial close while the close remains below that close, within the allowed window. Arm the stage and wait its confirmation delay. Then, still within the window, require a recovery candle, the common regime and crash gates, and technical confirmation: bullish divergence with rising RSI; a lower-band reclaim with wick rejection, bullish candle, or improving MACD; support reclaim or wick rejection with rising RSI or sufficient volume; or a sufficient weighted score of those recovery features. A recovery candle means a bullish candle or a close above the previous high, fast average, or stage price zone. With the index filter enabled, standard mode requires strong stress and strict mode requires a rare extreme or strong stress with fear cooling; also require a minimum score combining the technical score with basic stress, strong stress, rare extremes, and cooling. Record the signal close and bar.

For the second additional long, within its window arm when the low reaches a specified percentage below the first additional-entry close and the close is below the equal-weight average of the first two entry closes. After its confirmation delay, require a recovery candle, the common gates, and technical confirmation. Confirmation is higher-timeframe momentum improvement with local recovery; capitulation consisting of a volume spike, strong lower wick, and large range, either immediately or followed by a bullish recovery candle; a bullish structure shift with rising RSI; or a sufficient weighted score of these conditions, wick rejection, bullish engulfing, volume spike, and higher-timeframe MACD improvement. A recovery candle means a bullish candle, a close above the previous high, a structure shift, or a close above the stage price zone. With the index filter enabled, require final-stage index cooling; strict mode additionally requires a rare recent percentile peak or current rare extreme. Require a minimum score combining the technical score with index stress, rare extremes, fear cooling, recent high fear, and final-stage cooling.

Adapt each stage's timing window from historical successful intervals once enough samples exist, using lower and upper sample percentiles; otherwise use timeframe-specific defaults. Keep the minimum at least the default minimum and the maximum above the minimum. Reset the sequence after either pending stage's window expires or after a fixed period following the final stage, then impose the cooldown. Resetting is internal state management, not an exit signal. The indicator produces no short or exit decisions.
