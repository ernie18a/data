Use Bitcoin market capitalization, realized capitalization, and the selected Bitcoin price series. Divide market capitalization by realized capitalization, and derive net unrealized profit or loss from that ratio. Build a price-position proxy by subtracting the price's position within a long-term high-low range from its position within a short-term range, then smooth the result. Standardize each component against its rolling mean and standard deviation. Combine available standardized components using their configured weights, provided their available weight meets the minimum fraction of total weight. Subtract a moving average of the composite to obtain a cycle oscillator. Consider a long entry when the oscillator is at or below the extreme-bear threshold, or when it enters the positive early-bull range after a bear phase. Hold during the bull phase, when the oscillator is above the early-bull limit but at or below the overheated threshold. Reduce or exit the long position in stages when the oscillator exceeds the overheated threshold. No specific short decision is defined.
