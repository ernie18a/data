For a bullish swing setup, require every enabled trend filter to pass: close above the intermediate EMA, short EMA above intermediate EMA, intermediate EMA rising, and optionally close above the long-term SMA. Require quality filters: relative-strength rating and relative volume meet their configured minimums, optional average share-volume and dollar-volume liquidity floors pass, and optionally the sector ETF is up on the day. If an undercut-and-reclaim or near-support tap is active, quality may instead pass using the configured relaxed relative-strength and volume minimums, while retaining liquidity filters and waiving the sector filter. A reclaim trigger occurs when a recent daily low undercut the short or intermediate EMA, or a recent weekly low undercut the weekly EMA, and the current corresponding close is back above it; require a bullish reclaim candle when enabled. A tap trigger occurs when price tests just above one of those averages within its configured proximity band and closes back above it. Alternatively, a pullback trigger occurs when the daily low touches the configured zone above the short or intermediate EMA, the close is above that average and in the configured upper portion of the candle range, and the candle is bullish when required. If trend and quality pass but no enabled trigger fires, classify as watch; if both pass and a trigger fires, classify as ready; if either trend or effective quality fails, classify as not ready. Candlestick patterns and setup scores are informational and do not gate these outcomes. This logic produces bullish readiness only; it defines no short, exit, or options decisions.
