On each bar, identify a long-ready setup when all enabled trend filters pass, quality passes, and an enabled trigger fires. Use daily price and volume data and an optional weekly moving average, regardless of chart timeframe.

Trend: require each enabled condition: daily close above the intermediate exponential moving average, the faster exponential average above the intermediate average, the intermediate average rising, and close above a longer simple moving average.

Quality: require the stock's relative-strength rating against a broad-market index and its relative volume to meet their configured thresholds, plus any enabled minimum average share volume, minimum average dollar volume, and positive sector-ETF return filters. Calculate dollar volume as daily close times average daily volume. Calculate relative volume as current daily volume, or projected end-of-day volume after a minimum elapsed session time, divided by the average volume of prior closed daily sessions; round the percentage before comparison. Derive relative strength from weighted stock and index price ratios over several historical windows, then map their ratio to a rating using the external rating-curve data. Select the sector ETF from the stock's sector, falling back to a broad-market ETF.

An undercut-and-reclaim signal occurs when a recent low was below its corresponding fast or intermediate daily exponential average and the current daily close is above that average, optionally requiring a bullish daily candle. Apply the analogous test to the optional weekly average using weekly low, close and candle color. A tap occurs when no undercut of that average occurred in its lookback, the current low is strictly above the average but within a configured percentage above it, and the close is above it. Any undercut or tap activates relaxed quality when enabled: use the relaxed relative-strength and relative-volume thresholds, retain the liquidity filters, and waive the sector filter. Standard quality and relaxed quality are alternative ways to pass.

The trigger passes if enabled undercut-and-reclaim or tap signals occur, or if an enabled pullback occurs: the daily low is at or above either daily exponential average and within its configured zone above it, the close is above that average and sufficiently high within the candle's range, and, if required, the close is at least the open. Candlestick patterns do not gate readiness. If trend and quality pass without a trigger, watch; otherwise the setup is not ready. No short or coded exit decision is specified.
