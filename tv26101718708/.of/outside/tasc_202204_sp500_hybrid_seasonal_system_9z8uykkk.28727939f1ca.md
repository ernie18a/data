On each bar, sample the closing value of a configurable external volatility index at its configured timeframe. Calculate its percentage increase relative to the lowest value in the preceding lookback window. Calculate the traded instrument's ATR and its percentage increase relative to the lowest ATR in the preceding lookback window.

Calculate a money-flow indicator from the traded instrument: use typical price changes and the standard deviation of log typical-price changes to form a price-scaled cutoff. Cap volume at a multiple of its previous-bar moving average, assign that volume a positive or negative sign when the typical-price change exceeds the positive or negative cutoff, and otherwise assign zero. Sum the signed volumes over a lookback, divide by the previous-bar average volume, substitute zero for an unavailable result, and exponentially smooth it. Also calculate a simple moving average of this indicator.

While inside the optional observation dates and equity is positive, submit a long entry when the current month is in the entry season, defined as at or after a fixed start month or before the configurable sell month, and the preceding bar satisfies both: money flow is above its critical threshold, and either the index increase is below its threshold or the ATR increase is below that threshold multiplied by a configurable ratio.

Submit an exit, or a short entry if shorting is enabled, for each of these conditions: the current month is the sell month; the preceding bar's index increase exceeds a multiple of its threshold; or, on the preceding bar, money flow crossed its critical threshold in either direction while its moving average was falling. Evaluate the long order before these exit or short orders. Signals can recur on every qualifying bar. Outside the observation window or when equity is not positive, close all positions. Use the strategy's normal next-bar order execution.
