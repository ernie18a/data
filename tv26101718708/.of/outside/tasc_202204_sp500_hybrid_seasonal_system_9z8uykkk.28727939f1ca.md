Enter long during the seasonal holding period, from October until the configured seasonal exit month, only when the prior bar showed the money-flow indicator above its bearish threshold and either implied volatility (external volatility index rising from its recent low) or historical volatility (instrument ATR rising from its recent low) is below its high-volatility cutoff. Exit on the first bar of the seasonal exit month, after an extreme rise in the external volatility index, or after the money-flow indicator crosses below its bearish threshold while its moving average is declining. If short trading is enabled, enter short on those exit signals instead of closing the long; otherwise close the long.
