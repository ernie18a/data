Go long when all conditions hold: the smoothed percentage change in the log ratio of the instrument’s close to a benchmark close is above its moving average, above a positive strength threshold, at a recent lookback high, and below an overbought ceiling; the instrument’s RSI reached an oversold level during a recent lookback and is now rising; its short-term EMA is above a lagged simple moving average; average volume exceeds a minimum; and the benchmark’s recent rate of change and the instrument’s correlation with it satisfy the market-risk rule (benchmark decline above a cutoff requires positive correlation, while a decline below that cutoff requires sufficiently negative correlation). While long, exit if the relative-strength measure falls below a negative threshold while correlation with the benchmark is strong, the holding period reaches its limit, or the instrument reaches its profit target. The rules generate no short entries.
