Take a long position in the charted instrument when either (1) the daily SPX low has stayed above its 21-day EMA for four consecutive days, the daily QQQ low is above both its 10-day and 21-day EMAs, QQQ has not closed below each of the prior three daily closes, and SPX daily ADX indicates a trend; or (2) Bitcoin’s daily 10-day EMA is above its 21-day EMA, its low is above its 21-day EMA, it closes up on the day, and SPX closes above its 7-day average. Require the SPX close to be above its 200-day EMA by a configured margin if SPX has gone a configured number of bars without making a new high. Enter only after the configured start date, when flat, sizing the position from equity, leverage, and instrument price. Do not open short positions.

While long, place a stop below the instrument’s average entry price by the configured hard-stop percentage, and close the position if equity falls by the configured percentage from its peak during the trade. Track SPX’s all-time high. Once SPX falls by the configured crash threshold from that high, arm a recovery hold; if a position is open, hold it until SPX returns to the high that preceded the crash. This hold suppresses normal exits, but not the hard stop or equity drawdown exit.

Outside a recovery hold, close a losing position when QQQ closes below both its QQQ entry price and 21-day EMA while SPX closes below its 7-day average. Close a position that is at or above its QQQ entry price if either SPX’s 7-day average is below its prior-day 107-day average while SPX and QQQ close below their respective 7-day and 21-day averages, or QQQ’s low falls below the configured loss threshold from its QQQ entry price and is below its 50-day average.
