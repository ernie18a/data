Signal a buy on any of: (1) oversold RSI with a strongly negative deviation, or somewhat less oversold RSI with a negative deviation and high volatility; (2) a very negative deviation with high volatility, or a more extreme negative deviation with even higher volatility; (3) the index-to-average ratio at or below a configurable deep-value threshold, with a small threshold buffer allowed when volatility is high. Signal a sell on any of: (1) a cross below the index’s short-term average within a few bars after overbought RSI and a strongly positive deviation coincide; (2) that downward cross with an extremely positive deviation and very low volatility; (3) that downward cross within a few bars after the index-to-average ratio reaches a configurable euphoria threshold. Suppress repeat signals of each tier until its cooldown has elapsed.
