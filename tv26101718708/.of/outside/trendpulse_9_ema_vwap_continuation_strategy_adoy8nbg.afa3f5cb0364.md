On confirmed bars within the configured trading window, allow a long only when the fast trend average is above the slower average and price is above the dynamic basis; allow a short under the inverse alignment. Require the corresponding directional quality score to meet its configured minimum, adequate average dollar liquidity, sufficient relative volume when enabled, and benchmark price on the matching side of its long-term average when benchmark confirmation is enabled. Apply the configured cooldown and long-only/short-only restrictions. A qualifying trigger is either a close beyond the ATR-based channel and beyond the prior bar’s extreme, or a continuation: price maintains the matching trend alignment, recently pulls back near the fast average or basis, respects the basis retest boundary when enabled, then closes beyond the prior bar’s extreme while remaining on the matching side of the fast average. The directional quality score combines regime quality, relative volume, directional momentum, candle-body strength, channel extension, and fast/slow average separation. Enter only if the selected entry mode permits the trigger and the risk- and allocation-limited position size is positive. For an open position, place a stop based on the entry risk and a profit target at a configured reward-to-risk multiple; after price reaches one unit of initial risk in profit, optionally move the stop to break-even with a buffer and enable ATR trailing. Close at the stop, target, or configured holding-time limit; a qualifying opposite entry closes the current position and reverses it.
