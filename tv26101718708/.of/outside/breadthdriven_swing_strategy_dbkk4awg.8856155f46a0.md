Trade the S&P 500 instrument long-only using the percentage of its constituents trading above their own long-term moving averages. Enter when the smoothed breadth series forms a sufficiently prominent local trough below an oversold threshold, or when a faster-smoothed breadth series forms a sufficiently prominent local trough while recent raw breadth reaches an extreme-low threshold. Exit at the first sufficiently prominent local peak of the slower-smoothed breadth series above an overbought threshold. Attach a fixed percentage stop below the long entry price.
