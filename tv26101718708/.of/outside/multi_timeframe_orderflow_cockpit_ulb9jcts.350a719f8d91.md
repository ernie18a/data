For each enabled timeframe, estimate buy-versus-sell volume from intrabar flow, combine its directional imbalance with recent swing-break direction, and reduce conviction when price direction conflicts with flow. Scale conviction by flow imbalance, then average timeframe scores with greater influence from longer timeframes. Optionally add an inverse vote from an external volatility index: rising volatility is bearish and falling volatility is bullish. Convert the combined score to a probability: take a long bias when it is clearly bullish, a short bias when clearly bearish, and otherwise remain neutral; the indicator defines no exit rule. An optional reversion mode instead signals short when price is near the top of its recent range while cumulative flow fails to confirm strength, or long near the bottom when flow fails to confirm weakness; an optional flow-toxicity filter can require unusually one-sided flow. Historical calibration reports signal performance but does not gate these directions.
