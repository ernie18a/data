On each bar, evaluate signals using the previous completed bar’s data. A bullish setup requires its close above its Hull moving average plus an ATR buffer, ADX above its threshold with positive directional movement above negative directional movement, CCI above its positive threshold, and volume above its smoothed volume times a multiplier. A bearish setup requires the mirrored price, directional movement, and CCI conditions, with the same volume condition. All three directional conditions are mandatory; their count must also meet the configured minimum score.

If enabled, require these additional gates: (1) The Hurst estimate must indicate a trending regime. Calculate it as the logarithm of the rescaled range divided by the logarithm of the window length, using cumulative deviations of prior closes from their window mean and the standard deviation of those closes. The coded mean-reversion alternative cannot pass alongside the corresponding directional setup. (2) Multiply the directional score by a linearly declining factor based on the consecutive confirmed bars for which all directional conditions and volume have held; require the result to meet the minimum score. (3) Divide the latest absolute close-to-close change by ATR, then divide that by the ratio of smoothed absolute close changes to smoothed ATR; reject an entry if the resulting momentum ratio exceeds its threshold. (4) Calculate rolling Pearson correlation between the traded instrument’s previous close and a separately configured instrument’s close shifted further back by the configured lag. If the absolute correlation meets the threshold, allow longs only for positive correlation and shorts only for negative correlation; otherwise allow either direction.

Emit a long or short only when its complete entry condition changes from false to true and the shared minimum bar gap since the last emitted signal has elapsed. Update the gap counter on confirmed bars; a transition suppressed by the gap is not emitted later unless the condition becomes false and then true again.

Independently signal exit from a long when the faster CCI crosses below zero, and exit from a short when it crosses above zero, using the two most recent completed bars. For each entry, report the previous close as entry price, a stop at a configured ATR multiple against the position, and three targets at configured ATR multiples in its favor. These levels are projections, not additional coded exit triggers.
