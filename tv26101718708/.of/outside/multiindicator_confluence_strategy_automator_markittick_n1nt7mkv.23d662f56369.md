On each completed bar, signal long when the prior close is above the Hull moving average by an ATR-based buffer, ADX exceeds its threshold with positive directional movement greater than negative, CCI exceeds its positive threshold, and volume exceeds its rolling average times its sensitivity factor. Signal short on the inverse conditions. Apply enabled filters: decay must leave the directional confluence score above its minimum; the Hurst regime must be trending, or mean-reverting while the opposite-direction base setup is present; a sufficiently strong cross-asset correlation must have the same sign as the trade direction (weak correlation passes); and momentum inflation must not exceed its threshold. Emit only on a transition into a valid setup and when the minimum gap since any prior signal has elapsed. Exit a long when the faster CCI crosses below zero; exit a short when it crosses above zero. Signals and exits are evaluated from completed bars.
