Compute net liquidity as the central bank balance sheet minus the treasury account and reverse-repurchase balance. Derive the selected index’s fair value by scaling net liquidity and subtracting an offset, then calculate the index’s premium or discount to fair value. Trade only when net liquidity is positive: a crossing from below to above the overbought threshold is an overbought signal; a crossing from above to below the oversold threshold is an oversold signal. After the configured start date, in long/short mode, enter short on overbought and long on oversold; reverse those directions when inverse mode is enabled. In short-only mode, enter short on overbought and exit on oversold; inverse mode instead enters long on overbought and exits on oversold. In long-only mode, enter long on oversold and exit on overbought; inverse mode instead enters short on oversold and exits on overbought. When enabled, the configured close date closes open positions.
