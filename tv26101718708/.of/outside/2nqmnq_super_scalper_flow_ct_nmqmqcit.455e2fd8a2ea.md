Generate long and short signals from two reversal engines. The main engine considers: a market-structure reversal that breaks the prior opposing swing; a divergence where the traded market makes a new swing extreme but correlated ES does not; price or RSI divergence, including hidden RSI divergence; price-versus-order-flow delta divergence; rejection or trend hold at a high-volume zone; capitulation followed by a reversal close; small-range, high-volume absorption; and unusually dominant buying or selling flow. Require each setup’s confirmation score to clear its own threshold, with higher-timeframe directional checks where applicable. For delta divergence, also require corroborating structure, sweep, absorption, or trend context. Reject trades whose next relevant target leaves insufficient room for the minimum profit.

The countertrend engine permits longs in a downtrend or consolidation and shorts in an uptrend or consolidation. Require adequate volume and volatility, a direction-specific confidence score above its adaptive baseline, higher-timeframe alignment within a permissive gate, and exhaustion confirmation from trend strength or lack of persistence. Trigger on overextension with reversal evidence, price/flow divergence, a failed liquidity sweep, aggressive flow reversal, absorption, or a retest and rejection of a high-volume zone. Score exhaustion, distance from trend averages and VWAP, volatility bands, flow, structure, and regime; subtract penalties for strong ongoing momentum and conflicting divergence. Require setup confidence to meet its adaptive or setup-specific floor and enough room to the next opposing target. Only one countertrend entry per direction per bar.

For either engine, place stops beyond relevant swing structure; for sweep fades, beyond the sweep extreme. Place targets at the next suitable opposing structural, VWAP, volume-profile, or session level, with a buffer. Exit when the target or stop is reached; stops become active after the entry bar.
