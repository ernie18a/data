On each bar, compute a volatility measure by summing (rolling highest close − current low) / rolling highest close and (rolling lowest close − current high) / rolling lowest close, expressed as percentages. Compute a moving average of each component and scale each by its own configurable factor. Also compute a moving average of price for an optional entry filter, a MACD line on a configurable timeframe, and an extreme-volatility flag: the summed measure is extreme-high when it is above its Bollinger upper band or at/above a scaled rolling maximum and positive; it is extreme-low when it is below its Bollinger lower band or at/below a scaled rolling minimum and negative.

A buy signal occurs when the summed measure crosses above the scaled average of the first component while MACD is negative, or when it is above that average and extreme-high. Allow the long entry only when enabled price filters pass: price is below a configurable ceiling and, if enabled, below its moving average. A sell signal occurs when the summed measure crosses below the scaled average of the second component while MACD is positive, or when it is below that average and extreme-low. Enter short on a sell signal when the optional short-entry price threshold condition passes. Close open longs on a sell signal; close open shorts on a buy signal when shorting is enabled. Optional risk and time exits close positions at a configured loss stop, at the underlying contract’s expiration, or after a configured number of bars held.
