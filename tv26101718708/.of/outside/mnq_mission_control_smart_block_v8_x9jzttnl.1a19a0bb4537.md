During the regular market session, calculate separate long and short scores. For each direction, award two points for signal-timeframe trend alignment and one point each for confirmation-timeframe trend alignment, price on the directional side of session VWAP, sufficiently strong directional momentum, and RSI within the directional range. A trend is aligned when price is on the directional side of the trend EMA and the fast EMA is on the same side of the slow EMA.

Optionally add separate points for advance-decline breadth, normalized advancing-minus-declining volume, smoothed TICK with cumulative TICK rising or falling over both short and longer windows, and the traded instrument's relative performance against S&P futures since the session open. Breadth supports a direction when its level supports it without a decisive opposing slope, or when its slope decisively supports it. Relative performance supports a direction when its level or slope supports it and neither decisively conflicts. Missing enabled internals or relative-performance data prevents a signal.

Select the higher-scoring direction; a tie means wait. A trade-ready signal requires the selected score to exceed the GO threshold, lead the opposite score by the required margin, and, when enabled, have both signal-timeframe trend and VWAP aligned. Scale score thresholds when optional scoring components are disabled. Downgrade the signal if VIX moves strongly against the direction or multiple internals oppose it. Exceptional relative strength can reduce a two-internal opposition count, but cannot remove an all-internals opposition veto. A lower qualifying score produces caution; otherwise wait. If close confirmation is enabled, activate GO only after a completed signal candle confirms it, and disarm immediately when live conditions cease to qualify.

For the selected direction, calculate a stop beyond the nearest valid swing, slow EMA, VWAP, or enabled manual structure on the invalidation side, with a volatility buffer and minimum noise floor; use a volatility-based fallback if no structure is available. Calculate the target from the nearest enabled swing, prior-session extreme, initial-balance extreme, prior confirmed current-session extreme, volume-profile value-area edge, or manual structure ahead of price. In scalp mode cap the target at the planned objective; otherwise use a volatility projection if no target exists. Block GO or caution when the required stop exceeds the enabled scalp limit, target-to-stop reward/risk is too low, or available scalp room is too small.

When the pre-veto GO score, signal, confirmation and regime trends, VWAP, and momentum all align, treat a nearby current-session extreme, swing, or initial-balance level as a checkpoint and measure room to the next eligible destination or scalp objective. A nearby prior-session extreme, value-area edge, or manual level remains terminal.

Also block a directional setup on adverse divergence: price makes a recent extreme while cumulative TICK moves against it over both breadth windows. Downgrade divergence to a warning only when full live GO conditions, all trend frames, VWAP and momentum align, at least two internals support the direction, no more than one opposes it, internals and VIX do not conflict, and momentum and climax conditions do not deteriorate. Block exhaustion near directional swing structure and the corresponding VWAP band unless a strong continuation or fresh strong-close session-extreme expansion suppresses that exhaustion block. Divergence, poor reward/risk, inadequate room, and excessive stop distance are not suppressed by expansion.

For an otherwise unblocked GO, mark it extended and wait for a pullback when enough extension flags occur: proximity to the far edge of the session range, climax, excessive VWAP stretch, proximity to a value-area or manual-structure edge, and hard adverse divergence. In a confirmed trend-day or strict continuation regime, ignore the ordinary range-edge flag, use a wider VWAP-stretch threshold, and require at least one serious flag. Otherwise issue GO long or GO short; label qualifying checkpoint or divergence-warning cases as continuation. The framework produces entry guidance, not an exit decision.
