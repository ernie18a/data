On each daily close, enter long only when flat and all of the following hold:
- The last completed weekly close is above its weekly simple moving average, that average is higher than a specified number of weeks earlier, and weekly RSI meets its minimum.
- The daily close is above the specified intermediate and long simple moving averages and the short exponential moving average; the intermediate simple moving average is at least as high as a specified number of bars earlier. Daily RSI is not below its damage threshold, the current low is above the lowest low of the preceding specified lookback, and negative directional movement does not exceed positive directional movement while ADX is rising.
- The close exceeds the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback, exceeds the open, and lies at or above a specified fraction of the bar's range measured from the low. Its distance above the short exponential moving average, in ATR units, does not exceed the extension limit.
- Average daily close times volume over the liquidity lookback meets its minimum.
- A setup score reaches its minimum. Award one point for each: RSI has remained within a specified range throughout its lookback; RSI has turned upward after being flat or falling; RSI crosses above its simple moving average; that average is flat or rising; the fast MACD line crosses above its signal or its histogram rises; positive directional movement exceeds negative directional movement or crossed above it within a specified recent window; and the ratio of the instrument's close to the benchmark's previous completed daily close has risen over its comparison lookback.

Set the initial stop at the lowest low of the specified swing lookback minus an ATR buffer. Size the position as the whole number of shares obtained by dividing the specified fraction of equity by the greater of the close-to-stop distance and a specified ATR floor; enter only if this quantity is positive. While long, maintain a separate trailing stop at the highest high observed since the position became active minus an ATR multiple, allowing that stop only to rise. Exit intrabar if either stop is hit. Also exit on the daily close when the close is below the intermediate simple moving average, RSI is below its trend-failure threshold, and negative directional movement exceeds positive directional movement. Use configurable periods, thresholds, multipliers, benchmark and risk fraction throughout.
