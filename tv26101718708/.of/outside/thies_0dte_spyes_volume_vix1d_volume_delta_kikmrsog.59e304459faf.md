Combine volume from the cash ETF and its related futures contract. Sign each instrument’s volume positive when its bar closes at or above its open and negative otherwise; sum and smooth these signed volumes. Compare total volume with its moving average. When volume is above average and signed flow is positive and strengthening, with short-term implied volatility stable or falling, buy calls or call spreads. When volume is above average and signed flow is negative and weakening, with implied volatility rising, buy puts or put spreads. Consider a reversal when signed flow turns against the prevailing price move on rising volume; if implied volatility also spikes, fade the initial move. Stand aside when volume is below average or flow is flat. Exit if flow turns against the position or price breaks the combined-volume average; close positions before the session ends and avoid opening new ones late in the session.
