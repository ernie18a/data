Confirm support and resistance bases from a five-bar swing pattern: a low (or high) three bars ago is lower (or higher) than the two preceding bars, the two following bars reverse away from it, and volume at the swing exceeds its moving average. Use the latest confirmed support for longs and resistance for shorts. When the base is within the allowed age, enter long if the close falls below support by the configured percentage, or enter short if it rises above resistance by that percentage. Suppress repeated entries on consecutive qualifying bars unless enabled or a new base forms. Apply any configured date and session filters to entries. While a position is open, add safety orders at progressively stepped adverse price levels, increasing their size according to the configured scaling. Exit at a favorable price move from the position’s average entry, or, if enabled and set beyond the deepest safety-order level, at a stop-loss move from the initial entry; reverse these price directions for shorts.
