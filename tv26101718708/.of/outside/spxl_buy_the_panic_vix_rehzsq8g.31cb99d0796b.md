Track position stage and enforce a cooldown between trades. Buy an initial tranche when the external volatility index has reached a panic level within a recent lookback and has since fallen materially from its recent peak. Add a second tranche under the same volatility conditions after the initial buy. Add a final tranche only if volatility recently reached an extreme panic level, after the second add. Pause further adds when the open position’s drawdown exceeds the risk limit. After selling half and retaining a core holding, reload when panic conditions recur, volatility has fallen materially from its recent peak, the cooldown has elapsed, and enough cash is available. Sell half of the holding once the volatility index has remained in its low-volatility zone for the confirmation period, provided the instrument’s RSI is overbought, its price is sufficiently above its long-term moving average, and price is above that average. The partial sale leaves a core position; the state rules prevent repeated adds or reloads outside their designated stages.
