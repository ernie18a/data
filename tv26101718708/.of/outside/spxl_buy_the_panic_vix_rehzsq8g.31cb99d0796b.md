Use the external volatility index close at the traded instrument’s timeframe. On each bar, find its highest close over a configurable lookback. A panic reversal occurs when that high reached the panic threshold and the current index is strictly below the high by more than a configurable percentage. An extreme panic reversal also requires the high to have reached the higher extreme threshold.

Track a position stage: flat, initial entry, second entry, fully added, reduced, or reloaded. Start flat. After a configurable cooldown since the last buy, enter long on a panic reversal when flat; add on a panic reversal after the initial entry; and add on an extreme panic reversal after the second entry. Block the latter two adds if the traded instrument’s close has fallen more than a configurable percentage below the actual average position price. Size each entry as a configurable fraction of current equity divided by close, rounded down to whole shares with a minimum order size.

Count consecutive bars on which the volatility index is at or below a configurable low-volatility threshold, resetting the count otherwise. Only on the bar when the count first reaches a configurable confirmation length, reduce the position if the stage is initial entry, second entry, fully added, or reloaded, the traded instrument’s RSI exceeds its threshold, and its close is both above its long-period exponential moving average and above that average multiplied by a configurable stretch factor. Sell a configurable fraction of the internally tracked shares, rounded down with a minimum order size; set the tracked shares to the sold quantity and the stage to reduced.

From the reduced stage, after the buy cooldown, reload on a panic reversal if available cash, calculated as equity less position value, exceeds a configurable multiple of the current share price. Buy a configurable fraction of available cash divided by close, rounded down with a minimum order size, and mark the stage reloaded. Reset to flat and clear tracked shares when an actual long position becomes flat. Otherwise hold.
