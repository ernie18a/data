When flat and within the configured trading window, enter long at the next bar’s open if the close is above the higher-timeframe EMA and caution is false. Caution is true when the close is above the higher-timeframe EMA and either the recent swing high exceeds the current low by more than an ATR-based threshold, or the close is below the chart-timeframe EMA. While long, maintain a trailing stop at the recent high of the selected price source minus an ATR distance. Ratchet the stop upward only; tighten the ATR distance when the prior bar was in caution. Exit at the next bar’s open when a confirmed close falls below the trailing stop or the higher-timeframe EMA.
