Build bullish and bearish confluence scores from structure breaks, liquidity sweeps, fair-value-gap and order-block reactions, trend alignment, higher-timeframe context, premium/discount position, session, volume, and volatility. Set directional bias only when one score leads clearly; permit a directional setup only when its score reaches an adaptive, market-sensitive minimum. Suppress entries during time blackouts, compressed low-volume conditions without expanding momentum, or when opposing signals indicate trap risk. Prioritize: demand reaction with bullish confirmation → long; supply reaction with bearish confirmation → short; failed order-block retest as support/resistance → long/short; volume-confirmed support/resistance break → wait for retest in break direction; otherwise wait on weak breaks. Also allow reversal setups after a sell-side sweep and bullish structure change (long), or buy-side sweep and bearish structure change (short), including aligned higher-timeframe sweep or fair-value-gap retests. Trade high-quality gap or inversion retests in their direction, aligned breakouts after a retest, and trend continuation from discount in an uptrend or premium in a downtrend. For Bitcoin, allow cycle-discount reversal after a sell-side sweep and bullish structure change, or cycle-premium rejection with bearish confirmation. In neutral ranges, act only at liquidity edges: buy sell-side sweeps and sell buy-side sweeps; avoid middle entries. Require a multi-part checklist for READY status; otherwise wait for confirmation. Risk governance can block trading for lockout, unapproved research, extreme tail risk, bad conditions, or trap risk, and can reduce exposure for elevated risk or conflicting context. No explicit position-exit rule is defined.
