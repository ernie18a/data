Fetch the closes of configurable US and global equity proxies, investment-grade and high-yield bond proxies, two- and ten-year yields, a dollar index, a volatility index, and a Treasury-bill proxy on the calculation timeframe with no lookahead and gaps filled. On chart bars, form eight components: the log returns over a configurable window of the two equity proxies; the investment-grade/high-yield price ratio; the two-year yield; the ten-year minus two-year yield spread; the dollar index; the volatility index; and the Treasury-bill/US-equity price ratio. Standardize each using its rolling mean and standard deviation over a configurable lookback; use zero when the score is unavailable or its standard deviation is zero. Reverse the signs of the credit ratio, two-year yield, dollar index, volatility index and Treasury-bill ratio scores. Optionally cap each score symmetrically at a configurable limit. Assign either equal weights or weights proportional to the inverse of each score's rolling standard deviation raised to a configurable exponent; a zero standard deviation gives zero weight. Normalize available weights to sum to one, or use zero normalization if their sum is zero, and exponentially smooth the weighted sum to obtain the regime index. Compute all rolling operations on the chart-bar series after fetching the data.

A long signal occurs when the index crosses above its long entry threshold, its change over a configurable number of chart bars is positive, and the traded instrument's close is above its moving average if the price filter is enabled; it also occurs whenever the index is at or above the always-long floor, independently of those gates. A short signal occurs when the index crosses below its short entry threshold, its change over that lookback is negative, and the close is below its moving average if the filter is enabled. Crosses require the previous index to be on or beyond the opposite side of the threshold.

Process orders on bar close in this order: if long trading is enabled and there is a long signal, close both sides and enter long; if long trading is enabled and the index crosses below the long exit line or there is a short signal, close long; if short trading is enabled and there is a short signal, close both sides and enter short; finally, close short if short trading is enabled and the index crosses above the short exit line or there is a long signal, or whenever the index is below the forced short-exit guard regardless of enabled side. Repeated always-long signals close and re-enter the long position.
