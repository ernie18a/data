Calculate Heikin-Ashi candles from the traded instrument and use their true range and Wilder-smoothed ATR to maintain fast and slow SuperTrend bands. Each lower band is the midpoint of the Heikin-Ashi high and low minus an ATR multiple, held at the greater of its current and previous value when the previous Heikin-Ashi close was above the previous band. Calculate the upper band symmetrically, holding the lesser value when the previous close was below it. Start in an uptrend; switch up when the Heikin-Ashi close exceeds the previous upper band, and switch down when it falls below the previous lower band. Select the fast trend for fast mode and the slow trend otherwise.

In either unfiltered mode, generate a raw long or short signal when the selected trend flips up or down. In breakout mode, generate a raw long when the trend is up and the current real-price high exceeds the highest high of the preceding breakout window; generate a raw short symmetrically using the low. Alternate raw breakout directions strictly, updating the remembered direction even when a later filter blocks the signal.

When primary signals are enabled, suppress both directions within the configured window around a qualifying calendar event. The calendar's currency and impact selections also restrict the events considered. Optionally restrict the blackout further to high-impact events. When the macro filter is enabled, compare the selected daily dollar-index and/or Treasury-yield close series with their exponential averages calculated on the chart's mapped series. Suppress longs when the selected series is above its average, or when both are above if both are selected. Suppress shorts when the selected series is not above its average, or when neither is above if both are selected. A missing close counts as not above. Otherwise emit the raw direction.

Separately, generate continuation signals in the selected trend direction when the Heikin-Ashi candle has the matching color, is within the allowed initial bars of its color run, has a body at least the configured fraction of both Heikin-Ashi ATR and candle range, and has an opposing wick no larger than the configured fraction of range. Require the configured gap since the last raw continuation candidate of either direction; update that timestamp before applying the remaining filters. Emit the continuation only if enabled and there was no emitted primary signal on the current or previous bar. News and macro filters do not directly suppress continuation signals.

The displayed profit targets, stop and trailing level do not generate exit decisions.
