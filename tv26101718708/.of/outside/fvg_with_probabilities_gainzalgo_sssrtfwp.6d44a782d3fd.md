Detect bullish gaps when the current low is above the high two bars earlier, and bearish gaps when the current high is below the low two bars earlier; require a meaningful gap relative to volatility and, when enabled, a large-bodied, directionally aligned middle candle. Score each gap by combining gap size, short- and higher-timeframe trend alignment, displacement volume, location in the recent swing range, market-structure direction, active session, and volatility regime. A gap’s midpoint tap can trigger a setup if its score meets the quality threshold. Other triggers are a retest of a fresh opposing-candle order block formed before directional displacement, or a liquidity sweep beyond a recent swing extreme that reclaims back across that level. Evaluate triggers on the following bar and require directionally aligned displacement confirmation when enabled. For trend-following longs, require bullish fast-versus-slow trend, bullish structure, price clearly above the long-term average, and price in the lower half of the recent swing range when that filter is enabled. For trend-following shorts, require the inverse conditions and the upper half of the range. For reversal longs, require a bullish sweep while the fast-versus-slow trend is bearish; for reversal shorts, require a bearish sweep while that trend is bullish. All signal types also require sufficient relative volume, an allowed session, no time blackout, no volatility compression, and an elapsed same-direction cooldown. Signals may be restricted to trend mode, reversal mode, or both; an optional tier filter permits only the highest trend-setup tier, based on multiple triggers, expanded volatility, and directional displacement velocity.
