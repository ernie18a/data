For each scanned instrument and enabled timeframe, identify four candidate setups: (1) demand zone at the recent swing low, with zone width based on ATR; (2) supply zone at the recent swing high, likewise ATR-based; (3) bullish flag after a sufficiently large recent range expansion, with limited pullback, narrow consolidation, and a non-steep upward slope; or (4) bearish flag using the corresponding downward setup. A zone is active when price closes beyond its outer edge in the expected direction (or beyond its inner edge if close-out confirmation is disabled); a bullish flag is active while price remains above its consolidation low, and a bearish flag while below its consolidation high. Score qualifying candidates by setup freshness, distance from the zone, impulse size, pullback, consolidation range, and slope; select the highest-scoring candidate per timeframe, reducing the score of inactive candidates. Across timeframes, use the higher-scoring candidate, with a small confidence bonus when both timeframes identify the same setup. Rank active candidates ahead of inactive ones, then by confidence. Map bullish demand or flag signals to calls and bearish supply or flag signals to puts; in automatic mode choose that side, otherwise use the selected forced side. Suggest an at-the-money strike or an out-of-the-money strike in the signal direction, and set the target at the current price plus or minus an ATR-based distance in the signal direction. This is a setup and options suggestion screener; it does not issue position exits.
