Use the charted instrument’s bars to generate call and put signals separately. Treat indicator periods, thresholds, session windows, cooldowns, pattern switches, and risk multiples as configurable parameters.

For each direction, score one point each for: correctly ordered fast, medium, and slow EMAs; price on the corresponding side of VWAP with VWAP moving in that direction; matching higher-timeframe EMA ordering, or the higher-timeframe filter being disabled; RSI within the direction’s allowed range; MACD on the appropriate side of its signal with its histogram strengthening in that direction; and qualified volume. Volume qualifies when it exceeds its moving average by the configured multiplier and, if required, expands across successive bars. The optional trend agreement filter requires a configured minimum of EMA ordering, price relative to VWAP, and higher-timeframe agreement; disabling the higher-timeframe filter counts that component as satisfied.

A directional pattern is any enabled corresponding engulfing candle, long-wick reversal candle, strong-bodied break of the prior rolling high or low, strong-bodied bounce or rejection near a relevant level, or Wyckoff reversal. Relevant levels are the prior day’s extremes and close, current day’s running extreme, medium EMA, VWAP, prior week’s extreme, presession and opening-range extremes, and latest confirmed swing extreme. For a Wyckoff reversal, record a sweep of the prior rolling extreme while no same-direction sweep is pending; within the configured confirmation window, require a directional candle closing back across the recorded level and qualified volume. Clear the pending sweep on confirmation or expiry.

Outside the closing window, signal a call or put when the corresponding score meets an enabled top or lower tier, its pattern and optional trend filter agree, its directional cooldown has elapsed, and the time is outside enabled blocked windows. During the closing window, use the closing-tier minimum score, matching pattern and trend filter, qualified volume, a shared closing-signal cooldown, and sufficient clearance from the previous day’s high for calls or low for puts. Closing-window signals do not use the ordinary time filter. Update cooldowns whenever signals fire, even if that option side is already tracked.

On a signal, enter the corresponding option side only if that side is not already active; calls and puts are tracked independently. Record the underlying close as entry. Set the underlying-price stop using either an ATR distance or a buffered extreme of the current and previous bars and medium EMA. Set two underlying-price targets at configured multiples of the initial risk. On the first target touch, mark it reached and move the stop to entry. Exit the option when the second target or current stop is touched, an opposite pattern occurs with opposite EMA ordering and price on the opposite side of VWAP, or the optional holding-time limit expires. Process the first-target adjustment before checking exits, including on the entry bar. Reversal warnings alone do not trigger exits.
