During the configured trading period, calculate a rolling z-score of the product of OVX and one selected external series (oil, bond volatility, or equity volatility). Also calculate the traded instrument’s close position within its Bollinger bands, with 0.5 as the midpoint. Define a short signal when the z-score is positive and the band position is below the midpoint; when the EMA filter is enabled, require the close to be below its moving average too. On that signal, enter short only if currently long, and otherwise enter long if flat or short. Define a long signal when the z-score is negative and the band position is above the midpoint: close a long on this signal, and close a short when the short signal conditions hold without the optional EMA requirement. With the EMA filter disabled, omit the moving-average requirement.
