For each selected external series (volatility index, dollar index, and/or stablecoin dominance), evaluate the previous completed bar on each configured regime timeframe. Calculate the fractional distance of close from its configured simple or exponential moving average and the moving average's fractional change over its configured lookback. Classify the series as risk-on when both distance and slope are below their respective negative neutral bands, risk-off when both exceed their positive bands, and neutral otherwise.

Combine the main and confirmation timeframe states for each series according to the selected mode: use the main state alone; use the confirmation state alone; use the main state unless the confirmation state is opposite, in which case mark warning; or require both states to agree, marking opposing states as warning and all other combinations neutral. Count risk-on, risk-off, and warning states across selected series. Permit a long regime when risk-on votes reach the ceiling of half the selected series count and exceed risk-off votes; permit a short regime symmetrically. A warning exists if any series is warning or the directional vote counts are equal. Block both regime permissions during a warning unless warning trades are enabled.

Enter long when long trading is enabled, the instrument's close is above both configured exponential moving averages, and the long regime is permitted. Enter short under the symmetric conditions. Optionally require a confirmed chart bar. Depending on the entry setting, trigger only when the complete condition turns true or on every bar it remains true. Enter only while flat unless direct reversal is enabled, in which case an opposite position may be reversed. Optionally size the position by dividing a configured fraction of equity by the initial stop distance per unit.

For each position, set an initial stop at a configured percentage from the average entry price. Optionally activate a break-even stop, offset favorably from entry, after price moves a configured multiple of the initial risk in the favorable direction. Optionally trail the highest high since long entry or lowest low since short entry by a configured percentage, immediately or after a configured favorable price move. Use the most protective applicable stop. Optionally place a partial profit-taking limit at a configured multiple of initial risk for a configured fraction of the position. Optionally close the remaining position when the opposite regime is permitted, when price no longer satisfies its two-average filter, or after a configured number of bars. These discretionary closes obey the confirmed-bar setting.
