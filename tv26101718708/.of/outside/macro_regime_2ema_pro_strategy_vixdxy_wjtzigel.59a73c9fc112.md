On each selected external market indicator (volatility index, dollar index, and/or stablecoin dominance), use confirmed values from the selected higher or chart timeframes. Classify an indicator as bullish when its price is below its moving average and the average is falling beyond neutral bands; classify it as bearish when price is above its average and the average is rising beyond the bands; otherwise classify it as neutral. Depending on timeframe mode, use the main timeframe, confirmation timeframe, main direction with a warning for an opposing confirmation, or require both timeframes to agree. Aggregate selected indicators by majority: bullish votes permit longs, bearish votes permit shorts; opposing votes, any indicator warning, or a tie create a warning. Block entries during warnings unless warning trades are enabled.

Enter long when the traded instrument closes above both of its trend moving averages and the macro regime permits longs; enter short when it closes below both and the regime permits shorts. Signals may require the condition to turn true or recur while it remains true. Act on confirmed bars when enabled. By default, enter only while flat; optionally allow direct reversal. Position size may be fixed or based on a chosen fraction of equity at risk relative to the initial stop distance.

For longs, place an initial stop below entry; for shorts, above entry. Optionally activate a trailing stop after favorable movement and trail from the best price reached, with a fixed callback distance. Optionally move the stop to break-even plus a buffer after a favorable risk-multiple move, take partial profit at a risk-multiple target, close after a maximum holding period, close when the macro regime permits the opposite direction, or close when price no longer satisfies the two-average trend filter. Apply enabled exits to the open position.
