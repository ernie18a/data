Build a middle moving average and upper/lower bands using either volatility measured by standard deviation or ATR; optionally smooth the bands. From these, calculate normalized band width and the price’s position within the bands (%B). During the configured date window, evaluate enabled conditions: price crossing or being above/below any band; band width crossing or being above/below selected levels; and %B crossing above or below selected levels. Assign each enabled condition to open a long, close a long, or both. Require any, all, or a configured minimum number of the assigned conditions to trigger. If opening and closing trigger together, close takes priority. Open a long when the open requirement is met; close it when the close requirement is met, when its configured profit or loss limit is reached, or outside the date window. The strategy has no short-entry logic.
