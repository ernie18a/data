On each bar, maintain a setup using confirmed pivot lows. While waiting, replace support with each newly confirmed pivot low. A reversal occurs when the bar's low is within or below a configurable tolerance above support, the candle is bullish, and its close exceeds the previous high; freeze that bar's high. Then wait for a confirmed pivot low above support, followed by a close above the frozen high. Process these stages in order, allowing multiple stages on the same bar. Once continuation occurs, retain the setup until entry or a close below support; evaluate the buy signal before invalidating a broken setup.

Buy only when flat, calculated quantity is positive, continuation has occurred, volume is at least a configurable multiple of its moving average, and either: close is above the mid-term EMA, the fast EMA is above the mid-term EMA, and the mid-term EMA is rising over a configurable lookback; or close is above the long-term EMA, the fast EMA is above the mid-term EMA, the mid-term EMA is above the slow EMA, and the long-term EMA is rising over that lookback. If enabled, also require close above daily-reset VWAP. Independently, if enabled, require each selected market and sector reference instrument's close to exceed its own EMA on the chart timeframe. After entry, reset the setup.

Size the order as the smaller of equity risk budget divided by stop distance and the permitted equity allocation divided by close. For sizing, derive the stop from support less an ATR buffer when enabled, otherwise from close less an ATR multiple; constrain the distance between one minimum tick and the maximum ATR distance, subject to the code's final minimum-tick floor.

For an open long, place a stop at the greater of captured support less an ATR buffer and entry price less the maximum ATR distance, or use the latter when structure stops are disabled or support is unavailable; keep the stop at least one tick below entry. Optionally place a profit target at entry plus an ATR multiple. Also close on an enabled close below the mid-term EMA, an enabled close below captured support, or an enabled maximum holding period. The entry-bar flat-position cleanup clears staged support and ATR before the position is observed; on the first bar with a position, capture the then-current setup support if available and current ATR. Use that captured ATR for exits. No short or options trades are made.
