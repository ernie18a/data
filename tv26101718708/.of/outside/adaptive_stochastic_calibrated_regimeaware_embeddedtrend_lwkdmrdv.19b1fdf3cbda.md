Compute a smoothed stochastic oscillator from price or RSI and define overbought/oversold thresholds as rolling upper/lower percentiles of its own history. Classify the market as trending when directional strength and price efficiency exceed configurable thresholds; otherwise classify it as a range, except that unusually clustered volatility without a trend is a separate volatile regime. Track consecutive bars beyond either threshold as oscillator embedment. Optionally require sufficient cycle swing relative to noise, and for fades require the oscillator’s cycle phase to be near the corresponding trough or peak.

In a range, signal a long when the prior oscillator was at or below the lower threshold, then crosses above its signal line, provided it is not embedded on the downside. Signal a short when the prior oscillator was at or above the upper threshold, then crosses below its signal line, provided it is not embedded on the upside. In an uptrend, signal a long when the oscillator trend filter crosses bullishly, or when it is bullish and the oscillator crosses above its signal line below its midpoint. In a downtrend, signal a short on the bearish filter cross, or when the filter is bearish and the oscillator crosses below its signal line above its midpoint. Optionally block trend-following signals that oppose the higher-timeframe oscillator bias.

Score each candidate using configurable weighted contributions for stretch beyond the calibrated bands, fit to the regime, embedment and trend-filter alignment, multi-timeframe agreement, divergence, volume thrust, and optional cross-asset momentum. For fades, reward non-embedment, range conditions, aligned reversal divergence, and balanced timeframe readings. For trend signals, reward trend conditions, aligned filter/embedment, directional timeframe agreement, and hidden continuation divergence. Optionally adjust the score using the historical performance of its confidence tier relative to its matched directional base rate.

Block fades during an optional volatility-index spike. Block trend signals when too few configured timeframes agree, the higher-timeframe bias opposes the signal, or optional cross-asset momentum opposes it. Also block any candidate failing the optional noise gate. Emit LONG or SHORT only when a candidate remains after vetoes and its score meets the configured minimum; otherwise emit no position. Resolve simultaneous long and short candidates in favor of short. Signals may be restricted to confirmed bar closes. The indicator provides no position-exit rule.
