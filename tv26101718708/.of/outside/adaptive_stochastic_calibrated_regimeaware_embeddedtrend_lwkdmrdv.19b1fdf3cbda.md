Generate candidate signals from a smoothed, optionally transformed stochastic oscillator of the selected source, with extreme bands calibrated to rolling oscillator percentiles. In a range regime, go long when the oscillator crosses above its signal line after being oversold on the preceding bar and is not currently embedded below the lower band; reverse the conditions for a short. If enabled, require the detrended price cycle to be below zero and rising for a long, or above zero and falling for a short. In a trending regime, go long when price is above its trend average and the oscillator's adaptive moving average crosses above its volatility-adjusted trailing line, or is already above it while the oscillator crosses above its signal line below the midpoint; reverse for a short. A separate higher-timeframe stochastic bias must not oppose a trend signal when that gate is enabled. Optionally suppress candidates in low signal-to-noise conditions and require bar-close confirmation.

Score each candidate using a configurable weighted average of oscillator stretch or trail alignment, regime fit, embedment, multi-timeframe agreement, matching oscillator-price divergence, volume thrust, and signed cross-asset momentum. Optionally adjust regime fit using a variance-ratio test and scale the score by the previously resolved success rate of its original conviction tier relative to the matched directional base rate. For fades, veto a spike in the optional volatility-index feed. For trend signals, veto insufficient agreement across the configured timeframes or nonzero opposing momentum in the optional cross-asset feed. Emit the candidate's long or short verdict only when its score reaches the minimum and no veto applies; otherwise stand aside. No explicit position-exit rule is defined.
