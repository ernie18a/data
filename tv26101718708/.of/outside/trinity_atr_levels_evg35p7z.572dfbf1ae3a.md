Build fixed levels from the previous completed reference period’s close and ATR: a configurable trigger on each side, a gate-opening level farther in the same direction, and a gate target beyond it. On an eligible confirmed bar close crossing above the upper trigger, enter long; crossing below the lower trigger, enter short. Require the configured session and timeframe, and enforce the entry budget, cooldown, and any entry buffer. Exit a long on a confirmed close back below its trigger; exit a short on a confirmed close back above its trigger. After the gate has opened, signal a re-entry when price pulls back through the trigger and closes back on the trade side, or when it pulls back to the higher-timeframe short EMA while still inside the gate. Signal the gate opening when an active trade first closes through its gate level, and treat a close through the gate target as a scale-out and trailing-stop event. During the first session hour, a completed short-timeframe close reclaiming the prior-period close after opening on the opposite side is an advisory direction-flip signal, not a trigger entry. Apply the mirrored rules to long and short trades.
