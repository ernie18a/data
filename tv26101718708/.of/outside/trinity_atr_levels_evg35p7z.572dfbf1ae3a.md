Use the underlying instrument’s selected higher-period reference close and ATR to construct symmetric upper and lower entry thresholds, an inner gate boundary, and an outer gate target. The reference can come from the previous completed period or the forming period, using the configured market session.

On an eligible confirmed chart bar, buy a call when the close crosses above the upper entry threshold plus the configured ATR buffer; buy a put when it crosses below the lower threshold minus the buffer. Compare with the preceding eligible-session close when gap-safe crossing is enabled, otherwise with the preceding chart close. Require the corresponding direction to be inactive, its session entry allowance to remain, and its post-exit cooldown to have elapsed. A call entry deactivates the put setup and vice versa. Reset active setups, entry counts, gate state, and cooldowns at the configured session boundary.

Close the call setup when an eligible confirmed close falls below the unbuffered upper threshold; close the put setup when it rises above the unbuffered lower threshold. Count cooldown using eligible confirmed bars.

While a directional setup is active, an eligible close crossing its inner gate boundary is an additional entry signal. Once that boundary has been reached, an eligible bar that touches the unbuffered entry threshold and closes back on the favorable side is another entry signal. Also signal an entry when the bar touches the last completed lower-timeframe exponential moving average and closes back on the favorable side, provided the close has not passed the outer gate target and the chart timeframe is no coarser than that moving average’s timeframe. Mirror all comparisons for puts. Optionally suppress consecutive bars satisfying the same pullback condition.

The management guidance is to scale out at the outer gate target, trail the remainder, and close the rest at the full-range ATR level. The script’s mechanical active-state exit remains the unbuffered entry threshold.
