During the morning trading window, calculate the session-anchored VWAP and three progressively slower short-term EMAs. A long setup is armed when price reclaims VWAP from below, or satisfies the configured consecutive-close reclaim rule; disarm it after consecutive closes below VWAP. A short setup is armed symmetrically after a downward reclaim and disarmed after consecutive closes above VWAP. After arming, require a pullback candle that touches VWAP within a small tick buffer and closes back on the setup side, with the EMAs strictly ordered and all rising for a long or strictly ordered and all falling for a short. Optionally require volume to exceed the prior bar. Permit new setups only while the session VWAP has had fewer than the configured maximum price crosses that session. On a qualifying candle, place a stop entry just beyond its high for a long or low for a short; cancel the pending setup if its reclaim is invalidated, EMA alignment fails, the trading window ends, or the cross limit is reached. Set the protective stop beyond the setup candle’s opposite extreme by a tick buffer, and reject the setup if the stop distance exceeds the allowed maximum. While in a position, exit at the protective stop or at a fixed profit target measured from the average entry price.
