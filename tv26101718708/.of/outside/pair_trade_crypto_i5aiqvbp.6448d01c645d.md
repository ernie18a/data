Using closing prices for two selected instruments, log-transform both and calculate rolling means, standard deviations, and correlation. Estimate the hedge ratio as correlation times the ratio of their standard deviations, then calculate the current residual of the second instrument relative to the first using that ratio and the rolling means. Enter long when the residual falls below the negative entry threshold; enter short when it rises above the positive entry threshold. Close the long when the residual rises above the negative exit threshold, and close the short when it falls below the positive exit threshold.
