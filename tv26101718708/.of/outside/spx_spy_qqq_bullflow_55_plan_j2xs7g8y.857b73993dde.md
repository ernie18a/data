For QQQ, favor a long when QQQ is above its stronger bullish breakout level, VIX is below its specified threshold, and SPY is above its bullish reclaim level. For SPX, favor a downside trade when SPX breaks below its downside levels and VIX holds above its specified threshold. SPX longs are higher quality when VIX is below its threshold, especially if VIX is also falling; shorts are higher quality when VIX holds above that threshold and rises toward its higher levels. If SPX reclaims its bullish trigger but SPY has not reclaimed its corresponding level, do not chase index calls. Avoid trading SPX in its specified chop zone unless VIX provides strong confirmation.
