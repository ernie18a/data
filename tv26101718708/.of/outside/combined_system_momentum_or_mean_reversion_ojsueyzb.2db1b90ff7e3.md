On confirmed bars of the traded instrument, generate signals only during a configured New York morning window. Use a volatility index as external data. Calculate the same twin-range trailing filter for both instruments: average two separately parameterized, twice-EMA-smoothed absolute close-change ranges; move a range filter only when close exceeds its prior value by that range; then maintain trailing upper and lower bands and switch the active line between them when close crosses the active band. Treat the index as bullish when its current higher-timeframe close is above its filter, using lookahead. For the traded instrument's higher-timeframe states, compare the previous completed close with its filter, also using lookahead.

Define upside and downside overextension as the current close reaching the corresponding outer session VWAP deviation band or moving from the configured overnight anchor close by at least a configured fraction of the previous completed daily ATR. Calculate VWAP bands from the regular-session anchor.

Momentum long: the instrument's close crosses above its current-timeframe filter, the index is not bullish, the instrument is bullish on the intermediate timeframe and on at least one of two longer timeframes, and upside is not overextended. Momentum short: close crosses below its filter, the instrument is not bullish on the intermediate timeframe, and the index is bullish.

For mean reversion, classify each candle against its predecessor as outside if it breaks both extremes, inside if it breaks neither, upward if it breaks only the high, and downward otherwise. A bullish reversal is outside-downward-upward or outside-inside-upward; a bearish reversal reverses the upward and downward directions. For these three-candle patterns, require at least one pattern candle's open or close to reach the corresponding inner VWAP band, using that candle's own band. Also accept a two-candle downward-upward or upward-downward reversal if at least one pattern candle's open or close is strictly beyond the corresponding outer band and the current close is overextended in that direction. Signal mean-reversion long when a qualifying bullish reversal occurs and the index is not bullish. Signal mean-reversion short when a qualifying bearish reversal occurs and either the index is bullish or upside is overextended.

For each long signal, indicate a stop at the signal candle's low minus a configured multiple of current-timeframe ATR; for each short signal, use the high plus that distance. These are displayed stop levels, not executable exit rules.
