On the weekly REIT ETF, enter long if either: (1) the low falls below the lower Bollinger Band, then the close rebounds above it by an ATR-based margin and finishes above the bar’s midpoint; additionally, Treasury yields have fallen sufficiently from their recent high while their correlation with the ETF is below a ceiling, or yields are below a floor; and the ETF does not meet the stop-filter condition; or (2) the close breaks above the prior Donchian-channel high, SPY is above its moving average, and at least one holds: TNX’s recent high is sufficiently far in the past, TNX correlation with the ETF exceeds a ceiling, or yields are below a floor. The stop filter is met when the ETF is below its moving average, has fallen sufficiently from the prior close, and SPY has also fallen sufficiently or its correlation with the ETF is negative. Exit the position if any of these holds: (1) TNX has risen sufficiently from its recent low, or yields are above a high threshold while TNX is above its recent low, and the ETF close is sufficiently below its recent highest close; (2) the ETF is below its moving average, has fallen sufficiently from the prior close, and SPY has also fallen sufficiently or its correlation with the ETF is negative; or (3) the ETF has risen sufficiently from its recent low, its close is sufficiently below its recent highest high, and its stochastic reading exceeded an overbought threshold recently. If both entry and exit conditions apply on the same bar, process them in this order: oversold entry, breakout entry, trailing exit, overbought exit, stop-loss exit.
