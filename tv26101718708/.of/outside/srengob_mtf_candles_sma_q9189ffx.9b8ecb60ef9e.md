Track confirmed swing highs and lows. A close above a swing high establishes bullish structure and creates a bullish order-block zone from the relevant lowest candle; a close below a swing low establishes bearish structure and creates a bearish zone from the relevant highest candle. Remove a bullish zone when a candle body closes below its lower boundary, and a bearish zone when a candle body closes above its upper boundary. A bullish entry is signaled when the weighted evidence score meets its configured threshold and price has bullish structure, is inside a valid bullish zone, and is above its simple moving average. A bearish entry is signaled when the score meets the threshold and price has bearish structure, is inside a valid bearish zone, and is below the moving average. The score combines structure, proximity to an order block, order-block size relative to short-term volatility, rejection candles, volume confirmation from gold futures, sweeps of order-block boundaries, structure-aligned moving-average trend, active session, momentum, chop and reversal conditions, and momentum/volatility impulse evidence. There is no exit signal.
