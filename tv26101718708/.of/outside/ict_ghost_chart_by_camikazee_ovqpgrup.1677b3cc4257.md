Use the traded instrument and a configurable correlated instrument on the same timeframe. A bullish divergence occurs when the traded instrument makes a new lookback low while the correlated instrument does not; bearish divergence is the corresponding new high. Evaluate signals on each bar, with all periods, thresholds, time windows and optional filters configurable.

For a long, require a recent sell-side sweep: the low crosses below the previous daily low or the lowest low of the preceding lookback and the close returns above that level. After the sweep, require a bullish structure shift: the candle opens below and closes above the latest confirmed swing high, has sufficient body size and displacement relative to ATR, meets the configured displacement-continuation and EMA-distance requirements, and optionally has a rising fast EMA and bullish EMA alignment. Do not overwrite the stored sweep while a Fibonacci range is locked. Apply the mirror conditions for a short after a buy-side sweep.

On the structure-shift bar, require a same-direction three-candle fair-value gap, with the current low above the high two bars earlier for a long, or the current high below the low two bars earlier for a short. Require the current candle body to exceed the high-quality ATR threshold, price to be below the midpoint of the higher-timeframe rolling high-low range for a long or above it for a short, and the bar to fall within a configured trading window. Also require the current high's distance from the preceding wider-lookback low, or the preceding wider-lookback high's distance from the current low for a short, to reach the minimum ATR impulse size.

When enabled, require a minimum trend score: award one point each for price on the appropriate side of the fast EMA, fast EMA on the appropriate side of the middle EMA, middle EMA on the appropriate side of the slow EMA, and price in the appropriate discount or premium zone. Calculate a setup score from current-bar conditions: two points each for the directional sweep, structure shift, displacement, correlated divergence and appropriate discount or premium zone, plus one each for the trading window and volume spike. Aggressive mode imposes no setup-score threshold; balanced mode requires its configured threshold and, if enabled, displacement; conservative mode requires its higher threshold and, if enabled, displacement and a volume spike.

When probability filtering is enabled, require its configured minimum score. On the current bar award two points for the directional sweep, three for the structure shift, two each for a directional change of character and continuation break, one for the appropriate discount or premium zone, two for directional displacement, one each for a volume spike and trading window, two for correlated divergence, and one for directional EMA alignment. A change of character is a first close crossing a confirmed swing against the prior market state with directional displacement; a continuation break is the same crossing in the existing state. Update that state after these breaks.

A qualifying signal proposes entry at the fair-value gap midpoint. Set the stop at the stored sweep extreme, falling back to the corresponding extreme two bars earlier. Set the target to the previous daily high for a long or low for a short if it lies beyond entry; otherwise use the active first Fibonacci expansion target, or a configured risk-reward target if unavailable. Fibonacci ranges are formed from a sufficiently large displaced structure shift or break, anchored to the configured prior dealing-leg extreme or sweep/swing fallback, and remain locked until invalidation, completion, or an allowed reset. Their first expansion target is available only while the displayed range is valid and that target has not been hit.
