Generate a long setup when price sweeps below the prior day’s low or a recent swing low and closes back above it, then confirms a bullish market-structure break with a strong upward displacement candle. Require a qualifying bullish fair-value gap, price in the discount half of the recent higher-timeframe range, and an ICT trading window. Apply the selected setup mode’s score, displacement, volume, trend-strength, and probability-score filters; these scores reward liquidity sweeps, structure confirmation, displacement, correlated-market divergence, favorable range location, active trading windows, volume, and aligned trend. For a short setup, mirror the conditions: sweep above the prior day’s high or a recent swing high and close back below, confirm a strong bearish structure break, require a bearish fair-value gap, premium location, an ICT window, and the corresponding filters. Set entry at the fair-value gap midpoint; use the sweep extreme as the stop when available, otherwise the gap’s far edge. Target the prior day’s opposing high or low when it lies beyond entry; otherwise use the active directional range extension, falling back to the configured risk-to-reward target. The indicator defines no exit signal.
