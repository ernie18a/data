Daily-bar regime rotation between two instruments, always fully allocated to exactly one and never flat or short. The two instruments are spot BTC/USDT and the BTC-dominance perpetual contract (BTCDOM/USDT perpetual). The only signal is BTC's own daily trend, computed from BTC's close and an exponential moving average of BTC's close over a long lookback (about 120 days). Use the previous completed bar's values only, so there is no look-ahead. Rule for the position held during the current bar: if the previous bar's BTC close is strictly above the previous bar's BTC EMA, hold long BTC; otherwise, including when the EMA or previous close is not yet available, hold long the BTC-dominance perpetual. Entering the dominance perpetual is therefore decided by BTC data, not by the dominance series. A switch means exiting one asset and entering the other when this condition flips between consecutive bars. Each bar's return is the held asset's close-to-close return, with no costs, slippage or funding. The backtest only begins after a configured start date, which is when the dominance contract started trading. Both series must be aligned on the same daily timeframe, and the dominance series must use only completed-bar data.
