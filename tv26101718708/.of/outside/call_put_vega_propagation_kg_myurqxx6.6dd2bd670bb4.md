On each bar, calculate separate call and put Black–Scholes vegas from the instrument's close, a manual strike or the close rounded to the nearest configured strike increment, the risk-free rate, and time remaining until expiry, floored at a small positive value. Use the same-timeframe India VIX close plus separate call and put skew offsets for implied volatility when enabled and positive; floor each resulting volatility at a small positive value. Otherwise use the respective manual volatilities. For each side, calculate vega as spot multiplied by the standard normal density of d1 and the square root of time, where d1 is the Black–Scholes d1 for that side's volatility. Subtract its value a configured number of bars ago and smooth the change with an EMA. Use separate lookbacks for calls and puts; the call EMA length is its lookback divided by three and the put EMA length is its lookback divided by four, each floored at one. Generate a buy signal when the call series crosses above the put series and the call series is positive. Generate a sell signal when the put series crosses above the call series and the put series is positive. A cross requires the previously crossing series to have been at or below the other series on the preceding bar. Otherwise issue no new signal; the displayed signal retains the most recent buy or sell and starts at wait. No exit rule is specified.
