On each bar, calculate separate call and put Black–Scholes vegas from the instrument’s closing price, the configured strike (manual or nearest configured strike increment), time remaining to expiry, risk-free rate, and each side’s implied volatility. Use live volatility-index data with separately configured call and put offsets when enabled and valid; otherwise use the separate manual IV inputs. For each side, take the change in raw Vega over its configured lookback and smooth that change with an EMA using its configured smoothing length. Emit BUY when call momentum crosses above put momentum and call momentum is positive. Emit SELL when put momentum crosses above call momentum and put momentum is positive. Otherwise emit WAIT; retain the last BUY or SELL as the displayed signal state until the next such signal.
