For each confirmed pair of consecutive price pivot lows, signal long when their separation is within the configured range, the second low is at or below the first, the oscillator at the second pivot is higher than at the first, the first low is below its lower Keltner band, the oscillator remains strictly below its signal line throughout both pivots and the bars between them, and the external TICK low at the second pivot is higher than at the first. Signal short symmetrically for consecutive pivot highs: the second high is at or above the first, the oscillator is lower, the first high is above its upper Keltner band, the oscillator remains strictly above its signal line throughout the interval, and the external TICK high at the second pivot is lower than at the first. Evaluate these signals only when triple-divergence detection is enabled, using TICK data on the traded instrument's timeframe without lookahead. The oscillator is the difference between short and long simple moving averages of the traded instrument's close; its signal line is a simple moving average of that difference. The Keltner bands are an exponential moving average of close plus or minus a multiple of average true range. Emit each signal when the second pivot is confirmed. No exit rule is specified.
