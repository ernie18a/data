On each bar, compare the traded instrument with a configurable reference instrument. Encode each bar-to-bar change as up when nonnegative and down otherwise. Over a rolling window, estimate transfer entropy in each direction from the source's previous state to the target's current state, conditioned on a configurable number of target history states. Use configurable Shannon or escort-weighted Rényi transfer entropy, with optional finite-sample correction for Shannon entropy. Subtract the traded-to-reference estimate from the reference-to-traded estimate and optionally smooth the result with an EMA.

A follow signal is eligible when the smoothed net flow reaches the positive lead threshold. If the surrogate gate is enabled, also require the absolute z-score of the unsmoothed net flow against a null distribution to reach its significance threshold. Build that distribution by repeatedly shifting the source history by different multiples of a configurable lag and recalculating the net flow in both directions.

Apply the selected regime gate: either require a valid external regime classification indicating a trending state, or, if the internal trend filter is enabled, require a Hurst estimate at or above its random-walk midpoint. Estimate Hurst as the slope of log mean absolute changes in the traded source's log price against log lag over several fixed, increasing lags.

After a configurable cooldown since the last follow signal, go long when eligible and the reference price has risen over its configurable momentum lookback; go short when eligible and it has fallen. A flat or unavailable reference move produces no signal. Reset the shared cooldown after either signal. The calibration statistics do not gate these signals. No exit rule is specified.
