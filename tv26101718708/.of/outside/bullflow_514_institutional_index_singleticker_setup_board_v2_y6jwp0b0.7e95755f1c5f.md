For a mapped instrument during regular trading hours, use its predefined directional trigger levels. On a confirmed bar, signal long calls when the close crosses above the bullish trigger, or long puts when it crosses below the bearish trigger; for two-way instruments, allow either direction and wait between the triggers. Require the relevant side’s enabled filters to pass: price aligned with VWAP, short EMA above/below the longer EMA with price on the same side, and, if enabled, sufficient relative volume. Invalidate a directional setup when the close crosses its invalidation level against the position; flag target-level crossings for staged profit-taking. Apply a cooldown between signals. The indicator’s ticker-to-direction and level map is predefined; it does not calculate decisions from live external flow or gamma data.
