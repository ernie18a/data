For each mapped instrument, use its predetermined flow- and gamma-based setup: call-only, put-only, or two-way, with assigned trigger, confirmation, invalidation, and target levels. Unmapped instruments produce no signals. On a confirmed bar during the regular session, signal a call purchase when the close crosses above either the call trigger or confirmation level, if calls are enabled for that setup. Signal a put purchase when the close crosses below either the put trigger or confirmation level, if puts are enabled. If enabled, require the close to be on the appropriate side of VWAP, and require the close and short EMA to be aligned above or below the longer EMA in the signal direction; an optional relative-volume filter must also pass. Enforce a bar-based cooldown after any entry, invalidation, or target signal. For call-only setups, signal invalidation when the close crosses below either assigned invalidation level; for put-only setups, signal invalidation when it crosses above either level. Two-way setups have no automatic invalidation signal. Signal target hits when the high crosses above a target for call-only setups, the low crosses below it for put-only setups, or either crossing occurs for two-way setups. The opening range and narrative market-confirmation rules are displayed but do not filter the programmed signals.
