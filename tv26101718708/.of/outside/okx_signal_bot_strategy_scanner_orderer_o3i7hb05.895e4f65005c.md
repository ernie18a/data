For each configured instrument, evaluate up to six independently enabled conditions. Each condition compares two selected technical measures—ATR, a moving average, CCI, RSI, Bollinger Band width, ADX, MFI, or momentum—or compares one measure with a user-specified constant. Supported tests are greater than, less than, greater than or equal, less than or equal, equal, not equal, crossing above, and crossing below. Assign each condition to long or short. Combine enabled long conditions using the configured all-must-pass or any-may-pass rule, and do the same independently for short conditions. When the long rule passes, exit any short position and enter long; when the short rule passes, exit any long position and enter short. If neither rule passes, emit no action.
