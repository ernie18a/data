For gold, compare the current closes of the TIP ETF and the US Dollar Index with their closes a configurable number of chart bars earlier. Favor long when TIP has risen and the dollar index has fallen; favor short when TIP has fallen and the dollar index has risen. Otherwise, including unchanged or unavailable data, the signal is mixed and no trade is favored. On intraday charts, update this state only during the US cash session; retain the previous state outside it. These are directional filters, not entry or exit orders. Optionally, alert on a state change only when the non-mixed state agrees with the same calculation on a configurable higher timeframe.
