On each bar, evaluate the underlying instrument and use a qualifying bullish entry to suggest buying a call and a qualifying bearish entry to suggest buying a put. Choose the nearest at-the-money strike or one strike out of the money, according to the selected mode; use the selected expiry.

For either entry, require the configured session filter, a cooldown since the last entry, sufficient fast-to-slow EMA separation relative to ATR, and no excessive distance between close and its moving average. Optionally reject a range defined by both narrow EMA separation and close proximity to VWAP. A bullish trend requires the fast EMA above the slow EMA and close above the trend EMA; a bearish trend reverses both comparisons. Optionally require close on the corresponding side of VWAP, MACD on the corresponding side of its signal, and a sufficiently strong smoothed directional-movement measure.

A bullish setup additionally requires a close crossing above the fast EMA or VWAP; a bullish continuation bar above the fast EMA with close above the previous close; a bullish VWAP bounce; a fast EMA pullback and recovery; or a support bounce with bullish price structure. A bearish setup requires the mirrored crossover, continuation, VWAP bounce, or EMA pullback. Support is the rolling lowest low including the current bar; bullish structure means a new high or a higher low relative to the preceding rolling window.

Score each raw setup by adding tiered points for EMA separation relative to ATR, relative volume, directional MACD histogram momentum, ATR as a percentage of price, and session quality. Add points when the current close and higher-timeframe EMAs align with the direction and when close is near a long-period close average. Require the configured minimum score and, optionally, volume above its average. Higher-timeframe alignment contributes to the score regardless of the display setting.

Allow a bullish entry only when flat or bearish, and a bearish entry only when flat or bullish. An opposite entry replaces the active direction, records the current close and ATR-based stop and targets, and restarts the cooldown. Process entries before exits on the same bar.

For an active bullish trade, signal a one-time partial take profit when the high reaches the first target; exit when a bearish candle closes below the fast EMA, close falls below both VWAP and the slow EMA, the low reaches the fixed entry stop, or the trade exceeds the configured bar limit. Mirror these conditions for a bearish trade. The second target is informational and does not trigger an exit. An exit resets the direction to flat.
