For a long signal, require fast EMA above slow EMA, price above the trend EMA, and—when enabled—price above VWAP and bullish MACD confirmation. For a short signal, require the inverse. Both directions also require minimum EMA separation relative to ATR, must pass enabled ADX, range, session, and other market-condition filters, and must not be excessively extended from the recent price average. A directional setup is triggered by a reclaim or pullback of the fast EMA or VWAP, a confirming continuation candle, or, for longs, a support bounce or resistance breakout. Score eligible setups using trend strength, volume, MACD momentum, volatility, time of day, higher-timeframe directional alignment, and proximity to the volume-price center; signal only when the score meets the configured minimum and any enabled volume requirement passes. Suppress repeated entries during a cooldown, and enter only when not already in that direction. On a long signal, recommend buying a call; on a short signal, recommend buying a put, with an at-the-money or one-step out-of-the-money strike. Exit a long if price breaches its ATR-based stop, closes below VWAP and the slow EMA, closes below the fast EMA on a down candle, or exceeds the time limit in bars. Exit a short on the inverse conditions. Issue a one-time take-profit alert when price reaches the first ATR-based target.
