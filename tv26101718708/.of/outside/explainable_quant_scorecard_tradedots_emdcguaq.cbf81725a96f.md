Build a composite evidence score from trend slope, momentum, volatility regime, volume participation aligned with trend direction, risk-adjusted performance versus a benchmark, distance from the trend average, and alignment with higher-timeframe trends. Adjust the relative influence of trend and momentum upward in trending conditions and exhaustion risk upward in ranging conditions. Signal a long setup when the score reaches its high-score cutoff and the fast trend average is above the slow one; signal a short setup when the same cutoff is reached and the fast average is below the slow one. The indicator provides setup alerts, not entry or exit rules.
