On daily data, evaluate each portfolio holding alongside all other holdings. For several short, medium, and long lookback horizons, calculate raw price returns and log returns divided by the standard deviation of daily log returns scaled by the square root of the horizon. Convert each measure into a cross-sectional percentile using the number of valid portfolio values strictly below it; use the midpoint when only one value is valid. Combine these percentiles with a trend score and ATR-based health score using configured weights. The trend score is the weighted sum of whether price exceeds each of three EMAs, whether each faster EMA exceeds the next slower EMA, and whether the two slower EMAs are rising over a configured interval. The health score combines the clipped, linearly scaled ATR-normalized distances below the medium and slow EMAs; distances at or above each EMA receive full health. Calculate the same scores at two configured past offsets using the portfolio's data at those offsets. Rank scores descending at each time, assigning tied scores the same rank. If required current or historical data is missing, make no decision for that holding.

Apply these rules in order: Exit when the holding is in the configured bottom fraction of portfolio ranks, its price is sufficiently far below the slow EMA in ATR units, and its trend score is below the configured threshold. Otherwise, trim when it is in another configured bottom fraction and either its price is sufficiently far below the medium EMA in ATR units, or its score or rank has deteriorated rapidly over the shorter historical interval while price is below that EMA. Otherwise, if its score is below a configured threshold but has improved sufficiently over that interval, classify it as recovering. Otherwise, put it on trim watch when it is in a configured bottom fraction and either price is below the medium EMA or rapid deterioration has occurred. Otherwise, classify it as deteriorating when rapid deterioration coincides with a score or trend score below its respective threshold, or with price below the medium EMA. Otherwise, classify a holding in the configured top fraction with a sufficiently strong trend as a leader; classify all others as hold. Rapid deterioration means a sufficiently large decline in either score or rank.

For reduction review, order exit holdings first, then trim, then trim watch, then deteriorating and hold together, then recovering, then leaders. Within each tier, prioritize a clipped weighted combination of current score weakness and score declines over the two historical intervals, with each decline scaled and clipped to configured limits. Use medium-EMA ATR damage and then rank deterioration as additional components of the sort key.
