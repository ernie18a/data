Compute a one-sided kernel regression of closing price, optionally widening its bandwidth with normalized volatility. Form upper and lower thresholds around the regression line using smoothed residual volatility, with a volatility-based minimum width. Set a persistent bullish state after the required consecutive closes above the upper threshold, and a bearish state after the required consecutive closes below the lower threshold; retain the state between opposite confirmations. A state flip arms a trade in that direction. Enter only while the state still agrees and within the allowed waiting period, when session rules permit, the market is outside a detected low-activity chop zone, recent bars are not exceptionally large, the entry is sufficiently close to the breached band, and the kernel slope and candle direction agree with the trade when those filters are enabled. Enforce a minimum interval between entries. Also allow a flat position to arm a same-state re-entry when price crosses the kernel line in the state’s direction of momentum. Cancel an armed setup if the state reverses or its waiting period expires. Exit a long on a confirmed bearish state flip; otherwise exit on a bearish reversal candle at the upper threshold while profitable, a configured drawdown from a sufficiently large peak profit, or a profitable close below the recent swing low. Apply the mirrored rules to shorts: bullish flip, bullish reversal at the lower threshold while profitable, profit giveback, or profitable close above the recent swing high. Confirm trade actions at bar close.
