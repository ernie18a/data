Treat a bullish setup as present when at least half of six directional checks are bullish: relative strength versus the benchmark is positive, price is above the Supertrend line, MACD is above its signal, RSI is above its midpoint, price is above the fast EMA with the fast EMA above the slow EMA, and +DI exceeds -DI. Flag stronger long setups when most checks are bullish and either volume exceeds its rolling average by the configured expansion threshold or both ATR and Bollinger width are contracting; also flag unanimous bullish alignment. Flag unanimous bearish alignment when all six checks are bearish. The indicator defines no short-entry or exit rule.
