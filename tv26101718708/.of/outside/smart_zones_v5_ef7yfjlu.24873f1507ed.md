On each bar, calculate separate bullish and bearish confluence scores. Add one unit for each applicable condition: matching persistent higher-timeframe sweep bias; enabled trading session; volume spike; matching sweep in the enabled comparison instrument; traded-instrument return outperforming the comparison instrument for bullish or underperforming it for bearish; a matching hourly price gap not overridden by a sufficiently larger, nearby opposing daily gap; a matching daily gap; proximity to the previous daily low for bullish or high for bearish; falling volatility index for bullish or rising volatility index for bearish when that filter is enabled; repeated same-direction liquidity sweeps; and proximity to matching structure. Add twice that unit for an engulfing candle at matching structure and twice that unit for an engulfing entry inside a recent matching chart-timeframe gap. Cap each score at the configured maximum. Relative performance contributes even when comparison-instrument sweep confirmation is disabled.

A low sweep occurs when the preceding bar's low reaches or breaches the preceding rolling low and the current close is above that low; a high sweep is the mirror image. Apply the same definition to the comparison instrument. Higher-timeframe bias persists after a higher-timeframe sweep until an opposite sweep, with a low sweep taking precedence if both occur. Reset each directional sweep count when price reaches the corresponding recent opposite extreme; increment it on its matching sweep and reset the other count, processing the high sweep last.

A bullish engulfing candle has a sufficiently large positive body relative to ATR, closes sufficiently near its high, crosses the preceding candle's body, and has a volume spike; reverse these tests for bearish. Matching structure comprises the previous daily low or high, daily or weekly open, or the retained midpoint of the latest matching hourly or daily gap, each within its specified ATR distance. A bullish gap exists when a bar's low exceeds the high two bars earlier by the required minimum; reverse for bearish. For hourly and daily gaps use the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar before it, retain rounded midpoints, and compare rounded gap sizes when applying the daily override. For a chart-timeframe gap entry, require the close inside the most recent matching gap within its allowed age and a matching engulfing candle; consume that gap after the entry.

Issue a long setup on a low sweep when the bullish score reaches the configured minimum, and a short setup on a high sweep when the bearish score reaches it. Separately, on a sweep, issue a directional verdict only when that direction strictly dominates and its score reaches the higher verdict threshold, or reaches the lower verdict threshold with a sufficient lead. If a new bullish gap coincides with a high sweep or a new bearish gap with a low sweep, the verdict is to wait. Otherwise classify the verdict by score as strong, valid or marginal; a strong score is actionable, and a valid score with a volume spike calls for scaling in. A high sweep with a sufficiently strong dominant bullish score produces a fade-long signal; a low sweep with a sufficiently strong dominant bearish score produces a fade-short signal. Independently, the maximum bullish or bearish score threshold produces a corresponding confluence alert. These signals can coexist with the separate engulfing, gap-entry, structure-flip, breakout and confirmed-break alerts.

After a low sweep, save the prior rolling high as the bullish confirmation level and the prior rolling low less an ATR-based buffer as the long invalidation level; a close above the saved confirmation level confirms long. Mirror this after a high sweep for short. A close beyond the saved invalidation level cancels the active setup and its pending confirmation. On a qualifying setup, show that stop and a profit target at the corresponding recent opposite extreme. Session membership contributes to the score but does not gate signals.
