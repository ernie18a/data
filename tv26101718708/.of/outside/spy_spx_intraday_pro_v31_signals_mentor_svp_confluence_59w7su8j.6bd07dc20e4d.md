Use underlying price and volume to buy calls or puts. Evaluate on each execution bar, optionally requiring bar-close confirmation; treat periods, thresholds, weights, and switches as configurable parameters. Mirror all directional comparisons for puts.

Classify the higher-timeframe bias by close versus VWAP and the intermediate structure by close versus VWAP plus fast/slow EMA alignment. Block entries when execution-timeframe ADX is below the avoidance threshold and intermediate structure is mixed. For ordinary call entries, require bullish higher-timeframe bias, no bearish intermediate structure, the configured volume, chop, ADX and RSI gates, and a bullish candle whose body meets the entry threshold and whose close exceeds the previous close. Require close above VWAP and the fast EMA above the slow EMA, with close above the fast EMA; the opening-range trigger may optionally relax the EMA trigger. Permit configurable relaxation of the higher-timeframe and EMA alignment requirements.

An ordinary entry also needs an enabled setup: a recent upward VWAP cross with close above VWAP and the fast EMA; an upward close crossover of the locked regular-session opening-range high; a recent touch of VWAP or either EMA followed by a bullish resumption above VWAP and the fast EMA without excessive ATR-scaled extension; or an optional momentum continuation with the bullish EMA stack. Opening-range entries can optionally require a trend regime. Mirror these rules for puts.

Alternatively, buy calls on a counter-VWAP bounce when price is below VWAP but within an ATR-scaled distance, ADX is below its cap, RSI is oversold and rising, the fast EMA is rising, the low is higher than a prior low, the candle is bullish with sufficient body, volume meets the strong-volume threshold, and intermediate structure is not bearish. Mirror for puts above VWAP. These setups bypass the confluence gate but still face the final entry-volume gate.

For other setups, optionally require a minimum weighted confluence score. Add points for aligned higher-timeframe bias and intermediate structure, strong volume, sufficient ADX, directional RSI range, close on the favorable side of the slow EMA, proximity to a favorable session-volume-profile value-area edge, a reclaim or opening-range setup, favorable session timing, and unusually high directional volume. Give a smaller setup bonus for pullbacks; subtract points for a nearby volume-profile obstacle ahead or unfavorable session timing. Build the profile from regular-session volume binned by typical price, with the point of control at the largest bin and value-area edges expanded from it. If raw call and put setups occur together, select the side with the larger directional movement indicator; equal indicators produce neither entry. This comparison uses raw setups even if one later fails its score gate. A new entry replaces the tracked trade.

For the chart trade, record the entry close and signal-bar extremes. Set the initial call stop at the signal low or, if the ATR stop option is enabled, the lower of that low and entry minus an ATR distance; mirror for puts. After a configurable delay, ratchet the stop toward the slow EMA. On a confirmed bar, sell the option if close crosses the existing stop or the rolling swing extreme, or after a minimum holding period if at least two of these fail: the signal-bar extreme, the fast EMA, and VWAP. For counter-VWAP entries, exclude VWAP from that count and extend the minimum holding period. The trade-state calculation also checks the newly ratcheted stop on the same bar. Signal a partial trim when the favorable bar extreme reaches an ATR distance from entry.

The separate mentor advice uses dynamically recalculated targets: preferably directional Fibonacci extensions of a higher-timeframe swing if the first qualifying target is within an ATR cap; otherwise use the nearest eligible opening-range, previous-session, current-session, swing, VWAP, EMA, volume-profile and round-number levels, with ATR fallbacks. Its initial stop uses the previous completed regular-session higher-timeframe wick when valid, otherwise an ADX-adjusted ATR distance; favorable later higher-timeframe wicks can ratchet it. Keep the stop beyond entry by a minimum cushion until the first target is touched, then apply the direction-specific breakeven and EMA/ATR trailing rules. After a holding buffer, advise selling all on a trailing-stop hit, an ATR-sized adverse move with opposing structure and heavy opposing volume, a failed opening-range break before the first target, an aligned trend reversal or heavy opposing volume plus adverse swing after the first target, extreme RSI, the third target, or the second target with exhaustion or late-session conditions. Advise partial profit at target milestones and additional profit-taking at the first target when RSI is stretched or during lunch. Mentor advice is separate from the chart trade's exit state.
