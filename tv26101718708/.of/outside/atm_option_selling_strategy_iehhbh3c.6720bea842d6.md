Sell an at-the-money call when the underlying closes below both longer-term moving averages, its faster exponential moving average crosses below its slower one, and momentum is below its midpoint. Sell an at-the-money put when the underlying closes above both longer-term moving averages, the faster exponential moving average crosses above the slower one, and momentum is above its midpoint. Exit each position when its stop-loss or take-profit level is reached, and close all positions before the market closes.
