Track each configured instrument's highest observed high, including the current bar, and its number of open long entries. During the configured date range, first exit all entries in any instrument whose close is strictly above a configurable fraction of its highest high. Then process instruments in their configured order: if the shared limit on open entries has not been reached, add one long entry when the instrument's close is strictly below the configurable highest-high fraction assigned to its next entry. Allow at most one entry per instrument per bar and use successive thresholds for successive entries. Each entry counts separately toward the shared limit, so signals in other instruments can prevent an entry. Reset an instrument's entry count after an exit. Take no action outside the date range.
