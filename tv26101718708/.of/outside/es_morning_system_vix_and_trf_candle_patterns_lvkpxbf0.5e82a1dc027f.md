On confirmed bars of the traded instrument, track candle-color runs during the configured session; a doji inherits the preceding candle’s color. Reset the swing and setup state at each new day. At each red-to-green transition, start a candidate swing low as the minimum of the preceding red run and the current low; track its minimum through the green run, along with the minimum low of that green run. At the next green-to-red transition, confirm that swing low and a swing high equal to the maximum high of the green run and the current red candle. If both confirmed swing low and swing high are lower than their respective previous confirmed values, wait for a break below the green-run minimum; retain the confirmed swing high as the invalidation level. A newer qualifying pattern replaces the waiting levels.

On each confirmed session bar, including the bar that establishes the pattern, if its low reaches or falls below the break level, signal short only when signals are allowed by the configured time window, its close is below the overnight-anchored volume-weighted average of typical price, and the external volatility index is bullish on its configured higher timeframe. If any gate fails, keep waiting. Only when the break level was not reached, cancel the setup if the bar’s high exceeds the invalidation level. After a short signal, clear the setup; future patterns may signal again.

Determine whether the external index is bullish using its close above a twin-range trend filter, calculated in its higher-timeframe context without lookahead. For each of two configurable period and multiplier pairs, smooth the absolute close-to-close change with an EMA of that period, then another EMA whose period is twice the first period minus one, and multiply by its multiplier. Average the two results to obtain the range. Update a recursive filtered price by keeping its previous value while the close remains within one range of it, otherwise moving it to one range below or above the close. Form upper and lower bands at the filtered price plus and minus the range; trail the upper band downward unless the previous close exceeded its previous value, and trail the lower band upward unless the previous close fell below its previous value. The trend line follows the previous active band until the close crosses its current value, then switches to the other band. Use the most recently available higher-timeframe result.
