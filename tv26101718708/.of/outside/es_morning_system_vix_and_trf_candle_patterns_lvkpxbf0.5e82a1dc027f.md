On the one-minute chart during the morning session, identify swing highs and lows from candle-color reversals. For a long setup, require a higher swing high followed by a higher swing low; wait for price to break above the pullback’s highest high. Enter long only if a session or overnight VWAP touch has armed the setup, the close is above both available VWAPs, and the instrument’s 15-minute range-filter trend is bullish. After entry, wait for another VWAP touch before arming again. Cancel the setup if price falls below its higher low. For a short setup, require a lower swing low followed by a lower swing high; wait for price to break below the bounce’s lowest low. Enter short only if the close is below overnight VWAP and the VIX’s 15-minute range-filter trend is bullish. Cancel the setup if price rises above its lower high. Restrict both entries to the morning trading window after the opening delay. The indicator defines no exit rules.
