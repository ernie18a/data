During the configured intraday trading window, calculate the current regular-session volume-weighted average price (VWAP). Allow new setups only while the session’s price-to-VWAP crossing count remains below its configured limit. A long setup is armed when price reclaims VWAP from below, using the selected reclaim rule (either consecutive closes above VWAP or a close above after the prior close was at or below it); reset the armed state after consecutive closes below VWAP or at the start of a new session. A short setup is the mirror image, resetting after consecutive closes above VWAP. For a long hold candle, require the long state to be armed, fast/middle/slow EMAs strictly ordered upward and all rising, the candle to reach the VWAP touch zone and close above VWAP, and rising volume if volume confirmation is enabled. For a short hold candle, require the mirrored EMA ordering and slope, a touch of the VWAP zone and close below it, and the same optional volume confirmation. On a qualifying hold candle, place a stop entry just beyond that candle’s high for a long or low for a short. Cancel the pending setup if its directional EMA alignment or armed reclaim state is lost, the entry window ends, the crossing limit is reached, or the setup’s stop distance exceeds the configured maximum. Place the protective stop beyond the setup candle’s opposite extreme by the configured buffer, and exit at that stop or at the fixed favorable-price target. Do not open a new position while one is already open.
