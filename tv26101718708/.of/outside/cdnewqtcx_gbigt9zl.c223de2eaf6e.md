Generate a long signal when a cycle low is swept and confirmed by bullish divergence, a bullish fair value gap retest, or correlated-asset sweep/divergence; also accept bullish liquidity-level divergence. Confirm only after price closes above the open of the last bearish candle formed before the cycle low. Generate a short signal symmetrically after a cycle high sweep with bearish divergence, a bearish fair value gap retest, correlated-asset confirmation, or bearish liquidity-level divergence, confirmed by a close below the open of the last bullish candle formed before the cycle high. When bias filtering is enabled, require bullish higher-cycle bias for long signals and bearish higher-cycle bias for short signals. Higher-cycle bias comes from breaks beyond the prior cycle range or the direction of paired highs and lows; retain bias during containment and invalidate it when price breaks the opposing prior extreme.
