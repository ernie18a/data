For a configurable multi-asset universe, exclude assets until they have a full covariance-history window. At scheduled rebalances, estimate annualized return covariance and shrink cross-asset covariances; build a prior using equal weights, inverse volatility, or supplied weights. Derive equilibrium returns from the prior and covariance, then blend in each usable absolute-return or relative-outperformance view according to its stated confidence. Optimize the resulting expected returns against covariance to obtain portfolio weights. Zero ineligible assets, optionally remove shorts, scale to the gross-exposure target, and adjust exposure toward the portfolio-volatility target subject to leverage, per-asset, and total-gross caps. Hold those weights between rebalances. If the optional chart-symbol regime filter is enabled, hold the portfolio only while its fast EMA is above its slow EMA; otherwise flatten all positions, and force a rebalance when the regime returns to active.
