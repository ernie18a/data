A trim signal indicates reducing an existing position. Build a bounded sentiment index as a smoothed, normalized weighted average of these factors: standardized price rate of change; percentile rank of ATR divided by price, inverted when short-term return is negative and otherwise mapped above neutral; RSI; the share of up-close volume in up-close plus down-close volume; close position within the recent high-low range; the fraction of available major crypto assets trading above their EMAs; an inverse score for the change in stablecoin dominance; the average normalized distance of total crypto market capitalization and market capitalization excluding Bitcoin from their respective EMAs; and a categorical score based on the signs of total market capitalization change and Bitcoin dominance change. Substitute neutral scores for unavailable factors, then bound the result. Signal a trim when the index is above an extreme-greed threshold and the traded instrument’s short-period rate of change is below its longer-period rate of change, provided that this combined condition was false on the previous bar. Emit the marker only when signals are enabled and its independent cooldown has expired.
