During the regular session, go long when a bullish crossover of the market-breadth indicator’s smoothed lines coincides with bullish price momentum, volatility above its floor, positive breadth, and price above its trend average. If this setup occurred recently, wait for the stochastic oscillator to turn up below its midpoint-adjusted entry threshold; otherwise enter on the setup. Go short on the mirrored bearish conditions, waiting for the stochastic to turn down above its corresponding threshold when the setup is recent. Also allow long entries on regular bullish price-versus-breadth divergence when breadth trend, price trend, momentum, and volatility confirm; allow short entries on the mirrored bearish divergence. A re-entry after a recent position exit is allowed in the same direction when price continues beyond its exit-time level and breadth, momentum, trend, and stochastic filters confirm. Exit after a minimum holding period on a protective stop based on recent price extremes offset by a fraction of daily ATR; optionally take profit at entry price plus or minus a fraction of daily ATR. Also exit longs on bearish breadth-line alignment with falling momentum, bearish divergence, or an extreme positive breadth reading; exit shorts on the mirrored reversal, bullish divergence, or extreme negative breadth reading. Close positions near the end of the session, with partial overnight holds on selected weekdays when enabled.
