Compare the traded instrument’s price with a benchmark’s price and smooth that relative-strength ratio. Go long when the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average, relative strength is rising, RSI is between its configured lower and upper bounds, and volume meets its configured minimum. Also signal a long entry when the averages are bullish, price crosses back above the fast average after being below it, relative strength is rising, RSI is below a configured ceiling, and volume meets the minimum. Exit a long when the fast average crosses below the slow average, or when relative strength is falling while RSI is above its upper bound. Go short when the fast average crosses below the slow average, relative strength is falling, RSI is below the complement of the configured lower bound, and volume meets the minimum. Cover a short when the fast average crosses above the slow average or relative strength is rising.
