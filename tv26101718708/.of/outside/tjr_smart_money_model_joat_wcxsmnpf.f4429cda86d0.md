Track the latest confirmed swing high and low using symmetric pivot confirmation. Each swing can be broken only once: a close above the latest unbroken high is an upward break, and a close below the latest unbroken low is a downward break. A new pivot resets its corresponding break flag.

Maintain independent long and short setups. A low below the latest swing low followed by a close above it arms or resets the long setup and records that bar's low. A high above the latest swing high followed by a close below it similarly arms or resets the short setup and records that bar's high. Within the configured sweep-to-shift window, confirm the long setup when an upward break occurs on a bullish candle whose body exceeds a configured ATR multiple; confirm the short setup with the mirrored downward break and bearish body. Breaks are consumed even when their candles do not qualify as displacement. Expire unconfirmed setups after the window.

On confirmation, set the entry zone to the current three-bar fair-value gap if one exists in the setup direction and exceeds the configured ATR-based minimum: for long, the zone runs from the high two bars earlier to the current low; for short, from the current high to the low two bars earlier. Otherwise use a band around the midpoint between the recorded sweep extreme and the confirmation bar's opposite extreme, padded by a configured ATR multiple. Expire confirmed setups after the configured entry window. A new sweep in the same direction resets its setup, including an already confirmed one. Confirmation and entry may occur on the same bar.

A long entry candidate requires the bar's low at or below the zone top, close at or above the zone bottom, and close at or above open. A short candidate requires the high at or above the zone bottom, close at or below the zone top, and close at or below open. If the session filter is enabled, require the bar to fall within an enabled configured session in its configured timezone. If the divergence filter is enabled, require a specified correlated instrument and divergence at the time of entry: compare the two most recent confirmed traded-instrument pivots of the relevant type with correlated-instrument highs or lows at those same pivot bars, using data without lookahead. For a long, the traded instrument must have a lower pivot low while the correlated instrument's low is unchanged or higher; for a short, it must have a higher pivot high while the correlated high is unchanged or lower.

Consume each setup as soon as its entry candidate passes these filters, before applying signal spacing. If both candidates occur together, give the long priority while consuming both setups. Emit the remaining signal only if the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since the last emitted signal; a suppressed candidate remains consumed.

For each emitted signal, use the close as entry. Place the stop at a distance equal to the absolute difference between entry and the recorded sweep extreme padded outward by a configured ATR multiple; if that distance is zero, use ATR. Place configured profit targets at multiples of that risk in the trade direction. For the displayed outcome tally, resolve an open signal when its first target or stop is touched, checking the target first if both are touched on the same bar; a new signal replaces any still-open tally position and counts it as a loss.
