Track confirmed swing highs and lows. A bearish setup begins when price trades above a swing high but closes back below it; a bullish setup begins when price trades below a swing low but closes back above it. Within a configurable window, require a large directional candle to close through the opposing short-term swing level: upward for the bullish setup, downward for the bearish setup. After that shift, wait for price to retrace into the displacement fair-value gap, if one forms, or otherwise into a buffered midpoint of the reversal leg. Enter long when price reaches the bullish zone and closes bullish, or short when it reaches the bearish zone and closes bearish, before the setup expires. Optionally require session-window eligibility and divergence against a configured correlated instrument: for longs, the traded instrument makes a lower swing low while the correlated instrument does not; for shorts, it makes a higher swing high while the correlated instrument does not. Invalidate the setup after its entry window; place the stop beyond the swept extreme and derive profit targets from the entry-to-stop risk. No separate exit signal is defined.
