For each non-dollar instrument, calculate a momentum oscillator on each enabled timeframe. Assign a bullish direction when every timeframe designated for trend agrees above zero, bearish when they all agree below zero, and no direction otherwise. Qualify a directional candidate only when every enabled filter passes: higher-timeframe dollar-index readings agree and are not sign-flagged; fast dollar-index momentum is not strongly building against the candidate direction (reverse the dollar sign for pairs quoted with USD as the base currency); the instrument’s structural and entry-readiness scores exceed their thresholds; its trend timeframes are not sign-flagged and each has meaningful momentum; and its relative-to-dollar amplification is below its limit. Optional filters also require the euro pair’s higher-timeframe readings to agree. Structural score blends higher-timeframe momentum strength with absolute correlation strength to the dollar index; entry score blends fastest-timeframe momentum strength with direction-aligned momentum change, optionally rewarding a recent pause followed by a sustained recovery. Rank fully qualified candidates by the geometric mean of structural and entry scores; mark the highest-ranked three. This is screening and ranking logic: it produces directional trend candidates, but specifies no entry execution or exit rule.
