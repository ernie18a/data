During the defined intraday session, enter long when the MACD histogram is positive; the candle’s open and close are above the Alligator lips; close is above session VWAP; the range filter is rising; the high is above the Alligator teeth; lips are above the jaw; and the prior candle’s bullish body is below a size threshold. Enter short when the inverse conditions hold, with the bearish setup requiring the high below the teeth and the prior candle’s bearish body below a size threshold. Enter only if not already in that direction. Exit a long when close falls below the lips and the MACD histogram is sufficiently negative; exit a short when close rises above the lips and the histogram is sufficiently positive. Submit the exit as a stop at session VWAP.
