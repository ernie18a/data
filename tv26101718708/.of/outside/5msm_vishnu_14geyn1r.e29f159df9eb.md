On each bar close, calculate a MACD histogram from configurable fast and slow moving averages and a configurable signal average. Calculate short-, medium-, and long-period smoothed moving averages of the bar midpoint, seeded with simple moving averages. Calculate anchored VWAP, with the anchor selectable by calendar period or corporate event. Calculate a range filter from the selected source, optionally using Heikin-Ashi data: double-smooth the absolute source change with exponential averages, multiply by a configurable factor, and move the filter only when the source exceeds its prior value by that range. Track whether the filter's most recent movement was upward or downward, retaining that direction while it is flat.

During the configured session, enter long and issue a buy-call alert when the histogram is positive, open and close are above the short smoothed average, close is above VWAP, the range filter direction is upward, high is above the medium smoothed average, the short average is above the long average, and the previous candle's close minus open is below a configurable threshold. Enter short and issue a buy-put alert when the histogram is negative, open and close are below the short average, close is below VWAP, the filter direction is downward, high is below the medium average, the short average is below the long average, and the previous candle's open minus close is below the threshold. Do not repeat an entry when position size already equals the corresponding signed unit; an opposite entry reverses the position. Alert strikes are derived from the current open using direction-specific offsets and rounding down to the strike interval.

When in a long position, if close is below the short average and the histogram is below a negative threshold, submit a stop exit at the current VWAP. For a short position, if close is above the short average and the histogram is above the positive threshold, submit a stop exit at VWAP. These are stop orders, not immediate exits; associate them with the entry identifiers constructed from the close and direction-specific strike offsets.

Also alert to book profit when a long bar opens below and trades above a configurable distance above average entry price, or a short bar opens above and trades below the corresponding distance below entry. Alert to exit at a loss when the previous close is both below the previous medium average and below long entry price, or both above the previous medium average and above short entry price, provided the previous medium average is at or below the previous long ATR stop. That stop is the selected open, close, or rolling low, less a configurable ATR multiple. These alerts do not themselves submit exit orders.
