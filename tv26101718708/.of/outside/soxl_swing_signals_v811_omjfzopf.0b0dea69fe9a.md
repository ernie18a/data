For SOXL, generate a long signal when any long setup fires, long conviction meets its configured minimum, and the long cooldown has elapsed. Long setups are: (1) oversold stochastic, elevated VIX and VIX panic ratio, VIX no longer rising sharply, and price near a lower regression or Bollinger band; (2) deeply oversold stochastic, price below its short moving average, elevated VIX, lower-band location, and improving MACD histogram; (3) an uptrend with price pulling back to lower regression bands and its short moving average, a moderately weak stochastic, a small daily decline followed by an up close; (4) price below the lower Bollinger band and short moving average, oversold stochastic, elevated VIX, and improving MACD; (5) elevated VIX that has begun retreating from its recent peak, price materially below its short moving average, oversold stochastic, and lower-band location; (6) extremely oversold stochastic, deeply below its short moving average, elevated VIX panic ratio, and improving MACD; or (7) a severe single-day price drop with elevated VIX, oversold stochastic, and lower-band location. Long conviction adds one point each for a high VIX panic ratio; agreement that price is deep in both the long regression and Bollinger ranges; improving MACD; high VIX; positive long regression slope; price near the lower band of the pivot-low-anchored regression channel; and price near a major Fibonacci retracement of the prior upswing.

Generate a short signal when an enabled short setup fires and the short cooldown has elapsed. Legacy short setups require their master toggle: (1) price extended above its short moving average but not excessively above its medium moving average, regime-adjusted moving-average positioning, overbought stochastic, upper-band location, calm VIX, limited directional-strength gap, positive MACD, no recent VIX crash regime, and a pullback from the recent high; (2) optionally, a Bollinger breakout with extension, overbought stochastic, regime-adjusted positioning, calm VIX, and the same crash and pullback guards; (3) optionally, price rising while RSI falls, with minimum extension and RSI, calm VIX, a bull regime, and the pullback guard; (4) optionally, a sustained period above the short moving average followed by declining stochastic, continued extension, calm VIX, a bull regime, and the pullback guard; or (5) VIX rising while price remains extended, stochastic is elevated, VIX is below its upper limit, there is no recent crash regime, and the instrument is in a bull regime. The separate opt-in bear-cycle short requires the semiconductor index's long moving-average trend to be declining, SOXL below its medium moving average and making a new recent low, stochastic not already deeply oversold, and no recent VIX crash regime. The separate opt-in top-fade short requires price at the upper Bollinger band, overbought stochastic, strong extension above its short moving average, a stalled down close, and no recent VIX crash regime. The legacy and separate short switches are off by default. There is no exit signal logic. Long and short signals have separate cooldowns.
