Evaluate each bar using the traded instrument’s OHLC data, daily VIX close and daily semiconductor-index close. Treat all lookbacks, thresholds, multipliers, tolerances, enable switches and cooldowns as configurable parameters. A band position is (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band). Compute Bollinger bands and short- and long-window linear-regression channels; for the latter, use the standard deviation over the window of the series of close minus that bar’s rolling regression endpoint. Define the regression slope as the current fitted endpoint minus the preceding fitted point from the same regression, divided by the current endpoint. Calculate smoothed stochastic, MACD histogram, RSI, directional indicators, moving-average distances and daily close return. Bull regime means the medium moving average exceeds the long moving average. Panic is the recent maximum VIX divided by its moving average. Lower-band confirmation means any of the long regression, long Bollinger, short regression or short Bollinger positions is at or below its respective lower threshold. Crash recovery means the recent maximum VIX reaches its lockout threshold.

Generate a long candidate if any enabled setup holds: (1) stochastic is oversold, VIX and panic are elevated, VIX has not risen more than its allowed fraction since the previous bar, and lower-band confirmation holds; (2) stochastic is oversold, close is sufficiently below its short moving average, VIX is above its minimum, lower-band confirmation holds, and the MACD histogram is rising; (3) the long regression slope is sufficiently positive, both regression positions are low, close is at or below its short moving average, stochastic is below its pullback ceiling, the bull regime holds, daily return exceeds its loss floor, and close exceeds the previous close; (4) close is at or below the lower short Bollinger band, sufficiently below its short moving average, stochastic is oversold, VIX is above its minimum, and MACD histogram is rising; (5) VIX is elevated but has fallen sufficiently from its recent maximum, close is sufficiently below its short moving average, stochastic is oversold, and lower-band confirmation holds; (6) stochastic and distance below the short moving average are deeply oversold, VIX and panic exceed their minima, and MACD histogram is rising; or (7) daily return is sufficiently negative, VIX is elevated, stochastic is oversold, and lower-band confirmation holds.

Score long conviction with one point each for elevated panic; both long regression and long Bollinger positions being deeply low; rising MACD histogram; elevated VIX; sufficiently positive long regression slope; optionally, low position in a regression channel anchored to the latest confirmed pivot low; and optionally, close being within tolerance of either selected Fibonacci retracement of the rise from the prior low to the recent highest high. The anchored channel uses a clamped number of bars since that pivot, least-squares regression of closes and bands based on residual standard deviation. Issue a long signal only when a candidate exists, conviction reaches its minimum and the long cooldown has elapsed.

Generate a short candidate through independently enabled groups. All short setups require crash recovery to be false. The bear-cycle setup additionally requires the semiconductor index’s long moving average to have declined sufficiently over its slope lookback, the traded close below its medium moving average and at or below the lowest close of the preceding breakdown window, and stochastic above its minimum. The top-fade setup requires a high short Bollinger position, overbought stochastic, sufficient extension above the short moving average, and a close below the previous close with daily return below its ceiling.

The optional legacy group contains these alternatives: (1) sufficient extension above the short moving average but below the maximum extension above the medium average, the applicable regime-specific distance test, overbought stochastic, a high short Bollinger or short regression position, VIX below its ceiling, positive directional-indicator gap below its ceiling, positive MACD histogram, and sufficient drawdown from the recent highest close; (2) optionally, close at or above the upper short Bollinger band, sufficient short-average extension, overbought stochastic, the regime-specific distance test, VIX below its ceiling and sufficient drawdown; (3) optionally, close higher but RSI lower than their values at a divergence lookback, sufficient short-average extension, elevated RSI, calm VIX, bull regime and sufficient drawdown; (4) optionally, enough consecutive preceding closes at or above the short moving average, previously elevated stochastic now declining, sufficient short-average extension, calm VIX, bull regime and sufficient drawdown; or (5) VIX has risen sufficiently over its lookback while remaining below its ceiling, close is sufficiently above the short moving average, stochastic exceeds its minimum and the bull regime holds. For the regime-specific distance test, require a minimum distance above the medium average in a bull regime; otherwise require distance from the long average at or below its ceiling.

Issue a short signal when any enabled short candidate exists and the short cooldown has elapsed. Long and short cooldowns are independent and reset only on their own emitted signals. Short conviction is display-only and does not gate a signal. The indicator defines no position exit rule; stated holding horizons are informational.
