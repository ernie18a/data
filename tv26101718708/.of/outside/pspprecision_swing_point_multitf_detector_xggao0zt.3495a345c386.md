For each enabled timeframe at least as long as the chart timeframe, evaluate the most recently closed candle of three configured correlated instruments when a new candle begins. Only signal if the traded instrument is one of the three. If all three candles are non-doji, signal long when exactly one closed above its open and the other two closed below; signal short when exactly one closed below its open and the other two closed above. The direction does not depend on which instrument is the outlier. If the optional accumulation filter is enabled, suppress either signal when, over a configurable recent window on the traded instrument at that timeframe, at least a configurable number of candles have both volume above a configurable multiple of its moving average and high-low range below a configurable multiple of average true range. An unavailable accumulation state does not suppress the signal. No exit rule is specified.
