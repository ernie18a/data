For each enabled timeframe no shorter than the chart timeframe, evaluate the most recently closed candle for three correlated instruments, including the traded instrument. If all three candles are non-dojis and exactly one closes up while the other two close down, signal long; if exactly one closes down while the other two close up, signal short. Emit the signal only when the chart instrument is one of the three, at the timeframe candle close, and the same-timeframe traded-instrument data is not in an accumulation zone. Define an accumulation bar as one whose volume exceeds its rolling average and whose high-low range is below its average true range; a zone exists when enough accumulation bars occur within a recent rolling window. Ignore signals inside such a zone.
