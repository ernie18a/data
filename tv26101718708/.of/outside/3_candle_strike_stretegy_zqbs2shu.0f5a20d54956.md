Within the configured date and entry-session window, enter long on a bullish three-candle reversal (three consecutive non-bullish candles followed by a candle closing above the prior candle’s open) when the fast smoothed average is above and rising relative to the medium average and the smoothed linear-regression slope indicates a trend. Alternatively, enter long on that same pattern shortly after the fast exponential average crosses the fast smoothed average upward, while the fast and medium smoothed averages are below the slow smoothed average. Enter short on the inverse reversal, with the fast smoothed average below and falling relative to the medium average and a trending slope; alternatively, shortly after the fast exponential average crosses the fast smoothed average downward while the fast and medium smoothed averages are above the slow average. Suppress new entries during the configured pause interval or when the bar’s range exceeds a fraction of daily ATR. Exit a long on the short reversal pattern and a short on the long reversal pattern, or when its stop is hit. Set the initial stop beyond the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short across the recent pattern bars, with an ATR-based offset; set the profit target at a configurable risk multiple. If trailing after target is enabled, reaching the target activates a stop that follows recent prices; otherwise exit at the target or stop. Close all positions outside the entry session.
