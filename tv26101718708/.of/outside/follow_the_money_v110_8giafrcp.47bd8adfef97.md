For an eligible instrument and date, enter long after price has remained above its long-term trend average for several consecutive bars, the faster long-term average is above the slower one, and the slower average is rising. Require a breakout or proximity to a recent closing high, unusually high volume versus its recent average, positive relative performance versus an assigned sector benchmark, a bullish benchmark regime (benchmark above its medium-term average, which is above its long-term average), and a sufficiently high aggregate score. The score rewards trend alignment and price position, high volume and closes near the bar high, volatility expansion, rising-volume accumulation measures (OBV and accumulation/distribution), benchmark-relative strength, and a bullish benchmark regime. Add to the long position in stages: add after a favorable open-profit threshold while price remains above its trend average and relative strength is positive; add again at a larger profit threshold while price remains above that average. Close the entire position if price has stayed below its trend average for several consecutive bars and high volume occurred during that below-average streak, or if price falls below a catastrophic floor set beneath the trend average. Sell half once per trade if the trend average becomes nearly flat while price remains above it. Evaluate actions at bar close when close confirmation is enabled; after a full exit, wait through a cooldown before a new entry.
