Use the traded instrument’s mapped sector benchmark, or a default benchmark if unmapped. Define relative strength as the instrument’s lookback return minus the benchmark’s return; strength is positive when this difference is above zero. Define a bullish benchmark regime as its close above its fast simple moving average and that average above its slow simple moving average.

Calculate an entry score as the sum of weighted points for: the instrument’s fast moving average above its slow simple moving average; the slow average rising over its slope lookback; close above the fast average; volume expanding above its simple average by at least the required percentage; close sufficiently high within the bar’s range; ATR above or below its own simple average, with different points for expansion and compression; cumulative on-balance volume above its simple average; cumulative accumulation/distribution above its simple average; positive relative strength, with a larger award above a further strength threshold; and a bullish benchmark regime. Award an additional set of points for the same high-close-quality condition. Use the specified weights and thresholds as parameters.

When flat, enter long only after the cooldown following a full exit, provided the fast average exceeds the rising slow average, close has stayed above the fast average for the required consecutive bars, volume expansion and positive relative strength are present, the benchmark regime is bullish, and the score reaches its entry threshold. If breakout confirmation is enabled, also require the close to exceed either the prior lookback’s highest close or a specified fraction of a separate prior highest close. Apply the configured date range and symbol exclusion to entries and adds. Size the starter so two successive proportional adds would reach the configured total equity allocation.

At the first position stage, add a configured fraction of the current position when profit relative to the current average entry price reaches the first threshold, close is above the fast average, and relative strength is positive. At the second stage, add the same fraction when profit reaches the second threshold and close is above the fast average. Advance the stage after each entry; reset it when flat.

While long, close the entire position if close has remained at or below the fast average for the required consecutive bars and expanded volume occurred anywhere in that streak, or if close falls below a specified percentage of the fast average. Independently, once per trade, close the specified fraction of every open entry when close is above the fast average and the absolute percentage slope of that average over its lookback is within the flattening threshold. Exits and trims are not restricted by the entry date or symbol filters. Optionally require confirmed bar closes for all orders. No short or options trades are made.
