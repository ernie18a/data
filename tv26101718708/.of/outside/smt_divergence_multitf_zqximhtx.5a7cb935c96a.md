For each of those two pivots, select the closest already-confirmed comparison-asset pivot of the same type within a configurable time tolerance; resolve equal-distance matches in favor of the earlier stored pivot. If both matches exist, signal short when the traded instrument makes a strictly higher high while the comparison asset makes a strictly lower high. Signal long when the traded instrument makes a strictly lower low while the comparison asset makes a strictly higher low. Evaluate only when the traded-instrument pivot is reported, not when a comparison pivot arrives later. The logic specifies no exit rule.
