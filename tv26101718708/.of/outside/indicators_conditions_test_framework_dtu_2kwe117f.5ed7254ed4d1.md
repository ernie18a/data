Within a configurable trading period, evaluate separate configurable rules for long entry, short entry, long exit, and short exit. Each rule can contain up to four ordered comparisons using the traded instrument’s prices, configured indicators, or an externally supplied indicator. Compare the current first series with a constant or a selected historical value of the second series using a crossover, crossunder, either crossing, equality, inequality, or an ordered comparison. Rising and falling tests compare the first series with its preceding values over a configured lookback. Indicator values may be transformed to stochastic or percentile rank and smoothed before comparison. Join consecutive active comparisons from left to right using their configured AND or OR operators; the first comparison must be active, and evaluation stops at the first inactive comparison. Enter or exit the corresponding side when its rule is true. Optionally, also close each side when the opposite entry rule is true. No signals are acted on outside the configured period.
