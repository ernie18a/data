For each instrument, use a dated, externally researched directional map containing permitted sides, trigger, confirmation, invalidation, hard invalidation, action zone, three targets, setup score, event status and market-gate category. A valid manual map overrides the preset map. For eligible unmapped instruments, construct a two-sided map from the prior-day range and daily ATR, optionally requiring a prior inside day. Watch-only maps cannot generate entries.

For event setups, if the opening gap is large, crosses a hard invalidation, or consumes enough of the move to the first target, wait for the opening range and replace the mapped levels with opening-range boundaries, VWAP and range-based offsets. Otherwise, a one-sided map is disabled for the session if price closes beyond its hard invalidation after the opening freeze.

Allow entries only during the configured session, with a fresh map and sufficient operational integrity. Block entries during the opening-range build, macro-event freeze, after the new-risk cutoff, while an event remap is pending, or when the map is broken. Integrity is a weighted availability score for market internals, core market feeds, chart volume and map freshness, reduced for stale internals and non-intraday charts.

For a long, require consecutive closes above the mapped trigger, or a close above confirmation with sufficient relative volume and a bullish close location. Also require a recent trigger retest, recent touch of the mapped demand zone, high-volume bullish expansion, or the confirmation condition itself. Mirror these conditions for a short using closes below the trigger, a recent retest or supply-zone touch, and bearish expansion. Reject entries too far from the trigger or with insufficient first-target reward relative to trigger-to-invalidation risk; index, volatility and event maps have the specified exemptions from reward and relative-strength filters.

Apply enabled directional filters: price relative to VWAP; aligned execution, structure and selected higher-timeframe EMA pairs; rising or falling EMA momentum; directional RSI without exhaustion; prior-day range breakout context; and adequate trigger-bar relative volume, except for instruments with unreliable volume. Require the instrument's return since its previous close to exceed or lag its category benchmark by the configured edge, except for index, volatility and event maps.

Apply the instrument category's market gate. Index, growth, semiconductor and small-cap longs require the relevant broad-market or sector ETFs to clear their mapped repair levels with sufficiently low VIX; shorts require the corresponding breakdown or risk-off conditions. Software also uses software-versus-growth leadership; energy uses crude and energy-versus-broad-market performance; banks, healthcare, quality and biotech use their respective sector leadership, broad-market levels and volatility conditions. Volatility instruments use VIX levels directly. Generic instruments use broad-market and VIX gates.

During regular intraday trading, when enabled and available, require a directional market-internals score derived from up-volume share, smoothed TICK, the short-term volatility curve and VIX; use stricter thresholds for market-sensitive categories. Also require a directional composite surface: combine an ATR-normalized weighted measure of price versus VWAP, execution and structure EMA spreads, and relative return versus SPY with a weighted measure of internals, volatility conditions and broad-market repair or risk-off gates. The composite and structural component must both satisfy their directional thresholds.

Calculate a weighted setup-quality score from the map's base score and the acceptance, geometry and filter results. Fire only when all enabled hard gates pass, the score meets the selected strictness threshold, and the side's cooldown and per-session signal limit permit it. If both sides qualify on the same bar, choose the higher score, with a tie favoring long. Reset session signal limits at the regular-session open.

On a signal, record the close as entry and manage the active side against its mapped levels. Before the first target, exit if the bar reaches invalidation. On reaching the first target, mark it achieved and move the stop to entry; on a later bar, exit if price returns to entry before the third target. Mark the second target when reached and close the trade at the third target. Evaluate targets before invalidation on each bar, and reset the tracked trade at the next regular-session open.
