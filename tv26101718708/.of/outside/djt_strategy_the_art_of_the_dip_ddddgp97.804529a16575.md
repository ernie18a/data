On each confirmed chart bar, calculate the volatility index's percentage change from its previous confirmed daily close and its percentage rate of change over a configurable number of chart bars. Define a panic signal when both changes are available, either reaches its respective positive threshold, the volatility index is at or above its floor, and, if enabled, the short-dated volatility index is above it.

When flat, after the configured cooldown measured from the most recent closed trade's exit bar, and within each enabled New York time session filter, enter long on a panic signal. Use the base position size, multiplied and rounded to the nearest whole unit when the daily change reaches twice the panic threshold. If the optional complacency trade is enabled, enter short at base size when the daily change is at or below the negative crush threshold and there is no panic signal. An optional reversal mode swaps the entry directions of both trade types.

For every open position, place a stop loss and profit target at configured percentages of average entry price, adjusted for position direction. For a panic trade, close the entire position when the daily volatility change falls to or below the full-reversion threshold; otherwise, once per trade, close the floor of half the current position when it falls to or below the partial-exit threshold and at least one unit can be closed; otherwise, close after the configured maximum holding period. For a complacency trade, close after that holding period or whenever a panic signal occurs. Evaluate signals at bar close and execute market orders at the next bar open.
