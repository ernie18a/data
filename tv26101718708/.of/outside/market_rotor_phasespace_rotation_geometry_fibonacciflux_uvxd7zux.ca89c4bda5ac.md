After warm-up, allow at most one signal per cooldown interval. Go long on either: (1) the normalized phase-space position is below a negative extreme, the three-bar price return turns positive after being nonpositive, rotation consistency is strong, the spiral index is below its outward-spiral threshold, and phase-slip hazard is below its cap; or (2) position crosses above a positive breakout threshold, the three-bar return is positive, radius exceeds its minimum, the spiral index indicates outward expansion, and hazard is below its cap. Go short symmetrically: either position is above a positive extreme as the three-bar return turns negative after being nonnegative, with strong rotation consistency, spiral index below its outward threshold, and low hazard; or position crosses below a negative breakout threshold with negative three-bar return, sufficient radius, outward expansion, and low hazard. The phase-space drive may use the selected price-derived, order-flow, volatility, or open-interest data. No exit rule is defined.
