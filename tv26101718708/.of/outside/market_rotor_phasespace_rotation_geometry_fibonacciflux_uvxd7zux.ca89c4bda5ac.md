The optional experimental signals can use the traded instrument’s separately requested open-interest series. In that mode, form a price axis from log close minus its equilibrium exponential average, and a second axis from the lagged change in open interest. Standardize both using rolling means and standard deviations, optionally freezing those statistics after warm-up. Optionally whiten the pair using the inverse square root of their rolling covariance matrix, also frozen when anchored, then clip each coordinate to a symmetric limit. If open-interest data is unavailable, generate no signals.

For each bar, calculate the radius and the signed angle between consecutive coordinate vectors. Set the angle and change in log radius to zero when either endpoint is inside the central dead zone. Over the consistency window, divide the sum of signed angles by the sum of absolute angles to obtain rotation consistency; divide the sum of changes in log radius by the same denominator to obtain the spiral index. Treat both as unavailable unless total absolute turning exceeds its gate. Calculate phase-slip hazard as the capped sum of standardized angular surprise, a weighted decrease in absolute consistency, and a penalty for a sufficiently strong consistency sign flip. Angular surprise compares the current angle with its slow exponential average and scales the difference by its rolling standard deviation, subject to a minimum denominator.

After warm-up and any anchoring delay, and only when the shared signal cooldown has elapsed, evaluate these rules using the change in log close over the reversal lookback:
- Long reversal: the price coordinate is below the negative reversal threshold, the log-price change crosses from nonpositive to positive, absolute rotation consistency exceeds its threshold, the spiral index is below its threshold, and hazard is below the reversal hazard limit.
- Short reversal: the price coordinate is above the positive reversal threshold, the log-price change crosses from nonnegative to negative, and the same consistency, spiral, and hazard conditions hold.
- Long breakout: the price coordinate crosses above the positive breakout threshold, the log-price change is positive, radius exceeds its threshold, the spiral index exceeds its threshold, and hazard is below the breakout hazard limit.
- Short breakout: the price coordinate crosses below the negative breakout threshold, the log-price change is negative, and the same radius, spiral, and hazard conditions hold.

Reset the shared cooldown whenever any signal fires, including when signal arrows are hidden. The rules specify no exit.
