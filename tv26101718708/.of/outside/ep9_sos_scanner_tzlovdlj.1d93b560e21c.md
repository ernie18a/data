On each daily bar, identify a long setup when all of these hold: the previous close divided by the close before it is at or below a configured cap; the current range is positive and the close's position within that range is at or above a configured threshold; the current signed candle body exceeds the previous signed candle body; and the close and volume each meet their configured minimums. Calculate average daily range as the simple moving average of high minus low over a configured period, and express it as a percentage of the current close. Use the current close as entry. If the current range as a percentage of entry meets a configured multiple of the average-range percentage, use the current low as the stop; otherwise use entry minus a configured multiple of the average range. Accept the setup only if the entry-to-stop risk percentage does not exceed the average-range percentage. For an accepted setup, evaluate three additional filters: close is above its configured long-term simple moving average; that average is above its value a configured number of bars ago; and the ratio of the instrument's close to a configured benchmark's close is above its own configured-period simple moving average. A strict long go decision requires all three filters; a moderate long go decision requires at least two. The script also displays a stop and profit target levels based on entry risk, but does not issue an exit decision.
