Compare the dollar, volatility index, Treasury yield, money supply, and CPI with their own recent averages. Increase a macro risk score when the dollar, volatility, or yield is above its average, money supply is below its average, or CPI is above its average; the CPI condition contributes less than each other condition. A low score recommends a risk-on approach, such as trend trades, calendars, or long risk. A middle score recommends reduced size and selective trades. A high score recommends long volatility, breakouts, or defensive trades.
