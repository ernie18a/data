On each bar, allow a long entry only when the stock passes a trend template: price is above the intermediate and long-term averages, the intermediate average is above the long-term average, the long-term average is rising, the short-term average is above both, and price is sufficiently near its yearly high and above its yearly low. Also require relative strength versus the external market index to be above its own average and improving; contracting price range and volatility with tighter closes; price not excessively extended above its short-term average; a healthy external market index; and a supportive weekly trend. The market and weekly filters are configurable.

Enter long on any of these triggers while those quality conditions hold: (1) an early setup when price closes above a recent short-term high, volume exceeds its average by the configured early-entry threshold, and volume had been drying up; (2) a breakout when price closes above the prior pivot high, closes up, volume exceeds its average by the configured breakout threshold, and the close is in the upper portion of the candle; or (3) a retest after a recent qualifying breakout, when price trades near the pivot, closes near or above it, remains above the short-term average, and closes up. Record the pivot and recent swing low at entry. Do not open another position while already in one.

While long, raise the active stop to the higher of its prior value and the recent trailing swing low. Exit when the close falls below that stop; or falls below both the entry pivot and short-term average; or falls below the faster trend average on a down close; or a down candle closes lower on unusually high volume. Clear the position state on exit.
