On each chart bar, evaluate a long setup using the traded instrument and a selected market index. Require all of the following: the close is above the short, intermediate, and long simple moving averages; the short average is above both longer averages, the intermediate average is above the long average, and the long average is rising over a lookback; the close is sufficiently near its rolling high and above its rolling low; the instrument-to-index close ratio is above both its moving average and its value a lookback ago; the short-range high-low span is less than a specified fraction of the long-range span, ATR as a percentage of close is below its average, and the absolute close-to-close change as a percentage of close is below its average; and the close's percentage distance from the short moving average lies within specified bounds. If enabled, also require the index close above its short and long moving averages with the short average above the long average, and the instrument's weekly close above its short weekly average with that average above its longer weekly average.

When flat, enter long if the setup holds and any of these triggers occurs: (1) close exceeds the prior-bars short-lookback high, current volume exceeds a multiple of average volume, and on the previous bar short-average volume was below long-average volume; (2) close exceeds the prior-bars pivot high, closes above open and above a specified fraction of the bar's range, and volume exceeds a breakout multiple of average volume; or (3) a breakout trigger occurred within a specified number of bars, the low is no higher than a tolerance above the current pivot and the close is no lower than a tolerance below it, and the close is above both the short moving average and open. Track breakout triggers even while in a trade. The pivot is the highest high over its lookback excluding the current bar.

On entry, save the current pivot and set the stop to the lowest low over an initial lookback. On every bar in the trade, including the entry bar, raise the stop to the greater of its current value and the lowest low over a trailing lookback. Exit if the close is below the stop; or below both the saved pivot and the short moving average; or below the shorter exponential moving average and the prior close; or below both the open and prior close while volume exceeds a specified multiple of average volume. Evaluate exits after entries, so entry and exit can occur on the same bar. No short positions are generated.
