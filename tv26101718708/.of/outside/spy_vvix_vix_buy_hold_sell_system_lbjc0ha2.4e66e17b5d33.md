On each bar, compare the traded instrument’s price with its trend average and a volatility-adaptive range filter. The range filter follows price while limiting movement by a smoothed measure of recent price changes; classify it bullish when price is above the filter and the filter has been rising, bearish when below it and falling. From external volatility indices VVIX and VIX, classify each as rising when above its own average and prior value, and VVIX as falling when below both. Define calm risk as VVIX below a caution threshold and VIX not rising; danger as VVIX at or above a danger threshold; panic as VVIX at or above a higher panic threshold or both indices rising. Apply this priority: go long when the instrument trend and range are bullish, risk is calm, and VVIX is falling; otherwise exit or reduce when panic risk holds, or danger coincides with rising VVIX, or both instrument trend and range are bearish; otherwise hold when trend and range are bullish and panic risk is absent; in all other cases retain the prior state.
