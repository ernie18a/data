Calculate rolling Z-scores for Bitcoin’s NVT ratio (market capitalization divided by transaction volume), MVRV ratio (market capitalization divided by realized capitalization), AVIV ratio (spot price divided by the EMA of realized price), and SOPR. Smooth each Z-score, combine them as a weighted average, then smooth the composite. Enter or maintain a long regime when the composite is above the upper threshold; enter or maintain a short regime when it is below the lower threshold. Between the thresholds, keep the previous regime. There is no neutral state, and no separate exit condition.
