On each chart bar, calculate fast and slow EMAs of the traded instrument and of the external OTHERS.D index. Define the long signal as the instrument fast EMA being above its slow EMA, or equal while the index fast EMA is below its slow EMA. Define the short signal as the instrument fast EMA being below its slow EMA. Classify the combined EMA state by whether the instrument fast EMA is at least its slow EMA and whether the index fast EMA is at least its slow EMA; retain the previous bar's state.

Confirm swing highs and lows when the high or low a configured number of bars ago equals the highest high or lowest low over the symmetric window around it. Retain the latest confirmed levels. Set the persistent swing trend up when close exceeds the latest swing high and down when close falls below the latest swing low, processing the down condition last.

When flat, enter long if the long signal holds, swing trend is up, RSI is below its upper threshold, and long entry is not blocked. Enter short if the short signal holds, swing trend is down, RSI is above its lower threshold, and short entry is not blocked. While short, close on an eligible long signal; while long, close on an eligible short signal. These signal-based closes do not require the swing trend or RSI entry conditions. Separately, close a short when swing trend is up and a long when swing trend is down. Entries require the position to be flat at the time the entry condition is evaluated.

Calculate position return from close and the latest open trade's entry price. If return falls below the stop threshold, close a long only when there is no short signal, or a short only when there is no long signal, and block re-entry in that direction. Clear the long block when the preceding EMA state had instrument fast at least slow and the current state is not instrument fast below slow with index fast above slow. Clear the short block when the preceding state had instrument fast below slow and the current state is not instrument fast at least slow with index fast below slow.

Once per position, partially close a profitable long when the prior EMA state had both fast EMAs at least their slow EMAs and that state changes, provided there is no short signal. Partially close a profitable short when the prior state had both fast EMAs below their slow EMAs and that state changes, provided there is no long signal. Require return to exceed a configured positive threshold. Reset this scale-out flag on entry.

If take profit is enabled, set three targets at successive multiples of a scaled ATR from the entry signal's close, above for long and below for short. On each bar, independently close a fixed fraction of the current position at each untriggered target whose level is strictly exceeded by the high for a long or strictly undercut by the low for a short; clear each target after triggering. Orders are generated on bar evaluation, and closes use market orders rather than target-price limit orders. The higher-timeframe swing display does not filter trades. The color-change waiting flag is never activated, so it does not restrict entries.
