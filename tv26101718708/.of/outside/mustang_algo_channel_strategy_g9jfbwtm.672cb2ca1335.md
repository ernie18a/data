On a configurable analysis timeframe, obtain Bitcoin's USD close and the market capitalizations of DAI, USDT, and USDC without lookahead, carrying values forward across gaps and replacing missing values with zero. Divide a fixed Bitcoin supply assumption by the sum of those capitalizations divided by Bitcoin's price. Calculate RSI of this ratio, smooth it with a weighted moving average, then apply a second weighted moving average to produce a reference line. Carry the completed timeframe values onto the chart without lookahead.

Within the inclusive configured date range, when flat, enter long if long trading is enabled and the oscillator crosses above its reference while below the lower threshold. Enter short if short trading is enabled and the oscillator crosses below its reference while above the upper threshold. A crossover requires the previous oscillator value to be at or below the previous reference, and a crossunder requires it to be at or above it. Do not reverse or add to an open position.

For an open position, if either stop loss or take profit is enabled, place the enabled orders using the position's entry price: below and above entry respectively for a long, and above and below entry for a short. Calculate distances as configured percentages of entry, numbers of minimum price ticks, or numbers of pips, with one pip equal to ten minimum ticks. These orders remain active outside the date range. If both are disabled, close a long on the short signal or a short on the long signal, subject to the date range and the corresponding position direction being enabled. Signal exits are disabled whenever either protective order is enabled.
