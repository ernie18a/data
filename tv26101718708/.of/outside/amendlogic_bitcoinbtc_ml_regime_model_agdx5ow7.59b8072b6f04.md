On each confirmed bar, estimate the probability of an upward move over a future horizon with a walk-forward logistic model trained on normalized, clipped features: short-, medium-, and long-term returns; RSI; distance from a trend average; volume and signed-volume interaction; volatility and momentum-volatility interaction; DXY and TIP trends; the higher-timeframe price trend; and DXY-momentum interaction. Trust this estimate only after sufficient training and when its rolling directional hit rate meets a reliability threshold. Otherwise, use a fallback probability based on directional votes from the higher-timeframe price trend, DXY weakness, and TIP strength, giving the price-trend vote twice the weight of either macro vote. Optionally blend either probability with a short-term demand estimate derived from return and signed volume.

Block new entries when the absolute normalized one-bar return, normalized volume, or normalized volatility is anomalous. When trend alignment is required, permit longs only with the higher-timeframe price trend up and shorts only with it down. From flat, enter long when the final upward probability reaches the long-entry threshold, or enter short when it falls to the short-entry threshold. In a long position, request exit when probability falls below the long-exit threshold; in a short position, request exit when it rises above the short-exit threshold. Apply hysteresis through distinct entry and exit thresholds, and allow any position change, including exits, only after the minimum holding period. After an exit, evaluate entry conditions on that bar. Signals and state changes are evaluated on confirmed bars.
