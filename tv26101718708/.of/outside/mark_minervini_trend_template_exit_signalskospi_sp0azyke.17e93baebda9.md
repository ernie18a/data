Enter long when the instrument meets all of these trend conditions: its price is above its medium and long moving averages; the medium average is above the long average; the long average is rising compared with roughly one month earlier; the short average is above both longer averages; price is above the short average; price is sufficiently above its trailing 52-week low and not too far below its 52-week high; and its price relative to an external benchmark is above its own medium-term average. Signal entry only when this full setup becomes true after being false. Exit or sell when price crosses below the medium or long moving average, or crosses below the short moving average while the full setup was false on the prior bar. Also signal a sell warning when price first rises far above the medium moving average.
