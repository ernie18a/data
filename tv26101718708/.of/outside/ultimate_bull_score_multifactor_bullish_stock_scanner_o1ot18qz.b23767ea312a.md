On completed daily bars with sufficient history, calculate a bullish score from trend alignment and slopes; bullish momentum and oscillator readings; volume and accumulation; bullish price patterns and structure; relative performance versus a configurable benchmark; and available revenue, earnings, cash-flow, margin, and valuation data. Give partial neutral credit to missing business factors, but exclude them from business-data coverage. Subtract penalties for excessive extension or parabolic momentum, weakening momentum or volume, deteriorating revenue growth or cash flow, and expensive valuation under weak growth. A strong-bull signal requires the score to meet its configured threshold, business coverage to meet its minimum, risk deductions to stay within their maximum, price above the medium-term average, and valid volume data. A separate entry signal requires a configured entry score built from recent bullish moving-average and benchmark-strength crossovers, rising trend slope and momentum, accumulation, supportive volume, and limited extension; it also requires adequate business coverage, acceptable risk, price within the configured extension band, a bullish fast-over-medium average alignment, and valid volume. A pullback watch signal occurs when price is near the calculated support watch level and coverage, risk, and volume filters pass; this is a watch condition, not a standalone buy trigger. The indicator defines no short or exit signal.
