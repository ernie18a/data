On daily bars, use the last completed bar when configured. Require sufficient price history, available benchmark-relative returns, and valid average volume before issuing a long setup. Calculate relative strength as the instrument’s return minus a benchmark’s return over short and longer horizons, and compare the instrument-to-benchmark price ratio with its rising moving average.

Build a weighted bullish score from: moving-average position, alignment and slopes; benchmark-relative strength; bullish oscillator and price momentum conditions; relative volume, on-balance volume and accumulation/distribution behavior; bullish chart patterns; and price structure. Add a weighted business score based on quarterly revenue growth and acceleration, earnings positivity and improvement, net-margin improvement, free-cash-flow level and improvement, and valuation relative to revenue growth. Treat stale or unaligned financial data as missing; assign configurable neutral credit to missing factors, but exclude them from business-data coverage. Subtract scaled risk deductions for price extension, unusually rapid gains, momentum or volume divergence, decelerating revenue, worsening negative free cash flow, and high valuation combined with weak growth. Clamp the result to the score range.

Flag a strong bullish setup when the bullish score and business-data coverage meet their respective minimums, risk deductions do not exceed their limit, and price is above the intermediate exponential moving average.

Separately calculate a weighted entry score rewarding a recent bullish fast/intermediate exponential-average crossover, an accelerating intermediate-average slope, a recent reclaim of the long moving average, a recent crossover to positive short-term relative strength, favorable on-balance volume during a price pause, improving accumulation, accelerating positive momentum, and limited positive price extension. Flag a long entry setup when this score and business-data coverage meet their minimums, risk deductions are within their limit, extension is within its allowed positive range, and the fast average is above the intermediate average.

For a pullback long watch, require the bullish score to meet a lower eligibility threshold. Set a support watch price at the intermediate exponential average plus an ATR allowance, capped by the allowed extension above that average. If the score meets a higher threshold and the fast and intermediate averages are rising in a healthy trend above the long average, raise the watch price to the highest eligible nearby support among the fast average plus an ATR allowance, current broken resistance, and a recently broken resistance level; each candidate must satisfy the price, distance and extension restrictions. Flag a pullback setup when price is at or above the intermediate average and within an ATR tolerance above the watch price, business-data coverage is sufficient, risk deductions are within their limit, and volume is valid. A watch price above current price is a reclaim level, not an entry signal. No short or exit decision is specified.
