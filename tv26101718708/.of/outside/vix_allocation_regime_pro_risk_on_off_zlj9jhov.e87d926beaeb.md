Use the external VIX close and its moving average to classify the regime, with panic taking priority: if VIX exceeds the panic threshold, allocate mainly to cash with TLT and GLD; otherwise, if VIX exceeds the risk-off threshold and is above its moving average, allocate to TLT, GLD, and cash; otherwise, if VIX is below the risk-on threshold and below its moving average, allocate to SPY/QQQ, SOXX, and cash; otherwise use a neutral mix of SPY, GLD, TLT, and cash. Rebalance when the regime changes; otherwise hold.
