For each market, use weekly CFTC non-commercial positioning as external data. Fade crowded extremes: when the 52-week speculative net-position index is high and specs are net long, especially with commercials strongly opposite and several weeks of persistent long positioning, prepare to short after price confirms a reversal; when the index is low and specs are net short, especially with commercials strongly opposite and persistent short positioning, prepare to go long after price confirms a reversal. A first weekly change in net positioning against the crowded direction strengthens the fade. Ride a build when the speculative index and net-position changes point the same way and open interest is expanding: go long on a sustained net-long build or short on a sustained net-short build, after price confirms continuation. Treat rising open interest as confirmation; shrinking open interest with rising net positioning indicates covering rather than fresh conviction. Exit or reduce a trend position when positioning reaches the opposing crowded extreme (high for longs, low for shorts). Avoid trades when positioning is mid-range or directionally mixed. The dashboard itself supplies no price-entry trigger.
