On each confirmed bar, generate a long signal when an upward momentum trigger occurs and the selected trend filter is bullish; generate a short signal when a downward momentum trigger occurs and the filter is bearish. Momentum triggers are any upward or downward crossover among stochastic momentum, RSI across its directional threshold, MACD histogram across zero, or price across its fast moving average. The trend filter may use price relative to a longer moving average, Supertrend direction, a majority of sampled timeframes’ fast/slow moving-average trends, or no filter. Require the configured cooldown, sufficient history, and—if enabled—an allowed trading session. When flat, open at the signal bar’s close; place the stop beyond the recent swing range, with an ATR buffer, and choose up to three targets from the nearest structural, liquidity, volume, and higher-timeframe price levels in the trade direction, using risk-based fallback targets if needed. Optionally, an alternate setup engine can enter after a bullish or bearish order-block/inverted-gap zone is touched and a confirming candle closes in the setup direction, or after a buy-side/sell-side liquidity pool is swept and a confirming change of structure occurs; apply its enabled trend, location, volume, setup-class, and confirmation-window filters. After the first target, move the stop to entry. Exit a position on its stop, final target, opposing change of structure, maximum holding time, or—if enabled—opposing momentum crossover, opposing displacement, or two low-volume bars while profitable. Optionally close an active position immediately on an opposite momentum signal.
