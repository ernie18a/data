Use the instrument’s close divided by a benchmark index’s close as relative strength. Measure its proximity to its rolling high, percentage change over a configured period, and percentage deviation from its moving average. Measure short- and longer-term relative performance from changes in this ratio.

Define the base’s pivot and support as the highest high and lowest low over a configured lookback, excluding the current bar. A valid base requires short ATR to be below a configured fraction of longer ATR, the pivot-to-support range as a percentage of support to be below a configured maximum, and the close to remain within configured bounds around the pivot and support. A rising trend requires the close above a fast moving average, the fast average above a slow average, and the slow average rising over a configured period.

Signal a long early rotation when relative strength is near its rolling high, the base is valid, the close is below the pivot, and both relative-strength change and deviation from its average are positive.

Signal a long breakout when the close crosses above the buffered pivot, relative strength is near its rolling high, and volume exceeds its moving average by the configured multiplier. Classify it as confirmed when relative-strength change and deviation from its average are also positive and, if enabled, the rising-trend filter passes. The volume requirement applies to breakout signals regardless of the separate volume-check setting.

Signal the highest-conviction long setup when all of these hold: relative strength is near its rolling high; its change meets the configured minimum; its deviation from its average exceeds the configured minimum; short- and longer-term relative performance are positive and the short-term performance exceeds the longer-term performance scaled to the short horizon; the base is valid; ATR is contracting; base range and close’s percentage distance from the pivot are each within their configured bounds; volume meets its configured ratio if that check is enabled; and the rising trend passes if its filter is enabled. Also require the configured minimum composite score, calculated as a weighted sum of the passed checks other than volume. Trigger this setup on the first bar it becomes true.

For a highest-conviction long trade, use the base support as the stop. No short rule is specified.
