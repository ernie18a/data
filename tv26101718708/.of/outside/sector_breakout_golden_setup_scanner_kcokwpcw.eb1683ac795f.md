Use the traded instrument’s price, volume, and relative strength versus a benchmark. Mark an early-rotation watchlist signal when relative strength is at a recent high, rising above its moving average, and the instrument remains inside a valid contracting base below its pivot. Mark an emerging breakout when price crosses above the base pivot while relative strength is at a recent high and volume surges. Mark a confirmed breakout when that pivot break also has rising relative strength, positive relative strength versus its moving average, and the required long-term uptrend. Treat a golden setup as a high-conviction long signal when all strict checks pass: relative strength at a recent high, sufficiently strong positive slope and level, improving relative performance over short versus medium horizons, a valid contracting base within the allowed range, price near but not above the pivot, any required volume confirmation, the required long-term uptrend, and the minimum composite score. A near-complete checklist is a watchlist alert. The indicator defines no short or exit signal.
