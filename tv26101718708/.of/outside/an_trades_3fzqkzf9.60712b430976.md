At each confirmed bar, generate a long signal when price touches an active bullish inverted fair-value-gap zone from a lower timeframe or an active bullish fair-value-gap zone from a higher timeframe, and at least one of these is true: a recent swing-low sweep reclaimed by the close, a touch of a higher-timeframe bullish gap, or a new session low. Generate a short signal symmetrically: touch an active bearish inverted gap or higher-timeframe bearish gap, plus a recent reclaimed swing-high sweep, a touch of a higher-timeframe bearish gap, or a new session high. Track gaps from three-bar displacement patterns across multiple timeframes; a gap becomes an inverted gap when price trades into it and closes through its far boundary, and discard it if price instead closes through its other boundary. Require a change-of-delivery confirmation when enabled: for a long, close above the open of the latest bearish delivery candle; for a short, close below the open of the latest bullish delivery candle. A delivery candle has a sufficiently large body relative to its range. When enabled, also require bullish or bearish divergence respectively against a correlated instrument, based on one instrument making a fresh lookback extreme while the other does not. Block longs when the nearest opposing liquidity level is closer than the favorable one, and block shorts under the converse condition, when that filter is enabled. Block a long for an unusually large lower wick and a short for an unusually large upper wick, relative to recent average true range, when the wick filter is enabled. After a sweep, block that direction if price later closes back beyond the swept swing level, when this invalidation filter is enabled. Signals occur on the confirming bar close. There is no exit decision.
