Compute an adaptive RSI-like oscillator from price changes shaped by the rolling high-low range: Wilder-smooth the signed changes and their absolute values, divide the former by the latter, rescale around the midpoint, and smooth it with an exponential average as a signal line. Enter long when the oscillator crosses above its signal, is below an upper bound, price is above a long-term EMA of prior closes on a higher timeframe, volatility is above its own average, the candle's total range is not excessive relative to its body, cooldown has ended, and the position is flat or short. Enter short when the oscillator crosses below its signal, is above a lower bound, price is below a long-term EMA of prior closes on a distinct higher timeframe, and the same volatility, candle, and cooldown filters pass while the position is flat or long. After enough consecutive losing exits, pause new entries for a cooldown period; reset the loss count when an exit is not a loss. Set an initial stop at an ATR-based distance from entry. Move the stop to just beyond entry after favorable movement reaches an ATR-based trigger, and trail it by an ATR-based distance; for longs use the tighter, higher stop, and for shorts the tighter, lower stop. Exit when price reaches the active stop. For a long position only, close half when price reaches an ATR-based profit target; do this once per position.
