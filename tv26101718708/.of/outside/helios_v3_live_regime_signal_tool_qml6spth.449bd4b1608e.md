On each bar, sum six directional votes: MACD line and its signal both above zero vote positive, both below zero vote negative, otherwise neutral; compare the moving averages of close and open; compare close with its past value; compare close with its long moving average, voting negative when not above; compare on-balance volume with its moving average; and compare the traded instrument's close divided by a benchmark's close with that ratio's moving average, voting negative when not above. The comparisons other than the binary ones vote positive when above, negative when below, and neutral when equal. Disable the benchmark vote when configured off or when the instrument's base currency is Bitcoin. Start flat. Enter long when the total score stays at or above the entry threshold for the required consecutive bars, annualized realized volatility of log returns is at or below its cap, and the close's standardized distance above its long moving average is at or below the entry extension limit. Exit when long and the score stays at or below the exit threshold for the required consecutive bars, volatility is at or below its cap, and the standardized distance is at or above the exit extension limit. Volatility may optionally be exponentially smoothed; the cap and several lookbacks depend on the selected volatility profile. Keep checking qualified streaks after a gate blocks a signal, so a signal can fire later if the gates open while the streak continues. On entry, use full size only when the score meets a higher conviction threshold and the standardized distance is strictly below a separate sizing limit; otherwise use half size.
