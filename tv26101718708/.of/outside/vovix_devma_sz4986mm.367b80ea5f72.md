On each confirmed bar, when flat and trading is permitted by the configured session and weekend rules, compare a fast and slow moving average of the rolling standard deviation of normalized volatility thrust. Volatility thrust is the difference between short- and long-period ATR divided by the rolling standard deviation of the short-period ATR. Enter long when the fast average crosses above the slow average; enter short when it crosses below. Manage each position with an optional stop at entry plus or minus an ATR-based distance, an ATR-based profit target, and an optional ATR-based trailing stop. Close any position that reaches its maximum holding duration; that duration may be adjusted using recent win rate and winning-trade durations.
