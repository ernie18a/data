Long-only. On a confirmed bar, open a long when the selected signal mode authorizes it, the entry cooldown has elapsed, and—if trade-state blocking is enabled—no trade is already open. The legacy mode requires the trend and execution scores to pass, at least ten of twelve core filters to pass, and confirmation; its strongest grade requires all twelve. The filtered mode also requires the broader confluence grade to qualify; the independent mode uses that broader grade alone. Broader confluence is the share of enabled filters passed, subject to configured score, mandatory-filter, and module-confirmation requirements. Filters assess trend and moving-average alignment, price structure and higher-timeframe support, momentum, volume and money flow, candle strength, relative strength versus an external benchmark, volatility and extension, traps and distribution, and optional structure, order-block, Fibonacci, and divergence evidence. A confirmed bottom-reversal signal may also open a long if reversal entries are enabled and its configured prerequisites and score pass. No short-entry decision is defined. While long, raise the stop as configured using breakeven, moving-average, ATR, or swing-based trailing rules. Exit when the close falls below the active stop, when confirmed bearish structure invalidates the trade (weak close and momentum, weak RSI with price below the trend average, or a bearish swing-structure break), or when warning-based exits are enabled and a warning occurs. Warnings include traps, distribution, overextension, bearish divergence or structure risk, top reversal, trend break, bearish momentum flip, or price falling below the trend average.
