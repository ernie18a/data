Evaluate each bar using the traded instrument’s price and volume, a configurable benchmark, and optionally an external series for divergence. Compute relative strength as instrument close divided by benchmark close. Leadership is bullish when this ratio is above its EMA, that EMA is rising, and the instrument’s RSI exceeds its bullish floor; also detect a ratio breakout above its previous rolling high.

Build a weighted trend-and-structure score from EMA alignment and slope, range support and breakout, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, bullish market structure, order-block and Fibonacci location, momentum, relative leadership, and absence of risks. Build a separate weighted execution score from price location and reclaims, candle strength, stochastic momentum, volume and money flow, session, volatility phase, and absence of traps or bearish divergence. Compare each score with its configurable threshold and apply its mandatory trend, candle, session, safety, and confirmation conditions.

Count the passing enabled confirmation layers: the two scores; EMA trend; RSI and MACD strength and acceleration; volume participation and money flow; benchmark leadership; higher-timeframe support; candle strength; absence of traps and distribution; safe bullish volatility phase; bullish structure, order-block and Fibonacci location; and divergence. Also count confirmations from the optional structure, order-block, Fibonacci, divergence, micro-EMA, and Bollinger modules. Apply the configured minimum module confirmations and any mandatory score, safety, higher-timeframe, or location filters. The classic long condition requires both scores to pass and enough of the first twelve layers to pass. The independent long condition requires a sufficient fraction of enabled layers and the mandatory filters. Select classic, independent, or their conjunction according to the configured authority mode. Optionally allow a separately scored, confirmed bottom reversal to trigger a long. Only enter on an allowed confirmed bar after the cooldown, and optionally block repeat entries while already in a trade.

On entry, record the close, set the initial stop to the lower of an ATR-based stop and a buffered recent swing low, and derive profit targets from the initial risk. While in the trade, raise the stop to breakeven or a buffered EMA after their respective gain thresholds, and optionally ratchet it upward using the configured ATR, EMA, swing-low, or hybrid trail after its activation condition. Exit on a confirmed bar when the close falls below the active stop, when bearish price and momentum invalidate the structure, or when an optional warning exit is enabled and a warning event occurs. Warnings include traps, distribution, overextension, bearish divergence or structure, adverse location, a confirmed top reversal, a trendline breakdown, a bearish micro-EMA flip, fading momentum, or a close below the main EMA. Divergence may use the optional external series alongside instrument-derived indicators. Reset the position state on exit and restart the entry cooldown.
