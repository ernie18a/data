Use the traded instrument’s bar data and same-timeframe NYSE advancing, declining and unchanged issues. Form a breadth series by cumulatively summing the signed square root of (advances minus declines) divided by (unchanged issues plus one). Use a linear regression of the selected price source as the price baseline.

Build a composite from SAR, Supertrend, RSI, MFI, balance of power, Chande momentum, center of gravity, ADX, stochastic, symmetrically weighted moving average, true strength index, Williams %R, accumulation/distribution, intraday intensity, negative and positive volume indexes, on-balance volume, price-volume trend, true range, VWAP, Williams accumulation/distribution, short-minus-long SMA, short EMA-minus-long SMA, CCI, Chop Zone angle, ease of movement, detrended price oscillator, the breadth series, bull plus bear power, and smoothed high, low, open and close. Apply the selected smoothing to each component where configured. For each component multiply its rolling standard deviation by its rolling correlation with the price baseline; also multiply by the rolling standard deviation of its deviation from its short SMA, except for SAR and Supertrend. Sum these contributions.

For any series, define its correlation transform as the series times its rolling correlation with the price baseline, and its deviation transform as its rolling standard deviation times that correlation. Apply both transforms to the composite, then apply the deviation transform to each result. Also calculate the configured multi-bar change and its percentage rate of change for the composite and its correlation transform; for the deviation transform, calculate the one-bar change of its rolling standard deviation and the one-bar change of that result.

A falling-price signal requires the price baseline to be below all values in its configured falling lookback. Define six triggers, each additionally requiring falling price: the deviation transform of the composite’s correlation transform crosses below zero; the deviation transform of the composite’s deviation transform crosses zero; all three rate-of-change series cross zero on the same bar; all three change series cross zero on the same bar; the composite’s correlation transform, deviation transform and change each cross zero on the same bar; or all three change series cross each other pairwise on the same bar. Crosses are in either direction unless specified otherwise.

Within the optional trading window, open a long from flat on any of the first three triggers when the selected entry-price source is below the last stored prior rolling low. Update that stored low when the rolling low rises, except on a bar where the rolling high falls; separately store the prior rolling high when it falls. Add to a long on any of the first four triggers while the selected price average is below the position average by the configured margin and the open-trade count is at or below the configured fraction of its maximum. Above that fraction and up to the maximum, add only on the sixth trigger and when the selected price average is below the position average.

Generate a full-exit signal when a separate Supertrend switches from up to down, or remains down while any of the six triggers occurs. Close the long when that signal occurs, the selected price average exceeds the adjusted profit line, and the stored prior high and stored low are both nonzero and available. Initialize the profit line from average entry price plus the configured profit percentage, adjust it by the change in that target on additions, and retain it on partial reductions. If swing selling is enabled and the open-trade count is unchanged, also request a partial close when the close SMA exceeds the tracked minimum entry price by the swing profit percentage, both stored extrema are available and nonzero, and the position average exceeds the selected price average. Use the historical last-purchase quantity indexed by the maintained entry counter. Clear the tracked entry-price array when that SMA threshold is met. The strategy does not open short positions.
