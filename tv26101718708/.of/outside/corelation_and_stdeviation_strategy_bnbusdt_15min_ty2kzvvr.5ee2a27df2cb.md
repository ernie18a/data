Compute a composite signal by summing, across the strategy’s price, trend, momentum, and volume indicators, each indicator’s rolling standard deviation multiplied by its rolling correlation with a smoothed price series and its deviation from a Bollinger-band basis. Derive correlated and volatility-weighted versions of this composite, then their rolling standard deviations, changes, and rates of change. Define a falling-price state from the smoothed price series. A buy setup occurs during that state when any of these holds: the standard deviation of the volatility-weighted correlated composite crosses below zero; the standard deviation of the second volatility-weighted composite crosses zero; all three composite rate-of-change measures cross zero; all three composite change measures cross zero; the first correlated composite, its volatility measure, and its change measure all cross zero; or the three composite change measures cross one another in the specified pairwise directions. Open a long when flat on the first three setups, only if the configured lower-price source is below the tracked swing low and trading is within the enabled date window. Add to a long on any of the first four setups when smoothed price is sufficiently below the average entry price and trade count is within the early portion of its cap; on the sixth setup when smoothed price is below average entry price and trade count is in the remaining permitted portion. Close the long when either a trend-direction reversal or any buy setup occurs, and the profit target is below smoothed price, provided tracked swing-high and swing-low references are available. Optionally take a partial exit when the short moving average exceeds the lowest tracked entry price by the configured swing-profit margin, intermediate selling is enabled and eligible, average entry price is above smoothed price, and both swing references are available. The strategy takes no short positions.
