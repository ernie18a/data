Build an index for each major currency by combining its related FX crosses, reversing a cross’s return when the currency is quoted. For each currency, calculate a composite force from its lookback strength relative to the other currencies, breadth of confirming cross returns, directional efficiency, current impulse, persistence, and acceleration; normalize and combine these factors using configurable weights. Rank currencies by force. For the chart instrument, go long when base-currency force exceeds quote-currency force by the configured minimum gap; go short when it trails by that gap; otherwise show neutral. Also identify the strongest-versus-weakest pair when their force gap meets its configured minimum, buying the cross if the strongest currency is its base and selling it if the strongest is its quote. No exit condition is defined.
