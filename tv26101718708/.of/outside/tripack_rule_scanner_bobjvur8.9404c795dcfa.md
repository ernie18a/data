For each scanned instrument, calculate RSI, StochRSI, Bollinger %B, rate of change, percentage price oscillator, percentage distance from a simple moving average, and Chande momentum oscillator from close, along with open, high, low, and close, on the selected scan timeframe. Evaluate each enabled rule by comparing a selected price or indicator field with either a number or another selected field. Supported conditions are greater than, less than, greater than or equal, less than or equal, equal, not equal, crossing above, or crossing below. A BUY signal fires when at least one enabled BUY rule exists and its rules satisfy the configured AND or OR combination; a SELL signal follows the same logic independently for enabled SELL rules. With no enabled rules on a side, that side does not signal. Both signals may occur together. A signal becoming true when it was false on the prior scan bar is marked new.
