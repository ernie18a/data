On each bar, maintain an adaptive Supertrend direction using the selected source, ATR, volatility rank, trend efficiency and, when enabled, the return-path volatility adjustment. Optionally adapt the ATR length to the dominant cycle and tune the multiplier using past reversal rates. A raw reversal occurs when the close crosses the prior opposing trailing band. Confirm it only if the breach meets the ATR-scaled threshold, enough bars have passed since the last raw reversal, and the bar is closed when close confirmation is enabled. The direction changes on the raw reversal even if confirmation fails.

For each confirmed reversal, calculate conviction from regime alignment, agreement of confirmed higher-timeframe Supertrends, distance from the active band, trend efficiency, volume-delta direction and optional cross-asset momentum. Calculate flip strength separately from breach depth, trend strength, trend efficiency, higher-timeframe agreement, regime alignment, volume thrust, volume-delta direction and optional secondary Supertrend agreement. If enabled, learn the minimum strength from past confirmed flips grouped by strength and judged by whether their subsequent favorable excursion reached an ATR-scaled target; otherwise use the configured threshold.

Trigger long on a confirmed upward reversal or short on a confirmed downward reversal only when conviction and strength meet their thresholds and no veto applies. Veto a fresh reversal when the optional external volatility index spikes, all enabled higher timeframes oppose it when that gate is enabled, the regime is range or volatile when the chop gate is enabled, or nonzero signed momentum of the optional cross-asset feed opposes it. Missing external feeds do not veto. There is no explicit exit signal.

The displayed verdict follows different rules: on a confirmed reversal show veto if any veto applies, otherwise weak if conviction is below threshold, otherwise long or short according to the reversal. It does not apply the strength threshold. Between confirmed reversals, display the current raw Supertrend direction as a hold bias.
