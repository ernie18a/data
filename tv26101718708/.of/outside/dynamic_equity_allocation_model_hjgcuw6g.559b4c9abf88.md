On each chart bar, calculate a target long allocation to an S&P 500 ETF, with the remainder in cash. Use the ETF's prices, volume and fundamentals together with volatility indexes, Treasury yields, and bond ETF prices; use configured fallback values for unavailable fundamentals. All lookbacks, thresholds, weights, score tiers, caps and adjustment factors are parameters.

Score the market regime by combining: a weighted trend score from whether ETF price is above short, medium and long moving averages and whether the medium average is above the long average; a decreasing score for higher implied volatility; and a decreasing score for larger drawdown from the rolling price high. Map the combined score through ordered regime bands to corresponding long allocations, from full exposure in the strongest regime to cash in the weakest.

For risk allocation, use the greater of annualized standard deviation of ETF log returns and the annualized current-bar high-low range volatility estimate. With risk scaling enabled, take the smaller of target volatility divided by market volatility and maximum portfolio drawdown divided by market drawdown, bounding each allocation; apply a bounded feedback factor based on the greater of volatility and drawdown utilization estimated at a fixed reference equity allocation. Reduce the factor exponentially when utilization is high and increase it logarithmically when utilization is low. With scaling disabled, instead use a bounded target-to-market-volatility ratio multiplied by a bounded penalty for drawdown exceeding its target. Bound the result to the permitted allocation range.

For valuation, calculate a weighted equity risk premium from earnings yield less the long Treasury yield; earnings yield plus buyback yield less that yield; dividend yield plus sustainable growth less that yield; and dividend plus buyback yield plus a scaled revenue-growth estimate less that yield. Use operating earnings yield when positive, otherwise derive it from price and earnings; use forward valuation if earnings are nonpositive. Derive sustainable growth from return on equity and the dividend-to-earnings payout ratio, with a fallback payout ratio. Map the premium through increasing score tiers, adjust for revenue growth, net margin and return on equity, multiply by a debt-to-equity quality factor, and bound the score.

For sentiment, add to a neutral base an adjustment from ordered bands of the volatility index's rolling z-score, a term-structure adjustment comparing it with the shorter-dated volatility index, and an adjustment from the rolling volatility of its rate of change. Check the ordinary term-structure threshold before the steeper threshold, so the ordinary adjustment also applies when the steeper threshold is exceeded. Bound the score.

For macro conditions, sum tiered scores for the long-minus-short Treasury yield spreads, credit conditions and corporate financial stability. Construct the credit proxy from the high-yield bond ETF's long-lookback price-implied yield less the long Treasury yield, the difference between high-yield and investment-grade bond ETF return volatilities, and their positions within their respective rolling price ranges; apply a floor. Credit scoring also considers whether Treasury bonds outperform the average of the two corporate bond ETFs and whether high-yield bonds outperform investment-grade bonds. Score financial stability using debt-to-equity and return on equity. Bound the total.

Combine the regime allocation, risk allocation, valuation, sentiment and macro scores using normalized configured weights. If crisis detection is enabled, count stress points for high and extreme implied volatility, large and extreme ETF drawdown, excessive credit spread, simultaneous sharp ETF and Treasury bond declines, and unusually high ETF volume accompanied by a sharp daily decline. Cap the combined allocation at the severe-crisis cap when the higher point threshold is reached, otherwise at the crisis cap when the lower threshold is reached.

After the crisis cap, apply the selected model adjustment: subtract for conservative, add for aggressive, make no adjustment for balanced, or add or subtract in adaptive mode when the ETF's recent return exceeds the corresponding positive or negative momentum threshold. Bound the result and smooth it with a simple moving average. Rebalance long equity exposure to the smoothed target; the framework does not initiate a short position.
