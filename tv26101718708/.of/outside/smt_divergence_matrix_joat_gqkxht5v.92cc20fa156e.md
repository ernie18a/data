On each bar, confirm swing highs and lows on the traded instrument using a configurable number of bars on each side. For each confirmed swing, record the enabled reference instruments' highs or lows at the traded instrument's swing bar; reference swings need not be pivots. Compare each swing with the preceding traded-instrument swing of the same type and the reference values recorded at those two swings. Update these records on every confirmed swing, even if no signal results.

A long candidate occurs when the traded instrument makes a strictly lower swing low and at least one enabled, valid reference has a strictly higher low at the corresponding swing bars. A short candidate occurs when the traded instrument makes a strictly higher swing high and at least one enabled, valid reference has a strictly lower high at the corresponding swing bars. If the correlation filter is enabled, the triggering reference's rolling close-to-close correlation with the traded instrument, measured on the confirmation bar, must be available and at least the configured threshold.

On the confirmation bar, apply the selected confirmation mode: none; RSI rising for long or falling for short versus the previous bar; close above open for long or below open for short; or both RSI and candle conditions. Apply a separate configurable cooldown to each direction, measured from its last emitted signal. If both directions pass their filters and cooldowns on the same bar, emit neither; otherwise emit the qualifying long or short signal. Stop and target zones are visual and do not generate exit decisions.
