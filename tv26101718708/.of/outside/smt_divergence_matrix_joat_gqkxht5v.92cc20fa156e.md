On each confirmed swing low, compare the chart instrument’s low with its previous confirmed swing low and each enabled reference instrument’s low at those same pivot bars. A bullish signal requires the chart to make a lower low while at least one reference makes a higher low. On each confirmed swing high, a bearish signal requires the chart to make a higher high while at least one reference makes a lower high. Accept a reference’s divergence only when its rolling correlation with the chart meets the configured minimum, if correlation filtering is enabled. Apply the selected confirmation rule: none; RSI rising for bullish or falling for bearish; a bullish or bearish candle close; or both RSI and candle conditions. Enforce the minimum gap between signals of the same direction. If bullish and bearish signals both qualify on the same bar, emit neither; otherwise emit the qualifying direction. There is no separate exit signal. For a signal, the displayed trade plan uses the signal bar’s close as entry, an ATR-based stop on the adverse side, and a target at the configured risk/reward multiple.
