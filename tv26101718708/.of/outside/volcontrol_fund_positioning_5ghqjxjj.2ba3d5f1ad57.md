Estimate realized volatility from recent squared returns using fast and slow exponentially weighted estimates, short and long rolling windows, and a diversified equity–Treasury portfolio estimate. Convert each available estimate into equity exposure inversely proportional to volatility, capped at a leverage limit; blend the selected designs and apply the implementation delay. Increase the equity position when scheduled exposure rises, reduce it when exposure falls, and otherwise hold. This is a volatility-control positioning rule, not a directional market signal.
