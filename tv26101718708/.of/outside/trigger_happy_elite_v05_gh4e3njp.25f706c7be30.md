On each bar close, enter only when flat and all applicable gates pass. Daily realized net profit since the New York calendar day changed must be strictly below the profit cap and strictly above the negative loss cap. If enabled, the current bar must be within the configured New York session, ATR must exceed its minimum, and the daily CBOE VIX close must lie within its inclusive range.

Enter long when a pivot low is confirmed and, if enabled, RSI is below its long threshold and the instrument's close is above its EMA on the selected timeframe. Enter short when a pivot high is confirmed and, if enabled, RSI is above its short threshold and the close is below that EMA. Pivot confirmation uses bars on both sides of the pivot; evaluate the signal on the confirmation bar. Evaluate both entry conditions independently.

For each position, place a fixed take-profit and stop-loss at configured tick distances from entry, plus a trailing stop that activates after a configured favorable move and follows the best price by a specified tick offset. The first exit order reached closes the position. Session and daily limits block new entries but do not themselves close positions.
