For the charted coin, obtain daily realized capitalization, circulating supply, market capitalization, and price. In automatic mode, select the first fresh realized-cap source in this order: custom realized cap, CoinMetrics realized cap, custom MVRV, IntoTheBlock MVRV. Derive realized cap from an MVRV source as market capitalization divided by MVRV. If none is fresh, use whichever of CoinMetrics realized cap and IntoTheBlock MVRV has the more recent timestamp. Allow a specified source to override automatic selection; for the custom mode, prefer an available custom realized cap over custom MVRV. Select the first fresh supply source in this order: custom, Glassnode, IntoTheBlock; otherwise use market capitalization divided by daily price. Treat a source as fresh when its value exists and its age relative to the chart bar does not exceed the configured limit. Divide realized cap by supply, carry forward the last available result, and smooth it using the configured moving-average type and length. Form configured lower and upper bands as multiples of the smoothed value. Signal a buy when any lower band crosses below the chart close: the band was at or above the close on the previous bar and is below it now. Signal a sell when any upper band crosses above the chart close: the band was at or below the close on the previous bar and is above it now. Evaluate signals even when the selected data is stale; display settings do not gate alerts.
