Pair user-supplied SPX and NDX levels by position, and track each valid positive SPX level independently. Reset all states and failure counts when the day of month changes. A missing, invalid, or nonpositive NDX level imposes no NDX level condition. On each bar, process each level's current state once:

- Watching: Set a pending call when SPX closes above its level and NDX closes above its paired level, subject to the optional volume and moving-average filters. Set a pending put when both close below their levels, subject to the same filters. A crossing is not required. For the moving-average filter, calls require each index to close above its fast simple moving average and its fast average to be at least its slow average; puts require each close below its fast average and its fast average to be at most its slow average. The optional volume filter requires chart volume to be at least a configured multiple of its simple moving average.
- Pending call: On a later bar, if both indices remain above their respective levels and the volume filter passes, buy calls unless the level's failure count has reached the configured limit; in that case return to watching without an entry. If SPX closes below its level or a valid NDX pair closes below its level, increment the failure count and return to watching. Otherwise remain pending. The moving-average filter is not retested.
- Pending put: Apply the symmetric rule below the levels to buy puts; count a failure if SPX or a valid NDX pair closes above its level. Otherwise remain pending.
- After a call entry, return to watching only when SPX closes below its level. After a put entry, return to watching only when SPX closes above its level. These state resets are not option exit signals. Equality with a level triggers neither side.
