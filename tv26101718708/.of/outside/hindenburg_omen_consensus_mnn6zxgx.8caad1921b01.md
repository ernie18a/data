For each enabled market universe, use daily new-high and new-low counts and its benchmark index. Trigger when both counts, expressed as percentages of that universe's denominator, meet or exceed its breadth threshold and the benchmark is at least its value at the specified lookback. Use advancing plus declining NYSE issues as the NYSE denominator and fixed issue counts for the other universes. Keep each universe's window open from its latest trigger through the specified number of subsequent bars, inclusive; a new trigger resets it. Compute normalized NYSE advances minus declines divided by advances plus declines, using zero when the denominator is zero. Subtract its slower exponential moving average from its faster one. When at least the required number of universe windows overlap and this difference is negative, consider going short or exiting a long position. Otherwise, no trading action is specified.
