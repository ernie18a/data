For each enabled market universe, open a temporary signal window when both new 52-week highs and new 52-week lows exceed their configured shares of that universe, and its benchmark is at or above its value from a configured lookback period earlier. Count the universes with open windows. When that count reaches the configured consensus minimum and the McClellan Oscillator calculated from NYSE advances and declines is negative, treat the market as bearish: exit long positions or take a short position. If consensus is present but the oscillator is not negative, do not activate the bearish signal. No long-entry condition is defined.
