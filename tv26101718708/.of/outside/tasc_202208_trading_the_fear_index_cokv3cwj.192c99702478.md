Using external volatility-index and broad-market ETF data, compare smoothed fast and slow stochastic readings, measure the volatility index’s recent rise or fall and its correlation trend, and check its level. Enter long when volatility has surged, its trend is strong and rising, and both volatility stochastics exceed the corresponding market stochastics, with the fast volatility stochastic rising. Enter short when volatility has fallen sharply and for several days, spends enough time above a low-level threshold, and the rising market fast stochastic exceeds the volatility fast stochastic. Close or reverse positions when the corresponding opposing entry signal occurs; in long-only mode, close a long when the market fast stochastic crosses above the volatility fast stochastic or falls from its prior reading. Reverse the long and short signal assignments for the inverse volatility ETF. Trades are limited to the enabled observation window and allowed direction.
