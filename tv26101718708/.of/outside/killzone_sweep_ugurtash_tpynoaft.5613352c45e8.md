On closed bars, track previous-day and completed-session highs and lows as liquidity levels. A breach followed by a close back inside within the reclaim window creates a reversal setup: long after a low is swept, short after a high is swept. Measure the raid's extension from the chosen reference open and penetration beyond the level in ATR units, and require the selected depth buckets, direction, weekday, session and raid-count gates to pass. Optionally require the traded instrument to make a lookback low for a long or high for a short while a correlated instrument does not make the corresponding lookback extreme; if the peer is unavailable, this filter passes. Other optional gates require alignment with the selected price bias, position within the selected price range and session progress. An optional adaptive gate requires both depth buckets for that direction to have sufficient prior outcomes and positive net-return statistical strength. Optionally block new signals while a signalled setup is armed or open.

Enter at the reclaim close, or wait for a later close beyond the reclaim bar's opposite extreme. Alternatively, after that displacement, wait for a retracement to a configured depth in the newest eligible, unfilled directional fair value gap; optionally enter at the displacement close if no gap exists. Cancel an unconfirmed setup when its raid extreme is breached or its waiting window expires, unless the trigger or fill occurs on that same bar. Place the stop beyond the raid extreme by an ATR allowance and reject invalid or insufficient stop widths. Set the target at a risk multiple or the nearest qualifying unbreached opposite liquidity level, falling back to a risk-multiple target. Exit at the stop, target or resolution horizon; optionally take a partial at the first target, move the remainder's stop to entry and aim for a farther target. Optional breakeven and trailing rules tighten the stop after specified profit thresholds. Evaluate the existing stop before moving it, and use the configured priority when a bar touches both stop and target.
