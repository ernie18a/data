On each confirmed candle, compute an adaptive range as a smoothed average of absolute close-to-close changes, then move a filter line only when price exceeds it by more than one range; otherwise keep it flat. Signal long when the close is above the filter while the filter is rising, if the prior signal was short; signal short when the close is below the filter while the filter is falling, if the prior signal was long. In always-in mode, reverse at each signal, filling at the next open. In stop-and-target mode, enter only when flat; set the stop at the filter’s far range band on the signal candle and the target at a configurable reward-to-risk multiple of the distance from signal close to stop. In filtered mode, apply the same entry and exits only if the signal also passes one selected test: long above and short below a long-term exponential average; directional strength above a threshold; or signal-close distance from the filter at least a chosen number of ranges. Close stop-and-target positions when either level is reached, accounting for gaps and the assumed intrabar price path.
