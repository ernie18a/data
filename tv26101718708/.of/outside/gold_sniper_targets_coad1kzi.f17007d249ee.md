Trigger a long when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, or a short when it crosses below; optionally require the bar to close. Require the directional confluence score to meet its minimum. Award one point each when: price is on the directional side of session VWAP; RSI is on the directional side of its midpoint; MACD is on the directional side of its signal; the fast EMA is on the directional side of the slow EMA; ADX exceeds its threshold and price is on the directional side of the fast EMA; volume exceeds its moving average and the candle closes in the trade direction; and confirmation-timeframe RSI is on the directional side of its midpoint. If the dollar-index factor is enabled, award another point only when the rolling correlation between instrument and dollar-index closes is at or below the negative threshold and the dollar index is below its EMA for a long or above it for a short. If disabled, proportionally rescale and round the minimum score to the remaining factor count. Use strictly above or below comparisons for directional votes; equality earns no point.

Also require: the symbol passes the optional gold recognition guard using its base currency, ticker, futures root or custom keyword; an enabled session filter permits the bar within the configured London or New York window on intraday charts; an enabled range filter permits the bar when the current daily high-low range is below the configured fraction of the average completed daily range, applying only on intraday charts; and the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since the last trade opened. Multiple trades may remain open independently.

For each signal, enter at the signal bar's close, place a stop one ATR-based risk distance against the direction, and place successive targets at integer multiples of that distance in the trade direction. Manage each trade starting on its entry bar. Check the existing stop against the bar's low for a long or high for a short before checking targets; a stop touch closes the trade even if a target is also touched. Otherwise mark targets reached in order using the opposite bar extreme. Optionally move the stop to entry when the first target is reached; this new stop takes effect on the next bar. Exit when the final target is reached. Opposite signals do not themselves close existing trades.
