On a confirmed bullish cross of a fast exponential moving average above a slower one, consider a long; on a bearish cross, consider a short. Enter only if the direction’s confluence score meets the configured minimum and all enabled gates pass: the instrument is recognized as gold when gold-only mode is active; the time is within an allowed trading session when that filter applies; the day’s range has not reached its allowed share of average daily range when that filter applies; and enough bars have passed since the prior trade. Score directional agreement from price relative to session VWAP, RSI relative to its midpoint, MACD relative to its signal, fast versus slow average alignment, sufficiently strong trend with price on the matching side of the fast average, above-average volume with a candle closing in the matching direction, confirmation-timeframe RSI relative to its midpoint, and—when enabled—a sufficiently negative gold–dollar-index correlation together with the dollar index moving opposite the trade direction. Open at the signal close. Set an initial stop at an ATR-scaled distance and profit targets at successive multiples of that risk distance. If price reaches the first target and breakeven protection is enabled, move the stop to entry; close at the active stop if reached, or close the trade when the final target is reached.
