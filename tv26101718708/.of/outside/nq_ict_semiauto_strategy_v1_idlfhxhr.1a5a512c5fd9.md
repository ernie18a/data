Trade only during the configured New York session(s), on permitted days, while flat and below the daily trade limit. A manual directional stance permits longs, shorts, both, or neither. Define the prior-day range and its midpoint; allow shorts only above the midpoint’s configurable exclusion band and longs only below it. A short setup requires a sweep above the higher of the prior-day high and latest confirmed swing high, a strong bearish displacement bar, a close below the latest confirmed swing low, and a sufficiently large bearish three-bar gap; these events must each be recent. A long setup requires the inverse: sweep below the lower of the prior-day low and latest confirmed swing low, strong bullish displacement, close above the latest confirmed swing high, and a sufficiently large bullish gap. Confirm shorts when the S&P 500 is weak relative to its moving average, shows bearish divergence (the traded instrument makes a lookback high while the S&P 500 does not), or meets the selected looser confirmation rule; confirm longs using the inverse conditions. Record a qualifying gap and a stop beyond the setup’s sweep extreme. Within a limited validity period, enter short or long when price returns into that gap, the corresponding S&P confirmation still holds, and the stop distance is positive and within its configured maximum. Exit at the initial stop or a fixed reward multiple of initial risk; after price reaches a configured favorable risk multiple, move the stop to entry. Otherwise, take no trade.
