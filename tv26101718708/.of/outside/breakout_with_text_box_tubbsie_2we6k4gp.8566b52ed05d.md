Calculate configurable fast, intermediate, slow and long-term EMAs of the underlying close. During the configured regular session, signal buying puts when the fast EMA is below the intermediate EMA, which is below the long-term EMA, the candle closes above its open, and the close is within a configurable relative distance of the fast EMA. Signal buying calls for the inverse EMA ordering and a candle closing below its open, using the same distance test. Two independently enabled signal grades use separate distance thresholds. An independently enabled continuation signal indicates puts when the intermediate EMA is below the slow EMA, the slow EMA is below the long-term EMA, the candle closes above its open, and the close is within a configurable relative distance of the slow EMA; indicate calls for the inverse ordering and a candle closing below its open. These signals require the regular session. Separately, advise no trades when the intermediate EMA is strictly between the slow and long-term EMAs; otherwise advise calls above the premarket high if the intermediate EMA is above the slow EMA, and puts below the premarket low otherwise. Flag the regular-session close inside the inclusive premarket range as a no-trading chop zone. Warn when the intermediate or slow EMA is within its configured relative-distance threshold of the long-term EMA, prior-day high or low, or premarket high or low. The bias and warnings are displayed separately and do not filter the plotted signals. No option sale or exit rule is specified.
