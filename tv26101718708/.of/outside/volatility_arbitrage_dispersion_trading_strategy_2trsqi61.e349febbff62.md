Build a weighted basket from enabled external components, excluding the traded asset, and estimate its implied correlation from basket and component return variances. Compute the traded asset’s return residual after hedging against the basket with a rolling beta; fit an autoregressive mean-reversion model to cumulative residuals and derive its equilibrium, score, and half-life. Permit entries only when the model is valid, mean reversion is within configured half-life bounds, and the regime passes: implied correlation is high enough, its z-score is not spiking, short-term realized volatility is not expanding excessively relative to long-term volatility, and, if enabled, implied volatility exceeds realized volatility by the required premium. Enter long when the score crosses back above the negative entry threshold, or is below it if reversal hooks are disabled; enter short when it crosses back below the positive threshold, or is above it if hooks are disabled. Reject entries beyond the corresponding statistical stop boundary. An optional reverse mode swaps the resulting long and short orders. Enter only when flat and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed. For each position, place an ATR-based stop and, if selected, a take-profit at fair value when it lies in the favorable direction; otherwise use the configured risk-reward target. Also close when the score returns to the configured mean-reversion exit boundary, crosses its statistical stop boundary, or reaches a time limit based on the estimated half-life subject to a cap.
