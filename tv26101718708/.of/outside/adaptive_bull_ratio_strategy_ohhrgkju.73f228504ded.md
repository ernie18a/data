Use a chart-timeframe bullish reversal signal: either Supertrend changes from down to up, or an enabled external line was above the previous close and is below the current close. Record the signal close and bar. A later entry is permitted within the configured lookback if the trend remains bullish, a lower-timeframe close is at or below the signal close plus the configured tolerance, and no high since the signal has exceeded the signal close by two strike intervals. Check the current chart-bar high for invalidation before checking lower-timeframe bars. Enter immediately on a fresh signal or on a valid late entry only when flat, before the day preceding the next configured expiry, and no trade has already been entered for that expiry. Use the chart-bar close as the entry price even for a late entry.

Round the entry price to the nearest strike interval to obtain the reference strike. For the selected expiry, buy two calls two intervals below it, sell two calls three intervals above it, sell two calls four intervals above it, buy four far-out-of-the-money calls at the reference strike rounded to the deep-strike interval plus one such interval, buy one put at the higher sold-call strike, and sell one put at the initial long-call strike. Scale all quantities by the configured multiplier.

Set an initial stop below entry by the configured percentage; from the next calendar day, raise it if necessary to the level using half that percentage. Exit all remaining legs if the chart-bar low falls strictly below the stop or the configured expiry-day square-off time is reached, including within a lower-timeframe bar. Process exits before adjustments.

Monitor lower-timeframe highs or closes, as configured, for adjustments. On a move strictly above the lower sold-call strike, sell the two initial long calls, buy four calls at the lower sold-call strike, sell two calls one interval above the higher sold-call strike, sell one additional put at the initial long-call strike, and buy one put at that strike rounded to the deep-strike interval minus one such interval. On a subsequent move strictly above the higher sold-call strike, buy back both remaining short-call positions and buy two puts at the higher sold-call strike. If the first observed adjustment already exceeds the higher sold-call strike, perform the equivalent combined transactions directly. Upon reaching this second adjustment, set the stop to the higher sold-call strike minus two strike intervals plus a configured fraction of an interval.

After the second adjustment, calculate whole strike intervals above the higher sold-call strike from the chart-bar high. Once the configured trail-adjustment level is reached, optionally sell the remaining long calls and buy two calls one interval above the highest whole-interval level reached; independently, optionally close all put positions. Perform these optional adjustments only once. For stop trailing, cap the interval count at the configured maximum and, once it is at least one, raise the stop to the current high less the distance prescribed for that count: two intervals at the first level, one and a half at the second, and one at higher levels. Never lower the trailing stop.
