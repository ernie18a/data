Use chart price with either chart volume or volume and lower-timeframe order flow from a selected external instrument. Generate directional signals as follows:

For reversals, score five detectors in each direction: distributional instability opposite the prevailing drift; a changepoint or cumulative-sum break opposite a nonaccelerating drift; exhaustion of a stretched, formerly efficient trend; a rare high-volume, wide-range rejection climax; and a confirmed swing sweep followed within a window by an ATR-sized break through the opposite swing. Arm the direction with enough above-threshold detectors, preferring the bullish side on a tie. Promote it when an aligned climax appears and confirm it when aligned structure appears; structure can confirm directly from the armed state. Expire unconfirmed candidates after a window and emit on entry to the selected actionable stage.

Block reversal signals when the enabled mean-reversion or turbulence gate falls below its floor, outside an enabled session, against the selected higher-timeframe alignment when blocking is enabled, or without required lower-timeframe flow confirmation. The mean-reversion gate combines negative correlation between prior deviation from the mean and subsequent return with a variance-ratio score, optionally requiring a stationarity test. The turbulence gate suppresses signals during a high, nondecreasing shock-count burst. External volume affects exhaustion and climax; external lower-timeframe buy-minus-sell volume can supply the flow confirmation.

For continuation, choose trend direction from the configured fast/slow moving-average order and slow-average slope, an ordered and sufficiently spread Alligator, or the chart-timeframe double EMA's position relative to the nearest and a required number of higher-timeframe double EMAs. Signal in that direction on either a direction change, or a sufficiently deep pullback followed by the selected fast-average recross or directional momentum turn. Require aligned lower-timeframe flow if configured, and apply the turbulence gate. A visible reversal takes precedence over a simultaneous continuation.

Optionally filter visible signals by an options-favorable test: estimate realized volatility from chart OHLC, its historical percentile and slope, and the expected move over the chosen horizon. Require a sufficiently large expected move and enough favorable conditions among non-expensive volatility, rising volatility, sufficient expected move, and absence of configured late-session or expiry caution. Apply the selected grade filter and reversal cooldown; continuation has its own cooldown. Reversal grade uses calibrated conviction and higher-timeframe alignment. Conviction incorporates detector agreement and the gates, and may be adjusted by an external benchmark's momentum or relative-strength slope and by a linked external absorption shelf; these inputs can therefore affect signals when grade filtering is enabled. Calibration learns from prior reversal outcomes and shrinks confidence toward neutral while samples are limited. The script supplies stop and target levels but does not issue option buy or sell orders or explicit exit signals.
