Use an external market index’s daily and weekly closes to calculate a weighted momentum score from several lookback periods, then compare each score with its own exponential moving average. When the daily score crosses above its average, signal risk-on and seek long exposure; when it crosses below, signal risk-off and reduce or exit long exposure. Classify the regime as strong risk-on when both timeframes are above their averages, weak risk-on when only daily is above, short-term risk-off when only weekly is above, and strong risk-off when both are below. No short-entry rule is specified.
