At each UTC day/session rollover, calculate a long breakout level as the prior daily close plus a configurable fraction of the prior daily high–low range; set its stop halfway between that level and the prior daily low. Enter long when the current bar’s high exceeds the breakout level and no position is open. Exit the long at the session boundary or when the bar closes below its stop. Optionally enable shorts: calculate a short breakout level as the prior daily close minus the same fraction of the range, with a stop halfway between that level and the prior daily high; enter short when the current bar’s low falls below the level and no position is open, and exit at the session boundary or when the bar closes above its stop. An optional trend filter, disabled by default, permits longs only when the long-level-to-long-stop ratio exceeds the short-stop-to-short-level ratio, and shorts only when it is lower.
