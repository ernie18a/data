On a confirmed bar, go long when a smoothed close series crosses above a smoothed open series; go short when it crosses below. The series may use regular or Heikin Ashi prices and may be calculated on an alternate higher timeframe. Require the allowed trading session, volatility within a band relative to its recent average, sufficient ADX, and—when enabled—higher-timeframe trend alignment: fast EMA above/below slow EMA and higher-timeframe close above/below a baseline moving average. Also enforce the post-exit cooldown and, when enabled, allow only one trade per trend leg, resetting eligibility on a corresponding higher-timeframe trend flip. An opposite entry signal closes the current position and opens the new direction. While a position is open, place three partial profit targets and a stop, using either entry-time ATR distances or percentages; after the first target is reached, move the stop to entry when break-even protection is enabled.
