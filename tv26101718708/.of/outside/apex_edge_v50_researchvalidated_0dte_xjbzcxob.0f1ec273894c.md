During the morning entry window, consider a bullish entry when price breaks above the opening-range high on an up candle with elevated relative volume and confirmation from either the higher-timeframe trend or VWAP; consider a bearish entry on the corresponding break below the opening-range low. Also consider entries when price crosses beyond a volatility-scaled band around the daily open. During the final half-hour entry window, consider a bullish or bearish entry when either the first 30-minute direction is confirmed by a volatile opening range, VWAP, and above-average volume, or the move from the daily open is sufficiently strong and confirmed by VWAP and above-average volume. Block entries against a significant opening gap during the first hour. Require an A-level or better quality grade based on relative volume, trend strength, primary versus secondary signal, VWAP alignment, and higher-timeframe alignment; enforce a cooldown between signals, allow only one opening-range breakout per day, and do not enter after the hard cutoff. Enter only when flat. For a long position, exit at the configured profit target, when price reaches the active stop, after the maximum holding time, or at the hard clock exit; for a short position, apply the corresponding inverse conditions. After the first profit target is reached, move the stop to entry.
