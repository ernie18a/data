On each intraday bar, use the underlying instrument’s price and volume to generate directional 0DTE option entry recommendations. Treat all lookbacks, thresholds, session times, score weights, and risk multiples as configurable parameters.

At each new trading day, record the open and preceding close, and reset the opening-range state. During the configured opening session, accumulate its high and low and record its first open and last close. After the session, record the range direction and count bars from the first bar outside it. Calculate ATR, VWAP, relative volume, directional movement and ADX, and higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs.

Generate primary bullish or bearish candidates from either: (1) an eligible close beyond the corresponding opening-range extreme, with a candle in that direction, sufficient relative volume, and either matching higher-timeframe EMA alignment or a close on the corresponding side of VWAP; or (2) a close crossing the corresponding band around the daily open, where band distance is a multiple of the moving average of intraday true range. Both require the configured daytime entry window. The opening-range candidate is limited to a configured number of bars after the range and can occur only once per day; mark it used as soon as that candidate occurs, even if subsequent filters reject it.

Generate secondary candidates in the late entry window before the hard exit, with volume above its moving average and price on the corresponding side of VWAP. Require either an opening-range direction matching the candidate and an opening-range width exceeding an ATR-based threshold, or a daily-open-to-current-close return exceeding the directional threshold. Each candidate family can be independently enabled or disabled.

When the gap filter is enabled, block bearish candidates after a sufficiently positive opening gap and bullish candidates after a sufficiently negative gap until the configured number of bars after the opening range. Before the range is complete, treat this bar count as zero.

Score the combined candidates using relative-volume strength, a trending regime defined by ADX, directional movement and matching higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and whether any primary candidate exists. Add the VWAP-alignment score when the boolean ‘close above VWAP’ equals the allowed bullish-candidate boolean. Add the higher-timeframe-alignment score when either ‘fast EMA above slow EMA’ equals the allowed bullish-candidate boolean or ‘fast EMA below slow EMA’ equals the allowed bearish-candidate boolean. Issue each allowed directional recommendation only if its score reaches the entry threshold, the cooldown since any prior recommendation has elapsed, and the hard-exit time has not arrived. Recommendations update the cooldown even while a position is already active.

Track one position at a time, entering at the signal bar’s close when flat; if both directions qualify, process the bullish entry first. Set its initial stop at the opposite opening-range extreme when the corresponding opening-range candidate is present, otherwise at an ATR-based distance from entry. Set two profit targets as multiples of initial risk. Count the entry bar as held, and evaluate its high and low immediately. When the first target is touched, move the stop to entry before checking exits on that same bar. Exit when the second target or current stop is touched, the maximum holding period is reached, or the hard-exit time arrives.
