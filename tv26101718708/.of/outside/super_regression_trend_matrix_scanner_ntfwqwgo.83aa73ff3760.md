For each instrument, fit a rolling linear regression to closing prices and estimate residual volatility as root mean squared deviation from the regression. Form adaptive upper and lower bands around the regression using a configurable volatility multiple. Maintain a directional state, initially bullish: while bullish, raise the lower band only when the new lower band is higher; while bearish, lower the upper band only when the new upper band is lower. If the close crosses below the active lower band, switch to bearish and signal short; if it crosses above the active upper band, switch to bullish and signal long. Keep the current directional signal between flips; no separate exit rule is defined. ADX, volume expansion, price versus multiple EMAs, and RSI versus its moving average are displayed as context and do not gate the signals.
