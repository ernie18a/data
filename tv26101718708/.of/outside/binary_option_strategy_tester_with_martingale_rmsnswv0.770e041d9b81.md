Calculate a fast line by applying three successive EMAs to close and combining them as first EMA minus second EMA plus third EMA. Calculate a slow line as twice an EMA of close minus an EMA of that EMA. Calculate an upper envelope as an EMA of the rolling highest average of low and close, and a lower envelope as an EMA of the rolling lowest average of high and close. Use the greater envelope when the fast line exceeds the slow line; otherwise use the lesser envelope. The EMA and rolling-window lengths are parameters.

A call setup requires the fast line above the slow line, the selected envelope above the current low, and its latest change greater than its preceding change. A put setup requires the fast line below the slow line, the selected envelope below the current high, and its latest decline greater than its preceding decline. An allowed setup sets its direction's persistent flag and clears the other flag. Only enabled option directions can set flags. Generate a buy signal when a flag changes from false to true, no trade is active, and the optional session filter permits it. A signal blocked by a trade or session does not trigger later unless its flag first resets and rises again.

Model the initial binary option as entered at the next candle's open and expiring at its close. A call wins on a strictly rising candle; a put wins on a strictly falling candle. A flat candle loses. After a loss, continue evaluating successive candles up to the configured maximum attempt count. For subsequent attempts, choose the original direction, its opposite, the previous candle's color, or the opposite of that color according to the selected mode; a flat previous candle is treated as falling for this choice. End the sequence at the first win or after the final loss, and clear both setup flags. The script evaluates these continuation outcomes but emits only the initial call or put signal; it does not emit separate continuation orders or specify position sizes.
