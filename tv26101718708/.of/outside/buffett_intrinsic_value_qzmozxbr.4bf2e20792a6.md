Select trailing earnings per share when automatic data is enabled and positive; otherwise use the manual input. For growth, when automatic calculation is enabled, use return on equity multiplied by the retained fraction of earnings, with the retained fraction bounded between zero and one, if return on equity is positive and dividend data is nonnegative. Otherwise use the latest fiscal-year earnings-per-share change divided by the absolute previous value, if available and nonzero. Express growth as a percentage and clamp either automatic estimate to its configured bounds; otherwise use manual growth. Select the daily 10-year government bond yield for the instrument's country: use Germany for the listed euro-area countries, China for Hong Kong, Australia for New Zealand, and the US for unrecognized countries. Use the manual yield if automatic selection is disabled or the selected yield is unavailable or nonpositive. Calculate intrinsic value as earnings per share times the sum of a baseline multiplier and a growth coefficient times the growth percentage, multiplied by the reference yield and divided by the selected bond yield. Calculate the valuation margin as intrinsic value minus market price, divided by market price, expressed as a percentage; market price is either the close or a manual input. Recommend buy when the margin is at least the safety threshold, hold when it is nonnegative but below that threshold, and sell when it is negative.
