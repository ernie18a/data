On the first confirmed chart bar of each new month, rank the configured instruments by risk-adjusted momentum. For each instrument, divide its close-to-close return over the return lookback by the population standard deviation of its one-bar percentage returns over the volatility lookback, floored at a small positive value. Sort descending by comparing each position with each later position and swapping only when the later score is strictly greater; comparisons involving missing scores do not swap. Hold the highest-ranked fixed number of instruments long with equal weights, even if their scores are negative. Exit instruments that fall out of that group and retain the selected group until the next monthly rebalance. There are no short positions.
