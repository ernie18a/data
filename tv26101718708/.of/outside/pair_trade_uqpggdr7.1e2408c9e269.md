Using two selected instruments’ logarithmic closing prices, calculate a rolling relationship: estimate the slope as their rolling correlation multiplied by the ratio of their rolling standard deviations, and the intercept as the second instrument’s rolling mean minus that slope times the first instrument’s rolling mean. Compute the second instrument’s deviation from its fitted value. Enter short when the deviation rises above the positive entry threshold; exit that short when it falls below the exit threshold. Enter long when the deviation falls below the negative entry threshold; exit that long when it rises above the negative exit threshold.
