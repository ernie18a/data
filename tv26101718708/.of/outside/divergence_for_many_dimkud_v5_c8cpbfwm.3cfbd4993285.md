On each bar, detect regular bullish and bearish divergences independently across the enabled indicators, including an optional externally supplied indicator. The built-in choices are MACD and its histogram, RSI, stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin money flow, money flow index, logistic dominance, Williams VIX Fix, stochastic RSI, SMI ergodic oscillator, a volume-weighted MACD histogram, bull-bear power, smoothed balance of power, and relative volatility index. Use configured indicator parameters.

Find confirmed price pivots using the configured close or high/low source and pivot window. For each indicator, search recent eligible pivots within the configured count and age limits. A bullish divergence requires a lower price low and a higher indicator value than at a prior pivot low; a bearish divergence requires a higher price high and a lower indicator value than at a prior pivot high. Between the endpoints, neither the indicator nor close may cross below its connecting line for bullish divergence, or above it for bearish divergence. Without immediate detection, use the previous bar as the recent endpoint and additionally require the current indicator or close to have risen for bullish divergence, or fallen for bearish divergence. Immediate detection uses the current bar and skips that confirmation. A signal exists if any enabled indicator has the corresponding regular divergence.

Require all enabled filters for the relevant direction: a selected Keltner or Bollinger channel test; configured inclusive ranges for each of two timeframe-specific RSIs, CCI, and Chande momentum; a configured exclusive range for timeframe-specific money flow index; an optional rising-or-falling money flow test; a chosen greater-than or less-than comparison of two smoothed true ranges; the same kind of comparison for two smoothed volumes; and a Supertrend direction, optionally inverted. Channel tests select a current wick outside, previous wick outside followed by current close inside, current close outside, or previous close outside followed by current close inside; compare both bars with the current channel boundary. When either direction of an RSI, CCI, momentum, or money-flow range filter is enabled, a direction passes only if its own range is enabled and satisfied. For the second RSI, preserve the special case that an unavailable reading bypasses a short-only range filter. The money-flow rising and falling options are ORed and apply equally to both directions. Use the configured timeframes and the script's confirmed-bar sampling for RSI, CCI, and momentum; money-flow requests use the current requested value.

Enter long on a bullish signal and short on a bearish signal when that direction is enabled, all its filters pass, the bar is inside the configured testing period, and the most recently closed trade did not enter on this bar. Opposite entries can reverse a position. Optionally, a filtered bullish signal closes a normal short and a filtered bearish signal closes a normal long, regardless of the testing period or whether the opposite direction is enabled.

For normal entries, place a fixed percentage stop and profit target around average entry price, with the configured option to make the stop percentage equal the target percentage. Optionally, when any trade closes on the current bar and the most recently closed trade did not enter on that bar, place an opposite entry based on the last normal entry's direction; this applies to any exit reason, ignores entry filters, dates, and direction enables, and clears that remembered direction. These reversal entries use separate entry identifiers and are not covered by the normal entries' signal-close or percentage-exit orders.
