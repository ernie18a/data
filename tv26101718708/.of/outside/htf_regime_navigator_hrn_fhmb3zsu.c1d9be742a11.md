Build a higher-timeframe range from confirmed swing pivots, falling back to recent extremes if the pivot range is invalid. Call a breakout a trend only when the higher-timeframe close clears a range boundary by its configured buffer and enough active moving averages agree in both price position and slope; otherwise classify the range by majority evidence from prior price direction into the range, up/down volume share, recent failed breakdowns/breakouts, and regular RSI divergence. Ties or missing range evidence are neutral.

Create a directional forecast by weighting and combining: price position across the moving-average stack; regime direction and strength; value within the range, rewarding a favorable position for the regime and penalizing extension; recent RSI divergence; recent range-edge reversal events and up/down volume share; and manually supplied macro, catalyst, flow, and analyst evidence. Map the resulting signed score to bullish, neutral/chop, and bearish probabilities, keeping a configured minimum probability for the neutral case. Recommend a long or short bias only when the corresponding directional probability reaches its configured action threshold; otherwise stand aside. For the bias-based location cue, require price in the directional Fibonacci retracement value zone.

For mechanical entries, track selected moving averages as above, testing, or below using a configured proximity tolerance. A long structure requires no selected average below price; a short structure requires price below all selected averages, with the configured asymmetric allowance for the fastest average to be testing. A fresh reclaim after losing that structure, a newly qualified moving-average stack after it was sufficiently broken or oppositely ordered, or a recent matching range-edge reversal event can license a reversal entry. Under the selected entry mode, trigger on the fresh reclaim itself and/or the first eligible test of the fastest average while a license is active; other modes may trigger on an average test or reclaim transition. Require the configured slope alignment and freshness, moving-average order, Fibonacci signal zone, price inside that zone, EMA/Fibonacci confluence, regime veto or agreement, volume condition, and score threshold when enabled. In strict mode, fresh licenses do not bypass slope or zone requirements. Require the structure to persist for the configured confirmation closes before confirming; respect cooldowns and consume only the allowed early test episodes after each reclaim.

Exit a confirmed long when price closes below its selected invalidation reference, adjusted by its buffer where applicable, or when long structure is lost. Exit a confirmed short on the mirrored close above its invalidation reference or when short structure is lost. After an exit, apply the configured cooldown.
