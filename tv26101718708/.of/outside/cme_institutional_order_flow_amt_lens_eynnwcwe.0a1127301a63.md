Stand aside during the first 15 minutes of the cash session and avoid initiating breakouts in low-volume or tight-range chop. Classify the auction by its volume profile: on a balanced, bell-shaped profile, fade tests of the value-area boundaries toward the point of control; on a double-distribution profile, trade a breakout through the low-volume corridor toward the other distribution’s point of control; on a buying- or liquidation-drive profile, do not fade the morning drive and instead trade pullbacks with its direction toward the developing point of control or a supporting imbalance shelf. At overnight extremes, treat a sweep into an unmitigated shelf followed by buyer absorption near a low as a potential long, and seller absorption near a high as a potential short. Treat strong directional, high-volume expansion beyond value as continuation in the breakout direction.
