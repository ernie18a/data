On each bar, track the current or most recent accumulation-session high and low, updating them before evaluating signals. During the manipulation session, a lower sweep occurs when the low breaks below the accumulation low and the close returns above it; an upper sweep is the converse. Calculate a four-stage Laguerre RSI from close. Calculate a Hurst proxy as one minus the logarithm of the ratio of summed one-bar true ranges to the lookback high-low range, divided by the logarithm of the lookback length. When the regime detector is enabled, allow entries in volatility compression or whenever no directional trend is detected. Compression requires both a low ratio of ATR to its moving average and a low Hurst proxy. Otherwise, a directional trend requires a high Hurst proxy, strong ADX, and a winning bullish or bearish score meeting a minimum: score RSI direction, the matching directional indicator dominance with strong ADX, and the sign of volume assigned by candle direction. When the detector is disabled, allow entries only below the stationarity threshold. Go long on a lower sweep when Laguerre RSI is below its lower threshold, the regime is allowed, and the external dollar index's same-timeframe close has fallen from its previous bar. Go short on the corresponding upper sweep, upper Laguerre threshold, allowed regime, and rising dollar index, unless long-only mode is enabled. Enter when flat; add in the same direction only when the close is adverse to the position's average entry. Ignore opposite signals while positioned. Size each entry from allocated margin times leverage divided by close, subject to the platform's minimum quantity and pyramiding limit. After each entry, double allocated margin up to a cap based on the current base margin. Optionally scale base margin with equity, never below its initial value. After a position closes, reset allocation and accumulated losses if cumulative net profit has not decreased since the preceding closure; otherwise retain allocation and accumulate the loss. While flat with no accumulated loss, update allocation to the current base. Exit a long or short when profit reaches its threshold and close moves respectively below or above a fast EMA. Also exit on a close beyond the corresponding logical loss threshold, and maintain a separate adverse intrabar stop at the catastrophic loss threshold, both measured from average entry price.
