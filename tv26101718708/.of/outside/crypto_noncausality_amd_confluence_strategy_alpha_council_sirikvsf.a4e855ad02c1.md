For a long signal, require price to pierce below the current Asian-session low and close back above it during the London session; the Laguerre momentum oscillator must be oversold, the regime gate must indicate chop or volatility compression (or stationarity when the regime model is disabled), and the dollar index must be falling. For a short signal, require a pierce above the Asian-session high followed by a close back below it during London; the oscillator must be overbought, the same regime gate must pass, and the dollar index must be rising. The regime model identifies compression from low relative volatility and low estimated Hurst exponent; otherwise it classifies strong trends from Hurst, ADX, RSI, directional indicators, and signed volume change, allowing only chop or compression for entries. If flat, enter in the signal direction; while holding that direction, add on a repeated signal only when price has moved against the average entry price. Suppress short entries in long-only mode. Exit a profitable position when price crosses against a fast EMA after reaching the dynamic profit threshold. Also exit at a logical close-based loss threshold or a wider adverse intrabar wick stop.
