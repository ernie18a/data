Use the traded instrument’s bar data, Tether dominance, and the sum of open interest from three configured exchange contracts, treating missing open-interest values as zero. Compare dominance and aggregate open interest with their respective simple moving averages.

A bullish sweep occurs when the low falls below the prior rolling low and the close returns above it; a bearish sweep occurs when the high rises above the prior rolling high and the close returns below it. Keep each sweep valid for a configured number of bars. Confirm either direction when current volume exceeds its moving average by a configured number of standard deviations, or the candle body is less than a configured fraction of its range. Signal long when a bullish sweep is valid, confirmation is present, dominance is below its average, and aggregate open interest is at or below its average. Signal short when a bearish sweep is valid, confirmation is present, dominance is above its average, and aggregate open interest is above its average. Suppress subsequent signals of the same direction until more than the expiry interval has elapsed since the last accepted signal; update that timestamp even if no order is placed.

When flat, enter on an accepted signal. When already positioned in that direction, add only if the close is adverse to the average entry price. Ignore signals opposite to an existing position. Size each order from allocated margin times leverage divided by close, subject to a minimum quantity. After each entry or addition, set the next allocated margin to a capped multiple of the margin used for that order. If the optional regime gate identifies an opposing trend, reduce the margin used for that order before calculating its size and the next allocation.

For the optional regime gate, calculate a trend proxy as one minus normalized log choppiness, using the rolling sum of true range divided by the rolling high-low range, floored by minimum tick size. Maintain a persistent bullish, bearish, or neutral state. When this proxy and ADX exceed their trend thresholds, set bullish if RSI is above its midpoint, positive directional movement exceeds negative directional movement, the EMA of lower-timeframe signed-volume changes is nonnegative, and close exceeds its regime EMA; set bearish under the opposite inequalities. Signed volume adds lower-timeframe volume for an up candle, subtracts it for a down candle, and adds zero for a flat candle. In nonbinary mode, reset to neutral when the trend proxy or ADX falls below its neutral threshold, or when price crosses the regime EMA against the current state. Otherwise retain the state. Display a neutral state for the bar on which the state changes, then use the persistent state on subsequent bars. The displayed state determines whether an entry is counter-trend.

For an open position, exit at a configured maximum profit limit or at a catastrophe stop, measured from average entry. Replace the catastrophe stop with a profit lock once the favorable bar extreme reaches a configured first threshold; move it to a second configured profit level when the second threshold is reached. Track realized losses including commissions across closed positions. When losses remain, calculate a recovery price by adding loss divided by absolute position size to the long average entry, or subtracting it from the short average entry; if the favorable bar extreme touches that price, set the stop to the more protective of the current lock and recovery price. Apply the coded tier update before the recovery update on each bar. Reset the lock when flat. Also close the position if the close reaches the configured adverse loss threshold, or if profit at the close reaches a configured minimum and the close is below a fast EMA for a long or above it for a short. When a position closes, reset margin and accumulated losses if net profit has not decreased since the previous closure; otherwise add the net-profit decrease to accumulated losses and retain the current margin allocation.
