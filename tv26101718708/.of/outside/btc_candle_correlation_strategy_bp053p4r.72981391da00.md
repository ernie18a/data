During the configured date window, average BTC open, high, low, and close across several exchanges. Derive a price series as the square root of the mean of half the squared averaged OHLC values. Form upper and lower bands around its moving average, offset by the moving average of the averaged high-low range. When the derived series crosses above the upper band, enter long if the averaged BTC close is above its open; when it crosses below the lower band, close any long position. In mode allowing shorts, crossing above the upper band enters long and crossing below the lower band enters short, reversing an opposing position.
