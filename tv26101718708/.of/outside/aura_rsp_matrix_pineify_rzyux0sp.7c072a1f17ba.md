Compare the traded instrument’s close with a benchmark’s close on the same timeframe. Divide the instrument close by the benchmark close, normalize that relative-price series against its rolling weighted average, and scale it to an equilibrium of 100. Measure relative momentum by normalizing the resulting strength series against its own rolling weighted average, also scaled to 100. Smooth both series with exponential averages. Classify each bar: Leading when both smoothed values are above 100; Weakening when strength is above 100 and momentum is at or below 100; Lagging when both are at or below 100; Improving when strength is at or below 100 and momentum is above 100. Signal buy on the first bar entering Leading and sell on the first bar entering Lagging; do not repeat either signal while its phase continues. This indicator does not define exit logic.
