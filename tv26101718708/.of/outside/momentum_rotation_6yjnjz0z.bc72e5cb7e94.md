On the first trading day of each month, compare SPY, QQQ, and IWM using each asset’s percentage price change over roughly three months, adding a bonus when its price is above its 200-day moving average and a penalty otherwise. Select the highest-scoring asset, with ties resolved in the order SPY, QQQ, then IWM. If that top-scoring asset is above its moving average, close any existing position and go long it; otherwise close all positions and hold cash.
