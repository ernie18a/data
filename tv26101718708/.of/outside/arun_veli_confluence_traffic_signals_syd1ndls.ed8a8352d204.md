On a confirmed bar during the permitted trading session, and only after the signal cooldown, score long and short setups independently across seven conditions: aligned fast/medium/slow moving averages; price above or below session VWAP; price on the corresponding side of a Gaussian-filter midline whose slope agrees; MACD histogram positive and rising or negative and falling; RSI in the corresponding non-extreme half-zone; volume above its moving average by a configurable factor; and a directional trigger from a price cross of the fast average, a VWAP cross, or a break of the opening range. Emit a long signal when the long score meets the configured minimum and its trigger condition is true; emit a short signal by the mirrored rule. For either direction, plot a stop and two profit targets at configurable ATR multiples from the signal close. Separately emit a caution signal when price reaches or exceeds the outer VWAP deviation band and RSI is extreme, with a candle closing back toward the VWAP: bearish rejection at the upper band or bullish rejection at the lower band. Caution signals do not by themselves specify an entry or exit.
