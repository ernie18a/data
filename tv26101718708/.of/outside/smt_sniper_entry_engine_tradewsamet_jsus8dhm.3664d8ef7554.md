On each closed bar, compare the traded instrument with a selected positively correlated market on the same timeframe. Use each market’s prior rolling highs and lows, excluding the current bar. A bullish setup requires exactly one market to sweep its prior low and close back above it; a bearish setup mirrors this at prior highs. Require sufficient sweep depth relative to the sweeping market’s ATR, a directionally matching traded-instrument candle, a close near its favorable extreme, and sufficient candle range relative to its ATR. Require positive rolling correlation of the markets’ log returns, a traded-instrument extreme near the appropriate end of its prior external range, and sufficient ATR-normalized separation between the non-sweeping market’s extreme and its reference. Also require the preset’s context score: a weighted sum of candle quality, clipped positive correlation, range location, divergence separation, and sweep-depth tiers. Apply a separate cooldown to accepted setups in each direction.

An accepted setup replaces any pending setup; if both directions qualify on the same bar, retain the bearish one. Store the setup candle’s high, low, and ATR. Starting on a later bar and within the confirmation window, accumulate directional displacement, a close beyond the setup candle’s opposite extreme, a directional fair value gap, and a close beyond the prior rolling microstructure extreme. Displacement requires a directional candle with sufficient ATR-normalized body, body-to-range ratio, and limited opposing wick. A bullish gap occurs when the current low exceeds an earlier candle’s high; a bearish gap is the mirror image. The least restrictive preset requires displacement and the setup-candle break; the middle preset also requires a gap or microstructure break; the strictest requires both. Invalidate a bullish setup on a close below its stored low minus an ATR buffer, or a bearish setup on a close above its stored high plus the buffer. Expire it after the confirmation window. When all required evidence is present, the setup is valid, and the shared final-signal cooldown has elapsed, signal long for bullish or short for bearish and clear the setup.

For the associated trade tracking, open a trade at the signal close only when no tracked trade is active and ATR is available. Set an ATR-based stop and a reward-to-risk target in the trade direction. From the next bar onward, exit when the bar touches either level; give the stop priority if both are touched. A new signal on an exit bar may start another tracked trade.
