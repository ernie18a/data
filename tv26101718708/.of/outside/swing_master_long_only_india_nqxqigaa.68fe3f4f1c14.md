Calculate relative strength as the traded instrument's percentage distance from its selected moving average minus the comparison index's percentage distance from the same type and length of moving average. When the relative-strength entry filter is enabled, require this difference to be strictly above its threshold. Optionally require bullish trend for every entry. Apply the selected bar-confirmation setting to entries and exits; a confirmed bar or the latest bar after its scheduled close qualifies as closed.

Build the trend from ATR bands around the bar midpoint. Carry the upper band forward unless the new upper band is lower or the previous close exceeded the previous upper band; carry the lower band forward unless the new lower band is higher or the previous close fell below the previous lower band. Start bullish; switch from bearish to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper band, and from bullish to bearish when close falls below the previous lower band.

A long entry signal occurs when the entry gates pass and any of these events occurs: (1) on a bullish trend phase, close crosses above the highest high in the immediately preceding bearish phase, scanned back up to the configured limit; establish that level on the bullish flip and allow one qualifying signal per phase; (2) while bullish and the trigger is armed, close crosses above the EMA of highs; disarm it after a qualifying signal and rearm it when close crosses below the EMA of lows or reaches the configured multiple of the EMA band width above the EMA of highs; (3) the fast close EMA crosses above the slow close EMA. A crossing requires the previous value to be at or on the opposite side and the current value to be strictly beyond. Trigger state is consumed only when its entry gates pass.

When flat, enter long on any entry signal. While long, exit when relative strength is strictly below its threshold and the timing gate passes, even if the relative-strength entry filter is disabled. Exit takes priority over entry on the same bar and rearms the first two triggers; the first trigger still requires a fresh crossing of its stored level. Signals while already long do not add to the position. No short positions are opened.
