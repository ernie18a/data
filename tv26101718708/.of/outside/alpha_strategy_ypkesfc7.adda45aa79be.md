On each bar, calculate a risk-adjusted performance measure from the instrument’s lookback return, adjusted for dividend yield and the risk-free rate, divided by its normalized volatility; smooth it. Estimate the instrument’s beta to the S&P 500 from their returns, calculate active return relative to that benchmark using the estimated beta, and smooth it. Also calculate the strategy’s normalized recent-momentum filter from instrument closes. Enter long when the bar closes up, smoothed risk-adjusted performance is above its cutoff, smoothed active return exceeds its entry threshold, and the momentum filter exceeds its threshold. Exit the long when smoothed risk-adjusted performance falls below its cutoff or smoothed active return falls below zero. There are no short or options decisions.
