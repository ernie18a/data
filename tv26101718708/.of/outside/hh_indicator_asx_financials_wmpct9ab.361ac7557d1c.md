For each monitored financial stock, calculate each candle’s body high as the greater of its open and close. Mark a body high as a pivot high when it is at least as high as the body highs of the surrounding candles on both sides, including later candles needed to confirm it. Signal long when the stock’s close crosses from at or below its most recently confirmed pivot high to above it. The signal can recur if price falls back to or below that level and crosses above it again.
