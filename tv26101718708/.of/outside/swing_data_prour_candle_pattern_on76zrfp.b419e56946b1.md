Use daily data for the entry decision. A long setup is ready only when every enabled trend filter passes, every quality filter passes, and at least one enabled trigger occurs.

Trend filters independently require the close above the slower EMA, the faster EMA above the slower EMA, the slower EMA rising, and the close above a longer SMA. Disabled filters pass automatically.

Quality requires relative volume and a market-relative strength rating to meet their respective minimums. Optionally also require average share volume and current close multiplied by average share volume to meet minimums, and the relevant sector ETF's daily close to exceed its daily open. Map the instrument's sector to its corresponding sector ETF; use a broad-market ETF for unrecognized sectors. Compute relative volume as daily volume divided by its daily moving average. Compute relative strength from a weighted combination of price ratios over several lookbacks, divided by the equivalent weighted combination for a broad-market index; convert this score to a rating using the external rating-curve series and its piecewise rating bands. Insufficient history or missing required data fails the applicable quality check.

An enabled reclaim trigger occurs when the daily low touches or falls below either EMA and the close finishes above that EMA, or when the low stays above either EMA but within its configured tap zone and the close finishes above it. An enabled pullback trigger occurs when the low is at or above either EMA and within its configured zone, the close is above that EMA and sufficiently high within the day's range, and, if required, the close is at least the open. Either trigger is sufficient; the reclaim trigger takes precedence when both occur.

When trend and quality pass but no trigger occurs, watch and wait. When either trend or quality fails, the setup is not ready. A confirmed enabled bullish candle pattern can label an already-ready setup as stronger, but does not change entry eligibility. The framework specifies no short or exit decision.
