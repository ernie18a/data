For each selected market, use weekly COT data for the selected participant group. Calculate its net position as longs minus shorts and its positioning difference as its net position minus the other group's net position. Normalize the difference over three lookback windows and the selected group's net position over two lookback windows to their respective historical ranges; use the midpoint when a range is zero. Invert each normalized value for non-commercial participants, but retain it for commercial participants. Combine the five values using their respective weights to form a bullish positioning score. Signal a buy for the market when this score enters the upper extreme zone from below it, provided both the current and previous scores are available.
