Normalize the combined score to a 0–100 directional bias: strong long at 75 or higher, long bias from 60, neutral from 40 to below 60, short bias from 25 to below 40, and strong short below 25. Use this external-market bias to confirm the chart’s own signal: take its long or short signal when the bias agrees, and treat mixed or neutral readings as caution.
