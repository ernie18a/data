On each bar, calculate the instrument's return minus the benchmark's return over each of two lookback periods. Award one long point for each positive relative return, close above the long-period simple moving average, positive MACD histogram, and RSI strictly inside the long-side range. Award one short point for each negative relative return, close below that moving average, negative MACD histogram, and RSI strictly inside the short-side range. Unavailable factors score zero.

A long trigger occurs when close is within the configured percentage tolerance of the fast exponential moving average and above the mid-period simple moving average; or the MACD histogram crosses above zero with volume above its moving average times the volume multiplier; or close exceeds the highest high of the preceding swing lookback with that volume confirmation. A short trigger uses close below the mid-period average, a histogram cross below zero, or close below the lowest low of the preceding swing lookback, respectively; the same tolerance and volume rules apply.

Signal long when the long score meets its minimum and a long trigger occurs; signal short when the short score meets its minimum and a short trigger occurs. Entry markers are suppressed when both signals occur on the same bar, though both entry alerts still fire. No exit rule is specified.
