On each bar, score long and short setups by awarding one point per condition. Long points: the instrument outperformed a benchmark over both medium- and longer-term lookbacks; price is above its long-term moving average; MACD histogram is positive; RSI is in a healthy middle range. Short points: it underperformed the benchmark over both lookbacks; price is below its long-term moving average; MACD histogram is negative; RSI is in a lower-middle range. Enter long when the long score meets its configured threshold and any bullish trigger occurs: price is near its fast moving average while above its intermediate moving average; MACD histogram crosses above zero on above-average volume; or price breaks above a recent swing high on above-average volume. Enter short when the short score meets its threshold and any bearish trigger occurs: price is near its fast moving average while below its intermediate moving average; MACD histogram crosses below zero on above-average volume; or price breaks below a recent swing low on above-average volume. The indicator defines no exit decision.
