On each bar, score long and short separately, using configurable lookbacks, thresholds, timeframes, weights, and score caps. Add one unit per condition: matching persistent higher-timeframe sweep bias; enabled trading session; volume spike; matching sweep in an enabled correlated instrument; traded instrument outperforming the correlated instrument for long or underperforming for short; matching current hourly fair-value gap unless overridden by a sufficiently larger, nearby opposing daily gap; matching current daily gap; proximity to the prior daily low for long or high for short; falling external volatility index for long or rising for short when enabled; repeated matching local sweeps; and proximity to matching structure. Add double weight for a directional engulfing candle at matching structure and for a directional engulfing candle closing inside the latest unexpired matching chart-timeframe fair-value gap. Cap each score. Relative performance contributes even when correlation confirmation is disabled.

A low sweep occurs when the preceding bar's low reaches or passes the prior rolling low and the current close recovers above that level; a high sweep is symmetric. Higher-timeframe sweep bias persists until an opposite sweep, with a low sweep taking precedence if both occur. Reset each directional sweep count when price reaches the corresponding recent opposite extreme; increment it on a matching sweep and clear the opposite count, processing high sweeps last. Structure comprises the prior daily low or high, current daily and weekly opens, and retained rounded midpoints of matching hourly and daily gaps, each with its own ATR-based proximity. A gap compares the most recent and preceding requested higher-timeframe bars, or the current and earlier chart bars, and must exceed a price-relative minimum. Retain the latest midpoint for each higher-timeframe direction. A qualifying chart gap remains eligible until its age limit or first engulfing entry. An engulfing candle must have a sufficiently large ATR-relative body, close near its directional extreme, engulf the prior candle's body by the specified inequalities, and have a volume spike.

On a local sweep, call its matching direction only if its score strictly dominates and either reaches the high-score threshold or reaches the lower threshold with a sufficient score gap. An opposing gap and sweep conflict produces a wait verdict. Otherwise, a high score produces a take verdict if correlated confirmation and structure agree, or if a break-of-structure wait is already active; it produces a wait-for-break verdict only when neither applies. A medium score produces scale in with a volume spike and valid without one; a lower qualifying score is marginal. On a high sweep, also call fade-long when long strictly dominates and reaches the fade threshold; on a low sweep, symmetrically call fade-short. Independently issue long or short setup alerts when the matching sweep and minimum score occur, even without dominance.

Each low sweep starts or resets a pending long break at the preceding rolling high; each high sweep similarly sets a pending short break at the preceding rolling low. Confirm the direction when close crosses its stored level, then clear that pending state. Emit separate directional signals for matching structure flips, gap entries, and sufficiently large range-box breaks with a volume spike. A range box starts when the rolling high-low range contracts within an ATR multiple, retains and expands its boundaries while contraction continues, and ends on a qualifying close through a boundary from an open inside it. Also emit directional maximum-score and contrarian alerts at their respective score thresholds. Track invalidation independently: each sweep activates its matching side and deactivates the other, storing a stop beyond the prior rolling extreme by an ATR multiple. A close beyond that stop invalidates the active side and clears its pending break. The displayed profit targets are projections, not exit triggers. The session-filter switch does not gate signals.
