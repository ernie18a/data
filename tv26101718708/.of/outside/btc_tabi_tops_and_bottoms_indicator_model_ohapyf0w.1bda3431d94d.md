Calculate a weighted composite of Bitcoin cycle metrics, then smooth it with an exponential moving average. For each metric except the ordinary price RSI, use its rolling percentile rank: the fraction of observations in the lookback window less than or equal to the current value. Normalize the ordinary price RSI to a fraction. The metrics are: price divided by each of three differently sized long-term simple moving averages; the difference between a shorter moving average and a multiple of the longest moving average, divided by that longest average; the residual of log price from its rolling linear regression; ATR divided by its long-term simple moving average; the external Bitcoin MVRV score; one minus the percentile rank of external Bitcoin dominance; price minus its multi-year simple moving average, divided by price; the ratio of a medium-term to a multi-year price simple moving average; short-term price return divided by the absolute medium-term return plus a stabilizing constant, clipped to fixed bounds; price divided by the geometric mean of a multi-year price EMA and a medium-term price SMA; the previous completed monthly price RSI; the external ETH/BTC exchange rate; Bitcoin market capitalization divided by the sum of two stablecoin market capitalizations plus a stabilizing constant; an EMA of the percentage price premium of Bitcoin on Coinbase over Binance; and the ratio of short-term to long-term simple moving averages of external Bitcoin hashrate. Use price in place of hashrate when hashrate is unavailable. Substitute a neutral midpoint for unavailable MVRV, dominance, monthly RSI, ETH/BTC, or stablecoin-ratio data; treat a missing second stablecoin capitalization as zero. Treat the exchange premium as zero when either exchange price is unavailable. Divide the weighted sum by the total weight. In the lowest score zone, buy or accumulate Bitcoin, with stronger accumulation at lower scores. In the upper caution zone, reduce position size; in the sell and highest danger zones, sell or distribute. Intermediate scores are neutral or indicate caution. These zones do not specify short entries or exact position sizes.
