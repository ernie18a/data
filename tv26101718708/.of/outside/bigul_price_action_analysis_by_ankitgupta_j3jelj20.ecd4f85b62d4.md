In pair-bias mode, use NIFTY and BANKNIFTY continuous futures as external references. For each, calculate a daily central pivot range from the previous day's high, low, and close: take their average as the pivot, the high-low midpoint as one boundary, and twice the pivot minus that midpoint as the other boundary. Compare each futures contract's current chart-timeframe close with its ordered boundaries. Favor long when both closes are strictly above their ranges; favor short when both are strictly below. Otherwise, wait. The script specifies no exit rule.
