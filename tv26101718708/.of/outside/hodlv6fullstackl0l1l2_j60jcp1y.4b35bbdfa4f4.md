On each chart bar, calculate a composite score from macro, trend, and entry-timing scores, using configurable periods, thresholds, weights, and score mappings throughout.

Macro: Request daily closes with lookahead enabled for the dollar index, gold, Nasdaq index, volatility index, and copper. Calculate each instrument's percentage momentum over a fixed number of chart bars. Form a liquidity score from a weighted average of individually bounded, centered linear transforms: dollar and volatility momentum lower their contributions; gold and Nasdaq momentum raise theirs. Form a resonance score by counting falling dollar and volatility momentum and rising gold, Nasdaq, and copper momentum, then multiplying the count by a fixed increment without capping it. Form a bounded fear-greed score that decreases linearly with the volatility-index level. Combine these three scores by weighted average. When macro data is disabled or the dollar index is unavailable, use neutral liquidity and resonance scores; use a neutral fear-greed score when macro data is disabled or the volatility index is unavailable. Oil is fetched but does not affect decisions.

Trend: Calculate fast, middle, and slow EMAs, ADX, price momentum, and the percentile rank of ATR as a percentage of close. Classify strictly ascending EMA alignment as bullish and strictly descending alignment as bearish; distinguish strong from weak alignment using an ADX threshold, and classify all other alignments as range. Map each regime to a score, transform momentum into a bounded score, subtract the volatility percentile rank from the score-scale maximum, and combine the three by weighted average.

Entry timing: Track the highest close observed since the beginning of the chart and assign the current drawdown to the deepest qualifying configured band. Add points from ordered mappings for drawdown band, RSI, regime, distance from the slow EMA, and ATR as a percentage of close. Deeper drawdown, lower RSI, greater distance below the slow EMA, and higher ATR receive more points; the regime mapping gives the most points to range, followed by strong bear, weak bear, weak bull, and strong bull. Subtract an overheat penalty when RSI and the distance above the slow EMA both exceed their respective thresholds, then bound the result to the score scale.

Combine the macro, trend, and entry-timing scores by weighted average. Compare the result against descending thresholds to choose heavy buy, increased buy, normal buy, light buy, pause, or reduce position. Assign each tier its configured investment multiplier and target-position multiplier; pause and reduce invest nothing. On a confirmed bar where the tier changes, emit a buy label for normal buy or higher and a sell label for pause or reduce; the latter label suggests selling a configured fraction of the position even though the pause tier itself is named pause. Buy and reduce alerts are level conditions for normal buy or higher and pause or lower, respectively; separate deep-drawdown and oversold-RSI alerts do not alter the action.
