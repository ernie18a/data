Use configurable lengths, thresholds, weights and decay amounts throughout. On confirmed chart bars, estimate signed volume from lower-timeframe price changes and volume, preferring the finest available resolution; otherwise use volume weighted by the close’s position within the bar’s high-low range. Accumulate signed volume, volume, high and low until the absolute accumulated signed volume reaches a fixed threshold or a multiple of the rolling standard deviation of chart-bar signed volume. Then close a participation bar and reset the accumulator.

On each participation-bar close, identify bullish and bearish three-bar price gaps between the first and third bars, requiring a minimum ATR-scaled gap and sufficient absolute signed volume on the middle bar. Give each new gap full strength. On subsequent participation bars, decay its strength with time and further on touches, with deeper touches causing greater decay. A full penetration followed by a close beyond the far edge with sufficiently strong signed volume in the penetration direction kills the gap; a full penetration followed by a close beyond the near edge with sufficiently strong opposite signed volume restores it as a defended gap at configured strength. Kill other gaps when their strength reaches zero.

Determine the regime from participation-bar closes: divide the absolute net change over a window by the sum of absolute consecutive changes. A latest absolute change sufficiently large relative to the window’s average takes precedence as high volatility; otherwise classify efficiency above the trend threshold as an uptrend or downtrend according to net change, below the range threshold as range, and the remainder as transition. Calculate cumulative signed-volume change over a participation-bar window and flow toxicity as the sum of absolute participation-bar signed volumes divided by total participation-bar volume over another window.

In an uptrend, select the highest midpoint below the current close among live bullish gaps with sufficient strength; in a downtrend, select the lowest midpoint above the close among qualifying bearish gaps. This is the current structural trail. Compute confidence by applying a logistic transform to a weighted sum of selected-gap strength, regime, agreement between trend direction and the sign of cumulative signed-volume change, and a penalty for high flow toxicity. Optionally add the trend-aligned normalized external bias and the trend-aligned inverse rate of change of an external implied-volatility index; omit unavailable or disabled inputs. Normalize and clamp both external contributions to their configured ranges.

When signal generation is enabled and flat, enter only on a confirmed participation-bar close: go long in an uptrend with an available trail, sufficient confidence, positive cumulative signed-volume change, and no materially opposing enabled external bias; go short under the symmetric downtrend conditions. Set the stop to the selected trail. On every subsequent confirmed chart bar, ratchet a long stop upward or a short stop downward when a trail is available. Exit a long if the close falls below its stop or the regime ceases to be an uptrend; exit a short if the close rises above its stop or the regime ceases to be a downtrend. Do not enter and exit or reverse on the same bar.

The dashboard separately labels an uptrend or downtrend with sufficient confidence as long or short, respectively; otherwise it advises waiting or standing aside. This dashboard label does not require the additional entry conditions.
