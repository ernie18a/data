Compute the ratio between closes on two distinct time intervals, using the shorter-interval close divided by the longer-interval close minus one. Enter long when this ratio crosses upward through a positive threshold and a normalized oscillator of the traded instrument is above zero. Enter short when the ratio crosses downward through the corresponding negative threshold and the oscillator is below zero. Keep the current direction until the opposite entry signal occurs; on reversal, close the existing position and enter in the new direction. Exit a position when its holding period reaches the configured limit.
