Long-only strategy. While flat and within the configured trading dates, allow a long entry if at least one enabled Fibonacci setup qualifies. For each setup, calculate a Fibonacci level from the recent high-low range using the selected retracement border; require the selected price source to cross that level in the configured direction and ADX to be within its configured bounds. Optionally require +DI and -DI to be within configured ranges, +DI greater than -DI, and rising-range-volume confirmation (volume-range high increases while the candle closes above its open). Apply enabled entry filters: minimum volume, volume at or above its moving average, and RSI below its maximum. If both setups qualify, use the first setup. Once long, add safety-order longs when price falls to each configured adverse-price threshold, up to the configured limit, scaling order size and threshold spacing as configured. Close the position on any enabled exit: price crosses the selected Fibonacci stop border in the configured direction; a configured directional-index crossover occurs with the specified opposite-color prior candle; price crosses the selected Fibonacci take-profit border in the configured direction; or price reaches the configured profit percentage. For percentage or Fibonacci take-profit exits, if trailing is enabled, activate a trailing stop after the take-profit trigger and close when price reaches the highest tracked stop; otherwise close at the trigger.
