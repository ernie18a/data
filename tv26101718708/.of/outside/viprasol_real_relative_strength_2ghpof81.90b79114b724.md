For each benchmark, calculate the traded instrument's close change over a configurable lookback minus the benchmark's close change over the same lookback. Divide by the average of their ATRs over that lookback, multiplied by a configurable factor, and smooth the result with an EMA. If benchmark ATR is unavailable, substitute the instrument's ATR; if the resulting divisor is unavailable or nonpositive, use the instrument's ATR.

Signal bullish when the primary benchmark measure crosses above zero, and bearish when it crosses below zero. If second-benchmark confirmation is enabled, require its smoothed measure to be strictly positive for a bullish cross or strictly negative for a bearish cross. Require a configurable minimum number of bars since the last confirmed cross of either direction; only confirmed crosses reset this cooldown.

Also signal strong outperformance when the primary measure is at or above a configurable positive threshold, and strong underperformance when it is at or below the negative of that threshold. If divergence detection is enabled, compare consecutive confirmed pivot highs of the primary measure and the instrument's highs at those pivot bars: signal bearish divergence if the measure makes a lower high while price makes a higher high. Compare consecutive confirmed pivot lows and the instrument's lows at those bars: signal bullish divergence if the measure makes a higher low while price makes a lower low. Confirm each pivot after a configurable number of bars on each side. The framework specifies no explicit exit rule.
