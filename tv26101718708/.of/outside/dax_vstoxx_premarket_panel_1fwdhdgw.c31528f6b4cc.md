Use VSTOXX futures to determine the DAX directional bias. During the Frankfurt pre-open, compare the current VSTOXX close with its first available close of that hour; once the cash session opens, freeze this comparison using the first available VSTOXX close of the session. A decline beyond a configurable threshold signals bullish bias, and a rise beyond it signals bearish bias; a larger move produces a stronger bias. Otherwise the bias is neutral. Alert when the change crosses either threshold from the neutral side. During the cash session, compare the current VSTOXX close with its first available session close. A decline beyond the configurable bias threshold indicates bullish bias, with progressively stronger bullish trend classifications as it passes the trend and strong thresholds. A rise through the corresponding positive thresholds indicates bearish bias and progressively stronger bearish trend classifications. Values within the bias thresholds indicate range. Alert while the session change is in either the bullish or bearish trend zone. Reset the reference closes each day.
