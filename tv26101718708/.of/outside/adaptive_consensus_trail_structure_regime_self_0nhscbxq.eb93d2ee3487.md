When the traded instrument has not yet reported positive native volume and a proxy symbol is configured, use the proxy's same-timeframe volume; otherwise use native volume. On each bar, build separate support and resistance references from anchored volume-weighted price bands, the nearest developing or unvisited prior-session volume point of control, the latest fair-value-gap midpoint, the latest confirmed swing pivot, and the low or high of the strongest qualifying absorption bar. Estimate absorption from directional volume, optionally splitting volume by the standardized close-to-close move. Keep only enabled references on the appropriate side of the close.

Weight each reference type by the Wilson lower bound of its historical respect rate, using a neutral prior until enough observations exist and reducing accumulated counts when they reach their cap. Score ordinary references on fresh touches with an unambiguous same-bar rejection or break; score prior-session points of control by which of fixed ATR-scaled rejection and break barriers is touched first on subsequent bars, discarding ambiguous or timed-out events. For each side, select the reference-centered ATR-width neighborhood with the greatest total weight. Use its weighted median, or its member nearest price if configured, as consensus. Calculate confidence from the cluster's share of total weight, its tightness, average reliability, and regime quality, then reduce it for recent regime changes.

Determine regime quality from efficiency ratio, ADX, volatility clustering, recent regime changes, and optionally a rescaled-range Hurst estimate. A trend requires sufficient efficiency and ADX and, when enabled, no Hurst-indicated mean reversion; a volatility cluster prevents the trend from qualifying for the trail's trend treatment. Scale ATR band width by ATR percentile rank, inefficiency, and the non-trending regime. The fallback stop is an adaptive midpoint band, a recent-extreme chandelier band, or their average, as configured. If side confidence meets the structural threshold, instead use that side's consensus capped at a minimum ATR distance from the close.

Initialize direction long and the stop at the long target. In a long state, ratchet the stop upward to the greater of its previous value and the current long target; after the minimum holding period, flip short if the close falls below that stop by the ATR-scaled confirmation breach, enlarged by regime changes and a non-trending regime. On the flip, reset the stop to the short target. In a short state, ratchet the stop downward and flip long symmetrically when the close exceeds it by the confirmation breach, resetting the stop to the long target. The active direction is the long or short bias; stand aside when active-side confidence is below the trade threshold. For displayed buy or sell flip signals, additionally require the configured confidence threshold and, if enabled, matching direction of a higher-timeframe adaptive ATR trailing stop.
