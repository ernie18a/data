Using confirmed higher-timeframe data, calculate four valuation components: SOPR relative to 1, price divided by realized price, market cap divided by realized cap (using the selected ratio, z-score, or blend method), and price divided by its long-term moving average. Normalize each component as a rolling z-score, map it through a logistic scale, then average the available components with their configured weights and smooth the result into a 0–100 cycle score. Increase Bitcoin exposure in the bottom and cold valuation zones; reduce exposure in the hot and top zones. There is no short rule. On confirmed bars, flag a top extreme when the score crosses above its top threshold, a bottom extreme when it crosses below its bottom threshold, a hot rejection when it falls below the hot-zone boundary after being at or above it, and a cold reclaim when it rises above the cold-zone boundary after being at or below it. Also flag when the Mayer multiple crosses above its threshold.
