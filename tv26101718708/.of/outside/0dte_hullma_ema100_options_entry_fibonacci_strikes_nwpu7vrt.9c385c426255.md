On each bar, buy a PUT when the Hull moving average turns downward and, on that bar or within its configured confirmation window, the underlying closes below its exponential moving average. Buy a CALL when the Hull moving average turns upward and the underlying closes above its exponential moving average within the same window. Emit each signal only the first bar its conditions become true; ignore signals during the configured opening-bar exclusion and cooldown after a prior signal. There is no exit rule. At a signal, optionally calculate three candidate option strike prices by applying configurable fractions of the selected recent swing range in the signal direction; these are reference targets, not additional entry or exit conditions.
