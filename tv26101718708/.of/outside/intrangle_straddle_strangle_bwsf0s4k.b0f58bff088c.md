After the first trading hour, use its high and low to set option strikes: sell an at-the-money call and put when the underlying price approaches the midpoint between the rounded-up strike above the first-hour high and rounded-down strike below the first-hour low. Alternatively, sell an out-of-the-money call at the rounded-up strike above the first-hour high and an out-of-the-money put at the rounded-down strike below the first-hour low when price approaches that midpoint. Avoid the strangle if the first-hour range is not wide; if price does not approach the midpoint, stay lightly positioned. Adjust positions as price moves.
