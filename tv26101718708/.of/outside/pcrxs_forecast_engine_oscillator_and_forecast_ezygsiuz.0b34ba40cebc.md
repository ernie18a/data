Compute an oscillator as the ATR-normalized difference between fast and slow weighted moving averages, with bands at one standard deviation. Enter long when it crosses upward through the lower band and the higher-timeframe instrument trend is bullish (price above the faster EMA above the slower EMA), RSI is below its overbought level, the MACD histogram is nondecreasing, money flow is not strongly negative, volume is sufficient versus its average, and the halving-cycle filter permits longs. Enter short when it crosses downward through the upper band and the higher-timeframe trend is bearish, RSI is above its oversold level, the MACD histogram is nonincreasing, money flow is not strongly positive, volume is sufficient, and the cycle filter permits shorts. Exit a long when the oscillator crosses below zero, or when RSI crosses down from overbought while the higher-timeframe trend is bearish. Exit a short when the oscillator crosses above zero, or when RSI crosses up from oversold while the higher-timeframe trend is bullish. Mark an entry urgent when its confidence score is high, the market is trending, and volume is unusually elevated; confidence also reflects trend, macro-market direction, money flow, dominance, futures basis, and cycle alignment.
