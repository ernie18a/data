Calculate a price momentum oscillator as the difference between fast and slow weighted moving averages of close, divided by ATR when ATR is positive. Use its rolling standard deviation as the dynamic band. Determine higher-timeframe bullish bias when close is above the fast EMA and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA; bearish bias is the inverse. Compute a Bitcoin halving-cycle value as a cosine of elapsed wall-clock time since the reference halving, using a fixed cycle period and phase offset.

Signal long when the oscillator crosses above its negative standard-deviation band, provided the higher-timeframe bias is bullish, RSI is below its overbought threshold, the MACD histogram is nondecreasing, money flow is above its lower threshold, volume exceeds a fraction of average volume, and the halving-cycle value exceeds its lower threshold. Signal short when the oscillator crosses below its positive band, provided the higher-timeframe bias is bearish, RSI is above its oversold threshold, the MACD histogram is nonincreasing, money flow is below its upper threshold, volume exceeds the same volume filter, and the halving-cycle value is below its upper threshold.

Exit long when the oscillator crosses below zero; exit short when it crosses above zero. The additional RSI exit clauses require RSI simultaneously to be beyond a threshold and to cross back through that same threshold on the same bar, so they cannot trigger under ordinary crossover semantics.

Classify an entry as urgent when confidence reaches its threshold, ADX indicates a trend, and volume exceeds a higher fraction of its average. To calculate confidence, select the long or short agreement count according to whether the current linear-regression estimate of close exceeds its preceding-offset estimate. Count agreement across regression direction, higher-timeframe bias, equity-index and dollar-index positions relative to their EMAs, MACD histogram direction and acceleration, money-flow sign, halving-cycle sign, Bitcoin dominance relative to its EMA with the interpretation reversed for non-Bitcoin instruments, stablecoin dominance relative to its EMA, and Bitcoin futures basis relative to the traded instrument's close. For shorts, use the opposite tests as implemented, including logical negation for regression, equity-index and Bitcoin-dominance bullishness. Add bonuses for trending ADX, elevated volume, and stablecoin dominance near its rolling high while falling; round and cap the resulting percentage.
