Produce separate long-ready and short-ready signals when the checklist is enabled, at least one automatic rule is enabled, and all applicable enabled automatic rules pass. Emit an alert only when a side first becomes ready; manual confirmation prompts do not affect readiness. There is no exit rule.

Track confirmed hourly swing highs and lows. In primary mode retain one active swing per side, ignoring intermediate pivots until the active swing is breached; on a breach, re-anchor the opposite swing to the extreme of the intervening leg. Alternatively retain recent confirmed pivots. A fresh touch or breach of a swing low starts a long candidate; a swing high starts a short candidate. Record the level and the most extreme price reached. A confirmed close back above the low or below the high within the configured bar window validates the candidate. Expire it after the configured time, or cancel it if price subsequently exceeds its recorded sweep extreme. A new sweep replaces the candidate.

For each enabled rule, require: (1) a valid swing rejection on that side; (2) remaining daily range budget beyond the candidate's sweep extreme no greater than the configured points or budget percentage, using the current bar's low or high if no candidate exists; (3) when live gamma walls exist, a qualifying wall within the configured distance of that reference price, with exposure at least the configured fraction of the largest live wall on the same call or put side. Optionally restrict long candidates to put walls and short candidates to call walls. Skip the wall rule when no live walls exist; exclude expired same-day walls.

Calculate the daily budget from the configured historical completed-day range statistic, an implied move based on the matching external volatility index locked at the New York session open, or the larger of the two, with the configured override and historical fallback. Track the current session's high and low from that open. The lower budget boundary is session high minus budget; the upper boundary is session low plus budget. Long room is the reference low minus the lower boundary; short room is the upper boundary minus the reference high.
