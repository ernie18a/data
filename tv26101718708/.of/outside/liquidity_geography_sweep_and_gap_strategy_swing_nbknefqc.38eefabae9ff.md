When flat, evaluate the enabled entry modules; if both signal on the same bar, take the gap trade. Gap module: in a non-trending range, fade a sufficiently large opening gap—go long after a gap down or short after a gap up—with the prior close as the target and an ATR-based stop beyond the entry. Sweep module: identify the active high and low liquidity levels from either the latest confirmed swing pivots or prior rolling extremes. Go long when price trades below the active low and closes back above it; go short when price trades above the active high and closes back below it. Apply the configured regime filter (balance only, higher-timeframe trend aligned, adaptive balance-or-aligned, or off) and, if required, accept only when the swept level is near the matching prior-day extreme or a round-number level. Place the stop beyond the swept bar’s extreme with an ATR buffer. Target a risk multiple, or the opposite liquidity level when that option is enabled and the level lies beyond the entry. While a position is open, exit at its stop or target; optionally close all positions after a configured holding period.
