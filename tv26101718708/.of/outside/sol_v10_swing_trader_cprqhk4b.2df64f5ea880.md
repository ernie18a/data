Do not generate short signals. Classify stronger alignment when at least two of the fast/slow EMA, adaptive moving average, and OBV trend indicators are bullish. Report the highest-priority overlap when 3h and 4h signals coincide, then 2h and 3h, then a standalone 3h or 2h signal. On entry, set a fixed percentage profit target and fixed percentage stop loss according to the signal timeframe; close at market if neither is reached within the configured holding-time limit.
