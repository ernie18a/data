Within the configured trading window, compare the current-timeframe Donchian channel with the higher-timeframe channel. Set bullish bias when the current channel’s low is above the higher-timeframe channel midpoint; set bearish bias when its high is below that midpoint. In bullish bias, cancel pending short orders, close any short, and enter long on a break above the prior bar’s channel high. In bearish bias, cancel pending long orders, close any long, and enter short on a break below the prior bar’s channel low. The selected entry mode determines execution: market on the break, stop at the channel boundary, limit at that boundary after price crosses the channel midpoint, or stop-limit triggered by the break with an ATR-based offset limit. Close a long on a break below the prior bar’s channel low, or by a stop at the current channel low; close a short on a break above the prior bar’s channel high, or by a stop at the current channel high. The selected exit mode determines market versus stop exits. Optionally take partial profit at a risk/reward target measured in channel widths beyond the relevant boundary, advancing the target when price exceeds it. Allow direction and order settings to be configurable.
