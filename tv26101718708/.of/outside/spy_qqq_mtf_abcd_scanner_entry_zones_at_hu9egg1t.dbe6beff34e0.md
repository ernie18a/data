Track alternating swing pivots A–B–C. A bullish setup requires high–low–high pivots with C below A; project D below C by the A-to-B distance. A bearish setup requires low–high–low pivots with C above A; project D above C by that distance. Keep a setup only when the A–B move is large enough relative to ATR and the B–C retracement lies within configured bounds. When price reaches an ATR-sized zone around D, take a long for a bullish setup or a short for a bearish setup; the indicator also flags a rejection when the bar closes up for bullish or down for bearish. Place the stop beyond the far edge of the D zone, take partial profit at C, and final profit at A.
