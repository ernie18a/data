Compare the traded instrument with a correlated partner at matching reference levels: the previous bar’s high/low, a recent range extreme, a confirmed swing level, or the current session’s high/low. When the traded instrument sweeps a reference high by the required margin while the partner falls short of its own high by the required margin, signal short; when it sweeps a reference low while the partner falls short of its own low, signal long. Optionally allow the mirror case, where the partner sweeps and the traded instrument falls short. Skip unmatched or stale partner bars. A sweep occurring immediately after its level was set remains pending until a later bar retraces to the sweep bar’s extreme within the confirmation window; otherwise discard it. Cancel a signal if the partner also sweeps its corresponding level within the neutralization window.
