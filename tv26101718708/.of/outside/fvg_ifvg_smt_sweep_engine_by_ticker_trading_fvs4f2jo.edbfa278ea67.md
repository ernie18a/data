Compare the traded instrument with a distinct correlated instrument on the same timeframe. For each enabled reference source, independently use each instrument’s previous-bar high and low, recent closed-bar extremes, latest confirmed swing highs and lows, or current session extremes established before the current bar. Skip comparisons when required data is unavailable; optionally require matching bar timestamps. Evaluate on bar close or intrabar as configured, and update persistent reference levels only after closed-bar evaluation.

Signal short when the traded instrument’s high exceeds its own reference high by more than a configured penetration distance while the partner’s high remains more than a configured miss distance below its own reference high. Signal long when the traded instrument’s low falls below its own reference low by more than the penetration distance while the partner’s low remains more than the miss distance above its own reference low. Optionally also signal the same direction when the partner sweeps and the traded instrument misses. Express both distances in the traded instrument’s tick size. Signals fire immediately, including when pending confirmation.

Optionally mark a signal pending if the traded instrument’s reference was set on the immediately preceding bar, or require confirmation for every signal. A pending high-side signal confirms if a later traded-instrument high reaches the signal bar’s high; a pending low-side signal confirms if a later low reaches the signal bar’s low. Remove it if confirmation does not occur within the configured window. Optionally remove any signal if, within a separate window, the partner exceeds its saved high reference or falls below its saved low reference by more than the penetration distance, respectively. These later events do not generate new directional alerts. No exit or options decision is specified.
