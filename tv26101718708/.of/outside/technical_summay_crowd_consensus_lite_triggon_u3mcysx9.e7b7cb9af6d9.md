Calculate indicators from a selectable symbol and timeframe, which may differ from the traded instrument. Each enabled indicator casts one buy, sell, or neutral vote. Moving averages vote buy when source close is above the average and sell when below. MACD votes by its line’s position relative to its signal; the Awesome Oscillator, momentum, CCI, and close minus its EMA vote by their sign. RSI, Williams %R, and the Ultimate Oscillator vote sell at or above their upper extreme, buy at or below their lower extreme, and otherwise by their position relative to their midpoint. Stochastic and stochastic RSI vote sell at or above their upper extreme, buy at or below their lower extreme, and otherwise by their fast line’s position relative to its slow line. Equal comparisons produce neutral votes. Calculate consensus as the larger of buy and sell counts divided by all counted votes, including neutral votes; a buy/sell tie favors buy. Below a configurable neutral threshold, the reading is neutral; otherwise its direction is the dominant side.

On confirmed bars, maintain a daily directional phase only when the selected data timeframe is intraday. Reset it at the start of each chart day. The first nonneutral reading establishes the phase without trading; neutral readings leave it unchanged. A subsequent nonneutral reading on the opposite side flips the phase. On that flip only, enter in the new phase’s direction if consensus satisfies the configured at-least or at-most threshold and, when the optional EMA filter is enabled, the selected source candle’s low is strictly above its configurable EMA for a long or its high is strictly below it for a short. A qualifying opposite entry reverses the existing position; there are no other exits. Contrarian mode changes displayed labels only and does not reverse trades.
