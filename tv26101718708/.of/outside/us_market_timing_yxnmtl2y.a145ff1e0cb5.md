On or after the configured start date, enter a long position in the traded instrument when all of these hold: the S&P 500 daily low has stayed above its daily medium-term EMA for several consecutive sessions; the Nasdaq-100 ETF daily low is above both its short- and medium-term EMAs; its close is not simultaneously below each of the prior three closes; its broad-market trend-strength reading exceeds a threshold; and the S&P 500 open is at least as high as its close several sessions earlier. Do not enter while an early-loss lockout is active, unless the Nasdaq-100 ETF's medium-term average is above its long-term average. While holding, exit if either (1) the S&P 500 short-term average is below the prior session's long-term average, the S&P 500 closes below its short-term average, and the Nasdaq-100 ETF closes below its medium-term EMA, or (2) the ETF's low falls below a configured percentage stop from its price at entry and below its medium-term SMA. After an exit, activate the lockout if the ETF's medium-term average was below its long-term average at entry and the trade lasted no more than a configured short duration; clear the lockout on a later entry when that average relationship is favorable.
