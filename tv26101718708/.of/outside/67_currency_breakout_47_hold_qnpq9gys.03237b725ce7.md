For each major currency, assess its seven traded pairs against the previous completed session’s high and low during the current session. Count a pair as bullish for its base currency when it closes above that high, or bullish for its quote currency when it closes below that low; reverse these mappings for bearish signals. Signal the currency bullish or bearish when at least all but one of its seven pairs have confirmed the same direction and a majority remain closed outside their corresponding breakout levels in that direction. Emit at most one signal per currency per session.
