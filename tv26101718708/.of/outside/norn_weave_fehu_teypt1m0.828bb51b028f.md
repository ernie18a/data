On each bar, calculate the traded instrument’s EMA, ADX, ATR, and confirmed swing highs and lows. A swing is confirmed when the high or low a configured number of bars ago equals the extreme over a window extending that many bars on each side. Retain the latest confirmed swing levels. Maintain a trend state: set it bullish when the close exceeds the latest swing high, and bearish when the close falls below the latest swing low; otherwise retain its previous state. If both conditions occur, apply the bearish update last.

Optionally filter entries using the simple moving average of footprint buy volume minus sell volume for a selected external BTC or ETH market on the same timeframe. A positive average permits longs, a negative average permits shorts, and zero blocks both. If the filter is disabled or its data is unavailable, permit both directions.

Enter long when flat if the EMA is rising from its previous value, the trend is bullish, ADX exceeds its threshold, and the external filter permits longs. Enter short when flat under the symmetric conditions. An opposing signal closes the existing position; enter the new direction only when flat.

At the entry signal, fix three profit targets at successive multiples of the configured ATR distance from the signal close. When a bar’s high strictly exceeds a long target, or its low strictly falls below a short target, close the configured portion of the current position and retire that target. Each target can trigger once; multiple targets can trigger on the same bar.

Close the entire position if close-based percentage profit falls strictly below the configured loss threshold or the swing trend reverses. If break-even protection is enabled, activate it once close-based percentage profit reaches its threshold. Set its exit level at the actual entry price plus a small configured favorable buffer for a long, or minus that buffer for a short. Once active, close the entire long if the bar low reaches that level, or the entire short if the bar high reaches it. Reset the protection on exit and on a new entry. Submit these decisions as market orders on bar calculation.
