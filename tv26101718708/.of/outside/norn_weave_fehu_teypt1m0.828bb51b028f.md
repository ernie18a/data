Track the latest confirmed swing high and swing low. Set the market bias bullish when price closes above the latest swing high, and bearish when it closes below the latest swing low; retain the bias until the opposite break. Enter long when the bias is bullish, the moving average is rising, trend strength exceeds its threshold, and the selected external market’s smoothed order-flow delta is positive. Enter short when the bias is bearish, the moving average is falling, trend strength exceeds its threshold, and that delta is negative. If external delta data is unavailable or its filter is disabled, allow either direction through that filter. When flat or holding the opposite position, act on a qualifying entry; close an opposing position before reversing. While a position is open, close it if its unrealized percentage loss crosses the configured loss limit, if price reaches the active break-even stop after profit crosses its trigger, or if the swing bias reverses. Set the break-even stop near entry, on the profitable side. Also take three partial exits, each closing 30% of the position, when price reaches successive targets spaced at one, two, and three times an ATR-based distance from entry.
