On each bar, track the traded instrument’s opening-range high and low for the configured session. After the range ends, independently remember whether a close has broken above the high or below the low; both flags can become true and reset at the next regular-session open.

For each configured correlated instrument, calculate a positioning score from its close relative to five volume-weighted average prices: the current regular session, the completed or developing overnight session, the combined previous and current regular sessions, and periods anchored at the latest daily high and low. Add one for a close above each level, subtract one for below, and add zero for equality or an unavailable level. Add one for a close above its opening range, subtract one for below, and add zero inside or when unavailable. If correlation confirmation is enabled, a long requires the selected correlated instrument’s score to meet the positive threshold; a short requires it to meet the negative threshold. Apply the configured choice of the first, second, both, or either correlated instrument, regardless of display settings. Optionally apply the same score test to the traded instrument.

While flat, during the entry window and below the session trade limit, allow entries only on enabled weekdays and months, when the opening-range size passes any enabled points or prior completed daily ATR filter, and when any enabled account evaluation or daily profit halt permits trading. For a long, require long direction enabled, an earlier upside break, a close above the selected regular-session or continuously accumulating overnight-anchored VWAP, and all enabled long score confirmations. For a short, mirror these conditions and also enforce the configured short-only time, month and weekday restrictions. Place or refresh a buy limit at the selected VWAP plus the configured tick offset, or a sell limit at VWAP minus the offset; cancel the corresponding pending order whenever its conditions fail.

Compute the proposed stop from the configured fixed tick distance, prior completed daily ATR multiple, VWAP, opposite opening-range boundary, or recent retest swing, applying the configured buffer where applicable. Use the fixed-distance fallback when the required ATR or range boundary is unavailable. Require positive risk, an allowed stop-distance range, and at least one contract after fixed or risk-based sizing and the position cap. Set the target at the configured risk multiple, opening-range multiple, fixed distance, or no target. On fill, count the trade and fix initial risk as the distance from actual fill to the stored stop. Optionally move the stop to the fill price after a favorable move equal to the current entry-to-stop distance. Optionally trail from the best price since entry after the configured initial-risk activation threshold, at a fixed distance or initial-risk multiple, tightening only. When trailing and target removal are both enabled, omit the target immediately. Exit at the active stop or target, or close the position at the configured forced-flat time.
