On each bar, identify potential reversals using a repainting asymmetric smoothed envelope of highs and lows and, when enabled, a polynomial regression channel. Arm a short after a recent upper-boundary touch and a long after a recent lower-boundary touch. Suppress boundary-based shorts in a bullish trend and longs in a bearish trend. Determine the persistent trend state from a choppiness-derived Hurst proxy, ADX, RSI, directional movement, the EMA of lower-timeframe signed-volume changes, and price relative to its EMA; return to neutral when trend strength weakens or price crosses the EMA against the state, and use neutral on each state-change bar.

Independently arm either direction, overriding the trend gate, when a high-volume, unusually large-range candle has a sufficiently large rejection wick and an extreme price deviation from its EMA, or when price sweeps the latest confirmed local pivot, closes back through it in the reversal direction, and volume exceeds its average.

Validate an armed short when the close is at or above the smoothed-envelope midpoint and volume is unusually high with either a compressed body or a large upper wick; validate a long symmetrically below the midpoint with a compressed body or large lower wick. Lock each validation until price crosses the corresponding side of a volume-anomaly-anchored mean. A validated setup remains eligible for execution until that reset; pulse saturation does not block execution in the source logic.

After validation, enter short on any enabled, not-yet-fired confirmation: lower-timeframe cumulative signed volume does not increase, open interest decreases, the premium index standardized deviation crosses down from its positive extreme, stablecoin dominance does not decrease, or volume percentile collapses from a high to a low reading. Enter long on the symmetric signed-volume, premium, and dominance conditions, or on the same open-interest decrease or volume collapse. Each confirmation can fire once per direction until the setup resets. Lower-timeframe signed volume adds volume for an up candle, subtracts it for a down candle, and adds zero for an unchanged candle. Preserve previously recorded validation and confirmation signals when recalculating the repainting historical map.

Exit at the nearest still-unmitigated wick level from a previous validation: a long targets a prior bearish validation high and a short targets a prior bullish validation low. A level is mitigated once price touches it.
