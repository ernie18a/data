At each confirmed bar, capture the midpoint of the candle body crossing 9:29 a.m. New York time as the day's reference price. During the enabled morning session, signal long when price is stretched below that reference, closes above the prior swing highs on an up candle, and crosses above a rising moving-average trend line; signal short when price is stretched above the reference, closes below prior swing lows on a down candle, and crosses below a falling trend line. Require the trend direction to have been stable on the current and preceding bars, and enforce a cooldown between signals. Signals are emitted only when the session, stretch, structure-break, and trend conditions agree. The indicator also plots volatility-scaled stop and risk-multiple target levels after a signal, but emits no exit alerts.
