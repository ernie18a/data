Generate long and short signals through two independent rule sets. First, signal long when the fast moving average crosses above the medium moving average, and short when it crosses below. Require the candle to close in the signal direction, sufficient separation between the fast and medium averages and between the MACD line and its signal line, and—when enabled—price on the matching side of VWAP. Apply any enabled filters: MACD on the matching side of zero, ADX above its threshold with directional movement agreeing, RSI within the configured pullback zone, a close beyond the prior Donchian range, and sufficient relative volume. Second, signal long or short when price crosses the corresponding side of a volatility-adaptive trailing stop, with relative volume confirmation; optionally require a minimum price change, a swing-position filter, delayed confirmation, or price on the matching side of a trend line. Signals may be restricted to confirmed bar closes. For risk references after a trailing-stop signal, place a stop at the nearest applicable protective level among the trailing stop, trend line, VWAP, long moving average, and recent range boundary, with a volatility-based fallback and minimum risk distance; set enabled profit targets at configured multiples of that risk. These levels are visual planning references, not executed exits. The multi-timeframe table and composite scores provide context, not additional entry or exit triggers.
