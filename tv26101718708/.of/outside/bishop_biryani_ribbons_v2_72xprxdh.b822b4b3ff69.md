Classify the market as choppy when trend strength is below its configured threshold, except when it is rising sufficiently to qualify as an emerging trend. Otherwise, signal bullish when the selected moving averages are stacked from faster to slower and price closes above the slow trend average; signal bearish for the inverse stack and a close below it; classify other cases as mixed. A bullish pullback signal occurs when price reaches the configured touch average and closes back above the configured hold average during a bullish trend. Signal bearish pullbacks by the inverse conditions during a bearish trend. These are directional trend and pullback signals; the logic specifies no exit condition.
