Build session and higher-timeframe volume profiles using the traded instrument's prices and related futures volume when available; prefer futures-volume profiles over native tick-volume profiles when they diverge. Identify each profile's highest-volume price and value-area boundaries. Classify the regime before trading.

In balance, if the previous profile is bell-shaped with a centered highest-volume price and a value area stable across sessions, and price approaches the upper value-area boundary without increasing volume, short on rejection there. Exit at the highest-volume price or on a stop above the upper boundary with a buffer.

After price breaks above the previous upper boundary, remains above it for several candles, and the developing profile's highest-volume price forms above it, enter long on the first pullback to that boundary. Exit at the next significant higher-timeframe volume level or on a stop below the previous highest-volume price.

If price breaks below the lower boundary with a strong candle but returns inside the original value area without meaningful volume accumulating below it, enter long on the return. Exit at the highest-volume price, potentially extending the target to the opposite boundary.

When a higher-timeframe lower value-area boundary coincides with a developing lower-timeframe highest-volume price, use the higher-timeframe regime to direct a lower-timeframe entry: trade toward the higher-timeframe highest-volume price in balance, or in the direction of continuation toward the next relevant volume level in imbalance. Place the stop outside the lower-timeframe value area and target the higher-timeframe highest-volume price or opposite value-area boundary as appropriate to the regime.
