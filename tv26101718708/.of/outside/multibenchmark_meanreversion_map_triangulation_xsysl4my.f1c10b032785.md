For each tracked instrument, compare its price with a selected external benchmark and calculate a smoothed rolling z-score of that relative price. Signal bullish when the z-score crosses upward through the lower extreme threshold; signal bearish when it crosses downward through the upper extreme threshold. Keep each signal active only for a limited freshness window. Separately count how many of USD, SPY, and Gold show overextension or undervaluation; at least two agreeing benchmarks confirm the corresponding conviction. A rotation pair requires a fresh bearish signal with overextension conviction and a fresh bullish signal with undervaluation conviction; rank candidates by conviction, then by the magnitude of their primary-benchmark z-score. The indicator defines no exit rule.
