For each tracked instrument, divide its daily close by the selected benchmark: a fixed debased-dollar value, or the daily close of broad equities, gold, or Brent crude. Smooth the ratio with an EMA, standardize it using its rolling mean and standard deviation, then smooth the resulting z-score with another EMA. A zero standard deviation produces a zero z-score.

Generate a bullish reversal when the primary-benchmark z-score crosses upward through the negative extreme threshold, and a bearish reversal when it crosses downward through the positive extreme threshold. Keep the most recent signal active through the configured freshness window; a later cross replaces it.

For conviction, independently count how many of the dollar, broad-equity, and gold z-scores reach the overextension threshold and how many reach the undervaluation threshold. A rotation from one instrument into another requires a fresh bearish reversal with at least two overextension votes and a fresh bullish reversal with at least two undervaluation votes. Select each leg by the most matching votes, then the greatest absolute primary-benchmark z-score; retain the first instrument in row order on a tie. Suppress the rotation decision when the configured universe mixes stock and sector rows.
