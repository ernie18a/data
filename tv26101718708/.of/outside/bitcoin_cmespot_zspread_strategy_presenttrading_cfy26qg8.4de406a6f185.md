Mean-reversion framework that trades the chart instrument using a spread between two external instruments: a futures contract and a spot market. On each bar, take both instruments' closing prices at the chart's timeframe, without look-ahead. Define the spread as futures close minus spot close. Compute a rolling simple average and rolling population standard deviation of the spread over a fixed lookback window. Build an upper band as average plus a multiple of the standard deviation, and a lower band as average minus the same multiple. Go long on the bar where the spread crosses from at or above the lower band to below it, meaning the spread is abnormally low or oversold and expected to rise back. Go short on the bar where the spread crosses from at or below the upper band to above it, meaning the spread is abnormally high or overbought and expected to fall back. Act only on the crossing event, not while the spread merely stays outside a band. A new signal in the opposite direction reverses the existing position. Do not use any other filter, trend condition or external data to decide entries.
