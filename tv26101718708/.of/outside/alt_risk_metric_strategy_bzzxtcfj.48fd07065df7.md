On each chart bar, calculate a risk score using the selected altcoin/Bitcoin pair and optional external and chart-price adjustments. Use configurable periods, weights, thresholds and sizing parameters throughout.

For the base score, use weekly altcoin/Bitcoin data: calculate RSI, the fast EMA minus the slow EMA normalized over a rolling range and clamped to the indicator scale, and close's fractional deviation from a long-term SMA. Combine normalized MACD, RSI and shifted, scale-adjusted deviation with their respective weights.

Optionally add a fear-and-greed adjustment. Average the pair's percentage deviation from its long EMA and its percentage rate of change with two external weekly components: inverse percentage deviation of Bitcoin volatility from its EMA and Bitcoin dominance change, or, in traditional mode, the corresponding inverse VIX deviation and gold change. Include the pair's volume-weighted accumulation/distribution measure in the average when volume is nonzero. Smooth the average with a weekly running moving average, linearly normalize and clamp it to the indicator scale, then add its weighted deviation from neutral.

Optionally add the weighted deviation from neutral of the offset, SMA-smoothed daily US economic series, sampled on chart bars. The separately calculated dead-zone normalization is not used in the decision score.

Optionally add a chart-timeframe adjustment: center RSI at neutral, multiply it by an inverse sensitivity factor when ATR divided by close exceeds its moving average, add a signed boost at each configured RSI extreme, and EMA-smooth the result. Set it to zero inside its neutral zone, then add half of its weighted value.

EMA-smooth the combined score in the weekly altcoin/Bitcoin context, using gap-filled data without lookahead. On every chart bar at or below a buy threshold, enter or add to a long position: use the base cash amount at the first threshold, add the second amount at the deeper threshold, and add the third amount at the deepest threshold. Convert cash to units using chart close. Each buy resets all sell-stage flags.

When a long position exists, sell a configured percentage at each successive sell threshold. The first stage requires its flag to be unset; each later stage requires the preceding stage to have completed and its own flag to be unset. Evaluate sell conditions after any buy reset and before updating sell flags, so at most one stage advances per bar. Sales do not require a profit. There are no short entries.
