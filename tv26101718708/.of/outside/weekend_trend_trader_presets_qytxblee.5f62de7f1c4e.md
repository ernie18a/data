On weekly bars, enter long when the close exceeds the highest high of the preceding lookback period and rate of change over its lookback exceeds a threshold; enter only when flat. Track the highest high since entry and set a trailing stop below it, using a wider distance while a broad-market index is above its moving average and a tighter distance otherwise. Raise the stop when needed, never lower it, and exit when the close falls below it. The index trend affects the stop only, not entry.
