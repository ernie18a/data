Go long when the instrument’s monthly close is above a rising monthly moving average, its monthly RSI exceeds a bullish threshold, its price relative to a benchmark is above its own moving average and rising, and the current bar makes both a higher swing high and a higher swing low than the prior lookback range. Mark the long as stronger when, in addition, recent volume on up bars exceeds recent volume on down bars and the current bullish bar’s volume exceeds its average. Otherwise avoid a long. No short or exit rule.
