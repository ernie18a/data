Compute the stock’s weighted multi-horizon price performance relative to an equal-weighted benchmark of the relevant market indices, emphasizing recent performance. Count recent down days whose volume exceeds its moving average by a chosen factor. Classify the setup as strong when relative performance exceeds a strength threshold and high-volume down days remain below the allowed maximum; classify it as watchlist quality when relative performance is positive but below that threshold, is negative but its smoothed value is improving, or is strong with the high-volume count exactly at the allowed maximum, provided the count does not exceed the maximum. Otherwise classify it as weak. The indicator provides screening and setup status, not explicit long, short, or exit decisions.
