Use open interest when available, otherwise optionally use a volume-based participation proxy. Compare its recent change with its historical norm, and assess price movement relative to volatility, trend direction, persistence, and relative volume. Assign Long Build when participation expands unusually, price advances with an uptrend, and the combined commitment score clears its activation threshold. Assign Short Build when participation expands unusually, price falls with a downtrend, and the score clears the threshold. Assign OI Unwind when participation contracts unusually while price moves materially and the score clears the threshold. Assign Absorption when participation expands unusually but price remains compressed and the score clears the threshold. Otherwise assign Reset; if neither participation source is usable, assign Data Missing. These are context labels and alerts, not explicit trade or exit instructions.
