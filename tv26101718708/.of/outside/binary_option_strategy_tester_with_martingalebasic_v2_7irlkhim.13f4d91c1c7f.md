On each bar, calculate a fast EMA of the rolling highest value of the average of low and close, and a slow EMA of the rolling lowest value of the average of high and close. Calculate a reference as twice an EMA of close minus an EMA of that EMA. Compare close with the reference: if close is higher, select the larger of the two high/low-based EMAs; otherwise select the smaller. A call signal requires close above the reference, the selected value above the bar's low, and its latest change greater than its preceding change. A put signal requires close below the reference, the selected value below the bar's high, and its latest decrease greater than its preceding decrease.

When a signal is permitted by the selected option direction and trading session, latch that direction and clear the other direction's latch. The latches otherwise persist, including outside the session. If a direction's latch has just changed from false to true and no trade sequence is active, buy that binary option at the next bar's open, with expiry at that bar's close. Ignore new entries while a sequence is active. A call wins if the expiry close exceeds the open; a put wins if it is below the open. Equality is a loss.

After a loss, continue on the next bar until a win or the configured maximum number of attempts. For each retry, select the original direction, its opposite, the direction of the previous candle, or the opposite of that candle, according to the configured mode. A previous candle is treated as upward only when its close exceeds its open; otherwise it is treated as downward. A retry wins only if its expiry close moves strictly in the selected direction from its open; equality is a loss. End the sequence on a win or after the final loss and clear both direction latches.
