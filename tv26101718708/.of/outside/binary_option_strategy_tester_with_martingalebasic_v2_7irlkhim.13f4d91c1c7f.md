During the selected trading session, generate a CALL when the close is above a baseline formed by subtracting a further-smoothed short EMA of the close from twice the short EMA of the close, the signal line is above the low, and the signal line’s upward change is greater than its prior upward change. The signal line is the higher of two smoothed channel bounds when the close is above the baseline: one bound smooths rolling highs of the average of low and close; the other smooths rolling lows of the average of high and close. Generate a PUT when the close is below the baseline, the signal line is below the high, and its downward change is greater than its prior downward change; use the lower channel bound when the close is not above the baseline. Allow calls, puts, or both according to the direction setting. Enter on the bar after a signal, only when no option is active, and ignore subsequent entries until the active option expires.
