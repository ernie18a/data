Signal a long setup when the instrument is above its moving average and in a tight recent price range, relative strength versus a benchmark is contracting in volatility and has not weakened versus its earlier reading, and the instrument’s daily RSI reached the required strength threshold within the recent lookback. Classify the setup as a value-zone entry when relative strength is within the defined value band, or as a momentum entry when it is above that band. No short or exit condition is defined.
