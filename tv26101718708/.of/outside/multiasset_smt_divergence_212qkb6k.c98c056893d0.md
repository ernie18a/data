Use same-timeframe data for the traded instrument and configured comparison instruments, each marked as normally or inversely correlated. Select comparisons manually or automatically from the instrument's related equity index, metals, currency, cryptocurrency, energy, or Treasury group, using futures comparisons where applicable.

Run independent swing-detection tiers with configurable lookbacks. A swing is confirmed using bars on both sides; optionally detect the current swing without waiting for right-side confirmation, while retaining fully confirmed swings as history. Compare each instrument's high or low at the traded instrument's swing bar, not at an independently detected comparison-instrument swing. Evaluate a short signal at a swing high and a long signal at a swing low. Optionally require the swing candle to close below its open for a short or above its open for a long.

In sweep mode, maintain a separate stack of confirmed traded-instrument swing highs and lows for each tier and comparison instrument, recording the comparison instrument's corresponding high or low with each entry. At a new swing high, pop consecutive entries from the newest end while their traded-instrument highs are strictly lower than the new high; compare with the last entry popped. Signal short if the comparison high is strictly lower than its recorded high for a normal pair, or strictly higher for an inverse pair. At a new swing low, similarly pop consecutive entries while their traded-instrument lows are strictly higher than the new low. Signal long if the comparison low is strictly higher than its recorded low for a normal pair, or strictly lower for an inverse pair. Remove swept entries even when candle validation or comparison fails. Process detection before adding newly confirmed swings to history, and evaluate sweep mode on confirmed bars.

In the alternative direct-comparison mode, compare the new swing with the most recent previously confirmed swing of the same type. For a high, compare the Boolean results of whether the traded high and comparison high each strictly increased; for a low, compare whether their lows each strictly decreased. Signal short at a high or long at a low when the Booleans differ for a normal pair or match for an inverse pair. Update confirmed history after detection. A qualifying divergence with any enabled comparison instrument produces the corresponding signal. No exit rule is specified.
