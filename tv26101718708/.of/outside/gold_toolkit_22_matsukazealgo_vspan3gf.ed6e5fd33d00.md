Evaluate signals only for enabled modules. When the shared session filter is on, allow signals only in enabled trading sessions; bullish signals from modules that use the Dollar Index require it to be falling for the day, and bearish signals require it to be rising. If the index is unavailable or its filter is disabled, those checks pass. Apply each module’s configured cooldown, spacing, and confirmation rules.

- Supertrend: signal long or short when its direction flips, respectively, provided the corresponding RSI extreme occurred recently; apply the optional volume surge filter. Adapt the band width by session based on that session’s historical flip outcomes: widen after a win rate at or below half and narrow above half.
- Stop run: after price pierces a recent range low or high with a sufficiently large wick, score the event using wick size, penetration, relative volume, Dollar Index alignment, and proximity to a round price level. Within the confirmation window, signal long after a bullish reclaim of the low boundary or short after a bearish rejection of the high boundary, only if the score meets its threshold and Dollar Index alignment agrees.
- EMA inversion: require price beyond all three trend averages with adequate separation, then a retest of the fast average. Signal short when a tracked bullish gap later closes through its lower edge while the bearish setup remains active; signal long on the mirrored bearish-gap failure during an active bullish setup. Enter only when flat and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed. Exit an open position at its swing-based stop or projected equal-risk target.
- Parabolic trend: signal in the new direction when price crosses the parabolic stop and the session is allowed.
- Coral trend: signal long when price crosses above the smoothed trend and closes above its upper risk band; signal short on the inverse cross below the lower band. Require an allowed session and aligned Dollar Index.
- Arc momentum: signal when the smoothed RSI arc changes direction, long on an upward turn and short on a downward turn, subject to session and Dollar Index alignment.
- Swing regression: signal long or short on a change of character when the prior swing high or low is broken in the opposite structural state, subject to the session filter.
- Anchored channels: signal a breakout through the outer boundary against the current macro direction; a pullback at the macro channel boundary in its direction; or a micro-channel boundary break aligned with the macro direction during the early portion of a segment. Require channel visibility/quality conditions where applicable, session eligibility, and separate signal cooldowns.
- Support and resistance: signal long when price tests a live support zone and closes bullish within it; signal short when it tests live resistance and closes bearish within it. Require the session filter and avoid recently signaled nearby zones.
- Harmonic patterns: signal long or short when the latest alternating swing pivots complete a recognized bullish or bearish Fibonacci pattern, respectively, and the session is allowed. Avoid signaling a recently tracked pattern of the same direction.
- Fair value gaps: signal in the gap’s direction when a qualifying bullish or bearish gap forms during an allowed session.
- Institutional volume: signal long when enough high-volume bullish accumulation candles cluster, their bullish share clears its threshold, and price is above its long-term average; signal short on the mirrored bearish distribution condition below that average. Require an allowed session.
- RSI swing structure: at an RSI-defined overbought swing high, signal long when the higher-high/lower-high classification changes character against the prior structural bias; at an oversold swing low, signal short when the higher-low/lower-low classification changes character against that bias. Require the session filter.
- Order blocks: signal short when a bearish displacement follows a pivot high and qualifies under the zone score threshold; signal long on the corresponding bullish displacement after a pivot low. The score reflects trend, session, Dollar Index, and round-level confluence.
- Vector structure: signal long when a high-participation bearish candle sweeps a prior swing low and the next candle reverses upward; signal short on the mirrored swing-high sweep and downward reversal. Require an allowed session.
- VWAP: signal long when price crosses above the anchored volume-weighted average and short when it crosses below, subject to the session filter.

Session ranges, killzones, prior-day levels, whale candles, mini-chart divergences, and swing TPO produce analysis or markings but do not generate traded-instrument long, short, or exit decisions in this script.
