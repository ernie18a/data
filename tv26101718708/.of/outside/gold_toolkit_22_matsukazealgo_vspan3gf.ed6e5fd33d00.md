Use independently enabled signal modules. Apply the configured session filter on intraday bars. Where a dollar filter is specified, bypass it if the dollar feed is disabled or unavailable; otherwise permit long when the daily Dollar Index close is at or below its open and short when it is above its open.

- Adaptive trend: Go long on an upward ATR-band trend flip when RSI was recently oversold, the signal-spacing requirement is met, the optional volume-surge filter passes, and the dollar filter permits long. Go short on the corresponding downward flip with recent overbought RSI and short dollar permission. Adapt the band multiplier by session from the cumulative outcomes of sufficiently aged prior flips: tighten it when the session win rate exceeds its benchmark and widen it otherwise, within configured bounds.
- Stop-run reversal: On a confirmed bar, detect a sufficiently deep breach of the prior range low with a large lower wick, or the symmetric breach of the range high with a large upper wick. Score the setup using wick proportion, ATR-normalized penetration, relative volume, round-number proximity, and favorable dollar direction. Store a sufficiently scored setup, replacing any existing pending setup. Within the confirmation window, go long if price closes back above the breached low with sufficient body and high close location, the stored score meets the final threshold, and the dollar filter permits long; use symmetric conditions for a short. Expire unconfirmed setups.
- Smoothed trend: Go long when close crosses above the EMA of a weighted moving average and also exceeds its upper standard-deviation risk band, subject to long dollar permission. Go short on the symmetric lower crossover and short dollar permission. Narrow the risk bands near enabled round-number levels.
- Momentum arc: Smooth centered RSI with an EMA and measure its rolling standard deviation. Maintain an accelerating arc initialized below momentum; reverse its direction when momentum crosses beyond a buffer on the other side, reset the arc to the corresponding offset from momentum, and reset its velocity. Advance the arc at the configured interval, using separate Asian-session settings. Go long on an upward arc-direction flip with long dollar permission, and short on a downward flip with short dollar permission.
- Asymmetric trend: Maintain a trailing line and direction using close volatility as the movement threshold, with a reduced threshold near enabled round-number levels and a session-dependent continuation factor. In a rising state, advance the line after sufficient upward movement and flip down only after close falls beyond the full threshold; apply the symmetric rules in a falling state. Go long on a down-to-up flip with long dollar permission, and short on an up-to-down flip with short dollar permission.
- Order-block scoring: At a confirmed pivot high, find the nearest earlier bullish candle in the configured search window. If the subsequent downward displacement from that candle exceeds the ATR threshold, score the candidate using trend alignment, a fixed location component, session, favorable dollar direction, and round-number proximity. Go short if the score reaches the threshold. At a confirmed pivot low, use the nearest earlier bearish candle and symmetric upward displacement and trend/dollar alignment to score a long candidate; session scoring differs by direction. Dollar alignment adds to the score rather than acting as a mandatory filter.
