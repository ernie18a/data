Train two online logistic regression models on the chart instrument, then apply their shared weights to each scanned instrument. Compute six features for each instrument: the hyperbolic tangent of the fast-minus-slow closing-price exponential averages divided by ATR; centered and normalized RSI; one minus the ratio of short- to long-window average bar range; ATR divided by its moving average minus one; volume divided by its moving average minus one; and the close minus the midpoint of the recent high-low range, divided by ATR. Clamp each feature to its configured bounds and protect denominators from zero.

Initialize both models' intercepts and weights to zero. On each confirmed chart bar after the training horizon, use the chart features from that many bars ago. Label the opportunity model positive when the absolute close-to-close change over that horizon divided by current ATR reaches the configured move threshold. Label the direction model positive when the current close exceeds the earlier close. Update each model by one logistic-gradient step, applying L2 regularization to feature weights but not the intercept.

On the selected scan timeframe, score each nonblank instrument with both models, using data without lookahead. Classify it as a long candidate when its direction probability is at least the midpoint; otherwise classify it as a short candidate. Apply any selected direction filter and rank remaining candidates by descending opportunity probability. The models train from the chart instrument even when scoring other instruments. No exit condition is defined.
