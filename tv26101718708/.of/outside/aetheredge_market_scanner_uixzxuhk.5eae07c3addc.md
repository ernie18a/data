On each confirmed bar, train two online logistic classifiers on lagged chart-symbol observations: one predicts whether the absolute forward price move reaches a configurable ATR multiple within a configurable horizon, and the other predicts whether the forward price is higher. Train from ATR-normalized features comprising fast-versus-slow EMA spread, RSI, recent range compression, ATR regime, volume surge, and close position within the recent high-low range. Apply the learned classifiers to those features for each scanned instrument. Rank instruments by predicted move probability; classify as long when predicted up probability is at least one half, otherwise short. An optional direction filter limits displayed candidates, and a configurable threshold can alert when a new top-ranked instrument qualifies.
