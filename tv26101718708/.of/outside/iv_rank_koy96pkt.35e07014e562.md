Measure current volatility (default: annualized standard deviation of log returns; optionally use an external implied-volatility series) and rank it within its own rolling historical range. When the rank is in the low-volatility zone, favor buying options or other long-vega positions. When it is in the high-volatility zone, favor selling option premium or other short-vega positions. In the middle zone, make no volatility-based options trade and favor directional strategies. A sharp rank fall after previously elevated volatility signals a volatility crush; a sharp rise from previously low volatility signals expansion, but neither signal specifies an additional trade.
