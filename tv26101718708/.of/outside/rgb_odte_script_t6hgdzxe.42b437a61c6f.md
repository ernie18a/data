On each bar, calculate the prior completed daily average absolute candle body over the selected lookback. Multiply it by the selected buffer and optionally halve it to obtain the range distance. Require the average body to be available and positive and the distance to meet the minimum. Also require every enabled filter to pass: the current volatility index must be at or below its maximum and, separately, at or below its prior completed daily moving average; these two filters do not apply to futures or crypto presets. The prior completed daily ATR divided by current price must be at or below its percentage limit; daily ATR divided by average body must be at or below its limit; the absolute spread between the chart's fast and slow EMAs must be at or below the selected multiple of daily ATR; the external market's fast EMA must be at least its slow EMA; and the current bar time in the selected time zone must fall within the inclusive entry window. Disabled filters pass automatically. Presets determine which filters are enabled and their settings.

If any required condition fails, indicate no trade. Otherwise, calculate a daily anchored VWAP on the selected confirmation timeframe as cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting each day and substituting unit volume when volume is missing or nonpositive. Compare the confirmation close with that VWAP plus and minus a neutral buffer expressed as a percentage of the confirmation close. If VWAP selection is disabled or the close is above the upper buffer, sell a put spread. If the close is below the lower buffer, sell a call spread. Otherwise sell both spreads as an iron condor.

For options presets, set the short call strike to current close plus range distance rounded up to the strike increment, and the short put strike to current close minus range distance rounded down. Buy the call hedge at a configured number of increments above the short call and the put hedge the same number below the short put. For futures and crypto presets, round both range levels to the nearest increment instead. The script indicates setups; it does not specify exits.
