Use the traded instrument and a paired instrument to fit a rolling linear relationship. Test candidate lookback windows in order and use the first whose price spread passes a stationarity test. Set the expected traded price from that regression and form upper and lower bands by adding or subtracting its residual standard error. Enter long when the traded price crosses up through the lower band; exit long when it crosses up through the expected price. Enter short when it crosses down through the upper band; exit short when it crosses down through the expected price.
