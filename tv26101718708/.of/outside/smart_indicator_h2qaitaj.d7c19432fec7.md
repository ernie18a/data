On each chart bar, calculate separate long and short confluence scores. Award one unit for each applicable condition: persistent higher-timeframe sweep bias agrees; an enabled trading session is active; volume exceeds its moving-average threshold; the comparison instrument sweeps the corresponding prior extreme and reverses; the traded instrument outperforms the comparison instrument for long or underperforms for short over the relative-strength lookback; a matching hourly price gap exists without a sufficiently larger nearby opposing daily gap; a matching daily gap exists; price is near the previous day's low for long or high for short; the volatility index falls for long or rises for short; repeated same-direction sweeps have accumulated; and price is near a matching structure level. Award twice that unit each for a matching volume-backed engulfing candle at structure and for such a candle closing inside a recent matching chart-timeframe gap. Cap each score at the configured maximum. Comparison-instrument sweep and volatility-index contributions respect their respective enable switches; relative strength is always included.

A low sweep occurs when the preceding bar's low reaches or breaks the lowest low of the preceding lookback window and the current close is above that low; a high sweep is the symmetric condition. Define higher-timeframe and comparison-instrument sweeps analogously using their requested series. Higher-timeframe bias persists after a sweep until an opposite sweep; if both occur together, the low sweep takes precedence. Reset each directional sweep count when price reaches the opposite rolling swing extreme, then increment it on its corresponding sweep and clear the other count; process high sweeps after low sweeps.

A bullish gap has the later bar's low above the earlier bar's high by at least the applicable price-relative minimum; bearish is symmetric. For hourly and daily gaps use the two preceding bars of that timeframe, and for chart-timeframe gaps compare the current bar with the bar two bars earlier. Keep the latest rounded hourly and daily gap midpoints for proximity checks. A matching structure level is the previous day's directional extreme, daily or weekly open, or the latest matching hourly or daily gap midpoint, within its applicable ATR distance. A bullish engulfing candle has a sufficiently large bullish body relative to ATR, closes sufficiently high within its range, opens below the preceding close and closes above the preceding open, and has a volume spike; reverse these conditions for bearish. A gap entry additionally requires the close inside the latest matching chart-timeframe gap within its age limit; consume that gap when an entry occurs.

Emit a long setup on a low sweep when the long score reaches the configured minimum, and a short setup on a high sweep when the short score reaches it. For a directional verdict, require that side to have the strictly higher score and meet either the stronger score threshold or the lower threshold together with the minimum score advantage. Issue the matching verdict on its corresponding sweep, except that a current bullish gap combined with a high sweep or bearish gap combined with a low sweep produces a wait verdict. Classify qualifying scores into the configured strong, valid, and marginal bands; distinguish a volume-backed scale-in in the valid band. On an opposite sweep, issue a fade toward the dominant side if its score reaches the fade threshold. Also emit contrarian and maximum-confluence directional alerts when their respective score thresholds are met. These alerts are separate outputs and can coincide.

For a qualifying setup, display a stop beyond the swept prior extreme by an ATR allowance and a target at the corresponding rolling swing extreme. Independently track the latest sweep's stop: a close beyond it invalidates that directional setup. The score-dependent setup and verdict use external comparison-instrument and volatility data; the other standalone entry alerts do not.
