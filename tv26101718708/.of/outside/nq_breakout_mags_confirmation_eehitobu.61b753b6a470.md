Before the trading session, form reference levels from prior weekly and daily highs and lows and recent hourly swing highs and lows, combining nearby levels. Set directional bias from hourly structure: rising swing highs and lows permits longs; falling swing highs and lows permits shorts; mixed structure permits either. For each reference level, consider a long when the traded instrument crosses above it by a configured buffer, or a short when it crosses below by that buffer. Require the configured session, available daily trade capacity, and no open position. Optionally require a related market's structure bias, a breakout of its own reference level in the same direction, or both to agree; otherwise filter out the entry. Place the stop beyond the breakout candle, subject to configured minimum and maximum risk rules, and set the target at a configured multiple of that risk. Exit when price reaches the stop or target; if both are reached in one bar, count the stop as the exit.
