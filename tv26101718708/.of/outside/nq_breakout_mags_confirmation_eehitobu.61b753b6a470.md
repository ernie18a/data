At a configurable New York time each day, snapshot the traded instrument’s enabled previous-week and previous-day highs and lows and recent confirmed hourly swing highs and lows. Retain levels within a configurable distance of the current close, merging nearby levels in insertion order and keeping the first price. Snapshot the confirmation instrument’s previous-day highs and lows and recent confirmed hourly swings, retaining nearby levels and deduplicating them by distance relative to its current close. Reset the daily trade count and confirmation breakout direction.

Determine each instrument’s snapshot bias from its two latest confirmed hourly swing highs and lows: bullish if both latest values are higher, bearish if neither is higher, otherwise neutral; insufficient swings are neutral. The traded instrument’s bias may instead be manually set to bullish, bearish, or neutral. After the snapshot, track the confirmation instrument’s latest close crossing beyond either side of any of its levels by a configurable percentage; the latest crossing direction persists until another crossing or the next day’s reset. If several cross on the same bar, the last level processed wins.

During the configured trading window, while flat and below the daily trade limit, scan unused traded-instrument levels in order. A long breakout occurs when the close crosses above a level plus a configurable buffer; a short breakout occurs when the close crosses below the level minus the buffer. Alternatively, use high and low crossings and allow intrabar signals. A long requires a non-bearish traded-instrument bias; a short requires a non-bullish bias. Depending on the selected confirmation mode, also require a non-opposing confirmation-instrument bias, a latest confirmation breakout in the trade direction, both, or neither. Neutral bias permits either direction. Mark a level used when a raw breakout occurs even if confirmation rejects it; continue scanning after a rejection.

For an accepted breakout, mark the level used. Enter at the close in close-based mode; in wick mode, enter at the higher of the open and upper threshold for a long, or the lower of the open and lower threshold for a short. Set initial risk to the distance from entry to the breakout candle’s low for a long or high for a short. If risk exceeds the configured maximum, either skip the trade or cap risk, according to the selected mode; then apply the configured minimum risk. A skipped trade still consumes the level and ends the scan for that bar. Otherwise place a stop one risk unit against the position and a target at the configured reward multiple in its favor, and increment the daily trade count.

On subsequent bars, exit when the bar reaches the stop or target; if both are reached on the same bar, treat the stop as hit. After an exit, another entry may occur on that bar if all entry conditions permit. Keep open positions across daily resets.
