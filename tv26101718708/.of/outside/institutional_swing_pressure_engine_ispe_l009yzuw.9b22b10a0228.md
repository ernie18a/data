Compute bullish and bearish pressure as weighted combinations of volatility compression, directional price-volume flow, relative strength against a market benchmark and optionally a sector benchmark, price structure, and confirmed higher-timeframe trend. Normalize the underlying measures against their rolling historical distributions. Use relative weakness for bearish pressure. When bullish pressure exceeds bearish pressure and reaches the pressure threshold, present a long trade setup with an entry zone around prior resistance, a stop below the recent swing low adjusted for volatility, and targets based on entry-to-stop risk. When bearish pressure is at least as large as bullish pressure and reaches the threshold, present a short setup with an entry zone around prior support, a stop above the recent swing high adjusted for volatility, and risk-based targets. These are reference levels; the indicator does not execute trades or specify that reaching a level triggers an entry or exit.
