Build a macro score from global liquidity momentum phases, credit-spread direction, real-yield direction, dollar direction, copper-to-gold direction, yield-curve inversion, and volatility conditions. Treat expanding or recovering liquidity, falling credit spreads and real yields, a weakening dollar, a rising copper-to-gold ratio, a non-inverted curve, and calm volatility as bullish contributions; treat their adverse counterparts as bearish contributions, with liquidity phases, inversion, and panic volatility carrying their configured relative weights. Compare the combined score with its bullish boundary and compare the traded instrument’s price with its long-term moving average. When the score is bullish, signal long whether price is above trend or below it (the latter is a buy-the-dip state). When the score is not bullish and price is above trend, signal caution or exit long exposure; when the score is not bullish and price is below trend, signal bearish exposure or exit longs.
