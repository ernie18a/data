Build a momentum score from trend alignment, above-average volume and rising volume flow, RSI strength, performance versus a broad-market benchmark, and proximity to the instrument’s recent yearly high. Signal BUY when either a bullish price crossover with strong volume and momentum, or a price breakout on unusually strong volume, occurs and the score clears its buy cutoff. Signal SELL when price is below its long-term trend average, the trend averages are bearishly ordered, and the score is below its sell cutoff. Otherwise signal REDUCE when price has remained below its intermediate trend average across the current and a prior comparison point; signal ACCUMULATE when the score clears its accumulation cutoff; default to HOLD. Apply this priority: SELL, REDUCE, BUY, ACCUMULATE, HOLD. A sharp multi-session price fall adds a warning to the displayed signal but does not change the trade decision.
