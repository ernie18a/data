On confirmed intraday bars during the configured New York session, calculate a composite from five configurable external instruments. For each valid leg, divide the close's signed position within the high-low range by the range, scale it by capped relative volume using a session-gated running volume average, and reverse the sign of the inverse-hedge leg. Take the weighted average of valid, positive-weight legs, smooth it exponentially, and standardize it against a rolling window of valid smoothed values. Require the configured minimum number of active legs and a valid standardized value. Calculate directional breadth as the fraction of active weight whose current signed leg flow agrees with the direction. Calculate RSI in a separate configurable instrument's own bar sequence. When the optional RSI filter is enabled, bullish agreement requires RSI below its lower band, rising from below that band, or between the midpoint and upper band; bearish agreement requires RSI above its upper band, falling from above that band, or between the lower band and midpoint. A long agreement occurs when standardized flow is positive and rising, bullish breadth meets its threshold, and the bullish RSI condition passes. A short agreement is the mirror: negative and falling flow, sufficient bearish breadth, and the bearish RSI condition. Emit an agreement only on the first bar of a new qualifying state, subject to a separate per-direction cooldown and the agreement-marker setting. The dashboard labels a qualifying agreement Long or Short on its emitted bar and briefly thereafter while the state remains true. Divergences are warnings, not entries; no exit rule is specified.
