On each closed candle, calculate a Chandelier Exit from a rolling extreme and ATR, with its trailing lines moving only in the favorable direction; a close crossing the prior opposite line flips direction and creates a long or short signal. Also calculate a zero-lag least-squares moving average. Run each strategy independently, allowing only one open trade at a time: (A) long only: after a long signal, enter on the first close above the average; exit on a close below it. (B) enter on a long signal when the close is above the average, or a short signal when below; exit on the opposite Chandelier signal. (C) use the same signal-and-average entry filter in both directions; exit at the configured risk-multiple target. (D) long only: after a long signal, enter when a bullish candle crosses up through the average; ignore a signal if price is already above it; exit at the configured risk-multiple target. (E) use the signal-and-average entry filter in both directions; exit on an opposite signal, or on a close back across the average when the trade is profitable. (F) enter on every Chandelier direction flip in either direction; use the Chandelier line as the trailing stop and exit on the opposite signal. For strategies using a separate stop, place it at the selected recent swing extreme, Chandelier line, or moving average, keeping at least the configured minimum ATR-based distance from entry; define one unit of risk as the entry-to-stop distance and set any target at its configured multiple of that risk. Evaluate exits before entries, and check the stop before a target if both are touched on one candle. Signals may use regular or Heikin Ashi candles; trade prices use real candle prices unless Heikin Ashi pricing is selected.
