On each confirmed chart-bar close, assess gold using the dollar index, the US 10-year yield, and oil. Standardize each instrument's latest close-to-close change against the mean and standard deviation of its changes over a configurable lookback, separately for each timeframe.

Estimate each external market's relationship with gold using Pearson correlation of paired, nonzero completed hourly changes over a bounded history, requiring a minimum sample; fall back to the equivalent shorter-timeframe correlation when the hourly correlation is unavailable. If the correlation is sufficiently strong, use its sign as the market's directional multiplier. Otherwise use the default inverse relationship for the dollar, the configured prior direction for the yield, and no oil contribution. Separately maintain an unstable state from the shorter-timeframe dollar correlation: switch it on at an upper positive threshold and off at a lower threshold; retain its previous state between them.

Multiply each external market's standardized one-minute change by its directional multiplier. A market is active when the absolute adjusted change meets the configured movement threshold; oil must also have a nonzero multiplier. If both dollar and yield are active with opposite signs, suppress the trade. Otherwise choose the direction from the first active market in dollar, yield, oil priority order. Calculate the weighted average of active markets that agree with that direction, and subtract gold's standardized one-minute change. Room is the resulting difference only if it has the chosen direction; otherwise room is zero.

Independently determine the external-market direction on the five-minute and fifteen-minute timeframes using their own standardized changes and a separate activity threshold. Apply the same dollar-versus-yield split rule and priority order. Suppress a trade if either timeframe has a nonzero direction opposite to the one-minute direction.

Signal long or short when the corresponding direction exists, room meets its configured minimum, the higher-timeframe filter does not oppose it, and the unstable state is off. Otherwise give no entry signal. Entry alerts fire only on a transition into the respective signal, on a confirmed chart bar, within the optional configured session and after the shared alert cooldown. There is no explicit exit rule.
