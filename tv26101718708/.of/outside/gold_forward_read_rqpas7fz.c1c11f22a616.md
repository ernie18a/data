On each closed chart bar, compare the traded instrument’s latest normalized short-term move with normalized moves in the dollar, government bond yield, and oil. Orient each external market’s move toward its historically observed effect on the instrument: use the inverse relationship for the dollar and yield when correlation is weak or unavailable, and include oil only when it has a meaningful observed relationship. If the dollar and yield both make strong moves in opposite directions, do not take a side. Otherwise, form a directional macro push from sufficiently strong external moves that agree, using their configured relative weights; subtract the instrument’s own move to estimate how much it has yet to catch up. Consider a long when the aligned push is upward and the remaining upside gap is sufficiently large; consider a short when both are downward and the remaining downside gap is sufficiently large. Suppress either entry if the external markets’ 5-minute or 15-minute direction strongly opposes it, or if recent gold-dollar correlation indicates their usual relationship has broken down. The script defines entry signals only; it has no exit rule.
