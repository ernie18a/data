Use the underlying instrument’s price and volume on the chart timeframe and two higher timeframes. Treat all periods, thresholds, buffers, session times and counts as configurable parameters.

Define an uptrend when the fast, medium and slow exponential moving averages are strictly ordered upward, price closes above the fast average, and the market is neither compressed by a small fast-to-medium separation nor in a volatility squeeze. A downtrend is the mirror image. A squeeze occurs when Bollinger bands lie inside an ATR-based Keltner channel. On each higher timeframe, bullish alignment requires the fast average above the medium average and the close above the slow average; bearish alignment reverses both comparisons.

Score the regime by adding one point each for a chart-timeframe trend, agreement with each higher timeframe, price on the trend’s side of session VWAP, no squeeze, and being in the permitted session after the opening exclusion. Permit entries when this score reaches the minimum regime threshold. In same-day-expiry mode, also prohibit entries after the configured cutoff. Volume confirms calls when it exceeds its moving average and the close is at or above the candle midpoint; it confirms puts when volume exceeds its average and the close is below the midpoint.

A call entry occurs when the regime and expiry gates pass and any of these holds; put rules mirror the price directions:
- In an uptrend during the permitted session, price touches the fast average within a buffer and closes bullish above it with call volume confirmation.
- After a volume-confirmed upward cross of the stored premarket high or previous regular-session high during an uptrend and permitted session, price retests that level within a limited number of bars, closes bullish at or above it, and has call volume confirmation. Keep the breakout active until price falls sufficiently below the level. These retests themselves do not require the session or trend to remain valid.
- On the same bar as a volume-confirmed upward cross of the established opening-range high during an uptrend and permitted session, price also tests that level within a buffer, closes bullish at or above it, and has call volume confirmation.
- After a close crosses below the fast average, a bullish candle touches and closes back above it in an uptrend and permitted session with call volume confirmation. Clear the stored break when price closes sufficiently above the average, before evaluating the reclaim.
- In an uptrend, a bullish liquidity sweep occurs. A sweep penetrates below a reference by a buffer and closes bullish back above it. References are the previous regular-session low, previous-week low, fast average, or a recent low associated with a prior equal-low cluster. The fast-average sweep additionally requires the fast average above the medium average or an uptrend; the equal-low sweep additionally requires above-average volume. These sweep entries do not independently require volume confirmation or the permitted session. Bearish sweeps mirror these rules at highs, with the corresponding fast-average alignment.

Maintain the premarket levels exactly as stored at the daily boundary from the premarket accumulation since the preceding boundary; maintain previous-day levels from regular-session highs and lows. Reset the opening range at the daily boundary and establish it from bars in the configured opening window.

Emit raw buy signals whenever their conditions hold. For the displayed state and cut alerts, apply this priority: opening exclusion, same-day-expiry cutoff, choppy or no trend, low regime, call entry, put entry, call cut, put cut, then other waiting states. A call cut requires an uptrend and either a close below the fast average, a same-day-expiry no-bounce condition, or unchanged closes for the configured stall count; put cuts mirror this under a downtrend. The trend definition makes the fast-average break incompatible with its corresponding trend. For no-bounce tracking, set the counter to one on either buffered fast-average touch that closes on the same side, reset it to zero on any other close strictly above or below the average, and increment it only on equality; trigger at the configured count. State labels fire only when the state changes, while raw entry and cut alerts follow their conditions on every bar. No position tracking is used.
