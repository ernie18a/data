Classify the market as bullish when the fast, medium, and long moving averages are ordered upward, price is above the fast average, and the market is neither compressed nor choppy; classify it as bearish under the inverse conditions. Permit entries only when at least half of these structural checks pass: a clear local trend, agreement from each of the 5-minute and 15-minute trends, price on the trend-appropriate side of session VWAP, no volatility squeeze, and an eligible session period. Buy calls in a bullish trend after a bullish fast-average pullback or reclaim, a volume-supported retest of a broken premarket high, prior-day high, or opening-range high, or a bullish liquidity sweep. Buy puts by the inverse rules at premarket low, prior-day low, or opening-range low. Pullbacks and reclaims require above-average volume with buying or selling pressure, respectively; level breaks and retests require above-average volume and trend alignment. A liquidity sweep qualifies when price wicks beyond a prior-day or prior-week extreme, the fast average, or a confirmed cluster of equal highs/lows, then closes back through the level in the reversal direction; require trend alignment for the entry. In 0DTE mode, do not open new positions after 2 p.m. Exit calls when price closes below the fast average and exit puts when it closes above it; in 0DTE mode, also exit after an average touch fails to bounce within three bars. Either position may also be exited after several consecutive unchanged closes.
