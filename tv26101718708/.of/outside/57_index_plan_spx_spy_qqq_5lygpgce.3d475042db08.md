For each index product, configure its own bullish trigger and confirmation levels, bearish warning and trigger levels, pivot, failure level, and option contracts. Evaluate signals during the configured session, after the minimum time from the open, on the configured event date if enabled, and on confirmed bars if enabled. A cross above means the current close is above a level and the previous close was at or below it; a cross below is the reverse.

When enabled, bullish trend alignment requires close above the fast EMA, fast EMA at or above the slow EMA, and close at or above VWAP. Bearish alignment requires the opposite inequalities. Build the opening range from session highs and lows during its configured duration.

Buy calls on an index when its close crosses above its bullish trigger or confirmation level, bullish trend alignment holds, and VIX is below the bearish-confirmation threshold. For the ETF with the stricter dedicated reclaim signals, those signals instead require VIX below the lower risk-on threshold. The general trigger and confirmation signals also require the close to be at or above the opening-range low once the range is complete, if that filter is enabled. Separately, for the technology ETF, buy calls on a bounce when the bar touches its configured hold level, closes above that level and the fast EMA, and VIX is below the bearish-confirmation threshold.

Buy puts when the index close crosses below its bearish trigger, bearish trend alignment holds, and VIX is at or above the bearish-confirmation threshold. The general breakdown signal also requires the close to be at or below the completed opening-range high if that filter is enabled. If VIX is unavailable, the bearish confirmation can optionally pass; bullish signals that directly compare VIX with a threshold do not pass.

After the opening range completes, buy calls on a close crossing above its high when the close is above the product pivot, bullish trend alignment holds, and VIX is below the bearish-confirmation threshold. Buy puts on a close crossing below its low when the close is below the pivot, bearish trend alignment holds, and VIX confirms the bearish regime.

A bar whose high exceeds the bullish confirmation level but closes below the failure level, with bearish VIX confirmation, signals a failed breakout and puts to watch. A bar that trades above the pivot but closes below it with bearish VIX confirmation signals a failed reclaim and downside risk. A cross below the bearish warning level with bearish VIX confirmation is a warning, not by itself a put entry.

Use the product-specific suggested call or put contracts and configured target and invalidation levels. The alerts describe invalidation when price falls below support for calls or reclaims resistance for puts, but do not implement an automatic exit rule.
