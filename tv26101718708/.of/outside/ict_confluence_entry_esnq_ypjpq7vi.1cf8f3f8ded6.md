Generate a long signal when price forms a bearish fair value gap, then closes above its upper boundary; generate a short signal when price forms a bullish fair value gap, then closes below its lower boundary. Count this inversion trigger plus recent confirmations: a liquidity sweep that pierces a prior swing low/high and closes back above/below it; rejection of a 15-minute, 1-hour, or 4-hour fair value gap in the corresponding direction; and SMT divergence, where the instrument makes a lookback low/high but the correlated instrument does not make a comparable low/high. Assign higher grades when more confirmations coincide, with the strongest grade requiring all three. Emit the signal only if it meets the selected minimum grade, trade direction is allowed, and—when enabled—it occurs during an allowed session. No exit decision is defined.
