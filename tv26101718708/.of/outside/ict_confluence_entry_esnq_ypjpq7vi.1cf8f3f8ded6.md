On each chart bar, track the latest qualifying bearish and bullish three-candle price gaps separately. A bearish gap forms when the current high is below the low two bars earlier; a bullish gap forms when the current low is above the high two bars earlier. Require the configured minimum gap size. Replace the stored gap of the same direction whenever a new one forms. A close above the stored bearish gap's upper edge triggers a long candidate; a close below the stored bullish gap's lower edge triggers a short candidate. Deactivate the corresponding gap immediately when triggered, even if the candidate is later filtered out.

Grade each candidate using three recent confluences, regardless of whether their direction matches the candidate:
- Sweep: Within the configured recent window, price exceeded the preceding configured-lookback high or low by at least the minimum sweep size and closed back across that level. Only count sweeps allowed by the configured directional bias.
- Higher-timeframe gap rejection: Within its configured recent window, a currently detected bullish gap on any monitored higher timeframe had the chart bar's low at or below its upper edge and close above that edge, or a bearish gap had the chart bar's high at or above its lower edge and close below that edge. Compare the merged higher-timeframe high and low series with their values two chart bars earlier. For simultaneous gaps of the same direction, use the highest monitored timeframe's edges.
- Cross-instrument divergence: Within its configured recent window, the traded instrument's low equaled its rolling lookback low while the correlated instrument's low exceeded its own rolling lookback low multiplied by a configured lower tolerance factor, or the traded instrument's high equaled its rolling lookback high while the correlated instrument's high was below its own rolling lookback high multiplied by a configured upper tolerance factor.

Assign the highest grade when all three confluences are present; the next grade for sweep plus higher-timeframe rejection without divergence; the next for higher-timeframe rejection plus divergence without sweep; the next for exactly one confluence; and the lowest for none. Sweep plus divergence without higher-timeframe rejection receives no grade and produces no signal. Emit the candidate's long or short signal only if its grade meets the configured minimum, its direction is allowed by the bias, and, when session filtering is enabled, the bar falls within an enabled session in New York time. An optional setting excludes the two lowest grades. Signals are generated at the triggering close; the displayed stop, break-even and session-based target levels do not implement exit signals.
