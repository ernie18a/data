On each confirmed bar, classify recent confirmed swing pivots into bullish or bearish Elliott impulse pullbacks: three alternating pivots for wave 2, or five for wave 4, with the later impulse peak beyond the earlier one. Consider a long when price retraces into the impulse leg’s 38.2%–61.8% Fibonacci zone and closes back above its lower boundary with a bullish rejection or engulfing candle; require the pullback to hold beyond the impulse origin, sufficient leg size, and either matching market structure or, when structure is neutral, matching higher-timeframe trend. Wave 4 pullbacks require matching market structure. Also consider a long when price crosses above the wave-1 swing high from at or below it during a wave-2 or wave-4 phase, with matching directional structure and sufficient leg size. Mirror these rules for shorts. Optionally consider a countertrend ABC entry when price reaches and rejects the 61.8% retracement of the corrective leg, with matching structure and a directional trigger candle. For every candidate, require the configured minimum confluence score from structure, Fibonacci location, trigger candle, higher-timeframe EMA bias, volume expansion, low choppiness, and optional RSI and EMA direction filters; optionally hard-block against higher-timeframe bias. Block entries during extreme chop and enforce a cooldown between signals. Prefer wave-4 over wave-2 if both qualify. Emit long or short at the bar close; set a structural stop beyond the wave invalidation level with an ATR buffer and profit targets at the prior swing high/low or equal-leg projection, plus a Fibonacci extension. Flag potential exits when price makes a higher high with lower RSI in bullish structure, or a lower low with higher RSI in bearish structure; these are warnings, not automatic position closures.
