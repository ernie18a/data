For each of a basket of external equity ETFs, determine trend direction from whether its normalized adaptive moving-average oscillator is above or below zero. Multiply that direction by the ETF’s correlation with the chart instrument, then average the products across the basket. Optionally normalize the average once or twice. A positive aggregate above the signal threshold indicates a long bias for the chart instrument; a negative aggregate below the opposite threshold indicates a short bias; values between the thresholds are neutral. An optional Hull moving average of the selected aggregate can provide earlier directional signals when it crosses above or below zero. Separately, classify each of four macro regimes by counting whether selected ETFs’ performance relative to the basket’s average is positive or negative in the regime’s specified direction. The highest-scoring growth regime indicates risk-on; otherwise indicate risk-off.
