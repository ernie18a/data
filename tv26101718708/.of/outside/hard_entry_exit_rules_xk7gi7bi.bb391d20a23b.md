During the configured entry window, enter long when price is above VWAP and the faster average, the faster average is above the slower average, the close is above the slower average and above either the prior-day high or premarket high, and a bullish candle retests or first-touch reacts above one of those highs, or taps the faster average and closes back above it. Enter short using the inverse conditions: price below VWAP and the faster average, the faster average below the slower average, the close below the slower average and below either the prior-day low or premarket low, with a bearish reaction at one of those lows or a tap of the faster average followed by a close below it. While holding, trim part of the position at a new session high for longs or session low for shorts. Exit the remainder if price touches the slower average, or a candle closes across the faster average or the triggering price level against the position. Flatten any open position in the configured end-of-day window.
