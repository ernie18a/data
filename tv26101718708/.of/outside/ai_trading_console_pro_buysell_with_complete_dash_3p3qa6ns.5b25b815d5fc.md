On each bar, evaluate enabled entry directions only when flat and different from the last entered direction. An enabled long requires every enabled filter to pass: close crosses above the running opening-range high; fast EMA exceeds slow EMA; RSI exceeds its buy threshold; ADX exceeds its minimum; the external volatility index close is below its maximum; and higher-timeframe close exceeds its EMA. A short uses a cross below the opening-range low, fast EMA below slow EMA, RSI below its sell threshold, and higher-timeframe close below its EMA, with the same ADX and external-volatility filters. Disabled filters pass automatically. Reset the opening range each day and update its high and low during the configured opening window. Crosses compare current and previous bar values of both close and the range boundary. On entry, record close, direction and an initial stop at an ATR-based distance below close for a long or above close for a short. If both entry conditions are true on the same bar, execute the long entry and then the short entry, leaving both direction flags active and the short entry's stop and last direction in effect. On every bar in a trade, including the entry bar, optionally tighten the long stop to the greater of its current value and close minus an ATR-based trailing distance; tighten the short stop to the lesser of its current value and close plus that distance. If both direction flags are active, apply both updates in that order. Then exit if the long flag is active and close is below the stop, or the short flag is active and close is above the stop. Clear both direction flags on exit but retain the last entered direction. Pivot targets only track touches and do not cause exits.
