When flat, enter long on a close crossing above the day's opening-range high, provided the fast moving average is above the slow one, momentum is above its bullish threshold, trend strength exceeds its minimum, external volatility is below its maximum, and the higher-timeframe close is above its trend average. Enter short on the corresponding cross below the opening-range low when these conditions are bearish. Each enabled filter must pass; disabled filters are ignored. Do not repeat the previous direction until an entry in the opposite direction occurs. Set an initial stop a volatility-based distance from entry. While in a trade, trail the stop in the favorable direction using current price and volatility; exit when the close crosses the stop. Pivot levels are tracked as targets but do not trigger exits.
