On each bar, fit a volume-weighted polynomial regression to the selected price source from the selected rolling or confirmed swing-pivot anchor; use equal weights when volume is unavailable. Form upper and lower outer bands from the fitted current value plus or minus a configurable multiple of the weighted residual standard deviation.

A short setup requires the high to touch the upper outer band and at least one bearish tell; a long setup requires the low to touch the lower outer band and at least one bullish tell. Tells are: a close back inside the touched band; a recently confirmed divergence between successive price pivots and RSI or smoothed stochastic RSI, with the newer pivot reaching the configured channel band if that filter is enabled; a recent sweep of an ATR-tolerance equal-pivot level, or of the latest pivot with a directional reversal candle, closing back across the level; or enabled multi-factor extreme consensus on the corresponding side. Divergence recency is measured from pivot confirmation, not the pivot bar.

For multi-factor consensus, rolling-standardize each enabled, available factor and count those beyond the positive or negative extreme threshold. The factors are price minus session VWAP; signed standardized bar range; signed efficiency ratio; price minus an external reference instrument's close; signed ATR-normalized parabolic-SAR extension; RSI or smoothed stochastic RSI; ATR-normalized deviation from linear regression; the weighted, smoothed rate-of-change cycle indicator; and cumulative signed volume minus its exponential average. The external reference is sampled at the chart timeframe without lookahead. Require a configurable or timeframe-adaptive count, capped at the number of available factors. Positive consensus is bearish and negative consensus bullish. Exclude unavailable factors and volume-dependent factors when volume is unavailable.

Apply a shared cooldown to raw setups in either direction; every raw setup resets it even if subsequently rejected by a gate. Gate a raw setup using the configured choice of: variance of multi-bar log returns divided by horizon times variance of one-bar log returns below the reverting threshold; negative rolling correlation between the previous close's deviation from its moving average and the current close change above the reversion threshold; either test; both tests; or no regime test. If the optional consensus gate is enabled, also require the corresponding consensus tell, including its enable switch. Emit the gated short or long when all applicable conditions pass. Both directions can fire on the same bar. The confidence score, performance statistics, plotted targets and trend lines do not affect these decisions; no exit rule is specified.
