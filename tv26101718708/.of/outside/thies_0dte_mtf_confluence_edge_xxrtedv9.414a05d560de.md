For each instrument, calculate volume relative to its own moving average and an exponentially smoothed pressure measure by multiplying relative volume by the close’s position within the bar’s high–low range, scaled from negative at the low to positive at the high. Average the two relative-volume readings and add their pressure readings. Assign that timeframe +1 when average relative volume exceeds its activity threshold, combined pressure is positive, and both instruments close above their opens; assign −1 when volume exceeds the threshold, pressure is negative, and both close below their opens; otherwise assign 0. Outside the session, assign 0 for every timeframe. Sum the timeframe scores. Signal long when the sum meets or exceeds the configured bullish threshold for the configured number of consecutive chart bars, and signal short when it meets or falls below the negative threshold for that duration.
