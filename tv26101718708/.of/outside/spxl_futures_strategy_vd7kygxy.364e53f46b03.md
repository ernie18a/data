Use either of two selectable signal modes. In the default mode, for each of ES, NQ, YM, and RTY, calculate an equal-weight rating across available moving averages (SMA and EMA at short through long horizons, plus HMA and VWMA): +1 when price is above an average, −1 when below, and 0 when equal. Include an Ichimoku rating that is +1 or −1 only when its corresponding cloud, base-line, and conversion-line conditions are met; otherwise 0. Average the component ratings. Compare each market’s rating with its configurable sell threshold. Raise those four thresholds to a configurable less restrictive level when both daily ES RSI and MFI exceed their configurable overbought levels. In this mode, sell if any of the four ratings is at or below its threshold, or if all of these external risk conditions hold: front VIX futures premium over spot is below its threshold; the smoothed breadth measure for stocks above their long-term average is declining; VVIX or VXX is near the top of its Bollinger range; and SKEW is not at an extreme high Bollinger position (check current and prior readings). Buy only when there is no sell condition and, for each of the four markets, either its current and prior ratings both exceed the applicable threshold or its current rating is nonnegative. In the alternate trend mode, sell if any of the four hourly futures prices is at or below its long-term EMA; buy if all four are above it. On a sell signal, open a short position when shorting is enabled, otherwise close positions. On a buy signal, enter long. Apply strategy entries only within the configured date window; close all positions after that window.
