Use external ES, NQ, YM and RTY futures data to trade the instrument. Choose one of two signal modes. In moving-average rating mode, calculate a rating for each future on a configurable intraday timeframe: average the available votes from several simple and exponential moving averages, a Hull moving average, a volume-weighted moving average, and an Ichimoku vote. Each moving-average vote is positive when close is above the average, negative when below, and neutral when equal. The Ichimoku vote is positive when the first leading span is above the second, close is above the first span but below the base line, and close crosses above the conversion line; it is negative when the second span is above the first, close is below the second span but above the base line, and close crosses below the conversion line; otherwise it is neutral. Use separate configurable sell thresholds for the four ratings, replacing all four with a common configurable threshold when both daily ES RSI and money flow index exceed their overbought threshold. Also produce a volatility-and-breadth sell signal when the front VIX future's premium over spot VIX, divided by spot VIX, is at or below its threshold; the moving average of the percentage of stocks above their long-term moving average is declining over a configurable lookback; either VVIX or VXX Bollinger-band position is at or above its threshold; and SKEW Bollinger-band position exceeds its threshold on neither the current nor preceding chart bar. Bollinger-band position is the distance from the lower band divided by the distance between the bands. Sell if any futures rating is at or below its threshold or the volatility-and-breadth sell signal is present. Otherwise buy only if, for every future, its rating is nonnegative or both its current and preceding chart-bar ratings exceed that future's current threshold. In EMA trend mode, sell if any of the four futures closes at or below its configurable intraday EMA; buy if all are above their EMAs. If neither buy nor sell is true, retain the existing position. On eligible daily chart bars within the enabled strategy date window, a buy enters or reverses to long; a sell enters or reverses to short if shorting is enabled, otherwise it closes positions. Close all positions after the end date when the strategy is enabled. On daily realtime or last bars, use the previous chart-bar signal inputs; otherwise use current inputs. On intraday charts, use daily ES RSI and money flow values with higher-timeframe lookahead enabled.
