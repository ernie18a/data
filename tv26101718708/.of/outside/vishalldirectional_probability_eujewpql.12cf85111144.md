For the traded instrument, select a daily Heikin-Ashi strength score from the corresponding external index, sector proxy, or stock. Calculate each source’s relative Heikin-Ashi strength as (Heikin-Ashi close − regular close) / regular close × 100. For an index with futures data, use the futures score minus the cash-index score; otherwise use the available cash or proxy score. Treat a score above 0.5% as strong bullish bias, above 0.2% through 0.5% as mild bullish bias, from −0.2% through 0.2% as neutral, below −0.2% through −0.5% as mild bearish bias, and below −0.5% as strong bearish bias.
