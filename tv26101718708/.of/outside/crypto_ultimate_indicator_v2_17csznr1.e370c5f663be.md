Generate long and short signals from the traded instrument’s trend, momentum, volume and higher-timeframe data, with an optional external Bitcoin trend filter. Use configurable periods, thresholds, weights and switches throughout.

For a long, require a rising selected moving average, RSI above its midpoint, positive body-weighted volume delta, a bullish EMA ribbon score and bullish Supertrend. For a short, require the opposite, treating a flat moving average as bearish. Volume delta is volume multiplied by candle body divided by range and by the sign of close minus open. The ribbon score sums bullish or bearish comparisons of adjacent EMAs and of close versus the fastest EMA.

Apply these additional gates in the corresponding direction: sufficient weighted higher-timeframe confluence when enabled; adequate confidence; no opposing fair value gap containing the close when that filter is enabled; no strong ranging regime when the strict filter is enabled; sufficient distance from the ATR-based resistance for a long or support for a short; an adequately large candle body when enabled; and sufficient bars since either previous signal. Optional pivot-cluster and volume-profile filters also block entries near a qualifying level in the entry direction. Track fair value gaps only when their display is enabled, optionally removing filled gaps. The ATR resistance is the recent highest high plus an ATR multiple; support is the recent lowest low minus an ATR multiple.

For higher-timeframe confluence, use the last closed daily, weekly and configured timeframe bars. Score the sign of Hull moving-average slope and RSI relative to upper and lower thresholds; include Supertrend direction for daily and weekly. Reduce each timeframe’s sum to its sign, then combine them with configurable weights. Confidence adds weighted points for the absolute confluence score, market regime, absolute ribbon score, RSI distance from its midpoint, relative volume-delta magnitude, Bollinger squeeze release, directional cumulative-volume-delta divergence and, if enabled, a directional liquidity sweep. Compute regime as a weighted combination of normalized ADX, inverse choppiness and normalized Bollinger-band width. Divergence compares the latest two independently confirmed price and cumulative-delta pivots. A sweep breaks a prior-window extreme and closes back inside it.

When the external filter is enabled and the traded ticker does not contain the Bitcoin ticker text, allow a long only when Bitcoin’s close is above its EMA; allow a short when it is not above that EMA. Otherwise bypass this filter. If confirmation is enabled, all applicable gates must hold on consecutive bars for the configured confirmation count. Emit the signal and reset both directional confirmation counts and the shared signal-spacing clock.
