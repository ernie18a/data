For the selected currency pair, compare the base currency with the quote currency using each enabled, available external measure: central bank rate, 2-year government bond yield, and 10-year government bond yield. For each measure, give a vote to the currency with the higher value; a tie gives no directional vote. Average the directional votes. A sufficiently positive average gives a bullish bias on the pair (long base, short quote); a sufficiently negative average gives a bearish bias (short base, long quote); an average near zero gives a neutral bias. Missing measures do not contribute. The framework provides no exit rule.
