Smooth each bar’s open, high, low, and close with a moving average, then calculate a Supertrend from those smoothed candles using a smoothed true range. Exit a long when the trend is bearish and either it was bearish on the prior bar and the close has fallen, or the close is below the long stop. Exit a short when the trend is bullish and either it was bullish on the prior bar and the close has risen, or the close is above the short stop. Enter long when the short-exit condition occurs and the close is above either a threshold-adjusted prior-year high or prior-year low. Enter short when the long-exit condition occurs and the close is below either a threshold-adjusted prior-year low or prior-year high. Restrict entries to the configured date range and honor the configured trade direction.
