On each confirmed bar, evaluate enabled long and short triggers: (1) price touches the selected moving average and closes back above it for long or below it for short, optionally requiring the average to slope in that direction; (2) price retests an active bullish or bearish chart-timeframe fair-value gap, optionally only on its first retest; (3) price retests an active higher-timeframe gap on its first retest; and (4) absorption, where unusually high volume accompanies strongly negative estimated delta with a close in the bar’s upper half for long, or strongly positive delta with a close in its lower half for short. Add one score point when price is on the favorable side of VWAP and one when both the intermediate and higher-timeframe EMA biases agree; each bias is bullish when its timeframe closes above a rising EMA and bearish when below a falling EMA. Require the configured minimum number of directional triggers, a qualifying score grade, and—if enabled—the VWAP, aligned-bias, and session-window filters. Emit a long or short signal only when that side’s trigger count exceeds the opposite side’s and the signal cooldown has elapsed; process long first if both otherwise qualify. For each signal, the indicator tracks a hypothetical trade with an ATR-based stop and profit targets, counting an exit at the stop or first target (a bar touching both is counted as a stop). It does not issue a separate live position-exit signal.
