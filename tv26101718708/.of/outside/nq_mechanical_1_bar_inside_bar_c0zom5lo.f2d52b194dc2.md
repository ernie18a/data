On each confirmed chart bar within the configured New York entry window, seek a close beyond an active inside-bar range. An inside bar has a high no higher and a low no lower than its predecessor. For the chart-timeframe setup, activate the previous bar's range when it is inside and no setup is active; keep it until the close breaks the range or a bar extends outside it without a closing break. For the configured higher-timeframe setup, at each higher-timeframe boundary activate the last available completed bar's range if it is inside, otherwise deactivate it; allow one closing break per higher-timeframe interval. Either setup supplies an upward or downward break.

Compare the close with session-anchored VWAP. Measure continuity by comparing the current close with the current period's open for each configured timeframe at least as large as the chart timeframe: count closes above and below, and subtract the below count from the above count.

Determine the external volatility index regime on its configured timeframe using a twin-range trailing filter. For each of two configured periods, exponentially smooth the absolute change in index close, smooth that result again over twice the period minus one, and apply its configured multiplier; average the two ranges. Update a range-filtered price by retaining its prior value unless the current close exceeds it by more than the average range, in which case move it to the close minus or plus that range. Form upper and lower bands around the filtered price: the upper band resets to its new value when it falls below its prior value or the previous close was above the prior upper band, otherwise retain the prior upper band; symmetrically, the lower band resets when it rises above its prior value or the previous close was below the prior lower band. The trailing line switches between these bands when the close crosses the active band, defaulting to the upper band. The regime is up only when the index close is strictly above the trailing line. Request this external timeframe with lookahead enabled.

Below VWAP, signal long on an upward break when the index regime is not up and continuity is nonnegative; signal short on a downward break when the index regime is up. Above VWAP, signal long on an upward break when continuity is positive; signal short on a downward break when continuity is nonpositive and a key level was crossed recently. A key-level cross occurs when the chart close, high, or low crosses a level in either direction, on the current bar or within the configured chart-bar lookback. Levels are the previous daily high and low, the completed configured Asia and London session highs and lows, and the configured futures-session opening price. At VWAP, issue no signal.
