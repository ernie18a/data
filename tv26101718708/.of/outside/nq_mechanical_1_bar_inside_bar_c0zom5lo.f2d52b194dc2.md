On each confirmed bar during the morning entry window, detect an inside bar on either the 5-minute or 15-minute timeframe (its high is no higher than the prior bar’s high and its low no lower than the prior bar’s low). Signal on a subsequent bar’s close beyond that inside bar’s high or low. Compare the traded instrument’s close with its session VWAP. Classify the 15-minute VIX regime as rising or falling using a trend filter. Classify timeframe continuity by comparing the close with the open of each available tracked timeframe: bullish when more are up than down, bearish when more are down than up, and neutral on a tie. Go long on an upward inside-bar break if price is below VWAP while VIX is falling and continuity is not bearish, or if price is above VWAP and continuity is bullish. Go short on a downward break if price is below VWAP while VIX is rising, or if price is above VWAP after a recent cross of a key level and continuity is neutral or bearish. Key levels are the prior day’s high and low, completed Asia and London session highs and lows, and the futures session open. The indicator defines entry signals only; it has no exit rule.
