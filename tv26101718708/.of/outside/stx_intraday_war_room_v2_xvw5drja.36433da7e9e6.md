On a confirmed bar, enter long only when the 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute fast-versus-slow EMA directions all agree upward; price overlaps the swing-based Fibonacci retracement entry zone; recent swing structure is not bearish; the market is not classified as ranging and macro blocking is off; price has broken above a bullish 15-minute order block and retests it with a bullish rejection wick; and the 5-minute direction is upward with enough 1-minute confirmations (directional displacement, fair-value gap, order-block retest, and break of prior intrabar extremes, reduced by opposing displacement). Enter short using the mirrored bearish conditions. While a position is open, move its protective stop to entry after price reaches the specified fraction of the entry-to-first-target distance; exit when price hits the effective stop or the third projected target. Do not open another position while one is already open.
