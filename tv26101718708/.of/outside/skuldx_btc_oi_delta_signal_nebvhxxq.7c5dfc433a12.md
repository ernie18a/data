Aggregate open-interest changes across enabled perpetual-futures sources, converting USD-denominated values to BTC using spot price. A rising traded-instrument price with increasing aggregated open interest signals bullish trend (long bias); a falling price with decreasing open interest signals bearish trend (short bias). Rising price with decreasing open interest is a short squeeze; falling price with increasing open interest is a long squeeze. The squeeze states are descriptive and do not specify an entry or exit decision.
