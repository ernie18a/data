Compare the external risk asset and safe haven by taking the rolling standardized value of their log price ratio, then smooth it. When the smoothed value crosses above zero, favor long exposure to the traded risk asset; when it crosses below zero, favor short exposure or exit longs. At an extreme negative reading, a turn in momentum from falling to rising signals a bullish reversal and long entry. At an extreme positive reading, a turn from rising to falling signals a bearish reversal and exit of longs or short entry.
