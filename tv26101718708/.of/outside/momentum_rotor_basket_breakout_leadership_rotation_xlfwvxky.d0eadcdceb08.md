Rank the configured basket by each instrument’s rate of change over a lookback period. For the chart instrument, enter long when it is in the basket, ranks first, closes above the highest high of a prior lookback period, and—if enabled—closes above a long-term moving average; enter only when flat. Size the position from a fixed fraction of equity divided by the ATR-based stop distance. At entry, set a stop below price by an ATR multiple and a take-profit above price at a reward-to-risk multiple. While long, exit if the chart instrument loses first place in the basket; optionally require that loss to persist for a configured number of bars. The strategy does not open short positions.
