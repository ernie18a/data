Enter a short when the WaveTrend lines cross downward with the slower line at or above its bearish threshold, and both selected higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles are bearish. Require a confirmed bar, a valid ATR reading, no open position, the BTC perpetual reference price below its long-period regime average on the current or previous bar, and the traded instrument’s close below its hourly moving average on the current or previous bar. While short, exit at a fixed-percentage profit target below entry or a fixed-percentage stop above entry. When the moving-average exit option is enabled, also close the position if the traded instrument’s close is above its hourly moving average on the current or previous bar. The strategy opens no long positions.
