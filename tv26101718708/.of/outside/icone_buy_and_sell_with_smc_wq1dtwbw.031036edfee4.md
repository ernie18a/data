Calculate a gold macro score as the capped sum of thresholded, weighted contributions from confirmed external data: real-yield changes and dollar-index returns contribute inversely; central-bank balance-sheet changes and gold-ETF on-balance-volume changes contribute directly. Add contributions for miners’ returns relative to gold, silver returns, the gold-to-silver ratio with a volatility-index condition, high-yield credit returns, volatility-index returns, dollar-yen and dollar-yuan returns, the copper-to-gold ratio, and bitcoin returns. Include a contribution from whether the traded instrument is above or below both its medium- and long-term moving averages. Treat missing external changes as neutral.

When the macro gate is enabled, allow an otherwise eligible long entry only when the score is strictly above the negative gate threshold, and an otherwise eligible short entry only when it is strictly below the positive gate threshold. The index profile disables this gate. The gate applies to all entry types, including sweeps.

For the position guide, an adverse macro score is a warning when it crosses the applicable opposing threshold. On each confirmed bar, a newly appearing warning increases the persistent exit-advice level by one, subject to the first-target guard for automatic positions. Other warnings are adverse false breakouts, moving-average crosses, weakening volume, opposing volume flow, and nearby resistance or support. A severely adverse macro score raises the advice immediately to at least the larger partial-exit level, regardless of the first-target guard; an adverse structure break raises it directly to full exit. Successive levels advise a small partial exit, a larger partial exit, and full exit. Reset the level when position direction changes. This guide issues advice and alerts; it does not itself place exit orders.
