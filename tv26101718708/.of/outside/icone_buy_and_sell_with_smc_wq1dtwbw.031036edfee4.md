On a confirmed bar, evaluate long and short setups independently. Build a directional setup score from trend alignment (price versus long-term average and average slopes), impulse and path efficiency, range and volatility contraction, compression shape, breakout strength, momentum and volume, higher-timeframe direction, liquidity sweeps, and price location within the recent range. Permit a setup only when its score reaches the configured minimum; in strict mode also require the corresponding trend, impulse, volume, momentum, candle-strength, and higher-timeframe filters.

A breakout setup occurs when price closes beyond the prior structure range, with an ATR-scaled buffer, after valid compression. Depending on the selected entry mode, enter on that breakout, wait for price to retest the broken boundary and close back in the breakout direction, or prioritize retests while allowing especially strong breakouts directly. Cancel a pending retest after its allowed window or a decisive close back through the level.

Also allow trend-continuation entries when price recently touched the short-term average and then resumes direction: a bullish bar closes above that average and the prior bar's high for a long; a bearish bar closes below it and the prior bar's low for a short. Require the matching trend and higher-timeframe direction, and, in high-quality mode, supportive momentum, candle body, relative volume, and multi-timeframe breadth.

Allow reversal entries on confirmed false breakouts of a valid compressed range: a downside probe that closes back above the range low on a bullish bar can trigger a long; an upside probe that closes back below the range high on a bearish bar can trigger a short. Apply the configured sweep cooldown and, when enabled, require the broader regime to agree.

Before entry, apply the selected direction restriction, optional structural headroom, session, cooldown, daily trade limit, minimum-history, macro, and daily-trend gates. The macro gate uses a weighted score from real yields, dollar direction, central-bank liquidity, gold ETF flow, the traded instrument's technical trend, and cross-market confirmation from miners, silver, credit, volatility, currencies, copper, and bitcoin; block longs when the score is sufficiently bearish and shorts when sufficiently bullish. Daily trend gates use the daily price relative to its medium-term average, with sweep reversals exempted. Enter only while flat unless position flipping is enabled.

Set the initial stop using the selected ATR-based, structure-based, or more conservative combination. Size the position from equity risk divided by entry-to-stop distance, optionally scaling risk with setup score and limiting exposure. Set successive profit targets at configured risk multiples and close configured portions at the earlier targets, with the remainder at the final target. After the first target, optionally move the stop to entry with a volatility buffer; after the next target, optionally trail it by an ATR-based distance. These stops and target orders close the position when hit.

For an active position, the separate live guide escalates advice on newly arising opposing false breakouts, momentum reversal, weakening volume while profitable, opposing candle-volume pressure, nearby structure or key-level resistance/support while profitable, or an adverse macro turn. A hard close beyond the recent structure boundary advises closing all; an extreme adverse macro score advises a larger reduction. Otherwise, each new warning advances the advice from a small partial sale to a larger sale and then full exit, optionally waiting for the first profit target before ordinary warnings can trigger.
