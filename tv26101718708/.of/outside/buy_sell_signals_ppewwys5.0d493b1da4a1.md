Calculate fast and slow exponential moving averages of the chart instrument's close. Mark a candle when the averages cross or their absolute difference is below a tolerance. On the candle immediately after a marked candle, provided it is not itself marked, store the lowest low across that candle, the marked candle and its preceding candle, and reset the signal-used flag. Retain the last stored level until replaced. A new marked candle replaces the pending marked candle but does not itself reset the flag or clear the stored level. Buy a put at the reference strike plus the strike interval when the current low falls below the stored lowest low, the fast average is below the slow average, no signal has been used since the last reset, and the averages do not cross downward on the current candle. Mark the signal as used. Also mark it as used if, before the put condition is evaluated, the current high exceeds the stored highest high of the same three candles while the fast average is above the slow average, the flag is unused, and there is no upward cross on the current candle.
