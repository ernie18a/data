On each confirmed bar, calculate a volatility-based trailing stop whose distance adapts to instrument class, relative volatility, relative volume, and whether price movement is trending or choppy; ratchet it so it only tightens in the current direction. Generate a long signal when price crosses above the stop and a short signal when it crosses below. Emit a signal only if the close remains on the corresponding side and every enabled confirmation passes: directional persistence, minimum spacing between signals, trend-strength regime, volume and volatility thresholds, momentum and moving-average alignment, higher-timeframe trend alignment, support/resistance proximity, candle position relative to the stop, and optional swing, price-change, or delayed-confirmation checks. Alternate signal directions. At a signal, enter at the bar close and set a protective stop using the selected volatility-, percentage-, tick-, or swing-based method; set enabled profit targets at configured multiples of the entry-to-stop risk. Exit the position when its stop is touched, its farthest enabled target is reached, or an opposing signal establishes the next trade. Decisions are finalized at bar close. The confidence score and trend-duration forecast are descriptive and do not independently trigger trades.
