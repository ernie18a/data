Build a signed reversal score from outside-market evidence: positive evidence reflects oversold conditions, bullish flow turns or divergences, a sweep and reclaim of a prior low, bullish rejection, supportive higher-timeframe conditions, and optional funding or spot-perpetual basis; negative evidence reflects the symmetric overbought and bearish conditions. Weight and normalize the active evidence, then adjust its magnitude for event strength, volume, open-interest positioning, volatility regime, VWAP context, and market chop; penalize reversals against strong trends or continuation divergences. A long signal requires a positive score reaching the configured medium-strength threshold, a low-side liquidity sweep and reclaim when sweep filtering is enabled, enough independent same-direction evidence votes, and no active chop block. A short signal uses the symmetric conditions. If enabled, require a matching bullish or bearish reversal candle. In strict confirmation mode, require the next bar to close in the signal direction; otherwise signal on the setup bar's close. Apply the configured signal cooldown. A retest signal occurs when price returns to an existing active zone and rejects it with a sufficiently large direction-appropriate wick and a close back beyond the zone boundary. The indicator defines no traded-instrument exit decision.
