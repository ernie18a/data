On each closed bar, form a signed reversal score: positive favors long and negative favors short. Combine enabled, weighted measures of traded-instrument extremity, order-flow turns, decaying price-versus-volume-delta divergence, liquidity-sweep direction, candle rejection, higher-timeframe extremity, funding-rate bias, spot-versus-perpetual basis bias, and decaying divergence shared by RSI and OBV. Normalize by active weights and apply the largest applicable strong-trend, opposing higher-timeframe trend, or opposing hidden-divergence penalty. Funding and basis are contrarian: unusually high values favor short and unusually low values favor long.

Find sweeps by requiring price to pierce an enabled prior day, week or month extreme, confirmed pivot, rolling extreme, or prior-day volume-profile point of control by a minimum ATR-scaled depth and reclaim it within a configured window. A low sweep favors long and a high sweep favors short. Rank candidates by depth and level importance, with boosts for equal levels and round numbers; use the strongest side, or neither on a tie. Prevent the same level from firing again until price breaches it sufficiently anew. Boost sweep strength for proximity to higher-timeframe levels, swing-anchored VWAP, a matching unmitigated order block, or an unfilled matching fair-value gap.

Multiply direction by a bounded event gate containing aligned sweep and rejection, absorption, overextension, aligned volume climax, consolidation-box spring or upthrust, selected session, and aligned crowded-position squeeze stress. Scale the result by volume intensity and the average of open-interest, volatility-regime, VWAP/session and chop adjustments, then apply smooth saturation. For open interest, average available venues' relative changes after converting their levels to a common base-asset unit. Falling price with falling open interest strengthens long reversals; falling price with rising open interest weakens them. Rising price with falling open interest strengthens short reversals; rising price with rising open interest weakens them. Missing external feeds contribute no bias and a neutral multiplier.

A long setup needs a positive score direction, a medium score threshold, and, when enabled, a low sweep and bullish reversal candle; a short setup is symmetric. The threshold is either fixed or based on the recent percentile rank of absolute score with an absolute floor. Require a configured number of aligned confluence votes, capped by the number of enabled and available sources: extremity, flow or volume-delta divergence counted together, sweep, rejection, open-interest liquidation quadrant, higher-timeframe bias, funding, and basis. Block setups in a choppy market when the chop filter is enabled. In immediate mode issue the signal at the setup close; in strict mode use the setup-bar conditions and issue it only if the next bar closes higher for long or lower for short. Enforce the configured shared or per-direction cooldown.

Create a long zone between the setup low and swept level, or a short zone between the swept level and setup high; use the setup candle bounds if no sweep level exists. Enforce minimum ATR-scaled height and merge nearby overlapping live zones of the same side. After the first subsequent touch, a later bar that re-enters a live zone and rejects it with a sufficiently large side-appropriate wick and a close back beyond the zone's far edge produces a same-side retest signal, subject to a per-zone cooldown. Resolve a long zone when price reaches the configured favorable ATR distance above its creation close or closes below its bottom; resolve a short zone symmetrically, with favorable confirmation taking precedence. These zone resolutions are analytical states; no position exit or options decision is specified.
