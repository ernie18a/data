When flat, wait until cooldown ends, then take the first qualifying setup in this priority: bullish fair-value-gap pullback, bullish volume-exhaustion reversal, bullish swing-break retest, followed by the corresponding bearish setups. Require a directional trend: price and a rising or falling moving average agree, the trend has sufficient strength, price is not too far from the average, and the higher-timeframe price agrees when that filter is enabled; also honor the optional session filter. For a gap setup, require a sufficiently large recent three-bar gap, then a later touch of the gap and a confirming candle that closes on the valid side of the gap. For exhaustion, require unusually high volume, an extreme momentum reading, and a rejection wick against the preceding move; within a short validity window, enter only after price breaks the exhaustion candle’s trigger in the reversal direction with a confirming candle. For a swing retest, require a close through the latest confirmed swing high or low in the trend direction, then within a validity window a retest that closes back beyond the broken level with confirmation. For each setup, calculate a weighted score from its trend, higher-timeframe agreement, trend strength, trigger or retest, candle confirmation, volume, and gap size or momentum evidence; trade only when the score reaches the configured threshold. Queue the signal briefly and enter on a confirmed bar only while price remains near the signal entry and on the permitted side of the moving average. Size the position from a fixed fraction of equity and the stop distance. Place the stop beyond the setup’s low or high, gap boundary, or retested swing level, with an ATR-based buffer; set a take-profit at a fixed reward-to-risk multiple. Exit at the stop or target.
