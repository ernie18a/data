Go long when price closes above its prior rolling high, the candle passes a bullish close-position test, the trend EMA is rising, and RSI exceeds its threshold. For non-benchmark instruments, also require the instrument-to-benchmark price ratio to make a prior rolling high and its trend EMA to be rising; use a crypto benchmark for crypto instruments and the stock benchmark otherwise. Exit when price closes below a faster EMA, or below the trend EMA after the position has gained enough over its average entry price, or when the instrument-to-benchmark ratio falls below its own EMA. Benchmark instruments skip the relative-strength entry filter.
