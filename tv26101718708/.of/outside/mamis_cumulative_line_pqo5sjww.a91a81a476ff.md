On confirmed daily bars, treat a breadth kickoff as a long signal when the cumulative advance-minus-decline ratio is in a rising positive regime, its smoothed impulse is unusually strong relative to its recent history, the instrument closes above its long-term moving average, and the cooldown since the prior kickoff has elapsed. Treat a breakdown as a short signal when breadth is in a negative regime, its trend spread is unusually weak relative to recent history, and a recent confirmed price high exceeds the prior such high while breadth at that high is lower. Confirm regime changes across consecutive valid breadth observations; ignore signals when breadth data is stale or insufficient.
