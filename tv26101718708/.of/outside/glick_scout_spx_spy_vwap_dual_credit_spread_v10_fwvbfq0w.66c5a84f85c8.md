On a confirmed bar during the configured market session, score bearish and bullish setups separately. A bearish score adds one point each if the bar closes below its open, its close is below the current session VWAP, its close is below the prior session VWAP, and its close crosses below the current session VWAP. A bullish score adds one point each for the opposite conditions, including a cross above the current session VWAP. Signal a call credit spread when the bearish score meets the configured minimum, the downward cross occurred, the volatility index is within its configured range, and the minimum spacing between signals has elapsed. Signal a put credit spread under the corresponding bullish conditions, provided no call signal fires on that bar. The indicator defines no exit decision.
