Build an anchored, volume-weighted mean and volume-weighted price-deviation bands. On confirmed bars, optionally signal long or short when price crosses an enabled mean or band level; ignore crossings caused by anchor resets. Optional breakout filters require bullish or bearish alignment respectively: price beyond both fast and slow moving averages with the fast average in the same direction, price beyond the multi-timeframe trend level with matching trend direction, and price on the matching side of each selected session VWAP (or at least one selected VWAP, according to the configured rule).

Around the fixed 23.6% bands, form zones with configurable volatility- or tick-based width. Optional reaction entries are: (1) reclaim: after a close beyond an outer zone edge, signal in the direction of a later close crossing back through the zone core within the allowed window; (2) rejection: after approaching from outside, touch the zone and close back beyond its core in the approach-defense direction, with matching candle direction; (3) hold: on a zone touch, require the configured approach and close position to show defense, with sensitivity determining those boundaries; (4) acceptance: after a fresh edge break, signal in the break direction once the required consecutive closes remain beyond that edge, counting the break close. When multiple reactions qualify, prefer reclaim, rejection, hold, then acceptance. Enforce a shared cooldown between reaction entries. Reject signals on anchor resets and, when enabled, require bar confirmation.

If opposing long and short entries occur together, discard both. Accepted entries may initialize a shared trailing stop from the confirmed standard close, at a configurable volatility- or tick-based distance. Ratchet the stop only toward tighter risk. Exit a long when a later confirmed standard close is strictly below the previous stop; exit a short when it is strictly above. Ignore wick breaches and equal closes. When active-trade gating is enabled, block new entries while a trail is active and on its exit bar; same-direction entries do not reset the original entry or stop.
