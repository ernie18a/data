In day mode, external market data affects the grade of a long or short alert; it does not affect the underlying unfiltered signal. Use configurable parameters for all periods, thresholds, weights, scores, and limits.

Form a bullish trend when at least the required number of these conditions hold: the previous completed higher-timeframe fast EMA is above the slow EMA; the two latest confirmed swing highs and lows are both rising; the higher-timeframe slow EMA has risen over the slope lookback measured on chart bars. Reverse the comparisons for a bearish trend.

On the preceding chart bar, require the corresponding trend and a pullback: the recent low for a long, or high for a short, reaches the fast EMA within an ATR tolerance; the recent high-low range is at least the minimum ATR depth and no more than the maximum fraction of the range over the longer lookback. Also require a bullish engulfing candle or hammer for a long, or bearish engulfing candle or shooting star for a short. An engulfing candle has the opposite color to the prior candle, engulfs its body, and meets the minimum body-size ratio. A pin candle has a nonzero body, a sufficiently long rejection wick, and a sufficiently short opposite wick. On the current bar, require a break above the preceding high for a long or below the preceding low for a short, and bar-close confirmation if strict mode is enabled.

Use the current close as entry. Place the stop at the farther of the ATR-distance stop and the recent pullback extreme plus an ATR buffer. Set the first target at the configured multiple of stop distance. Reject a signal below the minimum target-to-risk ratio. Enforce the configured spacing between signals and daily signal cap; either direction consumes the shared allowance.

For grading, combine points for current trend strength, preceding pullback depth quality, preceding pattern strength, current break distance in ATR, and target-to-risk quality. Strong patterns receive more points. Below the full-credit target-to-risk threshold, reduce the risk-reward points by a linearly scaled penalty. Add a nonnegative, capped macro contribution favoring the signal direction; clamp the total score and map it to the configured grade thresholds.

To calculate the macro contribution, use the previous completed observations and lookback changes of a dollar index, silver, and a long-term government yield on the configured macro timeframe. Standardize each change against the rolling mean and standard deviation of its historical change series, using zero when the standard deviation is unavailable or zero. Classify each standardized change into strong, mild, or neutral bullish/bearish contributions: falling dollar and yields favor long gold, while rising silver favors long gold. Weight and sum available contributions, normalize by the maximum possible weight of available inputs, and scale and round to the configured maximum modifier. Reverse its sign for shorts. Missing inputs contribute nothing; disable the modifier when macro analysis is off.

Emit a grade-filtered alert only when the underlying signal passes and its resulting grade meets the selected alert minimum. Unfiltered signals and unfiltered alert conditions remain independent of the macro grade.
