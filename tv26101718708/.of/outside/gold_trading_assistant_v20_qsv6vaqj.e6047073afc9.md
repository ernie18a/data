Choose either trend-pullback mode or mean-reversion mode. In trend-pullback mode, require at least two of: higher-timeframe fast EMA above/below slow EMA, higher-timeframe slow EMA rising/falling, and recent swing structure forming higher highs with higher lows / lower highs with lower lows. For a long/short setup, require a pullback that reaches the fast EMA area, has depth within an ATR-based minimum and a fraction of the recent broader range maximum, and is followed by a bullish/bearish engulfing candle or hammer/shooting star; enter when the next bar breaks that pattern candle’s high/low. In mean-reversion mode, require the prior bar to reach the lower/upper Bollinger Band while RSI is oversold/overbought and form a hammer/shooting star; enter long/short when the current close returns inside that band. Require the projected reward-to-risk ratio to meet a minimum, and apply signal spacing and daily trade-count limits. Place the stop beyond the recent pullback or rejection extreme with an ATR buffer, using the farther of that structural stop and an ATR-based stop. For trend-pullback trades, set staged profit targets by reward-to-risk and trail the remainder using an EMA; for mean-reversion trades, target the Bollinger Band midpoint. Exit on stop or target; after a trend trade reaches its first target, exit the runner when price crosses the trailing EMA against the position.
