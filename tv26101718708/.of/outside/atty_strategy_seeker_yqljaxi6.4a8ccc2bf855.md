Continuously evaluate candidate long and short strategies that combine price or indicator entry signals with an exit rule and an optional market filter. Entry signals cover moving-average crosses, price crossing averages, oscillator extremes or crossings, band extremes, recent-high/low breakouts, MACD or trend-indicator turns, inside-bar breaks, consecutive closes, directional-index crosses, reversal patterns, and gap moves. Filters may require alignment with a long-term trend or momentum, or a volatility regime. Qualify candidates by trade count, optionally exclude unprofitable candidates, then rank them by the chosen performance metric. Select the top-ranked strategy or basket, updating membership as rankings change; go long or short when a selected strategy's entry condition and filter agree, and exit according to its assigned rule: after a holding period, at stop or target levels, by a trailing stop, or when the opposite entry signal occurs. Signal-close decisions normally execute at the next bar's open; stop exits account for gaps through the stop.
