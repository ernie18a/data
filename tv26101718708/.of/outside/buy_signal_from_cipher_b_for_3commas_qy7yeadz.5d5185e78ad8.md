On each confirmed bar, calculate a WaveTrend oscillator from the traded instrument's high-low-close average: subtract its exponential moving average, divide by 0.015 times the exponential moving average of the absolute deviation, and exponentially smooth the result. Smooth that oscillator with a simple moving average to obtain its signal line. When flat, enter long if the oscillator crosses above the signal line while the signal line is at or below an oversold threshold, and both trend filters pass. The external filter passes if BTC perpetual futures' five-minute close is above its five-minute simple moving average on the current or previous chart bar. The local filter passes if the traded instrument's close is above its hourly simple moving average on the current or previous chart bar. At entry, set a fixed percentage profit target and stop loss from the signal-bar close. Exit the long when either is reached. If the optional local-trend exit is enabled, also close any open position when the traded instrument's close is below its hourly simple moving average on the current or previous chart bar. Do not open short positions.
