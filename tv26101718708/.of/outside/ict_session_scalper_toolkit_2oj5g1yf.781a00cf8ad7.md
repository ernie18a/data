During a London or New York killzone, arm a long setup when price sweeps an unswept sellside pool; arm a short when it sweeps an unswept buyside pool. Within a limited period after the sweep, require a strong candle in the reversal direction, then signal on validation of an oppositely formed fair-value gap as an inverted gap or the first retrace into a qualifying gap in that direction. Use the signal bar’s close as entry. Place the stop beyond the most extreme price reached since the sweep, with a small tick offset; skip the signal if the stop distance exceeds the applicable session or volatility-based cap. Apply the configured minimum setup grade. Grade confluence using higher-timeframe gaps, proximity to session opening-gap reference levels, recent divergence against a correlated market, equal-level liquidity swept, available liquidity toward the target, buying in discount or selling in premium, and nearby unswept liquidity left behind. Set the first target at the nearest unswept pool in the trade direction and the final target no closer than the required risk multiple. No exit signal is defined.
