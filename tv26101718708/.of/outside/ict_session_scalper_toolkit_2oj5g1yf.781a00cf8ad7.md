Track unswept liquidity pools from confirmed swing highs and lows, optionally including the completed Asia range and previous day's extremes. Merge nearby same-side pools, retain their touch counts, and mark a pool swept when price strictly passes it. A sellside sweep arms a long setup; a buyside sweep arms a short setup. A new same-side sweep resets that setup and its displacement confirmation. While armed within the configured window, track the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short, and confirm displacement when a candle in the trade direction has a body larger than a configured multiple of the average body. Sweep and displacement may occur on the same bar.

Track three-candle fair value gaps, optionally requiring displacement on the middle candle. A bearish gap becomes a bullish inverted gap when the close rises above its top; a bullish gap becomes a bearish inverted gap when the close falls below its bottom. An inverted gap is invalidated by a close back through its opposite edge. A zone can produce one retracement event: for a bullish zone, the low reaches its top and the close remains at or above its bottom; mirror this for a bearish zone. Original gaps cannot generate retracement events on their creation bar. A previously tapped zone remains tapped after inversion.

Emit a long signal when an unexpired long setup has confirmed bullish displacement and the current bar validates a bullish inverted gap or retraces into a bullish zone. Mirror the conditions for a short. Optionally require the signal bar to be confirmed and inside a configured killzone. Use the signal close as entry and a stop beyond the tracked sweep extreme by a configured tick buffer. If strict stop limits are enabled, reject a signal whose stop distance exceeds the configured session-specific point cap or ATR-based cap; automatic mode selects point caps for the specified index futures and ATR caps for other instruments.

Grade each otherwise eligible setup by adding points for entry or stop inside an active completed higher-timeframe gap; entry near the midnight open or a daily or weekly opening-gap midpoint; recent same-direction divergence against a correlated instrument; a sweep of a pool with multiple touches; enough unswept pools ahead within a configured ATR distance; and a long in discount or short in premium relative to the midpoint of the recent price range. Subtract a point if enough opposite-side pools remain behind entry within another configured ATR distance. Bullish divergence means the traded instrument makes a new lookback low while the correlated instrument does not; bearish divergence uses new highs. Map the score to configured grade thresholds and emit only if the grade meets the selected minimum. Missing correlated data disables the divergence point. A rejected setup remains armed until it expires or is reset; an emitted signal disarms its direction.

For a signal, set the first target to the nearest unswept pool in the trade direction, if one exists. Set the final target to the farther of that pool and the configured minimum reward-to-risk distance from entry. The script does not track positions or issue exit signals.
