For Bitcoin pairs, use the sum of enabled external exchange volumes when positive, otherwise use the traded instrument's volume. Create support and resistance zones around confirmed swing lows and highs, subject to configured age, count, magnitude and overlap limits. Set each zone's initial magnitude to pivot-bar volume times range. Rank live zones by magnitude and combine that rank with a defense score based on held retests, average volume, rejection depth and defended sweeps; decay the defense contribution with inactivity. Trade only heavy, strong zones. Go long when price sweeps below a support zone by the required ATR-based depth and subsequently closes back above the configured reclaim level; go short for the symmetric sweep above and reclaim below resistance. Optionally require a recent oversold or overbought RSI extreme, respectively. A reclaim is checked starting on the bar after the sweep. If price instead closes decisively beyond the zone, or remains beyond it after the reclaim window expires, stand aside and either flip or remove the zone. Place the stop beyond the sweep extreme; target the zone's opposite side first, then the next zone or unswept level in the trade direction. Do not enter without a qualifying sweep and reclaim.
