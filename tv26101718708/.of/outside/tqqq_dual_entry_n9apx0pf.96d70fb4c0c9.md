Trade TQQQ long using QQQ data on the chart timeframe. Evaluate signals at each bar close and execute queued entries and exits at the next bar's open. Maintain one position and record its entry method.

While flat, queue a long entry when either entry state turns from false to true. The moving-average entry state requires all enabled buffers: a configurable number of bars since QQQ last closed strictly below a configurable SMA or EMA, and QQQ close at least a configurable percentage above that average. Equal closes do not reset the bar count; the count is unavailable until a below-average close has occurred. If both buffers are disabled, the state is always true. The EMA entry state requires QQQ close above a configurable EMA, optionally QQQ volume strictly above a configurable multiple of its volume SMA, and optionally that same price EMA strictly rising for either a configurable number of consecutive comparisons or two consecutive comparisons. If both entries trigger together, choose the moving-average entry.

For a moving-average entry, queue an exit when the following state turns from false to true: QQQ close is strictly below a separate configurable SMA or EMA, optionally for a configurable number of consecutive bars, and optionally at least a configurable percentage below that average. For an EMA entry, queue an exit when QQQ close crosses below the entry EMA, unless QQQ has closed strictly above the moving-average entry average during the position; once that happens, permanently switch the position to the moving-average exit rule. Apply this switch before evaluating the current bar's exit.

Optionally stop out an existing position when the traded instrument's intrabar low reaches or falls below a configurable percentage beneath its entry price. Fill at the bar's open if the open is at or below the stop, otherwise at the stop price. Check this stop before executing queued orders, cancel any queued exit on a stop, and do not check a newly opened position for a stop until the next bar. After executions, evaluate the current close for new signals. A statistics-only virtual exit at the end does not change the trading position.
