Trade only during the configured session. Enter long when a bullish Renko brick appears and, if price confirmation is enabled, the current bar’s close, high, and low are each above the prior bar’s; enter short on the corresponding bearish brick with all three below the prior bar’s. When reversing a position whose open profit is below a configured threshold, one opposite brick suffices; otherwise require two consecutive opposite bricks. Close positions outside the session. Apply configured stop-loss and take-profit levels; after open profit reaches a breakeven trigger, move the stop to entry. Track the position’s peak open profit and close if it falls by the configured percentage once that peak has reached a configured minimum.
