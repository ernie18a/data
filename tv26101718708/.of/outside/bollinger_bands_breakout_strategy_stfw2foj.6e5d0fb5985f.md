On each bar, calculate upper and lower Bollinger bands from the traded instrument’s closing price. Enter long when price crosses above the upper band, and enter short when it crosses below the lower band, subject to the selected trade direction and enabled filters: price must be above the selected moving average for longs or below it for shorts; closing-price volatility must exceed its own moving average; the change from the prior daily close to the current close must exceed a positive threshold; and the bar must fall within the selected date range. Close a long when price crosses below the lower band and close a short when it crosses above the upper band. Also exit positions at their configured stop loss, profit target, or trailing stop, and enforce a maximum intraday loss limit. Trade direction can be configured as long and short, long only, or short only; automatic mode allows both directions for symbols identified as perpetual contracts and otherwise allows longs only. Filters and protective exits can be enabled or disabled as configured.
