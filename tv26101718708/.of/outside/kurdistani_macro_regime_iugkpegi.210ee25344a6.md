Use externally sourced macroeconomic series to generate directional signals for gold, Bitcoin and the US dollar. Evaluate all historical offsets on chart bars, using the script's configurable thresholds, score weights, lookbacks and missing-data defaults.

Calculate inflation from the consumer price index when it changes, otherwise use the fallback inflation values. Derive real interest rates, the US–European policy-rate differential, the yield-curve slope, money-supply growth, GDP growth, a manufacturing-employment RSI-based PMI proxy, volatility and credit spreads. Score growth from GDP growth, the PMI proxy, unemployment and GDP trend; score inflation from its level and trend; and score liquidity from money-supply growth, the policy rate, the yield curve and volatility. Use growth and inflation scores to identify recession, stagflation, expansion and recovery conditions. Calculate recession risk from the yield curve, unemployment change, PMI, GDP, real rates and credit spreads; calculate risk appetite from growth, liquidity, recession risk, the yield curve and credit spreads. Separately score monetary-policy pivot potential from yield-curve change, policy rate, unemployment, inflation and GDP, and dollar-liquidity stress from rate differentials, emerging-market and credit spreads, volatility and real rates.

Score gold from real rates, inflation level and trend, regime, liquidity, inflation breakevens and dollar-liquidity stress, with regime-dependent weights. Signal long gold when its rounded, bounded score reaches its bullish threshold and real rates are negative.

Score Bitcoin from liquidity, real rates, risk appetite, growth, the policy-rate differential, volatility and pivot potential, with regime-dependent weights. Signal long Bitcoin when its rounded, bounded score reaches its bullish threshold and the risk-appetite score indicates risk-on.

Score the US dollar from the policy-rate differential, real rates, growth, risk appetite, liquidity and dollar-liquidity stress. Signal a flight to the dollar when its rounded, bounded score exceeds its threshold and either risk-off or recession conditions hold.

Emit each directional signal only on the transition from false to true. No short or exit rules are specified.
