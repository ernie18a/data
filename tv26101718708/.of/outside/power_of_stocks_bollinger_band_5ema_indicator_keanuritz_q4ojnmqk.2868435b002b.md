Use the underlying instrument's candles and a configurable exponential moving average. For a bearish setup, the preceding candle's low must be strictly above the average and the current candle must break below that low; buy a put or sell a call. For a bullish setup, the preceding candle's high must be strictly below the average and the current candle must break above that high; buy a call or sell a put. Optionally also require the current close to be below the preceding close for a bearish signal, or above it for a bullish signal. Use at-the-money options for the moving-average strategy. Set the underlying-based stop beyond the preceding candle's opposite extreme, optionally with an additional buffer, and the target at a configurable multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. For Bollinger-band options buying, use an at-the-money or slightly out-of-the-money option: buy a put when the preceding candle is entirely above the upper band and the current candle breaks its low, or buy a call when the preceding candle is entirely below the lower band and the current candle breaks its high. Apply the same optional close confirmation, stop and target construction. Exit when the underlying strictly crosses the stop or target; check the stop first if both are crossed. For options bought using the band strategy, close the position on its entry day unless its profit exceeds a configurable threshold for carrying it overnight.
