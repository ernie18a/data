When enabled, use one of three signal modes: combined, volatility bands, or moving average. For a short, mark a candle entirely above the upper volatility band, the moving average, or both, respectively. Enter short if the next candle breaks that signal candle’s low. For a long, mark a candle entirely below the lower band, the moving average, or both; enter long if the next candle breaks its high. An optional confirmation requires the entry candle to close lower than the signal candle for shorts or higher for longs; if so, enter at that close. Set the stop beyond the signal candle’s opposite extreme and the profit target at the configured risk multiple. Exit when either stop or target is reached.
