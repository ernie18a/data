When flat, enter long if the close crosses above the highest high of the preceding channel lookback and the market is in an uptrend: price is above a long-term EMA that is rising. Enter short if the close crosses below the lowest low of that prior range and price is below a falling long-term EMA. If higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, require the last closed higher-timeframe price to agree with the trade direction relative to its EMA. On entry, set an initial protective stop a volatility-based distance from entry. Then trail the stop from the best price reached since entry, using a volatility-based offset; ratchet it only toward profit and exit when price touches it. There is no fixed profit target.
