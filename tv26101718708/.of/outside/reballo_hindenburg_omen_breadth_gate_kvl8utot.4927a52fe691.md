Use daily NYSE market internals to flag a risk warning when all conditions hold: new highs and new lows each form at least a threshold share of issues; the NYSE Composite is above its value a lookback period earlier; the McClellan Oscillator is below zero; and new highs are fewer than a specified multiple of new lows. Keep the warning active for a defined period after it fires, extending it if it fires again. While active, freeze new risk. Override the warning when the weekly share of S&P 500 stocks above their 200-day average falls below an oversold threshold; in that state, avoid panic-cutting risk. Otherwise, provide no risk gate.
