Use confirmed daily USDMYR data and its rolling average to assess MYR strength. When USDMYR is materially above its average, treat the resulting soft MYR as a bullish FCPO bias; when materially below, treat strong MYR as a bearish FCPO bias. Apply either bias only when the rolling FCPO–USDMYR correlation is sufficiently strong in absolute value; otherwise ignore the MYR signal. This modifies position conviction or stop-loss sizing and does not generate entries or exits.
