Use PVSRA candle classifications from ETH/USDT on Binance, plus the traded chart’s 50- and 200-period EMAs. Record red-vector candles that open and close below the 50 EMA, counting red candles that close below it; count green vectors that close above the 200 EMA. Enter long on a confirmed green-vector candle opening above the 50 EMA when a prior red vector is recorded and the red-candle count is nonzero. Set the stop at the lowest ETH low over the recent lookback window and the target above entry by the same distance multiplied by the chosen risk/reward factor. Also record a sell setup when a blue or green vector opens above both EMAs. Close the long if the next bar is red and opens and closes above the 200 EMA, provided at least one green vector has closed above that EMA; close on the bar after a buy if it is red or violet, or on the bar after a sell setup if it is green. The strategy opens no short positions.
