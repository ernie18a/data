Within the configured trading date range, calculate the traded asset’s premium or discount against its price on a comparison market, converted into the same currency using an external exchange rate. For the designated benchmark asset, an alternate mode calculates this premium from its local-currency and dollar-market prices. Compare the current premium with configurable upper and lower thresholds. Thresholds may be absolute levels, volatility-band boundaries, or relative positions within the rolling range of smoothed premium highs and lows. On the first bar the premium falls below the lower threshold, enter long; on the first bar it rises above the upper threshold, enter short. In long-only mode, the upper-threshold event closes the long; in short-only mode, the lower-threshold event closes the short. In both-directions mode, each event enters the corresponding direction, reversing an opposing position if needed.
