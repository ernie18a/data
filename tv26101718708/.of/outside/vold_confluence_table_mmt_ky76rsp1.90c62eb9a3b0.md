Use external market breadth and tick data to score bullish and bearish alignment. Award bullish points for positive and strongly positive volume breadth, positive and strongly positive advancing breadth, positive tick, and elevated positive tick momentum; award bearish points for negative and strongly negative volume breadth, nonpositive advancing breadth, nonpositive tick, and elevated negative tick momentum. A score of at least five signals a trend day in that direction; a score of four signals high confidence. Favor trades with the indicated trend. If volume breadth is above its moving average, rising, and strongly positive, avoid shorts; if it is below its moving average, falling, and strongly negative, avoid longs. Low scores on both sides indicate chop and no clear directional edge. When advancing breadth and volume breadth have opposing signs, flag divergence and reduce risk.
