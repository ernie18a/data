On each bar, calculate fast and slow EMAs of the traded instrument’s close and of the CRYPTOCAP:OTHERS.D close on the same timeframe. Classify their relationships as: A, instrument fast at or above slow and index fast at or above slow; B, instrument fast at or above slow and index fast below slow; C, instrument fast below slow and index fast above slow; D, instrument fast below slow and index fast at or below slow. A long signal occurs when the instrument fast EMA is above its slow EMA, or when they are equal and the index fast EMA is below its slow EMA. A short signal occurs when the instrument fast EMA is below its slow EMA.

Maintain the latest confirmed swing high and low: a swing is confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side when its high or low equals the extreme over that entire window. Set the persistent swing trend upward when close exceeds the latest swing high and downward when close falls below the latest swing low; otherwise retain it. If both updates apply on the same bar, the downward update takes precedence.

For an open position, calculate directional percentage profit from its entry price to the current close. If profit falls below the stop threshold, close a long only if there is no short signal, or close a short only if there is no long signal, and block re-entry in that direction. Clear a long block when the previous classification was A or B and the current classification is not C; clear a short block when the previous classification was C or D and the current classification is not B.

Once per position, close half of a profitable long when classification changes from A to anything else, profit exceeds the scale-out threshold, and there is no short signal. Similarly, close half of a profitable short when classification changes from D to anything else, profit exceeds that threshold, and there is no long signal. Reset the partial-exit flag on a new entry.

Close a short if the swing trend is upward, and close a long if it is downward. Also, when the corresponding direction is not blocked, a long signal closes an existing short and a short signal closes an existing long. When flat, enter long if the long signal is present, long entry is not blocked, the swing trend is upward, and RSI is below its upper threshold. Enter short if the short signal is present, short entry is not blocked, the swing trend is downward, and RSI is above its lower threshold. Evaluate entries using the current position state; a closing order does not itself make the position flat during that same calculation.
