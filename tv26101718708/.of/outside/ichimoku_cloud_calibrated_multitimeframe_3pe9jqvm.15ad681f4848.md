On each eligible bar, determine the traded instrument’s cloud bias: long when the selected source is above both displaced cloud spans, short when below both, and neutral inside. Construct the spans from rolling high/low midpoint lines, using either fixed periods or periods derived from a smoothed dominant-cycle estimate. A fresh signal occurs only when bias changes from neutral or opposite to long or short; optionally require a confirmed bar.

For each nonneutral bias, calculate a weighted, normalized strength score from alignment of the fast and base midpoint lines, the selected source versus its displaced past value, displaced cloud color, undisplaced cloud color, base-line slope, the fraction of three confirmed higher-timeframe clouds agreeing with the bias, ATR-normalized cloud thickness, confirmed swing-pivot structure direction, and relative volume. Use neutral contributions when optional confirmation data is disabled or unavailable. Higher-timeframe clouds use fixed periods even when the chart cloud uses adaptive periods.

Classify the regime using ADX, source efficiency ratio, and a standardized exponentially decayed accumulation of absolute log returns: trend requires both trend measures above their thresholds; otherwise classify a volatility cluster as volatile and the remainder as range. Calculate conviction as a weighted combination of strength, regime, higher-timeframe agreement, agreement of cumulative signed lower-timeframe volume change with bias, and optional cross-asset momentum agreement. Sign lower-timeframe volume by whether its close is at least its open; use chart-bar signed volume when lower-timeframe data is unavailable. Cross-asset momentum is the sign of its price change over a lookback, multiplied by a configurable direction.

Optionally learn the minimum strength from historical fresh signals. After a fixed evaluation window, classify each signal as successful if its maximum favorable high or low excursion from the signal bar’s close reaches an ATR-based target. Group evaluated signals into strength bins, accumulate bins from strongest downward, and select the lowest threshold that meets the target success rate with enough observations, stopping after the rate falls below target once a qualifying threshold exists. Until a learned threshold is available, use the configured minimum.

On a fresh signal, issue the corresponding long or short action only if strength and conviction meet their minimums and no enabled veto applies. Veto for an optional volatility-index spike, all three higher-timeframe clouds opposing the signal, a range or volatile regime, nonzero cross-asset momentum opposing the signal, nonneutral swing structure opposing the signal, or relative volume below its minimum. Ignore unavailable optional feeds. Swing structure changes direction when close first breaks the latest confirmed pivot high or low. With no fresh signal, retain the displayed cloud-direction hold or flat state; the code defines no separate position-exit action.
