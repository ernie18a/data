On each confirmed bar, emit a long or short signal for every enabled setup that matches and passes its directional filters. Filters may require minimum volume, relative volume, bar range relative to ATR, price, and an allowed session. They may also require the instrument's close and a selected external index's close each to be above their respective EMAs for long signals or below them for short signals. Relative volume is current volume divided by its moving average, or zero if that average is not positive.

Inside bar: The preceding bar must lie within the bar before it, whose range must be positive. Signal long if the current close exceeds the inside bar's high, or short if it falls below its low. An optional additional filter requires the close on the corresponding side of the instrument's EMA.

High breakout: Signal long when the close crosses above the highest high of a preceding lookback window, with the previous close at or below that same level. Optionally require sufficient relative volume and that every close in a preceding consolidation window lies between the breakout level and a specified proximity below it.

Gap setup: At each new day, compare the open with the preceding close. Start tracking the gap if its size meets the minimum; otherwise deactivate the prior gap. Track its direction, endpoints, and whether its partial-fill threshold has ever been reached. Measure fill using the low for an upward gap and the high for a downward gap; deactivate the gap before evaluating signals once fill reaches the near-complete threshold. While a downward gap remains active and has reached partial fill, signal long on a bullish bar closing above the lower gap endpoint if partial triggers are allowed, or if full triggers are allowed and the bar's low is at or below that endpoint. While an upward gap remains active, signal short on a bearish bar whose high is below the upper gap endpoint and low is above the lower endpoint. Signals can recur while the gap remains active.

Outside bar: Require the current high above the preceding high, the current low below the preceding low, and a body at least a specified proportion of the current range. Signal long on a bullish bar closing at or above the preceding high; signal short on a bearish bar closing at or below the preceding low.
