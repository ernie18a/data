Generate a buy signal when the selected entry rule triggers: either price crosses above the adaptive centerline; price crosses above it and remains above while the trend slope rises, or price crosses up through the lower volatility band. Generate a sell signal when the corresponding bearish rule triggers: price crosses below the centerline; crosses below it and remains below while the trend slope falls, or crosses down through the upper band. Optionally require a confirmed bar close. Track bullish state: emit a buy only when entering bullish state and a sell only when leaving it. Separately, treat a close below either the peak-anchored trend stop or the recent-window entry-anchored stop as a stop breach; recovery above either stop is a recovery signal.
