In current-instrument mode, classify the current daily close above the band as long-biased, below it as short-biased, and inside it as neutral. In paired-index mode, classify as long-biased only when both Nifty and Bank Nifty closes are above their respective bands, short-biased only when both are below, and neutral otherwise.
