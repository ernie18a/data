Smooth the external NAAIM exposure index and equity put/call ratio. Convert each to a fear-to-greed score: NAAIM scores rise from fear to greed as exposure rises, while the put/call score rises as the ratio falls; cap each score at its fear and greed bounds, weight them, and sum them. Smooth the combined score. After the configured start date, enter long when the smoothed score forms a local trough below the buy threshold and then turns upward. Exit the long when it forms a local peak above the sell threshold and then turns downward, or on the configured closing date when date-based closing is enabled. The strategy makes no short entries.
