Calculate a market score from the Nifty 50, sector indices, and the fetched stocks in the selected sector or page. For the Nifty and each sector index, sum directional votes from price versus a fast EMA, fast versus slow EMA, RSI relative to upper and lower thresholds, Supertrend direction, and positive versus negative directional movement. RSI between its thresholds contributes zero; equality in the other comparisons takes the bearish vote. For the Nifty, optionally add a weighted futures open-interest buildup vote: rising price with rising open interest is bullish, rising price with falling open interest is half as bullish, falling price with rising open interest is bearish, and falling price with falling open interest is half as bearish. Treat small changes or missing data as a zero buildup vote. Classify each sector index as bullish, bearish, or sideways using its score and trend-strength threshold; weak ADX forces sideways. Compute sector breadth as bullish minus bearish sectors, divided by all classified sectors and scaled to the range of the directional vote score. Compute stock buildup breadth as the sum of the same full and half buildup votes across fetched stocks, divided by the number fetched and scaled to that range. Average the Nifty score and whichever breadth measures are enabled and available. Classify the average using symmetric bullish and bearish thresholds, with stronger categories at more extreme values; force sideways when Nifty ADX is weak and the absolute average is below the specified override threshold. A strong bullish verdict advises buying dips, a bullish verdict favors longs, and a strong bearish verdict advises selling rallies. A bearish verdict advises caution, while sideways advises trading the range. The script does not define dip, rally, entry, or exit conditions.
