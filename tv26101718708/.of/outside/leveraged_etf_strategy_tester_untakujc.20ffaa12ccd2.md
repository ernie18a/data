For the leveraged ETF and a separate reference ETF, track each instrument’s highest observed close and its current percentage drawdown from that high. Initially allocate capital between them using configurable base weights. Add contributions to cash each month.

Activate a higher leveraged-ETF target when its drawdown reaches a configurable threshold. While activated, record the calendar year when its close returns to its observed high; clear the activation on the first bar of a later calendar year. Independently, mark the reference ETF as having entered a deep drawdown when its drawdown reaches a configurable threshold. Once its drawdown subsequently falls to or below that threshold multiplied by one minus a configurable recovery fraction, start a bottom period and record the current year. Suppress rebalancing during that period. On the first bar in a later year, end the bottom period and clear the higher-allocation activation and its recovery year.

At each configured monthly, quarterly, or annual boundary, decide whether to rebalance using the bottom-period state before the current bar’s bottom-period reset. If permitted, value both holdings at their current closes, add accumulated cash, and rebalance to the higher leveraged-ETF target if activated, otherwise the base target; allocate the remainder to the reference ETF and use all accumulated cash. Apply trigger activation and resets before selecting the target.
