Calculate percentage growth over a configurable lookback for each selected country's M2 and for a configurable comparison index. Weight each M2 growth rate by its current M2 amount converted to USD, discounting China's amount by a configurable factor, and sum the weighted rates. Optionally blend growth in Fed assets minus the Treasury general account and reverse repos into the US contribution; if US M2 is excluded, blend it into the total using a standalone weight. Treat unavailable growth inputs or a zero prior value as zero growth. Go long or accumulate when weighted liquidity growth minus comparison-index growth is at or above a configurable threshold. A new entry alert occurs when the difference crosses above that threshold. No short or exit rule is specified.
