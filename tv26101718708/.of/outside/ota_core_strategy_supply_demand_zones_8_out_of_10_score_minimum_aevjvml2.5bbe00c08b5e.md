Use configurable parameters for all lookback lengths, thresholds, scoring weights, volatility assumptions, strike increments and expiry ranges.

For option alerts, independently construct rolling demand and supply zones from the current bar and a fixed lookback: demand runs from the lowest low to the highest open or close; supply runs from the lowest open or close to the highest high. Signal on the first bar whose close enters either zone. Optionally also signal on the first bar outside a zone but within the configured approach distance of its body-based boundary. These rolling zones include the current bar and are independent of the scored zones; consequently, with valid current OHLC data, the close normally lies inside both zones and approach conditions cannot occur.

For each demand or supply setup, calculate historical volatility as the annualized standard deviation of log returns and an implied-volatility proxy as annualized ATR divided by the current close. Classify their ratio as rich at or above the upper threshold, cheap at or below the lower threshold, and neutral otherwise; rich takes precedence if thresholds overlap. Use the proxy itself as historical volatility when close is nonpositive, and use a fallback ratio when historical volatility is nonpositive.

For demand, rich volatility selects a bull put credit spread: sell a put and buy a further-out put at the configured short and long absolute delta targets. Neutral selects a purchased call at the buffered demand stop level. Cheap selects a bull call debit spread: buy a call at that stop level and sell a call above it by the configured price-based spread width. For supply, rich selects a bear call credit spread using the corresponding call delta targets; neutral buys a put at the buffered supply stop; cheap buys a put at that stop and sells a put below it by the configured spread width. Set the demand stop below the zone's wick boundary and the supply stop above it by a configured fraction of zone width. Round strikes and spread width according to configured price bands. For credit spreads, calculate Black-Scholes delta using the midpoint of the expiry range, the volatility proxy and a configured interest rate; reproduce the source's bounded iterative strike search, including its put-search bound updates.

Separately, display option setups for qualifying historical zones. Scan candidate bases followed by bullish or bearish legs within the configured lookback. Require every leg candle to have the matching direction and minimum body move, and require the base's average body and every individual base body to be sufficiently small relative to the leg's average body. Demand uses the base's highest body edge and lowest wick; supply uses its lowest body edge and highest wick. Count subsequent candles overlapping the zone as tests, and mark it mitigated if a subsequent close breaches its wick boundary. Score base tightness, leg-to-base body ratio, freshness, the last leg candle's close location and volume spike, and total leg range relative to current ATR. Apply the configured minimum score, freshness and mitigation filters; retain only the first configured number of qualifying zones of each type in scan order, and select the highest score among those for each dashboard setup, keeping the first on ties. Apply the same option selection and buffered-stop rules to each selected zone. No option exit order is specified.
