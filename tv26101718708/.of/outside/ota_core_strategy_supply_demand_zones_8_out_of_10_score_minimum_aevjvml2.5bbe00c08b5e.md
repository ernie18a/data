Identify demand zones from a compact base followed by a strong bullish departure, and supply zones from a compact base followed by a strong bearish departure. Score each zone using base tightness, departure strength, freshness, candle close location, volume, and range expansion; retain zones meeting the configured score and freshness or mitigation rules. Use the body edge nearest price as the proximal boundary and the farthest base wick as the distal boundary. A close inside a demand zone signals prepare long / call; a close inside supply signals prepare short / put. Signal an approach when price is just outside the proximal edge and within the configured approach distance. Treat a close beyond the distal boundary as the zone's stop-loss reference and mark it mitigated; no separate exit alert is generated. For options, compare the volatility proxy derived from ATR with historical volatility derived from returns. When implied-to-historical volatility is rich, use a credit spread in the zone's direction: bull put at demand, bear call at supply, with short and farther-out protective long legs selected by target delta. When volatility is neutral, buy an in-the-money call at demand or put at supply, with strike at the stop reference. When volatility is cheap, use a debit spread in the zone's direction: buy the call or put at the stop reference and sell a farther out-of-the-money option. Select strikes and expirations using the configured delta, spread-width, and DTE rules.
