At the first chart bar after the opening chart bar of each selected higher-timeframe regular session closes, use the configured price source from that opening bar and the prior completed higher-timeframe OHLC to calculate the central pivot range and selected pivot levels. On enabled weekdays and within the configured date window: if the source is above the range, sell a put spread; if below, sell a call spread; if strictly inside, sell an iron condor; if equal to either boundary, make no decision. For a put spread, choose the short strike from the range’s lower boundary when the source exceeds the selected resistance trigger; otherwise use the average of the central pivot and the lower of the prior high/selected support level. For a call spread, choose the short strike from the range’s upper boundary when the source is below the selected support trigger; otherwise use the average of the central pivot and the higher of the prior low/selected resistance level. Round put strikes down and call strikes up to the configured strike increment; buy the corresponding farther-out option one increment beyond each short strike. For an iron condor, sell calls at the rounded-up first traditional resistance and puts at the rounded-down first traditional support, and buy each wing one increment farther out. The higher timeframe, source, pivot method, strike increment, weekday filters, and date window are configurable.
