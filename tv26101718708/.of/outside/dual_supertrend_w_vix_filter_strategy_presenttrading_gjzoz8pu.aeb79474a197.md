On each confirmed bar, calculate two SuperTrend directions from the traded instrument using separate configurable ATR lookbacks and multipliers. Enter long when both are bullish, long trading is enabled, and the optional external VIX filter passes; enter short when both are bearish, short trading is enabled, and its optional VIX filter passes. The long filter can require VIX below its mean minus two standard deviations, below its mean, falling below its short EMA, above its mean, rising above its EMA, or impose no restriction. The short filter can require VIX above its mean plus two standard deviations, above its mean, rising above its EMA, or impose no restriction. VIX mean and standard deviation use a configurable lookback. Entries may reverse an opposing position. Close a long if either trend turns bearish, and close a short if either turns bullish. Optional exits also support percentage-based or ATR-based staged profit targets, stop losses, and configurable standard or trailing exits.
