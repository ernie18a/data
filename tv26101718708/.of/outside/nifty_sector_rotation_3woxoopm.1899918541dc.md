For each sector, compare its smoothed price ratio to the benchmark and the momentum of that ratio, standardizing both against their recent histories around a neutral level. If both relative strength and momentum are at or above neutral, classify it as leading and favor longs. If relative strength is at or above neutral but momentum is below, classify it as weakening and tighten stops or exit. If both are below neutral, classify it as lagging and avoid or short. If relative strength is below neutral while momentum is at or above neutral, classify it as improving and treat it as an early reversal candidate.
