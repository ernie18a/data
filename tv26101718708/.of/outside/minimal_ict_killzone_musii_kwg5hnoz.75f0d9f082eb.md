On confirmed bars, signal long when price wicks below a recent swing low or completed session low and closes back above it; signal short when it wicks above a recent swing high or completed session high and closes back below it. Also signal long when price makes a lower confirmed swing low than its prior swing but a correlated comparison instrument does not make a lower low over the corresponding swing windows; signal short when price makes a higher confirmed swing high but the comparison instrument does not make a higher high. For inverse-correlated pairs, reverse the comparison. The first confirmed wick-through and close-back across the current day's 10:00 New York open signals in the direction of the close. These are directional entry signals; no exit rule is defined.
