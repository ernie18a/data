Use this as a directional filter for the traded instrument. Assign a bullish vote when the instrument and a related market are both rising over the same lookback, and a bearish vote when both are falling; otherwise assign neutral. Flag divergence when their rate-of-change difference exceeds its configured threshold while they do not share a direction. Assign a bullish vote when volatility is falling sharply, a bearish vote when it is rising sharply, and neutral otherwise. For market breadth, vote with the direction of smoothed breadth; at configured extremes, either vote with the extreme or against it according to whether the selected interpretation is continuation or exhaustion. Sum the three votes: a sufficiently positive total supports long setups, a sufficiently negative total supports short setups, and a middle or neutral total suggests standing aside or reducing exposure. Treat divergence as caution. This indicator is a confirmation filter, not a standalone entry or exit trigger.
