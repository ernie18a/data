On 15-minute or higher charts, use market structure to set direction: higher highs and higher lows favor longs; lower highs and lower lows favor shorts. Mark the previous day’s high and low and the 11:00 open. After a session high or low is swept, seek confirmation from correlated-asset SMT divergence and a CISD level at the sweep zone. Enter in the favored direction at a fair value gap and target opposing liquidity, such as the opposite buy-side or sell-side liquidity or the previous day’s opposite extreme.
