For each detector, calculate true range as the maximum of high minus low, absolute high minus previous close, and absolute low minus previous close; use the current close if the previous close is unavailable. Smooth true range with a simple moving average over a configurable period. Set the reversal threshold to the greater of this average times a configurable multiplier and the current dominance close times a configurable minimum percentage. In confirmation mode, generate an upward or downward event when both individual phases first agree in that direction, including agreement after a disagreement. Suppress consecutive events in the same direction until an opposite event occurs. With signal inversion enabled, a downward dominance event signals buy and an upward event signals sell; with inversion disabled, reverse that mapping.
