Track stablecoin dominance using the combined dominance of two major stablecoins, either index alone, or a signal requiring both indices to agree. For each selected series, follow its rising or falling phase and update its trailing high or low. A pullback from that extreme that reaches an ATR-scaled threshold reverses the phase. When dominance turns upward, signal short or sell the traded coin; when it turns downward, signal long or buy it. In confirmation mode, signal only when both indices enter the same phase, and suppress consecutive signals in the same direction. The framework defines no separate exit rule.
