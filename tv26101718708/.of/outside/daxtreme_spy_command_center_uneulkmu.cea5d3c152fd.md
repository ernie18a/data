On each bar, build the underlying's opening-range high and low during a configurable session and lock them afterward; reset the range, armed directions, daily signal flags, and position state on each new day. Compute session VWAP, fast and slow close EMAs, a bias RSI on a fixed higher timeframe, and an entry RSI on a configurable timeframe. Define directional EMA trend as the fast EMA moving strictly in that direction over two consecutive changes, and directional alignment as the fast EMA being on the corresponding side of the slow EMA. Compute cumulative volume delta by adding volume signed by close versus open, resetting to the signed bar volume on a new day; its direction is determined by comparing it with its previous-bar value.

For calls, the base conditions are close above VWAP and the fast EMA; if enabled, require bullish EMA trend and alignment. For puts, reverse these comparisons. If enabled, also require absolute close-to-fast-EMA distance as a percentage of close to be at most the contract-expiry-specific maximum, and absolute close-to-VWAP distance as a percentage of close to be at least the contract-expiry-specific minimum. When close crosses above the locked range high with call base conditions satisfied, arm calls and disarm puts; when close crosses below the locked range low with put base conditions satisfied, arm puts and disarm calls.

A call retest requires calls to be armed, the bar low to touch or cross the range high, and close to finish above it; a put retest requires puts to be armed, the bar high to touch or cross the range low, and close to finish below it. If retests are disabled, use the corresponding range breakout on the current bar instead. A call rejection requires the low to touch or cross the fast EMA, close above it, and close above open; a put rejection reverses these conditions. If rejection is disabled, treat it as satisfied.

Score each direction by adding one point for each of: close on the directional side of VWAP; close on the directional side of the fast EMA; directional fast-EMA trend; directional EMA alignment; the selected retest or breakout condition; rejection satisfied; bias RSI beyond its directional threshold; entry RSI beyond its directional threshold; cumulative volume delta moving in the direction; and the extension filter satisfied. A disabled extension filter or rejection requirement counts as satisfied. Buy a call or put when its base, retest or breakout, and rejection conditions all hold and its score reaches the configured minimum. If the daily limit is enabled, allow only one signal per direction per day. On a signal, record that direction as triggered, disarm it, and set the position state to that direction; a later opposite signal replaces the state.

For an active call, issue an exit warning and clear the position state if close falls below the fast EMA or VWAP, subject to each exit condition being enabled. For an active put, do so if close rises above either enabled level. Evaluate exits after updating the position state from any current-bar signals. Take-profit levels and hits are informational and do not change the position state.
