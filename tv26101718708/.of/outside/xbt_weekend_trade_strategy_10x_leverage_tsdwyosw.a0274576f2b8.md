During the active trading window, compare the instrument’s price with Friday’s daily close. On Saturday or Sunday, when flat, go short if price exceeds Friday’s close by the configured rise threshold; otherwise, go long if it falls below Friday’s close by the configured decline threshold. While short, add to the short if price rises beyond the configured threshold above the average entry price. While already long on Saturday or Sunday, submit a long entry. Close all positions when open profit exceeds the configured fraction of initial capital, or on Monday.
