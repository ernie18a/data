Using weekly Commitment of Traders data for the instrument, calculate commercial traders’ net position (long contracts minus short contracts) and its percentile rank over a multi-year history. Treat a low percentile as an extreme net-short position. If commercials have reached that extreme recently and their net position is rising while still net short, signal a potential bottom forming. If the net position then crosses above zero, signal a confirmed bottom tell and long bias. An extreme position that is not rising indicates commercials are still pressing shorts; otherwise remain neutral. This indicator provides no short or exit rule.
