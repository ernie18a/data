For each configured market, optionally invert prices by replacing the high with the reciprocal of the low and the low with the reciprocal of the high. Across each configured cycle, compare each market's current period high and low with those of its immediately preceding period. The cycles use weekly bars, daily bars, and successive New York-time sessions and their nested quarters, with intraday extremes accumulated from lower-timeframe bars. A bearish signal exists when at least one market exceeds its previous high and at least one does not; a bullish signal exists when at least one market falls below its previous low and at least one does not. For each signal, select the last market in configured order among either the breakers or the non-confirming markets, according to the display setting. Bullish and bearish signals can coexist. The scanner specifies no exit rule.
