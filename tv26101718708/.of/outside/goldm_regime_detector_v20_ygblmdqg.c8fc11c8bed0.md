On each daily futures bar, calculate ADX, fast and slow ATR, Bollinger %B, and the normalized per-bar slope of an EMA. Classify the slope as upward, downward, or flat using positive and negative thresholds. Rank current fast ATR by the fraction of prior ATR observations strictly below it; also rank the ATR from a fixed number of bars ago against that same set of prior observations. Calculate days to expiry by rounding the time remaining up to whole days and flooring at zero.

Apply these decisions in order:
1. At or below the forced-close expiry threshold, close all options.
2. If ADX crosses above its breakout threshold, the ATR percentile increase exceeds its spike threshold, or %B newly crosses either extreme boundary, take no new exposure. Close or roll the tested leg of existing positions; allow the untested leg to decay or trail it.
3. If ADX is below its no-trend threshold, ATR percentile is within the combined adequate-to-rich range, fast ATR divided by slow ATR does not exceed the expansion threshold, %B is within its middle range, expiry is beyond the full-size cutoff, and the event flag is off, sell a full-size two-sided position.
4. Otherwise, if ADX is at or above the no-trend threshold, ATR percentile is within the combined adequate-to-rich range, the ATR ratio does not exceed the expansion threshold, expiry is beyond the full-size cutoff, and the event flag and rich-volatility condition are not both present, sell a full-size directional position: puts for an upward EMA slope, calls for a downward slope, or both sides for a flat slope.
5. Otherwise, sell a reduced-size two-sided position.

For each sold call or put, place its strike above or below the futures close respectively by the regime-specific fast-ATR multiple, rounded to the nearest strike interval. For a hedged structure, buy a call above each sold call and a put below each sold put, each at the configured wing offset. The reduced-size position uses its own wider ATR multiple; the flat directional position uses the directional multiple on both sides.

For open positions, close at the regime-specific profit target, expressed as the fraction of entry premium remaining. Close if a single leg's premium reaches the configured multiple of its entry premium. Once the configured profit threshold is reached, trail the remaining position's stop to breakeven. These exit rules are dashboard guidance; the source does not execute orders or track positions.
