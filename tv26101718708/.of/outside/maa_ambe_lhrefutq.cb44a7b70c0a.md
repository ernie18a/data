During permitted trading sessions, and only while flat and below the daily trade limit, buy a call when the underlying closes above its long-term moving average, momentum is strongly bullish, trend strength is sufficient, and volume exceeds its recent average. Buy a put when the underlying closes below its long-term moving average, momentum is strongly bearish, trend strength is sufficient, and volume exceeds its recent average. Exit either position at a fixed favorable price move or a fixed adverse price move from entry.
