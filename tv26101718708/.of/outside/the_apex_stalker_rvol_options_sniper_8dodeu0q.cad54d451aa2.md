For each watched instrument, compare current volume with its moving average and measure the absolute change between consecutive macro-timeframe closes as a percentage of macro ATR. Signal a bullish continuation when relative volume meets its threshold, the scan-timeframe close is above its prior close, the macro close is above its macro EMA, and ATR usage is below its premium-warning threshold. Signal an exhaustion fade when relative volume meets its threshold and ATR usage reaches its exhaustion threshold; this fade may be expressed as a short after intraday structural breakdown or by selling a credit spread. The fade condition has no trend or price-direction filter.
