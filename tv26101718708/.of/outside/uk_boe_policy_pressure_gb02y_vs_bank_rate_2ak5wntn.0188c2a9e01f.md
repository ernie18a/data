Compare the UK two-year government yield with the Bank of England policy rate. Signal upward policy pressure when their spread exceeds a chosen positive minimum, is unusually high versus its rolling history, is above its short-term average, and all conditions persist for the required consecutive trading days. Signal downward policy pressure when the spread is below the corresponding negative minimum, is unusually low versus its rolling history, is below its short-term average, and all conditions persist for the required consecutive trading days. Otherwise, issue no pressure signal. Historical comparisons report how often comparable spread extremes preceded a same-direction policy-rate move; they are context, not trade signals.
