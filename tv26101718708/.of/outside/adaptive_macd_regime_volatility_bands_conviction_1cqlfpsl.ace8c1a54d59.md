On each bar, compute a normalized MACD histogram from the selected price source, optionally volume-weighted. Use fixed lengths or adapt them to a dominant-cycle estimate or price efficiency. Classify the market as a bullish or bearish trend when ADX and efficiency exceed their thresholds, using price relative to a moving average for direction; otherwise classify it as volatile or ranging from a smoothed absolute-return measure.

Form a weighted directional average of the normalized histogram, trend direction, and available enabled inputs: fixed-length MACD histogram signs on multiple timeframes; the sign of cumulative signed-volume change; price and open-interest changes; the standardized difference between traded and reference prices; and cross-asset momentum adjusted for its configured correlation. Give rising price with rising open interest a strong positive score, rising price with falling open interest a weaker positive score, and the opposite price movements corresponding negative scores. Also include a recent outer volatility-band rejection in its reversal direction, unless it opposes the corresponding trend. Exclude unavailable components and their weights. The average's sign determines direction and its absolute magnitude determines conviction.

Block a directional verdict if conviction is below its threshold, the histogram is too close to zero, or any enabled veto applies: an opposing trend, insufficient matching timeframe signs, opposing signed-volume change, opposing open-interest buildup, a volatility-index spike, or an opposing higher-timeframe MACD sign. A volatility-index spike also reduces conviction. Otherwise issue a long or short verdict in the average's direction.

On a permitted confirmed bar, enter at close if flat or holding the opposite direction, reversing an opposite position. Set a stop and two targets at configured ATR-based distances. On every bar, including the entry bar, move the stop to entry when the first target is touched, then exit if the second target is touched; otherwise exit if the stop is touched. Check the target before the stop when both occur in the same bar. A flat or vetoed verdict alone does not close a position.

When option hints are enabled, a long verdict suggests a call and a short verdict suggests a put. Round the underlying price to the configured strike interval and, for the session horizon, offset the call upward or the put downward by one interval.
