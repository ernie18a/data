Build a sentiment score as the equal-weight average of seven components, each standardized against its own rolling history, centered at neutral, and clipped to a bounded scale: broad-market momentum versus its long-term average; the balance of stocks making annual highs versus lows; advancing versus declining volume breadth; the inverse of the put/call ratio; the inverse of volatility relative to its medium-term average; stock returns relative to bond returns; and the inverse of the high-yield-minus-Treasury yield spread. Enter long when the composite is below its configured fear threshold and crosses above its short-term moving average. Exit the long after its configured holding period. There are no short entries.
