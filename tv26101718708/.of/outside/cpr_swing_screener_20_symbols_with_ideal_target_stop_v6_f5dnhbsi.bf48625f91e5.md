For each underlying, evaluate bars on the chart timeframe using its own prices and volume. Aggregate the preceding day's high, low, and close. Calculate the pivot as their average, the midpoint as the average of the high and low, and the reflected midpoint as twice the pivot minus the midpoint. The upper and lower central pivot boundaries are the greater and lesser of those two midpoints. First resistance is twice the pivot minus the previous low; first support is twice the pivot minus the previous high. Classify the central pivot range as narrow or wide when its width divided by the current close is respectively at or below a configurable narrow threshold or at or above a configurable wide threshold.

If the optional trend filter is enabled, bullish conditions require the close above its exponential moving average and bearish conditions require it below. If the optional volume filter is enabled, narrow-range breakouts additionally require volume above a configurable multiple of its moving average.

Buy a call when either: the range is narrow, the close is above both the upper boundary and first resistance, the candle closes above its open, and the bullish trend and volume conditions pass; or the range is wide, the low touches or crosses below the lower boundary, the close finishes above that boundary and above the open, and the bullish trend condition passes. Buy a put when either: the range is narrow, the close is below both the lower boundary and first support, the candle closes below its open, and the bearish trend and volume conditions pass; or the range is wide, the high touches or crosses above the upper boundary, the close finishes below that boundary and below the open, and the bearish trend condition passes. Volume confirmation does not apply to wide-range setups. If both option-buy conditions occur, the call takes priority.

For current-chart signals, suppress signals until more than a configurable number of bars have elapsed since the last emitted non-neutral status. A narrow-range call watch is a close above the pivot and at or below first resistance with the bullish trend condition; a put watch is a close below the pivot and at or above first support with the bearish trend condition. Buy-call, buy-put, call-watch, then put-watch is the priority order; any emitted watch also resets the cooldown. Screener signals have no cooldown. The calculated stop and target are informational levels and do not trigger option exits.
