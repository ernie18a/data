From price bars, calculate a smoothed stochastic oscillator, ATR-based price bands, a regression channel and slope, the share of recent closes above versus below the band basis (bull/bear heat), and a volatility-normalized breakout trend state. Use configurable thresholds throughout. The breakout state turns bullish or bearish on a channel break and remains active until price crosses back through its dynamic trend guide; also track whether a break is recent. Compute whether ATR is expanding, price is near the bands or basis, and price is outside the regression channel.

Recommend a bullish call calendar when the regression slope is rising, price is within the regression channel and near its lower area, basis, or a low stochastic reading, stochastic turns upward, bullish heat is at least balanced with bearish heat, and price is not in a fast bullish trend. Prefer a bullish breakout context when that filter is enabled. Mark this setup high quality only if the call strike, offset above spot by an ATR-based amount, is within the short-leg expected move; price is not near the upper band or above the regression channel; stochastic is not overbought; and its recent readings show a pullback. Otherwise mark it weak. Downgrade a high-quality call-calendar marker if, within the configured confirmation window, price fails to rise by the configured ATR amount while meeting the basis confirmation rule.

Recommend a bearish put calendar symmetrically: falling regression slope, price within the channel and near its upper area, basis, or a high stochastic reading, stochastic turns downward, bearish heat is at least balanced, and no fast bearish trend; prefer bearish breakout context when enabled. Its strike is offset below spot. High quality additionally requires the strike to be within expected move, price not near the lower band or below the channel, stochastic not oversold, and a recent stochastic rebound. Downgrade it if the configured downside move and basis confirmation fail within the confirmation window.

Recommend an ATM calendar when the regression slope is flat, price is near the basis, bull and bear heat are balanced, and ATR is not expanding. Recommend a bull call vertical when the trend slope and breakout context are bullish, bull heat dominates, price is above the basis, stochastic is below its configured ceiling, no high-chase warning applies, and price confirms strength through a breakout, upper-band reclaim, basis reclaim, or trend-guide retest. Recommend a bear put vertical symmetrically with bearish slope and context, dominant bear heat, price below basis, stochastic above its configured floor, no bottom-fishing warning, and a breakdown, lower-band rejection, basis rejection, or trend-guide retest. Enforce the configured minimum spacing between vertical signals; suppress a vertical marker when its corresponding calendar setup is present. Set vertical strikes around spot using ATR-based offsets and width.

Flag a fast bullish or bearish trend when price is stretched beyond the corresponding regression or volatility band, the regression slope and heat strongly favor that direction, and either ATR is expanding or stochastic momentum is extreme; warn that a plain calendar may be overrun. Flag a bullish fade trap when price is near or above the upper band/channel while slope and heat remain strongly bullish and stochastic is high; warn against an early contrarian put calendar. Flag a bearish fade trap symmetrically against an early contrarian call calendar. Warn against chasing a call calendar when price is extended above the upper band/channel, bullish heat dominates, and an overbought stochastic turns down or price rejects the upper band, unless a healthy active bullish breakout trend is present; suppress the warning during a strong rising close. Warn against bottom-fishing a call calendar when price is extended below the lower band/channel, bearish heat dominates, and an oversold stochastic turns up or price rejects the lower band; suppress it during a strong falling close.

When multiple scenes apply, display the first applicable scene in this order: high-chase warning, bottom-fishing warning, bull vertical, bear vertical, call calendar, put calendar, ATM calendar, fast bullish trend, fast bearish trend, bullish fade trap, bearish fade trap; honor the selected call/put analysis mode. Calendar and vertical strikes are references derived from spot and ATR; calendar expected move is estimated from spot, manually supplied implied volatility, and short-leg time to expiry.
