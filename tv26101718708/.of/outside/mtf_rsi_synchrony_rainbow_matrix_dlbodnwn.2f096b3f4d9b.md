When an external symbol is selected, calculate its close-based RSI independently on five configured timeframes, using a configurable period for each. Also calculate a weighted average of the available RSI readings, renormalizing the weights when readings are missing. For each individual RSI and the weighted average, indicate a long when the reading is at or below the oversold threshold and a short when it is at or above the overbought threshold; otherwise indicate neither. The indicator does not specify how to combine conflicting indications or when to exit.
