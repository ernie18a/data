For each monitored healthcare stock, define each candle’s body top as the higher of its open and close. A pivot high is confirmed when its body top is at least as high as the body tops of the three candles before it and the three candles after it; use the latest confirmed pivot high as the breakout level. Signal a new higher high when the stock’s close crosses from at or below that level to above it, and remains above it. This is a bullish signal only; no short or exit rule is defined.
