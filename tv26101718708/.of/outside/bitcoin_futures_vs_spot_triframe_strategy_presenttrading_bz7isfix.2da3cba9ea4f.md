On each of three configured timeframes, calculate the spread between weighted average BTC futures and spot prices from Binance and OKX, then standardize it using its rolling mean and standard deviation. Enter long when all three spread z-scores exceed the configured long threshold, if long trading is enabled; enter short when all three are below the configured short threshold, if short trading is enabled. Close an opposite position when taking the new direction. While a position is open, close it after the configured holding period if enabled, and apply configured take-profit and/or stop-loss exits when selected.
