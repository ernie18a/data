Go long when price sweeps below a recent swing low and closes back above it, that sweep is still recent, and either volume is unusually high or the candle has a small body relative to its range; require external market dominance to be below its moving average and aggregated open interest to be at or below its moving average. Go short on the mirrored swing-high sweep and close back below, with the same volume or candle-shape confirmation, external dominance above its moving average, and aggregated open interest above its moving average. Process each qualifying sweep only once during its validity window. Enter when flat; add only in the same direction when price has moved against the existing position. Do not open or add an opposite position. If the enabled regime filter identifies the entry as countertrend, reduce its size.

For an open long, exit when profit exceeds a small threshold and price closes below a short-term moving average; for a short, exit when that profit condition holds and price closes above it. Also exit at a catastrophic adverse-price boundary, a maximum favorable-price target, or when the close crosses a tighter adverse-loss boundary. After price reaches successive favorable excursion levels, ratchet the stop to lock in progressively more profit; if prior losses are being recovered, also move the stop to the recovery price once reached.
