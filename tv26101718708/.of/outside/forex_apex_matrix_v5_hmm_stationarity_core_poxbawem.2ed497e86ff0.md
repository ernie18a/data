On each bar, calculate stablecoin market dominance and its moving average, and sum the available open interest from the configured external exchanges, treating missing values as zero. Compare the sum with its moving average. Identify bullish liquidity sweeps when the traded instrument’s low breaks the prior lookback low and closes back above it; identify bearish sweeps symmetrically. A sweep remains eligible for a configured number of bars. Confirm it when volume exceeds its moving average by a configured number of standard deviations, or when the candle body is less than a configured fraction of its range. A long signal also requires dominance below its average and aggregate open interest at or below its average; a short signal requires dominance above its average and open interest above its average. Suppress same-direction signals until more than the expiry interval has elapsed since the last processed signal, even if that signal did not result in an order.

Enter on an eligible signal when flat. Add to an existing position only on a same-direction signal when price is adverse to the position’s average entry; ignore opposite-direction signals while holding a position. Respect the configured pyramiding limit. Size each entry from the current margin allocation and leverage divided by price. After each entry, double the margin actually used, subject to a cap. If the optional regime filter identifies an opposing trend, reduce the margin used for that entry by a configured factor before updating the next allocation.

For the optional regime filter, derive a trend proxy from the lookback sum of true range relative to the lookback high-low range, and combine it with directional movement strength. In a sufficiently strong trend, set a persistent bullish regime when RSI, directional indicators, the moving average of signed lower-timeframe volume, and price relative to its trend average all agree bullishly; set a bearish regime under the symmetric conditions. Otherwise retain the state, except that in non-binary mode weak trend conditions reset it to neutral and a price crossing against the regime’s trend average also resets it. Display neutral on any state-change bar and use the persistent state on subsequent bars for the sizing filter. Signed lower-timeframe volume is positive for an up candle, negative for a down candle, and zero for an unchanged candle.

Manage exits using the position’s average price. Close a profitable long when its return reaches the configured minimum and its close falls below a fast moving average; apply the symmetric rule to shorts. Close at the configured adverse close-based loss threshold. Also maintain a wick-triggered catastrophic stop and maximum-profit limit. Once price reaches either configured favorable profit tier, set the stop to that tier’s price; the higher tier takes precedence, while the lower tier is set only if no stop is locked. Track accumulated realized losses, including commissions, after losing position closures. If price reaches the average entry adjusted by accumulated loss divided by position size, lock the stop at least as favorably as that recovery price. Clear the locked stop when flat. On a profitable or breakeven position closure, reset accumulated loss and margin allocation to their base values; on a losing closure, add the loss and retain the current allocation.
