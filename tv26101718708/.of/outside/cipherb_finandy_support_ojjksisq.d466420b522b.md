In diamond mode, on a confirmed bar while flat, use an external Bitcoin perpetual market as an optional global trend filter. Compare its close with a simple moving average on the selected timeframe, using the requested bar's prior value while the chart bar is unconfirmed. Permit a long if either the current or previous sampled close is above its corresponding average; permit a short if either is below. Disabling the filter permits both directions.

For the traded instrument, calculate a wave oscillator by subtracting an exponential average of a selected price source from that source, dividing by an exponential average of the absolute deviation times a normalization factor, and exponentially smoothing the result. Smooth that oscillator with a simple moving average. A long signal requires the oscillator to cross above its smoothed line, the smoothed line to be at or below a bullish threshold, and Heikin-Ashi candles on both selected higher timeframes to close above their opens. A short signal requires the opposite cross, the smoothed line at or above a bearish threshold, and both higher-timeframe candles to close at or below their opens.

Also require the instrument's close to be above its selected-timeframe exponential moving average for a long, or below it for a short, unless the local filter is disabled. If the corresponding direction is enabled and the global filter permits it, submit a limit entry at the signal close, optionally shifted downward for a long or upward for a short. Set the stop and target as direction-specific percentages of that limit price; a long uses the separate divergence target when a bullish divergence or gold signal also occurs.
