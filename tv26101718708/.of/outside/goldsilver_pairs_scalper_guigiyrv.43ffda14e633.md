On each confirmed chart-timeframe bar, use OANDA gold and silver prices to calculate rolling correlation of their one-bar log returns and silver's beta to gold as correlation times the ratio of their return standard deviations. Maintain correlation validity with separate entry and lower exit thresholds; require beta to remain within its allowed range. Calculate silver's multi-bar log return minus beta times gold's, standardize that residual over a rolling window, and combine it with the negative rolling standardized log gold/silver price ratio using a weighted average. Use this unsmoothed score for decisions.

When flat, out of cooldown, and not liquidated, enter long gold and short silver if the model is valid, the score is at or above the positive entry threshold, and it has declined for the required consecutive bars while remaining extreme. Enter short gold and long silver under the symmetric negative-extreme and rising-score conditions. Lock the entry prices, beta, and score. Size silver units per gold unit as the entry gold/silver price ratio divided by entry beta.

Close both legs when the score reaches the mean-reversion exit threshold, moves adversely from its entry score by the stop distance, correlation or beta becomes invalid, or the holding limit is reached. For long gold, mean reversion is a score at or below the positive exit threshold and adverse movement is an increase; reverse these inequalities for short gold. Also liquidate if the leveraged adverse intrabar pair return reaches a total loss: use each long leg's low and each short leg's high, weight gold's return by the locked beta, divide the signed pair return by beta plus one, and apply leverage. After any exit, impose a bar-count cooldown; after liquidation, halt further trading until reset.
