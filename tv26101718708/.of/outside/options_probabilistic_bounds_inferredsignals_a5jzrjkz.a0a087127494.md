On the daily chart, calculate dividend-adjusted close-to-close log returns over a configurable recent window. If earnings-gap neutralization is enabled, set returns on earnings days and the following days to zero. Replace invalid returns with zero. Calculate the mean return divided by its standard deviation, multiplied by the square root of the window length, and apply a scaled hyperbolic tangent to obtain a drift score. If the score exceeds a positive threshold, favor selling puts; if it falls below the corresponding negative threshold, favor selling calls; otherwise, indicate no preference.
