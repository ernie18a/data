Trade the underlying only in synthetic-option mode, with a nonzero strike, after the configured start and before expiration, on bars whose minute is divisible by the hedge interval. Compute the target as configured position size times option delta, and subtract the signed current underlying position. If the difference is nonzero and its absolute value does not exceed the configured size, place an order for that absolute difference: for the long side, buy when the difference is positive and sell when negative; reverse those directions for the short side. Close the hedge after expiration. Other modes do not use external data in their trading decisions.

For delta, use the selected underlying price and an externally sourced risk-free rate. Convert stock yields from percent; for a premium-index rate, average the script's funding-rate transformation over its timeframe-dependent window. Select volatility as custom, externally sourced implied volatility divided by 100, or realized volatility from a log-return distribution over the rounded remaining days to expiry, with at least one day. For crypto implied volatility, multiply the external index by the ratio of the underlying's normalized price standard deviation to Bitcoin's over the same timeframe-dependent window. Express time to expiry in years. Calculate d1 = (ln(price/strike) + (rate + volatility²/2) × time) / (volatility × sqrt(time)). The vanilla call delta is exp(-rate × time) × normal-CDF(d1), clamped to [0,1]; the put delta is that unclamped expression minus 1, clamped to [-1,0].

The display selection also changes the traded delta: when the selected metric is option price or implied volatility, use vanilla delta for every option style. Otherwise, binary call delta is vanilla gamma divided by 100 and binary put delta is its negative; vanilla gamma is strike × exp(-rate × time) × normal-density(d2) / (price² × volatility × sqrt(time)), where d2 = d1 - volatility × sqrt(time). For geometric Asian options, use the absolute bar-to-bar change in the script's geometric Asian option price divided by the bar-to-bar change in underlying price, negated for puts. Calculate that price using the script's geometric Asian formulas: call = abs(price × exp(-(rate × volatility²/6) × time/2) × normal-CDF(d1) - strike × exp(-rate × time) × normal-CDF(d2)); put = abs(call - (price × exp((b-rate) × time) - strike × exp(-rate × time))), where b = (rate - (return standard deviation/sqrt(3))²/2)/2.
