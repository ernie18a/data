For the traded market, obtain weekly CFTC Legacy commercial long and short positions, using either futures-only or combined futures-and-options data. Compute net positioning as long minus short. Optionally delay each report until its Friday release time in New York; before release, use the previous report. For each enabled lookback, calculate the current net position’s normalized location within the minimum and maximum net positions over that many weekly reports. An index is unavailable if its history is incomplete or its range is zero. When extreme marking is enabled, signal long if at least one enabled index is available and every available enabled index is at or above the upper threshold; signal short if at least one is available and every available enabled index is at or below the lower threshold. Ignore unavailable and disabled indices. Otherwise, produce no signal. There is no exit rule.
