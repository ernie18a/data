Go long when a fast moving average crosses above a slower one while price is above a trend moving average, momentum is oversold, volatility is high relative to price, and—if enabled—price is near VWAP, the external volatility index is below its average, and the higher-timeframe moving averages confirm an upward cross. Go short on the inverse conditions: downward cross, price below the trend average, overbought momentum, high relative volatility, and enabled filters confirm via VWAP proximity, the external volatility index above its average, and a downward higher-timeframe cross. Reject signals when the cross conflicts with price relative to the trend average. The indicator has no exit rule.
