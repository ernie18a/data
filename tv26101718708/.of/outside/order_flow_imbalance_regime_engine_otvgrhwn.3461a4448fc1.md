Generate long and short signals on the first bar of their respective cascade conditions. Optionally require the bar to be closed. Reconstruct signed order-flow imbalance from the traded instrument using one selected method: classify lower-timeframe bar volume by whether each bar closes above or below its open, splitting unchanged bars equally; split chart-bar volume using the normal CDF of price change standardized by its rolling standard deviation; or split volume according to the close's position within the bar's range. Replace missing or zero volume with a unit count, and fall back to candle geometry when intrabar data is unavailable. Divide signed imbalance by total classified volume. Standardize raw signed imbalance using either a rolling mean and standard deviation or a rolling median and scaled median absolute deviation, then cap the result at symmetric bounds. Maintain separate buy and sell cascade intensities: decay each previous intensity exponentially and add the corresponding magnitude of normalized imbalance whenever it exceeds the positive or negative event threshold. Rank the sum of the intensities over a rolling window. A long cascade requires buy intensity to exceed sell intensity, the rank to exceed its threshold, and standardized imbalance to exceed a positive threshold; a short cascade requires the opposite intensity ordering and standardized imbalance below the corresponding negative threshold. If cross-asset confirmation is enabled, additionally require the previous bar's standardized reference-instrument imbalance to have the same sign as the traded instrument's standardized imbalance and exceed a magnitude threshold. Compute reference imbalance from its volume multiplied by the difference between its close's distances from the low and high, divided by its range; use unit volume if missing and zero imbalance for a zero-range bar. Standardize it using a rolling mean and standard deviation. Emit each signal only when its full cascade condition is true and was false on the preceding bar.
