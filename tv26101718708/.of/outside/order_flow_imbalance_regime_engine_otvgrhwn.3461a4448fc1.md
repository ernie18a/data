Estimate signed buying-versus-selling pressure from volume classified by intrabar price direction, standardized bar returns, or candle close location; standardize the resulting imbalance over a rolling history. Accumulate strong positive and negative imbalance events separately with exponential decay, and rank their combined intensity against recent history. A buy signal occurs when positive accumulated pressure dominates, its ranked intensity is high, and standardized imbalance is positive beyond its confirmation threshold. A sell signal uses the mirrored conditions. If cross-asset confirmation is enabled, require the prior bar’s standardized imbalance in the correlated reference instrument to have the same sign as the traded instrument’s imbalance and exceed a minimum magnitude. Emit each signal only on the first bar these conditions become true; optionally require bar close confirmation.
