Enter long when the traded instrument makes a lower low than its prior rolling low and at least one external-market confirmation occurs: volatility index high fails to exceed its prior rolling high, put/call measure high fails to exceed its prior rolling high, or the related futures market low stays above its prior rolling low. Enter short when the instrument makes a higher high than its prior rolling high and at least one confirmation occurs: volatility index low stays above its prior rolling low, put/call measure low stays above its prior rolling low, or related futures high stays below its prior rolling high. Require every enabled confirmation: for longs, oversold RSI with price below the lower Bollinger Band, price overlapping an active bullish swing order block, cumulative-volume-delta bullish divergence, and/or a bullish swing-failure reclaim of the prior higher-timeframe low; for shorts, overbought RSI with price above the upper band, overlap with an active bearish swing order block, cumulative-volume-delta bearish divergence, and/or a bearish swing-failure rejection of the prior higher-timeframe high. Enter only in enabled directions. Optionally close an open position on the opposite qualifying signal. While a position is open, optionally place a take-profit and stop-loss relative to entry, using either percentage distances or tick distances.
