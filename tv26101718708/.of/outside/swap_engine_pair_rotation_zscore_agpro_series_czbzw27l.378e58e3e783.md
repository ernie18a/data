Gate rotation signals using pair integrity: calculate rolling correlation of the assets' log returns, the absolute change in the ratio's rolling mean over the drift lookback divided by its rolling standard deviation, and the ratio of short-term to baseline standard deviations of the log ratio. Normalize correlation from its possible range to zero–one; normalize drift as one minus drift divided by its limit; normalize volatility as one minus the excess over one divided by the configured expansion limit minus one. Clamp each component to zero–one and use zero for missing components. Combine correlation, drift and volatility with respective weights 45:30:25. Allow rotation only if the resulting score meets its minimum, the symbols differ, and their recognized quote currencies do not conflict.

Independently, emit a deep-stretch rotation signal in the same direction when the absolute z-score crosses up through the deep threshold and integrity passes; this does not require the consecutive-bar confirmation.
