On each bar, identify bullish and bearish three-candle price gaps on the traded instrument. A long setup occurs when price closes above the upper edge of a recent bearish gap; a short setup occurs when price closes below the lower edge of a recent bullish gap. The inversion must occur within the configured timeframe-dependent age limit, with candle range above a configured multiple of ATR and body size above a configured fraction of range.

Score each direction using: price inside the latest persistent same-direction higher-timeframe gap, a recent same-direction swing liquidity sweep, an eligible New York session, confirmation from the external ES futures contract, and trendline liquidity. Give each of the first two conditions twice the weight of each remaining condition. A sweep means price penetrates the latest confirmed swing low and closes above it for longs, or penetrates the latest confirmed swing high and closes below it for shorts. Trendline liquidity is present when enough recent adjacent highs rise or adjacent lows fall. The extended session qualifies only when ATR exceeds a configured multiple of its moving average. External confirmation is satisfied when the optional ES filter is disabled or ES is within a configured percentage of either its previous daily high or low. Map the score to configured grades and emit the directional signal only if its grade meets the configured minimum; external confirmation contributes to the score rather than acting as a mandatory filter.

Maintain separate one-signal-per-leg flags. Reset the long flag when a new running high is made or price is outside both stored higher-timeframe gaps; reset the short flag when a new running low is made or price is outside both gaps. Set the appropriate flag only when a signal passes the grade filter. Update the stored bullish and bearish higher-timeframe gaps independently, prioritizing the longest configured timeframe when several are present; retain each until replaced.

The indicator displays a stop at the latest corresponding swept swing level, or an ATR-based fallback, and a target at the nearest eligible session extreme, previous daily extreme, or multi-timeframe gap boundary at least one risk distance from the entry. These are displayed levels; the source does not define an exit trigger.
