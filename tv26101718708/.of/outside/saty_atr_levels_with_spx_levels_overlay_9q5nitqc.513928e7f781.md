Choose a trading horizon and use its corresponding higher-timeframe close and ATR, either from the current period or the preceding period. Optionally use an external cash index as the source: translate its close into the traded instrument's price space by adding either the difference between their selected period closes, their current price difference, or their price difference captured at the start of the regular cash session; use the close-based difference as a fallback when the selected live or opening difference is unavailable. Use the index's ATR without adjusting it for the basis. Otherwise, use the traded instrument's own close and ATR. Buy calls, or take a long position, when the traded instrument's price is strictly above the selected close plus a configurable fraction of ATR. Buy puts, or take a short position, when its price is strictly below the selected close minus that fraction of ATR. No exit rule is specified.
