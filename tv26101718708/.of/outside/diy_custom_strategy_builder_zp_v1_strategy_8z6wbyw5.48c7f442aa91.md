On each bar, choose one leading indicator from the available trend, momentum, breakout, oscillator, or price-versus-reference indicators. Its bullish state supplies a long trigger; its bearish state supplies a short trigger. Optional confirmation filters must all agree with that direction. Filters compare price or indicator direction to moving averages, trend bands, cloud or channel boundaries, momentum or signal lines, directional movement, volume, or volatility conditions; use each selected indicator’s corresponding bullish and bearish state. A range filter requires price to be outside the detected range for either direction, and a choppiness filter only permits trades while the market is trending. Require the combined trigger and filters to hold within the configured expiry window after the leading state begins. By default, accept only signals alternating with the last accepted direction; when alternation is disabled, accept either direction whenever its conditions hold. Enter long on a valid long signal when flat or short, closing any short first; enter short on a valid short signal when flat or long, closing any long first. Place the stop beyond the latest valid swing low for longs or swing high for shorts, using an ATR-based distance when no suitable swing exists (or a fixed percentage fallback if that option is disabled). Set the profit target at the configured risk-to-reward multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. Submit entry and stop/limit exit only when the stop distance is positive; the position exits when either stop or target is reached.
