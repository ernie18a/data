Generate a bullish options setup after the instrument closes below its daily 200-period EMA, then retests the EMA area within a limited period and later confirms a bullish reclaim. Confirmation requires a close above the EMA plus the selected bullish trigger: a bullish candle, bullish engulfing candle, close above the prior candle’s high, or a higher high and higher low. Require enabled filters to pass: the daily EMA is flat or rising, the daily close is above the EMA (optionally using the prior confirmed daily bar), volume exceeds its recent average by a chosen margin, the broad-market benchmark is above its own long-term EMA, the instrument closes above its short-term EMA, and recent crosses of the long-term EMA remain below a chosen chop limit. When all conditions pass, signal a bullish options trade; assign a quality grade from the number of supporting conditions. The indicator provides no short-entry or exit decision.
