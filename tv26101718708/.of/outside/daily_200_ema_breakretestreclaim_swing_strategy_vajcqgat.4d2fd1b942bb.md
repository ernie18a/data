Track the traded instrument’s higher-timeframe EMA on each chart bar. When the chart close crosses below it, start a time-limited setup; a new cross below restarts the setup. On any subsequent bar, mark a retest once the bar’s range overlaps a configurable percentage zone around the EMA. The retest remains marked until the setup expires or resets.

While the setup is active and a retest has occurred, confirm a bullish reclaim when the close is above the higher-timeframe EMA and the selected confirmation condition holds: a bullish candle; a bullish engulfing candle; a bullish candle closing above the previous bar’s high; or a higher high and higher low than the previous bar. The retest and confirmation may occur on the same bar.

Require every enabled filter: the higher-timeframe EMA is flat or rising over a configurable number of chart bars; the selected current or previous confirmed higher-timeframe close is above its corresponding EMA; volume on a configurable timeframe exceeds its moving average by a configurable multiplier; the selected market instrument’s daily close is above its daily EMA; the chart close is above its chart-timeframe EMA; and the count of chart-close crosses of the higher-timeframe EMA over a configurable lookback does not exceed a limit.

When the setup, confirmation, and enabled filters all pass, score one point each for the close above the higher-timeframe EMA, retest seen, flat or rising EMA, qualifying volume, market close above its EMA, chart close above its EMA, and acceptable cross count. Score these conditions even when their filters are disabled. Classify the score using configurable upper and lower cutoffs: the upper and middle grades signal calls; the lowest grade is watch only. Reset the setup after any qualified signal. The script specifies no option sale or exit decision.
