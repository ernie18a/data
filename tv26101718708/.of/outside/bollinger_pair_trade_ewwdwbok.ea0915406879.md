Calculate configurable moving-average and standard-deviation bands on the traded series. When trading is allowed by the selected date and session windows, enter long when price crosses the selected lower band and short when it crosses the selected upper band. Optional filters gate entries by expected profit, the series’ daily moving average and deviation bands, and prior-day volatility-index level. Above the volatility threshold, either stop new trading and close positions or, if reversal mode is enabled, reverse open positions at the center average; optionally take reversed band-entry signals while flat. Exit by the selected rule: cross the center average; cross an exit band only when the center average’s direction agrees with the daily average; or, with the daily-average exit choice, also require a cross of that daily average. The no-exit choice omits these signal exits. Optional per-trade loss limits and end-of-window closure also exit positions; a strategy drawdown limit can halt trading.
