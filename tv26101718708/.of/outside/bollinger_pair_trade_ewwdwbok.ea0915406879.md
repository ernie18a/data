Calculate bands around a moving average of the traded instrument’s close using configurable standard deviation multiples, and calculate a daily simple moving average and daily deviation bands. A cross in either direction of the selected lower band signals long; a cross of the upper band signals short. Entries can reverse an existing position. When flat, optional daily filters suppress longs above the daily average or above the selected lower daily deviation band, and suppress shorts below the daily average or below the selected upper daily deviation band. An optional expected-profit filter requires the distance from close to the intraday average, multiplied by the most recently held position size, to meet a threshold after the first closed trade; when flat and already outside an entry band, it can also generate the corresponding entry signal without a new cross.

Allow signal-driven orders only within the selected date range and enabled trading sessions; the first session is excluded regardless of its setting. If the external volatility index’s daily close exceeds its threshold and the volatility switch is enabled, close all positions unless reversal is enabled. With reversal enabled, first close an open position on a cross of either boundary around the intraday average; otherwise reverse a long when close is below that average or a short when close is above it. If flat and new reverse trades are enabled, exchange the long and short entry directions. Outside this volatility mode, execute the normal signals, except that the daily trend filter closes an opposing position instead of reversing it when the proposed direction conflicts with the daily average.

For ordinary exits, a configurable mode closes on a cross of either boundary around the intraday average; another does so only for a long when the intraday average is above the daily average or a short when it is below; another additionally requires a simultaneous cross of the daily average. The remaining mode has no separate average exit. Opposite entry signals can still reverse positions. Apply the optional per-trade loss stop and the strategy-wide maximum drawdown stop independently, and close positions outside the date range when interval closing is enabled.
