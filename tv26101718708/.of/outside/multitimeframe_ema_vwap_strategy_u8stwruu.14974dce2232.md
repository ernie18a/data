When enabled, evaluate a CFD and a futures instrument independently on the same alignment timeframe, using the chart timeframe if none is specified. For each, a long setup requires its fast EMA to cross above its slow EMA, its close to be above both its higher-timeframe EMA and daily VWAP, and its close to be outside the configured width around every psychological price level. A short setup requires the opposite EMA cross, a close below its higher-timeframe EMA and at or below daily VWAP, and a close outside those zones. Use the chart instrument's classification to select the psychological-level interval and zone width for both instruments. On a confirmed chart bar, signal long or short only if both instruments produce the corresponding setup on the same alignment bar; do not carry a signal forward to match a later bar. For each instrument, suggest entry at its close, a stop at an ATR multiple against the direction, and a target at an ATR multiple in the direction of the trade.
