Build a 0–100 sentiment score by percentile-ranking each component over its historical window, orienting higher values to mean greater greed, averaging available components within the external-data basket and chart-derived internals, blending those averages by their configured weights, and smoothing the result. Select the external basket by asset type: for stocks/indices, use inverted implied volatility, selling pressure, put/call ratio, and high-yield credit spread ranks, plus breadth ranks for stocks above long- and medium-term averages. For crypto, use ranks of perpetual-versus-spot premium, US-exchange-versus-global spot premium, and medium-term ETH/BTC momentum, plus inverted stablecoin-dominance and dollar-strength ranks. For all assets, include ranks of chart RSI and price extension above or below its long-term average, plus the inverted rank of realized volatility. Skip unavailable components when averaging. Signal logic is configurable: in zone-exit mode, go long when the score crosses upward out of the fear zone and go short when it crosses downward out of the greed zone; in zone-entry mode, go long when it crosses downward into the fear zone and go short when it crosses upward into the greed zone. The fear and greed boundaries, component lookbacks, basket weight, smoothing, and trigger mode are configurable.
