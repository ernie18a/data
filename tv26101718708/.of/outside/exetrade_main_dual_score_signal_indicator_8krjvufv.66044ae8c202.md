Calculate separate long and short scores from trend, momentum, support/resistance, risk/reward, and higher-timeframe context. For longs, favor bullish alignment and nearby support with room to higher resistance; for shorts, favor bearish alignment and nearby resistance with room to lower support. Add points for favorable higher-timeframe trend, RSI, cloud position, and volume; reduce or block a direction when opposing higher-timeframe support or resistance is too close. Add bonuses for rejection or reversal patterns at support/resistance and penalize nearby opposing levels. Signal long or short when its score reaches the applicable entry grade, exceeds the opposite score by a clear margin, and passes trend-strength, higher-timeframe proximity, and support/resistance-break filters. Also allow a range entry when risk/reward is favorable and a direction-appropriate setup is present, such as a recent divergence, support/resistance bounce, band extreme, channel touch, or suitable RSI condition. While in a position, exit on a hit to its trailing stop, when the opposite-direction score reaches the normal signal grade, or when price is beyond the opposing cloud while the local trend points against the position.
