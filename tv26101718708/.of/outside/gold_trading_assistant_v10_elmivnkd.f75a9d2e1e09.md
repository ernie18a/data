Generate long and short signals independently from the traded instrument’s candles, external macro series, and manually scheduled news events. Treat all periods, thresholds, weights, buffers, and preset choices as parameters.

A long candidate requires an enabled bullish engulfing candle or hammer; a short candidate requires an enabled bearish engulfing candle or shooting star. Engulfing requires opposite-colored consecutive candles, the current body enclosing the previous body, and a sufficient current-to-previous body ratio. A hammer or shooting star requires a nonzero body, a sufficiently long directional wick, and a sufficiently small opposite wick; its body color does not matter. Also require optional volume confirmation, a body and recent high-low range within their ATR-based limits, sufficient bars since either previous signal, and, when strict mode is enabled, a confirmed bar.

For each direction, count alignment across the previous completed higher-timeframe bar, the current bar, and the previous completed lower-timeframe bar. Higher and current timeframes align bullishly when close is above the fast EMA and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, and bearishly for the reverse. The lower timeframe aligns according to close versus its fast EMA. If multi-timeframe filtering is enabled and alignment is required, require the specified minimum count. When multi-timeframe filtering is disabled, higher and lower alignment are false, but current-timeframe alignment still contributes to scoring.

Compute macro changes from the previous completed macro bar versus its lookback bar: percentage returns for the dollar index and silver, and the change in the government yield in basis points. Optionally standardize each change against its rolling mean and standard deviation, calculated on the chart timeframe from the requested change series with missing observations replaced by zero; use zero when deviation is not positive. Convert each raw or standardized change into strong, mild, or neutral directional scores using symmetric thresholds. Dollar and yield declines favor long; silver increases favor long. Weight and sum available series, then divide by the maximum absolute weighted score available; use zero if none are available. When macro filtering and opposition blocking are enabled, block long for a negative normalized score and short for a positive score. Macro scores still contribute to confluence even when macro filtering is disabled.

When news filtering is enabled, block both directions within the impact-dependent interval before or after any enabled event. Award the news-clear confluence bonus only when filtering is enabled, no blackout is active, and the next event is absent or sufficiently far away.

For each direction, calculate confluence as the sum of a fixed pattern contribution; a tiered score for the alignment count; the favorable portion of the normalized macro score; a volume score that distinguishes strong volume from merely passing the volume filter; a bonus for nearby support below a long entry or resistance above a short entry; a tiered directional fast-EMA slope score normalized by ATR; and the news-clear bonus. Volume receives the passing score when volume filtering is disabled unless strong volume earns the higher score. If confluence filtering is enabled, require the directional total to reach its minimum.

Finally require a minimum first-target reward-to-risk ratio. Use the close as entry. For long, place the stop at the lower of the recent swing low minus an ATR buffer and entry minus an ATR multiple; mirror this for short. Start the first target at a configured risk multiple. If hybrid targeting is enabled, find the nearest enabled resistance between long entry and that target, or support between the short target and entry, among previous day and week extremes, the two latest confirmed swing extremes, and the next round-number level. If found, move the target to the entry side of that structure by the configured buffer. Calculate reward-to-risk from this target and stop and require the minimum even when hybrid targeting is disabled.

Emit each direction whose complete conditions pass, and reset the shared signal-spacing counter when either passes. The indicator provides stop and target levels but does not issue position-exit decisions.
