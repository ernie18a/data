Generate a long signal when a bullish engulfing candle or lower-wick rejection candle forms; generate a short signal when a bearish engulfing candle or upper-wick rejection candle forms. Require the candle to pass configured volume, candle-size, recent-range, signal-spacing, and bar-confirmation filters. Then apply the enabled filters: higher-, chart-, and lower-timeframe trend alignment; macro direction inferred from changes in the dollar index, silver, and US Treasury yield; avoidance of configured news blackout windows; a directional confluence score meeting its minimum; and a first-target reward-to-risk ratio meeting its minimum. A bullish macro reading supports longs and an adverse reading can block them when macro blocking is enabled; apply the inverse to shorts. Confluence combines pattern, timeframe alignment, macro strength, volume, nearby price structure, trend slope, and news clearance. On a qualifying signal, mark the entry at the close and calculate a stop from recent swing extremes and volatility, with targets based on reward-to-risk multiples adjusted to nearby prior-day, prior-week, swing, or round-number levels. The indicator has no exit-signal logic.
