Use a configurable reference instrument’s higher-timeframe prices to generate signals for the traded instrument. Enter long only while flat when its MACD line crosses above its signal line while below a fraction of its recent minimum, trend strength exceeds a threshold, reference close is below its moving average and the midpoint of its recent high-low range, and RSI is below its own moving average. Enter short only while flat on the mirror conditions: MACD crosses below its signal while above a fraction of its recent maximum, trend strength exceeds the threshold, close is above its moving average and range midpoint, and RSI is above its moving average. For an open position, place an initial stop at a configured loss distance from average entry and a take-profit limit at a configured gain distance. Once price reaches a configured profit threshold, replace the initial stop with a trailing stop that follows favorable price movement by a configured distance and only tightens; exit when the stop or take-profit is reached. Reset trailing state when flat.
