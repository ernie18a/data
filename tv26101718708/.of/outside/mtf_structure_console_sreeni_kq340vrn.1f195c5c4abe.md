Classify bars by whether they break the prior bar’s high, low, both, or neither. When an inside bar forms, set its high and low as breakout triggers. Go long when price breaks above the high, or short when it breaks below the low; require a completed-bar close beyond the trigger unless touch-based confirmation is selected. If one bar breaks both sides, use its close to choose direction; if it closes back within the range, keep waiting. Use the multi-timeframe regime as a directional filter: stand aside in conflicted or standby conditions, and favor a break whose projected regime is aligned. Break alerts may also require alignment with relative strength versus a benchmark. Reject setups whose structural stop distance exceeds the allowed volatility-based risk. Place the stop beyond the opposite side of the setup range with a volatility buffer, or at a fixed volatility distance from entry. Exit at the stop, on a confirmed close back inside the setup range before any target is reached, or after the final profit target; a setup may also expire after its tracking period.
