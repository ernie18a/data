Track an inside-bar setup on the traded instrument, optionally requiring that the preceding bar is an outside bar. Classify a bar against the preceding bar's high and low, with configurable treatment of equal highs and lows. Use either the latest inside bar or the first in a consecutive run as the setup anchor. Freeze an average true range from the last completed bar of a configurable timeframe no lower than the chart timeframe. Set triggers at the anchor high and low; place stops either beyond the opposite side by an ATR buffer or a fixed ATR distance from each trigger. Optionally reject the setup if its larger trigger-to-stop risk divided by ATR exceeds a threshold.

While watching, identify an upside or downside break by a completed close beyond the corresponding trigger, or optionally by a live wick beyond it. If both triggers are breached on the same bar, use the close beyond a trigger to determine direction; if the close is inside, keep watching. An accepted upside break signals long and a downside break signals short, subject to the optional alert gates below. The setup becomes active on the break even if a gate blocks the alert.

For the optional regime gate, examine configured timeframes at or above the chart timeframe, using the last closed higher-timeframe bar when configured. Assign each bar a direction from close versus open. The regime leans bullish or bearish if every readable timeframe has that color, or if all but one do and the readable members of the two highest configured timeframes have that color, provided at least three timeframes are readable. Otherwise it has no leaning. Require the break direction to match this leaning. This gate uses the leaning even when the displayed regime is in standby.

For the optional relative-strength gate, compare the traded instrument's return with a benchmark over a configurable chart-bar lookback, using the ratio of their growth factors minus one. Require positive relative strength for a long signal and negative relative strength for a short signal. Bypass this gate when benchmark comparison is disabled or the benchmark is the traded instrument; unavailable relative strength does not pass an enabled gate.

After a break, monitor the frozen stop and configurable risk-multiple targets beginning with the next bar. A stop touch ends the setup and takes precedence over targets. Otherwise, record the highest target reached; before any target has been reached, a completed close back across the entry trigger ends the setup as failed. End tracking after the final target or a configurable number of bars. A new qualifying inside-bar setup can replace the current setup.
