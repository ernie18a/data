Enter only when flat, the daily trade limit has not been reached, volatility index is below its pause threshold, and trend strength is above the chop threshold. For a long, require the minimum number of bullish confluences: price above higher-timeframe VWAP, unusually high volume, bullish MACD crossover, bullish liquidity sweep, and positive bar-volume delta. Also require the fast trend average above the smoothed slow average and the long score to meet its threshold. For a short, use the corresponding bearish confluences, require the fast average below the slow average and the short score to meet its threshold, and reject unusually high ATR volatility. Score each direction from trend strength and direction, MACD/trend-average momentum agreement, volatility regime, volume confirmation, and sweep or pin-bar patterns; require the direction-specific minimum score. When ADX exceeds the trend threshold, additionally require directional movement and price relative to the medium-term EMA to confirm the trade direction. Reset the daily trade count on a new day and after an extended flat period. While in a position, close at an ATR-based hard stop or after the time limit; place a volatility-scaled take-profit for most of the position and a farther maximum-profit exit for the remainder. Activate a trailing exit on the remaining portion once price reaches the minimum favorable move. Apply the mirrored rules to longs and shorts.
