Use the daily VIX close as an entry filter; permit entries only while it is below a threshold. While flat and below the trade-count limit, evaluate long and short setups. Reset the count on a new day or after a specified number of consecutive flat bars; increment it when an entry is submitted.

For each direction, count these conditions: close on the corresponding side of higher-timeframe VWAP; volume at least a multiple of its moving average; MACD crossing its signal line in that direction; a directional liquidity sweep; and directional volume delta. Delta is positive on an up candle and negative otherwise. A bullish sweep breaks the previous rolling low and closes sufficiently above the previous candle's midpoint, with the required displacement proportional to its range; reverse this for a bearish sweep.

Calculate a separate score for each direction. Award trend points for directional movement agreeing with the direction, with more points above the strong ADX threshold than above the chop threshold. Award momentum points for a matching MACD crossover and for the triple EMA being on the matching side of the smoothed moving average, with a larger award when both agree. Add regime points according to whether ADX is above the strong threshold, below the chop threshold, or between them. Add volume points for high volume or, fewer, for volume above its average. Add pattern points for a matching sweep and a directional pin bar, with a larger award when both occur. A bullish pin bar breaks the previous rolling low, closes above its open, and has a body exceeding a specified multiple of its lower wick; reverse this for a bearish pin bar.

Enter long when bullish confluence and score meet their thresholds, the triple EMA exceeds the smoothed average, and ADX exceeds the chop threshold. If ADX also exceeds the strong threshold, require positive directional movement to exceed negative directional movement and close to exceed an EMA. Enter short using the opposite conditions, treating equality of the triple EMA and smoothed average as bearish; also reject shorts when ATR exceeds a multiple of its average.

Record the signal-bar close as entry price. Set a fixed hard stop at entry using a capped ATR distance adjusted by a bounded function of current ATR relative to average ATR, divided by instrument point value. Close the position when the close crosses that stop. Otherwise, place a partial profit limit using a direction-specific target adjusted by the same type of volatility factor, and a maximum-profit limit for the remaining position. Once the close reaches a specified favorable profit, activate a trailing exit for a specified fraction of the position. Convert tick-based distances using tick size and point value, but for the short trailing activation use the raw tick-count input divided by point value. Close any position held beyond the specified bar limit.
