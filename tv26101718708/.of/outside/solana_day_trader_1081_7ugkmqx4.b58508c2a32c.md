Evaluate the traded instrument on each enabled entry timeframe. Detect a washout when a volatility-fix measure, based on the distance from the rolling highest close to the current low, falls back below both its upper Bollinger band and a fraction of its rolling maximum after exceeding either on the preceding bar. Also require a higher low, a close above the previous high and a short-lookback close, and a close below either of two longer-lookback closes. Keep this event armed for a configurable window.

Build a configurable weighted score from: fast EMA above slow EMA and close above a Chande-momentum-adjusted moving average, each observed since a permitted interval before the armed washout; rising cumulative candle-signed volume; a positive EMA of candle-signed volume observed in its permitted interval; a recent bullish three-bar price gap; trend quality above its threshold; and high rolling percentile of ATR, less a penalty for low ATR percentile. Trend quality is the average of price efficiency, normalized ATR relative to its longer average, normalized distance from a close SMA in ATR units, and normalized rate of change, with the last three components clipped to their configured ranges. Candle-signed volume is positive when close is at least open and negative otherwise.

Require the armed washout when its gate is enabled and require the score to reach the lower entry threshold. Assign the lower tier; assign the middle tier when the higher score threshold is met; promote to the highest tier when ADX is also below its threshold. A recent close at or below the lower Bollinger band demotes a tier above the lowest by one when the anti-signal is enabled. Optionally suppress the middle tier and exclude a configured trend-quality interval. If the optional external-market gate is enabled and Bitcoin's close on the chart timeframe is not above its EMA, demote the highest tier to the middle tier.

Generate a long-entry event whenever an enabled entry timeframe's nonzero effective tier differs from its preceding chart-bar tier, including upgrades and downgrades. Apply the configured entry delay. Entry timeframes can be restricted to the primary timeframe. Thus a change in Bitcoin's condition can itself cause an entry event by changing the effective tier. No short or options decision uses external data.
