Generate a long-entry signal when the instrument is oversold: both a standard and a faster RSI are below their respective oversold thresholds, stochastic is oversold, the close has fallen sufficiently below its all-time high, and the daily volatility index exceeds its panic threshold. Confirm the signal only when volume is above its recent median and current volume exceeds its recent average by the required ratio. If the oversold conditions hold without both volume confirmations, treat the setup as waiting. No short or exit decision is defined.
