On each bar, obtain the two comparison legs' closes at the traded instrument's timeframe; a leg may instead be a numeric constant. Optionally replace unavailable or unchanged-over-a-configured-window feeds with a constant. When both legs are positive, calculate the log of their ratio, standardize it using its rolling simple mean and standard deviation, and optionally smooth the result with a simple moving average.

Calculate trend strength on the traded instrument using directional movement and true range: recursively smooth each as previous smoothed value minus previous value divided by the trend lookback plus the current value; calculate directional indices and their normalized absolute difference, then take its simple moving average. Separately, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low of the traded instrument. Compare the exponentially smoothed values of close divided by pivot high and pivot low divided by close: the former being greater is bullish bias; the latter being greater is bearish bias.

Arm a long signal when the standardized ratio crosses above a configurable negative threshold. If shorts are enabled, arm a short signal when it crosses above a configurable extreme-low threshold. Each signal remains armed for a configurable number of subsequent bars. Enter on the crossing bar, or while armed on a bar with the corresponding bullish or bearish bias. If the bias entry filter is enabled, also require the corresponding bias now or within the preceding match window. If the trend entry filter is enabled, require trend strength at or above its entry threshold. Entries must be within the optional date range. Clear the corresponding armed signal after entry; opposite entries can reverse a position.

For a long, the normal exit cross is the standardized ratio crossing below a configurable positive threshold; for a short, it is crossing above the long-entry threshold. Without bias-confirmed exits, close on the applicable cross, an enabled trend-strength cross below its exit threshold, or an enabled opposite-bias exit. With bias-confirmed exits, latch the applicable cross while the position remains open and close only when the opposite bias is present; the trend and independent bias exits are ignored in this mode. Close any open position outside the enabled date range.

Optionally place take-profit and stop-loss orders for each open position. On each bar, set their distances from the average entry price to the configured percentages of the current close, converted to ticks; use favorable and adverse directions respectively for longs and shorts.
