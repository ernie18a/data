After sufficient history, calculate fast and slow exponential moving averages. Consider a long when the fast average is above the slow average, the close crosses from below to above the fast average, price pierced below that average within a recent-bar window, and enabled volatility/spread and opposite-signal cooldown filters pass. Consider a short under the mirrored conditions: fast average below slow, close crosses below the fast average after a recent pierce above it, and filters pass. Suppress signals when the close lies between the averages. On a signal, set entry at that bar’s close and place a stop using the selected recent swing extreme, volatility-based distance, or percentage distance; cap stop distance by the configured price-risk limit and, for swing stops, the optional volatility cap. Set the target at the configured multiple of entry-to-stop risk. Keep the setup pending until a later bar reaches entry; cancel it if price reaches the stop first. Once entered, exit at the target or stop, on timeout, or when an opposite signal cancels the setup. Track whether price reached the configured favorable risk multiple before a stop, but this only marks a breakeven consideration and does not itself move the stop.
