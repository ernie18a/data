Within the configured trading date range, enter long when the close is below the Bollinger middle band, the bullish score meets its threshold, and an optional external-market filter permits entry. Calculate the bullish score as the sum of these eight conditions: RSI rises from the previous bar; negative directional indicator falls; close is strictly between the lower Bollinger band and its middle band; close is below the lower Keltner channel; both Bollinger bands extend beyond the corresponding Keltner boundaries; the lowest low over a lookback is at or below the current lower Bollinger band; ADX rises; and RSI is below an oversold threshold. Set the score to zero when RSI exceeds an overbought threshold. When enabled, the external-market filter blocks entry if the external instrument's daily close-to-close percentage change is at or above its upper limit or at or below its lower limit. Place a long limit order at the signal bar's close only when the position is not long; cancel it after a bar without a fresh signal. Exit the long position on the specified weekday once at least the configured number of bars has passed since the position increased. The squeeze display and filtering settings do not affect orders.
