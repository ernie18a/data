Compute normalized momentum for BTC dominance and each coin’s price relative to BTC, plus the coin’s dollar momentum and relative-price RSI. When BTC dominance momentum exceeds its positive regime threshold and the coin’s relative momentum is below its negative significance threshold, signal short/underweight the coin. When BTC dominance momentum is below its negative regime threshold and the coin’s relative momentum exceeds its positive significance threshold, signal long/overweight the coin. Otherwise, issue no directional signal. Mark a divergence setup when dollar momentum is positive despite relative weakness, or relative strength is positive while dollar momentum is not; add stronger timing confirmation when relative RSI is above its upper timing level on the weak setup or below its lower timing level on the strong setup. The indicator defines no exits.
