During the allowed trading session, after the opening delay and before the entry cutoff, issue a put credit spread signal when price is above and within the configured distance of the latest confirmed pivot low or prior-day low, the daily fast moving average is above the daily mid moving average, price is above VWAP, VWAP is rising if slope confirmation is enabled, and RSI is at or below its configured upper limit. Issue a call credit spread signal when price is below and within the configured distance of the latest confirmed pivot high or prior-day high, the daily fast moving average is below the daily mid moving average, price is below VWAP, VWAP is falling if slope confirmation is enabled, and RSI is at or above its configured lower limit. Each filter and prior-day level use may be disabled. Signal only when the qualifying condition has just become true, and enforce a shared minimum bar cooldown between signals. For a put spread, place the suggested short strike below the underlying by the configured cushion and the long strike further below by the configured spread width; for a call spread, place the short strike above by the cushion and the long strike further above by the width. Round each strike to the configured strike increment. No exit logic is defined.
