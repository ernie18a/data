On each bar, maintain the most recently confirmed pivot low and pivot high, replacing each only when a new pivot differs from the stored level by at least the configured minimum separation. A bullish level is present when the close is strictly above the stored pivot low and within the configured zone thickness, or, if enabled, strictly above the prior day's low and within its configured zone thickness. A bearish level is defined symmetrically below the stored pivot high or enabled prior-day high.

Allow signals only during the configured session, after the configured opening delay, and strictly before the optional New York time cutoff. Disabling the session restriction also disables the opening delay and cutoff.

A bullish setup requires a bullish level, plus each enabled filter: close above VWAP, positive VWAP change over the configured lookback, fast daily close SMA above slow daily close SMA, and RSI at or below its bullish threshold. A bearish setup requires a bearish level, plus each enabled filter: close below VWAP, negative VWAP change, fast daily SMA below slow daily SMA, and RSI at or above its bearish threshold. The VWAP alignment and slope filters are independently optional.

Signal only when the corresponding complete setup changes from false to true and the number of bars since the last signal of either direction is strictly greater than the configured cooldown. Evaluate both directions against the same previous signal bar, then update that bar if either signals. A setup that first becomes true during cooldown does not signal later unless it becomes false and then true again.

On a bullish signal, sell a put at the close minus the configured cushion, rounded to the nearest strike increment, and buy a put at that rounded strike minus the configured spread width, rounded to the nearest increment. On a bearish signal, sell a call at the close plus the cushion, rounded to the nearest increment, and buy a call at that rounded strike plus the spread width, rounded to the nearest increment. No option expiry or exit rule is specified.
