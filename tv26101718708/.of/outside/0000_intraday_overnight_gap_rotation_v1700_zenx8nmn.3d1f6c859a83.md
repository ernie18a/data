For each trading day within the configured date range, rank the enabled instruments and select up to the configured number that pass the applicable minimum-move threshold. Allocate equally among selected instruments; if none qualify, stay in cash. Choose one mode: (1) Intraday momentum: rank today's open divided by the previous close minus one, highest first; require the gap to be at least the threshold, buy at the open and exit at the close. (2) Intraday fade: rank the same gap lowest first; require it to be at most the negative threshold, buy at the open and exit at the close. (3) Overnight: rank the previous session's close divided by its open minus one, highest first; require that return to be at least the threshold, buy at that session's close and exit at today's open. Ignore disabled instruments and those without valid ranking data. If the optional seasonality filter blocks the current evaluation date, stay in cash; configure blocked periods separately for each month and its day ranges 1–7, 8–15, 16–22 and 23 through month-end. For overnight trades, the evaluation date is the exit date.
