At each trading day, apply the selected calendar-season filter; during blocked periods, hold cash. Otherwise, rank the enabled assets by the selected signal and go long equally across up to the chosen number of highest-ranked assets that pass the move threshold; if none qualify, hold cash. For intraday momentum, rank by the overnight return from prior close to current open, highest first, and require a sufficiently positive gap; enter at the open and exit at that day’s close. For intraday fade, rank by the same gap, most negative first, and require a sufficiently negative gap; enter at the open and exit at that day’s close. For overnight rotation, rank by the prior session’s open-to-close return, highest first, and require it to exceed the positive threshold; enter at the current close and exit at the next open. The framework takes long positions only.
