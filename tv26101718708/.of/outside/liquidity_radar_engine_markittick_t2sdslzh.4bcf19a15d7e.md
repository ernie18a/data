On each bar, track the previous day's high and low and the latest swing high and low. A swing is a bar whose high or low equals the corresponding extreme over a configured window ending at that bar; recognize it after a configured delay. Store RSI and MACD at each recognized swing. A high above the previous day's high followed by a close below it activates a potential short; a low below the previous day's low followed by a close above it activates a potential long. Both directions share the most recent sweep's bar and extreme; a new sweep overwrites those values without clearing the other direction. Expire both directions after a configured period. Within the configured window after the latest sweep, require a close crossing below the latest swing low for a short or above the latest swing high for a long. If displacement is enabled, also require an unusually large candle body relative to its moving average or a same-bar directional gap against the bar two bars earlier.

Apply enabled filters: session membership; RSI above its threshold and rising for long, below and falling for short; divergence between the latest sweep extreme and latest corresponding swing, confirmed by current RSI, MACD, or both versus their values at that swing; sweep-bar volume relative to its moving average, when those values are available; close on the appropriate side of its EMA; directional sign of volume times candle body divided by candle range; directional strength; sufficient ATR relative to its moving average; and exclusion of the configured news window. For the directional-strength filter, reproduce the source's assignment of the DMI outputs: require positive DI above the strength threshold, and require negative DI above ADX for long or ADX above negative DI for short. For the external-reference filter, compare the reference instrument's previous completed close with its previous completed EMA on the chart timeframe. For direct correlation, permit long only above the EMA and short only below it; reverse those conditions for inverse correlation. Equality permits neither.

At a confirmed bar, issue the directional signal if all applicable conditions pass and the direction's signal cooldown has elapsed. A short signal also supplies a close-long alert, and a long signal supplies a close-short alert, even if the following entry checks fail. For an entry, use the close, or, if gap entry is enabled, require a same-bar directional gap and use its older edge as the entry price; no subsequent retracement is checked. Use the shared sweep extreme as the stop and require nonzero absolute entry-to-stop distance. Set targets at configured risk multiples; optionally replace the first target with the latest swing and the second with the previous day's extreme when each lies beyond entry in the profitable direction. Clear the corresponding sweep state after a successful entry.

For tracked positions, check the existing stop and third target before updating management on each bar. Close a long if its close is below its stop, its low touches the stop, or its high reaches the third target; reverse the inequalities for a short. A close beyond the stop takes precedence over target processing. Record target touches, with the second and third target touches enabled only when their display settings are enabled. Optionally move the stop to entry after the first target and trail it favorably using an ATR offset from the current high or low, the latest opposite swing, or an ATR offset from the rolling high or low. A position is tracked for these exits only when its direction's position display is enabled; creating a new position can discard existing tracked positions according to the configured display limit and removes any remaining tracked position in the same direction.
