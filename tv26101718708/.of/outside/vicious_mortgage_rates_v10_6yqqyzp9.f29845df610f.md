For VIX trading, multiply the closing values of the one-month VIX, three-month VIX, volatility-of-volatility index, and short-term volatility index at the selected chart resolution. Calculate a short and a long exponential moving average of that product. Go long VIX when the product is at or above its short average and the short average is at or above the long average. Go short VIX when the product is below its short average and the short average is below the long average. When the product is at or above its short average but the short average is below the long average, do not trade. When the product is below its short average but the short average is at or above the long average, consider selling; no definite action is specified.
