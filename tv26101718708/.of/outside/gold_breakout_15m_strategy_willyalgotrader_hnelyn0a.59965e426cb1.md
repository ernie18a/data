At the start of each broker-time day, build a price range from the highs and lows during the configured range-building session. Once that session ends, and before the configured daily close time, place a buy stop at the range high and a sell stop at the range low if no position is open and no trade has been taken that day. The first filled entry cancels the other; allow at most one trade per day. Attach a stop-loss to each entry: either a configured percentage from the entry price, recalculated from the actual fill, or the opposite range boundary. Do not set a take-profit. Exit an open position when its stop is hit or at the configured daily close time. Optionally, if estimated drawdown from the day's equity peak reaches its configured limit, cancel pending entries, close any open position, and stop trading until the next day. If position sizing is risk-based, size from equity and entry-to-stop distance, cap by maximum leverage, and round down to the permitted quantity increment; skip an entry if its resulting size is zero.
