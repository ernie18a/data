On each bar close, use the configured broker clock to reset the daily range and drawdown state at the start of each broker day. Build the range from the traded instrument’s highs and lows during the configured range window; skip the first loaded day. Require the range start, range end and closing hours to be in order, and the range high to exceed its low.

When flat, with no trade yet taken that broker day, no drawdown halt, and the next bar opening between the range end and closing hours, place a buy stop at the range high and a sell stop at the range low. The first fill cancels the other order. Count even an entry and exit on the same bar as the day’s trade. Cancel pending entries when the setup is no longer valid.

For each side, place the initial stop at either the opposite range boundary or a configured percentage from its entry level, rounded to the instrument’s price tick in percentage mode. Require a positive entry-to-stop distance. Calculate quantity from either a fixed amount or equity risk divided by stop distance and point value; cap it by the configured leverage limit, then round down to the quantity step. Convert account equity to the instrument currency for these calculations, using the applicable currency conversion rate. Skip a side if its resulting quantity is not positive. After a percentage-mode entry fills, reset its stop once from the actual fill price, rounded to the price tick. Maintain the stop until exit; there is no profit target.

When enabled, the daily equity guard compares the current day’s prior equity peak with estimated worst equity on each bar. For an existing long, subtract the close-to-low adverse move times position size and point value from closing equity; for an existing short, use the high-to-close move. Convert that loss to account currency. On the entry bar, use closing equity without an intrabar adjustment. If the resulting drawdown reaches the configured limit, cancel orders, close the position and halt trading until the next broker day. Update the daily peak with closing equity after calculating drawdown.

Otherwise, exit an open position on its stop or submit a market close when the next bar opens at or after the closing hour or before the range end hour. Orders are calculated at bar close and become eligible for fills on following bars.
