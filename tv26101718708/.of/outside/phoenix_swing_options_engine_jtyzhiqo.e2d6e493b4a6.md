Arm a bullish or bearish setup when Bollinger Band width is in a low percentile of its recent range, ATR is contracting while average volume is rising, and a directional momentum score meets its corresponding threshold. Score momentum from the alignment of fast, medium, and slow exponential averages, whether RSI is in a bullish or bearish regime, and whether rate of change and its slope confirm direction. Keep the setup armed for a limited number of bars; cancel it if momentum turns against the setup or the window expires. Buy calls when an armed bullish setup sees the close cross above the Donchian-channel midpoint plus an ATR-scaled buffer on above-average volume. Buy puts when an armed bearish setup sees the close cross below the midpoint minus the buffer on above-average volume. Exit either options position when price reaches an ATR-scaled profit target or stop measured from entry, momentum crosses the opposite directional threshold, or the holding-time limit is reached.
