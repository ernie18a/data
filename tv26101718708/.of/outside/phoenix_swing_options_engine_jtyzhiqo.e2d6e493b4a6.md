On each bar, calculate normalized Bollinger Band width and its position between its rolling minimum and maximum; define compression as one minus that position, using a small denominator floor. Calculate a directional score by adding: an EMA alignment component of +2 or -2 for a fully bullish or bearish fast/middle/slow stack, otherwise +1 or -1 according to the fast EMA's position relative to the middle EMA; an RSI component of +1 above an upper threshold or -1 below a lower threshold; and a rate-of-change component of +2 when positive and rising over a fixed lag, +1 when positive otherwise, -2 when negative and falling over that lag, or -1 when negative otherwise. Components are zero when none of their conditions apply. Energy is building when a short simple moving average of ATR is below its value at a fixed lag and a simple moving average of volume is above its value at that lag.

Arm a bullish or bearish setup when compression reaches its threshold, energy is building, and the score reaches the corresponding positive or negative threshold. Arm each direction only if it is not already armed, recording the current bar in one shared arm timestamp. Disarm the bullish setup if the score becomes negative, and the bearish setup if it becomes positive; disarm either when the shared timestamp is older than the allowed window. Process arming before disarming.

Calculate the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low, then add or subtract an ATR-scaled buffer to form upper and lower triggers. Buy calls when bullishly armed and the close crosses above the upper trigger while volume exceeds a multiple of its moving average. Buy puts when bearishly armed and the close crosses below the lower trigger with the same volume condition. A crossover requires the current close to be beyond the current trigger and the previous close to be on or on the other side of the previous trigger. Disarm the direction that fires.

Each entry replaces any tracked position and records the underlying close, ATR, and bar, even if already in a position. For calls, exit when the underlying high reaches the entry close plus an entry-ATR-scaled target, the low reaches the entry close minus an entry-ATR-scaled stop, the score reaches the negative entry threshold, or the holding period reaches its limit. For puts, reverse the price directions and use the positive score threshold. Evaluate exits after entries, including on the entry bar, and clear the tracked position on exit.
