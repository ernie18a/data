At each daily close, calculate a reference level by averaging the exponential averages of the high, low, and close across the current and several preceding bars, then round it. Optionally require the close to also be above its simple moving average for a long signal, or below it for a short signal. When the close is above the reference level and any enabled filter agrees, enter long at the next open; when below and the filter agrees, enter short at the next open. Reverse direction when the opposite signal occurs; otherwise maintain the current position.
