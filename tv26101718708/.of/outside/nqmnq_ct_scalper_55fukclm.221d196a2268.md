On each bar, evaluate counter-trend long and short entries symmetrically. Require sufficient liquidity and ADX, a directional efficiency-ratio trend opposite the proposed trade, sufficient overextension and confidence, and no applicable hidden continuation divergence or strong opposing higher-timeframe veto. Calculate overextension from distance to EMA, VWAP and recent high-volume zones, structure, money flow, volume, rejection wicks, Bollinger and VWAP extremes, NYSE TICK, footprint flow, and divergence between the traded instrument’s swing pivots and ES futures pivots. Calculate confidence from those extremes, mean-reversion estimates, footprint and RSI divergence, relative performance against SPY, volume climax combined with VIX movement, momentum reversal, TICK, structure breaks, ES pivot divergence, and reversal candles. Adjust thresholds and weights for session, trend strength, regime, persistence, and higher-timeframe alignment.

In priority order, enter on the first enabled qualifying path per direction: exhaustion with reversal confirmations; divergence, delta flip or opposing momentum deceleration; a rejected liquidity sweep with confirming footprint flow; aggressive opposing footprint flow; absorption or confirming flow; or a retest of a previously departed high-volume zone with reversal confirmations. Increase required confirmation when opposing squeeze momentum is strong and not decelerating, especially in thin sessions; apply additional session and trend-strength requirements where specified by the path. The exhaustion path also requires a nonpersistent regime and a non-doji candle. Enter only if the calculated mean-reversion target offers sufficient profit. Set the target from eligible EMA, VWAP, pivot, session and footprint value levels, scaled by confidence and market context; place the stop beyond the relevant swing extreme with a context-dependent volatility buffer, extending it beyond the sweep for sweep entries.

Track each entry ticket independently, including its fixed target and stop. Starting after its entry bar, exit when price touches either level; if both are touched on one bar, record the target outcome. New entries can occur while earlier tickets remain open. The pattern-based exit cannot fire because its required score exceeds the number of available patterns.
