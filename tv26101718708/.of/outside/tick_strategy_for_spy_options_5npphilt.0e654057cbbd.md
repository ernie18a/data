During the intraday entry window, open a long when the market breadth index’s Hull moving average crosses above its smoothed version, its fast exponential average is positive, the traded instrument’s RSI is above its midpoint but below its upper band, volatility index is above its entry threshold, and the instrument closes above its fast exponential moving average. A recent long signal may also be entered while flat if the price is above that average. Open a short on the mirrored conditions: downward crossover, negative breadth average, RSI below its midpoint but above its lower band, volatility above threshold, and close below the instrument’s moving average. The implementation’s additional delayed short-entry condition is ineffective, so only the direct short entry applies.

For open positions, close a long when breadth’s Hull average is below its smoothed version while RSI momentum is falling, or when regular bearish divergence appears between price and breadth. Close a short on the mirrored momentum reversal or regular bullish divergence. Also use a protective stop for longs below the prior bar’s low by a fraction of daily average true range, and for shorts above the prior bar’s high by that amount; after entry, retain the more protective stop level. Apply the minimum holding period to these stop and signal exits for the main entries; the secondary long entry’s signal exits do not have that delay. Close all positions near the end of the trading day.
