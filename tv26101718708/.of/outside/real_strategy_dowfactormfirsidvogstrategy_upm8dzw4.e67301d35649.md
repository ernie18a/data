Choose either price RSI or money flow based on volume and price direction as the momentum input. Over a rolling window, estimate the adjusted multiple correlation of the traded instrument’s price with its volume and a market proxy: use a broad equity index for non-USD markets, the inverse of that index for USD-quoted FX, or a crypto index for cryptocurrencies. Use this estimate to weight bullish momentum and its bearish complement in opposite directions, then normalize the bullish weight against their combined total. Enter long when the normalized bullish score is above its midpoint; enter short when it is below. At the midpoint, issue no new entry.
