During permitted trading hours and days, calculate a normalized score for the ratio of short-term to long-term ATR. When that ratio is at a local high and its score exceeds the entry threshold, trade only if either recent volatility spikes are sufficiently clustered or the previous close is far enough from its rolling mean and volatility confirms the break. Go long when the current candle closes above its open; go short when it closes below. Use a larger position for exceptionally strong volatility spikes and a smaller one for other entries. Close either position when the volatility score falls below its configured exit threshold.
