For each configured asset, calculate relative momentum as the percentage spread between its fast and slow exponential moving averages. With two assets, switch to the other when its score crosses above the current leader or is already higher; choose the second asset initially when available, otherwise the first. With three to five assets, select the highest-scoring asset that passes the absolute-momentum filter (disabled by default). Above the broad-market index’s long-term simple moving average, rotate only when the new leader beats the active asset by the configured relative buffer; below it, rotate directly to the best eligible asset. Apply the anti-rotation filter to proposed switches: during elevated systemic risk, block a switch toward a more volatile asset unless a recent risk event permits it; during low risk with the index above its long-term exponential average, block a switch toward a less volatile asset unless a recent calm-zone entry permits it. When the applicable filter blocks a switch, retain the active asset. Emit a long signal when an asset becomes active and a close signal for the previously active asset when it is replaced; the system does not open shorts. Emit a close advisory for the active long when either the smoothed systemic-risk score is in its extreme zone while the index is below its medium-term average and consumer-staples momentum exceeds technology momentum, or the average relative-strength index of the broad index, technology asset, and staples asset falls into an exceptional oversold zone. The risk score ranks the broad index’s annual return, persistence of positive sessions, distance above or below its long-term average normalized by volatility, and medium-term return relative to volatility against their histories, then smooths and classifies the combined result with state persistence.
