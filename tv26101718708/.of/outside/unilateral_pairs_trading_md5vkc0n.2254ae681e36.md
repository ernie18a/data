On each bar, divide the traded instrument’s close by SPY’s close and calculate the deviation of this ratio from its rolling average. Subtract the rolling average of that deviation, then divide by the rolling standard deviation of the deviation to obtain a normalized ratio signal. Calculate the traded instrument’s percentage return from the prior close. Enter long when the normalized signal is below a lower entry threshold and the return is at or below a required long-side return threshold. Enter short when the signal is above an upper entry threshold and the return is at or above a required short-side return threshold. Exit a long position when the signal rises above a long exit threshold, unless a short entry is triggered on that bar. Exit a short position when the signal falls below a short exit threshold, unless a long entry is triggered on that bar.
