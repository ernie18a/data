When the optional fundamentals filter is enabled, permit an otherwise valid long or short entry only if active addresses and large-transaction count each exceed their respective moving averages, and sentiment exceeds the bullish threshold for a long or is below its complementary threshold for a short. Each missing fundamental series passes its own filter. Calculate the two moving averages on the requested fundamental-timeframe values mapped to chart bars, replacing missing observations with zero. The filter is disabled by default. An otherwise valid entry requires the permitted direction, a recent qualifying volatility-squeeze release, a directional Bollinger Band breakout with a sufficiently large candle body, accelerating directional momentum, a volume spike, and the enabled trend, RSI, volatility, regime, cooldown and session filters to pass. Enter only when flat.
