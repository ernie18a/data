Use synchronized external market data without lookahead. Treat unavailable CME futures data as a zero basis.

For entries, calculate the difference between short and long weighted moving averages of the instrument's close, normalized by ATR when ATR is positive. Compare it with its rolling standard deviation. A long trigger occurs when this oscillator crosses above its negative deviation band; a short trigger occurs when it crosses below its positive deviation band.

Require all of the following for a long entry: higher-timeframe close above its faster EMA and that EMA above its slower EMA; RSI below the overbought threshold; MACD histogram no lower than on the previous bar; money flow above its negative tolerance; volume above its minimum fraction of average volume; the current-time Bitcoin halving cosine cycle above its lower limit; USDT dominance not above its EMA; Bitcoin dominance above its EMA for BTC instruments or below it for other instruments; and CME Bitcoin futures basis relative to the traded instrument's close not excessively positive.

Require all of the following for a short entry: the opposite higher-timeframe EMA structure; RSI above the oversold threshold; MACD histogram no higher than on the previous bar; money flow below its positive tolerance; sufficient volume; the halving cycle below its upper limit; USDT dominance not below its EMA; Bitcoin dominance not in the bullish state defined above; and no falling USDT dominance near its recent high. The halving cycle is a phase-shifted cosine of elapsed wall-clock time since a fixed halving reference, with negative elapsed time clamped to zero.

Exit a long when the oscillator crosses below zero or a short entry occurs. Exit a short when the oscillator crosses above zero or a long entry occurs. The additional RSI exit clauses require RSI to be simultaneously beyond a threshold and crossing back through that same threshold, so they cannot trigger under ordinary crossover semantics. Entry signals take priority in the displayed recommendation.

The forecast also produces trade suggestions. Estimate a linear-regression slope and a dominant cycle from the average spacing of the latest confirmed pivot highs, bounded to a permitted range. Set cycle phase from bars since the latest confirmed high and amplitude from the larger of the latest pivot-high minus pivot-low range and an ATR-based floor. Form a weighted projection from the linear trend, a short sinusoid and a longer sinusoid, plus a halving-cycle offset. Add directional adjustments based on S&P 500, dollar index and gold relative to their EMAs; recent USDT-dominance change; Bitcoin-dominance momentum and its instrument-specific bullish state; CME futures basis; and the EMA states of major Asian and European stock indexes. The basis adjustment distinguishes moderate contango, backwardation and excessive contango.

In the forecast action panel, suggest long when the nearer projection exceeds the current close by a minimum margin and the farther projection also exceeds the close; suggest short for the symmetric downside conditions; otherwise hold. Forecast horizons are capped by the configured forecast length. At sampled local forecast peaks, suggest closing longs and possibly opening shorts; at sampled local troughs, suggest closing shorts and possibly opening longs. These are forward-looking suggestions, separate from current entry signals.
