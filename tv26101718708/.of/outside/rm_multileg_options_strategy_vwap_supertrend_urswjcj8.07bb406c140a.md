Choose two or four user-specified option legs. For a custom combination, assign each leg a positive sign for long or negative for short. For a straddle or strangle, assign positive signs to the first two legs and zero to the others. For an iron fly or iron condor, assign negative signs to the first two legs and positive signs to the others. Calculate synthetic high, low, and close as the signed sums of the corresponding leg prices, including the last two legs only in four-leg mode; replace missing leg prices with zero.

Choose either the synthetic high, low, and close or the specified spot index's high, low, and close as the trend source. Calculate ATR from the chart instrument's OHLC, regardless of the chosen trend source. Form basic upper and lower bands by adding and subtracting a configurable ATR multiple from the midpoint of the chosen high and low. Set the upper band to its basic value when it is below the previous upper band or the previous chosen close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Set the lower band to its basic value when it is above the previous lower band or the previous chosen close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize each band with its basic value and initialize the trend as up.

On each bar, switch from down to up when the chosen close exceeds the current upper band; switch from up to down when it falls below the current lower band; otherwise retain the trend. When the trend display is enabled, signal buy on a switch from down to up and sell on a switch from up to down.
