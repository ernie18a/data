On a 3-minute chart, when volume exceeds the prior bar’s volume and the time is within the specified New York morning session, go long when the short-period simple moving average crosses above the longer-period simple moving average; go short when it crosses below. Exit a long at either a fixed favorable price move or an equally defined adverse stop, and exit a short at the corresponding favorable move or adverse stop. Use the traded instrument’s price for entries and exits; the strategy is intended for 0DTE options on SPY.
