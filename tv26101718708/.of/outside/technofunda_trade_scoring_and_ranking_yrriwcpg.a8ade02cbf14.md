Long-entry-only signal from a composite score of 0 to 9 points, one point per satisfied condition, plus a bullish-bar filter. There are no short or exit rules.

Trend conditions on the traded instrument's own price (4 points):
1. Close is above its short, medium and long simple moving averages.
2. The moving averages are stacked in order: short above medium above long.
3. The long moving average is rising compared with its value some bars earlier.
4. Close is within a set fraction of the trailing roughly one-year high.

Fundamental conditions (3 points): user-supplied static numbers for earnings growth, revenue growth and return on equity are each compared with a fixed minimum. They are manual constants, not time-varying data.

Institutional volume condition (1 point), true only if both parts hold:
- Pocket pivot: the bar closes at or above the prior close, and its volume exceeds the largest volume of any down-close bar in a recent lookback window.
- Volume is at least a set multiple of its recent simple-average volume.

Relative strength condition (1 point), using data from outside the instrument: compare the instrument's percentage price change over a fixed lookback of about one month with the same change in a benchmark index's close, taken on the same timeframe. Score the point if the instrument's change exceeds the benchmark's.

Entry rule: go long on a bar when the total score reaches the top-tier threshold (a high score, out of the 9 possible) and that bar's close is above its open. Lower score tiers are only informational labels (buy, neutral, weak) and do not trigger trades.
