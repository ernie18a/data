In pair-trading mode, divide the traded instrument’s close by a secondary instrument’s close on the same timeframe, using the latest available secondary close without lookahead. Calculate the ratio’s rolling mean and standard deviation, standardize the ratio, substitute zero when the deviation is not positive, and smooth the result with an exponential moving average. Only generate entries when the ratio and smoothed score are valid.

Track extreme-to-zero cycles independently of positions. When idle, start a duration counter at one when the score reaches either extreme threshold. Increment it on each subsequent bar until the score reaches or crosses zero from that side; include the closing bar, record the duration in a bounded history, and reset the counter. Use the mean of the retained durations as the historical closure time. A time stop is active when a cycle is open and its duration exceeds that mean.

When flat, arm a long after the score crosses upward through the lower threshold and then remains strictly above it for the configured number of subsequent bars. Arm a short symmetrically after a downward crossing of the upper threshold and subsequent closes strictly below it. Cancel a pending entry if its threshold fails, replace it if the opposite crossing occurs, and clear pending entries while a position is active. Enter when armed.

Count position duration from one on the entry bar. Exit a long when the score reaches or exceeds zero, and a short when it reaches or falls below zero. Exit either side when its duration exceeds twice the historical closure time or the independent cycle’s time stop is active. Time-based exits are unavailable until a historical closure time exists. Evaluate exits on the entry bar as well.
