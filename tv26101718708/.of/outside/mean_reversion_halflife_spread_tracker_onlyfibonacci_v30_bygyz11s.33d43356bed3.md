Form a ratio from the traded asset’s close and either its moving average or a secondary asset’s close. Standardize that ratio against its rolling mean and standard deviation, then smooth the result. When flat, signal long after the smoothed score crosses upward through the lower extreme threshold and remains above it for the required confirmation period; signal short after it crosses downward through the upper extreme threshold and remains below it for the required confirmation period. For an active position, exit when the score reaches zero in the direction of reversion, when the active deviation lasts longer than the historical average time for extreme deviations to return to zero, or when the position lasts longer than twice that average.
