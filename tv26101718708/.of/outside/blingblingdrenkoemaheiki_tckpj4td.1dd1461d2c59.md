Generate a buy signal when the 4-hour ATR-based Renko bar is rising and the instrument’s close is above the 14-period EMA of the 4-hour Renko close. Generate a sell signal whenever the 4-hour Renko bar is not rising. Track which signal occurred most recently: enter long after a buy signal is more recent than the last sell signal, and enter short after a sell signal is more recent than the last buy signal. If neither signal has occurred, or their recency is tied, do not enter.
