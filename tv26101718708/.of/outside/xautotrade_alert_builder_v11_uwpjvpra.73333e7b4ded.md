For each enabled direction, compare two user-selected data series using a configurable rule: cross above, cross below, currently greater than, or currently less than. A satisfied buy rule enters long; a satisfied sell rule enters short. Exit behavior is selectable: delegate management to an external strategy, close a long when the sell entry rule fires and a short when the buy entry rule fires, or attach a stop-loss and profit target at configured distances from entry, with distances specified in ticks or percentages.
