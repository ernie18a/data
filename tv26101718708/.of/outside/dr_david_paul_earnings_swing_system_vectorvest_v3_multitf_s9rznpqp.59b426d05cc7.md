On each bar, calculate a bullish score: award one point each for the fast close EMA above the slow close EMA, the close above the long-term EMA, smoothed price-rate-of-change acceleration above its threshold, relative performance against an external benchmark at or above its threshold, volume at or above a multiple of its moving average, a bullish regime, RSI within its specified bullish range, smoothed stochastic K above D and below its upper limit, a positive and rising MACD histogram, close above session VWAP, and ADX at or above its threshold. Price-rate-of-change acceleration is the difference between close rate of change over a lookback and over twice that lookback, smoothed with an EMA. Relative performance is the instrument's close divided by its close at the lookback, divided by the equivalent benchmark ratio. A bullish regime requires close above a regime EMA; a bearish regime requires close below it. In strict mode, also require that EMA to be rising for a bullish regime or falling for a bearish regime over the specified window. Signal a buy when the score reaches the buy threshold, the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, the regime is bullish, their most recent upward crossover occurred within the buy lookback, and the buy cooldown has expired. Signal a sell or exit when the score is at or below the sell threshold, the fast EMA is below the slow EMA, the regime is bearish, their most recent downward crossover occurred within the sell lookback, and the sell cooldown has expired. Track buy and sell cooldowns independently; a signal is ready when cooldown is disabled or more than the cooldown number of bars have passed since the previous signal of that type. Separately, the dashboard labels scores at or above its upper threshold as buy, scores at or above its lower threshold as watch, and lower scores as avoid.
