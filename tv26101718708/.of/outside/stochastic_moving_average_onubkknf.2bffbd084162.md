Calculate fast and slow exponential moving averages of the traded instrument’s close, a stochastic oscillator from its high, low and close, and a simple moving average of the oscillator. Enter only when flat and the corresponding direction is enabled. For a long, require the fast average above the slow average, the close below the fast average and at or above the slow average, and the oscillator crossing above its average. For a short, require the fast average below the slow average, the close above the fast average and at or below the slow average, and the oscillator crossing below its average. Also require an oversold oscillator reading for a long or an overbought reading for a short within the configured lookback, including the current bar. When the option labeled 'all' is enabled, the implemented loop instead requires only the oldest reading in that lookback to meet the threshold. If the reference-market filter is enabled, a long additionally requires the reference close above its exponential moving average on either the current or previous chart bar; a short requires it below on either bar. Obtain reference data at the configured symbol and timeframe, using the previous reference bar when the chart bar is unconfirmed. Submit entries for the next available fill. At the signal close, fix a stop at an average-true-range multiple below the close for a long or above it for a short, and a target at the configured reward-to-risk multiple of that distance on the opposite side. Exit the position at the fixed stop or target. If risk-based sizing is enabled, divide the configured equity percentage or fixed currency risk by the stop distance, with equity sizing taking precedence; otherwise use the strategy’s default order size.
