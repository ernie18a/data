When configured to trade a mapped stock or CFD using the chart instrument as a signal, evaluate only real-time bars. Use configurable direction, entry level, optional stop and profit levels, required consecutive closes, profit mode, and optional chart-symbol lock. A level is enabled only when positive and the chart matches the lock. All comparisons are strict.

While flat in automatic position mode, arm a long entry when the chart low falls below the enabled entry level, or arm a short entry when its high rises above it. Starting on a later bar, enter long after the required consecutive confirmed closes above the entry level, or short after consecutive closes below it. The consecutive closes may include the arming bar or earlier bars.

While long, exit immediately if the low falls below an enabled stop; while short, exit if the high rises above it. Otherwise, in touch profit mode, exit long when the high rises above the enabled profit level, or short when the low falls below it. In close profit mode, that same intrabar profit-level breach arms the profit exit; subsequently exit long after the required consecutive confirmed closes below the profit level, or short after closes above it. The qualifying closes can include bars before the profit level was armed. An enabled stop remains active after profit arming and takes priority.

If no stop is enabled and the profit exit is not armed, exit long after the required consecutive confirmed closes below the entry level, or short after closes above it. This fallback applies even if the entry level is disabled or the chart does not match the lock. Reset profit state on entry or exit and allow a new entry cycle after exit. A manual position setting can restore a long or short position once per recalculation; it prevents new entry arming while selected.
