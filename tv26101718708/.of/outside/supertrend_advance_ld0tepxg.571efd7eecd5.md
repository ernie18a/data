Calculate Supertrend direction from the chart instrument’s candles, with the candle source selectable between Heikin-Ashi and chart candles. A change from bearish to bullish signals a long; a change from bullish to bearish signals a short. Enter only when the signal passes the enabled date and session-time filters, the corresponding trade side is enabled, and the current position is flat or opposite. Close an open position when an opposite entry signal occurs, at the configured session exit time, or when its profit target or stop loss is reached; the target may be capped by the distance to the Supertrend level. For options orders, a bullish signal selects calls and a bearish signal selects puts; the configured option action determines whether to buy or sell them.
