Calculate a trailing volatility-band trend on the chart timeframe. Use Heikin-Ashi candles for either the Heikin-Ashi or Candlestick setting, and chart candles for the same-as-chart setting. Calculate volatility as either Wilder ATR or a simple average of true range. Set preliminary bands at the chosen price source minus and plus a volatility multiple. Retain the higher lower band while the previous close is above the previous lower band, and retain the lower upper band while the previous close is below the previous upper band. Start in an uptrend; switch from down to up when the close exceeds the previous upper band, and from up to down when it falls below the previous lower band.

Within the inclusive configured date range and, if enabled, from the intraday entry time until before the intraday exit time, act only on trend changes. An upturn closes a short position and opens a long if long entries are enabled; a downturn closes a long position and opens a short if short entries are enabled. Opposite signals close positions even when the corresponding new entry side is disabled. At or after the intraday exit time, close any position.

Attach a profit target and stop loss to each position, measured from its entry price. Convert configured point distances or percentages of the current chart close to ticks, rounding to the nearest tick; recalculate percentage distances on each bar. Optionally cap the target distance at a multiple of the absolute distance between the signal-bar close and its active trend band, captured on the eligible signal bar and rounded to ticks.

For generated option alerts, a long entry buys a call in buy mode or sells a put in sell mode; a short entry buys a put in buy mode or sells a call in sell mode. Select the configured option symbol and strike using the chart close and configured strike proximity and adjustment. Closing a long or short emits the corresponding call-side or put-side exit alert. Custom alert text can replace these generated messages.

Use the configured base quantity unless loss-based sizing is enabled. After a loss, double the current quantity if it is below the configured maximum; after a win, restore the base quantity. Ignore the first completed winning or losing trade for this sizing state. If daily reset is enabled, restore the base quantity and clear the loss state on a day-of-month change. The doubling can exceed the maximum because the maximum is checked before doubling.
