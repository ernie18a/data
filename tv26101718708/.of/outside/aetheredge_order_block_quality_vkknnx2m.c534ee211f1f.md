On each confirmed bar, detect a bullish or bearish break when the close crosses the prior rolling swing high or low and the candle body is sufficiently large relative to ATR. For a bullish break, use the most recent opposing down candle within a bounded lookback as the zone; for a bearish break, use the most recent opposing up candle. Score each zone at formation with an online logistic model using ATR-scaled zone size and displacement, trend alignment, directional momentum, and relative volume. Update the model as zones resolve: label a zone broken if price closes beyond its far edge; label it held if, after a retest, price closes back beyond its near edge; at the resolution horizon, treat a retested zone as held and an untouched zone as not held. After a learning warm-up, go long on the first retest of a bullish zone whose formation score meets the quality threshold; go short on the first retest of a qualifying bearish zone. The indicator defines no exit decision.
