Compare the traded instrument’s closing prices with a user-selected external instrument over a rolling window. When their correlation is below a chosen threshold, enter long if the directional filter is bullish (either the traded instrument’s close is below its moving average or its RSI is below an oversold level); enter short if the filter is bearish (close above its moving average or RSI above an overbought level). The filter can use either moving-average or RSI mode, with its lookback optionally matched to the correlation window. An optional reverse setting swaps the long and short mappings. Entries are limited to the selected trading window. Optionally exit positions using a selected swing-based or volatility-based stop and corresponding risk-reward target, with an optional trailing stop; alternatively, close positions after a chosen number of bars.
