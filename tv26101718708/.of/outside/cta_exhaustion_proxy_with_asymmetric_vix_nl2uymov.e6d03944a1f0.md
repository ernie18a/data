On each chart bar, compare a configurable price source with fast, medium, and slow moving averages of the same configurable type. Assign +1 when the source is strictly above an average and -1 otherwise; sum the three values. Count consecutive bars at either extreme score, without resetting if the score switches directly between extremes. Measure velocity as the current score minus its score two bars earlier. A bullish fast-average crossing occurs when the close is above that average and the previous close was at or below its previous value; reverse the inequalities for a bearish crossing.

Use the daily VIX close, falling back to the daily front-month VIX futures close when unavailable; carry daily values across chart bars and use the daily value with lookahead enabled. If the configurable volatility filter is enabled, require VIX above the long threshold for all long signals and below the short threshold for all short signals.

Issue a long exhaustion entry when the previous score was maximally negative, the previous extreme-bar count met the slow threshold, and a bullish crossing occurs. Issue a long sharp entry when either of the two previous scores was maximally negative, the previous extreme-bar count met the sharp threshold but was below the slow threshold, velocity meets the positive threshold, and a bullish crossing occurs. If the configurable strong-candle filter is enabled, the sharp entry also requires a bullish candle whose body divided by its high-low range meets the body threshold, with a positive range. Issue a long velocity warning when the previous score is at or below the negative velocity-warning threshold, velocity meets the positive threshold, and a bullish crossing occurs.

Mirror these rules for shorts: use maximally positive previous scores, negative velocity, a bearish crossing, and, for filtered sharp entries, a bearish candle meeting the same body-to-range threshold. The velocity warning requires the previous score at or above the positive warning threshold. The stated trade guidance places a long stop below the fast average and a short stop above it; no precise stop execution rule is specified.
