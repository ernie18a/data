Estimate trend-following crowding by assigning a bullish or bearish vote according to whether price is above or below each of three moving averages of different speeds, then sum the votes. Count consecutive bars at either unanimous extreme and measure the score’s change over a short lookback. Generate a long reversal signal when recent extreme bearish positioning is followed by an upward cross back above the fastest average, with either sufficient persistence at the extreme or a rapid bullish score shift; require elevated daily volatility (VIX) for all long signals. Generate a short reversal signal symmetrically after recent extreme bullish positioning, on a downward cross below the fastest average, with sufficient persistence or a rapid bearish score shift; require subdued daily VIX. The rapid-reversal signal additionally requires a strong candle in the reversal direction when that filter is enabled. No exit rule is defined.
