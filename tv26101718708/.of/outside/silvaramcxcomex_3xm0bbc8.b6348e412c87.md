For silver and gold separately, convert the COMEX price from USD per ounce into INR per MCX pricing unit using USD/INR, then apply the configured import duty. Subtract the corresponding MCX price. Signal an upside entry when this difference crosses above its configured upside-entry level, a downside entry when it crosses below its configured downside-entry level, and an exit when it crosses the configured exit level in either direction. Separately, monitor the daily close of each gold/silver, gold/platinum, and platinum/silver price ratio. Signal buy when a ratio crosses below its configured buy level, sell when it crosses above its configured sell level, and exit when it crosses its configured free level in either direction. Crossings require the previous value to be on or beyond the opposite side of the level and the current value to be strictly across it. The signals do not specify which instrument or leg to trade.
