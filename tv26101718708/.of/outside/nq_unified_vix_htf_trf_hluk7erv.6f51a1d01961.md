On each chart bar, calculate a twin-range filter for the traded instrument and for VIX. For each of two configurable periods and multipliers, smooth the absolute close-to-close change with an EMA of that period, smooth the result again with an EMA of length twice the period minus one, and multiply by its multiplier. Average the two results to obtain the range. Recursively constrain a filtered price to remain unchanged until close moves more than the range beyond its prior value, then set it to close minus the range on an upward move or close plus the range on a downward move. Form upper and lower bands at the filtered price plus and minus the range. Retain the previous upper band unless the new upper band is lower or the previous close was above the previous upper band; retain the previous lower band unless the new lower band is higher or the previous close was below the previous lower band. The active filter follows the upper band until close reaches or exceeds it, then follows the lower band until close reaches or falls below it; use the upper band as the initial default. A cross requires the previous close to be on or beyond the opposite side of the previous active filter and the current close to be strictly across the current filter.

Use the current 15-minute VIX close above its filter as the VIX-up state; otherwise use the VIX-down state. Use the previous completed 15-minute traded-instrument close above its filter as the higher-timeframe bullish state. Match the source's higher-timeframe lookahead behavior.

Track one position. At a new day or from noon New York time onward, reset an open position to flat. While flat, on a confirmed chart bar from 9:30 inclusive to noon exclusive New York time, enter long at the close on an upward filter cross when VIX is down and the traded instrument's 15-minute state is bullish. Enter short at the close on a downward filter cross when VIX is up; no traded-instrument higher-timeframe agreement is required. Set the initial stop at a configurable ATR distance from entry and the target at a configurable multiple of that distance, on the appropriate sides.

For breakeven management, calculate upper and lower extensions of the previous day's range using a configurable extension ratio. After the first 15 minutes of the New York session, also calculate equivalent extensions of that opening range; use the higher upper extension and lower lower extension. Before checking exits on each bar, move a long stop to entry once close reaches or exceeds the upper extension, or a short stop to entry once close reaches or falls below the lower extension. Exit a long if the bar's low reaches its stop or its high reaches its target. Exit a short if its high reaches its stop, its low reaches its target, or VIX is down. Apply breakeven and exit checks even on the entry bar; do not re-enter after an exit until a later bar.
