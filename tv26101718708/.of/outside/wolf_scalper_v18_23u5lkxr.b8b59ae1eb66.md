Generate a long signal when a bullish setup occurs: a downside liquidity sweep that closes back above the prior swing low, an inverted bearish gap reclaimed by a bullish displacement candle, or a bullish order-block retest with a bullish displacement candle. Generate a short signal symmetrically from an upside sweep, a bearish inversion of a bullish gap, or a bearish order-block retest. A setup must meet its grade’s minimum count of directional confirmations: the setup itself, recent cross-market divergence (the traded instrument makes a new swing extreme while a correlated instrument fails to confirm), a recent matching liquidity sweep, matching market-structure shift or trend, overlap with a matching gap or order block, matching two-or-more-bar close progression, favorable premium/discount location, and position relative to the New York open. For the higher grade, also apply the configured session gate, volatility/chop filter, optional volume filter, and optional block on longs in premium or shorts in discount. The lower grade uses its lower confirmation threshold and chop filter, without the higher-grade session, volume, or premium/discount gates. Enforce separate directional cooldowns. On a higher-grade signal, define a protective stop at an ATR-based distance from entry and profit targets at multiples of that initial risk; these are levels for tracking, not separate signal-driven exit logic.
