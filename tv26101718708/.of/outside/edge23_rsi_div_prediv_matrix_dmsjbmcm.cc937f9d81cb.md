For each instrument and timeframe, calculate RSI and track the latest overbought price/RSI high and oversold price/RSI low, refreshing each reference when RSI makes a more extreme reading and discarding stale references after the configured lookback. A bearish divergence event occurs when RSI is overbought and price exceeds its tracked high while RSI is below the reference RSI; bullish divergence is the mirror condition in oversold territory. Confirm a direction when its divergence events meet the configured minimum count within the lookback.

Build separate bearish and bullish exhaustion scores from these clues: shrinking candle bodies while price advances in that direction; price makes a new directional extreme on lower volume; RSI remains elevated or depressed while its momentum weakens; the MACD histogram fades in the corresponding direction; price advances while ATR is flat or falling; price is far from its moving average and makes only weak progress; relative volume fades after a recent spike; or a prior strong impulse fails to extend to a new extreme. Each clue adds its assigned weight to the corresponding score. A pre-divergence state requires the score to meet its configured threshold and exceed the opposite score; distinguish stronger from watch-level thresholds.

Assign the highest-priority state in this order: long when RSI is oversold with confirmed bullish divergence; short when RSI is overbought with confirmed bearish divergence; bullish or bearish confirmed divergence; stronger bullish or bearish pre-divergence score; watch-level bullish or bearish exhaustion score; long-side RSI extreme alone when oversold; short-side RSI extreme alone when overbought; otherwise neutral. The indicator reports these directional states and has no exit rule.
