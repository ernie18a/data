When correlated-symbol divergence is enabled, compare each confirmed price pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type. At a low pivot, compare whether the traded instrument made a lower low with whether the correlated instrument's low at those same pivot bars made a lower low. Signal bullish divergence when these outcomes differ for a positively correlated pair or match for an inversely correlated pair. At a high pivot, apply the same rule to whether each instrument made a higher high, and signal bearish divergence. The correlated instrument is sampled at the traded instrument's pivot bars; its own pivots are not required. These signals are separate from the oscillator-based stack and do not specify entries or exits.
