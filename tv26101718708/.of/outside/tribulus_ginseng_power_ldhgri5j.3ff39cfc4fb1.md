Generate directional reversal events separately for the charted instrument, total crypto market capitalization, and Bitcoin dominance. For each series, combine a volatility-pulse-qualified reversal from a smoothed price envelope and ATR trailing-stop trend change with a structural reversal; require the two event types to occur in either order within a finite window. The chart’s bearish composite triggers a long candidate, and its bullish composite triggers a short candidate. Apply enabled chart filters to block the corresponding candidate when recent RSI, MACD histogram, or candle-body expansion meets its configured blocking condition. Treat each market-cap and dominance composite as active for a finite lookback after it occurs. Enter long when a chart long candidate occurs, or when total market cap and dominance both have active long-alignment events; also enter early long on the first bar of that macro alignment if no chart long candidate occurs. Enter short symmetrically using chart short candidates or active short-alignment events in both external series, with an early short on the first bar of that alignment if no chart short candidate occurs. For the chart and total market cap, long-alignment events are their bearish composites and short-alignment events are their bullish composites. For dominance, a bearish composite is a long-alignment event and a bullish composite is a short-alignment event. If already in the opposite position, close it before opening the new position; suppress repeated entries in the current direction. There is no independent flat or exit signal.
