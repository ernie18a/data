For each major currency, aggregate its signed flow across all available currency pairs: positive pair flow adds strength to the base currency and subtracts it from the quote currency; negative flow does the reverse. Pair flow combines ATR-normalized momentum, relative tick activity signed by momentum, fast-versus-slow trend, volatility expansion signed by momentum, and directional breadth, then smooths the result. Rank currencies by aggregate flow. For the pair joining the strongest and weakest currencies, go long if the strongest is its base currency and short if it is its quote currency. Pair-level signals also go long on materially positive pair flow, short on materially negative flow, and wait when flow is near neutral.
