On each bar, calculate fast and slow EMAs of the traded instrument’s close and its ATR, using preset-dependent periods and thresholds. When flat and the preset cooldown since the last entry has elapsed, enter long if the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, their current separation exceeds the ATR-scaled strength threshold, and the VIX close on the same timeframe is strictly below its threshold. Enter short if the fast EMA crosses below the slow EMA and their current separation exceeds the ATR-scaled strength threshold; VIX does not filter shorts. Use the configured position size. While in a position, update a stop and profit target on each bar from the current close using preset-dependent ATR multiples: below and above the close respectively for a long, and above and below it for a short. Exit when either order fills. SPX and sector data do not affect trading decisions.
