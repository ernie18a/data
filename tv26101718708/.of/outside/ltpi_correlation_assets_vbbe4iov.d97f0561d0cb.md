On the traded asset, assign an equal bullish or bearish vote based on whether rate of change exceeds its bullish threshold, RSI is above its midpoint, the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, and price is above its long-term SMA. Average the votes into a trend score. Compute the asset’s Pearson correlation with Bitcoin’s price across multiple lookback windows, combine valid correlations into a weighted average, and multiply that correlation by the trend score. Go long when the resulting score is above the neutral zone, short when below it, and exit or stay flat when it is inside the zone.
