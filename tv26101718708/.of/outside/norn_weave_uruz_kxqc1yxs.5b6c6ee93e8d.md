Enter long when the EMA is rising, the swing-breakout trend is bullish, ADX exceeds its threshold, and (if enabled) external BTC or ETH footprint delta smoothed over recent bars is positive; missing footprint data passes the filter. Enter short on the inverse conditions, with negative delta required when the filter applies. Detect swing highs and lows as local extrema over a symmetric window; update the trend bullish when the close breaks the latest swing high and bearish when it breaks the latest swing low. Set EMA length from the swing-detection setting and a scale ratio. The ADX threshold is either a manually set level or the recent historical median. For profit targets, use a longer-period ATR scaled by a factor that tightens when current volatility is above its recent baseline and widens when below; close successive portions at one, two, and three ATR-scaled distances from entry. Set the stop-loss as either a manual loss percentage or a smoothed ATR-derived loss percentage bounded by minimum and maximum loss limits. Once open profit reaches a configurable percentage, activate a stop at entry price and exit if price returns to that level. Exit the full position if the swing-breakout trend reverses against it, or if unrealized loss breaches the stop-loss. Reverse positions when opposite entry conditions are met.
