On each bar, use the selected BTC/USD or ETH/USD market's footprint buy volume minus sell volume, smoothed with a simple moving average, as an optional entry filter. A positive average permits longs, a negative average permits shorts, zero permits neither, and unavailable data permits both. Disabling the filter also permits both.

For the traded instrument, confirm swing highs and lows when the high or low a configurable number of bars ago equals the extreme over a centered window of that many bars on each side. Retain the latest confirmed levels. Set the trend upward when close exceeds the latest swing high and downward when close falls below the latest swing low; otherwise retain the previous trend.

Enter long when the close EMA is rising, the retained trend is upward, ADX exceeds its threshold, and the external filter permits longs. Enter short under the symmetric falling EMA, downward trend, ADX, and external-filter conditions. The EMA period is the swing period multiplied by a configurable scale. In automatic mode, use the historical ADX median as the threshold, falling back to a configured threshold until sufficient history exists; otherwise use the manual threshold. Enter only when flat; if an opposite position exists, close it first and wait until flat before entering.
