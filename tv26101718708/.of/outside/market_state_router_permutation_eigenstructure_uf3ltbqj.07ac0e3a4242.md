Use configurable lookbacks and thresholds throughout. Compute local structure as the historical percentile rank of normalized permutation entropy of delayed, four-close ordinal patterns, breaking ties by observation order. Compute directional efficiency as absolute net price change divided by total absolute bar-to-bar price change. Compute local impulse as the EMA of the traded instrument's log returns, multiplied by the square root of the smoothing length, divided by their rolling standard deviation, and clipped to [-1, 1].

For the traded instrument and four distinct external benchmarks, retain only synchronized, fresh log-return observations. From a rolling window of these observations, calculate the correlation matrix and its largest eigenvalue and corresponding eigenvector. Orient the eigenvector so the first benchmark's loading is nonnegative. Calculate systemic concentration as the average of the normalized largest-eigenvalue share and one minus normalized spectral entropy; rank it against a rolling baseline of synchronized concentration observations. Standardize each current synchronized return against its own rolling window and project the returns onto the oriented dominant eigenvector. Normalize and clip this factor return using the same impulse method as for local returns. Attribute the factor impulse to the traded instrument by multiplying it by the sign of its loading and by the absolute loading times the square root of the number of instruments, clipped to [0, 1]. Between valid synchronized updates, retain the last available factor impulse, eigenstructure and concentration percentile.

Classify each impulse direction as positive, negative or neutral using a symmetric dead zone. Once all required histories and measurements are available, apply these rules in priority order: (1) If concentration percentile is at or above its systemic threshold and either the nonneutral local and attributed factor directions conflict or absolute factor impulse reaches the event threshold, output event risk. (2) Otherwise, if structure percentile is at or below its ordered threshold, concentration is systemic, efficiency reaches its threshold, the nonneutral directions agree, and absolute factor impulse reaches the breakout threshold, output breakout long if the concentration-weighted average of local and attributed factor impulses is above the positive dead-zone boundary; otherwise output breakout short. (3) Otherwise, if structure is ordered, efficiency reaches its threshold, local direction is nonneutral, and either concentration is below its systemic threshold or the directions agree, output trend long or short according to local direction. (4) Otherwise, if structure percentile reaches its disordered threshold, concentration percentile is at or below its decoupled threshold, and efficiency is below its threshold, output mean reversion. (5) Otherwise output no trade. Before all required measurements are available, output loading. These classifications do not specify position exits.
