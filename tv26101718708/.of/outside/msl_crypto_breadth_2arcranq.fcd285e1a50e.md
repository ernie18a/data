On the selected timeframe, calculate participation as the percentage of available basket members whose closes are above their own moving averages. Exclude members without a valid close or average, and produce no reading unless the minimum live-feed requirement is met. A bullish divergence opens when, over the comparison window, the reference instrument falls by at least its configured move threshold while participation rises by at least its configured breadth threshold. After a bullish divergence opens while participation is below the configured washed-out threshold, a subsequent upward crossing of the participation midpoint marks the long entry window. Exit when participation crosses below the midpoint.
