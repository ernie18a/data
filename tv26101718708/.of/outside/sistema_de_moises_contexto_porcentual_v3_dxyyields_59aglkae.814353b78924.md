Build bullish and bearish context scores from multi-timeframe trend, momentum, structure breaks, impulses, imbalances, liquidity sweeps, and daily bias; subtract from each side when a recent opposing imbalance is active. For gold, add directional support from the dollar index and 10-year and 2-year Treasury yields: when at least two are rising and outnumber falling signals, favor bearish gold context; when at least two are falling and outnumber rising signals, favor bullish gold context. Compare the normalized scores. If the directional gap is low, wait for confirmation; otherwise, when bullish context leads, seek buys toward upper liquidity, and when bearish context leads, seek sells toward lower liquidity. If scores tie, wait for a break showing directional intent. This is contextual guidance, not a defined entry or exit system.
