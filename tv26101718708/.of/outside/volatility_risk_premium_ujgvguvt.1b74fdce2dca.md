On each chart bar, obtain the VIX close and calculate realized volatility from the chart's OHLC if its ticker is SPY, SPX, or ES1!; otherwise use SPY OHLC at the chart timeframe. Select one realized-volatility estimator over a configurable window: annualized standard deviation of close-to-close log returns; annualized square root of the mean squared log high/low ratio divided by four times the natural log of two; or annualized square root of the nonnegative mean of half the squared log high/low ratio minus (two times the natural log of two minus one) times the squared log close/open ratio. Express volatility in percent. Smooth VIX minus realized volatility with an exponential moving average. Compute its rolling mean and standard deviation, and standardize the current smoothed value using the standard deviation floored at a small positive constant. Signal long when the standardized value is at or below a configurable negative threshold and the smoothed value is rising from the previous bar. Signal short when it is at or above the corresponding positive threshold and the smoothed value is falling from the previous bar. Signals can repeat on consecutive bars; no exit rule is specified.
