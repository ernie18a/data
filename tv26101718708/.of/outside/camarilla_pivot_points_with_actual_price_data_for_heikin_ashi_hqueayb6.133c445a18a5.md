Use the prior session’s actual high, low, and close to calculate Camarilla levels. In ranging conditions, short when price reaches the upper third resistance level and go long when it reaches the lower third support level; place stops beyond the corresponding outer breakout level. If price convincingly breaks above the upper breakout level, go long toward the next projected resistance; if it breaks below the lower breakout level, go short toward the next projected support. Use actual market prices even on Heikin Ashi charts.
