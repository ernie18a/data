Use a long-only exposure rule. In graded mode, calculate a 0–1 ramp from each selected moving average to the price level one volatility-scaled ramp width above it; average the ramps. Optionally round this exposure to discrete levels, changing levels only after the raw exposure moves far enough past a level threshold. In binary mode, turn exposure on after consecutive closes above the trend average and off after consecutive closes below it; when enabled, require the average to be rising for the on state. Scale the resulting exposure and apply a minimum floor, capped at full exposure. The sell overlay can sell one equal fraction of the position on each upward cross of an upper band around a causal kernel smoother, and buy one fraction back on each downward cross of a lower band. In the gated-dial mode, preserve the dial’s target but block reductions below the smoother, allowing additions; other modes can use band crossings alone, allow either rule to reduce exposure, or disable the overlay. Reduce or exit the long position whenever the resulting target weight falls.
