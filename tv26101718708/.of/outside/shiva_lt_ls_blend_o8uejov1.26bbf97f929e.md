During the regular market session, signal a long when price sweeps below the latest confirmed swing low and closes back above it with a sufficiently large ATR-relative rejection, provided that level has not already been swept and the sweep cooldown has elapsed. Signal a short on the mirrored sweep above a confirmed swing high. Grade sweep entries more strongly when a composite momentum score agrees with direction and the swept price is near several key levels (VWAP, opening-range boundaries, prior-day levels, daily pivot levels, session retracements, or moving averages); the strongest grade requires stronger score agreement and more nearby levels, while a lesser grade requires moderate agreement and fewer levels. The score combines fast-versus-slow EMA direction on the chart, hourly, and daily timeframes, RSI relative to its midpoint, MACD histogram sign, and unusually high or low volume. Also signal a long or short on a VWAP touch and close back across it when the candle direction and score are not strongly opposed. Signal a pullback long when fast EMA is above slow EMA and price is above VWAP, or a pullback short under the reverse conditions, if price is near the slow EMA, VWAP, or a locked opening-range boundary and the candle closes in the signal direction. After the opening range is fixed, signal a long on the first close crossing above its high and a short on the first close crossing below its low; signal retests when price touches the broken boundary and closes back on the breakout side. The source defines no explicit position exit decision.
