On each confirmed bar, create demand and supply zones from enabled candle patterns: a small-body doji (bullish when close ≥ open, spanning low to open; bearish spanning close to high); a rejection candle with a dominant lower wick for demand or upper wick for supply, using the rejection wick region; a large-body, small-wick momentum candle, using low-to-open for bullish demand or close-to-high for bearish supply; and a sufficiently long, narrow cluster of small candles, creating both demand and supply over the cluster range. Apply the configured pattern thresholds. Merge same-type zones whose midpoints are within the configured distance, retain the stronger strength, cap the number of zones, expire zones after their maximum age, and neutralize demand after a close below its bottom or supply after a close above its top.

When flat, a long setup occurs if price tests an active demand zone (low reaches its top plus an ATR-based buffer and close remains above its bottom) and the bar closes up. A short setup occurs if price tests active supply (high reaches its bottom minus the buffer and close remains below its top) and the bar closes down. Also allow a long breakout setup when the up-close exceeds the prior bar’s high and closes have risen over the recent lookback; allow a short breakout when the down-close breaks the prior low and closes have fallen over the lookback. Trigger only on the first bar of a continuous setup. A long order is actually placed only if a demand-zone bottom was recorded on that bar; a short order only if a supply-zone top was recorded. For longs, stop below that demand bottom by an ATR-based offset; target the nearest qualifying supply bottom above entry, or an ATR-based profit target if none qualifies. For shorts, stop above the supply top by an ATR-based offset; target the nearest qualifying demand top below entry, or an ATR-based profit target if none qualifies. There are no separate reversal or other exit signals; positions exit at the stop or target.
