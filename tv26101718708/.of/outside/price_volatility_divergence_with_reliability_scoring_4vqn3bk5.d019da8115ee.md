Build a fear measure from an external implied-volatility index: select its level, its deviation from its exponential trend, its squared level, or its squared level minus annualized mean squared log returns of the traded instrument. Negate the measure, standardize it using a rolling mean and standard deviation or median and scaled median absolute deviation, optionally take the median of standardized values across short, medium, and long windows, and optionally smooth the result.

On confirmation of an oscillator low, compare it and the instrument price at that pivot with the previous oscillator low and its corresponding price. Signal long for a higher oscillator low with a lower price, or, if hidden divergences are enabled, a lower oscillator low with a higher price. On confirmation of an oscillator high, signal short for a lower oscillator high with a higher price, or, if hidden divergences are enabled, a higher oscillator high with a lower price. Require the latest two pivots to be within the allowed separation and their oscillator difference to reach the specified multiple of the oscillator's rolling standard deviation. If three pivots are required, the preceding pair must also follow the same oscillator and price directions. Evaluate signals on the confirmation bar, and update pivot history even when a signal is filtered out.

Optionally require the current fear measure to meet an elevated rolling percentile, an external regime reading to be valid and nonpositive, and the current intraday time slot to pass the configured statistical significance test. The reliability verdict and internal price-regime setting do not gate the directional signals. If both directions occur on the same bar, the exported direction is long. No exit or options decision is specified.
