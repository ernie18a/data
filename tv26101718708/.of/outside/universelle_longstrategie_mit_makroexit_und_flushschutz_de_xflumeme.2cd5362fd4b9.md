Evaluate on each bar close, using configurable parameters and optional switches for the start date, trend exit, macro filter, and flush protection. Trade long only, with no pyramiding; execute orders at the bar close.

For each of two external symbols on the traded instrument's timeframe, calculate its close, EMA, and highest high over a configured lookback excluding the current bar. External stress occurs when either symbol closes above its EMA, exceeds that EMA by more than its configured percentage, and closes above its preceding highest high. Confirm stress when this combined condition holds for the configured number of consecutive bars; the stressed symbol may change between bars. Macro conditions are calm when, for both symbols, the close is at or below its EMA or its percentage excess over the EMA is at or below its threshold. Confirm calm after the configured number of consecutive calm bars. When the macro filter is disabled, stress is false and calm is true.

A trend exit occurs when enabled and the instrument closes below its slow EMA reduced by the configured percentage buffer. A flush occurs when enabled and either the close falls by at least the configured percentage from the previous close, falls by at least another configured percentage from the close two bars ago, or the current high-low range exceeds ATR times a configured multiplier and the close is below the open. Volatility is calm when the high-low range is at most ATR times that multiplier; confirm it after the configured number of consecutive calm bars. Flush recovery is ready when protection is disabled, no flush has occurred within the configured cooldown number of bars inclusive, or the close is above a recovery EMA and volatility is confirmed calm. A new flush resets the cooldown.

Within the enabled trading window, close an existing long if a trend exit, confirmed macro stress, or current flush occurs. When flat, enter long if there is no trend exit, macro calm is confirmed, and flush recovery is ready. Evaluate entry and exit from the position at the start of the bar's decision, so an exit does not immediately re-enter on the same bar. Market presets may select the EMA lengths, flush thresholds, and ATR multiplier.
