On each new rates bar, calculate the change in the US 10-year yield minus the US 2-year yield over a configurable lookback, plus each yield’s change over the same period. Classify a steepening when the curve change exceeds a configurable positive threshold and a flattening when it falls below the corresponding negative threshold. Confirm regimes by the yield legs: bullish steepening requires the 2-year yield to fall beyond a leg threshold; bearish steepening requires the 10-year yield to rise beyond it; bullish flattening requires the 10-year yield to fall beyond it; bearish flattening requires the 2-year yield to rise beyond it. Treat bearish steepening as bearish only when that option is enabled. Set the candidate regime to bullish, bearish, or neutral from these classifications. Persist the last non-neutral regime. When a bullish candidate follows a bearish regime, or follows neutral if neutral entries are enabled, close any short and go long. When a bearish candidate follows a bullish regime, or follows neutral if neutral entries are enabled, close any long and go short. Gate signals on valid yield data and, if configured, a newly printed rates bar. Hold positions until an opposing regime flip; use no stops or targets.
