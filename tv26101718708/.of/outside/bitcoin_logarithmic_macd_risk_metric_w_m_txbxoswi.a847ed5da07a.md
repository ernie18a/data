On weekly or monthly bars, calculate Bitcoin momentum from the logarithm of the fast and slow exponential averages of the Bitcoin index close, then normalize it between historically fitted lower and upper momentum boundaries. When normalized momentum is at or below the configurable buy threshold, signal buying or going long the traded instrument. When it is at or above the configurable sell threshold, signal selling or exiting a long position. Otherwise, give no signal. Confirm using the completed timeframe bar.
