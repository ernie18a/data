Compute daily signals for each enabled, available market. A trend signal is positive when the fast moving average of close exceeds the slow moving average by more than a configured multiple of slow-period ATR, negative below the opposite threshold, and zero otherwise. Alternatively, use an adaptive volatility stop: track the running high and low of close, trail the stop upward by a configured volatility distance in an uptrend or downward in a downtrend, and reverse when close falls below the stop or rises to or above it, resetting the extremes and stop on reversal. Volatility is selectable as ATR, standard deviation, or a moving average of absolute deviations from a moving average. The combined signal is the sum of the trend and stop signals. Treat a positive score as bullish and a negative score as bearish; a zero score contributes nothing when neutral signals are enabled and is bullish otherwise. Exclude markets whose selected signal is not ready.

For bullish signals, assign one vote to each of Goldilocks and Reflation for the S&P 500, Russell 2000, STOXX 600, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, emerging-market equity futures, high-beta/low-volatility ratio, cyclicals/defensives ratio, industrial metals, silver/gold ratio, copper, Bitcoin futures, AUD/USD, EUR/USD, and GBP/USD. Assign one vote to each of Inflation and Deflation for the VIX, bond volatility, implied correlation, US dollar index, and distressed, high-yield, and investment-grade credit spreads. Assign one vote to each of Reflation and Inflation for Brent oil, agriculture, German, UK, Japanese and US government yields, and US breakeven inflation. A bearish signal votes for the two regimes complementary to its bullish pair.

Normalize each regime's vote total by the sum of all four totals and smooth each resulting share with a configurable simple moving average. Select the highest share; retain the previous regime if it is tied for highest, otherwise break ties in the order Goldilocks, Reflation, Inflation, Deflation. If scores are unavailable, retain the previous regime.

Assign an optional long asset to each regime. If no assets are specified, assign the chart instrument to Goldilocks and Reflation and cash to the other regimes; otherwise treat each unassigned regime as cash. A regime's asset is eligible only when its selected open or close price and its previous price are available. On the next chart bar after determining the target, hold the eligible asset for the prevailing regime, or cash if none is eligible. Enter long when moving from cash to an asset, exit when moving to cash, and sell the old asset and buy the new one when the assigned symbol changes. A change between regimes assigned the same symbol does not trigger a trade. There are no short positions.
