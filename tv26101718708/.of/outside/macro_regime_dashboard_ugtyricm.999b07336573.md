On each daily bar, signal a long entry at the start of a qualifying regime when all of the following hold: the volatility index exceeds its threshold; the effective federal funds rate is no higher than its value a specified number of trading days ago plus a tolerance; the latest quarterly broker-dealer margin-account balance is below the preceding quarter's balance; the strongest return among the specified sector and semiconductor ETFs exceeds the broad-market ETF return over the same lookback by more than a specified threshold; and a strict majority of configured bellwethers with available actual and estimated earnings have actual earnings at or above estimates. If no bellwether has both values, pass the earnings condition. Allow the policy-rate and earnings conditions to be independently forced to pass or fail. Also require each enabled fail-safe to pass: the high-yield credit spread must be below its ceiling, and monthly CPI year-over-year growth must be below its ceiling. Treat missing fail-safe data as passing; ignore disabled fail-safes. Missing data for the volatility, automatic policy-rate, margin-debt, or sector condition prevents that condition from passing; skip unavailable sector returns. The long signal occurs only when the full composite becomes true after being false. No short or exit decision is specified.
