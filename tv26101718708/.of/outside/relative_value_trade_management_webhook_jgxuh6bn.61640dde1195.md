Choose a long instrument and a short instrument. On the selected timeframe, divide the long instrument's close by the short instrument's close. Within the inclusive configured date window, when both the entry and stop levels are nonzero, the strategy is flat, and this ratio crosses the entry level in either direction, enter long on the designated long instrument or short on the designated short instrument. Ignore other instruments. The code attempts to place stop and take-profit exits only while long, using the configured levels as prices of the traded instrument and the configured exit percentage; however, it references an entry ID that is 'None' while a position is open, so these exits do not match the entry. It places no exits while short.
