In pairs mode, form a series from the traded instrument's selected source and a second instrument's close: either their ratio or the difference between their logarithms, weighted by a fixed hedge ratio or the rolling covariance of their logarithms divided by the rolling variance of the second logarithm. If the rolling hedge ratio is unavailable, use the fixed ratio. Calculate the series' rolling z-score using its mean and standard deviation. Estimate mean-reversion speed as the rolling covariance of the previous series value with its change, divided by the rolling variance of the previous value. Permit entries only when this coefficient is negative and its implied half-life, minus the natural logarithm of two divided by the coefficient, is positive and shorter than the statistical lookback.

Track flat, long, and short states. While flat and entry is permitted, aggressive mode enters long when the z-score crosses below the negative entry threshold, or short when it crosses above the positive threshold if shorts are enabled. Conservative mode arms a side when the z-score is strictly beyond its entry threshold and enters when it crosses back across that threshold toward zero, provided entry is still permitted. Optionally, independently arm and enter on the same pattern at a configurable near threshold in either entry mode. Arms persist if the gate later fails; while flat, clear long arms above zero and short arms below zero. Clear all arms on any entry or exit, and record the entry bar's high and low.

Exit a long when the z-score crosses above the negative normal-exit threshold; exit a short when it crosses below the positive normal-exit threshold. Optionally exit on a renewed adverse crossing of the entry threshold, on a close below the entry bar's low for a long or above its high for a short, or on an adverse crossing of a configurable emergency z-score threshold. Evaluate entries and exits from the state at the start of each bar.
