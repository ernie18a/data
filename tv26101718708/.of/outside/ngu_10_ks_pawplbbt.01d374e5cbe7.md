During the configured trading session, signal long when any of these occurs: in a confirmed trend, price crosses above descending swing resistance while above the Ichimoku cloud; in a range, price tests nearby support and the candle body is positioned in the upper portion of its range; or a regular bullish divergence (price lower low, RSI higher low) or hidden bullish divergence (price higher low, RSI lower low) aligns with nearby support. Signal short symmetrically: in a confirmed trend, price crosses below ascending swing support while below the cloud; in a range, price tests nearby resistance and the candle body is positioned in the lower portion of its range; or regular bearish divergence (price higher high, RSI lower high) or hidden bearish divergence (price lower high, RSI higher high) aligns with nearby resistance. Classify the regime from the direction of the recent linear price trend and the size of its range relative to volatility, requiring consecutive bars to confirm a regime change. Build nearby support and resistance from repeated swing lows and highs clustered by volatility. Enforce a cooldown between signals in the same direction. No trade exit condition is defined.
