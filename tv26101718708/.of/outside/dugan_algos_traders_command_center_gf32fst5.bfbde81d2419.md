On each bar, count six bullish checks: close above session VWAP, fast EMA above slow EMA, close above each of two SMAs, close above the previous daily close, and the external market-breadth instrument's daily close above zero. Every failed or unavailable check counts as bearish. A long bias requires all but at most one check to be bullish; a short bias requires all but at most one to be bearish. Otherwise the bias is mixed.

Qualify a directional bias using cumulative volume since the start of the current day divided by the average daily volume of completed prior days. Volume passes when this ratio meets its configured threshold. Measure momentum as the change in the MACD histogram over a configured lookback divided by current ATR, and reverse its sign for a short bias. Momentum is accelerating above the configured positive flat-zone boundary, fading below its negative boundary, and flat between them. The configurable momentum gate either always passes, passes unless momentum is fading, or passes only when momentum is accelerating. If momentum is unavailable, the first two modes pass and the accelerating-only mode fails.

An A+ long or short requires the corresponding bias and both gates to pass. Emit its setup alert only when that condition changes from false to true. A directional bias with failed volume is labeled low volume; if volume passes but momentum fails, it is labeled fading. When momentum newly becomes fading while a directional bias exists, issue a position-management warning to consider tightening or reducing the position; no definite exit rule is specified.
