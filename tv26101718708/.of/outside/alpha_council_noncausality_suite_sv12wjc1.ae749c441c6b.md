Evaluate each configured timeframe separately using the traded instrument and, when enabled, external premium-index, open-interest and stablecoin-dominance series. For each external signal, maintain its most recent direction: update to short on a bearish condition, otherwise to long on a bullish condition, otherwise retain the previous direction. In long-only mode ignore bearish state updates. Open-interest exhaustion sets both directions, so short takes priority unless long-only mode is active.

In retail mode, premium is bearish when it crosses below zero and bullish when it crosses above zero. Open interest is both bearish and bullish when it falls. Stablecoin dominance is bearish when rising below its moving average and bullish when falling above it. In institutional mode, premium is bearish when its rolling z-score crosses down through a positive threshold and bullish when it crosses up through the negative threshold. Open interest is both bearish and bullish when its rolling z-score falls by more than a threshold. For stablecoin dominance, save its value whenever traded-instrument volume has an unusually high rolling z-score. Among retained volume-anomaly anchors with nonzero cumulative signed volume, vote bearish when dominance rises while below its anchored value, and bullish when it falls while above its anchored value. A direction triggers when its votes reach the configured fraction of valid anchors, with at least one valid anchor.

For chart entry markers, evaluate those external triggers on the base timeframe independently by source and direction. First require that price has touched the corresponding upper boundary for a short or lower boundary for a long, using any enabled fitted asymmetric high/low boundary, Gaussian-kernel deviation envelope, or polynomial regression channel. Remember the touch until close crosses the rolling price mean in the opposite direction. Block arming during an opposing high-volatility trend, excessive kernel slope or acceleration, directional pulse saturation, or accelerating short-term ATR unless enabled volume absorption overrides that last block. Pulse saturation accumulates on strong directional candles, decays otherwise, and uses separate lock and release thresholds. Once armed, latch safety clearance after the enabled previous-candle break, true-range deceleration, and time-exhaustion checks pass; an appropriate previous-candle break overrides failed time exhaustion. Reset clearance, touch and each source's one-shot flag when close crosses the rolling mean in the opposite direction.

While armed and safety-cleared, issue a short marker for each enabled bearish external trigger on a bearish candle, or a long marker for each enabled bullish external trigger on a bullish candle. Each source can trigger only once per direction until its reset. If display of blocked signals is enabled, a blocked trigger on the matching candle also consumes that source's one-shot flag. The fitted boundaries and kernel safety checks are recomputed over the whole rolling window, so historical markers can repaint. No exit rule or options decision is specified.
