On each chart bar, calculate directional bias as (close minus EMA of close) divided by ATR, using zero when ATR is not positive. Classify it as bullish above the bullish threshold, bearish below the bearish threshold, and neutral otherwise. Calculate the ratio of short-window to long-window standard deviations of log close-to-close returns. Classify the ratio as expanding above the upper threshold, compressing below the lower threshold, and normal otherwise; if the ratio is unavailable, retain its previous classification, initially normal.

Use the previous confirmed daily India VIX close to select a position size from configured volatility bands, decreasing size as VIX rises and setting it to zero in the extreme band. If VIX is unavailable, retain the previous size, initially zero. When size is zero, recommend no trade. Otherwise: bullish bias with normal or expanding volatility recommends a put credit spread; bearish bias with normal or expanding volatility recommends a call credit spread. Either directional bias with compressing volatility recommends an iron condor, with the short strike on the bias side closer to price. Neutral bias with normal volatility recommends a symmetric iron condor; neutral bias with compressing volatility recommends a wider symmetric iron condor; neutral bias with expanding volatility recommends no trade.

For each recommended spread, place the short put below close and the short call above close by the configured regime-specific ATR multiples, rounding each away from close to the strike increment. Buy the corresponding protective put below the short put or protective call above the short call by the configured wing width. Use the VIX-based recommended lot size. The dashboard also advises exiting at a configured multiple of premium collected, but the script does not implement or monitor this stop.
