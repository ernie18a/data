Classify the broad market using its weekly close versus a weekly moving average and its daily close versus short- and long-term daily moving averages. Red: if the index closes below its weekly average for two consecutive weeks, or below its long-term daily average, exit all positions and hold cash. Yellow, unless red: if the index closes below its weekly average or its short-term daily average, make no new purchases; hold a stock only while it remains above its own weekly average, and sell it if it falls below. Green: when the index is above its weekly and long-term daily averages, use the full allocation, buy the highest-ranked stocks by relative strength, and sell when a stock falls in the ranking or its relative strength drops below parity. After a red state, resume buying only after the index closes back above its weekly average, using a freshly calculated relative-strength ranking.
