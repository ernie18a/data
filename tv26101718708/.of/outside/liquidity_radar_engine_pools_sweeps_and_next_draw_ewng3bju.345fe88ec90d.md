Track untouched liquidity pools at prior-period highs and lows, session highs and lows, and confirmed swing highs and lows; combine nearby levels into clusters. A pool is swept only when price pierces it by a meaningful volatility-adjusted distance and closes back inside. A close beyond a pool breaks and consumes it without a signal. After a sweep, require a close back through the pool within the allowed reclaim window; a sweep that fails or expires without reclaim is invalid. For a sell-side sweep followed by reclaim above the level, consider LONG; for a buy-side sweep followed by reclaim below it, consider SHORT. Require a confirming lower-timeframe structure break in the trade direction. Before that confirmation, stand down if higher-timeframe structure bias opposes the trade. Once armed, offer a fresh direction only if the nearest untouched pool in that direction provides at least the configured minimum reward-to-risk, the stop distance is within the configured volatility limit, and the stop is on the valid side of price. Place the initial stop beyond the swept wick; trail it to the latest confirmed higher low for a long or lower high for a short when that is more protective. End the trade premise on a close through the stop, a close through the confirmed trailing structure, a close back through the swept level before reclaim, or reclaim-window expiry. A stronger or equally weighted new sweep may take over the active story. Higher-timeframe bias and composite trend opposing an armed trade call for reduced size or skipping, not a veto. If already positioned against the active story, exit; after a favorable move of at least one unit of risk, move the stop to breakeven.
