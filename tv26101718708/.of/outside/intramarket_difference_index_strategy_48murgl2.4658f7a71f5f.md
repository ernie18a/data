On each chart bar, compare the traded instrument with a configurable base market on the chart timeframe. In moving-average mode, calculate for each market (close minus its simple moving average) divided by the square root of its ATR multiplied by the moving-average lookback; subtract the base market's result from the traded instrument's result. In RSI mode, subtract the base market's RSI from the traded instrument's RSI; use the RSI lookback itself as the signal threshold.

In trend mode, signal long when the difference first reaches or exceeds the positive threshold, and short when it first reaches or falls below the negative threshold. Confirm the crossing one bar later: the condition must have been true on the preceding bar and false two bars ago. In mean-reversion mode, normalize the difference within its rolling high-low range, recursively smooth it, clamp it near the limits, apply the Fisher logarithmic transform and recursively smooth the result. Use crossings of the positive and negative Fisher thresholds for long and short respectively, with the same one-bar confirmation. A zero rolling range produces no valid transformed signal.

Optionally filter confirmed signals using a smoothed Hurst estimate from the traded instrument's closes: trend signals require the estimate to be at least its threshold, and mean-reversion signals require it to be below that threshold. Estimate it from normalized close ranges over the full lookback and its two halves, recursively smooth the resulting fractal dimension, subtract that dimension from two, and apply a two-pole low-pass filter.

Enter only if the current close is strictly between the highest and lowest prior closes over their respective configurable Donchian lookbacks and the preceding bar's position was flat. Calculate those channel bounds on the selected chart or higher timeframe, excluding the current bar of that timeframe. Respect direction enablement and, if enabled, skip the next signal after the configured number of consecutive losing trades, then reset the loss count. Use the signal bar's open as the reference for a fixed ATR stop on the adverse side and an optional ATR profit target on the favorable side. Entry sizing is calculated within the configured backtest period; the entry submission itself is not separately gated by that period.

Exit a long when its stop or optional target is reached, or when the bar's low or close reaches the lower channel. Exit a short symmetrically using its stop, target, and the upper channel. Optionally close all positions when open trade loss exceeds its configured initial-capital limit. The optional daily equity rule evaluates its loss and gain limits only on a change of day, comparing equity then with the equity saved on the preceding same-day bar; close an open position immediately if either limit is reached. Opposite signals do not themselves exit an open position.
