Compare the traded market with a correlated reference market. Form a relative-strength difference by subtracting the reference market’s indicator from the traded market’s indicator, using either each market’s deviation from its moving average scaled by volatility or the difference between their RSI readings. In trend mode, go long when this difference first crosses above its positive threshold, or short when it first crosses below its negative threshold; require the trend regime when that filter is enabled. In mean-reversion mode, apply a Fisher transform to the difference and go long on a newly reached positive extreme or short on a newly reached negative extreme; require the mean-reverting regime when that filter is enabled. A signal is eligible only when price is inside its prior-period Donchian channel and there is no open position. Enter at the next bar’s open. Exit a long at its volatility-based stop, optional volatility-based profit target, or when price reaches or breaks the lower Donchian channel; exit a short at its corresponding stop, optional profit target, or upper channel. Optional risk controls can close positions at daily or per-trade loss limits and skip an entry after enough consecutive losing trades.
