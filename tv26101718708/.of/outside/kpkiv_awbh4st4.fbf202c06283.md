Choose a call or put, a strike, an underlying spot high and low, a level spacing, and how many levels to enable. Calculate intrinsic value as the greater of zero and either spot high minus strike for a call or strike minus spot low for a put. Build an ascending ladder by adding successive multiples of the spacing to intrinsic value. When entries are enabled, buy the selected option if its close crosses above an enabled ladder level: the current close is above the current level and the previous close was at or below the previous level. If several levels cross on the same bar, select the lowest one; enter only if its next higher level is enabled. Record the close as the entry price, the previous bar's low as the stop, and the next higher level as the target. A new entry replaces any active trade's recorded stop and target. On each bar, including the entry bar, if target exits are enabled and the high reaches or exceeds the target, exit; otherwise, if stop exits are enabled and the close is at or below the stop, exit. Clear the active trade after either exit.
