For a call, calculate intrinsic value as the greater of zero and spot high minus strike; for a put, use the greater of zero and strike minus spot low. From intrinsic value, form ascending levels at equal step intervals. Enter long when the traded instrument’s close crosses above one of the first six levels, targeting the next level above the crossed level and setting the stop at the previous candle’s low. Exit when the traded instrument’s high reaches the target or its close falls to or below the stop. No short-entry logic is defined.
