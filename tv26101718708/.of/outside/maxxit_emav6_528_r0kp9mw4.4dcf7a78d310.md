On each confirmed bar, allow entries only when trading is enabled for the instrument and timeframe, the configured session and weekday filters pass, the daily loss limit is not reached, and the position count is below its cap. Go long when the slow trend EMA is rising and the fast EMA crosses above the intermediate EMA; go short on the mirrored falling-trend and downward-cross conditions. If pullback confirmation is enabled, require price to have crossed back through the fast EMA in the trend direction, with the fast EMA aligned beyond the slow EMA. If rebound re-entry mode is enabled, require a flat position, aligned rising or falling trend and fast EMA, price moving in that direction, fast EMA still aligned with the slow EMA, a preceding pullback through the fast EMA, and trend extension below the excluded extreme states; re-entry also requires stop, target, or trailing protection to be enabled. Direction-only settings can suppress entries and close an opposing position. When enabled, exit long positions at their profit target or when price reaches the fixed or ratcheting trailing stop; mirror these rules for shorts. Automatic trailing protection may also manage exits. Opposing entry signals reverse the position when both directions are permitted.
