When mirror trading is enabled, use the chart instrument as the signal instrument and trade its mapped ETF or CFD. Calculate all signal indicators and exit levels from the signal instrument’s prices.

On confirmed bars, enter long when the slow trend EMA is higher than every value in its lookback window and the fast EMA crosses strictly above the intermediate EMA. Enter short on the exact inverse. If the pullback filter is enabled, a long also requires the previous low below the previous fast EMA, the current close above the current fast EMA, and the fast EMA above the trend EMA; reverse all comparisons for a short.

If re-entry is enabled and a stop or target is enabled, an additional signal is allowed only while flat: for a long, require the trend EMA, fast EMA, and bar highs each to be rising over their respective lookbacks; the fast EMA must be above the current trend EMA both now and at the specified prior bar; the previous low must be below the previous fast EMA and the current close above the current fast EMA. Reject this signal if the absolute fast-to-trend EMA distance divided by ATR is in either overextended range. Reverse the comparisons and use lows for a short. Re-entry supplements the crossover signal.

Permit signals only within the configured session and allowed days, after the backtest start, while trading is enabled and the direction’s pyramid limit is not reached. Stop new signals for the day when realized daily loss reaches its limit. A signal can add to an existing position or reverse an opposite position. In a direction-only mode, an opposite signal closes the permitted position instead of opening the other direction; enabling both direction-only modes permits both directions.

At a new position or reversal, set stop and target prices as volatility-adjusted percentages of the signal bar’s close. In manual-stop mode, once profit measured from the volume-weighted average entry exceeds the trailing activation distance, trail the initial stop by the current profit, ratcheting only in the favorable direction. On confirmed bars, exit at the target first if enabled, otherwise at the manual stop if enabled. In automatic mode, submit the initial stop and target and, for crossover entries, a trailing stop with its configured activation distance and offset. Opposite signals can also exit or reverse the position.
