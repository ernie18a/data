Build a positioning oscillator from external implied-volatility, cash and futures data. On the volatility timeframe, convert the volatility index's rolling percentile rank to standard-deviation units using the mean and standard deviation of a uniform percentile distribution. Also z-score the difference between the index and its exponential moving average over a rolling window. On the chart timeframe, z-score the futures premium relative to cash. Combine the negated volatility-rank and volatility-deviation scores with the positive basis score in a weighted average. Optionally add a rolling z-score of an external put-call ratio with a configurable sign, and subtract a rolling z-score of external option skew; include each optional leg's weight only when enabled and available. Treat an unavailable basis score as zero, retain its weight, and use zero if total weight is zero. Smooth the composite with an exponential moving average. Signal long when the oscillator crosses upward through the negative lean threshold; signal short when it crosses downward through the positive lean threshold. No exit rule is specified.
