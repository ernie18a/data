Using daily data, calculate a liquidity measure as the central bank balance sheet minus overnight reverse-repurchase balances minus the Treasury cash balance. Derive upper and lower rolling percentile bands from that measure. Enter long when either band is rising. Exit long when the measure crosses below both bands on the same bar, or when the upper band is falling. Enter short when the measure crosses below both bands on the same bar. Exit short when the measure crosses above both bands on the same bar.
