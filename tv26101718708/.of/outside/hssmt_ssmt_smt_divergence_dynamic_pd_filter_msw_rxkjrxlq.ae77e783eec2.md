On supported intraday chart intervals, divide time into the corresponding structural cycles and track each asset’s cycle low and high, using either closes or wicks. Compare the charted instrument with correlated assets: signal bullish when their cycle lows diverge (one makes a lower low while another makes a higher low), and bearish when their cycle highs diverge (one makes a higher high while another makes a lower high). Close-based comparisons use adjacent cycles; wick-based comparisons use the adjacent cycle or selected prior cycles. Include the additional comparison asset for bullish signals only. When the premium/discount filter is enabled and an applicable true open exists, keep bullish signals only when both compared lows are below it, and bearish signals only when both compared highs are above it. These are directional signals; the script specifies no trade entry, exit, or option action.
