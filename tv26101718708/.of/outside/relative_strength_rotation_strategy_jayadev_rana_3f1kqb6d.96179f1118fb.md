Compare the traded instrument with a benchmark, chosen by asset class or supplied explicitly. Measure relative strength from their price ratio. Rank its momentum over short, medium, and long horizons against its own history, then average the ranks into a rotation score. Go long when the score crosses above an upper threshold and the instrument passes enabled filters: relative performance is above its long-term average, relative-strength slope is sufficiently positive, and price is above its own moving average. Go short when the score crosses below the corresponding lower threshold and enabled filters pass in the opposite direction. After an exit, wait through a cooldown before entering again. Close a long when the score falls below its exit threshold or, if that filter is enabled, relative performance turns negative; close a short when the score rises above the mirrored exit threshold or enabled relative performance turns positive. Also close either position at a wide volatility-based stop.
