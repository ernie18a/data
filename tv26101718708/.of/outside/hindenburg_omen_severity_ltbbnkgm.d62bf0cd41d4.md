Using daily NYSE data, divide the counts of new 52-week highs and lows separately by advancing plus declining issues. When both proportions meet a configurable threshold and the NYSE Composite is at or above its value a configurable number of chart bars ago, open or restart a configurable signal window. Calculate a breadth oscillator as the difference between a shorter and a longer exponential moving average of normalized net advances: (advances minus declines) divided by (advances plus declines). Use zero for these ratios when their denominator is zero. While the window is open, if the oscillator is negative, consider going short or exiting a long position. A nonnegative oscillator deactivates the signal until it becomes negative again within the window. The window includes the trigger bar and remains open through the configured number of subsequent chart bars.
