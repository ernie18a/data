When both new-high and new-low counts, each divided by advancing plus declining issues, meet the breadth threshold and the broad market index is at or above its value from the trend lookback, open or refresh a signal window. Within that window, calculate the McClellan Oscillator from advancing and declining issues; while it is negative, treat the omen as active and consider shorting the traded instrument or exiting a long position. A nonnegative oscillator deactivates the signal until it turns negative again; the signal window expires after its configured duration. There is no long-entry rule.
