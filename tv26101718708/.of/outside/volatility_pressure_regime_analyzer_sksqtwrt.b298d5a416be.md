Use the traded instrument’s price, an optional related volatility index, and an optional companion underlying or derivative. Require valid data for every enabled external feed. Calculate compression as the inverse historical percentile of ATR; volatility dislocation as the historical percentile of the absolute gap between the volatility index and expected realized volatility; basis tension as the historical percentile of the absolute change in the derivative’s percentage premium over the underlying; convexity as the historical percentile of absolute log-return acceleration; and inefficiency as one minus the price efficiency ratio. Combine these components using their configured weights and divisor, omitting disabled external components, then percentile-rank the result against its own history. Expected realized volatility is trailing realized volatility until the optional forecast is ready; thereafter, forecast it with a rolling ridge regression of realized volatility on fast/slow exponential blends of lagged returns and the square root of blended lagged squared returns, flooring the forecast at zero.

A setup is loaded when the pressure percentile reached its configured threshold within the configured lookback. A release occurs when the current high-low range, divided by ATR and mapped linearly into a bounded expansion score, reaches its threshold. Count consecutive bars on which both conditions and feed validity hold. Generate a signal only on the exact confirmation-count bar, provided the minimum gap since the previous signal has elapsed and enabled filters pass; a failed filter or gap on that bar does not produce a later signal during the same uninterrupted run.

Determine direction on the signal bar by scoring an upside break of the preceding lookback’s high, a close in the upper half of the bar, and nonnegative price-source momentum; score the corresponding downside conditions separately. Give a breakout twice the weight of each other condition. Go long when the upside score is at least the downside score; otherwise go short.

If the optional regime filter is active, suppress signals in a pinning regime, except for expiry-session signals when their exemption is enabled. Classify pinning when the ratio of multi-bar return variance to the matching multiple of one-bar return variance falls below the configured neutral band and, if significance testing is enabled, its standardized deviation also passes the negative significance threshold. If the optional trend filter is active, require the configured number of chart and higher timeframes to agree with the signal direction, subject to the same separately configurable expiry exemption. A timeframe agrees when its close is above the displaced Ichimoku cloud for a long or below it for a short; use closed higher-timeframe bars when configured.

The script specifies no exit rule.
