Generate a directional signal when a composite pressure measure reaches a high percentile of its own recent history within a recent lookback and the current bar makes a sufficiently large ATR-normalized range expansion. Build pressure from inverse ATR percentile, implied-versus-forecast-realized volatility dislocation percentile, companion-instrument basis-momentum percentile, return-acceleration percentile, and directional inefficiency; omit unavailable external-data factors and renormalize the weights. Require the loaded-and-release condition to persist for the configured confirmation duration and enforce a minimum spacing between signals. Set direction by scoring a close beyond the prior lookback high or low more heavily than close location in the bar and recent momentum; choose up on a tie. Optionally suppress signals that disagree with a higher-timeframe Ichimoku cloud bias, or signals during a statistically significant mean-reverting variance-ratio regime; expiry signals may be exempted from either filter according to configuration. The indicator also optionally computes the same pressure-release and direction logic independently on higher timeframes using price-only inputs. These rules issue long/up or short/down signals; there is no exit or options-buy/sell decision.
