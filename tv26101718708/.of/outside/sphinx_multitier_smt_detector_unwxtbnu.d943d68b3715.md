Compare the traded instrument with one or two configured correlated instruments. For each completed session and each retained completed cycle at several nested intraday scales, record each instrument's own high and low. Reset these references and signal states at the configured daily reset.

For each reference, latch whether each instrument has subsequently traded strictly above its own high or below its own low. A high-side mismatch is bearish and a low-side mismatch is bullish when at least one, but not all, included instruments have swept. Track bullish and bearish states independently for each scale. Among qualifying references at a scale, prefer a mismatch involving the first comparison instrument, then the most recent reference; do not replace an active first-comparison mismatch with a second-comparison-only mismatch. Retire an active state when the traded instrument and its selected comparison instrument have matching sweep states, or when its retained reference expires. Apply the configured directional permission and no-trade gates when starting a state.

Signal long when the session-scale and largest cycle-scale bullish states are active together with a bullish state in either smaller cycle scale. Signal short under the equivalent bearish alignment. Emit a new signal when the corresponding alignment first becomes active and rearm after it ceases. The framework specifies no position exit.
