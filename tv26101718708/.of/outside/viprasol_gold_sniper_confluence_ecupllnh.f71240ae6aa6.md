On a fast EMA cross above or below a slow EMA, consider a long or short entry respectively; optionally require the bar to close. If confluence filtering is enabled, require the directional score to reach the configured minimum, capped at the number of enabled factors. Award one point for each of: close on the directional side of session VWAP; RSI on the directional side of its midpoint; MACD line on the directional side of its signal; fast EMA on the directional side of slow EMA; ADX above its trend threshold and close on the directional side of fast EMA; positive volume above its moving average with a candle closing in the direction; confirmation-timeframe RSI on the directional side of its midpoint. If the dollar-index factor is enabled, award one additional long or short point respectively when its rolling close correlation with the traded instrument is at or below the inverse-link threshold and its close is below or above its own EMA. Missing dollar data or correlation awards no point.

Also require all enabled entry filters: the instrument must qualify as gold by base currency, non-stock ticker name, futures root, or custom ticker keyword; intraday bars must fall within either configured session window; and, except on weekly or monthly charts, the current day's accumulated chart-bar range must be below the configured fraction of the average range of completed days. Missing or nonpositive average range does not block entry. Require the configured minimum number of bars since the last entry. Daily and higher charts bypass the session filter.

Track each accepted entry independently at the signal bar's close. Set its initial stop one configured ATR multiple against the direction and place the configured target ladder at successive multiples of that risk distance in the favorable direction. Starting on the next bar, check the working stop first; exit if touched. Otherwise record touched targets, optionally move the stop to entry after the first target, and exit if the final configured target is touched. A newly activated breakeven stop is first checked on the next bar. Advance existing trades before processing new entries; opposite or blocked crosses do not close them. Discard the oldest tracked trade when the configured tracking limit is exceeded.
