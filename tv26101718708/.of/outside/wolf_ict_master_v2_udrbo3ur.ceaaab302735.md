Use the traded instrument’s bars and same-timeframe highs and lows from two configurable correlated instruments. Confirm pivots only after the configured number of bars on each side. A bullish divergence occurs when the traded instrument makes a lower pivot low than its preceding pivot low while at least one correlated instrument makes a higher low at those same pivot times; bearish divergence is the inverse. Divergence detection requires its display setting to be enabled.

For real-time early signals, go long on an enabled test below the latest confirmed swing low, overlap with an enabled bullish inverted gap, or a newly confirmed bullish divergence; go short on the opposite conditions. Require an enabled active session and the optional volatility filter, and apply a separate same-direction early-signal cooldown. These signals are emitted only when early signals are enabled.

For confirmed signals, a bullish trigger is either an enabled sweep below the latest swing low that closes back above it with a sufficiently large lower wick, or an enabled overlap with a bullish inverted gap; in both cases the candle must close above its open and pass the optional body-size-versus-ATR displacement filter. Reverse these conditions for bearish triggers. A gap is formed when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier, or the current high is below the low two bars earlier, by at least the configured minimum. Retain only the configured number of most recent gaps in each direction. A bearish gap becomes bullish inverted when close rises above its upper boundary; a bullish gap becomes bearish inverted when close falls below its lower boundary. Remove an inverted gap when price crosses its far boundary. Gap creation and inversion require their respective display settings.

Score each direction by adding one point each for its confirmed trigger, a recent same-direction divergence, same-direction trend, overlap with a same-direction ordinary or inverted gap, and a same-direction consecutive-close confirmation firing on this bar. Trend changes upward when close exceeds the latest confirmed trend pivot high and downward when close falls below the latest confirmed trend pivot low. Consecutive-close confirmation fires on a confirmed bar after the configured run of directional closes, subject to alternating direction and a minimum spacing; it requires its display setting.

On a confirmed bar, emit a long or short setup when its trigger is present, its score reaches an enabled grade’s threshold, the active-session gate and optional ATR-based chop filter pass, and the same-direction confirmed-signal cooldown has elapsed. Use the higher grade at or above its threshold and the lower grade between the two thresholds. Update the cooldown only when the corresponding grade is displayed. The session gate is always closed when the hard-session setting is disabled. The volume calculation does not filter signals. In divergence-only pattern mode there are no confirmed triggers, although early divergence signals remain possible. No actual exit decision is specified; the displayed stop and targets are reference levels.
