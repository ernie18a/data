Maintain two directional states. For the reversal state, go long when the instrument closes above the prior close after the close two bars ago was below the prior close, and the smoothed fast stochastic is below the smoothed slow stochastic but above a midpoint threshold. Go short on the inverse price pattern when fast stochastic is above slow stochastic but below the midpoint. Otherwise retain the prior reversal state. For the relative-strength state, compare a moving average of the instrument’s closing-price ratio to an external benchmark’s closing price against upper and lower entry bands: go long above the upper band, short below the lower band. Exit a long when the ratio falls below a separate exit band, or a short when it rises above that band; otherwise retain the prior state. Signal long only when both states are long, short only when both are short, and exit or stay flat whenever they do not agree.
