Buy when the instrument’s price is above a fast EMA, the fast EMA is above a slower EMA, relative performance versus a benchmark is positive and improving, and relative volume meets its threshold on an up bar. Optional filters may also require price above intermediate and long-term simple moving averages and a rising slower EMA. Emit the buy signal only when this full setup first becomes true. Emit a risk signal when price is below the slower EMA, relative performance versus the benchmark is negative, and relative volume meets its threshold on a down bar; emit it only when this condition first becomes true. Bar direction is determined by either close versus open or close versus the previous close, according to the selected method. No short-entry or exit decision is defined.
