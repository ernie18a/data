On a configurable timeframe, calculate the instrument's and benchmark's close-to-close growth factors over a configurable lookback. Relative performance is 100 times the instrument growth factor divided by the benchmark growth factor minus 100. Calculate its earlier value using the same lookback shifted back by a configurable momentum period. On the chart timeframe, divide current volume by its simple moving average, including the current bar. Define a bullish bar as close above either open or previous close, according to the selected method.

Signal a long entry only on the first chart bar where all of these hold: close is above a fast exponential moving average, which is above a slower exponential moving average; relative performance exceeds a configurable minimum and its earlier value; and relative volume is at least its configured threshold on a bullish bar. Optional filters additionally require close above either or both configured simple moving averages and the slower exponential moving average above its previous-bar value. No explicit short or exit decision is specified.
