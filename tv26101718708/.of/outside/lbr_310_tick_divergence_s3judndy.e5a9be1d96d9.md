At each confirmed swing low, compare it with the preceding confirmed swing low within the configured spacing window. Emit a bullish signal when price makes a lower low and the fast-minus-slow moving-average momentum oscillator makes a higher low; when enabled, require the external market TICK low to also make a higher low, and when the zero-side filter is enabled, require both oscillator readings to be below zero. At each confirmed swing high, emit a bearish signal when price makes a higher high and the oscillator makes a lower high; when enabled, require the external TICK high to also make a lower high, and require both oscillator readings above zero if the zero-side filter is enabled. Otherwise emit no signal. These are divergence signals, not explicit entry or exit rules.
