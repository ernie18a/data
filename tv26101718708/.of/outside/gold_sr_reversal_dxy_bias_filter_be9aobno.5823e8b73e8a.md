Build support and resistance zones by clustering confirmed swing highs and lows within an ATR-based price width, retaining zones with sufficient pivot and price-touch strength. A support setup occurs when price is above a zone and a bar’s low wicks into it; signal long on the next bar only if its close crosses back above the zone’s top boundary, with the prior close at or below that boundary. A resistance setup occurs when price is below a zone and a bar’s high wicks into it; signal short on the next bar only if its close crosses back below the zone’s bottom boundary, with the prior close at or above that boundary. When the external-market filter is enabled, allow longs only when the US Dollar Index closes below its simple moving average on both the daily and four-hour timeframes; allow shorts only when it closes above its simple moving average on both. Missing index data fails the filter. No exit logic is specified.
