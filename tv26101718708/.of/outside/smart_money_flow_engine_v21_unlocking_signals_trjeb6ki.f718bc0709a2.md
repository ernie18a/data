When flat, buy a call if a bullish trigger occurs (RSI crosses above its buy threshold, bullish structure break, or bullish liquidity sweep) and all bullish filters pass: higher-timeframe trend direction and strength align, higher-timeframe volume is sufficient, chart-timeframe EMAs and price are bullish relative to VWAP, RSI is above its buy threshold but below its overbought limit, bullish score and net flow exceed their minimums, ADX exceeds its minimum, and any enabled reference-index, session, and bar-close checks pass. Buy a put by the symmetric bearish rules: RSI crosses below its sell threshold, bearish structure break, or bearish sweep; aligned bearish higher-timeframe and chart-timeframe trends; sufficient higher-timeframe volume and ADX; RSI below its sell threshold but above its oversold limit; and bearish score and net flow exceed their minimums in the bearish direction. Apply the daily signal cap and inter-signal cooldown, and allow entries only while flat. Exit a call when ADX weakens, price crosses below VWAP, bearish divergence or sweep occurs, volume dries up, or bullish score falls below its minimum. Exit a put on the corresponding opposite conditions: weak ADX, price crossing above VWAP, bullish divergence or sweep, dry volume, or bearish score below its minimum. Confirm exits on bar close when that setting is enabled. Displayed stop and target levels do not themselves trigger exits.
