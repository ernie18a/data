Use chart-timeframe price data and a configurable volume source; for specified spot indices, the default volume source is their corresponding futures, otherwise it is the chart instrument. Calculate fast, middle and slow close EMAs, daily-anchored VWAP and its deviation bands, RSI, ATR, ADX, and volume divided by its moving average. A volume spike occurs when that ratio reaches its threshold; volume is dry when it falls below its separate threshold.

Track confirmed swing highs and lows. Set the persistent structure direction bullish when the latest two highs and lows are both rising, bearish when both are falling, and otherwise retain its previous direction. A bullish structure break occurs when close crosses above the latest swing high while structure is bullish; a bearish break is the reverse. A bullish sweep occurs when low pierces the latest swing low and close recovers above it on a volume spike; a bearish sweep is the reverse. Bearish divergence means the latest swing high exceeds the preceding one while its pivot RSI is lower; bullish divergence means the latest swing low is lower while its pivot RSI is higher.

Build separate bullish and bearish scores by adding one point for each applicable condition: close on the corresponding side of VWAP, fast EMA on the corresponding side of middle EMA, a recent corresponding sweep, a recent corresponding structure break, a current volume spike, and close beyond the corresponding VWAP band. Flow is the normalized difference between these scores, or zero if their sum is zero.

For each completed bar of the longer confirmation timeframe, calculate trend strength as the sum of capped ADX contribution, EMA-stack contribution, capped absolute distance from VWAP in ATR units, and native relative-volume contribution. Require its strength to reach a threshold and its fast and middle EMAs to align with the intended direction. Also require the previous completed bar of the shorter higher timeframe to have matching EMA alignment and its selected-source volume divided by its volume average to reach a minimum. If reference-index confirmation is enabled and an index is specified, require its previous completed confirmation-timeframe fast and middle EMAs to align as well.

Buy a call when flat if all gates pass: bullish higher-timeframe and optional reference alignment; chart fast EMA above middle EMA, optionally middle above slow EMA, and close above VWAP; RSI strictly above its bullish entry threshold and below its overbought limit; bullish score at least its minimum; flow above its positive threshold; ADX above its effective minimum; and the optional session and bar-close requirements pass. The current bar must also have RSI cross above its bullish entry threshold, a bullish structure break, or a bullish sweep. Buy a put using the exact directional reverse, with RSI below its bearish entry threshold but above its oversold limit, bearish score at least its minimum, and flow below its negative threshold. Only enter if the daily signal cap and bar cooldown permit; reset the count at each new day and retain the last signal bar across days.

While holding a call, exit on a permitted confirmed bar if ADX falls below its minimum, close falls below current VWAP while the previous close was at or above current VWAP, bearish divergence exists, a bearish sweep occurs, volume is dry, or bullish score falls below its exit minimum. Exit a put on the corresponding opposite VWAP crossing, bullish divergence or sweep, dry volume, low ADX, or insufficient bearish score. Do not exit on the entry bar. Entry, stop and target levels are displayed but do not themselves trigger exits.
