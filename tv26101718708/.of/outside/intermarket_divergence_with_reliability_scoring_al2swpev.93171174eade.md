Use the close of a configurable external leader on the traded instrument’s timeframe, optionally lagged and inverted. Normalize it over a rolling window using either mean and standard deviation or median and scaled median absolute deviation; optionally use the median of normalized values from short, medium, and long windows. Optionally smooth the result.

Find confirmed local highs and lows in the leader oscillator and compare the traded instrument’s price at those same pivot bars. Compare each pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type, requiring a configurable maximum separation and minimum oscillator difference relative to its rolling standard deviation. For an optional three-pivot requirement, the preceding pair must also move in the same respective directions.

Signal long when a confirmed leader low is higher while the corresponding price is lower, or, if hidden divergence is enabled, when the leader low is lower while price is higher. Signal short when a confirmed leader high is lower while the corresponding price is higher, or, if hidden divergence is enabled, when the leader high is higher while price is lower. Emit the signal on the pivot-confirmation bar. If both directions occur together, the exported direction favors long.

Optionally require the absolute rolling correlation between price and the lagged, inverted-as-configured leader to meet a threshold. Optionally also require the current intraday time slot to pass the external session library’s significance test on price changes; the supplied code does not define that test. These gates apply to all four divergence classes. Reliability scores and the optional regime setting do not suppress the long or short signals. No exit rule is specified.
