Enter long when any shorter-period moving average crosses above the next longer-period moving average, or when flat and price closes above both the longest-period moving average and the high reached during the previous trade. Allow entries only when the external benchmark is no more than the specified drawdown from its running all-time high, or, during a deeper drawdown, when the benchmark is above its long-term moving average. On entry, set an initial stop below entry; as the trade’s peak gain reaches successive thresholds, raise the stop to a break-even-area level or to progressively tighter trailing levels below the trade’s highest price. Exit when price closes below the active stop or when any adjacent pair of the strategy’s moving averages crosses downward. Record the trade’s highest price for the next breakout re-entry. No short or options decisions.
