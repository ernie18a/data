On weekly bars, use the selected commodity's disaggregated COT report. Calculate producer/merchant long positions minus short positions, optionally adding swap dealer long minus short positions, and divide by open interest. Rank the current reading against its prior weekly history. After sufficient valid history and a valid reading on the preceding week, signal long when the rank enters the upper extreme, and signal short when it enters the lower extreme. An entry requires the preceding week to be outside that same extreme and the most recent week in that extreme to be more than the episode-separation period ago; the first entry has no separation requirement. Use configurable lookback, extreme-band width, separation period, hedger group, and futures-only versus futures-and-options data. No exit rule is specified.
