Go long when all trend-template tests pass: price is above the intermediate and long-term moving averages; the intermediate average is above the long-term average; the long-term average is rising; the short-term average is above both; price is above the short-term average; price has recovered sufficiently from its trailing-year low and remains sufficiently near its trailing-year high; and relative performance versus the broad-market benchmark meets the configured minimum. Then require price to cross above the latest confirmed swing high on volume exceeding its average by the configured surge threshold. The volatility-contraction and low-volume setup is a watch condition, not required by the breakout trigger. No short or exit rule is defined.
