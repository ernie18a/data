Build an ATR band around an exponential moving average, widening or tightening it according to recent volatility rank. Track direction from price crossing the prior opposing band, and ratchet the active band so it tightens but does not loosen. A direction reversal creates a candidate long or short. Score each candidate using higher-timeframe trend alignment, volume confirmation when available, graded directional RSI momentum, a regime score combining trend strength, inverse choppiness, and price fit to a linear trend, and the distance of the breakout beyond the prior band relative to volatility. Adapt the component weights by instrument type, volatility, regime, opening period, and expiry period; normalize automatic weights to a common total, or use configured custom weights. Accept a candidate only on a confirmed bar, when the session gate permits it, its score meets the configured minimum, and any enabled choppy-market exclusion permits it. The session gate applies to intraday charts; daily and higher charts bypass it. Higher-timeframe disagreement lowers the score rather than independently blocking a signal. Open long on an accepted upward reversal and short on an accepted downward reversal; an accepted opposite signal closes the existing direction and opens the new one. Set a volatility-based stop and staged profit targets in the trade direction. Close the position when the stop or final target is reached; if enabled, move the stop to entry after the first target is reached.
