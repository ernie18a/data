Trade only when flat and within the allowed trading dates. For longs, require the selected trend mode to be bullish (fast average above slow average, or price above the selected average) and a bullish crossover within the permitted recent-bar window. Also require the MACD histogram to be negative and at a recent extreme in absolute magnitude, with the optional filter that its magnitude exceeds a chosen multiple of the average magnitude of negative histogram values. Optional filters require RSI within the long range and rising, and enough recent down candles for a pullback. For shorts, apply the symmetric bearish trend and recent bearish crossover conditions, requiring a nonnegative MACD histogram at a recent high, optionally sufficiently large versus the average of positive histogram values; optional RSI must be within the short range and falling, and enough recent up candles must indicate a pullback. Apply configured post-trade and loss-streak pauses before either entry. Close an open long at its profit target or stop loss, and close an open short at its profit target or stop loss; each level is set as a percentage of the average entry price.
