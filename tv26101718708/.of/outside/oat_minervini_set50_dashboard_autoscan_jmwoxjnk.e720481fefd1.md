For each instrument, evaluate daily data. Mark the trend template as passed only when price is above the short-, intermediate-, and long-term moving averages; the intermediate average is above the long-term average; the long-term average is rising; the short-term average is above both longer averages; and price is sufficiently above its yearly low and near its yearly high. Calculate a contraction score from base depth, progressively tighter recent price ranges, declining volatility, reduced volume, and tightness on the base’s right side. Require the score to meet the configured minimum. Define the pivot as the highest high over the recent pivot lookback, excluding the current bar. Classify as BREAKOUT when the trend and contraction requirements pass, price is above the pivot but within the permitted chase distance, and current volume is sufficiently above its average. Classify as READY when the trend and contraction requirements pass and price is within the configured zone below the pivot. Classify as EXTENDED when the trend requirement passes but price is beyond the permitted chase distance above the pivot. Classify other trend-passing instruments as DEVELOP and those failing the required trend/setup conditions as SKIP. These are screening states and a breakout candidate, not an automatic order; no short or exit decision is defined.
