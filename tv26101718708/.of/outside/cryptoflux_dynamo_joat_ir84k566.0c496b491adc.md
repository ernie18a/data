Evaluate on each price update and after order fills. Compute a fast, middle, and slow EMA of the traded instrument, smoothed ATR as a percentage of close, MACD and its smoothed histogram, RSI, MFI, volume statistics, a cycle EMA, Bollinger Bands, and a Keltner Channel. Classify volatility as low, medium, or high using two ATR-percentage thresholds. A volume impulse occurs when volume exceeds either its moving-average multiple or its z-score threshold. The cycle is bullish or bearish when percentage deviation from its EMA exceeds the corresponding positive or negative threshold.

Calculate separate long and short scores. Add configured weights for these mirrored pathways: (1) aligned EMA ribbon, middle EMA slope in the trade direction, and close beyond the preceding rolling structure extreme; (2) smoothed MACD histogram beyond a directional adaptive baseline, MACD line beyond its signal, RSI and MFI beyond their directional thresholds, and volume impulse; (3) a squeeze release, meaning Bollinger Band width was below Keltner width on the previous bar but is no longer below it, with close beyond the previous corresponding Bollinger Band and directional histogram magnitude above its scaled adaptive baseline; (4) an aligned ribbon, close between the fast and middle EMAs, the preceding bar being the corresponding rolling pullback extreme, directional histogram, and volume impulse. The adaptive baseline is an EMA of the absolute raw MACD histogram multiplied by a sensitivity parameter. Adjust each score up or down for volume impulse, volatility regime, and directional cycle alignment, then clamp it to the permitted score range.

An entry requires the directional score to meet its threshold, directional cycle alignment, volume impulse, an active permitted session if session filtering is enabled, and maximum historical equity drawdown below the kill-switch threshold. When the optional external BTC dominance filter is enabled, calculate the moving average of changes in BTC dominance close at the chart timeframe without lookahead. Permit longs only when this slope is below the negative dominance threshold or its absolute value is strictly below the threshold; permit shorts only when it is above the positive threshold or its absolute value is strictly below the threshold. If both signals occur, choose long when the smoothed MACD histogram is positive and short otherwise. Short entries must also be enabled.

Size entries from equity risk divided by the current regime-adjusted smoothed-ATR stop distance, capped by an equity-based exposure limit and rounded to the quantity increment; require positive quantity. Close an opposite position on a qualifying signal and enter the new direction, subject to the same-bar re-entry setting and position limit.

For each open position, recalculate an average-entry-price stop and profit target using current smoothed ATR and regime-specific multipliers. If trailing is enabled, also place a close-based ATR-offset stop whenever it is tighter than the regular stop. Close a long early when the raw MACD histogram is negative or the bullish EMA alignment fails; close a short when the raw histogram is positive or bearish alignment fails.
