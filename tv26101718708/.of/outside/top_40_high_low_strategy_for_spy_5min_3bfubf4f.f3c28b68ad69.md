For each configured constituent, compare the selected current high/low or close with the previous lookback window's highest high and lowest low. Count a new high first if both extremes are exceeded; otherwise count a new low if applicable. Exclude constituents with neither extreme or unavailable data. Compute the percentage of new highs among new highs and lows, carrying forward the previous percentage when there are none and initializing it at the neutral midpoint. Smooth this percentage with a simple moving average to form the breadth index.

On the traded instrument, calculate a configurable MACD histogram and the change in a linear-regression curve. Smooth the regression change with an EMA and take an SMA of that smoothed slope. A long signal requires the histogram and breadth index both to rise, the absolute smoothed slope to exceed its trend threshold, the raw slope to be positive and above its SMA signal, and the breadth index to be below its upper entry limit. A short signal reverses all directions and requires the breadth index above its lower entry limit. The breadth index crossing its EMA is not required.

Enter the corresponding position only when flat, within the configured date and weekday entry session, and outside the optional entry pause. Do not pyramid. Option mode changes position sizing and estimated profit, not the signal.

For an open position, set the initial long stop to the lower of the entry-bar baseline stop and the preceding bar's low; the baseline is the low preceding entry minus a configurable multiple of ATR. Mirror this for shorts using highs and addition. Calculate the target from entry price and the unadjusted stop distance using the configured reward-to-risk ratio, then adjust the stop toward or away from entry by the configured risk offset. Without trailing, place stop and target exits during the entry session. With trailing enabled, initially place only the stop; after price strictly passes the target, update the long stop to the greater of its previous value and current low minus the ATR distance, or the short stop to the lesser of its previous value and current high plus the ATR distance. Continue trailing during the longer exit session. The trailing activation state is shared by both directional branches and persists from the prior bar.

If early exits are enabled and the open trade is profitable, close a long when breadth falls below its upper extreme after being above it, or when a short entry signal occurs; close a short when breadth rises above its lower extreme after being below it, or when a long entry signal occurs. If expiry handling is enabled, close all positions near the end of designated expiry weekdays and half on the alternate weekdays, regardless of profit. Cancel pending orders at the configured end-of-day cancellation window.
