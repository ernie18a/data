On each confirmed bar, consider a new trade only when no trade is active. Use the last completed higher-timeframe close versus its EMA to determine the instrument's trend, and the last completed dollar-index close versus its EMA to determine dollar strength. A falling dollar supports a long and a rising dollar supports a short; missing dollar data supports both. In strict dollar mode, block an unsupported direction; in soft mode, allow it but withhold its score contribution; in off mode, award both directions the contribution.

For each direction, calculate a weighted quality score from: aligned higher-timeframe trend; a directional liquidity sweep or the sweep requirement being disabled; price in the directional Fibonacci retracement zone or the Fibonacci requirement being disabled; dollar support or dollar confirmation being off; a directional EMA stack with price on the same side of the trend EMA; directional momentum; and an allowed session. Momentum requires price on the directional side of the fast EMA and VWAP, RSI on the directional side of its midpoint, MACD on the directional side of its signal, and ADX above its minimum. A bullish sweep breaks the previous lookback low and closes back above it; a bearish sweep breaks the previous lookback high and closes back below it. The Fibonacci zones use the current lookback high and low, with an ATR buffer, measured down from the high for longs and up from the low for shorts.

Enter long or short at the close when that direction's score meets the mode-dependent minimum and strictly exceeds the opposite score, and all enabled filters pass: aligned higher-timeframe trend, directional sweep, directional Fibonacci zone, allowed trading session, maximum ATR distance from the fast EMA, and sufficient distance from the relevant previous-day extreme. The previous-day filter passes if that extreme is unavailable or price lies outside its buffer on either side. Block entries within the configured time window around any enabled daily UTC news time. Also require a directional trigger: a sweep, a close crossing the fast EMA in the trade direction while beyond the mid EMA, or a close breaking the previous lookback extreme with directional momentum.

At entry, set three fixed-distance or ATR-based profit targets and a stop. If enabled and valid on the protective side of entry, use the previous liquidity-lookback extreme plus a buffer for the stop; otherwise use the fixed-distance or ATR-based stop. Starting on the next bar, track the best favorable high or low and mark the first two targets when touched; these marks do not close the trade. Exit at the third target if touched, even if the stop is also touched on that bar; otherwise exit at the stop if touched. After the first target was marked on an earlier bar, optionally exit when the close pulls back by the configured distance from the best favorable price. Independently of that option, after the first target was marked on an earlier bar, exit on a confirmed bar if price crosses to the adverse side of the fast EMA, RSI moves to the adverse side of its midpoint, or MACD moves to the adverse side of its signal. Stop and third-target exits take precedence over early exits. Do not open another trade on the bar that closes an active trade.
