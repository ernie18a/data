Compare the closing prices of two manually chosen option contracts, one call and one put, on the same underlying with similar expiry, both sampled at the chart's timeframe. Evaluate on each bar close. Bullish signal (go long the underlying, or favor calls): the call's close crosses from at-or-below to strictly above the put's close. Bearish signal (go short the underlying, or favor puts): the put's close crosses from at-or-below to strictly above the call's close. These two crossovers are the only trigger conditions; if neither occurs, no new signal is produced. The script has no explicit exit rule, so a position is held until the opposite crossover, which acts as a reverse or exit signal. Anchored VWAPs of the call and put (typical price HLC3, reset per session/week/month/quarter/year) and optional standard-deviation bands are computed for display and context only. They do not feed into any entry or exit decision.
