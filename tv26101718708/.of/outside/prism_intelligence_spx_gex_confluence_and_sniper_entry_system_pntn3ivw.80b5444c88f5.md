A high-confluence state is a total of at least 9; a middle state is 6 through 8.

Only consider long entries when price is above the supplied gamma-flip level and the 9-bar simple moving average is rising while price is above it. Issue a high-conviction long when that context holds and any of these occurs: price crosses above its 20-bar average; price crosses above the gamma-flip level; the total score crosses into the high-confluence state; price closes bullish within 0.3% of the gamma-flip level, put-support level, or lower support band; or, during session bars 3–8, price is above its 20-bar average, above its open and prior close, and the 9-bar average is rising (trigger only on the first bar this opening-momentum condition becomes true). Issue a faster long on a cross above the 9-bar average in the same high-confluence context if no high-conviction long fires on that bar. In the middle-confluence state, issue a cautionary long when price is above the gamma-flip level, the local uptrend filter holds, and price crosses above its 20-bar average or the gamma-flip level.

Signal an exit when price crosses below the gamma-flip level at any score, when the score crosses down below 6, or when price crosses below its 20-bar average while still above the gamma-flip level in the high-confluence state. The system defines no short-entry or options-trading decisions.
