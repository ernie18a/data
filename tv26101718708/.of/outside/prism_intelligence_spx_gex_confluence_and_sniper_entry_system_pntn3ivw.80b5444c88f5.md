On each chart bar, calculate a weighted confluence score from externally supplied session data and market series. Award tiered points for the stated market regime and composite score, and points for positive dealer gamma, spot volatility below its shorter simple moving average with that average below its longer average, spot volatility below longer-term volatility, subdued volatility of volatility, moderate tail-risk pricing, elevated equity put/call ratio, constructive trend-follower positioning, low hedging demand, a low volatility-control ratio, and positive market breadth. Calculate breadth as the difference between fast and slow exponential moving averages of ratio-adjusted advancing minus declining issues; use zero for the input when breadth data is unavailable or its denominator is not positive. Treat missing external observations as failing their corresponding conditions.

Require the close above the externally supplied gamma-flip level, a rising short simple moving average of the instrument's close, and the close above that average for every long signal. In the high-score regime, signal a primary long when any of these occurs: the close crosses above the longer close average or gamma-flip level; the score crosses into the high-score regime; a nonbearish bar closes within a specified relative tolerance of the gamma-flip, put-support, or lower-support level; or opening momentum first becomes true. Opening momentum requires a specified early-calendar-day bar window, high score, close above the gamma-flip level and longer average, close above both the bar open and prior close, and a rising short average. Count bars from each change of calendar day. Otherwise, in the same high-score context, signal an aggressive long when the close crosses above the short average. In the intermediate-score regime, signal a cautionary long when the close crosses above the longer average or gamma-flip level.

Signal exit when the close crosses below the gamma-flip level, when it crosses below the longer average while above the gamma-flip level in the high-score regime, or when the score crosses below the lower score threshold. A cross requires the current value to be strictly beyond the boundary and the prior value to be at or on the other side. Signals are evaluated independently on each bar; no position-state or bar-close confirmation filter is specified.
