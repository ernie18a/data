For each confirmed bar, select the current at-the-money strike of the underlying index, retaining the last valid strike if the current one is outside the available option range. Sum the closes of the call and put at that strike and the chosen expiry to form a rolling straddle premium. When that series confirms a pivot high using the specified left and right lookback lengths, signal selling both the call and put at the strike recorded on the pivot bar. The signal occurs only after the right-hand confirmation bars have elapsed. Record the combined premium when the selected contracts are next available and track their current, maximum, and minimum combined premiums. No exit condition is defined.
