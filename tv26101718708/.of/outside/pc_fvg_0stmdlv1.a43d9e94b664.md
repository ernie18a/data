On each supported intraday chart, identify bullish three-candle gaps when the current low exceeds the high two candles earlier, and bearish gaps when the current high is below the low two candles earlier. Each gap suggests a limit entry at its midpoint: long for bullish gaps and short for bearish gaps. Set the initial stop at the middle candle's low for a long or high for a short, and the profit target at a configured multiple of the entry-to-stop distance.

If price closes beyond the far side of an untouched gap, reverse its direction, keep the same entry, move the stop to the middle candle's opposite extreme, and recalculate the target using the same risk multiple. Mark a gap touched when a candle spans its midpoint. After a touch, remove an original gap if price closes beyond its near side; remove a reversed gap if price closes outside either side. A reversal resets the touch state.

When the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled on the shorter intraday charts, show trade levels only for gaps created in the immediately preceding configured time window. Compare that window's final high-low midpoint with the external index's open captured at the configured daily session. If the window midpoint is below that reference, exclude gaps whose midpoint is above the window's running open recorded when the gap formed; if above, exclude gaps whose midpoint is below that open. Equality imposes no side restriction. Also hide gaps older than the configured lookback.
