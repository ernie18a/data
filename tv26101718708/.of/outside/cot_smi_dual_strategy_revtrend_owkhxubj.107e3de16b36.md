Use futures-only COT long and short positions for the instrument’s automatically mapped CFTC market and the selected report, trader category, and position type. Subtract short from long and normalize net positioning within its rolling minimum–maximum range; use the midpoint if the range is zero.

Calculate momentum from the traded instrument: find the rolling highest high and lowest low, EMA-smooth both the close’s distance from their midpoint and the full high–low range, divide the former by half the latter, and scale to a percentage. Use zero when the smoothed range is zero, then smooth the result with an SMA. A bullish trigger is a cross above the oversold level; a bearish trigger is a cross below the overbought level.

When signal arrows are enabled, generate long and short signals from the bullish and bearish triggers respectively. In reversal mode, optionally require normalized COT positioning below the lower extreme for a long or above the upper extreme for a short. Permit only one signal per direction until positioning moves beyond that direction’s extreme threshold by a reset buffer; apply this restriction even when the COT filter is disabled. Reset the relevant signal flag before evaluating each bar, and set it whenever a signal occurs, regardless of whether trading that direction is allowed. In trend mode, optionally require positioning above its midpoint for a long or below it for a short. With the filter disabled, omit the positioning requirement.

Enter only allowed directions. If momentum exits are enabled, close a long on the bearish trigger and a short on the bullish trigger, regardless of COT; do not enter the opposite direction on that same bar. If momentum exits are disabled, an eligible opposite signal closes and reverses the position.

Optionally place take-profit and stop-loss orders at distances from the signal-bar close, using either ATR multiples or fixed percentages, above and below the close for a long and in reverse for a short. Recalculate these levels on every allowed same-direction signal. Optionally size the entry as a chosen fraction of equity divided by the signal-close-to-stop distance; otherwise use the default equity-based size.
