For gold, compare the current intraday percentage moves in the two-year Treasury yield and the US dollar index with a configurable minimum-move threshold. Score each driver +1 when it falls beyond the threshold, −1 when it rises beyond the threshold, and 0 otherwise. A total of +2 indicates a strong long bias, +1 a long bias, 0 neutral, −1 a short bias, and −2 a strong short bias. The dashboard also flags the ten-year real yield as gold-supportive when it is below a configurable threshold, and classifies curve steepening as gold-positive when driven by a falling two-year yield or gold-negative when driven by a rising ten-year yield; these are contextual readings, not additional entry or exit rules.
