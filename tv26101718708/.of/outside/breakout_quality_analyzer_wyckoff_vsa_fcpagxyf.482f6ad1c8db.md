After entering a full-size long position, sell covered calls once follow-through has been confirmed throughout the specified tracking period. The entry setup requires an external momentum screener’s breakout entry signal, a strong breakout-quality score, recent bullish volume-spread evidence with no recent bearish evidence, confirmation from the last closed higher-timeframe bar, and a separate cumulative-volume-delta panel showing bullish smoothed candles, delta above both moving averages, and no bearish divergence. Score breakout quality by combining relative price spread versus volume, close position, pre-breakout volume and range contraction, accumulation versus distribution days, breakout volume surge and rank, spread quality, cumulative-volume-delta confirmation or divergence, estimated buying share, and weighted bullish and bearish volume-spread patterns. Higher-timeframe confirmation uses the corresponding effort, close, volume-surge and volume-spread-pattern scores. Confirm follow-through on each tracked bar when enough of these checks pass: the close exceeds the current prior-high pivot, volume exceeds its relative threshold, and the close is in the upper portion of the bar’s range.
