Track three-candle wick gaps. A bullish gap forms when the current low is above the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap forms when the current high is below the low two bars earlier. On a confirmed close through the far edge, the gap reverses direction: closing below a bullish gap signals short; closing above a bearish gap signals long. Ignore gaps that fail minimum size or age limits, and require the inversion to occur within the configured window. Score each inversion for a recent direction-matched liquidity sweep, a tap into a live higher-timeframe gap in the trade direction, recent large-candle displacement, an untaken profit-side liquidity level with adequate room, and divergence where a correlated instrument does not take its corresponding level. The inversion itself is one point. Print only when the configured grade threshold is met and either a qualifying sweep sponsors the setup or, in permissive mode, at least one additional check passes. In strict sweep mode, require a sweep; associate it with the gap's move. Filter by enabled trading sessions. Suppress duplicate or overlapping same-direction setups, enforce the configured one-trade-per-direction rule, and allow an opposing setup only after it closes through the live setup's invalidation edge and meets the required grade, displacement, and optional swing-break proof. Enter at the inversion close. Set the stop beyond the gap edge or the relevant swing extreme, with a buffer. Set profit objectives at the nearest qualifying liquidity pool and at configured multiples of a chosen price-distance unit. Exit or release the direction when the selected objective is reached, the setup closes through its invalidation edge, or its progress stalls for the configured period; a completed setup cannot later be stopped out.
