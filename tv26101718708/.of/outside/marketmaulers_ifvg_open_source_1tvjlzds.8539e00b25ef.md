On confirmed bars, identify a three-candle price gap and watch it for a limited time. A close above a bearish gap proposes a long; a close below a bullish gap proposes a short. Optionally combine same-direction gaps formed within one swing leg and require a close through the entire combined zone. Apply minimum gap-size and enabled-direction filters.

Grade each inversion by counting the inversion itself and these independent checks: a recent, still-valid sweep of liquidity on the opposite side; a recent touch of a live higher-timeframe gap in the proposed direction; recent large-body displacement; unswept liquidity sufficiently far away on the profit side; and divergence at the sweep, where the traded instrument takes its level but a correlated instrument does not take its corresponding recent extreme. Use the paired index for supported index futures and a configured correlate otherwise. A sweep requires a breach followed by a close back inside within a confirmation window; its credit expires with time or when price exceeds the sweep extreme. Higher-timeframe gaps cease to be live when price closes through their far side. In required-sweep mode, the gap must form near a matching sweep and the higher-timeframe touch must be near that sweep. In graded mode, admit an unsponsored inversion only when it has at least one additional check. Apply the configured minimum grade and optional trading-session filter.

Track each printed setup and its direction. Under the one-trade rule, block another setup in the same direction until the existing setup stops, reaches the configured target rung, or stalls without new target progress. An opposite setup must meet the configured reversal grade and displacement requirements and close through the active opposing zones; optionally also require a break of the prior swing, or disallow reversals entirely. In graded mode also block an active same-direction setup until its liquidity target or stop, and enforce spacing between prints. A qualifying higher-grade setup using the same sweep can replace its predecessor before delivery begins; nearby stacked zones can be merged when combined detection is enabled. Consume inversions rejected by the session or lane rules, and retain other eligible inversions only for the remaining watch window. Preserve the first close-through as the proposed entry even if printing is delayed.

For a printed setup, use the inversion close as entry and place the indicated stop beyond the zone or the originating swing, with a buffer. Project profit rungs in the trade direction using a configured measure of the gap, swing, displacement leg, or entry-to-stop risk. Mark rungs on price touches and the liquidity target on a close beyond it; reaching the first rung gives a break-even cue. Mark the setup stopped on a confirmed close back through the far side of its zone, unless its liquidity target has already been reached.
